# Luke 9

[1] And calling the twelve together, he gave them power and authority over all the demons, and to heal diseases,

[2] and he sent them to proclaim the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and to heal the sick,

[3] and he said to them, "Take nothing for the road, neither staff nor bag nor bread nor silver, nor to have two tunics apiece.

[4] And into whatever house you enter, remain there, and from there go out.

[5] And as many as do not accept you, going out from that city shake off the dust from your feet for a testimony against them."

[6] And going out, they were going through village by village, preaching the gospel and healing everywhere.

[7] Now ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ the tetrarch heard all the things that were happening, and he was utterly at a loss, because it was said by some that ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ was raised from the dead,

[8] and by some that ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ appeared, and by others that a certain prophet of the ancients rose.

[9] But ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ said, "⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ I beheaded; but who is this about whom I hear such things?" And he was seeking to see him.

[10] And returning, the apostles related to him all that they had done. And taking them along, he retired privately to a city called ⟦bethsaida|Beit Tzaidah|Bethsaida⟧.

[11] But the crowds, having found out, followed him. And accepting them, he was speaking to them about the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and those having need of healing he was healing.

[12] Now the day began to decline; and the twelve, approaching, said to him, "Send the crowd away, that going into the villages and farms round about they may take lodging and find provisions, because here we are in a desert place."

[13] But he said to them, "You give them to eat." And they said, "We have no more than five loaves and two fish — unless we ourselves, going, should buy food for all this people."

[14] For there were about five thousand men. And he said to his disciples, "Make them recline in groups, about fifty each."

[15] And they did so and made them all recline.

[16] And taking the five loaves and the two fish, looking up into heaven he blessed them and broke them in pieces and was giving them to the disciples to set before the crowd.

[17] And they ate and were all filled; and what abounded to them was taken up, twelve baskets of fragments.

[18] And it came to pass, as he was praying alone, that the disciples were with him, and he asked them, saying, "Who do the crowds say that I am?"

[19] And they, answering, said, "⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ the ⟦immerser|Immerser|Baptist⟧; but others, ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧; and others, that a certain prophet of the ancients rose."

[20] And he said to them, "But you, who do you say that I am?" And ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, answering, said, "**The ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧ of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.**"

[21] But he, rebuking them, charged them to tell this to no one,

[22] saying, "The Son of Man must suffer many things and be rejected by the elders and high priests and scribes and be killed and on the third day be raised."

[23] And he was saying to all, "**If anyone wants to come after me, let him deny himself and take up his cross day by day and follow me.**

[24] For whoever wants to save his soul will lose it; but whoever loses his soul for my sake, this one will save it.

**[25] For what is a man profited, having gained the whole world but having lost or suffered the loss of himself?**

[26] For whoever is ashamed of me and of my words, of this one the Son of Man will be ashamed when he comes in his glory and that of the Father and of the holy angels.

[27] But I say to you truly, there are some of those standing here who will by no means taste death until they see the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[28] And it came to pass, about eight days after these words, that taking along ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ and ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ he went up into the mountain to pray.

[29] And as he was praying, the appearance of his face became different, and his clothing white, flashing like lightning.

[30] And behold, two men were talking with him, who were ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧,

[31] who, appearing in glory, were speaking of his exodus, which he was about to fulfill in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[32] Now ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and those with him were weighed down with sleep; but coming fully awake, they saw his glory and the two men standing with him.

[33] And it came to pass, as they were separating from him, that ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ said to ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, "Chief, it is good for us to be here, and let us make three tents, one for you and one for ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and one for ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧" — not knowing what he says.

[34] And while he was saying these things, there came a cloud and it was overshadowing them; and they were afraid as they entered into the cloud.

[35] And a voice came out of the cloud, saying, "**This is my Son, the chosen one; hear him.**"

[36] And when the voice came, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was found alone. And they were silent, and reported to no one in those days anything of the things which they had seen.

[37] And it came to pass on the next day, when they had come down from the mountain, that a great crowd met him.

[38] And behold, a man from the crowd cried, saying, "Teacher, I beseech you to look upon my son, because he is my only one,

[39] and behold, a spirit takes him, and suddenly cries out, and convulses him with foam, and hardly withdraws from him, crushing him;

[40] and I besought your disciples that they should cast it out, and they were not able."

[41] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, answering, said, "O unbelieving and perverted generation, how long shall I be with you and bear with you? Bring your son here."

[42] And while he was still approaching, the demon dashed him down and convulsed him; but ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ rebuked the unclean spirit and healed the boy and gave him back to his father.

[43] And all were astonished at the majesty of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

But while all were marveling at all the things that he was doing, he said to his disciples,

[44] "You, put these words into your ears: for the Son of Man is about to be delivered up into the hands of men."

[45] But they did not understand this word, and it was veiled from them, that they should not perceive it, and they were afraid to ask him about this word.

[46] And there entered a reasoning among them: who of them might be greater.

[47] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, knowing the reasoning of their heart, laying hold of a young child, stood it beside himself

[48] and said to them, "Whoever accepts this young child upon my name accepts me, and whoever accepts me accepts the one who sent me; for the one who is lesser among you all, this one is great."

[49] And ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, answering, said, "Chief, we saw someone casting out demons in your name, and we were hindering him, because he does not follow with us."

[50] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to him, "Do not hinder, for he who is not against you is for you."

[51] And it came to pass, when the days of his taking up were being fulfilled, that he himself set his face firmly to go to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,

[52] and he sent messengers before his face. And going, they entered into a village of the ⟦samaritans|Shomronim|Samaritans⟧, so as to make ready for him;

[53] and they did not accept him, because his face was going to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[54] And seeing it, the disciples ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ said, "Lord, do you want us to tell fire to come down from heaven and consume them?"

[55] But turning, he rebuked them.

[56] And they went to another village.

[57] And as they were going on the road, someone said to him, "I will follow you wherever you go."

[58] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to him, "The foxes have dens, and the birds of the heaven dwelling places, but the Son of Man has nowhere he may lay his head."

[59] And he said to another, "Follow me." But he said, "Lord, permit me, going away, first to bury my father."

[60] But he said to him, "Leave the dead to bury their own dead; but you, going away, declare the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[61] And another also said, "I will follow you, Lord; but first permit me to take leave of those in my house."

[62] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to him, "**No one having laid his hand on a plow and looking to the things behind is fit for the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.**"

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v28, 54) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv2, 11, 20, 27, 43, 60, 62) — divine name retained.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v20) — Hebrew Anointed; "*Kefa*, answering, said, 'The *Mashiach* of *Elohim*.'"
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (vv20, 28, 32, 33) — Aramaic name preserved.
- **"John the Baptist" → "John the Immerser"** (v19) — "Immerser" renders *baptistēs*; consistent rendering.
- **"to heal the sick" kept** (v2) — τοὺς ἀσθενεῖς "the sick" is read by Tregelles and NA28 (the Byzantine text has τοὺς ἀσθενοῦντας); Westcott-Hort omit the words. ἀσθενής is literally "weak". Both θεραπεύω (v1, 6) and ἰάομαι (v2, 11, 42) are "heal"; in v11 "healing" (θεραπεία) and "was healing" (ἰᾶτο) are the two roots side by side.
- **"scrip … money … two coats apiece" → "bag … silver … two tunics apiece"** (v3) — ἀργύριον is "silver"; here it means money. ἀνά "apiece" is read by Tregelles and NA28; Westcott-Hort omit it. The Greek passes from the command "take" to an infinitive, "nor to have"; the break is kept.
- **"there abide" → "remain there"** (v4) — Greek μένετε, the verb μένω ("abide" of persons), here of a stay in a place: "remain" (as 8:27; 10:7).
- **"receive" → "accept"** (v5, 11, 48, 53) — δέχομαι, ἀποδέχομαι (v11); "receive" is kept for λαμβάνω.
- **"shake off"** (v5) — ἀποτινάσσω, present imperative; Mark 6:11 and Matt 10:14 have ἐκτινάσσω. "Against them" is ἐπʼ αὐτούς (Mark: "to them").
- **"went through the towns" → "were going through village by village"** (v6) — κατὰ τὰς κώμας, distributive.
- **"was perplexed" → "was utterly at a loss"** (v7) — διηπόρει, a strengthened ἀπορέω "be at a loss".
- **"one of the old prophets was risen again" → "a certain prophet of the ancients rose"** (v8, 19) — προφήτης τις τῶν ἀρχαίων ἀνέστη; the aorists "was raised", "appeared", "rose" are kept as past forms.
- **"desired to see him" → "was seeking to see him"** (v9) — ἐζήτει, imperfect of ζητέω "seek".
- **"went aside privately into a desert place belonging to the city called Bethsaida" → "retired privately to a city called Bethsaida"** (v10) — εἰς πόλιν καλουμένην Βηθσαϊδά (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28); the longer "desert place of a city" is the Byzantine reading. ὑποχωρέω "retire" is a different verb from ἀναχωρέω "withdraw".
- **"began to wear away" → "began to decline"** (v12) — κλίνειν, the verb of v58 ("lay his head"): to lean, to bend down.
- **"lodge" → "take lodging"** (v12) — καταλύω, literally "unloose" (unyoke the animals, so: stop for the night); elsewhere the same verb is "tear down".
- **"victuals" → "provisions"** (v12) — ἐπισιτισμός, food for a journey; "food" in v13 is βρώματα.
- **"Make them sit down by fifties in a company" → "Make them recline in groups, about fifty each"** (v14–15) — κατακλίνω "make recline"; κλισία, a group reclining for a meal (Mark 6:39 has another word, συμπόσια).
- **"that remained" → "what abounded to them"** (v17) — τὸ περισσεῦσαν αὐτοῖς; "fragments" is κλάσματα, broken pieces.
- **"straitly charged them, and commanded" → "rebuking them, charged them"** (v21) — ἐπιτιμήσας … παρήγγειλεν; ἐπιτιμάω is "rebuke" everywhere (v42, 55), here with the sense "sternly warn".
- **"chief priests" → "high priests"** (v22) — ἀρχιερεῖς, the plural of "high priest".
- **"will come after me" → "wants to come after me"** (v23–24) — θέλει, θέλῃ "wants", not a future.
- **"daily" → "day by day"** (v23) — καθʼ ἡμέραν, as in Acts 2:46–47; Luke alone has the words in this saying.
- **"life" → "soul"** (v24) — Greek ψυχή *psychē*, the same word in both halves of the verse: the soul, the self, the life of a person.
- **"if he gain the whole world, and lose himself, or be cast away" → "having gained the whole world but having lost or suffered the loss of himself"** (v25) — three aorist participles, κερδήσας … ἀπολέσας ἢ ζημιωθείς; ζημιόω is "suffer loss, be fined". "Is profited" renders the passive ὠφελεῖται.
- **"in his own glory, and in his Father's" → "in his glory and that of the Father"** (v26) — ἐν τῇ δόξῃ αὐτοῦ καὶ τοῦ πατρὸς καὶ τῶν ἁγίων ἀγγέλων: one "glory" with three genitives; "his" is not repeated with "Father".
- **"I tell you of a truth" → "I say to you truly"** (v27) — λέγω δὲ ὑμῖν ἀληθῶς; Luke has ἀληθῶς where Mark 9:1 has "Amen". "By no means" renders the strong negation οὐ μή.
- **"sayings" → "words"** (v28, 44) — λόγοι. In v45 "word" is ῥῆμα, a different Greek word (the thing said).
- **"the fashion of his countenance was altered" → "the appearance of his face became different"** (v29) — τὸ εἶδος τοῦ προσώπου αὐτοῦ ἕτερον. The verse begins καὶ ἐγένετο; no other verb follows, so "became" renders it. Luke does not use Mark's "was transformed".
- **"white and glistering" → "white, flashing like lightning"** (v29) — ἐξαστράπτων, from ἀστραπή "lightning".
- **"decease" → "exodus"** (v31) — Greek ἔξοδος *exodos*, "a going out, departure"; the word is the Greek name of Israel's going out of Egypt, and it is also used for death (2 Pet 1:15). The word is kept; the sense is "departure". "Fulfill" is πληροῦν.
- **"when they were awake" → "coming fully awake"** (v32) — διαγρηγορήσαντες, found only here in the New Testament; it can also mean "having stayed awake throughout".
- **"Master" → "Chief"** (v33, 49) — Greek ἐπιστάτης *epistatēs*, "one set over" others; only Luke uses it. It is not διδάσκαλος "Teacher" (v38) nor κύριος "Lord" (v54); Mark 9:5 has "Rabbi" here.
- **"tabernacles" → "tents"** (v33) — σκηναί.
- **"not knowing what he said" → "not knowing what he says"** (v33) — λέγει, present tense.
- **"as they entered into the cloud"** (v34) — ἐν τῷ εἰσελθεῖν αὐτοὺς; the Greek does not say who "they" are (the three disciples, or *Yeshua*, Moses and Elijah). "Was overshadowing" is the imperfect ἐπεσκίαζεν.
- **"my beloved Son" → "my Son, the chosen one"** (v35) — ὁ ἐκλελεγμένος "the one who has been chosen" (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28); "the beloved" (ἀγαπητός) is the Byzantine reading, as in Mark 9:7. In the Greek "him" stands first: αὐτοῦ ἀκούετε.
- **"he is mine only child" → "he is my only one"** (v38) — μονογενής, "only"; "child" is not in the Greek.
- **"a spirit taketh him, and he suddenly crieth out; and it teareth him that he foameth again, and bruising him hardly departeth from him" → "a spirit takes him, and suddenly cries out, and convulses him with foam, and hardly withdraws from him, crushing him"** (v39) — the Greek names no new subject for κράζει "cries out"; it may be the spirit or the boy. μετὰ ἀφροῦ is "with foam".
- **"faithless and perverse" → "unbelieving and perverted"** (v41) — ἄπιστος καὶ διεστραμμένη, as in Matt 17:17.
- **"threw him down, and tare him" → "dashed him down and convulsed him"** (v42) — ἔρρηξεν … συνεσπάραξεν. "Boy" is παῖς (child or young servant); "son" in v38, 41 is υἱός.
- **"mighty power" → "majesty"** (v43) — μεγαλειότης.
- **"Jesus" omitted** (v43, 60) — the Greek has only "he said"; the name is the Byzantine reading in both verses. In v62 the text reads "*Yeshua* said to him" (πρὸς αὐτὸν ὁ Ἰησοῦς, Westcott-Hort and NA28; Tregelles has the same words in another order; the printed SBLGNT omits "to him").
- **"Let these sayings sink down into your ears" → "You, put these words into your ears"** (v44) — θέσθε ὑμεῖς εἰς τὰ ὦτα ὑμῶν; "you" is expressed and emphatic. "Is about to be delivered up" is μέλλει παραδίδοσθαι; the verb is "hand over", not "betray".
- **"it was hid from them" → "it was veiled from them"** (v45) — παρακεκαλυμμένον, "covered over". ἵνα μὴ αἴσθωνται: "that they should not perceive it" (purpose or result).
- **"there arose a reasoning among them, which of them should be greatest" → "there entered a reasoning among them: who of them might be greater"** (v46) — εἰσῆλθεν "entered"; ἐν αὐτοῖς is "among them" or "in them" (v47: "the reasoning of their heart"); μείζων is the comparative "greater".
- **"took a child" → "laying hold of a young child"** (v47) — ἐπιλαβόμενος; παιδίον, a little child. "Knowing" (εἰδώς) is read by Westcott-Hort and NA28; Tregelles and the Byzantine text have ἰδών "seeing".
- **"in my name" → "upon my name"** (v48) — ἐπὶ τῷ ὀνόματί μου; in v49 "in your name" is ἐν τῷ ὀνόματί σου.
- **"he that is least among you all, the same shall be great" → "the one who is lesser among you all, this one is great"** (v48) — μικρότερος, a comparative; ἐστιν "is" (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28), where the Byzantine text has "shall be".
- **"we forbad him" → "we were hindering him"** (v49–50) — ἐκωλύομεν, imperfect (also "we tried to hinder him"); κωλύω is "hinder". In v50 "hinder" has no object in the Greek. "Against you … for you" (ὑμῶν) is the reading of the critical editions; "against us … for us" is Byzantine, as in Mark 9:40.
- **"when the time was come that he should be received up" → "when the days of his taking up were being fulfilled"** (v51) — ἐν τῷ συμπληροῦσθαι τὰς ἡμέρας τῆς ἀναλήμψεως αὐτοῦ; ἀνάλημψις is the noun of the verb "be taken up" in Acts 1:2, 11, 22. The same "be fulfilled" stands in Acts 2:1.
- **"stedfastly set his face" → "set his face firmly"** (v51) — τὸ πρόσωπον ἐστήρισεν; στηρίζω is elsewhere "make stable". "Before his face" (v52) and "his face was going" (v53) keep the same word πρόσωπον.
- **"his disciples" → "the disciples"** (v54) — "his" is read only by Tregelles and the Byzantine text. The Byzantine addition "as Elijah also did" is not in the text.
- **"and said, Ye know not what manner of spirit ye are of. For the Son of man is not come to destroy men's lives, but to save them" omitted** (v55–56) — these words stand only in the Byzantine text; Westcott-Hort, Tregelles and NA28 do not have them.
- **"holes … nests" → "dens … dwelling places"** (v58) — φωλεοί, κατασκηνώσεις (places to camp or dwell; not the word for a nest), as in Matt 8:20, whose Greek is the same.
- **"suffer me first to go and bury" → "permit me, going away, first to bury"** (v59) — ἐπίτρεψόν μοι ἀπελθόντι πρῶτον θάψαι; Matt 8:21 has two infinitives. "Lord" is read by Tregelles and NA28; Westcott-Hort omit it.
- **"preach" → "declare"** (v60) — διάγγελλε, "announce far and wide" (as Rom 9:17); "proclaim" in v2 is κηρύσσω.
- **"bid them farewell, which are at home at my house" → "take leave of those in my house"** (v61) — ἀποτάξασθαι τοῖς εἰς τὸν οἶκόν μου.
- **"looking back" → "looking to the things behind"** (v62) — βλέπων εἰς τὰ ὀπίσω.

### Notes
- **v18, 28, 33, 37, 51** — "And it came to pass" renders Luke's ἐγένετο with a following clause, as in Acts.
- Much of this chapter stands also in Mark 6, 8–9 and Matt 8, 10, 14, 16–17; where the Greek is the same, the wording is the same.
