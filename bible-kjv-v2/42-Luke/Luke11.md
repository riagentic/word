# Luke 11

[1] And it came to pass, as he was in a certain place praying, that when he ceased, one of his disciples said to him, "Lord, teach us to pray, just as ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ also taught his disciples."

[2] And he said to them, "When you pray, say:

**Father, let your name be sanctified;
let your kingdom come;
[3] our daily bread give us day by day;
[4] and forgive us our sins,
for we ourselves also forgive everyone who owes us;
and do not bring us into trial.**"

[5] And he said to them, "Who of you will have a friend and will go to him at midnight and say to him, 'Friend, lend me three loaves,

[6] because a friend of mine has arrived to me from a journey, and I do not have what I will set before him';

[7] and that one from inside will answer and say, 'Do not give me troubles; already the door has been shut, and my young children are with me in the bed; I am not able to rise and give to you'?

[8] I say to you, even if he will not rise and give to him because he is his friend, yet because of his shamelessness he will arise and give him as many as he needs.

[9] "And I say to you, **ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you.**

**[10] For everyone who asks receives, and the one who seeks finds, and to the one who knocks it will be opened.**

[11] And what father is there of you whom the son will ask for a fish — and instead of a fish will he hand him a serpent?

[12] Or he will also ask for an egg — will he hand him a scorpion?

[13] If therefore you, being evil, know to give good gifts to your children, how much more will the Father who is from heaven give ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧ to those who ask him."

[14] And he was casting out a demon, and it was mute; and it came to pass, when the demon had gone out, that the mute man spoke. And the crowds marveled;

[15] but some of them said, "By Beelzebul, the ruler of the demons, he casts out the demons";

[16] and others, tempting, were seeking from him a sign out of heaven.

[17] But he, knowing their thoughts, said to them, "Every kingdom divided against itself is made desolate, and house falls upon house.

[18] And if Satan also has been divided against himself, how will his kingdom stand? Because you say that by Beelzebul I cast out the demons.

[19] But if I by Beelzebul cast out the demons, by whom do your sons cast them out? Because of this they themselves will be your judges.

[20] But if by the finger of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ I cast out the demons, then the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has come upon you.

[21] When the strong man, fully armed, guards his own court, what belongs to him is in peace;

[22] but when one stronger than he comes upon him and overcomes him, he takes away his full armor in which he had confidence, and distributes his spoils.

[23] The one who is not with me is against me, and the one who does not gather with me scatters.

[24] "When the unclean spirit goes out from the man, it passes through waterless places seeking rest, and not finding it, then it says, 'I will return into my house, from where I came out.'

[25] And coming, it finds it swept and adorned.

[26] Then it goes and takes along seven other spirits more evil than itself, and entering they dwell there; and the last things of that man become worse than the first."

[27] Now it came to pass, as he was saying these things, that a certain woman out of the crowd, lifting up her voice, said to him, "Blessed is the womb that bore you, and the breasts which you sucked!"

[28] But he said, "**Rather, blessed are those who hear the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and guard it.**"

[29] And as the crowds were gathering more and more, he began to say, "This generation is an evil generation; it seeks a sign, and a sign will not be given to it except the sign of ⟦jonah|Yonah|Jonah⟧.

[30] For just as ⟦jonah|Yonah|Jonah⟧ became a sign to the people of ⟦nineveh|Ninveh|Nineveh⟧, so also will the Son of Man be to this generation.

[31] The queen of the south will be raised in the judgment with the men of this generation and will condemn them, because she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧; and behold, something more than ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ is here.

[32] Men of ⟦nineveh|Ninveh|Nineveh⟧ will rise in the judgment with this generation and will condemn it, because they repented at the proclamation of ⟦jonah|Yonah|Jonah⟧; and behold, something more than ⟦jonah|Yonah|Jonah⟧ is here.

[33] "No one, having lit a lamp, puts it into a hidden place, nor under the bushel measure, but upon the lampstand, so that those who come in may see the light.

[34] The lamp of the body is your eye. When your eye is single, your whole body also is bright; but when it is evil, your body also is dark.

[35] Look therefore lest the light that is in you is darkness.

[36] If therefore your whole body is bright, not having any part dark, it will be wholly bright, as when the lamp with its flash illumines you."

[37] Now when he had spoken, a ⟦pharisee|Parush|Pharisee⟧ requests him to dine with him; and going in, he reclined.

[38] But the ⟦pharisee|Parush|Pharisee⟧, seeing it, marveled that he did not first ⟦immerse|immerse|baptize⟧ himself before the dinner.

[39] But the Lord said to him, "Now you ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ cleanse the outside of the cup and of the platter, but your inside is full of seizing and evil.

[40] Senseless ones, did not he who made the outside make the inside also?

[41] Yet give the things that are within as alms, and behold, all things are clean to you.

[42] "But woe to you, the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧, because you tithe the mint and the rue and every garden herb, and pass by the judgment and the love of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; but these things it was necessary to do, and those not to neglect.

[43] Woe to you, the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧, because you love the first seat in the synagogues and the greetings in the marketplaces.

[44] Woe to you, because you are as the tombs that are not seen, and the men who walk over them do not know it."

[45] And one of the lawyers, answering, says to him, "Teacher, saying these things you treat us also shamefully."

[46] But he said, "To you also, the lawyers, woe, because you load men with loads hard to bear, and you yourselves do not touch the loads with one of your fingers.

[47] Woe to you, because you build the tombs of the prophets, but your fathers killed them.

[48] So then you are witnesses and you consent to the works of your fathers, because they indeed killed them, and you build.

[49] Because of this the wisdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ also said, 'I will send to them prophets and apostles, and some of them they will kill and persecute,'

[50] so that the blood of all the prophets, which has been poured out from the foundation of the world, may be required from this generation,

[51] from the blood of ⟦abel|Hevel|Abel⟧ unto the blood of ⟦zechariah-nt|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧, who perished between the altar and the house. Yes, I say to you, it will be required from this generation.

[52] Woe to you, the lawyers, because you took away the key of knowledge; you yourselves did not enter, and those who were entering you hindered."

[53] And when he had gone out from there, the scribes and the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ began to hold it terribly against him and to press him to speak about more things,

[54] lying in ambush for him, to hunt something out of his mouth.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv20, 28, 42, 49) — divine name retained.
- **"Holy Spirit" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v13) — Greek πνεῦμα ἅγιον, here without the article.
- **"as he was praying" → "as he was in a certain place praying"** (v1) — Greek καὶ ἐγένετο ἐν τῷ εἶναι αὐτὸν … προσευχόμενον. The Greek does not name Jesus in v1 or v37 ("he").
- **"Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name … Thy will be done, as in heaven, so in earth … but deliver us from evil" → "Father, let your name be sanctified; let your kingdom come … and do not bring us into trial"** (vv2–4) — the three longer clauses stand in RP only: Πάτερ (WH Treg NA28; RP + ἡμῶν ὁ ἐν τοῖς οὐρανοῖς), σου (WH Treg NA28; RP + Γενηθήτω τὸ θέλημά σου ὡς ἐν οὐρανῷ καὶ ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς), πειρασμόν (WH Treg NA28; RP + ἀλλὰ ῥῦσαι ἡμᾶς ἀπὸ τοῦ πονηροῦ). Luke's prayer is shorter than Matt 6:9–13 and is not filled from it. ἁγιασθήτω is the verb ἁγιάζω "sanctify"; πειρασμός is one word for "testing, trial" and "temptation" (the verb πειράζω, "tempt", v16).
- **"Give us day by day our daily bread" → "our daily bread give us day by day"** (v3) — Greek order; δίδου is a present imperative, "keep giving" (Matt 6:11 δός … σήμερον, "give … today"); τὸ καθʼ ἡμέραν, "day by day". ἐπιούσιος is found nowhere before the gospels (here and Matt 6:11) and its meaning is not certain: (1) "for the coming day", (2) "needful for existence", (3) "for the present day". "Daily" is the traditional word; it is not certain.
- **"every one that is indebted to us" → "everyone who owes us"** (v4) — Greek παντὶ ὀφείλοντι ἡμῖν, the verb ὀφείλω "owe"; "sins" is ἁμαρτίας (Matt 6:12 "debts"). ἀφίομεν is a present, "we forgive" (Matt "have forgiven").
- **"Which of you shall have a friend" → "Who of you will have a friend"** (v5–7) — Greek τίς ἐξ ὑμῶν ἕξει φίλον; the question runs on through v7 and is not finished (SBLGNT prints a full stop after v7). ἐκεῖνος, "that one"; παιδία, "young children"; εἰς τὴν κοίτην, "in the bed".
- **"Trouble me not" → "Do not give me troubles"** (v7) — Greek μή μοι κόπους πάρεχε, as Gal 6:17.
- **"importunity" → "shamelessness"** (v8) — Greek ἀναίδεια, "lack of shame"; "persistence" is not in the word. "His" (αὐτοῦ) can be the one who asks or the one inside. ἀναστάς "rise" and ἐγερθείς "arise" are two verbs; ὅσων, "as many as".
- **"If a son shall ask bread of any of you that is a father, will he give him a stone? or if he ask a fish" → "And what father is there of you whom the son will ask for a fish"** (v11) — the bread and the stone (ἄρτον μὴ λίθον ἐπιδώσει αὐτῷ ἢ καί, Treg RP) are not in WH NA28; they come from Matt 7:9. καί "and" (NA28; WH Treg RP μή, "will he instead of a fish hand him a serpent?"; 2–2, NA28 decides). ἐπιδίδωμι, "hand to", as Matt 7:9–10.
- **"your heavenly Father" → "the Father who is from heaven"** (v13) — Greek ὁ πατὴρ ὁ ἐξ οὐρανοῦ, literally "the Father, the one out of heaven"; no "your". ὑπάρχοντες, "being" (Matt 7:11 ὄντες).
- **"and it was dumb" kept: "and it was mute"** (v14) — καὶ αὐτὸ ἦν (Treg NA28 RP; WH omits: "a mute demon"). κωφός is one word for "deaf" and "mute"; here the man then speaks.
- **"through Beelzebub the chief of the devils" → "By Beelzebul, the ruler of the demons"** (v15, 18, 19) — Greek ἐν Βεελζεβοὺλ τῷ ἄρχοντι τῶν δαιμονίων, as Matt 12:24, 27; Mark 3:22.
- **"tempting him" → "tempting"** (v16) — Greek πειράζοντες, without an object; ἐξ οὐρανοῦ, "out of heaven".
- **"a house divided against a house falleth" → "house falls upon house"** (v17) — Greek οἶκος ἐπὶ οἶκον πίπτει, four words. Either houses fall one upon another in the desolated kingdom, or "a house against a house falls" (a divided house, as Mark 3:25). διανοήματα, "thoughts" (Matt 12:25 ἐνθυμήσεις "reflections").
- **"I with the finger of God"** (v20) — ἐγώ "I" (WH Treg NA28; RP omits) is in the text; Matt 12:28 has "by the Spirit of God".
- **"a strong man armed keepeth his palace, his goods are in peace" → "the strong man, fully armed, guards his own court, what belongs to him is in peace"** (v21) — Greek ὁ ἰσχυρός with the article; αὐλή, "court"; τὰ ὑπάρχοντα, "what belongs to him"; ἐν εἰρήνῃ, "in peace".
- **"all his armour wherein he trusted" → "his full armor in which he had confidence"** (v22) — Greek πανοπλία, as Eph 6:11; ἐπεποίθει, pluperfect of πέποιθα; διαδίδωσιν, "distributes".
- **"and finding none, he saith" → "and not finding it, then it says"** (v24) — τότε "then" (WH NA28; Treg RP omit; 2–2, NA28 decides). "It": πνεῦμα is neuter; the gender is grammatical.
- **"swept and garnished" → "swept and adorned"** (v25) — Greek κεκοσμημένον; WH adds σχολάζοντα "unoccupied" (Matt 12:44), which is not in the text here. "It" (the house) is supplied in English.
- **"taketh to him" → "takes along"** (v26) — Greek παραλαμβάνει; Matt 12:45 adds "with itself". τὰ ἔσχατα … τῶν πρώτων, "the last things … the first".
- **"the paps which thou hast sucked" → "the breasts which you sucked"** (v27) — Greek μαστοὶ οὓς ἐθήλασας.
- **"Yea rather, blessed are they that hear the word of God, and keep it" → "Rather, blessed are those who hear the word of Elohim and guard it"** (v28) — Greek μενοῦν, which corrects or strengthens ("rather", "yes indeed, but"); φυλάσσοντες, the verb "guard" (τηρέω is "keep"). "It" is supplied (RP adds αὐτόν).
- **"when the people were gathered thick together" → "as the crowds were gathering more and more"** (v29) — Greek ἐπαθροιζομένων, only here in the NT: "gather in addition". ζητεῖ, "seeks" (Matt 12:39 ἐπιζητεῖ "seeks after"); "the prophet" (RP) is not in the text.
- **"shall rise up in the judgment" → "will be raised in the judgment"** (v31) — Greek ἐγερθήσεται, a passive; in v32 ἀναστήσονται, "will rise". πλεῖον is neuter: "something more".
- **"preaching" → "proclamation"** (v32) — Greek κήρυγμα.
- **"in a secret place, neither under a bushel, but on a candlestick" → "into a hidden place, nor under the bushel measure, but upon the lampstand"** (v33) — Greek κρύπτη, a hidden or covered place (a cellar, a vault). "Light" is φῶς (WH Treg NA28; RP φέγγος, also "light").
- **"The light of the body is the eye" → "The lamp of the body is your eye"** (v34) — Greek λύχνος, the word of v33, 36; σου "your" (WH Treg NA28; RP omits). ἁπλοῦς, "single, simple, without a second aim", also "generous"; its opposite is πονηρός "evil" (an "evil eye" is a grudging one). φωτεινόν, σκοτεινόν are plain adjectives, "bright", "dark" (as Matt 6:22–23).
- **"Take heed therefore that the light which is in thee be not darkness" → "Look therefore lest the light that is in you is darkness"** (v35) — Greek σκόπει … μή with the indicative ἐστίν: the darkness is feared as a fact.
- **"the bright shining of a candle" → "the lamp with its flash"** (v36) — Greek τῇ ἀστραπῇ, the word for "lightning" (17:24), here of the beam of a lamp.
- **"besought him to dine" → "requests him to dine"** (v37) — Greek ἐρωτᾷ, a present; ἀριστάω is to take the ἄριστον (v38), the earlier meal of the day (John 21:12 "have breakfast"; Matt 22:4 "dinner"). ἀνέπεσεν, "reclined", literally "fell back".
- **"that he had not first washed" → "that he did not first immerse himself"** (v38) — Greek ἐβαπτίσθη, the verb βαπτίζω "immerse", passive in form ("was not immersed"); the washing before a meal, as Mark 7:4.
- **"your inward part is full of ravening and wickedness" → "your inside is full of seizing and evil"** (v39) — Greek ἁρπαγή, "seizing, plunder" (as Matt 23:25); πονηρία, of one root with πονηρός "evil" (v13, 29, 34). πίναξ, "platter" (Matt 23:25 παροψίς "dish").
- **"Ye fools" → "Senseless ones"** (v40) — Greek ἄφρονες; "fool" is μωρός.
- **"give alms of such things as ye have" → "give the things that are within as alms"** (v41) — Greek τὰ ἐνόντα, "the things that are in" (the cup and platter); it has also been taken as "what you have". πλήν, "yet".
- **"all manner of herbs, and pass over judgment … and not to leave the other undone" → "every garden herb, and pass by the judgment … and those not to neglect"** (v42) — Greek παρέρχεσθε, "pass by"; κρίσις, "judgment"; παρεῖναι (WH Treg NA28; RP ἀφιέναι "leave", as Matt 23:23). "The love of Elohim" is a plain genitive: love for God, or God's love.
- **"the uppermost seats" → "the first seat"** (v43) — Greek πρωτοκαθεδρία, singular.
- **"scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye are as graves which appear not" → "you are as the tombs that are not seen"** (v44) — "scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites" (RP) is not in the text. μνημεῖα, "tombs"; ἄδηλα, "not evident, unseen". "Them" and "it" are supplied.
- **"thou reproachest us also" → "you treat us also shamefully"** (v45) — Greek ὑβρίζεις, as 1 Thess 2:2; here by words ("insult"). νομικός is a man learned in the law (νόμος), the *Torah*.
- **"burdens grievous to be borne" → "loads hard to bear"** (v46) — Greek φορτία δυσβάστακτα, as Matt 23:4; φορτίζετε is the verb of the same root, "you load".
- **"ye build the sepulchres of the prophets, and your fathers killed them" → "you build the tombs of the prophets, but your fathers killed them"** (v47) — Greek μνημεῖα; οἱ δὲ πατέρες, "and / but your fathers".
- **"Truly ye bear witness that ye allow the deeds … and ye build their sepulchres" → "So then you are witnesses and you consent to the works … and you build"** (v48) — μάρτυρές ἐστε (WH Treg NA28; RP μαρτυρεῖτε); "their tombs" (RP) is not in the text.
- **"may be required of this generation" kept: "may be required from this generation"** (v50, 51) — Greek ἐκζητηθῇ, the verb ἐκζητέω "seek out" (Heb 11:6), here of blood sought back from the one answerable, as Gen 9:5; ἐκκεχυμένον, "poured out".
- **"between the altar and the temple" → "between the altar and the house"** (v51) — Greek τοῦ οἴκου, the house of God (Matt 23:35 ναοῦ "sanctuary"). ἀπολομένου, "perished".
- **"them that were entering in ye hindered"** (v52) — Greek ἤρατε, "you took away"; εἰσήλθατε, ἐκωλύσατε are aorists.
- **"And as he said these things unto them … to urge him vehemently, and to provoke him to speak of many things" → "And when he had gone out from there … to hold it terribly against him and to press him to speak about more things"** (v53) — Κἀκεῖθεν ἐξελθόντος αὐτοῦ (WH Treg NA28; RP Λέγοντος δὲ αὐτοῦ ταῦτα πρὸς αὐτούς). ἐνέχειν as Mark 6:19; ἀποστοματίζειν, only here in the NT: elsewhere "to recite by heart, to dictate", here to draw answers from his mouth by questions; περὶ πλειόνων, "about more things".
- **"laying wait for him, and seeking to catch something out of his mouth, that they might accuse him" → "lying in ambush for him, to hunt something out of his mouth"** (v54) — Greek ἐνεδρεύοντες (as Acts 23:21), θηρεῦσαι "hunt, catch as prey"; "seeking" and "that they might accuse him" (Treg RP) are not in WH NA28.

### Notes
- **v2-4** — a shorter form of the Lord's Prayer than Matt 6:9-13; later MSS expand with Matthean wording.
- **v51** — Abel to Zechariah spans the Hebrew canon (Gen to Chron).
