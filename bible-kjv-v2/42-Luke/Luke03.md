## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v21, 23) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv2, 6, 8, 38) — divine name retained.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v15) — Hebrew Anointed.
- **"baptism of repentance" → "immersion of repentance"** (v3) — Greek *baptisma metanoias*; consistent rendering.
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (vv7, 12, 21) — Greek *baptizō*.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v16) — Hebrew divine Spirit.

### Notes
- **v4-6** quotes Isa 40:3-5 (LXX).
- **v23-38** — Luke's genealogy traces through Mary's line (via Heli/Eli) back to Adam, universalizing *Yeshua's* descent; Matthew's traces through Joseph's royal Davidic line.
