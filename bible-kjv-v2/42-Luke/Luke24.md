## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v10) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v3, 15, 19) — Hebrew name preserved. In v36 the Greek has only αὐτός, "he himself" ("Jesus" is in RP alone); the name supplied there earlier is removed.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v19, 53) — divine name retained.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v26, 46) — Hebrew Anointed.
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (v12) — Aramaic name preserved.
- **"Torah" for Law** — in v44, "Law of Moses" corresponds to *Torah*; the Notes reference the Hebrew term.
- **"Now upon the first day of the week, very early in the morning, they came unto the sepulchre, bringing the spices which they had prepared, and certain others with them" → "but on the first day of the week, at early dawn, they came to the grave, bringing the spices which they had made ready"** (v1) — Greek τῇ δὲ μιᾷ τῶν σαββάτων: the δέ answers the μέν of 23:56, and the sentence runs on from there, so the verse begins without a capital. μιᾷ τῶν σαββάτων, "day one of the week" (Mark 16:2); ὄρθρου βαθέως, literally "at deep dawn", the first of the dawn; μνῆμα, "grave" (in v2 and after μνημεῖον, "tomb"); ἡτοίμασαν, as 23:56; "and certain others with them" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"And they entered in, and found not the body of the Lord Jesus" → "but entering, they did not find the body of the Lord Yeshua"** (v3) — Greek εἰσελθοῦσαι δέ (WH Treg NA28). Editions: σῶμα + τοῦ κυρίου Ἰησοῦ Treg NA28 RP, "the body of the Lord Yeshua" ] σῶμα alone WH (and the printed SBLGNT), "the body": the verse follows the majority.
- **"as they were much perplexed thereabout, behold, two men stood by them in shining garments" → "while they were at a loss about this, that behold, two men stood by them in flashing clothing"** (v4) — Greek καὶ ἐγένετο … καὶ ἰδού; ἀπορεῖσθαι (WH Treg NA28), "be at a loss" (the verb of ἀπορία, 21:25); ἐπέστησαν (Acts 12:7); ἐσθῆτι ἀστραπτούσῃ, singular, "clothing flashing like lightning" (ἀστραπή).
- **"And as they were afraid, and bowed down their faces to the earth, they said unto them, Why seek ye the living among the dead?" → "And as they became afraid and were bowing their faces to the earth, they said to them, 'Why do you seek the living one with the dead?'"** (v5) — Greek ἐμφόβων γενομένων (Acts 10:4); κλινουσῶν, a present participle; τὸν ζῶντα μετὰ τῶν νεκρῶν, "the one who lives, with the dead".
- **"He is not here, but is risen: remember how he spake unto you when he was yet in Galilee" → "He is not here, but was raised. Remember how he spoke to you while he was still in Galilee"** (v6) — Greek ἠγέρθη, a passive and a simple past, "was raised" (as Mark 16:6; Matt 28:6). The words "He is not here, but was raised" are in the text of Treg and NA28 (and RP); WH prints them in double brackets. The verse follows the majority of the four editions.
- **"Saying, The Son of man must be delivered into the hands of sinful men" → "saying of the Son of Man that he must be delivered up into the hands of sinful men"** (v7) — Greek λέγων τὸν υἱὸν τοῦ ἀνθρώπου ὅτι δεῖ (WH Treg NA28), with "the Son of Man" put before "that"; παραδοθῆναι, "be delivered up"; ἀναστῆναι, "rise".
- **"and told all these things unto the eleven" → "they reported all these things to the eleven"** (v9) — Greek ἀπήγγειλαν.
- **"It was Mary Magdalene, and Joanna, and Mary the mother of James, and other women that were with them, which told these things unto the apostles" → "Now they were the Magdalene Mary and Joanna and Mary the mother of James and the rest with them; they were saying these things to the apostles"** (v10) — Greek ἡ Μαγδαληνὴ Μαρία, with "the Magdalene" first; Μαρία ἡ Ἰακώβου, literally "Mary the one of James": "the mother" is supplied as in Mark 16:1, where Mark 15:40 names the relation; αἱ λοιπαί, "the rest" (feminine); ἔλεγον, an imperfect, begins a new clause ("which", RP, is not in the critical text).
- **"And their words seemed to them as idle tales, and they believed them not" → "And these words appeared before them as nonsense, and they were disbelieving them"** (v11) — Greek ἐφάνησαν ἐνώπιον αὐτῶν; λῆρος, "nonsense, idle talk"; τὰ ῥήματα ταῦτα (WH Treg NA28); ἠπίστουν αὐταῖς, an imperfect, with "them" in the feminine: it is the women whom they disbelieved, which English "them" cannot show.
- **"Then arose Peter, and ran unto the sepulchre; and stooping down, he beheld the linen clothes laid by themselves, and departed, wondering in himself at that which was come to pass" → "But Kefa, rising, ran to the tomb; and stooping to look, he sees the linen cloths alone; and he went away to his home, marveling at what had come to pass"** (v12) — Greek παρακύψας βλέπει, a present (as John 20:5); τὰ ὀθόνια μόνα, "the linen cloths alone" ("laid", RP, is not in the critical text); ἀπῆλθεν πρὸς ἑαυτόν (NA28 RP; WH αὑτόν, the same word; Treg αὐτόν), "went away to his own (place)", as John 20:10; the words can also be joined with what follows, "marveling to himself".
- **"v12 textual note"** — the whole verse is in the text of Treg and NA28 (and RP); WH prints it in double brackets as a very early addition. The verse follows the majority of the four editions.
- **"two of them went that same day to a village called Emmaus, which was from Jerusalem about threescore furlongs" → "two of them on that very day were going to a village sixty stadia distant from Jerusalem, whose name is Emmaus"** (v13) — Greek ἦσαν πορευόμενοι; σταδίους ἑξήκοντα, sixty stadia (a stadion is about 185 metres; some eleven kilometres), the measure kept as the Greek has it; ᾗ ὄνομα Ἐμμαοῦς.
- **"And they talked together of all these things which had happened" → "and they were conversing with one another about all these things that had happened"** (v14) — Greek ὡμίλουν, an imperfect (Acts 24:26); συμβεβηκότων.
- **"while they communed together and reasoned, Jesus himself drew near, and went with them" → "while they were conversing and disputing, that Yeshua himself, drawing near, was going along with them"** (v15) — Greek ὁμιλεῖν καὶ συζητεῖν: συζητέω is "dispute, argue a question" (as 22:23); συνεπορεύετο, an imperfect.
- **"But their eyes were holden that they should not know him" → "but their eyes were held, so as not to recognize him"** (v16) — Greek ἐκρατοῦντο; ἐπιγνῶναι, "recognize" (also v31).
- **"What manner of communications are these that ye have one to another, as ye walk, and are sad?" → "What are these words which you are exchanging with one another as you walk? And they stood still, sullen"** (v17) — Greek ἀντιβάλλετε, "throw back and forth"; καὶ ἐστάθησαν σκυθρωποί (WH Treg NA28), a statement about the two; RP has καί ἐστε σκυθρωποί, "and are sad", inside the question, which the KJV follows. σκυθρωπός as Matt 6:16.
- **"Art thou only a stranger in Jerusalem, and hast not known the things which are come to pass there in these days?" → "Do you alone dwell as an alien in Jerusalem and have not come to know the things that came to pass in it in these days?"** (v18) — Greek σὺ μόνος παροικεῖς Ἰερουσαλήμ (παροικέω as Heb 11:9): "are you the only one staying in Jerusalem who …"; οὐκ ἔγνως; ἐν αὐτῇ.
- **"Concerning Jesus of Nazareth, which was a prophet mighty in deed and word" → "The things concerning Yeshua the Nazarene, who became a man, a prophet, powerful in work and word"** (v19) — Greek Ἰησοῦ τοῦ Ναζαρηνοῦ (WH Treg NA28; as Mark 16:6); ὃς ἐγένετο ἀνὴρ προφήτης; δυνατός, "powerful"; ἐν ἔργῳ καὶ λόγῳ.
- **"the chief priests and our rulers delivered him to be condemned to death" → "our high priests and rulers delivered him up to a sentence of death"** (v20) — Greek παρέδωκαν; εἰς κρίμα θανάτου.
- **"But we trusted that it had been he which should have redeemed Israel: and beside all this, to day is the third day since these things were done" → "But we were hoping that he is the one about to ransom Israel. But indeed also, with all these things, this is the third day that is passing since these things came to pass"** (v21) — Greek ἠλπίζομεν, an imperfect; ὁ μέλλων λυτροῦσθαι, "the one about to ransom" (λυτρόω as Titus 2:14); τρίτην ταύτην ἡμέραν ἄγει, literally "he (or it) is spending this third day"; "today" (Treg RP σήμερον) is not in WH NA28.
- **"Yea, and certain women also of our company made us astonished, which were early at the sepulchre" → "But also certain women from among us amazed us: having been early in the morning at the tomb"** (v22) — Greek ἐξέστησαν ἡμᾶς (Acts 8:9); ὀρθριναί, "at dawn".
- **"that they had also seen a vision of angels, which said that he was alive" → "that they had also seen a vision of angels, who say that he lives"** (v23) — Greek οἳ λέγουσιν αὐτὸν ζῆν, presents.
- **"And certain of them which were with us went to the sepulchre, and found it even so as the women had said" → "And some of those with us went away to the tomb and found it thus, even as the women also said"** (v24) — Greek καθὼς καὶ αἱ γυναῖκες εἶπον (καί: NA28 RP and the printed SBLGNT; not in WH Treg — 2–2, NA28 decides); "it" is supplied.
- **"Then he said unto them, O fools, and slow of heart to believe all that the prophets have spoken" → "And he himself said to them, 'O foolish ones and slow in heart to believe upon all that the prophets spoke'"** (v25) — Greek καὶ αὐτός; ἀνόητοι, "without understanding, foolish" (Gal 3:1); πιστεύειν ἐπὶ πᾶσιν, "to believe upon (rest faith on) all".
- **"Ought not Christ to have suffered these things, and to enter into his glory?" → "Did not the Mashiach have to suffer these things and to enter into his glory?"** (v26) — Greek οὐχὶ ταῦτα ἔδει παθεῖν τὸν χριστόν (as Acts 17:3); with the article.
- **"And beginning at Moses and all the prophets, he expounded unto them in all the scriptures" → "And beginning from Moses and from all the prophets, he interpreted to them in all the Scriptures"** (v27) — Greek ἀπό twice; διερμήνευσεν (WH Treg NA28), a simple past, "interpreted" (1 Cor 12:30).
- **"and he made as though he would have gone further" → "and he himself made as though to go farther"** (v28) — Greek καὶ αὐτός; προσεποιήσατο, "made as if, acted as though"; πορρώτερον.
- **"Abide with us: for it is toward evening, and the day is far spent. And he went in to tarry with them" → "Abide with us, because it is toward evening and the day has already declined. And he went in to abide with them"** (v29) — Greek παρεβιάσαντο (Acts 16:15); μεῖνον … τοῦ μεῖναι, one verb; κέκλικεν ἤδη (WH Treg NA28), "has already bent down".
- **"as he sat at meat with them, he took bread, and blessed it, and brake, and gave to them" → "when he had reclined with them, that taking the bread he blessed, and having broken it he was handing it to them"** (v30) — Greek κατακλιθῆναι; τὸν ἄρτον, with the article; εὐλόγησεν without an object; ἐπεδίδου, an imperfect (ἐπιδίδωμι also v42).
- **"And their eyes were opened, and they knew him; and he vanished out of their sight" → "and their eyes were opened up, and they recognized him; and he himself became unseen from them"** (v31) — Greek διηνοίχθησαν, the compound, "opened up" (also v32, 45; Acts 17:3); ἄφαντος ἐγένετο ἀπ' αὐτῶν.
- **"Did not our heart burn within us, while he talked with us by the way, and while he opened to us the scriptures?" → "Was not our heart burning in us as he was speaking to us on the way, as he was opening up the Scriptures to us?"** (v32) — Greek ἡ καρδία, singular; καιομένη ἦν; ἐν ἡμῖν (Treg NA28 RP; not in WH); "and" before the second "as" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"The Lord is risen indeed, and hath appeared to Simon" → "The Lord really was raised, and he appeared to Simon"** (v34) — Greek ὄντως ἠγέρθη, "really" (23:47), "was raised"; ὤφθη, "was seen by, appeared to".
- **"And they told what things were done in the way, and how he was known of them in breaking of bread" → "And they themselves were declaring the things on the way, and how he was known to them in the breaking of the bread"** (v35) — Greek ἐξηγοῦντο, an imperfect (Acts 15:12); τῇ κλάσει τοῦ ἄρτου, both with the article.
- **"And as they thus spake, Jesus himself stood in the midst of them, and saith unto them, Peace be unto you" → "And as they were speaking these things, he himself stood in the midst of them, and he says to them, 'Peace to you'"** (v36) — Greek αὐτός; λέγει, a present. Editions: + καὶ λέγει αὐτοῖς Εἰρήνη ὑμῖν Treg NA28 RP ] not in WH (nor in the printed SBLGNT): the verse follows the majority. Εἰρήνη ὑμῖν has no verb (John 20:19).
- **"But they were terrified and affrighted, and supposed that they had seen a spirit" → "But being terrified and becoming afraid, they were supposing that they beheld a spirit"** (v37) — Greek πτοηθέντες (21:9); ἔμφοβοι γενόμενοι (v5); ἐδόκουν, an imperfect; θεωρεῖν.
- **"why do thoughts arise in your hearts?" → "why do reasonings come up in your heart?"** (v38) — Greek διαλογισμοί, "reasonings"; ἐν τῇ καρδίᾳ, singular (WH Treg NA28).

- **"he shewed them his hands and his feet" → "he showed them the hands and the feet"** (v40) — Greek τὰς χεῖρας καὶ τοὺς πόδας, with the article and no "his". The whole verse is in the text of Treg and NA28 (and RP); WH prints it in double brackets, and the SBLGNT in single brackets. The verse follows the majority of the four editions.
- **"And while they yet believed not for joy, and wondered, he said unto them, Have ye here any meat?" → "And while they were still disbelieving from the joy and marveling, he said to them, 'Do you have anything eatable here?'"** (v41) — Greek ἀπιστούντων; ἀπὸ τῆς χαρᾶς; βρώσιμον.

- **"Thus it is written, and thus it behoved Christ to suffer, and to rise from the dead the third day" → "Thus it is written, that the Mashiach should suffer and rise from the dead on the third day"** (v46) — "and thus it behoved" (RP) is not in the critical text; Greek παθεῖν τὸν χριστὸν καὶ ἀναστῆναι ἐκ νεκρῶν.

The words "having bowed down to him" are in the text of Treg and NA28 (and RP); WH prints them in double brackets, and the SBLGNT in single brackets. The verse follows the majority of the four editions.

### Notes
- **v44** — Luke's tripartite scripture reference (Torah, Prophets, Writings/Psalms) corresponds to the Hebrew canon's three divisions (*TaNaK*).
