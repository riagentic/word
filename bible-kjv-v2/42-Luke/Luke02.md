# Luke 2

[1] Now it came to pass in those days that a decree went out from Caesar Augustus for all the inhabited earth to be registered.

[2] This registration was the first, made when Quirinius was governor of Syria.

[3] And all were going to be registered, each to his own city.

[4] And ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ also went up from ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧, out of the city of ⟦nazareth|Natzrat|Nazareth⟧, into ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧, to the city of David, which is called ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧, because he was of the house and family of David,

[5] to be registered with ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧, the one betrothed to him, who was pregnant.

[6] And it came to pass, while they were there, that the days were fulfilled for her to bear,

[7] and she bore her son, the firstborn, and swaddled him and laid him down in a manger, because there was no place for them in the guest room.

[8] And there were shepherds in the same country, living in the fields and keeping watches of the night over their flock.

[9] And an angel of the Lord stood by them, and glory of the Lord shone around them, and they feared a great fear.

[10] And the angel said to them, "Do not fear; for behold, I preach to you a great joy, which will be for all the people,

[11] for there was born to you today a Savior, who is ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧, Lord, in the city of David.

[12] And this is the sign for you: you will find a baby swaddled and lying in a manger."

[13] And suddenly there came to be with the angel a multitude of the heavenly host, praising ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and saying,

[14] "**Glory in the highest to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and upon earth peace among men of good pleasure.**"

[15] And it came to pass, when the angels went away from them into heaven, that the shepherds were speaking to one another, "Let us go through, then, as far as ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧, and let us see this word that has come to pass, which the Lord made known to us."

[16] And they came, hastening, and found both ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ and ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, and the baby lying in the manger.

[17] And seeing, they made known concerning the word that was spoken to them about this young child.

[18] And all who heard marveled concerning the things spoken by the shepherds to them.

[19] But ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ was keeping all these words safe, pondering them in her heart.

[20] And the shepherds returned, glorifying and praising ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for all that they heard and saw, just as it was spoken to them.

[21] And when eight days were fulfilled to circumcise him, then his name was called ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, the name called by the angel before he was conceived in the womb.

[22] And when the days of their purification according to the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ were fulfilled, they brought him up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to present him to the Lord

[23] (just as it is written in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of the Lord, "**Every male opening a womb shall be called holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧**")

[24] and to give a sacrifice according to what is said in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of the Lord, "**a pair of turtledoves or two young doves**."

[25] And behold, there was a man in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ whose name was ⟦simeon-nt|Shimon|Simeon⟧, and this man was righteous and reverent, awaiting the encouragement of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧ was upon him;

[26] and he had been divinely instructed by the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ that he would not see death before he should see the Lord's ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[27] And he came in the Spirit into the temple; and when the parents brought in the young child ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, for them to do according to the custom of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ concerning him,

[28] he himself also accepted him into his arms and blessed ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and said,

[29] "Now you release your slave, Master,
according to your word, in peace;
[30] for my eyes saw your salvation,
[31] which you made ready before the face of all the peoples,
[32] a light for revelation of nations
and glory of your people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[33] And his father and mother were marveling at the things being spoken about him.

[34] And ⟦simeon-nt|Shimon|Simeon⟧ blessed them and said to ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ his mother, "Behold, this one is set for the fall and rising of many in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and for a sign that is contradicted

[35] (and through your own soul also a sword will pass), so that reasonings out of many hearts may be revealed."

[36] And there was ⟦anna|Channah|Anna⟧, a prophetess, a daughter of ⟦phanuel|Penuel|Phanuel⟧, of the tribe of Asher. She was advanced in many days, having lived with a husband seven years from her virginity,

[37] and she was a widow up to eighty-four years; who did not depart from the temple, worshiping with fastings and supplications night and day.

[38] And in that very hour, standing by, she was giving thanks to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and was speaking about him to all those awaiting the redemption of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[39] And when they finished all the things according to the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of the Lord, they turned back into ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧, to their own city, ⟦nazareth|Natzrat|Nazareth⟧.

[40] And the young child was growing and being made mighty, being filled with wisdom, and the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was upon him.

[41] And his parents were going year by year to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ at the feast of the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧.

[42] And when he became twelve years old, as they were going up according to the custom of the feast

[43] and had completed the days, while they were returning, the boy ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ stayed behind in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and his parents did not know it.

[44] But supposing him to be in the company, they went a day's way, and they were seeking him out among the kinsmen and the acquaintances,

[45] and not finding him, they returned to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, seeking him out.

[46] And it came to pass that after three days they found him in the temple, sitting in the midst of the teachers, both hearing them and asking them;

[47] and all those hearing him were amazed at his understanding and his answers.

[48] And seeing him, they were astonished, and his mother said to him, "Child, why did you do thus to us? Behold, your father and I, being pained, were seeking you."

[49] And he said to them, "Why is it that you were seeking me? Did you not know that I must be in the things of my Father?"

[50] And they themselves did not understand the word which he spoke to them.

[51] And he went down with them and came to ⟦nazareth|Natzrat|Nazareth⟧, and he was subject to them. And his mother was carefully keeping all the words in her heart.

[52] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was advancing in wisdom and stature and grace with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and men.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (vv21, 27, 43, 52) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv13, 20, 28, 38, 40, 52) — divine name retained.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (vv11, 26) — Hebrew Anointed; "the Lord's Christ" → "the Lord's *Mashiach*."
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (vv25, 26) — Hebrew divine Spirit. In v25 the Greek has no article (πνεῦμα ἦν ἅγιον); in v26 it has (τοῦ πνεύματος τοῦ ἁγίου).
- **"inn" → "guest room"** (v7) — Greek κατάλυμα *katalyma*, a place to lodge, a guest room (the word of Mark 14:14; Luke 22:11); not the same word as *pandocheion* (inn, used in the Good Samaritan parable). The Greek says only that there was no place for them in the *katalyma*; a stable is not named.
- **"all the world should be taxed" → "for all the inhabited earth to be registered"** (v1; "registered", "registration" v2, 3, 5) — Greek ἀπογράφεσθαι πᾶσαν τὴν οἰκουμένην: ἀπογράφω is "enter in a register" (Acts 5:37); οἰκουμένη is "the inhabited earth" (Acts 11:28), not κόσμος "world". "decree" is δόγμα (Acts 17:7).
- **"And this taxing was first made when Cyrenius was governor of Syria" → "This registration was the first, made when Quirinius was governor of Syria"** (v2) — Greek αὕτη ἀπογραφὴ πρώτη ἐγένετο ἡγεμονεύοντος τῆς Συρίας Κυρηνίου, literally "this registration first came to be, Quirinius governing Syria". πρώτη is "first"; some take it as "before" ("this registration was before Quirinius governed Syria"), which the word order hardly allows. The parentheses of the printed text are the editors'.
- **"lineage" → "family"** (v4) — Greek πατριά, a clan descended from one father (Acts 3:25). "city" is πόλις throughout (v3, 4, 11, 39). εἰς πόλιν Δαυίδ, ἐν πόλει Δαυίδ (v11) have no article.
- **"with Mary his espoused wife, being great with child" → "with Miryam, the one betrothed to him, who was pregnant"** (v5) — Greek τῇ ἐμνηστευμένῃ αὐτῷ (WH Treg NA28); "wife" (γυναικί) is added in the Byzantine text. ἔγκυος, "pregnant", only here in the New Testament.
- **"the days were accomplished that she should be delivered" → "the days were fulfilled for her to bear"** (v6; also v21, 22) — Greek ἐπλήσθησαν αἱ ἡμέραι τοῦ τεκεῖν αὐτήν; ἐπλήσθησαν is literally "were filled".
- **"brought forth her firstborn son, and wrapped him in swaddling clothes, and laid him" → "bore her son, the firstborn, and swaddled him and laid him down"** (v7) — Greek ἔτεκεν τὸν υἱὸν αὐτῆς τὸν πρωτότοκον, with the article repeated: "her son, the firstborn". πρωτότοκος says that he was the first; it says nothing either way about later children. ἐσπαργάνωσεν is one verb, "wrapped in swaddling bands" (v12 its participle). ἀνέκλινεν is "made recline, laid back", the verb used of guests at table (Matt 14:19).
- **"abiding in the field, keeping watch over their flock by night" → "living in the fields and keeping watches of the night over their flock"** (v8) — Greek ἀγραυλοῦντες, "lodging in the open field"; φυλάσσοντες φυλακὰς τῆς νυκτός, the night watches (Matt 14:25).
- **"came upon them, and the glory of the Lord … and they were sore afraid" → "stood by them, and glory of the Lord … and they feared a great fear"** (v9) — Greek ἐπέστη (Acts 12:7); δόξα κυρίου without the article; ἐφοβήθησαν φόβον μέγαν (Mark 4:41). "lo" (ἰδού, Treg RP) is not in WH NA28 SBLGNT.
- **"I bring you good tidings of great joy" → "I preach to you a great joy"** (v10) — Greek εὐαγγελίζομαι ὑμῖν χαρὰν μεγάλην: the verb "preach the gospel, bring as good news", with "a great joy" as its object. παντὶ τῷ λαῷ is "all the people" (Israel), singular.
- **"For unto you is born this day in the city of David a Saviour, which is Christ the Lord" → "for there was born to you today a Savior, who is Mashiach, Lord, in the city of David"** (v11) — Greek ἐτέχθη, a past tense, the passive of τίκτω "bear" (v7); χριστὸς κύριος, two nouns without articles: "an anointed one, a lord", "Mashiach, Lord" (compare χριστὸν κυρίου, "the Lord's Mashiach", v26). "in the city of David" stands last.
- **"this shall be a sign unto you; Ye shall find the babe wrapped in swaddling clothes, lying" → "this is the sign for you: you will find a baby swaddled and lying"** (v12) — Greek τοῦτο ὑμῖν τὸ σημεῖον, without a verb; τὸ Treg NA28 SBLGNT RP, not WH ("a sign"). βρέφος has no article.
- **"there was with the angel" → "there came to be with the angel"** (v13) — Greek ἐγένετο. στρατιᾶς οὐρανίου, "of the heavenly host" (WH NA28 SBLGNT RP; Treg οὐρανοῦ, "host of heaven", as Acts 7:42).
- **"Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good will toward men" → "Glory in the highest to Elohim, and upon earth peace among men of good pleasure"** (v14) — Greek Δόξα ἐν ὑψίστοις θεῷ καὶ ἐπὶ γῆς εἰρήνη ἐν ἀνθρώποις εὐδοκίας. Editions: εὐδοκίας, genitive, WH Treg NA28 SBLGNT ] εὐδοκία, nominative, RP — "peace, good pleasure among men", three members, which the KJV follows. "men of good pleasure" are the men on whom God's good pleasure rests (εὐδοκία, Eph 1:5; Matt 11:26); the Greek does not say whose good pleasure. There is no verb: "Glory is" or "Glory be".
- **"Let us now go even unto Bethlehem, and see this thing which is come to pass" → "Let us go through, then, as far as Bethlehem, and let us see this word that has come to pass"** (v15) — Greek διέλθωμεν δὴ ἕως Βηθλέεμ; τὸ ῥῆμα τοῦτο τὸ γεγονός: ῥῆμα is "word" or "thing spoken of" (so v17, 19, 50, 51). "were speaking" is ἐλάλουν (WH NA28 SBLGNT; Treg RP "the men, the shepherds, said").
- **"came with haste, and found" → "came, hastening, and found"** (v16) — Greek σπεύσαντες; ἀνεῦραν, "found after searching" (Acts 21:4).
- **"when they had seen it, they made known abroad the saying" → "seeing, they made known concerning the word"** (v17) — Greek ἰδόντες δὲ ἐγνώρισαν περὶ τοῦ ῥήματος; the participle has no object. παιδίον is "young child" (v17, 27, 40).
- **"kept all these things, and pondered them" → "was keeping all these words safe, pondering them"** (v19) — Greek συνετήρει (Mark 6:20), imperfect; συμβάλλουσα, literally "throwing together": putting them side by side, weighing them. Editions: Μαρία WH Treg SBLGNT, Μαριάμ NA28 RP — the same name.
- **"when eight days were accomplished for the circumcising of the child, his name was called Jesus, which was so named of the angel" → "when eight days were fulfilled to circumcise him, then his name was called Yeshua, the name called by the angel"** (v21) — Greek τοῦ περιτεμεῖν αὐτόν ("the child" is the Byzantine reading); καὶ ἐκλήθη, "and" taking up the "when" clause; τὸ κληθέν.
- **"her purification" → "their purification"** (v22) — Greek τοῦ καθαρισμοῦ αὐτῶν, "their", in all the editions (WH Treg NA28 RP); "her" has almost no Greek support. Lev 12 speaks of the mother's purification.
- **"Every male that openeth the womb" → "Every male opening a womb"** (v23) — Greek πᾶν ἄρσεν διανοῖγον μήτραν; μήτρα is "womb", a different word from κοιλία (v21). ἐν νόμῳ κυρίου has no article.
- **"to offer a sacrifice … two young pigeons" → "to give a sacrifice … two young doves"** (v24) — Greek τοῦ δοῦναι θυσίαν; δύο νοσσοὺς περιστερῶν, "two young of doves": περιστερά is the "dove" of 3:22.
- **"just and devout, waiting for the consolation of Israel" → "righteous and reverent, awaiting the encouragement of Israel"** (v25) — Greek εὐλαβής (Acts 2:5; "devout" is ὅσιος); προσδεχόμενος (so v38; Mark 15:43); παράκλησις is "encouragement, comfort" (Acts 9:31; 13:15): the comfort promised to Israel (Isa 40:1, παρακαλεῖτε). "Jerusalem" is here Ἰερουσαλήμ, the Hebrew form (so v38, 41, 43, 45); in v22 Ἱεροσόλυμα, the Greek form.
- **"it was revealed unto him by the Holy Ghost, that he should not see death, before he had seen" → "he had been divinely instructed by the Ruach HaQodesh that he would not see death before he should see"** (v26) — Greek ἦν αὐτῷ κεχρηματισμένον, literally "it had been divinely told to him": χρηματίζω is used of an answer or warning from God (Matt 2:12, 22), not ἀποκαλύπτω "reveal" (v35). The Greek has no word "God" in the verb.
- **"by the Spirit … the child Jesus, to do for him after the custom of the law" → "in the Spirit … the young child Yeshua, for them to do according to the custom of the Torah concerning him"** (v27) — Greek ἐν τῷ πνεύματι; κατὰ τὸ εἰθισμένον τοῦ νόμου, literally "according to what is customary of the Torah". "temple" is ἱερόν, the whole precinct (so v37, 46).
- **"Then took he him up in his arms" → "he himself also accepted him into his arms"** (v28) — Greek καὶ αὐτὸς ἐδέξατο αὐτὸ εἰς τὰς ἀγκάλας; δέχομαι is "accept, receive what is offered". "his" is added in Treg RP.
- **"Lord, now lettest thou thy servant depart in peace, according to thy word" → "Now you release your slave, Master, according to your word, in peace"** (v29) — Greek Νῦν ἀπολύεις τὸν δοῦλόν σου, δέσποτα: δεσπότης is "master" (the owner of a slave), not κύριος "Lord"; δοῦλος is "slave"; ἀπολύω is "release, send away, dismiss" (of a prisoner, Matt 27:15). The verb is a statement in the present tense, not a request. "in peace" stands last.
- **"have seen thy salvation, which thou hast prepared" → "saw your salvation, which you made ready"** (v30–31) — Greek τὸ σωτήριον, a neuter adjective used as a noun (Acts 28:28), not σωτηρία (1:69); ἡτοίμασας, the verb of 1:17, 76; 3:4.
- **"A light to lighten the Gentiles, and the glory of thy people Israel" → "a light for revelation of nations and glory of your people Israel"** (v32) — Greek φῶς εἰς ἀποκάλυψιν ἐθνῶν καὶ δόξαν λαοῦ σου Ἰσραήλ. ἐθνῶν is a plain genitive, "of nations": a revelation given to the nations, or an unveiling of the nations (Isa 25:7). δόξαν can stand beside "light" ("a light … and glory") or beside "revelation" ("for revelation … and for glory").
- **"Joseph and his mother" → "his father and mother"** (v33) — Greek ὁ πατὴρ αὐτοῦ καὶ ἡ μήτηρ (WH Treg NA28); "Joseph" is the Byzantine reading.
- **"this child is set for the fall and rising again of many in Israel; and for a sign which shall be spoken against" → "this one is set for the fall and rising of many in Israel, and for a sign that is contradicted"** (v34) — Greek οὗτος κεῖται, "this one lies, is set"; no word "child". ἀνάστασις is the word rendered "resurrection" elsewhere: here a rising up after a fall. ἀντιλεγόμενον is a present participle of ἀντιλέγω "contradict, speak against" (Acts 28:22).
- **"(Yea, a sword shall pierce through thy own soul also,) that the thoughts of many hearts may be revealed" → "(and through your own soul also a sword will pass), so that reasonings out of many hearts may be revealed"** (v35) — Greek διελεύσεται, "will go through" (the verb of v15); ῥομφαία, a large sword (Rev 1:16), not μάχαιρα; διαλογισμοί, "reasonings" (5:22), with ἐκ "out of". The brackets are not in the Greek. WH omits δέ.
- **"she was of a great age, and had lived with an husband seven years from her virginity; and she was a widow of about fourscore and four years" → "She was advanced in many days, having lived with a husband seven years from her virginity, and she was a widow up to eighty-four years"** (v36–37) — Greek προβεβηκυῖα ἐν ἡμέραις πολλαῖς (as 1:7); χήρα ἕως ἐτῶν ὀγδοήκοντα τεσσάρων (WH Treg NA28; RP ὡς, "about"): either a widow until the age of eighty-four, or a widow for eighty-four years; the Greek leaves it open.
- **"served God with fastings and prayers" → "worshiping with fastings and supplications"** (v37) — Greek λατρεύουσα (1:74), without "God"; δεήσεσιν, "supplications" (1:13).
- **"coming in that instant gave thanks likewise unto the Lord … looked for redemption in Jerusalem" → "in that very hour, standing by, she was giving thanks to Elohim … awaiting the redemption of Jerusalem"** (v38) — Greek αὐτῇ τῇ ὥρᾳ ἐπιστᾶσα (the verb of v9); ἀνθωμολογεῖτο τῷ θεῷ (WH Treg NA28; RP "the Lord"), "was acknowledging in return, giving praise": not εὐχαριστέω, and only here in the New Testament. λύτρωσιν Ἰερουσαλήμ, a genitive without article (RP "in Jerusalem"). "about him" can be about God or about the child.
- **"had performed all things … they returned" → "finished all the things … they turned back"** (v39) — Greek ἐτέλεσαν; ἐπέστρεψαν (WH Treg NA28; RP ὑπέστρεψαν, the "returned" of v20, 43, 45).
- **"grew, and waxed strong in spirit, filled with wisdom" → "was growing and being made mighty, being filled with wisdom"** (v40) — Greek ηὔξανεν καὶ ἐκραταιοῦτο πληρούμενον σοφίᾳ, as 1:80; "in spirit" (RP) is not in the critical text here. χάρις θεοῦ has no articles.
- **"went … every year" → "were going year by year"** (v41) — Greek ἐπορεύοντο κατ' ἔτος, imperfect.
- **"when they went up to Jerusalem after the custom of the feast. And when they had fulfilled the days, as they returned, the child Jesus tarried behind … and Joseph and his mother knew not of it" → "as they were going up according to the custom of the feast and had completed the days, while they were returning, the boy Yeshua stayed behind … and his parents did not know it"** (v42–43) — one Greek sentence. "to Jerusalem" (Treg RP) is not in WH NA28 SBLGNT. τελειωσάντων is "having completed" (the verb "perfect"); ὑπέμεινεν, "stayed behind" (Acts 17:14); Ἰησοῦς ὁ παῖς, "Yeshua the boy"; οἱ γονεῖς αὐτοῦ (WH Treg NA28). "it" is supplied.
- **"they sought him among their kinsfolk and acquaintance" → "they were seeking him out among the kinsmen and the acquaintances"** (v44, 45) — Greek ἀνεζήτουν, ἀναζητοῦντες (Acts 11:25), a compound of the "seek" of v48, 49. συνοδία is a company of travelers, a caravan.
- **"both hearing them, and asking them questions" → "both hearing them and asking them"** (v46) — Greek ἐπερωτῶντα αὐτούς.
- **"astonished" → "amazed"; "amazed" → "astonished"** (v47, 48) — Greek ἐξίσταντο, then ἐξεπλάγησαν: the renderings of Mark 1:22; 2:12.
- **"Son, why hast thou thus dealt with us? behold, thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing" → "Child, why did you do thus to us? Behold, your father and I, being pained, were seeking you"** (v48) — Greek Τέκνον (Mark 2:5); ὀδυνώμενοι (Acts 20:38); ἐζητοῦμεν, imperfect (Treg NA28 RP SBLGNT; WH ζητοῦμεν, "are seeking").
- **"How is it that ye sought me? wist ye not that I must be about my Father's business" → "Why is it that you were seeking me? Did you not know that I must be in the things of my Father"** (v49) — Greek ἐν τοῖς τοῦ πατρός μου δεῖ εἶναί με, literally "in the [things] of my Father": the neuter plural article without a noun. It can mean "in my Father's house" (a known idiom for a person's home) or "among my Father's affairs"; the verse keeps the bare form.
- **"understood not the saying" → "did not understand the word"** (v50) — Greek τὸ ῥῆμα.
- **"kept all these sayings" → "was carefully keeping all the words"** (v51) — Greek διετήρει, "kept throughout" (Acts 15:29), a different compound from v19. Editions: τὰ ῥήματα WH NA28 ] + ταῦτα, "these words", Treg RP (and the printed SBLGNT) — 2–2 among the four editions, NA28 decides.
- **"increased in wisdom and stature, and in favour with God and man" → "was advancing in wisdom and stature and grace with Elohim and men"** (v52) — Greek προέκοπτεν (Gal 1:14); ἐν τῇ σοφίᾳ καὶ ἡλικίᾳ καὶ χάριτι NA28 ] without ἐν WH; ἡλικίᾳ καὶ σοφίᾳ Treg; σοφίᾳ καὶ ἡλικίᾳ RP and the printed SBLGNT — no difference in translation. ἡλικία is "stature" (19:3) or "age, span of life" (12:25); χάρις is "grace, favor" (v40; 1:30); ἀνθρώποις is plural.

### Notes
- **v23** quotes Ex 13:2, 12.
- **v24** cites Lev 12:8.
- **v29-32** — the *Nunc Dimittis*, echoing Isa 42:6; 49:6; 52:10.
- **v2** — Quirinius's census poses a famous chronological problem; Herod died in 4 BC, and Quirinius's known census was in AD 6.
