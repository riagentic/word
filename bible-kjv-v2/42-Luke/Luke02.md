# Luke 2

[1] In those days a decree went out from Caesar Augustus that all the world should be registered.

[2] This was the first registration when Quirinius was governor of Syria.

[3] And all went to be registered, each to his own town.

[4] And ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ also went up from ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧, from the town of ⟦nazareth|Natzrat|Nazareth⟧, to ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧, to the city of David, which is called ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧, because he was of the house and lineage of David,

[5] to be registered with ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧, his betrothed, who was with child.

[6] And while they were there, the time came for her to give birth.

[7] And she gave birth to her firstborn son and wrapped him in swaddling cloths and laid him in a manger, because there was no place for them in the guest room.

[8] And in the same region there were shepherds out in the field, keeping watch over their flock by night.

[9] And an angel of the Lord appeared to them, and the glory of the Lord shone around them, and they were filled with great fear.

[10] And the angel said to them, "Fear not, for behold, I bring you good news of great joy that will be for all the people.

[11] For unto you is born this day in the city of David a Savior, who is ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, the Lord.

[12] And this will be a sign for you: you will find a baby wrapped in swaddling cloths and lying in a manger."

[13] And suddenly there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly host praising ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and saying,

[14] "**Glory to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in the highest,
and on earth peace among those with whom he is pleased!**"

[15] When the angels went away from them into heaven, the shepherds said to one another, "Let us go over to ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧ and see this thing that has happened, which the Lord has made known to us."

[16] And they went with haste and found ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ and ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, and the baby lying in a manger.

[17] And when they saw it, they made known the saying that had been told them concerning this child.

[18] And all who heard it wondered at what the shepherds told them.

[19] But ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ treasured up all these things, pondering them in her heart.

[20] And the shepherds returned, glorifying and praising ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for all they had heard and seen, as it had been told them.

[21] And at the end of eight days, when he was circumcised, he was called ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, the name given by the angel before he was conceived in the womb.

[22] And when the time came for their purification according to the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, they brought him up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to present him to the Lord

[23] (as it is written in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of the Lord, "**Every male who first opens the womb shall be called holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧**")

[24] and to offer a sacrifice according to what is said in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of the Lord, "**a pair of turtledoves, or two young pigeons**."

[25] Now there was a man in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, whose name was ⟦simeon-nt|Shimon|Simeon⟧, and this man was righteous and devout, waiting for the consolation of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ was upon him.

[26] And it had been revealed to him by the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ that he would not see death before he had seen the Lord's ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[27] And he came in the Spirit into the temple, and when the parents brought in the child ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, to do for him according to the custom of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧,

[28] he took him up in his arms and blessed ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and said,

[29] "Lord, now you are letting your servant depart in peace,
according to your word;
[30] for my eyes have seen your salvation,

and for glory to your people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[33] And his father and his mother marveled at what was said about him.

[34] And ⟦simeon-nt|Shimon|Simeon⟧ blessed them and said to ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ his mother, "Behold, this child is appointed for the fall and rising of many in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and for a sign that is opposed

[38] And coming up at that very hour she began to give thanks to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and to speak of him to all who were waiting for the redemption of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[39] And when they had performed everything according to the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of the Lord, they returned into ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧, to their own town of ⟦nazareth|Natzrat|Nazareth⟧.

[43] And when the feast was ended, as they were returning, the boy ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ stayed behind in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. His parents did not know it,

[44] but supposing him to be in the group they went a day's journey, but then they began to search for him among their relatives and acquaintances,

[45] and when they did not find him, they returned to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, searching for him.

[46] After three days they found him in the temple, sitting among the teachers, listening to them and asking them questions.

[47] And all who heard him were amazed at his understanding and his answers.

[48] And when his parents saw him, they were astonished. And his mother said to him, "Son, why have you treated us so? Behold, your father and I have been searching for you in great distress."

[49] And he said to them, "Why were you looking for me? Did you not know that I must be in my Father's house?"

[50] And they did not understand the saying that he spoke to them.

[51] And he went down with them and came to ⟦nazareth|Natzrat|Nazareth⟧ and was submissive to them. And his mother treasured up all these things in her heart.

[52] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ increased in wisdom and in stature and in favor with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and man.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (vv21, 27, 43, 52) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv13, 20, 28, 38, 40, 52) — divine name retained.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (vv11, 26) — Hebrew Anointed; "the Lord's Christ" → "the Lord's *Mashiach*."
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (vv25, 26) — Hebrew divine Spirit.
- **"inn" → "guest room"** (v7) — Greek *katalyma* = guest room (same word as "upper room" in Lk 22:11); not the same word as *pandocheion* (inn, used in the Good Samaritan parable). The traditional "stable/inn" image is absent from the Greek.

### Notes
- **v23** quotes Ex 13:2, 12.
- **v24** cites Lev 12:8.
- **v29-32** — the *Nunc Dimittis*, echoing Isa 42:6; 49:6; 52:10.
- **v2** — Quirinius's census poses a famous chronological problem; Herod died in 4 BC, and Quirinius's known census was in AD 6.
