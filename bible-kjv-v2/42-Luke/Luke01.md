# Luke 1

[1] Inasmuch as many attempted to draw up a narrative concerning the matters that have been fully accomplished among us,

[2] just as those who from the beginning became eyewitnesses and assistants of the word delivered them to us,

[3] it seemed good to me also, having followed all things from the first accurately, to write to you in order, most excellent ⟦theophilus|Theophilos|Theophilus⟧,

[4] that you may come to know the certainty of the words in which you were instructed.

[5] There was in the days of ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧, king of ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧, a certain priest named ⟦zechariah-nt|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧, of the division of ⟦abijah|Aviyah|Abijah⟧; and he had a wife from the daughters of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, and her name was ⟦elizabeth|Elisheva|Elizabeth⟧.

[6] And they were both righteous before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, walking in all the commandments and righteous ordinances of the Lord, blameless.

[7] And they had no child, because ⟦elizabeth|Elisheva|Elizabeth⟧ was barren, and both were advanced in their days.

[8] Now it came to pass, while he was serving as priest in the order of his division before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[9] that according to the custom of the priestly office he obtained by lot to burn incense, having entered into the sanctuary of the Lord,

[10] and the whole multitude of the people was praying outside at the hour of the incense.

[11] And there appeared to him an angel of the Lord, standing at the right of the altar of the incense.

[12] And ⟦zechariah-nt|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧ was troubled, seeing him, and fear fell upon him.

[13] But the angel said to him, "Do not fear, ⟦zechariah-nt|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧, because your supplication has been heard, and your wife ⟦elizabeth|Elisheva|Elizabeth⟧ will give birth to a son for you, and you shall call his name ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧.

[14] And you will have joy and exultation, and many will rejoice at his birth,

[15] for he will be great before the Lord, and wine and strong drink he shall by no means drink, and he will be filled with ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧ even from his mother's womb,

[16] and many of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ he will turn to the Lord their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[17] and he himself will go on before him in the spirit and power of ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧, **to turn the hearts of fathers to children**, and the disobedient in the prudence of the righteous, to make ready for the Lord a people prepared."

[18] And ⟦zechariah-nt|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧ said to the angel, "By what shall I know this? For I am an old man, and my wife is advanced in her days."

[19] And answering, the angel said to him, "I am ⟦gabriel|Gavriel|Gabriel⟧, who stands before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and I was sent to speak to you and to preach these things to you;

[20] and behold, you will be silent and not able to speak until the day when these things come to pass, because you did not believe my words, which will be fulfilled in their time."

[21] And the people were expecting ⟦zechariah-nt|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧, and they were marveling at his delaying in the sanctuary.

[22] But coming out, he was not able to speak to them, and they recognized that he had seen a vision in the sanctuary; and he himself was making signs to them, and he remained mute.

[23] And it came to pass, when the days of his ministry were fulfilled, that he went away to his house.

[24] And after these days ⟦elizabeth|Elisheva|Elizabeth⟧ his wife conceived, and she was hiding herself five months, saying,

[25] "Thus has the Lord done to me in the days in which he looked on, to take away my reproach among men."

[26] Now in the sixth month the angel ⟦gabriel|Gavriel|Gabriel⟧ was sent from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to a city of ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧ whose name was ⟦nazareth|Natzrat|Nazareth⟧,

[27] to a virgin betrothed to a man whose name was ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, of the house of David; and the name of the virgin was ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧.

[28] And coming in to her, he said, "Hail, graced one! The Lord is with you."

[29] But she was greatly troubled at the word, and was reasoning of what sort this greeting might be.

[30] And the angel said to her, "Do not fear, ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧, for you found grace with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[31] and behold, you will conceive in the womb and bear a son, and you shall call his name ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[32] This one will be great and will be called Son of the Most High, and the Lord ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will give to him the throne of David his father,

[33] and he will reign over the house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ unto the ages, and of his kingdom there will be no end."

[34] But ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ said to the angel, "How will this be, since I do not know a man?"

[35] And answering, the angel said to her, "⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|The Holy Spirit⟧ will come upon you, and power of the Most High will overshadow you; therefore also that which is being born will be called holy, Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[36] and behold, ⟦elizabeth|Elisheva|Elizabeth⟧ your kinswoman, she also has conceived a son in her old age, and this is the sixth month for her who is called barren;

[37] for no word from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will be impossible."

[38] And ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ said, "Behold, the slave of the Lord; may it come to pass for me according to your word." And the angel went away from her.

[39] And ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧, rising in these days, went into the hill country with haste, into a city of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,

[40] and she entered into the house of ⟦zechariah-nt|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧ and greeted ⟦elizabeth|Elisheva|Elizabeth⟧.

[41] And it came to pass, when ⟦elizabeth|Elisheva|Elizabeth⟧ heard the greeting of ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧, that the baby leaped in her womb, and ⟦elizabeth|Elisheva|Elizabeth⟧ was filled with ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧,

[42] and she called out with a loud cry and said, "Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb.

[43] And whence is this to me, that the mother of my Lord should come to me?

[44] For behold, when the voice of your greeting came into my ears, the baby leaped in exultation in my womb.

[45] And blessed is she who believed that there will be a completion for the things that have been spoken to her from the Lord."

[46] And ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ said,

"My soul magnifies the Lord,
[47] and my spirit exulted over ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ my Savior,
[48] for he looked upon the humiliation of his slave.
For behold, from now on all the generations will call me blessed,
[49] for the Powerful One did great things for me,
and holy is his name,
[50] and his mercy is unto generations and generations
for those who fear him.
[51] He wrought might with his arm;
he scattered the proud in the mind of their heart;
[52] he pulled down rulers from thrones
and lifted up the humble;
[53] the hungering he filled up with good things,
and the rich he sent away empty.
[54] He helped ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ his servant,
to remember mercy,
[55] just as he spoke to our fathers,
to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ and to his seed forever."

[56] And ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ abode with her about three months, and returned to her house.

[57] Now for ⟦elizabeth|Elisheva|Elizabeth⟧ the time was fulfilled for her to bear, and she gave birth to a son.

[58] And those dwelling around and her kinsmen heard that the Lord magnified his mercy with her, and they were rejoicing with her.

[59] And it came to pass on the eighth day that they came to circumcise the young child, and they were calling him after the name of his father, ⟦zechariah-nt|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧.

[60] And answering, his mother said, "No, but he shall be called ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧."

[61] And they said to her, "There is no one of your kindred who is called by this name."

[62] And they were making signs to his father, as to what he would want him to be called.

[63] And asking for a little tablet, he wrote, saying, "⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ is his name." And they all marveled.

[64] And his mouth was opened instantly, and his tongue, and he was speaking, blessing ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[65] And fear came upon all those dwelling around them, and in the whole hill country of ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ all these words were being talked over,

[66] and all who heard laid them in their heart, saying, "What then will this young child be?" For indeed the hand of the Lord was with him.

[67] And ⟦zechariah-nt|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧ his father was filled with ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧ and prophesied, saying,

[68] "Blessed be the Lord, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
for he visited and wrought redemption for his people
[69] and raised a horn of salvation for us
in the house of David his servant,
[70] just as he spoke through the mouth of his holy prophets from of old,
[71] salvation from our enemies
and out of the hand of all who hate us,
[72] to do mercy with our fathers
and to remember his holy covenant,
[73] the oath which he swore to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ our father, to give to us
[74] that without fear, rescued out of the hand of enemies,
we should worship him
[75] in devoutness and righteousness before him all our days.
[76] And you also, young child, will be called prophet of the Most High,
for you will go on before the Lord to make ready his ways,
[77] to give knowledge of salvation to his people
in forgiveness of their sins,
[78] through the inward parts of mercy of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
in which a rising from on high will visit us,
[79] to appear to those sitting in darkness and shadow of death,
to direct our feet into a way of peace."

[80] And the young child was growing and being made mighty in spirit, and he was in the wildernesses until the day of his showing to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — divine name retained.
- **"Elisabeth" → "Elizabeth"** (vv5, 7, 13, 24, 36, 40–41, 57) — Greek Ἐλισάβετ *Elisabet* renders Hebrew *Elisheva* ("my God is my oath/abundance"); the text uses "Elizabeth" throughout.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v31) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"Holy Ghost" / "Holy Spirit" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (vv15, 35, 41, 67) — Hebrew divine Spirit title. The Greek has no article in any of the four places (πνεύματος ἁγίου, πνεῦμα ἅγιον).
- **"Mary" → "*Miryam*"** (v27) — Hebrew name *Miryam* underlying Greek *Mariam*; the Hebrew form is given at first occurrence (v27), thereafter "Mary" (vv30, 34, 38–39, 41, 46, 56).
- **"Lord God" → "the Lord, the *Elohim* of Israel"** (v68) — Greek Εὐλογητὸς κύριος ὁ θεὸς τοῦ Ἰσραήλ, the Old Testament blessing (Ps 41:13; 106:48), where κύριος stands for the name *Yahweh*. The Greek has no verb: "Blessed be" or "Blessed is".
- **"have taken in hand to set forth in order a declaration of those things which are most surely believed" → "attempted to draw up a narrative concerning the matters that have been fully accomplished"** (v1) — Greek ἐπεχείρησαν ἀνατάξασθαι διήγησιν περὶ τῶν πεπληροφορημένων ἐν ἡμῖν πραγμάτων. ἐπιχειρέω is "attempt" (Acts 9:29; 19:13). πληροφορέω is "fill fully": of events "fully accomplished"; of persons "fully convinced" (Rom 4:21; 14:5), which the KJV follows. πράγματα are "matters, deeds".
- **"delivered them unto us, which from the beginning were eyewitnesses, and ministers" → "those who from the beginning became eyewitnesses and assistants of the word delivered them to us"** (v2) — Greek παρέδοσαν, "deliver", the verb of handing down a tradition (Mark 7:13; 1 Cor 11:2, 23; 15:3); "them" is supplied, the Greek verb has no object. ὑπηρέται is "assistants" (Acts 13:5; 26:16); one article and one participle (γενόμενοι, "having become") cover both nouns.
- **"having had perfect understanding of all things from the very first" → "having followed all things from the first accurately"** (v3) — Greek παρηκολουθηκότι ἄνωθεν πᾶσιν ἀκριβῶς. παρακολουθέω is "follow alongside, accompany": here of following the course of the matters, whether by inquiry or by being present; "follow" leaves that open. πᾶσιν can also be masculine, "all of them" (the eyewitnesses). ἄνωθεν is "from the first" (Acts 26:5) or "from above" (John 3:31); ἀκριβῶς is "accurately" (Acts 18:25).
- **"know the certainty of those things, wherein thou hast been instructed" → "come to know the certainty of the words in which you were instructed"** (v4) — Greek ἵνα ἐπιγνῷς περὶ ὧν κατηχήθης λόγων τὴν ἀσφάλειαν, literally "that you may come to know, concerning the words in which you were instructed, the certainty"; λόγων is "words", and "the certainty" stands last, with emphasis.
- **"of the course of Abia" → "of the division of Abijah"** (v5, 8) — Greek ἐφημερία, a class of priests serving by turn for a week (1 Chr 24:10); the word means "daily service".
- **"ordinances" → "righteous ordinances"; "walking … blameless" kept** (v6) — Greek δικαιώματα (Rom 2:26; 8:4). "walking" is πορευόμενοι, "going", not περιπατέω.
- **"well stricken in years" → "advanced in their days"** (v7, 18) — Greek προβεβηκότες ἐν ταῖς ἡμέραις αὐτῶν.
- **"executed the priest's office … in the order of his course … the custom of the priest's office, his lot was to burn incense when he went into the temple" → "was serving as priest in the order of his division … the custom of the priestly office he obtained by lot to burn incense, having entered into the sanctuary"** (v8–9) — Greek ἱερατεύειν; ἱερατεία (Heb 7:5); ἔλαχε τοῦ θυμιᾶσαι εἰσελθών. "sanctuary" here and in v21–22 is ναός, the temple building itself; "temple" is ἱερόν, the whole precinct (2:27).
- **"on the right side" → "at the right"** (v11) — Greek ἐκ δεξιῶν; the Greek has no word "side".
- **"when Zacharias saw him, he was troubled" → "was troubled, seeing him"** (v12) — Greek ἐταράχθη Ζαχαρίας ἰδών; "him" is supplied, the participle has no object.
- **"Fear not … thy prayer is heard … shall bear thee a son" → "Do not fear … your supplication has been heard … will give birth to a son for you"** (v13) — Greek δέησις, "supplication", not προσευχή "prayer"; εἰσηκούσθη is a past tense (as Acts 10:31). "give birth to" is γεννάω, here and in v57 said of the mother; "bear" (v31, 57) is τίκτω.
- **"gladness" → "exultation"** (v14, 44; "exulted" v47) — Greek ἀγαλλίασις, ἀγαλλιάω, kept apart from χαρά "joy", χαίρω "rejoice". "birth" (v14) is γένεσις, the word rendered "origin" in Matt 1:1, 18.
- **"shall drink neither wine nor strong drink" → "wine and strong drink he shall by no means drink"** (v15) — Greek οὐ μὴ πίῃ, the strong negation. σίκερα is the Hebrew / Aramaic word *shekar*, any intoxicating drink other than wine (Num 6:3).
- **"even from his mother's womb" kept** (v15) — Greek ἔτι ἐκ κοιλίας μητρὸς αὐτοῦ, literally "still from the womb of his mother": from the time when he is still in the womb (v41).
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v16) — Greek υἱῶν.
- **"the fathers to the children, and the disobedient to the wisdom of the just; to make ready a people prepared for the Lord" → "fathers to children, and the disobedient in the prudence of the righteous, to make ready for the Lord a people prepared"** (v17) — Greek καρδίας πατέρων ἐπὶ τέκνα, without articles (Mal 4:6); ἐν φρονήσει δικαίων, "in" (not "to"), φρόνησις "prudence" (Eph 1:8), not σοφία "wisdom"; ἑτοιμάσαι "make ready" and κατεσκευασμένον "prepared" are the two verbs of Mark 1:2–3.
- **"Whereby" → "By what"** (v18) — Greek κατὰ τί.
- **"that stand in the presence of God … to shew thee these glad tidings" → "who stands before Elohim … to preach these things to you"** (v19) — Greek ὁ παρεστηκὼς ἐνώπιον τοῦ θεοῦ; εὐαγγελίσασθαί σοι ταῦτα, the verb "preach the gospel, bring as good news", with "these things" as its object.
- **"dumb" → "silent"; "in their season" → "in their time"** (v20) — Greek σιωπῶν; εἰς τὸν καιρὸν αὐτῶν, literally "unto their time".
- **"waited for … marvelled that he tarried so long" → "were expecting … were marveling at his delaying"** (v21) — Greek ἦν προσδοκῶν (Acts 3:5); ἐν τῷ χρονίζειν αὐτόν, which can also be "while he delayed".
- **"perceived … beckoned unto them, and remained speechless" → "recognized … was making signs to them, and he remained mute"** (v22) — Greek ἐπέγνωσαν; ἦν διανεύων, "was nodding, signaling"; κωφός is "deaf" or "mute" (Matt 9:32–33): in v62 they make signs to him, so he may be both.
- **"ministration were accomplished … departed to his own house" → "ministry were fulfilled … went away to his house"** (v23) — Greek λειτουργία; ἐπλήσθησαν, literally "were filled" (so also v57; "filled" in v15, 41, 67 is the same verb).
- **"hid herself" → "was hiding herself"** (v24) — Greek περιέκρυβεν, imperfect.
- **"wherein he looked on me" → "in which he looked on"** (v25) — Greek ἐν ἡμέραις αἷς ἐπεῖδεν ἀφελεῖν; the verb has no object.
- **"espoused" → "betrothed"** (v27) — Greek ἐμνηστευμένην, the word of Matt 1:18.
- **"And the angel came in unto her, and said, Hail, thou that art highly favoured, the Lord is with thee: blessed art thou among women" → "And coming in to her, he said, 'Hail, graced one! The Lord is with you.'"** (v28) — Greek Χαῖρε, the ordinary greeting, literally "rejoice". κεχαριτωμένη is the perfect passive participle of χαριτόω (Eph 1:6, "he graced us"): "one who has been graced", from the same root as "grace" in v30. The Greek has no verb: "the Lord with you", a statement ("is") or a wish ("be"). Editions: "the angel" Treg RP, not WH NA28 SBLGNT; after σοῦ, + εὐλογημένη σὺ ἐν γυναιξίν, "blessed are you among women", Treg RP, not WH NA28 SBLGNT: the verse follows the majority of the four editions. The words stand in v42.
- **"when she saw him, she was troubled at his saying, and cast in her mind what manner of salutation" → "she was greatly troubled at the word, and was reasoning of what sort this greeting might be"** (v29) — Greek διεταράχθη, a strengthened form of "trouble" (v12); διελογίζετο; ποταπός. "when she saw him" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"thou hast found favour" → "you found grace"** (v30) — Greek εὗρες χάριν (Acts 7:46).
- **"conceive in thy womb, and bring forth a son" → "conceive in the womb and bear a son"** (v31) — Greek συλλήμψῃ ἐν γαστρὶ καὶ τέξῃ υἱόν; "womb" is here γαστήρ, elsewhere in the chapter κοιλία.
- **"the Son of the Highest" → "Son of the Most High"** (v32; so v35, 76) — Greek υἱὸς Ὑψίστου, without articles.
- **"for ever" → "unto the ages"** (v33) — Greek εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας, plural; "forever" in v55 is εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα, "unto the age".
- **"seeing I know not a man" → "since I do not know a man"** (v34) — Greek ἐπεὶ ἄνδρα οὐ γινώσκω, present tense; "know" as in Matt 1:25. ἀνήρ is "man" or "husband".
- **"The Holy Ghost shall come upon thee, and the power of the Highest shall overshadow thee: therefore also that holy thing which shall be born of thee shall be called the Son of God" → "Ruach HaQodesh will come upon you, and power of the Most High will overshadow you; therefore also that which is being born will be called holy, Son of Elohim"** (v35) — Greek δύναμις Ὑψίστου, without the article; διὸ καὶ τὸ γεννώμενον ἅγιον κληθήσεται, υἱὸς θεοῦ. τὸ γεννώμενον is a neuter present passive participle of γεννάω: "that which is being born" (or "being begotten", as Matt 1:20). The words can also be joined "the holy thing being born will be called Son of Elohim"; the verse follows the printed punctuation. "of thee" (ἐκ σοῦ) is not in the four editions.
- **"cousin" → "kinswoman"** (v36) — Greek συγγενίς, a female relative of any degree. "has conceived" is a perfect (συνείληφεν).
- **"For with God nothing shall be impossible" → "for no word from Elohim will be impossible"** (v37) — Greek ὅτι οὐκ ἀδυνατήσει παρὰ τοῦ θεοῦ πᾶν ῥῆμα: παρὰ τοῦ θεοῦ is a genitive, "from God" (WH Treg NA28); "with God" is the dative τῷ θεῷ of the Byzantine text. ῥῆμα is "word" (so v38) or "thing spoken of"; ἀδυνατέω is "be impossible" (Matt 17:20) or "be without power".
- **"Behold the handmaid of the Lord; be it unto me" → "Behold, the slave of the Lord; may it come to pass for me"** (v38; "slave" also v48) — Greek δούλη, the feminine of δοῦλος "slave"; γένοιτο is a wish. The Greek has no verb in the first clause.
- **"in those days … a city of Juda" → "in these days … a city of Judah"** (v39) — Greek ἐν ταῖς ἡμέραις ταύταις; εἰς πόλιν Ἰούδα, the name of the tribe and its land, not Ἰουδαία "Judea" (v5, 65).
- **"the babe" → "the baby"** (v41, 44) — Greek βρέφος, an unborn or newborn child (Acts 7:19).
- **"spake out with a loud voice" → "called out with a loud cry"** (v42) — Greek ἀνεφώνησεν κραυγῇ μεγάλῃ (WH Treg NA28; RP φωνῇ, "voice").
- **"Blessed … blessed" kept** (v42, 45, 48, 68) — three Greek words: εὐλογημένη / εὐλογημένος "blessed, spoken well of" (v42); μακαρία "blessed, happy" (v45; "call me blessed" v48 is its verb); εὐλογητός, said of God (v68).
- **"the voice of thy salutation sounded in mine ears … for joy" → "the voice of your greeting came into my ears … in exultation"** (v44) — Greek ὡς ἐγένετο ἡ φωνὴ … εἰς τὰ ὦτά μου; ἐν ἀγαλλιάσει.
- **"for there shall be a performance of those things which were told her" → "that there will be a completion for the things that have been spoken to her"** (v45) — Greek ὅτι ἔσται τελείωσις τοῖς λελαλημένοις αὐτῇ. ὅτι is "that" (what she believed) or "because" (why she is blessed); both are possible. τελείωσις is from τελειόω "bring to completion, perfect" ("perfection" in Heb 7:11); "fulfil" is a different word (v20).
- **"hath rejoiced in God my Saviour" → "exulted over Elohim my Savior"** (v47) — Greek ἠγαλλίασεν, a past tense beside the present "magnifies"; ἐπὶ τῷ θεῷ, "over, at".
- **"hath regarded the low estate of his handmaiden" → "looked upon the humiliation of his slave"** (v48) — Greek ἐπέβλεψεν ἐπὶ τὴν ταπείνωσιν (1 Sam 1:11); ταπείνωσις is "humiliation, low state" (Acts 8:33), from the root of "humble" (v52).
- **"he that is mighty hath done to me great things" → "the Powerful One did great things for me"** (v49) — Greek ὁ δυνατός; the verbs of v48–54 are simple past tenses.
- **"from generation to generation" → "unto generations and generations"** (v50) — Greek εἰς γενεὰς καὶ γενεάς (WH Treg NA28). The Greek has no verb.
- **"hath shewed strength … in the imagination of their hearts" → "wrought might … in the mind of their heart"** (v51) — Greek ἐποίησεν κράτος, literally "did might"; διανοίᾳ καρδίας αὐτῶν.
- **"put down the mighty from their seats, and exalted them of low degree" → "pulled down rulers from thrones and lifted up the humble"** (v52) — Greek καθεῖλεν δυνάστας ἀπὸ θρόνων καὶ ὕψωσεν ταπεινούς. δυνάστης is one who holds power (a "court official" in Acts 8:27). ταπεινός is "low, lowly, humble"; ὑψόω is "lift up, exalt".
- **"filled" → "filled up"** (v53) — Greek ἐνέπλησεν, a compound; πεινῶντας and πλουτοῦντας are participles, "those hungering", "those being rich".
- **"hath holpen his servant Israel, in remembrance of his mercy" → "helped Israel his servant, to remember mercy"** (v54) — Greek ἀντελάβετο, "took hold of to help" (Acts 20:35); παιδὸς αὐτοῦ, "servant" or "child" (Isa 41:8–9); μνησθῆναι ἐλέους, an infinitive without "his".
- **"to Abraham, and to his seed for ever" kept** (v55) — Greek "to our fathers" is πρὸς τοὺς πατέρας, "to Abraham and to his seed" a plain dative: the dative may go with "to remember mercy" (v54), with "as he spoke to our fathers" set in between. σπέρμα is "seed".
- **"cousins" → "kinsmen"; "had shewed great mercy upon her" → "magnified his mercy with her"** (v58) — Greek συγγενεῖς; ἐμεγάλυνεν … τὸ ἔλεος αὐτοῦ μετ' αὐτῆς, the verb of v46.
- **"they called him" → "they were calling him"** (v59) — Greek ἐκάλουν, imperfect: they were about to, or tried to. παιδίον is "young child" (v59, 66, 76, 80).
- **"made signs … how he would have him called" → "were making signs … as to what he would want him to be called"** (v62) — Greek ἐνένευον, "nodded, signaled"; τὸ τί ἂν θέλοι καλεῖσθαι αὐτό.
- **"his tongue loosed" → "and his tongue"** (v64) — Greek ἀνεῴχθη … τὸ στόμα αὐτοῦ … καὶ ἡ γλῶσσα αὐτοῦ: one verb, "was opened", for both; "loosed" is not in the Greek. παραχρῆμα is "instantly".
- **"all these sayings were noised abroad" → "all these words were being talked over"** (v65) — Greek διελαλεῖτο πάντα τὰ ῥήματα ταῦτα; ῥήματα is "words" or "things spoken of".
- **"laid them up in their hearts … What manner of child shall this be! And the hand" → "laid them in their heart … What then will this young child be? For indeed the hand"** (v66) — Greek ἔθεντο … ἐν τῇ καρδίᾳ αὐτῶν; "them" is supplied. καὶ γὰρ (WH Treg NA28; RP has no γάρ).
- **"hath visited and redeemed his people" → "visited and wrought redemption for his people"** (v68) — Greek ἐπεσκέψατο καὶ ἐποίησεν λύτρωσιν τῷ λαῷ αὐτοῦ, literally "made redemption"; "visited" has no object of its own.
- **"which have been since the world began" → "from of old"** (v70) — Greek ἀπ' αἰῶνος, "from the age"; the wording of Acts 3:21.
- **"That we should be saved from our enemies" → "salvation from our enemies"** (v71) — Greek σωτηρίαν, a noun taking up "a horn of salvation" (v69). "from" is ἐξ, "out of", both times.
- **"To perform the mercy promised to our fathers" → "to do mercy with our fathers"** (v72) — Greek ποιῆσαι ἔλεος μετὰ τῶν πατέρων ἡμῶν; "promised" is not in the Greek.
- **"that he would grant unto us, that we being delivered out of the hand of our enemies might serve him without fear" → "to give to us that without fear, rescued out of the hand of enemies, we should worship him"** (v73–74) — Greek τοῦ δοῦναι ἡμῖν ἀφόβως ἐκ χειρὸς ἐχθρῶν ῥυσθέντας λατρεύειν αὐτῷ; "without fear" stands first. λατρεύω is the service of worship given to God. "our" with "enemies" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"In holiness … all the days of our life" → "in devoutness … all our days"** (v75) — Greek ὁσιότης (Eph 4:24), not ἁγιωσύνη; "of life" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"And thou, child … before the face of the Lord to prepare" → "And you also, young child … before the Lord to make ready"** (v76) — Greek καὶ σὺ δέ; ἐνώπιον κυρίου WH NA28 SBLGNT ] πρὸ προσώπου, "before the face of", Treg RP.
- **"by the remission" → "in forgiveness"** (v77) — Greek ἐν ἀφέσει.
- **"Through the tender mercy of our God; whereby the dayspring from on high hath visited us" → "through the inward parts of mercy of our Elohim, in which a rising from on high will visit us"** (v78) — Greek διὰ σπλάγχνα ἐλέους, "inward parts of mercy". ἀνατολή is "a rising" (of the sun or a star, Matt 2:2; the east); the Septuagint also uses it for the "Branch" (Jer 23:5; Zech 3:8; 6:12). It has no article. Editions: ἐπισκέψεται, "will visit", WH NA28 SBLGNT ] ἐπεσκέψατο, "visited", Treg RP.
- **"To give light to them that sit in darkness and in the shadow of death, to guide our feet into the way of peace" → "to appear to those sitting in darkness and shadow of death, to direct our feet into a way of peace"** (v79) — Greek ἐπιφᾶναι, "appear, show oneself" (Titus 2:11); κατευθῦναι (1 Thess 3:11); ὁδὸν εἰρήνης without the article.
- **"grew, and waxed strong in spirit, and was in the deserts till the day of his shewing" → "was growing and being made mighty in spirit, and he was in the wildernesses until the day of his showing"** (v80) — Greek ηὔξανε καὶ ἐκραταιοῦτο πνεύματι, imperfects; πνεύματι is "in spirit" or "by the Spirit". ἐν ταῖς ἐρήμοις is plural. ἀνάδειξις is a showing forth, the public presentation of one appointed (the verb in Acts 1:24).

### Notes
- **v17** echoes Mal 4:5-6.
- **v46-55** — the *Magnificat*, echoing Hannah's song (1 Sam 2:1-10).
- **v68-79** — the *Benedictus*, drawing on Ps 105:8-9; 106:10, 45; Mic 7:20; Isa 9:2.
- **v1–4** — one sentence in Greek.
