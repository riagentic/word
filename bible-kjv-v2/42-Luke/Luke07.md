# Luke 7

[1] After he had fulfilled all his words into the ears of the people, he entered into ⟦capernaum|Kfar Nachum|Capernaum⟧.

[2] Now a certain centurion's slave, being ill, was about to die, who was held in honor by him.

[3] And having heard about ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, he sent to him elders of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, requesting him that he would come and save his slave through.

[4] And they, arriving to ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, were appealing to him diligently, saying, "He is worthy that you should provide this for him,

[5] for he loves our nation, and the synagogue he himself built for us."

[6] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was going with them. But when he was already not far distant from the house, the centurion sent friends, saying to him, "Lord, do not bother yourself, for I am not fit that you should enter under my roof;

[7] therefore neither did I count myself worthy to come to you. But say it with a word, and let my boy be healed.

[8] For I also am a man set under authority, having soldiers under myself; and I say to this one, 'Go,' and he goes, and to another, 'Come,' and he comes, and to my slave, 'Do this,' and he does it."

[9] And hearing these things, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ marveled at him, and turning to the crowd following him, he said, "I say to you, not even in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ have I found so great faith."

[10] And returning to the house, those who had been sent found the slave healthy.

[11] And it came to pass in the time that followed that he went into a city called ⟦nain|Naim|Nain⟧, and his disciples were going along with him, and a great crowd.

[12] And as he drew near to the gate of the city, behold, there was being carried out one who had died, an only son to his mother, and she was a widow; and a considerable crowd of the city was with her.

[13] And seeing her, the Lord was moved with compassion over her and said to her, "Do not weep."

[14] And approaching, he touched the bier, and those bearing it stood; and he said, "Young man, to you I say, arise."

[15] And the dead man sat up and began to speak, and he gave him to his mother.

[16] And fear took all, and they were glorifying ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, saying, "A great prophet arose among us," and, "⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ visited his people."

[17] And this word went out in the whole of ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ concerning him, and all the surrounding region.

[18] And his disciples reported to ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ concerning all these things. And calling to himself a certain two of his disciples, ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧

[19] sent to the Lord, saying, "Are you the coming one, or are we to expect another?"

[20] And arriving to him, the men said, "⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ the ⟦immerser|Immerser|Baptist⟧ sent us to you, saying, 'Are you the coming one, or are we to expect another?'"

[21] In that hour he healed many from diseases and scourges and evil spirits, and to many blind he granted to see.

[22] And answering, he said to them, "Go and report to ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ what you saw and heard: **the blind receive sight, the lame walk, lepers are cleansed and the deaf hear, the dead are raised, the poor have the gospel preached to them**;

[23] and blessed is whoever is not made to stumble in me."

[24] And when ⟦john|Yochanan's|John's⟧ messengers had gone away, he began to say to the crowds concerning ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧: "What did you go out into the wilderness to behold? A reed shaken by wind?

[25] But what did you go out to see? A man arrayed in soft garments? Behold, those who are in glorious clothing and luxury are in the royal palaces.

[26] But what did you go out to see? A prophet? Yes, I say to you, and more than a prophet.

[27] This is he concerning whom it is written,

'**Behold, I send my messenger before your face,
who will prepare your way before you**.'

[28] I say to you, among those born of women there is no one greater than ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧; but the lesser in the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is greater than he."

[29] (And all the people, having heard, and the toll collectors, justified ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, having been ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ with the ⟦immersion|immersion|baptism⟧ of ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧;

[30] but the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ and the lawyers set aside the counsel of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for themselves, not having been ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ by him.)

[31] "To what then shall I liken the men of this generation, and to what are they like?

[32] They are like young children who sit in a marketplace and call to one another, who say,

'We played the flute for you, and you did not dance;
we lamented, and you did not weep.'

[33] For ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ the ⟦immerser|Immerser|Baptist⟧ has come not eating bread nor drinking wine, and you say, 'He has a demon.'

[34] The Son of Man has come eating and drinking, and you say, 'Behold, a man who is a glutton and a wine drinker, a friend of toll collectors and sinners.'

[35] And wisdom was justified by all her children."

[36] Now one of the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ was requesting him to eat with him; and entering into the ⟦pharisee|Parush's|Pharisee's⟧ house, he reclined.

[37] And behold, a woman who was in the city, a sinner; and having come to know that he is reclining in the ⟦pharisee|Parush's|Pharisee's⟧ house, bringing an alabaster flask of ointment,

[38] and standing behind at his feet, weeping, she began to wet his feet with the tears, and with the hairs of her head she was wiping them, and she was kissing his feet and anointing them with the ointment.

[39] But the ⟦pharisee|Parush|Pharisee⟧ who had called him, seeing it, spoke within himself, saying, "This one, if he were a prophet, would know who and of what sort the woman is who is touching him, that she is a sinner."

[40] And answering, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to him, "⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧, I have something to say to you." And he says, "Teacher, say it."

[41] "There were two debtors to a certain lender: the one owed five hundred denarii, and the other fifty.

[42] As they did not have the means to repay, he granted it to both. Which of them, then, will love him more?"

[43] Answering, ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ said, "I suppose that it is the one to whom he granted the more." And he said to him, "You judged rightly."

[44] And turning to the woman, he said to ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧, "Do you see this woman? I entered into your house; water for my feet you did not give me, but she wet my feet with the tears and wiped them with her hairs.

[45] A kiss you did not give me; but she, from the time I entered, did not cease kissing my feet.

[46] With oil you did not anoint my head; but she anointed my feet with ointment.

[47] For which reason, I say to you, her sins, the many, have been forgiven, for she loved much; but he to whom little is forgiven loves little."

[48] And he said to her, "Your sins have been forgiven."

[49] And those reclining with him began to say within themselves, "Who is this, who even forgives sins?"

[50] But he said to the woman, "Your faith has saved you; go in peace."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved. Where the Greek has only "he" (v15, 24) no name is supplied.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv16, 28, 29, 30) — divine name retained.
- **"John the Baptist" → "John the Immerser"** (vv20, 33) — "Immerser" renders *baptistēs*; consistent rendering.
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (vv29-30) — Greek *baptizō*; "baptism of John" → "immersion of John."
- **"ended all his sayings in the audience of the people" → "fulfilled all his words into the ears of the people"** (v1) — Greek ἐπλήρωσεν πάντα τὰ ῥήματα αὐτοῦ εἰς τὰς ἀκοὰς τοῦ λαοῦ: πληρόω "fill, fulfill" (Acts 14:26), not τελέω "finish" (Matt 7:28); ῥήματα, spoken words.
- **"servant" → "slave" / "boy"** (v2, 3, 7, 8, 10) — Greek δοῦλος "slave"; in v7 the centurion says ὁ παῖς μου "my boy" (child or young servant), as Matt 8:6–8.
- **"sick" → "ill"; "dear unto him" → "held in honor by him"** (v2) — Greek κακῶς ἔχων; ἔντιμος (Phil 2:29).
- **"heal his servant" → "save his slave through"** (v3) — Greek διασώσῃ, "bring safely through" (Matt 14:36), a compound of σῴζω "save"; here of saving from death by illness.
- **"besought him instantly" → "were appealing to him diligently"** (v4) — Greek παρεκάλουν σπουδαίως.
- **"for whom he should do this" → "that you should provide this for him"** (v4) — Greek ᾧ παρέξῃ τοῦτο (WH Treg NA28), second person; RP παρέξει.
- **"trouble not thyself" → "do not bother yourself"; "not worthy" → "not fit"** (v6) — Greek μὴ σκύλλου (Mark 5:35); ἱκανός, beside ἄξιος "worthy" in v4 and ἠξίωσα "count worthy" in v7.
- **"my servant shall be healed" → "let my boy be healed"** (v7) — Greek ἰαθήτω, imperative (WH Treg NA28; RP future, as Matt 8:8).
- **"set under authority"** (v8) — Greek ὑπὸ ἐξουσίαν τασσόμενος; Matt 8:9 lacks "set".
- **"whole" → "healthy"** (v10) — Greek ὑγιαίνοντα; "that had been sick" (KJV) is RP only.
- **"the day after" → "in the time that followed"** (v11) — Greek ἐν τῷ ἑξῆς (masculine: "time" understood). With a feminine article the phrase means "on the next day" (9:37). The verse reads ἐπορεύθη "he went" (WH NA28; Treg RP imperfect); "many of" his disciples is RP only.
- **"a dead man" → "one who had died"; "the only son of" → "an only son to"** (v12) — Greek τεθνηκώς; μονογενὴς υἱὸς τῇ μητρὶ αὐτοῦ. "considerable" is ἱκανός.
- **"had compassion on her" → "was moved with compassion over her"** (v13) — Greek ἐσπλαγχνίσθη (root σπλάγχνα "inward parts").
- **"Arise"** (v14) — Greek ἐγέρθητι, passive in form ("be raised"); the same verb in v16 ("arose") and v22 ("are raised").
- **"is risen up" → "arose"; "hath visited" → "visited"** (v16) — Greek ἠγέρθη (WH Treg NA28; RP perfect); ἐπεσκέψατο, aorists.
- **"rumour" → "word"** (v17) — Greek λόγος.
- **"sent them to Jesus" → "sent to the Lord"** (v19) — Greek πρὸς τὸν κύριον (WH Treg NA28; RP "Jesus"). "another" is ἄλλον (NA28 RP; 2–2, NA28 decides; WH Treg ἕτερον, as Matt 11:3), with no difference in English.
- **"he that should come" → "the coming one"; "look we for" → "are we to expect"** (v19, 20) — Greek ὁ ἐρχόμενος; προσδοκῶμεν, as Matt 11:3.
- **"infirmities and plagues" → "diseases and scourges"; "gave sight" → "granted to see"** (v21) — Greek νόσων καὶ μαστίγων; ἐχαρίσατο βλέπειν (χαρίζομαι "give as a favor", root χάρις).
- **"the deaf hear" → "and the deaf hear"** (v22) — the verse follows καὶ κωφοί (WH NA28; Treg RP without "and"). The words are those of Matt 11:5; "you saw and heard" is aorist.
- **"offended in me" → "made to stumble in me"** (v23) — Greek σκανδαλισθῇ ἐν ἐμοί.
- **"gorgeously apparelled, and live delicately, are in kings' courts" → "in glorious clothing and luxury are in the royal palaces"** (v25) — Greek οἱ ἐν ἱματισμῷ ἐνδόξῳ καὶ τρυφῇ ὑπάρχοντες ἐν τοῖς βασιλείοις.
- **"a greater prophet than John the Baptist" → "no one greater than *Yochanan*"** (v28) — the text has only Ἰωάννου (WH NA28; Treg + "prophet", RP + "prophet … the Baptist"). "the lesser" is a comparative (μικρότερος), as Matt 11:11.
- **"publicans" → "toll collectors"; "rejected the counsel of God against themselves" → "set aside the counsel of *Elohim* for themselves"** (v29–30) — Greek τελῶναι; ἠθέτησαν; εἰς ἑαυτούς, "as to themselves".
- **"calling one to another, and saying" → "call to one another, who say"** (v32) — Greek ἃ λέγει (WH NA28; Treg λέγοντες, RP καὶ λέγουσιν). "mourned to you" → "lamented": "to you" is RP only.
- **"neither eating bread nor drinking wine" → "not eating bread nor drinking wine"** (v33) — Greek μὴ … μήτε (WH NA28; Treg RP μήτε … μήτε).
- **"a gluttonous man, and a winebibber" → "a man who is a glutton and a wine drinker"** (v34) — Greek φάγος καὶ οἰνοπότης, as Matt 11:19.
- **"But wisdom is justified of all her children" → "And wisdom was justified by all her children"** (v35) — Greek καὶ ἐδικαιώθη, aorist; ἀπό, "from, on the part of".
- **"desired him" → "was requesting him"; "sat down to meat" → "reclined"** (v36) — Greek ἠρώτα, imperfect; κατεκλίθη.
- **"a woman in the city, which was a sinner" → "a woman who was in the city, a sinner"** (v37) — Greek γυνὴ ἥτις ἦν ἐν τῇ πόλει ἁμαρτωλός (WH Treg NA28). The Greek says no more of her than this; she is not named. The sentence runs on through v38 with participles, as in the Greek.
- **"when she knew" → "having come to know"** (v37) — Greek ἐπιγνοῦσα.
- **"to wash his feet with tears, and did wipe" → "to wet his feet with the tears … was wiping … was kissing … anointing"** (v38) — Greek βρέχειν "to wet" (the word for rain); ἐξέμασσεν, κατεφίλει, ἤλειφεν are imperfects.
- **"which had bidden him" → "who had called him"** (v39) — Greek ὁ καλέσας.
- **"would have known" → "would know"** (v39) — Greek ἐγίνωσκεν ἄν, an unreal condition of the present.
- **"Master, say on" → "Teacher, say it"** (v40) — Greek διδάσκαλε; "he says" (φησίν) is a present.
- **"creditor" → "lender"** (v41) — Greek δανιστής, of the verb "lend" (6:34).
- **"he frankly forgave them both" → "he granted it to both"** (v42, 43) — Greek ἐχαρίσατο, "gave as a favor" (as v21), not ἀφίημι "forgive" of v47–49; "it" is supplied. "Tell me" (KJV) is RP only.
- **"hath washed … hath anointed" → "wet … anointed"** (v44–46) — Greek aorists. "the hairs of her head" (KJV, v44) is RP; the text has "her hairs".
- **"Wherefore" → "For which reason"; "for she loved much" kept** (v47) — Greek οὗ χάριν; ὅτι ἠγάπησεν πολύ. ὅτι can give the cause ("because she loved") or the sign by which it is known ("as is seen from this, that she loved"); the parable (v42–43) and the second half of the verse favor the second. "for" leaves both open.
- **"are forgiven" → "have been forgiven"** (v47, 48) — Greek ἀφέωνται, perfect; "is forgiven" (ἀφίεται) in v47b is present.
- **"sat at meat with him" → "reclining with him"** (v49) — Greek οἱ συνανακείμενοι.
- **"thy faith hath saved thee"** (v50) — Greek σέσωκεν, the same words as to the healed (8:48; Mark 5:34); here of forgiveness. "in peace" is εἰς εἰρήνην, literally "into peace".
