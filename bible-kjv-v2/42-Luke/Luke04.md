# Luke 4

[1] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, full of ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧, returned from the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, and he was being led in the Spirit in the wilderness

[2] forty days, being tempted by the devil. And he ate nothing in those days, and when they were consummated, he hungered.

[3] And the devil said to him, "If you are Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, say to this stone that it should become a loaf."

[4] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered him, "It is written, '**Not on bread alone shall man live**.'"

[5] And leading him up, he showed him all the kingdoms of the inhabited earth in a moment of time;

[6] and the devil said to him, "To you I will give all this authority and their glory, because it has been delivered to me, and to whomever I want I give it;

[7] you therefore, if you bow down before me, it will all be yours."

[8] And answering, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to him, "It is written, '**You shall bow down to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and him alone shall you worship**.'"

[9] And he led him into ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and set him upon the pinnacle of the temple, and said to him, "If you are Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, cast yourself down from here;

[10] for it is written, '**He will give commandment to his angels concerning you, to guard you**,'

[11] and, '**On hands they will lift you, lest you strike your foot against a stone**.'"

[12] And answering, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to him, "It is said, '**You shall not tempt ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧**.'"

[13] And having consummated every temptation, the devil departed from him until a time.

[14] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ returned in the power of the Spirit into ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧, and a report went out through the whole surrounding region concerning him.

[15] And he himself was teaching in their synagogues, being glorified by all.

[16] And he came to ⟦nazareth|Natzrat|Nazareth⟧, where he had been brought up, and according to what was customary for him he entered on the day of the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ into the synagogue, and he rose to read.

[17] And there was handed to him a book of the prophet ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧, and having unrolled the book he found the place where it was written,

[18] "**The Spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ is upon me,
because he anointed me
to preach the gospel to the poor.
He has sent me to proclaim release to captives
and receiving of sight to the blind,
to send away the crushed in release,
[19] to proclaim an acceptable year of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧.**"

[20] And having rolled up the book and given it back to the assistant, he sat down; and the eyes of all in the synagogue were gazing at him.

[21] And he began to say to them, "**Today this Scripture has been fulfilled in your ears.**"

[22] And all were testifying to him and marveling at the words of grace going out of his mouth, and they were saying, "Is this one not ⟦joseph|Yosef's|Joseph's⟧ son?"

[23] And he said to them, "Surely you will say to me this parable: 'Physician, heal yourself'; as many things as we heard came to pass in ⟦capernaum|Kfar Nachum|Capernaum⟧, do here also in your fatherland."

[24] And he said, "Amen, I say to you that no prophet is acceptable in his fatherland.

[25] But in truth I say to you, there were many widows in the days of ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, when the heaven was shut for three years and six months, when a great famine came upon all the land,

[26] and to none of them was ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ sent, except to ⟦zarephath|Tzarfat|Zarephath⟧ of the land of ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧, to a woman, a widow.

[27] And there were many lepers in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ in the time of Elisha the prophet, and none of them was cleansed, except Naaman the ⟦aramean|Arammi|Syrian⟧."

[28] And all in the synagogue were filled with rage, hearing these things,

[29] and rising up they cast him out outside the city, and led him as far as the brow of the mountain on which their city had been built, so as to throw him down the cliff;

[30] but he himself, passing through the midst of them, was going on his way.

[31] And he came down to ⟦capernaum|Kfar Nachum|Capernaum⟧, a city of ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧. And he was teaching them on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧;

[32] and they were astonished at his teaching, because his word was with authority.

[33] And in the synagogue there was a man having a spirit of an unclean demon, and he cried out with a loud voice,

[34] "Ha! What have we to do with you, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦nazarenes|Notzri|Nazarene⟧? Did you come to destroy us? I know you, who you are: the Holy One of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[35] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ rebuked him, saying, "Be muzzled, and come out from him!" And the demon, casting him down into the midst, came out from him, having harmed him in nothing.

[36] And wonder came upon all, and they were talking with one another, saying, "What is this word, that with authority and power he orders the unclean spirits, and they come out?"

[37] And a sound concerning him was going out into every place of the surrounding region.

[38] And rising up from the synagogue, he entered into the house of ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧. Now the mother-in-law of ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ was held by a great fever, and they requested him concerning her.

[39] And standing by over her, he rebuked the fever, and it left her; and instantly rising up, she was serving them.

[40] Now as the sun was setting, all, as many as had any who were sick with various diseases, led them to him; and he, laying his hands on each one of them, was healing them.

[41] And demons also were coming out from many, shouting and saying, "You are the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!" And rebuking them, he was not letting them speak, because they knew him to be the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[42] And when day came, going out he went into a desert place; and the crowds were seeking after him, and they came as far as him, and they were restraining him, that he might not go from them.

[43] But he said to them, "To the other cities also I must preach the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, because for this I was sent."

[44] And he was proclaiming in the synagogues of ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv3, 8, 9, 12, 34, 41, 43) — divine name retained.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v41) — Hebrew Anointed.
- **"Holy Ghost" / "Spirit" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*" / "Spirit"** (vv1, 14) — Hebrew divine Spirit at first; thereafter "the Spirit" as the Greek has it (τῷ πνεύματι, τοῦ πνεύματος). In v1 πνεύματος ἁγίου has no article.
- **"devil" → "the devil"** (vv2, 3, 6, 13) — Greek *diabolos* = slanderer/accuser; distinct from *daimonia* (demons).
- **"devils" → "demons"** (v33, 35, 41) — Greek *daimonion*.
- **"was led by the Spirit into the wilderness, being forty days tempted" → "was being led in the Spirit in the wilderness forty days, being tempted"** (v1–2) — Greek ἤγετο ἐν τῷ πνεύματι ἐν τῇ ἐρήμῳ (WH Treg NA28; RP εἰς τὴν ἔρημον, "into"): an imperfect, "in" both times. Matt 4:1 has "was led up into the wilderness by the Spirit"; Mark 1:12 "the Spirit casts him out".
- **"when they were ended, he afterward hungered" → "when they were consummated, he hungered"** (v2; also v13) — Greek συντελεσθεισῶν αὐτῶν, the verb of Acts 21:27, "bring to a full end"; "afterward" (RP) is not in the critical text. ἐπείνασεν as Matt 4:2.
- **"If thou be the Son of God, command this stone that it be made bread" → "If you are Son of Elohim, say to this stone that it should become a loaf"** (v3; also v9) — Greek εἰ υἱὸς εἶ τοῦ θεοῦ, without the article before "Son", word for word as Matt 4:3, 6; εἰπὲ τῷ λίθῳ τούτῳ ἵνα γένηται ἄρτος, one stone and one loaf (Matt 4:3 "these stones … loaves").
- **"That man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word of God" → "Not on bread alone shall man live"** (v4) — Greek οὐκ ἐπ' ἄρτῳ μόνῳ ζήσεται ὁ ἄνθρωπος, word for word as the first line of Matt 4:4. Editions: + ἀλλ' ἐπὶ παντὶ ῥήματι θεοῦ, "but on every word of God", Treg RP, not WH NA28 SBLGNT.
- **"And the devil, taking him up into an high mountain, shewed unto him all the kingdoms of the world" → "And leading him up, he showed him all the kingdoms of the inhabited earth"** (v5) — Greek ἀναγαγὼν αὐτόν; "the devil … into a high mountain" (RP) is not in the critical text. οἰκουμένη as 2:1 (Matt 4:8 has κόσμος, "world"). στιγμὴ χρόνου is literally "a point of time".
- **"All this power will I give thee, and the glory of them: for that is delivered unto me; and to whomsoever I will I give it" → "To you I will give all this authority and their glory, because it has been delivered to me, and to whomever I want I give it"** (v6) — Greek ἐξουσία, "authority"; παραδέδοται, perfect.
- **"If thou therefore wilt worship me, all shall be thine" → "you therefore, if you bow down before me, it will all be yours"** (v7; "bow down" also v8) — Greek προσκυνέω; ἐνώπιον ἐμοῦ, "before me"; ἔσται σοῦ πᾶσα, "all of it [the authority] will be yours".
- **"Get thee behind me, Satan: for it is written, Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve" → "It is written, 'You shall bow down to Yahweh your Elohim, and him alone shall you worship'"** (v8) — "Get thee behind me, Satan" (RP) is not in the critical text. Κύριον τὸν θεόν σου προσκυνήσεις καὶ αὐτῷ μόνῳ λατρεύσεις, word for word as Matt 4:10; λατρεύω is the service of worship (1:74; 2:37).
- **"brought him to Jerusalem, and set him on a pinnacle … cast thyself down from hence" → "led him into Jerusalem and set him upon the pinnacle … cast yourself down from here"** (v9) — Greek ἤγαγεν (Matt 4:5 "takes him along into the holy city"); τὸ πτερύγιον τοῦ ἱεροῦ, literally "the little wing of the temple"; ἐντεῦθεν, "from here", is Luke's.
- **"He shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee: And in their hands they shall bear thee up, lest at any time thou dash thy foot" → "He will give commandment to his angels concerning you, to guard you … On hands they will lift you, lest you strike your foot"** (v10–11) — the wording of Matt 4:6, where the Greek is the same; Luke adds τοῦ διαφυλάξαι σε, "to guard you carefully" (Ps 91:11), and a second ὅτι.
- **"It is said, Thou shalt not tempt" kept** (v12) — Greek εἴρηται, perfect, "it has been said" (Matt 4:7 "it is written"); ἐκπειράσεις as Matt 4:7.
- **"when the devil had ended all the temptation, he departed from him for a season" → "having consummated every temptation, the devil departed from him until a time"** (v13) — Greek πάντα πειρασμόν, "every temptation", without the article; ἀπέστη; ἄχρι καιροῦ (Acts 13:11).
- **"there went out a fame of him through all the region round about" → "a report went out through the whole surrounding region concerning him"** (v14) — Greek φήμη (Matt 9:26); περίχωρος (3:3).
- **"he taught" → "he himself was teaching"** (v15) — Greek καὶ αὐτὸς ἐδίδασκεν, imperfect.
- **"as his custom was, he went into the synagogue on the sabbath day, and stood up for to read" → "according to what was customary for him he entered on the day of the Shabbat into the synagogue, and he rose to read"** (v16) — Greek κατὰ τὸ εἰωθὸς αὐτῷ, the wording of Acts 17:2; ἐν τῇ ἡμέρᾳ τῶν σαββάτων (Acts 13:14); ἀνέστη. "Nazareth" is here Ναζαρά (so Matt 4:13).
- **"there was delivered unto him the book of the prophet Esaias. And when he had opened the book" → "there was handed to him a book of the prophet Isaiah, and having unrolled the book"** (v17; also v20) — Greek ἐπεδόθη; βιβλίον, "book", here a roll, without the article. Editions: ἀναπτύξας, "having unrolled", NA28 RP SBLGNT ] ἀνοίξας, "having opened", WH Treg — 2–2 among the four editions, NA28 decides. v20 πτύξας is "having rolled up".
- **"because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised, to preach the acceptable year of the Lord" → "because he anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor. He has sent me to proclaim release to captives and receiving of sight to the blind, to send away the crushed in release, to proclaim an acceptable year of Yahweh"** (v18–19) — the Greek of the quotation as printed (Isa 61:1–2 with a line from Isa 58:6, in the Greek version): οὗ εἵνεκεν, "because" (literally "on account of which"); ἔχρισεν, a simple past, ἀπέσταλκεν, a perfect; κηρύξαι, "proclaim", twice; ἄφεσις, "release", twice: the word rendered "forgiveness" in 1:77; 3:3; ἀνάβλεψις, the noun of "receive sight" (the Hebrew has "opening to those who are bound"); ἀποστεῖλαι τεθραυσμένους ἐν ἀφέσει, literally "to send away crushed ones in release"; ἐνιαυτὸν κυρίου δεκτόν, without the article, δεκτός as in v24. "to heal the brokenhearted" (RP) is not in the critical text. Πνεῦμα κυρίου has no article and no verb: "is" is supplied. The stop after "the poor" is the editors'; the words can also be joined "he anointed me; to preach the gospel to the poor he has sent me".
- **"gave it again to the minister … were fastened on him" → "given it back to the assistant … were gazing at him"** (v20) — Greek ὑπηρέτης (1:2; Acts 13:5), here the synagogue attendant; ἀτενίζοντες.
- **"This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears" → "Today this Scripture has been fulfilled in your ears"** (v21) — Greek πεπλήρωται, perfect.
- **"all bare him witness, and wondered at the gracious words which proceeded out of his mouth. And they said, Is not this Joseph's son" → "all were testifying to him and marveling at the words of grace going out of his mouth, and they were saying, 'Is this one not Joseph's son'"** (v22) — Greek ἐμαρτύρουν αὐτῷ: a dative, "testifying to / for him" (it can also be taken as against him); τοῖς λόγοις τῆς χάριτος (Acts 14:3; 20:32). Οὐχὶ υἱός ἐστιν Ἰωσὴφ οὗτος, "son" without the article (WH NA28 SBLGNT; Treg RP "the son").
- **"Ye will surely say unto me this proverb … whatsoever we have heard done in Capernaum, do also here in thy country" → "Surely you will say to me this parable … as many things as we heard came to pass in Capernaum, do here also in your fatherland"** (v23; "fatherland" also v24) — Greek παραβολή, "parable", here a proverb; εἰς τὴν Καφαρναούμ, literally "unto Capernaum"; πατρίς (Mark 6:1, 4), one's native place.
- **"Verily I say unto you, No prophet is accepted in his own country" → "Amen, I say to you that no prophet is acceptable in his fatherland"** (v24) — Greek Ἀμὴν λέγω ὑμῖν; δεκτός, the word of v19. Mark 6:4 and Matt 13:57 have a different saying ("not without honor except …").
- **"when the heaven was shut up three years and six months, when great famine was throughout all the land" → "when the heaven was shut for three years and six months, when a great famine came upon all the land"** (v25) — Greek ὁ οὐρανός, singular; ἐπὶ ἔτη τρία NA28 RP SBLGNT ] without ἐπί WH Treg, no difference in English; ἐπὶ πᾶσαν τὴν γῆν, "land" or "earth".
- **"save unto Sarepta, a city of Sidon, unto a woman that was a widow" → "except to Zarephath of the land of Sidon, to a woman, a widow"** (v26; "except" also v27) — Greek εἰς Σάρεπτα τῆς Σιδωνίας (WH Treg NA28), "of the Sidonian [land]"; "a city" is not in the Greek. εἰ μή is "except", here in the sense "but only": the widow and Naaman were not of Israel.
- **"in the time of Eliseus the prophet … saving Naaman the Syrian" → "in the time of Elisha the prophet … except Naaman the Syrian"** (v27) — Greek ἐπὶ Ἐλισαίου τοῦ προφήτου (the construction of 3:2); ὁ Σύρος.
- **"filled with wrath" → "filled with rage"** (v28) — Greek θυμός; "wrath" is ὀργή (3:7).
- **"thrust him out of the city, and led him unto the brow of the hill whereon their city was built, that they might cast him down headlong" → "cast him out outside the city, and led him as far as the brow of the mountain on which their city had been built, so as to throw him down the cliff"** (v29) — Greek ἐξέβαλον, "cast out"; ὄρος, "mountain"; ὥστε (WH Treg NA28; RP εἰς τό); κατακρημνίσαι, "throw down a precipice", only here in the New Testament.
- **"went his way" → "was going on his way"** (v30) — Greek ἐπορεύετο, imperfect.
- **"taught them on the sabbath days" → "was teaching them on the Shabbat"** (v31) — Greek ἦν διδάσκων; ἐν τοῖς σάββασιν, a plural form, as Mark 1:21 (it can also mean "on the Shabbats").
- **"for his word was with power" → "because his word was with authority"** (v32) — Greek ἐν ἐξουσίᾳ, "in authority". The first clause is word for word as Mark 1:22; the reason is Luke's.
- **"which had a spirit of an unclean devil" → "having a spirit of an unclean demon"** (v33) — Greek ἔχων πνεῦμα δαιμονίου ἀκαθάρτου (Mark 1:23 "with an unclean spirit").
- **"Let us alone; what have we to do with thee, thou Jesus of Nazareth? art thou come to destroy us? I know thee who thou art" → "Ha! What have we to do with you, Yeshua Nazarene? Did you come to destroy us? I know you, who you are"** (v34) — after Ἔα the Greek is word for word that of Mark 1:24. Ἔα is a cry of surprise or displeasure; it can also be the imperative of ἐάω, "let be!" (the verb of v41). Ναζαρηνέ is the form of address "Nazarene", without an article; the place name is not in the verse. ἦλθες ἀπολέσαι ἡμᾶς can also be a statement. "saying" before it (Treg RP) is not in WH NA28 SBLGNT.
- **"Hold thy peace, and come out of him. And when the devil had thrown him in the midst, he came out of him, and hurt him not" → "Be muzzled, and come out from him! And the demon, casting him down into the midst, came out from him, having harmed him in nothing"** (v35) — Greek φιμώθητι (Mark 1:25); ἀπ' αὐτοῦ, "from him" (WH Treg NA28; Mark 1:25 and RP ἐξ, "out of"); ῥίψαν; μηδὲν βλάψαν αὐτόν.
- **"they were all amazed, and spake among themselves, saying, What a word is this! for with authority and power he commandeth" → "wonder came upon all, and they were talking with one another, saying, 'What is this word, that with authority and power he orders'"** (v36) — Greek ἐγένετο θάμβος (Acts 3:10; the verb in Mark 1:27, "astounded"); συνελάλουν; τίς ὁ λόγος οὗτος ὅτι: ὅτι is "that" or "because"; ἐπιτάσσει as Mark 1:27. δύναμις, "power", stands beside ἐξουσία, "authority".
- **"the fame of him went out" → "a sound concerning him was going out"** (v37) — Greek ἦχος, "sound, noise" (Acts 2:2), not φήμη (v14) nor Mark's ἀκοή.
- **"he arose out of the synagogue … was taken with a great fever; and they besought him for her" → "rising up from the synagogue … was held by a great fever, and they requested him concerning her"** (v38) — Greek ἀναστὰς ἀπὸ τῆς συναγωγῆς; συνεχομένη πυρετῷ μεγάλῳ (Acts 28:8); ἠρώτησαν, "requested".
- **"he stood over her … and immediately she arose and ministered unto them" → "standing by over her … and instantly rising up, she was serving them"** (v39) — Greek ἐπιστὰς ἐπάνω αὐτῆς (2:9, 38); "rebuked the fever" is Luke's; ἀφῆκεν αὐτήν and διηκόνει αὐτοῖς as Mark 1:31; παραχρῆμα.
- **"when the sun was setting, all they that had any sick with divers diseases brought them unto him; and he laid his hands on every one of them, and healed them" → "as the sun was setting, all, as many as had any who were sick with various diseases, led them to him; and he, laying his hands on each one of them, was healing them"** (v40) — Greek ἀσθενοῦντας, "being weak, sick"; ἤγαγον; ἐπιτιθεὶς ἐθεράπευεν, a present participle and an imperfect (WH Treg NA28; RP aorists).
- **"devils also came out of many, crying out, and saying, Thou art Christ the Son of God. And he rebuking them suffered them not to speak: for they knew that he was Christ" → "demons also were coming out from many, shouting and saying, 'You are the Son of Elohim!' And rebuking them, he was not letting them speak, because they knew him to be the Mashiach"** (v41) — Editions: κραυγάζοντα, "shouting", NA28 SBLGNT ] κράζοντα, "crying out", WH Treg RP — 2–2 among the four editions, NA28 decides. "the Christ" before "the Son of God" (RP) is not in the critical text. οὐκ εἴα is the imperfect of ἐάω "let" (Mark 1:34 has ἤφιεν); ὅτι ᾔδεισαν τὸν χριστὸν αὐτὸν εἶναι can also be "to speak, because they knew …" or "to say that they knew him to be the Mashiach".
- **"he departed and went into a desert place: and the people sought him, and came unto him, and stayed him, that he should not depart" → "going out he went into a desert place; and the crowds were seeking after him, and they came as far as him, and they were restraining him, that he might not go"** (v42) — Greek ἔρημος τόπος (Mark 1:35); οἱ ὄχλοι; ἐπεζήτουν (Mark 1:36 has "Simon and those with him pursued him"); κατεῖχον, imperfect, "were holding him back": they tried to.
- **"I must preach the kingdom of God to other cities also: for therefore am I sent" → "To the other cities also I must preach the kingdom of Elohim, because for this I was sent"** (v43) — Greek εὐαγγελίσασθαί με δεῖ τὴν βασιλείαν τοῦ θεοῦ: the verb "preach the gospel, bring as good news", with the kingdom as its object; ἐπὶ τοῦτο ἀπεστάλην (WH Treg NA28; Mark 1:38 "unto this I came out").
- **"he preached in the synagogues of Galilee" → "he was proclaiming in the synagogues of Judea"** (v44) — Greek ἦν κηρύσσων εἰς τὰς συναγωγάς, literally "into the synagogues" (as Mark 1:39). Editions: τῆς Ἰουδαίας WH NA28 SBLGNT ] τῆς Γαλιλαίας Treg RP (so Mark 1:39) — the verse follows the majority of the four editions. "Judea" can mean the whole land of the Jews, Galilee included (1:5; 23:5).

### Notes
- **v4** quotes Deut 8:3.
- **v8** quotes Deut 6:13.
- **v10-11** quotes Ps 91:11-12.
- **v12** quotes Deut 6:16.
- **v18-19** quotes Isa 61:1-2; 58:6.
- **v5–12** — Luke has the temptations in the order stone, kingdoms, temple; Matt 4:3–10 has stone, temple, kingdoms.
