# Luke 23

[1] Then the whole company of them arose and brought him before ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧.

[2] And they began to accuse him, saying, "We found this man misleading our nation and forbidding us to give tribute to Caesar, and saying that he himself is ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, a king."

[3] And ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ asked him, "Are you the King of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧?" And he answered him, "You have said so."

[4] Then ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ said to the chief priests and the crowds, "I find no guilt in this man."

[5] But they were urgent, saying, "He stirs up the people, teaching throughout all ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧, from ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧ even to this place."

[6] When ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ heard this, he asked whether the man was a Galilean.

[7] And when he learned that he belonged to ⟦herod|Herodes's|Herod's⟧ jurisdiction, he sent him over to ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧, who was himself in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ at that time.

[8] When ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ saw ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, he was very glad, for he had long desired to see him, because he had heard about him, and he was hoping to see some sign done by him.

[9] So he questioned him at some length, but he made no answer.

[10] The chief priests and the scribes stood by, vehemently accusing him.

[11] And ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ with his soldiers treated him with contempt and mocked him. Then, arraying him in splendid clothing, he sent him back to ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧.

[12] And ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ and ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ became friends with each other that very day, for before this they had been at enmity with each other.

[13] ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ then called together the chief priests and the rulers and the people,

[14] and said to them, "You brought me this man as one who was misleading the people. And after examining him before you, behold, I did not find this man guilty of any of your charges against him.

[15] Neither did ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧, for he sent him back to us. Look, nothing deserving death has been done by him.

[16] I will therefore punish and release him."

[18] But they all cried out together, "Away with this man, and release to us ⟦barabbas|Bar-Abba|Barabbas⟧!" —

[19] a man who had been thrown into prison for an insurrection started in the city and for murder.

[20] ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ addressed them once more, desiring to release ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧,

[21] but they kept shouting, "Crucify, crucify him!"

[22] A third time he said to them, "Why? What evil has he done? I have found in him no guilt deserving death. I will therefore punish and release him."

[23] But they were urgent, demanding with loud cries that he should be crucified. And their voices prevailed.

[24] So ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ decided that their demand should be granted.

[25] He released the man who had been thrown into prison for insurrection and murder, for whom they asked, but he delivered ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ over to their will.

[26] And as they led him away, they seized one ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ of ⟦cyrene|Kyrene|Cyrene⟧, who was coming in from the country, and laid on him the cross, to carry it behind ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[27] And there followed him a great multitude of the people and of women who were mourning and lamenting for him.

[28] But turning to them ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said, "Daughters of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, do not weep for me, but weep for yourselves and for your children.

[29] For behold, the days are coming when they will say, 'Blessed are the barren and the wombs that never bore and the breasts that never nursed!'

[30] Then they will begin to say to the mountains, '**Fall on us**,' and to the hills, '**Cover us**.'

[31] For if they do these things when the wood is green, what will happen when it is dry?"

[32] Two others, who were criminals, were led away to be put to death with him.

[33] And when they came to the place that is called The Skull, there they crucified him, and the criminals, one on his right and one on his left.

[34] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said, "**Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do.**" And they cast lots to divide his garments.

[35] And the people stood by, watching, but the rulers scoffed at him, saying, "He saved others; let him save himself, if he is the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧ of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, his Chosen One!"

[36] The soldiers also mocked him, coming up and offering him sour wine

[37] and saying, "If you are the King of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, save yourself!"

[38] There was also an inscription over him, "This is the King of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧."

[39] One of the criminals who were hanged railed at him, saying, "Are you not the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧? Save yourself and us!"

[40] But the other rebuked him, saying, "Do you not fear ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, since you are under the same sentence of condemnation?

[41] And we indeed justly, for we are receiving the due reward of our deeds; but this man has done nothing wrong."

[42] And he said, "⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, remember me when you come into your kingdom."

[43] And he said to him, "Truly, I say to you, today you will be with me in Paradise."

[44] It was now about the sixth hour, and there was darkness over the whole land until the ninth hour,

[45] while the sun's light failed. And the veil of the temple was torn in two.

[46] Then ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, calling out with a loud voice, said, "**Father, into your hands I commit my spirit!**" And having said this he breathed his last.

[47] Now when the centurion saw what had taken place, he praised ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, saying, "Certainly this man was righteous!"

[48] And all the crowds that had assembled for this spectacle, when they saw what had taken place, returned home beating their breasts.

[49] And all his acquaintances and the women who had followed him from ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧ stood at a distance watching these things.

[50] Now there was a man named ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, from ⟦arimathea|Arimathaia|Arimathea⟧, a town of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧. He was a member of the ⟦sanhedrin|Sanhedrin|council⟧, a good and righteous man,

[51] who had not consented to their decision and action; and he was looking for the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[52] This man went to ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ and asked for the body of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[53] Then he took it down and wrapped it in a linen shroud and laid him in a tomb cut in stone, where no one had ever yet been laid.

[54] It was the day of Preparation, and the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ was beginning.

[55] The women who had come with him from ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧ followed and saw the tomb and how his body was laid.

[56] Then they returned and prepared spices and ointments.

On the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ they rested according to the commandment.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv35, 40, 47) — divine name retained.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (vv2, 35, 39) — Hebrew Anointed.
- **"v17 absent"** — Luke 23:17 ("For of necessity he must release one unto them at the feast") is absent from earliest MSS; noted in ### Notes.
- **"Arimathaea" → "Arimathea, a town of the Jews"** (v50) — Greek ἀπὸ Ἁριμαθαίας πόλεως τῶν Ἰουδαίων; often identified with Ramathaim(-zophim), Samuel's town (1 Sam 1:1; LXX Αρμαθαιμ), but the identification is not certain.

### Notes
- **v17** absent from earliest MSS ("For of necessity he must release one unto them at the feast").
- **v30** quotes Hos 10:8.
- **v34a** absent from some early MSS.
- **v46** echoes Ps 31:5.
