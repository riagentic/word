# Luke 5

[1] Now it came to pass, while the crowd was pressing upon him and hearing the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, that he was standing beside the lake of ⟦gennesaret|Ginosar|Gennesaret⟧,

[2] and he saw two boats standing beside the lake; but the fishers, having gone out from them, were washing the nets.

[3] And stepping into one of the boats, which was ⟦simon|Shimon's|Simon's⟧, he requested him to put out a little from the land; and sitting down, he was teaching the crowds out of the boat.

[4] And when he ceased speaking, he said to ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧, "Put out into the deep, and lower your nets for a catch."

[5] And ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧, answering, said, "Chief, having labored through the whole night we took nothing; but at your word I will lower the nets."

[6] And having done this, they enclosed a great multitude of fish; and their nets were tearing.

[7] And they beckoned to the companions in the other boat to come and help them; and they came, and they filled both the boats, so that they were sinking.

[8] But ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, seeing it, fell down at ⟦yeshua|Yeshua's|Jesus'⟧ knees, saying, "Go out from me, for I am a sinful man, Lord."

[9] For wonder seized him and all those with him at the catch of the fish which they took,

[10] and likewise also ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, sons of ⟦zebedee|Zebedaios|Zebedee⟧, who were partners with ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧. And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧, "Do not fear; from now on you will be taking men alive."

[11] And having brought the boats to the land, leaving all, they followed him.

[12] And it came to pass, while he was in one of the cities, that behold, a man full of leprosy; and seeing ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, he fell upon his face and besought him, saying, "Lord, if you are willing, you are able to cleanse me."

[13] And stretching out his hand, he touched him, saying, "I am willing; be cleansed." And immediately the leprosy went away from him.

[14] And he charged him to tell no one, "but going away, show yourself to the priest, and offer concerning your purification just as ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ prescribed, for a testimony to them."

[15] But the word concerning him was spreading the more, and many crowds were coming together to hear and to be healed from their weaknesses;

[16] but he was retiring in the wildernesses and praying.

[17] And it came to pass on one of the days that he was teaching, and there were sitting ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ and teachers of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, who had come out of every village of ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧ and ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; and the power of the Lord was there for him to heal.

[18] And behold, men bringing on a bed a man who was paralyzed, and they were seeking to bring him in and to lay him before him.

[19] And not finding by what way they might bring him in because of the crowd, going up upon the housetop, they let him down through the tiles with the small bed into the midst, before ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[20] And seeing their faith, he said, "Man, your sins have been forgiven you."

[21] And the scribes and the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ began to reason, saying, "Who is this who speaks blasphemies? Who is able to forgive sins except ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ alone?"

[22] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, recognizing their reasonings, answering said to them, "Why do you reason in your hearts?

[23] Which is easier, to say, 'Your sins have been forgiven you,' or to say, 'Arise and walk'?

[24] But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority upon the earth to forgive sins" — he said to the paralyzed man — "To you I say, arise, and taking up your small bed, go to your house."

[25] And instantly, rising before them, taking up that on which he had been lying, he went away to his house, glorifying ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[26] And amazement took them all, and they were glorifying ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and they were filled with fear, saying, "We saw strange things today."

[27] And after these things he went out and beheld a toll collector, Levi by name, sitting at the toll booth, and he said to him, "Follow me."

[28] And leaving all behind, rising, he was following him.

[29] And Levi made a great reception for him in his house; and there was a great crowd of toll collectors and of others who were reclining with them.

[30] And the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ and their scribes were murmuring to his disciples, saying, "Why do you eat and drink with the toll collectors and sinners?"

[31] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, answering, said to them, "Those who are healthy have no need of a physician, but those who are ill;

[32] I have not come to call righteous ones, but sinners, to repentance."

[33] And they said to him, "⟦john|Yochanan's|John's⟧ disciples fast often and make supplications, likewise also those of the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧, but yours eat and drink."

[34] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "Are you able to make the sons of the bridal chamber fast while the bridegroom is with them?

[35] But days will come; and when the bridegroom is taken away from them, then they will fast in those days."

[36] And he was also speaking a parable to them: "No one, tearing a patch from a new garment, puts it upon an old garment; otherwise he will both tear the new, and the patch from the new will not agree with the old.

[37] And no one puts young wine into old wineskins; otherwise the young wine will burst the wineskins, and it will itself be poured out, and the wineskins will perish;

[38] but young wine must be put into new wineskins.

[39] And no one, having drunk old, wants young; for he says, 'The old is good.'"

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v10) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved. Where the Greek has only "he" (v13, 18) no name is supplied.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv1, 21, 25, 26) — divine name retained.
- **"Simon Peter" → "Simon *Kefa*"** (v8) — Aramaic *Kefa* = Rock; first use of the name in Luke.
- **"Master" → "Chief"** (v5) — Greek ἐπιστάτης *epistatēs*, "one set over": a word only Luke has, always said to *Yeshua*. It is not διδάσκαλος "Teacher", κύριος "Lord" (v8, 12) or δεσπότης "master".
- **"Launch out … let down"** (v4) — "put out" is singular (to Simon), "lower" is plural (χαλάσατε, to the crew).
- **"word"** (v5) — Greek ῥῆμα *rhēma*, the spoken word; in v1 and v15 "word" is λόγος.
- **"partners" → "companions"** (v7) — Greek μέτοχοι *metochoi*, "those who have a share with" (Heb 1:9); the "partners" of v10 are κοινωνοί, another word.
- **"Depart from me" → "Go out from me"** (v8) — Greek ἔξελθε ἀπʼ ἐμοῦ.
- **"he was astonished" → "wonder seized him"** (v9) — Greek θάμβος περιέσχεν αὐτόν; the noun is the subject.
- **"thou shalt catch men" → "you will be taking men alive"** (v10) — Greek ἀνθρώπους ἔσῃ ζωγρῶν: ζωγρέω is "to take alive" (2 Tim 2:26). Mark 1:17 has other words ("fishers of men").
- **"when he saw" / "saying"** (v12, 13) — the verse follows ἰδὼν δέ (WH NA28; Treg RP καὶ ἰδών) and λέγων (WH Treg NA28; RP εἰπών); no difference in meaning.
- **"if thou wilt, thou canst make me clean" → "if you are willing, you are able to cleanse me"** (v12–13) — the same Greek as Mark 1:40–41; καθαρίζω "cleanse".
- **"for thy cleansing, according as Moses commanded" → "concerning your purification just as *Moshe* prescribed"** (v14) — Greek περὶ τοῦ καθαρισμοῦ σου καθὼς προσέταξεν. No object stands with "offer". The sentence passes from reported to direct speech, as in the Greek.
- **"fame" → "word"; "infirmities" → "weaknesses"** (v15) — Greek λόγος; ἀσθένειαι. "by him" (KJV) is not in the text (RP only).
- **"withdrew himself into the wilderness" → "was retiring in the wildernesses"** (v16) — Greek ἦν ὑποχωρῶν ἐν ταῖς ἐρήμοις: a continued action, a plural, and ἐν "in". ὑποχωρέω is not ἀναχωρέω "withdraw".
- **"was present to heal them" → "was there for him to heal"** (v17) — Greek δύναμις κυρίου ἦν εἰς τὸ ἰᾶσθαι αὐτόν (WH NA28): "him" is the one who heals; "there" is supplied for English. Treg RP read αὐτούς, "to heal them". "of the Lord" has no article in Greek.
- **"taken with a palsy" → "paralyzed"** (v18, 24) — Greek παραλελυμένος, a participle; Mark's word is παραλυτικός "paralytic". v18 follows θεῖναι αὐτόν (WH NA28; Treg RP without "him").
- **"couch" → "small bed"** (v19, 24) — Greek κλινίδιον, the diminutive of κλίνη "bed" (v18); Mark has κράβαττος "pallet".
- **"are forgiven" → "have been forgiven"** (v20, 23) — Greek ἀφέωνται, perfect; Mark 2:5, 9 has the present ἀφίενται.
- **"perceived their thoughts" → "recognizing their reasonings"** (v22) — Greek ἐπιγνοὺς τοὺς διαλογισμούς; the noun of the verb "reason" in v21, 22.
- **"power" → "authority"** (v24) — Greek ἐξουσία.
- **"immediately" → "instantly"** (v25) — Greek παραχρῆμα; "immediately" (v13) is εὐθέως.
- **"they were all amazed" → "amazement took them all"** (v26) — Greek ἔκστασις ἔλαβεν ἅπαντας.
- **"strange things" kept** (v26) — Greek παράδοξα, "things contrary to expectation".
- **"saw" → "beheld"; "publican" → "toll collector"; "receipt of custom" → "toll booth"** (v27) — Greek ἐθεάσατο; τελώνης; τελώνιον.
- **"followed" → "was following"** (v28) — Greek ἠκολούθει, imperfect (WH Treg NA28; RP aorist).
- **"feast" → "reception"** (v29) — Greek δοχή, from δέχομαι "receive".
- **"sat down" → "were reclining"** (v29) — Greek κατακείμενοι.
- **"murmured against" → "were murmuring to"** (v30) — Greek ἐγόγγυζον πρός with the accusative.
- **"they that are whole" → "those who are healthy"; "sick" → "ill"** (v31) — Greek οἱ ὑγιαίνοντες (Mark 2:17 "the strong"); οἱ κακῶς ἔχοντες, literally "those having it badly".
- **"I came not" → "I have not come"** (v32) — Greek ἐλήλυθα, perfect; Mark 2:17 has the aorist.
- **"Why do the disciples of John fast" → no "Why"** (v33) — the printed text (WH NA28) is a statement; Treg RP read Διὰ τί "Why".
- **"prayers" → "supplications"** (v33) — Greek δεήσεις.
- **"And he said" → "And *Yeshua* said"** (v34) — the verse follows ὁ δὲ Ἰησοῦς (WH Treg NA28; RP without the name).
- **"children of the bridechamber" → "sons of the bridal chamber"** (v34) — Greek οἱ υἱοὶ τοῦ νυμφῶνος, as Mark 2:19. The question begins with μή and expects "no".
- **"putteth a piece of a new garment" → "tearing a patch from a new garment, puts it upon"** (v36) — Greek ἐπίβλημα ἀπὸ ἱματίου καινοῦ σχίσας ἐπιβάλλει (WH Treg NA28; RP has neither "from" nor "tearing").
- **"agreeth not" → "will not agree"** (v36) — Greek συμφωνήσει, future.
- **"new wine" → "young wine"; "new bottles" → "new wineskins"** (v37–39) — Greek νέος "young" of the wine, καινός "new" of the wineskins and the garment, as Mark 2:22.
- **"and both are preserved" not in the text** (v38) — Treg RP add καὶ ἀμφότεροι συντηροῦνται; WH NA28 do not.
- **"straightway" not in the text; "better" → "good"** (v39) — Greek χρηστός (WH Treg NA28; RP χρηστότερος "better", and + εὐθέως). Of wine the word means "good, pleasant"; of persons it is "kind". "wine" is not repeated in the Greek: "old … young".
