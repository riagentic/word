# Luke 12

[1] Meanwhile, the myriads of the crowd having been gathered together, so that they trampled one another, he began to say to his disciples first, "Take heed to yourselves of the leaven — which is hypocrisy — of the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧.

[2] But nothing is covered up that will not be revealed, and hidden that will not be known.

[3] Therefore whatever you said in the darkness will be heard in the light, and what you spoke to the ear in the inner rooms will be proclaimed upon the housetops.

[4] "But I say to you, my friends: do not be afraid of those who kill the body and after these things have nothing more to do.

[5] But I will show you whom you should fear: fear the one who, after killing, has authority to cast into ⟦gehenna|Gehenna|hell⟧; yes, I say to you, fear this one.

[6] Are not five sparrows sold for two assaria? And one of them is not forgotten before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[7] But even the hairs of your head have all been numbered. Do not fear; you are worth more than many sparrows.

[8] "And I say to you: everyone who confesses me before men, the Son of Man also will confess him before the angels of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[9] but the one who denied me before men will be denied before the angels of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[10] And everyone who will say a word against the Son of Man, it will be forgiven him; but to the one who blasphemed against the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ it will not be forgiven.

[11] And when they bring you in before the synagogues and the principalities and the authorities, do not be anxious about how or with what you should defend yourselves, or what you should say;

[12] for the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ will teach you in that very hour what it is necessary to say."

[13] And someone out of the crowd said to him, "Teacher, tell my brother to divide the inheritance with me."

[14] But he said to him, "Man, who appointed me a judge or divider over you?"

[15] And he said to them, "See, and guard yourselves from all covetousness, because not in anyone's abounding is his life out of what belongs to him."

[16] And he spoke a parable to them, saying, "The land of a certain rich man bore well.

[17] And he reasoned within himself, saying, 'What shall I do, because I have nowhere to gather my fruits?'

[18] And he said, 'This I will do: I will pull down my barns and build greater ones, and I will gather there all the wheat and my good things,

[19] and I will say to my soul, "Soul, you have many good things laid up for many years; rest, eat, drink, make merry."'

[20] But ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said to him, 'Senseless one, this night they ask back your soul from you; and the things which you made ready, whose will they be?'

[21] So is the one who stores up for himself and is not rich toward ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[22] And he said to his disciples, "For this reason I say to you: do not be anxious about the soul, what you shall eat, nor about the body, what you shall put on.

[23] For the soul is more than food, and the body than clothing.

[24] Observe the ravens, that they do not sow nor reap; they have no storeroom nor barn, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ feeds them. How much more are you worth than the birds!

[25] And which of you by being anxious can add a cubit to his span of life?

[26] If then you cannot do even the least thing, why are you anxious about the rest?

[27] Observe the lilies, how they grow: they do not labor nor spin; but I say to you, not even ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ in all his glory clothed himself like one of these.

[28] But if ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ so arrays the grass, which is in a field today and tomorrow is cast into an oven, how much more you, you of little faith!

[29] And you, do not seek what you shall eat and what you shall drink, and do not be in suspense;

[30] for all these things the nations of the world seek after, but your Father knows that you need these things.

[31] Yet **seek his kingdom, and these things will be added to you.**

[32] "Do not fear, little flock, because your Father was pleased to give you the kingdom.

[33] Sell what belongs to you and give alms; make for yourselves purses that do not grow old, an unfailing treasure in the heavens, where a thief does not draw near nor does a moth ruin.

[34] **For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also.**

[35] "Let your loins be girded and the lamps burning,

[36] and you yourselves be like men awaiting their own lord, when he departs from the wedding, so that when he comes and knocks they may immediately open to him.

[37] Blessed are those slaves whom the lord, coming, will find awake. Amen, I say to you that he will gird himself and make them recline, and coming up he will serve them.

[38] And if in the second, and if in the third watch he comes and finds it so, blessed are those.

[39] But know this, that if the master of the house had known at what hour the thief comes, he would not have let his house be dug through.

[40] You also, be ready, because the Son of Man comes at an hour when you do not think."

[41] And ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ said, "Lord, are you saying this parable to us, or also to all?"

[42] And the Lord said, "Who then is the faithful steward, the sensible one, whom the lord will appoint over his attendants, to give the measure of grain in season?

[43] Blessed is that slave whom his lord, coming, will find doing so;

[44] truly I say to you that he will appoint him over all that belongs to him.

[45] But if that slave says in his heart, 'My lord delays to come,' and begins to strike the servants and the slave women, and to eat and also to drink and get drunk,

[46] the lord of that slave will come on a day when he is not waiting and at an hour which he does not know, and will cut him in two and will set his part with the unfaithful.

[47] And that slave who knew the will of his lord and did not make ready or do according to his will, will be beaten with many blows;

[48] but the one who did not know, and did things worthy of blows, will be beaten with few. And **everyone to whom much was given, much will be sought from him; and to whom they entrusted much, they will ask of him the more.**

[49] "I came to cast fire upon the earth, and how I wish that it were already kindled!

[50] But I have ⟦immersion|an immersion|a baptism⟧ to be ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ with, and how I am constrained until it is finished!

[51] Do you think that I arrived to give peace in the earth? No, I say to you, but rather division.

[52] For from now on there will be five in one house divided, three against two and two against three;

[53] they will be divided, father against son and son against father, mother against daughter and daughter against the mother, mother-in-law against her daughter-in-law and daughter-in-law against the mother-in-law."

[54] And he was saying also to the crowds, "When you see a cloud rising in the west, immediately you say, 'A rainstorm is coming,' and so it happens;

[55] and when a south wind is blowing, you say, 'There will be scorching heat,' and it happens.

[56] Hypocrites! The face of the earth and of the heaven you know how to test, but this time — how do you not know how to test it?

[57] "And why do you not even of yourselves judge what is righteous?

[58] For as you are going with your adversary to a ruler, on the way give effort to be released from him, lest he drag you to the judge, and the judge will deliver you up to the exactor, and the exactor will cast you into prison.

[59] I say to you, you shall by no means come out from there until you have rendered even the last lepton."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv6, 8, 9, 20, 21, 24, 28) — divine name retained.
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (v41) — Aramaic name preserved.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (vv10, 12) — Hebrew divine Spirit.
- **"baptism" → "immersion"** (v50) — metaphorical use; "I have an immersion to be immersed with."
- **"farthings" → "assaria"** (v6) — Greek ἀσσαρίων δύο; the coin is kept, as in Matt 10:29 (a small Roman copper coin, a sixteenth of a denarius).
- **"In the mean time" → "Meanwhile"** (v1) — Greek ἐν οἷς, literally "in which things". "An innumerable multitude of people" → "the myriads of the crowd": Greek τῶν μυριάδων τοῦ ὄχλου, "the ten-thousands of the crowd".
- **"first of all" → "first"** (v1) — Greek πρῶτον may go with what precedes ("to his disciples first") or with what follows ("First, take heed"); the manuscripts have no punctuation.
- **"Beware ye of the leaven of the Pharisees, which is hypocrisy" → "Take heed to yourselves of the leaven — which is hypocrisy — of the Perushim"** (v1) — Greek προσέχετε ἑαυτοῖς ἀπό, the verb of Matt 16:6 with "to yourselves" added. The word order ἥτις ἐστὶν ὑπόκρισις, τῶν Φαρισαίων is that of WH and NA28; Treg and RP read τῶν Φαρισαίων ἥτις ἐστὶν ὑπόκρισις ("of the Pharisees, which is hypocrisy").
- **"covered" → "covered up"** (v2) — Greek συγκεκαλυμμένον, a compound; Matt 10:26 has the simple κεκαλυμμένον.
- **"Therefore" kept** (v3) — Greek ἀνθ' ὧν, literally "in return for which things"; elsewhere in Luke it means "because" (1:20; 19:44; Acts 12:23), and "because" is possible here too.
- **"in closets" → "in the inner rooms"** (v3) — Greek ἐν τοῖς ταμείοις, the word of Matt 6:6; in v24 the same word (ταμεῖον) is "storeroom", its first sense.
- **"Be not afraid of" kept** (v4) — Greek μὴ φοβηθῆτε ἀπό, as Matt 10:28; in v5 and v7 the verb stands without the preposition ("fear").
- **"have no more that they can do" → "have nothing more to do"** (v4) — Greek μὴ ἐχόντων περισσότερόν τι ποιῆσαι.
- **"forewarn" → "show"** (v5) — Greek ὑποδείξω. **"power" → "authority"**: Greek ἐξουσίαν.
- **"not one of them is forgotten" → "one of them is not forgotten"** (v6) — Greek καὶ ἓν ἐξ αὐτῶν οὐκ ἔστιν ἐπιλελησμένον, built as Matt 10:29.
- **"before"** (vv6, 8, 9) — v8 has ἔμπροσθεν twice; v6 and v9 have ἐνώπιον ("in the sight of"). English "before" stands for both.
- **"confess me … confess"** (v8) — Greek ὁμολογήσῃ ἐν ἐμοί … ὁμολογήσει ἐν αὐτῷ, literally "confess in me … in him" (a Semitic turn, as Matt 10:32); "him" renders ἐν αὐτῷ.
- **"he that denieth" → "the one who denied"** (v9) — Greek ὁ ἀρνησάμενος, an aorist participle. "Will be denied" is the compound ἀπαρνηθήσεται; English has one verb for both.
- **"blasphemeth" → "blasphemed"** (v10) — Greek τῷ βλασφημήσαντι, an aorist participle. "Against" is εἰς both times (Matt 12:32 has κατά).
- **"magistrates, and powers" → "the principalities and the authorities"** (v11) — Greek τὰς ἀρχὰς καὶ τὰς ἐξουσίας, the pair of Titus 3:1; here of human rulers.
- **"how or what thing ye shall answer" → "how or with what you should defend yourselves"** (v11) — Greek πῶς ἢ τί ἀπολογήσησθε; ἀπολογέομαι is to speak in one's own defense.
- **"in the same hour" → "in that very hour"** (v12) — Greek ἐν αὐτῇ τῇ ὥρᾳ, as Acts 16:18.
- **"made me a judge or a divider" → "appointed me a judge or divider"** (v14) — Greek κατέστησεν, the verb of vv42, 44; μεριστήν "divider" is from the verb of v13 (μερίσασθαι).
- **"a man's life consisteth not in the abundance of the things which he possesseth" → "not in anyone's abounding is his life out of what belongs to him"** (v15) — Greek οὐκ ἐν τῷ περισσεύειν τινὶ ἡ ζωὴ αὐτοῦ ἐστιν ἐκ τῶν ὑπαρχόντων αὐτῷ, a hard sentence kept in its own order: when someone has more than enough, his life still does not come out of his belongings. "Life" is ζωή, not ψυχή (vv19–23).
- **"ground" → "land"** (v16) — Greek χώρα, elsewhere "country"; here a man's fields. **"brought forth plentifully" → "bore well"**: Greek εὐφόρησεν.
- **"thought" → "reasoned"** (v17) — Greek διελογίζετο. **"bestow my fruits" → "gather my fruits"**: Greek συνάξω τοὺς καρπούς, the same verb as v18.
- **"all my fruits and my goods" → "all the wheat and my good things"** (v18) — Greek πάντα τὸν σῖτον καὶ τὰ ἀγαθά μου (WH, Treg, NA28); RP reads τὰ γενήματά μου ("my produce"). τὰ ἀγαθά, "the good things", is the same word in v19.
- **"take thine ease" → "rest"** (v19) — Greek ἀναπαύου. **"be merry" → "make merry"**: Greek εὐφραίνου.
- **"Thou fool" → "Senseless one"** (v20) — Greek ἄφρων, "without sense"; "fool" is μωρός.
- **"thy soul shall be required of thee" → "they ask back your soul from you"** (v20) — Greek τὴν ψυχήν σου ἀπαιτοῦσιν ἀπὸ σοῦ (NA28, RP; WH and Treg read the simple αἰτοῦσιν, "they ask"): a present tense and an unnamed "they". ψυχή is "soul" throughout vv19–23.
- **"layeth up treasure" → "stores up"** (v21) — Greek θησαυρίζων, as Matt 6:19. "Is rich" is the present participle πλουτῶν.
- **"your life … the body" → "the soul … the body"** (v22) — Greek τῇ ψυχῇ … τῷ σώματι, without "your" (WH adds ὑμῶν after σώματι; RP after ψυχῇ); Matt 6:25 has "your" twice.
- **"Consider" → "Observe"** (vv24, 27) — Greek κατανοήσατε; Matt 6:26, 28 have other verbs. **"storehouse" → "storeroom"** (v24): see v3.
- **"add to his stature one cubit" → "add a cubit to his span of life"** (v25) — Greek ἡλικία is age or stature; πῆχυν stands without "one" (WH, NA28; Treg and RP add ἕνα, as Matt 6:27).
- **"that thing which is least" → "even the least thing"** (v26) — Greek οὐδὲ ἐλάχιστον.
- **"they toil not" → "they do not labor"** (v27) — Greek κοπιᾷ; the verbs are singular in Greek (αὐξάνει, κοπιᾷ, νήθει) with the neuter plural subject. **"was not arrayed" → "clothed himself"**: Greek περιεβάλετο, middle voice.
- **"clothe" → "arrays"** (v28) — Greek ἀμφιέζει, a later form of the verb of Matt 6:30. The second half has no verb: "how much more you".
- **"neither be ye of doubtful mind" → "do not be in suspense"** (v29) — Greek μὴ μετεωρίζεσθε, literally "do not be lifted up in the air": to hang between hope and fear. The verb can also mean "do not be lifted up" in pride.
- **"But rather" → "Yet"** (v31) — Greek πλήν. **"the kingdom of God" → "his kingdom"**: Greek τὴν βασιλείαν αὐτοῦ (WH, Treg, NA28; RP τοῦ θεοῦ); "all" before "these things" is likewise RP only.
- **"it is your Father's good pleasure" → "your Father was pleased"** (v32) — Greek εὐδόκησεν, an aorist verb.
- **"that ye have" → "what belongs to you"** (v33) — Greek τὰ ὑπάρχοντα ὑμῶν, as v15 and v44. **"provide yourselves bags which wax not old" → "make for yourselves purses that do not grow old"**: Greek ποιήσατε ἑαυτοῖς βαλλάντια μὴ παλαιούμενα. **"approacheth" → "draw near"**, **"corrupteth" → "ruin"**: Greek ἐγγίζει, διαφθείρει (Matt 6:19–20 has ἀφανίζει, "destroy").
- **"your lights" → "the lamps"** (v35) — Greek οἱ λύχνοι.
- **"that wait for their lord, when he will return" → "awaiting their own lord, when he departs"** (v36) — Greek πότε ἀναλύσῃ ἐκ τῶν γάμων; ἀναλύω is "break up, depart" (Phil 1:23), here from the wedding, and so homeward. "Be" is supplied; the Greek has no verb.
- **"lord" / "slave"** (vv36–47) — Greek κύριος and δοῦλος. The lord of the parable is written small; "the Lord" of vv41–42 is the same Greek word.
- **"servants" → "slaves"** (vv37, 43, 45–47) — Greek δοῦλος.
- **"watching" → "awake"** (v37) — Greek γρηγοροῦντας. **"Verily" → "Amen"**: Greek ἀμήν. **"make them to sit down to meat" → "make them recline"**: Greek ἀνακλινεῖ. **"will come forth" → "coming up"**: Greek παρελθών, "having come alongside".
- **"blessed are those servants" → "blessed are those"** (v38) — Greek μακάριοί εἰσιν ἐκεῖνοι (WH, NA28); Treg and RP add οἱ δοῦλοι, "slaves". RP also repeats "he comes in the second watch".
- **"he would have watched, and not have suffered" → "he would not have let"** (v39) — Greek οὐκ ἂν ἀφῆκεν (NA28 and the SBLGNT); WH, Treg and RP read ἐγρηγόρησεν ἂν καὶ οὐκ, "he would have been awake and would not", as Matt 24:43. Two editions stand against two, and NA28 decides. **"goodman of the house" → "master of the house"**: Greek οἰκοδεσπότης. **"broken through" → "dug through"**: Greek διορυχθῆναι. γινώσκετε may also be a statement: "this you know".
- **"when ye think not"** (v40) — Greek ᾗ ὥρᾳ οὐ δοκεῖτε, kept as Matt 24:44.
- **"that faithful and wise steward" → "the faithful steward, the sensible one"** (v42) — Greek ὁ πιστὸς οἰκονόμος, ὁ φρόνιμος (WH, Treg, NA28; RP καὶ φρόνιμος, "and sensible"). Matt 24:45 has "slave" (δοῦλος) where Luke has "steward"; in v43 Luke too calls him "that slave".
- **"shall make ruler over his household" → "will appoint over his attendants"** (v42) — Greek καταστήσει ἐπὶ τῆς θεραπείας αὐτοῦ: θεραπεία is "service" and so the body of those who serve (elsewhere "healing"). Matt 24:45 has οἰκετεία, "household slaves", and the past tense.
- **"their portion of meat" → "the measure of grain"** (v42) — Greek τὸ σιτομέτριον, a measured ration of grain. "Them" and "their" are not in the Greek.
- **"Of a truth" → "truly"** (v44) — Greek ἀληθῶς, where Matt 24:47 has ἀμήν. **"make him ruler over all that he hath" → "appoint him over all that belongs to him"**: as Matt 24:47.
- **"delayeth his coming" → "delays to come"** (v45) — Greek χρονίζει … ἔρχεσθαι. **"beat" → "strike"**: Greek τύπτειν; "beat" is δέρω (vv47–48).
- **"the menservants and maidens" → "the servants and the slave women"** (v45) — Greek τοὺς παῖδας καὶ τὰς παιδίσκας. παῖς is "boy" and so a male slave; it is another word than δοῦλος.
- **"looketh not for him" → "is not waiting"** (v46) — Greek οὐ προσδοκᾷ, no object, as Matt 24:50. **"cut him in sunder" → "cut him in two"**: Greek διχοτομήσει. **"appoint him his portion" → "set his part"**: Greek τὸ μέρος αὐτοῦ … θήσει.
- **"unbelievers" → "the unfaithful"** (v46) — Greek τῶν ἀπίστων, the opposite of πιστός "faithful" in v42; the word is also "unbelievers" (1 Cor 6:6), and both senses are possible. Matt 24:51 has "hypocrites".
- **"prepared not himself" → "did not make ready"** (v47) — Greek μὴ ἑτοιμάσας, no object. **"neither did" → "or do"**: Greek ἢ ποιήσας (WH, NA28; Treg and RP μηδέ, "nor").
- **"shall be beaten with many stripes" → "will be beaten with many blows"** (vv47–48) — Greek δαρήσεται πολλάς … ὀλίγας, "many", "few", with πληγάς "blows" understood from ἄξια πληγῶν in v48; "blows" is supplied in v47.
- **"of him shall be much required" → "much will be sought from him"** (v48) — Greek πολὺ ζητηθήσεται παρ' αὐτοῦ; the sentence begins with a dative ("to everyone to whom") and breaks off, and the break is kept. **"to whom men have committed much" → "to whom they entrusted much"**: Greek παρέθεντο, an unnamed "they". **"ask the more"**: Greek περισσότερον αἰτήσουσιν αὐτόν.
- **"I am come to send fire on the earth" → "I came to cast fire upon the earth"** (v49) — Greek ἦλθον βαλεῖν, an aorist; βάλλω is "cast", as Matt 10:34.
- **"what will I, if it be already kindled?" → "how I wish that it were already kindled!"** (v49) — Greek καὶ τί θέλω εἰ ἤδη ἀνήφθη, word for word "and what do I will if it was already kindled". Two readings: (1) an exclamation, τί as "how" and εἰ after a verb of wishing as "that" — the fire is not yet kindled, as the immersion of v50 is not yet finished; (2) a question, "what do I wish, if it is already kindled?" — the fire burns already. The verse gives the first, which runs parallel to v50.
- **"how am I straitened till it be accomplished" → "how I am constrained until it is finished"** (v50) — Greek συνέχομαι, "held together, pressed" (as Acts 18:5); τελεσθῇ, the verb of John 19:30.
- **"Suppose ye that I am come" → "Do you think that I arrived"** (v51) — Greek δοκεῖτε ὅτι … παρεγενόμην, an aorist of παραγίνομαι "arrive", not the ἦλθον of v49. **"on earth" → "in the earth"**: Greek ἐν τῇ γῇ. **"but rather"**: Greek ἀλλ' ἤ, "nothing but". "Division" (διαμερισμόν) stands where Matt 10:34 has "a sword".
- **"The father shall be divided" → "they will be divided, father"** (v53) — Greek διαμερισθήσονται, plural (WH, Treg, NA28; RP singular). "Against" is ἐπί throughout (Matt 10:35 κατά); "the mother" and "the mother-in-law" have the article in Greek, "her" stands only with "daughter-in-law".
- **"he said" → "he was saying"** (v54) — Greek ἔλεγεν, imperfect. **"people" → "crowds"**: Greek τοῖς ὄχλοις. **"rise out of the west" → "rising in the west"**: Greek ἐπὶ δυσμῶν (WH, NA28; Treg and RP ἀπό, "from"). **"a shower" → "a rainstorm"**: Greek ὄμβρος, heavy rain.
- **"ye see the south wind blow" → "a south wind is blowing"** (v55) — the Greek has no second "you see": ὅταν νότον πνέοντα, "when a south wind blowing". **"heat" → "scorching heat"**: Greek καύσων, as Matt 20:12.
- **"discern" → "test"** (v56) — Greek δοκιμάζειν twice, "test, prove"; Matt 16:3 has διακρίνειν "discern". **"sky" → "heaven"**: Greek οὐρανοῦ. **"this time"**: Greek τὸν καιρὸν τοῦτον; the order καιρὸν δέ is that of WH and NA28 (Treg and RP δὲ καιρόν), with no difference in meaning. RP reads "how do you not test" (οὐ δοκιμάζετε).
- **"what is right" → "what is righteous"** (v57) — Greek τὸ δίκαιον.
- **"When thou goest" → "For as you are going"** (v58) — Greek ὡς γὰρ ὑπάγεις. **"magistrate" → "ruler"**: Greek ἄρχοντα. **"give diligence that thou mayest be delivered from him" → "give effort to be released from him"**: Greek δὸς ἐργασίαν (a Latin turn, *da operam*) ἀπηλλάχθαι ἀπ' αὐτοῦ, "to be rid of him" by settling. **"hale" → "drag"**: Greek κατασύρῃ, "drag down".
- **"officer" → "exactor"** (v58) — Greek πράκτωρ, the court officer who collects debts and fines; Matt 5:25 has ὑπηρέτης, "officer". "Will deliver … will cast" are future indicatives in Greek (παραδώσει, βαλεῖ; RP subjunctives).
- **"thou shalt not depart thence, till thou hast paid the very last mite" → "you shall by no means come out from there until you have rendered even the last lepton"** (v59) — Greek οὐ μή, the strong negation; ἀποδῷς "render" what is owed; καὶ τὸ ἔσχατον λεπτόν. Matt 5:26 has the quadrans, worth two lepta.
- **"you" / "your"** (vv58–59) — singular in Greek; vv54–57 are plural.

### Notes
- **v59** — *lepton*, smallest Greek coin.
- **v1–12, 22–34, 39–46, 51–53, 58–59** — sayings that Matthew has in other places (Matt 10:19–20, 26–33; 12:32; 6:19–21, 25–33; 24:43–51; 10:34–36; 5:25–26). Where Luke's Greek is the same, the wording of those verses is used; where it differs, the difference is kept.
