# Matthew 15

[1] Then ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ and scribes approach ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, saying,

[2] "Why do your disciples transgress the tradition of the elders? For they do not wash their hands when they eat bread."

[3] But he answered and said to them, "Why do you also transgress the commandment of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ because of your tradition?

[4] For ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, 'Honor the father and the mother,' and, 'The one who speaks evil of father or mother, let him die the death.'

[5] But you say, 'Whoever says to his father or his mother, "A gift is whatever you might be profited from me,"

[6] shall by no means honor his father.' And you annulled the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ because of your tradition.

[7] Hypocrites, well did ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ prophesy about you, saying:

[8] "'This people honors me with the lips,
but their heart is far away from me;

[9] but in vain do they revere me,
teaching doctrines, precepts of men.'"

[10] And calling the crowd to himself, he said to them, "Hear and understand:

[11] not that which enters into the mouth defiles the man, but that which comes out of the mouth, this defiles the man."

[12] Then, approaching, the disciples say to him, "Do you know that the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧, hearing the word, were made to stumble?"

[13] But he answered and said, "Every planting which my heavenly Father did not plant will be uprooted.

[14] Leave them; they are blind guides of the blind. And if a blind man guides a blind man, both will fall into a pit."

[15] And ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ answered and said to him, "Explain to us this parable."

[16] But he said, "Are you also even yet without understanding?

[17] Do you not perceive that everything entering into the mouth passes into the belly and is cast out into the latrine?

[18] But the things coming out of the mouth come forth out of the heart, and those defile the man.

[19] For out of the heart come forth evil reasonings, murders, adulteries, sexual immoralities, thefts, false testimonies, blasphemies.

[20] These are the things defiling the man; but to eat with unwashed hands does not defile the man."

[21] And going out from there, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ withdrew into the parts of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ and ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧.

[22] And behold, a ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧ woman, coming out from those borders, was crying out, saying, "Have mercy on me, Lord, Son of David; my daughter is badly demonized."

[23] But he did not answer her a word. And approaching, his disciples were requesting him, saying, "Send her away, because she cries out behind us."

[24] But he answered and said, "I was not sent except to the lost sheep of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[25] But she, coming, was bowing down to him, saying, "Lord, help me."

[26] But he answered and said, "It is not good to take the bread of the children and throw it to the little dogs."

[27] But she said, "Yes, Lord; for even the little dogs eat from the crumbs that fall from the table of their lords."

[28] Then ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered and said to her, "O woman, great is your faith; let it be done for you as you want." And her daughter was healed from that hour.

**⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ Heals Many:**

[29] And passing on from there, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ came beside the Sea of ⟦galilee|the Galil|Galilee⟧, and going up into the mountain he was sitting there.

[30] And many crowds approached him, having with them the lame, the blind, the maimed, the mute, and many others, and they cast them down beside his feet, and he healed them;

[31] so that the crowd marveled, seeing the mute speaking, the maimed healthy, and the lame walking and the blind seeing; and they glorified the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[32] Now ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, calling his disciples to himself, said, "I am moved with compassion for the crowd, because already three days they remain with me and have nothing to eat; and I do not want to send them away fasting, lest they faint on the way."

[33] And the disciples say to him, "From where would we have in a desert so many loaves as to fill so great a crowd?"

[34] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to them, "How many loaves do you have?" And they said, "Seven, and a few small fish."

[35] And having charged the crowd to recline on the ground,

[36] he took the seven loaves and the fish, and having given thanks he broke them and was giving them to the disciples, and the disciples to the crowds.

[37] And they all ate and were filled; and what abounded of the fragments they took up, seven large baskets full.

[38] And those who were eating were four thousand men, apart from women and young children.

[39] And having sent the crowds away, he got into the boat and came into the borders of ⟦magadan|Migdal|Magadan⟧.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv3–4, 6, 31) — Greek θεός *theos*; divine name preserved.
- **"came … saying" → "approach … saying"** (v1) — Greek προσέρχονται, a present, kept; in v12 λέγουσιν "say", in vv33–34 λέγουσιν, λέγει.
- **"transgress" kept; "they wash not their hands when they eat bread"** (vv2–3) — Greek παραβαίνουσιν (as παράβασις "transgression"); ἄρτον "bread" is in the Greek. In v3 καὶ ὑμεῖς "you also"; διά "because of".
- **"God commanded, saying" → "Elohim said"** (v4) — Greek ὁ γὰρ θεὸς εἶπεν (WH, Treg, NA28); the Byzantine text has ἐνετείλατο λέγων "commanded, saying". Mark 7:10 has "Moshe said".
- **"Honour thy father and mother" → "Honor the father and the mother"** (v4) — Greek Τίμα τὸν πατέρα καὶ τὴν μητέρα, without "your" (Mark 7:10 has σου twice). The second saying is word for word that of Mark 7:10 and has its wording: Ὁ κακολογῶν "the one who speaks evil of"; θανάτῳ τελευτάτω "let him die the death" (Exod 21:17).
- **"It is a gift, by whatsoever thou mightest be profited by me; and honour not his father or his mother, he shall be free" → "'A gift is whatever you might be profited from me,' shall by no means honor his father"** (vv5–6) — Greek Δῶρον ὃ ἐὰν ἐξ ἐμοῦ ὠφεληθῇς, οὐ μὴ τιμήσει τὸν πατέρα αὐτοῦ: the future with οὐ μή is the conclusion of what "you say". "He shall be free" is not in the Greek; "or his mother" is in Tregelles and the Byzantine text, not in WH and NA28. Matthew has "gift" alone, without Mark's *Qorban*.
- **"made the commandment of God of none effect" → "annulled the word of Elohim"** (v6) — Greek ἠκυρώσατε τὸν λόγον (WH, Treg, NA28; the Byzantine text has τὴν ἐντολήν "the commandment"), an aorist; Mark 7:13 has the participle "annulling".
- **"This people draweth nigh unto me with their mouth, and honoureth me with their lips" → "This people honors me with the lips"** (vv8–9) — Isa 29:13, translated from Matthew's Greek (WH, Treg, NA28), which is word for word that of Mark 7:6–7: πόρρω ἀπέχει "is far away"; σέβονται "revere"; διδασκαλίας ἐντάλματα ἀνθρώπων "doctrines, precepts of men", two nouns side by side. The longer first line is in the Byzantine text.
- **"multitude" → "crowd"** (vv10, 30–39) — Greek ὄχλος.
- **"defileth a man" → "defiles the man"** (vv11, 18, 20) — Greek κοινοῖ τὸν ἄνθρωπον, literally "makes the man common", as Mark 7:15; the article is generic.
- **"were offended, after they heard this saying" → "hearing the word, were made to stumble"** (v12) — Greek τὸν λόγον; ἐσκανδαλίσθησαν.
- **"Every plant" → "Every planting"; "rooted up" → "uprooted"** (v13) — Greek φυτεία, a thing planted; ἐκριζωθήσεται (as 13:29).
- **"Let them alone: they be blind leaders of the blind" → "Leave them; they are blind guides of the blind"** (v14) — Greek ἄφετε αὐτούς; ὁδηγοί "guides". "Of the blind" (Treg, NA28, RP); Westcott-Hort omits it.
- **"Declare unto us this parable" → "Explain to us this parable"** (v15) — Greek Φράσον. "This" (ταύτην: NA28, RP) is not in Westcott-Hort and Tregelles.
- **"Are ye also yet without understanding?" → "even yet"** (v16) — Greek ἀκμήν "even now, still".
- **"goeth into the belly, and is cast out into the draught" → "passes into the belly and is cast out into the latrine"** (v17) — Greek χωρεῖ; ἐκβάλλεται; ἀφεδρῶνα, as Mark 7:19. νοεῖτε "perceive".
- **"proceed out of the mouth come forth from the heart"** (vv11, 18–19) — two Greek verbs: ἐκπορεύομαι "come out" and ἐξέρχομαι "come forth".
- **"evil thoughts … fornications … false witness … blasphemies" → "evil reasonings … sexual immoralities … false testimonies … blasphemies"** (v19) — Greek διαλογισμοὶ πονηροί (Mark 7:21 has κακοί "bad"); all the nouns are plurals.
- **"departed into the coasts" → "withdrew into the parts"** (v21) — Greek ἀνεχώρησεν; τὰ μέρη "the parts"; in v22 ὅρια "borders", and so in v39.
- **"a woman of Canaan … cried unto him" → "a Canaanite woman … was crying out"** (v22) — Greek ἔκραζεν, an imperfect (WH, Treg, NA28). υἱὸς Δαυίδ is a nominative used in address.
- **"grievously vexed with a devil" → "badly demonized"** (v22) — Greek κακῶς δαιμονίζεται; one verb, no "possessed" in the Greek.
- **"besought him" → "were requesting him"** (v23) — Greek ἠρώτουν (ἐρωτάω "request" of asking for something; αἰτέω is "ask"); ὄπισθεν ἡμῶν "behind us".
- **"I am not sent but unto" → "I was not sent except to"** (v24) — Greek οὐκ ἀπεστάλην εἰ μή, an aorist.
- **"worshipped him" → "was bowing down to him"** (v25) — Greek προσεκύνει, an imperfect (WH, Treg, NA28), as 14:33.
- **"It is not meet" → "It is not good"** (v26) — Greek οὐκ ἔστιν καλόν; the sentence is that of Mark 7:27.
- **"dogs" → "little dogs"** (vv26–27) — Greek κυνάρια *kynaria*, a diminutive; the woman's reply uses the same word.
- **"Truth, Lord: yet the dogs eat" → "Yes, Lord; for even the little dogs eat"** (v27) — Greek Ναί, κύριε, καὶ γάρ: she agrees and gives the reason, not an objection. τῶν κυρίων αὐτῶν "of their lords", the plural of the word she has just said to him.
- **"be it unto thee even as thou wilt … made whole" → "let it be done for you as you want … healed"** (v28) — Greek γενηθήτω σοι ὡς θέλεις; ἰάθη (ἰάομαι; in v30 "healed" is θεραπεύω).
- **"departed" → "passing on"** (v29) — Greek μεταβάς, as 11:1; 12:9.
- **"lame, blind, dumb, maimed" → "the lame, the blind, the maimed, the mute"** (v30) — the order of NA28 (χωλούς, τυφλούς, κυλλούς, κωφούς); the editions give four different orders: WH "lame, maimed, blind, mute", Treg and RP "lame, blind, mute, maimed", the printed SBLGNT "mute, blind, lame, maimed". With two against one against one the vote follows NA28. κωφός "mute" here, since they then speak (v31; the word of 11:5 "deaf"); κυλλός "maimed" (as Mark 9:43).
- **"cast them down at Jesus' feet" → "cast them down beside his feet"** (v30) — Greek ἔρριψαν; αὐτοῦ (WH, Treg, NA28).
- **"the multitude wondered … the maimed to be whole" → "the crowd marveled … the maimed healthy"** (v31) — Greek τὸν ὄχλον (WH, NA28; Treg and RP have the plural); κυλλοὺς ὑγιεῖς (Treg, NA28, RP; Westcott-Hort omits the two words).
- **"I have compassion on the multitude, because they continue with me now three days" → "I am moved with compassion for the crowd, because already three days they remain with me"** (v32) — the words of Mark 8:2. νήστεις "fasting"; ἐκλυθῶσιν "faint".
- **"in the wilderness" → "in a desert"** (v33) — Greek ἐν ἐρημίᾳ; χορτάσαι "to fill".
- **"commanded the multitude to sit down" → "having charged the crowd to recline"** (v35) — Greek παραγγείλας (WH, Treg, NA28); ἀναπεσεῖν, as Mark 8:6.
- **"gave thanks, and brake them, and gave" → "having given thanks he broke them and was giving"** (v36) — Greek ἐδίδου, an imperfect (WH, Treg, NA28).
- **"the broken meat that was left seven baskets full" → "what abounded of the fragments … seven large baskets full"** (v37) — Greek σπυρίδας, the large basket, another word than the κόφινος of 14:20.
- **"took ship, and came into the coasts of Magdala" → "got into the boat and came into the borders of Magadan"** (v39) — Greek Μαγαδάν (WH, Treg, NA28); the Byzantine text has Μαγδαλά.
