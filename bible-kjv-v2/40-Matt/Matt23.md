# Matthew 23

[1] Then ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ spoke to the crowds and to his disciples,

[2] saying, "On the seat of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the scribes and the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ sat down.

[3] All things therefore whatever they say to you, do and keep; but do not do according to their works, for they say and do not do.

[4] And they bind loads heavy and hard to bear and lay them upon the shoulders of men, but they themselves do not want to move them with their finger.

[5] And all their works they do to be beheld by men; for they broaden their phylacteries and enlarge the fringes,

[6] and they have affection for the first couch at the suppers and the first seats in the synagogues

[7] and the greetings in the marketplaces and to be called by men, 'Rabbi.'

[8] "But you, do not be called 'Rabbi,' for one is your Teacher, and you all are brothers;

[9] and call no one on the earth your father, for one is your Father, the heavenly one;

[10] nor be called leaders, because your leader is one, the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[11] But the greater of you shall be your servant.

[12] And whoever will lift himself up will be humbled, and whoever will humble himself will be lifted up.

[13] "But woe to you, scribes and ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧, hypocrites, because you shut the kingdom of the heavens before men; for you do not enter, nor do you let those who are entering enter.

[15] "Woe to you, scribes and ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧, hypocrites, because you go about the sea and the dry land to make one proselyte, and when he becomes one, you make him a son of ⟦gehenna|Gehenna|hell⟧ twofold more than yourselves.

[16] "Woe to you, blind guides, who say, 'Whoever swears by the sanctuary, it is nothing; but whoever swears by the gold of the sanctuary, he owes.'

[17] Fools and blind! For which is greater, the gold or the sanctuary that sanctified the gold?

[18] And, 'Whoever swears by the altar, it is nothing; but whoever swears by the gift that is upon it, he owes.'

[19] Blind ones! For which is greater, the gift or the altar that sanctifies the gift?

[20] Therefore the one who swore by the altar swears by it and by all the things upon it;

[21] and the one who swore by the sanctuary swears by it and by the one who dwells in it;

[22] and the one who swore by heaven swears by the throne of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and by the one sitting upon it.

[23] "Woe to you, scribes and ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧, hypocrites, because you tithe the mint and the dill and the cumin, and you left the heavier things of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧: the judgment and the mercy and the faith; but these things it was necessary to do, and those not to leave.

[24] Blind guides, who strain out the gnat but swallow down the camel!

[25] "Woe to you, scribes and ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧, hypocrites, because you cleanse the outside of the cup and of the dish, but inside they are full from seizing and lack of self-control.

[26] Blind ⟦pharisee|Parush|Pharisee⟧, cleanse first the inside of the cup and of the dish, that the outside of it also may become clean.

[27] "Woe to you, scribes and ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧, hypocrites, because you are like whitewashed sepulchres, which outside indeed appear beautiful, but inside are full of bones of the dead and of all uncleanness;

[28] so you also outside indeed appear to men righteous, but inside you are full of hypocrisy and lawlessness.

[29] "Woe to you, scribes and ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧, hypocrites, because you build the sepulchres of the prophets and adorn the tombs of the righteous,

[30] and you say, 'If we had been in the days of our fathers, we would not have been their partners in the blood of the prophets.'

[31] So that you testify against yourselves that you are sons of those who murdered the prophets.

[32] And you, fill the measure of your fathers.

[33] Serpents, offspring of vipers, how are you to flee from the judgment of ⟦gehenna|Gehenna|hell⟧?

[34] Because of this, behold, I send to you prophets and wise men and scribes; some of them you will kill and crucify, and some of them you will scourge in your synagogues and persecute from city to city;

[35] so that upon you may come all righteous blood being poured out upon the earth, from the blood of ⟦abel|Hevel|Abel⟧ the righteous unto the blood of ⟦zechariah-nt|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧ son of ⟦barachiah|Berechyah|Barachiah⟧, whom you murdered between the sanctuary and the altar.

[36] Amen, I say to you, all these things will come upon this generation.

[37] "⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, the one killing the prophets and stoning those sent to her —
how often I wanted to gather your children together,
the way a hen gathers together her chicks under the wings,
and you did not want it!

[38] Behold, your house is left to you desolate.

[39] For I say to you, you shall by no means see me from now on until you say,
'**Blessed is the one coming in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧**.'"

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v1) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v22) — Greek θεός *theos*; "the throne of *Elohim*."
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v10) — Greek ὁ χριστός.
- **"the Lord" → "*Yahweh*"** (v39) — Greek κυρίου without the article, in the words of Ps 118:26.
- **"hell" → "*Gehenna*"** (v15, 33) — Greek γέεννα, the Valley of Hinnom; the word is kept.
- **"sit in Moses' seat" → "On the seat of Moshe … sat down"** (v2) — Greek Ἐπὶ τῆς Μωϋσέως καθέδρας ἐκάθισαν, a past tense: "sat down, took their seat".
- **"whatsoever they bid you observe, that observe and do; but do not ye after their works" → "whatever they say to you, do and keep; but do not do according to their works"** (v3) — Greek ὅσα ἐὰν εἴπωσιν ὑμῖν ποιήσατε καὶ τηρεῖτε (WH Treg NA28); λέγουσιν γὰρ καὶ οὐ ποιοῦσιν: "say" and "do" are the same two verbs throughout the verse.
- **"heavy burdens and grievous to be borne … they themselves will not move them with one of their fingers" → "loads heavy and hard to bear … they themselves do not want to move them with their finger"** (v4) — Greek φορτία (as Gal 6:5); the verse follows βαρέα καὶ δυσβάστακτα (Treg NA28 RP); WH and the SBLGNT have only βαρέα, "heavy". δεσμεύουσιν, "bind, tie up"; τῷ δακτύλῳ αὐτῶν, singular; οὐ θέλουσιν.
- **"to be seen of men: they make broad their phylacteries, and enlarge the borders of their garments" → "to be beheld by men; for they broaden their phylacteries and enlarge the fringes"** (v5) — Greek πρὸς τὸ θεαθῆναι, as 6:1; φυλακτήρια, "safeguards": the *tefillin*, small cases with words of the *Torah* bound on arm and forehead (Deut 6:8); μεγαλύνουσι, "make great" (elsewhere "magnify"); κράσπεδα, the fringes of Num 15:38 (as 9:20; 14:36); "of their garments" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"love the uppermost rooms at feasts, and the chief seats" → "have affection for the first couch at the suppers and the first seats"** (v6) — Greek φιλοῦσι (φιλέω "have affection for", not ἀγαπάω "love"); τὴν πρωτοκλισίαν, singular (Mark 12:39 plural); δείπνοις; πρωτοκαθεδρίας.
- **"to be called of men, Rabbi, Rabbi" → "to be called by men, 'Rabbi'"** (v7) — Greek Ῥαββί once (WH Treg NA28); Hebrew *rabbi*, "my great one, my master".
- **"one is your Master, even Christ" → "one is your Teacher"** (v8) — Greek ὁ διδάσκαλος (WH Treg NA28); "the *Mashiach*" here is RP only.
- **"call no man your father upon the earth: for one is your Father, which is in heaven" → "call no one on the earth your father, for one is your Father, the heavenly one"** (v9) — Greek πατέρα μὴ καλέσητε ὑμῶν ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς, with no word for "man" ("no one" is supplied); ὁ πατὴρ ὁ οὐράνιος (WH Treg NA28).
- **"Neither be ye called masters: for one is your Master" → "nor be called leaders, because your leader is one"** (v10) — Greek καθηγηταί, καθηγητής: "one who leads the way, guide", then "teacher"; only here in the New Testament; a different word from "Teacher" (v8) and from "guides" (ὁδηγοί, v16, 24).
- **"he that is greatest among you" → "the greater of you"** (v11) — Greek ὁ μείζων ὑμῶν, a comparative (as Mark 9:34), in sense "the greatest".
- **"exalt … abased … exalted" → "lift himself up … humbled … lifted up"** (v12) — Greek ὑψώσει … ταπεινωθήσεται … ὑψωθήσεται, as Jas 4:10; both verbs are futures.
- **"ye shut up the kingdom of heaven against men: for ye neither go in yourselves, neither suffer ye them that are entering to go in" → "you shut the kingdom of the heavens before men; for you do not enter, nor do you let those who are entering enter"** (v13) — Greek ἔμπροσθεν τῶν ἀνθρώπων, "in front of men"; τῶν οὐρανῶν, plural; one verb "enter" three times; ἀφίετε, "let". ὅτι after "woe to you" is "because" (v13, 15, 23, 25, 27, 29).
- **"ye compass sea and land to make one proselyte, and when he is made, ye make him twofold more the child of hell than yourselves" → "you go about the sea and the dry land to make one proselyte, and when he becomes one, you make him a son of Gehenna twofold more than yourselves"** (v15) — Greek περιάγετε; τὴν ξηράν, "the dry (land)"; ὅταν γένηται; υἱὸν γεέννης διπλότερον ὑμῶν. A προσήλυτος is one who has "come over" to the people of Israel.
- **"Whosoever shall swear by the temple … he is a debtor" → "Whoever swears by the sanctuary … he owes"** (v16, 18) — Greek ὀμόσῃ ἐν, literally "swear in"; ὀφείλει, "he owes": he is bound by the oath. "Sanctuary" in v16, 17, 21, 35 is ναός, the temple building itself; "temple" is ἱερόν, the whole precinct (21:12; 24:1).
- **"Ye fools and blind … that sanctifieth the gold" → "Fools and blind … that sanctified the gold"** (v17) — Greek μωροὶ καὶ τυφλοί; ὁ ἁγιάσας, a past (WH Treg NA28). In v19 "Fools and" (RP) is not in the critical text; the present "sanctifies" there is τὸ ἁγιάζον.
- **"whoso therefore shall swear … him that dwelleth therein" → "the one who swore … the one who dwells in it"** (v20–22) — Greek ὁ ὀμόσας … ὀμνύει, a past participle with a present; τῷ κατοικοῦντι (WH NA28; Treg RP κατοικήσαντι, "who came to dwell").
- **"ye pay tithe of mint and anise and cummin, and have omitted the weightier matters of the law, judgment, mercy, and faith: these ought ye to have done, and not to leave the other undone" → "you tithe the mint and the dill and the cumin, and you left the heavier things of the Torah: the judgment and the mercy and the faith; but these things it was necessary to do, and those not to leave"** (v23) — Greek ἀφήκατε … ἀφιέναι, one verb, "leave, let go"; τὰ βαρύτερα, of the root of "heavy" (v4); τὴν κρίσιν, "judgment", here doing what is just; τὴν πίστιν, "faith" or "faithfulness"; all three with the article. "But": the verse follows ταῦτα δέ (WH Treg NA28); the SBLGNT and RP have no δέ.
- **"which strain at a gnat, and swallow a camel" → "who strain out the gnat but swallow down the camel"** (v24) — Greek διϋλίζοντες, "filter out" (of wine); καταπίνοντες, "drink down"; both nouns with the article.
- **"ye make clean the outside of the cup and of the platter, but within they are full of extortion and excess" → "you cleanse the outside of the cup and of the dish, but inside they are full from seizing and lack of self-control"** (v25) — Greek καθαρίζετε; γέμουσιν ἐξ, "are full from": what fills them comes from these; ἁρπαγῆς, "seizing, plunder" (Heb 10:34); ἀκρασίας (WH Treg NA28; RP ἀδικίας, "unrighteousness").
- **"cleanse first that which is within the cup and platter, that the outside of them may be clean also" → "cleanse first the inside of the cup and of the dish, that the outside of it also may become clean"** (v26) — Greek καὶ τῆς παροψίδος is in WH Treg RP and the SBLGNT, not in NA28; τὸ ἐκτὸς αὐτοῦ, "of it", singular (WH Treg NA28); γένηται.
- **"whited sepulchres, which indeed appear beautiful outward … dead men's bones" → "whitewashed sepulchres, which outside indeed appear beautiful … bones of the dead"** (v27) — Greek τάφοις κεκονιαμένοις, "coated with lime"; τάφος "sepulchre" (v27, 29) is a different word from μνημεῖον "tomb" (v29). ὀστέων νεκρῶν.
- **"ye also outwardly appear righteous unto men, but within ye are full of hypocrisy and iniquity" → "you also outside indeed appear to men righteous, but inside you are full of hypocrisy and lawlessness"** (v28) — Greek ἔξωθεν … ἔσωθεν, the words of v25, 27; ἀνομίας.
- **"garnish the sepulchres of the righteous" → "adorn the tombs of the righteous"** (v29) — Greek κοσμεῖτε τὰ μνημεῖα.
- **"we would not have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets" → "we would not have been their partners in the blood of the prophets"** (v30) — Greek αὐτῶν κοινωνοί; the Greek has no "shedding".
- **"ye be witnesses unto yourselves, that ye are the children of them which killed the prophets" → "you testify against yourselves that you are sons of those who murdered the prophets"** (v31) — Greek μαρτυρεῖτε ἑαυτοῖς, literally "you testify to yourselves"; υἱοί; φονευσάντων, "murder" (also v35), a different verb from "kill" (v34, 37).
- **"Fill ye up then the measure of your fathers" → "And you, fill the measure of your fathers"** (v32) — Greek καὶ ὑμεῖς πληρώσατε, an imperative.
- **"Ye serpents, ye generation of vipers, how can ye escape the damnation of hell?" → "Serpents, offspring of vipers, how are you to flee from the judgment of Gehenna?"** (v33) — Greek γεννήματα ἐχιδνῶν (as 3:7; 12:34); πῶς φύγητε, a subjunctive; τῆς κρίσεως τῆς γεέννης, "the judgment of Gehenna", a plain genitive.
- **"Wherefore, behold, I send unto you prophets … some of them ye shall kill" → "Because of this, behold, I send to you prophets … some of them you will kill"** (v34) — Greek διὰ τοῦτο ἰδοὺ ἐγὼ ἀποστέλλω, a present; ἐξ αὐτῶν, "of them" ("some" is supplied, twice); μαστιγώσετε; ἀπὸ πόλεως εἰς πόλιν.
- **"all the righteous blood shed upon the earth … between the temple and the altar" → "all righteous blood being poured out upon the earth … between the sanctuary and the altar"** (v35) — Greek πᾶν αἷμα δίκαιον ἐκχυννόμενον, a present participle; ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς, "upon the earth" or "upon the land"; τοῦ ναοῦ. "Zechariah son of Barachiah" is as the Greek has it; the Zechariah killed in the court of the house of Yahweh (2 Chr 24:20–22) is called son of Jehoiada, and the prophet Zechariah son of Berechiah (Zech 1:1).
- **"Verily" → "Amen"** (v36) — Greek ἀμήν; ταῦτα πάντα (WH NA28).
- **"O Jerusalem, Jerusalem, thou that killest the prophets, and stonest them which are sent unto thee" → "Yerushalayim, Yerushalayim, the one killing the prophets and stoning those sent to her"** (v37) — Greek ἡ ἀποκτείνουσα … πρὸς αὐτήν, "to her", in the third person; the Greek has no "O".
- **"how often would I have gathered thy children together, even as a hen gathereth her chickens under her wings, and ye would not!" → "how often I wanted to gather your children together, the way a hen gathers together her chicks under the wings, and you did not want it!"** (v37) — Greek ἠθέλησα … οὐκ ἠθελήσατε, one verb twice, the second without an object ("it" is supplied); ὄρνις, "bird", in use a hen; τὰ νοσσία αὐτῆς; ὑπὸ τὰς πτέρυγας, "under the wings". The printed text ends the sentence with a question mark.
- **"your house is left unto you desolate"** (v38) — kept; Greek ἀφίεται, a present; ἔρημος (Treg NA28 RP; WH does not have it: "your house is left to you").
- **"Ye shall not see me henceforth, till ye shall say, Blessed is he that cometh" → "you shall by no means see me from now on until you say, Blessed is the one coming"** (v39) — Greek οὐ μή, the strong negation; ἀπ' ἄρτι; the words of the crowd in 21:9.
- **"whited" → "whitewashed"** (v27) — Greek κεκονιαμένοις *kekoniaménois* = whitewashed with lime.
- **v14 omitted** — KJV v14 ("Woe to you scribes and Pharisees… devour widows' houses") is not in WH, Treg or NA28; RP has it (before v13); omitted in the translation.

### Notes
- **v14** not in the critical text ("Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you devour widows' houses..." — harmonization from Mark 12:40 / Luke 20:47).
