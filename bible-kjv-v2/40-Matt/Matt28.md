# Matthew 28

[1] Now after the Sabbath, toward the dawn of the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary went to see the tomb.

[2] And behold, there was a great earthquake, for an angel of the Lord descended from heaven and came and rolled back the stone and sat on it.

[3] His appearance was like lightning, and his clothing white as snow.

[4] And for fear of him the guards trembled and became like dead men.

[5] But the angel said to the women, "**Do not be afraid**, for I know that you seek *Yeshua* who was crucified.

[6] He is not here, for he has risen, as he said. Come, see the place where he lay.

[7] Then go quickly and tell his disciples that he has risen from the dead, and behold, he is going before you to Galilee; there you will see him. See, I have told you."

[8] So they departed quickly from the tomb with fear and great joy, and ran to tell his disciples.

[9] And behold, *Yeshua* met them and said, "Greetings!" And they came up and took hold of his feet and worshiped him.

[10] Then *Yeshua* said to them, "Do not be afraid; go and tell my brothers to go to Galilee, and there they will see me."

[11] While they were going, behold, some of the guard went into the city and told the chief priests all that had taken place.

[12] And when they had assembled with the elders and taken counsel, they gave a sufficient sum of money to the soldiers

[13] and said, "Tell people, 'His disciples came by night and stole him away while we were asleep.'

[14] And if this comes to the governor's ears, we will satisfy him and keep you out of trouble."

[15] So they took the money and did as they were directed. And this story has been spread among the Jews to this day.

[16] Now the eleven disciples went to Galilee, to the mountain to which *Yeshua* had directed them.

[17] And when they saw him they worshiped him, but some doubted.

[18] And *Yeshua* came and said to them, "**All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me.**

[19] Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, immersing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the *Ruach HaQodesh*,

**[20] teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you. And behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age.**"

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (vv5, 9–10, 16, 18) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v19) — Greek πνεῦμα ἅγιον *pneuma hagion*; Hebrew divine breath/spirit preserved in full in the Great Commission baptismal formula.
- **"baptizing" → "immersing"** (v19) — Greek βαπτίζοντες *baptizontes* = immersing. The Great Commission uses *baptizo* = dip/immerse; "immersing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the *Ruach HaQodesh*" — rendered accurately.
- **"Mary Magdalene" — retained** (v1) — Established as *Miryam Magdalit* in ch. 27; here retained as "Mary Magdalene" for readability in the resurrection narrative.
- **"end of the world" → "end of the age"** (v20) — Greek τῆς συντελείας τοῦ αἰῶνος *tēs suntelias tou aiōnos* = completion/end of the age. "World" (KJV) imports spatial/physical meaning; "age" is the accurate eschatological term.
- **"alway" → "always"** (v20) — KJV "lo, I am with you alway" — archaic form; modernized.
- **"eleven" — retained** (v16) — Greek ἕνδεκα *hendeka* = eleven; post-Judas betrayal number.
- **Triadic formula** (v19) — "in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the *Ruach HaQodesh*" — this is the baptismal formula; *Ruach HaQodesh* replaces KJV "Holy Ghost."
- **"Go ye therefore" → "Go therefore"** (v19) — Archaic "ye" modernized.

### Notes
- **v19** — triadic baptismal formula; foundational for Trinitarian doctrine.
