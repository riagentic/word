# Matthew 22

[1] And answering, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ again spoke in parables to them, saying,

[2] "The kingdom of the heavens was likened to a man, a king, who made a wedding for his son,

[3] and he sent his slaves to call those who had been called to the wedding, and they did not want to come.

[4] Again he sent other slaves, saying, 'Say to those who have been called: Behold, I have made ready my dinner; my bulls and the fattened animals have been killed, and all things are ready; come to the wedding.'

[5] But they, being neglectful, went away, one to his own field, and another to his trade;

[6] and the rest, taking hold of his slaves, treated them shamefully and killed them.

[7] But the king was wrathful, and sending his armies, he destroyed those murderers and set their city on fire.

[8] Then he says to his slaves, 'The wedding indeed is ready, but those who had been called were not worthy;

[9] go therefore to the outlets of the ways, and as many as you find, call to the wedding.'

[10] And those slaves, going out into the ways, gathered together all whom they found, both evil and good; and the wedding was filled with those reclining.

[11] "But the king, coming in to behold those reclining, saw there a man who had not put on wedding clothing;

[12] and he says to him, 'Companion, how did you come in here not having wedding clothing?' And he was muzzled.

[13] Then the king said to the servants, 'Having bound his feet and hands, cast him out into the outer darkness; there will be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth.'

[14] For many are called, but few elect."

[15] Then the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧, going, took counsel how they might ensnare him in a word.

[16] And they send to him their disciples with the ⟦herodians|Herodianoi|Herodians⟧, saying, "Teacher, we know that you are true and teach the way of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in truth, and it is no concern to you about anyone, for you do not look at the face of men;

[17] tell us therefore what you think: Is it permitted to give the poll tax to Caesar or not?"

[18] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, knowing their evil, said, "Why do you tempt me, hypocrites?

[19] Show me the coin of the poll tax." And they brought to him a denarius.

[20] And he says to them, "Whose is this image and the inscription?"

[21] They say to him, "Caesar's." Then he says to them, "**Render therefore the things of Caesar to Caesar, and the things of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.**"

[22] And hearing, they marveled, and leaving him, they went away.

[23] On that day ⟦sadducees|Tzedukim|Sadducees⟧ approached him, saying there is no resurrection, and they asked him,

[24] saying, "Teacher, ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said, 'If anyone dies not having children, his brother shall marry his wife as brother-in-law and shall raise up seed for his brother.'

[25] Now there were with us seven brothers; and the first, having married, died, and not having seed, he left his wife to his brother;

[26] likewise also the second and the third, unto the seven;

[27] and after all of them the woman died.

[28] In the resurrection therefore, of which of the seven will she be wife? For all had her."

[29] But answering, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "You err, not knowing the scriptures nor the power of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[30] for in the resurrection they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are as angels in heaven;

[31] but concerning the resurrection of the dead, did you not read what was spoken to you by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, saying,

[32] '**I am the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ and the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ and the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧**'? He is not the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of the dead, but of the living."

[33] And hearing, the crowds were astonished at his teaching.

[34] But the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧, hearing that he muzzled the ⟦sadducees|Tzedukim|Sadducees⟧, were gathered together at the same place.

[35] And one of them, a lawyer, asked, tempting him,

[36] "Teacher, which commandment is great in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧?"

[37] And he said to him, "**You shall love ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ with your whole heart and with your whole soul and with your whole mind.**

[38] This is the great and first commandment.

[39] And a second is like it: **You shall love your neighbor as yourself.**

[40] On these two commandments the whole ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ hangs, and the Prophets."

[41] Now the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ having been gathered together, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ asked them,

[42] saying, "What do you think about the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧? Whose son is he?" They say to him, "David's."

[43] He says to them, "How then does David in the Spirit call him Lord, saying,

[44] "'⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The Lord⟧ said to my Lord,
Sit at my right hand
until I put your enemies beneath your feet'?

[45] If then David calls him Lord, how is he his son?"

[46] And no one was able to answer him a word, nor did anyone dare from that day to ask him any longer.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved. The name stands where the Greek has it (v1, 18, 29, 41); in v20 and v37 the critical text has "he" (RP adds the name in v37).
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv16, 21, 29–32, 37) — Greek θεός *theos*; divine name preserved throughout.
- **"the Lord" → "*Yahweh*"** (v37, 44) — Greek κύριος without the article, standing in these quotations for the name YHWH (Deut 6:5; Ps 110:1). "My Lord" in v44 is τῷ κυρίῳ μου, Hebrew *adoni*.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v42) — Greek τοῦ χριστοῦ, with the article.
- **"And" / present tenses kept** (throughout) — the Greek present tenses ("says", v8, 12, 20, 21, 43; "send", v16; "say", v21, 42) are kept as they are.
- **"Jesus answered and spake unto them again by parables" → "answering, Yeshua again spoke in parables to them"** (v1) — Greek ἀποκριθεὶς … εἶπεν ἐν παραβολαῖς.
- **"The kingdom of heaven is like unto a certain king, which made a marriage" → "The kingdom of the heavens was likened to a man, a king, who made a wedding"** (v2) — Greek Ὡμοιώθη, a past passive ("was made like"), as 13:24; 18:23; τῶν οὐρανῶν, plural; ἀνθρώπῳ βασιλεῖ, "a man, a king"; ἐποίησεν γάμους: γάμοι is the wedding with its feast (plural v2, 3, 4, 9; singular v8, 10–12); the Greek has no separate word "feast".
- **"servants" → "slaves"** (v3, 4, 6, 8, 10) — Greek δοῦλοι. In v13 "servants" is διακόνοις, a different word.
- **"to call them that were bidden … they would not come" → "to call those who had been called … they did not want to come"** (v3, 4, 8) — Greek καλέσαι τοὺς κεκλημένους, one verb twice, the verb of "called" in v14; οὐκ ἤθελον, imperfect.
- **"I have prepared my dinner: my oxen and my fatlings are killed" → "I have made ready my dinner; my bulls and the fattened animals have been killed"** (v4) — Greek ἡτοίμακα (WH Treg NA28), ἕτοιμα "ready"; τὸ ἄριστον, the earlier meal of the day (not δεῖπνον, the supper); ταῦροι; τὰ σιτιστά, "the grain-fed ones"; τεθυμένα, from θύω "sacrifice, kill for a meal".
- **"they made light of it, and went their ways, one to his farm, another to his merchandise" → "they, being neglectful, went away, one to his own field, and another to his trade"** (v5) — Greek ἀμελήσαντες, "not caring" (as Heb 2:3), without an object; τὸν ἴδιον ἀγρόν; ἐμπορίαν.
- **"the remnant took his servants, and entreated them spitefully, and slew them" → "the rest, taking hold of his slaves, treated them shamefully and killed them"** (v6) — Greek κρατήσαντες; ὕβρισαν (as 1 Thess 2:2).
- **"when the king heard thereof, he was wroth … burned up their city" → "the king was wrathful … set their city on fire"** (v7) — "heard" (RP) is not in the critical text; ὠργίσθη, of the root of ὀργή "wrath"; στρατεύματα, plural; ἐνέπρησεν.
- **"Go ye therefore into the highways, and as many as ye shall find, bid to the marriage" → "go therefore to the outlets of the ways, and as many as you find, call to the wedding"** (v9) — Greek ἐπὶ τὰς διεξόδους τῶν ὁδῶν: the places where the roads pass out (of the city), or where they cross; καλέσατε.
- **"both bad and good: and the wedding was furnished with guests" → "both evil and good; and the wedding was filled with those reclining"** (v10) — Greek πονηρούς; ἐπλήσθη ὁ γάμος ἀνακειμένων (Treg NA28 RP; WH νυμφών, "the bridal chamber"): "the wedding" here is the place of the feast; ἀνακείμενοι are those reclining at table (also v11).
- **"to see the guests … which had not on a wedding garment" → "to behold those reclining … who had not put on wedding clothing"** (v11) — Greek θεάσασθαι; οὐκ ἐνδεδυμένον ἔνδυμα γάμου, verb and noun of one root; ἔνδυμα is clothing (as 6:25), not ἱμάτιον "garment".
- **"Friend, how camest thou in hither … And he was speechless" → "Companion, how did you come in here … And he was muzzled"** (v12) — Greek Ἑταῖρε, "companion" (as 20:13; 26:50), not φίλος "friend"; ἐφιμώθη, from φιμόω "muzzle" (v34; Mark 1:25; 4:39): he was put to silence.
- **"Bind him hand and foot, and take him away, and cast him into outer darkness; there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth" → "Having bound his feet and hands, cast him out into the outer darkness; there will be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth"** (v13) — Greek Δήσαντες αὐτοῦ πόδας καὶ χεῖρας ἐκβάλετε αὐτόν (WH Treg NA28; "take him away" is RP); ὁ κλαυθμὸς καὶ ὁ βρυγμὸς τῶν ὀδόντων, with the articles.
- **"many are called, but few are chosen" → "many are called, but few elect"** (v14) — Greek κλητοί … ἐκλεκτοί (as 24:22, 24, 31); the Greek has "are" once.
- **"took counsel how they might entangle him in his talk" → "took counsel how they might ensnare him in a word"** (v15) — Greek συμβούλιον ἔλαβον; παγιδεύσωσιν, from παγίς "snare" (Mark 12:13 "catch"); ἐν λόγῳ.
- **"they sent out unto him" → "they send to him"** (v16) — Greek ἀποστέλλουσιν, a present. "Saying": λέγοντες (NA28 RP; WH Treg λέγοντας, which makes the disciples the speakers); the translation is the same.
- **"neither carest thou for any man: for thou regardest not the person of men" → "it is no concern to you about anyone, for you do not look at the face of men"** (v16) — Greek word for word as Mark 12:14: he shows no partiality. "In truth": ἐν ἀληθείᾳ (Mark ἐπ' ἀληθείας).
- **"What thinkest thou? Is it lawful to give tribute unto Caesar" → "what you think: Is it permitted to give the poll tax to Caesar"** (v17) — Greek εἰπὸν … τί σοι δοκεῖ, literally "tell us what seems to you"; ἔξεστιν; κῆνσον, the Latin word *census*: the tax paid per head to Rome (also v19).
- **"perceived their wickedness … Why tempt ye me, ye hypocrites?" → "knowing their evil … Why do you tempt me, hypocrites?"** (v18) — Greek γνούς (Mark 12:15 εἰδώς); πονηρίαν (Mark "hypocrisy"); πειράζετε, one root with "trial"; also v35.
- **"Shew me the tribute money. And they brought unto him a penny" → "Show me the coin of the poll tax. And they brought to him a denarius"** (v19) — Greek τὸ νόμισμα τοῦ κήνσου; δηνάριον, the Roman silver coin.
- **"Whose is this image and superscription?" → "Whose is this image and the inscription?"** (v20) — Greek as Mark 12:16.
- **"Render therefore unto Caesar the things which are Caesar's; and unto God the things that are God's" → "Render therefore the things of Caesar to Caesar, and the things of Elohim to Elohim"** (v21) — Greek Ἀπόδοτε οὖν τὰ Καίσαρος Καίσαρι καὶ τὰ τοῦ θεοῦ τῷ θεῷ, in this order (Mark 12:17 puts "the things of Caesar" first); ἀποδίδωμι is "give back, pay what is owed", as Rom 13:7.
- **"which say that there is no resurrection" → "saying there is no resurrection"** (v23) — Greek λέγοντες without the article (WH Treg NA28): they came saying it; RP οἱ λέγοντες, "who say" (so Mark 12:18).
- **"his brother shall marry his wife" → "his brother shall marry his wife as brother-in-law"** (v24) — Greek ἐπιγαμβρεύσει, "marry as the next of kin by marriage" (the word of Gen 38:8 in the Septuagint; Deut 25:5); a future, as in a law. "Seed" is σπέρμα (v24, 25). "Children": τέκνα.
- **"the first, when he had married a wife, deceased, and, having no issue, left his wife unto his brother" → "the first, having married, died, and not having seed, he left his wife to his brother"** (v25) — Greek γήμας ἐτελεύτησεν: τελευτάω, "come to one's end", a different verb from "die" (ἀποθνῄσκω) in v24, 27. "With us": παρ' ἡμῖν.
- **"unto the seventh" → "unto the seven"** (v26) — Greek ἕως τῶν ἑπτά.
- **"last of all the woman died also" → "after all of them the woman died"** (v27) — Greek ὕστερον δὲ πάντων; "also" (Treg RP) is not in WH NA28.
- **"whose wife shall she be of the seven? for they all had her"** (v28) — Greek τίνος τῶν ἑπτὰ ἔσται γυνή; πάντες γὰρ ἔσχον αὐτήν.
- **"Ye do err" → "You err"** (v29) — Greek πλανᾶσθε, "you wander, go astray", as Mark 12:24; the verb is "deceive" in the active (24:4).
- **"as the angels of God in heaven" → "as angels in heaven"** (v30) — the verse follows WH Treg NA28, which have no "of God"; the SBLGNT prints θεοῦ, RP τοῦ θεοῦ. ἐν τῷ οὐρανῷ is singular here (Mark 12:25 "in the heavens").
- **"have ye not read that which was spoken unto you by God" → "did you not read what was spoken to you by Elohim"** (v31) — Greek οὐκ ἀνέγνωτε τὸ ῥηθὲν ὑμῖν ὑπὸ τοῦ θεοῦ.
- **"God is not the God of the dead, but of the living" → "He is not the Elohim of the dead, but of the living"** (v32) — Greek οὐκ ἔστιν ὁ θεὸς νεκρῶν ἀλλὰ ζώντων (WH Treg NA28): it can be read "He is not the God of the dead" or "God is not (God) of the dead"; RP has "God" twice. The quotation (Exod 3:6) has "I am" in Matthew (Ἐγώ εἰμι).
- **"when the multitude heard this" → "hearing, the crowds"** (v33) — Greek ἀκούσαντες οἱ ὄχλοι, plural, without an object; ἐξεπλήσσοντο, imperfect; διδαχῇ "teaching".
- **"had put the Sadducees to silence, they were gathered together" → "muzzled the Sadducees, were gathered together at the same place"** (v34) — Greek ἐφίμωσεν, the verb of v12; συνήχθησαν ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό, "to the same (place)", the wording of Ps 2:2 in the Septuagint (Acts 4:26).
- **"asked him a question, tempting him, and saying" → "asked, tempting him"** (v35) — Greek ἐπηρώτησεν … πειράζων αὐτόν; "and saying" (RP) is not in the critical text. νομικός is a man learned in the law (νόμος), here the *Torah*.
- **"which is the great commandment in the law?" → "which commandment is great in the Torah?"** (v36) — Greek ποία ἐντολὴ μεγάλη, without the article: "which (kind of) commandment is great", in sense "the greatest".
- **"with all thy heart" → "with your whole heart"** (v37) — Greek ἐν ὅλῃ τῇ καρδίᾳ σου, literally "in"; so three times (Mark 12:30 ἐξ, "from", four times: Matthew has no "strength").
- **"This is the first and great commandment" → "This is the great and first commandment"** (v38) — Greek ἡ μεγάλη καὶ πρώτη (WH Treg NA28).
- **"On these two commandments hang all the law and the prophets" → "On these two commandments the whole Torah hangs, and the Prophets"** (v40) — Greek ὅλος ὁ νόμος κρέμαται καὶ οἱ προφῆται (WH Treg NA28): the verb is singular and stands before "and the Prophets".
- **"While the Pharisees were gathered together" → "the Pharisees having been gathered together"** (v41) — Greek Συνηγμένων, a perfect participle, the verb of v34.
- **"The son of David" → "David's"** (v42) — Greek Τοῦ Δαυίδ, "of David", without the word "son".
- **"How then doth David in spirit call him Lord" → "How then does David in the Spirit call him Lord"** (v43) — Greek ἐν πνεύματι, without the article: "in Spirit" or "in spirit" (Mark 12:36 "in the *Ruach HaQodesh*").
- **"till I make thine enemies thy footstool" → "until I put your enemies beneath your feet"** (v44) — Greek ὑποκάτω τῶν ποδῶν σου (WH Treg NA28), as Mark 12:36; RP ὑποπόδιον, "a footstool", the wording of Ps 110:1 in the Septuagint.
- **"neither durst any man from that day forth ask him any more questions" → "nor did anyone dare from that day to ask him any longer"** (v46) — Greek οὐδὲ ἐτόλμησέν τις … ἐπερωτῆσαι αὐτὸν οὐκέτι.

### Notes
- **v32** quotes Exod 3:6.
- **v37** quotes Deut 6:5.
- **v39** quotes Lev 19:18.
- **v44** quotes Ps 110:1 (most-cited OT text in NT).
