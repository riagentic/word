# Matthew 27

[1] When morning came, all the chief priests and the elders of the people took counsel against ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ to put him to death.

[2] And they bound him and led him away and delivered him over to Pontius ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ the governor.

[3] Then when ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧, his betrayer, saw that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was condemned, he changed his mind and brought back the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and the elders,

[4] saying, "I have sinned by betraying innocent blood." They said, "What is that to us? See to it yourself."

[5] And throwing down the pieces of silver into the temple, he departed, and he went and hanged himself.

[6] But the chief priests, taking the pieces of silver, said, "It is not lawful to put them into the treasury, since it is blood money."

[7] So they took counsel and bought with them the potter's field as a burial place for strangers.

[8] Therefore that field has been called the Field of Blood to this day.

[9] Then was fulfilled what had been spoken by the prophet ⟦jeremiah-nt|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧, saying, "And they took the thirty pieces of silver, the price of him on whom a price had been set by some of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[10] and they gave them for the potter's field, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ directed me."

[11] Now ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ stood before the governor, and the governor asked him, "Are you the King of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧?" ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said, "You have said so."

[12] But when he was accused by the chief priests and elders, he gave no answer.

[13] Then ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ said to him, "Do you not hear how many things they testify against you?"

[14] But he gave him no answer, not even to a single charge, so that the governor was greatly amazed.

[15] Now at the feast the governor was accustomed to release for the crowd any one prisoner whom they wanted.

[16] And they had then a notorious prisoner called ⟦barabbas|Bar-Abba|Barabbas⟧.

[17] So when they had gathered, ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ said to them, "Whom do you want me to release for you: ⟦barabbas|Bar-Abba|Barabbas⟧, or ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ who is called ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧?"

[18] For he knew that it was out of envy that they had delivered him up.

[19] Besides, while he was sitting on the judgment seat, his wife sent word to him, "Have nothing to do with that righteous man, for I have suffered much because of him today in a dream."

[20] Now the chief priests and the elders persuaded the crowd to ask for ⟦barabbas|Bar-Abba|Barabbas⟧ and destroy ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[21] The governor again said to them, "Which of the two do you want me to release for you?" And they said, "⟦barabbas|Bar-Abba|Barabbas⟧."

[22] ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ said to them, "Then what shall I do with ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ who is called ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧?" They all said, "Let him be crucified!"

[24] So when ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ saw that he was gaining nothing, but rather that a riot was beginning, he took water and washed his hands before the crowd, saying, "I am innocent of this man's blood; see to it yourselves."

[26] Then he released for them ⟦barabbas|Bar-Abba|Barabbas⟧, and having scourged ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, delivered him to be crucified.

[27] Then the soldiers of the governor took ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ into the praetorium, and they gathered the whole battalion before him.

[28] And they stripped him and put a scarlet robe on him,

[29] and twisting together a crown of thorns, they put it on his head and put a reed in his right hand. And kneeling before him, they mocked him, saying, "Hail, King of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧!"

[30] And they spit on him and took the reed and struck him on the head.

[31] And when they had mocked him, they stripped him of the robe and put his own clothes on him and led him away to crucify him.

[32] As they went out, they found a man of ⟦cyrene|Kyrene|Cyrene⟧, ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ by name. They compelled this man to carry his cross.

[36] Then they sat down and kept watch over him there.

[37] And over his head they put the charge against him, which read, "**This is ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, the King of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧**."

[40] and saying, "You who would destroy the temple and rebuild it in three days, save yourself! If you are the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, come down from the cross."

[41] So also the chief priests, with the scribes and elders, mocked him, saying,

[42] "He saved others; he cannot save himself. He is the King of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; let him come down now from the cross, and we will believe in him.

[61] ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ Magdalene and the other ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ were there, sitting opposite the tomb.

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v56) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"Christ" / "*Mashiach*"** (vv17, 22) — Greek *Christos*; "*Yeshua* who is called *Mashiach*."
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv40, 43, 54) — Greek θεός *theos*; divine name preserved.
- **Cry of dereliction** (v46) — "*Eli, Eli, lema sabachtani*" — preserved as Aramaic (as Matthew gives it). This is not translated further in the text but explained in parentheses following the Greek text's own gloss.
- **OT echoes** — v35 echoes Ps 22:18; v46 quotes Ps 22:1 (Aramaic form); v9–10 combines Zech 11:12–13 + Jer. All rendered following Matthew's Greek text.

### Notes
- **v46** quotes Ps 22:1 in Aramaic.
- **v35** echoes Ps 22:18.
- **v9-10** — ascribed to Jeremiah; combines Zech 11:12-13 with Jer 19 and 32.
