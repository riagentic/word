# Matthew 11

[1] And it came to pass, when ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ finished giving orders to his twelve disciples, that he passed on from there to teach and to proclaim in their cities.

[2] Now ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, having heard in the jail of the works of the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧, sent through his disciples

[3] and said to him, "Are you the coming one, or are we to expect another?"

[4] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered and said to them, "Go and report to ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ what you hear and see:

[5] the blind receive sight and the lame walk, lepers are cleansed and the deaf hear, and the dead are raised and the poor have the gospel preached to them;

[6] and blessed is whoever is not made to stumble in me."

[7] Now as these were going, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ began to say to the crowds concerning ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, "What did you go out into the wilderness to behold? A reed shaken by wind?

[8] But what did you go out to see? A man clothed in soft things? Behold, those who wear the soft things are in the houses of kings.

[9] But what did you go out to see? A prophet? Yes, I say to you, and more than a prophet.

[10] This is he concerning whom it is written,

"'Behold, I send my messenger before your face,
who will prepare your way before you.'

[11] Amen, I say to you, there has not arisen among those born of women one greater than ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ the ⟦immerser|Immerser|Baptist⟧; but the lesser in the kingdom of the heavens is greater than he.

[12] And from the days of ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ the ⟦immerser|Immerser|Baptist⟧ until now the kingdom of the heavens suffers violence, and violent men snatch it.

[13] For all the prophets and the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ prophesied until ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧;

[14] and if you are willing to accept, he himself is ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧, the one about to come.

[15] He who has ears, let him hear.

[16] "But to what shall I liken this generation? It is like young children sitting in the marketplaces, who call to the others

[17] and say, 'We played the flute for you, and you did not dance;
we lamented, and you did not wail.'

[18] For ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ came neither eating nor drinking, and they say, 'He has a demon.'

[19] The Son of Man came eating and drinking, and they say, 'Behold, a man who is a glutton and a wine drinker, a friend of toll collectors and sinners.' And wisdom was justified by her works."

[20] Then he began to reproach the cities in which most of his powers had taken place, because they did not repent:

[21] "Woe to you, ⟦chorazin|Korazin|Chorazin⟧! Woe to you, ⟦bethsaida|Beit Tzaidah|Bethsaida⟧! For if the powers that took place in you had taken place in ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ and ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧, long ago they would have repented in sackcloth and ashes.

[22] Nevertheless I say to you, for ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ and ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧ it will be more bearable in the day of judgment than for you.

[23] And you, ⟦capernaum|Kfar Nachum|Capernaum⟧, will you be lifted up as far as heaven? As far as Hades you will go down; for if the powers that took place in you had taken place in ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧, it would have remained until this day.

[24] Nevertheless I say to you that for the land of ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ it will be more bearable in the day of judgment than for you."

[25] At that time ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered and said, "I confess to you, Father, Lord of heaven and of earth, that you hid these things from the wise and understanding and revealed them to infants;

[26] yes, Father, because thus it became good pleasure before you.

[27] All things were delivered to me by my Father, and no one fully knows the Son except the Father, nor does anyone fully know the Father except the Son and he to whom the Son wishes to reveal him.

[28] "**Come to me, all who labor and are burdened, and I will give you rest.**

[29] Take my yoke upon you and learn from me, because I am meek and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls;

[30] for my yoke is kind and my load is light."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (vv1, 4, 7, 25) — Hebrew name preserved throughout.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v2) — Greek *Christos* = *Mashiach*; John hears of the works of the *Mashiach*.
- **"had made an end of commanding" → "finished giving orders to"** (v1) — Greek διατάσσων *diatassōn*, elsewhere "ordain" (Gal 3:19; 1 Cor 16:1); "ordaining for his disciples" would be read in English as appointing, so the plain sense is given. Καὶ ἐγένετο "And it came to pass" is kept; κηρύσσειν "to proclaim".
- **"in the prison" → "in the jail"** (v2) — Greek δεσμωτήριον *desmōtērion*, the place of bonds; "prison" is φυλακή (14:3, 10).
- **"sent two of his disciples" → "sent through his disciples"** (v2) — Greek διὰ τῶν μαθητῶν (WH, Treg, NA28); the Byzantine text reads δύο "two".
- **"he that should come … look for another" → "the coming one … expect another"** (v3) — Greek ὁ ἐρχόμενος, a present participle; ἕτερον "another, a different one"; προσδοκῶμεν can be "are we to expect" or "do we expect".
- **"the blind receive their sight"** (v5) — Greek ἀναβλέπουσιν *anablepousin*, "look up", of the blind "receive sight"; εὐαγγελίζονται is passive, "have the gospel preached to them".
- **"shall not be offended in me" → "is not made to stumble in me"** (v6) — Greek σκανδαλισθῇ ἐν ἐμοί, as Mark 6:3.
- **"to see" → "to behold" / "to see"** (v7, 8–9) — Greek θεάσασθαι (v7), then ἰδεῖν (v8, 9): two verbs.
- **"soft raiment" → "soft things"** (v8) — Greek ἐν μαλακοῖς … τὰ μαλακά, an adjective without a noun (WH, Treg, NA28; the Byzantine text adds ἱματίοις "garments").
- **"But what went ye out for to see? A prophet?" kept** (v9) — Greek ἀλλὰ τί ἐξήλθατε ἰδεῖν; προφήτην; (Treg, NA28, RP). Westcott-Hort and the SBLGNT order the words ἐξήλθατε; προφήτην ἰδεῖν; "But why did you go out? To see a prophet?"
- **"there hath not risen a greater" → "there has not arisen … one greater"** (v11) — Greek οὐκ ἐγήγερται, a perfect: "has not been raised, has not arisen".
- **"he that is least" → "the lesser"** (v11) — Greek ὁ μικρότερος *ho mikroteros*, a comparative: "the lesser, the smaller one"; it is often taken as a superlative, "the least".
- **"kingdom of heaven" → "kingdom of the heavens"** (vv11–12) — Greek ἡ βασιλεία τῶν οὐρανῶν, a plural with the article, as Hebrew *malkhut shamayim*; in v23, 25 "heaven" is singular in the Greek.
- **"John the Baptist" → "John the Immerser"** (vv11–12) — Greek Ἰωάννης ὁ βαπτιστής *Iōannēs ho baptistēs*; *baptistēs* = one who immerses; "Baptist" is a denominational term with modern baggage.
- **"suffereth violence, and the violent take it by force" → "suffers violence, and violent men snatch it"** (v12) — Greek βιάζεται *biazetai* is a present and can be passive ("suffers violence, is forced") or middle ("forces its way"); the verse keeps the passive, the other sense is open. βιασταί "violent men"; ἁρπάζουσιν "snatch, seize" (as John 10:12, 28).
- **"if ye will receive it" → "if you are willing to accept"** (v14) — Greek εἰ θέλετε δέξασθαι has no object ("it" or "him"); αὐτός "he himself"; ὁ μέλλων ἔρχεσθαι "the one about to come".
- **"ears to hear" → "ears"** (v15) — Greek ὁ ἔχων ὦτα ἀκουέτω (WH, NA28); Tregelles and the Byzantine text add ἀκούειν "to hear", as Mark 4:9 has it.
- **"unto their fellows" → "to the others"** (v16) — Greek τοῖς ἑτέροις (WH, Treg, NA28); the Byzantine text reads τοῖς ἑταίροις αὐτῶν "to their companions".
- **"mourned … lamented" → "lamented … wail"** (v17) — Greek ἐθρηνήσαμεν "we lamented, sang a dirge" (as John 16:20); οὐκ ἐκόψασθε, literally "you did not beat yourselves", the beating of the breast in mourning (as Rev 1:7). ηὐλήσαμεν "we played the flute" (1 Cor 14:7).
- **"winebibber" → "wine drinker"** (v19) — Greek οἰνοπότης *oinopotēs*, "wine drinker"; "drunkard" is another word (μέθυσος, 1 Cor 5:11).
- **"publicans" → "toll collectors"** (v19) — Greek τελῶναι *telōnai*, the men at the toll booth (τελώνιον, 9:9).
- **"wisdom is justified of her children" → "wisdom was justified by her works"** (v19) — Greek ἐδικαιώθη, an aorist, "was justified"; ἀπὸ τῶν ἔργων αὐτῆς, literally "from her works" (WH, Treg, NA28); the Byzantine text reads τέκνων "children", as Luke 7:35.
- **"upbraid" → "reproach"** (v20) — Greek ὀνειδίζειν *oneidizein*.
- **"mighty works" → "powers"** (vv20, 21, 23) — Greek δυνάμεις *dynameis*, "powers", of deeds of power (as Mark 6:2, 14).
- **"But" → "Nevertheless"; "more tolerable" → "more bearable"** (vv22, 24) — Greek πλήν; ἀνεκτότερον is the same word in both verses.
- **"which art exalted unto heaven" → "will you be lifted up as far as heaven?"** (v23) — Greek μὴ ἕως οὐρανοῦ ὑψωθήσῃ; a question that expects "no" (WH, Treg, NA28); the Byzantine text reads ἡ … ὑψωθεῖσα "who were exalted". καταβήσῃ "you will go down" (WH, Treg, NA28; the Byzantine text has καταβιβασθήσῃ "you will be brought down").
- **"hell" → "Hades"** (v23) — Greek ᾅδης *Hadēs*, the realm of the dead; the line echoes Isa 14:13–15, where the Septuagint's *Hadēs* renders Hebrew שְׁאוֹל *Sheol*. The verse gives the Greek word. "It would have remained" is ἔμεινεν, the verb μένω ("abide" of persons), here of a city: "remain".
- **"I thank thee" → "I confess to you"** (v25) — Greek ἐξομολογοῦμαί σοι *exomologoumai soi*, "I confess to you, I acknowledge you openly": the Septuagint's word for giving thanks and praise to God (Ps 136:1). ἀποκριθεὶς εἶπεν "answered and said".
- **"prudent" → "understanding"; "babes" → "infants"** (v25) — Greek συνετῶν *synetōn*, those who understand; νηπίοις *nēpiois*, infants. ἔκρυψας, ἀπεκάλυψας are aorists: "you hid", "you revealed".
- **"so it seemed good in thy sight" → "thus it became good pleasure before you"** (v26) — Greek οὕτως εὐδοκία ἐγένετο ἔμπροσθέν σου, word for word; ὁ πατήρ is a nominative used in address.
- **"knoweth" → "fully knows"** (v27) — Greek ἐπιγινώσκει *epiginōskei*, the compound verb of ἐπίγνωσις "full knowledge" (1 Cor 13:12); Luke 10:22 has the simple γινώσκει. In 14:35 the same verb is "recognize". "Reveal him": the Greek ἀποκαλύψαι has no object.
- **"will give you rest" kept** (v28) — Greek ἀναπαύσω ὑμᾶς, "I will cause you to rest"; elsewhere the active is "refresh" (Phlm 7), but here ἀνάπαυσις "rest" follows in v29 and the one root is kept as one word. πεφορτισμένοι "burdened" (φορτίζω, "load" at Luke 11:46) is of one root with φορτίον "load" (v30; "burden" is βάρος).
- **"meek and lowly in heart" → "meek and humble in heart"** (v29) — Greek πραΰς … ταπεινὸς τῇ καρδίᾳ; ταπεινός is "low, lowly, humble". "You will find rest for your souls" is Jer 6:16.
- **"easy" → "kind"** (v30) — Greek χρηστός *chrēstos*, "good, kind, pleasant to use"; the word of 1 Pet 2:3 ("the Lord is kind"). Of a yoke: one that does not chafe.
