# Matthew 3

[1] Now in those days ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ the ⟦immerser|Immerser|Baptist⟧ arrives, proclaiming in the wilderness of ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧

[2] and saying, "Repent, for the kingdom of the heavens has drawn near."

[3] For this is the one spoken of through ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ the prophet, saying,

"A voice of one crying in the wilderness:
'Make ready the way of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧,
make his paths straight.'"

[4] Now ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ himself had his clothing from camel's hair and a leather belt around his loins, and his food was locusts and wild honey.

[5] Then there was going out to him ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and all ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ and all the surrounding region of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧,

[6] and they were being ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ in the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ river by him, confessing their sins.

[7] But seeing many of the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ and ⟦sadducees|Tzedukim|Sadducees⟧ coming to his ⟦immersion|immersion|baptism⟧, he said to them, "Offspring of vipers, who showed you to flee from the wrath to come?

[8] Make therefore fruit worthy of repentance,

[9] and do not think to say within yourselves, 'We have ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ as father'; for I say to you that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is able out of these stones to raise children to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧.

[13] Then ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ arrives from ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧ at the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ to ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, to be ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ by him.

## Changes

- **"John the Baptist" → "John the Immerser"** — "Immerser" renders *baptistēs* (one who immerses).
- **"came John the Baptist, preaching … And saying" → "arrives, proclaiming … and saying"** (v1–2) — Greek παραγίνεται, a present tense in narrative (so also v13; ἀφίησιν, v15); κηρύσσων, "proclaiming as a herald". "and" (καί) Treg NA28 RP; WH omits it.
- **"the kingdom of heaven is at hand" → "the kingdom of the heavens has drawn near"** (v2) — Greek ἡ βασιλεία τῶν οὐρανῶν, plural with the article, Matthew's own phrase (Mark 1:15: "the kingdom of *Elohim*"); ἤγγικεν, a perfect.
- **"he that was spoken of by the prophet Esaias" → "the one spoken of through Isaiah the prophet"** (v3) — Greek διά, "through" (RP ὑπό).
- **"The voice … Prepare ye" → "A voice … Make ready"** (v3) — Greek φωνὴ βοῶντος, without the article; the words are those of Mark 1:3 and are rendered as there. κυρίου stands for the Name in Isa 40:3.
- **"the same John had his raiment of camel's hair, and a leathern girdle" → "John himself had his clothing from camel's hair and a leather belt"** (v4) — Greek αὐτὸς δὲ ὁ Ἰωάννης εἶχεν τὸ ἔνδυμα αὐτοῦ ἀπὸ τριχῶν καμήλου; Mark 1:6 says it with other words ("was clothed with").
- **"went out … were baptized" → "was going out … were being immersed"** (v5–6) — Greek ἐξεπορεύετο, ἐβαπτίζοντο, imperfects. περίχωρος, "surrounding region" (as Mark 1:28).
- **"O generation of vipers, who hath warned you" → "Offspring of vipers, who showed you"** (v7) — Greek γεννήματα ἐχιδνῶν, "things begotten of vipers"; the Greek has no "you" before it. ὑπέδειξεν, "showed, pointed out". "his" immersion: αὐτοῦ Treg NA28 RP; WH omits it.
- **"the wrath to come" kept** (v7) — Greek τῆς μελλούσης ὀργῆς, "the wrath that is about to be".
- **"Bring forth therefore fruits meet for repentance" → "Make therefore fruit worthy of repentance"** (v8, 10) — Greek ποιήσατε καρπὸν ἄξιον τῆς μετανοίας: ποιέω, "make"; καρπόν, singular.
- **"think not to say within yourselves" kept; "We have Abraham to our father" → "We have Abraham as father"** (v9) — Greek μὴ δόξητε λέγειν ἐν ἑαυτοῖς; πατέρα ἔχομεν τὸν Ἀβραάμ.
- **"to raise up children unto Abraham" → "to raise children to Abraham"** (v9) — Greek ἐγεῖραι, "raise"; τῷ Ἀβραάμ, a dative.
- **"now also the axe is laid unto the root" → "already the axe lies at the root"; "is hewn down, and cast into the fire" → "is cut down and cast into fire"** (v10) — Greek ἤδη … κεῖται, "lies"; ἐκκόπτεται, βάλλεται, present tenses; εἰς πῦρ, without the article.
- **"with water" → "in water"; "with the Holy Ghost, and with fire" → "in *Ruach HaQodesh* and fire"** (v11) — Greek ἐν ὕδατι; ἐν πνεύματι ἁγίῳ καὶ πυρί, one "in" for both and no article. Mark 1:8 has the bare dative ὕδατι, "with water". "and fire" WH Treg NA28; RP omits it.
- **"mightier" → "stronger"; "worthy" → "fit"; "shoes … to bear" → "sandals … to carry"** (v11) — Greek ἰσχυρότερος; ἱκανός (not ἄξιος, "worthy", which stands in v8); τὰ ὑποδήματα βαστάσαι. Mark 1:7 has "to stoop down and loose the strap of his sandals".
- **"Whose fan is in his hand, and he will throughly purge his floor … garner" → "whose winnowing fork is in his hand, and he will thoroughly cleanse his threshing floor … barn"** (v12) — Greek οὗ τὸ πτύον, a relative clause that continues v11; διακαθαριεῖ, from καθαρίζω "cleanse". Treg reads "his barn".
- **"cometh" → "arrives"** (v13) — Greek παραγίνεται, as v1.
- **"John forbad him" → "John was hindering him"** (v14) — Greek διεκώλυεν, an imperfect: he was trying to hinder him. WH omits the name.
- **"Suffer it to be so now … Then he suffered him" → "Let it be now … Then he lets him"** (v15) — Greek ἄφες ἄρτι … τότε ἀφίησιν αὐτόν: one verb, ἀφίημι, "let"; the first has no object in Greek ("it be" is supplied), the second is a present tense. πρέπον ἐστίν, "it is fitting"; πληρῶσαι, "fulfill" (as 1:22; 5:17).
- **"went up straightway out of the water" → "immediately came up from the water"** (v16) — Greek εὐθὺς ἀνέβη ἀπὸ τοῦ ὕδατος (Mark 1:10: "coming up out of", ἐκ).
- **"the heavens were opened unto him" kept** (v16) — editions: ἠνεῴχθησαν WH (and the printed SBLGNT), "were opened" ] + αὐτῷ, "to him", Treg NA28 RP — the verse follows the majority. Mark 1:10 has "being torn".
- **"the Spirit of God descending like a dove, and lighting upon him" → "the Spirit of *Elohim* coming down like a dove and coming upon him"** (v16) — editions: πνεῦμα θεοῦ, "a Spirit of God", without articles, WH (and the printed SBLGNT) ] τὸ πνεῦμα τοῦ θεοῦ Treg NA28 RP; περιστερὰν ἐρχόμενον WH (and the printed SBLGNT) ] + καί, "and coming", Treg NA28 RP — the verse follows the majority both times. ὡσεί, "like, as if"; Mark 1:10 has ὡς, "as", and εἰς αὐτόν, "unto him".
- **"a voice from heaven, saying, This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased" → "a voice out of the heavens, saying, This is my Son, the beloved, in whom I have taken pleasure"** (v17) — Greek ἐκ τῶν οὐρανῶν, plural; ὁ υἱός μου ὁ ἀγαπητός; εὐδόκησα, a past tense. The Greek has no verb with "a voice". Mark 1:11 has "You are … in you".

### Notes
- **Matt 3:3** quotes Isa 40:3.
- **v17** — echoes Ps 2:7 + Isa 42:1 (royal Son + Servant).
