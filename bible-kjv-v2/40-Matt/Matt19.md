# Matthew 19

[1] And it came to pass, when ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ finished these words, he moved away from ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧ and came into the borders of ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ beyond the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧.

[2] And many crowds followed him, and he healed them there.

[3] And ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ approached him, tempting him and saying, "Is it permitted for a man to send away his wife for every cause?"

[4] But he, answering, said, "Did you not read that the one who created from the beginning made them male and female,

[5] and said, '**On account of this a man will leave father and mother and will be joined to his wife, and the two will be one flesh**'?

[6] So that they are no longer two, but one flesh. What therefore ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ yoked together, let man not separate."

[7] They say to him, "Why then did ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ give commandment to give a document of divorce and to send her away?"

[8] He says to them, "⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, with regard to your hardness of heart, permitted you to send away your wives; but from the beginning it has not been so.

[9] And I say to you that whoever sends away his wife, not for sexual immorality, and marries another commits adultery."

[10] His disciples say to him, "If the case of the man with the wife is so, it is not profitable to marry."

[11] But he said to them, "Not all make room for this word, but those to whom it has been given.

[12] For there are eunuchs who were born so from a mother's womb, and there are eunuchs who were made eunuchs by men, and there are eunuchs who made themselves eunuchs because of the kingdom of the heavens. The one able to make room, let him make room."

[13] Then young children were brought to him, that he might lay his hands on them and pray; but the disciples rebuked them.

[14] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said, "Let the young children be, and do not hinder them from coming to me, for of such is the kingdom of the heavens."

[15] And laying his hands on them, he went from there.

[16] And behold, one, approaching him, said, "Teacher, what good thing shall I do that I may have eternal life?"

[17] But he said to him, "Why do you ask me about the good? One is the good one. But if you want to enter into life, keep the commandments."

[18] He says to him, "Which?" And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said, "You shall not murder, You shall not commit adultery, You shall not steal, You shall not testify falsely,

[19] Honor father and mother, and, You shall love your neighbor as yourself."

[20] The young man says to him, "All these things I guarded; in what do I still come short?"

[21] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to him, "If you want to be perfect, go, sell what belongs to you and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in the heavens; and come, follow me."

[22] But the young man, hearing the word, went away grieved, for he was one having many possessions.

[23] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to his disciples, "Amen, I say to you that a rich man will with difficulty enter into the kingdom of the heavens.

[24] And again I say to you, it is easier for a camel to enter through a needle's eye than a rich man into the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[25] And hearing it, the disciples were exceedingly astonished, saying, "Who then is able to be saved?"

[26] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, looking at them, said to them, "With men this is impossible, but with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ all things are possible."

[27] Then ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, answering, said to him, "Behold, we left all things and followed you; what then will there be for us?"

[28] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "Amen, I say to you that you who followed me, in the regeneration, when the Son of Man sits upon the throne of his glory, you also will sit upon twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[29] And everyone who left houses or brothers or sisters or father or mother or children or fields for the sake of my name will receive a hundredfold and will inherit eternal life.

[30] But many first will be last, and last first.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv6, 24, 26) — Greek θεός *theos*; divine name preserved.
- **"had finished these sayings, he departed" → "finished these words, he moved away"** (v1) — Greek Καὶ ἐγένετο ὅτε ἐτέλεσεν … τοὺς λόγους τούτους, Matthew's formula at the end of each discourse; μετῆρεν, "moved away" (13:53).
- **"coasts" → "borders"** (v1) — Greek τὰ ὅρια, as Mark 10:1. Mark has "and beyond the *Yarden*"; Matthew has no "and".
- **"great multitudes" → "many crowds"** (v2) — Greek ὄχλοι πολλοί.
- **"Is it lawful … to put away" → "Is it permitted … to send away"** (vv3, 7, 8, 9) — Greek ἔξεστιν; ἀπολῦσαι, "release, send away", the ordinary word for dismissing a wife; "divorce" names the legal act, which the verb itself does not. "For a man" (ἀνθρώπῳ): Tregelles, NA28, the Byzantine text; not in Westcott-Hort.
- **"for every cause" kept** (v3) — Greek κατὰ πᾶσαν αἰτίαν, "according to every cause", that is, for any cause whatever. Only Matthew has these words.
- **"tempting" kept** (v3) — Greek πειράζοντες, "tempt" and "test" in one word.
- **"he which made them at the beginning" → "the one who created from the beginning"** (v4) — Greek ὁ κτίσας (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28; the Byzantine text has ποιήσας, "made"). "From the beginning" can go with "created" or with "made them".
- **"For this cause … shall cleave … shall be one flesh" → "On account of this … will be joined … will be one flesh"** (v5) — Gen 2:24, translated from Matthew's Greek: τὸν πατέρα καὶ τὴν μητέρα without "his"; κολληθήσεται (Mark 10:7 has the compound προσκολληθήσεται); ἔσονται … εἰς σάρκα μίαν, word for word "will be into one flesh", the Hebrew way of saying "become".
- **"Wherefore they are no more twain" → "So that they are no longer two"** (v6) — Greek ὥστε οὐκέτι εἰσὶν δύο.
- **"hath joined together" → "yoked together"** (v6) — Greek συνέζευξεν, aorist; "put under one yoke".
- **"put asunder" → "separate"** (v6) — Greek χωριζέτω.
- **"say … saith" → "say … says"** (vv7, 8, 10, 18, 20) — Greek presents; kept.
- **"command" → "give commandment"; "a writing of divorcement" → "a document of divorce"** (v7) — Greek ἐνετείλατο; βιβλίον ἀποστασίου (Deut 24:1), as Mark 10:4. "Her" (αὐτήν): NA28, the Byzantine text; not in Westcott-Hort and Tregelles.
- **"because of the hardness of your hearts suffered you" → "with regard to your hardness of heart, permitted you"** (v8) — Greek πρὸς τὴν σκληροκαρδίαν ὑμῶν ἐπέτρεψεν. In Mark 10:3–4 *Yeshua* says "commanded" and they say "permitted"; in Matthew it is the other way.
- **"it was not so" → "it has not been so"** (v8) — Greek οὐ γέγονεν, a perfect.
- **"except it be for fornication" → "not for sexual immorality"** (v9) — Greek μὴ ἐπὶ πορνείᾳ, "not on the ground of πορνεία"; πορνεία is any unlawful sexual union. The words are not in Mark 10:11.
- **"and whoso marrieth her which is put away doth commit adultery" omitted** (v9) — the verse ends at "commits adultery" (Westcott-Hort, NA28); Tregelles, the Byzantine text and the printed SBLGNT add καὶ ὁ ἀπολελυμένην γαμήσας μοιχᾶται, "and the one who marries a woman sent away commits adultery".
- **"His disciples" kept** (v10) — the verse reads + αὐτοῦ (Tregelles, NA28, the Byzantine text); Westcott-Hort and the printed SBLGNT have "The disciples".
- **"If the case of the man be so with his wife, it is not good to marry" → "If the case of the man with the wife is so, it is not profitable to marry"** (v10) — Greek ἡ αἰτία, the same word as "cause" in v3; οὐ συμφέρει.
- **"All men cannot receive this saying" → "Not all make room for this word"** (vv11–12) — Greek χωροῦσι, "have room for, hold", as 2 Cor 7:2; τὸν λόγον, "word". "This" (τοῦτον): Tregelles, NA28, the Byzantine text; not in Westcott-Hort. In v12 the Greek has no object: "let him make room".
- **"which were so born from their mother's womb" → "who were born so from a mother's womb"** (v12) — Greek ἐκ κοιλίας μητρὸς ἐγεννήθησαν οὕτως.
- **"for the kingdom of heaven's sake" → "because of the kingdom of the heavens"** (v12) — Greek διὰ τὴν βασιλείαν τῶν οὐρανῶν.
- **"eunuchs" — retained** (v12) — Greek εὐνοῦχοι, εὐνουχίσθησαν, εὐνούχισαν: noun and verb of one root, five times.
- **"little children" → "young children"** (vv13–14) — Greek παιδία. Mark 10:13 has "that he might touch them".
- **"Suffer little children, and forbid them not, to come unto me" → "Let the young children be, and do not hinder them from coming to me"** (v14) — Greek Ἄφετε τὰ παιδία καὶ μὴ κωλύετε αὐτὰ ἐλθεῖν πρός με; "to come to me" can also depend on both verbs: "Let the young children come to me, and do not hinder them".
- **"departed thence" → "went from there"** (v15) — Greek ἐπορεύθη ἐκεῖθεν.
- **"Good Master, what good thing shall I do" → "Teacher, what good thing shall I do"** (v16) — the critical text has no "Good" with "Teacher" (the Byzantine text has it, from Mark 10:17).
- **"Why callest thou me good? there is none good but one, that is, God" → "Why do you ask me about the good? One is the good one"** (v17) — Greek Τί με ἐρωτᾷς περὶ τοῦ ἀγαθοῦ; εἷς ἐστιν ὁ ἀγαθός (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28). KJV follows the Byzantine text, which has the wording of Mark 10:18.
- **"if thou wilt enter" → "if you want to enter"** (vv17, 21) — Greek θέλεις.
- **"Thou shalt do no murder … Thou shalt not bear false witness" → "You shall not murder … You shall not testify falsely"** (v18) — Greek Οὐ with the future, as in the Septuagint (Exod 20:13–16); Mark 10:19 has Μή with the subjunctive, "Do not …", and adds "Do not deprive".
- **"Honour thy father and thy mother" → "Honor father and mother"** (v19) — Greek τὸν πατέρα καὶ τὴν μητέρα, without "your". Only Matthew adds Lev 19:18 here.
- **"have I kept from my youth up: what lack I yet?" → "I guarded; in what do I still come short?"** (v20) — Greek ἐφύλαξα; τί ἔτι ὑστερῶ. "From my youth" is in the Byzantine text only (from Mark 10:20).
- **"that thou hast" → "what belongs to you"** (v21) — Greek τὰ ὑπάρχοντα; "possessions" in v22 is κτήματα.
- **"in heaven" → "in the heavens"** (v21) — Greek ἐν οὐρανοῖς, plural (the Byzantine text and Mark 10:21 have the singular).
- **"that saying … sorrowful … great possessions" → "the word … grieved … many possessions"** (v22) — Greek τὸν λόγον; λυπούμενος; κτήματα πολλά, as Mark 10:22. Westcott-Hort add "this".
- **"Verily" → "Amen"** (vv23, 28) — Greek ἀμήν, left untranslated.
- **"shall hardly enter" → "will with difficulty enter"** (v23) — Greek δυσκόλως.
- **"to go through the eye of a needle, than for a rich man to enter into" → "to enter through a needle's eye than a rich man into"** (v24) — the verse follows εἰσελθεῖν, "enter", once (Westcott-Hort; with Tregelles for the first verb); NA28 and the Byzantine text read διελθεῖν, "go through", and repeat "to enter" with the rich man, as Mark 10:25. Tregelles reads "the kingdom of the heavens". "Eye" is τρύπημα, a bored hole (Mark: τρυμαλιά); the Greek has no articles.
- **"exceedingly amazed" → "exceedingly astonished"** (v25) — Greek ἐξεπλήσσοντο σφόδρα.
- **"beheld them" → "looking at them"** (v26) — Greek ἐμβλέψας.
- **"we have forsaken all … what shall we have therefore?" → "we left all things … what then will there be for us?"** (v27) — Greek ἀφήκαμεν, ἠκολουθήσαμεν, aorists; τί ἄρα ἔσται ἡμῖν. The question is in Matthew only.
- **"in the regeneration"** (v28) — Greek ἐν τῇ παλιγγενεσίᾳ, "birth again, becoming again", as Titus 3:5; here of the world made new. The words can go with "followed me" or with "will sit"; the verse leaves them where the Greek has them.
- **"shall sit in the throne of his glory" → "sits upon the throne of his glory"** (v28) — Greek ἐπὶ θρόνου δόξης αὐτοῦ.
- **"or wife" omitted** (v29) — the verse follows Westcott-Hort, Tregelles and NA28; the Byzantine text and the printed SBLGNT add ἢ γυναῖκα.
- **"lands" → "fields"** (v29) — Greek ἀγρούς, as Mark 10:29.
- **"for my name's sake" kept** (v29) — Greek ἕνεκεν τοῦ ὀνόματός μου; Mark 10:29 has "for the sake of me and for the sake of the gospel".
- **"an hundredfold" kept** (v29) — Greek ἑκατονταπλασίονα (NA28, the Byzantine text); Westcott-Hort and Tregelles read πολλαπλασίονα, "many times as much".
- **"many that are first shall be last; and the last shall be first" → "many first will be last, and last first"** (v30) — Greek πολλοὶ δὲ ἔσονται πρῶτοι ἔσχατοι καὶ ἔσχατοι πρῶτοι; no article with the second "last".

### Notes
- **v5** quotes Gen 2:24; **v4** Gen 1:27; **vv18–19** Exod 20:12–16; Lev 19:18.
- **vv10–12** — only in Matthew.
