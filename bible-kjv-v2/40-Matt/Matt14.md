# Matthew 14

[1] At that time ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ the tetrarch heard the report of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧,

[2] and he said to his servants, "This is ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ the ⟦immerser|Immerser|Baptist⟧; he himself was raised from the dead, and because of this the powers work in him."

[3] For ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧, having taken hold of ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, bound him and put him away in prison because of Herodias, the wife of ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧ his brother;

[4] for ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ was saying to him, "It is not permitted for you to have her."

[5] And wanting to kill him, he feared the crowd, because they held him as a prophet.

[6] But when ⟦herod|Herodes's|Herod's⟧ birthday came, the daughter of Herodias danced in the midst and pleased ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧,

[7] for which reason he promised with an oath to give her whatever she should ask.

[8] And she, prompted by her mother, says, "Give me here on a platter the head of ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ the ⟦immerser|Immerser|Baptist⟧."

[9] And the king, though grieved, because of the oaths and those reclining with him commanded it to be given;

[10] and sending, he beheaded ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ in the prison;

[11] and his head was brought on a platter and was given to the girl, and she brought it to her mother.

[12] And approaching, his disciples took up the corpse and buried him; and coming, they reported to ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[13] Now ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, hearing it, withdrew from there in a boat to a desert place privately; and the crowds, hearing it, followed him on foot from the cities.

[14] And coming out he saw a great crowd, and he was moved with compassion for them and healed their infirm.

[15] Now when evening came, the disciples approached him, saying, "The place is desert, and the hour has already passed; send the crowds away, that going away into the villages they may buy food for themselves."

[16] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "They have no need to go away; you give them to eat."

[17] But they say to him, "We have nothing here except five loaves and two fish."

[18] But he said, "Bring them here to me."

[19] And having commanded the crowds to recline on the grass, taking the five loaves and the two fish, looking up into heaven he blessed, and breaking them he gave the loaves to the disciples, and the disciples to the crowds.

[20] And they all ate and were filled; and they took up what abounded of the fragments, twelve baskets full.

[21] And those who were eating were about five thousand men, apart from women and young children.

[22] And immediately he compelled the disciples to get into a boat and to go before him to the other side, until he should send the crowds away.

[23] And having sent the crowds away, he went up into the mountain privately to pray. And when evening came, he was there alone.

[24] But the boat was already many stadia distant from the land, tormented by the waves, for the wind was against it.

[25] And in the fourth watch of the night he came to them, walking over the sea.

[26] But the disciples, seeing him walking on the sea, were troubled, saying, "It is a phantom!" And from fear they cried out.

[27] But immediately ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ spoke to them, saying, "Be of good courage; **it is I; do not fear**."

[28] And ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ answered him and said, "Lord, if it is you, command me to come to you over the waters."

[29] And he said, "Come." And going down from the boat, ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ walked over the waters and came to ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[30] But seeing the strong wind, he feared, and beginning to sink he cried out, saying, "Lord, save me!"

[31] And immediately ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, stretching out his hand, laid hold of him, and he says to him, "You of little faith, why did you doubt?"

[32] And when they went up into the boat, the wind ceased.

[33] And those in the boat bowed down to him, saying, "Truly you are ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ Son."

[34] And having crossed over, they came upon the land, to ⟦gennesaret|Ginosar|Gennesaret⟧.

[35] And recognizing him, the men of that place sent into all that surrounding region, and they brought to him all those who were ill,

[36] and they were appealing to him that they might only touch the fringe of his garment; and as many as touched it were saved through.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v33) — Greek θεός *theos*.
- **"John the Baptist" → "John the Immerser"** (vv2, 8) — Greek *baptistēs* = one who immerses; same correction as ch. 11.
- **"Herodias" — name retained** (v3) — Greek Ἡρῳδιάς *Hērōidias*; name preserved as-is (proper name, no Hebrew equivalent needed).
- **"the fame of Jesus" → "the report of Yeshua"** (v1) — Greek τὴν ἀκοὴν Ἰησοῦ, "the hearing, the report".
- **"he is risen from the dead; and therefore mighty works do shew forth themselves in him" → "he himself was raised from the dead, and because of this the powers work in him"** (v2) — Greek ἠγέρθη, an aorist passive; αἱ δυνάμεις ἐνεργοῦσιν, as Mark 6:14. "Servants" is παισίν (παῖς, also "boy"), not "slaves".
- **"had laid hold on John … put him in prison" → "having taken hold of … put him away in prison"** (v3) — Greek κρατήσας; ἀπέθετο "put away" (WH, NA28).
- **"It is not lawful" → "It is not permitted"** (v4) — Greek Οὐκ ἔξεστιν, as 12:2. The words are those of Mark 6:18, where the finished Mark has "lawful"; "permitted" keeps the one word of Matthew 12 and Mark 2–3.
- **"the multitude" → "the crowd"; "counted him" → "held him"** (v5) — Greek τὸν ὄχλον; εἶχον "had, held".
- **"danced before them" → "danced in the midst"** (v6) — Greek ἐν τῷ μέσῳ.
- **"promised with an oath" kept** (v7) — Greek ὡμολόγησεν *hōmologēsen*, elsewhere "confess": here "declare one's agreement, promise". ὅθεν "from which, for which reason".
- **"being before instructed of her mother" → "prompted by her mother"** (v8) — Greek προβιβασθεῖσα "put forward, urged on". φησίν "says" is a present, kept. πίναξ "platter" (KJV "charger").
- **"was sorry … them which sat with him at meat" → "though grieved … those reclining with him"** (v9) — Greek λυπηθείς (WH, Treg, NA28); συνανακειμένους.
- **"took up the body, and buried it" → "took up the corpse and buried him"** (v12) — Greek πτῶμα "corpse" (WH, Treg, NA28; the Byzantine text has σῶμα "body"), as Mark 6:29; αὐτόν "him" (WH, Treg, NA28; the Byzantine text has αὐτό "it").
- **"departed … into a desert place apart" → "withdrew … to a desert place privately"** (v13) — Greek ἀνεχώρησεν; εἰς ἔρημον τόπον κατʼ ἰδίαν, as Mark 6:32. "It" after "hearing" is supplied twice; the Greek has no object.
- **"multitudes" → "crowds"** (v13, 15, 19, 22–23) — Greek ὄχλους *ochlous*.
- **"went forth … was moved with compassion toward them … their sick" → "coming out … moved with compassion for them … their infirm"** (v14) — Greek ἐξελθών; ἐσπλαγχνίσθη (root σπλάγχνα "inward parts"), as Mark 6:34; ἀρρώστους "infirm".
- **"the time is now past" → "the hour has already passed"** (v15) — Greek ἡ ὥρα ἤδη παρῆλθεν. βρώματα "food" is a plural, "foods".
- **"to sit down on the grass" → "to recline on the grass"** (v19) — Greek ἀνακλιθῆναι. εὐλόγησεν "he blessed" has no object; κλάσας "breaking".
- **"were filled … the fragments that remained" → "were filled … what abounded of the fragments"** (v20) — Greek ἐχορτάσθησαν; τὸ περισσεῦον τῶν κλασμάτων; κοφίνους "baskets".
- **"constrained his disciples to get into a ship" → "compelled the disciples to get into a boat"** (v22) — Greek ἠνάγκασεν; εἰς πλοῖον without the article (WH, Treg; NA28 and the Byzantine text have τὸ πλοῖον "the boat"). ἕως οὗ ἀπολύσῃ "until he should send away".
- **"the ship was now in the midst of the sea, tossed with waves" → "the boat was already many stadia distant from the land, tormented by the waves"** (v24) — Greek σταδίους πολλοὺς ἀπὸ τῆς γῆς ἀπεῖχεν (WH, Treg, NA28); the Byzantine text has "in the midst of the sea". A stadion is about 185 meters. βασανιζόμενον "tormented", as Mark 6:48. "It" after "against" is supplied: ἦν γὰρ ἐναντίος ὁ ἄνεμος.
- **"fourth watch of the night"** — retained. Roman military time: 3–6 a.m.
- **"walking on the sea" → "walking over the sea" / "on the sea"** (vv25–26) — Greek ἐπὶ τὴν θάλασσαν (accusative, v25), ἐπὶ τῆς θαλάσσης (genitive, v26); in vv28–29 ἐπὶ τὰ ὕδατα "over the waters", a plural.
- **"It is a spirit" → "It is a phantom"** (v26) — Greek Φάντασμα, as Mark 6:49. ἐταράχθησαν "were troubled".
- **"Be of good cheer; it is I; be not afraid" → "Be of good courage; it is I; do not fear"** (v27) — Greek Θαρσεῖτε, ἐγώ εἰμι· μὴ φοβεῖσθε, the words of Mark 6:50.
- **"boisterous" → "strong"** (v30) — Greek ἰσχυρόν (Treg, NA28, RP); Westcott-Hort omits the word. καταποντίζεσθαι "to be sunk in the sea".
- **"caught him … O thou of little faith, wherefore didst thou doubt?" → "laid hold of him … You of little faith, why did you doubt?"** (v31) — Greek ἐπελάβετο; Ὀλιγόπιστε, one word; ἐδίστασας (διστάζω, "be of two minds"; the verb of 28:17, not διακρίνομαι). λέγει "says" is a present.
- **"worshipped him" → "bowed down to him"** (v33) — Greek προσεκύνησαν *prosekynēsan*, the bodily act of bowing down; "worship" is another verb (λατρεύω).
- **"thou art the Son of God" → "you are Elohim's Son"** (v33) — Greek θεοῦ υἱὸς εἶ, without articles: "God's Son" or "a son of God".
- **"into the land of Gennesaret" → "upon the land, to Gennesaret"** (v34) — Greek ἐπὶ τὴν γῆν εἰς Γεννησαρέτ (WH, Treg, NA28), as Mark 6:53.
- **"had knowledge of him … all that were diseased" → "recognizing him … all those who were ill"** (v35) — Greek ἐπιγνόντες; τοὺς κακῶς ἔχοντας, literally "those having it badly".
- **"besought him" → "were appealing to him"** (v36) — Greek παρεκάλουν, an imperfect, as Mark 6:56.
- **"hem of his garment" → "fringe of his garment"** (v36) — Greek κρασπέδου *kraspedou* = fringe/tassel (*tzitzit*); same correction as ch. 9.
- **"were made perfectly whole" → "were saved through"** (v36) — Greek διεσώθησαν *diesōthēsan*, the compound of σῴζω "save": "saved through, brought safely through" (as 1 Pet 3:20; Acts 28:1); Mark 6:56 has the simple verb, "were being saved". "It" after "touched" is supplied.

### Notes
- **v27** "It is I" — Greek *egō eimi*, "I am"; here the words identify the speaker. They are also the words of Exod 3:14 in the Septuagint.
