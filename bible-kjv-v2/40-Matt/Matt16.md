# Matthew 16

[1] And the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ and ⟦sadducees|Tzedukim|Sadducees⟧, approaching, tempting him, asked him to show them a sign out of heaven.

[2] But he, answering, said to them, "When evening has come, you say, 'Fair weather, for the heaven is red.'

[3] And early, 'Today a storm, for the heaven is red, being gloomy.' The face of the heaven you know how to discern, but the signs of the times you cannot.

[4] An evil and adulterous generation seeks after a sign, and a sign will not be given to it except the sign of ⟦jonah|Yonah|Jonah⟧." And leaving them behind, he went away.

[5] And the disciples, coming to the other side, forgot to take loaves.

[6] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "See, and take heed of the leaven of the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ and ⟦sadducees|Tzedukim|Sadducees⟧."

[7] But they were reasoning among themselves, saying, "We took no loaves."

[8] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, knowing it, said, "Why do you reason among yourselves, you of little faith, that you have no loaves?

[9] Do you not yet perceive, nor remember the five loaves of the five thousand, and how many baskets you took?

[10] Nor the seven loaves of the four thousand, and how many large baskets you took?

[11] How do you not perceive that I did not speak to you about loaves? But take heed of the leaven of the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ and ⟦sadducees|Tzedukim|Sadducees⟧."

[12] Then they understood that he did not say to take heed of the leaven of the loaves, but of the teaching of the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ and ⟦sadducees|Tzedukim|Sadducees⟧.

[13] Now ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, coming into the parts of Caesarea Philippi, was asking his disciples, saying, "Who do men say that the Son of Man is?"

[14] And they said, "Some, ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ the ⟦immerser|Immerser|Baptist⟧; but others, ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧; and different ones, ⟦jeremiah-nt|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ or one of the prophets."

[15] He says to them, "But you, who do you say that I am?"

[16] And ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, answering, said, "**You are the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧, the Son of the living ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.**"

[17] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, answering, said to him, "Blessed are you, ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ ⟦jonah|Bar-Yonah|Bar-Jonah⟧, because flesh and blood did not reveal it to you, but my Father who is in the heavens.

[18] And I also say to you that you are ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, and upon this rock I will build my church, and the gates of Hades will not prevail against it.

[19] I will give you the keys of the kingdom of the heavens, and whatever you bind upon the earth will have been bound in the heavens, and whatever you loose upon the earth will have been loosed in the heavens."

[20] Then he enjoined the disciples that they should tell no one that he is the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[21] From then ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ began to show his disciples that he must go away to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and suffer many things from the elders and high priests and scribes, and be killed, and on the third day be raised.

[22] And ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, taking him to himself, began to rebuke him, saying, "Far be it from you, Lord! This will by no means be to you."

[23] But he, turning, said to ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, "Go behind me, Satan! You are a stumbling block to me, because you do not think the things of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ but the things of men."

[24] Then ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to his disciples, "If anyone wants to come after me, let him deny himself and take up his cross and follow me.

[25] For whoever wants to save his soul will lose it; but whoever loses his soul for my sake will find it.

[26] For what will a man be profited if he gains the whole world but suffers the loss of his soul? Or what will a man give as an exchange for his soul?

[27] For the Son of Man is about to come in the glory of his Father with his angels, and then he will repay each one according to his practice.

[28] Amen, I say to you that there are some of those standing here who will by no means taste death until they see the Son of Man coming in his kingdom."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (vv16, 20) — Greek *Christos* = *Mashiach*; "You are the *Mashiach*."
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv16, 23) — Greek θεός *theos*; divine name preserved.
- **"tempting" kept; "from heaven" → "out of heaven"** (v1) — Greek πειράζοντες; πειράζω is "tempt" and "test" in one word. Greek ἐκ τοῦ οὐρανοῦ; Mark 8:11 has ἀπό, "from".
- **"the sky" → "the heaven"** (vv2–3) — Greek ὁ οὐρανός, the same word as "heaven" in v1: they ask for a sign out of heaven and are told that they read the face of the heaven. The Greek has no verb in "Fair weather" and "Today a storm".
- **vv2b–3** ("When evening has come … you cannot") — printed by Tregelles, NA28 and the Byzantine text; Westcott-Hort put the words in double brackets as a later addition. The verse follows the printed text.
- **"lowring" → "being gloomy"** (v3) — Greek στυγνάζων, the verb of Mark 10:22.
- **"in the morning" → "early"** (v3) — Greek πρωΐ, as in Mark.
- **"discern the face of the sky" → "the face of the heaven you know how to discern"** (v3) — Greek πρόσωπον, "face"; διακρίνειν, "discern".
- **"seeketh after" kept** (v4) — Greek ἐπιζητεῖ, the compound; Mark 8:12 has the simple ζητεῖ, "seek".
- **"left" → "leaving behind"** (v4) — Greek καταλιπών; Mark 8:13 has ἀφείς, "leaving".
- **"bread" → "loaves"** (vv5–12) — Greek ἄρτους, plural, the word of the two feedings (vv9–10).
- **"Take heed and beware of" → "See, and take heed of"** (vv6, 11–12) — Greek Ὁρᾶτε καὶ προσέχετε ἀπό; προσέχω is "give heed"; with ἀπό, "be on one's guard against". Mark 8:15 has a different verb (βλέπετε ἀπό, "beware of").
- **"It is because we have taken no bread" → "We took no loaves"** (v7) — Greek ὅτι Ἄρτους οὐκ ἐλάβομεν. ὅτι can introduce the words spoken (so here) or mean "because": "It is because we took no loaves."
- **"ye have brought no bread" → "you have no loaves"** (v8) — the verse reads ἔχετε, "you have" (Westcott-Hort, NA28); Tregelles, the Byzantine text and the printed SBLGNT read ἐλάβετε, "you took".
- **"O ye of little faith" → "you of little faith"** (v8) — Greek ὀλιγόπιστοι, one word; no "O".
- **"understand" → "perceive"; "understood" kept** (vv9, 11, 12) — Greek νοεῖτε (perceive) in vv9, 11; συνῆκαν (understand) in v12: two verbs.
- **"baskets" → "baskets" / "large baskets"** (vv9–10) — Greek κοφίνους and σπυρίδας, two words, as in the two feedings.
- **"the leaven of bread" → "the leaven of the loaves"** (v12) — Greek τῆς ζύμης τῶν ἄρτων.
- **"doctrine" → "teaching"** (v12) — Greek διδαχῆς.
- **"coasts" → "parts"** (v13) — Greek τὰ μέρη; Mark 8:27 has "the villages".
- **"Whom do men say that I the Son of man am?" → "Who do men say that the Son of Man is?"** (v13) — the critical text has no "me" (με: the Byzantine text).
- **"some … others" → "others … different ones"** (v14) — Greek ἄλλοι … ἕτεροι, two words. The Greek has no "say".
- **"saith" → "says"** (v15) — Greek λέγει, a present; kept as a present.
- **"Simon Barjona" → "*Shimon Bar-Yonah*"** (v17) — Greek Σίμων Βαριωνᾶ: the Aramaic *bar Yonah*, "son of *Yonah*", is written in Greek letters and not translated, so the verse leaves it.
- **"hath not revealed it" → "did not reveal it"** (v17) — Greek ἀπεκάλυψεν, aorist. The Greek has no object; "it" is supplied because English needs one.
- **"in heaven" → "in the heavens"** (vv17, 19) — Greek ἐν τοῖς οὐρανοῖς, plural; so also "the kingdom of the heavens" (τῶν οὐρανῶν), Matthew's own expression.
- **"thou art Peter, and upon this rock"** (v18) — two Greek words: Πέτρος *Petros*, the name (masculine), and πέτρα *petra*, "rock" (feminine): σὺ εἶ Πέτρος, καὶ ἐπὶ ταύτῃ τῇ πέτρᾳ. *Kefa* is the Aramaic name that Πέτρος renders (John 1:42); in Aramaic one word, *kefa*, would stand in both places. The verse does not say more than this.
- **"build" kept** (v18) — Greek οἰκοδομήσω, of a building; the verb is "build up" in the letters.
- **"church" kept** (v18) — Greek ἐκκλησία, "assembly"; here and 18:17 only in the Gospels.
- **"the gates of hell" → "the gates of Hades"** (v18) — Greek πύλαι ᾅδου, without articles. "It" (αὐτῆς, feminine) is the church by the nearest noun; "rock" is feminine too.
- **"shall be bound … shall be loosed" → "will have been bound … will have been loosed"** (v19) — Greek ἔσται δεδεμένον, ἔσται λελυμένον: the future of "to be" with a perfect passive participle, a future perfect. The other way of taking it: the participle names only the lasting state, "will be bound", "will be loosed". The same form stands in 18:18.
- **"charged" → "enjoined"** (v20) — Greek διεστείλατο (Tregelles, NA28, the Byzantine text); Westcott-Hort read ἐπετίμησεν, "rebuked", the verb of Mark 8:30.
- **"that he was Jesus the Christ" → "that he is the *Mashiach*"** (v20) — Greek ἐστιν, a present; "Jesus" is not in the critical text.
- **"From that time forth began Jesus" → "From then *Yeshua* began"** (v21) — Greek Ἀπὸ τότε. Westcott-Hort read "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*" (Ἰησοῦς Χριστός).
- **"go unto" → "go away to"** (v21) — Greek ἀπελθεῖν.
- **"chief priests" → "high priests"** (v21) — Greek ἀρχιερέων, the plural of "high priest".
- **"be raised again the third day" → "on the third day be raised"** (v21) — Greek ἐγερθῆναι, passive; Mark 8:31 has "after three days rise".
- **"took him" → "taking him to himself"** (v22) — Greek προσλαβόμενος, as Mark 8:32.
- **"Be it far from thee, Lord" → "Far be it from you, Lord!"** (v22) — Greek Ἵλεώς σοι, κύριε, two words without a verb, word for word "Propitious to you, Lord": ἵλεως is "propitious, gracious" (Heb 8:12). The phrase is a set one of refusal, "[may God be] gracious to you", said to turn away something dreaded; the verse gives its sense and supplies no "God".
- **"this shall not be unto thee" → "This will by no means be to you"** (v22) — Greek οὐ μὴ ἔσται σοι τοῦτο, the strong negation.
- **"Get thee behind me" → "Go behind me"** (v23) — Greek Ὕπαγε ὀπίσω μου, as Mark 8:33.
- **"offence" → "stumbling block"** (v23) — Greek σκάνδαλον, the noun of the verb "make stumble" (18:6): a thing one stumbles over. Only Matthew has this sentence.
- **"thou savourest not the things that be of God" → "you do not think the things of *Elohim*"** (v23) — Greek οὐ φρονεῖς τὰ τοῦ θεοῦ, as Mark 8:33.
- **"will come after me" → "wants to come after me"** (v24) — Greek θέλει, "wants"; so also v25.
- **"life" → "soul"** (v25) — Greek ψυχή, the same word as "soul" in v26; the passage plays on the one word.
- **"shall lose" (second) → "loses"** (v25) — Greek ἀπολέσῃ, subjunctive; Mark 8:35 has the future. Matthew has "find" where Mark has "save", and no "and of the gospel".
- **"what is a man profited" → "what will a man be profited"** (v26) — Greek ὠφεληθήσεται, a future passive (the Byzantine text has the present).
- **"lose his own soul" → "suffers the loss of his soul"** (v26) — Greek ζημιωθῇ, a different verb from "lose" in v25.
- **"in exchange for" → "as an exchange for"** (v26) — Greek ἀντάλλαγμα, a noun.
- **"shall come" → "is about to come"** (v27) — Greek μέλλει … ἔρχεσθαι.
- **"reward every man according to his works" → "repay each one according to his practice"** (v27) — Greek ἀποδώσει; πρᾶξιν, singular, a man's doing as a whole (Ps 62:12; Prov 24:12).
- **"Verily" → "Amen"** (v28) — Greek ἀμήν, left untranslated as Matthew leaves it.
- **"shall not taste of death" → "will by no means taste death"** (v28) — Greek οὐ μὴ γεύσωνται. Mark 9:1 ends "until they see the kingdom of *Elohim* having come in power".
- **"leaven" — retained** (vv6, 11–12) — Greek ζύμη *zumē* = leaven/yeast; figurative for pervasive influence.

### Notes
- **v18 *Kefa* / *petra*** — Aramaic *Kefa* = "rock"; Greek feminine *petra* = rock, bedrock; a play on Peter's name.
- **v19** — "you" is singular (σοι, δήσῃς, λύσῃς): spoken to *Kefa*. In 18:18 the same words are plural.
