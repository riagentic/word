# Matthew 17

[1] And after six days ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ takes along ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ his brother, and brings them up into a high mountain privately.

[2] And he was transformed before them, and his face shone as the sun, and his garments became white as the light.

[3] And behold, there appeared to them ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧, talking with him.

[4] And ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, answering, said to ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, "Lord, it is good for us to be here; if you want, I will make here three tents, one for you and one for ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and one for ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧."

[5] While he was still speaking, behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them, and behold, a voice out of the cloud, saying, "**This is my Son, the beloved, in whom I have taken pleasure; hear him.**"

[6] And hearing it, the disciples fell upon their face and were exceedingly afraid.

[7] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ approached and, touching them, said, "Arise, and do not be afraid."

[8] And lifting up their eyes, they saw no one except ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ himself alone.

[9] And as they were coming down from the mountain, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ gave them commandment, saying, "Tell the vision to no one until the Son of Man is raised from the dead."

[10] And the disciples asked him, saying, "Why then do the scribes say that ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ must come first?"

[11] But he, answering, said, "⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ indeed comes and will restore all things;

[12] but I say to you that ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ has already come, and they did not recognize him, but did with him whatever they wanted. So also the Son of Man is about to suffer by them."

[13] Then the disciples understood that he spoke to them about ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ the ⟦immerser|Immerser|Baptist⟧.

[14] And when they came to the crowd, a man approached him, kneeling to him

[15] and saying, "Lord, have mercy on my son, because he is moonstruck and suffers badly; for often he falls into the fire and often into the water.

[16] And I brought him to your disciples, and they were not able to heal him."

[17] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, answering, said, "O unbelieving and perverted generation, how long shall I be with you? How long shall I bear with you? Bring him here to me."

[18] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ rebuked him, and the demon came out from him, and the boy was healed from that hour.

[19] Then the disciples, approaching ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ privately, said, "Why were we not able to cast it out?"

[20] And he says to them, "Because of your little faith; for amen, I say to you, if you have faith as a grain of mustard, you will say to this mountain, 'Pass over from here to there,' and it will pass over, and nothing will be impossible for you."

[22] And while they were gathering together in ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "The Son of Man is about to be delivered up into the hands of men,

[23] and they will kill him, and on the third day he will be raised." And they were exceedingly grieved.

[24] And when they came into ⟦capernaum|Kfar Nachum|Capernaum⟧, those who receive the didrachma approached ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and said, "Does your Teacher not pay the didrachma?"

[25] He says, "Yes." And when he came into the house, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ anticipated him, saying, "What do you think, ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧? The kings of the earth, from whom do they receive tolls or poll tax? From their sons or from strangers?"

[26] And when he said, "From strangers," ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to him, "Then indeed the sons are free.

[27] But so that we may not make them stumble, go to the sea, cast a hook, and take the fish that comes up first, and opening its mouth you will find a stater; taking that, give it to them for me and you."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"taketh … bringeth" → "takes along … brings"** (v1) — Greek παραλαμβάνει, ἀναφέρει, presents; kept as presents, as Mark 9:2. Mark adds "alone".
- **"transfigured" → "transformed"** (v2) — Greek μετεμορφώθη, as Mark 9:2 and 2 Cor 3:18.
- **"raiment" → "garments"** (v2) — Greek ἱμάτια. Matthew alone has "his face shone as the sun".
- **"Then answered Peter" → "*Kefa*, answering, said"** (v4) — Greek ἀποκριθεὶς … εἶπεν, both verbs; Mark 9:5 has "Rabbi" where Matthew has "Lord" (Κύριε).
- **"let us make" → "I will make"** (v4) — Greek ποιήσω (Westcott-Hort, NA28); Tregelles and the Byzantine text read ποιήσωμεν, "let us make", as Mark 9:5.
- **"tabernacles" → "tents"** (v4) — Greek σκηνάς.
- **"my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased" → "my Son, the beloved, in whom I have taken pleasure"** (v5) — Greek ὁ υἱός μου ὁ ἀγαπητός, ἐν ᾧ εὐδόκησα: the adjective follows with its own article; εὐδόκησα is an aorist, as Mark 1:11. The Greek has no verb with "a voice".
- **"hear ye him" → "hear him"** (v5) — Greek ἀκούετε αὐτοῦ (Deut 18:15).
- **"sore afraid" → "exceedingly afraid"** (v6) — Greek ἐφοβήθησαν σφόδρα. "It" after "hearing" is supplied.
- **"Arise" kept** (v7) — Greek Ἐγέρθητε.
- **"save Jesus only" → "except *Yeshua* himself alone"** (v8) — Greek αὐτὸν Ἰησοῦν μόνον (Westcott-Hort, NA28); Tregelles and the Byzantine text have τὸν Ἰησοῦν μόνον, "*Yeshua* alone".
- **"charged" → "gave commandment"** (v9) — Greek ἐνετείλατο; Mark 9:9 has "enjoined".
- **"be risen again from the dead" → "is raised from the dead"** (v9) — Greek ἐγερθῇ, passive (the Byzantine text has ἀναστῇ, "rise", as Mark 9:9).
- **"shall first come, and restore" → "comes and will restore"** (v11) — Greek ἔρχεται, a present, and ἀποκαταστήσει, a future; "first" is not in the critical text here.
- **"knew him not" → "did not recognize him"** (v12) — Greek ἐπέγνωσαν.
- **"have done unto him" → "did with him"** (v12) — Greek ἐποίησαν ἐν αὐτῷ, "in him", that is, in his case; Mark 9:13 has the plain dative, "to him".
- **"shall also the Son of man suffer of them" → "the Son of Man is about to suffer by them"** (v12) — Greek μέλλει πάσχειν ὑπ' αὐτῶν.
- **"kneeling down to him" → "kneeling to him"** (v14) — Greek γονυπετῶν αὐτόν.
- **"lunatick" → "moonstruck"** (v15) — Greek σεληνιάζεται, from σελήνη, "moon": the word names the illness by the moon and does not say more. The signs described (falling into fire and water) are those of epilepsy; Mark 9:17 speaks of "a speechless spirit".
- **"sore vexed" → "suffers badly"** (v15) — Greek κακῶς πάσχει (NA28, the Byzantine text); Westcott-Hort and Tregelles read κακῶς ἔχει, "is ill".
- **"could not cure" → "were not able to heal"** (v16) — Greek οὐκ ἠδυνήθησαν … θεραπεῦσαι.
- **"faithless and perverse" → "unbelieving and perverted"** (v17) — Greek ἄπιστος καὶ διεστραμμένη (Deut 32:5); Mark 9:19 has "unbelieving" only.
- **"suffer you" → "bear with you"** (v17) — Greek ἀνέξομαι.
- **"rebuked the devil; and he departed out of him" → "rebuked him, and the demon came out from him"** (v18) — Greek ἐπετίμησεν αὐτῷ … ἐξῆλθεν ἀπ' αὐτοῦ τὸ δαιμόνιον. "Him" (αὐτῷ) can be the boy or the demon; the demon is named only in the next clause.
- **"the child was cured from that very hour" → "the boy was healed from that hour"** (v18) — Greek ὁ παῖς; ἀπὸ τῆς ὥρας ἐκείνης.
- **"Because of your unbelief" → "Because of your little faith"** (v20) — Greek ὀλιγοπιστίαν (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28), the noun of "you of little faith" (16:8); the Byzantine text has ἀπιστίαν, "unbelief".
- **"verily" → "amen"** (v20) — Greek ἀμήν, left untranslated.
- **"Remove hence to yonder place; and it shall remove" → "Pass over from here to there, and it will pass over"** (v20) — Greek Μετάβα … μεταβήσεται.
- **v21 omitted** — KJV v21 ("Howbeit this kind goeth not out but by prayer and fasting") is read by Tregelles and the Byzantine text; Westcott-Hort and NA28 do not have it, and it is absent from the earliest manuscripts (Sinaiticus, Vaticanus). The translation omits it.
- **"while they abode in Galilee" → "while they were gathering together in *Galil*"** (v22) — Greek Συστρεφομένων (the Byzantine text has Ἀναστρεφομένων, "living").
- **"shall be betrayed" → "is about to be delivered up"** (v22) — Greek μέλλει … παραδίδοσθαι, "hand over"; the verb does not say "betray".
- **"shall be raised again" → "will be raised"** (v23) — Greek ἐγερθήσεται, passive; Mark 9:31 has "after three days he will rise".
- **"exceeding sorry" → "exceedingly grieved"** (v23) — Greek ἐλυπήθησαν σφόδρα.
- **"they that received tribute money" → "those who receive the didrachma"; "tribute" → "the didrachma"** (v24) — Greek τὰ δίδραχμα, "the two-drachma pieces": the Greek names the coin, not a tax. It is the half-shekel that every Jewish man paid yearly for the temple (Exod 30:13), not a Roman tribute.
- **"master" → "Teacher"** (v24) — Greek διδάσκαλος.
- **"prevented him" → "anticipated him"** (v25) — Greek προέφθασεν, "came before him": *Yeshua* speaks before *Kefa* does.
- **"custom or tribute" → "tolls or poll tax"** (v25) — Greek τέλη ἢ κῆνσον; κῆνσος is the Latin *census*.
- **"of their own children, or of strangers" → "from their sons or from strangers"** (vv25–26) — Greek υἱῶν; ἀλλοτρίων, "those who belong to another".
- **"lest we should offend them" → "so that we may not make them stumble"** (v27) — Greek σκανδαλίσωμεν.
- **"a piece of money" → "a stater"** (v27) — Greek στατῆρα, a coin of four drachmas: two didrachma, the payment for two men.
- **"for me and thee" → "for me and you"** (v27) — Greek ἀντὶ ἐμοῦ καὶ σοῦ; ἀντί is "in place of, in exchange for", as in 20:28.

### Notes
- **v5** — echoes Yeshua's baptism (Matt 3:17) with "hear him" added (Deut 18:15).
- **v21** (absent in earliest manuscripts; traditional "this kind goes not out but by prayer and fasting") omitted.
- **vv24–27** — only in Matthew.
