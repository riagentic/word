# Matthew 2

[1] Now when ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was born in ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧ of ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ in the days of ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ the king, behold, ⟦magi|magi|wise men⟧ from the east arrived in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,

[2] saying, "Where is the one born king of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧? For we saw his star in the east and came to bow down to him."

[3] And hearing it, king ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ was troubled, and all ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ with him;

[4] and gathering together all the high priests and scribes of the people, he was inquiring from them where the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧ is born.

[5] And they said to him, "In ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧ of ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧; for thus it is written through the prophet:

[6] 'And you, ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧, land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,
are by no means least among the governors of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧;
for out of you will come out one who leads,
who will shepherd my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.'"

[7] Then ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧, secretly calling the ⟦magi|magi|wise men⟧, learned accurately from them the time of the appearing star,

[8] and sending them to ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧ he said, "Go and search out accurately concerning the young child; and when you find him, report to me, so that I also may come and bow down to him."

[9] And they, having heard the king, went; and behold, the star which they saw in the east was going before them, until it came and stood above where the young child was.

[10] And seeing the star, they rejoiced with exceedingly great joy.

[11] And coming into the house they saw the young child with ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ his mother, and falling down they bowed down to him, and opening their treasures they offered to him gifts: gold and frankincense and myrrh.

[12] And being divinely instructed in a dream not to turn back to ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧, they withdrew by another way into their country.

[13] Now when they had withdrawn, behold, an angel of the Lord appears in a dream to ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, saying, "Arise, take along the young child and his mother, and flee into ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and be there until I tell you; for ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ is about to seek the young child to destroy him."

[14] And he arose and took along the young child and his mother by night and withdrew into ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,

[15] and was there until the death of ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧; that what was spoken by the Lord through the prophet might be fulfilled, saying, "Out of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ I called my son."

[16] Then ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧, seeing that he was mocked by the ⟦magi|magi|wise men⟧, was exceedingly enraged, and sending, he did away with all the boys who were in ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧ and in all its borders, from two years old and under, according to the time which he had learned accurately from the ⟦magi|magi|wise men⟧.

[17] Then was fulfilled what was spoken through ⟦jeremiah-nt|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet, saying,

[18] "A voice was heard in Ramah,
weeping and much lamentation,
Rachel weeping for her children;
and she did not want to be comforted,
because they are not."

[19] But when ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ had died, behold, an angel of the Lord appears in a dream to ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,

[20] saying, "Arise, take along the young child and his mother, and go into the land of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, for those seeking the soul of the young child have died."

[21] And he arose and took along the young child and his mother and entered into the land of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[22] But hearing that ⟦archelaus|Archelaos|Archelaus⟧ reigns over ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ in place of his father ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧, he was afraid to go away there; and being divinely instructed in a dream, he withdrew into the parts of ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧,

[23] and coming he dwelt in a city called ⟦nazareth|Natzrat|Nazareth⟧, so that what was spoken through the prophets might be fulfilled, that he will be called a ⟦nazarenes|Notzri|Nazorean⟧.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew/Aramaic name he was actually called; Greek *Iēsous* is a transliteration of *Yeshua*.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v4) — Greek *Christos* = Hebrew *Mashiach*, "Anointed One."
- **"was born" kept; "he that is born" → "the one born"** (v1, 2, 4) — two Greek verbs: γεννηθέντος, γεννᾶται (γεννάω, "begot" in ch. 1; in the passive of a child "be born") and τεχθείς (τίκτω, "bear", 1:21, 23, 25).
- **"wise men" → "magi"** (v1, 7, 16) — Greek μάγοι, left as it stands (the word of Acts 13:6, 8).
- **"from the east … in the east" kept** (v1, 2, 9) — Greek ἀπὸ ἀνατολῶν (plural, no article) and ἐν τῇ ἀνατολῇ (singular, with the article): ἀνατολή is "rising", and so "the east"; v2 and v9 can also be read "at its rising".
- **"are come to worship him" → "came to bow down to him"** (v2, 8, 11) — Greek προσκυνέω, to bow or fall down before someone, in homage or in worship; one rendering in all books. ἤλθομεν is a simple past.
- **"When Herod the king had heard these things" → "And hearing it, king Herod"** (v3) — Greek ἀκούσας, without an object ("it" is supplied).
- **"chief priests" → "high priests"; "demanded of them where Christ should be born" → "was inquiring from them where the *Mashiach* is born"** (v4) — Greek ἀρχιερεῖς, the plural of "high priest"; ἐπυνθάνετο, an imperfect; γεννᾶται, a present tense.
- **"by the prophet" → "through the prophet"** (v5, 15, 17, 23) — Greek διά.
- **"in the land of Juda … among the princes of Juda … a Governor, that shall rule" → "land of *Yehudah* … among the governors of *Yehudah* … one who leads, who will shepherd"** (v6) — Greek γῆ Ἰούδα, no "in the"; ἡγεμόσιν, "governors" (as 10:18; 27:2); ἡγούμενος, a participle, "one leading"; ποιμανεῖ, "will shepherd". The quotation follows Matthew's Greek, which differs from both the Hebrew and the Septuagint of Mic 5:2.
- **"privily" → "secretly"; "enquired of them diligently what time the star appeared" → "learned accurately from them the time of the appearing star"** (v7, 16) — Greek λάθρᾳ (as 1:19); ἠκρίβωσεν, from ἀκριβής "exact"; τοῦ φαινομένου ἀστέρος, a present participle.
- **"search diligently for the young child … bring me word again" → "search out accurately concerning the young child … report to me"** (v8) — Greek ἐξετάσατε ἀκριβῶς περὶ τοῦ παιδίου; ἀπαγγείλατε. παιδίον, "young child", nine times in the chapter.
- **"went before them, till it came and stood over" → "was going before them, until it came and stood above"** (v9) — Greek προῆγεν, an imperfect; ἐστάθη (RP ἔστη).
- **"rejoiced with exceeding great joy" → "rejoiced with exceedingly great joy"** (v10) — Greek ἐχάρησαν χαρὰν μεγάλην σφόδρα, literally "rejoiced a great joy exceedingly".
- **"presented" → "offered"** (v11) — Greek προσήνεγκαν.
- **"warned of God in a dream" → "divinely instructed in a dream"** (v12, 22) — Greek χρηματισθέντες, "having received a divine answer / instruction"; the Greek has no word "God".
- **"departed" → "withdrew"** (v12, 13, 14, 22) — Greek ἀναχωρέω, one verb four times.
- **"appeareth" kept: "appears"** (v13, 19) — Greek φαίνεται, a present tense in narrative.
- **"Arise, and take" → "Arise, take along"** (v13, 14, 20, 21) — Greek ἐγερθεὶς παράλαβε; παραλαμβάνω of a person is "take along".
- **"will seek" → "is about to seek"** (v13) — Greek μέλλει ζητεῖν.
- **"death" kept** (v15) — Greek τελευτή, "end"; the verb τελευτάω, "die", in v19.
- **"of the Lord by the prophet" → "by the Lord through the prophet"** (v15) — Greek ὑπὸ κυρίου διὰ τοῦ προφήτου, as 1:22.
- **"was exceeding wroth, and sent forth, and slew all the children … in all the coasts thereof" → "was exceedingly enraged, and sending, he did away with all the boys … in all its borders"** (v16) — Greek ἐθυμώθη λίαν; ἀνεῖλεν, "took away, did away with" (killed); τοὺς παῖδας, masculine: "boys"; ὁρίοις, "borders".
- **"mocked" kept** (v16) — Greek ἐνεπαίχθη, the verb of 20:19; 27:29.
- **"lamentation, and weeping, and great mourning … would not be comforted" → "weeping and much lamentation … did not want to be comforted"** (v18) — Greek κλαυθμὸς καὶ ὀδυρμὸς πολύς (WH Treg NA28; RP adds θρῆνος καί); οὐκ ἤθελεν, "was not willing / did not want"; παρακληθῆναι, of mourners "be comforted", as 5:4 (the verb παρακαλέω is "appeal to" in 8:5).
- **"they are dead which sought the young child's life" → "those seeking the soul of the young child have died"** (v20) — Greek τεθνήκασιν, a perfect; ψυχή is "soul" throughout; here his life.
- **"came into" → "entered into"** (v21) — Greek εἰσῆλθεν (WH Treg NA28; RP ἦλθεν).
- **"did reign in Judaea in the room of" → "reigns over Judea in place of"** (v22) — Greek βασιλεύει, a present tense; WH NA28 have a bare genitive, Treg RP add ἐπί, "over".
- **"turned aside" → "withdrew"; "he came and dwelt" kept** (v22, 23) — Greek κατῴκησεν εἰς.
- **"He shall be called a Nazarene" → "that he will be called a Nazorean"** (v23) — Greek ὅτι Ναζωραῖος κληθήσεται. ὅτι can be "that" or can simply introduce the words spoken; no such sentence stands in the prophets, and "through the prophets" is plural. Ναζωραῖος, "Nazorean" (also 26:71), is a different form from Ναζαρηνός, "Nazarene" (Mark 1:24).
- **OT quotations** — Four OT quotations appear: one introduced by "it is written" (v6) and three by fulfillment formulas (vv15, 18, 23). Each OT source is cited in the Notes below; the quoted text is translated from Matthew's Greek as it stands.

### Notes
- **v6** quotes Mic 5:2.
- **v15** quotes Hos 11:1.
- **v18** quotes Jer 31:15.
- **v23** "Nazorean": possibly connected to *netzer* (branch) of Isa 11:1.
