# Acts 20

[1] After the uproar ceased, ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ sent for the disciples, and after encouraging them, he said farewell and departed for ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧.

[2] When he had gone through those regions and had given them much encouragement, he came to ⟦greece|Yavan|Greece⟧.

[3] There he spent three months, and when a plot was made against him by the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ as he was about to set sail for Syria, he decided to return through ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧.

[5] These went on ahead and were waiting for us at Troas,

[6] but we sailed away from ⟦philippi|Philippoi|Philippi⟧ after the days of ⟦unleavened-bread|Matzot|Unleavened Bread⟧, and in five days we came to them at Troas, where we stayed for seven days.

[7] On the first day of the week, when we were gathered together to break bread, ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ talked with them, intending to depart on the next day, and he prolonged his speech until midnight.

[8] There were many lamps in the upper room where we were gathered.

[9] And a young man named ⟦eutychus|Eutychos|Eutychus⟧, sitting at the window, sank into a deep sleep as ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ talked still longer. And being overcome by sleep, he fell down from the third story and was taken up dead.

[10] But ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ went down and bent over him, and taking him in his arms, said, "Do not be alarmed, for his life is in him."

[11] And when ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ had gone up and had broken bread and eaten, he conversed with them a long while, until daybreak, and so departed.

[12] And they took the youth away alive, and were not a little comforted.

[13] But going ahead to the ship, we set sail for Assos, intending to take ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ aboard there, for so he had arranged, intending himself to go by land.

[14] And when he met us at Assos, we took him on board and went to ⟦mitylene|Mytilene|Mitylene⟧.

[15] And sailing from there we came the following day opposite Chios; the next day we touched at Samos; and the day after that we went to ⟦miletus|Miletos|Miletus⟧.

[16] For ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ had decided to sail past ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧, so that he might not have to spend time in Asia, for he was hastening to be at ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, if possible, on the day of ⟦shavuot|Shavuot|Pentecost⟧.

[17] Now from ⟦miletus|Miletos|Miletus⟧ he sent to ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧ and called the elders of the church to come to him.

[19] serving the Lord with all humility and with tears and with trials that happened to me through the plots of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧;

[20] how I did not shrink from declaring to you anything that was profitable, and teaching you in public and from house to house,

[21] testifying both to ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and to ⟦greeks|Yevanim|Greeks⟧ of repentance toward ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and of faith in our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[25] "And now, behold, I know that none of you among whom I have gone about proclaiming the kingdom will see my face again.

[26] Therefore I testify to you this day that I am innocent of the blood of all,

[27] for I did not shrink from declaring to you the whole counsel of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[28] Pay careful attention to yourselves and to all the flock, in which the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ has made you overseers, **to care for the church of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, which he obtained with his own blood**.

[29] I know that after my departure fierce wolves will come in among you, not sparing the flock;

[30] and from among your own selves will arise men speaking twisted things, to draw away the disciples after them.

[31] Therefore be alert, remembering that for three years I did not cease night or day to admonish every one with tears.

[32] And now I commend you to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and to the word of his grace, which is able to build you up and to give you the inheritance among all those who are sanctified.

[33] I coveted no one's silver or gold or apparel.

[34] You yourselves know that these hands ministered to my necessities and to those who were with me.

[35] In all things I have shown you that by working hard in this way we must help the weak and remember the words of the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, how he himself said, '**It is more blessed to give than to receive**.'"

[36] And when he had said these things, he knelt down and prayed with them all.

[37] And there was much weeping on the part of all; they embraced ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ and kissed him,

[38] being sorrowful most of all because of the word he had spoken, that they would not see his face again. And they accompanied him to the ship.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v21, 24, 35) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v21) — Hebrew messianic title.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v22, 23, 28) — consistent rendering.
- **"Unleavened Bread"** (v6) — refers to the feast of Matzot (חַג הַמַּצּוֹת *Chag HaMatzot*), the seven days following Passover; retained.
- **"overseers" / "bishops"** (v28) — Greek ἐπισκόπους *episkopous* = "overseers/supervisors"; "bishops" is a later ecclesiastical term; "overseers" is the accurate translation.
- **v35 agraphon** — *Yeshua's* saying "It is more blessed to give than to receive" is not found in the Gospels; this is one of the few *agrapha* (unrecorded sayings) preserved in the NT.
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** modernized throughout.

### Notes
- **v35** — a saying of *Yeshua* not preserved in the Gospels (an *agraphon*).
