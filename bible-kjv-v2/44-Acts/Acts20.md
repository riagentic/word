# Acts 20

[1] And after the commotion had ceased, ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, having sent for the disciples and encouraged them, having greeted them, went out to go to ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧.

[2] And having gone through those parts and encouraged them with much word, he came to ⟦greece|Yavan|Greece⟧,

[3] and having spent three months, when a plot was made against him by the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ as he was about to put out to sea for Syria, he came to be of the opinion to return through ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧.

[4] And there accompanied him ⟦sopater|Sopatros|Sopater⟧ of ⟦pyrrhus|Pyrrhos|Pyrrhus⟧, of ⟦berea|Beroia|Berea⟧, and of the ⟦thessalonians|Thessalonikeis|Thessalonians⟧ ⟦aristarchus|Aristarchos|Aristarchus⟧ and Secundus, and Gaius of Derbe and ⟦timothy|Timotheos|Timothy⟧, and ⟦asians|Asianoi|Asians⟧, ⟦tychicus|Tychikos|Tychicus⟧ and ⟦trophimus|Trophimos|Trophimus⟧;

[5] and these, having come, were waiting for us in Troas;

[6] but we sailed out from ⟦philippi|Philippoi|Philippi⟧ after the days of ⟦unleavened-bread|Matzot|Unleavened Bread⟧, and came to them to Troas within five days, where we stayed seven days.

[7] And on the first day of the week, when we were gathered together to break bread, ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ was discussing with them, being about to go out on the morrow, and he prolonged the word until midnight.

[8] And there were a considerable number of lamps in the upper room where we were gathered together.

[9] And a certain young man named ⟦eutychus|Eutychos|Eutychus⟧, sitting on the window, being borne down by deep sleep as ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ was discussing still longer, having been borne down by the sleep fell down from the third story and was taken up dead.

[10] But ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, having gone down, fell upon him, and having embraced him said, "Do not make a commotion, for his soul is in him."

[11] And having gone up and broken the bread and tasted, and having conversed for a considerable while until dawn, so he went out.

[12] And they brought the boy alive, and were encouraged not moderately.

[13] But we, having gone ahead to the ship, put out to sea for Assos, being about to take up ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ from there, for so he had given orders, being about himself to go on foot.

[14] And when he met us at Assos, having taken him up we came to ⟦mitylene|Mytilene|Mitylene⟧,

[15] and having sailed away from there, on the following day we arrived opposite Chios, and on the next we crossed over to Samos, and on the day after we came to ⟦miletus|Miletos|Miletus⟧;

[16] for ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ had judged to sail past ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧, so that it might not come about for him to spend time in Asia; for he was hastening, if it were possible for him, to be in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ on the day of ⟦shavuot|Shavuot|Pentecost⟧.

[17] And from ⟦miletus|Miletos|Miletus⟧, having sent to ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧, he called to himself the elders of the church.

[18] And when they had come to him, he said to them:

"You know, from the first day from which I set foot in Asia, how I was with you the whole time,

[19] being a slave to the Lord with all humility and tears and trials that happened to me in the plots of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧;

[20] how in nothing of the things that are profitable did I draw back from announcing to you and teaching you publicly and from house to house,

[21] solemnly testifying both to ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and to ⟦greeks|Yevanim|Greeks⟧ the repentance toward ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and faith toward our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[22] And now behold, I, bound in the Spirit, am going to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, not knowing the things that will meet me in it,

[23] except that the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ solemnly testifies to me city by city, saying that chains and afflictions wait for me.

[24] But of no account do I make the soul as precious to myself, so as to perfect my course and the service which I received from the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, to solemnly testify the gospel of the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[25] "And now behold, I know that you all, among whom I went through proclaiming the kingdom, will see my face no more.

[26] Therefore I testify to you on this very day that I am clean from the blood of all,

[27] for I did not draw back from announcing to you the whole counsel of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[28] Give heed to yourselves and to all the flock, in which the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ set you as overseers, **to shepherd the church of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, which he obtained through the blood of his own**.

[29] I know that after my departure grievous wolves will enter in among you, not sparing the flock,

[30] and from among yourselves men will rise up, speaking perverted things, to draw away the disciples after themselves;

[31] therefore be awake, remembering that for three years, night and day, I did not cease admonishing each one with tears.

[32] And now I entrust you to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and to the word of his grace, able to build up and to give the inheritance among all those who have been sanctified.

[33] I desired no one's silver or gold or clothing.

[34] You yourselves know that these hands served my needs and those who were with me.

[35] In all things I showed you that, laboring thus, it is necessary to help those who are weak, and to remember the words of the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, that he himself said, '**It is more blessed to give than to receive**.'"

[36] And having said these things, having knelt down with them all, he prayed.

[37] And there came considerable weeping of all, and having fallen upon the neck of ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ they were kissing him,

[38] being pained most of all at the word which he had spoken, that they are about to behold his face no more. And they were sending him forward to the ship.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v21, 24, 35) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"Christ"** (v21) — not in the verse: τὸν κύριον ἡμῶν Ἰησοῦν (WH, NA28, SBLGNT, RP); Treg adds χριστόν.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v23, 28) — consistent rendering.
- **"Unleavened Bread"** (v6) — refers to the feast of Matzot (חַג הַמַּצּוֹת *Chag HaMatzot*), the seven days following Passover; retained.
- **"overseers" / "bishops"** (v28) — Greek ἐπισκόπους *episkopous* = "overseers/supervisors"; "bishops" is a later ecclesiastical term; "overseers" is the accurate translation. The same men are called "elders" (πρεσβυτέρους) in v17.
- **v35 agraphon** — *Yeshua's* saying "It is more blessed to give than to receive" is not found in the Gospels; this is one of the few *agrapha* (unrecorded sayings) preserved in the NT.
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** modernized throughout.
- **"uproar" → "commotion"; "called unto him the disciples, and embraced them" → "having sent for the disciples and encouraged them, having greeted them"** (v1) — Greek θόρυβον, as Mark 5:38; μεταπεμψάμενος … καὶ παρακαλέσας, ἀσπασάμενος (WH, Treg, NA28). παρακαλέω is "encourage" in v1, 2, 12 (it is also "exhort"); ἀσπάζομαι is "greet", here a greeting at parting. "Departed for to go" → "went out to go": ἐξῆλθεν πορεύεσθαι.
- **"had given them much exhortation" → "encouraged them with much word"** (v2) — Greek παρακαλέσας αὐτοὺς λόγῳ πολλῷ, singular, as 15:32. "Parts": μέρη.
- **"there abode three months" → "having spent three months"** (v3) — Greek ποιήσας μῆνας τρεῖς, as 15:33; no "there". "When the Jews laid wait for him" → "when a plot was made against him by the Jews": γενομένης ἐπιβουλῆς αὐτῷ, as 9:24. "To sail" → "to put out to sea": ἀνάγεσθαι, as 13:13. "He purposed" → "he came to be of the opinion": ἐγένετο γνώμης.
- **"accompanied him into Asia Sopater of Berea" → "accompanied him Sopater of Pyrrhus, of Berea"** (v4) — Greek Σώπατρος Πύρρου Βεροιαῖος (WH, Treg, NA28): "of Pyrrhus" is a bare genitive, most likely the father, and the verse supplies no "son"; Βεροιαῖος and Δερβαῖος are adjectives, "Berean", "Derbean". "As far as Asia" (ἄχρι τῆς Ἀσίας: Treg, RP) is not in WH, NA28, SBLGNT. "Of Asia" → "Asians": Ἀσιανοί.
- **"These going before tarried for us" → "these, having come, were waiting for us"** (v5) — προσελθόντες, "having come to [the place]" (WH, Treg, SBLGNT, RP); NA28 reads προελθόντες, "having gone ahead". ἔμενον with an object is "wait for" (also v23), not "abide".
- **"sailed away" → "sailed out"; "in five days" → "within five days"; "abode" → "stayed"** (v6) — Greek ἐξεπλεύσαμεν, as 15:39; ἄχρι ἡμερῶν πέντε, "up to five days"; διετρίψαμεν.
- **"when the disciples came together" → "when we were gathered together"** (v7) — Greek συνηγμένων ἡμῶν (WH, Treg, NA28); "the disciples" is Byzantine. "The first day of the week" is τῇ μιᾷ τῶν σαββάτων, "the one of the sabbaths", as John 20:1.
- **"preached unto them, ready to depart" → "was discussing with them, being about to go out"; "continued his speech" → "prolonged the word"** (v7) — Greek διελέγετο αὐτοῖς (also v9), as Mark 9:34; μέλλων ἐξιέναι; παρέτεινεν τὸν λόγον.
- **"many lights in the upper chamber, where they were gathered" → "a considerable number of lamps in the upper room where we were gathered"** (v8) — Greek λαμπάδες ἱκαναί; ἦμεν, "we were" (WH, Treg, NA28).
- **"being fallen into a deep sleep … he sunk down with sleep" → "being borne down by deep sleep … having been borne down by the sleep"** (v9) — Greek καταφερόμενος ὕπνῳ βαθεῖ … κατενεχθεὶς ἀπὸ τοῦ ὕπνου: one verb twice, first in the present, then in the aorist. "In a window" → "on the window": ἐπὶ τῆς θυρίδος. "Was long preaching" → "was discussing still longer": ἐπὶ πλεῖον.
- **"Trouble not yourselves; for his life is in him" → "Do not make a commotion, for his soul is in him"** (v10) — Greek Μὴ θορυβεῖσθε, the verb of θόρυβος (v1), as Mark 5:39; ψυχή, "soul". "Fell on him" kept: ἐπέπεσεν.
- **"When he therefore was come up again, and had broken bread, and eaten" → "having gone up and broken the bread and tasted"** (v11) — Greek κλάσας τὸν ἄρτον, with the article (WH, Treg, NA28); γευσάμενος, "having tasted", as 10:10. The subject is not named. "Talked a long while, even till break of day" → "conversed for a considerable while until dawn": ἐφʼ ἱκανόν … ὁμιλήσας ἄχρι αὐγῆς.
- **"the young man" → "the boy"; "were not a little comforted" → "were encouraged not moderately"** (v12) — Greek τὸν παῖδα (in v9 νεανίας, "young man"); παρεκλήθησαν οὐ μετρίως.
- **"sailed unto Assos, there intending to take in Paul: for so had he appointed, minding himself to go afoot" → "put out to sea for Assos, being about to take up Paul from there, for so he had given orders, being about himself to go on foot"** (v13) — Greek ἀνήχθημεν; μέλλοντες, μέλλων, one verb twice; ἀναλαμβάνειν; διατεταγμένος ἦν, διατάσσω of an everyday order, "give orders" ("ordain" of a standing rule, Gal 3:19); πεζεύειν, "go on foot" or "go by land". πλοῖον is "ship" of a seagoing vessel.
- **"when he met with us" kept as "when he met us"** (v14) — Greek συνέβαλλεν, an imperfect (WH, Treg, NA28): "as he was meeting us".
- **"the next day … the next day … the next day" → "on the following day … on the next … on the day after"** (v15) — Greek τῇ ἐπιούσῃ, τῇ ἑτέρᾳ ("on the other"), τῇ ἐχομένῃ: three different words. "Came over against" → "arrived opposite": κατηντήσαμεν ἄντικρυς. "We arrived at" → "we crossed over to": παρεβάλομεν, "put in at" or "crossed to". "And tarried at Trogyllium" is Byzantine only.
- **"had determined" → "had judged"; "because he would not spend the time" → "so that it might not come about for him to spend time"** (v16) — Greek κεκρίκει, as 15:19; ὅπως μὴ γένηται αὐτῷ χρονοτριβῆσαι.
- **"called" → "called to himself"** (v17) — Greek μετεκαλέσατο, as 7:14.
- **"after what manner I have been with you at all seasons" → "how I was with you the whole time"** (v18) — Greek πῶς μεθʼ ὑμῶν τὸν πάντα χρόνον ἐγενόμην; no word "lived". "Came into" → "set foot in": ἐπέβην. "Come to him" kept: παρεγένοντο πρὸς αὐτόν ("arrive" elsewhere).
- **"Serving" → "being a slave to"; "with many tears, and temptations, which befell me by the lying in wait" → "and tears and trials that happened to me in the plots"** (v19) — Greek δουλεύων; "many" is Byzantine; πειρασμῶν, of one root with "tempt"; ἐν ταῖς ἐπιβουλαῖς, "in", plural.
- **"I kept back nothing that was profitable unto you, but have shewed you" → "in nothing of the things that are profitable did I draw back from announcing to you"** (v20, also v27 "I have not shunned to declare" → "I did not draw back from announcing") — Greek οὐδὲν ὑπεστειλάμην … τοῦ μὴ ἀναγγεῖλαι, as Heb 10:38 "draw back"; ἀναγγέλλω "announce", as 14:27.
- **"Testifying" → "solemnly testifying"; "repentance toward God, and faith toward our Lord" → "the repentance toward God and faith toward our Lord"** (v21, also v23, 24) — Greek διαμαρτυρόμενος, as 2:40; τὴν εἰς θεὸν μετάνοιαν καὶ πίστιν εἰς τὸν κύριον: one article for both nouns, and εἰς both times.
- **"bound in the spirit" → "bound in the Spirit"** (v22) — Greek δεδεμένος ἐγὼ τῷ πνεύματι, a dative: "bound by the Spirit" (the Holy Spirit of v23) or "bound in my spirit". The Greek does not decide; the capital letter is the translator's. "The things that shall befall me there" → "the things that will meet me in it": τὰ ἐν αὐτῇ συναντήσοντά μοι.
- **"witnesseth in every city, saying that bonds and afflictions abide me" → "solemnly testifies to me city by city, saying that chains and afflictions wait for me"** (v23) — Greek κατὰ πόλιν διαμαρτύρεταί μοι (μοι: WH, Treg, NA28); δεσμά; μένουσιν, as v5.
- **"none of these things move me, neither count I my life dear unto myself, so that I might finish my course with joy" → "of no account do I make the soul as precious to myself, so as to perfect my course"** (v24) — Greek οὐδενὸς λόγου ποιοῦμαι τὴν ψυχὴν τιμίαν ἐμαυτῷ (WH, Treg, NA28), two turns of phrase in one: "I make the soul of no account" and "I do not hold the soul precious to myself". "Neither … I", "my" and "with joy" are Byzantine. ψυχή is "soul"; τελειῶσαι (NA28, SBLGNT, RP; 2–2, NA28 decides; WH, Treg τελειώσω, "so that I may perfect") is the verb of John 17:4, "bring to its end". "Ministry" → "service": διακονίαν.
- **"I have gone preaching the kingdom of God" → "I went through proclaiming the kingdom"** (v25) — Greek διῆλθον κηρύσσων τὴν βασιλείαν; "of God" is Byzantine.
- **"Wherefore I take you to record this day, that I am pure" → "Therefore I testify to you on this very day that I am clean"** (v26) — Greek διότι (WH, NA28, SBLGNT; elsewhere "because"; Treg διό "therefore"); μαρτύρομαι, as Eph 4:17; ἐν τῇ σήμερον ἡμέρᾳ, "on the today day", as 2 Cor 3:14; καθαρός.
- **"all the counsel of God" → "the whole counsel of God"** (v27) — Greek πᾶσαν τὴν βουλὴν τοῦ θεοῦ.
- **"Take heed therefore unto yourselves … over the which the Holy Ghost hath made you overseers, to feed" → "Give heed to yourselves … in which the Holy Spirit set you as overseers, to shepherd"** (v28) — Greek προσέχετε (no "therefore": WH, Treg, NA28); ἐν ᾧ, "in which"; ἔθετο, as 13:47; ποιμαίνειν, the verb of "flock" (ποίμνιον) and "shepherd".
- **"the church of God, which he hath purchased with his own blood" → "the church of God, which he obtained through the blood of his own"** (v28) — τὴν ἐκκλησίαν τοῦ θεοῦ (WH, NA28, SBLGNT); Treg reads τοῦ κυρίου, "of the Lord"; RP τοῦ κυρίου καὶ θεοῦ, "of the Lord and God". The last words are διὰ τοῦ αἵματος τοῦ ἰδίου (WH, Treg, NA28; RP διὰ τοῦ ἰδίου αἵματος, "through his own blood"). τοῦ ἰδίου can be taken in two ways: as an adjective with "blood" — "through his own blood"; or as a noun — "through the blood of his Own", that is, of his own Son (ὁ ἴδιος as in Rom 8:32 τοῦ ἰδίου υἱοῦ). The verse keeps the Greek order and adds no noun. περιεποιήσατο is "obtained, acquired for himself" (περιποίησις, 1 Thess 5:9).
- **"For I know this, that after my departing shall grievous wolves enter in" → "I know that after my departure grievous wolves will enter in"** (v29) — Greek ἐγὼ οἶδα (WH, Treg, NA28); βαρεῖς, "heavy" (1 John 5:3); ἄφιξιν, a word that elsewhere means "arrival".
- **"shall men arise, speaking perverse things, to draw away disciples after them" → "men will rise up, speaking perverted things, to draw away the disciples after themselves"** (v30) — Greek ἀναστήσονται; διεστραμμένα, as 13:10; τοὺς μαθητάς, with the article; ὀπίσω ἑαυτῶν (WH, Treg, SBLGNT; NA28, RP αὐτῶν, the same sense).
- **"watch" → "be awake"; "to warn" → "admonishing"** (v31) — Greek γρηγορεῖτε; νουθετῶν. "By the space of three years": τριετίαν.
- **"brethren, I commend you to God … which is able to build you up, and to give you an inheritance" → "I entrust you to God … able to build up and to give the inheritance"** (v32) — Greek παρατίθεμαι, as 14:23; "brethren" and both "you" are Byzantine or supplied; τὴν κληρονομίαν, with the article. τῷ δυναμένῳ, "the one able", can refer to God or to the word; the verse leaves it open. τῷ θεῷ (Treg, NA28, SBLGNT, RP); WH τῷ κυρίῳ, "to the Lord".
- **"coveted" → "desired"; "apparel" → "clothing"** (v33) — Greek ἐπεθύμησα, the verb of the commandment (Rom 7:7); ἱματισμοῦ, as John 19:24.
- **"ministered unto my necessities" → "served my needs"** (v34) — Greek ταῖς χρείαις μου … ὑπηρέτησαν, as 13:36.
- **"I have shewed you all things, how that so labouring ye ought to support the weak" → "In all things I showed you that, laboring thus, it is necessary to help those who are weak"** (v35) — Greek πάντα ὑπέδειξα ὑμῖν, "in all things" or "I showed you all things"; κοπιῶντας δεῖ, without "you" or "we"; ἀντιλαμβάνεσθαι, "take hold of, help" (Luke 1:54); τῶν ἀσθενούντων, "those who are weak" or "those who are sick". "How he said" → "that he himself said": ὅτι αὐτὸς εἶπεν.
- **"he kneeled down, and prayed with them all" → "having knelt down with them all, he prayed"** (v36) — Greek θεὶς τὰ γόνατα αὐτοῦ, "having set his knees", as 7:60.
- **"they all wept sore" → "there came considerable weeping of all"; "kissed him" → "were kissing him"** (v37) — Greek ἱκανὸς δὲ κλαυθμὸς ἐγένετο πάντων; κατεφίλουν, an imperfect, the compound verb of Mark 14:45.
- **"Sorrowing … for the words which he spake, that they should see his face no more. And they accompanied him" → "being pained … at the word which he had spoken, that they are about to behold his face no more. And they were sending him forward"** (v38) — Greek ὀδυνώμενοι, of pain; τῷ λόγῳ, singular; μέλλουσιν … θεωρεῖν; προέπεμπον, as 15:3.

### Notes
- **v35** — a saying of *Yeshua* not preserved in the Gospels (an *agraphon*).
