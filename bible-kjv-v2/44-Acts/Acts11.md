# Acts 11

[1] Now the apostles and the brothers who were throughout ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ heard that the nations also accepted the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[2] And when ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ went up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, those of the circumcision contended with him,

[3] saying, "You went in to men having uncircumcision and ate with them."

[4] But ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, beginning, set it forth to them in order, saying:

[5] "I was in the city of ⟦joppa|Yafo|Joppa⟧ praying, and I saw in a trance a vision: a certain vessel descending, like a great sheet let down by four corners out of heaven, and it came as far as me;

[6] into which having gazed, I was observing, and I saw the four-footed animals of the earth and the wild beasts and the creeping things and the birds of the heaven.

[7] And I also heard a voice saying to me, 'Rise, ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, slaughter and eat.'

[8] But I said, 'By no means, Lord, because a common or unclean thing has never entered into my mouth.'

[9] But a voice answered a second time out of heaven, 'What ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ cleansed, do not you make common.'

[10] And this happened three times, and all was drawn up again into heaven.

[11] And behold, at once three men stood at the house in which we were, sent from Caesarea to me.

[12] And the Spirit told me to go with them, making no distinction. And these six brothers also came with me, and we entered into the house of the man.

[13] And he reported to us how he saw the angel in his house, standing and saying, 'Send to ⟦joppa|Yafo|Joppa⟧ and send for ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ who is called ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧,

[14] who will speak words to you by which you will be saved, you and all your house.'

[15] And as I began to speak, the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ fell upon them, just as also upon us in the beginning.

[16] And I remembered the word of the Lord, how he said, '⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ indeed ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ with water, but you will be ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ in ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧.'

[17] If then ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave to them the equal gift as also to us, having believed upon the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, who was I, to be able to hinder ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?"

[18] And having heard these things they became quiet and glorified ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, saying, "**Then to the nations also ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave the repentance unto life.**"

[19] Now those who were scattered from the affliction that arose over ⟦stephen|Stephanos|Stephen⟧ went through as far as ⟦phoenicia|Phoinike|Phoenicia⟧ and ⟦cyprus|Kypros|Cyprus⟧ and ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧, speaking the word to no one except only to ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧.

[20] But there were some of them, men of ⟦cyprus|Kypros|Cyprus⟧ and of ⟦cyrene|Kyrene|Cyrene⟧, who, having come to ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧, were speaking also to the Hellenists, preaching the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[21] And the hand of the Lord was with them, and a great number that believed turned to the Lord.

[22] And the word about them was heard in the ears of the church that was in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and they sent out ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ as far as ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧;

[23] who, having arrived and seen the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, rejoiced and exhorted all to remain with the Lord in the purpose of the heart,

[24] for he was a good man and full of ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧ and of faith. And a considerable crowd was added to the Lord.

[25] And he went out to ⟦tarsus|Tarsos|Tarsus⟧ to seek out ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧,

[26] and having found him, he brought him to ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧. And it came to pass that even for a whole year they were gathered together in the church and taught a considerable crowd, and that in ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧ the disciples were first called ⟦christian|Christianoi|Christians⟧.

[27] Now in these days prophets came down from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧;

[28] and one of them, named ⟦agabus|Agabos|Agabus⟧, having risen, signified through the Spirit that a great famine was about to be over the whole inhabited earth, which came to pass under Claudius.

[29] And of the disciples, as any one had means, they determined, each of them, to send for service to the brothers dwelling in ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧;

[30] which they also did, sending to the elders through the hand of ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ and ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v15, 16, 24) — consistent rendering.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v17) — Greek Χριστόν *Christon*; Hebrew messianic title.
- **"Peter" / "*Kefa*"** (v2, 4, 7, 13) — Aramaic name used consistently.
- **"baptized / baptizeth" → "immersed"** (v16) — Greek *baptizo* = "to immerse."
- **"Cyrene" / "*Cyrene*"** (v20) — retained.
- **"Saul"** — retained as the established name; his Hebrew name *Sha'ul* was noted at first occurrence in ch. 7.
- **"the Gentiles" → "the nations"** (v1, 18) — Greek τὰ ἔθνη, the nations other than Israel.
- **"received" → "accepted"** (v1) — Greek ἐδέξαντο (δέχομαι).
- **"they that were of the circumcision contended with him" → "those of the circumcision contended with him"** (v2) — Greek οἱ ἐκ περιτομῆς, "those from circumcision"; no word "party" or "believers". διεκρίνοντο πρὸς αὐτόν: the middle of διακρίνω with πρός, "dispute with"; elsewhere the middle is "doubt" (Jas 1:6).
- **"men uncircumcised" → "men having uncircumcision"** (v3) — Greek ἄνδρας ἀκροβυστίαν ἔχοντας. Reading: Εἰσῆλθες … συνέφαγες "You went in … and ate" (NA28, SBLGNT; 2–2, NA28 decides); WH, Treg Εἰσῆλθεν … συνέφαγεν "He went in … and ate". The opening ὅτι can also be read as "Why": "Why did you go in …?"
- **"rehearsed the matter from the beginning, and expounded it by order" → "beginning, set it forth to them in order"** (v4) — Greek ἀρξάμενος … ἐξετίθετο αὐτοῖς καθεξῆς.
- **"in a trance" kept** (v5) — Greek ἐν ἐκστάσει; ἔκστασις is "a standing outside oneself", the word rendered "amazement" in Mark 5:42.
- **"by four corners" kept** (v5) — Greek τέσσαρσιν ἀρχαῖς, literally "by four beginnings"; no "its".
- **"fourfooted beasts of the earth, and wild beasts, and creeping things, and fowls of the air" → "the four-footed animals of the earth and the wild beasts and the creeping things and the birds of the heaven"** (v6) — every member has the article in Greek; τοῦ οὐρανοῦ is "of the heaven". κατενόουν "I was observing" and εἶδον "I saw" are two verbs.
- **"slay" → "slaughter"** (v7) — Greek θῦσον; θύω is "slaughter" and also "sacrifice". Ἀναστάς is a participle, "having risen".
- **"call not thou common" → "do not you make common"** (v9) — Greek σὺ μὴ κοίνου, the verb of κοινόν "common" (v8): "make common", that is, treat as common. The same verb is "defile" in Mark 7:15; here that word would hide the link with v8. ἐκαθάρισεν is an aorist, "cleansed". "A voice" has no article.
- **"immediately" → "at once"** (v11) — Greek ἐξαυτῆς, not εὐθύς. ἐπέστησαν is "stood at", "came upon".
- **"nothing doubting" → "making no distinction"** (v12) — Greek μηδὲν διακρίναντα, active (WH, Treg, NA28, SBLGNT); the Byzantine text has the middle διακρινόμενον, "doubting nothing", as 10:20.
- **"shewed" → "reported"; "call for" → "send for"** (v13) — Greek ἀπήγγειλεν, μετάπεμψαι. "Men" after "Send" is only in the Byzantine text.
- **"words" kept; "household" → "house"** (v14) — Greek ῥήματα (ῥῆμα, not λόγος) and οἶκος.
- **"with the Holy Ghost" → "in the *Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v16) — Greek ἐν πνεύματι ἁγίῳ, "in", and without the article; ὕδατι "with water" is a plain dative.
- **"the like gift" → "the equal gift"; "withstand" → "hinder"** (v17) — Greek τὴν ἴσην δωρεάν, κωλῦσαι. πιστεύσασιν "having believed" is a dative and can go with "us", with "them", or with both.
- **"held their peace" → "became quiet"; "granted" → "gave"; "repentance unto life" kept** (v18) — Greek ἡσύχασαν, ἔδωκεν, τὴν μετάνοιαν εἰς ζωήν; no "that leads to".
- **"persecution" → "affliction"** (v19) — Greek θλίψεως. ἐπὶ Στεφάνῳ is "over Stephen" (on account of him); it can also mean "in the time of Stephen".
- **"Grecians" → "Hellenists"** (v20) — Greek Ἑλληνιστάς (WH, NA28, SBLGNT, RP), Greek-speakers; Treg reads Ἕλληνας, "Greeks". εὐαγγελιζόμενοι "preaching" is "bringing as good news".
- **"tidings of these things" → "the word about them"** (v22) — Greek ὁ λόγος … περὶ αὐτῶν. After "Barnabas" NA28 and RP add διελθεῖν, "to go through"; WH, Treg and SBLGNT do not have it, and the verse follows them.
- **"with purpose of heart they would cleave unto the Lord" → "to remain with the Lord in the purpose of the heart"** (v23) — Greek τῇ προθέσει τῆς καρδίας προσμένειν τῷ κυρίῳ; προσμένω as Mark 8:2. No word "faithful" or "steadfast".
- **"much people" → "a considerable crowd"** (v24, 26) — Greek ὄχλος ἱκανός. "Holy Spirit" has no article in v24.
- **"Then departed Barnabas" → "And he went out"** (v25) — the name is not in the Greek (only the Byzantine text adds it).
- **"assembled themselves with the church" → "were gathered together in the church"** (v26) — Greek συναχθῆναι ἐν τῇ ἐκκλησίᾳ. "Him" is supplied twice (εὑρὼν ἤγαγεν has no object). "Were called" is χρηματίσαι, "bore the name"; the same verb is "be divinely instructed" in the passive (Heb 8:5).
- **"signified" kept; "throughout all the world" → "over the whole inhabited earth"** (v28) — Greek ἐσήμανεν; τὴν οἰκουμένην.
- **"in the days of Claudius Caesar" → "under Claudius"** (v28) — Greek ἐπὶ Κλαυδίου; no "days", and "Caesar" is only in the Byzantine text.
- **"every man according to his ability" → "as any one had means"; "relief" → "for service"** (v29) — Greek καθὼς εὐπορεῖτό τις; εἰς διακονίαν. "Determined" is ὥρισαν (ὁρίζω, "mark out", Rom 1:4), kept because "marked out to send" is not English.
- **"by the hands of" → "through the hand of"** (v30) — Greek διὰ χειρός, singular; no "it" after "sending".
