# Acts 11

[1] Now the apostles and the brothers who were throughout ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ heard that the Gentiles also had received the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[2] So when ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ went up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, the circumcision party criticized him, saying,

[3] "You went to uncircumcised men and ate with them."

[4] But ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ began and explained it to them in order:

[5] "I was in the city of ⟦joppa|Yafo|Joppa⟧ praying, and in a trance I saw a vision, something like a great sheet descending, being let down from heaven by its four corners, and it came down to me.

[6] Looking at it closely, I observed animals and beasts of prey and reptiles and birds of the air.

[7] And I heard a voice saying to me, 'Rise, ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧; kill and eat.'

[8] But I said, 'By no means, Lord; for nothing common or unclean has ever entered my mouth.'

[9] But the voice answered a second time from heaven, 'What ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has made clean, do not call common.'

[10] This happened three times, and all was drawn up again into heaven.

[11] And behold, at that very moment three men arrived at the house in which we were, sent to me from Caesarea.

[12] And the Spirit told me to go with them, making no distinction. These six brothers also accompanied me, and we entered the man's house.

[13] And he told us how he had seen the angel stand in his house and say, 'Send to ⟦joppa|Yafo|Joppa⟧ and bring ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ who is called ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧;

[15] As I began to speak, the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ fell on them just as on us at the beginning.

[16] And I remembered the word of the Lord, how he said, '⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ with water, but you will be ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ with the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧.'

[21] And the hand of the Lord was with them, and a great number who believed turned to the Lord.

[22] The report of this came to the ears of the church in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and they sent ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ to ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧.

[23] When he came and saw the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, he was glad, and he exhorted them all to remain faithful to the Lord with steadfast purpose,

[24] for he was a good man, full of the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ and of faith. And a great many people were added to the Lord.

[25] So ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ went to ⟦tarsus|Tarsos|Tarsus⟧ to look for ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧,

[26] and when he had found him, he brought him to ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧. For a whole year they met with the church and taught a great many people. And in ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧ the disciples were first called ⟦christian|Christianoi|Christians⟧.

[27] Now in these days prophets came down from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧.

[28] And one of them named ⟦agabus|Agabos|Agabus⟧ stood up and foretold by the Spirit that there would be a great famine over all the world (this took place in the days of Claudius).

[29] So the disciples determined, every one according to his ability, to send relief to the brothers living in ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧.

[30] And they did so, sending it to the elders by the hand of ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ and ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v15, 16, 24, 28) — consistent rendering.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v17) — Greek Χριστόν *Christon*; Hebrew messianic title.
- **"Peter" / "*Kefa*"** (v2, 4, 7, 13) — Aramaic name used consistently.
- **"baptized / baptizeth" → "immersed"** (v16) — Greek *baptizo* = "to immerse."
- **"Cyrene" / "*Cyrene*"** (v20) — retained.
- **"Saul"** — retained as the established name; his Hebrew name *Sha'ul* was noted at first occurrence in ch. 7.
- **"circumcision party"** (v2) — literally those of "the circumcision" (οἱ ἐκ περιτομῆς); retained as explanatory; refers to Jewish believers who insisted on circumcision for Gentiles.
