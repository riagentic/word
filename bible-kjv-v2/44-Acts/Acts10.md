# Acts 10

[1] Now a certain man in Caesarea, Cornelius by name, a centurion of the cohort called Italian,

[2] godly and fearing ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ with all his house, doing many alms for the people and beseeching ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ continually,

[3] saw manifestly in a vision, about the ninth hour of the day, an angel of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ coming in to him and saying to him, "Cornelius."

[4] And he, gazing at him and becoming afraid, said, "What is it, Lord?" And he said to him, "Your prayers and your alms have gone up for a memorial before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[5] and now send men to ⟦joppa|Yafo|Joppa⟧ and send for a certain ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ who is called ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧;

[6] this one is lodging with a certain ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧, a tanner, who has a house by the sea."

[7] And when the angel who was speaking to him went away, calling two of the household slaves and a godly soldier of those who persevered with him,

[8] and having declared all things to them, he sent them to ⟦joppa|Yafo|Joppa⟧.

[9] Now on the next day, as those were journeying and drawing near to the city, ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ went up on the housetop to pray, about the sixth hour.

[10] And he became very hungry and wanted to eat; and while they were preparing, a trance came upon him,

[11] and he beholds heaven opened and a certain vessel coming down, like a great sheet, being let down by four corners upon the earth,

[12] in which were all the four-footed animals and creeping things of the earth and birds of heaven.

[13] And there came a voice to him, "Rise, ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧; slaughter and eat."

[14] But ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ said, "By no means, Lord, for I have never eaten anything common and unclean."

[15] And a voice again, a second time, to him: "**What ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has cleansed, do not you make common.**"

[16] Now this happened three times, and immediately the vessel was taken up into heaven.

[17] Now as ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ was utterly at a loss in himself what the vision which he saw might be, behold, the men who had been sent by Cornelius, having asked their way to the house of ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧, stood at the gateway,

[18] and calling, they were inquiring whether ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ who is called ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ is lodging here.

[19] And as ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ was pondering about the vision, the Spirit said to him, "Behold, three men are seeking you;

[20] but rise, go down, and go with them, doubting nothing, for I have sent them."

[21] And ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, going down to the men, said, "Behold, I am the one whom you seek; what is the cause for which you are here?"

[22] And they said, "Cornelius, a centurion, a righteous man and fearing ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and having testimony borne to him by the whole nation of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, was divinely instructed by a holy angel to send for you to his house and to hear words from you."

[23] So, calling them in, he lodged them.

And on the next day he rose and went out with them, and some of the brothers from ⟦joppa|Yafo|Joppa⟧ went with him.

[24] And on the next day he entered into Caesarea. And Cornelius was expecting them, having called together his kinsmen and close friends.

[25] And when it came to pass that ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ entered, Cornelius, meeting him, fell at his feet and bowed down.

[26] But ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ raised him, saying, "Rise; I myself also am a man."

[27] And conversing with him he went in, and he finds many come together,

[28] and he said to them, "You know how it is unlawful for a man who is a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ to be joined to or to approach one of another tribe; and to me ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has shown that I should call no man common or unclean;

[29] therefore also I came without contradiction when I was sent for. I inquire therefore for what reason you sent for me."

[30] And Cornelius said, "From the fourth day until this hour, I was praying at the ninth hour in my house, and behold, a man stood before me in bright clothing,

[31] and he says, 'Cornelius, your prayer has been heard and your alms have been remembered before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[32] Send therefore to ⟦joppa|Yafo|Joppa⟧ and call to yourself ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ who is called ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧; this one is lodging in the house of ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧, a tanner, by the sea.'

[33] At once therefore I sent to you, and you have done well in coming. Now therefore we are all present before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to hear all the things prescribed to you by the Lord."

[34] And ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, opening his mouth, said: "In truth I grasp that **⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is not one who shows partiality,**

**[35] but in every nation the one who fears him and works righteousness is acceptable to him**.

[36] The word which he sent to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, preaching peace through ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ — this one is Lord of all —

[37] you know the word that came to be throughout the whole of ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧, beginning from ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧ after the ⟦immersion|immersion|baptism⟧ which ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ proclaimed:

[38] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, the one from ⟦nazareth|Natzrat|Nazareth⟧, how ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ anointed him with ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧ and power, who went through doing good and healing all who were oppressed by the devil, for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was with him;

[39] and we are witnesses of all the things which he did both in the country of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; whom also they did away with, hanging him upon a tree.

[40] This one ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ raised on the third day, and gave him to become visible,

[41] not to all the people, but to witnesses who had been chosen beforehand by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to us, who ate with him and drank with him after he rose from the dead;

[42] and he charged us to proclaim to the people and to solemnly testify that this one is the one marked out by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ as judge of the living and the dead.

[43] To this one all the prophets testify, that everyone who believes in him receives forgiveness of sins through his name."

[44] While ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ was still speaking these words, the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ fell upon all those hearing the word.

[45] And the faithful of the circumcision, as many as had come with ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, were amazed, because also upon the nations the gift of the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ has been poured out;

[46] for they were hearing them speaking in tongues and magnifying ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. Then ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ answered,

[47] "Can anyone hinder the water, that these should not be ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧, who received the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ as we also did?"

[48] And he prescribed that they be ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ in the name of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧. Then they requested him to remain some days.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v36, 38, 48) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v36, 48) — Hebrew messianic title.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v38, 44, 45, 47) — consistent rendering. In v38 the Greek has no article (πνεύματι ἁγίῳ καὶ δυνάμει); in v44, 45, 47 it has.
- **"Peter" / "*Kefa*"** (v5, 9, 13, 14, 17, 18, 19, 21, 25, 26, 32, 34, 44, 45, 46) — Aramaic name used consistently throughout; the Greek here has Πέτρος, also in "Simon who is called Peter" (v5, 18, 32).
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (v47, 48) — Greek *baptizo* = "to immerse"; consistent rendering.
- **"shalom" / "peace"** (v36) — Greek εἰρήνη *eirēnē* here clearly renders Hebrew שָׁלוֹם *shalom* (covenant peace, wholeness); rendered "peace" in the text.
- **"There was a certain man" → "Now a certain man"** (v1) — the Greek has no "was" (WH Treg NA28; RP adds ἦν): v1–3 are one sentence, "a certain man … saw". "Of the band called the Italian band" → "of the cohort called Italian": ἐκ σπείρης τῆς καλουμένης Ἰταλικῆς.
- **"A devout man, and one that feared God" → "godly and fearing Elohim"** (v2, 7) — Greek εὐσεβής, as εὐσέβεια "godliness". "Gave much alms" → "doing many alms": ποιῶν ἐλεημοσύνας πολλάς. "Prayed to God alway" → "beseeching Elohim continually": δεόμενος … διὰ παντός.
- **"evidently" → "manifestly"** (v3) — Greek φανερῶς.
- **"when he looked on him, he was afraid" → "gazing at him and becoming afraid"** (v4) — Greek ἀτενίσας, ἔμφοβος γενόμενος. "Are come up" → "have gone up": ἀνέβησαν.
- **"call for one Simon, whose surname is Peter" → "send for a certain Simon who is called Peter"** (v5) — Greek μετάπεμψαι (so in v22, 29); ὃς ἐπικαλεῖται.
- **"he lodgeth" kept** (v6, 18, 32) — Greek ξενίζεται, "is received as a guest" (root ξένος, stranger); the active is "lodged them" in v23. "He shall tell thee what thou oughtest to do" (KJV v6) is in none of the four editions nor in RP.
- **"two of his household servants, and a devout soldier of them that waited on him continually" → "two of the household slaves and a godly soldier of those who persevered with him"** (v7) — Greek οἰκετῶν; τῶν προσκαρτερούντων αὐτῷ, as in 8:13. The Greek has no "his" (WH Treg NA28).
- **"when he had declared all these things" → "having declared all things"** (v8) — Greek ἐξηγησάμενος ἅπαντα.
- **"he became very hungry, and would have eaten: but while they made ready, he fell into a trance" → "he became very hungry and wanted to eat; and while they were preparing, a trance came upon him"** (v10) — Greek γεύσασθαι, "to taste"; ἐγένετο ἐπ' αὐτὸν ἔκστασις (WH Treg NA28; RP ἐπέπεσεν, "fell upon").
- **"saw heaven opened, and a certain vessel descending unto him, as it had been a great sheet knit at the four corners" → "he beholds heaven opened and a certain vessel coming down, like a great sheet, being let down by four corners"** (v11) — Greek θεωρεῖ, a present; "unto him" and "knit (bound)" are not in WH Treg NA28. σκεῦος, "vessel", any object or container (also v16; 9:15); ἀρχαῖς, "beginnings": the corners or ends.
- **"all manner of fourfooted beasts of the earth, and wild beasts, and creeping things, and fowls of the air" → "all the four-footed animals and creeping things of the earth and birds of heaven"** (v12) — Greek πάντα τὰ τετράποδα καὶ ἑρπετὰ τῆς γῆς καὶ πετεινὰ τοῦ οὐρανοῦ (WH Treg NA28; RP adds "and the wild beasts").
- **"kill, and eat" → "slaughter and eat"** (v13) — Greek θῦσον, to slaughter, also to sacrifice.
- **"common or unclean" → "common and unclean"** (v14) — Greek καί (WH Treg NA28; RP ἤ, "or"); v28 has "or".
- **"What God hath cleansed, that call not thou common" → "What Elohim has cleansed, do not you make common"** (v15) — Greek σὺ μὴ κοίνου, the verb "make common", not "call"; "call" is said in v28 (λέγειν). The Greek has no verb with "a voice".
- **"the vessel was received up again" → "immediately the vessel was taken up"** (v16) — Greek εὐθύς (WH Treg NA28; RP πάλιν, "again").
- **"doubted in himself" → "was utterly at a loss in himself"** (v17) — Greek διηπόρει. "Had made enquiry for Simon's house, and stood before the gate" → "having asked their way to the house of Simon, stood at the gateway": διερωτήσαντες, πυλῶνα.
- **"Behold, three men seek thee" kept** (v19) — the verse follows ἄνδρες τρεῖς, "three men" (Treg NA28); WH reads "two men", RP and the SBLGNT "men" without a number. "Thought on" → "was pondering about": διενθυμουμένου.
- **"doubting nothing" kept** (v20) — Greek μηδὲν διακρινόμενος: without doubting, also "making no distinction".
- **"which were sent unto him from Cornelius" dropped** (v21) — not in the four editions nor in RP. "Behold" kept: Ἰδού.
- **"a just man … and of good report among all the nation of the Jews, was warned from God" → "a righteous man … and having testimony borne to him by the whole nation of the Jews, was divinely instructed"** (v22) — Greek μαρτυρούμενος (as in 6:3); ἐχρηματίσθη: the Greek has no word "God".
- **"they entered" → "he entered"** (v24) — Greek εἰσῆλθεν (WH Treg NA28; RP εἰσῆλθον). "Near friends" → "close friends": ἀναγκαίους φίλους.
- **"fell down at his feet, and worshipped him" → "fell at his feet and bowed down"** (v25) — Greek προσεκύνησεν, without an object.
- **"took him up, saying, Stand up" → "raised him, saying, 'Rise'"** (v26) — Greek ἤγειρεν, Ἀνάστηθι.
- **"found" → "finds"** (v27) — Greek εὑρίσκει, a present.
- **"an unlawful thing for a man that is a Jew to keep company, or come unto one of another nation" → "unlawful for a man who is a Jew to be joined to or to approach one of another tribe"** (v28) — Greek ἀθέμιτον, against what is laid down; ἀνδρὶ Ἰουδαίῳ; κολλᾶσθαι (as in 8:29; 9:26); ἀλλοφύλῳ, one of another tribe or race.
- **"without gainsaying" → "without contradiction"** (v29) — Greek ἀναντιρρήτως. "For what intent" → "for what reason": τίνι λόγῳ.
- **"Four days ago I was fasting until this hour; and at the ninth hour I prayed" → "From the fourth day until this hour, I was praying at the ninth hour"** (v30) — Greek Ἀπὸ τετάρτης ἡμέρας μέχρι ταύτης τῆς ὥρας ἤμην τὴν ἐνάτην προσευχόμενος (WH Treg NA28; RP adds "fasting and" and "hour"). Word for word: "from the fourth day until this hour I was praying the ninth". The usual sense given is "four days ago, counting to this very hour, I was at the ninth-hour prayer"; the verse keeps the Greek wording.
- **"said" → "he says"** (v31) — Greek φησί, a present. "Are had in remembrance in the sight of God" → "have been remembered before Elohim": ἐμνήσθησαν ἐνώπιον.
- **"call hither Simon" → "call to yourself Simon"** (v32) — Greek μετακάλεσαι, as in 7:14. "Who, when he cometh, shall speak unto thee" is not in WH Treg NA28 (RP has it).
- **"Immediately" → "At once"** (v33) — Greek ἐξαυτῆς. "Thou hast well done that thou art come" → "you have done well in coming": καλῶς ἐποίησας παραγενόμενος. "All things that are commanded thee of God" → "all the things prescribed to you by the Lord": προστεταγμένα; κυρίου (WH Treg NA28; RP θεοῦ).
- **"Of a truth I perceive that God is no respecter of persons" → "In truth I grasp that Elohim is not one who shows partiality"** (v34) — Greek καταλαμβάνομαι; προσωπολήμπτης, "one who receives the face", the noun of "partiality" (προσωπολημψία).
- **"he that feareth him, and worketh righteousness, is accepted with him" → "the one who fears him and works righteousness is acceptable to him"** (v35) — Greek ἐργαζόμενος δικαιοσύνην, δεκτός.
- **"The word which God sent unto the children of Israel, preaching peace by Jesus Christ: (he is Lord of all:)" → "The word which he sent to the sons of Israel, preaching peace through Yeshua the Mashiach — this one is Lord of all —"** (v36) — Greek τὸν λόγον ὃν ἀπέστειλεν (Treg NA28 RP; WH has no ὅν: "He sent the word"); the Greek has no "God" here. "Preaching peace": εὐαγγελιζόμενος εἰρήνην, bringing peace as good news. The sentence of v36–38 is broken in the Greek: "the word" (v36) and "Yeshua" (v38) stand in the accusative without a verb of their own, and "beginning" (v37) in the nominative. The verse leaves it so.
- **"That word, I say, ye know, which was published" → "you know the word that came to be"** (v37) — Greek τὸ γενόμενον ῥῆμα: ῥῆμα, the thing spoken, also "the matter"; another word than "the word" (λόγος) in v36.
- **"Jesus of Nazareth" → "Yeshua, the one from Nazareth"** (v38) — Greek Ἰησοῦν τὸν ἀπὸ Ναζαρέθ. "Went about doing good" → "went through doing good": διῆλθεν εὐεργετῶν. "Healing": ἰώμενος. "Oppressed": καταδυναστευομένους, held down under power.
- **"whom they slew and hanged on a tree" → "whom also they did away with, hanging him upon a tree"** (v39) — Greek ἀνεῖλαν κρεμάσαντες ἐπὶ ξύλου; ξύλον is wood, a tree (Deut 21:22–23). "And in Jerusalem" → "and Jerusalem": WH Treg and the SBLGNT have no second "in" (NA28 RP have).
- **"shewed him openly" → "gave him to become visible"** (v40) — Greek ἔδωκεν αὐτὸν ἐμφανῆ γενέσθαι.
- **"chosen before of God" → "chosen beforehand by Elohim"** (v41) — Greek προκεχειροτονημένοις.
- **"he commanded us to preach unto the people, and to testify that it is he which was ordained of God to be the Judge of quick and dead" → "he charged us to proclaim to the people and to solemnly testify that this one is the one marked out by Elohim as judge of the living and the dead"** (v42) — Greek παρήγγειλεν, κηρύξαι, διαμαρτύρασθαι, ὁ ὡρισμένος.
- **"give all the prophets witness" → "all the prophets testify"** (v43) — Greek μαρτυροῦσιν. "Remission" → "forgiveness": ἄφεσιν.
- **"While Peter yet spake these words"** (v44) — Greek ῥήματα "words"; "the word" at the end of the verse is λόγον.
- **"they of the circumcision which believed" → "the faithful of the circumcision"** (v45) — Greek οἱ ἐκ περιτομῆς πιστοί: πιστός, "faithful", here those who believe. "On the Gentiles" → "upon the nations": ἐπὶ τὰ ἔθνη. "Was poured out" → "has been poured out": ἐκκέχυται, a perfect.
- **"speak with tongues, and magnify God" → "speaking in tongues and magnifying Elohim"** (v46) — Greek λαλούντων γλώσσαις, μεγαλυνόντων.
- **"Can any man forbid water, that these should not be baptized" → "Can anyone hinder the water, that these should not be immersed"** (v47) — Greek Μήτι τὸ ὕδωρ δύναται κωλῦσαί τις: the question begins with μήτι and expects the answer "no"; κωλύω "hinder" (8:36).
- **"he commanded them to be baptized in the name of the Lord" → "he prescribed that they be immersed in the name of Yeshua the Mashiach"** (v48) — Greek προσέταξεν (as in v33); ἐν τῷ ὀνόματι Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ (WH Treg NA28; RP τοῦ κυρίου). "Prayed they him to tarry" → "they requested him to remain": ἠρώτησαν, ἐπιμεῖναι.
- **Gentile inclusion theme** — theologically central; *Kefa's* vision and Cornelius episode mark the first Gentile reception of the *Ruach HaQodesh*.
