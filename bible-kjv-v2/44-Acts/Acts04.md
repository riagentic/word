# Acts 4

[1] And as they were speaking to the people, the priests and the captain of the temple and the ⟦sadducees|Tzedukim|Sadducees⟧ came upon them,

[2] being greatly vexed because they were teaching the people and proclaiming in ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ the resurrection from the dead,

[3] and they laid hands on them and put them in custody unto the morrow, for it was already evening.

[4] But many of those who heard the word believed, and the number of the men became about five thousand.

[5] And it came to pass on the morrow that their rulers and the elders and the scribes were gathered together in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧

[6] (and ⟦annas|Chanan|Annas⟧ the high priest and ⟦caiaphas|Qayafa|Caiaphas⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ and ⟦alexander|Alexandros|Alexander⟧, and as many as were of high-priestly race),

[7] and having set them in the midst, they were inquiring, "In what power or in what name did you do this?"

[8] Then ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, filled with ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧, said to them, "Rulers of the people and elders,

[9] if we today are being examined about a good deed to a sick man, in what this one has been saved,

[10] let it be known to you all and to all the people of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ that **in the name of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ the ⟦nazarenes|Notzri|Nazorean⟧, whom you crucified, whom ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ raised from the dead — in this one this man stands before you healthy**.

[11] This is '**the stone that was despised by you, the builders, which has become the ⟦rosh-pinnah|rosh pinnah|cornerstone⟧**.'

[12] And **there is salvation in no other, for neither is there another name under heaven that has been given among men, in which we must be saved**."

[13] Now beholding the boldness of ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, and having grasped that they were unlettered and unskilled men, they were marveling, and they were recognizing them, that they had been with ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧,

[14] and seeing the man who had been healed standing with them, they had nothing to say in contradiction.

[15] But having commanded them to go away outside the ⟦sanhedrin|Sanhedrin|council⟧, they were conferring with one another,

[16] saying, "What shall we do to these men? For that a known sign has come about through them is manifest to all those dwelling in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and we cannot deny it;

[17] but that it may not be spread further among the people, let us threaten them to speak no longer upon this name to any man."

[18] And having called them, they charged them not to utter a sound nor teach at all upon the name of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[19] But ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, answering, said to them, "**Whether it is righteous before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to hear you rather than ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, judge,**

**[20] for we are not able not to speak the things which we saw and heard.**"

[21] And they, having threatened further, released them, finding nothing as to how they might punish them, on account of the people, because all were glorifying ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ over what had come about;

[22] for the man upon whom this sign of healing had come about was more than forty years old.

[23] And being released, they came to their own and reported all that the high priests and the elders had said to them.

[24] And they, having heard, with one accord lifted a voice to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and said, "Master, you who made the heaven and the earth and the sea and all the things in them,

[25] who through ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧, by the mouth of our father David your servant, said,

"'**Why did nations rage,
and peoples meditate empty things?
[26] The kings of the earth took their stand,
and the rulers were gathered together
against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ and against his ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧**' —

[27] for of a truth there were gathered together in this city against your holy servant ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, whom you anointed, both ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ and Pontius ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧, with nations and peoples of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[28] to do whatever your hand and your counsel predestined to come about.

[29] And now, Lord, look upon their threats, and give to your slaves to speak your word with all boldness,

[30] while you stretch out your hand for healing, and signs and wonders come about through the name of your holy servant ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧."

[31] And when they had made supplication, the place in which they were gathered together was shaken, and they were all filled with the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ and were speaking the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ with boldness.

[32] Now of the multitude of those who believed there was one heart and soul, and not even one said that any of the things belonging to him was his own, but all things were common to them.

[33] And with great power the apostles were rendering the testimony of the resurrection of the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, and great grace was upon them all.

[34] For neither was there anyone in want among them; for as many as were owners of plots of land or houses, selling them, were bringing the prices of the things that were being sold

[35] and laying them beside the feet of the apostles, and it was being distributed to each according as anyone had need.

[36] And ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, who was called ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ by the apostles (which is, being translated, Son of Encouragement), a ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧, of ⟦cyprus|Kypros|Cyprus⟧ by race,

[37] having a field, sold it and brought the money and laid it beside the feet of the apostles.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v10, 26) — Hebrew messianic title.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v8, 25, 31) — consistent rendering. In v8 and v25 the Greek has no article (πνεύματος ἁγίου); in v31 it has.
- **"Peter" / "*Kefa*"** (v8, 13, 19) — Aramaic name used consistently; the Greek has Πέτρος.
- **"baptized" / church gathering** — no direct occurrence of church in this chapter; church rendering noted in ch. 5.
- **"being grieved" → "being greatly vexed"** (v2) — Greek διαπονούμενοι, "worked up, pained", from πόνος "toil".
- **"preached through Jesus the resurrection from the dead" → "proclaiming in *Yeshua* the resurrection from the dead"** (v2) — Greek καταγγέλλειν ἐν τῷ Ἰησοῦ τὴν ἀνάστασιν τὴν ἐκ νεκρῶν.
- **"put them in hold unto the next day" → "put them in custody unto the morrow"** (v3) — Greek ἔθεντο εἰς τήρησιν, "into keeping"; ἐπέβαλον αὐτοῖς τὰς χεῖρας is "laid hands on them", not a word for arrest.
- **"the number of the men was about five thousand" → "became about five thousand"** (v4) — Greek ἐγενήθη.
- **"were gathered together at Jerusalem" → "in *Yerushalayim*"; "of the kindred of the high priest" → "of high-priestly race"** (v5, 6) — Greek ἐν (WH Treg NA28; εἰς RP); ἐκ γένους ἀρχιερατικοῦ, "of high-priestly descent, family". The names in v6 are nominatives hanging beside the sentence, which the printed text sets in brackets; the Byzantine text has accusatives.
- **"By what power, or by what name, have ye done this?" → "In what power or in what name did you do this?"** (v7) — Greek ἐν ποίᾳ δυνάμει ἢ ἐν ποίῳ ὀνόματι, "in what kind of"; "you" (ὑμεῖς) stands last, with stress. ἐν is "in" throughout the passage (v9, 10, 12).
- **"of the good deed done to the impotent man, by what means he is made whole" → "about a good deed to a sick man, in what this one has been saved"** (v9) — Greek ἐπὶ εὐεργεσίᾳ ἀνθρώπου ἀσθενοῦς, ἐν τίνι οὗτος σέσωσται. ἀσθενής is "weak", here of bodily infirmity (as 5:15–16); σῴζω "save" is the verb of v12; ἐν τίνι is "in what" or "in whom".
- **"by him doth this man stand here before you whole" → "in this one this man stands before you healthy"** (v10) — Greek ἐν τούτῳ, "in this (name)" or "in this one"; ὑγιής. "Of Nazareth" → "the Nazorean": Greek τοῦ Ναζωραίου, as 2:22.
- **"This is the stone which was set at nought of you builders, which is become the head of the corner" → "This is the stone that was despised by you, the builders, which has become the *rosh pinnah*"** (v11) — Greek οὗτος, "this one"; no name. ἐξουθενηθείς is "despised, counted as nothing" (the Septuagint of Ps 118:22 has "rejected"). εἰς κεφαλὴν γωνίας is "head of the corner", Hebrew *rosh pinnah*.
- **"Neither is there salvation in any other" → "there is salvation in no other"; "whereby we must be saved" → "in which we must be saved"** (v12) — Greek ἐν ἄλλῳ οὐδενί; ἕτερον "another, different"; ἐν ᾧ.
- **"when they saw the boldness … and perceived that they were unlearned and ignorant men" → "beholding the boldness … and having grasped that they were unlettered and unskilled men"** (v13) — Greek θεωροῦντες; καταλαβόμενοι; ἀγράμματοι, "without letters"; ἰδιῶται, private persons without training (1 Cor 14:16; 2 Cor 11:6).
- **"they could say nothing against it" → "they had nothing to say in contradiction"** (v14) — Greek οὐδὲν εἶχον ἀντειπεῖν.
- **"a notable miracle hath been done by them" → "a known sign has come about through them"** (v16) — Greek γνωστὸν σημεῖον γέγονεν δι' αὐτῶν; γνωστός "known" as v10.
- **"let us straitly threaten them, that they speak henceforth to no man in this name" → "let us threaten them to speak no longer upon this name to any man"** (v17) — Greek ἀπειλησώμεθα (the doubled "threaten with a threat" is the Byzantine reading); ἐπὶ τῷ ὀνόματι τούτῳ, as 2:38, also v18; ἐν τῷ ὀνόματι "in the name" in v7, 10.
- **"commanded them not to speak at all" → "charged them not to utter a sound … at all"** (v18) — Greek παρήγγειλαν; φθέγγεσθαι, "utter", not the usual verb for speaking (λαλεῖν, v17, 20).
- **"Whether it be right in the sight of God to hearken unto you more than unto God" → "Whether it is righteous before *Elohim* to hear you rather than *Elohim*"** (v19) — Greek δίκαιον; ἀκούειν.
- **"we cannot but speak" → "we are not able not to speak"** (v20) — Greek οὐ δυνάμεθα … μὴ λαλεῖν.
- **"all men glorified God for that which was done" → "all were glorifying *Elohim* over what had come about"** (v21) — Greek ἐδόξαζον.
- **"this miracle of healing was shewed" → "this sign of healing had come about"** (v22) — Greek τὸ σημεῖον τοῦτο τῆς ἰάσεως.
- **"they went to their own company" → "they came to their own"; "the chief priests" → "the high priests"** (v23) — Greek πρὸς τοὺς ἰδίους, no noun; οἱ ἀρχιερεῖς, the plural of "high priest" (v6).
- **"Lord, thou art God, which hast made" → "Master, you who made"** (v24) — Greek Δέσποτα, "master of slaves" (compare "your slaves", v29), not κύριος; "thou art God" is the Byzantine reading.
- **"who by the mouth of thy servant David hast said" → "who through *Ruach HaQodesh*, by the mouth of our father David your servant, said"** (v25) — Greek ὁ τοῦ πατρὸς ἡμῶν διὰ πνεύματος ἁγίου στόματος Δαυὶδ παιδός σου εἰπών (WH Treg NA28), a crowded phrase whose words do not fit together smoothly: literally "who of our father through *Ruach HaQodesh* of the mouth of David your servant said". The Byzantine text has neither "our father" nor "through *Ruach HaQodesh*". "Servant" is παῖς, as of *Yeshua* in v27, 30.
- **"Why did the heathen rage, and the people imagine vain things?" → "Why did nations rage, and peoples meditate empty things?"** (v25) — Greek ἔθνη, λαοί, both plural and without article; ἐμελέτησαν κενά.
- **"The kings of the earth stood up" → "took their stand"** (v26) — Greek παρέστησαν. After "were gathered together" (συνήχθησαν) the Greek adds ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό, "to the same place" (1:15).
- **"with the Gentiles, and the people of Israel" → "with nations and peoples of *Yisrael*"** (v27) — Greek σὺν ἔθνεσιν καὶ λαοῖς Ἰσραήλ, the two words of v25, plural and without article. "In this city" is in WH, Treg and NA28, not in RP.
- **"thy holy child Jesus" → "your holy servant *Yeshua*"** (v27, 30) — Greek παῖς, "child" or "servant", as 3:13.
- **"thy counsel determined before to be done" → "your counsel predestined to come about"** (v28) — Greek προώρισεν, as Rom 8:29; Eph 1:5. The verse follows ἡ βουλή σου "your counsel" (Treg NA28 RP; WH and the printed SBLGNT have no σου).
- **"behold their threatenings: and grant unto thy servants" → "look upon their threats, and give to your slaves"** (v29) — Greek δούλοις.
- **"by stretching forth thine hand to heal; and that signs and wonders may be done" → "while you stretch out your hand for healing, and signs and wonders come about"** (v30) — Greek ἐν τῷ … ἐκτείνειν σε εἰς ἴασιν καὶ σημεῖα καὶ τέρατα γίνεσθαι; here "signs" comes first.
- **"when they had prayed" → "when they had made supplication"** (v31) — Greek δεηθέντων, the verb of δέησις "supplication".
- **"were of one heart and of one soul" → "there was one heart and soul"** (v32) — Greek ἦν καρδία καὶ ψυχὴ μία. The verse follows ἅπαντα (Treg NA28 RP; WH and the printed SBLGNT πάντα): no change in translation.
- **"gave the apostles witness of the resurrection" → "the apostles were rendering the testimony of the resurrection"** (v33) — Greek ἀπεδίδουν τὸ μαρτύριον, "were giving back, paying" what was due.
- **"Neither was there any among them that lacked" → "neither was there anyone in want among them"** (v34) — Greek ἐνδεής. "Plots of land" is χωρίων, the word of 1:18; "field" in v37 is ἀγροῦ.
- **"at the apostles' feet" → "beside the feet of the apostles"** (v35, 37) — Greek παρὰ τοὺς πόδας (v37 πρός NA28).
- **"Joses, who by the apostles was surnamed Barnabas, (which is, being interpreted, The son of consolation,) a Levite, and of the country of Cyprus" → "*Yosef*, who was called *Bar-Nabba* by the apostles (which is, being translated, Son of Encouragement), a Levite, of *Kypros* by race"** (v36) — Greek Ἰωσήφ (WH Treg NA28); ἐπικληθείς; υἱὸς παρακλήσεως, "son of encouragement" or "of exhortation"; Κύπριος τῷ γένει, "a Cyprian by descent". The Greek has no "thus" and no "also".
- **v11** — OT quotation: Ps 118:22 ("The stone … the builders…").
- **v25-26** — OT quotation: Ps 2:1-2 ("Why did nations rage…").

### Notes
- **v11** quotes Ps 118:22.
- **v25-26** quotes Ps 2:1-2.
