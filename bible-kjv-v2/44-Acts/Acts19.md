[1] And it came to pass, while Apollos was in ⟦corinth|Korinthos|Corinth⟧, that ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, having gone through the upper parts, came to ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧ and found some disciples,

[2] and he said to them, "Did you receive ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧, having believed?" And they to him, "But we did not even hear whether there is ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧."

[3] And he said, "Into what then were you ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧?" And they said, "Into the ⟦immersion|immersion|baptism⟧ of ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧."

[8] And having entered into the synagogue, he was speaking boldly for three months, discussing and persuading concerning the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[9] But when some were being hardened and were disobeying, speaking evil of the Way before the multitude, having departed from them he set apart the disciples, discussing day by day in the school of ⟦tyrannus|Tyrannos|Tyrannus⟧.

[10] And this came about for two years, so that all those who dwelt in Asia heard the word of the Lord, both ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and ⟦greeks|Yevanim|Greeks⟧.

[11] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was doing powers, not the ordinary ones, through the hands of ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧,

[12] so that even to the sick there were carried away from his skin cloths or aprons, and the diseases departed from them, and the evil spirits went out.

[13] But some also of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who went about, exorcists, attempted to name over those who had the evil spirits the name of the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, saying, "I adjure you by the ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ whom ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ proclaims."

[14] And there were seven sons of a certain ⟦sceva|Skeuas|Sceva⟧, a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧, a high priest, doing this.

[15] But the evil spirit, answering, said to them, "⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ indeed I know, and ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ I am acquainted with, but you, who are you?"

[16] And the man in whom the evil spirit was, leaping upon them, having lorded it over both of them, had strength against them, so that they escaped out of that house naked and wounded.

[17] And this became known to all, both ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and ⟦greeks|Yevanim|Greeks⟧, who dwelt in ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧, and fear fell upon them all, and the name of the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was being magnified.

[18] And many of those who had believed were coming, confessing and announcing their practices.

[19] And a considerable number of those who had practiced the curious arts, having brought the books together, were burning them before all; and they counted up their prices and found fifty thousand in silver.

[20] Thus mightily the word of the Lord was growing and having strength.

[21] Now when these things were fulfilled, ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ set it in the Spirit, having gone through ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧ and Achaia, to go to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, saying, "After I have been there, I must also see ⟦rome|Roma|Rome⟧."

[22] And having sent into ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧ two of those who were serving him, ⟦timothy|Timotheos|Timothy⟧ and ⟦erastus|Erastos|Erastus⟧, he himself stayed on for a time in Asia.

[23] And there came about at that time no small stir concerning the Way.

[24] For a certain man, ⟦demetrius|Demetrios|Demetrius⟧ by name, a silversmith, making silver sanctuaries of Artemis, was providing no small business to the craftsmen;

[25] whom having gathered together, and the workers about such things, he said, "Men, you know that from this business we have our prosperity,

[26] and you behold and hear that not only of ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧ but of almost all Asia this ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, having persuaded, has turned away a considerable crowd, saying that those which come to be through hands are not gods.

[27] And not only is there danger for us that this part come into disrepute, but also that the temple of the great goddess Artemis be reckoned as nothing, and that she is even about to be pulled down from her majesty, whom all Asia and the inhabited earth reveres."

[28] And having heard, and having become full of rage, they were crying out, saying, "Great is Artemis of the ⟦ephesians|Ephesioi|Ephesians⟧!"

[29] And the city was filled with the confusion, and they rushed with one accord into the theater, having seized Gaius and ⟦aristarchus|Aristarchos|Aristarchus⟧, ⟦macedonians|Makedones|Macedonians⟧, fellow travelers of ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧.

[30] And when ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ wished to enter in to the populace, the disciples were not letting him;

[31] and some also of the Asiarchs, being friends to him, having sent to him, were appealing to him not to give himself into the theater.

[32] So some were crying out one thing, some another, for the assembly was confounded, and the greater number did not know for what cause they had come together.

[33] And out of the crowd they instructed ⟦alexander|Alexandros|Alexander⟧, the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ having put him forward; and ⟦alexander|Alexandros|Alexander⟧, having motioned with the hand, wanted to defend himself to the populace.

[34] But having come to know that he is a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧, there arose one voice from all, crying out for about two hours, "Great is Artemis of the ⟦ephesians|Ephesioi|Ephesians⟧!"

[35] And the clerk, having calmed the crowd, says, "Men of ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧, who then is there of men who does not know that the city of the ⟦ephesians|Ephesioi|Ephesians⟧ is temple keeper of the great Artemis and of that which fell from Zeus?

[36] These things then being undeniable, it is necessary for you to be calm and to do nothing rash.

[37] For you brought these men, who are neither temple robbers nor blaspheming our goddess.

[38] If then ⟦demetrius|Demetrios|Demetrius⟧ and the craftsmen with him have a word against anyone, court days are held and there are proconsuls; let them bring charges against one another.

[39] But if you seek after anything further, it will be resolved in the lawful assembly.

[40] For indeed we are in danger of being charged with insurrection concerning today, there being no cause, concerning which we will not be able to render an account concerning this gathering."

[41] And having said these things, he sent the assembly away.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v8, 11) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v4, 5, 13, 15, 17) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v2, 6) — consistent rendering. In v2 the Greek has no article both times (πνεῦμα ἅγιον); in v6 it has (τὸ πνεῦμα τὸ ἅγιον).
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (v3, 4, 5) — Greek *baptizo* = "to immerse"; consistent rendering.
- **"John's baptism" → "the immersion of John"** (v3, 4) — consistent rendering.
- **"the Way"** (v9, 23) — Greek ἡ ὁδός *hē hodos*; the movement is called "the Way" by both believers and outsiders; retained with significance noted. This chapter contains two of the Acts occurrences of this term (9:2; 19:9, 23; 22:4; 24:14, 22).
- **"assembly"** (v32, 39, 41) — Greek ἐκκλησία, the word rendered "church" elsewhere: here the gathered citizens of Ephesus (v39 "the lawful assembly", the regular meeting of the people). "Church" in English would say a body of believers, which this is not.
- **"Artemis / Diana"** (v24–40) — Greek Ἄρτεμις *Artemis*; the Ephesian Artemis; retained (KJV uses "Diana" = the Roman equivalent).
- **"the seven sons of Sceva"** — retained; dramatic episode; no name alterations needed.
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** modernized throughout.
- **"having passed through the upper coasts came to Ephesus: and finding certain disciples" → "having gone through the upper parts, came to Ephesus and found some disciples"** (v1) — Greek τὰ ἀνωτερικὰ μέρη, the higher inland parts; ἐλθεῖν (WH, Treg, RP), NA28 κατελθεῖν "came down"; εὑρεῖν (WH, Treg, NA28).
- **"Have ye received the Holy Ghost since ye believed? … We have not so much as heard whether there be any Holy Ghost" → "Did you receive … having believed? … But we did not even hear whether there is …"** (v2) — Greek ἐλάβετε πιστεύσαντες, an aorist with an aorist participle: "when you believed" or "after you believed"; Ἀλλʼ οὐδʼ … ἠκούσαμεν; εἰ πνεῦμα ἅγιον ἔστιν, "whether there is *Ruach HaQodesh*", which can also mean "whether it is given" (as John 7:39).
- **"Unto what … Unto John's baptism" → "Into what … Into the immersion of John"** (v3) — Greek εἰς, as v5.
- **"John verily baptized with the baptism of repentance, saying unto the people, that they should believe on him which should come after him, that is, on Christ Jesus" → "John immersed with an immersion of repentance, saying to the people that they should believe in the one coming after him, that is, in *Yeshua*"** (v4) — Greek ἐβάπτισεν βάπτισμα μετανοίας, "immersed an immersion", without article; εἰς τὸν ἐρχόμενον μετʼ αὐτόν stands first in its clause; εἰς τὸν Ἰησοῦν (WH, Treg, NA28), RP adds "Christ".
- **"When they heard this, they were baptized in the name" → "having heard, they were immersed into the name"** (v5) — Greek ἀκούσαντες, without object; εἰς τὸ ὄνομα, as 8:16.
- **"had laid his hands upon them … they spake with tongues, and prophesied" → "laid hands on them … they were speaking in tongues and prophesying"** (v6) — Greek χεῖρας (WH, Treg; NA28, RP "the hands"); ἐλάλουν γλώσσαις, ἐπροφήτευον, imperfects.
- **"spake boldly for the space of three months, disputing and persuading the things concerning" → "he was speaking boldly for three months, discussing and persuading concerning"** (v8) — Greek ἐπαρρησιάζετο; διαλεγόμενος, as 17:2; πείθων περί (WH, Treg, SBLGNT; NA28, RP "the things concerning").
- **"when divers were hardened, and believed not, but spake evil of that way" → "when some were being hardened and were disobeying, speaking evil of the Way"** (v9) — Greek ἐσκληρύνοντο, ἠπείθουν, imperfects (ἀπειθέω "disobey", not ἀπιστέω); κακολογοῦντες, as Mark 9:39. "He departed from them, and separated the disciples, disputing daily" → "having departed from them he set apart the disciples, discussing day by day": ἀποστάς, as 15:38; ἀφώρισεν, as 13:2; "school" is σχολή, a place of leisure and teaching, a lecture hall; RP "of a certain Tyrannus".
- **"this continued by the space of two years; so that all they which dwelt in Asia heard the word of the Lord Jesus" → "this came about for two years, so that all those who dwelt in Asia heard the word of the Lord"** (v10) — Greek ἐγένετο; τοὺς κατοικοῦντας; "Jesus" is Byzantine.
- **"special miracles by the hands of Paul" → "powers, not the ordinary ones, through the hands of Paul"** (v11) — Greek Δυνάμεις οὐ τὰς τυχούσας; ἐποίει, imperfect; διά.
- **"from his body were brought unto the sick handkerchiefs or aprons" → "even to the sick there were carried away from his skin cloths or aprons"** (v12) — Greek ἀποφέρεσθαι (WH, Treg, NA28); ἀπὸ τοῦ χρωτὸς αὐτοῦ, "from his skin"; σουδάρια, the Latin *sudaria*, as John 11:44; σιμικίνθια, the Latin *semicinctia*, workmen's aprons. No word "touched". "Went out of them" → "went out": ἐκπορεύεσθαι; "of them" is Byzantine.
- **"certain of the vagabond Jews, exorcists, took upon them to call … We adjure you by Jesus" → "some also of the *Yehudim* who went about, exorcists, attempted to name … I adjure you by the *Yeshua*"** (v13) — Greek τῶν περιερχομένων Ἰουδαίων ἐξορκιστῶν; ἐπεχείρησαν, as 9:29; ὀνομάζειν; Ὁρκίζω (WH, Treg, NA28), singular, as Mark 5:7; RP "We adjure". "Preacheth" → "proclaims": κηρύσσει.
- **"one Sceva, a Jew, and chief of the priests" → "a certain Sceva, a *Yehudi*, a high priest"** (v14) — Greek Σκευᾶ Ἰουδαίου ἀρχιερέως; ἀρχιερεύς is "high priest" (also of the men of the high-priestly families); no high priest of this name is known.
- **"Jesus I know, and Paul I know" → "*Yeshua* indeed I know, and Paul I am acquainted with"** (v15) — two verbs in the Greek, γινώσκω and ἐπίσταμαι (18:25; 19:25 "know"). "Indeed" is μέν (WH, NA28; the verse follows them: 2–2, NA28 decides); Treg, RP do not have it.
- **"leaped on them, and overcame them, and prevailed against them, so that they fled" → "leaping upon them, having lorded it over both of them, had strength against them, so that they escaped"** (v16) — Greek ἐφαλόμενος; κατακυριεύσας ἀμφοτέρων (WH, Treg, NA28), as Mark 10:42: ἀμφότεροι is "both"; in later Greek also "all", which would fit the seven of v14; RP "over them". ἴσχυσεν, as 15:10; ἐκφυγεῖν, as 16:27.
- **"was known to all the Jews and Greeks also dwelling at Ephesus" → "became known to all, both *Yehudim* and *Yevanim*, who dwelt in Ephesus"; "was magnified" → "was being magnified"** (v17) — Greek ἐγένετο γνωστόν; ἐμεγαλύνετο, imperfect.
- **"many that believed came, and confessed, and shewed their deeds" → "many of those who had believed were coming, confessing and announcing their practices"** (v18) — Greek τῶν πεπιστευκότων, a perfect; ἤρχοντο, imperfect; ἀναγγέλλοντες; τὰς πράξεις, as Col 3:9, here also a term for magic spells.
- **"Many of them also which used curious arts brought their books together, and burned them before all men" → "a considerable number of those who had practiced the curious arts, having brought the books together, were burning them before all"** (v19) — Greek ἱκανοί; τὰ περίεργα πραξάντων: περίεργος is "meddling, over-busy" (1 Tim 5:13 "busybodies"), τὰ περίεργα a set term for magic; "arts" is supplied. κατέκαιον, imperfect.
- **"they counted the price of them, and found it fifty thousand pieces of silver" → "they counted up their prices and found fifty thousand in silver"** (v19) — Greek τὰς τιμάς, plural; ἀργυρίου μυριάδας πέντε, "five ten-thousands of silver"; the coin is not named.
- **"So mightily grew the word of God and prevailed" → "Thus mightily the word of the Lord was growing and having strength"** (v20) — Greek κατὰ κράτος τοῦ κυρίου ὁ λόγος (WH, Treg, NA28): "the word of the Lord … with might", or, taking the genitive with the first noun, "according to the might of the Lord the word was growing"; ηὔξανεν καὶ ἴσχυεν, imperfects (ἰσχύω, v16).
- **"After these things were ended, Paul purposed in the spirit, when he had passed through" → "when these things were fulfilled, Paul set it in the Spirit, having gone through"** (v21) — Greek ἐπληρώθη; ἔθετο ἐν τῷ πνεύματι, as 5:4 ("set in your heart"); "it" is supplied. "In the Spirit" (as 20:22) or "in his spirit": the Greek does not decide.
- **"two of them that ministered unto him" → "two of those who were serving him"; "stayed in Asia for a season" → "stayed on for a time in Asia"** (v22) — Greek τῶν διακονούντων αὐτῷ; ἐπέσχεν χρόνον εἰς τὴν Ἀσίαν.
- **"the same time there arose no small stir about that way" → "there came about at that time no small stir concerning the Way"** (v23) — Greek κατὰ τὸν καιρὸν ἐκεῖνον; τάραχος οὐκ ὀλίγος, as 12:18.
- **"which made silver shrines for Diana, brought no small gain" → "making silver sanctuaries of Artemis, was providing no small business"** (v24) — Greek ναοὺς ἀργυροῦς, the word ναός "sanctuary" (small silver models of hers); παρείχετο … ἐργασίαν, as 16:16.
- **"Whom he called together with the workmen of like occupation … by this craft we have our wealth" → "whom having gathered together, and the workers about such things … from this business we have our prosperity"** (v25) — Greek συναθροίσας; τοὺς περὶ τὰ τοιαῦτα ἐργάτας; ἐργασίας; ἡ εὐπορία ἡμῖν ἐστιν (WH, Treg, NA28). "You know": ἐπίστασθε.
- **"ye see" → "you behold"; "hath persuaded and turned away much people, saying that they be no gods, which are made with hands" → "having persuaded, has turned away a considerable crowd, saying that those which come to be through hands are not gods"** (v26) — Greek θεωρεῖτε; πείσας μετέστησεν ἱκανὸν ὄχλον; οἱ διὰ χειρῶν γινόμενοι. "Of Ephesus … of almost all Asia" are genitives.
- **"this our craft is in danger to be set at nought … should be despised, and her magnificence should be destroyed, whom all Asia and the world worshippeth" → "there is danger for us that this part come into disrepute … be reckoned as nothing, and that she is even about to be pulled down from her majesty, whom all Asia and the inhabited earth reveres"** (v27) — Greek τὸ μέρος, "the part, the branch" (of business); εἰς ἀπελεγμὸν ἐλθεῖν; ἱερόν; εἰς οὐθὲν λογισθῆναι; μέλλειν τε καὶ καθαιρεῖσθαι τῆς μεγαλειότητος αὐτῆς (WH, Treg, NA28), a genitive: "be pulled down from her majesty", or "that some of her majesty be pulled down"; ἡ οἰκουμένη; σέβεται.
- **"they were full of wrath" → "having become full of rage"; "Great is Diana of the Ephesians" → "Great is Artemis of the Ephesians"** (v28, 34) — Greek θυμοῦ; ἔκραζον, imperfect.
- **"the whole city was filled with confusion: and having caught … Paul's companions in travel, they rushed" → "the city was filled with the confusion, and they rushed … having seized … fellow travelers of Paul"** (v29) — "whole" is Byzantine; τῆς συγχύσεως, the noun of "confounded" (v32; 2:6); συναρπάσαντες, as 6:12; συνεκδήμους, as 2 Cor 8:19.
- **"would have entered in unto the people, the disciples suffered him not" → "wished to enter in to the populace, the disciples were not letting him"** (v30) — Greek βουλομένου; δῆμον, as 12:22; 17:5; οὐκ εἴων, imperfect.
- **"certain of the chief of Asia, which were his friends, sent unto him, desiring him that he would not adventure himself" → "some also of the Asiarchs, being friends to him, having sent to him, were appealing to him not to give himself"** (v31) — Greek τῶν Ἀσιαρχῶν, the title of leading men of the province of Asia; παρεκάλουν; δοῦναι ἑαυτόν.
- **"the assembly was confused; and the more part knew not wherefore they were come together" → "the assembly was confounded, and the greater number did not know for what cause they had come together"** (v32) — Greek συγκεχυμένη; οἱ πλείους; τίνος ἕνεκα.
- **"they drew Alexander out of the multitude" → "out of the crowd they instructed Alexander"** (v33) — Greek συνεβίβασαν (WH, Treg, NA28), the verb of 9:22 ("proving"), 16:10 ("concluding") and 1 Cor 2:16 ("instruct"): "some of the crowd instructed Alexander", or "they concluded that it was Alexander"; the sense is not certain. RP προεβίβασαν, "they put forward". "Would have made his defence unto the people" → "wanted to defend himself to the populace": ἤθελεν ἀπολογεῖσθαι τῷ δήμῳ.
- **"when they knew that he was a Jew, all with one voice about the space of two hours cried out" → "having come to know that he is a *Yehudi*, there arose one voice from all, crying out for about two hours"** (v34) — Greek ἐπιγνόντες; φωνὴ ἐγένετο μία ἐκ πάντων; the Greek sentence changes its construction, and the verse keeps that.
- **"the townclerk had appeased the people, he said" → "the clerk, having calmed the crowd, says"** (v35) — Greek ὁ γραμματεύς, the word "scribe": at Ephesus the title of the chief officer of the city, who kept its records and brought business before the assembly; "town" is not in the Greek. καταστείλας, as v36; φησίν, a present.
- **"what man is there that knoweth not how that the city of the Ephesians is a worshipper of the great goddess Diana, and of the image which fell down from Jupiter" → "who then is there of men who does not know that the city of the Ephesians is temple keeper of the great Artemis and of that which fell from Zeus"** (v35) — Greek τίς γάρ ἐστιν ἀνθρώπων; νεωκόρον, "temple keeper" (ναός), a title of cities; "goddess" is Byzantine; τοῦ διοπετοῦς, "the thing fallen from Zeus" (or "from the sky"): no word "image" or "stone".
- **"cannot be spoken against, ye ought to be quiet, and to do nothing rashly" → "being undeniable, it is necessary for you to be calm and to do nothing rash"** (v36) — Greek ἀναντιρρήτων; δέον ἐστίν; κατεσταλμένους; πράσσειν.
- **"neither robbers of churches, nor yet blasphemers of your goddess" → "neither temple robbers nor blaspheming our goddess"** (v37) — Greek ἱεροσύλους; βλασφημοῦντας, a participle; τὴν θεὸν ἡμῶν (WH, Treg, NA28), "our", with the feminine article.
- **"have a matter against any man, the law is open, and there are deputies: let them implead one another" → "have a word against anyone, court days are held and there are proconsuls; let them bring charges against one another"** (v38) — Greek λόγον, "a word", a matter to bring; ἀγοραῖοι ἄγονται, "market [days] are held": the days on which the courts sat (ἀγοραῖος, 17:5); ἀνθύπατοι; ἐγκαλείτωσαν.
- **"if ye enquire any thing concerning other matters, it shall be determined in a lawful assembly" → "if you seek after anything further, it will be resolved in the lawful assembly"** (v39) — Greek περαιτέρω (WH, Treg, NA28); ἐπιζητεῖτε; ἐπιλυθήσεται, "will be loosed" (Mark 4:34 "explain"); ἐν τῇ ἐννόμῳ ἐκκλησίᾳ, with the article.
- **"we are in danger to be called in question for this day's uproar, there being no cause whereby we may give an account of this concourse" → "we are in danger of being charged with insurrection concerning today, there being no cause, concerning which we will not be able to render an account concerning this gathering"** (v40) — Greek ἐγκαλεῖσθαι στάσεως περὶ τῆς σήμερον: στάσις is "insurrection" (Mark 15:7; "discord" in 15:2); μηδενὸς αἰτίου ὑπάρχοντος περὶ οὗ οὐ δυνησόμεθα ἀποδοῦναι λόγον περὶ τῆς συστροφῆς ταύτης (WH, Treg, NA28). The Greek is hard: with the printed οὐ the last words say "about which we will not be able to render an account"; the relative can look back to "cause" or to the charge. The verse keeps the words in their order.
- **"he dismissed the assembly" → "he sent the assembly away"** (v41) — Greek ἀπέλυσεν, as 13:3; 15:30. In the Greek editions these words are the end of v40.
