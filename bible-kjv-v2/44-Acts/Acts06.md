# Acts 6

[1] Now in these days when the disciples were increasing in number, a complaint by the Hellenists arose against the ⟦hebrews|Ivrim|Hebrews⟧ because their widows were being neglected in the daily distribution.

[2] And the twelve summoned the full number of the disciples and said, "It is not right that we should give up preaching the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to serve tables.

[3] Therefore, brothers, pick out from among you seven men of good repute, full of the Spirit and of wisdom, whom we will appoint to this duty.

[4] But we will devote ourselves to prayer and to the ministry of the word."

[5] And what they said pleased the whole gathering, and they chose ⟦stephen|Stephanos|Stephen⟧, a man full of faith and of the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧, and ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧, and ⟦prochorus|Prochoros|Prochorus⟧, and ⟦nicanor|Nikanor|Nicanor⟧, and Timon, and Parmenas, and ⟦nicolaus|Nikolaos|Nicolaus⟧, a proselyte of ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧.

[6] These they set before the apostles, and they prayed and laid their hands on them.

[7] And the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ continued to increase, and the number of the disciples multiplied greatly in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and a great many of the priests became obedient to the faith.

[8] And ⟦stephen|Stephanos|Stephen⟧, full of grace and power, was doing great wonders and signs among the people.

[9] Then some of those who belonged to the synagogue of the Freedmen (as it was called), and of the ⟦cyrenians|Kyrenaioi|Cyrenians⟧, and of the ⟦alexandrians|Alexandreis|Alexandrians⟧, and of those from ⟦cilicia|Kilikia|Cilicia⟧ and Asia, rose up and disputed with ⟦stephen|Stephanos|Stephen⟧.

[10] But they could not withstand the wisdom and the Spirit with which he was speaking.

[11] Then they secretly instigated men who said, "We have heard him speak blasphemous words against ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[12] And they stirred up the people and the elders and the scribes, and they came upon him and seized him and brought him before the ⟦sanhedrin|Sanhedrin|council⟧,

[13] and they set up false witnesses who said, "This man never ceases to speak words against this holy place and the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧,

[14] for we have heard him say that this ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ of ⟦nazareth|Natzrat|Nazareth⟧ will destroy this place and will change the customs that ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ delivered to us."

[15] And gazing at him, all who sat in the ⟦sanhedrin|Sanhedrin|council⟧ saw that his face was like the face of an angel.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 7, 11) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Holy Ghost" / "the Spirit"** (v3, 5) — Greek πνεύματος ἁγίου *pneumatos hagiou*; rendered "*Ruach HaQodesh*" when referring to the Holy Spirit; consistent with Acts pattern.
- **"church" → "gathering" / congregation** — no direct church occurrence in this chapter; v5 references "the whole gathering" (implied church from context of ch. 5).
- **"Cyrene" / "*Cyrenians*"** (v9) — Greek Κυρηναῖοι *Kyrēnaioi*; first occurrence noted.
- **"Torah" / "the law"** (v13, 14) — Greek νόμον *nomon*; rendered "Torah" as it refers to Moses' instruction.
- **"customs" / "manner"** (v14) — Greek ἔθη *ethē* = "customs, traditions"; retained.
- **"Prochorus, Nicanor, Timon, Parmenas, Nicolas" → "Prochorus, Nicanor, Timon, Parmenas, Nicolaus"** (v5) — Greek Πρόχορος, Νικάνωρ, Τίμων, Παρμενᾶς, Νικόλαος; the familiar Latinized forms are kept, with "Nicolaus" matching the Greek *Nikolaos* more closely than KJV "Nicolas."
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** modernized throughout.
