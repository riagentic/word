# Acts 16

[1] ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ came also to Derbe and to Lystra. A disciple was there, named ⟦timothy|Timotheos|Timothy⟧, the son of a Jewish woman who was a believer, but his father was a ⟦greek|Yevani|Greek⟧.

[2] He was well spoken of by the brothers at Lystra and ⟦iconium|Ikonion|Iconium⟧.

[3] ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ wanted ⟦timothy|Timotheos|Timothy⟧ to accompany him, and he took him and circumcised him because of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who were in those places, for they all knew that his father was a ⟦greek|Yevani|Greek⟧.

[4] As they went on their way through the cities, they delivered to them for observance the decisions that had been reached by the apostles and elders who were in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[5] So the churches were strengthened in the faith, and they increased in numbers daily.

[6] And they went through the region of Phrygia and Galatia, having been forbidden by the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ to speak the word in Asia.

[7] And when they had come up to Mysia, they attempted to go into Bithynia, but the Spirit of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ did not allow them.

[8] So, passing by Mysia, they went down to Troas.

[9] And a vision appeared to ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ in the night: a man of ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧ was standing there, urging him and saying, "Come over to ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧ and help us."

[10] And when ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ had seen the vision, immediately we sought to go on into ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧, concluding that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had called us to preach the gospel to them.

[11] So, setting sail from Troas, we made a direct voyage to ⟦samothrace|Samothrake|Samothrace⟧, and the following day to Neapolis,

[12] and from there to ⟦philippi|Philippoi|Philippi⟧, which is a leading city of the district of ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧ and a Roman colony. We remained in this city some days.

[13] And on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ day we went outside the gate to the riverside, where we supposed there was a place of prayer, and we sat down and spoke to the women who had come together.

[14] One who heard us was a woman named Lydia, from the city of ⟦thyatira|Thyateira|Thyatira⟧, a seller of purple goods, who was a worshiper of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. The Lord opened her heart to pay attention to what was said by ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧.

[15] And after she was ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧, and her household as well, she urged us, saying, "If you have judged me to be faithful to the Lord, come to my house and stay." And she prevailed upon us.

[16] As we were going to the place of prayer, we were met by a slave girl who had a spirit of divination and brought her owners much gain by fortune-telling.

[17] She followed ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ and us, crying out, "These men are servants of the Most High ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who proclaim to you the way of salvation."

[18] And this she kept doing for many days. ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, having become greatly annoyed, turned and said to the spirit, "I command you in the name of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ to come out of her." And it came out that very hour.

[19] But when her owners saw that their hope of gain was gone, they seized ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ and ⟦silas|Sila|Silas⟧ and dragged them into the marketplace before the rulers.

[20] And when they had brought them to the magistrates, they said, "These men are ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, and they are disturbing our city.

[21] They advocate customs that are not lawful for us as Romans to accept or practice."

[22] The crowd joined in attacking them, and the magistrates tore the garments off them and gave orders to beat them with rods.

[23] And when they had inflicted many blows upon them, they threw them into prison, ordering the jailer to keep them safely.

[24] Having received this order, he put them into the inner prison and fastened their feet in the stocks.

[25] About midnight ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ and ⟦silas|Sila|Silas⟧ were praying and singing hymns to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and the prisoners were listening to them,

[26] and suddenly there was a great earthquake, so that the foundations of the prison were shaken. And immediately all the doors were opened, and everyone's bonds were unfastened.

[27] When the jailer woke and saw that the prison doors were open, he drew his sword and was about to kill himself, supposing that the prisoners had escaped.

[28] But ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ cried with a loud voice, "Do not harm yourself, for we are all here."

[29] And the jailer called for lights and rushed in, and trembling with fear he fell down before ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ and ⟦silas|Sila|Silas⟧.

[30] Then he brought them out and said, "Sirs, what must I do to be saved?"

[31] And they said, "**Believe in the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, and you will be saved, you and your household.**"

[32] And they spoke the word of the Lord to him and to all who were in his house.

[33] And he took them the same hour of the night and washed their wounds; and he was ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ at once, he and all his family.

[34] Then he brought them up into his house and set food before them. And he rejoiced along with his entire household that he had believed in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[35] But when it was day, the magistrates sent the police, saying, "Let those men go."

[36] And the jailer reported these words to ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, saying, "The magistrates have sent to let you go. Therefore come out now and go in peace."

[37] But ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ said to them, "They have beaten us publicly, uncondemned, men who are Roman citizens, and have thrown us into prison; and do they now throw us out secretly? No! Let them come themselves and take us out."

[38] The police reported these words to the magistrates, and they were afraid when they heard that they were Roman citizens.

[39] So they came and apologized to them. And they took them out and asked them to leave the city.

[40] So they went out of the prison and visited Lydia. And when they had seen the brothers, they encouraged them and departed.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v25, 34) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Holy Ghost" / "Spirit of Jesus"** (v6, 7) — Greek πνεύμα Ἰησοῦ *pneuma Iēsou* = "Spirit of *Yeshua*"; rendered "Spirit of *Yeshua*"; consistent with the *Ruach HaQodesh* policy.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v7, 18, 31) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v18) — Hebrew messianic title.
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (v15, 33) — Greek *baptizo* = "to immerse"; consistent rendering.
- **"Neapolis"** (v11) — Greek Νέαπολις *Neapolis* ("New City"); retained.
- **"spirit of divination" / "pythoness"** (v16) — Greek πνεῦμα πύθωνα *pneuma pythōna*; literally "python spirit" (from the Pythia/Delphi oracle cult); rendered "spirit of divination" as clearest modern equivalent.
- **"Believe in the Lord Jesus Christ"** (v31) — rendered "Believe in the Lord *Yeshua*"; consistent with name policy.
- **Roman citizenship** (v37–38) — significant legal detail; *civis Romanus* rights preserved in context.
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** modernized throughout.
