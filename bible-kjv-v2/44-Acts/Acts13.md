# Acts 13

[1] Now there were in ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧, in the church that was there, prophets and teachers: ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ and ⟦simeon-nt|Shimon|Simeon⟧ who was called Niger, and Lucius of ⟦cyrene|Kyrene|Cyrene⟧, and ⟦manaen|Menachem|Manaen⟧, one brought up with ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ the tetrarch, and ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧.

[2] And as they were ministering to the Lord and fasting, the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ said, "Set apart for me now ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ and ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ for the work to which I have called them."

[3] Then, having fasted and prayed and laid the hands on them, they sent them away.

[4] So they, sent out by the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧, went down to ⟦seleucia|Seleukeia|Seleucia⟧, and from there they sailed away to ⟦cyprus|Kypros|Cyprus⟧,

[5] and being in Salamis they proclaimed the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in the synagogues of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧; and they also had ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ as assistant.

[6] And having gone through the whole island as far as Paphos, they found a certain man, a magician, a false prophet, a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧, whose name was ⟦bar-jesus|Bar-Yeshua|Bar-Jesus⟧,

[7] who was with the proconsul Sergius Paulus, an understanding man. This man, having called to himself ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ and ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, sought to hear the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[8] but Elymas the magician — for so is his name translated — resisted them, seeking to pervert the proconsul from the faith.

[9] But ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, who is also ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, filled with ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧, having gazed at him,

[10] said, "O full of all guile and all villainy, son of the devil, enemy of all righteousness, will you not cease perverting the straight ways of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧?

[11] And now behold, the hand of the Lord is upon you, and you will be blind, not seeing the sun until a time." And instantly there fell upon him a mist and darkness, and going about he was seeking people to lead him by the hand.

[12] Then the proconsul, having seen what had happened, believed, being astonished at the teaching of the Lord.

[13] And having put out to sea from Paphos, those around ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ came to ⟦perga|Perge|Perga⟧ of Pamphylia; but ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, having withdrawn from them, returned to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[14] But they, having gone through from ⟦perga|Perge|Perga⟧, arrived at Pisidian ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧, and having entered into the synagogue on the day of the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ they sat down.

[15] And after the reading of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ and the Prophets the synagogue rulers sent to them, saying, "Men, brothers, if there is among you any word of encouragement to the people, speak."

[16] And ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, having risen and motioned with the hand, said:

"Men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and you who fear ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, hear.

[17] The ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of this people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ chose our fathers, and lifted up the people in the dwelling as aliens in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and with a high arm he led them out of it,

[18] and for about a forty-year time he bore with their ways in the wilderness,

[19] and having pulled down seven nations in the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, he gave their land as an inheritance,

[20] in about four hundred and fifty years. And after these things he gave judges until ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ the prophet.

[21] And from then they asked for a king, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave them ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ the son of ⟦kish|Qish|Kish⟧, a man of the tribe of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, for forty years;

[22] and having removed him, he raised up David for them as king, to whom he also said, testifying, '**I found David the son of ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧, a man according to my heart, who will do all my wills.**'

[23] From this man's seed ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, according to promise, brought to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ a Savior, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧,

[24] ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ having proclaimed beforehand, before the face of his entering in, ⟦immersion|an immersion|a baptism⟧ of repentance to all the people of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[25] And as ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ was fulfilling the course, he was saying, 'What do you suppose me to be? I am not he. But behold, there comes after me one of whom I am not worthy to loose the sandal of the feet.'

[26] "Men, brothers, sons of the race of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, and those among you who fear ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to us the word of this salvation was sent out.

[27] For those dwelling in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and their rulers, being ignorant of this one and of the voices of the prophets that are read every ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, by judging him fulfilled them,

[28] and having found no cause of death, they asked ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ that he be done away with;

[29] and when they had finished all the things written about him, having taken him down from the tree they laid him in a tomb.

[30] But ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ raised him from the dead;

[31] who was seen for many days by those who had come up with him from ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧ to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, who are now his witnesses to the people.

[32] And we preach to you the promise that was made to the fathers,

[33] that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has fulfilled this to our children, having raised up ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, as also in the second psalm it is written,

"'**You are my Son,
I today have begotten you.**'

[34] And that he raised him up from the dead, no longer about to return to corruption, he has spoken thus: '**I will give to you the devout things of David, the faithful ones.**'

[35] Because he says also in another psalm, '**You will not give your devout one to see corruption.**'

[36] For David indeed, having served his own generation by the counsel of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, fell asleep and was added to his fathers and saw corruption,

[37] but he whom ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ raised did not see corruption.

[38] Let it therefore be known to you, men, brothers, that **through this one forgiveness of sins is proclaimed to you, and from all things from which you could not be justified in ⟦moses|Moshe's|Moses'⟧ ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧,**

**[39] in this one everyone who believes is justified**.

[40] See therefore that what is said in the Prophets does not come:

[41] "'**See, you despisers,
and marvel and vanish away,
because I work a work in your days,
a work which you will by no means believe if someone relates it to you.**'"

[42] And as they were going out, they were appealing that these words be spoken to them on the next ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧.

[43] And when the synagogue had broken up, many of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and of the revering proselytes followed ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ and ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧, who, speaking to them, were persuading them to remain with the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[44] And on the coming ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ almost the whole city was gathered together to hear the word of the Lord.

[45] But the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, seeing the crowds, were filled with jealousy and were contradicting the things spoken by ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, blaspheming.

[46] And ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ and ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧, speaking boldly, said, "To you it was necessary that the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ be spoken first; since you thrust it away and judge yourselves not worthy of the eternal life, behold, we turn to the nations.

[47] For thus the Lord has given us commandment,

"'**I have set you for a light of nations,
for you to be for salvation unto the end of the earth.**'"

[48] And the nations, hearing, were rejoicing and glorifying the word of the Lord, and as many as were set in place unto eternal life believed;

[49] and the word of the Lord was being carried through the whole country.

[50] But the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ incited the revering women of high standing and the first men of the city, and raised up a persecution against ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ and ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧, and cast them out from their borders.

[51] But they, having shaken off the dust of the feet against them, came to ⟦iconium|Ikonion|Iconium⟧,

[52] and the disciples were being filled with joy and with ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v23, 33) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** — no direct occurrence in narrative; "Jesus" referred to as such in sermon.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v2, 4, 9, 52) — consistent rendering. In v9 and v52 the Greek has no article (πνεύματος ἁγίου).
- **"baptized / baptism" → "immersed / immersion"** (v24) — Greek *baptizo* = "to immerse"; consistent rendering.
- **"Torah" / reading "the law and the prophets"** (v15) — Greek νόμου καὶ προφητῶν; "the Torah and the Prophets" — standard synagogue reading order preserved.
- **"Simeon / Niger"** (v1) — Greek Συμεών *Symeōn*, transliterating Hebrew שִׁמְעוֹן *Shimon*, called *Niger* ("black" in Latin); the form "Simeon" is kept in the text.
- **"Bar-Jesus" → "*Bar-Yeshua*"** (v6) — Aramaic בַּר יֵשׁוּעַ *Bar-Yeshua* ("son of *Yeshua*/salvation"); first occurrence gives Aramaic form.
- **"in the church that was at Antioch" → "in Antioch, in the church that was there"** (v1) — Greek ἐν Ἀντιοχείᾳ κατὰ τὴν οὖσαν ἐκκλησίαν. "Which had been brought up with" kept as "one brought up with": σύντροφος, a foster brother or childhood companion; not "lifelong friend".
- **"ministered" kept** (v2) — Greek λειτουργούντων (λειτουργία "ministry"), not a word for "worship". "Now" is δή.
- **"sent them away" kept** (v3) — Greek ἀπέλυσαν.
- **"preached" → "proclaimed"; "to their minister" → "as assistant"** (v5) — Greek κατήγγελλον; ὑπηρέτην, as 1 Cor 4:1.
- **"a certain sorcerer, a false prophet, a Jew" → "a certain man, a magician, a false prophet, a *Yehudi*"** (v6) — Greek ἄνδρα τινὰ μάγον ψευδοπροφήτην Ἰουδαῖον: four nouns side by side; μάγος is the word of Matt 2:1.
- **"a prudent man" → "an understanding man"; "desired" → "sought"** (v7) — Greek συνετῷ; ἐπεζήτησεν.
- **"by interpretation" → "translated"; "withstood" → "resisted"** (v8) — Greek μεθερμηνεύεται; ἀνθίστατο.
- **"to turn away" → "to pervert"; "pervert" kept** (v8, 10) — Greek διαστρέψαι, διαστρέφων: one verb, "twist, turn aside", against "straight" (εὐθείας).
- **"who also is called Paul" → "who is also Paul"** (v9) — Greek ὁ καὶ Παῦλος; no "called".
- **"subtilty" → "guile"; "mischief" → "villainy"** (v10) — Greek δόλου, ῥᾳδιουργίας; the Greek order of the address is kept.
- **"the right ways of the Lord" → "the straight ways of the Lord"** (v10) — τὰς ὁδοὺς τοῦ κυρίου, with the article (WH, NA28); Treg, RP and the printed SBLGNT have no τοῦ. No difference in English.
- **"for a season" → "until a time"; "immediately" → "instantly"** (v11) — Greek ἄχρι καιροῦ; παραχρῆμα.
- **"doctrine" → "teaching"** (v12) — Greek διδαχῇ.
- **"Paul and his company loosed from Paphos" → "having put out to sea from Paphos, those around Paul"** (v13) — Greek οἱ περὶ Παῦλον, Paul and those with him. "Departing" → "having withdrawn": ἀποχωρήσας.
- **"Antioch in Pisidia" → "Pisidian Antioch"** (v14) — Greek Ἀντιόχειαν τὴν Πισιδίαν (WH, Treg, NA28), an adjective; RP "of Pisidia".
- **"Ye men and brethren" → "Men, brothers"** (v15, 26, 38) — Greek Ἄνδρες ἀδελφοί. "Exhortation" → "encouragement": παρακλήσεως, as Heb 13:22. "Rulers of the synagogue" is one word, ἀρχισυνάγωγοι.
- **"give audience" → "hear"** (v16) — Greek ἀκούσατε.
- **"exalted" → "lifted up"; "when they dwelt as strangers" → "in the dwelling as aliens"** (v17) — Greek ὕψωσεν, the verb of John 3:14 ("exalt"), and ὑψηλοῦ "high" is its adjective; ἐν τῇ παροικίᾳ.
- **"suffered he their manners" → "he bore with their ways"** (v18) — Greek ἐτροποφόρησεν, "bore their ways (τρόπος)", as all four editions print.
- **"destroyed" → "pulled down"; "divided their land to them by lot" → "gave their land as an inheritance"** (v19) — Greek καθελών, the verb of v29 ("taken down"); κατεκληρονόμησεν (WH, Treg, NA28), without "to them".
- **"about the space of four hundred and fifty years" → "in about four hundred and fifty years"** (v19–20) — in the critical text (WH, Treg, NA28) the years stand before "And after these things" and belong to v17–19; in the Byzantine text they stand after it and count the time of the judges (KJV).
- **"desired" → "asked for"; "by the space of forty years" → "for forty years"** (v21) — Greek ᾐτήσαντο; ἔτη τεσσεράκοντα.
- **"to whom also he gave testimony, and said" kept as "to whom he also said, testifying"** (v22) — Greek ᾧ καὶ εἶπεν μαρτυρήσας. "After mine own heart" → "according to my heart": κατὰ τὴν καρδίαν μου. "All my will" → "all my wills": πάντα τὰ θελήματά μου, plural, as Eph 2:3.
- **"hath God … raised unto Israel a Saviour, Jesus" → "God … brought to Israel a Savior, *Yeshua*"** (v23) — Greek ἤγαγεν (critical text); "seed" is σπέρματος. RP reads σωτηρίαν, "salvation".
- **"before his coming" → "before the face of his entering in"** (v24) — Greek πρὸ προσώπου τῆς εἰσόδου αὐτοῦ, a Hebrew turn of phrase; προκηρύξαντος is "having proclaimed beforehand".
- **"Whom think ye that I am?" → "What do you suppose me to be?"** (v25) — Greek Τί ἐμέ (WH, Treg, NA28). The words can also be read as one sentence: "What you suppose me to be, I am not." "He" after "I am not" is supplied. "Shoes" → "sandal": τὸ ὑπόδημα, singular.
- **"stock" → "race"; "to you" → "to us"** (v26) — Greek γένους; ἡμῖν (WH, NA28, SBLGNT), Treg and RP ὑμῖν "to you". "Was sent out": ἐξαπεστάλη, aorist.
- **"because they knew him not … they have fulfilled them in condemning him" → "being ignorant of this one and of the voices of the prophets … by judging him fulfilled them"** (v27) — Greek τοῦτον ἀγνοήσαντες καὶ τὰς φωνὰς … κρίναντες ἐπλήρωσαν. "Him" and "them" are supplied; κρίνω is "judge", not "condemn"; no word "understand".
- **"no cause of death" kept; "that he should be slain" → "that he be done away with"** (v28) — Greek αἰτίαν θανάτου; ἀναιρεθῆναι, as 12:2.
- **"fulfilled" → "finished"; "sepulchre" → "tomb"** (v29) — Greek ἐτέλεσαν (not πληρόω, v27); μνημεῖον.
- **"he was seen" kept** (v31) — Greek ὤφθη.
- **"we declare unto you glad tidings, how that the promise" → "we preach to you the promise"** (v32) — Greek εὐαγγελιζόμεθα with the promise as object: "bring as good news".
- **"unto us their children" → "to our children"** (v33) — τοῖς τέκνοις ἡμῶν (WH, Treg, SBLGNT); NA28 and RP read αὐτῶν ἡμῖν, "to us, their children". "Raised up" is ἀναστήσας, which can mean bringing *Yeshua* onto the scene (as v22 of David, with another verb) or raising him from the dead (v34). Treg reads "in the first psalm".
- **"the sure mercies of David" → "the devout things of David, the faithful ones"** (v34) — Greek τὰ ὅσια Δαυὶδ τὰ πιστά, the Greek of Isa 55:3 as printed (Hebrew חַסְדֵי דָוִד, "the steadfast loves of David"); ὅσιος is "devout", the word of v35.
- **"Wherefore" → "Because"; "thine Holy One" → "your devout one"; "suffer" → "give"** (v35) — Greek διότι; τὸν ὅσιόν σου (not ἅγιος "holy"); δώσεις, the verb of v34. "Psalm" is supplied: Greek "in another".
- **"after he had served his own generation by the will of God" → "having served his own generation by the counsel of God"** (v36) — Greek ἰδίᾳ γενεᾷ ὑπηρετήσας τῇ τοῦ θεοῦ βουλῇ. The two datives can be taken three ways: "served his own generation by the counsel of God"; "in his own generation served the counsel of God"; or "by the counsel of God fell asleep". "Laid unto" → "added to": προσετέθη, as 11:24.
- **"raised again" → "raised"** (v37) — Greek ἤγειρεν.
- **"this man" → "this one"; "preached" → "proclaimed"** (v38) — Greek διὰ τούτου; καταγγέλλεται.
- **"by him all that believe are justified from all things, from which ye could not be justified by the law of Moses" → "from all things from which you could not be justified in Moses' law, in this one everyone who believes is justified"** (v38–39) — Greek order and verse division kept; ἐν νόμῳ Μωϋσέως has no article; ἐν is "in" both times; δικαιόω is "justify", not "free".
- **"Beware" → "See"; "come upon you" → "come"** (v40) — Greek βλέπετε; "upon you" is only in the Byzantine text.
- **"Behold, ye despisers, and wonder, and perish" → "See, you despisers, and marvel and vanish away"** (v41) — Greek Ἴδετε, θαυμάσατε, ἀφανίσθητε (as Jas 4:14); Hab 1:5 as the Greek prints it. "In no wise": οὐ μή. "Relates" is ἐκδιηγῆται, "relates in full".
- **"the Gentiles besought" → "they were appealing"** (v42) — the subject is not named (WH, Treg, NA28); "the Gentiles" and "out of the synagogue of the Jews" are Byzantine. "Words" is ῥήματα.
- **"religious proselytes" → "the revering proselytes"** (v43, also v50 "the revering women") — Greek σεβομένων, "revering" (God is understood); no word "Judaism". "Continue in" → "remain with": προσμένειν, as 11:23.
- **"envy" → "jealousy"; "contradicting and blaspheming" → "contradicting …, blaspheming"** (v45) — Greek ζήλου (the word for "zeal"); βλασφημοῦντες has no object ("him" is not in the Greek); the second "contradicting" is Byzantine.
- **"waxed bold" → "speaking boldly"; "put it from you" → "thrust it away"; "Gentiles" → "nations"** (v46–48) — Greek παρρησιασάμενοι; ἀπωθεῖσθε; τὰ ἔθνη.
- **"hath the Lord commanded us" → "the Lord has given us commandment"** (v47) — Greek ἐντέταλται, the verb of ἐντολή "commandment".
- **"that thou shouldest be for salvation unto the ends of the earth" → "for you to be for salvation unto the end of the earth"** (v47) — Greek τοῦ εἶναί σε εἰς σωτηρίαν ἕως ἐσχάτου τῆς γῆς (Isa 49:6); no "bring"; ἐσχάτου is singular.
- **"ordained" → "set in place"** (v48) — Greek ἦσαν τεταγμένοι (τάσσω, as Rom 13:1): "set, ranked, appointed". The form can be passive ("had been set") or middle ("had set themselves"); the verse keeps the passive form.
- **"was published throughout all the region" → "was being carried through the whole country"** (v49) — Greek διεφέρετο; χώρας.
- **"honourable" → "of high standing"; "chief men" → "first men"; "expelled them out of their coasts" → "cast them out from their borders"** (v50) — Greek εὐσχήμονας; πρώτους; ἐξέβαλον … ἀπὸ τῶν ὁρίων.
