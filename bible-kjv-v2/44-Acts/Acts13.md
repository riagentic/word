[1] Now there were in the church at ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧ prophets and teachers: ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧, ⟦simeon-nt|Shimon|Simeon⟧ called Niger, Lucius of ⟦cyrene|Kyrene|Cyrene⟧, ⟦manaen|Menachem|Manaen⟧ a lifelong friend of ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ the tetrarch, and ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧.

[7] He was with the proconsul, Sergius Paulus, a man of intelligence, who summoned ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ and ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and sought to hear the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[9] But ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, who was also called ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, filled with the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧, looked intently at him

[11] And now, behold, the hand of the Lord is upon you, and you will be blind and unable to see the sun for a time." Immediately mist and darkness fell upon him, and he went about seeking people to lead him by the hand.

[12] Then the proconsul believed, when he saw what had occurred, for he was astonished at the teaching of the Lord.

[13] Now ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ and his companions set sail from Paphos and came to ⟦perga|Perge|Perga⟧ in Pamphylia. And ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ left them and returned to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,

[15] After the reading from the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ and the Prophets, the rulers of the synagogue sent a message to them, saying, "Brothers, if you have any word of encouragement for the people, say it."

[16] So ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ stood up, and motioning with his hand said:

"Men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and you who fear ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, listen.

[26] "Brothers, sons of the family of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, and those among you who fear ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to us has been sent the message of this salvation.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v23, 33) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** — no direct occurrence in narrative; "Jesus" referred to as such in sermon.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v2, 4, 9, 52) — consistent rendering.
- **"baptized / baptism" → "immersed / immersion"** (v24) — Greek *baptizo* = "to immerse"; consistent rendering.
- **"Torah" / reading "the law and the prophets"** (v15) — Greek νόμου καὶ προφητῶν; "the Torah and the Prophets" — standard synagogue reading order preserved.
- **"Simeon / Niger"** (v1) — Greek Συμεών *Symeōn*, transliterating Hebrew שִׁמְעוֹן *Shimon*, called *Niger* ("black" in Latin); the form "Simeon" is kept in the text.
- **"Bar-Jesus" → "*Bar-Yeshua*"** (v6) — Aramaic בַּר יֵשׁוּעַ *Bar-Yeshua* ("son of *Yeshua*/salvation"); first occurrence gives Aramaic form.
- **OT quotations documented:**
  - v22: Ps 89:20 + 1 Sam 13:14 ("a man after my heart")
  - v33: Ps 2:7 ("You are my Son, today I have begotten you")
  - v34: Isa 55:3 ("holy and sure blessings of David")
  - v35: Ps 16:10 ("You will not let your Holy One see corruption")
  - v41: Hab 1:5 ("Look, you scoffers…")
  - v47: Isa 49:6 ("I have made you a light for the Gentiles")
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** modernized throughout.
