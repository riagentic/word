# Acts 1

[1] The first account I made, O ⟦theophilus|Theophilos|Theophilus⟧, concerning all things that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ began both to do and to teach,

[2] until the day when he was taken up, having given commandment through ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧ to the apostles whom he had chosen;

[3] to whom he also presented himself living after he suffered, in many proofs, being seen by them through forty days and speaking the things concerning the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[4] And eating together with them, he charged them not to depart from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, but to wait for the promise of the Father, "which you heard from me;

[5] for ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ indeed ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ with water, but you will be ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ in ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧ not many days after these."

[6] So then those who had come together were asking him, saying, "Lord, do you at this time restore the kingdom to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?"

[7] But he said to them, "It is not yours to know times or seasons which the Father set in his own authority,

[8] **but you will receive power when the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ has come upon you, and you will be my witnesses both in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and in all ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ and ⟦samaria-nt|Shomron|Samaria⟧, and unto the end of the earth.**"

[9] And having said these things, as they were looking, he was lifted up, and a cloud took him up from their eyes.

[10] And as they were gazing into heaven while he was going, behold, two men were standing by them in white apparel,

[11] who also said, "Men of ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧, why do you stand looking into heaven? **This ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, who was taken up from you into heaven, will come thus, in the manner in which you beheld him going into heaven.**"

[12] Then they returned to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ from the mount called ⟦mount-of-olives|Har HaZeitim|Olivet⟧, which is near ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, a ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧'s journey away.

[13] And when they had entered, they went up into the upper room where they were staying: both ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ and ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ and ⟦andrew|Andreas|Andrew⟧, ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧ and ⟦thomas|Toma|Thomas⟧, ⟦bartholomew|Bar-Talmai|Bartholomew⟧ and ⟦matthew|Mattai|Matthew⟧, ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ the son of ⟦alphaeus|Alphaios|Alphaeus⟧ and ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ the Zealot and ⟦jude|Yehudah|Judas⟧ the son of ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧.

[14] These all were persevering with one accord in prayer, with women and ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ the mother of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, and with his brothers.

[15] And in these days ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, rising in the midst of the brothers, said (and there was a crowd of names together, about a hundred and twenty):

[16] "Men, brothers, the Scripture had to be fulfilled, which the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ spoke beforehand through the mouth of David concerning ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧, who became a guide to those who apprehended ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧,

[17] for he was numbered among us and obtained the lot of this service."

[18] (Now this one acquired a plot of land out of the wage of unrighteousness, and falling headlong he burst in the middle, and all his inward parts were poured out.

[19] And it became known to all those dwelling in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, so that that plot was called in their own language ⟦akeldama|Chaqel Dema|Akeldama⟧, that is, Plot of Blood.)

[20] "For it is written in the book of Psalms,

'**Let his homestead become desolate,
and let there be no one dwelling in it**';

and

'**His overseership let another take**.'

[21] Therefore, of the men who went with us in all the time in which the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ went in and went out among us,

[22] beginning from the ⟦immersion|immersion|baptism⟧ of ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ until the day when he was taken up from us, one of these must become with us a witness of his resurrection."

[23] And they put forward two, ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ called ⟦barsabbas|Bar-Sabba|Barsabbas⟧, who was called Justus, and ⟦matthias|Mattityahu|Matthias⟧.

[24] And praying they said, "You, Lord, knower of the hearts of all, show the one whom you chose out of these two

[25] to take the place of this service and apostleship, from which ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧ turned aside to go to his own place."

[26] And they gave lots for them, and the lot fell upon ⟦matthias|Mattityahu|Matthias⟧, and he was counted in with the eleven apostles.

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v13) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v2, 5, 8, 16) — Hebrew רוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ *Ruach HaQodesh* ("Spirit of Holiness"); archaic "Ghost" replaced throughout Acts. In v2 and v5 the Greek has no article (πνεύματος ἁγίου, ἐν πνεύματι … ἁγίῳ); in v8 and v16 it has.
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (v5) — Greek *baptizo* = "to immerse"; systematic rendering throughout Acts. ὕδατι is a plain dative ("with water"); ἐν πνεύματι is "in".
- **"The former treatise have I made" → "The first account I made"** (v1) — Greek Τὸν μὲν πρῶτον λόγον ἐποιησάμην; λόγος is "word", here a written account. The μέν has no answering "but": the sentence runs on into the narrative.
- **"after that he through the Holy Ghost had given commandments unto the apostles whom he had chosen" → "having given commandment through *Ruach HaQodesh* to the apostles whom he had chosen"** (v2) — Greek ἐντειλάμενος τοῖς ἀποστόλοις διὰ πνεύματος ἁγίου οὓς ἐξελέξατο. "Through *Ruach HaQodesh*" stands between "the apostles" and "whom he had chosen" and can go with "given commandment" or with "chosen".
- **"shewed himself alive after his passion by many infallible proofs" → "presented himself living after he suffered, in many proofs"** (v3) — Greek παρέστησεν ἑαυτὸν ζῶντα μετὰ τὸ παθεῖν αὐτὸν ἐν πολλοῖς τεκμηρίοις. τεκμήριον is a sure, convincing proof; the Greek has no separate adjective. ὀπτανόμενος "being seen" (only here in the New Testament); δι' ἡμερῶν τεσσεράκοντα "through forty days", that is, at intervals during them.
- **"being assembled together with them" → "eating together with them"** (v4) — Greek συναλιζόμενος, a rare word: "eating (salt) with" (so the old Latin, Syriac and Coptic versions; compare 10:41), or "being gathered with". "Staying with" renders a different spelling (συναυλιζόμενος) that the printed text does not have.
- **"commanded" → "charged"; "depart" kept** (v4) — Greek παρήγγειλεν; χωρίζεσθαι is "be separated from", the verb of 1 Cor 7:10.
- **"which, saith he, ye have heard of me" → "which you heard from me"** (v4) — the Greek passes from reported to direct speech with no "he said".
- **"not many days hence" → "not many days after these"** (v5) — Greek οὐ μετὰ πολλὰς ταύτας ἡμέρας.
- **"wilt thou at this time restore again" → "do you at this time restore"** (v6) — Greek εἰ … ἀποκαθιστάνεις, a present tense; ἠρώτων "were asking" is imperfect.
- **"which the Father hath put in his own power" → "which the Father set in his own authority"** (v7) — Greek ἔθετο ἐν τῇ ἰδίᾳ ἐξουσίᾳ: "placed within his own authority" or "fixed by his own authority". ἐξουσία "authority" is not the δύναμις "power" of v8.
- **"unto the uttermost part of the earth" → "unto the end of the earth"** (v8) — Greek ἕως ἐσχάτου τῆς γῆς, the wording of Isa 49:6 in the Septuagint.
- **"a cloud received him out of their sight" → "a cloud took him up from their eyes"** (v9) — Greek ὑπέλαβεν … ἀπὸ τῶν ὀφθαλμῶν αὐτῶν. "Was lifted up" is ἐπήρθη; "taken up" (v2, 11, 22) is ἀναλαμβάνω.
- **"shall so come in like manner as ye have seen him go" → "will come thus, in the manner in which you beheld him going"** (v11) — Greek οὕτως ἐλεύσεται ὃν τρόπον ἐθεάσασθε; θεάομαι "behold", beside βλέπω "look" in v9 and v11.
- **"a sabbath day's journey" → "a *Shabbat*'s journey away"** (v12) — Greek σαββάτου ἔχον ὁδόν, "having a *Shabbat*'s way"; no word "day". The mount is named Ἐλαιών, "Olive Grove".
- **"James the son of Alphaeus … Judas the brother of James" → "the son of" twice** (v13) — the Greek has only a genitive both times (Ἰάκωβος Ἁλφαίου, Ἰούδας Ἰακώβου): "*Yaaqov* of *Alphaios*", "*Yehudah* of *Yaaqov*". "The son of" is the ordinary sense and is supplied.
- **"continued with one accord in prayer and supplication, with the women" → "were persevering with one accord in prayer, with women"** (v14) — Greek προσκαρτεροῦντες ὁμοθυμαδὸν τῇ προσευχῇ σὺν γυναιξίν. "The prayer" has the article; "women" has none (it can also mean "wives"). "And supplication" is the Byzantine reading (RP); the four critical editions omit it.
- **"the number of names together were about an hundred and twenty" → "there was a crowd of names together, about a hundred and twenty"** (v15) — Greek ὄχλος ὀνομάτων ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό; "names" stands for persons. ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό is "at the same place, together" (2:1, 44, 47).
- **"Men and brethren" → "Men, brothers"** (v16) — Greek Ἄνδρες ἀδελφοί.
- **"them that took Jesus" → "those who apprehended *Yeshua*"** (v16) — Greek συλλαβοῦσιν.
- **"had obtained part of this ministry" → "obtained the lot of this service"** (v17) — Greek ἔλαχεν τὸν κλῆρον τῆς διακονίας ταύτης; λαγχάνω is "obtain by lot", κλῆρος the "lot" of v26. διακονία is "service" (also v25).
- **"with the reward of iniquity" → "out of the wage of unrighteousness"** (v18) — Greek ἐκ μισθοῦ τῆς ἀδικίας; no "his".
- **"field … The field of blood" → "plot of land … Plot of Blood"** (v18, 19) — Greek χωρίον, a piece of land (also 4:34; 5:3, 8); Matt 27:8 has ἀγρός, "Field of Blood".
- **"falling headlong" kept** (v18) — Greek πρηνὴς γενόμενος, "becoming prone"; some take πρηνής here as "swollen".
- **"all his bowels gushed out" → "all his inward parts were poured out"** (v18) — Greek ἐξεχύθη πάντα τὰ σπλάγχνα αὐτοῦ, here of the body itself.
- **"in their proper tongue" → "in their own language"** (v19) — Greek τῇ ἰδίᾳ διαλέκτῳ αὐτῶν; "tongue" is γλῶσσα (2:4).
- **"Let his habitation be desolate" → "Let his homestead become desolate"** (v20) — Greek ἡ ἔπαυλις αὐτοῦ, a farmstead or dwelling, as the Septuagint of Ps 69:25 (LXX 68:26). The Hebrew of the psalm has "their encampment" (טִירָתָם), plural; the verse gives the Greek as printed.
- **"his bishoprick let another take" → "His overseership let another take"** (v20) — Greek τὴν ἐπισκοπὴν αὐτοῦ, the charge of an overseer (ἐπίσκοπος); Ps 109:8 in the Septuagint (LXX 108:8).
- **"went in and out among us" → "went in and went out among us"** (v21) — Greek εἰσῆλθεν καὶ ἐξῆλθεν ἐφ' ἡμᾶς, literally "upon us" / "over us".
- **"must one be ordained to be a witness with us" → "one of these must become with us a witness"** (v22) — Greek δεῖ … μάρτυρα … γενέσθαι; no "ordained".
- **"they appointed two" → "they put forward two"; "who was surnamed Justus" → "who was called Justus"** (v23) — Greek ἔστησαν, "made to stand"; ἐπεκλήθη, "was called in addition", beside καλούμενον "called"; no "also".
- **"which knowest the hearts of all men" → "knower of the hearts of all"** (v24) — Greek καρδιογνῶστα πάντων, one noun (also 15:8).
- **"that he may take part of this ministry … from which Judas by transgression fell" → "to take the place of this service … from which *Yehudah* turned aside"** (v25) — Greek τὸν τόπον (the four critical editions; κλῆρον "lot" RP); παρέβη, "stepped aside, transgressed", the verb of παράβασις "transgression".
- **"they gave forth their lots" → "they gave lots for them"** (v26) — Greek ἔδωκαν κλήρους αὐτοῖς, "for them" or "to them" (αὐτῶν "their" RP).
- **"he was numbered with" → "he was counted in with"** (v26) — Greek συγκατεψηφίσθη, "was voted in together with"; a different verb from "numbered" in v17 (κατηριθμημένος).
- **v20** — OT quotations: Ps 69:25 ("Let his homestead become desolate…") + Ps 109:8 ("His overseership let another take").

### Notes
- **v20** quotes Ps 69:25 + 109:8.
- **v17–20** — the printed Greek marks v18–19 as an aside with dashes; the speech goes on in v20.
