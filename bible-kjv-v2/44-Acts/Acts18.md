# Acts 18

[1] After this ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ left ⟦athens|Athenai|Athens⟧ and went to ⟦corinth|Korinthos|Corinth⟧.

[2] And he found a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ named Aquila, a native of ⟦pontus|Pontos|Pontus⟧, recently come from ⟦italy|Italia|Italy⟧ with his wife Priscilla, because Claudius had commanded all the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ to leave ⟦rome|Roma|Rome⟧. And he went to see them,

[3] and because he was of the same trade he stayed with them and worked, for they were tentmakers by trade.

[4] And he reasoned in the synagogue every ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, and tried to persuade ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and ⟦greeks|Yevanim|Greeks⟧.

[5] When ⟦silas|Sila|Silas⟧ and ⟦timothy|Timotheos|Timothy⟧ arrived from ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧, ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ was occupied with the word, testifying to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ that the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧ was ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[6] And when they opposed and reviled him, he shook out his garments and said to them, "Your blood be on your own heads! I am innocent. From now on I will go to the Gentiles."

[7] And he left there and went to the house of a man named Titius Justus, a worshiper of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. His house was next door to the synagogue.

[8] Crispus, the ruler of the synagogue, believed in the Lord, together with his entire household. And many of the ⟦corinthians|Korinthioi|Corinthians⟧ hearing ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ believed and were ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧.

[9] And the Lord said to ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ one night in a vision, "Do not be afraid, but go on speaking and do not be silent,

[10] for I am with you, and no one will attack you to harm you, for I have many in this city who are my people."

[11] And he stayed a year and six months, teaching the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ among them.

[12] But when Gallio was proconsul of Achaia, the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ made a united attack on ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ and brought him before the tribunal,

[13] saying, "This man is persuading people to worship ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ contrary to the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧."

[14] But when ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ was about to open his mouth, Gallio said to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, "If it were a matter of wrongdoing or vicious crime, O ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, I would have reason to accept your complaint.

[15] But since it is a matter of questions about words and names and your own ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, see to it yourselves. I refuse to be a judge of these things."

[16] And he drove them from the tribunal.

[17] And they all seized Sosthenes, the ruler of the synagogue, and beat him in front of the tribunal. But Gallio paid no attention to any of this.

[18] After this, ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ stayed many days longer and then took leave of the brothers and set sail for Syria, and with him Priscilla and Aquila. At ⟦cenchreae|Kenchreai|Cenchreae⟧ he had cut his hair, for he was under a vow.

[19] And they came to ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧, and he left them there, but he himself went into the synagogue and reasoned with the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧.

[20] When they asked him to stay for a longer period, he declined.

[21] But on taking leave of them he said, "I will return to you if ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ wills," and he set sail from ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧.

[22] When he had landed at Caesarea, he went up and greeted the church, and then went down to ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧.

[23] After spending some time there, he departed and went from one place to the next through the region of Galatia and Phrygia, strengthening all the disciples.

[24] Now a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ named Apollos, a native of ⟦alexandria|Alexandreia|Alexandria⟧, came to ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧. He was an eloquent man, competent in the Scriptures.

[25] He had been instructed in the way of the Lord. And being fervent in spirit, he spoke and taught accurately the things concerning ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, though he knew only the ⟦immersion|immersion|baptism⟧ of ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧.

[26] He began to speak boldly in the synagogue, but when Priscilla and Aquila heard him, they took him aside and explained to him the way of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ more accurately.

[27] And when he wished to cross to Achaia, the brothers encouraged him and wrote to the disciples to welcome him. When he arrived, he greatly helped those who through grace had believed,

[28] for he powerfully refuted the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ in public, showing by the Scriptures that the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧ was ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v21) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v5, 28) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v5, 28) — Hebrew messianic title.
- **"Holy Ghost" / Spirit** — no explicit *Ruach HaQodesh* occurrence in this chapter.
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (v8) — Greek *baptizo* = "to immerse"; consistent rendering.
- **"John's baptism" → "the immersion of John"** (v25) — consistent rendering.
- **"Aquila" / "*Aquila*"** (v2) — Latin Aquila ("eagle"); Greek Ἀκύλας; retained.
- **"Priscilla"** (v2) — Latin diminutive of Prisca; retained.
- **"Gallio" / "Achaia"** (v12) — Lucius Junius Gallio Annaeanus; proconsul of Achaia c. 51–52 CE; important chronological anchor for Pauline chronology; retained.
- **"the way of God" → "the way of *Elohim*"** (v26) — consistent *Elohim* substitution.
- **"If God will" → "If *Elohim* wills"** (v21) — consistent substitution.
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** modernized throughout.
