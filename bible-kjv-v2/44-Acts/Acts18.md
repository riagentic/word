# Acts 18

[1] After these things, having departed from ⟦athens|Athenai|Athens⟧, he came to ⟦corinth|Korinthos|Corinth⟧.

[2] And having found a certain ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧, Aquila by name, of ⟦pontus|Pontos|Pontus⟧ by race, recently come from ⟦italy|Italia|Italy⟧, and Priscilla his wife, because Claudius had ordained that all the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ depart from ⟦rome|Roma|Rome⟧, he approached them,

[3] and because he was of the same craft, he was abiding with them and working, for they were tentmakers by craft.

[4] And he was discussing in the synagogue every ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, and was persuading both ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and ⟦greeks|Yevanim|Greeks⟧.

[5] And when both ⟦silas|Sila|Silas⟧ and ⟦timothy|Timotheos|Timothy⟧ came down from ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧, ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ was constrained by the word, solemnly testifying to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ that the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧ is ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[6] But when they opposed and blasphemed, having shaken out his garments he said to them, "Your blood be upon your head! I am clean; from now on I will go to the nations."

[7] And having passed on from there, he entered into the house of a certain man, Titius Justus by name, one revering ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, whose house was adjoining the synagogue.

[8] And Crispus the synagogue ruler believed the Lord with his whole house, and many of the ⟦corinthians|Korinthioi|Corinthians⟧, hearing, were believing and being ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧.

[9] And the Lord said in the night through a vision to ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, "Do not fear, but speak and do not be silent,

[10] because I am with you, and no one will set upon you to mistreat you, because I have much people in this city."

[11] And he settled a year and six months, teaching among them the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[12] But while Gallio was proconsul of Achaia, the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ with one accord rose up against ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ and brought him to the judgment seat,

[13] saying, "Contrary to the law this one persuades men to revere ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[14] And when ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ was about to open his mouth, Gallio said to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, "If indeed it were some wrong or evil villainy, O ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, according to reason I would have borne with you;

[15] but if they are questions about a word and names and your own ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, you will see to it yourselves; I do not wish to be a judge of these things."

[16] And he drove them away from the judgment seat.

[17] And all, having laid hold of Sosthenes the synagogue ruler, were striking him before the judgment seat; and none of these things was a care to Gallio.

[18] And ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, having remained yet a considerable number of days, having taken leave of the brothers, was sailing out to Syria, and with him Priscilla and Aquila, having shorn his head in ⟦cenchreae|Kenchreai|Cenchreae⟧, for he had a vow.

[19] And they arrived at ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧, and those he left behind there, but he himself, having entered into the synagogue, discussed with the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧.

[20] And when they requested him to remain a longer time, he did not consent,

[21] but having taken leave and said, "I will turn back again to you, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ willing," he put out to sea from ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧,

[22] and having come down to Caesarea, having gone up and greeted the church, he went down to ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧,

[23] and having spent some time, he went out, going through the Galatian country and Phrygia in order, making all the disciples stable.

[24] Now a certain ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧, Apollos by name, of ⟦alexandria|Alexandreia|Alexandria⟧ by race, an eloquent man, arrived at ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧, being powerful in the Scriptures.

[25] This one had been instructed in the way of the Lord, and being fervent in the spirit he was speaking and teaching accurately the things concerning ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, knowing only the ⟦immersion|immersion|baptism⟧ of ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧.

[26] And this one began to speak boldly in the synagogue; and having heard him, Priscilla and Aquila took him to themselves and set forth to him the way of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ more accurately.

[27] And when he wished to go through into Achaia, the brothers, urging, wrote to the disciples to accept him; who, having arrived, contributed much to those who had believed through the grace;

[28] for he was vigorously refuting the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ publicly, showing through the Scriptures that the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧ is ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v7, 11, 13, 21, 26) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v5, 25, 28) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v5, 28) — Hebrew messianic title.
- **"Holy Ghost" / Spirit** — no explicit *Ruach HaQodesh* occurrence in this chapter; on "the spirit" in v25 see below.
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (v8) — Greek *baptizo* = "to immerse"; consistent rendering.
- **"John's baptism" → "the immersion of John"** (v25) — consistent rendering.
- **"Aquila" / "*Aquila*"** (v2) — Latin Aquila ("eagle"); Greek Ἀκύλας; retained.
- **"Priscilla"** (v2) — Latin diminutive of Prisca; retained.
- **"Gallio" / "Achaia"** (v12) — Lucius Junius Gallio Annaeanus; proconsul of Achaia c. 51–52 CE; important chronological anchor for Pauline chronology; retained.
- **"the way of God" → "the way of *Elohim*"** (v26) — consistent *Elohim* substitution.
- **"if God will" → "*Elohim* willing"** (v21) — Greek τοῦ θεοῦ θέλοντος, a participle.
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** modernized throughout.
- **"After these things Paul departed from Athens" → "After these things, having departed from Athens, he"** (v1) — the name "Paul" is Byzantine; χωρισθείς, the verb of v2 ("depart"), as 1:4.
- **"born in Pontus, lately come from Italy" → "of Pontus by race, recently come from Italy"** (v2) — Greek Ποντικὸν τῷ γένει, as v24 and 4:36; προσφάτως. "Had commanded all Jews to depart" → "had ordained that all the *Yehudim* depart": διατεταχέναι, as Gal 3:19. "Came unto them" → "approached them": προσῆλθεν.
- **"abode with them, and wrought: for by their occupation they were tentmakers" → "was abiding with them and working, for they were tentmakers by craft"** (v3) — Greek ἔμενεν, ἠργάζετο, imperfects (Treg, NA28, RP "he was working"; WH ἠργάζοντο "they were working"); ὁμότεχνον, τῇ τέχνῃ: one word "craft" (17:29).
- **"reasoned" → "was discussing"; "persuaded the Jews and the Greeks" → "was persuading both *Yehudim* and *Yevanim*"** (v4) — Greek διελέγετο, as 17:2, 17; ἔπειθεν, imperfect: "was persuading", also "was trying to persuade"; no articles.
- **"were come from Macedonia" → "came down from Macedonia"; "Paul was pressed in the spirit" → "Paul was constrained by the word"** (v5) — Greek κατῆλθον; συνείχετο τῷ λόγῳ (WH, Treg, NA28; RP τῷ πνεύματι, "in the spirit"): the verb of 2 Cor 5:14, "was held fast by the word", so "wholly taken up with the word". "Testified" → "solemnly testifying": διαμαρτυρόμενος.
- **"that Jesus was Christ" → "that the *Mashiach* is *Yeshua*"** (v5, 28) — Greek εἶναι τὸν χριστὸν Ἰησοῦν, two accusatives: "that the *Mashiach* is *Yeshua*" (the Greek order) or "that *Yeshua* is the *Mashiach*".
- **"opposed themselves" → "opposed"; "shook his raiment" → "having shaken out his garments"; "upon your own heads" → "upon your head"; "the Gentiles" → "the nations"** (v6) — Greek ἀντιτασσομένων, as Jas 4:6; βλασφημούντων has no object; ἐκτιναξάμενος, as 13:51; ἐπὶ τὴν κεφαλὴν ὑμῶν, singular, no "own"; τὰ ἔθνη. "Be" is supplied. The words καθαρὸς ἐγώ· ἀπὸ τοῦ νῦν … can also be divided "I, clean, from now on will go to the nations".
- **"he departed thence, and entered into a certain man's house, named Justus, one that worshipped God" → "having passed on from there, he entered into the house of a certain man, Titius Justus by name, one revering God"** (v7) — Greek μεταβὰς ἐκεῖθεν, as Matt 12:9; εἰσῆλθεν (NA28, SBLGNT; WH, Treg, RP ἦλθεν "came": 2–2, NA28 decides); Τιτίου Ἰούστου (WH, Treg, NA28); σεβομένου τὸν θεόν, as 16:14. "Joined hard to" → "was adjoining": ἦν συνομοροῦσα.
- **"the chief ruler of the synagogue, believed on the Lord with all his house" → "the synagogue ruler believed the Lord with his whole house"** (v8) — Greek ἀρχισυνάγωγος, as 13:15; ἐπίστευσεν τῷ κυρίῳ, a dative, "believed the Lord", as 16:34; σὺν ὅλῳ τῷ οἴκῳ. "Hearing believed, and were baptized" → "hearing, were believing and being immersed": ἀκούοντες has no object ("Paul" is not in the Greek); ἐπίστευον, ἐβαπτίζοντο, imperfects.
- **"Then spake the Lord to Paul in the night by a vision, Be not afraid, but speak, and hold not thy peace" → "And the Lord said in the night through a vision to Paul, 'Do not fear, but speak and do not be silent'"** (v9) — Greek ἐν νυκτὶ διʼ ὁράματος; Μὴ φοβοῦ, λάλει, presents ("go on speaking"); μὴ σιωπήσῃς.
- **"For … no man shall set on thee to hurt thee: for I have much people" → "because … no one will set upon you to mistreat you, because I have much people"** (v10) — Greek διότι twice; ἐπιθήσεταί σοι; κακῶσαι, as 7:6; 12:1; λαός ἐστί μοι πολύς, "there is to me much people": λαός is singular, "a people", not "many persons".
- **"continued there" → "settled"** (v11) — Greek ἐκάθισεν, "sat down", so "stayed, settled"; "there" is not in the Greek. "Among them": ἐν αὐτοῖς.
- **"when Gallio was the deputy of Achaia" → "while Gallio was proconsul of Achaia"; "made insurrection with one accord against Paul" → "with one accord rose up against Paul"** (v12) — Greek ἀνθυπάτου ὄντος, as 13:7; κατεπέστησαν ὁμοθυμαδόν, as 16:22 ("rose up together against").
- **"This fellow persuadeth men to worship God contrary to the law" → "Contrary to the law this one persuades men to revere God"** (v13) — Greek Παρὰ τὸν νόμον stands first; ἀναπείθει, a strengthened "persuade", "wins over"; σέβεσθαι. "The law" is left without the *Torah* form here: before a Roman judge the accusers' words can mean the Roman law or the *Torah*; Gallio takes them of the *Yehudim*'s own law (v15).
- **"a matter of wrong or wicked lewdness … reason would that I should bear with you" → "some wrong or evil villainy … according to reason I would have borne with you"** (v14) — Greek ἀδίκημά τι ἢ ῥᾳδιούργημα πονηρόν (ῥᾳδιουργία "villainy", 13:10); κατὰ λόγον, λόγος "word, reason"; ἀνεσχόμην. "Was about to": μέλλοντος.
- **"a question of words and names, and of your law, look ye to it; for I will be no judge of such matters" → "questions about a word and names and your own *Torah*, you will see to it yourselves; I do not wish to be a judge of these things"** (v15) — Greek ζητήματα, plural (WH, Treg, NA28); περὶ λόγου, singular; νόμου τοῦ καθʼ ὑμᾶς, "law, the one among you"; ὄψεσθε αὐτοί, a future; οὐ βούλομαι; "for" is Byzantine.
- **"drave them from" → "drove them away from"** (v16) — Greek ἀπήλασεν ἀπό.
- **"Then all the Greeks took Sosthenes … and beat him" → "And all, having laid hold of Sosthenes …, were striking him"** (v17) — Greek πάντες (WH, Treg, NA28): "the Greeks" is Byzantine, and the Greek does not say who "all" are; ἐπιλαβόμενοι, as 16:19; ἔτυπτον, imperfect. "Gallio cared for none of those things" → "none of these things was a care to Gallio": οὐδὲν τούτων τῷ Γαλλίωνι ἔμελεν.
- **"tarried there yet a good while, and then took his leave … and sailed thence" → "having remained yet a considerable number of days, having taken leave …, was sailing out"** (v18) — Greek προσμείνας ἡμέρας ἱκανάς; ἀποταξάμενος, as Mark 6:46; ἐξέπλει, imperfect. "Having shorn his head … for he had a vow" kept: κειράμενος … εἶχεν γὰρ εὐχήν. By the grammar the one who shaved can be Paul (the subject of the sentence) or Aquila (the name that stands just before); the verse leaves it as the Greek does.
- **"he came to Ephesus, and left them there … and reasoned with" → "they arrived at Ephesus, and those he left behind there … discussed with"** (v19) — Greek κατήντησαν, plural (WH, Treg, NA28), as 16:1; κατέλιπεν; διελέξατο.
- **"desired him to tarry longer time with them" → "requested him to remain a longer time"; "consented not" kept** (v20) — Greek ἐρωτώντων; μεῖναι, the verb μένω ("abide" with persons, v3), here of a bare stay: "remain"; "with them" is Byzantine; ἐπένευσεν, "nodded assent".
- **"bade them farewell, saying, I must by all means keep this feast that cometh in Jerusalem: but I will return again unto you" → "having taken leave and said, 'I will turn back again to you'"** (v21) — the words about the feast are Byzantine (WH, Treg, NA28 do not have them); ἀνακάμψω; "sailed" → "put out to sea": ἀνήχθη, as 16:11.
- **"landed at" → "having come down to"; "saluted" → "greeted"** (v22) — Greek κατελθών, as v5; ἀσπασάμενος. "Gone up": ἀναβάς, with no place named; "the church" is usually taken of Jerusalem.
- **"went over all the country of Galatia and Phrygia in order, strengthening" → "going through the Galatian country and Phrygia in order, making … stable"** (v23) — Greek τὴν Γαλατικὴν χώραν καὶ Φρυγίαν, as 16:6; καθεξῆς, as 11:4; στηρίζων (WH, Treg, SBLGNT; NA28, RP ἐπιστηρίζων, the same rendering). "Spent some time": ποιήσας χρόνον τινά, as 15:33.
- **"born at Alexandria, an eloquent man, and mighty in the scriptures, came to Ephesus" → "of Alexandria by race, an eloquent man, arrived at Ephesus, being powerful in the Scriptures"** (v24) — Greek Ἀλεξανδρεὺς τῷ γένει; λόγιος, "eloquent" or "learned" (a man of λόγος); κατήντησεν; δυνατός.
- **"fervent in the spirit" kept; "taught diligently the things of the Lord" → "teaching accurately the things concerning *Yeshua*"** (v25) — Greek ζέων τῷ πνεύματι, "boiling in the spirit", as Rom 12:11: his own spirit or the Spirit, the Greek does not decide; ἀκριβῶς (v26 ἀκριβέστερον); τοῦ Ἰησοῦ (WH, Treg, NA28), RP "the Lord". "Instructed": κατηχημένος, as Gal 6:6. "Knowing": ἐπιστάμενος.
- **"expounded unto him the way of God more perfectly" → "set forth to him the way of God more accurately"** (v26) — Greek ἐξέθεντο, as 11:4; ἀκριβέστερον. "Took him unto them" → "took him to themselves": προσελάβοντο. The Greek names Priscilla first (WH, Treg, NA28).
- **"was disposed to pass into Achaia, the brethren wrote, exhorting the disciples to receive him" → "wished to go through into Achaia, the brothers, urging, wrote to the disciples to accept him"** (v27) — Greek βουλομένου; διελθεῖν; προτρεψάμενοι has no object: they urged Apollos on, or they urged the disciples in the letter; ἀποδέξασθαι, as 2:41. "Helped them much which had believed through grace" → "contributed much to those who had believed through the grace": συνεβάλετο; διὰ τῆς χάριτος, with the article, can go with "contributed" or with "had believed".
- **"mightily convinced the Jews, and that publickly, shewing by the scriptures" → "was vigorously refuting the *Yehudim* publicly, showing through the Scriptures"** (v28) — Greek εὐτόνως; διακατηλέγχετο, imperfect, a strengthened form of ἐλέγχω; δημοσίᾳ, as 16:37; ἐπιδεικνύς, as 9:39; διά.
