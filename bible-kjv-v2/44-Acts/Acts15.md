# Acts 15

[1] And some, having come down from ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧, were teaching the brothers, "Unless you are circumcised by the custom of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, you cannot be saved."

[2] And when no small discord and questioning had arisen for ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ and ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ with them, they arranged for ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ and ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ and some others of them to go up to the apostles and elders in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ about this question.

[3] So they, having been sent forward by the church, were going through both ⟦phoenicia|Phoinike|Phoenicia⟧ and ⟦samaria-nt|Shomron|Samaria⟧, relating in full the turning of the nations, and they were causing great joy to all the brothers.

[4] And having arrived in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, they were accepted by the church and the apostles and the elders, and they announced all that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ did with them.

[5] But there rose up some of those from the sect of the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ who had believed, saying, "It is necessary to circumcise them and to charge them to keep the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧."

[6] And the apostles and the elders were gathered together to see about this word.

[7] And when much questioning had arisen, ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, having risen, said to them, "Men, brothers, you know that from days of old ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ chose among you, that through my mouth the nations should hear the word of the gospel and believe;

[8] and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the knower of hearts, testified to them, giving the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ just as also to us,

[9] and he made no distinction between us and them, having cleansed their hearts by the faith.

[10] Now therefore why do you tempt ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to put upon the neck of the disciples a yoke which neither our fathers nor we had strength to bear?

[11] But through the grace of the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ we believe that we shall be saved, in the same way as they also."

[12] And all the multitude became silent, and they were listening to ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ and ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ declaring all the signs and wonders that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ did among the nations through them.

[13] And after they became silent, ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ answered, saying, "Men, brothers, hear me.

[14] ⟦simeon-nt|Shimon|Simeon⟧ declared how ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ first visited, to take out of nations a people for his name.

[15] And with this the words of the prophets agree, as it is written,

[16] "'**After these things I will return,
and I will build again the tent of David that has fallen,
and its ruins I will build again,
and I will set it upright again,**

**[17] so that the rest of men may seek out the Lord,
and all the nations upon whom my name has been called,
says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧, who does these things,**

**[18] known from the age.**'

[19] Therefore I judge not to harass those from the nations who are turning to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[20] but to write to them to abstain from the pollutions of the idols and from sexual immorality and from what is strangled and from blood.

[21] For ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ from ancient generations has in every city those who proclaim him, being read in the synagogues every ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧."

[22] Then it seemed good to the apostles and the elders, with the whole church, having chosen men from among themselves, to send them to ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧ with ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ and ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧: ⟦jude|Yehudah|Judas⟧ who is called ⟦barsabbas|Bar-Sabba|Barsabbas⟧, and ⟦silas|Sila|Silas⟧, leading men among the brothers,

[23] having written through their hand:

"The apostles and the elders, brothers, to the brothers from the nations throughout ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧ and Syria and ⟦cilicia|Kilikia|Cilicia⟧, greeting.

[24] Since we heard that some who went out from us troubled you with words, unsettling your souls, to whom we gave no injunction,

[25] it seemed good to us, having come to be of one accord, having chosen men, to send them to you with our beloved ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ and ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧,

[26] men who have delivered up their souls for the name of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[27] We have therefore sent ⟦jude|Yehudah|Judas⟧ and ⟦silas|Sila|Silas⟧, themselves also reporting the same things by word.

[28] For it seemed good to the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ and to us to lay upon you no further burden except these necessary things:

[29] to abstain from things sacrificed to idols and from blood and from things strangled and from sexual immorality; keeping yourselves from which, you will do well. Farewell."

[30] So they, having been sent away, went down to ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧, and having gathered the multitude together they handed over the letter.

[31] And having read it, they rejoiced at the encouragement.

[32] And ⟦jude|Yehudah|Judas⟧ and ⟦silas|Sila|Silas⟧, being themselves also prophets, through much word encouraged the brothers and made them stable.

[33] And having spent a time, they were sent away with peace from the brothers to those who had sent them.

[35] But ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ and ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ stayed in ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧, teaching and preaching, with many others also, the word of the Lord.

[36] And after some days ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ said to ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧, "Turning back now, let us visit the brothers in every city in which we proclaimed the word of the Lord, how they are."

[37] And ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ wished to take along with them ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ also, who is called ⟦mark|Marcus|Mark⟧;

[38] but ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ thought it right not to take along with them this one, who had departed from them from Pamphylia and had not gone with them to the work.

[39] And there arose a sharp contention, so that they were separated from one another, and ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧, taking along ⟦mark|Marcus|Mark⟧, sailed out to ⟦cyprus|Kypros|Cyprus⟧,

[40] but ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, having selected ⟦silas|Sila|Silas⟧, went out, given over to the grace of the Lord by the brothers,

[41] and he was going through Syria and ⟦cilicia|Kilikia|Cilicia⟧, making the churches stable.

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v13) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v8, 28) — consistent rendering.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v11, 26) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v26) — Hebrew messianic title.
- **"Peter" / "*Kefa*"** (v7) — Aramaic name used consistently.
- **"baptize" → "immerse"** — no direct occurrence in this chapter.
- **"law of Moses" / "Moses"** (v1, 5, 21) — retained as proper name reference.
- **"the Apostolic Decree"** (v20, 29) — four prohibitions (idols, blood, strangled things, sexual immorality) represent the Noachide framework; the halakhic context is significant.
- **v15-18** — OT quotation: Amos 9:11-12 (LXX) — Jacob cites the "tent of David" prophecy; notably the LXX differs from the MT here, allowing the Gentile inclusion reading. The verses translate the Greek as Acts prints it.
- **v34 absent** — KJV v34 ("Notwithstanding it pleased Silas to abide there still") is absent from the earliest MSS; omitted with note.
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** modernized throughout.
- **"certain men" → "some"; "after the manner of Moses" → "by the custom of Moses"** (v1) — Greek τινες, no "men"; τῷ ἔθει, a dative.
- **"dissension and disputation" → "discord and questioning"; "they determined" → "they arranged"** (v2) — Greek στάσεως καὶ ζητήσεως (ζήτησις as Titus 3:9; ζήτημα "question" is its noun); ἔταξαν, active, "they set in place" (τάσσω, 13:48): the subject is not named.
- **"being brought on their way" → "having been sent forward"; "declaring the conversion of the Gentiles" → "relating in full the turning of the nations"** (v3) — Greek προπεμφθέντες; ἐκδιηγούμενοι τὴν ἐπιστροφήν, the noun of ἐπιστρέφω "turn" (v19).
- **"received of" → "accepted by"; "declared" → "announced"** (v4) — Greek παρεδέχθησαν; ἀνήγγειλαν. "Jerusalem": Ἱερουσαλήμ (WH, NA28, RP) is followed; Treg and SBLGNT Ἱεροσόλυμα; no difference in English.
- **"certain of the sect of the Pharisees which believed" kept** (v5) — Greek τινες τῶν ἀπὸ τῆς αἱρέσεως τῶν Φαρισαίων πεπιστευκότες; αἵρεσις "sect" as Gal 5:20, not "party". "Command" → "charge": παραγγέλλειν.
- **"to consider of this matter" → "to see about this word"** (v6) — Greek ἰδεῖν περὶ τοῦ λόγου τούτου; λόγος, "word", here of the thing spoken about.
- **"a good while ago God made choice among us" → "from days of old God chose among you"** (v7) — Greek ἀφʼ ἡμερῶν ἀρχαίων ἐν ὑμῖν ἐξελέξατο (WH, Treg, NA28 ὑμῖν). The verb has no object in the Greek. "By my mouth" → "through my mouth": διά.
- **"which knoweth the hearts" → "the knower of hearts"; "bare them witness" → "testified to them"** (v8) — Greek ὁ καρδιογνώστης θεός; ἐμαρτύρησεν αὐτοῖς. The second "them" is Byzantine.
- **"put no difference" → "made no distinction"; "purifying … by faith" → "having cleansed … by the faith"** (v9) — Greek οὐθὲν διέκρινεν, as 11:12; τῇ πίστει, with the article; καθαρίσας, the verb of 11:9.
- **"were able to bear" → "had strength to bear"** (v10) — Greek ἰσχύσαμεν βαστάσαι. "Tempt" kept: πειράζετε.
- **"we believe that … we shall be saved, even as they" kept** (v11) — Greek πιστεύομεν σωθῆναι, an infinitive without tense: "we believe that we are saved / shall be saved" or "we believe, so as to be saved". κἀκεῖνοι is "those also"; no "will" after it.
- **"kept silence, and gave audience" → "became silent, and they were listening"; "declaring" kept** (v12) — Greek Ἐσίγησεν, ἤκουον; ἐξηγουμένων, the verb of John 1:18, also v14. "Multitude": πλῆθος.
- **"after they had held their peace" → "after they became silent"** (v13) — Greek μετὰ τὸ σιγῆσαι αὐτούς; not "finished speaking".
- **"how God at the first did visit the Gentiles, to take out of them" → "how God first visited, to take out of nations"** (v14) — Greek ἐπεσκέψατο λαβεῖν ἐξ ἐθνῶν λαόν: "visited" has no object, and "nations" has no article.
- **"After this" → "After these things"; "the tabernacle" → "the tent"; "I will set it up" → "I will set it upright again"** (v16) — Greek Μετὰ ταῦτα; σκηνήν; ἀνορθώσω. "Build again" stands twice (ἀνοικοδομήσω). "Its ruins" is τὰ κατεσκαμμένα αὐτῆς, "its things dug down" (NA28, SBLGNT, RP; 2–2, NA28 decides); WH, Treg κατεστραμμένα, "overturned".
- **"the residue of men might seek after the Lord" → "the rest of men may seek out the Lord"** (v17) — Greek οἱ κατάλοιποι τῶν ἀνθρώπων; ἐκζητήσωσιν. "Upon whom my name is called" kept: ἐφʼ οὓς ἐπικέκληται τὸ ὄνομά μου ἐπʼ αὐτούς, a Hebrew turn of phrase, with "upon them" said twice in the Greek.
- **"who doeth all these things. Known unto God are all his works from the beginning of the world" → "who does these things, known from the age"** (v17–18) — the critical text (WH, Treg, NA28) has only ποιῶν ταῦτα γνωστὰ ἀπʼ αἰῶνος; the rest is Byzantine. The words can be read "who does these things, [which are] known from the age" or "who makes these things known from the age"; the verse keeps the first, since Amos 9:12 ends with "who does these things". ἀπʼ αἰῶνος is "from the age", from of old.
- **"my sentence is, that we trouble not" → "I judge not to harass"** (v19) — Greek ἐγὼ κρίνω μὴ παρενοχλεῖν; παρενοχλέω is "cause trouble besides", a different word from "trouble" in v24 (ταράσσω). No "we" in the Greek.
- **"pollutions of idols … things strangled" → "the pollutions of the idols … what is strangled"** (v20) — Greek τῶν ἀλισγημάτων τῶν εἰδώλων; τοῦ πνικτοῦ, singular (in v29 plural).
- **"of old time hath in every city them that preach him" → "from ancient generations has in every city those who proclaim him"** (v21) — Greek ἐκ γενεῶν ἀρχαίων; κηρύσσοντας.
- **"pleased it" → "it seemed good"; "chief men" → "leading men"** (v22, 25, 28) — Greek ἔδοξε; ἡγουμένους. The Greek has no second verb "they sent": the names depend on "to send".
- **"And they wrote letters by them after this manner" → "having written through their hand"** (v23) — Greek γράψαντες διὰ χειρὸς αὐτῶν; "after this manner" is Byzantine.
- **"The apostles and elders and brethren" → "The apostles and the elders, brothers"** (v23) — Greek οἱ ἀπόστολοι καὶ οἱ πρεσβύτεροι ἀδελφοί (WH, Treg, NA28): "brothers" stands in apposition, or the words mean "the apostles and the elder brothers". RP adds "and the" before "brothers". "Of the Gentiles" → "from the nations": ἐξ ἐθνῶν.
- **"subverting your souls, saying, Ye must be circumcised, and keep the law: to whom we gave no such commandment" → "unsettling your souls, to whom we gave no injunction"** (v24) — Greek ἀνασκευάζοντες τὰς ψυχὰς ὑμῶν οἷς οὐ διεστειλάμεθα; the words about circumcision are Byzantine. "Who went out": ἐξελθόντες (Treg, NA28, SBLGNT, RP); WH omits.
- **"being assembled with one accord" → "having come to be of one accord"** (v25) — Greek γενομένοις ὁμοθυμαδόν.
- **"hazarded their lives" → "delivered up their souls"** (v26) — Greek παραδεδωκόσι τὰς ψυχὰς αὐτῶν.
- **"shall also tell you the same things by mouth" → "themselves also reporting the same things by word"** (v27) — Greek ἀπαγγέλλοντας, a present participle; διὰ λόγου.
- **"no greater burden than these necessary things" → "no further burden except these necessary things"** (v28) — Greek μηδὲν πλέον … βάρος πλήν.
- **"meats offered to idols" → "things sacrificed to idols"; "from which if ye keep yourselves" → "keeping yourselves from which"** (v29) — Greek εἰδωλοθύτων; διατηροῦντες. "Fare ye well" → "Farewell": Ἔρρωσθε, "be strong".
- **"when they were dismissed" → "having been sent away"; "delivered the epistle" → "handed over the letter"** (v30) — Greek ἀπολυθέντες; ἐπέδωκαν.
- **"consolation" → "encouragement"; "exhorted … and confirmed" → "encouraged … and made them stable"** (v31–32) — Greek παρακλήσει, παρεκάλεσαν (also "exhorted"), ἐπεστήριξαν. "With many words" → "through much word": διὰ λόγου πολλοῦ, singular.
- **"unto the apostles" → "to those who had sent them"** (v33) — Greek πρὸς τοὺς ἀποστείλαντας αὐτούς (WH, Treg, NA28).
- **"continued" → "stayed"** (v35) — Greek διέτριβον. "The word of the Lord" is the object of "preaching" (εὐαγγελιζόμενοι, "bringing as good news").
- **"Let us go again and visit our brethren … and see how they do" → "Turning back now, let us visit the brothers … how they are"** (v36) — Greek Ἐπιστρέψαντες δὴ ἐπισκεψώμεθα … πῶς ἔχουσιν; no "and see"; "our" is Byzantine.
- **"determined" → "wished"** (v37) — Greek ἐβούλετο (WH, Treg, NA28).
- **"thought not good" → "thought it right not"; "departed from them" kept** (v38) — Greek ἠξίου, "deemed it fitting"; ἀποστάντα, as 12:10 (in 13:13 another verb, "withdrawn"). "From Pamphylia" is ἀπὸ Παμφυλίας, not "in".
- **"the contention was so sharp between them, that they departed asunder" → "there arose a sharp contention, so that they were separated"** (v39) — Greek παροξυσμός, the word rendered "stirring up" in Heb 10:24; ἀποχωρισθῆναι.
- **"chose" → "having selected"; "being recommended … unto the grace of God" → "given over to the grace of the Lord"** (v40) — Greek ἐπιλεξάμενος (not ἐκλέγομαι, v22); παραδοθείς, as 14:26; κυρίου (WH, Treg, NA28), RP θεοῦ.
- **"confirming" → "making … stable"** (v41) — Greek ἐπιστηρίζων, as 14:22.

### Notes
- **v16-18** quotes Amos 9:11-12 (LXX).
- **v34** absent from earliest MSS ("Notwithstanding it pleased Silas to abide there still").
