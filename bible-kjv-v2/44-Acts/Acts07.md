# Acts 7

[1] And the high priest said, "Are these things so?"

[2] And he said:

"Men, brothers and fathers, hear. The ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of glory appeared to our father ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ while he was in Mesopotamia, before he dwelt in ⟦haran-city|Charan|Haran⟧,

[3] and said to him, '**Go out from your land and from your kindred, and come into the land which I will show you**.'

[4] Then, going out from the land of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧, he dwelt in ⟦haran-city|Charan|Haran⟧. And from there, after his father died, he resettled him into this land in which you now dwell,

[5] and he gave him no inheritance in it, not even a foot's step, and he promised to give it to him for a possession, and to his seed after him, though he had no child.

[6] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ spoke thus: that his seed would be an alien in a land belonging to others, and that they would enslave it and mistreat it four hundred years;

[7] 'and the nation to which they will be slaves I will judge,' ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, 'and after these things they will come out and worship me in this place.'

[8] And he gave him a covenant of circumcision; and so he begot ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ and circumcised him on the eighth day, and ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ begot ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, and ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ the twelve patriarchs.

[9] "And the patriarchs, being jealous of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, sold him into ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧; and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was with him,

[10] and he delivered him out of all his afflictions and gave him grace and wisdom before ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧, king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and he appointed him leader over ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and all his house.

[11] Now a famine came upon all ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, and great affliction, and our fathers were finding no sustenance.

[12] But ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, having heard that there was grain in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, sent out our fathers the first time;

[13] and at the second time ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ was made known to his brothers, and the race of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ became manifest to ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧.

[14] And ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ sent and called to himself ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ his father and all the kindred, in seventy-five souls,

[15] and ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ went down into ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧. And he died, he and our fathers,

[16] and they were carried over to Shechem and laid in the grave which ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ bought for a price of silver from the sons of ⟦hamor|Chamor|Hamor⟧ in Shechem.

[17] "But as the time of the promise was drawing near which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had declared to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, the people grew and was multiplied in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,

[18] until there rose up a different king over ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, who did not know ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧.

[19] This one, dealing subtly with our race, mistreated the fathers, to make their babies exposed, so that they would not be kept alive.

[20] At which season ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ was born, and he was beautiful to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and he was brought up three months in the house of his father,

[21] and when he was exposed, the daughter of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ took him up and brought him up for herself as a son.

[22] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ was trained in all the wisdom of the ⟦egyptians|Mitzrim|Egyptians⟧, and he was powerful in his words and works.

[23] "But when a time of forty years was being fulfilled for him, it came up upon his heart to visit his brothers, the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[24] And seeing one being wronged, he defended him and wrought vengeance for the one being oppressed, striking the ⟦egyptian|Mitzri|Egyptian⟧.

[25] Now he supposed that the brothers understood that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ through his hand was giving them salvation; but they did not understand.

[26] And on the following day he appeared to them as they were fighting, and was reconciling them unto peace, saying, 'Men, you are brothers; why do you wrong one another?'

[27] But the one wronging his neighbor thrust him away, saying, '**Who appointed you ruler and judge over us?**

**[28] Do you want to do away with me in the manner in which you did away with the ⟦egyptian|Mitzri|Egyptian⟧ yesterday?**'

[29] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ fled at this word and became an alien in the land of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧, where he begot two sons.

[30] "And when forty years were fulfilled, there appeared to him in the wilderness of Mount Sinai an angel in a flame of fire of a bush.

[31] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, seeing it, marveled at the vision; and as he was approaching to observe it, there came the voice of the Lord:

[32] '**I am the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of your fathers, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ and ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ and ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧**.' And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, beginning to tremble, did not dare to observe.

[33] And the Lord said to him, '**Loose the sandal of your feet, for the place on which you stand is holy ground.**

**[34] Seeing, I have seen the mistreatment of my people who are in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and I have heard their groaning, and I have come down to deliver them; and now come, let me send you to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧**.'

[35] "This ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, whom they denied, saying, 'Who appointed you ruler and judge?' — this one ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has sent as both ruler and ransomer, with the hand of the angel who appeared to him in the bush.

[36] This one led them out, doing wonders and signs in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and in the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧ and in the wilderness forty years.

[37] This is the ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ who said to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, '**A prophet will ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ raise up for you from among your brothers, like me**.'

[38] This is the one who was in the church in the wilderness with the angel who was speaking to him on Mount Sinai and with our fathers, who accepted living oracles to give to us,

[39] to whom our fathers were not willing to become obedient, but thrust him away and turned in their hearts to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,

[40] saying to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, '**Make for us gods who will go before us; for this ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, who led us out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ — we do not know what happened to him**.'

[41] And they made a calf in those days and brought up a sacrifice to the idol, and were rejoicing in the works of their hands.

[42] But ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ turned and delivered them up to worship the host of heaven, as it is written in the book of the prophets:

"'**Did you offer to me slain beasts and sacrifices
forty years in the wilderness, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?
[43] And you took up the tent of ⟦moloch|Molekh|Moloch⟧
and the star of the god ⟦rephan|Rhaiphan|Rephan⟧,
the figures which you made to bow down to them;
and I will resettle you beyond ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧**.'

[44] "Our fathers had the tent of the testimony in the wilderness, as he who spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ ordained, to make it according to the pattern which he had seen,

[45] which our fathers also, having received it in their turn, brought in with ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ in the taking possession of the nations whom ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ thrust out from the face of our fathers, until the days of David,

[46] who found grace before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and asked to find a dwelling place for the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧.

[47] But ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ built him a house.

[48] But the Most High does not dwell in things made by hands, as the prophet says,

[49] "'**Heaven is my throne,
and the earth a footstool of my feet.
What kind of house will you build for me, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧,
or what is the place of my rest?
[50] Did not my hand make all these things?**'

[51] "Stiff-necked and uncircumcised in hearts and ears, you always strive against the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧; as your fathers, so also you.

[52] Which of the prophets did your fathers not persecute? And they killed those who announced beforehand about the coming of the Righteous One, of whom you have now become betrayers and murderers,

[53] you who received the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ as ordinances of angels, and did not guard it."

[54] Now hearing these things, they were cut through in their hearts, and they were gnashing their teeth at him.

[55] But being full of ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧, gazing into heaven he saw the glory of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ standing at the right hand of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[56] and he said, "Behold, I behold the heavens opened up and the Son of Man standing at the right hand of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[57] But crying out with a loud voice, they stopped their ears and rushed with one accord upon him,

[58] and casting him outside the city, they were stoning him. And the witnesses laid aside their garments at the feet of a young man called ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧.

[59] And they were stoning ⟦stephen|Stephanos|Stephen⟧ as he was calling upon and saying, "Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, accept my spirit."

[60] And kneeling down, he cried out with a loud voice, "**Lord, do not set this sin against them.**" And having said this, he fell asleep.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v51, 55) — consistent rendering. In v51 the Greek has the article (τῷ πνεύματι τῷ ἁγίῳ), in v55 it has none (πλήρης πνεύματος ἁγίου).
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v55, 59) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"Saul" → "*Sha'ul*"** (v58) — Hebrew שָׁאוּל *Sha'ul* ("asked for"); at his first occurrence in Acts the text gives the Hebrew form (Greek Σαύλου).
- **The speech follows the Greek Old Testament** (v2–53) — Stephen quotes and retells the Scriptures in the wording of the Greek translation (LXX). The verses give the Greek as printed, also where it differs from the Hebrew books; the differences are listed in the lines below (v4, 14, 16, 32, 42–43).
- **"And he said" kept** (v2) — Greek ὁ δὲ ἔφη; the Greek does not name Stephen here.
- **"Men, brethren, and fathers, hearken" → "Men, brothers and fathers, hear"** (v2) — Greek Ἄνδρες ἀδελφοὶ καὶ πατέρες, ἀκούσατε; the Greek has no "me".
- **"dwelt" kept** (v2, 4, 48) — Greek κατοικέω, one verb in all three verses.
- **"when his father was dead, he removed him" → "after his father died, he resettled him"** (v4) — Greek μετῴκισεν, "moved his dwelling"; the same verb in v43 ("I will resettle you"). The Greek has no subject: "he" is God (v2). By the Hebrew numbers Terah was still alive when Abraham left Haran (Gen 11:26, 32; 12:4: 70 + 75 = 145 of his 205 years); the Samaritan Pentateuch gives Terah 145 years.
- **"not so much as to set his foot on" → "not even a foot's step"** (v5) — Greek οὐδὲ βῆμα ποδός (Deut 2:5 LXX).
- **"seed" kept** (v5, 6) — Greek σπέρμα, a singular; so "it" in v6 (αὐτό).
- **"should sojourn in a strange land" → "would be an alien in a land belonging to others"** (v6) — Greek πάροικον, ἐν γῇ ἀλλοτρίᾳ. "Bring them into bondage, and entreat them evil" → "enslave it and mistreat it": δουλώσουσιν, κακώσουσιν (the noun κάκωσις is "mistreatment" in v34).
- **"to whom they shall be in bondage" → "to which they will be slaves"** (v7) — Greek δουλεύσουσιν. "Serve me" → "worship me": λατρεύσουσιν, to serve God in worship.
- **"and so Abraham begat Isaac" → "and so he begot Isaac"** (v8) — Greek ἐγέννησεν; the Greek does not repeat the name, and it has no second and third verb ("Isaac … Jacob, and Jacob the twelve patriarchs"); "begot" is repeated once for English.
- **"moved with envy" → "being jealous of"** (v9) — Greek ζηλώσαντες. "But God was with him" → "and": καί.
- **"favour" → "grace"** (v10, 46) — Greek χάρις, here in the sense of favor. "Governor" → "leader": ἡγούμενον. By the grammar the subject of "appointed" is still God, and "his house" is Pharaoh's; "he" can also be read as Pharaoh (Gen 41:40–43). The verse has no second "over" (WH Treg and the SBLGNT; NA28 ἐφ' ὅλον).
- **"found no sustenance" → "were finding no sustenance"** (v11) — Greek οὐχ ηὕρισκον χορτάσματα, an imperfect; the word means fodder and food.
- **"corn" → "grain"** (v12) — Greek σιτία.
- **"Joseph's kindred was made known unto Pharaoh" → "the race of Joseph became manifest to Pharaoh"** (v13) — Greek φανερὸν ἐγένετο … τὸ γένος Ἰωσήφ; γένος ("race", descent) is the same word as in v19, and another word than "kindred" (συγγένεια, v3, 14).
- **"threescore and fifteen souls" → "in seventy-five souls"** (v14) — Greek ἐν ψυχαῖς ἑβδομήκοντα πέντε. This is the number of the Greek Old Testament (Gen 46:27; Exod 1:5 LXX); the Hebrew text has seventy (Gen 46:27; Exod 1:5; Deut 10:22).
- **"were carried over into Sychem … the sepulchre that Abraham bought … of the sons of Emmor" kept** (v16) — Greek as printed: ᾧ ὠνήσατο Ἀβραάμ … ἐν Συχέμ. In Genesis Abraham bought the cave at Hebron from Ephron the Hittite (Gen 23), and Jacob bought the field at Shechem from the sons of Hamor (Gen 33:19; Josh 24:32); Jacob was buried at Hebron (Gen 50:13), Joseph at Shechem. The verse is not harmonized. "In Shechem": ἐν Συχέμ (WH Treg NA28; RP τοῦ Συχέμ, "the father of Shechem"). "Sepulchre" → "grave": μνῆμα.
- **"which God had sworn to Abraham" → "which Elohim had declared to Abraham"** (v17) — Greek ὡμολόγησεν (WH Treg NA28; RP ὤμοσεν, "swore"). The verb is "confess" elsewhere; here it is used of a promise openly given, where "confess" would be wrong in English.
- **"another king arose" → "there rose up a different king over Egypt"** (v18) — Greek ἕτερος, a king of another kind (Exod 1:8 LXX); ἐπ' Αἴγυπτον is in WH Treg NA28, not in RP.
- **"dealt subtilly with our kindred, and evil entreated our fathers, so that they cast out their young children" → "dealing subtly with our race, mistreated the fathers, to make their babies exposed"** (v19) — Greek κατασοφισάμενος, ἐκάκωσεν, τοῦ ποιεῖν τὰ βρέφη ἔκθετα αὐτῶν. The Greek does not say who exposed them: the king had it done, or forced the fathers to do it. "The fathers": WH Treg and the SBLGNT; NA28 RP "our fathers".
- **"exceeding fair" → "beautiful to Elohim"** (v20) — Greek ἀστεῖος τῷ θεῷ, "beautiful to God", in God's eyes (Exod 2:2 LXX ἀστεῖον).
- **"nourished up" / "nourished him for her own son" → "brought up" / "brought him up for herself as a son"** (v20, 21) — Greek ἀνετράφη, ἀνεθρέψατο αὐτὸν ἑαυτῇ εἰς υἱόν. "Took him up": ἀνείλατο, lifted him up, also "adopted"; the same verb is "do away with" in v28.
- **"learned" → "trained"** (v22) — Greek ἐπαιδεύθη. "In all the wisdom": the verse follows ἐν πάσῃ σοφίᾳ (Treg NA28); WH RP and the SBLGNT have no ἐν ("with all the wisdom"). "Mighty in words and in deeds" → "powerful in his words and works": δυνατὸς ἐν λόγοις καὶ ἔργοις αὐτοῦ.
- **"when he was full forty years old" → "when a time of forty years was being fulfilled for him"** (v23) — Greek ὡς δὲ ἐπληροῦτο αὐτῷ τεσσερακονταετὴς χρόνος. "It came into his heart" → "it came up upon his heart": ἀνέβη ἐπὶ τὴν καρδίαν.
- **"suffer wrong" → "being wronged"** (v24) — Greek ἀδικούμενον; so "wrong" in v26, 27. The Greek has no "of them". "Avenged him that was oppressed, and smote" → "wrought vengeance for the one being oppressed, striking": ἐποίησεν ἐκδίκησιν … πατάξας.
- **"his brethren would have understood" → "the brothers understood"** (v25) — Greek συνιέναι τοὺς ἀδελφούς (WH Treg and the SBLGNT; NA28 RP add αὐτοῦ, "his"). "Would deliver them" → "was giving them salvation": δίδωσιν σωτηρίαν αὐτοῖς.
- **"would have set them at one again" → "was reconciling them unto peace"** (v26) — Greek συνήλλασσεν αὐτοὺς εἰς εἰρήνην, an imperfect: he was at it, he tried (RP συνήλασεν, "drove them together"). "As they strove" → "as they were fighting": μαχομένοις.
- **"Wilt thou kill me, as thou diddest" → "Do you want to do away with me in the manner in which you did away with"** (v28) — Greek ἀνελεῖν … ἀνεῖλες, "do away with", the usual word in Acts for killing. The question begins with μή and expects the answer "no".
- **"Then fled Moses at this saying, and was a stranger" → "And Moses fled at this word and became an alien"** (v29) — Greek ἐν τῷ λόγῳ τούτῳ, πάροικος (as in v6).
- **"when forty years were expired" → "when forty years were fulfilled"** (v30) — Greek πληρωθέντων. "An angel of the Lord in a flame of fire in a bush" → "an angel in a flame of fire of a bush": "of the Lord" is not in WH Treg NA28 (RP adds κυρίου); ἐν φλογὶ πυρὸς βάτου.
- **"he wondered at the sight: and as he drew near to behold it" → "marveled at the vision; and as he was approaching to observe it"** (v31) — Greek ἐθαύμασεν (WH Treg and the SBLGNT; NA28 RP ἐθαύμαζεν, "was marveling"), κατανοῆσαι (also in v32). "Unto him" is not in the critical text.
- **"the God of thy fathers"** (v32) — Greek τῶν πατέρων σου, plural; the Hebrew of Exod 3:6 has "of your father". "The God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob" → "and Isaac and Jacob": WH Treg NA28 do not repeat ὁ θεός (RP does).
- **"Then Moses trembled" → "And Moses, beginning to tremble"** (v32) — Greek ἔντρομος γενόμενος, "having become trembling".
- **"Put off thy shoes from thy feet" → "Loose the sandal of your feet"** (v33) — Greek Λῦσον τὸ ὑπόδημα τῶν ποδῶν σου, a singular.
- **"I have seen, I have seen the affliction" → "Seeing, I have seen the mistreatment"** (v34) — Greek ἰδὼν εἶδον, the Hebrew way of stressing a verb ("I have surely seen"); κάκωσις. "Their groaning": Greek αὐτοῦ, "its" (of the people; WH Treg and the SBLGNT; NA28 RP αὐτῶν). "I will send thee" → "let me send you": ἀποστείλω, a subjunctive (WH Treg NA28; RP ἀποστελῶ, "I will send").
- **"whom they refused" → "whom they denied"** (v35) — Greek ἠρνήσαντο. "Did God send to be a ruler and a deliverer by the hand" → "Elohim has sent as both ruler and ransomer, with the hand": ἀπέσταλκεν, a perfect; λυτρωτής, from λυτρόω "ransom"; σὺν χειρί (WH Treg NA28; RP ἐν χειρί).
- **"in the Red sea"** (v36) — Greek ἐν Ἐρυθρᾷ Θαλάσσῃ, "Red Sea", the Greek Old Testament's name for the Hebrew *Yam Suf*, "Sea of Reeds". "In the land of Egypt": ἐν γῇ Αἰγύπτῳ.
- **"the children of Israel" → "the sons of Israel"** (v23, 37) — Greek τοῖς υἱοῖς Ἰσραήλ.
- **"A prophet shall the Lord your God raise up unto you" → "A prophet will Elohim raise up for you"** (v37) — Greek ὁ θεός (WH Treg NA28; RP κύριος ὁ θεὸς ἡμῶν). "Him shall ye hear" is not in the critical text. "Raise up": ἀναστήσει.
- **"church in the wilderness" kept** (v38) — Greek ἐν τῇ ἐκκλησίᾳ, the assembly of Israel (Deut 18:16 LXX "the day of the assembly"); ἐκκλησία is "church" in every book.
- **"lively oracles" → "living oracles"** (v38) — Greek λόγια ζῶντα *logia zōnta*; archaic "lively" = living. "Received" → "accepted": ἐδέξατο. "To us": ἡμῖν (Treg NA28 RP; WH ὑμῖν, "to you").
- **"would not obey" → "were not willing to become obedient"** (v39) — Greek οὐκ ἠθέλησαν ὑπήκοοι γενέσθαι. "Thrust him from them" → "thrust him away": ἀπώσαντο, as in v27.
- **"we wot not what is become of him" → "we do not know what happened to him"** (v40) — Greek ἐγένετο (WH Treg NA28; RP γέγονεν). The sentence breaks off and starts anew, as in the Greek.
- **"offered sacrifice" → "brought up a sacrifice"** (v41) — Greek ἀνήγαγον θυσίαν.
- **"Then God turned" kept** (v42) — Greek ἔστρεψεν, "turned": he turned away from them, or turned them. "Delivered them up to worship": παρέδωκεν … λατρεύειν.
- **"have ye offered to me slain beasts"** (v42) — the question begins with μή and expects the answer "no". "The book of the prophets" is the scroll of the Twelve; the words are Amos 5:25–27 in the Greek.
- **"the star of your god Remphan, figures which ye made to worship them" → "the star of the god Rephan, the figures which you made to bow down to them"** (v43) — Greek τοῦ θεοῦ Ῥαιφάν (WH Treg and the SBLGNT; NA28 RP add ὑμῶν, "your"); the name is spelled Ῥαιφάν (NA28), Ῥομφά (WH), Ῥεφάν (Treg), Ῥεμφάν (RP). τύπους, "figures", is the same word as "pattern" in v44. προσκυνεῖν: "bow down to". The Hebrew of Amos 5:26 has other names (*Sikkut* your king and *Kiyyun* your images); the verse gives the Greek.
- **"beyond Babylon" kept** (v43) — Greek ἐπέκεινα Βαβυλῶνος. Amos 5:27 has "beyond Damascus", in the Hebrew and in the Greek Old Testament; the verse gives Stephen's word as printed.
- **"tabernacle of witness" → "tent of the testimony"** (v44) — Greek ἡ σκηνὴ τοῦ μαρτυρίου; σκηνή is "tent" also in v43. "Appointed" → "ordained": διετάξατο. "Fashion" → "pattern": τύπον.
- **"brought in with Jesus into the possession of the Gentiles" → "brought in with Joshua in the taking possession of the nations"** (v45) — Greek μετὰ Ἰησοῦ: Joshua, whose name in Greek is the same as "Jesus" (*Yehoshua* / *Yeshua*); ἐν τῇ κατασχέσει τῶν ἐθνῶν, the same word as "possession" in v5. "Drave out before the face" → "thrust out from the face": ἐξῶσεν ἀπὸ προσώπου.
- **"desired to find a tabernacle for the God of Jacob" → "asked to find a dwelling place for the Elohim of Jacob"** (v46) — Greek ᾐτήσατο; σκήνωμα, a tent-dwelling, from the root of σκηνή "tent" (Ps 132:5). "For the God of Jacob": τῷ θεῷ Ἰακώβ (WH Treg RP and the SBLGNT); NA28 reads τῷ οἴκῳ Ἰακώβ, "for the house of Jacob".
- **"dwelleth not in temples made with hands" → "does not dwell in things made by hands"** (v48) — Greek ἐν χειροποιήτοις, without a noun (WH Treg NA28; RP adds ναοῖς, "sanctuaries").
- **"earth is my footstool" → "the earth a footstool of my feet"** (v49) — Greek ὑποπόδιον τῶν ποδῶν μου. "Saith the Lord": λέγει κύριος, for the Name in Isa 66:1.
- **"Ye stiffnecked and uncircumcised in heart and ears, ye do always resist" → "Stiff-necked and uncircumcised in hearts and ears, you always strive against"** (v51) — Greek καρδίαις, a plural (WH Treg NA28; RP τῇ καρδίᾳ); ἀντιπίπτετε, "fall against", another verb than "resist" in 6:10 (ἀνθίστημι). "As your fathers did, so do ye" → "as your fathers, so also you": the Greek has no verb.
- **"which shewed before of the coming of the Just One; of whom ye have been now the betrayers and murderers" → "who announced beforehand about the coming of the Righteous One, of whom you have now become betrayers and murderers"** (v52) — Greek προκαταγγείλαντας, τῆς ἐλεύσεως τοῦ δικαίου, προδόται καὶ φονεῖς ἐγένεσθε. "Slain" → "killed": ἀπέκτειναν.
- **"by the disposition of angels, and have not kept it" → "as ordinances of angels, and did not guard it"** (v53) — Greek εἰς διαταγὰς ἀγγέλων, "unto ordinances of angels": as what angels ordained, or by the ordaining of angels (Gal 3:19; Heb 2:2). ἐφυλάξατε: "guard". "Law": Greek νόμον *nomon*; the Torah given at Sinai.
- **"they were cut to the heart" → "they were cut through in their hearts"** (v54) — Greek διεπρίοντο ταῖς καρδίαις, literally "were sawn through". "Gnashed on him with their teeth" → "were gnashing their teeth at him": ἔβρυχον, an imperfect.
- **"looked up stedfastly into heaven" → "gazing into heaven"** (v55) — Greek ἀτενίσας, as in 6:15.
- **"I see the heavens opened" → "I behold the heavens opened up"** (v56) — Greek θεωρῶ, διηνοιγμένους (WH Treg NA28; RP ἀνεῳγμένους).
- **"ran upon him" → "rushed … upon him"** (v57) — Greek ὥρμησαν.
- **"cast him out of the city, and stoned him" → "casting him outside the city, they were stoning him"** (v58) — Greek ἐλιθοβόλουν, an imperfect, here and in v59. "Laid down their clothes" → "laid aside their garments": ἀπέθεντο τὰ ἱμάτια. "Whose name was" → "called": καλουμένου.
- **"calling upon God, and saying" → "as he was calling upon and saying"** (v59) — Greek ἐπικαλούμενον καὶ λέγοντα; the Greek has no object: the one called upon is named in his words, "Lord Yeshua". "Receive" → "accept": δέξαι.
- **"he kneeled down" kept** (v60) — Greek θεὶς τὰ γόνατα, "placing the knees".
- **"lay not this sin to their charge" → "do not set this sin against them"** (v60) — Greek μὴ στήσῃς αὐτοῖς ταύτην τὴν ἁμαρτίαν, "do not make this sin stand for them".
