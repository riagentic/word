## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v35, 37 note) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v5, 12) — Hebrew messianic title.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v15, 17, 18, 19, 29) — consistent rendering.
- **"Peter" / "*Kefa*"** (v14, 20) — Aramaic name used consistently.
- **"baptized / baptizest" → "immersed / immersing"** (v12, 13, 16, 36, 38) — Greek *baptizo* = "to immerse"; consistent rendering.
- **"the Way" movement** — not yet directly named in this chapter; Philip preaches "the kingdom of *Elohim* and the name of *Yeshua*" (v12).
- **v32-33** — OT quotation: Isa 53:7-8 (LXX) — the Suffering Servant passage read by the Ethiopian official.
- **v37 absent** — KJV v37 ("I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God") is absent from the earliest MSS (p45, p74, Sinaiticus, Vaticanus); omitted with note.

### Notes
- **v32-33** quotes Isa 53:7-8.
- **v37** (absent from earliest MSS): "And Philip said, If thou believest with all thine heart, thou mayest. And he answered and said, I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God."
