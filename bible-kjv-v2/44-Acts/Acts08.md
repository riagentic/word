# Acts 8

[1] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ was consenting to his being done away with.

And there came on that day a great persecution upon the church which was in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; and all were scattered throughout the countries of ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ and ⟦samaria-nt|Shomron|Samaria⟧, except the apostles.

[2] And reverent men carried ⟦stephen|Stephanos|Stephen⟧ to burial and made great lamentation over him.

[3] But ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ was devastating the church, entering house by house; and dragging off both men and women, he was delivering them up to prison.

[4] So then those who were scattered went through, preaching the word.

[5] And ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧, going down to the city of ⟦samaria-nt|Shomron|Samaria⟧, was proclaiming to them the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[6] And the crowds with one accord were giving heed to the things said by ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧, as they heard and saw the signs which he was doing;

[7] for many of those who had unclean spirits — crying with a loud voice, they were coming out; and many who were paralyzed and lame were healed;

[8] and there came to be much joy in that city.

[9] But a certain man, ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ by name, was there beforehand in the city, practicing magic and amazing the nation of ⟦samaria-nt|Shomron|Samaria⟧, saying that he himself was someone great,

[10] to whom all were giving heed, from small to great, saying, "This one is the Power of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ which is called Great."

[11] And they were giving heed to him because for a considerable time he had amazed them with the magic arts.

[12] But when they believed ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧ preaching the gospel about the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and the name of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, they were being ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧, both men and women.

[13] And ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ himself also believed, and having been ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ he was persevering with ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧; and beholding signs and great powers taking place, he was amazed.

[14] Now the apostles in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, having heard that ⟦samaria-nt|Shomron|Samaria⟧ had accepted the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, sent to them ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧,

[15] who, having come down, prayed for them, that they might receive ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧;

[16] for it had not yet fallen upon any of them, but they had only been ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ into the name of the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[17] Then they were laying their hands upon them, and they were receiving ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧.

[18] Now ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧, seeing that through the laying on of the hands of the apostles the Spirit was given, offered them money,

[19] saying, "Give to me also this authority, that on whomever I lay my hands he may receive ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧."

[20] But ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ said to him, "May your silver be with you unto destruction, because you supposed that the gift of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is acquired through money.

[21] You have no part nor lot in this word, for your heart is not straight before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[22] Repent therefore from this malice of yours, and beseech the Lord, if perhaps the thought of your heart will be forgiven you;

[23] for I see that you are in gall of bitterness and a bond of unrighteousness."

[24] And ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧, answering, said, "Beseech the Lord for me yourselves, that nothing of the things you have said may come upon me."

[25] So then they, having solemnly testified and spoken the word of the Lord, were returning to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and they were preaching the gospel to many villages of the ⟦samaritans|Shomronim|Samaritans⟧.

[26] Now an angel of the Lord spoke to ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧, saying, "Rise and go toward the south, to the road that goes down from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧." This is desert.

[29] And the Spirit said to ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧, "Approach and be joined to this chariot."

[30] And ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧, running up, heard him reading ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ the prophet, and said, "Do you indeed know what you are reading?"

[31] And he said, "How indeed could I, unless someone will guide me?" And he appealed to ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧ to come up and sit with him.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v12, 16, 35, 37 note) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v5, 12) — Hebrew messianic title.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v15, 17, 19) — consistent rendering. In all three verses the Greek has no article (πνεῦμα ἅγιον), as in 2:4; v18 and v29 have "the Spirit" (τὸ πνεῦμα).
- **"Peter" / "*Kefa*"** (v14, 20) — Aramaic name used consistently; the Greek here has Πέτρος.
- **"baptized / baptizest" → "immersed / immersing"** (v12, 13, 16, 36, 38) — Greek *baptizo* = "to immerse"; consistent rendering.
- **"the Way" movement** — not yet directly named in this chapter; Philip preaches "the kingdom of *Elohim* and the name of *Yeshua*" (v12).
- **"was consenting unto his death" → "was consenting to his being done away with"** (v1) — Greek ἦν συνευδοκῶν τῇ ἀναιρέσει αὐτοῦ; ἀναίρεσις is the noun of ἀναιρέω, "do away with" (7:28).
- **"regions" → "countries"** (v1) — Greek χώρας. "Against the church" → "upon the church": ἐπί.
- **"devout men" → "reverent men"** (v2) — Greek εὐλαβεῖς. "Carried to his burial": συνεκόμισαν. "Lamentation": κοπετός, mourning with beating of the breast.
- **"made havock of" → "was devastating"** (v3) — Greek ἐλυμαίνετο, an imperfect, used of ruin and outrage; another verb than "ravage" in 9:21 (πορθέω). "Haling" → "dragging off": σύρων. "Committed them to prison" → "was delivering them up to prison": παρεδίδου.
- **"went every where preaching the word" → "went through, preaching the word"** (v4) — Greek διῆλθον εὐαγγελιζόμενοι τὸν λόγον: "bringing the word as good news"; the verb is "preach the gospel" in v12, 25, 40, and with the object "Yeshua" in v35.
- **"preached Christ" → "was proclaiming to them the Mashiach"** (v5) — Greek ἐκήρυσσεν, an imperfect, and another verb than in v4. "The city of Samaria": εἰς τὴν πόλιν (WH NA28); Treg RP have no article ("a city of Samaria").
- **"gave heed" kept** (v6, 10, 11) — Greek προσεῖχον, one verb in the three verses.
- **"For unclean spirits, crying with loud voice, came out of many that were possessed with them" → "for many of those who had unclean spirits — crying with a loud voice, they were coming out"** (v7) — Greek πολλοὶ γὰρ τῶν ἐχόντων πνεύματα ἀκάθαρτα βοῶντα φωνῇ μεγάλῃ ἐξήρχοντο (WH Treg NA28). The sentence changes its subject midway: it begins with "many of those who had" and ends with the spirits "crying … coming out". The verse leaves the break as it is. RP smooths it (πολλῶν … ἐξήρχετο, "out of many … they came out").
- **"taken with palsies" → "paralyzed"** (v7) — Greek παραλελυμένοι.
- **"used sorcery, and bewitched the people" → "practicing magic and amazing the nation"** (v9) — Greek μαγεύων, ἐξιστάνων τὸ ἔθνος; ἐξίστημι is the verb of "he was amazed" in v13: he amazed them (v9, 11), and then is amazed himself. "Was there beforehand": προϋπῆρχεν.
- **"from the least to the greatest" → "from small to great"** (v10) — Greek ἀπὸ μικροῦ ἕως μεγάλου. "This man is the great power of God" → "This one is the Power of Elohim which is called Great": ἡ καλουμένη Μεγάλη (WH Treg NA28; RP has no "called").
- **"of long time" → "for a considerable time"** (v11) — Greek ἱκανῷ χρόνῳ. "Sorceries" → "magic arts": ταῖς μαγείαις.
- **"preaching the things concerning the kingdom" → "preaching the gospel about the kingdom"** (v12) — Greek εὐαγγελιζομένῳ περί (WH Treg NA28; RP τὰ περί). "Were baptized" → "were being immersed": ἐβαπτίζοντο, an imperfect.
- **"continued with Philip" → "was persevering with Philip"** (v13) — Greek ἦν προσκαρτερῶν, as in 6:4. "Beholding the miracles and signs which were done" → "beholding signs and great powers taking place": σημεῖα καὶ δυνάμεις μεγάλας γινομένας (WH Treg NA28).
- **"had received the word" → "had accepted the word"** (v14) — Greek δέδεκται; "receive" in v15, 17, 19 is another verb (λαμβάνω).
- **"he was fallen upon none of them" → "it had not yet fallen upon any of them"** (v16) — Greek ἐπιπεπτωκός, a neuter participle agreeing with πνεῦμα; the gender is grammatical. "In the name" → "into the name": εἰς τὸ ὄνομα.
- **"laid they their hands … they received" → "were laying … were receiving"** (v17) — Greek ἐπετίθεσαν, ἐλάμβανον, imperfects: one after another.
- **"the Holy Ghost was given" → "the Spirit was given"** (v18) — Greek τὸ πνεῦμα (WH NA28; Treg RP add τὸ ἅγιον).
- **"power" → "authority"** (v19) — Greek ἐξουσίαν.
- **"Thy money perish with thee" → "May your silver be with you unto destruction"** (v20) — Greek τὸ ἀργύριόν σου σὺν σοὶ εἴη εἰς ἀπώλειαν. "Thou hast thought that the gift of God may be purchased with money" → "you supposed that the gift of Elohim is acquired through money": ἐνόμισας … διὰ χρημάτων κτᾶσθαι.
- **"in this matter" → "in this word"** (v21) — Greek ἐν τῷ λόγῳ τούτῳ: "word", here also the matter spoken of. "Right" → "straight": εὐθεῖα.
- **"wickedness" → "malice"** (v22) — Greek κακίας. "Pray God" → "beseech the Lord": δεήθητι τοῦ κυρίου (WH Treg NA28; RP θεοῦ); so "beseech" in v24, 34. "If perhaps … will be forgiven": εἰ ἄρα ἀφεθήσεται, a future.
- **"in the bond of iniquity" → "a bond of unrighteousness"** (v23) — Greek σύνδεσμον ἀδικίας. "In": Greek εἰς, "into".
- **"when they had testified" → "having solemnly testified"** (v25) — Greek διαμαρτυράμενοι. "Returned … and preached" → "were returning … and were preaching": ὑπέστρεφον, εὐηγγελίζοντο, imperfects (WH Treg NA28; RP aorists).
- **"toward the south" kept** (v26) — Greek κατὰ μεσημβρίαν, which can also mean "at noon". "Which is desert" → "This is desert": αὕτη ἐστὶν ἔρημος; "this" can be the road or Gaza, and the words can be the angel's or the narrator's.
- **"of great authority under Candace" → "a court official of Candace"** (v27) — Greek δυνάστης, one who holds power. "Had the charge of" → "was over": ἦν ἐπί. "For to worship" → "to bow down": προσκυνήσων.
- **"Go near, and join thyself" → "Approach and be joined"** (v29) — Greek Πρόσελθε καὶ κολλήθητι.
- **"Understandest thou what thou readest?" → "Do you indeed know what you are reading?"** (v30) — Greek Ἆρά γε γινώσκεις ἃ ἀναγινώσκεις; a play on the two verbs: "know" and "read" ("know again").
- **"How can I, except some man should guide me?" → "How indeed could I, unless someone will guide me?"** (v31) — Greek Πῶς γὰρ ἂν δυναίμην, an optative; ὁδηγήσει, a future (WH Treg NA28). "Desired" → "appealed to": παρεκάλεσεν.
- **"The place of the scripture" → "the passage of the Scripture"** (v32) — Greek περιοχή.
- **"like a lamb dumb before his shearer" → "as a lamb before the one who sheared it is voiceless"** (v32) — Greek τοῦ κείραντος (WH NA28, an aorist; Treg RP κείροντος, "the one shearing"), ἄφωνος.
- **"In his humiliation his judgment was taken away: and who shall declare his generation?" → "In the humiliation his judgment was taken away. His generation who will relate?"** (v33) — Greek ἐν τῇ ταπεινώσει without "his" (WH Treg and the SBLGNT; NA28 RP add αὐτοῦ); ἤρθη and αἴρεται are one verb, "taken away"; διηγήσεται. The words are Isa 53:7–8 in the Greek Old Testament. The Hebrew reads: "From oppression and from judgment he was taken; and his generation, who considers it? For he was cut off from the land of the living."
- **"I pray thee, of whom speaketh the prophet this?" → "I beseech you, about whom does the prophet say this?"** (v34) — Greek Δέομαί σου.
- **"preached unto him Jesus" → "preached Yeshua to him"** (v35) — Greek εὐηγγελίσατο αὐτῷ τὸν Ἰησοῦν: brought him Yeshua as good news.
- **"said" → "says"** (v36) — Greek φησιν, a present. "What doth hinder me to be baptized?": τί κωλύει με βαπτισθῆναι;
- **"he baptized him" kept** (v38) — Greek ἐβάπτισεν αὐτόν; the Greek does not repeat the name.
- **"caught away" → "snatched … away"** (v39) — Greek ἥρπασεν. "The Spirit of the Lord": πνεῦμα κυρίου, without articles. "And he went on his way" → "for he was going his way": γάρ.
- **"preached in all the cities" → "was preaching the gospel to all the cities"** (v40) — Greek εὐηγγελίζετο τὰς πόλεις πάσας, an imperfect.
- **v32-33** — OT quotation: Isa 53:7-8 (LXX) — the Suffering Servant passage read by the Ethiopian official.
- **v37 absent** — KJV v37 ("I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God") is not in WH, Treg, NA28 or RP; it stands in the Textus Receptus only. Omitted with note.

### Notes
- **v32-33** quotes Isa 53:7-8.
- **v37** (absent from the critical editions and the Byzantine text): "And Philip said, If thou believest with all thine heart, thou mayest. And he answered and said, I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God."
