# Acts 3

[1] Now ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ were going up into the temple at the hour of prayer, the ninth,

[2] and a certain man, being lame from his mother's womb, was being carried, whom they used to set daily at the door of the temple that is called Beautiful, to ask alms from those going into the temple,

[3] who, seeing ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ about to go into the temple, was requesting to receive alms.

[4] And ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, gazing at him, with ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, said, "Look at us."

[5] And he fixed his attention on them, expecting to receive something from them.

[6] But ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ said, "Silver and gold I have none, but what I have, this I give to you: **in the name of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ the ⟦nazarenes|Notzri|Nazorean⟧, arise and walk!**"

[7] And seizing him by the right hand, he raised him; and instantly his feet and ankles were made strong,

[8] and leaping up he stood and was walking, and he entered with them into the temple, walking and leaping and praising ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[9] And all the people saw him walking and praising ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[10] and they were recognizing him, that this was the one who used to sit for alms at the Beautiful Gate of the temple, and they were filled with wonder and amazement at what had happened to him.

[11] And as he was holding ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, all the people ran together to them at the colonnade called ⟦solomon|Shlomo's|Solomon's⟧, utterly astounded.

[12] And seeing it, ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ answered the people: "Men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, why do you marvel at this, or why do you gaze at us, as though by our own power or godliness we had made him walk?

[13] The ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ and ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ and ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of our fathers, glorified his servant ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, whom you delivered up and denied before the face of ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧, when that one had judged to release him.

[14] But you denied the Holy and Righteous One, and asked for a man, a murderer, to be granted to you,

[15] but the Author of life you killed, whom ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ raised from the dead, of which we are witnesses.

[16] And upon the faith of his name, his name made strong this man whom you behold and know, and the faith which is through him gave him this wholeness before you all.

[17] "And now, brothers, I know that you acted in ignorance, even as your rulers also;

[18] but the things which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ proclaimed beforehand through the mouth of all the prophets, that his ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧ should suffer, he fulfilled thus.

[19] Repent therefore and turn, that your sins may be blotted out,

[20] so that times of refreshing may come from the face of the Lord, and he may send the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧ appointed beforehand for you, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧,

[21] whom heaven must receive until times of restoration of all things, of which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ spoke through the mouth of his holy prophets from of old.

[22] ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ indeed said, '**⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The Lord⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will raise up for you a prophet from your brothers, like me; him you shall hear in all things, whatever he speaks to you.**

[23] And it shall be that every soul that does not hear that prophet shall be utterly destroyed from the people.'

[24] And all the prophets also, from ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ and those following after, as many as spoke, also proclaimed these days.

[25] You are the sons of the prophets and of the covenant which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ covenanted with your fathers, saying to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, '**And in your seed shall all the families of the earth be blessed.**'

[26] To you first ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, having raised up his servant, sent him blessing you, in turning each one away from your acts of evil."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v18, 20) — Hebrew messianic title.
- **"Peter" / "*Kefa*"** (v1, 4, 6, 11, 12) — Aramaic name used consistently; the Greek has Πέτρος.
- **"his Son Jesus" → "his servant *Yeshua*"** (v13, 26) — Greek παῖς *pais* = "child, boy" or "servant"; the Septuagint's word for the "Servant" of Isa 42:1; 52:13. Not the word for "slave" (δοῦλος) nor for "son" (υἱός).
- **"lame from his mother's womb" kept; "the gate of the temple which is called Beautiful" → "the door of the temple that is called Beautiful"** (v2) — Greek θύρα "door"; v10 has πύλη "gate". No word "birth" in the Greek.
- **"asked an alms" → "was requesting to receive alms"** (v3) — Greek ἠρώτα … λαβεῖν, imperfect; a different verb from "ask" (αἰτεῖν) in v2.
- **"fastening his eyes upon him with John" → "gazing at him, with *Yochanan*"** (v4) — Greek ἀτενίσας, as 1:10 and v12.
- **"he gave heed unto them" → "he fixed his attention on them"** (v5) — Greek ἐπεῖχεν αὐτοῖς.
- **"Silver and gold have I none; but such as I have give I thee" → "Silver and gold I have none, but what I have, this I give to you"** (v6) — two Greek verbs: οὐχ ὑπάρχει μοι, "there is none belonging to me", and ἔχω, "I have".
- **"rise up and walk" → "arise and walk"** (v6) — the verse follows ἔγειρε καὶ περιπάτει (Treg NA28; RP ἔγειραι καὶ περιπάτει); WH and the printed SBLGNT have only περιπάτει, "walk". "Of Nazareth" → "the Nazorean": Greek τοῦ Ναζωραίου, as 2:22.
- **"he took him by the right hand, and lifted him up" → "seizing him by the right hand, he raised him"** (v7) — Greek πιάσας, ἤγειρεν.
- **"immediately his feet and ancle bones received strength" → "instantly his feet and ankles were made strong"** (v7) — Greek παραχρῆμα; ἐστερεώθησαν, "were made firm, solid", the verb of v16.
- **"stood, and walked" → "stood and was walking"** (v8) — Greek περιεπάτει, imperfect; no "began".
- **"they knew that it was he which sat for alms" → "they were recognizing him, that this was the one who used to sit for alms"** (v10) — Greek ἐπεγίνωσκον, imperfect; πρὸς τὴν ἐλεημοσύνην, "for the alms"; no "asking".
- **"wonder and amazement" kept** (v10) — Greek θάμβους καὶ ἐκστάσεως; θάμβος is the root of "utterly astounded" (ἔκθαμβοι) in v11.
- **"as the lame man which was healed held Peter and John" → "as he was holding *Kefa* and *Yochanan*"** (v11) — Greek κρατοῦντος δὲ αὐτοῦ; "the lame man which was healed" is the Byzantine reading. "Colonnade" is στοά (KJV "porch"), as John 10:23.
- **"he answered unto the people" → "answered the people"** (v12) — Greek ἀπεκρίνατο. "At this" (ἐπὶ τούτῳ) can also be "at this man".
- **"why look ye so earnestly on us" → "why do you gaze at us"; "holiness" → "godliness"** (v12) — Greek ἀτενίζετε; εὐσεβείᾳ.
- **"The God of Abraham, and of Isaac, and of Jacob" → "The *Elohim* of *Avraham* and *Yitzchaq* and *Yaaqov*"** (v13) — the printed text has θεός once (WH, Treg, SBLGNT, RP); NA28 repeats it: "the God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob" (Exod 3:6).
- **"in the presence of Pilate, when he was determined to let him go" → "before the face of *Pilatus*, when that one had judged to release him"** (v13) — Greek κατὰ πρόσωπον Πιλάτου, κρίναντος ἐκείνου ἀπολύειν. "Delivered up" is παρεδώκατε.
- **"desired a murderer to be granted unto you" → "asked for a man, a murderer, to be granted to you"** (v14) — Greek ᾐτήσασθε ἄνδρα φονέα χαρισθῆναι ὑμῖν.
- **"the Prince of life" → "the Author of life"** (v15) — Greek τὸν ἀρχηγὸν τῆς ζωῆς, "leader, originator", as Heb 2:10; 12:2.
- **"whereof we are witnesses" → "of which we are witnesses"** (v15) — Greek οὗ, "of which" or "of whom", as 2:32. "Raised" here is ἤγειρεν.
- **"through faith in his name" → "upon the faith of his name"** (v16) — Greek ἐπὶ τῇ πίστει τοῦ ὀνόματος αὐτοῦ, a plain genitive: faith in his name, or the faith that belongs to his name (WH omits ἐπί).
- **"the faith which is by him hath given him this perfect soundness" → "the faith which is through him gave him this wholeness"** (v16) — Greek ἡ πίστις ἡ δι' αὐτοῦ; the Greek has "him", not the name. ὁλοκληρία is "wholeness, soundness in every part". "Behold" is θεωρεῖτε.
- **"through ignorance ye did it" → "you acted in ignorance"** (v17) — Greek κατὰ ἄγνοιαν ἐπράξατε, "according to ignorance"; no object.
- **"had shewed before" → "proclaimed beforehand"** (v18) — Greek προκατήγγειλεν, the verb of v24 (κατήγγειλαν) with προ-. "His *Mashiach*" (τὸν χριστὸν αὐτοῦ) with the four critical editions.
- **"be converted" → "turn"** (v19) — Greek ἐπιστρέψατε, active. The verse follows εἰς τὸ ἐξαλειφθῆναι (Treg NA28 RP; WH and the printed SBLGNT πρός): no change in translation.
- **"when the times of refreshing shall come from the presence of the Lord" → "so that times of refreshing may come from the face of the Lord"** (v20) — Greek ὅπως ἄν, a purpose; ἀπὸ προσώπου. ἀνάψυξις is "cooling, relief, refreshing" (the verb in 2 Tim 1:16).
- **"Jesus Christ, which before was preached unto you" → "the *Mashiach* appointed beforehand for you, *Yeshua*"** (v20) — Greek τὸν προκεχειρισμένον ὑμῖν χριστὸν Ἰησοῦν; προχειρίζομαι is "take in hand, choose, appoint" (22:14; 26:16), not "preach".
- **"restitution of all things" → "restoration of all things"** (v21) — Greek ἀποκαταστάσεως *apokatastaseōs*, the noun of "restore" in 1:6. "Of which" (ὧν) can refer to "all things" or to "times".
- **"whom the heaven must receive" kept** (v21) — Greek δέξασθαι, elsewhere "accept"; here "take in".
- **"since the world began" → "from of old"** (v21) — Greek ἀπ' αἰῶνος, "from the age".
- **"A prophet shall the Lord your God raise up unto you of your brethren, like unto me" kept in substance** (v22) — the verse follows ὁ θεὸς ὑμῶν "your God" (Treg NA28; ἡμῶν "our" RP; WH and the printed SBLGNT have no pronoun). ὡς ἐμέ is "like me" or "as he raised up me". ἀναστήσει "will raise up" is the verb used of the resurrection in 2:24, 32.
- **"shall be destroyed from among the people" → "shall be utterly destroyed from the people"** (v23) — Greek ἐξολεθρευθήσεται, from ὄλεθρος "ruin".
- **"have likewise foretold of these days" → "also proclaimed these days"** (v24) — Greek κατήγγειλαν, "proclaimed"; τῶν καθεξῆς is "those in order after".
- **"the covenant which God made" → "the covenant which *Elohim* covenanted"** (v25) — Greek διαθήκης ἧς διέθετο, noun and verb of one root. "Your fathers" with WH, Treg and NA28 ("our" RP).
- **"kindreds" → "families"; "seed" kept** (v25) — Greek πατριαί; σπέρματι.
- **"his Son Jesus" → "his servant"** (v26) — the four critical editions have no name here. "Having raised up" (ἀναστήσας) can mean raising up a prophet (v22) or raising from the dead.
- **"in turning away every one of you from his iniquities" → "in turning each one away from your acts of evil"** (v26) — Greek ἐν τῷ ἀποστρέφειν ἕκαστον ἀπὸ τῶν πονηριῶν ὑμῶν; the verb can also be intransitive: "when each one turns away".
- **v22-23** — OT quotation: Deut 18:15-19 (Moses' prophecy of a coming prophet) + Lev 23:29.
- **v25** — OT quotation: Gen 22:18 ("In your seed shall all the families of the earth be blessed").

### Notes
- **v22-23** quotes Deut 18:15-19 + Lev 23:29.
- **v25** quotes Gen 22:18.
