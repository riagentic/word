# Acts 5

[1] But a certain man, ⟦ananias|Chananyah|Ananias⟧ by name, with ⟦sapphira|Shappira|Sapphira⟧ his wife, sold a possession

[2] and kept back for himself from the price, his wife also being aware of it, and bringing a certain part, he laid it beside the feet of the apostles.

[3] But ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ said, "⟦ananias|Chananyah|Ananias⟧, why did Satan fill your heart, for you to lie to the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ and to keep back for yourself from the price of the plot?

[4] While it remained, did it not remain yours, and when sold, was it not in your authority? Why is it that you set this deed in your heart? You did not lie to men but to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[5] And ⟦ananias|Chananyah|Ananias⟧, hearing these words, fell down and expired. And great fear came upon all who were hearing.

[6] And the younger men, rising, wrapped him up, and carrying him out, they buried him.

[7] Now there came an interval of about three hours, and his wife came in, not knowing what had come about.

[8] And ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ answered her, "Tell me, did you sell the plot for so much?" And she said, "Yes, for so much."

[9] But ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ said to her, "Why is it that it was agreed between you to tempt the Spirit of the Lord? Behold, the feet of those who buried your husband are at the door, and they will carry you out."

[10] And she fell down instantly at his feet and expired. And the young men, coming in, found her dead, and carrying her out, they buried her beside her husband.

[11] And great fear came upon the whole church and upon all who were hearing these things.

[12] Now through the hands of the apostles many signs and wonders were coming about among the people. And they were all with one accord in ⟦solomon|Shlomo's|Solomon's⟧ Colonnade;

[13] but of the rest no one dared to join himself to them, yet the people were magnifying them,

[14] and all the more believers were being added to the Lord, multitudes both of men and of women,

[15] so that they even carried out the sick into the streets and laid them on little beds and pallets, so that, as ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ came, at least his shadow might overshadow someone of them.

[16] And the multitude also of the cities around ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ was coming together, bringing the sick and those harassed by unclean spirits, who were all being healed.

[17] But the high priest rose up, and all those with him (which is the sect of the ⟦sadducees|Tzedukim|Sadducees⟧), and they were filled with jealousy

[18] and laid hands on the apostles and put them in public custody.

[19] But an angel of the Lord during the night opened the doors of the prison, and leading them out, said,

[20] "Go, and standing, speak in the temple to the people all the words of this life."

[21] And having heard, they entered into the temple about daybreak and were teaching.

Now the high priest came, and those with him, and they called together the ⟦sanhedrin|Sanhedrin|council⟧ and all the senate of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and sent to the jail for them to be brought.

[22] But the officers who came did not find them in the prison; and turning back, they reported,

[23] saying, "The jail we found shut in all security and the guards standing at the doors, but having opened, we found no one inside."

[24] Now when both the captain of the temple and the high priests heard these words, they were utterly at a loss concerning them, what this might come to be.

[25] But someone came and reported to them, "Behold, the men whom you put in the prison are in the temple, standing and teaching the people."

[26] Then the captain, going off with the officers, was bringing them, not with force, for they feared the people, lest they be stoned.

[27] And having brought them, they set them in the ⟦sanhedrin|Sanhedrin|council⟧. And the high priest questioned them,

[28] saying, "With a charge we charged you not to teach upon this name, and behold, you have filled ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ with your teaching, and you wish to bring upon us the blood of this man."

[29] But ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and the apostles answered and said, "**One must obey ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ rather than men.**

[30] The ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of our fathers raised ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, whom you slew, hanging him upon a tree.

[31] This one ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ lifted up by his right hand as Author and Savior, to give repentance to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and forgiveness of sins.

[32] And we are witnesses of these words, and so is the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧, which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave to those who obey him."

[33] But they, having heard, were cut through and were wishing to do away with them.

[34] But a certain ⟦pharisee|Parush|Pharisee⟧ rising up in the ⟦sanhedrin|Sanhedrin|council⟧, ⟦gamaliel-pharisee|Gamliel|Gamaliel⟧ by name, a teacher of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ honored by all the people, commanded to put the men outside for a little,

[35] and he said to them, "Men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, give heed to yourselves as to these men, what you are about to do.

[36] For before these days Theudas rose up, saying that he himself was someone, to whom a number of men, about four hundred, attached themselves; who was done away with, and all, as many as were persuaded by him, were dispersed and came to nothing.

[37] After this one ⟦jude|Yehudah|Judas⟧ the Galilean rose up in the days of the registration and drew away people after him; that one also perished, and all, as many as were persuaded by him, were scattered.

[38] And now I say to you, stand away from these men and leave them; because if this counsel or this work is from men, it will be torn down,

[39] but **if it is from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you will not be able to tear them down — lest you even be found fighters against ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.**" And they were persuaded by him,

[40] and having called the apostles to them and beaten them, they charged them not to speak upon the name of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, and released them.

[41] So then they were going from the face of the ⟦sanhedrin|Sanhedrin|council⟧, rejoicing that they were counted worthy to be dishonored for the name;

[42] and every day, in the temple and from house to house, they did not cease teaching and preaching ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v3, 32) — consistent rendering. In v9 the Greek has τὸ πνεῦμα κυρίου, "the Spirit of the Lord".
- **"Peter" / "*Kefa*"** (v3, 8, 9, 15, 29) — Aramaic name used consistently; the Greek has Πέτρος.
- **"Satan"** (v3) — retained in Hebrew form as a name/title.
- **"hanged on a tree"** (v30) — Greek κρεμάσαντες ἐπὶ ξύλου; echoes Deut 21:22-23 (hanging on a tree = cursed); the OT legal framing is deliberate. ξύλον is "wood", as Gal 3:13.
- **"captain" / "chief captain"** — Greek στρατηγός *stratēgos* = military commander; ὁ στρατηγὸς τοῦ ἱεροῦ, "the captain of the temple" (v24, 26; 4:1). The Greek has no word "guard".
- **"sold a possession, and kept back part of the price" → "sold a possession and kept back for himself from the price"** (v1, 2) — Greek κτῆμα (as 2:45); ἐνοσφίσατο ἀπὸ τῆς τιμῆς, "set aside for himself", the verb of Titus 2:10 ("pilfering") and of Josh 7:1 in the Septuagint. No word "proceeds".
- **"his wife also being privy to it" → "his wife also being aware of it"** (v2) — Greek συνειδυίης, "knowing together with him".
- **"to lie to the Holy Ghost"** (v3) — Greek ψεύσασθαί σε τὸ πνεῦμα τὸ ἅγιον, with the accusative: "to lie to" or "to deceive"; in v4 "lie to" has the dative. "Plot" is χωρίον, as 4:34.
- **"was it not in thine own power? why hast thou conceived this thing in thine heart?" → "was it not in your authority? Why is it that you set this deed in your heart?"** (v4) — Greek ἐν τῇ σῇ ἐξουσίᾳ; ἔθου … τὸ πρᾶγμα τοῦτο. "Remained" is μένω, elsewhere "abide". "Men" is plural.
- **"gave up the ghost" → "expired"** (v5, 10) — Greek ἐξέψυξεν.
- **"the young men" → "the younger men"** (v6) — Greek οἱ νεώτεροι, a comparative; v10 has οἱ νεανίσκοι, "the young men".
- **"it was about the space of three hours after" → "there came an interval of about three hours"** (v7) — Greek Ἐγένετο δὲ ὡς ὡρῶν τριῶν διάστημα καί.
- **"Peter answered unto her" kept** (v8) — Greek ἀπεκρίθη; "you sold" (ἀπέδοσθε) is plural.
- **"Then Peter said unto her" → "But *Kefa* said to her"** (v9) — the printed text has no verb (ὁ δὲ Πέτρος πρὸς αὐτήν); "said" is supplied (RP adds εἶπεν).
- **"How is it that ye have agreed together to tempt the Spirit of the Lord?" → "Why is it that it was agreed between you to tempt the Spirit of the Lord?"** (v9) — Greek συνεφωνήθη ὑμῖν, a passive; πειράσαι is "tempt, put to the test".
- **"straightway" → "instantly"** (v10) — Greek παραχρῆμα.
- **"by the hands of the apostles were many signs and wonders wrought" → "through the hands of the apostles many signs and wonders were coming about"** (v12) — Greek ἐγίνετο, imperfect; no word "regularly".
- **"with one accord in Solomon's porch" → "with one accord in *Shlomo's* Colonnade"** (v12) — Greek ὁμοθυμαδόν; στοά as 3:11.
- **"durst no man join himself to them: but the people magnified them" kept in substance** (v13) — Greek κολλᾶσθαι, "be glued to"; ἐμεγάλυνεν.
- **"believers were the more added to the Lord" → "all the more believers were being added to the Lord"** (v14) — Greek μᾶλλον δὲ προσετίθεντο πιστεύοντες τῷ κυρίῳ. "The Lord" can go with "added" (as 11:24) or with "believing": "those believing the Lord were being added".
- **"on beds and couches" → "on little beds and pallets"** (v15) — Greek κλιναρίων (WH Treg NA28; κλινῶν RP) καὶ κραβάττων. "The sick" is τοὺς ἀσθενεῖς, "the weak". "Overshadow" is ἐπισκιάσῃ.
- **"them which were vexed with unclean spirits" → "those harassed by unclean spirits"** (v16) — Greek ὀχλουμένους. "Cities" is πόλεων.
- **"were filled with indignation" → "were filled with jealousy"** (v17) — Greek ζήλου, "zeal" or "jealousy". "Which is the sect" is ἡ οὖσα αἵρεσις.
- **"put them in the common prison" → "put them in public custody"** (v18) — Greek ἐν τηρήσει δημοσίᾳ, as 4:3; δημοσίᾳ can also be an adverb, "publicly". "Laid hands on" is ἐπέβαλον τὰς χεῖρας, not a word for arrest. Three words follow: τήρησις "custody" (v18), φυλακή "prison" (v19, 22, 25), δεσμωτήριον "jail" (v21, 23).
- **"all the words of this life" kept** (v20) — Greek τὰ ῥήματα τῆς ζωῆς ταύτης.
- **"the council together, and all the senate of the children of Israel" → "the *Sanhedrin* and all the senate of the sons of *Yisrael*"** (v21) — Greek τὸ συνέδριον καὶ πᾶσαν τὴν γερουσίαν τῶν υἱῶν Ἰσραήλ; the "and" may explain ("that is, all the senate") or add.
- **"The prison truly found we shut with all safety" → "The jail we found shut in all security"** (v23) — Greek ἐν πάσῃ ἀσφαλείᾳ.
- **"the high priest and the captain of the temple and the chief priests … doubted of them whereunto this would grow" → "both the captain of the temple and the high priests … were utterly at a loss concerning them, what this might come to be"** (v24) — the four critical editions have no "the high priest and" (ἱερεὺς καί RP); οἱ ἀρχιερεῖς is the plural of "high priest"; διηπόρουν as 2:12.
- **"brought them without violence" → "was bringing them, not with force"** (v26) — the verse follows ἦγεν, imperfect (WH NA28; 2–2, NA28 decides; ἤγαγεν "brought" Treg, RP and the printed SBLGNT). "Officers" is ὑπηρέται.
- **"Did not we straitly command you" → "With a charge we charged you"** (v28) — Greek Παραγγελίᾳ παρηγγείλαμεν, noun and verb of one root. The printed text (WH, Treg, SBLGNT) is a statement; NA28 and RP begin with Οὐ, making it a question: "Did we not charge you …?" "Upon this name" is ἐπὶ τῷ ὀνόματι τούτῳ, as 4:17. "You wish" is βούλεσθε.
- **"We ought to obey God rather than men" → "One must obey *Elohim* rather than men"** (v29) — Greek Πειθαρχεῖν δεῖ, impersonal; πειθαρχέω is "obey one in authority" (also v32).
- **"whom ye slew and hanged on a tree" → "whom you slew, hanging him upon a tree"** (v30) — Greek διεχειρίσασθε κρεμάσαντες; διαχειρίζομαι is "lay violent hands on, kill". "Raised" is ἤγειρεν: from the dead, or "raised up" as a deliverer.
- **"Him hath God exalted with his right hand to be a Prince and a Saviour" → "This one *Elohim* lifted up by his right hand as Author and Savior"** (v31) — Greek ἀρχηγὸν καὶ σωτῆρα ὕψωσεν τῇ δεξιᾷ αὐτοῦ; ἀρχηγός, "leader, originator", as 3:15; τῇ δεξιᾷ is "by" or "to his right hand", as 2:33.
- **"we are his witnesses of these things; and so is also the Holy Ghost, whom God hath given" → "we are witnesses of these words, and so is the *Ruach HaQodesh*, which *Elohim* gave"** (v32) — Greek τῶν ῥημάτων τούτων, "words" or "things spoken of"; "his" is the Byzantine reading. "So is" is supplied. The relative ὅ is neuter because πνεῦμα is neuter: the gender is grammatical.
- **"they were cut to the heart, and took counsel to slay them" → "they were cut through and were wishing to do away with them"** (v33) — Greek διεπρίοντο, "were sawn through" (7:54); no "heart" here. ἐβούλοντο; ἀνελεῖν "do away with", that is, kill (also v36).
- **"a doctor of the law, had in reputation among all the people" → "a teacher of the *Torah* honored by all the people"** (v34) — Greek νομοδιδάσκαλος τίμιος παντὶ τῷ λαῷ. "The men" (τοὺς ἀνθρώπους) with the four critical editions; "the apostles" RP.
- **"take heed to yourselves what ye intend to do as touching these men" → "give heed to yourselves as to these men, what you are about to do"** (v35) — Greek προσέχετε ἑαυτοῖς ἐπὶ τοῖς ἀνθρώποις τούτοις τί μέλλετε πράσσειν; "as to these men" can go with "give heed" or with "do".
- **"boasting himself to be somebody; to whom a number of men … joined themselves: who was slain; and all, as many as obeyed him, were scattered, and brought to nought" → "saying that he himself was someone, to whom a number of men … attached themselves; who was done away with, and all, as many as were persuaded by him, were dispersed and came to nothing"** (v36) — Greek λέγων; προσεκλίθη, "leaned toward"; ἀνῃρέθη; ἐπείθοντο.
- **"in the days of the taxing, and drew away much people after him" → "in the days of the registration and drew away people after him"** (v37) — Greek τῆς ἀπογραφῆς, "enrollment"; ἀπέστησε λαόν, "made people revolt"; "much" is the Byzantine reading. "That one also" is κἀκεῖνος.
- **"Refrain from these men, and let them alone: for if this counsel or this work be of men, it will come to nought" → "stand away from these men and leave them; because if this counsel or this work is from men, it will be torn down"** (v38) — Greek ἀπόστητε, the verb of "drew away" in v37; ἄφετε; καταλυθήσεται, the verb of v39.
- **"ye cannot overthrow it; lest haply ye be found even to fight against God" → "you will not be able to tear them down — lest you even be found fighters against *Elohim*"** (v39) — Greek αὐτούς "them" (the four critical editions; αὐτό "it" RP); θεομάχοι, "God-fighters", one word. The "lest" clause hangs on "leave them" in v38; the printed text sets the words between in brackets.
- **"to him they agreed" → "they were persuaded by him"** (v39) — Greek ἐπείσθησαν, the verb of v36, 37.
- **"they commanded that they should not speak in the name of Jesus" → "they charged them not to speak upon the name of *Yeshua*"** (v40) — Greek παρήγγειλαν; ἐπὶ τῷ ὀνόματι.
- **"they departed from the presence of the council, rejoicing that they were counted worthy to suffer shame for his name" → "they were going from the face of the *Sanhedrin*, rejoicing that they were counted worthy to be dishonored for the name"** (v41) — Greek ἀπὸ προσώπου; ἀτιμασθῆναι; ὑπὲρ τοῦ ὀνόματος, "on behalf of the name", with no "his" (the four critical editions; "the name of Jesus" RP).
- **"they ceased not to teach and preach Jesus Christ" → "they did not cease teaching and preaching the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v42) — Greek εὐαγγελιζόμενοι τὸν χριστὸν Ἰησοῦν, "bringing as good news the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*": the name and title as the object, or "that the *Mashiach* is *Yeshua*".
