# Philippians 4

[1] So then, my brothers, beloved and longed for, my joy and crown, thus stand in the Lord, beloved.

[2] Euodia I appeal to and Syntyche I appeal to, to think the same thing in the Lord.

[3] Yes, I request you also, genuine yokefellow, help them, who strove together with me in the gospel, with ⟦clement|Clemens|Clement⟧ also and the rest of my fellow workers, whose names are in the book of life.

[4] **Rejoice in the Lord always; again I will say, rejoice.**

[5] Let your forbearance be known to all people. The Lord is near;

[6] **be anxious about nothing, but in everything by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.**

[7] **And the peace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, which surpasses every mind, will guard your hearts and your thoughts in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.**

[8] For the rest, brothers, **whatever things are true, whatever dignified, whatever righteous, whatever pure, whatever lovely, whatever of good report, if there is any virtue and if any praise, consider these things**.

[9] The things which you also learned and received and heard and saw in me, these practice; and the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of peace will be with you.

[10] But I rejoiced in the Lord greatly that now at last you have made your thinking on my behalf bloom again; of this you were also thinking, but you lacked opportunity.

[11] Not that I speak according to lack, for I have learned, in the things in which I am, to be content.

[12] I know also how to be humbled, I know also how to abound; in everything and in all things I have been initiated, both to be filled and to hunger, both to abound and to lack.

[13] **I have strength for all things in him who empowers me.**

[14] Nevertheless you did well, having become fellow sharers in my affliction.

[15] And you also know, Philippians, that in the beginning of the gospel, when I went out from ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧, no church shared with me unto an account of giving and receiving except you only,

[16] for even in ⟦thessalonica|Thessalonike|Thessalonica⟧ you sent, both once and twice, unto my need.

[17] Not that I seek the gift, but I seek the fruit that increases unto your account.

[18] But I have received all things in full and abound; I have been filled, having accepted from ⟦epaphroditus|Epaphroditos|Epaphroditus⟧ the things from you, a fragrance of sweet smell, a sacrifice acceptable, well-pleasing to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[19] And my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will fill every need of yours according to his riches in glory in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[20] Now to our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and Father be the glory unto the ages of the ages. Amen.

[21] Greet every saint in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧. The brothers with me greet you.

[22] All the saints greet you, but especially those of Caesar's household.

[23] The grace of the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ be with your spirit.

## Changes

- **"Christ Jesus" / "Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*" / "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv6, 7, 9, 18, 19, 20) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (vv23) — in closing benediction.
- **"my brethren dearly beloved and longed for" → "my brothers, beloved and longed for"** (v1) — Greek ἀγαπητοὶ καὶ ἐπιπόθητοι, two adjectives (ἐπιποθέω as 1:8; 2:26); the last word is again ἀγαπητοί, without "my".
- **"so stand fast" → "thus stand"** (v1) — Greek οὕτως στήκετε, as 1:27.
- **"I beseech … that they be of the same mind" → "I appeal to … to think the same thing"** (v2) — Greek παρακαλῶ, twice; here in the sense "appeal" (as Phlm 9–10), the noun παράκλησις is "encouragement" (2:1); τὸ αὐτὸ φρονεῖν as 2:2.
- **"true yokefellow" → "genuine yokefellow"** (v3) — Greek γνήσιε σύζυγε, "one joined under the same yoke"; γνήσιος as 2:20. Σύζυγος may also be a man's name.
- **"help those women which laboured with me" → "help them, who strove together with me"** (v3) — Greek συλλαμβάνου αὐταῖς ("them", feminine), αἵτινες … συνήθλησάν μοι; the verb of 1:27.
- **"moderation" → "forbearance"** (v5) — Greek τὸ ἐπιεικές.
- **"at hand" → "near"** (v5) — Greek ἐγγύς, near in place or in time.
- **"Be careful for nothing" → "be anxious about nothing"** (v6) — Greek μηδὲν μεριμνᾶτε, the verb of 2:20.
- **"passeth all understanding" → "surpasses every mind"** (v7) — Greek ὑπερέχουσα πάντα νοῦν; ὑπερέχω as 2:3; 3:8.
- **"shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus" → "will guard your hearts and your thoughts in the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v7) — Greek φρουρήσει, "guard as a garrison"; νοήματα, "thoughts"; ἐν, "in".
- **"honest" → "dignified"; "just" → "righteous"** (v8) — Greek σεμνά (as Titus 2:2), δίκαια.
- **"think on these things" → "consider these things"** (v8) — Greek λογίζεσθε, "reckon, take into account", as 3:13.
- **"your care of me hath flourished again; wherein ye were also careful" → "you have made your thinking on my behalf bloom again; of this you were also thinking"** (v10) — Greek ἀνεθάλετε τὸ ὑπὲρ ἐμοῦ φρονεῖν, ἐφ' ᾧ καὶ ἐφρονεῖτε: φρονέω twice, as 1:7. ἀνεθάλετε can also be "you have bloomed again as to your thinking on my behalf".
- **"in respect of want" → "according to lack"** (v11) — Greek καθ' ὑστέρησιν; the root of "to lack" (v12) and of 2:30.
- **"content" kept** (v11) — Greek αὐτάρκης, "sufficient in oneself".
- **"in whatsoever state I am" → "in the things in which I am"** (v11) — Greek ἐν οἷς εἰμι.
- **"to be abased" → "to be humbled"** (v12) — Greek ταπεινοῦσθαι, as 2:8.
- **"I am instructed" → "I have been initiated"** (v12) — Greek μεμύημαι, the word for initiation into the mysteries; only here in the New Testament.
- **"to be full … to suffer need" → "to be filled … to lack"** (v12) — Greek χορτάζεσθαι, ὑστερεῖσθαι.
- **"I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me" → "I have strength for all things in him who empowers me"** (v13) — Greek πάντα ἰσχύω ἐν τῷ ἐνδυναμοῦντί με; no verb "do", and ἐν is "in". "Christ" is not in the critical text.
- **"ye did communicate with my affliction" → "having become fellow sharers in my affliction"** (v14) — Greek συγκοινωνήσαντες, as 1:7.
- **"no church communicated with me as concerning giving and receiving" → "no church shared with me unto an account of giving and receiving"** (v15) — Greek ἐκοινώνησεν εἰς λόγον δόσεως καὶ λήμψεως: κοινωνέω is the verb of "fellowship" (1:5); λόγος here is a business account, as in v17.
- **"once and again" → "both once and twice"** (v16) — Greek καὶ ἅπαξ καὶ δίς.
- **"I desire" → "I seek"** (v17) — Greek ἐπιζητῶ, twice.
- **"I have all" → "I have received all things in full"** (v18) — Greek ἀπέχω, the word written on receipts; "having accepted" is δεξάμενος, a different verb from "received" (παρελάβετε, v9).
- **"an odour of a sweet smell" → "a fragrance of sweet smell"** (v18) — Greek ὀσμὴν εὐωδίας, the Septuagint's phrase for the smell of an offering (Gen 8:21).
- **"shall supply all your need" → "will fill every need of yours"** (v19) — Greek πληρώσει, the verb of "I have been filled" in v18.
- **"for ever and ever" → "unto the ages of the ages"** (v20) — Greek εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας τῶν αἰώνων.
- **"chiefly" → "but especially"** (v22) — Greek μάλιστα δέ.
- **"with you all. Amen." → "with your spirit"** (v23) — NA28 μετὰ τοῦ πνεύματος ὑμῶν; "your" is plural, "spirit" singular. The closing "Amen" is not in the critical text.
