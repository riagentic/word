# Philippians 1

[1] ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ and ⟦timothy|Timotheos|Timothy⟧, slaves of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧,

to all the saints in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ who are in Philippi, with overseers and servants:

[2] Grace to you and peace from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ our Father and the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[3] I give thanks to my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ upon all the remembrance of you,

[4] always in every supplication of mine for you all making the supplication with joy,

[5] upon your fellowship unto the gospel from the first day until now,

[6] being confident of this very thing, that **he who began a good work in you will complete it until the day of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧**;

[7] even as it is right for me to think this on behalf of you all, because I have you in the heart, you all being, both in my chains and in the defense and confirmation of the gospel, fellow sharers with me of the grace.

[8] For ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is my witness, how I long for you all in the inward parts of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[9] And this I pray, that your love may abound yet more and more in full knowledge and all perception,

[10] so that you may approve the things that excel, that you may be sincere and without offense unto the day of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[11] having been filled with the fruit of righteousness which is through ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, unto the glory and praise of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[12] Now I want you to know, brothers, that the things concerning me have come rather unto the progress of the gospel,

[13] so that my chains have become manifest in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ in the whole praetorium and to all the rest,

[14] and most of the brothers in the Lord, being confident by my chains, dare more abundantly to speak the word without fear.

[15] Some indeed even because of envy and strife, but some also because of good will, preach ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧:

[16] the ones out of love, knowing that I am set for the defense of the gospel,

[17] but the others proclaim ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ out of selfish ambition, not purely, supposing to raise up affliction for my chains.

[18] What then? Only that in every way, whether in pretext or in truth, ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ is proclaimed, and in this I rejoice.

But I will also rejoice,

[19] for I know that this will turn out for me unto salvation through your supplication and the supply of the Spirit of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[20] according to my earnest expectation and hope that in nothing I will be put to shame, but in all boldness, as always, now also ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ will be magnified in my body, whether through life or through death.

[21] **For to me to live is ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, and to die is gain.**

[22] But if it is to live in the flesh, this is fruit of work for me — and what I will choose I do not know.

[23] But I am pressed from the two, having the desire to depart and be with ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, for that is very far better,

[24] but to remain on in the flesh is more necessary because of you.

[25] And being confident of this, I know that I will abide, and abide beside you all, unto your progress and joy of the faith,

[26] so that your boast may abound in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ in me through my coming to you again.

[27] Only live as citizens worthily of the gospel of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, so that, whether coming and seeing you or being absent, I may hear the things concerning you: that you stand in one spirit, with one soul striving together for the faith of the gospel,

[28] and not being frightened in anything by the opposers — which is to them a demonstration of destruction, but of your salvation, and this from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[29] because to you it was granted on behalf of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, not only to believe in him but also to suffer on behalf of him —

[30] having the same struggle such as you saw in me and now hear of in me.

## Changes

- **"Christ Jesus" / "Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*" / "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (throughout) — systematic; *Yeshua* = Hebrew/Aramaic name, *Mashiach* = "Anointed One."
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv3, 8, 11, 28) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"servants" → "slaves"** (v1) — Greek δοῦλοι.
- **"bishops and deacons" → "overseers and servants"** (v1) — Greek ἐπισκόποις καὶ διακόνοις, without articles: ἐπίσκοπος is "one who watches over", διάκονος "one who serves". "Deacon" is the Greek word taken over, not translated.
- **"upon every remembrance of you" → "upon all the remembrance of you"** (v3) — Greek ἐπὶ πάσῃ τῇ μνείᾳ ὑμῶν, with the article. "Of you" can mean Paul remembering them or their remembering Paul. The same ἐπί ("upon") opens v5.
- **"prayer" / "request" → "supplication"** (v4, 19) — Greek δέησις both times in v4, a different word from "pray" (προσεύχομαι, v9); also 4:6.
- **"fellowship in the gospel" → "fellowship unto the gospel"** (v5) — Greek εἰς τὸ εὐαγγέλιον.
- **"confident" kept** (v6, 14, 25) — Greek πεποιθώς; the same verb in 2:24; 3:3–4.
- **"will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ" → "will complete it until the day of the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v6) — Greek ἐπιτελέσει ἄχρι ἡμέρας Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ (NA28 order); ἄχρι is "until", not "at".
- **"to think this of you all" → "to think this on behalf of you all"** (v7) — Greek φρονεῖν ὑπέρ. φρονέω is "think" throughout the letter (2:2, 5; 3:15, 19; 4:2, 10).
- **"because I have you in my heart" → "because I have you in the heart"** (v7) — Greek διὰ τὸ ἔχειν με ἐν τῇ καρδίᾳ ὑμᾶς; the grammar also allows "because you have me in the heart".
- **"bonds" → "chains"** (v7, 13, 14, 17) — Greek δεσμοί, as Phlm 10, 13.
- **"partakers of my grace" → "fellow sharers with me of the grace"** (v7) — Greek συγκοινωνούς μου τῆς χάριτος; the root of "fellowship" (κοινωνία, v5).
- **"in the bowels" → "in the inward parts"** (v8) — Greek ἐν σπλάγχνοις, as Phlm 7, 12, 20; also 2:1.
- **"knowledge" → "full knowledge"** (v9) — Greek ἐπιγνώσει; the simple γνῶσις is "knowledge" (3:8).
- **"judgment" → "perception"** (v9) — Greek αἰσθήσει.
- **"approve things that are excellent" → "approve the things that excel"** (v10) — Greek δοκιμάζειν τὰ διαφέροντα; it can also mean "test the things that differ".
- **"without offence" kept** (v10) — Greek ἀπρόσκοποι, "not stumbling" or "not causing to stumble".
- **"the things which happened unto me" → "the things concerning me"** (v12) — Greek τὰ κατ' ἐμέ; no verb of happening.
- **"my bonds in Christ are manifest in all the palace" → "my chains have become manifest in the *Mashiach* in the whole praetorium"** (v13) — Greek φανεροὺς ἐν Χριστῷ γενέσθαι ἐν ὅλῳ τῷ πραιτωρίῳ, in this order. πραιτώριον (Latin *praetorium*) is the governor's residence or the praetorian guard.
- **"many of the brethren in the Lord, waxing confident" → "most of the brothers in the Lord, being confident"** (v14) — Greek τοὺς πλείονας, "the greater number". "In the Lord" can go with "brothers" or with "being confident"; the verse keeps the Greek order.
- **verse order** (v16–17) — NA28 has "out of love" first, then "out of selfish ambition"; the KJV follows the later text with the two verses in the reverse order.
- **"of contention" → "out of selfish ambition"** (v17) — Greek ἐξ ἐριθείας, self-seeking that works for its own party; a different word from "strife" (ἔρις, v15). Also 2:3.
- **"to add affliction to my bonds" → "to raise up affliction for my chains"** (v17) — NA28 θλῖψιν ἐγείρειν; the KJV follows the later reading ἐπιφέρειν, "to add".
- **"yea, and will rejoice" → "But I will also rejoice"** (v18) — Greek ἀλλὰ καὶ χαρήσομαι.
- **"salvation" kept** (v19, 28) — Greek σωτηρία, as in 2:12. v19 repeats Job 13:16 in the Septuagint word for word.
- **"I wot not" → "I do not know"** (v22) — Greek οὐ γνωρίζω. Elsewhere in the New Testament γνωρίζω is "make known" (4:6); here it can also be "I do not make known". The sentence is broken in Greek: "But if to live in the flesh — this for me fruit of work — and what I will choose …".
- **"in a strait betwixt two" → "pressed from the two"** (v23) — Greek συνέχομαι ἐκ τῶν δύο.
- **"abide and continue with you all" → "abide, and abide beside you all"** (v25) — Greek μενῶ καὶ παραμενῶ, one verb and its compound; v24 has a third form, ἐπιμένειν, "remain on" (ἐπιμένω is "remain" in every place).
- **"your rejoicing" → "your boast"** (v26) — Greek καύχημα; also 2:16; 3:3.
- **"by my coming to you again" → "through my coming to you again"** (v26) — Greek διὰ τῆς ἐμῆς παρουσίας πάλιν πρὸς ὑμᾶς; παρουσία is "coming" or "presence" (2:12).
- **"let your conversation be as it becometh" → "live as citizens worthily of"** (v27) — Greek πολιτεύεσθε, "live as a citizen (πολίτης)"; the same root as "citizenship" in 3:20.
- **"with one mind" → "with one soul"** (v27) — Greek μιᾷ ψυχῇ.
- **"striving together for the faith" kept** (v27) — Greek συναθλοῦντες τῇ πίστει; the dative can also be "with the faith".
- **"an evident token of perdition, but to you of salvation" → "a demonstration of destruction, but of your salvation"** (v28) — Greek ἔνδειξις ἀπωλείας, ὑμῶν δὲ σωτηρίας (NA28 ὑμῶν, "of you"). The Greek does not say whose destruction.
- **"it is given" → "it was granted"** (v29) — Greek ἐχαρίσθη, "given as a favor (χάρις)"; the same verb in 2:9.
- **"conflict" → "struggle"** (v30) — Greek ἀγῶνα.
