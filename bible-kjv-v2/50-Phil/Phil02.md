# Philippians 2

[1] If then there is any encouragement in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, if any comfort of love, if any fellowship of the Spirit, if any inward parts and compassions,

[2] fill up my joy, that you think the same thing, having the same love, joined in soul, thinking the one thing,

[3] nothing according to selfish ambition nor according to empty glory, but in humility counting one another as surpassing yourselves,

[4] not looking each at the things of yourselves, but each also at the things of others.

[5] Think this in you, which is also in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧,

[6] who, existing in the form of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
did not count being equal with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ a thing to be seized,
[7] but emptied himself,
taking the form of a slave,
coming to be in the likeness of men;
[8] and being found in fashion as a man,
he humbled himself, becoming obedient unto death,
even death of a cross.
[9] Therefore also ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ highly exalted him
and granted him the name that is above every name,
[10] so that **in the name of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ every knee should bow,
of those in heaven and on earth and under the earth**,
[11] and every tongue should confess that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ is Lord,
unto the glory of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ the Father.

[12] So then, my beloved, even as you always obeyed, not as in my presence only but now much more in my absence, with fear and trembling work out your own salvation,

[13] for it is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ who works in you both to will and to work, on behalf of the good will.

[14] Do all things without murmurings and disputings,

[15] that you may become blameless and innocent, children of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ without blemish in the midst of a crooked and perverted generation, among whom you shine as luminaries in the world,

[16] holding fast the word of life, for a boast to me unto the day of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, that I did not run in vain nor labor in vain.

[17] But even if I am being poured out as a drink offering upon the sacrifice and ministry of your faith, I rejoice and rejoice with you all;

[18] and in the same way you also rejoice and rejoice with me.

[19] But I hope in the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ to send ⟦timothy|Timotheos|Timothy⟧ to you soon, that I also may be of good cheer, knowing the things concerning you.

[20] For I have no one of equal soul, who will genuinely be anxious about the things concerning you;

[21] for they all seek the things of themselves, not the things of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[22] But you know the proof of him, that as a child to a father he was a slave with me unto the gospel.

[23] This one, then, I hope to send at once, as soon as I see the things concerning me;

[24] but I am confident in the Lord that I myself also will come soon.

[25] But I counted it necessary to send to you ⟦epaphroditus|Epaphroditos|Epaphroditus⟧, my brother and fellow worker and fellow soldier, but your apostle and minister of my need,

[26] since he was longing for you all and was distressed because you heard that he was sick.

[27] For indeed he was sick, near to death; but ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had mercy on him, and not on him only but also on me, that I might not have sorrow upon sorrow.

[28] The more eagerly, therefore, I have sent him, that seeing him again you may rejoice and I may be less sorrowful.

[29] Receive him therefore in the Lord with all joy, and hold such ones in honor,

[30] because on account of the work of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ he came near unto death, hazarding his soul, that he might fill up your lack of ministry toward me.

## Changes

- **"Christ Jesus" / "Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*" / "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv6, 9, 11, 13, 15, 27) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (vv10, 11, 19, 21) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
- **"consolation in Christ" → "encouragement in the *Mashiach*"; "comfort of love" kept** (v1) — Greek παράκλησις (as Phlm 7) and παραμύθιον ἀγάπης, a plain genitive.
- **"fellowship of the Spirit" kept** (v1) — Greek κοινωνία πνεύματος, without the article; a plain genitive, not "participation in".
- **"bowels and mercies" → "inward parts and compassions"** (v1) — Greek σπλάγχνα καὶ οἰκτιρμοί, as 1:8.
- **"Fulfil ye my joy, that ye be likeminded" → "fill up my joy, that you think the same thing"** (v2) — Greek ἵνα τὸ αὐτὸ φρονῆτε; φρονέω "think" twice in the verse and again in v5.
- **"being of one accord" → "joined in soul"** (v2) — Greek σύμψυχοι, from ψυχή "soul" (1:27); the same root in v19 (εὐψυχῶ, "be of good cheer") and v20 (ἰσόψυχον, "of equal soul").
- **"Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory" → "nothing according to selfish ambition nor according to empty glory"** (v3) — the Greek has no verb; v3–4 continue the sentence of v2. ἐριθεία as 1:17; κενοδοξία is κενός "empty" + δόξα "glory".
- **"let each esteem other better than themselves" → "counting one another as surpassing yourselves"** (v3) — Greek ἡγούμενοι ὑπερέχοντας; ἡγέομαι is "count" throughout (v6, 25; 3:7–8), ὑπερέχω "surpass" also in 3:8; 4:7.
- **"Look not every man on his own things" → "not looking each at the things of yourselves"** (v4) — Greek σκοποῦντες, a participle; no "interests" in the Greek.
- **"Let this mind be in you, which was also in Christ Jesus" → "Think this in you, which is also in the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v5) — Greek τοῦτο φρονεῖτε ἐν ὑμῖν ὃ καὶ ἐν Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ. The second half has no verb ("is" is supplied); ἐν ὑμῖν is "in you" or "among you".
- **"being in the form of God" → "existing in the form of *Elohim*"** (v6) — Greek ὑπάρχων, a participle; the Greek has no "though".
- **"thought it not robbery to be equal with God" → "did not count being equal with *Elohim* a thing to be seized"** (v6) — Greek οὐχ ἁρπαγμὸν ἡγήσατο. ἁρπαγμός (from ἁρπάζω, "seize") occurs only here in the New Testament: the act of seizing, or a thing seized or to be seized or held on to.
- **"made himself of no reputation" → "emptied himself"** (v7) — Greek ἑαυτὸν ἐκένωσεν.
- **"servant" → "slave"** (v7) — Greek δούλου.
- **"was made in the likeness of men" → "coming to be in the likeness of men"** (v7) — Greek γενόμενος, "having become"; not specifically "born".
- **"in fashion as a man" kept** (v8) — Greek σχήματι … ὡς ἄνθρωπος; σχῆμα is the outward appearance, a different word from "form" (μορφή, v6–7).
- **"in lowliness of mind" → "in humility"; "humbled himself" kept** (v3, 8) — Greek ταπεινοφροσύνῃ, ἐταπείνωσεν: one root (ταπεινός, "low"); also 3:21; 4:12.
- **"even the death of the cross" → "even death of a cross"** (v8) — Greek θανάτου δὲ σταυροῦ, without articles.
- **"given him a name" → "granted him the name"** (v9) — NA28 τὸ ὄνομα, with the article; ἐχαρίσατο as 1:29.
- **"at the name of Jesus" → "in the name of *Yeshua*"** (v10) — Greek ἐν τῷ ὀνόματι.
- **"of things in heaven, and things in earth, and things under the earth" → "of those in heaven and on earth and under the earth"** (v10) — Greek ἐπουρανίων καὶ ἐπιγείων καὶ καταχθονίων, three genitive adjectives depending on "knee"; they can be persons or things.
- **"in my presence" kept** (v12) — Greek παρουσίᾳ, elsewhere "coming" (1:26); here it stands against ἀπουσίᾳ, "absence", so "presence".
- **"of his good pleasure" → "on behalf of the good will"** (v13) — Greek ὑπὲρ τῆς εὐδοκίας; the Greek has no "his". εὐδοκία as 1:15.
- **"harmless" → "innocent"** (v15) — Greek ἀκέραιοι, "unmixed".
- **"be" → "become"** (v15) — Greek γένησθε.
- **"sons of God, without rebuke" → "children of *Elohim* without blemish"** (v15) — Greek τέκνα θεοῦ ἄμωμα.
- **"nation" → "generation"** (v15) — Greek γενεᾶς; the wording is that of Deut 32:5 in the Septuagint.
- **"ye shine as lights" → "you shine as luminaries"** (v15) — Greek φωστῆρες, light-giving bodies (Gen 1:14, 16 in the Septuagint). φαίνεσθε can be a statement or a command.
- **"holding forth" → "holding fast"** (v16) — Greek ἐπέχοντες; both meanings are possible.
- **"that I may rejoice in the day of Christ" → "for a boast to me unto the day of the *Mashiach*"** (v16) — Greek εἰς καύχημα ἐμοὶ εἰς ἡμέραν Χριστοῦ; καύχημα as 1:26.
- **"if I be offered upon the sacrifice and service" → "if I am being poured out as a drink offering upon the sacrifice and ministry"** (v17) — Greek σπένδομαι, present tense, "be poured out as a libation". λειτουργία is public or priestly service; the same root in v25 ("minister") and v30.
- **"be of good comfort" → "be of good cheer"** (v19) — Greek εὐψυχῶ.
- **"no man likeminded, who will naturally care for your state" → "no one of equal soul, who will genuinely be anxious about the things concerning you"** (v20) — Greek ἰσόψυχον; γνησίως as 4:3; μεριμνήσει is the verb of 4:6.
- **"the proof of him" kept; "as a son with the father, he hath served with me in the gospel" → "as a child to a father he was a slave with me unto the gospel"** (v22) — Greek ὡς πατρὶ τέκνον σὺν ἐμοὶ ἐδούλευσεν εἰς τὸ εὐαγγέλιον. The Greek has "him" (αὐτοῦ), not the name.
- **"messenger" → "apostle"** (v25) — Greek ἀπόστολον, "one sent": here sent by the church in Philippi.
- **"I supposed it necessary" → "I counted it necessary"; "I sent" → "I have sent"** (v25, 28) — Greek ἡγησάμην, ἔπεμψα: past tenses written from the readers' point of view; *Epaphroditos* carries the letter.
- **"full of heaviness" → "distressed"** (v26) — Greek ἀδημονῶν.
- **"when ye see him again, ye may rejoice" → "seeing him again you may rejoice"** (v28) — Greek ἰδόντες αὐτὸν πάλιν χαρῆτε; "again" can go with "seeing" or with "rejoice".
- **"not regarding his life" → "hazarding his soul"** (v30) — Greek παραβολευσάμενος τῇ ψυχῇ, "having gambled with his soul": ψυχή is "soul" throughout (1:27) and here means his life, as "near unto death" shows. The KJV follows the later reading παραβουλευσάμενος.
- **"to supply your lack of service toward me" → "that he might fill up your lack of ministry toward me"** (v30) — Greek ἀναπληρώσῃ τὸ ὑμῶν ὑστέρημα τῆς πρός με λειτουργίας.

### Notes
- **v6-11** — the *carmen Christi*, widely considered a pre-Pauline hymn.
- **v10-11** echoes Isa 45:23.
- **v7–8** — the critical editions count "and being found in fashion as a man" as the end of v7; the verse numbers here are those of the KJV.
