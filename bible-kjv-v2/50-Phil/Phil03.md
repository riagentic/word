# Philippians 3

[1] Finally, my brothers, rejoice in the Lord. To write the same things to you is no trouble to me and is safe for you.

[2] Look out for the dogs, look out for the evildoers, look out for those who mutilate the flesh.

[3] For we are the circumcision, who worship by the Spirit of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and glory in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ and put no confidence in the flesh —

[4] though I myself have reason for confidence in the flesh also. If anyone else thinks he has reason for confidence in the flesh, I have more:

[5] circumcised on the eighth day, of the people of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, of the tribe of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, ⟦hebrew|an Ivri|a Hebrew⟧ of ⟦hebrews|Ivrim|Hebrews⟧; as to the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, a ⟦pharisee|Parush|Pharisee⟧;

[6] as to zeal, a persecutor of the church; as to righteousness under the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, blameless.

[7] But whatever gain I had, I counted as loss for the sake of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[8] Indeed, I count everything as loss because of the surpassing worth of knowing ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ my Lord. For his sake I have suffered the loss of all things and count them as rubbish, in order that I may gain ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧

[9] and be found in him, not having a righteousness of my own that comes from the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, but that which comes through faith in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, the righteousness from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that depends on faith —

[10] that I may know him and the power of his resurrection, and may share his sufferings, becoming like him in his death,

[11] that by any means possible I may attain the resurrection from the dead.

[12] Not that I have already obtained this or am already perfect, but I press on to make it my own, because ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ has made me his own.

[13] Brothers, I do not consider that I have made it my own. But one thing I do: **forgetting what lies behind and straining forward to what lies ahead,**

**[14] I press on toward the goal for the prize of the upward call of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧**.

[15] Let those of us who are mature think this way, and if in anything you think otherwise, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will reveal that also to you.

[16] Only let us hold true to what we have attained.

[17] Brothers, join in imitating me, and keep your eyes on those who walk according to the example you have in us.

[18] For many, of whom I have often told you and now tell you even with tears, walk as enemies of the cross of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[19] Their end is destruction, their god is their belly, and they glory in their shame, with minds set on earthly things.

[20] But our citizenship is in heaven, and from it we await a Savior, the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[21] who will transform our lowly body to be like his glorious body, by the power that enables him even to subject all things to himself.

## Changes

- **"Christ Jesus" / "Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*" / "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv3, 9, 14, 15) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v20) — in "Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*."
