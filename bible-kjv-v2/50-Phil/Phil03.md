# Philippians 3

[1] For the rest, my brothers, rejoice in the Lord. To write the same things to you is not irksome to me, but for you it is safe.

[2] Watch the dogs, watch the evil workers, watch the mutilation.

[3] For we are the circumcision, who worship by the Spirit of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and boast in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ and have no confidence in the flesh —

[4] though I myself have confidence also in the flesh. If any other thinks to have confidence in the flesh, I more:

[5] in circumcision an eighth-day one, of the race of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, of the tribe of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, ⟦hebrew|an Ivri|a Hebrew⟧ of ⟦hebrews|Ivrim|Hebrews⟧; according to ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, a ⟦pharisee|Parush|Pharisee⟧;

[6] according to zeal, persecuting the church; according to the righteousness that is in ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, having become blameless.

[7] But whatever things were gains to me, these I have counted loss because of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[8] But indeed, I also count all things to be loss because of the surpassingness of the knowledge of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ my Lord, because of whom I suffered the loss of all things, and I count them refuse, that I may gain ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧

[9] and be found in him, not having a righteousness of my own, the one from ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, but the one through faith of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, the righteousness from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ upon the faith —

[10] to know him and the power of his resurrection and the fellowship of his sufferings, being conformed to his death,

[11] if somehow I may arrive at the resurrection from out of the dead.

[12] Not that I have already received, or have already been perfected, but I pursue, if I may also lay hold of that for which I also was laid hold of by ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[13] Brothers, I do not consider myself to have laid hold. But one thing: **forgetting the things behind and stretching out to the things ahead,**

**[14] toward the mark I pursue, unto the prize of the upward calling of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧**.

[15] As many, then, as are perfect, let us think this; and if you think anything otherwise, this also ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will reveal to you.

[16] Only, to what we have attained, by the same to walk in line.

[17] Become fellow imitators of me, brothers, and look at those who walk thus, even as you have us as a pattern.

[18] For many walk — of whom I often told you, and now tell you even weeping — the enemies of the cross of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[19] whose end is destruction, whose god is the belly and whose glory is in their shame, who think the earthly things.

[20] For our citizenship is in the heavens, from where also we await a Savior, the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[21] who will refashion the body of our humiliation, conformed to the body of his glory, according to the working of his being able even to subject all things to himself.

## Changes

- **"Christ Jesus" / "Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*" / "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv3, 9, 14, 15) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v20) — in "Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*."
- **"Finally" → "For the rest"** (v1) — Greek τὸ λοιπόν, "as to what remains"; also 4:8.
- **"not grievous" → "not irksome"** (v1) — Greek οὐκ ὀκνηρόν.
- **"Beware of dogs" → "Watch the dogs"** (v2) — Greek βλέπετε, "look at", three times, each time with the article.
- **"the concision" → "the mutilation"** (v2) — Greek κατατομήν, "a cutting to pieces"; a wordplay with "circumcision" (περιτομή, "a cutting around") in v3. The Greek names the thing, not the people.
- **"which worship God in the spirit" → "who worship by the Spirit of *Elohim*"** (v3) — NA28 πνεύματι θεοῦ; the KJV follows the later reading θεῷ. λατρεύω is to render sacred service.
- **"rejoice in Christ Jesus" → "boast in the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v3) — Greek καυχώμενοι, as 1:26; 2:16.
- **"Though I might also have confidence" → "though I myself have confidence"** (v4) — Greek ἔχων πεποίθησιν, a plain participle: "having confidence", not "reason for confidence". πεποιθ- as 1:6.
- **"Circumcised the eighth day, of the stock of Israel" → "in circumcision an eighth-day one, of the race of *Yisrael*"** (v5) — Greek περιτομῇ ὀκταήμερος, ἐκ γένους Ἰσραήλ.
- **"as touching the law" → "according to *Torah*"** (v5, 6, 9) — Greek νόμος without the article all three times; κατά is "according to" four times in v5–6.
- **"persecuting" kept** (v6) — Greek διώκων; the same verb is "pursue" in v12, 14.
- **"what things were gain to me, those I counted loss for Christ" → "whatever things were gains to me, these I have counted loss because of the *Mashiach*"** (v7) — Greek κέρδη, plural; ἥγημαι, perfect; διά with the accusative is "because of", three times in v7–8.
- **"for the excellency of the knowledge" → "because of the surpassingness of the knowledge"** (v8) — Greek τὸ ὑπερέχον, "that which surpasses", the verb of 2:3; 4:7. No word for "worth" in the Greek.
- **"dung" → "refuse"** (v8) — Greek σκύβαλα: what is thrown out — refuse, scraps, dung.
- **"the faith of Christ" kept** (v9) — Greek διὰ πίστεως Χριστοῦ, a genitive: "faith in the *Mashiach*" or "the faith(fulness) of the *Mashiach*".
- **"the righteousness which is of God by faith" → "the righteousness from *Elohim* upon the faith"** (v9) — Greek τὴν ἐκ θεοῦ δικαιοσύνην ἐπὶ τῇ πίστει; ἐκ "from" as in "from *Torah*".
- **"That I may know him" → "to know him"** (v10) — Greek τοῦ γνῶναι, an infinitive of purpose; no "may share" in the Greek: "fellowship of his sufferings" is a second object of "know".
- **"being made conformable unto" → "being conformed to"** (v10) — Greek συμμορφιζόμενος, from μορφή "form" (2:6–7); also v21.
- **"attain unto the resurrection of the dead" → "arrive at the resurrection from out of the dead"** (v11) — Greek τὴν ἐξανάστασιν τὴν ἐκ νεκρῶν (NA28): ἐξανάστασις, "a rising out", only here in the New Testament. εἴ πως is "if somehow".
- **"Not as though I had already attained, either were already perfect" → "Not that I have already received, or have already been perfected"** (v12) — Greek ἔλαβον, without an object; τετελείωμαι, passive.
- **"I follow after, if that I may apprehend that for which also I am apprehended" → "I pursue, if I may also lay hold of that for which I also was laid hold of"** (v12) — Greek διώκω … καταλάβω, ἐφ' ᾧ καὶ κατελήμφθην. ἐφ' ᾧ can also mean "because" ("because I also was laid hold of").
- **"of Christ Jesus" → "by the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v12) — the editions differ: Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ WH NA28 (τοῦ Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ RP); Χριστοῦ only Treg, SBLGNT. The verse follows the majority, Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ.
- **"I count not myself to have apprehended" → "I do not consider myself to have laid hold"** (v13) — Greek λογίζομαι, as 4:8; a different verb from "count" (ἡγέομαι, v7–8).
- **"but this one thing I do" → "But one thing"** (v13) — Greek ἓν δέ; no verb.
- **"I press toward the mark for the prize of the high calling" → "toward the mark I pursue, unto the prize of the upward calling"** (v14) — Greek κατὰ σκοπὸν διώκω εἰς τὸ βραβεῖον τῆς ἄνω κλήσεως; ἄνω is "above, upward".
- **"perfect" kept; "be thus minded" → "think this"** (v15) — Greek τέλειοι, the root of "perfected" in v12; φρονέω as 2:2, 5.
- **"let us walk by the same rule, let us mind the same thing" → "by the same to walk in line"** (v16) — NA28 τῷ αὐτῷ στοιχεῖν, an infinitive used as a command; "rule" and "let us mind the same thing" are not in the critical text.
- **"Brethren, be followers together of me, and mark them" → "Become fellow imitators of me, brothers, and look at those"** (v17) — Greek συμμιμηταί; σκοπεῖτε as 2:4.
- **"ensample" → "pattern"** (v17) — Greek τύπον.
- **"that they are the enemies" → "the enemies"** (v18) — Greek τοὺς ἐχθρούς, in apposition; the Greek has no "as" and no "that they are".
- **"who mind earthly things" → "who think the earthly things"** (v19) — Greek οἱ τὰ ἐπίγεια φρονοῦντες.
- **"conversation" → "citizenship"** (v20) — Greek πολίτευμα, the commonwealth one belongs to as a citizen, or that citizenship; the root of 1:27. γάρ is "For".
- **"heaven" → "the heavens"** (v20) — Greek ἐν οὐρανοῖς, plural.
- **"shall change our vile body, that it may be fashioned like unto his glorious body" → "will refashion the body of our humiliation, conformed to the body of his glory"** (v21) — Greek μετασχηματίσει (from σχῆμα, 2:8) τὸ σῶμα τῆς ταπεινώσεως ἡμῶν σύμμορφον τῷ σώματι τῆς δόξης αὐτοῦ; "that it may be fashioned" is not in the critical text. ταπείνωσις, "a being made low", is the noun of "humble" (2:3, 8).
- **"the working whereby he is able" → "the working of his being able"** (v21) — Greek κατὰ τὴν ἐνέργειαν τοῦ δύνασθαι αὐτόν; ἐνέργεια as "work" in 2:13.
- **"unto himself" kept** (v21) — the editions differ only in the breathing mark: αὐτῷ Treg NA28, αὑτῷ WH SBLGNT (ἑαυτῷ RP). The old manuscripts have no breathing marks, so the letters are the same; the sense is reflexive, "to himself".
