## Changes

- **"bands of soldiers" → "units of an army"** (v4) — *gedudei tzava*.
- **"went in to his wife" — retained** (v23) — Hebrew idiom for conjugal relations.
- **"Jehoshua" → "Joshua"** (v27).
- **"Gaza" → "Ayyah"** (v28) — Hebrew *Ayyah*; Ayyah/Aja, not Philistine Gaza (which Ephraim did not hold). KJV misidentified.
- **"Aramitess" → "Aramean concubine"** (v14).
- **"nether" → "Lower"** (v24).
- **"chief of the princes" → "chiefs of the leaders"** (v40) — *rashei ha-nesi'im*.

### Notes
- **Missing tribes** — Dan and Zebulun are absent from Chronicler's tribal catalog. Dan's apostasy (Judg 18, Jeroboam's cult) may explain exclusion; Zebulun's omission unclear.
- **Issachar's 87,000 warriors** — largest tribal count in this chapter.
- **Benjamin partial** — fuller genealogy in ch 8. Chronicler gives preview here.
- **Ephraim's tragic incident** (v21-24) — Ephraimite raid on Philistine Gath, killed. Sheerah rebuilds Beth-horon towns — rare mention of a woman founding cities.
- **Joshua's lineage** — through Ephraim. Chronicler traces from Ephraim to Joshua son of Nun (v20-27), ten generations. Note the two Tahaths and two Shuthelahs — possibly duplication from a corrupt list.
- **Manasseh with Huppim/Shuppim** (v15) — textually complex; some emend to "Machir took a wife from Huppim and Shuppim's house."
- **Zelophehad's daughters** — well-known inheritance case (Num 27, 36).
