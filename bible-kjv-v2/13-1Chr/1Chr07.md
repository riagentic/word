# 1 Chronicles 7

[1] The sons of Issachar: Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimron — four.

[2] The sons of Tola: Uzzi, Rephaiah, Jeriel, Jahmai, Ibsam, and Shemuel — heads of their fathers' houses, of Tola, valiant warriors in their generations. Their number in the days of David was twenty-two thousand six hundred.

[3] The sons of Uzzi: Izrahiah. The sons of Izrahiah: Michael, Obadiah, Joel, and Isshiah — five, all of them chief men.

[4] With them, by their generations, according to their fathers' houses, were units of an army for war — thirty-six thousand men; for they had many wives and sons.

[5] Their brothers, through all the families of Issachar, were valiant warriors, enrolled in all by their genealogies — eighty-seven thousand.

[6] The sons of Benjamin: Bela, Becher, and Jediael — three.

[7] The sons of Bela: Ezbon, Uzzi, Uzziel, Jerimoth, and Iri — five; heads of their fathers' houses, valiant warriors; enrolled by their genealogies, twenty-two thousand thirty-four.

[8] The sons of Becher: Zemirah, Joash, Eliezer, Elioenai, Omri, Jeremoth, Abijah, Anathoth, and Alemeth. All these were the sons of Becher.

[9] Their number, enrolled by genealogy, by their generations, heads of their fathers' houses, valiant warriors, was twenty thousand two hundred.

[10] The sons of Jediael: Bilhan. The sons of Bilhan: Jeush, Benjamin, Ehud, Chenaanah, Zethan, Tarshish, and Ahishahar.

[11] All these were the sons of Jediael by the heads of their fathers' houses, valiant warriors — seventeen thousand two hundred, fit to go out for war and battle.

[12] Shuppim and Huppim were the sons of Ir; Hushim, the sons of Aher.

[13] The sons of Naphtali: Jahziel, Guni, Jezer, and Shallum — the descendants of Bilhah.

[14] The sons of Manasseh: Asriel, whom his Aramean concubine bore; she bore Machir the father of Gilead.

[15] Machir took a wife for Huppim and for Shuppim; the name of their sister was Maacah. The name of the second was Zelophehad; Zelophehad had only daughters.

[16] Maacah the wife of Machir bore a son, and she named him Peresh; the name of his brother was Sheresh, and his sons were Ulam and Rakem.

[17] The sons of Ulam: Bedan. These were the sons of Gilead son of Machir son of Manasseh.

[18] His sister Hammolecheth bore Ishhod, Abiezer, and Mahlah.

[19] The sons of Shemida were: Ahian, Shechem, Likhi, and Aniam.

[20] The sons of Ephraim: Shuthelah, Bered his son, Tahath his son, Eleadah his son, Tahath his son,

[21] Zabad his son, Shuthelah his son, and Ezer and Elead, whom the men of Gath born in that land killed, because they had come down to take their livestock.

[22] Ephraim their father mourned many days; his brothers came to comfort him.

[23] He went in to his wife; she conceived and bore a son, and he named him Beriah, because it had gone evil — be-ra'ah — with his house.

[24] His daughter was Sheerah, who built Lower Beth-horon and Upper Beth-horon, and Uzzen-sheerah.

[25] Rephah was his son, also Resheph, Telah his son, Tahan his son,

[26] Ladan his son, Ammihud his son, Elishama his son,

[27] Non his son, Joshua his son.

[28] Their possessions and dwellings were Bethel and its towns, and eastward Naaran, westward Gezer with its towns, Shechem also and its towns, as far as Ayyah and its towns;

[29] along the borders of the children of Manasseh: Beth-shean and its towns, Taanach and its towns, Megiddo and its towns, Dor and its towns. In these the children of Joseph son of Israel dwelt.

[30] The sons of Asher: Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, Beriah, and Serah their sister.

[31] The sons of Beriah: Heber and Malchiel who was the father of Birzaith.

[32] Heber fathered Japhlet, Shomer, and Hotham, and Shua their sister.

[33] The sons of Japhlet: Pasach, Bimhal, and Ashvath. These were the children of Japhlet.

[34] The sons of Shemer: Ahi, Rohgah, Jehubbah, and Aram.

[35] The sons of his brother Helem: Zophah, Imna, Shelesh, and Amal.

[36] The sons of Zophah: Suah, Harnepher, Shual, Beri, Imrah,

[37] Bezer, Hod, Shamma, Shilshah, Ithran, and Beera.

[38] The sons of Jether: Jephunneh, Pispah, and Ara.

[39] The sons of Ulla: Arah, Hanniel, and Rizia.

[40] All these were the children of Asher, heads of their fathers' houses, choice and valiant warriors, chiefs of the leaders. Their number enrolled by genealogy, of those fit for war in battle, was twenty-six thousand men.

## Changes

- **"bands of soldiers" → "units of an army"** (v4) — *gedudei tzava*.
- **"went in to his wife" — retained** (v23) — Hebrew idiom for conjugal relations.
- **"Jehoshua" → "Joshua"** (v27).
- **"Gaza" → "Ayyah"** (v28) — Hebrew *Ayyah*; Ayyah/Aja, not Philistine Gaza (which Ephraim did not hold). KJV misidentified.
- **"Aramitess" → "Aramean concubine"** (v14).
- **"nether" → "Lower"** (v24).
- **"chief of the princes" → "chiefs of the leaders"** (v40) — *rashei ha-nesi'im*.

### Notes
- **Missing tribes** — Dan and Zebulun are absent from Chronicler's tribal catalog. Dan's apostasy (Judg 18, Jeroboam's cult) may explain exclusion; Zebulun's omission unclear.
- **Issachar's 87,000 warriors** — largest tribal count in this chapter.
- **Benjamin partial** — fuller genealogy in ch 8. Chronicler gives preview here.
- **Ephraim's tragic incident** (v21-24) — Ephraimite raid on Philistine Gath, killed. Sheerah rebuilds Beth-horon towns — rare mention of a woman founding cities.
- **Joshua's lineage** — through Ephraim. Chronicler traces from Ephraim to Joshua son of Nun (v20-27), ten generations. Note the two Tahaths and two Shuthelahs — possibly duplication from a corrupt list.
- **Manasseh with Huppim/Shuppim** (v15) — textually complex; some emend to "Machir took a wife from Huppim and Shuppim's house."
- **Zelophehad's daughters** — well-known inheritance case (Num 27, 36).
