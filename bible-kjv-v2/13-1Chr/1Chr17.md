# 1 Chronicles 17

[1] When David was settled in his house, David said to Nathan the prophet, "Behold, I dwell in a house of cedars, but the ark of the covenant of Yahweh is under curtains."

[2] Nathan said to David, "Do all that is in your heart, for *Elohim* is with you."

[3] That same night the word of *Elohim* came to Nathan, saying,

[4] "Go and tell David my servant, 'Thus says Yahweh: You shall not build me the house to dwell in.

[5] For I have not dwelt in a house from the day I brought up Israel to this day, but have gone from tent to tent and from one dwelling to another.

[6] Wherever I have walked with all Israel, did I ever speak a word to any of the judges of Israel, whom I commanded to shepherd my people, saying, "Why have you not built me a house of cedars?"'

[7] Now therefore thus shall you say to my servant David: 'Thus says Yahweh of Hosts: I took you from the pasture, from following the sheep, to be ruler over my people Israel.

[8] I have been with you wherever you have gone; I have cut off all your enemies from before you; and I will make you a name like the name of the great ones on the earth.

[9] I will appoint a place for my people Israel, and will plant them, so that they may dwell in their own place and be disturbed no more; the sons of wickedness shall not waste them any more, as at the beginning,

[10] and from the time that I commanded judges to be over my people Israel. I will subdue all your enemies. Moreover I tell you: Yahweh will build you a house.

[11] When your days are fulfilled and you must go to be with your fathers, I will raise up your seed after you, one of your sons, and I will establish his kingdom.

[12] He shall build me a house, and I will establish his throne forever.

[13] I will be his father, and he shall be my son; I will not take my steadfast love — away from him, as I took it from him who was before you.

[14] I will confirm him in my house and in my kingdom forever, and his throne shall be established forevermore.'"

[15] According to all these words, and according to all this vision, Nathan spoke to David.

[16] Then King David went in and sat before Yahweh, and said: "Who am I, O Yahweh *Elohim*, and what is my house, that you have brought me to this point?

[17] This was a small thing in your eyes, O *Elohim* — you have also spoken concerning your servant's house for a great while to come; you have regarded me according to the estate of a man of high station, O Yahweh *Elohim*.

[18] What more can David say to you for the honor of your servant? For you know your servant.

[19] O Yahweh, for your servant's sake and according to your own heart, you have done all this greatness, making known all these great things.

[20] O Yahweh, there is none like you, and there is no *Elohim* besides you, according to all that we have heard with our ears.

[21] What one nation on the earth is like your people Israel, whom *Elohim* went out to redeem to be his own people — to make for yourself a name of greatness and awe, by driving out nations from before your people whom you redeemed out of Egypt?

[22] You made your people Israel your own people forever, and you, Yahweh, became their *Elohim*.

[23] Now therefore, Yahweh, let the word that you have spoken concerning your servant and concerning his house stand forever, and do as you have spoken.

[24] Let it stand; let your name be magnified forever, so that men may say, 'Yahweh of Hosts, *Elohim* of Israel, is indeed *Elohim* for Israel'; and let the house of David your servant be established before you.

[25] For you, O my *Elohim*, have revealed to your servant that you will build him a house; therefore your servant has found courage to pray before you.

[26] And now, O Yahweh, you are *Elohim*, and you have promised this good thing to your servant.

[27] Now therefore be pleased to bless the house of your servant, that it may continue forever before you; for you, O Yahweh, have blessed, and it is blessed forever."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Lord of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v7, 24).
- **"sheepcote" → "pasture"** (v7) — *naveh*; pasture-dwelling.
- **"feed" → "shepherd"** (v6) — *ra'ah*.
- **"waste" → "disturbed / waste"** (v9) — *le-valloto*; afflict.
- **"take it from him that was before thee" → "him who was before you"** (v13) — implicit Saul.
- **"set him over my house and my kingdom" → "I will confirm him in my house and in my kingdom"** (v14) — notable Chronistic variant from 2 Sam 7:16 ("your house, your kingdom").

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Sam 7** — Davidic Covenant. Chronicler preserves with minor shifts.
- **"My house, my kingdom" (v14)** — Chronicler emphasizes Yahweh's ownership: the Davidic throne is Yahweh's house and kingdom. Theocentric, not merely dynastic.
- **"Him who was before you"** — Saul; Chronicler retains but more discreetly than 2 Sam ("from before you").
- **"House" — threefold pun**: Yahweh's temple (David wants to build), Yahweh's dynasty (for David), David's palace (cedar).
- **Messianic scope** — "his throne shall be established forever"; refers proximately to Solomon, typologically to Messiah. NT reads (Heb 1:5).
- **David's prayer** — humility, theology of election, covenant plea. Foundational Davidic piety.
- **Chronicler's elaboration** — slightly expanded prayer vs. 2 Sam 7:18-29. Emphasis on election and name.
