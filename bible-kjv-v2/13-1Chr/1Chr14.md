## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v10, 11, 14-16) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*.
- **"Baal-perazim" wordplay** (v11) — Hebrew *paratz* = "burst through"; place-name *Baal-perazim* = "Lord of breakings-through." English gloss in the verse dropped for clarity; wordplay noted here.
- **"confirmed" → "established"** (v2) — *hechin*.
- **"sound of going" → "sound of marching"** (v15) — *qol tze'adah*.
- **"Gazer" → "Gezer"** (v16).

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Sam 5:11-25** — Chronicler reorganizes: places Hiram-alliance + Philistine wars after ark episode. Chronicles order: coronation → Jerusalem → ark attempt → Hiram → Philistine victories → successful ark transfer.
- **Hiram of Tyre** — Phoenician ally, supplies cedar. Same partnership crucial for Solomon's temple.
- **Thirteen sons in Jerusalem** (v4-7) — more than 2 Sam 5:14-16 (11). Chronicler adds Nogah (and second Eliphelet?).
- **Two Philistine wars** — both in Valley of Rephaim (south-southwest of Jerusalem). First = frontal assault; second = flanking maneuver with divine auditory sign.
- **"Balsam trees" march sign** — wind in the treetops interpreted as divine army. Spirit-led tactical adjustment.
- **Gibeon to Gezer** — long pursuit, western descent from hill country.
- **Chronicler's theology** — Yahweh's dread on nations through David parallels the Conquest. Davidic kingdom = recapitulation of Joshua's conquest on national scale.
