# 1 Chronicles 14

[1] Hiram king of Tyre sent messengers to David, with cedar timber, masons, and carpenters, to build him a house.

[2] David perceived that Yahweh had established him as king over Israel, and that his kingdom was exalted for the sake of his people Israel.

[3] David took more wives in Jerusalem; David fathered more sons and daughters.

[4] These are the names of the children he had in Jerusalem: Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, Solomon,

[5] Ibhar, Elishua, Elpelet,

[6] Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia,

[7] Elishama, Beeliada, and Eliphelet.

[8] When the Philistines heard that David had been anointed king over all Israel, all the Philistines went up in search of David. David heard of it and went out against them.

[9] The Philistines came and made a raid in the Valley of Rephaim.

[10] David inquired of *Elohim*, saying, "Shall I go up against the Philistines? Will you deliver them into my hand?" Yahweh said to him, "Go up, for I will deliver them into your hand."

[11] So they came up to Baal-perazim; David struck them down there. Then David said, "*Elohim* has burst through my enemies by my hand, like the bursting of waters." Therefore they called the name of that place Baal-perazim.

[12] They left their gods there; David gave orders, and they were burned with fire.

[13] The Philistines once again made a raid in the valley.

[14] David again inquired of *Elohim*; *Elohim* said to him, "You shall not go up after them; circle around from them, and come upon them opposite the balsam trees.

[15] When you hear the sound of marching in the tops of the balsam trees, then you shall go out to battle; for *Elohim* has gone out before you to strike the army of the Philistines."

[16] David did as *Elohim* commanded him; they struck down the army of the Philistines from Gibeon to Gezer.

[17] The fame of David went out into all lands, and Yahweh brought the dread of him upon all nations.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v10, 11, 14-16) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*.
- **"Baal-perazim" wordplay** (v11) — Hebrew *paratz* = "burst through"; place-name *Baal-perazim* = "Lord of breakings-through." English gloss in the verse dropped for clarity; wordplay noted here.
- **"confirmed" → "established"** (v2) — *hechin*.
- **"sound of going" → "sound of marching"** (v15) — *qol tze'adah*.
- **"Gazer" → "Gezer"** (v16).

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Sam 5:11-25** — Chronicler reorganizes: places Hiram-alliance + Philistine wars after ark episode. Chronicles order: coronation → Jerusalem → ark attempt → Hiram → Philistine victories → successful ark transfer.
- **Hiram of Tyre** — Phoenician ally, supplies cedar. Same partnership crucial for Solomon's temple.
- **Thirteen sons in Jerusalem** (v4-7) — more than 2 Sam 5:14-16 (11). Chronicler adds Nogah (and second Eliphelet?).
- **Two Philistine wars** — both in Valley of Rephaim (south-southwest of Jerusalem). First = frontal assault; second = flanking maneuver with divine auditory sign.
- **"Balsam trees" march sign** — wind in the treetops interpreted as divine army. Spirit-led tactical adjustment.
- **Gibeon to Gezer** — long pursuit, western descent from hill country.
- **Chronicler's theology** — Yahweh's dread on nations through David parallels the Conquest. Davidic kingdom = recapitulation of Joshua's conquest on national scale.
