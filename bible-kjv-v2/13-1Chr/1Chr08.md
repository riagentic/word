## Changes

- **"Esh-baal / Merib-baal" — retained** (v33, 34) — Chronicler preserves original Baalistic theophoric names; Samuel euphemized them (Ish-bosheth, Mephibosheth). *Baal* here = generic "lord/owner" (title of Yahweh?), not pagan deity, in pre-Hoseanic Hebrew.
- **"Ehud's sons... removed them" → "deported them"** (v6-7) — *gallah*; forcible relocation.
- **"drove away" → "put to flight"** (v13) — *hivrichu*; caused to flee.
- **"fathers" in genealogies → "fathers' houses"** throughout.

### Notes
- **Benjamin's fuller genealogy** — this chapter sets up Saul's line. Chronicler positions Saul's genealogy immediately before David's reign.
- **Saulide line (v33-40)** — Ner → Kish → Saul → Jonathan → Merib-baal (=Mephibosheth) → Micah etc. Traced 13 generations post-Saul. Ancestors presumably contemporaries of the Chronicler's own time.
- **Shaharaim in Moab** (v8-11) — Benjaminite settler in Moab; unique detail. Suggests tribal mobility across Jordan.
- **Ono and Lod** (v12) — strategic Benjaminite-Ephraimite frontier towns; post-exilic returners settle here (Ezra 2:33, Neh 11:35).
- **Benjaminites putting Gittites to flight** (v13) — local skirmishes, lost to the main narrative. Chronicler preserves.
- **Jerusalem as Benjaminite city** (v28, 32) — Jerusalem was on Benjamin-Judah border; Chronicler lists Benjaminite settlers there.
