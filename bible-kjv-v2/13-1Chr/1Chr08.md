## Changes

- **"And" · lists** (throughout) — each "and" stands where the Hebrew has וְ; the lists of v14–27 have no verb, and "were" is supplied before "the sons of". **"begat" → "begot"** — Hebrew הוֹלִיד.
- **"And the sons of Bela were" → "And Bela had sons"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיִּהְיוּ בָנִים לְבָלַע, literally "and there were sons to Bela".
- **"Ehud"** (v6) — written here אֵחוּד, with ח, not the אֵהוּד of 7:10 and Judg 3:15; the name keeps its usual English form.
- **"these are the heads of the fathers of the inhabitants of Geba, and they removed them to Manahath: and Naaman, and Ahiah, and Gera, he removed them" → "… and they carried them into exile to Manahath: and Naaman and Ahijah and Gera, he carried them into exile"** (v6–7) — Hebrew וַיַּגְלוּם, an active plural, "and they carried them into exile" (the verse had "who were deported"); הוּא הֶגְלָם "he carried them into exile". הִגְלָה is rendered as 6:15; 9:1. Who carried whom away is not clear in the Hebrew and is left as it stands. "Heads of the fathers" (v6, 10, 13, 28) is רָאשֵׁי אָבוֹת: "houses" is not written.
- **"And Shaharaim begat children in the country of Moab, after he had sent them away; Hushim and Baara were his wives" → "And Shaharaim begot in the field of Moab, after he had sent them away, Hushim and Baara, his wives"** (v8) — Hebrew הוֹלִיד has no object ("children" is not written); שְׂדֵה מוֹאָב "the field of Moab" (Ruth 1:1); מִן־שִׁלְחוֹ אֹתָם, literally "from his sending them away"; the two names stand after it without a verb.
- **"begat of Hodesh his wife"** (v9) — Hebrew מִן־חֹדֶשׁ; the name is also the word "new moon / month". **"Sachyah"** (v10) — Hebrew שָׂכְיָה.
- **"who built Ono, and Lod, with the towns thereof" → "he built Ono and Lod and its daughters"** (v12) — Hebrew הוּא בָּנָה; בְּנֹתֶיהָ "its daughters", as 7:28.
- **"who drove away the inhabitants of Gath" → "they made the inhabitants of Gath flee"** (v13) — Hebrew הִבְרִיחוּ, the causative of בָּרַח "flee".
- **"Penuel"** (v25) — ketiv פניאל "Peniel"; the reading tradition (qere) is translated. **"Athaliah", "Elijah"** (v26, 27) — written here in the short forms עֲתַלְיָה, אֵלִיָּה. **"Obadiah"** (v38) — עֹבַדְיָה.
- **"These were heads of the fathers, by their generations, chief men" → "These were heads of the fathers, by their generations, heads"** (v28) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי אָבוֹת … רָאשִׁים: the same word twice.
- **"And at Gibeon dwelt the father of Gibeon; whose wife's name was Maachah" → "And in Gibeon dwelt the father of Gibeon, and the name of his wife was Maacah"** (v29) — Hebrew יָשְׁבוּ, a plural verb; the father is not named here (9:35 names Jeiel).
- **Verses 30–31** — the Hebrew of this chapter names Abdon, Zur, Kish, Baal, Nadab, Gedor, Ahio, Zecher. The verse had added "Ner" (v30) and "Mikloth" (v31) from 9:36–37; they are not printed here and are taken out. The name here is זָכֶר Zecher; 9:37 has Zechariah.
- **"And these also dwelt with their brethren in Jerusalem, over against them" → "And they also dwelt opposite their brothers in Jerusalem, with their brothers"** (v32) — Hebrew נֶגֶד אֲחֵיהֶם … עִם־אֲחֵיהֶם: "their brothers" twice.
- **"Esh-baal" / "Merib-baal"** (v33, 34) — the names as Chronicles has them, with בַּעַל *baal* "owner, lord"; Samuel has Ish-bosheth (2 Sam 2:8) and Mephibosheth (2 Sam 4:4: from memory).
- **"Rapha was his son"** (v37) — Hebrew רָפָה בְנוֹ, no verb; 9:43 has Rephaiah.
- **"Bocheru"** (v38) — Hebrew בֹּכְרוּ, pointed as a name; with other vowels the letters say "his firstborn".
- **"mighty men of valour, archers, and had many sons, and sons' sons" → "men, mighty ones of ability, who bend the bow, and who multiplied sons and sons' sons"** (v40) — Hebrew אֲנָשִׁים גִּבֹּרֵי־חַיִל, as 7:2; דֹּרְכֵי קֶשֶׁת, literally "treaders of the bow" (the bow is bent with the foot); מַרְבִּים "making many"; בְּנֵי בָנִים "sons of sons".

### Notes
- **Benjamin's fuller genealogy** — this chapter sets up Saul's line. Chronicler positions Saul's genealogy immediately before David's reign.
- **Saulide line (v33-40)** — Ner → Kish → Saul → Jonathan → Merib-baal (=Mephibosheth) → Micah etc. Traced many generations post-Saul.
- **Shaharaim in Moab** (v8-11) — Benjaminite in Moab; unique detail. Suggests tribal mobility across Jordan.
- **Ono and Lod** (v12) — strategic Benjaminite-Ephraimite frontier towns; post-exilic returners settle here (Ezra 2:33, Neh 11:35).
- **Benjaminites making the men of Gath flee** (v13) — local skirmishes, lost to the main narrative. Chronicler preserves.
- **Jerusalem as Benjaminite city** (v28, 32) — Jerusalem was on Benjamin-Judah border; Chronicler lists Benjaminite settlers there.
