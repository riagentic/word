## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins clauses and names with וַ / וְ "and"; it is kept, also in the lists, where the names are joined as the Hebrew joins them.
- **Parallel: 2 Sam 6:12–16 (v25–29)** — where the Hebrew is the same, the wording is that of the finished Samuel verse; the differences are named at each verse. Nothing is taken over from Samuel. v1–24 are only in Chronicles.
- **"David made him houses" → "he made houses for himself"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַשׂ־לוֹ בָתִּים; David is not named in this verse. **"prepared a place"** (v1, 3, 12) — Hebrew הֵכִין, the verb "establish" (14:2), of a place made ready. **"pitched for it a tent"** — Hebrew וַיֶּט, literally "stretched out".
- **"None ought to carry the ark of God but the Levites" → "None are to carry the ark of Elohim except the Levites"** (v2) — Hebrew לֹא לָשֵׂאת … כִּי אִם־הַלְוִיִּם, literally "not to carry … except the Levites". **"for them hath the LORD chosen"** — Hebrew כִּי־בָם בָּחַר יְהוָה, "them" set first. **"to minister unto him"** — Hebrew וּלְשָׁרְתוֹ; the suffix may be "him" (Yahweh) or "it" (the ark).
- **"gathered all Israel together" → "assembled all Israel"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיַּקְהֵל (as 13:5); in v4 another verb, וַיֶּאֱסֹף "gathered". **"the children of Aaron" → "the sons of Aaron"** (v4) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"the chief"** (v5–10, 16, 22, 27) — Hebrew שַׂר, "prince"; of the heads of the Levites' divisions "chief"; of the army in v25 "commanders" (the one word). **Numbers** (v5–10) — in words, in the ordinary English order.
- **"the Levites, for Uriel, Asaiah, and Joel, Shemaiah, and Eliel, and Amminadab"** (v11) — the names are joined as the Hebrew joins them.
- **"Ye are the chief of the fathers of the Levites" → "You are the heads of the fathers of the Levites"** (v12) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי הָאָבוֹת, the heads of the fathers' houses; "houses" (the earlier text) is not written. **"that ye may bring up" → "and bring up"** — Hebrew וְהַעֲלִיתֶם, a command joined by "and". **"unto the place that I have prepared for it" → "to where I have prepared for it"** — Hebrew אֶל־הֲכִינוֹתִי לוֹ, literally "to I-have-prepared for it": the word "place" is not written.
- **"because ye did it not at the first, the LORD our God made a breach upon us" → "because at the first it was not you, Yahweh our Elohim broke out against us"** (v13) — Hebrew כִּי לְמַבָּרִאשׁוֹנָה לֹא אַתֶּם: the second word is a unique form (commonly explained as "because of what [was] at the first"); the clause has no verb: "it was not you"; "did not carry it" (the earlier text) was supplied. פָּרַץ … בָּנוּ is the verb of 13:11. **"we sought him not after the due order" → "we did not seek him according to the ordinance"** — Hebrew לֹא דְרַשְׁנֻהוּ כַּמִּשְׁפָּט: מִשְׁפָּט "judgment", of a ruling "ordinance"; the suffix may again be "him" or "it" (the ark, as 13:3).
- **"the children of the Levites bare the ark of God upon their shoulders with the staves thereon" → "the sons of the Levites carried the ark of Elohim … on their shoulder, with the carrying poles upon them"** (v15) — Hebrew בִּכְתֵפָם בַּמֹּטוֹת עֲלֵיהֶם: "shoulder" is singular; מוֹט "carrying pole" (Num 4:10; another word than בַּד "pole", Exod 25:13). The Hebrew order of the clauses is kept.
- **"to appoint their brethren to be the singers with instruments of musick, psalteries and harps and cymbals, sounding, by lifting up the voice with joy" → "to make their brothers the singers stand with instruments of song, harps and lyres and cymbals, sounding, to raise the voice for gladness"** (v16, 17) — Hebrew לְהַעֲמִיד "make stand" (station, appoint); כְּלֵי־שִׁיר "instruments of song"; נְבָלִים "harps", כִּנֹּרוֹת "lyres" (the kinds are uncertain); מַשְׁמִיעִים "making heard"; לְהָרִים־בְּקוֹל לְשִׂמְחָה, literally "to raise with voice for gladness".
- **"their brethren of the second degree" → "their brothers, the seconds"** (v18) — Hebrew הַמִּשְׁנִים, those of the second rank. **"Zechariah, Ben, and Jaaziel"** — Hebrew זְכַרְיָהוּ בֵּן וְיַעֲזִיאֵל: בֵּן is the word "son" and stands here alone, without a father's name; it is kept as a name, as it is printed; it is missing in the list of v20 and may be a slip of the text. Jaaziel is Aziel in v20. **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (v18, 23, 24) — Hebrew שֹׁעֲרִים.
- **"were appointed to sound with cymbals of brass" → "with cymbals of bronze, to sound"** (v19) — Hebrew בִּמְצִלְתַּיִם נְחֹשֶׁת לְהַשְׁמִיעַ, no finite verb ("were appointed" is not written).
- **"with psalteries on Alamoth" → "with harps upon Alamoth"** (v20) — Hebrew עַל־עֲלָמוֹת: a musical term of unknown meaning (commonly connected with עַלְמָה "young woman": "in the manner of maidens", high voices?; Ps 46 heading); the word is kept as printed.
- **"with harps on the Sheminith to excel" → "with lyres upon the Sheminith, to oversee"** (v21) — Hebrew עַל־הַשְּׁמִינִית, literally "upon the eighth" (an eight-stringed instrument, or a lower pitch?; Ps 6; 12 headings): meaning unknown, the word kept as printed. לְנַצֵּחַ "to oversee, to lead" (the verb of the psalm heading לַמְנַצֵּחַ).
- **"Chenaniah, chief of the Levites, was for song: he instructed about the song, because he was skilful" → "Chenaniah, chief of the Levites in the burden, instructed in the burden, for he was one who had understanding"** (v22) — Hebrew שַׂר־הַלְוִיִּם בְּמַשָּׂא יָסֹר בַּמַּשָּׂא כִּי מֵבִין הוּא: מַשָּׂא is "burden", what is lifted or carried (Num 4:15–49 of the Levites' loads): either the carrying of the ark or the lifting up of the voice, the song (so the KJV); the verse does not decide and the word is kept. יָסֹר is an uncertain form ("he instructed", from יסר, or "he was in charge", from שׂרר). The earlier text had "the carrying".
- **"did blow with the trumpets" → "were sounding the trumpets"** (v24) — Hebrew מַחְצְרִים בַּחֲצֹצְרוֹת (the qere; a verb made from the noun "trumpet", literally "trumpeting with the trumpets"): the straight metal trumpets, not the shofar.
- **"So David, and the elders of Israel, and the captains over thousands, went to bring up" → "And it was David and the elders of Israel and the commanders of the thousands who were going to bring up"** (v25) — Hebrew וַיְהִי דָוִיד … הַהֹלְכִים, a participle with the article. **"with joy" → "with gladness"** — Hebrew בְּשִׂמְחָה. Samuel has "David went and brought up the ark of Elohim … to the city of David with gladness"; "the ark of the covenant of Yahweh" (v25, 26, 28, 29) is Chronicles' name for it here.
- **"when God helped the Levites that bare the ark … they offered seven bullocks" → "when Elohim helped the Levites, the carriers of the ark … they sacrificed seven bulls"** (v26) — Hebrew בֶּעְזֹר הָאֱלֹהִים; וַיִּזְבְּחוּ "sacrificed". Samuel has "when the carriers of the ark of Yahweh had marched six steps, that he sacrificed an ox and a fatling".
- **"clothed with a robe of fine linen" → "wrapped in a robe of fine linen"** (v27) — Hebrew מְכֻרְבָּל, found only here ("wrapped, mantled"); בּוּץ "fine linen, byssus" (another word than שֵׁשׁ). Samuel has in this place "David was whirling with all strength before Yahweh" (מְכַרְכֵּר: the two words are alike in their letters). **"Chenaniah the master of the song with the singers" → "Chenaniah the chief of the burden of the singers"** — Hebrew הַשַּׂר הַמַּשָּׂא הַמְשֹׁרְרִים, three words with the article set side by side, "the chief, the burden, the singers"; the grammar is rough and the sense as in v22. **"David also had upon him an ephod of linen" → "upon David was a linen ephod"** — Hebrew וְעַל־דָּוִיד אֵפוֹד בָּד (Samuel: "David was girded with a linen ephod").
- **"Thus all Israel brought up the ark … with shouting, and with sound of the cornet, and with trumpets, and with cymbals, making a noise with psalteries and harps" → "And all Israel were bringing up the ark … with shouting and with the voice of a shofar and with trumpets and with cymbals, sounding with harps and lyres"** (v28) — Hebrew מַעֲלִים, a participle; בְּקוֹל שׁוֹפָר "with the voice of a shofar" (the ram's horn), beside חֲצֹצְרוֹת "trumpets" (two instruments); מַשְׁמִעִים "making heard". Samuel has "David and all the house of Israel" and ends at "shofar".
- **"as the ark … came to the city of David" → "as the ark … was coming as far as the city of David"** (v29) — Hebrew בָּא עַד־עִיר דָּוִיד (Samuel: "into the city of David"). **"looking out at a window" → "looked down through the window"** — Hebrew נִשְׁקְפָה בְּעַד הַחַלּוֹן. **"dancing and playing" → "skipping and playing"** — Hebrew מְרַקֵּד וּמְשַׂחֵק: רִקֵּד "skip, leap about" (Ps 114:4), שִׂחֵק "play" (13:8); Samuel has two other verbs, "leaping and whirling before Yahweh".
- **"psalteries" → "harps"** (v16, 20, 28) — Hebrew נְבָלִים. **"harps" → "lyres"** (v16, 21, 28) — Hebrew כִּנֹּרוֹת. **"of brass" → "of bronze"** (v19).

### Notes
- **Chronicler's Levitical correction** — careful procedure now: Levites carry (Num 4:15, 7:9), poles used (Ex 25:14-15), sanctification, proper guild organization.
- **Three choir leaders** — Heman, Asaph, Ethan (=Jeduthun). Source-figures of many psalm titles.
- **"Broke out" echoes ch 13** — Chronicler explicitly names the error: wrong method + insufficient seeking.
- **David's linen ephod** — priestly-adjacent garment, unusual for a king. Cf. Samuel (1 Sam 2:18). David's worship leadership.
- **Michal's contempt** — inherited trope from 2 Sam 6; Chronicler records briefly, omits consequence (her childlessness).
- **Musical elaboration** — Chronicler details liturgical apparatus more than Samuel, reflecting his post-exilic interest in temple worship.
- **Seven bulls and seven rams** (v26) — perfect sacrificial number. Cf. 2 Sam 6:13 (an ox and a fatling after six steps).
