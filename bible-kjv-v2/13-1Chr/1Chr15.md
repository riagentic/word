# 1 Chronicles 15

[1] David built houses for himself in the city of David; he prepared a place for the ark of *Elohim*, and pitched a tent for it.

[2] Then David said, "No one ought to carry the ark of *Elohim* but the Levites; for Yahweh has chosen them to carry the ark of Yahweh and to minister to him forever."

[3] David assembled all Israel at Jerusalem to bring up the ark of Yahweh to its place, which he had prepared for it.

[4] David gathered the sons of Aaron and the Levites:

[5] of the sons of Kohath: Uriel the chief and his brothers — one hundred twenty;

[6] of the sons of Merari: Asaiah the chief and his brothers — two hundred twenty;

[7] of the sons of Gershom: Joel the chief and his brothers — one hundred thirty;

[8] of the sons of Elizaphan: Shemaiah the chief and his brothers — two hundred;

[9] of the sons of Hebron: Eliel the chief and his brothers — eighty;

[10] of the sons of Uzziel: Amminadab the chief and his brothers — one hundred twelve.

[11] David summoned Zadok and Abiathar the priests, and the Levites Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, and Amminadab,

[12] and said to them, "You are the heads of the fathers' houses of the Levites. Sanctify yourselves, you and your brothers, that you may bring up the ark of Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel to the place I have prepared for it.

[13] Because you did not carry it at the first, Yahweh our *Elohim* burst out against us, for we did not seek him according to the ordinance."

[14] So the priests and the Levites sanctified themselves to bring up the ark of Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel.

[15] The children of the Levites carried the ark of *Elohim* on their shoulders with the poles upon them, as Moses had commanded according to the word of Yahweh.

[16] David spoke to the chiefs of the Levites to appoint their brothers as singers with instruments of music — harps, lyres, and cymbals — to raise sounds of joy.

[17] So the Levites appointed Heman son of Joel; and of his brothers Asaph son of Berechiah; and of the sons of Merari their brothers, Ethan son of Kushaiah;

[18] and with them their brothers of the second rank: Zechariah, Ben, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-edom, and Jeiel, the gatekeepers.

[19] The singers Heman, Asaph, and Ethan were to sound with bronze cymbals;

[20] Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah with harps on alamot;

[21] Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-edom, Jeiel, and Azaziah with lyres on the sheminit, to lead.

[22] Chenaniah, chief of the Levites, was over the carrying — he directed the carrying because he was skilled in it.

[23] Berechiah and Elkanah were gatekeepers for the ark.

[24] Shebaniah, Joshaphat, Nethanel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer, the priests, blew the trumpets — before the ark of *Elohim*; Obed-edom and Jehiah were gatekeepers for the ark.

[25] So David, the elders of Israel, and the commanders of thousands went to bring up the ark of the covenant of Yahweh from the house of Obed-edom with joy.

[26] When *Elohim* helped the Levites who were carrying the ark of the covenant of Yahweh, they sacrificed seven bulls and seven rams.

[27] David was clothed with a robe of fine linen, as were all the Levites who carried the ark, the singers, and Chenaniah the director of the carrying with the singers; David also wore a linen ephod.

[28] So all Israel brought up the ark of the covenant of Yahweh with shouting, with the sound of the shofar, with the trumpets, and with cymbals, making music on harps and lyres.

[29] As the ark of the covenant of Yahweh came to the city of David, Michal daughter of Saul looked out of the window and saw King David dancing and celebrating; and she despised him in her heart.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"after the due order" → "according to the ordinance"** (v13) — *ka-mishpat*; according to the established rule.
- **"psalteries" → "harps"** (v16, 20, 28) — *nevalim*.
- **"harps" → "lyres"** (v16, 21, 28) — *kinorot*.
- **"Alamoth" / "Sheminith" — retained as musical terms** (v20, 21) — *alamot* = "maiden-voices" (soprano range?); *sheminit* = "the eighth" (lower octave?). Musical technical terms of uncertain meaning.
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (v18, 23, 24).
- **"about the song" / "master of the song" → "over the carrying" / "director of the carrying"** (v22, 27) — Hebrew *massa* can mean "burden/song"; here context of bearing the ark favors "carrying."
- **"of brass" → "bronze"** (v19).

### Notes
- **Chronicler's Levitical correction** — careful procedure now: Levites carry (Num 4:15, 7:9), poles used (Ex 25:14-15), sanctification, proper guild organization.
- **Three choir leaders** — Heman, Asaph, Ethan (=Jeduthun). Source-figures of many psalm titles.
- **"Burst out" echoes ch 13** — Chronicler explicitly names the error: wrong method + insufficient seeking.
- **David's linen ephod** — priestly-adjacent garment, unusual for a king. Cf. Samuel (1 Sam 2:18). David's worship leadership.
- **Michal's contempt** — inherited trope from 2 Sam 6; Chronicler records briefly, omits consequence (her childlessness).
- **Musical elaboration** — Chronicler details liturgical apparatus more than Samuel, reflecting his post-exilic interest in temple worship.
- **Seven bulls and seven rams** (v26) — perfect sacrificial number. Cf. 2 Sam 6:13 (only one ox and fatling every six paces).
