# 1 Chronicles 18

[1] After this, David struck down the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ and subdued them; he took ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧ and its towns out of the hand of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧.

[2] He struck down ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧; the ⟦moabites|Moavim|Moabites⟧ became David's servants and brought tribute.

[3] David struck down Hadadezer king of ⟦zobah|Tzovah|Zobah⟧ as far as ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧, as he went to establish his dominion at the ⟦euphrates|Perat|Euphrates⟧ River.

[4] David took from him a thousand chariots, seven thousand horsemen, and twenty thousand foot soldiers. David hamstrung all the chariot horses, but reserved of them enough for a hundred chariots.

[5] When the ⟦arameans|Arammim|Syrians⟧ of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧ came to help Hadadezer king of ⟦zobah|Tzovah|Zobah⟧, David struck down twenty-two thousand men of the ⟦arameans|Arammim|Syrians⟧.

[6] Then David put garrisons in ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧; the ⟦arameans|Arammim|Syrians⟧ became David's servants and brought tribute. ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ gave victory to David wherever he went.

[7] David took the shields of gold that were on the servants of Hadadezer, and brought them to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[8] From ⟦tibhath|Tivchat|Tibhath⟧ and from ⟦cun|Kun|Cun⟧, cities of Hadadezer, David took much bronze, with which ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ made the bronze Sea, the pillars, and the vessels of bronze.

[9] When Tou king of ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧ heard that David had struck down all the army of Hadadezer king of ⟦zobah|Tzovah|Zobah⟧,

[10] he sent Hadoram his son to King David to ask after his welfare and to congratulate him, because he had fought against Hadadezer and struck him down (Hadadezer had been at war with Tou); he brought with him all kinds of articles of gold, silver, and bronze.

[11] These also King David dedicated to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, along with the silver and gold that he had carried off from all the nations — from Edom, ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, the children of Ammon, the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, and ⟦amalek|Amaleq|Amalek⟧.

[12] Moreover ⟦abishai|Avishai|Abishai⟧ son of ⟦zeruiah|Tzeruyah|Zeruiah⟧ struck down eighteen thousand ⟦edomites|Edomim|Edomites⟧ in the Valley of Salt.

[13] He put garrisons in Edom; all the ⟦edomites|Edomim|Edomites⟧ became David's servants. ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ gave victory to David wherever he went.

[14] So David reigned over all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, executing justice and righteousness for all his people.

[15] ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ son of ⟦zeruiah|Tzeruyah|Zeruiah⟧ was over the army; ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ son of ⟦ahilud|Achilud|Ahilud⟧ was recorder;

[16] ⟦zadok|Tzadoq|Zadok⟧ son of ⟦ahitub|Achituv|Ahitub⟧ and ⟦ahimelech|Achimelech|Ahimelech⟧ son of ⟦abiathar|Evyatar|Abiathar⟧ were priests; Shavsha was scribe;

[17] ⟦benaiah|Benayahu|Benaiah⟧ son of ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ was over the ⟦cherethites|Keretim|Cherethites⟧ and the ⟦pelethites|Peletim|Pelethites⟧. The sons of David were chief men at the king's side.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"gifts" → "tribute"** (v2, 6) — *minchah*; in political context = tribute.
- **"houghed" → "hamstrung"** (v4) — *iqqer*; Israelites had no use for chariot horses (Deut 17:16), so disabled.
- **"Syrians / Syria" → "Arameans / Aram"** (v5, 6).
- **"preserved" → "gave victory to"** (v6, 13) — *yoshia*; common Chronistic phrase.
- **"brass" → "bronze"** (v8).
- **"Hadarezer" → "Hadadezer"** (throughout) — standard form.
- **"Tou" — retained** (v9) — 2 Sam 8:9 has "Toi."
- **"Abimelech" → "Ahimelech"** (v16) — textual; 2 Sam 8:17 has "Ahimelech."
- **"judgment and justice" → "justice and righteousness"** (v14) — *mishpat u-tzedaqah*.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Sam 8** — Chronicler's account of David's conquests.
- **Territorial extent** — Euphrates to Red Sea; matches promised boundaries (Gen 15:18, Deut 11:24).
- **Temple preparation** — Chronicler notes that bronze for Solomon's temple came from these conquests (v8). Emphasizes David's temple-building preparation.
- **Hadadezer** — Zobah's king; Aramean power north of Damascus. Major regional opponent.
- **Tou of Hamath** — sends embassy; Hamath a buffer state further north. Diplomatic alliance against common foe.
- **Cherethites and Pelethites** — royal bodyguard; likely Cretan mercenaries (cf. "Caphtor" = Crete, Amos 9:7).
- **Cabinet list** (v15-17) — matches 2 Sam 8:16-18 with minor variations. "Sons of David chief men" — Chronicler's euphemism for 2 Sam 8:18's "priests" (*kohanim*), avoiding non-Aaronic priesthood language.
