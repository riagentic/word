# 1 Chronicles 18

[1] After this, David struck down the Philistines and subdued them; he took Gath and its towns out of the hand of the Philistines.

[2] He struck down Moab; the Moabites became David's servants and brought tribute.

[3] David struck down Hadadezer king of Zobah as far as Hamath, as he went to establish his dominion at the Euphrates River.

[4] David took from him a thousand chariots, seven thousand horsemen, and twenty thousand foot soldiers. David hamstrung all the chariot horses, but reserved of them enough for a hundred chariots.

[5] When the Arameans of Damascus came to help Hadadezer king of Zobah, David struck down twenty-two thousand men of the Arameans.

[6] Then David put garrisons in Aram of Damascus; the Arameans became David's servants and brought tribute. Yahweh gave victory to David wherever he went.

[7] David took the shields of gold that were on the servants of Hadadezer, and brought them to Jerusalem.

[8] From Tibhath and from Cun, cities of Hadadezer, David took much bronze, with which Solomon made the bronze Sea, the pillars, and the vessels of bronze.

[9] When Tou king of Hamath heard that David had struck down all the army of Hadadezer king of Zobah,

[10] he sent Hadoram his son to King David to ask after his welfare and to congratulate him, because he had fought against Hadadezer and struck him down (Hadadezer had been at war with Tou); he brought with him all kinds of articles of gold, silver, and bronze.

[11] These also King David dedicated to Yahweh, along with the silver and gold that he had carried off from all the nations — from Edom, Moab, the children of Ammon, the Philistines, and Amalek.

[12] Moreover Abishai son of Zeruiah struck down eighteen thousand Edomites in the Valley of Salt.

[13] He put garrisons in Edom; all the Edomites became David's servants. Yahweh gave victory to David wherever he went.

[14] So David reigned over all Israel, executing justice and righteousness for all his people.

[15] Joab son of Zeruiah was over the army; Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was recorder;

[16] Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar were priests; Shavsha was scribe;

[17] Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and the Pelethites. The sons of David were chief men at the king's side.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"gifts" → "tribute"** (v2, 6) — *minchah*; in political context = tribute.
- **"houghed" → "hamstrung"** (v4) — *iqqer*; Israelites had no use for chariot horses (Deut 17:16), so disabled.
- **"Syrians / Syria" → "Arameans / Aram"** (v5, 6).
- **"preserved" → "gave victory to"** (v6, 13) — *yoshia*; common Chronistic phrase.
- **"brass" → "bronze"** (v8).
- **"Hadarezer" → "Hadadezer"** (throughout) — standard form.
- **"Tou" — retained** (v9) — 2 Sam 8:9 has "Toi."
- **"Abimelech" → "Ahimelech"** (v16) — textual; 2 Sam 8:17 has "Ahimelech."
- **"judgment and justice" → "justice and righteousness"** (v14) — *mishpat u-tzedaqah*.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Sam 8** — Chronicler's account of David's conquests.
- **Territorial extent** — Euphrates to Red Sea; matches promised boundaries (Gen 15:18, Deut 11:24).
- **Temple preparation** — Chronicler notes that bronze for Solomon's temple came from these conquests (v8). Emphasizes David's temple-building preparation.
- **Hadadezer** — Zobah's king; Aramean power north of Damascus. Major regional opponent.
- **Tou of Hamath** — sends embassy; Hamath a buffer state further north. Diplomatic alliance against common foe.
- **Kerethim and Pelethim** — royal bodyguard; likely Cretan mercenaries (cf. "Caphtor" = Crete, Amos 9:7).
- **Cabinet list** (v15-17) — matches 2 Sam 8:16-18 with minor variations. "Sons of David chief men" — Chronicler's euphemism for 2 Sam 8:18's "priests" (*kohanim*), avoiding non-Aaronic priesthood language.
