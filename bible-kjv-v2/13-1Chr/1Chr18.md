# 1 Chronicles 18

[1] And it came to pass after this that David struck the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ and brought them low; and he took ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧ and its daughters from the hand of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧.

[2] And he struck ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, and ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ became servants to David, bearers of an offering.

[3] And David struck Hadadezer king of ⟦zobah|Tzovah|Zobah⟧, toward ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧, as he went to set up his hand at the River ⟦euphrates|Perat|Euphrates⟧.

[4] And David captured from him a thousand chariots and seven thousand horsemen and twenty thousand men on foot; and David hamstrung all the chariots, and he left of them a hundred chariots.

[5] And ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧ came to help Hadadezer king of ⟦zobah|Tzovah|Zobah⟧, and David struck down in ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ twenty-two thousand men.

[6] And David put in ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, and ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ became servants to David, bearers of an offering. And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ brought salvation to David in all where he went.

[7] And David took the shields of gold that were on the servants of Hadadezer, and he brought them to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[8] And from ⟦tibhath|Tivchat|Tibhath⟧ and from ⟦cun|Kun|Cun⟧, cities of Hadadezer, David took very much bronze; with it ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ made the sea of bronze and the pillars and the utensils of bronze.

[9] And Tou king of ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧ heard that David had struck all the force of Hadadezer king of ⟦zobah|Tzovah|Zobah⟧.

[10] And he sent Hadoram his son to King David, to ask him about ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ and to bless him, because he had fought against Hadadezer and struck him, for Hadadezer was a man of wars with Tou; and all vessels of gold and silver and bronze.

[11] These also King David sanctified to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, with the silver and the gold that he had carried from all the nations: from Edom and from ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ and from the sons of Ammon and from the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ and from ⟦amalek|Amaleq|Amalek⟧.

[12] And ⟦abishai|Avishai|Abishai⟧ the son of ⟦zeruiah|Tzeruyah|Zeruiah⟧ struck down Edom in the Valley of Salt, eighteen thousand.

[13] And he put garrisons in Edom, and all Edom became servants to David. And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ saved David in all where he went.

[14] And David reigned over all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and he was doing judgment and righteousness for all his people.

[15] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ the son of ⟦zeruiah|Tzeruyah|Zeruiah⟧ was over the host; and ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ the son of ⟦ahilud|Achilud|Ahilud⟧ was recorder;

[16] and ⟦zadok|Tzadoq|Zadok⟧ the son of ⟦ahitub|Achituv|Ahitub⟧ and ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ the son of ⟦abiathar|Evyatar|Abiathar⟧ were priests; and Shavsha was scribe;

[17] and ⟦benaiah|Benayahu|Benaiah⟧ the son of ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ was over the ⟦cherethites|Keretim|Cherethites⟧ and the ⟦pelethites|Peletim|Pelethites⟧; and the sons of David were the first at the hand of the king.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **The chapter beside 2 Sam 8** — where the Hebrew of the two chapters is the same, the wording is that of 2 Sam 8; each difference below is a difference in the Hebrew of Chronicles. No name, number or word is taken over from Samuel.
- **"subdued them" → "brought them low"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּכְנִיעֵם. **"Gath and her towns" → "Gath and its daughters"** — Hebrew גַּת וּבְנֹתֶיהָ, the settlements that belong to the city; 2 Sam 8:1 has "Metheg-ammah".
- **"smote" → "struck" / "struck down"** (v1, 2, 3, 5, 9, 10, 12) — one Hebrew verb, הִכָּה; "struck down" where killing is meant.
- **"the Moabites" → "Moab"** (v2) — Hebrew מוֹאָב, the name, with a plural verb. The measuring with the cord (2 Sam 8:2) is not in Chronicles. **"brought gifts" → "bearers of an offering"** (v2, 6) — Hebrew נֹשְׂאֵי מִנְחָה; "tribute" is meant.
- **"unto Hamath" → "toward Hamath"** (v3) — Hebrew חֲמָתָה; not in 2 Sam 8:3, which has "the son of Rehob".
- **"to stablish his dominion" → "to set up his hand"** (v3) — Hebrew לְהַצִּיב יָדוֹ; "hand" is power, or a monument (as 1 Sam 15:12). 2 Sam 8:3 has "to bring back his hand". "his" may be David or Hadadezer.
- **"a thousand chariots and seven thousand horsemen"** (v4) — the figures as Chronicles prints them; 2 Sam 8:4 has "a thousand and seven hundred horsemen". **"houghed all the chariot horses" → "hamstrung all the chariots"** — Hebrew וַיְעַקֵּר … אֶת־כָּל־הָרֶכֶב; "horses" is not written.
- **"the Syrians of Damascus" → "Aram of Damascus"** (v5, 6) — Hebrew אֲרַם דַּרְמֶשֶׂק, the name of the people; Chronicles spells "Darmeseq".
- **"David put garrisons in Syria-damascus" → "David put in Aram of Damascus"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיָּשֶׂם דָּוִיד בַּאֲרַם דַּרְמֶשֶׂק: the verb has no object in the Hebrew of Chronicles. 2 Sam 8:6 has "garrisons", and so has v13 here; the word is not supplied.
- **"Thus the LORD preserved David" → "Yahweh brought salvation to David"** (v6) and **"saved David"** (v13) — Hebrew הוֹשִׁיעַ "save"; in v6 with לְ "to David", in v13 with the object.
- **"brass" → "bronze"** (v8, 10). **"Tibhath … Chun"** — the names as Chronicles has them; 2 Sam 8:8 has "Betah … Berothai". The sentence about Solomon is not in Samuel.
- **"the brasen sea" → "the sea of bronze"**, **"the vessels of brass" → "the utensils of bronze"** (v8) — Hebrew יָם הַנְּחֹשֶׁת, כְּלֵי הַנְּחֹשֶׁת, as 2 Kgs 25:14; the word כְּלִי is the same as "vessels" in v10.
- **"Tou"**, **"Hadoram"** (v9, 10) — 2 Sam 8:9–10 has "Toi" and "Joram". **"host" → "force"** (v9) — Hebrew חֵיל.
- **"to enquire of his welfare, and to congratulate him" → "to ask him about shalom and to bless him"** (v10) — Hebrew לִשְׁאָל־לוֹ לְשָׁלוֹם וּלְבָרֲכוֹ (the written form לשאול and the read form לִשְׁאָל mean the same).
- **"had war with Tou" → "was a man of wars with Tou"** (v10) — Hebrew אִישׁ מִלְחֲמוֹת תֹּעוּ.
- **"and with him all manner of vessels" → "and all vessels of gold and silver and bronze"** (v10) — Hebrew וְכֹל כְּלֵי זָהָב וָכֶסֶף וּנְחֹשֶׁת, with no verb and no "with him"; 2 Sam 8:10 has "and in his hand were".
- **"dedicated" → "sanctified"** (v11) — Hebrew הִקְדִּישׁ. **"that he brought" → "that he had carried"** — Hebrew נָשָׂא "lift, carry". **"from Edom"** — so Chronicles; 2 Sam 8:12 has "from Aram".
- **"children of Ammon" → "sons of Ammon"** (v11) — Hebrew בְּנֵי עַמּוֹן.
- **"Abishai … slew of the Edomites" → "Abishai … struck down Edom"** (v12) — Hebrew הִכָּה אֶת־אֱדוֹם; the name is written "Abshai" here. 2 Sam 8:13 names David and "Aram".
- **"all the Edomites" → "all Edom"** (v13) — Hebrew כָל־אֱדוֹם.
- **"executed judgment and justice" → "was doing judgment and righteousness"** (v14) — Hebrew עֹשֶׂה מִשְׁפָּט וּצְדָקָה.
- **"recorder"** (v15) — Hebrew מַזְכִּיר, lit. "one who brings to remembrance".
- **"Abimelech the son of Abiathar"** (v16) — the name as the Hebrew of Chronicles prints it, אֲבִימֶלֶךְ; 2 Sam 8:17 has "Ahimelech". **"Shavsha"** — 2 Sam 8:17 has "Seraiah".
- **"chief about the king" → "the first at the hand of the king"** (v17) — Hebrew הָרִאשֹׁנִים לְיַד הַמֶּלֶךְ; 2 Sam 8:18 has "were priests".

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Sam 8** — David's wars.
- **The bronze** (v8) — Chronicles says that Solomon made the sea, the pillars and the utensils of bronze from it.
- **Hadadezer** — king of Zobah, an Aramean kingdom.
- **Cherethites and Pelethites** — the king's guard.
