## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Aphses" → "Happizzez"** (v15) — standard form.
- **"one sort with another" → "these with those"** (v5) — Hebrew *elleh im elleh*.
- **"governors of the sanctuary" → "holy officials"** (v5) — *sarei-qodesh*.
- **"Jeshebeab" — retained** (v13).
- **"Hezekel" → "Jehezkel"** (v16) — Ezekiel-prototype name.

### Notes
- **Twenty-four priestly divisions** — weekly rotation; each division served roughly two weeks per year. System still extant in New Testament times: Zechariah served in the division of Abijah (Luke 1:5), the eighth course.
- **Disproportion** (v4) — Eleazar's line twice as large as Ithamar's; lot distribution accordingly.
- **Jehoiarib** (v7) — Hasmonean Maccabees traced lineage here (1 Macc 2:1). First course most prestigious.
- **Post-exilic continuation** — only four courses returned per Ezra 2:36-39; later reorganized to 24 again.
- **Chronicler's design** — establishes David as founder of the post-exilic temple order his readers inherit. Temple-worship institutions trace to Davidic authority, not just Mosaic.
- **Jaaziah** (v26-27) — unique here; possibly a Merarite figure not elsewhere attested, or a variant name.
