## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"enlarge my coast" → "enlarge my border"** (v10) — *gevul*.
- **"that it may not grieve me" → "that it may not bring me pain"** (v10) — *le-vilti otzbi*; closes the wordplay: pain-born Jabez asks Yahweh to keep him from pain.
- **"Jehudijah" → "Judahite wife"** (v18) — *ishto ha-yehudiyyah*; "his Judean/Judahite wife," probably a descriptive contrast with Bithiah the Egyptian princess.
- **"Jashubi-lehem" → "returned to Lehem"** (v22) — Hebrew *va-yashuvu lahem / le-lahem*; either proper name or "returned to Lehem" (reading ambiguous).
- **"potters, and those that dwelt among plants and hedges" → "potters, inhabitants of Netaim and Gederah"** (v23) — Hebrew *yoshvei neta'im u-gederah*; likely place names (Netaim, Gederah), not common nouns.
- **"they of Ham" → "were of Cham"** (v40) — Hebrew *min-Cham*; MT has the name, not a gentilic ("Hamites").
- **"rooms" → "place"** (v41) — *tachtehem*; "in their place/stead."
- **"habitations that were found there" → "Meunim who were found there"** (v41) — MT reading of consonantal text: *ha-me'inim* (Meunim = Arabian tribe).

### Notes
- **Prayer of Jabez** (v9-10) — isolated pious note in genealogy. Verbal pun between *otzev* (pain) and *Yabetz*. Became famous in modern piety ("Prayer of Jabez" movement).
- **Craftsman's Valley** — near Lod (Neh 11:35); artisan settlements.
- **Pharaoh's daughter Bithiah** — an Egyptian princess marrying into Judahite clan. Rabbinic tradition elaborates her story.
- **Linen-workers, potters with the king** (v21-23) — royal craft guilds; evidence of state-supervised industries.
- **Simeon absorbed into Judah** — Simeon's territory within Judah (Josh 19:1-9). Chronicler treats them together.
- **Hezekiah-era Simeonite expansion** (v41) — Simeonites take Meunite-Hamite pasturelands and Amalekite remnants. Supports Hezekiah's aggressive reclamation policy.
- **Amalekite remnant destroyed** (v43) — completes the exterminating mandate from Exod 17 / 1 Sam 15.
