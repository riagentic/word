## Changes

- **"The sons of …" / "And the sons of …"; "and" between names** (throughout) — as the Hebrew has or lacks וְ (v1 "Perez, Hezron and Carmi and Hur and Shobal"; v16 "Ziph and Ziphah, Tiria and Asarel"; v24 "Nemuel and Jamin, Jarib, Zerah, Shaul"). Supplied: the colon after "the sons of X" and the copula.
- **"begat" → "begot"** (v2, 8, 11, 12, 14) — הוֹלִיד.
- **"families" → "clans"** (v2, 8, 21, 27, 38) — מִשְׁפָּחוֹת. "the Zorathites": the Hebrew has the singular with the article.
- **"And these were of the father of Etam" → "And these are the father of Etam"** (v3) — וְאֵלֶּה אֲבִי עֵיטָם: the Hebrew says just this and is difficult ("these" plural, "father" singular); nothing is supplied. Many read "the sons of".
- **"And Penuel the father of Gedor, and Ezer the father of Hushah"** (v4) — no verb in the Hebrew.
- **"and Haahashtari"** (v6) — הָאֲחַשְׁתָּרִי has the article: "the Ahashtarite". **"and Zobebah"** (v8) — הַצֹּבֵבָה, also with the article. The tokens keep the name forms.
- **"Jezoar" → "Zohar"** (v7) — qere וְצֹחַר; the written form is יצחר (Izhar).
- **"And Jabez was more honourable than his brethren" → "was honored more than his brothers"** (v9) — נִכְבָּד מֵאֶחָיו.
- **"Because I bare him with sorrow" → "Because I bore in pain"** (v9) — כִּי יָלַדְתִּי בְּעֹצֶב: no "him"; עֹצֶב "pain", as Gen 3:16. The name יַעְבֵּץ plays on עֹצֶב with two letters changed in order.
- **"Oh that thou wouldest bless me indeed, and enlarge my coast, and that thine hand might be with me, and that thou wouldest keep me from evil, that it may not grieve me!" → "If you will surely bless me, and make my border large, and your hand is with me, and you act, away from evil, so that I am not pained —"** (v10) — אִם־בָּרֵךְ תְּבָרֲכֵנִי … : the Hebrew is a condition, "If …", with four verbs, and it has no clause that completes it (as the vow in Gen 28:20–22 has); the sentence is left unfinished, as it stands. It is commonly taken as a wish ("Oh that …"). בָּרֵךְ תְּבָרֲכֵנִי is the infinitive absolute with its verb, lit. "blessing you bless me"; הִרְבִּיתָ lit. "make many"; וְעָשִׂיתָ מֵּרָעָה lit. "and you do from evil" ("keep me" is not in the Hebrew); לְבִלְתִּי עָצְבִּי lit. "for not my paining", the word of v9: who or what would give the pain is not said.
- **"called on the God of Israel" → "called to the Elohim of Israel"** (v10) — קָרָא לְ. **"And God granted him that which he requested" → "And Elohim brought about what he asked"** — וַיָּבֵא, lit. "made come"; no "him".
- **"the sons of Othniel; Hathath"** (v13) — the Hebrew names one; "and Meonothai", which stood here before, is not in the Masoretic Text and is removed. v14 begins with Meonothai without saying whose son he is.
- **"the father of the valley of Charashim; for they were craftsmen"** (v14) — גֵּיא חֲרָשִׁים is kept as the name Ge-harashim, "valley of craftsmen"; the gloss that stood in the verse is taken out. The reason, "for they were craftsmen" (חֲרָשִׁים), uses the word of the name.
- **"and the sons of Elah, even Kenaz" → "And the sons of Elah, and Kenaz"** (v15) — וּבְנֵי אֵלָה וּקְנַז: the Hebrew has "and" before Kenaz; the sentence is rendered as it stands.
- **"And the sons of Ezra" → "And the son of Ezrah"** (v17) — singular בֶּן, though four names follow. **"and she bare Miriam" → "And she conceived Miriam"** — וַתַּהַר; the Hebrew does not say who "she" is.
- **"And his wife Jehudijah" → "And his wife, the Judahite"** (v18) — הַיְהֻדִיָּה, with the article. Whose wife she is, and how "these are the sons of Bithiah" belongs to what goes before, is not said; nothing is rearranged.
- **"And the sons of his wife Hodiah" → "And the sons of the wife of Hodiah"** (v19) — אֵשֶׁת הוֹדִיָּה, a genitive.
- **"and Tilon"** (v20) — qere וְתִילוֹן; written ותולון. "Ben-hanan" and "Ben-zoheth" can also be read "the son of Hanan", "and the son of Zoheth".
- **"the families of the house of them that wrought fine linen, of the house of Ashbea" → "the clans of the house of the service of the fine linen, of Beth-ashbea"** (v21) — בֵּית־עֲבֹדַת הַבֻּץ: עֲבֹדָה "service"; בּוּץ is the later word for fine linen (שֵׁשׁ in the Torah). לְבֵית אַשְׁבֵּעַ "of the house of Ashbea" is kept as a name.
- **"who had the dominion in Moab, and Jashubi-lehem. And these are ancient things" → "who became lords over Moab, and Jashubi-lehem. And the words are ancient"** (v22) — בָּעֲלוּ לְמוֹאָב: בָּעַל "be lord / owner of", also "become the husband of" (so: "who married into Moab"); not decided by the Hebrew. וְיָשֻׁבִי לָחֶם is a name as the Masoretic Text points it (the earlier "returned to Lehem" changed the pointing); the lexicon has no key for this name, so it stands in the token of the name Jashub, whose stem it shares. הַדְּבָרִים "the words" (also "the matters").
- **"These were the potters, and those that dwelt among plants and hedges: there they dwelt with the king for his work" → "They were the potters and the inhabitants of Netaim and Gederah; with the king, in his work, they dwelt there"** (v23) — נְטָעִים "plantings" and גְּדֵרָה "fold, walled place" are taken as place names; they can be common nouns. יוֹצְרִים "potters", lit. "formers".
- **"neither did all their family multiply, like to the children of Judah" → "all their clan did not multiply as the sons of Judah did"** (v27) — עַד־בְּנֵי יְהוּדָה, lit. "as far as the sons of Judah".
- **"unto the reign of David" → "until David reigned"** (v31) — עַד־מְלֹךְ דָּוִיד.
- **"These were their habitations, and their genealogy" → "These were their dwelling places, and they had their enrollment by genealogy"** (v33) — זֹאת מוֹשְׁבֹתָם וְהִתְיַחְשָׂם לָהֶם: lit. "this (was) their dwelling places, and their enrolling by genealogy (was) theirs"; הִתְיַחֵשׂ is a word of Chronicles, Ezra and Nehemiah.
- **"These mentioned by their names were princes in their families: and the house of their fathers increased greatly" → "these, entered by names, were chieftains in their clans; and their fathers' houses spread abroad greatly"** (v38) — הַבָּאִים בְּשֵׁמוֹת, lit. "those coming in by names"; נְשִׂיאִים "chieftains"; פָּרְצוּ "broke out, spread abroad"; לָרוֹב "to a multitude".
- **"unto the east side of the valley" → "as far as the east of the valley"** (v39) — literally "the sunrise".
- **"and the land was wide, and quiet, and peaceable; for they of Ham had dwelt there of old" → "the land was wide on both hands, and quiet and at ease; for those who dwelt there before were from Ham"** (v40) — רַחֲבַת יָדַיִם, as Gen 34:21; מִן־חָם "from Ham".
- **"smote their tents, and the habitations that were found there, and destroyed them utterly unto this day" → "struck their tents and the Meunim who were found there, and devoted them to destruction, to this day"** (v41) — qere הַמְּעוּנִים, the people (written המעינים; the KJV took it as "habitations"); וַיַּחֲרִימֻם, the verb of חֵרֶם. "Hezekiah" is written יְחִזְקִיָּהוּ here. "written by names": הַכְּתוּבִים בְּשֵׁמוֹת.
- **"rooms" → "in their place"** (v41) — תַּחְתֵּיהֶם.
- **"went to mount Seir, having for their captains Pelatiah …" → "went to the hill country of Seir, and Pelatiah … were at their head"** (v42) — הַר שֵׂעִיר as Gen 36:8; בְּרֹאשָׁם.
- **"And they smote the rest of the Amalekites that were escaped" → "And they struck down the remnant of Amalek that had escaped"** (v43) — שְׁאֵרִית הַפְּלֵטָה לַעֲמָלֵק, lit. "the remnant of the escape that belonged to Amalek"; the Hebrew has the name Amalek, not "the Amalekites".

### Notes
- **Jabez** (v9–10) — two verses of plain narrative inside the lists; the man is not tied to any name before or after. The town Jabez stands in 2:55.
- **Ge-harashim** (v14) — Neh 11:35.
- **Simeon** (v24–43) — the cities of v28–33 are those of Josh 19:2–8, with some names spelled differently; the Chronicles forms are kept.
