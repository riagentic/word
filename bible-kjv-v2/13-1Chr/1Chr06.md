## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"firstborn Vashni, and Abiah" → "firstborn Joel, and Abijah the second"** (v28) — MT defective; LXX and 1 Sam 8:2 supply "Joel." Hebrew *ve-shenî* ("and the second") misread as a name "Vashni" by KJV.
- **"castles" → "encampments"** (v54) — *tirot*; fortified camps / walled-in settlements.
- **"coasts" → "territory"** (v54, 66) — *gevulam*.
- **"suburbs" → "pasture lands"** (throughout) — *migrashim*; common lands around Levitical towns for grazing.
- **"tabernacle of the congregation" → "Tent of Meeting"** (v32) — *ohel mo'ed*.

### Notes
- **Priestly line (v1-15)** — Aaron-Eleazar-Phinehas-Zadok etc. 22 generations Aaron → Jehozadak (exile). Some generations missing; standard genealogical compression. Zadok the Davidic priest (v8) is the key pivot.
- **Jehozadak** — father of Joshua the high priest under Zerubbabel (Hag 1:1, Zech 6:11).
- **Three Levitical houses** — Gershom, Kohath, Merari. Three chief singers: Heman (Kohath), Asaph (Gershom), Ethan (Merari). David organized temple music.
- **Heman descended from Samuel via Korah** — Samuel's Ephraimite residence (Ramah) didn't preclude Levitical descent through his Kohathite line.
- **"Jeduthun" = Ethan** — David's Merarite musician, called both names.
- **Levitical cities** — 48 total (matching Josh 21). Priests (Aaron) get 13, Kohathites 10, Gershomites 13, Merarites 12.
- **Hebron as priestly city + city of refuge** — dual function.
- **Anathoth** (v60) — Jeremiah's hometown; Abiathar's priestly exile-home (1 Kgs 2:26).
