## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew הָאֱלֹהִים, with the article (v48, 49).
- **"And" · lists** (throughout) — the lists have no verb; nothing is supplied but a colon. Each "and" stands where the Hebrew has וְ, and none where it has none (v2 "Amram, Izhar and Hebron and Uzziel" beside v18 "Amram and Izhar and Hebron and Uzziel"). "The sons of" is written only where the Hebrew has בְּנֵי.
- **"children of" → "sons of"** (v3, 64, 65, 77) — Hebrew בְּנֵי; in v3 it stands also over Miriam.
- **"begat" → "begot"** (v4–14) — Hebrew הוֹלִיד. In v11 the form is the narrative וַיּוֹלֶד.
- **"he it is that executed the priest's office in the temple" → "he it is who was priest in the house"** (v10) — Hebrew כִּהֵן "be priest"; בַּיִת "house".
- **"Hilkiah", "Uzziah", "Amaziah", "Berechiah"** (v13, 24, 39, 45) — the original-name forms follow the spelling printed here: חִלְקִיָּה *Chilqiyah*, עֻזִּיָּה *Uzziyah*, אֲמַצְיָה *Amatzyah* (short forms), but בֶּרֶכְיָהוּ *Berechyahu* (long form). In v1 the name is גֵּרְשׁוֹן Gershon, from v16 on גֵּרְשֹׁם / גֵּרְשׁוֹם Gershom.
- **"And Jehozadak went into captivity, when the LORD carried away Judah" → "And Jehozadak went, when Yahweh carried Judah … into exile"** (v15) — Hebrew הָלַךְ, only "went"; where to is not written. הִגְלָה is "carry into exile", as 2 Kgs 24:14.
- **"the families of the Levites according to their fathers" → "the clans of the Levites, by their fathers"** (v19) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּחָה "clan"; הַלֵּוִי is a singular with the article, "the Levite"; so also "the Kohathite" (v33, 54).
- **Verse 26** — the written text (ketiv) has בנו, "Elkanah his son"; the reading tradition (qere) has בְּנֵי, "the sons of Elkanah". The qere is translated: literally "Elkanah, the sons of Elkanah, Zophai his son and Nahath his son".
- **"the firstborn Vashni, and Abiah" → "the firstborn Vashni, and Abijah"** (v28) — Hebrew הַבְּכֹר וַשְׁנִי וַאֲבִיָּה, translated as it is printed and pointed. The word וַשְׁנִי can also be read "and the second" (שֵׁנִי); then the name of the firstborn is not written. Verse 33 calls Heman "the son of Joel, the son of Samuel", and 1 Sam 8:2 names the firstborn Joel and the second Abijah; from there (and from the Septuagint: from memory) "Joel" is commonly put in here; the verse had done so ("the firstborn Joel, and Abijah the second"). The Masoretic Text has no "Joel" in this verse and it is not supplied.
- **"whom David set over the service of song in the house of the LORD, after that the ark had rest" → "whom David made stand over the song in the house of Yahweh, from when the ark had its resting place"** (v31) — Hebrew הֶעֱמִיד "make stand"; עַל־יְדֵי־שִׁיר, literally "upon the hands of song" (no word "service"); בֵּית יְהוָה has no preposition ("in" is supplied); מִמְּנוֹחַ הָאָרוֹן, literally "from the resting place of the ark".
- **"they ministered before the dwelling place of the tabernacle of the congregation with singing … and then they waited on their office according to their order" → "they were ministering before the dwelling of the tent of meeting with song … and they stood, according to their ordinance, over their service"** (v32) — Hebrew מִשְׁכַּן אֹהֶל־מוֹעֵד; וַיַּעַמְדוּ "they stood"; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment", here of a ruling; עֲבוֹדָה "service".
- **"these are they that waited with their children" → "these are those who stood, and their sons"** (v33) — Hebrew הָעֹמְדִים וּבְנֵיהֶם.
- **"Zuph"** (v35) — ketiv ציף "Ziph"; qere צוּף, translated.
- **"their brethren the sons of Merari stood on the left hand" → "the sons of Merari, their brothers, on the left"** (v44) — the Hebrew has no verb.
- **"were appointed unto all manner of service of the tabernacle" → "were given for all the service of the dwelling"** (v48) — Hebrew נְתוּנִים "given" (Num 3:9; 8:16); מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling".
- **"offered upon the altar of the burnt offering, and on the altar of incense, and were appointed for all the work of the place most holy" → "were making smoke on the altar of the burnt offering and on the altar of incense, for all the work of the Holy of Holies"** (v49) — Hebrew מַקְטִירִים, a participle with no object. The verb is "burn" elsewhere in this translation (literally "make smoke"); without an object "were burning" would say that they themselves burned, so the literal words stand. קֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים: "place" is not in the Hebrew. עֶבֶד "servant" (the word is also "slave").
- **"their dwelling places throughout their castles in their coasts" → "their dwelling places, by their encampments in their border"** (v54) — Hebrew מוֹשָׁב; טִירָה "encampment" (Gen 25:16); גְּבוּל "border", also the land within it. לְמִשְׁפַּחַת is singular, "of the clan".
- **"suburbs" → "pasture lands"** (v55–81) — Hebrew מִגְרָשׁ, the open land around a city. The Hebrew says each time "and its pasture lands", as Josh 21. **"the fields" → "the field"** (v56) — שְׂדֵה, singular.
- **Verses 54–81 and Joshua 21** — the wording is that of Joshua 21 where the Hebrew is the same. Only what is printed here is translated; nothing is filled in from Joshua. Chronicles says "the cities of refuge", plural, and without "of the murderer" (v57, 67; Josh 21:13, 21 "the city of refuge of the murderer"). Hebron (v57) and Jattir have no "and its pasture lands". The sub-totals are not written. Names that differ from the finished Joshua 21: Hilez (v58; Holon), Ashan (v59; Ain), Alemeth (v60; Almon), Jokmeam (v68; Kibzaim), Aner and Bileam (v70; Taanach, Gath-rimmon), Ashtaroth (v71; Beeshterah), Kedesh (v72; Kishion), Ramoth and Anem (v73; Jarmuth, En-gannim), Mashal (v74; Mishal), Hukok (v75; Helkath), Hammon and Kiriathaim (v76; Hammoth-dor, Kartan), Rimmono and Tabor (v77; Jokneam, Kartah, Dimnah, Nahalal). Not written here: Juttah (v59), Gibeon (v60), the tribe of Dan with Elteke and Gibbethon (v69), and in v61 Ephraim and Dan. So v60 says "thirteen cities" where eleven are named, and Aijalon and Gath-rimmon (v69) stand under Ephraim.
- **"out of the family of that tribe, were cities given out of the half tribe, namely, out of the half tribe of Manasseh, by lot, ten cities" → "had from the clan of the tribe, from the half of the tribe of the half of Manasseh, by lot, ten cities"** (v61) — Hebrew מִמִּשְׁפַּחַת הַמַּטֶּה מִמַּחֲצִית מַטֵּה חֲצִי מְנַשֶּׁה, word for word; which tribe is meant by "the tribe" is not said. In v61–63 the Hebrew has no verb ("and for the sons of Kohath … ten cities"); "had" renders the "for", as Josh 21:5–7.
- **"these cities, which are called by their names" → "these cities, which they call by names"** (v65) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־יִקְרְאוּ אֶתְהֶם בְּשֵׁמוֹת, an active plural verb and a plural noun.
- **"the residue of the families of the sons of Kohath had cities of their coasts" → "of the clans of the sons of Kohath: the cities of their border were"** (v66) — Hebrew וּמִמִּשְׁפְּחוֹת … וַיְהִי עָרֵי גְבוּלָם, literally "and of the clans of the sons of Kohath, and there were the cities of their border".
- **"on the other side Jordan by Jericho, on the east side of Jordan" → "across the Jordan of Jericho, to the east of the Jordan"** (v78) — Hebrew מֵעֵבֶר לְיַרְדֵּן יְרֵחוֹ, a genitive. **"Jahzah"** — Hebrew יַהְצָה.

### Notes
- **Verse numbers** — our 6:1–15 is 5:27–41 in the Hebrew, and our 6:16–81 is the Hebrew 6:1–66.
- **Priestly line (v1-15)** — Aaron-Eleazar-Phinehas-Zadok etc. 22 generations Aaron → Jehozadak (exile). Some generations missing; standard genealogical compression. Zadok the Davidic priest (v8) is the key pivot.
- **Jehozadak** — father of Joshua the high priest under Zerubbabel (Hag 1:1, Zech 6:11).
- **Three Levitical houses** — Gershom, Kohath, Merari. Three chief singers: Heman (Kohath), Asaph (Gershom), Ethan (Merari). David organized temple music.
- **Heman descended from Samuel via Korah** — Samuel's Ephraimite residence (Ramah) didn't preclude Levitical descent through his Kohathite line.
- **"Jeduthun" = Ethan** — David's Merarite musician, called both names.
- **Levitical cities** — 48 total in Josh 21. Priests (Aaron) get 13, Kohathites 10, Gershomites 13, Merarites 12; fewer are named here.
- **Hebron as priestly city + city of refuge** — dual function.
- **Anathoth** (v60) — Jeremiah's hometown; Abiathar's priestly exile-home (1 Kgs 2:26).
