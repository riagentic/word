## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"courses" → "divisions"** (throughout) — Hebrew מַחֲלֹקֶת, from חָלַק "divide".
- **"the children of Israel after their number, to wit, the chief fathers and captains of thousands and hundreds, and their officers that served the king in any matter of the courses, which came in and went out month by month" → "the sons of Israel, by their number: the heads of the fathers and the commanders of the thousands and the hundreds, and their officers who ministered to the king in every matter of the divisions, the one coming in and the one going out month by month"** (v1) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי הָאָבוֹת "heads of the fathers"; שָׂרֵי "princes, chiefs", of an army "commanders"; שֹׁטְרִים "officers"; הַמְשָׁרְתִים "who ministered to" (שֵׁרֵת, not עָבַד "serve"); הַבָּאָה וְהַיֹּצֵאת, two singular participles, of the division that comes on duty and the one that goes off. **"of every course were twenty and four thousand" → "the one division was twenty-four thousand"** — Hebrew הַמַּחֲלֹקֶת הָאַחַת.
- **"and in his course were twenty and four thousand" → "and in his division were twenty-four thousand"** (v2–15) — Hebrew וְעַל מַחֲלֻקְתּוֹ, literally "and upon his division": the same words twelve times (v4 has them after an added clause, v6 has not the number), and one English wording for all. The Hebrew number is "twenty and four thousand".
- **"the chief of all the captains of the host" → "the head of all the commanders of the hosts"** (v3) — Hebrew הָרֹאשׁ לְכָל־שָׂרֵי הַצְּבָאוֹת, "hosts" in the plural. "He was" is supplied.
- **"and of his course was Mikloth also the ruler" → "and his division, and Mikloth the leader"** (v4) — Hebrew וּמַחֲלֻקְתּוֹ וּמִקְלוֹת הַנָּגִיד, word for word; the clause is broken and its sense is not clear (perhaps: "and over his division was Mikloth the leader"). נָגִיד is "leader". Dodai: 11:12 has "Eleazar the son of Dodo the Ahohite"; the Masoretic Text is kept here as it stands.
- **"the son of Jehoiada, a chief priest" → "the son of Jehoiada the priest, as head"** (v5) — Hebrew בֶּן־יְהוֹיָדָע הַכֹּהֵן רֹאשׁ; רֹאשׁ "head" has no article and may go with Benaiah ("as head") or with "the priest" ("the head priest").
- **"mighty among the thirty, and above the thirty" → "a mighty one of the thirty and over the thirty"** (v6) — Hebrew גִּבּוֹר הַשְּׁלֹשִׁים וְעַל־הַשְּׁלֹשִׁים. **"and in his course was Ammizabad his son" → "and his division: Ammizabad his son"** — Hebrew וּמַחֲלֻקְתּוֹ עַמִּיזָבָד בְּנוֹ, without a preposition or a verb.
- **"the fifth captain … was Shamhuth the Izrahite" → "the commander Shamhuth the Izrahite"** (v8) — Hebrew הַשַּׂר שַׁמְהוּת הַיִּזְרָח: "the commander" stands before the name; the last word is printed "the Izrah", an unusual form (v11, 13 have "the Zerahite").
- **"of the Zarhites" → "of the Zerahites"** (v11, 13) — Hebrew לַזַּרְחִי, "belonging to the Zerahite". **"of the Benjamites"** (v12) — the reading tradition (qere) has two words, לַבֵּן יְמִינִי; the written text (ketiv) one, לבנימיני. No difference of meaning.
- **"Benaiah"** (v14) — here the Hebrew prints the short form בְּנָיָה *Benayah*; v5, 6, 34 have *Benayahu*.
- **"of Othniel" kept** (v15) — Hebrew לְעָתְנִיאֵל, "belonging to Othniel": of his line.
- **"the ruler of the Reubenites" → "of the Reubenites the leader was"** (v16) — Hebrew לָראוּבֵנִי נָגִיד; נָגִיד "leader" is written once and holds for the whole list. The Hebrew has the clan names in the singular ("the Reubenite", "the Simeonite").
- **"of the Levites" → "of Levi"** (v17) — Hebrew לְלֵוִי, the name of the tribe. **"of the Aaronites" → "of Aaron"** — Hebrew לְאַהֲרֹן; the name, not a clan name.
- **"one of the brethren of David" → "of the brothers of David"** (v18) — Hebrew מֵאֲחֵי דָוִיד. Elihu is not among David's brothers named in 2:13–15; the Septuagint is said to read "Eliab" (from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"Jerimoth"** (v19) — Hebrew יְרִימוֹת *Yerimot*. Other long forms as printed: *Shefatyahu* (v16), *Yishmayahu* (v19), *Pedayahu* (v20), *Zecharyahu* (v21).
- **"the half tribe of Manasseh" → "the half of the tribe of Manasseh"** (v20) — Hebrew לַחֲצִי שֵׁבֶט מְנַשֶּׁה. **"of the half tribe of Manasseh in Gilead" → "of the half of Manasseh in Gilead"** (v21) — Hebrew לַחֲצִי הַמְנַשֶּׁה גִּלְעָדָה, "the half of the Manasseh, toward Gilead": no word "tribe", and Gilead with the ending of direction.
- **"These were the princes of the tribes of Israel" kept** (v22) — Hebrew שָׂרֵי, the word rendered "commanders" in v1, 3, 5, 34 and "chiefs" in v31. Gad and Asher are not in the list.
- **"But David took not the number of them from twenty years old and under" → "And David did not lift up their number, from twenty years old and downward"** (v23) — Hebrew נָשָׂא מִסְפָּר, "lift up the number"; לְמִבֶּן עֶשְׂרִים שָׁנָה וּלְמָטָּה, literally "from a son of twenty years and downward". **"because the LORD had said he would increase Israel" → "for Yahweh had said that he would multiply Israel"** — Hebrew אָמַר … לְהַרְבּוֹת, literally "had said to multiply".
- **"began to number, but he finished not, because there fell wrath for it against Israel; neither was the number put in the account of the chronicles of king David" → "began to count, and he did not finish; and there was wrath upon Israel for this, and the number did not go up into the number of the chronicles of King David"** (v24) — Hebrew מָנָה "count"; וַיְהִי בָזֹאת קֶצֶף "and there was wrath for this"; וְלֹא עָלָה הַמִּסְפָּר בְּמִסְפַּר, "the number did not go up in the number": the word מִסְפָּר twice. The Septuagint is said to read "in the book" (סֵפֶר) for the second (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept. "Chronicles" is literally "the matters of the days".
- **"storehouses" / "cellars" → "treasuries"** (v25, 27, 28) — Hebrew אוֹצָרוֹת each time, the word of "the treasuries of the king": stores of any kind. **"in the fields" → "in the field"** (v25) — Hebrew בַּשָּׂדֶה: the open country. **"castles" → "towers"** — Hebrew מִגְדָּלוֹת. **"villages"** — Hebrew כְּפָרִים (חָצֵר is also "village").
- **"over them that did the work of the field for tillage of the ground" → "over those who did the work of the field, for the service of the ground"** (v26) — Hebrew מְלֶאכֶת "work" and עֲבֹדַת "service", side by side as in 26:30; of the ground, the "service" is its tilling.
- **"over the increase of the vineyards for the wine cellars" → "over what was in the vineyards for the treasuries of wine"** (v27) — Hebrew וְעַל שֶׁבַּכְּרָמִים, "and over what is in the vineyards"; no word "increase".
- **"in the low plains" → "in the Shephelah"** (v28) — Hebrew בַּשְּׁפֵלָה, the lowland between the hill country and the coast.
- **"over the herds that fed in Sharon was Shitrai" → "over the herd that pastured in the Sharon was Shirtai"** (v29) — Hebrew הַבָּקָר, a collective singular; בַּשָּׁרוֹן with the article. The name: the written text (ketiv) has "Shitrai", the reading tradition (qere) "Shirtai"; the qere is translated.
- **"over the asses" → "over the she-donkeys"** (v30) — Hebrew אֲתֹנוֹת, the female animals.
- **"Hagerite" → "Hagrite"** (v31). **"rulers of the substance" → "chiefs of the goods"** — Hebrew שָׂרֵי הָרְכוּשׁ; רְכוּשׁ is "goods".
- **"David's uncle" kept** (v32) — Hebrew דּוֹד, "uncle"; the word is also used more widely of a kinsman ("beloved"). **"a wise man" → "a man who has understanding"** — Hebrew אִישׁ־מֵבִין, a participle of בִּין "understand", as 25:7, 8.
- **"the king's companion" → "the friend of the king"** (v33) — Hebrew רֵעַ הַמֶּלֶךְ; a title at court (1 Kgs 4:5).
- **"the general of the king's army" → "the commander of the host of the king"** (v34) — Hebrew שַׂר־צָבָא לַמֶּלֶךְ, "commander of host to the king".

### Notes
- **Military rotation** — 24,000 × 12 = 288,000-man standing army, rotating monthly. Each soldier served one month/year.
- **Tribal leaders** (v16-22) — civil heads of each tribe. Lists Aaron separately from Levi; omits Gad and Asher (possibly textual loss).
- **Census reference** (v23-24) — links back to ch 21. Explicitly notes that the count was incomplete and unofficial (wrath).
- **Royal estates management** (v25-31) — 12 stewards over David's properties: treasuries, stores, agriculture (vineyards, olives), livestock (herds, camels, donkeys, sheep). Royal economy.
- **Stewards' names** — some (Obil the Ishmaelite, Jaziz the Hagrite) are non-Israelites; specialists in camel/sheep management. David employs foreigners in economic roles.
- **Court cabinet** (v32-34) — counselors, scribes, tutors, royal friends. Ahithophel's name present without reference to his betrayal (2 Sam 15-17); Chronicler omits Absalom rebellion.
- **"King's friend"** — official title; Hushai held it (2 Sam 15:37).
