# 1 Chronicles 27

[1] The children of Israel, according to their number — the heads of fathers' houses, the commanders of thousands and hundreds, and their officers who served the king in every matter of the divisions that came in and went out month by month throughout all the months of the year — each division was twenty-four thousand.

[2] Over the first division for the first month was Jashobeam son of Zabdiel; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

[3] He was of the children of Perez, chief of all the commanders of the army for the first month.

[4] Over the division for the second month was Dodai the Ahohite, with Mikloth as the deputy ruler of his division; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

[5] The third commander of the army, for the third month, was Benaiah son of Jehoiada the chief priest; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

[6] This is the Benaiah who was mighty among the Thirty and above the Thirty; his son Ammizabad was over his division.

[7] The fourth, for the fourth month, was Asahel brother of Joab, and Zebadiah his son after him; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

[8] The fifth, for the fifth month, was Shamhuth the Izrahite; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

[9] The sixth, for the sixth month, was Ira son of Ikkesh the Tekoite; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

[10] The seventh, for the seventh month, was Helez the Pelonite, of the children of Ephraim; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

[11] The eighth, for the eighth month, was Sibbecai the Hushathite, of the Zerahites; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

[12] The ninth, for the ninth month, was Abiezer the Anathothite, of the Benjaminites; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

[13] The tenth, for the tenth month, was Maharai the Netophathite, of the Zerahites; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

[14] The eleventh, for the eleventh month, was Benaiah the Pirathonite, of the children of Ephraim; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

[15] The twelfth, for the twelfth month, was Heldai the Netophathite, of Othniel's line; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

[16] Over the tribes of Israel: the chief of the Reubenites was Eliezer son of Zichri; of the Simeonites, Shephatiah son of Maacah;

[17] of the Levites, Hashabiah son of Kemuel; of the Aaronides, Zadok;

[18] of Judah, Elihu, one of David's brothers; of Issachar, Omri son of Michael;

[19] of Zebulun, Ishmaiah son of Obadiah; of Naphtali, Jeremoth son of Azriel;

[20] of the children of Ephraim, Hoshea son of Azaziah; of the half-tribe of Manasseh, Joel son of Pedaiah;

[21] of the half-tribe of Manasseh in Gilead, Iddo son of Zechariah; of Benjamin, Jaasiel son of Abner;

[22] of Dan, Azarel son of Jeroham. These were the chiefs of the tribes of Israel.

[23] David did not take the number of those twenty years old and under, because Yahweh had said he would multiply Israel like the stars of the heavens.

[24] Joab son of Zeruiah began the count but did not finish, because wrath came upon Israel because of it; the number was not entered in the Book of the Chronicles of King David.

[25] Over the king's treasuries was Azmaveth son of Adiel; over the storehouses in the fields, in the cities, in the villages, and in the fortified towers, was Jonathan son of Uzziah;

[26] over the field-workers who tilled the ground was Ezri son of Chelub;

[27] over the vineyards was Shimei the Ramathite; over the stores of wine in the vineyards was Zabdi the Shiphmite;

[28] over the olive trees and sycamore trees in the Shephelah was Baal-hanan the Gederite; over the oil stores was Joash;

[29] over the herds pasturing in Sharon was Shitrai the Sharonite; over the herds in the valleys was Shaphat son of Adlai;

[30] over the camels was Obil the Ishmaelite; over the donkeys was Jehdeiah the Meronothite;

[31] over the flocks was Jaziz the Hagrite. All these were officers over the property that belonged to King David.

[32] Jonathan, David's uncle, was a counselor, a man of understanding, and a scribe. Jehiel son of Hachmoni was with the king's sons.

[33] Ahithophel was the king's counselor; Hushai the Archite was the king's friend.

[34] After Ahithophel came Jehoiada son of Benaiah, and Abiathar. The commander of the king's army was Joab.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"courses" → "divisions"** (throughout).
- **"castles" → "fortified towers"** (v25) — *migdalot*.
- **"in the low plains" → "in the Shephelah"** (v28).
- **"Aaronites" → "Aaronides"** (v17).
- **"companion" → "friend"** (v33) — *re'eh ha-melek*; royal title for close confidant.
- **"Hagerite" → "Hagrite"** (v31).

### Notes
- **Military rotation** — 24,000 × 12 = 288,000-man standing army, rotating monthly. Each soldier served one month/year.
- **Tribal chiefs** (v16-22) — civil heads of each tribe. Lists Aaron separately from Levi; omits Gad and Asher (possibly textual loss).
- **Census reference** (v23-24) — links back to ch 21. Explicitly notes that the count was incomplete and unofficial (wrath).
- **Royal estates management** (v25-31) — 12 stewards over David's properties: treasuries, storehouses, agriculture (vineyards, olives), livestock (herds, camels, donkeys, sheep). Royal economy.
- **Tribal officials names** — some (Obil the Ishmaelite, Jaziz the Hagrite) are non-Israelites; specialists in camel/sheep management. David employs foreigners in economic roles.
- **Court cabinet** (v32-34) — counselors, scribes, tutors, royal friends. Ahithophel's name present without reference to his betrayal (2 Sam 15-17); Chronicler omits Absalom rebellion.
- **"King's friend"** — official title; Hushai held it (2 Sam 15:37).
