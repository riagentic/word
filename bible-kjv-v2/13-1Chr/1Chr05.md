# 1 Chronicles 5

[1] The sons of Reuben the firstborn of Israel. (He was the firstborn, but because he defiled his father's bed, his birthright was given to the sons of Joseph son of Israel; so he is not enrolled in the genealogy according to the birthright.

[2] For Judah prevailed over his brothers, and from him came the ruler; but the birthright was Joseph's.)

[3] The sons of Reuben the firstborn of Israel: Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi.

[4] The sons of Joel: Shemaiah his son, Gog his son, Shimei his son,

[5] Micah his son, Reaiah his son, Baal his son,

[6] Beerah his son, whom Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria carried away captive; he was leader of the Reubenites.

[7] His brothers, by their families, when enrolled in the genealogy of their generations, were: the chief Jeiel, and Zechariah,

[8] and Bela son of Azaz son of Shema son of Joel, who lived in Aroer, as far as Nebo and Baal-meon.

[9] To the east he lived as far as the entrance of the wilderness from the Euphrates River, because their livestock had multiplied in the land of Gilead.

[10] In the days of Saul they made war with the Hagrites, who fell into their hand; they lived in their tents throughout all the region east of Gilead.

[11] The children of Gad lived opposite them, in the land of Bashan as far as Salecah:

[12] Joel the chief, Shapham the second, then Janai, and Shaphat in Bashan.

[13] Their brothers by their fathers' houses: Michael, Meshullam, Sheba, Jorai, Jacan, Zia, and Eber — seven.

[14] These were the sons of Abihail son of Huri son of Jaroah son of Gilead son of Michael son of Jeshishai son of Jahdo son of Buz.

[15] Ahi son of Abdiel son of Guni was the head of their fathers' house.

[16] They lived in Gilead, in Bashan and its towns, and in all the pasture lands of Sharon to their limits.

[17] All of these were enrolled in the genealogies in the days of Jotham king of Judah and in the days of Jeroboam king of Israel.

[18] The sons of Reuben, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh — valiant men able to bear shield and sword, to draw the bow, and skilled in war — were forty-four thousand seven hundred sixty who went out to war.

[19] They made war with the Hagrites, Jetur, Naphish, and Nodab.

[20] They were helped against them, and the Hagrites were given into their hand, with all who were with them; for they cried out to *Elohim* in the battle, and he granted their entreaty, because they put their trust in him.

[21] They carried away their livestock: fifty thousand of their camels, two hundred fifty thousand sheep, two thousand donkeys, and one hundred thousand captives.

[22] For many fell slain, because the war was of *Elohim*. They lived in their place until the exile.

[23] The children of the half-tribe of Manasseh lived in the land; they multiplied from Bashan to Baal-hermon, Senir, and Mount Hermon.

[24] These were the heads of their fathers' houses: Epher, Ishi, Eliel, Azriel, Jeremiah, Hodaviah, and Jahdiel — valiant warriors, famous men, heads of their fathers' houses.

[25] But they acted unfaithfully against the *Elohim* of their fathers, and played the harlot after the gods of the peoples of the land, whom *Elohim* had destroyed before them.

[26] The *Elohim* of Israel stirred up the spirit of Pul king of Assyria, the spirit of Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria; he took them into exile — the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh — and brought them to Halah, Habor, Hara, and the river Gozan, where they remain to this day.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"chief ruler" → "ruler"** (v2) — *nagid*.
- **"Tilgath-pilneser" → "Tiglath-pileser"** (v6, 26) — standard form.
- **"Hagarites" → "Hagrites"** (v10, 19, 20) — Hebrew *Hagri'im*; Arabian tribe descended (per tradition) from Hagar.
- **"suburbs" → "pasture lands"** (v16) — *migrashim*.
- **"went a whoring" → "played the harlot"** (v25) — *zanah*; metaphor for covenant-breaking idolatry.
- **"their steads" → "their place"** (v22) — *tachtehem*.
- **"of God" → "of *Elohim*"** (v22).

### Notes
- **Reuben's demotion** — Gen 49:3-4, 35:22 rationale: defiled Bilhah. Birthright (double-portion) to Joseph (Ephraim+Manasseh = double tribe). Judah gets leadership (royal line).
- **Pul = Tiglath-pileser III** (v26) — single king, two names. Pul = Babylonian throne name. Chronicler conflates stirring of both "spirits" into one event.
- **Transjordanian exile** (734-732 BC) — Tiglath-pileser's Galilee-Gilead campaign; first wave of northern exile.
- **Reubenite-Gadite-half-Manasseh coalition** (v18-22) — Transjordanian tribes; pastoralists. War with Arabian tribes. Chronicler theologizes: "war was of *Elohim*."
- **Jeroboam II dating** (v17) — mid-8th century BC; contemporary with Jotham of Judah.
- **Ishmaelite descendants** (v19) — Jetur, Naphish = Ishmael's sons (Gen 25:15); Chronicler's earlier lists converge here.
- **Exile sites** — Halah, Habor, Gozan, Hara (Hara only here; elsewhere "cities of Medes"). Assyrian administrative provinces.
