[1] And David took counsel with the commanders of the thousands and the hundreds, with every leader.

[2] And David said to all the assembly of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, "If it is good to you, and from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, let us break out, let us send to our brothers who are left in all the lands of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and with them the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ in the cities of their pasture lands, and let them be gathered to us.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v5–8, 12, 14 with the article (הָאֱלֹהִים), in v10 without.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Parallel: 2 Sam 6:1–11 (v5–14)** — where the Hebrew is the same, the wording is that of the finished Samuel verse; every difference named below is a difference in the Hebrew of Chronicles. Nothing is taken over from Samuel. v1–4 are only in Chronicles.
- **"consulted with the captains of thousands and hundreds, and with every leader" → "took counsel with the commanders of the thousands and the hundreds, with every leader"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּוָּעַץ; שָׂרֵי "commanders"; לְכָל־נָגִיד, literally "as to every leader", without "and".
- **"If it seem good unto you, and that it be of the LORD our God, let us send abroad" → "If it is good to you, and from Yahweh our Elohim, let us break out, let us send"** (v2) — Hebrew אִם־עֲלֵיכֶם טוֹב, literally "if upon you it is good"; נִפְרְצָה נִשְׁלְחָה, two verbs side by side: פָּרַץ "break out, break through" (the verb of v11, "Yahweh had broken out"), commonly taken here as "send out in every direction". The word is kept so that v2 and v11 show the same verb. **"in all the land of Israel" → "in all the lands of Israel"** — Hebrew אַרְצוֹת, plural. **"in their cities and suburbs" → "in the cities of their pasture lands"** — Hebrew בְּעָרֵי מִגְרְשֵׁיהֶם. **"that they may gather themselves unto us" → "and let them be gathered to us"** — Hebrew וְיִקָּבְצוּ.
- **"let us bring again the ark" → "let us bring around the ark"** (v3) — Hebrew וְנָסֵבָּה, the hifil of "go around" (as 1 Sam 5:8); "back" is not written. **"we enquired not at it" → "we did not seek it"** — Hebrew לֹא דְרַשְׁנֻהוּ; the suffix may be "it" (the ark) or "him".
- **"said that they would do so" → "said to do so"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמְרוּ … לַעֲשׂוֹת כֵּן; "agreed" (the earlier text) is not the word.
- **"So David gathered all Israel together" → "And David assembled all Israel"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיַּקְהֵל, the verb of קָהָל "assembly" (v2, 4). Samuel has "David again gathered every chosen man in Israel, thirty thousand". **"unto the entering of Hemath" → "as far as Lebo-hamath"** — Hebrew לְבוֹא חֲמָת, "the coming to Hamath", kept as a name.
- **"to Baalah, that is, to Kirjath-jearim, which belonged to Judah" → "to Baalah, to Kiriath-jearim, which belongs to Judah"** (v6) — Hebrew בַּעֲלָתָה אֶל־קִרְיַת יְעָרִים אֲשֶׁר לִיהוּדָה; "that is" is not written. Samuel has "from Baale-judah".
- **"the ark of God the LORD, that dwelleth between the cherubims, whose name is called on it" → "the ark of Elohim, Yahweh, who sits upon the cherubim, where a name is called"** (v6) — Hebrew אֲרוֹן הָאֱלֹהִים יְהוָה יוֹשֵׁב הַכְּרוּבִים אֲשֶׁר־נִקְרָא שֵׁם: "sits", with the cherubim as the seat; "enthroned" (the earlier text) is an interpretation. The last three words are literally "which a name is called": the pointing is שֵׁם "name" (not שָׁם "there"), and the clause has no "upon it"; how it joins to what stands before is not certain ("where the name is called on", "which is called by the name"). Samuel has the fuller wording: "upon which a name is called, the name of Yahweh of Hosts, who sits upon the cherubim". "Who is called by the Name" (the earlier text) made the name a title of Yahweh.
- **"they carried the ark of God in a new cart" → "they made the ark of Elohim ride on a new wagon"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיַּרְכִּיבוּ … עַל־עֲגָלָה (Samuel: אֶל). **"drave the cart" → "were driving the wagon"** — Hebrew נֹהֲגִים בָּעֲגָלָה, a participle. Chronicles does not have "which was on the hill", "the sons of Abinadab" or Samuel's v4.
- **"played before God with all their might, and with singing" → "were playing before Elohim with all strength, and with songs"** (v8) — Hebrew מְשַׂחֲקִים "playing, making merry"; בְּכָל־עֹז וּבְשִׁירִים, no "their". Samuel has "before Yahweh with all woods of cypresses": the two differ by a few letters (בכל עז ובשירים / בכל עצי ברושים). **"psalteries" → "harps"; "timbrels" kept** — Hebrew נְבָלִים; תֻּפִּים (hand drums; the earlier text had "tambourines"). **"cymbals … trumpets"** — Hebrew מְצִלְתַּיִם (another form than Samuel's צֶלְצֶלִים) and חֲצֹצְרוֹת, the straight metal trumpets (Num 10:2), not the shofar; Samuel has "rattles" and "cymbals" and no trumpets.
- **"the threshingfloor of Chidon"** (v9) — Hebrew כִּידֹן; Samuel has "Nacon". **"put forth his hand to hold the ark" → "stretched out his hand to take hold of the ark"** — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁלַח … אֶת־יָדוֹ לֶאֱחֹז. **"the oxen stumbled" → "the cattle let go"** — Hebrew שָׁמְטוּ הַבָּקָר: the verb is "let drop, let loose", without an object; what the cattle did is not certain ("stumbled", "shook it", "let it slip").
- **"the anger of the LORD was kindled" → "the anger of Yahweh burned"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר־אַף; אַף is "nose", of anger. **"he smote him, because he put his hand to the ark" → "he struck him down because he had stretched out his hand upon the ark"** — Hebrew עַל אֲשֶׁר־שָׁלַח יָדוֹ עַל־הָאָרוֹן; Samuel has the unclear "for the error" (עַל־הַשַּׁל) in this place. **"before God"** — Hebrew לִפְנֵי אֱלֹהִים (Samuel: "by the ark of Elohim").
- **"David was displeased, because the LORD had made a breach upon Uzza" → "David burned with anger because Yahweh had broken out with a breach against Uzza"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר לְדָוִיד, literally "it burned to David"; פָרַץ … פֶּרֶץ, verb and noun of one root; the name Perez-uzza is "the breach of Uzza". **"that place is called" → "he called that place"** — Hebrew וַיִּקְרָא, active.
- **"David was afraid of God" → "David feared Elohim"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיִּירָא … אֶת־הָאֱלֹהִים (Samuel: "Yahweh"). **"How shall I bring the ark of God home to me?" → "How shall I bring the ark of Elohim to me?"** — Hebrew הֵיךְ אָבִיא אֵלַי; "home" is not written. Samuel has "How shall the ark of Yahweh come to me?"
- **"David brought not the ark home to himself" → "David did not remove the ark to himself"** (v13) — Hebrew וְלֹא־הֵסִיר (Samuel: "was not willing to remove"). **"carried it aside" → "turned it aside"** — Hebrew וַיַּטֵּהוּ.
- **"the ark of God remained with the family of Obed-edom in his house" → "the ark of Elohim dwelt with the house of Obed-edom, in his house"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיֵּשֶׁב … עִם־בֵּית עֹבֵד אֱדֹם בְּבֵיתוֹ: יָשַׁב "dwell"; בַּיִת "house" twice; "his house" may be Obed-edom's or the ark's own. Samuel has "in the house of Obed-edom the Gittite". **"all that he had" → "all that was his"** — Hebrew כָּל־אֲשֶׁר־לוֹ (Samuel: "Obed-edom and all his house").

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Sam 6** — Chronicler adds the consultation with the commanders (v1-4), showing ark-transfer as national decision.
- **"We did not seek it in the days of Saul"** — Chronicler's interpretation: ark neglected during Saul's reign. Explains David's urgency.
- **Shihor of Egypt to Lebo-hamath** — full Solomonic boundaries; maximal all-Israel involvement.
- **Kiriath-jearim** — where the ark rested since its return from Philistia (1 Sam 7); no prior king had moved it to the capital.
- **New wagon** — the method was wrong (Torah required Levite shoulder-carry, Num 4). Poles-and-Levite method observed in ch 15. Uzza's death exposes procedural error.
- **Obed-edom the Gittite** — probably a Levite from Gath-rimmon (a Levitical city), not a Philistine from Gath. Blessed by ark's presence.
- **Three-month pause** — blessing of Obed-edom signals that the ark is not dangerous per se; improper handling is.
