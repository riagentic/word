# 1 Chronicles 13

[1] David consulted with the commanders of thousands and hundreds, with every leader.

[2] David said to all the assembly of Israel, "If it seems good to you, and if it is from Yahweh our *Elohim*, let us send word to our brothers who are left throughout all the land of Israel, and also to the priests and Levites in their cities with their pasture lands, that they may gather to us.

[3] Let us bring back the ark of our *Elohim* to us; for we did not seek it in the days of Saul."

[4] All the assembly agreed to do so, for the thing was right in the eyes of all the people.

[5] So David assembled all Israel, from the Shihor of Egypt to Lebo-hamath, to bring the ark of *Elohim* from Kiriath-jearim.

[6] David went up with all Israel to Baalah — that is, Kiriath-jearim — which belongs to Judah, to bring up from there the ark of *Elohim*, Yahweh, who sits enthroned above the cherubim, who is called by the Name.

[7] They carried the ark of *Elohim* on a new cart, out of the house of Abinadab; Uzza and Ahio drove the cart.

[8] David and all Israel were celebrating before *Elohim* with all their might, with songs, lyres, harps, tambourines, cymbals, and shofarot.

[9] When they came to the threshing floor of Chidon, Uzza put out his hand to hold the ark, for the oxen stumbled.

[10] The anger of Yahweh was kindled against Uzza, and he struck him down because he put his hand to the ark; there he died before *Elohim*.

[11] David was distressed because Yahweh had burst out against Uzza; and that place is called Perez-uzza to this day.

[12] David was afraid of *Elohim* that day, saying, "How can I bring the ark of *Elohim* home to me?"

[13] So David did not take the ark with him to the city of David; instead he took it aside to the house of Obed-edom the Gittite.

[14] The ark of *Elohim* remained with the family of Obed-edom in his house three months; Yahweh blessed the house of Obed-edom and all that he had.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Hemath" → "Lebo-hamath"** (v5) — northern boundary.
- **"between the cherubims" → "enthroned above the cherubim"** (v6) — *yoshev ha-keruvim*.
- **"whose name is called on it" → "who is called by the Name"** (v6) — *asher niqra shem*.
- **"psalteries" → "harps"** (v8) — *nevalim*.
- **"timbrels" → "tambourines"** (v8) — *tuppim*.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Sam 6** — Chronicler adds the consultation with officers (v1-4), showing ark-transfer as national decision.
- **"We did not seek it in the days of Saul"** — Chronicler's interpretation: ark neglected during Saul's reign. Explains David's urgency.
- **Shihor of Egypt to Lebo-hamath** — full Solomonic boundaries; maximal all-Israel involvement.
- **Kiriath-jearim** — where the ark rested since its return from Philistia (1 Sam 7); no prior king had moved it to the capital.
- **New cart** — the method was wrong (Torah required Levite shoulder-carry, Num 4). Poles-and-Levite method observed in ch 15. Uzza's death exposes procedural error.
- **Obed-edom the Gittite** — probably a Levite from Gath-rimmon (a Levitical city), not a Philistine from Gath. Blessed by ark's presence.
- **Three-month pause** — blessing of Obed-edom signals that the ark is not dangerous per se; improper handling is.
