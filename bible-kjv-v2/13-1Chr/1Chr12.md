## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Antothite" → "Anathothite"** (v3).
- **"roes" → "gazelles"** (v8) — *tzevayim*.
- **"upon advisement" → "after consultation"** (v19).
- **"rovers" → "raiding band"** (v21) — *gedud*.
- **"the Aaronites" → "Aharon"** (v27) — Hebrew *le-Aharon*, "of Aaron"; MT has the name, not a gentilic.
- **"perfect heart" → "whole heart"** (v38) — *levav shalem*.
- **"meat, meal" → "flour"** (v40) — Hebrew *qemach*; "meat" in KJV = archaic for food-staple.

### Notes
- **Chronicler's emphasis** — progressive all-Israel support for David, from fugitive days (Ziklag) through stronghold to Hebron.
- **Benjaminite support** — unusual: Saul's own tribe defects to David. Chronicler signals theological legitimation.
- **Gadites' heroism** — crossing flooded Jordan in first month = high-water season. Feats of reconnaissance and valor.
- **Amasai's oracle** (v18) — poetic; "your *Elohim* helps you." Spirit-inspired loyalty pledge.
- **Issachar "understood the times"** (v32) — classic phrase; contextual political wisdom. 200 tribal heads as counselors.
- **"Not of double heart"** (v33) — Hebrew *be-lo-lev-va-lev*; unwavering commitment.
- **Total count** — ~340,000 men. Symbolic magnitude, showing comprehensive all-Israel backing.
- **Post-coronation feast** — three-day celebration. Unity theme.
