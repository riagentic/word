# 1 Chronicles 28

[1] And David assembled all the princes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, the princes of the tribes and the commanders of the divisions that ministered to the king, and the commanders of the thousands and the commanders of the hundreds, and the chiefs of all the goods and livestock of the king and of his sons, with the eunuchs and the mighty ones and every mighty one of ability, to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[2] And David the king rose upon his feet and said, "Hear me, my brothers and my people. As for me, it was with my heart to build a house of rest for the ark of the covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and for the footstool of the feet of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and I prepared to build.

[3] But ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said to me, 'You shall not build a house for my name, for you are a man of wars, and blood you have shed.'

[4] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ chose me out of all the house of my father to be king over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ forever; for ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ he chose as leader, and in the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ the house of my father, and among the sons of my father he was pleased with me, to make me king over all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[5] And out of all my sons, for many sons has ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ given me, he chose ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ my son to sit on the throne of the kingdom of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[6] And he said to me, '⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ your son, he shall build my house and my courts, for I have chosen him for a son to me, and I will be to him for a father.

[7] And I will establish his kingdom forever, if he is strong to do my commandments and my ordinances, as at this day.'

[8] And now, before the eyes of all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, the assembly of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and in the ears of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: keep and seek all the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, so that you may possess the good land and make your sons after you inherit it forever.

[9] And you, ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ my son, know the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of your father and serve him with a whole heart and with a delighting soul, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ seeks all hearts and understands every inclination of the thoughts. If you seek him, he will be found by you; and if you forsake him, he will cast you off forever.

[10] See now, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has chosen you to build a house for the sanctuary; be strong and do."

[11] And David gave to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ his son the pattern of the porch, and of its houses and its storerooms and its upper rooms and its inner chambers, and of the house of the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧,

[12] and the pattern of all that was in the spirit with him, for the courts of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and for all the chambers around, for the treasuries of the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and for the treasuries of the holy things,

[13] and for the divisions of the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, and for all the work of the service of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and for all the utensils of the service of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;

[14] for the gold, by the weight for the gold, for all the utensils of each service; for all the utensils of silver, by weight, for all the utensils of each service;

[15] and the weight for the lampstands of gold and their lamps of gold, by the weight of each lampstand and its lamps; and for the lampstands of silver, by weight for a lampstand and its lamps, according to the service of each lampstand;

[16] and the gold by weight for the tables of the arrangement, for each table; and silver for the tables of silver;

[17] and the forks and the bowls and the jars, of pure gold; and for the basins of gold, by weight for each basin; and for the basins of silver, by weight for each basin;

[18] and for the altar of incense, purified gold by weight; and for the pattern of the chariot, the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧, gold, for those spreading out and screening over the ark of the covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[19] "All of it in writing from the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ upon me; he gave insight into all the works of the pattern."

[20] And David said to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ his son, "Be strong and be firm, and do; do not fear and do not be dismayed, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, is with you. He will not let you drop and will not forsake you, until all the work of the service of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is finished.

[21] And behold, the divisions of the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, for all the service of the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and with you in all the work is every willing one with wisdom, for every service; and the princes and all the people, for all your words."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v3, 12, 21 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים. In v20 the Hebrew has יְהוָה אֱלֹהִים אֱלֹהַי, "Yahweh Elohim, my Elohim".
- **"princes" / "captains" / "stewards" → "princes" / "commanders" / "chiefs"** (v1, 21) — one Hebrew word, שָׂרֵי, six times in v1: "princes" of Israel and of the tribes, "commanders" of the army units (divisions, thousands, hundreds), "chiefs" over the goods. **"the companies that ministered to the king by course" → "the divisions that ministered to the king"** — Hebrew הַמַּחְלְקוֹת הַמְשָׁרְתִים אֶת־הַמֶּלֶךְ (ch 27). **"substance and possession" → "goods and livestock"** — Hebrew רְכוּשׁ וּמִקְנֶה.
- **"officers" → "eunuchs"** (v1) — Hebrew סָרִיסִים; the word is "eunuch" and is also used of a court officer whether or not he is castrated. **"the mighty men, and with all the valiant men" → "the mighty ones and every mighty one of ability"** — Hebrew הַגִּבּוֹרִים וּלְכָל־גִּבּוֹר חָיִל; the last has לְ before it, not rendered. **"unto Jerusalem"** stands last in the Hebrew.
- **"stood up upon his feet" → "rose upon his feet"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיָּקָם … עַל־רַגְלָיו. **"As for me, I had in mine heart" → "As for me, it was with my heart"** — Hebrew אֲנִי עִם־לְבָבִי, "I — with my heart", the words of 1 Kgs 8:17. **"the footstool of our God" → "the footstool of the feet of our *Elohim*"** — Hebrew הֲדֹם רַגְלֵי. **"had made ready for the building" → "I prepared to build"** — Hebrew וַהֲכִינוֹתִי לִבְנוֹת; הֵכִין is the verb rendered "establish" in v7 (of a kingdom) and 29:18 (of the heart): one root, "make firm, make ready".
- **"because thou hast been a man of war, and hast shed blood" → "for you are a man of wars, and blood you have shed"** (v3) — Hebrew אִישׁ מִלְחָמוֹת, plural; דָּמִים "bloods", plural, the object before the verb.
- **"for he hath chosen Judah to be the ruler" → "for Judah he chose as leader"** (v4) — Hebrew לְנָגִיד, "leader". **"he liked me to make me king" → "he was pleased with me, to make me king"** — Hebrew בִּי רָצָה לְהַמְלִיךְ.
- **"the throne of the kingdom of the LORD" kept** (v5) — Hebrew כִּסֵּא מַלְכוּת יְהוָה; מַלְכוּת is "kingdom", of the time or rule of one king "reign" (26:31; 29:30).
- **"I have chosen him to be my son, and I will be his father" → "I have chosen him for a son to me, and I will be to him for a father"** (v6) — Hebrew לִי לְבֵן … לוֹ לְאָב, as 2 Sam 7:14.
- **"if he be constant to do" → "if he is strong to do"** (v7) — Hebrew אִם־יֶחֱזַק, the verb of "be strong" in v10, 20. **"judgments" → "ordinances"** — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּטַי, "judgments", of rulings "ordinances". **"for ever"** — Hebrew עַד־לְעוֹלָם.
- **"in the sight of all Israel … and in the audience of our God" → "before the eyes of all Israel … and in the ears of our *Elohim*"** (v8) — Hebrew לְעֵינֵי … וּבְאָזְנֵי. **"the congregation of the LORD" → "the assembly of Yahweh"** — Hebrew קְהַל. **"that ye may possess this good land, and leave it for an inheritance for your children" → "so that you may possess the good land and make your sons after you inherit it"** — Hebrew הָאָרֶץ הַטּוֹבָה, without "this"; וְהִנְחַלְתֶּם, "make inherit"; בְּנֵיכֶם "your sons".
- **"with a perfect heart and with a willing mind" → "with a whole heart and with a delighting soul"** (v9) — Hebrew בְּלֵב שָׁלֵם וּבְנֶפֶשׁ חֲפֵצָה; חָפֵץ is "delight in" ("willing" is נָדִיב, v21). **"the LORD searcheth all hearts" → "Yahweh seeks all hearts"** — Hebrew דּוֹרֵשׁ, the verb of "seek" in v8 and of "if you seek him" in this verse: he seeks the hearts, and is to be sought. **"all the imaginations of the thoughts" → "every inclination of the thoughts"** — Hebrew יֵצֶר מַחֲשָׁבוֹת, as Gen 6:5. **"he will cast thee off for ever" kept** — Hebrew יַזְנִיחֲךָ לָעַד.
- **"Take heed now" → "See now"** (v10) — Hebrew רְאֵה עַתָּה. **"be strong, and do it" → "be strong and do"** — Hebrew חֲזַק וַעֲשֵׂה, without an object; so also in v20.
- **"the houses thereof, and of the treasuries thereof, and of the upper chambers thereof, and of the inner parlours thereof, and of the place of the mercy seat" → "its houses and its storerooms and its upper rooms and its inner chambers, and of the house of the *kapporet*"** (v11) — Hebrew בָּתָּיו "its houses"; גַּנְזַכָּיו, a word found only here (a Persian loanword for a treasury), kept apart from אוֹצָר "treasury" (v12); בֵּית הַכַּפֹּרֶת "the house of the atonement cover", a name of the inner room found only here. **"pattern"** (v11, 12, 18, 19) — Hebrew תַּבְנִית, as Exod 25:9.
- **"the pattern of all that he had by the spirit" → "the pattern of all that was in the spirit with him"** (v12) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר הָיָה בָרוּחַ עִמּוֹ: either what he had in mind (his own spirit) or what came to him by the Spirit; the Hebrew does not say which. **"chambers"** (v12) is לְשָׁכוֹת, another word than the "chambers" (חֲדָרִים) of v11. **"dedicated things" → "holy things"** — Hebrew קֳדָשִׁים, as 26:20.
- **"vessels" / "instruments" → "utensils"** (v13, 14) — Hebrew כְּלֵי.
- **"He gave of gold by weight for things of gold, for all instruments of all manner of service" → "for the gold, by the weight for the gold, for all the utensils of each service"** (v14) — Hebrew לַזָּהָב בַּמִּשְׁקָל לַזָּהָב, with no verb: the list of v14–18 still hangs on "gave … the pattern" (v11, 12). "Each service" is literally "service and service"; so "each lampstand" (v15), "each table" (v16), "each basin" (v17).
- **"candlesticks" → "lampstands"** (v15) — Hebrew מְנֹרוֹת. **"according to the use of every candlestick" → "according to the service of each lampstand"** — Hebrew כַּעֲבוֹדַת, the word "service".
- **"the tables of shewbread" → "the tables of the arrangement"** (v16) — Hebrew שֻׁלְחֲנוֹת הַמַּעֲרֶכֶת; "bread" is not written (the bread set in an arrangement, Lev 24:6).
- **"fleshhooks, and the bowls, and the cups" → "the forks and the bowls and the jars"** (v17) — Hebrew מִזְלָגוֹת, מִזְרָקוֹת, קְשָׂוֹת. **"basons" → "basins"** — Hebrew כְּפוֹרִים, a late word for a bowl (Ezra 1:10; 8:27); its exact shape is not known. English has the same word for כִּיּוֹר.
- **"refined gold" → "purified gold"** (v18) — Hebrew זָהָב מְזֻקָּק ("refined gold" stands for another word, זָהָב סָגוּר, 1 Kgs 6:20). **"the pattern of the chariot of the cherubims, that spread out their wings, and covered the ark" → "the pattern of the chariot, the *cherubim*, gold, for those spreading out and screening over the ark"** — Hebrew וּלְתַבְנִית הַמֶּרְכָּבָה הַכְּרֻבִים זָהָב לְפֹרְשִׂים וְסֹכְכִים: "the cherubim" stands beside "the chariot" (the cherubim are the chariot); "wings" is not written; סָכַךְ is "screen", as Exod 25:20.
- **"All this, said David, the LORD made me understand in writing by his hand upon me, even all the works of this pattern" → "All of it in writing from the hand of Yahweh upon me; he gave insight into all the works of the pattern"** (v19) — Hebrew הַכֹּל בִּכְתָב מִיַּד יְהוָה עָלַי הִשְׂכִּיל כֹּל מַלְאֲכוֹת הַתַּבְנִית. "Said David" is not written: the speech begins without a word of saying. הִשְׂכִּיל is "give insight"; "me" is not written, and "upon me" may go with "the hand of Yahweh" or with "he gave insight". The verse is difficult.
- **"Be strong and of good courage, and do it: fear not, nor be dismayed" → "Be strong and be firm, and do; do not fear and do not be dismayed"** (v20) — Hebrew חֲזַק וֶאֱמַץ, two verbs for being strong, as Deut 31:7; Josh 1:6–9; "courage" is not a word of the Hebrew. **"he will not fail thee, nor forsake thee" → "He will not let you drop and will not forsake you"** — Hebrew לֹא יַרְפְּךָ, literally "let sink, let go slack", as Deut 31:6, 8; Josh 1:5. **"until thou hast finished" → "until … is finished"** — Hebrew עַד־לִכְלוֹת, "until the finishing of".
- **"and there shall be with thee for all manner of workmanship every willing skilful man, for any manner of service" → "and with you in all the work is every willing one with wisdom, for every service"** (v21) — Hebrew לְכָל־נָדִיב בַּחָכְמָה; "man" is not written. **"will be wholly at thy commandment" → "for all your words"** — Hebrew לְכָל־דְּבָרֶיךָ; no verb in the verse.

### Notes
- **David's final assembly** — public ratification of succession and temple plan. Parallels Moses-Joshua transition (Deut 31).
- **"Footstool of the feet of our *Elohim*"** (v2) — ark as footstool; Yahweh's throne is above the cherubim.
- **Double choice motif** — Judah chosen from tribes, David from Judah, Solomon from David's sons. Elective theology.
- **Covenant conditionality** (v7) — Davidic line "if" Solomon is strong to do the commandments. Nuances the earlier unconditional promise — personal application conditional.
- **"In writing from the hand of Yahweh"** (v19) — blueprint divine, analogous to Sinai's tabernacle pattern. Temple is sanctioned by direct revelation.
- **The chariot, the cherubim** (v18) — Ezekiel's throne-chariot vision (Ezek 1, 10) references this conception.
- **"Be strong and be firm"** (v20) — Joshua 1 echoed; conquest-discipline for temple-building.
- **Steadfast co-workers** (v21) — priestly divisions, willing craftsmen, princes, people. David hands over an organized machine.
