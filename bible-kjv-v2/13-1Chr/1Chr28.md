# 1 Chronicles 28

[1] David assembled at Jerusalem all the leaders of Israel — the leaders of the tribes, the commanders of the divisions that served the king, the commanders of thousands, the commanders of hundreds, the stewards of all the property and livestock of the king and his sons, with the palace officials, the warriors, and all the valiant men.

[2] Then King David rose to his feet and said: "Hear me, my brothers and my people. As for me, it was in my heart to build a house of rest for the ark of the covenant of Yahweh, and for the footstool of our *Elohim*; and I made preparations for the building.

[3] But *Elohim* said to me, 'You shall not build a house for my name, because you are a man of war and have shed blood.'

[4] Yet Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel chose me out of all my father's house to be king over Israel forever; for he has chosen Judah to be ruler and, of the house of Judah, the house of my father; and among the sons of my father he was pleased to make me king over all Israel.

[5] Of all my sons — for Yahweh has given me many sons — he has chosen Solomon my son to sit on the throne of the kingdom of Yahweh over Israel.

[6] He said to me, 'Solomon your son shall build my house and my courts; for I have chosen him to be my son, and I will be his father.

[7] I will establish his kingdom forever, if he remains steadfast to do my commandments and my ordinances, as at this day.'

[8] Now therefore, in the sight of all Israel, the assembly of Yahweh, and in the hearing of our *Elohim*: keep and seek all the commandments of Yahweh your *Elohim*, that you may possess this good land and leave it as an inheritance for your children after you forever.

[9] As for you, Solomon my son, know the *Elohim* of your father; serve him with a whole heart and a willing soul. For Yahweh searches all hearts and understands every inclination of the thoughts. If you seek him, he will be found by you; but if you forsake him, he will cast you off forever.

[10] Take heed now, for Yahweh has chosen you to build a house for the sanctuary. Be strong, and act."

[11] Then David gave to Solomon his son the pattern of the portico, of its buildings, its treasuries, its upper rooms, its inner chambers, and of the place of the mercy seat —

[12] and the pattern of all that he had in mind: the courts of the house of Yahweh, all the surrounding chambers, the treasuries of the house of *Elohim*, and the treasuries of the dedicated things;

[13] for the divisions of the priests and the Levites, for all the work of the service of the house of Yahweh, and for all the vessels of service in the house of Yahweh.

[14] He gave gold by weight for all the gold utensils for every kind of service; silver for all the silver utensils by weight for every kind of service;

[15] the weight for the golden lampstands and their golden lamps, by weight for each lampstand and its lamps; and for the silver lampstands by weight, for each lampstand and its lamps, according to the use of each lampstand;

[16] the weight of gold for the tables of the bread of the Presence, for each table; and silver for the silver tables;

[17] pure gold for the forks, basins, and pitchers; gold by weight for each of the golden bowls, silver by weight for each of the silver bowls;

[18] refined gold by weight for the altar of incense; and gold for the pattern of the chariot of the cherubim, who spread their wings and covered the ark of the covenant of Yahweh.

[19] "All this," said David, "he made me understand in writing from the hand of Yahweh upon me — all the details of the pattern."

[20] David said to Solomon his son, "Be strong and courageous, and act. Do not be afraid nor dismayed, for Yahweh *Elohim*, my *Elohim*, is with you. He will not fail you nor forsake you, until all the work for the service of the house of Yahweh is finished.

[21] Behold, the divisions of the priests and the Levites are ready for all the service of the house of *Elohim*; with you for every kind of work will be every willing, skilled man for every service; the leaders and all the people will be wholly at your command."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"princes" → "leaders"** (v1, 21) — *sarim*.
- **"companies... by course" → "divisions that served the king"** (v1).
- **"officers" → "palace officials"** (v1) — *sarisim*.
- **"candlesticks" → "lampstands"** (v15) — *menorot*.
- **"shewbread" → "bread of the Presence"** (v16).
- **"fleshhooks" → "forks"** (v17) — *mazlegot*.
- **"basons" → "bowls"** (v17) — *mizraqot*.
- **"cups" → "pitchers"** (v17) — *qesot*.
- **"parlours" → "chambers"** (v11) — *chadarim*.

### Notes
- **David's final assembly** — public ratification of succession and temple plan. Parallels Moses-Joshua transition (Deut 31).
- **"Footstool of our *Elohim*"** (v2) — ark as footstool; Yahweh's throne is above the cherubim.
- **Double choice motif** — Judah chosen from tribes, David from Judah, Solomon from David's sons. Elective theology.
- **Covenant conditionality** (v7) — Davidic line "if" Solomon obeys. Nuances the earlier unconditional promise — personal application conditional.
- **"Written pattern" from Yahweh's hand** (v19) — blueprint divine, analogous to Sinai's tabernacle pattern. Temple is sanctioned by direct revelation.
- **Chariot of cherubim** (v18) — Ezekiel's throne-chariot vision (Ezek 1, 10) references this conception.
- **"Be strong and courageous"** (v20) — Joshua 1 echoed; conquest-discipline for temple-building.
- **Steadfast co-workers** (v21) — priestly courses, craftsmen, princes, people. David hands over an organized machine.
