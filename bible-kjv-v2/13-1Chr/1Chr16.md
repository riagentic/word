## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*.
- **"flagon of wine" → "cake of raisins"** (v3) — Hebrew *ashishah*; raisin-cake, not a bottle of wine. KJV mistake.
- **"psalm" → "song of thanksgiving"** (v7).
- **"heathen" → "nations"** (v24, 35).
- **"kindreds" → "families"** (v28) — *mishpechot*.
- **"beauty of holiness" → "splendor of holiness"** (v29) — *hadrat qodesh*.
- **"tabernacle" → "dwelling"** (v39) — *mishkan*.

### Notes
- **Composite psalm** — Chronicler compiles from Ps 105:1-15 (vv 8-22), Ps 96:1-13 (vv 23-33), Ps 106:1, 47-48 (vv 34-36). Liturgical anthology attributed to David.
- **Asaph the chief** — father of a guild of singers; many psalms (50, 73-83) bear his name.
- **Gibeon / Jerusalem parallel worship** (v37-42) — Chronicler acknowledges dual cultic centers: ark in David's tent at Jerusalem; tabernacle at Gibeon still housing the altar of burnt offering. Unified at Solomon's temple dedication.
- **Torah of Yahweh** mentioned explicitly (v40) — Chronicler's priestly frame.
- **Two-choir format** — Asaph's group at Jerusalem ark; Heman-Jeduthun's group at Gibeon altar. Liturgical division of labor.
