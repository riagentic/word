# 1 Chronicles 29

[1] King David said to all the assembly, "⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ my son, whom alone ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has chosen, is young and inexperienced; the work is great, for the citadel is not for man, but for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[2] Now I have provided with all my ability for the house of my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: the gold for the gold vessels, the silver for the silver, the bronze for the bronze, the iron for the iron, and wood for the wood; onyx stones and stones for setting, stones of antimony and of varied colors, all sorts of precious stones, and marble in abundance.

[5] gold for the gold work and silver for the silver, and for every kind of work to be done by craftsmen. Now who will offer willingly, consecrating himself today to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?"

[6] Then the heads of fathers' houses, the chiefs of the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, the commanders of thousands and hundreds, and the officers of the king's work, offered willingly.

[7] They gave for the service of the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: five thousand talents of gold, ten thousand darics of gold, ten thousand talents of silver, eighteen thousand talents of bronze, and one hundred thousand talents of iron.

[10] David blessed ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in the presence of all the assembly; David said:

[14] "But who am I, and what is my people, that we should be able to offer so willingly? For all things come from you, and from your own hand we have given to you.

[16] O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, all this store that we have provided for building you a house for your holy name is from your hand; it is all yours.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"affection" → "heart"** (v3) — *ratzon*.
- **"proper good" → "personal treasure"** (v3) — *segulah*.
- **"glistering stones, and of divers colours" → "stones of antimony and of varied colors"** (v2) — *avnei-puk we-riqmah*; *puk* = antimony/kohl stone (decorative black).
- **"there is none abiding" → "without hope"** (v15) — *ve-eyn miqweh*.
- **"submitted themselves" → "pledged allegiance"** (v24) — *natenu yad*; "gave the hand."
- **"bullocks" → "bulls"** (v21).
- **"book of Samuel the seer... Nathan the prophet... Gad the seer" → "Records of"** (v29) — *divrei*; written records (not "books" in modern sense).
- **"princes" → "leaders"** (v24).

### Notes
- **David's last public act** — financial consecration for temple. Personal gift (3,000 talents gold, 7,000 silver) + people's gift (5,000 gold + 10,000 darics + 10,000 silver + 18,000 bronze + 100,000 iron). Enormous quantities; symbolic.
- **"Consecrating himself" (v5)** — Hebrew *limmallo yad*, "to fill one's hand" — priestly consecration idiom. David extends ordination-language to lay donors.
- **David's prayer** (v10-19) — classic piece of biblical theology. Divine sovereignty, human smallness, heart uprightness, covenant continuity.
- **"Second anointing"** (v22) — first in 1 Kgs 1 at Gihon during Adonijah crisis; Chronicles omits crisis, gives this calmer public ratification.
- **"Throne of Yahweh"** (v23) — distinctively Chronistic: Davidic kingship is Yahweh's kingship.
- **Closing record** (v29) — Chronicler cites three prophetic sources for David's acts. Acknowledges his account is selective.
- **1 Chronicles closes** with transfer to Solomon and peaceful death of David. Chronicler's David: ideal king, temple-founder, liturgist.
