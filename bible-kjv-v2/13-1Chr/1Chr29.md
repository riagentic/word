# 1 Chronicles 29

[1] King David said to all the assembly, "Solomon my son, whom alone *Elohim* has chosen, is young and inexperienced; the work is great, for the citadel is not for man, but for Yahweh *Elohim*.

[2] Now I have provided with all my ability for the house of my *Elohim*: the gold for the gold vessels, the silver for the silver, the bronze for the bronze, the iron for the iron, and wood for the wood; onyx stones and stones for setting, stones of antimony and of varied colors, all sorts of precious stones, and marble in abundance.

[3] Moreover, because I have set my heart on the house of my *Elohim*, I have of my own personal treasure of gold and silver that I give to the house of my *Elohim* — over and above all that I have provided for the holy house:

[4] three thousand talents of gold, of the gold of Ophir, and seven thousand talents of refined silver, to overlay the walls of the buildings;

[5] gold for the gold work and silver for the silver, and for every kind of work to be done by craftsmen. Now who will offer willingly, consecrating himself today to Yahweh?"

[6] Then the heads of fathers' houses, the chiefs of the tribes of Israel, the commanders of thousands and hundreds, and the officers of the king's work, offered willingly.

[7] They gave for the service of the house of *Elohim*: five thousand talents of gold, ten thousand darics of gold, ten thousand talents of silver, eighteen thousand talents of bronze, and one hundred thousand talents of iron.

[8] Those with whom precious stones were found gave them to the treasury of the house of Yahweh, into the hand of Jehiel the Gershonite.

[9] The people rejoiced because they had offered willingly, for with a whole heart they had offered willingly to Yahweh; King David also rejoiced greatly.

[10] David blessed Yahweh in the presence of all the assembly; David said:

"Blessed are you, O Yahweh, the *Elohim* of Israel our father, from everlasting to everlasting.

[11] Yours, O Yahweh, is the greatness, the power, the glory, the victory, and the majesty — for all that is in the heavens and on the earth is yours; yours is the kingdom, O Yahweh, and you are exalted as head above all.

[12] Riches and honor come from you, and you rule over all; in your hand are power and might; in your hand it is to make great and to give strength to all.

[13] Now therefore, our *Elohim*, we give thanks to you and praise your glorious name."

[14] "But who am I, and what is my people, that we should be able to offer so willingly? For all things come from you, and from your own hand we have given to you.

[15] For we are sojourners before you, and settlers, as were all our fathers. Our days on earth are like a shadow, without hope.

[16] O Yahweh our *Elohim*, all this store that we have provided for building you a house for your holy name is from your hand; it is all yours.

[17] I know, my *Elohim*, that you test the heart and delight in uprightness. In the uprightness of my heart I have willingly offered all these things; now I have seen with joy your people who are present here, offering willingly to you.

[18] O Yahweh, the *Elohim* of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel our fathers, keep forever such purposes and thoughts in the hearts of your people; direct their hearts toward you.

[19] Give to Solomon my son a whole heart to keep your commandments, your testimonies, and your statutes, and to do all these things, and to build the citadel, for which I have made provision."

[20] David said to all the assembly, "Now bless Yahweh your *Elohim*." All the assembly blessed Yahweh the *Elohim* of their fathers, bowed their heads, and prostrated themselves before Yahweh and the king.

[21] They offered sacrifices to Yahweh and offered burnt offerings to Yahweh on the next day: a thousand bulls, a thousand rams, and a thousand lambs, with their drink offerings, and sacrifices in abundance for all Israel.

[22] They ate and drank before Yahweh on that day with great gladness. They made Solomon the son of David king a second time, and anointed him as prince for Yahweh, and Zadok as priest.

[23] Then Solomon sat on the throne of Yahweh as king in place of David his father; he prospered, and all Israel obeyed him.

[24] All the leaders, the warriors, and also all the sons of King David pledged allegiance to King Solomon.

[25] Yahweh highly exalted Solomon in the sight of all Israel, and bestowed on him such royal majesty as had not been on any king before him in Israel.

[26] Thus David son of Jesse reigned over all Israel.

[27] The time that he reigned over Israel was forty years; he reigned seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem.

[28] He died at a good old age, full of days, riches, and honor; Solomon his son reigned in his place.

[29] The acts of King David, first and last, behold, they are written in the Records of Samuel the Seer, the Records of Nathan the Prophet, and the Records of Gad the Seer —

[30] with all his reign, his might, and the times that went over him, Israel, and all the kingdoms of the lands.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"affection" → "heart"** (v3) — *ratzon*.
- **"proper good" → "personal treasure"** (v3) — *segulah*.
- **"glistering stones, and of divers colours" → "stones of antimony and of varied colors"** (v2) — *avnei-puk we-riqmah*; *puk* = antimony/kohl stone (decorative black).
- **"there is none abiding" → "without hope"** (v15) — *ve-eyn miqweh*.
- **"submitted themselves" → "pledged allegiance"** (v24) — *natenu yad*; "gave the hand."
- **"bullocks" → "bulls"** (v21).
- **"book of Samuel the seer... Nathan the prophet... Gad the seer" → "Records of"** (v29) — *divrei*; written records (not "books" in modern sense).
- **"princes" → "leaders"** (v24).

### Notes
- **David's last public act** — financial consecration for temple. Personal gift (3,000 talents gold, 7,000 silver) + people's gift (5,000 gold + 10,000 darics + 10,000 silver + 18,000 bronze + 100,000 iron). Enormous quantities; symbolic.
- **"Consecrating himself" (v5)** — Hebrew *limmallo yad*, "to fill one's hand" — priestly consecration idiom. David extends ordination-language to lay donors.
- **David's prayer** (v10-19) — classic piece of biblical theology. Divine sovereignty, human smallness, heart uprightness, covenant continuity.
- **"Second anointing"** (v22) — first in 1 Kgs 1 at Gihon during Adonijah crisis; Chronicles omits crisis, gives this calmer public ratification.
- **"Throne of Yahweh"** (v23) — distinctively Chronistic: Davidic kingship is Yahweh's kingship.
- **Closing record** (v29) — Chronicler cites three prophetic sources for David's acts. Acknowledges his account is selective.
- **1 Chronicles closes** with transfer to Solomon and peaceful death of David. Chronicler's David: ideal king, temple-founder, liturgist.
