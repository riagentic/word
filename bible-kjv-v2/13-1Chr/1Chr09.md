# 1 Chronicles 9

[1] So all Israel was enrolled by genealogies; behold, they are written in the Book of the Kings of Israel. Judah was carried away to Babylon for their unfaithfulness.

[2] The first inhabitants who lived again on their property in their cities were: Israelites, priests, Levites, and the temple servants.

[3] In Jerusalem dwelt some of the children of Judah, the children of Benjamin, and the children of Ephraim and Manasseh:

[4] Uthai son of Ammihud son of Omri son of Imri son of Bani, of the children of Perez son of Judah;

[5] of the Shilonites: Asaiah the firstborn and his sons;

[6] of the sons of Zerah: Jeuel and their brothers — six hundred ninety;

[7] of the sons of Benjamin: Sallu son of Meshullam son of Hodaviah son of Hassenuah;

[8] Ibneiah son of Jeroham; Elah son of Uzzi son of Michri; Meshullam son of Shephatiah son of Reuel son of Ibnijah;

[9] and their brothers, according to their generations — nine hundred fifty-six. All these men were heads of fathers' houses by their fathers' houses.

[10] Of the priests: Jedaiah, Jehoiarib, Jachin,

[11] Azariah son of Hilkiah son of Meshullam son of Zadok son of Meraioth son of Ahitub, officer of the house of *Elohim*;

[12] Adaiah son of Jeroham son of Pashhur son of Malchijah; Maasai son of Adiel son of Jahzerah son of Meshullam son of Meshillemith son of Immer;

[13] and their brothers, heads of their fathers' houses — one thousand seven hundred sixty, very able men for the work of the service of the house of *Elohim*.

[14] Of the Levites: Shemaiah son of Hasshub son of Azrikam son of Hashabiah, of the sons of Merari;

[15] Bakbakkar, Heresh, Galal, and Mattaniah son of Mica son of Zichri son of Asaph;

[16] Obadiah son of Shemaiah son of Galal son of Jeduthun; Berechiah son of Asa son of Elkanah, who lived in the villages of the Netophathites.

[17] The gatekeepers: Shallum, Akkub, Talmon, Ahiman, and their brothers; Shallum was the chief.

[18] Hitherto they stood at the king's gate on the east. They were the gatekeepers for the camps of the children of Levi.

[19] Shallum son of Kore son of Ebiasaph son of Korah, and his brothers of his father's house, the Korahites, were over the work of the service, keepers of the thresholds of the tent; their fathers had been over the camp of Yahweh, keepers of the entrance.

[20] Phinehas son of Eleazar had been ruler over them in time past — Yahweh was with him.

[21] Zechariah son of Meshelemiah was gatekeeper of the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

[22] All these, chosen as gatekeepers at the thresholds, were two hundred twelve. They were enrolled by genealogy in their villages; David and Samuel the seer established them in their office of trust.

[23] So they and their sons had oversight of the gates of the house of Yahweh, the house of the tent, by watches.

[24] On four sides were the gatekeepers: east, west, north, and south.

[25] Their brothers who were in their villages were to come in for seven days at a time in rotation, to be with them.

[26] For the four chief gatekeepers, who were Levites, were in a trust-office; they were over the chambers and the treasuries of the house of *Elohim*.

[27] They lodged around the house of *Elohim*, for the watch was their charge, and the opening every morning was their duty.

[28] Some of them had charge of the vessels used in the service; they would bring them in and out by count.

[29] Some of them were appointed over the furniture and over all the holy utensils, the fine flour, the wine, the oil, the frankincense, and the spices.

[30] Some of the sons of the priests prepared the mixing of the spices.

[31] Mattithiah, one of the Levites — he was the firstborn of Shallum the Korahite — had the office of trust over the things baked in pans.

[32] Some of their brothers, of the sons of the Kohathites, had charge of the bread of the Presence to prepare it every Sabbath.

[33] Now these are the singers, heads of fathers' houses of the Levites, who stayed in the chambers, free from other service, for they were on duty day and night.

[34] These were the heads of fathers' houses of the Levites, chief throughout their generations; these dwelt in Jerusalem.

[35] At Gibeon dwelt Jeiel the father of Gibeon, and the name of his wife was Maacah;

[36] his firstborn son was Abdon, then Zur, Kish, Baal, Ner, Nadab,

[37] Gedor, Ahio, Zechariah, and Mikloth.

[38] Mikloth fathered Shimeam. These also lived with their brothers in Jerusalem, alongside them.

[39] Ner fathered Kish; Kish fathered Saul; Saul fathered Jonathan, Malchi-shua, Abinadab, and Esh-baal.

[40] The son of Jonathan was Merib-baal; Merib-baal fathered Micah.

[41] The sons of Micah: Pithon, Melech, Tahrea, and Ahaz.

[42] Ahaz fathered Jarah; Jarah fathered Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri; Zimri fathered Moza;

[43] Moza fathered Binea; Rephaiah was his son, Eleasah his son, Azel his son.

[44] Azel had six sons, whose names were: Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. These were the sons of Azel.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (throughout) — *sho'arim*.
- **"king's gate" — retained** (v18) — eastern temple-gate used by royal entry.
- **"set office" → "office of trust"** (v22, 26, 31) — *be-emunatam*.
- **"tabernacle" / "tabernacle of the congregation" → "tent" / "Tent of Meeting"** (v19, 21) — *ohel / ohel mo'ed*.
- **"wards" → "watches"** (v23) — *mishmarot*.
- **"singers... were free" → "singers... free from other service"** (v33) — *ptirim*; exempt.

### Notes
- **Post-exilic repopulation list** — largely paralleled in Neh 11. Chronicler's intent: show continuity from pre-exile to post-exile temple staff.
- **Netinim** — temple dedicated servants (Ezra 8:20); likely descendants of non-Israelite groups (Gibeonites, Solomon's servants) set apart for temple labor.
- **1,760 priests** (v13) — large number; post-exilic community's religious infrastructure intact.
- **David and Samuel as co-founders** (v22) — legitimizes gatekeeper offices with prophetic + royal authority.
- **Saulide genealogy repeated** (v35-44) — nearly identical to 8:29-40. Deliberate: Chronicler prepares for Saul's death in ch 10 by restating his family line.
- **Mikloth fathered Shimeam** (8:32 "Shimeah"; here "Shimeam") — minor textual variant.
- **"Four chief gatekeepers"** (v26) — compass-point-based temple security.
