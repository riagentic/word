## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew הָאֱלֹהִים, with the article (v11, 13, 26, 27).
- **"And" · lists** (throughout) — each "and" stands where the Hebrew has וְ; the lists have no verb, and "was" / "were" is supplied.
- **"So all Israel were reckoned by genealogies … who were carried away to Babylon for their transgression" → "And all Israel were enrolled by genealogy … And Judah were carried into exile to Babylon for their unfaithfulness"** (v1) — Hebrew הִתְיַחְשׂוּ, as 7:5; עַל־סֵפֶר, literally "upon the book"; the Masoretic accents end the first sentence at "the kings of Israel" and begin a new one with "And Judah" (KJV: "the book of the kings of Israel and Judah, who were carried away"). הָגְלוּ "were carried into exile", a plural; מַעַל "unfaithfulness".
- **"the first inhabitants that dwelt in their possessions in their cities were, the Israelites, the priests, Levites, and the Nethinims" → "the first dwellers, who were in their possession, in their cities, were Israel, the priests, the Levites and the Nethinim"** (v2) — Hebrew הַיּוֹשְׁבִים הָרִאשֹׁנִים: יָשַׁב "dwell"; "again" is not written; אֲחֻזָּה, singular; יִשְׂרָאֵל "Israel", the name (the verse had "Israelites"); הַכֹּהֲנִים הַלְוִיִּם, two nouns side by side, without "and". נְתִינִים *netinim* is literally "given ones"; "temple" is not in the word.
- **"of the children of" → "some of the sons of"** (v3) — Hebrew מִן־בְּנֵי, three times.
- **"Bani, of the children of Pharez"** (v4) — the written text (ketiv) is בנימן, "Benjamin"; the reading tradition (qere) divides it בָּנִי מִן, "Bani, of"; the qere is translated.
- **"of the Shilonites" → "of the Shilonite"** (v5) — Hebrew הַשִּׁילוֹנִי, a singular with the article; so also "the Netophathite" (v16) and "the Kohathite" (v32).
- **"chief of the fathers in the house of their fathers" → "heads of the fathers, of their fathers' houses"** (v9) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי אָבוֹת לְבֵית אֲבֹתֵיהֶם.
- **"Hilkiah", "Obadiah", "Meshelemiah"** (v11, 16, 21) — the short forms printed here: חִלְקִיָּה, עֹבַדְיָה, מְשֶׁלֶמְיָה.
- **"the ruler of the house of God" → "the leader of the house of Elohim"** (v11; v20) — Hebrew נָגִיד.
- **"very able men for the work of the service" → "mighty ones of ability for the work of the service"** (v13) — Hebrew גִּבּוֹרֵי חֵיל מְלֶאכֶת עֲבוֹדַת, as 7:2; "for" is supplied: the Hebrew joins the words without a preposition.
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (throughout) — Hebrew שֹׁעֵר, from שַׁעַר "gate".
- **"and their brethren: Shallum was the chief" → "and their brother Shallum was the head"** (v17) — Hebrew וַאֲחִיהֶם, pointed as a singular, "their brother" (the verse had "their brothers"); הָרֹאשׁ "the head".
- **"Who hitherto waited in the king's gate eastward: they were porters in the companies of the children of Levi" → "And until now, in the gate of the king, to the east, they are the gatekeepers for the camps of the sons of Levi"** (v18) — Hebrew וְעַד־הֵנָּה, literally "and as far as here", of time. The first half, "and until now in the gate of the king eastward", has no subject and no verb; whether Shallum or all four are meant is not said. מַחֲנֶה "camp".
- **"keepers of the gates of the tabernacle … keepers of the entry" → "keepers of the thresholds of the tent … keepers of the entry"** (v19) — Hebrew סַף "threshold"; אֹהֶל "tent"; מָבוֹא "entry" (פֶּתַח in v21 is "entrance").
- **"and the LORD was with him" → "Yahweh was with him"** (v20) — Hebrew יְהוָה עִמּוֹ, without a verb and without "and"; it can also be read as a wish, "Yahweh be with him".
- **"These were reckoned by their genealogy in their villages, whom David and Samuel the seer did ordain in their set office" → "They — in their villages was their enrollment by genealogy; they it was whom David and Samuel the seer founded, in their faithfulness"** (v22) — Hebrew הֵמָּה בְחַצְרֵיהֶם הִתְיַחְשָׂם; יִסַּד "found, lay the foundation of" (here: set up); אֱמוּנָה "faithfulness" (v22, 26, 31). It is commonly taken here as "a position of trust, a standing office"; the word is kept, and whose faithfulness (the gatekeepers', or David's and Samuel's) is left open as in the Hebrew.
- **"had the oversight of the gates of the house of the LORD, namely, the house of the tabernacle, by wards" → "were over the gates of the house of Yahweh, the house of the tent, by watches"** (v23) — Hebrew עַל "over"; no word "oversight"; מִשְׁמָרוֹת "watches".
- **"In four quarters were the porters, toward the east, west, north, and south" → "To the four winds the gatekeepers were to be: east, west, north and south"** (v24) — Hebrew לְאַרְבַּע רוּחוֹת: רוּחַ "wind"; יִהְיוּ, an imperfect. The four directions are literally "sunrise, toward the sea, northward and toward the Negev".
- **"were to come after seven days from time to time with them" → "were to come for the seven days, from time to time, with these"** (v25) — Hebrew לְשִׁבְעַת הַיָּמִים מֵעֵת אֶל־עֵת עִם־אֵלֶּה.
- **"For these Levites, the four chief porters, were in their set office" → "For in faithfulness were they, the four mighty ones of the gatekeepers; they were the Levites"** (v26) — Hebrew אַרְבַּעַת גִּבֹּרֵי הַשֹּׁעֲרִים: גִּבּוֹר "mighty one"; הֵם הַלְוִיִּם.
- **"because the charge was upon them, and the opening thereof every morning pertained to them" → "for upon them was the charge, and they were over the key, and that morning by morning"** (v27) — Hebrew מִשְׁמֶרֶת "charge"; מַפְתֵּחַ "key" (Judg 3:25), literally "opener"; וְלַבֹּקֶר לַבֹּקֶר "and for the morning, for the morning"; "that" is supplied.
- **"the ministering vessels … the vessels, and all the instruments of the sanctuary" → "the utensils of the service … the utensils and all the holy utensils"** (v28, 29) — Hebrew כְּלִי three times; כְּלֵי הַקֹּדֶשׁ as 1 Kgs 8:4. מְמֻנִּים "appointed", literally "counted". לְבוֹנָה "frankincense".
- **"made the ointment of the spices" → "were blending the blend for the spices"** (v30) — Hebrew רֹקְחֵי הַמִּרְקַחַת, one root twice (Exod 30:25).
- **"had the set office over the things that were made in the pans" → "was in faithfulness over the work of the griddle cakes"** (v31) — Hebrew חֲבִתִּים, found only here; from מַחֲבַת "griddle" (Lev 2:5; 6:21): what is baked on the griddle. The exact sense is uncertain.
- **"the shewbread, to prepare it every sabbath" → "the bread of the arrangement, to prepare it Shabbat by Shabbat"** (v32) — Hebrew לֶחֶם הַמַּעֲרָכֶת (Lev 24:6–8), another name than "the bread of the face" (Exod 25:30); "showbread" and "bread of the Presence" render neither. שַׁבַּת שַׁבָּת, the word twice; "it" is supplied.
- **"who remaining in the chambers were free: for they were employed in that work day and night" → "in the chambers, set free; for by day and by night it was upon them, in the work"** (v33) — the written text (ketiv) is פטירים; the reading tradition (qere) פְּטוּרִים, "set free, released", is translated; from what they were free is not written, and the sense is uncertain. עֲלֵיהֶם בַּמְּלָאכָה, literally "upon them in the work"; "it was" is supplied.
- **Verses 35–44 and 8:29–38** — the same wording stands for the same Hebrew; what differs here is kept: the father of Gibeon is named, Jeiel (v35; ketiv יעואל "Jeuel", the qere is translated); Ner (v36) and Mikloth (v37) are in the list; Zechariah (v37; 8:31 Zecher); Shimeam (v38; 8:32 Shimeah); Tahrea (v41; 8:35 Tarea); Jarah (v42; 8:36 Jehoaddah); "and Rephaiah" (v43; 8:37 Rapha); "These" (v44; 8:38 "All these").
- **"Merib-baal … Meri-baal"** (v40) — the second time the name is written מְרִי־בַעַל *Meri-baal*; the original-name form follows the spelling.
- **"and Tahrea, and Ahaz" → "and Tahrea"** (v41) — the Hebrew of this verse ends with Tahrea; "and Ahaz" (8:35) is not printed here, though v42 goes on with Ahaz. The verse had added it; it is taken out.

### Notes
- **Post-exilic repopulation list** — largely paralleled in Neh 11. Chronicler's intent: show continuity from pre-exile to post-exile temple staff.
- **Netinim** — "given ones" (Ezra 8:20); likely descendants of non-Israelite groups (Gibeonites, Solomon's servants) set apart for labor at the house.
- **1,760 priests** (v13) — large number; post-exilic community's religious infrastructure intact.
- **David and Samuel** (v22) — the gatekeepers' standing is traced to the king and the seer together.
- **Saulide genealogy repeated** (v35-44) — nearly identical to 8:29-38. Chronicler prepares for Saul's death in ch 10 by restating his family line.
- **Mikloth begot Shimeam** (8:32 "Shimeah"; here "Shimeam") — minor textual variant.
- **"The four mighty ones of the gatekeepers"** (v26) — one for each of the four winds (v24).
