## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (throughout) — *sho'arim*.
- **"king's gate" — retained** (v18) — eastern temple-gate used by royal entry.
- **"set office" → "office of trust"** (v22, 26, 31) — *be-emunatam*.
- **"tabernacle" / "tabernacle of the congregation" → "tent" / "Tent of Meeting"** (v19, 21) — *ohel / ohel mo'ed*.
- **"wards" → "watches"** (v23) — *mishmarot*.
- **"singers... were free" → "singers... free from other service"** (v33) — *ptirim*; exempt.

### Notes
- **Post-exilic repopulation list** — largely paralleled in Neh 11. Chronicler's intent: show continuity from pre-exile to post-exile temple staff.
- **Netinim** — temple dedicated servants (Ezra 8:20); likely descendants of non-Israelite groups (Gibeonites, Solomon's servants) set apart for temple labor.
- **1,760 priests** (v13) — large number; post-exilic community's religious infrastructure intact.
- **David and Samuel as co-founders** (v22) — legitimizes gatekeeper offices with prophetic + royal authority.
- **Saulide genealogy repeated** (v35-44) — nearly identical to 8:29-40. Deliberate: Chronicler prepares for Saul's death in ch 10 by restating his family line.
- **Mikloth fathered Shimeam** (8:32 "Shimeah"; here "Shimeam") — minor textual variant.
- **"Four chief gatekeepers"** (v26) — compass-point-based temple security.
