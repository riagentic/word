## Changes

- **"The sons of …" / "And the sons of …"; "and" between names** (throughout) — as the Hebrew has or lacks וְ (v1–2 "Levi and Judah, Issachar and Zebulun, Dan, Joseph and Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad and Asher"; v25 "and Ozem, Ahijah"; v52 "Haroeh, half of the Menuhoth"; v55 three names with no "and"). Supplied: the colon after "the sons of X", the copula, and "were" in v4, 27, 28.
- **"the daughter of Shua the Canaanitess" → "Bath-shua the Canaanite"** (v3) — מִבַּת־שׁוּעַ הַכְּנַעֲנִית: "from the daughter of Shua" or the name Bath-shua (Gen 38:2, 12 names only her father, Shua); הַכְּנַעֲנִית is feminine. "three" has no "these" in the Hebrew.
- **"and he slew him" → "and he put him to death"** (v3) — וַיְמִיתֵהוּ; the subject is not named again in the Hebrew (Gen 38:7 names Yahweh).
- **"Achar, the troubler of Israel, who transgressed in the thing accursed" → "… who acted unfaithfully in the devoted thing"** (v7) — עָכָר עוֹכֵר: the name is spelled so that it sounds the verb "trouble" (Josh 7:1 has Achan; 7:25 the verb); מָעַל בַּחֵרֶם as Josh 7:1. The Hebrew says "the sons of Carmi" and names one; how Carmi belongs to Zerah is not said here.
- **"which were born unto him"** (v9) — the three names stand with אֶת, as objects, though no active verb goes before; the Hebrew is kept as "who were born to him: …". Chelubai (כְּלוּבָי) is the Caleb (כָּלֵב) of v18, 42.
- **"begat" → "begot"** (v10–13, 18, 20, 22, 36–41, 44, 46) — הוֹלִיד, as Ruth 4:18–22.
- **"prince of the children of Judah" → "the chieftain of the sons of Judah"** (v10) — נָשִׂיא; בְּנֵי "sons of".
- **"Salma"** (v11, 51, 54) — שַׂלְמָא, the spelling of Chronicles (Ruth 4:20 שַׂלְמָה, 4:21 Salmon).
- **"Jesse"** (v13) — written אִישַׁי here, with א, against יִשַׁי in v12; the token keeps one name. "Shimea" is שִׁמְעָא (1 Sam 16:9 Shammah). **"Abishai"** (v16) is written אַבְשַׁי, Abshai. "their sisters" follows the qere.
- **"Jether the Ishmaelite"** (v17) — יֶתֶר הַיִּשְׁמְעֵאלִי as written here; 2 Sam 17:25 has "Ithra the Israelite". Neither is changed to match the other.
- **"begat children of Azubah his wife, and of Jerioth" → "begot by Azubah, a wife, and by Jerioth"** (v18) — הוֹלִיד אֶת־עֲזוּבָה אִשָּׁה וְאֶת־יְרִיעוֹת: the Hebrew is difficult. אֶת may be "with / by" (so here; then the verb has no object, and none is supplied) or the object marker ("begot Azubah, a wife, and Jerioth"); אִשָּׁה has no "his". "her sons" is singular "her": which woman is meant is not said. v19 speaks of Azubah as Caleb's wife.
- **"whom he married when he was threescore years old" → "and he took her, and he was sixty years old"** (v21) — two plain clauses in the Hebrew; lit. "a son of sixty years".
- **"who had three and twenty cities" → "and he had twenty-three cities"** (v22) — וַיְהִי־לוֹ.
- **"And he took Geshur, and Aram, with the towns of Jair, from them, with Kenath, and the towns thereof, even threescore cities" → "And Geshur and Aram took the tent villages of Jair from them, Kenath and its daughters, sixty cities"** (v23) — the verb is singular and Geshur and Aram are its subject; חַוֺּת "tent villages" (as Num 32:41, where it is the name Havvoth-jair); בְּנֹתֶיהָ "its daughters", the settlements that belong to a city. "All these were the sons of Machir": בְּנֵי.
- **"And after that Hezron was dead in Caleb-ephratah, then Abiah Hezron's wife bare him Ashur" → "And after the death of Hezron in Caleb-ephrathah, the wife of Hezron, Abijah, bore to him Ashhur"** (v24) — the Hebrew runs "and after the death of Hezron in Caleb Ephrathah, and the wife of Hezron (was) Abijah, and she bore to him …"; the two "and" that English cannot carry are left out, nothing else. The Masoretic Text is kept (from memory: the Greek reads "Caleb went in to Ephrathah").
- **"and Ozem, and Ahijah" → "and Ozem, Ahijah"** (v25) — אֲחִיָּה stands without "and"; whether it is a fifth son or belongs otherwise to the sentence is not clear.
- **"died without children" → "died without sons"** (v30, 32) — לֹא בָנִים, lit. "not sons".
- **"And the children of Sheshan; Ahlai" → "And the sons of Sheshan: Ahlai"** (v31) — בְּנֵי; v34 says that Sheshan had no sons, but only daughters. The two statements stand side by side in the Hebrew.
- **"Jonathan"** (v32, 33) — יוֹנָתָן, the short form.
- **"but daughters" → "but only daughters"** (v34) — כִּי אִם after a negative, "except"; "servant" → "slave" (v34, 35) — עֶבֶד.
- **"Mesha his firstborn, which was the father of Ziph; and the sons of Mareshah the father of Hebron"** (v42) — rendered word for word; the second half has no verb and no names, and how "the sons of Mareshah" belong to Caleb is not said.
- **"Jorkoam"** (v44) — יָרְקֳעָם, Jorkeam.
- **"Maachah, Caleb's concubine, bare" → "The concubine of Caleb, Maacah, bore"** (v48) — the word order of the Hebrew; the verb is written in the masculine form (יָלַד).
- **"She bare also Shaaph" → "And she bore Shaaph"** (v49) — no "also"; "Sheva" follows with אֶת and no "and".
- **"These were the sons of Caleb the son of Hur, the firstborn of Ephratah" → "These were the sons of Caleb. The son of Hur, the firstborn of Ephrathah: …"** (v50) — אֵלֶּה הָיוּ בְּנֵי כָלֵב בֶּן־חוּר בְּכוֹר אֶפְרָתָה. The Hebrew can be read as one phrase ("Caleb the son of Hur") or as the close of Caleb's list, like v33, followed by a new heading, "The son of Hur" (singular בֶּן, though three names follow). The second is printed, because v19 makes Hur the son of Caleb and Ephrath; the first is equally possible in the words themselves.
- **"Haroeh, and half of the Manahethites" → "Haroeh, half of the Menuhoth"** (v52) — הָרֹאֶה חֲצִי הַמְּנֻחוֹת, with no "and"; the words can also be read as common nouns ("the seer", "half of the resting places"). The meaning is not certain.
- **"families" → "clans"** (v53, 55) — מִשְׁפָּחוֹת. The names in v53–54 are singular with the article in the Hebrew ("the Ithrite … the Zorathite and the Eshtaolite"); "came" → "went out" (יָצְאוּ).
- **"Ataroth, the house of Joab"** (v54) — taken as one name, Atroth-beth-joab; it can also be divided.
- **"the scribes which dwelt at Jabez" → "the scribes, the inhabitants of Jabez"** (v55) — qere יֹשְׁבֵי; written ישבו "they dwelt". סֹפְרִים "scribes" may also be a name (Sophrites). "the Kenites that came of Hemath" → "who came from Hammath": הַבָּאִים מֵחַמַּת; Hammath is a person ("the father of the house of Rechab") or a place.

### Notes
- **Judah first** (v3–4:23) — the tribe of David is set before the others.
- **The line to David** (v10–15) — the same names as Ruth 4:19–22 (there Salmah / Salmon; here Salma).
- **Bezalel** (v20) — Exod 31:2.
- **Jair** (v22) — Num 32:41; Deut 3:14; Judg 10:4.
