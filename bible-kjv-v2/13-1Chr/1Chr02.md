## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Pharez / Zarah" → "Perez / Zerah"** — standard spelling.
- **"Shimma" → "Shimea"** (v13) — variant of Shammah.
- **"Nethaneel" → "Nethanel"** (v14).
- **"Ishmeelite" → "Ishmaelite"** (v17).
- **"Ephrath / Ephratah" → "Ephrath / Ephrathah"** — standardized.
- **"prince" → "leader"** (v10) — *nasi*.

### Notes
- **Tribe of Judah first** — Chronicler's priority: David's tribe, Messianic line. Judah gets more space than any other tribe.
- **Bath-shua** (v3) — Hebrew *bat-shua* = "daughter of Shua"; KJV's "daughter of Shua" preserves the literal, but she can be referred to by name alone.
- **Achar/Achan** — Chronicler calls him Achar (matching *okher*), punning on his name to emphasize his role as "troubler." Note: MT Josh 7 has Achan; here Achar. Wordplay strengthened.
- **Messianic lineage** (v10-15) — Nahshon-Salma-Boaz-Obed-Jesse-David. Matches Ruth 4 and Matt 1.
- **Bezalel** (v20) — tabernacle master craftsman (Ex 31), whose lineage traced through Caleb's line.
- **Jether the Ishmaelite** — 2 Sam 17:25 calls him "Israelite"; likely textual corruption, Chronicles preserves correct reading.
- **Sheshan's Egyptian son-in-law** — unusual inclusion of a servant-becomes-heir line; genealogical note that slave lineage joined Judah through marriage.
- **Scribal families at Jabez** (v55) — Kenites joined to Judah. Connection to Rechabites (Jer 35) via Hammath. Kenites = Moses's father-in-law Jethro's people (Judg 1:16).
