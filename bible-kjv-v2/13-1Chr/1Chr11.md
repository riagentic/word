# 1 Chronicles 11

[1] Then all Israel gathered to David at Hebron, saying, "Behold, we are your bone and your flesh.

[2] In times past, even when Saul was king, it was you who led out and brought in Israel; Yahweh your *Elohim* said to you, 'You shall shepherd my people Israel, and you shall be ruler over my people Israel.'"

[3] So all the elders of Israel came to the king at Hebron; David made a covenant with them at Hebron before Yahweh, and they anointed David king over Israel, according to the word of Yahweh by Samuel.

[4] David and all Israel went to Jerusalem — that is, Jebus — where the Jebusites, the inhabitants of the land, were.

[5] The inhabitants of Jebus said to David, "You shall not come in here." Nevertheless David captured the stronghold of Zion; it is the city of David.

[6] David said, "Whoever strikes down the Jebusites first shall become chief and commander." Joab son of Zeruiah went up first, and he became chief.

[7] David dwelt in the stronghold; therefore it was called the city of David.

[8] He built the city all around, from the Millo around; Joab restored the rest of the city.

[9] David grew greater and greater, for Yahweh of Hosts was with him.

[10] These are the chiefs of David's warriors, who strongly supported him in his kingdom, together with all Israel, to make him king, according to the word of Yahweh concerning Israel.

[11] This is the list of David's warriors: Jashobeam son of a Hachmonite, chief of the Thirty; he wielded his spear against three hundred, whom he killed at one time.

[12] Next to him was Eleazar son of Dodo the Ahohite, who was one of the three mighty men.

[13] He was with David at Pas-dammim, when the Philistines were gathered there for battle, where there was a plot of ground full of barley; the people fled from before the Philistines.

[14] But they took their stand in the midst of the plot, defended it, and struck down the Philistines; Yahweh saved them by a great victory.

[15] Three of the thirty chiefs went down to the rock to David, to the cave of Adullam, while the army of the Philistines was encamped in the Valley of Rephaim.

[16] David was then in the stronghold, and the Philistine garrison was then at Bethlehem.

[17] David longed and said, "Oh that someone would give me water to drink from the well of Bethlehem that is by the gate!"

[18] The three broke through the camp of the Philistines, drew water from the well of Bethlehem that was by the gate, took it, and brought it to David. But David would not drink it; he poured it out to Yahweh,

[19] and said, "Far be it from me, my *Elohim*, to do this! Shall I drink the blood of these men who went at the risk of their lives? For at the risk of their lives they brought it." Therefore he would not drink it. Such were the deeds of the three mighty men.

[20] Abishai the brother of Joab was chief of the Thirty; he wielded his spear against three hundred, whom he killed, and won a name beside the three.

[21] Of the three in the Thirty he was doubly honored; he was their commander, but he did not attain to the three.

[22] Benaiah son of Jehoiada, son of a valiant man of Kabzeel, a doer of great deeds. He struck down the two ariel — lion-hearted — men of Moab. He also went down and struck down a lion in the middle of a pit on a snowy day.

[23] He struck down an Egyptian, a man of great stature — five cubits tall. In the Egyptian's hand was a spear like a weaver's beam; he went down to him with a staff, wrested the spear from the Egyptian's hand, and killed him with his own spear.

[24] These were the deeds of Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and he had a name among the three mighty men.

[25] Behold, he was honored among the Thirty, but he did not attain to the three. David set him over his bodyguard.

[26] The valiant men of the armies were: Asahel brother of Joab, Elhanan son of Dodo of Bethlehem,

[27] Shammoth the Harorite, Helez the Pelonite,

[28] Ira son of Ikkesh the Tekoite, Abiezer the Anathothite,

[29] Sibbecai the Hushathite, Ilai the Ahohite,

[30] Maharai the Netophathite, Heled son of Baanah the Netophathite,

[31] Ithai son of Ribai of Gibeah of the children of Benjamin, Benaiah the Pirathonite,

[32] Hurai of the brooks of Gaash, Abiel the Arbathite,

[33] Azmaveth the Baharumite, Eliahba the Shaalbonite,

[34] the sons of Hashem the Gizonite, Jonathan son of Shagee the Hararite,

[35] Ahiam son of Sachar the Hararite, Eliphal son of Ur,

[36] Hepher the Mecherathite, Ahijah the Pelonite,

[37] Hezro the Carmelite, Naarai son of Ezbai,

[38] Joel brother of Nathan, Mibhar son of Hagri,

[39] Zelek the Ammonite, Naharai the Berothite, the armor-bearer of Joab son of Zeruiah,

[40] Ira the Ithrite, Gareb the Ithrite,

[41] Uriah the Hittite, Zabad son of Ahlai,

[42] Adina son of Shiza the Reubenite, a chief of the Reubenites, and thirty with him,

[43] Hanan son of Maacah, Joshaphat the Mithnite,

[44] Uzzia the Ashterathite, Shama and Jeiel sons of Hotham the Aroerite,

[45] Jediael son of Shimri, and Joha his brother the Tizite,

[46] Eliel the Mahavite, Jeribai and Joshaviah sons of Elnaam, Ithmah the Moabite,

[47] Eliel, Obed, and Jaasiel the Mezobaite.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"feed my people" → "shepherd my people"** (v2) — *ro'eh*.
- **"castle" → "stronghold"** (v5, 7) — *metzudah*.
- **"captain" → "commander"** (v6, 21) — *sar*.
- **"guard" → "bodyguard"** (v25) — *mishma'at*.
- **"my God forbid it me" → "far be it from me, my *Elohim*"** (v19) — *chalilah li me-elohai*.
- **"Antothite" → "Anathothite"** (v28).
- **"Mesobaite" → "Mezobaite"** (v47).

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Sam 5, 23** — Chronicler combines David's coronation, Jerusalem capture, and warriors list.
- **"Your bone and flesh"** — tribal blood-kinship appeal.
- **Jebus taken via "gutter" (2 Sam 5:8) or Joab first up (here)** — Chronicler highlights Joab's initiative, which makes him chief.
- **"City of David"** — Ophel ridge; southeast Jerusalem; archaeologically identified.
- **Millo** — supporting terraces, stepped-stone structure.
- **Warriors list** — three tiers: Three, Thirty, broader army. Chronicler's list expands 2 Sam 23.
- **Uriah the Hittite** (v41) — preserved in the roll of honor despite David's later sin against him.
- **Additional names** (v41b-47) — Chronicler adds names not in 2 Sam 23; perhaps later additions, or a second company.
- **Bethlehem water exploit** — exemplifies sacrificial loyalty; David's refusal to drink honors it as holy libation.
