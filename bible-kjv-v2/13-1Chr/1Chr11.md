## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Parallels: 2 Sam 5:1–10 (v1–9) and 2 Sam 23:8–39 (v10–41)** — where the Hebrew is the same, the wording is that of the finished Samuel verse; every difference named below is a difference in the Hebrew of Chronicles. Nothing is taken over from Samuel.
- **"Then all Israel gathered themselves to David" → "And all Israel gathered to David"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּקָּבְצוּ כָל־יִשְׂרָאֵל; Samuel has "all the tribes of Israel came … and they said, saying".
- **"moreover in time past, even when Saul was king" → "Both yesterday and the day before, also when Saul was king"** (v2) — Hebrew גַּם־תְּמוֹל גַּם־שִׁלְשׁוֹם גַּם בִּהְיוֹת, three times גַּם; "yesterday and the day before" is the Hebrew for "formerly". Samuel has "king over us". **"feed my people" → "shepherd my people"** — Hebrew רָעָה. **"ruler" → "leader"** — Hebrew נָגִיד. Chronicles has "Yahweh your Elohim" and "over my people Israel" (Samuel: "Yahweh", "over Israel").
- **"made a covenant with them" → "cut a covenant for them"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיִּכְרֹת לָהֶם … בְּרִית. **"by Samuel" → "by the hand of Samuel"** — Hebrew בְּיַד. The last clause, "according to the word of Yahweh by the hand of Samuel", is not in Samuel; Samuel has "King David".
- **"where the Jebusites were, the inhabitants of the land" → "and there was the Jebusite, the inhabitants of the land"** (v4) — Hebrew וְשָׁם הַיְבוּסִי יֹשְׁבֵי הָאָרֶץ: the name of the people is singular, "inhabitants" plural. Samuel has "the king and his men went … to the Jebusite, the inhabitant of the land"; "that is Jebus" is only in Chronicles.
- **"Thou shalt not come hither"** (v5) — Samuel's words about the blind and the lame are not in Chronicles. **"Nevertheless David took the castle of Zion" → "And David captured the stronghold of Zion"** — Hebrew וַיִּלְכֹּד; מְצֻדָה "stronghold".
- **"Whosoever smiteth the Jebusites first shall be chief and captain" → "Everyone who strikes a Jebusite first shall become head and commander"** (v6) — Hebrew כָּל־מַכֵּה יְבוּסִי בָּרִאשׁוֹנָה יִהְיֶה לְרֹאשׁ וּלְשָׂר: רֹאשׁ "head", שַׂר "prince", of an army "commander". The saying and Joab's going up are only in Chronicles; Samuel has the saying about the water channel.
- **"castle" → "stronghold"** (v7) — Hebrew מְצָד, another form of the word of v5 and v16. **"they called it"** — Hebrew קָרְאוּ־לוֹ, plural; Samuel has "he called it".
- **"from Millo round about" → "from the Millo and as far as the surrounding part"** (v8) — Hebrew מִן־הַמִּלּוֹא וְעַד־הַסָּבִיב, literally "and to the around"; the sense is not certain. Samuel has "from the Millo and inward".
- **"Joab repaired the rest of the city" → "Joab let the rest of the city live"** (v8) — Hebrew יְחַיֶּה אֶת־שְׁאָר הָעִיר, the verb "make live, let live" (Exod 22:18; Deut 32:39). It is commonly taken as "restored, rebuilt" (so the KJV and the earlier text); it may also say that he spared the rest of the city. The Hebrew word is kept and the sense left open. The clause is only in Chronicles.
- **"So David waxed greater and greater: for the LORD of hosts was with him" → "And David went on growing greater, and Yahweh of Hosts was with him"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיהוָה, "and", not "for". Samuel has "Yahweh the Elohim of Hosts".
- **"the chief of the mighty men whom David had, who strengthened themselves with him" → "the heads of the mighty ones who were David's, who strengthened themselves with him"** (v10) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי הַגִּבּוֹרִים; הַמִּתְחַזְּקִים עִמּוֹ, "who made themselves strong with him": held firmly with him. "Warriors", "strongly supported" (the earlier text) are not the words. **"concerning Israel"** — Hebrew עַל־יִשְׂרָאֵל; it may also be joined to "to make him king": "king … over Israel". The verse is only in Chronicles.
- **"the number of the mighty men" kept** (v11) — Hebrew מִסְפַּר "number" (Samuel: "the names"). **"an Hachmonite" → "the son of Hachmoni"** — Hebrew בֶּן־חַכְמוֹנִי (as 27:32). **"the chief of the captains" → "head of the officers"** — the qere הַשָּׁלִישִׁים "the officers" (literally "third men", Exod 14:7) is translated; the written text has השלושים "the thirty". **"he lifted up his spear against three hundred slain by him" → "he roused his spear against three hundred slain"** — Hebrew עוֹרֵר אֶת־חֲנִיתוֹ; "whom he killed" is not written. Samuel names the man Josheb-basshebeth a Tahchemonite and has eight hundred.
- **"who was one of the three mighties" → "he was among the three mighty ones"** (v12) — Hebrew הוּא בִּשְׁלוֹשָׁה הַגִּבֹּרִים.
- **"a parcel of ground full of barley" → "a portion of the field full of barley"** (v13) — Hebrew חֶלְקַת הַשָּׂדֶה. In Samuel the field (of lentils) belongs to the deed of Shammah, the third man, who is not named in Chronicles; here the verbs of v14 are plural ("they took their stand"), in Samuel singular. Pas-dammim is named only here.
- **"the LORD saved them by a great deliverance" → "Yahweh saved with a great salvation"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיּוֹשַׁע יְהוָה תְּשׁוּעָה גְדוֹלָה, verb and noun of one root; "them" is not written; "victory" (the earlier text) is not the word. Samuel has "worked a great salvation".
- **"to the rock"** (v15) — Hebrew עַל־הַצֻּר, literally "upon the rock"; Samuel has "at harvest". **"the host of the Philistines encamped" → "the camp of the Philistines was camping"** — Hebrew מַחֲנֵה … חֹנָה (Samuel: "the band").
- **"the Philistines' garrison" → "the garrison of the Philistines"** (v16) — Hebrew נְצִיב, uncertain: a garrison, or a prefect, or a pillar. Samuel has another word, "outpost".
- **"David longed" → "David craved"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיִּתְאָו (the written form and the qere are two spellings of one verb). **"Oh that one would give me drink" → "Who will give me water to drink"** — Hebrew מִי יַשְׁקֵנִי. **"the well of Bethlehem, that is at the gate" → "the pit of Bethlehem that is in the gate"** (v17, 18) — Hebrew בּוֹר "pit, cistern"; בַּשַּׁעַר "in the gate".
- **"brake through the host" → "split into the camp"** (v18) — Hebrew וַיִּבְקְעוּ … בְּמַחֲנֵה. "The three": Samuel has "the three mighty ones". "David was not willing": the name is not in Samuel. "Poured it out" is here the piel וַיְנַסֵּךְ, in Samuel the hifil of the same verb.
- **"My God forbid it me, that I should do this thing" → "Far be it from me, from my Elohim, from doing this!"** (v19) — Hebrew חָלִילָה לִּי מֵאֱלֹהַי מֵעֲשׂוֹת זֹאת, literally "a profane thing to me, from my Elohim" (as 1 Sam 24:6 "from Yahweh"). The earlier text made it an address ("my Elohim"). Samuel has the address "Yahweh".
- **"shall I drink the blood of these men that have put their lives in jeopardy? for with the jeopardy of their lives they brought it" → "Shall I drink the blood of these men with their souls? For with their souls they brought it"** (v19) — Hebrew בְנַפְשׁוֹתָם, twice: "with their souls", that is, at the price of their lives; "risk" and "jeopardy" are not written. In Samuel the sentence breaks off without a verb; Chronicles has "shall I drink" and the second clause.
- **"Abishai"** (v20) — written אַבְשַׁי, "Abshai", here; the token is the same. **"chief of the three"** → "head of the three" — Hebrew רֹאשׁ הַשְּׁלוֹשָׁה; the earlier text had "the Thirty". **"and had a name among the three"** — the qere וְלוֹ "and to him" is translated; the written text has וְלֹא "and not": "and he had no name among the three".
- **"Of the three, he was more honourable than the two" → "Above the three, among the two, he was honored"** (v21) — Hebrew מִן־הַשְּׁלוֹשָׁה בַשְּׁנַיִם נִכְבָּד, word for word; what "among the two" (or "in the two") means is not known: "doubly" (the earlier text), "of the second rank", "more than the two" are guesses. Samuel has הֲכִי "indeed" in its place. **"captain" → "commander"** — Hebrew שַׂר. **"he attained not to the first three" → "as far as the three he did not come"** — Hebrew וְעַד־הַשְּׁלוֹשָׁה לֹא־בָא.
- **"the son of a valiant man of Kabzeel, who had done many acts" → "the son of a man of ability, great in deeds, from Kabzeel"** (v22) — Hebrew בֶּן־אִישׁ־חַיִל רַב־פְּעָלִים מִן־קַבְצְאֵל. The name is written in the short form בְּנָיָה here and without "And"; the token is the same.
- **"two lionlike men of Moab" → "the two Ariels of Moab"** (v22) — Hebrew שְׁנֵי אֲרִיאֵל מוֹאָב; the meaning of *ariel* is not known ("lion of El"? champions? altar hearths?); the word is kept. "Lion-hearted men" (the earlier text) was a gloss. **"a lion in a pit in a snowy day" → "the lion in the midst of the pit on the day of the snow"** — the Hebrew has the article three times.
- **"an Egyptian, a man of great stature, five cubits high" → "the Egyptian man, a man of stature, five cubits"** (v23) — Hebrew אֶת־הָאִישׁ הַמִּצְרִי אִישׁ מִדָּה חָמֵשׁ בָּאַמָּה, literally "a man of measure, five by the cubit"; "great" and "high" are not written. Samuel has "an Egyptian man, a man of appearance" and neither the measure nor the weavers' beam. **"with a staff" → "with the rod"** — Hebrew בַּשֵּׁבֶט. **"plucked the spear" → "robbed the spear"** — Hebrew וַיִּגְזֹל.
- **"Behold, he was honourable among the thirty" → "Above the thirty, behold, he was honored"** (v25) — Hebrew מִן־הַשְּׁלוֹשִׁים הִנּוֹ נִכְבָּד הוּא; "behold" is not in Samuel. **"set him over his guard" → "set him over his bodyguard"** — Hebrew עַל־מִשְׁמַעְתּוֹ (Samuel: אֶל "to"); מִשְׁמַעַת, literally "obedience", those who obey his call.
- **"Also the valiant men of the armies were" → "And the mighty ones of the forces"** (v26) — Hebrew וְגִבּוֹרֵי הַחֲיָלִים, the plural of חַיִל "ability, force"; no verb. Samuel has "was among the thirty" instead.
- **The list** (v26–41) — every name stands as Chronicles prints it; where Samuel has another name or form, it is not brought in: Shammoth the Harorite (Samuel: Shammah the Harodite, and Elika, who is not here), Helez the Pelonite (Paltite), Sibbecai (Mebunnai), Ilai (Zalmon), Heled (Heleb), Ithai (Ittai), Hurai (Hiddai), Abiel (Abi-albon), the Baharumite (Barhumite), "the sons of Hashem the Gizonite; Jonathan the son of Shagee the Hararite" ("the sons of Jashen; Jonathan; Shammah the Hararite"), Sachar (Sharar), "Eliphal the son of Ur; Hepher the Mecherathite; Ahijah the Pelonite" ("Eliphelet the son of Ahasbai, the son of the Maacathite; Eliam the son of Ahithophel the Gilonite"), Hezro (qere Hezrai), "Naarai the son of Ezbai" ("Paarai the Arbite"), "Joel the brother of Nathan; Mibhar the son of Hagri" ("Igal the son of Nathan, from Zobah; Bani the Gadite"), the Berothite (Beerothite). Samuel's total, "thirty-seven", is not in Chronicles.
- **"of the children of Benjamin" → "of the sons of Benjamin"** (v31) — Hebrew בְּנֵי. **"of the brooks of Gaash" → "from the wadis of Gaash"** (v32) — Hebrew נַחֲלֵי.
- **"the son of Hagri"** (v38) — Hebrew בֶּן־הַגְרִי; it may also be read "the son of a Hagrite".
- **"armourbearer" → "weapon-bearer"** (v39) — Hebrew נֹשֵׂא כְּלֵי, "the bearer of the gear of".
- **"a captain of the Reubenites, and thirty with him" → "a head of the Reubenites, and with him thirty"** (v42) — Hebrew רֹאשׁ לָראוּבֵנִי וְעָלָיו שְׁלוֹשִׁים: עָלָיו is literally "upon him" ("beside him", or "over him").
- **"Jeiel"** (v44) — the qere; the written text has "Jeuel". **"the Mahavite"** (v46) — Hebrew הַמַּחֲוִים, a plural form; what it names is not known. **"the Mezobaite"** (v47) — Hebrew הַמְּצֹבָיָה, a form found only here.
- **"Antothite" → "Anathothite"** (v28). **"Mesobaite" → "Mezobaite"** (v47).

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Sam 5, 23** — Chronicler combines David's coronation, Jerusalem capture, and the list of the mighty ones.
- **"Your bone and flesh"** — tribal blood-kinship appeal.
- **Jebus taken via the water channel (2 Sam 5:8) or Joab first up (here)** — Chronicler highlights Joab's initiative, which makes him head.
- **"City of David"** — Ophel ridge; southeast Jerusalem; archaeologically identified.
- **Millo** — supporting terraces, stepped-stone structure.
- **The list of the mighty ones** — three tiers: the three, the thirty, the broader forces. Chronicler's list expands 2 Sam 23.
- **Uriah the Hittite** (v41) — preserved in the roll of honor despite David's later sin against him.
- **Additional names** (v41b-47) — Chronicler adds names not in 2 Sam 23; perhaps later additions, or a second company.
- **Bethlehem water exploit** — exemplifies sacrificial loyalty; David's refusal to drink honors it as holy libation.
