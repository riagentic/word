## Changes

- **"Sheth / Henoch / Jered" → "Seth / Enoch / Jared"** — standardized to common English forms.
- **"Dodanim" → "Rodanim"** (v7) — Qere; likely = Rhodians (people of Rhodes). LXX, Samaritan Pentateuch, and many MT MSS read *Rodanim*.
- **"Zephi / Shephi / Alian" — retained** (v36, 40) — Gen 36 parallels read "Zepho," "Shepho," "Alvan" respectively; Chronicler's spellings differ.
- **"dukes" → "chiefs"** (v51-54) — *allupim*; tribal clan-chieftains, not European "dukes."
- **"begat" → "fathered"** (throughout).

### Notes
- **Chronicler's opening** — Universal history compressed to name-lists. Adam → Israel in 54 verses. Priestly genealogical scope of humanity.
- **Parallel material** — draws from Gen 5, 10, 11, 25, 36. Chronicler assumes reader has access; abbreviates commentary.
- **Table of Nations** (v5-23) reproduces Gen 10 with minor variants.
- **Esau/Edom lineage** — extensive; Edom has special Chronistic interest as Israel's relative-enemy.
- **Edomite kings "before any king over Israel"** — ironic: genealogy assumes Davidic kingship not yet extant at listing-time, even though Chronicler writes post-exile.
- **Hadad's wife** (v50) — only woman named in the king-list; ancestral naming pattern.
