# 1 Chronicles 25

[1] David and the commanders of the army set apart for the service some of the sons of Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun, who prophesied with lyres, harps, and cymbals. The number of the workers according to their service was:

[2] of the sons of Asaph: Zaccur, Joseph, Nethaniah, and Asarelah, the sons of Asaph under the direction of Asaph, who prophesied under the direction of the king;

[3] of Jeduthun: the sons of Jeduthun — Gedaliah, Zeri, Jeshaiah, Shimei, Hashabiah, and Mattithiah — six, under the direction of their father Jeduthun, who prophesied with the lyre for giving thanks and praise to Yahweh;

[4] of Heman: the sons of Heman — Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, Jerimoth, Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamti-ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, and Mahazioth.

[5] All these were sons of Heman the king's seer, by the words of *Elohim*, to lift up the horn. *Elohim* had given Heman fourteen sons and three daughters.

[6] All these were under the direction of their father for song in the house of Yahweh — with cymbals, harps, and lyres for the service of the house of *Elohim* — under the king's direction. Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman

[7] and their brothers, all trained in song for Yahweh, skilled ones — their number was two hundred eighty-eight.

[8] They cast lots for their duties, small and great alike, teacher and pupil alike.

[9] The first lot fell to Asaph, for Joseph; the second to Gedaliah, he, his sons, and his brothers — twelve;

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"psalteries" → "harps"** (v1, 6) — *nevalim*.
- **"harps" → "lyres"** (v1, 6) — *kinorot*.
- **"under the hands of" → "under the direction of"** (v2, 3, 6) — *al yedei*.
- **"ward against ward" → "for their duties, small and great alike"** (v8) — Hebrew *mishmeret le'ummat*; duty-shift alignment.
- **"cunning" → "skilled"** (v7).
- **"Azareel" → "Azarel"** (v18).

### Notes
- **Prophetic music** — Chronicler's distinctive view: temple music is prophetic. "Prophesied" with instruments = Spirit-led liturgical composition. Cf. 1 Sam 10:5.
- **Twenty-four musical courses** — parallel to priestly courses. Matching weekly rotations.
- **288 musicians** = 24 courses × 12. Symmetry.
- **Last nine sons of Heman** (v4) — Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamti-ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, Mahazioth — arrange in sequence and form a cryptic psalm-prayer: "Be gracious to me, Yah, be gracious to me; you are my God; I have magnified, and raised up help; sitting in distress, I have spoken abundantly, visions." Many commentators see a prayer embedded in the names.
- **"The king's seer"** (v5) — Heman as prophetic figure close to David. Titular status: royal prophet.
- **"Teacher as pupil" casting lots** (v8) — no seniority privilege; equal meritocracy in worship.
