## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"psalteries" → "harps"** (v1, 6) — *nevalim*.
- **"harps" → "lyres"** (v1, 6) — *kinorot*.
- **"under the hands of" → "under the direction of"** (v2, 3, 6) — *al yedei*.
- **"ward against ward" → "for their duties, small and great alike"** (v8) — Hebrew *mishmeret le'ummat*; duty-shift alignment.
- **"cunning" → "skilled"** (v7).
- **"Azareel" → "Azarel"** (v18).

### Notes
- **Prophetic music** — Chronicler's distinctive view: temple music is prophetic. "Prophesied" with instruments = Spirit-led liturgical composition. Cf. 1 Sam 10:5.
- **Twenty-four musical courses** — parallel to priestly courses. Matching weekly rotations.
- **288 musicians** = 24 courses × 12. Symmetry.
- **Last nine sons of Heman** (v4) — Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamti-ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, Mahazioth — arrange in sequence and form a cryptic psalm-prayer: "Be gracious to me, Yah, be gracious to me; you are my God; I have magnified, and raised up help; sitting in distress, I have spoken abundantly, visions." Many commentators see a prayer embedded in the names.
- **"The king's seer"** (v5) — Heman as prophetic figure close to David. Titular status: royal prophet.
- **"Teacher as pupil" casting lots** (v8) — no seniority privilege; equal meritocracy in worship.
