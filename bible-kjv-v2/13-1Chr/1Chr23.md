## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"princes" → "leaders"** (v2) — *sarim*.
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (v5).
- **"tabernacle" → "dwelling"** (v26) — *mishkan*.
- **"tabernacle of the congregation" → "Tent of Meeting"** (v32).
- **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** (v29) — *minchah*.
- **"fried" → "mixed offering"** (v29) — *murbach*; possibly oil-mixed offering.
- **"set feasts" → "appointed feasts"** (v31) — *mo'adim*.
- **"to dwell in Jerusalem for ever" → "he has dwelt in Jerusalem forever"** (v25) — Hebrew *va-yishkon be-Yerushalaim*; subject is Yahweh.
- **"chief of the fathers" → "heads of fathers' houses"** throughout.

### Notes
- **Chronicler's unique priestly material** — chapters 23-27 detail David's organizational reforms. No parallel in Samuel/Kings. Presents David as founder of temple structure, not just temple site.
- **Levite age-change** — Num 4 set Levitical service at 30; Num 8 at 25. David lowers to 20 (v24, 27), adapting for stationary temple (no need for wilderness-age vigor).
- **38,000 Levites** — post-census organization. 24k + 6k + 4k + 4k = 38k.
- **Four functions**: temple-work supervision, legal administration, gatekeeping, music. Music explicit: David made the instruments.
- **Moses's descendants among Levites** (v14) — Moses's line folded into the tribe, not a separate priestly line. Aaron elevated above Moses for priestly purposes.
- **No more carrying tabernacle** (v26) — shifts Levitical role from porterage to temple-service. Theological statement: rest has arrived; motion is done.
- **Fixed worship calendar** (v31) — daily, weekly, monthly, festal. Complete liturgical cycle.
