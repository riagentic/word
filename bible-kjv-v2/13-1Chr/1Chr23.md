## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v14, 28 with the article.
- **"So when David was old and full of days" → "And David was old and sated with days"** (v1) — Hebrew וְדָוִיד זָקֵן וְשָׂבַע יָמִים, two plain clauses; שָׂבַע "be sated" (Gen 25:8). "Made … king" is וַיַּמְלֵךְ, literally "made reign".
- **"princes"** (v2) — Hebrew שָׂרִים; "with" before the priests is not in the Hebrew.
- **"by their polls, man by man" → "head by head, of men"** (v3) — Hebrew לְגֻלְגְּלֹתָם לִגְבָרִים, literally "by their skulls, for the men" (the census words of Num 1:2); גֶּבֶר is the word "mighty man". The age stands as the Hebrew has it, **thirty years** here and **twenty years** in v24, 27; "thirty years old" is literally "from a son of thirty years".
- **"Of which, twenty and four thousand were to set forward the work" → "Of these, to oversee the work …, twenty-four thousand"** (v4, 5) — the Hebrew has no verb and no "said David"; "I have made" in v5 shows that David speaks, so the two verses stand in quotation marks. לְנַצֵּחַ עַל "to oversee". **"porters" → "gatekeepers"**. **"praised the LORD with the instruments which I made, said David, to praise therewith" → "praising Yahweh with the instruments that I have made for praising"** — Hebrew מְהַלְלִים, a participle; כֵּלִים is the general word "utensils, articles", here of instruments of music.
- **"divided them into courses among the sons of Levi, namely" → "divided them into divisions for the sons of Levi: for"** (v6) — Hebrew מַחְלְקוֹת "divisions", from the verb "divide"; the verb is pointed וַיֶּחָלְקֵם, an unusual form, understood as "and he divided them" (so also 24:3). לְ "for" stands before "the sons of Levi" and before "Gershon" only.
- **"Of the Gershonites"** (v7) — Hebrew לַגֵּרְשֻׁנִּי, singular with the article, "for the Gershonite", of the whole clan.
- **"the chief" → "the head"** (v8, 11, 16–20) — Hebrew הָרֹאשׁ; in v8 it stands before the name. In v19, 20 the earlier verse had "the first"; the Hebrew has the same word, "the head".
- **"Shelomith"** (v9) — the reading tradition (qere) שְׁלוֹמִית; the written text (ketiv) has שלמות, "Shelomoth". **"the chief of the fathers of Laadan" → "the heads of the fathers for Ladan"** — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי הָאָבוֹת לְלַעְדָּן; "houses" is not in the Hebrew here or in v24. The sons of Shimei in v9 are counted to Ladan; v10 gives another list under the same name.
- **"Zina"** (v10) and **"Zizah"** (v11) — the name is spelled זִינָא in v10 and זִיזָה in v11; each stands as printed.
- **"had not many sons; therefore they were in one reckoning, according to their father's house" → "did not have many sons, and they became a father's house, one appointment"** (v11) — Hebrew לֹא־הִרְבּוּ בָנִים, literally "did not make sons many"; וַיִּהְיוּ לְבֵית אָב לִפְקֻדָּה אֶחָת. פְּקֻדָּה, from פָּקַד "number, appoint", is here the class or duty to which a house is appointed (so 24:3, 19); in Num 3:32 the same word is "oversight", which would not give the sense here.
- **"Aaron was separated, that he should sanctify the most holy things" → "Aaron was separated, to sanctify him as most holy"** (v13) — Hebrew לְהַקְדִּישׁוֹ קֹדֶשׁ קָדָשִׁים, literally "to sanctify him, holiness of holinesses". The suffix may be the object ("to sanctify him as most holy") or the subject ("that he should sanctify the most holy things"); the verse takes it as the object. **"to burn incense" → "to make smoke"** — Hebrew לְהַקְטִיר without an object: incense or the offerings on the altar. "As" is supplied.
- **"his sons were named of the tribe of Levi" → "his sons were called by the tribe of Levi"** (v14) — Hebrew יִקָּרְאוּ עַל־שֵׁבֶט הַלֵּוִי, literally "were called upon the tribe of the Levite": they were counted to the tribe, not to the priests. The verse opens with "And Moses, the man of Elohim" standing alone.
- **"Of the sons of Gershom, Shebuel was the chief" → "The sons of Gershom: Shebuel the head"** (v16, 18–20) — Hebrew בְּנֵי, "the sons of", though one name follows. The name is שְׁבוּאֵל here and שׁוּבָאֵל in 24:20; each stands as printed.
- **"Rehabiah"** (v17) — spelled רְחַבְיָה here and רְחַבְיָהוּ in 24:21. **"were very many" → "multiplied exceedingly"** — Hebrew רָבוּ לְמָעְלָה, literally "multiplied upward".
- **"their brethren the sons of Kish took them" → "the sons of Kish, their brothers, took them as wives"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיִּשָּׂאוּם, "took them" (the verb of taking a wife); "as wives" is supplied. "Brothers" is said of kinsmen: they were cousins.
- **"These were the sons of Levi after the house of their fathers; even the chief of the fathers, as they were counted by number of names by their polls, that did the work" → "… by their fathers' houses, the heads of the fathers by those of them who were numbered, by the number of names, head by head, doers of the work"** (v24) — the census words of Num 1:2; לִפְקוּדֵיהֶם "by their numbered ones"; עֹשֵׂה הַמְּלָאכָה is singular, "doer of the work", said of the whole.
- **"that they may dwell in Jerusalem for ever" → "and he abides in Jerusalem forever"** (v25) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁכֹּן, "and he abode / has come to abide"; the subject is Yahweh; שָׁכַן is the root of "dwelling" in v26. עַד־לְעוֹלָם is a doubled form, "unto forever".
- **"And also unto the Levites; they shall no more carry the tabernacle, nor any vessels of it" → "And also for the Levites there is no carrying of the dwelling and all its utensils"** (v26) — Hebrew אֵין־לָשֵׂאת, "there is no carrying"; "no more" is not in the Hebrew. Where David's words end is not marked; the verse closes them here.
- **"the Levites were numbered from twenty years old and above" → "they are the number of the sons of Levi, from twenty years old and upward"** (v27) — Hebrew הֵמָּה מִסְפַּר בְּנֵי־לֵוִי, with no verb of counting. "The last words of David" (דִּבְרֵי … הָאַחֲרֹנִים) may also be "the last acts / matters of David".
- **"their office was to wait on the sons of Aaron" → "their station was at the hand of the sons of Aaron"** (v28) — Hebrew מַעֲמָדָם לְיַד, literally "their standing place at the hand of"; "was" is supplied. **"in the purifying of all holy things" → "over the cleansing of every holy thing"** — Hebrew טָהֳרַת לְכָל־קֹדֶשׁ. "Work" here is מַעֲשֶׂה; in v4, 24 it is מְלָאכָה.
- **"shewbread" → "the bread of the arrangement"** (v29) — Hebrew לֶחֶם הַמַּעֲרֶכֶת, the bread set out in order (Lev 24:6); elsewhere "the bread of the face" (לֶחֶם פָּנִים). **"meat offering" → "grain offering"**. **"unleavened cakes" → "the wafers of unleavened bread"**. **"that which is baked in the pan, and that which is fried" → "the griddle, and what is stirred"** — Hebrew מַחֲבַת "griddle" (Lev 2:5); מֻרְבֶּכֶת (Lev 6:21), meaning uncertain: well mixed or soaked. **"all manner of measure and size" → "every capacity and measure"** — Hebrew מְשׂוּרָה וּמִדָּה (Lev 19:35).
- **"every morning" → "morning by morning"** (v30) — Hebrew בַּבֹּקֶר בַּבֹּקֶר. "Give thanks" is הוֹדָה.
- **"to offer all burnt sacrifices … in the sabbaths, in the new moons, and on the set feasts" → "for every offering up of burnt offerings … for the Shabbats, for the months and for the appointed times"** (v31) — Hebrew לְכֹל הַעֲלוֹת עֹלוֹת; חֳדָשִׁים is the word "months": the new moon, the first day of each, is meant (Num 28:11, 14); מוֹעֲדִים "appointed times" (Lev 23:2). **"according to the order commanded unto them" → "according to the ordinance concerning them"** — Hebrew כְּמִשְׁפָּט עֲלֵיהֶם.
- **"And that they should keep" → "And they were to keep"** (v32) — Hebrew וְשָׁמְרוּ, a form that states a standing duty. **"tabernacle of the congregation" → "tent of meeting"**. **"the charge of the holy place" → "the charge of the Holy"** — Hebrew הַקֹּדֶשׁ; "place" is not in the Hebrew (Num 3:28).

### Notes
- **Chronicler's unique priestly material** — chapters 23-27 detail David's organizational reforms. No parallel in Samuel/Kings. Presents David as founder of temple structure, not just temple site.
- **Levite age-change** — v3 counts from 30 (as Num 4); v24, 27 from 20, "by the last words of David". Num 8 has 25. The text gives both numbers and does not reconcile them.
- **38,000 Levites** — 24k + 6k + 4k + 4k = 38k.
- **Four functions**: temple-work supervision, legal administration, gatekeeping, music. Music explicit: David made the instruments.
- **Moses's descendants among Levites** (v14) — Moses's line folded into the tribe, not a separate priestly line. Aaron elevated above Moses for priestly purposes.
- **No carrying of the dwelling** (v26) — shifts Levitical role from porterage to temple-service. Theological statement: rest has arrived; motion is done.
- **Fixed worship calendar** (v31) — daily, weekly, monthly, festal. Complete liturgical cycle.
