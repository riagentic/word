# 1 Chronicles 23

[1] When David was old and full of days, he made Solomon his son king over Israel.

[2] He gathered together all the leaders of Israel with the priests and the Levites.

[3] The Levites, thirty years old and upward, were counted; their number, man by man, was thirty-eight thousand.

[4] "Of these," said David, "twenty-four thousand are to oversee the work of the house of Yahweh; six thousand are to be officers and judges;

[5] four thousand are gatekeepers; and four thousand are to praise Yahweh with the instruments that I have made for praise."

[6] David organized them in divisions corresponding to the sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

[7] Of the Gershonites: Ladan and Shimei.

[8] The sons of Ladan: Jehiel the chief, Zetham, and Joel — three.

[9] The sons of Shimei: Shelomoth, Haziel, and Haran — three. These were the heads of the fathers' houses of Ladan.

[10] The sons of Shimei: Jahath, Zina, Jeush, and Beriah. These four were the sons of Shimei.

[11] Jahath was the chief, and Zizah the second; but Jeush and Beriah did not have many sons, so they were counted as one fathers' house, one office.

[12] The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel — four.

[13] The sons of Amram: Aaron and Moses. Aaron was set apart to consecrate the most holy things, he and his sons forever, to burn incense before Yahweh, to minister to him, and to bless in his name forever.

[14] As for Moses the man of *Elohim*, his sons were reckoned among the tribe of Levi.

[15] The sons of Moses: Gershom and Eliezer.

[16] Of the sons of Gershom: Shebuel the chief.

[17] The sons of Eliezer: Rehabiah the chief. Eliezer had no other sons, but the sons of Rehabiah were very many.

[18] Of the sons of Izhar: Shelomith the chief.

[19] Of the sons of Hebron: Jeriah the first, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, Jekameam the fourth.

[20] Of the sons of Uzziel: Micah the first, Isshiah the second.

[21] The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Mahli: Eleazar and Kish.

[22] Eleazar died and had no sons, but only daughters; their kinsmen, the sons of Kish, took them as wives.

[23] The sons of Mushi: Mahli, Eder, and Jeremoth — three.

[24] These were the sons of Levi according to their fathers' houses — the heads of fathers' houses as they were counted by name, individually, who did the work for the service of the house of Yahweh, from twenty years old and upward.

[25] For David said, "Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel has given rest to his people; he has dwelt in Jerusalem forever.

[26] Also, as for the Levites, they no longer need to carry the dwelling or any of the vessels for its service."

[27] For by David's last instructions, the Levites were numbered from twenty years old and upward.

[28] For their office was to assist the sons of Aaron for the service of the house of Yahweh — to have charge of the courts, the chambers, the cleansing of all holy things, and any work of the service of the house of *Elohim*;

[29] for the bread of the Presence for the fine flour of the grain offering, for the unleavened wafers, for the baked offering, for the mixed offering, and for all measures of quantity and size;

[30] and to stand every morning to thank and praise Yahweh, and likewise at evening;

[31] and to offer all burnt offerings to Yahweh on the Sabbaths, new moons, and appointed feasts, according to the number prescribed for them, continually before Yahweh.

[32] Thus they kept the charge of the Tent of Meeting, the charge of the sanctuary, and the charge of the sons of Aaron their brothers, in the service of the house of Yahweh.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"princes" → "leaders"** (v2) — *sarim*.
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (v5).
- **"tabernacle" → "dwelling"** (v26) — *mishkan*.
- **"tabernacle of the congregation" → "Tent of Meeting"** (v32).
- **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** (v29) — *minchah*.
- **"fried" → "mixed offering"** (v29) — *murbach*; possibly oil-mixed offering.
- **"set feasts" → "appointed feasts"** (v31) — *mo'adim*.
- **"to dwell in Jerusalem for ever" → "he has dwelt in Jerusalem forever"** (v25) — Hebrew *va-yishkon be-Yerushalaim*; subject is Yahweh.
- **"chief of the fathers" → "heads of fathers' houses"** throughout.

### Notes
- **Chronicler's unique priestly material** — chapters 23-27 detail David's organizational reforms. No parallel in Samuel/Kings. Presents David as founder of temple structure, not just temple site.
- **Levite age-change** — Num 4 set Levitical service at 30; Num 8 at 25. David lowers to 20 (v24, 27), adapting for stationary temple (no need for wilderness-age vigor).
- **38,000 Levites** — post-census organization. 24k + 6k + 4k + 4k = 38k.
- **Four functions**: temple-work supervision, legal administration, gatekeeping, music. Music explicit: David made the instruments.
- **Moses's descendants among Levites** (v14) — Moses's line folded into the tribe, not a separate priestly line. Aaron elevated above Moses for priestly purposes.
- **No more carrying tabernacle** (v26) — shifts Levitical role from porterage to temple-service. Theological statement: rest has arrived; motion is done.
- **Fixed worship calendar** (v31) — daily, weekly, monthly, festal. Complete liturgical cycle.
