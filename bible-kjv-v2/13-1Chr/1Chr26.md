## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v20, 32 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (throughout) — Hebrew שֹׁעֵר, from שַׁעַר "gate".
- **"Concerning the divisions of the porters" → "For the divisions of the gatekeepers"** (v1) — Hebrew לְמַחְלְקוֹת לְשֹׁעֲרִים, both with לְ "to / for". **"of the sons of Asaph"** — so the Masoretic Text; 9:19 has "Ebiasaph" for this Korahite line. The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **Name forms** (v2, 11, 14, 25, 30) — the Hebrew prints the long forms: זְכַרְיָהוּ *Zecharyahu*, זְבַדְיָהוּ *Zevadyahu*, שֶׁלֶמְיָהוּ *Shelemyahu* (v14; the same man as *Meshelemyahu*, v1, 2, 9), יְשַׁעְיָהוּ *Yeshayahu*, חֲשַׁבְיָהוּ *Chashavyahu*.
- **"for God blessed him" → "for *Elohim* had blessed him"** (v5) — "him" is Obed-edom (13:14).
- **"that ruled throughout the house of their father" → "the rulers of the house of their father"** (v6) — Hebrew הַמִּמְשָׁלִים, a noun ("the dominions", that is, the ruling ones), with לְבֵית אֲבִיהֶם, "their father's house" in the singular.
- **"mighty men of valour" → "mighty ones of ability"** (v6, 31) — Hebrew גִּבּוֹרֵי חַיִל. **"strong men" / "able men" / "men of valour" → "sons of ability" / "a man of ability"** (v7, 8, 9, 30, 32) — Hebrew בְּנֵי־חַיִל, אִישׁ־חַיִל: one word, חַיִל, in all of them.
- **"whose brethren were strong men, Elihu, and Semachiah" → "his brothers, sons of ability, were Elihu and Semachiah"** (v7) — Hebrew "Othni and Rephael and Obed, Elzabad, his brothers, sons of ability, Elihu and Semachiah": no "and" before Elzabad, and "his brothers" in the singular; how the names are grouped is not certain.
- **"able men for strength for the service" → "each a man of ability, with power for the service"** (v8) — Hebrew אִישׁ־חַיִל בַּכֹּחַ לַעֲבֹדָה; "each" carries the singular. **"of Obed-edom" → "belonging to Obed-edom"** — Hebrew לְעֹבֵד אֱדֹם.
- **"Simri the chief" → "Shimri the head"** (v10) — Hebrew הָרֹאשׁ. **"for though he was not the firstborn, yet his father made him the chief" → "for he was not the firstborn, and his father set him as head"** — Hebrew כִּי לֹא־הָיָה בְכוֹר, which may also be read "for there was no firstborn"; "though … yet" is not written.
- **"Among these were the divisions of the porters, even among the chief men, having wards one against another" → "To these, the divisions of the gatekeepers, to the heads of the mighty men, belonged watches alongside their brothers"** (v12) — Hebrew לְאֵלֶּה … לְרָאשֵׁי הַגְּבָרִים מִשְׁמָרוֹת לְעֻמַּת אֲחֵיהֶם; גֶּבֶר is "mighty man"; מִשְׁמֶרֶת is "charge", of guards "watch"; "belonged" is supplied.
- **"as well the small as the great, according to the house of their fathers, for every gate" → "the small as the great, by their fathers' houses, for each gate"** (v13) — Hebrew לְשַׁעַר וָשָׁעַר, literally "for gate and gate". **"cast lots" kept** (v13, 14) — Hebrew הִפִּיל גּוֹרָלוֹת, literally "made lots fall"; in v14 the lot "fell" and "came out".
- **"eastward" / "northward" / "southward" / "westward" → "to the east" …** (v14–18) — Hebrew מִזְרָחָה, צָפוֹנָה, נֶגְבָּה, לַמַּעֲרָב; "south" is literally "toward the Negev".
- **"a wise counsellor" → "a counselor with insight"** (v14) — Hebrew יוֹעֵץ בְּשֶׂכֶל.
- **"the house of Asuppim" → "the house of the stores"** (v15, 17) — Hebrew בֵּית הָאֲסֻפִּים, from אָסַף "gather": "the house of the gatherings". The word is found only here and in Neh 12:25; what building is meant is not certain.
- **"Shuppim" kept** (v16) — so the Masoretic Text; the name is not in the list of v1–11 and is often taken as a miswriting of the last word of v15 (הָאֲסֻפִּים). The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"the gate Shallecheth, by the causeway of the going up, ward against ward" → "the gate of Shallecheth, on the highway that goes up; watch alongside watch"** (v16) — Hebrew שַׁעַר שַׁלֶּכֶת, a gate named only here; the name may mean "casting out", and where it stood is not known. מְסִלָּה is a raised road, "highway". מִשְׁמָר לְעֻמַּת מִשְׁמָר: לְעֻמַּת "alongside", as in v12.
- **"Eastward were six Levites" → "To the east the Levites were six"** (v17) — Hebrew לַמִּזְרָח הַלְוִיִּם שִׁשָּׁה. The Septuagint is said to read "for the day" here as in the next two clauses (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept. **"a day" → "for the day"** — Hebrew לַיּוֹם. **"two and two" → "two by two"** — Hebrew שְׁנַיִם שְׁנָיִם.
- **"Parbar" kept** (v18) — Hebrew פַּרְבָּר, with the article; found only here (2 Kgs 23:11 has פַּרְוָרִים). It is a structure on the west side of the house; the meaning ("colonnade", "court", "open building") is not known. **"at the causeway" → "for the highway"** — Hebrew לַמְסִלָּה.
- **"among the sons of Kore" → "of the sons of Korah"** (v19) — Hebrew לִבְנֵי הַקָּרְחִי, "of the sons of the Korahite", the name of the clan with the article; the existing name token is kept.
- **"And of the Levites, Ahijah was over" → "And the Levites: Ahijah was over"** (v20) — Hebrew וְהַלְוִיִּם אֲחִיָּה, without "of". The Septuagint is said to read "their brothers" (אֲחֵיהֶם) for the name Ahijah (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"dedicated things" / "dedicated" → "holy things" / "sanctified"** (v20, 26, 27, 28) — Hebrew קֳדָשִׁים and הִקְדִּישׁ, noun and verb of one root, קדשׁ "holy".
- **"chief fathers" → "heads of the fathers"** (v21, 26, 32) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי הָאָבוֹת, short for "heads of the fathers' houses".
- **"As concerning the sons of Laadan; the sons of the Gershonite Laadan" → "The sons of Ladan, the sons of the Gershonite belonging to Ladan"** (v21) — Hebrew לְלַעְדָּן twice, "belonging to Ladan"; the verse is as heavy in Hebrew as in English.
- **"Of the Amramites, and the Izharites" → "Of the Amramites, of the Izharites …"** (v23) — Hebrew לַעַמְרָמִי לַיִּצְהָרִי …: each is a singular with the article and its own לְ, the clan named as one ("the Amramite"); so also in v29–32 ("the Hebronite", "the Reubenite", "the Gadite", "the Manassite").
- **"ruler of the treasures" → "leader over the treasuries"** (v24) — Hebrew נָגִיד, "leader".
- **"his brethren by Eliezer" → "his brothers, belonging to Eliezer"** (v25) — Hebrew וְאֶחָיו לֶאֱלִיעֶזֶר; "brothers" here is kinsmen.
- **"Shelomith" / "Shelomoth"** (v25, 26, 28) — in v25 the written text (ketiv) has "Shelomoth" and the reading tradition (qere) "Shelomith"; the qere is translated. v26 has "Shelomoth" and v28 "Shelomith": each as the Hebrew prints it. One man is meant.
- **"Which Shelomith and his brethren" → "He, Shelomoth, and his brothers"** (v26) — Hebrew הוּא שְׁלֹמוֹת. **"the captains over thousands and hundreds, and the captains of the host" → "the commanders of the thousands and the hundreds, and the commanders of the host"** — Hebrew שָׂרֵי; the first has לְ before it (לְשָׂרֵי), which may mean "namely" or "for"; it is not rendered. שַׂר is "prince, chief"; of an army "commander".
- **"Out of the spoils won in battles did they dedicate to maintain the house of the LORD" → "From the wars and from the spoil they had sanctified, to repair the house of Yahweh"** (v27) — Hebrew מִן־הַמִּלְחָמוֹת וּמִן־הַשָּׁלָל: two nouns, each with "from"; the verb has no object. לְחַזֵּק is literally "to make strong"; of a building it is "repair", the keeping up of the house (2 Kgs 12:5–14).
- **"and whosoever had dedicated any thing, it was under the hand of" → "everyone who sanctified, was at the hand of"** (v28) — Hebrew כֹּל הַמַּקְדִּישׁ עַל יַד, literally "upon the hand of": in his care. The first verb, הַהִקְדִּישׁ, has the article in place of the relative.
- **"for the outward business" → "for the outside work"** (v29) — Hebrew לַמְּלָאכָה הַחִיצוֹנָה: work outside the house of Yahweh. **"for officers and judges" → "as officers and as judges"** — Hebrew שֹׁטְרִים, lower officials who keep order and lists.
- **"men of valour, a thousand and seven hundred, were officers among them of Israel on this side Jordan westward" → "sons of ability, one thousand seven hundred, were over the oversight of Israel across the Jordan westward"** (v30) — Hebrew עַל פְּקֻדַּת יִשְׂרָאֵל; פְּקֻדָּה is "oversight". מֵעֵבֶר לַיַּרְדֵּן מַעְרָבָה: "across the Jordan" is said of either side; "westward" names the side. **"in all the business of the LORD" → "for all the work of Yahweh"** — Hebrew מְלֶאכֶת, "work"; עֲבֹדַת, "service".
- **"Among the Hebronites was Jerijah the chief, even among the Hebronites, according to the generations of his fathers" → "Of the Hebronites, Jerijah was the head of the Hebronites, by their generations, by fathers"** (v31) — Hebrew לְתֹלְדֹתָיו לְאָבוֹת, literally "by his generations, by fathers": the suffix is singular, of the clan. **"they were sought for" → "they were sought out"** — Hebrew נִדְרָשׁוּ, the verb דָּרַשׁ "seek".
- **"rulers" → "appointed them over"** (v32) — Hebrew וַיַּפְקִידֵם … עַל. **"the half tribe of Manasseh" → "the half of the tribe of Manasseh"** — Hebrew חֲצִי שֵׁבֶט הַמְנַשִּׁי, "of the Manassite". **"for every matter pertaining to God, and affairs of the king" → "for every matter of *Elohim* and matter of the king"** — Hebrew דְּבַר twice, the same word.
- **Numbers** (v8, 9, 11, 30, 32) — written in words in the ordinary English order; the Hebrew has "sixty and two", "a thousand and seven hundred".

### Notes
- **Three Levitical vocations** — gatekeeping (vv 1-19), treasury (vv 20-28), outside administration (vv 29-32). Comprehensive temple and civil service.
- **Obed-edom blessed** (v5) — reward for housing the ark (ch 13). Large family as sign of divine favor.
- **Sanctified spoil** (v26-27) — temple-funding model: war-gain becomes sanctuary-maintenance. Includes what Samuel, Saul, Abner and Joab had sanctified; all kept for Yahweh's house under Shelomith.
- **David's fortieth year** (v31) — end of reign; organizational completion.
- **Trans-Jordanian governance** (v32) — 2,700 Hebronites as administrators for Transjordan. Heavy Levitical presence in civil affairs.
- **Shelomoth = Shelomith** (v25-26, 28) — Moses' line via Eliezer (v25), Gershom's brother (1 Chr 23:15), beside Shebuel's line via Gershom (v24); Mosaic descent retains prestige, but Aaronic priority.
