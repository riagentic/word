# 1 Chronicles 26

[1] Concerning the divisions of the gatekeepers: of the Korahites, Meshelemiah son of Kore, of the sons of Asaph.

[2] The sons of Meshelemiah: Zechariah the firstborn, Jediael the second, Zebadiah the third, Jathniel the fourth,

[3] Elam the fifth, Jehohanan the sixth, Eliehoenai the seventh.

[4] The sons of Obed-edom: Shemaiah the firstborn, Jehozabad the second, Joah the third, Sachar the fourth, Nethanel the fifth,

[5] Ammiel the sixth, Issachar the seventh, Peullethai the eighth — for *Elohim* blessed him.

[6] Also to Shemaiah his son were sons born, who ruled in their fathers' house, for they were valiant warriors.

[7] The sons of Shemaiah: Othni, Rephael, Obed, Elzabad; his brothers Elihu and Semachiah were able men.

[8] All these were of the sons of Obed-edom: they, their sons, and their brothers, able men with strength for the service — sixty-two of Obed-edom's line.

[9] Meshelemiah had sons and brothers, able men — eighteen.

[10] Hosah, of the children of Merari, had sons: Shimri the chief (for though he was not the firstborn, his father made him chief);

[11] Hilkiah the second, Tebaliah the third, Zechariah the fourth. All the sons and brothers of Hosah were thirteen.

[12] These divisions of the gatekeepers, according to their chief men, had duties like their brothers, to minister in the house of Yahweh.

[13] They cast lots, small and great alike, according to their fathers' houses, for each gate.

[14] The east lot fell to Shelemiah. For Zechariah his son, a wise counselor, they cast lots; his lot came out to the north.

[15] To Obed-edom, the south; and to his sons, the storehouse.

[16] To Shuppim and Hosah, the west, with the Shallecheth Gate, on the ascending causeway — watch corresponding to watch.

[17] On the east there were six Levites daily; on the north, four daily; on the south, four daily; and for the storehouse, two and two;

[18] for the Parbar on the west, four at the causeway and two at the Parbar.

[19] These are the divisions of the gatekeepers, of the sons of Korah and the sons of Merari.

[20] Of the Levites, Ahijah was over the treasuries of the house of *Elohim* and over the treasuries of the dedicated things.

[21] The sons of Ladan, the sons of the Gershonites through Ladan, heads of the fathers' houses belonging to Ladan the Gershonite: Jehieli.

[22] The sons of Jehieli: Zetham and his brother Joel, were over the treasuries of the house of Yahweh.

[23] Of the Amramites, the Izharites, the Hebronites, and the Uzzielites:

[24] Shebuel son of Gershom son of Moses was chief officer over the treasuries.

[25] His kinsmen through Eliezer: Rehabiah his son, Jeshaiah his son, Joram his son, Zichri his son, and Shelomoth his son.

[26] This Shelomoth and his brothers were over all the treasuries of the dedicated things that David the king, the heads of fathers' houses, the commanders of thousands and hundreds, and the commanders of the army had dedicated.

[27] From the spoils won in battles they dedicated offerings for the maintenance of the house of Yahweh.

[28] Whatever Samuel the seer, Saul son of Kish, Abner son of Ner, and Joab son of Zeruiah had dedicated — whatever any had dedicated — was under the hand of Shelomoth and his brothers.

[29] Of the Izharites: Chenaniah and his sons were assigned to the outside business for Israel, as officers and judges.

[30] Of the Hebronites: Hashabiah and his brothers, one thousand seven hundred men of valor, had the oversight of Israel west of the Jordan in all the business of Yahweh and in the service of the king.

[31] Of the Hebronites: Jerijah was the chief of the Hebronites according to the generations of his fathers' houses. In the fortieth year of David's reign a search was made, and there were found among them mighty men of valor at Jazer of Gilead.

[32] His kinsmen, men of valor — two thousand seven hundred heads of fathers' houses — King David appointed over the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, for every matter pertaining to *Elohim* and to the affairs of the king.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (throughout).
- **"Asuppim" → "storehouse"** (v15, 17) — *asuppim*; literally "storehouse / gatherings-place."
- **"Parbar" — retained** (v18) — hapax; possibly "colonnade" or "court" on west side. Persian loanword in some readings.
- **"ward against ward" → "watch corresponding to watch"** (v16).
- **"outward business" → "outside business"** (v29) — tasks outside the temple, civic/judicial.
- **"on this side Jordan westward" → "west of the Jordan"** (v30).
- **"Shelemiah" and "Meshelemiah" = same person** (v1, 14) — shortened form.

### Notes
- **Three Levitical vocations** — gatekeeping (vv 1-19), treasury (vv 20-28), outside administration (vv 29-32). Comprehensive temple and civil service.
- **Obed-edom blessed** (v5) — reward for housing the ark (ch 13). Large family as sign of divine favor.
- **Dedicated spoils** (v26-27) — temple-funding model: war-gain becomes sanctuary-maintenance. Includes spoils attributed to Samuel, Saul, Abner, Joab; all dedicated to Yahweh's house through Davidic administration.
- **David's fortieth year** (v31) — end of reign; organizational completion.
- **Trans-Jordanian governance** (v32) — 2,700 Hebronites as administrators for Transjordan. Heavy Levitical presence in civil affairs.
- **Shelomoth = Shelomith** (v25-26, 28) — Moses' line via Gershom, then Eliezer; Mosaic descent retains prestige, but Aaronic priority.
