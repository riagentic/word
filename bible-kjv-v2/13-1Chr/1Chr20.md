# 1 Chronicles 20

[1] And it came to pass at the time of the return of the year, at the time when the kings go out, that ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ led the force of the host, and he ruined the land of the sons of Ammon, and he came and besieged Rabbah. And David was dwelling in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ struck Rabbah and threw it down.

[2] And David took the crown of their king from upon his head, and he found it the weight of a talent of gold, and in it was a precious stone, and it was upon the head of David. And the spoil of the city he brought out, very much.

[3] And the people who were in it he brought out, and he sawed with the saw and with the cutting tools of iron and with the saws; and thus David would do to all the cities of the sons of Ammon. And David and all the people returned to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[4] And it came to pass after this that war arose in Gezer with the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧. Then ⟦sibbecai|Sibbechai|Sibbecai⟧ the ⟦hushathite|Chushati|Hushathite⟧ struck down Sippai, of those born of the ⟦rephaim|Refaim|Rephaim⟧; and they were brought low.

[5] And there was again war with the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, and ⟦elhanan|Elchanan|Elhanan⟧ the son of ⟦jair|Yair|Jair⟧ struck down ⟦lahmi|Lachmi|Lahmi⟧ the brother of ⟦goliath|Golyat|Goliath⟧ the ⟦gittite|Gitti|Gittite⟧, and the shaft of his spear was like a weavers' beam.

[6] And there was again war in ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧, and there was a man of stature, and his fingers were six and six, twenty-four; and he also was born to the ⟦rapha|Rafa|Rapha⟧.

[7] And he reproached ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ the son of ⟦shimea|Shima|Shimea⟧, the brother of David, struck him down.

[8] These were born to the ⟦rapha|Rafa|Rapha⟧ in ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧, and they fell by the hand of David and by the hand of his servants.

## Changes

- **The chapter beside 2 Samuel** — v1–3 stand beside 2 Sam 11:1 and 12:26, 30–31; v4–8 beside 2 Sam 21:18–22. Where the Hebrew is the same, the wording is that of Samuel; each difference below is a difference in the Hebrew of Chronicles. No name, number or word is taken over from Samuel.
- **"after the year was expired" → "at the time of the return of the year"** (v1) — Hebrew לְעֵת תְּשׁוּבַת הַשָּׁנָה; 2 Sam 11:1 has no "at the time of" here. "Spring" is not written.
- **"Joab led forth the power of the army" → "Joab led the force of the host"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּנְהַג … אֶת־חֵיל הַצָּבָא; the verb is "drive, lead". In 2 Sam 11:1 David sends Joab, his servants and all Israel.
- **"wasted the country" → "ruined the land"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּשְׁחֵת אֶת־אֶרֶץ; 2 Sam 11:1 has "they ruined the sons of Ammon".
- **"But David tarried at Jerusalem" → "And David was dwelling in Jerusalem"** (v1) — Hebrew וְדָוִיד יֹשֵׁב.
- **"smote Rabbah, and destroyed it" → "struck Rabbah and threw it down"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּךְ … וַיֶּהֶרְסֶהָ; this sentence is in Chronicles only. David's coming to Rabbah (2 Sam 12:27–29) is not told here.
- **"and found it to weigh a talent of gold, and there were precious stones in it" → "and he found it the weight of a talent of gold, and in it was a precious stone"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיִּמְצָאָהּ מִשְׁקַל כִּכַּר־זָהָב וּבָהּ אֶבֶן יְקָרָה, singular "stone". 2 Sam 12:30 has "and its weight was a talent of gold, and a precious stone".
- **"their king"** (v2) — Hebrew מַלְכָּם; the same letters can be read "Milcom", the god of the sons of Ammon. The pointing is translated.
- **"cut them with saws, and with harrows of iron, and with axes" → "he sawed with the saw and with the cutting tools of iron and with the saws"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיָּשַׂר בַּמְּגֵרָה וּבַחֲרִיצֵי הַבַּרְזֶל וּבַמְּגֵרוֹת. The verb וַיָּשַׂר stands only here; it is taken from the root of מַשּׂוֹר "saw". No object "them" is written. 2 Sam 12:31 has וַיָּשֶׂם "and he put", which differs by one letter, and "the axes of iron" where Chronicles repeats "saws"; the making to pass through the brick mold is not in Chronicles. Whether the people were cut or were set to work with these tools the verse does not say; the earlier verse had "set them to work", which is not the word that is printed.
- **"dealt David" → "David would do"** (v3) — Hebrew יַעֲשֶׂה, of what was done each time.
- **"there arose war" → "war arose"** (v4) — Hebrew וַתַּעֲמֹד מִלְחָמָה, lit. "war stood". **"at Gezer"** — 2 Sam 21:18 has "in Gob".
- **"slew" → "struck down"** (v4, 5, 7) — Hebrew הִכָּה.
- **"Sippai, that was of the children of the giant" → "Sippai, of those born of the Rephaim"** (v4) — Hebrew מִילִדֵי הָרְפָאִים, plural; 2 Sam 21:18 has "Saph" and "the Rapha". **"and they were subdued" → "and they were brought low"** — Hebrew וַיִּכָּנֵעוּ, in Chronicles only.
- **"Elhanan the son of Jair slew Lahmi the brother of Goliath the Gittite"** (v5) — so the Hebrew of Chronicles: בֶּן־יָעִיר אֶת־לַחְמִי אֲחִי גָּלְיָת. The written form of the father's name is יעור (Jaur), the read form Jair. 2 Sam 21:19 reads "the son of Jaare-oregim the Bethlehemite struck down Goliath the Gittite"; the two texts are left as each stands.
- **"whose spear staff was like a weaver's beam" → "and the shaft of his spear was like a weavers' beam"** (v5) — Hebrew וְעֵץ חֲנִיתוֹ כִּמְנוֹר אֹרְגִים.
- **"a man of great stature" → "a man of stature"** (v6) — Hebrew אִישׁ מִדָּה, lit. "a man of measure". **"whose fingers and toes were four and twenty, six on each hand, and six on each foot" → "his fingers were six and six, twenty-four"** — Hebrew וְאֶצְבְּעֹתָיו שֵׁשׁ־וָשֵׁשׁ עֶשְׂרִים וְאַרְבַּע; hands and feet (2 Sam 21:20) are not named.
- **"the son of the giant" → "born to the Rapha"** (v6, 8) — Hebrew לְהָרָפָא, singular with the article; who or what "the Rapha" is, is not said.
- **"defied" → "reproached"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיְחָרֵף.
- **"These were born"** (v8) — Hebrew אֵל, a short form of "these"; "four" (2 Sam 21:22) is not written.

### Notes
- **Chronicles does not have** the account of Bathsheba and Uriah (2 Sam 11:2–12:25).
- **Rabbah** — the chief city of the sons of Ammon.
- **Lahmi and Goliath** (v5) — compare 1 Sam 17 and 2 Sam 21:19.
- **Jonathan the son of Shimea** — the son of David's brother (1 Sam 16:9 "Shammah").
