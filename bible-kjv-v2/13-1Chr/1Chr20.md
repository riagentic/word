# 1 Chronicles 20

[1] At the turn of the year, at the time when kings go out to battle, Joab led out the armed force and ravaged the country of the children of Ammon. He came and besieged Rabbah, but David remained at Jerusalem. Joab struck down Rabbah and overthrew it.

[2] David took the crown of their king from off his head; he found it to weigh a talent of gold, with precious stones in it, and it was set on David's head. He also brought out a great amount of spoil from the city.

[3] He brought out the people who were in it, and set them to work with saws, iron picks, and axes. Thus David did with all the cities of the children of Ammon. Then David and all the people returned to Jerusalem.

[4] After this, war arose at Gezer with the Philistines. At that time Sibbecai the Hushathite struck down Sippai, who was of the children of the Rapha — giant clan. So they were subdued.

[5] There was again war with the Philistines; Elhanan son of Jair struck down Lahmi the brother of Goliath the Gittite, the shaft of whose spear was like a weaver's beam.

[6] Yet again there was war at Gath, where was a man of great stature whose fingers and toes were twenty-four, six on each hand and six on each foot; he too was descended from the Rapha.

[7] When he taunted Israel, Jonathan son of Shimea, David's brother, struck him down.

[8] These were descended from the Rapha in Gath; they fell by the hand of David and by the hand of his servants.

## Changes

- **"the year was expired" → "the turn of the year"** (v1) — *teshuvat ha-shanah*; spring campaigning season.
- **"cut them with saws, and with harrows of iron" → "set them to work with saws, iron picks"** (v3) — Hebrew *va-yasar ba-megerah*; MT could read "set to work with" (corvée labor, *sur* root = "set / hew") or (per 2 Sam 12:31 different verb *va-yasem*) "put under saws" (implying execution/torture). Most modern readings (matching 2 Sam 12:31) favor forced labor interpretation: corvée with implements, not dismemberment.
- **"spear staff" → "shaft of whose spear"** (v5) — *chanito*.
- **"defied" → "taunted"** (v7).

### Notes
- **Bathsheba elision** — Chronicler jumps from Joab's campaign (v1) to crown capture (v2), omitting 2 Sam 11 (David with Bathsheba at Jerusalem during the siege). Deliberate silence: David as temple-builder/ideal king.
- **Rabbah** — modern Amman, Jordan. Ammonite capital.
- **"Lahmi brother of Goliath"** (v5) — harmonistic reading of 2 Sam 21:19 which says Elhanan killed Goliath. Chronicler (or his source) added "Lahmi brother of" to resolve tension with 1 Sam 17 David-and-Goliath account. Textual/theological question is old; multiple solutions: (1) two different Goliaths, (2) 2 Sam 21 is textually corrupt and Chronicles preserves the correct original.
- **Giants of Gath** — Rephaim lineage; Philistine Gath a known giant-territory (cf. Num 13:33, Josh 11:22). Recurrent wars against remaining champions.
- **Polydactyly** (v6) — genetic trait; rare historical curiosity preserved.
- **Jonathan son of Shimea** — David's nephew; from David's brother Shammah/Shimea (1 Sam 17:13).
