# 1 Chronicles 22

[1] And David said, "This is the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and this is an altar for burnt offering for ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[2] And David said to gather the sojourners who were in the land of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and he set hewers to hew hewn stones, to build the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] And iron in abundance, for the nails for the doors of the gates and for the joinings, David prepared, and bronze in abundance, without weight,

[4] and cedar wood without number, for the ⟦sidonians|Tzidonim|Sidonians⟧ and the ⟦tyrians|Tzorim|Tyrians⟧ brought cedar wood in abundance to David.

[6] And he called ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ his son and commanded him to build a house for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[7] And David said to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, "My son, as for me, it was with my heart to build a house for the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[11] Now, my son, may ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ be with you, and you shall prosper and build the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, as he spoke concerning you.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v1, 2, 19 with the article (הָאֱלֹהִים).
- **"Then David said" → "And David said"** (v1 and throughout) — Hebrew וַ, "and", is kept at the head of each clause. **"the altar of the burnt offering" → "an altar for burnt offering"** — Hebrew מִזְבֵּחַ לְעֹלָה, without the article; זֶה הוּא is literally "this, it is".
- **"commanded to gather together the strangers" → "said to gather the sojourners"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר … לִכְנוֹס, "said to gather" (an order is meant); גֵּרִים "sojourners". **"he set masons to hew wrought stones" → "he set hewers to hew hewn stones"** — Hebrew חֹצְבִים לַחְצוֹב, one root; גָּזִית "hewn stone" is another root (1 Kgs 5:17): English has one word for both.
- **"joinings"** (v3) — Hebrew מְחַבְּרוֹת, from חָבַר "join": clamps or braces. **"brass in abundance without weight" → "bronze in abundance, without weight"** (v3, 14) — Hebrew אֵין מִשְׁקָל, "there is no weight". The object stands first in the Hebrew: "And iron in abundance … David prepared".
- **"cedar trees" → "cedar wood"** (v4) — Hebrew עֲצֵי אֲרָזִים, literally "woods of cedars" (1 Kgs 5:8). **"Zidonians" → "Sidonians"**.
- **"young and tender" → "a boy and tender"** (v5) — Hebrew נַעַר וָרָךְ; נַעַר is "boy", also said of a young man (1 Kgs 3:7). **"must be exceeding magnifical, of fame and of glory throughout all countries" → "is to be made exceedingly great, for a name and for beauty to all the lands"** — Hebrew לְהַגְדִּיל לְמַעְלָה, literally "to make great upward"; לְשֵׁם וּלְתִפְאֶרֶת "for a name and for beauty" (Deut 26:19). **"I will therefore now make preparation for it" → "let me now prepare for it"** — Hebrew אָכִינָה נָּא, a cohortative with נָא; לוֹ may be "for it" (the house) or "for him" (Solomon). **"prepared abundantly"** → "prepared in abundance" (לָרֹב, the word of v3, 4, 8, 14, 15).
- **"charged him" → "commanded him"** (v6) — Hebrew צִוָּה.
- **"My son"** (v7) — the reading tradition (qere) בְּנִי; the written text (ketiv) has בנו, "his son": "And David said to Solomon his son". **"it was in my mind" → "it was with my heart"** — Hebrew הָיָה עִם־לְבָבִי, the words of 1 Kgs 8:17.
- **"came to me" → "came upon me"** (v8) — Hebrew עָלַי, "upon me" or "concerning me", not אֵלַי. **"Thou hast shed blood abundantly … much blood upon the earth in my sight" → "Blood in abundance you have shed … much blood you have shed to the earth before me"** — the objects stand first; the first "blood" is singular (דָּם לָרֹב), the second plural (דָּמִים רַבִּים, literally "many bloods"); אַרְצָה "to the earth / ground"; לְפָנָי "before me".
- **"a son shall be born to thee, who shall be a man of rest" → "a son shall be born to you; he shall be a man of rest"** (v9) — Hebrew נוֹלָד, a participle after "behold", of what is coming (1 Kgs 13:2); מְנוּחָה is the noun "rest, resting place" (1 Kgs 8:56), from the root of "give rest". **"round about" → "all around"** — Hebrew הֵנִיחַ … מִסָּבִיב, the words of Deut 12:10; Josh 21:44; 2 Sam 7:1; 1 Kgs 5:4. **"I will give peace and quietness unto Israel" → "shalom and quiet I will give upon Israel"** — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם וָשֶׁקֶט … עַל־יִשְׂרָאֵל. The name Solomon, שְׁלֹמֹה *Shlomo*, sounds like שָׁלוֹם *shalom*: "for" gives the name as the ground of the promise. The earlier verse broke the sentence with dashes that the Hebrew does not have.
- **"he shall be my son, and I will be his father" → "he, he shall be to me for a son, and I to him for a father"** (v10) — Hebrew הוּא … וְהוּא, the pronoun set out twice; לִי לְבֵן … לוֹ לְאָב, as 2 Sam 7:14; the second clause has no verb. "Establish" is הֵכִין, the verb rendered "prepare" in v3, 5, 14.
- **"the LORD be with thee; and prosper thou, and build" → "may Yahweh be with you, and you shall prosper and build"** (v11) — a wish (יְהִי) followed by two verbs that state what follows from it. **"as he hath said of thee" → "as he spoke concerning you"** — Hebrew דִּבֶּר עָלֶיךָ.
- **"wisdom and understanding, and give thee charge concerning Israel, that thou mayest keep" → "insight and understanding, and may he command you over Israel, and to keep"** (v12) — Hebrew שֵׂכֶל "insight" (the verb in Josh 1:7; 1 Kgs 2:3); וִיצַוְּךָ עַל־יִשְׂרָאֵל, "and may he command you over / concerning Israel" (set you in command, or give you his commands about Israel); וְלִשְׁמוֹר is an infinitive joined by "and", loosely: the verse keeps it.
- **"if thou takest heed to fulfil the statutes and judgments" → "if you keep to do the statutes and the ordinances"** (v13) — Hebrew תִּשְׁמוֹר לַעֲשׂוֹת, as in Deuteronomy; מִשְׁפָּטִים is the word "judgment", here of what has been ruled. **"be strong, and of good courage; dread not, nor be dismayed" → "Be strong and be firm; do not fear and do not be dismayed"** — Hebrew חֲזַק וֶאֱמָץ, two verbs for being strong, the words of Deut 31:7, 23; Josh 1:6–9; "courage" is not a word of the Hebrew. תֵּחָת is literally "be shattered".
- **"in my trouble" → "in my affliction"** (v14) — Hebrew בְעָנְיִי, the noun עֳנִי "affliction"; also understood "in my poverty" or "by my hard labor". **"a thousand thousand talents" → "a million talents"** — Hebrew אֶלֶף אֲלָפִים, literally "a thousand of thousands". **"of brass and iron without weight" → "for the bronze and for the iron there is no weight"** — the Hebrew has לְ before each. **"timber also and stone … and thou mayest add thereto" → "wood and stones … and to them you shall add"** — Hebrew עֵצִים וַאֲבָנִים, both plural; תּוֹסִיף.
- **"workmen with thee in abundance, hewers and workers of stone and timber, and all manner of cunning men for every manner of work" → "with you in abundance are doers of work, hewers and craftsmen of stone and wood, and everyone wise in every work"** (v15) — Hebrew עֹשֵׂי מְלָאכָה; חָרָשׁ "craftsman"; כָל־חָכָם, "every wise one": skill is meant (Exod 31:6). "Are" is supplied.
- **"Of the gold, the silver … there is no number. Arise therefore, and be doing" → "For the gold, for the silver … there is no number. Arise and do"** (v16) — Hebrew לְ before each metal, "and" only before the last two; קוּם וַעֲשֵׂה.
- **"princes"** (v17) — Hebrew שָׂרִים. The Hebrew has no "saying"; the speech follows at once.
- **"and hath he not given you rest on every side?" → "And he has given you rest all around"** (v18) — the question covers the first clause only; וְהֵנִיחַ … מִסָּבִיב as in v9. **"the land is subdued" → "the land has been subdued"** — Hebrew נִכְבְּשָׁה, a perfect.
- **"set your heart and your soul" → "give your heart and your soul"** (v19) — Hebrew תְּנוּ, "give". **"the holy vessels of God" → "the holy utensils of Elohim"** — Hebrew כְּלֵי קֹדֶשׁ הָאֱלֹהִים, literally "the utensils of the holiness of Elohim" (1 Kgs 8:4). **"that is to be built" → "that is being built"** — Hebrew הַנִּבְנֶה, a participle: being built, or about to be built.

### Notes
- **"This is the house"** (v1) — David's declaration fixes the temple site as Ornan's floor. Key Chronistic moment.
- **Why David cannot build** — "shed much blood" (v8). Warrior-king is unsuitable for temple of peace; task belongs to "man of rest."
- **Temple preparation** — Chronicler alone reports this extensive prep work by David. Unique material compared to 1 Kgs. Emphasizes David as temple-founder even if Solomon as temple-builder.
- **Astronomical quantities** (v14) — 100,000 talents of gold ≈ 3,750 tons; 1,000,000 talents silver ≈ 37,500 tons. Hyperbolic; emphasizes total commitment.
- **Insight and understanding** (v12) — anticipates Solomon's famous gift (1 Kgs 3).
- **"Be strong and be firm"** (v13) — Deuteronomic/Joshua language (Josh 1:6-9). Temple-building parallels conquest leadership.
- **Sojourner labor** (v2) — same class Solomon will use (1 Kgs 5:15-16, 2 Chr 2:17-18). Resolves tension: Israelites don't do forced labor.
