# 1 Chronicles 22

[1] Then David said, "This is the house of Yahweh *Elohim*, and this is the altar of burnt offering for Israel."

[2] David commanded to gather together the resident foreigners who were in the land of Israel; and he set stonecutters to hew dressed stones to build the house of *Elohim*.

[3] David prepared iron in abundance for the nails for the doors of the gates and for the joinings, and bronze in abundance beyond weighing,

[4] and cedar logs without number, for the Sidonians and Tyrians brought cedar logs in abundance to David.

[5] David said, "Solomon my son is young and inexperienced; the house that is to be built for Yahweh must be magnificent in fame and glory throughout all lands. Let me therefore make preparation for it." So David made ample preparation before his death.

[6] Then he called for Solomon his son and charged him to build a house for Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel.

[7] David said to Solomon, "My son, as for me, it was in my heart to build a house for the name of Yahweh my *Elohim*.

[8] But the word of Yahweh came to me, saying, 'You have shed much blood and have made great wars; you shall not build a house for my name, because you have shed much blood on the earth in my sight.

[9] Behold, a son shall be born to you, who shall be a man of rest; I will give him rest from all his enemies round about. For his name shall be Solomon — and I will give peace — and quiet to Israel in his days.

[10] He shall build a house for my name; he shall be my son, and I will be his father, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom over Israel forever.'

[11] Now, my son, may Yahweh be with you, so that you may prosper and build the house of Yahweh your *Elohim*, as he has said of you.

[12] Only may Yahweh give you wisdom and understanding, and give you charge concerning Israel, that you may keep the Law of Yahweh your *Elohim*.

[13] Then you shall prosper, if you are careful to observe the statutes and ordinances that Yahweh commanded Moses concerning Israel. Be strong and courageous; fear not, nor be dismayed.

[14] Behold, in my trouble I have prepared for the house of Yahweh one hundred thousand talents of gold, and a million talents of silver, and bronze and iron beyond weighing, for they are in abundance; timber also and stone I have prepared, and you may add to them.

[15] Moreover you have workmen in abundance — hewers, workers in stone and timber, and all kinds of skilled craftsmen for every sort of work.

[16] Of the gold, silver, bronze, and iron there is no reckoning. Arise and be doing, and may Yahweh be with you."

[17] David also commanded all the leaders of Israel to help Solomon his son, saying,

[18] "Is not Yahweh your *Elohim* with you? Has he not given you rest on every side? For he has given the inhabitants of the land into my hand, and the land is subdued before Yahweh and before his people.

[19] Now set your heart and your soul — to seek Yahweh your *Elohim*. Arise and build the sanctuary of Yahweh *Elohim*, to bring the ark of the covenant of Yahweh and the holy vessels of *Elohim* into the house that is to be built for the name of Yahweh."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"brass" → "bronze"** (v3, 14, 16).
- **"Zidonians" → "Sidonians"** (v4).
- **"magnifical" → "magnificent"** (v5).

### Notes
- **"This is the house"** (v1) — David's declaration fixes the temple site as Ornan's floor. Key Chronistic moment.
- **Why David cannot build** — "shed much blood" (v8). Warrior-king is unsuitable for temple of peace; task belongs to "man of rest."
- **Temple preparation** — Chronicler alone reports this extensive prep work by David. Unique material compared to 1 Kgs. Emphasizes David as temple-founder even if Solomon as temple-builder.
- **Astronomical quantities** (v14) — 100,000 talents of gold ≈ 3,750 tons; 1,000,000 talents silver ≈ 37,500 tons. Hyperbolic; emphasizes total commitment.
- **Wisdom and understanding** (v12) — anticipates Solomon's famous gift (1 Kgs 3).
- **"Be strong and courageous"** (v13) — Deuteronomic/Joshua language (Josh 1:6-9). Temple-building parallels conquest leadership.
- **Resident-foreigner labor** (v2) — same class Solomon will use (1 Kgs 5:15-16, 2 Chr 2:17-18). Resolves tension: Israelites don't do forced labor.
