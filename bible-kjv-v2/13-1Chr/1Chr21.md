# 1 Chronicles 21

[1] And an adversary stood against ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and he incited David to count ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[2] And David said to ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ and to the princes of the people, "Go, number ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from ⟦beersheba|Beer Sheva|Beersheba⟧ and as far as Dan, and bring it to me, and let me know their number."

[3] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ said, "May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ add to his people, as they are, a hundred times. Are they not, my lord the king, all of them my lord's, as servants? Why does my lord seek this? Why should it be for guilt to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?"

[4] And the word of the king was strong over ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧; and ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ went out and walked about in all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and he came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[5] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ gave the number of the numbering of the people to David; and all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ was one million one hundred thousand men drawing the sword, and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ four hundred and seventy thousand men drawing the sword.

[6] And Levi and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ he did not number among them, for the word of the king was abominable to ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧.

[7] And it was evil in the eyes of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ concerning this thing, and he struck ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[8] And David said to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, "I have sinned greatly in that I have done this thing. And now, make the iniquity of your servant pass over, please, for I have acted very foolishly."

[9] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to Gad, the seer of David, saying,

[10] "Go, and you shall speak to David, saying, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Three things I am stretching out over you; choose for yourself one of them, and I will do it to you.'"

[11] And Gad came to David and said to him, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Accept for yourself

[12] either three years of famine, or three months of being swept away from the face of your adversaries, with the sword of your enemies overtaking, or three days of the sword of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and pestilence in the land, and the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ destroying in all the border of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧. And now, see what word I shall return to him who sent me."

[13] And David said to Gad, "I am in great distress. Let me fall, please, into the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for his compassions are very many; and into the hand of man let me not fall."

[14] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave a pestilence in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and there fell of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ seventy thousand men.

[15] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ sent an angel to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to destroy it; and as he was destroying, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ saw, and he relented concerning the evil, and he said to the angel who was destroying, "Enough! Now let your hand drop." And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was standing by the threshing floor of Ornan the ⟦jebusite|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧.

[16] And David lifted up his eyes and saw the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ standing between the earth and the heavens, and his sword drawn in his hand, stretched out over ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And David and the elders, covered with sackcloth, fell on their faces.

[17] And David said to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, "Was it not I who said to count among the people? And I am he who has sinned, and I have surely done evil; and these, the flock, what have they done? ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, let your hand, please, be against me and against the house of my father, and against your people, not for a plague."

[18] And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to Gad to say to David that David should go up to raise up an altar to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ on the threshing floor of Ornan the ⟦jebusite|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧.

[19] And David went up at the word of Gad, which he spoke in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[20] And Ornan turned back and saw the angel; and his four sons with him were hiding themselves. And Ornan was threshing wheat.

[21] And David came as far as Ornan, and Ornan looked and saw David, and he went out from the threshing floor and bowed down to David, face to the earth.

[22] And David said to Ornan, "Give me the place of the threshing floor, and let me build on it an altar to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; for full silver give it to me; and let the plague be restrained from upon the people."

[23] And Ornan said to David, "Take it for yourself, and let my lord the king do what is good in his eyes. See, I give the herd for the burnt offerings, and the threshing sledges for the wood, and the wheat for the grain offering; all of it I give."

[24] And King David said to Ornan, "No, for I will surely buy for full silver; for I will not lift up what is yours to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, nor offer up a burnt offering for nothing."

[25] And David gave to Ornan for the place shekels of gold, six hundred by weight.

[26] And David built there an altar to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he offered up burnt offerings and peace offerings. And he called to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he answered him with fire from the heavens upon the altar of the burnt offering.

[27] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to the angel, and he returned his sword to its sheath.

[28] At that time, when David saw that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had answered him on the threshing floor of Ornan the ⟦jebusite|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧, he sacrificed there.

[29] And the dwelling of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ had made in the wilderness, and the altar of the burnt offering, were at that time in the high place in ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧.

[30] And David was not able to go before it to seek ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for he was terrified because of the sword of the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; with the article in v7, 8, 15, 17 (הָאֱלֹהִים), without it in v30.
- **The chapter beside 2 Sam 24** — where the Hebrew of the two chapters is the same, the wording is that of 2 Sam 24; each difference below is a difference in the Hebrew of Chronicles. No name, figure or word is taken over from Samuel.
- **"And Satan stood up against Israel" → "And an adversary stood against Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲמֹד שָׂטָן עַל־יִשְׂרָאֵל. The word שָׂטָן, "adversary", stands here without the article. Without the article it is the common noun, "an adversary" (so of men in 1 Sam 29:4; 1 Kgs 11:14, 23, 25, and of the angel of Yahweh in Num 22:22), or it is used as a name, "Satan"; with the article, "the adversary", it stands in Job 1–2 and Zech 3:1–2. The Hebrew of this verse does not say which is meant, nor who the adversary is. The verse has the common noun as everywhere else in this translation; "Satan" is the other reading. 2 Sam 24:1 has "the anger of Yahweh again burned against Israel, and he incited David against them"; Chronicles does not have those words, and they are not brought in here.
- **"provoked" → "incited"**, **"to number" → "to count"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיָּסֶת, לִמְנוֹת. Three Hebrew verbs stand in the chapter: מָנָה "count" (v1, 17), סָפַר "number" (v2), פָּקַד "number" (v6, and the noun in v5).
- **"the rulers of the people" → "the princes of the people"** (v2) — Hebrew שָׂרֵי הָעָם. **"from Beer-sheba even to Dan"** — the order of Chronicles; 2 Sam 24:2 has "from Dan and as far as Beersheba". **"bring the number of them to me, that I may know it" → "bring it to me, and let me know their number"** — Hebrew וְהָבִיאוּ אֵלַי וְאֵדְעָה אֶת־מִסְפָּרָם; "it" is supplied, the verb has no object.
- **"The LORD make his people an hundred times so many more as they be" → "May Yahweh add to his people, as they are, a hundred times"** (v3) — Hebrew יוֹסֵף יְהוָה עַל־עַמּוֹ כָּהֵם מֵאָה פְעָמִים.
- **"are they not all my lord's servants?" → "Are they not … all of them my lord's, as servants?"** (v3) — Hebrew כֻּלָּם לַאדֹנִי לַעֲבָדִים, lit. "all of them to my lord for servants".
- **"why then doth my lord require this thing?" → "Why does my lord seek this?"** (v3) — Hebrew יְבַקֵּשׁ. **"a cause of trespass" → "for guilt"** — Hebrew לְאַשְׁמָה. Both questions are in Chronicles only.
- **"the king's word prevailed against Joab" → "the word of the king was strong over Joab"** (v4) — Hebrew חָזַק עַל. **"went throughout all Israel" → "walked about in all Israel"** — Hebrew וַיִּתְהַלֵּךְ. The route (2 Sam 24:5–8) is not in Chronicles.
- **"a thousand thousand and an hundred thousand men" → "one million one hundred thousand men"**, **"four hundred threescore and ten thousand" → "four hundred and seventy thousand"** (v5) — the figures as Chronicles prints them, lit. "a thousand thousands and a hundred thousand"; 2 Sam 24:9 has eight hundred thousand and five hundred thousand. **"that drew sword" → "drawing the sword"** — Hebrew שֹׁלֵף חֶרֶב.
- **"counted he not" → "he did not number"** (v6) — Hebrew לֹא פָקַד. **"abominable"** — Hebrew נִתְעַב, the root of "abomination". The verse is in Chronicles only.
- **"And God was displeased with this thing" → "And it was evil in the eyes of Elohim concerning this thing"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיֵּרַע בְּעֵינֵי הָאֱלֹהִים עַל־הַדָּבָר הַזֶּה. **"smote" → "struck"** — Hebrew וַיַּךְ.
- **"because I have done this thing" → "in that I have done this thing"** (v8) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר עָשִׂיתִי אֶת־הַדָּבָר הַזֶּה; 2 Sam 24:10 has no "this thing". **"do away the iniquity" → "make the iniquity … pass over"** — Hebrew הַעֲבֶר־נָא.
- **"I offer thee three things" → "Three things I am stretching out over you"** (v10) — Hebrew נֹטֶה; 2 Sam 24:12 has another verb ("lifting"). "things" is supplied: the Hebrew has only "three".
- **"Choose thee" → "Accept for yourself"** (v11) — Hebrew קַבֶּל־לָךְ, the verb "receive, accept"; another verb than "choose" in v10.
- **"three years' famine"** (v12) — the figure as Chronicles prints it; 2 Sam 24:13 has "seven years".
- **"to be destroyed before thy foes, while that the sword of thine enemies overtaketh thee" → "being swept away from the face of your adversaries, with the sword of your enemies overtaking"** (v12) — Hebrew נִסְפֶּה מִפְּנֵי־צָרֶיךָ וְחֶרֶב אוֹיְבֶךָ לְמַשֶּׂגֶת, lit. "and the sword of your enemies to overtake"; "your enemies" may be read as singular. 2 Sam 24:13 has "your fleeing before your adversaries, while he pursues you".
- **"the sword of the LORD, even the pestilence … and the angel of the LORD destroying throughout all the coasts of Israel" → "the sword of Yahweh and pestilence in the land, and the angel of Yahweh destroying in all the border of Israel"** (v12) — Hebrew גְּבוּל "border", also the land within it. These words are in Chronicles only.
- **"angel"** (v12, 15, 16, 18, 20, 27, 30) — Hebrew מַלְאָךְ, the word for "messenger".
- **"destroy"** (v12, 15) — Hebrew הִשְׁחִית, the "ruin" verb of Gen 6:13; 2 Sam 24:16 has another form of it ("to ruin it").
- **"I am in a great strait" → "I am in great distress"** (v13) — Hebrew צַר־לִי מְאֹד. **"let me fall"** — singular in Chronicles; 2 Sam 24:14 has "let us fall". **"very great are his mercies" → "his compassions are very many"** — Hebrew רַבִּים רַחֲמָיו מְאֹד.
- **"there fell of Israel"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיִּפֹּל מִיִּשְׂרָאֵל; 2 Sam 24:15 has "there died of the people, from Dan and as far as Beersheba".
- **"And God sent an angel unto Jerusalem"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁלַח הָאֱלֹהִים מַלְאָךְ, "an angel", without the article; in 2 Sam 24:16 the angel stretches out his hand. **"the LORD beheld, and he repented him of the evil" → "Yahweh saw, and he relented concerning the evil"** — Hebrew וַיִּנָּחֶם עַל־הָרָעָה. **"It is enough, stay now thine hand" → "Enough! Now let your hand drop"** — Hebrew רַב עַתָּה הֶרֶף יָדֶךָ.
- **"stood by the threshingfloor of Ornan" → "was standing by the threshing floor of Ornan"** (v15) — Hebrew עֹמֵד; the name is "Ornan" in Chronicles, "Araunah" in 2 Sam 24.
- **"clothed in sackcloth" → "covered with sackcloth"** (v16) — Hebrew מְכֻסִּים בַּשַּׂקִּים. The verse is in Chronicles only.
- **"Is it not I that commanded the people to be numbered?" → "Was it not I who said to count among the people?"** (v17) — Hebrew הֲלֹא אֲנִי אָמַרְתִּי לִמְנוֹת בָּעָם. **"done evil indeed" → "surely done evil"** — Hebrew הָרֵעַ הֲרֵעוֹתִי, the infinitive with its verb; 2 Sam 24:17 has "I have done iniquity". **"these sheep" → "these, the flock"** — Hebrew וְאֵלֶּה הַצֹּאן.
- **"but not on thy people, that they should be plagued" → "and against your people, not for a plague"** (v17) — Hebrew וּבְעַמְּךָ לֹא לְמַגֵּפָה, word for word; in Chronicles only.
- **"commanded Gad to say to David" → "said to Gad to say to David"** (v18) — Hebrew אָמַר אֶל־גָּד לֵאמֹר לְדָוִיד; in 2 Sam 24:18 Gad speaks to David directly. **"set up" → "raise up"** — Hebrew לְהָקִים.
- **"at the saying of Gad" → "at the word of Gad"** (v19) — Hebrew בִּדְבַר־גָּד.
- **"And Ornan turned back, and saw the angel"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיָּשָׁב אָרְנָן וַיַּרְא אֶת־הַמַּלְאָךְ; in 2 Sam 24:20 Araunah looks down and sees the king. **"hid themselves" → "were hiding themselves"** — Hebrew מִתְחַבְּאִים.
- **"with his face to the ground" → "face to the earth"** (v21) — Hebrew אַפַּיִם אָרְצָה, lit. "nostrils to the earth"; no "his".
- **"Grant me" → "Give me"**, **"for the full price" → "for full silver"** (v22, 24) — Hebrew תְּנָה־לִּי, בְּכֶסֶף מָלֵא, as Gen 23:9; "silver" is the word for money.
- **"lo, I give thee the oxen" → "See, I give the herd"** (v23) — Hebrew הַבָּקָר; the verb is a perfect, "I have given". **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** — Hebrew מִנְחָה. The wheat is named in Chronicles only.
- **"I will verily buy it" → "I will surely buy"** (v24) — Hebrew קָנֹה אֶקְנֶה, the infinitive with its verb. **"I will not take that which is thine for the LORD" → "I will not lift up what is yours to Yahweh"** — Hebrew לֹא־אֶשָּׂא. **"without cost" → "for nothing"** — Hebrew חִנָּם.
- **"six hundred shekels of gold by weight" → "shekels of gold, six hundred by weight"** (v25) — Hebrew שִׁקְלֵי זָהָב מִשְׁקָל שֵׁשׁ מֵאוֹת, given "for the place"; 2 Sam 24:24 has "the threshing floor and the herd for silver, fifty shekels". The shekel is a weight, not a coin.
- **"offered" → "offered up"** (v26) — Hebrew וַיַּעַל. **"from heaven by fire" → "with fire from the heavens"** — Hebrew בָאֵשׁ מִן־הַשָּׁמַיִם. The answer by fire is in Chronicles only; 2 Sam 24:25 ends with the plague restrained.
- **"And the LORD commanded the angel" → "And Yahweh said to the angel"** (v27) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר, the verb "say"; what was said is not given.
- **"tabernacle" → "dwelling"** (v29) — Hebrew מִשְׁכָּן.
- **"to enquire of God" → "to seek Elohim"** (v30) — Hebrew לִדְרֹשׁ. **"he was afraid" → "he was terrified"** — Hebrew נִבְעַת. **"because of the sword"** — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי, lit. "from the face of".

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Sam 24.** The two chapters differ in who incites David (v1), in the figures (v5, 12, 25) and in the name Ornan / Araunah; each text is translated as it stands.
- **The threshing floor** — 2 Chr 3:1 names it as the place where Solomon built the house of Yahweh.
- **Fire from the heavens** (v26) — compare Lev 9:24; 1 Kgs 18:38; 2 Chr 7:1.
- **Gibeon** (v29) — the dwelling and the altar of the burnt offering were there (1 Chr 16:39).
