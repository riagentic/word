# 1 Chronicles 21

[1] A adversary — stood up against Israel and incited David to number Israel.

[2] David said to Joab and to the commanders of the people, "Go, number Israel from Beersheba to Dan, and bring me word, that I may know their number."

[3] Joab answered, "May Yahweh add to his people a hundred times as many as they are! But my lord the king — are they not all my lord's servants? Why does my lord require this thing? Why should he be a cause of guilt for Israel?"

[4] But the king's word prevailed against Joab. So Joab departed and went throughout all Israel, then came to Jerusalem.

[5] Joab gave the number of the enrollment of the people to David: all Israel were one million one hundred thousand men who drew the sword; Judah was four hundred seventy thousand men who drew the sword.

[6] But Levi and Benjamin he did not count among them, for the king's word was abhorrent to Joab.

[7] *Elohim* was displeased with this thing; so he struck Israel.

[8] David said to *Elohim*, "I have sinned greatly in that I have done this thing. Now, I beseech you, take away the iniquity of your servant; for I have acted very foolishly."

[9] Yahweh spoke to Gad, David's seer, saying,

[10] "Go and say to David: 'Thus says Yahweh: I hold out to you three things; choose one of them, that I may do it to you.'"

[11] So Gad came to David and said to him, "Thus says Yahweh: 'Take what you will —

[12] either three years of famine, or three months of being swept away before your foes while the sword of your enemies overtakes you, or else three days of the sword of Yahweh — pestilence in the land, with the Messenger of Yahweh destroying throughout all the territory of Israel.' Now therefore consider what word I shall return to him who sent me."

[13] David said to Gad, "I am in great distress; let me fall into the hand of Yahweh, for his mercies are very great; but do not let me fall into the hand of man."

[14] So Yahweh sent a pestilence upon Israel; seventy thousand men of Israel fell.

[15] *Elohim* sent a Messenger to Jerusalem to destroy it; but as he was destroying, Yahweh saw and relented of the evil, and said to the Messenger who was destroying, "Enough! Now stay your hand." The Messenger of Yahweh was standing by the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

[16] David lifted up his eyes and saw the Messenger of Yahweh standing between earth and heaven, with a drawn sword in his hand stretched out over Jerusalem. Then David and the elders, clothed in sackcloth, fell on their faces.

[17] David said to *Elohim*, "Is it not I who commanded the people to be numbered? I have sinned and done evil indeed; but these sheep, what have they done? O Yahweh my *Elohim*, please let your hand be against me and against my father's house, but not against your people, to plague them."

[18] Then the Messenger of Yahweh commanded Gad to say to David that David should go up and erect an altar to Yahweh on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

[19] David went up at the word of Gad, which he spoke in the name of Yahweh.

[20] Now Ornan had turned back and seen the Messenger; his four sons who were with him hid themselves. (Ornan was threshing wheat.)

[21] As David came to Ornan, Ornan looked and saw David; he went out from the threshing floor and bowed to David with his face to the ground.

[22] David said to Ornan, "Grant me the place of this threshing floor, that I may build on it an altar to Yahweh. Grant it to me at the full price, that the plague may be stayed from the people."

[23] Ornan said to David, "Take it for yourself; let my lord the king do what is good in his eyes. Behold, I give the oxen also for burnt offerings, the threshing sledges for wood, and the wheat for the grain offering — I give it all."

[24] King David said to Ornan, "No; but I will buy it for the full price. I will not take for Yahweh what is yours, nor offer burnt offerings without cost."

[25] So David gave to Ornan for the place six hundred shekels of gold by weight.

[26] David built there an altar to Yahweh and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings; he called upon Yahweh, and he answered him with fire from heaven on the altar of burnt offering.

[27] Yahweh commanded the Messenger, and he put his sword back into its sheath.

[28] At that time, when David saw that Yahweh had answered him at the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite, he sacrificed there.

[29] For the dwelling of Yahweh, which Moses had made in the wilderness, and the altar of burnt offering, were at that time at the high place at Gibeon.

[30] But David could not go before it to inquire of *Elohim*, for he was afraid because of the sword of the Messenger of Yahweh.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"rulers" → "commanders"** (v2) — *sarim*.
- **"cause of trespass" → "cause of guilt"** (v3) — *ashmah*.
- **"angel" → "Messenger"** (throughout).
- **"coasts" → "territory"** (v12).
- **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** (v23) — *minchah*.
- **"tabernacle" → "dwelling"** (v29) — *mishkan*.
- **"strait" → "distress"** (v13) — *tzar-li me'od*.

### Notes
- **2 Sam 24 parallel** — two main differences: (1) cause of anger: 2 Sam 24 "Yahweh incited David"; Chronicler: "a *satan* incited." Theological smoothing? Some propose reinterpreting 2 Sam 24's unspecified instigator. (2) Purchase price: 2 Sam 24:24 = 50 shekels silver for threshing floor and oxen; Chronicles 21:25 = 600 shekels gold for the whole site. Chronicler may count the wider temple-mount area.
- **Ornan = Araunah** — 2 Sam form; Chronicler's spelling.
- **Threshing floor = future temple site** (2 Chr 3:1). Chronicler's narrative purpose: locate the temple's origin in this incident. David buys the site, builds the altar; Solomon builds the temple here.
- **Fire from heaven** (v26) — legitimating sign; parallels Lev 9:24, 1 Kgs 18:38, 2 Chr 7:1.
- **Dual cult sites** (v29) — tabernacle at Gibeon, ark at Zion. This chapter establishes Jerusalem (Ornan's floor) as the legitimate sacrificial site, superseding Gibeon.
- **David's fear** (v30) — traumatized by what he saw, cannot approach Gibeon. Theological pivot: the proper sacrifice-site has shifted.
