# 1 Chronicles 10

[1] And the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ fought against ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ fled from the face of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ and fell slain on Mount Gilboa.

[2] And the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ clung close after ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and after his sons, and the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ struck down ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ and ⟦abinadab|Avinadav|Abinadab⟧ and ⟦malchishua|Malki-Shua|Malchi-shua⟧, the sons of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧.

[3] And the battle was heavy against ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, and the archers with the bow found him, and he was in anguish because of the shooters.

[4] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said to his weapon-bearer, "Draw your sword and pierce me with it, lest these uncircumcised come and deal ruthlessly with me." And his weapon-bearer was not willing, for he feared greatly. And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ took the sword and fell upon it.

[5] And his weapon-bearer saw that ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ was dead, and he also fell upon the sword and died.

[6] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ died, and his three sons; and all his house died together.

[7] And all the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ who were in the valley saw that they had fled and that ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and his sons had died, and they left their cities and fled, and the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ came and dwelt in them.

[8] And it came to pass on the morrow that the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ came to strip the slain, and they found ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and his sons fallen on Mount Gilboa.

[9] And they stripped him and lifted up his head and his gear, and they sent in the land of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ all around, to bring the news to their idols and to the people.

[10] And they put his gear in the house of their gods, and his skull they fastened in the house of Dagon.

[11] And all ⟦jabesh-gilead|Yavesh Gilad|Jabesh-gilead⟧ heard all that the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ had done to ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧.

[12] And every man of ability arose, and they lifted up the corpse of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and the corpses of his sons and brought them to ⟦jabesh|Yavesh|Jabesh⟧, and they buried their bones under the terebinth in ⟦jabesh|Yavesh|Jabesh⟧, and they fasted seven days.

[13] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ died in his unfaithfulness which he committed against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, on account of the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ which he did not keep, and also for asking of the ghost, to seek.

[14] And he did not seek by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he put him to death and turned the kingship about to David the son of ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Parallel: 1 Sam 31** — where the Hebrew of the two chapters is the same, the wording is that of 1 Samuel 31; every difference below is a difference in the Hebrew of Chronicles. Nothing is taken over from Samuel.
- **"Now the Philistines fought against Israel" → "And the Philistines fought against Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew נִלְחֲמוּ, a perfect; 1 Sam 31:1 has the participle ("were fighting"). **"the men of Israel"** (v1, 7) — Hebrew אִישׁ־יִשְׂרָאֵל, a collective singular, "the man of Israel", with a singular verb in v1 (וַיָּנָס); Samuel has the plural אַנְשֵׁי with a plural verb. English needs the plural (as Judg 20:20; 1 Sam 14:22). **"fled from before" → "fled from the face of"** — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי. **"on Mount Gilboa"** (v1, 8) — without the article here; Samuel has "the Gilboa".
- **"followed hard after Saul" → "clung close after Saul and after his sons"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיַּדְבְּקוּ … אַחֲרֵי … וְאַחֲרֵי, "clung after" (as 1 Sam 14:22); Samuel has the verb with אֶת, "caught up with". **"slew" → "struck down"** — Hebrew הִכָּה "strike". The names are joined by "and". Jonathan is written in the short form יוֹנָתָן here (Samuel: יְהוֹנָתָן); the token is the same.
- **"the battle went sore against Saul" → "the battle was heavy against Saul"** (v3) — Hebrew וַתִּכְבַּד הַמִּלְחָמָה עַל, the verb "be heavy"; Samuel has אֶל.
- **"the archers hit him, and he was wounded of the archers" → "the archers with the bow found him, and he was in anguish because of the shooters"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיִּמְצָאֻהוּ הַמּוֹרִים בַּקָּשֶׁת: מָצָא "find"; "with the bow" was dropped in the earlier text (Samuel has "the archers, men with the bow"). וַיָּחֶל מִן־הַיּוֹרִים: as the Masoretic Text is pointed, the verb is from חִיל "writhe, be in anguish" (Exod 15:14); the verse does not say that an arrow hit him. "He was wounded" (KJV, the earlier text) reads another verb (חָלַל or חָלָה; so the Septuagint is reported, from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept. Samuel has "in great anguish" (מְאֹד), which Chronicles lacks. The second word for the bowmen, הַיּוֹרִים, is another form (the simple stem) of the same verb "shoot" as הַמּוֹרִים: "shooters" beside "archers".
- **"armourbearer" → "weapon-bearer"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew נֹשֵׂא כֵלָיו, literally "the bearer of his gear": no armor is named. **"armour" → "gear"** (v9, 10) — the same word, כֵּלָיו.
- **"thrust me through" → "pierce me"** (v4) — Hebrew דָּקַר. **"abuse me" → "deal ruthlessly with me"** — Hebrew וְהִתְעַלְּלוּ־בִי, to treat a person as one pleases. Samuel has "come and pierce me and deal ruthlessly with me"; Chronicles has no second "pierce me". **"would not; for he was sore afraid" → "was not willing, for he feared greatly"** — Hebrew לֹא אָבָה; יָרֵא מְאֹד. **"took a sword" → "took the sword"** — the Hebrew has the article.
- **"he fell likewise on the sword, and died"** (v5) — Hebrew עַל־הַחֶרֶב "upon the sword" (Samuel: "upon his sword"); Samuel adds "with him".
- **"So Saul died, and his three sons, and all his house died together" → "And Saul died, and his three sons; and all his house died together"** (v6) — Hebrew וְכָל־בֵּיתוֹ יַחְדָּו מֵתוּ; the earlier text had "with his three sons". Samuel has "and his weapon-bearer, also all his men, on that day together" in place of "all his house".
- **"that were in the valley"** (v7) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־בָּעֵמֶק; Samuel has "across the valley and … across the Jordan". "They had fled": no subject is named here (Samuel: "the men of Israel had fled"). **"were dead" → "had died"** — Hebrew מֵתוּ. **"forsook their cities" → "left their cities"**; **"dwelt in them"** — Hebrew עָזַב; יָשַׁב "dwell". Samuel has "the cities".
- **"it came to pass on the morrow" kept** (v8) — Hebrew וַיְהִי מִמָּחֳרָת; the earlier text dropped it. "His sons": Samuel has "his three sons". **"fallen"** — Hebrew נֹפְלִים, a participle: lying fallen.
- **"when they had stripped him, they took his head, and his armour" → "they stripped him and lifted up his head and his gear"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיַּפְשִׁיטֻהוּ וַיִּשְׂאוּ: נָשָׂא "lift up, carry". Chronicles does not say here that the head was cut off (Samuel: "they cut off his head and stripped off his gear"). **"to carry tidings unto their idols" → "to bring the news to their idols"** — Hebrew לְבַשֵּׂר אֶת־עֲצַבֵּיהֶם; "good" (the earlier text) is not in the word; Samuel has "to the house of their idols". וַיְשַׁלְּחוּ "they sent" has no object: messengers, or the head and the gear.
- **"in the house of their gods"** (v10) — Hebrew בֵּית אֱלֹהֵיהֶם; the plural form may also be read "their god". Samuel names "the house of Ashtaroth". **"fastened his head in the temple of Dagon" → "his skull they fastened in the house of Dagon"** — Hebrew גֻּלְגָּלְתּוֹ "his skull" (another word than "head", v9); בֵּית "house", the same word as in the first half of the verse; תָּקְעוּ "thrust, drive in", rendered "fastened" as in 1 Sam 31:10. Samuel has instead "his body they fastened on the wall of Beth-shan".
- **"when all Jabesh-gilead heard" → "And all Jabesh-gilead heard all that"** (v11) — a sentence of its own, joined to v12 by "and". Samuel has "the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead heard of it, what".
- **"all the valiant men" → "every man of ability"** (v12) — Hebrew כָּל־אִישׁ חַיִל. **"took away the body" → "lifted up the corpse"** — Hebrew וַיִּשְׂאוּ אֶת־גּוּפַת: נָשָׂא "lift up" (Samuel: "took"); גּוּפָה, found only in this verse, is another word than Samuel's גְּוִיָּה "body": "corpse". Chronicles has neither the night march and the wall of Beth-shan nor the burning.
- **"under the oak" → "under the terebinth"** (v12) — Hebrew הָאֵלָה (Samuel: הָאֶשֶׁל "the tamarisk").
- **"for his transgression which he committed against the LORD" → "in his unfaithfulness which he committed against Yahweh"** (v13) — Hebrew בְּמַעֲלוֹ אֲשֶׁר מָעַל בַּיהוָה: noun and verb of one root, a breach of trust (Lev 5:15; Josh 7:1); בְּ is "in" (also "through, for"). **"even against the word of the LORD" → "on account of the word of Yahweh"** — Hebrew עַל־דְּבַר.
- **"for asking counsel of one that had a familiar spirit, to enquire of it" → "for asking of the ghost, to seek"** (v13) — Hebrew לִשְׁאוֹל בָּאוֹב לִדְרוֹשׁ: אוֹב is the spirit, not the person ("a medium", the earlier text); the exact sense is uncertain (1 Sam 28:7, 8). "Counsel", "for guidance" and "of it" are not written. לִשְׁאוֹל "to ask" has the letters of the name Saul (שָׁאוּל).
- **"And enquired not of the LORD: therefore he slew him" → "And he did not seek by Yahweh, and he put him to death"** (v14) — Hebrew דָּרַשׁ בְּ, the same verb as "to seek" in v13 (1 Sam 28:7 "seek by her"); וַיְמִיתֵהוּ, the hifil of "die". The subject is not named; the earlier text supplied "Yahweh" and "therefore".
- **"turned the kingdom unto David" → "turned the kingship about to David"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיַּסֵּב אֶת־הַמְּלוּכָה: מְלוּכָה is the office, "kingship"; הֵסֵב "make turn about" (1 Kgs 2:15).

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Sam 31** — Chronicler's abridged, theologically sharpened version. 1 Sam sets Saul's end in broader narrative (Ish-bosheth follows); Chronicles compresses to focus on dynastic transfer.
- **"All his house died together" (v6)** — overstatement (Ish-bosheth survives in 1 Sam); rhetorical closure of Saul's dynasty for Chronicler's purpose.
- **Theological gloss (v13-14)** — Chronicler adds the interpretation. Two reasons for Saul's fall: unfaithfulness to Yahweh, and asking of the ghost (En-dor episode, 1 Sam 28).
- **"Turned the kingship about" (v14)** — Yahweh actively transfers. David's kingship is Yahweh's doing.
- **Chronicler begins narrative at Saul's death** — no account of Saul's reign, no Samuel-Saul relationship. For Chronicler, history begins with David's rise.
