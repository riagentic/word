# John 14

[1] "Let not your heart be troubled. Believe in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and in me believe.

[2] In my Father's house are many abodes; but if not, I would have told you; for I go to prepare a place for you.

[3] And if I go and prepare a place for you, I come again and will take you along to myself, so that where I am, you also may be.

[4] And where I go, you know the way."

[5] ⟦thomas|Toma|Thomas⟧ says to him, "Lord, we do not know where you are going; how can we know the way?"

[6] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to him, "**I am the way and the truth and the life; no one comes to the Father except through me.**

[7] If you had come to know me, you would have known my Father also; from now on you know him and have seen him."

[8] ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧ says to him, "Lord, show us the Father, and it suffices us."

[9] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to him, "So long a time I am with you, and you have not come to know me, ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧? **The one who has seen me has seen the Father.** How do you say, 'Show us the Father'?

[10] Do you not believe that I am in the Father and the Father is in me? The words that I say to you I do not speak from myself; but the Father abiding in me does his works.

[11] Believe me that I am in the Father and the Father in me; but if not, believe because of the works themselves.

[12] Amen, amen, I say to you, the one who believes in me, the works that I do that one also will do, and greater than these will he do, because I go to the Father;

[13] and whatever you ask in my name, this I will do, so that the Father may be glorified in the Son;

[14] if you ask me anything in my name, I will do it.

[15] "**If you love me, you will keep my commandments;**

[16] and I will request the Father, and he will give you another advocate, that he may be with you forever,

[17] the Spirit of truth, which the world cannot receive, because it does not behold it nor know it; you know it, because it abides with you and will be in you.

[18] "I will not leave you orphans; I come to you.

[19] Yet a little while and the world beholds me no longer, but you behold me; because I live, you also will live.

[20] In that day you will know that I am in my Father, and you in me, and I in you.

[21] The one who has my commandments and keeps them, that one is the one who loves me; and the one who loves me will be loved by my Father, and I will love him and will disclose myself to him."

[22] ⟦jude|Yehudah|Judas⟧, not the Iscariot, says to him, "Lord, what has happened that you are about to disclose yourself to us and not to the world?"

[23] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered and said to him, "If anyone loves me, he will keep my word, and my Father will love him, and we will come to him and make an abode with him.

[24] The one who does not love me does not keep my words; and the word which you hear is not mine, but the Father's who sent me.

[25] "These things I have spoken to you while abiding with you;

[26] but the advocate, the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧, which the Father will send in my name, that one will teach you all things and will remind you of all things that I myself said to you.

[27] **Peace I leave to you; my peace I give to you; not as the world gives do I give to you.** Let not your heart be troubled, nor let it be cowardly.

[28] You heard that I said to you, 'I go, and I come to you.' If you loved me, you would have rejoiced that I go to the Father, because the Father is greater than I.

[29] And now I have told you before it comes to pass, so that when it comes to pass you may believe.

[30] No longer will I speak much with you, for the ruler of the world is coming; and in me he has nothing,

[31] but so that the world may know that I love the Father, and just as the Father commanded me, so I do. Arise, let us go from here."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v26) — Hebrew רוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ *Ruach HaQodesh* ("Spirit of Holiness"); archaic "Ghost" replaced.
- **"Verily, verily" → "Amen, amen"** (v12) — Greek ἀμὴν ἀμήν, left untranslated as the Greek leaves it.
- **"saith" → "says"** (v5, 6, 8, 9, 22) — the Greek presents (λέγει) are kept.
- **"Let not your heart be troubled" kept** (v1, 27) — Greek ἡ καρδία, singular; ταρασσέσθω, the verb of 13:21.
- **"ye believe in God, believe also in me" → "Believe in *Elohim*, and in me believe"** (v1) — Greek πιστεύετε … πιστεύετε, one form twice; each can be a command ("believe") or a statement ("you believe").
- **"mansions" → "abodes"** (v2) — Greek μοναί, places to abide (root of μένω "abide", v10, 17, 25); the word returns in v23.
- **"if it were not so, I would have told you. I go to prepare" → "but if not, I would have told you; for I go to prepare"** (v2) — Greek εἰ δὲ μή, εἶπον ἂν ὑμῖν ὅτι πορεύομαι (ὅτι in WH, Treg, NA28; not in RP). ὅτι is "for" or "that": the words can also be read as a question, "would I have told you that I go to prepare a place for you?"; the printed text has no question mark.
- **"I will come again, and receive you unto myself" → "I come again and will take you along to myself"** (v3) — Greek ἔρχομαι, a present; παραλήμψομαι, "take along".
- **"And whither I go ye know, and the way ye know" → "And where I go, you know the way"** (v4) — Greek ὅπου ἐγὼ ὑπάγω οἴδατε τὴν ὁδόν (WH, Treg, NA28); KJV follows the longer RP wording.
- **"how can we know the way?" kept** (v5) — δυνάμεθα τὴν ὁδὸν εἰδέναι (NA28, RP; 2–2, NA28 decides); WH and Treg read οἴδαμεν τὴν ὁδόν, "how do we know the way?".
- **"If ye had known me, ye should have known my Father also: and from henceforth" → "If you had come to know me, you would have known my Father also; from now on"** (v7) — the printed text: εἰ ἐγνώκειτέ με … ἂν ᾔδειτε (WH, Treg, SBLGNT), two Greek verbs, γινώσκω and οἶδα; "you know him" is γινώσκω again. NA28 reads εἰ ἐγνώκατέ με … γνώσεσθε, "If you have come to know me, you will know my Father also", and adds "and" before "from now on" (with RP). WH omits the last "him".
- **"it sufficeth us" → "it suffices us"** (v8) — Greek ἀρκεῖ ἡμῖν.
- **"Have I been so long time with you, and yet hast thou not known me" → "So long a time I am with you, and you have not come to know me"** (v9) — Greek εἰμι, a present; ἔγνωκας, a perfect of γινώσκω; "yet / still" is not in the Greek. "with you" is plural, "you have not come to know" singular.
- **"He that hath seen me" → "The one who has seen me"** (v9) — Greek ὁ ἑωρακώς, a perfect.
- **"the words that I speak unto you I speak not of myself" → "The words that I say to you I do not speak from myself"** (v10) — Greek λέγω (WH, Treg, NA28; RP λαλῶ) and λαλῶ, two verbs; ἀπ' ἐμαυτοῦ "from myself"; "authority" is not in the Greek. "words" is ῥήματα, not λόγος (v23–24).
- **"the Father that dwelleth in me, he doeth the works" → "the Father abiding in me does his works"** (v10) — Greek μένων, the gospel's "abide"; τὰ ἔργα αὐτοῦ "his works" (WH, Treg, NA28).
- **"or else believe me for the very works' sake" → "but if not, believe because of the works themselves"** (v11) — Greek εἰ δὲ μή, as in v2; the second "me" is not in the printed text (WH, NA28; Treg and RP add μοι).
- **"the works that I do shall he do also" → "the works that I do that one also will do"** (v12) — Greek κἀκεῖνος, "that one also"; the believer stands first in the sentence, outside the clause. "greater than these": "works" is not repeated in the Greek. "the Father" (WH, Treg, NA28; RP "my Father").
- **"If ye shall ask any thing" → "if you ask me anything"** (v14) — Greek αἰτήσητέ με (WH, NA28, RP; Treg omits "me"); ἐγὼ ποιήσω, "I will do" with an emphatic "I" (WH τοῦτο ποιήσω, "this I will do").
- **"keep my commandments" → "you will keep my commandments"** (v15) — Greek τηρήσετε, a future (WH, Treg, NA28); KJV follows the imperative τηρήσατε (RP). v15–17 are one sentence.
- **"I will pray the Father" → "I will request the Father"** (v16) — Greek ἐρωτήσω, a different verb from "ask" (αἰτέω) in v13–14; the two stand side by side again in 16:23–26.
- **"Comforter" → "advocate"** (v16, 26) — Greek παράκλητος, one called to someone's side to speak for him or help him; the word of 1 John 2:1. "another advocate" (ἄλλον παράκλητον) can also be read "another, an advocate".
- **"that he may abide with you for ever" → "that he may be with you forever"** (v16) — Greek ᾖ, "be" (WH, Treg, NA28, which sets it after αἰῶνα; RP μένῃ "abide"). "he" follows the masculine παράκλητος. εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα, literally "unto the age".
- **"whom the world cannot receive, because it seeth him not, neither knoweth him" → "which the world cannot receive, because it does not behold it nor know it"** (v17) — Greek ὅ … αὐτό, neuter, three times: the gender is grammatical, that of the noun πνεῦμα ("Spirit"); English "which" and "it" keep it and say nothing about whether the Spirit is a person. In v26 the pronoun ἐκεῖνος, "that one", is masculine, as the noun παράκλητος is. θεωρεῖ "behold".
- **"for he dwelleth with you, and shall be in you" → "because it abides with you and will be in you"** (v17) — Greek μένει; ἔσται "will be" (NA28, RP; 2–2, NA28 decides); WH and Treg read ἐστίν, "is in you". "but" before "you know" is not in the printed text.
- **"I will not leave you comfortless: I will come to you" → "I will not leave you orphans; I come to you"** (v18) — Greek ὀρφανούς; ἔρχομαι, a present.
- **"the world seeth me no more; but ye see me" → "the world beholds me no longer, but you behold me"** (v19) — Greek θεωρεῖ, θεωρεῖτε, presents. ὅτι ἐγὼ ζῶ καὶ ὑμεῖς ζήσετε can also be read "because I live and you will live".
- **"he it is that loveth me" → "that one is the one who loves me"** (v21) — Greek ἐκεῖνός ἐστιν ὁ ἀγαπῶν με.
- **"will manifest myself to him" → "will disclose myself to him"** (v21, 22) — Greek ἐμφανίζω, "make visible, make plain", a different verb from φανερόω "manifest" (17:6).
- **"Judas saith unto him, not Iscariot" → "*Yehudah*, not the Iscariot, says to him"** (v22) — Greek οὐχ ὁ Ἰσκαριώτης, with the article.
- **"how is it that thou wilt manifest" → "what has happened that you are about to disclose"** (v22) — Greek τί γέγονεν ὅτι … μέλλεις (WH, Treg, SBLGNT; NA28 and RP begin with καί, "and what has happened").
- **"Jesus answered and said unto him" kept** (v23) — Greek ἀπεκρίθη … καὶ εἶπεν, both verbs.
- **"make our abode with him" → "make an abode with him"** (v23) — Greek μονὴν … ποιησόμεθα, the word of v2; "our" is not in the Greek.
- **"being yet present with you" → "while abiding with you"** (v25) — Greek παρ' ὑμῖν μένων; "yet / still" is not in the Greek.
- **"whom the Father will send … he shall teach you" → "which the Father will send … that one will teach you"** (v26) — Greek ὅ, neuter, after τὸ πνεῦμα τὸ ἅγιον; ἐκεῖνος, masculine, taking up ὁ παράκλητος. See v17.
- **"bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you" → "will remind you of all things that I myself said to you"** (v26) — Greek ὑπομνήσει; εἶπον, an aorist; the verse follows ἃ εἶπον ὑμῖν ἐγώ, with an emphatic "I" (WH, NA28); Treg, RP and SBLGNT have no ἐγώ.
- **"Peace I leave with you" → "Peace I leave to you"** (v27) — Greek ἀφίημι ὑμῖν, a plain dative. Greek εἰρήνη *eirēnē* (Hebrew *shalom*), as in the greeting of ch. 20 (vv19, 21, 26).
- **"neither let it be afraid" → "nor let it be cowardly"** (v27) — Greek δειλιάτω, from δειλός "cowardly" (Mark 4:40); "fear" is a different word.
- **"I go away, and come again unto you" → "I go, and I come to you"** (v28) — Greek Ὑπάγω (as in v4, 5; 13:33, 36) καὶ ἔρχομαι, two presents; "again" is not in the Greek. "that I go to the Father" is a second verb, πορεύομαι (as in v2, 3, 12); "I said" before it is not in the critical text (RP adds εἶπον). ὅτι πορεύομαι can be "that I go" or "because I go". "the Father" (WH, Treg, NA28; RP "my Father").
- **"If ye loved me, ye would rejoice" → "If you loved me, you would have rejoiced"** (v28) — Greek εἰ ἠγαπᾶτε … ἐχάρητε ἄν.
- **"the prince of this world cometh, and hath nothing in me" → "the ruler of the world is coming; and in me he has nothing"** (v30) — Greek ὁ τοῦ κόσμου ἄρχων, without "this"; ἐν ἐμοὶ οὐκ ἔχει οὐδέν, literally "in me he has nothing"; "claim" is not in the Greek.
- **"But that the world may know … even so I do" → "but so that the world may know … so I do"** (v31) — Greek ἀλλ' ἵνα γνῷ ὁ κόσμος ὅτι ἀγαπῶ τὸν πατέρα, καὶ καθὼς ἐνετείλατό μοι ὁ πατὴρ οὕτως ποιῶ, in the Greek order. It can be read "but (this happens) so that the world may know that I love the Father and that I do just as the Father commanded me", or "but, so that the world may know …, I do just as the Father commanded me". ἐνετείλατο "commanded" (NA28, RP; 2–2, NA28 decides); WH and Treg read ἐντολὴν ἔδωκεν, "gave me a commandment".
- **"Arise, let us go hence" kept** (v31) — Greek Ἐγείρεσθε, ἄγωμεν ἐντεῦθεν.
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** (thee, thou, thy, ye, hast, art, etc.) modernized throughout.
