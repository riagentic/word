## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"law" → "Torah"** (v5, 17) — Hebrew תּוֹרָה *Torah* preserved.
- **"7:53–8:11 bracket notation"** — double-bracket [[ ]] marks the *Pericope Adulterae* as textually doubtful; absent from earliest/best MSS (p66, p75, Sinaiticus, Vaticanus); present in later manuscripts and likely authentic oral tradition.
- **"I am he"** (v24, 28) and **"before Abraham was, I am"** (v58) — Greek ἐγώ εἰμι *egō eimi* preserved; deliberate echo of Ex 3:14 (LXX: ἐγώ εἰμι ὁ ὤν).
- **"Your father the devil"** (v44) — retained; significant doctrinal statement left as-is.
- **"Abraham" / "father Abraham"** — retained as familiar form; no Hebrew alteration needed (*Avraham* would be pedantic here).
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** (thee, thou, thy, ye, hast, wilt, art, etc.) modernized throughout.

### Notes
- **7:53–8:11** — the *Pericope Adulterae* is absent from the earliest and most reliable MSS and appears in different locations in later ones. Most scholars regard it as non-Johannine but likely authentic tradition.
- **v58** — "I am" (*egō eimi*) echoes Ex 3:14.
