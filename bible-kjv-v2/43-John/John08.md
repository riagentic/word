## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"law" → "Torah"** (v5, 17) — Hebrew תּוֹרָה *Torah* preserved.
- **"7:53–8:11 bracket notation"** — double brackets [[ ]] mark the *Pericope Adulterae*: of the editions only RP has it in the text here; the SBLGNT prints it in double brackets, and the earliest manuscripts (p66, p75, Sinaiticus, Vaticanus) do not have it. It is translated as printed, like any other passage.
- **"Verily, verily" → "Amen, amen"** (v34, 51, 58) — Greek ἀμὴν ἀμήν, left untranslated.
- **"early in the morning he came again into the temple, and all the people came unto him; and he sat down, and taught them" → "at dawn he came again into the temple, and all the people were coming; and having sat down, he was teaching them"** (v2) — Greek ὄρθρου; ἤρχετο, ἐδίδασκεν, imperfects; the printed text has no "to him".
- **"brought unto him a woman taken in adultery; and when they had set her in the midst, they say unto him" → "bring to him a woman caught in adultery; and having set her in the midst, they say to him, tempting him"** (v3, 4) — Greek ἄγουσιν, λέγουσιν, presents; the printed text has πειράζοντες, "tempting" (putting to the test), in v4 as well as v6.
- **"this woman was taken in adultery, in the very act" → "this woman was caught in the very act, committing adultery"** (v4) — Greek κατελήφθη ἐπ' αὐτοφόρῳ μοιχευομένη, a participle.
- **"Moses in the law commanded us, that such should be stoned: but what sayest thou?" → "in the Torah Moses gave us commandment that such women be stoned; you therefore, what do you say?"** (v5) — Greek ἐνετείλατο (root of ἐντολή "commandment"); τὰς τοιαύτας, feminine; λιθοβολεῖσθαι, passive; σὺ οὖν.
- **"This they said, tempting him, that they might have to accuse him. But Jesus stooped down, and with his finger wrote on the ground, as though he heard them not" → "this they were saying, tempting him, that they might have something of which to accuse him. But *Yeshua*, stooping down, was writing with his finger on the ground, taking no notice"** (v6) — Greek ἔλεγον, ἔγραφεν, imperfects; μὴ προσποιούμενος, "not pretending, taking no notice" (so the printed text; the words are not in every form of this passage).
- **"So when they continued asking him, he lifted up himself, and said unto them, He that is without sin among you, let him first cast a stone at her" → "But when they continued asking him, he straightened up and said to them, Let the sinless one of you first cast the stone at her"** (v7) — Greek ἀνακύψας (the opposite of κύψας "stooping", v6, 8); ὁ ἀναμάρτητος ὑμῶν; τὸν λίθον, "the stone", with the article (Deut 17:7).
- **"they which heard it, being convicted by their own conscience, went out one by one, beginning at the eldest, even unto the last" → "they, having heard, and being reproved by their conscience, were going out one by one, beginning from the elders"** (v9) — Greek ἐλεγχόμενοι, the verb "reprove" of v46; ἐξήρχοντο, imperfect; ἀπὸ τῶν πρεσβυτέρων, "from the elders" (the older men or the elders); the printed text has no "unto the last".
- **"Jesus was left alone, and the woman standing in the midst" → "*Yeshua* was left alone, and the woman, being in the midst"** (v9) — Greek ἐν μέσῳ οὖσα; "before him" is not in the Greek.
- **"When Jesus had lifted up himself, and saw none but the woman, he said unto her, Woman, where are those thine accusers? hath no man condemned thee?" → "And *Yeshua*, having straightened up and beholding no one but the woman, said to her, Where are those accusers of yours? Did no one condemn you?"** (v10) — Greek θεασάμενος; ἐκεῖνοι οἱ κατήγοροί σου; κατέκρινεν, aorist. The printed text has no "Woman" here.
- **"Neither do I condemn thee: go, and sin no more" → "Neither do I judge you; go, and sin no longer"** (v11) — the printed text has κρίνω, "judge", beside κατέκρινεν, "condemned", in v10; μηκέτι ἁμάρτανε, as in 5:14. "From now on" is not in the printed text.
- **"Then spake Jesus again unto them … he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness" → "So again *Yeshua* spoke to them … the one who follows me shall by no means walk in the darkness"** (v12) — Greek οὐ μή, the strong negation; ἐν τῇ σκοτίᾳ, with the article. In v12, 18 ἐγώ εἰμι has a predicate.
- **"Thou bearest record of thyself; thy record is not true" → "You testify concerning yourself; your testimony is not true"** (v13, 14, 17, 18) — Greek μαρτυρέω, μαρτυρία, as in 5:31–39.
- **"Jesus answered and said unto them, Though I bear record of myself, yet my record is true: for I know whence I came, and whither I go; but ye cannot tell whence I come, and whither I go" → "*Yeshua* answered and said to them, Even if I testify concerning myself, my testimony is true, because I know where I came from and where I go; but you do not know where I come from or where I go"** (v14) — Greek ἀπεκρίθη … καὶ εἶπεν; κἄν; ὑπάγω, "go" (v14, 21, 22); ἤ "or" (WH Treg NA28; RP καί).
- **"And yet if I judge, my judgment is true: for I am not alone" → "And yet if I do judge, my judgment is true, because I am not alone"** (v16) — Greek ἀληθινή (WH Treg NA28; RP ἀληθής, the word of v13, 14, 17); μόνος οὐκ εἰμί, "I am not alone": no verb "judge" in this clause.
- **"It is also written in your law" → "And in your own Torah also it is written"** (v17) — Greek καὶ ἐν τῷ νόμῳ δὲ τῷ ὑμετέρῳ, with the emphatic "your" (Deut 19:15).
- **"Then said they unto him" → "So they were saying to him"** (v19, 22, 25) — Greek ἔλεγον, imperfect. ᾔδειτε, "knew", twice, the verb οἶδα.
- **"as he taught in the temple: and no man laid hands on him" → "teaching in the temple; and no one seized him"** (v20) — Greek ἐπίασεν, the verb of 7:30, 32, 44; καί is "and"; ῥήματα, "spoken words".
- **"I go my way, and ye shall seek me, and shall die in your sins: whither I go, ye cannot come" → "I go, and you will seek me, and in your sin you will die; where I go, you are not able to come"** (v21) — Greek ἐν τῇ ἁμαρτίᾳ ὑμῶν, singular, placed before the verb (v24 plural).
- **"Will he kill himself? because he saith" → "Will he kill himself, that he says"** (v22) — Greek Μήτι ἀποκτενεῖ ἑαυτόν, ὅτι λέγει; the question expects "no".
- **"Ye are from beneath; I am from above: ye are of this world" → "You are from the things below; I am from the things above. You are of this world"** (v23) — Greek ἐκ τῶν κάτω … ἐκ τῶν ἄνω, neuter plurals with the article; ἐκ four times ("out of"); ἔλεγεν, imperfect (RP εἶπεν).
- **"I said therefore unto you, that ye shall die in your sins: for if ye believe not that I am he, ye shall die in your sins" → "So I said to you that you will die in your sins; for unless you believe that I am, you will die in your sins"** (v24) — Greek ἐὰν γὰρ μὴ πιστεύσητε ὅτι ἐγώ εἰμι: ἐγώ εἰμι stands absolutely, with no predicate; "he" is not in the Greek. The same in v28 and v58 (compare Exod 3:14; Deut 32:39; Isa 41:4; 43:10 LXX ἐγώ εἰμι). In 6:20 the same words identify the speaker and are rendered "It is I".
- **"Who art thou? And Jesus saith unto them, Even the same that I said unto you from the beginning" → "You, who are you? *Yeshua* said to them, Why do I even speak to you at all?"** (v25) — Greek Τὴν ἀρχὴν ὅ τι καὶ λαλῶ ὑμῖν; the hardest sentence of the chapter. The letters are the same in all editions: ὅ τι (Treg NA28 RP) or ὅτι (WH) differ only in the spacing, and the punctuation is the editors'. Printed as a question, with τὴν ἀρχήν as "at all" and ὅ τι as "why": "Why do I speak to you at all?" As a statement, with τὴν ἀρχήν as "from the beginning": "(I am) from the beginning what I also speak to you", or "What I have been saying to you from the beginning". The verse follows the printed punctuation; λαλῶ is a present, "speak".
- **"I have many things to say and to judge of you … and I speak to the world those things which I have heard of him" → "I have many things to speak and to judge concerning you … and I, the things which I heard from him, these I speak unto the world"** (v26) — Greek λαλεῖν; ἀληθής; ἤκουσα, aorist; ταῦτα λαλῶ εἰς τὸν κόσμον, "speak into the world"; no "declare".
- **"They understood not that he spake to them of the Father" → "They did not come to know that he was speaking to them of the Father"** (v27) — Greek οὐκ ἔγνωσαν; τὸν πατέρα αὐτοῖς ἔλεγεν, imperfect.
- **"Then said Jesus unto them, When ye have lifted up the Son of man, then shall ye know that I am he, and that I do nothing of myself; but as my Father hath taught me, I speak these things" → "So *Yeshua* said, When you have lifted up the Son of Man, then you will know that I am, and from myself I do nothing, but just as the Father taught me, these things I speak"** (v28) — "to them" (NA28 RP) is not in WH Treg and the printed text; ὑψώσητε, "lift up, exalt" (3:14; 12:32); ἐγώ εἰμι, absolute as in v24; ἀπ' ἐμαυτοῦ, "from myself" (no word "authority"); ὁ πατήρ (RP adds "my"); ἐδίδαξεν, aorist.
- **"the Father hath not left me alone; for I do always those things that please him" → "he did not leave me alone, because I always do the things pleasing to him"** (v29) — Greek οὐκ ἀφῆκέν με μόνον, aorist ("the Father" only in RP); τὰ ἀρεστὰ αὐτῷ.
- **"As he spake these words" → "As he was speaking these things"** (v30) — Greek ταῦτα αὐτοῦ λαλοῦντος.
- **"Then said Jesus to those Jews which believed on him, If ye continue in my word, then are ye my disciples indeed" → "So *Yeshua* was saying to the Jews who had believed him, If you abide in my word, you are truly my disciples"** (v31) — Greek ἔλεγεν, imperfect; τοὺς πεπιστευκότας αὐτῷ, a perfect participle with the dative: "believed him" (v30 has εἰς, "believed in him"); μείνητε, the verb μένω "abide" (also v35).
- **"the truth shall make you free" → "the truth will set you free"** (v32, 36) — Greek ἐλευθερώσει.
- **"We be Abraham's seed, and were never in bondage to any man: how sayest thou, Ye shall be made free?" → "We are seed of Abraham, and to no one have we ever been slaves; how do you say, You will become free?"** (v33, 37) — Greek σπέρμα "seed"; οὐδενὶ δεδουλεύκαμεν πώποτε, a perfect of δουλεύω "be a slave to" (root of δοῦλος, v34, 35); ἐλεύθεροι γενήσεσθε.
- **"Whosoever committeth sin is the servant of sin" → "everyone who does sin is a slave of sin"** (v34) — Greek ὁ ποιῶν τὴν ἁμαρτίαν, "does sin" (1 John 3:4, 8), not "practices"; δοῦλος "slave", with the genitive "of sin".
- **"the servant abideth not in the house for ever: but the Son abideth ever" → "the slave does not abide in the house forever; the son abides forever"** (v35) — Greek μένει, twice; εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα, "unto the age"; ὁ υἱός: "the son" of the household in the saying, then "the Son" in v36 (the Greek has no capitals).
- **"ye shall be free indeed" → "you will really be free"** (v36) — Greek ὄντως.
- **"because my word hath no place in you" → "because my word finds no room in you"** (v37) — Greek οὐ χωρεῖ ἐν ὑμῖν: χωρέω is "make room, have room" or "go forward, make progress" ("my word makes no progress in you").
- **"I speak that which I have seen with my Father: and ye do that which ye have seen with your father" → "The things which I have seen with the Father I speak; and you therefore do the things which you heard from the father"** (v38) — Greek ἃ ἐγὼ ἑώρακα παρὰ τῷ πατρὶ λαλῶ· καὶ ὑμεῖς οὖν ἃ ἠκούσατε παρὰ τοῦ πατρὸς ποιεῖτε (WH Treg NA28): no "my" and no "your" (RP has both, and "seen" twice). ποιεῖτε is indicative ("you do") or imperative ("do") in form; and "the father" in the second half can be their father (so v41, 44) or the same Father ("do, therefore, what you heard from the Father"). The Greek has no capitals; the verse keeps the plain words.
- **"If ye were Abraham's children, ye would do the works of Abraham" → "If you are children of Abraham, you would be doing the works of Abraham"** (v39) — Greek εἰ … ἐστε, a present (WH Treg NA28; RP ἦτε "were"), with ἐποιεῖτε, an imperfect (Treg NA28 RP; WH ποιεῖτε, "do the works of Abraham"). λέγει, a present; ἀπεκρίθησαν καὶ εἶπαν, both verbs.
- **"a man that hath told you the truth, which I have heard of God: this did not Abraham" → "a man who has spoken to you the truth which I heard from *Elohim*; this Abraham did not do"** (v40) — Greek λελάληκα, a perfect; ἤκουσα, aorist; τοῦτο Ἀβραὰμ οὐκ ἐποίησεν.
- **"Ye do the deeds of your father. Then said they to him, We be not born of fornication; we have one Father, even God" → "You do the works of your father. They said to him, We have not been begotten of sexual immorality; one Father we have, *Elohim*"** (v41) — Greek τὰ ἔργα, as in v39; no "then" in WH Treg and the printed text (NA28 RP οὖν); οὐ γεγεννήμεθα, a perfect (NA28 RP) ] οὐκ ἐγεννήθημεν, aorist (WH Treg) — 2–2, NA28 decides; γεννάω "beget", of the father; πορνεία; ἕνα πατέρα ἔχομεν τὸν θεόν.
- **"for I proceeded forth and came from God; neither came I of myself, but he sent me" → "for I came out from *Elohim* and am here; for neither have I come from myself, but that one sent me"** (v42) — Greek ἐκ τοῦ θεοῦ ἐξῆλθον καὶ ἥκω: ἐκ … ἐξ-, "out of"; ἥκω, "I have come, I am here"; ἐλήλυθα, a perfect; ἐκεῖνος.
- **"Why do ye not understand my speech? even because ye cannot hear my word" → "Why do you not know my speech? Because you are not able to hear my word"** (v43) — Greek τὴν λαλιὰν τὴν ἐμὴν οὐ γινώσκετε: λαλιά, "speech, way of speaking", beside λόγος "word"; γινώσκω, "know, come to know". "Bear" is not in the Greek.
- **"Ye are of your father the devil, and the lusts of your father ye will do" → "You are of the father, the devil, and the desires of your father you want to do"** (v44) — Greek ὑμεῖς ἐκ τοῦ πατρὸς τοῦ διαβόλου ἐστέ, literally "you are of the father the devil" ("your" comes only in the next clause); ἐπιθυμίας "desires"; θέλετε ποιεῖν, "you want to do".
- **"He was a murderer from the beginning, and abode not in the truth, because there is no truth in him" → "That one was a murderer from the beginning, and did not stand in the truth, because truth is not in him"** (v44) — Greek ἐκεῖνος; ἀνθρωποκτόνος, "manslayer" (1 John 3:15). The editions differ only in the breathing: οὐκ ἔστηκεν (WH NA28, printed), an imperfect, "did not stand, was not standing", or οὐχ ἕστηκεν (Treg RP), a perfect, "does not stand"; the old manuscripts write no breathings, though the spelling οὐκ / οὐχ goes with one or the other.
- **"When he speaketh a lie, he speaketh of his own: for he is a liar, and the father of it" → "When he speaks the lie, he speaks out of his own things, because he is a liar and the father of it"** (v44) — Greek τὸ ψεῦδος, with the article; ἐκ τῶν ἰδίων, "out of his own (things)": "character" is not in the Greek; ὁ πατὴρ αὐτοῦ, "the father of it" (of the lie) or "of him" (of the liar).
- **"And because I tell you the truth, ye believe me not" → "But I, because I say the truth, you do not believe me"** (v45) — Greek ἐγὼ δὲ ὅτι τὴν ἀλήθειαν λέγω.
- **"Which of you convinceth me of sin?" → "Which of you reproves me concerning sin?"** (v46) — Greek ἐλέγχει, "reprove, convict, show to be in the wrong"; ἀλήθειαν here without the article.
- **"He that is of God heareth God's words: ye therefore hear them not, because ye are not of God" → "The one who is of *Elohim* hears the words of *Elohim*; because of this you do not hear, because you are not of *Elohim*"** (v47) — Greek ῥήματα; διὰ τοῦτο … ὅτι; "them" is not in the Greek.
- **"Say we not well that thou art a Samaritan, and hast a devil?" → "Do we not say well that you are a Samaritan and have a demon?"** (v48, 49, 52) — Greek καλῶς λέγομεν; δαιμόνιον "demon". ἀπεκρίθησαν … καὶ εἶπαν, both verbs.
- **"I seek not mine own glory: there is one that seeketh and judgeth" kept** (v50) — Greek ἔστιν ὁ ζητῶν καὶ κρίνων; "it" is not in the Greek.
- **"If a man keep my saying, he shall never see death" → "if anyone keeps my word, he will by no means behold death forever"** (v51, 52) — Greek τὸν ἐμὸν λόγον, "my word"; θάνατον οὐ μὴ θεωρήσῃ εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα: the strong negation with "unto the age", which together mean "never"; θεωρήσῃ "behold". In v52 the hearers repeat it with γεύσηται θανάτου, "taste of death".
- **"Now we know that thou hast a devil" → "Now we have come to know that you have a demon"** (v52) — Greek ἐγνώκαμεν, a perfect. "Then" (NA28 RP οὖν) is not in WH Treg and the printed text.
- **"whom makest thou thyself?" → "whom do you make yourself?"** (v53) — Greek τίνα σεαυτὸν ποιεῖς; (5:18). The question with μή expects "no".
- **"If I honour myself, my honour is nothing: it is my Father that honoureth me; of whom ye say, that he is your God" → "If I glorify myself, my glory is nothing; it is my Father who glorifies me, of whom you say, He is our *Elohim*"** (v54) — Greek δοξάσω, δόξα, δοξάζων, one root, "glorify, glory"; θεὸς ἡμῶν, "our God" (Treg NA28 RP; WH ὑμῶν, "your God"). The sentence runs on into v55.
- **"Yet ye have not known him; but I know him" → "and you have not come to know him, but I know him"** (v55) — two verbs: ἐγνώκατε (γινώσκω), οἶδα; καί is "and"; ὅμοιος ὑμῖν ψεύστης, "like you, a liar".
- **"Your father Abraham rejoiced to see my day: and he saw it, and was glad" → "Abraham your father exulted that he might see my day, and he saw and rejoiced"** (v56) — Greek ἠγαλλιάσατο ἵνα ἴδῃ, "exulted that he should see"; ἐχάρη, "rejoiced"; no "it".
- **"Thou art not yet fifty years old" → "You do not yet have fifty years"** (v57) — Greek πεντήκοντα ἔτη οὔπω ἔχεις, as in 5:5.
- **"Before Abraham was, I am" → "before Abraham came to be, I am"** (v58) — Greek πρὶν Ἀβραὰμ γενέσθαι ἐγὼ εἰμί: γενέσθαι, "came into being, was born", against the simple εἰμί "am"; ἐγώ εἰμι stands absolutely, as in v24, 28. The hearers take it as blasphemy (v59).
- **"Then took they up stones to cast at him: but Jesus hid himself, and went out of the temple, going through the midst of them, and so passed by" → "So they took up stones that they might cast them at him; but *Yeshua* was hidden and went out of the temple"** (v59) — Greek ἐκρύβη, a passive form: "was hidden" or "hid himself"; "going through the midst of them, and so passed by" (RP) is not in WH Treg NA28.

### Notes
- **7:53–8:11** — the *Pericope Adulterae* is absent from the earliest and most reliable MSS and appears in different locations in later ones (after 7:36, after 21:25, or after Luke 21:38), and its wording varies much between them. Most scholars regard it as non-Johannine but likely authentic tradition.
- **v58** — "I am" (*egō eimi*): compare Exod 3:14 (LXX ἐγώ εἰμι ὁ ὤν) and the "I am he" of Isa 43:10.
- **v17** — Deut 17:6; 19:15.
