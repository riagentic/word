# John 5

[1] After these things there was a feast of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, and ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ went up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[2] Now there is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ by the sheep gate a pool, which is called in ⟦hebrew|Ivrit|Hebrew⟧ ⟦bethesda|Beit Zata|Bethzatha⟧, having five colonnades.

[3] In these lay a multitude of those who were sick, blind, lame, withered.

[5] Now a certain man was there who had been thirty-eight years in his sickness.

[6] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, seeing this man lying and knowing that he has already been so a long time, says to him, "Do you want to become healthy?"

[7] The sick man answered him, "Lord, I have no man to put me into the pool when the water is troubled; but while I am coming, another goes down before me."

[8] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to him, "Arise, take up your pallet and walk."

[9] And immediately the man became healthy, and he took up his pallet and was walking.

Now it was a ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ on that day.

[10] So the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ were saying to the one who had been healed, "It is a ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, and it is not permitted for you to take up the pallet."

[11] But he answered them, "The one who made me healthy, that one said to me, 'Take up your pallet and walk.'"

[12] They asked him, "Who is the man who said to you, 'Take up and walk'?"

[13] But the one who had been healed did not know who it is, for ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ had turned aside, there being a crowd in the place.

[14] After these things ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ finds him in the temple and said to him, "See, you have become healthy; sin no longer, lest something worse happen to you."

[15] The man went away and announced to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ that it is ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ who made him healthy.

[16] And because of this the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ were persecuting ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, because he was doing these things on a ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧.

[17] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered them, "**My Father is working until now, and I am working.**"

[18] Because of this, therefore, the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ were seeking the more to kill him, because he not only was breaking the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, but also was calling ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ his own Father, making himself equal to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[19] So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered and was saying to them, "Amen, amen, I say to you, the Son is not able to do anything from himself, except what he sees the Father doing; for whatever that one does, these things the Son also does likewise.

[20] For the Father has affection for the Son and shows him all things that he himself does, and greater works than these he will show him, that you may marvel.

[21] For just as the Father raises the dead and makes alive, so also the Son makes alive whom he wants.

[22] For neither does the Father judge anyone, but he has given all judgment to the Son,

[23] that all may honor the Son just as they honor the Father. The one who does not honor the Son does not honor the Father who sent him.

[24] Amen, amen, I say to you, **the one who hears my word and believes the one who sent me has eternal life, and does not come into judgment, but has passed over out of death into life.**

[25] "Amen, amen, I say to you, an hour is coming, and now is, when the dead will hear the voice of the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and those who have heard will live.

[26] For just as the Father has life in himself, so he gave also to the Son to have life in himself;

[27] and he gave him authority to execute judgment, because he is Son of Man.

[28] Do not marvel at this, because an hour is coming in which all who are in the tombs will hear his voice

[29] and will come out, those who did the good things unto a resurrection of life, but those who practiced the bad things unto a resurrection of judgment.

[30] "I am not able to do anything from myself; just as I hear, I judge, and my judgment is righteous, because I do not seek my own will but the will of the one who sent me.

[31] If I testify concerning myself, my testimony is not true;

[32] there is another who testifies concerning me, and I know that the testimony which he testifies concerning me is true.

[33] You have sent to ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, and he has testified to the truth;

[34] but I do not receive the testimony from man, but I say these things that you may be saved.

[35] That one was the lamp that was burning and shining, and you were willing to exult for an hour in his light.

[36] But I have the testimony greater than ⟦john|Yochanan's|John's⟧; for the works which the Father has given me that I should perfect them, the works themselves which I do, testify concerning me that the Father has sent me,

[37] and the Father who sent me, that one has testified concerning me. Neither have you ever heard his voice nor seen his form,

[38] and his word you do not have abiding in you, because the one whom that one sent, this one you do not believe.

[39] You search the Scriptures, because you think that in them you have eternal life; and those are the ones that testify concerning me;

[40] and you do not want to come to me that you may have life.

[41] "Glory from men I do not receive,

[42] but I have come to know you, that you do not have the love of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in yourselves.

[43] I have come in the name of my Father, and you do not receive me; if another comes in his own name, that one you will receive.

[44] How are you able to believe, receiving glory from one another, and the glory that is from the only ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ you do not seek?

[45] Do not think that I will accuse you before the Father; there is one who accuses you, ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, in whom you have hoped.

[46] For if you believed ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, you would believe me, for that one wrote concerning me.

[47] But if you do not believe that one's writings, how will you believe my words?"

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **v4 absent** — KJV v3b–4 ("waiting for the moving of the water. For an angel went down at a certain season into the pool, and troubled the water …") stands only in RP; WH, Treg, NA28 and the SBLGNT do not have it.
- **"After this" → "After these things"** (v1, 14) — Greek μετὰ ταῦτα, plural. "A feast": ἑορτή without the article (WH Treg NA28; RP "the feast").
- **"by the sheep market a pool, which is called in the Hebrew tongue Bethesda, having five porches" → "by the sheep gate a pool, which is called in Hebrew Beit Zata, having five colonnades"** (v2) — Greek ἐπὶ τῇ προβατικῇ, "at the sheep-(place)": the noun is not expressed; "gate" is supplied from Neh 3:1. Ἑβραϊστί is "in Hebrew", the language of the Hebrews (the name itself is Aramaic in form). The name: Βηθζαθά WH NA28 ] Βηθεσδά Treg RP (and the printed SBLGNT) — 2–2, NA28 decides. στοά is a covered colonnade; "roofed" is not a separate word.
- **"a great multitude of impotent folk, of blind, halt, withered" → "a multitude of those who were sick, blind, lame, withered"** (v3) — Greek πλῆθος τῶν ἀσθενούντων; "great" (RP πολύ) is not in WH Treg NA28. ξηρῶν is "dry, withered", not "paralyzed".
- **"which had an infirmity thirty and eight years" → "who had been thirty-eight years in his sickness"** (v5) — Greek τριάκοντα καὶ ὀκτὼ ἔτη ἔχων ἐν τῇ ἀσθενείᾳ αὐτοῦ, literally "having thirty and eight years in his sickness" (καί WH NA28; Treg RP without it, the same number). ἀσθένεια is "weakness" elsewhere; here it stands with ἀσθενέω "be sick" (v3, 7).
- **"knew that he had been now a long time in that case, he saith … Wilt thou be made whole?" → "knowing that he has already been so a long time, says … Do you want to become healthy?"** (v6) — Greek πολὺν ἤδη χρόνον ἔχει, literally "he already has a long time" ("so" is supplied); λέγει, a present, kept as in v8, 14 (εὑρίσκει). ὑγιὴς γενέσθαι is "to become healthy" (v6, 9, 11, 14, 15): the adjective ὑγιής with "become" or "make", not a verb "heal".
- **"The impotent man answered him, Sir, I have no man, when the water is troubled, to put me" → "The sick man answered him, Lord, I have no man to put me … when the water is troubled"** (v7) — Greek ὁ ἀσθενῶν; Κύριε is the ordinary address "Lord"; ταραχθῇ "is troubled", the verb of 12:27; 14:1; βάλῃ is literally "throw".
- **"Rise, take up thy bed" → "Arise, take up your pallet"** (v8–12) — Greek ἔγειρε; κράβαττος, a poor man's bedding (Mark 2:4).
- **"was made whole … and walked" → "became healthy … and was walking"** (v9) — Greek περιεπάτει, imperfect.
- **"on the same day was the sabbath" → "it was a Sabbath on that day"** (v9, 10, 16) — Greek σάββατον without the article.
- **"The Jews therefore said unto him that was cured … it is not lawful for thee to carry thy bed" → "So the Jews were saying to the one who had been healed … it is not permitted for you to take up the pallet"** (v10) — Greek ἔλεγον, imperfect; οὐκ ἔξεστιν; ἆραι, the verb "take up" of v8–12. "The pallet": WH Treg RP; NA28 adds σου, "your pallet". Two verbs are "heal" here: θεραπεύω (v10) and ἰάομαι (v13).
- **"He that made me whole, the same said unto me" → "The one who made me healthy, that one said to me"** (v11) — Greek ἐκεῖνος. "But he": ὃς δέ WH Treg, ὁ δέ NA28; RP has neither.
- **"Then asked they him, What man is that which said unto thee, Take up thy bed, and walk?" → "They asked him, Who is the man who said to you, Take up and walk?"** (v12) — no οὖν in WH NA28 (Treg RP have it: "So they asked"); Ἆρον καὶ περιπάτει without an object in WH NA28 (Treg RP add "your pallet").
- **"he that was healed wist not who it was: for Jesus had conveyed himself away, a multitude being in that place" → "the one who had been healed did not know who it is, for *Yeshua* had turned aside, there being a crowd in the place"** (v13) — Greek τίς ἐστιν, present; ἐξένευσεν, "turned aside, slipped away"; ὄχλος is "crowd".
- **"Afterward Jesus findeth him … Behold, thou art made whole: sin no more, lest a worse thing come unto thee" → "After these things *Yeshua* finds him … See, you have become healthy; sin no longer, lest something worse happen to you"** (v14) — Greek Ἴδε "See"; γέγονας, a perfect; μηκέτι ἁμάρτανε, a present imperative: "sin no longer".
- **"told the Jews that it was Jesus, which had made him whole" → "announced to the Jews that it is *Yeshua* who made him healthy"** (v15) — Greek ἀνήγγειλεν (Treg NA28 RP; WH εἶπεν "said"); ἐστιν, present.
- **"therefore did the Jews persecute Jesus, and sought to slay him, because he had done these things on the sabbath day" → "because of this the Jews were persecuting *Yeshua*, because he was doing these things on a Sabbath"** (v16) — Greek διὰ τοῦτο … ὅτι; ἐδίωκον, ἐποίει, imperfects. "And sought to slay him" (RP) is not in WH Treg NA28.
- **"My Father worketh hitherto, and I work" → "My Father is working until now, and I am working"** (v17) — Greek ἕως ἄρτι ἐργάζεται κἀγὼ ἐργάζομαι, presents. The name "Jesus" stands in Treg NA28 RP; WH has only "But he".
- **"Therefore the Jews sought the more to kill him, because he not only had broken the sabbath, but said also that God was his Father" → "Because of this, therefore, the Jews were seeking the more to kill him, because he not only was breaking the Sabbath, but also was calling *Elohim* his own Father"** (v18) — Greek διὰ τοῦτο οὖν; ἐζήτουν, ἔλυε, ἔλεγε, imperfects; λύω is literally "loose" (so 7:23; 10:35); πατέρα ἴδιον, "his own Father".
- **"Then answered Jesus and said unto them, Verily, verily" → "So *Yeshua* answered and was saying to them, Amen, amen"** (v19, 24, 25) — Greek ἔλεγεν, imperfect (WH NA28; Treg RP εἶπεν "said"); ἀμὴν ἀμήν, the Hebrew אָמֵן left untranslated, as the Greek leaves it.
- **"The Son can do nothing of himself, but what he seeth the Father do: for what things soever he doeth" → "the Son is not able to do anything from himself, except what he sees the Father doing; for whatever that one does"** (v19, 30) — Greek οὐ δύναται … ἀφ' ἑαυτοῦ οὐδέν, "from himself"; ἐκεῖνος "that one"; the Greek does not repeat "the Father". "Of his own accord" and "only" are not in the Greek.
- **"loveth the Son" → "has affection for the Son"** (v20) — Greek φιλεῖ, the verb φιλέω (root φίλος "friend"), kept apart from ἀγαπάω "love" (3:35 has ἀγαπάω of the same thing).
- **"raiseth up the dead, and quickeneth them … quickeneth whom he will" → "raises the dead and makes alive … makes alive whom he wants"** (v21) — Greek ζῳοποιεῖ, twice, without an object the first time.
- **"For the Father judgeth no man" → "For neither does the Father judge anyone"** (v22) — Greek οὐδὲ γάρ, "for not even / neither".
- **"He that heareth my word, and believeth on him that sent me, hath everlasting life, and shall not come into condemnation; but is passed from death unto life" → "the one who hears my word and believes the one who sent me has eternal life, and does not come into judgment, but has passed over out of death into life"** (v24) — Greek πιστεύων τῷ πέμψαντί με, a dative: "believes him", not "believes in him"; εἰς κρίσιν οὐκ ἔρχεται, a present, and κρίσις "judgment" as in v22, 27, 29, 30; μεταβέβηκεν ἐκ τοῦ θανάτου εἰς τὴν ζωήν, a perfect, with the article on both nouns.
- **"The hour is coming, and now is … and they that hear shall live" → "an hour is coming, and now is … and those who have heard will live"** (v25, 28) — Greek ὥρα without the article; οἱ ἀκούσαντες, an aorist participle.
- **"so hath he given to the Son" → "so he gave also to the Son"** (v26, 27) — Greek ἔδωκεν, aorist; no "granted".
- **"authority to execute judgment also, because he is the Son of man" → "authority to execute judgment, because he is Son of Man"** (v27) — Greek κρίσιν ποιεῖν, "to do judgment" (Jude 15); "also" (RP καί) is not in WH Treg NA28. υἱὸς ἀνθρώπου stands without articles, only here in the gospels: "Son of Man" as a title or "a son of man", a human being (Dan 7:13).
- **"they that have done good, unto the resurrection of life; and they that have done evil, unto the resurrection of damnation" → "those who did the good things unto a resurrection of life, but those who practiced the bad things unto a resurrection of judgment"** (v29) — Greek οἱ τὰ ἀγαθὰ ποιήσαντες … οἱ δὲ τὰ φαῦλα πράξαντες: two verbs ("do", "practice"); φαῦλος is "bad, worthless", not πονηρός "evil"; ἀνάστασιν without the article; κρίσεως "of judgment". "But" (δέ) Treg NA28 RP; WH omits it.
- **"my judgment is just … the will of the Father which hath sent me" → "my judgment is righteous … the will of the one who sent me"** (v30) — Greek δικαία; "Father" (RP) is not in WH Treg NA28.
- **"If I bear witness of myself, my witness is not true" → "If I testify concerning myself, my testimony is not true"** (v31–39) — Greek μαρτυρέω, μαρτυρία: "testify", "testimony" throughout, as in 1 John. The Greek has no "alone" and no "deemed": the sentence is as blunt as this (compare 8:14).
- **"Ye sent unto John, and he bare witness" → "You have sent to John, and he has testified"** (v33) — Greek ἀπεστάλκατε, μεμαρτύρηκε, perfects.
- **"But I receive not testimony from man" → "but I do not receive the testimony from man"** (v34) — Greek ἐγὼ δὲ οὐ παρὰ ἀνθρώπου τὴν μαρτυρίαν λαμβάνω, with the article.
- **"He was a burning and a shining light: and ye were willing for a season to rejoice in his light" → "That one was the lamp that was burning and shining, and you were willing to exult for an hour in his light"** (v35) — Greek ἐκεῖνος; ὁ λύχνος, "the lamp", with the article (φῶς "light" comes at the end); ἀγαλλιαθῆναι "exult"; πρὸς ὥραν, literally "for an hour". "His light" can also be "its light" (the lamp's).
- **"I have greater witness than that of John … the works which the Father hath given me to finish, the same works that I do" → "I have the testimony greater than John's … the works which the Father has given me that I should perfect them, the works themselves which I do"** (v36) — Greek τὴν μαρτυρίαν μείζω τοῦ Ἰωάννου, literally "greater than John"; ἵνα τελειώσω αὐτά, the verb τελειόω "bring to completion, perfect" (4:34; 17:4). Two verbs are "send": ἀπέσταλκεν (v36, perfect), ἀπέστειλεν (v38), from ἀποστέλλω, and ὁ πέμψας (v23, 24, 30, 37), from πέμπω.
- **"the Father himself, which hath sent me, hath borne witness of me. Ye have neither heard his voice at any time, nor seen his shape" → "the Father who sent me, that one has testified concerning me. Neither have you ever heard his voice nor seen his form"** (v37) — Greek ἐκεῖνος (WH Treg NA28; RP αὐτός "himself"); εἶδος "form, visible appearance".
- **"ye have not his word abiding in you: for whom he hath sent, him ye believe not" → "his word you do not have abiding in you, because the one whom that one sent, this one you do not believe"** (v38) — Greek μένοντα "abiding"; ἀπέστειλεν ἐκεῖνος, aorist; τούτῳ, a dative: "believe him".
- **"Search the scriptures; for in them ye think ye have eternal life: and they are they which testify of me" → "You search the Scriptures, because you think that in them you have eternal life; and those are the ones that testify concerning me"** (v39) — Greek ἐραυνᾶτε is indicative ("you search") or imperative ("search") in form; the following "because you think" fits the statement. ἐκεῖναι "those".
- **"ye will not come to me" → "you do not want to come to me"** (v40) — Greek οὐ θέλετε; καί is "and", not "yet".
- **"I receive not honour from men" → "Glory from men I do not receive"** (v41, 44) — Greek δόξα "glory", three times; "honor" is τιμή (v23).
- **"I know you, that ye have not the love of God in you" → "I have come to know you, that you do not have the love of *Elohim* in yourselves"** (v42) — Greek ἔγνωκα, a perfect; τὴν ἀγάπην τοῦ θεοῦ is a plain genitive: love for God or God's love (1 John 2:5).
- **"him ye will receive" → "that one you will receive"** (v43) — Greek ἐκεῖνον.
- **"How can ye believe, which receive honour one of another, and seek not the honour that cometh from God only?" → "How are you able to believe, receiving glory from one another, and the glory that is from the only *Elohim* you do not seek?"** (v44) — Greek λαμβάνοντες, a participle, then a finite verb ζητεῖτε; παρὰ τοῦ μόνου θεοῦ, "from the only God" (some old witnesses lack θεοῦ: "from the Only One"; the four editions print it).
- **"accuse you to the Father: there is one that accuseth you, even Moses, in whom ye trust" → "accuse you before the Father; there is one who accuses you, Moses, in whom you have hoped"** (v45) — Greek πρὸς τὸν πατέρα, "to, before"; εἰς ὃν ὑμεῖς ἠλπίκατε, a perfect of ἐλπίζω "hope".
- **"for he wrote of me" → "for that one wrote concerning me"** (v46) — Greek ἐκεῖνος.
- **"if ye believe not his writings, how shall ye believe my words?" → "if you do not believe that one's writings, how will you believe my words?"** (v47) — Greek τοῖς ἐκείνου γράμμασιν, "the written letters" of that one (root of ἔγραψεν "wrote", v46; γραφαί "Scriptures", v39); ῥήμασιν, "spoken words", not λόγος.

### Notes
- **v2** — "there is" (ἔστιν) is a present tense.
- **v16, 18** — "the Jews" (οἱ Ἰουδαῖοι): here those in Jerusalem who oppose him; the word itself is the plain name of the people.
