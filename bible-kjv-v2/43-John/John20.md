# John 20

[1] Now on the first day of the week ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ Magdalene came to the tomb early, while it was still dark, and saw that the stone had been taken away from the tomb.

[2] So she ran and went to ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and the other disciple, the one whom ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ loved, and said to them, "They have taken the Lord out of the tomb, and we do not know where they have laid him."

[3] So ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ went out with the other disciple, and they were going toward the tomb.

[4] Both of them were running together, but the other disciple outran ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and reached the tomb first.

[5] And stooping to look in, he saw the linen cloths lying there, but he did not go in.

[6] Then ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ came, following him, and went into the tomb. He saw the linen cloths lying there,

[7] and the face cloth, which had been on ⟦yeshua|Yeshua's|Jesus'⟧ head, not lying with the linen cloths but folded up in a place by itself.

[8] Then the other disciple, who had reached the tomb first, also went in, and he saw and believed;

[9] for as yet they did not understand the Scripture, that he must rise from the dead.

[10] Then the disciples went back to their homes.

[11] But ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ stood weeping outside the tomb, and as she wept she stooped to look into the tomb.

[12] And she saw two angels in white, sitting where the body of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ had lain, one at the head and one at the feet.

[13] They said to her, "Woman, why are you weeping?" She said to them, "They have taken away my Lord, and I do not know where they have laid him."

[14] Having said this, she turned around and saw ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ standing, but she did not know that it was ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[15] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to her, "Woman, why are you weeping? Whom are you seeking?" Supposing him to be the gardener, she said to him, "Sir, if you have carried him away, tell me where you have laid him, and I will take him away."

[16] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to her, "⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧." She turned and said to him in ⟦aramaic|Aramit|Aramaic⟧, "**⟦rabbouni|Rabbouni|Rabboni⟧!**" (which means Teacher).

[17] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to her, "Do not cling to me, for I have not yet ascended to the Father; but go to my brothers and say to them, 'I am ascending to my Father and your Father, to my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.'"

[18] ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ Magdalene went and announced to the disciples, "I have seen the Lord" — and that he had said these things to her.

[19] On the evening of that day, the first day of the week, the doors being locked where the disciples were for fear of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ came and stood among them and said to them, "Peace be with you."

[20] When he had said this, he showed them his hands and his side. Then the disciples were glad when they saw the Lord.

[21] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them again, "Peace be with you. As the Father has sent me, even so I am sending you."

[22] And when he had said this, he breathed on them and said to them, "Receive the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧.

[23] If you forgive the sins of any, they are forgiven them; if you withhold forgiveness from any, it is withheld."

[24] Now ⟦thomas|Toma|Thomas⟧, one of the twelve, called the Twin, was not with them when ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ came.

[25] So the other disciples told him, "We have seen the Lord." But he said to them, "Unless I see in his hands the mark of the nails, and place my finger into the mark of the nails, and place my hand into his side, I will never believe."

[26] Eight days later, his disciples were inside again, and ⟦thomas|Toma|Thomas⟧ was with them. Although the doors were locked, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ came and stood among them and said, "Peace be with you."

[27] Then he said to ⟦thomas|Toma|Thomas⟧, "Put your finger here, and see my hands; and put out your hand, and place it in my side. Do not disbelieve, but believe."

[28] ⟦thomas|Toma|Thomas⟧ answered him, "**My Lord and my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!**"

[29] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to him, "Have you believed because you have seen me? Blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed."

[30] Now ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ did many other signs in the presence of the disciples, which are not written in this book;

[31] but these are written so that you may believe that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ is the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧, the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and that by believing you may have life in his name.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v17, 28) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v31) — Hebrew messianic title.
- **"Peter" / "*Kefa*"** (v2–6) — Aramaic name used consistently.
- **"Thomas" / "*Didymos*"** (v24) — Greek Δίδυμος *Didymos* ("Twin"); gloss retained; first introduction in ch. 11.
- **"Receive ye the Holy Ghost" → "Receive the *Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v22) — Hebrew רוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ preserved; "Ghost" is archaic.
- **"My Lord and my God" → "My Lord and my *Elohim*"** (v28) — Thomas's confession rendered with consistent *Elohim* substitution; theologically significant climax of the Gospel.
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** modernized throughout.
