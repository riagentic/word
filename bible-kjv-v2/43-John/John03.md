# John 3

[1] Now there was a man of the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧, ⟦nicodemus|Nikodemos|Nicodemus⟧ his name, a ruler of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧;

[2] this one came to him by night and said to him, "Rabbi, we know that you have come from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ as a teacher; for no one can do these signs which you do, unless ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is with him."

[3] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered and said to him, "Amen, amen, I say to you, **unless one is born from above, he cannot see the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.**"

[4] ⟦nicodemus|Nikodemos|Nicodemus⟧ says to him, "How can a man be born, being old? Can he enter a second time into the womb of his mother and be born?"

[5] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered, "Amen, amen, I say to you, unless one is born of water and Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[6] That which has been born of the flesh is flesh, and that which has been born of the Spirit is spirit.

[7] Do not marvel that I said to you, 'You must be born from above.'

[8] The wind blows where it wants, and you hear its sound, but you do not know from where it comes and where it goes; so is everyone who has been born of the Spirit."

[9] ⟦nicodemus|Nikodemos|Nicodemus⟧ answered and said to him, "How can these things come to be?"

[10] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered and said to him, "You are the teacher of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and you do not know these things?

[11] Amen, amen, I say to you, what we know we speak, and what we have seen we testify, and you do not receive our testimony.

[12] If I told you the earthly things and you do not believe, how will you believe if I tell you the heavenly things?

[13] And no one has ascended into heaven except the one who descended out of heaven, the Son of Man.

[14] And **as ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, so must the Son of Man be lifted up,**

**[15] that everyone who believes may in him have eternal life.**

[16] "**For ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ so loved the world that he gave the only Son, that everyone who believes in him should not perish but have eternal life.**

[17] For ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ did not send the Son into the world that he might judge the world, but that the world might be saved through him.

[18] The one who believes in him is not judged; but the one who does not believe has been judged already, because he has not believed in the name of the only Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[19] And this is the judgment, that the light has come into the world, and men loved the darkness rather than the light, for their works were evil.

[20] For everyone who practices bad things hates the light and does not come to the light, lest his works be reproved;

[21] but the one who does the truth comes to the light, that his works may be manifested, that they have been worked in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[22] After these things ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ and his disciples came into the Judean land, and there he was spending time with them and ⟦immerse|immersing|baptizing⟧.

[23] Now ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ also was ⟦immerse|immersing|baptizing⟧ in ⟦aenon|Ainon|Aenon⟧ near Salim, because there were many waters there, and they were coming and being ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧;

[24] for ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ had not yet been cast into the prison.

[25] So there arose a dispute on the part of the disciples of ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ with a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ concerning purification.

[26] And they came to ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ and said to him, "Rabbi, he who was with you beyond the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, to whom you have testified — see, this one ⟦immerse|immerses|baptizes⟧, and all are coming to him."

[27] ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ answered and said, "A man cannot receive even one thing unless it has been given to him out of heaven.

[28] You yourselves testify to me that I said, 'I am not the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧,' but, 'I have been sent ahead of that one.'

[29] The one who has the bride is the bridegroom; but the friend of the bridegroom, who stands and hears him, rejoices with joy because of the voice of the bridegroom. This joy of mine, therefore, has been made full.

[30] **That one must grow, but I must become less.**"

[31] The one coming from above is above all. The one who is of the earth is of the earth, and of the earth he speaks. The one coming out of heaven is above all.

[32] What he has seen and heard, this he testifies, and no one receives his testimony.

[33] The one who received his testimony has set his seal that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is true.

[34] For he whom ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ sent speaks the words of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for he does not give the Spirit by measure.

[35] The Father loves the Son, and has given all things in his hand.

[36] The one who believes in the Son has eternal life; but the one who disobeys the Son will not see life, but the wrath of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ abides upon him.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"John" → "*Yochanan*"** (v23–27) — Hebrew name; see ch. 1.
- **"baptizing" → "immersing"** (v22, 23, 26) — Greek βαπτίζω, "to immerse".
- **"named Nicodemus" → "*Nikodemos* his name"** (v1) — Greek Νικόδημος ὄνομα αὐτῷ, as 1:6. "Ruler" is ἄρχων.
- **"The same came to Jesus" → "this one came to him"** (v2) — Greek πρὸς αὐτόν; the name is not in the Greek (the verse continues 2:23–25).
- **"thou art a teacher come from God" → "you have come from *Elohim* as a teacher"** (v2) — Greek ἀπὸ θεοῦ ἐλήλυθας διδάσκαλος; "from *Elohim*" stands first.
- **"Verily, verily" → "Amen, amen"** (v3, 5, 11) — Greek ἀμὴν ἀμήν, as 1:51.
- **"born again" → "born from above"** (v3, 7) — Greek ἄνωθεν means "from above" (so v31; 19:11) and also "again, anew". The verse gives "from above"; *Nikodemos* answers as if he had heard "a second time" (v4, δεύτερον).
- **"born" kept** (v3–8) — one Greek verb, γεννάω, eight times in these verses; it is used of the father ("beget") and of the mother ("bear"), passive "be begotten" or "be born". The verse has "born" every time, so that *Nikodemos* and *Yeshua* are heard to use one word; "begotten of water and Spirit", "of the flesh", "of the Spirit" is equally what the Greek says (ἐκ names the father or the mother). The same verb is "begotten" in 1:13 and 1 John, where the father is in view.
- **"Nicodemus saith" → "*Nikodemos* says"** (v4) — the Greek present is kept. His second question begins with μή and expects the answer "no".
- **"of water and of the Spirit" → "of water and Spirit"** (v5) — Greek ἐξ ὕδατος καὶ πνεύματος: one preposition, no article with either noun. v6 and v8 have the article, "the Spirit".
- **"That which is born" → "That which has been born"** (v6, 8) — Greek γεγεννημένον, γεγεννημένος, perfect participles.
- **"Ye must be born again"** (v7) — "you" is plural (ὑμᾶς); "to you" before it is singular.
- **"The wind bloweth where it listeth" → "The wind blows where it wants"** (v8) — Greek τὸ πνεῦμα ὅπου θέλει πνεῖ: πνεῦμα is "wind, breath, spirit", the word rendered "Spirit" in v5, 6 and at the end of this verse; πνεῖ "blows" is its verb, φωνή is "sound" or "voice". The sentence can also be read "the Spirit breathes where he wills, and you hear his voice". The Greek has no "it is with" in "so is everyone".
- **"How can these things be?" → "How can these things come to be?"** (v9) — Greek γενέσθαι.
- **"Art thou a master of Israel" → "You are the teacher of *Yisrael*"** (v10) — Greek ὁ διδάσκαλος, with the article; γινώσκεις "know". It can be a statement or a question.
- **"We speak that we do know, and testify that we have seen" → "what we know we speak, and what we have seen we testify"** (v11) — the Greek order; μαρτυρέω "testify", μαρτυρία "testimony" (v11, 26, 28, 32, 33). "And you do not receive" is καί, not "but"; "you" is plural here and in v12.
- **"earthly things … heavenly things" → "the earthly things … the heavenly things"** (v12) — Greek τὰ ἐπίγεια … τὰ ἐπουράνια, with the article.
- **"even the Son of man which is in heaven" → "the Son of Man"** (v13) — ἀνθρώπου WH NA28 ] + ὁ ὢν ἐν τῷ οὐρανῷ "who is in heaven" Treg RP. "Ascended … descended" are ἀναβέβηκεν (perfect), καταβάς, as 1:51.
- **"lifted up" kept** (v14) — Greek ὕψωσεν, ὑψωθῆναι: ὑψόω is "lift up" and "exalt" (8:28; 12:32–34).
- **"That whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have eternal life" → "that everyone who believes may in him have eternal life"** (v15) — ἐν αὐτῷ WH Treg NA28 ] εἰς αὐτὸν μὴ ἀπόληται ἀλλ' RP (from v16). ἐν αὐτῷ "in him" can go with "have eternal life" (John's "believe in" is elsewhere εἰς) or with "believes"; the Greek order is kept.
- **"his only begotten Son" → "the only Son"** (v16) — Greek τὸν υἱὸν τὸν μονογενῆ: μονογενής is "only" (1:14, 18; 1 John 4:9). υἱόν WH NA28 ] + αὐτοῦ "his" Treg RP, here and in v17.
- **"so loved … that he gave" kept** (v16) — Greek Οὕτως … ὥστε with the indicative: an actual result. Οὕτως is "in this way" and "so much". The quotation marks are the editors'; where the words of *Yeshua* end (v15 or v21) the Greek does not mark.
- **"to condemn the world" → "that he might judge the world"; "is not condemned … is condemned already" → "is not judged … has been judged already"** (v17, 18) — Greek κρίνω, "judge" (κρίσις "judgment", v19); "condemn" is κατακρίνω. κέκριται is a perfect. "Sent" is ἀπέστειλεν.
- **"the only begotten Son of God" → "the only Son of *Elohim*"** (v18) — Greek τοῦ μονογενοῦς υἱοῦ τοῦ θεοῦ.
- **"condemnation" → "judgment"; "light is come" → "the light has come"; "darkness" kept** (v19) — Greek ἡ κρίσις; τὸ φῶς with the article; τὸ σκότος (here only in John; elsewhere σκοτία). "For" is γάρ; the colon of some versions hides the ὅτι "that".
- **"every one that doeth evil" → "everyone who practices bad things"** (v20) — Greek ὁ φαῦλα πράσσων: φαῦλος "bad, worthless" (not πονηρός "evil", v19); πράσσω "practice" against ποιέω "do" in v21.
- **"reproved" kept** (v20) — Greek ἐλεγχθῇ: "reproved, convicted, shown for what they are".
- **"he that doeth truth" → "the one who does the truth"** (v21) — Greek ὁ ποιῶν τὴν ἀλήθειαν, as 1 John 1:6.
- **"that his deeds may be made manifest, that they are wrought in God" → "that his works may be manifested, that they have been worked in *Elohim*"** (v21) — Greek φανερωθῇ; ἔργα … εἰργασμένα, one root. ὅτι is "that" or "because".
- **"into the land of Judaea" → "into the Judean land"** (v22) — Greek εἰς τὴν Ἰουδαίαν γῆν, an adjective. "After these things" is Μετὰ ταῦτα, plural; διέτριβεν and ἐβάπτιζεν are imperfects.
- **"because there was much water there" → "because there were many waters there"** (v23) — Greek ὕδατα πολλά, plural. "They were coming" has no subject noun in the Greek.
- **"cast into prison" → "cast into the prison"** (v24) — Greek εἰς τὴν φυλακήν. The parentheses of some versions are not in the printed Greek.
- **"Then there arose a question between some of John's disciples and the Jews" → "So there arose a dispute on the part of the disciples of *Yochanan* with a *Yehudi*"** (v25) — Greek ζήτησις ἐκ τῶν μαθητῶν Ἰωάννου μετὰ Ἰουδαίου: ἐκ is "from, on the part of" (the Greek has no "some"); Ἰουδαίου is singular in the editions compared.
- **"to whom thou barest witness, behold, the same baptizeth" → "to whom you have testified — see, this one immerses"** (v26) — Greek ᾧ σὺ μεμαρτύρηκας (perfect), ἴδε οὗτος. "Beyond the *Yarden*" as 1:28.
- **"A man can receive nothing" → "A man cannot receive even one thing"** (v27) — οὐδὲ ἕν SBLGNT NA28 ] οὐδέν "nothing" WH Treg RP. "Out of heaven" is ἐκ τοῦ οὐρανοῦ.
- **"I am sent before him" → "I have been sent ahead of that one"** (v28) — Greek Ἀπεσταλμένος εἰμὶ ἔμπροσθεν ἐκείνου; ἔμπροσθεν as 1:15, 30. The Greek sets ὅτι before each saying.
- **"rejoiceth greatly" → "rejoices with joy"** (v29) — Greek χαρᾷ χαίρει, the noun with its verb, a Hebrew turn of speech.
- **"this my joy therefore is fulfilled" → "This joy of mine, therefore, has been made full"** (v29) — Greek πεπλήρωται, perfect; as 1 John 1:4. The Greek has no "now".
- **"He must increase, but I must decrease" → "That one must grow, but I must become less"** (v30) — Greek ἐκεῖνον δεῖ αὐξάνειν, ἐμὲ δὲ ἐλαττοῦσθαι: αὐξάνω "grow"; ἐλαττόω "make less", from the word for "lesser" (2:10).
- **"he that is of the earth is earthly, and speaketh of the earth" → "The one who is of the earth is of the earth, and of the earth he speaks"** (v31) — Greek ἐκ τῆς γῆς three times; no adjective "earthly". "From above" is ἄνωθεν, as v3, 7.
- **"And what he hath seen and heard, that he testifieth" → "What he has seen and heard, this he testifies"** (v32) — ὅ WH Treg NA28 ] καὶ ὅ RP. "And no one receives" is καί, not "yet".
- **"He that hath received his testimony hath set to his seal" → "The one who received his testimony has set his seal"** (v33) — Greek ὁ λαβών, ἐσφράγισεν, aorists. ἀληθής "true, truthful".
- **"for God giveth not the Spirit by measure unto him" → "for he does not give the Spirit by measure"** (v34) — δίδωσιν WH NA28 ] + ὁ θεός Treg RP; "unto him" is not in the Greek. The subject of "give" is not named: *Elohim*, or the one whom he sent. "Words" is ῥήματα (not λόγοι); ἐκ μέτρου is "out of a measure".
- **"hath given all things into his hand" → "has given all things in his hand"** (v35) — Greek ἐν τῇ χειρὶ αὐτοῦ.
- **"he that believeth not the Son" → "the one who disobeys the Son"** (v36) — Greek ὁ ἀπειθῶν τῷ υἱῷ: ἀπειθέω is "disobey, refuse to be persuaded", a different verb from "believe" in the first half.
- **"abideth on him" → "abides upon him"** (v36) — Greek μένει, present.

### Notes
- **v3, 7** — ἄνωθεν: "from above" or "again"; *Nikodemos* takes it as a second birth.
- **v14** echoes Num 21:8–9.
- **v31–36** — the Greek does not mark who speaks here: *Yochanan* (continuing v27–30) or the evangelist.
