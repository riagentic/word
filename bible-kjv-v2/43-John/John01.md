# John 1

[1] **In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and the Word was ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.**

[2] This one was in the beginning with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] All things came to be through him, and apart from him not even one thing came to be. What has come to be

[4] in him was life, and the life was the light of men;

[5] and the light shines in the darkness, and the darkness did not lay hold of it.

[6] There came to be a man, sent from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; his name was ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧.

[7] This one came for testimony, that he might testify concerning the light, that all might believe through him.

[8] That one was not the light, but came that he might testify concerning the light.

[9] There was the true light which enlightens every man, coming into the world.

[10] He was in the world, and the world came to be through him, and the world did not know him.

[11] He came to what was his own, and those who were his own did not receive him.

[12] But as many as received him, to them he gave authority to become children of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to those who believe in his name,

[13] who were begotten not of bloods, nor of the will of flesh, nor of the will of a man, but of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[14] **And the Word became flesh and tented among us, and we beheld his glory, glory as of an only one from a father, full of grace and truth.**

[15] (⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ testifies concerning him and has cried out, saying, "This was he of whom I said, 'The one coming after me has come to be ahead of me, because he was before me.'")

[16] For from his fullness we all received, and grace in place of grace.

[17] For the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ was given through ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧; the grace and the truth came to be through ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[18] **No one has ever seen ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; the only one, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who is in the bosom of the Father, that one declared him.**

[19] And this is the testimony of ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, when the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ sent to him from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ priests and ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, that they might ask him, "Who are you?"

[20] And he confessed and did not deny, and he confessed, "I am not the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧."

[21] And they asked him, "What then? Are you ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧?" And he says, "I am not." "Are you the Prophet?" And he answered, "No."

[22] So they said to him, "Who are you? — that we may give an answer to those who sent us. What do you say concerning yourself?"

[23] He said, "I am a voice of one crying in the wilderness, '**Make straight the way of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧**,' as ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ the prophet said."

[24] And they had been sent from the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧.

[25] And they asked him and said to him, "Why then do you ⟦immerse|immerse|baptize⟧, if you are not the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧, nor ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧, nor the Prophet?"

[26] ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ answered them, saying, "I ⟦immerse|immerse|baptize⟧ in water; in the midst of you stands one whom you do not know,

[27] the one coming after me, the strap of whose sandal I am not worthy to loose."

[28] These things came to pass in ⟦bethany|Bethania|Bethany⟧ beyond the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, where ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ was ⟦immerse|immersing|baptizing⟧.

[29] The next day he sees ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ coming toward him, and says, "**See, the Lamb of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who takes away the sin of the world.**

[30] This is he about whom I said, 'After me comes a man who has come to be ahead of me, because he was before me.'

[31] And I did not know him; but that he might be manifested to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, for this reason I came ⟦immerse|immersing|baptizing⟧ in water."

[32] And ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ testified, saying, "I have beheld the Spirit coming down as a dove out of heaven, and it abode upon him.

[33] And I did not know him; but the one who sent me to ⟦immerse|immerse|baptize⟧ in water, that one said to me, 'Upon whomever you see the Spirit coming down and abiding upon him, this is the one who ⟦immerse|immerses|baptizes⟧ in ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧.'

[34] And I have seen, and have testified that this is the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[35] The next day again ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ was standing, and two of his disciples,

[36] and looking upon ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ as he walked, he says, "See, the Lamb of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[37] And the two disciples heard him speaking, and they followed ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[38] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, turning and beholding them following, says to them, "What do you seek?" And they said to him, "Rabbi" (which, being translated, is said Teacher), "where do you abide?"

[39] He says to them, "Come and you will see." So they came and saw where he was abiding, and they abode with him that day; the hour was about the tenth.

[40] ⟦andrew|Andreas|Andrew⟧, the brother of ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, was one of the two who heard from ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ and followed him.

[41] This one first finds his own brother ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ and says to him, "We have found the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Messiah⟧" (which is, being translated, Christ).

[42] He brought him to ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧. Looking upon him, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said, "You are ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ the son of ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧; you shall be called ⟦cephas|Kefa|Cephas⟧" (which is interpreted Peter).

[43] The next day he wanted to go out into ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧. And he finds ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧, and ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to him, "Follow me."

[44] Now ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧ was from ⟦bethsaida|Beit Tzaidah|Bethsaida⟧, of the city of ⟦andrew|Andreas|Andrew⟧ and ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧.

[45] ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧ finds ⟦nathanael|Netanel|Nathanael⟧ and says to him, "Him of whom ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ wrote in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧, and the prophets, we have found: ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, son of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, the one from ⟦nazareth|Natzrat|Nazareth⟧."

[46] And ⟦nathanael|Netanel|Nathanael⟧ said to him, "Can anything good be out of ⟦nazareth|Natzrat|Nazareth⟧?" ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧ says to him, "Come and see."

[47] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ saw ⟦nathanael|Netanel|Nathanael⟧ coming toward him and says concerning him, "See, truly an ⟦israelite|Yisraeli|Israelite⟧, in whom there is no guile."

[48] ⟦nathanael|Netanel|Nathanael⟧ says to him, "From where do you know me?" ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered and said to him, "Before ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧ called you, while you were under the fig tree, I saw you."

[49] ⟦nathanael|Netanel|Nathanael⟧ answered him, "Rabbi, you are the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; you are King of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[50] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered and said to him, "Because I said to you that I saw you underneath the fig tree, do you believe? Greater things than these you will see."

[51] And he says to him, "Amen, amen, I say to you, you will see heaven opened and the angels of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ ascending and descending upon the Son of Man."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v17, 29, etc.) — Hebrew יֵשׁוּעַ *Yeshua* ("YHWH saves"); the actual name; "Jesus" is a Greek/Latin transliteration via *Iesous*.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v17, 20, 25) — Hebrew מָשִׁיחַ *Mashiach* ("anointed"); Greek χριστός.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v33) — Hebrew רוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ; the Greek ἐν πνεύματι ἁγίῳ has no article, "in Holy Spirit".
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (v40, 44) — Aramaic כֵּיפָא *Kepha* ("rock"); the Greek has Πέτρος in both verses.
- **"Nathanael" → "*Netanel*"** (v45–49) — Greek Ναθαναήλ renders Hebrew נְתַנְאֵל ("El has given").
- **"baptize" → "immerse"** (v25, 26, 28, 31, 33) — Greek βαπτίζω, "to dip, plunge, immerse".
- **"law" → "Torah"** (v17, 45) — Greek ὁ νόμος, with the article, for Hebrew תּוֹרָה.
- **"with God" kept** (v1, 2) — Greek πρὸς τὸν θεόν: πρός with the accusative is "toward, with, in the presence of" (as 1 John 1:2, "with the Father"). Here θεόν has the article.
- **"the Word was God" kept** (v1) — Greek καὶ θεὸς ἦν ὁ λόγος, word for word "and God was the Word". "The Word" has the article and is the subject; θεός stands before the verb without the article, as the thing said of the Word. It is not ὁ θεός ("the Word was the God", that is, the same one he was with), and it is not the adjective θεῖος ("divine"). The form can state what the Word was ("was God", "what God was the Word was") and by grammar alone also "a god"; the same form without the article means *Elohim* himself in v6, 12, 13, 18. The verse keeps the Greek wording and adds nothing.
- **"The same" → "This one"** (v2, 7) — Greek οὗτος; "that one" (v8, 18, 33) is ἐκεῖνος.
- **"were made" → "came to be"** (v3, 6, 10, 17) — Greek ἐγένετο, γέγονεν, from γίνομαι "come to be, become", against ἦν "was" in v1–2, 4, 8–10; the same verb in v14 ("became"), v15, 30 ("has come to be"), v28 ("came to pass"). "By him" is διά, "through".
- **"without him was not any thing made that was made" → "apart from him not even one thing came to be. What has come to be in him was life"** (v3–4) — Greek χωρὶς αὐτοῦ ἐγένετο οὐδὲ ἕν. ὃ γέγονεν ἐν αὐτῷ ζωὴ ἦν. The oldest manuscripts have no punctuation. The printed text (SBLGNT, as NA28) puts the stop before ὃ γέγονεν, and the verse follows it; it can then be read "what has come to be in him was life" or "what has come to be — in it was life". The other punctuation puts the stop after ὃ γέγονεν: "not even one thing came to be that has come to be. In him was life" (so the KJV).
- **"comprehended it not" → "did not lay hold of it"** (v5) — Greek κατέλαβεν, aorist of καταλαμβάνω, "lay hold of, seize": either "overcome" (as 12:35) or "grasp with the mind". φαίνει "shines" is present.
- **"There was a man" → "There came to be a man"; "whose name was John" → "his name was *Yochanan*"** (v6) — Greek Ἐγένετο ἄνθρωπος … ὄνομα αὐτῷ Ἰωάννης, "a name to him, *Yochanan*"; "was" is supplied.
- **"for a witness, to bear witness of" → "for testimony, that he might testify concerning"** (v7, 8, 15, 19, 32, 34) — Greek μαρτυρία, μαρτυρέω: "testimony", "testify" throughout, as in 1 John.
- **"He was not that Light, but was sent to bear witness" → "That one was not the light, but came that he might testify"** (v8) — Greek ἀλλ' ἵνα μαρτυρήσῃ, "but that he might testify": the Greek has no verb before "that"; "came" is supplied from v7.
- **"That was the true Light, which lighteth every man that cometh into the world" → "There was the true light which enlightens every man, coming into the world"** (v9) — Greek ἦν τὸ φῶς τὸ ἀληθινὸν ὃ φωτίζει πάντα ἄνθρωπον ἐρχόμενον εἰς τὸν κόσμον. "Coming" (ἐρχόμενον) can belong to "the light" ("the true light … was coming into the world") or to "every man" ("every man who comes into the world"); the verse leaves both open. ἀληθινός is "true, real".
- **"and the world knew him not" kept as "and"** (v10) — Greek καί three times; not "yet".
- **"He came unto his own, and his own received him not" → "He came to what was his own, and those who were his own did not receive him"** (v11) — Greek εἰς τὰ ἴδια (neuter plural: his own things, his own home) … οἱ ἴδιοι (masculine plural: his own people). "Receive" here is παραλαμβάνω, in v12 and v16 λαμβάνω.
- **"power" → "authority"; "even to them that believe" → "to those who believe"** (v12) — Greek ἐξουσίαν; τοῖς πιστεύουσιν, a present participle; εἰς τὸ ὄνομα, "into his name".
- **"which were born, not of blood" → "who were begotten not of bloods"; "of man" → "of a man"** (v13) — Greek ἐγεννήθησαν, from γεννάω "beget" (as 1 John); αἱμάτων is plural; ἀνδρός is a male, a husband (not ἀνθρώπου).
- **"was made flesh, and dwelt among us" → "became flesh and tented among us"** (v14) — Greek σὰρξ ἐγένετο καὶ ἐσκήνωσεν ἐν ἡμῖν: σκηνόω is "to live in a tent (σκηνή)", the word for the tent of meeting in the Septuagint. ἐν ἡμῖν is "among us" or "in us".
- **"the glory as of the only begotten of the Father" → "glory as of an only one from a father"** (v14) — Greek δόξαν ὡς μονογενοῦς παρὰ πατρός: none of the three nouns has the article, and the Greek has no word "Son". μονογενής is "only, the only one of its kind", said of an only child (Luke 7:12; Heb 11:17). The words can be a comparison ("such glory as an only son has from a father") or definite ("as of the only one from the Father"); a noun after a preposition often has no article in Greek (v6 παρὰ θεοῦ). "Beheld" is ἐθεασάμεθα, an aorist.
- **"full of grace and truth" kept** (v14) — Greek πλήρης, a form that can go with "the Word", with "glory" or with "an only one".
- **"bare witness of him, and cried" → "testifies concerning him and has cried out"** (v15) — Greek μαρτυρεῖ (present), κέκραγεν (perfect).
- **"He that cometh after me is preferred before me: for he was before me" → "The one coming after me has come to be ahead of me, because he was before me"** (v15, 30) — Greek ἔμπροσθέν μου γέγονεν, ὅτι πρῶτός μου ἦν: ἔμπροσθεν is "in front of" (in place or in rank), γέγονεν "has come to be"; πρῶτός μου is literally "first of me", before me in time or in rank. The Greek has no word "ranks". Editions (v15): ὃν εἶπον Treg NA28 RP ] ὁ εἰπών WH ("this was he who said").
- **"And of his fulness have all we received, and grace for grace" → "For from his fullness we all received, and grace in place of grace"** (v16) — Greek ὅτι ("for, because": WH Treg NA28; Καί "And" RP) … ἐλάβομεν (aorist) καὶ χάριν ἀντὶ χάριτος. ἀντί is "in place of, in exchange for"; "grace upon grace" is an interpretation.
- **"but grace and truth came by Jesus Christ" → "the grace and the truth came to be through *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v17) — the Greek has no "but"; both nouns have the article (the grace and truth of v14); ἐγένετο, διά.
- **"No man hath seen God at any time" → "No one has ever seen *Elohim*"** (v18) — Greek θεὸν οὐδεὶς ἑώρακεν πώποτε; θεόν has no article and stands first.
- **"the only begotten Son" → "the only one, *Elohim*"** (v18) — Greek μονογενὴς θεός WH Treg NA28 ] ὁ μονογενὴς υἱός "the only Son" RP (the reading of the KJV). The reading followed has no article; "the" is supplied, because the words that follow (ὁ ὤν, "the one who is") point to one known person. μονογενής is the word of v14 and 3:16, 18 ("only", of an only child), not μόνος ("sole, alone", 5:44; 17:3): "the only God" would be heard as that other word, so the verse has "the only one, *Elohim*", as "an only one" in v14. The two Greek words can be taken as two nouns ("an only one, God") or as adjective and noun ("an only [-begotten] God"); the Greek does not have the word "Son" in this reading.
- **"which is in the bosom of the Father" → "who is in the bosom of the Father"** (v18) — Greek ὁ ὢν εἰς τὸν κόλπον τοῦ πατρός; εἰς is literally "into, toward".
- **"he hath declared him" → "that one declared him"** (v18) — Greek ἐκεῖνος ἐξηγήσατο, an aorist: "related, explained, made known". The Greek has no object; "him" is supplied.
- **"sent priests and Levites from Jerusalem" → "sent to him from *Yerushalayim* priests and *Leviyim*"** (v19) — ἀπέστειλαν NIV RP ] + πρὸς αὐτὸν WH Treg NA28. "The Jews" (οἱ Ἰουδαῖοι) is kept wherever John has it; who is meant (the people, the Judeans, the authorities in *Yerushalayim*) is decided by each passage.
- **"but confessed" → "and he confessed"** (v20) — Greek καί three times.
- **"he saith" → "he says"** (v21, 29, 36, 38, 39, 41, 43, 45–48, 51) — the Greek present in narrative is kept as a present.
- **"that we may give an answer" kept** (v22) — Greek ἵνα ἀπόκρισιν δῶμεν, a broken sentence; the Greek has no "we need to".
- **"I am the voice" → "I am a voice"** (v23) — Greek Ἐγὼ φωνὴ βοῶντος, without article and without verb ("am" is supplied); βοάω "cry". The quotation follows the Greek as printed (Isa 40:3, Septuagint); κυρίου "of the Lord" stands for the divine name.
- **"they which were sent were of the Pharisees" → "they had been sent from the *Perushim*"** (v24) — Greek Καὶ ἀπεσταλμένοι ἦσαν ἐκ τῶν Φαρισαίων (WH Treg NA28; + οἱ RP, "those who had been sent were of the Pharisees"). ἐκ is "from" or "of": sent by the Pharisees, or themselves Pharisees.
- **"with water" → "in water"; "but there standeth one among you" → "in the midst of you stands one"** (v26, 31, 33) — Greek ἐν ὕδατι; the text has no "but" (μέσος WH Treg NA28 ] + δέ NIV RP). ἕστηκεν NA28 RP ] στήκει WH Treg; both "stands".
- **"He it is, who coming after me is preferred before me" → "the one coming after me"** (v27) — ἐρχόμενος WH Treg NA28 ] + ὃς ἔμπροσθέν μου γέγονεν RP. οὐκ εἰμὶ ἐγώ WH Treg NA28 (the "I" is stressed). "Worthy" is ἄξιος (Mark 1:7 has ἱκανός, "fit").
- **"Bethabara" → "*Bethania*"** (v28) — Greek ἐν Βηθανίᾳ, a place beyond the *Yarden*, not the Bethany near *Yerushalayim*; "Bethabara" is a later reading.
- **"Behold" → "See"** (v29, 36, 47) — Greek Ἴδε; "Behold" is kept for ἰδού.
- **"which taketh away" → "who takes away"** (v29) — Greek ὁ αἴρων, a present participle; αἴρω is "take up, bear" and "take away". "Sin" is singular.
- **"of whom I said" → "about whom I said"** (v30) — Greek ὑπὲρ οὗ WH Treg NA28 ] περὶ οὗ RP; ὑπέρ is literally "on behalf of".
- **"made manifest" → "manifested"** (v31) — Greek φανερωθῇ. κἀγώ, "and I" (v31, 33, 34), can also be "I too".
- **"I saw the Spirit descending from heaven like a dove" → "I have beheld the Spirit coming down as a dove out of heaven"** (v32) — Greek Τεθέαμαι, a perfect. "It abode" (ἔμεινεν): μένω is "abide" throughout (v33, 38, 39), as in 1 John. "It" stands for the neuter πνεῦμα: the gender is grammatical.
- **"the same said unto me" → "that one said to me"** (v33) — Greek ἐκεῖνος.
- **"I saw, and bare record" → "I have seen, and have testified"** (v34) — Greek ἑώρακα, μεμαρτύρηκα, perfects.
- **"the Son of God" kept** (v34) — υἱός WH Treg NA28 RP ] ἐκλεκτός "the Chosen One" SBLGNT, WH margin. The printed text has ἐκλεκτός; the verse follows the other editions.
- **"John stood, and two of his disciples" → "*Yochanan* was standing, and two of his disciples"** (v35) — Greek εἱστήκει … καὶ ἐκ τῶν μαθητῶν αὐτοῦ δύο.
- **"as he walked" kept** (v36) — Greek περιπατοῦντι, "walking"; the Greek has no "by".
- **"heard him speak" → "heard him speaking"** (v37) — Greek ἤκουσαν … αὐτοῦ λαλοῦντος; no "say this".
- **"saw them following" → "beholding them following"** (v38) — Greek θεασάμενος, as v14, 32.
- **"which is to say, being interpreted, Master" → "which, being translated, is said Teacher"** (v38) — Greek ὃ λέγεται μεθερμηνευόμενον Διδάσκαλε.
- **"where dwellest thou?" → "where do you abide?"** (v38–39) — Greek μένω three times.
- **"for it was about the tenth hour" → "the hour was about the tenth"** (v39) — the Greek has no "for". The tenth hour counted from sunrise is about four in the afternoon. ὄψεσθε "you will see" WH Treg NA28 ] ἴδετε "see" RP.
- **"which heard John speak, and followed him" → "who heard from *Yochanan* and followed him"** (v40) — Greek τῶν ἀκουσάντων παρὰ Ἰωάννου καὶ ἀκολουθησάντων αὐτῷ; "him" is *Yeshua* (v37), whose name the Greek does not repeat here.
- **"Messias, which is, being interpreted, the Christ" → "*Mashiach*" (which is, being translated, Christ)** (v41) — Greek τὸν Μεσσίαν, the Hebrew / Aramaic word in Greek letters, then ὅ ἐστιν μεθερμηνευόμενον χριστός: the evangelist translates it by χριστός, "anointed"; the verse keeps the Greek word, "Christ", as in 4:25. πρῶτον "first" (adverb) WH Treg NA28 ] πρῶτος RP.
- **"A stone" → "Peter"** (v42) — Greek Κηφᾶς (ὃ ἑρμηνεύεται Πέτρος): the Aramaic name *Kefa* is interpreted by the Greek name Πέτρος *Petros*, "rock, stone". The Greek has only the name.
- **"Simon the son of Jona" → "son of *Yochanan*"** (v42) — Ἰωάννου WH Treg NA28 ] Ἰωνᾶ RP.
- **"Jesus would go forth" → "he wanted to go out … and *Yeshua* says to him"** (v43) — Greek ἠθέλησεν ἐξελθεῖν … καὶ εὑρίσκει Φίλιππον καὶ λέγει αὐτῷ ὁ Ἰησοῦς: the name stands only with "says".
- **"We have found him, of whom Moses in the law, and the prophets, did write" → "Him of whom *Moshe* wrote in the Torah, and the prophets, we have found"** (v45) — the Greek order; "son of *Yosef*" has no article, "the one from *Natzrat*" has it.
- **"Can there any good thing come out of Nazareth?" → "Can anything good be out of *Natzrat*?"** (v46) — Greek εἶναι, "be".
- **"an Israelite indeed, in whom is no guile" → "truly an Israelite, in whom there is no guile"** (v47) — Greek ἀληθῶς, an adverb; δόλος "guile".
- **"Whence knowest thou me?" → "From where do you know me?"** (v48) — Greek Πόθεν.
- **"thou art the King of Israel" → "you are King of *Yisrael*"** (v49) — Greek σὺ βασιλεὺς εἶ τοῦ Ἰσραήλ (WH Treg NA28; εἶ ὁ βασιλεύς RP): "King" has no article and stands before the verb, the same form as θεός in v1.
- **"believest thou?" kept as a question** (v50) — the printed text has a question mark; it can also be a statement ("you believe"). ὑποκάτω "underneath" (v48 ὑπό "under").
- **"Verily, verily" → "Amen, amen"** (v51) — Greek ἀμὴν ἀμήν, the Hebrew אָמֵן left untranslated by the evangelist; the doubled form is only in John. "You" in "I say to you, you will see" is plural (ὑμῖν, ὄψεσθε), though he speaks to *Netanel*. ὑμῖν WH Treg NA28 ] + ἀπ' ἄρτι "hereafter" RP (so the KJV).
- **"ascending and descending" kept** (v51) — Greek ἀναβαίνοντας καὶ καταβαίνοντας; καταβαίνω is "coming down" in v32–33.

### Notes
- **v1** — λόγος is "word, speech, account, reason"; "In the beginning" echoes Gen 1:1.
- **v14** — "tented": compare the tent (*mishkan*) of Exod 25:8–9.
- **v15** — the parentheses are the editors' (SBLGNT); v16 continues v14.
- **v23** quotes Isa 40:3.
- **v29–34** — the quotation marks follow the sense: *Yochanan* speaks from v29 to v31 and again in v32–34.
- **v51** alludes to Jacob's ladder (Gen 28:12).
