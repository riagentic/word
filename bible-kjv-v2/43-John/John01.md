## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — divine name kept in Hebrew rather than translated to the generic English "God."
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v17, 29, etc.) — Hebrew יֵשׁוּעַ *Yeshua* ("YHWH saves"); the actual name; "Jesus" is a Greek/Latin transliteration via *Iesous*.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v17, 20, etc.) — Hebrew מָשִׁיחַ *Mashiach* ("anointed"); "Christ" is a Greek calque (*Christos*) that has become a surname; the Hebrew title is preserved. First occurrence of *Christos* in v17 rendered *Mashiach*.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v33) — Hebrew רוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ *Ruach HaQodesh* ("the Spirit of Holiness"); "Holy Ghost" is archaic KJV for the same concept.
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (v40, 42) — Aramaic כֵּיפָא *Kepha* ("rock/stone"); text notes *Yeshua* renamed Simon as *Kefa* (v42). KJV "Peter" is the Greek *Petros* form. The text also preserves *Petros* gloss at v42 to explain the renaming.
- **"Nathanael" — retained** (vv45–49) — Greek Ναθαναήλ renders Hebrew נְתַנְאֵל *Netan'el* ("El has given"); the familiar form is kept in the text.
- **"baptize/baptizest" → "immerse/immersing"** (v25, 26, 28, 31, 33) — Greek βαπτίζω *baptizo* means "to dip, plunge, immerse"; "baptize" is a transliteration that obscures the physical act.
- **"law" → "Torah"** (v17) — Hebrew תּוֹרָה *Torah* ("instruction, teaching"); "law" is reductive.
- **"record" / archaic "bare record" → "bore witness / bore record"** (v15, 32, 34) — archaic KJV "bare" modernized to "bore."
- **"Esaias" → "Isaiah"** (v23) — standard anglicized form replaces KJV's Greek transliteration *Esaias*.
- **"Bethabara" → "Bethany"** (v28) — earlier MSS read Βηθανίᾳ *Bethania* beyond the Jordan (distinct from the Bethany near Jerusalem); KJV's "Bethabara" follows later manuscripts.
- **"Messias" / "*Mashiach*"** (v41) — text explains the meaning ("Anointed") as per the Greek parenthetical; consistent with *Mashiach* used throughout.
- **"Cephas" / "*Kefa*"** (v42) — the Aramaic name given by *Yeshua*; Greek gloss *Petros* preserved as "stone."
- **"Simon the son of Jona" → "son of John"** (v42) — Greek has Ιωάννου; "Jona" is a KJV contraction.
- **Archaic pronouns** (thee, thou, thy, art, saith, etc.) modernized throughout.
- **v23** — OT quotation: Isa 40:3 ("Make straight the way of the Lord").
- **v51** — allusion to Jacob's ladder (Gen 28:12).

### Notes
- **v1** — *Word* = "Word, Reason, discourse"; echoes Gen 1:1 and Hebrew Wisdom/Memra traditions.
- **v14** — *eskenosen* ("dwelt") = "tabernacled," evoking the *Mishkan*/Shekinah.
- **v18** — earliest MSS read *monogenes theos* ("only *Elohim*"), not "only Son."
- **v23** quotes Isa 40:3.
- **v51** alludes to Jacob's ladder (Gen 28:12).
