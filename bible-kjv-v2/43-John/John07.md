## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v26, 27, 31, 41, 42) — Hebrew messianic title.
- **"law" → "Torah"** (v19, 23, 49, 51) — Hebrew תּוֹרָה *Torah* preserved.
- **"Verily, verily" → "Truly, truly"** — no occurrence in this chapter; consistent usage.
- **"7:53–8:11 bracket notation"** — double-bracket [[ ]] used to indicate the *Pericope Adulterae*, absent from earliest MSS, continues into ch. 8.
- **"the feast" (last day, great day)** (v37) — *Hoshana Rabbah*, the seventh day of *Sukkot*, when water-pouring ceremony occurred; contextually significant for *Yeshua's* living water declaration.
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** (thyself, ye, hath, doest, believest, etc.) modernized throughout.
