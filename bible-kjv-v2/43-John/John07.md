## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v17) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v26, 27, 31, 41, 42) — Hebrew messianic title; Greek ὁ χριστός, "the Anointed One", with the article.
- **"law" → "Torah"** (v19, 23, 49, 51) — Hebrew תּוֹרָה *Torah* preserved; Greek ὁ νόμος.
- **"7:53–8:11 bracket notation"** — double brackets [[ ]] mark the *Pericope Adulterae*, which continues into ch. 8. Of the editions, only RP has it in the text here; the SBLGNT prints it in double brackets, and the earliest manuscripts do not have it. It is translated as printed.
- **"After these things Jesus walked in Galilee: for he would not walk in Jewry" → "And after these things *Yeshua* was walking in Galilee; for he did not want to walk in Judea"** (v1) — Greek καί at the head; περιεπάτει, imperfect; οὐ γὰρ ἤθελεν … περιπατεῖν, the same verb "walk" twice.
- **"Now the Jews' feast of tabernacles was at hand" → "Now the feast of the Jews was near, Sukkot"** (v2) — Greek ἡ ἑορτὴ τῶν Ἰουδαίων ἡ σκηνοπηγία: the name stands last. σκηνοπηγία is "tent-pitching" (σκηνή "tent"), the Greek name of חַג הַסֻּכּוֹת (Lev 23:34; Deut 16:13).
- **"Depart hence, and go into Judaea, that thy disciples also may see the works that thou doest" → "Depart from here and go into Judea, that your disciples also will behold your works which you do"** (v3) — Greek μετάβηθι, the verb "pass over" of 5:24, here of moving from a place; θεωρήσουσιν, a future indicative after ἵνα (WH Treg NA28; RP subjunctive); σοῦ τὰ ἔργα.
- **"there is no man that doeth any thing in secret, and he himself seeketh to be known openly … shew thyself to the world" → "no one does anything in secret and himself seeks to be in the open … manifest yourself to the world"** (v4) — Greek ἐν παρρησίᾳ εἶναι, "to be in openness" (παρρησία "boldness, openness"; "known" is not in the Greek); φανέρωσον, the verb "manifest". The Greek has "and", not "if".
- **"For neither did his brethren believe in him" → "For not even his brothers were believing in him"** (v5) — Greek οὐδὲ γάρ; ἐπίστευον, imperfect.
- **"Then Jesus said unto them, My time is not yet come: but your time is alway ready" → "So *Yeshua* says to them, My time is not yet present, but your time is always ready"** (v6, 8) — Greek λέγει, a present; ὁ καιρός, "the fitting time, season" (not ὥρα "hour", v30, nor χρόνος "time", v33); πάρεστιν "is present"; ἕτοιμος "ready".
- **"The world cannot hate you" → "The world is not able to hate you"** (v7) — Greek οὐ δύναται; μαρτυρῶ περὶ αὐτοῦ, "testify concerning it".
- **"Go ye up unto this feast: I go not up yet unto this feast; for my time is not yet full come" → "You go up to the feast; I am not going up to this feast, because my time has not yet been fulfilled"** (v8) — "this" stands only with the second "feast" (WH Treg NA28). οὐκ ἀναβαίνω, "I am not going up" (Treg NA28) ] οὔπω ἀναβαίνω, "I am not yet going up" (WH RP). πεπλήρωται, a perfect passive of "fulfill".
- **"When he had said these words unto them, he abode still in Galilee" → "And having said these things, he himself remained in Galilee"** (v9) — Greek αὐτός, "he himself" (Treg NA28) ] αὐτοῖς, "to them" (WH RP); ἔμεινεν, the verb μένω ("abide" of persons), here of a stay in a place: "remain".
- **"not openly, but as it were in secret" → "not manifestly, but as in secret"** (v10) — Greek οὐ φανερῶς (root of φανερόω, v4) ἀλλὰ ὡς ἐν κρυπτῷ; the same "in secret" as in v4.
- **"Then the Jews sought him at the feast, and said, Where is he?" → "So the Jews were seeking him at the feast and saying, Where is that one?"** (v11) — Greek ἐζήτουν, ἔλεγον, imperfects; ἐκεῖνος.
- **"there was much murmuring among the people concerning him: for some said, He is a good man: others said, Nay; but he deceiveth the people" → "there was much murmuring concerning him among the crowds; some were saying, He is good, but others were saying, No, but he deceives the crowd"** (v12) — Greek γογγυσμός, the noun of γογγύζω (6:41; v32); ἐν τοῖς ὄχλοις, plural; Ἀγαθός ἐστιν, "he is good" ("man" is not in the Greek); πλανᾷ "deceives, leads astray".
- **"no man spake openly of him for fear of the Jews" → "no one was speaking openly concerning him, because of the fear of the Jews"** (v13, 26) — Greek παρρησίᾳ; διὰ τὸν φόβον τῶν Ἰουδαίων.
- **"Now about the midst of the feast Jesus went up into the temple, and taught" → "Now when the feast was already at its middle, *Yeshua* went up into the temple and was teaching"** (v14) — Greek ἤδη δὲ τῆς ἑορτῆς μεσούσης; ἐδίδασκεν, imperfect ("began" is not in the Greek).
- **"How knoweth this man letters, having never learned?" → "How does this one know letters, not having learned?"** (v15) — Greek γράμματα οἶδεν μὴ μεμαθηκώς: "letters", written learning (the word of 5:47); no "never".
- **"My doctrine is not mine, but his that sent me" → "My teaching is not mine, but his who sent me"** (v16, 17) — Greek διδαχή, "teaching".
- **"If any man will do his will, he shall know of the doctrine, whether it be of God, or whether I speak of myself" → "if anyone wants to do his will, he will know concerning the teaching, whether it is of *Elohim* or I speak from myself"** (v17, 18, 28) — Greek τὸ θέλημα αὐτοῦ, "his will": the Greek does not say "God's will" here; θέλῃ … θέλημα, verb and noun of one root; ἐκ τοῦ θεοῦ; ἀπ' ἐμαυτοῦ, "from myself" (no word "authority").
- **"the same is true, and no unrighteousness is in him" → "this one is true, and unrighteousness is not in him"** (v18) — Greek ἀληθής; ἀδικία, "unrighteousness" (1 John 1:9; 5:17), not "falsehood".
- **"Did not Moses give you the law, and yet none of you keepeth the law? Why go ye about to kill me?" → "Has not Moses given you the Torah? And none of you does the Torah. Why do you seek to kill me?"** (v19) — δέδωκεν, a perfect (NA28 RP) ] ἔδωκεν, "gave" (WH Treg) — 2–2, NA28 decides; καί is "and"; ποιεῖ, "does", not "keeps" (τηρέω).
- **"The people answered and said, Thou hast a devil: who goeth about to kill thee?" → "The crowd answered, You have a demon; who seeks to kill you?"** (v20) — Greek ὁ ὄχλος; δαιμόνιον, "a demon"; "and said" (RP) is not in WH Treg NA28.
- **"I have done one work, and ye all marvel" → "One work I did, and you all marvel"** (v21) — Greek ἓν ἔργον ἐποίησα, aorist; "at it" is not in the Greek.
- **"Moses therefore gave unto you circumcision" → "Because of this Moses has given you circumcision"** (v22) — Greek διὰ τοῦτο, printed with v22; without punctuation in the old manuscripts it can also end v21: "you all marvel because of this". δέδωκεν, a perfect; ἐκ τοῦ Μωϋσέως … ἐκ τῶν πατέρων, "of, out of"; ἐν σαββάτῳ without the article (v22, 23).
- **"are ye angry at me, because I have made a man every whit whole on the sabbath day?" → "are you angry with me because I made a whole man healthy on a Sabbath?"** (v23) — Greek ὅλον ἄνθρωπον ὑγιῆ ἐποίησα, "I made a whole man healthy" (5:11, 15); "body" is not in the Greek. λυθῇ "be loosed, broken", the verb of 5:18.
- **"Judge not according to the appearance, but judge righteous judgment" → "Do not judge according to appearance, but judge the righteous judgment"** (v24) — Greek κατ' ὄψιν; τὴν δικαίαν κρίσιν κρίνετε, verb and noun of one root, with the article.
- **"Then said some of them of Jerusalem" → "So some of those of Jerusalem were saying"** (v25) — Greek ἐκ τῶν Ἱεροσολυμιτῶν, "of the Jerusalemites"; ἔλεγον, imperfect.
- **"But, lo, he speaketh boldly … Do the rulers know indeed that this is the very Christ?" → "And see, he speaks openly … Can it be that the rulers truly came to know that this is the *Mashiach*?"** (v26) — Greek ἴδε; μήποτε ἀληθῶς ἔγνωσαν οἱ ἄρχοντες, an aorist, with μήποτε "perhaps, can it be that"; οἱ ἄρχοντες, "the rulers" (v48). The second "truly" ("the very Christ", RP) is not in WH Treg NA28.
- **"Howbeit we know this man whence he is: but when Christ cometh, no man knoweth whence he is" → "But this one, we know where he is from; but the *Mashiach*, when he comes, no one realizes where he is from"** (v27) — two verbs: οἴδαμεν "we know", γινώσκει "comes to know, realizes"; ἔρχηται "comes", not "appears"; γινώσκει is a present, not "will know".
- **"Then cried Jesus in the temple as he taught, saying, Ye both know me, and ye know whence I am: and I am not come of myself, but he that sent me is true" → "So *Yeshua* cried out in the temple, teaching and saying, You both know me and know where I am from; and I have not come from myself, but the one who sent me is true"** (v28) — Greek ἔκραξεν "cried out"; κἀμὲ οἴδατε καὶ οἴδατε; καί is "and", not "but"; ἀληθινός, "true, real" (v18 has ἀληθής). The first sentence can also be read as a question.
- **"for I am from him, and he hath sent me" → "because I am from him, and that one sent me"** (v29) — Greek παρ' αὐτοῦ; κἀκεῖνός με ἀπέστειλεν, aorist.
- **"Then they sought to take him: but no man laid hands on him" → "So they were seeking to seize him, and no one laid his hand upon him"** (v30, 32, 44) — Greek πιάσαι, "seize" (not a word for arrest); καί "and"; τὴν χεῖρα, singular (v44 plural).
- **"And many of the people believed on him, and said, When Christ cometh, will he do more miracles than these which this man hath done?" → "But of the crowd many believed in him, and they were saying, The *Mashiach*, when he comes, will he do more signs than those which this one did?"** (v31) — Greek ἐκ τοῦ ὄχλου δὲ πολλοί; σημεῖα "signs"; ἐποίησεν, aorist. The question with μή expects the answer "no".
- **"The Pharisees heard that the people murmured such things concerning him; and the Pharisees and the chief priests sent officers to take him" → "The Pharisees heard the crowd murmuring these things concerning him, and the high priests and the Pharisees sent officers that they might seize him"** (v32, 45) — Greek γογγύζοντος; οἱ ἀρχιερεῖς, the plural of "high priest"; ὑπηρέτας, "assistants, officers" (1 Cor 4:1), the men under their orders.
- **"Yet a little while am I with you, and then I go unto him that sent me" → "Yet a little time I am with you, and I go to the one who sent me"** (v33) — Greek ἔτι χρόνον μικρόν; ὑπάγω, a present; "then" is not in the Greek.
- **"Ye shall seek me, and shall not find me: and where I am, thither ye cannot come" → "You will seek me and will not find me, and where I am, you are not able to come"** (v34, 36) — "me" after "find": με WH NA28; Treg RP (and the printed SBLGNT) omit it — 2–2, NA28 decides. εἰμί is "I am" (with another accent, εἶμι, "I go"; the editions print "am").
- **"Then said the Jews among themselves, Whither will he go, that we shall not find him? will he go unto the dispersed among the Gentiles, and teach the Gentiles?" → "So the Jews said to themselves, Where is this one about to go, that we will not find him? Is he about to go to the Dispersion of the Greeks and teach the Greeks?"** (v35) — Greek πρὸς ἑαυτούς; μέλλει πορεύεσθαι, twice; τὴν διασπορὰν τῶν Ἑλλήνων, "the Dispersion of the Greeks": the Jews scattered among the Greeks (1 Pet 1:1; Jas 1:1); Ἕλληνας, "Greeks". The second question with μή expects "no".
- **"What manner of saying is this that he said" → "What is this word which he said"** (v36) — Greek τίς ἐστιν ὁ λόγος οὗτος; the saying is quoted as one sentence.
- **"In the last day, that great day of the feast, Jesus stood and cried" → "Now on the last day, the great day of the feast, *Yeshua* stood and cried out"** (v37) — Greek εἱστήκει, "was standing" (a pluperfect form with imperfect sense); ἔκραξεν.
- **"He that believeth on me, as the scripture hath said, out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water" → "The one who believes in me, just as the Scripture said, rivers of living water will flow out of his belly"** (v38) — Greek ἐκ τῆς κοιλίας αὐτοῦ, "belly, womb", the inner part of the body; "heart" is καρδία. εἶπεν, aorist. The printed punctuation is followed; with a stop after "in me" the sentence reads: "If anyone thirsts, let him come to me, and let him drink who believes in me. Just as the Scripture said, rivers … will flow out of his belly" — then "his" can be the *Mashiach's*. No single passage has these words (compare Isa 44:3; 55:1; 58:11; Ezek 47:1; Zech 14:8).
- **"this spake he of the Spirit, which they that believe on him should receive: for the Holy Ghost was not yet given; because that Jesus was not yet glorified" → "this he said concerning the Spirit, which those who believed in him were about to receive; for there was not yet Spirit, because *Yeshua* had not yet been glorified"** (v39) — Greek οὗ (WH Treg RP; NA28 ὅ), a neuter relative: "which" is grammatical gender; οἱ πιστεύσαντες, an aorist participle (RP present); οὔπω γὰρ ἦν πνεῦμα, literally "for Spirit was not yet" (WH NA28): no "given" and no article in the Greek; Treg RP add ἅγιον, "Holy Spirit". οὐδέπω NA28 RP, οὔπω WH Treg, the same sense.
- **"Many of the people therefore, when they heard this saying, said, Of a truth this is the Prophet" → "So some of the crowd, having heard these words, were saying, This is truly the Prophet"** (v40) — Greek ἐκ τοῦ ὄχλου, "(some) of the crowd" (WH Treg NA28; RP "many"); τῶν λόγων τούτων, plural.
- **"But some said, Shall Christ come out of Galilee?" → "But some were saying, Does the *Mashiach* then come out of Galilee?"** (v41) — Greek Μὴ γὰρ … ἔρχεται; a present; the question expects "no".
- **"Hath not the scripture said, That Christ cometh of the seed of David, and out of the town of Bethlehem, where David was?" → "Did not the Scripture say that of the seed of David, and from Bethlehem, the village where David was, the *Mashiach* comes?"** (v42) — Greek ἐκ τοῦ σπέρματος Δαυίδ … ἀπὸ Βηθλέεμ τῆς κώμης; "the *Mashiach*" stands once, at the end; σπέρμα "seed"; κώμη "village" (2 Sam 7:12; Mic 5:2).
- **"So there was a division among the people because of him" → "So a schism came to be in the crowd because of him"** (v43) — Greek σχίσμα, "a split, a tear" (9:16; 10:19; 1 Cor 1:10); ἐγένετο.
- **"And some of them would have taken him" → "And some of them wanted to seize him"** (v44) — Greek ἤθελον … πιάσαι. ἐπέβαλεν NA28 RP; WH Treg ἔβαλεν, the same sense.
- **"Then came the officers to the chief priests and Pharisees; and they said unto them" → "So the officers came to the high priests and Pharisees, and those said to them"** (v45) — Greek ἐκεῖνοι.
- **"Never man spake like this man" → "Never did a man speak thus"** (v46) — Greek Οὐδέποτε ἐλάλησεν οὕτως ἄνθρωπος (WH NA28); Treg RP add "as this man".
- **"Are ye also deceived? Have any of the rulers or of the Pharisees believed on him?" → "Have you also been deceived? Has any of the rulers believed in him, or of the Pharisees?"** (v47, 48) — Greek πεπλάνησθε, a perfect passive of πλανάω (v12); both questions with μή expect "no".
- **"But this people who knoweth not the law are cursed" → "But this crowd that does not know the Torah — they are cursed"** (v49) — Greek ὁ ὄχλος οὗτος … ἐπάρατοί εἰσιν: the subject is singular, the verb plural; ἐπάρατος, "under a curse" (WH Treg NA28; RP ἐπικατάρατοι).
- **"Nicodemus saith unto them, (he that came to Jesus by night, being one of them,)" → "Nicodemus says to them — he who came to him formerly, being one of them —"** (v50) — Greek λέγει, a present; "by night" (RP) is not in WH Treg NA28; πρότερον WH Treg, τὸ πρότερον NA28, the same sense; αὐτόν "him".
- **"Doth our law judge any man, before it hear him, and know what he doeth?" → "Does our Torah judge the man unless it first hears from him and comes to know what he does?"** (v51) — Greek τὸν ἄνθρωπον, with the article; ἐὰν μὴ ἀκούσῃ πρῶτον παρ' αὐτοῦ καὶ γνῷ. The question with μή expects "no".
- **"Art thou also of Galilee? Search, and look: for out of Galilee ariseth no prophet" → "Are you also of Galilee? Search and see that out of Galilee a prophet does not arise"** (v52) — Greek ἐραύνησον καὶ ἴδε ὅτι; προφήτης without the article; ἐγείρεται, a present (RP perfect), "is raised, arises". ἀπεκρίθησαν καὶ εἶπαν, both verbs.
- **"And every man went unto his own house" → "And each went to his house"** (v53) — Greek καὶ ἐπορεύθη ἕκαστος εἰς τὸν οἶκον αὐτοῦ; the sentence runs on into 8:1.

### Notes
- **v37** — "the last day, the great day of the feast": the seventh day of *Sukkot* (*Hoshana Rabbah*) or the eighth day of assembly (Lev 23:36); on the days of the feast water was poured out at the altar, which gives the saying about living water its setting.
- **v8, 10** — "I am not going up … then he himself also went up": the reading "not yet" (WH RP) removes the tension; the verse follows "not" (Treg NA28).
