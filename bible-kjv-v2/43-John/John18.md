# John 18

[1] When ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ had spoken these words, he went out with his disciples across the brook ⟦kidron|Qidron|Kidron⟧, where there was a garden, which he and his disciples entered.

[2] Now ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧, who betrayed him, also knew the place, for ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ often met there with his disciples.

[3] So ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧, having procured a band of soldiers and some officers from the chief priests and the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧, went there with lanterns and torches and weapons.

[4] Then ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, knowing all that would happen to him, came forward and said to them, "Whom do you seek?"

[5] They answered him, "⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ of ⟦nazareth|Natzrat|Nazareth⟧." ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "**I am he**." ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧, who betrayed him, was standing with them.

[6] When ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "I am he," they drew back and fell to the ground.

[7] So he asked them again, "Whom do you seek?" And they said, "⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ of ⟦nazareth|Natzrat|Nazareth⟧."

[8] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered, "I told you that I am he. So, if you seek me, let these men go."

[9] This was to fulfill the word that he had spoken: "Of those whom you gave me I have lost not one."

[10] Then ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, having a sword, drew it and struck the high priest's servant and cut off his right ear. (The servant's name was ⟦malchus|Malchos|Malchus⟧.)

[11] So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, "Put your sword into its sheath; shall I not drink the cup that the Father has given me?"

[12] So the band of soldiers and their captain and the officers of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ arrested ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ and bound him.

[13] First they led him to ⟦annas|Chanan|Annas⟧, for he was the father-in-law of ⟦caiaphas|Qayafa|Caiaphas⟧, who was high priest that year.

[14] It was ⟦caiaphas|Qayafa|Caiaphas⟧ who had advised the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ that it would be expedient that one man should die for the people.

[15] ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ followed ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, and so did another disciple. Since that disciple was known to the high priest, he entered with ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ into the courtyard of the high priest,

[16] but ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ stood outside at the door. So the other disciple, who was known to the high priest, went out and spoke to the servant girl who kept watch at the door, and brought ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ in.

[17] The servant girl at the door said to ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, "You also are not one of this man's disciples, are you?" He said, "I am not."

[18] Now the servants and officers had made a charcoal fire, because it was cold, and they were standing and warming themselves. ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ also was with them, standing and warming himself.

[19] The high priest then questioned ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ about his disciples and his teaching.

[20] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered him, "I have spoken openly to the world. I have always taught in synagogues and in the temple, where all ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ come together. I have said nothing in secret.

[21] Why do you ask me? Ask those who have heard me what I said to them; they know what I said."

[22] When he had said these things, one of the officers standing by struck ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ with his hand, saying, "Is that how you answer the high priest?"

[23] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered him, "If what I said is wrong, bear witness about the wrong; but if what I said is right, why do you strike me?"

[24] ⟦annas|Chanan|Annas⟧ then sent him bound to ⟦caiaphas|Qayafa|Caiaphas⟧ the high priest.

[25] Now ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ was standing and warming himself. So they said to him, "You also are not one of his disciples, are you?" He denied it and said, "I am not."

[26] One of the servants of the high priest, a relative of the man whose ear ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ had cut off, asked, "Did I not see you in the garden with him?"

[27] ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ again denied it, and at once a rooster crowed.

[28] Then they led ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ from the house of ⟦caiaphas|Qayafa|Caiaphas⟧ to the governor's headquarters. It was early morning. They themselves did not enter the headquarters, so that they would not be defiled, but could eat the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧.

[29] So ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ went outside to them and said, "What accusation do you bring against this man?"

[30] They answered him, "If this man were not doing evil, we would not have delivered him over to you."

[31] ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ said to them, "Take him yourselves and judge him by your own ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧." The ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ said to him, "It is not lawful for us to put anyone to death."

[32] This was to fulfill the word that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ had spoken to show by what kind of death he was going to die.

[33] So ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ entered his headquarters again and called ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ and said to him, "Are you the King of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧?"

[34] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered, "Do you say this of your own accord, or did others say it to you about me?"

[35] ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ answered, "Am I a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧? Your own nation and the chief priests have delivered you over to me. What have you done?"

[36] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered, "**My kingdom is not of this world. If my kingdom were of this world, my servants would have been fighting, that I might not be delivered over to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧. But my kingdom is not from the world.**"

[37] Then ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ said to him, "So you are a king?" ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered, "You say that I am a king. For this purpose I was born and for this purpose I have come into the world — to bear witness to the truth. Everyone who is of the truth listens to my voice."

[38] ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ said to him, "What is truth?"

After he had said this, he went back outside to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and told them, "I find no guilt in him.

[39] But you have a custom that I should release one man for you at the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧. So do you want me to release to you the King of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧?"

[40] They cried out again, "Not this man, but ⟦barabbas|Bar-Abba|Barabbas⟧!" Now ⟦barabbas|Bar-Abba|Barabbas⟧ was a robber.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** — no direct occurrence; "Father" used in *Yeshua's* speech.
- **"Peter" / "*Kefa*"** (v10, 11, 15–27) — Aramaic name used consistently.
- **"Caiaphas"** (v13, 24, 28) — retained as familiar form; Aramaic קַיָּפָא *Qayyapha*.
- **"hall of judgment / praetorium" → "governor's headquarters"** (v28, 33) — Greek πραιτώριον *praitorion* = the praetorium/headquarters; "hall of judgment" is a KJV periphrasis.
- **"I am he"** (v5, 6, 8) — Greek ἐγώ εἰμι *egō eimi*; retained as theologically significant (cf. Ex 3:14; Jhn 8:58).
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** (thee, thou, art, etc.) modernized throughout.
