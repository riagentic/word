# John 9

[1] And passing by, he saw a man blind from birth.

[2] And his disciples asked him, saying, "Rabbi, who sinned, this one or his parents, that he should be born blind?"

[3] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered, "Neither did this one sin nor his parents, but that the works of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ might be manifested in him.

[4] We must work the works of the one who sent me while it is day; night comes, when no one can work.

[5] When I am in the world, I am light of the world."

[6] Having said these things, he spat on the ground and made mud of the spittle, and he smeared the mud upon his eyes

[7] and said to him, "Go, wash in the pool of ⟦siloam|Shiloach|Siloam⟧" (which is interpreted, Sent). So he went away and washed, and came seeing.

[8] So the neighbors and those who used to behold him before, that he was a beggar, were saying, "Is this not the one who sits and begs?"

[9] Others were saying, "This is he." Others were saying, "No, but he is like him." That one was saying, "It is I."

[10] So they were saying to him, "So how were your eyes opened?"

[11] That one answered, "The man who is called ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ made mud and smeared my eyes and said to me, 'Go to ⟦siloam|Shiloach|Siloam⟧ and wash.' So having gone away and washed, I received sight."

[12] And they said to him, "Where is that one?" He says, "I do not know."

[13] They bring him to the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧, the one formerly blind.

[14] Now it was a ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ on the day when ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ made the mud and opened his eyes.

[15] So again the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ also were asking him how he received sight. And he said to them, "He put mud upon my eyes, and I washed, and I see."

[16] So some of the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ were saying, "This man is not from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, because he does not keep the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧." But others were saying, "How can a man who is a sinner do such signs?" And there was a schism among them.

[17] So they say to the blind man again, "What do you say about him, seeing that he opened your eyes?" And he said, "He is a prophet."

[18] So the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ did not believe concerning him that he had been blind and received sight, until they called the parents of the one who had received sight

[19] and asked them, saying, "Is this your son, of whom you say that he was born blind? So how does he now see?"

[20] So his parents answered and said, "We know that this is our son and that he was born blind;

[21] but how he now sees we do not know, or who opened his eyes we do not know. Ask him; he is of age; he himself will speak concerning himself."

[22] These things his parents said because they feared the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧; for the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ had already agreed that if anyone should confess him as ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧, he should be put out of the synagogue.

[23] For this reason his parents said, "He is of age; ask him."

[24] So they called a second time the man who had been blind and said to him, "Give glory to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; we know that this man is a sinner."

[25] So that one answered, "Whether he is a sinner I do not know; one thing I know, that, being blind, now I see."

[26] So they said to him, "What did he do to you? How did he open your eyes?"

[27] He answered them, "I told you already, and you did not hear; why do you want to hear again? You do not also want to become his disciples, do you?"

[28] And they reviled him and said, "You are a disciple of that one, but we are disciples of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧;

[29] we know that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has spoken to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, but this one — we do not know where he is from."

[30] The man answered and said to them, "Why, in this is the marvelous thing, that you do not know where he is from, and he opened my eyes.

[31] We know that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ does not hear sinners, but if anyone is one who reveres ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and does his will, this one he hears.

[32] From the age it was not heard that anyone opened the eyes of one born blind;

[33] if this one were not from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, he could do nothing."

[34] They answered and said to him, "In sins you were born, wholly, and you teach us?" And they cast him out.

[35] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ heard that they had cast him out, and having found him he said, "Do you believe in the Son of Man?"

[36] That one answered and said, "And who is he, Lord, that I may believe in him?"

[37] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to him, "You have both seen him, and the one speaking with you is that one."

[38] And he said, "I believe, Lord"; and he bowed down to him.

[39] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said, "For judgment I came into this world, that those who do not see may see, and those who see may become blind."

[40] Those of the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ who were with him heard these things and said to him, "We also are not blind, are we?"

[41] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "If you were blind, you would have no sin; but now you say, 'We see': your sin abides."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration. The Greek does not name him in v1 ("And passing by, he saw") and has "him" in v22; the verses follow it.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved. In v31 the Greek names him once and has θεοσεβής once; the last clause has no noun ("this one he hears").
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v22) — Hebrew messianic title; Greek χριστόν without the article, "confess him as *Mashiach*".
- **"which was blind from his birth" → "blind from birth"** (v1) — Greek ἐκ γενετῆς.
- **"that he was born blind" → "that he should be born blind"** (v2) — Greek ἵνα τυφλὸς γεννηθῇ. γεννάω is "beget" in this translation; in the passive, of a child, it is "be born" (v2, 19, 20, 32, 34).
- **"but that the works of God should be made manifest" → "but that the works of *Elohim* might be manifested"** (v3) — Greek ἀλλ' ἵνα φανερωθῇ: the sentence has no verb before "that" ("but [it was] that …"); the verse leaves it so.
- **"I must work" → "We must work"** (v4) — Greek ἡμᾶς δεῖ (WH Treg NA28); RP reads ἐμέ, "I". "Work" and "works" are one root (ἐργάζεσθαι, ἔργα).
- **"As long as I am in the world" → "When I am in the world"** (v5) — Greek ὅταν, "when, whenever". "Light of the world": Greek φῶς stands without the article ("the light" or "a light") and without ἐγώ, unlike 8:12.
- **"clay" → "mud"; "anointed the eyes of the blind man with the clay" → "smeared the mud upon his eyes"** (v6, 11) — Greek πηλός, wet earth; ἐπέχρισεν, "smeared on" (root χρίω, "anoint"; Treg NA28 RP; WH reads ἐπέθηκεν, "put upon", as in v15). "Of the blind man" is the RP reading; WH Treg NA28 have "his".
- **"which is by interpretation, Sent" → "which is interpreted, Sent"** (v7) — Greek ὃ ἑρμηνεύεται Ἀπεσταλμένος. "In the pool" is literally "into the pool" (εἰς).
- **"they which before had seen him that he was blind" → "those who used to behold him before, that he was a beggar"** (v8) — Greek οἱ θεωροῦντες … ὅτι προσαίτης ἦν (WH Treg NA28; RP τυφλός, "blind").
- **"I am he" → "It is I"** (v9) — Greek Ἐγώ εἰμι, literally "I am": here the man plainly says who he is. The words are the same as in 8:24, 28, 58.
- **"He" → "That one"** (v9, 11, 12, 25, 28, 36, 37) — Greek ἐκεῖνος.
- **"How were thine eyes opened?" → "So how were your eyes opened?"** (v10) — the verse follows the reading with οὖν, "so" (WH NA28); Treg RP and the SBLGNT omit it.
- **"I received sight" kept** (v11, 15, 18) — Greek ἀναβλέπω, "look up" or "see again"; of a man born blind it can only mean that he gained sight.
- **"They brought" → "They bring"; "He said" → "He says"; "They say" kept** (v12, 13, 17) — the Greek presents are kept.
- **"it was the sabbath day when" → "it was a *Shabbat* on the day when"** (v14) — Greek ἦν δὲ σάββατον ἐν ᾗ ἡμέρᾳ (WH Treg NA28; RP ὅτε).
- **"division" → "schism"** (v16) — Greek σχίσμα, a split or tear. "But others": the verse follows ἄλλοι δέ (WH NA28); Treg RP and the SBLGNT have no δέ.
- **"now" kept** (v19, 21, 25) — Greek ἄρτι in v19 and v25, νῦν in v21 and v41.
- **"he is of age" kept** (v21, 23) — Greek ἡλικίαν ἔχει, literally "he has age". "Ask" in v23 is the compound ἐπερωτήσατε (WH NA28; Treg RP ἐρωτήσατε, as in v21).
- **"synagogue" / "put out of the synagogue"** (v22) — Greek ἀποσυνάγωγος γένηται, "should become one put out of the synagogue"; a significant social-religious sanction in first-century Jewish context.
- **"whereas I was blind" → "being blind"** (v25) — Greek τυφλὸς ὤν, a participle: "I who was blind".
- **"will ye also be his disciples?" → "You do not also want to become his disciples, do you?"** (v27) — Greek μή begins the question and expects the answer "no"; so also in v40. "You did not hear … to hear": one verb, ἀκούω, as in v31.
- **"Then they reviled him" → "And they reviled him"** (v28) — the verse follows καὶ ἐλοιδόρησαν (WH NA28); Treg reads οἱ δὲ ἐλοιδόρησαν, RP and the SBLGNT ἐλοιδόρησαν alone.
- **"a marvellous thing" → "the marvelous thing"** (v30) — Greek τὸ θαυμαστόν with the article (WH Treg NA28); γάρ is given as "Why,".
- **"a worshipper of God" → "one who reveres *Elohim*"** (v31) — Greek θεοσεβής, one word: "God-revering".
- **"Since the world began was it not heard" → "From the age it was not heard"** (v32) — Greek ἐκ τοῦ αἰῶνος, the idiom for "from of old, ever".
- **"Thou wast altogether born in sins" → "In sins you were born, wholly"** (v34) — Greek Ἐν ἁμαρτίαις σὺ ἐγεννήθης ὅλος; "sins" is plural. "Cast him out": ἐξέβαλον αὐτὸν ἔξω, literally "cast him out outside".
- **"dost thou teach us?" → "you teach us?"** (v34) — Greek καὶ σὺ διδάσκεις ἡμᾶς; a plain present.
- **"the Son of God" → "the Son of Man"** (v35) — Greek τὸν υἱὸν τοῦ ἀνθρώπου (WH NA28); Treg RP read τοῦ θεοῦ, "of God".
- **"Lord"** (v36, 38) — Greek κύριε both times; the word is also the ordinary respectful address, "sir".
- **"Thou hast both seen him, and it is he that talketh with thee" → "You have both seen him, and the one speaking with you is that one"** (v37) — Greek Καὶ ἑώρακας αὐτὸν καὶ ὁ λαλῶν μετὰ σοῦ ἐκεῖνός ἐστιν.
- **"worshipped" → "bowed down to"** (v38) — Greek προσεκύνησεν, to bow down before someone.
- **"For judgment" kept** (v39) — Greek κρίμα, the verdict that judging brings ("sentence" in the letters); only here in John, who elsewhere has κρίσις, "judgment". "A sentence" would not be clear English here.
- **"ye should have no sin … your sin remaineth" → "you would have no sin … your sin abides"** (v41) — Greek ἁμαρτίαν, "sin" (not "guilt"); μένει, "abides". "Now you say, 'We see'" is a statement of its own in the Greek; RP adds οὖν, "therefore", before "your sin".
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** (thee, thou, thy, hast, art, doest, etc.) modernized throughout.
