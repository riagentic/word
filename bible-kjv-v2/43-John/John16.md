# John 16

[1] "These things I have spoken to you so that you may not be made to stumble.

[2] They will put you out of the synagogue; but an hour is coming that everyone who kills you will think he is offering worship to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] And these things they will do because they did not come to know the Father nor me.

[4] But these things I have spoken to you so that when their hour comes you may remember them, that I told you.

"And these things I did not say to you from the beginning, because I was with you.

[5] But now I go to the one who sent me, and none of you questions me, 'Where are you going?'

[6] But because I have spoken these things to you, sorrow has filled your heart.

[7] But I tell you the truth: it is profitable for you that I go away. For if I do not go away, the advocate will by no means come to you; but if I go, I will send him to you.

[8] And having come, that one will reprove the world concerning sin and concerning righteousness and concerning judgment:

[9] concerning sin, because they do not believe in me;

[10] concerning righteousness, because I go to the Father and you behold me no longer;

[11] concerning judgment, because the ruler of this world has been judged.

[12] "I have yet many things to say to you, but you cannot bear them now;

[13] but when that one comes, the Spirit of truth, he will guide you in all the truth; for he will not speak from himself, but whatever he will hear he will speak, and the things that are coming he will announce to you.

[14] That one will glorify me, because he will receive from what is mine and will announce it to you.

[15] All things whatsoever the Father has are mine; because of this I said that he receives from what is mine and will announce it to you.

[16] "A little while, and you behold me no longer; and again a little while, and you will see me."

[17] So some of his disciples said to one another, "What is this that he says to us, 'A little while, and you do not behold me; and again a little while, and you will see me'; and, 'Because I go to the Father'?"

[18] So they were saying, "What is this that he says, 'a little while'? We do not know what he is speaking."

[19] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ knew that they wanted to question him, and he said to them, "Concerning this do you seek with one another, that I said, 'A little while, and you do not behold me; and again a little while, and you will see me'?

[20] Amen, amen, I say to you that you will weep and lament, but the world will rejoice; you will be grieved, but **your sorrow will become joy**.

[21] The woman, when she is giving birth, has sorrow, because her hour has come; but when she has borne the young child, she no longer remembers the affliction, because of the joy that a human being was born into the world.

[22] And you therefore now have sorrow; but I will see you again, and your heart will rejoice, and your joy no one takes away from you.

[23] And in that day you will question me about nothing. Amen, amen, I say to you, if you ask the Father anything, he will give it to you in my name.

[24] Until now you asked nothing in my name; ask, and you will receive, so that your joy may be made full.

[25] "These things I have spoken to you in figures of speech; an hour is coming when I will no longer speak to you in figures of speech, but will declare to you plainly concerning the Father.

[26] In that day you will ask in my name, and I do not say to you that I will request the Father concerning you;

[27] for the Father himself has affection for you, because you have had affection for me and have believed that I came out from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[28] I came out from the Father and have come into the world; again, I leave the world and go to the Father."

[29] His disciples say, "See, now you speak plainly and say no figure of speech.

[30] Now we know that you know all things and have no need that anyone should question you; by this we believe that you came out from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[31] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered them, "Do you now believe?

[32] Behold, an hour is coming, and has come, that you should be scattered, each to his own, and should leave me alone; and I am not alone, because the Father is with me.

[33] These things I have spoken to you so that in me you may have peace. **In the world you have affliction; but be of good courage, I have overcome the world.**"

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v19, 31) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 27, 30) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Verily, verily" → "Amen, amen"** (v20, 23) — Greek ἀμὴν ἀμήν, left untranslated as the Greek leaves it.
- **"These things have I spoken unto you" kept** (v1, 4, 6, 25, 33) — Greek ταῦτα λελάληκα ὑμῖν, the same words each time; "all" is not in the Greek. v4 has a second verb, εἶπον "told / said", twice.
- **"that ye should not be offended" → "so that you may not be made to stumble"** (v1) — Greek σκανδαλισθῆτε, a passive.
- **"They shall put you out of the synagogues: yea, the time cometh, that whosoever killeth you will think that he doeth God service" → "They will put you out of the synagogue; but an hour is coming that everyone who kills you will think he is offering worship to *Elohim*"** (v2) — Greek ἀποσυναγώγους ποιήσουσιν ὑμᾶς, literally "they will make you persons out of the synagogue" (9:22; 12:42); ἀλλ' "but"; ὥρα without the article, with ἵνα; πᾶς ὁ ἀποκτείνας, "everyone who has killed"; λατρείαν προσφέρειν, "to offer worship", the service given to God.
- **"because they have not known the Father, nor me" → "because they did not come to know the Father nor me"** (v3) — Greek ἔγνωσαν, an aorist of γινώσκω.
- **"when the time shall come, ye may remember that I told you of them" → "when their hour comes you may remember them, that I told you"** (v4) — Greek ἡ ὥρα αὐτῶν μνημονεύητε αὐτῶν, "their" and "them" (WH, Treg, NA28; RP omits the first); ἐγώ, an emphatic "I".
- **"I go my way to him that sent me" → "I go to the one who sent me"** (v5, 10, 17) — Greek ὑπάγω; in v7 ἀπέλθω "go away" (twice) and πορευθῶ "go", and in v28 πορεύομαι "go", are two other verbs.
- **"none of you asketh me" → "none of you questions me"** (v5, 19, 23, 30) — Greek ἐρωτάω, here of asking a question; "question" keeps it apart from αἰτέω "ask" (for something) in v23–26. In v26 the same verb ἐρωτάω is "request", as in 14:16. In v23 ἐμὲ οὐκ ἐρωτήσετε οὐδέν can also mean "you will request nothing of me".
- **"It is expedient for you" → "it is profitable for you"** (v7) — Greek συμφέρει.
- **"the Comforter will not come unto you" → "the advocate will by no means come to you"** (v7) — Greek παράκλητος, as in 14:16, 26; 15:26; οὐ μὴ ἔλθῃ, the strong negation (WH, Treg, SBLGNT; NA28 and RP read οὐκ ἐλεύσεται, "will not come"). "him" (αὐτόν) is masculine, as the noun is.
- **"when he is come, he will reprove the world of sin" → "having come, that one will reprove the world concerning sin"** (v8) — Greek ἐλθὼν ἐκεῖνος, masculine, the advocate of v7; ἐλέγξει, "reprove": show to be in the wrong, convict; περί "concerning", three times, and again in v9–11.
- **"ye see me no more" → "you behold me no longer"** (v10, 16, 17, 19) — Greek θεωρεῖτε, a present; "you will see me" (v16, 17, 19) is a different verb and a future, ὄψεσθε. "the Father" (v10: WH, Treg, NA28; RP "my Father").
- **"the prince of this world is judged" → "the ruler of this world has been judged"** (v11) — Greek κέκριται, a perfect.
- **"when he, the Spirit of truth, is come, he will guide you into all truth" → "when that one comes, the Spirit of truth, he will guide you in all the truth"** (v13) — Greek ἐκεῖνος, masculine, still the advocate of v7–8; τὸ πνεῦμα τῆς ἀληθείας stands beside it in apposition. The "he" of v13–15 follows this masculine pronoun (see 14:17, 26). ἐν τῇ ἀληθείᾳ πάσῃ, "in all the truth" (NA28, SBLGNT; 2–2, NA28 decides); WH and Treg read εἰς τὴν ἀλήθειαν πᾶσαν, "into all the truth" (so also RP).
- **"he shall not speak of himself; but whatsoever he shall hear" → "he will not speak from himself, but whatever he will hear"** (v13) — Greek ἀφ' ἑαυτοῦ, as in 14:10; ἀκούσει, a future (Treg, NA28; WH ἀκούει "hears").
- **"he will shew you things to come" → "the things that are coming he will announce to you"** (v13, 14, 15) — Greek τὰ ἐρχόμενα; ἀναγγελεῖ, "announce" (1 John 1:5), three times. In v25 ἀπαγγελῶ, "declare", is a different verb (WH, Treg, NA28; RP ἀναγγελῶ).
- **"he shall receive of mine" → "he will receive from what is mine"** (v14, 15) — Greek ἐκ τοῦ ἐμοῦ λήμψεται; in v15 λαμβάνει is a present, "receives" (KJV "shall take"). λαμβάνω is the verb of v24 ("you will receive").
- **"because I go to the Father" omitted** (v16) — not in the critical text (WH, Treg, NA28; RP adds it from v17). οὐκέτι "no longer" (RP οὐ).
- **"What is this that he saith, A little while? we cannot tell what he saith" → "What is this that he says, 'a little while'? We do not know what he is speaking"** (v18) — Greek ὃ λέγει μικρόν (WH, Treg, SBLGNT; NA28 and RP τὸ μικρόν, "this 'a little while'"); οὐκ οἴδαμεν τί λαλεῖ.
- **"Now Jesus knew that they were desirous to ask him" → "*Yeshua* knew that they wanted to question him"** (v19) — Greek ἔγνω (γινώσκω: came to know, perceived) … ἤθελον; "Now" is not in the printed text (RP οὖν).
- **"Do ye enquire among yourselves of that I said" → "Concerning this do you seek with one another, that I said"** (v19) — Greek ζητεῖτε, "seek", as in 13:33; μετ' ἀλλήλων.
- **"ye shall be sorrowful, but your sorrow shall be turned into joy" → "you will be grieved, but your sorrow will become joy"** (v20) — Greek λυπηθήσεσθε, the verb of the noun λύπη "sorrow" (v6, 20, 21, 22); εἰς χαρὰν γενήσεται.
- **"A woman when she is in travail … as soon as she is delivered of the child, she remembereth no more the anguish, for joy that a man is born" → "The woman, when she is giving birth … when she has borne the young child, she no longer remembers the affliction, because of the joy that a human being was born"** (v21) — Greek ἡ γυνή, with the article; γεννήσῃ and ἐγεννήθη, the verb γεννάω of the mother ("bear") and of the child ("be born"); παιδίον; θλῖψις "affliction", as in v33; ἄνθρωπος, a human being; ἐγεννήθη is an aorist.
- **"your joy no man taketh from you" → "your joy no one takes away from you"** (v22) — Greek αἴρει, a present (NA28, RP; 2–2, NA28 decides); WH and Treg read ἀρεῖ, "will take away". "your heart" is singular.
- **"Whatsoever ye shall ask the Father in my name, he will give it you" → "if you ask the Father anything, he will give it to you in my name"** (v23) — the printed order: ἄν τι αἰτήσητε τὸν πατέρα δώσει ὑμῖν ἐν τῷ ὀνόματί μου (WH, Treg, SBLGNT); NA28 and RP set "in my name" before "he will give", so that it goes with "ask".
- **"Hitherto have ye asked nothing … that your joy may be full" → "Until now you asked nothing … so that your joy may be made full"** (v24) — Greek ᾐτήσατε, an aorist; ᾖ πεπληρωμένη, as in 1 John 1:4.
- **"proverbs" → "figures of speech"** (v25, 29) — Greek παροιμία, a veiled or figurative saying (10:6).
- **"I shall shew you plainly of the Father" → "will declare to you plainly concerning the Father"** (v25, 29) — Greek παρρησίᾳ (v29 ἐν παρρησίᾳ), the dative of the noun "boldness, openness of speech": here "plainly", against "in figures of speech", as in 10:24; 11:14.
- **"I will pray the Father for you" → "I will request the Father concerning you"** (v26) — Greek ἐρωτήσω … περὶ ὑμῶν; "you will ask" is αἰτήσεσθε.
- **"the Father himself loveth you, because ye have loved me" → "the Father himself has affection for you, because you have had affection for me"** (v27) — Greek φιλεῖ … πεφιλήκατε, the verb φιλέω (15:19), not ἀγαπάω "love"; πεφιλήκατε and πεπιστεύκατε are perfects.
- **"I came out from God" kept** (v27) — παρὰ τοῦ θεοῦ (NA28, RP; 2–2, NA28 decides); WH and Treg read παρὰ τοῦ πατρός, "from the Father". Three prepositions stand with "came out": παρά "from beside" (v27), ἐκ "out of" (v28: WH, Treg, SBLGNT; NA28 and RP παρά) and ἀπό "from" (v30); English "from" covers all three.
- **"I came forth from the Father, and am come into the world: again, I leave the world" kept** (v28) — Greek πάλιν "again"; "now" is not in the Greek; ἀφίημι "leave".
- **"His disciples said unto him, Lo, now speakest thou plainly, and speakest no proverb" → "His disciples say, 'See, now you speak plainly and say no figure of speech'"** (v29) — Greek Λέγουσιν, a present, without "to him" (RP adds αὐτῷ); Ἴδε; λαλεῖς … λέγεις, two verbs.
- **"needest not that any man should ask thee" → "have no need that anyone should question you"** (v30) — Greek οὐ χρείαν ἔχεις ἵνα τίς σε ἐρωτᾷ. ἐν τούτῳ "by this".
- **"Do ye now believe?" kept** (v31) — Greek Ἄρτι πιστεύετε; it can also be a statement, "Now you believe."
- **"the hour cometh, yea, is now come, that ye shall be scattered, every man to his own" → "an hour is coming, and has come, that you should be scattered, each to his own"** (v32) — Greek ὥρα without the article; καὶ ἐλήλυθεν (WH, Treg, NA28; RP νῦν "now"); ἵνα; εἰς τὰ ἴδια, "to the things that are his own" (as in 1:11; 19:27); "home" is not in the Greek. καὶ οὐκ εἰμὶ μόνος, "and I am not alone".
- **"In the world ye shall have tribulation: but be of good cheer" → "In the world you have affliction; but be of good courage"** (v33) — Greek ἔχετε, a present; θλῖψις; θαρσεῖτε.
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** modernized throughout.
