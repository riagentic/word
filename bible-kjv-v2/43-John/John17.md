# John 17

[1] These things ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ spoke, and lifting up his eyes to heaven he said, "Father, the hour has come; glorify your Son, so that the Son may glorify you,

[2] just as you gave him authority over all flesh, so that — all that you have given him — he may give to them eternal life.

[3] **And this is the eternal life, that they may know you, the only true ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and the one whom you sent, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.**

[4] I glorified you on the earth, having perfected the work which you have given me to do;

[5] and now glorify me, you, Father, with yourself, with the glory which I had with you before the world was.

[6] "I manifested your name to the people whom you gave me out of the world. Yours they were, and to me you gave them, and they have kept your word.

[7] Now they have come to know that all things whatsoever you have given me are from you;

[8] because the words which you gave me I have given to them, and they received them and came to know truly that I came out from you, and they believed that you sent me.

[9] I request concerning them; not concerning the world do I request, but concerning those whom you have given me, because they are yours,

[10] and all things that are mine are yours, and yours are mine, and I have been glorified in them.

[11] And I am no longer in the world, and they are in the world, and I am coming to you. Holy Father, keep them in your name, which you have given me, **that they may be one, just as we are**.

[12] When I was with them, I was keeping them in your name, which you have given me, and I guarded them, and none of them perished except the son of destruction, so that the Scripture might be fulfilled.

[13] "But now I am coming to you, and these things I speak in the world, so that they may have my joy made full in themselves.

[14] I have given them your word, and the world hated them, because they are not of the world, just as I am not of the world.

[15] I do not request that you take them away out of the world, but that you keep them from the evil one.

[16] They are not of the world, just as I am not of the world.

[17] Sanctify them in the truth; your word is truth.

[18] Just as you sent me into the world, I also sent them into the world;

[19] and on their behalf I sanctify myself, so that they also may be sanctified in truth.

[20] "And not concerning these only do I request, but also concerning those who believe in me through their word,

[21] **that they all may be one, just as you, Father, are in me and I in you, that they also may be in us, that the world may believe that you sent me.**

[22] And I, the glory which you have given me I have given to them, that they may be one just as we are one,

[23] I in them and you in me, that they may be perfected into one, that the world may know that you sent me and loved them just as you loved me.

[24] Father, that which you have given me — I want that where I am, those also may be with me, that they may behold my glory which you have given me, because you loved me before the foundation of the world.

[25] Righteous Father, the world did not know you, but I knew you, and these knew that you sent me;

[26] and I made known to them your name, and will make it known, so that the love with which you loved me may be in them, and I in them."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v1, 3) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3) — Hebrew divine name preserved; elsewhere "Father" is used in this prayer chapter.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v3) — Hebrew messianic title.
- **"These words spake Jesus, and lifted up his eyes" → "These things *Yeshua* spoke, and lifting up his eyes"** (v1) — Greek Ταῦτα ἐλάλησεν … καὶ ἐπάρας … εἶπεν (WH, Treg, NA28; KJV follows RP ἐπῆρεν … καὶ εἶπεν). "that the Son" (WH, Treg, NA28; RP "that your Son also").
- **"As thou hast given him power over all flesh, that he should give eternal life to as many as thou hast given him" → "just as you gave him authority over all flesh, so that — all that you have given him — he may give to them eternal life"** (v2) — Greek ἔδωκας, an aorist, then δέδωκας, a perfect; ἐξουσίαν πάσης σαρκός, literally "authority of all flesh"; πᾶν ὃ δέδωκας αὐτῷ, "all that you have given him", is neuter singular and stands first, outside the clause, and is taken up by the plural αὐτοῖς "to them". The dashes keep that broken construction. δώσῃ (Treg, NA28; WH and RP δώσει).
- **"this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent" → "this is the eternal life, that they may know you, the only true *Elohim*, and the one whom you sent, *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v3) — Greek ἡ αἰώνιος ζωή, with the article; γινώσκωσι, a present of γινώσκω; καὶ ὃν ἀπέστειλας Ἰησοῦν Χριστόν, in the Greek order; ἀπέστειλας, an aorist (ἀποστέλλω, the verb "send" of this chapter; 13:16, 20 and 14–16 have πέμπω).
- **"I have glorified thee on the earth: I have finished the work which thou gavest me" → "I glorified you on the earth, having perfected the work which you have given me"** (v4) — Greek ἐδόξασα, an aorist; τελειώσας, a participle (WH, Treg, NA28; RP ἐτελείωσα) of τελειόω "perfect": bring to its end, complete; the verb returns in v23. δέδωκας is a perfect.
- **"glorify thou me with thine own self with the glory which I had with thee" → "glorify me, you, Father, with yourself, with the glory which I had with you"** (v5) — Greek σύ, an emphatic "you"; παρὰ σεαυτῷ … παρὰ σοί, "beside yourself … beside you"; "presence" is not in the Greek.
- **"I have manifested thy name unto the men" → "I manifested your name to the people"** (v6) — Greek Ἐφανέρωσα, an aorist of φανερόω "manifest"; τοῖς ἀνθρώποις, human beings. "you gave" twice is ἔδωκας, an aorist (WH, Treg, NA28; RP δέδωκας).
- **"Now they have known" → "Now they have come to know"** (v7) — Greek ἔγνωκαν, a perfect of γινώσκω; δέδωκας "you have given" (Treg, NA28, RP; WH ἔδωκας); εἰσιν "are" (WH, Treg, NA28; RP ἐστιν).
- **"they have received them, and have known surely … and they have believed" → "they received them and came to know truly … and they believed"** (v8) — Greek ἔλαβον, ἔγνωσαν, ἐπίστευσαν, three aorists; "them" after "received" is supplied; ἀληθῶς "truly". "words" is ῥήματα (v6, 14, 17 λόγος "word"). "that you sent me": σύ, an emphatic "you", here and in v21, 23, 25.
- **"I pray for them: I pray not for the world" → "I request concerning them; not concerning the world do I request"** (v9, 15, 20) — Greek ἐρωτῶ περί, as in 14:16; 16:26; the verb is not "pray" (προσεύχομαι).
- **"I am glorified in them" → "I have been glorified in them"** (v10) — Greek δεδόξασμαι, a perfect. τὰ ἐμὰ πάντα is neuter: "all things that are mine".
- **"but these are in the world" → "and they are in the world"** (v11) — Greek καὶ αὐτοί (WH, NA28; Treg and RP οὗτοι "these"); καί "and" three times.
- **"keep through thine own name those whom thou hast given me, that they may be one, as we are" → "keep them in your name, which you have given me, that they may be one, just as we are"** (v11, 12) — Greek ἐν τῷ ὀνόματί σου ᾧ δέδωκάς μοι: the relative ᾧ is singular and refers to the name (WH, Treg, NA28; in v12 RP reads οὕς, "those whom"). καθὼς ἡμεῖς, "just as we", without a second "one".
- **"While I was with them in the world, I kept them … those that thou gavest me I have kept" → "When I was with them, I was keeping them … and I guarded them"** (v12) — Greek ἐτήρουν, an imperfect of τηρέω "keep"; ἐφύλαξα, an aorist of φυλάσσω "guard"; "in the world" is not in the critical text (RP adds it).
- **"none of them is lost, but the son of perdition" → "none of them perished except the son of destruction"** (v12) — Greek ἀπώλετο … ἀπωλείας, the verb and its noun: "perished … the son of perishing".
- **"that they might have my joy fulfilled in themselves" → "so that they may have my joy made full in themselves"** (v13) — Greek πεπληρωμένην, as in 15:11; 16:24; ἐν ἑαυτοῖς (WH, Treg, NA28; RP αὐτοῖς).
- **"the world hath hated them" → "the world hated them"** (v14) — Greek ἐμίσησεν, an aorist.
- **"I pray not that thou shouldest take them out of the world, but that thou shouldest keep them from the evil" → "I do not request that you take them away out of the world, but that you keep them from the evil one"** (v15) — Greek ἄρῃς (αἴρω "take away") … ἐκ τοῦ κόσμου; τηρήσῃς … ἐκ τοῦ πονηροῦ, the same preposition ἐκ, "out of", twice. τοῦ πονηροῦ can be masculine, "the evil one" (as in 1 John 2:13–14; 5:18–19), or neuter, "evil".
- **"Sanctify them through thy truth" → "Sanctify them in the truth"** (v17) — Greek ἐν τῇ ἀληθείᾳ (WH, Treg, NA28; RP adds σου "your").
- **"As thou hast sent me into the world, even so have I also sent them" → "Just as you sent me into the world, I also sent them"** (v18) — Greek ἀπέστειλας … ἀπέστειλα, aorists.
- **"for their sakes I sanctify myself" → "on their behalf I sanctify myself"** (v19) — Greek ὑπὲρ αὐτῶν; ἁγιάζω "sanctify", the one verb of v17 and v19 ("set apart as holy, consecrate"); ἐν ἀληθείᾳ in v19 has no article.
- **"them also which shall believe on me through their word" → "those who believe in me through their word"** (v20) — Greek τῶν πιστευόντων, a present participle; "shall" is not in the Greek.
- **"that they also may be one in us: that the world may believe that thou hast sent me" → "that they also may be in us, that the world may believe that you sent me"** (v21) — the second "one" is not in the critical text (WH, Treg, NA28; RP adds ἕν); ἵνα "that" three times; πιστεύῃ, a present (RP πιστεύσῃ); ἀπέστειλας, an aorist.
- **"And the glory which thou gavest me I have given them" → "And I, the glory which you have given me I have given to them"** (v22) — Greek κἀγώ, "and I", set first; δέδωκας … δέδωκα, two perfects. "just as we are one": καθὼς ἡμεῖς ἕν, without "are" (RP adds ἐσμεν).
- **"that they may be made perfect in one; and that the world may know" → "that they may be perfected into one, that the world may know"** (v23) — Greek τετελειωμένοι εἰς ἕν, the verb of v4; γινώσκῃ, a present ("may come to know, may keep knowing"); "and" before the second "that" is not in the critical text.
- **"Father, I will that they also, whom thou hast given me, be with me where I am" → "Father, that which you have given me — I want that where I am, those also may be with me"** (v24) — Greek ὃ δέδωκάς μοι, neuter singular, "that which you have given me" (WH, Treg, NA28; RP οὕς "those whom"), set first as in v2 and taken up by κἀκεῖνοι "those also"; θέλω "I want".
- **"that they may behold my glory, which thou hast given me: for thou lovedst me" → "that they may behold my glory which you have given me, because you loved me"** (v24) — Greek θεωρῶσιν; ὅτι "because" (it can also be read with "given": "which you have given me because you loved me").
- **"O righteous Father, the world hath not known thee: but I have known thee, and these have known" → "Righteous Father, the world did not know you, but I knew you, and these knew"** (v25) — Greek ἔγνω, ἔγνων, ἔγνωσαν, three aorists of γινώσκω; "O" is not in the Greek. The Greek has καί before "the world" (καὶ ὁ κόσμος … ἐγὼ δέ … καὶ οὗτοι), which English cannot carry: "both the world did not know you — but I knew you — and these knew".
- **"I have declared unto them thy name, and will declare it" → "I made known to them your name, and will make it known"** (v26) — Greek ἐγνώρισα … γνωρίσω, as in 15:15; "continue" is not in the Greek; "it" is supplied.
- **"the love wherewith thou hast loved me" → "the love with which you loved me"** (v26) — Greek ἠγάπησας, an aorist, as in v23, 24.
- **"the world" used 18 times** — Greek κόσμος *kosmos*; retained throughout.
- **"Holy Father"** (v11) — retained; only occurrence of this address in the Gospels.
- **"son of perdition" → "son of destruction"** (v12) — Greek υἱὸς τῆς ἀπωλείας *huios tēs apōleias*, as in 2 Thess 2:3.
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** modernized throughout.
