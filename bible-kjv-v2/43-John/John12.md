# John 12

[1] So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, six days before the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧, came to ⟦bethany|Bethania|Bethany⟧, where ⟦lazarus|Elazar|Lazarus⟧ was, whom ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ raised from the dead.

[2] So they made him a supper there, and ⟦martha|Marta|Martha⟧ was serving, but ⟦lazarus|Elazar|Lazarus⟧ was one of those reclining with him;

[3] so ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧, taking a pound of ointment of genuine nard, of great price, anointed the feet of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ and wiped his feet with her hair; and the house was filled with the fragrance of the ointment.

[4] But ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧ Iscariot, one of his disciples, the one about to deliver him up, says,

[5] "Why was this ointment not sold for three hundred denarii and given to poor people?"

[6] Now he said this, not because he cared about the poor, but because he was a thief, and having the money box he was carrying the things put into it.

[7] So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said, "Leave her, that she may keep it for the day of my burial;

[8] for the poor you always have with yourselves, but me you do not always have."

[9] So the great crowd of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ came to know that he is there, and they came, not because of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ only, but that they might also see ⟦lazarus|Elazar|Lazarus⟧, whom he raised from the dead.

[10] But the high priests took counsel that they might kill ⟦lazarus|Elazar|Lazarus⟧ also,

[11] because on account of him many of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ were going and were believing in ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[12] On the next day the great crowd that had come to the feast, having heard that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ is coming to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,

[13] took the branches of the palm trees and went out to meet him, and were shouting, "**Hosanna! Blessed is he who comes in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧**, even the King of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧!"

[14] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, having found a young donkey, sat upon it, just as it is written,

[15] "**Do not fear, daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧;
behold, your king comes,
sitting upon a donkey's colt.**"

[16] These things his disciples did not know at the first, but when ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was glorified, then they remembered that these things were written of him and that they did these things to him.

[17] So the crowd that was with him when he called ⟦lazarus|Elazar|Lazarus⟧ out of the tomb and raised him from the dead was testifying.

[18] For this reason also the crowd met him, because they heard that he had done this sign.

[19] So the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ said to themselves, "You behold that you profit nothing; see, the world has gone away after him."

[20] Now there were some ⟦greeks|Yevanim|Greeks⟧ among those going up that they might bow down at the feast;

[21] so these approached ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧, the one from ⟦bethsaida|Beit Tzaidah|Bethsaida⟧ of ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧, and were requesting him, saying, "Sir, we want to see ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧."

[22] ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧ comes and tells ⟦andrew|Andreas|Andrew⟧; ⟦andrew|Andreas|Andrew⟧ comes, and ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧, and they tell ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[23] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answers them, saying, "The hour has come that the Son of Man may be glorified.

[24] Amen, amen, I say to you, **unless the grain of wheat, having fallen into the earth, dies, it remains alone; but if it dies, it bears much fruit.**

[25] The one who has affection for his soul loses it, and the one who hates his soul in this world will guard it unto eternal life.

[26] If anyone serves me, let him follow me, and where I am, there my servant also will be; if anyone serves me, the Father will honor him.

[27] "Now my soul has been troubled, and what shall I say? Father, save me out of this hour. But for this reason I came to this hour.

[28] Father, glorify your name." So a voice came out of heaven, "I both glorified it and will glorify it again."

[29] So the crowd that stood and heard was saying that it had thundered; others were saying, "An angel has spoken to him."

[30] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered and said, "Not on my account has this voice come, but on your account.

[31] Now is the judgment of this world; now the ruler of this world will be cast out;

[32] and I, if I am lifted up from the earth, will draw all to myself."

[33] Now this he was saying, signifying by what kind of death he was about to die.

[34] So the crowd answered him, "We heard out of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ that the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧ abides forever, and how do you say that the Son of Man must be lifted up? Who is this Son of Man?"

[35] So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "Yet a little time the light is among you. Walk as you have the light, that darkness may not lay hold of you; and the one who walks in the darkness does not know where he goes.

[36] As you have the light, believe in the light, that you may become sons of light."

These things ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ spoke, and going away he was hidden from them.

[37] But though he had done so many signs before them, they were not believing in him,

[38] that the word of ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ the prophet might be fulfilled, which he said:

"**Lord, who believed our report?
And the arm of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧, to whom was it revealed?**"

[39] For this reason they could not believe, because again ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ said,

[40] "**He has blinded their eyes
and hardened their heart,
that they might not see with the eyes
and perceive with the heart and turn,
and I will heal them.**"

[41] These things ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ said because he saw his glory, and he spoke concerning him.

[42] Nevertheless even of the rulers many believed in him, but because of the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ they were not confessing, that they might not be put out of the synagogue;

[43] for they loved the glory of men more than the glory of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[44] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ cried out and said, "The one who believes in me does not believe in me but in the one who sent me,

[45] and the one who beholds me beholds the one who sent me.

[46] I have come as light into the world, that everyone who believes in me may not abide in the darkness.

[47] And if anyone hears my words and does not guard them, I do not judge him; for I did not come that I might judge the world, but that I might save the world.

[48] The one who sets me aside and does not receive my words has the one who judges him: the word which I spoke, that will judge him in the last day;

[49] because I did not speak from myself, but the Father who sent me, he himself has given me a commandment, what I should say and what I should speak.

[50] And I know that his commandment is eternal life. So the things I speak, just as the Father has said to me, so I speak."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration. In v1 the name stands twice (WH NA28; RP has it once and adds "who had been dead" after "Lazarus"); in v9 it stands with "because of", not with "is there".
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v34) — Hebrew messianic title.
- **"Mary" → "*Miryam*"** (v3) — Greek Μαριάμ (WH Treg NA28).
- **"There they made him a supper; and Martha served" → "they made him a supper there, and *Marta* was serving"** (v2) — Greek δεῖπνον; διηκόνει, an imperfect, the verb of "servant" (v26). "Reclining with him": τῶν ἀνακειμένων σὺν αὐτῷ; "at table" is not in the Greek.
- **"spikenard, very costly" → "genuine nard, of great price"** (v3) — Greek νάρδου πιστικῆς πολυτίμου; πιστικός is probably "genuine, trustworthy" (from πίστις). "Pound": λίτρα, the Roman pound, about 327 g. "Fragrance": ὀσμή. "Ointment" (μύρον) stands twice.
- **"Then saith … Judas Iscariot, Simon's son, which should betray him" → "But *Yehudah* Iscariot … the one about to deliver him up, says"** (v4) — Greek λέγει, a present; παραδιδόναι, "deliver up, hand over". "Simon's son" is the RP reading.
- **"to the poor" → "to poor people"** (v5) — Greek πτωχοῖς without the article; with it in v6 and v8.
- **"had the bag, and bare what was put therein" → "having the money box he was carrying the things put into it"** (v6) — Greek γλωσσόκομον, a case or box; ἐβάσταζεν, "was carrying": either he carried it as keeper, or he carried off (took) what was put in.
- **"Let her alone: against the day of my burying hath she kept this" → "Leave her, that she may keep it for the day of my burial"** (v7) — Greek Ἄφες αὐτήν, ἵνα … τηρήσῃ αὐτό (WH Treg NA28; RP has no ἵνα and reads τετήρηκεν, "she has kept"). The words can also be taken together: "Let her keep it".
- **"Much people of the Jews therefore knew" → "So the great crowd of the *Yehudim* came to know"** (v9) — the verse follows ὁ ὄχλος πολύς with the article (WH NA28); Treg RP and the SBLGNT have none ("a great crowd").
- **"chief priests consulted that they might put Lazarus also to death" → "high priests took counsel that they might kill Lazarus also"** (v10) — Greek ἀρχιερεῖς; ἐβουλεύσαντο, as in 11:53.
- **"went away" → "were going"** (v11) — Greek ὑπῆγον, an imperfect of ὑπάγω, "go": they were going over, leaving their side.
- **"took branches of palm trees … and cried" → "took the branches of the palm trees … and were shouting"** (v13) — Greek τὰ βαΐα τῶν φοινίκων with the articles; ἐκραύγαζον (WH Treg NA28; RP ἔκραζον). "Even the King": καὶ ὁ βασιλεύς (WH Treg NA28; RP has no "even the").
- **"Fear not … cometh, sitting on an ass's colt" → "Do not fear … comes, sitting upon a donkey's colt"** (v15) — Greek as printed: Μὴ φοβοῦ, θυγάτηρ Σιών· ἰδοὺ ὁ βασιλεύς σου ἔρχεται, καθήμενος ἐπὶ πῶλον ὄνου. "Young donkey" in v14 is ὀνάριον.
- **"understood not" → "did not know"** (v16) — Greek οὐκ ἔγνωσαν. "Written of him": ἐπ' αὐτῷ γεγραμμένα, literally "written upon him".
- **"bare record" → "was testifying"** (v17) — Greek ἐμαρτύρει, an imperfect. "When" is ὅτε.
- **"Perceive ye how ye prevail nothing?" → "You behold that you profit nothing"** (v19) — Greek Θεωρεῖτε, a statement or a command ("Behold that …"); ὠφελεῖτε. "Has gone away": ἀπῆλθεν.
- **"to worship" → "that they might bow down"** (v20) — Greek προσκυνήσωσιν.
- **"Sir" kept** (v21) — Greek Κύριε, here the respectful address to Philip; the same word is "Lord" when *Yeshua* is addressed.
- **"cometh and telleth" → "comes and tells"; "answered" → "answers"** (v22, 23) — the Greek presents are kept (v23 ἀποκρίνεται: WH Treg NA28; RP a past).
- **"Verily, verily" → "Amen, amen"** (v24) — Greek ἀμὴν ἀμήν, left untranslated.
- **"Except a corn of wheat fall into the ground and die, it abideth alone" → "unless the grain of wheat, having fallen into the earth, dies, it remains alone"** (v24) — Greek ὁ κόκκος τοῦ σίτου with the article, πεσών a participle; μένει, the verb μένω ("abide" of persons, v34, 46), here of a thing: "remains".
- **"He that loveth his life shall lose it … shall keep it" → "The one who has affection for his soul loses it … will guard it"** (v25) — Greek φιλῶν (φιλέω, not ἀγαπάω); ψυχή, "soul", the person's own life, set beside ζωὴ αἰώνιος, "eternal life"; ἀπολλύει, a present (WH Treg NA28; RP ἀπολέσει, "will lose"); φυλάξει.
- **"Father, save me from this hour:" → "Father, save me out of this hour."** (v27) — Greek σῶσόν με ἐκ τῆς ὥρας ταύτης, printed as a prayer; it can also be read as a question that continues "what shall I say?" ("'Father, save me out of this hour'?"). "Has been troubled": τετάρακται, a perfect.
- **"I have both glorified it, and will glorify it again" → "I both glorified it and will glorify it again"** (v28) — Greek Καὶ ἐδόξασα καὶ πάλιν δοξάσω, an aorist and a future; "it" is not in the Greek and is supplied twice.
- **"This voice came not because of me" → "Not on my account has this voice come"** (v30) — Greek Οὐ δι' ἐμὲ … γέγονεν, in the Greek order.
- **"prince of this world" → "ruler of this world"** (v31) — Greek ἄρχων. "Cast out": ἐκβληθήσεται ἔξω.
- **"will draw all men unto me" → "will draw all to myself"** (v32) — Greek πάντας, "all" (masculine: all people); the Greek has no noun. "If": ἐάν. "Lifted up": ὑψωθῶ, also "exalted".
- **"signifying what death he should die" → "signifying by what kind of death he was about to die"** (v33) — Greek σημαίνων ποίῳ θανάτῳ ἤμελλεν ἀποθνῄσκειν.
- **"out of the law" → "out of the *Torah*"; "abideth for ever" kept** (v34) — Greek ἐκ τοῦ νόμου; μένει εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα, "abides unto the age".
- **"Yet a little while is the light with you. Walk while ye have the light, lest darkness come upon you" → "Yet a little time the light is among you. Walk as you have the light, that darkness may not lay hold of you"** (v35–36) — Greek ἐν ὑμῖν, "in / among you" (WH Treg NA28; RP μεθ' ὑμῶν, "with you"); ὡς, "as, while" (RP ἕως, "until, while"); καταλάβῃ, the verb of 1:5. "Darkness" is σκοτία.
- **"did hide himself" → "was hidden"** (v36) — Greek ἐκρύβη, passive in form: "was hidden" or "hid himself".
- **"they believed not on him" → "they were not believing in him"** (v37) — Greek οὐκ ἐπίστευον, an imperfect.
- **"who hath believed our report? and to whom hath the arm of the Lord been revealed?" → "who believed our report? And the arm of *Yahweh*, to whom was it revealed?"** (v38) — Greek as printed: τίς ἐπίστευσεν τῇ ἀκοῇ ἡμῶν; καὶ ὁ βραχίων κυρίου τίνι ἀπεκαλύφθη; aorists, in the Greek order. ἀκοή is "hearing", what is heard: the message. κυρίου without the article stands for the divine name.
- **"Therefore they could not believe, because that Esaias said again" → "For this reason they could not believe, because again *Yeshayahu* said"** (v39) — Greek διὰ τοῦτο … ὅτι.
- **"He hath blinded their eyes, and hardened their heart; that they should not see with their eyes, nor understand with their heart, and be converted, and I should heal them" → "He has blinded their eyes and hardened their heart, that they might not see with the eyes and perceive with the heart and turn, and I will heal them"** (v40) — Greek as printed: Τετύφλωκεν (a perfect) … ἐπώρωσεν (an aorist; WH Treg NA28) … ἵνα μὴ ἴδωσιν τοῖς ὀφθαλμοῖς καὶ νοήσωσιν τῇ καρδίᾳ καὶ στραφῶσιν, καὶ ἰάσομαι αὐτούς. The last verb is a future indicative, "and I will heal them" (WH Treg NA28; RP ἰάσωμαι, a subjunctive that continues "that … not"). ἐπώρωσεν is the "hardening" of Mark 8:17.
- **"when he saw his glory" → "because he saw his glory"** (v41) — Greek ὅτι (WH Treg NA28); RP reads ὅτε, "when".
- **"among the chief rulers also many believed … lest they should be put out of the synagogue" → "even of the rulers many believed … that they might not be put out of the synagogue"** (v42) — Greek ἐκ τῶν ἀρχόντων; οὐχ ὡμολόγουν, an imperfect without an object; ἀποσυνάγωγοι γένωνται, as in 9:22.
- **"the praise of men more than the praise of God" → "the glory of men more than the glory of *Elohim*"** (v43) — Greek τὴν δόξαν τῶν ἀνθρώπων … τὴν δόξαν τοῦ θεοῦ, plain genitives: the glory that men give, or that men have; so also of God. δόξα is the "glory" of v41.
- **"seeth" → "beholds"** (v45) — Greek θεωρῶν, θεωρεῖ.
- **"I am come a light into the world … should not abide in darkness" → "I have come as light into the world … may not abide in the darkness"** (v46) — Greek φῶς without the article; ἐν τῇ σκοτίᾳ μὴ μείνῃ.
- **"hear my words, and believe not" → "hears my words and does not guard them"** (v47) — Greek μὴ φυλάξῃ (WH Treg NA28; RP πιστεύσῃ, "believe"). "Words" in v47–48 is ῥήματα; "the word" in v48 is ὁ λόγος.
- **"He that rejecteth me … hath one that judgeth him … the same shall judge him" → "The one who sets me aside … has the one who judges him … that will judge him"** (v48) — Greek ὁ ἀθετῶν; τὸν κρίνοντα αὐτόν; ἐκεῖνος, here of the word.
- **"of myself" → "from myself"; "gave me a commandment" → "has given me a commandment"** (v49) — Greek ἐξ ἐμαυτοῦ; δέδωκεν, a perfect (WH Treg NA28; RP ἔδωκεν). "Say" and "speak" are two verbs (εἴπω, λαλήσω).
- **"life everlasting" → "eternal life"** (v50) — Greek ζωὴ αἰώνιος.
- **v13** — OT quotation: Ps 118:25-26 ("Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord").
- **v15** — OT quotation: Zech 9:9 + Zeph 3:16 ("Fear not, daughter of Zion…").
- **v38** — OT quotation: Isa 53:1 ("Lord, who believed…"); "Lord" at the beginning is in the Greek of the quotation.
- **v40** — OT quotation: Isa 6:10 ("He has blinded their eyes…").
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** modernized throughout.

### Notes
- **v13** — "Hosanna": Greek Ὡσαννά, the Hebrew *Hoshia-na*, "save now" (Ps 118:25).
- **v15** echoes Zech 9:9 + Zeph 3:16.
- **v38** quotes Isa 53:1.
- **v40** quotes Isa 6:10.
