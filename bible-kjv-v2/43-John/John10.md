# John 10

[1] "Amen, amen, I say to you, the one who does not enter through the door into the fold of the sheep but goes up from elsewhere, that one is a thief and a robber;

[2] but the one who enters through the door is shepherd of the sheep.

[3] To this one the doorkeeper opens, and the sheep hear his voice, and he calls his own sheep by name and leads them out.

[4] When he has put forth all his own, he goes before them, and the sheep follow him, because they know his voice;

[5] but a stranger they will by no means follow, but will flee from him, because they do not know the voice of strangers."

[6] This figure of speech ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ spoke to them, but those did not know what the things were which he was speaking to them.

[7] So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said again, "Amen, amen, I say to you, **I am the door of the sheep.**

[8] All who came before me are thieves and robbers, but the sheep did not hear them.

[9] I am the door; through me if anyone enters, he will be saved, and will go in and go out and find pasture.

[10] The thief does not come except that he may steal and slaughter and destroy; I came that they may have life and have abundance.

[11] "**I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his soul for the sheep.**

[12] The one who is a hired man and not a shepherd, whose own the sheep are not, beholds the wolf coming and leaves the sheep and flees — and the wolf snatches them and scatters —

[13] because he is a hired man and does not care about the sheep.

[14] I am the good shepherd, and I know my own and my own know me,

[15] just as the Father knows me and I know the Father; and I lay down my soul for the sheep.

[16] And other sheep I have, which are not of this fold; those also I must bring, and they will hear my voice, and they will become one flock, one shepherd.

[17] For this reason the Father loves me, because I lay down my soul, that I may take it again.

[18] No one takes it away from me, but I lay it down of myself. I have authority to lay it down, and I have authority to take it again; this commandment I received from my Father."

[19] A schism came again among the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ because of these words.

[20] And many of them were saying, "He has a demon and is mad; why do you hear him?"

[21] Others were saying, "These are not the words of one demonized; a demon cannot open the eyes of the blind, can it?"

[22] Then ⟦hanukkah|Chanukkah|the Feast of Dedication⟧ took place in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; it was winter,

[23] and ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was walking in the temple, in the colonnade of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧.

[24] So the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ encircled him and were saying to him, "How long do you keep our soul in suspense? If you are the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧, tell us plainly."

[25] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered them, "I told you, and you do not believe; the works that I do in the name of my Father, these testify concerning me;

[26] but you do not believe, because you are not of my sheep.

[27] My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me,

[28] and I give them eternal life, and they will by no means perish forever, and no one will snatch them out of my hand.

[29] What my Father has given me is greater than all, and no one can snatch out of the hand of the Father.

[30] **I and the Father are one.**"

[31] The ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ again took up stones that they might stone him.

[32] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered them, "Many good works I showed you from the Father; for which work of them do you stone me?"

[33] The ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ answered him, "For a good work we do not stone you, but for blasphemy, and because you, being a man, make yourself ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[34] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered them, "Is it not written in your ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧, '**I said, You are gods**'?

[35] If he called those gods, to whom the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ came — and the Scripture cannot be broken —

[36] of him whom the Father sanctified and sent into the world do you say, 'You blaspheme,' because I said, 'I am Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧'?

[37] If I do not do the works of my Father, do not believe me;

[38] but if I do, even if you do not believe me, believe the works, that you may come to know and go on knowing that the Father is in me and I in the Father."

[39] So they were seeking again to seize him; and he went out of their hand.

[40] And he went away again beyond the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ to the place where ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ was at the first ⟦immerse|immersing|baptizing⟧, and he remained there.

[41] And many came to him and were saying, "⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ indeed did no sign, but all things whatever ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ said concerning this one were true."

[42] And many believed in him there.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v24) — Hebrew messianic title.
- **"baptizing" → "immersing"** (v40) — Greek *baptizo* = "to immerse."
- **"Verily, verily" → "Amen, amen"** (v1, 7) — Greek Ἀμὴν ἀμήν, left untranslated.
- **"He that entereth not by the door into the sheepfold, but climbeth up some other way, the same" → "the one who does not enter through the door into the fold of the sheep but goes up from elsewhere, that one"** (v1) — Greek διὰ τῆς θύρας, εἰς τὴν αὐλὴν τῶν προβάτων (αὐλή, an enclosed court), ἀναβαίνων ἀλλαχόθεν, ἐκεῖνος. "Robber": λῃστής, one who takes by force.
- **"the shepherd of the sheep" → "shepherd of the sheep"** (v2) — Greek ποιμήν without the article: "the shepherd" or "a shepherd".
- **"porter" → "doorkeeper"** (v3) — Greek θυρωρός, from θύρα, "door".
- **"when he putteth forth his own sheep" → "When he has put forth all his own"** (v4) — Greek τὰ ἴδια πάντα (WH Treg NA28; RP "his own sheep"). "Put forth" is ἐκβάλῃ, the verb "cast out" of 9:34–35; of a shepherd it is to drive the sheep out.
- **"will they not follow" → "they will by no means follow"** (v5) — Greek οὐ μή, the strong denial.
- **"parable" → "figure of speech"** (v6) — Greek παροιμία, a veiled or figurative saying; not παραβολή, "parable". "Those": ἐκεῖνοι. "Did not know what the things were": οὐκ ἔγνωσαν τίνα ἦν.
- **"said Jesus unto them again" → "*Yeshua* said again"** (v7) — the verse follows the reading without αὐτοῖς, "to them" (WH NA28); Treg RP and the SBLGNT have it.
- **"I am the door" → "I am the door of the sheep"** (v7) — the full phrase, as in KJV; Greek θύρα is a door, the word of v1–2.
- **"did not hear them" kept** (v8) — Greek ἀκούω, "hear, listen to": one verb in v3, 8, 16, 20, 27. "Before me" (πρὸ ἐμοῦ) is not in RP.
- **"by me if any man enter in" → "through me if anyone enters"** (v9) — Greek δι' ἐμοῦ, set first.
- **"to steal, and to kill, and to destroy … have it more abundantly" → "that he may steal and slaughter and destroy … have abundance"** (v10) — Greek θύσῃ, to slaughter (also to sacrifice); περισσὸν ἔχωσιν, "have what is over and above"; the Greek has no "it".
- **"giveth his life" → "lays down his soul"** (v11, 15, 17) — Greek τὴν ψυχὴν αὐτοῦ τίθησιν; ψυχή is "soul" in this translation, here the person's own life; ζωή, "life", stands in v10 and v28. "Good" is καλός (not ἀγαθός) in v11, 14 and of the works in v32–33.
- **"hireling" → "hired man"** (v12, 13) — Greek μισθωτός, one who works for a wage.
- **"seeth" → "beholds"; "catcheth them, and scattereth the sheep. The hireling fleeth" → "snatches them and scatters —"** (v12–13) — Greek θεωρεῖ, ἁρπάζει (as in v28–29). WH Treg NA28 have no "the sheep" after "scatters" and no "the hired man flees" in v13 (RP has both): v13 gives the reason for "flees", and the clause about the wolf stands between.
- **"am known of mine" → "my own know me"** (v14) — Greek γινώσκουσί με τὰ ἐμά (WH Treg NA28; RP "I am known by my own").
- **"there shall be one fold, and one shepherd" → "they will become one flock, one shepherd"** (v16) — Greek γενήσονται, "they will become" (WH Treg NA28; RP γενήσεται, "there will be"); ποίμνη is "flock", not "fold" (αὐλή).
- **"that I might take it again" kept; "No man taketh it from me" → "No one takes it away from me"** (v17–18) — Greek λάβω, λαβεῖν: λαμβάνω is "take" or "receive", and it is the verb of "I received" in the last clause of v18. "Takes away" is another verb, αἴρει (Treg NA28 RP; WH ἦρεν, "took away"). "Of myself": ἀπ' ἐμαυτοῦ, "from myself".
- **"power" → "authority"; "commandment have I received" → "commandment I received"** (v18) — Greek ἐξουσίαν; ἐντολήν, ἔλαβον (an aorist).
- **"division" → "schism"** (v19) — Greek σχίσμα, as in 9:16.
- **"He hath a devil" → "He has a demon"; "of him that hath a devil" → "of one demonized"** (v20–21) — Greek δαιμόνιον; δαιμονιζομένου. "Words" in v21 is ῥήματα, in v19 λόγους. The question with μή expects "no".
- **"the feast of the dedication" → "*Chanukkah*"** (v22) — Greek τὰ ἐγκαίνια, "the Dedication", literally "the renewal"; the Greek has no word "feast". "Then": τότε (WH NA28; Treg RP δέ, "and").
- **"Solomon's porch" → "the colonnade of *Shlomo*"** (v23) — Greek στοά, a roofed row of columns. "Temple": ἱερόν, the whole precinct.
- **"How long dost thou make us to doubt?" → "How long do you keep our soul in suspense?"** (v24) — Greek Ἕως πότε τὴν ψυχὴν ἡμῶν αἴρεις; literally "Until when do you lift up (or: take away) our soul?" — the verb of v18.
- **"bear witness of me" → "testify concerning me"** (v25) — Greek μαρτυρεῖ περὶ ἐμοῦ.
- **"as I said unto you" dropped** (v26) — WH Treg NA28 end the verse with "of my sheep"; RP adds καθὼς εἶπον ὑμῖν.
- **"they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them" → "they will by no means perish forever, and no one will snatch them"** (v28) — Greek οὐ μὴ ἀπόλωνται εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα, the strong denial with "unto the age": never. "Snatch": ἁρπάσει, the verb of v12.
- **"My Father, which gave them me, is greater than all" → "What my Father has given me is greater than all"** (v29) — the verse follows ὃ δέδωκέν μοι πάντων μεῖζόν ἐστιν, a neuter (WH Treg NA28); RP reads ὃς … μείζων πάντων, "My Father, who has given to me, is greater than all", and the SBLGNT ὃ … μείζων. "Snatch" has no object in the Greek. "The Father": WH NA28; Treg RP "my Father".
- **"Then the Jews took up stones again" → "The *Yehudim* again took up stones"** (v31) — the verse follows the reading without οὖν (WH NA28); Treg RP and the SBLGNT have it. Greek ἐβάστασαν, "lifted, carried".
- **"makest thyself God"** (v33) — Greek θεόν without the article: "*Elohim*", or "a god", as the answer in v34–35 takes it up.
- **"your law" → "your *Torah*"** (v34) — Greek νόμος, here of the Scriptures as a whole: the words are from a psalm.
- **"unto whom the word of God came" / "broken" kept** (v35) — Greek ἐγένετο, "came to be"; λυθῆναι, "be loosed, undone".
- **"I am the Son of God" → "I am Son of *Elohim*"** (v36) — Greek Υἱὸς τοῦ θεοῦ εἰμι, without the article before "Son": "the Son" or "a son".
- **"that ye may know, and believe" → "that you may come to know and go on knowing"** (v38) — Greek ἵνα γνῶτε καὶ γινώσκητε, one verb twice, first as an event, then as lasting (WH Treg NA28; RP πιστεύσητε, "believe"). "In the Father": WH Treg NA28; RP "in him".
- **"they sought again to take him: but he escaped out of their hand" → "they were seeking again to seize him; and he went out of their hand"** (v39) — Greek ἐζήτουν, πιάσαι, ἐξῆλθεν; "hand" is singular.
- **"there he abode" → "he remained there"** (v40) — Greek ἔμεινεν, the verb μένω ("abide" of persons), here of a stay in a place: "remain".
- **"v34"** — OT quotation: Ps 82:6 ("I said, You are gods"); *Yeshua* argues from a Psalm where humans receive the divine address.
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** modernized throughout.

### Notes
- **v22** — *Chanukkah* commemorates the Maccabean rededication of the temple (165 BC).
- **v34** quotes Ps 82:6.
