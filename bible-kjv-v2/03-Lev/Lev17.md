# Leviticus 17

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and to his sons and to all the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and say to them: 'This is the word that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded, saying:

[3] Any man of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ who slaughters an ox or a lamb or a goat in the camp, or who slaughters outside the camp,

[4] and to the entrance of the tent of meeting has not brought it, to bring near an offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ before the dwelling of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ — blood shall be reckoned to that man; he has shed blood; and that man shall be cut off from the midst of his people;

[5] so that the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ may bring their sacrifices that they are sacrificing on the face of the field, and they shall bring them to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to the entrance of the tent of meeting, to the priest, and they shall sacrifice them as sacrifices of peace offerings to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] And the priest shall dash the blood on the altar of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and he shall turn the fat into smoke for a soothing smell to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] And they shall no more sacrifice their sacrifices to the male goats after whom they play the prostitute. An everlasting statute this shall be to them, for their generations.'

[8] And to them you shall say: 'Any man of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and of the sojourner who sojourns in their midst, who offers up a burnt offering or a sacrifice,

[9] and to the entrance of the tent of meeting does not bring it, to offer it to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ — that man shall be cut off from his peoples.

[10] And any man of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and of the sojourner who sojourns in their midst, who eats any blood — I will set my face against the soul that eats the blood, and I will cut it off from the midst of its people.

[11] For the soul of the flesh is in the blood, and I, I have given it to you on the altar to make atonement for your souls; for the blood, it makes atonement by the soul.

[12] Therefore I have said to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: No soul of you shall eat blood, and the sojourner who sojourns in your midst shall not eat blood.

[13] And any man of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and of the sojourner who sojourns in their midst, who hunts game, a living thing or a bird that may be eaten — he shall pour out its blood and cover it with dust.

[14] For as for the soul of all flesh, its blood is in its soul; and I said to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: The blood of all flesh you shall not eat, for the soul of all flesh is its blood; everyone who eats it shall be cut off.

[15] And every soul that eats a carcass or a torn animal, among the native or among the sojourner — he shall wash his garments and wash in water and be unclean until the evening; and he shall be clean.

[16] And if he does not wash them, and his flesh he does not wash, then he shall bear his iniquity.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v2, 5, 12, 13, 14) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל. **"This is the thing" → "This is the word"** (v2) — Hebrew הַדָּבָר, of a spoken command, as Exod 16:16.
- **"What man soever there be" → "Any man"** (v3, 8, 10, 13) — Hebrew אִישׁ אִישׁ, literally "a man, a man".
- **"killeth" → "slaughters"** (v3) — Hebrew שָׁחַט, the verb for cutting the throat of an animal. **"ox, or lamb, or goat"** — Hebrew שׁוֹר, כֶּשֶׂב, עֵז: one head of cattle, a sheep, a goat.
- **"to offer an offering" → "to bring near an offering"** (v4) — Hebrew לְהַקְרִיב קָרְבָּן, verb and noun of one root: "to bring near what is brought near". The Samaritan text and the Septuagint (from memory) have a longer verse here; the Masoretic Text is translated. **"tabernacle of the congregation" → "tent of meeting"** (v4, 5, 6, 9) — Hebrew אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד. **"the tabernacle of the LORD" → "the dwelling of Yahweh"** — Hebrew מִשְׁכָּן.
- **"blood shall be imputed" → "blood shall be reckoned"** (v4) — Hebrew יֵחָשֵׁב, the "reckon" of Gen 15:6. **"from among his people" → "from the midst of his people"** (v4, 10) — Hebrew מִקֶּרֶב עַמּוֹ, singular; in v9 it is מֵעַמָּיו, plural, "from his peoples" (his kinsfolk).
- **"To the end that" → "so that"** (v5) — Hebrew לְמַעַן אֲשֶׁר; the verse goes on from v4. **"which they offer in the open field" → "that they are sacrificing on the face of the field"** — Hebrew זֹבְחִים עַל־פְּנֵי הַשָּׂדֶה; "open" is not in the Hebrew. **"offer them for peace offerings" → "sacrifice them as sacrifices of peace offerings"** — Hebrew וְזָבְחוּ זִבְחֵי שְׁלָמִים, the root זבח three times in the verse.
- **"sprinkle the blood" → "dash the blood"** (v6) — Hebrew זָרַק, to throw from a bowl; "sprinkle" is another verb (הִזָּה). **"burn the fat" → "turn the fat into smoke"** — Hebrew הִקְטִיר; "burn" is another verb (שָׂרַף). **"a sweet savour" → "a soothing smell"** — Hebrew רֵיחַ נִיחֹחַ.
- **"unto devils" → "to the male goats"** (v7) — Hebrew לַשְּׂעִירִם: the word is שָׂעִיר, "hairy one, male goat", the same word as the goats of ch 16. Here it names beings to whom sacrifice was brought; they are commonly understood as demons in goat form ("goat-demons", "satyrs"; cf. 2 Chr 11:15; Isa 13:21; 34:14). The Hebrew word itself says only "male goats", and the verse keeps it. **"after whom they have gone a whoring" → "after whom they play the prostitute"** — Hebrew זֹנִים, a participle; the verb of Exod 34:15, used of unfaithfulness to Yahweh. **"a statute for ever" → "an everlasting statute"** — Hebrew חֻקַּת עוֹלָם, literally "a statute of the age".
- **"the strangers which sojourn among you" → "the sojourner who sojourns in their midst"** (v8, 10, 13) — Hebrew הַגֵּר אֲשֶׁר־יָגוּר בְּתוֹכָם, singular, and "their", not "your". **"offereth" → "offers up"** (v8) — Hebrew יַעֲלֶה עֹלָה, literally "makes go up a going-up". **"to offer it" kept** (v9) — Hebrew לַעֲשׂוֹת, literally "to do / make it".
- **"I will even set my face against that soul … and will cut him off" → "I will set my face against the soul … and I will cut it off"** (v10) — Hebrew וְנָתַתִּי פָנַי בַּנֶּפֶשׁ, literally "I will give my face against the soul"; נֶפֶשׁ is "soul" in every place in this chapter (v10, 11, 12, 14, 15).
- **"the life of the flesh is in the blood" → "the soul of the flesh is in the blood"** (v11) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ הַבָּשָׂר בַּדָּם הִוא: the same word נֶפֶשׁ as "your souls" in the same verse. **"it is the blood that maketh an atonement for the soul" → "the blood, it makes atonement by the soul"** — Hebrew כִּי־הַדָּם הוּא בַּנֶּפֶשׁ יְכַפֵּר: בְּ is here most often taken as "by means of" (the blood atones by the soul that is in it); it may also be "for, at the price of" ("for the soul", so KJV) or "as" ("as the soul"). כִּפֶּר, "make atonement", is at its root "cover".
- **"No soul of you shall eat blood"** (v12) — Hebrew כָּל־נֶפֶשׁ מִכֶּם לֹא־תֹאכַל.
- **"which hunteth and catcheth any beast or fowl" → "who hunts game, a living thing or a bird"** (v13) — Hebrew יָצוּד צֵיד חַיָּה אוֹ־עוֹף, literally "hunts a hunting of living thing or bird"; "catches" is not in the Hebrew. חַיָּה is "living thing", not בְּהֵמָה "beast".
- **"For it is the life of all flesh; the blood of it is for the life thereof" → "For as for the soul of all flesh, its blood is in its soul"** (v14) — Hebrew כִּי־נֶפֶשׁ כָּל־בָּשָׂר דָּמוֹ בְנַפְשׁוֹ הוּא, literally "for the soul of all flesh: its blood, in its soul it is". The clause is hard; it is also read "its blood is its soul" (so the second half of the verse, and, from memory, the Septuagint) or "its blood is with its soul". "As for" is supplied. **"therefore I said" → "and I said"** — Hebrew וָאֹמַר.
- **"that which died of itself, or that which was torn with beasts" → "a carcass or a torn animal"** (v15) — Hebrew נְבֵלָה וּטְרֵפָה, literally "a carcass and a torn thing". **"whether it be one of your own country, or a stranger" → "among the native or among the sojourner"** — Hebrew בָּאֶזְרָח וּבַגֵּר. **"bathe himself" → "wash"** (v15, 16) — Hebrew רָחַץ; the washing of garments is another verb (כִּבֵּס), and English has "wash" for both. In v16 "them" is supplied after the first verb, which is used only of clothes.
- **"his flesh"** (v16) — Hebrew בְּשָׂרוֹ.

### Notes

- **Opening of the Holiness Code** — Lev 17–26 forms a distinct literary unit known as the Holiness Code (H), characterized by the refrain "you shall be holy, for I am holy" and addresses to the whole people (not only priests).
- **Slaughter at the tent of meeting** (v3–9) — every ox, lamb or goat that is slaughtered is to be brought to the tent of meeting, so that no sacrifice is made elsewhere. Compare Deut 12:20–25.
- **Blood and soul** (v11, 14) — the ground of the ban on eating blood: the soul of the flesh is in the blood, and the blood is given on the altar to make atonement. Heb 9:22 draws on it.
- **Blood of hunted game** (v13) — the blood of game is poured out and covered with dust.
- **Apostolic decree** (Acts 15:20, 29) — the Jerusalem council asked believers from the nations to abstain from blood and from things strangled; this chapter binds the sojourner also (v10, 12, 13).
