# Leviticus 9

[1] And it came to pass on the eighth day that ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ called ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons and the elders of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[2] And he said to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, "Take for yourself a calf of the herd for a sin offering and a ram for a burnt offering, without blemish, and bring them near before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] And to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ you shall speak, saying, 'Take a male goat for a sin offering, and a calf and a lamb, a year old, without blemish, for a burnt offering,

[4] and an ox and a ram for peace offerings, to sacrifice before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and a grain offering mixed with oil; for today ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has appeared to you.'"

[5] And they took what ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ commanded to the front of the tent of meeting, and all the congregation came near and stood before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said, "This is the thing that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded that you do, that the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ may appear to you."

[7] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, "Come near to the altar, and offer your sin offering and your burnt offering, and make atonement on behalf of yourself and on behalf of the people; and offer the offering of the people, and make atonement on behalf of them, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded."

[8] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ came near to the altar and slaughtered the calf of the sin offering that was for himself.

[9] And the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ brought the blood near to him, and he dipped his finger in the blood and placed it on the horns of the altar; and the blood he poured at the base of the altar.

[10] And the fat and the kidneys and the lobe from the liver, from the sin offering, he turned into smoke on the altar, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[11] And the flesh and the skin he burned with fire outside the camp.

[12] And he slaughtered the burnt offering, and the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ handed the blood to him, and he dashed it against the altar around.

[13] And the burnt offering they handed to him, by its pieces, and the head, and he turned them into smoke on the altar.

[14] And he washed the entrails and the shanks, and he turned them into smoke upon the burnt offering, on the altar.

[15] And he brought near the offering of the people, and he took the male goat of the sin offering that was for the people, and slaughtered it and offered it as a sin offering, like the first.

[16] And he brought near the burnt offering and offered it according to the ordinance.

[17] And he brought near the grain offering, and filled his palm from it, and turned it into smoke on the altar, besides the burnt offering of the morning.

[18] And he slaughtered the ox and the ram, the sacrifice of peace offerings that was for the people; and the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ handed the blood to him, and he dashed it against the altar around;

[19] and the fat parts from the ox, and from the ram the fat tail, and that which covers, and the kidneys, and the lobe of the liver:

[20] and they put the fat parts on the breasts, and he turned the fat parts into smoke on the altar.

[21] And the breasts and the right leg ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ waved as a wave offering before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, as ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ commanded.

[22] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ lifted up his hands toward the people and blessed them; and he came down from offering the sin offering and the burnt offering and the peace offerings.

[23] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ went into the tent of meeting, and they came out and blessed the people; and the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ appeared to all the people.

[24] And fire came out from before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and consumed on the altar the burnt offering and the fat parts; and all the people saw, and they shouted for joy and fell on their faces.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ "and"; the chain is kept. **"And it came to pass"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי.
- **"a young calf" → "a calf of the herd"** (v2) — Hebrew עֵגֶל בֶּן־בָּקָר, literally "a calf, a son of the herd"; the words do not say "young". **"without blemish"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew תְּמִימִם, "whole, complete": the word that is "blameless" of a man.
- **"offer them" → "bring them near"** (v2) — Hebrew וְהַקְרֵב, hifil of קָרַב "come near", with no object in the Hebrew: "them" is supplied because English cannot stand without it. So "brought near" in v9, 15, 16, 17; "came near" (v5, 7, 8) is the simple stem of the same verb.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל. **"a kid of the goats" → "a male goat"** (v3, 15) — Hebrew שְׂעִיר־עִזִּים. **"both of the first year" → "a year old"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּנֵי־שָׁנָה, literally "sons of a year".
- **"a bullock" → "an ox"** (v4, 18, 19) — Hebrew שׁוֹר, one head of cattle. **"meat offering mingled with oil" → "grain offering mixed with oil"** (v4, 17) — Hebrew מִנְחָה בְּלוּלָה בַשָּׁמֶן.
- **"for to day the LORD will appear unto you" → "for today Yahweh has appeared to you"** (v4) — Hebrew נִרְאָה, pointed as a perfect ("has appeared"), though the appearing comes in v23: the thing is spoken of as done. With other vowels the same letters are a participle, "is appearing". (The Septuagint is said to have a future here: from memory, not checked.)
- **"before the tabernacle of the congregation" → "to the front of the tent of meeting"** (v5) — Hebrew אֶל־פְּנֵי אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד.
- **"This is the thing which the LORD commanded that ye should do: and the glory of the LORD shall appear unto you" → "… that you do, that the glory of Yahweh may appear to you"** (v6) — Hebrew וְיֵרָא, a jussive: "and let the glory of Yahweh appear", after a command a statement of what is aimed at.
- **"Go unto the altar" → "Come near to the altar"** (v7) — Hebrew קְרַב. **"offer"** (v7, 16, 22) — Hebrew עָשָׂה "do, make", the usual verb for preparing a sacrifice; it is another verb than "bring near". **"make an atonement for thyself, and for the people" → "make atonement on behalf of yourself and on behalf of the people"** — Hebrew כִּפֶּר בְּעַד; elsewhere the preposition is עַל "for". כִּפֶּר is literally "cover". **"the offering of the people"** (v7, 15) — Hebrew קָרְבַּן הָעָם, "what is brought near".
- **"slew" → "slaughtered"** (v8, 12, 15, 18) — Hebrew שָׁחַט.
- **"put it upon the horns" → "placed it on the horns"** (v9) — Hebrew נָתַן. **"poured out the blood at the bottom of the altar" → "the blood he poured at the base of the altar"** — Hebrew יָצַק אֶל־יְסוֹד.
- **"the caul above the liver" → "the lobe from the liver"** (v10); **"the lobe of the liver"** (v19) — Hebrew הַיֹּתֶרֶת מִן־הַכָּבֵד; יֹתֶרֶת הַכָּבֵד: an appendage of the liver, the exact part is uncertain.
- **"burnt" → "turned into smoke"** (v10, 13, 14, 17, 20); **"burnt with fire" kept** (v11) — two Hebrew verbs: on the altar הִקְטִיר, literally "make smoke"; outside the camp שָׂרַף "burn".
- **"the hide" → "the skin"** (v11) — Hebrew עוֹר.
- **"presented unto him the blood" → "handed the blood to him"** (v12, 13, 18) — Hebrew הִמְצִיא, literally "made it reach him". **"sprinkled round about upon the altar" → "dashed it against the altar around"** (v12, 18) — Hebrew זָרַק, to throw from a bowl.
- **"with the pieces thereof" → "by its pieces"** (v13) — Hebrew לִנְתָחֶיהָ.
- **"legs" → "shanks"** (v14) — Hebrew כְּרָעַיִם, the lower legs.
- **"offered it for sin, as the first" → "offered it as a sin offering, like the first"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיְחַטְּאֵהוּ, the piel of חָטָא "sin", made from the name of the sin offering (6:26).
- **"according to the manner" → "according to the ordinance"** (v16) — Hebrew כַּמִּשְׁפָּט.
- **"took an handful thereof" → "filled his palm from it"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיְמַלֵּא כַפּוֹ מִמֶּנָּה. **"beside the burnt sacrifice of the morning"** — Hebrew מִלְּבַד עֹלַת הַבֹּקֶר.
- **"the fat" → "the fat parts"** (v19, 20, 24) — Hebrew הַחֲלָבִים, plural. **"the rump" → "the fat tail"; "that which covereth the inwards" → "that which covers"** (v19) — Hebrew הָאַלְיָה; הַמְכַסֶּה, with "the entrails" not named. The verse has no verb of its own; v20 takes it up.
- **"the right shoulder" → "the right leg"** (v21) — Hebrew שׁוֹק, the upper hind leg. **"waved for a wave offering"** — Hebrew הֵנִיף … תְּנוּפָה, verb and noun of one root: to move to and fro (or to lift) before Yahweh. **"as Moses commanded"** — so the Hebrew as printed. (Some Hebrew manuscripts and old versions are said to read "as Yahweh commanded Moses": from memory, not checked.)
- **"lifted up his hand" → "lifted up his hands"** (v22) — the written text has "his hand" (ידו); the reading tradition (qere) has "his hands" (יָדָיו), and the verse follows it.
- **"consumed"** (v24) — Hebrew וַתֹּאכַל, "ate". **"which when all the people saw, they shouted" → "and all the people saw, and they shouted for joy"** — Hebrew וַיָּרֹנּוּ, to give a ringing cry; mostly it is a cry of joy.

### Notes

- **The eighth day** (v1) — the day after the seven days of the filling (8:33–35).
- **"Today Yahweh has appeared to you"** (v4) — taken up in v6 and v23.
- **Order of the offerings** — for Aaron: sin offering, burnt offering (v8–14); for the people: sin offering, burnt offering, grain offering, peace offerings (v15–21).
- **Fire from before Yahweh** (v24) — compare 1 Kgs 18:38; 2 Chr 7:1. The same words open 10:2.
- **The blessing** (v22) — its words are not given here; see Num 6:24–26.
