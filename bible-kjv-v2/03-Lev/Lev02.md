# Leviticus 2

[1] "And when a soul brings near an offering of a grain offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, his offering shall be fine flour; and he shall pour oil on it and place frankincense on it.

[2] And he shall bring it to the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, the priests; and he shall take from there his full handful of its fine flour and of its oil, together with all its frankincense; and the priest shall turn its memorial portion into smoke on the altar, an offering by fire of a soothing smell to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] And what remains of the grain offering is for ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and for his sons: a most holy thing from the offerings by fire of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[4] And when you bring near an offering of a grain offering, a thing baked in the oven: fine flour, unleavened cakes mixed with oil and unleavened wafers anointed with oil.

[5] And if your offering is a grain offering on the griddle, it shall be fine flour mixed with oil, unleavened.

[6] Break it into bits, and you shall pour oil on it; it is a grain offering.

[7] And if your offering is a grain offering of the deep pan, of fine flour with oil it shall be made.

[8] And you shall bring the grain offering that is made of these to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and he shall bring it near to the priest, and he shall bring it forward to the altar.

[9] And the priest shall lift up from the grain offering its memorial portion and turn it into smoke on the altar, an offering by fire of a soothing smell to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[10] And what remains of the grain offering is for ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and for his sons: a most holy thing from the offerings by fire of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[11] No grain offering that you bring near to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be made as what is leavened; for all leaven and all honey, you shall not turn any of it into smoke as an offering by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] As an offering of the first you shall bring them near to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, but to the altar they shall not go up for a soothing smell.

[13] And every offering of your grain offering you shall salt with salt, and you shall not make the salt of the covenant of your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ cease from upon your grain offering; upon every offering of yours you shall bring near salt.

[14] And if you bring near a grain offering of firstfruits to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, grain in the ear parched with fire, groats of fresh grain, you shall bring near as the grain offering of your firstfruits.

[15] And you shall place oil on it and put frankincense on it; it is a grain offering.

[16] And the priest shall turn its memorial portion into smoke, from its groats and from its oil, together with all its frankincense, an offering by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v13) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"when any will offer" → "when a soul brings near"** (v1) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ כִּי־תַקְרִיב: נֶפֶשׁ "soul", here a person; the verb is feminine with "soul", and the Hebrew then goes on with "his offering", "he shall pour".
- **"a meat offering" → "an offering of a grain offering"** (v1, 4, 13) — Hebrew קָרְבַּן מִנְחָה, two nouns: קָרְבָּן "offering" (what is brought near, the noun of the verb "bring near") and מִנְחָה, a gift or tribute, in this law the offering of flour or grain. "Meat" in the English of 1611 meant food.
- **"put frankincense" → "place frankincense"** (v1, 15) — Hebrew נָתַן, literally "give"; "put" (v15) is שִׂים. The two change places between v1 and v15.
- **"he shall take thereout his handful" → "he shall take from there his full handful"** (v2) — Hebrew וְקָמַץ מִשָּׁם מְלֹא קֻמְצוֹ, literally "he shall take a handful from there, the fullness of his handful": verb and noun of one root, what the closed hand holds. The subject is not named; KJV keeps "he".
- **"with all the frankincense thereof" → "together with all its frankincense"** (v2, 16) — Hebrew עַל, literally "upon".
- **"burn the memorial of it" → "turn its memorial portion into smoke"** (v2, 9, 16) — Hebrew הִקְטִיר, literally "make smoke"; and אַזְכָּרָה, from זָכַר "remember": the part of the grain offering that goes on the altar. "Portion" is supplied.
- **"an offering made by fire, of a sweet savour" → "an offering by fire of a soothing smell"** (v2, 9) — Hebrew אִשֵּׁה רֵיחַ נִיחֹחַ; see 1:9. The derivation of אִשֶּׁה from אֵשׁ "fire" is not certain.
- **"the remnant of the meat offering shall be Aaron's" → "what remains of the grain offering is for Aaron"** (v3, 10) — Hebrew וְהַנּוֹתֶרֶת … לְאַהֲרֹן, without a verb. **"a thing most holy of the offerings of the LORD made by fire" → "a most holy thing from the offerings by fire of Yahweh"** — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ קָדָשִׁים, literally "holiness of holinesses".
- **"baken in the oven" → "a thing baked in the oven"** (v4) — Hebrew מַאֲפֵה תַנּוּר, "a baking of the oven". **"it shall be unleavened cakes of fine flour … or unleavened wafers" → "fine flour, unleavened cakes mixed with oil and unleavened wafers"** — the Hebrew has no verb, and joins the cakes and the wafers with וּ "and".
- **"baken in a pan" → "on the griddle"** (v5) — Hebrew עַל־הַמַּחֲבַת, a flat plate. **"baken in the fryingpan" → "of the deep pan"** (v7) — Hebrew מַרְחֶשֶׁת, a deeper vessel. How the two differ is not certain.
- **"Thou shalt part it in pieces" → "Break it into bits"** (v6) — Hebrew פָּתוֹת אֹתָהּ פִּתִּים, verb and noun of one root; the verb is an infinitive used as a command.
- **"when it is presented unto the priest, he shall bring it unto the altar" → "he shall bring it near to the priest, and he shall bring it forward to the altar"** (v8) — three Hebrew verbs in one verse: הֵבִיא "bring", הִקְרִיב "bring near", הִגִּישׁ "bring forward" (the verb of נָגַשׁ "draw near"). The subject changes from "you" to "he" and is not named again; the second "he" is the priest.
- **"take from the meat offering a memorial" → "lift up from the grain offering its memorial portion"** (v9) — Hebrew הֵרִים, the verb of תְּרוּמָה "contribution".
- **"made with leaven" → "made as what is leavened"** (v11) — Hebrew חָמֵץ, the leavened thing; "leaven" in the next clause is שְׂאֹר, the sourdough. **"ye shall burn no leaven, nor any honey" → "all leaven and all honey, you shall not turn any of it into smoke"** — Hebrew לֹא־תַקְטִירוּ מִמֶּנּוּ, "you shall not turn into smoke from it".
- **"the oblation of the firstfruits" → "an offering of the first"** (v12) — Hebrew קָרְבַּן רֵאשִׁית: רֵאשִׁית is "the first, the beginning" (Gen 1:1); "firstfruits" in v14 is another word, בִּכּוּרִים. **"they shall not be burnt on the altar" → "to the altar they shall not go up"** — Hebrew לֹא־יַעֲלוּ, the verb of עֹלָה "burnt offering".
- **"season with salt" → "salt with salt"** (v13) — Hebrew בַּמֶּלַח תִּמְלָח, verb and noun of one root. **"neither shalt thou suffer the salt … to be lacking from" → "you shall not make the salt … cease from upon"** — Hebrew וְלֹא תַשְׁבִּית … מֵעַל, the verb of שָׁבַת "cease".
- **"the salt of the covenant of your Elohim"** (v13) — Hebrew מֶלַח בְּרִית אֱלֹהֶיךָ; a "covenant of salt" is named in Num 18:19; 2 Chr 13:5.
- **"green ears of corn dried by the fire, even corn beaten out of full ears" → "grain in the ear parched with fire, groats of fresh grain"** (v14) — Hebrew אָבִיב קָלוּי בָּאֵשׁ גֶּרֶשׂ כַּרְמֶל: אָבִיב the young ears (Exod 9:31), קָלוּי "parched", גֶּרֶשׂ crushed grain. The sense of כַּרְמֶל is uncertain: newly ripe grain.
- **"part of the beaten corn thereof, and part of the oil thereof" → "from its groats and from its oil"** (v16) — Hebrew מִגִּרְשָׂהּ וּמִשַּׁמְנָהּ.

### Notes

- **No leaven, no honey (v11)** — neither may be turned into smoke on the altar; as an offering of the first they may be brought near (v12).
- **Salt (v13)** — every offering is salted. Compare Mark 9:49.
