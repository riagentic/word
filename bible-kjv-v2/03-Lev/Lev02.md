# Leviticus 2

[1] "When anyone brings a grain offering to Yahweh, his offering shall be of fine flour; he shall pour oil on it and put frankincense on it,

[2] and bring it to Aaron's sons the priests. The priest shall take from it a handful of its fine flour and its oil, with all its frankincense, and he shall turn this memorial portion into smoke on the altar — an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma to Yahweh.

[3] The remainder of the grain offering shall belong to Aaron and his sons. It is a most holy part of the offerings of Yahweh made by fire.

[4] When you bring a grain offering baked in an oven, it shall be unleavened cakes of fine flour mixed with oil, or unleavened wafers anointed with oil.

[5] If your offering is a grain offering prepared on a griddle, it shall be of fine flour, unleavened, mixed with oil.

[6] Break it into pieces and pour oil on it; it is a grain offering.

[7] If your offering is a grain offering cooked in a pan, it shall be made of fine flour with oil.

[8] You shall bring the grain offering that is made of these things to Yahweh; and when it is presented to the priest, he shall bring it to the altar.

[9] The priest shall take up from the grain offering a memorial portion and turn it into smoke on the altar — an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma to Yahweh.

[10] What is left of the grain offering shall belong to Aaron and his sons. It is a most holy part of the offerings of Yahweh made by fire.

[11] No grain offering that you bring to Yahweh shall be made with leaven; for you shall not turn into smoke any leaven or any honey in an offering of Yahweh made by fire.

[12] As for the offering of firstfruits, you shall bring them to Yahweh, but they shall not come up on the altar as a pleasing aroma.

[13] Every grain offering of yours you shall season with salt; you shall not let the salt of the covenant of your *Elohim* be missing from your grain offering. With all your offerings you shall offer salt.

[14] If you offer a grain offering of firstfruits to Yahweh, you shall offer for your grain offering of firstfruits new ears roasted in fire — coarsely ground grain from fresh ears.

[15] You shall put oil on it and lay frankincense on it; it is a grain offering.

[16] The priest shall turn into smoke the memorial portion of it — some of the coarsely ground grain and some of the oil, with all its frankincense — an offering made by fire to Yahweh."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** (throughout) — Hebrew מִנְחָה *minchah* = gift / tribute / grain-offering. "Meat" in 1611 English = food; here specifically non-animal offering. Corrected.
- **"corn" → "grain"** (v14, 16) — as elsewhere.
- **"memorial" (v2, 9, 16)** — Hebrew אַזְכָּרָה *azkarah* = memorial-portion. The handful burnt symbolically represents the whole; the bulk goes to the priests.
- **"salt of the covenant"** (v13) — Hebrew מֶלַח בְּרִית *melach berit*. Salt as covenantal symbol (its preservative quality making it an apt metaphor for enduring covenant; cf. Num 18:19, 2 Chr 13:5).

### Notes

- **No leaven, no honey** (v11) — both fermentable substances excluded from burnt offerings. Symbolic: no "corruption" in the altar-offering.
- **Salt always included** — the salting of offerings makes the offering covenantal / purified. Jesus draws on this: "Everyone will be salted with fire" (Mk 9:49); "You are the salt of the earth" (Matt 5:13).
