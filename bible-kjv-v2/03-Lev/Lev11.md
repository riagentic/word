# Leviticus 11

[1] Yahweh spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying to them,

[2] "Speak to the children of Israel, saying: 'These are the living creatures that you may eat among all the animals that are on the earth.

[3] Whatever has a divided hoof — cleft in two — and chews the cud, among the animals, that you may eat.

[4] But among those that chew the cud or have divided hooves, these you shall not eat: the camel, because it chews the cud but does not have a divided hoof — it is unclean to you;

[5] and the rock badger, because it chews the cud but does not have a divided hoof — it is unclean to you;

[6] and the hare, because it chews the cud but does not have a divided hoof — it is unclean to you;

[7] and the pig, because it has a divided hoof — cleft in two — but does not chew the cud — it is unclean to you.

[8] You shall not eat of their flesh, and their carcasses you shall not touch; they are unclean to you.

[9] These you may eat of all that are in the waters: whatever has fins and scales in the waters — in the seas and in the rivers — these you may eat.

[10] But anything that does not have fins and scales in the seas and rivers, of all the swarming things in the waters and of any living creature that is in the waters, they are detestable to you.

[11] They shall be detestable to you; you shall not eat of their flesh, and their carcasses you shall regard as detestable.

[12] Everything in the waters that has no fins or scales is detestable to you.

[13] These you shall regard as detestable among the birds; they shall not be eaten, they are detestable: the eagle, the bearded vulture, the black vulture,

[14] the kite, the falcon of any kind,

[15] every raven of any kind,

[16] the ostrich, the nighthawk, the sea gull, the hawk of any kind,

[17] the little owl, the cormorant, the great owl,

[18] the white owl, the desert owl, the Egyptian vulture,

[19] the stork, the heron of any kind, the hoopoe, and the bat.

[20] All winged swarming creatures that walk on all fours are detestable to you.

[21] Yet these you may eat of all winged swarming creatures that walk on all fours: those that have jointed legs above their feet with which to leap on the ground.

[22] Of them you may eat these: the locust of any kind, the bald locust of any kind, the cricket of any kind, and the grasshopper of any kind.

[23] But all other winged swarming creatures that have four feet are detestable to you.

[24] By these you become unclean: whoever touches their carcass shall be unclean until evening;

[25] and whoever carries any of their carcass shall wash his clothes and be unclean until evening.

[26] Every animal with a divided hoof that is not cleft in two, or that does not chew the cud, is unclean to you; everyone who touches them shall be unclean.

[27] Whatever walks on its paws, among all the animals that walk on all fours, is unclean to you; whoever touches their carcass shall be unclean until evening.

[28] And whoever carries their carcass shall wash his clothes and be unclean until evening; they are unclean to you.

[29] These are unclean to you among the swarming things that swarm on the earth: the weasel, the mouse, the large lizard of any kind,

[30] the gecko, the land crocodile, the lizard, the sand lizard, and the chameleon.

[31] These are unclean to you among all that swarm; whoever touches them when they are dead shall be unclean until evening.

[32] Anything on which any of them falls when they are dead shall be unclean — any article of wood, clothing, skin, or sackcloth, any article used for any purpose — it must be put into water, and be unclean until evening; then it shall be clean.

[33] Any earthenware vessel into which any of them falls — whatever is in it shall be unclean, and you shall break the vessel.

[34] Any food that may be eaten, on which water from such a vessel comes, shall be unclean; and any drink that may be drunk from every such vessel shall be unclean.

[35] Everything on which any part of their carcass falls shall be unclean — whether an oven or a stove for pots, it shall be broken down; they are unclean, and shall be unclean to you.

[36] However, a spring or cistern holding water shall remain clean; but whatever touches their carcass shall be unclean.

[37] And if any part of their carcass falls on any seed for sowing that is to be sown, it is clean.

[38] But if water is put on the seed, and any part of their carcass falls on it, it is unclean to you.

[39] If any animal which you may eat dies, whoever touches its carcass shall be unclean until evening.

[40] Whoever eats any of its carcass shall wash his clothes and be unclean until evening; and whoever carries its carcass shall wash his clothes and be unclean until evening.

[41] Every swarming thing that swarms on the earth is detestable; it shall not be eaten.

[42] Whatever crawls on its belly, and whatever walks on all fours, or whatever has many feet — any swarming thing that swarms on the earth — you shall not eat, for they are detestable.

[43] You shall not make yourselves detestable with any swarming thing that swarms, and you shall not make yourselves unclean with them, that you should be defiled by them.

[44] For I am Yahweh your *Elohim*; consecrate yourselves and be holy, for I am holy. You shall not defile yourselves with any swarming thing that moves on the earth.

[45] For I am Yahweh who brought you up out of the land of Egypt to be your *Elohim*; you shall therefore be holy, for I am holy.

[46] This is the law concerning the animal, the bird, every living creature that moves in the waters, and every creature that swarms on the earth,

[47] to distinguish between the unclean and the clean, and between the living creatures that may be eaten and those that may not be eaten.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"coney" → "rock badger"** (v5) — Hebrew שָׁפָן *shaphan*. Hyrax / rock badger of the Levant, not European rabbit. *Coney* in 1611 = any rabbit-like creature; modernized.
- **"swine" → "pig"** (v7) — modernized.
- **"abomination" → "detestable"** (v10, 11, 13, 20, 23, 41, 42) — Hebrew שֶׁקֶץ *sheqets*. Distinct from תּוֹעֵבָה *to'evah* (moral abomination). *Sheqets* = ritually abhorrent / polluting food-source; narrower meaning.
- **Bird list (v13–19)** — many Hebrew names uncertain. Translations give best modern zoological guesses; *ossifrage* (KJV) = bearded vulture (*peres*); *ospray* = black vulture (*azniyyah*); *swan* more likely white owl / water hen (*tinshemet*); *pelican* likely desert owl (*qa'at*); *gier eagle* = Egyptian vulture (*racham*); *cuckow* = sea gull (*shachaph*); *lapwing* = hoopoe (*dukhifat*). KJV's bird identifications are often conjectural; modern Hebraic scholarship revises.
- **"beetle" → "cricket"** (v22) — KJV *beetle* erroneous (beetles do not leap). Hebrew חַרְגֹּל *chargol* — a jumping insect, cricket or katydid.
- **"creeping things" → "swarming things"** (v29–31, 41–43) — Hebrew שֶׁרֶץ *sheretz*. Small, teeming creatures — *swarming* better reflects the teeming / multiplying quality.
- **"tortoise" → "large lizard"** (v29) — Hebrew צָב *tsav*. Likely the dabb lizard, not a tortoise. KJV *tortoise* is a guess.
- **"ferret / chameleon / lizard / snail / mole" → "gecko / land crocodile / lizard / sand lizard / chameleon"** (v30) — the Hebrew list (*anaqah, koach, leta'ah, chomet, tinshemet*) all refer to reptiles. KJV identifications uncertain; modern herpetology identifies them as various Levantine lizards.
- **"make a difference" → "distinguish"** (v47) — modernized.

### Notes
- **Dietary laws' logic** — scholars have long debated: hygiene? symbolism? anti-idolatry? The text itself gives holiness (v44–45) as the reason — boundary-marking for Israel as Yahweh's consecrated people.
- **Clean / unclean grid** — land animals (hoof + cud), water creatures (fins + scales), birds (by list, mostly carrion/predator = unclean), swarming insects (leaping legs = clean). Each realm has its "proper" locomotion; creatures that cross categories are anomalous.
- **Carcass uncleanness** (v24–40) — touching a carcass renders unclean until evening; carrying one requires clothes-washing. Earthenware is porous and cannot be purified — must be broken.
- **"You shall be holy, for I am holy"** (v44, 45) — key refrain of the Holiness Code (Lev 17–26), previewed here. Holiness = imitatio Dei and separation.
- **Abolition in NT** — Acts 10:15 (Peter's vision) and Mark 7:19 signal abrogation of these dietary laws in the new covenant.
