# Leviticus 11

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, saying to them,

[2] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying: 'These are the living things that you may eat, of all the beasts that are on the earth.

[3] Everything that has a hoof and cleaves a cleft in the hoofs, that brings up the cud, among the beasts — that you may eat.

[4] Yet this you shall not eat, of those that bring up the cud and of those that have a hoof: the camel, for it brings up the cud, but a hoof it does not have; it is unclean to you.

[5] And the hyrax, for it brings up the cud, but a hoof it does not have; it is unclean to you.

[6] And the hare, for it brings up the cud, but a hoof it does not have; it is unclean to you.

[7] And the pig, for it has a hoof and cleaves a cleft in the hoof, but it does not chew the cud; it is unclean to you.

[8] Of their flesh you shall not eat, and their carcass you shall not touch; they are unclean to you.

[9] This you may eat, of all that is in the waters: everything that has fins and scales in the waters, in the seas and in the torrents — them you may eat.

[10] And everything that has no fins and scales, in the seas and in the torrents, of every swarming thing of the waters and of every living soul that is in the waters — they are a detestable thing to you.

[11] And a detestable thing they shall be to you; of their flesh you shall not eat, and their carcass you shall detest.

[12] Everything that has no fins and scales in the waters — it is a detestable thing to you.

[13] And these you shall detest of the birds; they shall not be eaten; they are a detestable thing: the eagle and the bearded vulture and the black vulture,

[14] and the kite, and the falcon after its kind,

[15] every raven after its kind,

[16] and the ostrich and the nighthawk and the sea gull, and the hawk after its kind,

[17] and the little owl and the cormorant and the great owl,

[18] and the white owl and the desert owl and the Egyptian vulture,

[19] and the stork, the heron after its kind, and the hoopoe and the bat.

[20] Every swarming thing that flies, that goes on four — it is a detestable thing to you.

[21] Yet this you may eat, of every swarming thing that flies, that goes on four: that which has shanks above its feet, to leap with them on the earth.

[22] These of them you may eat: the locust after its kind, and the bald locust after its kind, and the cricket after its kind, and the grasshopper after its kind.

[23] And every swarming thing that flies, that has four feet — it is a detestable thing to you.

[24] And by these you defile yourselves; everyone who touches their carcass shall be unclean until the evening.

[25] And everyone who carries any of their carcass shall wash his garments and be unclean until the evening.

[26] As for every beast that has a hoof but does not cleave a cleft, and does not bring up the cud — they are unclean to you; everyone who touches them shall be unclean.

[27] And everything that goes on its paws, among all the living things that go on four — they are unclean to you; everyone who touches their carcass shall be unclean until the evening.

[28] And he who carries their carcass shall wash his garments and be unclean until the evening; they are unclean to you.

[29] And this is what is unclean to you among the swarming things that swarm on the earth: the weasel and the mouse, and the large lizard after its kind,

[30] and the gecko and the monitor lizard and the lizard and the sand lizard and the chameleon.

[31] These are the unclean to you among all the swarming things; everyone who touches them when they are dead shall be unclean until the evening.

[32] And everything on which any of them falls when they are dead shall be unclean: of any article of wood, or garment, or skin, or sack — any article with which work is done; it shall be brought into water and be unclean until the evening, and then it shall be clean.

[33] And any earthen vessel into the midst of which any of them falls — all that is in its midst shall be unclean, and it you shall break.

[34] Of all the food that is eaten, that on which water comes shall be unclean; and all drink that is drunk, in any vessel, shall be unclean.

[35] And everything on which any of their carcass falls shall be unclean; oven and stove shall be broken down; they are unclean, and unclean they shall be to you.

[36] Yet a spring and a pit, a gathering of waters, shall be clean; but he who touches their carcass shall be unclean.

[37] And if any of their carcass falls on any seed for sowing that is to be sown, it is clean.

[38] But if water is put on seed and any of their carcass falls on it, it is unclean to you.

[39] And if any of the beasts that are yours for food dies, he who touches its carcass shall be unclean until the evening.

[40] And he who eats of its carcass shall wash his garments and be unclean until the evening; and he who carries its carcass shall wash his garments and be unclean until the evening.

[41] And every swarming thing that swarms on the earth — it is a detestable thing; it shall not be eaten.

[42] Everything that goes on the belly, and everything that goes on four, up to everything that has many feet, of every swarming thing that swarms on the earth — you shall not eat them, for they are a detestable thing.

[43] Do not make your souls detestable with any swarming thing that swarms, and you shall not defile yourselves by them and be defiled by them.

[44] For I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and you shall sanctify yourselves and be holy, for I am holy; and you shall not defile your souls with any swarming thing that creeps on the earth.

[45] For I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who brought you up from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, to be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to you; and you shall be holy, for I am holy.

[46] This is ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ of the beast and of the birds and of every living soul that creeps in the waters, and for every soul that swarms on the earth,

[47] to separate the unclean from the clean, and the living thing that is eaten from the living thing that shall not be eaten.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v44, 45) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"These are the beasts which ye shall eat among all the beasts" → "These are the living things that you may eat, of all the beasts"** (v2) — two Hebrew words: חַיָּה "living thing" and בְּהֵמָה "beast" (four-footed animal). The Hebrew is a collective singular, "This is the living thing". So also v27, 47.
- **"parteth the hoof" → "has a hoof"** (v3–7, 26) — Hebrew מַפְרֶסֶת פַּרְסָה, a verb made from the noun פַּרְסָה "hoof": literally "hoofs a hoof". The traditional "divides the hoof" takes the verb from פָּרַס "break". The verse does not decide between them in v3; in v26 the animal that "hoofs a hoof" is set against the one that cleaves a cleft (the horse and the donkey have a hoof and no cleft), so "has a hoof" is followed. The three verbs of v4, 5, 6 are a participle, an imperfect and a perfect in the Hebrew; English has one form for them.
- **"is clovenfooted" → "cleaves a cleft in the hoofs"** (v3, 7, 26) — Hebrew שֹׁסַעַת שֶׁסַע פְּרָסֹת, literally "cleaving a cleft of hoofs"; verb and noun of one root.
- **"cheweth the cud" → "brings up the cud"** (v3–6, 26); **"cheweth not the cud" → "does not chew the cud"** (v7) — two Hebrew verbs. הֶעֱלָה גֵרָה is "make the cud come up"; in v7 it is גֵּרָה לֹא־יִגָּר, a verb of the same root as "cud", literally "it does not cud the cud".
- **"coney" → "hyrax"** (v5) — Hebrew שָׁפָן, the rock hyrax (Ps 104:18; Prov 30:26). The hyrax and the hare (v6) move their jaws as cud-chewers do; the text names them by what is seen.
- **"swine" → "pig"** (v7) — Hebrew חֲזִיר.
- **"their carcase"** (v8, 11, 24–40) — Hebrew נְבֵלָה, the body of an animal that has died; singular in the Hebrew throughout, kept.
- **"in the rivers" → "in the torrents"** (v9, 10) — Hebrew נְחָלִים, the plural of נַחַל "wadi": the stream bed and the water that runs in it. Here the water is meant.
- **"of all that move in the waters" → "of every swarming thing of the waters"** (v10) — Hebrew שֶׁרֶץ הַמַּיִם. **"any living thing" → "every living soul"** (v10, 46) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ הַחַיָּה, as Gen 1:20.
- **"abomination" → "a detestable thing"; "have in abomination" → "detest"** (v10–13, 20, 23, 41–43) — Hebrew שֶׁקֶץ and its verb שִׁקֵּץ. It is another word than תּוֹעֵבָה "abomination". In v43 the same verb has "your souls" as its object: "make detestable".
- **"fowls" → "birds"** (v13, 46) — Hebrew עוֹף, a collective: all that flies.
- **The birds of v13–19** — twenty names. The identification of most of them is uncertain; the renderings give the best-supported bird and are kept the same in Deut 14. Fairly sure are נֶשֶׁר "eagle" (the word also covers the great vultures), עֹרֵב "raven", נֵץ "hawk", חֲסִידָה "stork", עֲטַלֵּף "bat". The others: פֶּרֶס "bearded vulture" (KJV "ossifrage", literally "breaker"), עָזְנִיָּה "black vulture" (KJV "ospray"), דָּאָה "kite" (KJV "vulture"), אַיָּה "falcon" (KJV "kite"), בַּת הַיַּעֲנָה "ostrich" (KJV "owl"; literally "daughter of the ostrich"; some take it for an owl), תַּחְמָס "nighthawk" (unknown; perhaps an owl), שַׁחַף "sea gull" (KJV "cuckow"), כּוֹס "little owl", שָׁלָךְ "cormorant" (literally "the plunger"), יַנְשׁוּף "great owl", תִּנְשֶׁמֶת "white owl" (KJV "swan"), קָאָת "desert owl" (KJV "pelican"; Ps 102:6 puts it in the wilderness), רָחָם "Egyptian vulture" (KJV "gier eagle"), אֲנָפָה "heron", דּוּכִיפַת "hoopoe" (KJV "lapwing"). "After its kind" stands in the Hebrew only where the verse has it. In v15 and before "the heron" in v19 the Hebrew has no "and".
- **"All fowls that creep" → "Every swarming thing that flies"** (v20, 21, 23) — Hebrew שֶׁרֶץ הָעוֹף, literally "swarming thing of the flying things": the winged insects. עוֹף is the "birds" of v13. **"going upon all four" → "that goes on four"** (v20, 21, 27, 42) — Hebrew עַל־אַרְבַּע; "all" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"which have legs above their feet" → "that which has shanks above its feet"** (v21) — Hebrew כְּרָעַיִם, the lower legs (Exod 12:9; 29:17). The written text has לֹא "not" ("which has no shanks"); the reading tradition (qere) has לוֹ "to it", which the sense requires and the verse follows.
- **"the beetle" → "the cricket"** (v22) — the four names אַרְבֶּה, סָלְעָם, חַרְגֹּל, חָגָב are kinds of leaping locust that cannot be told apart now; "bald locust" (KJV) rests on later Jewish tradition. The renderings are uncertain.
- **"all other flying creeping things" → "every swarming thing that flies"** (v23) — "other" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"for these ye shall be unclean" → "by these you defile yourselves"** (v24) — Hebrew תִּטַּמָּאוּ, the reflexive stem of the root of "unclean": literally "you make yourselves unclean", as in v43.
- **"wash his clothes" → "wash his garments"** (v25, 28, 40) — Hebrew כִּבֵּס בְּגָדָיו; כִּבֵּס is the washing of cloth.
- **"The carcases of every beast which divideth the hoof" → "As for every beast that has a hoof"** (v26) — the Hebrew begins לְכָל־הַבְּהֵמָה, "for every beast"; "the carcases" is not in the verse.
- **"paws"** (v27) — Hebrew כַּפָּיו, literally "its palms".
- **"creeping things that creep" → "swarming things that swarm"** (v29, 31, 41–43, 46) — Hebrew שֶׁרֶץ … שֹׁרֵץ; "creep" (v44, 46) is רָמַשׂ.
- **The swarming things of v29–30** — eight names; only עַכְבָּר "mouse" is fairly sure. חֹלֶד "weasel" (or the mole rat), צָב "large lizard" (KJV "tortoise"), אֲנָקָה "gecko" (KJV "ferret"), כֹּחַ "monitor lizard" (KJV "chameleon"), לְטָאָה "lizard", חֹמֶט "sand lizard" (KJV "snail"), תִּנְשֶׁמֶת "chameleon" (KJV "mole"). The last is the same Hebrew name as the bird of v18; two animals are meant, so English has two words.
- **"when they be dead"** (v31, 32) — Hebrew בְּמֹתָם, "in their death".
- **"vessel" → "article"** (v32) — Hebrew כְּלִי, any made thing; of earthenware (v33, 34) "vessel". It is one Hebrew word. **"sack"** — Hebrew שָׂק, the coarse cloth and the bag made of it.
- **"that on which such water cometh" → "that on which water comes"; "in every such vessel" → "in any vessel"** (v34) — "such" is not in the Hebrew. The verse is short: literally "Of all the food that is eaten, on which water comes, it shall be unclean; and every drink that is drunk in any vessel shall be unclean." Whether the vessel of v33 is meant is left open.
- **"ranges for pots" → "stove"** (v35) — Hebrew כִּירַיִם, a dual found only here: a hearth for two pots; the exact sense is uncertain.
- **"a fountain or pit, wherein there is plenty of water" → "a spring and a pit, a gathering of waters"** (v36) — Hebrew מַעְיָן וּבוֹר מִקְוֵה־מַיִם; מִקְוֵה is the "gathering" of Gen 1:10. **"that which toucheth" → "he who touches"** — Hebrew וְנֹגֵעַ, "one touching": a person or a thing.
- **"any sowing seed which is to be sown"** (v37) — Hebrew זֶרַע זֵרוּעַ אֲשֶׁר יִזָּרֵעַ, three words of one root.
- **"of which ye may eat" → "that are yours for food"** (v39) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־הִיא לָכֶם לְאָכְלָה.
- **"goeth upon the belly"** (v42) — the tool that prints the Hebrew gives the word after עַל־ as a single letter (the large ו of גָּחוֹן); "belly", as Gen 3:14, is taken from the traditional text. **"hath more feet" → "has many feet"** — Hebrew מַרְבֵּה רַגְלַיִם, "multiplying feet".
- **"Ye shall not make yourselves abominable" → "Do not make your souls detestable"** (v43) — Hebrew אַל־תְּשַׁקְּצוּ אֶת־נַפְשֹׁתֵיכֶם; נֶפֶשׁ is "soul". The first clause has אַל ("do not"), the second לֹא ("you shall not"). **"neither shall ye make yourselves unclean with them, that ye should be defiled thereby" → "you shall not defile yourselves by them and be defiled by them"** — Hebrew וְלֹא תִטַּמְּאוּ בָּהֶם וְנִטְמֵתֶם בָּם, the reflexive and the passive of one root, that of "unclean".
- **"sanctify yourselves"** (v44) — Hebrew וְהִתְקַדִּשְׁתֶּם, of the root of "holy". **"defile yourselves" → "defile your souls"** — Hebrew תְטַמְּאוּ אֶת־נַפְשֹׁתֵיכֶם, literally "make your souls unclean": the root of "unclean" in this chapter.
- **"that bringeth you up" → "who brought you up"** (v45) — Hebrew הַמַּעֲלֶה, a participle: "the one bringing you up". **"to be your God" → "to be Elohim to you"** — Hebrew לִהְיֹת לָכֶם לֵאלֹהִים.
- **"the law" → "the torah"** (v46) — Hebrew תּוֹרַת, the instruction for one matter. **"every living creature that moveth in the waters" → "every living soul that creeps in the waters"** — Hebrew הָרֹמֶשֶׂת, as Gen 1:21.
- **"To make a difference between" → "to separate … from"** (v47) — Hebrew לְהַבְדִּיל בֵּין … וּבֵין, literally "to separate between the unclean and between the clean", the verb of Gen 1:4.

### Notes
- **Dietary laws' logic** — scholars have long debated: hygiene? symbolism? anti-idolatry? The text itself gives holiness (v44–45) as the reason — boundary-marking for Israel as Yahweh's consecrated people.
- **Clean / unclean grid** — land animals (hoof with a cleft + cud), water creatures (fins + scales), birds (by list, mostly carrion/predator = unclean), swarming insects (leaping legs = clean). Each realm has its "proper" locomotion; creatures that cross categories are anomalous.
- **Carcass uncleanness** (v24–40) — touching a carcass renders unclean until evening; carrying one requires the washing of garments. An earthen vessel cannot be made clean — it must be broken.
- **"You shall be holy, for I am holy"** (v44, 45) — key refrain of the Holiness Code (Lev 17–26), previewed here. Holiness = imitatio Dei and separation.
- **Abolition in NT** — Acts 10:15 (Peter's vision) and Mark 7:19 signal abrogation of these dietary laws in the new covenant.
