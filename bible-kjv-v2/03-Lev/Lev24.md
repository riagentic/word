# Leviticus 24

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Command the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, that they take to you pure oil of olives, beaten, for the light, to set up a lamp continually.

[3] Outside the veil of the testimony, in the tent of meeting, ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall arrange it from evening until morning before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ continually: an everlasting statute for your generations.

[4] On the pure lampstand he shall arrange the lamps before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ continually.

[5] And you shall take fine flour and bake it into twelve cakes; two tenths shall the one cake be.

[6] And you shall put them in two arrangements, six to the arrangement, on the pure table before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] And you shall place on the arrangement pure frankincense, and it shall be for the bread as a memorial portion, an offering by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[8] On the day of the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, on the day of the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, he shall arrange it before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ continually, from the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: an everlasting covenant.

[9] And it shall be for ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and for his sons, and they shall eat it in a holy place, for it is most holy to him from the offerings by fire of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: an everlasting statute."

[10] And the son of ⟦israelite|a Yisraeli|an Israelite⟧ woman — and he was the son of ⟦egyptian|a Mitzri|an Egyptian⟧ man — went out in the midst of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and they struggled in the camp, the son of the ⟦israelite|Yisraeli|Israelite⟧ woman and the ⟦israelite|Yisraeli|Israelite⟧ man.

[11] And the son of the ⟦israelite|Yisraeli|Israelite⟧ woman pronounced the Name and cursed; and they brought him to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧. And the name of his mother was ⟦shelomith|Shelomit|Shelomith⟧, the daughter of ⟦dibri|Divri|Dibri⟧, of the tribe of Dan.

[12] And they put him in custody, to have it declared distinctly to them at the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[14] "Bring out the one who cursed, to the outside of the camp; and all who heard shall lay their hands on his head, and all the congregation shall stone him.

[15] And to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ you shall speak, saying: 'Any man, when he curses his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, shall bear his sin.

[16] And he who pronounces the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall surely be put to death; all the congregation shall surely stone him. As the sojourner, so the native: when he pronounces the Name, he shall be put to death.

[17] And a man, when he strikes down any soul of man, shall surely be put to death.

[23] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ spoke to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they brought out the one who cursed, to the outside of the camp, and they stoned him with stone. And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ did as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"**, **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — the Hebrew names kept.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v2, 8, 10, 15, 23) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"that they bring unto thee pure oil olive beaten for the light, to cause the lamps to burn continually" → "that they take to you pure oil of olives, beaten, for the light, to set up a lamp continually"** (v2) — the same Hebrew as Exod 27:20 and the same English: לָקַח "take"; נֵר is singular; לְהַעֲלֹת is literally "to make go up" (set the lamp up on the lampstand, or make its flame go up, light it). זָךְ "pure" (v2, 7) is another word than טָהוֹר "pure", literally "clean" (v4, 6).
- **"Without the vail of the testimony … shall Aaron order it" → "Outside the veil of the testimony … Aaron shall arrange it"** (v3, 4, 8) — Hebrew עָרַךְ "arrange", as Exod 27:21. **"a statute for ever in your generations" → "an everlasting statute for your generations"** — Hebrew חֻקַּת עוֹלָם, literally "a statute of the age".
- **"candlestick" → "lampstand"** (v4) — Hebrew מְנוֹרָה, a stand for oil lamps.
- **"two tenth deals shall be in one cake" → "two tenths shall the one cake be"** (v5) — Hebrew עֶשְׂרֹנִים "tenths"; the measure (the ephah) is not named. חַלָּה is a pierced or ring-shaped loaf. "Bake it, twelve cakes" is the literal form.
- **"in two rows, six on a row" → "in two arrangements, six to the arrangement"** (v6, 7) — Hebrew מַעֲרָכָה / מַעֲרֶכֶת, the noun of עָרַךְ "arrange" (v3, 4, 8; Exod 40:23): whether rows or piles is not said. In v7 the singular "on the arrangement" stands for each of the two.
- **"that it may be on the bread for a memorial" → "and it shall be for the bread as a memorial portion"** (v7) — Hebrew וְהָיְתָה לַלֶּחֶם לְאַזְכָּרָה: the frankincense, not the bread, is the part given to Yahweh. אַזְכָּרָה is from זָכַר "remember". **"an offering made by fire" → "an offering by fire"** (v7, 9) — Hebrew אִשֶּׁה, commonly derived from אֵשׁ "fire"; the derivation is not certain. The bread is nowhere named here "bread of the face" (Exod 25:30); the Hebrew says only "the bread".
- **"Every sabbath" → "On the day of the Shabbat, on the day of the Shabbat"** (v8) — the Hebrew repeats the phrase (= Shabbat by Shabbat). **"being taken from the children of Israel by an everlasting covenant" → "from the sons of Israel: an everlasting covenant"** — "being taken" and "by" are not in the Hebrew; בְּרִית עוֹלָם is literally "a covenant of the age".
- **"And it shall be Aaron's and his sons'" → "And it shall be for Aaron and for his sons"** (v9) — the verb is feminine (וְהָיְתָה), the pronouns after it masculine ("eat it", "it is"). **"most holy"** — literally "holiness of holinesses". **"by a perpetual statute" → "an everlasting statute"** — Hebrew חָק־עוֹלָם, the due laid down for the priests (Exod 29:28).
- **"whose father was an Egyptian" → "and he was the son of an Egyptian man"** (v10) — word for word. **"strove together" → "struggled"** — Hebrew נָצָה nifal, a fight with the hands (Exod 21:22). **"a man of Israel" → "the Israelite man"** — Hebrew אִישׁ הַיִּשְׂרְאֵלִי.
- **"blasphemed the name of the LORD, and cursed" → "pronounced the Name and cursed"** (v11, 16) — two verbs, kept apart. וַיִּקֹּב, v16 נֹקֵב, is from נָקַב "pierce, bore", then "mark out, name distinctly" (Num 1:17; Isa 62:2); "blaspheme" is not in the word. The form in v11 could also be read from קָבַב "curse" (Num 23:8); v16 has נָקַב plainly. "Of the LORD" is not in the Hebrew of v11: it says "the Name". קִלֵּל is "make light of, curse" (the root "be light"), another word than אָרַר. Whether the pronouncing alone or the pronouncing with a curse is the capital act (v16) the words do not decide.
- **"that the mind of the LORD might be shewed them" → "to have it declared distinctly to them at the mouth of Yahweh"** (v12) — Hebrew לִפְרֹשׁ לָהֶם עַל־פִּי יְהוָה: an active infinitive with no subject, "to make distinct for them", and "at the mouth of Yahweh". "Put" is הִנִּיחַ "set down, lay up" (Exod 16:23).
- **"without the camp" → "to the outside of the camp"** (v14, 23) — Hebrew אֶל־מִחוּץ לַמַּחֲנֶה. **"all that heard him" → "all who heard"** (v14) — no object in the Hebrew. **"stone"** (v14, 16, 23) — Hebrew רָגַם, another word than סָקַל "stone" (Exod 19:13); English has one word. In v23 "with stone" is the singular אָבֶן.
- **"Whosoever curseth" → "Any man, when he curses"** (v15) — Hebrew אִישׁ אִישׁ כִּי, literally "a man, a man, when". אֱלֹהָיו "his Elohim" can also be read "his gods".
- **"shall surely be put to death … shall certainly stone him"** (v16, 17) — Hebrew מוֹת יוּמָת, literally "dying he shall be made to die", and רָגוֹם יִרְגְּמוּ "stoning they shall stone": both infinitives rendered "surely".
- **"as well the stranger, as he that is born in the land" → "As the sojourner, so the native"** (v16, 22) — Hebrew כַּגֵּר כָּאֶזְרָח, "like the sojourner, like the native". **"when he blasphemeth the name of the LORD" → "when he pronounces the Name"** (v16) — Hebrew בְּנָקְבוֹ־שֵׁם: "of the LORD" is not in the Hebrew, and שֵׁם stands without the article ("a name"); the Septuagint has "the name of the Lord" (from memory).
- **"he that killeth any man" → "a man, when he strikes down any soul of man"** (v17) — Hebrew יַכֶּה כָּל־נֶפֶשׁ אָדָם: הִכָּה is "strike" (here a fatal blow, v21), נֶפֶשׁ "soul", אָדָם "man, mankind". So v18 "the soul of a beast … soul for soul" (נֶפֶשׁ תַּחַת נָפֶשׁ, as Exod 21:23); v21 has no "soul".
- **"shall make it good" / "shall restore it" → "shall repay it"** (v18, 21) — Hebrew שִׁלֵּם, one verb, as Exod 21:34.
- **"cause a blemish in his neighbour" → "puts a blemish in his fellow"** (v19, 20) — Hebrew נָתַן מוּם בְּ, literally "gives a blemish in"; in v20 "so shall it be given in him". עָמִית is not רֵעַ "neighbor": a fellow member of one's people (a word almost only in Leviticus).
- **"Breach for breach" → "Fracture for fracture"** (v20) — Hebrew שֶׁבֶר, from שָׁבַר "break". "For" is תַּחַת "in the place of".
- **"one manner of law" → "One judgment"** (v22) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּט אֶחָד; "law" would be תּוֹרָה (Exod 12:49).

### Notes

- **Two standing duties** (v1–9) — the lamp (Exod 27:20–21) and the bread on the table, renewed every Shabbat and eaten by the priests (Exod 25:30).
- **The bread** — David asks for it and eats it (1 Sam 21:1–6), which Jesus cites (Mark 2:25–26).
- **The man who cursed** (v10–23) — one of the few narratives in Leviticus: a case with no ruling, kept until Yahweh rules (as Num 15:32–36); general laws follow.
- **The Name** (v11, 16) — later Jewish usage says "the Name" (*ha-Shem*) for the Tetragrammaton; the reading of v16 as a ban on pronouncing it at all is already in the Septuagint (from memory: "he who names the name of the Lord").
- **Eye for eye** (v19–20) — the same rule in Exod 21:23–25 and Deut 19:21; cited in Matt 5:38.
- **One judgment** (v22) — the sojourner and the native stand under the same ruling.
