# Leviticus 23

[1] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[2] "Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them: 'The appointed feasts of Yahweh, which you shall proclaim as holy convocations — these are my appointed feasts.

[3] Six days shall work be done, but the seventh day is a sabbath of solemn rest, a holy convocation; you shall do no work. It is a sabbath to Yahweh in all your dwellings.

[4] These are the appointed feasts of Yahweh, holy convocations, which you shall proclaim at their appointed times.

[5] In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month at twilight, is Yahweh's Passover.

[6] On the fifteenth day of the same month is the Feast of Unleavened Bread to Yahweh; seven days you shall eat unleavened bread.

[7] On the first day you shall have a holy convocation; you shall do no laborious work.

[8] You shall present an offering made by fire to Yahweh for seven days; on the seventh day is a holy convocation; you shall do no laborious work.'"

[9] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[10] "Speak to the children of Israel and say to them: 'When you come into the land that I am giving you and reap its harvest, you shall bring the sheaf of the firstfruits of your harvest to the priest.

[11] He shall elevate the sheaf before Yahweh, to be accepted on your behalf; on the day after the sabbath the priest shall elevate it.

[12] On the day when you elevate the sheaf, you shall offer a year-old lamb without blemish as a burnt offering to Yahweh.

[13] Its grain offering shall be two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil, an offering made by fire to Yahweh for a pleasing aroma; and its drink offering shall be wine, a quarter of a hin.

[14] You shall eat neither bread, nor roasted grain, nor fresh ears, until the very day when you bring the offering of your *Elohim*. It shall be a perpetual statute throughout your generations in all your dwellings.

[15] You shall count for yourselves from the day after the sabbath — from the day you brought the sheaf of the elevation offering — seven sabbaths; they shall be complete.

[16] You shall count fifty days, up to the day after the seventh sabbath, and you shall present a new grain offering to Yahweh.

[17] You shall bring from your dwellings two loaves as an elevation offering; they shall be of two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour, baked with leaven; they are the firstfruits to Yahweh.

[18] With the bread you shall offer seven year-old lambs without blemish, one young bull, and two rams; they shall be a burnt offering to Yahweh, with their grain offerings and their drink offerings, an offering made by fire of pleasing aroma to Yahweh.

[19] You shall also offer one male goat for a sin offering and two year-old lambs for a sacrifice of peace offerings.

[20] The priest shall elevate them together with the bread of the firstfruits as an elevation offering before Yahweh, with the two lambs; they shall be holy to Yahweh for the priest.

[21] On that same day you shall proclaim a holy convocation for yourselves; you shall do no laborious work. It shall be a perpetual statute in all your dwellings throughout your generations.

[22] When you reap the harvest of your land, you shall not reap to the very edges of your field, nor gather the gleanings of your harvest; you shall leave them for the poor and for the foreigner. I am Yahweh your *Elohim*.'"

[23] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[24] "Speak to the children of Israel, saying: 'In the seventh month, on the first day of the month, you shall have a sabbath-rest, a memorial proclaimed with trumpet-blasts, a holy convocation.

[25] You shall do no laborious work, and you shall present an offering made by fire to Yahweh.'"

[26] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[27] "But on the tenth day of this seventh month is the Day of Atonement; it shall be a holy convocation for you, and you shall afflict yourselves and present an offering made by fire to Yahweh.

[28] You shall do no work on that same day, for it is a Day of Atonement, to make atonement for you before Yahweh your *Elohim*.

[29] Any person who does not afflict himself on that same day shall be cut off from his people.

[30] Any person who does any work on that same day — that person I will destroy from among his people.

[31] You shall do no work at all; it is a perpetual statute throughout your generations in all your dwellings.

[32] It shall be to you a sabbath of solemn rest, and you shall afflict yourselves. On the ninth day of the month at evening, from evening to evening, you shall keep your sabbath."

[33] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[34] "Speak to the children of Israel, saying: 'On the fifteenth day of this seventh month is the Feast of Booths for seven days to Yahweh.

[35] On the first day there shall be a holy convocation; you shall do no laborious work.

[36] Seven days you shall present an offering made by fire to Yahweh. On the eighth day there shall be a holy convocation for you, and you shall present an offering made by fire to Yahweh. It is a solemn assembly; you shall do no laborious work.

[37] These are the appointed feasts of Yahweh, which you shall proclaim as holy convocations to present an offering made by fire to Yahweh — a burnt offering and a grain offering, a sacrifice and drink offerings, each on its proper day —

[38] besides the sabbaths of Yahweh, and besides your gifts, and besides all your vows, and besides all your freewill offerings that you give to Yahweh.

[39] Yet on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, when you have gathered in the produce of the land, you shall celebrate the feast of Yahweh for seven days; on the first day there shall be a sabbath-rest, and on the eighth day a sabbath-rest.

[40] On the first day you shall take for yourselves the fruit of majestic trees, branches of palm trees, boughs of leafy trees, and willows of the brook; and you shall rejoice before Yahweh your *Elohim* for seven days.

[41] You shall celebrate it as a feast to Yahweh for seven days in the year; it is a perpetual statute throughout your generations; you shall celebrate it in the seventh month.

[42] You shall dwell in booths for seven days; all who are native-born in Israel shall dwell in booths,

[43] so that your generations may know that I made the children of Israel dwell in booths when I brought them out of the land of Egypt. I am Yahweh your *Elohim*.'"

[44] So Moses declared to the children of Israel the appointed feasts of Yahweh.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"feasts" → "appointed feasts"** (throughout) — Hebrew מוֹעֲדִים *mo'adim* = appointed times / festivals. Scheduled sacred times, not merely feasts.
- **"servile work" → "laborious work"** (v7, 8, 21, 25, 35, 36) — Hebrew מְלֶאכֶת עֲבֹדָה *mele'khet avodah* = occupational / laboring work. Distinct from "any work" on Yom Kippur (v28, 31).
- **"feast of tabernacles" → "Feast of Booths"** (v34) — Hebrew סֻכּוֹת *Sukkot*. *Sukkah* = temporary hut/booth, not permanent tent/tabernacle.
- **"wave offering" → "elevation offering"** (v11, 15, 17, 20) — as elsewhere.
- **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** (v13, 16, 18, 37) — as elsewhere.
- **"parched corn" → "roasted grain"** (v14).
- **"blowing of trumpets" → "trumpet-blasts"** (v24) — Hebrew תְּרוּעָה *teru'ah* = blast/shout. Trumpet later taken as *shofar*. Jewish New Year (Rosh Hashanah).
- **"afflict your souls" → "afflict yourselves"** (v27, 29, 32) — as in ch 16.
- **"tenth deals" → "tenths of an ephah"** (v13, 17) — archaic measure term.
- **"boughs of goodly trees" → "fruit of majestic trees"** (v40) — Hebrew פְּרִי עֵץ הָדָר *peri etz hadar* = the fruit of splendid trees. Rabbinic tradition identifies as *etrog* / citron.

### Notes
- **Israel's liturgical calendar** — the seven annual holy seasons: Sabbath, Passover/Unleavened Bread, Firstfruits, Weeks (Pentecost), Trumpets, Atonement, Booths. Each keyed to the agricultural cycle and salvation history.
- **Passover + Unleavened Bread** — Passover begins 14th Nisan evening; Unleavened Bread days 15–21. Commemorates exodus deliverance.
- **Firstfruits and Weeks (Shavuot)** — firstfruits of barley harvest in spring; fifty days later (Pentecost), wheat-harvest offering. NT Pentecost descends on this seventh-sabbath-plus-one day (Acts 2).
- **Trumpets, Atonement, Booths** — the seventh-month cluster: Rosh Hashanah (New Year / awakening), Yom Kippur (purification), Sukkot (tabernacles / harvest ingathering + wilderness memory).
- **Booths** (v42–43) — dwelling in temporary shelters recalls exodus. Both agricultural (harvest-hut) and historical (wilderness tent) significance.
- **Sukkot and John 7** — Jesus attended this feast (Jn 7:2, 37); his "rivers of living water" saying occurs at Sukkot water-drawing ritual.
- **Pentecost & Weeks** — Later Jewish tradition connects Shavuot with the giving of the Torah at Sinai (50 days after exodus).
