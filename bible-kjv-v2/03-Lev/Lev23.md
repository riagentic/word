## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"feasts" → "appointed feasts"** (throughout) — Hebrew מוֹעֲדִים *mo'adim* = appointed times / festivals. Scheduled sacred times, not merely feasts.
- **"servile work" → "laborious work"** (v7, 8, 21, 25, 35, 36) — Hebrew מְלֶאכֶת עֲבֹדָה *mele'khet avodah* = occupational / laboring work. Distinct from "any work" on Yom Kippur (v28, 31).
- **"feast of tabernacles" → "Feast of Booths"** (v34) — Hebrew סֻכּוֹת *Sukkot*. *Sukkah* = temporary hut/booth, not permanent tent/tabernacle.
- **"wave offering" → "elevation offering"** (v11, 15, 17, 20) — as elsewhere.
- **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** (v13, 16, 18, 37) — as elsewhere.
- **"parched corn" → "roasted grain"** (v14).
- **"blowing of trumpets" → "trumpet-blasts"** (v24) — Hebrew תְּרוּעָה *teru'ah* = blast/shout. Trumpet later taken as *shofar*. Jewish New Year (Rosh Hashanah).
- **"afflict your souls" → "afflict yourselves"** (v27, 29, 32) — as in ch 16.
- **"tenth deals" → "tenths of an ephah"** (v13, 17) — archaic measure term.
- **"boughs of goodly trees" → "fruit of majestic trees"** (v40) — Hebrew פְּרִי עֵץ הָדָר *peri etz hadar* = the fruit of splendid trees. Rabbinic tradition identifies as *etrog* / citron.

### Notes
- **Israel's liturgical calendar** — the seven annual holy seasons: Sabbath, Passover/Unleavened Bread, Firstfruits, Weeks (Pentecost), Trumpets, Atonement, Booths. Each keyed to the agricultural cycle and salvation history.
- **Passover + Unleavened Bread** — Passover begins 14th Nisan evening; Unleavened Bread days 15–21. Commemorates exodus deliverance.
- **Firstfruits and Weeks (Shavuot)** — firstfruits of barley harvest in spring; fifty days later (Pentecost), wheat-harvest offering. NT Pentecost descends on this seventh-sabbath-plus-one day (Acts 2).
- **Trumpets, Atonement, Booths** — the seventh-month cluster: Rosh Hashanah (New Year / awakening), Yom Kippur (purification), Sukkot (tabernacles / harvest ingathering + wilderness memory).
- **Booths** (v42–43) — dwelling in temporary shelters recalls exodus. Both agricultural (harvest-hut) and historical (wilderness tent) significance.
- **Sukkot and John 7** — Jesus attended this feast (Jn 7:2, 37); his "rivers of living water" saying occurs at Sukkot water-drawing ritual.
- **Pentecost & Weeks** — Later Jewish tradition connects Shavuot with the giving of the Torah at Sinai (50 days after exodus).
