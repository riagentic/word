# Leviticus 19

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to all the congregation of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and say to them: 'You shall be holy, for I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, am holy.

[3] You shall fear, each man, his mother and his father, and my ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ you shall keep. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[4] Do not turn to the worthless idols, and molten gods you shall not make for yourselves. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[5] And when you sacrifice a sacrifice of peace offerings to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, that you may be found pleasing you shall sacrifice it.

[6] On the day of your sacrifice it shall be eaten, and on the morrow; and what remains until the third day shall be burned with fire.

[7] And if it is surely eaten on the third day, it is a foul thing; it shall not be found pleasing.

[8] And he who eats it shall bear his iniquity, for he has profaned the holy thing of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and that soul shall be cut off from its peoples.

[9] And when you reap the harvest of your land, you shall not finish the edge of your field in reaping, and the gleanings of your harvest you shall not gather.

[10] And your vineyard you shall not glean, and the fallen grapes of your vineyard you shall not gather; for the afflicted and for the sojourner you shall leave them. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[11] You shall not steal, and you shall not lie, and you shall not deal falsely, each man to his fellow.

[12] And you shall not swear by my name for falsehood, and so profane the name of your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] You shall not extort from your neighbor, and you shall not rob. The wage of a hired man shall not lodge with you until morning.

[14] You shall not curse a deaf man, and before a blind man you shall not put a stumbling block; and you shall fear your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[15] You shall do no injustice in judgment: you shall not lift up the face of the poor, and you shall not show deference to the face of the great; in righteousness you shall judge your fellow.

[16] You shall not go about as a talebearer among your peoples; you shall not stand against the blood of your neighbor. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[17] You shall not hate your brother in your heart. You shall surely reprove your fellow, and you shall not bear sin because of him.

[18] You shall not take vengeance, and you shall not keep anger against the sons of your people; and you shall love your neighbor as yourself. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[19] My statutes you shall keep. Your beasts you shall not let mate in two kinds; your field you shall not sow with two kinds; and a garment of two kinds, of mixed stuff, shall not come up upon you.

[20] And a man, when he lies with a woman with an emission of seed, and she is a maidservant assigned to a man, and she has surely not been ransomed, or freedom has not been given to her: there shall be an inquiry; they shall not be put to death, for she was not freed.

[21] And he shall bring his guilt offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to the entrance of the tent of meeting: a ram of guilt offering.

[22] And the priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of the guilt offering before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for his sin that he has sinned; and it shall be pardoned him, for his sin that he has sinned.

[23] And when you come into the land and plant any tree for food, you shall regard its fruit as its foreskin; three years it shall be uncircumcised to you; it shall not be eaten.

[24] And in the fourth year all its fruit shall be a holy thing of praise to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[25] And in the fifth year you shall eat its fruit, to increase its yield for you. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[26] You shall not eat with the blood. You shall not divine, and you shall not practice soothsaying.

[27] You shall not round off the edge of your head, and you shall not ruin the edge of your beard.

[28] And a cut for a soul you shall not put in your flesh, and the writing of a tattoo you shall not put on yourselves. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[29] Do not profane your daughter, to make her play the prostitute, that the land may not play the prostitute and the land be filled with depravity.

[30] My ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ you shall keep, and my sanctuary you shall fear. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[31] Do not turn to the ghosts and to the knowing spirits; do not seek them out, to become unclean by them. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[32] Before gray hair you shall rise, and you shall show deference to the face of an old man; and you shall fear your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[33] And when a sojourner sojourns with you in your land, you shall not wrong him.

[34] As a native among you shall the sojourner who sojourns with you be to you, and you shall love him as yourself, for you were sojourners in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[35] You shall do no injustice in judgment, in measure, in weight, or in capacity.

[36] Righteous balances, righteous weights, a righteous ephah and a righteous hin you shall have. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who brought you out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[37] And you shall keep all my statutes and all my ordinances, and do them. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (v1, 5, 7–10, 12, 20–25, 28, 33, 37) — the Hebrew וַ / וְ "and" at the head of a clause is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל. **"congregation"** (v2) — Hebrew עֵדָה, the gathered body of Israel.
- **"Ye shall fear every man his mother, and his father" → "You shall fear, each man, his mother and his father"** (v3) — Hebrew אִישׁ אִמּוֹ וְאָבִיו תִּירָאוּ: יָרֵא is "fear", the same verb as "fear your Elohim" (v14, 32) and "fear my sanctuary" (v30). The verb is plural, "each man" singular. The mother stands first.
- **"idols" → "the worthless idols"** (v4) — Hebrew הָאֱלִילִים, a word of contempt for other gods and their images: "things of nothing, worthless things" (the form plays on אֵל / אֱלֹהִים); one Hebrew word, as in 26:1. **"molten gods"** kept — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי מַסֵּכָה, gods of poured metal (Exod 34:17).
- **"ye shall offer it at your own will" → "that you may be found pleasing you shall sacrifice it"** (v5) — Hebrew לִרְצֹנְכֶם, literally "for your acceptance, for pleasure in you": that you may be received with pleasure (so v7 "it shall not be found pleasing", the verb of the same root; 1:3, 4). It can also be read "at your own good pleasure" (so the KJV). **"offer" → "sacrifice"** — Hebrew זָבַח, the verb of זֶבַח "sacrifice", three times.
- **"the same day ye offer it" → "On the day of your sacrifice"** (v6) — Hebrew בְּיוֹם זִבְחֲכֶם, a noun. **"if ought remain" → "what remains"** — Hebrew וְהַנּוֹתָר.
- **"if it be eaten at all" → "if it is surely eaten"** (v7) — Hebrew הֵאָכֹל יֵאָכֵל, literally "being eaten it is eaten": the verb is doubled for stress. **"abominable" → "a foul thing"** — Hebrew פִּגּוּל, a word used only of sacrificial flesh kept too long (7:18; Isa 65:4; Ezek 4:14); the exact sense is not certain. It is not the word "abomination" (תּוֹעֵבָה).
- **"every one that eateth it" → "he who eats it"** (v8) — the Hebrew has a plural participle with a singular verb ("its eaters, he shall bear"). **"the hallowed thing of the LORD" → "the holy thing of Yahweh"** — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ יְהוָה. **"that soul shall be cut off from among his people" → "from its peoples"** — Hebrew מֵעַמֶּיהָ, a plural: his kinsfolk. נֶפֶשׁ "soul" is the person; the word is feminine in Hebrew ("from her peoples").
- **"thou shalt not wholly reap the corners of thy field" → "you shall not finish the edge of your field in reaping"** (v9) — Hebrew לֹא תְכַלֶּה פְּאַת שָׂדְךָ לִקְצֹר, literally "you shall not finish the edge of your field to reap". פֵּאָה is the edge or side (the same word in v27). "You reap" is plural, the rest of v9–10 singular. **"gleanings … gather"** — Hebrew לֶקֶט and לִקֵּט, noun and verb of one root: what is picked up from the ground.
- **"thou shalt not glean thy vineyard"** kept (v10) — Hebrew עוֹלֵל, to go over the vines a second time (another verb than "gather"). **"every grape" → "the fallen grapes"** — Hebrew פֶּרֶט, found only here: what drops off; the sense is fairly sure. **"the poor and stranger" → "the afflicted and the sojourner"** — Hebrew עָנִי, the poor man bowed down (another word than "the poor" of v15), and גֵּר, one who lives among a people not his own.
- **"neither deal falsely, neither lie one to another" → "and you shall not lie, and you shall not deal falsely, each man to his fellow"** (v11) — two verbs: כִּחֵשׁ, to lie to a person by denying what is true (as in 6:2, 3), and שִׁקֵּר, the verb of שֶׁקֶר "falsehood" (v12). **"one to another" → "each man to his fellow"** — Hebrew אִישׁ בַּעֲמִיתוֹ. עָמִית (v11, 15, 17) is a member of one's own people, found almost only in Leviticus; it is kept apart from רֵעַ "neighbor" (v13, 16, 18).
- **"swear by my name falsely, neither shalt thou profane" → "swear by my name for falsehood, and so profane"** (v12) — Hebrew לַשָּׁקֶר "to falsehood"; וְחִלַּלְתָּ is not a second prohibition but the result: "and you would profane". "You shall not swear" is plural, "you would profane" singular.
- **"defraud … rob him" → "extort from … rob"** (v13) — Hebrew עָשַׁק, to press a person down by keeping or wringing from him what is his (wages, a pledge; 6:2, 4), and גָּזַל, to take by force; "him" is not in the Hebrew. **"the wages of him that is hired shall not abide with thee all night" → "The wage of a hired man shall not lodge with you"** — Hebrew פְּעֻלָּה, literally "work", here what the work has earned (not the usual word for "wages", שָׂכָר); לִין is "lodge, spend the night"; "all night" is not a separate word.
- **"but shalt fear thy God" → "and you shall fear your Elohim"** (v14, 32) — Hebrew וְיָרֵאתָ מֵּאֱלֹהֶיךָ, literally "and you shall be afraid from your Elohim". **"curse"** — Hebrew קִלֵּל, literally "make light of".
- **"no unrighteousness" → "no injustice"** (v15, 35) — Hebrew עָוֶל, wrong, perversion of right. **"respect the person of the poor, nor honour the person of the mighty" → "lift up the face of the poor … show deference to the face of the great"** — Hebrew נָשָׂא פָּנִים "lift up the face" (show regard to) and הָדַר פָּנִים (the same verb in v32); גָּדוֹל is "great". **"thy neighbour" → "your fellow"** — עָמִית.
- **"go up and down as a talebearer among thy people" → "go about as a talebearer among your peoples"** (v16) — Hebrew לֹא־תֵלֵךְ רָכִיל, literally "you shall not go a talebearer": רָכִיל is one who carries talk about like a trader his wares; a slanderer. עַמֶּיךָ is plural.
- **"stand against the blood of thy neighbour"** kept (v16) — Hebrew לֹא תַעֲמֹד עַל־דַּם רֵעֶךָ. עַל is "upon, by, against". What is forbidden is not certain: to rise against his life (in court, with the talebearer of the first half), or to stand by while his blood is shed, or to make a living at the cost of his blood.
- **"thou shalt in any wise rebuke thy neighbour, and not suffer sin upon him" → "You shall surely reprove your fellow, and you shall not bear sin because of him"** (v17) — Hebrew הוֹכֵחַ תּוֹכִיחַ, literally "reproving you shall reprove". וְלֹא־תִשָּׂא עָלָיו חֵטְא, literally "and you shall not lift / bear sin upon him": either "and so not bear sin on his account", or "and not lay sin upon him".
- **"nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people" → "you shall not keep anger against the sons of your people"** (v18) — Hebrew נָטַר, "keep, guard", with no object: to keep one's anger (Jer 3:5, 12; Nah 1:2; Ps 103:9). "Anger" is supplied. "Bear a grudge" is another verb (Gen 27:41).
- **"but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself" → "and you shall love your neighbor as yourself"** (v18) — Hebrew וְאָהַבְתָּ לְרֵעֲךָ כָּמוֹךָ. The Hebrew has "and", and the preposition לְ before "your neighbor" (so also v34 "love him"; elsewhere the verb takes a plain object); the sense is not changed. רֵעַ is the fellow man one has to do with; here it stands beside "your brother", "your fellow", "the sons of your people" (v17–18), and v34 says the same of the sojourner. Quoted in Matt 5:43; 19:19; 22:39; Mark 12:31; Luke 10:27; Rom 13:9; Gal 5:14; Jas 2:8, where the Greek has the same words.
- **"Thou shalt not let thy cattle gender with a diverse kind … mingled seed … a garment mingled of linen and woollen" → "Your beasts you shall not let mate in two kinds … with two kinds … a garment of two kinds, of mixed stuff"** (v19) — Hebrew כִּלְאַיִם, a dual, "two kinds", three times. בְּהֵמָה is "beast" (a collective); הִרְבִּיעַ is the causative of רָבַע "lie down" (to mate; 18:23; 20:16). "Seed" is not in the Hebrew. שַׁעַטְנֵז is a word of unknown origin, found only here and in Deut 22:11, where it is explained as "wool and linen together"; "linen and woollen" is taken from there. "Come up upon you" is literal.
- **"lieth carnally with a woman" → "lies with a woman with an emission of seed"** (v20) — Hebrew שִׁכְבַת־זֶרַע, literally "a lying of seed" (15:16–18; 22:4). **"a bondmaid, betrothed to an husband" → "a maidservant assigned to a man"** — Hebrew שִׁפְחָה, and נֶחֱרֶפֶת לְאִישׁ: the verb is found only here in this sense and its meaning is not certain (promised, pledged, assigned to a man); it is not the usual word "betrothed" (אֵרַשׂ). **"not at all redeemed" → "surely not been ransomed"** — Hebrew וְהָפְדֵּה לֹא נִפְדָּתָה, literally "and being ransomed she has not been ransomed"; פָּדָה is "ransom".
- **"she shall be scourged" → "there shall be an inquiry"** (v20) — Hebrew בִּקֹּרֶת תִּהְיֶה. The noun is found only here; it belongs to בִּקֵּר "look into, examine" (13:36; 27:33). The sense is not certain: an inquiry, or a penalty that follows it (so the KJV with later Jewish tradition; others, "damages"). The Hebrew does not say "she". **"because she was not free" → "for she was not freed"** — Hebrew חֻפָּשָׁה, a passive verb, of one root with חֻפְשָׁה "freedom".
- **"trespass offering" → "guilt offering"** (v21, 22) — Hebrew אָשָׁם, "guilt". **"the door of the tabernacle of the congregation" → "the entrance of the tent of meeting"** — Hebrew פֶּתַח אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד.
- **"the sin which he hath done shall be forgiven him" → "it shall be pardoned him, for his sin that he has sinned"** (v22) — Hebrew וְנִסְלַח לוֹ מֵחַטָּאתוֹ אֲשֶׁר חָטָא, literally "and it shall be pardoned to him, from his sin that he sinned". סָלַח is said only of God. "Make atonement" is כִּפֶּר (literally "cover").
- **"ye shall count the fruit thereof as uncircumcised" → "you shall regard its fruit as its foreskin"** (v23) — Hebrew וַעֲרַלְתֶּם עָרְלָתוֹ אֶת־פִּרְיוֹ, literally "you shall foreskin its foreskin, its fruit": a verb made from עָרְלָה "foreskin". **"uncircumcised"** — Hebrew עֲרֵלִים, a plural.
- **"holy to praise the LORD withal" → "a holy thing of praise to Yahweh"** (v24) — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ הִלּוּלִים לַיהוָה, literally "a holiness of praises to Yahweh". הִלּוּלִים is found only here and in Judg 9:27 (a feast of rejoicing at the vintage).
- **"that it may yield unto you the increase thereof" → "to increase its yield for you"** (v25) — Hebrew לְהוֹסִיף לָכֶם תְּבוּאָתוֹ, literally "to add to you its yield".
- **"Ye shall not eat any thing with the blood" → "You shall not eat with the blood"** (v26) — Hebrew לֹא תֹאכְלוּ עַל־הַדָּם, literally "upon / over the blood"; "any thing" is not in the Hebrew. Either flesh with its blood still in it (1 Sam 14:32–34) or a meal held over blood (a rite of divination) may be meant. The Greek reads "upon the mountains" (cited from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"neither shall ye use enchantment, nor observe times" → "You shall not divine, and you shall not practice soothsaying"** (v26) — Hebrew נִחֵשׁ, to seek omens (Gen 44:5), and עוֹנֵן, a kind of fortune-telling whose exact nature is not known (from "cloud", or from a root "to murmur"; cf. Deut 18:10).
- **"Ye shall not round the corners of your heads, neither shalt thou mar the corners of thy beard" → "You shall not round off the edge of your head, and you shall not ruin the edge of your beard"** (v27) — Hebrew פֵּאָה "edge, side" (singular both times, the word of v9); תַשְׁחִית is the causative of שָׁחַת "ruin". The first verb is plural, the second singular. A mourning rite is probably meant (21:5; Jer 9:26).
- **"cuttings in your flesh for the dead" → "a cut for a soul … in your flesh"** (v28) — Hebrew וְשֶׂרֶט לָנֶפֶשׁ: נֶפֶשׁ "soul" here stands for a dead person (21:1, 11; 22:4; Num 5:2). **"nor print any marks upon you" → "the writing of a tattoo you shall not put on yourselves"** — Hebrew כְּתֹבֶת קַעֲקַע; קַעֲקַע is found only here and its sense is not certain (a mark cut or pricked into the skin). "Put" is literally "give" both times.
- **"Do not prostitute thy daughter, to cause her to be a whore; lest the land fall to whoredom" → "Do not profane your daughter, to make her play the prostitute, that the land may not play the prostitute"** (v29) — Hebrew חִלֵּל "profane", and זָנָה twice (the causative, then the simple verb with the land as subject). The second clause is literally "and the land shall not play the prostitute". **"wickedness" → "depravity"** — Hebrew זִמָּה, a shameful design, used of sexual wrong (18:17; 20:14).
- **"reverence my sanctuary" → "my sanctuary you shall fear"** (v30) — Hebrew תִּירָאוּ, "fear", as in v3.
- **"Regard not them that have familiar spirits, neither seek after wizards" → "Do not turn to the ghosts and to the knowing spirits; do not seek them out"** (v31) — Hebrew הָאֹבֹת and הַיִּדְּעֹנִים. אוֹב is the spirit of a dead person that is called up (Isa 29:4 "your voice shall be like a ghost from the ground"; 1 Sam 28:7 "a woman who is mistress of a ghost"), and then also the one who calls it up; יִדְּעֹנִי, from יָדַע "know", is "a knowing one", a spirit that has knowledge, or the one who has such a spirit. The exact sense of both is not certain. In 20:27 they are "in" a man or a woman, so the spirits are named here, not "mediums" and "wizards". "Them" is supplied; the Hebrew can also be read "do not seek to become unclean by them". **"to be defiled by them" → "to become unclean by them"** — Hebrew לְטָמְאָה.
- **"rise up before the hoary head, and honour the face of the old man" → "Before gray hair you shall rise, and you shall show deference to the face of an old man"** (v32) — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי שֵׂיבָה, literally "from the face of gray hair"; הָדַר as in v15, here in a good sense; זָקֵן is "old man" (elsewhere "elder").
- **"if a stranger sojourn with thee … ye shall not vex him" → "when a sojourner sojourns with you … you shall not wrong him"** (v33) — Hebrew גֵּר / גּוּר, one root, and הוֹנָה "wrong, oppress". "With you" is singular, "your land" and the verb plural.
- **"as one born among you" → "As a native among you"** (v34) — Hebrew כְּאֶזְרָח מִכֶּם, literally "as a native from you". **"strangers" → "sojourners"** — גֵּרִים. "You shall love him" is singular: וְאָהַבְתָּ לוֹ כָּמוֹךָ, the words of v18.
- **"in meteyard, in weight, or in measure" → "in measure, in weight, or in capacity"** (v35) — Hebrew מִדָּה, measure of length, and מְשׂוּרָה, a measure for liquids or grain.
- **"Just balances, just weights, a just ephah, and a just hin" → "Righteous balances, righteous weights, a righteous ephah and a righteous hin"** (v36) — Hebrew צֶדֶק "righteousness" four times, literally "balances of righteousness, stones of righteousness …": the weights were stones. The ephah is a dry measure, the hin a liquid measure.
- **"observe … judgments" → "keep … ordinances"** (v37) — Hebrew שָׁמַר "keep" and מִשְׁפָּטִים, here the rulings laid down.

### Notes
- **Chapter summary** — the densest ethical chapter in the Torah: blends cultic, ethical, social, and civic laws. "You shall love your neighbor as yourself" (v18) is its most famous single line — quoted by Jesus as the second great commandment (Mk 12:31, Mt 22:39).
- **Holiness = ethical** — "You shall be holy, for I am holy" (v2) introduces not only ritual but ethical imitation of Yahweh. Holiness manifests in fair weights, gleaning-leavings, honesty, love of neighbor.
- **Gleaning-leavings** (v9–10) — the edge and fallen-grape laws are the legal basis of Ruth's story (Ruth 2). Proto-welfare.
- **"Love the sojourner as yourself"** (v34) — the same formula as "love your neighbor" (v18), extended to the sojourner. Remarkable in ancient Near Eastern law.
- **Tattoos, mourning-cuts, beard-edges** (v27–28) — probably aimed at specific Canaanite mourning / idolatrous practices; historical context colors modern applicability discussions.
