# Leviticus 14

[1] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[2] "This shall be the law of the one with skin disease on the day of his cleansing. He shall be brought to the priest,

[3] and the priest shall go outside the camp. The priest shall examine him, and if the infection of skin disease is healed in the afflicted person,

[4] then the priest shall command that two live clean birds, cedarwood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop be taken for the one to be cleansed.

[5] The priest shall command that one of the birds be slaughtered in an earthenware vessel over fresh water.

[6] As for the living bird, he shall take it together with the cedarwood, the scarlet yarn, and the hyssop, and dip them and the living bird in the blood of the bird that was slaughtered over the fresh water.

[7] He shall sprinkle seven times on the one to be cleansed from the skin disease, and shall pronounce him clean, and shall release the living bird into the open field.

[8] The one to be cleansed shall wash his clothes, shave off all his hair, and wash himself in water; then he shall be clean. After that he may come into the camp, but he shall remain outside his tent for seven days.

[9] On the seventh day he shall shave all his hair — his head, his beard, and his eyebrows — all his hair he shall shave off. He shall wash his clothes and bathe his body in water, and he shall be clean.

[10] On the eighth day he shall take two male lambs without blemish, one ewe lamb a year old without blemish, and three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour for a grain offering, mixed with oil, and one log of oil.

[11] The priest who cleanses him shall present the one to be cleansed, with these things, before Yahweh at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

[12] The priest shall take one of the male lambs and offer it for a guilt offering, along with the log of oil, and elevate them as an elevation offering before Yahweh.

[13] He shall slaughter the lamb in the place where the sin offering and the burnt offering are slaughtered, in the holy place; for as the sin offering belongs to the priest, so does the guilt offering — it is most holy.

[14] The priest shall take some of the blood of the guilt offering, and the priest shall put it on the tip of the right ear of the one to be cleansed, and on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot.

[15] The priest shall take some of the log of oil and pour it into the palm of his own left hand.

[16] The priest shall dip his right finger into the oil that is in his left hand, and sprinkle some of the oil with his finger seven times before Yahweh.

[17] From the rest of the oil that is in his hand the priest shall put some on the tip of the right ear of the one to be cleansed, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot — upon the blood of the guilt offering.

[18] What is left of the oil in the priest's hand he shall put on the head of the one to be cleansed; and the priest shall make atonement for him before Yahweh.

[19] The priest shall then offer the sin offering and make atonement for the one to be cleansed from his uncleanness; afterward he shall slaughter the burnt offering.

[20] The priest shall offer the burnt offering and the grain offering on the altar. Thus the priest shall make atonement for him, and he shall be clean.

[21] But if he is poor and cannot afford so much, he shall take one male lamb as a guilt offering to be elevated, to make atonement for him, and one-tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering, and a log of oil,

[22] and two turtledoves or two young pigeons, whichever he can afford — one for a sin offering and the other for a burnt offering.

[23] On the eighth day he shall bring them for his cleansing to the priest, at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting before Yahweh.

[24] The priest shall take the lamb of the guilt offering and the log of oil, and the priest shall elevate them as an elevation offering before Yahweh.

[25] He shall slaughter the lamb of the guilt offering; the priest shall take some of the blood of the guilt offering and put it on the tip of the right ear of the one to be cleansed, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot.

[26] The priest shall pour some of the oil into the palm of his own left hand;

[27] and the priest shall sprinkle with his right finger some of the oil that is in his left hand seven times before Yahweh.

[28] The priest shall put some of the oil that is in his hand on the tip of the right ear of the one to be cleansed, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot — on the place of the blood of the guilt offering.

[29] What is left of the oil in the priest's hand he shall put on the head of the one to be cleansed, to make atonement for him before Yahweh.

[30] Then he shall offer one of the turtledoves or young pigeons, whichever he can afford —

[31] whichever he can afford: one for a sin offering and the other for a burnt offering, with the grain offering. Thus the priest shall make atonement before Yahweh for the one to be cleansed.

[32] This is the law for the person in whom there is an infection of skin disease, who cannot afford the means for his cleansing."

[33] Yahweh spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

[34] "When you come into the land of Canaan, which I give you as a possession, and I put an infection of skin disease in a house in the land of your possession,

[35] then the owner of the house shall come and tell the priest, saying, 'Something like an infection has appeared to me in my house.'

[36] The priest shall command that they empty the house before the priest goes in to examine the infection, so that everything in the house may not become unclean; then afterward the priest shall go in to examine the house.

[37] He shall examine the infection; and if the infection in the walls of the house consists of greenish or reddish depressions that appear deeper than the surface of the wall,

[38] then the priest shall go out of the house, to the entrance of the house, and close up the house for seven days.

[39] The priest shall come back on the seventh day and examine it; and if the infection has spread in the walls of the house,

[40] the priest shall command that the stones in which the infection is be pulled out and thrown into an unclean place outside the city.

[41] He shall have the house scraped all around on the inside; and they shall pour the dust that they scrape off into an unclean place outside the city.

[42] They shall take other stones and put them in the place of those stones; and he shall take other plaster and plaster the house.

[43] If the infection breaks out again in the house, after he has pulled out the stones, scraped the house, and replastered,

[44] then the priest shall come and examine it; and if the infection has spread in the house, it is a malignant skin disease in the house; it is unclean.

[45] He shall break down the house — its stones, its timbers, and all its plaster — and carry them out of the city to an unclean place.

[46] Whoever enters the house during all the days that it is closed up shall be unclean until evening.

[47] Whoever lies down in the house shall wash his clothes, and whoever eats in the house shall wash his clothes.

[48] But if the priest comes in and examines it, and the infection has not spread in the house after the house was plastered, then the priest shall pronounce the house clean, because the infection is healed.

[49] To cleanse the house he shall take two birds, cedarwood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop;

[50] and he shall slaughter one of the birds in an earthenware vessel over fresh water,

[51] and shall take the cedarwood, the hyssop, the scarlet yarn, and the living bird, and dip them in the blood of the slaughtered bird and in the fresh water, and sprinkle the house seven times.

[52] He shall cleanse the house with the blood of the bird, the fresh water, the living bird, the cedarwood, the hyssop, and the scarlet yarn.

[53] He shall release the living bird outside the city into the open fields; and he shall make atonement for the house, and it shall be clean.

[54] This is the law for every kind of infection of skin disease — for a scall,

[55] for the skin disease of a garment or a house,

[56] for a swelling, a scab, or a bright spot —

[57] to determine when something is unclean and when it is clean. This is the law of skin disease."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"running water" → "fresh water"** (v5, 50, 51, 52) — Hebrew מַיִם חַיִּים *mayim chayyim* = living / fresh / flowing water.
- **"tenth deal" → "tenth of an ephah"** (v10, 21) — archaic *deal* = portion. Specifies measure.
- **"log" (v10, 12, 15, etc.)** — Hebrew לֹג *log*. Liquid measure ~1/3 liter. Retained as Hebrew unit.
- **"hollow strakes" → "depressions"** (v37) — Hebrew שְׁקַעֲרוּרֹת *sheqa'aruroth* = indentations / pits; *strake* archaic.
- **"morter" → "plaster"** (v42, 45) — Hebrew עָפָר *afar* — applied to walls; *mortar* in 1611 English was the binding between stones, now distinct from wall plaster.

### Notes
- **Two-bird rite** (v4–7, 49–53) — archaic ritual: one bird killed, one released bearing symbolic transfer of impurity. Parallels the Day-of-Atonement scapegoat (ch 16).
- **Cedar, hyssop, scarlet** — same triad used in red-heifer purification (Num 19) and at the cross (Jn 19:29, the hyssop branch with sour wine). Early Christian interpretation tied these to Christ's cleansing.
- **Ear / thumb / toe with blood + oil** (v14–18) — the cleansed person receives priestly-ordination-like treatment (cf. Lev 8:23–24). Return from *tsara'at* = rebirth into sanctified life.
- **House *tsara'at*** (v33–53) — likely physical mildew / dry rot / nitre. Pastoral hypothesis: Yahweh "puts" it (v34), perhaps identifying house disease as a message to renovate; Rabbinic tradition read it as divine chastisement for stinginess.
- **Jesus and the cleansed leper** — Mt 8:4 / Mk 1:44 / Lk 5:14: Jesus tells the healed man to present himself to the priest and offer as Moses commanded — i.e., this chapter's rite.
