# Leviticus 14

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "This shall be ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ of the leper on the day of his cleansing. And he shall be brought to the priest;

[3] and the priest shall go out to the outside of the camp; and the priest shall see, and behold, the plague of leprosy is healed from the leper;

[4] then the priest shall command, and one shall take for the one being cleansed two living clean birds, and cedar wood, and scarlet, and hyssop.

[5] And the priest shall command, and one shall slaughter the one bird into an earthen vessel, over living water.

[6] The living bird — he shall take it, and the cedar wood and the scarlet and the hyssop, and he shall dip them and the living bird in the blood of the bird that was slaughtered over the living water.

[7] And he shall sprinkle on the one being cleansed from the leprosy seven times, and pronounce him clean; and he shall let the living bird go over the face of the field.

[8] And the one being cleansed shall wash his garments and shave all his hair and wash in water, and be clean; and afterward he shall come into the camp, and he shall dwell outside his tent seven days.

[9] And it shall be, on the seventh day he shall shave all his hair — his head and his beard and his eyebrows, and all his hair he shall shave; and he shall wash his garments and wash his flesh in water, and be clean.

[10] And on the eighth day he shall take two lambs without blemish, and one ewe lamb a year old without blemish, and three tenths of fine flour, a grain offering mixed with oil, and one log of oil.

[11] And the priest who cleanses shall make the man who is being cleansed, and them, stand before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

[12] And the priest shall take the one lamb and bring it near for a guilt offering, and the log of oil, and he shall wave them as a wave offering before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] And he shall slaughter the lamb in the place where one slaughters the sin offering and the burnt offering, in the holy place; for like the sin offering, the guilt offering is the priest's; it is most holy.

[14] And the priest shall take of the blood of the guilt offering, and the priest shall place it on the lobe of the right ear of the one being cleansed, and on the thumb of his right hand, and on the great toe of his right foot.

[15] And the priest shall take of the log of oil and pour it on the left palm of the priest.

[16] And the priest shall dip his right finger in the oil that is on his left palm, and he shall sprinkle of the oil with his finger seven times before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[17] And of the rest of the oil that is on his palm the priest shall place on the lobe of the right ear of the one being cleansed, and on the thumb of his right hand, and on the great toe of his right foot, upon the blood of the guilt offering.

[18] And what remains of the oil that is on the palm of the priest he shall place on the head of the one being cleansed; and the priest shall make atonement for him before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[19] And the priest shall offer the sin offering and make atonement for the one being cleansed from his uncleanness; and afterward he shall slaughter the burnt offering.

[20] And the priest shall offer up the burnt offering and the grain offering on the altar; and the priest shall make atonement for him, and he shall be clean.

[21] But if he is poor and his hand does not attain to it, then he shall take one lamb, a guilt offering, for a wave offering, to make atonement for him, and one tenth of fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering, and a log of oil,

[22] and two turtledoves or two sons of a dove, to which his hand attains; and the one shall be a sin offering and the other a burnt offering.

[23] And he shall bring them on the eighth day for his cleansing to the priest, to the entrance of the tent of meeting, before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[24] And the priest shall take the lamb of the guilt offering and the log of oil, and the priest shall wave them as a wave offering before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[25] And he shall slaughter the lamb of the guilt offering; and the priest shall take of the blood of the guilt offering and place it on the lobe of the right ear of the one being cleansed, and on the thumb of his right hand, and on the great toe of his right foot.

[26] And of the oil the priest shall pour on the left palm of the priest.

[27] And the priest shall sprinkle with his right finger of the oil that is on his left palm seven times before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[28] And the priest shall place of the oil that is on his palm on the lobe of the right ear of the one being cleansed, and on the thumb of his right hand, and on the great toe of his right foot, upon the place of the blood of the guilt offering.

[29] And what remains of the oil that is on the palm of the priest he shall place on the head of the one being cleansed, to make atonement for him before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[30] And he shall offer the one of the turtledoves or of the sons of the dove, of that to which his hand attains —

[31] that to which his hand attains: the one a sin offering and the other a burnt offering, together with the grain offering; and the priest shall make atonement for the one being cleansed before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[32] This is ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ of him in whom is a plague of leprosy, whose hand does not attain in his cleansing."

[33] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, saying,

[34] "When you come into the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, which I am giving to you for a possession, and I put a plague of leprosy in a house of the land of your possession,

[35] then he whose the house is shall come and tell the priest, saying, 'Something like a plague has appeared to me in the house.'

[36] And the priest shall command, and they shall clear the house before the priest comes to see the plague, so that all that is in the house may not become unclean; and afterward the priest shall come to see the house.

[37] And he shall see the plague, and behold, the plague is in the walls of the house, greenish or reddish depressions, and their appearance is lower than the wall;

[38] then the priest shall go out from the house to the entrance of the house, and he shall shut up the house seven days.

[39] And the priest shall return on the seventh day and see, and behold, the plague has spread in the walls of the house;

[40] then the priest shall command, and they shall pull out the stones in which the plague is and throw them outside the city, to an unclean place.

[41] And the house he shall have scraped from within, all around; and they shall pour out the dust that they scraped off outside the city, to an unclean place.

[42] And they shall take other stones and bring them into the place of the stones; and other dust he shall take and plaster the house.

[43] And if the plague returns and breaks out in the house after he has pulled out the stones, and after the scraping of the house, and after the plastering,

[44] then the priest shall come and see, and behold, the plague has spread in the house — it is a malignant leprosy in the house; it is unclean.

[45] And he shall break down the house, its stones and its timbers and all the dust of the house; and he shall bring them out outside the city, to an unclean place.

[46] And he who comes into the house all the days that he has shut it up shall be unclean until the evening.

[47] And he who lies in the house shall wash his garments; and he who eats in the house shall wash his garments.

[48] But if the priest surely comes and sees, and behold, the plague has not spread in the house after the plastering of the house, then the priest shall pronounce the house clean, for the plague is healed.

[49] And he shall take, to purge the house of sin, two birds and cedar wood and scarlet and hyssop.

[50] And he shall slaughter the one bird into an earthen vessel, over living water.

[51] And he shall take the cedar wood and the hyssop and the scarlet and the living bird, and dip them in the blood of the slaughtered bird and in the living water, and sprinkle toward the house seven times.

[52] And he shall purge the house of sin with the blood of the bird and with the living water and with the living bird and with the cedar wood and with the hyssop and with the scarlet.

[53] And he shall let the living bird go outside the city, to the face of the field; and he shall make atonement for the house, and it shall be clean.

[54] This is ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ for every plague of leprosy and for the scall,

[55] and for the leprosy of the garment and for the house,

[56] and for the swelling and for the scab and for the bright spot,

[57] to teach on the day of the unclean and on the day of the clean. This is ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ of leprosy."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"the law" → "the torah"** (v2, 32, 54, 57) — Hebrew תּוֹרַת, הַתּוֹרָה: the instruction for one matter.
- **"leper", "leprosy", "plague" kept** (throughout) — Hebrew מְצֹרָע (v2) and צָרוּעַ (v3), two forms of one root; צָרַעַת; נֶגַע. צָרַעַת is one word for the mark on a person (v2–32), on a garment (v55) and in the walls of a house (v34–53); it is not the leprosy of modern medicine, and the verse names no disease. See 13:2. נֶגַע is literally "a touch, a stroke".
- **"in the day of his cleansing"** (v2, 23, 32) — Hebrew טָהֳרָה, the noun of "clean". **"him that is to be cleansed" → "the one being cleansed"** (v4–31) — Hebrew הַמִּטַּהֵר, a reflexive participle, literally "the one cleansing himself": the rite is done to him. **"pronounce him clean"** (v7, 48) and **"the priest who cleanses"** (v11) are one verb, טִהַר "make clean".
- **"look" → "see"** (v3, 36–48) — Hebrew רָאָה, as in chapter 13.
- **"if the plague of leprosy be healed in the leper" → "the plague of leprosy is healed from the leper"** (v3) — Hebrew נִרְפָּא … מִן.
- **"Then shall the priest command to take" → "then the priest shall command, and one shall take"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew וְצִוָּה הַכֹּהֵן וְלָקַח, "and the priest shall command, and he shall take": the one who takes and slaughters is not named.
- **"two birds alive and clean"** (v4) — Hebrew צִפֳּרִים, small birds; the kind is not named. **"scarlet"** (v4, 6, 49–52) — Hebrew שְׁנִי תוֹלַעַת, literally "scarlet of the worm": the dye, and what is dyed with it; "yarn" is not in the Hebrew. **"hyssop"** — Hebrew אֵזוֹב; the plant is not certainly known.
- **"be killed" → "slaughter"** (v5, 6, 13, 19, 25, 50, 51) — Hebrew שָׁחַט. **"in an earthen vessel over running water" → "into an earthen vessel, over living water"** (v5, 6, 50–52) — Hebrew אֶל־כְּלִי־חֶרֶשׂ עַל־מַיִם חַיִּים; "living water" is water from a spring or a stream, as Gen 26:19.
- **"let the living bird loose into the open field" → "let the living bird go over the face of the field"** (v7); **"out of the city into the open fields" → "outside the city, to the face of the field"** (v53) — Hebrew שִׁלַּח, "send away, let go"; עַל־פְּנֵי הַשָּׂדֶה, אֶל־פְּנֵי הַשָּׂדֶה.
- **"wash his clothes … wash himself"** (v8, 9) — two Hebrew verbs: כִּבֵּס of cloth, רָחַץ of the body. **"shall tarry abroad out of his tent" → "shall dwell outside his tent"** (v8) — Hebrew וְיָשַׁב.
- **"his eyebrows"** (v9) — Hebrew גַּבֹּת עֵינָיו, "the brows of his eyes".
- **"two he lambs" → "two lambs"; "one ewe lamb of the first year" → "one ewe lamb a year old"** (v10) — Hebrew כְבָשִׂים, כַּבְשָׂה … בַּת־שְׁנָתָהּ, literally "a daughter of her year". **"without blemish"** — Hebrew תְּמִימִים, "whole, complete": the word that is "blameless" of a man.
- **"three tenth deals of fine flour for a meat offering" → "three tenths of fine flour, a grain offering"** (v10, 21) — Hebrew עֶשְׂרֹנִים; the measure (an ephah) is not named. מִנְחָה is the grain offering. **"log"** — Hebrew לֹג, a small measure of liquids found only in this chapter; its size is not given.
- **"shall present" → "shall make … stand"** (v11) — Hebrew וְהֶעֱמִיד. **"and those things" → "and them"** — Hebrew וְאֹתָם.
- **"offer him for a trespass offering" → "bring it near for a guilt offering"** (v12) — Hebrew וְהִקְרִיב … לְאָשָׁם; אָשָׁם is the one word "guilt". **"wave them for a wave offering"** (v12, 24) — Hebrew וְהֵנִיף … תְּנוּפָה, verb and noun of one root.
- **"in the holy place"** (v13) — Hebrew בִּמְקוֹם הַקֹּדֶשׁ, "in the place of holiness". **"as the sin offering is the priest's, so is the trespass offering" → "like the sin offering, the guilt offering is the priest's"** — Hebrew כַּחַטָּאת הָאָשָׁם הוּא לַכֹּהֵן. **"most holy"** — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ קָדָשִׁים, literally "holiness of holinesses".
- **"put" → "place"** (v14, 17, 18, 25, 28, 29) — Hebrew נָתַן, "give". **"the tip of the right ear" → "the lobe of the right ear"** — Hebrew תְּנוּךְ אֹזֶן, the lobe (or the upper rim) of the ear, as Exod 29:20. "Thumb" and "great toe" are one word, בֹּהֶן.
- **"pour it into the palm of his own left hand" → "pour it on the left palm of the priest"** (v15, 26) — Hebrew עַל־כַּף הַכֹּהֵן הַשְּׂמָאלִית; "his own" and "hand" are not in the Hebrew. In v16–18, 27–29 "palm" is the same word כַּף.
- **"the remnant of the oil" → "what remains of the oil"** (v18, 29) — Hebrew הַנּוֹתָר; "the rest" (v17) is יֶתֶר, of the same root.
- **"make an atonement"** (v18–21, 29, 31, 53) — Hebrew כִּפֶּר (literally "cover").
- **"offer"** (v19, 30) — Hebrew עָשָׂה "do, make", the usual verb for preparing a sacrifice. **"offer" → "offer up"** (v20) — Hebrew הֶעֱלָה, "make go up", the verb of עֹלָה "burnt offering"; "on the altar" is הַמִּזְבֵּחָה, "to the altar".
- **"cannot get so much" → "his hand does not attain to it"** (v21); **"such as he is able to get" → "to which his hand attains"** (v22, 30–32) — Hebrew אֵין יָדוֹ מַשֶּׂגֶת, אֲשֶׁר תַּשִּׂיג יָדוֹ: what he can afford. "To it" is supplied. **"one lamb for a trespass offering to be waved" → "one lamb, a guilt offering, for a wave offering"** (v21) — Hebrew כֶּבֶשׂ אֶחָד אָשָׁם לִתְנוּפָה.
- **"two young pigeons" → "two sons of a dove"** (v22, 30) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יוֹנָה; see 12:6.
- **"with the meat offering" → "together with the grain offering"** (v31) — Hebrew עַל־הַמִּנְחָה, "upon the grain offering".
- **"whose hand is not able to get that which pertaineth to his cleansing" → "whose hand does not attain in his cleansing"** (v32) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר לֹא־תַשִּׂיג יָדוֹ בְּטָהֳרָתוֹ; nothing more stands in the Hebrew.
- **"which I give to you" → "which I am giving to you"** (v34) — Hebrew נֹתֵן, a participle. **"I put the plague of leprosy in a house"** — Hebrew וְנָתַתִּי, "and I give / put": Yahweh is the subject.
- **"he that owneth the house" → "he whose the house is"** (v35) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־לוֹ הַבַּיִת. **"It seemeth to me there is as it were a plague" → "Something like a plague has appeared to me"** — Hebrew כְּנֶגַע נִרְאָה לִי, "as a plague it has appeared to me".
- **"empty the house" → "clear the house"** (v36) — Hebrew וּפִנּוּ.
- **"hollow strakes" → "depressions"** (v37) — Hebrew שְׁקַעֲרוּרֹת, found only here; the sense is uncertain. **"in sight are lower than the wall" → "their appearance is lower than the wall"** — as 13:20.
- **"take away the stones" → "pull out the stones"** (v40, 43) — Hebrew חִלֵּץ. **"cast" → "throw"** — Hebrew הִשְׁלִיךְ.
- **"cause the house to be scraped" → "he shall have scraped"; "that they scrape off"** (v41, 43) — two Hebrew verbs, הִקְצִיעַ and הִקְצָה. **"morter" → "dust"** (v41, 42, 45) — Hebrew עָפָר, the word "dust": the clay with which the walls are coated. **"plaister"** (v42, 43, 48) — Hebrew טוּחַ, "coat, daub".
- **"a fretting leprosy" → "a malignant leprosy"** (v44) — Hebrew מַמְאֶרֶת, as 13:51; the sense is uncertain.
- **"all the while that it is shut up" → "all the days that he has shut it up"** (v46) — Hebrew כָּל־יְמֵי הִסְגִּיר אֹתוֹ, active: the priest is the subject.
- **"if the priest shall come in" → "if the priest surely comes"** (v48) — the infinitive absolute with its verb: literally "coming he comes".
- **"to cleanse the house" → "to purge the house of sin"** (v49, 52) — Hebrew חִטֵּא, the piel of חָטָא "sin": literally "de-sin", as Exod 29:36. It is another verb than "be clean", "pronounce clean" (v48, 53).
- **"sprinkle the house" → "sprinkle toward the house"** (v51) — Hebrew אֶל־הַבַּיִת.
- **"To teach when it is unclean, and when it is clean" → "to teach on the day of the unclean and on the day of the clean"** (v57) — Hebrew לְהוֹרֹת בְּיוֹם הַטָּמֵא וּבְיוֹם הַטָּהֹר; לְהוֹרֹת is the verb of "torah".

### Notes
- **Two-bird rite** (v4–7, 49–53) — one bird is slaughtered, one is let go. Compare the two goats of the Day of Atonement (ch 16).
- **Cedar, hyssop, scarlet** — the same three are used with the red heifer (Num 19:6); hyssop also in John 19:29.
- **Ear / thumb / toe with blood + oil** (v14–18) — the same rite as at the installing of the priests (Lev 8:23–24; Exod 29:20).
- **House *tsara'at*** (v33–53) — commonly taken for mould or a mineral growth in the walls. The text says that Yahweh puts it there (v34) and gives no reason.
- **Jesus and the cleansed leper** — Mt 8:4 / Mk 1:44 / Lk 5:14: Jesus tells the healed man to show himself to the priest and offer what Moses commanded — i.e., this chapter's rite.
