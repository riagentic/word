# Leviticus 8

[1] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[2] "Take Aaron and his sons with him, and the garments, and the anointing oil, and the bull of the sin offering, and the two rams, and the basket of unleavened bread;

[3] and assemble all the congregation at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting."

[4] Moses did as Yahweh commanded him, and the assembly gathered at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

[5] Moses said to the congregation, "This is what Yahweh has commanded to be done."

[6] Then Moses brought Aaron and his sons forward and washed them with water.

[7] He put the tunic on him and fastened the sash around him, clothed him with the robe, and put the ephod on him; he fastened the skillfully woven band of the ephod on him and bound it to him.

[8] He placed the breastpiece on him, and in the breastpiece he put the Urim and the Thummim.

[9] He set the turban on his head, and on the turban, at its front, he set the gold plate — the holy diadem — as Yahweh commanded Moses.

[10] Moses took the anointing oil and anointed the dwelling and all that was in it, and consecrated them.

[11] He sprinkled some of it on the altar seven times, and anointed the altar and all its utensils, and the basin and its stand, to consecrate them.

[12] He poured some of the anointing oil on Aaron's head and anointed him to consecrate him.

[13] Moses brought Aaron's sons forward, clothed them with tunics, sashed them with sashes, and bound headdresses on them, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

[14] Then he brought the bull of the sin offering, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the bull of the sin offering.

[15] Moses slaughtered it, took some of the blood, and put it on the horns of the altar all around with his finger, purifying the altar. The rest of the blood he poured out at the base of the altar, and consecrated it to make atonement for it.

[16] He took all the fat that was on the entrails, the lobe above the liver, the two kidneys and their fat, and Moses turned them into smoke on the altar.

[17] But the bull itself — its hide, its flesh, and its dung — he burned with fire outside the camp, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

[18] Then he brought the ram of the burnt offering, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the ram.

[19] Moses slaughtered it and threw the blood against the altar all around.

[20] He cut the ram into its pieces, and Moses turned the head, the pieces, and the fat into smoke.

[21] He washed the entrails and the legs with water, and Moses turned the whole ram into smoke on the altar. It was a burnt offering for a pleasing aroma, an offering made by fire to Yahweh — as Yahweh commanded Moses.

[22] Then he brought the second ram, the ram of ordination, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the ram.

[23] Moses slaughtered it, took some of its blood, and put it on the tip of Aaron's right ear, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot.

[24] He brought forward Aaron's sons, and Moses put some of the blood on the tips of their right ears, on the thumbs of their right hands, and on the big toes of their right feet. Then Moses threw the blood against the altar all around.

[25] He took the fat tail, all the fat that was on the entrails, the lobe above the liver, the two kidneys with their fat, and the right thigh.

[26] From the basket of unleavened bread that was before Yahweh he took one unleavened cake, one cake of bread made with oil, and one wafer, and laid them on the fat and on the right thigh.

[27] He placed all these in Aaron's hands and in his sons' hands, and elevated them as an elevation offering before Yahweh.

[28] Then Moses took them from their hands and turned them into smoke on the altar, upon the burnt offering. They were an ordination offering for a pleasing aroma, an offering made by fire to Yahweh.

[29] Moses took the breast and elevated it as an elevation offering before Yahweh; it was Moses' portion of the ram of ordination, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

[30] Moses took some of the anointing oil and some of the blood that was on the altar, and sprinkled it on Aaron and on his garments, and on his sons and on his sons' garments with him. Thus he consecrated Aaron and his garments, and his sons and his sons' garments with him.

[31] Moses said to Aaron and his sons, "Boil the flesh at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, and there eat it with the bread that is in the basket of the ordination offering, as I commanded, saying, 'Aaron and his sons shall eat it.'

[32] What is left of the flesh and the bread you shall burn with fire.

[33] You shall not go out from the entrance of the Tent of Meeting for seven days, until the days of your ordination are completed; for your ordination shall last seven days.

[34] As has been done today, so Yahweh has commanded to be done, to make atonement for you.

[35] At the entrance of the Tent of Meeting you shall remain day and night for seven days, and keep the charge of Yahweh, so that you do not die; for so I have been commanded."

[36] So Aaron and his sons did all the things that Yahweh had commanded by the hand of Moses.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"girdle" → "sash"** (v7, 13) — Hebrew אַבְנֵט *avnet*. Priestly sash, not belt.
- **"curious girdle" → "skillfully woven band"** (v7) — Hebrew חֵשֶׁב *chesheb*. Woven waistband of the ephod; *curious* in 1611 English = skillfully crafted.
- **"breastplate" → "breastpiece"** (v8) — Hebrew חֹשֶׁן *choshen*. Priestly pectoral pouch, not armor.
- **"mitre" → "turban"** (v9) — Hebrew מִצְנֶפֶת *mitznefet*. Priestly head wrap.
- **"bonnets" → "headdresses"** (v13) — Hebrew מִגְבָּעוֹת *migba'ot*. Priestly head-coverings for ordinary priests.
- **"laver" → "basin"** (v11) — Hebrew כִּיֹּר *kiyor*, as elsewhere.
- **"ram of consecration" → "ram of ordination"** (v22, 29) — Hebrew אֵיל הַמִּלֻּאִים *eil ha-millu'im*. *Millu'im* lit. "fillings" — from "filling the hand" of the new priest.
- **"great toe" → "big toe"** (v23, 24) — Hebrew בֹּהֶן רֶגֶל *bohen regel*. Modernized.
- **"rump" → "fat tail"** (v25) — as elsewhere.
- **"wave offering" → "elevation offering"** (v27, 29) — as in ch 7.
- **"shoulder" → "thigh"** (v25, 26) — as in ch 7.
- **"reconciliation" → "atonement"** (v15) — Hebrew כִּפֶּר *kipper*. Primary ritual verb of Leviticus; to purge / cover / atone.

### Notes
- **Ordination ceremony** — fulfills Ex 29. Seven-day rite in which Moses acts as "ordainer"; Aaron and sons are made priests.
- **Blood on ear/thumb/toe** (v23–24) — extremities marked with sacrificial blood — consecration of hearing, action, and walking. Same pattern repeats at the cleansing of a leper (Lev 14:14).
- **Urim and Thummim** (v8) — sacred lots carried in the breastpiece. Used for oracular decisions (Num 27:21); literal meaning uncertain, possibly "Lights and Perfections."
- **"Filling the hand"** — the idiom behind *millu'im*: the ordinand's hands are literally filled with offerings (v27) and then he "fills his own hand" henceforth by serving at the altar.
- **Seven-day enclosure** (v33–35) — new priests stay inside the sanctuary court a full week. Parallel to the creation-week structure: priesthood is itself a creative / cosmological act.
