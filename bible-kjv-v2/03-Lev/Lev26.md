# Leviticus 26

[1] "You shall make for yourselves no idols, nor shall you set up a carved image or a pillar, nor shall you place any figured stone in your land to bow down to it; for I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[2] You shall keep my ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ and revere my sanctuary. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] If you walk in my statutes and keep my commandments and do them,

[4] then I will give you rains in their season, and the land shall yield its produce, and the trees of the field shall yield their fruit.

[5] Your threshing shall overtake the vintage, and the vintage shall overtake the sowing; you shall eat your bread to the full and dwell in your land in safety.

[6] I will grant peace in the land; you shall lie down, and none shall make you afraid. I will remove wild beasts from the land, and the sword shall not pass through your land.

[7] You shall pursue your enemies, and they shall fall before you by the sword.

[8] Five of you shall pursue a hundred, and a hundred of you shall put ten thousand to flight; your enemies shall fall before you by the sword.

[9] I will turn toward you with favor, and make you fruitful, and multiply you; and I will establish my covenant with you.

[10] You shall eat the old grain long stored, and clear out the old to make way for the new.

[11] I will set my dwelling among you, and my soul shall not abhor you.

[12] I will walk among you, and I will be your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and you shall be my people.

[13] I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who brought you out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ so that you should not be their slaves; I have broken the bars of your yoke and made you walk upright.

[14] But if you will not listen to me and will not do all these commandments,

[15] and if you despise my statutes, or if your soul abhors my ordinances, so that you will not do all my commandments but break my covenant,

[16] then I will do this to you: I will appoint over you sudden terror, wasting disease and burning fever that consume the eyes and cause life to pine away; you shall sow your seed in vain, for your enemies shall eat it.

[17] I will set my face against you, and you shall be struck down before your enemies; those who hate you shall reign over you, and you shall flee when none pursues you.

[18] And if in spite of this you will not listen to me, then I will punish you seven times more for your sins.

[19] I will break the pride of your power, and I will make your heavens like iron and your earth like bronze;

[20] your strength shall be spent in vain, for your land shall not yield its produce, nor shall the trees of the land yield their fruit.

[21] If then you walk contrary to me and are unwilling to listen to me, I will bring seven times more blows upon you, according to your sins.

[22] I will send wild beasts among you, which shall bereave you of your children and destroy your cattle, and make you few in number, so that your roads shall be desolate.

[23] And if by these things you are not turned back to me but walk contrary to me,

[24] then I also will walk contrary to you, and I myself will strike you seven times over for your sins.

[25] I will bring a sword upon you that shall execute vengeance for the covenant. When you gather together within your cities, I will send pestilence among you, and you shall be delivered into the hand of the enemy.

[26] When I break your staff of bread, ten women shall bake your bread in one oven, and they shall dole out your bread again by weight; you shall eat and not be satisfied.

[27] And if in spite of this you will not listen to me but walk contrary to me,

[28] then I will walk contrary to you in fury, and I myself will discipline you seven times over for your sins.

[29] You shall eat the flesh of your sons, and the flesh of your daughters you shall eat.

[30] I will destroy your high places and cut down your incense altars, and cast your corpses upon the corpses of your idols, and my soul shall abhor you.

[31] I will lay your cities in ruins and desolate your sanctuaries, and I will not smell the aroma of your pleasing offerings.

[32] I myself will desolate the land, so that your enemies who settle in it shall be appalled at it.

[33] I will scatter you among the nations and unsheathe the sword after you; your land shall become a desolation, and your cities a ruin.

[34] Then the land shall make up for its ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧, all the days of its desolation, while you are in the land of your enemies; then the land shall rest and enjoy its ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧.

[35] All the days of its desolation it shall rest — the rest it did not have on your ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ when you dwelt on it.

[36] And as for those of you who are left, I will send faintness into their hearts in the lands of their enemies; the sound of a driven leaf shall chase them, and they shall flee as one flees from a sword, and they shall fall when none pursues.

[37] They shall stumble over one another, as before a sword, though none pursues; and you shall have no power to stand before your enemies.

[38] You shall perish among the nations, and the land of your enemies shall devour you.

[39] Those of you who are left shall waste away in their iniquity in your enemies' lands; also in the iniquities of their fathers they shall waste away with them.

[40] But if they confess their iniquity and the iniquity of their fathers, in their breach of faith by which they broke faith with me, and also in their walking contrary to me —

[41] so that I also walked contrary to them and brought them into the land of their enemies — if then their uncircumcised heart is humbled and they accept the punishment of their iniquity,

[42] then I will remember my covenant with ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, and also my covenant with ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧, and also my covenant with ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ I will remember; and the land I will remember.

[43] The land will be forsaken by them and will make up for its ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧, lying desolate without them; and they shall accept their punishment, because they rejected my ordinances and their soul abhorred my statutes.

[44] Yet for all that, when they are in the land of their enemies, I will not reject them, nor will I abhor them so as to destroy them utterly and break my covenant with them; for I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[45] But I will remember for them the covenant with their ancestors, whom I brought out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ in the sight of the nations, to be their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[46] These are the statutes, the ordinances, and the laws that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ made between himself and the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ on Mount Sinai by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"graven image" → "carved image"** (v1) — Hebrew פֶּסֶל *pesel* — carved image.
- **"standing image / image of stone" → "pillar / figured stone"** (v1) — Hebrew מַצֵּבָה *matzevah* (cultic pillar) / אֶבֶן מַשְׂכִּית *even maskit* (sculpted / figured stone).
- **"my tabernacle" → "my dwelling"** (v11) — Hebrew מִשְׁכָּן *mishkan* = dwelling-place.
- **"consumption and the burning ague" → "wasting disease and burning fever"** (v16) — Hebrew שַׁחֶפֶת *shachefet* / קַדַּחַת *qaddachat*. Tuberculosis-like wasting and fever.
- **"images" → "incense altars"** (v30) — Hebrew חַמָּנִים *chammanim* — incense stands associated with solar/Baal worship.
- **"heathen" → "nations"** (v33, 38, 45) — Hebrew *goyim*.
- **"sabbaths" of the land (v34–35, 43)** — the uncelebrated sabbatical years "make up" for themselves in the exile. 2 Chr 36:21 references this directly: the 70-year exile corresponds to uncelebrated sabbaticals.

### Notes
- **Covenant blessings and curses** — parallels Deut 28 and the broader Mesopotamian treaty tradition. Blessings for obedience (v3–13); escalating curses for disobedience (v14–39); promise of restoration upon repentance (v40–45).
- **Structure of the curses** — in four increasing "if you still will not listen" escalations (v18, 21, 23, 27), each adding sevenfold intensification: famine, wild beasts, sword + pestilence, cannibalism + exile.
- **"Walk contrary"** (Heb *halakh be-qeri*) — debated idiom; likely "walk in hostility / disregard." The reciprocity: if they walk contrary, Yahweh walks contrary.
- **Land's sabbaths** (v34–35, 43) — the land collects what the people failed to give. 2 Chr 36:21 reads the 70-year Babylonian exile as the land's sabbatical makeup.
- **"Uncircumcised heart"** (v41) — spiritual counterpart of physical circumcision. Echoed in Deut 10:16, Jer 4:4, Rom 2:29.
- **Remember Jacob, Isaac, Abraham** (v42) — reverse order of the patriarchs; possibly to emphasize ongoing lineage back to the original promise.
- **Covenant not annulled** (v44) — even the worst-case exile does not dissolve the covenant. The chapter ends in hope: Yahweh remembers.
