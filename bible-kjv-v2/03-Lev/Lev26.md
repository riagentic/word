# Leviticus 26

[1] "You shall not make for yourselves worthless idols, and a carved image and a pillar you shall not raise up for yourselves, and a figured stone you shall not place in your land to bow down upon it; for I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[2] My ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ you shall keep, and my sanctuary you shall fear; I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] If in my statutes you walk, and my commandments you keep and do them,

[4] then I will give your rains in their time, and the land shall give its produce, and the tree of the field shall give its fruit.

[5] And for you threshing shall reach the vintage, and the vintage shall reach the sowing; and you shall eat your bread to the full, and you shall dwell in security in your land.

[6] And I will give ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ in the land, and you shall lie down, and there shall be none who makes you tremble; and I will make evil living things cease from the land, and a sword shall not pass through your land.

[7] And you shall pursue your enemies, and they shall fall before you to the sword.

[8] And five of you shall pursue a hundred, and a hundred of you shall pursue ten thousand; and your enemies shall fall before you to the sword.

[9] And I will turn to you, and I will make you fruitful and multiply you, and I will establish my covenant with you.

[10] And you shall eat the old, long stored, and the old you shall bring out because of the new.

[11] And I will place my dwelling in your midst, and my soul shall not abhor you.

[12] And I will walk about in your midst, and I will be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to you, and you, you shall be a people to me.

[13] I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who brought you out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, from being slaves to them; and I broke the bars of your yoke and made you walk upright.

[14] And if you do not listen to me and do not do all these commandments,

[15] and if my statutes you reject, and if my ordinances your soul abhors, so as not to do all my commandments, so that you break my covenant,

[16] I also, I will do this to you: I will appoint over you dismay, the consumption and the fever, that wear out the eyes and make the soul pine; and you shall sow your seed in vain, and your enemies shall eat it.

[17] And I will set my face against you, and you shall be smitten before your enemies; and those who hate you shall have dominion over you, and you shall flee when none pursues you.

[18] And if even up to these you do not listen to me, then I will discipline you yet more, seven times for your sins.

[19] And I will break the pride of your strength, and I will make your heavens like iron and your land like bronze.

[20] And your power shall come to an end in vain, and your land shall not give its produce, and the tree of the land shall not give its fruit.

[21] And if you walk with me in opposition and are not willing to listen to me, then I will add upon you a blow, seven times according to your sins.

[22] And I will send among you the living things of the field, and they shall bereave you and cut off your beasts and make you few; and your ways shall be desolate.

[23] And if by these you are not disciplined to me, and you walk with me in opposition,

[24] then I also, I will walk with you in opposition, and I will strike you, I also, seven times for your sins.

[25] And I will bring upon you a sword avenging the vengeance of the covenant; and you shall be gathered into your cities, and I will send pestilence in your midst, and you shall be given into the hand of an enemy.

[26] When I break for you the staff of bread, then ten women shall bake your bread in one oven, and they shall return your bread by weight; and you shall eat and not be sated.

[27] And if by this you do not listen to me, and you walk with me in opposition,

[28] then I will walk with you in the fury of opposition, and I will discipline you, I also, seven times for your sins.

[29] And you shall eat the flesh of your sons, and the flesh of your daughters you shall eat.

[30] And I will destroy your high places and cut off your incense altars, and I will put your carcasses upon the carcasses of your idols; and my soul shall abhor you.

[31] And I will make your cities a ruin, and I will make your sanctuaries desolate, and I will not smell your soothing smell.

[32] And I, I will make the land desolate, and your enemies who dwell in it shall be appalled over it.

[33] And you I will strew among the nations, and I will draw a sword after you; and your land shall be a desolation, and your cities shall be a ruin.

[34] Then the land shall pay off its ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧, all the days of its being made desolate, while you are in the land of your enemies; then the land shall cease and pay off its ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧.

[35] All the days of its being made desolate it shall cease, that which it did not cease in your ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ when you dwelt on it.

[36] And those who are left among you — I will bring faintness into their heart in the lands of their enemies, and the sound of a driven leaf shall pursue them, and they shall flee as one flees from a sword, and they shall fall when none pursues.

[37] And they shall stumble, each man over his brother, as from before a sword, when there is no pursuer; and you shall have no standing before your enemies.

[38] And you shall perish among the nations, and the land of your enemies shall eat you.

[39] And those who are left among you shall rot away in their iniquity in the lands of your enemies, and also in the iniquities of their fathers, with them they shall rot away.

[40] And they shall confess their iniquity and the iniquity of their fathers, in their unfaithfulness with which they were unfaithful to me, and also that they walked with me in opposition —

[41] I also, I will walk with them in opposition, and I will bring them into the land of their enemies — or then their uncircumcised heart shall be humbled, and then they shall pay off their iniquity;

[42] and I will remember my covenant with ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, and also my covenant with ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧, and also my covenant with ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ I will remember, and the land I will remember.

[43] And the land shall be forsaken by them and shall pay off its ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ while it is made desolate without them, and they, they shall pay off their iniquity, because, even because, they rejected my ordinances, and their soul abhorred my statutes.

[44] And yet even so, when they are in the land of their enemies, I have not rejected them and I have not abhorred them, to make an end of them, to break my covenant with them; for I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[45] And I will remember for them the covenant of the former ones, whom I brought out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ before the eyes of the nations, to be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to them; I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[46] These are the statutes and the ordinances and ⟦torah|the torot|the laws⟧ that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave between himself and the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ on Mount Sinai by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"**, **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — the Hebrew names kept.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain of blessing and curse is kept clause by clause, in the Hebrew order.
- **"idols" → "worthless idols"** (v1) — Hebrew אֱלִילִם, a word that means "worthless, of no account" (Job 13:4; Zech 11:17) and sounds like אֵל / אֱלֹהִים; "idols" in v30 is another word, גִּלּוּלִים, a term of contempt (perhaps "dung things" or "blocks"). **"graven image" → "carved image"** — Hebrew פֶּסֶל. **"standing image" → "pillar"** — Hebrew מַצֵּבָה, a stone set up. **"image of stone" → "figured stone"** — Hebrew אֶבֶן מַשְׂכִּית; the exact sense of מַשְׂכִּית (a carved figure, Num 33:52) is not certain. **"to bow down unto it" → "to bow down upon it"** — Hebrew עָלֶיהָ "on it, over it, beside it".
- **"reverence my sanctuary" → "my sanctuary you shall fear"** (v2) — Hebrew תִּירָאוּ, the verb "fear".
- **"rain in due season" → "your rains in their time"** (v4) — plural, with "your". **"increase" → "produce"** (v4, 20) — Hebrew יְבוּל, another word than "yield" (תְּבוּאָה, 25:3). "Give" is the same verb נָתַן three times in the verse; "the tree" is singular.
- **"your threshing shall reach unto the vintage" → "for you threshing shall reach the vintage"** (v5) — Hebrew הִשִּׂיג "reach, overtake", the verb of 25:26 ("attain").
- **"peace" → "shalom"** (v6) — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם. **"none shall make you afraid" → "there shall be none who makes you tremble"** — Hebrew מַחֲרִיד, from חָרַד "tremble". **"rid evil beasts out of the land" → "make evil living things cease from the land"** — Hebrew וְהִשְׁבַּתִּי, the hifil of שָׁבַת "cease" (v34, 35), and חַיָּה רָעָה (Gen 37:20), a collective singular.
- **"by the sword" → "to the sword"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew לֶחָרֶב. **"put ten thousand to flight" → "pursue ten thousand"** (v8) — the same verb רָדַף as in the first half.
- **"have respect unto you" → "turn to you"** (v9) — Hebrew וּפָנִיתִי אֲלֵיכֶם "turn the face to".
- **"eat old store, and bring forth the old because of the new" → "eat the old, long stored, and the old you shall bring out because of the new"** (v10) — Hebrew יָשָׁן נוֹשָׁן "old, grown old"; מִפְּנֵי "from the face of, because of".
- **"set my tabernacle among you" → "place my dwelling in your midst"** (v11) — Hebrew מִשְׁכָּן. **"abhor"** (v11, 15, 30, 43, 44) — Hebrew גָּעַל with נֶפֶשׁ "soul" as subject; "reject" (v15, 43, 44) is מָאַס.
- **"walk among you" → "walk about in your midst"** (v12) — Hebrew הִתְהַלֵּךְ, the form of Gen 3:8. **"will be your God, and ye shall be my people" → "I will be Elohim to you, and you, you shall be a people to me"** — word for word; "you" is emphatic.
- **"that ye should not be their bondmen" → "from being slaves to them"** (v13) — Hebrew מִהְיֹת לָהֶם עֲבָדִים; "them" is Egypt. **"the bands of your yoke" → "the bars of your yoke"** — Hebrew מֹטֹת, the wooden bars. **"upright"** — Hebrew קוֹמְמִיּוּת, found only here.
- **"hearken unto me" → "listen to me"** (v14, 18, 21, 27) — Hebrew שָׁמַע לְ.
- **"despise my statutes" → "my statutes you reject"** (v15) — Hebrew מָאַס. "So as not to do … so that you break" are two Hebrew infinitives side by side.
- **"I will even appoint over you terror, consumption, and the burning ague, that shall consume the eyes, and cause sorrow of heart" → "I will appoint over you dismay, the consumption and the fever, that wear out the eyes and make the soul pine"** (v16) — Hebrew בֶּהָלָה, from the root "be dismayed": sudden alarm. שַׁחֶפֶת and קַדַּחַת (only here and Deut 28:22) are a wasting sickness and a burning one; what diseases they are is not known. מְכַלּוֹת is "bringing to an end"; מְדִיבֹת, found only here, is taken as "making pine away"; נֶפֶשׁ is "soul". "In vain" (v16, 20) is לָרִיק "for emptiness".
- **"ye shall be slain" → "you shall be smitten"** (v17) — Hebrew נִגַּף. **"shall reign over you" → "shall have dominion over you"** — Hebrew רָדָה, as 25:43.
- **"if ye will not yet for all this hearken" → "if even up to these you do not listen"** (v18) — Hebrew עַד־אֵלֶּה "as far as these". **"punish you seven times more" → "discipline you yet more, seven times"** (v18, 28) — Hebrew יִסֵּר "discipline, correct" (so v23, nifal); "yet more" is literally "I will add to". שֶׁבַע is "seven", not the "sevenfold" of Gen 4:15.
- **"the pride of your power" → "the pride of your strength"** (v19) — Hebrew גְּאוֹן עֻזְּכֶם; גָּאוֹן is the "exaltation" of Exod 15:7, here of what a people is proud of. "Power" (v20) is כֹּחַ. **"your earth as brass" → "your land like bronze"** — Hebrew אֶרֶץ, נְחֻשָׁה.
- **"walk contrary unto me" → "walk with me in opposition"** (v21, 23, 24, 27, 28, 40, 41) — Hebrew קֶרִי, a word found only in this chapter. Its meaning is uncertain: either "a meeting in opposition, hostility" (from קָרָה "meet"), which is rendered here, or "chance" (as מִקְרֶה): to walk with Yahweh "as it happens", without regard. One rendering is kept in all seven places; in v21 and v23 the word stands without "in"; v28 has "the fury of opposition" (חֲמַת־קֶרִי).
- **"bring seven times more plagues upon you" → "add upon you a blow, seven times"** (v21) — Hebrew מַכָּה, the noun of הִכָּה "strike" (v24), singular.
- **"wild beasts … rob you of your children … destroy your cattle" → "the living things of the field … bereave you … cut off your beasts"** (v22) — Hebrew חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה, a collective singular with singular verbs; שִׁכֵּל "bereave" already says "of children"; הִכְרִית "cut off". "Send" is the hifil of שָׁלַח. "Make you few" is the verb rendered "lessen" in 25:16.
- **"if ye will not be reformed by me by these things" → "if by these you are not disciplined to me"** (v23) — Hebrew תִוָּסְרוּ לִי.
- **"avenge the quarrel of my covenant" → "avenging the vengeance of the covenant"** (v25) — Hebrew נֹקֶמֶת נְקַם־בְּרִית, one root twice; "my" is not in the Hebrew. "You shall be gathered" may also be "you shall gather yourselves" (a nifal).
- **"deliver you your bread again by weight" → "return your bread by weight"** (v26) — Hebrew הֵשִׁיב "bring back". "The staff of bread" is word for word (מַטֵּה־לֶחֶם).
- **"contrary unto you also in fury" → "in the fury of opposition"** (v28) — Hebrew בַּחֲמַת־קֶרִי.
- **"your images" → "your incense altars"** (v30) — Hebrew חַמָּנִים; the meaning is not certain (small altars for incense, or pillars of the sun). **"cast your carcases" → "put your carcasses"** — Hebrew נָתַן.
- **"I will not smell the savour of your sweet odours" → "I will not smell your soothing smell"** (v31) — Hebrew רֵיחַ נִיחֹחַ, the phrase used of the sacrifices (1:9); literally "smell at the smell of your soothing". **"waste" → "a ruin"** (v31, 33) — Hebrew חָרְבָּה. "Sanctuaries" is plural.
- **"bring the land into desolation … shall be astonished at it" → "make the land desolate … shall be appalled over it"** (v32) — one Hebrew root (שָׁמֵם) for "make desolate" and "be appalled"; so "desolation" (v33), "being made desolate" (v34, 35, 43).
- **"scatter" → "strew"** (v33) — Hebrew זֵרָה "strew, winnow" (Exod 32:20), not the הֵפִיץ "scatter" of Gen 11:8. **"draw out a sword"** — literally "empty out".
- **"Then shall the land enjoy her sabbaths … then shall the land rest, and enjoy her sabbaths" → "Then the land shall pay off its Shabbats … then the land shall cease and pay off its Shabbats"** (v34, 43) — Hebrew רָצָה (the second time in v34 a hifil found only here). The verb is either the common רָצָה "be pleased with" ("enjoy") or a second רָצָה "pay off, make good" (Isa 40:2); the sense is uncertain. Because the same verb is used of the land with its Shabbats and of the people with their iniquity in the same breath (v41, 43), one rendering is kept for all five places. **"rest" → "cease"** (v34, 35) — Hebrew שָׁבַת, the verb of "Shabbat".
- **"because it did not rest in your sabbaths" → "that which it did not cease in your Shabbats"** (v35) — Hebrew אֵת אֲשֶׁר לֹא־שָׁבְתָה: the ceasing it did not have.
- **"a faintness" → "faintness"** (v36) — Hebrew מֹרֶךְ, found only here, from the root "be soft". **"a shaken leaf shall chase them" → "the sound of a driven leaf shall pursue them"** — Hebrew קוֹל "voice, sound"; רָדַף "pursue" as in v7, 8, 17. **"as fleeing from a sword"** — literally "they shall flee the flight of a sword".
- **"one upon another" → "each man over his brother"** (v37) — Hebrew אִישׁ־בְּאָחִיו. **"no power to stand" → "no standing"** — Hebrew תְּקוּמָה, found only here.
- **"pine away" → "rot away"** (v39) — Hebrew נָמַק "rot, fester". "With them" may go with the fathers' iniquities (which are with them) or with the fathers.
- **"If they shall confess" → "And they shall confess"** (v40–42) — the Hebrew has no "if": v40–42 are one chain of "and" clauses, "they shall confess … I will walk … I will bring … or then their heart shall be humbled … and I will remember". That the confession is the condition of the remembering is an inference; it is not put into the verse. **"their trespass which they trespassed against me" → "their unfaithfulness with which they were unfaithful to me"** (v40) — Hebrew מַעַל, a breach of faith.
- **"if then their uncircumcised hearts be humbled, and they then accept of the punishment of their iniquity" → "or then their uncircumcised heart shall be humbled, and then they shall pay off their iniquity"** (v41) — Hebrew אוֹ־אָז "or then" (commonly taken as "if then", "perhaps then"); לְבָבָם הֶעָרֵל "their heart, the uncircumcised one", singular; יִרְצוּ אֶת־עֲוֺנָם, the verb of v34: "punishment" is not in the Hebrew. "I will walk … I will bring" are future forms; most translations turn them into a past.
- **"my covenant with Jacob"** (v42) — the Hebrew has no "with": בְּרִיתִי יַעֲקוֹב "my covenant, Jacob", three times.
- **"The land also shall be left of them" → "And the land shall be forsaken by them"** (v43) — Hebrew תֵּעָזֵב מֵהֶם. **"because, even because"** — Hebrew יַעַן וּבְיַעַן.
- **"And yet for all that" → "And yet even so"** (v44) — Hebrew וְאַף־גַּם־זֹאת, literally "and also even this". **"I will not cast them away, neither will I abhor them, to destroy them utterly" → "I have not rejected them and I have not abhorred them, to make an end of them"** — Hebrew perfects; כִּלָּה "bring to an end" (Exod 32:10).
- **"the covenant of their ancestors" → "the covenant of the former ones"** (v45) — Hebrew בְּרִית רִאשֹׁנִים "of the first ones". **"in the sight of the heathen" → "before the eyes of the nations"** — Hebrew לְעֵינֵי הַגּוֹיִם.
- **"judgments and laws, which the LORD made" → "the ordinances and the torot that Yahweh gave"** (v46) — Hebrew הַתּוֹרֹת, the plural of *torah*; נָתַן "give". "By the hand of" is word for word.

### Notes

- **The speech** — v1–45 continue the words given on Mount Sinai from 25:1; v46 closes them.
- **Blessing and curse** — v3–13 "if you walk in my statutes"; v14–39 "and if you do not listen", in five steps (v14, 18, 21, 23, 27), four of them with "seven times"; v40–45 the remembering of the covenant. Deut 28 is the parallel.
- **The Shabbats of the land** (v34–35, 43) — the years of 25:1–7 that were not kept; 2 Chr 36:21 cites this of the seventy years.
- **"Uncircumcised heart"** (v41) — so Deut 10:16; Jer 4:4; 9:26; Ezek 44:7; taken up in Rom 2:29.
- **Jacob, Isaac, Abraham** (v42) — named in the reverse of the usual order.
- **The covenant not broken** (v44) — "to break my covenant" is the phrase of v15, where the people break it.
