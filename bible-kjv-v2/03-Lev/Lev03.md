# Leviticus 3

[1] "And if his offering is a sacrifice of peace offerings, if he is bringing near from the herd, whether male or female, without blemish he shall bring it near before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[2] And he shall lay his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it at the entrance of the tent of meeting; and the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, the priests, shall dash the blood against the altar around.

[3] And he shall bring near from the sacrifice of the peace offerings an offering by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is on the entrails,

[4] and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is on the flanks, and the lobe on the liver; together with the kidneys he shall remove it.

[5] And the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall turn it into smoke on the altar, upon the burnt offering that is on the wood that is on the fire, an offering by fire of a soothing smell to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] And if his offering for a sacrifice of peace offerings to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is from the flock, male or female, without blemish he shall bring it near.

[7] If he is bringing near a lamb as his offering, then he shall bring it near before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[8] And he shall lay his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it before the tent of meeting; and the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall dash its blood against the altar around.

[9] And he shall bring near from the sacrifice of the peace offerings an offering by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: its fat, the fat tail whole — alongside the backbone he shall remove it — and the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is on the entrails,

[10] and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is on the flanks, and the lobe on the liver; together with the kidneys he shall remove it.

[11] And the priest shall turn it into smoke on the altar: bread of an offering by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] And if his offering is a goat, then he shall bring it near before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] And he shall lay his hand on its head and slaughter it before the tent of meeting; and the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall dash its blood against the altar around.

[14] And he shall bring near from it his offering, an offering by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is on the entrails,

[15] and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is on the flanks, and the lobe on the liver; together with the kidneys he shall remove it.

[16] And the priest shall turn them into smoke on the altar: bread of an offering by fire, for a soothing smell. All fat is for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[17] An everlasting statute for your generations in all your dwelling places: all fat and all blood you shall not eat."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"if his oblation be a sacrifice of peace offering" → "if his offering is a sacrifice of peace offerings"** (v1, 3, 6, 9) — Hebrew קָרְבָּן "offering" (what is brought near; KJV "oblation" here, "offering" in v2) and זֶבַח שְׁלָמִים: זֶבַח is a slaughtered sacrifice, שְׁלָמִים a plural of the root of שָׁלוֹם "peace, wholeness". What the name says of the sacrifice ("peace", "well-being", "repayment") is not certain.
- **"if he offer it of the herd" → "if he is bringing near from the herd"** (v1, 7) — Hebrew הוּא מַקְרִיב, a participle; the verb is הִקְרִיב "bring near", the root of קָרְבָּן. The Hebrew has no "it" here.
- **"without blemish"** (v1, 6) — Hebrew תָּמִים, "whole, complete": the word that is "blameless" of a man. In v9 the same word is "whole" of the fat tail.
- **"kill it at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation" → "slaughter it at the entrance of the tent of meeting"** (v2, 8, 13) — Hebrew שָׁחַט; פֶּתַח אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד.
- **"Aaron's sons the priests shall sprinkle the blood upon the altar round about" → "the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall dash the blood against the altar around"** (v2, 8, 13) — Hebrew זָרַק, to throw from a bowl.
- **"an offering made by fire" → "an offering by fire"** (v3, 5, 9, 11, 14, 16) — Hebrew אִשֶּׁה, commonly derived from אֵשׁ "fire"; the derivation is not certain.
- **"the inwards" → "the entrails"** (v3, 9, 14) — Hebrew קֶרֶב.
- **"which is by the flanks" → "which is on the flanks"** (v4, 10, 15) — Hebrew עַל־הַכְּסָלִים, the loin muscles by the kidneys. **"the caul above the liver, with the kidneys, it shall he take away" → "the lobe on the liver; together with the kidneys he shall remove it"** — Hebrew הַיֹּתֶרֶת עַל־הַכָּבֵד, literally "what is over on the liver": an appendage of the liver, the exact part uncertain (Exod 29:13). עַל־הַכְּלָיוֹת is literally "upon the kidneys": with them, or close by them.
- **"burn it on the altar upon the burnt sacrifice" → "turn it into smoke on the altar, upon the burnt offering"** (v5, 11, 16) — Hebrew הִקְטִיר, literally "make smoke"; "burn" is another verb. עַל־הָעֹלָה: upon the burnt offering already lying on the wood.
- **"of a sweet savour" → "of a soothing smell"** (v5, 16) — Hebrew רֵיחַ נִיחֹחַ, from נוּחַ "rest".
- **"If he offer a lamb for his offering"** (v7) — Hebrew כֶּשֶׂב. The verse begins without "and".
- **"the fat thereof, and the whole rump, it shall he take off hard by the backbone" → "its fat, the fat tail whole — alongside the backbone he shall remove it"** (v9) — Hebrew חֶלְבּוֹ הָאַלְיָה תְמִימָה, with no "and": the fat tail of the fat-tailed sheep is named as its fat. עָצֶה is found only here: the bone at the root of the tail, the backbone.
- **"it is the food of the offering made by fire" → "bread of an offering by fire"** (v11, 16) — Hebrew לֶחֶם אִשֶּׁה: לֶחֶם is "bread", and so food in general. The two nouns may also stand side by side: "bread, an offering by fire". The Hebrew has no "it is".
- **"all the fat is the LORD'S" → "All fat is for Yahweh"** (v16) — Hebrew כָּל־חֵלֶב לַיהוָה, without the article.
- **"It shall be a perpetual statute for your generations throughout all your dwellings" → "An everlasting statute for your generations in all your dwelling places"** (v17) — Hebrew חֻקַּת עוֹלָם, literally "a statute of the age"; the Hebrew has no verb. **"that ye eat neither fat nor blood" → "all fat and all blood you shall not eat"** — Hebrew כָּל־חֵלֶב וְכָל־דָּם לֹא תֹאכֵלוּ.

### Notes

- **Three animals** — the sacrifice of peace offerings may be from the herd (v1–5), a lamb (v6–11) or a goat (v12–16); male or female.
- **Fat and blood (v17)** — neither is eaten. Of the blood see Gen 9:4; Lev 17:11.
- **Who eats** — this chapter names only what goes on the altar; the flesh and who eats it are laid down in 7:11–36.
