# Leviticus 3

[1] "If his offering is a sacrifice of peace offering — if he offers it from the herd, whether male or female — he shall offer it without blemish before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[2] He shall lay his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting; and ⟦aaron|Aharon's|Aaron's⟧ sons the priests shall throw the blood against the altar all around.

[3] From the sacrifice of the peace offering he shall bring as an offering made by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: the fat that covers the entrails, and all the fat that is on the entrails,

[4] and the two kidneys and the fat on them at the flanks, and the lobe above the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys.

[5] ⟦aaron|Aharon's|Aaron's⟧ sons shall turn it into smoke on the altar, on top of the burnt offering that is on the wood that is on the fire; it is an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] If his offering for a sacrifice of peace offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is from the flock — male or female — he shall offer it without blemish.

[7] If he offers a lamb for his offering, he shall offer it before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[8] He shall lay his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it before the Tent of Meeting; and ⟦aaron|Aharon's|Aaron's⟧ sons shall throw its blood against the altar all around.

[9] From the sacrifice of the peace offering he shall bring as an offering made by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: its fat and the whole fat tail — which he shall remove close to the backbone — and the fat that covers the entrails, and all the fat that is on the entrails,

[10] and the two kidneys and the fat on them at the flanks, and the lobe above the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys.

[11] The priest shall turn it into smoke on the altar; it is food, an offering made by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] If his offering is a goat, he shall offer it before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] He shall lay his hand on its head and slaughter it before the Tent of Meeting; and ⟦aaron|Aharon's|Aaron's⟧ sons shall throw its blood against the altar all around.

[14] From it he shall bring as his offering, an offering made by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is on the entrails,

[15] and the two kidneys and the fat on them at the flanks, and the lobe above the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys.

[16] The priest shall turn them into smoke on the altar; it is food, an offering made by fire, for a pleasing aroma. All fat belongs to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[17] It shall be a perpetual statute throughout your generations, in all your dwellings: you shall eat no fat and no blood."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"peace offering" (v1, 3, 6, 9)** — Hebrew זֶבַח שְׁלָמִים *zevach shelamim*. *Shalom*-related — "well-being / completeness" offerings. Part is burned, part goes to the priests, part is eaten by the worshipers in a communal meal — the most convivial of sacrifices.
- **"rump" → "fat tail"** (v9) — as in Ex 29:22. The *alyah* of fat-tailed sheep.
- **"it is the food of the offering" → "it is food, an offering"** (v11, 16) — Hebrew לֶחֶם אִשֶּׁה *lechem ishsheh*. "Food-offering." *Lechem* = bread / food generally. The offering is spoken of as food for Yahweh — anthropomorphic ritual language.

### Notes

- **No fat, no blood** (v17) — two universal prohibitions in Israelite dietary law. Blood = life (Gen 9:4, Lev 17:11); fat = the choicest portion, reserved for Yahweh.
- **Peace offering meal** — Unlike burnt offering (wholly burned) and sin offering (priest's portion), the peace offering generates a sacred meal shared by priest and worshiper before Yahweh. Communal / relational.
