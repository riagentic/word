# Leviticus 18

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and say to them: 'I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] You shall not do as they do in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, where you lived; and you shall not do as they do in the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, where I am bringing you; you shall not walk in their statutes.

[4] You shall do my ordinances and keep my statutes, to walk in them. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[5] You shall therefore keep my statutes and my ordinances; the one who does them shall live by them. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] None of you shall approach any close relative to uncover nakedness. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] The nakedness of your father — that is, the nakedness of your mother — you shall not uncover; she is your mother, you shall not uncover her nakedness.

[8] The nakedness of your father's wife you shall not uncover; it is your father's nakedness.

[9] The nakedness of your sister — the daughter of your father, or the daughter of your mother, whether born at home or born abroad — their nakedness you shall not uncover.

[10] The nakedness of your son's daughter or your daughter's daughter — their nakedness you shall not uncover; for their nakedness is your own.

[11] The nakedness of your father's wife's daughter, begotten of your father — she is your sister; you shall not uncover her nakedness.

[12] You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father's sister; she is your father's near kinswoman.

[13] You shall not uncover the nakedness of your mother's sister; for she is your mother's near kinswoman.

[14] You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father's brother; you shall not approach his wife: she is your aunt.

[15] You shall not uncover the nakedness of your daughter-in-law: she is your son's wife; you shall not uncover her nakedness.

[16] You shall not uncover the nakedness of your brother's wife; it is your brother's nakedness.

[17] You shall not uncover the nakedness of a woman and her daughter; nor shall you take her son's daughter or her daughter's daughter to uncover her nakedness — they are near kinswomen; it is wickedness.

[18] You shall not take a woman as a rival wife to her sister, uncovering her nakedness while her sister is still alive.

[19] You shall not approach a woman to uncover her nakedness while she is in her menstrual separation.

[20] You shall not lie sexually with your neighbor's wife, to defile yourself with her.

[21] You shall not give any of your offspring to pass through the fire to Molech, and you shall not profane the name of your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[22] You shall not lie with a male as with a woman; it is an abomination.

[23] You shall not lie with any animal to defile yourself with it; and a woman shall not stand before an animal to mate with it; it is perversion.

[24] Do not defile yourselves by any of these things; for by all these the nations I am casting out before you have defiled themselves.

[25] The land has been defiled; therefore I am visiting its iniquity upon it, and the land itself is vomiting out its inhabitants.

[26] You therefore shall keep my statutes and my ordinances, and shall not commit any of these abominations — neither the native nor the foreigner who sojourns among you.

[27] For all these abominations were done by the men of the land who were before you, and the land has become defiled.

[28] Lest the land vomit you out also when you defile it, as it vomited out the nation that was before you.

[29] For whoever commits any of these abominations — those who do so shall be cut off from among their people.

[30] So you shall keep my charge, that you do not follow any of these abominable customs that were practiced before you, and that you do not defile yourselves by them. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"put apart for her uncleanness" → "in her menstrual separation"** (v19) — Hebrew נִדָּה *niddah*.
- **"lie carnally" → "lie sexually"** (v20) — Hebrew שְׁכָבְתְּךָ לְזָרַע *shekhavtekha le-zara* ("your lying for seed"). Sexual intercourse with seed-emission.
- **"pass through the fire to Molech"** (v21) — Hebrew מֹלֶךְ *Molekh*. Canaanite / Phoenician deity associated with child-sacrifice. Kept; proper name. Possibly related to *melek* "king" with vowel-pointing of *bosheth* "shame."
- **"mankind / womankind" → "male / woman"** (v22) — Hebrew זָכָר *zakar* / אִשָּׁה *ishah*. Simplified; *-kind* archaic.
- **"abomination" (v22) → "abomination"** — Hebrew תּוֹעֵבָה *to'evah*. Kept; a moral / covenantal abomination (distinct from *sheqets* ritual-abhorrence, ch 11).
- **"confusion" → "perversion"** (v23) — Hebrew תֶּבֶל *tevel*. Lit. "mixing / perversion / category-violation." Crossing of species-boundary.
- **"take a wife to her sister to vex her" → "take a woman as a rival wife to her sister"** (v18) — Hebrew לִצְרֹר *litzror* = to make her a rival-co-wife. Prohibits marrying two sisters simultaneously.

### Notes
- **Ethical center of the Holiness Code** — sexual ethics, idolatry, and land-covenant here fused: sexual/idolatrous impurity pollutes the land, which eventually "vomits out" its inhabitants.
- **"Uncover nakedness"** — biblical euphemism for sexual intercourse (Heb גָּלָה עֶרְוָה *galah ervah*).
- **Forbidden degrees** (v6–18) — parallel list in ch 20 (with penalties). The Anglican "Table of Kindred and Affinity" is drawn from these verses.
- **Molech child-sacrifice** (v21) — pagan ritual of offspring-offering. Condemned also in 2 Kgs 23:10, Jer 32:35. The juxtaposition with sexual laws groups all "life-misuse" offenses.
- **Land-vomit motif** (v25, 28) — moral and ritual pollution is physical for the land; the land itself is a party to the covenant. Deut 30 picks this up: land-curse and land-blessing follow behavior.
- **Paul's "live by them"** (v5) — Rom 10:5, Gal 3:12 quote this verse as encapsulating the old covenant's principle of life through law-keeping.
