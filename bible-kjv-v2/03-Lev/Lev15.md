# Leviticus 15

[1] Yahweh spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

[2] "Speak to the children of Israel and say to them: 'When any man has a discharge from his body, his discharge is unclean.

[3] This shall be his uncleanness in his discharge: whether his body runs with the discharge or his body is blocked from the discharge, it is his uncleanness.

[4] Every bed on which the one with the discharge lies shall be unclean, and every object on which he sits shall be unclean.

[5] Whoever touches his bed shall wash his clothes and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until evening.

[6] Whoever sits on anything on which the one with the discharge has sat shall wash his clothes and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until evening.

[7] Whoever touches the body of the one with the discharge shall wash his clothes and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until evening.

[8] If the one with the discharge spits on someone who is clean, that person shall wash his clothes and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until evening.

[9] Any saddle on which the one with the discharge rides shall be unclean.

[10] Whoever touches anything that was under him shall be unclean until evening; and whoever carries such things shall wash his clothes and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until evening.

[11] Whomever the one with the discharge touches without having rinsed his hands in water shall wash his clothes and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until evening.

[12] Any earthenware vessel that the one with the discharge touches shall be broken, and every wooden vessel shall be rinsed in water.

[13] When the one with the discharge is cleansed of his discharge, then he shall count for himself seven days for his cleansing. He shall wash his clothes and bathe his body in fresh water, and he shall be clean.

[14] On the eighth day he shall take two turtledoves or two young pigeons, and come before Yahweh to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, and give them to the priest.

[15] The priest shall offer them — one for a sin offering and the other for a burnt offering — and the priest shall make atonement for him before Yahweh for his discharge.

[16] If a man has an emission of semen, he shall bathe his whole body in water, and be unclean until evening.

[17] Every garment and every skin on which the emission of semen is shall be washed with water, and be unclean until evening.

[18] When a man lies with a woman and there is an emission of semen, they shall both bathe themselves in water, and be unclean until evening.

[19] When a woman has a discharge, and her discharge in her body is blood, she shall be in her menstrual separation seven days; whoever touches her shall be unclean until evening.

[20] Anything on which she lies during her menstrual separation shall be unclean, and anything on which she sits shall be unclean.

[21] Whoever touches her bed shall wash his clothes and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until evening.

[22] Whoever touches anything on which she has sat shall wash his clothes and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until evening.

[23] Whether it is on the bed or on anything on which she sits when he touches it, he shall be unclean until evening.

[24] If a man lies with her at all and her menstrual flow comes upon him, he shall be unclean seven days, and every bed on which he lies shall be unclean.

[25] If a woman has a flow of blood for many days, not at the time of her menstrual separation, or if it continues beyond the time of her menstrual separation, all the days of the discharge of her uncleanness she shall be as in the days of her menstrual separation: she shall be unclean.

[26] Every bed on which she lies all the days of her discharge shall be to her as the bed of her menstrual separation; and everything on which she sits shall be unclean, as the uncleanness of her menstrual separation.

[27] Whoever touches these things shall be unclean; he shall wash his clothes and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until evening.

[28] But when she is cleansed of her discharge, she shall count for herself seven days, and after that she shall be clean.

[29] On the eighth day she shall take two turtledoves or two young pigeons and bring them to the priest, at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

[30] The priest shall offer one for a sin offering and the other for a burnt offering; and the priest shall make atonement for her before Yahweh for the discharge of her uncleanness.

[31] Thus you shall separate the children of Israel from their uncleanness, so that they do not die in their uncleanness by defiling my dwelling that is among them.

[32] This is the law of the one who has a discharge; of the one whose semen goes out from him and defiles him;

[33] of her who is menstruating in her uncleanness; of him who has a discharge, whether male or female; and of the man who lies with an unclean woman.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"running issue / issue" → "discharge"** (throughout) — Hebrew זוֹב *zov* = flow / discharge. Abnormal genital emission — likely gonorrhea or similar; not ordinary urine.
- **"seed of copulation" → "emission of semen"** (v16–18) — Hebrew שִׁכְבַת־זֶרַע *shikhvat-zera*. Ejaculation / semen — technical biological term.
- **"her flowers" → "her menstrual flow"** (v24) — Hebrew נִדָּתָהּ *niddatah*. *Flowers* archaic English for menses.
- **"put apart / separation" → "menstrual separation"** (v19, 20, 24–26) — Hebrew נִדָּה *niddah*, as in ch 12.
- **"turtles" → "turtledoves"** (v29) — archaic.
- **"she is sick of her flowers" → "she is menstruating"** (v33) — modernized.
- **"running water" → "fresh water"** (v13).

### Notes
- **Four categories of discharge** — (1) abnormal male discharge (v2–15), (2) normal semen emission (v16–18), (3) normal menstruation (v19–24), (4) abnormal female bleeding (v25–30). Abnormal flows require sacrifice; normal flows only water-washing.
- **Transmission of uncleanness** — sit/lie/touch/carry all transmit. Pragmatic: ritual impurity is not moral taint but a physical-category state.
- **Why sacrifice?** — the discharge defiles the sanctuary (v31). Sin offering purges the accumulated impurity that the person's condition has loaded onto the holy space.
- **Woman with flow of blood** (v25) — NT Mk 5:25–34 / Lk 8:43–48: the woman with twelve-year *zov* touches Jesus; she is ritually unclean and knows it. Jesus reverses the flow of contagion — his holiness cleanses rather than her uncleanness defiling.
- **"Lest they die in their uncleanness"** (v31) — unpurified impurity accumulates at the sanctuary; if left, it eventually drives Yahweh's presence out (cf. Ezek 8–11). The purification system preserves divine-human proximity.
