# Leviticus 15

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and say to them: 'Any man, when he is flowing from his flesh — his flow, it is unclean.

[3] And this shall be his uncleanness in his flow: his flesh runs with his flow, or his flesh is stopped up from his flow — it is his uncleanness.

[4] Every bed on which the one who flows lies shall be unclean, and every article on which he sits shall be unclean.

[5] And a man who touches his bed shall wash his garments and wash in water and be unclean until the evening.

[6] And he who sits on the article on which the one who flows sits shall wash his garments and wash in water and be unclean until the evening.

[7] And he who touches the flesh of the one who flows shall wash his garments and wash in water and be unclean until the evening.

[8] And when the one who flows spits on the clean one, then he shall wash his garments and wash in water and be unclean until the evening.

[9] And every saddle on which the one who flows rides shall be unclean.

[10] And everyone who touches anything that is under him shall be unclean until the evening; and he who carries them shall wash his garments and wash in water and be unclean until the evening.

[11] And everyone whom the one who flows touches, and he has not rinsed his hands in water — he shall wash his garments and wash in water and be unclean until the evening.

[12] And an earthen vessel that the one who flows touches shall be broken, and every article of wood shall be rinsed in water.

[13] And when the one who flows is clean from his flow, then he shall number for himself seven days for his cleansing, and he shall wash his garments, and he shall wash his flesh in living water, and he shall be clean.

[14] And on the eighth day he shall take for himself two turtledoves or two sons of a dove, and he shall come before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to the entrance of the tent of meeting and give them to the priest.

[15] And the priest shall offer them, one a sin offering and the other a burnt offering; and the priest shall make atonement for him before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from his flow.

[16] And a man, when an emission of seed goes out from him, then he shall wash all his flesh in water and be unclean until the evening.

[17] And every garment and every skin on which there is an emission of seed shall be washed in water and be unclean until the evening.

[18] And a woman with whom a man lies with an emission of seed — they shall wash in water and be unclean until the evening.

[19] And a woman, when she is flowing — blood is her flow in her flesh — seven days she shall be in her impurity; and everyone who touches her shall be unclean until the evening.

[20] And everything on which she lies in her impurity shall be unclean, and everything on which she sits shall be unclean.

[21] And everyone who touches her bed shall wash his garments and wash in water and be unclean until the evening.

[22] And everyone who touches any article on which she sits shall wash his garments and wash in water and be unclean until the evening.

[23] And if it is on the bed, or on the article on which she is sitting, when he touches it he shall be unclean until the evening.

[24] And if a man surely lies with her, and her impurity is on him, then he shall be unclean seven days; and every bed on which he lies shall be unclean.

[25] And a woman, when the flow of her blood flows many days, not at the time of her impurity, or when she flows beyond her impurity — all the days of the flow of her uncleanness she shall be as in the days of her impurity; she is unclean.

[26] Every bed on which she lies all the days of her flow shall be to her as the bed of her impurity; and every article on which she sits shall be unclean, as the uncleanness of her impurity.

[27] And everyone who touches them shall be unclean; and he shall wash his garments and wash in water and be unclean until the evening.

[28] And if she is clean from her flow, then she shall number for herself seven days, and afterward she shall be clean.

[29] And on the eighth day she shall take for herself two turtledoves or two sons of a dove, and she shall bring them to the priest, to the entrance of the tent of meeting.

[30] And the priest shall offer the one a sin offering and the other a burnt offering; and the priest shall make atonement for her before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from the flow of her uncleanness.

[31] And you shall keep the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ apart from their uncleanness, and they shall not die in their uncleanness by their defiling my dwelling that is in their midst.

[32] This is ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ of the one who flows, and of him from whom an emission of seed goes out, to become unclean by it,

[33] and of her who is unwell in her impurity, and of the one who flows with his flow, for the male and for the female, and of the man who lies with an unclean woman.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v2, 31) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל. **"When any man" → "Any man, when"** (v2) — Hebrew אִישׁ אִישׁ כִּי, literally "a man, a man, when".
- **"hath a running issue out of his flesh, because of his issue he is unclean" → "is flowing from his flesh — his flow, it is unclean"** (v2) — Hebrew זָב מִבְּשָׂרוֹ זוֹבוֹ טָמֵא הוּא. The verb זוּב is "flow" (as "flowing with milk and honey"); the noun זוֹב is "flow" and the participle הַזָּב "the one who flows", one root and one English word through the chapter (also of the woman, v19, 25–30). What sickness is meant the text does not say. טָמֵא הוּא may be "it is unclean" (the flow) or "he is unclean"; the Hebrew accents join it to "his flow". **"flesh" kept** (v2, 3, 7, 13, 16, 19) — Hebrew בָּשָׂר; in v2, 3 and 19 it is commonly understood of the private parts, which the word itself does not say.
- **"whether his flesh run with his issue, or his flesh be stopped from his issue" → "his flesh runs with his flow, or his flesh is stopped up from his flow"** (v3) — Hebrew רָר … אוֹ־הֶחְתִּים: there is no "whether". רָר stands only here (the noun רִיר is "slime, spittle"); הֶחְתִּים is from חָתַם "seal". The Samaritan text and the Septuagint (from memory) have a longer verse; the Masoretic Text is translated.
- **"every thing, whereon he sitteth" → "every article on which he sits"** (v4, 6, 22, 23, 26) — Hebrew כְּלִי, any made thing; in v12 the same word is "vessel" of earthenware and "article" of wood.
- **"wash his clothes, and bathe himself in water" → "wash his garments and wash in water"** (v5–11, 13, 21, 22, 27) — Hebrew יְכַבֵּס בְּגָדָיו וְרָחַץ בַּמַּיִם: two verbs, כִּבֵּס of washing cloth and רָחַץ of washing the body; English has "wash" for both. "Himself" is not in the Hebrew. **"until the even" → "until the evening"** — Hebrew עַד־הָעָרֶב.
- **"if he that hath the issue spit upon him that is clean" → "when the one who flows spits on the clean one"** (v8) — Hebrew בַּטָּהוֹר.
- **"what saddle soever" → "every saddle"** (v9) — Hebrew מֶרְכָּב, literally "a thing ridden on" (a saddle, or the seat of a chariot).
- **"he that beareth any of those things" → "he who carries them"** (v10) — Hebrew וְהַנּוֹשֵׂא אוֹתָם.
- **"whomsoever he toucheth that hath the issue, and hath not rinsed his hands" → "everyone whom the one who flows touches, and he has not rinsed his hands"** (v11) — Hebrew וְיָדָיו לֹא־שָׁטַף: the hands are those of the one who flows; the Hebrew sentence is kept as it stands.
- **"the vessel of earth … every vessel of wood" → "an earthen vessel … every article of wood"** (v12) — Hebrew כְּלִי־חֶרֶשׂ and כְּלִי־עֵץ, the one word כְּלִי (see v4).
- **"is cleansed of his issue" → "is clean from his flow"** (v13, 28) — Hebrew יִטְהַר, the simple verb "be clean", as at the end of the verse. **"running water" → "living water"** (v13) — Hebrew מַיִם חַיִּים: water from a spring or a stream, as 14:5. **"bathe his flesh" → "wash his flesh"** — Hebrew רָחַץ.
- **"two young pigeons" → "two sons of a dove"** (v14, 29) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יוֹנָה, as 1:14; 12:8; whether בֵּן says "young" or only "one of the kind" is not certain. **"take to him" → "take for himself"** — Hebrew לוֹ. **"tabernacle of the congregation" → "tent of meeting"** (v14, 29) — Hebrew אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד.
- **"the one for a sin offering" → "one a sin offering"** (v15, 30) — Hebrew אֶחָד חַטָּאת, without "for". "Offer" is עָשָׂה, literally "do, make". **"for his issue" → "from his flow"** — Hebrew מִזּוֹבוֹ, with מִן "from" (v30: "from the flow of her uncleanness").
- **"if any man's seed of copulation go out from him" → "a man, when an emission of seed goes out from him"** (v16) — Hebrew שִׁכְבַת־זֶרַע, literally "a lying of seed" (or "an outpouring of seed": the derivation of שְׁכָבָה is not certain; it is the "layer" of dew in Exod 16:13); so also v17, 18, 32. זֶרַע is "seed".
- **"The woman also with whom man shall lie with seed of copulation" → "a woman with whom a man lies with an emission of seed"** (v18) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר יִשְׁכַּב אִישׁ אֹתָהּ שִׁכְבַת־זָרַע; "they shall both" has no "both" in the Hebrew.
- **"if a woman have an issue, and her issue in her flesh be blood, she shall be put apart seven days" → "a woman, when she is flowing — blood is her flow in her flesh — seven days she shall be in her impurity"** (v19) — Hebrew כִּי־תִהְיֶה זָבָה, the same root as of the man; בְּנִדָּתָהּ, "in her impurity": נִדָּה is the impurity of the monthly flow (12:2), kept as one word in v19, 20, 24, 25, 26, 33; its root sense ("separation" or "expulsion") is not certain.
- **"whether it be on the bed" → "if it is on the bed"** (v23) — Hebrew וְאִם עַל־הַמִּשְׁכָּב הוּא: הוּא is "it" (a thing lying on the bed) or "he"; the verse is hard and is kept word for word.
- **"if any man lie with her at all, and her flowers be upon him" → "if a man surely lies with her, and her impurity is on him"** (v24) — Hebrew שָׁכֹב יִשְׁכַּב, literally "lying he lies"; וּתְהִי נִדָּתָהּ עָלָיו, "and her impurity is on him" (the verb has a wish form, "and let … be").
- **"if a woman have an issue of her blood many days out of the time of her separation, or if it run beyond the time of her separation" → "a woman, when the flow of her blood flows many days, not at the time of her impurity, or when she flows beyond her impurity"** (v25) — Hebrew כִּי־יָזוּב זוֹב דָּמָהּ, verb and noun of one root; תָזוּב is "she flows"; "the time of" stands only once in the Hebrew. **"she shall be unclean" → "she is unclean"** — Hebrew טְמֵאָה הִוא.
- **"whosoever toucheth those things" → "everyone who touches them"** (v27) — Hebrew בָּם; "things" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Thus shall ye separate the children of Israel from their uncleanness; that they die not" → "And you shall keep the sons of Israel apart from their uncleanness, and they shall not die"** (v31) — Hebrew וְהִזַּרְתֶּם, from נָזַר "keep apart" (the root of "Nazirite"); the Samaritan text and the Septuagint, from memory, have "you shall warn" (וְהִזְהַרְתֶּם); the Masoretic Text is translated. **"when they defile my tabernacle" → "by their defiling my dwelling"** — Hebrew בְּטַמְּאָם אֶת־מִשְׁכָּנִי; "defile" is "make unclean", of the root of "uncleanness".
- **"the law" → "the torah"** (v32) — Hebrew תּוֹרַת, the instruction for one matter. **"and is defiled therewith" → "to become unclean by it"** — Hebrew לְטָמְאָה־בָהּ.
- **"her that is sick of her flowers" → "her who is unwell in her impurity"** (v33) — Hebrew הַדָּוָה בְּנִדָּתָהּ, as 12:2. **"him that hath an issue, of the man, and of the woman" → "the one who flows with his flow, for the male and for the female"** — Hebrew וְהַזָּב אֶת־זוֹבוֹ לַזָּכָר וְלַנְּקֵבָה. **"her that is unclean" → "an unclean woman"** — Hebrew טְמֵאָה.

### Notes

- **Four cases** — (1) the flow of a man (v2–15), (2) an emission of seed (v16–18), (3) the monthly impurity of a woman (v19–24), (4) a flow of blood outside that time (v25–30). For (1) and (4) two birds are brought on the eighth day; for (2) and (3) there is washing and waiting only.
- **How uncleanness passes on** — by lying, sitting, touching and carrying. The chapter does not call any of these cases sin.
- **The dwelling** (v31) — the uncleanness of Israel makes the dwelling of Yahweh in their midst unclean; this is the reason given for the whole chapter (cf. 16:16).
- **Woman with a flow of blood** (v25) — Mark 5:25–34 and Luke 8:43–48: the woman who had a flow of blood twelve years touches the garment of Jesus and is healed.
