# Leviticus 4

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying: 'If any person sins unintentionally against any of the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ concerning things that ought not to be done, and does any of them —

[3] if it is the anointed priest who sins, so as to bring guilt on the people, then he shall offer for his sin that he has committed a young bull without blemish to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ as a sin offering.

[4] He shall bring the bull to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, lay his hand on the bull's head, and slaughter the bull before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] The anointed priest shall take some of the bull's blood and bring it to the Tent of Meeting.

[6] The priest shall dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle some of the blood seven times before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, in front of the veil of the sanctuary.

[7] The priest shall put some of the blood on the horns of the altar of fragrant incense, which is before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in the Tent of Meeting; and all the rest of the blood of the bull he shall pour out at the base of the altar of burnt offering, which is at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

[8] He shall take from it all the fat of the bull of the sin offering: the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is on the entrails,

[9] and the two kidneys with the fat that is on them at the flanks, and the lobe above the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys —

[10] as it is taken from the bull of the sacrifice of peace offerings — and the priest shall turn them into smoke on the altar of burnt offering.

[11] But the hide of the bull, all its flesh with its head and its legs, its entrails and its dung —

[12] the whole bull — he shall carry outside the camp to a clean place, where the ashes are poured out, and burn it on wood with fire. Where the ashes are poured out, it shall be burned.

[13] If the whole congregation of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ sins unintentionally, and the matter is hidden from the eyes of the assembly, and they have done any one of the things that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded not to be done, and are guilty;

[14] when the sin that they have committed becomes known, the congregation shall bring a young bull as a sin offering, and they shall bring it before the Tent of Meeting.

[15] The elders of the congregation shall lay their hands on the head of the bull before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the bull shall be slaughtered before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[16] The anointed priest shall bring some of the bull's blood to the Tent of Meeting,

[17] and the priest shall dip his finger in some of the blood and sprinkle it seven times before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, in front of the veil.

[18] He shall put some of the blood on the horns of the altar that is before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in the Tent of Meeting, and he shall pour out all the rest of the blood at the base of the altar of burnt offering that is at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

[19] And all its fat he shall remove from it and turn into smoke on the altar.

[20] He shall do with the bull as he did with the bull of the sin offering; so shall he do with this. And the priest shall make atonement for them, and they shall be forgiven.

[21] He shall carry the bull outside the camp and burn it as he burned the first bull; it is a sin offering for the assembly.

[22] When a ruler sins unintentionally and does any one of the things that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has commanded not to be done, and is guilty;

[23] or if his sin that he has committed is made known to him, he shall bring as his offering a male goat without blemish.

[24] He shall lay his hand on the head of the goat and slaughter it in the place where they slaughter the burnt offering before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. It is a sin offering.

[25] The priest shall take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and pour out its blood at the base of the altar of burnt offering.

[26] He shall turn all its fat into smoke on the altar, like the fat of the sacrifice of peace offerings. Thus the priest shall make atonement for him on account of his sin, and he shall be forgiven.

[27] If any one of the common people sins unintentionally, by doing any one of the things that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded not to be done, and is guilty;

[28] or if his sin that he has committed is made known to him, he shall bring as his offering a female goat without blemish for his sin that he has committed.

[29] He shall lay his hand on the head of the sin offering and slaughter the sin offering in the place of the burnt offering.

[30] The priest shall take some of its blood with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and pour out the rest of its blood at the base of the altar.

[31] And he shall remove all its fat, as the fat is removed from the sacrifice of peace offerings; and the priest shall turn it into smoke on the altar, as a pleasing aroma to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. So the priest shall make atonement for him, and he shall be forgiven.

[32] If he brings a lamb as his offering for a sin offering, he shall bring a female without blemish.

[33] He shall lay his hand on the head of the sin offering and slaughter it as a sin offering in the place where they slaughter the burnt offering.

[34] The priest shall take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and pour out the rest of its blood at the base of the altar.

[35] He shall remove all its fat, as the fat of the lamb is removed from the sacrifice of peace offerings; and the priest shall turn them into smoke on the altar, upon the offerings by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. ## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"soul" → "person"** (v2) — *Nephesh* as "person / individual," not "soul" in philosophical sense.
- **"through ignorance" → "unintentionally"** (v2, 13, 22, 27) — Hebrew בִּשְׁגָגָה *bi-shegagah*. Inadvertent sin — from *shagag* "to err / stray." The sin offering is specifically for unwitting violations (intentional "high-handed" sin carries different sanctions, Num 15:30–31).
- **"sin offering" (throughout)** — Hebrew חַטָּאת *chatta't*. Purification / sin-ritual offering — serves to purify persons and sanctuary from defilement caused by sin.
- **"ruler" (v22)** — Hebrew נָשִׂיא *nasi* = tribal chieftain / leader.

### Notes

- **Sin offerings by rank** — different sacrifices for different ranks: high priest (bull), whole congregation (bull), chieftain (male goat), common person (female goat or lamb), poor person (birds or grain — ch 5). Gradated economic accessibility while maintaining ritual logic.
- **Blood on altar horns / at altar base / before veil** — graded blood-application corresponds to rank of sinner. The more central the sinner (high priest, whole congregation), the more the blood penetrates toward the Most Holy Place.
- **"Outside the camp" disposal** (v12, 21) — high-rank sin offerings burned outside camp — the flesh has absorbed impurity and cannot be eaten. Heb 13:11–13 draws on this: "Jesus also suffered outside the gate... let us go to him outside the camp."
