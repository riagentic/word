# Leviticus 20

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "And to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ you shall say: 'Any man of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and of the sojourner who sojourns in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, who gives of his seed to Molech, shall surely be put to death; the people of the land shall stone him with stone.

[3] And I, I will set my face against that man and will cut him off from the midst of his people, for he has given of his seed to Molech, so as to defile my sanctuary and to profane my holy name.

[4] And if the people of the land indeed hide their eyes from that man when he gives of his seed to Molech, so as not to put him to death,

[5] then I, I will put my face against that man and against his clan, and I will cut off him and all who play the prostitute after him, to play the prostitute after Molech, from the midst of their people.

[6] And the soul that turns to the ghosts and to the knowing spirits, to play the prostitute after them: I will set my face against that soul and will cut him off from the midst of his people.

[7] And you shall sanctify yourselves and be holy, for I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[8] And you shall keep my statutes and do them. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who sanctifies you.

[9] For any man who curses his father or his mother shall surely be put to death; his father or his mother he has cursed; his bloodguilt is on him.

[10] And a man who commits adultery with a man's wife, who commits adultery with his neighbor's wife: the adulterer and the adulteress shall surely be put to death.

[11] And a man who lies with his father's wife: his father's nakedness he has uncovered; both of them shall surely be put to death; their bloodguilt is on them.

[12] And a man who lies with his daughter-in-law: both of them shall surely be put to death; they have done confusion; their bloodguilt is on them.

[13] And a man who lies with a male the lyings of a woman: both of them have done an abomination; they shall surely be put to death; their bloodguilt is on them.

[14] And a man who takes a woman and her mother: it is depravity; with fire they shall burn him and them, and there shall be no depravity in your midst.

[15] And a man who gives his lying with a beast shall surely be put to death; and the beast you shall kill.

[16] And a woman who comes near to any beast to mate with it: you shall kill the woman and the beast; they shall surely be put to death; their bloodguilt is on them.

[17] And a man who takes his sister, his father's daughter or his mother's daughter, and sees her nakedness, and she sees his nakedness: it is a disgrace; and they shall be cut off before the eyes of the sons of their people; his sister's nakedness he has uncovered; his iniquity he shall bear.

[18] And a man who lies with a woman who is unwell and uncovers her nakedness: her fountain he has laid bare, and she has uncovered the fountain of her blood; and both of them shall be cut off from the midst of their people.

[19] And the nakedness of your mother's sister and of your father's sister you shall not uncover, for he has laid bare his near kin; their iniquity they shall bear.

[20] And a man who lies with his aunt: his uncle's nakedness he has uncovered; their sin they shall bear; childless they shall die.

[21] And a man who takes his brother's wife: it is impurity; his brother's nakedness he has uncovered; childless they shall be.

[22] And you shall keep all my statutes and all my ordinances, and do them; and the land to which I am bringing you, to dwell in it, shall not vomit you out.

[23] And you shall not walk in the statutes of the nation that I am sending away from before you; for all these things they did, and I loathed them.

[24] And I said to you: You, you shall possess their ground, and I, I will give it to you to possess it, a land flowing with milk and honey. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who has separated you from the peoples.

[25] And you shall separate the clean beast from the unclean, and the unclean birds from the clean; and you shall not make your souls detestable by beast or by bird or by anything with which the ground creeps, which I have separated for you to pronounce unclean.

[26] And you shall be holy to me, for I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, am holy; and I have separated you from the peoples to be mine.

[27] And a man or a woman, when there is in them a ghost or a knowing spirit, shall surely be put to death; with stone they shall stone them; their bloodguilt is on them.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"Again, thou shalt say to the children of Israel" → "And to the sons of Israel you shall say"** (v2) — Hebrew וְאֶל־בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל תֹּאמַר; "again" is not in the Hebrew. **"Whosoever he be" → "Any man"** (v2, 9) — Hebrew אִישׁ אִישׁ, literally "a man, a man". **"the strangers that sojourn" → "the sojourner who sojourns"** — Hebrew הַגֵּר הַגָּר, a singular for the whole class.
- **"giveth any of his seed unto Molech" → "gives of his seed to Molech"** (v2, 3, 4) — Hebrew יִתֵּן מִזַּרְעוֹ לַמֹּלֶךְ. זֶרַע is "seed": his children. The Hebrew has the article every time (v5 הַמֹּלֶךְ, written out), "the Molech"; English "Molech" is kept without it, as in 18:21. The word has the consonants of מֶלֶךְ "king" with other vowels. Who or what is meant — a god, or a kind of sacrifice — is disputed; the word is kept as it stands. What the "giving" was is said in 18:21 ("to make pass over") and 2 Kgs 23:10 ("through the fire").
- **"shall surely be put to death"** (v2, 9–13, 15, 16, 27) — Hebrew מוֹת יוּמָת, literally "dying he shall be made to die". **"stone him with stones" → "stone him with stone"** (v2, 27) — Hebrew רָגַם … בָאָבֶן, "stone" in the singular (as 24:23); רָגַם is another verb than the "stone" of Exod 19:13; 21:28 (סָקַל); English has one word for both.
- **"I will set my face against" / "put my face against"** (v3, 5, 6) — two Hebrew verbs: נָתַן "give, set" (v3, 6; 17:10) and שִׂים "put" (v5). The "I" is stressed in v3 and v5 (וַאֲנִי, וְשַׂמְתִּי אֲנִי). **"from among his people" → "from the midst of his people"** (v3, 5, 6, 18) — Hebrew מִקֶּרֶב.
- **"to defile my sanctuary" kept; "my holy name"** (v3) — Hebrew טַמֵּא, literally "make unclean"; שֵׁם קָדְשִׁי, literally "the name of my holiness".
- **"do any ways hide their eyes" → "indeed hide their eyes"** (v4) — Hebrew הַעְלֵם יַעְלִימוּ, literally "hiding they hide". **"and kill him not" → "so as not to put him to death"** — Hebrew לְבִלְתִּי הָמִית אֹתוֹ.
- **"his family" → "his clan"** (v5) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּחָה, the kin group. **"all that go a whoring after him, to commit whoredom with Molech" → "all who play the prostitute after him, to play the prostitute after Molech"** (v5, 6) — Hebrew זָנָה אַחֲרֵי both times: unfaithfulness to Yahweh pictured as prostitution.
- **"the soul that turneth after such as have familiar spirits, and after wizards" → "the soul that turns to the ghosts and to the knowing spirits"** (v6) — Hebrew הָאֹבֹת and הַיִּדְּעֹנִים, as in 19:31. נֶפֶשׁ "soul" is the person. **"cut him off"** — the Hebrew pronouns are masculine ("him", "his people"), though "soul" is a feminine word.
- **"Sanctify yourselves therefore" → "And you shall sanctify yourselves"** (v7) — Hebrew וְהִתְקַדִּשְׁתֶּם; "therefore" is not in the Hebrew. **"which sanctify you" → "who sanctifies you"** (v8) — Hebrew מְקַדִּשְׁכֶם.
- **"his father or his mother"** (v9) — the Hebrew has "and" both times (אָבִיו וְאֶת־אִמּוֹ), with the sense "or", as in Exod 21:17. **"curses"** — Hebrew קִלֵּל, literally "make light of". **"his blood shall be upon him" → "his bloodguilt is on him"** (v9, 11–13, 16, 27) — Hebrew דָּמָיו בּוֹ, literally "his bloods [are] in him": the plural of "blood" is the guilt for a death; it rests on the one put to death, not on those who put him to death.
- **"the man that committeth adultery with another man's wife, even he that committeth adultery with his neighbour's wife" → "a man who commits adultery with a man's wife, who commits adultery with his neighbor's wife"** (v10) — the Hebrew has the clause twice (אֲשֶׁר יִנְאַף אֶת־אֵשֶׁת אִישׁ אֲשֶׁר יִנְאַף אֶת־אֵשֶׁת רֵעֵהוּ); it is often taken as a copyist's doubling, but the Masoretic Text is kept. "Another" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"they have wrought confusion" → "they have done confusion"** (v12) — Hebrew תֶּבֶל, from בָּלַל "mix": a mixing of what must be kept apart (18:23).
- **"If a man also lie with mankind, as he lieth with a woman" → "a man who lies with a male the lyings of a woman"** (v13) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר יִשְׁכַּב אֶת־זָכָר מִשְׁכְּבֵי אִשָּׁה, word for word as 18:22: זָכָר is "male"; מִשְׁכְּבֵי אִשָּׁה, a plural, is "the lyings of a woman", lying as one lies with a woman.
- **"it is wickedness … they shall be burnt with fire, both he and they" → "it is depravity; with fire they shall burn him and them"** (v14) — Hebrew זִמָּה, a shameful design (18:17; 19:29); the verb is active with no subject named; "them" is feminine. "Takes" is the word for taking a wife.
- **"if a man lie with a beast" → "a man who gives his lying with a beast"** (v15) — Hebrew יִתֵּן שְׁכָבְתּוֹ בִּבְהֵמָה, as 18:23. **"slay" → "kill"** (v15, 16) — Hebrew הָרַג. "You shall kill" is plural in v15, singular in v16.
- **"approach unto any beast, and lie down thereto" → "comes near to any beast to mate with it"** (v16) — Hebrew לְרִבְעָה אֹתָהּ; רָבַע is "lie down" of animals mating (18:23; 19:19).
- **"it is a wicked thing" → "it is a disgrace"** (v17) — Hebrew חֶסֶד הוּא. The word has the letters of חֶסֶד *chesed*, "steadfast love", but here it is another word, "shame, disgrace", known from Aramaic and found again in Prov 14:34 (and the verb in Prov 25:10). The sense "steadfast love" is impossible in this verse. **"in the sight of their people" → "before the eyes of the sons of their people"** — literal.
- **"having her sickness" → "who is unwell"** (v18) — Hebrew דָּוָה, "faint, unwell": said of the monthly flow (12:2; 15:33). **"he hath discovered her fountain" → "her fountain he has laid bare"** — Hebrew הֶעֱרָה "make bare", another verb than "uncover" (גִּלָּה) in the same verse; מָקוֹר "fountain" (12:7). "Her blood" is a plural in Hebrew.
- **"for he uncovereth his near kin" → "for he has laid bare his near kin"** (v19) — Hebrew שְׁאֵרוֹ הֶעֱרָה; שְׁאֵר is "flesh", one's own flesh and blood (18:6, 12, 13). The verse begins with "you" (singular) and goes on with "he" and "they", as the Hebrew does.
- **"his uncle's wife" → "his aunt"** (v20) — Hebrew דֹּדָתוֹ, the feminine of דּוֹד "uncle": here his uncle's wife (18:14).
- **"it is an unclean thing" → "it is impurity"** (v21) — Hebrew נִדָּה, the word used of the monthly flow (15:19; 18:19): what is to be kept away from.
- **"that the land … spue you not out" → "and the land … shall not vomit you out"** (v22) — Hebrew וְלֹא־תָקִיא, a plain "and … not". **"judgments" → "ordinances"** — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּטִים.
- **"the manners of the nation, which I cast out before you" → "the statutes of the nation that I am sending away from before you"** (v23) — Hebrew חֻקֹּת, the word of v8, 22; מְשַׁלֵּחַ "send away"; מִפְּנֵיכֶם is literally "from your face". **"therefore I abhorred them" → "and I loathed them"** — Hebrew וָאָקֻץ בָּם; "therefore" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Ye shall inherit their land" → "You, you shall possess their ground"** (v24) — Hebrew אַתֶּם תִּירְשׁוּ אֶת־אַדְמָתָם: the pronouns "you" and "I" are stressed; יָרַשׁ is "take possession of"; אֲדָמָה is "ground" (then אֶרֶץ "land"). **"other people" → "the peoples"** (v24, 26) — Hebrew הָעַמִּים.
- **"put difference between clean beasts and unclean" → "separate the clean beast from the unclean"** (v25) — Hebrew וְהִבְדַּלְתֶּם בֵּין … לְ, literally "you shall separate between the clean beast to the unclean": the verb of v24, 26 (and Gen 1:4). עוֹף "birds" is a collective.
- **"ye shall not make your souls abominable" → "you shall not make your souls detestable"** (v25) — Hebrew שִׁקֵּץ, the verb of שֶׁקֶץ "detestable thing" (11:43). **"any manner of living thing that creepeth on the ground" → "anything with which the ground creeps"** — Hebrew בְּכֹל אֲשֶׁר תִּרְמֹשׂ הָאֲדָמָה: the ground is the subject of the verb. **"which I have separated from you as unclean" → "which I have separated for you to pronounce unclean"** — Hebrew לְטַמֵּא, literally "to make unclean": to hold or declare unclean.
- **"that ye should be mine" → "to be mine"** (v26) — Hebrew לִהְיוֹת לִי.
- **"A man also or woman that hath a familiar spirit, or that is a wizard" → "a man or a woman, when there is in them a ghost or a knowing spirit"** (v27) — Hebrew כִּי־יִהְיֶה בָהֶם אוֹב אוֹ יִדְּעֹנִי, literally so: the אוֹב and the יִדְּעֹנִי are "in" them. On the two words see 19:31; their exact sense is not certain.

### Notes
- **Penalties chapter** — parallel to ch 18 but now with specified sanctions. Capital crimes: giving seed to Molech, cursing parents, adultery, certain incests, bestiality, male-male intercourse, mother-and-daughter combination, having a ghost or a knowing spirit.
- **"His bloodguilt is on him"** — idiom for self-incurred guilt. The condemned is the cause of his own death; executioners bear no bloodguilt.
- **Holiness linked to separation** (v24, 26) — Israel's separation is geographic (from the peoples), dietary (from unclean food), sexual (from forbidden unions), and cultic (from Molech / ghosts).
- **Childlessness** (v20, 21) — for some offenses, the penalty named is not execution but dying or being childless.
