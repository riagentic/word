# Leviticus 20

[1] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[2] "Again, say to the children of Israel: 'Any person of the children of Israel, or of the foreigners who sojourn in Israel, who gives any of his offspring to Molech shall surely be put to death; the people of the land shall stone him with stones.

[3] I myself will set my face against that man and will cut him off from among his people, because he has given some of his offspring to Molech, so as to defile my sanctuary and profane my holy name.

[4] If the people of the land ever shut their eyes to that man, when he gives of his offspring to Molech, and do not put him to death,

[5] then I myself will set my face against that man and his family; and I will cut him off from among his people — him and all who whore after him to whore after Molech.

[6] The person who turns to mediums or spiritists, to whore after them, I will set my face against that person and cut him off from among his people.

[7] Consecrate yourselves, therefore, and be holy; for I am Yahweh your *Elohim*.

[8] You shall keep my statutes and do them; I am Yahweh who consecrates you.

[9] Anyone who curses his father or his mother shall surely be put to death; he has cursed his father or his mother — his blood is on him.

[10] The man who commits adultery with another man's wife — the one who commits adultery with his neighbor's wife — the adulterer and the adulteress shall surely be put to death.

[11] The man who lies with his father's wife has uncovered his father's nakedness; both of them shall surely be put to death; their blood is on them.

[12] If a man lies with his daughter-in-law, both of them shall surely be put to death; they have committed perversion; their blood is on them.

[13] If a man lies with a male as with a woman, both of them have committed an abomination; they shall surely be put to death; their blood is on them.

[14] If a man takes a woman and her mother also, it is wickedness; they shall be burned with fire, both he and they, that there may be no wickedness among you.

[15] If a man lies with an animal, he shall surely be put to death; and you shall kill the animal.

[16] If a woman approaches any animal to lie with it, you shall kill the woman and the animal; they shall surely be put to death; their blood is on them.

[17] If a man takes his sister — his father's daughter or his mother's daughter — and sees her nakedness, and she sees his nakedness, it is a shameful thing. They shall be cut off in the sight of their people; he has uncovered his sister's nakedness; he shall bear his iniquity.

[18] If a man lies with a woman in her menstrual flow and uncovers her nakedness, he has laid bare her source and she has exposed the source of her blood; both of them shall be cut off from among their people.

[19] You shall not uncover the nakedness of your mother's sister or your father's sister; for that is to lay bare one's near kin; they shall bear their iniquity.

[20] If a man lies with his uncle's wife, he has uncovered his uncle's nakedness; they shall bear their sin; they shall die childless.

[21] If a man takes his brother's wife, it is impurity; he has uncovered his brother's nakedness; they shall be childless.

[22] You shall therefore keep all my statutes and all my ordinances, and do them, so that the land to which I am bringing you to dwell shall not vomit you out.

[23] You shall not walk in the customs of the nation that I am driving out before you; for they did all these things, and therefore I abhorred them.

[24] But I have said to you, "You shall inherit their land, and I will give it to you to possess it — a land flowing with milk and honey." I am Yahweh your *Elohim*, who has separated you from the peoples.

[25] You shall therefore distinguish between clean animal and unclean, and between unclean bird and clean; you shall not make yourselves detestable by beast or by bird or by anything that creeps on the ground, which I have separated for you as unclean.

[26] You shall be holy to me, for I Yahweh am holy, and have separated you from the peoples, that you should be mine.

[27] A man or a woman who is a medium or a spiritist shall surely be put to death; they shall be stoned with stones; their blood is on them.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"his seed" → "his offspring"** (v2, 3, 4) — Hebrew זַרְעוֹ *zar'o* = his seed / offspring. Referring to children given in Molech sacrifice.
- **"go a whoring / whoredom" → "whore after"** (v5, 6) — Hebrew זָנָה *zanah*. Religious infidelity as prostitution-metaphor.
- **"familiar spirits / wizards" → "mediums / spiritists"** (v6, 27) — as in ch 19.
- **"wrought confusion" → "committed perversion"** (v12) — Hebrew *tevel*.
- **"sickness" → "menstrual flow"** (v18) — Hebrew דָּוָה *davah* = menstruating / ailing.
- **"uncovered her fountain / fountain of her blood" → "laid bare her source / source of her blood"** (v18) — Hebrew מְקֹרָהּ *meqorah* — "source / spring." Euphemistic.
- **"unclean thing" → "impurity"** (v21) — Hebrew נִדָּה *niddah* — here applied metaphorically to incest.
- **"have severed you from other people" → "separated you from the peoples"** (v24, 26) — Hebrew הִבְדִּיל *hivdil* = separated. Same verb used for dividing light/dark, holy/common, clean/unclean.

### Notes
- **Penalties chapter** — parallel to ch 18 but now with specified sanctions. Capital crimes: Molech-worship, cursing parents, adultery, certain incests, bestiality, male-male intercourse, mother-and-daughter combination, mediumship.
- **"His blood is on him"** — idiom for self-incurred guilt. The condemned is the cause of his own death; executioners bear no bloodguilt.
- **Holiness linked to separation** (v24, 26) — "holy" (*qadosh*) in Hebrew = "set apart." Israel's set-apartness is geographic (from nations), dietary (from unclean food), sexual (from forbidden unions), and cultic (from Molech / mediums).
- **Childlessness as covenantal curse** (v20, 21) — for some offenses, the penalty is not execution but divinely-imposed sterility.
