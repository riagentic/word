# Leviticus 12

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying: 'A woman, when she yields seed and bears a male, shall be unclean seven days; as in the days of the impurity of her infirmity she shall be unclean.

[3] And on the eighth day the flesh of his foreskin shall be circumcised.

[4] And thirty-three days she shall remain in the blood of cleansing; no holy thing shall she touch, and to the sanctuary she shall not come, until the days of her cleansing are full.

[5] And if she bears a female, she shall be unclean two weeks, as in her impurity; and sixty-six days she shall remain upon the blood of cleansing.

[6] And when the days of her cleansing are full, for a son or for a daughter, she shall bring a lamb a year old for a burnt offering, and a son of a dove or a turtledove for a sin offering, to the entrance of the tent of meeting, to the priest.

[7] And he shall bring it near before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and make atonement for her, and she shall be clean from the fountain of her blood. This is ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ of her who bears, for the male or for the female.

[8] And if her hand does not find enough for a sheep, she shall take two turtledoves or two sons of a dove, one for a burnt offering and one for a sin offering; and the priest shall make atonement for her, and she shall be clean.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"If a woman have conceived seed" → "A woman, when she yields seed"** (v2) — Hebrew תַזְרִיעַ, the causative of זָרַע "sow": "bring forth seed", the verb used of plants in Gen 1:11, 12. It means that she conceives. **"a man child" → "a male"; "a maid child" → "a female"** (v2, 5, 7) — Hebrew זָכָר, נְקֵבָה; "child" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"according to the days of the separation for her infirmity" → "as in the days of the impurity of her infirmity"** (v2); **"as in her separation" → "as in her impurity"** (v5) — Hebrew נִדַּת דְּוֹתָהּ. נִדָּה is the monthly flow and the uncleanness it brings; its root sense ("separation" or "expulsion") is not certain. דְּוֹתָהּ is "her being unwell", the same monthly flow. It is another word than "unclean" (טָמֵא) in the same verse.
- **"the flesh of his foreskin shall be circumcised"** (v3) — Hebrew יִמּוֹל בְּשַׂר עָרְלָתוֹ, passive, as Gen 17:14.
- **"continue" → "remain"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew תֵּשֵׁב, the verb יָשַׁב "sit, dwell": she stays as she is. "Dwell" or "sit in the blood" would say something else in English. **"in the blood of her purifying" → "in the blood of cleansing"** — Hebrew בִּדְמֵי טָהֳרָה, literally "in bloods of cleansing"; "her" is not in the Hebrew here (it is in "the days of her cleansing"). טָהֳרָה is the noun of טָהוֹר "clean". In v5 the preposition is עַל "upon, over".
- **"three and thirty days" → "thirty-three days"; "threescore and six days" → "sixty-six days"** (v4, 5) — the Hebrew has "thirty days and three days", "sixty days and six days".
- **"she shall touch no hallowed thing" → "no holy thing shall she touch"** (v4) — Hebrew בְּכָל־קֹדֶשׁ, "anything of holiness". **"be fulfilled" → "are full"** (v4, 6) — Hebrew מְלֹאת, of מָלֵא "be full".
- **"a lamb of the first year" → "a lamb a year old"** (v6) — Hebrew כֶּבֶשׂ בֶּן־שְׁנָתוֹ, literally "a lamb, a son of its year". **"a young pigeon" → "a son of a dove"** (v6, 8) — Hebrew בֶּן־יוֹנָה, בְּנֵי יוֹנָה; יוֹנָה is the "dove" of Gen 8:8. Whether "son" says "young" or only "one of the kind" is not certain; the usual rendering is "young pigeons".
- **"door of the tabernacle of the congregation" → "entrance of the tent of meeting"** (v6) — Hebrew פֶּתַח אֹהֶל־מוֹעֵד.
- **"Who shall offer it" → "And he shall bring it near"** (v7) — Hebrew וְהִקְרִיבוֹ, hifil of קָרַב "come near". **"make an atonement"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew כִּפֶּר (literally "cover").
- **"she shall be cleansed from the issue of her blood" → "she shall be clean from the fountain of her blood"** (v7) — Hebrew וְטָהֲרָה מִמְּקֹר דָּמֶיהָ; מָקוֹר is a source of water, "fountain"; "blood" is plural in the Hebrew.
- **"the law for her that hath born" → "the torah of her who bears"** (v7) — Hebrew תּוֹרַת הַיֹּלֶדֶת; תּוֹרָה is the instruction for one matter.
- **"if she be not able to bring a lamb" → "if her hand does not find enough for a sheep"** (v8) — Hebrew וְאִם־לֹא תִמְצָא יָדָהּ דֵּי שֶׂה: she cannot afford it. שֶׂה is one animal of the flock, sheep or goat; "lamb" in v6 is כֶּבֶשׂ. **"two turtles" → "two turtledoves"** — Hebrew תֹרִים, the bird.

### Notes
- **Asymmetry male/female** (v4–5) — the time is double for a girl (14+66 days) vs. a boy (7+33 days). No textual explanation given; various theories (none certain).
- **Not a moral category** — the uncleanness after birth is ritual, not sinful. The "sin offering" (*chatta't*, the one word "sin") is here an offering that cleanses, not one for guilt.
- **Circumcision on eighth day** (v3) — consistent with Gen 17:12. Not delayed by the mother's ritual state.
- **Mary and Joseph** — Luke 2:22–24 records the offering brought after the birth of Jesus: the two birds of v8, the offering of one who cannot afford a sheep.
