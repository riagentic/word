# Leviticus 12

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying: 'If a woman conceives and bears a male child, she shall be unclean for seven days; as in the days of her menstrual separation she shall be unclean.

[3] On the eighth day the flesh of his foreskin shall be circumcised.

[4] She shall then continue in the blood of her purification for thirty-three days. She shall touch no holy thing, nor come into the sanctuary, until the days of her purification are completed.

[5] But if she bears a female child, she shall be unclean two weeks, as in her menstrual separation; and she shall continue in the blood of her purification for sixty-six days.

[6] When the days of her purification are completed — whether for a son or for a daughter — she shall bring to the priest at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting a year-old lamb as a burnt offering, and a young pigeon or a turtledove as a sin offering.

[7] He shall offer it before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and make atonement for her, and she shall be cleansed from the flow of her blood. This is the law for her who bears a child, male or female.

[8] And if she cannot afford a lamb, she shall take two turtledoves or two young pigeons — one for a burnt offering and the other for a sin offering; and the priest shall make atonement for her, and she shall be clean.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"conceived seed" → "conceives"** (v2) — Hebrew תַזְרִיעַ *tazria* = conceives / brings forth seed. Idiomatic; simplified.
- **"man child / maid child" → "male child / female child"** (v2, 5) — modernized.
- **"days of the separation for her infirmity" → "days of her menstrual separation"** (v2) — Hebrew נִדַּת דְּוֹתָהּ *niddat devotah*. Technical term: *niddah* = menstrual separation; not "infirmity" in medical/moral sense.
- **"threescore and six" → "sixty-six"** (v5) — modernized.
- **"issue of her blood" → "flow of her blood"** (v7) — Hebrew מְקוֹר דָּמֶיהָ *meqor dameha* = source / flow of her blood.
- **"turtles" → "turtledoves"** (v8) — archaic *turtle* = turtledove bird, not chelonian.

### Notes
- **Asymmetry male/female** (v4–5) — purification period is double for a girl (14+66 days) vs. boy (7+33 days). No textual explanation given; various theories (none certain): ritual parity with extended female uncleanness patterns, or simply a distinction with unstated rationale.
- **Not a moral category** — post-partum uncleanness is ritual, not sinful. The "sin offering" (*chatta't*) here is a purification offering, not for guilt; translation "sin offering" is traditional but misleading in this context.
- **Circumcision on eighth day** (v3) — consistent with Gen 17:12. Not delayed by the mother's ritual state.
- **Mary and Joseph** — Luke 2:22–24 records Mary's purification offering of two turtledoves — i.e., the poor person's offering of v8.
