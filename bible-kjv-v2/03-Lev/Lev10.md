# Leviticus 10

[1] And the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, ⟦nadab|Nadav|Nadab⟧ and ⟦abihu|Avihu|Abihu⟧, took each man his firepan, and they placed fire in them and put incense on it, and they brought near before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ strange fire, which he had not commanded them.

[2] And fire came out from before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and consumed them, and they died before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, "This is what ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke, saying:

'In those who are near to me I will be sanctified,
and before the face of all the people I will be glorified.'"

And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ was still.

[4] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ called to Mishael and to ⟦elizaphan|Eltzafan|Elzaphan⟧, the sons of Uzziel the uncle of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, and said to them, "Come near; carry your brothers from before the Holy to outside the camp."

[5] And they came near and carried them in their tunics to outside the camp, as ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ had spoken.

[6] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, and to ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ and to ⟦ithamar|Itamar|Ithamar⟧ his sons, "Do not let your heads go loose, and you shall not rip your garments, and you shall not die, nor shall he be wrathful against all the congregation; and your brothers, all the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, shall weep for the burning that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has burned.

[7] And from the entrance of the tent of meeting you shall not go out, lest you die; for the oil of the anointing of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is upon you." And they did according to the word of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[8] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, saying,

[9] "Wine and strong drink do not drink, you and your sons with you, when you go into the tent of meeting, and you shall not die: an everlasting statute for your generations;

[10] and to separate the holy from the common, and the unclean from the clean;

[11] and to teach the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ all the statutes that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken to them by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧."

[12] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ spoke to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, and to ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ and to ⟦ithamar|Itamar|Ithamar⟧, his sons who remained, "Take the grain offering that remains from the offerings by fire of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and eat it as unleavened bread beside the altar, for it is most holy.

[13] And you shall eat it in a holy place, for it is your fixed portion and the fixed portion of your sons from the offerings by fire of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; for so I have been commanded.

[14] And the breast of the wave offering and the leg of the contribution you shall eat in a clean place, you and your sons and your daughters with you; for as your fixed portion and the fixed portion of your sons they have been given, from the sacrifices of the peace offerings of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[15] The leg of the contribution and the breast of the wave offering they shall bring, upon the offerings by fire of the fat parts, to wave as a wave offering before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and it shall be for you and for your sons with you as an everlasting statute, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded."

[16] And the male goat of the sin offering ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ surely sought, and behold, it was burned; and he was wrathful against ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ and against ⟦ithamar|Itamar|Ithamar⟧, the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ who remained, saying,

[17] "Why did you not eat the sin offering in the holy place? For it is most holy, and it he has given to you to bear the iniquity of the congregation, to make atonement for them before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[18] Behold, its blood was not brought into the Holy, within. You should surely have eaten it in the holy place, as I commanded."

[19] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Behold, today they have brought near their sin offering and their burnt offering before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and such things as these have befallen me; and had I eaten a sin offering today, would it have been good in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?"

[20] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ heard, and it was good in his eyes.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"took either of them his censer, and put fire therein, and put incense thereon" → "took each man his firepan, and they placed fire in them and put incense on it"** (v1) — Hebrew אִישׁ מַחְתָּתוֹ; מַחְתָּה is the pan for coals (Exod 27:3). The two verbs are נָתַן "place" and שִׂים "put"; "in them" is plural and "on it" singular in the Hebrew.
- **"offered strange fire before the LORD, which he commanded them not" → "brought near before Yahweh strange fire, which he had not commanded them"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּקְרִבוּ … אֵשׁ זָרָה אֲשֶׁר לֹא צִוָּה אֹתָם. הִקְרִיב is "bring near"; זָרָה is the feminine of זָר "stranger": fire that does not belong. The verse says no more than this of what the fire was.
- **"there went out fire from the LORD, and devoured them" → "fire came out from before Yahweh and consumed them"** (v2) — Hebrew מִלִּפְנֵי יְהוָה, the same words as 9:24; וַתֹּאכַל, "ate".
- **"I will be sanctified in them that come nigh me" → "In those who are near to me I will be sanctified"** (v3) — Hebrew בִּקְרֹבַי אֶקָּדֵשׁ: קָרוֹב is "one who is near" (an adjective, not the verb "come near"); בְּ is "in", "among" or "through"; the verb may also be taken as "I will show myself holy". **"before all the people" → "before the face of all the people"** — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי. "I will be glorified" is the root of כָּבוֹד "glory".
- **"And Aaron held his peace" → "And Aaron was still"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיִּדֹּם, "be still, be silent" (דָּמַם, as Exod 15:16).
- **"called Mishael" → "called to Mishael"** (v4) — Hebrew קָרָא אֶל. **"from before the sanctuary out of the camp" → "from before the Holy to outside the camp"** — Hebrew מֵאֵת פְּנֵי־הַקֹּדֶשׁ, "from the face of the holiness": the holy place; "sanctuary" is another word, מִקְדָּשׁ.
- **"coats" → "tunics"** (v5) — Hebrew כֻּתֳּנֹת. **"as Moses had said" → "had spoken"** — Hebrew דִּבֶּר.
- **"Uncover not your heads" → "Do not let your heads go loose"** (v6) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁיכֶם אַל־תִּפְרָעוּ; פָּרַע is "let loose": the hair left unbound and untended, or the head bared, as a sign of mourning (13:45; 21:10). The verse does not decide. **"neither rend your clothes" → "you shall not rip your garments"** — Hebrew פָּרַם, another verb than קָרַע "tear".
- **"lest ye die, and lest wrath come upon all the people" → "and you shall not die, nor shall he be wrathful against all the congregation"** (v6) — Hebrew וְלֹא תָמֻתוּ וְעַל כָּל־הָעֵדָה יִקְצֹף, literally "and you shall not die, and against all the congregation he will be wrathful": the "not" of the first clause carries over. The subject of "be wrathful" is not named.
- **"bewail the burning which the LORD hath kindled" → "weep for the burning that Yahweh has burned"** (v6) — Hebrew יִבְכּוּ אֶת־הַשְּׂרֵפָה אֲשֶׁר שָׂרַף יְהוָה, noun and verb of one root, שָׂרַף "burn".
- **"the anointing oil of the LORD" → "the oil of the anointing of Yahweh"** (v7) — Hebrew שֶׁמֶן מִשְׁחַת יְהוָה.
- **"Do not drink wine nor strong drink, thou, nor thy sons"** (v9) — "do not drink" is singular in the Hebrew, "when you go" and "you shall not die" plural. שֵׁכָר is a fermented drink other than wine. **"lest ye die" → "and you shall not die"** — Hebrew וְלֹא תָמֻתוּ. **"a statute for ever" → "an everlasting statute"** (v9, 15) — Hebrew חֻקַּת עוֹלָם; לְחָק־עוֹלָם, literally "a statute of the age".
- **"that ye may put difference between holy and unholy" → "to separate the holy from the common"** (v10) — Hebrew לְהַבְדִּיל בֵּין הַקֹּדֶשׁ וּבֵין הַחֹל, literally "to separate between the holiness and between the common, and between the unclean and between the clean". חֹל is what is not holy, ordinary; it is not a word of blame.
- **"teach"** (v11) — Hebrew לְהוֹרֹת, the verb of תּוֹרָה. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v11, 14). **"by the hand of Moses"** — Hebrew בְּיַד־מֹשֶׁה.
- **"his sons that were left" → "his sons who remained"; "the meat offering that remaineth" → "the grain offering that remains"** (v12, 16) — Hebrew הַנּוֹתָרִים, הַנּוֹתֶרֶת, one verb. **"eat it without leaven" → "eat it as unleavened bread"** — Hebrew מַצּוֹת. **"offerings of the LORD made by fire" → "offerings by fire of Yahweh"** (v12, 13) — Hebrew אִשֵּׁי יְהוָה; אִשֶּׁה is commonly derived from אֵשׁ "fire", but the derivation is not certain. **"most holy"** (v12, 17) — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ קָדָשִׁים, literally "holiness of holinesses".
- **"thy due, and thy sons' due" → "your fixed portion and the fixed portion of your sons"** (v13, 14) — Hebrew חָקְךָ וְחָק־בָּנֶיךָ: חֹק is the word "statute", here what is laid down as a person's share (Gen 47:22). In v15 the same word is "statute".
- **"the wave breast and heave shoulder" → "the breast of the wave offering and the leg of the contribution"** (v14, 15) — Hebrew חֲזֵה הַתְּנוּפָה, שׁוֹק הַתְּרוּמָה: שׁוֹק is the upper hind leg; תְּרוּמָה is what is lifted off and set aside; תְּנוּפָה is from הֵנִיף "wave" (to move to and fro, or to lift).
- **"with the offerings made by fire of the fat" → "upon the offerings by fire of the fat parts"** (v15) — Hebrew עַל אִשֵּׁי הַחֲלָבִים.
- **"diligently sought" → "surely sought"** (v16) — Hebrew דָּרֹשׁ דָּרַשׁ, the infinitive absolute with its verb: literally "seeking he sought". So v18 "You should surely have eaten", אָכוֹל תֹּאכְלוּ, "eating you shall eat". **"the goat" → "the male goat"** — Hebrew שְׂעִיר. **"he was angry with" → "he was wrathful against"** — Hebrew וַיִּקְצֹף עַל, the verb of v6.
- **"God hath given it you" → "it he has given to you"** (v17) — the Hebrew names no subject; KJV supplies "God". **"to bear the iniquity of the congregation"** — Hebrew לָשֵׂאת אֶת־עֲוֺן: נָשָׂא is "bear" or "take away".
- **"in the holy place"** (v17, 18b); **"within the holy place" → "into the Holy, within"** (v18a) — Hebrew בִּמְקוֹם הַקֹּדֶשׁ (v17), literally "in the place of holiness"; אֶל־הַקֹּדֶשׁ פְּנִימָה and בַּקֹּדֶשׁ (v18). The one word הַקֹּדֶשׁ stands twice in v18: first of the inner room of the tent, into which the blood was not brought (6:30), then of the holy precinct where the flesh is eaten (6:26). The verse marks the difference; the Hebrew word is the same.
- **"Behold"** (v18, 19) — Hebrew הֵן.
- **"they have offered" → "they have brought near"** (v19) — Hebrew הִקְרִיבוּ. **"and if I had eaten the sin offering to day, should it have been accepted in the sight of the LORD?" → "and had I eaten a sin offering today, would it have been good in the eyes of Yahweh?"** — Hebrew וְאָכַלְתִּי חַטָּאת הַיּוֹם הַיִּיטַב בְּעֵינֵי יְהוָה; "sin offering" has no article.
- **"And when Moses heard that, he was content" → "And Moses heard, and it was good in his eyes"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיִּיטַב בְּעֵינָיו, the words of Aaron's question.

### Notes

- **Nadab and Abihu** (v1–2) — the text gives two things only: the fire was "strange", and Yahweh had not commanded it.
- **No mourning** (v6–7) — compare 21:10–12.
- **Yahweh speaks to Aaron** (v8) — to Aaron alone, without Moses.
- **Separating and teaching** (v10–11) — the priests are to keep the holy and the common, the unclean and the clean apart, and to teach the statutes.
- **The sin offering not eaten** (v16–20) — by 6:26, 30 the priests were to eat it, since its blood had not been brought inside. Aaron's answer points to what had befallen him that day.
