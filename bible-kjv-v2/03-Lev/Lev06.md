# Leviticus 6

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "When a soul sins and commits unfaithfulness against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and lies to his fellow about a deposit, or about a security, or about a robbery, or has extorted from his fellow,

[3] or has found a lost thing and lies about it, and swears upon a falsehood — concerning one of all the things that a man does, to sin in them —

[4] then it shall be, when he sins and is guilty, that he shall return the thing robbed that he robbed, or the thing extorted that he extorted, or the deposit that was deposited with him, or the lost thing that he found,

[5] or anything about which he swears to falsehood: he shall repay it in its principal, and its fifth he shall add to it; to him whose it is he shall give it, on the day of his guilt.

[6] And his guilt offering he shall bring to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: a ram without blemish from the flock, by your valuation, for a guilt offering, to the priest.

[7] And the priest shall make atonement for him before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and it shall be pardoned him, for any one of all the things that he does, to be guilty by it."

[8] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[9] "Command ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons, saying: 'This is ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ of the burnt offering: it is the burnt offering on the hearth on the altar all the night until the morning, and the fire of the altar shall be kept burning on it.

[10] And the priest shall put on his linen garment, and linen breeches he shall put on, on his flesh; and he shall lift up the fat ashes to which the fire consumes the burnt offering on the altar, and he shall put them beside the altar.

[11] And he shall strip off his garments and put on other garments, and he shall bring out the fat ashes to outside the camp, to a clean place.

[12] And the fire on the altar shall be kept burning on it; it shall not go out. And the priest shall kindle wood on it morning by morning, and he shall arrange the burnt offering on it, and he shall turn into smoke on it the fat parts of the peace offerings.

[13] Fire shall be kept burning on the altar continually; it shall not go out.

[14] And this is ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ of the grain offering: the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall bring it near before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to the front of the altar.

[15] And he shall lift up from it in his handful, of the fine flour of the grain offering and of its oil, and all the frankincense that is on the grain offering, and he shall turn it into smoke on the altar: a soothing smell, its memorial portion, to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[16] And what remains of it ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons shall eat; as unleavened bread it shall be eaten, in a holy place; in the court of the tent of meeting they shall eat it.

[17] It shall not be baked leavened. As their share I have given it from my offerings by fire; it is most holy, like the sin offering and like the guilt offering.

[18] Every male among the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall eat it: an everlasting statute for your generations, from the offerings by fire of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. All that touches them shall be holy.'"

[19] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[20] "This is the offering of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons, which they shall bring near to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ on the day when he is anointed: a tenth of the ephah of fine flour, a continual grain offering, half of it in the morning and half of it in the evening.

[21] On a griddle, with oil, it shall be made; stirred you shall bring it; the baked things of a grain offering of morsels you shall bring near, a soothing smell to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[22] And the priest who is anointed in his place from among his sons shall offer it: an everlasting statute; to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ it shall be turned into smoke whole.

[23] And every grain offering of a priest shall be a whole offering; it shall not be eaten."

[24] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[25] "Speak to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and to his sons, saying: 'This is ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ of the sin offering: in the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered, the sin offering shall be slaughtered before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; it is most holy.

[26] The priest who offers it as a sin offering shall eat it; in a holy place it shall be eaten, in the court of the tent of meeting.

[27] All that touches its flesh shall be holy; and when any of its blood spatters on a garment, that on which it spatters you shall wash in a holy place.

[28] And an earthen vessel in which it is boiled shall be broken; and if it was boiled in a bronze vessel, then it shall be scoured and rinsed with water.

[29] Every male among the priests shall eat it; it is most holy.

[30] And every sin offering of whose blood any is brought into the tent of meeting, to make atonement in the Holy, shall not be eaten; with fire it shall be burned.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"If a soul sin" → "When a soul sins"** (v2) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ כִּי תֶחֱטָא. נֶפֶשׁ "soul" is feminine and its verbs are feminine; from "his fellow" on the Hebrew goes over to the masculine, and the verse follows it.
- **"commit a trespass against the LORD" → "commits unfaithfulness against Yahweh"** (v2) — Hebrew וּמָעֲלָה מַעַל בַּיהוָה, verb and noun of one root: a breach of trust. The wrong done to the fellow man is called unfaithfulness against Yahweh.
- **"lie unto his neighbour in that which was delivered him to keep" → "lies to his fellow about a deposit"** (v2) — Hebrew וְכִחֵשׁ בַּעֲמִיתוֹ בְּפִקָּדוֹן: כִּחֵשׁ is "lie, deny"; עָמִית "fellow" is another word than רֵעַ "neighbor"; פִּקָּדוֹן is a thing entrusted for keeping.
- **"in fellowship" → "about a security"** (v2) — Hebrew תְּשׂוּמֶת יָד, literally "a placing of the hand". The phrase is found only here and its meaning is uncertain: something put into a man's hand as a pledge or a loan.
- **"in a thing taken away by violence, or hath deceived his neighbour" → "about a robbery, or has extorted from his fellow"** (v2) — Hebrew גָּזֵל "robbery" and עָשַׁק אֶת־עֲמִיתוֹ, with the fellow as direct object: to wring from someone what is his, by force or by withholding.
- **"sweareth falsely" → "swears upon a falsehood"; "swears to falsehood"** (v3, 5) — Hebrew וְנִשְׁבַּע עַל־שָׁקֶר (v3), יִשָּׁבַע עָלָיו לַשֶּׁקֶר (v5); שֶׁקֶר is the noun "falsehood".
- **"in any of all these that a man doeth, sinning therein" → "concerning one of all the things that a man does, to sin in them"** (v3) — Hebrew עַל־אַחַת מִכֹּל אֲשֶׁר־יַעֲשֶׂה הָאָדָם לַחֲטֹא בָהֵנָּה.
- **"because he hath sinned, and is guilty" → "when he sins and is guilty"** (v4) — Hebrew כִּי־יֶחֱטָא וְאָשֵׁם. אָשֵׁם is "be guilty"; the noun of the same root is the "guilt offering".
- **"restore that which he took violently away" → "return the thing robbed that he robbed"** (v4) — Hebrew וְהֵשִׁיב אֶת־הַגְּזֵלָה אֲשֶׁר גָּזָל; each of the four things is named with the noun and the verb of one root where the Hebrew has it so.
- **"restore it in the principal" → "repay it in its principal"** (v5) — Hebrew וְשִׁלַּם אֹתוֹ בְּרֹאשׁוֹ, literally "in its head": the full sum. שִׁלֵּם is "repay, make whole". **"the fifth part more" → "its fifth"** — Hebrew וַחֲמִשִׁתָיו, a plural form ("its fifths").
- **"in the day of his trespass offering" → "on the day of his guilt"** (v5) — Hebrew בְּיוֹם אַשְׁמָתוֹ. אַשְׁמָה is "guilt"; the word "offering" is not in the Hebrew. The day when his guilt is established, or when he makes it good.
- **"trespass offering" → "guilt offering"** (v6, 17) — Hebrew אָשָׁם, the one word "guilt". **"with thy estimation" → "by your valuation"** (v6) — Hebrew בְּעֶרְכְּךָ; עֵרֶךְ is elsewhere "arrangement", here the value set on the ram.
- **"it shall be forgiven him" → "it shall be pardoned him"** (v7) — Hebrew וְנִסְלַח לוֹ, an impersonal passive of סָלַח, a verb used only of God. **"for any thing of all that he hath done in trespassing therein" → "for any one of all the things that he does, to be guilty by it"** — Hebrew לְאַשְׁמָה בָהּ.
- **"the law" → "the torah"** (v9, 14, 25) — Hebrew תּוֹרָה, here the instruction for one kind of offering.
- **"because of the burning upon the altar" → "on the hearth on the altar"** (v9) — Hebrew עַל מוֹקְדָה, the place of burning; the form of the word is unusual (perhaps "on its hearth"). **"shall be burning in it" → "shall be kept burning on it"** (v9, 12, 13) — Hebrew תּוּקַד בּוֹ, a passive: "shall be made to burn on it".
- **"his linen garment"** (v10) — Hebrew מִדּוֹ בַד; מַד is another word than בֶּגֶד "garment" (v11). **"upon his flesh"** — Hebrew עַל־בְּשָׂרוֹ.
- **"take up the ashes which the fire hath consumed with the burnt offering" → "lift up the fat ashes to which the fire consumes the burnt offering"** (v10) — Hebrew וְהֵרִים אֶת־הַדֶּשֶׁן אֲשֶׁר תֹּאכַל הָאֵשׁ אֶת־הָעֹלָה, literally "the fat ashes which the fire eats the burnt offering". דֶּשֶׁן is the ash soaked with fat.
- **"put off" → "strip off"; "carry forth" → "bring out"** (v11) — Hebrew פָּשַׁט, הוֹצִיא.
- **"burn wood on it every morning" → "kindle wood on it morning by morning"** (v12) — Hebrew וּבִעֵר … בַּבֹּקֶר בַּבֹּקֶר. "On it" is feminine three times in the second half of the verse: on the fire.
- **"burn thereon the fat" → "turn into smoke on it the fat parts"** (v12, also 15, 22) — Hebrew הִקְטִיר, literally "make smoke"; "burn" is kept for שָׂרַף (v30). "Fat parts" is the plural חֲלָבִים.
- **"The fire shall ever be burning" → "Fire shall be kept burning … continually"** (v13) — Hebrew אֵשׁ תָּמִיד תּוּקַד.
- **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** (v14, 15, 20, 21, 23) — Hebrew מִנְחָה. **"shall offer it" → "shall bring it near"** (v14, 20, 21) — Hebrew הִקְרִיב, hifil of קָרַב "come near"; in v14 it is an infinitive, "to bring it near". **"before the altar" → "to the front of the altar"** (v14) — Hebrew אֶל־פְּנֵי, "to the face of".
- **"he shall take of it his handful" → "he shall lift up from it in his handful"** (v15) — Hebrew וְהֵרִים מִמֶּנּוּ בְּקֻמְצוֹ. "From it" is masculine although "grain offering" is feminine.
- **"a sweet savour, even the memorial of it" → "a soothing smell, its memorial portion"** (v15) — Hebrew רֵיחַ נִיחֹחַ אַזְכָּרָתָהּ; נִיחֹחַ is from נוּחַ "rest"; אַזְכָּרָה is from זָכַר "remember". The part that goes on the altar is so called.
- **"with unleavened bread shall it be eaten" → "as unleavened bread it shall be eaten"** (v16) — Hebrew מַצּוֹת תֵּאָכֵל. **"the tabernacle of the congregation" → "the tent of meeting"** (v16, 26, 30) — Hebrew אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד.
- **"baken with leaven" → "baked leavened"** (v17) — Hebrew חָמֵץ, "what is leavened". **"their portion" → "their share"** — Hebrew חֶלְקָם. **"offerings made by fire" → "offerings by fire"** (v17, 18) — Hebrew אִשֶּׁה, commonly derived from אֵשׁ "fire"; the derivation is not certain.
- **"most holy"** (v17, 25, 29) — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ קָדָשִׁים, literally "holiness of holinesses".
- **"All the males among the children of Aaron" → "Every male among the sons of Aaron"** (v18) — Hebrew כָּל־זָכָר בִּבְנֵי אַהֲרֹן. **"a statute for ever" → "an everlasting statute"** (v18, 22) — Hebrew חָק־עוֹלָם, literally "a statute of the age": the due laid down for the priests. **"every one that toucheth them shall be holy" → "All that touches them shall be holy"** (v18, 27) — Hebrew כֹּל אֲשֶׁר־יִגַּע … יִקְדָּשׁ: "everyone who" or "everything that"; the Hebrew does not decide.
- **"offering"** (v20) — Hebrew קָרְבָּן, "what is brought near", the noun of הִקְרִיב. **"in the day when he is anointed"** — Hebrew בְּיוֹם הִמָּשַׁח אֹתוֹ. **"a meat offering perpetual" → "a continual grain offering"** — Hebrew מִנְחָה תָּמִיד.
- **"In a pan it shall be made with oil; and when it is baken, thou shalt bring it in: and the baken pieces of the meat offering shalt thou offer" → "On a griddle, with oil, it shall be made; stirred you shall bring it; the baked things of a grain offering of morsels you shall bring near"** (v21) — Hebrew מֻרְבֶּכֶת (stirred, well mixed or soaked: uncertain) and תֻּפִינֵי, a word found only here whose meaning is not known; "baked things" follows the usual derivation from אָפָה "bake". פִּתִּים are broken bits.
- **"the priest of his sons that is anointed in his stead" → "the priest who is anointed in his place from among his sons"** (v22) — Hebrew הַכֹּהֵן הַמָּשִׁיחַ תַּחְתָּיו מִבָּנָיו. **"shall offer it"** — Hebrew יַעֲשֶׂה אֹתָהּ, "shall do / make it".
- **"it shall be wholly burnt" → "it shall be turned into smoke whole"; "shall be wholly burnt" → "shall be a whole offering"** (v22, 23) — Hebrew כָּלִיל תָּקְטָר; כָּלִיל תִּהְיֶה, "shall be whole". כָּלִיל is "whole, entire": none of it is eaten. "Offering" in v23 is supplied.
- **"where the burnt offering is killed" → "is slaughtered"** (v25) — Hebrew שָׁחַט.
- **"The priest that offereth it for sin" → "The priest who offers it as a sin offering"** (v26) — Hebrew הַמְחַטֵּא אֹתָהּ, the piel of חָטָא "sin", made from the name of the sin offering. Of the altar the same verb is "purge of sin" (8:15).
- **"when there is sprinkled of the blood thereof upon any garment" → "when any of its blood spatters on a garment"** (v27) — Hebrew יִזֶּה, the simple stem of the verb whose hifil is "sprinkle". "That on which it spatters" is feminine in the Hebrew although "garment" is masculine.
- **"the earthen vessel wherein it is sodden" → "an earthen vessel in which it is boiled"; "brasen pot" → "bronze vessel"** (v28) — Hebrew בִּשֵּׁל; כְּלִי נְחֹשֶׁת.
- **"to reconcile withal in the holy place" → "to make atonement in the Holy"** (v30) — Hebrew לְכַפֵּר בַּקֹּדֶשׁ: כִּפֶּר is "make atonement" (literally "cover"); הַקֹּדֶשׁ is the first room of the tent. **"burnt in the fire"** — Hebrew שָׂרַף, the plain word "burn".

### Notes

- **Verse numbers** — the Hebrew chapter 6 begins at our 6:8. Our 6:1–7 is Hebrew 5:20–26, and our 6:8–30 is Hebrew 6:1–23.
- **Restitution first** (v2–7) — the principal and a fifth are repaid to the owner, and then the guilt offering is brought.
- **The fire on the altar** (v9–13) — three times the text says that it shall not go out.
- **The priest's own grain offering** (v20–23) — it goes wholly to the altar; no one eats of it.
- **"Shall be holy"** (v18, 27) — what touches the most holy things itself comes under the rules of the holy.
- **Blood brought into the tent** (v30) — the sin offerings of chapter 4 whose blood is brought inside are not eaten but burned; see Heb 13:11.
