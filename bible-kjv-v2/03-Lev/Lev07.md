# Leviticus 7

[1] "And this is ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ of the guilt offering; it is most holy.

[2] In the place where they slaughter the burnt offering they shall slaughter the guilt offering, and its blood he shall dash against the altar around.

[3] And all its fat he shall bring near from it: the fat tail, and the fat that covers the entrails,

[4] and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is on the flanks, and the lobe on the liver; together with the kidneys he shall remove it.

[5] And the priest shall turn them into smoke on the altar, an offering by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; it is a guilt offering.

[6] Every male among the priests shall eat it; in a holy place it shall be eaten; it is most holy.

[7] As the sin offering, so the guilt offering: ⟦torah|one torah|one law⟧ is for them; the priest who makes atonement with it, his it shall be.

[8] And the priest who brings near a man's burnt offering: the skin of the burnt offering that he has brought near shall be for the priest; his it shall be.

[9] And every grain offering that is baked in the oven, and all that is made in the deep pan and on a griddle, shall be for the priest who brings it near; his it shall be.

[10] And every grain offering mixed with oil, and dry, shall be for all the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, each man as his brother.

[11] And this is ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ of the sacrifice of peace offerings that one brings near to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

[12] If for thanksgiving he brings it near, then he shall bring near, with the sacrifice of thanksgiving, unleavened cakes mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil, and fine flour stirred, cakes mixed with oil.

[13] With cakes of leavened bread he shall bring near his offering, with the sacrifice of the thanksgiving of his peace offerings.

[14] And he shall bring near from it one out of every offering, a contribution to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; it shall be for the priest who dashes the blood of the peace offerings; his it shall be.

[15] And the flesh of the sacrifice of the thanksgiving of his peace offerings shall be eaten on the day of his offering; he shall not leave any of it until morning.

[16] And if the sacrifice of his offering is a vow or a freewill offering, on the day when he brings near his sacrifice it shall be eaten; and on the morrow also what remains of it shall be eaten.

[17] And what remains of the flesh of the sacrifice, on the third day it shall be burned with fire.

[18] And if any of the flesh of the sacrifice of his peace offerings is surely eaten on the third day, it shall not be found pleasing; he who brings it near, it shall not be reckoned to him; it shall be a foul thing, and the soul that eats of it shall bear its iniquity.

[19] And the flesh that touches any unclean thing shall not be eaten; with fire it shall be burned. And the flesh: everyone who is clean shall eat flesh.

[20] And the soul that eats flesh from the sacrifice of the peace offerings that is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh's|the LORD's⟧, with his uncleanness upon him, that soul shall be cut off from its peoples.

[21] And when a soul touches any unclean thing, the uncleanness of man or an unclean beast or any unclean detestable thing, and eats of the flesh of the sacrifice of the peace offerings that is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh's|the LORD's⟧, that soul shall be cut off from its peoples."

[22] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[23] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying: 'Any fat of ox and lamb and goat you shall not eat.

[24] And the fat of a carcass and the fat of a torn animal may be used for any work, but you shall surely not eat it.

[25] For everyone who eats fat from the beast from which one brings near an offering by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the soul that eats shall be cut off from its peoples.

[26] And any blood you shall not eat in all your dwelling places, of the birds or of the beasts.

[27] Every soul that eats any blood, that soul shall be cut off from its peoples.'"

[28] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[29] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying: 'He who brings near the sacrifice of his peace offerings to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall bring his offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from the sacrifice of his peace offerings.

[30] His hands shall bring the offerings by fire of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; the fat upon the breast he shall bring, the breast to wave it as a wave offering before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[31] And the priest shall turn the fat into smoke on the altar, and the breast shall be for ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and for his sons.

[32] And the right leg you shall give as a contribution to the priest, from the sacrifices of your peace offerings.

[33] He who brings near the blood of the peace offerings and the fat, from among the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, his shall be the right leg for a portion.

[34] For the breast of the wave offering and the leg of the contribution I have taken from the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, from the sacrifices of their peace offerings, and I have given them to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ the priest and to his sons as an everlasting statute from the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.'"

[35] This is the anointing of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and the anointing of his sons from the offerings by fire of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, on the day he brought them near to be priests to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[36] which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded to give them, on the day he anointed them, from the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: an everlasting statute for their generations.

[37] This is ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ for the burnt offering, for the grain offering, and for the sin offering, and for the guilt offering, and for the filling, and for the sacrifice of peace offerings,

[38] which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ on Mount Sinai, on the day he commanded the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to bring near their offerings to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, in the wilderness of Sinai.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"the law" → "the torah"** (v1, 7, 11, 37) — Hebrew תּוֹרָה, here the instruction for one kind of offering.
- **"trespass offering" → "guilt offering"** (v1, 2, 5, 7, 37) — Hebrew אָשָׁם, the one word "guilt". **"most holy"** (v1, 6) — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ קָדָשִׁים, literally "holiness of holinesses".
- **"kill" → "slaughter"** (v2) — Hebrew שָׁחַט. **"shall he sprinkle round about upon the altar" → "he shall dash against the altar around"** (v2, 14) — Hebrew זָרַק, to throw from a bowl. The Hebrew passes from "they shall slaughter" to "he shall dash".
- **"he shall offer of it all the fat thereof" → "all its fat he shall bring near from it"** (v3) — Hebrew יַקְרִיב, hifil of קָרַב "come near"; so in the whole chapter (v8, 9, 11–14, 16, 18, 25, 29, 33, 35, 38), also where KJV has "offer" or "present".
- **"the rump" → "the fat tail"; "the inwards" → "the entrails"** (v3) — Hebrew הָאַלְיָה, the heavy tail of the fat-tailed sheep; קֶרֶב.
- **"which is by the flanks, and the caul that is above the liver, with the kidneys, it shall he take away" → "which is on the flanks, and the lobe on the liver; together with the kidneys he shall remove it"** (v4) — Hebrew הַכְּסָלִים; עַל־הַכְּלָיֹת, literally "upon the kidneys"; הַיֹּתֶרֶת עַל־הַכָּבֵד, literally "what is over on the liver": an appendage of the liver, the exact part is uncertain.
- **"burn" → "turn into smoke"** (v5, 31); **"burnt with fire" kept** (v17, 19) — two Hebrew verbs: on the altar הִקְטִיר, literally "make smoke"; of what is destroyed שָׂרַף "burn". **"an offering made by fire" → "an offering by fire"** (v5, 25, 30, 35) — Hebrew אִשֶּׁה, commonly derived from אֵשׁ "fire"; the derivation is not certain.
- **"As the sin offering is, so is the trespass offering: there is one law for them" → "As the sin offering, so the guilt offering: one torah is for them"** (v7) — Hebrew כַּחַטָּאת כָּאָשָׁם תּוֹרָה אַחַת לָהֶם. **"the priest that maketh atonement therewith shall have it" → "his it shall be"** — Hebrew לוֹ יִהְיֶה; so in v8, 9, 14.
- **"any man's burnt offering"; "the skin"** (v8) — Hebrew עֹלַת אִישׁ; עוֹר.
- **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** (v9, 10, 37) — Hebrew מִנְחָה. **"in the fryingpan, and in the pan" → "in the deep pan and on a griddle"** (v9) — Hebrew מַרְחֶשֶׁת, a deep vessel for cooking in oil, and מַחֲבַת, a flat plate.
- **"mingled with oil, and dry" → "mixed with oil, and dry"** (v10) — Hebrew בְלוּלָה־בַשֶּׁמֶן וַחֲרֵבָה. **"one as much as another" → "each man as his brother"** — Hebrew אִישׁ כְּאָחִיו.
- **"which he shall offer" → "that one brings near"** (v11) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר יַקְרִיב, "which he brings near", with no subject named.
- **"If he offer it for a thanksgiving" → "If for thanksgiving he brings it near"** (v12) — Hebrew עַל־תּוֹדָה; "with" in v12 and 13 is the same preposition עַל, "upon". **"cakes mingled with oil, of fine flour, fried" → "fine flour stirred, cakes mixed with oil"** — Hebrew וְסֹלֶת מֻרְבֶּכֶת חַלֹּת בְּלוּלֹת בַּשָּׁמֶן; מֻרְבֶּכֶת (stirred, well mixed or soaked) is uncertain; it is another word than בְּלוּלָה "mixed".
- **"Besides the cakes, he shall offer for his offering leavened bread" → "With cakes of leavened bread he shall bring near his offering"** (v13) — Hebrew עַל־חַלֹּת לֶחֶם חָמֵץ יַקְרִיב קָרְבָּנוֹ. קָרְבָּן "offering" is "what is brought near", the noun of the verb.
- **"an heave offering" → "a contribution"** (v14, 32, 34) — Hebrew תְּרוּמָה, from הֵרִים "lift up": what is lifted off and set aside.
- **"he shall not leave any of it until the morning"** (v15) — Hebrew לֹא־יַנִּיחַ, "he shall not lay up".
- **"a voluntary offering" → "a freewill offering"** (v16) — Hebrew נְדָבָה. **"and on the morrow also the remainder of it shall be eaten" → "and on the morrow also what remains of it shall be eaten"** — Hebrew וּמִמָּחֳרָת וְהַנּוֹתָר מִמֶּנּוּ יֵאָכֵל, literally "and on the morrow, and what remains of it shall be eaten".
- **"if any … be eaten at all" → "is surely eaten"** (v18) — Hebrew הֵאָכֹל יֵאָכֵל, the infinitive absolute with its verb: literally "being eaten is eaten". So v24 "you shall surely not eat it", וְאָכֹל לֹא תֹאכְלֻהוּ.
- **"it shall not be accepted, neither shall it be imputed unto him that offereth it" → "it shall not be found pleasing; he who brings it near, it shall not be reckoned to him"** (v18) — Hebrew לֹא יֵרָצֶה, the passive of רָצָה "be pleased with"; the accents end the clause there, so that "he who brings it near" begins the next: לֹא יֵחָשֵׁב לוֹ, "it shall not be reckoned to him" (חָשַׁב as Gen 15:6). Without the accents the words could also be read "he who brings it near shall not be found pleasing".
- **"it shall be an abomination" → "it shall be a foul thing"** (v18) — Hebrew פִּגּוּל, used only of sacrificial flesh kept beyond its time (19:7; Isa 65:4; Ezek 4:14); the exact sense is uncertain. It is not the word "abomination" (תּוֹעֵבָה).
- **"the soul that eateth of it shall bear his iniquity" → "shall bear its iniquity"** (v18) — Hebrew עֲוֺנָהּ תִּשָּׂא, feminine, of the soul. נָשָׂא עָוֹן is to carry the guilt and what it brings.
- **"and as for the flesh, all that be clean shall eat thereof" → "And the flesh: everyone who is clean shall eat flesh"** (v19) — Hebrew וְהַבָּשָׂר כָּל־טָהוֹר יֹאכַל בָּשָׂר.
- **"that pertain unto the LORD" → "that is Yahweh's"** (v20, 21) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר לַיהוָה. **"having his uncleanness upon him" → "with his uncleanness upon him"** (v20) — the Hebrew is masculine here, though "soul" is feminine.
- **"shall be cut off from his people" → "shall be cut off from its peoples"** (v20, 21, 25, 27) — Hebrew וְנִכְרְתָה … מֵעַמֶּיהָ: "peoples" is plural (his kinsfolk), and the suffix is feminine, of the soul. The verb is the root of כָּרַת "cut" a covenant.
- **"the uncleanness of man, or any unclean beast, or any abominable unclean thing" → "the uncleanness of man or an unclean beast or any unclean detestable thing"** (v21) — Hebrew שֶׁקֶץ, another word than תּוֹעֵבָה "abomination". (Some Hebrew manuscripts and versions are said to read שֶׁרֶץ "swarming thing" here: from memory, not checked.)
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v23, 29, 34, 36, 38) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל. **"of ox, or of sheep, or of goat" → "of ox and lamb and goat"** (v23) — Hebrew שׁוֹר וְכֶשֶׂב וָעֵז; כֶּשֶׂב is the sheep of the flock beside the goat.
- **"the beast that dieth of itself, and the fat of that which is torn with beasts, may be used in any other use" → "a carcass … a torn animal may be used for any work"** (v24) — Hebrew נְבֵלָה, the body of an animal that died of itself; טְרֵפָה, "a thing torn" by a beast of prey; יֵעָשֶׂה לְכָל־מְלָאכָה, literally "shall be made for any work".
- **"that soul … shall be cut off" → "the soul that eats shall be cut off"** (v25) — Hebrew הַנֶּפֶשׁ הָאֹכֶלֶת. **"of which men offer" → "from which one brings near"** — no subject is named.
- **"whether it be of fowl or of beast, in any of your dwellings" → "in all your dwelling places, of the birds or of the beasts"** (v26) — Hebrew בְּכֹל מוֹשְׁבֹתֵיכֶם לָעוֹף וְלַבְּהֵמָה; the Hebrew has "and" and collective singulars.
- **"His own hands shall bring" → "His hands shall bring"** (v30) — Hebrew יָדָיו תְּבִיאֶינָה. **"the fat with the breast, it shall he bring, that the breast may be waved for a wave offering" → "the fat upon the breast he shall bring, the breast to wave it as a wave offering"** — Hebrew הֵנִיף … תְּנוּפָה, verb and noun of one root: to move to and fro (or to lift) before Yahweh.
- **"the right shoulder" → "the right leg"** (v32, 33, 34) — Hebrew שׁוֹק, the upper hind leg. **"for his part" → "for a portion"** (v33) — Hebrew לְמָנָה.
- **"by a statute for ever" → "as an everlasting statute"** (v34, 36) — Hebrew לְחָק־עוֹלָם (v34), חֻקַּת עוֹלָם (v36), literally "a statute of the age": in v34 the due laid down for the priests.
- **"This is the portion of the anointing of Aaron" → "This is the anointing of Aaron"** (v35) — Hebrew זֹאת מִשְׁחַת אַהֲרֹן. KJV supplies "the portion of". The word has the form of מִשְׁחָה "anointing" (v36 has the verb "he anointed them"); many take it here as another word of the same form, "measured share, portion". The verse keeps the word as it stands.
- **"in the day when he presented them to minister unto the LORD in the priest's office" → "on the day he brought them near to be priests to Yahweh"** (v35) — Hebrew בְּיוֹם הִקְרִיב אֹתָם לְכַהֵן לַיהוָה; the subject is not named.
- **"of the consecrations" → "for the filling"** (v37) — Hebrew לַמִּלּוּאִים, from מִלֵּא יָד "fill the hand", the set expression for installing a priest (8:22–33; Exod 29).
- **"to offer their oblations" → "to bring near their offerings"** (v38) — Hebrew לְהַקְרִיב אֶת־קָרְבְּנֵיהֶם.

### Notes

- **One speech** — v1–21 continue the words begun at 6:25.
- **Six kinds named** (v37) — burnt offering, grain offering, sin offering, guilt offering, filling, sacrifice of peace offerings.
- **Fat and blood** (v22–27) — as 3:17, now with the cutting off named. For the blood see 17:11.
- **Thanksgiving, vow, freewill offering** (v12–16) — the thanksgiving is eaten on its own day, the other two also on the morrow.
- **What the priests receive** — the breast and the right leg (v31–34), the skin of the burnt offering (v8), and the grain offerings (v9–10).
- **"On Mount Sinai … in the wilderness of Sinai"** (v38) — the close of the instruction on the offerings, chapters 1–7.
