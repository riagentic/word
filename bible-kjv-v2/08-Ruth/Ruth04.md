# Ruth 4

[1] And Boaz had gone up to the gate and sat there; and behold, the redeemer of whom Boaz had spoken was passing by; and he said, "Turn aside, sit here, So-and-so." And he turned aside and sat.

[2] And he took ten men of the elders of the city and said, "Sit here." And they sat.

[3] And he said to the redeemer, "The portion of the field that belonged to our brother, to Elimelech, ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ has sold, she who returned from the field of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧.

[4] And I, I said, I will uncover your ear, saying, 'Buy it before those who sit here and before the elders of my people.' If you will redeem, redeem; and if he will not redeem, tell me, that I may know, for there is none besides you to redeem, and I am after you." And he said, "I will redeem."

[5] And Boaz said, "On the day you buy the field from the hand of ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ and from ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧ the ⟦moabites|Moaviyah|Moabitess⟧, the wife of the dead, you have bought it to raise up the name of the dead on his inheritance."

[6] And the redeemer said, "I cannot redeem for myself, lest I ruin my inheritance. Redeem for yourself, you, my redemption, for I cannot redeem."

[7] And this was formerly in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ concerning the redemption and concerning the exchange, to make any matter stand: a man drew off his sandal and gave it to his neighbor; and this was the attestation in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[8] And the redeemer said to Boaz, "Buy for yourself." And he drew off his sandal.

[9] And Boaz said to the elders and all the people, "You are witnesses today that I have bought all that was Elimelech's and all that was ⟦chilion|Kilyon|Chilion⟧'s and ⟦mahlon|Machlon|Mahlon⟧'s from the hand of ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧.

[10] And also ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧ the ⟦moabites|Moaviyah|Moabitess⟧, the wife of ⟦mahlon|Machlon|Mahlon⟧, I have gotten for myself as a wife, to raise up the name of the dead on his inheritance, and the name of the dead shall not be cut off from among his brothers and from the gate of his place. You are witnesses today."

[11] And all the people who were in the gate, and the elders, said, "Witnesses. May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ make the woman who is coming into your house like Rachel and like Leah, who built, the two of them, the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and act with ability in ⟦ephrathah|Efratah|Ephrathah⟧, and call a name in ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧.

[12] And may your house be like the house of ⟦perez|Peretz|Perez⟧, whom Tamar bore to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, from the seed that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will give you from this girl."

[13] And Boaz took ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧, and she became his wife, and he went in to her; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave her conception, and she bore a son.

[14] And the women said to ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧, "Blessed is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who has not let a redeemer cease for you today; and may his name be called in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[15] And he shall be to you a restorer of the soul, and one to sustain your old age; for your daughter-in-law who loves you has borne him, she who is better to you than seven sons."

[16] And ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ took the child and set him in her bosom, and she became a foster mother to him.

[17] And the neighbor women called him a name, saying, "A son has been born to ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧." And they called his name ⟦obed|Oved|Obed⟧. He is the father of ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧, the father of David.

[18] And these are the generations of ⟦perez|Peretz|Perez⟧: ⟦perez|Peretz|Perez⟧ begot ⟦hezron|Chetzron|Hezron⟧,

[19] and ⟦hezron|Chetzron|Hezron⟧ begot Ram, and Ram begot ⟦amminadab|Amminadav|Amminadab⟧,

[20] and ⟦amminadab|Amminadav|Amminadab⟧ begot ⟦nahshon|Nachshon|Nahshon⟧, and ⟦nahshon|Nachshon|Nahshon⟧ begot ⟦salmah|Salmah|Salmon⟧,

[21] and Salmon begot Boaz, and Boaz begot ⟦obed|Oved|Obed⟧,

[22] and ⟦obed|Oved|Obed⟧ begot ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧, and ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧ begot David.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton kept.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Then went Boaz up" → "And Boaz had gone up"** (v1) — Hebrew וּבֹעַז עָלָה, the subject before a perfect: a new scene beside the last. **"the kinsman" → "the redeemer"** (v1, 3, 6, 8, 14) — Hebrew הַגֹּאֵל; the verb גָּאַל "redeem" and the noun גְּאֻלָּה "redemption" (v6, 7) are one root, kept as one. "Kinsman" is not in the word.
- **"Ho, such a one!" → "So-and-so"** (v1) — Hebrew פְּלֹנִי אַלְמֹנִי, a pair of rhyming words used where a name is not given (of a place: 1 Sam 21:2; 2 Kgs 6:8). Whether Boaz spoke so or the narrator has put the words in place of the man's name is not said; the earlier "friend" was not in the Hebrew. Of a place the same words are "such and such" (1 Sam 21:2); of a man English says "So-and-so".
- **"sat down" → "sat"** (v1, 2) — Hebrew יָשַׁב, four times in two verses.
- **"Naomi … selleth a parcel of land" → "The portion of the field … Naomi has sold"** (v3) — Hebrew מָכְרָה, a perfect: "has sold", or of a thing now resolved and being done, "is selling" (v5 and v9 speak of buying it from her hand). חֶלְקַת הַשָּׂדֶה is the phrase of 2:3. **"which was our brother Elimelech's"** — "brother" (אָח) of a man of the same clan. **"the country of Moab" → "the field of Moab"** — as 1:6.
- **"I thought to advertise thee" → "I said, I will uncover your ear"** (v4) — Hebrew אֶגְלֶה אָזְנְךָ, word for word: to tell a thing to someone. **"before the inhabitants" → "before those who sit here"** — Hebrew הַיֹּשְׁבִים, "the ones sitting" or "the inhabitants"; "here" is supplied, and so is "it" after "Buy" (v4) and after "gave" (v7). **"but if thou wilt not redeem it" → "and if he will not redeem"** — the Masoretic Text has יִגְאַל, third person ("if he will not redeem", as if turning to the elders); "if you will not redeem" is the reading of many manuscripts and versions (cited from memory). The printed text is translated. "That I may know" follows the qere וְאֵדְעָה.
- **"What day thou buyest the field of the hand of Naomi, thou must buy it also of Ruth the Moabitess, the wife of the dead" → "On the day you buy the field from the hand of Naomi and from Ruth the Moabitess, the wife of the dead, you have bought it to raise up the name of the dead on his inheritance"** (v5) — Hebrew בְּיוֹם־קְנוֹתְךָ הַשָּׂדֶה מִיַּד נָעֳמִי וּמֵאֵת רוּת … קָנִיתָה לְהָקִים. The Masoretic Text has וּמֵאֵת "and from" Ruth, and the reading tradition (qere) קָנִיתָה "you have bought" (no object is written; "it" is supplied). The written form (ketiv) קניתי is "I have bought". The verse is commonly emended to "you also acquire Ruth" (גַּם אֶת for וּמֵאֵת, after v10; so the Vulgate is cited, from memory); that is not in the Hebrew and was the earlier wording here. The printed text with its qere is translated.
- **"to raise up the name of the dead upon his inheritance" kept** (v5, 10) — Hebrew לְהָקִים שֵׁם־הַמֵּת, the words of Deut 25:7 ("to raise up for his brother a name").
- **"lest I mar mine own inheritance" → "lest I ruin my inheritance"** (v6) — Hebrew אַשְׁחִית. **"redeem thou my right to thyself" → "Redeem for yourself, you, my redemption"** — Hebrew גְּאַל־לְךָ אַתָּה אֶת־גְּאֻלָּתִי: גְּאֻלָּה is the right and the act of redeeming (Lev 25:24). "To redeem" (first) follows the qere לִגְאָל.
- **"Now this was the manner in former time in Israel concerning redeeming and concerning changing, for to confirm all things; a man plucked off his shoe, and gave it to his neighbour: and this was a testimony in Israel" → "And this was formerly in Israel concerning the redemption and concerning the exchange, to make any matter stand: a man drew off his sandal and gave it to his neighbor; and this was the attestation in Israel"** (v7) — word for word. "The manner", "the custom" is not written: the Hebrew has only וְזֹאת "and this". לְקַיֵּם is the piel of קוּם "stand": to make valid. שָׁלַף is "draw off" (of a sword, "draw"); Deut 25:9 has another verb (חָלַץ) and another act. Who drew off whose sandal ("a man … his neighbor") the sentence does not say more exactly. תְּעוּדָה is "attestation" (Isa 8:16, 20), from the root of עֵד "witness".
- **"Buy it for thee. So he drew off his shoe" → "Buy for yourself. And he drew off his sandal"** (v8) — "it" is not written. "He" is, by the order of the words, the redeemer.
- **"I have bought" kept (v9); "have I purchased to be my wife" → "I have gotten for myself as a wife"** (v10) — one Hebrew verb, קָנָה "get, buy" (v4, 5, 8, 9, 10). Of the field "buy"; of Ruth "get", because no price is paid for her (so Gen 4:1). **"that the name of the dead be not cut off" → "and the name of the dead shall not be cut off"** — Hebrew וְלֹא־יִכָּרֵת.
- **"We are witnesses" → "Witnesses"** (v11) — Hebrew עֵדִים, one word. **"The LORD make the woman … like Rachel and like Leah, which two did build" → "May Yahweh make … like Rachel and like Leah, who built, the two of them"** — Hebrew יִתֵּן "give, make"; "the two of them" is masculine in form (see 1:8).
- **"do thou worthily in Ephratah, and be famous in Bethlehem" → "act with ability in Ephrathah, and call a name in Bethlehem"** (v11) — Hebrew וַעֲשֵׂה־חַיִל, literally "and do / make חַיִל", the word of 2:1 and 3:11; the phrase is "make wealth" in Deut 8:17, 18 and "do mightily" elsewhere, and which is meant here is not said; the one word is kept. וּקְרָא־שֵׁם is literally "and call a name" (be named, have a name). Both are commands in form.
- **"of the seed which the LORD shall give thee of this young woman" → "from the seed that Yahweh will give you from this girl"** (v12) — Hebrew זֶרַע and הַנַּעֲרָה, the word of 2:5, 6.
- **"she was his wife" → "she became his wife"** (v13) — Hebrew וַתְּהִי־לוֹ לְאִשָּׁה. **"gave her conception"** — Hebrew הֵרָיוֹן.
- **"Blessed be the LORD, which hath not left thee this day without a kinsman" → "Blessed is Yahweh, who has not let a redeemer cease for you today"** (v14) — Hebrew לֹא הִשְׁבִּית לָךְ גֹּאֵל, the hifil of שָׁבַת "cease": has not let you be without one. The redeemer of this verse is the child (v15). **"that his name may be famous" → "and may his name be called"** — Hebrew וְיִקָּרֵא שְׁמוֹ.
- **"a restorer of thy life, and a nourisher of thine old age" → "a restorer of the soul, and one to sustain your old age"** (v15) — Hebrew לְמֵשִׁיב נֶפֶשׁ (the verb שׁוּב of chapter 1: "one who brings back the soul"; no "your") and וּלְכַלְכֵּל אֶת־שֵׂיבָתֵךְ (שֵׂיבָה "gray hair"). **"which loveth thee"** — Hebrew אֲהֵבַתֶךְ, a perfect of a verb of feeling.
- **"laid it in her bosom, and became nurse unto it" → "set him in her bosom, and she became a foster mother to him"** (v16) — Hebrew אֹמֶנֶת, one who brings up a child (Num 11:12; 2 Sam 4:4); "nurse" (מֵינֶקֶת, one who suckles) is another word.
- **"the women her neighbours gave it a name" → "the neighbor women called him a name"** (v17) — Hebrew וַתִּקְרֶאנָה לוֹ הַשְּׁכֵנוֹת שֵׁם. **"There is a son born to Naomi"** — Hebrew יֻלַּד־בֵּן לְנָעֳמִי. **"Obed"** — Hebrew עוֹבֵד "one who serves".
- **"begat" → "begot"** (v18–22) — Hebrew הוֹלִיד. **"Pharez" → "Perez"; "Salmon" → "Salmah" / "Salmon"** (v20, 21) — the Hebrew has שַׂלְמָה *Salmah* in v20 and שַׂלְמוֹן *Salmon* in v21, two spellings side by side; both are kept. The line of ten names is taken up in Matt 1:3–6.

### Notes
- **The gate** — the place of the town's court; ten elders sit as witnesses.
- **The field and the widow** (v3–6) — the redeemer is willing to redeem the field (v4) and unwilling when the name of the dead is to be raised up on it (v5–6): the son of Ruth would hold the inheritance as Mahlon's. How the Hebrew of v5 ties the field to Ruth is disputed (see Changes); v10 says plainly that Boaz takes her as wife for this purpose. The duty named is close to that of Deut 25:5–10, though Boaz is not a husband's brother.
- **The sandal** (v7–8) — the narrator explains a usage that was no longer known to his readers. Deut 25:9 has a different act with a sandal.
- **The blessing** (v11–12) — Rachel and Leah, the mothers of the house of Israel; Tamar, who bore Perez to Judah (Gen 38).
- **Obed, Jesse, David** (v17, 22) — the book ends with the name of David. Ruth the Moabitess is the mother of his grandfather.
- **Matt 1:5** — the genealogy there names Ruth, with Tamar, Rahab and the wife of Uriah.
