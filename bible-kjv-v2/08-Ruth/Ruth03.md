# Ruth 3

[1] And ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ her mother-in-law said to her, "My daughter, shall I not seek for you a resting place, that it may go well with you?

[2] And now, is not Boaz our relative, with whose girls you were? Behold, he is winnowing the threshing floor of barley tonight.

[3] And you shall wash and anoint yourself and put your cloaks on you and go down to the threshing floor; do not make yourself known to the man until he has finished eating and drinking.

[4] And let it be, when he lies down, that you shall know the place where he lies, and you shall go and uncover the place of his feet and lie down; and he will tell you what you shall do."

[5] And she said to her, "All that you say to me I will do."

[6] And she went down to the threshing floor and did according to all that her mother-in-law had commanded her.

[7] And Boaz ate and drank, and his heart was good, and he went to lie down at the end of the heap; and she came quietly and uncovered the place of his feet and lay down.

[8] And it came to pass in the middle of the night that the man trembled and turned himself, and behold, a woman lying at the place of his feet.

[9] And he said, "Who are you?" And she said, "I am ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧, your slave woman; and you shall spread your wing over your slave woman, for you are a redeemer."

[10] And he said, "Blessed are you to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, my daughter. You have made your latter ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ better than the first, in not going after the young men, whether poor or rich.

[11] And now, my daughter, do not fear; all that you say I will do for you, for all the gate of my people knows that you are a woman of ability.

[12] And now, it is indeed true that I am a redeemer; and also there is a redeemer nearer than I.

[13] Lodge tonight; and it shall be in the morning, if he will redeem you, good, let him redeem; and if he has no delight to redeem you, then I will redeem you, I myself, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives. Lie down until the morning."

[14] And she lay at the place of his feet until the morning, and she arose before a man could recognize his neighbor; and he said, "Let it not be known that the woman came to the threshing floor."

[15] And he said, "Give the shawl that is on you, and take hold of it." And she took hold of it, and he measured six of barley and put it on her; and he went into the city.

[16] And she came to her mother-in-law, and she said, "Who are you, my daughter?" And she told her all that the man had done for her.

[17] And she said, "These six of barley he gave me, for he said to me, 'Do not come empty to your mother-in-law.'"

[18] And she said, "Sit, my daughter, until you know how the matter falls; for the man will not be quiet unless he has finished the matter today."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v10, 13) — the Tetragrammaton kept.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"shall I not seek rest for thee, that it may be well with thee?" → "shall I not seek for you a resting place, that it may go well with you?"** (v1) — Hebrew מָנוֹחַ "resting place" (Gen 8:9), as מְנוּחָה in 1:9; אֲשֶׁר יִיטַב־לָךְ as Deut 4:40; 6:3.
- **"of our kindred" → "our relative"** (v2) — Hebrew מֹדַעְתָּנוּ, the feminine form of the word of 2:1 (literally "one known to us"); not "redeemer" and not "near". **"maidens" → "girls"** — Hebrew נַעֲרוֹת. **"he winnoweth barley to night in the threshingfloor" → "he is winnowing the threshing floor of barley tonight"** — Hebrew זֹרֶה אֶת־גֹּרֶן הַשְּׂעֹרִים, word for word: the threshing floor stands for what lies on it.
- **"Wash thyself therefore, and anoint thee, and put thy raiment upon thee, and get thee down" → "And you shall wash and anoint yourself and put your cloaks on you and go down"** (v3) — four verbs in the form of what is to follow (weqatal). סוּךְ is to rub the body with oil (Deut 28:40), not מָשַׁח "anoint". The reading tradition (qere) is translated twice: שִׂמְלֹתַיִךְ "your cloaks" (the written form, ketiv, is singular, "your cloak") and וְיָרַדְתְּ "and you shall go down" (the ketiv וירדתי has the look of "and I will go down"; it is an older form of the second person).
- **"thou shalt mark the place" → "you shall know the place"** (v4) — Hebrew וְיָדַעַתְּ. **"uncover his feet" → "uncover the place of his feet"** (v4, 7, 8, 14) — Hebrew מַרְגְּלֹתָיו, a noun of place from רֶגֶל "foot" ("where his feet are"; so Dan 10:6), not the plain word "feet". In v8 and v14 it stands without a preposition: "lying [at] the place of his feet". "And lie down" follows the qere וְשָׁכָבְתְּ (the ketiv ושכבתי is the older form, as in v3).
- **"All that thou sayest unto me"** (v5) — "to me" (אֵלַי) is read (qere) but not written; so also in v17.
- **"his heart was merry" → "his heart was good"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיִּיטַב לִבּוֹ, as Judg 19:6, 9. **"the heap of corn" → "the heap"** — Hebrew הָעֲרֵמָה; "of corn" is not written. **"softly" → "quietly"** — Hebrew בַלָּט, "in secret, unnoticed".
- **"at midnight" → "in the middle of the night"** (v8) — Hebrew בַּחֲצִי הַלַּיְלָה, literally "in the half of the night". **"the man was afraid, and turned himself" → "the man trembled and turned himself"** — Hebrew וַיֶּחֱרַד (חָרַד "tremble": he started) and וַיִּלָּפֵת, a rare verb ("twist, turn"; Job 6:18); its sense here is not certain ("turned over", "bent forward", "groped").
- **"thine handmaid" → "your slave woman"** (v9) — Hebrew אֲמָתֶךָ, twice: אָמָה is "slave woman", said in deference; in 2:13 she said שִׁפְחָה "maidservant".
- **"spread therefore thy skirt over thine handmaid" → "you shall spread your wing over your slave woman"** (v9) — Hebrew וּפָרַשְׂתָּ כְנָפֶךָ עַל־אֲמָתְךָ. כָּנָף is "wing", and of a garment its "corner, skirt": to spread the skirt of one's garment over a woman is to take her as wife (Ezek 16:8; cf. Deut 22:30). The word list has "corner" for this sense; "wing" is kept here because it is the word of 2:12 ("under whose wings you have come to take refuge"), and Ruth's words take it up. The form may be singular ("your wing", as pointed) or plural written short.
- **"for thou art a near kinsman" → "for you are a redeemer"** (v9, 12, 13) — Hebrew גֹאֵל, and the verb גָּאַל "redeem" four times in v13; "kinsman" is not in the word.
- **"Blessed be thou of the LORD" → "Blessed are you to Yahweh"** (v10) — Hebrew בְּרוּכָה אַתְּ לַיהוָה, without a verb, as 2:20. **"thou hast shewed more kindness in the latter end than at the beginning" → "You have made your latter *chesed* better than the first"** — Hebrew הֵיטַבְתְּ חַסְדֵּךְ הָאַחֲרוֹן מִן־הָרִאשׁוֹן: her first *chesed* was to Naomi (2:11), the latter is this. **"young men"** — Hebrew הַבַּחוּרִים, "the chosen ones", men in their prime (Judg 20:15 "chosen men").
- **"all that thou requirest" → "all that you say"** (v11) — Hebrew תֹּאמְרִי. **"all the city of my people" → "all the gate of my people"** — Hebrew כָּל־שַׁעַר עַמִּי: the gate, where the town meets and judges (4:1).
- **"a virtuous woman" → "a woman of ability"** (v11) — Hebrew אֵשֶׁת חַיִל, the phrase of Prov 31:10. חַיִל is strength and worth; it is the word said of Boaz in 2:1 ("a mighty one of ability") and in the blessing of 4:11, and one English word is kept for it. "Worthy woman", "woman of valor" are other renderings.
- **"it is true that I am thy near kinsman: howbeit there is a kinsman nearer than I" → "it is indeed true that I am a redeemer; and also there is a redeemer nearer than I"** (v12) — Hebrew כִּי אָמְנָם כִּי … גֹאֵל אָנֹכִי; after the second כִּי the word אם is written but not read (ketiv without qere). קָרוֹב מִמֶּנִּי is "near, more than I" (2:20).
- **"Tarry this night" → "Lodge tonight"** (v13) — Hebrew לִינִי, as 1:16. **"if he will perform unto thee the part of a kinsman, well; let him do the kinsman's part" → "if he will redeem you, good, let him redeem"** — Hebrew אִם־יִגְאָלֵךְ טוֹב יִגְאָל. **"if he will not do the part of a kinsman to thee" → "if he has no delight to redeem you"** — Hebrew לֹא יַחְפֹּץ לְגָאֳלֵךְ, the verb of Deut 25:7, 8. **"then will I do the part of a kinsman to thee" → "then I will redeem you, I myself"** — Hebrew וּגְאַלְתִּיךְ אָנֹכִי.
- **"before one could know another" → "before a man could recognize his neighbor"** (v14) — Hebrew בְּטֶרֶם יַכִּיר אִישׁ אֶת־רֵעֵהוּ (the qere בְּטֶרֶם; the ketiv בטרום is another spelling). **"that a woman came into the floor" → "that the woman came to the threshing floor"** — Hebrew הָאִשָּׁה, with the article. To whom he says it (to himself, to her, to his men) is not said.
- **"Bring the vail that thou hast upon thee, and hold it" → "Give the shawl that is on you, and take hold of it"** (v15) — Hebrew הָבִי "give" and מִטְפַּחַת, a wrap (Isa 3:22), another word than the "cloak" of v3; אָחַז "take hold". **"six measures of barley" → "six of barley"** (v15, 17) — Hebrew שֵׁשׁ־שְׂעֹרִים; the measure is not named. **"and she went into the city" → "and he went into the city"** — the Masoretic Text has וַיָּבֹא "and he went"; "she went" is the reading of many manuscripts and versions (cited from memory). The printed text is translated.
- **"Who art thou, my daughter?" restored** (v16) — Hebrew מִי־אַתְּ בִּתִּי, word for word, the question of v9. It is commonly taken as "How is it with you?"; the earlier "How did you fare" gave that sense and not the words.
- **"Go not empty unto thy mother in law" → "Do not come empty to your mother-in-law"** (v17) — Hebrew רֵיקָם "empty", the word of 1:21 ("empty Yahweh has brought me back").
- **"Sit still, my daughter, until thou know how the matter will fall" → "Sit, my daughter, until you know how the matter falls"** (v18) — Hebrew שְׁבִי and אֵיךְ יִפֹּל דָּבָר (דָּבָר "word, matter", without the article). **"will not be in rest, until he have finished the thing this day" → "will not be quiet unless he has finished the matter today"** — Hebrew לֹא יִשְׁקֹט … כִּי־אִם־כִּלָּה.

### Notes
- **The threshing floor** — the grain is winnowed in the evening wind and guarded at night; the owner sleeps by the heap.
- **"The place of his feet"** (v4, 7, 8, 14) — the narrative says what Ruth did in these words and no others.
- **"Spread your wing"** (v9) — Ruth asks Boaz to take her as wife, and gives the reason: "you are a redeemer". Naomi had said only "he will tell you what you shall do" (v4).
- **The nearer redeemer** (v12) — Boaz cannot act before the one who stands nearer has been asked; so chapter 4.
- **"Empty"** (v17) — the word of Naomi's complaint in 1:21.
