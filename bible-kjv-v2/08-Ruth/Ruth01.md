# Ruth 1

[1] And it came to pass in the days when the judges judged that there was a famine in the land; and a man from ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧ of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ went to sojourn in the fields of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, he and his wife and his two sons.

[2] And the name of the man was Elimelech, and the name of his wife ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧, and the name of his two sons ⟦mahlon|Machlon|Mahlon⟧ and ⟦chilion|Kilyon|Chilion⟧, ⟦ephrathite|Efratim|Ephrathites⟧ from ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧ of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧; and they came to the fields of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ and were there.

[3] And Elimelech, the husband of ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧, died, and she was left, she and her two sons.

[4] And they took for themselves ⟦moabites|Moavi|Moabite⟧ wives; the name of the one was Orpah, and the name of the second ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧; and they dwelt there about ten years.

[5] And the two of them also died, ⟦mahlon|Machlon|Mahlon⟧ and ⟦chilion|Kilyon|Chilion⟧; and the woman was left without her two children and without her husband.

[6] And she arose, she and her daughters-in-law, and returned from the fields of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, for she had heard in the field of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had visited his people, to give them bread.

[7] And she went out from the place where she had been, and her two daughters-in-law with her, and they went on the way to return to the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[8] And ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ said to her two daughters-in-law, "Go, return, each woman to the house of her mother. May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ do ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ with you, as you have done with the dead and with me.

[9] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ give to you that you may find a resting place, each woman in the house of her husband." And she kissed them, and they lifted up their voice and wept.

[10] And they said to her, "Surely with you we will return to your people."

[11] And ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ said, "Return, my daughters. Why would you go with me? Have I yet sons in my inward parts, that they might become husbands for you?

[12] Return, my daughters, go; for I am too old to become a man's. If I said, 'I have hope,' even if I became a man's tonight and also bore sons,

[13] would you wait for them until they were grown? Would you for them keep yourselves shut up, so as not to become a man's? No, my daughters; for it is far more bitter for me than for you, for the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has gone out against me."

[14] And they lifted up their voice and wept again; and Orpah kissed her mother-in-law, and ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧ clung to her.

[15] And she said, "Behold, your sister-in-law has returned to her people and to her gods; return after your sister-in-law."

[16] And ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧ said, "Do not entreat me to forsake you, to return from after you; for where you go I will go, and where you lodge I will lodge; your people, my people, and your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[17] Where you die I will die, and there I will be buried. Thus may ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ do to me, and thus may he add: only death shall part me and you."

[18] And she saw that she was making herself strong to go with her, and she ceased to speak to her.

[19] And the two of them went until they came to ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧. And it came to pass, when they came to ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧, that all the city resounded over them, and they said, "Is this ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧?"

[20] And she said to them, "Do not call me ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧; call me Mara, for ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ has made it very bitter for me.

[21] I went full, and empty ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has brought me back. Why do you call me ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧, when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has answered against me, and ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ has done evil to me?"

[22] And ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ returned, and ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧ the ⟦moabites|Moaviyah|Moabitess⟧, her daughter-in-law, with her, who returned from the fields of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧; and they came to ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧ at the beginning of the barley harvest.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton kept.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"when the judges ruled" → "when the judges judged"** (v1) — Hebrew בִּימֵי שְׁפֹט הַשֹּׁפְטִים, literally "in the days of the judging of the judges": verb and noun of one root.
- **"a certain man of Bethlehemjudah" → "a man from Bethlehem of Judah"** (v1, 2) — Hebrew אִישׁ מִבֵּית לֶחֶם יְהוּדָה, a genitive, as Judg 19:1.
- **"the country of Moab" → "the fields of Moab" / "the field of Moab"** (v1, 2, 6, 22) — Hebrew שְׂדֵי מוֹאָב (plural) and once שְׂדֵה מוֹאָב (singular, v6b): the open land of Moab.
- **"continued there" → "were there"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיִּהְיוּ־שָׁם.
- **"Naomi's husband"** (v3) — Hebrew אִישׁ נָעֳמִי, literally "the man of Naomi"; אִישׁ is "man" and "husband" (v3, 5, 9, 11–13).
- **"took them wives of the women of Moab" → "took for themselves Moabite wives"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׂאוּ לָהֶם נָשִׁים מֹאֲבִיּוֹת, literally "lifted up for themselves Moabite women" (the later expression for taking a wife, as Judg 21:23). **"the other" → "the second"** — Hebrew הַשֵּׁנִית.
- **"was left of her two sons and her husband" → "was left without her two children and without her husband"** (v5) — Hebrew וַתִּשָּׁאֵר … מִשְּׁנֵי יְלָדֶיהָ וּמֵאִישָׁהּ, literally "was left from her two children and from her husband"; יֶלֶד is "child", not the "son" of v1–3.
- **"that she might return" → "and returned"** (v6) — Hebrew וַתָּשָׁב, a plain past. **"in giving them bread" → "to give them bread"** — Hebrew לָתֵת לָהֶם לָחֶם; "visited" is פָּקַד, to attend to (Gen 21:1; 50:24).
- **"each to her mother's house" → "each woman to the house of her mother"** (v8) — Hebrew אִשָּׁה לְבֵית אִמָּהּ.
- **"the LORD deal kindly with you" → "May Yahweh do *chesed* with you"** (v8) — Hebrew עָשָׂה חֶסֶד עִם; חֶסֶד is faithful love shown in deeds. The reading tradition (qere) יַעַשׂ, a wish, is translated; the written form (ketiv) יעשה can also be "will do". "With you … you have done" (v8) and "to you" (v9, 11), "than for you" (v13) are masculine in form though women are addressed; so also "the two of them" (v19) and "they" (v22). The book has these forms often; they are not marked in the verse.
- **"The LORD grant you that ye may find rest" → "May Yahweh give to you that you may find a resting place"** (v9) — Hebrew יִתֵּן יְהוָה לָכֶם וּמְצֶאןָ מְנוּחָה, literally "may Yahweh give to you — and find (imperative) a resting place"; מְנוּחָה is a place of rest (Deut 12:9), the word of 3:1 (מָנוֹחַ). "In" before "the house of her husband" is not written.
- **"lifted up their voice" kept** (v9, 14) — Hebrew קוֹלָן, singular.
- **"Surely we will return with thee" → "Surely with you we will return"** (v10) — Hebrew כִּי־אִתָּךְ נָשׁוּב: כִּי here gives weight ("no, but with you"); "with you" stands first.
- **"Turn again" → "Return"** (v11, 12, 15) — Hebrew שׁוּב, one verb through the chapter (v6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 15, 16, 21, 22: twelve times). **"are there yet any more sons in my womb" → "Have I yet sons in my inward parts"** — Hebrew בְּמֵעַי, "inward parts"; "womb" is another word. **"that they may be your husbands" → "that they might become husbands for you"** — Hebrew וְהָיוּ לָכֶם לַאֲנָשִׁים, literally "and they would become for you men".
- **"too old to have an husband" → "too old to become a man's"** (v12, 13) — Hebrew מִהְיוֹת לְאִישׁ, literally "from being for a man": the Hebrew way of saying that a woman marries (Lev 22:12; Deut 24:2). **"if I should have an husband also to night" → "even if I became a man's tonight"**.
- **"Would ye tarry for them" → "would you wait for them … Would you for them keep yourselves shut up"** (v13) — Hebrew הֲלָהֵן, twice. The printed form לָהֵן is "for them" with a feminine ending where sons are meant (the book is loose with such endings, see v8), and so the old versions are commonly cited as taking it (from memory); the same letters are also read as the Aramaic word "therefore" ("would you therefore wait"). "For them" is kept; the other sense is possible. תְּשַׂבֵּרְנָה is "wait, hope"; תֵּעָגֵנָה is found only here and its meaning is not certain ("be shut up, held back").
- **"it grieveth me much for your sakes" → "it is far more bitter for me than for you"** (v13) — Hebrew כִּי־מַר־לִי מְאֹד מִכֶּם, literally "for it is very bitter for me from you": "more than for you", or "because of you" ("it is very bitter for me on your account"). The root is that of "Mara" (v20).
- **"clave unto her" → "clung to her"** (v14) — Hebrew דָּבְקָה בָּהּ, the verb of Gen 2:24; so 2:8, 21, 23.
- **"thy sister in law" kept** (v15) — Hebrew יְבִמְתֵּךְ, the feminine of יָבָם "husband's brother" (Deut 25:5–9): here the wife of the husband's brother. The word list has "brother's widow" for this word, which would say the wrong kinship from Ruth's side. **"unto her gods"** — Hebrew אֱלֹהֶיהָ, the same word as "your Elohim, my Elohim" in v16; it may be read "her god" (Chemosh, Num 21:29) or "her gods".
- **"Intreat me not to leave thee, or to return from following after thee" → "Do not entreat me to forsake you, to return from after you"** (v16) — Hebrew אַל־תִּפְגְּעִי־בִי (פָּגַע בְּ, literally "meet, press upon") לְעָזְבֵךְ לָשׁוּב מֵאַחֲרָיִךְ; there is no "or".
- **"thy people shall be my people, and thy God my God" → "your people, my people, and your Elohim, my Elohim"** (v16) — Hebrew עַמֵּךְ עַמִּי וֵאלֹהַיִךְ אֱלֹהָי, four words without a verb; "is" or "shall be" is left to the reader.
- **"the LORD do so to me, and more also, if ought but death part thee and me" → "Thus may Yahweh do to me, and thus may he add: only death shall part me and you"** (v17) — Hebrew כֹּה יַעֲשֶׂה יְהוָה לִי וְכֹה יֹסִיף כִּי הַמָּוֶת יַפְרִיד בֵּינִי וּבֵינֵךְ, literally "Thus may Yahweh do to me and thus may he add, that / for the death shall part between me and between you". The first half is the oath: the speaker calls an unnamed harm on herself. What she swears follows with כִּי; "only" gives the sense of the oath (nothing but death) and is not a Hebrew word here. This is the only place in the book where Ruth names Yahweh.
- **"stedfastly minded" → "making herself strong"** (v18) — Hebrew מִתְאַמֶּצֶת (hitpael of אָמֵץ "be strong"). **"left speaking" → "ceased to speak"** — Hebrew וַתֶּחְדַּל.
- **"all the city was moved about them" → "all the city resounded over them"** (v19) — Hebrew וַתֵּהֹם, "was thrown into commotion", as 1 Sam 4:5. **"and they said"** — the verb is feminine plural: the women of the city speak; "women" is not written.
- **"Call me not Naomi, call me Mara" (v20)** — the names are left as names. נָעֳמִי *No'omi* is from נֹעַם "pleasantness"; מָרָא *Mara* is "bitter" (written with the Aramaic ending א), and הֵמַר "has made bitter" plays on it. The earlier explanations in brackets were not in the Hebrew and are removed from the verse.
- **"the Almighty" → "Shaddai"** (v20, 21) — Hebrew שַׁדַּי; the meaning of the name is not certain. **"hath dealt very bitterly with me" → "has made it very bitter for me"** — Hebrew הֵמַר … לִי מְאֹד.
- **"I went out full, and the LORD hath brought me home again empty" → "I went full, and empty Yahweh has brought me back"** (v21) — Hebrew הָלַכְתִּי "I went"; "empty" stands first in its clause.
- **"the LORD hath testified against me" → "Yahweh has answered against me"** (v21) — Hebrew עָנָה בִי, the expression of a witness in court (Exod 20:16). With other vowels (עִנָּה) the letters say "has humbled me", and so the Septuagint is commonly cited (from memory); the Masoretic vowels are translated. **"hath afflicted me" → "has done evil to me"** — Hebrew הֵרַע לִי.
- **"which returned out of the country of Moab" → "who returned from the fields of Moab"** (v22) — Hebrew הַשָּׁבָה, said of Ruth, though she had never been in Judah. **"and they came"** — Hebrew וְהֵמָּה בָּאוּ "and they, they came", masculine in form.

### Notes
- **In the days of the judges** — the story is set in the time of the book before it (Judg 21:25).
- **The names** — Elimelech "my El is king"; Naomi "pleasantness"; Mahlon and Chilion are commonly connected with "sickness" and "wasting away"; the meanings of Ruth and Orpah are not certain.
- **Moab** — the people of Num 22–25; a Moabite may not come into the assembly of Yahweh (Deut 23:3). From this Moabitess comes David (4:17).
- **Ruth's words** (v16–17) — six short clauses and an oath by the name of Yahweh.
- **Barley harvest** (v22) — the first harvest of the year, in spring; the setting of chapter 2.
