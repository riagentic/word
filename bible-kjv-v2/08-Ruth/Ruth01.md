# Ruth 1

[1] In the days when the judges ruled, there was a famine in the land. A certain man of ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧ in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ went to sojourn in the country of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ — he, his wife, and his two sons.

[2] The name of the man was Elimelech, and the name of his wife ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧, and the names of his two sons ⟦mahlon|Machlon|Mahlon⟧ and ⟦chilion|Kilyon|Chilion⟧. They were ⟦ephrathite|Efratim|Ephrathites⟧ of ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧ in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧. They came to the country of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ and remained there.

[3] Elimelech, ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧'s husband, died, and she was left with her two sons.

[4] They took wives from the ⟦moabites|Moavi|Moabite⟧ women: the name of one was Orpah, and the name of the other ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧; and they dwelt there about ten years.

[5] ⟦mahlon|Machlon|Mahlon⟧ and ⟦chilion|Kilyon|Chilion⟧ also died — both of them. The woman was left bereft of her two sons and her husband.

[6] Then she arose with her daughters-in-law to return from the country of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, for she had heard in the country of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had visited his people in giving them bread.

[7] So she went out from the place where she had been, with her two daughters-in-law with her, and they went on the way to return to the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[8] ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ said to her two daughters-in-law, "Go, return each of you to her mother's house. May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ deal kindly with you, as you have dealt with the dead and with me.

[9] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ grant that each of you may find rest in the house of her husband." Then she kissed them, and they lifted up their voices and wept.

[10] They said to her, "Surely we will return with you to your people."

[11] ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ said, "Turn back, my daughters; why will you go with me? Are there yet sons in my womb that they may be your husbands?

[12] Turn back, my daughters, go your way, for I am too old to have a husband. Even if I should say I have hope — if I should take a husband this night and also bear sons —

[13] would you wait for them until they were grown? Would you shut yourselves off from having husbands? No, my daughters; for it is bitterer for me than for you, because the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has gone out against me."

[14] They lifted up their voices and wept again; Orpah kissed her mother-in-law, but ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧ clung to her.

[15] She said, "Behold, your sister-in-law has gone back to her people and to her gods; return after your sister-in-law."

[16] But ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧ said, "Do not urge me to leave you, to turn back from following you. For where you go, I will go; and where you lodge, I will lodge. Your people shall be my people, and your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[17] Where you die, I will die, and there I will be buried. May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ do so to me, and more also, if anything but death parts me from you."

[18] When she saw that she was determined to go with her, she stopped speaking to her.

[19] So the two of them went on until they came to ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧. When they came to ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧, the whole city was stirred because of them; the women said, "Is this ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧?"

[20] She said to them, "Do not call me ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ ("Pleasant"); call me Mara ("Bitter"), for ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ has dealt very bitterly with me.

[21] I went out full, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has brought me back empty. Why do you call me ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧, when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has testified against me, and ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ has brought calamity upon me?"

[22] So ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ returned, and ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧ the ⟦moabites|Moaviyah|Moabitess⟧ her daughter-in-law with her, who returned out of the country of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧; they came to ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧ at the beginning of the barley harvest.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"the Almighty" → "Shaddai"** (v20, 21) — Hebrew *Shaddai* kept.
- **"Mara / Naomi"** (v20) — Hebrew נָעֳמִי *No'omi* = "pleasant"; מָרָא *Mara* = "bitter." Etymology added.
- **"intreat me not" → "do not urge me"** (v16).
- **"stedfastly minded" → "determined"** (v18).
- **"steadfast love" Hebrew chesed** (v8) — translated as "deal kindly."
- **"hap"** (KJV archaism — no longer used; elsewhere).

### Notes
- **Setting in Judges era** — Ruth takes place "when the judges ruled," providing bright contrast to the moral chaos of the Judges-appendix. A story of chesed (covenant faithfulness) amid faithlessness.
- **Names meaningful** — Elimelech "my El is king"; Mahlon "sickness"; Chilion "destruction"; Naomi "pleasant"; Ruth possibly "friend / companion"; Orpah "back of the neck / turning away."
- **Moab connection** — the Israelites' ancient enemy (Num 22–25) becomes the place of refuge and the origin of David's great-grandmother. Striking theological point: Moab incorporated into Davidic line.
- **Ruth's pledge** (v16–17) — one of the great loyalty-statements in world literature. Marriage-vow language repurposed for daughter-in-law loyalty.
- **Barley harvest** (v22) — March-April. Provides the agricultural setting for the gleaning narrative of ch 2.
