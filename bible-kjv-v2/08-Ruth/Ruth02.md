# Ruth 2

[1] ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ had a kinsman of her husband's, a man of standing and wealth, of the clan of Elimelech, whose name was Boaz.

[2] ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧ the ⟦moabites|Moaviyah|Moabitess⟧ said to ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧, "Let me go to the field and glean ears of grain after someone in whose eyes I may find favor." She said to her, "Go, my daughter."

[3] So she went, came, and gleaned in the field after the reapers; and by chance she happened upon the portion of field belonging to Boaz, who was of the clan of Elimelech.

[4] Behold, Boaz came from ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧ and said to the reapers, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ be with you." They answered him, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ bless you."

[5] Boaz said to his servant who was set over the reapers, "Whose young woman is this?"

[6] The servant who was set over the reapers answered and said, "She is the young ⟦moabites|Moavi|Moabite⟧ woman who came back with ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ from the country of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧.

[7] She said, 'Please let me glean and gather among the sheaves after the reapers.' So she came, and has continued from morning until now, except that she sat a little while in the shelter."

[8] Then Boaz said to ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧, "Will you not listen, my daughter? Do not go to glean in another field, and do not leave from here; stay close by my young women.

[9] Let your eyes be on the field that they reap, and go after them. Have I not commanded the young men not to touch you? When you are thirsty, go to the vessels and drink what the young men have drawn."

[10] Then she fell on her face, bowed to the ground, and said to him, "Why have I found favor in your eyes, that you take notice of me, though I am a foreigner?"

[11] Boaz answered and said to her, "It has been fully shown to me, all that you have done for your mother-in-law since the death of your husband — how you left your father and mother and the land of your birth, and came to a people whom you did not know before.

[12] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ repay your work; may a full wage be given to you by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, under whose wings you have come for refuge."

[13] She said, "May I find favor in your eyes, my lord, for you have comforted me and spoken to the heart of your maidservant — though I am not like one of your maidservants."

[14] At mealtime Boaz said to her, "Come here and eat some bread; dip your morsel in the sour wine." She sat beside the reapers; he passed her roasted grain, and she ate and was satisfied, and had some left over.

[15] When she rose to glean, Boaz commanded his young men, saying, "Let her glean even among the sheaves, and do not reproach her.

[16] Pull out some from the bundles for her also, and leave them for her to glean, and do not rebuke her."

[17] So she gleaned in the field until evening; she beat out what she had gleaned, and it was about an ephah of barley.

[18] She took it up and came into the city; her mother-in-law saw what she had gleaned. She also brought out and gave her what was left over after she had eaten her fill.

[19] Her mother-in-law said to her, "Where did you glean today? Where have you worked? Blessed be the one who took notice of you." So she told her mother-in-law with whom she had worked, and said, "The man's name with whom I worked today is Boaz."

[20] ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ said to her daughter-in-law, "May he be blessed by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who has not left off his kindness to the living and to the dead." ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ said to her, "The man is near of kin to us — one of our kinsman-redeemers."

[21] ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧ the ⟦moabites|Moaviyah|Moabitess⟧ said, "He also said to me, 'You shall stay close by my young men until they have completed all my harvest.'"

[22] ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ said to ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧ her daughter-in-law, "It is good, my daughter, that you go out with his young women, so that they do not meet you in another field."

[23] So she kept close by the young women of Boaz to glean, until the end of the barley harvest and of the wheat harvest; and she dwelt with her mother-in-law.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"mighty man of wealth" → "man of standing and wealth"** (v1) — Hebrew אִישׁ גִּבּוֹר חַיִל *ish gibbor chayil*. Man of substantial character and means.
- **"corn" → "grain"** (v2, 14).
- **"damsel" → "young woman"** (v5, 6).
- **"stranger" → "foreigner"** (v10) — *nokhri*.
- **"vinegar" → "sour wine"** (v14) — Hebrew *chomets*. Fermented wine-vinegar, a common refreshing drink.
- **"parched corn" → "roasted grain"** (v14).
- **"kinsman / one of our next kinsmen" → "kinsman-redeemers"** (v20) — Hebrew *go'el*.
- **"hap was to light" → "by chance she happened"** (v3) — Hebrew *yiqqer miqreha*. "Her chance chanced" — narrative irony signaling providence.
- **"under whose wings" (v12)** — Hebrew *kenafaim*. Yahweh's protective shelter; later a key metaphor.

### Notes
- **Gleaning rights** (Lev 19:9–10, 23:22, Deut 24:19–21) — Ruth operates within these provisions; Boaz exceeds them in hospitality.
- **"By chance she happened"** (v3) — narrator's wink at providence. The text acknowledges apparent chance but frames it as divine guidance.
- **Boaz's generosity** — goes beyond legal minimum: access to water, protection from harassment, food at mealtime, dropped bundles for her to glean.
- **Ruth's status** — three times noted she is a Moabitess (v2, 6, 21) and foreigner (v10). Boaz's inclusive welcome is the theological heart of the book.
- **Go'el** (v20) — key term introduced. The kinsman-redeemer who has rights and duties to preserve the family's line and land (Lev 25, Deut 25:5–10).
