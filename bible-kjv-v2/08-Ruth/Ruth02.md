# Ruth 2

[1] And ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ had a relative of her husband's, a man, a mighty one of ability, from the clan of Elimelech, and his name was Boaz.

[2] And ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧ the ⟦moabites|Moaviyah|Moabitess⟧ said to ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧, "Let me go, please, to the field, and let me glean among the ears of grain after him in whose eyes I find favor." And she said to her, "Go, my daughter."

[3] And she went and came and gleaned in the field after the reapers; and her chance chanced upon the portion of the field that belonged to Boaz, who was from the clan of Elimelech.

[4] And behold, Boaz came from ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧, and he said to the reapers, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ be with you." And they said to him, "May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ bless you."

[5] And Boaz said to his young man who was stationed over the reapers, "Whose is this girl?"

[6] And the young man who was stationed over the reapers answered and said, "She is a ⟦moabites|Moavi|Moabite⟧ girl, who returned with ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ from the field of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧.

[7] And she said, 'Let me glean, please, and I will gather among the sheaves after the reapers.' And she came and has stood from then, the morning, until now; this her sitting in the house has been little."

[8] And Boaz said to ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧, "Have you not heard, my daughter? Do not go to glean in another field, and also do not pass on from this one; and so you shall cling to my girls.

[9] Let your eyes be on the field that they reap, and you shall go after them. Have I not commanded the young men not to touch you? And when you are thirsty, you shall go to the vessels and drink from what the young men draw."

[10] And she fell on her face and bowed down to the ground, and she said to him, "Why have I found favor in your eyes, that you should recognize me, when I am a foreigner?"

[11] And Boaz answered and said to her, "It has surely been told to me, all that you have done with your mother-in-law after the death of your husband, and that you forsook your father and your mother and the land of your kindred and went to a people that you did not know yesterday and the day before.

[12] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ repay your deed, and may your wages be whole from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, under whose wings you have come to take refuge."

[13] And she said, "May I find favor in your eyes, my lord, for you have comforted me, and for you have spoken to the heart of your maidservant; and I, I am not as one of your maidservants."

[14] And Boaz said to her at the time of the food, "Draw near here, and you shall eat of the bread and dip your bit in the vinegar." And she sat at the side of the reapers, and he held out to her parched grain, and she ate and was sated and had some left over.

[15] And she arose to glean; and Boaz commanded his young men, saying, "Also between the sheaves let her glean, and you shall not put her to shame.

[16] And also you shall surely pull out for her from the bundles and leave it, and she shall glean, and you shall not rebuke her."

[17] And she gleaned in the field until the evening, and she beat out what she had gleaned, and it was about an ephah of barley.

[18] And she lifted it up and came into the city, and her mother-in-law saw what she had gleaned; and she brought out and gave her what she had left over after she was sated.

[19] And her mother-in-law said to her, "Where did you glean today, and where did you work? May he who recognized you be blessed." And she told her mother-in-law with whom she had worked, and she said, "The name of the man with whom I worked today is Boaz."

[20] And ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ said to her daughter-in-law, "Blessed is he to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who has not forsaken his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ with the living and with the dead." And ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ said to her, "The man is near to us; he is one of our redeemers."

[21] And ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧ the ⟦moabites|Moaviyah|Moabitess⟧ said, "Also, he said to me, 'To the young men who are mine you shall cling, until they have finished all the harvest that is mine.'"

[22] And ⟦naomi|Noomi|Naomi⟧ said to ⟦ruth|Rut|Ruth⟧ her daughter-in-law, "It is good, my daughter, that you go out with his girls, and that they do not fall upon you in another field."

[23] And she clung to the girls of Boaz, to glean, until the barley harvest and the wheat harvest were finished; and she dwelt with her mother-in-law.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton kept.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v12) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"a kinsman of her husband's" → "a relative of her husband's"** (v1) — the reading tradition (qere) מוֹדַע is translated: literally "one known", a word for a member of the wider family (so 3:2 in its feminine form); the written form (ketiv) מְיֻדָּע is "one well known, a close friend" (Ps 55:13). It is another word than "near" (קָרוֹב, v20) and "redeemer" (גֹּאֵל, v20), and is kept apart from them.
- **"a mighty man of wealth" → "a man, a mighty one of ability"** (v1) — Hebrew אִישׁ גִּבּוֹר חַיִל: חַיִל is strength, worth and means; of a fighter "valor", of goods "wealth". The same phrase is said of warriors (Josh 1:14) and of David (1 Sam 16:18); the same word is said of Ruth in 3:11 and stands in 4:11. One English word is kept for it.
- **"family" → "clan"** (v1, 3) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּחָה.
- **"glean ears of corn after him in whose sight I shall find grace" → "glean among the ears of grain after him in whose eyes I find favor"** (v2) — Hebrew וַאֲלַקֳטָה בַשִּׁבֳּלִים; לִקֵּט is "gather up" what is left on the ground, the word of Lev 19:9; חֵן is "favor" (v2, 10, 13).
- **"her hap was to light on" → "her chance chanced upon"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיִּקֶר מִקְרֶהָ, verb and noun of one root, literally "her happening happened [upon]". **"a part of the field belonging unto Boaz" → "the portion of the field that belonged to Boaz"** — Hebrew חֶלְקַת הַשָּׂדֶה לְבֹעַז, as 4:3.
- **"The LORD be with you" kept; "The LORD bless thee" → "May Yahweh bless you"** (v4) — Hebrew יְהוָה עִמָּכֶם, without a verb ("Yahweh is with you" is also possible), and יְבָרֶכְךָ יְהוָה.
- **"his servant that was set over the reapers" → "his young man who was stationed over the reapers"** (v5, 6) — Hebrew נַעַר, of an attendant, and הַנִּצָּב עַל. **"Whose damsel is this?" → "Whose is this girl?"** — Hebrew לְמִי הַנַּעֲרָה הַזֹּאת; נַעֲרָה is the feminine of נַעַר (v5, 6; "my girls", v8, 22, 23).
- **"the Moabitish damsel that came back" → "a Moabite girl, who returned"** (v6) — Hebrew נַעֲרָה מוֹאֲבִיָּה הִיא, without the article; הַשָּׁבָה as 1:22. **"the country of Moab" → "the field of Moab"** — as 1:6.
- **"I pray you, let me glean and gather after the reapers among the sheaves" → "Let me glean, please, and I will gather among the sheaves after the reapers"** (v7) — two verbs: לִקֵּט "glean" and אָסַף "gather".
- **"hath continued even from the morning until now, that she tarried a little in the house" → "has stood from then, the morning, until now; this her sitting in the house has been little"** (v7) — Hebrew וַתַּעֲמוֹד מֵאָז הַבֹּקֶר וְעַד־עַתָּה זֶה שִׁבְתָּהּ הַבַּיִת מְעָט, word for word: "and she stood from then, the morning, and until now; this, her sitting, the house, a little". The last four words are not clear and the meaning is uncertain: either she has rested in the house (the shelter at the field) only a little, or "this" points to her and the words say that she has hardly sat down. The versions are commonly cited as reading otherwise ("she has not rested in the field even a little": from memory). The Masoretic Text is translated as it stands.
- **"Hearest thou not, my daughter?" → "Have you not heard, my daughter?"** (v8) — Hebrew הֲלוֹא שָׁמַעַתְּ, a perfect. **"neither go from hence" → "and also do not pass on from this one"** — Hebrew לֹא תַעֲבוּרִי מִזֶּה. **"abide here fast by my maidens" → "so you shall cling to my girls"** — Hebrew וְכֹה תִדְבָּקִין עִם, the verb of 1:14 with עִם "with"; so v21 (with the young men) and v23 (with בְּ).
- **"go thou after them"** (v9) — "them" is feminine: the girls. **"that they shall not touch thee" → "not to touch you"** — Hebrew לְבִלְתִּי נָגְעֵךְ. **"of that which the young men have drawn" → "from what the young men draw"** — Hebrew יִשְׁאֲבוּן, an imperfect.
- **"that thou shouldest take knowledge of me, seeing I am a stranger" → "that you should recognize me, when I am a foreigner"** (v10) — Hebrew לְהַכִּירֵנִי וְאָנֹכִי נָכְרִיָּה: "recognize" (הִכִּיר) and "foreigner" (נָכְרִיָּה) sound alike; so again "he who recognized you" (v19).
- **"It hath fully been shewed me" → "It has surely been told to me"** (v11) — Hebrew הֻגֵּד הֻגַּד, the infinitive absolute with its verb: literally "being told it has been told". **"unto thy mother in law" → "with your mother-in-law"** — Hebrew אֶת. **"hast left" → "forsook"** — Hebrew וַתַּעַזְבִי, as 1:16. **"the land of thy nativity" → "the land of your kindred"** — Hebrew אֶרֶץ מוֹלַדְתֵּךְ, as Gen 12:1; 24:7. **"heretofore" → "yesterday and the day before"** — Hebrew תְּמוֹל שִׁלְשׁוֹם.
- **"The LORD recompense thy work, and a full reward be given thee of the LORD" → "May Yahweh repay your deed, and may your wages be whole from Yahweh"** (v12) — Hebrew יְשַׁלֵּם … פָּעֳלֵךְ and מַשְׂכֻּרְתֵּךְ שְׁלֵמָה: "repay" and "whole" are one root (שׁלם); מַשְׂכֹּרֶת is "wages" (Gen 29:15; 31:7).
- **"under whose wings thou art come to trust" → "under whose wings you have come to take refuge"** (v12) — Hebrew לַחֲסוֹת תַּחַת־כְּנָפָיו. כָּנָף is "wing"; the same word stands in 3:9 of Boaz.
- **"Let me find favour in thy sight" → "May I find favor in your eyes"** (v13) — Hebrew אֶמְצָא־חֵן, an imperfect: a wish, or thanks ("I find favor"). **"spoken friendly unto" → "spoken to the heart of"** — Hebrew דִבַּרְתָּ עַל־לֵב, as Gen 34:3; 50:21; Judg 19:3. **"handmaid" → "maidservant"** — Hebrew שִׁפְחָה; in 3:9 she calls herself אָמָה, another word. **"though I be not like" → "and I, I am not as"** — Hebrew וְאָנֹכִי לֹא אֶהְיֶה, literally "and I, I shall not be".
- **"At mealtime come thou hither" → "And Boaz said to her at the time of the food, Draw near here"** (v14) — the accents join "at the time of the food" to "said". **"morsel" → "bit"; "parched corn" → "parched grain"; "vinegar" kept** — Hebrew פַּת, קָלִי, חֹמֶץ (a sour drink of wine). **"he reached her" → "he held out to her"** — Hebrew וַיִּצְבָּט, found only here; the sense is not certain ("held out", or "heaped up"). **"was sufficed, and left" → "was sated and had some left over"**.
- **"reproach her not" → "you shall not put her to shame"** (v15) — Hebrew תַכְלִימוּהָ.
- **"let fall also some of the handfuls of purpose for her" → "you shall surely pull out for her from the bundles"** (v16) — Hebrew שֹׁל־תָּשֹׁלּוּ, the infinitive absolute with its verb; צְבָתִים is found only here ("bundles", handfuls of stalks; uncertain).
- **"So she gleaned" / "she took it up"** (v17, 18) — "it" in v18 is supplied; the Hebrew has only וַתִּשָּׂא "and she lifted". **"what she had reserved after she was sufficed" → "what she had left over after she was sated"** — Hebrew מִשָּׂבְעָהּ, literally "from her being sated".
- **"where wroughtest thou?" → "where did you work?"** (v19) — Hebrew עָשִׂית, the verb "do, make"; so "with whom she had worked". **"blessed be he that did take knowledge of thee" → "May he who recognized you be blessed"** — Hebrew יְהִי מַכִּירֵךְ בָּרוּךְ.
- **"Blessed be he of the LORD, who hath not left off his kindness to the living and to the dead" → "Blessed is he to Yahweh, who has not forsaken his *chesed* with the living and with the dead"** (v20) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ הוּא לַיהוָה אֲשֶׁר לֹא־עָזַב חַסְדּוֹ אֶת־הַחַיִּים וְאֶת־הַמֵּתִים. "Who" can be Yahweh or Boaz; the Hebrew leaves it open (Gen 24:27 has the same words of Yahweh). עָזַב is "forsake" (1:16; 2:11).
- **"The man is near of kin unto us, one of our next kinsmen" → "The man is near to us; he is one of our redeemers"** (v20) — Hebrew קָרוֹב לָנוּ הָאִישׁ מִגֹּאֲלֵנוּ הוּא: קָרוֹב is "near"; גֹּאֵל is "redeemer", the one who buys back what a relative has lost (Lev 25:25, 48). "Kinsman" is not in either word.
- **"He said unto me also" → "Also, he said to me"** (v21) — Hebrew גַּם כִּי־אָמַר. **"keep fast by my young men" → "To the young men who are mine you shall cling"** — Hebrew הַנְּעָרִים, masculine (v8 had "my girls"). **"ended" → "finished"**.
- **"that they meet thee not in any other field" → "and that they do not fall upon you in another field"** (v22) — Hebrew וְלֹא יִפְגְּעוּ־בָךְ: פָּגַע בְּ is "meet, come upon", of hostile meeting "fall upon"; in 1:16 the same verb is "entreat".
- **"kept fast by" → "clung to"** (v23) — Hebrew וַתִּדְבַּק בְּ. **"unto the end of barley harvest and of wheat harvest" → "until the barley harvest and the wheat harvest were finished"** — Hebrew עַד־כְּלוֹת. **"dwelt with"** — Hebrew וַתֵּשֶׁב אֶת.

### Notes
- **Gleaning** (Lev 19:9–10; 23:22; Deut 24:19–21) — what the reapers leave belongs to the poor and the sojourner; Ruth asks for this, and Boaz gives more.
- **"Her chance chanced"** (v3) — the narrator names no cause; Naomi names Yahweh (v20).
- **What Boaz grants** — a place with his girls, water, a share of the meal, and stalks pulled out for her (v8–16).
- **"The Moabitess"** (v2, 6, 21) and "foreigner" (v10) — her origin is named again and again.
- **The redeemer** (v20) — the word comes here for the first time in the book; see Lev 25:25–55 and chapters 3–4.
