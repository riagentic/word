# Ephesians 4

[1] I therefore, the prisoner in the Lord, exhort you to walk worthily of the calling with which you were called,

[2] with all humility and meekness, with patience, bearing with one another in love,

[3] being diligent to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace;

[4] **one body and one Spirit, just as also you were called in one hope of your calling;**

[5] one Lord, one faith, one ⟦immersion|immersion|baptism⟧;

**[6] one ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and Father of all, who is over all and through all and in all**.

[7] But to each one of us the grace was given according to the measure of the gift of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[8] Therefore it says,
"**Having ascended on high he led captivity captive,
he gave gifts to men**."

[9] Now this, "He ascended" — what is it but that he also descended into the lower parts of the earth?

[10] He who descended is himself also the one who ascended far above all the heavens, that he might fill all things.

[11] And he himself gave some as apostles, and some as prophets, and some as evangelists, and some as shepherds and teachers,

[12] for the equipping of the saints unto a work of service, unto the building up of the body of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[13] until we all arrive at the unity of the faith and of the full knowledge of the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, at a perfect man, at the measure of the stature of the fullness of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[14] that we may no longer be infants, tossed by waves and carried about by every wind of the doctrine, in the sleight of men, in craftiness unto the scheming of error,

[15] but, speaking the truth in love, may grow up into him in all things, who is the head, ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[16] from whom all the body, being fitted together and knit together through every joint of the supply, according to the working in measure of each one part, makes the growth of the body unto the building up of itself in love.

[17] This therefore I say and testify in the Lord, that you no longer walk just as also the nations walk, in the vanity of their mind,

[18] being darkened in the understanding, alienated from the life of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, because of the ignorance that is in them, because of the hardening of their heart,

[19] who, having become callous, delivered themselves up to licentiousness unto the practice of all uncleanness in covetousness.

[20] But you did not so learn ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[21] if indeed you heard him and were taught in him, just as truth is in ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧,

[22] that you put away, as regards the former conduct, the old man, who is being corrupted according to the desires of deceit,

[23] and be renewed in the spirit of your mind,

[24] and put on the new man, who was created according to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in righteousness and devoutness of the truth.

[25] Therefore, having put away the lie, speak truth each one with his neighbor, because we are members of one another.

[26] **Be angry and do not sin; let not the sun go down upon your provocation**,

[27] nor give place to the devil.

[28] Let the one who steals steal no longer, but rather let him labor, working with his own hands what is good, that he may have something to share with the one who has need.

[29] Let no rotten word go out of your mouth, but if any is good for the building up of the need, that it may give grace to those who hear.

[30] And do not grieve the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, in which you were sealed unto the day of redemption.

[31] Let all bitterness and rage and wrath and clamor and blasphemy be taken away from you, with all malice.

[32] But become kind to one another, tender in inward parts, forgiving each other, just as also ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ forgave you.

## Changes

- **"Christ" / "Jesus" → "*Mashiach*" / "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv6, 13, 18, 24, 30, 32) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"baptism" → "immersion"** (v5) — Greek βάπτισμα *baptisma* = the act of immersing; "one immersion" renders ἓν βάπτισμα.
- **"Holy Spirit" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v30) — Greek τὸ πνεῦμα τὸ ἅγιον; Hebrew רוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ *Ruach HaQodesh*, "Spirit of Holiness."
- **"the prisoner of the Lord, beseech you that ye walk worthy of the vocation wherewith ye are called" → "the prisoner in the Lord, exhort you to walk worthily of the calling with which you were called"** (v1) — Greek ὁ δέσμιος ἐν κυρίῳ, "in the Lord" (not "for"); παρακαλῶ is rendered "exhort" in this letter where it begins instruction, "encourage" in 6:22; ἀξίως περιπατῆσαι as Col 1:10.
- **"With all lowliness and meekness, with longsuffering, forbearing one another" → "with all humility and meekness, with patience, bearing with one another"** (v2) — Greek ταπεινοφροσύνης, πραΰτητος, μακροθυμίας, ἀνεχόμενοι ἀλλήλων, as Col 3:12–13.
- **"Endeavouring to keep" → "being diligent to keep"** (v3) — Greek σπουδάζοντες τηρεῖν; σύνδεσμος "bond" as Col 3:14.
- **"There is one body, and one Spirit" → "one body and one Spirit"** (v4) — the Greek has no verb in v4–6; "There is" is supplied in the KJV. "In one hope of your calling": ἐν μιᾷ ἐλπίδι τῆς κλήσεως ὑμῶν.
- **"who is above all, and through all, and in you all" → "who is over all and through all and in all"** (v6) — Greek ἐπὶ πάντων καὶ διὰ πάντων καὶ ἐν πᾶσιν; "you" is not in the critical text. "All" can be persons or things each time.
- **"unto every one of us is given grace" → "to each one of us the grace was given"** (v7) — Greek ἐδόθη ἡ χάρις, aorist, with the article in the printed text.
- **"When he ascended up on high, he led captivity captive, and gave gifts unto men" → "Having ascended on high he led captivity captive, he gave gifts to men"** (v8) — Greek Ἀναβὰς, a participle; ᾐχμαλώτευσεν αἰχμαλωσίαν, verb and noun of one root (αἰχμαλωσία, captivity, or the captives as a body); no "and" before "he gave" (SBLGNT NA28; WH, Treg and RP read καὶ ἔδωκεν, "and gave": 2–2, NA28 decides). Ps 68:18 in the Hebrew reads "you received gifts among men".
- **"that he also descended first into the lower parts of the earth" → "that he also descended into the lower parts of the earth"** (v9) — "first" is not in the critical text. τῆς γῆς is a genitive: "the lower parts, which are the earth" or "the parts lower than the earth / the lower parts belonging to the earth"; the verse leaves it open. The KJV brackets of v9–10 are not in the Greek.
- **"And he gave some, apostles" → "And he himself gave some as apostles"** (v11) — Greek αὐτὸς ἔδωκεν τοὺς μὲν … τοὺς δὲ: "some … others"; the last pair, "shepherds and teachers", shares one article.
- **"For the perfecting of the saints, for the work of the ministry, for the edifying of the body" → "for the equipping of the saints unto a work of service, unto the building up of the body"** (v12) — Greek πρὸς τὸν καταρτισμὸν … εἰς ἔργον διακονίας, εἰς οἰκοδομὴν: one πρός and two εἰς; the Greek does not say whether the three stand side by side or the second depends on the first. καταρτισμός is from καταρτίζω, "fit together, mend, restore"; διακονία is "service"; οἰκοδομή "building up" (v16, 29; "building" in 2:21).
- **"Till we all come in the unity of the faith, and of the knowledge" → "until we all arrive at the unity of the faith and of the full knowledge"** (v13) — Greek καταντήσωμεν εἰς; ἐπιγνώσεως as 1:17.
- **"unto a perfect man" → "at a perfect man"** (v13) — Greek εἰς ἄνδρα τέλειον; τέλειος is "perfect, full-grown" (Col 1:28), ἀνήρ a grown male, set against "infants" (v14). ἡλικία is "stature" or "age".
- **"children, tossed to and fro, and carried about with every wind of doctrine" → "infants, tossed by waves and carried about by every wind of the doctrine"** (v14) — Greek νήπιοι; κλυδωνιζόμενοι, from κλύδων "wave"; διδασκαλία "doctrine" as Col 2:22.
- **"by the sleight of men, and cunning craftiness, whereby they lie in wait to deceive" → "in the sleight of men, in craftiness unto the scheming of error"** (v14) — Greek ἐν τῇ κυβείᾳ τῶν ἀνθρώπων ἐν πανουργίᾳ πρὸς τὴν μεθοδείαν τῆς πλάνης. κυβεία is dice-playing, hence trickery; μεθοδεία "scheming" as 6:11; πλάνη "error". The Greek has no "they lie in wait".
- **"But speaking the truth in love" kept** (v15) — Greek ἀληθεύοντες, one verb, "being truthful, holding to truth"; not only speech.
- **"which is the head, even Christ" → "who is the head, the *Mashiach*"** (v15) — Greek ὅς ἐστιν ἡ κεφαλή, Χριστός. "May grow up" (αὐξήσωμεν) still depends on "that" in v14; the Greek has no "we are to".
- **"fitly joined together and compacted by that which every joint supplieth, according to the effectual working in the measure of every part, maketh increase of the body unto the edifying of itself" → "being fitted together and knit together through every joint of the supply, according to the working in measure of each one part, makes the growth of the body unto the building up of itself"** (v16) — Greek συναρμολογούμενον (2:21) καὶ συμβιβαζόμενον (Col 2:19) διὰ πάσης ἁφῆς τῆς ἐπιχορηγίας κατʼ ἐνέργειαν ἐν μέτρῳ ἑνὸς ἑκάστου μέρους. ἁφή is "joint" or "contact"; "of the supply" is a genitive (the joint that serves the supply). The Greek has no "when each part is working properly".
- **"as other Gentiles walk" → "just as also the nations walk"** (v17) — "other" is not in the critical text; ματαιότης "vanity". μαρτύρομαι "testify" as 1 Thess 2:12.
- **"Having the understanding darkened … because of the blindness of their heart" → "being darkened in the understanding … because of the hardening of their heart"** (v18) — Greek διάνοια (beside νοῦς "mind", v17); πώρωσις, a hardening, as of a callus. v17–19 are one sentence in Greek.
- **"Who being past feeling have given themselves over unto lasciviousness, to work all uncleanness with greediness" → "who, having become callous, delivered themselves up to licentiousness unto the practice of all uncleanness in covetousness"** (v19) — Greek ἀπηλγηκότες, "having ceased to feel pain"; ἀσέλγεια; ἐργασία, "practice, trade"; πλεονεξία "covetousness" as 5:3 and Col 3:5.
- **"If so be that ye have heard him, and have been taught by him, as the truth is in Jesus" → "if indeed you heard him and were taught in him, just as truth is in *Yeshua*"** (v21) — Greek αὐτὸν ἠκούσατε, "heard him", not "about him"; ἐν αὐτῷ, "in him"; ἀλήθεια without the article.
- **"That ye put off concerning the former conversation the old man, which is corrupt according to the deceitful lusts" → "that you put away, as regards the former conduct, the old man, who is being corrupted according to the desires of deceit"** (v22) — Greek ἀποθέσθαι (the verb of v25 and Col 3:8); ἀναστροφή "conduct"; φθειρόμενον, present; τὰς ἐπιθυμίας τῆς ἀπάτης, a genitive. The infinitives of v22–24 give the content of "you were taught".
- **"which after God is created in righteousness and true holiness" → "who was created according to *Elohim* in righteousness and devoutness of the truth"** (v24) — Greek τὸν κατὰ θεὸν κτισθέντα, no "likeness" in the Greek; ὁσιότητι τῆς ἀληθείας: ὁσιότης is from ὅσιος "devout" (not ἅγιος "holy"), and "of the truth" is a genitive, set against "of deceit" (v22).
- **"putting away lying" → "having put away the lie"** (v25) — Greek ἀποθέμενοι τὸ ψεῦδος, an aorist participle and a noun.
- **"let not the sun go down upon your wrath" → "let not the sun go down upon your provocation"** (v26) — Greek παροργισμῷ, the state of having been provoked to wrath; the verb in 6:4. "Be angry" (ὀργίζεσθε) is the verb of ὀργή, "wrath" (v31).
- **"working with his hands the thing which is good, that he may have to give to him that needeth" → "working with his own hands what is good, that he may have something to share with the one who has need"** (v28) — Greek ταῖς ἰδίαις χερσὶν τὸ ἀγαθόν; μεταδιδόναι. "The one who steals": ὁ κλέπτων, a present participle. The Greek has no "honest".
- **"Let no corrupt communication proceed out of your mouth, but that which is good to the use of edifying" → "Let no rotten word go out of your mouth, but if any is good for the building up of the need"** (v29) — Greek λόγος σαπρός, "rotten", as of fruit or fish; εἴ τις ἀγαθὸς πρὸς οἰκοδομὴν τῆς χρείας, literally "for building up of the need": the building up that the need calls for.
- **"whereby ye are sealed" → "in which you were sealed"** (v30) — Greek ἐν ᾧ ἐσφραγίσθητε, aorist (1:13); "which" is neuter, the grammatical gender of πνεῦμα.
- **"wrath, and anger, and clamour, and evil speaking" → "rage and wrath and clamor and blasphemy"** (v31) — Greek θυμός, ὀργή, κραυγή, βλασφημία, as Col 3:8; κακία "malice".
- **"be ye kind one to another, tenderhearted" → "But become kind to one another, tender in inward parts"** (v32) — Greek γίνεσθε δέ (WH Treg NA28 RP; SBLGNT, with the WH margin, omits δέ, "but"); εὔσπλαγχνοι, from σπλάγχνα "inward parts" (Col 3:12), not "heart".
- **"even as God for Christ's sake hath forgiven you" → "just as also *Elohim* in the *Mashiach* forgave you"** (v32) — Greek ἐν Χριστῷ ἐχαρίσατο ὑμῖν; χαρίζομαι is "forgive" and "give freely" (Col 3:13).

### Notes
- **v8** quotes Ps 68:18.
- **v25** echoes Zech 8:16.
- **v26** echoes Ps 4:4.
