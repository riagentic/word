# Ephesians 2

[1] And you, being dead in your trespasses and sins,

[2] in which you once walked according to the age of this world, according to the ruler of the authority of the air, of the spirit that is now working in the sons of disobedience;

[3] among whom we also all once conducted ourselves in the desires of our flesh, doing the wills of the flesh and of the thoughts, and we were by nature children of wrath, as also the rest;

[4] but ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, being rich in mercy, because of his great love with which he loved us,

[5] even when we were dead in the trespasses, made us alive together with ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ — by grace you have been saved —

[6] and raised us together and seated us together in the heavenlies in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧,

[7] that he might demonstrate in the ages that are coming the exceeding riches of his grace in kindness toward us in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[8] **For by grace you have been saved through faith; and this not from you, it is the gift of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;**

**[9] not from works, that no one may boast.**

[10] For we are his workmanship, created in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ for good works, which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ prepared beforehand that we should walk in them.

[11] Therefore remember that once you, the nations in the flesh, who are called uncircumcision by what is called circumcision in the flesh, made with hands,

[12] that you were at that time apart from ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, alienated from the citizenship of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and strangers of the covenants of the promise, not having hope and without ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in the world.

[13] But now in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ you who once were far off became near in the blood of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[14] **For he himself is our peace, who made both one and broke down the middle wall of the fence, the enmity in his flesh,**

[15] having abolished the law of the commandments in decrees, that he might create the two in himself into one new man, making peace,

[16] and might reconcile both in one body to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ through the cross, having killed the enmity in it;

[17] and having come he preached peace to you who were far off and peace to those who were near;

[18] because through him we both have the access in one Spirit to the Father.

[19] So then you are no longer strangers and aliens, but you are fellow citizens of the saints and members of the household of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[20] having been built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ himself being the cornerstone,

[21] in whom every building, being fitted together, grows into a holy sanctuary in the Lord,

[22] in whom you also are being built together into a dwelling place of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in the Spirit.

## Changes

- **"Christ Jesus" / "Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv4, 8, 10, 12, 16, 19, 22) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And you hath he quickened, who were dead in trespasses and sins" → "And you, being dead in your trespasses and sins,"** (v1) — Greek Καὶ ὑμᾶς ὄντας νεκροὺς: an object without a verb. The sentence breaks off and is taken up in v4–5; "hath he quickened" (KJV) and "you were dead" (modern versions) are supplied. As Col 2:13.
- **"according to the course of this world" → "according to the age of this world"** (v2) — Greek κατὰ τὸν αἰῶνα τοῦ κόσμου τούτου; αἰών is "age" throughout (1:21; 2:7; 3:9, 11, 21).
- **"the prince of the power of the air, the spirit that now worketh" → "the ruler of the authority of the air, of the spirit that is now working"** (v2) — Greek ἄρχοντα "ruler", ἐξουσίας "authority" (Col 1:13); τοῦ πνεύματος is a genitive: it stands beside "of the authority" (or "of the air"), not beside "the ruler". ἐνεργοῦντος as 1:11, 20.
- **"the children of disobedience" → "the sons of disobedience"** (v2) — Greek υἱοῖς, as 5:6 and Col 3:6.
- **"had our conversation in times past in the lusts of our flesh" → "once conducted ourselves in the desires of our flesh"** (v3) — Greek ἀνεστράφημεν (the noun in 4:22), ἐπιθυμίαις. ἐν οἷς is "among whom" or "in which" (the trespasses).
- **"fulfilling the desires of the flesh and of the mind" → "doing the wills of the flesh and of the thoughts"** (v3) — Greek τὰ θελήματα, plural of the word "will" (1:1, 5, 9, 11), a different word from "desires"; διανοιῶν, plural of διάνοια ("understanding", 4:18).
- **"even as others" → "as also the rest"** (v3) — Greek ὡς καὶ οἱ λοιποί; no "of mankind".
- **"for his great love" → "because of his great love"** (v4) — Greek διὰ τὴν πολλὴν ἀγάπην αὐτοῦ.
- **"hath quickened us together with Christ" → "made us alive together with the *Mashiach*"** (v5) — Greek συνεζωοποίησεν, aorist, as Col 2:13; "the trespasses" has no "our".
- **"hath raised us up together, and made us sit together in heavenly places" → "raised us together and seated us together in the heavenlies"** (v6) — Greek συνήγειρεν καὶ συνεκάθισεν, two verbs with συν-, "together with"; the Greek does not repeat "us" or add "with him". "The heavenlies": see 1:3.
- **"That in the ages to come he might shew the exceeding riches" → "that he might demonstrate in the ages that are coming the exceeding riches"** (v7) — Greek ἐνδείξηται (the noun in Phil 1:28); ὑπερβάλλον, as 1:19.
- **"and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God" → "and this not from you, it is the gift of *Elohim*"** (v8) — Greek καὶ τοῦτο οὐκ ἐξ ὑμῶν, θεοῦ τὸ δῶρον, without a verb ("it is" is supplied). τοῦτο, "this", is neuter; "grace" and "faith" are feminine, so it points to the whole of what precedes. The printed text has "through faith" without the article.
- **"Not of works, lest any man should boast" → "not from works, that no one may boast"** (v9) — Greek οὐκ ἐξ ἔργων, the same ἐξ as in v8; no "a result of".
- **"his workmanship" kept** (v10) — Greek ποίημα, "a thing made" (from ποιέω), not the word "work" (ἔργον) that follows.
- **"which God hath before ordained" → "which *Elohim* prepared beforehand"** (v10) — Greek προητοίμασεν.
- **"ye being in time past Gentiles in the flesh" → "once you, the nations in the flesh"** (v11) — Greek τὰ ἔθνη, "the nations", as 3:1, 6, 8; 4:17.
- **"who are called Uncircumcision by that which is called the Circumcision in the flesh made by hands" kept in substance** (v11) — Greek οἱ λεγόμενοι … ὑπὸ τῆς λεγομένης, the same participle twice; χειροποιήτου "made with hands" (the opposite in Col 2:11). The verse has no main verb; v12 repeats "that".
- **"without Christ, being aliens from the commonwealth of Israel" → "apart from the *Mashiach*, alienated from the citizenship of *Yisrael*"** (v12) — Greek χωρὶς Χριστοῦ; ἀπηλλοτριωμένοι as 4:18 and Col 1:21; πολιτεία is "citizenship" or "commonwealth", the root of "fellow citizens" (v19).
- **"strangers from the covenants of promise" → "strangers of the covenants of the promise"** (v12) — Greek ξένοι τῶν διαθηκῶν τῆς ἐπαγγελίας, genitives, with the article.
- **"without God" kept** (v12) — Greek ἄθεοι, one word, "godless".
- **"are made nigh by the blood of Christ" → "became near in the blood of the *Mashiach*"** (v13) — Greek ἐγενήθητε ἐγγὺς ἐν τῷ αἵματι.
- **"hath broken down the middle wall of partition between us" → "broke down the middle wall of the fence"** (v14) — Greek τὸ μεσότοιχον τοῦ φραγμοῦ; "between us" is not in the Greek. "Both" is neuter here (τὰ ἀμφότερα) and masculine in v16, 18.
- **"Having abolished in his flesh the enmity, even the law of commandments contained in ordinances" → "the enmity in his flesh, having abolished the law of the commandments in decrees"** (v14–15) — Greek τὴν ἔχθραν ἐν τῇ σαρκὶ αὐτοῦ, τὸν νόμον τῶν ἐντολῶν ἐν δόγμασιν καταργήσας, in this order and with the printed commas. "The enmity" is either a second name of the wall (object of "broke down") or the object of "having abolished", with "the law" explaining it; "in his flesh" goes with either verb. The verse keeps the Greek order and leaves this open. δόγμασιν "decrees" as Col 2:14; the Greek has no "contained" or "expressed".
- **"for to make in himself of twain one new man" → "that he might create the two in himself into one new man"** (v15) — Greek κτίσῃ, the verb of v10; no "in place of". "In himself": SBLGNT and WH print ἐν αὑτῷ, Treg and NA28 ἐν αὐτῷ ("in him"); the difference is only the breathing mark, which the old manuscripts do not have, and the sense here is reflexive.
- **"that he might reconcile both unto God in one body by the cross, having slain the enmity thereby" → "and might reconcile both in one body to *Elohim* through the cross, having killed the enmity in it"** (v16) — Greek ἀποκαταλλάξῃ as Col 1:20, 22; ἐν αὐτῷ is "in it" (the cross) or "in himself".
- **"came and preached peace to you which were afar off, and to them that were nigh" → "having come he preached peace to you who were far off and peace to those who were near"** (v17) — Greek εὐηγγελίσατο, "announced as good news" (1 Thess 3:6); the printed text has "peace" twice.
- **"we both have access by one Spirit" → "we both have the access in one Spirit"** (v18) — Greek τὴν προσαγωγὴν … ἐν ἑνὶ πνεύματι.
- **"strangers and foreigners, but fellowcitizens with the saints, and of the household of God" → "strangers and aliens, but you are fellow citizens of the saints and members of the household of *Elohim*"** (v19) — Greek ξένοι (v12) καὶ πάροικοι, "aliens" (Heb 11:9), those who live beside the citizens without their rights; not the root of "alienated" (v12); συμπολῖται τῶν ἁγίων, a genitive; οἰκεῖοι, those who belong to the house; the printed text repeats "you are".
- **"Jesus Christ himself being the chief corner stone" → "the *Mashiach* *Yeshua* himself being the cornerstone"** (v20) — Greek ἀκρογωνιαίου, "at the extreme corner": the cornerstone of the foundation, or the stone that crowns the building. αὐτοῦ can also be read "its cornerstone being the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*".
- **"all the building fitly framed together" → "every building, being fitted together"** (v21) — the printed text has πᾶσα οἰκοδομή without the article, in classical usage "every building"; in later Greek it can also mean "the whole building". συναρμολογουμένη as 4:16.
- **"for an habitation of God through the Spirit" → "into a dwelling place of *Elohim* in the Spirit"** (v22) — Greek εἰς κατοικητήριον τοῦ θεοῦ ἐν πνεύματι; πνεύματι has no article: "in the Spirit" or "in spirit".

### Notes
- **v17** echoes Isa 57:19.
- **v1–7** — one sentence in Greek; the object "you" (v1) and "us" (v5) waits for the verb "made alive together" in v5.
- **v8–9** — "you have been saved" is a perfect (ἐστε σεσῳσμένοι), as in v5.
