# Ephesians 1

[1] ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, an apostle of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ through the will of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

to the saints who are in ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧ and faithful in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧:

[2] Grace to you and peace from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ our Father and the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[3] Blessed be the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and Father of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, who blessed us in every spiritual blessing in the heavenlies in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[4] just as he chose us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blemish before him in love,

[5] having predestined us unto adoption as sons through ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ unto him, according to the good pleasure of his will,

[6] unto the praise of the glory of his grace, with which he graced us in the Beloved,

[7] in whom we have the redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of the trespasses, according to the riches of his grace,

[8] which he made to abound toward us in all wisdom and prudence,

[9] having made known to us the mystery of his will, according to his good pleasure, which he purposed in him

[10] unto a stewardship of the fullness of the times, to sum up all things in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, the things upon the heavens and the things upon the earth; in him,

[11] in whom also we were allotted, having been predestined according to the purpose of the one who works all things according to the counsel of his will,

[12] that we should be unto the praise of his glory, we who have hoped beforehand in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧;

[13] in whom you also, having heard the word of the truth, the gospel of your salvation, in whom also having believed, you were sealed with the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ of the promise,

[14] which is a pledge of our inheritance, unto the redemption of the possession, unto the praise of his glory.

[15] For this reason I also, having heard of the faith among you in the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ and the love toward all the saints,

[16] do not cease giving thanks for you, making mention in my prayers,

[17] that the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, the Father of the glory, may give you a spirit of wisdom and revelation in the full knowledge of him,

[18] the eyes of your heart enlightened, that you may know what is the hope of his calling, what is the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints,

[19] and what is the exceeding greatness of his power toward us who believe, according to the working of the might of his strength,

[20] which he worked in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, having raised him from the dead and having seated him at his right hand in the heavenlies,

[21] far above all principality and authority and power and lordship and every name that is named, not only in this age but also in the one to come;

[22] and **he subjected all things under his feet, and gave him as head over all things to the church,**

**[23] which is his body, the fullness of the one who fills all things in all**.

## Changes

- **"Christ Jesus" / "Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*" / "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (throughout) — systematic; *Yeshua* = Hebrew/Aramaic name, *Mashiach* = "Anointed One."
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv1, 2, 3, 17) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"that holy Spirit of promise" → "the *Ruach HaQodesh* of the promise"** (v13) — Greek τῷ πνεύματι τῆς ἐπαγγελίας τῷ ἁγίῳ, "the Spirit of the promise, the Holy"; Hebrew רוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ *Ruach HaQodesh*, "Spirit of Holiness." A plain genitive, not "the promised Spirit".
- **"by the will of God" → "through the will of *Elohim*"** (v1) — Greek διὰ θελήματος θεοῦ, as Col 1:1.
- **"to the saints which are at Ephesus, and to the faithful" → "to the saints who are in Ephesos and faithful"** (v1) — Greek τοῖς ἁγίοις τοῖς οὖσιν [ἐν Ἐφέσῳ] καὶ πιστοῖς: one article, one group. SBLGNT prints "in Ephesos" in square brackets; WH, Treg, NA28 and RP all have the words, and WH notes their omission in its apparatus.
- **"who hath blessed us with all spiritual blessings in heavenly places" → "who blessed us in every spiritual blessing in the heavenlies"** (v3) — Greek ὁ εὐλογήσας, aorist; ἐν πάσῃ εὐλογίᾳ πνευματικῇ, singular, with ἐν three times in a row. ἐν τοῖς ἐπουρανίοις is an adjective used as a noun, "in the heavenly [places / things]"; the Greek names no noun (also v20; 2:6; 3:10; 6:12).
- **"without blame before him in love" → "without blemish before him in love"** (v4) — Greek ἀμώμους, as Col 1:22. The printed text puts the comma after "in love"; the words can also be read with v5 ("in love having predestined us").
- **"Having predestinated us unto the adoption of children by Jesus Christ to himself" → "having predestined us unto adoption as sons through *Yeshua* the *Mashiach* unto him"** (v5) — Greek προορίσας, a participle; υἱοθεσία is "placing as a son"; εἰς αὐτόν, "unto him", is printed as the plain pronoun (not the reflexive, as in 2:15).
- **"the good pleasure of his will" kept** (v5, 9) — Greek εὐδοκία, as 2 Thess 1:11; in Phil 1:15; 2:13 "good will". Not "purpose": that is πρόθεσις (v11).
- **"wherein he hath made us accepted" → "with which he graced us"** (v6) — Greek ἧς ἐχαρίτωσεν ἡμᾶς; the verb is from χάρις, "grace".
- **"To the praise of the glory of his grace" kept** (v6) — Greek εἰς ἔπαινον δόξης τῆς χάριτος αὐτοῦ, two nouns; not "glorious grace".
- **"In whom we have redemption … the forgiveness of sins" → "in whom we have the redemption … the forgiveness of the trespasses"** (v7) — Greek τὴν ἀπολύτρωσιν, with the article, as Col 1:14; παραπτωμάτων (2:1, 5), not "sins". The Greek has no "our". v3–14 are one sentence in Greek; the relative clauses are kept.
- **"Wherein he hath abounded toward us" → "which he made to abound toward us"** (v8) — Greek ἧς ἐπερίσσευσεν εἰς ἡμᾶς; "in all wisdom and prudence" can go with what precedes or with "having made known" (v9).
- **"which he hath purposed in himself" → "which he purposed in him"** (v9) — Greek ἣν προέθετο ἐν αὐτῷ: "which" is the good pleasure; "in him" is the *Mashiach* or *Elohim* himself. The Greek does not name the *Mashiach* here.
- **"That in the dispensation of the fulness of times" → "unto a stewardship of the fullness of the times"** (v10) — Greek εἰς οἰκονομίαν, the management of a household; "stewardship" as 3:2, 9 and Col 1:25. Not "a plan".
- **"he might gather together in one all things in Christ" → "to sum up all things in the *Mashiach*"** (v10) — Greek ἀνακεφαλαιώσασθαι, "to bring to a head, to sum up" (Rom 13:9); the root is κεφάλαιον, "main point", close to κεφαλή, "head" (v22).
- **"both which are in heaven, and which are on earth; even in him" → "the things upon the heavens and the things upon the earth; in him,"** (v10) — Greek ἐπί both times; the verse ends ἐν αὐτῷ, which v11 takes up ("in whom").
- **"In whom also we have obtained an inheritance" → "in whom also we were allotted"** (v11) — Greek ἐκληρώθημεν, passive of κληρόω, "to appoint or assign by lot" (κλῆρος, "lot", Col 1:12); only here in the New Testament. Either "we were assigned a lot" (received an inheritance) or "we were made a lot" (became his portion, Deut 32:9).
- **"who first trusted in Christ" → "we who have hoped beforehand in the *Mashiach*"** (v12) — Greek τοὺς προηλπικότας, perfect of προ-ελπίζω, "hope before".
- **"In whom ye also trusted, after that ye heard" → "in whom you also, having heard"** (v13) — the Greek has no "trusted"; the clause runs on to "you were sealed". "and believed in him" (modern versions) is not the Greek either: ἐν ᾧ καὶ πιστεύσαντες, "in whom also having believed".
- **"Which is the earnest of our inheritance" → "which is a pledge of our inheritance"** (v14) — Greek ὅ ἐστιν ἀρραβών: "which" is neuter, the grammatical gender of πνεῦμα. ἀρραβών is a first payment that binds the payer to pay the rest.
- **"until the redemption of the purchased possession" → "unto the redemption of the possession"** (v14) — Greek εἰς ἀπολύτρωσιν τῆς περιποιήσεως; περιποίησις is "an obtaining" (1 Thess 5:9; 2 Thess 2:14) or the thing obtained, a possession (1 Pet 2:9). The Greek has no "until we acquire".
- **"after I heard of your faith in the Lord Jesus, and love unto all the saints" → "having heard of the faith among you in the Lord *Yeshua* and the love toward all the saints"** (v15) — Greek τὴν καθʼ ὑμᾶς πίστιν, "the faith among you"; no "because".
- **"making mention of you in my prayers" → "making mention in my prayers"** (v16) — the printed text has no "of you"; μνείαν ποιούμενος as Phlm 4.
- **"the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him" → "a spirit of wisdom and revelation in the full knowledge of him"** (v17) — Greek πνεῦμα without the article: "a spirit" or "the Spirit"; ἐπίγνωσις, "full knowledge", as Col 1:9. "The Father of the glory": ὁ πατὴρ τῆς δόξης.
- **"The eyes of your understanding being enlightened" → "the eyes of your heart enlightened"** (v18) — Greek πεφωτισμένους τοὺς ὀφθαλμοὺς τῆς καρδίας ὑμῶν, an accusative: a second object of "may give" or a loose "having the eyes … enlightened". "Heart" is singular.
- **"the hope of his calling … the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints" kept** (v18) — Greek genitives ἡ ἐλπὶς τῆς κλήσεως αὐτοῦ, ὁ πλοῦτος τῆς δόξης τῆς κληρονομίας αὐτοῦ; not "the hope to which he has called you", not "glorious inheritance". ἐν τοῖς ἁγίοις is "in" or "among the saints".
- **"the exceeding greatness" kept; "according to the working of his mighty power" → "according to the working of the might of his strength"** (v19) — Greek ὑπερβάλλον (2:7; 3:19); ἐνέργεια "working", κράτος "might" (Col 1:11), ἰσχύς "strength" (2 Thess 1:9): three nouns beside δύναμις "power".
- **"Which he wrought in Christ, when he raised him" → "which he worked in the *Mashiach*, having raised him"** (v20) — Greek ἣν ἐνήργησεν, aorist (Treg NA28 RP), the verb of ἐνέργεια; SBLGNT and WH print the perfect ἐνήργηκεν, "has worked". Participles ἐγείρας, καθίσας.
- **"Far above all principality, and power, and might, and dominion" → "far above all principality and authority and power and lordship"** (v21) — Greek ἀρχή, ἐξουσία, δύναμις, κυριότης, as Col 1:16; 2:10.
- **"in this world" → "in this age"** (v21) — Greek αἰών.
- **"hath put all things under his feet" → "he subjected all things under his feet"** (v22) — Greek ὑπέταξεν, the verb of 5:21, 24.
- **"the fulness of him that filleth all in all" → "the fullness of the one who fills all things in all"** (v23) — Greek τοῦ τὰ πάντα ἐν πᾶσιν πληρουμένου; the participle is middle ("who fills") or passive ("who is being filled"); ἐν πᾶσιν is "in all (people)" or "in all things" (Col 3:11).

### Notes
- **v22** echoes Ps 8:6.
- **v3–14** — one sentence in Greek; the refrain "unto the praise of his glory" (v6, 12, 14) divides it.
