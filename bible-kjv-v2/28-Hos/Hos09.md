# Hosea 9

[1] Rejoice not, O ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧!
Exult not like the peoples;
for you have played the whore, forsaking your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.
You have loved a prostitute's wages
on all threshing floors.

[2] Threshing floor and wine vat shall not feed them,
and the new wine shall fail them.

[3] They shall not remain in the land of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
but ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ shall return to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
and they shall eat unclean food in ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧.

[4] They shall not pour drink offerings of wine to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and their sacrifices shall not please him.
It shall be like mourners' bread to them;
all who eat of it shall be defiled;
for their bread shall be for their hunger only;
it shall not come to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] What will you do on the day of the appointed festival,
and on the day of the feast of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?

[6] For behold, they are going away from destruction;
but ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ shall gather them;
⟦memphis|Mof|Memphis⟧ shall bury them.
Nettles shall possess their precious things of silver;
thorns shall be in their tents.

[7] The days of punishment have come;
the days of recompense have come;
⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall know it.
The prophet is a fool;
the man of the spirit is mad,
because of your great iniquity and great hatred.

[8] The prophet is the watchman of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ with my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
yet a fowler's snare is on all his ways,
and hatred in the house of his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[9] They have deeply corrupted themselves
as in the days of ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧:
he will remember their iniquity;
he will punish their sins.

[10] Like grapes in the wilderness,
I found ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.
Like the first fruit on the fig tree in its first season,
I saw your fathers.
But they came to ⟦baal-peor|Baal Peor|Baal-peor⟧
and consecrated themselves to the thing of shame,
and became detestable like the thing they loved.

[11] ⟦ephraim|Efrayim's|Ephraim's⟧ glory shall fly away like a bird —
no birth, no pregnancy, no conception!

[12] Even if they rear children,
I will bereave them till none is left.
Woe to them
when I depart from them!

[13] ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, as I have seen, was like a young palm planted in a meadow;
but ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ must lead his children out to slaughter.

[14] Give them, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ —
what will you give?
Give them a miscarrying womb
and dry breasts.

[15] Every evil of theirs is in Gilgal;
there I began to hate them.
Because of the wickedness of their deeds
I will drive them out of my house.
I will love them no more;
all their princes are rebels.

[16] ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ is stricken;
their root is dried up;
they shall bear no fruit.
Even though they give birth,
I will put their beloved children to death.

[17] My ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will reject them
because they have not listened to him;
they shall be wanderers among the nations.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv8, 17) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; Hebrew is poetry.
- **"for joy, as other people" → "Exult not like the peoples"** (v1) — Hebrew כַּעַמִּים *ka'ammim*; "like the peoples" captures the contrast between Israel and other nations at harvest festivals.
- **"gone a whoring from thy God" → "played the whore, forsaking your *Elohim*"** (v1) — consistent vocabulary; *Elohim* retained.
- **"reward upon every cornfloor" → "prostitute's wages on all threshing floors"** (v1) — Hebrew אֶתְנָן *etnan* = prostitute's fee; "wages" consistent with ch. 2; "threshing floors" were also sites of pagan fertility rites.
- **"the bread of mourners" → "mourners' bread"** (v4) — Hebrew לֶחֶם אֹנִים; "mourners' bread" = food eaten during mourning, ritually defiling (cf. Deut 26:14).
- **"the solemn day" → "the day of the appointed festival"** (v5) — Hebrew מוֹעֵד *mo'ed*; "appointed festival" more precise.
- **"their pleasant places for their silver" → "their precious things of silver"** (v6) — Hebrew מַחְמַד כַּסְפָּם; restructured.
- **"their tabernacles" → "their tents"** (v6) — Hebrew בְּאָהֳלֵיהֶם; "tents" is more literal; "tabernacles" implies cultic setting.
- **"the days of visitation" → "the days of punishment"** (v7) — Hebrew יְמֵי הַפְּקֻדָּה; *piqudah* = punishment/reckoning; "visitation" is opaque.
- **"from the birth, and from the womb, and from the conception" → "no birth, no pregnancy, no conception"** (v11) — restructured from reversal to positive items removed; same content, clearer modern English.
- **"Ephraim, as I saw Tyrus, is planted in a pleasant place" → "Ephraim, as I have seen, was like a young palm planted in a meadow"** (v13) — Hebrew טִירוֹס can be "Tyre" or "young palm/fortress"; "young palm" is an alternative reading (the plant metaphor fits the agricultural context better here).
- **"the murderer" → "to slaughter"** (v13) — "lead his children out to slaughter" more vivid.
- **"All their wickedness is in Gilgal" → "Every evil of theirs is in Gilgal"** (v15) — modernized.
- **"revolters" → "rebels"** (v15) — Hebrew סָרִים *sarim*; modernized.
- **"slay even the beloved fruit of their womb" → "put their beloved children to death"** (v16) — modernized.
