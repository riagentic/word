# Hosea 9

[1] Do not rejoice, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to joy like the peoples,
for you have played the prostitute from upon your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
you have loved a hire
upon all the threshing floors of grain.

[2] Threshing floor and winepress shall not feed them,
and new wine shall lie to her.

[3] They shall not dwell in the land of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ shall return to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
and in ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ they shall eat what is unclean.

[4] They shall not pour out wine to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and they shall not be pleasing to him;
their sacrifices are like the bread of mourning to them:
all who eat it shall be defiled;
for their bread is for their soul;
it shall not come into the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] What will you do for the day of appointed time,
and for the day of the feast of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?

[6] For behold, they have gone from havoc;
⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ shall gather them,
⟦memphis|Mof|Memphis⟧ shall bury them;
the desirable things of their silver,
nettles shall possess them;
thistle is in their tents.

[7] The days of the visitation have come,
the days of the recompense have come;
⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall know.
A fool is the prophet,
a madman is the man of the spirit,
because of the greatness of your iniquity,
and great is the hostility.

[8] A watchman is ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ with my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
the prophet — a fowler's trap is on all his ways,
hostility in the house of his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[9] They have gone deep, they have wrought ruin,
as in the days of ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧;
he will remember their iniquity,
he will visit their sins.

[10] Like grapes in the wilderness
I found ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
like a first-ripe fig on the fig tree at its beginning
I saw your fathers.
They, they came to ⟦baal-peor|Baal Peor|Baal-peor⟧
and kept themselves apart for the Shame,
and they became detestable things like their love.

[11] ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ — like birds their glory shall fly away:
from birth, and from the belly, and from conception.

[12] For even if they bring up their sons,
I will bereave them of man;
for woe also to them
when I turn aside from them!

[13] ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, as I have seen, is to ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, planted in a pasture;
and ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ is to bring out his sons to the killer.

[14] Give them, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ —
what will you give?
Give them a bereaving womb
and shriveling breasts.

[15] All their evil is in Gilgal,
for there I hated them;
because of the evil of their deeds
from my house I will drive them out;
I will not again love them;
all their princes are stubborn.

[16] ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ is struck;
their root is dried out;
fruit they shall not make;
even if they bear,
I will put to death the desirable ones of their belly.

[17] My ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will reject them,
for they have not listened to him;
and they shall be wanderers among the nations.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 8, 17) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **Layout** — poetry, one Hebrew line per printed line, as the accents divide them.
- **"Rejoice not, O Israel, for joy, as other people" → "Do not rejoice, Israel, to joy like the peoples"** (v1) — Hebrew אַל־תִּשְׂמַח … אֶל־גִּיל, literally "do not rejoice … to joy" (גִּיל noun, "joy" as the גִּילָה row); כָּעַמִּים "like the peoples" (no "other"); no "O" (the Hebrew has no word for it). **"for thou hast gone a whoring from thy God" → "for you have played the prostitute from upon your Elohim"** — זָנָה "play the prostitute" (its row); מֵעַל "from upon" (away from beside him). **"thou hast loved a reward upon every cornfloor" → "you have loved a hire upon all the threshing floors of grain"** — אֶתְנָן "hire" (its row: what a prostitute is given); כָּל־גָּרְנוֹת דָּגָן "all the threshing floors of grain" (גֹּרֶן · דָּגָן their rows).
- **"The floor and the winepress shall not feed them, and the new wine shall fail in her" → "Threshing floor and winepress shall not feed them, and new wine shall lie to her"** (v2) — no articles in the Hebrew; יֶקֶב "winepress" (its row); רָעָה "feed" (its row: of grazing); כִּחֵשׁ בְּ "lie to" (its row: here the wine that does not come, "fail"); בָּהּ "to her" (feminine: the land, or Israel as a woman; the versions read "them").
- **"They shall not dwell in the LORD's land; but Ephraim shall return to Egypt, and they shall eat unclean things in Assyria" → "They shall not dwell in the land of Yahweh, and Ephraim shall return to Egypt, and in Assyria they shall eat what is unclean"** (v3) — יָשַׁב "dwell" (its row); וְשָׁב "and … shall return" (no "but"); טָמֵא "unclean" (its row), singular, with Assyria first.
- **"They shall not offer wine offerings to the LORD, neither shall they be pleasing unto him: their sacrifices shall be unto them as the bread of mourners" → "They shall not pour out wine to Yahweh, and they shall not be pleasing to him; their sacrifices are like the bread of mourning to them"** (v4) — נָסַךְ "pour out" (a drink offering; the הִסִּיךְ row); עָרַב לְ "be pleasing to" (another sense than "be surety", the עָרַב row); the accents end the line at לוֹ, so זִבְחֵיהֶם "their sacrifices" opens the next clause, which has no verb ("are" supplied); אוֹנִים "mourning" (the אֹנִי row, as Deut 26:14). **"all that eat thereof shall be polluted: for their bread for their soul shall not come into the house of the LORD" → "all who eat it shall be defiled; for their bread is for their soul; it shall not come into the house of Yahweh"** — יִטַּמָּאוּ "be defiled" (the נִטְמָא row); לַחְמָם לְנַפְשָׁם "their bread is for their soul" (נֶפֶשׁ "soul", its row: = for their own appetite), the accents part it from "it shall not come".
- **"What will ye do in the solemn day" → "What will you do for the day of appointed time"** (v5) — לְיוֹם "for the day"; מוֹעֵד "appointed time" (its row); חַג "feast" (its row).
- **"For, lo, they are gone because of destruction" → "For behold, they have gone from havoc"** (v6) — הִנֵּה "behold"; שֹׁד "havoc" (its row); מִן "from". **"Egypt shall gather them up, Memphis shall bury them"** — קִבֵּץ "gather"; מֹף "Memphis" (token). **"the pleasant places for their silver, nettles shall possess them: thorns shall be in their tabernacles" → "the desirable things of their silver, nettles shall possess them; thistle is in their tents"** — מַחְמַד לְכַסְפָּם, literally "a desirable thing for their silver" (what they bought, or kept, with silver); קִמּוֹשׂ "nettles" (kind uncertain); יָרַשׁ "possess" (its row); חוֹחַ "thistle" (its row), singular, no verb; אֹהֶל "tent".
- **"The days of visitation are come, the days of recompence are come; Israel shall know it" → "The days of the visitation have come, the days of the recompense have come; Israel shall know"** (v7) — פְּקֻדָּה "visitation" (its row) and שִׁלֻּם "recompense", both with the article; יֵדְעוּ "they shall know" (plural verb with Israel), no "it". **"the prophet is a fool, the spiritual man is mad, for the multitude of thine iniquity, and the great hatred" → "A fool is the prophet, a madman is the man of the spirit, because of the greatness of your iniquity, and great is the hostility"** — אֱוִיל "fool"; מְשֻׁגָּע "madman" (its row); אִישׁ הָרוּחַ "the man of the spirit" (רוּחַ its row); whether the people say this of the prophet or the prophet is mad by their sin is not said; מַשְׂטֵמָה "hostility" (noun of שָׂטַם "bear a grudge", its row), וְרַבָּה "and great (is)".
- **"The watchman of Ephraim was with my God: but the prophet is a snare of a fowler in all his ways, and hatred in the house of his God" → "A watchman is Ephraim with my Elohim; the prophet — a fowler's trap is on all his ways, hostility in the house of his Elohim"** (v8) — Hebrew צֹפֶה אֶפְרַיִם עִם־אֱלֹהָי נָבִיא: no verb; the accents put the break after "my Elohim", so נָבִיא "the prophet" opens the next line. Read as "Ephraim is a watchman with my Elohim" (or "Ephraim watches beside my Elohim", or "The watchman of Ephraim, with my Elohim, is the prophet": all three are possible; uncertain). צֹפֶה "watchman" (its row); פַּח יָקוֹשׁ "a fowler's trap" (פַּח its row); מַשְׂטֵמָה "hostility" (as v7); no "but".
- **"They have deeply corrupted themselves, as in the days of Gibeah: therefore he will remember their iniquity" → "They have gone deep, they have wrought ruin, as in the days of Gibeah; he will remember their iniquity"** (v9) — Hebrew הֶעְמִיקוּ־שִׁחֵתוּ, two verbs side by side ("they made deep, they ruined" = they ruined deeply); שִׁחֵת "work ruin" (its row); הַגִּבְעָה with the article (the town, Judg 19–21); no "therefore"; פָּקַד "visit" (its row).
- **"I found Israel like grapes in the wilderness; I saw your fathers as the firstripe in the fig tree at her first time" → "Like grapes in the wilderness I found Israel; like a first-ripe fig on the fig tree at its beginning I saw your fathers"** (v10) — the Hebrew order kept (the likeness first); בִּכּוּרָה "first-ripe fig"; בְּרֵאשִׁיתָהּ "at its beginning" (the fig tree's first season). **"but they went to Baalpeor, and separated themselves unto that shame" → "They, they came to Baal-peor and kept themselves apart for the Shame"** — הֵמָּה בָּאוּ, the pronoun stressed, "came" (בּוֹא), no "but"; נָזַר nifal לְ "keep oneself apart for" (as the נָזִיר rows: the word of the Nazirite's vow); הַבֹּשֶׁת "the Shame", a name put for Baal (as in names like Ish-bosheth for Ish-baal). **"and their abominations were according as they loved" → "and they became detestable things like their love"** — וַיִּהְיוּ שִׁקּוּצִים, "and they became detestable things" (שִׁקּוּץ its row); כְּאָהֳבָם "like their love" (the thing they loved, or their loving).
- **"As for Ephraim, their glory shall fly away like a bird, from the birth, and from the womb, and from the conception" → "Ephraim — like birds their glory shall fly away: from birth, and from the belly, and from conception"** (v11) — אֶפְרַיִם stands first and alone; עוֹף "birds" (its row: a collective); the three מִן "from" kept: there shall be no birth, no time in the belly, no conception (the order goes back from birth to conception); בֶּטֶן "belly" (its row).
- **"Though they bring up their children, yet will I bereave them, that there shall not be a man left" → "For even if they bring up their sons, I will bereave them of man"** (v12) — כִּי אִם "for even if" (here not after a negative); גִּדֵּל of children "bring up" (lit. "make great": the גִּדֵּל row is of a man made great); בְּנֵיהֶם "their sons" (its row); שִׁכֵּל "bereave" (its row); מֵאָדָם, literally "from man" (so that no man is left). **"yea, woe also to them when I depart from them!" → "for woe also to them when I turn aside from them!"** — כִּי־גַם־אוֹי "for also woe" (אוֹי its row); בְּשׂוּרִי is written with שׂ: taken as סוּר "turn aside" (uncertain).
- **"Ephraim, as I saw Tyrus, is planted in a pleasant place" → "Ephraim, as I have seen, is to Tyre, planted in a pasture"** (v13) — Hebrew אֶפְרַיִם כַּאֲשֶׁר־רָאִיתִי לְצוֹר שְׁתוּלָה בְנָוֶה, no verb: "Ephraim, as I have seen, to Tyre, planted in a pasture" (the line is obscure; לְצוֹר "to / for / like Tyre", or read as "for a palm" by some; the versions have "for a prey": from memory). נָוֶה "pasture" (the נָוֶה "habitation" row: here of planting, the pasture-land; said). The token for Tyre kept. **"but Ephraim shall bring forth his children to the murderer" → "and Ephraim is to bring out his sons to the killer"** — לְהוֹצִיא, an infinitive: "is to bring out" (הוֹצִיא "bring out", its row); הֹרֵג "killer" (participle of הָרַג "kill", its row); no "but".
- **"Give them, O LORD: what wilt thou give? give them a miscarrying womb and dry breasts" → "Give them, Yahweh — what will you give? Give them a bereaving womb and shriveling breasts"** (v14) — no "O"; רֶחֶם מַשְׁכִּיל "a bereaving womb" (hifil of שָׁכַל, the verb of v12: a womb that loses its children, miscarries); שָׁדַיִם צֹמְקִים "shriveling breasts" (participle).
- **"All their wickedness is in Gilgal: for there I hated them" → "All their evil is in Gilgal, for there I hated them"** (v15) — רָעָה "evil" (its row); שְׂנֵאתִים perfect "I hated them". **"for the wickedness of their doings I will drive them out of mine house, I will love them no more" → "because of the evil of their deeds from my house I will drive them out; I will not again love them"** — רֹעַ "evil" (as רָעָה); מַעֲלָלִים "deeds"; גֵּרֵשׁ "drive out" (its row); לֹא אוֹסֵף אַהֲבָתָם, literally "I will not add to love them" (the יָסַף row). **"all their princes are revolters" → "all their princes are stubborn"** — שָׂרֵיהֶם סֹרְרִים, a play of sound (*sarehem sorerim*); סוֹרֵר "stubborn" (its row).
- **"Ephraim is smitten, their root is dried up, they shall bear no fruit" → "Ephraim is struck; their root is dried out; fruit they shall not make"** (v16) — הֻכָּה "is struck" (הִכָּה its row); יָבֵשׁ "be dried out" (its row); the written text has בלי "without", the qere בַל "not": the qere translated (the sense is the same); פְּרִי … יַעֲשׂוּן "fruit … make" (the עָשָׂה פְּרִי row), the fruit first; פְּרִי sounds like אֶפְרַיִם. **"yea, though they bring forth, yet will I slay even the beloved fruit of their womb" → "even if they bear, I will put to death the desirable ones of their belly"** — גַּם כִּי "even if"; הֵמִית "put to death" (its row); מַחֲמַדֵּי בִטְנָם "the desirable ones of their belly" (as מַחְמַד v6; בֶּטֶן "belly", its row).
- **"My God will cast them away, because they did not hearken unto him" → "My Elohim will reject them, for they have not listened to him"** (v17) — מָאַס "reject"; שָׁמַע לְ "listen to" (the שָׁמַע אֶל row). **"wanderers among the nations"** — נֹדְדִים, of נָדַד "wander" (the Isa 21–24 row has "flee" for its sense of flight; here those with no place: said).

### Notes
- **Shame** (v10) — הַבֹּשֶׁת stands for the Baal of Peor (Num 25:1–9); Jer 3:24; 11:13 use the word the same way.
- **Gibeah** (v9; 10:9) — the crime of Judg 19–21.
