## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*" / "El"** (v7; v10) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים (v7) and אֵל (v10, "sons of the living El").
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept (v3–11).
- **"Hezekiah"** (v1) — the Hebrew has no "and" before the last king.
- **"The beginning of the word of the LORD by Hosea" → "The beginning of the speaking of Yahweh with Hoshea"** (v2) — Hebrew תְּחִלַּת דִּבֶּר־יְהוָה בְּהוֹשֵׁעַ, "the beginning of — Yahweh spoke"; דִּבֶּר בְּ of Yahweh "speak with" (also "through, in"). **"take unto thee a wife of whoredoms" → "take yourself a woman of prostitution"** — קַח־לְךָ, imperative with לְךָ (as "get yourself"); אֵשֶׁת זְנוּנִים: אִשָּׁה is "woman" and "wife" (one word); זְנוּנִים "prostitution" (plural noun, rendered singular), the same word for unfaithfulness to Yahweh; "whoredom" is not used. **"the land hath committed great whoredom, departing from the LORD" → "the land surely plays the prostitute from after Yahweh"** — infinitive absolute זָנֹה תִזְנֶה, literally "playing the prostitute it plays the prostitute"; מֵאַחֲרֵי יְהוָה, literally "from after Yahweh" (= away from following him); "departing" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"So he went"** (v3) — וַיֵּלֶךְ "And he went". **Gomer, Diblaim** — the names kept; גֹּמֶר may mean "completion", דִּבְלַיִם "two fig cakes" (both uncertain).
- **"yet a little while"** (v4) — כִּי־עוֹד מְעַט. **"I will avenge the blood of Jezreel upon the house of Jehu" → "I will visit the bloodguilt of Jezreel upon the house of Jehu"** — Hebrew פָּקַד … עַל "visit … upon"; דְּמֵי, literally "bloods" (blood shed, and its guilt: 2 Kgs 9–10). **"will cause to cease the kingdom" → "will make the kingdom … cease"** — הִשְׁבַּתִּי, hifil of שָׁבַת "cease". **Jezreel** — יִזְרְעֶאל "El sows"; the place of Jehu's bloodshed (2 Kgs 9–10); the name returns in 2:22.
- **"And it shall come to pass at that day, that" → "And it shall be in that day that"** (v5) — וְהָיָה בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא, the וְ before "I will break" taken as "that". **"valley"** — עֵמֶק.
- **"And God said unto him" → "And he said to him"** (v6) — the Hebrew has no subject (also v9). **Lo-Ruchamah** — לֹא רֻחָמָה, "she has not been shown compassion" (pual of רִחַם). **"I will no more have mercy" → "I will no more again have compassion"** — לֹא אוֹסִיף עוֹד אֲרַחֵם, literally "I will not add again to have compassion"; רִחַם "have compassion" (root of רַחֲמִים, Hos 2:19). **"but I will utterly take them away" → "that I should surely forgive them"** — Hebrew כִּי־נָשֹׂא אֶשָּׂא לָהֶם, literally "for lifting up I will lift up for them"; נָשָׂא לְ "forgive" (as Gen 18:24); here taken under the negative ("no more … that I should forgive them"). Uncertain: the KJV reads נָשָׂא as "take away"; others read "for I will surely lift up (= punish?)" or emend; the MT kept.
- **"But I will have mercy upon the house of Judah" → "And on the house of Judah I will have compassion"** (v7) — the object first, as the Hebrew. **"and will not save them by bow" → "and I will not save them by bow and by sword and by war, by horses and by horsemen"** — the "and"s as the Hebrew has them (none before "by horses"); פָּרָשִׁים "horsemen".
- **"Now when she had weaned" → "And she weaned"** (v8) — וַתִּגְמֹל, a narrative past.
- **"Then said God" → "And he said"** (v9) — no subject in the Hebrew. **Lo-Ammi** — לֹא עַמִּי "not my people". **"I will not be your God" → "I am not to you"** — Hebrew וְאָנֹכִי לֹא־אֶהְיֶה לָכֶם, literally "and I, I will not be to you"; אֶהְיֶה is the "I will be" of Exod 3:14; "God" is not in the Hebrew (supplied from 2:23).
- **"Yet the number of the children of Israel" → "And the number of the sons of Israel"** (v10) — וְהָיָה, "and it shall be"; בְּנֵי "sons" (also v11). **"which cannot be measured nor numbered" → "which cannot be measured and cannot be numbered"** — two negated verbs. **"it was said"** — יֵאָמֵר, an imperfect (also "it is said"); the second יֵאָמֵר "it shall be said". **"Ye are the sons of the living God" → "Sons of the living El"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי אֵל־חָי, no "You are" (supplied by the KJV); "the" is supplied for English (no article).
- **"appoint themselves one head" → "set for themselves one head"** (v11) — וְשָׂמוּ לָהֶם. **"come up out of the land" → "go up from the land"** — וְעָלוּ מִן־הָאָרֶץ. **"great shall be the day" → "great is the day"** — a nominal clause, no verb.

### Notes
- **Numbering** — our 1:10–11 = Hebrew 2:1–2.
- **Sign-children**: Jezreel = "El sows"; Lo-Ruchamah = "she is not shown compassion"; Lo-Ammi = "not my people"; reversed in 1:10–2:1 and 2:23.
- **v10** — quoted Rom 9:26 (the Greek).
