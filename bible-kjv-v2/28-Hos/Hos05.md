# Hosea 5

[1] Hear this, O priests!
Pay attention, O house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧!
Give ear, O house of the king!
For the judgment is for you;
for you have been a snare at ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧
and a net spread upon ⟦tabor|Tavor|Tabor⟧.

[2] And they have made deep the slaughter of revolters,
but I will discipline all of them.

[3] I know ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧,
and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ is not hidden from me;
for now, O ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, you have played the whore;
⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ is defiled.

[4] Their deeds do not permit them
to return to their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.
For the spirit of whoredom is within them,
and they know not ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] The pride of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ testifies to his face;
⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ shall stumble in his guilt;
⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ also shall stumble with them.

[6] With their flocks and herds they shall go
to seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
but they will not find him;
he has withdrawn from them.

[7] They have dealt faithlessly with ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
for they have borne alien children.
Now the new moon shall devour them with their fields.

[8] Blow the ⟦shofar|shofar|horn⟧ in ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧,
the trumpet in Ramah.
Sound the alarm at ⟦beth-aven|Beit Aven|Beth-aven⟧;
we follow you, O ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧!

[9] ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ shall become a desolation
in the day of punishment;
among the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧
I make known what is sure.

[10] The princes of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ have become
like those who move the landmark;
upon them I will pour out
my wrath like water.

[11] ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ is oppressed, crushed in judgment,
because he was determined to go after filth.

[12] But I am like a moth to ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧,
and like rottenness to the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[13] When ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ saw his sickness,
and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ his wound,
then ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ went to ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧,
and sent to the great king.
But he is not able to cure you
or heal your wound.

[14] For I will be like a lion to ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧,
and like a young lion to the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.
I, even I, will tear and go away;
I will carry off, and no one shall rescue.

[15] I will return again to my place,
until they acknowledge their guilt and seek my face,
and in their distress earnestly seek me.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; Hebrew is poetry throughout.
- **"hearken" → "pay attention"** (v1) — modernized.
- **"judgment is toward you" → "the judgment is for you"** (v1) — modernized phrasing.
- **"the revolters are profound to make slaughter" → "they have made deep the slaughter of revolters"** (v2) — the KJV rendering is very opaque; Hebrew שַׁחֲטָה הֶעְמִיקוּ; "deepened the slaughter" = dug deep pits for victims.
- **"rebuker of them all" → "I will discipline all of them"** (v2) — Hebrew *musar* = correction/discipline.
- **"shall fall in their iniquity" → "shall stumble in his guilt"** (v5) — singular "his" matches MT.
- **"dealt treacherously" → "dealt faithlessly"** (v7) — Hebrew בָּגַד *bagad*; "faithlessly" better captures covenant betrayal.
- **"cornet" → "horn"** (v8) — Hebrew שׁוֹפָר *shofar*; "horn" is more accurate; "cornet" is anachronistic.
- **"the great king" — kept** (v13) — Hebrew מֶלֶךְ יָרֵב *melek yarev*; disputed — either "King Jareb" (proper name) or "the great/contentious king" (epithet for Assyria); rendered as "the great king."
- **"acknowledge their offence" → "acknowledge their guilt"** (v15) — Hebrew יֶאְשְׁמוּ *ye'shemu* = "become guilty/acknowledge guilt."
- **"seek me early" → "earnestly seek me"** (v15) — Hebrew יְשַׁחֲרֻנְנִי *yeshacharuneni*; "early" is not temporal but intensive (diligently/earnestly).
