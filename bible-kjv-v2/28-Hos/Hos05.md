## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵיהֶם.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept (v1–5, 12, 13).
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order.
- **"O priests … O house of Israel … O house of the king" → "priests … house of Israel … house of the king"** (v1) — no "O" (no word for it). **"hearken" → "give heed"** — הַקְשִׁיבוּ; הַאֲזִינוּ "give ear". **"for judgment is toward you" → "For to you is the judgment"** — כִּי לָכֶם הַמִּשְׁפָּט, the article kept (the judgment that is coming, or the judgment that was in their charge: the Hebrew allows both). **"a snare on Mizpah" → "a trap at Mizpah"** — פַּח, the fowler's trap; רֶשֶׁת "net".
- **"And the revolters are profound to make slaughter, though I have been a rebuker of them all" → "And slaughter the revolters have made deep, and I am discipline to them all"** (v2) — Hebrew וְשַׁחֲטָה שֵׂטִים הֶעְמִיקוּ, as printed: שַׁחֲטָה "slaughter" (found only here, from שָׁחַט), שֵׂטִים "those who turn aside" (Ps 101:3). The line is hard; a common emendation reads "and the pit of Shittim they have made deep" (שַׁחַת הַשִּׁטִּים, beside the trap and net of v1); not followed. **"rebuker" → "discipline"** — וַאֲנִי מוּסָר, a noun: "and I am discipline" (no verb, no "though").
- **"I know Ephraim" → "I, I know Ephraim"** (v3) — אֲנִי יָדַעְתִּי, the pronoun stands out. **"thou committest whoredom" → "you have played the prostitute"** — הִזְנֵיתָ, a perfect of the hifil (as 4:10, 18); no "O". **"defiled"** — נִטְמָא.
- **"They will not frame their doings to turn" → "Their doings do not let them return"** (v4) — Hebrew לֹא יִתְּנוּ מַעַלְלֵיהֶם לָשׁוּב, "their doings do not give (them) to return"; the KJV takes "they" as the subject and "their doings" as the object: the Hebrew allows both. **"the spirit of whoredoms is in the midst of them" → "a spirit of prostitution is in their midst"** — רוּחַ זְנוּנִים, no article (as 4:12). **"they have not known the LORD" → "Yahweh they do not know"** — the Hebrew order.
- **"the pride of Israel doth testify to his face" → "the pride of Israel answers against his face"** (v5) — וְעָנָה … בְּפָנָיו, עָנָה בְּ "answer against" (= testify against, as Exod 20:16). **"shall Israel and Ephraim fall in their iniquity; Judah also shall fall with them" → "Israel and Ephraim shall stumble in their iniquity; Judah also has stumbled with them"** — כָּשַׁל "stumble" (as 4:5); בַּעֲוֺנָם "their iniquity"; the last verb is a perfect.
- **"with their flocks and with their herds" → "with their flock and with their herd"** (v6) — צֹאן, בָּקָר, collectives. **"but they shall not find him" → "and they shall not find"** — no object. **"he hath withdrawn himself" → "he has drawn off"** — חָלַץ, the verb of pulling off a sandal (Deut 25:9): he has pulled himself away.
- **"They have dealt treacherously against the LORD" → "With Yahweh they have dealt treacherously"** (v7) — בַּיהוָה בָּגָדוּ, the Hebrew order. **"strange children" → "strange sons"** — בָּנִים זָרִים. **"begotten"** — יָלָדוּ. **"now shall a month devour them"** — חֹדֶשׁ "month" (or "new moon", the feast of 2:11); חֶלְקֵיהֶם "their portions" (of land).
- **"Blow ye the cornet in Gibeah, and the trumpet in Ramah" → "Blow a shofar in Gibeah, a trumpet in Ramah"** (v8) — שׁוֹפָר (token) and חֲצֹצְרָה "trumpet" (the straight metal trumpet), no articles, no "and". **"cry aloud at Bethaven" → "sound a blast at Beth-aven"** — הָרִיעוּ, the blast of the instruments named. **"after thee, O Benjamin" → "after you, Benjamin!"** — אַחֲרֶיךָ בִּנְיָמִין, word for word (= behind you!, or "we are after you"); no "O".
- **"shall be desolate" → "shall become a thing that appalls"** (v9) — לְשַׁמָּה תִהְיֶה (שַׁמָּה, root of שָׁמֵם). **"in the day of rebuke" → "in the day of reproof"** — תּוֹכֵחָה (as 2 Kgs 19:3). **"have I made known that which shall surely be" → "I have made known what is sure"** — נֶאֱמָנָה, niphal of אָמַן "be firm".
- **"were like them that remove the bound" → "have become like those who move back the border"** (v10) — Hebrew הָיוּ … כְּמַסִּיגֵי גְּבוּל; no "landmark": no stone is named (Deut 19:14). **"I will pour out my wrath upon them like water" → "upon them I will pour out like water my raging"** — the Hebrew order; עֶבְרָה "raging" (beside אַף "anger", חֵמָה "fury").
- **"Ephraim is oppressed and broken in judgment" → "Ephraim is extorted from, crushed in judgment"** (v11) — עָשׁוּק, רְצוּץ מִשְׁפָּט (passives of עָשַׁק "extort", רָצַץ "crush"); "crushed in judgment" is literally "crushed of judgment" (crushed by the judgment, or one whose right is crushed). **"because he willingly walked after the commandment" → "for he undertook it: he walked after a command"** — Hebrew כִּי הוֹאִיל הָלַךְ אַחֲרֵי־צָו, two verbs side by side (הוֹאִיל "undertake, be willing"); צַו "command" (Isa 28:10, 13; meaning here uncertain: a human command, or a mocking word); the versions read "vanity" (שָׁוְא, from memory) and some emend to "filth" (צֹאָה); not followed.
- **"Therefore will I be unto Ephraim as a moth" → "And I am like the moth to Ephraim"** (v12) — וַאֲנִי כָעָשׁ, no verb; עָשׁ "moth" (some take "pus"; uncertain); רָקָב "rottenness".
- **"When Ephraim saw his sickness, and Judah saw his wound, then went Ephraim" → "And Ephraim saw his sickness, and Judah his wound; and Ephraim went"** (v13) — וַיַּרְא … וַיֵּלֶךְ, narrative pasts; the second "saw" is not repeated. **"to king Jareb" → "to King Jareb"** — מֶלֶךְ יָרֵב, as printed; יָרֵב is "he contends" (from רִיב): "a king who contends" (cf. 4:4); others divide the letters as מַלְכִּי רָב "the great king" (Assyrian *šarru rabû*); the meaning is not certain. **"yet could he not heal you, nor cure you of your wound" → "and he is not able to heal you, and the wound shall not go away from you"** — וְהוּא לֹא יוּכַל לִרְפֹּא לָכֶם וְלֹא־יִגְהֶה מִכֶּם מָזוֹר; גָּהָה "go away" (of a sickness: be healed), מָזוֹר "wound" the subject.
- **"For I will be unto Ephraim as a lion" → "For I am like the lion to Ephraim"** (v14) — no verb; שַׁחַל "lion" (another word than אֲרִי; English has one word), כְּפִיר "young lion". **"I, even I, will tear and go away" → "I, I will tear to pieces and go"** — אֲנִי אֲנִי אֶטְרֹף וְאֵלֵךְ; טָרַף "tear to pieces". **"I will take away, and none shall rescue him" → "I will carry off, and there is none who delivers"** — אֶשָּׂא וְאֵין מַצִּיל.
- **"I will go and return to my place" → "I will go, I will return to my place"** (v15) — אֵלֵךְ אָשׁוּבָה, two verbs without "and" (the second a cohortative). **"till they acknowledge their offence" → "until they bear guilt"** — יֶאְשְׁמוּ, the verb אָשֵׁם "be guilty" (as 4:15): "be held guilty", "feel guilt", "acknowledge guilt" are all read; "bear" is supplied. **"in their affliction they will seek me early" → "in their distress they will seek me eagerly"** — בַּצַּר לָהֶם, literally "in the distress to them"; שִׁחֵר "seek eagerly" (root of שַׁחַר "dawn": "early" in the KJV).
