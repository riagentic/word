# Hosea 7

[1] When I heal ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
then the iniquity of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ is revealed,
and the evils of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧;
for they have worked falsehood,
and a thief comes in,
a raiding band makes a raid outside.

[2] And they do not say to their heart
that all their evil I remember;
now their doings have surrounded them;
they are before my face.

[3] With their evil they make the king glad,
and with their lies, princes.

[4] All of them are adulterers,
like an oven
burning from the baker;
he ceases from stirring,
from the kneading of the dough until it is leavened.

[5] The day of our king:
the princes have become sick with the heat from wine;
he has drawn out his hand with scoffers.

[6] For they have brought their heart near like the oven in their lying in ambush;
all the night their baker sleeps;
in the morning it burns like a fire of flame.

[7] All of them grow hot like the oven,
and they devour their judges;
all their kings have fallen;
there is none among them who calls to me.

[8] ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, among the peoples he mixes himself;
⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ has become a cake not turned.

[9] Strangers have eaten his power,
and he does not know;
also gray hair is tossed upon him,
and he does not know.

[10] And the pride of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ answers against his face,
and they have not returned to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and they have not sought him in all this.

[11] And ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ has become like a dove, simple, without heart;
⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ they have called, ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ they have gone to.

[12] As they go, I will spread over them my net;
like the birds of the heavens I will bring them down;
I will discipline them according to the report to their congregation.

[13] Woe to them, for they have fled from me!
Havoc to them, for they have transgressed against me!
And I, I would ransom them,
and they have spoken lies against me.

[14] And they have not cried out to me with their heart,
for they wail upon their beds;
for grain and new wine they gather themselves;
they turn aside against me.

[15] And I, I disciplined, I made their arms strong,
and against me they devise evil.

[16] They return, not upward;
they have become like a bow of treachery;
their princes shall fall by the sword because of the indignation of their tongue;
this is their derision in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v10) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵיהֶם.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order (v4, 5, 16 set by the accents).
- **"When I would have healed Israel" → "When I heal Israel"** (v1) — כְּרָפְאִי, an infinitive ("as I heal"); 6:11b "when I restore the fortunes" stands just before it. **"was discovered" → "is revealed"** — וְנִגְלָה. **"the wickedness of Samaria" → "the evils of Samaria"** — רָעוֹת, plural of רָעָה "evil". **"they commit falsehood" → "they have worked falsehood"** — פָעֲלוּ שָׁקֶר. **"the thief cometh in, and the troop of robbers spoileth without" → "a thief comes in, a raiding band makes a raid outside"** — וְגַנָּב יָבוֹא פָּשַׁט גְּדוּד בַּחוּץ, no articles; פָּשַׁט "strip, make a raid".
- **"they consider not in their hearts" → "they do not say to their heart"** (v2) — וּבַל־יֹאמְרוּ לִלְבָבָם, kept (= they do not think). **"that I remember all their wickedness" → "that all their evil I remember"** — the Hebrew order. **"their own doings have beset them about" → "their doings have surrounded them"** — סְבָבוּם. **"before my face"** — נֶגֶד פָּנַי.
- **"They make the king glad with their wickedness, and the princes with their lies" → "With their evil they make the king glad, and with their lies, princes"** (v3) — the Hebrew order; שָׂרִים without the article; כַּחַשׁ "lie".
- **"They are all adulterers, as an oven heated by the baker, who ceaseth from raising after he hath kneaded the dough, until it be leavened" → "All of them are adulterers, like an oven burning from the baker; he ceases from stirring, from the kneading of the dough until it is leavened"** (v4) — Hebrew כֻּלָּם מְנָאֲפִים כְּמוֹ תַנּוּר בֹּעֵרָה מֵאֹפֶה יִשְׁבּוֹת מֵעִיר מִלּוּשׁ בָּצֵק עַד־חֻמְצָתוֹ, as printed: בֹּעֵרָה "burning" is feminine beside the masculine תַּנּוּר (a mismatch in the text); מֵאֹפֶה "from the baker" (= kindled by him); מֵעִיר "from stirring" (hifil of עוּר, of the fire; the same letters read "from the city"). The line is hard (v4–6); emendations ("their anger" for "their baker" in v6) are not followed.
- **"In the day of our king the princes have made him sick with bottles of wine" → "The day of our king: the princes have become sick with the heat from wine"** (v5) — Hebrew יוֹם מַלְכֵּנוּ, no "in"; הֶחֱלוּ שָׂרִים חֲמַת מִיָּיִן: הֶחֱלוּ, hifil of חָלָה "be sick", without an object ("made him sick" supplies "him"; others derive it from חָלַל "began"); חֵמָה "heat" (also "fury", "poison"), "from wine". **"he stretched out his hand with scorners" → "he has drawn out his hand with scoffers"** — מָשַׁךְ יָדוֹ אֶת־לֹצְצִים (the king joins the scoffers).
- **"For they have made ready their heart like an oven, whiles they lie in wait" → "For they have brought their heart near like the oven in their lying in ambush"** (v6) — כִּי־קֵרְבוּ כַתַּנּוּר לִבָּם בְּאָרְבָּם; קֵרַב "bring near", אָרַב "lie in ambush". **"their baker sleepeth all the night" → "all the night their baker sleeps"** — יָשֵׁן אֹפֵהֶם, as printed (the emendation אַפְּהֶם "their anger" is not followed). **"it burneth as a flaming fire" → "it burns like a fire of flame"** — בֹּקֶר הוּא בֹעֵר כְּאֵשׁ לֶהָבָה, "morning — it burns".
- **"They are all hot as an oven" → "All of them grow hot like the oven"** (v7) — יֵחַמּוּ. **"have devoured their judges" → "they devour their judges"** — וְאָכְלוּ, "and". **"there is none among them that calleth unto me"** — אֵין־קֹרֵא בָהֶם אֵלָי.
- **"Ephraim, he hath mixed himself among the people" → "Ephraim, among the peoples he mixes himself"** (v8) — בָּעַמִּים, plural; יִתְבּוֹלָל, from בָּלַל "mix". **"Ephraim is a cake not turned" → "has become a cake not turned"** — הָיָה; עֻגָה בְּלִי הֲפוּכָה.
- **"devoured his strength" → "have eaten his power"** (v9) — אָכְלוּ … כֹּחוֹ. **"he knoweth it not" → "he does not know"** — no object (twice). **"gray hairs are here and there upon him" → "gray hair is tossed upon him"** — שֵׂיבָה זָרְקָה בּוֹ, a singular; זָרַק of a dry thing "toss", here an active form with שֵׂיבָה as subject.
- **"And the pride of Israel testifieth to his face" → "answers against his face"** (v10) — as 5:5. **"and they do not return … nor seek him for all this" → "and they have not returned … and they have not sought him in all this"** — perfects; בְּכָל־זֹאת "in all this".
- **"Ephraim also is like a silly dove without heart" → "And Ephraim has become like a dove, simple, without heart"** (v11) — וַיְהִי; פוֹתָה "simple" (easily enticed, root of פִּתָּה "entice"); אֵין לֵב "there is no heart" (= no sense). **"they call to Egypt, they go to Assyria" → "Egypt they have called, Assyria they have gone to"** — perfects, the names first, no "to" before Egypt.
- **"When they shall go, I will spread my net upon them" → "As they go, I will spread over them my net"** (v12) — כַּאֲשֶׁר יֵלֵכוּ, the Hebrew order. **"as the fowls of the heaven" → "like the birds of the heavens"** — כְּעוֹף הַשָּׁמַיִם. **"I will chastise them, as their congregation hath heard" → "I will discipline them according to the report to their congregation"** — Hebrew אַיְסִרֵם (an unusual spelling of the hifil or piel of יָסַר) כְּשֵׁמַע לַעֲדָתָם, literally "as the hearing to their congregation" (what was announced to them; uncertain; emendations are not followed).
- **"Woe unto them! for they have fled from me" → "Woe to them, for they have fled from me!"** (v13) — אוֹי, beside הוֹי "Woe" (one English word); נָדַד "flee". **"destruction unto them! because they have transgressed against me" → "Havoc to them, for they have transgressed against me!"** — שֹׁד "havoc"; פָּשַׁע בְּ. **"though I have redeemed them" → "And I, I would ransom them"** — וְאָנֹכִי אֶפְדֵּם, an imperfect ("I would / I will"); פָּדָה "ransom". **"yet they have spoken lies against me" → "and they have spoken lies against me"** — וְהֵמָּה, "and they"; כְּזָבִים "lies".
- **"And they have not cried unto me with their heart, when they howled upon their beds" → "And they have not cried out to me with their heart, for they wail upon their beds"** (v14) — כִּי "for" (or "but"); יְיֵלִילוּ "they wail" (imperfect). **"they assemble themselves for corn and wine" → "for grain and new wine they gather themselves"** — יִתְגּוֹרָרוּ as printed (from גּוּר, "gather themselves", "drag themselves"; uncertain); some manuscripts and the versions read יִתְגּוֹדָדוּ "they gash themselves" (1 Kgs 18:28; from memory): not followed. **"they rebel against me" → "they turn aside against me"** — יָסוּרוּ בִי (סוּר "turn aside").
- **"Though I have bound and strengthened their arms" → "And I, I disciplined, I made their arms strong"** (v15) — וַאֲנִי יִסַּרְתִּי חִזַּקְתִּי זְרוֹעֹתָם: יִסַּר "discipline" (no object), חִזֵּק "make strong". **"yet do they imagine mischief against me" → "and against me they devise evil"** — וְאֵלַי יְחַשְּׁבוּ־רָע, the Hebrew order.
- **"They return, but not to the most High" → "They return, not upward"** (v16) — יָשׁוּבוּ לֹא עָל, literally "not on high" (עָל "height"; "to the Most High" reads it as a title; uncertain). **"they are like a deceitful bow" → "they have become like a bow of treachery"** — הָיוּ כְּקֶשֶׁת רְמִיָּה (as Ps 78:57: a bow that fails the archer). **"for the rage of their tongue" → "because of the indignation of their tongue"** — מִזַּעַם לְשׁוֹנָם. **"this shall be their derision" → "this is their derision"** — זוֹ לַעְגָּם, no verb.
