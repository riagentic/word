# Hosea 7

[1] when I would heal Israel,
the iniquity of Ephraim is revealed,
and the evil deeds of Samaria;
for they deal falsely;
the thief breaks in,
and the bandits raid outside.

[2] But they do not consider
that I remember all their evil.
Now their deeds surround them;
they are before my face.

[3] By their evil they make the king glad,
and the princes by their treachery.

[4] They are all adulterers;
they are like a heated oven
whose baker ceases to stir the fire,
from the kneading of the dough
until it is leavened.

[5] On the day of our king, the princes
became sick with the heat of wine;
he stretched out his hand with mockers.

[6] For with hearts like an oven they approach their intrigue;
all night their anger smolders;
in the morning it blazes like a flaming fire.

[7] All of them are hot as an oven,
and they devour their rulers.
All their kings have fallen,
and none of them calls upon me.

[8] Ephraim mixes himself with the peoples;
Ephraim is a cake not turned.

[9] Strangers devour his strength,
and he knows it not;
gray hairs are sprinkled upon him,
and he knows it not.

[10] The pride of Israel testifies to his face;
yet they do not return to Yahweh their *Elohim*,
nor seek him, for all this.

[11] Ephraim is like a dove, silly and without sense,
calling to Egypt, going to Assyria.

[12] As they go, I will spread over them my net;
I will bring them down like birds of the heavens;
I will discipline them according to the report made to their congregation.

[13] Woe to them, for they have strayed from me!
Destruction to them, for they have rebelled against me!
I would redeem them, but they speak lies against me.

[14] They do not cry to me from the heart,
but they wail upon their beds;
for grain and wine they gash themselves;
they rebel against me.

[15] Although I trained and strengthened their arms,
yet they devise evil against me.

[16] They return, but not upward;
they are like a deceitful bow;
their princes shall fall by the sword
because of the insolence of their tongue.
This shall be their derision in the land of Egypt.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v10) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; Hebrew is poetry.
- **"the wickedness of Samaria" → "the evil deeds of Samaria"** (v1) — Hebrew רָעַת שֹׁמְרוֹן; "evil deeds" more concrete.
- **"commit falsehood" → "deal falsely"** (v1) — Hebrew שֶׁקֶר עָשׂוּ; "deal falsely" better captures the ongoing action.
- **"troop of robbers" → "bandits"** (v1) — Hebrew גְּדוּד *gedud*; raiding band; "bandits" more natural.
- **"their own doings have beset them about" → "their deeds surround them"** (v2) — modernized.
- **"as an oven heated by the baker, who ceaseth from raising after he hath kneaded" → "like a heated oven whose baker ceases to stir the fire, from the kneading of the dough until it is leavened"** (v4) — restructured for clarity; the oven metaphor spans vv4-7.
- **"made him sick with bottles of wine" → "became sick with the heat of wine"** (v5) — Hebrew חֵמָה יַיִן *chemat yayin* = "fever/heat of wine"; "bottles" is a KJV misreading.
- **"scorners" → "mockers"** (v5) — Hebrew לֵצִים; modernized.
- **"their baker sleepeth all the night" → "all night their anger smolders"** (v6) — the baker/oven metaphor: the fire smolders through the night; restructured to clarify the metaphor.
- **"their judges" → "their rulers"** (v7) — Hebrew שֹׁפְטֵיהֶם; in context of political coups ("kings have fallen"), "rulers" is clearer.
- **"the fowls of the heaven" → "birds of the heavens"** (v12) — Hebrew עוֹף הַשָּׁמַיִם; "fowls" is archaic.
- **"I have bound and strengthened their arms" → "I trained and strengthened their arms"** (v15) — Hebrew יִסַּרְתִּי *yissarti* = "trained/disciplined"; "bound" (KJV) is a mistranslation.
- **"to the most High" → "upward"** (v16) — Hebrew עַל עָל *al-al*; "not upward" = they don't turn to God above; "to the most High" is an interpretive gloss.
- **"for the rage of their tongue" → "because of the insolence of their tongue"** (v16) — modernized.
