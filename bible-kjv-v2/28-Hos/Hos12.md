## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 5, 6, 9) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **Layout** — poetry, one Hebrew line per printed line, as the accents divide them.
- **"Ephraim feedeth on wind, and followeth after the east wind" → "Ephraim shepherds wind and pursues the east wind"** (v1) — רֹעֶה רוּחַ, participle of רָעָה "shepherd" (its row: Ephraim tends the wind as a flock; "feeds on" is also possible); רָדַף "pursue" (its row); קָדִים "the east wind" (the רוּחַ קָדִים row). **"he daily increaseth lies and desolation" → "all the day lies and havoc he multiplies"** — כָּל־הַיּוֹם "all the day"; כָּזָב "lie"; שֹׁד "havoc" (its row). **"and they do make a covenant with the Assyrians, and oil is carried into Egypt" → "and a covenant with Assyria they cut, and oil is brought to Egypt"** — כָּרַת בְּרִית "cut a covenant" (its row), the covenant first; אַשּׁוּר the name, not "the Assyrians"; יוּבָל "is brought" (as 10:6).
- **"The LORD hath also a controversy with Judah, and will punish Jacob according to his ways" → "And Yahweh has a case with Judah, and is to visit upon Jacob according to his ways"** (v2) — וְרִיב "and a case" (רִיב its row: in law "case"; no "also"); וְלִפְקֹד, the infinitive "and to visit" (פָּקַד עַל "visit upon", its row). **"according to his doings will he recompense him" → "according to his deeds he will bring back to him"** — מַעֲלָלִים "deeds" (as 9:15); הֵשִׁיב לוֹ "bring back to him" (= repay; as v14).
- **"He took his brother by the heel in the womb, and by his strength he had power with God" → "In the belly he took his brother by the heel, and in his vigor he strove with Elohim"** (v3) — בַּבֶּטֶן "in the belly" (בֶּטֶן its row), first; עָקַב "take by the heel" (the root of יַעֲקֹב "Jacob": Gen 25:26; 27:36, where the same verb is "supplant"); אוֹן "vigor" (the strength of manhood: not אָוֶן "wickedness"; v8 has the same word); שָׂרָה "strive" (its row, which names this verse: the root of the name יִשְׂרָאֵל, Gen 32:28).
- **"Yea, he had power over the angel, and prevailed" → "And he strove with an angel and overcame"** (v4) — וָיָּשַׂר, pointed as from שָׂרַר or שָׂרָה (taken as "strove", the verb of v3; "ruled" is the other reading); אֶל־מַלְאָךְ "with an angel" (no article; מַלְאָךְ is "messenger", its row; here the one who wrestled with Jacob, Gen 32:24–30); וַיֻּכָל "and overcame" (יָכֹל, its row of a fight). **"he wept, and made supplication unto him" → "he wept and pleaded with him"** — הִתְחַנֵּן לְ "plead with" (its row, literally "seek favor"); who wept is not said (Jacob, as Gen 32:26, or the angel). **"he found him in Bethel, and there he spake with us" → "at Bethel he found him, and there he spoke with us"** — imperfects, here of the past; who found whom is not said; עִמָּנוּ "with us" (the versions read "with him": from memory, the MT kept): what was said to Jacob was said to his sons.
- **"Even the LORD God of hosts; the LORD is his memorial" → "and Yahweh, the Elohim of Hosts, Yahweh is his remembrance"** (v5) — וַיהוָה "and Yahweh"; אֱלֹהֵי הַצְּבָאוֹת "the Elohim of Hosts" (הַצְּבָאוֹת has the article; "of Hosts" as the prophets' formula); זֵכֶר "remembrance" (its row: how one is remembered and named, as Exod 3:15).
- **"Therefore turn thou to thy God: keep mercy and judgment, and wait on thy God continually" → "And you, by your Elohim you shall return; keep chesed and judgment, and wait for your Elohim continually"** (v6) — וְאַתָּה, the pronoun stressed; בֵּאלֹהֶיךָ "by your Elohim" (with his help; or "to", uncertain); תָשׁוּב imperfect "you shall return" (שׁוּב its row); חֶסֶד token; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (its row); שָׁמַר "keep" (its row); קִוָּה אֶל "wait for" (its row); תָּמִיד "continually" (its row).
- **"He is a merchant, the balances of deceit are in his hand: he loveth to oppress" → "Canaan — in his hand are balances of deceit; to extort he loves"** (v7) — כְּנַעַן is the name "Canaan" and also the word "trader" (the Canaanites were traders: Prov 31:24; Zeph 1:11): the name kept, the sense said here; "He is" not written; מֹאזְנֵי מִרְמָה "balances of deceit" (מִרְמָה its row); עָשַׁק "extort" (its row), first.
- **"And Ephraim said, Yet I am become rich, I have found me out substance" → "And Ephraim said, 'Surely I have become rich; I have found vigor for myself'"** (v8) — אַךְ "surely"; אוֹן "vigor" (as v3: here the strength that wealth gives; "wealth" is the sense, the one word kept for the play with v3). **"in all my labours they shall find none iniquity in me that were sin" → "all my labors shall not find for me iniquity that is sin"** — כָּל־יְגִיעַי "all my labors" is the subject (the accents part it from what goes before; "in" is not written); יְגִיעַ "labor" (noun of the יָגַע row); עָוֹן אֲשֶׁר־חֵטְא "iniquity that is sin" (both nouns, their rows).
- **"And I that am the LORD thy God from the land of Egypt will yet make thee to dwell in tabernacles, as in the days of the solemn feast" → "And I am Yahweh your Elohim from the land of Egypt; I will yet make you dwell in tents, as in the days of appointed time"** (v9) — two clauses, as the accents part them; עוֹד "yet, again"; הוֹשִׁיב "make dwell" (as 11:11); אֹהָלִים "tents"; מוֹעֵד "appointed time" (its row: the feast, Sukkot, is meant).
- **"I have also spoken by the prophets, and I have multiplied visions, and used similitudes, by the ministry of the prophets" → "And I spoke to the prophets, and I, I multiplied vision, and by the hand of the prophets I use likenesses"** (v10) — דִּבֶּר עַל "speak to" (literally "upon"); וְאָנֹכִי, the pronoun stressed; חָזוֹן "vision" (its row), singular; בְּיַד "by the hand of"; אֲדַמֶּה, piel of דָּמָה "liken" (I speak in likenesses; the same letters are "I will destroy", the דָּמָה of 10:7: uncertain).
- **"Is there iniquity in Gilead? surely they are vanity" → "If Gilead is wickedness, surely they have become emptiness"** (v11) — אִם "if" (also read as a question); אָוֶן "wickedness" (its row); שָׁוְא "emptiness" (the noun of the שָׁוְא "empty" row); הָיוּ "they have become". **"they sacrifice bullocks in Gilgal; yea, their altars are as heaps in the furrows of the fields" → "in Gilgal they sacrifice oxen; also their altars are like heaps on the furrows of the field"** — שְׁוָרִים "oxen" (the שׁוֹר row: not "bull"); גַּם "also"; גַּלִּים "heaps" (its row) sounds like גִּלְגָּל "Gilgal", as גִּלְעָד "Gilead" sounds like it too; תַּלְמֵי שָׂדָי as 10:4.
- **"And Jacob fled into the country of Syria, and Israel served for a wife, and for a wife he kept sheep" → "And Jacob fled to the field of Aram, and Israel served for a wife, and for a wife he kept watch"** (v12) — שְׂדֵה אֲרָם "the field of Aram" (as Gen 28:2 פַּדֶּנָה אֲרָם; token `aram`); בָּרַח "flee" (its row); עָבַד בְּ "serve for" (the price); שָׁמָר "he kept watch" (שָׁמַר "keep", its row; "sheep" is not written: Gen 30:31); the same verb in v13.
- **"And by a prophet the LORD brought Israel out of Egypt, and by a prophet was he preserved" → "And by a prophet Yahweh brought Israel up from Egypt, and by a prophet he was kept"** (v13) — הֶעֱלָה "bring up" (its row: not "bring out", הוֹצִיא); נִשְׁמָר "he was kept" (nifal of שָׁמַר: as Jacob kept for a wife, Israel was kept by a prophet).
- **"Ephraim provoked him to anger most bitterly: therefore shall he leave his blood upon him, and his reproach shall his Lord return unto him" → "Ephraim has vexed bitterly; and his blood he will leave upon him, and his reproach his Lord will bring back to him"** (v14) — הִכְעִיס "vex" (its row; "him" and "to anger" are not written); תַּמְרוּרִים "bitterly" (literally "bitternesses"); דָּמָיו "his blood" (plural: blood shed, and its guilt, as 1:4); נָטַשׁ "leave" (its row); no "therefore"; חֶרְפָּה "reproach" (its row); הֵשִׁיב as v2; אֲדֹנָיו "his Lord" (אָדוֹן with the plural of majesty; not the name אֲדֹנָי).

### Notes
- **Numbering** — our 12:1–14 = Hebrew 12:2–15.
- **Jacob** (v3–4, 12) — the story of Gen 25:26; 28:10–22; 29:15–30; 32:24–30; 35:9–15; v3 plays עָקַב on יַעֲקֹב, v3–4 שָׂרָה on יִשְׂרָאֵל.
