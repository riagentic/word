# Hosea 12

[1] ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ feeds on the wind
and pursues the east wind all day long;
they multiply falsehood and violence;
they make a covenant with ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧,
and oil is carried to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[2] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has a dispute with ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧
and will punish ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ according to his ways;
he will repay him according to his deeds.

[3] In the womb he took his brother by the heel,
and in his manhood he strove with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[4] He strove with the messenger and prevailed;
he wept and sought his favor.
He met ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ at ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧,
and there ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ spoke with us —

[5] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is his memorial name:

[6] "So you, by the help of your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, return,
hold fast to ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and justice,
and wait continually for your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[7] A merchant, in whose hands are false balances,
he loves to oppress.

[8] ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ has said, "Ah, but I am rich;
I have found wealth for myself;
in all my labors they cannot find in me iniquity or sin."

[9] I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧
from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧;
I will again make you dwell in tents,
as in the days of the appointed feast.

[10] I spoke to the prophets;
it was I who multiplied visions,
and through the prophets gave parables.

[11] If there is iniquity in ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧,
they shall surely come to nothing:
in Gilgal they sacrifice bulls;
their altars also are like stone heaps
on the furrows of the field.

[12] ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ fled to the land of Aram;
there ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ served for a wife,
and for a wife he guarded sheep.

[13] By a prophet ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ brought ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ up from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
and by a prophet he was guarded.

[14] ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ has given bitter provocation;
so his Lord will leave his bloodguilt on him
and repay him for his disgraceful deeds.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim* consistently.
- **"LORD God of hosts" → "*Elohim* of Hosts"** (v5) — Hebrew יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵי הַצְּבָאוֹת; retained *Tzeva'ot*.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; Hebrew is poetry.
- **"The LORD hath also a controversy with Judah" → "Yahweh has a dispute with Judah"** (v2) — Hebrew רִיב *riv* = covenant lawsuit; retained with prior gloss (see ch. 4).
- **"by his strength he had power with God" → "in his manhood he strove with *Elohim*"** (v3) — Hebrew כֹּחוֹ שָׂרָה אֶת אֱלֹהִים; "strove" reflects the same root as "Israel" (יִשְׂרָאֵל = "he strives with El").
- **"the angel" → "the messenger"** (v4) — Hebrew מַלְאָךְ *mal'akh* = messenger; systematic change.
- **"made supplication unto him" → "sought his favor"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיִּתְחַנֶּן לוֹ; modernized.
- **"the LORD is his memorial" → "Yahweh is his memorial name"** (v5) — Hebrew זִכְרוֹ; the divine name is the "memorial/remembrance."
- **"the balances of deceit are in his hand" → "in whose hands are false balances"** (v7) — restructured for clarity.
- **"I have found me out substance" → "I have found wealth for myself"** (v8) — modernized.
- **"tabernacles" → "tents"** (v9) — Hebrew בְּאֹהָלִים; "tents" — the Feast of Booths/Sukkot uses temporary shelters.
- **"used similitudes" → "gave parables"** (v10) — Hebrew בְּיַד הַנְּבִיאִים אֲדַמֶּה; "parables" clearer than "similitudes."
- **"the country of Syria" → "the land of Aram"** (v12) — Hebrew אֲרַם *aram*; "Aram" is more precise than "Syria."
- **"he was preserved" → "he was guarded"** (v13) — Hebrew נִשְׁמָר; "guarded" consistent with shepherd image.
- **"his reproach shall his Lord return unto him" → "repay him for his disgraceful deeds"** (v14) — modernized.
