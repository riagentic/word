# Hosea 3

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, "Go again, love a woman loved by a friend and committing adultery, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ loves the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they are turning to other gods and are lovers of raisin cakes."

[2] And I bought her for myself for fifteen of silver and a homer of barley and a lethech of barley.

[3] And I said to her, "Many days you shall dwell for me; you shall not play the prostitute, and you shall not become a man's; and I also toward you."

[4] For many days the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall dwell with no king and no prince, and no sacrifice and no pillar, and no ephod and ⟦teraphim|teraphim|household gods⟧.

[5] Afterward the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall return and seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and David their king, and they shall be in dread toward ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and toward his goodness in the latter days.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵיהֶם; "other gods" (v1) אֱלֹהִים אֲחֵרִים, plain text.
- **"Then said the LORD unto me" → "And Yahweh said to me"** (v1) — וַיֹּאמֶר. **"Go yet" → "Go again"** — עוֹד לֵךְ (עוֹד may also go with "said": "And Yahweh said to me again"). **"beloved of her friend, yet an adulteress" → "loved by a friend and committing adultery"** — Hebrew אֲהֻבַת רֵעַ, "her" is not in the Hebrew; רֵעַ "friend" (whether the husband or another man is not said); וּמְנָאָפֶת, a participle, "and". **"according to the love of the LORD toward the children of Israel" → "as Yahweh loves the sons of Israel"** — כְּאַהֲבַת יְהוָה, "as the loving of Yahweh", an infinitive. **"who look to other gods" → "and they are turning to other gods"** — וְהֵם פֹּנִים, participle. **"love flagons of wine" → "are lovers of raisin cakes"** — אֹהֲבֵי אֲשִׁישֵׁי עֲנָבִים, literally "lovers of raisin cakes of grapes" (2 Sam 6:19; Isa 16:7); no wine is named.
- **"So I bought her to me" → "And I bought her for myself"** (v2) — וָאֶכְּרֶהָ לִּי (כָּרָה "buy"; uncertain). **"fifteen pieces of silver" → "fifteen of silver"** — חֲמִשָּׁה עָשָׂר כָּסֶף: neither "pieces" nor "shekels" is in the Hebrew. **"an half homer of barley" → "a lethech of barley"** — לֶתֶךְ, found only here: a measure, commonly taken as half a homer (uncertain).
- **"Thou shalt abide for me many days" → "Many days you shall dwell for me"** (v3) — יָמִים רַבִּים תֵּשְׁבִי לִי, the Hebrew order; יָשַׁב "dwell" (also "sit, wait"). **"play the harlot" → "play the prostitute"** — תִזְנִי. **"thou shalt not be for another man" → "you shall not become a man's"** — לֹא תִהְיִי לְאִישׁ (no "another"). **"so will I also be for thee" → "and I also toward you"** — Hebrew וְגַם־אֲנִי אֵלָיִךְ, no verb: what he will do toward her is left unsaid.
- **"without a king, and without a prince, and without a sacrifice, and without an image, and without an ephod, and without teraphim" → "with no king and no prince, and no sacrifice and no pillar, and no ephod and teraphim"** (v4) — Hebrew אֵין "there is not", five times, the last before both "ephod and teraphim"; מַצֵּבָה "pillar" (a stone set up). **"abide" → "dwell"** — יֵשְׁבוּ, as v3.
- **"children" → "sons"** (v4, 5) — בְּנֵי. **"return, and seek"** (v5) — יָשֻׁבוּ … וּבִקְשׁוּ. **"shall fear the LORD and his goodness" → "shall be in dread toward Yahweh and toward his goodness"** — Hebrew וּפָחֲדוּ אֶל־יְהוָה וְאֶל־טוּבוֹ, פָּחַד "be in dread" with אֶל "toward" (= come trembling to). **"in the latter days"** — בְּאַחֲרִית הַיָּמִים, literally "in the after-part of the days".

### Notes
- **v4** — *ephod* and *teraphim*: the ephod is the priest's garment and the means of asking of Yahweh (1 Sam 23:9–12); *teraphim* are household images (Gen 31:19).
