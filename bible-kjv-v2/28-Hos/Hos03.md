# Hosea 3

[1] And Yahweh said to me, "Go again, love a woman who is loved by another man and is an adulteress, even as Yahweh loves the children of Israel, though they turn to other gods and love cakes of raisins."

[2] So I bought her for fifteen shekels of silver and a homer and a lethech of barley.

[3] And I said to her, "You must dwell as mine for many days. You shall not play the whore, or belong to another man; so will I also be to you."

[4] For the children of Israel shall dwell many days without king or prince, without sacrifice or pillar, without ephod or household gods.

[5] Afterward the children of Israel shall return and seek Yahweh their *Elohim*, and David their king, and they shall come in fear to Yahweh and to his goodness in the latter days.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5) — retains Hebrew.
- **"Then said the LORD unto me" → "And Yahweh said to me"** (v1) — modernized syntax.
- **"beloved of her friend" → "loved by another man"** (v1) — Hebrew אֲהֻבַת רֵעַ *ahuva re'a*; "friend" is misleading; "another man" clarifies the adultery context.
- **"flagons of wine" → "cakes of raisins"** (v1) — Hebrew אֲשִׁישֵׁי עֲנָבִים *ashishei anavim*; these were likely pressed-raisin cakes used in pagan fertility cult worship, not flagons of wine; "cakes of raisins" (cf. 2 Sam 6:19; Isa 16:7) is the standard gloss.
- **"fifteen pieces of silver" → "fifteen shekels of silver"** (v2) — Hebrew חֲמִשָּׁה עָשָׂר כֶּסֶף; "pieces" is vague; "shekels" is the standard unit.
- **"an half homer of barley" → "a lethech of barley"** (v2) — Hebrew וְלֶתֶךְ שְׂעֹרִים; *lethech* = half-homer; retaining the Hebrew term preserves the measure.
- **"Thou shalt abide for me" → "You must dwell as mine"** (v3) — modernized second-person singular.
- **"play the harlot" → "play the whore"** (v3) — consistent vocabulary throughout Hosea.
- **"image" → "pillar"** (v4) — Hebrew מַצֵּבָה *matzevah*; standing stone, not an image/statue; "pillar" is more precise.
- **"ephod" and "teraphim" — kept** (v4) — technical cultic terms; *ephod* = priestly vestment/oracle object; *teraphim* = household gods/figurines; better left untranslated.
