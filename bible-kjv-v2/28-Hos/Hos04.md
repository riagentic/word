# Hosea 4

[1] Hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has a case with the inhabitants of the land,
for there is no truth and no ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧
and no knowledge of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in the land.

[2] Cursing and lying and murdering and stealing and committing adultery!
They break out, and bloodshed touches bloodshed.

[3] Therefore the land mourns,
and everyone who dwells in it languishes,
with the living things of the field and with the birds of the heavens;
and also the fish of the sea are gathered away.

[4] Only let no man contend, and let no man reprove;
and your people are like those who contend with a priest.

[5] And you shall stumble by day,
and the prophet also shall stumble with you by night;
and I will destroy your mother.

[6] **My people are undone for lack of the knowledge;**
because you have rejected the knowledge,
I will reject you from being priest to me;
and you have forgotten the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧:
I also will forget your sons.

[7] As they multiplied, so they sinned against me;
their glory I will change into disgrace.

[8] The sin of my people they eat,
and to their iniquity they lift up each his soul.

[9] And it shall be: like people, like priest;
and I will visit upon him his ways,
and his doings I will bring back to him.

[10] And they shall eat and not be sated;
they have played the prostitute and shall not spread abroad,
for they have forsaken keeping ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[11] Prostitution and wine and new wine
take the heart.

[12] My people asks of his wood,
and his staff tells him;
for a spirit of prostitution has caused them to wander,
and they have played the prostitute from under their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[13] On the tops of the mountains they sacrifice,
and on the hills they make smoke,
under oak and poplar and terebinth,
for its shade is good;
therefore your daughters play the prostitute,
and your daughters-in-law commit adultery.

[14] I will not visit upon your daughters when they play the prostitute,
nor upon your daughters-in-law when they commit adultery;
for they themselves go aside with the prostitutes,
and with the cult prostitutes they sacrifice;
and a people that does not understand shall be thrown down.

[15] If you play the prostitute, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
let not ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ become guilty;
and do not come to Gilgal,
and do not go up to ⟦beth-aven|Beit Aven|Beth-aven⟧,
and do not swear, "As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives."

[16] For like a stubborn cow
⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ has been stubborn;
now ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will shepherd them
like a lamb in the broad place.

[17] Joined to idols is ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧;
let him alone.

[18] Their drink has turned aside;
they surely play the prostitute;
they have loved, "Give!" — disgrace, her shields.

[19] A wind has bound her up in its wings,
and they shall be ashamed because of their sacrifices.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 6, 12) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept (v3–5, 9, 10, 15, 19).
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order (v18 now three lines, by the accents).
- **"ye children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v1) — בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל; no "O" (no word for it). **"hath a controversy with" → "has a case with"** — רִיב, a case at law. **"there is no truth, nor mercy, nor knowledge of God" → "there is no truth and no chesed and no knowledge of Elohim"** — אֵין three times; אֱמֶת "truth", חֶסֶד (token), דַּעַת אֱלֹהִים.
- **"By swearing, and lying, and killing, and stealing, and committing adultery, they break out" → "Cursing and lying and murdering and stealing and committing adultery! They break out"** (v2) — five infinitives absolute standing alone, as an outcry; אָלָה "curse" (the curse of an oath, as Lev 5:1), כִּחֵשׁ "lie", רָצַח "murder" (the commandment's word, Exod 20:13), גָּנַב, נָאַף. **"blood toucheth blood" → "bloodshed touches bloodshed"** — דָמִים בְּדָמִים נָגָעוּ, literally "bloods touch on bloods" (one deed of blood upon another).
- **"shall mourn" → "mourns"** (v3) — תֶּאֱבַל, an imperfect (also "shall mourn"). **"every one that dwelleth therein shall languish" → "everyone who dwells in it languishes"** — אֻמְלַל. **"with the beasts of the field, and with the fowls of heaven" → "with the living things of the field and with the birds of the heavens"** — בְּחַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה וּבְעוֹף הַשָּׁמָיִם. **"shall be taken away" → "are gathered away"** — יֵאָסֵפוּ (אָסַף "gather").
- **"Yet let no man strive, nor reprove another: for thy people are as they that strive with the priest" → "Only let no man contend, and let no man reprove; and your people are like those who contend with a priest"** (v4) — Hebrew אַךְ אִישׁ אַל־יָרֵב וְאַל־יוֹכַח אִישׁ וְעַמְּךָ כִּמְרִיבֵי כֹהֵן, as printed: "a man" twice, "and", "a priest" without the article. The line is hard: the reading "and with you is my case, priest" (a change of vowels and letters) is common in modern versions and is not followed; who "you" is (the priest, v4–6) is not said.
- **"Therefore shalt thou fall in the day" → "And you shall stumble by day"** (v5) — וְכָשַׁלְתָּ, "and"; כָּשַׁל "stumble" twice; הַיּוֹם "today / by day". **"I will destroy thy mother"** — וְדָמִיתִי, from דָּמָה "cease, be cut off", the verb of v6 "are undone" (nifal; the Hos 8–14 row, as 10:7, 15) (also "be silenced").
- **"for lack of knowledge" → "for lack of the knowledge"** (v6) — מִבְּלִי הַדָּעַת, the article kept twice ("the knowledge" of Elohim, v1). **"that thou shalt be no priest to me" → "from being priest to me"** — מִכַּהֵן לִי. וְאֶמְאָסְאךָ is written with an extra א. **"seeing thou hast forgotten the law of thy God" → "and you have forgotten the torah of your Elohim"** — וַתִּשְׁכַּח, "and"; תּוֹרָה (token). **"thy children" → "your sons"** — בָּנֶיךָ; גַּם־אָנִי "I also" stands last in the Hebrew.
- **"As they were increased" → "As they multiplied"** (v7) — כְּרֻבָּם. **"therefore will I change their glory into shame" → "their glory I will change into disgrace"** — the Hebrew order; קָלוֹן "disgrace"; no "therefore". Jewish tradition counts this among the scribes' corrections, with an older "my glory they have changed" (from memory).
- **"They eat up the sin of my people" → "The sin of my people they eat"** (v8) — חַטַּאת עַמִּי, the Hebrew order; חַטָּאת is "sin" and "sin offering" (the priests eat the people's sin offerings, Lev 6:26). **"they set their heart on their iniquity" → "to their iniquity they lift up each his soul"** — וְאֶל־עֲוֺנָם יִשְׂאוּ נַפְשׁוֹ, "lift up the soul to" (= long for); נַפְשׁוֹ "his soul", singular ("each" supplied).
- **"And there shall be, like people, like priest" → "And it shall be: like people, like priest"** (v9) — וְהָיָה כָעָם כַּכֹּהֵן. **"I will punish them for their ways, and reward them their doings" → "I will visit upon him his ways, and his doings I will bring back to him"** — Hebrew singulars: פָקַדְתִּי עָלָיו דְּרָכָיו, "visit upon"; מַעֲלָלָיו "his doings"; אָשִׁיב "bring back".
- **"For they shall eat, and not have enough" → "And they shall eat and not be sated"** (v10) — וְאָכְלוּ, "and"; שָׂבַע "be sated". **"they shall commit whoredom, and shall not increase" → "they have played the prostitute and shall not spread abroad"** — הִזְנוּ, a perfect of the hifil (as v18; also "lead into prostitution"); פָּרַץ "break out, spread abroad" (of increase, as Gen 28:14). **"because they have left off to take heed to the LORD" → "for they have forsaken keeping Yahweh"** — כִּי־אֶת־יְהוָה עָזְבוּ לִשְׁמֹר, literally "for Yahweh they have forsaken, to keep (= heed)"; by the accents the verse ends here; others carry לִשְׁמֹר into v11 ("to keep prostitution").
- **"Whoredom and wine and new wine take away the heart" → "Prostitution and wine and new wine take the heart"** (v11) — זְנוּת "prostitution"; יִקַּח־לֵב, literally "takes the heart" (= the understanding: in Hebrew the heart is the seat of the mind).
- **"My people ask counsel at their stocks" → "My people asks of his wood"** (v12) — עַמִּי בְּעֵצוֹ יִשְׁאָל, singulars; שָׁאַל בְּ "ask of" (as of an oracle); עֵץ "wood, tree" (an image or a rod). **"their staff declareth unto them" → "his staff tells him"** — וּמַקְלוֹ יַגִּיד לוֹ. **"hath caused them to err" → "has caused them to wander"** — הִתְעָה. **"they have gone a whoring from under their God" → "they have played the prostitute from under their Elohim"** — מִתַּחַת, literally "from under" (from under his authority, as a wife).
- **"They sacrifice upon the tops of the mountains" → "On the tops of the mountains they sacrifice"** (v13) — the Hebrew order. **"burn incense" → "make smoke"** — יְקַטֵּרוּ, without an object. **"oaks and poplars and elms" → "oak and poplar and terebinth"** — אַלּוֹן, לִבְנֶה, אֵלָה (singulars; the kinds are not certain). **"because the shadow thereof is good" → "for its shade is good"** — צִלָּהּ. **"commit whoredom" → "play the prostitute"** (v13–15) — זָנָה. **"spouses" → "daughters-in-law"** — כַּלּוֹתֵיכֶם (כַּלָּה "daughter-in-law, bride").
- **"I will not punish" → "I will not visit upon"** (v14) — לֹא־אֶפְקוֹד עַל. **"for themselves are separated with whores" → "for they themselves go aside with the prostitutes"** — כִּי־הֵם (masculine: the men) עִם־הַזֹּנוֹת יְפָרֵדוּ, the article kept. **"they sacrifice with harlots" → "with the cult prostitutes they sacrifice"** — הַקְּדֵשׁוֹת, literally "the consecrated women" (root קדשׁ); that they served at a shrine is inferred from the word, beside זוֹנָה "prostitute". **"the people that doth not understand shall fall" → "a people that does not understand shall be thrown down"** — וְעָם לֹא־יָבִין יִלָּבֵט (לָבַט nifal, Prov 10:8; uncertain).
- **"Though thou, Israel, play the harlot" → "If you play the prostitute, Israel"** (v15) — אִם־זֹנֶה אַתָּה, a participle; no "O". **"offend" → "become guilty"** — יֶאְשַׁם. **"come not ye unto Gilgal, neither go ye up to Bethaven, nor swear" → "and do not come to Gilgal, and do not go up to Beth-aven, and do not swear"** — three prohibitions with וְאַל. **Beth-aven** — בֵּית אָוֶן "house of wickedness", for Bethel, "house of El". **"The LORD liveth" → "As Yahweh lives"** — חַי־יְהוָה, the oath formula.
- **"For Israel slideth back as a backsliding heifer" → "For like a stubborn cow Israel has been stubborn"** (v16) — Hebrew כְּפָרָה סֹרֵרָה סָרַר יִשְׂרָאֵל; פָּרָה "cow"; סָרַר "be stubborn", adjective and verb. **"now the LORD will feed them as a lamb in a large place" → "now Yahweh will shepherd them like a lamb in the broad place"** — a statement in the Hebrew, without a question word (read as irony, or as a question: "will Yahweh now shepherd them …?"); רָעָה "shepherd"; מֶרְחָב "the broad place".
- **"Ephraim is joined to idols" → "Joined to idols is Ephraim"** (v17) — the Hebrew order; חֲבוּר, a passive participle. **"let him alone"** — הַנַּח־לוֹ, literally "leave (it) to him".
- **"Their drink is sour" → "Their drink has turned aside"** (v18) — סָר סָבְאָם; סוּר "turn aside" (= is gone, or has turned sour: the Hebrew says neither). **"they have committed whoredom continually" → "they surely play the prostitute"** — הַזְנֵה הִזְנוּ, infinitive absolute with its verb (hifil). **"her rulers with shame do love, Give ye" → "they have loved, 'Give!' — disgrace, her shields"** — Hebrew אָהֲבוּ הֵבוּ קָלוֹן מָגִנֶּיהָ, as printed: אָהֲבוּ "they have loved", הֵבוּ "give!", קָלוֹן "disgrace", מָגִנֶּיהָ "her shields" (= her rulers, Ps 47:9). The line is hard: אָהֲבוּ הֵבוּ is often read as one doubled verb, "they dearly love", or emended; no reading is put into the verse.
- **"The wind hath bound her up in her wings" → "A wind has bound her up in its wings"** (v19) — צָרַר רוּחַ אוֹתָהּ בִּכְנָפֶיהָ; רוּחַ "wind" (also "spirit": the "spirit of prostitution" of v12); "her" is the people; בִּכְנָפֶיהָ "in its wings" (the feminine suffix agrees with רוּחַ). **"because of their sacrifices"** — מִזִּבְחוֹתָם, a feminine plural found only here.
