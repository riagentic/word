# Hosea 4

[1] Hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, O children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has a lawsuit with the inhabitants of the land.
There is no faithfulness or ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
and no knowledge of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in the land;

[2] there is swearing, lying, murder, stealing, and committing adultery;
they break all bounds, and bloodshed follows bloodshed.

[3] Therefore the land mourns,
and all who dwell in it languish,
and also the beasts of the field and the birds of the heavens,
and even the fish of the sea are taken away.

[4] Yet let no one contend, and let none accuse,
for with you is my contention, O priest.

[5] You shall stumble by day;
the prophet also shall stumble with you by night;
and I will destroy your mother.

[6] **My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge;**
because you have rejected knowledge,
I reject you from being a priest to me.
And since you have forgotten the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
I also will forget your children.

[7] The more they increased, the more they sinned against me;
I will change their glory into shame.

[8] They feed on the sin of my people;
they are greedy for their iniquity.

[9] And it shall be like people, like priest;
I will punish them for their ways
and repay them for their deeds.

[10] They shall eat, but not be satisfied;
they shall play the whore, but not multiply,
because they have forsaken ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
to cherish

[11] whoredom, wine, and new wine,
which take away the understanding.

[12] My people inquire of a piece of wood,
and their walking stick gives them oracles.
For a spirit of whoredom has led them astray,
and they have gone a-whoring from under their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[13] They sacrifice on the tops of the mountains
and burn offerings on the hills,
under oak, poplar, and terebinth,
because their shade is good.
Therefore your daughters play the whore,
and your brides commit adultery.

[14] I will not punish your daughters when they play the whore,
nor your brides when they commit adultery;
for the men themselves go aside with prostitutes
and sacrifice with cult prostitutes,
and a people without understanding shall come to ruin.

[15] Though you play the whore, O ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
let not ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ become guilty.
Enter not into Gilgal,
nor go up to ⟦beth-aven|Beit Aven|Beth-aven⟧,
and swear not, "As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives."

[16] Like a stubborn heifer,
⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ is stubborn;
can ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ now feed them
like a lamb in a broad pasture?

[17] ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ is joined to idols;
leave him alone.

[18] When their drink is gone, they give themselves to whoring;
their rulers dearly love shame.

[19] A wind has wrapped them in its wings,
and they shall be ashamed because of their sacrifices.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv1, 6) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV renders as prose; the chapter is sustained prophetic poetry; line breaks added per parallelism structure.
- **"truth" → "faithfulness"** (v1) — Hebrew אֱמֶת *emet*; better rendered "faithfulness/truth" than bare "truth," which in English can be purely epistemological.
- **"knowledge of God" → "knowledge of *Elohim*"** (v1) — Hebrew דַּעַת אֱלֹהִים; retains *Elohim*.
- **"they break out, and blood toucheth blood" → "they break all bounds, and bloodshed follows bloodshed"** (v2) — modernized; the Hebrew idiom פָּרַץ "break out" applied to social violence.
- **"with the fowls of heaven" → "the birds of the heavens"** (v3) — Hebrew עוֹף הַשָּׁמַיִם; "fowls" is archaic.
- **"for thy people are as they that strive with the priest" → "for with you is my contention, O priest"** (v4) — the MT is difficult; this rendering follows most modern critical translations: address to the priest directly.
- **"the law of thy God" → "the Law of your *Elohim*"** (v6) — Hebrew תּוֹרַת אֱלֹהֶיךָ; *Torah* retained; "law" is reductive.
- **"reward them their doings" → "repay them for their deeds"** (v9) — modernized.
- **"take away the heart" → "take away the understanding"** (v11) — Hebrew לֵב *lev* can mean "mind/understanding" not just "heart."
- **"their stocks" → "a piece of wood"** (v12) — Hebrew עֵצוֹ *etzo*; a wooden idol/divination object.
- **"burn incense" → "burn offerings"** (v13) — Hebrew יְקַטֵּרוּ; more general than "incense"; sacrifice at high places.
- **"elms" → "terebinth"** (v13) — Hebrew אֵלָה *elah*; terebinth (pistachio tree family), not elm; KJV "elms" is a mistranslation.
- **"separated with whores" → "go aside with prostitutes"** (v14) — modernized.
- **"harlots" → "cult prostitutes"** (v14) — Hebrew קְדֵשׁוֹת *qedeshoth*; these are cult/sacred prostitutes (*qedesh/qedeshah*), not ordinary prostitutes; retained as "cult prostitutes" for accuracy.
- **"play the harlot" → "play the whore"** (v15) — consistent vocabulary.
- **"slideth back as a backsliding heifer" → "Like a stubborn heifer, Israel is stubborn"** (v16) — the Hebrew סוֹרֵרָה *sorerah* = stubborn/rebellious; "backsliding" is a misleading metaphor; "stubborn heifer" is vivid.
- **"The wind hath bound her up in her wings" → "A wind has wrapped them in its wings"** (v19) — modernized pronoun and imagery.

### Notes
- **v15 Beth-Aven** — pun for Beth-El (House of God → House of Wickedness) reflecting idolatrous practices.
