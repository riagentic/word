# Hosea 2

[1] Say to your brothers, "⟦ammi|Ammi|My people⟧," and to your sisters, "⟦ruchamah|Ruchamah|Received mercy⟧."

[2] Contend with your mother, contend,
for she is not my wife,
and I am not her man;
and let her remove her prostitution from her face,
and her adulteries from between her breasts;

[3] lest I strip her naked
and place her as in the day she was born,
and make her like the wilderness,
and set her like a land of dryness,
and put her to death with thirst.

[4] And on her sons I will not have compassion,
for they are sons of prostitution.

[5] For their mother has played the prostitute;
she who conceived them has acted shamefully.
For she said, "Let me go after my lovers,
who give my bread and my water,
my wool and my flax, my oil and my drinks."

[6] Therefore, behold, I am hedging up your way with thorns,
and I will wall up her wall,
and her tracks she shall not find.

[7] And she shall pursue her lovers and shall not overtake them,
and she shall seek them and shall not find.
And she shall say, "Let me go and return to my first man,
for it was better for me then than now."

[8] And she did not know
that it was I who gave her
the grain and the new wine and the fresh oil,
and silver I multiplied to her,
and gold, which they made for the Baal.

[9] Therefore I will return,
and I will take my grain in its time,
and my new wine in its appointed time,
and I will snatch away my wool and my flax,
to cover her nakedness.

[10] And now I will uncover her lewdness
before the eyes of her lovers,
and no man shall deliver her out of my hand.

[11] And I will make all her exultation cease,
her feast, her new moon, and her ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧,
and all her appointed times.

[12] And I will make desolate her vine and her fig tree,
of which she said,
"They are my hire,
which my lovers have given me";
and I will make them a forest,
and the living things of the field shall eat them.

[13] And I will visit upon her the days of the Baals,
to whom she made smoke,
and she adorned herself with her ring and her jewelry,
and went after her lovers,
and me she forgot, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[14] Therefore, behold, I am enticing her,
and I will make her walk into the wilderness,
and I will speak to her heart.

[15] And I will give her her vineyards from there,
and the valley of Achor
for an entrance of hope;
and she shall answer there as in the days of her youth,
and as in the day of her coming up from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[16] And it shall be in that day, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
you shall call, "⟦ishi-husband|Ishi|My man⟧,"
and you shall no more call me, "⟦baali|Baali|My Baal⟧."

[17] And I will remove the names of the Baals from her mouth,
and they shall no more be remembered by their name.

[18] And I will cut for them a covenant in that day
with the living things of the field and with the birds of the heavens
and the creeping things of the ground;
and bow and sword and war I will break from the land,
and I will make them lie down in security.

[19] And I will betroth you to me forever;
and I will betroth you to me in righteousness and in judgment,
and in ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and in compassion.

[20] And I will betroth you to me in faithfulness,
and you shall know ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[21] And it shall be in that day, I will answer, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
I will answer the heavens,
and they shall answer the earth;

[22] and the earth shall answer the grain and the new wine and the fresh oil,
and they shall answer ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧.

[23] And I will sow her for myself in the land,
and I will have compassion on ⟦lo-ruhamah|Lo-Ruchamah|No Mercy⟧,
and I will say to ⟦lo-ammi|Lo-Ammi|Not My People⟧, "You are my people,"
and he shall say, "My ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v23) — Hebrew אֱלֹהָי "my Elohim".
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Layout** (v2–23) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v1 stays one line. No outer quotation marks: the speaker of v2–23 is Yahweh (v13, 16, 21).
- **"Say ye unto your brethren, Ammi; and to your sisters, Ruhamah" → "Say to your brothers, 'Ammi,' and to your sisters, 'Ruchamah'"** (v1) — Hebrew עַמִּי "my people" and רֻחָמָה "she has been shown compassion": the names of 1:6, 9 without לֹא "not". The plural "brothers … sisters" is the Hebrew's.
- **"Plead with your mother, plead" → "Contend with your mother, contend"** (v2) — רִיבוּ, the verb of a case at law. **"her husband" → "her man"** — אִישָׁהּ; אִישׁ is "man" and "husband" (one word), kept "man" here, in v7 and v16, where the play of v16 needs it. **"let her therefore put away her whoredoms" → "and let her remove her prostitution"** — וְתָסֵר, a jussive; זְנוּנִים "prostitution" (no "whoredom"). **"adulteries"** — נַאֲפוּפִים, found only here, from נָאַף "commit adultery".
- **"set her as in the day" → "place her as in the day"** (v3) — הִצַּגְתִּיהָ (הִצִּיג "set, place"); three verbs kept apart: הִצִּיג "place", שִׂים "make", שִׁית "set". **"a dry land" → "a land of dryness"** — אֶרֶץ צִיָּה. **"slay her" → "put her to death"** — הֵמִית.
- **"children" → "sons"** (v4) — בָּנֶיהָ, בְּנֵי זְנוּנִים "sons of prostitution". **"have mercy" → "have compassion"** — רִחַם as 1:6.
- **"hath played the harlot" → "has played the prostitute"** (v5) — זָנְתָה. **"done shamefully" → "acted shamefully"** — הֹבִישָׁה, hifil of בּוֹשׁ. **"I will go" → "Let me go"** — אֵלְכָה, a cohortative (also v7). **"that give me my bread" → "who give my bread"** — נֹתְנֵי לַחְמִי, no "me". **"my drink" → "my drinks"** — שִׁקּוּיַי, plural.
- **"I will hedge up thy way" → "behold, I am hedging up your way"** (v6) — Hebrew הִנְנִי־שָׂךְ, "behold me hedging", a participle; דַּרְכֵּךְ "your way" (feminine singular: she is addressed), then "her" — as printed. **"make a wall, that she shall not find her paths" → "I will wall up her wall, and her tracks she shall not find"** — גָּדַרְתִּי אֶת־גְּדֵרָהּ, verb and noun of one root; נְתִיבָה "track"; וְ "and", not "that".
- **"follow after" → "pursue"** (v7) — רִדְּפָה. **"shall not find them" → "shall not find"** — no object in the Hebrew. **"my first husband" → "my first man"** — אִישִׁי הָרִאשׁוֹן (see v2).
- **"corn, and wine, and oil" → "the grain and the new wine and the fresh oil"** (v8, 22) — דָּגָן, תִּירוֹשׁ, יִצְהָר, with the article. **"that I gave her" → "that it was I who gave her"** — the pronoun אָנֹכִי stands out in the Hebrew. **"multiplied her silver and gold, which they prepared for Baal" → "silver I multiplied to her, and gold, which they made for the Baal"** — Hebrew וְכֶסֶף הִרְבֵּיתִי לָהּ וְזָהָב עָשׂוּ לַבָּעַל, the article kept; "made for the Baal" may mean "made into the Baal" (an image) or "used for the Baal": the Hebrew does not say which.
- **"Therefore will I return, and take away" → "Therefore I will return, and I will take"** (v9) — אָשׁוּב וְלָקַחְתִּי, "return and take" (= take back). **"in the season thereof" → "in its appointed time"** — מוֹעֲדוֹ. **"will recover" → "will snatch away"** — הִצַּלְתִּי (הִצִּיל "snatch away, deliver"). **"given to cover her nakedness" → "to cover her nakedness"** — an infinitive: what was meant to cover her.
- **"lewdness"** (v10) — נַבְלֻת, found only here; from the root of נָבָל "senseless, disgraceful": "shame", "lewdness" (uncertain). **"in the sight of" → "before the eyes of"** — לְעֵינֵי. **"none shall deliver her" → "no man shall deliver her"** — וְאִישׁ לֹא־יַצִּילֶנָּה.
- **"I will also cause all her mirth to cease" → "I will make all her exultation cease"** (v11) — הִשְׁבַּתִּי (as 1:4); מָשׂוֹשׂ "exultation". **"her feast days, her new moons, and her sabbaths, and all her solemn feasts" → "her feast, her new moon, and her Shabbat, and all her appointed times"** — all four nouns are singular in the Hebrew (collectives; "appointed times" plural for English); חֹדֶשׁ "month, new moon"; מוֹעֵד "appointed time".
- **"destroy her vines" → "make desolate her vine"** (v12) — הֲשִׁמֹּתִי (hifil of שָׁמֵם); גַּפְנָהּ, singular. **"These are my rewards" → "They are my hire"** — Hebrew אֶתְנָה הֵמָּה, אֶתְנָה found only here, as אֶתְנַן (a prostitute's hire, Hos 9:1); a play on תְּאֵנָה "fig tree" is often seen. **"the beasts of the field shall eat them" → "the living things of the field shall eat them"** — חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה (also v18).
- **"I will visit upon her the days of Baalim" → "the days of the Baals"** (v13) — הַבְּעָלִים, the article kept (also v17). **"wherein she burned incense to them" → "to whom she made smoke"** — תַּקְטִיר לָהֶם, without an object. **"her earrings and her jewels" → "her ring and her jewelry"** — נִזְמָהּ (the word says only "ring"), חֶלְיָתָהּ (found only here). **"forgat me" → "me she forgot"** — the Hebrew order. **"saith the LORD" → "declares Yahweh"** (v13, 16, 21) — נְאֻם יְהוָה.
- **"I will allure her" → "behold, I am enticing her"** (v14) — מְפַתֶּיהָ, a participle of פִּתָּה "entice" (as Exod 22:16). **"bring her into the wilderness" → "make her walk into the wilderness"** — הֹלַכְתִּיהָ, hifil of הָלַךְ. **"speak comfortably unto her" → "speak to her heart"** — דִּבַּרְתִּי עַל לִבָּהּ, literally "upon her heart".
- **"for a door of hope" → "for an entrance of hope"** (v15) — פֶּתַח תִּקְוָה; פֶּתַח "entrance" (דֶּלֶת is "door"); עָכוֹר "trouble" (Josh 7:24–26). **"she shall sing there" → "she shall answer there"** — עָנְתָה, the verb of v21–22 ("answer"; "sing, respond" in Changes only). **"as in the day when she came up" → "as in the day of her coming up"** — וּכְיוֹם עֲלֹתָהּ, an infinitive.
- **"thou shalt call me Ishi; and shalt call me no more Baali" → "you shall call, 'Ishi / My man,' and you shall no more call me, 'Baali / My Baal'"** (v16) — Hebrew תִּקְרְאִי אִישִׁי (no "me" in the first clause) וְלֹא־תִקְרְאִי־לִי עוֹד בַּעְלִי; אִישִׁי "my man" (= my husband, as v2, 7), בַּעְלִי "my baal": בַּעַל is "owner, master", also "husband" (Exod 21:22), and the name of the god: she shall not even call her husband by the word that names Baal (v17).
- **"they shall no more be remembered by their name"** (v17) — בִּשְׁמָם, singular.
- **"make a covenant" → "cut a covenant"** (v18) — כָּרַתִּי בְרִית. **"in that day"** — the Hebrew has it before "with". **"I will break the bow and the sword and the battle out of the earth" → "bow and sword and war I will break from the land"** — the objects first; מִלְחָמָה "war"; אֶרֶץ "land" (or "earth"). **"safely" → "in security"** — לָבֶטַח.
- **"in judgment, and in lovingkindness, and in mercies" → "in judgment, and in chesed and in compassion"** (v19) — מִשְׁפָּט "judgment", חֶסֶד (token), רַחֲמִים "compassion".
- **"I will hear … they shall hear" → "I will answer … they shall answer"** (v21–22) — עָנָה five times. **"it shall come to pass" → "it shall be"** — וְהָיָה. **"they shall hear Jezreel"** (v22) — Jezreel, "El sows", turns the name of 1:4 to good (v23 "I will sow her").
- **"I will sow her unto me" → "I will sow her for myself"** (v23) — זְרַעְתִּיהָ לִּי. **"her that had not obtained mercy" → "Lo-Ruchamah"**; **"them which were not my people" → "Lo-Ammi"** — the names as in 1:6, 9, without "her" and "them": לְלֹא־עַמִּי "to Lo-Ammi". **"Thou art my God" → "My Elohim"** — אֱלֹהָי, one word; "Thou art" is supplied by the KJV.

### Notes
- **Numbering** — our 2:1–23 = Hebrew 2:3–25.
- **v23** — quoted Rom 9:25; 1 Pet 2:10 (the Greek, worded otherwise).
