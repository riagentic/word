# Hosea 13

[1] When Ephraim spoke, there was trembling;
he was exalted in Israel,
but he incurred guilt through Baal and died.

[2] And now they sin more and more,
and make for themselves metal images,
idols skillfully made of their silver,
all of them the work of craftsmen.
It is said of them,
"Those who offer human sacrifice kiss calves!"

[3] Therefore they shall be like the morning mist
or like the dew that goes early away,
like the chaff that swirls from the threshing floor
or like smoke from a window.

[4] But I am Yahweh your *Elohim*
from the land of Egypt;
you know no *Elohim* but me,
and besides me there is no savior.

[5] It was I who knew you in the wilderness,
in the land of drought;

[6] but when they had grazed, they became full,
they were filled, and their heart was lifted up;
therefore they forgot me.

[7] So I am to them like a lion;
like a leopard I will lurk beside the way.

[8] I will fall upon them like a bear robbed of her cubs;
I will tear open their breast,
and there I will devour them like a lion,
as a wild beast would rip them open.

[9] He destroys you, O Israel,
for you are against me, against your helper.

[10] Where now is your king, to save you in all your cities?
Where are all your rulers —
those of whom you said,
"Give me a king and princes"?

[11] I gave you a king in my anger,
and I took him away in my wrath.

[12] The iniquity of Ephraim is bound up;
his sin is kept in store.

[13] The pangs of childbirth come for him,
but he is an unwise son,
for at the proper time he does not present himself
at the opening of the womb.

[14] **I shall ransom them from the power of Sheol;
I shall redeem them from death.
O death, where are your plagues?
O Sheol, where is your sting?**
Compassion is hidden from my eyes.

[15] Though he may flourish among his brothers,
the east wind, the wind of Yahweh, shall come,
rising from the wilderness,
and his fountain shall dry up;
his spring shall be parched;
it shall strip his treasury
of every precious thing.

[16] Samaria shall bear her guilt,
because she has rebelled against her *Elohim*;
they shall fall by the sword;
their little ones shall be dashed in pieces,
and their pregnant women ripped open.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv4, 16) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; Hebrew is poetry.
- **"When Ephraim spake trembling" → "When Ephraim spoke, there was trembling"** (v1) — restructured for clarity; "trembling" refers to the trembling he inspired, not his manner of speaking.
- **"when he offended in Baal, he died" → "he incurred guilt through Baal and died"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיֶּאְשַׁם בַּבַּעַל *vayyesham babba'al*; "incurred guilt" more precise than "offended."
- **"molten images of their silver" → "metal images … of their silver"** (v2) — Hebrew מַסֵּכָה *massekah* = cast/molten metal; "metal images" is slightly more precise than "molten images."
- **"Let the men that sacrifice kiss the calves" → "Those who offer human sacrifice kiss calves"** (v2) — Hebrew זֹבְחֵי אָדָם עֲגָלִים יִשָּׁקוּן; *zovchei adam* = "those who sacrifice men/humans"; the shocking verse combines human sacrifice with calf-kissing.
- **"as the chaff that is driven with the whirlwind" → "like the chaff that swirls from the threshing floor"** (v3) — modernized; "swirls" is more vivid than "driven with whirlwind."
- **"as the smoke out of the chimney" → "like smoke from a window"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּעַד אֲרֻבָּה *be'ad arubbah* = through a lattice/window; "window" rather than "chimney."
- **"According to their pasture, so were they filled" → "when they had grazed, they became full"** (v6) — modernized.
- **"as a leopard by the way will I observe them" → "like a leopard I will lurk beside the way"** (v7) — Hebrew עַל דֶּרֶךְ אֶשּׁוּר; "lurk" is more vivid than "observe."
- **"rend the caul of their heart" → "tear open their breast"** (v8) — Hebrew סְגוֹר לִבָּם *segor libbam* = "enclosure of their heart"; "breast" is more natural.
- **"O Israel, thou hast destroyed thyself; but in me is thine help" → "He destroys you, O Israel, for you are against me, against your helper"** (v9) — Hebrew שִׁחֶתְךָ יִשְׂרָאֵל כִּי בִי בְעֶזְרֶךָ; the subject can be "It destroys you" or "He destroys you" — Yahweh is the helper; restructured.
- **"I will be thy king" → "Where now is your king"** (v10) — Hebrew אֱהִי מַלְכְּךָ; rhetorical question; "Where is your king?" follows the interrogative context.
- **"his sin is hid" → "his sin is kept in store"** (v12) — Hebrew צְפוּנָה חַטָּאתוֹ = "stored up"; "in store" more vivid.
- **"for he should not stay long in the place of the breaking forth of children" → "for at the proper time he does not present himself at the opening of the womb"** (v13) — modernized; the child refuses to be born = stubborn refusal to repent.
- **"the grave" → "Sheol"** (v14) — Hebrew שְׁאוֹל *Sheol*; the underworld; *Sheol* retained throughout.
- **"O death, I will be thy plagues; O grave, I will be thy destruction" → "O death, where are your plagues? O Sheol, where is your sting?"** (v14) — Hebrew אֶהְיֶה דְּבָרֶיךָ מָוֶת אֶהְיֶה קָטָבְךָ שְׁאוֹל; the Hebrew is ambiguous: could be threat against Sheol ("I will be your plagues") or ironic taunt ("where are your plagues?"); 1 Cor 15:55 reads the LXX as a taunt; translation follows NT/LXX reading.
- **"repentance shall be hid from mine eyes" → "Compassion is hidden from my eyes"** (v14) — Hebrew נִחוּם *nichum* = compassion/consolation; not "repentance" (the divine relenting is withdrawn).
- **"fruitful among his brethren" → "flourish among his brothers"** (v15) — Hebrew פָּרָא *para'* = "be fruitful."
- **"whose women with child shall be ripped up" → "their pregnant women ripped open"** (v16) — modernized phrasing.

### Notes
- **v14** — "O death, where is your sting?" quoted 1 Cor 15:55.
