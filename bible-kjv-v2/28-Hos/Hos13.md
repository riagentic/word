# Hosea 13

[1] When ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ spoke — trembling;
he was lifted up in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
and he became guilty through the Baal and died.

[2] And now they sin yet more,
and they have made for themselves a molten image of their silver,
according to their understanding, idols,
the work of craftsmen, all of it;
of them they say,
"Sacrificers of man kiss calves."

[3] Therefore they shall be like a cloud of morning,
and like the dew that goes away early,
like chaff that is stormed away from a threshing floor,
and like smoke from a window.

[4] And I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧
from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧;
and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ besides me you do not know,
and a savior there is none but me.

[5] I, I knew you in the wilderness,
in a land of drought.

[6] According to their pasture, so they were sated;
they were sated, and their heart was lifted up;
therefore they forgot me.

[7] And I am to them like a lion;
like a leopard by the way I will watch.

[8] I will meet them like a bereaved bear,
and I will tear the enclosure of their heart,
and I will devour them there like a lioness;
the living thing of the field shall split them.

[9] It has ruined you, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
for against me, against your help.

[10] Where is your king then,
that he may save you in all your cities,
and your judges, of whom you said,
"Give me a king and princes"?

[11] I give you a king in my anger,
and I take him in my rage.

[12] Bound up is the iniquity of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧;
stored up is his sin.

[13] The pangs of a woman giving birth shall come to him;
he is a son not wise,
for it is time, and he does not stand
in the breaking forth of sons.

[14] **From the hand of Sheol I will ransom them;
from death I will redeem them.
Where are your pestilences, death?
Where is your destruction, Sheol?**
Relenting shall be hidden from my eyes.

[15] For he, among brothers, bears fruit;
an east wind shall come, the wind of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
from the wilderness going up,
and his fountain shall be dried out,
and his spring shall dry up;
he, he shall pillage the treasury
of every vessel of desire.

[16] ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ shall be guilty,
for she has rebelled against her ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
by the sword they shall fall;
their infants shall be dashed in pieces,
and his pregnant women shall be split open.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4, 16) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **Layout** — poetry, one Hebrew line per printed line, as the accents divide them.
- **"When Ephraim spake trembling, he exalted himself in Israel; but when he offended in Baal, he died" → "When Ephraim spoke — trembling; he was lifted up in Israel; and he became guilty through the Baal and died"** (v1) — the verse is hard and is kept as the MT has it. כְּדַבֵּר "when (Ephraim) spoke"; רְתֵת "trembling" (found only here: the trembling of others before him, or his own; uncertain); נָשָׂא הוּא "he lifted up / was lifted up" (no object written; "himself" not supplied); אָשֵׁם "become guilty" (its row); בַּבַּעַל "through the Baal" (with the article); וַיָּמֹת "and died"; no "but".
- **"And now they sin more and more, and have made them molten images of their silver, and idols according to their own understanding, all of it the work of the craftsmen" → "And now they sin yet more, and they have made for themselves a molten image of their silver, according to their understanding, idols, the work of craftsmen, all of it"** (v2) — יוֹסִפוּ לַחֲטֹא, literally "they add to sin" (the יָסַף row); מַסֵּכָה "molten image" (its row), singular; כִּתְבוּנָם "according to their understanding"; עֲצַבִּים "idols" (as 8:4); חָרָשִׁים "craftsmen" (as 8:6); כֻּלֹּה "all of it", last. **"they say of them, Let the men that sacrifice kiss the calves" → "of them they say, 'Sacrificers of man kiss calves'"** — לָהֶם הֵם אֹמְרִים "to / of them they say"; זֹבְחֵי אָדָם "sacrificers of man": men who sacrifice, or those who sacrifice men (uncertain; both in the Hebrew); עֲגָלִים יִשָּׁקוּן "calves they kiss" (imperfect; not "let"; נָשַׁק "kiss", its row).
- **"Therefore they shall be as the morning cloud, and as the early dew that passeth away" → "Therefore they shall be like a cloud of morning, and like the dew that goes away early"** (v3) — עֲנַן־בֹּקֶר "a cloud of morning" (as 6:4); מַשְׁכִּים הֹלֵךְ "rising early, going". **"as the chaff that is driven with the whirlwind out of the floor, and as the smoke out of the chimney" → "like chaff that is stormed away from a threshing floor, and like smoke from a window"** — מֹץ "chaff"; יְסֹעֵר "is stormed away" (verb of סְעָרָה "storm"); גֹּרֶן "threshing floor" (its row); אֲרֻבָּה "window" (its row: a latticed opening; the smoke went out by it).
- **"Yet I am the LORD thy God from the land of Egypt, and thou shalt know no god but me: for there is no saviour beside me" → "And I am Yahweh your Elohim from the land of Egypt; and Elohim besides me you do not know, and a savior there is none but me"** (v4) — וְאָנֹכִי "and I" (no "yet"); וֵאלֹהִים זוּלָתִי לֹא תֵדָע "and Elohim besides me you do not know" (the token kept for אֱלֹהִים; "a god" or "gods" is the sense here: the Hebrew word is the same); תֵדָע imperfect (also "you shall not know"); מוֹשִׁיעַ "savior" (participle of יָשַׁע "save", its row); וּ "and" (not "for"); בִּלְתִּי "but me".
- **"I did know thee in the wilderness, in the land of great drought" → "I, I knew you in the wilderness, in a land of drought"** (v5) — אֲנִי stressed; יָדַע "know" (its row); מִדְבָּר "wilderness" (its row); תַּלְאֻבוֹת "drought" (found only here; "great" is not written).
- **"According to their pasture, so were they filled; they were filled, and their heart was exalted; therefore have they forgotten me" → "According to their pasture, so they were sated; they were sated, and their heart was lifted up; therefore they forgot me"** (v6) — כְּמַרְעִיתָם "according to their pasture" (noun of רָעָה "shepherd"); שָׂבַע "be sated" (its row), twice; רוּם "be lifted up".
- **"Therefore I will be unto them as a lion: as a leopard by the way will I observe them" → "And I am to them like a lion; like a leopard by the way I will watch"** (v7) — וָאֱהִי, the short form of "I am / became" (the same letters as אֱהִי in v10, 14: see there); no "therefore"; שַׁחַל "lion" (a poetic word; kind uncertain; beside לָבִיא "lioness" v8); נָמֵר "leopard"; אָשׁוּר "I will watch" (שׁוּר its row; no "them"); the word sounds like אַשּׁוּר "Assyria".
- **"I will meet them as a bear that is bereaved of her whelps, and will rend the caul of their heart, and there will I devour them like a lion: the wild beast shall tear them" → "I will meet them like a bereaved bear, and I will tear the enclosure of their heart, and I will devour them there like a lioness; the living thing of the field shall split them"** (v8) — פָּגַשׁ "meet"; דֹּב שַׁכּוּל "a bereaved bear" (the שָׁכֹל row: "of her whelps" is not written); קָרַע "tear"; סְגוֹר לִבָּם "the enclosure of their heart" (the breast); לָבִיא "lioness" (its row); חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה "the living thing of the field" (the חַיָּה row: "wild" is not written); בָּקַע "split" (its row).
- **"O Israel, thou hast destroyed thyself; but in me is thine help" → "It has ruined you, Israel, for against me, against your help"** (v9) — שִׁחֶתְךָ, piel perfect, third person: "it (or he) has ruined you" (שִׁחֵת its row; "thyself" is not written); no "O"; כִּי־בִי בְעֶזְרֶךָ, literally "for in me, in your help": בְּ "against" (you turned against me, your help), no verb; עֵזֶר "help" (its row).
- **"I will be thy king: where is any other that may save thee in all thy cities? and thy judges of whom thou saidst, Give me a king and princes?" → "Where is your king then, that he may save you in all your cities, and your judges, of whom you said, 'Give me a king and princes'?"** (v10) — אֱהִי taken as "where" (as אַיֵּה, with אֵפוֹא "then"); the same letters are the verb "I will be" (KJV "I will be thy king"; v7 has the verb): uncertain; "any other" is not written; שֹׁפְטִים "judges" (its row); שָׂרִים "princes" (its row).
- **"I gave thee a king in mine anger, and took him away in my wrath" → "I give you a king in my anger, and I take him in my rage"** (v11) — אֶתֶּן, אֶקַּח imperfects (what I do, or what I did again and again); "away" is not written; אַף "anger" (its row); עֶבְרָה "rage" (literally "overflowing"; noun of the הִתְעַבֵּר row).
- **"The iniquity of Ephraim is bound up; his sin is hid" → "Bound up is the iniquity of Ephraim; stored up is his sin"** (v12) — the participles first, as the Hebrew; צָפוּן "stored up" (צָפַן "hide", its row: hidden away to be kept).
- **"The sorrows of a travailing woman shall come upon him: he is an unwise son; for he should not stay long in the place of the breaking forth of children" → "The pangs of a woman giving birth shall come to him; he is a son not wise, for it is time, and he does not stand in the breaking forth of sons"** (v13) — חֶבְלֵי יוֹלֵדָה "the pangs of one giving birth"; לוֹ "to him"; חָכָם "wise"; כִּי־עֵת לֹא־יַעֲמֹד "for (it is) time, he does not stand" (or "for at the time he does not stand": uncertain); מִשְׁבַּר בָּנִים "the breaking forth of sons" (the mouth of the womb; בָּנִים "sons", its row).
- **"I will ransom them from the power of the grave; I will redeem them from death" → "From the hand of Sheol I will ransom them; from death I will redeem them"** (v14) — מִיַּד "from the hand of"; שְׁאוֹל "Sheol" (its row; not "the grave"); פָּדָה "ransom", גָּאַל "redeem" (their rows); statements as printed (no question word: some read them as questions, "Shall I ransom them …?", for the verse goes on to judgment). **"O death, I will be thy plagues; O grave, I will be thy destruction" → "Where are your pestilences, death? Where is your destruction, Sheol?"** — אֱהִי as in v10: taken as "where" (the KJV "I will be" reads the verb; both in the letters, uncertain); דְבָרֶיךָ "your pestilences" (דֶּבֶר its row, plural); קָטָבְךָ "your destruction" (קֶטֶב, its row: uncertain); no "O". 1 Cor 15:55 quotes the lines from the Greek ("Where, death, is your victory? Where, death, is your sting?"): the Hebrew is not levelled to it. **"repentance shall be hid from mine eyes" → "Relenting shall be hidden from my eyes"** — נֹחַם "relenting" (as נִחוּמִים 11:8: Yahweh will not relent; or, after "where", compassion hidden from the enemy death; uncertain); סָתַר "hide".
- **"Though he be fruitful among his brethren" → "For he, among brothers, bears fruit"** (v15) — כִּי הוּא "for he"; בֵּן אַחִים "among brothers" (בֵּן for בֵּין; or "a son of brothers"); יַפְרִיא "bears fruit" (spelled with א; a play on אֶפְרַיִם "Ephraim"); "though" is not written. **"an east wind shall come, the wind of the LORD shall come up from the wilderness" → "an east wind shall come, the wind of Yahweh, from the wilderness going up"** — קָדִים "east wind" (as 12:1); עֹלֶה "going up", participle; the verb is not repeated. **"and his spring shall become dry, and his fountain shall be dried up" → "and his fountain shall be dried out, and his spring shall dry up"** — מָקוֹר "fountain", מַעְיָן "spring" (their rows; KJV has them crossed); יָבֵשׁ "be dried out", חָרֵב "dry up" (their rows). **"he shall spoil the treasure of all pleasant vessels" → "he, he shall pillage the treasury of every vessel of desire"** — הוּא stressed (the enemy, or the wind); שָׁסָה "pillage" (its row); אוֹצָר "treasury" (its row); כְּלִי חֶמְדָּה "vessel of desire" (כְּלִי its row; desirable things).
- **"Samaria shall become desolate; for she hath rebelled against her God" → "Samaria shall be guilty, for she has rebelled against her Elohim"** (v16) — תֶּאְשַׁם "shall be guilty" (אָשֵׁם its row; KJV reads שָׁמֵם "be desolate"); מָרָה בְּ "rebel against" (its row). **"their infants shall be dashed in pieces, and their women with child shall be ripped up" → "their infants shall be dashed in pieces, and his pregnant women shall be split open"** — עֹלְלִים "infants"; רֻטַּשׁ "be dashed in pieces" (as 10:14); הָרִיּוֹתָיו "his pregnant women" (masculine "his": the people); בֻּקַּע "be split open" (בָּקַע its row, as v8).

### Notes
- **Numbering** — our 13:16 = Hebrew 14:1.
- **v14** — 1 Cor 15:55 quotes the lines from the Greek.
