# Malachi 2

[1] And now, to you is this commandment, priests.

[2] If you will not hear, and if you will not lay it upon the heart, to give glory to my name, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, then I will send among you the curse, and I will curse your blessings; and indeed I have cursed it, because you are not laying it upon the heart.

[3] Behold, I am rebuking the seed for you, and I will strew dung upon your faces, the dung of your feasts; and one shall carry you to it.

[4] And you shall know that I have sent to you this commandment, for my covenant to be with Levi, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[5] My covenant was with him, the life and the ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, and I gave them to him, fear; and he feared me, and before the face of my name he was dismayed.

[6] A ⟦torah|torah|instruction⟧ of truth was in his mouth, and injustice was not found on his lips; in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ and in uprightness he walked with me, and many he turned back from iniquity.

[7] For the lips of a priest keep knowledge, and ⟦torah|torah|instruction⟧ they seek from his mouth; for the messenger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts is he.

[8] And you, you have turned aside from the way; you have made many stumble in the ⟦torah|torah|instruction⟧; you have ruined the covenant of the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[9] And also I, I have made you despised and low to all the people, according as you are not keeping my ways, and are lifting up faces in the ⟦torah|torah|instruction⟧.

[10] Is there not one father to all of us? Has not one ⟦el|El|God⟧ created us? Why do we deal treacherously, each man with his brother, to profane the covenant of our fathers?

[11] ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ has dealt treacherously, and an abomination has been done in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; for ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ has profaned the holiness of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, which he loves, and has become husband of the daughter of a foreign ⟦el|el|god⟧.

[12] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ cut off, for the man who does it, one who wakes and one who answers, from the tents of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, and one who brings forward an offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[13] And this a second time you do: covering with tears the altar of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, with weeping and groaning, because there is no more turning to the offering, or taking with pleasure from your hand.

[14] And you say, Because of what? Because ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has borne witness between you and the wife of your youth, with whom you have dealt treacherously; and she is your companion and the wife of your covenant.

[15] And not one has done it, and a remnant of spirit is his. And what is the one? Seeking a seed of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. And you shall guard yourselves in your spirit, and with the wife of your youth let him not deal treacherously.

[16] For he hates sending away, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and he covers violence over his garment, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts; and you shall guard yourselves in your spirit, and you shall not deal treacherously.

[17] You have wearied ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with your words. And you say, In what have we wearied? In your saying, Everyone who does evil is good in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and in them he delights; or, Where is the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of the judgment?

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (throughout) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת, plain "of Hosts" (no token).
- **"God" → "*Elohim*" / "El"** (v10, 11, 15, 16, 17) — Hebrew אֵל *El* in v10 and of the foreign god in v11 (small letter, the אֵל נֵכָר row); אֱלֹהִים *Elohim* in v15, 16, 17.
- **Layout** — the quotation marks are dropped (the Hebrew marks none; v1–9 are spoken to the priests, v10–16 move between "we", "you" plural and "you" singular as the Hebrew does).
- **"O ye priests, this commandment is for you" → "to you is this commandment, priests"** (v1) — Hebrew אֲלֵיכֶם הַמִּצְוָה הַזֹּאת הַכֹּהֲנִים, the order kept; no "O"; מִצְוָה "commandment" (its row; "decree" would be another word).
- **"If ye will not hear, and if ye will not lay it to heart, to give glory unto my name … I will even send a curse upon you, and I will curse your blessings: yea, I have cursed them already" → "If you will not hear, and if you will not lay it upon the heart, to give glory … then I will send among you the curse, and I will curse your blessings; and indeed I have cursed it"** (v2) — שָׂם עַל־לֵב "lay upon the heart" (kept, twice); נָתַן כָּבוֹד "give glory" (כָּבוֹד row); שִׁלַּח בְּ "send among"; הַמְּאֵרָה "the curse" with the article (מְאֵרָה, its row; noun of אָרַר, beside which אָרוֹתִי "I will curse" stands); אָרוֹתִיהָ "I have cursed it" (singular: each blessing, or the blessing as one); אֵינְכֶם שָׂמִים participle "you are not laying".
- **"I will corrupt your seed, and spread dung upon your faces, even the dung of your solemn feasts; and one shall take you away with it" → "I am rebuking the seed for you, and I will strew dung upon your faces, the dung of your feasts; and one shall carry you to it"** (v3) — הִנְנִי גֹעֵר "behold, I am rebuking" (participle; גָּעַר "rebuke", its row; KJV "corrupt" follows another reading); לָכֶם "for you"; הַזֶּרַע "the seed" (its row; the Greek and some read זְרֹעַ "the arm", from memory: not followed); זֵרִיתִי "I will strew" (זָרָה row); פֶּרֶשׁ "dung" (the contents of the bowels of the sacrificed beasts, its row); חַגֵּיכֶם "your feasts" (חַג row); וְנָשָׂא אֶתְכֶם אֵלָיו "and one shall carry you to it" (lit. "and he / it shall lift you to it": no subject named; to the dung heap, as the dung is carried out, Lev 4:11–12; uncertain).
- **"that my covenant might be with Levi" → "for my covenant to be with Levi"** (v4) — לִהְיוֹת בְּרִיתִי אֶת־לֵוִי, infinitive "to be"; Levi without the article (the tribe in its father); וִידַעְתֶּם "and you shall know" (weqatal).
- **"My covenant was with him of life and peace; and I gave them to him for the fear wherewith he feared me, and was afraid before my name" → "My covenant was with him, the life and the shalom, and I gave them to him, fear; and he feared me, and before the face of my name he was dismayed"** (v5) — הַחַיִּים וְהַשָּׁלוֹם with articles, in apposition to the covenant; שָׁלוֹם token (the reviewer row); וָאֶתְּנֵם־לוֹ מוֹרָא "and I gave them to him — fear": מוֹרָא (its row) set after "them", either as what was given or as the purpose ("for fear"): left as the Hebrew stands; וַיִּירָאֵנִי "and he feared me" (wayyiqtol); מִפְּנֵי שְׁמִי "before the face of my name" (the מִפְּנֵי row); נִחַת nifal of חָתַת "be dismayed" (its row; lit. "be shattered").
- **"The law of truth was in his mouth, and iniquity was not found in his lips: he walked with me in peace and equity" → "A torah of truth was in his mouth, and injustice was not found on his lips; in shalom and in uprightness he walked with me"** (v6) — תּוֹרַת אֱמֶת "a torah of truth" (token `torah` lower case: the instruction a priest gives, as Hag 2:11; also v7, 8, 9); עַוְלָה "injustice" (the עָוֶל row; עָוֹן "iniquity" at the end of the verse is another word); מִישׁוֹר "uprightness" (lit. "level ground", the tableland of its row; here of conduct, the same English as יֹשֶׁר: note); the order kept; הֵשִׁיב "turned back" (hifil of שׁוּב).
- **"For the priest's lips should keep knowledge, and they should seek the law at his mouth" → "For the lips of a priest keep knowledge, and torah they seek from his mouth"** (v7) — יִשְׁמְרוּ, יְבַקְשׁוּ imperfects: "keep", "seek" (what is due: "should" is a reading of the sense); מַלְאַךְ "messenger" (its row; of a man, not "angel"); the predicate "the messenger of Yahweh of Hosts" first.
- **"ye have caused many to stumble at the law; ye have corrupted the covenant of Levi" → "you have made many stumble in the torah; you have ruined the covenant of the Levite"** (v8) — הִכְשַׁלְתֶּם בַּתּוֹרָה "made stumble in / by the torah"; שִׁחַתֶּם "you have ruined" (the שָׁחַת row); בְּרִית הַלֵּוִי with the article: "the Levite" (token `levites`, as the Deut rows; v4 has "Levi" without it).
- **"Therefore have I also made you contemptible and base before all the people, according as ye have not kept my ways, but have been partial in the law" → "And also I, I have made you despised and low to all the people, according as you are not keeping my ways, and are lifting up faces in the torah"** (v9) — וְגַם־אֲנִי "and also I" (not "therefore"); נָתַתִּי "I have given / made" (perfect); נִבְזִים "despised" (בָּזָה, as 1:6, 7); שְׁפָלִים "low" (its row); כְּפִי אֲשֶׁר "according as" (lit. "according to the mouth that"); participles "are not keeping … are lifting up"; נָשָׂא פָנִים "lift up faces" (the row: show partiality; kept).
- **"Have we not all one father? hath not one God created us? why do we deal treacherously every man against his brother" → "Is there not one father to all of us? Has not one El created us? Why do we deal treacherously, each man with his brother"** (v10) — הֲלוֹא אָב אֶחָד לְכֻלָּנוּ "is not one father to all of us"; whether the father is Yahweh (as 1:6) or Jacob / Abraham is not said: "father" lower case, open; אֵל "El" (token); בָּרָא "create" (its row); נִבְגַּד "deal treacherously" (the בָּגַד בְּ row); אִישׁ בְּאָחִיו "each man with his brother" (its row); לְחַלֵּל "to profane" (חִלֵּל row).
- **"Judah hath dealt treacherously … hath profaned the holiness of the LORD which he loved, and hath married the daughter of a strange god" → "Judah has dealt treacherously … has profaned the holiness of Yahweh, which he loves, and has become husband of the daughter of a foreign el"** (v11) — בָּגְדָה feminine (Judah as a land or people), English "has"; תּוֹעֵבָה "abomination" (its row), נֶעֶשְׂתָה "has been done"; קֹדֶשׁ יְהוָה "the holiness of Yahweh" (קֹדֶשׁ row: holiness / holy thing; the sanctuary, or the people as Yahweh's holy thing, Jer 2:3: open); אֲשֶׁר אָהֵב "which he loves" (perfect of a state; who loves — Yahweh, or Judah — is not said); בָּעַל "become husband of" (its row; lit. "become owner of"); בַּת־אֵל נֵכָר "the daughter of a foreign el" (the אֵל נֵכָר row: a woman of another god's people).
- **"The LORD will cut off the man that doeth this, the master and the scholar, out of the tabernacles of Jacob, and him that offereth an offering" → "May Yahweh cut off, for the man who does it, one who wakes and one who answers, from the tents of Jacob, and one who brings forward an offering"** (v12) — יַכְרֵת jussive "may … cut off" (a wish, not "will"); לָאִישׁ "for / belonging to the man"; עֵר וְעֹנֶה "one who wakes and one who answers": a pair that means "every one" (who belongs to him), the exact sense uncertain (KJV "master and scholar" is a guess; "witness and answerer" by another pointing); אֹהָלִים "tents" (not "tabernacles"); מַגִּישׁ "one who brings forward" (הִגִּישׁ, as 1:7).
- **"And this have ye done again, covering the altar of the LORD with tears … insomuch that he regardeth not the offering any more, or receiveth it with good will at your hand" → "And this a second time you do: covering with tears the altar of Yahweh … because there is no more turning to the offering, or taking with pleasure from your hand"** (v13) — שֵׁנִית "a second time"; תַּעֲשׂוּ imperfect "you do"; כַּסּוֹת infinitive "covering"; דִּמְעָה "tears" (the Hebrew singular, collective); אֲנָקָה "groaning"; מֵאֵין עוֹד פְּנוֹת "because there is no more turning" (infinitive: who turns is not said; "he" of KJV supplied); לָקַחַת רָצוֹן "taking with pleasure" (רָצוֹן, the root of the רָצָה row; lit. "taking pleasure"); מִנְחָה "offering" (as 1:10).
- **"Yet ye say, Wherefore? Because the LORD hath been witness between thee and the wife of thy youth … yet is she thy companion, and the wife of thy covenant" → "And you say, Because of what? Because Yahweh has borne witness between you and the wife of your youth … and she is your companion and the wife of your covenant"** (v14) — עַל־מָה "because of what" answered by עַל כִּי "because" (the one preposition kept); הֵעִיד בֵּין "has borne witness between" (the הֵעִיד row: a formal witness); from here "you" is singular (Czech „ty“); בָּגַדְתָּה בָּהּ "you have dealt treacherously with her"; וְהִיא "and she" (not "yet"); חֲבֶרְתְּךָ "your companion" (feminine; the root of joining, חָבַר); אֵשֶׁת בְּרִיתֶךָ "the wife of your covenant".
- **"And did not he make one? Yet had he the residue of the spirit. And wherefore one? That he might seek a godly seed. Therefore take heed to your spirit, and let none deal treacherously against the wife of his youth" → "And not one has done it, and a remnant of spirit is his. And what is the one? Seeking a seed of Elohim. And you shall guard yourselves in your spirit, and with the wife of your youth let him not deal treacherously"** (v15) — the verse is one of the hardest in the prophets; it is given word for word: וְלֹא־אֶחָד עָשָׂה "and not one has done" (or, as a question, "and did not one make …?", with God or Abraham as "the one"; no object written); וּשְׁאָר רוּחַ לוֹ "and a remnant of spirit is to him" (שְׁאָר "remnant", its row; רוּחַ "spirit", small letter); וּמָה הָאֶחָד "and what is the one?"; מְבַקֵּשׁ זֶרַע אֱלֹהִים "seeking a seed of Elohim" (participle; "godly offspring" interprets); וְנִשְׁמַרְתֶּם בְּרוּחֲכֶם "and you shall guard yourselves in your spirit" (weqatal, a command; also v16); "your youth" singular, יִבְגֹּד third person "let him not deal treacherously" (the subject not named; KJV "none"; the Greek and other versions read "you", from memory). The readings are many; none is put into the verse.
- **"For the LORD, the God of Israel, saith that he hateth putting away: for one covereth violence with his garment" → "For he hates sending away, says Yahweh, the Elohim of Israel, and he covers violence over his garment"** (v16) — כִּי־שָׂנֵא שַׁלַּח: שָׂנֵא as pointed, third person "he hates" (verbal adjective or perfect); שַׁלַּח piel infinitive "sending away" (the שִׁלַּח row of a wife: "send away", not "divorce"). Who hates is not certain: Yahweh (though "says Yahweh" would lead one to expect "I hate"), or the husband ("for one who hates — sending away …"); the pointing שַׁלַּח as an imperative, "if he hates, send her away" (as the Greek and a Qumran scroll are read, from memory), and the emendation "I hate" are not followed; the doubt stands. וְכִסָּה "and he covers" (weqatal; subject as the first clause); חָמָס "violence" (its row); עַל־לְבוּשׁוֹ "over his garment" (the garment as a figure: the covering of violence, or the garment spread over a wife, Ruth 3:9; open). וְלֹא תִבְגֹּדוּ "and you shall not deal treacherously" (plural).
- **"Ye have wearied the LORD with your words. Yet ye say, Wherein have we wearied him? When ye say … Where is the God of judgment?" → "You have wearied Yahweh with your words. And you say, In what have we wearied? In your saying … or, Where is the Elohim of the judgment?"** (v17) — הוֹגַעְתֶּם hifil of יָגַע "weary" (make to labor); הוֹגָעְנוּ without an object ("him" supplied by KJV); בֶּאֱמָרְכֶם "in your saying" (as 1:7, 12); כָּל־עֹשֵׂה רָע "everyone who does evil" (רַע row); חָפֵץ "delights" (the חָפֵץ בְּ row), the object first; אוֹ "or"; אֱלֹהֵי הַמִּשְׁפָּט "the Elohim of the judgment" (מִשְׁפָּט "judgment", its row; article kept).

### Notes
- **v10–16** — the passage joins the faithlessness to the covenant of the fathers (v10), the marrying of a foreign god's daughter (v11) and the treachery against "the wife of your youth" (v14–16); it does not say whether the wife of v14 was sent away for the foreign wife of v11 (compare Ezra 9–10; Neh 13:23–27).
