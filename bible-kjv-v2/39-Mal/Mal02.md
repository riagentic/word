# Malachi 2

[1] "And now, O priests, this command is for you.

[2] If you will not listen, if you will not take it to heart to give honor to my name, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, then I will send the curse upon you and I will curse your blessings. Indeed, I have already cursed them, because you do not lay it to heart.

[3] Behold, I will rebuke your offspring, and spread dung on your faces, the dung of your offerings, and you shall be taken away with it.

[4] So shall you know that I have sent this command to you, that my covenant with Levi may stand, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[5] "My covenant with him was one of life and peace, and I gave them to him. It was a covenant of fear, and he feared me. He stood in awe of my name.

[6] True ⟦torah|Torah|instruction⟧ was in his mouth, and no wrong was found on his lips. He walked with me in peace and uprightness, and he turned many from iniquity.

[7] For the lips of a priest should guard knowledge, and people should seek ⟦torah|Torah|instruction⟧ from his mouth, for he is the messenger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[8] But you have turned aside from the way. You have caused many to stumble by your ⟦torah|Torah|instruction⟧. You have corrupted the covenant of Levi, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts,

[9] and so I make you despised and abased before all the people, inasmuch as you do not keep my ways but show partiality in your ⟦torah|Torah|instruction⟧."

[10] Have we not all one Father? Has not one ⟦el|El|God⟧ created us? Why then are we faithless to one another, profaning the covenant of our fathers?

[11] ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ has been faithless, and abomination has been committed in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. For ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ has profaned the sanctuary of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, which he loves, and has married the daughter of a foreign god.

[12] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ cut off from the tents of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ any descendant of the man who does this, who brings an offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts!

[13] And this second thing you do. You cover ⟦yhwh|Yahweh's|the LORD's⟧ altar with tears, with weeping and groaning because he no longer regards the offering or accepts it with favor from your hand.

[14] But you say, "Why does he not?" Because ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was witness between you and the wife of your youth, to whom you have been faithless, though she is your companion and your wife by covenant.

[15] Did he not make them one, with a portion of the Spirit in their union? And what was the one ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ seeking? Godly offspring. So guard yourselves in your spirit, and let none be faithless to the wife of his youth.

[16] "For the man who does not love his wife but divorces her, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, covers his garment with violence, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts. So guard yourselves in your spirit, and do not be faithless."

[17] You have wearied ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with your words. But you say, "How have we wearied him?" By saying, "Everyone who does evil is good in the sight of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he delights in them." Or by asking, "Where is the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of justice?"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (throughout) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת *Yahweh Tzeva'ot*; the military-covenantal divine title preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*" / "El"** (vv15, 16, 17; v10) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*, and אֵל *El* in v10 ("Has not one El created us?"); "what was the one *Elohim* seeking?" and "the *Elohim* of Israel" and "the *Elohim* of justice."
