# 2 Thessalonians 3

[1] Finally, pray, brothers, for us, that the word of the Lord may run and be glorified, even as also with you,

[2] and that we may be delivered from perverse and evil men; for not all have faith.

[3] But the Lord is faithful, who will establish you and guard you from the evil one.

[4] And we have confidence in the Lord about you, that what we command, you both do and will do.

[5] And may the Lord direct your hearts into the love of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and into the endurance of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[6] Now we command you, brothers, in the name of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, to keep away from every brother walking disorderly and not according to the tradition which they received from us.

[7] For you yourselves know how you ought to imitate us, because we were not disorderly among you,

[8] nor did we eat bread from anyone for nothing, but in labor and toil, working night and day so as not to burden any of you;

[9] not that we do not have the right, but that we might give ourselves to you as a pattern, for you to imitate us.

[10] For also when we were with you, this we used to command you: "**If anyone is not willing to work, neither let him eat.**"

[11] For we hear of some walking among you disorderly, working not at all, but being busybodies.

[12] Now such ones we command and exhort in the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, that working with quietness they eat their own bread.

[13] But you, brothers, do not grow weary in doing good.

[14] And if anyone does not obey our word through the letter, mark this one, not to mix with him, that he may be put to shame;

[15] and do not regard him as an enemy, but admonish him as a brother.

[16] Now may the Lord of peace himself give you peace always in every way. The Lord be with you all.

[17] The greeting of ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ with my own hand, which is a sign in every letter; so I write.

[18] The grace of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ be with you all.

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (vv6, 12, 18) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v5) — systematic.
- **"may have free course" → "may run"** (v1) — Greek τρέχῃ; "be glorified" (δοξάζηται) kept. "Even as also with you" has no verb in Greek.
- **"unreasonable and wicked men" → "perverse and evil men"** (v2) — Greek ἀτόπων, literally "out of place"; πονηρῶν is the same word as "the evil one" in v3.
- **"for all men have not faith" → "for not all have faith"** (v2) — Greek οὐ γὰρ πάντων ἡ πίστις, literally "for the faith is not of all"; with the article it can be "faith" or "the faith". "Faithful" (πιστός, v3) plays on it.
- **"keep you from evil" → "guard you from the evil one"** (v3) — Greek ἀπὸ τοῦ πονηροῦ: masculine ("the evil one") or neuter ("evil"); the form does not decide.
- **"that ye both do and will do" kept** (v4) — Greek καὶ ποιεῖτε καὶ ποιήσετε, "both … and".
- **"patient waiting for Christ" → "endurance of the *Mashiach*"** (v5) — Greek τὴν ὑπομονὴν τοῦ Χριστοῦ, a plain genitive like "the love of *Elohim*": his endurance, or endurance directed to him. ὑπομονή as in 1:4.
- **"walketh disorderly" kept** (v6, 7, 11) — Greek ἀτάκτως, ἠτακτήσαμεν: "out of order, out of rank", not "idle".
- **"which he received" → "which they received"** (v6) — παρελάβοσαν, "they received": SBLGNT NA28 (RP παρέλαβον, also "they received"); παρελάβετε, "you received": WH Treg NIV. The verse follows "they" (2–2, NA28 decides).
- **"for nought" → "for nothing"** (v8) — Greek δωρεάν, "as a gift".
- **"wrought with labour and travail night and day" → "in labor and toil, working night and day"** (v8) — Greek ἐργαζόμενοι, a participle.
- **"power" → "the right"** (v9) — Greek ἐξουσίαν, "authority, right".
- **"ensample" → "pattern"** (v9) — Greek τύπον, as Titus 2:7.
- **"we commanded" → "we used to command"** (v10) — Greek παρηγγέλλομεν, imperfect; the same verb as "command" in v4, 6, 12.
- **"working not at all, but are busybodies" kept** (v11) — a wordplay: ἐργαζομένους … περιεργαζομένους, "working … working around".
- **"exhort by our Lord Jesus Christ" → "exhort in the Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v12) — Greek ἐν κυρίῳ Ἰησοῦ Χριστῷ. παρακαλοῦμεν: "exhort" here, beside "command"; the same verb is "encourage" in 2:17 (of hearts).
- **"with quietness they work, and eat their own bread" kept** (v12) — Greek μετὰ ἡσυχίας ἐργαζόμενοι τὸν ἑαυτῶν ἄρτον ἐσθίωσιν; "to earn their own living" is not in the Greek.
- **"our word by this epistle" → "our word through the letter"** (v14) — Greek τῷ λόγῳ ἡμῶν διὰ τῆς ἐπιστολῆς.
- **"note that man, and have no company with him" → "mark this one, not to mix with him"** (v14) — NA28 μὴ συναναμίγνυσθαι, an infinitive without "and"; "mix together with".
- **"that he may be ashamed" → "that he may be put to shame"** (v14) — Greek ἐντραπῇ, passive; as Titus 2:8.
- **"Yet count him not" → "and do not regard him"** (v15) — Greek καί.
- **"by all means" → "in every way"** (v16) — Greek ἐν παντὶ τρόπῳ; διὰ παντός, "always, through all".
- **"The salutation of Paul with mine own hand, which is the token in every epistle" kept as "The greeting of *Paulus* with my own hand, which is a sign in every letter"** (v17) — Greek Ὁ ἀσπασμὸς τῇ ἐμῇ χειρὶ Παύλου, ὅ ἐστιν σημεῖον: no verb "I write this", no "of genuineness", no "of mine". σημεῖον is the same word as "signs" in 2:9.
- **"Amen" omitted** (v18) — not in NA28.
