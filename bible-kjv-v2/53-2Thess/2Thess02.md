# 2 Thessalonians 2

[1] Now we request you, brothers, concerning the coming of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ and our gathering together unto him,

[2] that you be not quickly shaken from your mind, nor be alarmed, neither through a spirit nor through a word nor through a letter as through us, as that the day of the Lord is present.

[3] Let no one deceive you in any way; because unless the apostasy comes first and **the man of lawlessness is revealed, the son of destruction**,

[4] he who opposes and exalts himself above all that is called god or object of worship, so that he sits down in the sanctuary of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, showing himself forth that he is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ —

[5] Do you not remember that, being yet with you, I was telling you these things?

[6] And now you know what restrains, that he may be revealed in his own time.

[7] For the mystery of lawlessness is already working; only there is he who restrains at present, until he comes to be out of the midst.

[8] And then the lawless one will be revealed, whom the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ will kill with the breath of his mouth and bring to nothing by the appearing of his coming,

[9] whose coming is according to the working of Satan in all power and signs and wonders of falsehood,

[10] and in all deceit of unrighteousness for those who are perishing, because they did not receive the love of the truth so as to be saved.

[11] And because of this ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ sends them a working of error, that they should believe the falsehood,

[12] that they all may be judged who did not believe the truth but took pleasure in unrighteousness.

[13] But we ought to give thanks to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ always for you, brothers beloved by the Lord, because ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ chose you as firstfruits unto salvation in sanctification of the Spirit and faith in the truth,

[14] unto which he called you through our gospel, unto the obtaining of the glory of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[15] So then, brothers, stand firm, and hold fast the traditions which you were taught, whether through word or through our letter.

[16] Now may our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ himself, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ our Father, who loved us and gave eternal encouragement and good hope in grace,

[17] encourage your hearts and establish them in every good work and word.

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (vv1, 8, 14, 16) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv4, 11, 13, 16) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v8) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*; Ἰησοῦς: SBLGNT WH Treg NA28; RP omits it ("the Lord").
- **"we beseech you … by the coming" → "we request you … concerning the coming"** (v1) — Greek Ἐρωτῶμεν … ὑπέρ; the request stands first. "Coming" is παρουσία, "presence, arrival" (also v8, 9).
- **"shaken in mind" → "shaken from your mind"** (v2) — Greek ἀπὸ τοῦ νοός.
- **"by spirit, nor by word, nor by letter as from us" → "through a spirit nor through a word nor through a letter as through us"** (v2) — Greek διά each time, also ὡς διʼ ἡμῶν. "As through us" can belong to the letter only or to all three; there is no "seeming" and no "spoken".
- **"the day of Christ is at hand" → "the day of the Lord is present"** (v2) — NA28 τοῦ κυρίου; ἐνέστηκεν, perfect of "to be present, to have set in" (as "things present", Rom 8:38), not "is near".
- **"for that day shall not come, except" → "because unless"** (v3) — the Greek sentence has no main clause: it breaks off after v4. The words "that day will not come" are supplied from v2 by the versions; the verse leaves the break.
- **"a falling away" → "the apostasy"** (v3) — Greek ἡ ἀποστασία with the article: a standing away, defection, revolt (political or religious).
- **"man of sin" → "man of lawlessness"** (v3) — NA28 ἀνομίας; other MSS ἁμαρτίας, "of sin". "Lawlessness" (v7) and "the lawless one" (v8) are the same root.
- **"perdition" → "destruction"** (v3) — Greek ἀπωλείας, the noun of "those who are perishing" (v10); a different word from ὄλεθρος (1:9).
- **"above all that is called God, or that is worshipped" → "above all that is called god or object of worship"** (v4) — Greek ἐπὶ πάντα λεγόμενον θεὸν ἢ σέβασμα; ἐπί can be "above" or "against".
- **"he as God sitteth in the temple of God, shewing himself that he is God" → "he sits down in the sanctuary of *Elohim*, showing himself forth that he is *Elohim*"** (v4) — NA28 has no "as God". ναός "sanctuary" is the temple building itself ("temple" is ἱερόν); καθίσαι εἰς, "to sit down into". ἀποδεικνύντα, "showing forth, exhibiting", not "proclaiming". The last θεός has no article: "is *Elohim*" or "is a god".
- **"I told you" → "I was telling you"** (v5) — Greek ἔλεγον, imperfect.
- **"what withholdeth" → "what restrains"** (v6, 7) — Greek τὸ κατέχον (neuter, v6), ὁ κατέχων (masculine, v7): "hold back" or "hold fast". The Greek gives no object ("him", "it"). "Now" (νῦν) stands first and can go with "you know" or with "what restrains".
- **"doth already work" → "is already working"** (v7) — Greek ἐνεργεῖται; the noun ἐνέργεια is "working" in v9, 11.
- **"only he who now letteth will let, until he be taken out of the way" → "only there is he who restrains at present, until he comes to be out of the midst"** (v7) — Greek μόνον ὁ κατέχων ἄρτι ἕως ἐκ μέσου γένηται: the clause has no main verb (only "there is" is supplied) and no "taken": γένηται, "comes to be".
- **"that Wicked" → "the lawless one"** (v8) — Greek ὁ ἄνομος.
- **"consume with the spirit of his mouth" → "kill with the breath of his mouth"** (v8) — NA28 ἀνελεῖ, "do away with, kill"; πνεῦμα is "breath" or "spirit", the same word as in v2, 13.
- **"destroy with the brightness" → "bring to nothing by the appearing"** (v8) — Greek καταργήσει, "make idle, void"; ἐπιφανείᾳ, "appearing" (as Titus 2:13).
- **"after the working of Satan with all power and signs and lying wonders" → "according to the working of Satan in all power and signs and wonders of falsehood"** (v9) — Greek κατʼ ἐνέργειαν; ψεύδους, a noun, "of falsehood", which can go with all three; the same word as "the falsehood" in v11.
- **"deceivableness of unrighteousness in them that perish" → "deceit of unrighteousness for those who are perishing"** (v10) — NA28 ἀπάτῃ ἀδικίας τοῖς ἀπολλυμένοις, a dative without "in".
- **"received not the love of the truth, that they might be saved" kept** (v10) — Greek οὐκ ἐδέξαντο, "did not receive / welcome", not "refused to love".
- **"shall send them strong delusion" → "sends them a working of error"** (v11) — NA28 πέμπει, present; ἐνέργειαν πλάνης: ἐνέργεια as in v9, πλάνη "a going astray, error".
- **"damned" → "judged"** (v12) — Greek κριθῶσιν.
- **"from the beginning" → "as firstfruits"** (v13) — ἀπαρχήν, "firstfruits": SBLGNT NA28; ἀπʼ ἀρχῆς, "from the beginning": WH Treg NIV RP. The verse follows ἀπαρχήν (2–2, NA28 decides).
- **"through sanctification of the Spirit and belief of the truth" → "in sanctification of the Spirit and faith in the truth"** (v13) — Greek ἐν ἁγιασμῷ πνεύματος καὶ πίστει ἀληθείας, two genitives without articles; πνεύματος can be the Spirit who sanctifies or the spirit that is sanctified. πίστις is "faith" throughout the letter.
- **"Whereunto he called you" → "unto which he called you"** (v14) — εἰς ὃ ἐκάλεσεν: SBLGNT WH Treg RP; εἰς ὃ καὶ ἐκάλεσεν, "unto which he also called you": NA28.
- **"to the obtaining of the glory" kept** (v14) — Greek εἰς περιποίησιν δόξης, a noun.
- **"whether by word, or our epistle" → "whether through word or through our letter"** (v15) — Greek εἴτε διὰ λόγου εἴτε διʼ ἐπιστολῆς ἡμῶν; "our" stands last and can cover both. There is no "by us".
- **"everlasting consolation … comfort your hearts" → "eternal encouragement … encourage your hearts"** (v16, 17) — Greek παράκλησιν, παρακαλέσαι, one root; the same verb is "exhort" in 3:12, where it stands beside "command". The Greek has no "us" after "gave".
- **"through grace" → "in grace"** (v16) — Greek ἐν χάριτι.
- **"in every good word and work" → "in every good work and word"** (v17) — NA28 order ἔργῳ καὶ λόγῳ; "them" is supplied (NA28 has no object after "establish").

### Notes
- **v3–4** — the broken sentence (anacoluthon) is in the Greek.
- **v8** echoes Isa 11:4.
- **v16–17** — two subjects, but the verbs "encourage" and "establish" are singular in Greek.
