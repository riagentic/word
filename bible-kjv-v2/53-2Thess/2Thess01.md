## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" / "Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*" / "*Mashiach*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (vv7, 8, 12) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
- **"our Father" kept** (v2) — πατρὸς ἡμῶν, "our Father": Treg RP NA28; πατρός without "our" ("from *Elohim* the Father"): SBLGNT WH NIV. The verse follows the reading with "our" (2–2, NA28 decides).
- **"as it is meet" → "as is fitting"** (v3) — Greek ἄξιον, "worthy"; the same root as "counted worthy" (v5 καταξιωθῆναι) and "count worthy" (v11 ἀξιώσῃ).
- **"so that we ourselves glory in you" → "so that we ourselves boast in you"** (v4) — Greek ὥστε with the infinitive, a result that continues v3; ἐν ὑμῖν, "in you".
- **"patience" → "endurance"** (v4) — Greek ὑπομονή; also 3:5. "Bear" (ἀνέχεσθε) is a different verb.
- **"a manifest token" → "a proof"** (v5) — Greek ἔνδειγμα stands alone, without "which is" / "this is"; it stands in apposition to what goes before.
- **"seeing it is a righteous thing" → "if indeed it is righteous"** (v6) — Greek εἴπερ, "if indeed"; δίκαιον is the same word as "righteous" in v5. "Vengeance" (v8 ἐκδίκησις) and "penalty" (v9 δίκη) are from the same root.
- **"rest" → "relief"** (v7) — Greek ἄνεσις, a loosening, release from pressure; "to grant" is not in the Greek, the verb is still "repay" (v6).
- **"when the Lord Jesus shall be revealed" → "at the revelation of the Lord *Yeshua*"** (v7) — Greek ἐν τῇ ἀποκαλύψει, a noun.
- **"his mighty angels" → "angels of his power"** (v7) — Greek ἀγγέλων δυνάμεως αὐτοῦ; "his" belongs to "power".
- **"in flaming fire" → "in a fire of flame"** (v8) — ἐν πυρὶ φλογός, "in a fire of flame": WH NA28 RP; ἐν φλογὶ πυρός, "in a flame of fire": SBLGNT Treg. Two nouns either way; the sense is the same.
- **"taking vengeance on" → "giving vengeance to"** (v8) — Greek διδόντος ἐκδίκησιν with the dative; the participle (genitive) agrees with "the Lord *Yeshua*" (v7), not with the fire.
- **"who shall be punished with everlasting destruction from the presence" → "who will pay the penalty, eternal ruin from the face"** (v9) — Greek δίκην τίσουσιν, "will pay a penalty"; ὄλεθρον stands in apposition. ἀπὸ προσώπου, "from the face": "from" can mean "away from" or "coming from"; the verse adds no "away". ὄλεθρος, "ruin", is a different word from ἀπώλεια, "destruction" (2:3).
- **"power" → "strength"** (v9) — Greek ἰσχύος; "power" is δύναμις (v7, 11).
- **"in all them that believe" → "in all who believed"** (v10) — Greek πιστεύσασιν, aorist; ἐν as in "in his saints". "In that day" stands last in the Greek.
- **"Wherefore" → "Unto which"** (v11) — Greek εἰς ὅ.
- **"count you worthy of this calling" → "count you worthy of the calling"** (v11) — Greek ἀξιώσῃ, "deem worthy", not "make worthy"; τῆς κλήσεως has no "his".
- **"all the good pleasure of his goodness, and the work of faith with power" → "every good pleasure of goodness and work of faith in power"** (v11) — Greek πᾶσαν εὐδοκίαν ἀγαθωσύνης καὶ ἔργον πίστεως ἐν δυνάμει. There is no "his": the good pleasure and the goodness can be *Elohim*'s or the readers'. "Every" (feminine) belongs to "good pleasure" only. εὐδοκία is the noun of "took pleasure" (2:12).
- **"our God and the Lord Jesus Christ" → "our *Elohim* and Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v12) — Greek τοῦ θεοῦ ἡμῶν καὶ κυρίου Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ: one article. It can be read of two (κύριος often has no article, as in v1, 2) or of one; the verse adds no second article.

### Notes
- **v3–10** — one sentence in Greek.
