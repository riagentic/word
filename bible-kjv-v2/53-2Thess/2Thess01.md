# 2 Thessalonians 1

[1] ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, Silvanus, and ⟦timothy|Timotheos|Timothy⟧,

To the church of the ⟦thessalonians|Thessalonikeis|Thessalonians⟧ in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ our Father and the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧:

[2] Grace to you and peace from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ our Father and the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[3] We ought always to give thanks to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for you, brothers, as is right, because your faith is growing abundantly, and the love of every one of you for one another is increasing.

[4] Therefore we ourselves boast about you in the churches of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for your steadfastness and faith in all your persecutions and in the afflictions that you are enduring.

[5] This is evidence of the righteous judgment of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, that you may be considered worthy of the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for which you are also suffering —

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" / "Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*" / "*Mashiach*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (vv7, 8, 12) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
