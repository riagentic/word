# James 4

[1] What causes quarrels and what causes fights among you? Is it not this, that your passions are at war within you?

[2] You desire and do not have, so you murder. You covet and cannot obtain, so you fight and quarrel. You do not have, because you do not ask.

[3] You ask and do not receive, because you ask wrongly, to spend it on your passions.

[4] You adulterous people! Do you not know that friendship with the world is enmity with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧? Therefore whoever wishes to be a friend of the world makes himself an enemy of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[5] Or do you suppose it is to no purpose that the Scripture says, "He yearns jealously over the spirit that he has made to dwell in us"?

[6] But he gives more grace. Therefore it says, "**⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble**."

[7] **Submit yourselves therefore to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.**

[8] **Draw near to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and he will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners, and purify your hearts, you double-minded.**

[9] Be wretched and mourn and weep. Let your laughter be turned to mourning and your joy to gloom.

[10] Humble yourselves before the Lord, and he will exalt you.

[11] Do not speak evil against one another, brothers. The one who speaks against a brother or judges his brother, speaks evil against the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ and judges the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧. But if you judge the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, you are not a doer of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ but a judge.

[12] There is only one lawgiver and judge, he who is able to save and to destroy. But who are you to judge your neighbor?

[13] Come now, you who say, "Today or tomorrow we will go into such and such a town and spend a year there and trade and make a profit" —

[14] yet you do not know what tomorrow will bring. What is your life? For you are a mist that appears for a little time and then vanishes.

[15] Instead you ought to say, "If the Lord wills, we will live and do this or that."

[16] As it is, you boast in your arrogance. All such boasting is evil.

[17] So whoever knows the right thing to do and fails to do it, for him it is sin.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"your lusts that war in your members" → "your passions are at war within you"** (v1) — Greek ἐκ τῶν ἡδονῶν ὑμῶν τῶν στρατευομένων ἐν τοῖς μέλεσιν ὑμῶν *ek tōn hēdonōn hymōn tōn strateuomenōn en tois melesin hymōn*; "lusts" → "passions"; "members" → "within you."
- **"ye lust, and have not … ye fight and war, yet ye have not, because ye ask not" → "You desire and do not have … you fight and quarrel. You do not have, because you do not ask"** (v2) — modernized.
- **"adulterers and adulteresses!" → "You adulterous people!"** (v4) — Greek μοιχαλίδες *moichalides* = "adulteresses" (feminine plural); used metaphorically for spiritual unfaithfulness to God (the OT prophetic image of Israel as unfaithful wife). Translation uses the inclusive "people."
- **"God resisteth the proud, but giveth grace unto the humble" → "*Elohim* opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble"** (v6) — OT quotation (Prov 3:34 LXX); "God" → "*Elohim*"; "resisteth" → "opposes."
- **"Draw nigh to God, and he will draw nigh to you" → "Draw near to *Elohim*, and he will draw near to you"** (v8) — "God" → "*Elohim*"; "nigh" → "near."
- **"Be afflicted, and mourn, and weep" → "Be wretched and mourn and weep"** (v9) — Greek ταλαιπωρήσατε *talaipōrēsate* = "be miserable / wretched"; "afflicted" is too passive; "wretched" captures the self-humiliation called for.
- **"your laughter be turned to mourning, and your joy to heaviness" → "your joy to gloom"** (v9) — "heaviness" → "gloom"; modernized.
- **"There is one lawgiver, who is able to save and to destroy" → "There is only one lawgiver and judge, he who is able to save and to destroy"** (v12) — "lawgiver" and "judge" both from the Greek: νομοθέτης καὶ κριτής *nomothetēs kai kritēs*.
- **"a vapour, that appeareth for a little time, and then vanisheth away" → "a mist that appears for a little time and then vanishes"** (v14) — "vapour" → "mist"; modernized.

### Notes
- **v6** quotes Prov 3:34.
