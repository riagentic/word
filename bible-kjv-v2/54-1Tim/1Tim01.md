# 1 Timothy 1

[1] ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, an apostle of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ according to the command of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ our Savior and of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ our hope,

[2] to ⟦timothy|Timotheos|Timothy⟧, a genuine child in faith:

Grace, mercy, peace from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ the Father and ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ our Lord.

[3] Even as I exhorted you to remain in ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧, when I was going into ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧, that you might charge some not to teach a different doctrine

[4] nor to give heed to myths and endless genealogies, which provide inquiries rather than the stewardship of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that is in faith —

[5] but the end of the charge is love out of a pure heart and a good conscience and unfeigned faith,

[6] of which some, having missed the mark, were turned aside to vain talking,

[7] wanting to be teachers of the law, understanding neither what they say nor the things concerning which they affirm strongly.

[8] But we know that the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ is good, if anyone uses it lawfully,

[9] knowing this, that law is not laid down for a righteous one, but for the lawless and insubordinate, for the ungodly and sinners, for the undevout and profane, for smiters of fathers and smiters of mothers, for manslayers,

[10] for the sexually immoral, for men who lie with males, for enslavers, for liars, for perjurers, and if anything else is opposed to the healthy doctrine,

[11] according to the gospel of the glory of the blessed ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, with which I was entrusted.

[12] I am thankful to him who strengthened me, ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ our Lord, that he counted me faithful, appointing me unto service,

[13] who was formerly a blasphemer and a persecutor and an insolent man; but I was shown mercy, because I acted not knowing, in unbelief,

[14] and the grace of our Lord increased beyond measure, with faith and love that is in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[15] Faithful is the word and worthy of all acceptance, that **⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ came into the world to save sinners**, of whom I am first,

[16] but for this reason I was shown mercy, that in me as first ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ might demonstrate the whole patience, for a pattern of those who are going to believe on him unto eternal life.

[17] Now to the King of the ages, incorruptible, invisible, the only ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, be honor and glory unto the ages of the ages. Amen.

[18] This charge I entrust to you, child ⟦timothy|Timotheos|Timothy⟧, according to the prophecies going before upon you, that in them you may war the good warfare,

[19] having faith and a good conscience, which some, having thrust away, suffered shipwreck concerning the faith;

[20] of whom are ⟦hymenaeus|Hymenaios|Hymenaeus⟧ and ⟦alexander|Alexandros|Alexander⟧, whom I delivered up to Satan, that they may be trained not to blaspheme.

## Changes

- **"Christ Jesus" / "Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*" / "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv1, 2, 4, 11, 17) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"by the commandment" → "according to the command"** (v1) — Greek κατ᾽ ἐπιταγήν, as Titus 1:3; "commandment" is ἐντολή (6:14).
- **"Lord Jesus Christ, which is our hope" → "the *Mashiach* *Yeshua* our hope"** (v1) — Greek Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ τῆς ἐλπίδος ἡμῶν; "Lord" is not in the critical text.
- **"my own son in the faith" → "a genuine child in faith"** (v2) — Greek γνησίῳ τέκνῳ ἐν πίστει, as Titus 1:4; there is no "my" and no article. "Grace, mercy, peace" stand without "and".
- **"besought" → "exhorted"** (v3) — Greek παρεκάλεσα; παρακαλέω is "exhort" throughout the letter (2:1; 5:1; 6:2), as in Titus.
- **"abide still" → "remain"** (v3) — Greek προσμεῖναι, a compound of μένω "abide"; the same verb in 5:5.
- **"charge" kept** (v3, 5, 18) — Greek παραγγέλλω, παραγγελία: one root; "the commandment" (KJV v5) is the same noun as "this charge" (v18).
- **"teach no other doctrine" → "not to teach a different doctrine"** (v3) — Greek ἑτεροδιδασκαλεῖν, one verb, "teach differently"; also 6:3.
- **v3–4 unfinished** — the sentence that begins "Even as I exhorted you" has no main clause in Greek; the dash keeps it open. No "so do" (KJV) is supplied.
- **"fables" → "myths"; "minister questions" → "provide inquiries"** (v4) — Greek μύθοις; ἐκζητήσεις παρέχουσι, "searchings-out" (a compound of ζήτησις "questioning", 6:4); παρέχω "provide" as 6:17.
- **"godly edifying" → "the stewardship of *Elohim*"** (v4) — Greek οἰκονομίαν θεοῦ, the management of a house (οἰκονόμος "steward", Titus 1:7); KJV follows the later reading οἰκοδομίαν. A plain genitive: the stewardship that *Elohim* gives, or his own ordering.
- **"the end of the commandment is charity" → "the end of the charge is love"** (v5) — Greek τὸ τέλος, the end as goal; ἀγάπη "love".
- **"from which some having swerved have turned aside unto vain jangling" → "of which some, having missed the mark, were turned aside to vain talking"** (v6) — Greek ἀστοχήσαντες, as 6:21; 2 Tim 2:18; ἐξετράπησαν, passive in form, as 5:15; 2 Tim 4:4; ματαιολογία, the noun of "vain talkers" (Titus 1:10).
- **"desiring" → "wanting"; "whereof they affirm" → "concerning which they affirm strongly"** (v7) — Greek θέλοντες; διαβεβαιοῦνται, as Titus 3:8.
- **"the law" → "the *Torah*" / "law"** (v8, 9) — Greek ὁ νόμος with the article in v8, νόμος without it in v9; "lawfully" (νομίμως) is the same root.
- **"is not made for a righteous man" → "is not laid down for a righteous one"** (v9) — Greek δικαίῳ … οὐ κεῖται.
- **"disobedient" → "insubordinate"; "unholy" → "undevout"** (v9) — Greek ἀνυποτάκτοις, as Titus 1:6, 10; ἀνοσίοις, the negative of ὅσιος "devout", as 2 Tim 3:2.
- **"murderers of fathers and murderers of mothers" → "smiters of fathers and smiters of mothers"** (v9) — Greek πατρολῴαις, μητρολῴαις, from ἀλοάω "thresh, strike": one who strikes, or strikes dead, a father or a mother.
- **"whoremongers" → "the sexually immoral"** (v10) — Greek πόρνοις, the persons of πορνεία "sexual immorality".
- **"them that defile themselves with mankind" → "men who lie with males"** (v10) — Greek ἀρσενοκοίταις, from ἄρσην "male" and κοίτη "bed"; the two words stand together in Lev 18:22; 20:13 in the Septuagint.
- **"menstealers" → "enslavers"** (v10) — Greek ἀνδραποδισταῖς, those who make people slaves (ἀνδράποδον) or deal in them.
- **"contrary to sound doctrine" → "opposed to the healthy doctrine"** (v10) — Greek ἀντίκειται, as 5:14; ὑγιαινούσῃ, "being in health", as 6:3; Titus 1:9.
- **"the glorious gospel" → "the gospel of the glory"** (v11) — Greek τὸ εὐαγγέλιον τῆς δόξης, a noun.
- **"I thank" → "I am thankful to"** (v12) — Greek Χάριν ἔχω, as 2 Tim 1:3.
- **"who hath enabled me" → "who strengthened me"** (v12) — Greek ἐνδυναμώσαντι, aorist; as 2 Tim 4:17.
- **"putting me into the ministry" → "appointing me unto service"** (v12) — Greek θέμενος εἰς διακονίαν, no article; τίθημι as 2:7.
- **"injurious" → "an insolent man"** (v13) — Greek ὑβριστήν, one who acts with ὕβρις, insolence that does violence.
- **"I obtained mercy" → "I was shown mercy"** (v13, 16) — Greek ἠλεήθην, passive of "have mercy".
- **"I did it ignorantly" → "I acted not knowing"** (v13) — Greek ἀγνοῶν ἐποίησα; the verb has no object.
- **"was exceeding abundant" → "increased beyond measure"** (v14) — Greek ὑπερεπλεόνασεν; the verse has no "for me".
- **"This is a faithful saying" → "Faithful is the word"** (v15) — Greek πιστὸς ὁ λόγος, as 3:1; 4:9; Titus 3:8; 2 Tim 2:11.
- **"chief" → "first"** (v15, 16) — Greek πρῶτος, πρώτῳ, one word in both verses: first in rank, or first in order.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v16) — printed text Χριστὸς Ἰησοῦς; KJV follows the later order.
- **"all longsuffering" → "the whole patience"** (v16) — Greek τὴν ἅπασαν μακροθυμίαν, with the article; the verse has no "his". "Demonstrate": ἐνδείξηται.
- **"believe on him to life everlasting" → "believe on him unto eternal life"** (v16) — Greek πιστεύειν ἐπ᾽ αὐτῷ εἰς ζωὴν αἰώνιον.
- **"pattern" kept** (v16) — Greek ὑποτύπωσιν, as 2 Tim 1:13; a compound of τύπος "pattern" (4:12).
- **"the King eternal, immortal, … the only wise God" → "the King of the ages, incorruptible, … the only *Elohim*"** (v17) — Greek τῷ βασιλεῖ τῶν αἰώνων, ἀφθάρτῳ; "wise" is not in the critical text. "Unto the ages of the ages": εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας τῶν αἰώνων.
- **"I commit unto thee, son Timothy" → "I entrust to you, child *Timotheos*"** (v18) — Greek παρατίθεμαι, the verb of "deposit" (6:20); τέκνον.
- **"the prophecies which went before on thee" → "the prophecies going before upon you"** (v18) — Greek τὰς προαγούσας ἐπὶ σὲ προφητείας: prophecies made earlier about him, or prophecies leading the way to him; προάγω again in 5:24.
- **"by them" → "in them"; "war a good warfare" → "war the good warfare"** (v18) — Greek ἐν αὐταῖς; τὴν καλὴν στρατείαν, military service (the verb as 2 Tim 2:4).
- **"holding faith" → "having faith"; "having put away" → "having thrust away"** (v19) — Greek ἔχων; ἀπωσάμενοι. "Which" is singular and refers to "a good conscience".
- **"I have delivered" → "I delivered up"; "that they may learn" → "that they may be trained"** (v20) — Greek παρέδωκα (as 1 Cor 5:5); παιδευθῶσι, passive of παιδεύω "train" (Titus 2:12; 2 Tim 2:25).

### Notes
- **v3–7** — one sentence in Greek, left unfinished.
- **v12–17** — one sentence in Greek up to the doxology.
