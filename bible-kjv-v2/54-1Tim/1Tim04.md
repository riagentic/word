# 1 Timothy 4

[1] But the Spirit says expressly that in later times some will depart from the faith, giving heed to deceiving spirits and doctrines of demons,

[2] in hypocrisy of speakers of lies, branded in their own conscience,

[3] forbidding to marry, bidding to abstain from foods which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ created for partaking with thanksgiving by those who are faithful and have come to know the truth fully.

[4] For every creature of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is good, and nothing is to be thrown away, being received with thanksgiving,

[5] for it is sanctified through the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and petition.

[6] Laying these things before the brothers, you will be a good servant of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, being nourished in the words of the faith and of the good doctrine which you have followed closely,

[7] but the profane and old-womanish myths refuse. And exercise yourself unto godliness;

[8] for bodily exercise is profitable for a little, but godliness is profitable for all things, having promise of the life that now is and of that which is to come.

[9] Faithful is the word and worthy of all acceptance,

[10] for unto this we labor and struggle, because we have hoped upon the living ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who is Savior of all people, especially of the faithful.

[11] Charge these things and teach.

[12] Let no one despise your youth, but become a pattern of the faithful in word, in conduct, in love, in faith, in purity.

[13] Until I come, give heed to the reading, to the exhortation, to the doctrine.

[14] Do not neglect the gift that is in you, which was given to you through prophecy with the laying on of the hands of the eldership.

[15] Take care of these things, be in them, that your advance may be manifest to all;

[16] attend to yourself and to the doctrine; remain in them; for doing this you will save both yourself and those who hear you.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Christ Jesus" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v6) — systematic name restoration.
- **"in the latter times" → "in later times"** (v1) — Greek ἐν ὑστέροις καιροῖς, a comparative without the article.
- **"depart from the faith" kept** (v1) — Greek ἀποστήσονται, as 2 Tim 2:19.
- **"seducing spirits" → "deceiving spirits"** (v1) — Greek πνεύμασι πλάνοις; πλάνος is "deceiver". "Doctrines" (διδασκαλίαις) is the plural of the word in v6, 13, 16.
- **"Speaking lies in hypocrisy" → "in hypocrisy of speakers of lies"** (v2) — Greek ἐν ὑποκρίσει ψευδολόγων, a noun in the genitive; the genitives of v2–3 describe these people, not the "some" of v1.
- **"having their conscience seared with a hot iron" → "branded in their own conscience"** (v2) — Greek κεκαυστηριασμένων τὴν ἰδίαν συνείδησιν, marked with a hot iron (καυστήριον): burned so as to be without feeling, or branded as a slave is.
- **"commanding to abstain" → "bidding to abstain"** (v3) — the Greek has one participle, "forbidding" (κωλυόντων), for both infinitives; "bidding" is supplied because English cannot say "forbidding … to abstain".
- **"meats … to be received" → "foods … for partaking"** (v3) — Greek βρωμάτων; εἰς μετάλημψιν, a noun.
- **"them which believe and know the truth" → "those who are faithful and have come to know the truth fully"** (v3) — Greek τοῖς πιστοῖς καὶ ἐπεγνωκόσι, one article for both; πιστός is "faithful" or "believing"; ἐπιγινώσκω is the verb of "full knowledge" (2:4).
- **"nothing to be refused" → "nothing is to be thrown away"** (v4) — Greek ἀπόβλητον; "refuse" renders παραιτέομαι (v7).
- **"prayer" → "petition"** (v5) — Greek ἐντεύξεως, as 2:1; "prayer" is προσευχή.
- **"If thou put the brethren in remembrance of these things" → "Laying these things before the brothers"** (v6) — Greek ὑποτιθέμενος, a participle; no "if".
- **"minister" → "servant"** (v6) — Greek διάκονος, as 3:8.
- **"nourished up" → "being nourished"; "whereunto thou hast attained" → "which you have followed closely"** (v6) — Greek ἐντρεφόμενος, present; παρηκολούθηκας, as 2 Tim 3:10.
- **"old wives' fables" → "old-womanish myths"** (v7) — Greek γραώδεις μύθους; μῦθος as 1:4. "Refuse" (παραιτοῦ) as 5:11; Titus 3:10.
- **"exercise" kept** (v7, 8) — Greek γύμναζε, γυμνασία, one root; not "train", which renders παιδεύω (1:20).
- **"profiteth little" → "is profitable for a little"** (v8) — Greek πρὸς ὀλίγον: for a little, in extent or in time; set against "for all things" (πρὸς πάντα).
- **"we both labour and suffer reproach" → "we labor and struggle"** (v10) — the verse follows ἀγωνιζόμεθα, "we struggle" (WH, Treg, NA28), as 6:12; the SBLGNT prints ὀνειδιζόμεθα, "we are reproached" (so also RP).
- **"we trust in" → "we have hoped upon"** (v10) — Greek ἠλπίκαμεν ἐπί, perfect of "hope"; also 5:5; 6:17.
- **"specially of those that believe" → "especially of the faithful"** (v10) — Greek πιστῶν, "faithful" or "believing"; the same word in v3 and v12.
- **"command" → "charge"** (v11) — Greek Παράγγελλε, as 1:3.
- **"be thou an example of the believers" → "become a pattern of the faithful"** (v12) — Greek τύπος γίνου τῶν πιστῶν, a genitive: a pattern for them, or of what they are.
- **"in conversation, in charity, in spirit" → "in conduct, in love"** (v12) — Greek ἐν ἀναστροφῇ, ἐν ἀγάπῃ; "in spirit" is not in the critical text.
- **"Till I come" kept** (v13) — Greek ἕως ἔρχομαι, present: "until I come" or "while I am coming".
- **"give attendance to reading, to exhortation, to doctrine" → "give heed to the reading, to the exhortation, to the doctrine"** (v13) — Greek πρόσεχε, as v1; each noun has the article. παράκλησις is elsewhere "encouragement"; here it is the noun of "exhort" (παρακαλέω, 6:2), which stands beside "teach".
- **"the presbytery" → "the eldership"** (v14) — Greek τοῦ πρεσβυτερίου, the body of elders (πρεσβύτερος, 5:17).
- **"by prophecy, with the laying on of the hands" → "through prophecy with the laying on of the hands"** (v14) — Greek διὰ προφητείας μετὰ ἐπιθέσεως τῶν χειρῶν.
- **"Meditate upon these things; give thyself wholly to them" → "Take care of these things, be in them"** (v15) — Greek ταῦτα μελέτα, the opposite of "neglect" (ἀμέλει) in v14: take care of, practice, or ponder; ἐν τούτοις ἴσθι, "be in these things".
- **"thy profiting may appear" → "your advance may be manifest"** (v15) — Greek προκοπή, the noun of "advance" (2 Tim 2:16); φανερά.
- **"Take heed unto thyself" → "attend to yourself"; "continue in them" → "remain in them"** (v16) — Greek ἔπεχε; ἐπίμενε αὐτοῖς, ἐπιμένω "remain", a compound of μένω; "them" is plural.
