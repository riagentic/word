# 1 Timothy 6

[1] As many as are slaves under a yoke, let them count their own masters worthy of all honor, that the name of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and the doctrine may not be blasphemed.

[2] But those who have faithful masters, let them not despise them because they are brothers, but rather let them be slaves to them, because those who take part in the benefaction are faithful and beloved.

These things teach and exhort.

[3] If anyone teaches a different doctrine and does not approach healthy words, those of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, and to the doctrine that is according to godliness,

[4] he is puffed up, understanding nothing, but being sick about questionings and fights about words, out of which come envy, strife, blasphemies, evil suspicions,

[5] constant frictions of people corrupted in mind and deprived of the truth, supposing godliness to be a source of gain.

[6] But **godliness with contentment is a great source of gain**;

[7] for we brought nothing into the world, because neither are we able to carry anything out;

[8] but having sustenance and coverings, with these we shall be content.

[9] But those who wish to be rich fall into trial and a snare and many foolish and harmful desires, which sink people into ruin and destruction;

[10] **for a root of all the evils is the love of money**, reaching after which some were led astray from the faith and pierced themselves through with many pains.

[11] But you, O man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, flee these things; and pursue righteousness, godliness, faith, love, endurance, meekness.

[12] **Struggle the good struggle of the faith; lay hold of the eternal life unto which you were called** and confessed the good confession before many witnesses.

[13] I charge you before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who gives life to all things, and ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, who testified the good confession before Pontius ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧,

[14] that you keep the commandment spotless, irreproachable, until the appearing of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[15] which in his own times the blessed and only Potentate will show, the **King of those who reign as kings and Lord of those who rule as lords**,

[16] who alone has immortality, dwelling in unapproachable light, whom no one of men has seen nor is able to see; to whom be honor and eternal might. Amen.

[17] The rich in the present age charge not to be high-minded, nor to have hoped upon the uncertainty of riches, but upon ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who provides us all things richly for enjoyment,

[18] to do good, to be rich in good works, to be ready to give, ready to share,

[19] treasuring up for themselves a good foundation for that which is to come, that they may lay hold of what is really life.

[20] O ⟦timothy|Timotheos|Timothy⟧, guard the deposit, turning aside from the profane empty talkings and oppositions of the falsely named knowledge,

[21] which some professing, missed the mark concerning the faith.

Grace be with you.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus Christ" / "Christ Jesus" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*" / "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (vv3, 13, 14) — systematic name restoration.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v14) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
- **"servants" → "slaves"; "do them service" → "be slaves to them"** (v1, 2) — Greek δοῦλοι, δουλευέτωσαν.
- **"his doctrine" → "the doctrine"** (v1) — Greek ἡ διδασκαλία; there is no "his". "Blasphemed" (βλασφημῆται) kept, as Titus 2:5.
- **"believing masters" → "faithful masters"; "faithful and beloved" kept** (v2) — Greek πιστούς, πιστοί: one word, "faithful" or "believing". "Them" is supplied twice.
- **"partakers of the benefit" → "those who take part in the benefaction"** (v2) — Greek οἱ τῆς εὐεργεσίας ἀντιλαμβανόμενοι, "those who take hold of the good deed": the masters, who receive the benefit of the service, or who give themselves to doing good.
- **"exhort" kept** (v2) — Greek παρακάλει, as 1:3.
- **"teach otherwise" → "teaches a different doctrine"** (v3) — Greek ἑτεροδιδασκαλεῖ, as 1:3.
- **"consent not to wholesome words" → "does not approach healthy words"** (v3) — Greek μὴ προσέρχεται, "come to, approach", that is, agree with; ὑγιαίνουσι λόγοις, as 2 Tim 1:13, without the article.
- **"He is proud, knowing nothing, but doting about questions and strifes of words" → "he is puffed up, understanding nothing, but being sick about questionings and fights about words"** (v4) — Greek τετύφωται, as 3:6; νοσῶν, "being sick", against "healthy" in v3; ζητήσεις, as Titus 3:9; λογομαχίας, as 2 Tim 2:14.
- **"railings" → "blasphemies"** (v4) — Greek βλασφημίαι, the noun of the verb in v1.
- **"Perverse disputings of men of corrupt minds, and destitute of the truth" → "constant frictions of people corrupted in mind and deprived of the truth"** (v5) — Greek διαπαρατριβαί, "rubbings against one another"; διεφθαρμένων … τὸν νοῦν, as 2 Tim 3:8; ἀπεστερημένων, passive.
- **"supposing that gain is godliness: from such withdraw thyself" → "supposing godliness to be a source of gain"** (v5) — Greek πορισμὸν εἶναι τὴν εὐσέβειαν: "godliness" has the article and is the subject; πορισμός is a means of gain. The last clause is not in the critical text.
- **"great gain" → "a great source of gain"** (v6) — Greek πορισμὸς μέγας, the word of v5.
- **"contentment" kept; "let us be therewith content" → "with these we shall be content"** (v6, 8) — Greek αὐταρκείας, ἀρκεσθησόμεθα (future): one root, "sufficiency"; as Phil 4:11.
- **"and it is certain we can carry nothing out" → "because neither are we able to carry anything out"** (v7) — Greek ὅτι οὐδὲ ἐξενεγκεῖν τι δυνάμεθα; "it is certain" is not in the critical text. ὅτι is "because" or "that"; how the two halves are joined is not clear.
- **"food and raiment" → "sustenance and coverings"** (v8) — Greek διατροφὰς καὶ σκεπάσματα, plural: what covers, clothing and perhaps shelter.
- **"they that will be rich" → "those who wish to be rich"** (v9) — Greek οἱ βουλόμενοι, as 2:8.
- **"temptation" → "trial"** (v9) — Greek πειρασμόν, "trial" and "temptation" in one word.
- **"lusts" → "desires"; "drown men in destruction and perdition" → "sink people into ruin and destruction"** (v9) — Greek ἐπιθυμίας; βυθίζουσι; ὄλεθρον καὶ ἀπώλειαν.
- **"the root of all evil" → "a root of all the evils"** (v10) — Greek ῥίζα … πάντων τῶν κακῶν: "root" has no article and stands first; it can be "a root" or "the root". "Evils" is plural with the article; the verse has no "kinds of".
- **"which while some coveted after, they have erred from the faith" → "reaching after which some were led astray from the faith"** (v10) — Greek ὀρεγόμενοι, as 3:1; ἀπεπλανήθησαν, passive, a compound of πλανάω "deceive" ("were deceived away from" is not English; so also Jas 5:19).
- **"sorrows" → "pains"** (v10) — Greek ὀδύναις.
- **"follow after" → "pursue"; "patience, meekness" → "endurance, meekness"** (v11) — Greek δίωκε, as 2 Tim 2:22; ὑπομονήν; πραϋπαθίαν, only here, "meekness of feeling" (the common word is πραΰτης, Titus 3:2).
- **"Fight the good fight of faith" → "Struggle the good struggle of the faith"** (v12) — Greek ἀγωνίζου τὸν καλὸν ἀγῶνα τῆς πίστεως, the contest of the games; with the article. The same words in 2 Tim 4:7.
- **"lay hold on" → "lay hold of"** (v12, 19) — Greek ἐπιλαβοῦ, ἐπιλάβωνται.
- **"hast professed a good profession" → "confessed the good confession"** (v12, 13) — Greek ὡμολόγησας τὴν καλὴν ὁμολογίαν; the same noun in v13.
- **"I give thee charge in the sight of God, who quickeneth all things" → "I charge you before *Elohim*, who gives life to all things"** (v13) — Greek παραγγέλλω; ζῳογονοῦντος, "bring forth alive" or "keep alive".
- **"who before Pontius Pilate witnessed a good confession" → "who testified the good confession before Pontius Pilate"** (v13) — Greek μαρτυρήσαντος ἐπὶ Ποντίου Πιλάτου: ἐπί is "before" or "in the time of".
- **"without spot, unrebukeable" → "spotless, irreproachable"** (v14) — Greek ἄσπιλον ἀνεπίλημπτον, as 3:2; the two words can describe "the commandment" or "you".
- **"in his times he shall shew" → "in his own times … will show"** (v15) — Greek καιροῖς ἰδίοις δείξει, as 2:6; Titus 1:3.
- **"King of kings, and Lord of lords" → "King of those who reign as kings and Lord of those who rule as lords"** (v15) — Greek ὁ βασιλεὺς τῶν βασιλευόντων καὶ κύριος τῶν κυριευόντων, two participles; the nouns stand in Rev 17:14; 19:16.
- **"dwelling in the light which no man can approach unto" → "dwelling in unapproachable light"** (v16) — Greek φῶς οἰκῶν ἀπρόσιτον.
- **"whom no man hath seen" → "whom no one of men has seen"** (v16) — Greek ὃν εἶδεν οὐδεὶς ἀνθρώπων.
- **"power everlasting" → "eternal might"** (v16) — Greek κράτος αἰώνιον.
- **"in this world" → "in the present age"** (v17) — Greek ἐν τῷ νῦν αἰῶνι, as Titus 2:12.
- **"nor trust in uncertain riches, but in the living God" → "nor to have hoped upon the uncertainty of riches, but upon *Elohim*"** (v17) — Greek ἠλπικέναι ἐπὶ πλούτου ἀδηλότητι, perfect, as 4:10; "living" is not in the critical text.
- **"giveth us richly all things to enjoy" → "provides us all things richly for enjoyment"** (v17) — Greek παρέχοντι, as 1:4; εἰς ἀπόλαυσιν, a noun.
- **"ready to distribute, willing to communicate" → "ready to give, ready to share"** (v18) — Greek εὐμεταδότους, κοινωνικούς (the root of "fellowship").
- **"Laying up in store" → "treasuring up"; "against the time to come" → "for that which is to come"** (v19) — Greek ἀποθησαυρίζοντας; εἰς τὸ μέλλον.
- **"eternal life" → "what is really life"** (v19) — Greek τῆς ὄντως ζωῆς, "the really life", as 5:3; KJV follows the later reading αἰωνίου.
- **"keep that which is committed to thy trust" → "guard the deposit"** (v20) — Greek τὴν παραθήκην φύλαξον, as 2 Tim 1:14.
- **"avoiding profane and vain babblings, and oppositions of science falsely so called" → "turning aside from the profane empty talkings and oppositions of the falsely named knowledge"** (v20) — Greek ἐκτρεπόμενος, as 1:6; κενοφωνίας, as 2 Tim 2:16; ἀντιθέσεις; τῆς ψευδωνύμου γνώσεως.
- **"have erred concerning the faith" → "missed the mark concerning the faith"** (v21) — Greek ἠστόχησαν, as 1:6.
- **"Grace be with thee. Amen." → "Grace be with you."** (v21) — Greek μεθ᾽ ὑμῶν, plural; elsewhere "you" is *Timotheos* alone. "Amen" is not in the critical text.

### Notes
- **v15–16** — the words can be read of *Elohim* (v13), who will show the appearing; "whom no one of men has seen".
