# 1 Timothy 2

[1] I exhort therefore, first of all, that supplications, prayers, petitions, thanksgivings be made for all people,

[2] for kings and all who are in high position, that we may lead a tranquil and quiet life in all godliness and dignity.

[3] This is good and acceptable before our Savior ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[4] who wants all people to be saved and to come to full knowledge of the truth.

[5] For **there is one ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, one mediator also of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and of men, a man, ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧,**

[6] **who gave himself a ransom for all**, the testimony in its own times;

[7] for which I was appointed a herald and an apostle — I speak truth, I do not lie — a teacher of nations in faith and truth.

[8] I wish therefore that the men pray in every place, lifting up devout hands without wrath and disputing.

[9] Likewise also that women adorn themselves in orderly apparel, with modesty and sober-mindedness, not in braids and gold or pearls or costly clothing,

[10] but, which befits women professing reverence for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, through good works.

[11] Let a woman learn in quietness, in all subjection;

[12] but to teach I do not permit a woman, nor to have authority over a man, but to be in quietness.

[13] For Adam was formed first, then ⟦eve|Chavvah|Eve⟧;

[14] and Adam was not beguiled, but the woman, having been utterly beguiled, has come to be in transgression.

[15] But she will be saved through the childbearing, if they abide in faith and love and sanctification with sober-mindedness.

## Changes

- **"Christ Jesus" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v5) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv3, 5, 10) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"I exhort" kept** (v1) — Greek Παρακαλῶ; "exhort" throughout the letter (1:3).
- **"intercessions" → "petitions"** (v1) — Greek ἐντεύξεις, an approach to someone with a request; not only on behalf of others. The same noun in 4:5. The four nouns stand without "and".
- **"for all men" → "for all people"** (v1, 4) — Greek ἀνθρώπων, ἀνθρώπους, human beings; the same word as "men" and "a man" in v5. The male (ἀνήρ) comes in v8 and v12.
- **"all that are in authority" → "all who are in high position"** (v2) — Greek ἐν ὑπεροχῇ, "in eminence"; "authority" is ἐξουσία.
- **"a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and honesty" → "a tranquil and quiet life in all godliness and dignity"** (v2) — Greek ἤρεμον καὶ ἡσύχιον βίον; ἡσύχιος is the adjective of "quietness" (v11, 12); σεμνότης "dignity" as 3:4; Titus 2:7. βίος is "livelihood" elsewhere (2 Tim 2:4); here it is the life a person leads, so "life".
- **"in the sight of God our Saviour" → "before our Savior *Elohim*"** (v3) — Greek ἐνώπιον τοῦ σωτῆρος ἡμῶν θεοῦ, in this order, as Titus 1:3; ἀπόδεκτον "acceptable", also 5:4.
- **"will have" → "wants"** (v4) — Greek θέλει; a different verb from "I wish" (βούλομαι) in v8.
- **"the knowledge of the truth" → "full knowledge of the truth"** (v4) — Greek ἐπίγνωσιν ἀληθείας, without articles; as 2 Tim 2:25; 3:7.
- **"one mediator between God and men, the man" → "one mediator also of *Elohim* and of men, a man"** (v5) — Greek εἷς καὶ μεσίτης θεοῦ καὶ ἀνθρώπων ἄνθρωπος: two genitives, no "between"; ἄνθρωπος has no article and is the word for a human being, the same as "people" in v1, 4.
- **"a ransom for all" kept** (v6) — Greek ἀντίλυτρον ὑπὲρ πάντων; λύτρον "ransom" with ἀντί, "in place of".
- **"to be testified in due time" → "the testimony in its own times"** (v6) — Greek τὸ μαρτύριον καιροῖς ἰδίοις, a noun in apposition with no verb. "Own times" as Titus 1:3; 1 Tim 6:15: the times proper to the testimony, or his own times.
- **"I am ordained a preacher" → "I was appointed a herald"** (v7) — Greek ἐτέθην … κῆρυξ, as 2 Tim 1:11.
- **"I speak the truth in Christ" → "I speak truth"** (v7) — Greek ἀλήθειαν λέγω; "in Christ" is not in the critical text.
- **"a teacher of the Gentiles" → "a teacher of nations"** (v7) — Greek διδάσκαλος ἐθνῶν, without the article.
- **"I will therefore that men pray every where" → "I wish therefore that the men pray in every place"** (v8) — Greek Βούλομαι; τοὺς ἄνδρας, the males, with the article; ἐν παντὶ τόπῳ.
- **"holy hands" → "devout hands"** (v8) — Greek ὁσίους, not ἁγίους.
- **"doubting" → "disputing"** (v8) — Greek διαλογισμοῦ, singular: reasoning, either as dispute with others (Phil 2:14) or as doubt within.
- **"in modest apparel, with shamefacedness and sobriety" → "in orderly apparel, with modesty and sober-mindedness"** (v9) — Greek ἐν καταστολῇ κοσμίῳ, the adjective of the verb "adorn" (κοσμεῖν) and of 3:2; αἰδοῦς; σωφροσύνης, of the σωφρ- words (v15; 3:2; Titus 2).
- **"broided hair" → "braids"** (v9) — Greek πλέγμασιν, things plaited.
- **"godliness" → "reverence for *Elohim*"** (v10) — Greek θεοσέβειαν, "reverence for God"; a different word from εὐσέβεια "godliness" (v2). "Professing" (ἐπαγγελλομέναις, also 6:21): the verb is "promise" elsewhere; here it is to declare oneself for something.
- **"which becometh" → "which befits"** (v10) — Greek ὃ πρέπει, as Titus 2:1. The clause stands between "but" and "through good works"; "through good works" goes back to "adorn themselves".
- **"in silence" → "in quietness"** (v11, 12) — Greek ἐν ἡσυχίᾳ, the noun of "quiet" in v2.
- **"usurp authority over" → "have authority over"** (v12) — Greek αὐθεντεῖν, only here in the New Testament: to have authority over, or to domineer over. It is not the common word ἐξουσία. "Woman" and "man" (γυνή, ἀνήρ) are also the words for wife and husband.
- **"deceived" / "being deceived" → "beguiled" / "having been utterly beguiled"** (v14) — Greek ἠπατήθη, ἐξαπατηθεῖσα: the simple verb and its strengthened compound; "deceive" renders πλανάω elsewhere.
- **"was in the transgression" → "has come to be in transgression"** (v14) — Greek ἐν παραβάσει γέγονεν, perfect, no article.
- **"she shall be saved in childbearing" → "she will be saved through the childbearing"** (v15) — Greek διὰ τῆς τεκνογονίας, with the article: the bearing of children (5:14), or the one childbearing. The verb is singular ("she"), the next one plural ("they"); the verse does not say who "they" are.
- **"continue" → "abide"; "holiness with sobriety" → "sanctification with sober-mindedness"** (v15) — Greek μείνωσιν; ἁγιασμῷ; σωφροσύνης as v9.

### Notes
- **v8–15** — the infinitives of v8–10 depend on "I wish".
