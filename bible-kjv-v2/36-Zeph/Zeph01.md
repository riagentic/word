# Zephaniah 1

[1] The word of Yahweh that came to Zephaniah son of Cushi, son of Gedaliah, son of Amariah, son of Hezekiah, in the days of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah.

[2] "I will utterly sweep away everything
from the face of the earth," declares Yahweh.

[3] "I will sweep away man and beast;
I will sweep away the birds of the heavens
and the fish of the sea,
and the rubble with the wicked.
I will cut off mankind
from the face of the earth," declares Yahweh.

[4] "I will stretch out my hand against Judah
and against all the inhabitants of Jerusalem;
and I will cut off from this place the remnant of Baal
and the name of the idolatrous priests with the priests,

[5] those who bow down on the roofs
to the host of the heavens,
those who bow down and swear to Yahweh
and yet swear by Milkom,

[6] those who have turned back from following Yahweh,
who do not seek Yahweh or inquire of him."

[7] Be silent before the Lord Yahweh!
For the Day of Yahweh is near;
Yahweh has prepared a sacrifice
and consecrated his guests.

[8] "And on the day of Yahweh's sacrifice —

"I will punish the officials and the king's sons
and all who array themselves in foreign attire.

[9] On that day I will punish
everyone who leaps over the threshold,
and those who fill their master's house
with violence and fraud.

[10] "On that day," declares Yahweh,
"a cry will be heard from the Fish Gate,
a wail from the Second Quarter,
a loud crash from the hills.

[11] Wail, O inhabitants of the Mortar!
For all the traders are no more;
all who weigh out silver are cut off.

[12] "At that time I will search Jerusalem with lamps,
and I will punish the men
who are complacent, thickening on their dregs,
those who say in their hearts,
'Yahweh will not do good,
nor will he do ill.'

[13] Their goods shall be plundered,
and their houses laid waste.
Though they build houses,
they shall not inhabit them;
though they plant vineyards,
they shall not drink wine from them."

[14] The great Day of Yahweh is near,
near and hastening fast;
the sound of the Day of Yahweh is bitter;
the mighty man cries aloud there.

[15] A day of wrath is that day,
a day of distress and anguish,
a day of ruin and devastation,
a day of darkness and gloom,
a day of clouds and thick darkness,

[16] a day of trumpet blast and battle cry
against the fortified cities
and against the lofty battlements.

[17] I will bring distress on mankind,
so that they shall walk like the blind,
because they have sinned against Yahweh;
their blood shall be poured out like dust,
and their flesh like dung.

[18] Neither their silver nor their gold
shall be able to deliver them
on the day of the wrath of Yahweh.
In the fire of his jealousy,
all the earth shall be consumed;
for a full and sudden end
he will make of all the inhabitants of the earth.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v7) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוָה.
- **"Hizkiah" → "Hezekiah"** (v1) — KJV "Hizkiah" is the Hebrew form; "Hezekiah" is the standard English form for the king.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (vv2-18) — KJV prose; the Day of Yahweh oracles are poetry.
- **"I will utterly consume all things from off the land" → "'I will utterly sweep away everything from the face of the earth'"** (v2) — Hebrew אָסֹף אָסֵף כֹּל מֵעַל פְּנֵי הָאֲדָמָה *asof asef kol me'al peney ha'adamah*; "sweep away" for *asaf* = gather/sweep; "face of the earth" for *peney ha'adamah*.
- **"I will consume man and beast; I will consume the fowls of the heaven, and the fishes of the sea, and the stumblingblocks with the wicked" → "'I will sweep away man and beast; I will sweep away the birds of the heavens and the fish of the sea, and the rubble with the wicked'"** (v3) — "sweep away" consistent; "rubble" for *hamakshilot* = stumbling blocks/ruins.
- **"them that worship and that swear by the Lord, and that swear by Malcham" → "those who bow down and swear to Yahweh and yet swear by Milkom"** (v5) — Hebrew מַלְכָּם *Milkom* = Milkom/Molech, the Ammonite deity; Hebrew name retained in transliteration; "yet" emphasizes the syncretic contradiction.
- **"Hold thy peace at the presence of the Lord God" → "Be silent before the Lord Yahweh!"** (v7) — Hebrew הַס מִפְּנֵי אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *has mipeney Adonai Yahweh*; *has* = hushed/silent (solemn command); *Adonai* retained.
- **"the day of the LORD is at hand" → "the Day of Yahweh is near"** (v7) — consistent eschatological formula.
- **"he hath bid his guests" → "consecrated his guests"** (v7) — Hebrew הִזְמִין קְרֻאָיו *hizmin qeruav* = summoned/consecrated his invited ones; Yahweh prepares a sacrificial feast of judgment.
- **"all such as are clothed with strange apparel" → "all who array themselves in foreign attire"** (v8) — Hebrew לְבוּשׁ נָכְרִי *levush nokhri* = foreign clothing; adoption of pagan dress = religious apostasy.
- **"all those that leap on the threshold" → "everyone who leaps over the threshold"** (v9) — Hebrew הַדּוֹלֵג עַל הַמִּפְתָּן *haddoleg al hammiftan*; superstitious ritual of leaping over thresholds (cf. 1 Sam 5:5 — Philistine priests at Dagon's temple).
- **"all the merchant people are cut down" → "all the traders are no more"** (v11) — Hebrew עַם כְּנַעַן *am Kena'an* = the Canaanite people (= traders); "traders" more precise.
- **"settled on their lees" → "complacent, thickening on their dregs"** (v12) — Hebrew הַקֹּפְאִים עַל שִׁמְרֵיהֶם *haqofe'im al shimreyhem* = those who thicken/congeal on their wine dregs; vivid metaphor for spiritual complacency.
- **"The LORD will not do good, neither will he do evil" → "Yahweh will not do good, nor will he do ill"** (v12) — modernized; the deist/fatalist attitude.
- **"The great day of the LORD is near, it is near, and hasteth greatly, even the voice of the day of the LORD: the mighty man shall cry there bitterly" → "The great Day of Yahweh is near, near and hastening fast; the sound of the Day of Yahweh is bitter; the mighty man cries aloud there"** (v14) — consistent formula; "hastening fast" for *maher me'od*; "sound … is bitter" for *qol yom Yahweh mar*.
- **"a day of wasteness and desolation" → "a day of ruin and devastation"** (v15) — Hebrew שׁוֹאָה וּמְשׁוֹאָה *sho'ah umesho'ah* = desolation and destruction; "ruin and devastation" more vivid.
- **"a day of the trumpet and alarm against the fenced cities, and against the high towers" → "a day of trumpet blast and battle cry against the fortified cities and against the lofty battlements"** (v16) — Hebrew תְּרוּעָה *teru'ah* = trumpet blast/war cry; הַפִּנּוֹת *hapinnot* = the corner towers/battlements.
- **"but the whole land shall be devoured by the fire of his jealousy: for he shall make even a speedy riddance of all them that dwell in the land" → "In the fire of his jealousy, all the earth shall be consumed; for a full and sudden end he will make of all the inhabitants of the earth"** (v18) — Hebrew כָּלָה אַךְ נִבְהָלָה *kalah akh nivhalah* = a full and sudden end; "sudden" for *nivhalah* = hastened/alarmed.

### Notes
- **v15** — "Dies irae, dies illa" of medieval hymn derives from this day-of-wrath text.
