## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v7) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; the quotation marks are dropped (the Hebrew has none).
- **"which came unto Zephaniah"** (v1) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר הָיָה אֶל, literally "which was to".
- **"I will utterly consume all things from off the land, saith the LORD" → "I will surely take away everything from upon the face of the ground, declares Yahweh"** (v2) — Hebrew אָסֹף אָסֵף: an infinitive of אָסַף "gather, take away" with a verb from סוּף "come to an end" (hifil "make an end"), a play of two roots ("surely"; literally "taking away I will make an end"); מֵעַל פְּנֵי הָאֲדָמָה "from upon the face of the ground" (אֲדָמָה "ground"; also v3); נְאֻם "declares".
- **"I will consume man and beast; I will consume the fowls of the heaven, and the fishes of the sea, and the stumblingblocks with the wicked: and I will cut off man from off the land" → "I will take away man and beast; I will take away the birds of the heavens and the fish of the sea, and the stumbling blocks with the wicked; and I will cut off man from upon the face of the ground"** (v3) — Hebrew אָסֵף twice; אָדָם "man" (mankind); הַמַּכְשֵׁלוֹת "the stumbling blocks" (the idols that make fall; the former "rubble" was a guess); הִכְרַתִּי "cut off".
- **"the remnant of Baal, and the name of the Chemarims with the priests" → "the remnant of the Baal, the name of the idol-priests with the priests"** (v4) — Hebrew הַבַּעַל with the article; כְּמָרִים "idol-priests" (another word for "priest", only of other cults; as 2 Kgs 23:5); no "and" before "the name".
- **"And them that worship the host of heaven upon the housetops; and them that worship and that swear by the LORD, and that swear by Malcham" → "and those who bow down on the roofs to the host of the heavens, and those who bow down, who swear to Yahweh and swear by their king"** (v5) — Hebrew הַמִּשְׁתַּחֲוִים "who bow down"; הַנִּשְׁבָּעִים לַיהוָה "who swear to Yahweh"; בְּמַלְכָּם, as pointed, "by their king" (KJV "Malcham"); the Septuagint, Syriac and Vulgate read the god's name Milcom (from memory), as at Jer 49:1, 3: the MT stands, the `milcom` token is removed. No "yet".
- **"And them that are turned back from the LORD; and those that have not sought the LORD, nor enquired for him" → "and those who turn back from after Yahweh, and who have not sought Yahweh and have not inquired of him"** (v6) — Hebrew הַנְּסוֹגִים מֵאַחֲרֵי "who turn back from after"; בִּקְשׁוּ "sought", דְרָשֻׁהוּ "inquired of him" (two verbs).
- **"Hold thy peace at the presence of the Lord GOD: for the day of the LORD is at hand: for the LORD hath prepared a sacrifice, he hath bid his guests" → "Hush before the Lord Yahweh! For near is the day of Yahweh, for Yahweh has prepared a sacrifice; he has sanctified his called ones"** (v7) — Hebrew הַס "Hush!"; מִפְּנֵי "before" (literally "from the face of"); יוֹם יְהוָה "the day of Yahweh" (lower-case "day": no title in the Hebrew); הִקְדִּישׁ "sanctified"; קְרֻאָיו "his called ones".
- **"And it shall come to pass in the day of the LORD's sacrifice, that I will punish the princes, and the king's children, and all such as are clothed with strange apparel" → "And it shall be, on the day of the sacrifice of Yahweh, that I will visit upon the princes and upon the sons of the king, and upon all who clothe themselves in foreign clothing"** (v8) — Hebrew וְהָיָה "and it shall be"; פָּקַדְתִּי עַל "I will visit upon" (also v9, 12; פָּקַד "visit", for punishment here); שָׂרִים "princes"; בְּנֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ "the sons of the king"; מַלְבּוּשׁ נָכְרִי "foreign clothing".
- **"In the same day also will I punish all those that leap on the threshold, which fill their masters' houses with violence and deceit" → "And I will visit upon everyone who leaps over the threshold in that day, who fill the house of their lord with violence and deceit"** (v9) — Hebrew הַדּוֹלֵג עַל־הַמִּפְתָּן "who leaps over the threshold" (a custom, cf. 1 Sam 5:5; or a leap into houses to rob: open); בֵּית אֲדֹנֵיהֶם "the house of their lord" (בֵּית singular; the plural אֲדֹנִים of one master, or of their god); מִרְמָה "deceit".
- **"that there shall be the noise of a cry from the fish gate, and an howling from the second, and a great crashing from the hills" → "the sound of an outcry from the Fish Gate, and wailing from the Second quarter, and a great breaking from the hills"** (v10) — Hebrew קוֹל צְעָקָה "the sound of an outcry"; יְלָלָה "wailing"; הַמִּשְׁנֶה "the Second" ("quarter" supplied, as 2 Kgs 22:14); שֶׁבֶר "breaking".
- **"Howl, ye inhabitants of Maktesh, for all the merchant people are cut down; all they that bear silver are cut off" → "Wail, inhabitants of the Hollow, for all the people of Canaan are undone; all who are laden with silver are cut off"** (v11) — Hebrew הֵילִילוּ "wail"; no "O"; הַמַּכְתֵּשׁ "the Hollow" (literally "the mortar": a low part of Jerusalem); כָּל־עַם כְּנַעַן "all the people of Canaan" (= the traders: KJV "merchant people"; the word kept, token); נִדְמָה "are undone" (nifal of דָּמָה "cease, be cut off", as Jer 47:5; or "are silenced"); נְטִילֵי כָסֶף "those laden with silver".
- **"And it shall come to pass at that time, that I will search Jerusalem with candles, and punish the men that are settled on their lees: that say in their heart, The LORD will not do good, neither will he do evil" → "And it shall be at that time, I will search Jerusalem with the lamps, and I will visit upon the men who are congealing upon their lees, who say in their heart, Yahweh will not do good, and he will not do evil"** (v12) — Hebrew בַּנֵּרוֹת "with the lamps"; הַקֹּפְאִים "who are congealing" (קָפָא "congeal", of wine left on its lees: thickened); בִּלְבָבָם "in their heart" (singular); יֵיטִיב … יָרֵעַ "do good … do evil".
- **"Therefore their goods shall become a booty, and their houses a desolation: they shall also build houses, but not inhabit them; and they shall plant vineyards, but not drink the wine thereof" → "And their wealth shall become pillage, and their houses a desolation; and they shall build houses and shall not dwell, and plant vineyards and shall not drink their wine"** (v13) — Hebrew וְהָיָה "and shall become"; חֵילָם "their wealth" (חַיִל, of goods); מְשִׁסָּה "pillage"; שְׁמָמָה "desolation"; וְלֹא יֵשֵׁבוּ "and shall not dwell" (no "in them"); "and", not "but".
- **"The great day of the LORD is near, it is near, and hasteth greatly, even the voice of the day of the LORD: the mighty man shall cry there bitterly" → "Near is the great day of Yahweh, near and hastening greatly; the sound of the day of Yahweh — bitterly the mighty one cries out there"** (v14) — Hebrew order; מַהֵר מְאֹד "hastening greatly"; קוֹל "sound"; מַר צֹרֵחַ שָׁם גִּבּוֹר "bitter, crying out there, is the mighty one" (צָרַח "cry out").
- **"That day is a day of wrath, a day of trouble and distress, a day of wasteness and desolation, a day of darkness and gloominess, a day of clouds and thick darkness" → "A day of raging is that day, a day of distress and straits, a day of devastation and desolation, a day of darkness and gloom, a day of cloud and dark cloud"** (v15) — word for word, each pair as the Hebrew has it: יוֹם עֶבְרָה "a day of raging" (עֶבְרָה "raging", as Ps 7:6; 90:9; the traditional "day of wrath", Latin *dies irae*; "wrath" is קֶצֶף); צָרָה וּמְצוּקָה "distress and straits"; שֹׁאָה וּמְשׁוֹאָה "devastation and desolation" (a play of sounds, *shoah umeshoah*); חֹשֶׁךְ וַאֲפֵלָה "darkness and gloom"; עָנָן וַעֲרָפֶל "cloud and dark cloud" (singular).
- **"A day of the trumpet and alarm against the fenced cities, and against the high towers" → "a day of shofar and blast against the fortified cities and against the high corners"** (v16) — Hebrew שׁוֹפָר (token); תְּרוּעָה with the shofar "blast"; בְּצֻרוֹת "fortified"; הַפִּנּוֹת הַגְּבֹהוֹת "the high corners" (the corner towers of the walls).
- **"And I will bring distress upon men, that they shall walk like blind men, because they have sinned against the LORD: and their blood shall be poured out as dust, and their flesh as the dung" → "And I will bring distress on man, and they shall walk like the blind, for against Yahweh they have sinned; and their blood shall be poured out like dust, and their flesh like dung"** (v17) — Hebrew הֲצֵרֹתִי לָאָדָם "I will distress man"; וְהָלְכוּ "and they shall walk"; לְחֻמָם "their flesh" (found only here and Job 20:23; "their bowels" or "their sap" are also proposed; uncertain); גְּלָלִים "dung".
- **"Neither their silver nor their gold shall be able to deliver them in the day of the LORD's wrath; but the whole land shall be devoured by the fire of his jealousy: for he shall make even a speedy riddance of all them that dwell in the land" → "Neither their silver nor their gold shall be able to deliver them on the day of the raging of Yahweh; and in the fire of his jealousy all the earth shall be consumed; for a full end, indeed a terrifying one, he will make of all the inhabitants of the earth"** (v18) — Hebrew גַּם … גַּם "also … also" (with the negative "neither … nor"); עֶבְרַת "raging" as v15; וּבְאֵשׁ קִנְאָתוֹ "and in the fire of his jealousy" (קִנְאָה "jealousy" or "zeal"); תֵּאָכֵל, אָכַל "consume"; כָּלָה אַךְ־נִבְהָלָה "a full end, only (= indeed) a terrifying one" (נִבְהָל "be dismayed": "sudden" in KJV); הָאָרֶץ "the earth" (or "the land": the one word, twice).
