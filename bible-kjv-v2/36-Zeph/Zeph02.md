# Zephaniah 2

[1] Gather together, yes, gather,
O shameless nation,

[2] before the decree takes effect —
before the day passes away like chaff —
before there comes upon you
the burning anger of Yahweh,
before there comes upon you
the day of the anger of Yahweh.

[3] Seek Yahweh, all you humble of the land,
who do his just commands;
seek righteousness; seek humility;
perhaps you may be hidden
on the day of the anger of Yahweh.

[4] For Gaza shall be deserted,
and Ashkelon shall become a desolation;
Ashdod's people shall be driven out at noon,
and Ekron shall be uprooted.

[5] Woe to you inhabitants of the seacoast,
you nation of the Cherethites!
The word of Yahweh is against you,
O Canaan, land of the Philistines;
and I will destroy you until no inhabitant is left.

[6] And you, O seacoast, shall be pastures,
with meadows for shepherds
and folds for flocks.

[7] The seacoast shall become the possession
of the remnant of the house of Judah,
on which they shall graze,
and in the houses of Ashkelon they shall lie down at evening.
For Yahweh their *Elohim* will be mindful of them
and restore their fortunes.

[8] "I have heard the taunts of Moab
and the revilings of the Ammonites,
how they have taunted my people
and made boasts against their territory.

[9] Therefore, as I live," declares Yahweh of Hosts,
the *Elohim* of Israel,
"Moab shall become like Sodom,
and the Ammonites like Gomorrah,
a land possessed by nettles and salt pits,
and a waste forever.
The remnant of my people shall plunder them,
and the survivors of my nation shall possess them."

[10] This shall be their lot in return for their pride,
because they taunted and boasted
against the people of Yahweh of Hosts.

[11] Yahweh will be awesome against them;
for he will famish all the gods of the earth,
and to him shall bow down,
each in its place,
all the lands of the nations.

[12] You also, O Cushites,
shall be slain by my sword.

[13] And he will stretch out his hand against the north
and destroy Assyria,
and he will make Nineveh a desolation,
a dry waste like the desert.

[14] Herds shall lie down in her midst,
all kinds of beasts;
even the owl and the hedgehog
shall lodge in her capitals;
a voice shall hoot in the window;
devastation will be on the threshold;
for her cedar work will be laid bare.

[15] This is the exultant city
that lived securely,
that said in her heart,
"I am, and there is no one else."
What a desolation she has become,
a lair for wild beasts!
Everyone who passes by her
hisses and shakes his fist.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (vv9, 10) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת *Yahweh Tzeva'ot*; the military-covenantal divine title preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv7, 9) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*; "Yahweh their *Elohim*" and "the *Elohim* of Israel."
- **"nation not desired" → "shameless nation"** (v1) — Hebrew גּוֹי לֹא נִכְסָף *goy lo nikhsaf*; *nikhsaf* = longed for/desired/shameless; "shameless" captures the sense of a nation without longing for God.
- **"Before the decree bring forth" → "before the decree takes effect"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּטֶרֶם לֶדֶת חֹק *betérem ledet choq*; "takes effect" for *ledet* = be born/take effect; modernized.
- **"all ye meek of the earth, which have wrought his judgment" → "all you humble of the land, who do his just commands"** (v3) — Hebrew כָּל עַנְוֵי הָאָרֶץ אֲשֶׁר מִשְׁפָּטוֹ פְּעָלְתֶּם *kol anvey ha'aretz asher mishpato pe'altem*; "humble" for *anav*; "just commands" for *mishpat*.
- **"the Lord their God shall visit them, and turn away their captivity" → "Yahweh their *Elohim* will be mindful of them and restore their fortunes"** (v7) — Hebrew וּפְקָדָם יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵיהֶם וְשָׁב שְׁבוּתָם *ufaqadam Yahweh Eloheyhem vesháv shevutam*; "mindful of them" for *paqad* = visit/attend to; "restore their fortunes" for *shav shevut* (standard formula); not literal captivity.
- **"all the isles of the heathen" → "all the lands of the nations"** (v11) — Hebrew כָּל אִיֵּי הַגּוֹיִם *kol iyei hagoyim*; *iyei* = coastlands/shores/distant lands; "nations" for *goyim* consistently.
- **"Ye Ethiopians also" → "You also, O Cushites"** (v12) — Hebrew גַּם אַתֶּם כּוּשִׁים *gam attem Kushim*; *Kush* = the region south of Egypt (Sudan/Ethiopia); Hebrew name retained.
- **"both the cormorant and the bittern shall lodge in the upper lintels" → "even the owl and the hedgehog shall lodge in her capitals"** (v14) — Hebrew קָאַת וְקִפֹּד *qa'at veqippod*; *qa'at* = owl/pelican; *qippod* = hedgehog/porcupine; "owl and hedgehog" more accurate than KJV "cormorant and bittern"; "capitals" for the column tops.
- **"the rejoicing city that dwelt carelessly" → "the exultant city that lived securely"** (v15) — Hebrew הָעִיר הָעַלִּיזָה הַיּוֹשֶׁבֶת לָבֶטַח *ha'ir ha'allizah hayyoshevet lavetach*; "exultant" for *alliz* = jubilant/arrogant; "securely" for *lavetach* = in security/without care.
- **"wag his hand" → "shakes his fist"** (v15) — Hebrew יָנֹד יָדוֹ *yanod yado* = waves/shakes his hand; "shakes his fist" captures the contemptuous gesture.
