# Isaiah 7

[1] And it came to pass in the days of ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ son of ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧, son of ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, that ⟦rezin|Retzin|Rezin⟧ king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ and ⟦pekah|Peqach|Pekah⟧ son of ⟦remaliah|Remalyahu|Remaliah⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ for war against it, and he was not able to fight against it.

[2] And it was told to the house of David, saying, "⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ has rested upon ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧." And his heart and the heart of his people swayed as the trees of the forest sway before a wind.

[3] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧, "Go out, please, to meet ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧, you and ⟦shear-jashub|Shear Yashuv|Shear-jashub⟧ your son, at the end of the conduit of the upper pool, at the highway of the field of the washer.

[4] And say to him:

'Take heed, and be quiet; do not fear,
and let not your heart be soft
because of the two tails of these smoking firebrands,
at the burning of anger of ⟦rezin|Retzin|Rezin⟧ and ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ and the son of ⟦remaliah|Remalyahu|Remaliah⟧.

[5] Because ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ has counseled evil against you, ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ and the son of ⟦remaliah|Remalyahu|Remaliah⟧, saying,

[6] "Let us go up against ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and terrify it,
and let us break it open for ourselves,
and let us make a king reign in its midst, the son of ⟦tabeel|Taveel|Tabeel⟧,"

[7] thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:
"It shall not stand, and it shall not be.

[8] For the head of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ is ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧,
and the head of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧ is ⟦rezin|Retzin|Rezin⟧;
and within yet sixty-five years ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ shall be shattered from being a people.

[9] And the head of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ is ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧,
and the head of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ is the son of ⟦remaliah|Remalyahu|Remaliah⟧.
If you do not believe,
surely you shall not be made firm."'"

[10] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ again spoke to ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧, saying,

[11] "Ask for yourself a sign from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; make it deep, ask, or make it high above."

[12] And ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ said, "I will not ask, and I will not test ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[13] And he said, "Hear, please, house of David! Is it too little for you to weary men, that you weary my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ also?

[14] Therefore ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ himself will give you a sign: Behold, the young woman is with child and shall bear a son, and she shall call his name ⟦immanuel|Immanu El|Immanuel⟧.

[15] Curds and honey he shall eat, by the time he knows to reject the evil and choose the good.

[16] For before the boy knows to reject the evil and choose the good, the ground whose two kings you dread shall be forsaken.

[17] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will bring upon you and upon your people and upon the house of your father days that have not come since the day that ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ turned aside from upon ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ — the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧."

[18] And it shall be in that day: ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will whistle for the fly that is at the end of the ⟦nile|Yeorim|Niles⟧ of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and for the bee that is in the land of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧.

[19] And they shall come and rest, all of them, in the wadis of the precipices, and in the clefts of the crags, and on all the thornbushes, and on all the watering places.

[20] In that day ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ will shave with the hired razor, in the parts across the River, with the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, the head and the hair of the feet; and also the beard it shall sweep away.

[21] And it shall be in that day: a man shall keep alive a heifer of the herd and two of the flock.

[22] And it shall be, from the abundance of making milk, he shall eat curds; for curds and honey shall everyone eat who remains in the midst of the land.

[23] And it shall be in that day: every place where there are a thousand vines at a thousand of silver shall be for briers and for thorns.

[24] With the arrows and with the bow one shall come there, for briers and thorns all the land shall be.

[25] And all the mountains that are hoed with the hoe: the fear of briers and thorns shall not come there; and it shall be for the letting loose of the ox and for the trampling of the sheep.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"Lord" → "Adonai"** (v14, 20) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי. **"the Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v7) — אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v11, 13) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים; "my Elohim" (v13) is the prophet's word.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept (v2, 3, 10, 12, 13, 19). **"And it came to pass"** (v1) — וַיְהִי. **"And it shall be"** (v18, 21–23) — וְהָיָה, kept.
- **"but could not prevail against it" → "and he was not able to fight against it"** (v1) — Hebrew וְלֹא יָכֹל, singular (2 Kgs 16:5 has the plural "they were not able"); לְהִלָּחֵם "to fight"; לַמִּלְחָמָה "for war".
- **"Syria is confederate with Ephraim" → "Aram has rested upon Ephraim"** (v2) — Hebrew נָחָה עַל, "has rested, settled upon" (encamped in Ephraim's land, or leaned on it as an ally: the word does not say which). **"And his heart was moved" → "And his heart … swayed"** — וַיָּנַע, as נוּעַ "sway"; "his heart" (the name Ahaz is not repeated); מִפְּנֵי־רוּחַ "before a wind", no article.
- **"Go forth now" → "Go out, please"** (v3) — Hebrew צֵא־נָא. **"Shear-jashub"** — the name שְׁאָר יָשׁוּב means "a remnant shall return" (the meaning was in the verse; it stands here). **"in the highway of the fuller's field" → "at the highway of the field of the washer"** — as 2 Kgs 18:17; Isa 36:2.
- **"Take heed, and be quiet"** (v4) — Hebrew הִשָּׁמֵר וְהַשְׁקֵט, kept. **"neither be fainthearted" → "let not your heart be soft"** — לְבָבְךָ אַל־יֵרַךְ. **"for the two tails of these smoking firebrands, for the fierce anger" → "because of the two tails of these smoking firebrands, at the burning of anger"** — מִן "from, because of"; בָּחֳרִי־אַף "in the burning of anger".
- **"have taken evil counsel against thee" → "has counseled evil against you"** (v5) — Hebrew יָעַץ … רָעָה, singular verb with Aram, then "Ephraim and the son of Remaliah" (no "with").
- **"vex it, and let us make a breach therein for us, and set a king in the midst of it" → "terrify it, and let us break it open for ourselves, and let us make a king reign in its midst"** (v6) — Hebrew נְקִיצֶנָּה, hifil of קוּץ "dread" (uncertain: also "tear apart"); נַבְקִעֶנָּה אֵלֵינוּ, hifil of בָּקַע "split", literally "split it open to us"; וְנַמְלִיךְ מֶלֶךְ. **"the son of Tabeal" → "the son of Tabeel"** — the MT points טָבְאַל *Tabal* ("good-for-nothing"); the name is טָבְאֵל "El is good", as the token has it.
- **"neither shall it come to pass" → "and it shall not be"** (v7) — Hebrew וְלֹא תִהְיֶה.
- **"within threescore and five years shall Ephraim be broken, that it be not a people" → "within yet sixty-five years Ephraim shall be shattered from being a people"** (v8) — Hebrew וּבְעוֹד, "and in yet"; יֵחַת מֵעָם, literally "be shattered from a people".
- **"If ye will not believe, surely ye shall not be established" → "If you do not believe, surely you shall not be made firm"** (v9) — Hebrew אִם לֹא תַאֲמִינוּ כִּי לֹא תֵאָמֵנוּ: הֶאֱמִין "believe" (hifil) and נֶאֱמַן "be made firm" (nifal) are one root, אמן: "if you do not hold firm, you shall not be held firm"; כִּי "surely" (or "for": "for you are not made firm"). Plural "you": the house of David.
- **"Moreover the LORD spake again" → "And Yahweh again spoke … saying"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיּוֹסֶף יְהוָה דַּבֵּר, literally "and Yahweh added to speak"; לֵאמֹר "saying".
- **"Ask thee a sign of the LORD thy God; ask it either in the depth, or in the height above" → "Ask for yourself a sign from Yahweh your Elohim; make it deep, ask, or make it high above"** (v11) — Hebrew שְׁאַל־לְךָ; מֵעִם "from"; הַעְמֵק שְׁאָלָה אוֹ הַגְבֵּהַּ לְמָעְלָה: two hifil imperatives, "make deep … make high"; שְׁאָלָה as pointed is the imperative "ask"; the same letters pointed שְׁאֹלָה would be "to Sheol" (so most translations); "heaven" is not in the verse (לְמָעְלָה "upward").
- **"neither will I tempt" → "and I will not test"** (v12) — Hebrew נִסָּה "test".
- **"Hear ye now, O house of David" → "Hear, please, house of David"** (v13) — Hebrew שִׁמְעוּ־נָא; no "O". **"Is it a small thing for you" → "Is it too little for you"** — הַמְעַט מִכֶּם.
- **"a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel" → "the young woman is with child and shall bear a son, and she shall call his name Immanu El"** (v14) — Hebrew הָעַלְמָה, with the article: "the young woman", a girl of marriageable age; the word does not say "virgin" (that is בְּתוּלָה). הָרָה is an adjective, "with child" (as Gen 16:11; Judg 13:5), not a future "shall conceive"; יֹלֶדֶת a participle, "is about to bear". וְקָרָאת may be the third person ("she shall call") or the second ("you shall call"). עִמָּנוּ אֵל, two words in the Hebrew: "with us is El". Matt 1:23 quotes the verse in Greek: Ἰδοὺ ἡ παρθένος ἐν γαστρὶ ἕξει … καὶ καλέσουσιν, "Behold, the virgin shall have in the womb … and they shall call"; παρθένος is the Septuagint's word (from memory), and Matthew explains the name μεθʼ ἡμῶν ὁ θεός "with us God". The Hebrew is translated as it stands.
- **"Butter and honey shall he eat, that he may know to refuse the evil" → "Curds and honey he shall eat, by the time he knows to reject the evil"** (v15) — Hebrew חֶמְאָה "curds"; לְדַעְתּוֹ, "at his knowing" (the לְ may also be "so that he may know"); מָאַס בְּ "reject", בָּחַר בְּ "choose".
- **"the child" → "the boy"** (v16) — Hebrew הַנַּעַר. **"the land that thou abhorrest shall be forsaken of both her kings" → "the ground whose two kings you dread shall be forsaken"** — הָאֲדָמָה "the ground"; אַתָּה קָץ מִפְּנֵי שְׁנֵי מְלָכֶיהָ, "you are in dread from the face of its two kings"; תֵּעָזֵב "shall be forsaken".
- **"thy father's house, days that have not come … that Ephraim departed" → "the house of your father days that have not come … that Ephraim turned aside from upon Judah"** (v17) — Hebrew בֵּית אָבִיךָ; סוּר … מֵעַל.
- **"the uttermost part of the rivers of Egypt" → "the end of the Niles of Egypt"** (v18) — Hebrew יְאֹרֵי מִצְרָיִם, plural of יְאֹר "Nile": its arms and canals (as Exod 7:19).
- **"rest all of them in the desolate valleys … upon all thorns, and upon all bushes" → "rest, all of them, in the wadis of the precipices … on all the thornbushes, and on all the watering places"** (v19) — Hebrew נָחוּ "rest"; נַחֲלֵי הַבַּתּוֹת, "wadis of the steep places" (בַּתָּה only here: uncertain); נְקִיקֵי הַסְּלָעִים "clefts of the crags"; נַעֲצוּצִים "thornbushes"; נַהֲלֹלִים "watering places" (uncertain: also "pastures").
- **"with a razor that is hired, namely, by them beyond the river, by the king of Assyria" → "with the hired razor, in the parts across the River, with the king of Assyria"** (v20) — Hebrew בְּתַעַר הַשְּׂכִירָה (article); בְּעֶבְרֵי נָהָר "in the parts across the River" (the Euphrates); "the hair of the feet" (רַגְלַיִם, perhaps a covering word for the private parts: so most commentators); תִּסְפֶּה "it shall sweep away" (the razor).
- **"a man shall nourish a young cow, and two sheep" → "a man shall keep alive a heifer of the herd and two of the flock"** (v21) — Hebrew יְחַיֶּה, piel of חָיָה "let live, keep alive"; עֶגְלַת בָּקָר; שְׁתֵּי־צֹאן, צֹאן the flock of sheep and goats.
- **"for the abundance of milk that they shall give" → "from the abundance of making milk"** (v22) — Hebrew מֵרֹב עֲשׂוֹת חָלָב, an infinitive with no subject; עָשָׂה "make", as 5:2. **"left in the land" → "who remains in the midst of the land"** — הַנּוֹתָר בְּקֶרֶב הָאָרֶץ.
- **"a thousand vines at a thousand silverlings, it shall even be for briers and thorns" → "a thousand vines at a thousand of silver shall be for briers and for thorns"** (v23) — Hebrew בְּאֶלֶף כָּסֶף, the unit (shekels) is not named; שָׁמִיר וָשַׁיִת as 5:6.
- **"With arrows and with bows shall men come thither" → "With the arrows and with the bow one shall come there"** (v24) — Hebrew בַּחִצִּים וּבַקֶּשֶׁת, articles, "bow" singular; יָבוֹא with no subject.
- **"on all hills that shall be digged with the mattock, there shall not come thither the fear of briers and thorns: but it shall be for the sending forth of oxen, and for the treading of lesser cattle" → "all the mountains that are hoed with the hoe: the fear of briers and thorns shall not come there; and it shall be for the letting loose of the ox and for the trampling of the sheep"** (v25) — Hebrew הֶהָרִים "the mountains"; לֹא־תָבוֹא שָׁמָּה יִרְאַת שָׁמִיר וָשָׁיִת: יִרְאַת "the fear of" is read as the subject, as the KJV; the verb may also be "you shall not come there", with "fear" as the reason ("for fear of briers and thorns"); מִשְׁלַח שׁוֹר "the letting loose of the ox", מִרְמַס שֶׂה "the trampling of the sheep" (singular collectives).

### Notes
- **v14** — the Immanuel sign; quoted in Matt 1:23 (Greek, after the Septuagint).
- **v9** — the play on one root: *im lo ta'aminu ki lo te'amenu*.
