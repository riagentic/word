# Isaiah 51

[1] Listen to me, you who pursue righteousness,
you who seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:
look to the rock from which you were hewn,
and to the hole of the pit from which you were dug.

[2] Look to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ your father
and to Sarah who was in labor with you;
for one I called him,
that I might bless him and multiply him.

[3] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has comforted ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧;
he has comforted all her ruins,
and he has set her wilderness like Eden,
and her steppe like the garden of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
joy and gladness shall be found in her,
thanksgiving and the sound of music.

[4] Give heed to me, my people,
and my folk, give ear to me;
for ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ shall go out from me,
and my judgment for a light of peoples I will settle.

[5] Near is my righteousness,
my salvation has gone out,
and my arms shall judge peoples;
for me the coastlands wait,
and for my arm they hope.

[6] Lift up your eyes to the heavens,
and look to the earth beneath;
for the heavens like smoke vanish,
and the earth like a garment shall wear out,
and those who dwell in it shall die in like manner;
but my salvation shall be forever,
and my righteousness shall not be shattered.

[7] Listen to me, you who know righteousness,
the people with my ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ in their heart:
do not fear the reproach of mortal man,
and at their revilings do not be dismayed.

[8] For like a garment a moth shall eat them,
and like wool a grub shall eat them;
but my righteousness shall be forever,
and my salvation to the generation of generations.

[9] Awake, awake, put on strength, arm of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!
Awake as in the days of old,
the generations of ages!
Is it not you who hewed ⟦rahab-monster|Rahav|Rahab⟧,
who pierced the sea monster?

[10] Is it not you who dried up the sea,
the waters of the great deep,
who made the deep places of the sea a way
for the redeemed to pass over?

[11] And the ransomed of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall return
and come to ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ with a ringing cry,
and everlasting gladness upon their head;
joy and gladness they shall overtake;
sorrow and sighing have fled.

[12] I, I am he who comforts you;
who are you, and you are afraid of mortal man, who dies,
and of a son of man, who is made grass?

[13] And you have forgotten ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your Maker,
who stretches out the heavens and founds the earth,
and you dread continually all the day
because of the fury of the one who straitens,
as he made ready to ruin.
And where is the fury of the one who straitens?

[14] The one bowed down hastens to be set loose,
and he shall not die to the pit,
and his bread shall not lack.

[15] And I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
who stirs up the sea, and its waves roar;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts is his name.

[16] And I have put my words in your mouth,
and in the shade of my hand I have covered you,
to plant the heavens and to found the earth,
and to say to ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, "My people you are."

[17] Rouse yourself, rouse yourself, arise, ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
you who have drunk from the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the cup of his fury;
the bowl of the cup of reeling you have drunk, you have drained.

[18] There is none guiding her
of all the sons she has borne,
and there is none taking hold of her hand
of all the sons she has brought up.

[19] Two are these that befall you —
who shall show grief for you? —
the havoc and the breaking, and the famine and the sword;
by whom shall I comfort you?

[20] Your sons have fainted;
they have lain at the head of all streets
like an antelope in a net,
who are full of the fury of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
the rebuke of your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[21] Therefore hear, please, this, afflicted one,
and drunk, and not from wine:

[22] Thus says your Lord, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who contends for his people:

"Behold, I have taken from your hand the cup of reeling,
the bowl of the cup of my fury;
you shall drink it no more again.

[23] And I will put it in the hand of those who torment you,
who have said to your soul,
'Bow down, that we may pass over';
and you have set your back like the earth,
and like the street for those who pass over."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v15) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת. **"God" → "Elohim"** (v15, 20, 22).
- **Layout and speech marks** (throughout) — one Hebrew colon per printed line: v9a ("Awake, awake, put on strength, arm of Yahweh") is one colon, as are v13b, v16c, v17 (three cola, it was six lines) and v22 (three cola, it was two lines, merged and reordered); the halves of v9b stay apart. The quotation marks the earlier text set around v1–8 and v12–16 are taken out (no word of speaking introduces them, as in 48); the marks of v16, v22–23 stay.
- **Vocatives** (v9, 17, 21) — no "O": the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"Hearken to me" → "Listen to me"** (v1, 7) — Hebrew שִׁמְעוּ אֵלַי (שָׁמַע אֶל "listen to", its row). **"ye that follow after righteousness" → "you who pursue righteousness"** — רֹדְפֵי צֶדֶק (רָדַף "pursue", its row). **"look unto the rock whence ye are hewn, and to the hole of the pit whence ye are digged" → "look to the rock from which you were hewn, and to the hole of the pit from which you were dug"** — הַבִּיטוּ אֶל (הִבִּיט "look to"), צוּר "rock", חֻצַּבְתֶּם pual of חָצַב "hew" (the same verb as v9), מַקֶּבֶת "hole, quarry" (from נָקַב "bore"), בּוֹר "pit" (its row), נֻקַּרְתֶּם pual of נָקַר "dig out"; "from which" is English for the bare relative.
- **"and unto Sarah that bare you" → "and to Sarah who was in labor with you"** (v2) — Hebrew תְּחוֹלֶלְכֶם, polel of חִיל "writhe, be in labor" (the Deut 32:18 row). **"for I called him alone" → "for one I called him"** — כִּי־אֶחָד קְרָאתִיו, אֶחָד "one" (a single man). **"and blessed him, and increased him" → "that I might bless him and multiply him"** — וַאֲבָרְכֵהוּ וְאַרְבֵּהוּ: as pointed, "and" with imperfects (not the narrative form), after the perfect "I called": the purpose or the outcome of the calling; "and I blessed him and multiplied him" (KJV) reads them as past. הִרְבָּה, hifil of רָבָה "multiply" (its row).
- **"For the LORD shall comfort Zion: he will comfort all her waste places" → "For Yahweh has comforted Zion; he has comforted all her ruins"** (v3) — Hebrew נִחַם twice, perfects (said of what is certain to come; English keeps the perfect); חָרְבָּה "ruin" (its row; also 49:19). **"and he will make her wilderness like Eden" → "and he has set her wilderness like Eden"** — וַיָּשֶׂם, wayyiqtol of שִׂים "set"; מִדְבָּר "wilderness". **"and her desert like the garden of the LORD" → "and her steppe like the garden of Yahweh"** — עֲרָבָה, the dry plain (the name of the Arabah, Deut 1:1, as a common noun). **"joy and gladness shall be found therein"** — שָׂשׂוֹן "joy" (as Ps 105:43), שִׂמְחָה "gladness" (its row), the same pair in v11. **"thanksgiving, and the voice of melody" → "thanksgiving and the sound of music"** — תּוֹדָה "thanksgiving"; קוֹל of a thing "sound" (its row); זִמְרָה "music" (the Ps 98–102 row).
- **"Hearken unto me, my people; and give ear unto me, O my nation" → "Give heed to me, my people, and my folk, give ear to me"** (v4) — Hebrew הַקְשִׁיבוּ אֵלַי (הִקְשִׁיב "give heed"), וּלְאוּמִּי (לְאֹם "folk", reviewer row; "nation" is גּוֹי), הַאֲזִינוּ (הֶאֱזִין "give ear to"), in the Hebrew order. **"for a law shall proceed from me" → "for torah shall go out from me"** — תוֹרָה without the article (the token kept). **"and I will make my judgment to rest for a light of the people" → "and my judgment for a light of peoples I will settle"** — אַרְגִּיעַ, hifil of רָגַע "be at rest": "make rest, settle" (uncertain; the same consonants are רֶגַע "a moment", and some join the word to v5: "in a moment my righteousness is near"); the Masoretic accents keep it at the end of v4; עַמִּים plural. מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (its row; "justice" was not the word).
- **"My righteousness is near" → "Near is my righteousness"** (v5) — Hebrew קָרוֹב צִדְקִי, the Hebrew order; יֶשַׁע "salvation" (beside יְשׁוּעָה v6, 8: one English word, two nouns). **"the isles shall wait upon me, and on mine arm shall they trust" → "for me the coastlands wait, and for my arm they hope"** — אֵלַי אִיִּים יְקַוּוּ (קִוָּה "wait for", its row), וְאֶל־זְרֹעִי יְיַחֵלוּן (יִחֵל "hope", beside קִוָּה); אִי "coastland".
- **"for the heavens shall vanish away like smoke" → "for the heavens like smoke vanish"** (v6) — Hebrew נִמְלָחוּ, a perfect, found only here ("be torn in pieces, dispersed": uncertain); the Hebrew order. **"and the earth shall wax old like a garment" → "and the earth like a garment shall wear out"** — תִּבְלֶה (בָּלָה, its row). **"and they that dwell therein shall die in like manner"** — כְּמוֹ־כֵן "in like manner" (as pointed; the consonants also allow "like gnats", כֵּן: uncertain). **"and my righteousness shall not be abolished" → "and my righteousness shall not be shattered"** — לֹא תֵחָת, חָתַת "be shattered" (the row of 1 Sam 2:4; of persons "be dismayed", v7).
- **"the people in whose heart is my law" → "the people with my torah in their heart"** (v7) — Hebrew עַם תּוֹרָתִי בְלִבָּם, "people — my torah in their heart". **"fear ye not the reproach of men, neither be ye afraid of their revilings" → "do not fear the reproach of mortal man, and at their revilings do not be dismayed"** — חֶרְפַּת אֱנוֹשׁ (חֶרְפָּה "reproach"; אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man", the standing decision, also v12), וּמִגִּדֻּפֹתָם אַל־תֵּחָתּוּ (גִּדּוּף "reviling"; חָתַת of persons "be dismayed").
- **"For the moth shall eat them up like a garment, and the worm shall eat them like wool" → "For like a garment a moth shall eat them, and like wool a grub shall eat them"** (v8) — Hebrew כַבֶּגֶד יֹאכְלֵם עָשׁ וְכַצֶּמֶר יֹאכְלֵם סָס, the Hebrew order; עָשׁ "moth" (as 50:9), סָס found only here, the moth's grub (uncertain). **"and my salvation from generation to generation" → "and my salvation to the generation of generations"** — לְדוֹר דּוֹרִים.
- **"Awake, awake, put on strength, O arm of the LORD"** (v9) — Hebrew עוּרִי עוּרִי לִבְשִׁי־עֹז זְרוֹעַ יְהוָה, feminine imperatives (the arm, זְרוֹעַ, is feminine); עֹז "strength" (its row). **"as in the ancient days, in the generations of old" → "as in the days of old, the generations of ages"** — כִּימֵי קֶדֶם דֹּרוֹת עוֹלָמִים, no "in" before the second (it stands beside "days"). **"Art thou not it that hath cut Rahab, and wounded the dragon?" → "Is it not you who hewed Rahav, who pierced the sea monster?"** — Hebrew הֲלוֹא אַתְּ־הִיא הַמַּחְצֶבֶת רַהַב מְחוֹלֶלֶת תַּנִּין: חָצַב "hew" (as v1; the Isaiah scroll reads המוחצת "who struck through", from memory); רַהַב keeps its token `rahab-monster` (the sea monster of the old poetry, as Job 26:12; Ps 89:10; a name for Egypt in Ps 87:4; Isa 30:7); מְחוֹלֶלֶת, polel of חָלַל "pierce" (the same form as "was in labor with" in v2, there from חִיל: one form, two roots); תַּנִּין "sea monster" (its row, as Job 7:12; Ps 74:13; set beside the sea and the deep of v10; exact sense uncertain; not "whale"; "dragon" is KJV). No token key exists for תַּנִּין: plain text.
- **"Art thou not it which hath dried the sea" → "Is it not you who dried up the sea"** (v10) — Hebrew הַמַּחֲרֶבֶת יָם (hifil of חָרֵב "dry up", as 50:2), מֵי תְּהוֹם רַבָּה "the waters of the great deep" (תְּהוֹם "deep", its row). **"that hath made the depths of the sea a way for the ransomed to pass over?" → "who made the deep places of the sea a way for the redeemed to pass over?"** — הַשָּׂמָה, מַעֲמַקֵּי־יָם (מַעֲמַקִּים "deep places", the Ps 69–71 row), גְּאוּלִים "the redeemed" (גָּאַל "redeem"; v11 has פְּדוּיִם "the ransomed": two roots, kept apart), לַעֲבֹר "to pass over" (עָבַר, its row).
- **"Therefore the redeemed of the LORD shall return" → "And the ransomed of Yahweh shall return"** (v11) — Hebrew וּפְדוּיֵי יְהוָה, "and" (not "therefore"); פָּדָה "ransom" (its row). **"and come with singing unto Zion" → "and come to Zion with a ringing cry"** — בְּרִנָּה (its row). **"and everlasting joy shall be upon their head" → "and everlasting gladness upon their head"** — וְשִׂמְחַת עוֹלָם (עוֹלָם as a genitive "everlasting", its row), no verb; שִׂמְחָה "gladness" as in the next line (שָׂשׂוֹן "joy"). **"they shall obtain gladness and joy" → "joy and gladness they shall overtake"** — שָׂשׂוֹן וְשִׂמְחָה יַשִּׂיגוּן, the Hebrew order of the nouns (as v3); הִשִּׂיג "overtake, reach". **"and sorrow and mourning shall flee away" → "sorrow and sighing have fled"** — נָסוּ, a perfect; יָגוֹן "sorrow" (its row), אֲנָחָה "sighing" (the Job 3 row).
- **"I, even I, am he that comforteth you" → "I, I am he who comforts you"** (v12) — Hebrew אָנֹכִי אָנֹכִי הוּא מְנַחֶמְכֶם, "you" plural. **"who art thou, that thou shouldest be afraid of a man that shall die" → "who are you, and you are afraid of mortal man, who dies"** — מִי־אַתְּ וַתִּירְאִי, "you" feminine singular, with "and"; אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man" (as v7); יָמוּת imperfect. **"and of the son of man which shall be made as grass" → "and of a son of man, who is made grass"** — וּמִבֶּן־אָדָם (no article), חָצִיר יִנָּתֵן, literally "grass is he given (made)"; no "as".
- **"And forgettest the LORD thy maker" → "And you have forgotten Yahweh your Maker"** (v13) — Hebrew וַתִּשְׁכַּח, wayyiqtol; "your" now masculine singular. **"that hath stretched forth the heavens, and laid the foundations of the earth" → "who stretches out the heavens and founds the earth"** — נוֹטֶה … וְיֹסֵד, participles. **"and hast feared continually every day" → "and you dread continually all the day"** — וַתְּפַחֵד (פִּחֵד, verb of פַּחַד "dread"). **"because of the fury of the oppressor, as if he were ready to destroy" → "because of the fury of the one who straitens, as he made ready to ruin"** — מִפְּנֵי "because of" (its row), חֵמָה "fury" (its row), הַמֵּצִיק, participle of הֵצִיק "straiten" (its row; לָחַץ is "oppress"), כַּאֲשֶׁר כּוֹנֵן לְהַשְׁחִית: כּוֹנֵן "make ready, establish" (no "as if"), הִשְׁחִית "ruin" (the שָׁחַת row).
- **"The captive exile hasteneth that he may be loosed" → "The one bowed down hastens to be set loose"** (v14) — Hebrew צֹעֶה, participle of צָעָה "bend, stoop" (the one bowed under a load or in chains; "captive exile" is an explanation); לְהִפָּתֵחַ, nifal of פָּתַח "be opened, set loose"; מִהַר "hasten" (its row). **"and that he should not die in the pit" → "and he shall not die to the pit"** — וְלֹא־יָמוּת לַשַּׁחַת (שַׁחַת "pit", the Ps 28–31 row; לְ "to"). **"nor that his bread should fail" → "and his bread shall not lack"** — וְלֹא יֶחְסַר, חָסֵר "lack" (its row).
- **"But I am the LORD thy God, that divided the sea, whose waves roared" → "And I am Yahweh your Elohim, who stirs up the sea, and its waves roar"** (v15) — Hebrew וְאָנֹכִי, "and"; רֹגַע הַיָּם, participle of רָגַע "stir up" (as Job 26:12; the same root as "settle" in v4, there hifil); וַיֶּהֱמוּ "and they roar" (הָמָה).
- **"And I have put my words in thy mouth" → "And I have put my words in your mouth"** (v16) — Hebrew וָאָשִׂים, wayyiqtol. **"and I have covered thee in the shadow of mine hand" → "and in the shade of my hand I have covered you"** — צֵל "shade" (as 49:2), the Hebrew order; כִּסָּה "cover". **"that I may plant the heavens, and lay the foundations of the earth" → "to plant the heavens and to found the earth"** — לִנְטֹעַ, נָטַע "plant", as the Masoretic Text has it (the earlier "establishing" was not the word; "stretch out", as v13, is a common correction); יָסַד "found". **"Thou art my people" → "My people you are"** — עַמִּי־אָתָּה, the Hebrew order.
- **"Awake, awake, stand up, O Jerusalem" → "Rouse yourself, rouse yourself, arise, Jerusalem"** (v17) — Hebrew הִתְעוֹרְרִי, hitpolel of עוּר (v9 has the qal עוּרִי "awake"); קוּמִי "arise". **"which hast drunk at the hand of the LORD the cup of his fury" → "you who have drunk from the hand of Yahweh the cup of his fury"** — חֵמָה "fury" (its row), as v13, 20, 22. **"thou hast drunken the dregs of the cup of trembling, and wrung them out" → "the bowl of the cup of reeling you have drunk, you have drained"** — אֶת־קֻבַּעַת כּוֹס הַתַּרְעֵלָה "the bowl of the cup of reeling" (קֻבַּעַת "bowl, goblet"; תַּרְעֵלָה "reeling", as Ps 60:3), שָׁתִית מָצִית, two verbs without "and" (מָצָה "drain", its row).
- **"There is none to guide her among all the sons whom she hath brought forth" → "There is none guiding her of all the sons she has borne"** (v18) — Hebrew אֵין־מְנַהֵל לָהּ, participle of נֵהֵל "guide"; מִכָּל־בָּנִים "of all sons". **"neither is there any that taketh her by the hand" → "and there is none taking hold of her hand"** — וְאֵין מַחֲזִיק בְּיָדָהּ. **"of all the sons that she hath brought up"** — גִּדֵּל, as 49:21.
- **"These two things are come unto thee; who shall be sorry for thee?" → "Two are these that befall you — who shall show grief for you? —"** (v19) — Hebrew שְׁתַּיִם הֵנָּה קֹרְאֹתַיִךְ, "two (feminine) are they", קֹרְאֹתַיִךְ a participle of קָרָא = קָרָה "befall" (its row); מִי יָנוּד לָךְ, נוּד לְ "show grief" (lit. "sway for": the Job 42:11 row). **"desolation, and destruction, and the famine, and the sword" → "the havoc and the breaking, and the famine and the sword"** — הַשֹּׁד וְהַשֶּׁבֶר וְהָרָעָב וְהַחֶרֶב, all four with the article; שֹׁד "havoc" (the Prov 21–24 row), שֶׁבֶר "breaking" (not the שֶׁבֶר "grain" of Genesis). "Two" and the four nouns are the Hebrew's own count (two pairs). **"by whom shall I comfort thee?"** — מִי אֲנַחֲמֵךְ, literally "who — I shall comfort you?"; the Isaiah scroll, the Greek and the Syriac have "who will comfort you?" (from memory); the verse keeps the Masoretic first person.
- **"they lie at the head of all the streets, as a wild bull in a net" → "they have lain at the head of all streets like an antelope in a net"** (v20) — Hebrew שָׁכְבוּ, perfect; כָּל־חוּצוֹת without the article; כְּתוֹא מִכְמָר: תְּאוֹ "antelope" (the Deut 14:5 row; the animal is uncertain), מִכְמָר "net". **"they are full of the fury of the LORD" → "who are full of the fury of Yahweh"** — הַמְלֵאִים, with the article; גְּעָרָה "rebuke".
- **"Therefore hear now this, thou afflicted, and drunken, but not with wine" → "Therefore hear, please, this, afflicted one, and drunk, and not from wine"** (v21) — Hebrew שִׁמְעִי־נָא, נָא "please" (OT.md); עֲנִיָּה "afflicted one" (עָנִי, its row), וּשְׁכֻרַת וְלֹא מִיָּיִן, "and … and not from wine".
- **"Thus saith thy Lord the LORD, and thy God that pleadeth the cause of his people" → "Thus says your Lord, Yahweh, and your Elohim, who contends for his people"** (v22) — Hebrew אֲדֹנַיִךְ "your Lord" (אָדוֹן with a suffix, plural of majesty: no `adonai` token, as Isa 1:24), וֵאלֹהַיִךְ "and your Elohim" (the "and" kept), יָרִיב עַמּוֹ (רִיב "contend"; "for" carries the sense). **"Behold, I have taken out of thine hand the cup of trembling, even the dregs of the cup of my fury; thou shalt no more drink it again" → "Behold, I have taken from your hand the cup of reeling, the bowl of the cup of my fury; you shall drink it no more again"** — the three cola of the Hebrew, in its order (the earlier text merged the second and third and left out "the cup"); לֹא־תוֹסִיפִי לִשְׁתּוֹתָהּ עוֹד, literally "you shall not add to drink it again" (the יָסַף row: "no more again").
- **"But I will put it into the hand of them that afflict thee" → "And I will put it in the hand of those who torment you"** (v23) — Hebrew וְשַׂמְתִּיהָ, "and"; מוֹגַיִךְ, hifil participle of יָגָה "grieve, torment". **"which have said to thy soul" → "who have said to your soul"** — לְנַפְשֵׁךְ, נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (the plain word). **"Bow down, that we may go over" → "Bow down, that we may pass over"** — שְׁחִי וְנַעֲבֹרָה, cohortative with "and"; עָבַר "pass over" (as v10). **"and thou hast laid thy body as the ground" → "and you have set your back like the earth"** — וַתָּשִׂימִי … גֵּוֵךְ, גֵּו "back" (as 50:6), כָאָרֶץ "like the earth" (אֶרֶץ, its row; אֲדָמָה is "ground"). **"and as the street, to them that went over" → "and like the street for those who pass over"** — וְכַחוּץ לַעֹבְרִים.

### Notes
- **v9** — ***Rahav***: the sea monster of the old poetry (Job 9:13; 26:12; Ps 89:10), beside תַּנִּין and the sea and the deep of v10; v10 then names the drying of the sea and the way for the redeemed (Exod 14–15).
