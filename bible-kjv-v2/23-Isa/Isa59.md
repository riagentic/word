# Isaiah 59

[1] Behold, the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is not too short to save,
and his ear is not too heavy to hear;

[2] but your iniquities have been separating
between you and your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and your sins have hidden the face from you,
so as not to hear.

[3] For your palms are defiled with blood,
and your fingers with iniquity;
your lips have spoken falsehood;
your tongue utters injustice.

[4] There is none calling in righteousness,
and none entering into judgment in faithfulness;
trusting in emptiness, and speaking what is empty,
conceiving toil, and begetting wickedness.

[5] Eggs of an adder they hatch,
and webs of a spider they weave;
he who eats of their eggs dies,
and the crushed one hatches out a viper.

[6] Their webs shall not become a garment,
and they shall not cover themselves with their works;
their works are works of wickedness,
and the work of violence is in their palms.

[7] Their feet run to evil,
and they hasten to shed innocent blood;
their thoughts are thoughts of wickedness;
havoc and breaking are in their highways.

[8] The way of ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ they have not known,
and there is no judgment in their courses;
their tracks they have made crooked for themselves;
everyone who treads on it does not know ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧.

[9] Therefore judgment is far from us,
and righteousness does not overtake us;
we wait for light, and behold, darkness;
for brightness — in gloom we walk.

[10] We grope like the blind for the wall,
and like those without eyes we grope;
we have stumbled at noon as in the twilight,
among the stout ones like the dead.

[11] We growl like bears, all of us,
and like doves we surely moan;
we wait for judgment, and there is none;
for salvation — it is far from us.

[12] For our transgressions have become many before you,
and our sins answer against us;
for our transgressions are with us,
and our iniquities, we know them:

[13] transgressing, and lying to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and turning back from after our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
speaking oppression and turning aside,
conceiving and uttering from the heart words of falsehood.

[14] And judgment is turned back backward,
and righteousness stands far off;
for truth has stumbled in the square,
and uprightness cannot come in.

[15] And the truth was lacking,
and he who turns aside from evil makes himself a prey.
And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ saw, and it was evil in his eyes
that there was no judgment.

[16] And he saw that there was no man,
and he was appalled that there was none interceding;
and his arm saved for him,
and his righteousness, it upheld him.

[17] And he put on righteousness like armor,
and a helmet of salvation on his head;
and he put on garments of vengeance as clothing,
and wrapped himself in zeal like a robe.

[18] According to dealings, accordingly he will repay:
fury to his adversaries, dealing to his enemies;
to the coastlands he will repay dealing.

[19] And they shall fear the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from the west,
and his glory from the rising of the sun;
for he will come like the narrow river,
that the wind of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ drives on.

[20] And a redeemer will come to ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
and to those who turn back from transgression in ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[21] "And I — this is my covenant with them," says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: "My spirit that is upon you, and my words that I have put in your mouth, shall not depart from your mouth, and from the mouth of your seed, and from the mouth of the seed of your seed," says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "from now and forever."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv2, 13) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **"peace" → "shalom"** (v8) — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם, token `shalom` in every place.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; the existing lines stand; v21 stays prose.
- **"the LORD'S hand is not shortened, that it cannot save; neither his ear heavy, that it cannot hear" → "the hand of Yahweh is not too short to save, and his ear is not too heavy to hear"** (v1) — Hebrew לֹא־קָצְרָה יַד־יְהוָה מֵהוֹשִׁיעַ וְלֹא־כָבְדָה אָזְנוֹ מִשְּׁמוֹעַ: מִן of comparison, literally "short from saving … heavy from hearing"; כָּבֵד "heavy".
- **"But your iniquities have separated" → "but your iniquities have been separating"** (v2) — Hebrew כִּי אִם־עֲוֹנֹתֵיכֶם הָיוּ מַבְדִּלִים: כִּי אִם "but rather" after the negatives of v1; הָיוּ with a participle, a lasting action; הִבְדִּיל "separate" as 56:3. **"have hid his face from you, that he will not hear" → "have hidden the face from you, so as not to hear"** — הִסְתִּירוּ פָנִים מִכֶּם מִשְּׁמוֹעַ: פָנִים has no "his"; מִשְּׁמוֹעַ as in v1, literally "from hearing"; "he" is not written.
- **"your hands" → "your palms"** (v3, 6) — Hebrew כַּפֵּיכֶם, כַּף "palm". **"defiled"** — נְגֹאֲלוּ, from גָּאַל II "defile" (another root of the same letters as "redeem", v20). **"your lips have spoken lies, your tongue hath muttered perverseness" → "your lips have spoken falsehood; your tongue utters injustice"** — שֶׁקֶר "falsehood"; עַוְלָה "injustice"; תֶהְגֶּה imperfect, הָגָה of plotting "utter" (as v13).
- **"None calleth for justice, nor any pleadeth for truth" → "There is none calling in righteousness, and none entering into judgment in faithfulness"** (v4) — Hebrew אֵין־קֹרֵא בְצֶדֶק וְאֵין נִשְׁפָּט בֶּאֱמוּנָה: קָרָא "call" (in a court: summon); צֶדֶק "righteousness"; נִשְׁפָּט nifal of שָׁפַט "enter into judgment" (as 2 Chr 22:8); אֱמוּנָה "faithfulness". **"they trust in vanity, and speak lies; they conceive mischief, and bring forth iniquity" → "trusting in emptiness, and speaking what is empty, conceiving toil, and begetting wickedness"** — four infinitives absolute, without a subject: בָּטוֹחַ עַל־תֹּהוּ (תֹּהוּ "emptiness", as 40:17), דַּבֶּר־שָׁוְא (שָׁוְא "what is empty": a second word, kept apart), הָרוֹ עָמָל (עָמָל "toil"), הוֹלֵיד אָוֶן (הוֹלִיד "beget"; אָוֶן "wickedness", not עָוֹן "iniquity").
- **"They hatch cockatrice' eggs" → "Eggs of an adder they hatch"** (v5) — Hebrew בֵּיצֵי צִפְעוֹנִי בִּקֵּעוּ, object first; בִּקַּע "split open" (of eggs: "hatch"); צִפְעוֹנִי "adder" (the kind of snake is not certain). **"and that which is crushed breaketh out into a viper" → "and the crushed one hatches out a viper"** — וְהַזּוּרֶה תִּבָּקַע אֶפְעֶה: זוּרֶה "the crushed (egg)"; תִּבָּקַע nifal of the same בָּקַע; אֶפְעֶה "viper".
- **"Their webs shall not become garments" → "Their webs shall not become a garment"** (v6) — Hebrew לְבֶגֶד, singular. **"their works are works of iniquity, and the act of violence is in their hands" → "their works are works of wickedness, and the work of violence is in their palms"** — אָוֶן "wickedness" (also v7); פֹּעַל beside מַעֲשֶׂה, two words for "work" (English has one).
- **"they make haste to shed innocent blood" → "they hasten to shed innocent blood"** (v7) — Hebrew וִימַהֲרוּ לִשְׁפֹּךְ דָּם נָקִי. **"wasting and destruction are in their paths" → "havoc and breaking are in their highways"** — שֹׁד וָשֶׁבֶר בִּמְסִלּוֹתָם: שֹׁד "havoc", שֶׁבֶר "breaking", מְסִלָּה "highway". Rom 3:15–17 quotes this in the Greek, shortened (ὀξεῖς οἱ πόδες αὐτῶν ἐκχέαι αἷμα, "their feet are swift to shed blood"; σύντριμμα καὶ ταλαιπωρία "ruin and misery in their ways"; then, for v8, καὶ ὁδὸν εἰρήνης οὐκ ἔγνωσαν "and the way of peace they have not known"); the verses follow the Hebrew.
- **"The way of peace they know not; and there is no judgment in their goings" → "The way of shalom they have not known, and there is no judgment in their courses"** (v8) — Hebrew דֶּרֶךְ שָׁלוֹם לֹא יָדָעוּ, perfect; מַעְגָּל "course". **"they have made them crooked paths" → "their tracks they have made crooked for themselves"** — נְתִיבוֹתֵיהֶם עִקְּשׁוּ לָהֶם; נְתִיבָה "track". **"whosoever goeth therein shall not know peace" → "everyone who treads on it does not know shalom"** — כֹּל דֹּרֵךְ בָּהּ, בָּהּ "on it" (singular: the way).
- **"neither doth justice overtake us" → "and righteousness does not overtake us"** (v9) — Hebrew צְדָקָה "righteousness" (as v14, 16, 17). **"we wait for light, but behold obscurity; for brightness, but we walk in darkness" → "we wait for light, and behold, darkness; for brightness — in gloom we walk"** — נְקַוֶּה, קִוָּה "wait for" (also v11); חֹשֶׁךְ "darkness"; נְגֹהוֹת "brightness" and אֲפֵלוֹת "gloom" are plural; no "but".
- **"We grope for the wall like the blind, and we grope as if we had no eyes" → "We grope like the blind for the wall, and like those without eyes we grope"** (v10) — Hebrew נְגַשְׁשָׁה כַעִוְרִים קִיר וּכְאֵין עֵינַיִם נְגַשֵּׁשָׁה, the Hebrew order. **"we stumble at noonday as in the night" → "we have stumbled at noon as in the twilight"** — כָּשַׁלְנוּ perfect; נֶשֶׁף "twilight". **"we are in desolate places as dead men" → "among the stout ones like the dead"** — בָּאַשְׁמַנִּים, only here: "among the stout (the vigorous)", or "in dark / desolate places"; the meaning is uncertain.
- **"We roar all like bears, and mourn sore like doves" → "We growl like bears, all of us, and like doves we surely moan"** (v11) — Hebrew נֶהֱמֶה … הָגֹה נֶהְגֶּה: הָמָה "growl"; הָגָה of an animal "moan", the infinitive absolute with its verb ("moaning we moan"). **"but there is none; for salvation, but it is far off from us" → "and there is none; for salvation — it is far from us"** — וָאַיִן; no "but".
- **"are multiplied before thee" → "have become many before you"** (v12) — Hebrew רַבּוּ. **"our sins testify against us" → "our sins answer against us"** — חַטֹּאותֵינוּ עָנְתָה בָּנוּ: עָנָה בְּ "answer against" (= testify against; kept); the verb is singular beside the plural subject. **"and as for our iniquities, we know them" → "and our iniquities, we know them"** — וַעֲוֹנֹתֵינוּ יְדַעֲנוּם.
- **"In transgressing and lying against the LORD, and departing away from our God" → "transgressing, and lying to Yahweh, and turning back from after our Elohim"** (v13) — Hebrew פָּשֹׁעַ וְכַחֵשׁ בַּיהוָה וְנָסוֹג מֵאַחַר אֱלֹהֵינוּ, infinitives absolute: כִּחֵשׁ בְּ "lie to"; נָסוֹג, nifal of סוּג "turn back" (the verb of v14). **"speaking oppression and revolt" → "speaking oppression and turning aside"** — סָרָה, from סוּר "turn aside" (as v15). **"conceiving and uttering from the heart words of falsehood"** — הֹרוֹ וְהֹגוֹ, הָגָה "utter".
- **"judgment is turned away backward" → "judgment is turned back backward"** (v14) — Hebrew וְהֻסַּג אָחוֹר, hofal of סוּג (as v13). **"justice standeth afar off" → "righteousness stands far off"** — צְדָקָה. **"truth is fallen in the street" → "truth has stumbled in the square"** — כָּשְׁלָה "stumble" (as v10); רְחוֹב "square". **"equity cannot enter" → "uprightness cannot come in"** — נְכֹחָה "uprightness" (root of 57:2); לָבוֹא "come".
- **"Yea, truth faileth" → "And the truth was lacking"** (v15) — Hebrew וַתְּהִי הָאֱמֶת נֶעְדֶּרֶת: wayyiqtol, with the article; נֶעְדֶּרֶת "lacking". **"he that departeth from evil" → "he who turns aside from evil"** — סָר מֵרָע, סוּר "turn aside". **"maketh himself a prey"** — מִשְׁתּוֹלֵל, reflexive of שָׁלַל "plunder". **"and the LORD saw it, and it displeased him" → "And Yahweh saw, and it was evil in his eyes"** — וַיַּרְא יְהוָה וַיֵּרַע בְּעֵינָיו: no "it" after "saw"; "evil in his eyes" as Gen 38:10.
- **"wondered that there was no intercessor" → "he was appalled that there was none interceding"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁתּוֹמֵם, from שָׁמֵם "be desolate, be appalled"; מַפְגִּיעַ "one interceding" (as 53:12). **"therefore his arm brought salvation unto him" → "and his arm saved for him"** — וַתּוֹשַׁע לוֹ זְרֹעוֹ, הוֹשִׁיעַ "save"; וַ "and", not "therefore". **"it sustained him" → "it upheld him"** — סְמָכָתְהוּ, סָמַךְ.
- **"as a breastplate" → "like armor"** (v17) — Hebrew כַּשִּׁרְיָן, the body armor of 1 Sam 17:5. **"and was clad with zeal as a cloke" → "and wrapped himself in zeal like a robe"** — וַיַּעַט כַּמְּעִיל קִנְאָה: עָטָה "wrap oneself"; מְעִיל "robe" (as 1 Sam 15:27); קִנְאָה "zeal" (also "jealousy": one word). The verse begins with וַ "And" (not "For").
- **"According to their deeds, accordingly he will repay" → "According to dealings, accordingly he will repay"** (v18) — Hebrew כְּעַל גְּמֻלוֹת כְּעַל יְשַׁלֵּם, literally "as upon dealings, as upon (them) he will repay"; גְּמוּל "dealing" (what one has done to others), no "their"; שִׁלֵּם "repay". **"recompence to his enemies; to the islands he will repay recompence" → "dealing to his enemies; to the coastlands he will repay dealing"** — גְּמוּל, the same word three times; אִיִּים "coastlands"; חֵמָה "fury"; צַר "adversary", אֹיֵב "enemy".
- **"So shall they fear" → "And they shall fear"** (v19) — Hebrew וְיִירְאוּ; מַעֲרָב "west" (literally "the setting"). **"When the enemy shall come in like a flood, the Spirit of the LORD shall lift up a standard against him" → "for he will come like the narrow river, that the wind of Yahweh drives on"** — כִּי־יָבוֹא כַנָּהָר צָר רוּחַ יְהוָה נֹסְסָה בוֹ: כִּי "for"; צָר is "narrow" (a pent-up river) or a noun, "an adversary" / "distress" ("for an adversary will come like the river"); נֹסְסָה, of uncertain root: "drives on" (from נוּס), or "raises a banner against" (from נֵס, the KJV). רוּחַ is "spirit", "wind" and "breath" in one word: "wind" beside the river; the KJV reads "the Spirit of Yahweh". The Hebrew allows both readings of the line; the first is followed.
- **"And the Redeemer shall come to Zion" → "And a redeemer will come to Zion"** (v20) — Hebrew וּבָא לְצִיּוֹן גּוֹאֵל: גֹּאֵל "redeemer" without the article; לְצִיּוֹן "to Zion". **"unto them that turn from transgression in Jacob" → "and to those who turn back from transgression in Jacob"** — וּלְשָׁבֵי פֶשַׁע, literally "and to the turners of transgression", with וּ "and". **"saith the LORD" → "declares Yahweh"** — נְאֻם יְהוָה. Rom 11:26 quotes the Greek: ἥξει ἐκ Σιὼν ὁ ῥυόμενος, ἀποστρέψει ἀσεβείας ἀπὸ Ἰακώβ ("the deliverer will come from Zion, he will turn away ungodliness from Jacob"); the Hebrew has "to Zion" and "to those who turn back from transgression"; the verse follows the Hebrew.
- **"As for me, this is my covenant with them" → "And I — this is my covenant with them"** (v21) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי זֹאת בְּרִיתִי אוֹתָם; Rom 11:27 continues from this line (καὶ αὕτη αὐτοῖς ἡ παρʼ ἐμοῦ διαθήκη). The "you" of the rest of the verse is singular. **"shall not depart out of thy mouth, nor out of the mouth of thy seed, nor out of the mouth of thy seed's seed" → "shall not depart from your mouth, and from the mouth of your seed, and from the mouth of the seed of your seed"** — מִפִּיךָ וּמִפִּי זַרְעֲךָ וּמִפִּי זֶרַע זַרְעֲךָ: וּ "and" twice; זֶרַע "seed". **"from henceforth and for ever" → "from now and forever"** — מֵעַתָּה וְעַד־עוֹלָם.

### Notes
- **v17** — armor imagery cited in Eph 6:14-17.
- **v20-21** — quoted Rom 11:26-27.
