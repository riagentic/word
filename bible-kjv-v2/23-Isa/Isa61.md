# Isaiah 61

[1] The spirit of ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ is upon me,
because ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has anointed me
to bring news to the humble;
he has sent me to bind up the broken of heart,
to proclaim liberty to the captives,
and to the bound, opening;

[2] to proclaim a year of favor for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and a day of vengeance for our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
to comfort all who mourn;

[3] to set for the mourners of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
to give them a headdress instead of ashes,
the oil of joy instead of mourning,
a wrap of praise instead of a dim spirit;
and they shall be called terebinths of righteousness,
the planting of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to glorify himself.

[4] And they shall build the ruins of old,
the desolations of former times they shall raise up;
and they shall renew the cities of waste,
the desolations of generation and generation.

[5] And strangers shall stand and shepherd your flock,
and foreigners shall be your plowmen and your vinedressers.

[6] And you, the priests of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ you shall be called,
the ministers of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ it shall be said of you;
the wealth of nations you shall eat,
and in their glory you shall boast yourselves.

[7] Instead of your shame, double;
and instead of dishonor they shall shout for joy over their share;
therefore in their land they shall possess double;
everlasting gladness shall be theirs.

[8] For I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, love judgment,
hating robbery with injustice;
and I will give their wage in truth,
and an everlasting covenant I will cut for them.

[9] And their seed shall be known among the nations,
and their offspring in the midst of the peoples;
all who see them shall recognize them,
that they are a seed ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has blessed.

[10] I will surely exult in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
my soul shall be glad in my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
for he has clothed me with the garments of salvation,
with the robe of righteousness he has wrapped me,
as the bridegroom acts the priest with a headdress,
and as the bride adorns herself with her ornaments.

[11] For as the earth brings forth its sprout,
and as a garden causes what is sown in it to spring up,
so ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ will cause righteousness and praise
to spring up before all the nations.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"Lord GOD" → "the Lord Yahweh"** (v1, 11) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both names (token `adonai-yhwh`). **"God" → "Elohim"** (v2, 6, 10).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; kept (v4–6, 8, 9), also where KJV has "but" (v6).
- **"The Spirit of the Lord GOD" → "The spirit of the Lord Yahweh"** (v1) — Hebrew רוּחַ, "spirit, wind, breath" (one word; here the spirit of Adonai Yahweh); no capital, as the Hebrew has none. **"to preach good tidings unto the meek" → "to bring news to the humble"** — לְבַשֵּׂר, the verb of bringing news (good news is meant: the word itself says "news"); עֲנָוִים "humble, bowed low" (as Num 12:3), not "meek". **"the brokenhearted" → "the broken of heart"** — נִשְׁבְּרֵי־לֵב. **"liberty"** — דְּרוֹר, release (as Lev 25:10, the release of the Jubilee). **"and the opening of the prison to them that are bound" → "and to the bound, opening"** — Hebrew וְלַאֲסוּרִים פְּקַח־קוֹחַ: "to the bound" first, then פְּקַח־קוֹחַ, a doubled form of פָּקַח "open" (literally "opening-opening", a full opening); "of the prison" is not in the Hebrew. פָּקַח is said elsewhere of opening the eyes (2 Kgs 6:17; Isa 42:7), hence the reading "opening of the eyes" for those bound in darkness; some manuscripts write it as one word.
- **Luke 4:18–19** (v1–2) — read in the synagogue at Nazareth. The Greek there has, beside the Hebrew of these verses, τυφλοῖς ἀνάβλεψιν "recovering of sight to the blind" (where the Hebrew has "to the bound, opening"), ἀποστεῖλαι τεθραυσμένους ἐν ἀφέσει "to send away the crushed in release" (the words of Isa 58:6), no "to bind up the broken of heart", and ends with "a year of the Lord, acceptable", before "and a day of vengeance". The Septuagint is said to have "recovering of sight to the blind" here as well (from memory). The Hebrew is translated as it stands.
- **"the acceptable year of the LORD" → "a year of favor for Yahweh"** (v2) — Hebrew שְׁנַת־רָצוֹן לַיהוָה, no article; רָצוֹן "favor" (as 60:7, 10); לַיהוָה "for Yahweh, Yahweh's". **"the day of vengeance of our God" → "a day of vengeance for our Elohim"** — וְיוֹם נָקָם לֵאלֹהֵינוּ, the same build. **"all that mourn" → "all who mourn"** — אֲבֵלִים; נִחַם piel "comfort".
- **"To appoint unto them that mourn in Zion" → "to set for the mourners of Zion"** (v3) — Hebrew לָשׂוּם לַאֲבֵלֵי צִיּוֹן, "to set / to put for": the object follows only after לָתֵת "to give" (the first verb stays without one, as in the Hebrew). **"beauty for ashes" → "a headdress instead of ashes"** — פְּאֵר, a headdress or turban (as v10; Exod 39:28), a play on אֵפֶר "ashes" (same letters reordered); תַּחַת "instead of" three times. **"the oil of joy for mourning"** — שֶׁמֶן שָׂשׂוֹן "oil of joy"; אֵבֶל "mourning". **"the garment of praise for the spirit of heaviness" → "a wrap of praise instead of a dim spirit"** — מַעֲטֵה, a wrap or mantle (from עָטָה "wrap", as v10); רוּחַ כֵּהָה, "dim, faint" (as a lamp or the eyes grow dim). **"that they might be called" → "and they shall be called"** — וְקֹרָא, pual perfect with וְ: a statement, not a purpose. **"trees of righteousness" → "terebinths of righteousness"** — אֵילֵי הַצֶּדֶק, the great trees (as 1:29; the word is also "rams", 60:7); kind not certain. **"that he might be glorified" → "to glorify himself"** — לְהִתְפָּאֵר, hitpael (as 60:21); "himself" (Yahweh) is supplied for English.
- **"the old wastes" → "the ruins of old"** (v4) — Hebrew חָרְבוֹת עוֹלָם, "ruins of the age" (of time long past). **"the former desolations" → "the desolations of former times"** — שֹׁמְמוֹת רִאשֹׁנִים, "of the former ones" (times or people); Hebrew order "… they shall raise up". **"they shall repair the waste cities" → "they shall renew the cities of waste"** — חִדֵּשׁ "renew, make new"; עָרֵי חֹרֶב, "cities of waste" (חֹרֶב "dryness"). **"of many generations" → "of generation and generation"** — דּוֹר וָדוֹר (as 60:15). The subject "they" is not named (the mourners of v3, or those of 60:10).
- **"feed your flocks" → "shepherd your flock"** (v5) — Hebrew רָעָה "shepherd", צֹאנְכֶם, one collective word; "your" is now plural (the people addressed). **"the sons of the alien" → "foreigners"** — בְּנֵי נֵכָר (as 60:10).
- **"But ye shall be named the Priests of the LORD: men shall call you the Ministers of our God" → "And you, the priests of Yahweh you shall be called, the ministers of our Elohim it shall be said of you"** (v6) — Hebrew וְאַתֶּם, "And you" first; יֵאָמֵר לָכֶם "it shall be said of / to you" ("men" is not in the Hebrew); מְשָׁרְתֵי "ministers" (as Exod 24:13); no capitals. **"the riches of the Gentiles" → "the wealth of nations"** — חֵיל גּוֹיִם (as 60:5). **"shall ye boast yourselves"** — תִּתְיַמָּרוּ, a rare form: taken from אָמַר (hitpael "boast, speak of oneself") or from מוּר "exchange" ("enter into their glory in exchange"): uncertain.
- **"For your shame ye shall have double; and for confusion they shall rejoice in their portion" → "Instead of your shame, double; and instead of dishonor they shall shout for joy over their share"** (v7) — Hebrew תַּחַת בָּשְׁתְּכֶם מִשְׁנֶה, no verb; מִשְׁנֶה "double" (twice in the verse); וּכְלִמָּה יָרֹנּוּ חֶלְקָם, literally "and dishonor they shall shout their share": "instead of" carried on from the first line, "over" supplied (the line is hard); כְּלִמָּה "dishonor"; רָנַן "shout for joy"; חֵלֶק "share". The person changes from "your" to "they", as in the Hebrew. **"everlasting joy shall be unto them" → "everlasting gladness shall be theirs"** — שִׂמְחַת עוֹלָם (שִׂמְחָה "gladness"; שָׂשׂוֹן "joy" of v3 is the other word).
- **"I hate robbery for burnt offering" → "hating robbery with injustice"** (v8) — Hebrew שֹׂנֵא, a participle like אֹהֵב "loving"; גָזֵל בְּעוֹלָה: the pointed word may be עַוְלָה "injustice" written short (as Job 5:16; Ps 92:15) or עוֹלָה "burnt offering" (the same letters and vowels; KJV "robbery for burnt offering"); the parallel with מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" favors "injustice": uncertain. **"I will direct their work in truth" → "I will give their wage in truth"** — וְנָתַתִּי פְעֻלָּתָם, "I will give their wage" (פְּעֻלָּה, literally "work", what is earned: as Lev 19:13; 62:11); בֶּאֱמֶת "in truth" (not "faithfully"). **"I will make an everlasting covenant with them" → "an everlasting covenant I will cut for them"** — Hebrew order; כָּרַת בְּרִית "cut a covenant"; לָהֶם "for them".
- **"among the Gentiles" → "among the nations"** (v9). **"and their offspring among the people" → "and their offspring in the midst of the peoples"** — צֶאֱצָאֵיהֶם "what comes forth from them"; בְּתוֹךְ הָעַמִּים. **"acknowledge" → "recognize"** — הִכִּיר. **"the seed which the LORD hath blessed" → "a seed Yahweh has blessed"** — זֶרַע בֵּרַךְ יְהוָה, no article, no relative word; זֶרַע "seed" (twice in the verse).
- **"I will greatly rejoice in the LORD, my soul shall be joyful in my God" → "I will surely exult in Yahweh; my soul shall be glad in my Elohim"** (v10) — Hebrew שׂוֹשׂ אָשִׂישׂ, infinitive absolute with its verb (literally "exulting I will exult"), rendered "surely"; שׂוּשׂ "exult", גִּיל "be glad" (two verbs). **"he hath covered me with the robe of righteousness" → "with the robe of righteousness he has wrapped me"** — Hebrew order; מְעִיל "robe"; יְעָטָנִי, from יָעַט (a by-form of עָטָה "wrap"). **"salvation"** — יֶשַׁע. **"as a bridegroom decketh himself with ornaments" → "as the bridegroom acts the priest with a headdress"** — Hebrew כֶּחָתָן יְכַהֵן פְּאֵר: כִּהֵן is the verb "be priest, act as priest" (as Exod 28:41): he puts on the headdress as a priest puts on the turban (פְּאֵר as v3; Exod 39:28 of the priests' headdresses); the article "the" as the Hebrew. **"and as a bride adorneth herself with her jewels" → "and as the bride adorns herself with her ornaments"** — כֵלֶיהָ, literally "her articles" (כְּלִי, any made thing): her finery; "jewels" is narrower.
- **"her bud" → "its sprout"** (v11) — Hebrew צִמְחָהּ (צֶמַח "sprout", as 4:2). **"to spring forth" → "to spring up"** — הִצְמִיחַ "cause to spring up" (twice). **"the garden" → "a garden"**; **"the things that are sown in it"** — זֵרוּעֶיהָ "its sown things".

### Notes
- **v1–2** — read by Yeshua in the synagogue at Nazareth (Luke 4:16–21).
- **v2** — "a year of favor" recalls the year of release of Lev 25:10 (the word דְּרוֹר "liberty" of v1).
