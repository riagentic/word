# Isaiah 44

[1] And now hear, ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ my servant,
and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, whom I have chosen.

[2] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who made you and formed you from the belly, who will help you:

"Do not fear, ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ my servant,
and ⟦jeshurun|Yeshurun|Jeshurun⟧, whom I have chosen.

[3] For I will pour water on the thirsty,
and streams on the dry land;
I will pour my spirit on your seed,
and my blessing on your offspring.

[4] And they shall spring up among the grass,
like willows by watercourses.

[5] This one shall say, 'I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh's|the LORD's⟧,'
and this one shall call by the name of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and this one shall write on his hand, 'Of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,'
and by the name of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall give a title."

[6] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the King of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and his Redeemer, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts:

"I am first and I am last,
and apart from me there is no ⟦elohim|Elohim|god⟧.

[7] And who is like me? Let him call out,
and let him tell it and arrange it for me,
since I set the everlasting people;
and the things to come, and what shall come, let them tell to them.

[8] Do not dread and do not be afraid;
have I not made you hear from long ago, and told it?
And you are my witnesses.
Is there ⟦eloah|an Eloah|a God⟧ apart from me?
And there is no Rock; I know none."

[9] The formers of a carved image, all of them are emptiness,
and the things they desire do not profit;
and their witnesses, they do not see and do not know,
so that they shall be ashamed.

[10] Who has formed ⟦el|an el|a god⟧ and cast a carved image
to profit nothing?

[11] Behold, all his companions shall be ashamed,
and the craftsmen, they are of mankind.
Let them all gather themselves, let them stand;
they shall dread, they shall be ashamed together.

[12] A craftsman of iron, a cutting tool: and he works in the charcoal, and with hammers he forms it, and he works it with the arm of his power. Also he is hungry, and there is no power; he has not drunk water, and he faints.

[13] A craftsman of wood stretched out a line; he marks it out with a stylus; he makes it with planes, and with the compass he marks it out; and he makes it like the pattern of a man, like the beauty of mankind, to dwell in a house.

[14] To cut down cedars for himself — and he took a cypress and an oak, and he made it strong for himself among the trees of the forest; he planted a pine, and the rain makes it grow.

[15] And it becomes for a man for burning, and he takes from them and warms himself; also he kindles and bakes bread; also he works ⟦el|an el|a god⟧ and bows down; he makes it a carved image and falls down to them.

[16] Half of it he has burned in the fire; on half of it he eats flesh; he roasts a roast and is sated. Also he warms himself and says, "Aha, I am warm, I have seen the blaze!"

[17] And its remnant he makes into ⟦el|an el|a god⟧, into his carved image; he falls down to it and bows down and prays to it, and says, "Deliver me, for you are my ⟦el|el|god⟧!"

[18] They do not know and do not understand, for he has smeared over their eyes from seeing, their hearts from having insight.

[19] And he does not bring back to his heart, and there is no knowledge and no understanding, to say, "Half of it I have burned in the fire, and also I have baked bread on its coals; I roast flesh and eat; and the rest of it shall I make into an abomination? To a block of wood shall I fall down?"

[20] He feeds on ashes; a cheated heart has turned him aside, and he does not deliver his soul, and does not say, "Is there not falsehood in my right hand?"

[21] Remember these, ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, for you are my servant;
I formed you, a servant to me you are;
⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, you shall not be forgotten by me.

[22] I have wiped out your transgressions like a thick cloud,
and your sins like a cloud;
return to me, for I have redeemed you.

[23] Shout for joy, heavens, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has done it;
shout, lowest parts of the earth;
break forth into a ringing cry, mountains,
forest, and every tree in it!
For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has redeemed ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ he will glorify himself.

[24] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, your Redeemer, and he who formed you from the belly:

"I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who makes all,
who stretches out the heavens alone,
who spreads out the earth by myself,

[25] who makes void the signs of the babblers
and makes diviners mad,
who turns wise men back
and makes their knowledge foolish,

[26] who makes the word of his servant stand
and completes the counsel of his messengers,
who says to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, 'She shall be inhabited,'
and to the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, 'They shall be built,
and her ruins I will raise up';

[27] who says to the deep, 'Be dry,
and your rivers I will dry up';

[28] who says to **⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧**, 'My shepherd,
and all my delight he shall complete';
and saying to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, 'She shall be built,'
and the temple, 'You shall be founded.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v6) — divine title preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v6) — Hebrew *Elohim*; here of any god ("no god" in the traditional form).
- **"Cyrus" — first named explicitly** (v28) — bolded in text to mark the extraordinary prophetic specificity. Hebrew כּוֹרֶשׁ *Koresh*.
- **"God" → "Eloah"** (v8) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ *Eloah*, not *Elohim*.
- **Layout** — v1–11 and v21–28 one Hebrew line per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v12–20 (the maker of the image) stay prose, as before, translated word for word.
- **"Yet now hear, O Jacob" → "And now hear, Jacob"** (v1) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה "and now"; no "O" before a name (the Hebrew has no word for it). "and Israel" keeps its וְ.
- **"formed thee from the womb" → "formed you from the belly"** (v2, 24) — Hebrew מִבֶּטֶן; בֶּטֶן is "belly", רֶחֶם "womb" (46:3 has both). **"Fear not … and thou, Jesurun" → "Do not fear … and Jeshurun"** — אַל־תִּירָא; "thou" is not written.
- **"upon him that is thirsty, and floods" → "on the thirsty, and streams"** (v3) — Hebrew צָמֵא, "thirsty" (no "land" or "him" written: a person or the ground); נֹזְלִים "streams", literally "flowing ones"; יַבָּשָׁה "dry land". **"seed … offspring"** — זֶרַע "seed", צֶאֱצָאִים "offspring" (literally "what comes out").
- **"And they shall spring up … by the water courses"** (v4) — Hebrew וְצָמְחוּ with its "and"; בְּבֵין חָצִיר, literally "in between grass" (the versions read "like between waters", from memory; MT kept); יִבְלֵי־מָיִם "watercourses"; עֲרָבִים "willows" (kind uncertain).
- **"One … another … another" → "This one … and this one … and this one"** (v5) — Hebrew זֶה … וְזֶה … וְזֶה. **"shall call himself by the name of"** → "shall call by the name of": יִקְרָא בְשֵׁם, "himself" is not written. **"subscribe with his hand unto the LORD" → "write on his hand, 'Of Yahweh'"** — יִכְתֹּב יָדוֹ לַיהוָה: "his hand" stands without a preposition ("on" or "with" his hand; the Hebrew leaves it open); לַיהוָה "of / belonging to Yahweh", as "I am Yahweh's" (לַיהוָה אָנִי) in the first line. **"surname himself" → "give a title"** — יְכַנֶּה, piel of כִּנָּה "give a title, an honorific name" (as 45:4; Job 32:21); "himself" is not written.
- **"his redeemer" → "his Redeemer"** (v6) — גֹּאֲלוֹ "his redeemer" (with a capital as a title of Yahweh, as v24). **"I am the first, and I am the last" → "I am first and I am last"** — רִאשׁוֹן … אַחֲרוֹן without the article. **"beside me" → "apart from me"** (v6, 8) — מִבַּלְעָדַי "apart from me" (as 45:6, 21; זוּלָתִי is "besides me", 45:5, 21).
- **"who, as I, shall call … since I appointed the ancient people" → "who is like me? Let him call out … since I set the everlasting people"** (v7) — Hebrew יִקְרָא "let him call (out)"; יַגִּידֶהָ "let him tell it" (הִגִּיד "tell"); יַעְרְכֶהָ "arrange it" (עָרַךְ, as of a case set in order); מִשּׂוּמִי עַם־עוֹלָם "from my setting the people of the age", עוֹלָם as a genitive "everlasting" (literally "of the age"; "ancient people" is the other way of taking it). **"the things that are coming" → "the things to come"** — אֹתִיּוֹת (participle of אָתָה "come", as 45:11). **"let them shew unto them" → "let them tell to them"** — לָמוֹ "to them" (or "for themselves"; open).
- **"Fear ye not, neither be afraid" → "Do not dread and do not be afraid"** (v8) — Hebrew תִּפְחֲדוּ (פָּחַד "dread", as v11) and תִּרְהוּ (only here; "be afraid", uncertain). **"have not I told thee from that time" → "have I not made you hear from long ago, and told it"** — הִשְׁמַעְתִּיךָ "I made you hear" (you singular; the next clause has plural "you"); מֵאָז "from long ago" (literally "from then"). **"yea, there is no God" → "And there is no Rock"** — צוּר "rock", a name of Yahweh here (capital); "I know none" — בַּל־יָדָעְתִּי, perfect "I have not known".
- **"They that make a graven image" → "The formers of a carved image"** (v9) — Hebrew יֹצְרֵי־פֶסֶל, יָצַר "form" (as v2, 10, 12, 21, 24); פֶּסֶל "carved image" (v9, 10, 15, 17; "idol" is not the word). **"vanity" → "emptiness"** — תֹּהוּ (Gen 1:2 "waste"; of what is not land "emptiness"). **"their delectable things" → "the things they desire"** — חֲמוּדֵיהֶם, from חָמַד "desire, covet". **"they are their own witnesses" → "their witnesses, they"** — וְעֵדֵיהֶם הֵמָּה; the Masoretes put dots over הֵמָּה (extraordinary points: the word was doubted); it is translated. **"that they may be ashamed" → "so that they shall be ashamed"** — לְמַעַן יֵבֹשׁוּ; בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed" (v9, 11).
- **"Who hath formed a god, or molten" → "Who has formed an el and cast"** (v10) — Hebrew אֵל, the word of "El", here of a made god (token with a small letter, as v15, 17; the traditional form "god"); וּ "and" (not "or"); נָסַךְ "cast" (pour). **"that is profitable for nothing" → "to profit nothing"** — לְבִלְתִּי הוֹעִיל, the יָעַל of v9.
- **"the workmen, they are of men" → "the craftsmen, they are of mankind"** (v11) — Hebrew חָרָשִׁים … מֵאָדָם (אָדָם "mankind"; אִישׁ "a man" stands beside it in v13). **"let them stand up; yet they shall fear" → "let them stand; they shall dread"** — יַעֲמֹדוּ "stand"; יִפְחֲדוּ "dread" (no "yet" in the Hebrew).
- **"The smith with the tongs both worketh in the coals" → "A craftsman of iron, a cutting tool: and he works in the charcoal"** (v12) — Hebrew חָרַשׁ בַּרְזֶל מַעֲצָד, three nouns without a verb: "a craftsman of iron [makes] a cutting tool", or "the craftsman in iron [takes] a cutting tool"; the verse keeps the words as they stand. מַעֲצָד "cutting tool" (axe or adze, uncertain); פֶּחָם "charcoal" (as Prov 26:21; גֶּחָלִים in v19 is "coals"); מַקָּבוֹת "hammers"; יִצְּרֵהוּ "he forms it" (יָצַר). **"the strength of his arms … his strength faileth" → "the arm of his power … there is no power"** — בִּזְרוֹעַ כֹּחוֹ (singular "arm") … וְאֵין כֹּחַ; כֹּחַ "power". **"and is faint" → "and he faints"** — וַיִּיעָף (יָעֵף "faint", as 40:28–31); "he has not drunk" — לֹא־שָׁתָה, perfect.
- **"The carpenter stretcheth out his rule; he marketh it out with a line" → "A craftsman of wood stretched out a line; he marks it out with a stylus"** (v13) — Hebrew חָרַשׁ עֵצִים (as v12, "craftsman"); נָטָה קָו, perfect, קָו "line"; יְתָאֲרֵהוּ "marks it out" (both times); שֶׂרֶד (only here) "stylus" (a marking tool, uncertain); מַקְצֻעוֹת (only here) "planes" (scraping tools, uncertain); מְחוּגָה (only here) "compass". **"after the figure of a man, according to the beauty of a man" → "like the pattern of a man, like the beauty of mankind"** — כְּתַבְנִית אִישׁ כְּתִפְאֶרֶת אָדָם: תַּבְנִית "pattern" (as Deut 4:16–18), תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty" (reviewer row; "splendor" only beside צְבִי); אִישׁ "a man" and אָדָם "mankind" kept apart. **"that it may remain in the house" → "to dwell in a house"** — לָשֶׁבֶת בָּיִת (יָשַׁב "dwell", or of the image "sit").
- **"He heweth him down cedars" → "To cut down cedars for himself —"** (v14) — Hebrew לִכְרָת־לוֹ, an infinitive with no main verb before it; the dash keeps the open construction. **"the cypress … an ash" → "a cypress … a pine"** — תִּרְזָה and אֹרֶן are found only here; the kinds of tree are uncertain (תִּרְזָה also taken as "holm oak", אֹרֶן as "laurel" or "fir"); אַלּוֹן "oak". **"which he strengtheneth for himself" → "and he made it strong for himself"** — וַיְאַמֶּץ־לוֹ, wayyiqtol (אָמֵץ piel "make strong"; = he let it grow strong, or he chose it). **"doth nourish it" → "makes it grow"** — יְגַדֵּל, piel "make great".
- **"Then shall it be for a man to burn: for he will take thereof" → "And it becomes for a man for burning, and he takes from them"** (v15) — Hebrew וְהָיָה … לְבָעֵר; מֵהֶם "from them" (the trees, plural). **"yea, he kindleth it" → "also he kindles"** — אַף "also" (three times in v15–16); יַשִּׂיק has no object. **"he maketh a god, and worshippeth it" → "he works an el and bows down"** — יִפְעַל־אֵל (פָּעַל "work", as v12); וַיִּשְׁתָּחוּ "and bows down", no "it". **"falleth down thereto" → "falls down to them"** — יִסְגָּד־לָמוֹ: סָגַד "fall down" (only here, v17, 19 and 46:6; beside הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down"); לָמוֹ is plural, "to them".
- **"He burneth part thereof … with part thereof he eateth flesh" → "Half of it he has burned … on half of it he eats flesh"** (v16) — Hebrew חֶצְיוֹ "half of it" (twice), שָׂרַף perfect; עַל "on". **"is satisfied" → "is sated"** — וְיִשְׂבָּע. **"I have seen the fire" → "I have seen the blaze"** — אוּר (the light of a fire; אֵשׁ "fire" stands earlier in the verse; as 47:14).
- **"the residue thereof he maketh a god, even his graven image" → "its remnant he makes into an el, into his carved image"** (v17) — Hebrew וּשְׁאֵרִיתוֹ "its remnant" (שְׁאֵרִית, as 46:3; v19 has יִתְרוֹ "the rest of it", another word); לְאֵל … לְפִסְלוֹ. Qere יִסְגָּד, ketiv יסגוד: the same word, written in full. **"thou art my god" → "you are my el"** — אֵלִי.
- **"They have not known nor understood: for he hath shut their eyes, that they cannot see" → "They do not know and do not understand, for he has smeared over their eyes from seeing"** (v18) — Hebrew טַח "smear over, plaster" (subject not named: "he"); מֵרְאוֹת "from seeing", מֵהַשְׂכִּיל "from having insight" (הִשְׂכִּיל).
- **"none considereth in his heart" → "he does not bring back to his heart"** (v19) — Hebrew וְלֹא־יָשִׁיב אֶל־לִבּוֹ, literally (= he does not consider; as 46:8). **"knowledge nor understanding"** — דַּעַת … תְּבוּנָה. **"upon the coals thereof" → "on its coals"** — גֶּחָלָיו (as Prov 25:22). **"I have roasted flesh, and eaten it" → "I roast flesh and eat"** — אֶצְלֶה … וְאֹכֵל, imperfect and participle; no "it". **"the residue thereof" → "the rest of it"** — יִתְרוֹ. **"the stock of a tree" → "a block of wood"** — בּוּל עֵץ (בּוּל literally "produce"; here a block, uncertain).
- **"He feedeth on ashes" → "He feeds on ashes"** (v20) — Hebrew רֹעֶה אֵפֶר, a participle: "one grazing on ashes" (רָעָה of what animals eat; also taken from רָעָה "be a companion": "a companion of ashes"). **"a deceived heart" → "a cheated heart"** — לֵב הוּתַל, hofal of the verb הֵתֵל "cheat"; הִטָּהוּ "has turned him aside". **"that he cannot deliver his soul, nor say" → "and he does not deliver his soul, and does not say"** — וְלֹא־יַצִּיל … וְלֹא יֹאמַר. **"a lie" → "falsehood"** — שֶׁקֶר.
- **"O Jacob and Israel" → "Jacob, and Israel"** (v21) — no "O". **"thou art my servant" → "a servant to me you are"** — the second clause is עֶבֶד־לִי אַתָּה. **"thou shalt not be forgotten of me"** — לֹא תִנָּשֵׁנִי, nifal with an object suffix, literally "you shall not be forgotten (by) me"; the versions read "you shall not forget me" (from memory). MT kept.
- **"I have blotted out, as a thick cloud … as a cloud" → "I have wiped out … like a thick cloud … like a cloud"** (v22) — Hebrew מָחִיתִי (מָחָה "wipe out"); עָב "thick cloud", עָנָן "cloud".
- **"Sing, O ye heavens … shout, ye lower parts of the earth … ye mountains, O forest" → "Shout for joy, heavens … shout, lowest parts of the earth … mountains, forest"** (v23) — Hebrew רָנּוּ (רָנַן "shout for joy"), הָרִיעוּ (הֵרִיעַ "shout"), פִּצְחוּ … רִנָּה "break forth into a ringing cry"; תַּחְתִּיּוֹת אָרֶץ "lowest parts of the earth"; no "O". **"hath done it"** — עָשָׂה has no object: "it" supplied. **"and glorified himself in Israel" → "and in Israel he will glorify himself"** — וּבְיִשְׂרָאֵל יִתְפָּאָר, imperfect (hitpael of פָּאַר, root of תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty").
- **"thy redeemer, and he that formed thee from the womb" → "your Redeemer, and he who formed you from the belly"** (v24) — מִבָּטֶן (see v2). **"that maketh all things" → "who makes all"** — עֹשֶׂה כֹּל; "things" is not written. **"that spreadeth abroad the earth by myself"** — רֹקַע (רָקַע "spread out, beat flat"); qere מֵאִתִּי "from with me" = "by myself" is in the verse; the written text (ketiv) מי אתי reads "who was with me?".
- **"That frustrateth the tokens of the liars" → "who makes void the signs of the babblers"** (v25) — Hebrew מֵפֵר (הֵפֵר "make void, break"); אֹתוֹת "signs"; בַּדִּים "babblers" (empty talkers; also "liars", Jer 50:36; uncertain). **"maketh diviners mad"** — קֹסְמִים "diviners" (קָסַם "practise divination"); יְהוֹלֵל, poel of הָלַל "be mad". **"turneth wise men backward" → "turns wise men back"** — מֵשִׁיב … אָחוֹר; יְשַׂכֵּל "makes foolish".
- **"confirmeth the word of his servant, and performeth the counsel" → "makes the word of his servant stand and completes the counsel"** (v26) — Hebrew מֵקִים (הֵקִים "make stand", as of a vow); יַשְׁלִים "complete" (hifil of שָׁלֵם); מַלְאָכָיו "his messengers". **"to Jerusalem, Thou shalt be inhabited … Ye shall be built" → "to Jerusalem, 'She shall be inhabited' … 'They shall be built'"** — תּוּשָׁב and תִּבָּנֶינָה are third person; לְ after "says" is "to" (also "of, concerning": the Hebrew word is the same in v26–28). **"the decayed places thereof" → "her ruins"** — חָרְבוֹתֶיהָ, feminine singular "her" (of Jerusalem or of Judah; not "their").
- **"to the deep, Be dry" → "to the deep, 'Be dry'"** (v27) — Hebrew צוּלָה (only here; the depth of the sea, as מְצוֹלָה); חֳרָבִי "be dry"; אוֹבִישׁ "I will dry up" (hifil of יָבֵשׁ).
- **"That saith of Cyrus, He is my shepherd, and shall perform all my pleasure" → "who says to Cyrus, 'My shepherd, and all my delight he shall complete'"** (v28) — Hebrew לְכוֹרֶשׁ (לְ as in v26, 27); רֹעִי "my shepherd", no "He is"; חֶפְצִי "my delight" (as Ps 1:2); יַשְׁלִם "he shall complete" (as v26). **"even saying to Jerusalem, Thou shalt be built; and to the temple, Thy foundation shall be laid" → "and saying to Jerusalem, 'She shall be built,' and the temple, 'You shall be founded'"** — וְלֵאמֹר "and saying"; תִּבָּנֶה "she shall be built"; וְהֵיכָל without לְ; תִּוָּסֵד "you shall be founded" (masculine, to the temple; or "she shall be founded", of Jerusalem: the form is both).

### Notes
- **v28** — **Cyrus** named explicitly 150+ years before his rise (539 BC).
- **v6** — "first and last": echoed in Rev 1:17; 22:13 (applied to Yeshua).
- **v9–20** — the maker of the carved image: the one tree burned for warmth, for bread, and bowed down to.
