# Isaiah 45

[1] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to his ⟦anointed|mashiach|anointed⟧, to ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧, whose right hand I have taken hold of, to beat down nations before him, and the loins of kings I will loose, to open doors before him, and gates shall not be shut:

[2] "I will go before you,
and the exalted places I will make straight;
doors of bronze I will break in pieces,
and bars of iron I will hew down;

[3] and I will give you the treasuries of darkness
and the hidden treasures of secret places,
so that you may know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
who calls by your name, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[4] On account of my servant ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ my chosen,
and I called you by your name;
I give you a title, and you have not known me.

[5] I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and there is none else;
besides me there is no ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
I gird you, and you have not known me,

[6] so that they may know, from the rising of the sun
and from its setting, that there is none apart from me;
I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and there is none else.

[7] Forming light and creating darkness,
making ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ and creating evil;
I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who makes all these.

[8] Drip, heavens, from above,
and let the skies trickle righteousness;
let the earth open, and let them be fruitful in salvation,
and let it make righteousness spring up together;
I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have created it.

[9] Woe to him who strives with the one who formed him,
a potsherd with the potsherds of the ground!
Shall the clay say to the one who forms it, 'What are you making?'
and, 'Your work has no hands'?

[10] Woe to him who says to a father, 'What are you begetting?'
and to a woman, 'With what are you in labor?'"

[11] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and the one who formed him:

"Ask me the things to come;
concerning my sons and concerning the work of my hands will you command me?

[12] I made the earth,
and man upon it I created;
I, my hands stretched out the heavens,
and all their host I commanded.

[13] I have stirred him up in righteousness,
and all his ways I will make straight;
he shall build my city
and let my exiles go,
not for a price and not for a bribe,"
says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[14] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"The labor of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and the trade of ⟦cush|Kush|Cush⟧,
and the ⟦sabeans-seba|Sevaim|Sabeans⟧, men of stature,
shall pass over to you and be yours;
after you they shall walk; in chains they shall pass over;
and to you they shall bow down,
to you they shall pray:
'Only in you is ⟦el|El|God⟧, and there is none else,
there is no ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.'"

[15] Truly, you are ⟦el|an El|a God⟧ who hides himself,
the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, a savior.

[16] They have been ashamed and also put to shame, all of them;
together they have gone in dishonor, the craftsmen of images.

[17] ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ has been saved in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
with an everlasting salvation;
you shall not be ashamed and you shall not be put to shame
to the ages of eternity.

[18] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the creator of the heavens — he is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ —, who formed the earth and made it — he established it; not as a waste did he create it, for dwelling he formed it:

"I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and there is none else.

[19] I did not speak in secret,
in a place of a land of darkness;
I did not say to the seed of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
'Seek me in emptiness.'
I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who speaks righteousness,
who tells uprightness.

[20] Gather yourselves and come;
draw near together,
fugitives of the nations!
They do not know,
those who bear the wood of their carved image
and pray to ⟦el|an el|a god⟧ that does not save.

[21] Tell and bring forward;
yes, let them take counsel together.
Who has made this heard from ancient times,
told it from long ago?
Was it not I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?
And there is no other ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ apart from me,
a righteous ⟦el|El|God⟧ and a savior;
there is none besides me.

[22] Turn to me and be saved,
all the ends of the earth!
For I am ⟦el|El|God⟧, and there is none else.

[23] By myself I have sworn;
from my mouth has gone out righteousness,
a word, and it shall not return:
that to me every knee shall bow,
every tongue shall swear.

[24] Only in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, he has said to me,
are righteousnesses and strength;
to him shall come, and they shall be ashamed,
all who are incensed against him.

[25] In ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be righteous and shall glory
all the seed of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v13) — divine title preserved.
- **"God" → "El"** (v14, 15, 21, 22) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*; v20 אֵל of a made god (token with a small letter, as 44:10).
- **Layout** — v2–17 and v18b–25 one Hebrew line per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v1 and the opening of v18 stay prose, as before.
- **"to his anointed, to Cyrus" → "to his mashiach, to Koresh"** (v1) — Hebrew לִמְשִׁיחוֹ לְכוֹרֶשׁ, "to his anointed, to Cyrus" (token `anointed` as Ps 2:2; 1 Sam; no capital, no title: the word is the one used of kings and priests). **"whose right hand I have holden" → "whose right hand I have taken hold of"** — הֶחֱזַקְתִּי בִימִינוֹ. **"to subdue nations" → "to beat down nations"** — לְרַד, from רָדַד "beat down" (כָּבַשׁ is "subdue"). **"I will loose the loins of kings"** — וּמָתְנֵי מְלָכִים אֲפַתֵּחַ, literally (= disarm them; "belts" is not written). **"the two leaved gates; and the gates shall not be shut" → "doors … and gates shall not be shut"** — דְּלָתַיִם "doors" (dual: a pair of leaves), שְׁעָרִים "gates"; "and" kept, no "that".
- **"make the crooked places straight" → "the exalted places I will make straight"** (v2) — Hebrew הֲדוּרִים, uncertain ("swellings", "exalted places", from הָדַר; the Isaiah scroll and the Greek read "mountains", from memory; MT kept). Qere אֲיַשֵּׁר "I will make straight" (as v13); ketiv אושר, the same verb in another spelling. **"gates of brass" → "doors of bronze"** — דַּלְתוֹת נְחוּשָׁה; שִׁבֵּר "break in pieces"; גִּדַּע "hew down" (cut through; the word of hewing down a tree or pole).
- **"treasures of darkness, and hidden riches of secret places" → "the treasuries of darkness and the hidden treasures of secret places"** (v3) — Hebrew אוֹצְרוֹת "treasuries" (stores; also their contents) and מַטְמֻנֵי מִסְתָּרִים (טָמַן "hide"; מִסְתָּר "secret place"). **"that I, the LORD, which call thee by thy name, am the God of Israel" → "so that you may know that I am Yahweh, who calls by your name, the Elohim of Israel"** — לְמַעַן "so that"; הַקּוֹרֵא בְשִׁמְךָ "who calls by your name" ("you" is not written apart from "your name").
- **"For Jacob my servant's sake" → "On account of my servant Jacob"** (v4) — Hebrew לְמַעַן before a noun. **"I have even called thee by thy name" → "and I called you by your name"** — וָאֶקְרָא לְךָ בִּשְׁמֶךָ, wayyiqtol with its "and". **"I have surnamed thee" → "I give you a title"** — אֲכַנְּךָ (כִּנָּה "give a title", as 44:5). **"though thou hast not known me" → "and you have not known me"** (v4, 5) — וְלֹא יְדַעְתָּנִי, "and" (the contrast is the reader's).
- **"there is no God beside me" → "besides me there is no Elohim"** (v5) — זוּלָתִי "besides me", in the Hebrew order. **"I girded thee" → "I gird you"** — אֲאַזֶּרְךָ, imperfect.
- **"That they may know … and from the west, that there is none beside me" → "so that they may know … and from its setting, that there is none apart from me"** (v6) — Hebrew לְמַעַן "so that"; "they" not named (no "people"); מִמַּעֲרָבָהּ "from its (the sun's) setting", the west; אֶפֶס בִּלְעָדַי, אֶפֶס "nothing, end" = "there is none".
- **"I form the light, and create darkness: I make peace, and create evil" → "forming light and creating darkness, making shalom and creating evil"** (v7) — Hebrew יוֹצֵר … וּבוֹרֵא … עֹשֶׂה … וּבוֹרֵא, four participles, said of the "I am Yahweh" of the third line (and of v6); יָצַר "form", בָּרָא "create", עָשָׂה "make". שָׁלוֹם is *shalom* (token); רַע is "evil", the plain word set against שָׁלוֹם as חֹשֶׁךְ against אוֹר. The verse is translated as the Hebrew stands; "calamity", "disaster", "woe" are explanations of the word in the context of v1–6 (Cyrus, the nations) and are not put in the verse. **"I the LORD do all these things" → "I am Yahweh, who makes all these"** — עֹשֶׂה כָל־אֵלֶּה; "things" is not written.
- **"Drop down, ye heavens … let the skies pour down righteousness" → "Drip, heavens … let the skies trickle righteousness"** (v8) — Hebrew הַרְעִיפוּ "make drip"; שְׁחָקִים "skies" (also "clouds"); יִזְּלוּ (נָזַל "trickle, flow"); צֶדֶק "righteousness". **"let them bring forth salvation" → "let them be fruitful in salvation"** — וְיִפְרוּ־יֶשַׁע, פָּרָה "be fruitful" with יֶשַׁע "salvation" as its object; the plural subject is not named (the heavens and the earth, or "let salvation be fruitful" with other pointing). **"let righteousness spring up together" → "let it make righteousness spring up together"** — וּצְדָקָה תַצְמִיחַ, hifil "make spring up", the earth as subject. **"I the LORD have created it"** — בְּרָאתִיו (masculine "it").
- **"striveth with his Maker" → "strives with the one who formed him"** (v9) — Hebrew רָב אֶת־יֹצְרוֹ (יָצַר "form", v7, 11, 18). **"Let the potsherd strive with the potsherds of the earth" → "a potsherd with the potsherds of the ground"** — חֶרֶשׂ אֶת־חַרְשֵׂי אֲדָמָה, no verb (the striving of the first line is understood); אֲדָמָה "ground". **"him that fashioneth it" → "the one who forms it"** — לְיֹצְרוֹ. **"or thy work, He hath no hands" → "and, 'Your work has no hands'"** — וּפָעָלְךָ אֵין־יָדַיִם לוֹ, "and your work: no hands to it / to him" (said by the clay of the potter's work, or by the work of its maker; "handles" is not the word). חֹמֶר "clay" (the potter's material; "mortar" in Exodus).
- **"unto his father … or to the woman, What hast thou brought forth?" → "to a father … and to a woman, 'With what are you in labor?'"** (v10) — Hebrew לְאָב, לְאִשָּׁה without "his" and "the"; וּ "and"; תְּחִילִין (חִיל "writhe in labor").
- **"and his Maker" → "and the one who formed him"** (v11) — וְיֹצְרוֹ. **"Ask me of things to come concerning my sons, and concerning the work of my hands command ye me" → "Ask me the things to come; concerning my sons and concerning the work of my hands will you command me?"** — הָאֹתִיּוֹת שְׁאָלוּנִי, imperative with "the coming things" as object (as 44:7); תְּצַוֻּנִי imperfect "you command me": read as a question (the Hebrew does not mark it; KJV takes it as a command: both in the Hebrew). בָּנַי "my sons".
- **"I, even my hands, have stretched out" → "I, my hands stretched out"** (v12) — אֲנִי יָדַי נָטוּ, the Hebrew order; "I made … I created … I commanded", each in the Hebrew order.
- **"I have raised him up … I will direct all his ways" → "I have stirred him up … all his ways I will make straight"** (v13) — הַעִירֹתִהוּ (הֵעִיר "stir up"); אֲיַשֵּׁר "make straight" (as v2). **"he shall let go my captives" → "let my exiles go"** — גָּלוּתִי "my exile" (collective: the exiles); יְשַׁלֵּחַ (שִׁלַּח "let go"). **"not for price nor reward" → "not for a price and not for a bribe"** — מְחִיר "price", שֹׁחַד "bribe".
- **"The labour of Egypt, and merchandise of Ethiopia" → "The labor of Egypt and the trade of Cush"** (v14) — Hebrew יְגִיעַ "labor" (what labor yields); סְחַר "trade" (what is traded); כּוּשׁ *Kush*. "you" is feminine throughout the verse (Zion addressed; CZ shows it where Czech can). **"they shall come after thee" → "after you they shall walk"** — יֵלֵכוּ. **"they shall make supplication unto thee, saying" → "to you they shall pray"** — יִתְפַּלָּלוּ (הִתְפַּלֵּל "pray"), "to you" twice in the Hebrew order; "saying" is not written. **"Surely God is in thee" → "Only in you is El"** — אַךְ "only" (also "surely"; "and there is none else" follows); **"there is no God" → "there is no Elohim"** — אֶפֶס אֱלֹהִים, literally "nothing of Elohim"; "besides him" is not written.
- **"Verily thou art a God that hidest thyself, O God of Israel, the Saviour" → "Truly, you are an El who hides himself, the Elohim of Israel, a savior"** (v15) — Hebrew אָכֵן; אֵל מִסְתַּתֵּר (a participle: "who hides himself"); מוֹשִׁיעַ without the article; no "O".
- **"They shall be ashamed, and also confounded … they shall go to confusion together that are makers of idols" → "They have been ashamed and also put to shame … together they have gone in dishonor, the craftsmen of images"** (v16) — Hebrew perfects בּוֹשׁוּ, נִכְלְמוּ, הָלְכוּ (said of what is sure: "they are ashamed" is the other English); בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed", נִכְלַם "be put to shame", כְּלִמָּה "dishonor"; חָרָשֵׁי צִירִים "craftsmen of images" (צִיר, from צוּר "form"; only here in this sense, uncertain).
- **"But Israel shall be saved in the LORD" → "Israel has been saved in Yahweh"** (v17) — the Hebrew has no "but"; נוֹשַׁע, nifal perfect. **"with an everlasting salvation"** — תְּשׁוּעַת עוֹלָמִים, literally "a salvation of ages" (plural). **"world without end" → "to the ages of eternity"** — עַד־עוֹלְמֵי עַד; "you shall not be ashamed and you shall not be put to shame" — each verb with its לֹא.
- **"that created the heavens; God himself that formed the earth" → "the creator of the heavens — he is Elohim —, who formed the earth"** (v18) — Hebrew בּוֹרֵא הַשָּׁמַיִם הוּא הָאֱלֹהִים: "he is the Elohim", with the article (the one who is Elohim; as Deut 4:35, 39). **"he created it not in vain, he formed it to be inhabited" → "not as a waste did he create it, for dwelling he formed it"** — לֹא־תֹהוּ בְרָאָהּ (תֹּהוּ "waste", as Gen 1:2); לָשֶׁבֶת "for dwelling" (יָשַׁב). The parentheses and exclamation marks are not in the Hebrew.
- **"in a dark place of the earth" → "in a place of a land of darkness"** (v19) — בִּמְקוֹם אֶרֶץ חֹשֶׁךְ. **"in secret"** — בַּסֵּתֶר (סֵתֶר "hiding place"; here adverbial). **"Seek ye me in vain" → "Seek me in emptiness"** — תֹּהוּ בַקְּשׁוּנִי (תֹּהוּ as 44:9). **"I the LORD speak righteousness, I declare things that are right" → "I am Yahweh, who speaks righteousness, who tells uprightness"** — דֹּבֵר צֶדֶק מַגִּיד מֵישָׁרִים, participles; מֵישָׁרִים "uprightness".
- **"ye that are escaped of the nations" → "fugitives of the nations"** (v20) — Hebrew פְּלִיטֵי הַגּוֹיִם (פָּלִיט "fugitive, one who has escaped"). **"they have no knowledge that set up the wood of their graven image" → "They do not know, those who bear the wood of their carved image"** — לֹא יָדְעוּ; הַנֹּשְׂאִים "who bear" (נָשָׂא, as 46:1, 3, 4, 7); עֵץ פִּסְלָם. **"a god that cannot save" → "an el that does not save"** — אֵל לֹא יוֹשִׁיעַ.
- **"Tell ye, and bring them near" → "Tell and bring forward"** (v21) — הַגִּידוּ וְהַגִּישׁוּ (הִגִּישׁ "bring forward"; no object written: "them" is not in the Hebrew). **"who hath declared this from ancient time? who hath told it from that time?" → "Who has made this heard from ancient times, told it from long ago?"** — הִשְׁמִיעַ "made heard" (as 44:8); מִקֶּדֶם "from ancient times", מֵאָז "from long ago"; one question. **"a just God and a Saviour" → "a righteous El and a savior"** — אֵל־צַדִּיק וּמוֹשִׁיעַ; אַיִן זוּלָתִי "there is none besides me".
- **"Look unto me" → "Turn to me"** (v22) — Hebrew פְּנוּ (פָּנָה "turn, face toward"); "be saved" (הִוָּשְׁעוּ, nifal imperative).
- **"the word is gone out of my mouth in righteousness, and shall not return, That unto me … every tongue shall swear" → "from my mouth has gone out righteousness, a word, and it shall not return: that to me … every tongue shall swear"** (v23) — Hebrew יָצָא מִפִּי צְדָקָה דָּבָר וְלֹא יָשׁוּב; the accents set צְדָקָה with "from my mouth" and דָּבָר after it: "righteousness … a word" (or "a word in righteousness": both are read). כִּי "that" (what is sworn). תִּשָּׁבַע "shall swear" (not "swear allegiance"). Rom 14:11 and Phil 2:10–11 quote the verse from the Greek, which has ἐξομολογήσεται "shall confess" for "shall swear"; the verse here follows the Hebrew.
- **"Surely, shall one say, in the LORD have I righteousness and strength" → "Only in Yahweh, he has said to me, are righteousnesses and strength"** (v24) — Hebrew אַךְ בַּיהוָה לִי אָמַר צְדָקוֹת וָעֹז: אַךְ "only" (as v14); לִי אָמַר "to me he has said" (or "of me one has said": the subject is not named); צְדָקוֹת is plural ("righteous acts"); עֹז "strength". **"even to him shall men come; and all … shall be ashamed" → "to him shall come, and they shall be ashamed, all who are incensed against him"** — עָדָיו יָבוֹא, singular verb, then plural יֵבֹשׁוּ, with "all" as subject of both ("men" is not written); הַנֶּחֱרִים "who are incensed" (literally "who burn").
- **"shall all the seed of Israel be justified, and shall glory" → "shall be righteous and shall glory all the seed of Israel"** (v25) — Hebrew יִצְדְּקוּ (צָדַק qal "be righteous"), יִתְהַלְלוּ "glory", in the Hebrew order; בַּיהוָה "in Yahweh" (v17, 24, 25).

### Notes
- **v7** — "making *shalom* and creating evil": the whole range set under the one Creator, light and darkness, *shalom* and evil.
- **v23** — quoted Phil 2:10-11; Rom 14:11 (applied to Yeshua), from the Greek ("confess").
