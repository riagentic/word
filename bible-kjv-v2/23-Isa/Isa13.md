# Isaiah 13

[1] The burden of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, which ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ son of ⟦amoz|Amotz|Amoz⟧ beheld.

[2] On a bare mountain lift up a banner;
raise the voice to them;
wave the hand,
and let them come into the entrances of the nobles.

[3] I myself have commanded my sanctified ones;
also I have called my mighty ones for my anger,
the jubilant ones of my exaltation.

[4] The sound of a multitude on the mountains,
the likeness of a great people!
The sound of the tumult of the kingdoms of nations gathered together!
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ of Hosts is mustering a host of war.

[5] They come from a land far off,
from the end of the heavens,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and the weapons of his indignation,
to ravage all the earth.

[6] Wail, for the day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is near;
like havoc from ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ it will come.

[7] Therefore all hands will go slack,
and every heart of mortal man will melt.

[8] And they will be dismayed; pangs and throes will seize them;
like a woman giving birth they will writhe;
each man at his neighbor they will stare in astonishment;
faces of flames are their faces.

[9] Behold, the day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ comes,
cruel, and raging, and the burning of anger,
to make the earth a thing that appalls;
and its sinners it will destroy from it.

[10] For the stars of the heavens and their ⟦orion|Kesilim|Orions⟧
will not flash forth their light;
the sun is dark in its going forth,
and the moon will not make its light shine.

[11] And I will visit evil upon the world,
and upon the wicked their iniquity;
and I will make the pride of the presumptuous cease,
and the haughtiness of the ruthless I will make low.

[12] I will make mortal man more rare than fine gold,
and man than the gold of ⟦ophir|Ofir|Ophir⟧.

[13] Therefore I will make the heavens tremble,
and the earth will quake out of its place,
in the raging of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts,
and in the day of the burning of his anger.

[14] And it shall be: like a gazelle driven away,
and like a flock with no one gathering,
each man to his people they will turn,
and each man to his land they will flee.

[15] Everyone who is found will be thrust through,
and everyone who is swept away will fall by the sword.

[16] And their infants will be dashed in pieces before their eyes;
their houses will be pillaged,
and their wives will be lain with.

[17] Behold, I am stirring up against them ⟦medes|Madai|the Medes⟧,
who do not regard silver,
and gold, they do not delight in it.

[18] And bows will dash young men in pieces,
and on the fruit of the belly they will have no compassion;
on sons their eye will not have pity.

[19] And ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, the beauty of kingdoms,
the splendor of the pride of ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|the Chaldeans⟧,
shall be like the overthrow by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧
of ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ and ⟦gomorrah|Amorah|Gomorrah⟧.

[20] It will never be dwelt in,
and it will not be abided in to generation and generation;
and an ⟦arab|Arav|Arab⟧ will not tent there,
and shepherds will not make their flocks lie down there.

[21] But desert dwellers will lie down there,
and their houses will be full of howling creatures;
and daughters of the ostrich will abide there,
and ⟦sair|sa'irim|wild goats⟧ will dance there.

[22] And hyenas will answer in his widows,
and jackals in the palaces of delight;
and near to come is her time,
and her days will not be drawn out.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v4, 13) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת, as the earlier books.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, by the accents; v8 keeps וְנִבְהָלוּ "and they will be dismayed" in the line it belongs to.
- **"The burden of Babylon, which Isaiah the son of Amoz did see" → "The burden of Babylon, which Isaiah son of Amoz beheld"** (v1) — Hebrew מַשָּׂא בָּבֶל: מַשָּׂא is "burden", "what is lifted up" (from נָשָׂא "lift up"), the word for a prophet's pronouncement as for a load ("oracle" in modern versions names the sense, not the word). חָזָה "behold" (as 1:1).
- **"Lift ye up a banner upon the high mountain, exalt the voice unto them, shake the hand, that they may go into the gates of the nobles" → "On a bare mountain lift up a banner; raise the voice to them; wave the hand, and let them come into the entrances of the nobles"** (v2) — Hebrew הַר־נִשְׁפֶּה, "a mountain swept bare" (uncertain: from שָׁפָה, "be bare"); נֵס "banner"; הֵרִים "raise"; הֵנִיף "wave"; וְיָבֹאוּ, "and let them come" (a wish or a purpose: "that they may come"); פִּתְחֵי, "entrances" (פֶּתַח, not שַׁעַר "gate"); נְדִיבִים "nobles".
- **"I have commanded my sanctified ones, I have also called my mighty ones for mine anger, even them that rejoice in my highness" → "I myself have commanded my sanctified ones; also I have called my mighty ones for my anger, the jubilant ones of my exaltation"** (v3) — Hebrew אֲנִי set out before the verb; מְקֻדָּשַׁי, pual of קִדֵּשׁ "sanctify" (men set apart for the war); גִּבּוֹרַי "my mighty ones"; לְאַפִּי "for my anger" (אַף, literally "nose"); עַלִּיזֵי גַּאֲוָתִי, "the jubilant ones of my exaltation" (עַלִּיז "jubilant"; גַּאֲוָה of God as Deut 33:26; also read "my proudly jubilant ones", as Zeph 3:11 of men).
- **"The noise of a multitude in the mountains, like as of a great people; a tumultuous noise of the kingdoms of nations gathered together: the LORD of hosts mustereth the host of the battle" → "The sound of a multitude on the mountains, the likeness of a great people! The sound of the tumult of the kingdoms of nations gathered together! Yahweh of Hosts is mustering a host of war"** (v4) — Hebrew קוֹל "sound" (the voice of v2); הָמוֹן "multitude"; דְּמוּת "likeness"; שְׁאוֹן "tumult" (as 17:12, 13); מַמְלְכוֹת גּוֹיִם "kingdoms of nations"; מְפַקֵּד, participle of פָּקַד "number, muster"; צְבָא מִלְחָמָה, "a host of war": צָבָא "host" is the word of "Yahweh of Hosts".
- **"from the end of heaven, even the LORD, and the weapons of his indignation, to destroy the whole land" → "from the end of the heavens, Yahweh and the weapons of his indignation, to ravage all the earth"** (v5) — Hebrew מֶרְחָק "far off"; שָׁמַיִם "heavens"; כְּלֵי, "weapons" (כְּלִי, any made article: of war "weapons"); זַעַם "indignation"; חִבֵּל "ravage" (kept apart from הִשְׁמִיד "destroy", v9). כָּל־הָאָרֶץ "all the earth" or "all the land": the heavens of v5, 10, 13 and the "world" of v11 give the wider sense, but the oracle is against Babylon; "land" in v9 is as open.
- **"the day of the LORD is at hand; it shall come as a destruction from the Almighty" → "the day of Yahweh is near; like havoc from Shaddai it will come"** (v6, 9) — Hebrew יוֹם יְהוָה: "day" without a capital, as the Hebrew has no title here; כְּשֹׁד מִשַּׁדַּי, a play of sound (*shod mi-Shaddai*); שֹׁד "havoc" (noun of שָׁדַד "lay waste", 15:1; 16:4). שַׁדַּי keeps its token (the sense of the name is not certain; "Almighty" is the traditional form).
- **"all hands be faint, and every man's heart shall melt" → "all hands will go slack, and every heart of mortal man will melt"** (v7) — Hebrew רָפָה "sink, go slack"; לְבַב אֱנוֹשׁ, "the heart of mortal man" (אֱנוֹשׁ); מָסַס nifal "melt".
- **"they shall be afraid: pangs and sorrows shall take hold of them; they shall be in pain as a woman that travaileth: they shall be amazed one at another; their faces shall be as flames" → "And they will be dismayed; pangs and throes will seize them; like a woman giving birth they will writhe; each man at his neighbor they will stare in astonishment; faces of flames are their faces"** (v8) — Hebrew נִבְהַל "be dismayed"; צִירִים "pangs", חֲבָלִים "throes"; חִיל "writhe" (the anguish of labor); אִישׁ אֶל־רֵעֵהוּ "each man at his neighbor"; תָּמַהּ "be astonished, stare"; פְּנֵי לְהָבִים פְּנֵיהֶם, word for word, no verb and no "as".
- **"cruel both with wrath and fierce anger, to lay the land desolate: and he shall destroy the sinners thereof out of it" → "cruel, and raging, and the burning of anger, to make the earth a thing that appalls; and its sinners it will destroy from it"** (v9) — Hebrew אַכְזָרִי וְעֶבְרָה וַחֲרוֹן אַף, an adjective and two nouns standing beside "the day", each with "and"; עֶבְרָה "raging" (also v13); חֲרוֹן אַף "the burning of anger" (as Deut 13:17); שַׁמָּה, "a thing that appalls" (root שָׁמֵם "be desolate, appalled"); יַשְׁמִיד, "it will destroy": the subject is the day of v9a, or Yahweh ("he"); the Hebrew does not name it.
- **"the constellations thereof shall not give their light" → "their Kesilim / Orions will not flash forth their light"** (v10) — Hebrew כְּסִילֵיהֶם, plural of כְּסִיל, the star or constellation of Job 9:9; 38:31 (traditionally Orion): "their Orions" are the great constellations; the token keeps the name. יָהֵלּוּ, hifil of הָלַל "flash, shine"; חָשַׁךְ, perfect, "is dark"; בְּצֵאתוֹ "in its going forth"; יַגִּיהַּ "make shine".
- **"I will punish the world for their evil … and I will cause the arrogancy of the proud to cease, and will lay low the haughtiness of the terrible" → "And I will visit evil upon the world … and I will make the pride of the presumptuous cease, and the haughtiness of the ruthless I will make low"** (v11) — Hebrew וּפָקַדְתִּי עַל־תֵּבֵל רָעָה, "I will visit upon the world evil": פָּקַד "visit" (attend to, here for punishment), and the evil is what is visited, not "for its evil"; תֵּבֵל "world"; הִשְׁבִּית "make cease"; גְּאוֹן "pride" (גָּאוֹן, "exaltation" of God, Exod 15:7), גַּאֲוַת "haughtiness": English has one word "pride" for both, and they stand side by side here and in 16:6, so גַּאֲוָה of men is "haughtiness" in these chapters; זֵדִים "the presumptuous"; עָרִיצִים "ruthless"; הִשְׁפִּיל "make low".
- **"I will make a man more precious than fine gold; even a man than the golden wedge of Ophir" → "I will make mortal man more rare than fine gold, and man than the gold of Ophir"** (v12) — Hebrew אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man", אָדָם "man"; פָּז "fine gold", כֶּתֶם "gold" (another word; "wedge" is not in it).
- **"I will shake the heavens, and the earth shall remove out of her place, in the wrath of the LORD of hosts, and in the day of his fierce anger" → "I will make the heavens tremble, and the earth will quake out of its place, in the raging of Yahweh of Hosts, and in the day of the burning of his anger"** (v13) — Hebrew הִרְגִּיז "make tremble" (רָגַז); רָעַשׁ "quake"; עֶבְרָה "raging"; חֲרוֹן אַפּוֹ.
- **"And it shall be as the chased roe, and as a sheep that no man taketh up: they shall every man turn to his own people" → "And it shall be: like a gazelle driven away, and like a flock with no one gathering, each man to his people they will turn"** (v14) — Hebrew וְהָיָה "and it shall be" (the subject is not named); צְבִי "gazelle"; מֻדָּח, hofal of נָדַח "drive away, thrust"; צֹאן "flock"; מְקַבֵּץ "one gathering"; no "own".
- **"every one that is joined unto them shall fall by the sword" → "everyone who is swept away will fall by the sword"** (v15) — Hebrew הַנִּסְפֶּה, nifal of סָפָה "sweep away" (as Gen 19:15, 17); נִמְצָא "be found"; דָּקַר "thrust through".
- **"their wives ravished" → "their wives will be lain with"** (v16) — the qere תִּשָּׁכַבְנָה "they will be lain with" (שָׁכַב); the written text has תשגלנה, from שָׁגַל, a coarse word for the act ("violated"). עֹלְלִים "infants"; רִטֵּשׁ "dash in pieces" (also v18); לְעֵינֵיהֶם "before their eyes"; יִשַּׁסּוּ "be pillaged".
- **"I will stir up the Medes against them, which shall not regard silver; and as for gold, they shall not delight in it" → "I am stirring up against them Madai / the Medes, who do not regard silver, and gold, they do not delight in it"** (v17) — Hebrew הִנְנִי מֵעִיר, "behold me stirring up" (participle); מָדַי without article (the land and people); חָשַׁב "reckon, regard"; חָפֵץ בְּ "delight in".
- **"Their bows also shall dash the young men to pieces; and they shall have no pity on the fruit of the womb; their eye shall not spare children" → "And bows will dash young men in pieces, and on the fruit of the belly they will have no compassion; on sons their eye will not have pity"** (v18) — Hebrew קְשָׁתוֹת "bows" (no "their"); נְעָרִים "young men" (נַעַר); פְּרִי־בֶטֶן "the fruit of the belly" (בֶּטֶן, not רֶחֶם "womb"); רִחַם "have compassion"; בָּנִים "sons"; חוּס "have pity".
- **"the glory of kingdoms, the beauty of the Chaldees' excellency, shall be as when God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah" → "the beauty of kingdoms, the splendor of the pride of the Kasdim / Chaldeans, shall be like the overthrow by Elohim of Sodom and Gomorrah"** (v19) — Hebrew צְבִי "beauty" (the word of "gazelle", v14), תִּפְאֶרֶת "splendor" (alone "beauty"; beside צְבִי "splendor", as Isa 28:1–5); גְּאוֹן "pride" (what they are proud of); כְּמַהְפֵּכַת אֱלֹהִים אֶת־סְדֹם, "like the overthrow of Elohim of Sodom" (מַהְפֵּכָה "overthrow", Deut 29:23; Amos 4:11).
- **"It shall never be inhabited, neither shall it be dwelt in from generation to generation: neither shall the Arabian pitch tent there; neither shall the shepherds make their fold there" → "It will never be dwelt in, and it will not be abided in to generation and generation; and an Arab will not tent there, and shepherds will not make their flocks lie down there"** (v20) — Hebrew לֹא־תֵשֵׁב לָנֶצַח, "she will not sit / dwell forever": of a city, "be dwelt in" (יָשַׁב); לֹא תִשְׁכֹּן, "she will not abide" (שָׁכַן); עַד־דּוֹר וָדוֹר "to generation and generation"; יַהֵל, from אָהַל "tent" (verb); יַרְבִּצוּ, hifil of רָבַץ "make lie down": "their flocks" is supplied, the Hebrew has no object.
- **"But wild beasts of the desert shall lie there; and their houses shall be full of doleful creatures; and owls shall dwell there, and satyrs shall dance there" → "But desert dwellers will lie down there, and their houses will be full of howling creatures; and daughters of the ostrich will abide there, and sa'irim / wild goats will dance there"** (v21) — the animals in the plainest sense; all four are uncertain: צִיִּים "desert dwellers" (from צִיָּה "dry land"; men or beasts, Ps 72:9; 74:14); אֹחִים, only here, "howling creatures" (perhaps from the cry *oh*; "owls" in some versions); בְּנוֹת יַעֲנָה, "daughters of the ostrich", ostriches (Lev 11:16; "owls" in KJV); שְׂעִירִים, plural of שָׂעִיר "hairy one", the male goat (token, as Isa 34:14; the same word in Lev 17:7 of the beings sacrificed to: "goat-demons", "satyrs" are read in, not written). שָׁכַן "abide".
- **"And the wild beasts of the islands shall cry in their desolate houses, and dragons in their pleasant palaces: and her time is near to come, and her days shall not be prolonged" → "And hyenas will answer in his widows, and jackals in the palaces of delight; and near to come is her time, and her days will not be drawn out"** (v22) — Hebrew אִיִּים, "howlers" (uncertain: jackals, wolves or hyenas; not the אִיִּים "coastlands" of the same letters); עָנָה "answer" (one howling to another); בְּאַלְמנוֹתָיו, as written and pointed, "in his widows" (אַלְמָנָה "widow"): it stands where a building is expected, and is commonly taken as another spelling of אַרְמְנוֹתָיו "his citadels" (ל for ר); the MT is kept. The suffix is masculine ("his"; Babylon is feminine in the next line). תַּנִּים "jackals" (not תַּנִּין "dragon"); הֵיכְלֵי עֹנֶג "palaces of delight" (הֵיכָל of a king); נִמְשַׁךְ "be drawn out".

### Notes
- **Begins the oracles against the nations** (ch. 13–23); each opens with מַשָּׂא "the burden of …" (13:1; 15:1; 17:1; 19:1; 21:1, 11, 13; 22:1; 23:1; 14:28).
- **v21–22** — the same animals stand in Isa 34:13–14 and Jer 50:39.
