# Isaiah 50

[1] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"Where is the document of cutting off of your mother, whom I sent away?
Or which of my creditors is it to whom I sold you?
Behold, for your iniquities you were sold,
and for your transgressions your mother was sent away.

[2] Why did I come, and there was no man;
did I call, and there was none answering?
Is my hand surely too short for ransoming,
or is there no power in me to deliver?
Behold, by my rebuke I dry up the sea,
I make rivers a wilderness;
their fish stink because there is no water,
and die of thirst.

[3] I clothe the heavens with blackness,
and sackcloth I make their covering."

[4] ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|The Lord GOD⟧ has given me a tongue of taught ones,
to know how to sustain the weary one with a word;
he wakens morning by morning,
he wakens my ear to hear like taught ones.

[5] ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|The Lord GOD⟧ has opened my ear,
and I did not rebel;
backward I did not draw back.

[6] My back I gave to those who strike,
and my cheeks to those who pluck;
my face I did not hide
from dishonors and spitting.

[7] And ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ helps me;
therefore I have not been put to shame;
therefore I have set my face like flint,
and I know that I shall not be ashamed.

[8] Near is he who justifies me;
who will contend with me? Let us stand together!
Who is the owner of my judgment? Let him draw near to me.

[9] Behold, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ helps me;
who is he who will condemn me?
Behold, all of them like a garment shall wear out;
a moth shall eat them.

[10] Who among you fears ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
listening to the voice of his servant?
He who has walked in dark places, and there is no brightness for him,
let him trust in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
and lean on his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[11] Behold, all of you, kindlers of fire,
who gird on firebrands:
walk in the flame of your fire,
and among the firebrands you have set burning!
From my hand this has come to you:
in pain you shall lie down.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v4, 5, 7, 9) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words, four times in the servant's words. **"God" → "Elohim"** (v10).
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew colon per printed line: v1 has four cola (it was printed in six lines), v4 four (it was seven), v8 three, by the accents (zaqef, atnach), v10 five (it was six).
- **"Where is the bill of your mother's divorcement, whom I have put away?" → "Where is the document of cutting off of your mother, whom I sent away?"** (v1) — Hebrew אֵי זֶה סֵפֶר כְּרִיתוּת אִמְּכֶם: סֵפֶר כְּרִיתוּת "a document of cutting off" (its row; כְּרִיתוּת from כָּרַת "cut off"; not "certificate of divorce"); זֶה strengthens the question ("where, then"); אֲשֶׁר שִׁלַּחְתִּיהָ, with the pronoun "her": "whom I sent away" refers to the mother (שִׁלַּח of a wife "send away", its row; not "divorce"). **"or which of my creditors is it to whom I have sold you?"** — מִנּוֹשַׁי, נֹשֶׁה "creditor" (the נָשָׁה row); one colon. **"for your iniquities have ye sold yourselves" → "for your iniquities you were sold"** — נִמְכַּרְתֶּם, nifal "you were sold" (not reflexive); בְּ "for, by"; עָוֹן "iniquity", פֶּשַׁע "transgression" (their rows); **"is your mother put away" → "your mother was sent away"** — שֻׁלְּחָה, pual of שִׁלַּח.
- **"Wherefore, when I came, was there no man? when I called, was there none to answer?" → "Why did I come, and there was no man; did I call, and there was none answering?"** (v2) — Hebrew מַדּוּעַ בָּאתִי וְאֵין אִישׁ קָרָאתִי וְאֵין עוֹנֶה, two perfects each with "and there was no …"; עוֹנֶה participle; the one מַדּוּעַ stands over both. **"Is my hand shortened at all, that it cannot redeem?" → "Is my hand surely too short for ransoming"** — הֲקָצוֹר קָצְרָה, infinitive absolute (literally "being short is it short"); מִפְּדוּת, מִן with פְּדוּת "ransoming" (the פִּדְיֹן · פְּדוּת row; פָּדָה "ransom", גָּאַל "redeem"). **"or have I no power to deliver?" → "or is there no power in me to deliver?"** — וְאִם־אֵין־בִּי כֹחַ לְהַצִּיל, כֹּחַ "power", הִצִּיל "deliver". **"I dry up the sea, I make the rivers a wilderness" → "I dry up the sea, I make rivers a wilderness"** — אַחֲרִיב יָם (hifil of חָרֵב: of water "dry up"; the same verb is "lay waste" of a city, 49:17), נְהָרוֹת without the article, מִדְבָּר "wilderness" (its row; was "desert"); גְּעָרָה "rebuke". **"their fish stinketh, because there is no water, and dieth for thirst" → "their fish stink because there is no water, and die of thirst"** — תִּבְאַשׁ (בָּאַשׁ "stink", its row), מֵאֵין מַיִם "from there being no water", וְתָמֹת בַּצָּמָא; דָּגָה is a collective singular.
- **"I clothe the heavens with blackness, and I make sackcloth their covering"** (v3) — Hebrew קַדְרוּת "blackness" (from קָדַר "be dark, mourn"); שַׂק "sackcloth", כְּסוּת "covering" (their rows); the Hebrew order of the second line kept ("and sackcloth I make").
- **The servant's words (v4–9), word for word** — each line follows the Hebrew; nothing is supplied beyond what English grammar needs (named below).
- **"The Lord GOD hath given me the tongue of the learned, that I should know how to speak a word in season to him that is weary" → "Lord Yahweh has given me a tongue of taught ones, to know how to sustain the weary one with a word"** (v4) — Hebrew לְשׁוֹן לִמּוּדִים "a tongue of taught ones" (לִמּוּד, from לָמַד "learn": one who is taught, a disciple; no article); לָדַעַת לָעוּת אֶת־יָעֵף דָּבָר: לָעוּת is found only here, and its sense is uncertain ("sustain, help"; a link with עֵת "time" gave KJV "in season"); יָעֵף "the weary one" (singular); דָּבָר "a word" stands at the end of the line without a preposition ("with" supplied); "how" is English grammar after "know". **"he wakeneth morning by morning, he wakeneth mine ear to hear as the learned" → "he wakens morning by morning, he wakens my ear to hear like taught ones"** — יָעִיר בַּבֹּקֶר בַּבֹּקֶר, literally "he wakens, in the morning, in the morning"; יָעִיר לִי אֹזֶן, literally "he wakens for me an ear"; כַּלִּמּוּדִים "like the taught ones". The subject "he" is Adonai Yahweh, not named again.
- **"The Lord GOD hath opened mine ear, and I was not rebellious, neither turned away back" → "Lord Yahweh has opened my ear, and I did not rebel; backward I did not draw back"** (v5) — Hebrew פָּתַח־לִי אֹזֶן, literally "opened for me an ear"; וְאָנֹכִי לֹא מָרִיתִי (מָרָה "rebel against", its row; the pronoun אָנֹכִי "I" set first); אָחוֹר לֹא נְסוּגֹתִי, נָסוֹג nifal "draw back" (not "turn": שׁוּב), in the Hebrew order.
- **"I gave my back to the smiters, and my cheeks to them that plucked off the hair" → "My back I gave to those who strike, and my cheeks to those who pluck"** (v6) — Hebrew גֵּוִי נָתַתִּי לְמַכִּים (גֵּו "back"; מַכִּים, participle of הִכָּה "strike"), וּלְחָיַי לְמֹרְטִים (מָרַט "pluck, make bare": the hair of the beard is meant; neither "hair" nor "beard" is in the verse; the Greek has "blows" for the second word, from memory). **"I hid not my face from shame and spitting" → "my face I did not hide from dishonors and spitting"** — פָּנַי לֹא הִסְתַּרְתִּי (הִסְתִּיר "hide", as "hide the face" in the Psalms), מִכְּלִמּוֹת plural of כְּלִמָּה "dishonor" (reviewer row), וָרֹק "and spitting" (רֹק "spittle").
- **"For the Lord GOD will help me" → "And Lord Yahweh helps me"** (v7) — Hebrew וַאדֹנָי יְהוִה יַעֲזָר־לִי, "and"; an imperfect ("helps", or "will help"; the same in v9). **"therefore shall I not be confounded" → "therefore I have not been put to shame"** — לֹא נִכְלָמְתִּי, perfect; נִכְלַם "be put to shame" (its row; the noun כְּלִמָּה of v6 is "dishonor"). **"therefore have I set my face like a flint" → "therefore I have set my face like flint"** — שַׂמְתִּי, חַלָּמִישׁ "flint" (its row). **"and I know that I shall not be ashamed"** — וָאֵדַע, בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed" (its row; kept apart from נִכְלַם).
- **"He is near that justifieth me" → "Near is he who justifies me"** (v8) — Hebrew קָרוֹב מַצְדִּיקִי, in the Hebrew order; הִצְדִּיק "justify (declare righteous)", set against הִרְשִׁיעַ "condemn" in v9 (their row). **"who will contend with me? let us stand together" → "who will contend with me? Let us stand together!"** — מִי־יָרִיב אִתִּי (רִיב "contend"), נַעַמְדָה, cohortative. **"who is mine adversary? let him come near to me" → "Who is the owner of my judgment? Let him draw near to me"** — מִי־בַעַל מִשְׁפָּטִי, literally "who is the owner of my judgment" (בַּעַל + noun: the one who has the thing, as בַּעַל דְּבָרִים "one who has a matter"; = the one who has a case against me, "adversary"); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (of a case brought, its row); יִגַּשׁ אֵלָי, נָגַשׁ "draw near" (its row).
- **"Behold, the Lord GOD will help me; who is he that shall condemn me?"** (v9) — Hebrew הֵן, יַעֲזָר־לִי (as v7), מִי־הוּא יַרְשִׁיעֵנִי (הִרְשִׁיעַ "condemn, declare wicked"). **"lo, they all shall wax old as a garment" → "Behold, all of them like a garment shall wear out"** — הֵן כֻּלָּם כַּבֶּגֶד יִבְלוּ (בָּלָה "wear out", its row; בֶּגֶד "garment"), in the Hebrew order. **"the moth shall eat them up" → "a moth shall eat them"** — עָשׁ, no article.
- **"Who is among you that feareth the LORD, that obeyeth the voice of his servant" → "Who among you fears Yahweh, listening to the voice of his servant?"** (v10) — Hebrew יְרֵא יְהוָה, שֹׁמֵעַ בְּקוֹל עַבְדּוֹ, a participle: שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל "listen to the voice of" (its row; "obey" is not the word). **"that walketh in darkness, and hath no light? let him trust" → "He who has walked in dark places, and there is no brightness for him, let him trust"** — אֲשֶׁר הָלַךְ חֲשֵׁכִים, a perfect, חֲשֵׁכִים plural "dark places"; וְאֵין נֹגַהּ לוֹ, נֹגַהּ "brightness" (not אוֹר "light"); by the accents (atnach after "his servant") the line about the walker belongs with "let him trust". Whether the one who walked in the dark is the servant or the one who fears Yahweh the Hebrew leaves open. **"and stay upon his God" → "and lean on his Elohim"** — וְיִשָּׁעֵן, נִשְׁעַן "lean"; יִבְטַח "let him trust" and וְיִשָּׁעֵן are jussive in sense (imperfects).
- **"all ye that kindle a fire, that compass yourselves about with sparks" → "all of you, kindlers of fire, who gird on firebrands"** (v11) — Hebrew קֹדְחֵי אֵשׁ (קָדַח "kindle"), מְאַזְּרֵי זִיקוֹת, piel participle of אָזַר "gird" (also read "light up"); זִיקוֹת "firebrands, sparks". **"walk in the light of your fire, and in the sparks that ye have kindled" → "walk in the flame of your fire, and among the firebrands you have set burning"** — בְּאוּר אֶשְׁכֶם, אוּר "flame, fire-light" (not אוֹר "light"); בִּעַרְתֶּם, בִּעֵר "set burning" (kept apart from קָדַח). **"This shall ye have of mine hand" → "From my hand this has come to you"** — מִיָּדִי הָיְתָה־זֹּאת לָכֶם, a perfect, literally "this has been to you". **"ye shall lie down in sorrow" → "in pain you shall lie down"** — לְמַעֲצֵבָה, noun of עָצַב (as עֶצֶב "pain", its row): "to (a place of) pain", in the Hebrew order.

### Notes
- **v4–9** — the speaker is not named; Adonai Yahweh is named four times (v4, 5, 7, 9). v10 names "his servant".
