# Isaiah 2

[1] The word that ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ son of ⟦amoz|Amotz|Amoz⟧ beheld concerning ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[2] And it shall be in the latter days:
established shall be the mountain of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
at the head of the mountains,
and it shall be lifted up above the hills;
and all the nations shall stream to it,

[3] and many peoples shall go and say:
"Come, and let us go up to the mountain of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
to the house of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and let him teach us of his ways,
and let us walk in his paths."
For from ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ shall go out ⟦torah|Torah|the law⟧,
and the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[4] And he shall judge between the nations,
and shall decide for many peoples;
and they shall beat their swords into mattocks,
and their spears into pruning knives;
nation shall not lift up sword against nation,
and they shall not learn war any more.

[5] House of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
come, and let us walk in the light of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] For you have abandoned your people, the house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
for they are full from the east,
and practice soothsaying like the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧,
and with the children of foreigners they clap hands.

[7] And his land has been filled with silver and gold,
and there is no end to his treasures;
and his land has been filled with horses,
and there is no end to his chariots.

[8] And his land has been filled with worthless idols;
to the work of his hands they bow down,
to what his fingers have made.

[9] And mankind has been bowed down,
and man has been brought low;
and do not forgive them!

[10] Go into the rock,
and hide yourself in the dust,
from the face of the dread of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and from the splendor of his exaltation.

[11] The haughty eyes of mankind are brought low,
and the loftiness of men shall be bowed down,
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ alone shall be set on high in that day.

[12] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts has a day
against all that is proud and high,
and against all that is lifted up — and it shall be brought low —

[13] and against all the cedars of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧, the high and the lifted up,
and against all the oaks of Bashan;

[14] and against all the high mountains,
and against all the lifted up hills;

[15] and against every high tower,
and against every fortified wall;

[16] and against all the ships of Tarshish,
and against all the desirable craft.

[17] And the haughtiness of mankind shall be bowed down,
and the loftiness of men shall be brought low,
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ alone shall be set on high in that day.

[18] And the worthless idols shall wholly pass away.

[19] And they shall go into the caves of the rocks
and into the holes of the dust,
from the face of the dread of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and from the splendor of his exaltation,
when he rises to terrify the earth.

[20] In that day mankind shall throw
his worthless idols of silver
and his worthless idols of gold,
which they made for him to bow down to,
to the moles and to the bats,

[21] to go into the crevices of the rocks
and into the clefts of the crags,
from the face of the dread of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and from the splendor of his exaltation,
when he rises to terrify the earth.

[22] Cease, for yourselves, from mankind,
who has breath in his nostrils,
for at what is he reckoned?

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v12) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת, plain words as the earlier books. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, by the accents; v6–9 are poetry in the Hebrew and are now set in lines.
- **"The word that Isaiah the son of Amoz saw" → "The word that Isaiah son of Amoz beheld"** (v1) — Hebrew חָזָה "behold" (as the חָזוֹן of 1:1).
- **"And it shall come to pass in the last days, that the mountain of the LORD's house shall be established in the top of the mountains, and shall be exalted above the hills" → "And it shall be in the latter days: established shall be the mountain of the house of Yahweh at the head of the mountains, and it shall be lifted up above the hills"** (v2) — Hebrew וְהָיָה "and it shall be"; בְּאַחֲרִית הַיָּמִים "in the latter days" (literally "in the after-part of the days"); נָכוֹן יִהְיֶה "established shall be", first in the line, kept; בְּרֹאשׁ "at the head of" (on the top, or as the chief); וְנִשָּׂא "lifted up"; מִגְּבָעוֹת "above the hills" (literally "from the hills"). **"flow unto it" → "stream to it"** — נָהַר, the verb of נָהָר "river".
- **"Come ye, and let us go up … and he will teach us of his ways, and we will walk in his paths" → "Come, and let us go up … and let him teach us of his ways, and let us walk in his paths"** (v3) — Hebrew וְיֹרֵנוּ (hifil of יָרָה "teach") and וְנֵלְכָה (cohortative) continue "let us go up": a wish, or purpose ("that he may teach us … that we may walk"); מִן partitive, "of his ways"; אֹרַח "path". **"for out of Zion shall go forth the law" → "For from Zion shall go out torah"** — תּוֹרָה without the article: token *torah*, "instruction" (the word of the root of "teach" in the same verse).
- **"he shall judge among the nations, and shall rebuke many people" → "he shall judge between the nations, and shall decide for many peoples"** (v4) — Hebrew בֵּין "between"; הוֹכִיחַ לְ "decide for" (the verb of deciding between parties, Gen 31:37); עַמִּים plural. **"plowshares" → "mattocks"** — Hebrew אִתִּים, the tool of 1 Sam 13:20 (there beside the plowshare, מַחֲרֶשֶׁת). **"pruninghooks" → "pruning knives"** — מַזְמֵרוֹת, from זָמַר "prune". **"neither shall they learn war any more" → "and they shall not learn war any more"** — וְלֹא.
- **Isa 2:2–4 = Mic 4:1–3** — the same oracle; this wording is set first, Micah follows where the Hebrew is the same (the Hebrew differs in small things: Mic 4:1 has "established" after "the mountain" and "lifted up" with הוּא; "nations" and "peoples" change places in Mic 4:1–3; Mic 4:3 adds "strong, far away").
- **"O house of Jacob, come ye" → "House of Jacob, come"** (v5) — the Hebrew has no "O". **"let us walk"** — וְנֵלְכָה, the "and" kept.
- **"Therefore thou hast forsaken thy people … because they be replenished from the east, and are soothsayers like the Philistines, and they please themselves in the children of strangers" → "For you have abandoned your people … for they are full from the east, and practice soothsaying like the Philistines, and with the children of foreigners they clap hands"** (v6) — Hebrew כִּי "for" both times; נָטַשׁ "abandon"; מָלְאוּ מִקֶּדֶם "they are full from the east" — with what is not said (קֶדֶם is also "of old"); עֹנְנִים participle of עוֹנֵן "practice soothsaying" (exact sense unknown); יַשְׂפִּיקוּ only here in this sense: "clap (hands)", the hands supplied (as סָפַק כַּף, Num 24:10), or "abound in"; uncertain. יַלְדֵי נָכְרִים "children of foreigners" (יֶלֶד "child", נָכְרִי "foreigner").
- **"Their land also is full of silver" → "And his land has been filled with silver"** (v7–8) — Hebrew וַתִּמָּלֵא, the wayyiqtol going on from the perfect of v6: "has been filled"; אַרְצוֹ "his land", the people as one man ("his treasures", "his chariots", "his hands", "his fingers"), while "they bow down" is plural; kept as written. מַרְכָּבָה "chariot".
- **"idols" → "worthless idols"** (v8, 18, 20) — Hebrew אֱלִילִים, "worthless things", sounding like אֵל.
- **"And the mean man boweth down, and the great man humbleth himself: therefore forgive them not" → "And mankind has been bowed down, and man has been brought low; and do not forgive them!"** (v9) — Hebrew אָדָם "mankind" and אִישׁ "man", the two words of v11, 17, 20, 22 (not "mean man … great man"); שָׁחַח "be bowed down", שָׁפֵל "be low"; וְאַל־תִּשָּׂא לָהֶם "and do not lift up for them" (= forgive, as Gen 18:24); "therefore" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Enter into the rock, and hide thee in the dust, for fear of the LORD, and for the glory of his majesty" → "Go into the rock, and hide yourself in the dust, from the face of the dread of Yahweh, and from the splendor of his exaltation"** (v10, 19, 21) — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי "from the face of" (fleeing); פַּחַד "dread"; הֲדַר "splendor"; גְּאוֹנוֹ "his exaltation" (גָּאוֹן, as Exod 15:7). The imperatives of v10 are singular.
- **"The lofty looks of man shall be humbled, and the haughtiness of men shall be bowed down" → "The haughty eyes of mankind are brought low, and the loftiness of men shall be bowed down"** (v11) — Hebrew עֵינֵי גַּבְהוּת אָדָם, literally "the eyes of the haughtiness of mankind"; שָׁפֵל is a perfect, "are brought low" (the future seen as done), then weqatal וְשַׁח "shall be bowed down"; v17 has the two verbs the other way round. **"the LORD alone shall be exalted" → "Yahweh alone shall be set on high"** (v11, 17) — וְנִשְׂגַּב, nifal of שָׂגַב "set on high". **"in that day"** (v11, 17, 20) — בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא, one wording through the prophets.
- **"For the day of the LORD of hosts shall be upon every one that is proud and lofty, and upon every one that is lifted up; and he shall be brought low" → "For Yahweh of Hosts has a day against all that is proud and high, and against all that is lifted up — and it shall be brought low —"** (v12) — Hebrew יוֹם לַיהוָה צְבָאוֹת, "a day is to Yahweh of Hosts"; עַל "against" through v12–16, each line with its "and"; וְשָׁפֵל "and low", kept as a clause.
- **"high and lifted up" → "the high and the lifted up"** (v13, 14) — Hebrew הָרָמִים וְהַנִּשָּׂאִים with the article; אַלּוֹן "oak".
- **"pleasant pictures" → "the desirable craft"** (v16) — Hebrew שְׂכִיּוֹת הַחֶמְדָּה; שְׂכִיָּה only here: a ship (beside "ships of Tarshish"), or a carved image, uncertain; חֶמְדָּה "desirableness".
- **"And the idols he shall utterly abolish" → "And the worthless idols shall wholly pass away"** (v18) — Hebrew יַחֲלֹף, qal "pass away", singular after the plural; כָּלִיל "wholly".
- **"And they shall go into the holes of the rocks, and into the caves of the earth" → "And they shall go into the caves of the rocks and into the holes of the dust"** (v19) — Hebrew מְעָרוֹת צֻרִים "caves of rocks", מְחִלּוֹת עָפָר "holes of dust" (עָפָר "dust", as v10); "they", no "men". **"when he ariseth to shake terribly the earth" → "when he rises to terrify the earth"** (v19, 21) — לַעֲרֹץ, עָרַץ "terrify".
- **"In that day a man shall cast his idols of silver, and his idols of gold, which they made each one for himself to worship" → "In that day mankind shall throw his worthless idols of silver and his worthless idols of gold, which they made for him to bow down to"** (v20) — Hebrew הָאָדָם "mankind" with "his"; עָשׂוּ־לוֹ "they made for him" (the makers are not named); לְהִשְׁתַּחֲוֹת "to bow down". **"to the moles"** — the Leningrad Codex writes לַחְפֹּר פֵּרוֹת as two words ("to dig holes"?); read as one word, חֲפַרְפָּרוֹת, "diggers" from חָפַר "dig": moles or other burrowing animals. Uncertain; the plainest sense kept.
- **"To go into the clefts of the rocks, and into the tops of the ragged rocks" → "to go into the crevices of the rocks and into the clefts of the crags"** (v21) — Hebrew נִקְרוֹת הַצֻּרִים (נְקָרָה, Exod 33:22 "the cleft of the rock") and סְעִפֵי הַסְּלָעִים (סְעִיף, Judg 15:8 "the cleft of the crag"): two words, so נְקָרָה "crevice" here; סֶלַע "crag".
- **"Cease ye from man, whose breath is in his nostrils: for wherein is he to be accounted of?" → "Cease, for yourselves, from mankind, who has breath in his nostrils, for at what is he reckoned?"** (v22) — Hebrew חִדְלוּ לָכֶם, the לָכֶם "for yourselves" kept; הָאָדָם "mankind"; נְשָׁמָה "breath", אַף "nostrils" (the body); בַמֶּה נֶחְשָׁב "at what is he reckoned" (חָשַׁב "reckon"). The verse is not in the Septuagint (from memory).

### Notes
- **v2–4** — the same oracle as Micah 4:1–3.
- **"A day to Yahweh of Hosts"** (v12) — the day of Yahweh, a prophetic theme (Amos 5:18; Joel; Zephaniah).
