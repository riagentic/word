# Isaiah 4

[1] And seven women shall take hold of one man in that day, saying, "We will eat our own bread and wear our own clothes, only let us be called by your name; take away our reproach."

[2] In that day the Branch — of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be beautiful and glorious,
and the fruit of the land shall be the pride and honor
of the survivors of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[3] And he who is left in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ and remains in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧
will be called holy,
everyone who has been recorded for life in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,

[4] when ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ shall have washed away
the filth of the daughters of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧
and cleansed the bloodstains of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ from its midst
by a spirit of judgment and by a spirit of burning.

[5] Then ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will create over the whole site of Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧
and over her assemblies
a cloud by day, and smoke and the shining of a flaming fire by night;
for over all the glory there will be a canopy.

[6] There will be a booth for shade by day from the heat,
and for a refuge and a shelter
from the storm and rain.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Poetic structure** — vv2-6 set as verse; KJV treated them as prose.

### Notes
- **v2** — "**Branch (*Tzemach*) of Yahweh**": messianic term; also Jer 23:5; Zech 3:8; 6:12.
