# Isaiah 4

[1] And seven women shall hold fast to one man in that day, saying, "Our bread we will eat, and our cloak we will wear; only let your name be called over us; take away our reproach."

[2] In that day the sprout of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be
for beauty and for glory,
and the fruit of the land for exaltation and for splendor
for the escape of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[3] And it shall be: the one left in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ and the one remaining in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ —
"holy" shall be said of him,
everyone written for life in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,

[4] when ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ has washed away
the filth of the daughters of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
and the blood of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ he rinses away from its midst
by a spirit of judgment and by a spirit of burning.

[5] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will create over every established place of Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧
and over her convocations
a cloud by day, and smoke,
and the shining of a fire of flame by night;
for over all glory, a canopy.

[6] And a booth shall be for a shade by day from the heat,
and for a refuge and for a hiding place
from the downpour and from the rain.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"the Lord" → "Adonai"** (v4) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי.
- **Layout** — v1 prose; v2–6 one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, by the accents. Hebrew numbering as ours (4:1 is not joined to chapter 3).
- **"in that day"** (v1, 2) — בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא, one wording through the prophets.
- **"And in that day seven women shall take hold of one man, saying, We will eat our own bread, and wear our own apparel" → "And seven women shall hold fast to one man in that day, saying, 'Our bread we will eat, and our cloak we will wear'"** (v1) — Hebrew order (the day after "one man"); הֶחֱזִיק בְּ "hold fast to"; לַחְמֵנוּ … שִׂמְלָתֵנוּ "our bread … our cloak", set first (no "own" in the Hebrew); שִׂמְלָה "cloak". **"let us be called by thy name" → "let your name be called over us"** — יִקָּרֵא שִׁמְךָ עָלֵינוּ, the name of the owner called over what is his. **"to take away our reproach" → "take away our reproach"** — אֱסֹף, an imperative, literally "gather (away)"; חֶרְפָּה "reproach".
- **"shall the branch of the LORD be beautiful and glorious" → "the sprout of Yahweh shall be for beauty and for glory"** (v2) — Hebrew צֶמַח, the noun of צָמַח "spring up": what springs up, a growth (Gen 19:25 "what grew on the ground"), here beside "the fruit of the land". Jer 23:5; 33:15; Zech 3:8; 6:12 use the word of a man of David's line ("Branch" in the tradition); what Isaiah means here is debated, and the word is kept plain. לִצְבִי וּלְכָבוֹד "for beauty and for glory" (צְבִי "beauty", also "gazelle"). The dash after "the Branch" is removed. **"excellent and comely" → "for exaltation and for splendor"** — לְגָאוֹן וּלְתִפְאֶרֶת: גָּאוֹן "exaltation" (2:10); תִּפְאֶרֶת "splendor" (reviewer row; "beauty" is צְבִי has in this verse; as 3:18). **"for them that are escaped of Israel" → "for the escape of Israel"** — לִפְלֵיטַת יִשְׂרָאֵל: פְּלֵיטָה "escape" (those who have escaped).
- **"he that is left in Zion, and he that remaineth in Jerusalem, shall be called holy" → "And it shall be: the one left in Zion and the one remaining in Jerusalem — 'holy' shall be said of him"** (v3) — Hebrew וְהָיָה "and it shall be"; הַנִּשְׁאָר "the one left" and הַנּוֹתָר "the one remaining", two verbs; קָדוֹשׁ יֵאָמֶר לוֹ "holy shall be said of him" (literally "to him"). **"every one that is written among the living" → "everyone written for life"** — הַכָּתוּב לַחַיִּים, literally "for life".
- **"When the Lord shall have washed away the filth … and shall have purged the blood of Jerusalem" → "when Adonai has washed away the filth … and the blood of Jerusalem he rinses away"** (v4) — Hebrew אִם "when" with a perfect, then the imperfect יָדִיחַ, hifil of דּוּחַ "rinse" (2 Chr 4:6); צֹאָה "filth" (of excrement, Isa 28:8); דְּמֵי plural, "bloods" (blood shed, as 1:15). **"by the spirit of judgment, and by the spirit of burning" → "by a spirit of judgment and by a spirit of burning"** — no article; רוּחַ is "spirit", "wind" or "breath": one word, the sense left open; בָּעֵר "burning" (or "sweeping away").
- **"And the LORD will create upon every dwelling place of mount Zion, and upon her assemblies" → "And Yahweh will create over every established place of Mount Zion and over her convocations"** (v5) — Hebrew וּבָרָא "and he will create" (בָּרָא); מְכוֹן "established place" (Exod 15:17); מִקְרָאֶהָ "her convocations" (מִקְרָא, as 1:13). **"a cloud and smoke by day, and the shining of a flaming fire by night" → "a cloud by day, and smoke, and the shining of a fire of flame by night"** — the Hebrew order ("a cloud by day and smoke"); נֹגַהּ "shining"; אֵשׁ לֶהָבָה "fire of flame". **"for upon all the glory shall be a defence" → "for over all glory, a canopy"** — כִּי עַל־כָּל־כָּבוֹד חֻפָּה, without a verb or article; חֻפָּה "canopy" (the bridal chamber of Ps 19:5; Joel 2:16).
- **"And there shall be a tabernacle for a shadow in the daytime from the heat, and for a place of refuge, and for a covert from storm and from rain" → "And a booth shall be for a shade by day from the heat, and for a refuge and for a hiding place from the downpour and from the rain"** (v6) — Hebrew סֻכָּה "booth"; צֵל "shade"; חֹרֶב "heat" (Gen 31:40; the "dryness" of Judg 6:37–40 is the same word); מַחְסֶה "refuge"; מִסְתּוֹר "hiding place"; זֶרֶם "downpour" (a rainstorm); מָטָר "rain".

### Notes
- **v2** — *tzemach* "sprout": the same word in Jer 23:5; 33:15; Zech 3:8; 6:12.
- **v5** — the cloud and the fire recall the wilderness (Exod 13:21–22).
