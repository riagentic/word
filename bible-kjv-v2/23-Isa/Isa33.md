# Isaiah 33

[1] Woe, you who lay waste,
and you not laid waste;
and you who deal treacherously,
and they have not dealt treacherously with him!
When you have completed laying waste,
you will be laid waste;
when you have finished dealing treacherously,
they will deal treacherously with you.

[2] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, show us favor; for you we have waited.
Be their arm every morning,
yes, our salvation in the time of distress.

[3] At the sound of a multitude peoples have fled;
at your rising up nations have scattered.

[4] And your spoil shall be gathered, the gathering of the devourer;
like the rushing of locusts one rushes upon it.

[5] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is set on high, for he abides on high;
he has filled ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ with judgment and righteousness.

[6] And he shall be the faithfulness of your times,
a store of salvations, wisdom and knowledge;
the fear of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, it is his treasure.

[7] Behold, their heroes have cried out outside;
the messengers of ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ weep bitterly.

[8] The highways are desolate;
the one who passes on the path has ceased.
He has broken a covenant;
he has rejected cities;
he has not esteemed mortal man.

[9] The land mourns, it languishes;
⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ is abashed, it withers;
the Sharon has become like the steppe,
and Bashan and ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧ shake off their leaves.

[10] "Now I will rise," says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
"now I will lift myself up;
now I will be lifted up.

[11] You conceive chaff; you bear stubble;
your breath is a fire that will consume you.

[12] And peoples shall be burnings of lime;
thorns cut down, in the fire they shall be set ablaze.

[13] Hear, you who are far off, what I have done;
and know, you who are near, my might."

[14] Sinners in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ are in dread;
trembling has seized the godless:
"Who of us shall sojourn with a consuming fire?
Who of us shall sojourn with everlasting burnings?"

[15] One who walks righteously and speaks uprightly,
who rejects the unjust gain of extortions,
who shakes his palms from holding a bribe,
who stops his ear from hearing of bloodshed
and shuts his eyes from looking on evil,

[16] he shall abide on the heights;
strongholds of crags shall be his secure height;
his bread shall be given; his waters are sure.

[17] A king in his beauty your eyes shall behold;
they shall see a land of far distances.

[18] Your heart shall muse on dread:
"Where is the one who counts?
Where is the one who weighs?
Where is the one who counts the towers?"

[19] The insolent people you shall not see,
a people deep of lip, past understanding,
stammering of tongue, without understanding.

[20] Behold ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, the city of our appointed times!
Your eyes shall see ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
a habitation at ease,
a tent that shall not be moved,
whose pegs shall never be pulled up,
and none of whose cords shall be torn apart.

[21] But there ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is mighty for us,
a place of rivers, of streams broad on both sides;
no ship with oars shall go on it,
and no mighty vessel shall pass over it.

[22] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is our judge;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is our commander;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is our king;
he will save us.

[23] Your cords are let loose;
they do not hold firm the stand of their mast;
they have not spread out a sail.
Then prey of spoil in abundance is divided;
the lame plunder the plunder.

[24] And no inhabitant shall say, "I am sick";
the people who dwell in it are forgiven iniquity.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v1 keeps the existing break of each colon at its minor accent.
- **"Woe to thee that spoilest, and thou wast not spoiled; and dealest treacherously, and they dealt not treacherously with thee" → "Woe, you who lay waste, and you not laid waste; and you who deal treacherously, and they have not dealt treacherously with him"** (v1) — Hebrew הוֹי "Woe" (the prophets' formula); שׁוֹדֵד, בּוֹגֵד participles, here addressed ("you who"); שָׁדַד "lay waste" (the Prov 21–24 row; four times in the verse); שָׁדוּד passive participle, no verb ("you not laid waste"). בָּגְדוּ בוֹ has "with him" (third person) in the Hebrew, though the one who lays waste is addressed: kept as printed; "with you" is the smoothing.
- **"when thou shalt cease to spoil … when thou shalt make an end to deal treacherously" → "When you have completed laying waste … when you have finished dealing treacherously"** (v1) — Hebrew כַּהֲתִמְךָ, of תָּמַם "be complete"; כַּנְּלֹתְךָ is found only here: "when you have finished" is the common reading (from a root נלה, or a slip for כְּכַלֹּתְךָ, from memory); uncertain.
- **"O LORD, be gracious unto us; we have waited for thee" → "Yahweh, show us favor; for you we have waited"** (v2) — no "O" (vocative); חָנֵּנוּ, the חָנַן "show favor"; קִוִּינוּ perfect, קִוָּה "wait for", with "for you" first as in the Hebrew.
- **"be thou their arm" → "Be their arm"** (v2) — Hebrew זְרֹעָם "their arm" (MT); "our arm" in some versions (from memory) is not followed. **"our salvation also" → "yes, our salvation"** — אַף. **"trouble" → "distress"** — צָרָה.
- **"At the noise of the tumult the people fled" → "At the sound of a multitude peoples have fled"** (v3) — Hebrew קוֹל הָמוֹן, "sound" of a crowd (קוֹל of a thing), הָמוֹן "multitude"; עַמִּים plural; perfects. **"the lifting up of thyself" → "your rising up"** — רוֹמְמֻתֶךָ, from רוּם (only here).
- **"like the gathering of the caterpiller … shall he run upon them" → "the gathering of the devourer; like the rushing of locusts one rushes upon it"** (v4) — the first line has no "like": אֹסֶף הֶחָסִיל stands beside "your spoil" as its picture; חָסִיל "devourer" (a kind or stage of locust, as 1 Kgs 8:37; uncertain). גֵּבִים "locusts"; מַשַּׁק / שׁוֹקֵק "rush to and fro"; בּוֹ "upon it" (the spoil). "Your" is plural.
- **"The LORD is exalted; for he dwelleth on high" → "Yahweh is set on high, for he abides on high"** (v5) — Hebrew נִשְׂגָּב, nifal of שׂגב "set on high"; שֹׁכֵן, שָׁכַן "abide"; מָרוֹם "height". **"he fills" → "he has filled"** — perfect מִלֵּא. **"justice" → "judgment"** — מִשְׁפָּט.
- **"And wisdom and knowledge shall be the stability of thy times, and strength of salvation: the fear of the LORD is his treasure" → "And he shall be the faithfulness of your times, a store of salvations, wisdom and knowledge; the fear of Yahweh, it is his treasure"** (v6) — Hebrew וְהָיָה (masculine: "he", that is Yahweh of v5, or "it"; the feminine nouns cannot be its subject); אֱמוּנָה "faithfulness" (not "stability"); חֹסֶן "store" (the Prov 13–16 row; "wealth"); יְשׁוּעֹת plural. אוֹצָרוֹ "his treasure" (the Hebrew does not name "Zion"; "his" may be the faithful man or Yahweh); אוֹצָר is elsewhere "treasury": here what is stored.
- **"their valiant ones shall cry without" → "their heroes have cried out outside"** (v7) — Hebrew אֶרְאֶלָּם, a word found only here: "their heroes" is the common reading ("their Ariel", as 29:1, or "I will appear to them" are also proposed, from memory); uncertain. צָעֲקוּ perfect; חֻצָה "outside". **"ambassadors of peace" → "messengers of shalom"** — מַלְאֲכֵי שָׁלוֹם (מַלְאָךְ "messenger"; token `shalom`).
- **"the wayfaring man ceaseth" → "the one who passes on the path has ceased"** (v8) — Hebrew שָׁבַת עֹבֵר אֹרַח. **"he hath broken the covenant, he hath despised the cities, he regardeth no man" → "He has broken a covenant; he has rejected cities; he has not esteemed mortal man"** — בְּרִית without article; מָאַס "reject"; עָרִים "cities" (MT; the Isaiah scroll reads עֵדִים "witnesses", from memory: not followed); חָשַׁב "reckon, esteem"; אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man".
- **"Lebanon is ashamed and hewn down … shake off their fruits" → "Lebanon is abashed, it withers … shake off their leaves"** (v9) — Hebrew הֶחְפִּיר (the חָפֵר "be abashed" row), קָמַל "wither, decay"; אֻמְלְלָה "languishes". הַשָּׁרוֹן has the article: "the Sharon". עֲרָבָה "steppe" (dry country; as 35:1, 6). נֹעֵר "shakes off" has no object in the Hebrew: "their leaves" is supplied for English (the Hebrew may also mean "is shaken bare").
- **"now will I be exalted; now will I lift up myself" → "now I will lift myself up; now I will be lifted up"** (v10) — Hebrew אֵרוֹמָם (of רוּם, reflexive) then אֶנָּשֵׂא (nifal of נָשָׂא "lift up"), in this order. **"saith" → "says"** — יֹאמַר, not the formula כֹּה אָמַר.
- **"your breath, as fire, shall devour you" → "your breath is a fire that will consume you"** (v11) — Hebrew רוּחֲכֶם אֵשׁ תֹּאכַלְכֶם, word for word "your breath — a fire will consume you"; רוּחַ "breath" (also "spirit"). חֲשַׁשׁ "chaff (dry grass)", קַשׁ "stubble".
- **"as the burnings of lime: as thorns cut up shall they be burned" → "burnings of lime; thorns cut down, in the fire they shall be set ablaze"** (v12) — Hebrew מִשְׂרְפוֹת שִׂיד, no "as"; עַמִּים "peoples"; יִצַּתּוּ, nifal of יָצַת "set ablaze" (not שָׂרַף "burn").
- **"acknowledge my might" → "know … my might"** (v13) — Hebrew דְּעוּ, יָדַע "know"; גְּבוּרָה "might".
- **"are afraid; fearfulness hath surprised the hypocrites" → "are in dread; trembling has seized the godless"** (v14) — Hebrew פָּחֲדוּ "dread"; רְעָדָה "trembling"; חֲנֵפִים "the godless" (profane, polluted; not "hypocrites", as Job). **"shall dwell with the devouring fire" → "shall sojourn with a consuming fire"** — יָגוּר, גּוּר "sojourn" (twice); לָנוּ "of us"; אֵשׁ אוֹכֵלָה without article; מוֹקְדֵי עוֹלָם "everlasting burnings" (lit. "burnings of the age"). No verb of saying in the Hebrew.
- **"despiseth the gain of oppressions" → "rejects the unjust gain of extortions"** (v15) — Hebrew מֹאֵס, בֶּצַע "unjust gain", מַעֲשַׁקּוֹת "extortions". **"shaketh his hands from holding of bribes" → "shakes his palms from holding a bribe"** — כַּפָּיו "his palms"; תָּמַךְ "hold"; שֹׁחַד singular. **"his ears" → "his ear"** — אָזְנוֹ singular. "Righteously … uprightly" render the plural nouns צְדָקוֹת, מֵישָׁרִים used as adverbs ("walks righteousnesses, speaks uprightnesses").
- **"He shall dwell on high: his place of defence shall be the munitions of rocks: bread shall be given him" → "he shall abide on the heights; strongholds of crags shall be his secure height; his bread shall be given"** (v16) — Hebrew יִשְׁכֹּן, שָׁכַן "abide"; מְרוֹמִים "heights"; מְצָדוֹת סְלָעִים "strongholds of crags"; מִשְׂגָּב "secure height"; לַחְמוֹ "his bread", no "him". נֶאֱמָנִים "sure" (lit. "made firm").
- **"Thine eyes shall see the king in his beauty: they shall behold the land that is very far off" → "A king in his beauty your eyes shall behold; they shall see a land of far distances"** (v17) — Hebrew מֶלֶךְ without article, first in the line; תֶּחֱזֶינָה (חָזָה "behold") then תִּרְאֶינָה ("see"); אֶרֶץ מַרְחַקִּים "a land of distances".
- **"Thine heart shall meditate terror. Where is the scribe? where is the receiver?" → "Your heart shall muse on dread: Where is the one who counts? Where is the one who weighs?"** (v18) — Hebrew יֶהְגֶּה, הָגָה of the heart (inward: "muse"; "softly recite" is for words said); אֵימָה "dread". סֹפֵר participle "one who counts" (also the noun "scribe": one word, kept as one rendering with the third line, where it counts the towers); שֹׁקֵל "one who weighs" ("the tribute" is not in the Hebrew). The questions are present participles.
- **"Thou shalt not see a fierce people" → "The insolent people you shall not see"** (v19) — Hebrew אֶת־עַם נוֹעָז, definite, first; נוֹעָז only here: "insolent" or "fierce" (uncertain); "no more" is not in the Hebrew. **"a people of a deeper speech than thou canst perceive" → "a people deep of lip, past understanding"** — עִמְקֵי שָׂפָה "deep of lip"; מִשְּׁמוֹעַ, שָׁמַע of speech: "understand" (lit. "from hearing"; the reviewer row). **"of a stammering tongue, that thou canst not understand" → "stammering of tongue, without understanding"** — נִלְעַג (of לָעַג "mock", here of halting speech, as 28:11; uncertain); אֵין בִּינָה "there is no understanding".
- **"Look upon Zion, the city of our solemnities" → "Behold Zion, the city of our appointed times"** (v20) — Hebrew חֲזֵה (חָזָה "behold"); קִרְיַת מוֹעֲדֵנוּ, מוֹעֵד "appointed time" (the feasts are meant). **"a quiet habitation, a tabernacle that shall not be taken down" → "a habitation at ease, a tent that shall not be moved"** — נָוֶה שַׁאֲנָן; אֹהֶל "tent"; יִצְעָן only here ("be moved, be packed up"). **"not one of the stakes thereof shall ever be removed, neither shall any of the cords thereof be broken" → "whose pegs shall never be pulled up, and none of whose cords shall be torn apart"** — יִסַּע, נָסַע "pull up" (the tent pegs); בַּל … לָנֶצַח "never" (lit. "not … forever"); יָתֵד "peg"; חֶבֶל "cord"; נִתַּק "be torn apart".
- **"But there the glorious LORD will be unto us a place of broad rivers and streams" → "But there Yahweh is mighty for us, a place of rivers, of streams broad on both sides"** (v21) — Hebrew כִּי אִם "but" after the negatives of v20; אַדִּיר "mighty", the same word as the "mighty vessel" of the last line (the play kept); no verb. יְאֹרִים "streams" (the word of the arms of the Nile; no token: no Nile here); רַחֲבֵי יָדַיִם "broad of hands", broad on both sides. **"galley with oars … gallant ship" → "ship with oars … mighty vessel"** — אֳנִי־שַׁיִט; צִי אַדִּיר.
- **"our lawgiver" → "our commander"** (v22) — Hebrew מְחֹקְקֵנוּ, מְחֹקֵק of a person (the row; from חָקַק "inscribe, decree": "lawgiver" is the other reading).
- **"Thy tacklings are loosed; they could not well strengthen their mast, they could not spread the sail" → "Your cords are let loose; they do not hold firm the stand of their mast; they have not spread out a sail"** (v23) — Hebrew נִטְּשׁוּ, of נָטַשׁ "abandon, let go"; חֲבָלָיִךְ "your cords" (feminine: the city as a ship); כֵן־תָּרְנָם "the stand (base) of their mast" (the כֵּן row); נֵס "sail" here (elsewhere "banner"; the same word, Ezek 27:7); יְחַזְּקוּ imperfect, פָּרְשׂוּ perfect. **"then is the prey of a great spoil divided; the lame take the prey" → "Then prey of spoil in abundance is divided; the lame plunder the plunder"** — עַד "prey" (Gen 49:27), שָׁלָל "spoil", מַרְבֶּה "abundance"; בָּזְזוּ בַז, verb and noun of one root.
- **"shall be forgiven their iniquity" → "are forgiven iniquity"** (v24) — Hebrew נְשֻׂא עָוֹן, lit. "lifted of iniquity" (נָשָׂא "bear, take away"); "their" is not in the Hebrew. שָׁכֵן "inhabitant" (one who abides; also "neighbor"); בָּהּ "in it" (the city).

### Notes
- **Structure** — woe against the one who lays waste (v1), the people's prayer (v2–4), the hymn (v5–6), the lament (v7–9), Yahweh's word (v10–13), the question at the gate (v14–16, as Ps 15; 24:3–6), the king and the city (v17–24).
