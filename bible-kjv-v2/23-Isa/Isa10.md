# Isaiah 10

[1] Woe to those who decree decrees of wickedness,
and to the writers who have written toil,

[2] to turn aside the poor from the cause
and to rob the judgment of the afflicted of my people,
that widows may be their spoil,
and the fatherless they may plunder!

[3] And what will you do for the day of visitation,
and for the devastation that will come from afar?
To whom will you flee for help,
and where will you leave your glory?

[4] Nothing but bowing down under the prisoners,
and under the slain they fall.
For all this his anger has not turned back,
and still his hand is stretched out.

[5] Woe to ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, the rod of my anger!
And the staff in their hand is my indignation.

[6] Against a godless nation I send him,
and against the people of my raging I command him,
to take spoil and to plunder plunder,
and to make it a trampling like the mire of the streets.

[7] But he does not think so,
and his heart does not mean so;
but to destroy is in his heart,
and to cut off nations not a few.

[8] For he says:

"Are not my princes together kings?

[9] Is not ⟦calno|Kalno|Calno⟧ like ⟦carchemish|Karkemish|Carchemish⟧?
Or is not ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧ like Arpad?
Or is not ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ like ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧?

[10] As my hand has found the kingdoms of the worthless idol,
and their carved images were more than ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧'s and ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧'s,

[11] shall I not, as I have done to ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ and to her worthless idols,
so do to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and to her idols?"

[12] And it shall be, when ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ has finished all his work on Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, I will visit upon the fruit of the greatness of heart of the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, and upon the splendor of the height of his eyes.

[13] For he has said:

"By the power of my hand I have done it,
and by my wisdom, for I have understanding;
and I remove the borders of peoples,
and their stores I have plundered,
and like a mighty one I bring down those who dwell there.

[14] And my hand has found like a nest
the wealth of the peoples;
and as one gathers forsaken eggs,
all the earth I have gathered;
and there was none that fluttered a wing,
or opened a mouth, or chirped."

[15] Shall the axe vaunt itself over the one who hews with it?
Or shall the saw make itself great over the one who waves it?
As if a rod should wave those who raise it!
As if a staff should raise not-wood!

[16] Therefore the Lord, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, will send
among his fat ones leanness,
and under his glory a burning shall burn
like the burning of fire.

[17] And the light of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall become a fire,
and his Holy One a flame;
and it shall burn and devour
his thorns and his briers in one day.

[18] And the glory of his forest and of his garden land,
from soul and to flesh, he will make an end of,
and it shall be like the wasting away of one who is sick.

[19] And the remnant of the trees of his forest shall be a number,
and a boy shall write them down.

[20] And it shall be in that day,
the remnant of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and the escape of the house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧
shall no more again lean on him who struck it,
but shall lean on ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, in truth.

[21] A remnant shall return, the remnant of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
to Mighty ⟦el|El|God⟧.

[22] For though your people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ be like the sand of the sea,
a remnant in it shall return.
An annihilation is decided, overflowing with righteousness.

[23] For a full end, and one decided,
⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ of Hosts is making
in the midst of all the earth.

[24] Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ of Hosts: "Do not fear, my people who dwell in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧; with the rod he strikes you, and his staff he lifts up against you in the way of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[25] For yet a very little while, and the indignation shall come to an end, and my anger shall be toward their wearing away.

[26] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts will stir up against him a whip, like the blow on ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ at the rock of ⟦oreb|Orev|Oreb⟧; and his staff shall be over the sea, and he will lift it up in the way of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[27] And it shall be in that day, his burden shall depart from upon your shoulder, and his yoke from upon your neck, and the yoke shall be destroyed because of oil."

[28] He has come upon ⟦aiath|Ayyat|Aiath⟧,
he has passed through Migron;
at ⟦michmash|Michmas|Michmash⟧ he lays up his gear.

[29] They have passed over the pass;
"⟦geba|Geva|Geba⟧ is a lodging place for us";
Ramah trembles,
⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧ of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ has fled.

[30] Cry aloud with your voice, daughter of Gallim!
Give heed, ⟦laishah|Layshah|Laishah⟧!
Afflicted one, ⟦anathoth|Anatot|Anathoth⟧!

[31] Madmenah has fled;
the inhabitants of ⟦gebim|Gevim|Gebim⟧ have brought to safety.

[32] Yet this day, at ⟦nob|Nov|Nob⟧, to stand;
he waves his hand at the mountain of the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
the hill of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[33] Behold, the Lord, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts,
lops the bough with a terrifying crash;
and the high of stature are hewn down,
and the lofty shall be brought low.

[34] And he will strike down the thickets of the forest with iron,
and the ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ shall fall by a mighty one.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v16, 23, 24, 26, 33) — Hebrew צְבָאוֹת. **"the Lord GOD of hosts" → "the Lord Yahweh of Hosts"** (v23, 24) — אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה צְבָאוֹת, both names (the `adonai-yhwh` token, the prophets' wording). **"the Lord" kept** (v16, 33) — הָאָדוֹן, the common noun with the article (not אֲדֹנָי); **"Adonai"** (v12) — אֲדֹנָי.
- **Layout** — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line; v28–32 (short lines in the Hebrew, marked by the accents) are now set as verse; v12 and v24–27 stay prose.
- **"Woe unto them that decree unrighteous decrees, and to the writers that write grievousness which they have prescribed" → "Woe to those who decree decrees of wickedness, and to the writers who have written toil"** (v1) — הוֹי "Woe" (no preposition in the Hebrew: "to" is English); חִקְקֵי־אָוֶן "decrees of wickedness" (אָוֶן); מְכַתְּבִים עָמָל כִּתֵּבוּ "writers (who) have written toil" (עָמָל "toil", the trouble they cause).
- **"To turn aside the needy from judgment, and to take away the right from the poor of my people" → "to turn aside the poor from the cause and to rob the judgment of the afflicted of my people"** (v2) — דַּלִּים "poor", עֲנִיִּים "afflicted" (the two words in their own places); מִדִּין "from the cause" (דִּין); גָּזַל מִשְׁפָּט "rob the judgment". **"that they may rob the fatherless" → "and the fatherless they may plunder"** — יָבֹזּוּ (בָּזַז), Hebrew order; שָׁלָל "spoil".
- **"in the day of visitation, and in the desolation" → "for the day of visitation, and for the devastation"** (v3) — לְ "for" both times; פְּקֻדָּה "visitation" (of פָּקַד "visit"; not "punishment"); שׁוֹאָה "devastation". **"where will ye leave your glory" kept** — כָּבוֹד "glory" (here the wealth and honor of the rich). The verse opens with וּ "And".
- **"Without me they shall bow down under the prisoners, and they shall fall under the slain" → "Nothing but bowing down under the prisoners, and under the slain they fall"** (v4) — Hebrew בִּלְתִּי כָרַע תַּחַת אַסִּיר וְתַחַת הֲרוּגִים יִפֹּלוּ: בִּלְתִּי "except, nothing but"; כָרַע a perfect singular, literally "except one has bowed down"; אַסִּיר singular as a collective; תַּחַת "under" (or "in place of, among"). The line is hard; KJV supplies "me". A conjecture (from memory) reads the names of two foreign gods in the first words; the MT is kept. The refrain: see 9:12.
- **"O Assyrian, the rod of mine anger, and the staff in their hand is mine indignation" → "Woe to Assyria, the rod of my anger! And the staff in their hand is my indignation"** (v5) — הוֹי "Woe"; אַשּׁוּר the name of the land and people (token `assyria`, also v12, 24); אַף "anger"; זַעַם "indignation" (also v25); וּמַטֶּה־הוּא, literally "and a staff it (is)".
- **"against an hypocritical nation" → "against a godless nation"** (v6) — חָנֵף "godless" (as 9:17); עֶבְרָתִי "my raging" (as 9:19). **"to take the spoil, and to take the prey" → "to take spoil and to plunder plunder"** — the cognates שָׁלָל … בַּז kept. **"to tread them down like the mire of the streets" → "to make it a trampling like the mire of the streets"** — qere וּלְשׂוּמוֹ; the written text ולשימו is the same verb in another spelling (שִׂים), no change of meaning. מִרְמָס "a trampling"; חֹמֶר "clay, mortar", here the mud of the streets ("mire"). "It" is the people.
- **"Howbeit he meaneth not so, neither doth his heart think so" → "But he does not think so, and his heart does not mean so"** (v7) — דִּמָּה "think" (Ps 50:21), חָשַׁב "mean" (Gen 50:20); כִּי "but" after the negatives; לְהַשְׁמִיד "to destroy", לְהַכְרִית "to cut off".
- **"Are not my princes altogether kings?" → "Are not my princes together kings?"** (v8) — שָׂרַי "my princes" (שַׂר), יַחְדָּו "together". The verbs of saying (v8 יֹאמַר, v13 אָמַר) are kept: "he says", "he has said".
- **"Is not Hamath as Arpad? is not Samaria as Damascus?" → "Or is not Hamath like Arpad? Or is not Samaria like Damascus?"** (v9) — אִם־לֹא "or (is it) not", kept.
- **"As my hand hath found the kingdoms of the idols, and whose graven images did excel them of Jerusalem and of Samaria" → "As my hand has found the kingdoms of the worthless idol, and their carved images were more than Jerusalem's and Samaria's"** (v10) — הָאֱלִיל singular, a collective ("the worthless idol": the אֱלִיל row); פְּסִילִים "carved images"; מִן comparative.
- **"Shall I not, as I have done unto Samaria and her idols, so do to Jerusalem and her idols?" → "shall I not, as I have done to Samaria and to her worthless idols, so do to Jerusalem and to her idols?"** (v11) — Hebrew order (Samaria first); אֱלִילִים "worthless idols" (Samaria), עֲצַבִּים "idols" (Jerusalem): two words.
- **"Wherefore it shall come to pass, that when the Lord hath performed his whole work" → "And it shall be, when Adonai has finished all his work"** (v12) — וְהָיָה; בִּצַּע "finish" (literally "cut off" the work). **"I will punish the fruit of the stout heart of the king of Assyria, and the glory of his high looks" → "I will visit upon the fruit of the greatness of heart of the king of Assyria, and upon the splendor of the height of his eyes"** — פָּקַד עַל "visit upon"; פְּרִי "fruit" (the earlier text had "speech"); גֹּדֶל לֵבָב as 9:9; תִּפְאֶרֶת "splendor" (the boasting display; "beauty" would say looks here); רוּם עֵינָיו "the height of his eyes" (= his haughty look). The speaker turns from "Adonai" to "I", as the Hebrew does.
- **"For he saith, By the strength of my hand I have done it, and by my wisdom; for I am prudent" → "For he has said: By the power of my hand I have done it, and by my wisdom, for I have understanding"** (v13) — אָמַר perfect; כֹּחַ "power"; נְבֻנוֹתִי "I have understanding" (נָבוֹן). **"and have robbed their treasures" → "and their stores I have plundered"** — qere וַעֲתוּדוֹתֵיהֶם "their stores (what is laid ready)"; the written text ועתידתיהם "their things to come" (the same root עתד); שׁוֹשֵׂתִי "I have plundered" (שָׁסָה). **"and I have put down the inhabitants like a valiant man" → "and like a mighty one I bring down those who dwell there"** — כַּאבִּיר "like a mighty one" (אַבִּיר; "bull" is a common sense: the Ps 50:13 row); יוֹשְׁבִים "those who dwell", "there" supplied; "on thrones" (the modern versions) is not written. וְאָסִיר … וְאוֹרִיד are imperfects with simple "and": "I remove … I bring down".
- **"the riches of the people" → "the wealth of the peoples"** (v14) — חֵיל הָעַמִּים, plural. **"as one gathereth eggs that are left, have I gathered all the earth" → "as one gathers forsaken eggs, all the earth I have gathered"** — עֲזֻבוֹת "forsaken" (עָזַב); Hebrew order; אֶרֶץ "earth" or "land". **"none that moved the wing, or opened the mouth, or peeped" → "none that fluttered a wing, or opened a mouth, or chirped"** — נֹדֵד "flutter" (the verb of "fled" in v31), no articles; "or" for וְ under the negation.
- **"Shall the axe boast itself against him that heweth therewith? or shall the saw magnify itself against him that shaketh it?" → "Shall the axe vaunt itself over the one who hews with it? Or shall the saw make itself great over the one who waves it?"** (v15) — הִתְפָּאֵר "vaunt itself" (root of תִּפְאֶרֶת "splendor", v12); עַל "over"; מֵנִיף / הָנִיף "wave" (the verb of the wave offering). **"as if the rod should shake itself against them that lift it up, or as if the staff should lift up itself, as if it were no wood" → "As if a rod should wave those who raise it! As if a staff should raise not-wood!"** — מְרִימָיו "those who raise it" (plural); לֹא־עֵץ "not-wood" (the man who holds it; as the "no-el" of Deut 32:21).
- **"shall the Lord, the Lord of hosts, send among his fat ones leanness" → "the Lord, Yahweh of Hosts, will send among his fat ones leanness"** (v16) — הָאָדוֹן יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת; מִשְׁמַנָּיו "his fat ones" (the earlier text had "stout warriors"); רָזוֹן "leanness" (as Ps 106:15; the earlier text had "wasting sickness"). **"he shall kindle a burning like the burning of a fire" → "a burning shall burn like the burning of fire"** — יֵקַד יְקֹד כִּיקוֹד, the cognates kept; no subject.
- **"his thorns and his briers"** (v17) — שִׁיתוֹ וּשְׁמִירוֹ, the pair of 9:18 in reverse order; each noun has "his". קְדוֹשׁוֹ "his Holy One". וּבָעֲרָה וְאָכְלָה "and it shall burn and devour" (the flame).
- **"And shall consume the glory of his forest, and of his fruitful field, both soul and body" → "And the glory of his forest and of his garden land, from soul and to flesh, he will make an end of"** (v18) — כַּרְמֶל "garden land" (as 2 Kgs 19:23); מִנֶּפֶשׁ וְעַד־בָּשָׂר "from soul and to flesh" (בָּשָׂר "flesh", not "body"); יְכַלֶּה "he will make an end of" (כִּלָּה with persons); the subject is not named (the earlier text supplied "Yahweh"). **"and they shall be as when a standardbearer fainteth" → "and it shall be like the wasting away of one who is sick"** — כִּמְסֹס נֹסֵס, both words of uncertain root: מָסַס "melt, waste away" and נָסַס "be sick" (so here); KJV takes נֹסֵס from נֵס "banner".
- **"And the rest of the trees of his forest shall be few, that a child may write them" → "And the remnant of the trees of his forest shall be a number, and a boy shall write them down"** (v19) — שְׁאָר "remnant" (as v20–22; 11:11, 16; the word of the name *Shear-yashuv*, 7:3); עֵץ singular collective; מִסְפָּר יִהְיוּ "they shall be a number" (= few enough to count); נַעַר "boy"; וְ "and".
- **"And it shall come to pass in that day" → "And it shall be in that day"** (v20, 27) — וְהָיָה בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא. **"such as are escaped of the house of Jacob" → "the escape of the house of Jacob"** — פְּלֵיטַת, as 2 Kgs 19:30. **"shall no more again stay upon him that smote them; but shall stay upon the LORD" → "shall no more again lean on him who struck it, but shall lean on Yahweh"** — לֹא־יוֹסִיף עוֹד "no more again"; נִשְׁעַן עַל "lean on" (both times); "it" is the remnant (singular suffix). **"in truth"** — בֶּאֱמֶת.
- **"unto the mighty God" → "to Mighty El"** (v21) — אֶל־אֵל גִּבּוֹר, the name of 9:6; no article. The line order of the Hebrew is kept.
- **"yet a remnant of them shall return" → "a remnant in it shall return"** (v22) — שְׁאָר יָשׁוּב בּוֹ; "only" and "yet" are not written. **"the consumption decreed shall overflow with righteousness" → "An annihilation is decided, overflowing with righteousness"** — כִּלָּיוֹן "annihilation" (from כָּלָה "be finished"); חָרוּץ "decided" (literally "cut"); שׁוֹטֵף "overflowing" (of a flood). Rom 9:27–28 quotes v22–23 from the Greek ("a remnant shall be saved"), which differs from the Hebrew; the Hebrew stands here.
- **"For the Lord GOD of hosts shall make a consumption, even determined" → "For a full end, and one decided, the Lord Yahweh of Hosts is making"** (v23) — כָלָה וְנֶחֱרָצָה, Hebrew order (the end first); עֹשֶׂה a participle, "is making". **"in the midst of all the land" → "in the midst of all the earth"** — אֶרֶץ, "land" or "earth".
- **"O my people that dwellest in Zion, be not afraid of the Assyrian" → "Do not fear, my people who dwell in Zion, of Assyria"** (v24) — no "O" (vocative row); Hebrew order; מֵאַשּׁוּר "of Assyria". **"he shall smite thee with a rod, and shall lift up his staff against thee, after the manner of Egypt" → "with the rod he strikes you, and his staff he lifts up against you in the way of Egypt"** — בְּדֶרֶךְ מִצְרָיִם "in the way of Egypt" (also v26), with the `egypt` token (the earlier text had the `egyptians` token and "as the Egyptians did").
- **"For yet a very little while, and the indignation shall cease, and mine anger in their destruction" → "For yet a very little while, and the indignation shall come to an end, and my anger shall be toward their wearing away"** (v25) — מְעַט מִזְעָר "a little, a trifle"; כָּלָה זַעַם "the indignation shall come to an end" (no "my"); תַּבְלִית "wearing away" (only here; from בָּלָה "wear out"); "shall be" supplied.
- **"And the LORD of hosts shall stir up a scourge for him according to the slaughter of Midian at the rock of Oreb" → "And Yahweh of Hosts will stir up against him a whip, like the blow on Midian at the rock of Oreb"** (v26) — עוֹרֵר "stir up"; עָלָיו "against him" (singular: Assyria); שׁוֹט "whip"; מַכָּה "blow" (of מִדְיָן, Judg 7:25). **"and as his rod was upon the sea, so shall he lift it up after the manner of Egypt" → "and his staff shall be over the sea, and he will lift it up in the way of Egypt"** — no "as … so"; מַטֶּה "staff".
- **"his burden shall be taken away from off thy shoulder" → "his burden shall depart from upon your shoulder"** (v27) — סוּר מֵעַל "depart from upon"; צַוָּאר "neck". **"and the yoke shall be destroyed because of the anointing" → "and the yoke shall be destroyed because of oil"** — מִפְּנֵי־שָׁמֶן: שֶׁמֶן "oil" (also "fatness"); the sense is not known (a yoke broken by a fat neck, or by the oil of anointing); a conjecture (from memory) joins the word to v28 as a place name. חֻבַּל "be destroyed".
- **"He is come to Aiath" → "He has come upon Aiath"** (v28) — עַל "upon". **"he hath laid up his carriages" → "he lays up his gear"** — יַפְקִיד "deposit, lay up" (the hifil of פָּקַד, elsewhere "appoint over"); כֵּלָיו "his gear".
- **"They are gone over the passage: they have taken up their lodging at Geba" → "They have passed over the pass; 'Geba is a lodging place for us'"** (v29) — מַעְבָּרָה "pass" (the place of passing over; "ford" of a river); גֶּבַע מָלוֹן לָנוּ, the soldiers' words, "for us". חָרְדָה "trembles" (חָרַד); נָסָה "has fled" (נוּס).
- **"Lift up thy voice, O daughter of Gallim: cause it to be heard unto Laish, O poor Anathoth" → "Cry aloud with your voice, daughter of Gallim! Give heed, Laishah! Afflicted one, Anathoth!"** (v30) — צַהֲלִי "cry aloud" (also 12:6, of joy); no "O"; הַקְשִׁיבִי "give heed"; עֲנִיָּה "afflicted" (עָנִי); many read עֲנִיהָ "answer her" (a change of pointing), the MT is kept.
- **"Madmenah is removed; the inhabitants of Gebim gather themselves to flee" → "Madmenah has fled; the inhabitants of Gebim have brought to safety"** (v31) — נָדְדָה "has fled" (נָדַד, the "flutter" of v14); הֵעִיזוּ "bring to safety" (as Exod 9:19), with no object written.
- **"As yet shall he remain at Nob that day" → "Yet this day, at Nob, to stand"** (v32) — עוֹד הַיּוֹם בְּנֹב לַעֲמֹד, an infinitive, word for word (= he will halt there). **"he shall shake his hand against the mount of the daughter of Zion" → "he waves his hand at the mountain of the daughter of Zion"** — יְנֹפֵף (נוּף "wave"); qere בַּת "daughter"; the written text בית "house" ("the mountain of the house of Zion").
- **"Behold, the Lord, the LORD of hosts, shall lop the bough with terror" → "Behold, the Lord, Yahweh of Hosts, lops the bough with a terrifying crash"** (v33) — מְסָעֵף "lops" (participle); פֻּארָה "bough" (singular); מַעֲרָצָה only here, from עָרַץ "terrify" (uncertain: "terror", "a terrifying crash"). **"and the high ones of stature shall be hewn down, and the haughty shall be humbled" → "and the high of stature are hewn down, and the lofty shall be brought low"** — גְּדוּעִים (גִּדַּע); שָׁפֵל "be low".
- **"And he shall cut down the thickets of the forest with iron, and Lebanon shall fall by a mighty one" → "And he will strike down the thickets of the forest with iron, and the Lebanon shall fall by a mighty one"** (v34) — נִקַּף piel "strike down" (the earlier text had "axe" for בַּרְזֶל "iron"); הַלְּבָנוֹן with the article; אַדִּיר "mighty".

### Notes
- **"Remnant" theme** (*she'ar*) — picks up the son's name *Shear-yashuv* (7:3): v19–22.
- **v22–23** — quoted in Rom 9:27–28 from a Greek form of the text.
- **v28–32** — the march of an army on Jerusalem from the north, town by town.
