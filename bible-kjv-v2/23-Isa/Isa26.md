# Isaiah 26

[1] In that day this song shall be sung in the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧:

"A strong city is ours;
salvation he sets as walls and rampart.

[2] Open the gates,
and let a righteous nation come in, keeping faithfulness.

[3] The inclination that is upheld
you guard in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧,
for in you it is trusting.

[4] Trust in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ perpetually,
for in ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧ ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|GOD⟧ is a Rock of ages.

[5] For he has bowed down those who dwell on high;
the lofty town he lays low,
he lays it low to the earth,
he makes it reach to the dust.

[6] The foot tramples it,
the feet of the afflicted,
the steps of the poor."

[7] The path for the righteous is uprightness;
Upright one, you make level the course of the righteous.

[8] Yes, in the path of your judgments,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, we have waited for you;
to your name and to your remembrance
is the desire of the soul.

[9] With my soul I have desired you in the night;
yes, with my spirit within me I seek you eagerly.
For when your judgments are for the earth,
the dwellers of the world learn righteousness.

[10] If favor is shown to the wicked,
he does not learn righteousness;
in the land of straightness he acts unjustly
and does not see the exaltation of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[11] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, your hand is lifted high,
they do not behold it.
They will behold the zeal for the people, and be ashamed;
yes, the fire for your adversaries will consume them.

[12] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, you will set ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ for us,
for also all our works you have worked for us.

[13] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
lords besides you have been owners over us;
only through you do we make mention of your name.

[14] The dead will not live;
the shades will not rise;
therefore you have visited and destroyed them,
and done away with every remembrance of them.

[15] You have added to the nation, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
you have added to the nation; you have been glorified;
you have put far off all the ends of the land.

[16] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, in the distress they visited you;
they poured out a whisper;
your discipline was on them.

[17] As a woman with child
draws near to give birth,
she writhes, she cries out in her pangs,
so we have been because of you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧.

[18] We have conceived, we have writhed;
as it were, we have given birth to wind.
Salvation we do not work for the land,
and the dwellers of the world do not fall.

[19] Your dead shall live;
my dead body, they shall rise.
Awake and shout for joy, abiders in the dust!
For a dew of lights is your dew,
and the earth will make the shades fall.

[20] Go, my people, enter into your chambers,
and shut your door behind you;
hide yourself for a little moment
until the indignation passes by.

[21] For behold, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is going out from his place
to visit the iniquity of the dweller of the earth upon him,
and the earth will uncover its blood
and will no more cover over its slain.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v13) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵינוּ.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"the LORD JEHOVAH" → "Yah Yahweh"** (v4) — Hebrew יָהּ יְהוָה, both forms of the divine name.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) or a part of it per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v3, 5 now follow the Hebrew order. The quotation marks for the song (v1–6) are the editors'; the Hebrew does not mark where it ends.
- **Vocative** (v7, 8, 11–13, 15–17) — no "O": the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"We have a strong city; salvation will God appoint for walls and bulwarks" → "A strong city is ours; salvation he sets as walls and rampart"** (v1) — Hebrew עִיר עָז־לָנוּ "a city of strength (is) to us"; יָשִׁית "he sets" (no subject named; KJV supplies "God"); חוֹמוֹת וָחֵל "walls and rampart" (חֵל singular).
- **"that the righteous nation which keepeth the truth may enter in" → "and let a righteous nation come in, keeping faithfulness"** (v2) — Hebrew וְיָבֹא, "and let … come in"; גּוֹי־צַדִּיק without the article; שֹׁמֵר אֱמֻנִים "keeping faithfulness" (אֱמֻנִים, a plural of the root of אֱמוּנָה; also read "the trustworthy").
- **"Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee" → "The inclination that is upheld you guard in shalom, shalom, for in you it is trusting"** (v3) — Hebrew יֵצֶר סָמוּךְ תִּצֹּר שָׁלוֹם שָׁלוֹם כִּי בְךָ בָּטוּחַ. יֵצֶר "inclination" (lit. "what is formed": the mind's bent; KJV "mind"); סָמוּךְ "upheld" (supported, leaning); נָצַר "guard"; שָׁלוֹם is written twice (KJV "perfect peace" gives the force of the doubling; "in" is supplied for the Hebrew accusative); בָּטוּחַ "trusting" (a passive participle, of the inclination, or of the man who has it).
- **"for ever … everlasting strength" → "perpetually … a Rock of ages"** (v4) — Hebrew עֲדֵי־עַד "perpetually"; צוּר עוֹלָמִים "rock of ages" (צוּר "rock", with a capital where it names Yahweh; עוֹלָמִים, plural of עוֹלָם "age"). בְּיָהּ "in Yah" kept.
- **"For he bringeth down them that dwell on high; the lofty city, he layeth it low; he layeth it low, even to the ground; he bringeth it even to the dust" → "For he has bowed down those who dwell on high; the lofty town he lays low, he lays it low to the earth, he makes it reach to the dust"** (v5) — Hebrew הֵשַׁח, a perfect, "he has bowed down" (as 25:12), then three imperfects; קִרְיָה "town"; נִשְׂגָּבָה "lofty" (set on high); יַגִּיעֶנָּה "he makes it reach" (as 25:12).
- **"even the feet of the poor, and the steps of the needy" → "the feet of the afflicted, the steps of the poor"** (v6) — Hebrew עָנִי "afflicted", דַּלִּים "poor" (plural); no "and".
- **"The way of the just is uprightness: thou, most upright, dost weigh the path of the just" → "The path for the righteous is uprightness; Upright one, you make level the course of the righteous"** (v7) — Hebrew אֹרַח "path" (also v8), מֵישָׁרִים "uprightness" (plural), מַעְגָּל "course"; צַדִּיק "righteous". יָשָׁר may be the one addressed ("Upright one", of Yahweh) or a predicate ("straight is the course of the righteous; you make it level"). פִּלֵּס "make level" (KJV "weigh").
- **"have we waited for thee; the desire of our soul is to thy name, and to the remembrance of thee" → "we have waited for you; to your name and to your remembrance is the desire of the soul"** (v8) — Hebrew קִוִּינוּךָ "we have waited for you"; תַּאֲוַת־נָפֶשׁ "the desire of (the) soul", no "our". תַּאֲוָה is elsewhere "craving"; here a good wish, so "desire". זֵכֶר "remembrance".
- **"will I seek thee early" → "I seek you eagerly"** (v9) — Hebrew אֲשַׁחֲרֶךָּ, שִׁחֵר "seek eagerly" (from the root of שַׁחַר "dawn": KJV "early"). נַפְשִׁי "with my soul" (lit. "my soul": the noun stands before the verb "I have desired you"). **"in the earth … will learn" → "for the earth … learn"** — Hebrew לָאָרֶץ "for / to the earth"; לָמְדוּ, a perfect: "they learn" (what is true when the judgments come). יֹשְׁבֵי תֵבֵל "the dwellers of the world" (also v18).
- **"Let favour be shewed to the wicked, yet will he not learn righteousness: in the land of uprightness will he deal unjustly, and will not behold the majesty of the LORD" → "If favor is shown to the wicked, he does not learn righteousness; in the land of straightness he acts unjustly and does not see the exaltation of Yahweh"** (v10) — Hebrew יֻחַן, a passive imperfect without "if": the condition is given by the sentence ("let favor be shown" as KJV is also possible). נְכֹחוֹת "straightness" (what is straight ahead; beside מֵישָׁרִים v7); יְעַוֵּל "acts unjustly" (verb of עָוֶל); גֵּאוּת "exaltation".
- **"LORD, when thy hand is lifted up, they will not see: but they shall see, and be ashamed for their envy at the people; yea, the fire of thine enemies shall devour them" → "Yahweh, your hand is lifted high, they do not behold it. They will behold the zeal for the people, and be ashamed; yes, the fire for your adversaries will consume them"** (v11) — Hebrew רָמָה "is high"; חָזָה "behold" (twice); קִנְאַת־עָם "the zeal for (the) people" (no "their", no "your"; or "the zeal of a people"; קִנְאָה is "jealousy / zeal"); the noun stands after "and be ashamed" in the Hebrew. אֵשׁ צָרֶיךָ "the fire of your adversaries": the fire meant for them, or their own fire. The imperfects may also be wishes ("let them behold … let the fire consume them").
- **"thou wilt ordain peace for us: for thou also hast wrought all our works in us" → "you will set shalom for us, for also all our works you have worked for us"** (v12) — Hebrew תִּשְׁפֹּת שָׁלוֹם, שָׁפַת "set" (as a pot set on the fire); the `shalom` token added for the noun; פָּעַל "work", מַעֲשֶׂה "work"; לָּנוּ "for us" (not "in us").
- **"other lords beside thee have had dominion over us: but by thee only will we make mention of thy name" → "lords besides you have been owners over us; only through you do we make mention of your name"** (v13) — Hebrew בְּעָלוּנוּ, verb of בַּעַל "owner, husband": "have been owners over us"; אֲדֹנִים "lords" (no "other"); לְבַד־בְּךָ "only through you" (or "you alone"); הִזְכִּיר "make mention of" (lit. "cause to be remembered").
- **"They are dead, they shall not live; they are deceased, they shall not rise" → "The dead will not live; the shades will not rise"** (v14) — Hebrew מֵתִים "dead ones"; רְפָאִים "the shades", the dead in Sheol (also v19; the people of the Rephaim is another use of the word). **"made all their memory to perish" → "done away with every remembrance of them"** — Hebrew וַתְּאַבֵּד כָּל־זֵכֶר לָמוֹ, piel of אָבַד; פָּקַד "visit" (KJV keeps it).
- **"Thou hast increased the nation … thou hadst removed it far unto all the ends of the earth" → "You have added to the nation … you have put far off all the ends of the land"** (v15) — Hebrew יָסַפְתָּ לַגּוֹי "you have added to the nation" (twice); נִכְבַּדְתָּ "you have been glorified"; רִחַקְתָּ כָּל־קַצְוֵי־אָרֶץ "you have put far off all the ends of the land" (= pushed its borders out; or "removed (them) far to all the ends of the earth", KJV). אֶרֶץ "land" or "earth".
- **"in trouble have they visited thee, they poured out a prayer when thy chastening was upon them" → "in the distress they visited you; they poured out a whisper; your discipline was on them"** (v16) — Hebrew בַּצַּר "in the distress"; פְּקָדוּךָ "they visited you" (פָּקַד; "sought" would be another verb); צָקוּן לַחַשׁ "they poured out a whisper" (לַחַשׁ is a whispered prayer or a charm; the form צָקוּן is unusual: uncertain); מוּסָרְךָ לָמוֹ "your discipline (was) to them", no "when".
- **"Like as a woman with child, that draweth near the time of her delivery" → "As a woman with child draws near to give birth"** (v17) — Hebrew הָרָה "with child"; תַּקְרִיב לָלֶדֶת "draws near to give birth", then two verbs without "and": תָּחִיל "she writhes", תִּזְעַק "she cries out"; חֲבָלִים "pangs". **"in thy sight" → "because of you"** — Hebrew מִפָּנֶיךָ, lit. "from your face" (the cause).
- **"We have been with child, we have been in pain, we have as it were brought forth wind; we have not wrought any deliverance in the earth; neither have the inhabitants of the world fallen" → "We have conceived, we have writhed; as it were, we have given birth to wind. Salvation we do not work for the land, and the dwellers of the world do not fall"** (v18) — Hebrew הָרִינוּ "we have conceived"; יְשׁוּעֹת, plural "salvations"; בַּל־נַעֲשֶׂה אֶרֶץ "we do not make (for) the land" ("for" is supplied); נָפַל "fall" (of birth, "be born", is also proposed; uncertain).
- **"Thy dead men shall live, together with my dead body shall they arise" → "Your dead shall live; my dead body, they shall rise"** (v19) — word for word: יִחְיוּ מֵתֶיךָ "your dead shall live" (to Yahweh); נְבֵלָתִי יְקוּמוּן "my dead body, they shall rise" — a singular noun with "my" and a plural verb (the dead of the people as the prophet's own; KJV supplies "together with"; many read נִבְלֹתָם "their bodies": not followed). **"Awake and sing, ye that dwell in dust" → "Awake and shout for joy, abiders in the dust"** — הָקִיצוּ וְרַנְּנוּ; שֹׁכְנֵי עָפָר, שָׁכַן "abide". **"for thy dew is as the dew of herbs, and the earth shall cast out the dead" → "For a dew of lights is your dew, and the earth will make the shades fall"** — טַל אוֹרֹת "a dew of lights" (אוֹרֹת is also "herbs, mallows", 2 Kgs 4:39; uncertain); וָאָרֶץ רְפָאִים תַּפִּיל "and the earth will make the shades fall" (= cast them out, as at a birth; or "and on the land of the shades you will make it fall", of the dew); רְפָאִים "the shades".
- **"Come, my people … shut thy doors about thee: hide thyself as it were for a little moment, until the indignation be overpast" → "Go, my people … shut your door behind you; hide yourself for a little moment until the indignation passes by"** (v20) — Hebrew לֵךְ "go"; the qere דְּלָתְךָ "your door" is translated, the written form דלתיך is "your doors"; the qere יַעֲבָר and the written יעבור are the same verb, "passes by"; זַעַם "indignation".
- **"to punish the inhabitants of the earth for their iniquity: the earth also shall disclose her blood, and shall no more cover her slain" → "to visit the iniquity of the dweller of the earth upon him, and the earth will uncover its blood and will no more cover over its slain"** (v21) — Hebrew לִפְקֹד עֲוֺן יֹשֵׁב־הָאָרֶץ עָלָיו: פָּקַד "visit" (iniquity upon a person), יֹשֵׁב singular; יֹצֵא "is going out"; גִּלָּה "uncover"; דָּמֶיהָ, plural "bloods" (bloodshed); עַל "over"; הֲרוּגֶיהָ "its slain" (of הָרַג "kill").

### Notes
- **v19** — "Your dead shall live": one of the clearest Old Testament texts on the rising of the dead (cf. Dan 12:2).
- **v3** — *shalom shalom*: the doubled word.
