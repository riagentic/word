# Isaiah 23

[1] The burden of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧.

Wail, ships of Tarshish,
for it is laid waste, without house, without entering;
from the land of Kittim it has been revealed to them.

[2] Be silent, inhabitants of the coastland!
The traders of ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧, passing over the sea, have filled you.

[3] And on many waters the seed of ⟦shihor|Shichor|Shihor⟧,
the harvest of the ⟦nile|Yeor|Nile⟧, was her yield,
and she became the trade of nations.

[4] Be ashamed, ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧, for the sea has said,
the strong place of the sea, saying:
"I have not writhed, and I have not given birth,
and I have not reared young men, nor raised up virgins."

[5] As at the report concerning ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
they shall writhe at the report of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧.

[6] Pass over to Tarshish;
wail, inhabitants of the coastland!

[7] Is this your jubilant one?
From the days of old is her antiquity;
her feet shall carry her far off to sojourn.

[8] Who has counseled this against ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, the bestower of crowns,
whose traders are princes,
whose merchants are the honored of the earth?

[9] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ of Hosts has counseled it,
to profane the exaltation of all beauty,
to make light of all the honored of the earth.

[10] Pass over your land like the ⟦nile|Yeor|Nile⟧,
daughter of Tarshish:
there is no girdle any more.

[11] His hand he has stretched out over the sea;
he has made kingdoms tremble;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded concerning ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧
to destroy her strong places.

[12] And he said: "You shall no more again be jubilant,
oppressed one, virgin daughter of ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧!
To Kittim arise, pass over;
even there you shall have no rest."

[13] Behold, the land of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧:
this is the people that was not;
⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ founded it for desert dwellers.
They raised up his siege towers,
they laid bare her palaces;
he made her a ruin.

[14] Wail, ships of Tarshish,
for your strong place is laid waste.

[15] And it shall be in that day that ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ shall be forgotten seventy years, like the days of one king. At the end of seventy years it shall be for ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ like the song of the prostitute:

[16] "Take a lyre,
go about the city,
forgotten prostitute!
Play well,
make much song,
so that you may be remembered."

[17] And it shall be at the end of seventy years that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will visit ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, and she shall return to her hire, and she shall play the prostitute with all the kingdoms of the earth on the face of the ground.

[18] And her trade and her hire shall be holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; it shall not be stored up and shall not be hoarded, for her trade shall be for those who dwell before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for eating to the full and for splendid covering.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v11, 17, 18) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v9).
- **Layout** — v1–14 and v16 are poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line in the Hebrew order (v6 now two lines, at the main accent); v15, 17–18 are prose.
- **"The burden of Tyre"** (v1) — Hebrew מַשָּׂא, "what is lifted up", a load; usually rendered "oracle".
- **"Howl, ye ships of Tarshish" → "Wail, ships of Tarshish"** (v1, 14) — Hebrew הֵילִילוּ; no "ye". **"for it is laid waste, so that there is no house, no entering in" → "for it is laid waste, without house, without entering"** — שֻׁדַּד, pual of שָׁדַד "lay waste" (also v14), with no subject named ("Tyre" was supplied); מִבַּיִת מִבּוֹא, literally "from house, from entering": no house left, no way in (of a harbour or of a home). **"it is revealed to them" → "it has been revealed to them"** — נִגְלָה, a perfect.
- **"Be still, ye inhabitants of the isle" → "Be silent, inhabitants of the coastland"** (v2, 6) — Hebrew דֹּמּוּ; אִי "coastland" (coast or island). **"thou whom the merchants of Zidon, that pass over the sea, have replenished" → "The traders of Sidon, passing over the sea, have filled you"** — סֹחֵר, a singular used of all of them, with a plural verb; עֹבֵר יָם "passing over the sea"; מִלְאוּךְ "have filled you", "you" feminine singular (the coastland, or Tyre).
- **"And by great waters the seed of Sihor, the harvest of the river, is her revenue; and she is a mart of nations" → "And on many waters the seed of Shihor, the harvest of the Nile, was her yield, and she became the trade of nations"** (v3) — Hebrew זֶרַע "seed", קְצִיר "harvest", תְּבוּאָתָהּ "her yield", יְאוֹר the Nile (token); וַתְּהִי, a past form, "and she became"; סְחַר "trade" (the trading place of the nations).
- **"for the sea hath spoken, even the strength of the sea, saying" → "for the sea has said, the strong place of the sea, saying"** (v4) — Hebrew אָמַר; מָעוֹז "strong place" (also v11, 14); לֵאמֹר "saying", kept. **"I travail not, nor bring forth children, neither do I nourish up young men, nor bring up virgins" → "I have not writhed, and I have not given birth, and I have not reared young men, nor raised up virgins"** — four perfects; חַלְתִּי, of חִיל "writhe" (in labor; the same verb "they shall writhe", v5); גִּדַּלְתִּי "reared" (literally "made great"); רוֹמַמְתִּי "raised up" (the second negation is carried over from the first).
- **"As at the report concerning Egypt, so shall they be sorely pained at the report of Tyre" → "As at the report concerning Egypt, they shall writhe at the report of Tyre"** (v5) — Hebrew כַּאֲשֶׁר שֵׁמַע לְמִצְרָיִם יָחִילוּ כְּשֵׁמַע צֹר, no "so"; לְמִצְרָיִם "concerning Egypt" (or "when the report comes to Egypt"); יָחִילוּ "they shall writhe".
- **"Pass ye over to Tarshish; howl, ye inhabitants of the isle" → "Pass over to Tarshish; wail, inhabitants of the coastland"** (v6) — Hebrew עִבְרוּ (עָבַר "pass over", also v10, 12).
- **"Is this your joyous city, whose antiquity is of ancient days? her own feet shall carry her afar off to sojourn" → "Is this your jubilant one? From the days of old is her antiquity; her feet shall carry her far off to sojourn"** (v7) — Hebrew הֲזֹאת לָכֶם עַלִּיזָה, "is this to you a jubilant one": no "city" (as 22:2 "a jubilant town"); מִימֵי־קֶדֶם קַדְמָתָהּ; יֹבִלוּהָ, an imperfect ("shall carry her", or "carried her"); מֵרָחוֹק "far off"; לָגוּר "to sojourn".
- **"Who hath taken this counsel against Tyre, the crowning city" → "Who has counseled this against Tyre, the bestower of crowns"** (v8, 9) — Hebrew יָעַץ "counsel"; הַמַּעֲטִירָה "the one who crowns" (or "the crowned one"). **"whose merchants are princes, whose traffickers are the honourable of the earth" → "whose traders are princes, whose merchants are the honored of the earth"** — סֹחֲרֶיהָ "her traders"; כִּנְעָנֶיהָ "her merchants" (literally "her Canaanites"); נִכְבַּדֵּי־אָרֶץ "the honored of the earth" (nifal of כָּבֵד, root of כָּבוֹד).
- **"to stain the pride of all glory, and to bring into contempt all the honourable of the earth" → "to profane the exaltation of all beauty, to make light of all the honored of the earth"** (v9) — Hebrew לְחַלֵּל "to profane"; גְּאוֹן "exaltation" (also "pride"; 24:14 of Yahweh); צְבִי "beauty"; לְהָקֵל "to make light of".
- **"Pass through thy land as a river, O daughter of Tarshish: there is no more strength" → "Pass over your land like the Nile, daughter of Tarshish: there is no girdle any more"** (v10) — Hebrew כַּיְאֹר "like the Nile"; מֵזַח, found only here, uncertain: "girdle" (as מְזִיחַ, Ps 109:19), or "harbour", or "restraint"; no "O".
- **"He stretched out his hand over the sea, he shook the kingdoms" → "His hand he has stretched out over the sea; he has made kingdoms tremble"** (v11) — Hebrew order kept; הִרְגִּיז, hifil of רָגַז "tremble"; מַמְלָכוֹת without the article. **"the LORD hath given a commandment against the merchant city, to destroy the strong holds thereof" → "Yahweh has commanded concerning Canaan to destroy her strong places"** — אֶל־כְּנַעַן "concerning (or against) Canaan" (the coast of Tyre and Sidon); לַשְׁמִד "to destroy"; מָעֻזְנֶיהָ "her strong places".
- **"Thou shalt no more rejoice, O thou oppressed virgin, daughter of Zidon" → "You shall no more again be jubilant, oppressed one, virgin daughter of Sidon!"** (v12) — Hebrew לֹא־תוֹסִיפִי עוֹד לַעְלוֹז, literally "you shall not add again to be jubilant"; הַמְעֻשָּׁקָה "the oppressed one"; בְּתוּלַת בַּת־צִידוֹן "virgin daughter of Sidon". **"arise, pass over to Chittim; there also shalt thou have no rest" → "To Kittim arise, pass over; even there you shall have no rest"** — the written form כתיים, the qere כִּתִּים: the same name; לֹא־יָנוּחַ לָךְ, literally "it shall not rest for you".
- **"Behold the land of the Chaldeans; this people was not, till the Assyrian founded it for them that dwell in the wilderness" → "Behold, the land of the Chaldeans: this is the people that was not; Assyria founded it for desert dwellers"** (v13) — Hebrew הֵן "behold"; זֶה הָעָם לֹא הָיָה; יְסָדָהּ "founded it" (the land); צִיִּים "desert dwellers" (men or beasts: uncertain). **"they set up the towers thereof, they raised up the palaces thereof; and he brought it to ruin" → "They raised up his siege towers, they laid bare her palaces; he made her a ruin"** — the written form בחיניו, the qere בַּחוּנָיו: both "his siege towers" (uncertain word); עֹרְרוּ "laid bare" (= razed); שָׂמָהּ "he set her", singular. The whole verse is hard: who is "he", and whether Chaldeans or (by a conjecture that changes the word, from memory) Canaanites are meant, is not certain; the Masoretic Text is translated.
- **"your strength is laid waste" → "your strong place is laid waste"** (v14) — מָעֻזְּכֶן, "your" feminine plural (the ships).
- **"And it shall come to pass in that day" → "And it shall be in that day"** (v15, 17) — Hebrew וְהָיָה. **"shall Tyre sing as an harlot" → "it shall be for Tyre like the song of the prostitute"** — יִהְיֶה לְצֹר כְּשִׁירַת הַזּוֹנָה; זוֹנָה "prostitute" (also v16). Tyre is feminine throughout (נִשְׁכַּחַת).
- **"Take an harp" → "Take a lyre"** (v16) — כִּנּוֹר "lyre". **"make sweet melody, sing many songs" → "Play well, make much song"** — הֵיטִיבִי נַגֵּן "do well playing"; הַרְבִּי־שִׁיר "multiply song". **"that thou mayest be remembered"** — לְמַעַן תִּזָּכֵרִי "so that you may be remembered".
- **"the LORD will visit Tyre"** (v17) — פָּקַד, kept. **"she shall turn to her hire, and shall commit fornication with all the kingdoms of the world upon the face of the earth" → "she shall return to her hire, and she shall play the prostitute with all the kingdoms of the earth on the face of the ground"** — אֶתְנַן "hire" (what a prostitute is given); זָנָה "play the prostitute"; הָאָרֶץ "the earth", הָאֲדָמָה "the ground".
- **"her merchandise and her hire shall be holiness to the LORD" → "her trade and her hire shall be holy to Yahweh"** (v18) — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ, the noun "holiness" as a predicate. **"it shall not be treasured nor laid up" → "it shall not be stored up and shall not be hoarded"**. **"for them that dwell before the LORD, to eat sufficiently, and for durable clothing" → "for those who dwell before Yahweh, for eating to the full and for splendid covering"** — לַיֹּשְׁבִים "those who dwell (sit)"; לֶאֱכֹל לְשָׂבְעָה "for eating to the full"; מְכַסֶּה עָתִיק "covering", עָתִיק uncertain: "splendid" or "durable, old".
