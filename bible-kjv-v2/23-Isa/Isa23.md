# Isaiah 23

[1] An oracle concerning ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧.

Wail, O ships of Tarshish,
for ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ is laid waste, without house or harbor!
From the land of Kittim it is revealed to them.

[2] Be still, O inhabitants of the coast;
the merchants of ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧, who cross the sea, have filled you.

[3] And on many waters your revenue was the grain of ⟦shihor|Shichor|Shihor⟧,
the harvest of the ⟦nile|Yeor|Nile⟧;
you were the merchant of the nations.

[4] Be ashamed, O ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧, for the sea has spoken,
the stronghold of the sea, saying:
"I have neither labored nor given birth,
I have neither reared young men nor brought up young women."

[5] When the report comes to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
they will be in anguish over the report about ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧.

[6] Cross over to Tarshish; wail, O inhabitants of the coast!

[7] Is this your exultant city whose origin is from days of old,
whose feet carried her to settle far away?

[8] Who has purposed this against ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, the bestower of crowns,
whose merchants were princes,
whose traders were the honored of the earth?

[9] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ of Hosts has purposed it,
to defile the pride of all glory,
to dishonor all the honored of the earth.

[10] Cross over your land like the ⟦nile|Yeor|Nile⟧,
O daughter of Tarshish;
there is no restraint anymore.

[11] He has stretched out his hand over the sea;
he has shaken the kingdoms;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has given command concerning ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧
to destroy its strongholds.

[12] And he said: "You will no more exult,
O oppressed virgin daughter of ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧;
arise, cross over to Kittim;
even there you will have no rest."

[13] Behold the land of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧!
This is the people that was not;
⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ destined it for wild beasts.
They erected their siege towers,
they stripped her palaces bare,
they made her a ruin.

[14] Wail, O ships of Tarshish,
for your stronghold is laid waste.

[15] In that day ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ will be forgotten for seventy years, like the days of one king. At the end of seventy years, it will happen to ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ as in the song of the prostitute:

[16] "Take a harp;
go about the city,
O forgotten prostitute!
Make sweet melody;
sing many songs,
that you may be remembered."

[17] At the end of seventy years, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will visit ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, and she will return to her wages and will prostitute herself with all the kingdoms of the world on the face of the earth.

[18] Her merchandise and her wages will be holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. It will not be stored or hoarded, but her merchandise will supply abundant food and fine clothing for those who dwell before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (vv11, 17, 18) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v9) — divine title preserved.
- **Poetic structure** — vv1-14, 16 formatted as verse; prose narrative vv15, 17-18 preserved.
