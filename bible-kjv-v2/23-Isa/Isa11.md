# Isaiah 11

[1] And there shall come out a shoot from the stump of ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧,
and a branch from his roots shall be fruitful.

[2] And the Spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall rest upon him,
the spirit of wisdom and understanding,
the spirit of counsel and might,
the spirit of knowledge and of the fear of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] And his smelling shall be in the fear of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
and not by the sight of his eyes shall he judge,
and not by the hearing of his ears shall he decide;

[4] and he shall judge the poor with righteousness,
and decide with equity for the humble of the earth;
and he shall strike the earth with the rod of his mouth,
and with the breath of his lips he shall put the wicked to death.

[5] And righteousness shall be the belt of his waist,
and faithfulness the belt of his loins.

[6] And the wolf shall sojourn with the lamb,
and the leopard shall lie down with the young goat,
and the calf and the young lion and the fatling together,
and a small boy driving them.

[7] And the cow and the bear shall graze,
together their young shall lie down,
and the lion like cattle shall eat straw.

[8] And the nursing child shall delight over the hole of the cobra,
and on the den of the viper the weaned child shall put his hand.

[9] They shall not do evil and they shall not ruin
in all my holy mountain;
for the earth shall be full of the knowledge of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
as the waters cover the sea.

[10] And it shall be in that day: the root of ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧, who stands for a banner of the peoples — him shall the nations seek, and his resting place shall be glory.

[11] And it shall be in that day, ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ will again, a second time, set his hand to get the remnant of his people that remains, from ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ and from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and from ⟦pathros|Patros|Pathros⟧ and from ⟦cush|Kush|Cush⟧ and from Elam and from Shinar and from ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧ and from the coastlands of the sea.

[12] And he shall lift up a banner for the nations,
and shall gather the outcasts of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
and the scattered of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ he shall gather together
from the four corners of the earth.

[13] And the envy of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ shall depart,
and the harassers of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ shall be cut off;
⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ shall not envy ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,
and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ shall not harass ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧.

[14] And they shall fly on the shoulder of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ westward;
together they shall plunder the sons of the east;
Edom and ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ shall be the stretching out of their hand,
and the sons of Ammon their obedience.

[15] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will devote to destruction the tongue of the sea of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and will wave his hand over the River with the scorching of his wind, and will strike it into seven wadis, and will make them tread in sandals.

[16] And there shall be a highway for the remnant of his people that remains, from ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, as there was for ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ in the day he came up from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"the Lord" → "Adonai"** (v11) — אֲדֹנָי.
- **Layout** — v1–9 and v12–14 are verse (one Hebrew line per printed line); v10–11 and v15–16 stay prose.
- **"And there shall come forth a rod out of the stem of Jesse, and a Branch shall grow out of his roots" → "And there shall come out a shoot from the stump of Jesse, and a branch from his roots shall be fruitful"** (v1) — word for word: וְיָצָא "and there shall come out"; חֹטֶר "shoot" (a twig; "rod" in Prov 14:3); גֵּזַע "stump" (as Job 14:8); נֵצֶר "branch" (a green shoot; also 14:19; 60:21; not צֶמַח "sprout" of 4:2, another word); חֹטֶר and נֵצֶר are kept apart from צֶמַח; יִפְרֶה "shall be fruitful" (פָּרָה); the versions read "shall sprout" (יִפְרַח, from memory). No capital: the verse names no one. Matt 2:23 ("Nazarene") is often tied to נֵצֶר; that is a reading of the NT, not of this verse.
- **"the spirit of the LORD" → "the Spirit of Yahweh"** (v2) — רוּחַ יְהוָה; the same word רוּחַ in the next three lines ("the spirit of wisdom …"); the capital marks only the first, where it is Yahweh's. גְּבוּרָה "might"; **"and of the fear of Yahweh"** — the second genitive with "of", as the Hebrew joins it to "spirit".
- **"And shall make him of quick understanding in the fear of the LORD" → "And his smelling shall be in the fear of Yahweh"** (v3) — וַהֲרִיחוֹ, infinitive of הֵרִיחַ "smell", with "his": literally "and his smelling (is) in the fear of Yahweh" (= his delight, as of a soothing smell; KJV and the modern versions give the sense). **"after the sight of his eyes … after the hearing of his ears" → "by the sight of his eyes … by the hearing of his ears"** — מַרְאֵה "sight, appearance", מִשְׁמַע "hearing". **"reprove" → "decide"** (v3, 4) — הוֹכִיחַ of deciding a case (the row: "decide" between parties; "reprove" elsewhere).
- **"but with righteousness shall he judge the poor" → "and he shall judge the poor with righteousness"** (v4) — וְשָׁפַט "and he shall judge" (no "but"); דַּלִּים "poor"; **"for the meek" → "for the humble"** — עֲנָוִים "humble" (עָנָו); בְּמִישׁוֹר "with equity" (literally "with level ground", root of יָשָׁר "upright"). **"the earth"** — אֶרֶץ both times ("land" or "earth"). **"the breath of his lips" kept** — רוּחַ "breath" (the same word as "spirit", v2). **"slay the wicked" → "put the wicked to death"** — יָמִית; רָשָׁע singular ("the wicked one").
- **"the girdle of his loins … the girdle of his reins" → "the belt of his waist … the belt of his loins"** (v5) — אֵזוֹר "belt"; מָתְנַיִם "waist" and חֲלָצַיִם "loins" (elsewhere both are "loins": kept apart here); הָאֱמוּנָה has the article.
- **"The wolf also shall dwell with the lamb" → "And the wolf shall sojourn with the lamb"** (v6) — גָּר (גּוּר "sojourn", as a guest; יָשַׁב is "dwell"). **"the kid" → "the young goat"** — גְּדִי; **"the fatling"** — מְרִיא; **"and a little child shall lead them" → "and a small boy driving them"** — נַעַר קָטֹן "a small boy"; נֹהֵג a participle, "driving" (qal of נָהַג, as of driving cattle, Gen 31:18), no verb "shall".
- **"And the cow and the bear shall feed" → "shall graze"** (v7) — תִּרְעֶינָה, feminine (both are female); **"like the ox" → "like cattle"** — בָּקָר "herd, cattle" (שׁוֹר is "ox"); תֶּבֶן "straw".
- **"And the sucking child shall play on the hole of the asp, and the weaned child shall put his hand on the cockatrice' den" → "And the nursing child shall delight over the hole of the cobra, and on the den of the viper the weaned child shall put his hand"** (v8) — שִׁעֲשַׁע "delight" (as Ps 119:16); פֶּתֶן "cobra" (Ps 91:13), צִפְעֹנִי "viper" (Prov 23:32), both kinds uncertain; מְאוּרַת "den" (only here; uncertain, perhaps "light-hole"); הָדָה "put, stretch out" (only here). Hebrew order kept.
- **"They shall not hurt nor destroy" → "They shall not do evil and they shall not ruin"** (v9) — יָרֵעוּ (הֵרַע "do evil"), יַשְׁחִיתוּ (הִשְׁחִית "ruin"); "and" each time. **"for the earth shall be full"** — מָלְאָה, a perfect. **"the knowledge of the LORD"** — דֵּעָה אֶת־יְהוָה, literally "knowing Yahweh" (the verbal noun with an object). **"as the waters cover the sea"** — כַּמַּיִם לַיָּם מְכַסִּים, literally "as the waters, for the sea, covering".
- **"And in that day there shall be a root of Jesse, which shall stand for an ensign of the people; to it shall the Gentiles seek: and his rest shall be glorious" → "And it shall be in that day: the root of Jesse, who stands for a banner of the peoples — him shall the nations seek, and his resting place shall be glory"** (v10) — וְהָיָה בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא (also v11); עֹמֵד a participle, "stands"; נֵס "banner"; עַמִּים plural; אֵלָיו גּוֹיִם יִדְרֹשׁוּ, literally "to him nations shall seek" (דָּרַשׁ אֶל "seek to"); מְנֻחָתוֹ "his resting place"; כָּבוֹד "glory", a noun. Rom 15:12 quotes the Greek ("he who arises to rule the nations; in him the nations shall hope"), which differs; the Hebrew stands here.
- **"the Lord shall set his hand again the second time to recover the remnant of his people, which shall be left" → "Adonai will again, a second time, set his hand to get the remnant of his people that remains"** (v11) — יוֹסִיף … שֵׁנִית יָדוֹ, literally "will add, a second time, his hand": יָסַף "again" (its row), "set" supplied; לִקְנוֹת "to get" (קָנָה "get, buy"); שְׁאָר "remnant" (as 10:19–22). **"from Assyria, and from Egypt …"** — וּ "and from" before each name, kept; אִיֵּי הַיָּם "the coastlands of the sea".
- **"And he shall set up an ensign for the nations, and shall assemble the outcasts of Israel, and gather together the dispersed of Judah" → "And he shall lift up a banner for the nations, and shall gather the outcasts of Israel, and the scattered of Judah he shall gather together"** (v12) — נָשָׂא "lift up"; אָסַף "gather" and קִבֵּץ "gather together" (two verbs); נִדְחֵי "the outcasts" (literally "those driven away"); נְפֻצוֹת "the scattered" (feminine); Hebrew order. **"the four corners of the earth"** — כַּנְפוֹת, literally "wings".
- **"The envy also of Ephraim shall depart" → "And the envy of Ephraim shall depart"** (v13) — קִנְאָה / קִנֵּא of rivals: "envy" (the row); סוּר "depart"; צֹרְרֵי "the harassers" and יָצֹר "harass" (צָרַר, one verb; the earlier text had "harass" too); **"shall be cut off"** — יִכָּרֵתוּ.
- **"But they shall fly upon the shoulders of the Philistines toward the west" → "And they shall fly on the shoulder of the Philistines westward"** (v14) — וְעָפוּ "and they shall fly" (עוּף); כָתֵף "shoulder" (singular; the slope of their land); יָמָּה "toward the sea" = westward. **"they shall spoil them of the east" → "they shall plunder the sons of the east"** — בְּנֵי־קֶדֶם. **"they shall lay their hand upon Edom and Moab; and the children of Ammon shall obey them" → "Edom and Moab shall be the stretching out of their hand, and the sons of Ammon their obedience"** — מִשְׁלוֹחַ יָדָם, מִשְׁמַעְתָּם, two nouns, no verb ("shall be" supplied once); בְּנֵי עַמּוֹן "the sons of Ammon" (the earlier `ammonites` token gives way to the plain name, as Judges).
- **"And the LORD shall utterly destroy the tongue of the Egyptian sea" → "And Yahweh will devote to destruction the tongue of the sea of Egypt"** (v15) — הֶחֱרִים (חָרַם; many read הֶחֱרִיב "dry up", a change of one letter, from memory); יָם־מִצְרַיִם "the sea of Egypt". **"with his mighty wind shall he shake his hand over the river" → "will wave his hand over the River with the scorching of his wind"** — הֵנִיף "wave"; הַנָּהָר "the River" (the Euphrates, as "across the River"); בַּעְיָם only here, uncertain ("scorching", "might"); רוּחַ "wind". **"and shall smite it in the seven streams, and make men go over dryshod" → "and will strike it into seven wadis, and will make them tread in sandals"** — נְחָלִים "wadis"; הִדְרִיךְ "make tread" with no object written ("them" supplied); בַּנְּעָלִים "in sandals".
- **"And there shall be an highway for the remnant of his people, which shall be left, from Assyria" kept in the Hebrew order** (v16) — the earlier text put "from Assyria" before "the remnant". **"in the day that he came up" → "in the day he came up"** — בְּיוֹם עֲלֹתוֹ, "his coming up" (singular: Israel).

### Notes
- **v1, v10** — "shoot", "branch", "root" of Jesse: the line of David cut down to a stump.
- **v2** — Christian tradition counts here a sevenfold spirit (with the Greek "godliness"); the Hebrew has six nouns after "the Spirit of Yahweh".
- **v6–9** — the peace of the wild beasts with the tame; v9 recurs in Hab 2:14 in another form.
- **v10** — quoted in Rom 15:12 from a Greek form of the text.
