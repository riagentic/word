# Isaiah 56

[1] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"Keep judgment, and do righteousness,
for my salvation is near to come,
and my righteousness to be revealed.

[2] Happy is the mortal man who does this,
and the son of man who holds fast to it,
who keeps ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|the Sabbath⟧ from profaning it,
and keeps his hand from doing any evil."

[3] And let not the foreigner who has been joined to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ say, saying,
"⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will surely separate me from his people";
and let not the eunuch say,
"Behold, I am a dry tree."

[4] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"To the eunuchs who keep my ⟦shabbat|Shabbats|Sabbaths⟧,
and choose that in which I delight,
and hold fast my covenant,

[5] I will give to them in my house and within my walls
a monument and a name,
better than sons and than daughters;
an everlasting name I will give to him,
that shall not be cut off.

[6] And the foreigners who are joined to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
to minister to him, and to love the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
to be his servants,
everyone who keeps ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|the Sabbath⟧ from profaning it,
and who hold fast my covenant —

[7] these I will bring to my holy mountain,
and make them glad in my house of prayer;
their burnt offerings and their sacrifices
shall be found pleasing on my altar;
for my house shall be called
a house of prayer for all the peoples."

[8] Declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, who gathers the driven-away of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: "Yet I will gather to him, to his gathered ones."

[9] All the living things of the field,
come to devour, all the living things in the forest.

[10] His watchmen are blind, all of them;
they do not know;
all of them are mute dogs; they cannot bark;
dreaming, lying down, loving to slumber.

[11] And the dogs are strong of soul;
they do not know satiety.
And they are shepherds; they do not know how to understand;
all of them have turned to their way,
each man to his unjust gain, from his end.

[12] "Come, let me take wine,
and let us drink deep of strong drink;
and tomorrow shall be like this,
great, exceedingly abundant."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v8) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה: both names, *Adonai* and the Tetragrammaton (token `adonai-yhwh`).
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v9 now breaks at the athnach (after "of the field"); vv3 and 8 keep their prose layout.
- **"Keep ye judgment, and do justice: for my salvation is near to come" → "Keep judgment, and do righteousness, for my salvation is near to come"** (v1) — Hebrew שִׁמְרוּ מִשְׁפָּט וַעֲשׂוּ צְדָקָה: מִשְׁפָּט "judgment", צְדָקָה "righteousness" (the same word as "my righteousness" at the end of the verse); קְרוֹבָה … לָבוֹא "near to come" (the earlier "soon" lost "near").
- **"Blessed is the man that doeth this, and the son of man that layeth hold on it" → "Happy is the mortal man who does this, and the son of man who holds fast to it"** (v2) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי "happy" (not בָּרוּךְ "blessed"); אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man", beside בֶּן־אָדָם "son of man" in the parallel line; יַחֲזִיק בָּהּ "holds fast to it". **"keepeth the sabbath from polluting it" → "keeps Shabbat from profaning it"** — שֹׁמֵר שַׁבָּת מֵחַלְּלוֹ, no article on שַׁבָּת; חִלֵּל "profane"; the participle שֹׁמֵר "keeping" stands twice ("keeps … and keeps his hand").
- **"the son of the stranger, that hath joined himself to the LORD" → "the foreigner who has been joined to Yahweh"** (v3, 6) — Hebrew בֶּן־הַנֵּכָר, literally "the son of foreignness"; בְּנֵי הַנֵּכָר "the foreigners" (v6). הַנִּלְוָה is nifal, "who has been joined" (v6 הַנִּלְוִים "who are joined"; v3 with אֶל, v6 with עַל). **"speak, saying" → "say, saying"** — יֹאמַר … לֵאמֹר, the same verb both times.
- **"The LORD hath utterly separated me from his people" → "Yahweh will surely separate me from his people"** (v3) — Hebrew הַבְדֵּל יַבְדִּילַנִי: infinitive absolute with the imperfect, literally "separating he will separate me"; מֵעַל עַמּוֹ literally "from upon his people". **"eunuch"** — סָרִיס, a castrated man or a court officer; here the "dry tree" and the sons and daughters of v5 show that the man without offspring is meant.
- **"choose the things that please me" → "choose that in which I delight"** (v4) — Hebrew וּבָחֲרוּ בַּאֲשֶׁר חָפָצְתִּי: חָפֵץ "delight in"; the verbs are joined by וְ "and", not new relative clauses. **"Sabbaths" → "Shabbats"** — שַׁבְּתוֹתַי, plural.
- **"a place and a name" → "a monument and a name"** (v5) — Hebrew יָד וָשֵׁם, literally "a hand and a name": יָד of a memorial stone, as 2 Sam 18:18 ("the Monument of Absalom"); *yad va-shem*. **"better than of sons and of daughters" → "better than sons and than daughters"** — מִבָּנִים וּמִבָּנוֹת, מִן twice. **"I will give them an everlasting name" → "an everlasting name I will give to him"** — שֵׁם עוֹלָם אֶתֶּן־לוֹ: the Hebrew changes to the singular, "to him" (each one of them), and puts the object first; עוֹלָם as a genitive, literally "a name of the age".
- **"to serve him … to be his servants" → "to minister to him … to be his servants"** (v6) — Hebrew לְשָׁרְתוֹ, שֵׁרֵת "minister to"; לִהְיוֹת לוֹ לַעֲבָדִים, literally "to be to him for slaves": עֶבֶד is "slave", of Yahweh's men "servant". **"every one that keepeth the sabbath from polluting it, and taketh hold of my covenant"** — the participle שֹׁמֵר is singular, וּמַחֲזִיקִים "and who hold fast" plural, as the Hebrew.
- **"make them joyful" → "make them glad"** (v7) — Hebrew שִׂמַּחְתִּים, piel of שָׂמַח. **"my holy mountain"** — הַר קָדְשִׁי, literally "the mountain of my holiness". **"shall be accepted upon mine altar" → "shall be found pleasing on my altar"** — עוֹלֹתֵיהֶם וְזִבְחֵיהֶם לְרָצוֹן עַל־מִזְבְּחִי has no verb: literally "their burnt offerings and their sacrifices, for good pleasure on my altar"; "shall be found pleasing" as Lev 22:20, 21 (לְרָצוֹן).
- **"an house of prayer for all people" → "a house of prayer for all the peoples"** (v7) — Hebrew בֵּית־תְּפִלָּה יִקָּרֵא לְכָל־הָעַמִּים: עַמִּים "peoples", with the article; the Hebrew order is "for my house, a house of prayer it shall be called, for all the peoples". Mark 11:17 quotes the Greek, πᾶσιν τοῖς ἔθνεσιν "for all the nations"; the verse follows the Hebrew.
- **"The Lord GOD which gathereth the outcasts of Israel saith" → "Declares Lord Yahweh, who gathers the driven-away of Israel"** (v8) — Hebrew נְאֻם "declaration" (the formula "declares", one wording through the prophets) stands first; נִדְחֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל, nifal of נָדַח "drive away", as Deut 30:4 "your driven-away one". **"Yet will I gather others to him, beside those that are gathered unto him" → "Yet I will gather to him, to his gathered ones"** — עוֹד אֲקַבֵּץ עָלָיו לְנִקְבָּצָיו: the Hebrew has no "others" and no "besides"; literally "yet I will gather upon him, to his gathered ones" (= in addition to those already gathered).
- **"All ye beasts of the field, come to devour, yea, all ye beasts in the forest" → "All the living things of the field, come to devour, all the living things in the forest"** (v9) — Hebrew כֹּל חַיְתוֹ שָׂדָי … כָּל־חַיְתוֹ בַּיָּעַר: חַיָּה "living thing" (in the old construct form חַיְתוֹ, the poetic שָׂדָי "field"); no "ye", no "yea".
- **"His watchmen are blind: they are all ignorant" → "His watchmen are blind, all of them; they do not know"** (v10) — Hebrew צֹפָיו עִוְרִים כֻּלָּם לֹא יָדָעוּ: the qere צֹפָיו "his watchmen" is translated; the written form צפו is the same word in an older spelling. "all of them" may also go with "do not know". **"dumb dogs" → "mute dogs"** — אִלְּמִים. **"sleeping, lying down" → "dreaming, lying down"** — הֹזִים, found only here: the sense is uncertain ("dreaming", or "panting, raving").
- **"they are greedy dogs which can never have enough" → "the dogs are strong of soul; they do not know satiety"** (v11) — Hebrew וְהַכְּלָבִים עַזֵּי־נֶפֶשׁ, literally "strong of soul" (= of fierce appetite); לֹא יָדְעוּ שָׂבְעָה "they do not know satiety" (root שָׂבַע "be sated"). **"shepherds that cannot understand" → "they are shepherds; they do not know how to understand"** — וְהֵמָּה רֹעִים לֹא יָדְעוּ הָבִין, the same "they do not know" as in the line before. **"they all look to their own way, every one for his gain, from his quarter" → "all of them have turned to their way, each man to his unjust gain, from his end"** — פָּנוּ perfect "have turned"; no "own"; בֶּצַע "unjust gain"; מִקָּצֵהוּ "from his end", as Gen 19:4 מִקָּצֶה "from the end" (= every last one).
- **"Come ye, say they, I will fetch wine" → "Come, let me take wine"** (v12) — Hebrew אֵתָיוּ אֶקְחָה־יַיִן: the speaker is not named ("they say" was supplied); אֶקְחָה cohortative, "let me take". **"we will fill ourselves with strong drink" → "let us drink deep of strong drink"** — וְנִסְבְּאָה, סָבָא "drink heavily" (the word of "drunkards" in Prov 23:20; שָׁכַר "be drunk" is another verb). **"to morrow shall be as this day, and much more abundant" → "tomorrow shall be like this, great, exceedingly abundant"** — וְהָיָה כָזֶה יוֹם מָחָר גָּדוֹל יֶתֶר מְאֹד: literally "and like this shall be the day of tomorrow, great, excess, very"; יֶתֶר "what is over".

### Notes
- **v7** — "house of prayer for all the peoples": quoted by Yeshua (Matt 21:13; Mark 11:17; Luke 19:46).
- **"monument and a name"** (v5) — Hebrew יָד וָשֵׁם *yad va-shem*; now the name of Israel's Holocaust memorial.
