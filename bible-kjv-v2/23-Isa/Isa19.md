## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (vv4, 12, 17, 18, 20, 25) — divine title preserved throughout.
- **"Lord" / "Lord GOD" → "Lord, Yahweh of Hosts"** (v4) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת; full triple title preserved.
- **"God" / "LORD" combined → "Yahweh"** — all instances of divine names consistently rendered.
- **Poetic structure** — vv1-15 formatted as verse oracle; vv16-25 prose fulfillment narrative preserved.
