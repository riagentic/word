# Isaiah 31

[1] Woe to those who go down to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ for help,
and on horses they lean,
and they trust in chariots because they are many,
and in horsemen because they are very mighty,
and they have not looked to the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ they have not sought!

[2] And he too is wise, and he brings evil,
and his words he has not removed;
and he will arise against the house of evildoers,
and against the help of the workers of wickedness.

[3] And ⟦egyptians|Mitzrim|the Egyptians⟧ are man, and not ⟦el|el|god⟧,
and their horses flesh, and not spirit;
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will stretch out his hand,
and the helper shall stumble, and the helped shall fall,
and together all of them shall come to an end.

[4] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to me:

"As the lion growls, and the young lion, over his prey,
when the fullness of shepherds is called out against him,
at their voice he is not dismayed,
and at their multitude he is not bowed low,
so ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts will come down
to wage war upon Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ and upon its hill.

[5] Like birds flying, so ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts
will shield ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧;
shielding, and he will deliver;
passing over, and he will rescue."

[6] Turn back to him from whom the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ have gone deep in defection.

[7] For in that day they shall reject, each man, his worthless idols of silver and his worthless idols of gold, which your hands have made for you, a sin.

[8] And ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ shall fall by a sword, not of a man,
and a sword, not of mankind, shall devour him;
and he shall flee away from the face of the sword,
and his young men shall become forced labor.

[9] And his crag shall pass on from terror,
and his princes shall be dismayed at the banner,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, whose fire is in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
and whose oven is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת.
- **"God" → "el"** (v3) — Hebrew אֵל, set against אָדָם "man": "a god", with a small letter, as "another el" (Exod 34:14).
- **Layout** — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v6–7 stay prose.
- **"Woe to them that go down to Egypt for help; and stay on horses" → "Woe to those who go down to Egypt for help, and on horses they lean"** (v1) — Hebrew הוֹי + participle, "to those who" supplied; then finite verbs, each with וְ "and", in the Hebrew order: יִשָּׁעֵנוּ "they lean" (נִשְׁעַן), וַיִּבְטְחוּ "and they trust". **"chariots"** — רֶכֶב, a collective singular ("because it is much": כִּי רָב). **"because they are very strong" → "because they are very mighty"** — עָצְמוּ. **"they look not unto the Holy One of Israel, neither seek the LORD" → "they have not looked to the Holy One of Israel, and Yahweh they have not sought"** — perfects; שָׁעָה עַל "look to"; דָּרַשׁ "seek" (not "consult").
- **"Yet he also is wise, and will bring evil" → "And he too is wise, and he brings evil"** (v2) — וְגַם־הוּא; וַיָּבֵא; רַע "evil" (not "disaster"). **"will not call back his words" → "his words he has not removed"** — הֵסִיר, perfect. **"the house of the evildoers"** — מְרֵעִים, no article. **"the help of them that work iniquity" → "the help of the workers of wickedness"** — עֶזְרַת פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן: עֶזְרָה "help" (the earlier "helpers" is not the word); אָוֶן "wickedness".
- **"Now the Egyptians are men, and not God" → "And the Egyptians are man, and not el"** (v3) — Hebrew וּמִצְרַיִם אָדָם וְלֹא־אֵל: "Egypt", with plural suffixes after it ("their horses"), so the token `egyptians`; אָדָם "man" (mankind), singular. **"When the LORD shall stretch out his hand" → "and Yahweh will stretch out his hand"** — וַיהוָה יַטֶּה יָדוֹ. **"he that helpeth shall fall, and he that is holpen shall fall down" → "the helper shall stumble, and the helped shall fall"** — כָּשַׁל "stumble", נָפַל "fall". **"they all shall fail together" → "together all of them shall come to an end"** — יִכְלָיוּן, of כָּלָה "come to an end".
- **"For thus hath the LORD spoken unto me" → "For thus says Yahweh to me"** (v4) — כֹּה אָמַר יְהוָה, the one wording, here with אֵלַי "to me". **"Like as the lion and the young lion roaring on his prey" → "As the lion growls, and the young lion, over his prey"** — יֶהְגֶּה, הָגָה of an animal "growl"; כְּפִיר "young lion". **"a multitude of shepherds" → "the fullness of shepherds"** — מְלֹא רֹעִים, "fullness" (a full company). **"he will not be afraid of their voice" → "at their voice he is not dismayed"** — חָתַת "be dismayed". **"nor abase himself for the noise of them" → "at their multitude he is not bowed low"** — הָמוֹן "multitude"; יַעֲנֶה, of עָנָה "be bowed low". **"to fight for mount Zion, and for the hill thereof" → "to wage war upon Mount Zion and upon its hill"** — Hebrew לִצְבֹּא עַל, the verb of צָבָא "host" (the play on "of Hosts"); עַל may be "upon" (for its defence, as v5) or "against" (as 29:7, 8); the Hebrew does not decide.
- **"As birds flying" kept as "Like birds flying"** (v5) — צִפֳּרִים עָפוֹת (the earlier "hovering" was a gloss). **"defend" → "shield"** — יָגֵן, of גָּנַן (root of מָגֵן "shield"). **"defending also he will deliver it; and passing over he will preserve it" → "shielding, and he will deliver; passing over, and he will rescue"** — Hebrew גָּנוֹן וְהִצִּיל פָּסֹחַ וְהִמְלִיט: two infinitives absolute, each followed by a verb with וְ; "it" is not written. פָּסַח "pass over", the verb of *pesach* (Exod 12:13, 23, 27); here it may mean "spare" or "stand guard over"; the one word kept. הִצִּיל "deliver"; הִמְלִיט "rescue".
- **"Turn ye unto him from whom the children of Israel have deeply revolted" → "Turn back to him from whom the sons of Israel have gone deep in defection"** (v6) — Hebrew שׁוּבוּ לַאֲשֶׁר הֶעְמִיקוּ סָרָה בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל: הֶעְמִיק "go deep" (as 29:15); סָרָה "defection" (of סוּר "turn aside"); בְּנֵי "sons". "the sons of Israel" may be the subject of "have gone deep" (so the verse) or an address: "Turn back, sons of Israel, to him from whom they have gone deep in defection"; the earlier text took the second and added "O".
- **"every man shall cast away his idols of silver, and his idols of gold" → "they shall reject, each man, his worthless idols of silver and his worthless idols of gold"** (v7) — Hebrew יִמְאָסוּן אִישׁ: מָאַס "reject"; אֱלִילִים "worthless idols". **"which your own hands have made unto you for a sin" → "which your hands have made for you, a sin"** — חֵטְא, a noun standing alone at the end (the earlier "sinfully" was an adverb the Hebrew does not have).
- **"Then shall the Assyrian fall with the sword, not of a mighty man; and the sword, not of a mean man, shall devour him" → "And Assyria shall fall by a sword, not of a man, and a sword, not of mankind, shall devour him"** (v8) — Hebrew לֹא־אִישׁ … לֹא־אָדָם: אִישׁ "man", אָדָם "mankind", kept apart. **"but he shall flee from the sword" → "and he shall flee away from the face of the sword"** — Hebrew וְנָס לוֹ, literally "he shall flee for himself" ("away" stands for לוֹ); the reading לֹא "not" ("he shall not flee from the sword") is named by some (from memory); the MT kept. **"his young men shall be discomfited" → "his young men shall become forced labor"** — לָמַס יִהְיוּ; מַס "forced labor"; בָּחוּר "young man".
- **"And he shall pass over to his strong hold for fear" → "And his crag shall pass on from terror"** (v9) — Hebrew וְסַלְעוֹ מִמָּגוֹר יַעֲבוֹר: סֶלַע "crag" (his rock, perhaps his king), the subject; מָגוֹר "terror"; עָבַר "pass on". **"his princes shall be afraid of the ensign" → "his princes shall be dismayed at the banner"** — חָתַת "be dismayed"; נֵס "banner"; שַׂר "prince". **"saith the LORD" → "declares Yahweh"** — נְאֻם יְהוָה. **"his furnace" → "whose oven"** — תַּנּוּר "oven"; אוּר "fire".
