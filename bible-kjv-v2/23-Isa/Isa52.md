# Isaiah 52

[1] Awake, awake,
put on your strength, O ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧;
put on your beautiful garments,
O ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, the holy city;
for there shall no more come into you
the uncircumcised and the unclean.

[2] Shake yourself from the dust and arise;
be seated, O ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧;
loose the bonds from your neck,
O captive daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

[3] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: "You were sold for nothing, and you shall be redeemed without money."

[4] For thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: "My people went down at the first into ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ to sojourn there, and ⟦assyria|Ashur|the Assyrian⟧ oppressed them for nothing.

[5] Now therefore what have I here," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "seeing that my people are taken away for nothing? Their rulers wail," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "and continually all the day my name is despised.

[6] Therefore my people shall know my name. Therefore in that day they shall know that it is I who speak; here I am."

[7] How beautiful upon the mountains
are the feet of him who brings good news,
who publishes peace,
who brings good news of happiness,
who publishes salvation,
who says to ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, "Your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ reigns."

[8] The voice of your watchmen — they lift up their voice;
together they sing for joy;
for eye to eye they see
the return of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

[9] Break forth together into singing,
you waste places of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has comforted his people;
he has redeemed ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[10] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has bared his holy arm
before the eyes of all the nations,
and all the ends of the earth shall see
the salvation of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[11] Depart, depart, go out from there;
touch no unclean thing;
go out from the midst of her; purify yourselves,
you who bear the vessels of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] For you shall not go out in haste,
and you shall not go in flight,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will go before you,
and the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ will be your rear guard.

[13] Behold, my servant shall act wisely;
he shall be high and lifted up,
and shall be exalted.

[14] As many were astonished at you —
his appearance was so marred, beyond human semblance,
and his form beyond that of the children of mankind —

[15] so shall he sprinkle many nations;
kings shall shut their mouths because of him;
for that which has not been told them they see,
and that which they have not heard they understand.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (vv4, 5) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוָה preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv7, 10, 12) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **Fourth Servant Song opening (vv13-15)** — formatted as verse; transition from Zion-poem (vv1-12) to Servant Song marked structurally.
- **"sprinkle" / "startle" — kept** (v15) — Hebrew יַזֶּה *yazzeh*; debated: "sprinkle" (cultic sprinkling, LXX, Vulg.) vs. "startle" (context of astonishment); "sprinkle" kept as the more defensible reading given the *asham* of 53:10.
- **Poetic structure** — vv1-2 (Zion awakening), vv3-6 (prose oracle), vv7-10 (verse herald), vv11-12 (prose), vv13-15 (Servant Song opening verse).

### Notes
- **v7** — "how beautiful are the feet": quoted Rom 10:15.
- **v11** — "depart, depart": quoted 2 Cor 6:17.
- **v13-15** — opens the climactic **Fourth Servant Song** (52:13–53:12).
