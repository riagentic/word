# Isaiah 52

[1] Awake, awake,
put on your strength, ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧;
put on the garments of your beauty,
⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, the holy city;
for there shall no more again come into you
the uncircumcised and the unclean.

[2] Shake yourself from the dust;
arise, sit, ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧;
loose the bonds of your neck,
captive daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

[3] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: "For nothing you were sold, and not with money shall you be redeemed."

[4] For thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: "To ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ my people went down at the first to sojourn there, and ⟦assyria|Ashur|the Assyrian⟧ extorted from him without cause.

[5] And now, what is there for me here," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "that my people has been taken for nothing? His rulers wail," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "and continually, all the day, my name is spurned.

[6] Therefore my people shall know my name; therefore, in that day, that I am he who speaks: here I am."

[7] How beautiful upon the mountains
are the feet of one who brings news,
who makes ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ heard,
who brings news of good,
who makes salvation heard,
who says to ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, "Your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ reigns."

[8] The voice of your watchmen! They have lifted up a voice;
together they shout for joy;
for eye to eye they shall see
when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ returns to ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

[9] Break forth, shout for joy together,
ruins of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has comforted his people;
he has redeemed ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[10] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has bared his holy arm
before the eyes of all the nations,
and all the ends of the earth shall see
the salvation of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[11] Turn aside, turn aside, go out from there;
touch nothing unclean;
go out from the midst of her; purify yourselves,
you who carry the vessels of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] For not in haste shall you go out,
and in flight you shall not go,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ goes before you,
and your rear guard is the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[13] Behold, my servant shall act with insight;
he shall be exalted, and lifted up, and be very high.

[14] As many were appalled at you —
so ruined from a man was his appearance,
and his form from the sons of man —

[15] so shall he sprinkle many nations;
at him kings shall shut their mouth;
for what had not been recounted to them they have seen,
and what they had not heard they have understood.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v4) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v7, 10, 12) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v3–6 stay prose. v2 breaks after "dust" (the former break after "arise" fell inside a joined phrase); v13 is two lines, as the accents divide it.
- **"O Zion" → "Zion"** (v1, 2) — the Hebrew has no word for "O": the vocative is bare.
- **"put on thy strength" → "put on your strength"** (v1) — Hebrew עֻזֵּךְ, עֹז "strength". **"thy beautiful garments" → "the garments of your beauty"** — בִּגְדֵי תִפְאַרְתֵּךְ, תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty" (a noun, not an adjective; the reviewer row). **"the holy city"** — עִיר הַקֹּדֶשׁ, literally "the city of holiness". **"for henceforth there shall no more come into thee" → "for there shall no more again come into you"** — לֹא יוֹסִיף יָבֹא … עוֹד, literally "he shall not add to come … again". "The uncircumcised and the unclean" — singulars without the article in the Hebrew (עָרֵל וְטָמֵא), each standing for its kind.
- **"arise, and sit down" → "arise, sit"** (v2) — Hebrew קוּמִי שְּׁבִי, two imperatives, no "and"; שְׁבִי from יָשַׁב "sit" (to sit enthroned, after the dust). **"loose thyself from the bands of thy neck" → "loose the bonds of your neck"** — the qere הִתְפַּתְּחִי "loose (for yourself)" is translated; the written form (ketiv) הִתְפַּתְּחוּ reads "the bonds of your neck have come loose". "From" is not in the Hebrew. **"captive daughter of Zion"** — שְׁבִיָּה בַּת־צִיּוֹן, "captive one, daughter of Zion".
- **"Ye have sold yourselves for nought; and ye shall be redeemed without money" → "For nothing you were sold, and not with money shall you be redeemed"** (v3) — Hebrew חִנָּם נִמְכַּרְתֶּם, a passive, "for nothing" first; וְלֹא בְכֶסֶף, literally "and not with silver" (כֶּסֶף is "silver"; "money" where a payment is meant).
- **"My people went down aforetime into Egypt" → "To Egypt my people went down at the first"** (v4) — Hebrew order kept; בָּרִאשֹׁנָה "at the first". **"the Assyrian oppressed them without cause" → "Ashur extorted from him without cause"** — וְאַשּׁוּר בְּאֶפֶס עֲשָׁקוֹ: עָשַׁק "extort" (of a person "extort from", as Lev 6:2–4; לָחַץ is "oppress"); "him" is "my people" (singular in the Hebrew); בְּאֶפֶס, literally "for nothing / at no cost", is a second word beside חִנָּם (v3, 5): "without cause" keeps the two apart.
- **"Now therefore, what have I here" → "And now, what is there for me here"** (v5) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה מַה־לִּי־פֹה, the qere מַה־לִּי (written מי־לי, "who is for me"). **"that my people is taken away for nought" → "that my people has been taken for nothing"** — לֻקַּח, pual of לָקַח "take"; "away" is not in the Hebrew. **"they that rule over them make them to howl" → "His rulers wail"** — the qere מֹשְׁלָיו "his rulers" (written משלו, "his ruler"); יְהֵילִילוּ, hifil of יָלַל "wail" (KJV makes it causative); "his" is "my people". **"my name continually every day is blasphemed" → "continually, all the day, my name is spurned"** — מִנֹּאָץ, an unusual form of נִאֵץ "spurn".
- **"therefore they shall know in that day that I am he that doth speak: behold, it is I" → "therefore, in that day, that I am he who speaks: here I am"** (v6) — the second לָכֵן has no verb: "shall know" of the first clause carries on. הִנֵּנִי, literally "behold me", the answer "Here I am".
- **"How beautiful … are the feet of him that bringeth good tidings, that publisheth peace; that bringeth good tidings of good, that publisheth salvation" → "How beautiful … are the feet of one who brings news, who makes shalom heard, who brings news of good, who makes salvation heard"** (v7) — Hebrew נָאווּ "are lovely"; מְבַשֵּׂר "one bringing news" (as 1 Chr 16:23 "bring the news"; that the news is good is said by טוֹב, "of good"); מַשְׁמִיעַ, hifil of שָׁמַע, "making heard" (KJV "publisheth"); שָׁלוֹם *shalom*. **"Thy God reigneth" → "Your Elohim reigns"** — מָלַךְ, a perfect: "has become king". Rom 10:15 quotes the line in a shorter Greek form ("How beautiful are the feet of those who bring good news of good things"); the Hebrew is translated as it stands.
- **"Thy watchmen shall lift up the voice; with the voice together shall they sing" → "The voice of your watchmen! They have lifted up a voice; together they shout for joy"** (v8) — Hebrew קוֹל צֹפַיִךְ, an exclamation; נָשְׂאוּ, a perfect; רִנֵּן "shout for joy" (as 1 Chr 16:33). **"for they shall see eye to eye, when the LORD shall bring again Zion" → "for eye to eye they shall see when Yahweh returns to Zion"** — עַיִן בְּעַיִן, literally "eye in eye"; בְּשׁוּב יְהוָה צִיּוֹן, "in Yahweh's returning (to) Zion"; the Hebrew may also mean "when Yahweh brings back Zion". The former "the return of Yahweh" made it the object of "see".
- **"Break forth into joy, sing together, ye waste places" → "Break forth, shout for joy together, ruins of Jerusalem"** (v9) — Hebrew פִּצְחוּ רַנְּנוּ, two imperatives; חָרְבוֹת "ruins".
- **"his holy arm"** (v10) — Hebrew זְרוֹעַ קָדְשׁוֹ, literally "the arm of his holiness". **"in the eyes of all the nations" → "before the eyes of"** — לְעֵינֵי.
- **"Depart ye, depart ye" → "Turn aside, turn aside"** (v11) — Hebrew סוּרוּ סוּרוּ (סוּר "turn aside"). **"touch no unclean thing" → "touch nothing unclean"** — טָמֵא, the adjective alone. **"be ye clean" → "purify yourselves"** — הִבָּרוּ, nifal of בָּרַר "be pure". **"that bear the vessels" → "who carry the vessels"** — נֹשְׂאֵי כְּלֵי יְהוָה. 2 Cor 6:17 quotes the verse freely.
- **"For ye shall not go out with haste, nor go by flight" → "For not in haste shall you go out, and in flight you shall not go"** (v12) — Hebrew order and "and" kept; חִפָּזוֹן "haste" (as Exod 12:11). **"will go before you … will be your rereward" → "goes before you, and your rear guard is"** — הֹלֵךְ, a participle; מְאַסִּפְכֶם, literally "your gatherer", the troop that brings up the rear.
- **"my servant shall deal prudently" → "my servant shall act with insight"** (v13) — Hebrew יַשְׂכִּיל, hifil of שָׂכַל "have insight" (the same verb is "prosper" by its result, Josh 1:8): one word, its range named. **"he shall be exalted and extolled, and be very high" → "he shall be exalted, and lifted up, and be very high"** — three verbs: יָרוּם (רוּם "be high": "exalted" to keep it apart from the third), וְנִשָּׂא (נָשָׂא "lift up"), וְגָבַהּ מְאֹד (גָּבַהּ "be high", as 55:9). The pair רָם וְנִשָּׂא stands of Adonai in 6:1.
- **"As many were astonied at thee" → "As many were appalled at you"** (v14) — Hebrew שָׁמְמוּ, שָׁמֵם "be desolate", of persons "be appalled". עָלֶיךָ "at you" is the MT; the Syriac and the Targum read "at him" (from memory); the MT is kept. **"his visage was so marred more than any man, and his form more than the sons of men" → "so ruined from a man was his appearance, and his form from the sons of man"** — מִשְׁחַת, a noun found only here, from שָׁחַת "ruin" ("a ruin of"); מִן "from" may be comparative ("more than a man") or separating ("away from a man": no longer like a man): both are left open. מַרְאֶה "appearance", תֹּאַר "form"; בְּנֵי אָדָם "sons of man". The sentence is broken off (as in the Hebrew) and resumes with "so" in v15.
- **"So shall he sprinkle many nations" → "so shall he sprinkle many nations"** (v15) — Hebrew יַזֶּה, as pointed the hifil of נָזָה, "sprinkle" (of blood or water on persons and things, Lev 4:6; 14:7; 16:14). Because v14 speaks of astonishment and the verb has no "on", many render "startle" (from an Arabic root "leap"; the Septuagint, from memory, reads "many nations shall marvel at him"); the pointed word is translated. **"shall shut their mouths at him" → "at him kings shall shut their mouth"** — עָלָיו "at him, over him"; פִּיהֶם "their mouth", singular. **"for that which had not been told them shall they see" → "for what had not been recounted to them they have seen"** — סֻפַּר, pual of סִפֵּר "recount"; רָאוּ and הִתְבּוֹנָנוּ are perfects (the prophet sees it done). הִתְבּוֹנֵן "consider, come to understand". Rom 15:21 quotes the last two lines in a Greek form with futures and "concerning him" ("Those to whom it was not announced concerning him shall see, and those who have not heard shall understand"); the Hebrew is translated as it stands.

### Notes
- **v7** — quoted Rom 10:15 (see Changes).
- **v11** — echoed 2 Cor 6:17.
- **v13–15** — opens the fourth servant passage (52:13–53:12). עַבְדִּי "my servant": עֶבֶד is "slave", of Yahweh's man "servant".
- **v15** — quoted Rom 15:21.
