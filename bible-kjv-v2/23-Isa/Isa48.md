# Isaiah 48

[1] Hear this, house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
who are called by the name of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
and from the waters of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ have come out,
who swear by the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
and make mention of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
not in truth and not in righteousness.

[2] For from the holy city they are called,
and on the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ they lean;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts is his name.

[3] The former things from long ago I told,
and from my mouth they went out, and I made them heard;
suddenly I acted, and they came.

[4] Because I knew that you are hard,
and your neck a sinew of iron,
and your forehead bronze,

[5] and I told you from long ago;
before it came I made you hear,
lest you say, 'My idol has done them,
and my carved image and my cast image commanded them.'

[6] You have heard; behold it all;
and you, will you not tell?
I make you hear new things from now,
and things kept guarded, and you have not known them.

[7] Now they have been created, and not from long ago,
and before today — and you have not heard them,
lest you say, 'Behold, I knew them.'

[8] Yes, you have not heard; yes, you have not known;
yes, from long ago your ear did not open.
For I knew that you would surely deal treacherously,
and a transgressor from the belly you were called.

[9] On account of my name I defer my anger,
and on account of my praise I restrain it for you,
so as not to cut you off.

[10] Behold, I have refined you, and not as silver;
I have chosen you in the furnace of affliction.

[11] For my own sake, for my own sake I will act,
for how should it be profaned?
And my glory to another I will not give.

[12] Listen to me, ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, my called one:
I am he; I am first,
also I am last.

[13] Also my hand founded the earth,
and my right hand spread out the heavens;
I call to them,
they stand together.

[14] Gather, all of you, and hear!
Who among them has told these things?
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ loves him;
he shall do his pleasure on ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧,
and his arm on the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧.

[15] I, I have spoken; also I have called him;
I have brought him, and he shall make his way prosper.

[16] Come near to me, hear this:
from the beginning I have not spoken in secret;
from the time of its being, there am I.

And now ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ has sent me, and his spirit.

[17] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, your Redeemer, the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:

I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
who teaches you to profit,
who makes you tread in the way you shall go.

[18] Oh that you had given heed to my commandments!
And your ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ would have been like the river,
and your righteousness like the waves of the sea;

[19] and your seed would have been like the sand,
and the offspring of your inward parts like its grains;
his name would not be cut off and would not be destroyed from before me.

[20] Go out from ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, flee from ⟦chaldea|Kasdim|Chaldea⟧!
With a voice of a ringing cry tell, make this heard,
bring it out to the end of the earth;
say, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has redeemed his servant ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧!"

[21] And they did not thirst; through the dry places he made them walk;
water from a rock he made flow for them;
and he split a rock, and water flowed.

[22] There is no ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for the wicked.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v2) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v16) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words. **"God" → "Elohim"** (v1, 2, 17).
- **Layout and speech marks** (throughout) — one Hebrew colon per printed line; v19b, one colon, is no longer split. The quotation marks the earlier text set around v3–16 and v17–19 are removed: the Hebrew does not mark where the words of Yahweh begin (v1–2 already speak to the house of Jacob). Only the quoted words inside the verses (v5, 7, 20) keep marks.
- **"O house of Jacob" → "house of Jacob"** (v1; also v12) — no "O": the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"which are come forth out of the waters of Judah" → "and from the waters of Judah have come out"** (v1) — Hebrew וּמִמֵּי יְהוּדָה יָצָאוּ, with "and", in the Hebrew order. **"make mention of the God of Israel"** — Hebrew בֵאלֹהֵי … יַזְכִּירוּ, hifil of זָכַר, "cause to be remembered" (KJV kept; "confess" was not the word). **"but not in truth, nor in righteousness" → "not in truth and not in righteousness"** — לֹא … וְלֹא; צְדָקָה "righteousness" (was "right").
- **"they call themselves of the holy city, and stay themselves" → "from the holy city they are called, and … they lean"** (v2) — Hebrew מֵעִיר הַקֹּדֶשׁ נִקְרָאוּ, nifal "they are called", literally "from the city of holiness" (= they take their name from it); נִסְמָכוּ nifal of סָמַךְ "lean, be supported".
- **"I have declared the former things from the beginning" → "The former things from long ago I told"** (v3) — Hebrew הָרִאשֹׁנוֹת מֵאָז הִגַּדְתִּי, in the Hebrew order; הִגִּיד "tell"; מֵאָז "from long ago" (literally "from then"; the Ps 90–93 row), the same word in v5, 7, 8. **"and I shewed them" → "and I made them heard"** — וְאַשְׁמִיעֵם, hifil of שָׁמַע "make hear"; the same verb in v5, 6, 20. **"I did them suddenly, and they came to pass" → "suddenly I acted, and they came"** — עָשִׂיתִי has no object; וַתָּבֹאנָה "and they came".
- **"Because I knew that thou art obstinate" → "Because I knew that you are hard"** (v4) — Hebrew קָשֶׁה "hard" (its row); **"an iron sinew … brass" → "a sinew of iron … bronze"** — גִּיד בַּרְזֶל; נְחוּשָׁה "bronze".
- **"I have even from the beginning declared it to thee" → "and I told you from long ago"** (v5) — Hebrew וָאַגִּיד, the "and" kept after the "because" of v4 (it opens the main clause). **"before it came to pass I shewed it thee" → "before it came I made you hear"** — בְּטֶרֶם תָּבוֹא הִשְׁמַעְתִּיךָ; no "it" after the verb. **"Mine idol … my graven image, and my molten image" → "My idol … my carved image and my cast image"** — עָצְבִּי (עֹצֶב, "idol", from עָצַב "shape"), פִּסְלִי "my carved image" (its row), וְנִסְכִּי: נֶסֶךְ is elsewhere "drink offering" (its row); here a thing poured out of metal, as Isa 41:29, so "cast image" (different sense: note). "and" kept before "my carved image".
- **"see all this" → "behold it all"** (v6) — Hebrew חֲזֵה כֻּלָּהּ, חָזָה "behold" (its row). **"and will not ye declare it?" → "and you, will you not tell?"** — וְאַתֶּם, plural, kept; no "it". **"I have shewed thee new things from this time" → "I make you hear new things from now"** — הִשְׁמַעְתִּיךָ is a perfect (literally "I have made you hear"), said of the moment of speaking; English cannot join "from now" to a past. **"even hidden things" → "and things kept guarded"** — וּנְצֻרוֹת, passive participle of נָצַר "guard": things kept back, hidden.
- **"not from the beginning; even before the day when thou heardest them not" → "and not from long ago, and before today — and you have not heard them"** (v7) — Hebrew וְלֹא מֵאָז וְלִפְנֵי־יוֹם וְלֹא שְׁמַעְתָּם, literally "and before a day, and you did not hear them": before this day; each "and" kept.
- **"Yea, thou heardest not; yea, thou knewest not" kept as "Yes, you have not heard; yes, you have not known"** (v8) — Hebrew גַּם three times, "yes, also". **"thine ear was not opened" → "your ear did not open"** — פִתְּחָה is piel with "your ear" as subject. **"thou wouldest deal very treacherously" → "you would surely deal treacherously"** — בָּגוֹד תִּבְגּוֹד, infinitive absolute (literally "dealing treacherously you would deal treacherously"). **"and wast called a transgressor from the womb" → "and a transgressor from the belly you were called"** — פֹשֵׁעַ, participle of the root of פֶּשַׁע "transgression"; מִבֶּטֶן "from the belly" (its row; not "womb").
- **"For my name's sake will I defer mine anger" → "On account of my name I defer my anger"** (v9) — Hebrew לְמַעַן "on account of" (its row, as Ps 23:3); אַאֲרִיךְ אַפִּי, literally "I make my anger long" (= I hold it off; the literal form would say the opposite in English); אַף "anger". **"and for my praise will I refrain for thee" → "and on account of my praise I restrain it for you"** — וּתְהִלָּתִי אֶחֱטָם־לָךְ: the one לְמַעַן stands over both lines, and the verb has no object ("it", the anger, supplied); חָטַם, found only here, "restrain, muzzle". **"that I cut thee not off" → "so as not to cut you off"** — לְבִלְתִּי הַכְרִיתֶךָ.
- **"but not with silver" → "and not as silver"** (v10) — Hebrew וְלֹא בְכֶסֶף, literally "and not in silver" (as silver is refined, or "not for silver"). **"I have chosen thee in the furnace of affliction" kept against "tried"** — the Masoretic Text has בְּחַרְתִּיךָ "I have chosen you" (בָּחַר, its row); "tried" follows the reading בחנתיך of the Isaiah scroll from Qumran (1QIsaᵃ; from memory) or an Aramaic sense of the verb; כּוּר "furnace" (for smelting).
- **"For mine own sake … will I do it" → "For my own sake … I will act"** (v11) — Hebrew לְמַעֲנִי לְמַעֲנִי אֶעֱשֶׂה, as 2 Kgs 19:34 ("for my own sake"); no object. **"for how should my name be polluted?" → "for how should it be profaned?"** — כִּי אֵיךְ יֵחָל, the verb only (nifal of חָלַל, "be profaned"); the subject is not named (my name, v9, is meant); "my name" is in the Isaiah scroll and the Greek (from memory). **"and I will not give my glory unto another"** — in the Hebrew order, with "and".
- **"Hearken unto me" → "Listen to me"** (v12) — שְׁמַע אֵלַי, "listen to" (its row). **"my called" → "my called one"** — מְקֹרָאִי, pual participle. **"I am the first, I also am the last" → "I am first, also I am last"** — רִאשׁוֹן, אַחֲרוֹן without the article; אַף "also".
- **"Mine hand also hath laid the foundation of the earth" → "Also my hand founded the earth"** (v13) — Hebrew אַף־יָדִי יָסְדָה אֶרֶץ, אַף first; **"spanned the heavens" → "spread out the heavens"** — טִפְּחָה, from the noun "handbreadth" (טֹפַח): "spread out" or "spanned with the hand" (uncertain). **"when I call unto them, they stand up together" → "I call to them, they stand together"** — קֹרֵא אֲנִי אֲלֵיהֶם, a participle; no "when".
- **"All ye, assemble yourselves" → "Gather, all of you"** (v14) — Hebrew הִקָּבְצוּ, nifal. **"which among them hath declared these things?" → "Who among them has told these things?"** — מִי, הִגִּיד "tell". **"The LORD hath loved him" → "Yahweh loves him"** — אֲהֵבוֹ, a perfect of a state; it may also be read "he whom Yahweh loves shall do …". **"he will do his pleasure on Babylon, and his arm shall be on the Chaldeans" → "he shall do his pleasure on Babylon, and his arm on the Chaldeans"** — חֶפְצוֹ, noun of חָפֵץ "delight" ("his delight" would not be English with "do"); וּזְרֹעוֹ כַּשְׂדִּים has no verb and no preposition: "his arm" is a second object of "do", and "on" carries the בְּ of the first line. Who "he" is (the one loved, or Yahweh) the Hebrew leaves open.
- **"I, even I, have spoken; yea, I have called him" → "I, I have spoken; also I have called him"** (v15) — Hebrew אֲנִי אֲנִי, אַף "also". **"and he shall make his way prosperous" → "and he shall make his way prosper"** — וְהִצְלִיחַ דַּרְכּוֹ, hifil (the הִצְלִיחַ דֶּרֶךְ row); the subject is "he" (the one called; or "one shall make his way prosper").
- **"Come ye near unto me" → "Come near to me"** (v16) — קִרְבוּ אֵלַי (קָרַב אֶל, its row). **"I have not spoken in secret from the beginning" kept** — Hebrew לֹא מֵרֹאשׁ בַּסֵּתֶר דִּבַּרְתִּי. **"from the time that it was, there am I" → "from the time of its being, there am I"** — מֵעֵת הֱיוֹתָהּ שָׁם אָנִי, a noun clause ("am", not "have been"). **"and his Spirit" → "and his spirit"** — וְרוּחוֹ; whether "his spirit" is a second subject (has sent me, and his spirit) or a second object (has sent me and his spirit) the Hebrew leaves open; the speaker of the last line is not named.
- **"which teacheth thee to profit, which leadeth thee by the way that thou shouldest go" → "who teaches you to profit, who makes you tread in the way you shall go"** (v17) — Hebrew מְלַמֶּדְךָ, מַדְרִיכֲךָ, participles; הִדְרִיךְ, hifil of דָּרַךְ "tread" (its row); תֵּלֵךְ, imperfect.
- **"O that thou hadst hearkened to my commandments!" → "Oh that you had given heed to my commandments!"** (v18) — Hebrew לוּא, a wish that was not fulfilled; הִקְשִׁיב "give heed" (its row). **"then had thy peace been as a river" → "And your shalom would have been like the river"** — וַיְהִי, "and"; שָׁלוֹם carries its token (reviewer row; also v22); כַּנָּהָר with the article, "the river" (in Isaiah often the Euphrates; not decided here).
- **"Thy seed also had been as the sand, and the offspring of thy bowels like the gravel thereof" → "and your seed would have been like the sand, and the offspring of your inward parts like its grains"** (v19) — Hebrew זַרְעֶךָ "your seed" (its row; not "offspring"), צֶאֱצָאֵי מֵעֶיךָ "the offspring of your inward parts" (מֵעִים, its row), כִּמְעֹתָיו "like its grains" (found only here; the word plays on מֵעֶיךָ; uncertain). **"his name should not have been cut off nor destroyed" → "his name would not be cut off and would not be destroyed"** — לֹא … וְלֹא, imperfects (also readable as "shall not be"); "his" is the seed's, or Israel's.
- **"flee ye from the Chaldeans"** (v20) — Hebrew מִכַּשְׂדִּים, "from the Chaldeans"; the verse keeps the existing token `chaldea`. **"with a voice of singing declare ye, tell this" → "With a voice of a ringing cry tell, make this heard"** — בְּקוֹל רִנָּה (רִנָּה "ringing cry", its row), הַגִּידוּ הַשְׁמִיעוּ; **"utter it even to the end of the earth" → "bring it out to the end of the earth"** — הוֹצִיאוּהָ, hifil of יָצָא "bring out". **"The LORD hath redeemed his servant Jacob"** — עַבְדּוֹ, "servant" (the עֶבֶד row, of Yahweh's).
- **"And they thirsted not when he led them through the deserts" → "And they did not thirst; through the dry places he made them walk"** (v21) — Hebrew בָּחֳרָבוֹת, plural of חָרְבָּה ("ruins" of a city in Isa 49:19; 51:3; here of the dry land: one word, two senses); הוֹלִיכָם, hifil of הָלַךְ "make walk" (its row); no "when". **"he caused the waters to flow out of the rock for them: he clave the rock also, and the waters gushed out" → "water from a rock he made flow for them; and he split a rock, and water flowed"** — מַיִם … הִזִּיל, צוּר twice without the article; בָּקַע "split", זוּב "flow" (their rows); וַיִּבְקַע … וַיָּזֻבוּ "and … and".
- **"There is no peace" → "There is no shalom"** (v22) — Hebrew אֵין שָׁלוֹם; רְשָׁעִים "the wicked" (its row).
