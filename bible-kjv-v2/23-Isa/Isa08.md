# Isaiah 8

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, "Take for yourself a large tablet, and write on it with the stylus of mortal man: For ⟦maher-shalal-hash-baz|Maher Shalal Chash Baz|Maher-shalal-hash-baz⟧."

[2] And I will take to witness for myself faithful witnesses, ⟦uriah|Uriyah|Uriah⟧ the priest and ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧ son of ⟦jeberechiah|Yeverechyahu|Jeberechiah⟧.

[3] And I came near to the prophetess, and she conceived and bore a son. And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, "Call his name ⟦maher-shalal-hash-baz|Maher Shalal Chash Baz|Maher-shalal-hash-baz⟧;

[4] for before the boy knows to call 'My father' and 'My mother,' one shall carry away the wealth of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧ and the spoil of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ before the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧."

[5] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to me yet again, saying,

[6] "Because this people has rejected the waters of the ⟦shiloah|Shiloach|Shiloah⟧ that go gently, and there is exultation with ⟦rezin|Retzin|Rezin⟧ and the son of ⟦remaliah|Remalyahu|Remaliah⟧,

[7] and therefore, behold, ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ is bringing up upon them the waters of the River, the mighty and the many, the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ and all his glory; and it shall go up over all its channels and go over all its banks,

[8] and it shall sweep on into ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, it shall overflow and pass over, it shall reach to the neck; and the spreading of its wings shall be the fullness of the breadth of your land, ⟦immanuel|Immanu El|Immanuel⟧."

[9] Be broken, peoples, and be shattered;
and give ear, all far places of the earth;
gird yourselves and be shattered;
gird yourselves and be shattered.

[10] Counsel a counsel, and it shall be made void;
speak a word, and it shall not stand;
for with us is ⟦el|El|God⟧.

[11] For thus ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, with strength of the hand, and he disciplined me against walking in the way of this people, saying,

[12] "You shall not say 'Conspiracy' of all that this people says 'Conspiracy'; and its fear you shall not fear, and you shall not be in dread.

[13] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ of Hosts, him you shall sanctify; and he is your fear, and he is your dread.

[14] And he shall be for a sanctuary, and for a stone of striking and for a rock of stumbling to the two houses of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, for a trap and for a snare to the inhabitant of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[15] And many shall stumble on them, and fall and be broken, and be snared and be captured."

[16] Bind up the attestation; seal the ⟦torah|Torah|teaching⟧ among my taught ones.

[17] And I will await ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who is hiding his face from the house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, and I will wait for him.

[18] Behold, I and the children whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has given me are for signs and for wonders in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, who abides on Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

[19] And when they say to you, "Seek to the ghosts and to the knowing spirits who chirp and who moan," should not a people seek to its ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧? On behalf of the living, to the dead?

[20] To the ⟦torah|Torah|teaching⟧ and to the attestation! If they do not speak according to this word, there is no dawn for him.

[21] And he shall pass through it, hard pressed and hungry; and it shall be, when he is hungry, he shall be wrathful and curse by his king and by his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and turn upward.

[22] And to the earth he shall look, and behold, distress and darkness, the dimness of anguish; and into gloom he is driven.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"Lord" → "Adonai"** (v7) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v13, 18) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*" / "*El*"** (v19, 21; v8, 10) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים; in v19 אֱלֹהָיו "its Elohim" and in v21 "his Elohim" may also be "its / his gods" (the form is the same). In v8, 10 the word is אֵל.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept (v1–3, 5, 9, 15, 17, 21, 22).
- **"Take thee a great roll, and write in it with a man's pen concerning Maher-shalal-hash-baz" → "Take for yourself a large tablet, and write on it with the stylus of mortal man: For Maher-shalal-hash-baz"** (v1) — Hebrew קַח־לְךָ; גִּלָּיוֹן "tablet, sheet" (uncertain: here and 3:23 only); חֶרֶט אֱנוֹשׁ "the stylus of mortal man" (= ordinary writing, "common letters"); לְמַהֵר שָׁלָל חָשׁ בַּז, "for (belonging to) Maher-shalal-hash-baz". The name is printed as the Hebrew has it; its words mean "hasten, spoil; hurry, plunder" (or "swift is the spoil, speedy the plunder"; the meaning stood in the verse in the former text).
- **"And I took unto me faithful witnesses to record" → "And I will take to witness for myself faithful witnesses"** (v2) — Hebrew וְאָעִידָה לִּי, a cohortative ("and let me / I will call to witness"), not a past; the Septuagint and 1QIsa (from memory) read an imperative "and take as witnesses for me", which is not followed. נֶאֱמָנִים "faithful".
- **"I went unto the prophetess" → "I came near to the prophetess"** (v3) — Hebrew וָאֶקְרַב אֶל. **"Call his name"** — קְרָא שְׁמוֹ.
- **"the child shall have knowledge to cry, My father, and my mother, the riches … shall be taken away" → "the boy knows to call 'My father' and 'My mother,' one shall carry away the wealth"** (v4) — Hebrew הַנַּעַר; קְרֹא "to call"; וְאִמִּי "and my mother"; יִשָּׂא, active with no subject; חַיִל "wealth" (of goods).
- **"spake also unto me again" → "spoke to me yet again, saying"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיֹּסֶף … דַּבֵּר … עוֹד, literally "added to speak … yet"; לֵאמֹר "saying".
- **"refuseth the waters of Shiloah that go softly, and rejoice in Rezin" → "has rejected the waters of the Shiloah that go gently, and there is exultation with Rezin"** (v6) — Hebrew מָאַס "reject"; הַשִּׁלֹחַ with the article; הַהֹלְכִים "that go"; וּמְשׂוֹשׂ is a noun, "and an exultation", with אֶת (as 65:18) "with" (difficult: the text is often changed; MT kept).
- **"Now therefore … bringeth up upon them the waters of the river, strong and many" → "and therefore … is bringing up upon them the waters of the River, the mighty and the many"** (v7) — Hebrew וְלָכֵן; מַעֲלֶה participle; הַנָּהָר "the River" (the Euphrates); הָעֲצוּמִים וְהָרַבִּים with articles. **"he shall come up over all his channels" → "it shall go up over all its channels"** — עָלָה "go up"; אֲפִיקִים "channels"; גְּדוֹתָיו "its banks".
- **"he shall pass through Judah; he shall overflow and go over, he shall reach even to the neck; and the stretching out of his wings shall fill the breadth of thy land, O Immanuel" → "it shall sweep on into Judah, it shall overflow and pass over, it shall reach to the neck; and the spreading of its wings shall be the fullness of the breadth of your land, Immanuel"** (v8) — Hebrew חָלַף "sweep on, pass through"; שָׁטַף "overflow"; עָבַר "pass over"; מֻטּוֹת כְּנָפָיו "the spreadings of its wings"; מְלֹא "fullness" (a noun: "shall be the fullness of"); no "O". עִמָּנוּ אֵל is here a name addressed (as 7:14) or the words "with us is El" (as v10).
- **"Associate yourselves, O ye people, and ye shall be broken in pieces" → "Be broken, peoples, and be shattered"** (v9) — Hebrew רֹעוּ, from רָעַע "break" (or from רוּעַ "raise a war cry"; uncertain; the Septuagint, from memory, has "know"); עַמִּים without article; no "O". וָחֹתּוּ "and be shattered" (חָתַת, as 7:8). **"give ear, all ye of far countries" → "and give ear, all far places of the earth"** — מֶרְחַקֵּי־אָרֶץ. **"gird yourselves"** — הִתְאַזְּרוּ (not "strap on your armor").
- **"Take counsel together, and it shall come to nought" → "Counsel a counsel, and it shall be made void"** (v10) — Hebrew עֻצוּ עֵצָה וְתֻפָר (הֵפֵר "make void"). **"for God is with us" → "for with us is El"** — כִּי עִמָּנוּ אֵל, the words of the name in 7:14; 8:8.
- **"spake thus to me with a strong hand, and instructed me that I should not walk" → "thus Yahweh said to me, with strength of the hand, and he disciplined me against walking"** (v11) — Hebrew כְּחֶזְקַת הַיָּד, literally "as in the strength of the hand" (the hand of Yahweh upon the prophet, as Ezek 3:14; "upon me" is not written); וְיִסְּרֵנִי, from יִסֵּר "discipline" (also read as from סוּר "turn aside"); מִלֶּכֶת "from walking".
- **"Say ye not, A confederacy" → "You shall not say 'Conspiracy'"** (v12) — Hebrew לֹא־תֹאמְרוּן, plural; קֶשֶׁר "conspiracy". **"neither fear ye their fear, nor be afraid" → "and its fear you shall not fear, and you shall not be in dread"** — מוֹרָאוֹ "its fear" (what this people fears); תַעֲרִיצוּ, hifil of עָרַץ "dread".
- **"Sanctify the LORD of hosts himself; and let him be your fear, and let him be your dread" → "Yahweh of Hosts, him you shall sanctify; and he is your fear, and he is your dread"** (v13) — Hebrew אֶת־יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת אֹתוֹ תַקְדִּישׁוּ, the object first; וְהוּא מוֹרַאֲכֶם וְהוּא מַעֲרִצְכֶם, no verb: "and he (is) your fear … your dread" (מַעֲרִיץ "one who causes dread"). מוֹרָא is "the Fear" where it names the one feared (Ps 76:11); with "your" it stands here as the plain noun. 1 Pet 3:14–15 takes up v12–13 in Greek (τὸν δὲ φόβον αὐτῶν μὴ φοβηθῆτε … κύριον δὲ τὸν Χριστὸν ἁγιάσατε, "do not fear their fear … sanctify the Lord, the Christ").
- **"he shall be for a sanctuary; but for a stone of stumbling and for a rock of offence" → "he shall be for a sanctuary, and for a stone of striking and for a rock of stumbling"** (v14) — Hebrew לְמִקְדָּשׁ "for a sanctuary" (MT kept; a change to "snare" is often proposed); אֶבֶן נֶגֶף "a stone of striking" (one strikes the foot on it; נֶגֶף is elsewhere "plague, blow"); צוּר מִכְשׁוֹל "a rock of stumbling"; פַּח "trap", מוֹקֵשׁ "snare"; לִשְׁנֵי בָתֵּי "to the two houses"; לְיוֹשֵׁב singular. Rom 9:33 and 1 Pet 2:8 quote the stone in Greek: λίθον προσκόμματος καὶ πέτραν σκανδάλου, "a stone of stumbling and a rock of offence"; the Hebrew is translated as it stands.
- **"many among them shall stumble … and be taken" → "many shall stumble on them … and be captured"** (v15) — Hebrew בָם "on them" (the stone and the rock); נִלְכְּדוּ "be captured".
- **"Bind up the testimony, seal the law among my disciples" → "Bind up the attestation; seal the torah among my taught ones"** (v16, 20) — Hebrew צוֹר, חֲתוֹם, infinitives used as commands; תְּעוּדָה "attestation" (as Ruth 4:7; a witnessed document; עֵדוּת is "testimony"); לִמֻּדַי "my taught ones" (from לָמַד "learn").
- **"I will wait upon the LORD … and I will look for him" → "I will await Yahweh … and I will wait for him"** (v17) — Hebrew חִכָּה "await" and קִוָּה "wait for", two verbs. Heb 2:13 has in Greek "I will put my trust in him", the Septuagint's wording (from memory).
- **"are for signs and for wonders in Israel from the LORD of hosts, which dwelleth in mount Zion"** (v18) — Hebrew לְאֹתוֹת וּלְמוֹפְתִים "for signs and for wonders"; הַשֹּׁכֵן "who abides" (שָׁכַן). Heb 2:13 quotes the first words.
- **"Seek unto them that have familiar spirits, and unto wizards that peep, and that mutter" → "Seek to the ghosts and to the knowing spirits who chirp and who moan"** (v19) — Hebrew דִּרְשׁוּ אֶל; הָאֹבוֹת "the ghosts", הַיִּדְּעֹנִים "the knowing spirits" (the spirits, not the persons who consult them; traditionally "mediums", "necromancers"); הַמְצַפְצְפִים "who chirp", הַמַּהְגִּים "who moan" (הָגָה of a low sound, as a dove's, 38:14; 59:11). **"should not a people seek unto their God? for the living to the dead?" → "should not a people seek to its Elohim? On behalf of the living, to the dead?"** — בְּעַד הַחַיִּים אֶל־הַמֵּתִים, no verb.
- **"if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them" → "If they do not speak according to this word, there is no dawn for him"** (v20) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר אֵין־לוֹ שָׁחַר, "that / who there is no dawn for him": אֲשֶׁר is left unrendered (its reference is uncertain); לוֹ singular; שַׁחַר "dawn". אִם־לֹא may also be the oath "surely they shall speak".
- **"they shall pass through it, hardly bestead and hungry … they shall fret themselves, and curse their king and their God, and look upward" → "he shall pass through it, hard pressed and hungry … he shall be wrathful and curse by his king and by his Elohim, and turn upward"** (v21) — Hebrew singulars; בָּהּ "through it" (feminine: the land, not named); נִקְשֶׁה "hard pressed"; הִתְקַצַּף "be wrathful"; קִלֵּל בְּ "curse by" (or "curse"; as 1 Sam 17:43); וּפָנָה לְמָעְלָה "and turn upward" ("faces" is not written).
- **"they shall look unto the earth; and behold trouble and darkness, dimness of anguish; and they shall be driven to darkness" → "to the earth he shall look, and behold, distress and darkness, the dimness of anguish; and into gloom he is driven"** (v22) — Hebrew יַבִּיט singular; צָרָה "distress"; מְעוּף "dimness" (only here: uncertain); צוּקָה "anguish"; אֲפֵלָה "gloom"; מְנֻדָּח "driven, thrust out" ("into" is supplied).

### Notes
- **Maher-shalal-hash-baz** (v1, 3) — a sign-name of the plunder of Damascus and Samaria (v4).
- **v14** — quoted in Rom 9:33; 1 Pet 2:8. **v17–18** — in Heb 2:13.
- **Numbering** — the Hebrew chapter 8 has a verse 23, which is our 9:1.
