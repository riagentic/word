# Isaiah 42

[1] Behold my servant, I uphold him,
my chosen, my soul is pleased;
I have put my spirit upon him;
judgment to the nations he shall bring out.

[2] He shall not cry out, and he shall not lift up,
and he shall not make his voice heard in the street.

[3] A crushed reed he shall not break,
and a dim wick he shall not quench;
to truth he shall bring out judgment.

[4] He shall not grow dim, and he shall not be crushed,
until he sets judgment in the earth;
and for his ⟦torah|torah|law⟧ coastlands shall hope.

[5] Thus says ⟦el|the El|God⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
who creates the heavens and stretches them out,
who spreads out the earth and what comes out of it,
who gives breath to the people upon it,
and spirit to those who walk in it:

[6] "I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have called you in righteousness,
and I will take hold of your hand;
and I will guard you, and I will give you for a covenant of a people,
for a light of nations,

[7] to open blind eyes,
to bring out the prisoner from the dungeon,
from the house of confinement those who dwell in darkness.

[8] I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, that is my name;
and my glory I will not give to another,
nor my praise to carved images.

[9] The former things, behold, they have come,
and new things I am telling;
before they spring up
I make you hear them."

[10] Sing to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ a new song,
his praise from the end of the earth,
you who go down to the sea, and its fullness,
coastlands and their dwellers.

[11] Let the wilderness and its cities lift up,
the villages where ⟦kedar|Qedar|Kedar⟧ dwells;
let the dwellers of the crag shout for joy,
from the top of the mountains let them cry aloud.

[12] Let them give glory to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and his praise in the coastlands let them tell.

[13] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ goes out like a mighty one,
like a man of wars he stirs up jealousy;
he shouts, yes, he roars;
over his enemies he prevails.

[14] I have been silent from of old,
I keep still, I restrain myself;
like a woman giving birth I will groan,
I will gasp and pant together.

[15] I will lay waste mountains and hills,
and all their herbage I will dry up;
and I will make rivers into coastlands,
and pools I will dry up.

[16] And I will make the blind walk
in a way that they have not known;
in tracks that they have not known
I will make them tread;
I will make the dark place before them into light,
and crooked places into level land.
These are the things: I have done them,
and I have not forsaken them.

[17] They are turned back, they shall be ashamed with shame,
those who trust in the carved image,
who say to the molten image,
"You are our gods."

[18] Deaf ones, hear;
and blind ones, look, to see.

[19] Who is blind, except my servant,
and deaf as my messenger whom I send?
Who is blind as the one at peace,
and blind as the servant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?

[20] Seeing many things, and you do not keep them;
opening the ears, and he does not hear.

[21] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ was pleased, on account of his righteousness;
he magnifies ⟦torah|torah|law⟧ and makes it glorious.

[22] And it is a people plundered and pillaged;
all of them are snared in holes,
and in houses of confinement they are hidden;
they have become plunder, and there is none who delivers,
pillage, and none who says, "Bring back!"

[23] Who among you will give ear to this,
will attend and hear for afterward?

[24] Who gave ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ for pillage,
and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to plunderers?
Was it not ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, he against whom we have sinned?
And they were not willing to walk in his ways,
and they did not listen to his ⟦torah|torah|law⟧.

[25] And he poured out upon him fury, his anger,
and the strength of war;
and it set him ablaze all around, and he did not know;
and it burned against him, and he does not lay it to heart.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God the LORD" → "the El, Yahweh"** (v5) — Hebrew הָאֵל יְהוָה *ha-El Yahweh* (*El* with the article, not *Elohim*). **"Thus saith" → "Thus says"** — כֹּה אָמַר.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v6 is laid out by the accents (the etnach after "your hand").
- **v1–4 word for word** — the first passage of "the servant" (עַבְדִּי "my servant", as Israel in 41:8, 9; עֶבֶד יְהוָה "the servant of Yahweh" in v19): translated as the Hebrew stands, without levelling to Matt 12:18–21, which follows a Greek text. Matthew (as printed by the tools) differs: "whom I chose" (ᾑρέτισα, for אֶתְמָךְ־בּוֹ "I uphold him"); "my beloved (ἀγαπητός) in whom my soul was well pleased" (for "my chosen, my soul is pleased"); "I will put" (θήσω, for the perfect "I have put"); "he will announce judgment" (ἀπαγγελεῖ); v2 "he will not quarrel nor shout, nor will anyone hear his voice in the streets"; v3 "smoking flax" (λίνον τυφόμενον) and "until he sends out judgment to victory" (εἰς νῖκος, for "to truth"), and Matthew has nothing for v4a ("He shall not grow dim …"); v4b "in his name nations will hope" (τῷ ὀνόματι αὐτοῦ, for "for his torah").
- **"whom I uphold; mine elect, in whom my soul delighteth" → "I uphold him, my chosen, my soul is pleased"** (v1) — Hebrew אֶתְמָךְ־בּוֹ בְּחִירִי רָצְתָה נַפְשִׁי: no relative words ("whom", "in whom" are supplied in the KJV); תָּמַךְ "uphold" (as 41:10); רָצָה "be pleased" (no object written). **"I have put my spirit upon him"** — נָתַתִּי רוּחִי עָלָיו, literally "I have given"; a perfect. **"he shall bring forth judgment to the Gentiles" → "judgment to the nations he shall bring out"** — מִשְׁפָּט לַגּוֹיִם יוֹצִיא, Hebrew order; הוֹצִיא "bring out" (v1, 3, 7); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (v1, 3, 4).
- **"He shall not cry, nor lift up, nor cause his voice to be heard in the street" → "He shall not cry out, and he shall not lift up, and he shall not make his voice heard in the street"** (v2) — Hebrew לֹא יִצְעַק וְלֹא יִשָּׂא וְלֹא־יַשְׁמִיעַ: three verbs with וְלֹא "and not"; יִשָּׂא has no object (the "voice" of the next line).
- **"A bruised reed shall he not break, and the smoking flax shall he not quench" → "A crushed reed he shall not break, and a dim wick he shall not quench"** (v3) — Hebrew קָנֶה רָצוּץ … וּפִשְׁתָּה כֵהָה: רָצוּץ "crushed"; פִּשְׁתָּה "flax" (the wick of a lamp); כֵּהֶה "dim" (not "smoking"). **"he shall bring forth judgment unto truth" → "to truth he shall bring out judgment"** — לֶאֱמֶת יוֹצִיא מִשְׁפָּט, Hebrew order; לֶאֱמֶת "to / for truth" (= faithfully, or so that it stands as truth).
- **"He shall not fail nor be discouraged" → "He shall not grow dim, and he shall not be crushed"** (v4) — Hebrew לֹא יִכְהֶה וְלֹא יָרוּץ: the two roots of v3 (כָּהָה "grow dim", רָצַץ "crush"); what is not done to the reed and the wick is not done to him. יָרוּץ is also read "run" (from רוּץ). **"till he have set judgment in the earth" → "until he sets judgment in the earth"** — עַד־יָשִׂים. **"and the isles shall wait for his law" → "and for his torah coastlands shall hope"** — וּלְתוֹרָתוֹ אִיִּים יְיַחֵילוּ, Hebrew order; יִחֵל "hope" (קִוָּה is "wait for"); תּוֹרָה "torah" (instruction).
- **"he that created the heavens, and stretched them out" → "who creates the heavens and stretches them out"** (v5) — Hebrew בּוֹרֵא … וְנוֹטֵיהֶם, participles (the plural נוֹטֵיהֶם is a plural of majesty or a spelling: uncertain). **"he that spread forth the earth, and that which cometh out of it" → "who spreads out the earth and what comes out of it"** — רֹקַע (rq' "beat / stamp flat"); צֶאֱצָאִים "what comes out". **"giveth breath unto the people upon it, and spirit to them that walk therein"** — נְשָׁמָה "breath" beside רוּחַ "spirit".
- **"I the LORD have called thee in righteousness" → "I, Yahweh, have called you in righteousness"** (v6) — Hebrew אֲנִי יְהוָה קְרָאתִיךָ; also read "I am Yahweh; I have called you". **"and will hold thine hand" → "and I will take hold of your hand"** — וְאַחְזֵק בְּיָדֶךָ, hifil of חָזַק (as 41:9, 13). **"and will keep thee" → "and I will guard you"** — וְאֶצָּרְךָ, from נָצַר "guard"; the form can also come from יָצַר "form" ("and I will form you"): the Hebrew allows both. **"for a covenant of the people, for a light of the Gentiles" → "for a covenant of a people, for a light of nations"** — לִבְרִית עָם לְאוֹר גּוֹיִם: no articles.
- **"to open the blind eyes" → "to open blind eyes"** (v7) — Hebrew עֵינַיִם עִוְרוֹת, no article. **"the prisoners" → "the prisoner"** — אַסִּיר, singular (collective). **"from the prison house" → "from the house of confinement"** — בֵּית כֶּלֶא (כֶּלֶא "confinement"; as v22 בָּתֵּי כְלָאִים). **"them that sit in darkness" → "those who dwell in darkness"** — יֹשְׁבֵי חֹשֶׁךְ, יָשַׁב "dwell" (as v10, 11).
- **"I am the LORD: that is my name"** (v8) — אֲנִי יְהוָה הוּא שְׁמִי. **"and my glory will I not give to another, neither my praise to graven images" → "and my glory I will not give to another, nor my praise to carved images"** — פְּסִילִים "carved images".
- **"Behold, the former things are come to pass" → "The former things, behold, they have come"** (v9) — Hebrew הָרִאשֹׁנוֹת הִנֵּה־בָאוּ, Hebrew order; "to pass" is not written. **"new things do I declare" → "new things I am telling"** — אֲנִי מַגִּיד, participle; הִגִּיד "tell" (v12). **"I tell you of them" → "I make you hear them"** — אַשְׁמִיעַ אֶתְכֶם, "make you hear"; "them" supplied. צָמַח "spring up".
- **"ye that go down to the sea, and all that is therein" → "you who go down to the sea, and its fullness"** (v10) — Hebrew יוֹרְדֵי הַיָּם וּמְלֹאוֹ; מְלֹא "fullness". **"the isles, and the inhabitants thereof" → "coastlands and their dwellers"** — אִיִּים וְיֹשְׁבֵיהֶם.
- **"Let the wilderness and the cities thereof lift up their voice" → "Let the wilderness and its cities lift up"** (v11) — Hebrew יִשְׂאוּ מִדְבָּר וְעָרָיו, no object ("their voice" is supplied in the KJV; the voice of v2 is the same ellipsis). **"the villages that Kedar doth inhabit" → "the villages where Kedar dwells"** — חֲצֵרִים תֵּשֵׁב קֵדָר. **"the inhabitants of the rock" → "the dwellers of the crag"** — יֹשְׁבֵי סֶלַע; סֶלַע "crag" (as Num 20:8); also read as the town Sela in Edom. **"let them shout from the top of the mountains" → "from the top of the mountains let them cry aloud"** — מֵרֹאשׁ הָרִים יִצְוָחוּ, Hebrew order; רָנַן "shout for joy", צָוַח "cry aloud": two verbs.
- **"Let them give glory unto the LORD"** (v12) — Hebrew יָשִׂימוּ לַיהוָה כָּבוֹד, literally "set glory"; **"and declare his praise in the islands" → "and his praise in the coastlands let them tell"** — Hebrew order; הִגִּיד "tell".
- **"as a mighty man" → "like a mighty one"** (v13) — Hebrew כַּגִּבּוֹר. **"like a man of war" → "like a man of wars"** — אִישׁ מִלְחָמוֹת, plural. **"he shall stir up jealousy"** — יָעִיר קִנְאָה, הֵעִיר "stir up" (as 41:2, 25); קִנְאָה "jealousy" (also "zeal"). **"he shall cry, yea, roar" → "he shouts, yes, he roars"** — יָרִיעַ אַף־יַצְרִיחַ. **"he shall prevail against his enemies" → "over his enemies he prevails"** — עַל־אֹיְבָיו יִתְגַּבָּר, hitpael of גָּבַר "prevail" (the root of גִּבּוֹר), Hebrew order.
- **"I have long time holden my peace" → "I have been silent from of old"** (v14) — Hebrew הֶחֱשֵׁיתִי מֵעוֹלָם; חָשָׁה "be silent"; מֵעוֹלָם "from of old". **"I have been still, and refrained myself" → "I keep still, I restrain myself"** — אַחֲרִישׁ אֶתְאַפָּק, imperfects; הֶחֱרִישׁ is "be silent" elsewhere: "keep still" here because חָשָׁה stands beside it. **"now will I cry like a travailing woman" → "like a woman giving birth I will groan"** — כַּיּוֹלֵדָה אֶפְעֶה: "now" is not written; פָּעָה "groan, cry out" (only here). **"I will destroy and devour at once" → "I will gasp and pant together"** — אֶשֹּׁם וְאֶשְׁאַף יָחַד: נָשַׁם "gasp", שָׁאַף "pant"; יַחַד "together".
- **"I will make waste mountains and hills, and dry up all their herbs" → "I will lay waste mountains and hills, and all their herbage I will dry up"** (v15) — Hebrew אַחֲרִיב … וְכָל־עֶשְׂבָּם אוֹבִישׁ, Hebrew order; הֶחֱרִיב "lay waste", הוֹבִישׁ "dry up". **"I will make the rivers islands" → "I will make rivers into coastlands"** — וְשַׂמְתִּי נְהָרוֹת לָאִיִּים; אִי is the "coastland" of 40:15; 41:1 (here dry land rising out of the water).
- **"And I will bring the blind by a way that they knew not" → "And I will make the blind walk in a way that they have not known"** (v16) — Hebrew וְהוֹלַכְתִּי עִוְרִים בְּדֶרֶךְ לֹא יָדָעוּ; הוֹלִיךְ "make walk". **"I will lead them in paths" → "in tracks … I will make them tread"** — בִּנְתִיבוֹת … אַדְרִיכֵם; נְתִיבָה "track"; הִדְרִיךְ hifil of דָּרַךְ "tread". **"I will make darkness light before them" → "I will make the dark place before them into light"** — מַחְשָׁךְ "dark place". **"and crooked things straight" → "and crooked places into level land"** — מַעֲקַשִּׁים לְמִישׁוֹר (מִישׁוֹר as 40:4). **"These things will I do unto them, and not forsake them" → "These are the things: I have done them, and I have not forsaken them"** — אֵלֶּה הַדְּבָרִים עֲשִׂיתִם וְלֹא עֲזַבְתִּים, perfects (the prophetic perfect: future in English is the other reading); דָּבָר "thing" (no speech is meant).
- **"they shall be greatly ashamed" → "they shall be ashamed with shame"** (v17) — Hebrew יֵבֹשׁוּ בֹשֶׁת, the verb with its noun (not the infinitive absolute). **"in graven images … to the molten images" → "in the carved image … to the molten image"** — בַּפָּסֶל … לְמַסֵּכָה, singulars. **"Ye are our gods"** — אַתֶּם אֱלֹהֵינוּ, plural, plain "gods".
- **"Hear, ye deaf; and look, ye blind, that ye may see" → "Deaf ones, hear; and blind ones, look, to see"** (v18) — Hebrew הַחֵרְשִׁים שְׁמָעוּ וְהַעִוְרִים הַבִּיטוּ לִרְאוֹת, Hebrew order; the article marks the vocative ("ye" not written); לִרְאוֹת "to see".
- **"Who is blind, but my servant? or deaf, as my messenger that I sent?" → "Who is blind, except my servant, and deaf as my messenger whom I send?"** (v19) — Hebrew מִי עִוֵּר כִּי אִם־עַבְדִּי וְחֵרֵשׁ כְּמַלְאָכִי אֶשְׁלָח; מַלְאָךְ "messenger" (the word for "angel" too); אֶשְׁלָח imperfect "I send". **"as he that is perfect" → "as the one at peace"** — כִּמְשֻׁלָּם, pual of שָׁלֵם (root of שָׁלוֹם): "the one made at peace / repaid / made whole"; meaning uncertain ("the one in covenant", "the dedicated one" are also defended). **"the LORD'S servant" → "the servant of Yahweh"** — עֶבֶד יְהוָה.
- **"Seeing many things, but thou observest not" → "Seeing many things, and you do not keep them"** (v20) — the qere רָאוֹת is an infinitive, "seeing" (the ketiv רָאִיתָ "you have seen"); שָׁמַר "keep" (heed); "them" supplied. **"opening the ears, but he heareth not" → "opening the ears, and he does not hear"** — פָּקוֹחַ, infinitive; the person changes from "you" to "he" in the Hebrew, kept.
- **"The LORD is well pleased for his righteousness' sake; he will magnify the law, and make it honourable" → "Yahweh was pleased, on account of his righteousness; he magnifies torah and makes it glorious"** (v21) — Hebrew יְהוָה חָפֵץ לְמַעַן צִדְקוֹ יַגְדִּיל תּוֹרָה וְיַאְדִּיר: חָפֵץ "be pleased, delight"; לְמַעַן "on account of"; תּוֹרָה has no article and no "his"; הֶאְדִּיר "make glorious".
- **"But this is a people robbed and spoiled" → "And it is a people plundered and pillaged"** (v22) — Hebrew וְהוּא עַם־בָּזוּז וְשָׁסוּי: וְ "and"; הוּא "it / he"; בָּזַז "plunder", שָׁסָה "pillage". **"they are all of them snared in holes"** — הָפֵחַ בַּחוּרִים כֻּלָּם; the consonants of בַּחוּרִים can also be read "young men" ("all their young men are snared"): the pointing gives "in holes". **"in prison houses" → "in houses of confinement"** — בָּתֵּי כְלָאִים (as v7). **"they are for a prey, and none delivereth; for a spoil, and none saith, Restore" → "they have become plunder, and there is none who delivers, pillage, and none who says, Bring back!"** — הָיוּ לָבַז … מְשִׁסָּה; הִצִּיל "deliver"; הָשַׁב "bring back".
- **"Who among you will give ear to this? who will hearken and hear for the time to come?" → "Who among you will give ear to this, will attend and hear for afterward?"** (v23) — Hebrew יַקְשִׁב וְיִשְׁמַע לְאָחוֹר: the question word stands once; לְאָחוֹר "for afterward" (as 41:23).
- **"Who gave Jacob for a spoil" → "Who gave Jacob for pillage"** (v24) — the qere לִמְשִׁסָּה "for pillage" (as v22; the ketiv למשוסה, "to the pillager"). **"to the robbers" → "to plunderers"** — לְבֹזְזִים. **"did not the LORD, he against whom we have sinned?" → "Was it not Yahweh, he against whom we have sinned?"** — הֲלוֹא יְהוָה זוּ חָטָאנוּ לוֹ, זוּ "he who" (relative). **"for they would not walk in his ways" → "And they were not willing to walk in his ways"** — וְלֹא־אָבוּ בִדְרָכָיו הָלוֹךְ; וְ "and" (not "for"); אָבָה "be willing"; the person changes from "we" to "they", kept. **"neither were they obedient unto his law" → "and they did not listen to his torah"** — שָׁמַע בְּ "listen to" ("obey" is not a Hebrew word).
- **"Therefore he hath poured upon him the fury of his anger" → "And he poured out upon him fury, his anger"** (v25) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁפֹּךְ עָלָיו חֵמָה אַפּוֹ: וַ "and"; חֵמָה is not in the construct form: "fury, (namely) his anger"; חֵמָה "fury" (lit. "heat"), אַף "anger". **"the strength of battle" → "the strength of war"** — וֶעֱזוּז מִלְחָמָה. **"and it hath set him on fire round about, yet he knew not" → "and it set him ablaze all around, and he did not know"** — וַתְּלַהֲטֵהוּ … וְלֹא יָדָע, וְ "and". **"and it burned him, yet he laid it not to heart" → "and it burned against him, and he does not lay it to heart"** — וַתִּבְעַר־בּוֹ וְלֹא־יָשִׂים עַל־לֵב: בְּ "against / in him"; יָשִׂים imperfect; "it" supplied; literally "set upon heart".

### Notes
- **v1–4** — the first passage of the servant in chs 42–53 (also 49:1–6; 50:4–9; 52:13–53:12); quoted in Matt 12:18–21 of Jesus.
- **"a light of nations" (v6)** — Luke 2:32; Acts 13:47; see Isa 49:6.
