# Isaiah 65

[1] I let myself be sought by those who did not ask;
I let myself be found by those who did not seek me.
I said, "Here I am, here I am,"
to a nation that was not called by my name.

[2] I spread out my hands all the day
to a stubborn people,
who walk in the way that is not good,
after their thoughts;

[3] the people who vex me
to my face continually,
sacrificing in the gardens
and making smoke on the bricks;

[4] who sit in the graves,
and in the guarded places lodge;
who eat the flesh of the pig,
and broth of foul things is in their vessels;

[5] who say, "Keep to yourself,
do not draw near to me, for I am holy to you."
These are a smoke in my nose,
a fire burning all the day.

[6] Behold, it is written before me:
"I will not be silent, but I will repay,
and I will repay upon their bosom

[7] your iniquities and the iniquities of your fathers together,"
says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
"who made smoke on the mountains,
and on the hills reproached me;
and I will measure their former work
into their bosom."

[8] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"As the new wine is found in the cluster,
and one says, 'Do not ruin it, for a blessing is in it,'
so I will do on account of my servants,
so as not to ruin the whole.

[9] And I will bring out from ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ seed,
and from ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ a possessor of my mountains;
and my chosen ones shall possess it,
and my servants shall abide there.

[10] And the Sharon shall become a pasture for flocks,
and the Valley of Achor a lying place for herds,
for my people who have sought me.

[11] But you who forsake ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
who forget my holy mountain,
who arrange a table for ⟦gad-fortune|Gad|Fortune⟧,
and who fill mixed wine for ⟦meni|Meni|Destiny⟧,

[12] I will count you for the sword,
and all of you shall bow down to the slaughter;
because I called and you did not answer,
I spoke and you did not hear,
and you did what was evil in my eyes,
and what I did not delight in, you chose."

[13] Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:

"Behold, my servants shall eat,
but you shall be hungry;
behold, my servants shall drink,
but you shall be thirsty;
behold, my servants shall rejoice,
but you shall be ashamed;

[14] behold, my servants shall shout for joy from goodness of heart,
but you shall cry out from pain of heart,
and from breaking of spirit you shall wail.

[15] And you shall leave your name for an oath to my chosen ones,
and ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ shall put you to death,
and for his servants he shall call another name;

[16] so that he who blesses himself in the land
shall bless himself by the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of amen,
and he who swears in the land
shall swear by the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of amen;
because the former distresses are forgotten,
and because they are hidden from my eyes.

[17] For behold, I am creating new heavens and a new earth;
and the former things shall not be remembered,
and they shall not come up on the heart.

[18] But exult and be glad perpetually
in what I am creating;
for behold, I am creating ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ a joy,
and her people an exultation.

[19] And I will be glad in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧
and exult in my people;
and there shall no more be heard in her
the voice of weeping and the voice of outcry.

[20] There shall no more be from there
an infant of days,
and an old man who does not fill his days;
for the boy shall die a hundred years old,
and the sinner a hundred years old shall be cursed.

[21] And they shall build houses and dwell,
and they shall plant vineyards and eat their fruit.

[22] They shall not build and another dwell;
they shall not plant and another eat;
for like the days of the tree are the days of my people,
and the work of their hands my chosen ones shall wear out.

[23] They shall not labor in vain,
and they shall not bear for dismay;
for the seed of the blessed of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are they,
and their offspring with them.

[24] And it shall be: before they call, I will answer;
while they are still speaking, I will hear.

[25] The wolf and the lamb shall graze as one,
and the lion like cattle shall eat straw,
and the serpent — dust is its bread.
They shall not do evil and they shall not ruin
in all my holy mountain," says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v13, 15) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both names.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v16) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order, as the existing layout has it.
- **"I am sought of them that asked not for me; I am found of them that sought me not" → "I let myself be sought by those who did not ask; I let myself be found by those who did not seek me"** (v1) — Hebrew נִדְרַשְׁתִּי · נִמְצֵאתִי, nifal perfects: the reflexive sense "let myself be" (plain passive "I was sought … I was found" in Changes); דָּרַשׁ and בִּקֵּשׁ are both "seek" in English; שָׁאָלוּ "ask" ("for me" is not written). **"Behold me, behold me" → "Here I am, here I am"** — הִנֵּנִי, literally "behold me". **"a nation that was not called by my name"** — לֹא־קֹרָא, pointed as a passive (pual); the reading "that did not call on my name" (qal) is not followed. Rom 10:20–21 quotes v1–2 in Greek.
- **"a rebellious people … after their own thoughts" → "a stubborn people … after their thoughts"** (v2) — Hebrew סוֹרֵר "stubborn" (as 1:23; Deut 21:18); הַדֶּרֶךְ לֹא־טוֹב "the way that is not good" ("in" supplied); מַחְשְׁבֹתֵיהֶם, no "own".
- **"A people that provoketh me to anger continually to my face … burneth incense upon altars of brick" → "the people who vex me to my face continually … making smoke on the bricks"** (v3) — Hebrew הָעָם with the article; הִכְעִיס "vex"; עַל־פָּנַי "to (lit. upon) my face"; מְקַטְּרִים without an object, "making smoke" (as v7; incense is not named); הַלְּבֵנִים "the bricks" ("altars" is not written).
- **"Which remain among the graves, and lodge in the monuments" → "who sit in the graves, and in the guarded places lodge"** (v4) — Hebrew הַיֹּשְׁבִים, יָשַׁב of the body, "sit" ("dwell" in Changes); נְצוּרִים "guarded places" (from נָצַר "guard"; "secret places" is also read: uncertain); לִין "lodge". **"swine's flesh, and broth of abominable things is in their vessels" → "the flesh of the pig, and broth of foul things"** — qere וּמְרַק "and broth" (ketiv פרק "and a piece of"); פִּגֻּלִים "foul things" (as Lev 7:18: flesh kept too long); "is" supplied.
- **"Stand by thyself, come not near to me; for I am holier than thou" → "Keep to yourself, do not draw near to me, for I am holy to you"** (v5) — Hebrew קְרַב אֵלֶיךָ, literally "come near to yourself" (= keep where you are); נָגַשׁ "draw near"; קְדַשְׁתִּיךָ, the qal "be holy" with "you" as object: "I am holy (in regard) to you", taken as "too holy for you" or "holier than you" (KJV); the repointing "I would make you holy" (קִדַּשְׁתִּיךָ) is not followed. **"These are a smoke in my nose"** — אַף of the body, "nose"; אֵשׁ יֹקֶדֶת "a fire burning".
- **"I will not keep silence, but will recompense, even recompense into their bosom" → "I will not be silent, but I will repay, and I will repay upon their bosom"** (v6) — Hebrew חָשָׁה "be silent"; כִּי אִם־שִׁלַּמְתִּי, "but (= rather) I will have repaid", a perfect; "except" of the כִּי אִם row would say "unless"; שִׁלֵּם "repay" twice; עַל־חֵיקָם "upon their bosom".
- **"Your iniquities, and the iniquities of your fathers together … which have burned incense upon the mountains, and blasphemed me upon the hills: therefore will I measure their former work into their bosom" → "your iniquities and the iniquities of your fathers together … who made smoke on the mountains, and on the hills reproached me; and I will measure their former work into their bosom"** (v7) — Hebrew קִטְּרוּ without an object, "made smoke"; חֵרֵף "reproach"; וּמַדֹּתִי "and I will measure" (no "therefore"); פְעֻלָּתָם רִאשֹׁנָה, "their former work" (or "their work first": רִאשֹׁנָה can be an adverb); qere אֶל "into" (ketiv עַל "upon", as v6).
- **"As the new wine is found in the cluster, and one saith, Destroy it not" → "and one says, 'Do not ruin it'"** (v8) — Hebrew וְאָמַר, "and he says", with no subject ("one"); הִשְׁחִית "ruin" (also "so as not to ruin the whole", and v25, as 11:9). **"for my servants' sake" → "on account of my servants"** — לְמַעַן before a noun. **"that I may not destroy them all" → "so as not to ruin the whole"** — לְבִלְתִּי הַשְׁחִית הַכֹּל, "them" is not written.
- **"I will bring forth a seed out of Jacob, and out of Judah an inheritor of my mountains: and mine elect shall inherit it" → "And I will bring out from Jacob seed, and from Judah a possessor of my mountains; and my chosen ones shall possess it"** (v9) — Hebrew זֶרַע "seed"; יוֹרֵשׁ singular, יָרַשׁ "possess"; בְחִירַי "my chosen ones" (also v15, 22); שָׁכַן "abide".
- **"Sharon shall be a fold of flocks, and the valley of Achor a place for the herds to lie down in" → "And the Sharon shall become a pasture for flocks, and the Valley of Achor a lying place for herds"** (v10) — Hebrew הַשָּׁרוֹן with the article; נְוֵה־צֹאן, נָוֶה "habitation, pasture" (as Exod 15:13), here of flocks: "pasture"; רֵבֶץ, from רָבַץ "lie"; דְּרָשׁוּנִי "have sought me".
- **"that prepare a table for that troop, and that furnish the drink offering unto that number" → "who arrange a table for Gad, and who fill mixed wine for Meni"** (v11) — Hebrew עָרַךְ "arrange"; לַגַּד · לַמְנִי, with the article: Gad ("Fortune") and Meni ("Destiny"), names of gods of luck; the gloss that stood inside the tokens is moved here; מִמְסָךְ "mixed wine" (no "cups"). Wordplay: לַמְנִי … וּמָנִיתִי (v12).
- **"Therefore will I number you to the sword … ye did not hear; but did evil before mine eyes, and did choose that wherein I delighted not" → "I will count you for the sword … you did not hear, and you did what was evil in my eyes, and what I did not delight in, you chose"** (v12) — Hebrew וּמָנִיתִי, מָנָה "count" (the play on Meni); תִּכְרָעוּ "bow down"; שָׁמַע "hear"; וַתַּעֲשׂוּ "and you did"; the object first, as the Hebrew. The same words in 66:4.
- **"shall rejoice … shall be ashamed"** (v13) — Hebrew שָׂמַח "rejoice"; בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed". Four times וְאַתֶּם, "but you".
- **"shall sing for joy of heart … for sorrow of heart, and shall howl for vexation of spirit" → "shall shout for joy from goodness of heart … from pain of heart, and from breaking of spirit you shall wail"** (v14) — Hebrew רָנַן "shout for joy"; מִטּוּב לֵב "from goodness of heart"; כְּאֵב "pain"; שֵׁבֶר "breaking"; הֵילִיל "wail".
- **"ye shall leave your name for a curse unto my chosen: for the Lord GOD shall slay thee, and call his servants by another name" → "And you shall leave your name for an oath to my chosen ones, and Lord Yahweh shall put you to death, and for his servants he shall call another name"** (v15) — Hebrew שְׁבוּעָה "oath" (a name used in a sworn curse: "curse" in Changes only); וֶהֱמִיתְךָ "and he shall put you (singular) to death": perhaps the words of that oath ("and may Lord Yahweh put you to death"); וְלַעֲבָדָיו יִקְרָא שֵׁם אַחֵר, "for his servants he shall call another name".
- **"That he who blesseth himself in the earth shall bless himself in the God of truth; and he that sweareth in the earth shall swear by the God of truth" → "so that he who blesses himself in the land shall bless himself by the Elohim of amen …"** (v16) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי אָמֵן, the word "amen" ("firm, so be it"); "truth" is אֱמֶת; אֶרֶץ "land" (or "earth"); נִשְׁבַּע "swear". **"because the former troubles are forgotten, and because they are hid from mine eyes"** — צָרוֹת "distresses"; כִּי … וְכִי "because … and because".
- **"For, behold, I create new heavens and a new earth: and the former shall not be remembered, nor come into mind" → "For behold, I am creating new heavens and a new earth; and the former things shall not be remembered, and they shall not come up on the heart"** (v17) — Hebrew הִנְנִי בוֹרֵא, a participle (also v18); שָׁמַיִם חֲדָשִׁים וָאָרֶץ חֲדָשָׁה without articles (66:22 has them); הָרִאשֹׁנוֹת "the former (things)"; עָלָה עַל־לֵב, literally "come up upon the heart" (= come to mind). 2 Pet 3:13; Rev 21:1 take up the words.
- **"But be ye glad and rejoice for ever … I create Jerusalem a rejoicing, and her people a joy" → "But exult and be glad perpetually … I am creating Jerusalem a joy, and her people an exultation"** (v18) — Hebrew כִּי־אִם "but"; שׂוּשׂ "exult", גִּיל "be glad" (v18–19, as 1 Chr 16:31); עֲדֵי־עַד "perpetually" (as 26:4); גִּילָה "joy"; מָשׂוֹשׂ "exultation" (8:6 has "rejoicing" for מְשׂוֹשׂ).
- **"no more heard in her, nor the voice of crying" → "in her … the voice of outcry"** (v19) — Hebrew בָּהּ "in her"; קוֹל … וְקוֹל "the voice … and the voice"; זְעָקָה "outcry".
- **"There shall be no more thence an infant of days, nor an old man that hath not filled his days: for the child shall die an hundred years old; but the sinner being an hundred years old shall be accursed" → "There shall no more be from there an infant of days, and an old man who does not fill his days; for the boy shall die a hundred years old, and the sinner a hundred years old shall be cursed"** (v20) — Hebrew מִשָּׁם "from there"; עוּל יָמִים, עוּל "suckling": an infant who lives (only) days; הַנַּעַר "the boy"; בֶּן־מֵאָה שָׁנָה, literally "a son of a hundred years"; הַחוֹטֶא "the sinner" (also read "the one who misses (the hundred)"); יְקֻלָּל, pual of קִלֵּל, "be cursed" (lit. "be made light of").
- **"build houses, and inhabit them" → "build houses and dwell"** (v21–22) — Hebrew וְיָשָׁבוּ, "them" is not written; יָשַׁב "dwell".
- **"for as the days of a tree are the days of my people, and mine elect shall long enjoy the work of their hands" → "for like the days of the tree are the days of my people, and the work of their hands my chosen ones shall wear out"** (v22) — Hebrew הָעֵץ with the article; יְבַלּוּ, piel of בָּלָה "wear out" (= use to the end; "long enjoy" is the sense taken); the Hebrew order.
- **"They shall not labour in vain, nor bring forth for trouble; for they are the seed of the blessed of the LORD, and their offspring with them" → "and they shall not bear for dismay; for the seed of the blessed of Yahweh are they"** (v23) — Hebrew יָגַע "labor"; לָרִיק "in vain" (lit. "for emptiness"); יָלַד "bear"; בֶּהָלָה "dismay" (as Lev 26:16); זֶרַע "seed"; צֶאֱצָאִים "offspring" (lit. "what comes out").
- **"And it shall come to pass, that before they call, I will answer" → "And it shall be: before they call, I will answer"** (v24) — Hebrew וְהָיָה; וַאֲנִי אֶעֱנֶה … וַאֲנִי אֶשְׁמָע, literally "and I, I will answer … and I, I will hear".
- **"The wolf and the lamb shall feed together, and the lion shall eat straw like the bullock: and dust shall be the serpent's meat. They shall not hurt nor destroy" → "The wolf and the lamb shall graze as one, and the lion like cattle shall eat straw, and the serpent — dust is its bread. They shall not do evil and they shall not ruin"** (v25) — Hebrew רָעָה "graze"; כְאֶחָד "as one"; טָלֶה "lamb" (as 1 Sam 7:9; 11:6 has כֶּבֶשׂ); וְאַרְיֵה כַּבָּקָר יֹאכַל־תֶּבֶן and לֹא־יָרֵעוּ וְלֹא־יַשְׁחִיתוּ בְּכָל־הַר קָדְשִׁי are word for word 11:7, 9, and have the same wording; עָפָר לַחְמוֹ "dust is its bread" (לֶחֶם "bread", = food). Hebrew order kept.

### Notes
- **v1–2** — quoted in Rom 10:20–21.
- **v17** — "new heavens and a new earth": taken up in 2 Pet 3:13; Rev 21:1.
- **v11** — Gad and Meni: gods of fortune and destiny.
- **v25** — beside 11:6–9.
