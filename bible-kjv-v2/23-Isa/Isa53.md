# Isaiah 53

[1] Who has believed what they heard from us?
And to whom has the arm of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ been revealed?

[2] For he grew up before him like a young plant,
and like a root out of dry ground;
he had no form or majesty that we should look at him,
and no beauty that we should desire him.

[3] He was despised and rejected by men,
a man of sorrows and acquainted with grief;
and as one from whom men hide their faces
he was despised, and we esteemed him not.

[4] Surely he has borne our griefs
and carried our sorrows;
yet we esteemed him stricken,
smitten by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and afflicted.

[5] But he was pierced for our transgressions;
he was crushed for our iniquities;
upon him was the chastisement that brought us peace,
and with his wounds we are healed.

[6] All we like sheep have gone astray;
we have turned — every one — to his own way;
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has laid on him
the iniquity of us all.

[7] He was oppressed, and he was afflicted,
yet he opened not his mouth;
like a lamb that is led to the slaughter,
and like a sheep that before its shearers is silent,
so he opened not his mouth.

[8] By oppression and judgment he was taken away;
and as for his generation, who considered
that he was cut off out of the land of the living,
stricken for the transgression of my people?

[9] And they made his grave with the wicked
and with a rich man in his death,
although he had done no violence,
and there was no deceit in his mouth.

[10] Yet it was the will of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to crush him;
he has put him to grief;
when his soul makes a guilt offering,
he shall see his offspring; he shall prolong his days;
the will of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall prosper in his hand.

[11] Out of the anguish of his soul he shall see and be satisfied;
by his knowledge shall the righteous one, my servant,
make many to be accounted righteous,
and he shall bear their iniquities.

[12] Therefore I will divide him a portion with the many,
and he shall divide the spoil with the strong,
because he poured out his soul to death
and was numbered with the transgressors;
yet he bore the sin of many,
and makes intercession for the transgressors.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (vv6, 10) — Tetragrammaton preserved at key moments: "Yahweh has laid on him the iniquity of us all" (v6); "it was the will of Yahweh to crush him" (v10).
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4) — "smitten by *Elohim*, and afflicted" — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים preserved.
- **"griefs" / "sorrows" → "griefs" / "sorrows"** (v4) — Hebrew חֳלָיֵנוּ *cholayenu* and מַכְאֹבֵינוּ *mach'ovenu*; traditional rendering retained; noting that Matt 8:17 quotes this as "diseases and pains" (physical healing), while the Hebrew may support both spiritual and physical dimension.
- **"transgression" → "transgressions" / "sin"** — KJV and this version align; key vocabulary retained.
- **Poetic structure** — entire chapter formatted as verse; Fourth Servant Song climax (continuation from 52:13). Five strophes structurally visible: vv1-3 (servant's rejection), vv4-6 (vicarious suffering), vv7-9 (submission and burial), vv10-11 (vindication), v12 (triumph).

### Notes
- **Isaiah 53** — the climactic Suffering Servant passage; New Testament applies it to Yeshua (Matt 8:17; Mark 15:28; Luke 22:37; John 12:38; Acts 8:32-35; Rom 10:16; 1 Pet 2:22-25).
- **v10 *asham*** — guilt/reparation offering (cf. Lev 5:14-19).
- **Traditional Jewish readings**: collective Israel, or righteous remnant/prophet; rabbinic sources also include messianic readings.
- **Dead Sea Scrolls (1QIsaᵃ)** — preserves this chapter nearly identically to MT.
