# Isaiah 12

[1] And you shall say in that day:

"I will give thanks to you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
for you were angry with me;
may your anger turn back,
and may you comfort me.

[2] Behold, ⟦el|El|God⟧ is my salvation;
I will trust and will not dread;
for my strength and song is ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧ ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|GOD⟧,
and he has become my salvation."

[3] And you shall draw water with joy
from the springs of salvation.

[4] And you shall say in that day:

"Give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
call on his name,
make known among the peoples his doings,
mention that his name is set on high.

[5] Make music to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for he has done exaltation;
this is made known in all the earth.

[6] Cry aloud and ring out, inhabitant of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
for great in your midst is the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Layout** — v3 is now two lines, as the two halves of the Hebrew verse (it was printed as one prose line).
- **"And in that day thou shalt say" → "And you shall say in that day"** (v1, 4) — וְאָמַרְתָּ (singular) in v1, וַאֲמַרְתֶּם (plural) in v4; בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא "in that day".
- **"O LORD, I will praise thee: though thou wast angry with me, thine anger is turned away, and thou comfortedst me" → "I will give thanks to you, Yahweh, for you were angry with me; may your anger turn back, and may you comfort me"** (v1) — Hebrew order (the name after "you"); no "O" (vocative row); הוֹדָה "give thanks to"; כִּי "for" ("though" is not written); יָשֹׁב is the short (jussive) form, "may it turn back" (שׁוּב), and וּתְנַחֲמֵנִי, with simple "and", follows it as a wish: "and may you comfort me" (נִחַם piel). The versions and KJV read both as past ("turned away … comforted", from memory); the MT pointing is kept.
- **"Behold, God is my salvation" → "Behold, El is my salvation"** (v2) — הִנֵּה אֵל יְשׁוּעָתִי (also read "the El of my salvation"); יְשׁוּעָה "salvation" (v2, 3). **"I will trust, and not be afraid" → "I will trust and will not dread"** — בָּטַח "trust"; פָּחַד "dread" (not יָרֵא "fear").
- **"for the LORD JEHOVAH is my strength and my song; he also is become my salvation" → "for my strength and song is Yah Yahweh, and he has become my salvation"** (v2) — the line of Exod 15:2 (finished wording kept): עָזִּי וְזִמְרָת "my strength and song" (זִמְרָת has no "my"; "song", or "might": uncertain, as Exod 15:2), here with both names, יָהּ יְהוָה *Yah Yahweh*, in one token as before (commas removed: the Hebrew has none); וַיְהִי־לִי לִישׁוּעָה, literally "and he became to me for salvation". Also Ps 118:14.
- **"Therefore with joy shall ye draw water out of the wells of salvation" → "And you shall draw water with joy from the springs of salvation"** (v3) — וּשְׁאַבְתֶּם "and you (plural) shall draw" (no "therefore"); שָׂשׂוֹן "joy"; מַעְיָנֵי "springs" (מַעְיָן; בְּאֵר is "well"); הַיְשׁוּעָה with the article.
- **"Praise the LORD, call upon his name, declare his doings among the people" → "Give thanks to Yahweh, call on his name, make known among the peoples his doings"** (v4) — הוֹדוּ "give thanks to"; קָרָא בְשֵׁם "call on the name"; הוֹדִיעוּ "make known"; עַמִּים plural; עֲלִילֹתָיו "his doings". **"make mention that his name is exalted" → "mention that his name is set on high"** — הִזְכִּירוּ "mention" (literally "cause to be remembered"); נִשְׂגָּב "set on high" (שָׂגַב, as 9:11).
- **"Sing unto the LORD; for he hath done excellent things: this is known in all the earth" → "Make music to Yahweh, for he has done exaltation; this is made known in all the earth"** (v5) — זַמְּרוּ "make music" (זִמֵּר); גֵאוּת "exaltation" (as 9:18; Ps 93:1), the object of עָשָׂה: "done exaltation" = acted in majesty. Qere מוּדַעַת "made known" (hofal participle); the written text מידעת is another form of the same verb ("known"); no wish ("let") is written.
- **"Cry out and shout, thou inhabitant of Zion" → "Cry aloud and ring out, inhabitant of Zion"** (v6) — צַהֲלִי "cry aloud" (as 10:30), רָנַן "ring out" (verb of רִנָּה "a ringing cry"); no "O"; יוֹשֶׁבֶת feminine ("she who dwells in Zion"). **"great is the Holy One of Israel in the midst of thee" → "great in your midst is the Holy One of Israel"** — Hebrew order.

### Notes
- **Yeshuah** (v2, 3) — יְשׁוּעָה "salvation"; the same root as the name *Yeshua*.
- **Closes chapters 7–12** — the song of the people in that day, with the words of the Song of the Sea (Exod 15:2).
