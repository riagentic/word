# Isaiah 43

[1] And now thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
he who created you, ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and he who formed you, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:
"Do not fear, for I have redeemed you;
I have called by your name, you are mine.

[2] When you pass through the waters, I am with you;
and through the rivers, they shall not overflow you;
when you walk through fire, you shall not be scorched,
and the flame shall not burn against you.

[3] For I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, your Savior;
I have given ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ as your ransom,
⟦cush|Kush|Cush⟧ and ⟦seba|Seva|Seba⟧ for you.

[4] Since you are precious in my eyes,
you are honored, and I have loved you;
and I give man for you,
and folks for your soul.

[5] Do not fear, for I am with you;
from the east I will bring your seed,
and from the west I will gather you.

[6] I will say to the north, 'Give!'
and to the south, 'Do not keep back!'
Bring my sons from afar,
and my daughters from the end of the earth,

[7] everyone who is called by my name,
and for my glory I have created him,
I have formed him, yes, I have made him."

[8] Bring out a blind people, and there are eyes,
and deaf ones, and they have ears!

[9] All the nations have gathered together,
and let folks be assembled.
Who among them can tell this,
and make us hear former things?
Let them give their witnesses, and be righteous,
and let them hear and say, "Truth."

[10] "You are my witnesses," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
"and my servant whom I have chosen,
so that you may know and believe me,
and understand that I am he.
Before me no ⟦el|el|god⟧ was formed,
and after me there shall be none.

[11] I, I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and apart from me there is no savior.

[12] I have told, and I have saved, and I have made it heard,
and there is no stranger among you;
and you are my witnesses," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "and I am ⟦el|El|God⟧.

[13] Also from the day I am he,
and there is none who delivers from my hand;
I work, and who turns it back?"

[14] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, your Redeemer, the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:

"For your sake I have sent to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧,
and I have brought down all of them as fugitives,
and the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ — in the ships their ringing cry.

[15] I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, your Holy One,
the Creator of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, your King."

[16] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
who gives a way in the sea,
and a track in strong waters,

[17] who brings out chariots and horses,
force and strength;
together they lie down, they shall not rise;
they are extinguished, like a wick they are quenched:

[18] "Do not remember former things,
and to ancient things do not give heed.

[19] Behold, I am doing a new thing;
now it springs up; do you not know it?
Yes, I will set a way in the wilderness,
rivers in the desert.

[20] The living things of the field shall honor me,
jackals and daughters of the ostrich,
for I have given water in the wilderness,
rivers in the desert,
to give drink to my people, my chosen,

[21] the people which I formed for myself;
my praise they shall recount.

[22] And it is not me that you have called, ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
for you have grown weary of me, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[23] You have not brought me the sheep of your burnt offerings,
and with your sacrifices you have not honored me;
I have not made you serve with a grain offering,
and I have not made you weary with frankincense.

[24] You have not bought me cane with silver,
and with the fat of your sacrifices you have not drenched me;
only, you have made me serve with your sins,
you have made me weary with your iniquities.

[25] I, I am he
who wipes out your transgressions for my own sake,
and your sins I will not remember.

[26] Remind me; let us enter into judgment together;
recount, you, so that you may be righteous.

[27] Your first father sinned,
and your interpreters transgressed against me.

[28] And I will profane holy princes,
and I will give ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ to a thing devoted to destruction,
and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to revilings."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"God" → "El"** (v10, 12) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*: in v12 "I am El" (token as a name); in v10 "no el was formed" (the same word of another being, small letters, as Exod 34:14).
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order.
- **"But now" → "And now"** (v1) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה. **"that created thee, O Jacob, and he that formed thee, O Israel" → "he who created you, Jacob, and he who formed you, Israel"** — בֹּרַאֲךָ … וְיֹצֶרְךָ, participles; בָּרָא "create", יָצַר "form", עָשָׂה "make" (v7) kept apart; no "O". **"Fear not" → "Do not fear"** — אַל־תִּירָא (v1, 5). **"I have called thee by thy name" → "I have called by your name"** — קָרָאתִי בְשִׁמְךָ has no "you": "I have called (out) your name" (as Exod 31:2 "I have called by name Bezalel"). **"thou art mine" → "you are mine"** — לִי־אָתָּה, literally "to me you are".
- **"I will be with thee" → "I am with you"** (v2) — Hebrew אִתְּךָ־אָנִי, no verb (as v5). **"they shall not overflow thee"** — שָׁטַף "overflow". **"thou shalt not be burned" → "you shall not be scorched"** — תִכָּוֶה, כָּוָה "scorch"; **"neither shall the flame kindle upon thee" → "and the flame shall not burn against you"** — לֹא תִבְעַר־בָּךְ, בָּעַר בְּ as 42:25.
- **"thy Saviour"** (v3) — Hebrew מוֹשִׁיעֶךָ, participle of הוֹשִׁיעַ "save" (as v11, 12). **"I gave Egypt for thy ransom" → "I have given Egypt as your ransom"** — נָתַתִּי כָפְרְךָ, כֹּפֶר "ransom" (the price that covers a life); a perfect. **"Ethiopia and Seba for thee" → "Cush and Seba for you"** — כּוּשׁ (the name, token); תַּחְתֶּיךָ "for you" (lit. "in your place"; v4 the same).
- **"Since thou wast precious in my sight, thou hast been honourable" → "Since you are precious in my eyes, you are honored"** (v4) — Hebrew מֵאֲשֶׁר יָקַרְתָּ בְעֵינַי נִכְבַּדְתָּ: "in my eyes"; נִכְבַּד, nifal of כָּבֵד (root of כָּבוֹד "glory"): "be made heavy, be honored". **"and I have loved thee"** — וַאֲנִי אֲהַבְתִּיךָ, a perfect, "I" written out. **"therefore will I give men for thee, and people for thy life" → "and I give man for you, and folks for your soul"** — וְאֶתֵּן אָדָם תַּחְתֶּיךָ וּלְאֻמִּים תַּחַת נַפְשֶׁךָ: וְ "and"; אָדָם "man" (mankind, collective); לְאֹם "folk"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (not "life").
- **"I will bring thy seed from the east"** (v5) — Hebrew מִמִּזְרָח אָבִיא זַרְעֶךָ, Hebrew order; זֶרַע "seed"; **"from the west"** — מִמַּעֲרָב, literally "from the setting (of the sun)".
- **"Give up" → "Give!"** (v6) — Hebrew תֵּנִי, "give" (feminine); **"Keep not back" → "Do not keep back!"** — אַל־תִּכְלָאִי, כָּלָא "keep back". **"to the south"** — לְתֵימָן, literally "to the right side" (facing east). **"bring my sons from far"** — הָבִיאִי בָנַי, בָּנִים "sons".
- **"for I have created him for my glory, I have formed him; yea, I have made him" → "and for my glory I have created him, I have formed him, yes, I have made him"** (v7) — Hebrew וְלִכְבוֹדִי בְּרָאתִיו יְצַרְתִּיו אַף־עֲשִׂיתִיו: וְ "and"; Hebrew order; three verbs (create, form, make).
- **"Bring forth the blind people that have eyes, and the deaf that have ears" → "Bring out a blind people, and there are eyes, and deaf ones, and they have ears!"** (v8) — Hebrew הוֹצִיא עַם־עִוֵּר וְעֵינַיִם יֵשׁ וְחֵרְשִׁים וְאָזְנַיִם לָמוֹ: הוֹצִיא is pointed as an infinitive (or a perfect, "he brought out"), taken as a command ("bring out"); no article ("a blind people"); the two clauses with וְ, literally "and eyes there are", "and ears are to them".
- **"Let all the nations be gathered together, and let the people be assembled" → "All the nations have gathered together, and let folks be assembled"** (v9) — Hebrew נִקְבְּצוּ (perfect) … וְיֵאָסְפוּ (jussive): kept as written (the perfect may also be read as a summons). **"who among them can declare this, and shew us former things?" → "Who among them can tell this, and make us hear former things?"** — יַגִּיד … יַשְׁמִיעֻנוּ. **"let them bring forth their witnesses, that they may be justified" → "Let them give their witnesses, and be righteous"** — יִתְּנוּ עֵדֵיהֶם וְיִצְדָּקוּ: נָתַן "give"; צָדַק "be righteous" (here in a case at law: be shown to be in the right). **"or let them hear, and say, It is truth" → "and let them hear and say, Truth."** — וְיִשְׁמְעוּ וְיֹאמְרוּ אֱמֶת: וְ "and"; one word, אֱמֶת "truth".
- **"Ye are my witnesses, saith the LORD, and my servant whom I have chosen" → "You are my witnesses, declares Yahweh, and my servant whom I have chosen"** (v10) — Hebrew אַתֶּם עֵדַי נְאֻם־יְהוָה וְעַבְדִּי אֲשֶׁר בָּחָרְתִּי: the plural "witnesses" and the singular "my servant" stand side by side as the Hebrew has them (the servant is Israel, as 41:8; 44:1). **"that ye may know"** — לְמַעַן "so that"; הֶאֱמִין לְ "believe me". **"I am he"** — אֲנִי הוּא (as 41:4; 43:13, 25). **"neither shall there be after me" → "and after me there shall be none"** — וְאַחֲרַי לֹא יִהְיֶה, literally "and after me there shall not be"; "none" for English. נוֹצַר "was formed", the verb of v1, 7, 21.
- **"I, even I, am the LORD" → "I, I am Yahweh"** (v11) — Hebrew אָנֹכִי אָנֹכִי יְהוָה, "even" not written (also v25). **"beside me there is no saviour" → "apart from me there is no savior"** — מִבַּלְעָדַי, בִּלְעֲדֵי "apart from".
- **"I have declared, and have saved, and I have shewed" → "I have told, and I have saved, and I have made it heard"** (v12) — Hebrew הִגַּדְתִּי וְהוֹשַׁעְתִּי וְהִשְׁמַעְתִּי; "it" supplied. **"when there was no strange god among you" → "and there is no stranger among you"** — וְאֵין בָּכֶם זָר: זָר "stranger, foreigner"; "god" is not written (others take it of a foreign god, as Deut 32:16 זָרִים); "when" is not written. **"that I am God" → "and I am El"** — וַאֲנִי־אֵל.
- **"Yea, before the day was I am he" → "Also from the day I am he"** (v13) — Hebrew גַּם־מִיּוֹם אֲנִי הוּא: מִיּוֹם "from the day" (= from of old, or from this day on: the Hebrew allows both). **"and there is none that can deliver out of my hand"** — וְאֵין מִיָּדִי מַצִּיל, הִצִּיל "deliver". **"I will work, and who shall let it?" → "I work, and who turns it back?"** — אֶפְעַל וּמִי יְשִׁיבֶנָּה, הֵשִׁיב "turn back".
- **"your redeemer"** (v14) — Hebrew גֹּאַלְכֶם. **"For your sake I have sent to Babylon"** — לְמַעַנְכֶם שִׁלַּחְתִּי בָבֶלָה, a perfect (no object: whom he sent is not said). **"and have brought down all their nobles" → "and I have brought down all of them as fugitives"** — וְהוֹרַדְתִּי בָרִיחִים כֻּלָּם: בָּרִיחַ "fugitive" (as 27:1); the consonants also give בְּרִיחִים "bars" (the bars of the gates); "nobles" is not the word. **"and the Chaldeans, whose cry is in the ships" → "and the Chaldeans — in the ships their ringing cry"** — וְכַשְׂדִּים בָּאֳנִיּוֹת רִנָּתָם, no verb; רִנָּה "a ringing cry" (of joy; here perhaps turned to wailing): the sense is uncertain.
- **"I am the LORD, your Holy One, the creator of Israel, your King"** (v15) — Hebrew אֲנִי יְהוָה קְדוֹשְׁכֶם בּוֹרֵא יִשְׂרָאֵל מַלְכְּכֶם.
- **"which maketh a way in the sea" → "who gives a way in the sea"** (v16) — Hebrew הַנּוֹתֵן, "gives". **"and a path in the mighty waters" → "and a track in strong waters"** — וּבְמַיִם עַזִּים נְתִיבָה; נְתִיבָה "track"; עַז "strong".
- **"Which bringeth forth the chariot and horse, the army and the power" → "who brings out chariots and horses, force and strength"** (v17) — Hebrew הַמּוֹצִיא רֶכֶב־וָסוּס חַיִל וְעִזּוּז: רֶכֶב, סוּס collectives; חַיִל of an army "force"; עִזּוּז "strength" (as 42:25). **"they shall lie down together, they shall not rise" → "together they lie down, they shall not rise"** — יַחְדָּו יִשְׁכְּבוּ בַּל־יָקוּמוּ, Hebrew order. **"they are extinct, they are quenched as tow" → "they are extinguished, like a wick they are quenched"** — דָּעֲכוּ כַּפִּשְׁתָּה כָבוּ; פִּשְׁתָּה the wick of 42:3.
- **"Remember ye not the former things, neither consider the things of old" → "Do not remember former things, and to ancient things do not give heed"** (v18) — Hebrew אַל־תִּזְכְּרוּ רִאשֹׁנוֹת וְקַדְמֹנִיּוֹת אַל־תִּתְבֹּנָנוּ: no articles; Hebrew order; הִתְבּוֹנֵן "give heed to".
- **"Behold, I will do a new thing" → "Behold, I am doing a new thing"** (v19) — Hebrew הִנְנִי עֹשֶׂה, "behold me, doing": a participle. **"now it shall spring forth; shall ye not know it?" → "now it springs up; do you not know it?"** — עַתָּה תִצְמָח הֲלוֹא תֵדָעוּהָ; צָמַח "spring up". **"I will even make a way in the wilderness" → "Yes, I will set a way in the wilderness"** — אַף אָשִׂים, שִׂים "set". **"rivers in the desert"** — בִּישִׁמוֹן, יְשִׁימוֹן "desert" (the עֲרָבָה of 40:3; 41:19 is "steppe").
- **"The beast of the field shall honour me, the dragons and the owls" → "The living things of the field shall honor me, jackals and daughters of the ostrich"** (v20) — Hebrew חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה תַּנִּים וּבְנוֹת יַעֲנָה: חַיָּה "living thing" (singular, collective); תַּנִּים "jackals" (not תַּנִּין "dragon"); בְּנוֹת יַעֲנָה "daughters of the ostrich" (the ostrich hens; as 13:21; 34:13). **"because I give waters" → "for I have given water"** — כִּי־נָתַתִּי, a perfect. **"to my people, my chosen"** — עַמִּי בְחִירִי, two nouns side by side ("my chosen people" in the KJV).
- **"This people have I formed for myself; they shall shew forth my praise" → "the people which I formed for myself; my praise they shall recount"** (v21) — Hebrew עַם־זוּ יָצַרְתִּי לִי תְּהִלָּתִי יְסַפֵּרוּ: זוּ relative "which"; סִפֵּר "recount"; Hebrew order.
- **"But thou hast not called upon me, O Jacob" → "And it is not me that you have called, Jacob"** (v22) — Hebrew וְלֹא־אֹתִי קָרָאתָ יַעֲקֹב: וְ "and"; אֹתִי stands first (emphasis: not me); קָרָא with an object, "call" (not קָרָא בְּשֵׁם "call on the name"). **"but thou hast been weary of me, O Israel" → "for you have grown weary of me, Israel"** — כִּי־יָגַעְתָּ בִּי; כִּי "for" (also read "but", "that"); יָגַע "grow weary" (as 40:28–31; hifil "make weary" v23, 24).
- **"Thou hast not brought me the small cattle of thy burnt offerings" → "You have not brought me the sheep of your burnt offerings"** (v23) — Hebrew שֵׂה עֹלֹתֶיךָ; שֶׂה "sheep" (one animal of the flock, sheep or goat; singular, collective here). **"neither hast thou honoured me with thy sacrifices" → "and with your sacrifices you have not honored me"** — Hebrew order; כִּבֵּד "honor". **"I have not caused thee to serve with an offering" → "I have not made you serve with a grain offering"** — הֶעֱבַדְתִּיךָ בְּמִנְחָה; מִנְחָה "grain offering". **"nor wearied thee with incense" → "and I have not made you weary with frankincense"** — הוֹגַעְתִּיךָ בִּלְבוֹנָה; לְבוֹנָה "frankincense".
- **"Thou hast bought me no sweet cane with money" → "You have not bought me cane with silver"** (v24) — Hebrew לֹא־קָנִיתָ לִּי בַכֶּסֶף קָנֶה: a play on קָנָה "buy" and קָנֶה "cane, reed" (the reed of 42:3; here the aromatic cane, as Exod 30:23); כֶּסֶף "silver" (here money paid). **"neither hast thou filled me with the fat of thy sacrifices" → "and with the fat of your sacrifices you have not drenched me"** — הִרְוִיתָנִי, hifil of רָוָה "drink one's fill": "saturate, drench". **"but thou hast made me to serve with thy sins" → "only, you have made me serve with your sins"** — אַךְ הֶעֱבַדְתַּנִי, the verb of v23 turned back on Yahweh (kept, not softened to "burdened"); אַךְ "only". **"thou hast wearied me with thine iniquities" → "you have made me weary with your iniquities"** — הוֹגַעְתַּנִי, the verb of v23.
- **"I, even I, am he that blotteth out thy transgressions" → "I, I am he who wipes out your transgressions"** (v25) — Hebrew אָנֹכִי אָנֹכִי הוּא מֹחֶה פְשָׁעֶיךָ; מָחָה "wipe out" (of writing "blot out"). **"and will not remember thy sins" → "and your sins I will not remember"** — Hebrew order.
- **"Put me in remembrance" → "Remind me"** (v26) — Hebrew הַזְכִּירֵנִי, hifil of זָכַר, "cause me to remember". **"let us plead together" → "let us enter into judgment together"** — נִשָּׁפְטָה יָחַד, nifal of שָׁפַט. **"declare thou, that thou mayest be justified" → "recount, you, so that you may be righteous"** — סַפֵּר אַתָּה לְמַעַן תִּצְדָּק: אַתָּה written out; סִפֵּר "recount"; צָדַק "be righteous" (as v9).
- **"Thy first father hath sinned, and thy teachers have transgressed against me" → "Your first father sinned, and your interpreters transgressed against me"** (v27) — Hebrew אָבִיךָ הָרִאשׁוֹן חָטָא וּמְלִיצֶיךָ פָּשְׁעוּ בִי: מֵלִיץ "interpreter" (as Gen 42:23: one who speaks between two parties; "spokesmen", "mediators" are its senses here); פָּשַׁע בְּ "transgress against". Who the "first father" is (Jacob, Abraham, Adam) is not said.
- **"Therefore I have profaned the princes of the sanctuary" → "And I will profane holy princes"** (v28) — Hebrew וַאֲחַלֵּל שָׂרֵי קֹדֶשׁ: pointed with the simple וְ (not the narrative form): "and I will profane" (many read the past, "so I profaned": both are defended); שָׂרֵי קֹדֶשׁ "princes of holiness" = holy princes (no article; "of the sanctuary" is the other reading); חִלֵּל "profane". **"and have given Jacob to the curse" → "and I will give Jacob to a thing devoted to destruction"** — וְאֶתְּנָה לַחֵרֶם יַעֲקֹב; חֵרֶם "a thing devoted to destruction" (as Josh 6:17). **"and Israel to reproaches" → "and Israel to revilings"** — לְגִדּוּפִים, plural.

### Notes
- **v1** — Jacob / Israel created and formed: the verbs of Gen 1–2 (בָּרָא, יָצַר).
- **v10** — "my witnesses … my servant": Israel as the servant, as 41:8; 44:1, 21.
