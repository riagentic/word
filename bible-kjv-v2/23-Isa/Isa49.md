# Isaiah 49

[1] Listen to me, coastlands,
and give heed, folks, from afar.
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ from the belly called me;
from the inward parts of my mother he made mention of my name.

[2] And he set my mouth like a sharp sword;
in the shade of his hand he hid me;
and he set me for a polished arrow;
in his quiver he concealed me.

[3] And he said to me, "My servant you are,
⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, in whom I will glorify myself."

[4] But I said, "For an empty thing I have labored;
for emptiness and vapor I have spent my power;
surely my judgment is with ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and my wage with my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[5] And now says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
who formed me from the belly to be a servant to him,
to bring back ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ to him,
and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall be gathered to him —
and I shall be honored in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has been my strength —

[6] and he said:
"It is too light a thing that you should be a servant to me,
to raise up the tribes of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧
and to bring back the guarded ones of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
and I will give you for a light of nations,
to be my salvation to the end of the earth."

[7] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the Redeemer of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, his Holy One, to one despised of soul, to one whom the nation abominates, to a slave of rulers:

"Kings shall see and arise,
princes, and they shall bow down,
on account of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who is faithful,
the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ — and he has chosen you."

[8] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"In a time of acceptance I have answered you,
and in a day of salvation I have helped you;
and I will guard you and give you for a covenant of people,
to raise up the land,
to make inherit desolate inheritances,

[9] saying to the bound, 'Go out,'
to those who are in darkness, 'Be uncovered.'
On the ways they shall feed,
and on all bare heights shall be their pasture.

[10] They shall not hunger and shall not thirst,
and burning heat and sun shall not strike them,
for he who has compassion on them shall lead them,
and to springs of water shall guide them.

[11] And I will set all my mountains for the way,
and my highways shall be raised high.

[12] Behold, these shall come from afar,
and behold, these from the north and from the west,
and these from the land of ⟦sinim|Sinim|Syene⟧."

[13] Shout for joy, heavens, and be glad, earth,
and break forth, mountains, into a ringing cry!
For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has comforted his people,
and on his afflicted ones he will have compassion.

[14] And ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ said, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has forsaken me,
and ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ has forgotten me."

[15] "Will a woman forget her nursing child,
so as not to have compassion on the son of her belly?
Even these may forget,
but I will not forget you.

[16] Behold, on the palms I have engraved you;
your walls are before me continually.

[17] Your sons hasten;
those who tear you down and those who lay you waste go out from you.

[18] Lift up your eyes around and see:
all of them have gathered, they have come to you.
As I live, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
surely all of them like ornaments you shall put on,
and you shall bind them on like the bride.

[19] For your ruins and your desolate places
and the land of your tearing down —
for now you shall be too narrow for the inhabitant,
and those who swallow you shall be far away.

[20] Yet they shall say in your ears,
the sons of your bereavement:
'The place is too narrow for me;
make room for me, and let me dwell.'

[21] And you shall say in your heart:
'Who has begotten these for me,
and I was bereaved and barren,
an exile and turned aside?
And these, who has brought them up?
Behold, I was left alone;
these — where are they?'"

[22] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:

"Behold, I will lift up my hand to nations,
and to peoples I will raise my banner;
and they shall bring your sons in the lap,
and your daughters on the shoulder shall be carried.

[23] And kings shall be your foster fathers,
and their princesses your nurses;
with faces to the earth they shall bow down to you,
and the dust of your feet they shall lick;
and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:
those who wait for me shall not be ashamed."

[24] Shall booty be taken from a mighty one,
or the captives of a righteous one escape?

[25] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:
"Even the captives of a mighty one shall be taken,
and the booty of a ruthless one shall escape;
and with the one who contends with you I will contend,
and your sons I will save.

[26] And I will make those who wrong you eat their flesh,
and as with sweet wine with their blood they shall be drunk;
and all flesh shall know
that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, your Savior,
and your Redeemer, the Mighty One of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"Lord" → "Adonai"** (v14) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v22) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words. **"God" → "Elohim"** (v4, 5).
- **Vocatives** (v1, 13) — no "O": the Hebrew has no word for it ("Listen to me, coastlands"; "Shout for joy, heavens").
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew colon per printed line, in the Hebrew order: v8 "and I will guard you and give you for a covenant of people" is one colon (no longer split); v20 has the Hebrew order of its two first lines ("Yet they shall say in your ears, the sons of your bereavement").
- **The servant passage (v1–6), word for word** — the words of the speaker ("me", "my") and of Yahweh to him are kept as the Hebrew has them; עֶבֶד is "servant" where Yahweh speaks of his own (v3, 5, 6; the עֶבֶד row), "slave" of rulers (v7). Who the speaker is the verses do not say beyond v3.
- **"Listen, O isles, unto me; and hearken, ye people, from far" → "Listen to me, coastlands, and give heed, folks, from afar"** (v1) — Hebrew שִׁמְעוּ אִיִּים אֵלַי (שָׁמַע אֶל "listen to"; אִי "coastland", its row), וְהַקְשִׁיבוּ לְאֻמִּים (הִקְשִׁיב "give heed"; לְאֹם "folk", reviewer row). **"The LORD hath called me from the womb; from the bowels of my mother hath he made mention of my name" → "Yahweh from the belly called me; from the inward parts of my mother he made mention of my name"** — מִבֶּטֶן "from the belly" (the בֶּטֶן row; also v5, 15), מִמְּעֵי "from the inward parts" (מֵעִים); הִזְכִּיר שְׁמִי "made mention of my name" (hifil of זָכַר, the "mention" row); the Hebrew order kept.
- **"And he hath made my mouth like a sharp sword" → "And he set my mouth like a sharp sword"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיָּשֶׂם, שִׂים "set" (also וַיְשִׂימֵנִי "and he set me"), "and" kept. **"in the shadow of his hand" → "in the shade of his hand"** — צֵל, the "shade" row (also 51:16). **"hath he hid me … hath he hid me" → "he hid me … he concealed me"** — two verbs: הֶחְבִּיאָנִי (הֶחְבִּיא "hide", its row) and הִסְתִּירָנִי (הִסְתִּיר, the verb of "hide the face", 50:6); "concealed" keeps them apart here. **"a polished shaft" → "a polished arrow"** — חֵץ בָּרוּר, בָּרוּר "purified, cleaned" (= polished or sharpened). **"in his quiver"** — אַשְׁפָּה.
- **"Thou art my servant, O Israel, in whom I will be glorified" → "My servant you are, Israel, in whom I will glorify myself"** (v3) — Hebrew עַבְדִּי־אָתָּה יִשְׂרָאֵל אֲשֶׁר־בְּךָ אֶתְפָּאָר. "Israel" stands in the Masoretic Text and is printed here as it stands; whether it is spoken to the servant ("Israel") or said of him ("you are my servant, Israel") the Hebrew leaves open. אֶתְפָּאָר, hitpael of פָּאַר (root of תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty"): "glorify myself, show my beauty"; it is not the root of כָּבוֹד (v5).
- **"Then I said" → "But I said"** (v4) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי אָמַרְתִּי, the pronoun set against v3. **"I have laboured in vain, I have spent my strength for nought, and in vain" → "For an empty thing I have labored; for emptiness and vapor I have spent my power"** — לְרִיק "for an empty thing" (רִיק, its row), לְתֹהוּ "for emptiness" (תֹּהוּ, of what is not land), וְהֶבֶל "and vapor" (its row; "vanity" in KJV); כֹּחַ "power" (its row); כִּלָּה piel, "finish, use up". **"yet surely my judgment is with the LORD, and my work with my God" → "surely my judgment is with Yahweh, and my wage with my Elohim"** — אָכֵן "surely" (no "yet"); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (its row; "right" in Changes); פְּעֻלָּה of what is earned, "wage" (its row).
- **"that formed me from the womb to be his servant" → "who formed me from the belly to be a servant to him"** (v5) — Hebrew לְעֶבֶד לוֹ. **"to bring Jacob again to him" → "to bring back Jacob to him"** — לְשׁוֹבֵב, polel of שׁוּב (v6 has the hifil לְהָשִׁיב: both "bring back"). **"Though Israel be not gathered" → "and Israel shall be gathered to him"** — the qere is לוֹ "to him", and the verse follows it (OT.md); the ketiv לֹא gives "and Israel is not gathered" (KJV). **"yet shall I be glorious in the eyes of the LORD" → "and I shall be honored in the eyes of Yahweh"** — וְאֶכָּבֵד, nifal of כָּבֵד with "and" (not "for", not "yet"). **"and my God shall be my strength" → "and my Elohim has been my strength"** — הָיָה, perfect; עֹז "strength" (its row). The two last lines stand between "says Yahweh" and "and he said" (v6): the dashes mark them, as the earlier text had it.
- **"And he said"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר, wayyiqtol: "and he said" (was "he says"). **"It is a light thing that thou shouldest be my servant" → "It is too light a thing that you should be a servant to me"** — נָקֵל מִהְיוֹתְךָ לִי עֶבֶד, מִן of comparison ("too light for your being"). **"to restore the preserved of Israel" → "to bring back the guarded ones of Israel"** — the qere וּנְצוּרֵי, passive participle of נָצַר "guard" (its row); the ketiv ונצירי ("the preserved ones") has the same sense. **"I will also give thee for a light to the Gentiles" → "and I will give you for a light of nations"** — וּנְתַתִּיךָ לְאוֹר גּוֹיִם, נָתַן "give" (was "make … as"), no article. **"that thou mayest be my salvation unto the end of the earth" → "to be my salvation to the end of the earth"** — לִהְיוֹת יְשׁוּעָתִי, an infinitive without a subject: "that you may be my salvation" or "that my salvation may be (reach)"; the Hebrew leaves it open, and so does the verse.
- **Acts 13:47** — Paul and Barnabas quote v6b from the Greek: Τέθεικά σε εἰς φῶς ἐθνῶν τοῦ εἶναί σε εἰς σωτηρίαν ἕως ἐσχάτου τῆς γῆς, "I have set you for a light of nations, for you to be for salvation unto the end of the earth" (as that verse is printed). The Greek has "I have set", "you" as the subject of "to be", and "for salvation" without "my"; the Hebrew has "I will give you", no subject for "to be", and "my salvation". Each text is translated as it stands.
- **"the Redeemer of Israel, and his Holy One" → "the Redeemer of Israel, his Holy One"** (v7) — Hebrew גֹּאֵל יִשְׂרָאֵל קְדוֹשׁוֹ, no "and". **"to him whom man despiseth, to him whom the nation abhorreth, to a servant of rulers" → "to one despised of soul, to one whom the nation abominates, to a slave of rulers"** — לִבְזֹה־נֶפֶשׁ, literally "to a despising of soul" (despised in soul, or despised by people: the noun נֶפֶשׁ "soul", not "man"); לִמְתָעֵב גּוֹי, a piel participle of תִּעֵב "abominate" (its row), pointed active ("to one abominating", or with the sense "made abominable to the nation"); לְעֶבֶד מֹשְׁלִים "to a slave of rulers" (עֶבֶד of men: "slave", its row). **"princes also shall worship" → "princes, and they shall bow down"** — שָׂרִים וְיִשְׁתַּחֲוּוּ, הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down". **"because of the LORD that is faithful" → "on account of Yahweh, who is faithful"** — לְמַעַן "on account of"; נֶאֱמָן "faithful" (its row). **"and he shall choose thee" → "and he has chosen you"** — וַיִּבְחָרֶךָּ, wayyiqtol with "and".
- **"In an acceptable time have I heard thee" → "In a time of acceptance I have answered you"** (v8) — Hebrew בְּעֵת רָצוֹן (רָצוֹן "acceptance", its row), עֲנִיתִיךָ "I have answered you" (עָנָה "answer"); 2 Cor 6:2 quotes v8a from the Greek. **"and I will preserve thee" → "and I will guard you"** — וְאֶצָּרְךָ, from נָצַר "guard" (or from יָצַר "form": the form allows both; Isa 42:6 has the same pair). **"for a covenant of the people" → "for a covenant of people"** — לִבְרִית עָם, no article. **"to establish the earth" → "to raise up the land"** — לְהָקִים אֶרֶץ, הֵקִים "raise up" (as v6). **"to cause to inherit the desolate heritages" → "to make inherit desolate inheritances"** — לְהַנְחִיל נְחָלוֹת שֹׁמֵמוֹת (הִנְחִיל "make inherit", נַחֲלָה "inheritance": their rows); who is made to inherit is not said.
- **"to the prisoners … Shew yourselves" → "to the bound … Be uncovered"** (v9) — Hebrew אֲסוּרִים "bound ones" (as "the house of the bound"); הִגָּלוּ, nifal of גָּלָה "uncover": "be uncovered, show yourselves". **"They shall feed in the ways, and their pastures shall be in all high places" → "On the ways they shall feed, and on all bare heights shall be their pasture"** — יִרְעוּ (רָעָה of the flock itself, "feed": its row); שְׁפָיִים "bare heights"; מַרְעִיתָם singular; "and" kept.
- **"They shall not hunger nor thirst; neither shall the heat nor sun smite them" → "They shall not hunger and shall not thirst, and burning heat and sun shall not strike them"** (v10) — Hebrew לֹא … וְלֹא … וְלֹא; שָׁרָב "burning heat" (also taken as "mirage"), וָשָׁמֶשׁ, no article; הִכָּה "strike". **"for he that hath mercy on them shall lead them, even by the springs of water shall he guide them" → "for he who has compassion on them shall lead them, and to springs of water shall guide them"** — מְרַחֲמָם, participle of רִחַם "have compassion"; יְנַהֲגֵם, piel of נָהַג "drive, lead" (of a shepherd, "lead"); יְנַהֲלֵם, נֵהֵל "guide" (its row); וְעַל־מַבּוּעֵי מַיִם "and to springs of water" (עַל "upon, by, to").
- **"And I will make all my mountains a way" → "And I will set all my mountains for the way"** (v11) — Hebrew וְשַׂמְתִּי … לַדָּרֶךְ, with the article. **"and my highways shall be exalted" → "and my highways shall be raised high"** — מְסִלָּה "highway" (its row), יְרֻמוּן from רוּם.
- **"from the north and from the west"** (v12) — Hebrew מִצָּפוֹן וּמִיָּם, literally "and from the sea" (the Directions row). **"from the land of Sinim"** — the token kept; where the land lies is not certain (Syene in the south of Egypt is commonly proposed; the Isaiah scroll reads סונים, from memory).
- **"Sing, O heavens; and be joyful, O earth; and break forth into singing, O mountains" → "Shout for joy, heavens, and be glad, earth, and break forth, mountains, into a ringing cry"** (v13) — Hebrew רָנּוּ (רָנַן "shout for joy"), וְגִילִי (גִּיל "be glad"); the qere is וּפִצְחוּ "and break forth" (פָּצַח, its row), the ketiv יפצחו "let them break forth"; רִנָּה "a ringing cry" (its row). **"and will have mercy upon his afflicted" → "and on his afflicted ones he will have compassion"** — וַעֲנִיָּו יְרַחֵם, in the Hebrew order.
- **"But Zion said" → "And Zion said"** (v14) — Hebrew וַתֹּאמֶר. **"and my Lord hath forgotten me" → "and Adonai has forgotten me"** — וַאדֹנָי, the divine title.
- **"Can a woman forget her sucking child, that she should not have compassion on the son of her womb?" → "Will a woman forget her nursing child, so as not to have compassion on the son of her belly?"** (v15) — Hebrew הֲתִשְׁכַּח, imperfect; מֵרַחֵם, מִן with the infinitive ("away from having compassion"); בֶּן־בִּטְנָהּ "the son of her belly". **"yet will I not forget thee" → "but I will not forget you"** — וְאָנֹכִי with the pronoun; "yea, they may forget" → "Even these may forget" (גַּם־אֵלֶּה).
- **"upon the palms of my hands" → "on the palms"** (v16) — Hebrew עַל־כַּפַּיִם, without "my hands" (the article is English). חָקַק "engrave". **"Behold"** — הֵן.
- **"Thy children shall make haste" → "Your sons hasten"** (v17) — Hebrew בָּנַיִךְ "your sons", as the Masoretic Text points it; "your builders" (the earlier text) follows the reading בוניך of the Isaiah scroll and the versions (from memory); the verse keeps the Masoretic Text. מִהֲרוּ, perfect, "hasten" (מִהַר, its row). **"thy destroyers and they that made thee waste shall go forth of thee" → "those who tear you down and those who lay you waste go out from you"** — מְהָרְסַיִךְ (הָרַס "tear down") and מַחֲרִבַיִךְ (hifil of חָרֵב, "lay waste": of a city; 50:2; 51:10 of the sea "dry up").
- **"all these gather themselves together, and come to thee" → "all of them have gathered, they have come to you"** (v18) — Hebrew perfects. **"As I live, saith the LORD" → "As I live, declares Yahweh"** — נְאֻם יְהוָה (the formula row). **"thou shalt surely clothe thee with them all, as with an ornament, and bind them on thee, as a bride doeth" → "surely all of them like ornaments you shall put on, and you shall bind them on like the bride"** — כִּי after the oath "surely"; עֲדִי "ornaments" (a collective singular, its row); וּתְקַשְּׁרִים, קִשֵּׁר "bind on"; כַּכַּלָּה, with the article.
- **"For thy waste and thy desolate places, and the land of thy destruction" → "For your ruins and your desolate places and the land of your tearing down —"** (v19) — Hebrew חָרְבֹתַיִךְ (חָרְבָּה "ruin", its row), שֹׁמְמֹתַיִךְ (participle of שָׁמֵם "be desolate"), הֲרִסֻתֵךְ (noun of הָרַס, v17); the sentence breaks off, and a second כִּי starts again: the dash keeps it. **"shall even now be too narrow by reason of the inhabitants" → "for now you shall be too narrow for the inhabitant"** — כִּי עַתָּה תֵּצְרִי מִיּוֹשֵׁב, יוֹשֵׁב singular. **"and they that swallowed thee up" → "and those who swallow you"** — מְבַלְּעָיִךְ, piel participle of בָּלַע.
- **"The children which thou shalt have, after thou hast lost the other" → "the sons of your bereavement"** (v20) — Hebrew בְּנֵי שִׁכֻּלָיִךְ, literally "sons of your bereavement" (the sons born when she was bereaved); בְּאָזְנַיִךְ "in your ears" (its row). **"give place to me that I may dwell" → "make room for me, and let me dwell"** — גְּשָׁה־לִּי, literally "draw near for me" (= move up, make room; the literal words would say "come to me" in English); וְאֵשֵׁבָה, a cohortative with "and"; יָשַׁב "dwell".
- **"Who hath begotten me these, seeing I have lost my children, and am desolate, a captive, and removing to and fro?" → "Who has begotten these for me, and I was bereaved and barren, an exile and turned aside?"** (v21) — Hebrew מִי יָלַד־לִי אֶת־אֵלֶּה, יָלַד of the one who gives the children ("begotten": who the father was; "borne" would make the bereaved one the mother); וַאֲנִי שְׁכוּלָה וְגַלְמוּדָה, a clause with "and" ("and I was …"); גַּלְמוּד "barren" (the Job 3 row); גֹּלָה, participle of גָּלָה "go into exile"; סוּרָה "turned aside" (סוּר, its row). **"and who hath brought up these?" → "And these, who has brought them up?"** — וְאֵלֶּה מִי גִדֵּל, the Hebrew order; גִּדֵּל of children "bring up" (lit. "make great": the row's sense for persons; said in Changes). **"these, where had they been?" → "these — where are they?"** — אֵלֶּה אֵיפֹה הֵם, a noun clause.
- **"and set up my standard to the people" → "and to peoples I will raise my banner"** (v22) — Hebrew אָרִים נִסִּי, נֵס "banner" (its row; the reviewer keeps "banner" outside Num 26:10); עַמִּים plural, no article; also גּוֹיִם "nations" without the article. **"in their arms" → "in the lap"** — בְּחֹצֶן, no "their": חֹצֶן "lap, bosom of a garment" (the Ps 129:7 row). **"upon their shoulders" → "on the shoulder"** — עַל־כָּתֵף, singular, no "their".
- **"their queens thy nursing mothers" → "their princesses your nurses"** (v23) — Hebrew שָׂרוֹתֵיהֶם, feminine of שַׂר "prince" (not מַלְכָּה "queen"); מֵינִיקֹת "nurses" (the מֵינֶקֶת row). **"they shall bow down to thee with their face toward the earth" → "with faces to the earth they shall bow down to you"** — אַפַּיִם אֶרֶץ, literally "nostrils to the earth" (the אַף row), in the Hebrew order. **"for they shall not be ashamed that wait for me" → "those who wait for me shall not be ashamed"** — אֲשֶׁר לֹא־יֵבֹשׁוּ קוָֹי, literally "(I) whose waiters shall not be ashamed"; קִוָּה "wait for", בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed" (their rows).
- **"or the lawful captive delivered?" → "or the captives of a righteous one escape?"** (v24) — Hebrew שְׁבִי צַדִּיק, "the captives of a righteous one", as the Masoretic Text has it (the Isaiah scroll, the Greek and the Syriac read עריץ "a ruthless one", as v25: from memory; the earlier "tyrant" followed them); יִמָּלֵט, nifal of מָלַט "escape" (its row). מַלְקוֹחַ "booty" and שְׁבִי "captives" (their rows; was "prey"); גִּבּוֹר "mighty one" (its row).
- **"the prey of the terrible shall be delivered" → "the booty of a ruthless one shall escape"** (v25) — Hebrew עָרִיץ "ruthless one" (its row). **"for I will contend with him that contendeth with thee" → "and with the one who contends with you I will contend"** — וְאֶת־יְרִיבֵךְ אָנֹכִי אָרִיב, "and", the Hebrew order, יָרִיב singular, רִיב "contend". **"and I will save thy children" → "and your sons I will save"** — בָּנַיִךְ "your sons".
- **"I will feed them that oppress thee with their own flesh" → "And I will make those who wrong you eat their flesh"** (v26) — Hebrew וְהַאֲכַלְתִּי אֶת־מוֹנַיִךְ, hifil of יָנָה "wrong" (the הוֹנָה row; לָחַץ is "oppress"); בְּשָׂרָם "their flesh", no "own". **"as with sweet wine"** — כֶעָסִיס (also "juice", Song 8:2), in the Hebrew order. **"that I the LORD am thy Saviour" → "that I am Yahweh, your Savior"** — כִּי אֲנִי יְהוָה מוֹשִׁיעֵךְ, a noun clause that can be read either way; מוֹשִׁיעַ participle of יָשַׁע "save". **"the mighty One of Jacob"** — אֲבִיר יַעֲקֹב (its row).

### Notes
- **v1–6** — the servant speaks (v1–5) and Yahweh answers (v6); v6b is quoted in Acts 13:47, v8a in 2 Cor 6:2.
