# Isaiah 17

[1] The burden of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧.

Behold, ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧ is removed from being a city,
and it shall be a heap of ruin.

[2] Forsaken are the cities of Aroer;
for droves they shall be,
and they shall lie down, and there is none who makes them tremble.

[3] And the fortress shall cease from ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧,
and the kingdom from ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧,
and the remnant of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧;
like the glory of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ they shall be,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[4] And it shall be in that day: the glory of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ shall be brought low,
and the fatness of his flesh shall grow lean.

[5] And it shall be as when one gathers the harvest, the standing grain,
and his arm reaps ears;
and it shall be as one who gleans ears
in the valley of ⟦rephaim|Refaim|Rephaim⟧.

[6] And gleanings shall be left in it,
as at the beating of an olive tree:
two, three berries
in the top of the highest branch,
four, five
in its boughs, the fruitful one,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[7] In that day man shall look to his Maker,
and his eyes shall look on the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[8] And he shall not look to the altars, the work of his hands,
and what his fingers have made he shall not look on,
and the Asherim and the incense altars.

[9] In that day the cities of his strong place shall be
like the forsaken place of the wood and of the highest branch,
which they forsook from the face of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
and it shall be a desolation.

[10] For you have forgotten the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of your salvation,
and the Rock of your strong place you have not remembered;
therefore you plant plantings of pleasant ones,
and with the slip of a stranger you sow it.

[11] In the day of your planting you make it grow,
and in the morning you make your seed bud;
a heap is the harvest in the day of sickness
and of incurable pain.

[12] Woe, the multitude of many peoples —
like the roaring of the seas they roar;
and the crashing of folks —
like the crashing of mighty waters they crash.

[13] Folks — like the crashing of many waters they crash;
and he shall rebuke it, and it shall flee far off,
and it shall be chased like the chaff of the mountains before the wind,
and like a whirl before the tempest.

[14] At the time of evening — and behold, terror;
before morning he is not.
This is the share of those who pillage us,
and the lot for those who plunder us.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v3) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v6, 10) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order, by the accents; v9 is now four lines (the old first line held two cola).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept (v3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 13).
- **"saith the LORD" → "declares Yahweh"** (v3, 6) — Hebrew נְאֻם יְהוָה, literally "the declaration of Yahweh".
- **"The burden of Damascus"** (v1) — Hebrew מַשָּׂא, "burden", literally "what is lifted up" (from נָשָׂא "lift up": lifted up by the voice, or laid on the prophet); many translations have "oracle". **"is taken away from being a city" → "is removed from being a city"** — מוּסָר, hofal of סוּר "turn aside", "made to turn aside". **"a ruinous heap" → "a heap of ruin"** — מְעִי (only here; from עִי "heap of ruins") and מַפָּלָה "a ruin" (literally "a falling").
- **"The cities of Aroer are forsaken: they shall be for flocks, which shall lie down, and none shall make them afraid" → "Forsaken are the cities of Aroer; for droves they shall be, and they shall lie down, and there is none who makes them tremble"** (v2) — Hebrew order kept (the participle עֲזֻבוֹת first); עֲדָרִים "droves" (עֵדֶר, a herd or flock); מַחֲרִיד hifil of חָרַד "tremble", "one who makes tremble"; "them" is supplied (as Lev 26:6). The Septuagint has "forsaken forever" (as if עֲדֵי עַד for עָרֵי עֲרֹעֵר; from memory); the MT is kept.
- **"The fortress also shall cease … and the remnant of Syria: they shall be as the glory" → "And the fortress shall cease … and the remnant of Aram; like the glory … they shall be"** (v3) — Hebrew וְנִשְׁבַּת "and shall cease"; אֲרָם "Aram" (the token has "Syria"). The accents close the first half after "the remnant of Aram", so the three nouns may all go with "shall cease"; the remnant may also be the subject of "they shall be" (plural verb). Both readings are left open. **"the children of Israel" → "the sons of Israel"** — בְּנֵי.
- **"And in that day it shall come to pass, that the glory of Jacob shall be made thin" → "And it shall be in that day: the glory of Jacob shall be brought low"** (v4) — Hebrew וְהָיָה בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא; יִדַּל, from דָּלַל "be low" (also "be thin"). **"the fatness of his flesh shall wax lean" → "shall grow lean"** — מִשְׁמַן "fatness", יֵרָזֶה "grow lean".
- **"as when the harvestman gathereth the corn, and reapeth the ears with his arm" → "as when one gathers the harvest, the standing grain, and his arm reaps ears"** (v5) — Hebrew כֶּאֱסֹף קָצִיר קָמָה, literally "as the gathering of harvest, standing grain"; קָצִיר is "harvest" (many read it as "harvester", the pointing of קֹצֵר); "one" is supplied for the English infinitive. וּזְרֹעוֹ … יִקְצוֹר "and his arm … reaps": the arm is the subject. **"as he that gathereth ears" → "as one who gleans ears"** — מְלַקֵּט, piel of לָקַט "gather up", "glean" as Ruth 2; kept apart from אָסַף "gather" in the same verse.
- **"Yet gleaning grapes shall be left in it, as the shaking of an olive tree" → "And gleanings shall be left in it, as at the beating of an olive tree"** (v6) — Hebrew עוֹלֵלֹת "gleanings" (of a vine gone over again; "grapes" is not written); נֹקֶף "beating" (striking the tree). **"two or three … four or five" → "two, three … four, five"** — no "or". **"in the top of the uppermost bough" → "in the top of the highest branch"** — אָמִיר (only here and v9; meaning uncertain: "top branch"). **"in the outmost fruitful branches thereof" → "in its boughs, the fruitful one"** — בִּסְעִפֶיהָ פֹּרִיָּה, literally "in its boughs, fruitful (one)": the tree is meant; "outmost" is not written. **"the LORD God of Israel" → "Yahweh, the Elohim of Israel"** — אֱלֹהֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"shall a man look to his Maker, and his eyes shall have respect to" → "man shall look to his Maker, and his eyes shall look on"** (v7) — Hebrew הָאָדָם "man" (mankind, with the article); שָׁעָה עַל "look to" (as Gen 4:4); עֹשֵׂהוּ "his Maker", literally "the one making him"; in the second line רָאָה אֶל, literally "see toward", "look on".
- **"neither shall respect that which his fingers have made, either the groves, or the images" → "and what his fingers have made he shall not look on, and the Asherim and the incense altars"** (v8) — Hebrew order kept, the object first; לֹא יִרְאֶה "he shall not see / look on" (as v7). הָאֲשֵׁרִים "the Asherim" (wooden objects of worship bearing the goddess's name; not "groves"); הַחַמָּנִים "the incense altars" (meaning uncertain: perhaps "sun pillars", from חַמָּה "sun"). The two nouns are joined by "and … and" as written.
- **"shall his strong cities be as a forsaken bough, and an uppermost branch, which they left because of the children of Israel" → "the cities of his strong place shall be like the forsaken place of the wood and of the highest branch, which they forsook from the face of the sons of Israel"** (v9) — Hebrew עָרֵי מָעֻזּוֹ "the cities of his strong place" (מָעוֹז as Judg 6:26); כַּעֲזוּבַת "like the forsaken (place) of"; הַחֹרֶשׁ "the wood, the thicket" and הָאָמִיר "the highest branch" (as v6); the Septuagint has "the Amorites and the Hivites" here (from memory), the MT is kept. עָזַב "forsake" in both places; מִפְּנֵי "from the face of" (of flight). **"and there shall be desolation" → "and it shall be a desolation"** — וְהָיְתָה שְׁמָמָה, feminine: the land or the whole.
- **"Because thou hast forgotten … and hast not been mindful of the rock of thy strength" → "For you have forgotten … and the Rock of your strong place you have not remembered"** (v10) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; feminine singular "you" (the city or land), through v11; צוּר מָעֻזֵּךְ, "Rock" with a capital as a name of Yahweh, מָעוֹז "strong place" (as v9); Hebrew order kept. **"therefore shalt thou plant pleasant plants, and shalt set it with strange slips" → "therefore you plant plantings of pleasant ones, and with the slip of a stranger you sow it"** — נִטְעֵי נַעֲמָנִים "plantings of pleasant ones" (נַעֲמָן "the pleasant one" may name a god of the Adonis kind: uncertain); זְמֹרַת זָר "the slip (vine shoot) of a stranger"; תִּזְרָעֶנּוּ "you sow it". The imperfects are rendered present.
- **"In the day shalt thou make thy plant to grow, and in the morning shalt thou make thy seed to flourish" → "In the day of your planting you make it grow, and in the morning you make your seed bud"** (v11) — Hebrew בְּיוֹם נִטְעֵךְ "in the day of your planting"; תְּשַׂגְשֵׂגִי (only here; uncertain: "make grow", or "hedge in"); תַּפְרִיחִי "make bud" (פָּרַח). **"but the harvest shall be a heap in the day of grief and of desperate sorrow" → "a heap is the harvest in the day of sickness and of incurable pain"** — the MT points נֵד "heap" (as of the waters, Exod 15:8); read as נָד it would be "the harvest flees" (the reading of the old text of this chapter): the MT is kept. נַחֲלָה is pointed as "sickness" (from חָלָה); the same letters spell "inheritance". כְּאֵב אָנוּשׁ "incurable pain".
- **"Woe to the multitude of many people, which make a noise like the noise of the seas" → "Woe, the multitude of many peoples — like the roaring of the seas they roar"** (v12) — Hebrew הוֹי "Woe" with a noun, no "to"; הֲמוֹן "multitude" and כַּהֲמוֹת … יֶהֱמָיוּן "like the roaring … they roar" are one root (הָמָה). **"and to the rushing of nations, that make a rushing like the rushing of mighty waters" → "and the crashing of folks — like the crashing of mighty waters they crash"** — שְׁאוֹן … יִשָּׁאוּן, one root (שָׁאָה "crash"), three times; לְאֻמִּים "folks". The dashes mark the two nominal openings of the Hebrew.
- **"The nations shall rush … but God shall rebuke them, and they shall flee far off" → "Folks — like the crashing of many waters they crash; and he shall rebuke it, and it shall flee far off"** (v13) — Hebrew לְאֻמִּים "folks" (as v12); no "God" is written: the subject is "he"; בּוֹ "it" and וְנָס "it shall flee" are singular (the multitude); מִמֶּרְחָק literally "from a distance". **"like a rolling thing before the whirlwind" → "like a whirl before the tempest"** — גַּלְגַּל "whirl" (whirling dust or a wind-blown plant: uncertain; "wheel" is the same word); סוּפָה "tempest".
- **"And behold at eveningtide trouble; and before the morning he is not" → "At the time of evening — and behold, terror; before morning he is not"** (v14) — Hebrew לְעֵת עֶרֶב "at the time of evening", then וְהִנֵּה "and behold"; בַּלָּהָה "terror"; אֵינֶנּוּ "he is not" (masculine singular: the multitude or the enemy; "it" also possible). **"the portion of them that spoil us, and the lot of them that rob us" → "the share of those who pillage us, and the lot for those who plunder us"** — חֵלֶק "share"; שֹׁסִים "pillage", בָּזַז "plunder"; וְגוֹרָל לְבֹזְזֵינוּ "and the lot for those who plunder us" (לְ "for").
