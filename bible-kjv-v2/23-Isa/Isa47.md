# Isaiah 47

[1] Come down and sit on the dust,
virgin daughter of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧;
sit on the ground, there is no throne,
daughter of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧!
For you shall not again be called
tender and delicate.

[2] Take millstones and grind meal;
uncover your veil,
strip off the skirt, uncover the leg,
pass over rivers.

[3] Your nakedness shall be uncovered;
also your reproach shall be seen.
Vengeance I will take,
and I will not meet a man.

[4] Our Redeemer — ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts is his name —
the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[5] Sit silent and go into the darkness,
daughter of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧;
for you shall not again be called
mistress of kingdoms.

[6] I was wrathful with my people;
I profaned my inheritance,
and I gave them into your hand;
you set no compassion for them;
on the old man you made your yoke very heavy.

[7] And you said, "Forever I shall be mistress";
so that you did not set these on your heart;
you did not remember its end.

[8] And now hear this, lover of pleasures,
who dwells securely,
who says in her heart,
"I am, and there is none besides me;
I shall not dwell as a widow,
and I shall not know bereavement."

[9] And these two shall come to you in a moment, in one day:
bereavement and widowhood;
in their completeness they have come upon you,
in the multitude of your sorceries,
in the very great might of your enchantments.

[10] And you trusted in your evil;
you said, "There is none who sees me";
your wisdom and your knowledge, it has turned you back;
and you said in your heart,
"I am, and there is none besides me."

[11] And evil shall come upon you;
you shall not know its dawn;
and destruction shall fall upon you;
you shall not be able to make atonement for it;
and devastation shall come upon you suddenly,
which you do not know.

[12] Stand now in your enchantments
and in the multitude of your sorceries,
in which you have labored from your youth;
perhaps you will be able to profit;
perhaps you will cause terror.

[13] You are wearied with the multitude of your counsels;
let them stand now and save you,
the dividers of the heavens,
who gaze at the stars,
who make known at the new moons,
from what shall come upon you.

[14] Behold, they have become like stubble;
fire has burned them;
they shall not deliver their soul
from the hand of the flame;
there is no coal to warm them,
nor a blaze to sit before it.

[15] So have they become to you, those with whom you have labored,
your traders from your youth;
each to his own side they have wandered;
there is none who saves you.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** — Tetragrammaton preserved (chapter attributes title to God's name in context).
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v4) — divine title preserved; Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת *Yahweh Tzeva'ot*.
- **Layout** — one Hebrew line per printed line, in the Hebrew order; the "you" of the whole chapter is feminine (Babylon as a woman).
- **"O virgin daughter … O daughter" → "virgin daughter … daughter"** (v1, 5) — no "O" (the Hebrew has no word for it). **"sit in the dust" → "sit on the dust"** — עַל־עָפָר. **"sit on the ground: there is no throne"** — שְׁבִי־לָאָרֶץ אֵין־כִּסֵּא, two clauses ("without a throne" joined them). **"thou shalt no more be called" → "you shall not again be called"** (v1, 5) — לֹא תוֹסִיפִי יִקְרְאוּ־לָךְ, literally "you shall not add (that) they call you". **"tender and delicate"** — רַכָּה וַעֲנֻגָּה (רַךְ "tender", עָנֹג "delicate", as Deut 28:54, 56).
- **"Take the millstones, and grind meal" → "Take millstones and grind meal"** (v2) — Hebrew רֵחַיִם without the article (a pair of millstones, a hand mill); קֶמַח "meal". **"uncover thy locks, make bare the leg, uncover the thigh" → "uncover your veil, strip off the skirt, uncover the leg"** — גַּלִּי צַמָּתֵךְ (צַמָּה "veil", Song 4:1; "locks" is the other way of taking it) חֶשְׂפִּי־שֹׁבֶל (שֹׁבֶל "skirt, train", only here) גַּלִּי־שׁוֹק (שׁוֹק "leg"); גִּלָּה "uncover" is the same verb twice here and in v3 ("your nakedness shall be uncovered"), and is kept so. **"pass over the rivers" → "pass over rivers"** — עִבְרִי נְהָרוֹת, no article.
- **"yea, thy shame shall be seen" → "also your reproach shall be seen"** (v3) — Hebrew גַּם; חֶרְפָּה "reproach". **"I will not meet thee as a man" → "I will not meet a man"** — וְלֹא אֶפְגַּע אָדָם: פָּגַע "meet, fall upon, entreat"; the sense is uncertain ("I will spare no man", "no man shall intercede", or "I will not be entreated by a man"); the words are kept.
- **"As for our redeemer, the LORD of hosts is his name, the Holy One of Israel" → "Our Redeemer — Yahweh of Hosts is his name — the Holy One of Israel"** (v4) — Hebrew גֹּאֲלֵנוּ … קְדוֹשׁ יִשְׂרָאֵל, a cry without a verb; "is" only in the middle line, where the Hebrew has שְׁמוֹ "his name".
- **"Sit thou silent, and get thee into darkness" → "Sit silent and go into the darkness"** (v5) — שְׁבִי דוּמָם וּבֹאִי בַחֹשֶׁךְ (the article). **"The lady of kingdoms" → "mistress of kingdoms"** — גְּבֶרֶת (as v7; Gen 16:4).
- **"I was wroth … I have polluted mine inheritance" → "I was wrathful … I profaned my inheritance"** (v6) — Hebrew קָצַפְתִּי (קָצַף "be wrathful"); חִלַּלְתִּי (חִלֵּל "profane"); נַחֲלָה "inheritance". **"thou didst shew them no mercy" → "you set no compassion for them"** — לֹא־שַׂמְתְּ לָהֶם רַחֲמִים, literally "you did not set compassion for them"; רַחֲמִים "compassion". **"upon the ancient hast thou very heavily laid thy yoke" → "on the old man you made your yoke very heavy"** — עַל־זָקֵן (זָקֵן "old man", collective; the "elder" of a town is not meant) הִכְבַּדְתְּ (הִכְבִּיד "make heavy") עֻלֵּךְ מְאֹד.
- **"I shall be a lady for ever" → "Forever I shall be mistress"** (v7) — the Hebrew order. **"so that thou didst not lay these things to thy heart, neither didst remember the latter end of it" → "so that you did not set these on your heart; you did not remember its end"** — עַד לֹא־שַׂמְתְּ אֵלֶּה עַל־לִבֵּךְ: עַד is literally "until" (here "so far that"; the Isaiah scroll reads עוֹד, "forever … still", from memory; MT kept); שִׂים עַל־לֵב "set on the heart"; לֹא זָכַרְתְּ without "and"; אַחֲרִיתָהּ "its end" (of "these").
- **"Therefore hear now this, thou that art given to pleasures, that dwellest carelessly, that sayest in thine heart" → "And now hear this, lover of pleasures, who dwells securely, who says in her heart"** (v8) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה "and now"; עֲדִינָה "lover of pleasures" (from עָדַן "delight, luxury"); הַיּוֹשֶׁבֶת לָבֶטַח, הָאֹמְרָה בִּלְבָבָהּ: participles with the article, and "in her heart", third person (v10 has "in your heart"). **"I am, and none else beside me" → "I am, and there is none besides me"** (v8, 10) — אֲנִי וְאַפְסִי עוֹד, literally "I, and my nothing still" (the ־ִי is the old ending of אֶפֶס, or "me"); the words that Yahweh says of himself in 45:5, 6, 18, 21. **"I shall not sit as a widow" → "I shall not dwell as a widow"** — אֵשֵׁב, the verb of "who dwells" above. **"the loss of children" → "bereavement"** (v8, 9) — שְׁכוֹל (the loss of children is what the word says; "of children" is not written).
- **"But these two things" → "And these two"** (v9) — Hebrew וְתָבֹאנָה … שְׁתֵּי־אֵלֶּה; "things" is not written. **"they shall come upon thee in their perfection" → "in their completeness they have come upon you"** — כְּתֻמָּם בָּאוּ עָלַיִךְ, perfect. **"for the multitude of thy sorceries, and for the great abundance of thine enchantments" → "in the multitude of your sorceries, in the very great might of your enchantments"** — בְּרֹב כְּשָׁפַיִךְ בְּעָצְמַת חֲבָרַיִךְ מְאֹד: בְּ "in" (also "for", or "in spite of": open); עָצְמָה "might"; כְּשָׁפִים "sorceries", חֲבָרִים "enchantments" (literally "bindings", spells), as v12.
- **"For thou hast trusted in thy wickedness" → "And you trusted in your evil"** (v10) — Hebrew וַתִּבְטְחִי, wayyiqtol with "and"; רָעָה "evil" (v10, 11). **"None seeth me" → "There is none who sees me"** — אֵין רֹאָנִי. **"it hath perverted thee" → "it has turned you back"** — שׁוֹבְבָתֶךְ, polel of שׁוּב "turn back" (= lead astray).
- **"Therefore shall evil come upon thee" → "And evil shall come upon you"** (v11) — Hebrew וּבָא. **"thou shalt not know from whence it riseth" → "you shall not know its dawn"** — שַׁחְרָהּ: from שַׁחַר "dawn" (where it rises), or from another root שָׁחַר "charm away" ("you shall not know how to charm it away"): uncertain. **"mischief shall fall upon thee; thou shalt not be able to put it off" → "destruction shall fall upon you; you shall not be able to make atonement for it"** — הֹוָה "destruction"; כַּפְּרָהּ (כִּפֶּר "make atonement, cover"; here: buy it off with a ransom). **"desolation … which thou shalt not know" → "devastation … which you do not know"** — שׁוֹאָה "devastation"; לֹא תֵדָעִי.
- **"Stand now with thine enchantments" → "Stand now in your enchantments"** (v12) — Hebrew עִמְדִי־נָא בַחֲבָרַיִךְ; נָא "now". **"if so be thou shalt be able to profit, if so be thou mayest prevail" → "perhaps you will be able to profit; perhaps you will cause terror"** — אוּלַי "perhaps"; הוֹעִיל "profit" (as 44:9, 10); תַּעֲרוֹצִי (עָרַץ "cause terror").
- **"Thou art wearied in the multitude of thy counsels" → "You are wearied with the multitude of your counsels"** (v13) — Hebrew נִלְאֵית. **"Let now the astrologers, the stargazers, the monthly prognosticators, stand up, and save thee from these things that shall come upon thee" → "let them stand now and save you, the dividers of the heavens, who gaze at the stars, who make known at the new moons, from what shall come upon you"** — the Hebrew order: the subjects follow the verbs, and מֵאֲשֶׁר "from what" closes the verse (it belongs to "save you"). Qere הֹבְרֵי שָׁמַיִם "the dividers of the heavens" (הָבַר, only here: "divide", the reading of the sky by its parts, uncertain); the written text (ketiv) הברו reads as a verb, "they divided". הַחֹזִים בַּכּוֹכָבִים "who gaze at the stars"; לֶחֳדָשִׁים "at the new moons" (or "month by month").
- **"Behold, they shall be as stubble; the fire shall burn them" → "Behold, they have become like stubble; fire has burned them"** (v14) — Hebrew perfects הָיוּ … שְׂרָפָתַם; אֵשׁ without the article. **"they shall not deliver themselves from the power of the flame" → "they shall not deliver their soul from the hand of the flame"** — אֶת־נַפְשָׁם "their soul" (= themselves); מִיַּד "from the hand of". **"there shall not be a coal to warm at, nor fire to sit before it" → "there is no coal to warm them, nor a blaze to sit before it"** — אֵין־גַּחֶלֶת לַחְמָם אוּר לָשֶׁבֶת נֶגְדּוֹ: גַּחֶלֶת "coal" (as 44:19); לַחְמָם "to warm them" (infinitive of חָמַם; the same letters say "their bread", 44:15, 19: a play on words); אוּר "blaze" (as 44:16); one אֵין for both: "nor" carries it to the second.
- **"Thus shall they be unto thee with whom thou hast laboured, even thy merchants" → "So have they become to you, those with whom you have labored, your traders"** (v15) — Hebrew כֵּן הָיוּ־לָךְ, perfect; אֲשֶׁר יָגָעַתְּ has no preposition ("with whom" is supplied; v12 has בַּאֲשֶׁר "in which"); סֹחֲרַיִךְ "your traders". **"they shall wander every one to his quarter; none shall save thee" → "each to his own side they have wandered; there is none who saves you"** — אִישׁ לְעֶבְרוֹ תָּעוּ (עֵבֶר "side"); אֵין מוֹשִׁיעֵךְ.

### Notes
- **"I am, and there is none besides me"** (v8, 10) — Babylon's blasphemous parody of Yahweh's self-identification.
