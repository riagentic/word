# Isaiah 35

[1] The wilderness and the dry land shall exult;
and the steppe shall rejoice and bud like the crocus.

[2] It shall surely bud
and rejoice, yes, with rejoicing and ringing out;
the glory of the ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ has been given to it,
the splendor of the ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧ and the Sharon.
They shall see the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
the splendor of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] Make strong the slack hands,
and the stumbling knees strengthen.

[4] Say to those hasty of heart,
"Be strong; do not fear!
Behold, your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!
Vengeance shall come,
the dealing of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
he shall come and save you."

[5] Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened,
and the ears of the deaf shall be opened.

[6] Then the lame shall leap like the deer,
and the tongue of the mute shall ring out.
For waters have broken out in the wilderness,
and wadis in the steppe;

[7] and the burning sand shall become a pool,
and the thirsty ground springs of water;
in the habitation of jackals, its lying place,
grass shall become reed and papyrus.

[8] And a highway shall be there, and a way,
and the Holy Way it shall be called;
the unclean shall not pass over it,
and it shall be for them.
The one who walks the way, and the senseless, shall not wander.

[9] No lion shall be there,
and the violent one of the living things shall not go up on it;
it shall not be found there;
and the redeemed shall walk.

[10] And the ransomed of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall return
and come to ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ with a ringing cry,
and everlasting gladness upon their head;
joy and gladness they shall overtake,
and sorrow and sighing shall flee.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v2, 10) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v8 now breaks at the main pause (after "for them") as the accents have it.
- **"shall be glad for them" → "shall exult"** (v1) — Hebrew יְשֻׂשׂוּם, of שׂוּשׂ "exult"; the final ם is read as "for them" (KJV) or as a letter carried over before the מ of מִדְבָּר: uncertain, left out of the verse. **"the solitary place … the desert" → "the dry land … the steppe"** — צִיָּה "dry land", עֲרָבָה "steppe" (dry country; also v6). **"rejoice, and blossom as the rose" → "rejoice and bud like the crocus"** — תָּגֵל, גִּיל "rejoice"; תִפְרַח, פָּרַח "bud" (the row); חֲבַצֶּלֶת "crocus" (a meadow flower, as Song 2:1; uncertain; not a rose).
- **"It shall blossom abundantly" → "It shall surely bud"** (v2) — Hebrew פָּרֹחַ תִּפְרַח, infinitive absolute with its verb: "surely" (lit. "budding it shall bud"). **"rejoice even with joy and singing" → "rejoice, yes, with rejoicing and ringing out"** — וְתָגֵל אַף גִּילַת וְרַנֵּן: the one root גִּיל twice ("rejoice … rejoicing"), אַף "yes", רַנֵּן infinitive of רָנַן "ring out" (as v6). **"shall be given" → "has been given"** — perfect נִתַּן. **"the excellency of Carmel and Sharon … the excellency of our God" → "the splendor of the Carmel and the Sharon … the splendor of our Elohim"** — הֲדַר, הָדָר "splendor" (twice); הַלְּבָנוֹן, הַכַּרְמֶל, הַשָּׁרוֹן have the article. **"they"** — הֵמָּה, stressed.
- **"Strengthen ye the weak hands, and confirm the feeble knees" → "Make strong the slack hands, and the stumbling knees strengthen"** (v3) — Hebrew חַזְּקוּ (חִזֵּק "make strong") … אַמֵּצוּ (אִמֵּץ "strengthen"): two roots, kept apart; רָפוֹת "slack"; כֹּשְׁלוֹת "stumbling" (כָּשַׁל); the second line keeps the Hebrew order, verb last.
- **"them that are of a fearful heart" → "those hasty of heart"** (v4) — Hebrew נִמְהֲרֵי־לֵב, of מָהַר "hasten": hurried, rash or anxious of heart. **"fear not" → "do not fear"**. **"behold, your God will come with vengeance, even God with a recompence" → "Behold, your Elohim! Vengeance shall come, the dealing of Elohim"** — הִנֵּה אֱלֹהֵיכֶם נָקָם יָבוֹא, word for word "behold your Elohim, vengeance shall come"; "with" is not in the Hebrew; נָקָם is the subject of יָבוֹא (or an adverbial "with vengeance": the reading of KJV; uncertain). גְּמוּל "dealing" (the row: what one has done, here paid back). **"He will come and save you" → "he shall come and save you"** — הוּא יָבוֹא, "he" written.
- **"shall be unstopped" → "shall be opened"** (v5) — word for word: Hebrew תִּפָּקַחְנָה (פָּקַח, the word for opening eyes) and תִּפָּתַחְנָה (פָּתַח "open"); English has one word for both, the second line keeps the Hebrew order. Matt 11:5; Luke 7:22 take up v5–6.
- **"shall the lame man leap as an hart, and the tongue of the dumb sing" → "the lame shall leap like the deer, and the tongue of the mute shall ring out"** (v6) — word for word: פִּסֵּחַ "lame" ("man" not written); כָּאַיָּל with the article; אִלֵּם "mute"; תָרֹן, רָנַן "ring out". **"shall waters break out … and streams" → "waters have broken out … and wadis"** — נִבְקְעוּ, perfect nifal of בָּקַע (lit. "have been split open"); נְחָלִים "wadis".
- **"And the parched ground shall become a pool" → "and the burning sand shall become a pool"** (v7) — Hebrew שָׁרָב (also 49:10, "scorching heat"; here the glare of the hot ground, the "mirage": uncertain); אֲגַם "pool"; צִמָּאוֹן "thirsty ground"; מַבּוּעֵי מָיִם "springs of water" (lit. "gushings"). **"in the habitation of dragons, where each lay, shall be grass with reeds and rushes" → "in the habitation of jackals, its lying place, grass shall become reed and papyrus"** — תַּנִּים "jackals"; רִבְצָהּ "its lying place" (רָבַץ "lie"); חָצִיר לְקָנֶה וָגֹמֶא, with the לְ of the first line ("become"); חָצִיר "grass" (34:13 has the same letters for "abode": uncertain); קָנֶה "reed", גֹּמֶא "papyrus".
- **"And an highway shall be there, and a way, and it shall be called The way of holiness" → "And a highway shall be there, and a way, and the Holy Way it shall be called"** (v8) — Hebrew מַסְלוּל (only here, beside מְסִלָּה "highway"); דֶּרֶךְ הַקֹּדֶשׁ, קֹדֶשׁ as a genitive "holy" (lit. "the way of holiness"), first in the clause as in the Hebrew; יִקָּרֵא לָהּ "shall be called to it". **"but it shall be for those: the wayfaring men, though fools, shall not err therein" → "and it shall be for them. The one who walks the way, and the senseless, shall not wander"** — וְהוּא־לָמוֹ "and it (is) for them" (who "they" are is not said); הֹלֵךְ דֶּרֶךְ וֶאֱוִילִים לֹא יִתְעוּ word for word; "though" and "therein" are not in the Hebrew; the sense is open ("whoever walks the way — even fools — shall not go astray", or "fools shall not wander on it"). אֱוִיל "senseless one" (as Proverbs); תָּעָה "wander".
- **"nor any ravenous beast shall go up thereon" → "and the violent one of the living things shall not go up on it"** (v9) — Hebrew פְּרִיץ חַיּוֹת, פָּרִיץ "violent one" (robber, Jer 7:11), חַיָּה "living thing" (the row). **"it shall not be found there"** — singular. **"but the redeemed shall walk there" → "and the redeemed shall walk"** — וְהָלְכוּ גְּאוּלִים; "there" is not in the Hebrew. גָּאַל "redeem", פָּדָה "ransom" (v10).
- **"with songs and everlasting joy upon their heads" → "with a ringing cry, and everlasting gladness upon their head"** (v10) — Hebrew בְּרִנָּה "with a ringing cry" (singular); שִׂמְחַת עוֹלָם "everlasting gladness" (lit. "gladness of the age"); רֹאשָׁם "their head" (singular). **"they shall obtain joy and gladness" → "joy and gladness they shall overtake"** — שָׂשׂוֹן וְשִׂמְחָה first; יַשִּׂיגוּ, הִשִּׂיג "overtake" (as Deut 28:2). **"flee away" → "flee"** — נָסוּ. The verse stands again at 51:11.

### Notes
- **v5–6** — quoted by Yeshua in Matt 11:5; Luke 7:22 as messianic sign.
- **Chapter** — the counterpart of ch. 34: the wilderness of Edom's ruin and the wilderness made to bud; the way home to Zion.
