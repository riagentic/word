# Isaiah 55

[1] "Ho, everyone who thirsts,
come to the waters;
and he who has no money,
come, buy and eat;
and come, buy without money and without price
wine and milk.

[2] Why do you weigh out money for what is not bread,
and your labor for what does not sate?
Surely listen to me, and eat what is good,
and let your soul delight itself in fatness.

[3] Incline your ear, and come to me;
hear, and your soul shall live;
and I will cut for you an everlasting covenant,
the sure ⟦steadfast-love|chasadim|acts of steadfast love⟧ of David.

[4] Behold, a witness to folks I have given him,
a leader and commander of folks.

[5] Behold, a nation you do not know you shall call,
and a nation that has not known you shall run to you,
on account of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and for the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, for he has beautified you.

[6] Seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ while he may be found;
call him while he is near.

[7] Let the wicked forsake his way,
and the man of wickedness his thoughts;
and let him return to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he will have compassion on him,
and to our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for he will abundantly pardon.

[8] For my thoughts are not your thoughts,
and your ways are not my ways, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[9] For the heavens are higher than the earth;
so my ways are higher than your ways,
and my thoughts than your thoughts.

[10] For as the rain comes down, and the snow, from the heavens,
and does not return there
except it has watered the earth
and made it bear and made it spring up,
and given seed to the sower
and bread to the eater,

[11] so shall my word be that goes out from my mouth;
it shall not return to me empty,
except it has done what I desired,
and prospered in that for which I sent it.

[12] For in gladness you shall go out,
and in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ you shall be led;
the mountains and the hills
shall break forth before you into a ringing cry,
and all the trees of the field shall clap the palm.

[13] Instead of the thornbush shall come up the cypress,
and instead of the nettle shall come up the myrtle;
and it shall be to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ for a name,
for an everlasting sign that shall not be cut off."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5, 7) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order. v1, 5, 10 and 12 were set in other places or merged; they now follow the accents (v10 has six lines).
- **"Ho, every one that thirsteth" → "Ho, everyone who thirsts"** (v1) — Hebrew הוֹי. The prophets' row gives הוֹי as "Woe"; here it is a call to come (the invitation of "come … buy … eat"), and "Woe" would say a lament or threat that the verse does not have; "Ho" is kept, as for an attention-cry (also Zech 2:6). צָמֵא "thirsty", singular, with plural commands. **"buy, and eat; yea, come, buy wine and milk without money and without price" → "buy and eat; and come, buy without money and without price wine and milk"** — Hebrew order and "and" (וּלְכוּ) kept; שָׁבַר "buy" (elsewhere "buy grain", Gen 41–47: here of food, wine and milk, so "buy"); כֶּסֶף "silver", here "money" (payment); מְחִיר "price".
- **"Wherefore do ye spend money" → "Why do you weigh out money"** (v2) — Hebrew תִשְׁקְלוּ־כֶסֶף, "weigh out silver" (שָׁקַל "weigh": silver was weighed). **"for that which is not bread" → "for what is not bread"** — בְּלוֹא־לֶחֶם, literally "for no-bread". **"and your labour for that which satisfieth not" → "and your labor for what does not sate"** — יְגִיעַ "labor"; בְּלוֹא לְשָׂבְעָה, literally "for not-to-satiety". **"hearken diligently unto me" → "Surely listen to me"** — שִׁמְעוּ שָׁמוֹעַ אֵלַי, the imperative with its infinitive absolute, literally "listen, listening, to me" (as 6:9). **"and let your soul delight itself in fatness"** — וְתִתְעַנַּג … נַפְשְׁכֶם, a jussive with "your soul" (singular) as subject; דֶּשֶׁן "fatness" (elsewhere the fat ashes of the altar).
- **"hear, and your soul shall live"** (v3) — Hebrew וּתְחִי, "and (so) it shall live". **"and I will make an everlasting covenant with you" → "and I will cut for you an everlasting covenant"** — וְאֶכְרְתָה לָכֶם בְּרִית עוֹלָם: כָּרַת בְּרִית "cut a covenant"; לָכֶם "for you"; עוֹלָם, literally "of the age". **"even the sure mercies of David" → "the sure chasadim of David"** — חַסְדֵי דָוִד הַנֶּאֱמָנִים, the plural of *chesed* (*chasadim*, "acts of steadfast love") with הַנֶּאֱמָנִים "the sure (ones)" (נֶאֱמָן, as 2 Sam 7:16 "your house shall be sure"); "even" is not in the Hebrew. "Of David" may be the *chasadim* shown to David (2 Sam 7:15) or by him: undecided. Acts 13:34 quotes the Greek "the holy (things) of David, the sure (ones)" (τὰ ὅσια Δαυὶδ τὰ πιστά).
- **"Behold, I have given him for a witness to the people" → "Behold, a witness to folks I have given him"** (v4) — Hebrew order; לְאוּמִּים, plural of לְאֹם "folk" (not עַם "people"), without the article both times. **"a leader and commander to the people" → "a leader and commander of folks"** — נָגִיד "leader"; מְצַוֵּה, participle of צִוָּה "command".
- **"Behold, thou shalt call a nation that thou knowest not" → "Behold, a nation you do not know you shall call"** (v5) — Hebrew order. **"and nations that knew not thee shall run unto thee" → "and a nation that has not known you shall run to you"** — גּוֹי singular, with plural verbs. **"because of the LORD thy God" → "on account of Yahweh your Elohim"** — לְמַעַן. **"and for the Holy One of Israel; for he hath glorified thee" → "and for the Holy One of Israel, for he has beautified you"** — פֵּאֲרָךְ, פֵּאֵר "beautify, adorn" (the root of תִּפְאֶרֶת "splendor", 52:1).
- **"call ye upon him" → "call him"** (v6) — Hebrew קְרָאֻהוּ, with a direct object ("call to him").
- **"and the unrighteous man his thoughts" → "and the man of wickedness his thoughts"** (v7) — Hebrew אִישׁ אָוֶן. **"and let him return unto the LORD, and he will have mercy upon him" → "and let him return to Yahweh, and he will have compassion on him"** — וְיָשֹׁב, jussive of שׁוּב "return"; רִחַם "have compassion". **"for he will abundantly pardon"** — כִּי־יַרְבֶּה לִסְלוֹחַ, literally "for he will multiply to pardon"; סָלַח "pardon" (said only of God).
- **"neither are your ways my ways" → "and your ways are not my ways"** (v8) — Hebrew וְלֹא. נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh".
- **"For as the heavens are higher than the earth, so are my ways higher" → "For the heavens are higher than the earth; so my ways are higher"** (v9) — Hebrew כִּי־גָבְהוּ שָׁמַיִם מֵאָרֶץ: there is no "as", only the "so" of the second line; גָּבַהּ "be high" (as 52:13).
- **"For as the rain cometh down, and the snow from heaven" → "For as the rain comes down, and the snow, from the heavens"** (v10) — Hebrew יֵרֵד, singular, before both nouns; שָׁמַיִם "heavens". **"and returneth not thither, but watereth the earth" → "and does not return there except it has watered the earth"** — כִּי אִם "except" after the negative (= without first having watered); the perfects of the כִּי אִם clause are kept ("has watered … made … given"). **"and maketh it bring forth and bud" → "and made it bear and made it spring up"** — הוֹלִידָהּ, hifil of יָלַד (elsewhere "beget", of a father; here of the earth "bear"); הִצְמִיחַ "cause to spring up".
- **"it shall accomplish that which I please" → "except it has done what I desired"** (v11) — Hebrew כִּי אִם־עָשָׂה, as v10; חָפַצְתִּי, חָפֵץ "desire" (as 53:10). **"and it shall prosper in the thing whereto I sent it" → "and prospered in that for which I sent it"** — וְהִצְלִיחַ, hifil of צָלַח ("prosper", or "make prosper" with an object: "and made what I sent it for prosper").
- **"For ye shall go out with joy" → "For in gladness you shall go out"** (v12) — Hebrew order; שִׂמְחָה "gladness". **"and be led forth with peace" → "and in shalom you shall be led"** — תּוּבָלוּן, hofal of יָבַל "be led" (as 53:7). **"shall break forth before you into singing" → "shall break forth before you into a ringing cry"** — רִנָּה. **"shall clap their hands" → "shall clap the palm"** — יִמְחֲאוּ־כָף, כַּף "palm", singular.
- **"Instead of the thorn shall come up the fir tree" → "Instead of the thornbush shall come up the cypress"** (v13) — נַעֲצוּץ "thornbush"; בְּרוֹשׁ "cypress" (or juniper, fir; the tree is not certain). **"and instead of the brier" → "and instead of the nettle"** — the qere וְתַחַת "and instead of" is translated (written without "and"); סִרְפַּד, found only here: a nettle or brier (uncertain); הֲדַס "myrtle". **"and it shall be to the LORD for a name"** — וְהָיָה לַיהוָה לְשֵׁם, kept (the former "it shall make a name for Yahweh" changed the verb). **"for an everlasting sign"** — לְאוֹת עוֹלָם, with לְ "for".

### Notes
- **v3** — quoted Acts 13:34 (see Changes).
- **End of chs 40–55** — the invitation closes the section that begins at 40:1.
