[11] Behold, you have heard what the kings of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ have done to all lands, devoting them to destruction. And shall you be delivered?

'She despises you, she scorns you —

the daughter of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[23] Whom have you mocked and reviled?

Against the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧!

[24] By your servants you have mocked ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧, and you have said,
"With my many chariots

to the far recesses of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧,
to cut down its tallest cedars,
its choicest cypresses,

[25] I dug wells and drank waters,

[26] Have you not heard

[30] "And this shall be the sign for you: this year eat what grows of itself, and in the second year what springs from that. Then in the third year sow and reap and plant vineyards, and eat their fruit.
