# Isaiah 28

[1] Woe to the crown of pride of the drunkards of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧,
and to the withering flower of the beauty of its splendor,
which is on the head of the valley of fatness
of those struck down by wine!

[2] Behold, ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ has one strong and firm;
like a storm of hail, a tempest of destruction,
like a storm of mighty waters overflowing,
he has set it down to the earth with the hand.

[3] Under feet will be trampled
the crown of pride of the drunkards of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧;

[4] and the withering flower of the beauty of its splendor,
which is on the head of the valley of fatness,
will be like its first-ripe fig before the summer,
which the one who sees it sees,
and while it is yet in his palm he swallows it.

[5] In that day ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts will be for a crown of beauty
and for a diadem of splendor
to the remnant of his people,

[6] and for a spirit of judgment to him who sits on the judgment,
and for might to those who turn back the battle at the gate.

[7] And these also reel through wine
and stagger through strong drink;
priest and prophet reel through strong drink,
they are swallowed up by the wine,
they stagger from the strong drink,
they reel in the seeing,
they totter in deciding.

[8] For all tables are full of vomit, of filth,
without a place.

[9] "Whom will he teach knowledge,
and whom will he make understand the report?
Those weaned from the milk,
those removed from the breasts?

[10] For tzav latzav, tzav latzav,
qav laqav, qav laqav,
a little there, a little there."

[11] For with mockings of lip and with another tongue he will speak to this people,

[12] to whom he said, "This is the resting place; give rest to the weary; and this is the repose"; and they were not willing to hear.

[13] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will be to them tzav latzav, tzav latzav, qav laqav, qav laqav, a little there, a little there, in order that they may go and stumble backward, and be broken, and be snared, and be captured.

[14] Therefore hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, men of scoffing, rulers of this people that is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧!

[15] For you have said, "We have cut a covenant with death, and with Sheol we have made an agreement; the overflowing whip, when it passes over, will not come to us, for we have made a lie our refuge, and in falsehood we have hidden ourselves";

[16] therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:

"Behold, I am the one who has laid as a foundation in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ a stone,
a tried stone,
a precious corner, a foundation founded:
the one who believes will not hasten.

[17] And I will make judgment the line,
and righteousness the plummet;
and hail will sweep away the refuge of lies,
and the hiding place waters will overflow."

[18] And your covenant with death will be annulled,
and your agreement with Sheol will not stand;
the overflowing whip, when it passes over,
you will be to it for a trampling.

[19] As often as it passes over it will take you;
for morning by morning it will pass over,
by day and by night;
and it will be only horror to understand the report.

[20] For the bed is too short to stretch oneself out,
and the covering too narrow when one gathers oneself in.

[21] For as at Mount ⟦perazim|Peratzim|Perazim⟧ ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will rise up;
as in the valley at ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧ he will be agitated,
to do his deed — strange is his deed —
and to work his work — foreign is his work.

[22] And now do not scoff,
lest your bonds grow strong;
for a full end, and one decided, I have heard
from the ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧ of Hosts upon all the earth.

[23] Give ear and hear my voice;
give attention and hear my saying.

[24] Does the plowman plow all the day to sow,
does he open and harrow his ground?

[25] Is it not so: when he has levelled its face,
he scatters black cumin, and cumin he tosses,
and he puts wheat in rows
and barley in its marked place,
and spelt as its border?

[26] And he disciplines him for the ordinance;
his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ teaches him.

[27] For black cumin is not threshed with a threshing sledge,
and a wagon wheel is not turned about upon cumin;
but with a staff black cumin is beaten out,
and cumin with a rod.

[28] Bread grain is crushed,
for not forever will he surely thresh it;
and he drives the wheel of his wagon and his horsemen,
he does not crush it.

[29] This also has gone out from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts;
he has made counsel wonderful,
he has made sound wisdom great.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v5, 22, 29) — divine title preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v16, 22) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words. **"the Lord" → "Adonai"** (v2) — Hebrew לַאדֹנָי.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v26) — Hebrew אֱלֹהָיו.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) or a part of it per printed line, in the Hebrew order (v3 now in the Hebrew order); v11–15 stay prose. The quotation marks in v9–10 and v16–17 are the editors'.
- **"Woe to the crown of pride, to the drunkards of Ephraim" → "Woe to the crown of pride of the drunkards of Ephraim"** (v1, 3) — Hebrew הוֹי "Woe" ("to" is supplied for the English); עֲטֶרֶת גֵּאוּת שִׁכֹּרֵי אֶפְרַיִם, a chain of construct nouns: "the crown of pride of the drunkards of Ephraim"; גֵּאוּת "pride".
- **"whose glorious beauty is a fading flower" → "the withering flower of the beauty of its splendor"** (v1, 4) — Hebrew צִיץ נֹבֵל צְבִי תִפְאַרְתּוֹ: צִיץ "flower" (v4 צִיצַת), נָבֵל "wither"; צְבִי "beauty" (the same letters as "gazelle"); תִּפְאֶרֶת "splendor" here ("beauty" where it stands alone: English needs a second word beside צְבִי; also v5).
- **"the fat valleys of them that are overcome with wine" → "the valley of fatness of those struck down by wine"** (v1, 4) — Hebrew גֵּיא־שְׁמָנִים "valley of fat things" (singular valley); הֲלוּמֵי יָיִן "struck down by wine" (הָלַם "strike").
- **"the Lord hath a mighty and strong one" → "Adonai has one strong and firm"** (v2) — Hebrew חָזָק וְאַמִּץ לַאדֹנָי: חָזָק "strong", אַמִּץ "firm" (root of אָמֵץ "be firm"); לְ "to / belonging to".
- **"as a tempest of hail and a destroying storm, as a flood of mighty waters overflowing, shall cast down to the earth with the hand" → "like a storm of hail, a tempest of destruction, like a storm of mighty waters overflowing, he has set it down to the earth with the hand"** (v2) — Hebrew זֶרֶם "storm" (twice; as 25:4), שַׂעַר "tempest", קֶטֶב "destruction"; כַּבִּירִים "mighty"; שֹׁטְפִים "overflowing" (also v15, 17, 18); הִנִּיחַ, a perfect, "he has set down" (no object written: "it" supplied).
- **"shall be trodden under feet" → "Under feet will be trampled"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּרַגְלַיִם תֵּרָמַסְנָה, the verb first and plural.
- **"as the hasty fruit before the summer; which when he that looketh upon it seeth, while it is yet in his hand he eateth it up" → "like its first-ripe fig before the summer, which the one who sees it sees, and while it is yet in his palm he swallows it"** (v4) — Hebrew כְּבִכּוּרָהּ "like its first-ripe fig"; יִרְאֶה הָרֹאֶה "the one who sees sees" (one root); כַּף "palm"; בָּלַע "swallow".
- **"a crown of glory, and for a diadem of beauty, unto the residue" → "for a crown of beauty and for a diadem of splendor to the remnant"** (v5) — Hebrew לַעֲטֶרֶת צְבִי וְלִצְפִירַת תִּפְאָרָה: צְבִי "beauty", as v1; צְפִירָה "diadem" (a circlet; uncertain); שְׁאָר "remnant".
- **"to him that sitteth in judgment, and for strength to them that turn the battle to the gate" → "to him who sits on the judgment, and for might to those who turn back the battle at the gate"** (v6) — Hebrew עַל־הַמִּשְׁפָּט "on the judgment" (the seat of judging); גְּבוּרָה "might"; שָׁעְרָה "gateward" (at, toward the gate).
- **"have erred through wine, and through strong drink are out of the way … they err in vision, they stumble in judgment" → "reel through wine and stagger through strong drink … they reel in the seeing, they totter in deciding"** (v7) — Hebrew שָׁגָה "reel" (of wine and love, Prov 5:19–20; 20:1), תָּעָה "stagger" (wander); וְגַם "And … also"; כֹּהֵן וְנָבִיא without articles; נִבְלְעוּ מִן־הַיַּיִן "swallowed up by (lit. from) the wine"; בָּרֹאֶה "in the seeing" (the participle "seer" or "vision"); פָּקוּ "they totter"; פְּלִילִיָּה "deciding" (only here; a judge's ruling).
- **"full of vomit and filthiness, so that there is no place clean" → "full of vomit, of filth, without a place"** (v8) — Hebrew קִיא צֹאָה "vomit, filth" (two nouns, no "and"; צֹאָה is what comes out of the body, as Deut 23:13); בְּלִי מָקוֹם "without a place" ("clean" is not written).
- **"doctrine … drawn from the breasts" → "the report … removed from the breasts"** (v9) — Hebrew שְׁמוּעָה "report" (what is heard; also v19); יָבִין "make understand"; גְּמוּלֵי "weaned ones"; עַתִּיקֵי "removed".
- **"precept upon precept, precept upon precept; line upon line, line upon line; here a little, and there a little" → "tzav latzav, tzav latzav, qav laqav, qav laqav, a little there, a little there"** (v10, 13) — Hebrew צַו לָצָו … קַו לָקָו, printed as it sounds: the words are said over as a child's lesson or as mockery. צַו may be "command" (from צִוָּה) and קַו "line" (as v17), so KJV "precept upon precept … line upon line"; they may also be sounds without meaning, or the names of the letters צ and ק spelled out to a learner. זְעֵיר שָׁם "a little there", twice (KJV "here … there").
- **"with stammering lips and another tongue will he speak" → "with mockings of lip and with another tongue he will speak"** (v11) — Hebrew לַעֲגֵי שָׂפָה "mockings of lip" (לַעַג "mocking", of foreign speech as it sounds: "stammering"); לָשׁוֹן "tongue"; the subject is not named (the old "Yahweh" was supplied). 1 Cor 14:21 quotes it in another form (Ἐν ἑτερογλώσσοις καὶ ἐν χείλεσιν ἑτέρων "by other tongues and by lips of others").
- **"This is the rest wherewith ye may cause the weary to rest; and this is the refreshing: yet they would not hear" → "This is the resting place; give rest to the weary; and this is the repose"; and they were not willing to hear"** (v12) — Hebrew מְנוּחָה "resting place"; הָנִיחוּ, an imperative, "give rest"; מַרְגֵּעָה "repose" (only here); אָבוּא "they were willing" (אָבָה; the written form has an aleph).
- **"But the word of the LORD was unto them … that they might go, and fall backward" → "And the word of Yahweh will be to them … in order that they may go and stumble backward"** (v13) — Hebrew וְהָיָה, "and it will be" (a future); כָּשַׁל "stumble"; נוֹקְשׁוּ "be snared"; נִלְכָּדוּ "be captured".
- **"ye scornful men, that rule this people which is in Jerusalem" → "men of scoffing, rulers of this people that is in Jerusalem"** (v14) — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי לָצוֹן "men of scoffing"; מֹשְׁלֵי "rulers of" (the same letters are "speakers of parables", Num 21:27: a play is possible).
- **"We have made a covenant with death, and with hell are we at agreement" → "We have cut a covenant with death, and with Sheol we have made an agreement"** (v15) — Hebrew כָּרַתְנוּ בְרִית "we have cut a covenant"; שְׁאוֹל "Sheol"; חֹזֶה is elsewhere "seer, gazer" (from חָזָה "behold"); here, as חָזוּת in v18, it is the pact (a "vision" agreed on with Sheol): "agreement" is the sense, uncertain.
- **"when the overflowing scourge shall pass through" → "the overflowing whip, when it passes over"** (v15, 18) — Hebrew שׁוֹט שׁוֹטֵף: the qere שׁוֹט "whip" is translated (the written form שיט: "oar"? uncertain); the qere יַעֲבֹר "passes over" is translated (the written form עבר "has passed over"). שׁוֹטֵף "overflowing" (as a flood; one sound with שׁוֹט). **"lies … under falsehood" → "a lie … in falsehood"** — כָּזָב "lie"; בְּ "in"; שֶׁקֶר "falsehood".
- **"Behold, I lay in Zion for a foundation a stone, a tried stone, a precious corner stone, a sure foundation: he that believeth shall not make haste" → "Behold, I am the one who has laid as a foundation in Zion a stone, a tried stone, a precious corner, a foundation founded: the one who believes will not hasten"** (v16) — Hebrew הִנְנִי יִסַּד, "behold me, (who) has founded" (a perfect after "behold me"; also read as "I am laying"); אֶבֶן בֹּחַן "a tried stone" (lit. "a stone of trying": בֹּחַן, noun of בָּחַן "try"); פִּנַּת יִקְרַת "a precious corner" ("stone" is not repeated); מוּסָד מוּסָּד "a foundation founded"; הַמַּאֲמִין "the one who believes"; לֹא יָחִישׁ "will not hasten" (חוּשׁ "hasten"). The old inner quotation marks round the last line are taken out: the Hebrew does not mark it as words cut on the stone. The verse is quoted in Rom 9:33; 10:11; 1 Pet 2:6 from the Greek: ὁ πιστεύων ἐπʼ αὐτῷ οὐ καταισχυνθήσεται "the one who believes on him will not be put to shame" (Rom 9:33 joins it to Isa 8:14). The Hebrew is translated as it stands.
- **"Judgment also will I lay to the line, and righteousness to the plummet: and the hail shall sweep away the refuge of lies, and the waters shall overflow the hiding place" → "And I will make judgment the line, and righteousness the plummet; and hail will sweep away the refuge of lies, and the hiding place waters will overflow"** (v17) — Hebrew וְשַׂמְתִּי … לְ "I will make … (into)"; קָו "line", מִשְׁקֹלֶת "plummet" (as 2 Kgs 21:13); יָעָה "sweep away" (only here); סֵתֶר "hiding place", placed first in its line.
- **"shall be disannulled" → "will be annulled"** (v18) — Hebrew וְכֻפַּר, pual of כִּפֶּר, the verb of "make atonement" (27:9; lit. "be covered", here "be wiped out, cancelled"; the one verb named). חָזוּת "agreement" (lit. "vision": see v15); קוּם "stand". **"then ye shall be trodden down by it" → "you will be to it for a trampling"** — לְמִרְמָס "for a trampling" (root of v3).
- **"From the time that it goeth forth it shall take you … and it shall be a vexation only to understand the report" → "As often as it passes over it will take you … and it will be only horror to understand the report"** (v19) — Hebrew מִדֵּי עָבְרוֹ "as often as it passes over"; זְוָעָה "horror" (as Deut 28:25); רַק "only".
- **"shorter than that a man can stretch himself on it: and the covering narrower than that he can wrap himself in it" → "too short to stretch oneself out, and the covering too narrow when one gathers oneself in"** (v20) — Hebrew מֵהִשְׂתָּרֵעַ "from stretching oneself out"; מַסֵּכָה "covering" (as 25:7); כְּהִתְכַּנֵּס "as one gathers oneself in".
- **"as in mount Perazim, he shall be wroth as in the valley of Gibeon, that he may do his work, his strange work; and bring to pass his act, his strange act" → "as at Mount Perazim … as in the valley at Gibeon he will be agitated, to do his deed — strange is his deed — and to work his work — foreign is his work"** (v21) — Hebrew כְּהַר־פְּרָצִים "as the mount of Perazim" (2 Sam 5:20); עֵמֶק בְּגִבְעוֹן "a valley at Gibeon" (1 Chr 14:16; Josh 10:10–12); רָגַז "be agitated" (in anger or trembling: the word does not say); מַעֲשֶׂה "deed", זָר "strange"; עָבַד … עֲבֹדָה "work … work" (elsewhere "serve … service"), נָכְרִיָּה "foreign".
- **"be ye not mockers, lest your bands be made strong: for I have heard from the Lord GOD of hosts a consumption, even determined upon the whole earth" → "do not scoff, lest your bonds grow strong; for a full end, and one decided, I have heard from the Lord Yahweh of Hosts upon all the earth"** (v22) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה "And now"; הִתְלוֹצֵץ "scoff"; יֶחְזְקוּ "grow strong" (חָזַק qal); כָלָה וְנֶחֱרָצָה "a full end and a decided (one)" (as 10:23); אֶרֶץ "earth" or "land".
- **"hearken, and hear my speech" → "give attention and hear my saying"** (v23) — Hebrew הֶאֱזִין "give ear", הִקְשִׁיב "give attention", אִמְרָה "saying".
- **"Doth the plowman plow all day to sow? doth he open and break the clods of his ground?"** (v24) — Hebrew הֲכֹל הַיּוֹם "all the day"; one question mark: the second line has no question word of its own. שִׂדֵּד "harrow"; אֲדָמָה "ground".
- **"When he hath made plain the face thereof, doth he not cast abroad the fitches, and scatter the cummin, and cast in the principal wheat and the appointed barley and the rie in their place?" → "Is it not so: when he has levelled its face, he scatters black cumin, and cumin he tosses, and he puts wheat in rows and barley in its marked place, and spelt as its border?"** (v25) — Hebrew הֲלוֹא אִם "is it not (so), when"; שִׁוָּה "level"; קֶצַח "black cumin" (nigella; uncertain: "dill", KJV "fitches"; also v27); כַּמֹּן "cumin"; זָרַק "toss" (of a dry thing); שׂוֹרָה "in rows" and נִסְמָן "marked (place)", both found only here: uncertain; כֻּסֶּמֶת "spelt" (not "rye"); גְּבֻלָתוֹ "its border".
- **"For his God doth instruct him to discretion, and doth teach him" → "And he disciplines him for the ordinance; his Elohim teaches him"** (v26) — Hebrew וְיִסְּרוֹ לַמִּשְׁפָּט אֱלֹהָיו יוֹרֶנּוּ: יִסֵּר "discipline" (here of training a man in his work); מִשְׁפָּט "ordinance" (the right way of doing it); the accents put אֱלֹהָיו with "teaches him"; הוֹרָה "teach".
- **"with a threshing instrument, neither is a cart wheel turned about upon the cummin; but the fitches are beaten out with a staff" → "with a threshing sledge, and a wagon wheel is not turned about upon cumin; but with a staff black cumin is beaten out"** (v27) — Hebrew חָרוּץ "threshing sledge" (lit. "sharp"); אוֹפַן עֲגָלָה "wagon wheel"; סָבַב "turn about"; מַטֶּה "staff", חָבַט "beat out", שֵׁבֶט "rod".
- **"Bread corn is bruised; because he will not ever be threshing it, nor break it with the wheel of his cart, nor bruise it with his horsemen" → "Bread grain is crushed, for not forever will he surely thresh it; and he drives the wheel of his wagon and his horsemen, he does not crush it"** (v28) — Hebrew לֶחֶם "bread" ("grain" is supplied: the grain for bread is meant); יוּדָק "is crushed" (דָּקַק "crush fine"; also the last verb); אָדוֹשׁ יְדוּשֶׁנּוּ, infinitive absolute with its verb, lit. "threshing he will thresh it": "surely thresh"; וְהָמַם "and he drives" (הָמַם, elsewhere "throw into confusion": here of setting the wheel noisily in motion; uncertain); גִּלְגַּל "wheel"; פָּרָשָׁיו "his horsemen" (horses may be meant; "horseman" is the row's word).
- **"which is wonderful in counsel, and excellent in working" → "he has made counsel wonderful, he has made sound wisdom great"** (v29) — Hebrew יָצָאָה "has gone out"; מֵעִם "from with"; הִפְלִיא עֵצָה "he has made counsel wonderful" (hifil of פלא, a perfect); הִגְדִּיל תּוּשִׁיָּה "he has made sound wisdom great" (תּוּשִׁיָּה "sound wisdom", uncertain; KJV "working").

### Notes
- **v10, 13** — *tzav latzav, qav laqav*: the scoffers' mimicry of the prophet's teaching, as if to small children (v9); in v13 Yahweh's word comes to them in the same form.
- **v16** — quoted in Rom 9:33; 10:11; 1 Pet 2:6 — the cornerstone applied to Yeshua.
