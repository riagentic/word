# Isaiah 3

[1] For behold, the Lord, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts,
is taking away from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and from ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧
support and stay,
every support of bread
and every support of water;

[2] mighty one and man of war,
judge and prophet,
and diviner and elder;

[3] commander of fifty
and one held in regard,
and counselor and the wise among craftsmen
and one understanding in charm.

[4] And I will give boys as their princes,
and caprices shall rule over them.

[5] And the people shall be pressed,
man by man,
and each man by his neighbor;
the boy shall act arrogantly against the elder,
and the one made light of against the honored.

[6] For a man shall seize his brother, of the house of his father:
"A cloak is yours;
a captain you shall be for us,
and this ruin under your hand";

[7] he shall lift up his voice in that day, saying:
"I will not be one who binds up,
and in my house there is no bread and no cloak;
you shall not set me as captain of a people."

[8] For ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ has stumbled,
and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ has fallen,
for their tongue and their doings are against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
to rebel against the eyes of his glory.

[9] The look of their faces answers against them,
and their sin like ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ they tell; they do not conceal it.
Woe to their soul!
For they have dealt evil to themselves.

[10] Say of the righteous that it is good,
for the fruit of their doings they shall eat.

[11] Woe to the wicked! It is evil,
for the dealing of his hands shall be done to him.

[12] My people — its taskmasters are a child,
and women rule over it.
My people, those who lead you straight cause you to wander,
and the way of your paths they have swallowed up.

[13] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ takes his stand to contend,
and is standing to judge peoples.

[14] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will enter into judgment
with the elders of his people and his princes:
"And you, you have grazed the vineyard;
what was robbed from the afflicted is in your houses.

[15] What do you mean? You crush my people,
and the face of the afflicted you grind,"
declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ of Hosts.

[16] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said:

"Because the daughters of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ are haughty,
and walk with stretched-out neck
and ogling eyes,
walking and tripping they go,
and with their feet they tinkle,

[17] then ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ will make scabby
the crown of the head of the daughters of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will lay bare their secret parts."

[18] In that day ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ will take away the beauty of the anklets, and the headbands, and the crescents;

[19] the pendants, and the bracelets, and the scarves;

[20] the headdresses, and the step-chains, and the sashes, and the perfume boxes, and the charms;

[21] the signet rings, and the rings of the nose;

[22] the festal robes, and the wraps, and the shawls, and the bags;

[23] and the mirrors, and the linen wraps, and the turbans, and the veils.

[24] And it shall be: instead of spice there shall be rot,
and instead of a girdle a rope,
and instead of well-set hair baldness,
and instead of a rich robe a girding of sackcloth,
branding instead of beauty.

[25] Your men shall fall by the sword,
and your might in war.

[26] And her entrances shall lament and mourn,
and emptied, she shall sit on the ground.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton.
- **"the Lord, the LORD of hosts" → "the Lord, Yahweh of Hosts"** (v1) — Hebrew הָאָדוֹן יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת: הָאָדוֹן "the lord" with the article, not אֲדֹנָי, so plain "Lord" (as 1:24). **"saith the Lord GOD of hosts" → "declares the Lord Yahweh of Hosts"** (v15) — Hebrew נְאֻם־אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה צְבָאוֹת: both names, *Adonai* and the Tetragrammaton (token `adonai-yhwh`); נְאֻם "declares", the one wording. **"the Lord" → "Adonai"** (v17, 18) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי, beside יְהוָה in v17.
- **Layout** — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, by the accents; v6–9 and v13–14 are poetry in the Hebrew and are now set in lines; the list of v18–23 stays prose.
- **"the stay and the staff, the whole stay of bread, and the whole stay of water" → "support and stay, every support of bread and every support of water"** (v1) — Hebrew מַשְׁעֵן וּמַשְׁעֵנָה, one noun in the masculine and the feminine (= every kind of support), then מִשְׁעַן, a third form of the same root; כֹּל "every".
- **"The mighty man, and the man of war" → "mighty one and man of war"** (v2) — Hebrew גִּבּוֹר וְאִישׁ מִלְחָמָה, without the article. **"the prudent" → "diviner"** — קֹסֵם, one who practises divination (קֶסֶם).
- **"the captain of fifty, and the honourable man, and the counsellor, and the cunning artificer, and the eloquent orator" → "commander of fifty and one held in regard, and counselor and the wise among craftsmen and one understanding in charm"** (v3) — Hebrew שַׂר־חֲמִשִּׁים; נְשׂוּא פָנִים, literally "lifted up as to the face" (= one to whom regard is shown, as נָשָׂא פָּנִים Gen 19:21); חֲכַם חֲרָשִׁים "wise of craftsmen" (or "skilled in magic arts", חֲרָשִׁים taken as "arts"); נְבוֹן לָחַשׁ "understanding in charm" (לַחַשׁ "whisper, charm", the word of v20).
- **"children … and babes shall rule over them" → "boys … and caprices shall rule over them"** (v4) — Hebrew נְעָרִים "boys"; תַּעֲלוּלִים, from עָלַל "deal wantonly": "caprices, wanton acts" (as Isa 66:4); "babes" takes it as עוֹלָלִים. Uncertain. **"I will give"** — וְנָתַתִּי, "give".
- **"the people shall be oppressed, every one by another, and every one by his neighbour: the child shall behave himself proudly against the ancient, and the base against the honourable" → "the people shall be pressed, man by man, and each man by his neighbor; the boy shall act arrogantly against the elder, and the one made light of against the honored"** (v5) — Hebrew וְנִגַּשׂ, nifal of נָגַשׂ "press" (the verb of the taskmaster, v12); אִישׁ בְּאִישׁ "man by man"; רָהַב "act arrogantly" (Ps 40:4 "the arrogant"); הַנִּקְלֶה "the one made light of" against הַנִּכְבָּד "the honored" (literally "made heavy").
- **"When a man shall take hold of his brother of the house of his father, saying" → "For a man shall seize his brother, of the house of his father"** (v6) — Hebrew כִּי־יִתְפֹּשׂ "for (when) a man seizes"; there is no "saying" in this verse. **"Thou hast clothing, be thou our ruler, and let this ruin be under thy hand" → "A cloak is yours; a captain you shall be for us, and this ruin under your hand"** — שִׂמְלָה "cloak"; קָצִין "captain" (as 1:10); מַכְשֵׁלָה, from כָּשַׁל "stumble" (v8): what has stumbled, a ruin; the last line has no verb.
- **"In that day shall he swear, saying, I will not be an healer" → "he shall lift up his voice in that day, saying: 'I will not be one who binds up'"** (v7) — Hebrew יִשָּׂא "he shall lift up", without an object: "his voice" is supplied (as Num 14:1), not "swear"; חֹבֵשׁ "one who binds up" (חָבַשׁ, 1:6). **"neither bread nor clothing: make me not a ruler of the people" → "no bread and no cloak; you shall not set me as captain of a people"** — עָם without the article.
- **"because their tongue and their doings are against the LORD, to provoke the eyes of his glory" → "for their tongue and their doings are against Yahweh, to rebel against the eyes of his glory"** (v8) — Hebrew לָשׁוֹן "tongue"; מַעַלְלִים "doings"; לַמְרוֹת, מָרָה "rebel against"; עֵנֵי is "eyes" written without the yod.
- **"The shew of their countenance doth witness against them; and they declare their sin as Sodom, they hide it not. Woe unto their soul!" → "The look of their faces answers against them, and their sin like Sodom they tell; they do not conceal it. Woe to their soul!"** (v9) — Hebrew הַכָּרַת פְּנֵיהֶם, "the look of their faces" (or "their regard for faces", partiality, from הִכִּיר פָּנִים Deut 1:17); עָנְתָה בָּם "answers against them" (= testifies, Exod 20:16); הִגִּיד "tell", כִּחֵד "conceal"; אוֹי "woe" (English has one word for אוֹי and הוֹי). **"for they have rewarded evil unto themselves" → "For they have dealt evil to themselves"** — גָּמַל "deal".
- **"Say ye to the righteous, that it shall be well with him" → "Say of the righteous that it is good"** (v10) — Hebrew אִמְרוּ צַדִּיק כִּי־טוֹב, singular "righteous" with plural "they shall eat", kept; "to" or "of" is not written.
- **"Woe unto the wicked! it shall be ill with him: for the reward of his hands shall be given him" → "Woe to the wicked! It is evil, for the dealing of his hands shall be done to him"** (v11) — Hebrew רָע "evil"; גְּמוּל "dealing" (noun of גָּמַל, v9); יֵעָשֶׂה "shall be done".
- **"children are their oppressors" → "its taskmasters are a child"** (v12) — Hebrew נֹגְשָׂיו מְעוֹלֵל: נֹגֵשׂ "taskmaster" (Exod 5:6), plural with the suffix of "my people" ("its"); מְעוֹלֵל a singular participle, taken as "one who acts as a child" (the root of עוֹלֵל "child"); also "gleans it" or "deals wantonly with it" (עָלַל). Uncertain. **"women rule over them" → "women rule over it"** — נָשִׁים; the pointing נֹשִׁים "creditors" is a conjecture, and the Septuagint has "exactors" (from memory). **"they which lead thee cause thee to err, and destroy the way of thy paths" → "those who lead you straight cause you to wander, and the way of your paths they have swallowed up"** — מְאַשְּׁרֶיךָ, piel of אָשַׁר as 1:17 "lead straight" (the same verb is "call happy" in Gen 30:13, and "those who call you happy" is possible here); מַתְעִים "cause to wander"; בִּלֵּעוּ "they have swallowed up".
- **"The LORD standeth up to plead, and standeth to judge the people" → "Yahweh takes his stand to contend, and is standing to judge peoples"** (v13) — Hebrew נִצָּב "stationed, taking a stand" and עֹמֵד "standing", two words; רִיב "contend", דִּין "judge"; עַמִּים plural "peoples" (the Septuagint has "his people", from memory).
- **"for ye have eaten up the vineyard; the spoil of the poor is in your houses" → "And you, you have grazed the vineyard; what was robbed from the afflicted is in your houses"** (v14) — Hebrew וְאַתֶּם "and you"; בִּעַרְתֶּם, בָּעַר piel of a field "graze" (or "burn"; uncertain, as Exod 22:5); גְּזֵלַת "what was robbed"; עָנִי "afflicted". **"his princes"** — שָׂרָיו.
- **"What mean ye that ye beat my people to pieces, and grind the faces of the poor?" → "What do you mean? You crush my people, and the face of the afflicted you grind"** (v15) — the reading tradition (qere) מַה־לָּכֶם, "what to you?"; the written text joins the letters as one word (מלכם), with the same sense; דִּכָּא "crush", טָחַן "grind"; פְּנֵי "face"; עֲנִיִּים "afflicted".
- **"Moreover the LORD saith" → "And Yahweh said"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר. **"walk with stretched forth necks and wanton eyes, walking and mincing as they go, and making a tinkling with their feet" → "walk with stretched-out neck and ogling eyes, walking and tripping they go, and with their feet they tinkle"** — the qere נְטוּיוֹת "stretched out" (the written form נטוות has the same sense); גָּרוֹן "neck", singular; מְשַׂקְּרוֹת עֵינָיִם, שָׂקַר only here: "ogling", or "painting the eyes" (uncertain); הָלוֹךְ וְטָפֹף, two infinitives, "walking and tripping" (small steps); תְּעַכַּסְנָה, verb from עֶכֶס "anklet": "they tinkle (with anklets)".
- **"Therefore the Lord will smite with a scab the crown of the head … and the LORD will discover their secret parts" → "then Adonai will make scabby the crown of the head … and Yahweh will lay bare their secret parts"** (v17) — Hebrew וְ "and" opening the result ("then"); שִׂפַּח only here, from סַפַּחַת "scab" (Lev 13:2); קָדְקֹד "crown of the head", singular; עֵרָה "lay bare"; פָּתְהֵן, from פֹּת (the "sockets" of 1 Kgs 7:50): "their secret parts", or "their foreheads" by the parallel with the crown of the head. Uncertain.
- **The ornaments and garments of v18–23** — each Hebrew word has its own English word, in the plainest sense known; many are found only here and their meaning is uncertain. **"the bravery of their tinkling ornaments about their feet, and their cauls, and their round tires like the moon" → "the beauty of the anklets, and the headbands, and the crescents"** (v18) — תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty" (the row; "splendor" only beside צְבִי, as 4:2; here, and in 4:2 beside צְבִי "beauty", the other English word); עֲכָסִים "anklets" (the verb of v16); שְׁבִיסִים only here: "headbands" or "sun-discs"; שַׂהֲרֹנִים "crescents" (Judg 8:21, 26). **"The chains, and the bracelets, and the mufflers" → "the pendants, and the bracelets, and the scarves"** (v19) — נְטִיפוֹת "pendants" (literally "drops", Judg 8:26); שֵׁירוֹת "bracelets" (or "chains"); רְעָלוֹת only here: "scarves" (veils; "veils" is kept for רְדִידִים, v23, as Song 5:7). **"The bonnets, and the ornaments of the legs, and the headbands, and the tablets, and the earrings" → "the headdresses, and the step-chains, and the sashes, and the perfume boxes, and the charms"** (v20) — פְּאֵרִים "headdresses" (Ezek 24:17; "adornments" of the caps, Exod 39:28); צְעָדוֹת "step-chains" (from צָעַד "step"; chains between the ankles; uncertain); קִשֻּׁרִים "sashes" (from קָשַׁר "bind"; Jer 2:32); בָּתֵּי הַנֶּפֶשׁ, literally "houses of the soul" (נֶפֶשׁ also "breath, scent"): "perfume boxes", uncertain; לְחָשִׁים "charms" (amulets; the word of v3). **"The rings, and nose jewels" → "the signet rings, and the rings of the nose"** (v21) — טַבָּעוֹת "signet rings" beside נִזְמֵי הָאָף "rings of the nose" (נֶזֶם "ring"). **"The changeable suits of apparel, and the mantles, and the wimples, and the crisping pins" → "the festal robes, and the wraps, and the shawls, and the bags"** (v22) — מַחֲלָצוֹת "festal robes" (garments taken off, put on for occasions; Zech 3:4); מַעֲטָפוֹת only here, "wraps" (from עָטַף "wrap"); מִטְפָּחוֹת "shawls" (Ruth 3:15); חֲרִיטִים "bags" (2 Kgs 5:23). **"The glasses, and the fine linen, and the hoods, and the vails" → "and the mirrors, and the linen wraps, and the turbans, and the veils"** (v23) — גִּלְיֹנִים "mirrors" (polished tablets; the word of "tablet", Isa 8:1; or "garments of gauze"; uncertain); סְדִינִים "linen wraps" (Judg 14:12); צְנִיפוֹת "turbans"; רְדִידִים "veils" (Song 5:7).
- **"And it shall come to pass, that instead of sweet smell there shall be stink; and instead of a girdle a rent; and instead of well set hair baldness; and instead of a stomacher a girding of sackcloth; and burning instead of beauty" → "And it shall be: instead of spice there shall be rot, and instead of a girdle a rope, and instead of well-set hair baldness, and instead of a rich robe a girding of sackcloth, branding instead of beauty"** (v24) — Hebrew וְהָיָה "and it shall be"; בֹּשֶׂם "spice"; מַק "rot"; חֲגוֹרָה "girdle"; נִקְפָּה only here: "rope" (or "a rent"; uncertain); מַעֲשֶׂה מִקְשֶׁה, literally "a work of hammered (or turned) work" (מִקְשָׁה of the lampstand, Exod 25:31), set against "baldness": "well-set hair" is the sense given; קָרְחָה "baldness"; פְּתִיגִיל only here: "rich robe" (uncertain); מַחֲגֹרֶת שָׂק "a girding of sackcloth"; כִּי "branding" (a burn mark), or the word "for" ("for instead of beauty …"); יֹפִי "beauty". The Isaiah scroll from Qumran adds "shame" (from memory).
- **"Thy men shall fall by the sword, and thy mighty in the war" → "Your men shall fall by the sword, and your might in war"** (v25) — Hebrew מְתַיִךְ "your men" (feminine "your": Zion); גְּבוּרָתֵךְ "your might", the abstract noun, kept; מִלְחָמָה "war".
- **"And her gates shall lament and mourn; and she being desolate shall sit upon the ground" → "And her entrances shall lament and mourn, and emptied, she shall sit on the ground"** (v26) — Hebrew פְּתָחֶיהָ "her entrances" (פֶּתַח; שַׁעַר is "gate"); אָנוּ "lament", אָבְלוּ "mourn"; נִקָּתָה, nifal of נָקָה "be clear" — of a city, "be cleaned out, emptied" (another sense than the clearing from guilt or an oath: note).

### Notes
- **v1–7** — the fall of the leaders: the support taken away, and no one willing to rule.
- **v18–23** — the longest list of women's ornaments in the Hebrew Bible; many of its words are found only here.
