# Isaiah 27

[1] In that day ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with his hard and great and strong sword
will punish ⟦leviathan|Livyatan|Leviathan⟧ —
the fleeing serpent,
⟦leviathan|Livyatan|Leviathan⟧ the twisting serpent,
and he will slay the sea monster — sea dragon.

[2] In that day,
"A pleasant vineyard, sing of it!

[3] I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, am its keeper;
every moment I water it.
Lest anyone punish it,
I keep it night and day;

[4] I have no wrath.
Would that I had thorns and briers to battle!
I would march against them,
I would burn them up together.

[5] Or let them lay hold of my protection,
let them make peace with me,
let them make peace with me."

[6] In days to come ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ shall take root,
⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall blossom and put forth shoots
and fill the whole world with fruit.

[7] Has he struck them as he struck those who struck them?
Or have they been slain as their slayers were slain?

[8] Measure by measure, by exile you contended with them;
he removed them with his fierce breath
in the day of the east wind.

[9] Therefore by this the guilt of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ will be atoned for,
and this will be the full fruit of the removal of his sin:
when he makes all the stones of the altars
like chalkstones crushed to pieces,
no Asherah poles or incense altars will remain standing.

[10] For the fortified city is solitary,
a habitation deserted and forsaken, like the wilderness;
there the calf grazes;
there it lies down and strips its branches.

[11] When its boughs are dry, they are broken;
women come and make a fire of them.
For this is a people without discernment;
therefore he who made them will not have compassion on them;
he who formed them will show them no favor.

[12] In that day from ⟦the-river|the River|the river Euphrates⟧ to the Brook of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will thresh out the grain, and you will be gleaned one by one, O children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[13] And in that day a great ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧ will be blown, and those who were lost in the land of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ and those who were driven out to the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ will come and worship ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ on the holy mountain at ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"the piercing serpent" / "the crooked serpent" — kept** (v1) — KJV phrases match Hebrew *nachash bariach* (fleeing serpent) and *nachash aqalaton* (twisting serpent); retained as character descriptions.
- **"the channel of the river" → "the River"** (v12) — Hebrew הַנָּהָר *ha-nahar*; the Euphrates is meant but not named in MT.
- **Poetic structure** — vv1-11 formatted as verse; vv12-13 prose conclusion preserved.

### Notes
- **v1** — *Leviathan*: cosmic sea-monster of chaos (Ugaritic *Lotan*); YHWH's victory = cosmic renewal.
- **v13** — "great trumpet": echoes Matt 24:31.
