# Isaiah 36

[1] In the fourteenth year of King ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧, ⟦sennacherib|Sancheriv|Sennacherib⟧ king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ came up against all the fortified cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and took them.

[2] And the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ sent the ⟦rabshakeh|Rav-Shaqeh|Rabshakeh⟧ — chief cupbearer/general — from Lachish to King ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ at ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, with a great army. And he stood by the conduit of the upper pool on the highway to the Washer's Field.

[3] And there came out to him ⟦eliakim|Elyaqim|Eliakim⟧ son of ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧, who was over the household, and ⟦shebna|Shevna|Shebna⟧ the scribe, and ⟦joah|Yoach|Joah⟧ son of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧, the recorder.

[4] And the ⟦rabshakeh|Rav-Shaqeh|Rabshakeh⟧ said to them, "Say to ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧: Thus says the great king, the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧: On what do you rest this trust of yours?

[5] Do you think that mere words are strategy and power for war? In whom do you now trust, that you have rebelled against me?

[6] Behold, you are trusting in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, that broken reed of a staff, which will pierce the hand of any man who leans on it. Such is ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ to all who trust in him.

[7] But if you say to me, 'We trust in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,' is it not he whose high places and altars ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ has removed, saying to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, 'You shall worship before this altar'?

[8] Come now, make a wager with my master the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧: I will give you two thousand horses, if you are able on your part to set riders on them.

[9] How then can you repulse a single captain among the least of my master's servants, when you trust in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ for chariots and for horsemen?

[10] Moreover, is it without ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that I have come up against this land to destroy it? ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ said to me, 'Go up against this land and destroy it.'"

[11] Then ⟦eliakim|Elyaqim|Eliakim⟧, ⟦shebna|Shevna|Shebna⟧, and ⟦joah|Yoach|Joah⟧ said to the ⟦rabshakeh|Rav-Shaqeh|Rabshakeh⟧, "Please speak to your servants in ⟦aramaic|Aramit|Aramaic⟧, for we understand it. Do not speak to us in ⟦judahite|Yehudit|the language of Judah⟧ within the hearing of the people who are on the wall."

[12] But the ⟦rabshakeh|Rav-Shaqeh|Rabshakeh⟧ said, "Has my master sent me to speak these words to your master and to you, and not to the men sitting on the wall, who are doomed with you to eat their own dung and drink their own urine?"

[13] Then the ⟦rabshakeh|Rav-Shaqeh|Rabshakeh⟧ stood and called out in a loud voice in ⟦judahite|Yehudit|the language of Judah⟧: "Hear the words of the great king, the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧!

[14] Thus says the king: Do not let ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ deceive you, for he will not be able to deliver you.

[15] Do not let ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ make you trust in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ by saying, '⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will surely deliver us; this city will not be given into the hand of the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧.'

[16] Do not listen to ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧. For thus says the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧: Make your peace with me and come out to me. Then each one of you will eat of his own vine, and each one of his own fig tree, and each one of you will drink the water of his own cistern,

[17] until I come and take you away to a land like your own land, a land of grain and wine, a land of bread and vineyards.

[18] Beware lest ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ mislead you by saying, '⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will deliver us.' Has any of the gods of the nations delivered his land out of the hand of the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧?

[19] Where are the gods of ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧ and Arpad? Where are the gods of ⟦sepharvaim|Sefarvayim|Sepharvaim⟧? Have they delivered ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ out of my hand?

[20] Who among all the gods of these lands have delivered their lands out of my hand, that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ should deliver ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ out of my hand?"

[21] But they were silent and answered him not a word, for the king's command was, "Do not answer him."

[22] Then ⟦eliakim|Elyaqim|Eliakim⟧ son of ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧, who was over the household, and ⟦shebna|Shevna|Shebna⟧ the scribe, and ⟦joah|Yoach|Joah⟧ son of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧, the recorder, came to ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ with their clothes torn, and told him the words of the ⟦rabshakeh|Rav-Shaqeh|Rabshakeh⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v7) — Hebrew *Elohim*; the Rabshakeh's taunting quotation "Yahweh our *Elohim*" preserved. The "gods of the nations/lands" (vv18-20, Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי) are plural beings and stay "gods".
- **Entirely prose chapter** — no poetic structure changes; narrative preserved as-is.

### Notes
- **Parallels 2 Kgs 18-20** — likely Isaiah source for the Kings version.
- **Sennacherib's invasion** — 701 BC.
