# Isaiah 25

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, you are my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
I will exalt you; I will give thanks to your name,
for you have done a wonder,
counsels from afar, faithfulness, firmness.

[2] For you have made from a city a heap,
a fortified town a ruin,
a palace of strangers from being a city;
forever it shall not be built.

[3] Therefore a strong people will honor you;
the town of ruthless nations will fear you.

[4] For you have been a strong place to the poor,
a strong place to the needy in his distress,
a refuge from the storm, a shade from the heat;
for the breath of the ruthless
is like a storm against a wall.

[5] Like heat in a dry place
you bring low the tumult of strangers;
heat by the shade of a thick cloud:
the song of the ruthless sinks low.

[6] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts will make for all the peoples
on this mountain
a feast of fat things,
a feast of lees,
of fat things full of marrow,
of lees refined.

[7] And he will swallow up on this mountain
the face of the veil, the veil that is over all the peoples,
and the covering that is woven over all the nations.

[8] He has swallowed up death forever;
and ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ will wipe away tears from upon all faces,
and the reproach of his people he will remove from upon all the earth,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken.

[9] And he will say in that day:

"Behold, this is our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
we have waited for him, and he will save us.
This is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; we have waited for him;
let us be glad and rejoice in his salvation."

[10] For the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will rest on this mountain,
and ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ shall be threshed in his place,
as straw is threshed in a dung pit.

[11] And he will spread out his hands in the midst of it,
as the swimmer spreads them out to swim;
and he will bring low his pride
together with the devices of his hands.

[12] And the fortress, the secure height of your walls,
he has bowed down, made low, made to reach the earth, to the dust.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v6) — divine title preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v8) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 9) — Hebrew אֱלֹהַי, אֱלֹהֵינוּ.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) or a part of it per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v5 now follows the Hebrew order of its four parts; v9 keeps its spoken words set apart.
- **"O LORD, thou art my God" → "Yahweh, you are my Elohim"** (v1) — the Hebrew has no word for "O"; the name stands alone as the address.
- **"I will praise thy name" → "I will give thanks to your name"** (v1) — Hebrew אוֹדֶה, hifil of ידה, "give thanks to" (not הִלֵּל "praise").
- **"wonderful things" → "a wonder"** (v1) — Hebrew פֶּלֶא, a singular noun.
- **"thy counsels of old are faithfulness and truth" → "counsels from afar, faithfulness, firmness"** (v1) — Hebrew עֵצוֹת מֵרָחוֹק אֱמוּנָה אֹמֶן: no "thy", no verb; מֵרָחוֹק is "from afar" (of time "long ago" as KJV, or of place); אֱמוּנָה "faithfulness"; אֹמֶן is found only here, from the root of אָמֵן "be firm" ("truth", "sureness" in other versions). The nouns stand side by side; how they relate is not said.
- **"of a city an heap; of a defenced city a ruin" → "from a city a heap, a fortified town a ruin"** (v2) — Hebrew מֵעִיר "from a city" (MT; many read הָעִיר "the city"); קִרְיָה "town" beside עִיר "city"; בְּצוּרָה "fortified"; מַפֵּלָה "ruin" (lit. "a falling").
- **"a palace of strangers to be no city; it shall never be built" → "a palace of strangers from being a city; forever it shall not be built"** (v2) — Hebrew אַרְמוֹן זָרִים מֵעִיר: זָרִים "strangers"; מֵעִיר "from (being) a city"; לְעוֹלָם לֹא "forever … not".
- **"the strong people … the city of the terrible nations" → "a strong people … the town of ruthless nations"** (v3) — Hebrew עַם־עָז, singular, no article; קִרְיַת "town of"; עָרִיצִים "ruthless" (one who strikes terror; also v4, 5). **"glorify" → "honor"** — Hebrew כִּבֵּד, "honor" (lit. "make heavy").
- **"a strength … a refuge … a shadow" → "a strong place … a refuge … a shade"** (v4) — Hebrew מָעוֹז "strong place" (twice), מַחְסֶה "refuge", צֵל "shade". חֹרֶב "heat" (v4, 5; the word is "dryness, drought": here the heat of a dry land; in Judg 6 "dryness").
- **"when the blast of the terrible ones is as a storm against the wall" → "for the breath of the ruthless is like a storm against a wall"** (v4) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; רוּחַ "breath" (also "wind, spirit": one word); זֶרֶם "storm" (a driving rain, as 28:2); קִיר "wall" as the MT has it (many read קֹר "cold": "a winter storm"; not followed).
- **"Thou shalt bring down the noise of strangers, as the heat in a dry place; even the heat with the shadow of a cloud: the branch of the terrible ones shall be brought low" → "Like heat in a dry place you bring low the tumult of strangers; heat by the shade of a thick cloud: the song of the ruthless sinks low"** (v5) — Hebrew order kept. צָיוֹן "dry place"; שְׁאוֹן "tumult" (a roaring noise); תַּכְנִיעַ "you bring low" (הִכְנִיעַ); עָב "thick cloud" (beside עָנָן "cloud"). זְמִיר is "song" or "pruning" (KJV "branch"): the sense is uncertain. יַעֲנֶה is taken from ענה "be bowed low"; as from ענה "answer" it would read "the song of the ruthless answers"; the meaning of the line is uncertain.
- **"in this mountain shall the LORD of hosts make unto all people" → "And Yahweh of Hosts will make for all the peoples on this mountain"** (v6) — Hebrew וְעָשָׂה "And … will make", in the Hebrew order; הָעַמִּים "the peoples" (plural, with the article; also v7).
- **"a feast of wines on the lees … of wines on the lees well refined" → "a feast of lees … of lees refined"** (v6) — Hebrew שְׁמָרִים "lees" (the dregs on which wine is kept to age); "wines" is not written. שְׁמָנִים "fat things"; מְמֻחָיִם "full of marrow"; מְזֻקָּקִים "refined" (strained clear). מִשְׁתֶּה "feast" (a drinking).
- **"he will destroy … the face of the covering cast over all people, and the vail that is spread over all nations" → "he will swallow up … the face of the veil, the veil that is over all the peoples, and the covering that is woven over all the nations"** (v7) — Hebrew בִּלַּע "swallow up" (the same verb as v8); פְּנֵי־הַלּוֹט הַלּוֹט "the face of the veil, the veil" (the word is repeated); "cast" is not written. מַסֵּכָה is here "covering", from נָסַךְ "weave" (הַנְּסוּכָה "that is woven"; as 28:20); the same letters are elsewhere the "molten image" (from נָסַךְ "pour"): another sense.
- **"He will swallow up death in victory" → "He has swallowed up death forever"** (v8) — Hebrew בִּלַּע הַמָּוֶת לָנֶצַח: a perfect, "he has swallowed up", said of what Yahweh will do (a perfect in prophecy; the verbs around it look forward); לָנֶצַח "forever" (KJV "in victory", from the Aramaic sense of the root). 1 Cor 15:54 quotes it as Κατεπόθη ὁ θάνατος εἰς νῖκος, "Death has been swallowed up into victory": passive and "into victory". The Hebrew is translated as it stands.
- **"the Lord GOD will wipe away tears from off all faces; and the rebuke of his people shall he take away" → "Lord Yahweh will wipe away tears from upon all faces, and the reproach of his people he will remove"** (v8) — Hebrew מָחָה "wipe away" (of writing "blot out": one word); דִּמְעָה "tears" (a collective singular); מֵעַל "from upon"; חֶרְפָּה "reproach"; יָסִיר "he will remove". "it" after "spoken" is not written.
- **"And it shall be said in that day, Lo" → "And he will say in that day: Behold"** (v9) — Hebrew וְאָמַר, "and he will say" (the speaker is not named: "one will say"); הִנֵּה "behold".
- **"we will be glad and rejoice in his salvation" → "let us be glad and rejoice in his salvation"** (v9) — Hebrew נָגִילָה וְנִשְׂמְחָה, two cohortatives ("let us …"): גִּיל "be glad", שָׂמַח "rejoice" (as Ps 96:11).
- **"Moab shall be trodden down under him, even as straw is trodden down for the dunghill" → "Moab shall be threshed in his place, as straw is threshed in a dung pit"** (v10) — Hebrew נָדוֹשׁ … כְּהִדּוֹשׁ, דּוּשׁ "thresh" both times; תַּחְתָּיו "in his place" (or "under him", of Yahweh). The qere בְּמוֹ "in" is translated; the written form בְּמֵי reads "in the waters of a dung pit". מַדְמֵנָה "dung pit" (or a place name, Madmenah, Isa 10:31).
- **"in the midst of them … as he that swimmeth spreadeth forth his hands to swim" → "in the midst of it … as the swimmer spreads them out to swim"** (v11) — Hebrew בְּקִרְבּוֹ "in its midst" (singular); the second verb has no object written ("them" = his hands). Who spreads his hands, Yahweh or Moab, the Hebrew does not say.
- **"he shall bring down their pride together with the spoils of their hands" → "he will bring low his pride together with the devices of his hands"** (v11) — Hebrew וְהִשְׁפִּיל גַּאֲוָתוֹ, singular suffixes ("his"; Moab's); הִשְׁפִּיל "bring low"; אָרְבּוֹת יָדָיו is found only here: "devices / tricks" (from אָרַב "lie in wait") or "skill" of his hands; uncertain.
- **"And the fortress of the high fort of thy walls shall he bring down, lay low, and bring to the ground" → "And the fortress, the secure height of your walls, he has bowed down, made low, made to reach the earth"** (v12) — Hebrew מִבְצַר מִשְׂגַּב חוֹמֹתֶיךָ "your walls" (masculine singular "you", Moab addressed); מִשְׂגָּב "secure height". Three perfects (as v8): הֵשַׁח "bowed down" (hifil of שָׁחַח, as 26:5), הִשְׁפִּיל "made low", הִגִּיעַ "made to reach", "even to the dust" עַד־עָפָר.

### Notes
- **v8** — "He has swallowed up death forever": quoted in 1 Cor 15:54; cf. Rev 21:4 (tears wiped away).
- **v6** — eschatological feast; Matt 8:11; Rev 19:9.
