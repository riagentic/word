# Isaiah 40

[1] ⟦nachamu|Nachamu, nachamu ami —|"Comfort,⟧
⟦nachamu|"Comfort, comfort my people,"|comfort my people,"⟧
says your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[2] "Speak to the heart of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
and call to her
that her warfare is fulfilled,
that her iniquity is paid off,
that she has taken from the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
double for all her sins."

[3] A voice calling:
"In the wilderness prepare the way of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
make straight in the steppe a highway for our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[4] Every valley shall be lifted up,
and every mountain and hill shall be made low;
and the uneven shall become level land,
and the rough places a plain.

[5] And the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be revealed,
and all flesh shall see together,
for the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken."

[6] A voice saying, "Call!"
And he said, "What shall I call?"
"All flesh is grass,
and all its ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ is like the flower of the field.

[7] The grass dries out, the flower withers,
for the breath of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has blown on it;
surely the people is grass.

[8] The grass dries out, the flower withers,
but the word of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ shall stand forever."

[9] Upon a high mountain get yourself up,
bearer of news, ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧;
raise your voice with power,
bearer of news, ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧;
raise it, do not fear;
say to the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,
"Behold your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!"

[10] Behold, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ comes with strength,
and his arm rules for him;
behold, his reward is with him,
and his wage before him.

[11] Like a shepherd he shall shepherd his drove;
with his arm he shall gather lambs,
and in his bosom he shall carry;
those that give suck he shall guide.

[12] Who has measured the waters in the hollow of his hand,
and gauged the heavens with the span,
and held in a measure the dust of the earth,
and weighed mountains in the balance,
and hills in scales?

[13] Who has gauged the spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and as the man of his counsel makes him know?

[14] With whom did he take counsel,
and he made him understand,
and taught him in the path of judgment,
and taught him knowledge,
and makes him know the way of understandings?

[15] Behold, nations are like a drop from a bucket,
and as the fine dust of scales they are reckoned;
behold, coastlands like fine powder he lifts up.

[16] And ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ is not enough for burning,
and its living things are not enough for a burnt offering.

[17] All the nations are as nothing before him;
as less than naught and emptiness they are reckoned by him.

[18] And to whom will you liken ⟦el|El|God⟧,
and what likeness will you arrange for him?

[19] The carved image — a craftsman cast it,
and a refiner beats it out with gold,
and chains of silver he refines.

[20] He who is too poor for a contribution
chooses wood that will not rot;
he seeks for himself a wise craftsman
to set firm a carved image that will not be shaken.

[21] Do you not know? Do you not hear?
Has it not been told to you from the beginning?
Have you not understood the foundations of the earth?

[22] He who dwells upon the circle of the earth,
and its dwellers are like grasshoppers;
who stretches out the heavens like a thin cloth,
and spreads them like a tent to dwell in;

[23] who gives dignitaries to nothing;
the judges of the earth he makes like emptiness.

[24] Scarcely are they planted, scarcely are they sown,
scarcely has their stock taken root in the earth,
and he also blows on them, and they dry out,
and the storm carries them away like stubble.

[25] "And to whom will you liken me, that I should be equal?"
says the Holy One.

[26] Lift up your eyes on high and see:
who created these?
He who brings out their host by number;
all of them he calls by name;
from the greatness of vigor,
and because he is strong in power,
not one is missing.

[27] Why do you say, ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and speak, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
"My way is hidden from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and from my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ my judgment passes away"?

[28] Have you not known? Or have you not heard?
The everlasting ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
the Creator of the ends of the earth,
does not faint and does not grow weary;
there is no searching out of his understanding.

[29] He gives power to the faint,
and to him who has no vigor he multiplies might.

[30] And youths shall faint and grow weary,
and young men shall surely stumble;

[31] but those who wait for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall renew power;
they shall go up with pinions like the eagles;
they shall run and not grow weary;
they shall walk and not faint.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v10) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"God" → "El"** (v18) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"Comfort ye" / "Nachamu" — opening** (v1) — Hebrew נַחֲמוּ נַחֲמוּ עַמִּי *Nachamu, nachamu ami*, "Comfort, comfort my people"; יֹאמַר is an imperfect, "says" (or "will say").
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v25 now breaks at the etnach (before "says the Holy One").
- **"Speak ye comfortably to Jerusalem" → "Speak to the heart of Jerusalem"** (v2) — Hebrew דַּבְּרוּ עַל־לֵב, literally "speak upon the heart" (= speak to comfort; as Gen 34:3; 50:21). **"cry unto her" → "call to her"** — קָרָא is "call" in v2, 3, 6 alike.
- **"her warfare is accomplished" → "her warfare is fulfilled"** (v2) — Hebrew מָלְאָה צְבָאָהּ, "her host / service is full"; צָבָא is otherwise "host" (v26), here the term of hard service, as Job 7:1; 14:14.
- **"her iniquity is pardoned" → "her iniquity is paid off"** (v2) — Hebrew נִרְצָה, nifal of רָצָה as in Lev 26:34, 41, 43 (the land's Shabbats and the people's iniquity "paid off"); also read "accepted". **"received" → "taken"** — לָקְחָה, "she has taken". "the hand of Yahweh" — מִיַּד, the Hebrew order.
- **"The voice of him that crieth in the wilderness, Prepare" → "A voice calling: In the wilderness prepare"** (v3) — Hebrew קוֹל קוֹרֵא בַּמִּדְבָּר פַּנּוּ. The accents (zaqef after קוֹרֵא) put "in the wilderness" with "prepare", and the parallel line has "in the steppe make straight". Matt 3:3; Mark 1:3; Luke 3:4; John 1:23 follow the Greek (LXX), "a voice of one crying in the wilderness: Prepare", where "in the wilderness" goes with the voice; the verse follows the Hebrew. פִּנָּה is "clear, make room": "prepare" as Gen 24:31.
- **"in the desert" → "in the steppe"** (v3) — Hebrew בָּעֲרָבָה, the dry steppe (*arabah*; here a common noun, not the place name); מִדְבָּר is "wilderness".
- **"shall be exalted" → "shall be lifted up"** (v4) — Hebrew יִנָּשֵׂא, nifal of נָשָׂא. **"the crooked shall be made straight, and the rough places plain" → "the uneven shall become level land, and the rough places a plain"** — וְהָיָה הֶעָקֹב לְמִישׁוֹר; עָקֹב "uneven, steep" (also "crooked, deceitful"); מִישׁוֹר "level land"; רְכָסִים found only here (perhaps "ridges"; uncertain); בִּקְעָה "plain". The verse has "and" (וְ).
- **"shall see it together" → "shall see together"** (v5) — Hebrew וְרָאוּ כָל־בָּשָׂר יַחְדָּו has no object; "it" was supplied. Luke 3:6 follows the Greek (LXX), "shall see the salvation of God", which the Hebrew does not have.
- **"The voice said, Cry. And he said, What shall I cry?" → "A voice saying, Call! And he said, What shall I call?"** (v6) — Hebrew קוֹל אֹמֵר קְרָא וְאָמַר. The MT has וְאָמַר "and he said"; 1QIsa and the Septuagint read "and I said" (from memory), which the earlier text followed: the MT is restored.
- **"all the goodliness thereof" → "all its chesed"** (v6) — Hebrew כָּל־חַסְדּוֹ, the word *chesed* (token as everywhere): the faithfulness / steadfastness of flesh is as short-lived as the flower. 1 Pet 1:24 follows the Greek (LXX), "all its glory" (δόξα); the verse follows the Hebrew.
- **"The grass withereth, the flower fadeth" → "The grass dries out, the flower withers"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew יָבֵשׁ … נָבֵל: two verbs, kept apart ("dry out" יָבֵשׁ, "wither" נָבֵל, as in v24 "dry out").
- **"because the spirit of the LORD bloweth upon it" → "for the breath of Yahweh has blown on it"** (v7) — Hebrew כִּי רוּחַ יְהוָה נָשְׁבָה בּוֹ; רוּחַ is spirit, wind and breath in one word: "breath" for the blowing; כִּי "for" (also read "when"); נָשְׁבָה perfect. **"surely the people is grass"** — אָכֵן "surely"; עָם singular.
- **"shall stand for ever"** (v8) — Hebrew יָקוּם לְעוֹלָם. 1 Pet 1:25 has "the word of the Lord" (the Greek: ῥῆμα κυρίου); the Hebrew has "our Elohim". 1QIsa and the Septuagint differ in v6–8 (shorter text; from memory); the MT is kept.
- **"O Zion, that bringest good tidings, get thee up into the high mountain" → "Upon a high mountain get yourself up, bearer of news, Zion"** (v9) — Hebrew עַל הַר־גָּבֹהַּ עֲלִי־לָךְ מְבַשֶּׂרֶת צִיּוֹן, Hebrew order; עֲלִי־לָךְ "get yourself up" (the reflexive לָךְ, as Gen 12:1). מְבַשֶּׂרֶת, participle of בִּשֵּׂר "bring news" (mostly good news, but the word alone does not say "good": 1 Sam 4:17; Isa 52:7 adds טוֹב), is feminine: Zion / Jerusalem herself as the bearer of news (apposition), or "bearer of news to Zion"; the Hebrew allows both. The vocative has no "O". **"lift up thy voice with strength" → "raise your voice with power"** — הָרִימִי בַכֹּחַ; הֵרִים "raise", כֹּחַ "power". **"be not afraid" → "do not fear"** — אַל־תִּירָאִי.
- **"will come with strong hand" → "comes with strength"** (v10) — Hebrew בְּחָזָק יָבוֹא, "with strength" (or "as a strong one"); there is no "hand". **"his work before him" → "his wage before him"** — פְּעֻלָּה, what is earned (as Lev 19:13); שָׂכָר "reward".
- **"He shall feed his flock like a shepherd" → "Like a shepherd he shall shepherd his drove"** (v11) — Hebrew כְּרֹעֶה עֶדְרוֹ יִרְעֶה, one root (רֹעֶה / רָעָה), Hebrew order; עֵדֶר "drove". **"with his arm" … "carry them"** — בִּזְרֹעוֹ singular; יִשָּׂא has no object ("them" not written). **"gently lead those that are with young" → "those that give suck he shall guide"** — עָלוֹת, the nursing ewes; נֵהֵל "guide".
- **"meted out heaven with the span" → "gauged the heavens with the span"** (v12) — Hebrew תִּכֵּן, "measure out, regulate", the same verb as v13 ("Who has gauged the spirit"); מָדַד "measure" beside it. **"comprehended the dust of the earth in a measure" → "held in a measure"** — כָּל "contain, hold"; שָׁלִשׁ a measure, literally "a third". **"in scales … in a balance" → "in the balance … in scales"** — פֶּלֶס "balance" (with the article; as Prov 16:11) and מֹאזְנַיִם "scales" (a dual: the pair of pans), two words. "mountains", "hills" have no article.
- **"Who hath directed the Spirit of the LORD, or being his counsellor hath taught him?" → "Who has gauged the spirit of Yahweh, and as the man of his counsel makes him know?"** (v13) — Hebrew מִי־תִכֵּן אֶת־רוּחַ יְהוָה וְאִישׁ עֲצָתוֹ יוֹדִיעֶנּוּ: literally "and a man of his counsel makes him know"; "as" is supplied (the man stands beside "who"); יוֹדִיעַ "make know". Rom 11:34 and 1 Cor 2:16 quote the Greek (LXX), "who has known the mind (νοῦς) of the Lord, or who has been his counselor?"; the verse follows the Hebrew.
- **"With whom took he counsel, and who instructed him" → "With whom did he take counsel, and he made him understand"** (v14) — Hebrew אֶת־מִי נוֹעָץ וַיְבִינֵהוּ: the question word stands once; the following verbs are chained with וַ ("and he made him understand … and taught him … and makes him know"), not new questions. **"the path of judgment" … "the way of understanding"** — מִשְׁפָּט "judgment"; תְּבוּנוֹת plural, "understandings".
- **"the small dust of the balance" → "the fine dust of scales"** (v15) — Hebrew שַׁחַק מֹאזְנַיִם (the "scales" of v12); נֶחְשָׁבוּ "they are reckoned". **"he taketh up the isles as a very little thing" → "coastlands like fine powder he lifts up"** — אִיִּים "coastlands" (no article); דַּק "fine (powder)"; Hebrew order.
- **"Lebanon is not sufficient to burn, nor the beasts thereof" → "And Lebanon is not enough for burning, and its living things"** (v16) — Hebrew וּלְבָנוֹן … וְחַיָּתוֹ: both וְ "and"; חַיָּה "living things".
- **"less than nothing, and vanity" → "less than naught and emptiness"** (v17) — Hebrew מֵאֶפֶס וָתֹהוּ; מִן "less than" (also read "of naught"); אֶפֶס "naught", kept apart from אַיִן "nothing" in the first line (as 41:12, 29); תֹּהוּ "emptiness" (as Gen 1:2 "waste"). לוֹ "by him" (lit. "to him").
- **"what likeness will ye compare unto him?" → "what likeness will you arrange for him?"** (v18) — Hebrew תַּעַרְכוּ לוֹ, עָרַךְ "arrange, set in order" (= set side by side, compare).
- **"The workman melteth a graven image" → "The carved image — a craftsman cast it"** (v19) — Hebrew הַפֶּסֶל נָסַךְ חָרָשׁ, the object first; פֶּסֶל "carved image" (not "idol"); נָסַךְ "pour, cast". **"the goldsmith spreadeth it over with gold, and casteth silver chains" → "a refiner beats it out with gold, and chains of silver he refines"** — צֹרֵף "refiner", רִקַּע "beat out"; the last word, צוֹרֵף, is the same participle ("refining"); "casts" is a guess.
- **"He that is so impoverished that he hath no oblation" → "He who is too poor for a contribution"** (v20) — Hebrew הַמְסֻכָּן תְּרוּמָה: meaning uncertain. As pointed, "the impoverished one — a contribution", i.e. too poor to give a contribution (תְּרוּמָה); many take מְסֻכָּן as a kind of wood (Akkadian *musukkannu*, from memory). **"a cunning workman" → "a wise craftsman"** — חָרָשׁ חָכָם; יְבַקֶּשׁ־לוֹ "seeks for himself". **"to prepare a graven image, that shall not be moved" → "to set firm a carved image that will not be shaken"** — הֵכִין "set firm"; מוֹט nifal "be shaken" (as Ps 96:10).
- **"Have ye not understood from the foundations of the earth?" → "Have you not understood the foundations of the earth?"** (v21) — Hebrew הֲבִינֹתֶם מוֹסְדוֹת הָאָרֶץ has no "from"; it is read as the object, or adverbially "since the foundations" (as the KJV). **"from the beginning"** — מֵרֹאשׁ, literally "from the head".
- **"he that sitteth upon the circle of the earth" → "He who dwells upon the circle of the earth"** (v22) — Hebrew הַיֹּשֵׁב, the verb of יֹשְׁבֶיהָ "its dwellers" and לָשָׁבֶת "to dwell in" in the same verse: one verb, "dwell" (of one enthroned also "sit"). **"as a curtain" → "like a thin cloth"** — דֹּק, found only here (gauze, a fine veil; uncertain).
- **"bringeth the princes to nothing" → "gives dignitaries to nothing"** (v23) — Hebrew הַנּוֹתֵן רוֹזְנִים לְאָיִן; רוֹזְנִים "dignitaries" (Ps 2:2). **"as vanity" → "like emptiness"** — כַּתֹּהוּ; עָשָׂה "he makes" (perfect; also "has made"); Hebrew order.
- **"Yea, they shall not be planted" → "Scarcely are they planted"** (v24) — Hebrew אַף בַּל־נִטָּעוּ, three times; literally "also not (yet)": they are hardly in the ground. **"stock"** — גִּזְעָם. **"and he shall also blow upon them" → "and he also blows on them"** — וְגַם־נָשַׁף. **"they shall wither" → "they dry out"** — וַיִּבָשׁוּ, יָבֵשׁ as v7. **"the whirlwind shall take them away" → "the storm carries them away"** — סְעָרָה (with no article; "the" for English).
- **"To whom then will ye liken me, or shall I be equal?" → "And to whom will you liken me, that I should be equal?"** (v25) — Hebrew וְאֶל־מִי תְדַמְּיוּנִי וְאֶשְׁוֶה; the verb דִּמָּה as v18 ("liken"). **"saith the Holy One"** — יֹאמַר קָדוֹשׁ has no article ("says a Holy One" / "says the Holy"); "the" is supplied for English.
- **"by the greatness of his might, for that he is strong in power" → "from the greatness of vigor, and because he is strong in power"** (v26) — Hebrew מֵרֹב אוֹנִים וְאַמִּיץ כֹּחַ; אוֹנִים "vigor" (as v29), כֹּחַ "power"; "his" is not written. **"not one faileth" → "not one is missing"** — אִישׁ לֹא נֶעְדָּר, literally "a man is not missing".
- **"O Jacob … O Israel" → "Jacob … Israel"** (v27) — no "O" in the Hebrew. **"my judgment is passed over from my God" → "from my Elohim my judgment passes away"** — Hebrew וּמֵאֱלֹהַי מִשְׁפָּטִי יַעֲבוֹר, Hebrew order; עָבַר "pass over / away"; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (here the right due to me).
- **"hast thou not heard" → "Or have you not heard?"** (v28) — Hebrew אִם־לֹא. **"the everlasting God, the LORD, the Creator … fainteth not"** — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי עוֹלָם יְהוָה בּוֹרֵא קְצוֹת הָאָרֶץ לֹא יִיעַף: one sentence (the accents go on to the etnach at יִיגָע): "The everlasting Elohim, Yahweh, the Creator …, does not faint"; "Yahweh is the everlasting Elohim" (a supplied "is") is the other reading. עוֹלָם "everlasting" (lit. "of the age"). **"neither is weary" → "does not grow weary"** — יָעֵף "faint", יָגַע "grow weary" (v28–31). **"there is no searching of his understanding" → "there is no searching out of his understanding"** — חֵקֶר, noun of חָקַר "search out".
- **"he increaseth strength" → "he multiplies might"** (v29) — Hebrew עָצְמָה יַרְבֶּה; אוֹנִים "vigor", כֹּחַ "power", עָצְמָה "might": three words kept apart.
- **"Even the youths … and the young men shall utterly fall" → "And youths … and young men shall surely stumble"** (v30) — Hebrew וְיִעֲפוּ נְעָרִים … וּבַחוּרִים כָּשׁוֹל יִכָּשֵׁלוּ: וְ "and"; נְעָרִים "youths" (נַעַר is elsewhere "boy"; here grown youths, beside בַּחוּרִים "young men"); the infinitive absolute "stumbling they shall stumble" = "shall surely stumble".
- **"they that wait upon the LORD" → "those who wait for Yahweh"** (v31) — Hebrew קוֹיֵ יְהוָה, קִוָּה "wait for". **"shall renew their strength" → "shall renew power"** — יַחֲלִיפוּ כֹחַ, "shall exchange (renew) power"; "their" not written. **"they shall mount up with wings as eagles" → "they shall go up with pinions like the eagles"** — יַעֲלוּ אֵבֶר כַּנְּשָׁרִים, literally "they shall go up pinion" ("with" supplied; also read "put forth pinions", as feathers grow); נֶשֶׁר "eagle" (the word also covers the great vultures).

### Notes
- **Begins Second Isaiah** (chs 40-55) — comfort to Babylonian exiles; Yahweh as incomparable Creator.
- **v3-5** — cited in all four Gospels applied to John the Baptist (Matt 3:3; Mark 1:3; Luke 3:4-6; John 1:23).
- **v6-8** — quoted 1 Pet 1:24-25.
- **v13** — quoted Rom 11:34; 1 Cor 2:16.
