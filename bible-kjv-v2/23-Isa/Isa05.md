# Isaiah 5

[1] Let me sing, please, for my beloved
the song of my loved one concerning his vineyard:
A vineyard my beloved had
on a fertile hill.

[2] And he dug it and cleared it of stones,
and planted it with choice vines,
and built a tower in its midst,
and also hewed out a winepress in it;
and he waited for it to make grapes,
and it made stinking ones.

[3] And now, inhabitant of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧
and man of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,
judge, please, between me and my vineyard.

[4] What more is there to do for my vineyard
that I have not done in it?
Why did I wait for it to make grapes,
and it made stinking ones?

[5] And now let me make known to you, please,
what I am doing to my vineyard:
I will remove its hedge,
and it shall be for grazing;
I will break down its fence,
and it shall be for trampling.

[6] And I will make it a waste;
it shall not be pruned and shall not be hoed,
and briers and thorns shall come up;
and the thick clouds I will command
that they rain no rain upon it.

[7] For the vineyard of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts
is the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
and the man of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧
the planting of his delight;
and he waited for judgment,
and behold, bloodshed;
for righteousness,
and behold, an outcry!

[8] Woe to those who join house to house;
field to field they bring near,
until there is no more place,
and you are made to dwell alone
in the midst of the land.

[9] In my ears, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts:
"Surely many houses shall become a desolation,
great and good, without inhabitant.

[10] For ten yokes of vineyard shall make one bath,
and the seed of a homer shall make an ephah."

[11] Woe to those who rise early in the morning,
strong drink they pursue;
who linger late in the twilight,
wine inflames them!

[12] And lyre and harp,
timbrel and flute and wine are their feasts;
and the deed of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ they do not look at,
and the work of his hands they have not seen.

[13] Therefore my people has gone into exile
for lack of knowledge;
and its glory are men of hunger,
and its multitude is parched with thirst.

[14] Therefore Sheol has enlarged her soul
and opened her mouth without limit;
and her splendor and her multitude shall go down,
and her din, and he who is jubilant in her.

[15] And mankind was bowed down, and man was brought low,
and the eyes of the lofty shall be brought low.

[16] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts was exalted in the judgment,
and the holy ⟦el|El|God⟧ was sanctified in righteousness.

[17] And lambs shall graze as in their pasture,
and the ruins of the fat ones sojourners shall eat.

[18] Woe to those who draw the iniquity with the cords of emptiness,
and, as with the ropes of the wagon, sin;

[19] who say, "Let him hasten,
let him hurry his work,
so that we may see;
and let it draw near and come,
the counsel of the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, so that we may know!"

[20] Woe to those who say of evil, "Good,"
and of good, "Evil";
who put darkness for light
and light for darkness;
who put bitter for sweet
and sweet for bitter!

[21] Woe to those wise in their eyes,
and before their faces discerning!

[22] Woe to those mighty at drinking wine,
and men of ability at mixing strong drink;

[23] who declare the wicked righteous in return for a bribe,
and the righteousness of the righteous they take away from him!

[24] Therefore, as a tongue of fire devours stubble,
and dry grass sinks down in the flame,
their root shall be as rottenness,
and their blossom shall go up as dust;
for they have rejected the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts,
and the saying of the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ they have spurned.

[25] Therefore the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ burned against his people,
and he stretched out his hand against it and struck it,
and the mountains trembled;
and their dead bodies were like refuse in the midst of the streets.
For all this his anger has not turned back,
and still his hand is stretched out.

[26] And he will lift up a banner to the nations from afar,
and whistle for it from the end of the earth;
and behold, quickly, swiftly it comes!

[27] There is none weary and none stumbling in it;
it does not slumber and does not sleep;
and the belt of its loins is not loosed,
and the strap of its sandals is not snapped;

[28] whose arrows are sharpened,
and all its bows are bent;
the hoofs of its horses are reckoned as flint,
and its wheels as the tempest.

[29] Its roaring is like the lioness,
it roars like the young lions;
and it growls and seizes prey,
and carries it off, and there is none to deliver.

[30] And it shall growl over it in that day
like the growling of the sea;
and one shall look to the land,
and behold, darkness, distress;
and the light is darkened in its clouds.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v7, 9, 16, 24) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept (v2, 7, 12, 15–17, 26, 30).
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; the existing breaks inside a line are kept where the accents mark a pause.
- **"Now will I sing to my wellbeloved a song of my beloved touching his vineyard" → "Let me sing, please, for my beloved the song of my loved one concerning his vineyard"** (v1) — Hebrew אָשִׁירָה נָּא, a cohortative with נָא "please"; two words: יְדִידִי "my beloved" (twice) and דּוֹדִי "my loved one"; no capital letter (the Hebrew does not mark either as a title). שִׁירַת דּוֹדִי may also be "my love song". **"in a very fruitful hill" → "on a fertile hill"** — literally "on a horn, a son of oil": קֶרֶן "horn" of a hilltop, בֶּן־שָׁמֶן "son of oil" = rich, fertile.
- **"fenced it, and gathered out the stones" → "dug it and cleared it of stones"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיְעַזְּקֵהוּ, found only here (sense uncertain: "dig, hoe"; "fence" is a guess); סִקֵּל "clear of stones" (piel of סָקַל "stone"). **"tower"** — מִגְדָּל, no "watch". **"winepress"** — יֶקֶב (the vat).
- **"looked that it should bring forth grapes, and it brought forth wild grapes" → "waited for it to make grapes, and it made stinking ones"** (v2, 4) — Hebrew קִוָּה "wait for"; עָשָׂה "make" (as Gen 1:11 "making fruit"), the same verb as "do" in v4, 5; בְּאֻשִׁים, from בָּאַשׁ "stink": "stinking ones"; "wild grapes" names no Hebrew word, and "grapes" is not in it.
- **"O inhabitants of Jerusalem, and men of Judah" → "inhabitant of Jerusalem and man of Judah"** (v3) — Hebrew singulars יוֹשֵׁב, אִישׁ (collective); no "O" (no word for it). שִׁפְטוּ־נָא "judge, please".
- **"What could have been done more" → "What more is there to do"** (v4) — Hebrew מַה־לַּעֲשׂוֹת עוֹד, an infinitive. **"wherefore, when I looked … brought it forth" → "Why did I wait … and it made"** — the Hebrew order kept.
- **"go to; I will tell you" → "let me make known to you, please"** (v5) — Hebrew אוֹדִיעָה־נָּא, cohortative. **"what I will do" → "what I am doing"** — participle עֹשֶׂה. **"I will take away … I will break down"** — infinitives absolute הָסֵר, פָּרֹץ, used as the main verbs: "I will" is supplied. **"it shall be eaten up" → "it shall be for grazing"** — לְבָעֵר, "for grazing" (the verb is also "burn": uncertain); **"trodden down" → "for trampling"** — noun מִרְמָס. **"wall" → "fence"** — גָּדֵר; מְשׂוּכָּה "hedge".
- **"I will lay it waste" → "I will make it a waste"** (v6) — Hebrew בָתָה, found only here ("end, waste"; uncertain). **"there shall come up briers and thorns"** — שָׁמִיר וָשַׁיִת (singular collectives; the pair recurs in Isaiah: 7:23–25). **"the clouds" → "the thick clouds"** — עָבִים; עַל … אֲצַוֶּה "command upon" (= command), with the clouds first; מֵהַמְטִיר, literally "from raining".
- **"his pleasant plant" → "the planting of his delight"** (v7) — Hebrew נְטַע שַׁעֲשׁוּעָיו; שַׁעֲשֻׁעִים is a plural noun, rendered singular (as Ps 119:24). **"he looked for judgment, but behold oppression; for righteousness, but behold a cry" → "he waited for judgment, and behold, bloodshed; for righteousness, and behold, an outcry"** — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּט / מִשְׂפָּח and צְדָקָה / צְעָקָה, a play of sound that English cannot keep. מִשְׂפָּח is found only here: "bloodshed" (an outpouring of blood) is the commonly given sense, uncertain. וְהִנֵּה "and behold" (twice). צְעָקָה "outcry", the cry of the wronged.
- **"Woe unto them that"** (v8, 11, 18, 20–22) — Hebrew הוֹי + participle, literally "Woe, those who join …": "to those who" is supplied once in each line. **"that lay field to field" → "field to field they bring near"** (v8) — Hebrew יַקְרִיבוּ, the Hebrew order kept. **"till there be no place" → "until there is no more place"** — עַד אֶפֶס מָקוֹם, literally "until the end of place". **"that ye may be placed alone" → "and you are made to dwell alone"** — הוּשַׁבְתֶּם, hofal of יָשַׁב; the change to "you" is the Hebrew's.
- **"In mine ears said the LORD of hosts" → "In my ears, Yahweh of Hosts:"** (v9) — the Hebrew has no verb ("said", "has sworn" are supplied by translations). **"Of a truth" → "Surely"** — the oath formula אִם־לֹא, literally "if not". **"shall be desolate" → "shall become a desolation"** — לְשַׁמָּה יִהְיוּ. **"great and fair" → "great and good"** — גְּדֹלִים וְטוֹבִים.
- **"ten acres" → "ten yokes"** (v10) — Hebrew צִמְדֵּי, "yokes": the land a yoke of oxen plows (in a day). **"shall yield … shall yield" → "shall make … shall make"** — עָשָׂה as v2. **"the seed of an homer"** — זֶרַע חֹמֶר, the seed sown from a homer; no "but" in the Hebrew. A homer is ten ephahs (Ezek 45:11).
- **"that they may follow strong drink" → "strong drink they pursue"** (v11) — Hebrew שֵׁכָר יִרְדֹּפוּ, a new clause, the object first; רָדַף "pursue". **"continue until night" → "linger late in the twilight"** — מְאַחֲרֵי, piel of אָחַר "delay"; נֶשֶׁף "twilight".
- **"the harp, and the viol, the tabret, and pipe" → "lyre and harp, timbrel and flute"** (v12) — כִּנּוֹר, נֵבֶל, תֹּף, חָלִיל (kinds uncertain). **"are in their feasts" → "are their feasts"** — וְהָיָה … מִשְׁתֵּיהֶם, no "in". **"they regard not the work of the LORD, neither consider the operation of his hands" → "the deed of Yahweh they do not look at, and the work of his hands they have not seen"** — פֹּעַל "deed", מַעֲשֶׂה "work"; הִבִּיט "look at" (imperfect), רָאָה "see" (perfect).
- **"are gone into captivity" → "has gone into exile"** (v13) — Hebrew גָּלָה, a perfect: what is sure to come is told as done. **"their honourable men are famished" → "its glory are men of hunger"** — Hebrew כְּבוֹדוֹ "its glory" (= its honored ones), singular suffix for the people; מְתֵי רָעָב "men of hunger" as pointed (pointed מֵתֵי it would be "dead of hunger"). **"dried up with thirst" → "parched with thirst"** — צִחֵה צָמָא.
- **"hell hath enlarged herself" → "Sheol has enlarged her soul"** (v14) — Hebrew שְׁאוֹל, the plain word; נַפְשָׁהּ "her soul" (here the throat, the appetite). **"without measure" → "without limit"** — לִבְלִי־חֹק, literally "for lack of a statute (a fixed limit)". **"their glory … their multitude, and their pomp, and he that rejoiceth" → "her splendor and her multitude … and her din, and he who is jubilant in her"** — feminine suffixes: the city (not named in the Hebrew; "Jerusalem" was supplied by the former text) or Sheol; הֲדָר "splendor", שָׁאוֹן "din, roar"; עָלֵז "jubilant". וְיָרַד "shall go down".
- **"the mean man shall be brought down, and the mighty man shall be humbled" → "mankind was bowed down, and man was brought low"** (v15) — Hebrew אָדָם and אִישׁ, kept apart; וַיִּשַּׁח, וַיִּשְׁפַּל are past forms (wayyiqtol), as in v16, set beside the future "shall be brought low": what is sure is told as done (as 2:9). **"the lofty"** — גְבֹהִים.
- **"shall be exalted in judgment, and God that is holy shall be sanctified" → "was exalted in the judgment, and the holy El was sanctified"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיִּגְבַּה (wayyiqtol), בַּמִּשְׁפָּט with the article; הָאֵל הַקָּדוֹשׁ "the El, the Holy"; נִקְדָּשׁ nifal "be sanctified" (= "show himself holy", as Lev 10:3).
- **"Then shall the lambs feed after their manner, and the waste places of the fat ones shall strangers eat" → "And lambs shall graze as in their pasture, and the ruins of the fat ones sojourners shall eat"** (v17) — Hebrew כְּדָבְרָם, "as their pasture" (דֹּבֶר, Mic 2:12; uncertain: "as they are driven"); מֵחִים "fat ones" (fatlings, or the rich: the word does not say which); גָּרִים "sojourners"; the Hebrew order kept.
- **"draw iniquity with cords of vanity, and sin as it were with a cart rope" → "draw the iniquity with the cords of emptiness, and, as with the ropes of the wagon, sin"** (v18) — Hebrew בְּחַבְלֵי הַשָּׁוְא, שָׁוְא "emptiness, falsehood"; עֲבוֹת הָעֲגָלָה, the articles kept; חַטָּאָה "sin".
- **"Let him make speed, and hasten his work, that we may see it" → "Let him hasten, let him hurry his work, so that we may see"** (v19) — Hebrew יְמַהֵר יָחִישָׁה; no "it" after "see" or "know". **"let the counsel … draw nigh and come"** — the Hebrew has the two verbs first, then "the counsel of the Holy One of Israel"; וְנֵדָעָה "so that we may know".
- **"call evil good, and good evil" → "say of evil, 'Good,' and of good, 'Evil'"** (v20) — Hebrew הָאֹמְרִים לָרַע טוֹב, "who say to (of) evil, good".
- **"wise in their own eyes, and prudent in their own sight" → "wise in their eyes, and before their faces discerning"** (v21) — Hebrew בְּעֵינֵיהֶם; נֶגֶד פְּנֵיהֶם "before their faces"; נְבֹנִים "discerning". No "own".
- **"mighty to drink wine, and men of strength to mingle strong drink" → "mighty at drinking wine, and men of ability at mixing strong drink"** (v22) — Hebrew גִּבּוֹרִים; אַנְשֵׁי־חַיִל "men of ability" (the words of valor, said in scorn).
- **"justify the wicked for reward, and take away the righteousness of the righteous from him" → "declare the wicked righteous in return for a bribe, and the righteousness of the righteous they take away from him"** (v23) — Hebrew מַצְדִּיקֵי רָשָׁע; עֵקֶב שֹׁחַד, literally "on the heel (in consequence) of a bribe"; צַדִּיקִים is plural and מִמֶּנּוּ "from him" singular, as written.
- **"as the fire devoureth the stubble, and the flame consumeth the chaff, so their root" → "as a tongue of fire devours stubble, and dry grass sinks down in the flame, their root"** (v24) — Hebrew לְשׁוֹן אֵשׁ "a tongue of fire"; וַחֲשַׁשׁ לֶהָבָה יִרְפֶּה, "and dry grass of flame sinks" (or "and flame makes dry grass sink"; רָפָה "sink, slacken"); no "so". **"cast away … despised the word" → "rejected … the saying … they have spurned"** — מָאַס "reject"; אִמְרָה "saying"; נִאֵץ "spurn", with the Hebrew order.
- **"is the anger of the LORD kindled" → "the anger of Yahweh burned"** (v25) — Hebrew חָרָה אַף. **"against them … smitten them" → "against it and struck it"** — singular suffixes, of the people. **"the hills did tremble" → "the mountains trembled"** — הֶהָרִים; רָגַז "be agitated, tremble". **"their carcases were torn in the midst of the streets" → "their dead bodies were like refuse in the midst of the streets"** — נִבְלָתָם, singular collective, "dead body" of men (the word of an animal's carcass); כַּסּוּחָה, found only here: "like refuse" (uncertain). **"is not turned away" → "has not turned back"** — שָׁב.
- **"he will lift up an ensign to the nations from far, and will hiss unto them" → "he will lift up a banner to the nations from afar, and whistle for it"** (v26) — Hebrew נֵס "banner"; לַגּוֹיִם plural, then לוֹ "for it" and יָבוֹא "it comes" singular: the one nation that comes (the reading "to a nation from afar", a change of one letter, is not followed). **"with speed swiftly" → "quickly, swiftly"** — מְהֵרָה קַל. **"end of the earth"** — מִקְצֵה, singular.
- **"None shall be weary nor stumble among them" → "There is none weary and none stumbling in it"** (v27) — Hebrew אֵין … בּוֹ, singular; **"the girdle of their loins" → "the belt of its loins"**; **"the latchet of their shoes be broken" → "the strap of its sandals is not snapped"** — נִתַּק "be snapped (torn away)".
- **"their arrows are sharp" → "whose arrows are sharpened"** (v28) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר; שְׁנוּנִים passive. **"bent"** — דְּרֻכוֹת, literally "trodden" (strung by treading). **"shall be counted like flint" → "are reckoned as flint"** — חָשַׁב nifal. **"a whirlwind" → "the tempest"** — סוּפָה.
- **"Their roaring shall be like a lion, they shall roar like young lions" → "Its roaring is like the lioness, it roars like the young lions"** (v29) — Hebrew לָבִיא "lioness" (gender not certain), כְּפִירִים "young lions". The qere יִשְׁאַג "it roars" is translated; the ketiv וְשָׁאַג "and it shall roar" says the same. **"lay hold of the prey, and shall carry it away safe, and none shall deliver it" → "seizes prey, and carries it off, and there is none to deliver"** — Hebrew הִפְלִיט "bring away safe"; אֵין מַצִּיל.
- **"they shall roar against them" → "it shall growl over it"** (v30) — Hebrew וְיִנְהֹם עָלָיו, singular. **"in that day"** — בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא. **"if one look unto the land" → "one shall look to the land"** — וְנִבַּט, no "if". **"darkness and sorrow, and the light is darkened in the heavens thereof" → "darkness, distress; and the light is darkened in its clouds"** — Hebrew חֹשֶׁךְ צַר, no "and"; the accents may also join צַר וָאוֹר "distress and light". עֲרִיפִים is found only here ("clouds", uncertain); the suffix is feminine ("its" = the land).

### Notes
- **v1–7** — Song of the Vineyard. The play of sound in v7: *mishpat / mispach*, *tzedaqah / tze'aqah*.
- **Six woes** (v8, 11, 18, 20, 21, 22) — v23 continues the woe of v22.
