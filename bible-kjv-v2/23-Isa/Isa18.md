# Isaiah 18

[1] Woe, land of the whirring of wings,
which is beyond the rivers of ⟦cush|Kush|Cush⟧,

[2] which sends envoys by the sea,
and in vessels of papyrus on the face of the waters:
Go, swift messengers,
to a nation drawn out and polished,
to a people fearsome from it and onward,
a nation of line, line, and of trampling,
whose land the rivers divide.

[3] All who dwell in the world
and who abide in the earth,
when a banner is lifted up on the mountains, you shall see,
and when a ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧ is blown, you shall hear.

[4] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to me:

"Let me be quiet, and let me look on in my established place,
like clear heat upon light,
like a cloud of dew in the heat of harvest."

[5] For before harvest, when the bud has come to an end,
and the blossom becomes a ripening sour grape,
then he shall cut off the shoots with the pruning knives,
and the tendrils he has removed, has struck away.

[6] They shall be left together
to the birds of prey of the mountains
and to the beasts of the earth;
and the birds of prey shall summer on it,
and all the beasts of the earth shall winter on it.

[7] At that time a present shall be brought to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts — a people drawn out and polished, and from a people fearsome from it and onward, a nation of line, line, and of trampling, whose land the rivers divide — to the place of the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v4, 7) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v7) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת.
- **Layout** — v1–6 one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, by the accents (v6 keeps the existing break after "together", where the accents mark a pause); v7 stays prose as before.
- **"Woe to the land shadowing with wings" → "Woe, land of the whirring of wings"** (v1) — Hebrew הוֹי אֶרֶץ, "Woe" with a noun, no "to"; צִלְצַל כְּנָפָיִם as printed: צִלְצַל is "a whirring" (Deut 28:42 of an insect, "the whirring one"; the same letters give "spear" and "cymbals"). Doubt: the phrase may mean whirring insects, the beating wings of birds, or the sails of boats; "shadowing" reads צֵל "shadow" into the word. **"Ethiopia" → "Cush"** — כּוּשׁ, the land south of Egypt.
- **"That sendeth ambassadors by the sea, even in vessels of bulrushes upon the waters, saying" → "which sends envoys by the sea, and in vessels of papyrus on the face of the waters:"** (v2) — Hebrew הַשֹּׁלֵחַ "which sends"; צִירִים "envoys"; וּבִכְלֵי־גֹמֶא "and in vessels of papyrus"; עַל־פְּנֵי־מַיִם "on the face of the waters"; "saying" is not written. **"to a nation scattered and peeled" → "to a nation drawn out and polished"** — מְמֻשָּׁךְ "drawn out" (tall, or long-haired; "scattered" is a guess), מוֹרָט "polished" (smooth-skinned, or with hair plucked bare). **"terrible from their beginning hitherto" → "fearsome from it and onward"** — נוֹרָא "fearsome"; מִן־הוּא וָהָלְאָה, word for word "from it and further": in time ("from its beginning onward") or in space ("far and wide"); left open. **"a nation meted out and trodden down" → "a nation of line, line, and of trampling"** — קַו־קָו, "line, line" (קַו "measuring line"; as 28:10, 13 קַו לָקָו; "strength, strength" from an Arabic word in Changes only), מְבוּסָה "trampling" (from בּוּס "tread down"): who treads or is trodden is not said. **"have spoiled" → "divide"** — בָּזְאוּ (only here and v7; uncertain: "divide, cut through").
- **"All ye inhabitants of the world, and dwellers on the earth" → "All who dwell in the world and who abide in the earth"** (v3) — Hebrew יֹשְׁבֵי (יָשַׁב "dwell") and שֹׁכְנֵי (שָׁכַן "abide") kept apart. **"see ye, when he lifteth up an ensign on the mountains; and when he bloweth a trumpet, hear ye" → "when a banner is lifted up on the mountains, you shall see, and when a shofar is blown, you shall hear"** — Hebrew כִּנְשֹׂא, כִּתְקֹעַ, infinitives with no subject ("he" is supplied in KJV); נֵס "banner"; the Hebrew order puts the verbs last; תִּרְאוּ, תִּשְׁמָעוּ are imperfects, "you shall see … hear" (a command is also possible).
- **"For so the LORD said unto me" → "For thus says Yahweh to me"** (v4) — Hebrew כֹּה אָמַר יְהוָה, the fixed formula. **"I will take my rest, and I will consider in my dwelling place" → "Let me be quiet, and let me look on in my established place"** — qere אֶשְׁקֳטָה (the ketiv אשקוטה is the same word written with ו), and וְאַבִּיטָה, both cohortatives; שָׁקַט "be quiet"; הִבִּיט "look"; מָכוֹן "established place" (as Exod 15:17). **"like a clear heat upon herbs" → "like clear heat upon light"** — Hebrew עֲלֵי־אוֹר "upon light" ("sunshine" is meant; "herbs" reads אוֹרָה, as 2 Kgs 4:39). **"like a cloud of dew in the heat of harvest"** — kept.
- **"when the bud is perfect, and the sour grape is ripening in the flower" → "when the bud has come to an end, and the blossom becomes a ripening sour grape"** (v5) — Hebrew כְּתָם־פֶּרַח, תַּם "come to an end" (the bud is over); וּבֹסֶר גֹּמֵל יִהְיֶה נִצָּה, word for word "and a ripening sour grape becomes the blossom": the masculine verb agrees with בֹּסֶר, but the sense is that the blossom turns into the grape. **"he shall both cut off the sprigs with pruning hooks" → "then he shall cut off the shoots with the pruning knives"** — וְכָרַת (weqatal), הַזַּלְזַלִּים (only here: "shoots"), בַּמַּזְמֵרוֹת (from זָמַר "prune"), with the articles. **"and take away and cut down the branches" → "and the tendrils he has removed, has struck away"** — הַנְּטִישׁוֹת "tendrils" (from נָטַשׁ "spread out"), the object first; הֵסִיר הֵתַז are two perfects side by side, no "and" (הֵתַז only here: "strike off"); the perfect tells what is sure as done.
- **"They shall be left together unto the fowls of the mountains" → "They shall be left together to the birds of prey of the mountains"** (v6) — Hebrew יַחְדָּו "together"; עֵיט "birds of prey"; בֶּהֱמָה "beasts". **"upon them" → "on it"** — עָלָיו, singular (the whole cut-off growth).
- **"shall the present be brought … of a people scattered and peeled, and from a people terrible" → "a present shall be brought … — a people drawn out and polished, and from a people fearsome"** (v7) — Hebrew יוּבַל־שַׁי "a present shall be brought" (no article); the MT has עַם "a people" without "from" first, then וּמֵעַם "and from a people": as printed, the first people is itself the present. The Isaiah scroll from Qumran and the Septuagint read "from a people" both times (from memory); the MT is kept. The descriptions as v2. **"the mount Zion" → "Mount Zion"** — הַר־צִיּוֹן.
