# Isaiah 60

[1] Arise, shine, for your light has come,
and the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has dawned upon you.

[2] For behold, the darkness shall cover the earth,
and dark cloud the folks;
and upon you ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall dawn,
and his glory shall be seen upon you.

[3] And nations shall walk to your light,
and kings to the brightness of your dawning.

[4] Lift up your eyes round about and see:
all of them have gathered, they have come to you;
your sons shall come from afar,
and your daughters shall be carried at the side.

[5] Then you shall see and be radiant,
and your heart shall tremble and grow wide,
for the multitude of the sea shall be turned to you,
the wealth of nations shall come to you.

[6] A throng of camels shall cover you,
young camels of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ and ⟦ephah|Efah|Ephah⟧;
all of them from ⟦sheba|Sheva|Sheba⟧ shall come.
Gold and frankincense they shall carry,
and the praises of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ they shall bring as news.

[7] All the flock of ⟦kedar|Qedar|Kedar⟧ shall be gathered to you,
the rams of ⟦nebaioth|Nevayot|Nebaioth⟧ shall minister to you;
they shall go up with favor on my altar,
and the house of my beauty I will beautify.

[8] Who are these that fly like a thick cloud,
and like doves to their windows?

[9] For the coastlands shall wait for me,
and the ships of Tarshish at the first,
to bring your sons from afar,
their silver and their gold with them,
for the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and for the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
for he has beautified you.

[10] And foreigners shall build your walls,
and their kings shall minister to you;
for in my wrath I struck you,
and in my favor I have had compassion on you.

[11] And they shall open your gates continually;
day and night they shall not be shut,
to bring to you the wealth of nations,
and their kings led along.

[12] For the nation and the kingdom that will not serve you shall perish,
and the nations shall surely be laid waste.

[13] The glory of the ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ shall come to you,
cypress, plane and pine together,
to beautify the place of my sanctuary,
and the place of my feet I will honor.

[14] And there shall walk to you, bowed down,
the sons of those who afflicted you,
and they shall bow down at the soles of your feet,
all who spurned you;
and they shall call you the City of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ of the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[15] Instead of your being forsaken and hated,
with none passing through,
I will make you an everlasting exaltation,
an exultation of generation and generation.

[16] And you shall suck the milk of nations,
and the breast of kings you shall suck;
and you shall know that I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, am your savior
and your redeemer, the Mighty One of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧.

[17] Instead of the bronze I will bring gold,
and instead of the iron I will bring silver,
and instead of the wood, bronze,
and instead of the stones, iron;
and I will make your oversight ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧
and your taskmasters righteousness.

[18] Violence shall no more be heard in your land,
havoc and breaking within your borders;
and you shall call your walls ⟦yeshuah|Yeshuah|Salvation⟧,
and your gates ⟦tehillah|Tehillah|Praise⟧.

[19] The sun shall no more be to you
for light by day,
and for brightness the moon shall not give light to you;
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be to you an everlasting light,
and your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ your beauty.

[20] Your sun shall no more go down,
and your moon shall not be withdrawn;
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be to you an everlasting light,
and the days of your mourning shall be complete.

[21] And your people, all of them shall be righteous;
forever they shall possess the land,
the shoot of my planting,
the work of my hands,
that I may glorify myself.

[22] The small one shall become a thousand,
and the least one a mighty nation;
I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; in its time I will hasten it.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"God" → "Elohim"** (v9, 19) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **Speaker and person** (throughout) — Zion is addressed in the feminine singular ("you", "your"); "I" is Yahweh.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept (v2, 4, 5, 9–11, 14, 16–21), also where KJV has "but", "therefore", "yea" (v2, 10, 11, 12).
- **"is risen upon thee" → "has dawned upon you"** (v1) — Hebrew זָרַח "dawn, shine forth" (of the sun rising); the same verb in v2 (יִזְרַח "shall dawn") and its noun in v3 (זַרְחֵךְ "your dawning"); בָא, זָרָח perfects ("has come", "has dawned").
- **"gross darkness the people" → "dark cloud the folks"** (v2) — Hebrew עֲרָפֶל "dark cloud" (as Exod 20:21), לְאֻמִּים "folks" (plural); הַחֹשֶׁךְ "the darkness" with the article; אֶרֶץ "earth" without it. **"but the LORD shall arise upon thee" → "and upon you Yahweh shall dawn"** — וְ "and"; "upon you" stands first, set against the darkness of the first line.
- **"the Gentiles shall come" → "nations shall walk"** (v3) — Hebrew גּוֹיִם "nations" (no article); הָלַךְ "walk, go". The same in v5, 11, 16 ("nations"; KJV "Gentiles").
- **"all they gather themselves together, they come to thee" → "all of them have gathered, they have come to you"** (v4) — Hebrew perfects נִקְבְּצוּ בָאוּ. **"shall be nursed at thy side" → "shall be carried at the side"** — Hebrew עַל־צַד תֵּאָמַנָה, "upon the side", no "your"; אָמַן nifal, literally "be supported, be nursed" (as by an אֹמֵן, a foster parent): carried by those who tend them; "on the hip" says more than the word.
- **"and flow together, and thine heart shall fear, and be enlarged" → "and be radiant, and your heart shall tremble and grow wide"** (v5) — Hebrew נָהַר "shine, be radiant" (the same letters mean "stream", hence KJV "flow together"); פָּחַד "tremble, be in dread" (root of פַּחַד "dread"); רָחַב "be wide". **"because the abundance of the sea shall be converted unto thee" → "for the multitude of the sea shall be turned to you"** — הֲמוֹן "multitude"; יֵהָפֵךְ עָלַיִךְ "shall be turned upon you". **"the forces of the Gentiles" → "the wealth of nations"** — חֵיל גּוֹיִם; חַיִל is "ability, force, wealth": here what is brought (as v11; 61:6).
- **"The multitude of camels" → "A throng of camels"** (v6) — Hebrew שִׁפְעַת, "abundance, throng" (as 2 Kgs 9:17); הֲמוֹן "multitude" is the word of v5. **"the dromedaries" → "young camels"** — בִּכְרֵי, young male camels (meaning fairly sure). **"all they from Sheba" → "all of them from Sheba"**. **"they shall bring gold and incense" → "Gold and frankincense they shall carry"** — יִשָּׂאוּ "they shall lift up, carry"; לְבוֹנָה "frankincense"; Hebrew order. **"they shall shew forth the praises" → "the praises … they shall bring as news"** — יְבַשֵּׂרוּ, the verb of bringing news (בִּשֵּׂר); Matt 2:11 gold and frankincense.
- **"All the flocks" → "All the flock"** (v7) — Hebrew צֹאן, one collective word. **"they shall come up with acceptance on mine altar" → "they shall go up with favor on my altar"** — יַעֲלוּ עַל־רָצוֹן מִזְבְּחִי, literally "they shall go up upon favor, my altar" (the rams as offerings); רָצוֹן "favor" as v10; 61:2. **"I will glorify the house of my glory" → "the house of my beauty I will beautify"** — Hebrew וּבֵית תִּפְאַרְתִּי אֲפָאֵר, one root (פאר) twice: תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty", פֵּאֵר "beautify" (also v9, 13, 21); כָּבוֹד "glory" is another word.
- **"as a cloud" → "like a thick cloud"** (v8) — Hebrew עָב, the dense cloud (beside עָנָן "cloud"). **"their windows"** — אֲרֻבֹּת, latticed openings (of a dovecote).
- **"Surely the isles shall wait for me" → "For the coastlands shall wait for me"** (v9) — Hebrew כִּי־לִי אִיִּים יְקַוּוּ: כִּי "for", לִי "for me" stands first; אִיִּים "coastlands" (land reached by sea); קִוָּה "wait for". **"first" → "at the first"** — בָּרִאשֹׁנָה (first in time or first in order). **"thy sons"** — בָּנַיִךְ. **"unto the name … and to the Holy One" → "for the name … and for the Holy One"** — לְ "for, to". **"he hath glorified thee" → "he has beautified you"** — פֵאֲרָךְ, the verb of v7.
- **"the sons of strangers" → "foreigners"** (v10) — Hebrew בְּנֵי־נֵכָר, literally "sons of foreignness" (as 2 Sam 22:45); also 62:8. **"build up" → "build"** — וּבָנוּ. **"wrath"** — קֶצֶף. **"but in my favour have I had mercy on thee" → "and in my favor I have had compassion on you"** — וּבִרְצוֹנִי רִחַמְתִּיךְ; רִחַם "have compassion" (verb of רַחֲמִים).
- **"Therefore thy gates shall be open" → "And they shall open your gates"** (v11) — Hebrew וּפִתְּחוּ, pointed as piel perfect with וְ, "and they shall open" (no subject named: as one says "gates shall be opened"); the pointing as a passive is not what stands. תָּמִיד "continually" (as Exod 25:30). **"they shall not be shut day nor night" → "day and night they shall not be shut"** — Hebrew order. **"that men may bring unto thee the forces of the Gentiles" → "to bring to you the wealth of nations"** — לְהָבִיא, an infinitive; "men" is not in the Hebrew. **"and that their kings may be brought" → "and their kings led along"** — נְהוּגִים, passive participle of נָהַג "lead, drive" (as captives are driven or as a procession is led: the word does not say which; "in procession" is not in the Hebrew).
- **"yea, those nations shall be utterly wasted" → "and the nations shall surely be laid waste"** (v12) — Hebrew וְהַגּוֹיִם "and the nations" (article, no "those"); חָרֹב יֶחֱרָבוּ, infinitive absolute with its verb (literally "laying waste they shall be laid waste"), rendered "surely"; חָרֵב "be dry, be laid waste" (of water "dry up": the sense here is of lands).
- **"The glory of Lebanon" → "The glory of the Lebanon"** (v13) — Hebrew הַלְּבָנוֹן with the article. **"the fir tree, the pine tree, and the box together" → "cypress, plane and pine together"** — בְּרוֹשׁ, תִּדְהָר, תְּאַשּׁוּר, no articles (also Isa 41:19): the kinds are not certain (בְּרוֹשׁ cypress or juniper; תִּדְהָר plane or elm; תְּאַשּׁוּר pine, cypress or box); יַחְדָּו "together". **"I will make the place of my feet glorious" → "the place of my feet I will honor"** — אֲכַבֵּד, piel of כָּבֵד ("make heavy, give weight": honor; root of כָּבוֹד "glory"); Hebrew order.
- **"The sons also of them that afflicted thee shall come bending unto thee" → "And there shall walk to you, bowed down, the sons of those who afflicted you"** (v14) — Hebrew וְהָלְכוּ אֵלַיִךְ שְׁחוֹחַ בְּנֵי מְעַנַּיִךְ, in this order; שְׁחוֹחַ "bowing low"; עִנָּה "afflict". **"all they that despised thee shall bow themselves down at the soles of thy feet" → "and they shall bow down at the soles of your feet, all who spurned you"** — Hebrew order; נִאֵץ "spurn" (as Num 14:11); כַּפּוֹת רַגְלַיִךְ "the soles of your feet". **"The Zion of" → "Zion of"** — no article.
- **"Whereas thou hast been forsaken and hated" → "Instead of your being forsaken and hated"** (v15) — Hebrew תַּחַת הֱיוֹתֵךְ, "instead of your being". **"so that no man went through thee" → "with none passing through"** — וְאֵין עוֹבֵר. **"an eternal excellency" → "an everlasting exaltation"** — לִגְאוֹן עוֹלָם; גָּאוֹן "exaltation" (as Exod 15:7; Isa 2:10); עוֹלָם as a genitive "everlasting" (literally "of the age"). **"a joy of many generations" → "an exultation of generation and generation"** — מְשׂוֹשׂ "exultation" (as 8:6; 65:18), דּוֹר וָדוֹר "generation and generation" (as Ps 33:11).
- **"the milk of the Gentiles" → "the milk of nations"** (v16). **"and shalt suck the breast of kings" → "and the breast of kings you shall suck"** — Hebrew order; the second verb יָנַק is the same "suck". **"thy Saviour and thy Redeemer, the mighty One of Jacob" → "your savior and your redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob"** — מוֹשִׁיעֵךְ (participle of הוֹשִׁיעַ "save"), גֹאֲלֵךְ (of גָּאַל "redeem"); אֲבִיר יַעֲקֹב "the Mighty One of Jacob" (as Gen 49:24; Isa 49:26).
- **"For brass … and for wood brass" → "Instead of the bronze … and instead of the wood, bronze"** (v17) — Hebrew תַּחַת "instead of" four times, each with וְ "and" after the first; הַנְּחֹשֶׁת, הַבַּרְזֶל, הָעֵצִים, הָאֲבָנִים with the article; נְחֹשֶׁת "bronze". **"thy officers peace, and thine exactors righteousness" → "your oversight shalom, and your taskmasters righteousness"** — פְּקֻדָּתֵךְ "your oversight" (a singular noun: those set over you, as Num 3:32); נֹגְשַׂיִךְ "your taskmasters" (as Exod 3:7); שָׁלוֹם carries its token.
- **"wasting nor destruction" → "havoc and breaking"** (v18) — Hebrew שֹׁד וָשֶׁבֶר, "havoc" (as Prov 21:7) and שֶׁבֶר "breaking, shattering" (of a limb or a wall); no negative before them: the "no more be heard" of the first line governs them. **"but thou shalt call" → "and you shall call"** — וְ. **"Salvation" / "Praise"** — יְשׁוּעָה, תְּהִלָּה given as names (tokens; the token no longer carries a gloss in its form).
- **"The sun shall be no more thy light by day" → "The sun shall no more be to you for light by day"** (v19) — Hebrew לֹא־יִהְיֶה־לָּךְ עוֹד הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ לְאוֹר יוֹמָם; "and Yahweh shall be to you". **"and thy God thy glory" → "and your Elohim your beauty"** — לְתִפְאַרְתֵּךְ, תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty" (as v7), not כָּבוֹד.
- **"neither shall thy moon withdraw itself" → "and your moon shall not be withdrawn"** (v20) — Hebrew לֹא יֵאָסֵף, nifal of אָסַף "gather": literally "shall not be gathered in". **"go down"** — יָבוֹא, literally "come in" (of the setting sun). **"shall be ended" → "shall be complete"** — וְשָׁלְמוּ, from שָׁלֵם "be whole, complete".
- **"Thy people also shall be all righteous" → "And your people, all of them shall be righteous"** (v21) — Hebrew וְעַמֵּךְ כֻּלָּם צַדִּיקִים, no verb ("shall be" supplied). **"inherit" → "possess"** — יָרַשׁ (as Gen 22:17); אָרֶץ without the article ("the land" is the English). **"the branch of my planting" → "the shoot of my planting"** — נֵצֶר "shoot" (as 11:1); qere מַטָּעַי "my planting" is translated; the written form (ketiv) מטעו reads "his planting". **"that I may be glorified" → "that I may glorify myself"** — לְהִתְפָּאֵר, hitpael of the root of v7 ("glorify oneself, show one's beauty"; as Exod 8:9), not a passive.
- **"A little one shall become a thousand, and a small one a strong nation" → "The small one shall become a thousand, and the least one a mighty nation"** (v22) — Hebrew הַקָּטֹן, הַצָּעִיר with the article; לָאֶלֶף "a thousand" (אֶלֶף is also the word for a clan, a unit of a tribe: the former "a clan" gave that sense only); עָצוּם "mighty" (strong in number). **"I the LORD will hasten it in his time" → "I am Yahweh; in its time I will hasten it"** — אֲנִי יְהוָה; בְּעִתָּהּ "in its time" (feminine: the thing promised).

### Notes
- **v6** — Matt 2:11: gold and frankincense brought to the child.
- **v19–20** — echoed in Rev 21:23; 22:5; v3, 11 in Rev 21:24–26.
