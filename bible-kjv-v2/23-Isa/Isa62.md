# Isaiah 62

[1] On account of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ I will not keep silent,
and on account of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ I will not be quiet,
until her righteousness goes forth like brightness,
and her salvation like a torch that burns.

[2] And nations shall see your righteousness,
and all kings your glory;
and you shall be called by a new name,
which the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall pronounce.

[3] And you shall be a crown of beauty in the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and a turban of kingship in the palm of your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[4] It shall no more be said to you, "Forsaken,"
and to your land it shall no more be said, "Desolation";
for you shall be called ⟦hephzibah|Cheftzi-Vah|Hephzibah⟧,
and your land Beulah;
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ delights in you,
and your land shall be married.

[5] For as a young man becomes the husband of a virgin,
your sons shall become your husbands;
and with the exultation of a bridegroom over a bride
your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ shall exult over you.

[6] Upon your walls, ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
I have appointed watchmen;
all the day and all the night
continually they shall not be silent.
You who make ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ remember,
let there be no stillness for you,

[7] and do not give him stillness,
until he establishes, and until he makes ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧
a praise in the earth.

[8] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has sworn by his right hand
and by the arm of his strength:
"I will not again give your grain
as food to your enemies,
and foreigners shall not drink your new wine,
for which you have labored;

[9] but those who gather it shall eat it
and praise ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and those who collect it shall drink it
in the courts of my holiness."

[10] Pass through, pass through the gates;
clear the way of the people;
build up, build up the highway;
clear it of stones;
lift up a banner over the peoples.

[11] Behold, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has made it heard
to the end of the earth:
Say to the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
"Behold, your salvation comes;
behold, his reward is with him,
and his wage before him."

[12] And they shall call them "The holy people,
the redeemed of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧";
and you shall be called "Sought Out,
a city not forsaken."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"God" → "Elohim"** (v3, 5).
- **Speaker and person** (throughout) — Zion / Jerusalem is addressed in the feminine singular; "I" in v1 is not named (the prophet or Yahweh), in v6 and v8–9 it is Yahweh.
- **"For Zion's sake … for Jerusalem's sake" → "On account of Zion … on account of Jerusalem"** (v1) — Hebrew לְמַעַן before a noun (as Gen 18:24). **"will I not hold my peace … I will not rest" → "I will not keep silent … I will not be quiet"** — חָשָׁה "keep silent" (as v6), שָׁקַט "be quiet". **"the righteousness thereof … the salvation thereof" → "her righteousness … her salvation"** — feminine suffixes (the city). **"as a lamp that burneth" → "like a torch that burns"** — לַפִּיד "torch"; יִבְעָר "it burns".
- **"And the Gentiles shall see" → "And nations shall see"** (v2) — גּוֹיִם, no article. **"thou shalt be called" → "you shall be called"** — וְקֹרָא לָךְ, pual perfect with וְ, literally "and it shall be called to you". **"which the mouth of the LORD shall name" → "… shall pronounce"** — יִקֳּבֶנּוּ, from נָקַב "pierce, mark out": name distinctly (as Num 1:17).
- **"a crown of glory" → "a crown of beauty"** (v3) — Hebrew עֲטֶרֶת תִּפְאֶרֶת; תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty" (as 60:7). **"a royal diadem in the hand of thy God" → "a turban of kingship in the palm of your Elohim"** — qere וּצְנִיף "and a turban" is translated; the written form (ketiv) וצנוף is the same word spelled otherwise; מְלוּכָה "kingship"; בְּכַף "in the palm" (beside בְּיַד "in the hand").
- **"Thou shalt no more be termed Forsaken; neither shall thy land any more be termed Desolate" → "It shall no more be said to you, 'Forsaken,' and to your land it shall no more be said, 'Desolation'"** (v4) — Hebrew לֹא־יֵאָמֵר לָךְ עוֹד עֲזוּבָה, "it shall not be said to you any more"; עֲזוּבָה "forsaken" (feminine participle), שְׁמָמָה "desolation" (a noun). **"but thou shalt be called Hephzi-bah, and thy land Beulah" → "for you shall be called Hephzibah, and your land Beulah"** — כִּי "for"; the two new names stand as printed: חֶפְצִי־בָהּ *Cheftzi-vah*, "my delight is in her" (the name of the mother of Manasseh, 2 Kgs 21:1: token), בְּעוּלָה *Be'ulah*, "married" (feminine passive participle of בָּעַל, "owned by a husband"; no token key exists, so the plain form). The first two words are given as words, as the Hebrew uses them, and the new names as names; both pairs say the same: forsaken → delighted in, desolate → married. **"delighteth in thee"** — חָפֵץ בָּךְ "delights in you", the root of the name. **"shall be married"** — תִּבָּעֵל, nifal of בָּעַל, the root of *Be'ulah*.
- **"For as a young man marrieth a virgin, so shall thy sons marry thee" → "For as a young man becomes the husband of a virgin, your sons shall become your husbands"** (v5) — Hebrew כִּי־יִבְעַל בָּחוּר בְּתוּלָה יִבְעָלוּךְ בָּנָיִךְ: בָּעַל "become the husband of" (literally "become owner of", as Deut 24:1); בְּתוּלָה "virgin"; no "so"; MT בָּנָיִךְ "your sons" is kept (the change of pointing to בֹּנַיִךְ "your builder", that is Yahweh, is a conjecture). **"and as the bridegroom rejoiceth over the bride, so shall thy God rejoice over thee" → "and with the exultation of a bridegroom over a bride your Elohim shall exult over you"** — Hebrew וּמְשׂוֹשׂ חָתָן עַל־כַּלָּה, a noun "exultation" (as 60:15) with no "as"; יָשִׂישׂ "exult" (as 61:10); no articles.
- **"I have set watchmen upon thy walls, O Jerusalem, which shall never hold their peace day nor night" → "Upon your walls, Jerusalem, I have appointed watchmen; all the day and all the night continually they shall not be silent"** (v6) — Hebrew order; no "O" (the Hebrew has no word for it); הִפְקַדְתִּי "I have appointed" (as Gen 39:4); שֹׁמְרִים "watchmen" (as Ps 127:1); תָּמִיד "continually", לֹא יֶחֱשׁוּ "they shall not be silent" (the verb of v1). **"ye that make mention of the LORD" → "You who make Yahweh remember"** — הַמַּזְכִּרִים, hifil of זָכַר, "who cause to remember" (here: who remind Yahweh); "mention" is the same verb in other places. **"keep not silence" → "let there be no stillness for you"** — אַל־דֳּמִי לָכֶם, no verb; דֳּמִי "stillness" (root דָּמַם "be still").
- **"And give him no rest" → "and do not give him stillness"** (v7) — the same דֳּמִי. **"till he establish, and till he make Jerusalem a praise in the earth" → "until he establishes, and until he makes Jerusalem a praise in the earth"** — כּוֹנֵן "establish" without an object; עַד twice.
- **"by the arm of his strength"** (v8) — וּבִזְרוֹעַ עֻזּוֹ, kept. **"Surely I will no more give" → "I will not again give"** — oath form אִם־אֶתֵּן, literally "if I give …": a sworn "I will not" (the second line, וְאִם־יִשְׁתּוּ, likewise "shall not drink"). **"thy corn to be meat" → "your grain as food"** — דָּגָן "grain", מַאֲכָל "food". **"the sons of the stranger" → "foreigners"** — בְּנֵי־נֵכָר (as 60:10). **"thy wine" → "your new wine"** — תִּירוֹשׁ. **"laboured"** — יָגַע.
- **"But they that have gathered it … and they that have brought it together" → "but those who gather it … and those who collect it"** (v9) — participles מְאַסְפָיו (אָסַף "gather") and מְקַבְּצָיו (קִבֵּץ "collect"), two verbs; כִּי after the oath "but". **"in the courts of my holiness"** — בְּחַצְרוֹת קָדְשִׁי, kept (the former "of my sanctuary" was מִקְדָּשׁ, which does not stand here).
- **"prepare ye the way of the people" → "clear the way of the people"** (v10) — Hebrew פַּנּוּ, piel of פָּנָה "turn": "clear" (as Lev 14:36). **"cast up, cast up" → "build up, build up"** — סֹלּוּ סֹלּוּ (סָלַל, the verb of מְסִלָּה "highway"). **"gather out the stones" → "clear it of stones"** — סַקְּלוּ מֵאֶבֶן, piel of סָקַל "stone": literally "unstone from stone". **"lift up a standard for the people" → "lift up a banner over the peoples"** — נֵס "banner"; עַל־הָעַמִּים, plural.
- **"hath proclaimed unto the end of the world" → "has made it heard to the end of the earth"** (v11) — Hebrew הִשְׁמִיעַ, hifil of שָׁמַע "hear": "make heard"; "it" supplied; קְצֵה הָאָרֶץ "the end of the earth". **"Behold, thy salvation cometh" → "Behold, your salvation comes"** — יִשְׁעֵךְ "your salvation" (lower case: the Hebrew does not make it a title; "his reward … his wage" then speak of the one who brings it). Compare Isa 40:10 (the same last two lines) and Matt 21:5 (which quotes "Say to the daughter of Zion" with Zech 9:9). **"his work before him" → "his wage before him"** — פְּעֻלָּתוֹ, "his wage" (what is earned: as 61:8; Lev 19:13); שָׂכָר "reward" (as 40:10).
- **"The holy people, The redeemed of the LORD … Sought out, A city not forsaken"** (v12) — Hebrew עַם־הַקֹּדֶשׁ, literally "the people of holiness"; גְּאוּלֵי יְהוָה "the redeemed of Yahweh" (passive participle of גָּאַל); דְּרוּשָׁה "sought out" (feminine passive participle of דָּרַשׁ "seek"), עִיר לֹא נֶעֱזָבָה "a city not forsaken" (the root of "Forsaken" in v4). These are given as words, like "Forsaken" in v4; only *Cheftzi-vah* and *Be'ulah* stand as names.

### Notes
- **v4** — the names: *Cheftzi-vah* "my delight is in her" (2 Kgs 21:1, the mother of Manasseh), *Be'ulah* "married".
- **v11** — Isa 40:10; Rev 22:12.
