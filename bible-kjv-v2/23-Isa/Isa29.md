# Isaiah 29

[1] Woe, Ariel, Ariel,
town where David camped!
Add year upon year;
let feasts go round.

[2] And I will straiten Ariel,
and there shall be mourning and moaning,
and she shall be to me like an Ariel.

[3] And I will camp like a circle against you,
and I will besiege you with a post,
and I will raise up siege works against you.

[4] And you shall be low; from the earth you shall speak,
and from the dust your saying shall be bowed down;
and your voice shall be like a ghost from the earth,
and from the dust your saying shall chirp.

[5] And like fine dust shall be the multitude of your strangers,
and like passing chaff the multitude of the ruthless;
and it shall be suddenly, in an instant.

[6] From ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts you shall be visited
with thunder and with quaking and a great sound,
tempest and storm,
and the flame of a devouring fire.

[7] And it shall be like a dream, a vision of the night,
the multitude of all the nations that wage war against Ariel,
and all who wage war against her and her stronghold, and those who straiten her.

[8] And it shall be as when the hungry one dreams,
and behold, he is eating,
and he awakes, and his soul is empty;
and as when the thirsty one dreams,
and behold, he is drinking,
and he awakes, and behold, he is weary,
and his soul is longing:
so shall the multitude of all the nations be
that wage war against Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

[9] Linger, and be astounded;
blind yourselves, and be blind!
They are drunk, and not with wine;
they sway, and not with strong drink.

[10] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has poured out upon you
a spirit of deep sleep,
and he has shut your eyes, the prophets,
and your heads, the seers, he has covered.

[11] And the vision of all this has become to you like the words of the sealed book, which they give to one who knows writing, saying, "Please read this," and he says, "I cannot, for it is sealed."

[12] And the book is given to one who does not know writing, saying, "Please read this," and he says, "I do not know writing."

[13] And ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ said:

"Because this people has drawn near with its mouth,
and with its lips they have honored me,
and its heart it has put far from me,
and their fear of me has become a commandment of men, taught,

[14] therefore, behold, I will again
do wonderfully with this people,
wonderfully, and a wonder;
and the wisdom of its wise ones shall perish,
and the understanding of its understanding ones shall hide itself."

[15] Woe to those who go deep from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to hide counsel,
and their deeds are in a dark place,
and they say, "Who sees us? And who knows us?"

[16] Your turning upside down!
Shall the potter be reckoned as the clay,
that the work should say of its maker,
"He did not make me";
and the thing formed say of its potter,
"He does not understand"?

[17] Is it not yet a little, a very little,
and ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ shall turn into the garden land,
and the garden land shall be reckoned as the forest?

[18] And in that day the deaf shall hear
the words of a book,
and out of gloom and out of darkness
the eyes of the blind shall see.

[19] And the humble shall add gladness in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and the needy of mankind shall be glad in the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[20] For the ruthless is gone,
and the scoffer is finished,
and all who watch for wickedness shall be cut off,

[21] who make a man sin by a word,
and for him who reproves in the gate they lay a snare,
and turn aside the righteous by emptiness.

[22] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to the house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, who ransomed ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧:

"Not now shall ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ be ashamed,
and not now shall his face grow pale.

[23] For when he sees his children,
the work of my hands, in his midst,
they shall sanctify my name;
and they shall sanctify the Holy One of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ they shall dread.

[24] And those who wander in spirit shall know understanding,
and murmurers shall learn teaching."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v23) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v6) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order (v5, 7, 8 were reordered); v11–12 stay prose; v13 and v22 keep the speech formula on a line of its own.
- **"Woe to Ariel" → "Woe, Ariel, Ariel"** (v1; so v15 "Woe to those who") — Hebrew הוֹי, the prophets' word of lament and threat (KJV "Woe", the earlier text "Ah"); in v15 with a participle, "to those who" supplied.
- **"Ariel" kept** (v1, 2, 7) — Hebrew אֲרִיאֵל, a name for Jerusalem (the token's Czech forms). Its meaning is not certain: "lion of El" (אֲרִי + אֵל), or "altar hearth", as הַאֲרִאֵיל in Ezek 43:15, 16; v2 "she shall be to me like an Ariel" plays on the second sense (a city burning like the altar hearth). The verse keeps the name each time.
- **"the city where David dwelt" → "town where David camped"** (v1) — Hebrew קִרְיַת חָנָה דָוִד: קִרְיָה "town" (not עִיר), חָנָה "camp" (the same verb as v3 "I will camp against you").
- **"Add ye year to year; let them kill sacrifices" → "Add year upon year; let feasts go round"** (v1) — Hebrew סְפוּ שָׁנָה עַל־שָׁנָה חַגִּים יִנְקֹפוּ: נָקַף "go round" (the round of the year); חַגִּים "feasts" (not "sacrifices"), no article.
- **"Yet I will distress Ariel" → "And I will straiten Ariel"** (v2; so v7 "those who straiten her") — Hebrew וַהֲצִיקוֹתִי, hifil of צוּק "press, straiten" (kept apart from צָרַר "distress"). **"heaviness and sorrow" → "mourning and moaning"** — Hebrew תַאֲנִיָּה וַאֲנִיָּה, two nouns of one root (the sound kept). **"and there shall be"** — the verb is feminine, agreeing with the noun; "she shall be mourning" is also possible.
- **"round about" → "like a circle"** (v3) — Hebrew כַדּוּר "like a ball, a circle" (the Septuagint is said to read כְּדָוִד "like David", from memory; the MT kept). **"lay siege against thee with a mount" → "besiege you with a post"** — Hebrew וְצַרְתִּי עָלַיִךְ מֻצָּב: מֻצָּב "a thing set up" (a post, palisade or outpost: uncertain). **"forts" → "siege works"** — Hebrew מְצֻרֹת, of the root of מָצוֹר "siege".
- **"thou shalt be brought down" → "you shall be low"** (v4) — Hebrew וְשָׁפַלְתְּ. **"thy speech shall be low" → "your saying shall be bowed down"** — אִמְרָה "saying", תִּשַּׁח of שָׁחַח "be bowed down". **"as of one that hath a familiar spirit" → "like a ghost"** — Hebrew כְּאוֹב: אוֹב "ghost" (the spirit, not the person who calls it up; the sense is not certain); "the voice of" is not written. **"whisper" → "chirp"** — Hebrew תְּצַפְצֵף, the thin sound of a bird, said of the dead (as Isa 8:19).
- **"thy strangers" kept as "your strangers"** (v5) — Hebrew זָרַיִךְ, "strangers" (the earlier "foreign foes" was a gloss). **"the terrible ones" → "the ruthless"** (v5, 20) — Hebrew עָרִיצִים. **"chaff that passeth away" → "passing chaff"** — מֹץ עֹבֵר. **"at an instant suddenly" → "suddenly, in an instant"** — Hebrew לְפֶתַע פִּתְאֹם, in that order.
- **"Thou shalt be visited of the LORD of hosts" → "From Yahweh of Hosts you shall be visited"** (v6) — Hebrew מֵעִם יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת תִּפָּקֵד, literally "from with Yahweh"; פָּקַד "visit" (attend to, here for punishment); the form may also be "she shall be visited". **"great noise" → "a great sound"** — קוֹל גָּדוֹל, the word "voice" of a sound. **"storm and tempest" → "tempest and storm"** — סוּפָה וּסְעָרָה. **"earthquake" → "quaking"** — רַעַשׁ.
- **"that fight against" → "that wage war against"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew הַצֹּבְאִים, the verb of צָבָא "host". **"her munition" → "her stronghold"** — מְצֹדָתָהּ. "her and her stronghold" may be the two objects of the warring or "all who war against her, and her stronghold"; the Hebrew does not decide.
- **"a hungry man" → "the hungry one"** (v8; so "the thirsty one") — Hebrew הָרָעֵב, with the article; "man" is not written. **"his soul is empty"** — Hebrew וְרֵיקָה נַפְשׁוֹ, נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (the earlier "his hunger" was an explanation). **"faint" → "weary"** — עָיֵף. **"his soul hath appetite" → "his soul is longing"** — שׁוֹקֵקָה.
- **"Stay yourselves, and wonder" → "Linger, and be astounded"** (v9) — Hebrew הִתְמַהְמְהוּ וּתְמָהוּ: the first verb is of מָהַהּ "linger, delay" (Gen 19:16), set beside תָּמַהּ "be astounded" for the sound. **"cry ye out, and cry" → "blind yourselves, and be blind"** — הִשְׁתַּעַשְׁעוּ וָשֹׁעוּ, of שָׁעַע "be smeared over, blind" (the same root as 32:3; the like-sounding hitpalpel "delight oneself" is another root). **"they are drunken … they stagger" kept as statements** — Hebrew שָׁכְרוּ … נָעוּ are perfects, not commands; נוּעַ "sway".
- **"the LORD hath poured out upon you"** (v10) — Hebrew נָסַךְ, the verb of the drink offering. **"closed" → "shut"** — עִצֵּם. **"your rulers, the seers" → "your heads, the seers"** — Hebrew רָאשֵׁיכֶם "your heads"; הַחֹזִים "the seers" (חֹזֶה, the "gazer"). "the prophets" and "the seers" stand in apposition to "your eyes" and "your heads"; many take them as later explanations; the Hebrew kept.
- **"the vision of all" → "the vision of all this"** (v11) — Hebrew חָזוּת הַכֹּל, literally "the vision of the whole". **"one that is learned" → "one who knows writing"** (v11, 12) — Hebrew יוֹדֵעַ סֵפֶר, literally "one who knows book (letters)": the qere סֵפֶר; the written form הספר "the book". **"Read this, I pray thee" → "Please read this"** — Hebrew נָא.
- **"Forasmuch as this people draw near me with their mouth" → "Because this people has drawn near with its mouth"** (v13) — Hebrew נִגַּשׁ, nifal, "has drawn near"; "me" is not written. The Hebrew passes from the singular (its mouth, its heart) to the plural (they have honored, their fear), as kept. **"have removed their heart far from me" → "its heart it has put far from me"** — רִחַק, piel. **"is taught by the precept of men" → "has become a commandment of men, taught"** — Hebrew וַתְּהִי יִרְאָתָם אֹתִי מִצְוַת אֲנָשִׁים מְלֻמָּדָה. Quoted in Matt 15:8–9 (and Mark 7:6–7) in Greek: "in vain they worship me, teaching as teachings commandments of men" (μάτην δὲ σέβονταί με, διδάσκοντες διδασκαλίας ἐντάλματα ἀνθρώπων); the Greek follows the Septuagint's wording (from memory). The Hebrew is translated as it stands.
- **"I will proceed to do a marvellous work among this people, even a marvellous work and a wonder" → "I will again do wonderfully with this people, wonderfully, and a wonder"** (v14) — Hebrew הִנְנִי יוֹסִף לְהַפְלִיא … הַפְלֵא וָפֶלֶא: יָסַף + infinitive "again" (literally "I add to do wonderfully"); the infinitive absolute הַפְלֵא and the noun פֶּלֶא "wonder" follow. **"their wise men … their prudent men" → "its wise ones … its understanding ones"** — חֲכָמָיו, נְבֹנָיו; בִּינָה "understanding". **"shall be hid" → "shall hide itself"** — תִּסְתַּתָּר, hitpael.
- **"seek deep to hide their counsel from the LORD" → "go deep from Yahweh to hide counsel"** (v15) — Hebrew הַמַּעֲמִיקִים מֵיהוָה לַסְתִּר עֵצָה, in that order; "their" is not written. **"their works are in the dark" → "their deeds are in a dark place"** — מַחְשָׁךְ. **"and they say"** — וַיֹּאמְרוּ; "And who knows us" keeps the second וּ.
- **"Surely your turning of things upside down" → "Your turning upside down!"** (v16) — Hebrew הַפְכְּכֶם, one word, an outcry. **"shall be esteemed as the potter's clay" → "Shall the potter be reckoned as the clay"** — Hebrew אִם־כְּחֹמֶר הַיֹּצֵר יֵחָשֵׁב, a question (אִם); חָשַׁב "reckon" (v16, 17). **"him that framed it" → "its potter"** — the Hebrew has the one word יוֹצֵר "former, potter" in both lines (הַיֹּצֵר · לְיוֹצְרוֹ), and יֵצֶר "the thing formed" beside it. **"He had no understanding" → "He does not understand"** — לֹא הֵבִין. לְ after "say" may be "to" or "of"; "of" fits the third person.
- **"a very little while" → "yet a little, a very little"** (v17) — Hebrew עוֹד מְעַט מִזְעָר. **"shall be turned into a fruitful field" → "shall turn into the garden land"** (v17; so 32:15, 16) — Hebrew וְשָׁב … לַכַּרְמֶל, שׁוּב "turn"; כַּרְמֶל, with the article, is the planted land, orchard and vineyard (as "the forest of its garden land", Isa 37:24), not Mount Carmel. **"the forest"** — לַיַּעַר, with the article.
- **"out of obscurity, and out of darkness" → "out of gloom and out of darkness"** (v18) — מֵאֹפֶל וּמֵחֹשֶׁךְ; "their" is not written.
- **"The meek also shall increase their joy" → "And the humble shall add gladness"** (v19) — Hebrew וְיָסְפוּ עֲנָוִים … שִׂמְחָה: עָנָו "humble" (bowed low; not "meek"), שִׂמְחָה "gladness". **"the poor among men" → "the needy of mankind"** — אֶבְיוֹנֵי אָדָם. **"rejoice" → "be glad"** — גִּיל.
- **"is brought to nought … is consumed" → "is gone … is finished"** (v20) — Hebrew perfects אָפֵס, כָלָה, said of what is certain; then וְנִכְרְתוּ "and shall be cut off". **"the scorner" → "the scoffer"** — לֵץ. **"all that watch for iniquity" → "all who watch for wickedness"** — שֹׁקְדֵי אָוֶן: שָׁקַד "keep awake, watch"; אָוֶן "wickedness".
- **"That make a man an offender for a word" → "who make a man sin by a word"** (v21) — Hebrew מַחֲטִיאֵי אָדָם בְּדָבָר, hifil "cause to sin"; in a court the sense may be "declare guilty in a case" (דָּבָר "word" or "matter"): the Hebrew kept. **"turn aside the just for a thing of nought" → "turn aside the righteous by emptiness"** — Hebrew וַיַּטּוּ בַתֹּהוּ צַדִּיק, תֹּהוּ "emptiness" (the word of Gen 1:2).
- **"who redeemed Abraham, concerning the house of Jacob" → "to the house of Jacob, who ransomed Abraham"** (v22) — Hebrew אֶל־בֵּית יַעֲקֹב אֲשֶׁר פָּדָה אֶת־אַבְרָהָם, in that order: אֶל "to", פָּדָה "ransom" (גָּאַל is "redeem"). **"shall not now be ashamed, neither shall his face now wax pale" → "Not now shall Jacob be ashamed, and not now shall his face grow pale"** — Hebrew לֹא־עַתָּה … וְלֹא עַתָּה, "not now", that is, no longer.
- **"his children" kept** (v23) — Hebrew יְלָדָיו, "children" (יֶלֶד). **"shall fear the God of Israel" → "the Elohim of Israel they shall dread"** — Hebrew יַעֲרִיצוּ, hifil of עָרַץ "dread" (the root of עָרִיץ "ruthless", v5, 20), in the Hebrew order.
- **"They also that erred in spirit shall come to understanding" → "And those who wander in spirit shall know understanding"** (v24) — Hebrew תֹעֵי־רוּחַ (תָּעָה "wander"), וְיָדְעוּ בִינָה "shall know understanding". **"they that murmured" → "murmurers"** — רוֹגְנִים (the root of נִרְגַּן "murmur"). **"shall learn doctrine" → "shall learn teaching"** — לֶקַח, literally "what is received".

### Notes
- **Ariel** — Jerusalem (v8 "Mount Zion"); the name and its sense: see Changes v1.
- **v13** — quoted in Matt 15:8–9; Mark 7:6–7.
- **v14** — quoted in 1 Cor 1:19, in the wording of the Septuagint (from memory).
