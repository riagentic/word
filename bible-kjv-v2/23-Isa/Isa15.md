# Isaiah 15

[1] The burden of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧.

For in the night Ar of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is laid waste,
it is undone;
for in the night ⟦kir|Qir|Kir⟧ of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is laid waste,
it is undone.

[2] He has gone up to the house, and ⟦dibon|Divon|Dibon⟧,
to the high places, to weep;
over ⟦nebo|Nevo|Nebo⟧ and over ⟦medeba|Meidva|Medeba⟧
⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ wails;
on all its heads is baldness,
every beard is clipped.

[3] In its streets they have girded on sackcloth;
on its roofs and in its squares
all of it wails, going down in weeping.

[4] And ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧ has cried out, and ⟦elealeh|Elaleh|Elealeh⟧;
as far as ⟦jahaz|Yahatz|Jahaz⟧ their voice is heard;
therefore the armed men of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ shout;
his soul quivers within him.

[5] My heart cries out for ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧;
her fugitives, as far as ⟦zoar|Tzoar|Zoar⟧,
⟦eglath-shelishiyah|Eglat Shelishiyah|Eglath-shelishiyah⟧;
for the ascent of ⟦luhith|Luchit|Luhith⟧,
with weeping he goes up on it;
for on the way of ⟦horonaim|Choronayim|Horonaim⟧ they raise an outcry of breaking.

[6] For the waters of Nimrim
will be desolations;
for the grass is dried out, the vegetation is at an end,
green there is none.

[7] Therefore the abundance he has made
and their store,
over the wadi of the willows
they carry them.

[8] For the outcry has gone around
the border of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧;
as far as ⟦eglaim|Eglayim|Eglaim⟧ is her wailing,
and Beer-Elim, her wailing.

[9] For the waters of ⟦dimon|Dimon|Dibon⟧ are full of blood;
for I will set upon ⟦dimon|Dimon|Dibon⟧ added things:
for the escape of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ a lion,
and for the remnant of the ground.

## Changes

- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, by the accents; the heading "The burden of Moab" is the first part of v1 in the Hebrew. v5 keeps the last colon in one line.
- **"The burden of Moab"** (v1) — Hebrew מַשָּׂא מוֹאָב, "burden" (as 13:1). **"Because in the night Ar of Moab is laid waste, and brought to silence" → "For in the night Ar of Moab is laid waste, it is undone"** — Hebrew כִּי "for" (or "surely"); בְּלֵיל, "in the night (when)"; שֻׁדַּד, pual of שָׁדַד "lay waste" (the שֹׁד "havoc" of 13:6); נִדְמָה "it is undone" (from דָּמָה "cease, be cut off"; KJV "brought to silence" from דָּמַם "be silent"); the subject is the city (feminine): "it", not "Moab". עָר "Ar" and קִיר "Kir" are names (עִיר / קִיר also "city", "wall").
- **"He is gone up to Bajith, and to Dibon, the high places, to weep" → "He has gone up to the house, and Dibon, to the high places, to weep"** (v2) — Hebrew עָלָה הַבַּיִת, "he has gone up to the house" (with the article: a temple; KJV took it as a place name "Bajith"); the subject is not named (Moab); וְדִיבֹן הַבָּמוֹת, "and Dibon, to the high places". **"Moab shall howl" → "Moab wails"** — Hebrew יְיֵלִיל, hifil of יָלַל (also v3; 16:7). **"on all their heads shall be baldness, and every beard cut off" → "on all its heads is baldness, every beard is clipped"** — Hebrew רֹאשָׁיו "his heads"; קָרְחָה "baldness"; גְּרוּעָה "clipped" (גָּרַע "cut short").
- **"In their streets they shall gird themselves with sackcloth: on the tops of their houses, and in their streets, every one shall howl, weeping abundantly" → "In its streets they have girded on sackcloth; on its roofs and in its squares all of it wails, going down in weeping"** (v3) — Hebrew חוּצוֹת "streets", רְחֹבוֹת "squares" (two words); גַּגּוֹת "roofs"; כֻּלֹּה "all of it"; יֹרֵד בַּבֶּכִי, "going down in weeping" (as tears run down). The suffixes are masculine and feminine ("his streets", "her roofs": Moab and the city).
- **"And Heshbon shall cry, and Elealeh … therefore the armed soldiers of Moab shall cry out; his life shall be grievous unto him" → "And Heshbon has cried out, and Elealeh … therefore the armed men of Moab shout; his soul quivers within him"** (v4) — Hebrew וַתִּזְעַק, a past form; חֲלֻצֵי מוֹאָב "the armed men of Moab"; יָרִיעוּ "shout" (of voices); נַפְשׁוֹ יָרְעָה לּוֹ, "his soul quivers to him" (יָרַע, only here: "tremble" or "be grieved"; uncertain).
- **"his fugitives shall flee unto Zoar, an heifer of three years old: for by the mounting up of Luhith with weeping shall they go it up; for in the way of Horonaim they shall raise up a cry of destruction" → "her fugitives, as far as Zoar, Eglath-shelishiyah; for the ascent of Luhith, with weeping he goes up on it; for on the way of Horonaim they raise an outcry of breaking"** (v5) — Hebrew בְּרִיחֶהָ "her fugitives" (as pointed, the form is that of בְּרִיחַ "bar": "her bars", her defenders; uncertain); no verb "flee". עֶגְלַת שְׁלִשִׁיָּה, "a heifer of three years", here taken as a place name (token) beside Zoar; as a figure for Moab it is also possible. מַעֲלֵה הַלּוּחִית "the ascent of Luhith"; יַעֲלֶה singular, "he goes up"; זַעֲקַת־שֶׁבֶר "an outcry of breaking"; יְעֹעֵרוּ, a rare form of עוּר "rouse": "they raise".
- **"For the waters of Nimrim shall be desolate: for the hay is withered away, the grass faileth, there is no green thing" → "For the waters of Nimrim will be desolations; for the grass is dried out, the vegetation is at an end, green there is none"** (v6) — Hebrew מְשַׁמּוֹת "desolations"; יָבֵשׁ "be dried out"; חָצִיר "grass", דֶּשֶׁא "vegetation", יֶרֶק "green"; כָּלָה "come to an end"; לֹא הָיָה "there has not been".
- **"Therefore the abundance they have gotten, and that which they have laid up, shall they carry away to the brook of the willows" → "Therefore the abundance he has made and their store, over the wadi of the willows they carry them"** (v7) — Hebrew יִתְרָה עָשָׂה "the abundance he has made" (singular verb); פְּקֻדָּתָם, "their store" (פְּקֻדָּה, elsewhere "oversight": what is laid up in charge); עַל "over"; נַחַל "wadi"; עֲרָבִים "willows" (or poplars).
- **"For the cry is gone round about the borders of Moab; the howling thereof unto Eglaim, and the howling thereof unto Beer-elim" → "For the outcry has gone around the border of Moab; as far as Eglaim is her wailing, and Beer-Elim, her wailing"** (v8) — Hebrew הִקִּיף "go around"; הַזְּעָקָה "the outcry"; גְּבוּל "border"; יְלָלָה "wailing".
- **"For the waters of Dimon shall be full of blood: for I will bring more upon Dimon, lions upon him that escapeth of Moab, and upon the remnant of the land" → "For the waters of Dimon are full of blood; for I will set upon Dimon added things: for the escape of Moab a lion, and for the remnant of the ground"** (v9) — Hebrew דִּימוֹן "Dimon" as printed (twice; the Isaiah scroll from Qumran and the Vulgate have "Dibon", from memory; a play on דָּם "blood"); אָשִׁית "I will set"; נוֹסָפוֹת "added things"; פְּלֵיטָה "escape" (those who escape); אַרְיֵה "a lion" (singular); שְׁאֵרִית "remnant"; אֲדָמָה "ground".

### Notes
- **Isa 15–16 beside Jer 48** — much of the lament over Moab stands again in Jer 48 (Jer 48:29–38, from memory); each is translated as printed.
