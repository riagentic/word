# Isaiah 15

[1] An oracle concerning ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧.

Because Ar of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is laid waste in a night,
⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is undone;
because ⟦kir|Qir|Kir⟧ of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is laid waste in a night,
⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is undone.

[2] He has gone up to the temple, and to ⟦dibon|Divon|Dibon⟧,
to the high places to weep;
over ⟦nebo|Nevo|Nebo⟧ and over ⟦medeba|Meidva|Medeba⟧
⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ wails.
On every head is baldness;
every beard is shorn;

[3] in the streets they wear sackcloth;
on the housetops and in the squares
everyone wails and melts in tears.

[4] ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧ and ⟦elealeh|Elaleh|Elealeh⟧ cry out;
their voice is heard as far as ⟦jahaz|Yahatz|Jahaz⟧;
therefore the armed men of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ cry aloud;
his soul trembles.

[5] My heart cries out for ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧;
her fugitives flee to ⟦zoar|Tzoar|Zoar⟧,
to ⟦eglath-shelishiyah|Eglat Shelishiyah|Eglath-shelishiyah⟧.
For at the ascent of ⟦luhith|Luchit|Luhith⟧
they go up weeping;
on the road to ⟦horonaim|Choronayim|Horonaim⟧
they raise a cry of destruction;

[6] the waters of Nimrim
are a desolation;
the grass is withered, the vegetation fails,
the greenery is no more.

[7] Therefore the abundance they have gained
and what they have laid up
they carry away
over the Brook of the Willows.

[8] For a cry has gone
around the land of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧;
her wailing reaches to ⟦eglaim|Eglayim|Eglaim⟧;
her wailing reaches to Beer-elim.

[9] For the waters of ⟦dimon|Dimon|Dibon⟧ are full of blood;
but I will bring upon ⟦dimon|Dimon|Dibon⟧ even more —
a lion for those of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ who escape,
for the remnant of the land.

## Changes

- **"Dimon" kept** (v9) — Hebrew דִּימוֹן *Dimon* (MT); "Dibon" (ESV, with the Dead Sea Scrolls and Vulgate) is the traditional reading only.
- **Poetic structure** — entire chapter formatted as verse/lamentation; KJV prose paragraphs missed the dirge structure.
