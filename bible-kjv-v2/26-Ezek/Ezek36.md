# Ezekiel 36

[1] "And you, son of man, prophesy to the mountains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and say: Mountains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[2] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Because the enemy has said against you, 'Aha!' and, 'The everlasting high places have become ours for a possession,'

[3] therefore prophesy, and say: Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Because, even because of making you desolate and panting after you from all around, that you should be a possession for the remnant of the nations, and you have been taken up on the lip of the tongue and the report of the people —

[4] therefore, mountains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, hear the word of ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ to the mountains and to the hills, to the channels and to the valleys, and to the desolate ruins and to the forsaken cities, which have become a plunder and a derision to the remnant of the nations that are all around —

[5] therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Surely in the fire of my jealousy I have spoken against the remnant of the nations and against Edom, all of it, who have given my land to themselves as a possession with the gladness of all the heart, with contempt of soul, in order that its pasture land be for plunder.

[6] Therefore prophesy concerning the ground of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and say to the mountains and to the hills, to the channels and to the valleys: Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, in my jealousy and in my fury I have spoken, because the dishonor of the nations you have borne.

[7] Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: I have lifted up my hand: surely the nations that are around you — they shall bear their dishonor.

[8] But you, mountains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, your bough you shall give, and your fruit you shall bear for my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; for they have drawn near to come.

[9] For behold, I am toward you, and I will turn to you, and you shall be worked and sown.

[10] And I will multiply man on you, all the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, all of it; and the cities shall be inhabited, and the ruins shall be built.

[11] And I will multiply on you man and beast, and they shall multiply and be fruitful; and I will cause you to be inhabited as in your former times, and I will do better than at your beginnings; and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] And I will make man walk on you, my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they shall possess you, and you shall be their inheritance, and you shall no more again bereave them.

[13] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Because they say to you, 'A devourer of man are you, and a bereaver of your nations you have been,'

[14] therefore man you shall devour no more, and your nations you shall bereave no more, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[15] And I will no more let you hear the dishonor of the nations, and the reproach of the peoples you shall bear no more, and your nations you shall make stumble no more, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

[16] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[17] "Son of man, the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were dwelling on their ground, and they defiled it by their way and by their doings; like the uncleanness of the impurity was their way before me.

[18] And I poured out my fury upon them for the blood that they had shed on the land, and with their idols they had defiled it.

[19] And I scattered them among the nations, and they were strewn among the lands; according to their way and according to their doings I judged them.

[20] And he came to the nations to which they came, and they profaned my holy name, in that it was said of them, 'These are the people of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and from his land they have gone out.'

[21] And I had pity on my holy name, which the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had profaned among the nations to which they came.

[22] "Therefore say to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Not for your sake am I acting, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, but for my holy name, which you have profaned among the nations to which you came.

[23] And I will sanctify my great name, which has been profaned among the nations, which you have profaned in their midst; and the nations shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, when I am sanctified in you before their eyes.

[24] And I will take you from the nations, and gather you from all the lands, and bring you to your ground.

[25] And I will dash clean water on you, and you shall be clean; from all your uncleannesses and from all your idols I will cleanse you.

[26] And I will give you a new heart, and a new spirit I will put within you; and I will remove the heart of stone from your flesh, and I will give you a heart of flesh.

[27] And my spirit I will put within you, and I will bring it about that you walk in my statutes, and keep my ordinances and do them.

[28] And you shall dwell in the land that I gave to your fathers; and you shall be a people to me, and I will be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to you.

[29] "And I will save you from all your uncleannesses; and I will call to the grain and multiply it, and I will not lay famine upon you.

[30] And I will multiply the fruit of the tree and the crops of the field, in order that you may no more take the reproach of famine among the nations.

[31] And you shall remember your evil ways and your doings that were not good, and you shall loathe yourselves in your own faces for your iniquities and for your abominations.

[32] Not for your sake am I acting, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧; let it be known to you. Be ashamed and be put to shame for your ways, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[33] "Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: In the day that I cleanse you from all your iniquities, I will cause the cities to be inhabited, and the ruins shall be built.

[34] And the desolate land shall be worked, instead of its being a desolation before the eyes of everyone passing by.

[35] And they shall say, 'This land that was desolate has become like the garden of Eden; and the cities that were ruined and desolate and thrown down are fortified, inhabited.'

[36] And the nations that are left around you shall know that I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have built the things thrown down, I have planted what was desolate; I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have spoken, and I will do.

[37] "Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: This also I will let the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ seek of me, to do for them: I will multiply them like a flock of man.

[38] Like the flock of holy things, like the flock of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ in her appointed times, so shall the ruined cities be full of flocks of man; and they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v2–7, 13–15, 22, 23, 32, 33, 37) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words (token `adonai-yhwh`; v4 "the word of Lord Yahweh" lost a stray "the"). **"God" → "Elohim"** (v28).
- **Layout** (v24–28) — the chapter is prose; v24–28 had been printed as poetry lines and are now set as prose, as the Hebrew stands.
- **"O mountains of Israel" → "Mountains of Israel"** (v1, 4, 8) — vocative without "O" (reviewer row; also "house of Israel", v22, 32).
- **"Aha, even the ancient high places are ours in possession" → "'Aha!' and, 'The everlasting high places have become ours for a possession'"** (v2) — Hebrew הֶאָח "Aha" (its row), וּבָמוֹת עוֹלָם לְמוֹרָשָׁה הָיְתָה לָּנוּ: בָּמוֹת "high places" (its row; here the heights of the land), עוֹלָם as a genitive "everlasting" (its row); מוֹרָשָׁה "possession" (its row; also v3, 5). **"said against you"** — עֲלֵיכֶם.
- **"Because they have made you desolate, and swallowed you up on every side" → "Because, even because of making you desolate and panting after you from all around"** (v3) — Hebrew יַעַן בְּיַעַן, the word doubled; שַׁמּוֹת וְשָׁאֹף, two infinitives: שָׁאַף "pant after" (its row, which names this verse; a second root "trample, crush" in Changes there: KJV "swallowed up"). **"unto the residue of the heathen" → "for the remnant of the nations"** — שְׁאֵרִית "remnant" (its row; also v4, 5). **"and ye are taken up in the lips of talkers, and are an infamy of the people" → "and you have been taken up on the lip of the tongue and the report of the people"** — וַתֵּעֲלוּ עַל־שְׂפַת לָשׁוֹן וְדִבַּת־עָם: שָׂפָה "lip", לָשׁוֹן "tongue", דִּבָּה "report" (a report that defames: their rows). The sentence breaks off and starts again in v4 (dash).
- **"to the rivers, and to the valleys, to the desolate wastes, and to the cities that are forsaken, which became a prey and derision" → "to the channels and to the valleys, and to the desolate ruins and to the forsaken cities, which have become a plunder and a derision"** (v4, 6) — Hebrew אָפִיק "channel" (reviewer row), גֵּיא "valley", חָרְבָּה "ruin" (its row), עָזַב "forsake", בַּז "plunder" (its row), לַעַג "derision". v4 is a second "therefore" that v5 takes up again.
- **"Surely in the fire of my jealousy have I spoken against the residue of the heathen, and against all Idumea" → "Surely in the fire of my jealousy I have spoken against the remnant of the nations and against Edom, all of it"** (v5) — Hebrew אִם־לֹא, the oath form, "surely" (also v7); קִנְאָה "jealousy" (its row; also v6); וְעַל־אֱדוֹם כֻּלָּא, כֻּלָּא an Aramaic-like form of "all of it" (KJV "Idumea" from the Greek). **"with the joy of all their heart, with despiteful minds, to cast it out for a prey" → "with the gladness of all the heart, with contempt of soul, in order that its pasture land be for plunder"** — בְּשִׂמְחַת כָּל־לֵבָב (שִׂמְחָה "gladness"), בִּשְׁאָט נֶפֶשׁ (as 25:6, 15), לְמַעַן מִגְרָשָׁהּ לָבַז: מִגְרָשׁ "pasture land" (its row); also read as the verb גָּרַשׁ "to drive it out" (KJV "cast it out"): the line is uncertain.
- **"concerning the land of Israel" → "concerning the ground of Israel"** (v6) — Hebrew עַל־אַדְמַת יִשְׂרָאֵל (אֲדָמָה "ground"; also v17, 24). **"Behold, I have spoken in my jealousy and in my fury, because ye have borne the shame of the heathen"** — הִנְנִי … דִּבַּרְתִּי, literally "behold me … I have spoken"; חֵמָה "fury" (its row; also v18); כְּלִמָּה "dishonor" (reviewer row; also v7, 15), set first.
- **"I have lifted up mine hand, Surely the heathen that are about you, they shall bear their shame" → "I have lifted up my hand: surely the nations that are around you — they shall bear their dishonor"** (v7) — Hebrew אֲנִי נָשָׂאתִי אֶת־יָדִי, "lift up the hand" (= swear; its row, kept); אֲשֶׁר לָכֶם מִסָּבִיב, literally "that are yours all around".
- **"ye shall shoot forth your branches, and yield your fruit to my people of Israel; for they are at hand to come" → "your bough you shall give, and your fruit you shall bear for my people Israel; for they have drawn near to come"** (v8) — Hebrew עַנְפְּכֶם תִּתֵּנוּ, עָנָף "bough" (singular; the Ezek 17–20 row), נָתַן "give"; וּפֶרְיְכֶם תִּשְׂאוּ (נָשָׂא "bear, lift up"); כִּי קֵרְבוּ לָבוֹא, a perfect.
- **"For, behold, I am for you, and I will turn unto you, and ye shall be tilled and sown" → "For behold, I am toward you, and I will turn to you, and you shall be worked and sown"** (v9) — Hebrew הִנְנִי אֲלֵיכֶם: אֶל "toward" (the same words are "I am against you" elsewhere, e.g. 13:8; here the context is for them); נֶעֱבַדְתֶּם, nifal of עָבַד (of the ground "work", its row).
- **"I will multiply men upon you" → "I will multiply man on you"** (v10–12) — Hebrew אָדָם "man" (mankind, singular collective; Standing decisions; also v13, 14, 37, 38). **"the wastes shall be builded" → "the ruins shall be built"** — וְהֶחֳרָבוֹת תִּבָּנֶינָה (also v33).
- **"and they shall increase and bring fruit" → "and they shall multiply and be fruitful"** (v11) — Hebrew וְרָבוּ וּפָרוּ (רָבָה "multiply", פָּרָה "be fruitful": their row, here in this order). **"I will settle you after your old estates" → "I will cause you to be inhabited as in your former times"** — וְהוֹשַׁבְתִּי אֶתְכֶם, hifil of יָשַׁב "dwell" (with a land as object: "cause to be inhabited"; also v33 of cities; not "settle", its row). **"and will do better unto you than at your beginnings" → "and I will do better than at your beginnings"** — וְהֵטִבֹתִי מֵרִאשֹׁתֵיכֶם ("unto you" is not written).
- **"Yea, I will cause men to walk upon you" → "And I will make man walk on you"** (v12) — Hebrew וְהוֹלַכְתִּי (הוֹלִיךְ "make walk", its row). **"and they shall possess thee, and thou shalt be their inheritance" → "and they shall possess you, and you shall be their inheritance"** — וִירֵשׁוּךָ, וְהָיִיתָ, the land now addressed in the singular (also v13–15, feminine). **"and thou shalt no more henceforth bereave them of men" → "and you shall no more again bereave them"** — וְלֹא־תוֹסִף עוֹד לְשַׁכְּלָם (יָסַף + infinitive "again": its row; literally "you shall not add again to bereave them"; שִׁכֵּל "bereave", its row: "of children" or "of men" is not written).
- **"Thou land devourest up men, and hast bereaved thy nations" → "A devourer of man are you, and a bereaver of your nations you have been"** (v13) — Hebrew אֹכֶלֶת אָדָם אָתְּ וּמְשַׁכֶּלֶת גּוֹיַיִךְ הָיִית: two feminine participles; qere אָתְּ "you", ketiv אתי; qere גּוֹיַיִךְ "your nations", ketiv גויך "your nation" (also v14, 15).
- **"neither bereave thy nations any more" → "and your nations you shall bereave no more"** (v14) — Hebrew qere תְשַׁכְּלִי "bereave"; ketiv תכשלי "make stumble".
- **"Neither will I cause men to hear in thee the shame of the heathen any more, neither shalt thou bear the reproach of the people any more, neither shalt thou cause thy nations to fall any more" → "And I will no more let you hear the dishonor of the nations, and the reproach of the peoples you shall bear no more, and your nations you shall make stumble no more"** (v15) — Hebrew וְלֹא־אַשְׁמִיעַ אֵלַיִךְ, literally "I will not make heard to you"; חֶרְפָּה "reproach" (its row), עַמִּים "peoples"; תַכְשִׁלִי, hifil of כָּשַׁל "make stumble" (its row; here no qere; many read "bereave" as v14, from memory).
- **"Moreover the word of the LORD came unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v16) — the prophets' formula.
- **"when the house of Israel dwelt in their own land, they defiled it by their own way and by their doings" → "the house of Israel were dwelling on their ground, and they defiled it by their way and by their doings"** (v17) — Hebrew יֹשְׁבִים, a participle; וַיְטַמְּאוּ (טִמֵּא "defile", its row; also v18); עֲלִילוֹת "doings" (its row; also v19; מַעֲלָלִים v31). **"their way was before me as the uncleanness of a removed woman" → "like the uncleanness of the impurity was their way before me"** — כְּטֻמְאַת הַנִּדָּה: טֻמְאָה "uncleanness", נִדָּה "impurity" (its row: the monthly flow; "woman" is not written).
- **"for the blood that they had shed upon the land, and for their idols wherewith they had polluted it" → "for the blood that they had shed on the land, and with their idols they had defiled it"** (v18) — Hebrew שָׁפַךְ, of blood "shed" (its row; "poured out" of fury); וּבְגִלּוּלֵיהֶם טִמְּאוּהָ, a new clause; גִּלּוּלִים "idols" (its row; also v25).
- **"and they were dispersed through the countries" → "and they were strewn among the lands"** (v19) — Hebrew וַיִּזָּרוּ, nifal of זָרָה "strew" (its row); הֵפִיץ "scatter".
- **"And when they entered unto the heathen, whither they went, they profaned my holy name, when they said to them" → "And he came to the nations to which they came, and they profaned my holy name, in that it was said of them"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיָּבוֹא, singular (the house of Israel as one; the versions have the plural, from memory); חִלֵּל "profane" (its row; also v21–23); שֵׁם קָדְשִׁי, literally "the name of my holiness" (as 20:39); בֶּאֱמֹר לָהֶם, impersonal "in its being said of them". **"and are gone forth out of his land" → "and from his land they have gone out"** — וּמֵאַרְצוֹ יָצָאוּ.
- **"But I had pity for mine holy name" → "And I had pity on my holy name"** (v21) — Hebrew וָאֶחְמֹל עַל, חָמַל "have pity (spare)" (its row).
- **"I do not this for your sakes … but for mine holy name's sake" → "Not for your sake am I acting … but for my holy name"** (v22, 32) — Hebrew לֹא לְמַעַנְכֶם אֲנִי עֹשֶׂה, a participle, no object; כִּי אִם־לְשֵׁם־קָדְשִׁי: כִּי אִם "but" (reviewer row), לְ "for" (not לְמַעַן).
- **"And I will sanctify my great name" → "And I will sanctify my great name"** (v23) — Hebrew וְקִדַּשְׁתִּי (קִדֵּשׁ "sanctify", its row). **"when I shall be sanctified in you before their eyes" → "when I am sanctified in you before their eyes"** — בְּהִקָּדְשִׁי בָכֶם לְעֵינֵיהֶם (לְעֵינֵי "before the eyes of", its row). One early Greek papyrus is said to lack 36:23b–38, from "and the nations shall know" on (from memory): MT kept.
- **"For I will take you from among the heathen … and will bring you into your own land" → "And I will take you from the nations … and bring you to your ground"** (v24) — Hebrew לָקַח "take", אֶל־אַדְמַתְכֶם.
- **"Then will I sprinkle clean water upon you" → "And I will dash clean water on you"** (v25) — Hebrew וְזָרַקְתִּי, זָרַק "dash" (throw from a bowl; its row: the word used of the blood of the altar; הִזָּה is "sprinkle"); טָהוֹר "clean", טָהֵר "be clean", טִהַר "cleanse" (their rows); טֻמְאוֹת plural "uncleannesses" (also v29).
- **"A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you an heart of flesh" → "And I will give you a new heart, and a new spirit I will put within you; and I will remove the heart of stone from your flesh, and I will give you a heart of flesh"** (v26) — Hebrew וְנָתַתִּי לָכֶם לֵב חָדָשׁ וְרוּחַ חֲדָשָׁה אֶתֵּן בְּקִרְבְּכֶם, as 11:19 (there "one heart"); רוּחַ "spirit" (its row; lower case, as the finished books); לֵב הָאֶבֶן · לֵב בָּשָׂר, the Ezek 9–12 row, word for word as 11:19; הֵסִיר "remove".
- **"And I will put my spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes, and ye shall keep my judgments, and do them" → "And my spirit I will put within you, and I will bring it about that you walk in my statutes, and keep my ordinances and do them"** (v27) — Hebrew וְאֶת־רוּחִי אֶתֵּן בְּקִרְבְּכֶם, "my spirit" (lower case, as v26; the person of the Spirit is not decided by the word: say so); וְעָשִׂיתִי אֵת אֲשֶׁר־בְּחֻקַּי תֵּלֵכוּ, literally "and I will make that in my statutes you walk"; מִשְׁפָּט of a ruling "ordinance" (its row), as 11:20; שָׁמַר "keep".
- **"and ye shall be my people, and I will be your God" → "and you shall be a people to me, and I will be Elohim to you"** (v28) — Hebrew וִהְיִיתֶם לִי לְעָם וְאָנֹכִי אֶהְיֶה לָכֶם לֵאלֹהִים, as 11:20; the pronoun אָנֹכִי written.
- **"I will also save you" → "And I will save you"** (v29) — Hebrew וְהוֹשַׁעְתִּי. **"and I will call for the corn, and will increase it, and lay no famine upon you" → "and I will call to the grain and multiply it, and I will not lay famine upon you"** — קָרָא אֶל "call to", דָּגָן "grain" (its row), הִרְבָּה "multiply" (also v30, 37), נָתַן עַל "lay upon" (the Ezek 5–8 row), רָעָב "famine" (its row).
- **"and the increase of the field, that ye shall receive no more reproach of famine" → "and the crops of the field, in order that you may no more take the reproach of famine"** (v30) — Hebrew תְּנוּבָה "crops" (its row), לְמַעַן אֲשֶׁר, לָקַח "take" (its row).
- **"and shall lothe yourselves in your own sight for your iniquities" → "and you shall loathe yourselves in your own faces for your iniquities"** (v31) — Hebrew וּנְקֹטֹתֶם בִּפְנֵיכֶם (the Ezek 17–20 row; as 6:9; 20:43); מַעֲלָלִים "doings".
- **"be it known unto you: be ashamed and confounded for your own ways" → "let it be known to you. Be ashamed and be put to shame for your ways"** (v32) — Hebrew יִוָּדַע לָכֶם, jussive; בּוֹשׁוּ וְהִכָּלְמוּ (בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed", נִכְלַם "be put to shame": the Ps 35–36 row); מִדַּרְכֵיכֶם, literally "from your ways".
- **"In the day that I shall have cleansed you … I will also cause you to dwell in the cities" → "In the day that I cleanse you … I will cause the cities to be inhabited"** (v33) — Hebrew בְּיוֹם טַהֲרִי אֶתְכֶם; וְהוֹשַׁבְתִּי אֶת־הֶעָרִים, the cities are the object (as v11).
- **"And the desolate land shall be tilled, whereas it lay desolate in the sight of all that passed by" → "And the desolate land shall be worked, instead of its being a desolation before the eyes of everyone passing by"** (v34) — Hebrew תֵּעָבֵד (as v9), תַּחַת אֲשֶׁר הָיְתָה שְׁמָמָה, לְעֵינֵי כָּל־עוֹבֵר.
- **"and the waste and desolate and ruined cities are become fenced, and are inhabited" → "and the cities that were ruined and desolate and thrown down are fortified, inhabited"** (v35) — Hebrew הַחֲרֵבוֹת וְהַנְשַׁמּוֹת וְהַנֶּהֱרָסוֹת (הָרַס "throw down", its row; also v36), בְּצוּרוֹת יָשָׁבוּ (בָּצוּר "fortified", its row; יָשָׁבוּ, literally "they dwell" = are inhabited); "Eden" as the lexicon (no token in English).
- **"Then the heathen that are left round about you shall know that I the LORD build the ruined places, and plant that that was desolate: I the LORD have spoken it, and I will do it" → "And the nations that are left around you shall know that I, Yahweh, have built the things thrown down, I have planted what was desolate; I, Yahweh, have spoken, and I will do"** (v36) — Hebrew כִּי אֲנִי יְהוָה בָּנִיתִי, perfects; "that I, Yahweh, have …" (not the "that I am Yahweh" formula; the Ezek 5–8 row); אֲנִי יְהוָה דִּבַּרְתִּי וְעָשִׂיתִי, "I, Yahweh, have spoken" (that row), no "it".
- **"I will yet for this be enquired of by the house of Israel, to do it for them; I will increase them with men like a flock" → "This also I will let the house of Israel seek of me, to do for them: I will multiply them like a flock of man"** (v37) — Hebrew עוֹד זֹאת אִדָּרֵשׁ לְבֵית־יִשְׂרָאֵל, nifal of דָּרַשׁ "let myself be sought"; אַרְבֶּה אֹתָם כַּצֹּאן אָדָם, literally "I will multiply them like the flock, man" (אָדָם after the noun; as v38 צֹאן אָדָם "a flock of man").
- **"As the holy flock, as the flock of Jerusalem in her solemn feasts" → "Like the flock of holy things, like the flock of Jerusalem in her appointed times"** (v38) — Hebrew כְּצֹאן קָדָשִׁים (קֹדֶשׁ plural "holy things": the flock for the offerings); בְּמוֹעֲדֶיהָ, מוֹעֵד "appointed time" (its row; never "feasts"). **"so shall the waste cities be filled with flocks of men" → "so shall the ruined cities be full of flocks of man"** — מְלֵאוֹת צֹאן אָדָם.
