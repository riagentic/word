# Ezekiel 33

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[2] "Son of man, speak to the sons of your people, and say to them: A land — when I bring a sword upon it, and the people of the land take one man from among them and give him to themselves as a watchman,

[3] and he sees the sword coming upon the land, and blows on the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧ and warns the people,

[4] and the hearer hears the sound of the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧ and does not take warning, and a sword comes and takes him, his blood shall be on his head.

[5] The sound of the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧ he heard, and he did not take warning; his blood shall be on him. But had he taken warning, he would have rescued his soul.

[6] But the watchman — if he sees the sword coming and does not blow on the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧, and the people do not take warning, and a sword comes and takes a soul from them, he is taken away in his iniquity, and his blood I will require from the hand of the watchman.

[7] "And you, son of man, a watchman I have made you for the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and you shall hear a word from my mouth, and you shall warn them from me.

[8] When I say to the wicked, 'Wicked one, you shall surely die,' and you do not speak to warn the wicked from his way, he, the wicked, shall die in his iniquity, and his blood I will require from your hand.

[9] But you — if you warn the wicked from his way, to turn back from it, and he does not turn back from his way, he shall die in his iniquity; and you — you have delivered your soul.

[10] "And you, son of man, say to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Thus you have said, saying, 'Our transgressions and our sins are upon us, and in them we are rotting away; and how shall we live?'

[11] Say to them: As I live, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, I have no delight in the death of the wicked, but in the wicked turning back from his way and living. Turn back, turn back from your evil ways! And why will you die, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?

[12] "And you, son of man, say to the sons of your people: The righteousness of the righteous shall not deliver him in the day of his transgression; and the wickedness of the wicked — he shall not stumble by it in the day of his turning back from his wickedness; and the righteous shall not be able to live by it in the day of his sinning.

[13] When I say to the righteous, 'He shall surely live,' and he trusts in his righteousness and does injustice, all his righteousnesses shall not be remembered, and in his injustice that he has done, in it he shall die.

[14] And when I say to the wicked, 'You shall surely die,' and he turns back from his sin and does judgment and righteousness —

[15] the wicked restores a pledge, repays what he has robbed, walks in the statutes of life, not doing injustice — he shall surely live; he shall not die.

[16] All his sins that he has sinned shall not be remembered to him; judgment and righteousness he has done; he shall surely live.

[17] "And the sons of your people say, 'The way of ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ is not fair'; but they — their way is not fair.

[18] When the righteous turns back from his righteousness and does injustice, he shall die by them.

[19] And when the wicked turns back from his wickedness and does judgment and righteousness, for them he shall live.

[20] And you say, 'The way of ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ is not fair.' Each man according to his ways I will judge you, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[21] And it came to pass in the twelfth year, in the tenth, on the fifth of the month, of our exile, that the fugitive from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ came to me, saying, "The city has been struck."

[22] And the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had been upon me in the evening before the fugitive came, and he opened my mouth by the time he came to me in the morning; and my mouth was opened, and I was mute no more.

[23] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[24] "Son of man, those who dwell in these ruins on the ground of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ say, saying, '⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ was one, and he possessed the land; and we are many; to us the land has been given as a possession.'

[25] Therefore say to them: Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: With the blood you eat, and your eyes you lift up to your idols, and blood you shed — and the land you shall possess?

[26] You have stood on your sword, you have done abomination, and each man his neighbor's wife you have defiled — and the land you shall possess?

[27] Thus you shall say to them: Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: As I live, surely those who are in the ruins shall fall by the sword, and whoever is on the face of the field I have given to the living things to devour him, and those who are in the strongholds and in the caves shall die by pestilence.

[28] And I will make the land a desolation and a devastation, and the pride of her strength shall cease, and the mountains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall be desolate, with none passing through.

[29] And they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, when I make the land a desolation and a devastation because of all their abominations that they have done.

[30] "And you, son of man — the sons of your people who talk together about you beside the walls and in the entrances of the houses, and speak one with another, each man with his brother, saying, 'Come, please, and hear what the word is that goes out from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧' —

[31] they come to you as a people comes, and they sit before you, my people, and they hear your words, but them they do not do; for desires with their mouth they are doing; after their unjust gain their heart goes.

[32] And behold, you are to them like a song of desires, one beautiful of voice and playing well; and they hear your words, but doers of them they are not.

[33] And when it comes — behold, it comes! — then they shall know that a prophet has been among them."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v11, 25, 27) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words (token `adonai-yhwh`). **"the Lord" → "Adonai"** (v17, 20) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי alone.
- **"Again the word of the LORD came unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v1, 23) — Hebrew וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר, the prophets' formula, one wording.
- **"speak to the children of thy people" → "speak to the sons of your people"** (v2, 12, 17, 30) — Hebrew בְּנֵי־עַמְּךָ, "sons" (בֵּן row). **"When I bring the sword upon a land" → "A land — when I bring a sword upon it"** — אֶרֶץ כִּי־אָבִיא עָלֶיהָ חָרֶב: the land is set first; חֶרֶב without the article. **"take a man of their coasts" → "take one man from among them"** — אִישׁ אֶחָד מִקְצֵיהֶם, literally "one man from their ends" (= from their whole number). **"and set him for their watchman" → "and give him to themselves as a watchman"** — וְנָתְנוּ אֹתוֹ לָהֶם לְצֹפֶה; צֹפֶה "watchman" (its row).
- **"he blow the trumpet, and warn the people" → "and blows on the shofar and warns the people"** (v3) — Hebrew וְתָקַע בַּשּׁוֹפָר (תָּקַע בְּ "blow on", its row), הִזְהִיר "warn" (its row); v2–6 are one long condition joined by "and", kept so.
- **"whosoever heareth … and taketh not warning" → "the hearer hears … and does not take warning"** (v4–6) — Hebrew וְשָׁמַע הַשֹּׁמֵעַ, verb and participle of one root; נִזְהָר, nifal of the same verb as הִזְהִיר: "take warning" each time, also v6 וְהָעָם לֹא־נִזְהָר "and the people do not take warning" (was "are not warned"). **"upon his own head" → "on his head"** — בְרֹאשׁוֹ, no "own". **"take him away" → "takes him"** — וַתִּקָּחֵהוּ, לָקַח "take".
- **"But he that taketh warning shall deliver his soul" → "But had he taken warning, he would have rescued his soul"** (v5) — Hebrew וְהוּא נִזְהָר נַפְשׁוֹ מִלֵּט, literally "and he — taking warning, his soul he rescued": two perfects; read as unreal condition by the sense of the verse ("he did not take warning"); KJV takes it of anyone who takes warning: both in view. מִלֵּט "rescue" (piel; הִצִּיל in v9, 12 is "deliver").
- **"take any person from among them" → "takes a soul from them"** (v6) — Hebrew וַתִּקַּח מֵהֶם נָפֶשׁ, נֶפֶשׁ "soul". **"but his blood will I require at the watchman's hand" → "and his blood I will require from the hand of the watchman"** — וְ "and" (as v8); דָּרַשׁ "require" (its row), מִיַּד "from the hand of".
- **"I have set thee a watchman unto the house of Israel" → "a watchman I have made you for the house of Israel"** (v7) — Hebrew צֹפֶה נְתַתִּיךָ (נָתַן, literally "I have given you"), word for word as 3:17, and its wording is reused. **"therefore thou shalt hear the word at my mouth" → "and you shall hear a word from my mouth, and you shall warn them from me"** — וְשָׁמַעְתָּ מִפִּי דָּבָר וְהִזְהַרְתָּ, "and", דָּבָר without the article. v8–9 differ from 3:18–19 (here a vocative רָשָׁע and לָשׁוּב מִמֶּנָּה; there וְלֹא הִזְהַרְתּוֹ, הָרְשָׁעָה, לְחַיֹּתוֹ): the shared clauses keep 3:18–19's wording.
- **"O wicked man, thou shalt surely die" → "'Wicked one, you shall surely die'"** (v8) — Hebrew רָשָׁע מוֹת תָּמוּת: vocative without "O" (reviewer row); infinitive absolute, literally "dying you shall die" (also v14; v13, 15, 16 חָיֹה יִחְיֶה / חָיוֹ יִחְיֶה "living he shall live" = "surely live"). **"that wicked man shall die" → "he, the wicked, shall die"** — הוּא רָשָׁע. **"but his blood will I require at thine hand" → "and his blood I will require from your hand"** — וְ "and", as 3:18; **"but thou hast delivered thy soul" → "and you — you have delivered your soul"** (v9) — וְאַתָּה נַפְשְׁךָ הִצַּלְתָּ, the pronoun written, as 3:19. In v8 אֲבַקֵּשׁ is בִּקֵּשׁ (its row: "require" at someone's hand): another verb than דָּרַשׁ in v6; English has one word.
- **"Nevertheless, if thou warn the wicked of his way to turn from it; if he do not turn" → "But you — if you warn the wicked from his way, to turn back from it, and he does not turn back"** (v9) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה כִּי־הִזְהַרְתָּ, the pronoun first; שׁוּב "turn back" (its row) throughout the chapter (v9, 11, 12, 14, 18, 19).
- **"Thus ye speak, saying, If our transgressions … how should we then live?" → "Thus you have said, saying, 'Our transgressions … and how shall we live?'"** (v10) — Hebrew כֵּן אֲמַרְתֶּם לֵאמֹר, a perfect; כִּי introduces the quoted words (not "if"); פֶּשַׁע "transgression", חַטָּאת "sin"; וּבָם אֲנַחְנוּ נְמַקִּים "and in them we are rotting away" (נָמַק "rot away", its row); וְאֵיךְ "and how".
- **"I have no pleasure in the death of the wicked; but that the wicked turn from his way and live" → "I have no delight in the death of the wicked, but in the wicked turning back from his way and living"** (v11) — Hebrew אִם־אֶחְפֹּץ, the oath form, literally "if I delight" = "I do not delight" (Standing decisions); חָפֵץ בְּ "delight in" (its row); כִּי אִם "but" (reviewer row), בְּשׁוּב … וְחָיָה "in (his) turning back … and he lives". **"turn ye, turn ye" → "Turn back, turn back"** — שׁוּבוּ שׁוּבוּ. **"for why will ye die" → "And why will you die"** — וְלָמָּה. **"O house of Israel" → "house of Israel"** — no "O" (reviewer row; also v20).
- **"he shall not fall thereby" → "he shall not stumble by it"** (v12) — Hebrew לֹא־יִכָּשֶׁל בָּהּ, כָּשַׁל "stumble". **"in the day that he turneth … in the day that he sinneth" → "in the day of his turning back … in the day of his sinning"** — infinitives בְּיוֹם שׁוּבוֹ, בְּיוֹם חֲטֹאתוֹ. **"to live for his righteousness" → "to live by it"** — בָּהּ "by it" (feminine: the righteousness); "his righteousness" is not repeated. **"as for the wickedness of the wicked" → "and the wickedness of the wicked —"** — וְרִשְׁעַת הָרָשָׁע, set first.
- **"that he shall surely live; if he trust to his own righteousness, and commit iniquity" → "'He shall surely live,' and he trusts in his righteousness and does injustice"** (v13) — Hebrew חָיֹה יִחְיֶה, third person; וְהוּא־בָטַח, וְעָשָׂה עָוֶל: עָוֶל "injustice" (its row; עָוֹן "iniquity" is another word) also v15, 18. **"all his righteousnesses shall not be remembered"** kept — כָּל … לֹא, "all … not" (= "none"), as 18:24 and 3:20 have it; qere צִדְקֹתָיו plural, ketiv צדקתו "his righteousness". **"but for his iniquity … he shall die for it" → "and in his injustice that he has done, in it he shall die"** — וּבְעַוְלוֹ … בּוֹ יָמוּת, בְּ twice.
- **"do that which is lawful and right" → "does judgment and righteousness"** (v14, 16, 19) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּט וּצְדָקָה (their rows: מִשְׁפָּט "judgment", צְדָקָה "righteousness").
- **"If the wicked restore the pledge, give again that he had robbed, walk in the statutes of life, without committing iniquity" → "the wicked restores a pledge, repays what he has robbed, walks in the statutes of life, not doing injustice"** (v15) — Hebrew חֲבֹל יָשִׁיב רָשָׁע (חֲבֹל "pledge", no article; root of חָבַל "take in pledge"), גְּזֵלָה יְשַׁלֵּם (גְּזֵלָה "thing robbed", שִׁלֵּם "repay": their rows), בְּחֻקּוֹת הַחַיִּים הָלַךְ (חֻקָּה "statute"), לְבִלְתִּי עֲשׂוֹת עָוֶל; no "if": the verse goes on with v14's condition.
- **"None of his sins that he hath committed shall be mentioned unto him" → "All his sins that he has sinned shall not be remembered to him"** (v16) — Hebrew כָּל־חַטֹּאתָיו אֲשֶׁר חָטָא לֹא תִזָּכַרְנָה לוֹ ("all … not" = "none", as 18:22): qere plural, ketiv חטאתו singular; "sinned" (חָטָא, one root with the noun); נִזְכַּר "be remembered" (as v13); לוֹ "to him". **"he hath done that which is lawful and right" → "judgment and righteousness he has done"** — Hebrew order.
- **"The way of the Lord is not equal: but as for them, their way is not equal" → "The way of Adonai is not fair; but they — their way is not fair"** (v17, 20) — Hebrew לֹא יִתָּכֵן דֶּרֶךְ אֲדֹנָי, nifal of תָּכַן "measure, weigh out" (the תִּכֵּן row): literally "is not weighed (out)"; "fair" as 18:25, 29, where the same words stand. אֲדֹנָי "Adonai" (token `adonai`).
- **"he shall even die thereby" → "he shall die by them"** (v18) — Hebrew וּמֵת בָּהֶם, plural "by them" (the deeds of injustice). **"he shall live thereby" → "for them he shall live"** (v19) — עֲלֵיהֶם, literally "upon them" (as 18:26 "for them").
- **"I will judge you every one after his ways" → "Each man according to his ways I will judge you"** (v20) — Hebrew אִישׁ כִּדְרָכָיו אֶשְׁפּוֹט אֶתְכֶם, in the Hebrew order.
- **"in the twelfth year of our captivity, in the tenth month, in the fifth day of the month, that one that had escaped out of Jerusalem came unto me" → "in the twelfth year, in the tenth, on the fifth of the month, of our exile, that the fugitive from Jerusalem came to me"** (v21) — Hebrew בִּשְׁתֵּי עֶשְׂרֵה שָׁנָה בָּעֲשִׂרִי בַּחֲמִשָּׁה לַחֹדֶשׁ לְגָלוּתֵנוּ, in the Hebrew order; "month" is not written after "tenth"; הַפָּלִיט "the fugitive" (its row), with the article. Some Hebrew manuscripts and the Syriac are said to read "eleventh year" (from memory): MT kept. **"The city is smitten" → "The city has been struck"** — הֻכְּתָה הָעִיר, hofal of הִכָּה "strike" (its row).
- **"Now the hand of the LORD was upon me in the evening, afore he that was escaped came; and had opened my mouth, until he came to me in the morning" → "And the hand of Yahweh had been upon me in the evening before the fugitive came, and he opened my mouth by the time he came to me in the morning"** (v22) — Hebrew וְיַד־יְהוָה הָיְתָה אֵלַי, a perfect after the subject (before-past); וַיִּפְתַּח; עַד־בּוֹא אֵלַי, literally "until (his) coming to me". **"and I was no more dumb" → "and I was mute no more"** — וְלֹא נֶאֱלַמְתִּי עוֹד; the muteness of 3:26; 24:27.
- **"they that inhabit those wastes of the land of Israel speak, saying" → "those who dwell in these ruins on the ground of Israel say, saying"** (v24) — Hebrew יֹשְׁבֵי הֶחֳרָבוֹת הָאֵלֶּה (יָשַׁב "dwell", חָרְבָּה "ruin": their rows; also v27), עַל־אַדְמַת יִשְׂרָאֵל (אֲדָמָה "ground"), אֹמְרִים לֵאמֹר. **"Abraham was one, and he inherited the land" → "Abraham was one, and he possessed the land"** — אֶחָד הָיָה אַבְרָהָם וַיִּירַשׁ, יָרַשׁ "possess" (its row; also v25, 26); "man" is not written. **"the land is given us for inheritance" → "to us the land has been given as a possession"** — לָנוּ נִתְּנָה הָאָרֶץ לְמוֹרָשָׁה, מוֹרָשָׁה "possession" (its row).
- **"Ye eat with the blood" → "With the blood you eat"** (v25) — Hebrew עַל־הַדָּם תֹּאכֵלוּ, literally "upon the blood" (Lev 19:26). **"toward your idols" → "to your idols"** — גִּלּוּלִים "idols" (its row). **"and shall ye possess the land?" → "and the land you shall possess?"** (v25, 26) — וְהָאָרֶץ תִּירָשׁוּ has no question word; the question is in the tone, kept as a question.
- **"Ye stand upon your sword, ye work abomination, and ye defile every one his neighbour's wife" → "You have stood on your sword, you have done abomination, and each man his neighbor's wife you have defiled"** (v26) — Hebrew three perfects; עֲמַדְתֶּם עַל־חַרְבְּכֶם, literally "you have stood on your sword" (= relied on it); עֲשִׂיתֶן has a feminine ending (unexplained); תּוֹעֵבָה "abomination" singular; טִמֵּא "defile" (its row).
- **"and him that is in the open field will I give to the beasts to be devoured" → "and whoever is on the face of the field I have given to the living things to devour him"** (v27) — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי הַשָּׂדֶה "on the face of the field"; לַחַיָּה נְתַתִּיו לְאָכְלוֹ, a perfect (prophetic), חַיָּה "living thing" (its row; collective); אִם־לֹא, the oath form, "surely". **"in the forts and in the caves" → "in the strongholds and in the caves"** — מְצָדוֹת (מְצָד "stronghold", its row), מְעָרוֹת "caves"; דֶּבֶר "pestilence".
- **"For I will lay the land most desolate" → "And I will make the land a desolation and a devastation"** (v28, 29) — Hebrew וְנָתַתִּי אֶת־הָאָרֶץ שְׁמָמָה וּמְשַׁמָּה, two nouns of the root שָׁמֵם (שְׁמָמָה "desolation", its row; מְשַׁמָּה "devastation"); "and", not "for". **"and the pomp of her strength shall cease" → "and the pride of her strength shall cease"** — וְנִשְׁבַּת גְּאוֹן עֻזָּהּ, שָׁבַת "cease", גָּאוֹן "pride" (of men, the Isa 13–16 row). **"that none shall pass through" → "with none passing through"** — מֵאֵין עוֹבֵר, literally "from there being no one passing".
- **"Then shall they know that I am the LORD" → "And they shall know that I am Yahweh"** (v29) — Hebrew וְיָדְעוּ כִּי־אֲנִי יְהוָה, Ezekiel's formula, one wording (PROPHETS.md). **"which they have committed" → "that they have done"** — עָשׂוּ.
- **"the children of thy people still are talking against thee" → "the sons of your people who talk together about you"** (v30) — Hebrew הַנִּדְבָּרִים בְּךָ, nifal participle "who speak together"; בְּךָ "about you" or "against you" (both possible; "still" is not written). **"by the walls and in the doors of the houses" → "beside the walls and in the entrances of the houses"** — אֵצֶל "beside", פֶּתַח "entrance" (their rows). **"and speak one to another, every one to his brother" → "and speak one with another, each man with his brother"** — וְדִבֶּר־חַד אֶת־אַחַד אִישׁ אֶת־אָחִיו (חַד, a short form of אֶחָד). **"Come, I pray you" → "Come, please"** — בֹּאוּ־נָא. **"that cometh forth" → "that goes out"** — הַיּוֹצֵא. v30 opens a sentence that v31 finishes ("the sons of your people … they come to you").
- **"as the people cometh, and they sit before thee as my people" → "as a people comes, and they sit before you, my people"** (v31) — Hebrew כִּמְבוֹא־עָם "as the coming of a people"; וְיֵשְׁבוּ לְפָנֶיךָ עַמִּי: "my people" stands beside the verb without "as". **"but they will not do them" → "but them they do not do"** — וְאוֹתָם לֹא יַעֲשׂוּ. **"for with their mouth they shew much love, but their heart goeth after their covetousness" → "for desires with their mouth they are doing; after their unjust gain their heart goes"** — כִּי־עֲגָבִים בְּפִיהֶם הֵמָּה עֹשִׂים: עֲגָבִים "desires" (sensual love; the root עָגַב of 23:5–20), the line is hard and its sense uncertain; the Greek is said to read "lies" here (from memory): MT kept. בֶּצַע "unjust gain" (its row); no "but".
- **"as a very lovely song of one that hath a pleasant voice, and can play well on an instrument" → "like a song of desires, one beautiful of voice and playing well"** (v32) — Hebrew כְּשִׁיר עֲגָבִים יְפֵה קוֹל וּמֵטִב נַגֵּן: עֲגָבִים as v31 ("a love song" is the sense); נִגֵּן "play (a stringed instrument)" (its row); "instrument" is not written. **"for they hear thy words, but they do them not" → "and they hear your words, but doers of them they are not"** — וְשָׁמְעוּ … וְעֹשִׂים אֵינָם אוֹתָם, a participle: "and doing them they are not".
- **"And when this cometh to pass, (lo, it will come,)" → "And when it comes — behold, it comes!"** (v33) — Hebrew וּבְבֹאָהּ הִנֵּה בָאָה, "it" (feminine, unnamed); הִנֵּה "behold".

### Notes
- **v1–20** — the watchman (as 3:16–21) and the righteous and the wicked (as 18:21–32).
- **v21** — news that the city has fallen reaches Ezekiel in exile.
- **v22** — his muteness ends (3:26; 24:27).
