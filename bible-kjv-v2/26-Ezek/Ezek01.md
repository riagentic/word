# Ezekiel 1

[1] And it came to pass in the thirtieth year, in the fourth, on the fifth of the month, and I was in the midst of the exiles by the river ⟦chebar|Kevar|Chebar⟧: the heavens were opened, and I saw visions of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[2] On the fifth of the month — it was the fifth year of the exile of King ⟦jehoiachin|Yehoyachin|Jehoiachin⟧ —

[3] the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ surely came to ⟦ezekiel|Yechezqel|Ezekiel⟧ son of Buzi, the priest, in the land of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ by the river ⟦chebar|Kevar|Chebar⟧; and the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was upon him there.

[4] And I looked, and behold, a storm wind coming from the north, a great cloud and fire flashing, and brightness all around it; and from its midst, like the look of the amber, from the midst of the fire.

[5] And from its midst, the likeness of four living creatures. And this was their appearance: they had the likeness of a man.

[6] And each one had four faces, and each one of them had four wings.

[7] And their feet were a straight foot, and the sole of their feet was like the sole of the foot of a calf; and they were sparkling like the look of burnished bronze.

[8] And hands of a man were under their wings on their four sides; and the four of them had their faces and their wings.

[9] Their wings were joining one to another; they did not turn as they went; each went straight forward.

[10] And the likeness of their faces: the face of a man; and the four of them had the face of a lion on the right; and the four of them had the face of an ox on the left; and the four of them had the face of an eagle.

[11] And their faces and their wings were parted from above; each had two joining one another, and two covering their bodies.

[12] And each went straight forward; wherever the spirit was to go, they went; they did not turn as they went.

[13] And the likeness of the living creatures: their appearance was like coals of fire, burning, like the appearance of the torches; it was going about among the living creatures; and the fire had brightness, and from the fire lightning was going out.

[14] And the living creatures were running and returning, like the appearance of the flash.

[15] And I saw the living creatures, and behold, one wheel on the earth beside the living creatures, for its four faces.

[16] The appearance of the wheels and their work was like the look of beryl; and the four of them had one likeness; and their appearance and their work were as though the wheel were in the midst of the wheel.

[17] On their four sides they went, when they went; they did not turn as they went.

[18] And their rims — they had height, and they had fear; and their rims were full of eyes all around, the four of them.

[19] And when the living creatures went, the wheels went beside them; and when the living creatures were lifted up from upon the earth, the wheels were lifted up.

[20] Wherever the spirit was to go, they went — there the spirit was to go — and the wheels were lifted up alongside them, for the spirit of the living creature was in the wheels.

[21] When they went, they went; and when they stood, they stood; and when they were lifted up from upon the earth, the wheels were lifted up alongside them, for the spirit of the living creature was in the wheels.

[22] And over the heads of the living creature there was a likeness: an expanse, like the look of the fearsome ice, stretched out over their heads from above.

[23] And under the expanse their wings were straight, one toward another; each had two that covered them, and each had two that covered them — their bodies.

[24] And I heard the sound of their wings, like the sound of many waters, like the voice of ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧, when they went, a sound of roaring, like the sound of a camp. When they stood, they let down their wings.

[25] And there came a voice from above the expanse that was over their heads. When they stood, they let down their wings.

[26] And above the expanse that was over their heads, like the appearance of a sapphire stone, was the likeness of a throne; and on the likeness of the throne was a likeness like the appearance of a man, upon it from above.

[27] And I saw like the look of amber, like the appearance of fire, enclosing it all around, from the appearance of his loins and upward; and from the appearance of his loins and downward I saw like the appearance of fire, and he had brightness all around.

[28] Like the appearance of the bow that is in the cloud on the day of the rain, so was the appearance of the brightness all around. That was the appearance of the likeness of the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

And I saw, and I fell on my face, and I heard a voice of one speaking.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*; "visions of *Elohim*" — the divine visions Ezekiel receives.
- **"Almighty" → "Shaddai"** (v24) — Hebrew שַׁדַּי *Shaddai*; ancient divine name, traditionally "Almighty"; the Hebrew form preserved for the "voice of Shaddai" theophany.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Now it came to pass … in the fourth month, in the fifth day of the month" → "And it came to pass … in the fourth, on the fifth of the month"** (v1, 2) — Hebrew וַיְהִי … בָּרְבִיעִי בַּחֲמִשָּׁה לַחֹדֶשׁ: "month" and "day" are not written; English can stand without them. **"as I was among the captives" → "and I was in the midst of the exiles"** — וַאֲנִי בְתוֹךְ־הַגּוֹלָה; גּוֹלָה "exile(s)", collective.
- **"river of Chebar" → "river Chebar"** (v1, 3) — Hebrew נְהַר־כְּבָר; נָהָר is "river" (its row). The Kevar was probably a canal of the Euphrates near Nippur (outside knowledge, not in the text).
- **"the word of the LORD came expressly" → "the word of Yahweh surely came"** (v3) — infinitive absolute with its verb, הָיֹה הָיָה, literally "being, it was". **The shift from "I" (v1) to "him" (v3)** stands as printed: v2–3 speak of Ezekiel in the third person, v4 returns to "I".
- **"Ezekiel the priest, the son of Buzi" → "Ezekiel son of Buzi, the priest"** (v3) — Hebrew יְחֶזְקֵאל בֶּן־בּוּזִי הַכֹּהֵן: "the priest" may belong to Ezekiel or to Buzi; the Hebrew order is kept and the question left open.
- **"a whirlwind" → "a storm wind"** (v4) — רוּחַ סְעָרָה (its row). **"a fire infolding itself" → "fire flashing"** — מִתְלַקַּחַת, hitpael of לָקַח "take": fire "taking hold of itself" (as Exod 9:24). **"a brightness was about it" → "brightness all around it"** — נֹגַהּ לוֹ סָבִיב.
- **"as the colour of amber" → "like the look of the amber"** (v4, 27) — Hebrew כְּעֵין הַחַשְׁמַל; עַיִן "eye" = the look of a thing (its row). חַשְׁמַל is found only in Ezekiel (1:4, 27; 8:2); **its meaning is not known**. The Septuagint has ἤλεκτρον, which is amber or electrum, a gold-silver alloy (from memory); "gleaming metal" is a guess from the context. "Amber" is the oldest English rendering and is kept as the plainest; the article stands in v4, not in v27.
- **"likeness" / "appearance" / "like" kept each time** (throughout) — דְּמוּת "likeness", מַרְאֶה "appearance", עַיִן "look", the particle כְּ "like": Ezekiel heaps them up, most of all in v26–28 ("the appearance of the likeness of the glory"); none is smoothed away.
- **"living creatures"** (v5, 13–15, 19) — Hebrew חַיּוֹת, plural of חַיָּה "living one"; in v20, 21, 22 the singular הַחַיָּה "the living creature" stands for all four (also 10:15, 17, 20), kept. **"they had the likeness of a man"** (v5) — דְּמוּת אָדָם לָהֵנָּה; also "hands of a man" (v8), "the face of a man" (v10), "the appearance of a man" (v26): אָדָם "man", not "human".
- **"every one had four faces" kept in sense** (v6) — literally "four faces to one". The pronouns change between masculine and feminine through the vision (v5–11, 23); English cannot show it.
- **"their feet were straight feet" → "their feet were a straight foot"** (v7) — רַגְלֵיהֶם רֶגֶל יְשָׁרָה: רֶגֶל "foot" (also "leg") in the singular. **"the sole of their feet was like the sole of a calf's foot"** — כַּף "palm, sole" (its row). **"they sparkled like the colour of burnished brass" → "they were sparkling like the look of burnished bronze"** — נֹצְצִים, a participle; נְחֹשֶׁת "bronze"; קָלָל "burnished" is found only here and Dan 10:6 (uncertain).
- **"they had the hands of a man" → "hands of a man were"** (v8) — the qere וִידֵי "and hands of"; the written form (ketiv) וידו is "and his hands". **"and they four had their faces and their wings"** — literally "and their faces and their wings were to the four of them".
- **"Their wings were joined one to another" → "Their wings were joining one to another"** (v9, 23) — חֹבְרֹת, a participle; "one to another" is literally "a woman to her sister" (feminine, of the wings), as in v23 and 3:13. **"they went every one straight forward" → "each went straight forward"** (v9, 12) — literally "each toward the side of his face they went".
- **"the face of an ox"** (v10) — שׁוֹר "ox" (its row). "On the right" / "on the left" are literal here.
- **"Thus were their faces: and their wings were stretched upward" → "And their faces and their wings were parted from above"** (v11) — וּפְנֵיהֶם וְכַנְפֵיהֶם פְּרֻדוֹת מִלְמָעְלָה. The accent (zaqef) after וּפְנֵיהֶם sets "and their faces" a little apart, which is why the KJV closes a sentence there ("Thus were their faces"); the words themselves join faces and wings, as in v8. פְּרֻדוֹת "parted, separated" (the פָּרַד row). **"two wings of every one were joined one to another" → "each had two joining one another"** — חֹבְרוֹת אִישׁ, literally "joining a man" (= each the next one's).
- **"whither the spirit was to go"** (v12, 20) — Hebrew הָרוּחַ, "the spirit" or "the wind": one word.
- **"like burning coals of fire, and like the appearance of lamps: it went up and down" → "like coals of fire, burning, like the appearance of the torches; it was going about"** (v13) — לַפִּיד "torch"; הִיא מִתְהַלֶּכֶת, feminine singular: what goes about may be the fire or the likeness; not said. **"and the fire was bright" → "and the fire had brightness"** — וְנֹגַהּ לָאֵשׁ. **"went forth lightning" → "lightning was going out"** — יוֹצֵא, a participle.
- **"ran and returned" → "were running and returning"** (v14) — רָצוֹא וָשׁוֹב, two infinitives absolute. **"a flash of lightning" → "the flash"** — בָּזָק, found only here; "lightning" (בָּרָק) is the word of v13; the sense "flash" is a guess from the context.
- **"with his four faces" → "for its four faces"** (v15) — לְאַרְבַּעַת פָּנָיו: whether the wheel stands at each creature, or the wheel itself has four faces, is not clear.
- **"and their work was like unto the colour of a beryl" → "and their work was like the look of beryl"** (v16) — Hebrew תַּרְשִׁישׁ, the "Tarshish stone" (the breastpiece row: a traditional identification). **"as it were a wheel in the middle of a wheel" → "as though the wheel were in the midst of the wheel"** — כַּאֲשֶׁר יִהְיֶה הָאוֹפַן בְּתוֹךְ הָאוֹפָן, with the article.
- **"they went upon their four sides" → "On their four sides they went"** (v17) — רִבְעֵיהֶן, literally "their fourths"; the Hebrew order is kept.
- **"As for their rings, they were so high that they were dreadful" → "And their rims — they had height, and they had fear"** (v18) — וְגַבֵּיהֶן וְגֹבַהּ לָהֶם וְיִרְאָה לָהֶם, word for word; גַּב "rim" (the bases row); יִרְאָה "fear" (its row): fear went with them, i.e. they were fearsome. A change of וְיִרְאָה to וָאֵרֶא "and I saw" is proposed (from memory); not followed.
- **"were lifted up" kept** (v19–21) — נִשָּׂא nifal, "be lifted up"; "rose" would make it their own act. **"over against them" → "alongside them"** (v20, 21) — לְעֻמָּתָם (the reviewer's לְעֻמַּת row). **"the spirit of the living creature"** (v20, 21) — singular in the Hebrew. **"When those went, these went" → "When they went, they went"** (v21) — the Hebrew has no "those" and "these"; who goes with whom is left as it stands.
- **"the likeness of the firmament upon the heads of the living creature" → "over the heads of the living creature there was a likeness: an expanse"** (v22) — וּדְמוּת עַל־רָאשֵׁי הַחַיָּה רָקִיעַ: "likeness" and "expanse" are separated by the words between. רָקִיעַ "expanse" (its row). **"the terrible crystal" → "the fearsome ice"** — קֶרַח is "ice" (Job 6:16; 37:10; its row); "crystal" is a sense drawn from it. נוֹרָא "fearsome" (its row).
- **"every one had two, which covered on this side, and every one had two, which covered on that side, their bodies" → "each had two that covered them, and each had two that covered them — their bodies"** (v23) — the Hebrew repeats the same words, לְאִישׁ שְׁתַּיִם מְכַסּוֹת לָהֵנָּה, twice; "on this side / on that side" is not written.
- **"the noise of their wings … as the voice of the Almighty, the voice of speech, as the noise of an host" → "the sound of their wings … like the voice of Shaddai, when they went, a sound of roaring, like the sound of a camp"** (v24) — one word, קוֹל, "sound" of things and "voice" of Shaddai (its row); הֲמֻלָּה "roaring" (as Jer 11:16), not "speech"; מַחֲנֶה "camp" (its row), not "host, army". **"they let down their wings"** (v24, 25) — תְּרַפֶּינָה, from רָפָה "sink, slacken".
- **"over their heads"** (v25, 26) — Hebrew עַל־רֹאשָׁם, singular "their head".
- **"as the appearance of a sapphire stone"** (v26) — אֶבֶן־סַפִּיר; סַפִּיר is probably lapis lazuli (the breastpiece row). **"the likeness as the appearance of a man above upon it" → "a likeness like the appearance of a man, upon it from above"** — the Hebrew order.
- **"as the appearance of fire round about within it" → "like the appearance of fire, enclosing it all around"** (v27) — בֵּית־לָהּ סָבִיב, literally "a house to it all around": a casing; what "it" is (feminine) is not said. **"loins"** — מָתְנַיִם. **"it had brightness round about" → "he had brightness all around"** — לוֹ, masculine.
- **"As the appearance of the bow"** (v28) — קֶשֶׁת "bow" (its row: the same word as the weapon). **"in the day of rain" → "on the day of the rain"** — בְּיוֹם הַגֶּשֶׁם, with the article. **"This was the appearance of the likeness of the glory of the LORD" → "That was the appearance of the likeness of the glory of Yahweh"** — the earlier text had dropped this clause; כְּבוֹד יְהוָה "the glory of Yahweh". **"And when I saw it, I fell" → "And I saw, and I fell"** — two wayyiqtol verbs. **"the voice of one that spake" → "a voice of one speaking"** — קוֹל מְדַבֵּר, without the article.

### Notes
- **Merkavah vision** — foundational for Jewish mystical tradition (*Ma'aseh Merkavah*).
- **Four faces** — man, lion, ox, eagle: later symbols of the four Gospels.
- **593 BC** — fifth year of Jehoiachin's exile.
- **v28** — careful use of "likeness" (*demut*): Ezekiel guards against anthropomorphism.
