# Ezekiel 41

[1] And he brought me to the temple, and he measured the pilasters: six cubits the width on this side and six cubits the width on that side, the width of the tent.

[2] And the width of the entrance was ten cubits, and the shoulders of the entrance five cubits on this side and five cubits on that side. And he measured its length, forty cubits, and the width, twenty cubits.

[3] And he went inward and measured the pilaster of the entrance, two cubits; and the entrance, six cubits; and the width of the entrance, seven cubits.

[4] And he measured its length, twenty cubits, and the width, twenty cubits, facing the temple. And he said to me, "This is the Holy of Holies."

[5] And he measured the wall of the house, six cubits; and the width of the side chamber, four cubits, all around, all around the house, around.

[6] And the side chambers were side chamber upon side chamber, thirty-three times; and they went into the wall that belonged to the house for the side chambers all around, all around, so as to be held, and they were not held in the wall of the house.

[7] And it widened and went around, higher and higher, for the side chambers; for the house was surrounded higher and higher, all around, all around the house. Therefore the house had width upward; and so the lowest goes up to the highest by the middle one.

[8] And I saw that the house had a height all around, all around: the foundations of the side chambers were a full reed, six cubits, to the joint.

[9] The width of the wall that belonged to the side chamber, toward the outside, was five cubits; and what was left free was the house of side chambers that belonged to the house.

[10] And between the chambers was a width of twenty cubits around the house, all around, all around.

[11] And the entrance of the side chamber was toward what was left free: one entrance toward the north, and one entrance to the south; and the width of the place that was left free was five cubits all around, all around.

[12] And the building that was facing the separate place, at the side toward the west, was seventy cubits wide; and the wall of the building was five cubits wide all around, all around, and its length ninety cubits.

[13] And he measured the house: the length, a hundred cubits; and the separate place and the building and its walls: the length, a hundred cubits.

[14] And the width of the face of the house and of the separate place toward the east, a hundred cubits.

[15] And he measured the length of the building facing the separate place that was at its rear, and its galleries on this side and on that side, a hundred cubits.

And the inner temple, and the porches of the court,

[16] the thresholds, and the closed windows, and the galleries around the three of them, opposite the threshold, were a paneling of wood all around, all around; and from the ground up to the windows; and the windows were covered,

[17] to above the entrance, and to the inner house, and to the outside, and on all the wall all around, all around, on the inner and on the outer: measures.

[18] And it was made with ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ and palm trees, and a palm tree between ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ and ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧; and the ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ had two faces:

[19] and the face of a man toward the palm tree on this side, and the face of a young lion toward the palm tree on that side; it was made on all the house all around, all around.

[20] From the ground to above the entrance the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ and the palm trees were made; and the wall of the temple.

[21] The temple: a squared doorpost; and the face of the Holy: the appearance was like the appearance.

[22] The altar was wood, three cubits high, and its length two cubits; and it had its corners, and its length and its walls were wood. And he spoke to me, "This is the table that is before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[23] And the temple and the Holy had two doors.

[24] And the doors had two leaves: two turning leaves, two for the one door and two leaves for the other.

[25] And there were made on them, on the doors of the temple, ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ and palm trees, as they were made on the walls; and there was a canopy of wood on the face of the porch outside.

[26] And there were closed windows and palm trees on this side and on that side, on the shoulders of the porch; and the side chambers of the house, and the canopies.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v22) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Building words** (throughout) — as 1 Kgs 6–7: הֵיכָל "temple" (here the front room, v1, 4, 15, 20, 21, 23, 25; the former text had "nave"), בַּיִת "house" (the whole building), אוּלָם "porch", צֵלָע "side chamber" (lit. "side"), לִשְׁכָּה "chamber" (v10), פֶּתַח "entrance", דֶּלֶת "door", אַיִל "pilaster" (a projecting part of a doorway; uncertain), כָּתֵף "shoulder" (the stretch of wall beside an opening, v2, 26), רֹחַב "width". Many words of this chapter are rare and the plan is not certain: the plainest sense is given and nothing is filled in from the Septuagint.
- **"Afterward" → "And"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְבִיאֵנִי. **"posts" → "pilasters"** — הָאֵילִים (as 1 Kgs 6:31). **"on the one side … on the other side" → "on this side … on that side"** — מִפּוֹ … מִפּוֹ, literally "from here … from here" (also v2, 15, 19, 26). **"which was the breadth of the tabernacle" → "the width of the tent"** — Hebrew רֹחַב הָאֹהֶל, as the Masoretic Text has it; what "the tent" means here is not known. The Septuagint reads "the pilaster" (אַיִל) instead (from memory); not followed.
- **"the sides of the door" → "the shoulders of the entrance"** (v2) — כִּתְפוֹת הַפֶּתַח.
- **"Then went he inward" → "And he went inward"** (v3) — וּבָא לִפְנִימָה; the subject is not named. **"and the breadth of the door, seven cubits"** — Hebrew וְרֹחַב הַפֶּתַח שֶׁבַע אַמּוֹת; the former text had "the sidewalls on either side of the entrance, seven cubits", which is the Septuagint's reading (from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept, though "six" and "seven" for the same entrance are not explained.
- **"before the temple" → "facing the temple"** (v4) — אֶל־פְּנֵי הַהֵיכָל, literally "to the face of". **"the most holy place" → "the Holy of Holies"** — קֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים with the article, as the inner room in 1 Kgs 6:16; "place" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"round about the house on every side" → "all around, all around the house, around"** (v5) — Hebrew סָבִיב סָבִיב לַבַּיִת סָבִיב. The doubled סָבִיב סָבִיב is kept double, "all around, all around" (v5–8, 10, 11, 12, 16, 17, 19; as the Ezek 40–48 word list), the single סָבִיב "around" (v5, 10, 16).
- **"three, one over another, and thirty in order" → "side chamber upon side chamber, thirty-three times"** (v6) — Hebrew צֵלָע אֶל־צֵלָע שָׁלוֹשׁ וּשְׁלֹשִׁים פְּעָמִים, literally "three and thirty times". Whether thirty-three chambers are meant, or three stories of thirty, the Hebrew does not say. **"they entered into the wall"** — וּבָאוֹת "and they went in". **"that they might have hold, but they had not hold" → "so as to be held, and they were not held"** — אֲחוּזִים, passive participle of אָחַז "take hold" (1 Kgs 6:6, 10).
- **"there was an enlarging, and a winding about still upward" → "it widened and went around, higher and higher"** (v7) — Hebrew וְרָחֲבָה וְנָסְבָה לְמַעְלָה לְמַעְלָה, two verbs. **"the winding about of the house went still upward" → "the house was surrounded higher and higher"** — מוּסַב־הַבַּיִת, taken as the passive participle of סָבַב "go around"; or a noun, "the winding (passage) of the house". **"and so increased from the lowest chamber to the highest by the midst" → "and so the lowest goes up to the highest by the middle one"** — Hebrew וְכֵן הַתַּחְתּוֹנָה יַעֲלֶה עַל־הָעֶלְיוֹנָה לַתִּיכוֹנָה: "chamber" and "from" are not in the Hebrew; the verb is masculine, the adjectives feminine, so who or what goes up is not clear (a stair, or one who climbs). The verse is difficult throughout.
- **"I saw also the height of the house" → "And I saw that the house had a height"** (v8) — וְרָאִיתִי לַבַּיִת גֹּבַהּ, literally "I saw to the house a height": a raised base is commonly meant. **"foundations"** — qere מוּסְדוֹת; the written form מיסדות has the same sense. **"six great cubits" → "six cubits, to the joint"** — Hebrew אַצִּילָה, found in this sense only here; אַצִּיל is a joint of the arm (Jer 38:12). "Great cubits" and "to the corner" are guesses; the sense is uncertain.
- **"which was for the side chamber without" → "that belonged to the side chamber, toward the outside"** (v9) — אֲשֶׁר־לַצֵּלָע אֶל־הַחוּץ. **"that which was left was the place of the side chambers that were within" → "what was left free was the house of side chambers that belonged to the house"** — Hebrew וַאֲשֶׁר מֻנָּח בֵּית צְלָעוֹת: מֻנָּח "left, laid down" (from נוּחַ), "the free space"; בֵּית "house of". Many read בֵּין "between" (the former text had "between the side chambers"); the Masoretic Text is kept. The former text also ran v9 into v10 by adding "and the other chambers".
- **"the wideness" → "a width"** (v10) — רֹחַב. **"chambers"** — הַלְּשָׁכוֹת, another word than צְלָעוֹת "side chambers".
- **"the doors of the side chambers" → "the entrance of the side chamber"** (v11) — Hebrew פֶּתַח הַצֵּלָע, singular. **"toward the place that was left" → "toward what was left free"** — לַמֻּנָּח (v9). **"another door" → "one entrance"** — פֶּתַח אֶחָד twice.
- **"the separate place"** (v12, 13, 14, 15) — Hebrew הַגִּזְרָה, from גָּזַר "cut": a cut-off, set-apart area; the word is used so only in Ezek 41–42; "yard" is not in the Hebrew. **"at the end toward the west" → "at the side toward the west"** — פְּאַת דֶּרֶךְ־הַיָּם, literally "the side of the way of the sea": the sea is the west. **"five cubits thick" → "five cubits wide"** — רֹחַב "width".
- **"an hundred cubits long" → "the length, a hundred cubits"** (v13) — אֹרֶךְ מֵאָה אַמָּה, twice. **"the building, with the walls thereof"** — וְהַבִּנְיָה וְקִירוֹתֶיהָ; בִּנְיָה is another form of בִּנְיָן "building".
- **"the face of the house"** (v14) — פְּנֵי הַבַּיִת, kept.
- **"which was behind it" → "that was at its rear"** (v15) — עַל־אַחֲרֶיהָ. **"the galleries thereof" → "its galleries"** — qere אַתִּיקֶיהָא (the written form ואתוקיהא differs only in spelling); אַתִּיק is found only in Ezek 41–42; "gallery" (a stepped walk or terrace) is the common guess, the sense is uncertain (also v16; 42:3, 5). **"with the inner temple, and the porches of the court"** — וְהַהֵיכָל הַפְּנִימִי וְאֻלַמֵּי הֶחָצֵר: the list begins a new sentence that runs into v16–17 (the line break is kept); "the inside of the nave" was not the Hebrew.
- **"The door posts" → "the thresholds"** (v16) — Hebrew הַסִּפִּים, plural of סַף "threshold" (as נֶגֶד הַסַּף "opposite the threshold" in the same verse). **"narrow windows" → "closed windows"** — הַחַלּוֹנִים הָאֲטֻמוֹת, as 1 Kgs 6:4 (perhaps latticed or narrowing; uncertain; also v26). **"round about on their three stories" → "around the three of them"** — סָבִיב לִשְׁלָשְׁתָּם. **"cieled with wood" → "were a paneling of wood"** — שְׂחִיף עֵץ, שְׂחִיף only here: uncertain ("a covering of planks"). **"from the ground"** — וְהָאָרֶץ "and the earth / the ground"; the floor is meant. **"covered"** — מְכֻסּוֹת, of כִּסָּה "cover". The sentence of v15b–17 has no main verb; the copula "were" is supplied in v16.
- **"by measure" → "measures"** (v17) — Hebrew מִדּוֹת, one word at the end of the verse: "measured (panels)" or "according to measure"; uncertain. **"within and without" → "on the inner and on the outer"** — בַּפְּנִימִי וּבַחִיצוֹן.
- **"so that a palm tree was between a cherub and a cherub" → "and a palm tree between cherub and cherub"** (v18) — וְתִמֹרָה בֵּין־כְּרוּב לִכְרוּב; תִּמֹרָה "palm tree" (the ornament, as 1 Kgs 6:29). **"every cherub had two faces" → "the cherub had two faces"** — לַכְּרוּב, singular with the article.
- **"So that the face of a man" → "and the face of a man"** (v19) — וּפְנֵי אָדָם (as Ezek 1:10; 10:14). כְּפִיר "young lion". **"it was made through all the house" → "it was made on all the house"** — עָשׂוּי אֶל־כָּל־הַבַּיִת.
- **"From the ground"** (v20) — מֵהָאָרֶץ (v16). **"and on the wall of the temple" → "and the wall of the temple"** — וְקִיר הַהֵיכָל, without a preposition, standing alone at the end of the verse. The Masoretic Text sets the extraordinary dots over the letters of הַהֵיכָל here; their meaning is not known.
- **"The posts of the temple were squared, and the face of the sanctuary; the appearance of the one as the appearance of the other" → "The temple: a squared doorpost; and the face of the Holy: the appearance was like the appearance"** (v21) — Hebrew הַהֵיכָל מְזוּזַת רְבֻעָה וּפְנֵי הַקֹּדֶשׁ הַמַּרְאֶה כַּמַּרְאֶה, word for word: מְזוּזָה "doorpost" (singular), רְבֻעָה "squared"; הַקֹּדֶשׁ "the Holy" (here the inner room, v4, 23). The former text ran the end of the verse into v22 ("something resembling an altar"); the Masoretic accents end the sentence here.
- **"The altar of wood was three cubits high" → "The altar was wood, three cubits high"** (v22) — הַמִּזְבֵּחַ עֵץ. **"and the length thereof" → "and its length and its walls were wood"** — Hebrew וּמִקְצֹעוֹתָיו לוֹ וְאָרְכּוֹ וְקִירֹתָיו עֵץ, literally "and its corners (were) to it, and its length and its walls, wood". The former text added "two cubits broad" and "its base", which the Septuagint has (from memory); not in the Masoretic Text. **"and he said unto me" → "And he spoke to me"** — וַיְדַבֵּר (דִּבֶּר "speak").
- **"the sanctuary" → "the Holy"** (v23) — לַקֹּדֶשׁ. **"had two doors"** — וּשְׁתַּיִם דְּלָתוֹת, two to each, or two in all: not said. "Each a double door" was an explanation.
- **"two leaves apiece, two turning leaves" → "two leaves: two turning leaves, two for the one door and two leaves for the other"** (v24) — Hebrew וּשְׁתַּיִם דְּלָתוֹת לַדְּלָתוֹת שְׁתַּיִם מוּסַבּוֹת דְּלָתוֹת: the leaves are also called דְּלָתוֹת "doors"; "leaves" keeps them apart. מוּסַבּוֹת "turning" (hofal of סָבַב, v7).
- **"thick planks" → "a canopy of wood"** (v25) — Hebrew וְעָב עֵץ: עָב as 1 Kgs 7:6 "canopy"; the sense is uncertain (a beam, a projecting roof). **"upon the face of the porch"** — אֶל־פְּנֵי הָאוּלָם.
- **"on the sides of the porch" → "on the shoulders of the porch"** (v26) — אֶל־כִּתְפוֹת הָאוּלָם (v2). **"and upon the side chambers of the house, and thick planks" → "and the side chambers of the house, and the canopies"** — וְצַלְעוֹת הַבַּיִת וְהָעֻבִּים, without a preposition; עֻבִּים plural of עָב (v25).

### Notes
- **The plan** (v1–15) — the porch (40:48–49), the temple (forty by twenty cubits, as 1 Kgs 6:17) and the Holy of Holies (twenty by twenty, as 1 Kgs 6:20); around them the side chambers in stories (1 Kgs 6:5–10); behind them "the building" facing "the separate place".
- **The table before Yahweh** (v22) — a wooden altar called "the table"; compare 44:16 "my table" and the table of the bread of the face (Exod 25:23–30).
