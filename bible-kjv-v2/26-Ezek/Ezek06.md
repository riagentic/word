# Ezekiel 6

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[2] "Son of man, put your face toward the mountains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and prophesy to them,

[3] and say, 'Mountains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, hear the word of ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧! Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ to the mountains and to the hills, to the channels and to the valleys: Behold, I, I am bringing a sword upon you, and I will do away with your high places.

[4] And your altars shall be desolate, and your incense altars shall be broken, and I will make your slain fall before your idols.

[5] And I will set the carcasses of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ before their idols, and I will scatter your bones around your altars.

[6] In all your dwelling places the cities shall be laid waste and the high places shall be desolate, so that your altars may be laid waste and bear guilt, and your idols be broken and cease, and your incense altars be hewn down, and your works be wiped out.

[7] And the slain shall fall in your midst, and **you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧**.

[8] "'And I will leave some over, when you have fugitives of the sword among the nations, when you are scattered in the lands.

[9] And your fugitives shall remember me among the nations where they have been taken captive, how I was broken by their heart that plays the prostitute, which turned aside from me, and by their eyes that play the prostitute after their idols; and they shall loathe themselves in their own faces for the evils that they have done, for all their abominations.

[10] And they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; not for nothing did I speak of doing this evil to them.'

[11] "Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Strike with your palm, and stamp with your foot, and say, 'Ah!' over all the evil abominations of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, who shall fall by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence.

[12] He who is far off shall die by the pestilence, and he who is near shall fall by the sword, and he who is left and he who is kept shall die by the famine; and I will spend my fury on them.

[13] And you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, when their slain are in the midst of their idols around their altars, on every high hill, on all the tops of the mountains, and under every luxuriant tree, and under every leafy terebinth, the place where they gave a soothing smell to all their idols.

[14] And I will stretch out my hand against them, and make the land a desolation and a thing that appalls, from the wilderness to ⟦divlah|Divlah|Riblah⟧, in all their dwelling places; and they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*, both words; Ezekiel's distinctive doubled divine title.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept (v4–6, 9, 12–14).
- **"And the word of the LORD came unto me, saying"** (v1) — the prophets' formula, one wording; "saying" (לֵאמֹר) kept.
- **"Son of man, set thy face toward" → "Son of man, put your face toward"** (v2) — שִׂים פָּנֶיךָ אֶל (the שִׂים פָּנִים row: "put"; נָתַן פָּנִים is "set"). **"prophesy against them" → "prophesy to them"** — אֲלֵיהֶם "to them".
- **"Ye mountains of Israel" → "Mountains of Israel"** (v3) — vocative, no "you" / "O" in the Hebrew. **"to the rivers, and to the valleys" → "to the channels and to the valleys"** — אֲפִיקִים "channels" (the reviewer's אָפִיק row: stream beds); written וּלְגֵאָית, read (qere) וְלַגֵּאָיוֹת "and to the valleys": the same word in two spellings. **"Behold, I, even I, will bring" → "Behold, I, I am bringing"** — הִנְנִי אֲנִי מֵבִיא, a participle. **"I will destroy your high places" → "I will do away with your high places"** — אִבַּדְתִּי, piel of אָבַד "perish" (the אִבֵּד row); בָּמוֹת "high places" (also v6).
- **"And your altars shall be desolate, and your images shall be broken" → "And your altars shall be desolate, and your incense altars shall be broken"** (v4) — נָשַׁם, nifal of שָׁמֵם (the שָׁמֵם row; also v6 תִּישָׁמְנָה); חַמָּנִים "incense altars" (the חַמָּנִים row; uncertain: also "sun pillars"; also v6). **"I will cast down your slain men" → "I will make your slain fall"** — הִפַּלְתִּי, hifil of נָפַל "fall"; חָלָל "slain" (one pierced; also v7, 13).
- **"idols"** (v4, 5, 6, 9, 13) — גִּלּוּלִים, a word of contempt (the גִּלּוּלִים row). Its source is uncertain: it is often taken from the root of גֵּל / גָּלָל "dung" (hence "dung-things", a slur), or from גָּלַל "roll" ("blocks, logs"); the plain word "idols" is kept in the verse.
- **"I will lay the dead carcases of the children of Israel" → "I will set the carcasses of the sons of Israel"** (v5) — וְנָתַתִּי "and I will give / set"; פֶּגֶר "carcass" (the פֶּגֶר row); בְּנֵי "sons of". The address passes from "your" to "their" and back, as in the Hebrew.
- **"In all your dwellingplaces the cities shall be laid waste" → "In all your dwelling places the cities shall be laid waste"** (v6) — מוֹשָׁב "dwelling place" (also v14); חָרֵב of cities "be laid waste" (the חָרֵב "dry up" row is said of water). **"that your altars may be laid waste and made desolate" → "so that your altars may be laid waste and bear guilt"** — לְמַעַן "so that"; וְיֶאְשְׁמוּ is pointed from אָשֵׁם "be guilty"; the common reading יֵשְׁמוּ "be desolate" (from יָשַׁם) changes the vowels (from memory: so the versions): the MT is kept. **"and your idols may be broken and cease" → "and your idols be broken and cease"** — נִשְׁבְּתוּ, nifal of שָׁבַת "cease". **"your images may be cut down" → "your incense altars be hewn down"** — נִגְדְּעוּ (the גִּדַּע "hew down" row). **"your works may be abolished" → "your works be wiped out"** — נִמְחוּ (the מָחָה "wipe out" row).
- **"Yet will I leave a remnant" → "And I will leave some over"** (v8) — וְהוֹתַרְתִּי, no object (hifil of יָתַר "be left over"; the accents join it to what follows). **"that ye may have some that shall escape the sword" → "when you have fugitives of the sword"** — בִּהְיוֹת לָכֶם, literally "in there being to you"; פָּלִיט "fugitive" (also v9). **"through the countries" → "in the lands"** — בָּאֲרָצוֹת.
- **"And they that escape of you" → "And your fugitives"** (v9). **"where they shall be carried captives" → "where they have been taken captive"** — נִשְׁבּוּ, a perfect. **"because I am broken with their whorish heart" → "how I was broken by their heart that plays the prostitute"** — אֲשֶׁר נִשְׁבַּרְתִּי אֶת־לִבָּם, MT "I was broken" (nifal of שָׁבַר), with אֶת before "their heart" (here "by"; the sense of the construction is uncertain); many read שִׁבַּרְתִּי "I broke their heart" (from memory: so the Targum and others): the MT is kept. הַזּוֹנֶה … הַזֹּנוֹת, participles of זָנָה "play the prostitute" (the זָנָה row; of unfaithfulness to Yahweh the same word). **"which hath departed from me" → "which turned aside from me"** — סָר, of סוּר "turn aside". **"and they shall lothe themselves for the evils" → "and they shall loathe themselves in their own faces for the evils"** — וְנָקֹטּוּ בִּפְנֵיהֶם, nifal of קוּט "loathe" (another root than קוּץ), literally "in their faces"; אֶל־הָרָעוֹת "over the evils".
- **"and that I have not said in vain that I would do this evil unto them" → "not for nothing did I speak of doing this evil to them"** (v10) — לֹא אֶל־חִנָּם דִּבַּרְתִּי, literally "not to nothing" (the חִנָּם row: "for nothing"); דִּבֶּר "speak"; no "that" before it in the Hebrew; רָעָה "evil" (the harm brought).
- **"Smite with thine hand, and stamp with thy foot" → "Strike with your palm, and stamp with your foot"** (v11) — בְכַפְּךָ "with your palm"; רְקַע of רָקַע "beat, stamp". **"Alas for all the evil abominations" → "'Ah!' over all the evil abominations"** — אָח, a cry; whether of grief or of scorn (as הֶאָח with clapping and stamping over another's fall, 25:3, 6) the word does not say; אֶל "over". **"for they shall fall" → "who shall fall"** — אֲשֶׁר. **"by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence"** — each with the article (also v12).
- **"he that remaineth and is besieged" → "he who is left and he who is kept"** (v12) — וְהַנִּשְׁאָר וְהַנָּצוּר, two participles each with the article; נָצוּר is the passive of נָצַר "guard, keep" (the נָצַר row); "besieged" takes it from צוּר "besiege" (as Isa 1:8): uncertain. **"Thus will I accomplish my fury upon them" → "and I will spend my fury on them"** — וְכִלֵּיתִי, piel of כָּלָה, as 5:13.
- **"when their slain men shall be among their idols" → "when their slain are in the midst of their idols"** (v13) — בִּהְיוֹת … בְּתוֹךְ. **"upon every high hill, in all the tops of the mountains" → "on every high hill, on all the tops of the mountains"** — אֶל כָּל־גִּבְעָה רָמָה בְּכֹל רָאשֵׁי הֶהָרִים. **"under every green tree, and under every thick oak" → "under every luxuriant tree, and under every leafy terebinth"** — עֵץ רַעֲנָן (the רַעֲנָן row: "luxuriant", not a colour); אֵלָה עֲבֻתָּה "a thick (leafy) terebinth" (the אֵלָה and עָבֹת rows). **"the place where they did offer sweet savour" → "the place where they gave a soothing smell"** — נָתְנוּ "they gave"; רֵיחַ נִיחֹחַ "soothing smell" (the רֵיחַ row).
- **"and make the land desolate, yea, more desolate than the wilderness toward Diblath" → "and make the land a desolation and a thing that appalls, from the wilderness to Divlah"** (v14) — שְׁמָמָה וּמְשַׁמָּה (as 5:15); מִמִּדְבַּר דִּבְלָתָה, "from the wilderness toward Diblah" (the ending ־ָה "toward"), or "more than the wilderness of Diblah" (KJV; מִן of comparison): both are possible. MT דִּבְלָה; "Riblah" (the traditional form of the token) changes ד to ר, a common correction (from memory): the MT name stands.

### Notes
- **"You shall know that I am Yahweh"** (v7, 10, 13, 14) — Ezekiel's refrain (more than sixty times): וִידַעְתֶּם / וְיָדְעוּ כִּי־אֲנִי יְהוָה, one wording through the book.
- **v1–7** — spoken to the mountains; the "you" plural is the land and its people together.
