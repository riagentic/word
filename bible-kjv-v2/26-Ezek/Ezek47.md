# Ezekiel 47

[1] And he brought me back to the entrance of the house; and behold, water was going out from under the threshold of the house eastward, for the face of the house was east; and the water was coming down from under, from the right shoulder of the house, from the south of the altar.

[2] And he brought me out by the way of the gate northward, and led me around by the way outside to the outer gate, by the way of the one facing east; and behold, water was trickling from the right shoulder.

[3] When the man went out eastward, and a line in his hand, he measured a thousand by the cubit, and made me pass over through the water, water of the ankles.

[4] And he measured a thousand, and made me pass over through the water, water of the knees; and he measured a thousand, and made me pass over, water of the loins.

[5] And he measured a thousand: a torrent that I could not pass over, for the water had risen, water of swimming, a torrent that could not be passed over.

[6] And he said to me, "Have you seen, son of man?" And he led me, and brought me back to the shore of the torrent.

[7] When I returned, behold, at the shore of the torrent were very many trees, on this side and on that side.

[8] And he said to me, "These waters go out to the eastern district, and go down to the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧, and come to the sea; into the sea, the waters brought out, and the waters shall be healed.

[9] And it shall be that every living soul that swarms, wherever the two torrents come, shall live; and the fish shall be very many, for these waters have come there, and they shall be healed; and everything shall live where the torrent comes.

[10] And it shall be that fishermen shall stand by it from ⟦en-gedi|Ein Gedi|Engedi⟧ to ⟦eneglaim|Ein Eglayim|Eneglaim⟧; a spreading place for dragnets they shall be; after its kind their fish shall be, like the fish of the Great Sea, very many.

[11] Its swamps and its marshes shall not be healed; to salt they are given.

[12] And by the torrent, on its shore, on this side and on that side, shall come up every tree for food; its leaf shall not wither, and its fruit shall not come to an end; by its months it shall bear first fruit, for its waters go out from the sanctuary; and its fruit shall be for food, and its leaf for healing."

[13] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: "This is the border by which you shall inherit the land for the twelve tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, portions.

[14] And you shall inherit it, each man as his brother, concerning which I lifted up my hand to give it to your fathers; and this land shall fall to you as an inheritance.

[15] And this is the border of the land: on the north side, from the Great Sea, the way of ⟦hethlon|Chetlon|Hethlon⟧, as you come to ⟦zedad|Tzedad|Zedad⟧;

[16] ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧, ⟦berothah|Berotah|Berothah⟧, ⟦sibraim|Sivrayim|Sibraim⟧, which is between the border of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧ and the border of ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧; ⟦hazer-hatticon|Chatzer Hattichon|Hazer-hatticon⟧, which is by the border of ⟦hauran|Chavran|Hauran⟧.

[17] And the border shall be from the sea to ⟦hazar-enan|Chatzar Enan|Hazar-enan⟧, the border of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, and north northward, and the border of ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧; and the north side.

[18] And the east side: from between ⟦hauran|Chavran|Hauran⟧ and from between ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, and from between the ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ and from between the land of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧; from the border to the eastern sea you shall measure; and the east side.

[19] And the south side, southward: from Tamar to the waters of ⟦meribah-kadesh|Merivot Qadesh|Meribah-kadesh⟧, toward the wadi, to the Great Sea; and the south side, southward.

[20] And the west side: the Great Sea, from the border to opposite ⟦lebo-hamath|Levo Chamat|Lebo-hamath⟧; this is the west side.

[21] And you shall divide this land for yourselves by the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[22] And it shall be: you shall make it fall as an inheritance for yourselves and for the sojourners who sojourn in your midst, who have begotten sons in your midst; and they shall be to you as a native among the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; with you they shall fall in inheritance in the midst of the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[23] And it shall be: in the tribe with which the sojourner sojourns, there you shall give his inheritance, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v13, 23) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*, both names.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept ("afterward", "then", "now", "so" are not in the Hebrew: v1, 2, 7, 21).
- **"unto the door of the house" → "to the entrance of the house"** (v1) — פֶּתַח "entrance" (דֶּלֶת is "door"); הַבַּיִת "the house" (also "the threshold of the house"). **"waters issued out" → "water was going out"** — יֹצְאִים, a participle (also "coming down"). **"for the forefront of the house stood toward the east" → "for the face of the house was east"** — כִּי־פְנֵי הַבַּיִת קָדִים. **"from the right side of the house, at the south side of the altar" → "from the right shoulder of the house, from the south of the altar"** — מִכֶּתֶף הַבַּיִת הַיְמָנִית (as 1 Kgs 7:39 "the right shoulder of the house": "right" kept, the house faces east, so its right is the south); מִנֶּגֶב לַמִּזְבֵּחַ "from the south of".
- **"there ran out waters on the right side" → "water was trickling from the right shoulder"** (v2) — מְפַכִּים, found only here (perhaps from פַּךְ "flask": "trickling" is uncertain); מִן־הַכָּתֵף הַיְמָנִית. **"unto the utter gate by the way that looketh eastward" → "to the outer gate, by the way of the one facing east"** — דֶּרֶךְ הַפּוֹנֶה קָדִים.
- **"when the man that had the line in his hand went forth" → "When the man went out eastward, and a line in his hand"** (v3) — בְּצֵאת־הָאִישׁ קָדִים וְקָו בְּיָדוֹ, a circumstantial clause. **"a thousand cubits" → "a thousand by the cubit"** — אֶלֶף בָּאַמָּה (the unit is named here only; v4–5 "a thousand"). **"he brought me through the waters" → "made me pass over through the water"** — וַיַּעֲבִרֵנִי, hifil of עָבַר (also v4). **"the waters were to the ankles" → "water of the ankles"** — מֵי אָפְסָיִם, literally (= water up to the ankles; אֶפֶס "end", here the ankle, uncertain); so "water of the knees", "water of the loins" (v4: בִּרְכַּיִם, מָתְנַיִם).
- **"it was a river" → "a torrent"** (v5) — נַחַל, a stream bed with its water (the wadi row: "torrent" where the water itself is meant; נָהָר is "river"; also v6–9, 12). **"waters to swim in" → "water of swimming"** — מֵי שָׂחוּ, שָׂחוּ found only here. **"that could not be passed over"** — לֹא־יֵעָבֵר.
- **"Son of man, hast thou seen this?" → "Have you seen, son of man?"** (v6) — הֲרָאִיתָ בֶן־אָדָם: no "this"; "son of man" as the Ezekiel row. **"Then he brought me, and caused me to return to the brink of the river" → "And he led me, and brought me back to the shore of the torrent"** — וַיּוֹלִכֵנִי וַיְשִׁבֵנִי שְׂפַת הַנָּחַל: שָׂפָה, literally "lip", of a thing "shore" (also v7, 12).
- **"Now when I had returned" → "When I returned"** (v7) — בְּשׁוּבֵנִי. **"very many trees"** — עֵץ רַב מְאֹד, a collective singular ("tree, very much").
- **"toward the east country" → "to the eastern district"** (v8) — הַגְּלִילָה הַקַּדְמוֹנָה: גְּלִילָה "district" (literally "circle"). **"into the desert" → "to the Arabah"** — הָעֲרָבָה, the rift of the Jordan and the Salt Sea (token). **"which being brought forth into the sea, the waters shall be healed" → "into the sea, the waters brought out, and the waters shall be healed"** — אֶל־הַיָּמָּה הַמּוּצָאִים: the phrase is difficult and kept word for word (הַמּוּצָאִים "those brought out", hofal participle; read by some as "the foul (waters)", from memory); the written form ונרפאו and the qere וְנִרְפּוּ are the same verb, נִרְפָּא "be healed" (רָפָא "heal"; also v9, 11); "made fresh" is not the word.
- **"every thing that liveth, which moveth" → "every living soul that swarms"** (v9) — כָל־נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה אֲשֶׁר־יִשְׁרֹץ: נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה "living soul" (as Gen 1:20); שָׁרַץ "swarm". **"whithersoever the rivers shall come" → "wherever the two torrents come"** — נַחֲלַיִם, a dual, as printed (the Septuagint and others have the singular, from memory). **"because these waters shall come thither: for they shall be healed" → "for these waters have come there, and they shall be healed"** — כִּי בָאוּ … וְיֵרָפְאוּ: a perfect, then "and".
- **"the fishers shall stand upon it" → "fishermen shall stand by it"** (v10) — the written form יעמדו (imperfect) and the qere עָמְדוּ (perfect with "and it shall be") say the same; עָלָיו, literally "upon it" (= along it). **"a place to spread forth nets" → "a spreading place for dragnets"** — מִשְׁטוֹחַ לַחֲרָמִים: חֵרֶם "dragnet" (as Eccl 7:26). **"their fish shall be according to their kinds" → "after its kind their fish shall be"** — לְמִינָה, singular; דָּגָה "fish" (collective). **"the great sea" → "the Great Sea"** — הַיָּם הַגָּדוֹל, the western sea (also v15, 19, 20).
- **"the miry places thereof and the marishes thereof" → "Its swamps and its marshes"** (v11) — the written form בצאתו, qere בִּצֹּאתָיו "its swamps"; גְּבָאָיו "its marshes" (uncertain). "But" is not in the Hebrew. **"they shall be given to salt" → "to salt they are given"** — לְמֶלַח נִתָּנוּ, a perfect.
- **"shall grow all trees for meat" → "shall come up every tree for food"** (v12) — יַעֲלֶה … כָּל־עֵץ־מַאֲכָל. **"whose leaf shall not fade, neither shall the fruit thereof be consumed" → "its leaf shall not wither, and its fruit shall not come to an end"** — לֹא־יִבּוֹל עָלֵהוּ וְלֹא־יִתֹּם פִּרְיוֹ: נָבֵל "wither", תַּם "come to an end". **"it shall bring forth new fruit according to his months" → "by its months it shall bear first fruit"** — לָחֳדָשָׁיו יְבַכֵּר: בִּכֵּר, piel of the root of בְּכוֹר "firstborn" and בִּכּוּרִים "firstfruits" (= bear fruit early, each month). **"the leaf thereof for medicine" → "its leaf for healing"** — לִתְרוּפָה, found only here, from the root of רָפָא "heal" (as v8–9). The written form והיו "and they shall be", qere וְהָיָה "and it shall be".
- **"This shall be the border" → "This is the border"** (v13) — גֵּה, a form found only here, for זֶה "this". **"whereby ye shall inherit the land according to the twelve tribes" → "by which you shall inherit the land for the twelve tribes"** — תִּתְנַחֲלוּ, hitpael of נָחַל "inherit" (also v14 qal). **"Joseph shall have two portions" → "Joseph, portions"** — יוֹסֵף חֲבָלִים: two words, no number; חֶבֶל, literally "cord, measuring line", of a measured share "portion": the plural gives Joseph more than one share (two, by 48:4–5 Manasseh and Ephraim).
- **"one as well as another" → "each man as his brother"** (v14) — אִישׁ כְּאָחִיו. **"concerning the which I lifted up mine hand" → "concerning which I lifted up my hand"** — נָשָׂאתִי אֶת־יָדִי (= swore; as Exod 6:8).
- **"from the great sea, the way of Hethlon, as men go to Zedad" → "from the Great Sea, the way of Hethlon, as you come to Zedad"** (v15) — הַדֶּרֶךְ חֶתְלֹן לְבוֹא צְדָדָה, as printed: "Lebo-hamath" is not in the MT here (48:1 has it; the Septuagint has "Hamath" here, from memory); the earlier text added it; the MT is kept. North side: צָפוֹנָה "northward".
- **"Hamath, Berothah, Sibraim"** (v16) — the MT begins the verse with חֲמָת (the earlier text left it out). **"Hazar-hatticon, which is by the coast of Hauran" → "Hazer-hatticon, which is by the border of Hauran"** — חָצֵר הַתִּיכוֹן, literally "the middle Hazer (village)" (token); גְּבוּל "border".
- **"And the border from the sea shall be Hazar-enan" → "And the border shall be from the sea to Hazar-enan"** (v17) — "to" supplied for English; the Hebrew sets the names side by side. **"and the north northward" → "and north northward"** — וְצָפוֹן צָפוֹנָה. **"And this is the north side" → "and the north side"** — וְאֵת פְּאַת צָפוֹן: אֵת, the object sign (also v18, 19); commonly read זֹאת "this is", as v20 has it; the MT is kept.
- **"ye shall measure from Hauran, and from Damascus, and from Gilead" → "from between Hauran and from between Damascus, and from between the Gilead and from between the land of Israel, the Jordan"** (v18) — מִבֵּין … וּמִבֵּין, four times, as printed (the Gilead with the article). **"from the border unto the east sea" → "from the border to the eastern sea you shall measure"** — תָּמֹדּוּ "you shall measure" (MT; the Septuagint has "to Tamar", from memory, which the earlier text followed); הַיָּם הַקַּדְמוֹנִי "the eastern sea" (the Salt Sea).
- **"the south side southward" → "the south side, southward"** (v19) — פְּאַת נֶגֶב תֵּימָנָה, literally "the side of the Negev, toward Teman (the right hand)": the quarters by their names, in the verse "south" (also v19b). **"the waters of strife in Kadesh" → "the waters of Meribah-kadesh"** — מֵי מְרִיבוֹת קָדֵשׁ, the plural מְרִיבוֹת "strifes" (token; 48:28 has the singular מְרִיבַת). **"the river to the great sea" → "toward the wadi, to the Great Sea"** — נַחֲלָה, "to the wadi" (the Wadi of Egypt, not named here); "the Brook of Egypt" in the earlier text is not in the Hebrew.
- **"till a man come over against Hamath" → "to opposite Lebo-hamath"** (v20) — עַד־נֹכַח לְבוֹא חֲמָת: לְבוֹא חֲמָת, literally "the coming to Hamath" (token as the name of the place); נֹכַח "opposite". **"This is the west side"** — זֹאת.
- **"ye shall divide it by lot" → "you shall make it fall"** (v22) — תַּפִּלוּ (as 45:1; also "they shall fall in inheritance"). **"the strangers that sojourn among you" → "the sojourners who sojourn in your midst"** — הַגֵּרִים הַגָּרִים, one root. **"which shall beget children" → "who have begotten sons"** — הוֹלִדוּ בָנִים, a perfect. **"as born in the country among the children of Israel" → "as a native among the sons of Israel"** — כְּאֶזְרָח, singular.
- **"there shall ye give him his inheritance" → "there you shall give his inheritance"** (v23) — שָׁם תִּתְּנוּ נַחֲלָתוֹ: no "him". **"in what tribe the stranger sojourneth" → "in the tribe with which the sojourner sojourns"** — אֲשֶׁר־גָּר הַגֵּר אִתּוֹ.

### Notes

- **Temple river** — healing waters; echoes Gen 2:10–14 (Eden rivers); Rev 22:1–2 takes up v1, 12.
- **v12 "its leaf for healing"** — taken up in Rev 22:2.
