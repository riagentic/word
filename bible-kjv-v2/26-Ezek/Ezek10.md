# Ezekiel 10

[1] And I looked, and behold, on the expanse that was over the head of the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧, something like a sapphire stone, like the appearance of the likeness of a throne, appeared above them.

[2] And he said to the man clothed in linen, and he said, "Go in between the whirl, to beneath the ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧, and fill the hollows of your hands with coals of fire from between the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧, and toss over the city." And he went in before my eyes.

[3] And the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ were standing on the south of the house when the man went in, and the cloud filled the inner court.

[4] And the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ rose from upon the ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ to the threshold of the house; and the house was filled with the cloud, and the court was full of the brightness of the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] And the sound of the wings of the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ was heard as far as the outer court, like the voice of ⟦el-shaddai|El Shaddai|God Almighty⟧ when he speaks.

[6] And it came to pass, when he commanded the man clothed in linen, saying, "Take fire from between the whirl, from between the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧," that he went in and stood beside the wheel.

[7] And the ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ stretched out his hand from between the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ to the fire that was between the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧, and lifted it and put it into the hollows of the hands of the one clothed in linen; and he took it and went out.

[8] And there appeared on the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ the pattern of the hand of a man under their wings.

[9] And I looked, and behold, four wheels beside the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧, one wheel beside one ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧, and one wheel beside one ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧; and the appearance of the wheels was like the look of a stone of beryl.

[10] And as for their appearance, the four of them had one likeness, as though the wheel were in the midst of the wheel.

[11] When they went, toward their four sides they went; they did not turn as they went; for to the place to which the head faced, after it they went; they did not turn as they went.

[12] And all their flesh, and their backs, and their hands, and their wings, and the wheels were full of eyes all around — the four of them, their wheels.

[13] As for the wheels, to them it was called in my ears, "The whirl!"

[14] And four faces were to each one: the face of the first was the face of the ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧, and the face of the second the face of a man, and the third the face of a lion, and the fourth the face of an eagle.

[15] And the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ rose up. It is the living creature that I saw by the river ⟦chebar|Kevar|Chebar⟧.

[16] And when the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ went, the wheels went beside them; and when the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ lifted up their wings to rise from upon the earth, the wheels also did not turn from beside them.

[17] When they stood, these stood; and when they rose, these rose with them; for the spirit of the living creature was in them.

[18] And the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ went out from upon the threshold of the house and stood over the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧.

[19] And the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ lifted up their wings and rose from the earth before my eyes as they went out, and the wheels alongside them; and it stood at the entrance of the east gate of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the glory of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ was over them, above.

[20] It is the living creature that I saw under the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ by the river ⟦chebar|Kevar|Chebar⟧; and I knew that they were ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧.

[21] Four, four faces were to each one, and four wings to each one, and the likeness of the hands of a man under their wings.

[22] And as for the likeness of their faces, they were the faces that I saw by the river ⟦chebar|Kevar|Chebar⟧, their appearance and they themselves; each went straight forward.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v19, 20) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל, "the *Elohim* of Israel".
- **"the Almighty God" → "El Shaddai"** (v5) — Hebrew אֵל־שַׁדַּי, both words kept as a name; the meaning of שַׁדַּי is not certain ("Almighty" is the traditional rendering).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Ezek 1** — this chapter shows again the vision of chapter 1, where the beings are called חַיּוֹת "living creatures"; here they are כְּרֻבִים, and v15, 20 say that they are the same. Where the Hebrew of the two chapters is the same, the wording of chapter 1 is kept (v9, 10, 11, 12, 16, 17, 21, 22).
- **"Then I looked, and, behold, in the firmament" → "And I looked, and behold, on the expanse"** (v1) — Hebrew וָאֶרְאֶה וְהִנֵּה אֶל־הָרָקִיעַ: רָקִיעַ "expanse" (as Gen 1); אֶל "to, on". **"the head of the cherubims"** — רֹאשׁ, singular. **"there appeared over them as it were a sapphire stone, as the appearance of the likeness of a throne" → "something like a sapphire stone, like the appearance of the likeness of a throne, appeared above them"** — Hebrew כְּאֶבֶן סַפִּיר כְּמַרְאֵה דְּמוּת כִּסֵּא נִרְאָה עֲלֵיהֶם: נִרְאָה "appeared" has no subject in the Hebrew, "something" is supplied; סַפִּיר "sapphire" (probably lapis lazuli).
- **"And he spake unto the man … and said" → "And he said to the man … and he said"** (v2) — וַיֹּאמֶר twice. **"Go in between the wheels, even under the cherub" → "Go in between the whirl, to beneath the cherub"** — Hebrew בֹּא אֶל־בֵּינוֹת לַגַּלְגַּל אֶל־תַּחַת לַכְּרוּב: גַּלְגַּל is not אוֹפַן "wheel" (v6, 9–19), but a word for whirling things (the whirl of a storm, Ps 77:18; a wheel, Isa 5:28): "whirl" keeps the two words apart, "whirling wheels" or "wheelwork" is the common reading (also v6, 13); כְּרוּב singular (also v4, 7, 9, 14). **"fill thine hand with coals of fire" → "fill the hollows of your hands with coals of fire"** — חָפְנֶיךָ, dual of חֹפֶן "the hollow of the hand" (also v7). **"and scatter them over the city" → "and toss over the city"** — וּזְרֹק עַל־הָעִיר: זָרַק of a dry thing "toss"; no object in the Hebrew. **"in my sight" → "before my eyes"** — לְעֵינַי (also v19).
- **"on the right side of the house" → "on the south of the house"** (v3) — Hebrew מִימִין לַבַּיִת, literally "from the right of the house": facing east, the right hand is the south. **"the cloud filled the inner court"** — הֶעָנָן "the cloud", with the article; מָלֵא "was filling, full".
- **"went up from the cherub, and stood over the threshold" → "rose from upon the cherub to the threshold"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיָּרָם … מֵעַל הַכְּרוּב עַל מִפְתַּן הַבָּיִת; "stood" is not in this verse (it is in v18); נֹגַהּ "brightness".
- **"the sound of the cherubims' wings … as the voice of the Almighty God" → "the sound of the wings … like the voice of El Shaddai"** (v5) — קוֹל, one word: "sound" of the wings, "voice" of El Shaddai.
- **"when he had commanded … saying" → "And it came to pass, when he commanded …, saying"** (v6) — וַיְהִי kept; לֵאמֹר "saying". **"from between the wheels" → "from between the whirl"** — מִבֵּינוֹת לַגַּלְגַּל. **"beside the wheels" → "beside the wheel"** — הָאוֹפָן, singular with the article.
- **"one cherub stretched forth his hand" → "the cherub stretched out his hand"** (v7) — הַכְּרוּב, with the article. **"took thereof, and put it into the hands of him … who took it" → "lifted it and put it into the hollows of the hands of the one clothed in linen; and he took it"** — וַיִּשָּׂא וַיִּתֵּן … וַיִּקַּח: three verbs without an object; "it" is supplied each time.
- **"And there appeared in the cherubims the form of a man's hand" → "And there appeared on the cherubim the pattern of the hand of a man"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיֵּרָא לַכְּרֻבִים תַּבְנִית יַד־אָדָם: תַּבְנִית "pattern" (a shape after which a thing is made); אָדָם "man".
- **"one wheel by one cherub, and another wheel by another cherub" → "one wheel beside one cherub, and one wheel beside one cherub"** (v9) — Hebrew אוֹפַן אֶחָד אֵצֶל הַכְּרוּב אֶחָד, twice the same words. **"as the colour of a beryl stone" → "like the look of a stone of beryl"** — כְּעֵין אֶבֶן תַּרְשִׁישׁ: עַיִן "look" (literally "eye"); תַּרְשִׁישׁ "beryl", the "Tarshish stone" (the kind is uncertain).
- **"they four had one likeness" → "the four of them had one likeness"** (v10) — דְּמוּת אֶחָד לְאַרְבַּעְתָּם. **"as if a wheel had been in the midst of a wheel" → "as though the wheel were in the midst of the wheel"** — הָאוֹפַן בְּתוֹךְ הָאוֹפָן, with the article (as 1:16).
- **"they went upon their four sides" → "toward their four sides they went"** (v11) — Hebrew אֶל־אַרְבַּעַת רִבְעֵיהֶם (רֶבַע "a fourth, a side"). **"to the place whither the head looked they followed it" → "to the place to which the head faced, after it they went"** — הָרֹאשׁ "the head" (the former text had "the front wheel", which the Hebrew does not say; what "the head" is the verse does not explain); לֹא יִסַּבּוּ בְּלֶכְתָּם "they did not turn as they went", twice.
- **"And their whole body, and their backs, and their hands" → "And all their flesh, and their backs, and their hands"** (v12) — Hebrew כָּל־בְּשָׂרָם "all their flesh"; גַּבֵּהֶם "their backs" (in 1:18 the same word is the "rims" of the wheels; here it stands in a list of the parts of the cherubim). **"even the wheels that they four had" → "the four of them, their wheels"** — לְאַרְבַּעְתָּם אוֹפַנֵּיהֶם, literally "to the four of them, their wheels".
- **"As for the wheels, it was cried unto them in my hearing, O wheel" → "As for the wheels, to them it was called in my ears, 'The whirl!'"** (v13) — Hebrew לָאוֹפַנִּים לָהֶם קוֹרָא הַגַּלְגַּל בְּאָזְנָי: קוֹרָא "it was called" (pual); whether the wheels are called out to or are named "the whirl" the Hebrew does not decide.
- **"every one had four faces" → "four faces were to each one"** (v14) — וְאַרְבָּעָה פָנִים לְאֶחָד. **"the first face was the face of a cherub" → "the face of the first was the face of the cherub"** — פְּנֵי הָאֶחָד פְּנֵי הַכְּרוּב; in 1:10 the fourth face is the face of an ox, here the face of the cherub stands in the list (not explained). **"the face of a man"** — פְּנֵי אָדָם. נֶשֶׁר "eagle" (the word also covers the great vultures).
- **"And the cherubims were lifted up. This is the living creature" → "And the cherubim rose up. It is the living creature"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיֵּרֹמּוּ (רוּם "rise", also v16, 17, 19); הִיא הַחַיָּה, singular, the four as one (also v17, 20). **"by the river of Chebar" → "by the river Chebar"** — נְהַר־כְּבָר: נָהָר "river" (the former text had "canal", an explanation; also v20, 22).
- **"the same wheels also turned not from beside them" → "the wheels also did not turn from beside them"** (v16) — גַּם־הֵם "they also"; מֵעַל הָאָרֶץ "from upon the earth".
- **"for the spirit of the living creature was in them"** (v17) — רוּחַ הַחַיָּה, singular; רוּחַ is also "wind, breath".
- **"departed from off the threshold" → "went out from upon the threshold"** (v18) — וַיֵּצֵא מֵעַל.
- **"the wheels also were beside them" → "and the wheels alongside them"** (v19) — וְהָאוֹפַנִּים לְעֻמָּתָם (לְעֻמַּת "alongside"). **"every one stood" → "it stood"** — וַיַּעֲמֹד, singular, no subject; "east" is הַקַּדְמוֹנִי (also "former"). **"over them above" → "over them, above"** — מִלְמָעְלָה.
- **"This is the living creature" → "It is the living creature"** (v20) — הִיא הַחַיָּה. **"they were the cherubims" → "they were cherubim"** — כְרוּבִים, no article.
- **"Every one had four faces apiece" → "Four, four faces were to each one"** (v21) — Hebrew אַרְבָּעָה אַרְבָּעָה פָנִים לְאֶחָד: the number is repeated (= four each). **"the likeness of the hands of a man"** — דְּמוּת יְדֵי אָדָם.
- **"their appearances and themselves: they went every one straight forward" → "their appearance and they themselves; each went straight forward"** (v22) — מַרְאֵיהֶם וְאוֹתָם; אִישׁ אֶל־עֵבֶר פָּנָיו יֵלֵכוּ, literally "they went, a man toward the side of his face" (as 1:9, 12).

### Notes
- **Departure of the glory** (v4, 18–19) — the glory of Yahweh moves from the cherub to the threshold (9:3; 10:4), then to the east gate (10:19), and in 11:23 out of the city.
