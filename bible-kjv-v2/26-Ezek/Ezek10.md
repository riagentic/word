# Ezekiel 10

[1] Then I looked, and behold, on the expanse that was over the heads of the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ there appeared above them something like a sapphire, in appearance like a throne.

[2] And he said to the man clothed in linen, "Go in among the whirling wheels underneath the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧. Fill your hands with burning coals from between the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧, and scatter them over the city." And he went in before my eyes.

[3] Now the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ were standing on the south side of the house, when the man went in; and a cloud filled the inner court.

[4] And the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ went up from the ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ to the threshold of the house, and the house was filled with the cloud, and the court was filled with the brightness of the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] And the sound of the wings of the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ was heard as far as the outer court, like the voice of ⟦el-shaddai|El Shaddai|God Almighty⟧ when he speaks.

[6] And when he commanded the man clothed in linen, "Take fire from between the whirling wheels, from between the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧," he went in and stood beside a wheel.

[7] And a ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ stretched out his hand from between the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ to the fire that was between the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧, and took some of it and put it into the hands of the man clothed in linen, who took it and went out.

[8] The ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ appeared to have the form of a human hand under their wings.

[9] And I looked, and behold, there were four wheels beside the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧, one beside each ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧, and the appearance of the wheels was like sparkling beryl.

[10] And as for their appearance, the four had the same likeness, as if a wheel were within a wheel.

[11] When they went, they went in any of their four directions, without turning as they went, but in whatever direction the front wheel faced, the others followed without turning as they went.

[12] And their whole body, their rims, and their spokes, their wings, and the wheels were full of eyes all around — the wheels that the four of them had.

[13] As for the wheels, they were called in my hearing "the whirling wheels."

[14] And every one had four faces: the first face was the face of the ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧, and the second face was a human face, and the third the face of a lion, and the fourth the face of an eagle.

[15] And the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ mounted up. These were the living creatures that I saw by the ⟦chebar|Kevar|Chebar⟧ canal.

[16] And when the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ went, the wheels went beside them. And when the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ lifted up their wings to mount up from the earth, the wheels did not turn from beside them.

[17] When they stood still, these stood still, and when they mounted up, these mounted up with them, for the spirit of the living creatures was in them.

[18] Then the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ went out from the threshold of the house, and stood over the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧.

[19] And the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ lifted up their wings and mounted up from the earth before my eyes as they went out, with the wheels beside them. And they stood at the entrance of the east gate of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the glory of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ was over them.

[20] These were the living creatures that I saw underneath the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ by the ⟦chebar|Kevar|Chebar⟧ canal, and I knew that they were ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧.

[21] Each had four faces, and each four wings, and underneath their wings the likeness of human hands.

[22] And as for the likeness of their faces, they were the same faces whose appearance I had seen by the ⟦chebar|Kevar|Chebar⟧ canal. Each one went straight forward.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"God of Israel" → "*Elohim* of Israel"** (vv19, 20) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל *Elohey Yisra'el*; *Elohim* retained.
- **"the Almighty God" → "El Shaddai"** (v5) — Hebrew אֵל שַׁדַּי *El Shaddai*.
- **"firmament" → "expanse"** (v1) — Hebrew רָקִיעַ *raqi'a*; "expanse" consistent with Genesis 1; "firmament" is an older Latin-derived term.
- **"the river of Chebar" → "the Chebar canal"** (vv15, 20, 22) — Hebrew נְהַר כְּבָר *nehar Kevar*; "canal" more accurate for Babylonian *naru* (irrigation channel).
- **"O wheel" → "the whirling wheels"** (v13) — Hebrew אוֹפַן *ofan* = wheel; "it was cried … O wheel" is the literal rendering of the obscure *galgal*; "whirling wheels" captures *galgal* = whirling/rolling; more meaningful translation.
- **"turned not as they went, but to the place whither the head looked they followed it" → "in whatever direction the front wheel faced, the others followed without turning"** (v11) — Hebrew יֵלְכוּ אֶל אַרְבַּע רוּחוֹתָם לֹא יִסַּבּוּ בְּלֶכְתָּם *yelekhu el arba' ruchotam lo yissavu belekhtam*; modernized for clarity.

### Notes
- **Departure of the glory** (vv18-19) — the *Kevod Yahweh* leaves the temple, foreshadowed chapter 8, completed chapter 11.
