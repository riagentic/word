# Ezekiel 32

[1] And it came to pass in the twelfth year, in the twelfth month, on the first of the month, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying:

[2] "Son of man, lift up a dirge over ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and say to him:

You were likened to a young lion of the nations,
and you are like the sea monster in the seas;
and you burst forth in your rivers,
and you troubled the waters with your feet,
and you fouled their rivers.

[3] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:

And I will spread my net over you
with an assembly of many peoples,
and they shall bring you up in my dragnet.

[4] And I will abandon you on the land;
on the face of the field I will hurl you;
and I will make all the birds of the heavens abide on you,
and I will sate with you the living things of all the earth.

[5] And I will put your flesh on the mountains,
and fill the valleys with your height.

[6] And I will water the land of your outflow with your blood,
to the mountains,
and the channels shall be filled from you.

[7] And when you are quenched, I will cover the heavens,
and I will make their stars dark;
the sun I will cover with a cloud,
and the moon shall not make its light shine.

[8] All the lights of light in the heavens
I will make dark over you,
and I will put darkness on your land,
declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[9] And I will vex the heart of many peoples,
when I bring your breaking among the nations,
to lands that you have not known.

[10] And I will make many peoples appalled at you,
and their kings shall shudder with shuddering over you,
when I brandish my sword before their faces;
and they shall tremble at every moment,
each man for his soul,
in the day of your fall.

[11] For thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: The sword of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ shall come upon you.

[12] By the swords of mighty ones I will make your multitude fall, the ruthless of the nations, all of them; and they shall devastate the pride of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and all her multitude shall be destroyed.

[13] And I will make all her beasts perish from beside many waters; and the foot of man shall not trouble them any more, and the hoofs of beast shall not trouble them.

[14] Then I will make their waters settle, and their rivers I will make go like oil, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[15] When I make the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ a desolation, and the land is made desolate of its fullness, when I strike all who dwell in it, and they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[16] This is a dirge, and they shall chant it; the daughters of the nations shall chant it; over ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and over all her multitude they shall chant it, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

[17] And it came to pass in the twelfth year, on the fifteenth of the month, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying:

[18] "Son of man, wail over the multitude of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and bring it down, her and the daughters of mighty nations, to the land of the depths, with those who go down to the pit.

[19] Whom do you surpass in loveliness? Go down, and be laid with the uncircumcised.

[20] In the midst of the slain of the sword they shall fall; a sword is given; they have dragged her away, and all her multitudes.

[21] The mighty ones of the mighty shall speak to him from the midst of Sheol, with his helpers: They have gone down, they lie, the uncircumcised, the slain of the sword.

[22] There is ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ and all her assembly; around him are his graves; all of them slain, the ones fallen by the sword,

[23] whose graves are set in the remotest parts of the pit, and her assembly is around her grave; all of them slain, fallen by the sword, who put terror in the land of the living.

[24] There is Elam and all her multitude around her grave; all of them slain, the ones fallen by the sword, who went down uncircumcised to the land of the depths, who put their terror in the land of the living; and they bore their dishonor with those who go down to the pit.

[25] In the midst of the slain they have set a bed for her, with all her multitude; around him are her graves; all of them uncircumcised, slain of the sword; for their terror was put in the land of the living, and they bore their dishonor with those who go down to the pit; in the midst of the slain he is put.

[26] There is Meshech, ⟦tubal|Tuval|Tubal⟧, and all her multitude; around him are her graves; all of them uncircumcised, pierced by the sword; for they put their terror in the land of the living.

[27] And they do not lie with the mighty ones, the fallen of the uncircumcised, who went down to Sheol with their weapons of war, and they put their swords under their heads, and their iniquities were upon their bones; for the terror of the mighty ones was in the land of the living.

[28] And you, in the midst of the uncircumcised you shall be broken, and you shall lie with the slain of the sword.

[29] There is Edom, her kings and all her chieftains, who were put in their might with the slain of the sword; they shall lie with the uncircumcised and with those who go down to the pit.

[30] There are the princes of the north, all of them, and every ⟦sidonians|Tzidoni|Sidonian⟧, who went down with the slain; in their terror, from their might, they are ashamed; and they lay uncircumcised with the slain of the sword, and they bore their dishonor with those who go down to the pit.

[31] Them ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ shall see, and he shall be comforted over all his multitude, the slain of the sword, ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ and all his force, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[32] For I have put my terror in the land of the living; and he shall be laid in the midst of the uncircumcised, with the slain of the sword, ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ and all his multitude, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*; Ezekiel's distinctive doubled divine title.
- **Layout** — the dirge v2b–10 one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, by the accents; v11–32 prose, as before. The quotation marks that opened v2b, 3, 11, 16, 19–30 are dropped: the Hebrew runs on as one speech.
- **"And it came to pass … in the twelfth month, in the first day of the month" → "on the first of the month"** (v1) — וַיְהִי kept; "day" not written. **"came unto me, saying"** — לֵאמֹר kept (also v17).
- **"take up a lamentation" → "lift up a dirge"** (v2; also v16 "This is a dirge") — שָׂא קִינָה, נָשָׂא "lift up"; קִינָה "dirge". **"Thou art like a young lion of the nations" → "You were likened to a young lion of the nations"** — כְּפִיר גּוֹיִם נִדְמֵיתָ, nifal of דָּמָה "be like" (no preposition before "young lion"); the same letters can be דָּמָה "be silenced, cut off": "Young lion of the nations, you are cut off": uncertain. **"and thou art as a whale in the seas" → "and you are like the sea monster in the seas"** — כַּתַּנִּים, the form with final ם for תַּנִּין, as 29:3: "sea monster" (its row; the crocodile is the common explanation, uncertain). **"thou camest forth with thy rivers, and troubledst the waters with thy feet, and fouledst their rivers" → "and you burst forth in your rivers, and you troubled the waters with your feet, and you fouled their rivers"** — וַתָּגַח (גִּיחַ "burst forth", as Job 40:23), וַתִּדְלַח (דָּלַח "make muddy", also v13), וַתִּרְפֹּס (רָפַס "trample, foul"): wayyiqtol, past, each with "and"; בְּנַהֲרוֹתֶיךָ "in your rivers".
- **"I will therefore spread out my net over thee with a company of many people" → "And I will spread my net over you with an assembly of many peoples"** (v3) — וּפָרַשְׂתִּי; קְהַל עַמִּים רַבִּים, קָהָל "assembly". **"they shall bring thee up in my net" → "they shall bring you up in my dragnet"** — חֶרְמִי, חֵרֶם "dragnet" (as Hab 1:15–17; another word than חֵרֶם "devoted thing"; רֶשֶׁת "net" stands beside it).
- **"Then will I leave thee upon the land" → "And I will abandon you on the land"** (v4) — וּנְטַשְׁתִּיךָ, as 29:5. **"I will cast thee forth upon the open field" → "on the face of the field I will hurl you"** — אֲטִילֶךָ (טוּל hifil "hurl"). **"will cause all the fowls of the heaven to remain upon thee" → "I will make all the birds of the heavens abide on you"** — הִשְׁכַּנְתִּי, hifil of שָׁכַן. **"I will fill the beasts of the whole earth with thee" → "I will sate with you the living things of all the earth"** — הִשְׂבַּעְתִּי "sate"; חַיַּת כָּל־הָאָרֶץ.
- **"lay thy flesh" → "put your flesh"** (v5) — נָתַתִּי. **"with thy height"** — רָמוּתֶךָ (only here): from רוּם "be high", the heap of the great body; others read רִמָּה "worms" or "your carcass": uncertain.
- **"I will also water with thy blood the land wherein thou swimmest, even to the mountains" → "And I will water the land of your outflow with your blood, to the mountains"** (v6) — אֶרֶץ צָפָתְךָ (צָפָה only here; from צוּף "flow, swim": what flows from you, or "where you swim"): uncertain. **"the rivers shall be full of thee" → "the channels shall be filled from you"** — אֲפִקִים "channels" (אָפִיק, as 31:12); מִמֶּךָּ "from you".
- **"when I shall put thee out" → "when you are quenched"** (v7) — בְכַבּוֹתְךָ, infinitive of כָּבָה "be quenched" (of a lamp or fire). **"make the stars thereof dark" → "make their stars dark"** — הִקְדַּרְתִּי (קָדַר, as 31:15). **"the moon shall not give her light" → "the moon shall not make its light shine"** — לֹא־יָאִיר אוֹרוֹ.
- **"All the bright lights of heaven" → "All the lights of light in the heavens"** (v8) — כָּל־מְאוֹרֵי אוֹר: מָאוֹר "light" (a thing that gives light, Gen 1:14–16) with אוֹר "light"; word for word. **"set darkness" → "put darkness"** — וְנָתַתִּי חֹשֶׁךְ.
- **"I will also vex the hearts of many people" → "And I will vex the heart of many peoples"** (v9) — וְהִכְעַסְתִּי לֵב, singular "heart". **"thy destruction" → "your breaking"** — שִׁבְרְךָ, from שָׁבַר "break" (the Septuagint has "your captivity", reading שִׁבְיְךָ, from memory). **"into the countries" → "to lands"** — עַל־אֲרָצוֹת.
- **"I will make many people amazed at thee" → "I will make many peoples appalled at you"** (v10) — וַהֲשִׁמּוֹתִי, hifil of שָׁמֵם (of persons "be appalled"). **"their kings shall be horribly afraid for thee" → "their kings shall shudder with shuddering over you"** — יִשְׂעֲרוּ … שַׂעַר, verb and noun of one root (שַׂעַר "horror, bristling"; the old text's "hair … bristle" explains the root). **"when I shall brandish my sword before them" → "before their faces"** — בְּעוֹפְפִי, literally "when I make fly"; עַל־פְּנֵיהֶם. **"every man for his own life" → "each man for his soul"** — אִישׁ לְנַפְשׁוֹ. **"in the day of thy fall"** — מַפַּלְתֶּךָ (as 31:16).
- **"The sword of the king of Babylon shall come upon thee"** (v11) — תְּבוֹאֶךָ. The quotation mark before it is dropped (one speech).
- **"By the swords of the mighty will I cause thy multitude to fall, the terrible of the nations, all of them" → "By the swords of mighty ones I will make your multitude fall, the ruthless of the nations, all of them"** (v12) — גִּבּוֹרִים "mighty ones", no article; עָרִיצֵי גוֹיִם כֻּלָּם (the mighty ones, not the multitude). **"they shall spoil the pomp of Egypt" → "they shall devastate the pride of Egypt"** — וְשָׁדְדוּ (שָׁדַד "lay waste"); גְּאוֹן "pride" (as 30:6). **"shall be destroyed"** — נִשְׁמַד.
- **"I will destroy also all the beasts thereof from beside the great waters" → "And I will make all her beasts perish from beside many waters"** (v13) — וְהַאֲבַדְתִּי (as 30:13); בְּהֶמְתָּהּ "her beasts"; מַיִם רַבִּים "many waters". **"neither shall the foot of man trouble them any more"** — תִדְלָחֵם (as v2).
- **"Then will I make their waters deep" → "Then I will make their waters settle"** (v14) — אַשְׁקִיעַ, hifil of שָׁקַע "sink": the mud sinks and the water stands clear. **"cause their rivers to run like oil" → "their rivers I will make go like oil"** — אוֹלִיךְ, Hebrew order.
- **"and the country shall be destitute of that whereof it was full" → "and the land is made desolate of its fullness"** (v15) — וּנְשַׁמָּה אֶרֶץ מִמְּלֹאָהּ. **"when I shall smite" → "when I strike"** — בְּהַכּוֹתִי. **"then shall they know" → "and they shall know"** — וְיָדְעוּ, the one wording of the formula, here after a clause of time.
- **"This is the lamentation wherewith they shall lament her" → "This is a dirge, and they shall chant it"** (v16) — קִינָה הִיא וְקוֹנְנוּהָ, קוֹנֵן "chant a dirge" (three times); "her multitude".
- **"In the twelfth year, in the fifteenth day of the month" → "And it came to pass in the twelfth year, on the fifteenth of the month"** (v17) — the Hebrew does not name the month (the old text added "in the first month"; the Septuagint has "of the first month", from memory).
- **"wail for the multitude of Egypt, and cast them down, even her" → "wail over the multitude of Egypt, and bring it down, her"** (v18) — נְהֵה "wail" (נָהָה, beside the נְהִי "wailing" row); וְהוֹרִדֵהוּ "bring it (the multitude) down" (hifil of יָרַד, the verb of v18–30); אוֹתָהּ "her" (Egypt). **"the daughters of the famous nations" → "the daughters of mighty nations"** — גּוֹיִם אַדִּרִם, אַדִּיר "mighty". **"unto the nether parts of the earth" → "to the land of the depths"** — אֶרֶץ תַּחְתִּיּוֹת, plural (beside the singular אֶרֶץ תַּחְתִּית "the land below", 31:14, 16, 18; also v24). **"with them that go down into the pit"** — אֶת־יוֹרְדֵי בוֹר, בּוֹר "pit" (v18, 23, 24, 25, 29, 30).
- **"Whom dost thou pass in beauty? go down, and be thou laid with the uncircumcised" → "Whom do you surpass in loveliness? Go down, and be laid with the uncircumcised"** (v19) — מִמִּי נָעָמְתָּ, literally "than whom are you pleasant?" (נָעֵם "be pleasant"); הָשְׁכְּבָה, hofal imperative; עֲרֵלִים "uncircumcised" (v19–32).
- **"They shall fall in the midst of them that are slain by the sword: she is delivered to the sword: draw her and all her multitudes" → "In the midst of the slain of the sword they shall fall; a sword is given; they have dragged her away, and all her multitudes"** (v20) — חַלְלֵי־חֶרֶב "the slain of the sword" (v20–32; חָלָל "slain", one pierced); חֶרֶב נִתָּנָה "a sword is given" (feminine verb with "sword"; "she is given to the sword" reads in a preposition); מָשְׁכוּ is pointed as a perfect, "they have dragged" (the imperative "drag" would be מִשְׁכוּ): the MT is kept.
- **"The strong among the mighty shall speak to him out of the midst of hell with them that help him: they are gone down, they lie uncircumcised, slain by the sword" → "The mighty ones of the mighty shall speak to him from the midst of Sheol, with his helpers: They have gone down, they lie, the uncircumcised, the slain of the sword"** (v21) — אֵלֵי גִבּוֹרִים, אַיִל "mighty one" (as 31:11, 14); לוֹ "to him" (or "of him"); שְׁאוֹל "Sheol" (KJV "hell"; also v27); הָעֲרֵלִים with the article.
- **"Asshur is there and all her company: his graves are about him" → "There is Assyria and all her assembly; around him are his graves"** (v22) — שָׁם first; קָהָל "assembly" (also v23); סְבִיבוֹתָיו קִבְרֹתָיו, masculine "him" (the king) beside "her" (the land): as written (also v25, 26). **"all of them slain, fallen by the sword"** — כֻּלָּם חֲלָלִים הַנֹּפְלִים, with the article "the ones fallen".
- **"whose graves are set in the sides of the pit" → "in the remotest parts of the pit"** (v23) — יַרְכְּתֵי־בוֹר (lit. "the flanks of the pit"). **"which caused terror" → "who put terror"** — נָתְנוּ חִתִּית, נָתַן "put" (as 26:17; v23–32); חִתִּית "terror" (only in Ezekiel).
- **"There is Elam … and have they borne their shame" → "There is Elam … and they bore their dishonor"** (v24) — עֵילָם; וַיִּשְׂאוּ, wayyiqtol, past; כְּלִמָּה "dishonor" (also v25, 30). **"which are gone down uncircumcised into the nether parts of the earth" → "who went down uncircumcised to the land of the depths"**.
- **"They have set her a bed" → "they have set a bed for her"** (v25) — נָתְנוּ מִשְׁכָּב לָהּ, מִשְׁכָּב "bed". **"though their terror was caused" → "for their terror was put"** — כִּי־נִתַּן. **"he is put in the midst of them that be slain" → "in the midst of the slain he is put"** — בְּתוֹךְ חֲלָלִים נִתָּן, Hebrew order, singular.
- **"There is Meshech, Tubal, and all her multitude" → "There is Meshech, Tubal, and all her multitude"** (v26) — מֶשֶׁךְ תֻּבַל, two names side by side (the old text joined them as one, "Meshech-Tubal"). **"slain by the sword" → "pierced by the sword"** — מְחֻלְלֵי חֶרֶב, pual participle of the root of חָלָל, a different form from חַלְלֵי־חֶרֶב "the slain of the sword". **"though they caused their terror" → "for they put their terror"** — כִּי.
- **"And they shall not lie with the mighty that are fallen of the uncircumcised" → "And they do not lie with the mighty ones, the fallen of the uncircumcised"** (v27) — וְלֹא יִשְׁכְּבוּ, a statement (some read it as a question, "Do they not lie …?"); נֹפְלִים מֵעֲרֵלִים "the fallen of the uncircumcised" (the Septuagint has "of old", reading מֵעוֹלָם, from memory). **"which are gone down to hell" → "who went down to Sheol"**. **"and they have laid their swords under their heads" → "and they put their swords under their heads"** — וַיִּתְּנוּ, active, subject not named. **"but their iniquities shall be upon their bones" → "and their iniquities were upon their bones"** — וַתְּהִי עֲוֹנֹתָם, past; many emend to צִנּוֹתָם "their shields" (a conjecture): the MT is kept. **"though they were the terror of the mighty" → "for the terror of the mighty ones was"** — כִּי־חִתִּית גִּבּוֹרִים.
- **"Yea, thou shalt be broken in the midst of the uncircumcised" → "And you, in the midst of the uncircumcised you shall be broken"** (v28) — וְאַתָּה first, Hebrew order; "and you shall lie".
- **"There is Edom" → "There is Edom"** (v29) — שָׁמָּה (literally "thither", as v30). **"her princes" → "her chieftains"** — נְשִׂיאֶיהָ, נָשִׂיא "chieftain". **"which with their might are laid by them that were slain" → "who were put in their might with the slain"** — נִתְּנוּ בִגְבוּרָתָם. **"they shall lie"** — יִשְׁכָּבוּ, imperfect.
- **"There be the princes of the north, all of them, and all the Zidonians" → "There are the princes of the north, all of them, and every Sidonian"** (v30) — נְסִיכֵי צָפוֹן, נָסִיךְ "prince" (שַׂר has the same English); כָּל־צִדֹנִי singular. **"with their terror they are ashamed of their might" → "in their terror, from their might, they are ashamed"** — בְּחִתִּיתָם מִגְבוּרָתָם בּוֹשִׁים, word for word (ashamed, for all the terror that came of their might: the sense is debated). **"and they lie uncircumcised" → "and they lay uncircumcised"** — וַיִּשְׁכְּבוּ, wayyiqtol, past; "bore" as v24.
- **"Pharaoh shall see them, and shall be comforted over all his multitude, even Pharaoh and all his army slain by the sword" → "Them Pharaoh shall see, and he shall be comforted over all his multitude, the slain of the sword, Pharaoh and all his force"** (v31) — אוֹתָם first; qere הֲמוֹנוֹ "his multitude" (ketiv המונה, the same word); נִחַם nifal "be comforted"; חַיִל "force".
- **"For I have caused my terror in the land of the living" → "For I have put my terror in the land of the living"** (v32) — qere חִתִּיתִי "my terror" (ketiv חתיתו "his terror"); the old text had dropped "my". **"and he shall be laid" (no "to rest")** — וְהֻשְׁכַּב, hofal of שָׁכַב "lie".

### Notes
- **v17–32** — the descent: one by one the nations that "put terror in the land of the living" lie in the pit, "uncircumcised" and "slain of the sword" — Assyria (v22–23), Elam (v24–25), Meshech and Tubal (v26–27), Edom (v29), the princes of the north and Sidon (v30) — and Pharaoh is laid among them (v28, 31–32).
