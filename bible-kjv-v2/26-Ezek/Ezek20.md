# Ezekiel 20

[1] And it came to pass in the seventh year, in the fifth month, on the tenth of the month, that men from the elders of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ came to seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they sat before me.

[2] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[3] "Son of man, speak with the elders of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and you shall say to them, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Is it to seek me that you are coming? As I live, I will not be sought by you, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[4] Will you judge them, will you judge, son of man? Make them know the abominations of their fathers,

[5] and you shall say to them, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:

On the day when I chose ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, then I lifted up my hand to the seed of the house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, and I made myself known to them in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and I lifted up my hand to them, saying, I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[6] On that day I lifted up my hand to them, to bring them out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ to a land that I had explored for them, flowing with milk and honey; it is the beauty of all the lands.

[7] And I said to them, Each man, cast away the detestable things of his eyes, and with the idols of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ do not defile yourselves; I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[8] And they rebelled against me and were not willing to listen to me; each man did not cast away the detestable things of their eyes, and the idols of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ they did not forsake. And I said that I would pour out my fury upon them, to finish my anger on them in the midst of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[9] And I acted for the sake of my name, so that it should not be profaned before the eyes of the nations in whose midst they were, before whose eyes I made myself known to them, to bring them out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[10] And I brought them out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and brought them to the wilderness.

[11] And I gave them my statutes, and my ordinances I made known to them, which the man shall do and live by them.

[12] And also my ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ I gave them, to be a sign between me and them, to know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who sanctifies them.

[13] And the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ rebelled against me in the wilderness: in my statutes they did not walk, and my ordinances they rejected, which the man shall do and live by them; and my ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ they profaned greatly.

And I said that I would pour out my fury upon them in the wilderness, to make an end of them.

[14] And I acted for the sake of my name, so that it should not be profaned before the eyes of the nations before whose eyes I had brought them out.

[15] And I also lifted up my hand to them in the wilderness, not to bring them into the land that I had given, flowing with milk and honey; it is the beauty of all the lands,

[16] because they rejected my ordinances, and in my statutes they did not walk, and my ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ they profaned; for after their idols their heart was going.

[17] And my eye pitied them, so as not to ruin them, and I did not make a full end of them in the wilderness.

[18] And I said to their sons in the wilderness, In the statutes of your fathers do not walk, and their ordinances do not keep, and with their idols do not defile yourselves.

[19] I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: in my statutes walk, and my ordinances keep, and do them,

[20] and my ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ sanctify, and they shall be a sign between me and you, to know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[21] And the sons rebelled against me: in my statutes they did not walk, and my ordinances they did not keep to do them, which the man shall do and live by them; my ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ they profaned.

And I said that I would pour out my fury upon them, to finish my anger on them in the wilderness.

[22] And I turned back my hand and acted for the sake of my name, so that it should not be profaned before the eyes of the nations before whose eyes I had brought them out.

[23] I also lifted up my hand to them in the wilderness, to scatter them among the nations and to strew them through the lands,

[24] because my ordinances they had not done, and my statutes they had rejected, and my ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ they had profaned, and after the idols of their fathers were their eyes.

[25] And I also gave them statutes that were not good, and ordinances by which they shall not live;

[26] and I defiled them by their gifts, in making pass over all that opens the womb, in order that I might make them desolate, in order that they might know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[27] Therefore speak to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, son of man, and you shall say to them, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Still in this your fathers reviled me, in their unfaithfulness that they committed against me.

[28] And I brought them to the land that I had lifted up my hand to give it to them; and they saw every high hill and every leafy tree, and they sacrificed there their sacrifices, and they gave there the vexation of their offering, and they put there their soothing smells, and they poured out there their drink offerings.

[29] And I said to them, What is the high place to which you are coming? And its name is called Bamah to this day.

[30] Therefore say to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: In the way of your fathers are you defiling yourselves, and after their detestable things are you playing the prostitute?

[31] And in lifting up your gifts, in making your sons pass over in the fire, you are defiling yourselves with all your idols to this day; and shall I be sought by you, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧? As I live, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, I will not be sought by you.

[32] And what comes up on your spirit shall surely not be — that you are saying, Let us be like the nations, like the clans of the lands, to minister to wood and stone.

[33] As I live, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, surely with a strong hand and with an outstretched arm and with fury poured out I will reign over you.

[34] And I will bring you out from the peoples and gather you from the lands in which you have been scattered, with a strong hand and with an outstretched arm and with fury poured out.

[35] And I will bring you to the wilderness of the peoples, and I will enter into judgment with you there face to face.

[36] As I entered into judgment with your fathers in the wilderness of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, so I will enter into judgment with you, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[37] And I will make you pass over under the rod, and I will bring you into the bond of the covenant.

[38] And I will purge out from you the rebels and those who transgress against me; from the land of their sojournings I will bring them out, but to the ground of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ he shall not come; and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[39] And you, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Each man his idols — go, serve; and afterward, if you are not listening to me — but my holy name you shall no more profane with your gifts and with your idols.

[40] For on my holy mountain, on the mountain of the height of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, there all the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall serve me, all of it, in the land; there I will be pleased with them, and there I will seek your contributions and the first of your lifted gifts, with all your holy things.

[41] With a soothing smell I will be pleased with you, when I bring you out from the peoples and gather you from the lands in which you have been scattered; and I will be sanctified in you before the eyes of the nations.

[42] And you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, when I bring you to the ground of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to the land that I lifted up my hand to give it to your fathers.

[43] And there you shall remember your ways and all your doings by which you have been defiled, and you shall loathe yourselves in your own faces for all your evils that you have done.

[44] And you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, when I deal with you for the sake of my name, not according to your evil ways and according to your ruined doings, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

[45] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[46] "Son of man, set your face the way toward the south, and drip toward the south, and prophesy against the forest of the field, the ⟦negev|Negev|Negeb⟧.

[47] And you shall say to the forest of the ⟦negev|Negev|Negeb⟧, Hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I am kindling a fire in you, and it shall devour in you every fresh tree and every dry tree; the flame of the blaze shall not be quenched, and all faces shall be scorched by it from the south to the north.

[48] And all flesh shall see that I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have kindled it; it shall not be quenched."

[49] And I said, "Ah, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧! They are saying of me, 'Is he not a speaker of parables?'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v3, 5, 27, 30, 31, 33, 36, 39, 40, 44, 47, 49) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words (token `adonai-yhwh`); נְאֻם "declares" (v3, 31, 33, 36, 40, 44), literally "the declaration of". **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5, 7, 19, 20).
- **Numbering** — v45–49 are Heb 21:1–5 (see Notes).
- **"came to enquire of the LORD" → "came to seek Yahweh"** (v1) — וַיְהִי "And it came to pass" (kept); דָּרַשׁ "seek" (the row; also v3, 31 nifal "be sought", v40). "month" is not in the Hebrew after "the fifth": supplied. **"certain of the elders" → "men from the elders"** — אֲנָשִׁים מִזִּקְנֵי.
- **"And the word of the LORD came unto me, saying"** (v2, 45) — the prophets' formula (one wording).
- **"Speak unto the elders of Israel" → "speak with the elders of Israel"** (v3) — דַּבֵּר אֶת "speak with"; וְאָמַרְתָּ "and you shall say" (also v5, 27, 47). **"Are ye come to enquire of me? As I live, saith the Lord GOD, I will not be enquired of by you" → "Is it to seek me that you are coming? As I live, I will not be sought by you, declares Lord Yahweh"** — הֲלִדְרֹשׁ אֹתִי, the infinitive first: "Is it to seek me"; the oath חַי־אָנִי אִם־אִדָּרֵשׁ, literally "As I live, if I shall be sought": "I will not be sought" (the oath row; also v31); נְאֻם stands last, in the Hebrew order.
- **"Wilt thou judge them, son of man, wilt thou judge them?" → "Will you judge them, will you judge, son of man?"** (v4) — the second verb has no object; the Hebrew order. **"cause them to know" → "Make them know"** — הוֹדִיעֵם, imperative.
- **"In the day when I chose Israel, and lifted up mine hand unto the seed of the house of Jacob" → "On the day when I chose Israel, then I lifted up my hand to the seed of the house of Jacob"** (v5) — בָּחַר "choose"; נָשָׂא יָד, "lift up the hand" = swear (the row; also v6, 15, 23, 28, 42): kept, "I swore" of the old verse was the sense only. זֶרַע "seed". **"and made myself known unto them" → "and I made myself known to them"** — וָאִוָּדַע, nifal of יָדַע (also v9 נוֹדַעְתִּי).
- **"to bring them forth of the land of Egypt into a land that I had espied for them" → "to bring them out of the land of Egypt to a land that I had explored for them"** (v6) — הוֹצִיא "bring out"; תּוּר "explore" (as Num 13). **"flowing with milk and honey, which is the glory of all lands" → "flowing with milk and honey; it is the beauty of all the lands"** — זָבַת חָלָב וּדְבַשׁ (the row); צְבִי "beauty" (the row; the word is also "gazelle"); הָאֲרָצוֹת with the article (also v15).
- **"Cast ye away every man the abominations of his eyes, and defile not yourselves with the idols of Egypt" → "Each man, cast away the detestable things of his eyes, and with the idols of Egypt do not defile yourselves"** (v7) — אִישׁ "each man" (the row); שִׁקּוּצִים "detestable things" (not תּוֹעֵבוֹת "abominations", v4; also v8, 30); הִשְׁלִיךְ "cast away"; גִּלּוּלִים "idols" (a word of contempt; throughout); הִטַּמֵּא "defile oneself" (also v18, 30, 31; v43 נִטְמָא "be defiled").
- **"But they rebelled against me, and would not hearken unto me" → "And they rebelled against me and were not willing to listen to me"** (v8) — מָרָה hifil "rebel against" (also v13, 21; מָרַד is another verb, v38: English has one word); אָבָה "be willing"; שָׁמַע אֶל "listen to". **"they did not every man cast away" → "each man did not cast away"** — "of their eyes" after "each man", as printed. **"then I said, I will pour out my fury upon them, to accomplish my anger against them" → "And I said that I would pour out my fury upon them, to finish my anger on them"** — וָאֹמַר לִשְׁפֹּךְ, literally "and I said to pour out"; חֵמָה "fury" (also v13, 21, 33, 34); כִּלָּה with "anger" "finish" (v13 with persons "make an end of"); אַף "anger".
- **"But I wrought for my name's sake, that it should not be polluted before the heathen" → "And I acted for the sake of my name, so that it should not be profaned before the eyes of the nations"** (v9, 14, 22) — עָשָׂה "act"; לְבִלְתִּי הֵחֵל, nifal of חלל, "profane" (the row; also v13, 16, 21, 24, 39: חִלֵּל); לְעֵינֵי "before the eyes of" (the row; also v41). "Polluted" in KJV for the same verb.
- **"I gave them my statutes, and shewed them my judgments, which if a man do, he shall even live in them" → "And I gave them my statutes, and my ordinances I made known to them, which the man shall do and live by them"** (v11, 13, 21) — חֻקּוֹת "statutes", מִשְׁפָּטִים "ordinances" (the rows, as Lev 18:5, which the line takes up word for word; also v13, 16, 18, 19, 21, 24, 25); הָאָדָם "the man" with the article; וָחַי בָּהֶם "and live by them".
- **"Moreover also I gave them my sabbaths, to be a sign between me and them, that they might know that I am the LORD that sanctify them" → "And also my Shabbats I gave them, to be a sign … to know that I am Yahweh who sanctifies them"** (v12) — וְגַם "And also"; שַׁבָּתוֹת (token `shabbat`); לָדַעַת "to know" (also v20); מְקַדְּשָׁם, a participle, "who sanctifies them" (קִדֵּשׁ; v20 "sanctify").
- **"my sabbaths they greatly polluted: then I said, I would pour out my fury upon them in the wilderness, to consume them" → "my Shabbats they profaned greatly. And I said that I would pour out my fury upon them in the wilderness, to make an end of them"** (v13) — מָאַס "reject" (also v16, 24); מְאֹד "greatly"; לְכַלּוֹתָם (the "make an end of" row).
- **"Yet also I lifted up my hand unto them in the wilderness, that I would not bring them into the land which I had given them" → "And I also lifted up my hand to them in the wilderness, not to bring them into the land that I had given"** (v15) — וְגַם־אֲנִי "and also I"; לְבִלְתִּי הָבִיא "not to bring"; נָתַתִּי has no "them".
- **"for their heart went after their idols" → "for after their idols their heart was going"** (v16) — הֹלֵךְ, a participle; the Hebrew order.
- **"Nevertheless mine eye spared them from destroying them, neither did I make an end of them in the wilderness" → "And my eye pitied them, so as not to ruin them, and I did not make a full end of them in the wilderness"** (v17) — חוּס "pity" (the row); מִשַּׁחֲתָם, שָׁחַת "ruin" (the row; also v44); עָשָׂה כָּלָה, literally "make an end": "a full end", to keep it apart from כִּלָּה (v13).
- **"But I said unto their children" → "And I said to their sons"** (v18) — בָּנִים "sons" (also v21). **"Walk ye not in the statutes of your fathers" → "In the statutes of your fathers do not walk"** — חוּקֵּי, the masculine plural of חֹק (beside חֻקּוֹת): the same English, as the row has them.
- **"walk in my statutes, and keep my judgments, and do them" → "in my statutes walk, and my ordinances keep, and do them"** (v19) — שָׁמַר "keep" (also v18, 21: "keep to do them").
- **"And hallow my sabbaths; and they shall be a sign" → "and my Shabbats sanctify, and they shall be a sign"** (v20) — קַדְּשׁוּ "sanctify"; וְהָיוּ, a weqatal.
- **"Notwithstanding the children rebelled against me" → "And the sons rebelled against me"** (v21) — הַבָּנִים. **"neither kept my judgments to do them" → "and my ordinances they did not keep to do them"** — לַעֲשׂוֹת (the "keep to do" row).
- **"Nevertheless I withdrew mine hand" → "And I turned back my hand"** (v22) — הֲשִׁבֹתִי "turned back" (the הֵשִׁיב row).
- **"that I would scatter them among the heathen, and disperse them through the countries" → "to scatter them among the nations and to strew them through the lands"** (v23) — הֵפִיץ "scatter" (the row); זָרָה "strew" (the row; of grain "winnow"); אֲרָצוֹת "lands" (also v32, 34, 41).
- **"Because they had not executed my judgments" → "because my ordinances they had not done"** (v24) — the Hebrew order. **"and their eyes were after their fathers' idols" → "and after the idols of their fathers were their eyes"** — the Hebrew order.
- **"Wherefore I gave them also statutes that were not good, and judgments whereby they should not live" → "And I also gave them statutes that were not good, and ordinances by which they shall not live"** (v25) — וְגַם־אֲנִי נָתַתִּי לָהֶם חֻקִּים לֹא טוֹבִים, word for word: the subject is Yahweh, the verb "gave"; nothing is added to explain it (the old Notes' "gave them up to corrupt worship" is an interpretation, not the verse). חֻקִּים "statutes" (חֹק), מִשְׁפָּטִים "ordinances", both without "my" (set against "my statutes … my ordinances", v11, 13, 19, 21, 24); לֹא יִחְיוּ בָּהֶם, against "live by them" of v11. Readings of the verse are named in the Notes.
- **"And I polluted them in their own gifts, in that they caused to pass through the fire all that openeth the womb" → "and I defiled them by their gifts, in making pass over all that opens the womb"** (v26) — טִמֵּא "defile" (the row; KJV "polluted"); מַתָּנוֹת "gifts" (also v31, 39); בְּהַעֲבִיר "in making pass over" (the Molech row: "through the fire" is not in this verse; v31 has בָּאֵשׁ); פֶּטֶר רֶחַם "all that opens the womb" (the row: the firstling). **"that I might make them desolate, to the end that they might know that I am the LORD" → "in order that I might make them desolate, in order that they might know that I am Yahweh"** — לְמַעַן twice; אֲשִׁמֵּם, hifil of שָׁמֵם ("make desolate", or "appall": one root); לְמַעַן אֲשֶׁר יֵדְעוּ אֲשֶׁר אֲנִי יְהוָה, the formula with אֲשֶׁר "that".
- **"Yet in this your fathers have blasphemed me, in that they have committed a trespass against me" → "Still in this your fathers reviled me, in their unfaithfulness that they committed against me"** (v27) — עוֹד זֹאת "still this"; גִּדֵּף "revile" (the row, which names this verse; not "blaspheme"); בְּמַעֲלָם בִּי מָעַל (the "commit unfaithfulness" row).
- **"For when I had brought them into the land, for the which I lifted up mine hand to give it to them, then they saw every high hill, and all the thick trees" → "And I brought them to the land that I had lifted up my hand to give it to them; and they saw every high hill and every leafy tree"** (v28) — וָאֲבִיאֵם "And I brought them" (no "when … then"); לָתֵת אוֹתָהּ "to give it" (kept; also v42); עֵץ עָבֹת "a leafy tree" (lit. "thick": the Lev 23:40 row; one root with 19:11 עֲבֹתִים). **"there they presented the provocation of their offering: there also they made their sweet savour" → "they gave there the vexation of their offering, and they put there their soothing smells"** — כַּעַס "vexation" (the row); קָרְבָּן "offering" (lit. "what is brought near"); רֵיחַ נִיחוֹחֵיהֶם, literally "the smell of their soothings": "soothing smell" (the row; plural here, singular v41); שִׂים "put". **"and poured out there their drink offerings"** — הִסִּיךְ נֶסֶךְ (the row). "there" (שָׁם) four times, as printed.
- **"What is the high place whereunto ye go? And the name thereof is called Bamah unto this day" → "What is the high place to which you are coming? And its name is called Bamah to this day"** (v29) — מָה הַבָּמָה … הַבָּאִים, a sound play (*mah habbamah* … *habba'im*); בָּמָה "high place", and as a name "Bamah" (plain text: the lexicon has the same form in both modes). The play is named here, not explained in the verse.
- **"Are ye polluted after the manner of your fathers? and commit ye whoredom after their abominations?" → "In the way of your fathers are you defiling yourselves, and after their detestable things are you playing the prostitute?"** (v30) — נִטְמְאִים, a participle; זָנָה "play the prostitute" (the row: of unfaithfulness to Yahweh the same word; not "whore"); שִׁקּוּצִים "detestable things".
- **"For when ye offer your gifts, when ye make your sons to pass through the fire" → "And in lifting up your gifts, in making your sons pass over in the fire"** (v31) — בִּשְׂאֵת, נָשָׂא "lift up"; בָּנִים "sons"; בָּאֵשׁ "in the fire". **"and shall I be enquired of by you" → "and shall I be sought by you"** — וַאֲנִי אִדָּרֵשׁ; no "O" before "house of Israel" (the reviewer's vocative row; also v39, 44).
- **"And that which cometh into your mind shall not be at all, that ye say, We will be as the heathen" → "And what comes up on your spirit shall surely not be — that you are saying, Let us be like the nations"** (v32) — הָעֹלָה עַל־רוּחֲכֶם "what comes up on your spirit" (רוּחַ "spirit"); הָיוֹ לֹא תִהְיֶה, literally "being it shall not be": "shall surely not be"; נִהְיֶה, a cohortative, "Let us be". **"as the families of the countries, to serve wood and stone" → "like the clans of the lands, to minister to wood and stone"** — מִשְׁפָּחוֹת "clans"; שֵׁרֵת "minister to" (the row: not עָבַד "serve", v39, 40).
- **"surely with a mighty hand, and with a stretched out arm, and with fury poured out, will I rule over you" → "surely with a strong hand and with an outstretched arm and with fury poured out I will reign over you"** (v33, 34) — the oath אִם־לֹא "surely" (the row); "with a strong hand and with an outstretched arm" (the reviewer's row; not "mighty"); חֵמָה שְׁפוּכָה "fury poured out"; מָלַךְ "reign".
- **"And I will bring you out from the people, and will gather you out of the countries wherein ye are scattered" → "And I will bring you out from the peoples and gather you from the lands in which you have been scattered"** (v34, 41) — עַמִּים plural; נְפוֹצֹתֶם, nifal of פּוּץ "be scattered".
- **"and there will I plead with you face to face" → "and I will enter into judgment with you there face to face"** (v35, 36) — נִשְׁפַּט אֶת / אִת "enter into judgment with" (as 17:20); פָּנִים אֶל־פָּנִים "face to face". מִדְבַּר הָעַמִּים "the wilderness of the peoples".
- **"And I will cause you to pass under the rod, and I will bring you into the bond of the covenant" → "And I will make you pass over under the rod, and I will bring you into the bond of the covenant"** (v37) — הֶעֱבִיר "make pass over" (the row; as v26, 31); שֵׁבֶט "rod" (of a shepherd counting his flock, Lev 27:32); מָסֹרֶת is found only here: "bond" (from אָסַר "bind"; uncertain; the Greek has "in number", from memory).
- **"And I will purge out from among you the rebels, and them that transgress against me" → "And I will purge out from you the rebels and those who transgress against me"** (v38) — בָּרַר "purge out"; מֹרְדִים (מָרַד "rebel"); פֹּשְׁעִים "those who transgress". **"I will bring them forth out of the country where they sojourn, and they shall not enter into the land of Israel" → "from the land of their sojournings I will bring them out, but to the ground of Israel he shall not come"** — מְגוּרִים "sojournings" (גּוּר "sojourn"); אַדְמַת יִשְׂרָאֵל "the ground of Israel" (also v42); לֹא יָבוֹא singular, as printed. **"and ye shall know that I am the LORD"** — וִידַעְתֶּם כִּי־אֲנִי יְהוָה, the formula (one wording; also v42, 44; v20, 26 its forms).
- **"Go ye, serve ye every one his idols, and hereafter also, if ye will not hearken unto me" → "Each man his idols — go, serve; and afterward, if you are not listening to me —"** (v39) — אִישׁ גִּלּוּלָיו לְכוּ עֲבֹדוּ, the Hebrew order; עָבַד "serve"; וְאַחַר אִם־אֵינְכֶם שֹׁמְעִים אֵלַי, a sentence that breaks off (the dash; uncertain: also read as an oath, "surely you shall listen to me"). **"but pollute ye my holy name no more with your gifts, and with your idols" → "but my holy name you shall no more profane"** — שֵׁם קָדְשִׁי, literally "the name of my holiness"; לֹא תְחַלְּלוּ־עוֹד, a statement ("you shall not"). The written divine name here is יְהֹוִה, the same name.
- **"For in mine holy mountain, in the mountain of the height of Israel" → "For on my holy mountain, on the mountain of the height of Israel"** (v40) — הַר קָדְשִׁי; הַר מְרוֹם יִשְׂרָאֵל (as 17:23). **"there shall all the house of Israel, all of them in the land, serve me" → "there all the house of Israel shall serve me, all of it, in the land"** — כֻּלֹּה "all of it", the Hebrew order. **"there will I accept them, and there will I require your offerings, and the firstfruits of your oblations, with all your holy things" → "there I will be pleased with them, and there I will seek your contributions and the first of your lifted gifts, with all your holy things"** — רָצָה "be pleased with" (the row; not "accept"; also v41); דָּרַשׁ "seek" (as v1, 3; "require" is the sense); תְּרוּמוֹת "contributions" (the row: "what is lifted off"); רֵאשִׁית "first"; מַשְׂאוֹת "lifted gifts" (from נָשָׂא "lift up"); קֳדָשִׁים "holy things".
- **"I will accept you with your sweet savour" → "With a soothing smell I will be pleased with you"** (v41) — בְּרֵיחַ נִיחֹחַ, the Hebrew order (the row). **"and I will be sanctified in you before the heathen" → "and I will be sanctified in you before the eyes of the nations"** — נִקְדַּשְׁתִּי, nifal ("show myself holy" is the sense: the row); לְעֵינֵי.
- **"for the which I lifted up mine hand to give it to your fathers" → "to the land that I lifted up my hand to give it to your fathers"** (v42) — אֶל־הָאָרֶץ "to the land", beside אֶל־אַדְמַת "to the ground".
- **"and all your doings, wherein ye have been defiled; and ye shall lothe yourselves in your own sight for all your evils" → "and all your doings by which you have been defiled, and you shall loathe yourselves in your own faces for all your evils"** (v43) — עֲלִילוֹת "doings" (also v44); נִטְמֵאתֶם "you have been defiled"; וּנְקֹטֹתֶם בִּפְנֵיכֶם, נָקֹט "loathe", בִּפְנֵיכֶם "in your faces" (kept; "in your own sight" is the sense); רָעוֹת "evils".
- **"when I have wrought with you for my name's sake, not according to your wicked ways, nor according to your corrupt doings" → "when I deal with you for the sake of my name, not according to your evil ways and according to your ruined doings"** (v44) — עָשָׂה אֵת "deal with"; הָרָעִים "evil" (the רַע row); הַנִּשְׁחָתוֹת, nifal of שָׁחַת "be ruined" (the row: KJV "corrupt"; one word for "corrupt" and "destroy"); וְכַ "and according to" (no "nor").
- **"Son of man, set thy face toward the south, and drop thy word toward the south, and prophesy against the forest of the south field" → "Son of man, set your face the way toward the south, and drip toward the south, and prophesy against the forest of the field, the Negev"** (v46) — three words for the south stand side by side: דֶּרֶךְ תֵּימָנָה "the way toward the south" (תֵּימָן, lit. "the right hand"; as a name Teman), אֶל־דָּרוֹם "toward the south" (דָּרוֹם, a word found in Ezekiel, Deuteronomy, Job and Ecclesiastes), נֶגֶב (the region's name, token `negev`; in v47b the quarter of the sky, "south": the Directions row); English has one word for the first two. הַטֵּף "drip", without an object (of speech, as Amos 7:16; Mic 2:6, 11; "thy word" of KJV and "preach" of the old verse are supplied); יַעַר הַשָּׂדֶה נֶגֶב, literally "the forest of the field, Negev": the article before Negev supplied.
- **"And say to the forest of the south" → "And you shall say to the forest of the Negev"** (v47) — יַעַר הַנֶּגֶב. **"Behold, I will kindle a fire in thee" → "Behold, I am kindling a fire in you"** — הִנְנִי מַצִּית, a participle. **"every green tree in thee, and every dry tree" → "every fresh tree and every dry tree"** — עֵץ לַח … עֵץ יָבֵשׁ, the same pair as 17:24. **"the flaming flame shall not be quenched" → "the flame of the blaze shall not be quenched"** — לַהֶבֶת שַׁלְהֶבֶת, two nouns of fire. **"all faces from the south to the north shall be burned therein" → "all faces shall be scorched by it from the south to the north"** — צָרַב nifal "be scorched"; the Hebrew order.
- **"And all flesh shall see that I the LORD have kindled it" → "And all flesh shall see that I, Yahweh, have kindled it"** (v48) — בָּשָׂר "flesh"; בִּעַרְתִּיהָ "kindled it"; as 17:21, 24 the verb follows the name.
- **"Then said I, Ah Lord GOD! they say of me, Doth he not speak parables?" → "And I said, Ah, Lord Yahweh! They are saying of me, Is he not a speaker of parables?"** (v49) — וָאֹמַר "And I said"; אֲהָהּ "Ah"; הֵמָּה אֹמְרִים לִי, a participle, לִי "to / of me"; מְמַשֵּׁל מְשָׁלִים, a participle with its noun, "one who speaks parables" (the מָשָׁל row; as 17:2).

### Notes
- **Numbering** — our 20:45–49 are Heb 21:1–5 (the Hebrew Bible begins chapter 21 there); our 21:1–32 are Heb 21:6–37.
- **v1** — the seventh year (of the exile of Jehoiachin, as 1:2; 8:1), the fifth month, the tenth day.
- **v25–26** — the verse says that Yahweh gave statutes "not good" and defiled Israel by their gifts when they made every firstborn pass over. Readers have taken it as Yahweh handing them over to their own wrong statutes (v18, 24; cf. Ps 81:12), as the laws of the firstborn (Exod 13:2, 12; 22:29) turned by Israel into child sacrifice (v31), or as words spoken in irony; the chapter does not decide, and the verse is left as it stands.
- **v32–38** — Yahweh brings Israel out "with a strong hand and with an outstretched arm" (the words of the exodus, Deut 4:34; 5:15), but "with fury poured out", into "the wilderness of the peoples", as once into the wilderness of Egypt (v35–36).
