## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*; Ezekiel's distinctive doubled divine title.
- **Layout** — v2b–8, 10–19 one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, as before; v9, 20–26 prose. The quotation marks that opened each "Thus says" (v2, 6, 10, 13) are dropped: the Hebrew runs on as one speech.
- **"And the word of the LORD came again unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v1) — וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר: "And" and "saying" kept; no "again" in the Hebrew. **v20** — "And it came to pass … in the first, on the seventh of the month": וַיְהִי kept; "month" and "day" not written (as 29:1).
- **"Howl ye, Woe worth the day!" → "Wail, 'Alas for the day!'"** (v2) — הֵילִילוּ "wail"; הָהּ "alas" (only here).
- **"For the day is near, even the day of the LORD is near, a cloudy day; it shall be the time of the heathen" → "For near is a day, and near is a day for Yahweh; a day of cloud, a time of nations it shall be"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּי־קָרוֹב יוֹם וְקָרוֹב יוֹם לַיהוָה: no article, "for Yahweh" (לְ, not the construct "of"); עָנָן "cloud", singular; עֵת גּוֹיִם "a time of nations" (the old text added "doom").
- **"And the sword shall come upon Egypt, and great pain shall be in Ethiopia" → "And a sword shall come into Egypt, and there shall be anguish in Cush"** (v4) — וּבָאָה חֶרֶב בְּמִצְרַיִם; חַלְחָלָה "anguish" (writhing, as v9; Isa 21:3); כּוּשׁ "Cush". **"when the slain shall fall"** — חָלָל, a collective singular. **"they shall take away her multitude" → "they take her multitude"** — וְלָקְחוּ הֲמוֹנָהּ, plural active; הָמוֹן "multitude" (her crowd or her abundance; "wealth" in many versions). **"broken down" → "thrown down"** — נֶהֶרְסוּ, הָרַס.
- **"Ethiopia, and Libya, and Lydia, and all the mingled people, and Chub, and the men of the land that is in league" → "Cush, and Put, and Lud, and all the mixed people, and Libya, and the sons of the land of the covenant — with them they shall fall"** (v5) — Hebrew כּוּשׁ וּפוּט וְלוּד: the names as written (KJV "Libya" and "Lydia" for Put and Lud). **"all Arabia" → "all the mixed people"** — הָעֶרֶב, pointed "the mixture" (as Jer 25:20, 24; "people" supplied); read as עֲרָב "Arabia" it would be another word: the MT pointing is kept. **"Chub" → "Kuv"** — the MT has כּוּב *Kuv*, a people or land found only here, plain as printed (reviewer 2026-10-16); "Libya" (לוּב, one letter changed, as the Septuagint, from memory) is not in the verse. **"the men of the land that is in league" → "the sons of the land of the covenant"** — בְּנֵי אֶרֶץ הַבְּרִית, word for word: an allied land, or (as many hold) the men of Judah in Egypt: uncertain. **"with them"** — אִתָּם first, Hebrew order.
- **"They also that uphold Egypt shall fall; and the pride of her power shall come down" → "And those who support Egypt shall fall, and the pride of her strength shall come down"** (v6) — Hebrew וְנָפְלוּ סֹמְכֵי מִצְרַיִם; גְּאוֹן עֻזָּהּ, גָּאוֹן "pride", עֹז "strength" (also v18). **"from the tower of Syene" → "from Migdol to Syene"** — מִמִּגְדֹּל סְוֵנֵה, as 29:10. **"fall in it" → "fall in her"** — יִפְּלוּ־בָהּ. **"saith" → "declares"** — נְאֻם.
- **"in the midst of the countries that are desolate … cities that are wasted" → "desolated lands … ruined cities"** (v7) — as 29:12; עָרָיו "his cities", masculine (Egypt is spoken of as "he" and "she" in one passage).
- **"Then they shall know" → "And they shall know"** (v8, 19, 25, 26) — וְיָדְעוּ. **"when I have set a fire in Egypt" → "when I put fire in Egypt"** — בְּתִתִּי־אֵשׁ, נָתַן "put" (also v14, 16). **"destroyed" → "broken"** — נִשְׁבְּרוּ, שָׁבַר.
- **"In that day shall messengers go forth from me" → "In that day messengers shall go out from before me"** (v9) — בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא; מִלְּפָנַי "from before me"; צִים "ships" (a rare word, Num 24:24; Isa 33:21). **"to make the careless Ethiopians afraid" → "to make Cush tremble in its security"** — לְהַחֲרִיד "make tremble"; בֶּטַח "in security" (a noun used as adverb). **"as in the day of Egypt" → "in the day of Egypt"** — בְּיוֹם מִצְרַיִם (no "doom"). **"lo, it cometh" → "behold, it comes"** — הִנֵּה.
- **"I will also make the multitude of Egypt to cease" → "And I will make the multitude of Egypt cease"** (v10) — וְהִשְׁבַּתִּי, hifil of שָׁבַת "cease" (also v13; nifal v18); הָמוֹן "multitude". **"Nebuchadrezzar"** — נְבוּכַדְרֶאצַּר, the spelling with r.
- **"the terrible of the nations" → "the ruthless of the nations"** (v11) — עָרִיצֵי גוֹיִם. **"shall be brought" → "are brought in"** — מוּבָאִים, participle. **"to destroy the land" → "to ruin the land"** — לְשַׁחֵת. **"they shall draw their swords"** — הֵרִיקוּ, literally "empty out" (the sheath). **"fill the land with the slain"** — חָלָל.
- **"I will make the rivers dry" → "I will make the Niles dry ground"** (v12) — וְנָתַתִּי יְאֹרִים חָרָבָה, literally "give the Niles (as) dry ground"; plural of יְאֹר. **"the wicked" → "evil men"** — רָעִים. **"I will make the land waste, and all that is therein" → "I will make desolate the land and its fullness"** — וַהֲשִׁמֹּתִי אֶרֶץ וּמְלֹאָהּ. **"strangers"** — זָרִים.
- **"I will also destroy the idols, and I will cause their images to cease out of Noph" → "And I will make the idols perish, and I will make the worthless idols cease from Memphis"** (v13) — Hebrew וְהַאֲבַדְתִּי גִלּוּלִים וְהִשְׁבַּתִּי אֱלִילִים מִנֹּף: גִּלּוּלִים "idols", אֱלִילִים "worthless idols" (two words); נֹף *Nof* (token "Memphis"). **"there shall be no more a prince of the land of Egypt" → "a chieftain from the land of Egypt there shall be no more"** — נָשִׂיא "chieftain" (lit. "one lifted up"), Hebrew order. **"and I will put a fear" → "and I will put fear"** — וְנָתַתִּי יִרְאָה (the old text had "so").
- **"I will make Pathros desolate, and will set fire in Zoan, and will execute judgments in No" → each verb with "and I will"** (v14) — וַהֲשִׁמֹּתִי … וְנָתַתִּי … וְעָשִׂיתִי; שְׁפָטִים "judgments" (acts of judgment; also v19); נֹא *No* (token "Thebes").
- **"my fury upon Sin, the strength of Egypt" → "my fury on Sin, the strong place of Egypt"** (v15) — חֵמָה "fury"; סִין *Sin* (token "Pelusium"); מָעוֹז "strong place".
- **"Sin shall have great pain" → "Sin shall surely writhe"** (v16) — qere תָּחוּל (ketiv תחיל, the same verb): חוּל תָּחוּל, literally "writhing it shall writhe". **"No shall be rent asunder" → "No shall be split open"** — תִּהְיֶה לְהִבָּקֵעַ, literally "shall be for being split open". **"and Noph shall have distresses daily" → "and Memphis — adversaries by day"** — וְנֹף צָרֵי יוֹמָם, a clause without a verb (literally "and Memphis, adversaries of daytime"): צַר "adversary"; יוֹמָם "by day" (or "every day"); uncertain.
- **"The young men of Aven" → "The young men of Aven"** (v17) — אָוֶן *Aven*, pointed as the word "wickedness": a play on On (Heliopolis, Gen 41:45). **"and these cities shall go into captivity" → "and they shall go into captivity"** — וְהֵנָּה, feminine plural "they": the cities, or their women; nothing supplied. בַּחוּרֵי "young men".
- **"At Tehaphnehes also the day shall be darkened" → "And at Tehaphnehes the day shall hold back"** (v18) — MT חָשַׂךְ (with ש) "hold back, withhold"; many Hebrew manuscripts and the versions read חָשַׁךְ "grow dark" (from memory): the MT is kept, its sense uncertain. **"the yokes" → "the yoke-bars"** — מֹטוֹת. **"the pomp of her strength shall cease in her" → "the pride of her strength shall cease in her"** — גְּאוֹן עֻזָּהּ as v6; נִשְׁבַּת. **"as for her, a cloud shall cover her"** — הִיא עָנָן יְכַסֶּנָּה. **"captivity"** — שְׁבִי.
- **"I have broken the arm of Pharaoh" → "the arm of Pharaoh … I have broken"** (v21) — Hebrew order, the object first. **"to be healed, to put a roller to bind it, to make it strong to hold the sword" → "to give remedies, to put on a bandage to bind it, to make it strong to seize the sword"** — לָתֵת רְפֻאוֹת "to give remedies" (as Jer 46:11); חִתּוּל "bandage" (only here); לִתְפֹּשׂ "seize".
- **"I am against Pharaoh … and will break his arms, the strong, and that which was broken" → "and I will break his arms, the strong one and the broken one"** (v22) — הַחֲזָקָה וְאֶת־הַנִּשְׁבָּרֶת.
- **"the Egyptians … disperse them through the countries" → "the Egyptians … strew them through the lands"** (v23, 26) — מִצְרַיִם with the plural "them"; זֵרָה "strew" (as 29:12).
- **"I will strengthen … but I will break … and he shall groan before him with the groanings of a deadly wounded man" → "I will make strong … and I will break … and he shall groan before him with the groanings of a slain man"** (v24) — וְחִזַּקְתִּי, piel; וְ "and" (not "but", also v25); וְנָאַק נַאֲקוֹת חָלָל, verb and noun of one root; חָלָל "slain" (one pierced).
- **"But I will strengthen … and the arms of Pharaoh shall fall down" → "And I will strengthen … shall fall"** (v25) — וְהַחֲזַקְתִּי, hifil (beside the piel of v24: two stems, two English words); תִּפֹּלְנָה. **"stretch it out upon the land of Egypt" → "against the land of Egypt"** — נָטָה … אֶל.

### Notes
- **v14–18** — the towns: Pathros (Upper Egypt), Zoan (Tanis), No (Thebes), Sin (Pelusium), Memphis, Aven (On, Heliopolis), Pi-beseth (Bubastis), Tehaphnehes (Tahpanhes, Jer 43:7–9).
