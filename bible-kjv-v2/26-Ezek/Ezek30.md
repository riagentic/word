## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*; Ezekiel's distinctive doubled divine title.
- **"Put" / "Lud" retained** (v5) — Hebrew פּוּט *Put* and לוּד *Lud*; African/Lydian peoples in Pharaoh's armies; proper names retained.
- **"Noph" retained with gloss** (v13) — Hebrew נֹף *Noph* = Memphis, the ancient Egyptian capital; "*Noph* — Memphis"; the gloss explains the modern identification.
- **"No" retained with gloss** (v14) — Hebrew נֹא *No* = Thebes, the great Egyptian city of Amon; "*No* — Thebes."
- **"Sin" retained with gloss** (v15) — Hebrew סִין *Sin* = Pelusium, the eastern gateway fortress of Egypt; "*Sin* — Pelusium."
- **"Tehaphnehes" retained** (v18) — Hebrew תַּחְפַּנְחֵס *Tahpanches/Tehaphnehes*; a city in the Nile Delta, also called Tahpanhes; retained as proper name.
- **"Pathros" retained** (v14) — Hebrew פַּתְרוֹס *Pathros* = Upper Egypt.
