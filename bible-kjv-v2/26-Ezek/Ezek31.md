# Ezekiel 31

[1] And it came to pass in the eleventh year, in the third, on the first of the month, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying:

[2] "Son of man, say to ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and to his multitude:

Whom are you like in your greatness?

[3] Behold, ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ was a cedar in ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧,
beautiful of bough, and a shading thicket,
and high of stature;
and among the thick boughs was its crown.

[4] Waters made it great;
the deep raised it high,
with its rivers going round
the place of its planting;
and its conduits it sent out
to all the trees of the field.

[5] Therefore its stature was high
above all the trees of the field;
and its offshoots multiplied,
and its limbs grew long,
from many waters, as it sent them out.

[6] In its offshoots
all the birds of the heavens nested,
and under its limbs all the living things of the field gave birth,
and in its shade dwelt all the many nations.

[7] And it was beautiful in its greatness,
in the length of its branches;
for its root was by many waters.

[8] Cedars did not eclipse it in the garden of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
cypresses were not like its offshoots,
and plane trees were not as its limbs;
no tree in the garden of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was like it in its beauty.

[9] Beautiful I made it
in the multitude of its branches;
and all the trees of Eden envied it,
that were in the garden of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[10] Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Because you were high in stature, and it set its crown among the thick boughs, and its heart was lifted up in its height,

[11] I will give it into the hand of the mighty one of the nations; he shall surely deal with it; according to its wickedness I have driven it out.

[12] And strangers, the ruthless of the nations, cut it down and abandoned it; on the mountains and in all the valleys its branches fell, and its limbs were broken in all the channels of the land; and all the peoples of the earth went down from its shade and abandoned it.

[13] On its fallen mass all the birds of the heavens abide, and on its limbs are all the living things of the field;

[14] so that none of the trees by the waters may be high in their stature, nor set their crown among the thick boughs, and that their mighty ones may not stand up in their height, all that drink water; for they are all given over to death, to the land below, in the midst of the sons of man, to those who go down to the pit.

[15] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: In the day it went down to Sheol I caused mourning; I covered the deep over it, and I held back its rivers, and many waters were kept back; and I made ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ go dark over it, and all the trees of the field fainted over it.

[16] At the sound of its fall I made the nations quake, when I brought it down to Sheol with those who go down to the pit; and all the trees of Eden, the choice and best of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧, all that drink water, were comforted in the land below.

[17] They also went down with it to Sheol, to the slain of the sword; and its arm — they dwelt in its shade in the midst of the nations.

[18] Whom are you thus like in glory and in greatness among the trees of Eden? And you shall be brought down with the trees of Eden to the land below; in the midst of the uncircumcised you shall lie, with the slain of the sword. This is ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ and all his multitude, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*; Ezekiel's distinctive doubled divine title.
- **Layout** — v2b–9 one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, by the accents (v6 and v9 now four lines: the old layout ran over the main pause); v10–18 prose, as before. The quotation marks that opened v2b, 10, 15, 18 are dropped: the Hebrew runs on as one speech.
- **"And it came to pass … in the third month, in the first day of the month" → "And it came to pass … in the third, on the first of the month"** (v1) — וַיְהִי kept; "month" and "day" not written (as 29:1). **"came unto me, saying"** — לֵאמֹר kept.
- **"Behold, the Assyrian was a cedar in Lebanon" → "Behold, Assyria was a cedar in Lebanon"** (v3) — Hebrew הִנֵּה אַשּׁוּר אֶרֶז בַּלְּבָנוֹן, the name of the land (no "the Assyrian"); "was" supplied (no verb). The MT is kept. Many read תְּאַשּׁוּר "a cypress" (a tree named in Isa 41:19; 60:13), so that the whole chapter would speak of Egypt as a tree, "Behold, a cypress, a cedar in Lebanon": a conjecture, from memory. **"with fair branches, and with a shadowing shroud" → "beautiful of bough, and a shading thicket"** — יְפֵה עָנָף, עָנָף "bough" (as 17:8, 23); חֹרֶשׁ מֵצַל "a thicket that gives shade" (חֹרֶשׁ "wooded height, thicket", 2 Chr 27:4). **"of an high stature" → "high of stature"** — גְּבַהּ קוֹמָה. **"his top was among the thick boughs" → "among the thick boughs was its crown"** — וּבֵין עֲבֹתִים הָיְתָה צַמַּרְתּוֹ: MT עֲבֹתִים "thick (interwoven) boughs" (as 19:11; also v10, 14); the old text had "clouds", reading עָבוֹת (the Septuagint, from memory). צַמֶּרֶת "crown" (as 17:3, 22).
- **"The waters made him great, the deep set him up on high with her rivers running round about his plants, and sent out her little rivers" → "Waters made it great; the deep raised it high, with its rivers going round the place of its planting; and its conduits it sent out"** (v4) — Hebrew גִּדְּלוּהוּ, רֹמְמָתְהוּ (piel "make great", polel "raise high"); הֹלֵךְ "going"; מַטָּעָהּ "the place of its planting"; תְּעָלֹת "conduits" (water channels; אָפִיק "channel" is another word, v12). The suffixes "its" in v4 are feminine and go with the deep (תְּהוֹם).
- **"Therefore his height was exalted above all the trees" → "Therefore its stature was high above all the trees"** (v5) — גָּבְהָא קֹמָתוֹ (an unusual spelling with א). **"his boughs were multiplied, and his branches became long" → "its offshoots multiplied, and its limbs grew long"** — סַרְעַפֹּתָיו (only here; taken as another form of סְעַפֹּת, v6, 8) "offshoots"; qere פֹארֹתָיו "its limbs" (ketiv פארתו, the same word); five words for the parts of the tree are kept apart through the chapter: עָנָף "bough", סְעַפָּה "offshoot", פֹּארָה "limb", דָּלִיָּה "branch", צַמֶּרֶת "crown". **"because of the multitude of waters, when he shot forth" → "from many waters, as it sent them out"** — מִמַּיִם רַבִּים בְּשַׁלְּחוֹ; "them" supplied (the verb has no object: the tree sending out its limbs, or the deep its waters).
- **"made their nests" → "nested"** (v6) — קִנְנוּ. **"brought forth their young" → "gave birth"** — יָלְדוּ, no object. **"the beasts of the field" → "the living things of the field"** — חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה (also v13). **"under his shadow dwelt all great nations" → "in its shade dwelt all the many nations"** — בְּצִלּוֹ; כֹּל גּוֹיִם רַבִּים, רַב "many" as v5, 7 ("the" supplied for English).
- **"Thus was he fair in his greatness" → "And it was beautiful in its greatness"** (v7) — וַיְּיִף. **"his root was by great waters"** — שָׁרְשׁוֹ singular; אֶל "by, toward".
- **"The cedars in the garden of God could not hide him" → "Cedars did not eclipse it in the garden of Elohim"** (v8) — עֲמָמֻהוּ, from עָמַם "be dim" (Lam 4:1): "did not dim it" = did not outshine it (uncertain; "hide", "rival" in the versions). **"the fir trees" → "cypresses"**; **"the chesnut trees" → "plane trees"** — בְּרוֹשִׁים, עַרְמֹנִים (as Gen 30:37; the trees are not certainly known). **"nor any tree … was like unto him in his beauty"** — לֹא־דָמָה אֵלָיו בְּיָפְיוֹ.
- **"I have made him fair by the multitude of his branches: so that all the trees of Eden … envied him" → "Beautiful I made it in the multitude of its branches; and all the trees of Eden envied it"** (v9) — יָפֶה עֲשִׂיתִיו first; וַיְקַנְאֻהוּ "and they envied" (קִנֵּא of a rival). **"the garden of God"** — בְּגַן הָאֱלֹהִים, with the article (v8 without it): "Elohim" as everywhere, no "the" (the reviewer's row).
- **"Because thou hast lifted up thyself in height, and he hath shot up his top among the thick boughs, and his heart is lifted up in his height" → "Because you were high in stature, and it set its crown among the thick boughs, and its heart was lifted up in its height"** (v10) — Hebrew גָּבַהְתָּ "you were high" (second person), then וַיִּתֵּן "and it set", third person: the change of person is in the Hebrew and is kept (the versions level it, from memory). רָם לְבָבוֹ "its heart was lifted up".
- **"I have therefore delivered him into the hand of the mighty one of the heathen; he shall surely deal with him: I have driven him out for his wickedness" → "I will give it into the hand of the mighty one of the nations; he shall surely deal with it; according to its wickedness I have driven it out"** (v11) — וְאֶתְּנֵהוּ, pointed as a simple וְ with the imperfect ("and I will give"); אֵיל גּוֹיִם, אַיִל "ram", of a leader "mighty one" (as Exod 15:15; also v14); עָשׂוֹ יַעֲשֶׂה לוֹ, literally "doing he shall do to it"; כְּרִשְׁעוֹ "according to its wickedness"; גֵּרַשְׁתִּהוּ "I have driven it out" (גֵּרֵשׁ).
- **"And strangers, the terrible of the nations, have cut him off, and have left him" → "And strangers, the ruthless of the nations, cut it down and abandoned it"** (v12; also end of the verse) — וַיִּכְרְתֻהוּ, וַיִּטְּשֻׁהוּ (נָטַשׁ "abandon"); עָרִיצֵי גוֹיִם. **"by all the rivers of the land" → "in all the channels of the land"** — אֲפִיקֵי הָאָרֶץ. **"are gone down from his shadow" → "went down from its shade"** — וַיֵּרְדוּ.
- **"Upon his ruin shall all the fowls of the heaven remain" → "On its fallen mass all the birds of the heavens abide"** (v13) — מַפַּלְתּוֹ "its fallen thing" (the fallen tree; v16 "its fall"); יִשְׁכְּנוּ, שָׁכַן "abide".
- **"To the end that none of all the trees by the waters exalt themselves for their height … neither their trees stand up in their height, all that drink water" → "so that none of the trees by the waters may be high in their stature … and that their mighty ones may not stand up in their height, all that drink water"** (v14) — לְמַעַן אֲשֶׁר "so that"; וְלֹא־יַעַמְדוּ אֵלֵיהֶם: pointed אֵלֵיהֶם, "their mighty ones" (plural of the אַיִל of v11; KJV "their trees"); read as אֲלֵיהֶם it would be "stand up to them": uncertain. **"to the nether parts of the earth, in the midst of the children of men, with them that go down to the pit" → "to the land below, in the midst of the sons of man, to those who go down to the pit"** — אֶרֶץ תַּחְתִּית "the land below" (also v16, 18); בְּנֵי אָדָם "sons of man"; יוֹרְדֵי בוֹר, בּוֹר "pit".
- **"In the day when he went down to the grave I caused a mourning: I covered the deep for him" → "In the day it went down to Sheol I caused mourning; I covered the deep over it"** (v15) — שְׁאוֹלָה "to Sheol" (KJV "the grave"; also v16, 17 KJV "hell"); הֶאֱבַלְתִּי "I caused mourning", then כִּסֵּתִי without "and" (the verbs may also be read "I caused mourning, I covered …" as one act: the deep covered in mourning). **"I restrained the floods thereof, and the great waters were stayed" → "I held back its rivers, and many waters were kept back"** — וָאֶמְנַע (מָנַע "hold back"), וַיִּכָּלְאוּ (כָּלָא "keep back"); מַיִם רַבִּים "many waters". **"I caused Lebanon to mourn for him" → "I made Lebanon go dark over it"** — וָאַקְדִּר, from קָדַר "be dark" (of mourners in dark clothing). **"fainted for him" → "fainted over it"** — עָלָיו עֻלְפֶּה.
- **"I made the nations to shake at the sound of his fall, when I cast him down to hell" → "At the sound of its fall I made the nations quake, when I brought it down to Sheol"** (v16) — הִרְעַשְׁתִּי, הוֹרִדִי (hifil of יָרַד "go down"). **"shall be comforted in the nether parts of the earth" → "were comforted in the land below"** — וַיִּנָּחֲמוּ, a wayyiqtol (past); נִחַם nifal "be comforted".
- **"unto them that be slain with the sword; and they that were his arm, that dwelt under his shadow" → "to the slain of the sword; and its arm — they dwelt in its shade"** (v17) — חַלְלֵי־חֶרֶב "the slain of the sword" (also v18); וּזְרֹעוֹ יָשְׁבוּ, literally "and its arm, they dwelt": "arm" = its helpers (singular noun, plural verb); nothing supplied.
- **"yet shalt thou be brought down" → "And you shall be brought down"** (v18) — וְהוּרַדְתָּ. **"thou shalt lie in the midst of the uncircumcised" → "in the midst of the uncircumcised you shall lie"** — בְּתוֹךְ עֲרֵלִים first; עָרֵל "uncircumcised". **"This is Pharaoh"** — הוּא פַרְעֹה, literally "he is Pharaoh". **"saith" → "declares"** — נְאֻם.

### Notes
- **v2–18** — the cedar is a picture held up to Pharaoh: "Whom are you like?" (v2, 18). In the MT the tree is Assyria (v3), whose fall Pharaoh is to see in his own.
- **v8, 9, 16, 18** — "the garden of Elohim" and "the trees of Eden" as Ezek 28:13; Gen 2:8.
