# Ezekiel 13

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[2] "Son of man, prophesy against the prophets of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ who prophesy, and say to the prophets out of their own heart, Hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Woe to the foolish prophets, who walk after their own spirit, and have not seen!

[4] Like foxes in the ruins your prophets have been, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[5] You have not gone up into the breaches, nor built a fence for the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to stand in the battle in the day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] They have beheld emptiness and a divination of a lie, who say, 'Declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,' and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has not sent them; and they have hoped to make the word stand.

[7] Have you not beheld a vision of emptiness, and spoken a divination of a lie, and you say, 'Declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,' and I have not spoken?

[8] Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Because you have spoken emptiness and beheld a lie, therefore behold, I am against you, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[9] And my hand shall be against the prophets who behold emptiness and who divine a lie. In the secret counsel of my people they shall not be, and in the writing of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ they shall not be written, and into the ground of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ they shall not come; and you shall know that I am ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[10] Because, and because they have led my people astray, saying, '⟦shalom|Shalom|Peace⟧,' and there is no ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧; and he builds a thin wall, and behold, they are plastering it with whitewash.

[11] Say to those who plaster with whitewash that it shall fall. An overflowing rain has come, and you, stones of hail, shall fall, and a wind of storms shall split it.

[12] And behold, the wall has fallen. Shall it not be said to you, 'Where is the plastering that you plastered?'

[13] Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: And I will make a wind of storms burst out in my fury, and an overflowing rain shall be in my anger, and stones of hail in fury, for a full end.

[14] And I will throw down the wall that you have plastered with whitewash, and bring it down to the ground, and its foundation shall be uncovered; and it shall fall, and you shall be finished in the midst of it; and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[15] And I will spend my fury on the wall and on those who plaster it with whitewash; and I will say to you, The wall is not, and those who plastered it are not:

[16] the prophets of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ who prophesy concerning ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and who behold for her a vision of ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, and there is no ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[17] "And you, son of man, set your face against the daughters of your people who prophesy out of their own heart, and prophesy against them,

[18] and say, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Woe to the women who sew bands on all the joints of my hands, and make veils for the head of every stature, to hunt souls! The souls of my people you hunt, and souls for yourselves you keep alive.

[19] And you have profaned me among my people for handfuls of barley and for bits of bread, to put to death souls that should not die, and to keep alive souls that should not live, by your lying to my people, who hear a lie.

[20] "Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I am against your bands, with which you hunt there the souls as flying things, and I will tear them from upon your arms, and I will let go the souls, the souls that you hunt as flying things.

[21] And I will tear your veils, and deliver my people from your hand, and they shall be no more in your hand as prey; and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[22] Because you have disheartened the heart of the righteous with falsehood, when I had not pained him, and have strengthened the hands of the wicked, so that he does not turn back from his evil way, to keep him alive,

[23] therefore you shall behold emptiness no more, and divination you shall not divine any more; and I will deliver my people from your hand; and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v3, 8, 9, 13, 16, 18, 20) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both names.
- **"And the word of the LORD came unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר, the prophets' formula; "And" and "saying" kept.
- **"say thou unto them that prophesy out of their own hearts" → "say to the prophets out of their own heart"** (v2) — Hebrew לִנְבִיאֵי מִלִּבָּם, literally "to the prophets of from their heart"; לֵב singular. אֶל "against" (also "to").
- **"that follow their own spirit, and have seen nothing" → "who walk after their own spirit, and have not seen"** (v3) — Hebrew הֹלְכִים אַחַר רוּחָם "walking after their spirit"; וּלְבִלְתִּי רָאוּ, literally "and to not-they-saw": they speak without having seen; "nothing" is not in the Hebrew. הוֹי עַל "Woe upon / to".
- **"O Israel, thy prophets are like the foxes in the deserts" → "Like foxes in the ruins your prophets have been, Israel"** (v4) — Hebrew כְּשֻׁעָלִים בָּחֳרָבוֹת נְבִיאֶיךָ יִשְׂרָאֵל הָיוּ, the Hebrew order; שׁוּעָל "fox" (as Judg 15:4; jackals are תַּנִּים); חָרְבָּה "ruin"; הָיוּ perfect "have been"; no "O".
- **"neither made up the hedge for the house of Israel to stand" → "nor built a fence for the house of Israel, to stand"** (v5) — Hebrew וַתִּגְדְּרוּ גָדֵר "and wall a wall", verb and noun of one root (גָּדֵר "fence", as Num 22:24); the negative runs on into the second clause. **"in the battle"** — בַּמִּלְחָמָה, the battle of the day.
- **"They have seen vanity and lying divination" → "They have beheld emptiness and a divination of a lie"** (v6, 7, 8, 9, 23) — Hebrew חָזָה "behold" (the verb of חָזוֹן "vision"; רָאָה is "see"); שָׁוְא "emptiness"; קֶסֶם כָּזָב "divination of a lie" (כָּזָב "lie", beside שֶׁקֶר "falsehood", v22). **"saying, The LORD saith" → "who say, Declares Yahweh"** — הָאֹמְרִים נְאֻם־יְהוָה, the formula they misuse (also v7). **"and they have made others to hope that they would confirm the word" → "and they have hoped to make the word stand"** — וְיִחֲלוּ לְקַיֵּם דָּבָר: יִחֵל "hope"; קִיֵּם "make stand" (= confirm); who is to make it stand is not said: "others" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Have ye not seen a vain vision" → "Have you not beheld a vision of emptiness"** (v7) — Hebrew מַחֲזֵה־שָׁוְא; מִקְסַם כָּזָב "a divination of a lie". **"whereas ye say … albeit I have not spoken" → "and you say … and I have not spoken"** — וְאֹמְרִים … וַאֲנִי לֹא דִבַּרְתִּי, two clauses with "and".
- **"And mine hand shall be upon the prophets" → "And my hand shall be against the prophets"** (v9) — Hebrew אֶל. **"that divine lies" → "who divine a lie"** — הַקֹּסְמִים כָּזָב, קָסַם "practise divination" (noun קֶסֶם). **"the assembly of my people" → "the secret counsel of my people"** — סוֹד "secret counsel" (the close circle and what is said in it, as Jer 23:18, 22; "council" in other versions). **"neither shall they be written in the writing of the house of Israel"** — the Hebrew order kept, each clause first with its place. **"the land of Israel" → "the ground of Israel"** — אַדְמַת יִשְׂרָאֵל, אֲדָמָה "ground" (Ezekiel's own phrase; אֶרֶץ is "land"). **"and ye shall know that I am the Lord GOD"** — וִידַעְתֶּם כִּי אֲנִי אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, the knowledge formula, here with both names.
- **"Because, even because they have seduced my people" → "Because, and because they have led my people astray"** (v10) — Hebrew יַעַן וּבְיַעַן, the doubled word with "and"; הִטְעָה "lead astray" (only here; another root than הִתְעָה "cause to wander"). **"Peace; and there was no peace" → "Shalom, and there is no shalom"** — שָׁלוֹם (also v16); a noun clause, present. **"and one built up a wall, and, lo, others daubed it with untempered morter" → "and he builds a thin wall, and behold, they are plastering it with whitewash"** — וְהוּא בֹּנֶה חַיִץ וְהִנָּם טָחִים אֹתוֹ תָּפֵל, participles; הוּא "he" (the people, or one of them) set against הִנָּם "behold, they" (the prophets); חַיִץ is found only here: a thin partition wall (uncertain; קִיר, v12, 14, 15, is the ordinary wall); טוּחַ "plaster" (as Lev 14:42); תָּפֵל "whitewash": the word means "tasteless, unseasoned" (Job 6:6), a wash without binding lime; the exact stuff is uncertain (also v11, 14, 15).
- **"there shall be an overflowing shower" → "An overflowing rain has come"** (v11) — Hebrew הָיָה גֶּשֶׁם שׁוֹטֵף, a perfect: told as already come (also "shall be"); גֶּשֶׁם "rain". **"and ye, O great hailstones, shall fall" → "and you, stones of hail, shall fall"** — אַבְנֵי אֶלְגָּבִישׁ, literally "stones of ice / hail" (אֶלְגָּבִישׁ only in Ezekiel 13 and 38:22); no "O", no "great". **"and a stormy wind shall rend it" → "and a wind of storms shall split it"** — רוּחַ סְעָרוֹת "a wind of storms"; תְּבַקֵּעַ, בִּקַּע "split"; "it" (the wall) is supplied: the verb has no object written.
- **"Lo, when the wall is fallen" → "And behold, the wall has fallen"** (v12) — Hebrew וְהִנֵּה נָפַל הַקִּיר, a perfect. **"Where is the daubing wherewith ye have daubed it?" → "Where is the plastering that you plastered?"** — הַטִּיחַ אֲשֶׁר טַחְתֶּם, noun and verb of one root; no "it".
- **"I will even rend it with a stormy wind in my fury" → "And I will make a wind of storms burst out in my fury"** (v13) — Hebrew וּבִקַּעְתִּי רוּחַ־סְעָרוֹת, literally "and I will split a wind of storms": the wind is the object (let loose, burst out), not the means; חֵמָה "fury" (also v15), אַף "anger". **"great hailstones in my fury to consume it" → "stones of hail in fury, for a full end"** — בְּחֵמָה לְכָלָה, no "my" and no "it"; כָּלָה "full end".
- **"So will I break down the wall" → "And I will throw down the wall"** (v14) — Hebrew וְהָרַסְתִּי, הָרַס "throw down". **"so that the foundation thereof shall be discovered" → "and its foundation shall be uncovered"** — וְנִגְלָה יְסֹדוֹ, "and" kept. **"and ye shall be consumed" → "and you shall be finished"** — וּכְלִיתֶם, qal of כָּלָה "come to an end" (the root of v13 "full end" and v15 "spend"); "consume" is אָכַל.
- **"Thus will I accomplish my wrath" → "And I will spend my fury"** (v15) — Hebrew וְכִלֵּיתִי אֶת־חֲמָתִי, piel of כָּלָה. **"The wall is no more, neither they that daubed it" → "The wall is not, and those who plastered it are not"** — אֵין … וְאֵין, twice.
- **"To wit, the prophets of Israel which prophesy … and which see visions of peace for her" → "the prophets of Israel who prophesy … and who behold for her a vision of shalom"** (v16) — Hebrew participles, present; חֲזוֹן "vision" singular; "To wit" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Likewise, thou son of man" → "And you, son of man"** (v17) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה. **"which prophesy out of their own heart"** — הַמִּתְנַבְּאוֹת, hitpael (one English for both stems, as Jer 14:14).
- **"Woe to the women that sew pillows to all armholes" → "Woe to the women who sew bands on all the joints of my hands"** (v18) — Hebrew לִמְתַפְּרוֹת כְּסָתוֹת עַל כָּל־אַצִּילֵי יָדַי. כֶּסֶת (only here and v20) is uncertain: from a word "bind" (bands, amulets tied on) or from כָּסָה "cover" (KJV "pillows"); "bands" is the plainest sense; "magic" is not in the word. אַצִּילֵי יָדַי as printed: "the joints of my hands" (the wrists or elbows); the suffix is commonly taken as an old dual, "of the hands", or corrected to it (ESV "wrists"): uncertain, the MT kept. **"and make kerchiefs upon the head of every stature" → "and make veils for the head of every stature"** — מִסְפָּחוֹת (only here and v21; not the מִסְפַּחַת "scab" of Lev 13) is uncertain: a covering laid over or wrapped round the head; "veils" is the plainest sense. כָּל־קוֹמָה "every stature" (of every size, tall or short). **"to hunt souls!"** — לְצוֹדֵד נְפָשׁוֹת, נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (also v19–20). **"Will ye hunt the souls of my people, and will ye save the souls alive that come unto you?" → "The souls of my people you hunt, and souls for yourselves you keep alive"** — הַנְּפָשׁוֹת תְּצוֹדֵדְנָה לְעַמִּי וּנְפָשׁוֹת לָכֶנָה תְחַיֶּינָה: no question word (ESV, KJV make it a question; it may be one); לְעַמִּי "for / belonging to my people"; לָכֶנָה "for yourselves"; חִיָּה piel "keep alive"; "that come unto you" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"And will ye pollute me among my people" → "And you have profaned me among my people"** (v19) — Hebrew וַתְּחַלֶּלְנָה, a past form, not a question; חִלֵּל "profane". **"pieces of bread" → "bits of bread"** — פְּתוֹתֵי, פַּת "bit". **"to slay the souls" → "to put to death souls"** — לְהָמִית, הֵמִית "put to death". **"that hear your lies" → "who hear a lie"** — שֹׁמְעֵי כָזָב, no "your".
- **"Wherefore" → "Therefore"** (v20) — Hebrew לָכֵן. **"wherewith ye there hunt the souls to make them fly" → "with which you hunt there the souls as flying things"** — לְפֹרְחוֹת (twice), from פָּרַח in the sense "fly" (as Aramaic): "as flying ones, as birds"; uncertain ("to make them fly", "like birds" in other versions). **"from your arms" → "from upon your arms"** — מֵעַל זְרוֹעֹתֵיכֶם. **"and will let the souls go, even the souls that ye hunt" → "and I will let go the souls, the souls that you hunt"** — שִׁלַּח "let go"; the second "souls" is נְפָשִׁים, a masculine plural found only here (the MT kept, word for word).
- **"Your kerchiefs also will I tear, and deliver my people" → "And I will tear your veils, and deliver my people"** (v21) — Hebrew וְקָרַעְתִּי, "and" first. **"to be hunted" → "as prey"** — לִמְצוּדָה "for a thing hunted" (root צוּד "hunt").
- **"Because with lies ye have made the heart of the righteous sad, whom I have not made sad" → "Because you have disheartened the heart of the righteous with falsehood, when I had not pained him"** (v22) — Hebrew הַכְאוֹת לֵב־צַדִּיק שֶׁקֶר וַאֲנִי לֹא הִכְאַבְתִּיו: two verbs of like sound, הִכְאָה "dishearten" and הִכְאִיב "cause pain"; שֶׁקֶר "falsehood". **"and strengthened the hands of the wicked" → "and have strengthened the hands of the wicked"** — וּלְחַזֵּק, an infinitive going on from v22a. **"that he should not return from his wicked way" → "so that he does not turn back from his evil way"** — לְבִלְתִּי־שׁוּב, שׁוּב "turn back"; דַּרְכּוֹ הָרָע "his evil way". **"by promising him life" → "to keep him alive"** — לְהַחֲיֹתוֹ, הֶחֱיָה "keep alive": the turning would have kept him alive.
- **"Therefore ye shall see no more vanity, nor divine divinations: for I will deliver" → "therefore you shall behold emptiness no more, and divination you shall not divine any more; and I will deliver"** (v23) — Hebrew לָכֵן שָׁוְא לֹא תֶחֱזֶינָה וְקֶסֶם לֹא־תִקְסַמְנָה עוֹד וְהִצַּלְתִּי, the objects first; "and", not "for".
- **Layout** — prose, as printed. The verbs and pronouns for the prophetesses in v17–23 are feminine plural, with some masculine forms mixed in (v20 אַתֶּם, זְרוֹעֹתֵיכֶם; v21 מִסְפְּחֹתֵיכֶם), as printed.
