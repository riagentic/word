# Ezekiel 40

[1] In the twenty-fifth year of our exile, at the beginning of the year, on the tenth of the month, in the fourteenth year after the city was struck, on that very day the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was upon me, and he brought me there.

[2] In visions of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ he brought me to the land of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and set me down on a very high mountain; and on it was something like the structure of a city, on the south.

[3] And he brought me there, and behold, a man whose appearance was like the appearance of bronze, with a cord of linen in his hand and the measuring reed; and he was standing in the gate.

[4] And the man spoke to me, "Son of man, see with your eyes, and hear with your ears, and set your heart on all that I am showing you; for in order to show you, you have been brought here. Declare all that you are seeing to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[5] And behold, a wall outside the house, all around, all around; and in the man's hand the measuring reed, six cubits, by the cubit and a handbreadth; and he measured the width of the building, one reed, and the height, one reed.

[6] And he came to the gate whose face was toward the east, and went up by its steps, and measured the threshold of the gate, one reed in width, and the one threshold, one reed in width.

[7] And the room, one reed long and one reed wide; and between the rooms, five cubits; and the threshold of the gate beside the porch of the gate, from the house, one reed.

[8] And he measured the porch of the gate, from the house, one reed.

[9] And he measured the porch of the gate, eight cubits, and its pilasters, two cubits; and the porch of the gate was from the house.

[10] And the rooms of the gate toward the east were three on this side and three on that side; one measure for the three of them, and one measure for the pilasters on this side and on that side.

[11] And he measured the width of the entrance of the gate, ten cubits; the length of the gate, thirteen cubits.

[12] And a border before the rooms, one cubit, and one cubit a border on this side; and the room, six cubits on this side and six cubits on that side.

[13] And he measured the gate from the roof of the room to its roof, a width of twenty-five cubits, entrance opposite entrance.

[14] And he made pilasters, sixty cubits; and to the pilaster of the court, the gate, all around, all around.

[15] And from the face of the gate of the entrance to the face of the porch of the inner gate, fifty cubits.

[16] And there were closed windows to the rooms and to their pilasters, inward to the gate, all around, all around, and so to the porches; and windows all around, all around, inward; and on the pilaster, palm trees.

[17] And he brought me into the outer court; and behold, chambers and a pavement made for the court, all around, all around; thirty chambers on the pavement.

[18] And the pavement was by the shoulder of the gates, alongside the length of the gates, the lower pavement.

[19] And he measured the width from before the lower gate to before the inner court, outside, a hundred cubits, the east and the north.

[20] And the gate whose face was toward the north, belonging to the outer court: he measured its length and its width.

[21] And its rooms, three on this side and three on that side, and its pilasters and its porches were according to the measure of the first gate: fifty cubits its length, and the width twenty-five, by the cubit.

[22] And its windows and its porches and its palm trees were according to the measure of the gate whose face was toward the east; and by seven steps they went up into it, and its porches were before them.

[23] And there was a gate to the inner court opposite the gate, to the north and to the east; and he measured from gate to gate, a hundred cubits.

[24] And he made me walk toward the south, and behold, a gate toward the south; and he measured its pilasters and its porches according to these measures.

[25] And there were windows to it and to its porches, all around, all around, like these windows: fifty cubits the length, and the width twenty-five cubits.

[26] And seven steps were its ascents, and its porches were before them; and it had palm trees, one on this side and one on that side, on its pilasters.

[27] And there was a gate to the inner court toward the south; and he measured from gate to gate toward the south, a hundred cubits.

[28] And he brought me into the inner court by the south gate; and he measured the south gate according to these measures.

[29] And its rooms and its pilasters and its porches were according to these measures; and there were windows to it and to its porches, all around, all around: fifty cubits the length, and the width twenty-five cubits.

[30] And there were porches all around, all around: the length twenty-five cubits, and the width five cubits.

[31] And its porches were toward the outer court, and palm trees on its pilasters; and eight steps were its ascent.

[32] And he brought me into the inner court toward the east; and he measured the gate according to these measures.

[33] And its rooms and its pilasters and its porches were according to these measures; and there were windows to it and to its porches, all around, all around: the length fifty cubits, and the width twenty-five cubits.

[34] And its porches were toward the outer court, and palm trees on its pilasters, on this side and on that side; and eight steps were its ascent.

[35] And he brought me to the north gate; and he measured according to these measures:

[36] its rooms, its pilasters and its porches; and there were windows to it, all around, all around: the length fifty cubits, and the width twenty-five cubits.

[37] And its pilasters were toward the outer court, and palm trees on its pilasters, on this side and on that side; and eight steps were its ascent.

[38] And there was a chamber with its entrance by the pilasters of the gates; there they rinse off the burnt offering.

[39] And in the porch of the gate were two tables on this side and two tables on that side, to slaughter on them the burnt offering and the sin offering and the guilt offering.

[40] And at the shoulder outside, for one going up to the entrance of the gate toward the north, were two tables; and at the other shoulder, which belonged to the porch of the gate, two tables.

[41] Four tables on this side and four tables on that side, by the shoulder of the gate: eight tables, on which they slaughter.

[42] And four tables for the burnt offering were of hewn stone, a cubit and a half long, and a cubit and a half wide, and one cubit high; on them they lay the utensils with which they slaughter the burnt offering and the sacrifice.

[43] And the hooks, one handbreadth, were fixed in the house, all around, all around; and on the tables was the flesh of the offering.

[44] And outside the inner gate were the chambers of the singers, in the inner court, which was at the shoulder of the north gate, and their faces toward the south; one at the shoulder of the east gate, its face toward the north.

[45] And he spoke to me, "This chamber, whose face is toward the south, is for the priests who keep the charge of the house;

[46] and the chamber whose face is toward the north is for the priests who keep the charge of the altar. These are the **sons of ⟦zadok|Tzadoq|Zadok⟧**, those of the sons of Levi who come near to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to minister to him."

[47] And he measured the court, a hundred cubits long and a hundred cubits wide, square; and the altar was before the house.

[48] And he brought me to the porch of the house, and measured the pilaster of the porch, five cubits on this side and five cubits on that side; and the width of the gate, three cubits on this side and three cubits on that side.

[49] The length of the porch was twenty cubits, and the width eleven cubits, and by the steps by which they went up to it; and there were pillars by the pilasters, one on this side and one on that side.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 46) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept ("Then", "So", "When" before were not in the words).
- **Building words** (v3–49; one wording through Ezek 40–48, as 1 Kgs 6–7 and their rows) — שַׁעַר "gate"; תָּא "room" (as 1 Kgs 14:28; KJV "little chamber", others "guardroom"; what it was used for is not said); אוּלָם and the plural אֵלַמּוֹת / אֵילַמָּיו (qere) "porch(es)" (the 1 Kgs 6–7 row; אֵילָם is found only in Ezek 40: whether it is another form of אוּלָם or another part of the gate is not known, so the one English word is kept); אַיִל, plural אֵילִים, "pilaster" (the 1 Kgs 6:31 row; a projecting pier or jamb of the wall: uncertain; עַמּוּד "pillar" v49 is another word); סַף "threshold"; פֶּתַח "entrance"; לִשְׁכָּה "chamber"; חָצֵר "court"; רִצְפָּה "pavement"; כָּתֵף "shoulder" (the 1 Kgs 6:8 row: a side of the gate); מַעֲלוֹת "steps"; חַלּוֹנוֹת אֲטֻמוֹת "closed windows" (as 1 Kgs 6:4 "closed frames": narrowed, latticed or fixed: uncertain); תִּמֹרִים "palm trees" (carved figures, the 1 Kgs 6 row); קְנֵה הַמִּדָּה "the measuring reed"; רֹחַב · אֹרֶךְ · קוֹמָה / גֹּבַהּ "width · length · height"; the directions as written: קָדִים "east", צָפוֹן "north", דָּרוֹם "south" (not נֶגֶב, which v2 has: "on the south"); דֶּרֶךְ "toward" (literally "the way of"); מִפֹּה וּמִפֹּה "on this side and on that side". The measurements stand as printed; the plan of the gates is not certain at many points, and the verse is never filled out to make it clear.
- **"he brought me thither" → "and he brought me there"** (v1) — שָׁמָּה "there" (the earlier "to the city" supplied a place). **"in the beginning of the year"** — בְּרֹאשׁ הַשָּׁנָה, literally "at the head of the year" (whether the spring or the autumn new year is meant is not said). **"in the fourteenth year after that the city was smitten" → "after the city was struck"** — הֻכְּתָה, hofal of נכה (the הִכָּה row). **"in the selfsame day" → "on that very day"** — בְּעֶצֶם הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה (its row: literally "in the bone of this day").
- **"and set me upon a very high mountain, by which was as the frame of a city on the south" → "and set me down on a very high mountain; and on it was something like the structure of a city, on the south"** (v2) — וַיְנִיחֵנִי "set me down" (as 37:1); אֶל "on, to"; וְעָלָיו "and on it"; כְּמִבְנֵה־עִיר "like the structure of a city" ("something" supplied: the כְּ stands before a noun with no subject); מִנֶּגֶב "on the south" (literally "from the Negev side"). מַרְאוֹת "visions" (the מַרְאָה row; as 1:1; 8:3).
- **"And he brought me thither" → "And he brought me there"** (v3) — the written form וַיָּבֵיא has an extra י (the sense is the same). **"whose appearance was like the appearance of brass" → "… of bronze"** — נְחֹשֶׁת "bronze" (its row); מַרְאֶה "appearance" twice, kept. **"with a line of flax in his hand, and a measuring reed" → "with a cord of linen in his hand and the measuring reed"** — פְּתִיל־פִּשְׁתִּים "a cord of flax" (פִּשְׁתִּים "linen", its row: the word is "flax"); קְנֵה הַמִּדָּה with the article (also v5). **"in the gate"** — בַּשָּׁעַר (no "way").
- **"And the man said unto me" → "And the man spoke to me"** (v4) — וַיְדַבֵּר (also v45). **"set thine heart upon all that I shall shew thee" → "set your heart on all that I am showing you"** — מַרְאֶה, a participle. **"for to the intent that I might shew them unto thee art thou brought hither" → "for in order to show you, you have been brought here"** — לְמַעַן הַרְאוֹתְכָה "in order to show you" (no object). **"all that thou seest" → "all that you are seeing"** — רֹאֶה, a participle.
- **"And behold a wall on the outside of the house round about" → "And behold, a wall outside the house, all around, all around"** (v5) — בַּיִת "house" (the earlier "temple area" interpreted); סָבִיב סָבִיב doubled, kept through the chapter. **"a measuring reed of six cubits long by the cubit and an hand breadth" → "the measuring reed, six cubits, by the cubit and a handbreadth"** — שֵׁשׁ־אַמּוֹת בָּאַמָּה וָטֹפַח: each cubit is a cubit and a handbreadth (a long cubit); "long", the brackets and the figure in cm are not in the text. **"the breadth of the building" → "the width of the building"** — רֹחַב "width" (as 1 Kgs 6:2; "thickness" was a reading); בִּנְיָן "building" (v2 מִבְנֶה "structure", another noun of בָּנָה); קוֹמָה "height".
- **"Then came he unto the gate which looketh toward the east" → "And he came to the gate whose face was toward the east"** (v6) — אֲשֶׁר פָּנָיו דֶּרֶךְ הַקָּדִימָה "whose face (was) the way toward the east" (also v20, 22). **"and went up the stairs thereof" → "and went up by its steps"** — written במעלותו, read (qere) בְּמַעֲלוֹתָיו "by its steps" (plural). **"and measured the threshold of the gate, which was one reed broad; and the other threshold of the gate, which was one reed broad" → "and measured the threshold of the gate, one reed in width, and the one threshold, one reed in width"** — וְאֵת סַף אֶחָד "and the one threshold": the Hebrew has no "other" (the clause is often taken as a repetition by error; the Septuagint does not have it, from memory): the MT is kept, word for word. The earlier "one reed deep" left the second clause out.
- **"And every little chamber" → "And the room"** (v7) — וְהַתָּא, singular with the article; תָּא "room" (the building words above; plural v7b, 10, 12). **"and between the little chambers were five cubits" → "and between the rooms, five cubits"** — no verb. **"and the threshold of the gate by the porch of the gate within was one reed" → "and the threshold of the gate beside the porch of the gate, from the house, one reed"** — מֵאֵצֶל "from beside"; מֵהַבַּיִת "from the house" (= on the side toward the house, inward; also v8, 9).
- **"He measured also the porch of the gate within, one reed"** (v8) — kept as printed; many Hebrew manuscripts lack this verse (from memory: it repeats v7 and is taken as a doubling); the MT is kept.
- **"and the posts thereof, two cubits" → "and its pilasters, two cubits"** (v9) — written ואילו "and its pilaster", read (qere) וְאֵילָיו "and its pilasters"; אַיִל "pilaster" (the building words). **"and the porch of the gate was inward" → "and the porch of the gate was from the house"** — מֵהַבָּיִת, as v7.
- **"And the little chambers of the gate eastward were three on this side, and three on that side; they three were of one measure" → "And the rooms of the gate toward the east were three on this side and three on that side; one measure for the three of them"** (v10) — מִדָּה אַחַת לִשְׁלָשְׁתָּם; מִפֹּה … מִפֹּה (also מִפּוֹ) "on this side … on that side" through the chapter; לָאֵילִם (a defective spelling of אֵילִים) "for the pilasters".
- **"the breadth of the entry of the gate" → "the width of the entrance of the gate"** (v11) — פֶּתַח "entrance" (its row). **"and the length of the gate, thirteen cubits"** — אֹרֶךְ, no "and" in the Hebrew.
- **"The space also before the little chambers was one cubit on this side" → "And a border before the rooms, one cubit, and one cubit a border on this side"** (v12) — גְּבוּל "border" (its row; here a low dividing edge or barrier before the rooms: uncertain), twice, the Hebrew order kept. **"and the little chambers were six cubits on this side, and six cubits on that side" → "and the room, six cubits …"** — singular וְהַתָּא.
- **"from the roof of one little chamber to the roof of another" → "from the roof of the room to its roof"** (v13) — מִגַּג הַתָּא לְגַגּוֹ. **"door against door" → "entrance opposite entrance"** — פֶּתַח נֶגֶד פָּתַח.
- **"He made also posts of threescore cubits, even unto the post of the court round about the gate" → "And he made pilasters, sixty cubits; and to the pilaster of the court, the gate, all around, all around"** (v14) — MT וַיַּעַשׂ אֶת־אֵילִים שִׁשִּׁים אַמָּה וְאֶל־אֵיל הֶחָצֵר הַשַּׁעַר סָבִיב סָבִיב, word for word: וַיַּעַשׂ "and he made" (in a chapter of measuring; many read וַיָּמָד "and he measured", from memory); "sixty cubits" (the Septuagint has "twenty", and "porch" for "pilasters", from memory); the end of the verse has no verb, and its sense is not known. The MT is kept. The earlier "jambs" is now "pilasters" (the row).
- **"And from the face of the gate of the entrance unto the face of the porch of the inner gate were fifty cubits"** (v15) — written היאתון, read (qere) הָאִיתוֹן "the entrance" (found only here; from אָתָה "come"), beside פֶּתַח "entrance" (v11, 13): one English word, said here; עַל־לִפְנֵי "to before, to the face of".
- **"And there were narrow windows to the little chambers, and to their posts within the gate round about" → "And there were closed windows to the rooms and to their pilasters, inward to the gate, all around, all around"** (v16) — חַלֹּנוֹת אֲטֻמוֹת (the building words; "narrowing inwards" was a guess); אֵלֵיהֵמָה "their pilasters" (אֵלֵי, a short form of אֵילֵי). **"and likewise to the arches" → "and so to the porches"** — לָאֵלַמּוֹת (the building words). **"and upon each post were palm trees" → "and on the pilaster, palm trees"** — אַיִל singular.
- **"lo, there were chambers, and a pavement made for the court round about: thirty chambers were upon the pavement" → "and behold, chambers and a pavement made for the court, all around, all around; thirty chambers on the pavement"** (v17) — הִנֵּה "behold"; עָשׂוּי "made" (masculine, of the pavement); אֶל "on, toward" ("faced" interpreted).
- **"And the pavement by the side of the gates over against the length of the gates was the lower pavement" → "And the pavement was by the shoulder of the gates, alongside the length of the gates, the lower pavement"** (v18) — כֶּתֶף "shoulder" (the building words); לְעֻמַּת "alongside" (the reviewer's row).
- **"Then he measured the breadth from the forefront of the lower gate unto the forefront of the inner court without" → "And he measured the width from before the lower gate to before the inner court, outside"** (v19) — מִלִּפְנֵי … לִפְנֵי; הַשַּׁעַר הַתַּחְתּוֹנָה, a masculine noun with a feminine adjective (as printed); מִחוּץ "outside". **"an hundred cubits eastward and northward" → "a hundred cubits, the east and the north"** — הַקָּדִים וְהַצָּפוֹן, two nouns without a preposition: whether the hundred cubits hold on the east and the north sides, or the verse closes the east and opens the north, is not said.
- **"And the gate of the outward court that looked toward the north, he measured the length thereof" → "And the gate whose face was toward the north, belonging to the outer court: he measured its length and its width"** (v20) — the Hebrew order (the object first); לֶחָצֵר "belonging to the court".
- **"And the little chambers thereof … and the posts thereof and the arches thereof were after the measure of the first gate" → "And its rooms … and its pilasters and its porches were according to the measure of the first gate"** (v21) — written ותאו ואילו ואלמו (singular "its room …"), read (qere) וְתָאָיו וְאֵילָיו וְאֵלַמָּיו (plural); the written and read forms differ this way through v21–37 (the sense is the same); הָיָה "it was", singular. **"and the breadth five and twenty cubits" → "and the width twenty-five, by the cubit"** — בָּאַמָּה "by the cubit" (as v5).
- **"And their windows, and their arches, and their palm trees" → "And its windows and its porches and its palm trees"** (v22) — qere singular suffix ("its": of the gate); "their" was a guess. **"and they went up unto it by seven steps" → "and by seven steps they went up into it"** — יַעֲלוּ־בוֹ. **"and the arches thereof were before them" → "and its porches were before them"** — qere וְאֵילַמָּיו.
- **"And the gate of the inner court was over against the gate toward the north, and toward the east" → "And there was a gate to the inner court opposite the gate, to the north and to the east"** (v23) — נֶגֶד "opposite"; לַצָּפוֹן וְלַקָּדִים, as printed.
- **"After that he brought me toward the south" → "And he made me walk toward the south"** (v24) — וַיּוֹלִכֵנִי, hifil of הָלַךְ (the הוֹלִיךְ row: "make walk"). **"and he measured the posts thereof and the arches thereof according to these measures" → "and he measured its pilasters and its porches according to these measures"** — qere אֵילָיו וְאֵילַמָּיו; כַּמִּדּוֹת הָאֵלֶּה "according to these measures" (also v28, 29, 32, 33, 35); "the others" was supplied.
- **"And there were windows in it and in the arches thereof round about" → "And there were windows to it and to its porches, all around, all around"** (v25) — qere וּלְאֵילַמָּיו. **"like those windows" → "like these windows"** — כְּהַחֲלֹּנוֹת הָאֵלֶּה.
- **"And there were seven steps to go up to it, and the arches thereof were before them" → "And seven steps were its ascents, and its porches were before them"** (v26) — written עלותו, read (qere) עֹלוֹתָיו "its ascents" (noun of עָלָה "go up"; v31, 34, 37 מַעֲלָיו "its ascent", qere: another noun of the same root); qere וְאֵלַמָּיו, אֵילָיו. **"one on this side, and another on that side, upon the posts thereof" → "one on this side and one on that side, on its pilasters"** — אֶחָד … אֶחָד.
- **"from gate to gate toward the south an hundred cubits"** (v27) — מִשַּׁעַר אֶל־הַשַּׁעַר, the second with the article, as printed.
- **"by the south gate" → "by the south gate"** (v28) — בְּשַׁעַר הַדָּרוֹם; "It was of the same size as the others" → "according to these measures" (as v24).
- **"And the little chambers thereof … and there were windows in it and in the arches thereof round about"** (v29, 33) — qere plurals (as v21); "both it and its vestibule" → "to it and to its porches" (לוֹ וּלְאֵלַמָּיו).
- **"And the arches round about were five and twenty cubits long, and five cubits broad" → "And there were porches all around, all around: the length twenty-five cubits, and the width five cubits"** (v30) — אֵלַמּוֹת; some Hebrew manuscripts and the Septuagint lack this verse (from memory): the MT is kept; how these "porches" fit the other figures is not clear.
- **"And the arches thereof were toward the utter court" → "And its porches were toward the outer court"** (v31) — אֵלַמָּו, a short form of אֵלַמָּיו; אֶל "toward". **"and palm trees were upon the posts thereof" → "and palm trees on its pilasters"** — qere אֵילָיו. **"and the going up to it had eight steps" → "and eight steps were its ascent"** — written מעלו, read (qere) מַעֲלָיו "its ascent" (also v34, 37).
- **"And he brought me into the inner court toward the east" → "… toward the east"** (v32) — דֶּרֶךְ הַקָּדִים.
- **"And the arches thereof were toward the outward court … on this side, and on that side" → "And its porches were toward the outer court … on this side and on that side"** (v34) — qere וְאֵלַמָּיו, אֵלָיו.
- **"And he brought me to the north gate, and measured it according to these measures" → "and he measured according to these measures"** (v35) — וּמָדַד, no object; the list of v36 follows as its object.
- **"The little chambers thereof, the posts thereof, and the arches thereof, and the windows to it round about" → "its rooms, its pilasters and its porches; and there were windows to it, all around, all around"** (v36) — written תאו אלו ואלמו, read (qere) plurals.
- **"And the posts thereof were toward the utter court" → "And its pilasters were toward the outer court"** (v37) — written ואילו, read (qere) וְאֵילָיו "and its pilasters", where v31 and v34 have "its porches"; the Septuagint has "porches" here (from memory): the MT is kept.
- **"And the chambers and the entries thereof were by the posts of the gates" → "And there was a chamber with its entrance by the pilasters of the gates"** (v38) — וְלִשְׁכָּה וּפִתְחָהּ, singular; בְּאֵילִים "by / at the pilasters". **"where they washed the burnt offering" → "there they rinse off the burnt offering"** — יָדִיחוּ, hifil of דּוּחַ (the הֵדִיחַ row: "rinse off"; רָחַץ is "wash"); an imperfect.
- **"two tables on this side, and two tables on that side, to slay thereon the burnt offering and the sin offering and the trespass offering" → "… to slaughter on them the burnt offering and the sin offering and the guilt offering"** (v39) — שָׁחַט "slaughter" (its row); חַטָּאת "sin offering", אָשָׁם "guilt offering" (the Leviticus row); אֲלֵיהֶם "on / to them".
- **"And at the side without, as one goeth up to the entry of the north gate" → "And at the shoulder outside, for one going up to the entrance of the gate toward the north"** (v40) — הַכָּתֵף "the shoulder" (the building words); לָעוֹלֶה "for the one going up" (others take it as "for the burnt offering", לָעֹלָה, the same letters: uncertain); הַצָּפוֹנָה "toward the north". **"and on the other side, which was at the porch of the gate" → "and at the other shoulder, which belonged to the porch of the gate"** — אֲשֶׁר לְאֻלָם.
- **"by the side of the gate; eight tables, whereupon they slew their sacrifices" → "by the shoulder of the gate: eight tables, on which they slaughter"** (v41) — לְכֶתֶף; יִשְׁחָטוּ, no object ("their sacrifices" was supplied).
- **"And the four tables were of hewn stone for the burnt offering" → "And four tables for the burnt offering were of hewn stone"** (v42) — אַבְנֵי גָזִית "stones of hewing" (the גָּזִית row: "hewn stone"); no article on "four tables". **"whereupon also they laid the instruments wherewith they slew the burnt offering and the sacrifice" → "on them they lay the utensils with which they slaughter the burnt offering and the sacrifice"** — וְיַנִּיחוּ (the הִנִּיחַ row: "set"; the וְ before it as printed); כֵּלִים "utensils" (the כְּלִי row); זֶבַח "sacrifice", singular.
- **"And within were hooks, an hand broad, fastened round about" → "And the hooks, one handbreadth, were fixed in the house, all around, all around"** (v43) — וְהַשְׁפַתַּיִם, a dual with the article, found only here in this sense (Ps 68:13 has a like word for "sheepfolds"): "hooks" (double pegs) or "ledges, rims": uncertain; מוּכָנִים "fixed, set up"; בַּבַּיִת "in the house" (KJV "within"). **"and upon the tables was the flesh of the offering"** — קָרְבָּן "offering" (its row: what is brought near); "to be laid" was supplied.
- **"And without the inner gate were the chambers of the singers in the inner court" → "And outside the inner gate were the chambers of the singers, in the inner court"** (v44) — MT לִשְׁכוֹת שָׁרִים "chambers of singers" (שָׁרִים, participle of שִׁיר "sing"); the Septuagint has "two chambers" (reading שְׁתַּיִם), and v45–46 speak of priests (from memory): the MT is kept; the earlier "two chambers" followed the Greek. **"which was at the side of the north gate; and their prospect was toward the south" → "which was at the shoulder of the north gate, and their faces toward the south"** — כֶּתֶף "shoulder"; פְּנֵיהֶם "their faces". **"one at the side of the east gate having the prospect toward the north" → "one at the shoulder of the east gate, its face toward the north"** — MT הַקָּדִים "the east" (the Septuagint "south", from memory, which the earlier "south gate" followed): the MT is kept.
- **"This chamber, whose prospect is toward the south, is for the priests, the keepers of the charge of the house" → "This chamber, whose face is toward the south, is for the priests who keep the charge of the house"** (v45) — שֹׁמְרֵי מִשְׁמֶרֶת הַבַּיִת, "keepers of the charge of the house" (the מִשְׁמֶרֶת row: "charge"; "who have charge of the temple" interpreted; בַּיִת "house").
- **"which among the sons of Levi come near to the LORD to minister unto him" → "those of the sons of Levi who come near to Yahweh to minister to him"** (v46) — הַקְּרֵבִים מִבְּנֵי־לֵוִי; "alone" (the earlier wording) is not written; שֵׁרֵת "minister to" (its row).
- **"an hundred cubits long, and an hundred cubits broad, foursquare" → "a hundred cubits long and a hundred cubits wide, square"** (v47) — מְרֻבַּעַת "square" (made four-sided); לִפְנֵי הַבַּיִת "before the house".
- **"and measured each post of the porch, five cubits on this side, and five cubits on that side" → "and measured the pilaster of the porch, five cubits on this side and five cubits on that side"** (v48) — אֵל אֻלָם, singular (a short form of אֵיל). **"and the breadth of the gate was three cubits on this side, and three cubits on that side" → "and the width of the gate, three cubits on this side and three cubits on that side"** — MT; the earlier "the breadth of the gate was fourteen cubits, and the sidewalls of the entrance" follows the Septuagint (from memory: "the width of the gate fourteen cubits, and the side walls of the gate three cubits"): the MT is kept.
- **"the breadth eleven cubits" → "the width eleven cubits"** (v49) — עַשְׁתֵּי עֶשְׂרֵה "eleven" (MT; the earlier "twelve" followed the Septuagint, from memory). **"and he brought me by the steps whereby they went up to it" → "and by the steps by which they went up to it"** — וּבַמַּעֲלוֹת אֲשֶׁר יַעֲלוּ אֵלָיו: the MT has no number and no verb before it (KJV supplied "he brought me"; the earlier "by ten steps" follows the Septuagint, from memory): the MT is kept; the clause is incomplete as printed. **"and there were pillars by the posts" → "and there were pillars by the pilasters"** — עַמּוּדִים "pillars" (its row) beside אֵילִים "pilasters" (the two words kept apart).

### Notes
- **v1** — the twenty-fifth year of the exile of Jehoiachin (1:2); the fourteenth year after the fall of the city (33:21). The vision of the house runs to 48:35.
- **The plan** — the outer wall (v5), the east gate with its rooms, pilasters and porch (v6–16), the outer court (v17–19), the north and south gates (v20–27), the inner court and its three gates (v28–37), the tables and chambers (v38–47), the porch of the house (v48–49). The gates match the plan of gates found at Megiddo, Hazor and Gezer (from memory); the text alone does not settle the plan.
- **Sons of Zadok** (v46) — the priests of the line of Zadok (1 Kgs 2:35); 44:15–16.
