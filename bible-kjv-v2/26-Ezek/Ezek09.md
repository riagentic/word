# Ezekiel 9

[1] And he called in my ears with a loud voice, saying, "Bring near the visitations of the city, and each his weapon of ruin in his hand."

[2] And behold, six men were coming from the way of the upper gate, which faces north, and each his weapon of shattering in his hand; and one man in their midst was clothed in linen, with the writing case of a scribe at his loins. And they came in and stood beside the altar of bronze.

[3] And the glory of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had gone up from upon the ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ on which it had been, to the threshold of the house. And he called to the man clothed in linen, who had the writing case of the scribe at his loins.

[4] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to him, "Pass through the midst of the city, through the midst of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and you shall mark a mark on the foreheads of the men who sigh and who groan over all the abominations that are done in its midst."

[5] And to these he said in my ears, "Pass through the city after him, and strike; let not your eye pity, and do not have pity.

[6] Old man, young man and virgin, and little ones and women you shall kill, to destroy; but to any man on whom is the mark do not draw near; and from my sanctuary you shall begin." And they began with the men, the elders, who were before the house.

[7] And he said to them, "Defile the house, and fill the courts with slain. Go out!" And they went out and struck in the city.

[8] And it came to pass, while they were striking and I was left, I, that I fell on my face and cried out and said, "Ah, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧! Are you destroying all the remnant of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ in your pouring out of your fury upon ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧?"

[9] And he said to me, "The iniquity of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ is very, very great, and the land is filled with blood, and the city is full of perversion; for they say, '⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has forsaken the land, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ does not see.'

[10] And I also — my eye shall not pity, and I will not have pity; their way upon their head I have laid."

[11] And behold, the man clothed in linen, at whose loins was the writing case, was bringing back word, saying, "I have done according to all that you commanded me."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v8) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*, both names.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל, "the *Elohim* of Israel".
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept (v1–3, 7, 8, 11).
- **"He cried also in mine ears" → "And he called in my ears"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּקְרָא בְאָזְנַי (קָרָא "call"; בְּאָזְנֵי "in the ears of", also v5); קוֹל גָּדוֹל "a loud voice" (literally "a great voice"). **"Cause them that have charge over the city to draw near" → "Bring near the visitations of the city"** — Hebrew קָרְבוּ פְּקֻדּוֹת הָעִיר: פְּקֻדּוֹת, plural of פְּקֻדָּה, from פָּקַד "visit" (to punish), here of persons: those who bring the visitation on the city; "executioners", "those in charge of the city" are interpretations. קָרְבוּ can be read as a command ("bring near", piel) or as "the visitations of the city have drawn near" (qal perfect): the consonants and vowels allow both; the command is kept. **"every man with his destroying weapon" → "and each his weapon of ruin"** — Hebrew וְאִישׁ כְּלִי מַשְׁחֵתוֹ, literally "and a man, the article of his ruin": כְּלִי "article, gear", here a weapon; מַשְׁחֵת, noun of שָׁחַת "ruin".
- **"came from the way of the higher gate" → "were coming from the way of the upper gate"** (v2) — Hebrew בָּאִים, a participle; מִדֶּרֶךְ "from the way of". **"which lieth toward the north" → "which faces north"** — מָפְנֶה צָפוֹנָה "which is turned northward". **"a slaughter weapon" → "his weapon of shattering"** — כְּלִי מַפָּצוֹ, מַפָּץ from נָפַץ "shatter". **"one man among them" → "one man in their midst"** — בְּתוֹכָם. **"with a writer's inkhorn by his side" → "with the writing case of a scribe at his loins"** — קֶסֶת הַסֹּפֵר: קֶסֶת is found only in this chapter (an inkhorn or writing case: uncertain); בְּמָתְנָיו "at his loins" (also v3, 11). **"the brasen altar" → "the altar of bronze"** — מִזְבַּח הַנְּחֹשֶׁת.
- **"was gone up from the cherub, whereupon he was" → "had gone up from upon the cherub on which it had been"** (v3) — Hebrew נַעֲלָה מֵעַל הַכְּרוּב אֲשֶׁר הָיָה עָלָיו; the glory is the subject ("it"), the cherub is singular.
- **"through the midst of the city" → "through the midst of the city, through the midst of Jerusalem"** (v4) — בְּתוֹךְ twice. The written text has אלו, the qere אֵלָיו "to him": the same meaning. **"set a mark" → "you shall mark a mark"** — Hebrew וְהִתְוִיתָ תָּו, verb and noun of one root; תָּו is also the name of the last letter of the alphabet, in the old script a cross-shaped sign (×); the verse does not say which mark is meant. **"that sigh and that cry" → "who sigh and who groan"** — הַנֶּאֱנָחִים וְהַנֶּאֱנָקִים (נֶאֱנַח "sigh", the root of נְאָקָה "groaning"). **"in the midst thereof" → "in its midst"** — בְּתוֹכָהּ.
- **"to the others" → "to these"** (v5) — Hebrew וּלְאֵלֶּה. **"let not your eye spare, neither have ye pity" → "let not your eye pity, and do not have pity"** — the written text has עַל־ (for אַל) and עיניכם "your eyes", the qere אַל … עֵינְכֶם "your eye"; חוּס with עַיִן "pity", חָמַל "have pity" (two verbs, also v10).
- **"Slay utterly old and young, both maids, and little children, and women" → "Old man, young man and virgin, and little ones and women you shall kill, to destroy"** (v6) — Hebrew זָקֵן בָּחוּר וּבְתוּלָה וְטַף וְנָשִׁים תַּהַרְגוּ לְמַשְׁחִית: the Hebrew order; בְּתוּלָה "virgin"; טַף "little ones"; לְמַשְׁחִית "to destroy" (the participle of שָׁחַת "ruin", as Exod 12:13). **"come not near any man upon whom is the mark" → "to any man on whom is the mark do not draw near"** — אַל־תִּגַּשׁוּ "do not draw near". **"Then they began at the ancient men" → "And they began with the men, the elders"** — בָּאֲנָשִׁים הַזְּקֵנִים; זָקֵן is "old man" in the first line and "elder" here: one word.
- **"And they went forth, and slew" → "And they went out and struck"** (v7) — וְיָצְאוּ וְהִכּוּ (הִכָּה "strike"); "Defile" is טִמְּאוּ, piel of טָמֵא "unclean".
- **"while they were slaying them, and I was left" → "while they were striking and I was left, I"** (v8) — Hebrew כְּהַכּוֹתָם וְנֵאשֲׁאַר אָנִי: the form נֵאשֲׁאַר is unusual (a mixed form of נִשְׁאַר "be left"); "alone" is not said, the pronoun אָנִי stands after the verb. **"Ah Lord GOD! wilt thou destroy" → "Ah, Lord Yahweh! Are you destroying"** — אֲהָהּ; הֲמַשְׁחִית אַתָּה, a participle (הִשְׁחִית of the killing of men, as 1 Sam 26:9). **"in thy pouring out of thy fury" → "in your pouring out of your fury"** — בְּשָׁפְכְּךָ אֶת־חֲמָתְךָ; חֵמָה "fury".
- **"is exceeding great" → "is very, very great"** (v9) — Hebrew גָּדוֹל בִּמְאֹד מְאֹד, literally "great in very, very". **"the land is full of blood" → "the land is filled with blood"** — וַתִּמָּלֵא הָאָרֶץ דָּמִים, a verb; דָּמִים is plural, literally "bloods" (blood shed). **"the city full of perverseness" → "the city is full of perversion"** — מֻטֶּה, found only here, from נָטָה "bend": what is bent, perverted (justice perverted is a common reading). **"seeth not" → "does not see"** — וְאֵין יְהוָה רֹאֶה, a participle with אֵין.
- **"And as for me also" → "And I also"** (v10) — וְגַם־אֲנִי, the sentence turns after it. **"but I will recompense their way upon their head" → "their way upon their head I have laid"** — Hebrew דַּרְכָּם בְּרֹאשָׁם נָתַתִּי: דֶּרֶךְ "way"; נָתַתִּי a perfect, literally "I have given" (= I have brought their conduct back upon them; "recompense" is the sense, as 7:3, 4).
- **"which had the inkhorn by his side, reported the matter" → "at whose loins was the writing case, was bringing back word"** (v11) — מֵשִׁיב דָּבָר, a participle. **"I have done as thou hast commanded me" → "I have done according to all that you commanded me"** — the qere כְּכֹל אֲשֶׁר "according to all that"; the written text כאשר "as".

### Notes
- **v4 tav** — in the old Hebrew script *tav* was written as a cross or "X"; Rev 7:3; 9:4 speak of a seal on the foreheads of God's servants.
