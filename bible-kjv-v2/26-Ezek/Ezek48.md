## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v29) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*, both names.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept (also at the head of each tribe, v2–7, 24–28).
- **"From the north end to the coast of the way of Hethlon, as one goeth to Hamath, Hazar-enan" → "from the end northward, alongside the way of Hethlon, Lebo-hamath, Hazar-enan"** (v1) — מִקְצֵה צָפוֹנָה אֶל־יַד דֶּרֶךְ־חֶתְלֹן לְבוֹא־חֲמָת חֲצַר עֵינָן: אֶל־יַד "alongside" (literally "to the hand of"; also "alongside Hamath"); לְבוֹא חֲמָת, literally "the coming to Hamath", the token as the name of the place (as 47:20); the names stand side by side. **"for these are his sides east and west" → "and they shall be his, the east side, the west"** — וְהָיוּ־לוֹ פְאַת־קָדִים הַיָּם, literally "the side of the front, the sea": the quarters named by the front (east) and the sea (west), in the verse by the direction (also v2–8, 10, 16–18, 21, 23–27, 32, 34). **"a portion for Dan" → "Dan, one"** — דָּן אֶחָד: "portion" is not in the Hebrew (also v2–7, 23–27).
- **"by the border of Dan" → "And by the border of Dan"** (v2) — וְעַל גְּבוּל, literally "and upon the border" (also v3–8, 24–28).
- **"shall be the offering which ye shall offer" → "shall be the contribution that you shall lift up"** (v8) — הַתְּרוּמָה אֲשֶׁר תָּרִימוּ: תְּרוּמָה "contribution" (literally "what is lifted off") and its verb הֵרִים "lift up", one root (also v9, 10, 12, 18, 20, 21). **"of five and twenty thousand reeds in breadth" → "twenty-five thousand in breadth"** — no unit in the Hebrew (also v9–21, 30–35; KJV supplied "reeds", others "cubits"); numbers in words in the ordinary order (Hebrew "five and twenty thousand", "five hundred and four thousand"). **"in length as one of the other parts" → "in length as one of the shares"** — כְּאַחַד הַחֲלָקִים: חֵלֶק "share" (also v21).
- **"ten thousand"** (v9) — MT וְרֹחַב עֲשֶׂרֶת אֲלָפִים "breadth ten thousand" (the priests' portion, v10), as 45:1 MT; the Septuagint reads "twenty thousand" (from memory), which many modern versions follow; the MT is kept.
- **"for them, even for the priests, shall be this holy oblation" → "And for these shall be the holy contribution: for the priests"** (v10) — וּלְאֵלֶּה תִּהְיֶה תְרוּמַת־הַקֹּדֶשׁ לַכֹּהֲנִים: קֹדֶשׁ as a genitive "holy". **"toward the north … toward the west"** — צָפוֹנָה, יָמָּה, קָדִימָה, נֶגְבָּה with ה of direction: "northward", "westward", "eastward", "southward" (also v17, 18).
- **"It shall be for the priests that are sanctified of the sons of Zadok" → "For the priests, what is sanctified, of the sons of Zadok"** (v11) — לַכֹּהֲנִים הַמְקֻדָּשׁ: הַמְקֻדָּשׁ is singular, so not an adjective of "priests" (= the sanctified portion, for the priests); the Septuagint and others read "for the sanctified priests" (from memory); the MT is kept. **"which have kept my charge" → "who kept my charge"** — שָׁמְרוּ מִשְׁמַרְתִּי: מִשְׁמֶרֶת "charge" (what is to be kept). **"which went not astray when the children of Israel went astray" → "who did not go astray when the sons of Israel went astray"** — תָּעָה "go astray", three times.
- **"this oblation of the land that is offered shall be unto them a thing most holy" → "it shall be theirs, a special contribution from the contribution of the land, most holy"** (v12) — תְּרוּמִיָּה, found only here, a form made from תְּרוּמָה (= a contribution of its own, taken from the whole); קֹדֶשׁ קָדָשִׁים "most holy" (literally "holiness of holinesses"). **"by the border of the Levites"** — אֶל־גְּבוּל, literally "to the border".
- **"And over against the border of the priests the Levites shall have" → "And the Levites, alongside the border of the priests"** (v13) — וְהַלְוִיִּם לְעֻמַּת גְּבוּל: no verb; לְעֻמַּת "alongside".
- **"neither exchange, nor alienate the firstfruits of the land" → "and he shall not exchange, and he shall not make the first of the land pass over"** (v14) — יָמֵר "exchange" and the qere יַעֲבִיר "make pass over" (to another; the written form יעבור "it shall pass over") are singular after the plural "they shall not sell", as printed; רֵאשִׁית "the first" (the first part, the best; as Exod 23:19). **"for it is holy unto the LORD"** — כִּי־קֹדֶשׁ לַיהוָה, literally "holiness to Yahweh".
- **"that are left in the breadth over against the five and twenty thousand" → "that are left in the breadth, facing the twenty-five thousand"** (v15) — עַל־פְּנֵי "facing". **"shall be a profane place for the city" → "is common, for the city"** — חֹל "common" (what is not holy; not "profane"). **"for dwelling, and for suburbs" → "for dwelling and for pasture land"** — מוֹשָׁב "dwelling place"; מִגְרָשׁ "pasture land" (also v17). **"in the midst thereof"** — the written form בתוכה "in her midst", qere בְּתוֹכוֹ "in its midst" (the same sense; also v21).
- **"four thousand and five hundred"** (v16) — for the south side the Masoretic text writes חמש "five" twice; the second is unpointed and not read (a written form with no reading); the number is as the other sides.
- **"its yield"** (v18) — the written form תבואתה "her yield", qere תְבוּאָתוֹ "its yield" (the same sense); תְּבוּאָה "yield". **"for food unto them that serve the city" → "for bread for those who serve the city"** — לְלֶחֶם לְעֹבְדֵי הָעִיר: לֶחֶם "bread"; עָבַד "serve".
- **"And they that serve the city shall serve it out of all the tribes" → "And he who serves the city, they shall serve it out of all the tribes"** (v19) — וְהָעֹבֵד הָעִיר יַעַבְדוּהוּ: a singular collective, then a plural verb, as printed.
- **"All the oblation shall be five and twenty thousand by five and twenty thousand: ye shall offer the holy oblation foursquare, with the possession of the city" → "All the contribution is twenty-five thousand by twenty-five thousand; foursquare you shall lift up the holy contribution with the possession of the city"** (v20) — רְבִיעִית, literally "a fourth": here "foursquare" (as מְרֻבָּע 45:2); uncertain. אֶל "with" (literally "to").
- **"And the residue shall be for the prince" → "And what is left is for the chieftain"** (v21) — וְהַנּוֹתָר לַנָּשִׂיא: נָשִׂיא "chieftain" (literally "one lifted up"), one word through Ezekiel (also v22); שַׂר is "prince". **"over against the portions for the prince" → "alongside the shares, for the chieftain"** — לְעֻמַּת חֲלָקִים לַנָּשִׂיא. **"and the sanctuary of the house"** — וּמִקְדַּשׁ הַבַּיִת.
- **"Moreover from the possession of the Levites … being in the midst of that which is the prince's" → "And from the possession of the Levites and from the possession of the city, in the midst of what is the chieftain's"** (v22) — the sentence as printed (= the chieftain's land lies on both sides of these). **"shall be for the prince" → "it shall be the chieftain's"** — לַנָּשִׂיא יִהְיֶה.
- **"As for the rest of the tribes" → "And the rest of the tribes"** (v23) — וְיֶתֶר הַשְּׁבָטִים: יֶתֶר "rest".
- **"unto the south side southward" → "at the south side southward"** (v28) — אֶל־פְּאַת נֶגֶב תֵּימָנָה, literally "the side of the Negev, toward Teman": the quarters by their names, in the verse "south". **"from Tamar unto the waters of strife in Kadesh" → "from Tamar, the waters of Meribath-kadesh"** — מֵי מְרִיבַת קָדֵשׁ, the singular מְרִיבַת "strife" (47:19 has the plural מְרִיבוֹת; each its own token); no "to" between the two. **"and to the river toward the great sea" → "toward the wadi, to the Great Sea"** — נַחֲלָה "toward the wadi" (the Wadi of Egypt, not named); "the Brook of Egypt" of the earlier text is not in the Hebrew.
- **"which ye shall divide by lot unto the tribes of Israel for inheritance" → "that you shall make fall as an inheritance to the tribes of Israel"** (v29) — אֲשֶׁר־תַּפִּילוּ מִנַּחֲלָה, literally "from inheritance"; מִן of the means: "by inheritance" (reviewer 2026-10-16: the MT kept; the Septuagint and others "as an inheritance", בְּנַחֲלָה, from memory). **"and these are their portions" → "and these are their divisions"** — מַחְלְקוֹתָם, from חָלַק "divide".
- **"And these are the goings out of the city" → "And these are the outgoings of the city"** (v30) — תּוֹצְאֹת "outgoings" (where the city ends: its sides); מִדָּה "measure".
- **"And the gates of the city shall be after the names of the tribes" → "and the gates of the city by the names of the tribes"** (v31) — עַל־שְׁמוֹת, no verb. **"one gate of Reuben" → "the gate of Reuben, one"** — שַׁעַר רְאוּבֵן אֶחָד, the Hebrew order (also v32–34).
- **"with their three gates" → "their gates three"** (v34) — שַׁעֲרֵיהֶם שְׁלֹשָׁה, "their gates", as printed (v31–33 "three gates").
- **"It was round about eighteen thousand measures" → "All around, eighteen thousand"** (v35) — סָבִיב שְׁמֹנָה עָשָׂר אָלֶף: no verb, no unit. **"the name of the city from that day shall be, The LORD is there" → "the name of the city from the day: Yahweh is there"** — וְשֵׁם־הָעִיר מִיּוֹם יְהוָה שָׁמָּה: מִיּוֹם "from the day" (= from now on; "that" is not in the Hebrew). The name יְהוָה שָׁמָּה *Yahweh shammah* is rendered by its meaning, "Yahweh is there" (שָׁמָּה, "there", with ה of direction, also "thither"); KJV in the margin "Jehovah-shammah" (the form "Jehovah" puts the vowels of *Adonai* on the letters YHWH). No token key exists for the name; the divine name keeps its own token.

### Notes

- **The last words of Ezekiel** — "Yahweh is there"; the glory that left the house (10:18–19; 11:23) has come back to it (43:1–5).
- **Twelve gates** — taken up in Rev 21:12–13.
