# Ezekiel 39

[1] "And you, son of man, prophesy against Gog, and say, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I am against you, Gog, chieftain, head of Meshech and ⟦tubal|Tuval|Tubal⟧.

[2] And I will turn you around, and drive you on, and bring you up from the remote parts of the north, and bring you to the mountains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[3] And I will strike your bow out of your left hand, and your arrows out of your right hand I will make fall.

[4] On the mountains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ you shall fall, you and all your troops and the peoples who are with you; to the birds of prey, birds of every wing, and to the living things of the field I have given you for food.

[5] On the face of the field you shall fall, for I have spoken, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[6] And I will send fire on Magog and on those who dwell in the coastlands in security; and they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] And my holy name I will make known in the midst of my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and I will not profane my holy name any more; and the nations shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, holy in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[8] Behold, it is coming, and it shall be done, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧; that is the day of which I have spoken.

[9] And those who dwell in the cities of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall go out, and kindle and burn weapons, shield and large shield, bow and arrows, hand staff and spear; and they shall kindle fire with them seven years.

[10] And they shall not carry wood from the field, and shall not hew any from the forests, for with the weapons they shall kindle fire; and they shall spoil those who spoiled them, and plunder those who plundered them, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[11] And it shall be in that day that I will give to Gog a place there, a grave in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, the valley of the passers-by, east of the sea; and it shall stop the passers-by; and there they shall bury Gog and all his multitude, and they shall call it the valley of ⟦hamon-gog|Hamon Gog|Hamon-gog⟧.

[12] And the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall bury them, in order to cleanse the land, seven months.

[13] And all the people of the land shall bury, and it shall be to them for a name, the day that I am glorified, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[14] And men for continual duty they shall set apart, passing through the land, burying, with the passers-through, those who remain on the face of the land, to cleanse it; at the end of seven months they shall search out.

[15] And the passers-through shall pass through the land, and when one sees a bone of a man, he shall build a marker beside it, until the buriers have buried it in the valley of ⟦hamon-gog|Hamon Gog|Hamon-gog⟧.

[16] And also the name of a city is Hamonah. And they shall cleanse the land.

[17] And you, son of man, thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Say to the birds of every wing and to every living thing of the field, Assemble and come, gather from all around to my sacrifice that I am sacrificing for you, a great sacrifice on the mountains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and you shall eat flesh and drink blood.

[18] The flesh of mighty ones you shall eat, and the blood of the chieftains of the earth you shall drink, rams, lambs and he-goats, bulls, fatlings of Bashan all of them.

[19] And you shall eat fat to the full, and drink blood to drunkenness, from my sacrifice that I have sacrificed for you.

[20] And you shall be sated at my table with horse and chariots, mighty one and every man of war, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[21] And I will set my glory among the nations, and all the nations shall see my judgment that I have done, and my hand that I have laid on them.

[22] And the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, from that day and onward.

[23] And the nations shall know that for their iniquity the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went into exile, because they acted unfaithfully against me; and I hid my face from them, and gave them into the hand of their adversaries, and they fell by the sword, all of them.

[24] According to their uncleanness and according to their transgressions I dealt with them, and I hid my face from them.

[25] Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Now I will restore the fortunes of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, and have compassion on the whole house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and I will be jealous for my holy name.

[26] And they shall bear their dishonor and all their unfaithfulness that they committed against me, when they dwell on their ground in security, and none makes them tremble,

[27] when I bring them back from the peoples, and gather them out of the lands of their enemies, and I am sanctified through them before the eyes of the many nations.

[28] And they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, when I sent them into exile to the nations and gathered them to their ground; and I will leave none of them there any more.

[29] And I will not hide my face from them any more, for I have poured out my spirit upon the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*, both words; Ezekiel's distinctive doubled divine title (v17 pointed יְהֹוִה, the same name).
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v22, 28) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*; "I am Yahweh their *Elohim*."
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept ("Then", "so that", "Thus" before were not in the words). The quotation marks that opened new paragraphs inside the one speech (v7, 9, 11, 17, 21, 25) are dropped: the speech runs from v1 to v29.
- **"Therefore, thou son of man, prophesy against Gog" → "And you, son of man, prophesy against Gog"** (v1) — וְאַתָּה; עַל "against". **"O Gog, the chief prince of Meshech and Tubal" → "Gog, chieftain, head of Meshech and Tubal"** — as 38:2–3: the accents set נְשִׂיא apart, so רֹאשׁ "head" goes with Meshech and Tubal ("prince of Rosh, Meshech and Tubal" in 38:2 Changes); נָשִׂיא "chieftain" (its row); no "O".
- **"And I will turn thee back, and leave but the sixth part of thee" → "And I will turn you around, and drive you on"** (v2) — שֹׁבַבְתִּיךָ (as 38:4); וְשִׁשֵּׁאתִיךָ is found only here: its meaning is not known. "Drive on, lead on" is the sense most take from the context; KJV took it from שֵׁשׁ "six" ("leave a sixth"). **"and will cause thee to come up from the north parts" → "and bring you up from the remote parts of the north"** — הֶעֱלָה "bring up"; יַרְכְּתֵי צָפוֹן (as 38:6). **"and will bring thee upon the mountains of Israel" → "and bring you to the mountains of Israel"** — עַל "upon, to".
- **"and will cause thine arrows to fall out of thy right hand" → "and your arrows out of your right hand I will make fall"** (v3) — אַפִּיל, hifil of נָפַל, last as the Hebrew; הִכָּה "strike" (its row).
- **"thou, and all thy bands" → "you and all your troops"** (v4) — אֲגַפִּים (as 38:6). **"I will give thee unto the ravenous birds of every sort, and to the beasts of the field to be devoured" → "to the birds of prey, birds of every wing, and to the living things of the field I have given you for food"** — לְעֵיט "to the birds of prey" (עַיִט, its row), צִפּוֹר כָּל־כָּנָף "birds of every wing" (also v17); חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה "the living things of the field" (the חַיָּה row; also v17); נְתַתִּיךָ, a perfect, "I have given you" (the thing is as good as done); לְאָכְלָה "for food".
- **"Thou shalt fall upon the open field" → "On the face of the field you shall fall"** (v5) — עַל־פְּנֵי הַשָּׂדֶה. **"for I have spoken it" → "for I have spoken"** — "it" was supplied; אֲנִי written out.
- **"among them that dwell carelessly in the isles" → "on those who dwell in the coastlands in security"** (v6) — אִיִּים "coastlands" (its row); לָבֶטַח "in security" (as 38:8).
- **"So will I make my holy name known" → "And my holy name I will make known"** (v7) — שֵׁם קָדְשִׁי, literally "the name of my holiness" (the קֹדֶשׁ genitive row: "holy"; also v25). **"and I will not let them pollute my holy name any more" → "and I will not profane my holy name any more"** — אַחֵל, hifil of חלל "profane" (as the הֵחֵל "profane one's word" row): Yahweh is the subject; "let them" was supplied (the sense: he will no longer let his name be dragged down by his people's fate). **"the Holy One in Israel" → "holy in Israel"** — קָדוֹשׁ בְּיִשְׂרָאֵל, an adjective without the article (not the title קְדוֹשׁ יִשְׂרָאֵל "the Holy One of Israel").
- **"Behold, it is come, and it is done" → "Behold, it is coming, and it shall be done"** (v8) — בָאָה, a participle or a perfect (the form is the same); וְנִהְיָתָה, a weqatal: "and it shall be done" (or "it has come and it has been done", KJV). **"this is the day whereof I have spoken" → "that is the day of which I have spoken"** — הוּא "that".
- **"and shall set on fire and burn the weapons, both the shields and the bucklers" → "and kindle and burn weapons, shield and large shield"** (v9) — וּבִעֲרוּ וְהִשִּׂיקוּ בְּנֶשֶׁק: בִּעֵר "kindle" (the בָּעַר row, of fire), הִשִּׂיק "burn, set alight"; נֶשֶׁק "weapons"; מָגֵן וְצִנָּה "shield and large shield" (as 38:4, the order reversed). **"the handstaves, and the spears" → "hand staff and spear"** — מַקֵּל יָד "staff of the hand" (a club or a javelin: uncertain); רֹמַח "spear". **"and they shall burn them with fire seven years" → "and they shall kindle fire with them seven years"** — וּבִעֲרוּ בָהֶם אֵשׁ.
- **"So that they shall take no wood out of the field, neither cut down any out of the forests" → "And they shall not carry wood from the field, and shall not hew any from the forests"** (v10) — נָשָׂא "carry"; חָטַב "hew" (the חֹטֵב "hewer" root). **"and they shall spoil those that spoiled them, and rob those that robbed them" → "and they shall spoil those who spoiled them, and plunder those who plundered them"** — שָׁלַל and בָּזַז (as 38:12).
- **"I will give unto Gog a place there of graves in Israel" → "I will give to Gog a place there, a grave in Israel"** (v11) — מְקוֹם־שָׁם קֶבֶר, literally "a place of there, a grave" (= a place where a grave is; the construction is unusual); קֶבֶר "grave" singular (its row). **"the valley of the passengers on the east of the sea" → "the valley of the passers-by, east of the sea"** — גֵּי הָעֹבְרִים, "those who pass over / by" (עָבַר, its row); the same participle stands in v11b, 14, 15 ("the passers-through": the play is in the Hebrew). Many take it as the region Abarim, east of the Dead Sea (Num 27:12; 33:47; the same letters): uncertain, so the word is translated. קִדְמַת הַיָּם "east of the sea" (the Directions row: קֶדֶם "front"). **"and it shall stop the noses of the passengers" → "and it shall stop the passers-by"** — וְחֹסֶמֶת הִיא אֶת־הָעֹבְרִים, literally "and it muzzles / blocks the passers-by" ("noses" is not written; whether the valley is blocked by the dead or the passers-by are kept back is not said). **"all his multitude"** — הֲמוֹנֹה (the הָמוֹן row). **"and they shall call it The valley of Hamon-gog"** — הֲמוֹן גּוֹג, "the multitude of Gog" (token).
- **"And seven months shall the house of Israel be burying of them, that they may cleanse the land" → "And the house of Israel shall bury them, in order to cleanse the land, seven months"** (v12) — the Hebrew order; לְמַעַן + infinitive "in order to"; טִהַר "cleanse" (its row; also v14, 16).
- **"Yea, all the people of the land shall bury them; and it shall be to them a renown" → "And all the people of the land shall bury, and it shall be to them for a name"** (v13) — no object ("them" supplied before); לְשֵׁם "for a name" (= renown, said here). **"the day that I shall be glorified" → "the day that I am glorified"** — הִכָּבְדִי, nifal of כָּבֵד (root of כָּבוֹד "glory").
- **"And they shall sever out men of continual employment" → "And men for continual duty they shall set apart"** (v14) — אַנְשֵׁי תָמִיד, literally "men of continually" (the תָּמִיד row): "duty" is supplied, because English cannot stand without a noun; הִבְדִּיל "set apart". **"passing through the land to bury with the passengers those that remain upon the face of the earth" → "passing through the land, burying, with the passers-through, those who remain on the face of the land"** — מְקַבְּרִים אֶת־הָעֹבְרִים אֶת־הַנּוֹתָרִים: the first אֶת is taken as "with" (KJV); taken as the object marker it says "burying the passers-by, those who remain" (the dead invaders called "passers"); both are possible; נוֹתָר "remain" (its row). **"after the end of seven months shall they search" → "at the end of seven months they shall search out"** — מִקְצֵה; חָקַר "search out" (the reviewer's row).
- **"And the passengers that pass through the land, when any seeth a man's bone" → "And the passers-through shall pass through the land, and when one sees a bone of a man"** (v15) — וְעָבְרוּ הָעֹבְרִים, verb and participle of one root; עֶצֶם אָדָם "a bone of a man" (אָדָם "man, mankind"). **"then shall he set up a sign by it" → "he shall build a marker beside it"** — בָּנָה "build"; צִיּוּן "marker" (its row, as 2 Kgs 23:17); אֵצֶל "beside" (its row).
- **"And also the name of the city shall be Hamonah" → "And also the name of a city is Hamonah"** (v16) — שֶׁם־עִיר הֲמוֹנָה, no verb, no article on "city"; הֲמוֹנָה "multitude" (feminine of הָמוֹן): the meaning of the clause in its place is not clear. **"Thus shall they cleanse the land" → "And they shall cleanse the land"** — וְטִהֲרוּ.
- **"And, thou son of man" → "And you, son of man"** (v17). **"Speak unto every feathered fowl" → "Say to the birds of every wing"** — אֱמֹר "say"; צִפּוֹר כָּל־כָּנָף (as v4). **"gather yourselves on every side to my sacrifice that I do sacrifice for you, even a great sacrifice" → "gather from all around to my sacrifice that I am sacrificing for you, a great sacrifice"** — זֶבַח "sacrifice" (its row; not "feast"), זֹבֵחַ participle; הִקָּבְצוּ … הֵאָסְפוּ "assemble … gather" (two verbs: קִבֵּץ, אָסַף).
- **"Ye shall eat the flesh of the mighty, and drink the blood of the princes of the earth" → "The flesh of mighty ones you shall eat, and the blood of the chieftains of the earth you shall drink"** (v18) — the Hebrew order; גִּבּוֹרִים "mighty ones" (its row); נְשִׂיאִים "chieftains" (the נָשִׂיא row; as 38:2). **"of rams, of lambs, and of goats, of bullocks, all of them fatlings of Bashan" → "rams, lambs and he-goats, bulls, fatlings of Bashan all of them"** — the animals stand beside the men without "of": the slain are named as sacrificial animals; עַתּוּדִים "he-goats" (its row); פָּרִים "bulls"; מְרִיאִים "fatlings" (its row).
- **"till ye be full … till ye be drunken" → "to the full … to drunkenness"** (v19) — לְשָׂבְעָה (the "to the full" row), לְשִׁכָּרוֹן (the שָׁכַר row: "be drunk"). **"of my sacrifice which I have sacrificed for you" → "from my sacrifice that I have sacrificed for you"** — זָבַחְתִּי, a perfect.
- **"Thus ye shall be filled at my table with horses and chariots, with mighty men, and with all men of war" → "And you shall be sated at my table with horse and chariots, mighty one and every man of war"** (v20) — שָׂבַע "be sated" (its row); סוּס וָרֶכֶב, both singular collectives (the רֶכֶב row: "chariots"); גִּבּוֹר "mighty one"; אִישׁ מִלְחָמָה "man of war" (its row).
- **"And I will set my glory among the heathen" → "And I will set my glory among the nations"** (v21) — נָתַתִּי "give, set"; גּוֹיִם "nations". **"my judgment that I have executed" → "my judgment that I have done"** — עָשִׂיתִי "do, make". **"and my hand that I have laid upon them"** — שַׂמְתִּי "put".
- **"from that day and forward" → "from that day and onward"** (v22) — וָהָלְאָה.
- **"went into captivity for their iniquity" → "for their iniquity … went into exile"** (v23) — בַעֲוֹנָם first; גָּלוּ "went into exile" (the גָּלָה qal row). **"because they trespassed against me" → "because they acted unfaithfully against me"** — מָעַל בְּ (the מָעַל row). **"therefore hid I my face from them" → "and I hid my face from them"** — וָאַסְתִּר, a wayyiqtol: "and" (also v24). **"into the hand of their enemies" → "into the hand of their adversaries"** — צָרִים "adversaries" (אֹיְבִים "enemies" v27).
- **"According to their uncleanness and according to their transgressions have I done unto them" → "… I dealt with them"** (v24) — עָשִׂיתִי אֹתָם, literally "I did them" (= dealt with them); טֻמְאָה "uncleanness".
- **"Now will I bring again the captivity of Jacob" → "Now I will restore the fortunes of Jacob"** (v25) — אָשִׁיב אֶת־שְׁבוּת, the reviewer's row (literally "turn back the captivity / the turning"); written שבית, read (qere) שְׁבוּת: the same phrase. **"and have mercy upon the whole house of Israel" → "and have compassion on the whole house of Israel"** — רִחַם (the רִחַם row: "have compassion"). **"and will be jealous for my holy name"** — קִנֵּא לְ (the קִנֵּא row).
- **"After that they have borne their shame, and all their trespasses" → "And they shall bear their dishonor and all their unfaithfulness"** (v26) — MT וְנָשׂוּ, a weqatal of נָשָׂא "bear": "and they shall bear". The common reading "they shall forget" (וְנָשׁוּ, from נָשָׁה "forget", the same letters with another point) is not the MT: the MT is kept. כְּלִמָּה "dishonor" (the reviewer's row); מַעַל … מָעֲלוּ "unfaithfulness … committed", one root (the מָעַל מַעַל row). **"when they dwelt safely in their land" → "when they dwell on their ground in security"** — בְּשִׁבְתָּם עַל־אַדְמָתָם לָבֶטַח. **"and none made them afraid" → "and none makes them tremble"** — מַחֲרִיד (as Jer 46:27: "one who makes tremble").
- **"When I have brought them again from the people" → "when I bring them back from the peoples"** (v27) — בְּשׁוֹבְבִי (שׁוּב polel, as 38:8); עַמִּים plural. **"and am sanctified in them in the sight of many nations" → "and I am sanctified through them before the eyes of the many nations"** — נִקְדַּשְׁתִּי בָם (as 38:16); לְעֵינֵי הַגּוֹיִם רַבִּים, the article on "nations" only (literally "the nations, many").
- **"which caused them to be led into captivity among the heathen" → "when I sent them into exile to the nations"** (v28) — בְּהַגְלוֹתִי, an infinitive with בְּ "in my making them go into exile" (the הִגְלָה row); אֶל "to". **"but I have gathered them unto their own land" → "and gathered them to their ground"** — וְכִנַּסְתִּים (כָּנַס "gather in"); אַדְמָתָם "their ground". **"and have left none of them any more there" → "and I will leave none of them there any more"** — אוֹתִיר, an imperfect.
- **"for I have poured out my spirit upon the house of Israel"** (v29) — אֲשֶׁר "for, in that"; שָׁפַכְתִּי, a perfect, "I have poured out" (the שָׁפַךְ row); רוּחִי lower case, as 37:14 ("when I pour out", the earlier wording, made the perfect a future).

### Notes
- **v1–16** — the end of Gog: fall on the mountains (v1–6), the weapons burned (v9–10), the burial in the valley (v11–16); v17–20 turn the slain into Yahweh's "sacrifice" for birds and living things (as Isa 34:6; Jer 46:10; Rev 19:17–18).
- **v21–29** — the close of the Gog chapters looks back on the exile (v23–24) and forward to the return (v25–29).
