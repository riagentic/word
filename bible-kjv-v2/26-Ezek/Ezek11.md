# Ezekiel 11

[1] The Spirit lifted me up and brought me to the east gate of the house of Yahweh, which faces east. And behold, at the entrance of the gateway there were twenty-five men. I saw among them Jaazaniah son of Azzur, and Pelatiah son of Benaiah, princes of the people.

[2] And he said to me, "Son of man, these are the men who devise iniquity and who give wicked counsel in this city;

[3] who say, 'The time is not near to build houses. This city is the cauldron, and we are the flesh.'

[4] Therefore prophesy against them, prophesy, O son of man."

[5] And the Spirit of Yahweh fell upon me, and he said to me, "Say, Thus says Yahweh: So you think, O house of Israel. For I know the things that come into your mind.

[6] You have multiplied your slain in this city and have filled its streets with the slain.

[7] Therefore thus says Lord Yahweh: Your slain whom you have laid in the midst of it, they are the flesh, and this city is the cauldron, but you shall be brought out of the midst of it.

[8] You have feared the sword, and I will bring the sword upon you, declares Lord Yahweh.

[9] And I will bring you out of the midst of it, and give you into the hands of foreigners, and execute judgments upon you.

[10] You shall fall by the sword. I will judge you at the border of Israel, and you shall know that I am Yahweh.

[11] This city shall not be your cauldron, nor shall you be the flesh in the midst of it. I will judge you at the border of Israel,

[12] and you shall know that I am Yahweh. For you have not walked in my statutes, nor obeyed my rules, but have acted according to the rules of the nations that are around you."

[13] And it came to pass, while I was prophesying, that Pelatiah son of Benaiah died. Then I fell down on my face and cried out with a loud voice and said, "Ah, Lord Yahweh! Will you make a full end of the remnant of Israel?"

[14] And the word of Yahweh came to me:

[15] "Son of man, your brothers, even your brothers, your kinsmen, the whole house of Israel, all of them, are those of whom the inhabitants of Jerusalem have said, 'They have gone far from Yahweh; to us this land is given for a possession.'

[16] Therefore say, 'Thus says Lord Yahweh: Though I removed them far off among the nations, and though I scattered them among the countries, yet I have been to them a sanctuary in small measure in the countries where they have gone.'

[17] Therefore say, 'Thus says Lord Yahweh: I will gather you from the peoples and assemble you out of the countries where you have been scattered, and I will give you the land of Israel.'

[18] And when they come there, they will remove from it all its detestable things and all its abominations.

[19] And I will give them one heart, and a new spirit I will put within them. I will remove the heart of stone from their flesh and give them a heart of flesh,

[20] that they may walk in my statutes and keep my rules and obey them. And they shall be my people, and I will be their *Elohim*.

[21] But as for those whose heart goes after their detestable things and their abominations, I will bring their deeds upon their own heads, declares Lord Yahweh."

[22] Then the cherubim lifted up their wings, with the wheels beside them, and the glory of the *Elohim* of Israel was over them.

[23] And the glory of Yahweh went up from the midst of the city and stood on the mountain that is on the east side of the city.

[24] And the Spirit lifted me up and brought me in the vision by the Spirit of *Elohim* into Chaldea, to the exiles. Then the vision that I had seen went up from me.

[25] And I told the exiles all the things that Yahweh had shown me.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (vv7, 8, 13, 16, 17, 21) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv20, 22, 24) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*; "their *Elohim*," "*Elohim* of Israel," "Spirit of *Elohim*."
- **"the Spirit of the LORD" → "the Spirit of Yahweh"** (v5) — Hebrew רוּחַ יְהוָה *Ruach Yahweh*.
- **"caldron" → "cauldron"** (vv3, 7, 11) — spelling modernized; the city-as-cauldron metaphor: flesh/meat boiling in a pot.
- **"have done after the manners of the heathen" → "acted according to the rules of the nations"** (v12) — Hebrew כְּמִשְׁפְּטֵי הַגּוֹיִם *kemishpetey haggoyim*; "nations" for *goyim*; "rules" for *mishpat*.
- **"a little sanctuary" → "a sanctuary in small measure"** (v16) — Hebrew מִקְדָּשׁ מְעַט *miqdash me'at*; the basis of the rabbinic concept of exilic synagogue as "small sanctuary."
- **"the captivity" → "the exiles"** (vv24, 25) — Hebrew גּוֹלָה *golah* = exile/exiles.

### Notes
- **v23** — *Kevod Yahweh* leaves temple and hovers over Mount of Olives (the "mountain east of the city").
- **v19-20** — first "heart of flesh" promise; parallel 36:26.
