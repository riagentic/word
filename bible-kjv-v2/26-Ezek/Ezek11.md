# Ezekiel 11

[1] The Spirit lifted me up and brought me to the east gate of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, which faces east. And behold, at the entrance of the gateway there were twenty-five men. I saw among them ⟦jaazaniah|Yaazanyah|Jaazaniah⟧ son of Azzur, and ⟦pelatiah|Pelatyah|Pelatiah⟧ son of ⟦benaiah|Benayahu|Benaiah⟧, princes of the people.

[2] And he said to me, "Son of man, these are the men who devise iniquity and who give wicked counsel in this city;

[3] who say, 'The time is not near to build houses. This city is the cauldron, and we are the flesh.'

[4] Therefore prophesy against them, prophesy, O son of man."

[5] And the Spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ fell upon me, and he said to me, "Say, Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: So you think, O house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧. For I know the things that come into your mind.

[6] You have multiplied your slain in this city and have filled its streets with the slain.

[7] Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Your slain whom you have laid in the midst of it, they are the flesh, and this city is the cauldron, but you shall be brought out of the midst of it.

[8] You have feared the sword, and I will bring the sword upon you, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[9] And I will bring you out of the midst of it, and give you into the hands of foreigners, and execute judgments upon you.

[10] You shall fall by the sword. I will judge you at the border of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[11] This city shall not be your cauldron, nor shall you be the flesh in the midst of it. I will judge you at the border of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[12] and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. For you have not walked in my statutes, nor obeyed my rules, but have acted according to the rules of the nations that are around you."

[13] And it came to pass, while I was prophesying, that ⟦pelatiah|Pelatyah|Pelatiah⟧ son of ⟦benaiah|Benayahu|Benaiah⟧ died. Then I fell down on my face and cried out with a loud voice and said, "Ah, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧! Will you make a full end of the remnant of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?"

[14] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me:

[15] "Son of man, your brothers, even your brothers, your kinsmen, the whole house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, all of them, are those of whom the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ have said, 'They have gone far from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; to us this land is given for a possession.'

[16] Therefore say, 'Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Though I removed them far off among the nations, and though I scattered them among the countries, yet I have been to them a sanctuary in small measure in the countries where they have gone.'

[17] Therefore say, 'Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: I will gather you from the peoples and assemble you out of the countries where you have been scattered, and I will give you the land of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.'

[18] And when they come there, they will remove from it all its detestable things and all its abominations.

[19] And I will give them one heart, and a new spirit I will put within them. I will remove the heart of stone from their flesh and give them a heart of flesh,

[20] that they may walk in my statutes and keep my rules and obey them. And they shall be my people, and I will be their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[21] But as for those whose heart goes after their detestable things and their abominations, I will bring their deeds upon their own heads, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

[22] Then the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ lifted up their wings, with the wheels beside them, and the glory of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ was over them.

[23] And the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ went up from the midst of the city and stood on the mountain that is on the east side of the city.

[24] And the Spirit lifted me up and brought me in the vision by the Spirit of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ into ⟦chaldea|Kasdim|Chaldea⟧, to the exiles. Then the vision that I had seen went up from me.

[25] And I told the exiles all the things that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had shown me.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (vv7, 8, 13, 16, 17, 21) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv20, 22, 24) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*; "their *Elohim*," "*Elohim* of Israel," "Spirit of *Elohim*."
- **"the Spirit of the LORD" → "the Spirit of Yahweh"** (v5) — Hebrew רוּחַ יְהוָה *Ruach Yahweh*.
- **"caldron" → "cauldron"** (vv3, 7, 11) — spelling modernized; the city-as-cauldron metaphor: flesh/meat boiling in a pot.
- **"have done after the manners of the heathen" → "acted according to the rules of the nations"** (v12) — Hebrew כְּמִשְׁפְּטֵי הַגּוֹיִם *kemishpetey haggoyim*; "nations" for *goyim*; "rules" for *mishpat*.
- **"a little sanctuary" → "a sanctuary in small measure"** (v16) — Hebrew מִקְדָּשׁ מְעַט *miqdash me'at*; the basis of the rabbinic concept of exilic synagogue as "small sanctuary."
- **"the captivity" → "the exiles"** (vv24, 25) — Hebrew גּוֹלָה *golah* = exile/exiles.

### Notes
- **v23** — *Kevod Yahweh* leaves temple and hovers over Mount of Olives (the "mountain east of the city").
- **v19-20** — first "heart of flesh" promise; parallel 36:26.
