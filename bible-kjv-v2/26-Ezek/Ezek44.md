# Ezekiel 44

[1] And he brought me back by the way of the outer gate of the sanctuary, which faces east; and it was shut.

[2] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, "This gate shall be shut; it shall not be opened, and no man shall come in by it; for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, has come in by it; and it shall be shut.

[3] The chieftain — a chieftain is he — he shall sit in it to eat bread before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; by the way of the porch of the gate he shall come in, and by its way he shall go out."

[4] And he brought me by the way of the north gate to the face of the house; and I looked, and behold, the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ filled the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and I fell on my face.

[5] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, "Son of man, set your heart, and see with your eyes, and hear with your ears all that I speak to you concerning all the statutes of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and concerning all its ⟦torah|torot|laws⟧; and set your heart to the entry of the house with all the goings out of the sanctuary.

[6] And you shall say to rebellion, to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Enough for you of all your abominations, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[7] in your bringing in foreigners, uncircumcised of heart and uncircumcised of flesh, to be in my sanctuary, to profane it, my house, when you bring near my bread, fat and blood; and they broke my covenant, in addition to all your abominations.

[8] And you have not kept the charge of my holy things, and you have set keepers of my charge in my sanctuary for yourselves.

[9] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: No foreigner, uncircumcised of heart and uncircumcised of flesh, shall come into my sanctuary, of any foreigner who is in the midst of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[10] But the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ who went far from me when ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went astray, who went astray from me after their idols — they shall bear their iniquity.

[11] And they shall be ministers in my sanctuary, with oversight at the gates of the house, and ministering to the house; they shall slaughter the burnt offering and the sacrifice for the people, and they shall stand before them to minister to them.

[12] Because they ministered to them before their idols, and became to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ a stumbling block of iniquity, therefore I have lifted up my hand against them, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, and they shall bear their iniquity.

[13] And they shall not draw near to me to be priest to me, nor draw near to any of my holy things, to the most holy things; and they shall bear their dishonor and their abominations that they did.

[14] And I will make them keepers of the charge of the house, for all its service and for all that is done in it.

[15] But **the priests, the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, the sons of ⟦zadok|Tzadoq|Zadok⟧**, who kept the charge of my sanctuary when the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went astray from me, they shall come near to me to minister to me; and they shall stand before me to bring near to me fat and blood, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[16] They shall come into my sanctuary, and they shall come near to my table to minister to me, and they shall keep my charge.

[17] And it shall be, when they come into the gates of the inner court, they shall put on garments of linen; and no wool shall come up on them while they minister in the gates of the inner court and within.

[18] Headdresses of linen shall be on their heads, and breeches of linen shall be on their loins; they shall not gird themselves with sweat.

[19] And when they go out to the outer court, to the outer court, to the people, they shall strip off their garments in which they minister and lay them up in the holy chambers; and they shall put on other garments, and they shall not sanctify the people with their garments.

[20] And their head they shall not shave, and loose hair they shall not let go free; they shall surely trim their heads.

[21] And wine no priest shall drink when they come into the inner court.

[22] And a widow or a divorced woman they shall not take for themselves as wives; but virgins of the seed of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, or the widow who is a widow from a priest, they may take.

[23] And my people they shall teach between holy and common, and between unclean and clean they shall make them know.

[24] And over a case they shall stand for judgment; by my judgments they shall judge it. And my ⟦torah|torot|laws⟧ and my statutes they shall keep in all my appointed times, and my ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ they shall sanctify.

[25] And to a dead man he shall not come, to become unclean; but for father and for mother, and for son and for daughter, for brother and for a sister who has not been a man's, they may defile themselves.

[26] And after his cleansing they shall number seven days for him.

[27] And on the day of his coming into the Holy, into the inner court, to minister in the Holy, he shall bring near his sin offering, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[28] And it shall be to them for an inheritance: I am their inheritance; and a possession you shall not give them in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: I am their possession.

[29] The grain offering and the sin offering and the guilt offering, they shall eat them; and every devoted thing in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall be theirs.

[30] And the first of all the firstfruits of all, and every contribution of all, of all your contributions, shall be for the priests; and the first of your doughs you shall give to the priest, to make a blessing rest on your house.

[31] Any carcass or torn animal, of the birds or of the beasts, the priests shall not eat.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v6, 9, 12, 15, 27) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2) — אֱלֹהֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept, also where the Hebrew puts the object first ("And wine no priest shall drink", v20–24).
- **"the gate of the outward sanctuary" → "the outer gate of the sanctuary"** (v1) — שַׁעַר הַמִּקְדָּשׁ הַחִיצוֹן: the adjective goes with "gate". **"which looketh toward the east" → "which faces east"** — הַפֹּנֶה קָדִים.
- **"no man shall enter in by it" → "no man shall come in by it"** (v2) — וְאִישׁ לֹא־יָבֹא בוֹ (בּוֹא "come in", also "has come in"). **"therefore it shall be shut" → "and it shall be shut"** — וְהָיָה סָגוּר; "therefore" is not in the Hebrew. The gate stays shut because Yahweh came in by it (43:4).
- **"It is for the prince; the prince, he shall sit in it" → "The chieftain — a chieftain is he — he shall sit in it"** (v3) — Hebrew אֶת־הַנָּשִׂיא נָשִׂיא הוּא יֵשֶׁב־בּוֹ, word for word; the sentence is broken. נָשִׂיא "chieftain" (literally "one lifted up"), as Ezek 7:27; 12:10, 12 (the former text had "prince"; שַׂר is "prince"). **"to eat bread"** — לֶאֱכָול־לֶחֶם (the written form has an extra vav). **"by the way of the same" → "by its way"** — וּמִדַּרְכּוֹ.
- **"Then brought he me the way of the north gate before the house" → "And he brought me by the way of the north gate to the face of the house"** (v4) — אֶל־פְּנֵי הַבַּיִת. **"filled the house of the LORD"** — מָלֵא … אֶת־בֵּית יְהוָה (as 43:5; the former text had "temple").
- **"mark well" → "set your heart"** (v5) — שִׂים לִבְּךָ, twice in the verse (literal; = pay attention). **"behold with thine eyes, and hear with thine ears" → "see with your eyes, and hear with your ears"** — the Hebrew order of the second clause is "and with your ears hear". **"all that I say unto thee" → "all that I speak to you"** — מְדַבֵּר, participle of דִּבֶּר. **"ordinances" → "statutes"** — חֻקּוֹת. **"all the laws thereof" → "all its torot"** — qere תּוֹרֹתָיו; the written form תורתו is singular. **"the entering in of the house" → "the entry of the house"** — לִמְבוֹא; מוֹצָאֵי "goings out".
- **"to the rebellious" → "to rebellion"** (v6) — Hebrew אֶל־מֶרִי, the noun (as 2:7): the house is called "rebellion". **"O ye house of Israel, let it suffice you of all your abominations" → "Enough for you of all your abominations, house of Israel"** — רַב־לָכֶם (as Num 16:3; Deut 1:6), and the vocative stands at the end, without "O".
- **"In that ye have brought into my sanctuary strangers" → "in your bringing in foreigners"** (v7) — Hebrew בַּהֲבִיאֲכֶם בְּנֵי־נֵכָר, literally "sons of a foreign land" (as Gen 17:12, "foreigner"; also v9). **"uncircumcised in heart, and uncircumcised in flesh" → "uncircumcised of heart and uncircumcised of flesh"** — עַרְלֵי־לֵב וְעַרְלֵי בָשָׂר. **"to pollute it, even my house" → "to profane it, my house"** — לְחַלְּלוֹ (חִלֵּל "profane"). **"when ye offer my bread" → "when you bring near my bread"** — הַקְרִיב "bring near" (of an offering "offer"; also v15, 27); לֶחֶם "bread". **"and they have broken my covenant" → "and they broke my covenant"** — Hebrew וַיָּפֵרוּ, "they": the foreigners, or the house. The former text had "You have broken", the reading of the Septuagint and other versions (from memory); not followed. **"because of all your abominations" → "in addition to all your abominations"** — אֶל כָּל־תּוֹעֲבוֹתֵיכֶם, literally "to all".
- **"the charge" kept** (v8) — מִשְׁמֶרֶת, what is to be kept (also v14–16). **"but ye have set keepers of my charge" → "and you have set keepers of my charge"** — וַתְּשִׂימוּן לְשֹׁמְרֵי מִשְׁמַרְתִּי; who is set is not said (the foreigners of v7 are meant). **"for yourselves"** — לָכֶם, at the end.
- **"No stranger … of any stranger that is among the children of Israel" → "No foreigner … of any foreigner who is in the midst of the sons of Israel"** (v9) — בֶּן־נֵכָר twice; בְּתוֹךְ "in the midst of".
- **"And the Levites that are gone away far from me" → "But the Levites who went far from me"** (v10) — Hebrew כִּי אִם־הַלְוִיִּם: "but" after the negative of v9. **"when Israel went astray, which went astray away from me after their idols"** — the order of the Hebrew is kept. גִּלּוּלִים "idols" (also v12). **"shall bear their iniquity"** — וְנָשְׂאוּ עֲוֺנָם (נָשָׂא עָוֹן: the guilt and what it brings; the former text had "punishment"; also v12).
- **"Yet they shall be ministers" → "And they shall be ministers"** (v11) — וְהָיוּ; מְשָׁרְתִים "ministers" (שֵׁרֵת "minister to", also v12, 15–17, 19, 27). **"having charge at the gates" → "with oversight at the gates"** — פְּקֻדּוֹת, plural of פְּקֻדָּה "oversight" (Num 3:32). **"ministering to the house"** — מְשָׁרְתִים אֶת־הַבָּיִת. **"they shall slay" → "they shall slaughter"** — יִשְׁחֲטוּ. **"before them"** — לִפְנֵיהֶם, the people.
- **"caused the house of Israel to fall into iniquity" → "became to the house of Israel a stumbling block of iniquity"** (v12) — Hebrew וְהָיוּ לְבֵית־יִשְׂרָאֵל לְמִכְשׁוֹל עָוֺן (as 7:19; 14:3). **"therefore have I lifted up mine hand against them" → "therefore I have lifted up my hand against them"** — נָשָׂאתִי יָדִי עֲלֵיהֶם: the lifted hand is the gesture of an oath (20:5–6, 15, 23); here with עַל "against, upon": an oath against them, or a hand raised to strike. The former text had "I have sworn concerning them".
- **"to do the office of a priest unto me" → "to be priest to me"** (v13) — לְכַהֵן לִי. **"nor to come near to any of my holy things, in the most holy place" → "nor draw near to any of my holy things, to the most holy things"** — אֶל־קָדְשֵׁי הַקְּדָשִׁים, literally "the holy things of the holy things"; "place" is not in the Hebrew. נָגַשׁ "draw near" (twice). **"their shame" → "their dishonor"** — כְּלִמָּתָם. **"which they have committed" → "that they did"** — עָשׂוּ.
- **"But I will make them" → "And I will make them"** (v14) — וְנָתַתִּי אוֹתָם, literally "I will give them (as)". **"for all the service thereof"** — עֲבֹדָתוֹ.
- **"the priests the Levites" → "the priests, the Levites"** (v15) — הַכֹּהֲנִים הַלְוִיִּם, two nouns side by side, as Deut 17:9; 18:1 and 43:19 ("the Levitical priests" in the ESV and the former text). **"the children of Israel" → "the sons of Israel"** — בְּנֵי. **"to offer unto me the fat and the blood" → "to bring near to me fat and blood"** — חֵלֶב וָדָם, without the article (also v7).
- **"they shall come near to my table"** (v16) — יִקְרְבוּ אֶל־שֻׁלְחָנִי (the table of 41:22).
- **"And it shall come to pass, that when they enter in" → "And it shall be, when they come into"** (v17) — וְהָיָה. **"they shall be clothed with linen garments" → "they shall put on garments of linen"** — בִּגְדֵי פִשְׁתִּים יִלְבָּשׁוּ: פִּשְׁתִּים "linen" (the word is "flax"; also v18). **"no wool shall come upon them" → "no wool shall come up on them"** — וְלֹא־יַעֲלֶה עֲלֵיהֶם צֶמֶר. **"and within"** — וָבָיְתָה, literally "and housewards".
- **"linen bonnets" → "Headdresses of linen"** (v18) — פַּאֲרֵי, plural of פְּאֵר "headdress" (Exod 39:28). **"linen breeches upon their loins"** — מִכְנְסֵי … עַל־מָתְנֵיהֶם. **"with any thing that causeth sweat" → "with sweat"** — Hebrew בַּיָּזַע, יֶזַע found only here, literally "in sweat"; "with what causes sweat" is the common explanation.
- **"into the utter court, even into the utter court" → "to the outer court, to the outer court"** (v19) — the Hebrew repeats אֶל־הֶחָצֵר הַחִיצוֹנָה; the Septuagint has it once (from memory). **"they shall put off" → "they shall strip off"** — יִפְשְׁטוּ. **"lay them" → "lay them up"** — הִנִּיחוּ (as 42:14). **"they shall not sanctify the people with their garments"** — וְלֹא־יְקַדְּשׁוּ אֶת־הָעָם (קִדֵּשׁ "sanctify": the holiness of the garments is not to pass to the people; the former text had "transmit holiness").
- **"Neither shall they shave their heads" → "And their head they shall not shave"** (v20) — וְרֹאשָׁם, singular; at the end of the verse רָאשֵׁיהֶם "their heads", plural. **"nor suffer their locks to grow long" → "and loose hair they shall not let go free"** — Hebrew וּפֶרַע לֹא יְשַׁלֵּחוּ: פֶּרַע "loose hair" (Num 6:5), שִׁלַּח "let go"; "to grow long" is the sense commonly taken. **"they shall only poll their heads" → "they shall surely trim their heads"** — כָּסוֹם יִכְסְמוּ, literally "trimming they shall trim".
- **"Neither shall any priest drink wine, when they enter" → "And wine no priest shall drink when they come"** (v21) — the plural "they" after "no priest" is the Hebrew's.
- **"for their wives" → "for themselves as wives"** (v22) — לָהֶם לְנָשִׁים. **"her that is put away" → "a divorced woman"** — גְּרוּשָׁה, literally "one driven out". **"maidens" → "virgins"** — בְּתוּלֹת. **"or a widow that had a priest before" → "or the widow who is a widow from a priest"** — וְהָאַלְמָנָה אֲשֶׁר תִּהְיֶה אַלְמָנָה מִכֹּהֵן: widowed by a priest. **"they may take"** — יִקָּחוּ, at the end of the verse.
- **"the difference between the holy and profane" → "between holy and common"** (v23) — Hebrew יוֹרוּ בֵּין קֹדֶשׁ לְחֹל, without "the difference" and without the article (as 42:20; 22:26). **"cause them to discern" → "they shall make them know"** — יוֹדִעֻם.
- **"in controversy they shall stand in judgment" → "over a case they shall stand for judgment"** (v24) — Hebrew וְעַל־רִיב … לְמִשְׁפָּט (qere; the written form לשפט "to judge"): רִיב in law "case". **"they shall judge it"** — qere יִשְׁפְּטֻהוּ; the written form ושפטהו "and they shall judge it". **"in all mine assemblies" → "in all my appointed times"** — מוֹעֲדַי (מוֹעֵד "appointed time"). **"they shall hallow my sabbaths" → "my Shabbatot they shall sanctify"** — יְקַדֵּשׁוּ.
- **"they shall come at no dead person to defile themselves" → "to a dead man he shall not come, to become unclean"** (v25) — Hebrew וְאֶל־מֵת אָדָם לֹא יָבוֹא לְטָמְאָה: the verb is singular here ("he", each priest), plural in the second half; מֵת אָדָם "a dead one of mankind". **"sister that hath had no husband" → "a sister who has not been a man's"** — אֲשֶׁר־לֹא־הָיְתָה לְאִישׁ (= unmarried). **"they may defile themselves"** — יִטַּמָּאוּ, hitpael.
- **"after he is cleansed" → "after his cleansing"** (v26) — אַחֲרֵי טָהֳרָתוֹ. **"they shall reckon unto him" → "they shall number … for him"** — יִסְפְּרוּ־לוֹ.
- **"in the day that he goeth into the sanctuary" → "on the day of his coming into the Holy"** (v27) — בְּיוֹם בֹּאוֹ אֶל־הַקֹּדֶשׁ (twice "the Holy", as 42:14). **"he shall offer" → "he shall bring near"** — יַקְרִיב.
- **"And it shall be unto them for an inheritance" kept** (v28) — וְהָיְתָה לָהֶם לְנַחֲלָה: "it" has no named subject. The former text had "This shall be their inheritance"; the Vulgate reads "they shall have no inheritance" (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"the meat offering … the trespass offering" → "the grain offering … the guilt offering"** (v29) — מִנְחָה, אָשָׁם. **"they shall eat them"** — יֹאכְלוּם. **"every dedicated thing" → "every devoted thing"** — חֵרֶם, given to Yahweh beyond recall (Lev 27:28; Num 18:14).
- **"every oblation of all, of every sort of your oblations" → "every contribution of all, of all your contributions"** (v30) — תְּרוּמָה "contribution" (literally "what is lifted off"). **"shall be the priest's" → "shall be for the priests"** — לַכֹּהֲנִים, plural; the next clause has "the priest", singular. **"the first of your dough" → "the first of your doughs"** — עֲרִסוֹתֵיכֶם, plural (the word is uncertain: "dough" or "coarse meal"). **"that he may cause the blessing to rest in thine house" → "to make a blessing rest on your house"** — לְהָנִיחַ בְּרָכָה אֶל־בֵּיתֶךָ: no subject; "your" is singular here, after the plurals.
- **"any thing that is dead of itself, or torn" → "Any carcass or torn animal"** (v31) — נְבֵלָה וּטְרֵפָה (as Lev 22:8). **"fowl or beast" → "of the birds or of the beasts"** — מִן־הָעוֹף וּמִן־הַבְּהֵמָה.

### Notes
- **The shut east gate** (v1–3) — Yahweh's glory came in through it (43:1–5), so it stays shut; only the chieftain sits in its porch to eat before Yahweh. The walled-up "Golden Gate" of Jerusalem is often linked with this verse (later tradition).
- **Levites and the sons of Zadok** (v10–16) — the Levites who went astray keep the gates and slaughter for the people; the priests of the line of Zadok, who kept the charge, come near to minister. Compare 1 Sam 2:35; 1 Kgs 2:35.
