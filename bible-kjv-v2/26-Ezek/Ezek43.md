# Ezekiel 43

[1] And he made me walk to the gate, the gate that faces toward the east.

[2] And behold, the glory of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ was coming from the way of the east; and his voice was like the sound of many waters, and the earth shone from his glory.

[3] And like the appearance of the vision that I saw, like the vision that I saw when I came to destroy the city, and visions like the vision that I saw by the river ⟦chebar|Kevar|Chebar⟧; and I fell on my face.

[4] And the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came into the house by the way of the gate whose face was toward the east.

[5] And a spirit lifted me up and brought me into the inner court; and behold, the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ filled the house.

[6] And I heard one speaking to me from the house; and a man was standing beside me.

[7] And he said to me, "Son of man, the place of my throne and the place of the soles of my feet, where I will abide in the midst of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ forever! And the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall no more defile my holy name, they and their kings, by their prostitution and by the corpses of their kings, their high places,

[8] in their setting their threshold by my threshold and their doorpost beside my doorpost, and the wall between me and them; and they defiled my holy name by their abominations that they did, and I made an end of them in my anger.

[9] Now let them put their prostitution and the corpses of their kings far from me, and I will abide in their midst forever.

[10] "You, son of man, tell the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ the house, and let them be put to shame because of their iniquities; and let them measure the plan.

[11] And if they are put to shame because of all that they have done, the form of the house and its arrangement, and its goings out and its comings in, and all its forms, and all its statutes, and all its forms, and all its ⟦torah|torot|laws⟧, make known to them, and write it before their eyes, that they may keep all its form and all its statutes, and do them.

[12] This is the ⟦torah|torah|law⟧ of the house: on the top of the mountain all its border all around, all around is most holy. Behold, this is the ⟦torah|torah|law⟧ of the house.

[13] "And these are the measures of the altar in cubits — the cubit is a cubit and a handbreadth: and the bosom, a cubit, and a cubit the width, and its border on its edge around, one span; and this is the back of the altar.

[14] And from the bosom on the ground to the lower ledge, two cubits, and the width one cubit; and from the small ledge to the great ledge, four cubits, and the width the cubit.

[15] And the hearth, four cubits; and from the hearth and upward, the horns, four.

[16] And the hearth, twelve in length by twelve in width, square, to its four sides.

[17] And the ledge, fourteen in length by fourteen in width, to its four sides; and the border around it, half the cubit; and the bosom to it, a cubit around; and its steps face east."

[18] And he said to me, "Son of man, thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: These are the statutes of the altar on the day it is made, to offer up burnt offering on it and to dash blood on it.

[19] And you shall give to the priests, the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, who are of the seed of ⟦zadok|Tzadoq|Zadok⟧, who are near to me, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, to minister to me, a bull of the herd for a sin offering.

[20] And you shall take of its blood and put it on its four horns and on the four corners of the ledge and on the border around; and you shall purge it of sin and make atonement for it.

[21] And you shall take the bull of the sin offering, and he shall burn it in the appointed place of the house, outside the sanctuary.

[22] And on the second day you shall bring near a male goat without blemish for a sin offering; and they shall purge the altar of sin, as they purged it of sin with the bull.

[23] When you have finished purging of sin, you shall bring near a bull of the herd without blemish and a ram from the flock without blemish.

[24] And you shall bring them near before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the priests shall throw salt on them, and they shall offer them up as a burnt offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[25] Seven days you shall offer a male goat of sin offering for each day; and a bull of the herd and a ram from the flock, without blemish, they shall offer.

[26] Seven days they shall make atonement for the altar and cleanse it, and they shall fill its hands.

[27] And they shall complete the days; and it shall be, on the eighth day and onward, the priests shall offer on the altar your burnt offerings and your peace offerings; and I will be pleased with you, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v18, 19, 27) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2) — אֱלֹהֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Afterward he brought me" → "And he made me walk"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיּוֹלִכֵנִי, hifil of הָלַךְ "walk", as Deut 8:2. **"that looketh toward the east" → "that faces toward the east"** — פֹּנֶה דֶּרֶךְ הַקָּדִים.
- **"came from the way of the east" → "was coming from the way of the east"** (v2) — בָּא, participle after הִנֵּה. **"his voice was like a noise of many waters" → "his voice was like the sound of many waters"** — וְקוֹלוֹ כְּקוֹל מַיִם רַבִּים: קוֹל is one word, "voice" of the One who comes, "sound" of the waters, as 1:24. **"shined with his glory" → "shone from his glory"** — מִכְּבֹדוֹ.
- **The return of the glory** (v1–5) — the wording follows the departure in 10:4, 18–19; 11:22–24 and the visions of 1:24, 28; 3:23 ("the glory of Yahweh", "the river Chebar", "I fell on my face", "a spirit lifted me up").
- **"And it was according to the appearance of the vision" → "And like the appearance of the vision"** (v3) — Hebrew וּכְמַרְאֵה הַמַּרְאֶה; no verb. מַרְאֶה (here "vision", as 11:24) and the plural מַרְאוֹת "visions" (of מַרְאָה) are two words of one root. **"when I came to destroy the city"** — בְּבֹאִי, "when I came", as the Masoretic Text has it: the prophet came to announce its destruction (Ezek 9). The former text had "when he came", which some manuscripts and versions read (from memory); not followed. **"the river Chebar"** — נְהַר־כְּבָר (as 10:15; the former text had "canal").
- **"the glory of the LORD came into the house" kept** (v4) — וּכְבוֹד יְהוָה בָּא אֶל־הַבָּיִת; בַּיִת "house" (the former text had "temple", which is הֵיכָל). **"whose prospect is toward the east" → "whose face was toward the east"** — אֲשֶׁר פָּנָיו.
- **"So the spirit took me up" → "And a spirit lifted me up"** (v5) — וַתִּשָּׂאֵנִי רוּחַ, without the article (as 11:24; רוּחַ is also "wind"). **"filled the house"** — מָלֵא, adjective or perfect.
- **"I heard him speaking unto me" → "I heard one speaking to me"** (v6) — Hebrew מִדַּבֵּר, pointed as a reflexive participle (as 2:2; Num 7:89: "making himself heard"); the speaker is not named. **"and the man stood by me" → "and a man was standing beside me"** — וְאִישׁ, without the article.
- **"the place of my throne … and my holy name, shall the house of Israel no more defile" → "the place of my throne and the place of the soles of my feet, where I will abide … forever! And the house of Israel shall no more defile my holy name"** (v7) — Hebrew אֶת־מְקוֹם כִּסְאִי וְאֶת־מְקוֹם כַּפּוֹת רַגְלַי: the place is marked as an object but has no verb ("this is", "see" are supplied by others); the sentence breaks off. **"dwell" → "abide"** — שָׁכַן (also v9). **"the children of Israel" → "the sons of Israel"** — בְּנֵי. **"neither they, nor their kings" → "they and their kings"** — הֵמָּה וּמַלְכֵיהֶם. **"whoredom" → "prostitution"** — זְנוּת (also v9).
- **"the carcases of their kings" → "the corpses of their kings"** (v7, 9) — Hebrew פִּגְרֵי מַלְכֵיהֶם, פֶּגֶר "dead body". Many take it here as "memorial stones / monuments" of the kings (a sense known from Ugaritic and Phoenician, from memory); the Hebrew word is kept. **"in their high places" → "their high places"** — Hebrew בָּמוֹתָם, as pointed "their high places", standing beside "corpses" without a preposition; read בְּמוֹתָם it is "at their death" (some manuscripts and the Targum, from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"In their setting of their threshold" kept** (v8) — בְּתִתָּם סִפָּם, literally "in their giving". **"their post by my posts" → "their doorpost beside my doorpost"** — singular both times. **"they have even defiled" → "and they defiled"** — וְטִמְּאוּ (טִמֵּא "defile"). **"that they have committed" → "that they did"** — עָשׂוּ. **"wherefore I have consumed them" → "and I made an end of them"** — וָאֲכַל, piel of כָּלָה "make an end of" (not אָכַל "consume"). **"in mine anger"** — בְּאַפִּי (אַף "nose", of anger).
- **"put away … far from me" → "put … far from me"** (v9) — יְרַחֲקוּ, jussive "let them put far".
- **"Thou son of man" → "You, son of man"** (v10) — אַתָּה בֶן־אָדָם. **"shew the house to the house of Israel" → "tell the house of Israel the house"** — הַגֵּד (הִגִּיד "tell"); the former text had "the temple", which is not the word. **"that they may be ashamed" → "and let them be put to shame"** — וְיִכָּלְמוּ (נִכְלַם "be put to shame", also v11). **"the pattern" → "the plan"** — תָּכְנִית, from תכן "measure, fix" (only here and 28:12): a measured design.
- **"shew them the form of the house" → "the form of the house … make known to them"** (v11) — Hebrew צוּרַת הַבַּיִת … הוֹדַע אוֹתָם: the list comes first, the verb after it, as in the Hebrew. **"and all the forms thereof … and all the forms thereof"** — "forms" stands twice in the Hebrew: the first is written צוּרֹתָו (a plural without the yod), the second has the written form צורתי and the qere צוּרֹתָיו. **"the laws thereof" → "its torot"** — qere תּוֹרֹתָיו "its instructions"; the written form תורתו is singular. תְּכוּנָה "arrangement" (root תכן, as תָּכְנִית v10). מוֹצָאָיו וּמוֹבָאָיו "its goings out and its comings in". **"in their sight" → "before their eyes"** — לְעֵינֵיהֶם. **"that they may keep the whole form thereof" → "that they may keep all its form"** — כָּל־צוּרָתוֹ.
- **"the law of the house" → "the torah of the house"** (v12) — Hebrew תּוֹרַת הַבָּיִת, the instruction for one matter (lower case, as Lev 6:9). **"the whole limit thereof round about" → "all its border all around, all around"** — גְּבֻלוֹ סָבִיב סָבִיב: גְּבוּל "border" (the land within it is meant), one word with the "border" of the altar (v13, 17, 20); סָבִיב סָבִיב kept double, the single סָבִיב "around" (v13, 17, 20). **"most holy"** — קֹדֶשׁ קָדָשִׁים, literally "holiness of holinesses".
- **The altar** (v13–17) — the words for its parts are rare and their sense is not certain; the plainest sense is kept: חֵיק "bosom" (v13, 14, 17: a hollow or base at the foot; the former text had "base"), עֲזָרָה "ledge" (v14, 17, 20: a step of the altar; in 2 Chr 4:9; 6:13 the same word is "court"), גְּבוּל "border" (v13, 17, 20: a rim), גַּב "back" (v13: the raised part; "height", "base" are guesses). No unit is written in v16, 17 ("twelve in length"); "cubits" is not supplied.
- **"after the cubits" → "in cubits"** (v13) — בָּאַמּוֹת. **"The cubit is a cubit and an hand breadth" kept** — אַמָּה אַמָּה וָטֹפַח (as 40:5). **"even the bottom shall be a cubit" → "and the bosom, a cubit"** — וְחֵיק הָאַמָּה, literally "the bosom, the cubit"; no "shall be" in the Hebrew. **"the border thereof by the edge thereof" → "its border on its edge"** — אֶל־שְׂפָתָהּ, שָׂפָה "lip, edge". **"this shall be the higher place of the altar" → "this is the back of the altar"** — וְזֶה גַּב הַמִּזְבֵּחַ.
- **"from the bottom upon the ground even to the lower settle" → "from the bosom on the ground to the lower ledge"** (v14) — מֵחֵיק הָאָרֶץ עַד־הָעֲזָרָה הַתַּחְתּוֹנָה. **"the lesser settle … the greater settle" → "the small ledge … the great ledge"** — הַקְּטַנָּה … הַגְּדוֹלָה. **"the breadth one cubit"** — the second time הָאַמָּה, "the cubit".
- **"So the altar … from the altar" → "the hearth … from the hearth"** (v15, 16) — the Hebrew has three spellings: הַהַרְאֵל (v15a; by its letters "the mountain of El"), הָאֲרִאֵיל (v15b), and v16 the written form והאראיל with the qere וְהָאֲרִיאֵל, the word of Isa 29:1–2 (there a name of Jerusalem, "Ariel"). The top of the altar, where the fire burns, is meant; "hearth" is the plainest sense and is used for all three. The former text had "altar hearth". **"shall be four horns" → "the horns, four"** — הַקְּרָנוֹת אַרְבַּע.
- **"square in the four squares thereof" → "square, to its four sides"** (v16) — רָבוּעַ אֶל אַרְבַּעַת רְבָעָיו (רֶבַע "a fourth, a side", as 10:11). **"twelve cubits long, twelve broad" → "twelve in length by twelve in width"** — no unit.
- **"the settle" → "the ledge"** (v17) — הָעֲזָרָה. **"half a cubit" → "half the cubit"** — חֲצִי הָאַמָּה. **"the bottom thereof shall be a cubit about" → "the bosom to it, a cubit around"** — וְהַחֵיק־לָהּ אַמָּה סָבִיב. **"his stairs shall look toward the east" → "its steps face east"** — מַעֲלֹתֵהוּ פְּנוֹת קָדִים (מַעֲלָה "step", Exod 20:26 forbids steps to an altar).
- **"These are the ordinances of the altar" → "These are the statutes of the altar"** (v18) — חֻקּוֹת (חֻקָּה "statute"). **"in the day when they shall make it" → "on the day it is made"** — בְּיוֹם הֵעָשׂוֹתוֹ, nifal. **"to offer burnt offerings thereon" → "to offer up burnt offering on it"** — לְהַעֲלוֹת עָלָיו עוֹלָה, literally "to make go up a going-up". **"to sprinkle blood" → "to dash blood"** — זָרַק (הִזָּה is "sprinkle").
- **"the priests the Levites" → "the priests, the Levites"** (v19) — הַכֹּהֲנִים הַלְוִיִּם, two nouns side by side, as Deut 17:9; 18:1 ("the Levitical priests" in the ESV and the former text). **"that be of the seed of Zadok" → "who are of the seed of Zadok"** — מִזֶּרַע צָדוֹק; "family" was not the word. **"which approach unto me" → "who are near to me"** — הַקְּרֹבִים אֵלַי, as 42:13. **"saith" → "declares"** — נְאֻם. The Hebrew order is kept: "declares Lord Yahweh" stands before "to minister to me". **"a young bullock" → "a bull of the herd"** — פַּר בֶּן־בָּקָר, literally "a bull, a son of the herd" (also v23, 25).
- **"on the four horns of it" → "on its four horns"** (v20) — קַרְנֹתָיו. **"on the four corners of the settle" → "of the ledge"** — פִּנּוֹת הָעֲזָרָה. **"thus shalt thou cleanse and purge it" → "and you shall purge it of sin and make atonement for it"** — Hebrew וְחִטֵּאתָ אוֹתוֹ וְכִפַּרְתָּהוּ: חִטֵּא piel "purge of sin" (literally "de-sin", as Exod 29:36; also v22, 23); כִּפֶּר "make atonement" (literally "cover"), here with a direct object.
- **"and he shall burn it in the appointed place of the house" kept** (v21) — וּשְׂרָפוֹ, "and he shall burn it" (the one who burns is not named). מִפְקַד הַבַּיִת, literally "the appointment of the house" (as 2 Chr 31:13; "the Muster Gate", Neh 3:31): a set place. **"without the sanctuary" → "outside the sanctuary"** — מִחוּץ לַמִּקְדָּשׁ.
- **"thou shalt offer" → "you shall bring near"** (v22, 23, 24) — תַּקְרִיב, hifil of קָרַב "come near" (of an offering "offer": one word). **"a kid of the goats" → "a male goat"** — שְׂעִיר־עִזִּים. **"without blemish"** — תָּמִים, literally "whole" (also v23, 25). **"and they shall cleanse the altar, as they did cleanse it with the bullock" → "and they shall purge the altar of sin, as they purged it of sin with the bull"** — חִטְּאוּ twice.
- **"When thou hast made an end of cleansing it" → "When you have finished purging of sin"** (v23) — בְּכַלּוֹתְךָ מֵחַטֵּא; "it" is not in the Hebrew. **"out of the flock" → "from the flock"** — מִן־הַצֹּאן.
- **"the priests shall cast salt upon them" → "the priests shall throw salt on them"** (v24) — הִשְׁלִיכוּ (הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw"). **"they shall offer them up for a burnt offering"** — וְהֶעֱלוּ אוֹתָם עֹלָה.
- **"thou shalt prepare every day a goat for a sin offering" → "you shall offer a male goat of sin offering for each day"** (v25) — Hebrew תַּעֲשֶׂה שְׂעִיר־חַטָּאת לַיּוֹם: עָשָׂה is "do, make" (of a sacrifice "offer", also v27); שְׂעִיר־חַטָּאת "a male goat of sin offering". **"they shall also prepare" → "they shall offer"** — יַעֲשׂוּ.
- **"Seven days shall they purge the altar and purify it" → "Seven days they shall make atonement for the altar and cleanse it"** (v26) — יְכַפְּרוּ … וְטִהֲרוּ (כִּפֶּר "make atonement", טִהֵר "make clean"). **"and they shall consecrate themselves" → "and they shall fill its hands"** — Hebrew וּמִלְאוּ יָדָיו (qere; the written form ידו "its hand"): "fill the hand" is the phrase for installing a priest (Exod 28:41); here of the altar, which is set apart for its service. "Consecrate" in Changes only.
- **"And when these days are expired" → "And they shall complete the days"** (v27) — וִיכַלּוּ אֶת־הַיָּמִים, a sentence of its own (the Masoretic Text has a section mark after it). **"it shall be, that upon the eighth day, and so forward" → "and it shall be, on the eighth day and onward"** — וְהָיָה … וָהָלְאָה. **"the priests shall make your burnt offerings" → "the priests shall offer … your burnt offerings"** — יַעֲשׂוּ (v25). **"I will accept you" → "I will be pleased with you"** — וְרָצִאתִי אֶתְכֶם (רָצָה of a person: "be pleased with").

### Notes
- **Return of the glory** (v1–5) — it reverses the departure of 10–11: the glory went out by the east gate (10:19) and stood on the mountain east of the city (11:23); it comes back from the east and fills the house. The gate is then shut (44:1–2).
- **The place of the throne** (v7) — Yahweh's throne and footstool in the house; compare Jer 3:17; 17:12; Ps 99:5; 132:7.
