# Ezekiel 28

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[2] "Son of man, say to the leader of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:

Because your heart is high,
and you have said, 'I am ⟦el|el|a god⟧;
in the seat of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ I sit,
in the heart of the seas,'
and you are man, and not ⟦el|el|god⟧,
and you have set your heart as the heart of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ —

[3] behold, you are wiser than ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧;
no hidden thing is dark to you;

[4] by your wisdom and by your understanding
you have made wealth for yourself,
and you have made gold and silver
in your treasuries;

[5] by the abundance of your wisdom, by your merchandise,
you have increased your wealth,
and your heart has become high in your wealth —

[6] therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:
Because you have set your heart
as the heart of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[7] therefore behold, I am bringing against you strangers,
the ruthless of the nations;
and they shall draw their swords against the beauty of your wisdom,
and profane your radiance.

[8] To the pit they shall bring you down,
and you shall die the deaths of the slain
in the heart of the seas.

[9] Will you surely say, 'I am ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,'
before the one who kills you?
And you are man, and not ⟦el|el|god⟧,
in the hand of those who pierce you.

[10] The deaths of the uncircumcised you shall die
by the hand of strangers;
for I have spoken, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

[11] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[12] "Son of man, lift up a dirge over the king of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, and say to him, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:

You were a sealer of pattern,
full of wisdom and perfect of beauty.

[13] In Eden, the garden of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you were;
every precious stone was your covering:
carnelian, topaz and diamond,
beryl, onyx and jasper,
sapphire, turquoise and emerald, and gold;
the work of your timbrels and your hollows was in you;
on the day you were created they were established.

[14] You were a ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ of anointing, the one who screens,
and I set you;
on the holy mountain of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ you were;
in the midst of stones of fire you walked about.

[15] Blameless you were in your ways
from the day you were created,
until injustice was found in you.

[16] In the abundance of your merchandise
they filled your midst with violence, and you sinned;
and I profaned you from the mountain of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and I made you perish, ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ that screens,
from the midst of the stones of fire.

[17] Your heart was high because of your beauty;
you ruined your wisdom on account of your radiance;
on the earth I cast you;
before kings I set you,
to look upon you.

[18] From the abundance of your iniquities,
in the injustice of your merchandise,
you profaned your sanctuaries;
and I brought out fire from your midst;
it has consumed you;
and I made you ashes on the earth
before the eyes of all who see you.

[19] All who know you among the peoples
are appalled over you;
terrors you have become,
and you shall be no more forever."

[20] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[21] "Son of man, set your face toward ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧, and prophesy against her,

[22] and say, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:

Behold, I am against you, ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧,
and I will be glorified in your midst;
and they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
when I execute judgments in her,
and am sanctified in her.

[23] And I will send into her pestilence,
and blood into her streets;
and the slain shall fall in her midst,
by the sword against her all around;
and they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[24] And there shall be no more for the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ a malignant brier or a paining thorn, from all around them who despise them; and they shall know that I am ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[25] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: When I gather the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from the peoples among whom they were scattered, and am sanctified in them before the eyes of the nations, then they shall dwell on their ground that I gave to my servant, to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧.

[26] And they shall dwell on it in security, and build houses and plant vineyards, and dwell in security, when I execute judgments on all who despise them from all around them; and they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v2, 6, 10, 12, 22, 24, 25) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both names.
- **"The word of the LORD came again unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v1, 11, 20) — Hebrew וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר; "again", "Moreover" are not in the Hebrew.
- **"say unto the prince of Tyrus … Because thine heart is lifted up" → "say to the leader of Tyre … Because your heart is high"** (v2) — Hebrew נְגִיד צֹר, נָגִיד "leader" (its row; not "prince": שַׂר; v12 has מֶלֶךְ "king"); גָּבַהּ לֵב "the heart is high" (also v5, 17).
- **"I am a God, I sit in the seat of God … yet thou art a man, and not God, though thou set thine heart as the heart of God" → "I am el; in the seat of Elohim I sit … and you are man, and not el, and you have set your heart as the heart of Elohim"** (v2) — Hebrew אֵל אָנִי, אֵל without article: "el" (as Isa 31:3 "man, and not el": the claim is to be a god; the word is also the name El); מוֹשַׁב אֱלֹהִים יָשַׁבְתִּי, the Hebrew order (מוֹשָׁב "seat", the noun of יָשַׁב "sit"); אֱלֹהִים "Elohim" each time in v2, 6, 9 (the one God; "gods", "a god" is the other reading: say so); וְאַתָּה … וַתִּתֵּן, two clauses with "and" (no "yet", "though"); נָתַן לֵב כְּ "set the heart as" (lit. "give"; also v6).
- **"Behold, thou art wiser than Daniel; there is no secret that they can hide from thee" → "behold, you are wiser than Daniel; no hidden thing is dark to you"** (v3) — Hebrew מִדָּנִיֵּאל "than Daniel", the qere (the written form מדנאל, *Dan'el*, as Ezek 14:14, 20: see the note there); כָּל־סָתוּם לֹא עֲמָמוּךָ, lit. "every hidden thing, they have not darkened you" (עָמַם "dim, darken").
- **"With thy wisdom and with thine understanding thou hast gotten thee riches, and hast gotten gold and silver into thy treasures" → "by your wisdom and by your understanding you have made wealth for yourself, and you have made gold and silver in your treasuries"** (v4) — Hebrew עָשָׂה "make" twice; חַיִל "wealth" (its row: of goods; also v5); אוֹצָר "treasury" (its row).
- **"By thy great wisdom and by thy traffick hast thou increased thy riches" → "by the abundance of your wisdom, by your merchandise, you have increased your wealth"** (v5) — Hebrew בְּרֹב חָכְמָתְךָ בִּרְכֻלָּתְךָ, no "and"; רְכֻלָּה "merchandise" (as 26:12; also v16, 18).
- **"Behold, therefore I will bring strangers upon thee, the terrible of the nations: and they shall draw their swords against the beauty of thy wisdom, and they shall defile thy brightness" → "therefore behold, I am bringing against you strangers, the ruthless of the nations; and they shall draw their swords against the beauty of your wisdom, and profane your radiance"** (v7) — Hebrew הִנְנִי מֵבִיא, a participle; זָרִים "strangers" (also v10); עָרִיצֵי גּוֹיִם "the ruthless of the nations" (its row); הֵרִיק "draw" (lit. "empty out", its row); חִלֵּל "profane" (its row; also v16, 18); יִפְעָה "radiance" (only here and v17).
- **"They shall bring thee down to the pit, and thou shalt die the deaths of them that are slain" → "To the pit they shall bring you down, and you shall die the deaths of the slain"** (v8) — Hebrew לַשַּׁחַת "to the pit" (שַׁחַת, its row; 26:20 has בּוֹר: English has one word); מְמוֹתֵי חָלָל, plural "deaths" (also v10); חָלָל "slain" (its row).
- **"Wilt thou yet say before him that slayeth thee, I am God? but thou shalt be a man, and no God, in the hand of him that slayeth thee" → "Will you surely say, 'I am Elohim,' before the one who kills you? And you are man, and not el, in the hand of those who pierce you"** (v9) — Hebrew הֶאָמֹר תֹּאמַר, the infinitive absolute with its verb, "surely say" (lit. "saying, will you say"); הֹרֶג "one who kills" (singular); מְחַלְלֶיךָ "those who pierce you" (plural; from חָלַל "pierce", the root of חָלָל "slain"; the same letters give "profane", v7, 16, 18).
- **"Thou shalt die the deaths of the uncircumcised by the hand of strangers: for I have spoken it" → "The deaths of the uncircumcised you shall die by the hand of strangers; for I have spoken"** (v10) — the Hebrew order; כִּי אֲנִי דִבַּרְתִּי "for I have spoken" (no "it").
- **"take up a lamentation upon the king of Tyrus" → "lift up a dirge over the king of Tyre"** (v12) — Hebrew נָשָׂא קִינָה עַל (as 27:2). **"Thou sealest up the sum, full of wisdom, and perfect in beauty" → "You were a sealer of pattern, full of wisdom and perfect of beauty"** — Hebrew אַתָּה חוֹתֵם תָּכְנִית: חוֹתֵם is pointed as a participle, "one who seals"; with other vowels, חוֹתָם, it would be "a seal, signet" (so ESV "the signet of perfection"); תָּכְנִית "pattern" (only here and 43:10; root תכן "measure", its row); a noun clause: the copula "were" is supplied (past, as v13–15); כְּלִיל יֹפִי "perfect of beauty" (as 27:3).
- **"Thou hast been in Eden the garden of God; every precious stone was thy covering, the sardius, topaz, and the diamond, the beryl, the onyx, and the jasper, the sapphire, the emerald, and the carbuncle, and gold" → "In Eden, the garden of Elohim, you were; every precious stone was your covering: carnelian, topaz and diamond, beryl, onyx and jasper, sapphire, turquoise and emerald, and gold"** (v13) — Hebrew בְּעֵדֶן גַּן־אֱלֹהִים הָיִיתָ, the Hebrew order; מְסֻכָה "covering" (only here; from סָכַךְ "screen", as v14, 16); the nine stones as the breastpiece row of Exod 28:17–20 (אֹדֶם carnelian, פִּטְדָה topaz, יָהֲלֹם diamond, תַּרְשִׁישׁ beryl, שֹׁהַם onyx, יָשְׁפֵה jasper, סַפִּיר sapphire, נֹפֶךְ turquoise, בָּרֶקֶת emerald), in the order of this verse: only the first is fairly sure, the rest are traditional identifications; they are nine of the breastpiece's twelve, three of its rows of three in another order (the Septuagint lists all twelve, from memory). **"the workmanship of thy tabrets and of thy pipes was prepared in thee in the day that thou wast created" → "the work of your timbrels and your hollows was in you; on the day you were created they were established"** — Hebrew מְלֶאכֶת תֻּפֶּיךָ וּנְקָבֶיךָ בָּךְ: תֹּף "timbrel" (its row), נֶקֶב "hollow" (from נָקַב "pierce"); the sense is uncertain: drums and pipes, or the settings and mountings of the stones (ESV "settings … engravings"): the words are kept; הִבָּרֵא "be created" (בָּרָא, its row; also v15); כּוֹנָנוּ "they were established" (כּוֹנֵן, its row).
- **"Thou art the anointed cherub that covereth; and I have set thee so: thou wast upon the holy mountain of God; thou hast walked up and down in the midst of the stones of fire" → "You were a cherub of anointing, the one who screens, and I set you; on the holy mountain of Elohim you were; in the midst of stones of fire you walked about"** (v14) — Hebrew אַתְּ־כְּרוּב מִמְשַׁח הַסּוֹכֵךְ: אַתְּ is pointed as the feminine "you", which is odd for the masculine king addressed through v12–19 (a short masculine form, as Num 11:15; Deut 5:27, is the usual explanation); the Septuagint reads אֶת "with": "with the cherub I set you", from memory; the MT is kept; כְּרוּב "cherub" (token, its row); מִמְשַׁח, only here, taken from מָשַׁח "anoint" ("of anointing"; uncertain: also "of extension, wide-spreading"); הַסּוֹכֵךְ "the one who screens" (סָכַךְ "screen", its row: as the cherubim screen the ark with their wings, Exod 25:20); וּנְתַתִּיךָ "and I set you" (lit. "gave you"), its own phrase by the accents; הַר קֹדֶשׁ "holy mountain" (the קֹדֶשׁ genitive row); אַבְנֵי־אֵשׁ "stones of fire" (no article; v16 has none either); הִתְהַלֵּךְ "walk about" (its row). The verse names a cherub and a mountain; it does not name any other being.
- **"Thou wast perfect in thy ways from the day that thou wast created, till iniquity was found in thee" → "Blameless you were in your ways from the day you were created, until injustice was found in you"** (v15) — Hebrew תָּמִים "blameless" (its row); עַוְלָה "injustice" (the עָוֶל row; also v18; not עָוֹן "iniquity").
- **"By the multitude of thy merchandise they have filled the midst of thee with violence, and thou hast sinned: therefore I will cast thee as profane out of the mountain of God: and I will destroy thee, O covering cherub" → "In the abundance of your merchandise they filled your midst with violence, and you sinned; and I profaned you from the mountain of Elohim, and I made you perish, cherub that screens"** (v16) — Hebrew מָלוּ, plural, no subject named ("they filled"; for מָלְאוּ); חָמָס "violence" (its row); וַתֶּחֱטָא "and you sinned"; וָאֶחַלֶּלְךָ "and I profaned you" (חִלֵּל "profane": put out as a thing no longer holy), past (wayyiqtol), as v17–18; אִבֵּד "make perish" (piel of אָבַד); כְּרוּב הַסֹּכֵךְ stands beside "you": the one addressed (no "O").
- **"Thine heart was lifted up because of thy beauty, thou hast corrupted thy wisdom by reason of thy brightness: I will cast thee to the ground, I will lay thee before kings, that they may behold thee" → "Your heart was high because of your beauty; you ruined your wisdom on account of your radiance; on the earth I cast you; before kings I set you, to look upon you"** (v17) — Hebrew שִׁחֵת "ruin" (its row); עַל־אֶרֶץ "on the earth" (also "to the ground"); הִשְׁלִיךְ "cast"; perfects, past; לְרַאֲוָה בָךְ "for looking upon you" (a spectacle).
- **"Thou hast defiled thy sanctuaries by the multitude of thine iniquities, by the iniquity of thy traffick; therefore will I bring forth a fire from the midst of thee, it shall devour thee, and I will bring thee to ashes" → "From the abundance of your iniquities, in the injustice of your merchandise, you profaned your sanctuaries; and I brought out fire from your midst; it has consumed you; and I made you ashes"** (v18) — Hebrew מֵרֹב עֲוֹנֶיךָ "from the abundance of your iniquities" (עָוֹן "iniquity", its row); עֶוֶל "injustice" (its row); חִלַּלְתָּ מִקְדָּשֶׁיךָ "you profaned your sanctuaries" (plural, as printed); וָאוֹצִא, past; הִיא אֲכָלַתְךָ "it has consumed you" (lit. "eaten"); נָתַן לְאֵפֶר "make ashes" (lit. "give to ashes"); לְעֵינֵי "before the eyes of" (its row).
- **"All they that know thee among the people shall be astonished at thee: thou shalt be a terror, and never shalt thou be any more" → "All who know you among the peoples are appalled over you; terrors you have become, and you shall be no more forever"** (v19) — as 27:35–36.
- **"set thy face against Zidon, and prophesy against it" → "set your face toward Sidon, and prophesy against her"** (v21) — Hebrew אֶל "toward" (as 25:2); עָלֶיהָ "against her" (Sidon feminine).
- **"Behold, I am against thee, O Zidon; and I will be glorified in the midst of thee … when I shall have executed judgments in her, and shall be sanctified in her" → "Behold, I am against you, Sidon, and I will be glorified in your midst … when I execute judgments in her, and am sanctified in her"** (v22) — Hebrew נִכְבַּד "be glorified" (its row); שְׁפָטִים "judgments" (its row; also v26); נִקְדַּשׁ "be sanctified" (its row; "show myself holy" is the reflexive sense: also v25); no "O".
- **"For I will send into her pestilence … and the wounded shall be judged in the midst of her by the sword upon her on every side" → "And I will send into her pestilence … and the slain shall fall in her midst, by the sword against her all around"** (v23) — Hebrew וְשִׁלַּחְתִּי "And I will send"; וְנִפְלַל, an odd form, taken as "shall fall" (נָפַל; KJV from פָּלַל "judge"); חָלָל "slain" (as v8).
- **"And there shall be no more a pricking brier unto the house of Israel, nor any grieving thorn of all that are round about them, that despised them" → "And there shall be no more for the house of Israel a malignant brier or a paining thorn, from all around them who despise them"** (v24) — Hebrew סִלּוֹן מַמְאִיר "a malignant brier" (מַמְאִיר, the מַמְאֶרֶת row, uncertain; KJV "pricking"); קוֹץ מַכְאִב "a paining thorn" (hifil of כָּאַב "be in pain"; the מַכְאֹב row); הַשָּׁאטִים "who despise" (participle of the שְׁאָט "contempt" of 25:6; also v26). **"and they shall know that I am the Lord GOD"** — כִּי אֲנִי אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, the knowledge formula with both names.
- **"then shall they dwell in their land that I have given to my servant Jacob" → "then they shall dwell on their ground that I gave to my servant, to Jacob"** (v25) — Hebrew וְיָשְׁבוּ, the main clause after "when"; עַל־אַדְמָתָם "on their ground" (as 25:3); לְעַבְדִּי לְיַעֲקֹב "to my servant, to Jacob" (עֶבֶד of Yahweh "servant", its row); נָפֹצוּ "were scattered".
- **"And they shall dwell safely therein … yea, they shall dwell with confidence" → "And they shall dwell on it in security … and dwell in security"** (v26) — Hebrew לָבֶטַח twice "in security" (its row); "yea" is not in the Hebrew; מִסְּבִיבוֹתָם "from all around them".
- **Layout** — v2b–10, v12b–19 and v22b–23 as poetry, one Hebrew line per printed line by the accents; the rest prose.

### Notes
- **The king of Tyre** (v12–19) — the dirge speaks to the king of Tyre (v12) in the words of Eden, the garden of Elohim, a cherub, the holy mountain and stones of fire. Later readers took it of the fall of Satan; the verse names the king of Tyre and no other being.
- **Daniel** (v3) — see Ezek 14:14, 20.
