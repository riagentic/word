# Ezekiel 23

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[2] "Son of man, there were two women, the daughters of one mother;

[3] and they played the prostitute in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧; in their youth they played the prostitute; there their breasts were squeezed, and there they pressed the nipples of their virginity.

[4] And their names: Oholah the elder, and ⟦oholibah|Oholivah|Oholibah⟧ her sister. And they became mine, and they bore sons and daughters. And their names: ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ is Oholah, and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ is ⟦oholibah|Oholivah|Oholibah⟧.

[5] "And Oholah played the prostitute under me, and she lusted after her lovers, after ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, near ones,

[6] clothed in blue, governors and prefects, young men of desire all of them, horsemen riding on horses.

[7] And she gave her prostitutions upon them, the choice of the sons of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ all of them; and with all after whom she lusted, with all their idols, she defiled herself.

[8] And her prostitutions from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ she did not forsake; for they lay with her in her youth, and they pressed the nipples of her virginity, and they poured out their prostitution upon her.

[9] Therefore I gave her into the hand of her lovers, into the hand of the sons of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, after whom she lusted.

[10] They uncovered her nakedness; her sons and her daughters they took; and her they killed with the sword; and she became a name to women, and they did judgments on her.

[11] "And her sister ⟦oholibah|Oholivah|Oholibah⟧ saw, and she ruined her lust more than she, and her prostitutions more than the prostitution of her sister.

[12] After the sons of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ she lusted, governors and prefects, near ones, clothed in perfection, horsemen riding on horses, young men of desire all of them.

[13] And I saw that she had defiled herself; one way was for the two of them.

[14] And she added to her prostitutions; and she saw men carved on the wall, images of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ carved in vermilion,

[15] girded with a belt on their loins, with dyed turbans hanging down on their heads, the appearance of officers all of them, the likeness of the sons of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, ⟦chaldea|Kasdim|Chaldea⟧, the land of their kindred.

[16] And she lusted after them at the sight of her eyes, and she sent messengers to them to ⟦chaldea|Kasdim|Chaldea⟧.

[17] And the sons of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ came to her, to the bed of love, and they defiled her with their prostitution; and she was defiled by them, and her soul was wrenched from them.

[18] And she uncovered her prostitutions, and she uncovered her nakedness; and my soul was wrenched from upon her, as my soul had been wrenched from upon her sister.

[19] And she multiplied her prostitutions, remembering the days of her youth, when she played the prostitute in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[20] And she lusted after their paramours, whose flesh is the flesh of donkeys, and whose emission is the emission of horses.

[21] And you visited the depravity of your youth, in the pressing of your nipples from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, on account of the breasts of your youth.

[22] "Therefore, ⟦oholibah|Oholivah|Oholibah⟧, thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I am stirring up your lovers against you, those from whom your soul was wrenched, and I will bring them against you from around:

[23] the sons of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ and all the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧, ⟦pekod|Peqod|Pekod⟧ and Shoa and ⟦koa|Qoa|Koa⟧, all the sons of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ with them, young men of desire, governors and prefects all of them, officers and called ones, riding on horses all of them.

[24] And they shall come against you with weapons, chariots and wheels, and with an assembly of peoples; large shield and shield and helmet they shall set against you all around; and I will set judgment before them, and they shall judge you by their judgments.

[25] And I will set my jealousy against you, and they shall deal with you in fury; your nose and your ears they shall remove, and what is left of you shall fall by the sword; they shall take your sons and your daughters, and what is left of you shall be devoured by the fire.

[26] And they shall strip you of your garments, and they shall take the articles of your beauty.

[27] And I will make your depravity cease from you, and your prostitution from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧; and you shall not lift up your eyes to them, and ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ you shall not remember anymore.

[28] "For thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I am giving you into the hand of those whom you hate, into the hand of those from whom your soul was wrenched.

[29] And they shall deal with you in hatred, and they shall take all your labor, and they shall leave you naked and bare; and the nakedness of your prostitution shall be uncovered, and your depravity and your prostitutions.

[30] These things shall be done to you because you played the prostitute after nations, because you defiled yourself with their idols.

[31] In the way of your sister you have walked; and I will give her cup into your hand.

[32] "Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:

The cup of your sister you shall drink,
the deep and the wide;
you shall be for laughter and for derision;
it holds much.

[33] With drunkenness and sorrow you shall be filled,
a cup of appalling and desolation,
the cup of your sister ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧.

[34] And you shall drink it and drain it,
and its shards you shall gnaw,
and your breasts you shall tear;
for I have spoken, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[35] "Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Because you have forgotten me and thrown me behind your back, you also, bear your depravity and your prostitutions."

[36] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, "Son of man, will you judge Oholah and ⟦oholibah|Oholivah|Oholibah⟧? Then declare to them their abominations.

[37] For they have committed adultery, and blood is on their hands; and with their idols they have committed adultery; and also their sons, whom they bore to me, they have made pass over to them for food.

[38] Yet this they have done to me: they have defiled my sanctuary in that day, and my ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ they have profaned.

[39] And when they had slaughtered their sons to their idols, they came into my sanctuary in that day to profane it; and behold, thus they have done in the midst of my house.

[40] "And moreover, they have sent for men coming from afar, to whom a messenger was sent; and behold, they came — for whom you washed, painted your eyes, and adorned yourself with ornaments.

[41] And you sat on a glorious couch, with a table arranged before it, and my incense and my oil you set on it.

[42] And the sound of a multitude at ease was in her; and to men from the multitude of mankind drunkards were brought from the wilderness; and they put bracelets on their hands, and a crown of beauty on their heads.

[43] And I said of her who was worn out with adulteries, 'Now they will play the prostitute with her prostitution, and she!'

[44] And they went in to her, as one goes in to a woman, a prostitute; so they went in to Oholah and to ⟦oholibah|Oholivah|Oholibah⟧, the women of depravity.

[45] And righteous men, they shall judge them with the judgment of adulteresses and with the judgment of women who shed blood; for adulteresses they are, and blood is on their hands.

[46] "For thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Bring up an assembly against them, and give them to horror and to plunder.

[47] And the assembly shall stone them with stones and cut them down with their swords; their sons and their daughters they shall kill, and their houses they shall burn with fire.

[48] And I will make depravity cease from the land, and all the women shall be disciplined, and they shall not do according to your depravity.

[49] And they shall lay your depravity upon you, and the sins of your idols you shall bear; and you shall know that I am ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v22, 28, 32, 34, 35, 46, 49) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words (token `adonai-yhwh`); נְאֻם "declares" (v34). In v49 כִּי אֲנִי אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, "that I am Lord Yahweh".
- **The words for prostitution** — the Hebrew uses words of one root: זָנָה "play the prostitute" (v3, 5, 19, 30, 43; the זָנָה row), זוֹנָה "prostitute" (v44), זְנוּנִים / זְנוּת "prostitution" (v11, 27, 29), and Ezekiel's own word תַּזְנוּת (v7, 8, 11, 14, 17, 18, 19, 29, 35, 43; Ezek 16 often), also "prostitution" by the Ezekiel row, singular or plural as the form is ("her prostitutions", "their prostitution"). Where תַּזְנוּת and זְנוּנִים stand together (v11 "her prostitutions more than the prostitution of her sister"; v29 "the nakedness of your prostitution … and your prostitutions") English has one word for both: the first is זְנוּנִים, the last תַּזְנוּת. Not "whore", "harlot", "whoredom" (the row). The earlier "whoring lust" and "whoring begun" added words. עָגַב "lust after" (v5, 7, 9, 12, 16, 20; עֹגְבִים "lovers" in Jer 4:30), noun עַגְבָה "lust" (v11); מְאַהֲבִים "lovers" (v5, 9, 22; its row); נָאַף "commit adultery", נֹאֶפֶת "adulteress", נִאוּפִים "adulteries" (v37, 43, 45; the נָאַף row). The language is as the Hebrew has it: neither softened nor made coarser.
- **"Moreover the word of the LORD came unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying,"** (v1) — the prophets' formula.
- **"there were two women, the daughters of one mother" → "there were two women, the daughters of one mother;"** (v2) — שְׁתַּיִם נָשִׁים בְּנוֹת אֵם־אַחַת הָיוּ; the sentence runs on into v3 with "and" (וַתִּזְנֶינָה).
- **"there were their breasts pressed, and there they bruised the teats of their virginity" → "there their breasts were squeezed, and there they pressed the nipples of their virginity"** (v3; also v8, 21) — Hebrew מֹעֲכוּ שְׁדֵיהֶן (pual of מָעַךְ "squeeze"), עִשּׂוּ דַּדֵּי בְּתוּלֵיהֶן (piel of עָשָׂה "press"; the subject is not named); דַּד "nipple", שַׁד "breast" (two words); בְּתוּלִים "virginity" (the noun of בְּתוּלָה; the earlier "virgin bosoms" made it an adjective).
- **"And the names of them were Aholah the elder, and Aholibah her sister" → "And their names: Oholah the elder, and Oholibah her sister"** (v4) — וּשְׁמוֹתָן, twice, as printed. The earlier glosses in the verse are removed; the meanings belong here: אָהֳלָה "her tent" (or "tent-woman"), אָהֳלִיבָה "my tent is in her" (אֹהֶל "tent"); the tent may point to the sanctuary — the one in Jerusalem being Yahweh's, the other her own (1 Kgs 12:28–33): a reading, not said in the text. **"and they were mine" → "And they became mine"** — וַתִּהְיֶינָה לִי, wayyiqtol. **"Thus were their names; Samaria is Aholah, and Jerusalem Aholibah" → "And their names: Samaria is Oholah, and Jerusalem is Oholibah"** — the Hebrew order, city first.
- **"And Aholah played the harlot when she was mine" → "And Oholah played the prostitute under me"** (v5) — תַּחְתָּי, literally "under me": while under my authority as a wife (as "under your husband", Num 5:19, 20). **"and she doted on her lovers, on the Assyrians her neighbours" → "and she lusted after her lovers, after Assyria, near ones"** — עַל־מְאַהֲבֶיהָ אֶל־אַשּׁוּר קְרוֹבִים: קְרוֹבִים "near ones" (near in place or in kinship, or "drawing near": not decided; the earlier "warriors" is a correction, not followed); אַשּׁוּר is the land or people ("Assyria"); בְּנֵי אַשּׁוּר "the sons of Assyria" (v7, 9, 12, 23).
- **"Which were clothed with blue, captains and rulers, all of them desirable young men, horsemen riding upon horses" → "clothed in blue, governors and prefects, young men of desire all of them, horsemen riding on horses"** (v6; also v12, 23) — תְּכֵלֶת "blue" (its row; the earlier "purple" is אַרְגָּמָן); פַּחוֹת "governors", סְגָנִים "prefects" (their rows); בַּחוּרֵי חֶמֶד, literally "young men of desire" (חֶמֶד, root of חָמַד "covet"); פָּרָשִׁים "horsemen", רֹכְבֵי סוּסִים "riders of horses" (the rows).
- **"Thus she committed her whoredoms with them" → "And she gave her prostitutions upon them"** (v7) — וַתִּתֵּן תַּזְנוּתֶיהָ עֲלֵיהֶם, literally "gave … upon". **"with all that were the chosen men of Assyria" → "the choice of the sons of Assyria all of them"** — מִבְחַר. **"and with all on whom she doted: with all their idols she defiled herself" → "and with all after whom she lusted, with all their idols, she defiled herself"** — בְּכֹל … בְּכָל־גִּלּוּלֵיהֶם נִטְמָאָה, nifal "be defiled" (the נִטְמָא row; also v13, 30).
- **"Neither left she her whoredoms brought from Egypt" → "And her prostitutions from Egypt she did not forsake"** (v8) — עָזַב "forsake" (its row). **"for in her youth they lay with her" → "for they lay with her in her youth"** — שָׁכְבוּ אוֹתָהּ (the שָׁכַב row). **"and poured their whoredom upon her" → "and they poured out their prostitution upon her"** — שָׁפַךְ "pour out" (its row).
- **"into the hand of her lovers" → "into the hand of her lovers"** (v9) — בְּיַד, singular "hand" (also v28: the earlier "hands").
- **"These discovered her nakedness: they took her sons and her daughters, and slew her with the sword" → "They uncovered her nakedness; her sons and her daughters they took; and her they killed with the sword"** (v10) — גִּלָּה "uncover" (its row; also v18, 29), הָרַג "kill" (its row; also v47); the objects first, as printed. **"and she became famous among women" → "and she became a name to women"** — וַתְּהִי־שֵׁם לַנָּשִׁים, word for word (a byword, or a warning). **"for they had executed judgment upon her" → "and they did judgments on her"** — וּשְׁפוּטִים עָשׂוּ בָהּ, plural (שְׁפוּטִים, beside שְׁפָטִים of 16:41).
- **"she was more corrupt in her inordinate love than she" → "she ruined her lust more than she"** (v11) — וַתַּשְׁחֵת עַגְבָתָהּ מִמֶּנָּה, hifil of שָׁחַת "ruin" (its row) with an object; עַגְבָה "lust", only here. **"and in her whoredoms more than her sister in her whoredoms" → "and her prostitutions more than the prostitution of her sister"** — תַּזְנוּתֶיהָ beside זְנוּנֵי: two words, one English (see above).
- **"clothed most gorgeously" → "clothed in perfection"** (v12) — לְבֻשֵׁי מִכְלוֹל, מִכְלוֹל "perfection, what is complete" (as 38:4; Ps 50:2 מִכְלַל): full dress, or full armor (the earlier "full armor" decides).
- **"that they took both one way" → "one way was for the two of them"** (v13) — דֶּרֶךְ אֶחָד לִשְׁתֵּיהֶן, verbless.
- **"And that she increased her whoredoms" → "And she added to her prostitutions"** (v14) — וַתּוֹסֶף אֶל (the יָסַף row). **"men pourtrayed upon the wall … pourtrayed with vermilion" → "men carved on the wall … carved in vermilion"** — מְחֻקֶּה, חֲקֻקִים from חָקַק "carve, engrave" (relief work, painted red); שָׁשַׁר "vermilion" (red pigment; Jer 22:14). The reading (qere) כַשְׂדִּים "Chaldeans"; the written text (ketiv) כשדיים, the same name in another spelling. צַלְמֵי "images" (the צֶלֶם row).
- **"Girded with girdles upon their loins, exceeding in dyed attire upon their heads, all of them princes to look to" → "girded with a belt on their loins, with dyed turbans hanging down on their heads, the appearance of officers all of them"** (v15) — חֲגוֹרֵי אֵזוֹר בְּמָתְנֵיהֶם (אֵזוֹר "belt", as 2 Kgs 1:8); סְרוּחֵי טְבוּלִים, "hanging down (with) dyed turbans" (טְבוּלִים only here: uncertain, "turbans" from the root "dip, dye"); מַרְאֵה שָׁלִשִׁים "the appearance of officers" (the שָׁלִישׁ row; also v23). **"after the manner of the Babylonians of Chaldea, the land of their nativity" → "the likeness of the sons of Babylon, Chaldea, the land of their kindred"** — דְּמוּת בְּנֵי־בָבֶל כַּשְׂדִּים אֶרֶץ מוֹלַדְתָּם: בְּנֵי "sons of" (the earlier `babylonians` token gave the gentilic for two words; here `babylon`, as v17, 23); אֶרֶץ מוֹלֶדֶת "the land of kindred" (its row).
- **"And as soon as she saw them with her eyes, she doted upon them" → "And she lusted after them at the sight of her eyes"** (v16) — the reading (qere) וַתַּעְגְּבָה; the written text (ketiv) ותעגב, the same verb in a shorter form. לְמַרְאֵה עֵינֶיהָ "at the sight of her eyes"; מַלְאָכִים "messengers" (the מַלְאָךְ row); כַּשְׂדִּימָה "to Chaldea".
- **"into the bed of love" → "to the bed of love"** (v17) — לְמִשְׁכַּב דֹּדִים (מִשְׁכָּב "bed", its row; דּוֹדִים "love", plural). **"and she was polluted with them, and her mind was alienated from them" → "and she was defiled by them, and her soul was wrenched from them"** — וַתִּטְמָא־בָם, qal "become unclean / be defiled"; וַתֵּקַע נַפְשָׁהּ, יָקַע "be wrenched" (as Jer 6:8; also v18, 22, 28), נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (its row; the earlier "in disgust" gives the sense, not the words).
- **"So she discovered her whoredoms, and discovered her nakedness: then my mind was alienated from her" → "And she uncovered her prostitutions, and she uncovered her nakedness; and my soul was wrenched from upon her"** (v18) — וַתְּגַל … וַתְּגַל, one verb twice; מֵעָלֶיהָ "from upon her".
- **"Yet she multiplied her whoredoms, in calling to remembrance the days of her youth" → "And she multiplied her prostitutions, remembering the days of her youth"** (v19) — לִזְכֹּר, infinitive.
- **"For she doted upon their paramours, whose flesh is as the flesh of asses, and whose issue is like the issue of horses" → "And she lusted after their paramours, whose flesh is the flesh of donkeys, and whose emission is the emission of horses"** (v20) — Hebrew עַל פִּלַגְשֵׁיהֶם, פִּילֶגֶשׁ is "concubine": here of the men she lusts after (literally "their concubines", which in English would name women): "paramours". בְּשַׂר־חֲמוֹרִים בְּשָׂרָם, word for word, no "as" (בָּשָׂר "flesh", its row: the member is meant); זִרְמַת סוּסִים זִרְמָתָם, זִרְמָה "emission, flow" (of semen; only here). The earlier "members" and "issue … like" softened and added.
- **"Thus thou calledst to remembrance the lewdness of thy youth" → "And you visited the depravity of your youth"** (v21) — וַתִּפְקְדִי, פָּקַד "visit" (its row: here "look to again, long for"); זִמָּה "depravity" (its row; also v27, 29, 35, 44, 48, 49). **"in bruising thy teats by the Egyptians for the paps of thy youth" → "in the pressing of your nipples from Egypt, on account of the breasts of your youth"** — בַּעְשׂוֹת מִמִּצְרַיִם דַּדַּיִךְ, an infinitive with "from Egypt" (no "Egyptians": מִצְרַיִם, the land); לְמַעַן שְׁדֵי נְעוּרָיִךְ, MT "on account of the breasts of your youth" ("to squeeze", לְמַעֵךְ, is a correction of the letters, not followed).
- **"O Aholibah" → "Oholibah"** (v22) — no "O" (the reviewer's vocative row). **"Behold, I will raise up thy lovers against thee, from whom thy mind is alienated" → "Behold, I am stirring up your lovers against you, those from whom your soul was wrenched"** — הִנְנִי מֵעִיר, a participle; הֵעִיר "stir up". **"on every side" → "from around"** — מִסָּבִיב (also v24 סָבִיב "all around").
- **"Pekod, and Shoa, and Koa" → "Pekod and Shoa and Koa"** (v23) — names of peoples of Babylonia (tokens kept; Shoa has no separate English original form). **"and all the Assyrians with them" → "all the sons of Assyria with them"** — כָּל־בְּנֵי אַשּׁוּר אוֹתָם (no "and"). **"great lords and renowned" → "officers and called ones"** — שָׁלִשִׁים וּקְרוּאִים (the קְרוּאִים row: "the called ones", the summoned; the earlier "men of renown" is a guess).
- **"with chariots, wagons, and wheels" → "with weapons, chariots and wheels"** (v24) — Hebrew הֹצֶן רֶכֶב וְגַלְגַּל: הֹצֶן is found only here: "weapons" (or "armor", from the context; uncertain); the earlier "from the north" follows the Greek (from memory), not followed. רֶכֶב "chariots" (its row, a collective), גַּלְגַּל "wheel" (collective). **"and with an assembly of people" → "and with an assembly of peoples"** — קְהַל עַמִּים (its row). **"which shall set against thee buckler and shield and helmet round about" → "large shield and shield and helmet they shall set against you all around"** — צִנָּה "large shield" (its row), מָגֵן "shield", קוֹבַע "helmet". **"and I will set judgment before them" → "and I will set judgment before them"** — נָתַן לִפְנֵי "set before" (the reviewer row; the earlier "commit the judgment to them"). **"according to their judgments" → "by their judgments"** — בְּמִשְׁפְּטֵיהֶם.
- **"And I will set my jealousy against thee" → "And I will set my jealousy against you"** (v25) — וְנָתַתִּי קִנְאָתִי בָּךְ, literally "give my jealousy in you" (קִנְאָה, its row). **"they shall deal furiously with thee" → "they shall deal with you in fury"** — בְּחֵמָה (its row). **"they shall take away thy nose and thine ears" → "your nose and your ears they shall remove"** — אַף "nose" here of the body (Standing decisions); הֵסִיר "remove" (its row). **"and thy remnant shall fall by the sword … and thy residue shall be devoured by the fire" → "and what is left of you shall fall by the sword … and what is left of you shall be devoured by the fire"** — אַחֲרִיתֵךְ twice, one word, literally "your after-part, your end": the earlier "survivors" twice was close; the same rendering kept for both.
- **"They shall also strip thee out of thy clothes, and take away thy fair jewels" → "And they shall strip you of your garments, and they shall take the articles of your beauty"** (v26) — הִפְשִׁיט (its row), בֶּגֶד "garment" (its row); כְּלֵי תִפְאַרְתֵּךְ, כְּלִי "article" (its row), תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty" (the reviewer row; also v42).
- **"Thus will I make thy lewdness to cease from thee, and thy whoredom brought from the land of Egypt" → "And I will make your depravity cease from you, and your prostitution from the land of Egypt"** (v27; also v48) — הִשְׁבַּתִּי, hifil of שָׁבַת "cease" (its row); זְנוּת "prostitution". **"nor remember Egypt any more" → "and Egypt you shall not remember anymore"** — the Hebrew order.
- **"I will deliver thee into the hand of them whom thou hatest" → "I am giving you into the hand of those whom you hate"** (v28) — הִנְנִי נֹתְנָךְ, a participle.
- **"and shall take away all thy labour" → "and they shall take all your labor"** (v29) — יְגִיעַ "labor" (the fruit of toil; the earlier "fruit of your labor" adds). **"naked and bare" → "naked and bare"** — עֵירֹם וְעֶרְיָה (the עֵירֹם row). **"and the nakedness of thy whoredoms shall be discovered, both thy lewdness and thy whoredoms" → "and the nakedness of your prostitution shall be uncovered, and your depravity and your prostitutions"** — nifal נִגְלָה; the verse ends here by the accents (the earlier text ran it into v30 with "have brought").
- **"I will do these things unto thee" → "These things shall be done to you"** (v30) — עָשֹׂה אֵלֶּה לָךְ, an infinitive absolute with no subject, literally "doing these to you". **"because thou hast gone a whoring after the heathen" → "because you played the prostitute after nations"** — בִּזְנוֹתֵךְ אַחֲרֵי גוֹיִם, "after", no article. **"because thou art polluted with their idols" → "because you defiled yourself with their idols"** — עַל אֲשֶׁר־נִטְמֵאת (nifal).
- **"Thou hast walked in the way of thy sister" → "In the way of your sister you have walked"** (v31) — the Hebrew order; "therefore" is not written (וְנָתַתִּי "and I will give").
- **Layout** — v32 (after the opening words) to v34 are set as poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line; the rest of the chapter is prose.
- **"Thou shalt drink of thy sister's cup deep and large: thou shalt be laughed to scorn and had in derision; it containeth much" → "The cup of your sister you shall drink, the deep and the wide; you shall be for laughter and for derision; it holds much"** (v32) — הָעֲמֻקָּה וְהָרְחָבָה with the article; תִּהְיֶה לִצְחֹק וּלְלַעַג may be "you shall be" or "it (the cup) shall be" (one form for both); צְחֹק "laughter" (the צָחַק row); מִרְבָּה לְהָכִיל, literally "much to hold" (לְהָכִיל as 21:28).
- **"Thou shalt be filled with drunkenness and sorrow, with the cup of astonishment and desolation" → "With drunkenness and sorrow you shall be filled, a cup of appalling and desolation"** (v33) — שִׁכָּרוֹן (noun of שָׁכַר "be drunk"), יָגוֹן "sorrow" (its row); כּוֹס שַׁמָּה וּשְׁמָמָה, שַׁמָּה "a thing that appalls" (its row) and שְׁמָמָה "desolation" (its row), one root: the sound play cannot be kept.
- **"Thou shalt even drink it and suck it out, and thou shalt break the sherds thereof, and pluck off thine own breasts" → "And you shall drink it and drain it, and its shards you shall gnaw, and your breasts you shall tear"** (v34) — מָצָה "drain" (its row); תְּגָרֵמִי, piel of גָּרַם "gnaw (bones)"; תְּנַתֵּקִי, piel of נָתַק "tear away".
- **"and cast me behind thy back" → "and thrown me behind your back"** (v35) — הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw" (its row); גֵּו "back". **"therefore bear thou also thy lewdness and thy whoredoms" → "you also, bear your depravity and your prostitutions"** — וְגַם־אַתְּ שְׂאִי, an imperative; the earlier "the consequences of" adds: נָשָׂא "bear" with the deed as object (as the עָוֹן … נָשָׂא row: the deed and what it brings).
- **"The LORD said moreover unto me" → "And Yahweh said to me"** (v36) — וַיֹּאמֶר. **"wilt thou judge Aholah and Aholibah? yea, declare unto them" → "will you judge Oholah and Oholibah? Then declare to them"** — הֲתִשְׁפּוֹט … וְהַגֵּד, as 22:2.
- **"and with their idols have they committed adultery, and have also caused their sons, whom they bare unto me, to pass for them through the fire, to devour them" → "and with their idols they have committed adultery; and also their sons, whom they bore to me, they have made pass over to them for food"** (v37) — בִּידֵיהֶן "on (literally in) their hands" (also v45); הֶעֱבִירוּ לָהֶם לְאָכְלָה: "make pass over" (as the Molech row; "through the fire" is not written); לְאָכְלָה "for food" (as 21:32; 15:4). בְּנֵיהֶן "their sons" (the earlier "children").
- **"Moreover this they have done unto me" → "Yet this they have done to me"** (v38) — עוֹד זֹאת. **"in the same day" → "in that day"** (v38, 39) — בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא, the prophets' wording.
- **"For when they had slain their children to their idols, then they came the same day into my sanctuary to profane it" → "And when they had slaughtered their sons to their idols, they came into my sanctuary in that day to profane it"** (v39) — שָׁחַט "slaughter" (its row); "in sacrifice" is not written. **"and, lo, thus have they done" → "and behold, thus they have done"** — הִנֵּה.
- **"And furthermore, that ye have sent for men to come from far" → "And moreover, they have sent for men coming from afar"** (v40) — וְאַף כִּי תִשְׁלַחְנָה, third person feminine plural ("they", the two women; the verse then turns to "you", feminine singular). **"for whom thou didst wash thyself, paintedst thy eyes, and deckedst thyself with ornaments" → "for whom you washed, painted your eyes, and adorned yourself with ornaments"** — רָחַצְתְּ "washed" (its row), כָּחַלְתְּ (only here), עָדִית עֶדִי (the עֲדִי row).
- **"And satest upon a stately bed" → "And you sat on a glorious couch"** (v41) — מִטָּה כְבוּדָּה, כְּבוּדָּה "glorious" (root of כָּבוֹד). **"and a table prepared before it, whereupon thou hast set mine incense and mine oil" → "with a table arranged before it, and my incense and my oil you set on it"** — עָרַךְ "arrange" (its row); קְטֹרֶת "incense" (its row).
- **"And a voice of a multitude being at ease was with her" → "And the sound of a multitude at ease was in her"** (v42) — קוֹל "sound" (its row), הָמוֹן "multitude" (its row), שָׁלֵו "at ease". **"and with the men of the common sort were brought Sabeans from the wilderness" → "and to men from the multitude of mankind drunkards were brought from the wilderness"** — וְאֶל־אֲנָשִׁים מֵרֹב אָדָם, word for word; the reading (qere) סָבָאִים "drunkards" (from סָבָא "drink heavily"); the written text (ketiv) סובאים, read by KJV as "Sabeans" (סְבָאִים). The line is difficult. **"which put bracelets upon their hands, and beautiful crowns upon their heads" → "and they put bracelets on their hands, and a crown of beauty on their heads"** — צָמִיד "bracelet" (its row); עֲטֶרֶת תִּפְאֶרֶת singular "a crown of beauty".
- **"Then said I unto her that was old in adulteries, Will they now commit whoredoms with her, and she with them?" → "And I said of her who was worn out with adulteries, 'Now they will play the prostitute with her prostitution, and she!'"** (v43) — לַבָּלָה נִאוּפִים, בָּלָה "worn out" (its row); the reading (qere) עַתָּה יִזְנוּ "now they will play the prostitute"; the written text (ketiv) עת יזנה "now she will play the prostitute"; תַזְנוּתֶהָ וָהִיא, word for word "her prostitution, and she": the line is broken; nothing is supplied, the question of KJV is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Yet they went in unto her, as they go in unto a woman that playeth the harlot" → "And they went in to her, as one goes in to a woman, a prostitute"** (v44) — וַיָּבוֹא, singular ("and one went in"), with the plural בָּאוּ after it; בּוֹא אֶל "go in to" (its row); אִשָּׁה זוֹנָה "a woman, a prostitute" (the זוֹנָה row). **"the lewd women" → "the women of depravity"** — אִשֹּׁת הַזִּמָּה.
- **"And the righteous men, they shall judge them after the manner of adulteresses, and after the manner of women that shed blood" → "And righteous men, they shall judge them with the judgment of adulteresses and with the judgment of women who shed blood"** (v45) — מִשְׁפַּט twice, "judgment" (its row); no article on "righteous men".
- **"I will bring up a company upon them, and will give them to be removed and spoiled" → "Bring up an assembly against them, and give them to horror and to plunder"** (v46) — הַעֲלֵה … וְנָתֹן, infinitives absolute used as commands; קָהָל "assembly" (its row; also v47); זַעֲוָה "horror" (its row, which names this verse; KJV "to be removed" takes the word from זוּעַ "tremble, move"), בַּז "plunder".
- **"And the company shall stone them with stones, and dispatch them with their swords" → "And the assembly shall stone them with stones and cut them down with their swords"** (v47) — רָגַם (its row), אֶבֶן singular collective; בֵּרֵא piel "cut down" (its row, which names this verse). **"and burn up their houses with fire" → "and their houses they shall burn with fire"** — שָׂרַף "burn" (its row).
- **"that all women may be taught not to do after your lewdness" → "and all the women shall be disciplined, and they shall not do according to your depravity"** (v48) — וְנִוַּסְּרוּ, a rare reflexive stem of יָסַר (the נוֹסַר "be disciplined" row); two clauses with "and", as printed.
- **"And they shall recompense your lewdness upon you" → "And they shall lay your depravity upon you"** (v49) — וְנָתְנוּ … עֲלֵיכֶן, the Ezekiel row "lay upon" (literally "give upon"). **"and ye shall bear the sins of your idols" → "and the sins of your idols you shall bear"** — חֲטָאֵי גִלּוּלֵיכֶן (the earlier "sinful idolatry" made one word of two); the Hebrew order.
