# Ezekiel 15

[1] The word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me:

[2] "Son of man, how does the wood of the vine surpass any wood, the vine branch that is among the trees of the forest?

[3] Is wood taken from it to make anything? Do people take a peg from it to hang any vessel on it?

[4] Behold, it is given to the fire for fuel. When the fire has consumed both ends of it, and the middle of it is charred, is it useful for anything?

[5] Behold, when it was whole, it was used for nothing. How much less, when the fire has consumed it and it is charred, can it ever be used for anything!

[6] Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Like the wood of the vine among the trees of the forest, which I have given to the fire for fuel, so have I given up the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[7] And I will set my face against them. Though they escape from the fire, the fire shall yet consume them, and you will know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, when I set my face against them.

[8] And I will make the land desolate, because they have acted faithlessly, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (vv6, 8) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*.
- **"What is the vine tree more than any tree" → "how does the wood of the vine surpass any wood"** (v2) — Hebrew מַה יִּהְיֶה עֵץ הַגֶּפֶן מִכָּל עֵץ *mah yihyeh etz haggefen mikkal etz*; "surpass" for *yihyeh min* = be more than; restructured for clarity.
- **"a pin of it to hang any vessel thereon" → "a peg from it to hang any vessel on it"** (v3) — Hebrew יְתֵד *yated* = peg/tent pin; "peg" more natural than "pin."
- **"Is it meet for any work?" → "is it useful for anything?"** (v4) — Hebrew הֲיִצְלַח לִמְלָאכָה *hayitzlach limla'khah* = is it fit for work?; "useful" more natural.
- **"committed a trespass" → "acted faithlessly"** (v8) — Hebrew מָעֲלוּ מַעַל *ma'alu ma'al* = acted with treachery/unfaithfulness; "faithlessly" consistent with ch. 14.
