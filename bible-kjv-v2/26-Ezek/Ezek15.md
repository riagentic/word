# Ezekiel 15

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[2] "Son of man, what shall the wood of the vine be more than any wood, the branch that was among the trees of the forest?

[3] Is wood taken from it to make it for work? Or do they take from it a peg to hang any article on it?

[4] Behold, to the fire it is given for food; both its ends the fire has consumed, and its middle is charred. Is it fit for work?

[5] Behold, when it was whole, it was not made for work; how much less, when fire has consumed it and it is charred, shall it yet be made for work!

[6] Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: As the wood of the vine among the trees of the forest, which I have given to the fire for food, so I have given the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[7] And I will set my face against them. From the fire they have gone out, and the fire shall consume them; and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, when I put my face against them.

[8] And I will make the land a desolation, because they have committed an unfaithfulness, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v6, 8) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both names.
- **"And the word of the LORD came unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר, the prophets' formula.
- **"What is the vine tree more than any tree, or than a branch which is among the trees of the forest?" → "what shall the wood of the vine be more than any wood, the branch that was among the trees of the forest?"** (v2) — Hebrew מַה־יִּהְיֶה עֵץ־הַגֶּפֶן מִכָּל־עֵץ הַזְּמוֹרָה אֲשֶׁר הָיָה בַּעֲצֵי הַיָּעַר: עֵץ "wood" (also "tree": one word, v2–6); "more" is supplied for the comparing מִן; הַזְּמוֹרָה "the branch" stands beside "the wood of the vine" (no "or than"; also taken as "more than any wood of branch"); הָיָה a perfect, "was".
- **"Shall wood be taken thereof to do any work?" → "Is wood taken from it to make it for work?"** (v3) — Hebrew הֲיֻקַּח מִמֶּנּוּ עֵץ לַעֲשׂוֹת לִמְלָאכָה, literally "to make for work"; מְלָאכָה "work" (also v4, 5). **"or will men take a pin of it to hang any vessel thereon?" → "Or do they take from it a peg to hang any article on it?"** — אִם־יִקְחוּ … יָתֵד, יָתֵד "peg"; כְּלִי "article" (of wood or the like; "vessel" of earthenware).
- **"it is cast into the fire for fuel" → "to the fire it is given for food"** (v4) — Hebrew לָאֵשׁ נִתַּן לְאָכְלָה, the Hebrew order; נָתַן "give" (also v6, 8); אָכְלָה "food", the noun of אָכַל "eat, consume" (also v6). **"the fire devoureth both the ends of it, and the midst of it is burned" → "both its ends the fire has consumed, and its middle is charred"** — אֵת שְׁנֵי קְצוֹתָיו אָכְלָה הָאֵשׁ וְתוֹכוֹ נָחָר, the object first; perfect; נָחַר "be scorched, charred" (also v5). **"Is it meet for any work?" → "Is it fit for work?"** — הֲיִצְלַח לִמְלָאכָה, literally "will it prosper for work".
- **"when it was whole, it was meet for no work" → "when it was whole, it was not made for work"** (v5) — Hebrew לֹא יֵעָשֶׂה לִמְלָאכָה, nifal of עָשָׂה "be made"; תָּמִים "whole". **"how much less shall it be meet yet for any work, when the fire hath devoured it" → "how much less, when fire has consumed it and it is charred, shall it yet be made for work"** — אַף כִּי־אֵשׁ אֲכָלַתְהוּ וַיֵּחָר וְנַעֲשָׂה עוֹד לִמְלָאכָה, the Hebrew order; "fire" without the article.
- **"As the vine tree among the trees of the forest" → "As the wood of the vine among the trees of the forest"** (v6) — Hebrew כַּאֲשֶׁר עֵץ־הַגֶּפֶן בְּעֵץ הַיַּעַר, as v2. **"so will I give the inhabitants of Jerusalem" → "so I have given the inhabitants of Jerusalem"** — כֵּן נָתַתִּי, a perfect: told as done.
- **"they shall go out from one fire, and another fire shall devour them" → "From the fire they have gone out, and the fire shall consume them"** (v7) — Hebrew מֵהָאֵשׁ יָצָאוּ וְהָאֵשׁ תֹּאכְלֵם: "one" and "another" are not in the Hebrew; the same "fire" twice, with the article. **"when I set my face against them" → "when I put my face against them"** — בְּשׂוּמִי אֶת־פָּנַי בָּהֶם, שִׂים "put", beside נָתַן פָּנִים "set my face" at the start of the verse (two verbs, as Lev 20:3–6).
- **"And I will make the land desolate, because they have committed a trespass" → "And I will make the land a desolation, because they have committed an unfaithfulness"** (v8) — Hebrew וְנָתַתִּי אֶת־הָאָרֶץ שְׁמָמָה, literally "give the land a desolation"; מָעֲלוּ מַעַל "committed an unfaithfulness" (as 14:13).
