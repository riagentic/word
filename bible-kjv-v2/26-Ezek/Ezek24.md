# Ezekiel 24

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me in the ninth year, in the tenth month, on the tenth of the month, saying,

[2] "Son of man, write for yourself the name of the day, this very day: the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ has pressed upon ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ on this very day.

[3] And speak a parable to the house of rebellion, and say to them, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:

Set on the pot, set it on,
and also pour water into it;

[4] gather its pieces into it,
every good piece, thigh and shoulder;
with the choice of the bones fill it.

[5] Take the choice of the flock,
and also pile the bones under it;
boil its boilings;
also its bones are cooked in its midst.

[6] "Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Woe to the city of bloodguilt, a pot whose rust is in it, and whose rust has not gone out of it! By its pieces, by its pieces bring it out; no lot has fallen upon it.

[7] For her blood has been in her midst; on the bare surface of a crag she set it; she did not pour it out on the earth, to cover it with dust.

[8] To make fury go up, to take vengeance, I have set her blood on the bare surface of a crag, so that it may not be covered.

[9] "Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Woe to the city of bloodguilt! I too will make the pile great.

[10] Make the wood many, kindle the fire, bring the flesh to an end, and blend the blend, and let the bones be charred.

[11] And make it stand empty on its coals, in order that it may grow hot, and its bronze may glow, and its uncleanness be melted in its midst, its rust come to an end.

[12] It has wearied with toils, and its great rust does not go out from it; in the fire, its rust!

[13] In your uncleanness is depravity. Because I cleansed you and you were not clean, from your uncleanness you shall not be clean again until I make my fury rest on you.

[14] I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have spoken; it is coming, and I will do it; I will not let go, and I will not pity, and I will not relent; according to your ways and according to your deeds they shall judge you, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

[15] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[16] "Son of man, behold, I am taking from you the desire of your eyes with a plague; and you shall not lament, and you shall not weep, and your tears shall not come.

[17] Groan, be still; mourning for the dead you shall not make; bind your headdress on you, and put your sandals on your feet; and you shall not cover over the moustache, and the bread of men you shall not eat."

[18] And I spoke to the people in the morning, and my wife died in the evening; and I did in the morning as I was commanded.

[19] And the people said to me, "Will you not tell us what these things are to us, that you are doing?"

[20] And I said to them, "The word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[21] 'Say to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I am profaning my sanctuary, the pride of your strength, the desire of your eyes and the pity of your soul; and your sons and your daughters whom you have left behind shall fall by the sword.

[22] And you shall do as I have done: the moustache you shall not cover over, and the bread of men you shall not eat;

[23] and your headdresses shall be on your heads, and your sandals on your feet; you shall not lament, and you shall not weep; and you shall rot away in your iniquities, and you shall moan each man to his brother.

[24] And ⟦ezekiel|Yechezqel|Ezekiel⟧ shall be to you for a wonder; according to all that he has done you shall do; when it comes, then you shall know that I am ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.'

[25] "And you, son of man — shall it not be, in the day when I take from them their strong place, the exultation of their beauty, the desire of their eyes and the lifting up of their soul, their sons and their daughters,

[26] in that day the fugitive shall come to you, to make ears hear?

[27] In that day your mouth shall be opened with the fugitive, and you shall speak and be mute no more; and you shall be to them for a wonder; and they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v3, 6, 9, 14, 21, 24) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words (token `adonai-yhwh`); נְאֻם "declares" (v14). In v24 כִּי אֲנִי אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, "that I am Lord Yahweh".
- **Layout** — v3 (after "Thus says Lord Yahweh:") to v5 is the song of the pot, set as poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line; the rest is prose.
- **"Again in the ninth year … the word of the LORD came unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me in the ninth year … on the tenth of the month, saying,"** (v1) — וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי … לֵאמֹר, the Hebrew order (the formula first, then the date); בֶּעָשׂוֹר לַחֹדֶשׁ "on the tenth of the month".
- **"write thee the name of the day, even of this same day" → "write for yourself the name of the day, this very day"** (v2) — the reading (qere) כְּתָב, an imperative; the written text (ketiv) כתוב, an infinitive used as a command: the same sense. אֶת־עֶצֶם הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה, literally "the bone of this day" (the בְּעֶצֶם הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה row "that very day"). **"the king of Babylon set himself against Jerusalem" → "has pressed upon Jerusalem"** — סָמַךְ … אֶל, literally "leaned, laid himself upon" (the סָמַךְ row: "lean, press"): the siege begins.
- **"utter a parable unto the rebellious house" → "speak a parable to the house of rebellion"** (v3) — מְשֹׁל … מָשָׁל, verb and noun (the מָשָׁל row "parable"); בֵּית הַמֶּרִי "the house of rebellion" (the Ezekiel row). **"Set on a pot, set it on, and also pour water into it" → "Set on the pot, set it on, and also pour water into it"** — שְׁפֹת הַסִּיר שְׁפֹת, infinitives absolute as commands, the article kept; יָצַק "pour" (its row).
- **"Gather the pieces thereof into it, even every good piece, the thigh, and the shoulder; fill it with the choice bones" → "gather its pieces into it, every good piece, thigh and shoulder; with the choice of the bones fill it"** (v4) — נֵתַח "piece" (its row); יָרֵךְ "thigh" (its row); מִבְחַר עֲצָמִים, a noun "the choice of". The earlier "pieces of meat" adds.
- **"Take the choice of the flock, and burn also the bones under it, and make it boil well, and let them seethe the bones of it therein" → "Take the choice of the flock, and also pile the bones under it; boil its boilings; also its bones are cooked in its midst"** (v5) — לָקוֹחַ, infinitive absolute as a command; צֹאן "flock" (its row); דּוּר הָעֲצָמִים, דּוּר "pile" (MT "the bones" as fuel; "the wood", הָעֵצִים, as v10, is a correction of the letters, not followed; the earlier "logs" follows it); רַתַּח רְתָחֶיהָ, verb and noun of one root (רְתָחִים "boilings", only here); בָּשְׁלוּ, qal perfect "are cooked" (beside בִּשֵּׁל "boil", its row).
- **"Woe to the bloody city, to the pot whose scum is therein" → "Woe to the city of bloodguilt, a pot whose rust is in it"** (v6; also v9) — אוֹי "Woe" (the Isaiah row; "to" supplied); עִיר הַדָּמִים, literally "the city of bloods" (the דָּמִים row, as 22:2). חֶלְאָה "rust" (v6, 11, 12; the corrosion of a metal pot, or filth: the root sense is "be sick"; KJV "scum"; one word kept throughout). **"bring it out piece by piece; let no lot fall upon it" → "By its pieces, by its pieces bring it out; no lot has fallen upon it"** — לִנְתָחֶיהָ לִנְתָחֶיהָ הוֹצִיאָהּ; לֹא־נָפַל עָלֶיהָ גּוֹרָל, a perfect, a statement (no lot chooses which piece is spared; the גּוֹרָל row: here the verb is נָפַל "fall").
- **"For her blood is in the midst of her" → "For her blood has been in her midst"** (v7) — דָּמָהּ … הָיָה, the blood she shed. **"she set it upon the top of a rock" → "on the bare surface of a crag she set it"** (v7, 8) — צְחִיחַ "bare, glaring surface", סֶלַע "crag" (its row). **"she poured it not upon the ground, to cover it with dust" → "she did not pour it out on the earth, to cover it with dust"** — שָׁפַךְ עַל־הָאָרֶץ … עָפָר (as Lev 17:13; אֶרֶץ "earth", its row).
- **"That it might cause fury to come up to take vengeance" → "To make fury go up, to take vengeance"** (v8) — לְהַעֲלוֹת "to make go up" (hifil of עָלָה, its row); לִנְקֹם נָקָם, verb and noun ("avenge an avenging"; the נָקַם row). **"I have set her blood" → "I have set her blood"** — נָתַתִּי, literally "I have given". **"that it should not be covered" → "so that it may not be covered"** — לְבִלְתִּי הִכָּסוֹת.
- **"I will even make the pile for fire great" → "I too will make the pile great"** (v9) — גַּם־אֲנִי, מְדוּרָה "pile" (of wood); "for fire" is not written.
- **"Heap on wood, kindle the fire, consume the flesh, and spice it well, and let the bones be burned" → "Make the wood many, kindle the fire, bring the flesh to an end, and blend the blend, and let the bones be charred"** (v10) — infinitives absolute as commands: הַרְבֵּה "make many"; הָתֵם "bring to an end" (the הֵתֵם row: cook it through); וְהַרְקַח הַמֶּרְקָחָה, verb and noun of רָקַח "blend" (its row: spices mixed in); יֵחָרוּ, nifal of חָרַר "be charred" (as 15:4).
- **"Then set it empty upon the coals thereof" → "And make it stand empty on its coals"** (v11) — הַעֲמִידֶהָ (the הֶעֱמִיד "make stand" row). **"that the brass of it may be hot, and may burn" → "in order that it may grow hot, and its bronze may glow"** — תֵּחַם … וְחָרָה; נְחֹשֶׁת "bronze" (its row). **"that the filthiness of it may be molten in it, that the scum of it may be consumed" → "and its uncleanness be melted in its midst, its rust come to an end"** — נִתְּכָה (the verb of 22:20–22), טֻמְאָה "uncleanness" (its row; also v13), תִּתֻּם (the תַּם "come to an end" row).
- **"She hath wearied herself with lies, and her great scum went not forth out of her: her scum shall be in the fire" → "It has wearied with toils, and its great rust does not go out from it; in the fire, its rust!"** (v12) — Hebrew תְּאֻנִים הֶלְאָת: תְּאֻנִים is found only here, taken from אָוֶן "trouble, toil" ("lies" from another root); הֶלְאָת "it has wearied" (hifil, with no object: "me", or "itself"); the line is difficult, the plainest sense kept. בְּאֵשׁ חֶלְאָתָהּ, verbless.
- **"In thy filthiness is lewdness" → "In your uncleanness is depravity"** (v13) — בְּטֻמְאָתֵךְ זִמָּה, verbless; זִמָּה "depravity" (its row). **"because I have purged thee, and thou wast not purged" → "Because I cleansed you and you were not clean"** — טִהַרְתִּיךְ וְלֹא טָהַרְתְּ, piel then qal of טָהֵר (the earlier "I would have cleansed you" reads the perfect as an attempt: possible, not written). **"till I have caused my fury to rest upon thee" → "until I make my fury rest on you"** — הֲנִיחִי חֲמָתִי בָּךְ (as 5:13; 21:17).
- **"I the LORD have spoken it: it shall come to pass, and I will do it" → "I, Yahweh, have spoken; it is coming, and I will do it"** (v14) — אֲנִי יְהוָה דִּבַּרְתִּי (the Ezekiel row; by the accents also "I am Yahweh; I have spoken"); בָּאָה, a participle. **"I will not go back, neither will I spare, neither will I repent" → "I will not let go, and I will not pity, and I will not relent"** — לֹא־אֶפְרַע (the פָּרַע "let loose" row: "let go", leave it undone); חוּס "pity" (its row); נִחַם nifal "relent" (its row: of a planned act given up). **"they shall judge thee" → "they shall judge you"** — שְׁפָטוּךְ, a perfect with "they" (the Greek and some Hebrew manuscripts have "I will judge you": from memory, not followed).
- **"Also the word of the LORD came unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying,"** (v15).
- **Verses 16–17 as printed.** **"behold, I take away from thee the desire of thine eyes with a stroke" → "behold, I am taking from you the desire of your eyes with a plague"** (v16) — הִנְנִי לֹקֵחַ, a participle; מַחְמַד עֵינֶיךָ "the desire of your eyes" (as 1 Kgs 20:6; also v21, 25; from חָמַד); בְּמַגֵּפָה "with a plague" (its row: literally "a blow", the sudden death of v18). **"yet neither shalt thou mourn nor weep, neither shall thy tears run down" → "and you shall not lament, and you shall not weep, and your tears shall not come"** — סָפַד "lament" (its row; also v23), תָּבוֹא "come".
- **"Forbear to cry, make no mourning for the dead" → "Groan, be still; mourning for the dead you shall not make"** (v17) — Hebrew הֵאָנֵק דֹּם: an imperative "groan" (נָאַק, verb of נְאָקָה "groaning", its row) and דֹּם "be still" (the דָּמַם row), joined by the accents; "groan silently" is the sense; KJV "forbear to cry" reverses the first verb. מֵתִים "the dead", plural. **"bind the tire of thine head upon thee" → "bind your headdress on you"** — פְּאֵר "headdress" (as Isa 61:3, 10; מִצְנֶפֶת is "turban"). **"put on thy shoes upon thy feet" → "put your sandals on your feet"** — נַעַל "sandal" (its row). **"and cover not thy lips" → "and you shall not cover over the moustache"** — וְלֹא תַעְטֶה עַל־שָׂפָם (the row: שָׂפָם "moustache", עָטָה עַל "cover over", as Lev 13:45; also v22), a sign of mourning. **"and eat not the bread of men" → "and the bread of men you shall not eat"** — MT לֶחֶם אֲנָשִׁים (the bread others bring to mourners; "bread of mourners", אוֹנִים, is a correction, not followed).
- **"So I spake unto the people in the morning: and at even my wife died; and I did in the morning as I was commanded" → "And I spoke to the people in the morning, and my wife died in the evening; and I did in the morning as I was commanded"** (v18) — three wayyiqtols with "and"; "next" is not written.
- **"Wilt thou not tell us what these things are to us, that thou doest so?" → "Will you not tell us what these things are to us, that you are doing?"** (v19) — כִּי אַתָּה עֹשֶׂה, a participle with no object.
- **"Then I answered them" → "And I said to them"** (v20) — וָאֹמַר. **"The word of the LORD came unto me, saying" → "The word of Yahweh came to me, saying,"** — דְּבַר־יְהוָה הָיָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר.
- **"Behold, I will profane my sanctuary, the excellency of your strength" → "Behold, I am profaning my sanctuary, the pride of your strength"** (v21) — מְחַלֵּל, a participle; גְּאוֹן עֻזְּכֶם (the גָּאוֹן row "pride"; עֹז "strength", its row). **"and that which your soul pitieth" → "and the pity of your soul"** — מַחְמַל נַפְשְׁכֶם, only here: what the soul has pity on (from חָמַל "have pity", its row). **"and your sons and your daughters whom ye have left shall fall by the sword" → "whom you have left behind"** — עֲזַבְתֶּם (the עָזַב row: "leave" of what is left behind).
- **"ye shall not cover your lips, nor eat the bread of men" → "the moustache you shall not cover over, and the bread of men you shall not eat"** (v22) — as v17.
- **"And your tires shall be upon your heads" → "and your headdresses shall be on your heads"** (v23) — פְּאֵרֵכֶם. **"ye shall pine away for your iniquities" → "you shall rot away in your iniquities"** — וּנְמַקֹּתֶם בַּעֲוֺנֹתֵיכֶם, as Lev 26:39; עָוֹן "iniquity" (its row). **"and mourn one toward another" → "and you shall moan each man to his brother"** — נָהַם "moan" (of a lion "growl", Prov 28:15; here of men), אִישׁ אֶל־אָחִיו (its row).
- **"Thus Ezekiel is unto you a sign" → "And Ezekiel shall be to you for a wonder"** (v24; also v27) — לְמוֹפֵת (the מוֹפֵת row "wonder", as 12:6, 11; אוֹת is "sign"). **"and when this cometh, ye shall know" → "when it comes, then you shall know"** — בְּבֹאָהּ וִידַעְתֶּם: the וְ of the formula opens the main clause after the time phrase ("then").
- **"Also, thou son of man, shall it not be in the day when I take from them their strength, the joy of their glory, the desire of their eyes, and that whereupon they set their minds" → "And you, son of man — shall it not be, in the day when I take from them their strong place, the exultation of their beauty, the desire of their eyes and the lifting up of their soul"** (v25) — הֲלוֹא, a question running to v26; מָעוֹז "strong place" (its row); מְשׂוֹשׂ תִּפְאַרְתָּם (the reviewer rows: מָשׂוֹשׂ "exultation", תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty"); מַשָּׂא נַפְשָׁם, literally "the lifting up of their soul" (what they long for: the נָשָׂא נֶפֶשׁ אֶל row). **"their sons and their daughters"** — no "and" before them.
- **"That he that escapeth in that day shall come unto thee, to cause thee to hear it with thine ears?" → "in that day the fugitive shall come to you, to make ears hear?"** (v26) — בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא "in that day" (the prophets' wording; also v27); הַפָּלִיט "the fugitive" (its row; also v27), with the article; לְהַשְׁמָעוּת אָזְנָיִם, a noun found only here, literally "for a causing of ears to hear".
- **"In that day shall thy mouth be opened to him which is escaped" → "In that day your mouth shall be opened with the fugitive"** (v27) — אֶת־הַפָּלִיט "with the fugitive" (at his coming; compare 3:26–27; 33:21–22). **"and be no more dumb" → "and be mute no more"** — וְלֹא תֵאָלֵם עוֹד.

### Notes
- **v1–2** — the same day as the beginning of the siege in 2 Kgs 25:1; Jer 52:4 (there the ninth year of Zedekiah, the tenth month, the tenth day).
- **v15–27** — the death of Ezekiel's wife and his silence are a wonder (sign-act) for the house of Israel; the fugitive's coming is told in 33:21–22.
