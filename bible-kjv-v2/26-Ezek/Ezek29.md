# Ezekiel 29

[1] In the tenth year, in the tenth month, on the twelfth day of the month, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me:

[2] "Son of man, set your face against ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and prophesy against him and against all ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧;

[3] speak, and say, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:

"Behold, I am against you,
⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
the great dragon that lies
in the midst of his streams,
that says, 'My ⟦nile|Yeor|Nile⟧ is my own;
I made it for myself.'

[4] I will put hooks in your jaws,
and make the fish of your streams stick to your scales;
and I will draw you up out of the midst of your streams,
with all the fish of your streams
that stick to your scales.

[5] And I will cast you out into the wilderness,
you and all the fish of your streams;
you shall fall on the open field,
and not be brought together or gathered.
To the beasts of the earth and to the birds of the heavens
I give you as food.

[6] Then all the inhabitants of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ shall know
that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.
"Because you have been a staff of reed to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[7] when they grasped you with the hand, you broke and tore all their shoulders;
and when they leaned on you, you broke and made all their loins to shake.

[8] "Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I will bring a sword upon you, and will cut off from you man and beast,

[9] and the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ shall be a desolation and a waste. Then they will know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

"Because you said, 'The ⟦nile|Yeor|Nile⟧ is mine, and I made it,'

[10] therefore, behold, I am against you and against your streams, and I will make the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ an utter waste and desolation, from Migdol to ⟦syene|Seveneh|Syene⟧, as far as the border of ⟦cush|Kush|Cush⟧.

[11] No foot of man shall pass through it, and no foot of beast shall pass through it; it shall be uninhabited forty years.

[12] And I will make the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ a desolation in the midst of desolated countries, and her cities shall be a desolation forty years among cities that are laid waste. I will scatter the ⟦egyptians|Mitzrim|Egyptians⟧ among the nations, and disperse them through the countries.

[13] "For thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: At the end of forty years I will gather the ⟦egyptians|Mitzrim|Egyptians⟧ from the peoples among whom they were scattered,

[14] and I will restore the fortunes of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and bring them back to the land of ⟦pathros|Patros|Pathros⟧, the land of their origin, and there they shall be a lowly kingdom.

[15] It shall be the most lowly of the kingdoms, and never again exalt itself above the nations. And I will make them so small that they will never again rule over the nations.

[16] And it shall never again be the reliance of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, recalling their iniquity, when they turn to them for aid. Then they will know that I am ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

[17] In the twenty-seventh year, in the first month, on the first day of the month, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me:

[18] "Son of man, ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ made his army labor hard against ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧. Every head was made bald, and every shoulder was rubbed bare, yet neither he nor his army got anything from ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ to pay for the labor that he had performed against her.

[19] Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I will give the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ to ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, and he shall carry off its wealth and despoil it and plunder it; and it shall be the wages for his army.

[20] I have given him the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ as his payment for which he labored, because they worked for me, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[21] "On that day I will cause a horn to spring up for the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and I will open your lips among them. Then they will know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*; Ezekiel's distinctive doubled divine title.
- **"Pathros" retained** (v14) — Hebrew פַּתְרוֹס *Pathros*; Upper Egypt (the Nile valley south of Memphis); proper name retained.

### Notes
- **v21** — "horn to spring up": Davidic messianic image.
