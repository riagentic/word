# Ezekiel 21

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[2] "Son of man, set your face toward ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and drip toward the sanctuaries, and prophesy toward the ground of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[3] and say to the ground of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Behold, I am against you, and I will bring out my sword from its sheath, and I will cut off from you righteous and wicked.

[4] Because I have cut off from you righteous and wicked, therefore my sword shall go out from its sheath against all flesh from south to north.

[5] And all flesh shall know that I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have brought out my sword from its sheath; it shall not turn back again.

[6] "And you, son of man, sigh; with the breaking of the loins and with bitterness you shall sigh before their eyes.

[7] And it shall be, when they say to you, 'Why are you sighing?' then you shall say, 'Because of the report, for it is coming; and every heart shall melt, and all hands shall be slack, and every spirit shall grow dim, and all knees shall go as water. Behold, it is coming, and it shall come to pass,' declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

[8] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[9] "Son of man, prophesy, and say, Thus says ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧; say:

A sword, a sword is sharpened,
and also polished;

[10] in order to slaughter a slaughter it is sharpened,
in order that it may have lightning it is polished.
Or shall we rejoice?
The rod of my son — it despises every tree.

[11] And he gave it to be polished,
to seize it in the palm;
it is sharpened, the sword, and it is polished,
to give it into the hand of a killer.

[12] Cry out and wail, son of man,
for it is against my people,
it is against all the chieftains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
thrown down to the sword they are, with my people;
therefore strike upon the thigh.

[13] For it has been tried;
and what if even the rod that despises shall not be? declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[14] And you, son of man, prophesy,
and strike palm to palm;
and let the sword be doubled, the third time, the sword of the slain;
it is the sword of the great slain one,
which surrounds them,

[15] in order that the heart may melt
and the stumblings be many;
against all their gates I have given the point of the sword.
Ah! it is made for lightning,
it is wrapped up for slaughter.

[16] Gather yourself, go to the right;
set yourself, go to the left,
wherever your face is designated.

[17] And I too will strike my palm to my palm,
and I will make my fury rest;
I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have spoken."

[18] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[19] "And you, son of man, set for yourself two ways for the sword of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ to come; from one land the two of them shall go out. And cut out a signpost; at the head of the way to a city cut it out.

[20] A way you shall set for the sword to come to Rabbah of the sons of Ammon, and to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, into fortified ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[21] For the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ has stood at the parting of the way, at the head of the two ways, to divine a divination; he has shaken the arrows, he has asked of the ⟦teraphim|teraphim|household gods⟧, he has looked at the liver.

[22] In his right hand was the divination, ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧: to set battering rams, to open the mouth with murder, to lift up the voice with a shout, to set battering rams against the gates, to pour out a mound, to build a siege wall.

[23] And it shall be to them as an empty divination in their eyes; sworn oaths are theirs; but he brings iniquity to remembrance, that they may be seized.

[24] "Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Because you have brought your iniquity to remembrance, in that your transgressions are uncovered, so that your sins appear in all your deeds — because you have been remembered, in the palm you shall be seized.

[25] And you, slain wicked one, chieftain of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, whose day has come, in the time of the iniquity of the end,

[26] thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Remove the turban, and lift off the crown; this is not this; make high what is low, and bring low what is high.

[27] A ruin, a ruin, a ruin I will make it; this also shall not be, until he comes whose is the judgment, and I will give it.

[28] "And you, son of man, prophesy, and say, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ concerning the sons of Ammon and concerning their reproach; and say:

A sword, a sword is drawn,
for slaughter it is polished,
to hold, in order to be lightning —

[29] while they behold emptiness for you,
while they divine a lie for you —
to put you on the necks of the slain of the wicked,
whose day has come,
in the time of the iniquity of the end.

[30] Return it to its sheath!
In the place where you were created,
in the land of your origin,
I will judge you.

[31] And I will pour out on you my indignation;
with the fire of my raging I will blow on you;
and I will give you into the hand of brutish men,
craftsmen of destruction.

[32] To the fire you shall be for food;
your blood shall be in the midst of the land;
you shall not be remembered,
for I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have spoken."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v7, 13, 24, 26, 28) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words (token `adonai-yhwh`); in v9 the Hebrew has אֲדֹנָי alone ("Thus says Adonai", token `adonai`). נְאֻם "declares" (v7, 13).
- **Layout** — v1–8, 18–27 are prose; v9 (after "say:") to v17 and v28 (after "and say:") to v32 are the two sword songs, set as poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line in the Hebrew order.
- **"Moreover the word of the LORD came unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying,"** (v1, 8, 18) — וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר, the prophets' formula; לֵאמֹר "saying" kept.
- **"drop thy word toward the holy places" → "drip toward the sanctuaries"** (v2) — Hebrew הַטֵּף אֶל־מִקְדָּשִׁים: הִטִּיף, hifil of נָטַף "drip", "drip" (as 20:46, the Ezek 17–20 row; of words let fall: Amos 7:16; Mic 2:6, 11); KJV supplies "thy word", the Hebrew has no object. אֶל "toward" in all three clauses (also "prophesy toward"), as the verb "set your face toward". **"the land of Israel" → "the ground of Israel"** (v2, 3) — אַדְמַת יִשְׂרָאֵל, אֲדָמָה "ground" (its row; Ezekiel's own phrase, as 7:2; 13:9).
- **"Behold, I am against thee, and will draw forth my sword" → "Behold, I am against you, and I will bring out my sword"** (v3; also v5) — הִנְנִי אֵלַיִךְ, literally "behold me toward you"; הוֹצִיא "bring out" (its row). **"both the righteous and the wicked" → "righteous and wicked"** (v3, 4) — צַדִּיק וְרָשָׁע, no "both", no article.
- **"Seeing then that I will cut off" → "Because I have cut off"** (v4) — יַעַן אֲשֶׁר־הִכְרַתִּי, a perfect. **"shall my sword go forth" → "my sword shall go out"** — תֵּצֵא. **"from the south to the north"** — מִנֶּגֶב צָפוֹן, literally "from Negev (to) north" (the Directions row).
- **"that I the LORD have drawn forth my sword" → "that I, Yahweh, have brought out my sword"** (v5) — כִּי אֲנִי יְהוָה הוֹצֵאתִי: "I, Yahweh" is the subject of the verb, so the refrain "that I am Yahweh" is not the reading here. **"it shall not return any more" → "it shall not turn back again"** — לֹא תָשׁוּב עוֹד, שׁוּב (its row).
- **"Sigh therefore … with the breaking of thy loins; and with bitterness sigh" → "sigh; with the breaking of the loins and with bitterness you shall sigh"** (v6) — Hebrew הֵאָנַח בְּשִׁבְרוֹן מָתְנַיִם וּבִמְרִירוּת תֵּאָנַח: an imperative, then a yiqtol; נֶאֱנַח "sigh" (its row, also v7); מָתְנַיִם "loins" kept (the earlier "breaking heart" changed the body word).
- **"Wherefore sighest thou?" → "Why are you sighing?"** (v7) — עַל־מָה, literally "on account of what"; נֶאֱנָח a participle. **"For the tidings; because it cometh" → "Because of the report, for it is coming"** — אֶל־שְׁמוּעָה כִי־בָאָה; שְׁמוּעָה "report" (as 7:26). **"shall be feeble" → "shall be slack"** — רָפָה (its row, as 7:17). **"every spirit shall faint" → "every spirit shall grow dim"** — כָּהָה (its row: of the eye "grow dim"). **"all knees shall be weak as water" → "all knees shall go as water"** — תֵּלַכְנָה מַּיִם, literally "shall go water" ("as" supplied, as 7:17): weakness or the wetting of terror, the words do not say. **"and shall be brought to pass" → "and it shall come to pass"** — וְנִהְיָתָה, nifal of הָיָה.
- **"A sword, a sword is sharpened, and also furbished" → "… sharpened, and also polished"** (v9; also v10, 11, 15, 28) — הוּחַדָּה, hofal of חָדַד "be sharp"; מְרוּטָה / מֹרָטָּה from מָרַט, literally "made bare, plucked smooth" (of a bald head, Lev 13:40): "polished".
- **"It is sharpened to make a sore slaughter; it is furbished that it may glitter" → "in order to slaughter a slaughter it is sharpened, in order that it may have lightning it is polished"** (v10) — לְמַעַן טְבֹחַ טֶבַח … לְמַעַן־הֱיֵה־לָהּ בָּרָק: the verb and its own noun (the טָבַח row has "butcher" beside שָׁחַט "slaughter"; there is no שָׁחַט here, and the noun טֶבַח is "slaughter", so the verb follows the noun); בָּרָק "lightning" (its row; also v15, 28). **"should we then make mirth?" → "Or shall we rejoice?"** — אוֹ נָשִׂישׂ.
- **"it contemneth the rod of my son, as every tree" → "The rod of my son — it despises every tree"** (v10) — Hebrew שֵׁבֶט בְּנִי מֹאֶסֶת כָּל־עֵץ, word for word "rod of my son, despising every tree"; the meaning is not certain. מֹאֶסֶת is feminine, so the one who despises is the sword (שֵׁבֶט is masculine); "the rod of my son" may be the scepter of Judah (שֵׁבֶט, Gen 49:9–10), or the rod of discipline of Israel "my son"; "every tree" may be every other rod, or all wood. KJV "as every tree" and the earlier "with everything of wood" supply a word. The verse keeps the Hebrew words and order. The same words return in v13 (שֵׁבֶט מֹאֶסֶת).
- **"And he hath given it to be furbished, that it may be handled" → "And he gave it to be polished, to seize it in the palm"** (v11) — וַיִּתֵּן, a wayyiqtol, subject unnamed ("he"); לִתְפֹּשׂ בַּכָּף, תָּפַשׂ "seize" (its row), כַּף "palm" (its row; also v14, 17, 24). **"this sword is sharpened" → "it is sharpened, the sword"** — הִיא־הוּחַדָּה חֶרֶב, the Hebrew order. **"the slayer" → "a killer"** — הוֹרֵג, participle of הָרַג "kill" (its row), no article.
- **"Cry and howl" → "Cry out and wail"** (v12) — זְעַק וְהֵילֵל (the זָעַק and הֵילִיל rows). **"for it shall be upon my people" → "for it is against my people"** — כִּי־הִיא הָיְתָה בְעַמִּי, a perfect, literally "it has been in my people"; so "it is against all the chieftains" (הִיא בְּ). **"princes" → "chieftains"** (v12, 25) — נָשִׂיא (its row: "one lifted up"). **"terrors by reason of the sword shall be upon my people" → "thrown down to the sword they are, with my people"** — מְגוּרֵי אֶל־חֶרֶב הָיוּ אֶת־עַמִּי: מְגוּרֵי is the passive participle of מָגַר "throw down" (Ps 89:44), not מָגוֹר "terror"; the chieftains are cast to the sword together with the people. **"smite therefore upon thy thigh" → "therefore strike upon the thigh"** — סְפֹק אֶל־יָרֵךְ, no "your"; a gesture of grief (Jer 31:19).
- **"Because it is a trial, and what if the sword contemn even the rod? it shall be no more" → "For it has been tried; and what if even the rod that despises shall not be?"** (v13) — Hebrew כִּי בֹחַן וּמָה אִם־גַּם־שֵׁבֶט מֹאֶסֶת לֹא יִהְיֶה: the hardest line of the song. בֹּחַן is taken as the pual "it has been tried" (בָּחַן "try", the reviewer row; or a noun "a testing"); "the rod that despises" takes up v10. The plainest sense is kept, the line is not completed: it may mean that the trial has come and the despised (or despising) rod will be no more. Readings from the versions are not used.
- **"smite thine hands together" → "strike palm to palm"** (v14; v17 "strike my palm to my palm") — הַךְ כַּף אֶל־כָּף, הִכָּה "strike" (its row). **"and let the sword be doubled the third time" → "and let the sword be doubled, the third time"** — וְתִכָּפֵל חֶרֶב שְׁלִישִׁתָה, nifal of כָּפַל "double"; the word order is kept. **"the sword of the slain: it is the sword of the great men that are slain, which entereth into their privy chambers" → "the sword of the slain; it is the sword of the great slain one, which surrounds them"** — חֶרֶב חֲלָלִים … חֶרֶב חָלָל הַגָּדוֹל הַחֹדֶרֶת לָהֶם: חָלָל "slain" (its row; also v25, 29), here singular with "the great" (perhaps the king of v25: not decided); הַחֹדֶרֶת, found only here as a verb, is "surrounding" (Aramaic) or "entering the inner room" (from חֶדֶר "chamber"): uncertain.
- **"that their heart may faint, and their ruins be multiplied" → "in order that the heart may melt and the stumblings be many"** (v15) — לָמוּג לֵב, מוּג "melt away" (its row); הַמִּכְשֹׁלִים "the stumblings" (no "their"). **"I have set the point of the sword against all their gates" → "against all their gates I have given the point of the sword"** — נָתַתִּי "I have given" (נָתַן); אִבְחַת־חָרֶב, אִבְחָה is found only here: "point", "glitter" and "slaughter" are proposed; the meaning is unknown. **"ah! it is made bright, it is wrapped up for the slaughter" → "Ah! it is made for lightning, it is wrapped up for slaughter"** — אָח עֲשׂוּיָה לְבָרָק מְעֻטָּה לְטָבַח; מְעֻטָּה from עָטָה "wrap" (the earlier "taken up" has no Hebrew base); what "wrapped" means here (gripped, or covered with polish) is uncertain.
- **"Go thee one way or other, either on the right hand, or on the left, whithersoever thy face is set" → "Gather yourself, go to the right; set yourself, go to the left, wherever your face is designated"** (v16) — feminine imperatives to the sword: הִתְאַחֲדִי (hitpael of אָחַד "be one": "gather yourself", or of חָדַד "show yourself sharp"; uncertain), הֵימִנִי "go to the right", הָשִׂימִי "set" ("yourself" supplied: the verb has no object), הַשְׂמִילִי "go to the left"; מֻעָדוֹת, hofal of יָעַד "designate" (its row).
- **"I will cause my fury to rest" → "I will make my fury rest"** (v17) — וַהֲנִחֹתִי חֲמָתִי (as 5:13); חֵמָה "fury" (its row). **"I the LORD have said it" → "I, Yahweh, have spoken"** (v17; also v32) — אֲנִי יְהוָה דִּבַּרְתִּי (the Ezekiel row; by the accents also "I am Yahweh; I have spoken").
- **"appoint thee two ways" → "set for yourself two ways"** (v19, 20) — שִׂים־לְךָ, שִׂים "set". **"both twain shall come forth out of one land" → "from one land the two of them shall go out"** — מֵאֶרֶץ אֶחָד יֵצְאוּ שְׁנֵיהֶם. **"choose thou a place, choose it at the head of the way" → "cut out a signpost; at the head of the way to a city cut it out"** (v19) — וְיָד בָּרֵא … בָּרֵא: יָד, literally "hand", is a marker set up (as 1 Sam 15:12; 2 Sam 18:18 "monument"); בֵּרֵא piel "cut" (the row has "cut down" of a forest: here a sign is cut out); "the way of a city".
- **"Rabbath of the Ammonites" → "Rabbah of the sons of Ammon"** (v20; also v28) — Hebrew רַבַּת בְּנֵי־עַמּוֹן: בְּנֵי "sons of" (Standing decisions), as Jer 49:2; the earlier `ammonites` token gave the gentilic for two words. **"to Judah in Jerusalem the defenced" → "to Judah, into fortified Jerusalem"** — בִירוּשָׁלִַם בְּצוּרָה, בָּצוּר "fortified" (its row), feminine of the city.
- **"For the king of Babylon stood at the parting of the way" → "has stood"** (v21) — עָמַד and the three verbs after it are perfects, kept as perfects; אֵם הַדֶּרֶךְ, literally "the mother of the way" (where the ways divide). **"to use divination" → "to divine a divination"** — לִקְסָם־קָסֶם, verb and noun (the קֶסֶם row; also v22, 23, 29). **"he made his arrows bright" → "he has shaken the arrows"** — קִלְקַל בַּחִצִּים, pilpel of קָלַל "shake" (casting lots with arrows); "bright" takes the other sense of the root (Eccl 10:10). **"he consulted with images" → "he has asked of the teraphim"** — שָׁאַל בַּתְּרָפִים (the token kept; as "ask of Elohim", Judg 18:5). **"he looked in the liver" → "he has looked at the liver"** — רָאָה בַּכָּבֵד.
- **"At his right hand was the divination for Jerusalem" → "In his right hand was the divination, Jerusalem"** (v22) — בִּימִינוֹ הָיָה הַקֶּסֶם יְרוּשָׁלִַם: the lot that came up in the right hand named Jerusalem; "for" is not written. **"to appoint captains" → "to set battering rams"** — לָשׂוּם כָּרִים (twice), כַּר "ram" of war (as 4:2). **"to open the mouth in the slaughter" → "to open the mouth with murder"** — בְּרֶצַח, רֶצַח the noun of רָצַח "murder" (its row); "with a war cry" (בִּצְרַח) is a correction of the letters, not followed. **"with shouting" → "with a shout"** — תְּרוּעָה of the voice (the reviewer row). **"to cast a mount, and to build a fort" → "to pour out a mound, to build a siege wall"** — לִשְׁפֹּךְ סֹלְלָה, literally "pour out" (as Jer 6:6); דָּיֵק "siege wall" (its row; uncertain).
- **"And it shall be unto them as a false divination in their sight" → "as an empty divination in their eyes"** (v23) — the reading (qere) כִּקְסָם "as a divination"; the written text (ketiv) כקסום "as divining", the same sense; שָׁוְא "empty" (its row), בְּעֵינֵיהֶם "in their eyes". **"to them that have sworn oaths" → "sworn oaths are theirs"** — שְׁבֻעֵי שְׁבֻעוֹת לָהֶם, literally "sworn ones of oaths (are) to them": who swore to whom (Judah to Babylon, 17:13–19) is not said; uncertain. **"but he will call to remembrance the iniquity, that they may be taken" → "but he brings iniquity to remembrance, that they may be seized"** — וְהוּא־מַזְכִּיר עָוֺן לְהִתָּפֵשׂ, a participle; עָוֺן "iniquity" (its row; also v24, 25, 29), no "their"; תָּפַשׂ "seize" (also v24).
- **"Because ye have made your iniquity to be remembered, in that your transgressions are discovered, so that in all your doings your sins do appear" → "Because you have brought your iniquity to remembrance, in that your transgressions are uncovered, so that your sins appear in all your deeds"** (v24) — הַזְכַּרְכֶם (hifil of זָכַר), בְּהִגָּלוֹת (nifal of גָּלָה), לְהֵרָאוֹת; עֲלִילוֹת "deeds". **"because, I say, that ye are come to remembrance, ye shall be taken with the hand" → "because you have been remembered, in the palm you shall be seized"** — הִזָּכֶרְכֶם, nifal; בַּכַּף "in the palm" (no "I say").
- **"And thou, profane wicked prince of Israel" → "And you, slain wicked one, chieftain of Israel"** (v25) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה חָלָל רָשָׁע נְשִׂיא יִשְׂרָאֵל: חָלָל is the word of v14 ("slain", one pierced; as v29 חַלְלֵי רְשָׁעִים "the slain of the wicked"); KJV "profane" takes the adjective "profaned" of the same letters (Lev 21:7). No "O" (the reviewer's vocative row). **"when iniquity shall have an end" → "in the time of the iniquity of the end"** (v25, 29) — בְּעֵת עֲוֺן קֵץ, word for word: the iniquity that brings the end (or the punishment of the end; עָוֺן includes what guilt brings, its row).
- **"Remove the diadem, and take off the crown" → "Remove the turban, and lift off the crown"** (v26) — הָסִיר … וְהָרִים, infinitives absolute used as commands; הָרִים "raise, lift" (its row): the crown is lifted off; מִצְנֶפֶת "turban" (its row: the high priest's headdress, Exod 28:4), עֲטָרָה "crown". **"this shall not be the same" → "this is not this"** — זֹאת לֹא־זֹאת, word for word: things shall not remain as they are. **"exalt him that is low, and abase him that is high" → "make high what is low, and bring low what is high"** — infinitives absolute, הַשָּׁפָלָה (feminine) and הַגָּבֹהַ (masculine), as 17:24.
- **"I will overturn, overturn, overturn, it" → "A ruin, a ruin, a ruin I will make it"** (v27) — Hebrew עַוָּה עַוָּה עַוָּה אֲשִׂימֶנָּה: עַוָּה, found only here, from עָוָה "twist, distort": "a ruin, a wreck"; "I will make it" (שִׂים). **"and it shall be no more" → "this also shall not be"** — גַּם־זֹאת לֹא הָיָה, a perfect ("this also has not been"), taken of the future in the prophecy. **"until he come whose right it is; and I will give it him" → "until he comes whose is the judgment, and I will give it"** — עַד־בֹּא אֲשֶׁר־לוֹ הַמִּשְׁפָּט וּנְתַתִּיו, word for word: מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (its row: the right to judge, or the judgment to be carried out: not decided); וּנְתַתִּיו "and I will give it" (or "him": the suffix allows both). The words recall Gen 49:10 עַד כִּי־יָבֹא שִׁילֹה, "until Shiloh comes", read by many as "until he comes to whom it belongs" — a reading of that verse, not of this one; nothing of it goes into the verse.
- **"concerning the Ammonites, and concerning their reproach" → "concerning the sons of Ammon and concerning their reproach"** (v28) — אֶל … וְאֶל, חֶרְפָּה "reproach" (its row). **"The sword, the sword is drawn" → "A sword, a sword is drawn"** — פְּתוּחָה, literally "opened" (Ps 37:14). **"for the slaughter it is furbished, to consume because of the glittering" → "for slaughter it is polished, to hold, in order to be lightning"** — לְהָכִיל, hifil of כּוּל "hold, contain" (as Jer 2:13); "to consume" (from אָכַל) is a correction, not followed; the sense of "to hold" here is uncertain. לְמַעַן בָּרָק, literally "for the sake of lightning".
- **"Whiles they see vanity unto thee, whiles they divine a lie unto thee" → "while they behold emptiness for you, while they divine a lie for you"** (v29) — בַּחֲזוֹת לָךְ שָׁוְא בִּקְסָם־לָךְ כָּזָב, the words of 13:6–9, 23 (חָזָה "behold", שָׁוְא "emptiness", כָּזָב "lie"); infinitives, "they" supplied for English; "you" is feminine (the sword, or the city). **"to bring thee upon the necks of them that are slain, of the wicked" → "to put you on the necks of the slain of the wicked"** — לָתֵת, literally "to give".
- **"Shall I cause it to return into his sheath?" → "Return it to its sheath!"** (v30) — הָשַׁב, hifil imperative; there is no question in the Hebrew. **"in the land of thy nativity" → "in the land of your origin"** — מְכֻרוֹתַיִךְ (as 16:3; the root is uncertain).
- **"mine indignation" → "my indignation"** (v31) — זַעַם (its row). **"I will blow against thee in the fire of my wrath" → "with the fire of my raging I will blow on you"** — עֶבְרָה "raging" (its row), the Hebrew order. **"into the hand of brutish men, and skilful to destroy" → "into the hand of brutish men, craftsmen of destruction"** — בֹּעֲרִים "brutish" (its row; the same letters say "burning", a play with the fire of the line before: not decided); חָרָשֵׁי מַשְׁחִית, חָרָשׁ "craftsman" (its row).
- **"Thou shalt be for fuel to the fire" → "To the fire you shall be for food"** (v32) — לָאֵשׁ תִּהְיֶה לְאָכְלָה, אָכְלָה "food" (as 15:4, 6). **"thou shalt be no more remembered" → "you shall not be remembered"** — לֹא תִזָּכֵרִי, no "more".

### Notes
- **Numbering** — our 21:1–32 are Hebrew 21:6–37; our 20:45–49 are Hebrew 21:1–5.
