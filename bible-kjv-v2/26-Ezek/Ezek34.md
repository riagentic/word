# Ezekiel 34

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[2] "Son of man, prophesy against the shepherds of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; prophesy, and say to them, to the shepherds: Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Woe, shepherds of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, who have been shepherding themselves! Is it not the flock that the shepherds should shepherd?

[3] The fat you eat, and with the wool you clothe yourselves; the fat one you slaughter; the flock you do not shepherd.

[4] The weakened ones you have not strengthened, and the sick one you have not healed, and the broken one you have not bound up, and the driven-away one you have not brought back, and the lost one you have not sought; and with force you have had dominion over them, and with harshness.

[5] And they were scattered for lack of a shepherd, and they became food for every living thing of the field, and they were scattered.

[6] My flock strays on all the mountains and on every high hill; and over all the face of the earth my flock has been scattered, and there is none seeking out and none seeking.

[7] "Therefore, shepherds, hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

[8] As I live, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, surely because my flock has become a plunder, and my flock has become food for every living thing of the field for lack of a shepherd, and my shepherds have not sought out my flock, and the shepherds have shepherded themselves, and my flock they have not shepherded —

[9] therefore, shepherds, hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

[10] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I am against the shepherds, and I will require my flock from their hand, and make them cease from shepherding the flock, and the shepherds shall shepherd themselves no more; and I will deliver my flock from their mouth, and they shall not be food for them.

[11] "For thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I, I will seek out my flock and search them out.

[12] As a shepherd's searching of his drove in the day that he is among his flock that are spread out, so I will search my flock, and I will deliver them from all the places where they have been scattered in a day of cloud and dark cloud.

[13] And I will bring them out from the peoples, and gather them from the lands, and bring them to their ground; and I will shepherd them on the mountains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, by the channels, and in all the dwelling places of the land.

[14] In a good pasture I will shepherd them, and on the mountains of the height of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall be their habitation; there they shall lie down in a good habitation, and on fat pasture they shall feed on the mountains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[15] I myself will shepherd my flock, and I myself will make them lie down, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[16] The lost one I will seek, and the driven-away one I will bring back, and the broken one I will bind up, and the sick one I will strengthen; and the fat one and the strong one I will destroy; I will shepherd it in judgment.

[17] "And you, my flock, thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I am judging between sheep and sheep, as to the rams and the he-goats.

[18] Is it too little for you that you feed on the good pasture, and the rest of your pastures you trample with your feet; and that you drink the settled water, and what is left you foul with your feet?

[19] And my flock — what your feet have trampled they feed on, and what your feet have fouled they drink.

[20] "Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ to them: Behold, I, I will judge between the fat sheep and the lean sheep.

[21] Because with side and with shoulder you push, and with your horns you gore all the weakened ones, until you have scattered them outside,

[22] I will save my flock, and they shall no longer be a plunder; and I will judge between sheep and sheep.

[23] And I will raise up over them one shepherd, and he shall shepherd them: **my servant David**; he shall shepherd them, and he shall be their shepherd.

[24] And I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, will be their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and my servant David chieftain among them; I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have spoken.

[25] "And I will cut for them a covenant of ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, and I will make the evil living thing cease from the land; and they shall dwell in the wilderness in security, and sleep in the forests.

[26] And I will make them and the surroundings of my hill a blessing; and I will bring down the rain in its time; rains of blessing they shall be.

[27] And the tree of the field shall give its fruit, and the land shall give its produce, and they shall be on their ground in security; and they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, when I break the yoke-bars of their yoke and deliver them from the hand of those who made them serve.

[28] And they shall no longer be a plunder to the nations, and the living thing of the land shall not devour them; and they shall dwell in security, and none shall make them tremble.

[29] And I will raise up for them a planting for a name, and they shall no longer be gathered away by famine in the land, and they shall no longer bear the dishonor of the nations.

[30] And they shall know that I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, am with them, and they, the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, are my people, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[31] And you, my flock, the flock of my pasture — man you are; I am your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v2, 8, 10, 11, 15, 17, 20, 30, 31) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words (token `adonai-yhwh`). **"God" → "Elohim"** (v24, 30, 31).
- **"And the word of the LORD came unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר, the prophets' formula.
- **"feed" → "shepherd"** (throughout) — רָעָה, the verb of רֹעֶה "shepherd" (its rows): "shepherd" where the shepherds or Yahweh act (v2, 3, 8, 10, 13, 14, 15, 16, 23); "feed" where the flock itself grazes (v14, 18, 19). צֹאן "flock" (one collective word, sheep and goats: its row; KJV "flock" / "sheep"); מַרְעִית "pasture" (v31, as Jer 23:1).
- **"Thus saith the Lord GOD unto the shepherds; Woe be to the shepherds of Israel that do feed themselves!" → "and say to them, to the shepherds: Thus says Lord Yahweh: Woe, shepherds of Israel, who have been shepherding themselves!"** (v2) — Hebrew וְאָמַרְתָּ אֲלֵיהֶם לָרֹעִים כֹּה אָמַר: "to the shepherds" stands before "Thus says"; הוֹי "Woe" (PROPHETS.md) with the vocative, no "to"; אֲשֶׁר הָיוּ רֹעִים אוֹתָם, literally "who have been shepherding them(selves)". **"should not the shepherds feed the flocks?" → "Is it not the flock that the shepherds should shepherd?"** — הֲלוֹא הַצֹּאן יִרְעוּ הָרֹעִים, the flock set first.
- **"Ye eat the fat … ye kill them that are fed" → "The fat you eat … the fat one you slaughter"** (v3) — Hebrew אֶת־הַחֵלֶב "the fat" (its row; read with other vowels הֶחָלָב "the milk", as the Greek is said to have it, from memory: MT kept); הַבְּרִיאָה "the fat one" (בָּרִיא "fat", its row: English has one word for both); זָבַח of killing for food "slaughter" (its row). In the Hebrew order throughout.
- **"The diseased have ye not strengthened … that which was driven away … that which was lost" → "The weakened ones … the driven-away one … the lost one"** (v4, 16, 21) — Hebrew הַנַּחְלוֹת, nifal participle of חָלָה, "the ones made weak" (plural); הַחוֹלָה "the sick one"; הַנִּשְׁבֶּרֶת "the broken one"; הַנִּדַּחַת "the driven-away one" (the נִדָּח row); הָאֹבֶדֶת, participle of אָבַד "perish": "the lost one" (the one perishing); חִזֵּק "strengthen" (the חִזֵּק "make strong" row), חָבַשׁ "bind up", הֵשִׁיב "bring back", בִּקֵּשׁ "seek". **"but with force and with cruelty have ye ruled them" → "and with force you have had dominion over them, and with harshness"** — וּבְחָזְקָה רְדִיתֶם אֹתָם וּבְפָרֶךְ: רָדָה "have dominion" (its row), פֶּרֶךְ "harshness" (its row names this verse); "and with harshness" stands last, as in the Hebrew.
- **"because there is no shepherd: and they became meat to all the beasts of the field, when they were scattered" → "for lack of a shepherd, and they became food for every living thing of the field, and they were scattered"** (v5, 8) — Hebrew מִבְּלִי רֹעֶה; לְאָכְלָה "for food"; כָּל־חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה "every living thing of the field" (חַיָּה, its row; not "wild beasts"); וַתְּפוּצֶינָה is said twice (v5), the second time closing the verse.
- **"My sheep wandered through all the mountains" → "My flock strays on all the mountains"** (v6) — Hebrew יִשְׁגּוּ, imperfect of שָׁגָה (of animals "stray"; the root is the "err" of its row). **"yea, my flock was scattered" → "and … my flock has been scattered"** — נָפֹצוּ, nifal of פּוּץ (its row). **"and none did search or seek after them" → "and there is none seeking out and none seeking"** — וְאֵין דּוֹרֵשׁ וְאֵין מְבַקֵּשׁ: two verbs. In this chapter דָּרַשׁ is "seek out" (v6, 8, 11; with מִיָּד "require", v10), בִּקֵּשׁ "seek" (v4, 6, 16), בִּקֵּר "search" (v11, 12; noun בַּקָּרָה "searching").
- **"surely because my flock became a prey" → "surely because my flock has become a plunder"** (v8, 22, 28) — Hebrew אִם־לֹא יַעַן, the oath form, "surely"; לָבַז / בַּז "plunder" (its row). **"neither did my shepherds search for my flock, but the shepherds fed themselves" → "and my shepherds have not sought out my flock, and the shepherds have shepherded themselves"** — וְלֹא־דָרְשׁוּ, וַיִּרְעוּ, joined by "and". The sworn sentence breaks off (dash) and starts again in v9 "therefore".
- **"Therefore, O ye shepherds" → "therefore, shepherds"** (v9) — Hebrew לָכֵן הָרֹעִים, vocative without "O" (reviewer row; also v7).
- **"and cause them to cease from feeding the flock" → "and make them cease from shepherding the flock"** (v10) — Hebrew וְהִשְׁבַּתִּים, hifil of שָׁבַת "cease" (also v25). **"for I will deliver my flock from their mouth" → "and I will deliver my flock from their mouth"** — וְהִצַּלְתִּי (הִצִּיל "deliver"). **"that they may not be meat for them" → "and they shall not be food for them"** — וְלֹא־תִהְיֶיןָ.
- **"Behold, I, even I, will both search my sheep, and seek them out" → "Behold, I, I will seek out my flock and search them out"** (v11, 20) — Hebrew הִנְנִי־אָנִי, literally "behold me, I"; וְדָרַשְׁתִּי … וּבִקַּרְתִּים.
- **"As a shepherd seeketh out his flock in the day that he is among his sheep that are scattered" → "As a shepherd's searching of his drove in the day that he is among his flock that are spread out"** (v12) — Hebrew כְּבַקָּרַת רֹעֶה עֶדְרוֹ, the noun "searching"; עֵדֶר "drove" (its row) beside צֹאן "flock"; נִפְרָשׁוֹת "spread out" (another verb than פּוּץ "scatter"). **"in the cloudy and dark day" → "in a day of cloud and dark cloud"** — בְּיוֹם עָנָן וַעֲרָפֶל (עָנָן "cloud", עֲרָפֶל "dark cloud": their rows).
- **"from the people … from the countries … to their own land … by the rivers, and in all the inhabited places of the country" → "from the peoples … from the lands … to their ground … by the channels, and in all the dwelling places of the land"** (v13) — Hebrew מִן־הָעַמִּים, מִן־הָאֲרָצוֹת, אֶל־אַדְמָתָם (אֲדָמָה "ground"; also v27), בָּאֲפִיקִים (אָפִיק "channel", reviewer row), מוֹשְׁבֵי הָאָרֶץ (מוֹשָׁב "dwelling place", its row).
- **"upon the high mountains of Israel shall their fold be: there shall they lie in a good fold, and in a fat pasture" → "on the mountains of the height of Israel shall be their habitation; there they shall lie down in a good habitation, and on fat pasture"** (v14) — Hebrew בְּהָרֵי מְרוֹם־יִשְׂרָאֵל; נָוֶה "habitation" (its row; lit. "pasture, abode"; as Jer 23:3); רָבַץ "lie (down)" (its row; hifil "make lie down", v15); מִרְעֶה "pasture"; שָׁמֵן "fat" (also v16): English has one word for בָּרִיא, שָׁמֵן and the noun חֵלֶב.
- **"I will feed my flock, and I will cause them to lie down" → "I myself will shepherd my flock, and I myself will make them lie down"** (v15) — Hebrew אֲנִי … וַאֲנִי, the pronoun written twice.
- **"but I will destroy the fat and the strong; I will feed them with judgment" → "and the fat one and the strong one I will destroy; I will shepherd it in judgment"** (v16) — Hebrew אַשְׁמִיד "I will destroy" (the Greek and Syriac are said to have "I will guard", אֶשְׁמֹר, from memory: MT kept); אֶרְעֶנָּה "I will shepherd her", singular (the flock); בְמִשְׁפָּט "in judgment".
- **"Behold, I judge between cattle and cattle, between the rams and the he goats" → "Behold, I am judging between sheep and sheep, as to the rams and the he-goats"** (v17, 20, 22) — Hebrew הִנְנִי שֹׁפֵט, participle; בֵּין־שֶׂה לָשֶׂה: שֶׂה "sheep" (one animal of the flock, sheep or goat: its row; not "cattle"); לָאֵילִים וְלָעַתּוּדִים "as to the rams and the he-goats" (לְ; עַתּוּד "he-goat", its row).
- **"Seemeth it a small thing unto you to have eaten up the good pasture" → "Is it too little for you that you feed on the good pasture"** (v18) — Hebrew הַמְעַט מִכֶּם, literally "is it little, from you"; תִּרְעוּ "you feed" (imperfect). **"the residue of your pastures" → "the rest of your pastures"** — יֶתֶר "rest" (its row). **"tread down" → "trample"** — רָמַס. **"to have drunk of the deep waters" → "that you drink the settled water"** — מִשְׁקַע־מַיִם, literally "the settling of water" (water that has settled clear; only here). **"but ye must foul the residue with your feet" → "and what is left you foul with your feet"** — הַנּוֹתָרִים (נוֹתַר "remain", its row), רָפַשׂ "foul" (make muddy).
- **"And as for my flock, they eat that which ye have trodden" → "And my flock — what your feet have trampled they feed on"** (v19) — Hebrew מִרְמַס רַגְלֵיכֶם, מִרְפַּשׂ רַגְלֵיכֶם, nouns: "the trampling of your feet", "the fouling of your feet"; תִּרְעֶינָה "they feed".
- **"between the fat cattle and between the lean cattle" → "between the fat sheep and the lean sheep"** (v20) — Hebrew בֵּין־שֶׂה בִרְיָה וּבֵין שֶׂה רָזָה (בָּרִיא "fat" as v3; רָזֶה "lean").
- **"Because ye have thrust with side and with shoulder, and pushed all the diseased with your horns" → "Because with side and with shoulder you push, and with your horns you gore all the weakened ones"** (v21) — Hebrew תֶּהְדֹּפוּ (הָדַף "push"), תְּנַגְּחוּ (נָגַח "gore": their rows), imperfects; הֲפִיצוֹתֶם אוֹתָנָה אֶל־הַחוּצָה "you have scattered them to the outside".
- **"Therefore will I save my flock" → "I will save my flock"** (v22) — Hebrew וְהוֹשַׁעְתִּי, the וְ opening the result of v21 (no "therefore" written).
- **"And I will set up one shepherd over them, and he shall feed them, even my servant David" → "And I will raise up over them one shepherd, and he shall shepherd them: my servant David"** (v23) — Hebrew וַהֲקִמֹתִי (הֵקִים "raise up"), וְרָעָה אֶתְהֶן, אֵת עַבְדִּי דָוִיד (the object marker before the name: "my servant David" is the object of "I will raise up"); עֶבֶד of Yahweh "servant" (its row), as printed. **"and he shall be their shepherd" → "and he shall be their shepherd"** — וְהוּא־יִהְיֶה לָהֶן לְרֹעֶה.
- **"And I the LORD will be their God, and my servant David a prince among them" → "And I, Yahweh, will be their Elohim, and my servant David chieftain among them"** (v24) — Hebrew אֶהְיֶה לָהֶם לֵאלֹהִים, literally "I will be to them for Elohim"; נָשִׂיא "chieftain" (its row; lit. "one lifted up"; KJV "prince"; as 7:27; 12:10). **"I the LORD have spoken it" → "I, Yahweh, have spoken"** — דִּבַּרְתִּי, no "it".
- **"And I will make with them a covenant of peace" → "And I will cut for them a covenant of shalom"** (v25) — Hebrew וְכָרַתִּי לָהֶם בְּרִית שָׁלוֹם (כָּרַת בְּרִית "cut a covenant", its row; שָׁלוֹם token, reviewer row). **"and will cause the evil beasts to cease out of the land" → "and I will make the evil living thing cease from the land"** — חַיָּה־רָעָה, singular (collective). **"and they shall dwell safely in the wilderness, and sleep in the woods" → "and they shall dwell in the wilderness in security, and sleep in the forests"** — לָבֶטַח "in security" (its row; also v27, 28), יַעַר "forest" (its row).
- **"and the places round about my hill" → "and the surroundings of my hill"** (v26) — Hebrew סְבִיבוֹת גִּבְעָתִי (גִּבְעָה "hill", its row). **"and I will cause the shower to come down in his season; there shall be showers of blessing" → "and I will bring down the rain in its time; rains of blessing they shall be"** — הוֹרַדְתִּי הַגֶּשֶׁם (גֶּשֶׁם "rain", its row), בְּעִתּוֹ, גִּשְׁמֵי בְרָכָה יִהְיוּ in the Hebrew order.
- **"And the tree of the field shall yield her fruit, and the earth shall yield her increase" → "And the tree of the field shall give its fruit, and the land shall give its produce"** (v27) — Hebrew וְנָתַן … וְהָאָרֶץ תִּתֵּן יְבוּלָהּ: נָתַן "give" twice; יְבוּל "produce" (its row); הָאָרֶץ "the land" (as v25, 28). **"and they shall be safe in their land, and shall know" → "and they shall be on their ground in security; and they shall know"** — עַל־אַדְמָתָם לָבֶטַח; וְיָדְעוּ כִּי־אֲנִי יְהוָה, Ezekiel's formula. **"when I have broken the bands of their yoke" → "when I break the yoke-bars of their yoke"** — מֹטוֹת עֻלָּם (מוֹטָה "yoke-bar", its row; עֹל "yoke"). **"those that served themselves of them" → "those who made them serve"** — הָעֹבְדִים בָּהֶם (עָבַד בְּ "make serve", its row; literally "who served by them").
- **"a prey to the heathen, neither shall the beast of the land devour them" → "a plunder to the nations, and the living thing of the land shall not devour them"** (v28) — Hebrew לַגּוֹיִם (גּוֹי "nation"), חַיַּת הָאָרֶץ. **"and none shall make them afraid" → "and none shall make them tremble"** — וְאֵין מַחֲרִיד (הֶחֱרִיד "make tremble", its row), literally "and there is none making tremble".
- **"And I will raise up for them a plant of renown" → "And I will raise up for them a planting for a name"** (v29) — Hebrew מַטָּע לְשֵׁם, literally "a planting for a name" (= one of renown); the Greek is said to read "a planting of peace" (from memory): MT kept. **"and they shall be no more consumed with hunger in the land" → "and they shall no longer be gathered away by famine in the land"** — אֲסֻפֵי רָעָב, literally "ones gathered (away) of famine" (אָסַף "gather"; רָעָב "famine", its row). **"neither bear the shame of the heathen any more" → "and they shall no longer bear the dishonor of the nations"** — כְּלִמָּה "dishonor" (reviewer row).
- **"Thus shall they know that I the LORD their God am with them" → "And they shall know that I, Yahweh their Elohim, am with them"** (v30) — Hebrew וְיָדְעוּ כִּי אֲנִי יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵיהֶם אִתָּם: also readable as the formula "that I am Yahweh their Elohim", with "with them" added; "and" (not "thus"). **"and that they, even the house of Israel, are my people" → "and they, the house of Israel, are my people"** — וְהֵמָּה עַמִּי בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"And ye my flock, the flock of my pasture, are men" → "And you, my flock, the flock of my pasture — man you are"** (v31) — Hebrew אָדָם אַתֶּם, literally "man (mankind) you are" (אָדָם, Standing decisions; the Greek is said to lack the word, from memory). **"and I am your God" → "I am your Elohim"** — no "and".
