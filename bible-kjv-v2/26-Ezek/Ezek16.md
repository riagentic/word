# Ezekiel 16

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[2] "Son of man, make ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ know her abominations,

[3] and say, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧: Your origins and your births are from the land of the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧; your father was the ⟦amorite|Emori|Amorite⟧, and your mother a ⟦hittite|Chitti|Hittite⟧.

[4] And as for your births, in the day you were born your navel was not cut, and in water you were not washed for cleansing, and you were surely not rubbed with salt, and you were surely not swaddled.

[5] No eye pitied you, to do to you one of these, for pity on you; and you were thrown out on the face of the field, in the abhorring of your soul, in the day you were born.

[6] And I passed by you and saw you kicking about in your blood, and I said to you in your blood, Live! And I said to you in your blood, Live!

[7] Ten thousand, like the sprout of the field, I made you; and you grew many, and became great, and came into the ornament of ornaments. Breasts were set firm, and your hair had sprouted; and you were naked and bare.

[8] And I passed by you and saw you, and behold, your time was the time of love; and I spread my corner over you and covered your nakedness; and I swore to you and entered into a covenant with you, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, and you became mine.

[9] And I washed you with water, and rinsed your blood from upon you, and anointed you with oil.

[10] And I clothed you with embroidery, and shod you with tachash, and bound you round with fine linen, and covered you with silk.

[11] And I adorned you with ornaments, and put bracelets on your hands and a chain on your throat.

[12] And I put a ring on your nose, and earrings on your ears, and a crown of beauty on your head.

[13] And you were adorned with gold and silver, and your clothing was fine linen and silk and embroidery; fine flour and honey and oil you ate. And you were very, very beautiful, and you prospered into kingship.

[14] And a name went out for you among the nations for your beauty, for it was whole through my splendor that I had set upon you, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[15] "And you trusted in your beauty, and played the prostitute because of your name, and poured out your prostitutions on everyone passing by: to him let it be.

[16] And you took of your garments, and made for yourself spotted high places, and played the prostitute on them — things not coming, and it shall not be.

[17] And you took the articles of your beauty, of my gold and of my silver, which I had given you, and made for yourself images of a male, and played the prostitute with them.

[18] And you took your garments of embroidery and covered them, and my oil and my incense you set before them.

[19] And my bread which I gave you — fine flour and oil and honey I fed you — you set it before them for a soothing smell; and it was, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[20] "And you took your sons and your daughters, whom you had borne to me, and you sacrificed them to them for eating. Were your prostitutions a little thing,

[21] that you slaughtered my sons, and gave them, in making them pass over to them?

[22] And in all your abominations and your prostitutions you did not remember the days of your youth, when you were naked and bare; kicking about in your blood you were.

[23] "And it came to pass after all your evil — woe, woe to you! declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ —

[24] that you built for yourself a mound, and made for yourself a raised place in every square.

[25] At every head of a way you built your raised place, and made your beauty abominable, and spread wide your legs to everyone passing by, and multiplied your prostitutions.

[26] And you played the prostitute with the sons of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, your neighbors, great of flesh, and multiplied your prostitution, to vex me.

[27] And behold, I stretched out my hand against you, and diminished your fixed portion, and gave you into the soul of those who hate you, the daughters of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, who were put to shame by your way of depravity.

[28] And you played the prostitute with the sons of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, because you were not sated; and you played the prostitute with them, and still you were not sated.

[29] And you multiplied your prostitution to the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, to ⟦chaldea|Kasdim|Chaldea⟧, and even with this you were not sated.

[30] "How languishing is your heart, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, in your doing all these, the work of a domineering prostitute woman,

[31] in your building your mound at the head of every way, and making your raised place in every square! And you were not like the prostitute, in scorning hire.

[32] The wife who commits adultery: in place of her husband she takes strangers!

[33] To all prostitutes they give a gift; and you, you gave your gifts to all your lovers, and bribed them to come to you from all around in your prostitutions.

[34] And there was in you the reverse from the women in your prostitutions: and after you none goes as a prostitute; and in your giving hire, while hire is not given to you, you have become the reverse.

[35] "Therefore, prostitute, hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[36] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Because your copper was poured out, and your nakedness uncovered in your prostitutions with your lovers, and with all the idols of your abominations, and according to the blood of your sons, which you gave to them,

[37] therefore behold, I am gathering all your lovers, to whom you were pleasing, and all whom you loved, together with all whom you hated; and I will gather them against you from all around, and uncover your nakedness to them, and they shall see all your nakedness.

[38] And I will judge you with the judgments of women who commit adultery and who pour out blood, and I will give you blood of fury and jealousy.

[39] And I will give you into their hand, and they shall throw down your mound and break down your raised places, and strip you of your garments, and take the articles of your beauty, and leave you naked and bare.

[40] And they shall bring up an assembly against you, and stone you with stones, and cut you in pieces with their swords.

[41] And they shall burn your houses with fire, and do judgments on you before the eyes of many women; and I will make you cease from being a prostitute, and also hire you shall give no more.

[42] And I will make my fury rest on you, and my jealousy shall turn aside from you; and I will be quiet, and I will not be vexed any more.

[43] Because you did not remember the days of your youth, and were agitated against me in all these, then I too, behold, have laid your way upon your head, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧; and have you not done the depravity on top of all your abominations?

[44] "Behold, everyone who speaks a proverb shall speak this proverb against you, saying, As the mother, her daughter.

[45] Your mother's daughter you are, who abhorred her husband and her sons; and the sister of your sisters you are, who abhorred their husbands and their sons. Your mother was a ⟦hittite|Chitti|Hittite⟧, and your father an ⟦amorite|Emori|Amorite⟧.

[46] And your elder sister is ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, she and her daughters, who dwells north of you; and your sister younger than you, who dwells south of you, is ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ and her daughters.

[47] And not in their ways have you walked, nor according to their abominations have you done — as a very little thing — and you worked ruin more than they in all your ways.

[48] As I live, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ your sister has not done, she and her daughters, as you have done, you and your daughters.

[49] Behold, this was the iniquity of ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ your sister: pride, satiety of bread, and the ease of being quiet were hers and her daughters'; and the hand of the afflicted and the needy she did not strengthen.

[50] And they were haughty, and did an abomination before me; and I removed them when I saw it.

[51] And ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ has not sinned even half of your sins; and you have multiplied your abominations more than they, and have made your sisters righteous by all your abominations which you have done.

[52] You also, bear your dishonor, you who have intervened for your sisters; by your sins, in which you acted more abominably than they, they are more righteous than you. And you also, be ashamed and bear your dishonor, in your making your sisters righteous.

[53] "And I will restore their fortunes, the fortunes of ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ and her daughters, and the fortunes of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ and her daughters, and the fortunes of your fortunes in their midst,

[54] in order that you may bear your dishonor and be put to shame for all that you have done, in your comforting them.

[55] And your sisters, ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ and her daughters, shall return to their former state, and ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ and her daughters shall return to their former state, and you and your daughters shall return to your former state.

[56] And ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ your sister was not a report in your mouth in the day of your pride,

[57] before your evil was uncovered, as at the time of the reproach of the daughters of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ and all around her, the daughters of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, who despise you from all around.

[58] Your depravity and your abominations, you have borne them, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[59] "For thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: And I will do with you as you have done, you who despised the oath, to break the covenant.

[60] And I, I will remember my covenant with you in the days of your youth, and I will establish for you an everlasting covenant.

[61] And you shall remember your ways and be put to shame, when you take your sisters, those older than you, together with those younger than you; and I will give them to you as daughters, but not from your covenant.

[62] And I, I will establish my covenant with you, and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[63] in order that you may remember and be ashamed, and there may be to you no more an opening of the mouth because of your dishonor, when I make atonement for you for all that you have done, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v3, 8, 14, 19, 23, 30, 36, 43, 48, 59, 63) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both names; v58 has יְהוָה alone.
- **The language of this chapter** — the Hebrew speaks plainly of birth, the body, sex and prostitution, as a picture of Jerusalem and her worship of other gods and her trust in other nations. The verses keep its words as they stand, neither softened nor made coarser. זָנָה "play the prostitute", זוֹנָה "prostitute", and Ezekiel's noun תַּזְנוּת "prostitution", plural "prostitutions" (as זְנוּת, Jer 3:2, 9; 13:27); not "harlot", "whore", "fornication".
- **"Again the word of the LORD came unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר; "Again" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Thy birth and thy nativity is of the land of Canaan" → "Your origins and your births are from the land of the Canaanite"** (v3) — Hebrew מְכֹרֹתַיִךְ וּמֹלְדֹתַיִךְ מֵאֶרֶץ הַכְּנַעֲנִי: both nouns plural; מְכוּרָה "origin" (also 21:30; 29:14); מוֹלֶדֶת "birth" (also v4); הַכְּנַעֲנִי "the Canaanite", singular with the article (as Exod 13:5). **"thy father was an Amorite" → "your father was the Amorite"** — הָאֱמֹרִי, with the article; חִתִּית "a Hittite" (feminine).
- **"thy navel was not cut" → "your navel was not cut"** (v4) — Hebrew לֹא־כָרַּת שָׁרֵּךְ, שֹׁר "navel" (the cord). **"neither wast thou washed in water to supple thee" → "and in water you were not washed for cleansing"** — לְמִשְׁעִי, found only here: "for cleansing / smoothing" (uncertain). **"thou wast not salted at all, nor swaddled at all" → "you were surely not rubbed with salt, and you were surely not swaddled"** — וְהָמְלֵחַ לֹא הֻמְלַחַתְּ וְהָחְתֵּל לֹא חֻתָּלְתְּ, two infinitives absolute with their verbs (literally "and salting you were not salted, and swaddling you were not swaddled"); מָלַח "salt", here "rub with salt". בְּיוֹם הוּלֶּדֶת אֹתָךְ "in the day of the being born of you" (also v5).
- **"None eye pitied thee, to do any of these unto thee, to have compassion upon thee" → "No eye pitied you, to do to you one of these, for pity on you"** (v5) — Hebrew לֹא־חָסָה עָלַיִךְ עַיִן … אַחַת מֵאֵלֶּה לְחֻמְלָה עָלָיִךְ: חוּס "pity" (as 5:11), חֶמְלָה "pity", the noun of חָמַל "have pity" (two roots, one English); אַחַת "one". **"but thou wast cast out in the open field, to the lothing of thy person" → "and you were thrown out on the face of the field, in the abhorring of your soul"** — וַתֻּשְׁלְכִי אֶל־פְּנֵי הַשָּׂדֶה בְּגֹעַל נַפְשֵׁךְ: הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw"; "the face of the field"; גֹּעַל "abhorring", the noun of גָּעַל "abhor" (v45): your soul was abhorred (or "in the abhorrence felt for you").
- **"and saw thee polluted in thine own blood" → "and saw you kicking about in your blood"** (v6, 22) — Hebrew מִתְבּוֹסֶסֶת בְּדָמָיִךְ, hitpolel of בּוּס "trample": "kicking, struggling" (uncertain; "polluted" is not the word); דָּמִים plural "bloods" (also v9, 22, 36), here the blood of birth. **"I said unto thee when thou wast in thy blood, Live; yea, I said unto thee when thou wast in thy blood, Live" → "and I said to you in your blood, Live! And I said to you in your blood, Live!"** — וָאֹמַר לָךְ בְּדָמַיִךְ חֲיִי twice, word for word; "in your blood" may go with "I said" or with "Live": left open; "when thou wast" and "yea" are not in the Hebrew.
- **"I have caused thee to multiply as the bud of the field" → "Ten thousand, like the sprout of the field, I made you"** (v7) — Hebrew רְבָבָה כְּצֶמַח הַשָּׂדֶה נְתַתִּיךְ, the Hebrew order; רְבָבָה "ten thousand" (= past counting); נָתַן "give, make"; צֶמַח "sprout". **"and thou hast increased and waxen great" → "and you grew many, and became great"** — וַתִּרְבִּי וַתִּגְדְּלִי, wayyiqtol. **"and thou art come to excellent ornaments" → "and came into the ornament of ornaments"** — וַתָּבֹאִי בַּעֲדִי עֲדָיִים, literally "in the ornament of ornaments" (עֲדִי "ornaments", as v11): the highest beauty (MT kept; by another pointing "into the time of the monthly flow", from memory). **"thy breasts are fashioned" → "Breasts were set firm"** — שָׁדַיִם נָכֹנוּ, no "your"; כּוּן nifal "be set firm". **"thine hair is grown" → "your hair had sprouted"** — צִמֵּחַ, the verb of צֶמַח. **"whereas thou wast naked and bare" → "and you were naked and bare"** — וְאַתְּ עֵרֹם וְעֶרְיָה (also v22, 39): עֵרֹם "naked", עֶרְיָה "bareness" (a noun).
- **"Now when I passed by thee, and looked upon thee" → "And I passed by you and saw you"** (v8) — Hebrew וָאֶעֱבֹר … וָאֶרְאֵךְ, as v6. **"thy time was the time of love" → "your time was the time of love"** — עֵת דֹּדִים, דּוֹדִים "love" (plural: the love of lovers, as Song 1:2). **"and I spread my skirt over thee" → "and I spread my corner over you"** — וָאֶפְרֹשׂ כְּנָפִי, כָּנָף "corner" of a garment, literally "wing" (as Ruth 3:9: the man's pledge to take the woman). **"and covered thy nakedness"** — עֶרְוָה "nakedness". **"yea, I sware unto thee, and entered into a covenant with thee" → "and I swore to you and entered into a covenant with you"** — וָאֶשָּׁבַע לָךְ וָאָבוֹא בִבְרִית אֹתָךְ, "and" each time; בּוֹא בִבְרִית "enter into a covenant" (as 2 Chr 15:12). **"and thou becamest mine"** — וַתִּהְיִי לִי, literally "and you became to me".
- **"Then washed I thee with water; yea, I throughly washed away thy blood from thee" → "And I washed you with water, and rinsed your blood from upon you"** (v9) — Hebrew וָאֶרְחָצֵךְ … וָאֶשְׁטֹף דָּמַיִךְ מֵעָלָיִךְ, רָחַץ "wash", שָׁטַף "rinse" (two verbs); סוּךְ "anoint" (with oil, not the מָשַׁח of kings).
- **"I clothed thee also with broidered work" → "And I clothed you with embroidery"** (v10, 13, 18) — Hebrew רִקְמָה (as Judg 5:30). **"and shod thee with badgers' skin" → "and shod you with tachash"** — תַּחַשׁ, the word of the dwelling's skins (Exod 25:5; 26:14): the animal or kind of leather is not known; the word kept. **"and I girded thee about with fine linen" → "and bound you round with fine linen"** — וָאֶחְבְּשֵׁךְ בַּשֵּׁשׁ, חָבַשׁ "bind"; שֵׁשׁ "fine linen". **"and I covered thee with silk"** — מֶשִׁי, found only here and v13: "silk" is a guess (perhaps a fine thread or gauze): uncertain.
- **"I decked thee also with ornaments" → "And I adorned you with ornaments"** (v11) — Hebrew וָאֶעְדֵּךְ עֶדִי, verb and noun of one root (also v13 "you were adorned"). **"bracelets upon thy hands, and a chain on thy neck" → "bracelets on your hands and a chain on your throat"** — צְמִידִים עַל־יָדַיִךְ, יָד "hand" (the wrist is meant); רָבִיד "chain" (as Gen 41:42); גָּרוֹן "throat".
- **"And I put a jewel on thy forehead" → "And I put a ring on your nose"** (v12) — Hebrew נֶזֶם עַל־אַפֵּךְ, נֶזֶם "ring", אַף "nose" (not "forehead"). **"earrings in thine ears"** — עֲגִילִים עַל־אָזְנָיִךְ, "on". **"a beautiful crown" → "a crown of beauty"** — עֲטֶרֶת תִּפְאֶרֶת; תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty" (also v17, 39), the same English as יֹפִי "beauty" (v13–15, 25): two words.
- **"Thus wast thou decked … and thy raiment was of fine linen" → "And you were adorned … and your clothing was fine linen"** (v13) — Hebrew וַתַּעְדִּי; שֵׁשׁ is the qere, the written text has ששי (another spelling, the same word). **"thou didst eat fine flour, and honey, and oil" → "fine flour and honey and oil you ate"** — the objects first; the qere אָכָלְתְּ "you ate", the written אכלתי (an old spelling of the same form, or "I ate"): the qere kept. **"and thou wast exceeding beautiful" → "And you were very, very beautiful"** — וַתִּיפִי בִּמְאֹד מְאֹד. **"and thou didst prosper into a kingdom" → "and you prospered into kingship"** — וַתִּצְלְחִי לִמְלוּכָה, צָלַח "prosper"; מְלוּכָה "kingship".
- **"And thy renown went forth among the heathen" → "And a name went out for you among the nations"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיֵּצֵא לָךְ שֵׁם בַּגּוֹיִם, שֵׁם "name" (also v15). **"for it was perfect through my comeliness" → "for it was whole through my splendor"** — כִּי כָּלִיל הוּא בַּהֲדָרִי, כָּלִיל "whole"; הָדָר "splendor".
- **"But thou didst trust in thine own beauty" → "And you trusted in your beauty"** (v15) — Hebrew וַתִּבְטְחִי, wayyiqtol "and". **"and playedst the harlot because of thy renown" → "and played the prostitute because of your name"** — עַל־שְׁמֵךְ, literally "upon your name". **"and pouredst out thy fornications on every one that passed by; his it was" → "and poured out your prostitutions on everyone passing by: to him let it be"** — לוֹ־יֶהִי, a jussive, literally "to him let it be" (her beauty, or herself, given to each one); the two words are uncertain (some take them as a scribe's repetition from v16, from memory).
- **"and deckedst thy high places with divers colours" → "and made for yourself spotted high places"** (v16) — Hebrew וַתַּעֲשִׂי־לָךְ בָּמוֹת טְלֻאוֹת, בָּמָה "high place"; טָלֻא "spotted, patched" (as Gen 30:32; Josh 9:5): of cloth of many pieces or colours. **"the like things shall not come, neither shall it be so" → "things not coming, and it shall not be"** — לֹא בָאוֹת וְלֹא יִהְיֶה, word for word; the sense is not known (perhaps "such things have not come and shall not be").
- **"Thou hast also taken thy fair jewels" → "And you took the articles of your beauty"** (v17, 39) — Hebrew כְּלֵי תִפְאַרְתֵּךְ, כְּלִי "article". **"images of men" → "images of a male"** — צַלְמֵי זָכָר, זָכָר "male" (the male figure is the point); צֶלֶם "image". **"and didst commit whoredom with them" → "and played the prostitute with them"** — זָנָה as v15.
- **"And tookest thy broidered garments, and coveredst them" → "And you took your garments of embroidery and covered them"** (v18) — Hebrew בִּגְדֵי רִקְמָתֵךְ. **"and thou hast set mine oil and mine incense before them" → "and my oil and my incense you set before them"** — the objects first; the qere נָתַתְּ "you set", the written נתתי as v13; נָתַן לִפְנֵי "set before".
- **"My meat also which I gave thee" → "And my bread which I gave you"** (v19) — Hebrew לַחְמִי "my bread". **"wherewith I fed thee" → "I fed you"** — הֶאֱכַלְתִּיךְ, a clause of its own. **"for a sweet savour" → "for a soothing smell"** — לְרֵיחַ נִיחֹחַ, the word of the altar. **"and thus it was" → "and it was"** — וַיֶּהִי alone ("thus" is not in the Hebrew; = "and so it happened").
- **"and these hast thou sacrificed unto them to be devoured" → "and you sacrificed them to them for eating"** (v20) — Hebrew וַתִּזְבָּחִים לָהֶם לֶאֱכוֹל, זָבַח "sacrifice"; לֶאֱכוֹל "to eat" (for them to eat: who eats is not said). **"Is this of thy whoredoms a small matter" → "Were your prostitutions a little thing"** — הַמְעַט מִתַּזְנוּתָיִךְ (the qere; the written מתזנתך, singular), literally "is it a little thing, from your prostitutions": too little, with more to follow (v21).
- **"That thou hast slain my children, and delivered them to cause them to pass through the fire for them?" → "that you slaughtered my sons, and gave them, in making them pass over to them?"** (v21) — Hebrew וַתִּשְׁחֲטִי אֶת־בָּנַי וַתִּתְּנִים בְּהַעֲבִיר אוֹתָם לָהֶם: שָׁחַט "slaughter"; בָּנַי "my sons"; הֶעֱבִיר "make pass over" ("through the fire" is not in the Hebrew here; it is in 20:31, from memory).
- **"thou hast not remembered" → "you did not remember"** (v22, 43) — Hebrew זָכַרְתְּ is the qere, the written זכרתי as v13. **"and wast polluted in thy blood" → "kicking about in your blood you were"** — מִתְבּוֹסֶסֶת בְּדָמֵךְ הָיִית, as v6, with הָיִית "you were" last.
- **"And it came to pass after all thy wickedness, (woe, woe unto thee! saith the Lord GOD;)" → "And it came to pass after all your evil — woe, woe to you! declares Lord Yahweh —"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיְהִי אַחֲרֵי כָּל־רָעָתֵךְ אוֹי אוֹי לָךְ: וַיְהִי "And it came to pass" kept; רָעָה "evil"; אוֹי "woe" (beside הוֹי, the same English).
- **"That thou hast also built unto thee an eminent place, and hast made thee an high place in every street" → "that you built for yourself a mound, and made for yourself a raised place in every square"** (v24, 31, 39) — Hebrew גַּב "back, mound" (a raised platform or vaulted structure: uncertain); רָמָה "a raised place" (from רוּם "be high"; another word than בָּמָה "high place", v16); רְחוֹב "square".
- **"Thou hast built thy high place at every head of the way" → "At every head of a way you built your raised place"** (v25, 31) — Hebrew אֶל־כָּל־רֹאשׁ דֶּרֶךְ, the place first. **"and hast made thy beauty to be abhorred" → "and made your beauty abominable"** — וַתְּתַעֲבִי אֶת־יָפְיֵךְ, piel of תָּעַב (the verb of תּוֹעֵבָה "abomination"), here "make abominable". **"and hast opened thy feet to every one that passed by" → "and spread wide your legs to everyone passing by"** — וַתְּפַשְּׂקִי אֶת־רַגְלַיִךְ, פִּשֵּׂק "open wide"; רַגְלַיִם "feet, legs" (one word). **"and multiplied thy whoredoms" → "and multiplied your prostitutions"** — the qere תַּזְנוּתָיִךְ (plural), the written תזנתך (singular).
- **"Thou hast also committed fornication with the Egyptians thy neighbours, great of flesh" → "And you played the prostitute with the sons of Egypt, your neighbors, great of flesh"** (v26) — Hebrew בְּנֵי־מִצְרַיִם "the sons of Egypt"; גִּדְלֵי בָשָׂר "great of flesh", word for word (בָּשָׂר here of the male member, as 23:20; Lev 15:2). **"to provoke me to anger" → "to vex me"** — לְהַכְעִיסֵנִי, הִכְעִיס "vex".
- **"Behold, therefore I have stretched out my hand over thee" → "And behold, I stretched out my hand against you"** (v27) — Hebrew וְהִנֵּה נָטִיתִי יָדִי עָלַיִךְ, "therefore" is not in the Hebrew. **"and have diminished thine ordinary food" → "and diminished your fixed portion"** — וָאֶגְרַע חֻקֵּךְ, חֹק here "fixed portion" (of food, as Gen 47:22; elsewhere "statute": one word). **"and delivered thee unto the will of them that hate thee" → "and gave you into the soul of those who hate you"** — בְּנֶפֶשׁ שֹׂנְאוֹתַיִךְ, נֶפֶשׁ "soul", here its craving (as Ps 27:12); the participle is feminine. **"which are ashamed of thy lewd way" → "who were put to shame by your way of depravity"** — הַנִּכְלָמוֹת מִדַּרְכֵּךְ זִמָּה, נִכְלַם "be put to shame"; זִמָּה "depravity" (also v43, 58).
- **"Thou hast played the whore also with the Assyrians, because thou wast unsatiable" → "And you played the prostitute with the sons of Assyria, because you were not sated"** (v28) — Hebrew אֶל־בְּנֵי אַשּׁוּר "the sons of Ashur"; מִבִּלְתִּי שָׂבְעָתֵךְ, literally "from your not being sated"; שָׂבַע "be sated" (also v29). **"and yet couldest not be satisfied" → "and still you were not sated"** — וְגַם לֹא שָׂבָעַתְּ.
- **"Thou hast moreover multiplied thy fornication in the land of Canaan unto Chaldea" → "And you multiplied your prostitution to the land of Canaan, to Chaldea"** (v29) — Hebrew אֶל־אֶרֶץ כְּנַעַן כַּשְׂדִּימָה: כְּנַעַן "Canaan" (also "merchant", Hos 12:7: the land of traders, from memory); כַּשְׂדִּימָה "to Chaldea", in apposition.
- **"How weak is thine heart" → "How languishing is your heart"** (v30) — Hebrew מָה אֲמֻלָה לִבָּתֵךְ: אָמַל "languish" (as 1 Sam 2:5); the form and the feminine לִבָּה "heart" are found only here: uncertain (also read "how I am filled with anger against you", from another root, from memory). **"seeing thou doest all these things, the work of an imperious whorish woman" → "in your doing all these, the work of a domineering prostitute woman"** — בַּעֲשׂוֹתֵךְ … מַעֲשֵׂה אִשָּׁה־זוֹנָה שַׁלָּטֶת, שַׁלֶּטֶת from שָׁלַט "have power over": a woman under no one's rule.
- **"and hast not been as an harlot, in that thou scornest hire" → "And you were not like the prostitute, in scorning hire"** (v31) — Hebrew וְלֹא הָיִית כַּזּוֹנָה לְקַלֵּס אֶתְנָן: the qere עָשִׂית "you made" and הָיִית "you were", the written forms as v13; קִלֵּס "scorn"; אֶתְנַן "hire" (what a prostitute is given; also v34, 41).
- **"But as a wife that committeth adultery, which taketh strangers instead of her husband!" → "The wife who commits adultery: in place of her husband she takes strangers!"** (v32) — Hebrew הָאִשָּׁה הַמְּנָאָפֶת תַּחַת אִישָׁהּ תִּקַּח אֶת־זָרִים: "But as" is not in the Hebrew; אִשָּׁה / אִישׁ "woman / man" of a married pair, "wife / husband"; נָאַף "commit adultery" (also v38); תַּחַת "in place of"; זָר "stranger".
- **"They give gifts to all whores" → "To all prostitutes they give a gift"** (v33) — Hebrew לְכָל־זֹנוֹת יִתְּנוּ־נֵדֶה, the Hebrew order; נֵדֶה and נָדָן (only here) "gift" (a prostitute's fee; uncertain). **"and hirest them" → "and bribed them"** — וַתִּשְׁחֳדִי, the verb of שֹׁחַד "bribe". **"on every side" → "from all around"** — מִסָּבִיב (also v37, 57).
- **"And the contrary is in thee from other women in thy whoredoms" → "And there was in you the reverse from the women in your prostitutions"** (v34) — Hebrew וַיְהִי־בָךְ הֵפֶךְ מִן־הַנָּשִׁים, הֵפֶךְ "the reverse" (from הָפַךְ "turn over"); "other" is not in the Hebrew. **"whereas none followeth thee to commit whoredoms" → "and after you none goes as a prostitute"** — וְאַחֲרַיִךְ לֹא זוּנָּה, pual of זָנָה with no subject: no one goes after you for prostitution. **"and in that thou givest a reward, and no reward is given unto thee, therefore thou art contrary" → "and in your giving hire, while hire is not given to you, you have become the reverse"** — אֶתְנַן "hire" twice; וַתְּהִי לְהֶפֶךְ, literally "and you became for a reverse"; "therefore" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Wherefore, O harlot, hear the word of the LORD" → "Therefore, prostitute, hear the word of Yahweh"** (v35) — Hebrew לָכֵן זוֹנָה; no "O".
- **"Because thy filthiness was poured out" → "Because your copper was poured out"** (v36) — Hebrew יַעַן הִשָּׁפֵךְ נְחֻשְׁתֵּךְ: נְחֹשֶׁת "bronze, copper" (as metal: "copper" money, the hire of v31–34, from memory); also taken from another root, "lust" or the flow of the body (KJV "filthiness"): uncertain; the word kept. **"and thy nakedness discovered through thy whoredoms with thy lovers" → "and your nakedness uncovered in your prostitutions with your lovers"** — וַתִּגָּלֶה עֶרְוָתֵךְ, גִּלָּה "uncover" (also v37, 57); עַל "with", literally "upon"; מְאַהֲבִים "lovers" (also v33, 37). **"and with all the idols of thy abominations"** — גִּלּוּלֵי תוֹעֲבוֹתַיִךְ. **"and by the blood of thy children" → "and according to the blood of your sons"** — וְכִדְמֵי בָנַיִךְ, כְּ "according to"; דְּמֵי plural.
- **"Behold, therefore I will gather all thy lovers, with whom thou hast taken pleasure" → "therefore behold, I am gathering all your lovers, to whom you were pleasing"** (v37) — Hebrew הִנְנִי מְקַבֵּץ, participle; אֲשֶׁר עָרַבְתְּ עֲלֵיהֶם, עָרַב "be pleasing, sweet" (another root than עָרַב "be surety"). **"with all them that thou hast hated" → "together with all whom you hated"** — עַל כָּל־אֲשֶׁר שָׂנֵאת. **"I will even gather them round about against thee" → "and I will gather them against you from all around"** — וְקִבַּצְתִּי. **"that they may see" → "and they shall see"** — וְרָאוּ.
- **"And I will judge thee, as women that break wedlock and shed blood are judged" → "And I will judge you with the judgments of women who commit adultery and who pour out blood"** (v38) — Hebrew וּשְׁפַטְתִּיךְ מִשְׁפְּטֵי נֹאֲפוֹת וְשֹׁפְכֹת דָּם, the noun of the verb: "judge you the judgments of"; שָׁפַךְ "pour out" (also v15, 36). **"and I will give thee blood in fury and jealousy" → "and I will give you blood of fury and jealousy"** — וּנְתַתִּיךְ דַּם חֵמָה וְקִנְאָה, word for word (= make you the blood that fury and jealousy shed); חֵמָה "fury", קִנְאָה "jealousy" (also v42).
- **"And I will also give thee into their hand" → "And I will give you into their hand"** (v39) — Hebrew בְּיָדָם, singular. **"and they shall throw down thine eminent place, and shall break down thy high places" → "and they shall throw down your mound and break down your raised places"** — הָרַס "throw down", נָתַץ "break down". **"they shall strip thee also of thy clothes" → "and strip you of your garments"** — הִפְשִׁיט "strip"; בֶּגֶד "garment". **"and leave thee naked and bare"** — הִנִּיחַ "leave".
- **"They shall also bring up a company against thee" → "And they shall bring up an assembly against you"** (v40) — Hebrew קָהָל "assembly". **"and thrust thee through with their swords" → "and cut you in pieces with their swords"** — וּבִתְּקוּךְ, בִּתֵּק found only here: "cut in pieces" (uncertain). רָגַם "stone".
- **"and execute judgments upon thee in the sight of many women" → "and do judgments on you before the eyes of many women"** (v41) — Hebrew וְעָשׂוּ־בָךְ שְׁפָטִים לְעֵינֵי, שְׁפָטִים "judgments" (acts of judgment); לְעֵינֵי "before the eyes of". **"and I will cause thee to cease from playing the harlot" → "and I will make you cease from being a prostitute"** — וְהִשְׁבַּתִּיךְ מִזּוֹנָה, literally "from a prostitute". **"and thou also shalt give no hire any more" → "and also hire you shall give no more"** — the object first.
- **"So will I make my fury toward thee to rest" → "And I will make my fury rest on you"** (v42) — Hebrew וַהֲנִחֹתִי חֲמָתִי בָּךְ (as 5:13). **"and my jealousy shall depart from thee" → "and my jealousy shall turn aside from you"** — וְסָרָה, סוּר "turn aside". **"and I will be quiet, and will be no more angry" → "and I will be quiet, and I will not be vexed any more"** — שָׁקַט "be quiet"; כָּעַס "be vexed" (the qal of v26 "vex").
- **"but hast fretted me in all these things" → "and were agitated against me in all these"** (v43) — Hebrew וַתִּרְגְּזִי־לִי, רָגַז "be agitated". **"behold, therefore I also will recompense thy way upon thine head" → "then I too, behold, have laid your way upon your head"** — וְגַם־אֲנִי הֵא דַּרְכֵּךְ בְּרֹאשׁ נָתַתִּי, a perfect; הֵא "behold" (a rare form); נָתַן "lay upon", literally "give" (as 7:3; "recompense" is the sense). **"and thou shalt not commit this lewdness above all thine abominations" → "and have you not done the depravity on top of all your abominations?"** — וְלֹא עָשִׂית אֶת־הַזִּמָּה עַל כָּל־תּוֹעֲבֹתָיִךְ (the qere; the written עשיתי, "and I have not done depravity"): no question word; read as a question, as the context needs; also taken as a statement ("and you did not commit …" or "so that you shall not commit …").
- **"every one that useth proverbs shall use this proverb against thee" → "everyone who speaks a proverb shall speak this proverb against you"** (v44) — Hebrew כָּל־הַמֹּשֵׁל עָלַיִךְ יִמְשֹׁל, מָשַׁל "speak a proverb" (the verb of מָשָׁל); "this" is supplied. **"As is the mother, so is her daughter" → "As the mother, her daughter"** — כְּאִמָּה בִּתָּהּ, two words, no verb.
- **"Thou art thy mother's daughter, that lotheth her husband and her children" → "Your mother's daughter you are, who abhorred her husband and her sons"** (v45) — Hebrew בַּת־אִמֵּךְ אַתְּ גֹּעֶלֶת אִישָׁהּ וּבָנֶיהָ, the Hebrew order; גָּעַל "abhor"; בָּנִים "sons". **"your mother was an Hittite, and your father an Amorite"** — אִמְּכֶן … אֲבִיכֶן, "your" plural (of the sisters together).
- **"And thine elder sister is Samaria, she and her daughters that dwell at thy left hand" → "And your elder sister is Samaria, she and her daughters, who dwells north of you"** (v46) — Hebrew הַיּוֹשֶׁבֶת עַל־שְׂמֹאולֵךְ, literally "who dwells on your left" (one facing east has the north on the left); הַגְּדוֹלָה "the great" (= elder). **"and thy younger sister, that dwelleth at thy right hand" → "and your sister younger than you, who dwells south of you"** — הַקְּטַנָּה מִמֵּךְ הַיּוֹשֶׁבֶת מִימִינֵךְ, literally "smaller than you … from your right"; יָשַׁב "dwell" (also v46a).
- **"Yet hast thou not walked after their ways, nor done after their abominations: but, as if that were a very little thing, thou wast corrupted more than they" → "And not in their ways have you walked, nor according to their abominations have you done — as a very little thing — and you worked ruin more than they"** (v47) — Hebrew וְלֹא בְדַרְכֵיהֶן הָלַכְתְּ וּבְתוֹעֲבוֹתֵיהֶן עָשִׂית כִּמְעַט קָט וַתַּשְׁחִתִי מֵהֵן: the qere עָשִׂית; כִּמְעַט קָט, קָט found only here: "as a very little" (that was too little for you; also "in a very little while"); הִשְׁחִית "work ruin" (KJV "be corrupted").
- **"As I live … Sodom thy sister hath not done" → "As I live … Sodom your sister has not done"** (v48) — Hebrew חַי־אָנִי … אִם־עָשְׂתָה, the oath, literally "if Sodom has done": a sworn "has not done".
- **"pride, fulness of bread, and abundance of idleness was in her and in her daughters" → "pride, satiety of bread, and the ease of being quiet were hers and her daughters'"** (v49) — Hebrew גָּאוֹן שִׂבְעַת־לֶחֶם וְשַׁלְוַת הַשְׁקֵט הָיָה לָהּ וְלִבְנוֹתֶיהָ: גָּאוֹן "pride" (of men); שִׂבְעָה "satiety" (root שָׂבַע); שַׁלְוָה "ease" (as Ps 122:7); הַשְׁקֵט "being quiet" (שָׁקַט). **"neither did she strengthen the hand of the poor and needy" → "and the hand of the afflicted and the needy she did not strengthen"** — וְיַד־עָנִי וְאֶבְיוֹן לֹא הֶחֱזִיקָה, the object first; עָנִי "afflicted", אֶבְיוֹן "needy"; הֶחֱזִיק "strengthen" (literally "make strong").
- **"And they were haughty, and committed abomination before me: therefore I took them away as I saw good" → "And they were haughty, and did an abomination before me; and I removed them when I saw it"** (v50) — Hebrew וַתִּגְבְּהֶינָה, literally "and they were high"; תּוֹעֵבָה "an abomination", singular; וָאָסִיר "and I removed" (no "therefore"); כַּאֲשֶׁר רָאִיתִי "when / as I saw" ("good" is not in the Hebrew; "it" supplied).
- **"Neither hath Samaria committed half of thy sins" → "And Samaria has not sinned even half of your sins"** (v51) — Hebrew וְשֹׁמְרוֹן כַּחֲצִי חַטֹּאתַיִךְ לֹא חָטָאָה, literally "like half your sins she has not sinned". **"and hast justified thy sisters" → "and have made your sisters righteous"** — וַתְּצַדְּקִי אֶת־אֲחוֹתַיִךְ (the qere, plural; the written "your sister"), piel of צָדַק: made them righteous (= look righteous beside you; also v52); the qere עָשִׂית.
- **"Thou also, which hast judged thy sisters, bear thine own shame" → "You also, bear your dishonor, you who have intervened for your sisters"** (v52) — Hebrew גַּם־אַתְּ שְׂאִי כְלִמָּתֵךְ אֲשֶׁר פִּלַּלְתְּ לַאֲחוֹתֵךְ: כְּלִמָּה "dishonor" (also v54, 63); פִּלֵּל "intervene" (step in and decide; as Ps 106:30): by her worse sins she has spoken for them. **"for thy sins that thou hast committed more abominable than they" → "by your sins, in which you acted more abominably than they"** — הִתְעִיב "act abominably" (as 1 Kgs 21:26). **"they are more righteous than thou" → "they are more righteous than you"** — תִּצְדַּקְנָה מִמֵּךְ, צָדַק qal "be righteous". **"yea, be thou confounded also" → "And you also, be ashamed"** — בּוֹשִׁי, בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed". **"in that thou hast justified thy sisters" → "in your making your sisters righteous"** — בְּצַדֶּקְתֵּךְ.
- **"When I shall bring again their captivity … then will I bring again the captivity of thy captives in the midst of them" → "And I will restore their fortunes … and the fortunes of your fortunes in their midst"** (v53) — Hebrew וְשַׁבְתִּי אֶת־שְׁבִיתְהֶן … וּשְׁבוּת שְׁבִיתַיִךְ: שׁוּב שְׁבוּת / שְׁבִית "restore the fortunes" (one idiom; literally "turn back the captivity" or "turn the turning", traditional "bring again the captivity"); the qere שְׁבוּת three times, the written שבית; the last phrase doubles the noun, kept word for word. "When … then" is not in the Hebrew: וְ "And".
- **"That thou mayest bear thine own shame, and mayest be confounded in all that thou hast done, in that thou art a comfort unto them" → "in order that you may bear your dishonor and be put to shame for all that you have done, in your comforting them"** (v54) — Hebrew לְמַעַן תִּשְׂאִי כְלִמָּתֵךְ וְנִכְלַמְתְּ … בְּנַחֲמֵךְ אֹתָן: נִכְלַם "be put to shame" (also v61); נִחַם piel "comfort", an infinitive with an object.
- **"When thy sisters … shall return to their former estate … then thou and thy daughters shall return" → "And your sisters … shall return to their former state … and you and your daughters shall return"** (v55) — Hebrew וַאֲחוֹתַיִךְ … תָּשֹׁבְןָ לְקַדְמָתָן, "and" each time; no "when … then"; קַדְמָה "former state".
- **"For thy sister Sodom was not mentioned by thy mouth in the day of thy pride" → "And Sodom your sister was not a report in your mouth in the day of your pride"** (v56) — Hebrew וְלוֹא הָיְתָה סְדֹם אֲחוֹתֵךְ לִשְׁמוּעָה בְּפִיךְ בְּיוֹם גְּאוֹנָיִךְ: שְׁמוּעָה "report" (a thing heard); also read as a question ("Was not Sodom a byword …?"); גְּאוֹנַיִךְ "your pride" (plural).
- **"Before thy wickedness was discovered, as at the time of thy reproach of the daughters of Syria" → "before your evil was uncovered, as at the time of the reproach of the daughters of Aram"** (v57) — Hebrew בְּטֶרֶם תִּגָּלֶה רָעָתֵךְ כְּמוֹ עֵת חֶרְפַּת בְּנוֹת־אֲרָם: רָעָה "evil"; חֶרְפָּה "reproach"; אֲרָם "Aram" as printed (many manuscripts and versions read אֱדוֹם "Edom", from memory: the MT kept). **"which despise thee round about" → "who despise you from all around"** — הַשָּׁאטוֹת אוֹתָךְ מִסָּבִיב, שׁוּט "despise" (also 25:6, 15; 28:24, 26).
- **"Thou hast borne thy lewdness and thine abominations, saith the LORD" → "Your depravity and your abominations, you have borne them, declares Yahweh"** (v58) — Hebrew אֶת־זִמָּתֵךְ וְאֶת־תּוֹעֲבוֹתַיִךְ אַתְּ נְשָׂאתִים, the objects first, picked up by "them"; a perfect.
- **"I will even deal with thee as thou hast done" → "And I will do with you as you have done"** (v59) — Hebrew וְעָשִׂיתִי (the qere; the written ועשית, "and you will do"), "and". **"which hast despised the oath in breaking the covenant" → "you who despised the oath, to break the covenant"** — אֲשֶׁר־בָּזִית אָלָה לְהָפֵר בְּרִית: בָּזָה "despise"; אָלָה "oath" (a sworn bond that calls down a curse on its breaker; the word is also "curse"); הֵפֵר בְּרִית "break a covenant", an infinitive.
- **"Nevertheless I will remember my covenant with thee" → "And I, I will remember my covenant with you"** (v60) — Hebrew וְזָכַרְתִּי אֲנִי, "and", with אֲנִי "I" stressed (also v62); "Nevertheless" is not in the Hebrew. **"an everlasting covenant"** — בְּרִית עוֹלָם, literally "a covenant of the age"; הֵקִים בְּרִית "establish a covenant" (also v62).
- **"Then thou shalt remember thy ways, and be ashamed, when thou shalt receive thy sisters, thine elder and thy younger" → "And you shall remember your ways and be put to shame, when you take your sisters, those older than you, together with those younger than you"** (v61) — Hebrew וְזָכַרְתְּ … וְנִכְלַמְתְּ בְּקַחְתֵּךְ אֶת־אֲחוֹתַיִךְ הַגְּדֹלוֹת מִמֵּךְ אֶל־הַקְּטַנּוֹת מִמֵּךְ, literally "the greater than you, to the smaller than you"; לָקַח "take". **"but not by thy covenant" → "but not from your covenant"** — וְלֹא מִבְּרִיתֵךְ, word for word (not on the ground of your covenant; or "not from your side of the covenant").
- **"And I will establish my covenant with thee; and thou shalt know that I am the LORD" → "And I, I will establish my covenant with you, and you shall know that I am Yahweh"** (v62) — Hebrew וַהֲקִימוֹתִי אֲנִי אֶת־בְּרִיתִי אִתָּךְ וְיָדַעַתְּ כִּי־אֲנִי יְהוָה, the knowledge formula (as 13:14).
- **"That thou mayest remember, and be confounded, and never open thy mouth any more because of thy shame" → "in order that you may remember and be ashamed, and there may be to you no more an opening of the mouth because of your dishonor"** (v63) — Hebrew לְמַעַן תִּזְכְּרִי וָבֹשְׁתְּ וְלֹא יִהְיֶה־לָּךְ עוֹד פִּתְחוֹן פֶּה מִפְּנֵי כְּלִמָּתֵךְ: פִּתְחוֹן פֶּה "an opening of the mouth" (also 29:21); מִפְּנֵי "because of". **"when I am pacified toward thee for all that thou hast done" → "when I make atonement for you for all that you have done"** — בְּכַפְּרִי־לָךְ לְכָל־אֲשֶׁר עָשִׂית, כִּפֶּר "make atonement" (literally "cover"), with לְ "for".

### Notes
- **v1–63** — one oracle in two parts: Jerusalem as a foundling taken as wife who turns prostitute (v1–43), and her sisters Samaria and Sodom (v44–63). The figure is the Hebrew's own; the verses carry it, Changes explain words only.
- **Sodom's iniquity** (v49–50) — the text names pride, satiety of bread, careless ease and the afflicted left unhelped, then "an abomination" (v50).
