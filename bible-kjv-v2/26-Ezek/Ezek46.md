# Ezekiel 46

[1] "Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: The gate of the inner court that faces east shall be shut the six days of work; and on the day of the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ it shall be opened, and on the day of the month it shall be opened.

[2] And the chieftain shall come in by the way of the porch of the gate from outside, and shall stand at the doorpost of the gate; and the priests shall offer his burnt offering and his peace offerings, and he shall bow down at the threshold of the gate, and shall go out; and the gate shall not be shut until the evening.

[3] And the people of the land shall bow down at the entrance of that gate, on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ and on the months, before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[4] And the burnt offering that the chieftain shall bring near to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ on the day of the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧: six lambs without blemish and a ram without blemish;

[5] and a grain offering, an ephah for the ram, and for the lambs a grain offering, the gift of his hand; and oil, a hin for the ephah.

[6] And on the day of the month: a bull of the herd without blemish, and six lambs and a ram; without blemish they shall be.

[7] And an ephah for the bull and an ephah for the ram he shall offer as a grain offering, and for the lambs as his hand can reach; and oil, a hin for the ephah.

[8] And when the chieftain comes in, by the way of the porch of the gate he shall come in, and by its way he shall go out.

[9] And when the people of the land come in before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ at the appointed times, he who comes in by the way of the north gate to bow down shall go out by the way of the south gate, and he who comes in by the way of the south gate shall go out by the way of the gate northward. He shall not return by the way of the gate by which he came in, for straight before him he shall go out.

[10] And the chieftain, in their midst, when they come in he shall come in, and when they go out they shall go out.

[11] And at the feasts and at the appointed times the grain offering shall be an ephah for the bull and an ephah for the ram, and for the lambs the gift of his hand; and oil, a hin for the ephah.

[12] And when the chieftain offers a freewill offering, a burnt offering or peace offerings, a freewill offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, he shall open for him the gate that faces east, and he shall offer his burnt offering and his peace offerings as he offers on the day of the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧; and he shall go out, and he shall shut the gate after his going out.

[13] And a lamb a year old without blemish you shall offer as a burnt offering for the day to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; morning by morning you shall offer it.

[14] And a grain offering you shall offer with it morning by morning, a sixth of the ephah, and oil, a third of the hin, to moisten the fine flour: a grain offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, everlasting statutes, continually.

[15] And they shall offer the lamb and the grain offering and the oil morning by morning, a continual burnt offering.

[16] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: If the chieftain gives a gift to a man of his sons, it is his inheritance; it shall be his sons'; it is their possession by inheritance.

[17] And if he gives a gift from his inheritance to one of his servants, it shall be his until the year of liberty, and it shall return to the chieftain; only his inheritance, his sons', shall be theirs.

[18] And the chieftain shall not take from the inheritance of the people, wronging them out of their possession; from his own possession he shall make his sons inherit, so that my people are not scattered, each man from his possession."

[19] And he brought me through the entry that was at the shoulder of the gate to the holy chambers for the priests, that faced north; and behold, there was a place there in the rear, westward.

[20] And he said to me, "This is the place where the priests shall boil the guilt offering and the sin offering, where they shall bake the grain offering, so as not to bring out to the outer court, to sanctify the people."

[21] And he brought me out to the outer court, and made me pass over to the four corners of the court; and behold, a court in a corner of the court, a court in a corner of the court.

[22] In the four corners of the court were courts enclosed, forty in length and thirty in breadth; one measure for the four of them, made in corners.

[23] And a row was around in them, around the four of them, and boiling places were made under the rows all around.

[24] And he said to me, "These are the house of those who boil, where the ministers of the house shall boil the sacrifice of the people."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v1, 16) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*, both names.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept ("then", "but", "likewise" are not in the Hebrew: v2, 3).
- **"the six working days" → "the six days of work"** (v1) — שֵׁשֶׁת יְמֵי הַמַּעֲשֶׂה. **"in the day of the new moon" → "on the day of the month"** — בְּיוֹם הַחֹדֶשׁ: חֹדֶשׁ "month" (also v3 "on the months", v6); that the new moon, the first day of the month, is meant goes here.
- **"the prince" → "the chieftain"** (v2, 4, 8, 10, 12, 16–18) — נָשִׂיא, literally "one lifted up", one word through Ezekiel (as 7:27; 12:10); שַׂר is "prince". **"by the way of the porch of that gate without" → "by the way of the porch of the gate from outside"** (v2) — אוּלָם "porch" (as 1 Kgs 6:3). **"stand by the post of the gate" → "stand at the doorpost of the gate"** — מְזוּזָה "doorpost". **"the priests shall prepare" → "the priests shall offer"** — עָשׂוּ, literally "shall do / make" (of a sacrifice "offer": also v7, 12–15). **"he shall worship" → "he shall bow down"** — וְהִשְׁתַּחֲוָה, the same verb toward men and God (also v3, 9).
- **"at the door of this gate" → "at the entrance of that gate"** (v3) — פֶּתַח הַשַּׁעַר הַהוּא: פֶּתַח "entrance"; הַהוּא "that". **"before the LORD in the sabbaths and in the new moons" → "on the Shabbats and on the months, before Yahweh"** — the Hebrew order.
- **"the burnt offering that the prince shall offer" → "that the chieftain shall bring near"** (v4) — יַקְרִיב, hifil of קָרַב "come near". **"shall be six lambs" → "six lambs"** — no verb in the Hebrew.
- **"as he shall be able to give" → "the gift of his hand"** (v5) — מַתַּת יָדוֹ, literally (= what he is able to give; also v11). **"an hin of oil to an ephah" → "oil, a hin for the ephah"** — וְשֶׁמֶן הִין לָאֵיפָה (also v7, 11). ephah and hin are kept as words; their sizes are not in the Hebrew.
- **"a young bullock without blemish" → "a bull of the herd without blemish"** (v6) — פַּר בֶּן־בָּקָר, literally "a bull, a son of the herd": nothing about age; תְּמִימִם "without blemish", literally "whole" (also v4, 13). **"a ram: they shall be without blemish" → "a ram; without blemish they shall be"** — the Hebrew order.
- **"according as his hand shall attain unto" → "as his hand can reach"** (v7) — כַּאֲשֶׁר תַּשִּׂיג יָדוֹ.
- **"he shall go forth by the way thereof" → "by its way he shall go out"** (v8) — וּבְדַרְכּוֹ יֵצֵא.
- **"in the solemn feasts" → "at the appointed times"** (v9) — בַּמּוֹעֲדִים, never "feasts" (חַג is "feast"; also v11). **north, south** — צָפוֹן, נֶגֶב of a quarter; the last time צָפוֹנָה "northward" ("by the way of the gate northward"). **"but shall go forth over against it" → "for straight before him he shall go out"** — כִּי נִכְחוֹ: נֹכַח "opposite, in front of". The written form יצאו "they shall go out", qere יֵצֵא "he shall go out", which fits the singulars around it.
- **"when they go forth, shall go forth" → "when they go out they shall go out"** (v10) — וּבְצֵאתָם יֵצֵאוּ: the second verb is plural in the MT (the chieftain with them); the Septuagint and others read a singular (from memory); the MT is kept.
- **"a voluntary burnt offering or peace offerings voluntarily" → "a freewill offering, a burnt offering or peace offerings, a freewill offering"** (v12) — נְדָבָה twice. **"one shall then open him the gate" → "he shall open for him the gate"** — וּפָתַח לוֹ: the subject is not named (not the chieftain, who is "for him"); "one" is not supplied (also "he shall shut the gate"). **"after his going forth"** — אַחֲרֵי צֵאתוֹ.
- **"Thou shalt daily prepare a burnt offering … of a lamb of the first year" → "And a lamb a year old without blemish you shall offer as a burnt offering for the day"** (v13) — כֶּבֶשׂ בֶּן־שְׁנָתוֹ, literally "a lamb, a son of its year"; לַיּוֹם "for the day". **"every morning" → "morning by morning"** — בַּבֹּקֶר בַּבֹּקֶר, literally "in the morning, in the morning" (also v14, 15). "you" is singular in v13–14 (the Septuagint has "he shall", from memory); v15 has "they".
- **"to temper with the fine flour" → "to moisten the fine flour"** (v14) — לָרֹס, found only here (from רָסַס "moisten"; uncertain). **"continually by a perpetual ordinance" → "everlasting statutes, continually"** — חֻקּוֹת עוֹלָם תָּמִיד, the plural as printed; עוֹלָם as a genitive "everlasting" (literally "of the age"); תָּמִיד "continually" (also v15 "a continual burnt offering").
- **"Thus shall they prepare" → "And they shall offer"** (v15) — the written form ועשו "and they shall make", qere יַעֲשׂוּ "they shall make" (the same sense).
- **"unto any of his sons" → "to a man of his sons"** (v16) — לְאִישׁ מִבָּנָיו, literally (= to one of his sons). **"the inheritance thereof shall be his sons'" → "it is his inheritance; it shall be his sons'"** — נַחֲלָתוֹ הִיא לְבָנָיו תִּהְיֶה: two clauses. **"it shall be their possession by inheritance" → "it is their possession by inheritance"** — אֲחֻזָּתָם הִיא בְּנַחֲלָה: no verb.
- **"to one of his servants"** (v17) — עֲבָדָיו, the chieftain's men ("servant" of a ruler's men, as the עֶבֶד row). **"the year of liberty"** — שְׁנַת הַדְּרוֹר (as Lev 25:10). **"after it shall return" → "and it shall return"** — וְשָׁבַת. **"but his inheritance shall be his sons' for them" → "only his inheritance, his sons', shall be theirs"** — אַךְ נַחֲלָתוֹ בָּנָיו לָהֶם תִּהְיֶה: אַךְ "only".
- **"by oppression, to thrust them out of their possession" → "wronging them out of their possession"** (v18) — לְהוֹנֹתָם מֵאֲחֻזָּתָם, one verb, הוֹנָה "wrong" (as 45:8). **"he shall give his sons inheritance" → "he shall make his sons inherit"** — יַנְחִל, hifil of נָחַל. **"that my people be not scattered every man from his possession" → "so that my people are not scattered, each man from his possession"** — לְמַעַן אֲשֶׁר.
- **"After he brought me" → "And he brought me"** (v19) — וַיְבִיאֵנִי. **"at the side of the gate" → "at the shoulder of the gate"** — עַל־כֶּתֶף הַשַּׁעַר (as Exod 27:14). **"the holy chambers of the priests, which looked toward the north" → "the holy chambers for the priests, that faced north"** — אֶל־הַלִּשְׁכוֹת הַקֹּדֶשׁ אֶל־הַכֹּהֲנִים הַפֹּנוֹת צָפוֹנָה: the second אֶל "to" (= belonging to the priests). **"there was a place on the two sides westward" → "there was a place there in the rear, westward"** — the written form בירכתם "in their rear", qere בַּיַּרְכָתַיִם "in the rear" (literally "the two flanks", the far end); "there" (שָׁם) kept; יָמָּה "westward" (literally "seaward"; the quarter by the sea).
- **"to sanctify the people" → "so as not to bring out to the outer court, to sanctify the people"** (v20) — לְבִלְתִּי הוֹצִיא … לְקַדֵּשׁ אֶת־הָעָם: no object for "bring out"; קִדֵּשׁ "sanctify" (= that holiness should not pass to the people, as 44:19). בִּשֵּׁל "boil" (also v24); אָשָׁם "guilt offering", חַטָּאת "sin offering".
- **"caused me to pass by the four corners" → "made me pass over to the four corners"** (v21) — וַיַּעֲבִירֵנִי אֶל: הֶעֱבִיר "make pass over". **"in every corner of the court there was a court" → "a court in a corner of the court, a court in a corner of the court"** — the phrase is said twice, as printed (= in each corner).
- **"courts joined" → "courts enclosed"** (v22) — קְטֻרוֹת, found only here; uncertain: "enclosed", "joined", or (from קְטֹרֶת) "smoking", in Changes only. **"of forty cubits long and thirty broad" → "forty in length and thirty in breadth"** — no unit in the Hebrew. **"these four corners were of one measure" → "one measure for the four of them, made in corners"** — מְהֻקְצָעוֹת, a pual participle of the root of מִקְצוֹעַ "corner" (= set in the corners); in the Masoretic text every letter of the word carries a dot above (puncta extraordinaria), the scribes' sign of doubt about the word; it is translated as printed.
- **"a row of building round about in them" → "a row was around in them"** (v23) — וְטוּר סָבִיב בָּהֶם: טוּר "row" (of stone, as 1 Kgs 6:36); "building" is not in the Hebrew. **"under the rows"** — הַטִּירוֹת, a plural of the same sense (טִירָה elsewhere is "encampment", Gen 25:16: the form here is taken as the plural of טוּר; uncertain). **"boiling places"** — מְבַשְּׁלוֹת, piel participle of בִּשֵּׁל "boil".
- **"These are the places of them that boil" → "These are the house of those who boil"** (v24) — אֵלֶּה בֵּית הַמְבַשְּׁלִים: בֵּית singular, as printed; "places" is not in the Hebrew. **"the sacrifice of the people"** — זֶבַח singular.
