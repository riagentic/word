# Ezekiel 7

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[2] "And you, son of man, thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ to the ground of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:

'An end! The end has come
upon the four corners of the land.

[3] Now the end is upon you,
and I will send my anger against you,
and I will judge you according to your ways,
and I will lay upon you all your abominations.

[4] And my eye shall not pity you,
and I will not have pity;
for your ways I will lay upon you,
and your abominations shall be in your midst;
and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.'

[5] "Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:

'An evil, a single evil — behold, it comes.

[6] An end has come; the end has come;
it has awakened against you;
behold, it comes.

[7] The turn has come to you,
inhabitant of the land;
the time has come; the day is near —
tumult, and not the shout of the mountains.

[8] Now, soon, I will pour out my fury upon you,
and I will spend my anger on you,
and I will judge you according to your ways,
and I will lay upon you all your abominations.

[9] And my eye shall not pity,
and I will not have pity;
according to your ways I will lay upon you,
and your abominations shall be in your midst;
and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who strikes.

[10] Behold, the day!
Behold, it comes!
The turn has gone out;
the staff has blossomed;
presumption has budded.

[11] Violence has risen up into a staff of wickedness;
nothing of them, and nothing of their multitude,
and nothing of their uproar, and no eminence among them.

[12] The time has come; the day has arrived.
The buyer — let him not rejoice,
and the seller — let him not mourn;
for burning anger is upon all her multitude.

[13] For the seller shall not return to what was sold,
though their life is yet among the living;
for the vision upon all her multitude shall not turn back;
and none in his iniquity shall make his life strong.

[14] They have blown on the trumpet and made everything ready,
and there is none going to the battle,
for my burning anger is upon all her multitude.

[15] The sword is outside,
and the pestilence and the famine within;
he who is in the field shall die by the sword,
and he who is in the city, famine and pestilence shall devour him.

[16] And their fugitives shall escape,
and they shall be on the mountains
like doves of the valleys, all of them moaning,
each in his iniquity.

[17] All hands shall be slack,
and all knees shall go as water.

[18] And they shall gird on sackcloth,
and shuddering shall cover them;
and on all faces shame,
and on all their heads baldness.

[19] Their silver they shall throw into the streets,
and their gold shall become an impurity;
their silver and their gold shall not be able to deliver them
in the day of the raging of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
their soul they shall not satisfy,
and their inward parts they shall not fill;
for it has been the stumbling block of their iniquity.

[20] And the beauty of his ornament — for pride he set it,
and the images of their abominations, their detestable things, they made in it;
therefore I have made it an impurity to them.

[21] And I will give it into the hand of the strangers for plunder,
and to the wicked of the earth for spoil,
and they shall profane it.

[22] And I will turn my face from them,
and they shall profane my hidden place;
and violent men shall come into it and profane it.

[23] Make the chain!
For the land is full of judgment of bloodguilt,
and the city is full of violence.

[24] And I will bring the evilest of the nations,
and they shall take possession of their houses;
and I will make the pride of the strong cease,
and their sanctuaries shall be profaned.

[25] Anguish has come;
and they shall seek ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, and there is none.

[26] Destruction upon destruction shall come,
and report upon report shall be;
and they shall seek a vision from a prophet,
and ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ shall perish from a priest,
and counsel from elders.

[27] The king shall mourn,
and the chieftain shall be clothed with desolation,
and the hands of the people of the land shall be dismayed.
According to their way I will do to them,
and by their judgments I will judge them;
and they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*, both words; Ezekiel's distinctive doubled divine title.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its lines with וְ "and"; the chain is kept (v3–4, 8–9, 12–27).
- **"Moreover the word of the LORD came unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v1) — the prophets' formula, one wording.
- **"Also, thou son of man" → "And you, son of man"** (v2) — no "O". **"unto the land of Israel" → "to the ground of Israel"** — לְאַדְמַת, אֲדָמָה "ground" (הָאָרֶץ "the land" in the next line). **"the four corners of the land"** — written אַרְבַּעַת, read (qere) אַרְבַּע: the same number, two forms; כַּנְפוֹת, literally "wings" (the כָּנָף row: "corners").
- **"I will recompense thee for all thine abominations" → "I will lay upon you all your abominations"** (v3, 8) — וְנָתַתִּי עָלַיִךְ, literally "I will give upon you": what they did is put back on them (also v4, 9 "your ways I will lay upon you"). **"I will send mine anger upon thee" → "against you"** — בָּךְ.
- **"and thine abominations shall be in the midst of thee"** (v4, 9) — kept as a statement (תִּהְיֶיןָ, imperfect). **"ye shall know"** — the plural, as the Hebrew, after the address to one (feminine) "you".
- **"An evil, an only evil" → "An evil, a single evil"** (v5) — רָעָה אַחַת רָעָה, MT "one" (אַחַת); many Hebrew manuscripts and the Targum read אַחַר "after", "evil after evil" (from memory): the MT is kept. רָעָה "evil" (the harm that comes).
- **"it watcheth for thee" → "it has awakened against you"** (v6) — הֵקִיץ, of קוּץ / יָקַץ "wake", a play on קֵץ "end"; אֵלָיִךְ "to / against you".
- **"The morning is come unto thee" → "The turn has come to you"** (v7, 10) — הַצְּפִירָה, meaning uncertain: in Isa 28:5 a "garland, crown"; here taken as "the turn" (of fate, what comes round), "the doom", or (KJV, from the Aramaic) "the morning". **"O thou that dwellest in the land" → "inhabitant of the land"** — no "O". **"the day of trouble is near, and not the sounding again of the mountains" → "the day is near — tumult, and not the shout of the mountains"** — קָרוֹב הַיּוֹם מְהוּמָה, "tumult" stands alone (no "day of"); הֵד found only here, taken as "shout" (of joy or harvest; as הֵידָד): uncertain.
- **"Now will I shortly pour out my fury" → "Now, soon, I will pour out my fury"** (v8) — מִקָּרוֹב "from near" (= soon); חֵמָה "fury", אַף "anger"; וְכִלֵּיתִי "I will spend", as 5:13.
- **"that I am the LORD that smiteth" → "that I am Yahweh, who strikes"** (v9) — מַכֶּה, participle of הִכָּה "strike".
- **"the morning is gone forth; the rod hath blossomed, pride hath budded" → "The turn has gone out; the staff has blossomed; presumption has budded"** (v10) — צָץ of צִיץ "blossom", פָּרַח "bud" (the פָּרַח row); מַטֶּה "staff" (also v11; as 5:16 "the staff of bread"); זָדוֹן "presumption" (the זָדוֹן row).
- **"Violence is risen up into a rod of wickedness: none of them shall remain, nor of their multitude, nor of any of theirs: neither shall there be wailing for them" → "Violence has risen up into a staff of wickedness; nothing of them, and nothing of their multitude, and nothing of their uproar, and no eminence among them"** (v11) — the line has no verb: "shall remain" is not in the Hebrew; לֹא־מֵהֶם, literally "not from them". הָמוֹן "multitude" (also v12–14). מֶהֱמֵהֶם and נֹהַּ are found only here: the first is taken from הָמָה "roar, be in uproar" (also read "their wealth"), the second as "eminence" (KJV "wailing"): both uncertain.
- **"the day draweth near" → "the day has arrived"** (v12) — הִגִּיעַ "has reached". **"let not the buyer rejoice, nor the seller mourn"** — jussives (אַל), kept; קוֹנֶה of קָנָה "buy" (the קָנָה row). **"for wrath is upon all the multitude thereof" → "for burning anger is upon all her multitude"** — חָרוֹן "burning anger" (the חָרוֹן row; also v14 חֲרוֹנִי "my burning anger"); הֲמוֹנָהּ "her multitude" (the land).
- **"although they were yet alive" → "though their life is yet among the living"** (v13) — וְעוֹד בַּחַיִּים חַיָּתָם, literally "and yet among the living is their life". **"for the vision is touching the whole multitude thereof, which shall not return" → "for the vision upon all her multitude shall not turn back"** — חָזוֹן "vision"; לֹא יָשׁוּב twice in the verse, "return" of the seller and "turn back" of the vision (one verb, the שׁוּב row). **"neither shall any strengthen himself in the iniquity of his life" → "and none in his iniquity shall make his life strong"** — וְאִישׁ בַּעֲוֺנוֹ חַיָּתוֹ לֹא יִתְחַזָּקוּ, literally "and a man, in his iniquity his life, they shall not strengthen themselves": a singular subject with a plural verb; the sense is uncertain. מִמְכָּר "what was sold" (the מִמְכָּר row: "sale").
- **"They have blown the trumpet, even to make all ready" → "They have blown on the trumpet and made everything ready"** (v14) — תָּקְעוּ בַתָּקוֹעַ, a perfect (not an imperative); תָּקוֹעַ, found only here, a thing blown (from תָּקַע "blow"; not חֲצֹצְרָה or שׁוֹפָר); וְהָכִין, an infinitive absolute, "and making ready". **"but none goeth" → "and there is none going"** — וְאֵין הֹלֵךְ. מִלְחָמָה "battle" (the fight at hand).
- **"The sword is without, and the pestilence and the famine within" → "The sword is outside, and the pestilence and the famine within"** (v15) — בַּחוּץ "outside", מִבָּיִת "from the house" (= within); "pestilence" and "famine" with the article.
- **"But they that escape of them shall escape" → "And their fugitives shall escape"** (v16) — וּפָלְטוּ פְּלִיטֵיהֶם, verb and noun of one root (the פָּלִיט row). **"mourning" → "moaning"** — הֹמוֹת, of הָמָה "moan, roar" (of doves). **"every one for his iniquity" → "each in his iniquity"** — אִישׁ בַּעֲוֺנוֹ (as v13).
- **"All hands shall be feeble" → "All hands shall be slack"** (v17) — רָפָה "sink, slacken" (the רָפָה row). **"all knees shall be weak as water" → "all knees shall go as water"** — תֵּלַכְנָה מַּיִם, literally "shall go water" ("as" is supplied); whether weakness or the wetting of terror is meant the words do not say.
- **"horror" → "shuddering"** (v18) — פַּלָּצוּת (beside זַעֲוָה "horror"). שַׂקִּים "sackcloth" (the שַׂק row); קָרְחָה "baldness" (shaved in mourning).
- **"their gold shall be removed" → "their gold shall become an impurity"** (v19, 20) — לְנִדָּה (the נִדָּה row: "impurity", what is cast off as unclean). **"in the day of the wrath of the LORD" → "in the day of the raging of Yahweh"** — עֶבְרָה "raging" (as Isaiah). **"they shall not satisfy their souls, neither fill their bowels" → "their soul they shall not satisfy, and their inward parts they shall not fill"** — נַפְשָׁם "their soul" (here the appetite; the plain word kept); מֵעִים "inward parts" (the מֵעִים row). **"because it is the stumblingblock of their iniquity" → "for it has been the stumbling block of their iniquity"** — הָיָה, a perfect; מִכְשׁוֹל "stumbling block".
- **"As for the beauty of his ornament, he set it in majesty" → "And the beauty of his ornament — for pride he set it"** (v20) — צְבִי "beauty" (the צְבִי row); עֲדִי "ornament" (collective; the עֲדִי row); לְגָאוֹן "for pride" (גָּאוֹן of men: the Isa 13–16 row); "his" and "he" are singular in the Hebrew (the people as one, or the silver and gold), then "they made". **"images of their abominations and of their detestable things" → "the images of their abominations, their detestable things"** — צַלְמֵי תוֹעֲבֹתָם שִׁקּוּצֵיהֶם, the second noun stands beside the first (no "and"). **"therefore have I set it far from them" → "therefore I have made it an impurity to them"** — נְתַתִּיו … לְנִדָּה, a perfect, "I have given it for an impurity".
- **"into the hands of the strangers for a prey" → "into the hand of the strangers for plunder"** (v21) — בְּיַד singular; זָר "stranger"; בַּז "plunder", שָׁלָל "spoil" (the שָׁלָל row). **"and they shall pollute it" → "and they shall profane it"** — written וחללה (with a feminine "it"), read (qere) וְחִלְּלוּהוּ "and they shall profane it" (masculine, the thing given); חִלֵּל "profane" (the חִלֵּל row; also v22, 24).
- **"they shall pollute my secret place: for the robbers shall enter into it" → "they shall profane my hidden place; and violent men shall come into it"** (v22) — צְפוּנִי, literally "my hidden one" (the thing or place I keep hidden; commonly the sanctuary, or the land): the verse does not name it; פָּרִיצִים "violent men" (or "robbers"); no "for".
- **"Make a chain: for the land is full of bloody crimes" → "Make the chain! For the land is full of judgment of bloodguilt"** (v23) — הָרַתּוֹק, with the article, found only here (a chain or fetter: uncertain); מִשְׁפַּט דָּמִים, literally "judgment of bloods" (the דָּמִים row "bloodguilt"; as the "judgment of death" row: cases that call for judgment of blood). חָמָס "violence" (also v11).
- **"the worst of the heathen" → "the evilest of the nations"** (v24) — רָעֵי גוֹיִם, literally "the evil ones of nations". **"and they shall possess their houses" → "and they shall take possession of their houses"** — the יָרַשׁ row. **"I will also make the pomp of the strong to cease" → "and I will make the pride of the strong cease"** — הִשְׁבַּתִּי, hifil of שָׁבַת "cease"; גְּאוֹן "pride" (as v20). **"and their holy places shall be defiled" → "and their sanctuaries shall be profaned"** — וְנִחֲלוּ, nifal of חָלַל "be profaned" (not נָחַל "inherit"); מְקַדְשֵׁיהֶם is pointed as a participle ("those who make them holy"), commonly taken as the plural of מִקְדָּשׁ "sanctuary": the sense of the pointing is uncertain.
- **"Destruction cometh; and they shall seek peace" → "Anguish has come; and they shall seek shalom"** (v25) — קְפָדָה, found only here (shuddering, anguish: uncertain); בָא, a perfect; שָׁלוֹם with its token; וָאָיִן "and there is none".
- **"Mischief shall come upon mischief, and rumour shall be upon rumour" → "Destruction upon destruction shall come, and report upon report shall be"** (v26) — הֹוָה (Isa 47:11 and here; the form beside הַוָּה, the reviewer's "destruction" row); שְׁמוּעָה "report" (what is heard). **"then shall they seek a vision of the prophet; but the law shall perish from the priest, and counsel from the ancients" → "and they shall seek a vision from a prophet, and torah shall perish from a priest, and counsel from elders"** — no articles in the Hebrew; תּוֹרָה with its token ("instruction"); "and" (not "but"); זְקֵנִים "elders".
- **"the prince shall be clothed with desolation" → "the chieftain shall be clothed with desolation"** (v27) — נָשִׂיא "chieftain" (the נָשִׂיא row: "one lifted up"; KJV "prince"); שְׁמָמָה "desolation". **"shall be troubled" → "shall be dismayed"** — תִּבָּהַלְנָה, nifal of בָּהַל (the נִבְהַל row). **"I will do unto them after their way" → "According to their way I will do to them"** — מִדַּרְכָּם, literally "from their way". **"and according to their deserts will I judge them" → "and by their judgments I will judge them"** — וּבְמִשְׁפְּטֵיהֶם, the judgments they themselves gave.

### Notes
- **Layout** — v2b–27 are set as lines, as the Hebrew runs in short parallel clauses; the frame (v1–2a, 5a) is prose.
- **v3–4 and v8–9** — nearly the same lines twice, each kept as printed (v9 has no "you" after "pity" and adds "who strikes").
