# Ezekiel 3

[1] And he said to me, "Son of man, what you find, eat; eat this scroll, and go, speak to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[2] And I opened my mouth, and he made me eat this scroll.

[3] And he said to me, "Son of man, you shall feed your belly and fill your inward parts with this scroll that I am giving you." And I ate, and it was in my mouth like honey for sweetness.

[4] And he said to me, "Son of man, go, come to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and speak with my words to them.

[5] For not to a people deep of lip and heavy of tongue are you sent — to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;

[6] not to many peoples deep of lip and heavy of tongue, whose words you do not understand. Surely, had I sent you to them, they would listen to you.

[7] And the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ will not be willing to listen to you, for they are not willing to listen to me; for all the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ are strong of forehead and hard of heart.

[8] Behold, I have made your face strong against their faces, and your forehead strong against their forehead.

[9] Like diamond, stronger than flint, I have made your forehead. You shall not fear them, and you shall not be dismayed before their faces, for they are a house of rebellion."

[10] And he said to me, "Son of man, all my words that I will speak to you, take into your heart, and hear with your ears.

[11] And go, come to the exiles, to the sons of your people, and speak to them and say to them, 'Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧,' whether they hear or whether they cease."

[12] And a spirit lifted me up, and I heard behind me the sound of a great quaking: "Blessed is the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from his place!"

[13] And the sound of the wings of the living creatures touching one another, and the sound of the wheels alongside them, and the sound of a great quaking.

[14] And a spirit lifted me up and took me, and I went bitter, in the fury of my spirit; and the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was strong upon me.

[15] And I came to the exiles at ⟦tel-abib|Tel Aviv|Tel-abib⟧, who were dwelling by the river ⟦chebar|Kevar|Chebar⟧, and I sat — they were dwelling there — and I sat there seven days, appalled, in their midst.

[16] And it came to pass at the end of seven days that the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying:

[17] "Son of man, a watchman I have made you for the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and you shall hear a word from my mouth, and you shall warn them from me.

[18] When I say to the wicked, 'You shall surely die,' and you do not warn him, and do not speak to warn the wicked from his wicked way, to keep him alive, he, the wicked, shall die in his iniquity, and his blood I will require from your hand.

[19] And you — when you warn the wicked, and he does not turn back from his wickedness and from his wicked way, he shall die in his iniquity; and you — you have delivered your soul.

[20] And when a righteous one turns back from his righteousness and does injustice, and I put a stumbling block before him, he shall die; because you did not warn him, in his sin he shall die, and his righteousnesses that he did shall not be remembered, and his blood I will require from your hand.

[21] And you — when you warn him, the righteous one, that the righteous one should not sin, and he does not sin, he shall surely live, because he was warned; and you — you have delivered your soul."

[22] And the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was upon me there, and he said to me, "Arise, go out to the plain, and there I will speak with you."

[23] And I arose and went out to the plain, and behold, there the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was standing, like the glory that I had seen by the river ⟦chebar|Kevar|Chebar⟧; and I fell on my face.

[24] And a spirit came into me and set me on my feet, and he spoke with me and said to me, "Go in, shut yourself in, in the midst of your house.

[25] And you, son of man, behold, they have put cords upon you, and they will bind you with them, and you shall not go out in their midst.

[26] And your tongue I will make cling to your palate, and you shall be mute, and you shall not be to them a man who reproves, for they are a house of rebellion.

[27] And when I speak with you, I will open your mouth, and you shall say to them, 'Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.' He who hears, let him hear; and he who ceases, let him cease; for they are a house of rebellion."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v11, 27) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both names (the `adonai-yhwh` token, the prophets' wording).
- **"Son of man" kept** (v1, 3, 4, 10, 17, 25) — בֶּן־אָדָם, one wording through the book.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept ("So", "Then", "But", "Moreover" removed).
- **"eat that thou findest" → "what you find, eat"** (v1) — אֵת אֲשֶׁר־תִּמְצָא אֱכוֹל, the Hebrew order; the earlier "here" is not in the Hebrew. **"he caused me to eat that roll" → "he made me eat this scroll"** (v2) — וַיַּאֲכִלֵנִי, hifil of אָכַל.
- **"cause thy belly to eat, and fill thy bowels" → "you shall feed your belly and fill your inward parts"** (v3) — imperfects, בִּטְנְךָ תַאֲכֵל וּמֵעֶיךָ תְמַלֵּא; בֶּטֶן "belly", מֵעִים "inward parts" (their rows). **"as honey for sweetness" → "like honey for sweetness"** — כִּדְבַשׁ לְמָתוֹק, word for word.
- **"go, get thee unto" → "go, come to"** (v4, 11) — לֶךְ־בֹּא, two imperatives.
- **"of a strange speech and of an hard language" → "deep of lip and heavy of tongue"** (v5, 6) — Hebrew עִמְקֵי שָׂפָה וְכִבְדֵי לָשׁוֹן: speech too "deep" to follow and a "heavy" tongue, i.e. a language one cannot follow (שָׂפָה "lip", לָשׁוֹן "tongue", כָּבֵד "heavy": their rows). **"whose words thou canst not understand" → "whose words you do not understand"** (v6) — לֹא־תִשְׁמַע, literally "you do not hear" (the reviewer's שָׁמַע row: "understand" where "hear" would say they are not heard at all). **"Surely, had I sent thee to them, they would have hearkened unto thee"** — אִם־לֹא, the oath form, "surely" (literally "if not"); שָׁמַע אֶל "listen to" (its row).
- **"will not hearken" → "will not be willing to listen"** (v7) — לֹא יֹאבוּ, אָבָה "be willing" (twice). **"impudent and hardhearted" → "strong of forehead and hard of heart"** — חִזְקֵי־מֵצַח וּקְשֵׁי־לֵב: חָזָק of the forehead, קָשֶׁה of the heart, as 2:4 has them the other way about.
- **"I have made thy face strong against their faces"** (v8) — נָתַתִּי, literally "I have given"; לְעֻמַּת "against", that is facing them, equal to them (the reviewer's row has "alongside" of things set side by side; here faces are set against faces).
- **"As an adamant harder than flint" → "Like diamond, stronger than flint"** (v9) — כְּשָׁמִיר חָזָק מִצֹּר: שָׁמִיר a very hard stone (as Jer 17:1 "diamond"; "emery" is also proposed, uncertain); חָזָק "strong", the same word as v8. **"fear them not, neither be dismayed at their looks" → "You shall not fear them, and you shall not be dismayed before their faces"** — לֹא + imperfect (in 2:6 אַל + jussive "do not").
- **"receive in thine heart" → "take into your heart"** (v10) — קַח בִּלְבָבְךָ.
- **"thy people" → "the sons of your people"** (v11) — בְּנֵי עַמֶּךָ. **"whether they will hear, or whether they will forbear" → "whether they hear or whether they cease"** (v11, 27) — חָדַל "cease", as 2:5, 7.
- **"the spirit took me up" → "a spirit lifted me up"** (v12, 14) — רוּחַ without the article (or "wind"); נָשָׂא "lift up". **"a voice of a great rushing" → "the sound of a great quaking"** (v12, 13) — קוֹל רַעַשׁ גָּדוֹל; רַעַשׁ "quaking, shaking" (of the earth "earthquake", 1 Kgs 19:11; Amos 1:1). **"Blessed be the glory of the LORD from his place" → "Blessed is the glory of Yahweh from his place"** — בָּרוּךְ, no verb ("is", as the Deut 27–28 row); מִמְּקוֹמוֹ "from his / its place". A change of בָּרוּךְ to בְּרוּם "as it rose" (one letter) is proposed (from memory); the MT is kept.
- **"I heard also the noise of the wings … that touched one another" → "And the sound of the wings of the living creatures touching one another"** (v13) — מַשִּׁיקוֹת, literally "kissing"; "one another" is "a woman to her sister" (as 1:9). No verb in the Hebrew. **"over against them" → "alongside them"** — לְעֻמָּתָם, as 1:20.
- **"and I went in bitterness, in the heat of my spirit" → "and I went bitter, in the fury of my spirit"** (v14) — מַר, an adjective; חֵמָה "fury" (its row; literally "heat"). **"took me away" → "took me"** — לָקַח.
- **"Then I came to them of the captivity at Telabib" → "And I came to the exiles at Tel Aviv"** (v15) — תֵּל אָבִיב, "mound of young ears" (Akkadian *til abubi*, "mound of the flood", is proposed: outside knowledge). **"and I sat where they sat" → "and I sat — they were dwelling there —"** — the qere וָאֵשֵׁב "and I sat"; the written form (ketiv) is וַאֲשֶׁר "and where": "… who were dwelling by the river Chebar, and where they were dwelling". יָשַׁב is one verb, "dwell" of the people, "sit" of Ezekiel (its row). **"astonished" → "appalled"** — מַשְׁמִים, hifil participle of שָׁמֵם (its row: of persons "be appalled").
- **"And it came to pass at the end of seven days, that the word of the LORD came unto me, saying" kept** (v16) — וַיְהִי … וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר, the prophets' formula. The Hebrew text has a paragraph mark (פ) in the middle of this verse.
- **"I have made thee a watchman" → "a watchman I have made you"** (v17) — צֹפֶה נְתַתִּיךָ, the Hebrew order; נָתַן "give". **"give them warning" → "warn"** (v17–21) — הִזְהִיר "warn" (its row, Ezek 3; 33).
- **"Thou shalt surely die"** (v18) — מוֹת תָּמוּת, literally "dying you shall die". **"to save his life" → "to keep him alive"** — לְחַיֹּתוֹ, piel of חָיָה "let live". **"shall die in his iniquity"** (v18, 19) — בַּעֲוֹנוֹ "in his iniquity" (not "for"). **"will I require at thine hand" → "I will require from your hand"** (v18, 20) — בִּקֵּשׁ מִיָּד, literally "seek from the hand" (its row: "require").
- **"thou hast delivered thy soul"** (v19, 21) — הִצַּלְתָּ, a perfect: "you have delivered"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul".
- **"a righteous man" → "a righteous one"** (v20, 21) — צַדִּיק, no "man". **"commit iniquity" → "does injustice"** — עָוֶל (its row). **"I lay a stumblingblock" → "I put a stumbling block"** — וְנָתַתִּי מִכְשׁוֹל, "a thing to stumble over". **"his righteousness" → "his righteousnesses"** — צִדְקֹתָו, plural (his righteous acts).
- **"if thou warn the righteous man" → "when you warn him, the righteous one"** (v21) — הִזְהַרְתּוֹ צַדִּיק, the object twice. **"he shall surely live, because he is warned"** — חָיוֹ יִחְיֶה (infinitive absolute); נִזְהָר nifal "was warned".
- **"into the plain" → "to the plain"** (v22, 23) — הַבִּקְעָה, a broad valley floor (its row: "plain", not "valley"). **"stood there" → "there … was standing"** (v23) — עֹמֵד, a participle.
- **"Go, shut thyself within thine house" → "Go in, shut yourself in, in the midst of your house"** (v24) — בֹּא הִסָּגֵר בְּתוֹךְ בֵּיתֶךָ.
- **"they shall put bands upon thee" → "they have put cords upon you"** (v25) — נָתְנוּ, a perfect, with no subject named; וַאֲסָרוּךָ "and they will bind you". **"thou shalt not go out among them" → "you shall not go out in their midst"** — בְּתוֹכָם.
- **"I will make thy tongue cleave to the roof of thy mouth" → "your tongue I will make cling to your palate"** (v26) — חֵךְ "palate"; the Hebrew order. **"shalt not be to them a reprover" → "shall not be to them a man who reproves"** — לְאִישׁ מוֹכִיחַ; הוֹכִיחַ "reprove" (its row).
- **"He that heareth, let him hear; and he that forbeareth, let him forbear" → "He who hears, let him hear; and he who ceases, let him cease"** (v27) — הַשֹּׁמֵעַ יִשְׁמָע וְהֶחָדֵל יֶחְדָּל.
