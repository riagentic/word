# Ezekiel 27

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[2] "And you, son of man, lift up a dirge over ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧,

[3] and say to ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, who dwells at the entrances of the sea, merchant of the peoples to many coastlands, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:

⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, you have said,
'I am perfect of beauty.'

[4] In the heart of the seas are your borders;
your builders perfected your beauty.

[5] Of cypresses from Senir they built for you
all the planks;
a cedar from ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ they took
to make a mast upon you.

[6] Of oaks from Bashan they made your oars;
your board they made of ivory, a daughter of ⟦asshurim|Ashurim|Asshurim⟧,
from the coastlands of Kittim.

[7] Fine linen with embroidery from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ was your sail,
to be for you a banner;
blue and purple from the coastlands of Elishah
was your covering.

[8] The inhabitants of ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧ and Arvad
were rowers for you;
your wise men, ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, were in you;
they were your sailors.

[9] The elders of ⟦gebal|Geval|Gebal⟧ and her wise men were in you,
the repairers of your breach;
all the ships of the sea and their mariners were in you,
to barter your barter.

[10] ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧ and Lud and Put were in your force,
your men of war;
shield and helmet they hung in you;
they gave your splendor.

[11] The sons of Arvad and your force were on your walls all around,
and the ⟦gamad|Gammadim|Gamadites⟧ were in your towers;
their shields they hung on your walls all around;
they perfected your beauty.

[12] Tarshish was your trader because of the abundance of all wealth; with silver, iron, tin and lead they gave your wares.

[13] ⟦javan|Yavan|Javan⟧, ⟦tubal|Tuval|Tubal⟧ and Meshech, they were your merchants; with the souls of man and vessels of bronze they gave your barter.

[14] From ⟦beth-togarmah|Beit Togarmah|Beth-togarmah⟧, horses and horsemen and mules they gave as your wares.

[15] The sons of Dedan were your merchants; many coastlands were the trade of your hand; horns of ivory and ebony they brought back as your tribute.

[16] ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ was your trader because of the abundance of your works; with turquoise, purple and embroidery and fine linen and coral and ruby they gave for your wares.

[17] ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and the land of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, they were your merchants; with wheat of ⟦minnith|Minnit|Minnith⟧, and pannag and honey and oil and balm they gave your barter.

[18] ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧ was your trader in the abundance of your works, because of the abundance of all wealth, with wine of ⟦helbon|Chelbon|Helbon⟧ and white wool.

[19] And Dan and ⟦javan|Yavan|Javan⟧, from Uzal, gave for your wares; wrought iron, cassia and cane were among your barter.

[20] Dedan was your merchant in saddlecloths for riding.

[21] ⟦arabia|Arav|Arabia⟧ and all the chieftains of ⟦kedar|Qedar|Kedar⟧, they were the traders of your hand; in lambs and rams and he-goats, in these they were your traders.

[22] The merchants of ⟦sheba|Sheva|Sheba⟧ and Raamah, they were your merchants; with the chief of all spices and with every precious stone and gold they gave your wares.

[23] ⟦haran-city|Charan|Haran⟧ and ⟦canneh|Kanneh|Canneh⟧ and Eden, the merchants of ⟦sheba|Sheva|Sheba⟧, ⟦asshur|Ashur|Asshur⟧, ⟦chilmad|Kilmad|Chilmad⟧ was your merchant.

[24] They were your merchants in choice things, in cloaks of blue and embroidery, and in chests of many-colored cloth, with cords bound and made firm, in your market.

[25] The ships of Tarshish were your caravans for your barter;
and you were filled and were very heavy
in the heart of the seas.

[26] Into many waters they have brought you,
those who row you;
the east wind has broken you
in the heart of the seas.

[27] Your wealth and your wares, your barter,
your mariners and your sailors,
the repairers of your breach and the barterers of your barter,
and all your men of war who are in you,
and with all your assembly that is in your midst,
shall fall in the heart of the seas
on the day of your fall.

[28] At the sound of the cry of your sailors
the pasture lands shall quake.

[29] And they shall come down from their ships,
all who handle the oar,
the mariners, all the sailors of the sea;
on the land they shall stand.

[30] And they shall make their voice heard over you,
and they shall cry out bitterly;
and they shall bring up dust on their heads;
in the ashes they shall roll themselves.

[31] And they shall make a baldness for you
and gird on sackcloths;
and they shall weep over you in bitterness of soul,
a bitter lamentation.

[32] And they shall lift up over you, in their wailing, a dirge,
and chant a dirge over you:
'Who is like ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧,
like one silenced in the midst of the sea?

[33] When your wares went out from the seas,
you sated many peoples;
with the abundance of your wealth and your barter
you enriched the kings of the earth.

[34] At the time you are broken by the seas,
in the depths of the waters,
your barter and all your assembly in your midst
have fallen.

[35] All the inhabitants of the coastlands
are appalled over you,
and their kings shudder with shuddering;
their faces are troubled.

[36] The traders among the peoples hiss over you;
terrors you have become,
and you shall be no more forever.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v3) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both names.
- **"The word of the LORD came again unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר; "again" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Now, thou son of man, take up a lamentation for Tyrus" → "And you, son of man, lift up a dirge over Tyre"** (v2) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה; נָשָׂא קִינָה "lift up a dirge" (קִינָה, its row; also v32).
- **"O thou that art situate at the entry of the sea, which art a merchant of the people for many isles … O Tyrus, thou hast said, I am of perfect beauty" → "who dwells at the entrances of the sea, merchant of the peoples to many coastlands … Tyre, you have said, 'I am perfect of beauty'"** (v3) — Hebrew הַיֹּשֶׁבֶת "who dwells" (the qere; the ketiv הישבתי is the same word with an old feminine ending); מְבוֹאֹת יָם "the entrances of the sea" (plural: Tyre's harbors); רֹכֶלֶת "merchant" (feminine; the trade words of the chapter are kept apart: רָכַל "merchant", סָחַר "trader", v12); אִיִּים "coastlands" (its row; also v6, 7, 15, 35); אַתְּ אָמַרְתְּ "you have said", the pronoun written; no "O"; כְּלִילַת יֹפִי "perfect of beauty" (כָּלִיל "whole, complete"; the verb כָּלַל "perfect" in v4, 11 is the same root).
- **"Thy borders are in the midst of the seas" → "In the heart of the seas are your borders"** (v4) — Hebrew בְּלֵב יַמִּים, "heart" (also v25–27); the Hebrew order.
- **"They have made all thy ship boards of fir trees of Senir: they have taken cedars from Lebanon to make masts for thee" → "Of cypresses from Senir they built for you all the planks; a cedar from Lebanon they took to make a mast upon you"** (v5) — Hebrew בְּרוֹשִׁים "cypresses" (as Isa 41:19; 60:13: the kind is uncertain, cypress or juniper); בָּנָה "build"; לֻחֹתָיִם "planks", a dual form found only here (both sides of the hull: uncertain); אֶרֶז singular "a cedar"; תֹּרֶן singular "mast"; עָלָיִךְ "upon you". Senir is Hermon (Deut 3:9).
- **"Of the oaks of Bashan have they made thine oars; the company of the Ashurites have made thy benches of ivory, brought out of the isles of Chittim" → "Of oaks from Bashan they made your oars; your board they made of ivory, a daughter of Ashurim, from the coastlands of Kittim"** (v6) — Hebrew אַלּוֹנִים "oaks" (its row); מִשּׁוֹט "oar" (also מָשׁוֹט v29); קַרְשֵׁךְ "your board" (קֶרֶשׁ, its row: plank or deck, uncertain); שֵׁן "ivory" (lit. "tooth"; also v15); בַּת־אֲשֻׁרִים as printed, "a daughter of Ashurim" (a people, as Gen 25:3, or a place; בַּת may mean "product of"); it is commonly read as one word, בִּתְאַשֻּׁרִים "in te'ashur wood" (the tree of Isa 41:19; 60:13: box or pine), which the MT does not have; כִּתִּיִּים "Kittim" (the qere; the ketiv כתים, the same name spelled short; Cyprus and the western coasts).
- **"Fine linen with broidered work from Egypt was that which thou spreadest forth to be thy sail; blue and purple from the isles of Elishah was that which covered thee" → "Fine linen with embroidery from Egypt was your sail, to be for you a banner; blue and purple from the coastlands of Elishah was your covering"** (v7) — Hebrew שֵׁשׁ "fine linen" (its row); רִקְמָה "embroidery" (also v16, 24); מִפְרָשׂ "sail" (lit. "what is spread out"); לִהְיוֹת לָךְ לְנֵס "to be for you a banner" (נֵס, its row: the ship's sign); תְּכֵלֶת וְאַרְגָּמָן "blue and purple" (the Exod 25 row; no "yarn"); מְכַסֶּה "covering" (an awning, or a deck covering).
- **"The inhabitants of Zidon and Arvad were thy mariners: thy wise men, O Tyrus, that were in thee, were thy pilots" → "The inhabitants of Sidon and Arvad were rowers for you; your wise men, Tyre, were in you; they were your sailors"** (v8) — Hebrew שָׁטִים "rowers" (also v26: "those who row you"); חֲכָמִים "wise men" (also v9: skilled men); חֹבְלִים "sailors" (from חֶבֶל "rope": the men who work the ropes; KJV "pilots"; also v27–29); מַלָּח "mariner" (another word, v9, 27, 29).
- **"The ancients of Gebal and the wise men thereof were in thee thy calkers: all the ships of the sea with their mariners were in thee to occupy thy merchandise" → "The elders of Gebal and her wise men were in you, the repairers of your breach; all the ships of the sea and their mariners were in you, to barter your barter"** (v9) — Hebrew זְקֵנִים "elders"; מַחֲזִיקֵי בִּדְקֵךְ "the repairers of your breach" (lit. "those who make strong your breach": the 2 Kgs 12 row; also v27); לַעֲרֹב מַעֲרָבֵךְ "to barter your barter", verb and noun of one root (עָרַב "exchange"; מַעֲרָב "barter", the goods exchanged; KJV "market", "merchandise"; also v13, 17, 19, 25, 27, 33, 34).
- **"They of Persia and of Lud and of Phut were in thine army … they set forth thy comeliness" → "Persia and Lud and Put were in your force … they gave your splendor"** (v10) — Hebrew חַיִל "force" (its row: of an army; also v11); מָגֵן "shield"; כּוֹבַע "helmet"; נָתְנוּ הֲדָרֵךְ "they gave your splendor" (הָדָר "splendor"). Lud and Put: peoples of the far west and south (Lydia? Libya?), uncertain.
- **"The men of Arvad with thine army were upon thy walls round about, and the Gammadims were in thy towers" → "The sons of Arvad and your force were on your walls all around, and the Gammadim were in your towers"** (v11) — Hebrew בְּנֵי אַרְוַד "the sons of Arvad"; וְחֵילֵךְ "and your force", as pointed (the same word as v10; the reading "Helech", a place name, divides the consonants otherwise and is not the MT); גַּמָּדִים "Gammadim" (unknown: a people or a town; the word kept); שְׁלָטִים "shields" (the שֶׁלֶט row, uncertain; another word than מָגֵן of v10: English has one); כָּלְלוּ "perfected" (as v4).
- **"Tarshish was thy merchant by reason of the multitude of all kind of riches; with silver, iron, tin, and lead, they traded in thy fairs" → "Tarshish was your trader because of the abundance of all wealth; with silver, iron, tin and lead they gave your wares"** (v12) — Hebrew סֹחַרְתֵּךְ "your trader" (a feminine participle; also v16, 18; סֹחֵר "trader", its row; v21, 36); מֵרֹב כָּל־הוֹן "because of (lit. "from") the abundance of all wealth" (הוֹן "wealth", the Proverbs row; also v18, 27, 33); נָתְנוּ עִזְבוֹנָיִךְ "they gave your wares": עִזְבוֹנִים, only in this chapter (v12, 14, 16, 19, 22, 27, 33), from עָזַב "leave", is uncertain: the goods handed over or left in exchange (KJV "fairs"). Through v12–24 the בְּ before the goods ("with silver") may mean that they paid with them, or that these were what they brought: the Hebrew is kept.
- **"Javan, Tubal, and Meshech, they were thy merchants: they traded the persons of men and vessels of brass in thy market" → "Javan, Tubal and Meshech, they were your merchants; with the souls of man and vessels of bronze they gave your barter"** (v13) — Hebrew רֹכְלַיִךְ "your merchants" (also v15, 17, 22–24); נֶפֶשׁ אָדָם "souls of man" (the Num 31 row: slaves are meant); נְחֹשֶׁת "bronze" (its row).
- **"They of the house of Togarmah traded in thy fairs with horses and horsemen and mules" → "From Beth-togarmah, horses and horsemen and mules they gave as your wares"** (v14) — Hebrew מִבֵּית תּוֹגַרְמָה "from Beth-togarmah"; פָּרָשִׁים "horsemen" (its row; here perhaps "steeds": uncertain); פְּרָדִים "mules".
- **"The men of Dedan were thy merchants; many isles were the merchandise of thine hand: they brought thee for a present horns of ivory and ebony" → "The sons of Dedan were your merchants; many coastlands were the trade of your hand; horns of ivory and ebony they brought back as your tribute"** (v15) — Hebrew בְּנֵי דְדָן "the sons of Dedan" (the Septuagint has "Rhodians", reading ר for ד, from memory); סְחֹרַת יָדֵךְ "the trade of your hand" (noun of סָחַר); קַרְנוֹת שֵׁן "horns of ivory" (tusks); הָבְנִים "ebony" (the qere; the ketiv והובנים is a fuller spelling); הֵשִׁיבוּ "brought back" (in return); אֶשְׁכָּר "tribute" (its row, uncertain: payment or gift).
- **"Syria was thy merchant by reason of the multitude of the wares of thy making: they occupied in thy fairs with emeralds, purple, and broidered work, and fine linen, and coral, and agate" → "Aram was your trader because of the abundance of your works; with turquoise, purple and embroidery and fine linen and coral and ruby they gave for your wares"** (v16) — Hebrew אֲרָם "Aram" (some Hebrew manuscripts and the Syriac read אֱדוֹם "Edom", one letter apart, from memory; the MT kept); מַעֲשַׂיִךְ "your works" (what you make); נֹפֶךְ "turquoise" (the breastpiece row, uncertain); בּוּץ "fine linen" (the later word, its row; v7 has שֵׁשׁ); רָאמוֹת "coral" (the Job 28 row, uncertain); כַּדְכֹּד "ruby" (as Isa 54:12, uncertain); נָתְנוּ בְּעִזְבוֹנָיִךְ "they gave for (lit. "in") your wares".
- **"they traded in thy market wheat of Minnith, and Pannag, and honey, and oil, and balm" → "with wheat of Minnith, and pannag and honey and oil and balm they gave your barter"** (v17) — Hebrew חִטֵּי מִנִּית "wheat of Minnith" (an Ammonite town, Judg 11:33); פַּנַּג, found only here: unknown (a meal, a sweet, or a place), the word kept; צֳרִי "balm" (its row, uncertain).
- **"Damascus was thy merchant in the multitude of the wares of thy making, for the multitude of all riches; in the wine of Helbon, and white wool" → "Damascus was your trader in the abundance of your works, because of the abundance of all wealth, with wine of Helbon and white wool"** (v18) — Hebrew בְּרֹב … מֵרֹב, "in" and "from" the abundance; צֶמֶר צָחַר "white wool" (צַחַר "whiteness", as צְחֹרוֹת "white" Judg 5:10; taken by some as a place name, "wool of Zahar": uncertain; צֶמֶר "wool", its row).
- **"Dan also and Javan going to and fro occupied in thy fairs: bright iron, cassia, and calamus, were in thy market" → "And Dan and Javan, from Uzal, gave for your wares; wrought iron, cassia and cane were among your barter"** (v19) — Hebrew וְדָן "And Dan" (the vav taken by some as part of a name, "Vedan"); מְאוּזָּל "from Uzal" (מִן + the place of Gen 10:27; KJV "going to and fro", from another root; uncertain); נָתָנּוּ "they gave"; בַּרְזֶל עָשׁוֹת "wrought iron" (lit. "iron made"); קִדָּה "cassia" (its row); קָנֶה "cane" (lit. "reed": the aromatic cane, the קְנֵה־בֹשֶׂם of Exod 30:23); בְּמַעֲרָבֵךְ הָיָה "was in your barter".
- **"Dedan was thy merchant in precious clothes for chariots" → "Dedan was your merchant in saddlecloths for riding"** (v20) — Hebrew בְּבִגְדֵי־חֹפֶשׁ לְרִכְבָּה, lit. "in garments of *chofesh* for riding": חֹפֶשׁ found only here (spread cloths: saddlecloths is the plainest sense; uncertain); רִכְבָּה "riding" (only here).
- **"Arabia, and all the princes of Kedar, they occupied with thee in lambs, and rams, and goats: in these were they thy merchants" → "Arabia and all the chieftains of Kedar, they were the traders of your hand; in lambs and rams and he-goats, in these they were your traders"** (v21) — Hebrew נְשִׂיאֵי "chieftains" (its row); סֹחֲרֵי יָדֵךְ "the traders of your hand"; כָּרִים "lambs" (its row); עַתּוּדִים "he-goats" (its row); בָּם "in them".
- **"they occupied in thy fairs with chief of all spices, and with all precious stones, and gold" → "with the chief of all spices and with every precious stone and gold they gave your wares"** (v22) — Hebrew בְּרֹאשׁ כָּל־בֹּשֶׂם "with the chief (lit. "head") of all spice" (the best); כָּל־אֶבֶן יְקָרָה "every precious stone".
- **"Haran, and Canneh, and Eden, the merchants of Sheba, Asshur, and Chilmad, were thy merchants" → "Haran and Canneh and Eden, the merchants of Sheba, Asshur, Chilmad was your merchant"** (v23) — Hebrew רֹכְלֵי שְׁבָא "the merchants of Sheba" stands in the list as printed (some take it as a gloss, from memory); אַשּׁוּר כִּלְמַד רֹכַלְתֵּךְ, no "and", the participle singular: "was your merchant". Eden here is a town of Mesopotamia (2 Kgs 19:12), not the garden.
- **"These were thy merchants in all sorts of things, in blue clothes, and broidered work, and in chests of rich apparel, bound with cords, and made of cedar, among thy merchandise" → "They were your merchants in choice things, in cloaks of blue and embroidery, and in chests of many-colored cloth, with cords bound and made firm, in your market"** (v24) — Hebrew מַכְלֻלִים "choice things" (only here; root כָּלַל "perfect": things made perfect, costly garments); גְּלוֹמִים "cloaks" (only here, "wrappings"); גִּנְזֵי "chests" (lit. "treasuries", as the Esther row; uncertain here); בְּרֹמִים "many-colored cloth" (only here, uncertain); חֲבָלִים חֲבֻשִׁים "cords bound" (twisted); אֲרֻזִים "made firm" (uncertain; KJV "made of cedar", from אֶרֶז); מַרְכֹּלֶת "market" (only here; root רָכַל).
- **"The ships of Tarshish did sing of thee in thy market: and thou wast replenished, and made very glorious in the midst of the seas" → "The ships of Tarshish were your caravans for your barter; and you were filled and were very heavy in the heart of the seas"** (v25) — Hebrew שָׁרוֹתַיִךְ "your caravans" (uncertain: from שׁוּר "travel"; KJV from שִׁיר "sing"); מַעֲרָבֵךְ "your barter" (no "for" written; KJV "in thy market"); וַתִּמָּלְאִי וַתִּכְבְּדִי מְאֹד "and you were filled and were very heavy" (laden; כָּבֵד "be heavy", its row; not "glorious").
- **"Thy rowers have brought thee into great waters: the east wind hath broken thee in the midst of the seas" → "Into many waters they have brought you, those who row you; the east wind has broken you in the heart of the seas"** (v26) — Hebrew בְּמַיִם רַבִּים "into many waters"; הַשָּׁטִים אֹתָךְ "those who row you" (as v8); רוּחַ הַקָּדִים "the east wind" (its row); שָׁבַר "break".
- **"Thy riches, and thy fairs, thy merchandise, thy mariners, and thy pilots, thy calkers, and the occupiers of thy merchandise … and in all thy company … shall fall into the midst of the seas in the day of thy ruin" → "Your wealth and your wares, your barter, your mariners and your sailors, the repairers of your breach and the barterers of your barter … and with all your assembly … shall fall in the heart of the seas on the day of your fall"** (v27) — the words of v8–9, 12; עֹרְבֵי מַעֲרָבֵךְ "the barterers of your barter"; וּבְכָל־קְהָלֵךְ "and with (lit. "in") all your assembly" (קָהָל, its row; also v34); מַפֶּלֶת "fall" (as 26:15, 18).
- **"The suburbs shall shake at the sound of the cry of thy pilots" → "At the sound of the cry of your sailors the pasture lands shall quake"** (v28) — Hebrew לְקוֹל זַעֲקַת "at the sound of the cry of"; מִגְרָשׁוֹת "pasture lands" (מִגְרָשׁ, its row: the open land round a town; here perhaps the coast or the waves: uncertain); רָעַשׁ "quake" (as 26:10, 15).
- **"And all that handle the oar … shall come down from their ships, they shall stand upon the land" → "And they shall come down from their ships, all who handle the oar, the mariners, all the sailors of the sea; on the land they shall stand"** (v29) — the Hebrew order; תֹּפְשֵׂי מָשׁוֹט "those who handle the oar" (תָּפַשׂ "seize, take hold": its row); אֶל־הָאָרֶץ "on (lit. "to") the land".
- **"And shall cause their voice to be heard against thee, and shall cry bitterly, and shall cast up dust upon their heads, they shall wallow themselves in the ashes" → "And they shall make their voice heard over you, and they shall cry out bitterly; and they shall bring up dust on their heads; in the ashes they shall roll themselves"** (v30) — Hebrew הִשְׁמִיעַ "make heard" (its row); זָעַק "cry out"; הֶעֱלָה עָפָר "bring up dust" (עָפָר, its row); אֵפֶר "ashes" (its row); הִתְפַּלֵּשׁ "roll oneself".
- **"And they shall make themselves utterly bald for thee, and gird them with sackcloth, and they shall weep for thee with bitterness of heart and bitter wailing" → "And they shall make a baldness for you and gird on sackcloths; and they shall weep over you in bitterness of soul, a bitter lamentation"** (v31) — Hebrew הִקְרִיחוּ … קָרְחָה "make a baldness" (its row); שַׂקִּים plural "sackcloths" (its row); מַר־נֶפֶשׁ "bitterness of soul" (נֶפֶשׁ "soul", not "heart"); מִסְפֵּד מָר "a bitter lamentation" (noun of סָפַד "lament", its row), no "and" written.
- **"And in their wailing they shall take up a lamentation for thee, and lament over thee, saying, What city is like Tyrus, like the destroyed in the midst of the sea?" → "And they shall lift up over you, in their wailing, a dirge, and chant a dirge over you: 'Who is like Tyre, like one silenced in the midst of the sea?'"** (v32) — Hebrew בְּנִיהֶם "in their wailing" (נִי, a rare form of נְהִי "wailing"); קוֹנֵן "chant a dirge" (its row); מִי "who" ("city" and "saying" are not in the Hebrew); כְּדֻמָה "like one silenced" (from דָּמַם "be silent", or דָּמָה "be cut off": uncertain).
- **"When thy wares went forth out of the seas, thou filledst many people; thou didst enrich the kings of the earth with the multitude of thy riches and of thy merchandise" → "When your wares went out from the seas, you sated many peoples; with the abundance of your wealth and your barter you enriched the kings of the earth"** (v33) — Hebrew הִשְׂבִּיעַ "sate" (its row); עַמִּים plural "peoples"; הוֹנַיִךְ plural (lit. "your wealths"); the Hebrew order.
- **"In the time when thou shalt be broken by the seas in the depths of the waters thy merchandise and all thy company in the midst of thee shall fall" → "At the time you are broken by the seas, in the depths of the waters, your barter and all your assembly in your midst have fallen"** (v34) — Hebrew עֵת נִשְׁבֶּרֶת מִיַּמִּים "a time, you are broken by (lit. "from") the seas" (many read עַתָּה "now", with the versions, from memory: the MT kept); נָפָלוּ perfect "have fallen".
- **"All the inhabitants of the isles shall be astonished at thee, and their kings shall be sore afraid, they shall be troubled in their countenance" → "All the inhabitants of the coastlands are appalled over you, and their kings shudder with shuddering; their faces are troubled"** (v35) — Hebrew שָׁמְמוּ perfect "are appalled" (its row); שָׂעֲרוּ שַׂעַר "shudder with shuddering" (as 32:10) (verb and noun of one root: hair bristling with horror); רָעֲמוּ פָּנִים "their faces are troubled" (lit. "they quiver as to face"; רָעַם "thunder", here "be agitated": uncertain).
- **"The merchants among the people shall hiss at thee; thou shalt be a terror, and never shalt be any more" → "The traders among the peoples hiss over you; terrors you have become, and you shall be no more forever"** (v36) — Hebrew סֹחֲרִים "traders" (v12); שָׁרַק "hiss" (whistle in horror or scorn); בַּלָּהוֹת הָיִית "terrors you have become" (plural, as 26:21); וְאֵינֵךְ עַד־עוֹלָם "and you are not, forever".
- **Layout** — v3b–11 and v25b–36 are the dirge, as poetry (v25 with its first line), one Hebrew line per printed line by the accents; v12–24, the list of trade, prose. The goods and peoples are given in their plainest sense; where the kind is unknown, the Changes says so.

### Notes
- **Tyre as a ship** — the dirge describes Tyre as a merchant ship, built (v4–11), laden (v12–25) and wrecked (v26–36). Arvad and Gebal (Byblos) are Phoenician ports north of Tyre; Javan is Greece (Ionia); Tubal, Meshech and Togarmah lie in Asia Minor; Dedan, Sheba and Raamah in Arabia; Haran, Canneh, Eden, Asshur and Chilmad in Mesopotamia.
