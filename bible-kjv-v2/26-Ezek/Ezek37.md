[1] The hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was upon me, and he brought me out in the Spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and set me down in the middle of the valley; it was full of bones.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*; Ezekiel's distinctive doubled divine title.
- **"Spirit of the LORD" → "Spirit of Yahweh"** (v1) — Hebrew רוּחַ יְהוָה *Ruach Yahweh*; "the hand of Yahweh was upon me, and he brought me out in the *Ruach* of Yahweh"; the *Ruach* as the divine transport of prophetic vision.

### Notes
- **Valley of dry bones** — one of Bible's most famous visions; national resurrection imagery read in synagogue on Sabbath of Passover.
- **v27** — echoed Rev 21:3.
- ***Ruach*** plays: "breath/wind/spirit" all same word.
