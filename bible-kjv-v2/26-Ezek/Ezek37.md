# Ezekiel 37

[1] The hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was upon me, and he brought me out by the spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and set me down in the midst of the plain; and it was full of bones.

[2] And he made me pass by them all around, all around; and behold, they were very many on the face of the plain, and behold, they were very dry.

[3] And he said to me, "Son of man, can these bones live?" And I said, "⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧, you know."

[4] And he said to me, "Prophesy over these bones, and say to them, Dry bones, hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ to these bones: Behold, I am bringing breath into you, and you shall live.

[6] And I will put sinews upon you, and make flesh come up upon you, and spread skin over you, and put breath in you, and you shall live; and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[7] And I prophesied as I was commanded; and there was a sound as I prophesied, and behold, a quaking, and bones came near, bone to its bone.

[8] And I looked, and behold, there were sinews upon them, and flesh had come up, and skin spread over them from above; but breath there was none in them.

[9] And he said to me, "Prophesy to the breath; prophesy, son of man, and say to the breath, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: From the four winds come, breath, and breathe into these slain, that they may live."

[10] And I prophesied as he commanded me, and the breath came into them, and they lived and stood on their feet, an exceedingly great force.

[11] And he said to me, "Son of man, these bones, they are the whole house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧. Behold, they are saying, 'Our bones are dried up, and our hope has perished; we are cut off, for our part.'

[12] Therefore prophesy, and say to them, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I am opening your graves, and I will bring you up out of your graves, my people, and I will bring you to the ground of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[13] And you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, when I open your graves and when I bring you up out of your graves, my people.

[14] And I will put my spirit in you, and you shall live, and I will place you on your ground; and you shall know that I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have spoken, and I will do it, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[15] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying:

[16] "And you, son of man, take yourself one stick, and write on it, 'For ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, his companions'; and take one stick, and write on it, 'For ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, the stick of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, and all the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, his companions.'

[17] And bring them near, one to the other, for yourself into one stick, and they shall become one in your hand.

[18] And when the sons of your people say to you, saying, 'Will you not tell us what these are to you?'

[19] speak to them, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I am taking the stick of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, which is in the hand of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, and the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, his companions; and I will put them with it, with the stick of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and make them into one stick, and they shall be one in my hand.

[20] And the sticks on which you write shall be in your hand before their eyes.

[21] And speak to them, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I am taking the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from among the nations where they have gone, and I will gather them together from all around, and bring them to their ground.

[22] And I will make them one nation in the land, on the mountains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and one king shall be king to them all; and they shall no more be two nations, and they shall no more be divided into two kingdoms any more.

[23] And they shall no more defile themselves with their idols and with their detestable things and with all their transgressions; and I will save them out of all their dwelling places in which they have sinned, and I will cleanse them; and they shall be a people to me, and I will be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to them.

[24] And my servant David shall be king over them, and one shepherd there shall be for them all; and in my ordinances they shall walk, and my statutes they shall keep, and do them.

[25] And they shall dwell on the land that I gave to my servant, to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, in which your fathers dwelt; and they shall dwell on it, they and their sons and their sons' sons, forever; and David my servant shall be chieftain to them forever.

[26] And I will cut for them a covenant of ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧; an everlasting covenant it shall be with them. And I will set them, and multiply them, and I will set my sanctuary in their midst forever.

[27] And my dwelling shall be over them; and I will be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to them, and they shall be a people to me.

[28] And the nations shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who sanctifies ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, when my sanctuary is in their midst forever."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v23, 27) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*, both words; Ezekiel's distinctive doubled divine title.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept ("Then", "So", "But" before were not in the words).
- **רוּחַ "spirit", "breath", "wind"** (v1, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 14) — one Hebrew word, and the passage turns on it: v1 "by the spirit of Yahweh" (בְרוּחַ יְהוָה, what carries the prophet); v5, 6, 8, 9b, 10 "breath" (what makes the bodies live; the breath "comes" and "breathes", פְּחִי, into the slain); v9a "the four winds" (אַרְבַּע רוּחוֹת, the quarters from which it comes); v14 "my spirit" (רוּחִי, Yahweh's own). The English cannot keep one word; the reader should hear that "spirit", "breath" and "winds" are the same Hebrew word, so that Yahweh's "my spirit" in v14 is the breath of v5–10. Lower case "spirit" as the finished books.
- **"in the Spirit of the LORD" → "by the spirit of Yahweh"** (v1) — בְּ "by / in" (the means); lower case. **"in the midst of the valley" → "in the midst of the plain"** — בִּקְעָה "plain" (a broad valley floor; the בִּקְעָה row; also v2), as 3:22–23; 8:4. **"which was full of bones" → "and it was full of bones"** — וְהִיא.
- **"And caused me to pass by them round about" → "And he made me pass by them all around, all around"** (v2) — הֶעֱבִירַנִי, hifil of עָבַר (the הֶעֱבִיר row); עֲלֵיהֶם "by / over them"; סָבִיב סָבִיב doubled, kept. **"in the open valley" → "on the face of the plain"** — עַל־פְּנֵי.
- **"And I answered" → "And I said"** (v3) — וָאֹמַר; no "O" before the name (the vocative row). **"thou knowest"** — אַתָּה יָדָעְתָּ, the pronoun written out ("you, you know").
- **"O ye dry bones" → "Dry bones"** (v4) — vocative with the article, no "O".
- **"I will cause breath to enter into you" → "I am bringing breath into you"** (v5) — מֵבִיא, a participle.
- **"I will lay sinews upon you, and will bring up flesh upon you, and cover you with skin" → "I will put sinews upon you, and make flesh come up upon you, and spread skin over you"** (v6) — נָתַן "give, put"; הֶעֱלָה "make come up" (hifil of עָלָה; v8 עָלָה "came up"); קָרַם "spread over" (only here and v8: to form a covering, as a crust).
- **"there was a noise" → "there was a sound"** (v7) — קוֹל "voice; sound" (its row). **"a shaking" → "a quaking"** — רַעַשׁ (the Ezekiel row; "rattling" is a guess from the bones). **"and the bones came together" → "and bones came near"** — וַתִּקְרְבוּ עֲצָמוֹת, no article; קָרַב "come near" (its row).
- **"the sinews and the flesh came up upon them" → "there were sinews upon them, and flesh had come up"** (v8) — the Hebrew order. **"but there was no breath in them" → "but breath there was none in them"** — the noun first.
- **"Come from the four winds, O breath" → "From the four winds come, breath"** (v9) — the Hebrew order; vocative with the article, no "O". **"breathe upon these slain" → "breathe into these slain"** — פְּחִי בַּ (נָפַח "breathe / blow", its row, as Gen 2:7); הֲרוּגִים "slain" (killed ones, from הָרַג "kill"; another word than חָלָל "slain"). **"that they may live"** — וְיִחְיוּ, "and let them live".
- **"an exceeding great army" → "an exceedingly great force"** (v10) — חַיִל "force" (the חַיִל row, of an army); מְאֹד־מְאֹד "very, very".
- **"these bones are the whole house of Israel" → "these bones, they are the whole house of Israel"** (v11) — הֵמָּה, the pronoun kept. **"behold, they say" → "Behold, they are saying"** — אֹמְרִים, a participle. **"our hope is lost" → "our hope has perished"** — אָבַד "perish" (its row). **"we are cut off for our parts" → "we are cut off, for our part"** — נִגְזַרְנוּ לָנוּ, literally "we are cut off, to us".
- **"I will open your graves, and cause you to come up out of your graves, O my people" → "I am opening your graves, and I will bring you up out of your graves, my people"** (v12) — פֹתֵחַ participle; הֶעֱלָה "bring up" (also v13); no "O". **"into the land of Israel" → "to the ground of Israel"** — אַדְמַת יִשְׂרָאֵל, אֲדָמָה "ground" (Ezekiel's own phrase; also v14, 21 "your / their ground"; אֶרֶץ "land" v22, 25).
- **"brought you up" → "when I bring you up"** (v13) — וּבְהַעֲלוֹתִי, the second "when" written.
- **"shall put my spirit in you … and I shall place you in your own land" → "I will put my spirit in you … and I will place you on your ground"** (v14) — רוּחִי lower case; וְהִנַּחְתִּי, hifil of נוּחַ (v1 וַיְנִיחֵנִי "set me down", another hifil form of the same root); עַל "on"; "own" is not written. **"then shall ye know that I the LORD have spoken it, and performed it" → "and you shall know that I, Yahweh, have spoken, and I will do it"** — כִּי־אֲנִי יְהוָה דִּבַּרְתִּי וְעָשִׂיתִי: the verb follows the name ("I, Yahweh, have spoken"; as 17:24); וְעָשִׂיתִי, a weqatal, "and I will do" (or "and I have done", as KJV); "it" supplied.
- **"The word of the LORD came again unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v15) — the prophets' formula, one wording.
- **"Moreover, thou son of man, take thee one stick" → "And you, son of man, take yourself one stick"** (v16) — וְאַתָּה; קַח־לְךָ "take yourself" (as 4:1); עֵץ אֶחָד "one stick" both times (KJV "another stick" the second time): the two "one sticks" become "one stick" in v17, 19. עֵץ is "wood, tree"; here a piece of wood one can write on and hold, "stick" (also "rod"; whether a writing tablet is meant is not said). **"the children of Israel his companions" → "the sons of Israel, his companions"** — written חברו "his companion", read (qere) חֲבֵרָיו "his companions" (also v19); the sense is the same. **"For Joseph, the stick of Ephraim"** — no brackets: the Hebrew sets the words side by side.
- **"And join them one to another into one stick; and they shall become one in thine hand" → "And bring them near, one to the other, for yourself into one stick, and they shall become one in your hand"** (v17) — קָרַב piel "bring near" (as קָרַב v7); לְךָ "for yourself"; לַאֲחָדִים, a plural, literally "into ones": "one" as the sense.
- **"the children of thy people" → "the sons of your people"** (v18) — בְּנֵי עַמְּךָ; לֵאמֹר "saying" kept. **"what thou meanest by these" → "what these are to you"** — מָה־אֵלֶּה לָּךְ.
- **"Say unto them" → "speak to them"** (v19, 21) — דַּבֵּר "speak". **"I will take" → "I am taking"** — לֹקֵחַ, a participle (also v21). **"and will put them with him, even with the stick of Judah" → "and I will put them with it, with the stick of Judah"** — וְנָתַתִּי אוֹתָם עָלָיו אֶת־עֵץ יְהוּדָה, literally "and I will give them upon it, the stick of Judah": the construction is hard; עַל "upon / to" taken as "with". **"and they shall be one in mine hand"** — וְהָיוּ אֶחָד.
- **"And the sticks whereon thou writest shall be in thine hand before their eyes"** (v20) — וְהָיוּ, a weqatal: a new command, not a "when"-clause; לְעֵינֵיהֶם "before their eyes" (the row).
- **"from among the heathen, whither they be gone" → "from among the nations where they have gone"** (v21) — גּוֹיִם "nations". **"and will gather them on every side" → "and I will gather them together from all around"** — קִבֵּץ "gather together" (as 11:17); מִסָּבִיב. **"into their own land" → "to their ground"** — אַדְמָתָם.
- **"and they shall be no more two nations, neither shall they be divided into two kingdoms any more at all" → "and they shall no more be two nations, and they shall no more be divided into two kingdoms any more"** (v22) — written יהיה "it shall be", read (qere) יִהְיוּ "they shall be"; עוֹד "any more" three times (the third at the end, kept); "and" (וְלֹא), not "neither". **"one king shall be king to them all"** — לְכֻלָּם, kept.
- **"neither shall they defile themselves any more with their idols, nor with their detestable things, nor with any of their transgressions" → "And they shall no more defile themselves with their idols and with their detestable things and with all their transgressions"** (v23) — יִטַּמְּאוּ hitpael "defile themselves"; גִּלּוּלִים "idols" (its row: a word of contempt); שִׁקּוּצִים "detestable things"; each with "and with". **"out of all their dwellingplaces" → "out of all their dwelling places"** — MT מוֹשְׁבֹתֵיהֶם "their dwelling places" (מוֹשָׁב, its row). The earlier "backslidings" followed the common correction מְשׁוּבֹתֵיהֶם "their turnings away" (one letter moved; the Septuagint reads "lawlessnesses", from memory): the MT is kept. **"so shall they be my people, and I will be their God" → "and they shall be a people to me, and I will be Elohim to them"** — וְהָיוּ־לִי לְעָם … אֶהְיֶה לָהֶם לֵאלֹהִים, as 11:20 (also v27).
- **"And David my servant shall be king over them; and they all shall have one shepherd" → "And my servant David shall be king over them, and one shepherd there shall be for them all"** (v24) — the Hebrew order (עַבְדִּי דָוִד; עֶבֶד of Yahweh's man: "servant", the עֶבֶד row); רֹעֶה "shepherd" (its row). **"they shall also walk in my judgments, and observe my statutes, and do them" → "and in my ordinances they shall walk, and my statutes they shall keep, and do them"** — מִשְׁפָּטִים "ordinances" (the מִשְׁפָּט row: of rulings), חֻקּוֹת "statutes", שָׁמַר "keep"; the object first, as the Hebrew.
- **"wherein your fathers have dwelt; and they shall dwell therein, even they, and their children, and their children's children for ever" → "in which your fathers dwelt; and they shall dwell on it, they and their sons and their sons' sons, forever"** (v25) — יָשַׁב "dwell" both times (was "lived"); עַל "on"; בָּנִים "sons" (the בֵּן row); עַד־עוֹלָם "forever" (also לְעוֹלָם). **"unto Jacob my servant" → "to my servant, to Jacob"** — לְעַבְדִּי לְיַעֲקֹב, the order kept. **"and my servant David shall be their prince for ever" → "and David my servant shall be chieftain to them forever"** — נָשִׂיא "chieftain" (its row, "one lifted up"; KJV "prince"; as 7:27).
- **"Moreover I will make a covenant of peace with them" → "And I will cut for them a covenant of shalom"** (v26) — כָּרַת "cut" (the בְּרִית row); שָׁלוֹם with its token. **"it shall be an everlasting covenant with them"** — בְּרִית עוֹלָם, "a covenant of the age" (the עוֹלָם genitive row: "everlasting"). **"and I will place them, and multiply them" → "And I will set them, and multiply them"** — וּנְתַתִּים, literally "and I will give them"; "in their land" (the earlier wording) is not in the Hebrew; the word is short and hard (perhaps "establish them"). **"in the midst of them for evermore" → "in their midst forever"** — לְעוֹלָם (also v28); מִקְדָּשׁ "sanctuary".
- **"My tabernacle also shall be with them" → "And my dwelling shall be over them"** (v27) — מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling" (its row); עֲלֵיהֶם "over / upon them".
- **"And the heathen shall know that I the LORD do sanctify Israel" → "And the nations shall know that I am Yahweh who sanctifies Israel"** (v28) — וְיָדְעוּ הַגּוֹיִם כִּי אֲנִי יְהוָה מְקַדֵּשׁ: the knowledge formula, with the participle after it ("who sanctifies"; also read "that I, Yahweh, sanctify Israel", KJV). **"when my sanctuary shall be in the midst of them" → "when my sanctuary is in their midst"** — בִּהְיוֹת.

### Notes
- **v1–14** — the vision of the bones in the plain; v11 says what it means: the bones are the house of Israel in exile, who say their hope is gone; the opening of the graves (v12–13) is spoken to them as "my people".
- **v15–28** — the sign of the two sticks: Judah, and Joseph / Ephraim (the northern tribes), made one in the prophet's hand and in Yahweh's; one nation, one king, "my servant David" (v24, 25; as 34:23–24).
- **v27** — echoed in Rev 21:3 (and in 2 Cor 6:16, with Lev 26:12).
