# Ezekiel 25

[1] The word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me:

[2] "Son of man, set your face toward the ⟦ammonites|Ammonim|Ammonites⟧ and prophesy against them.

[3] Say to the ⟦ammonites|Ammonim|Ammonites⟧, Hear the word of the ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧: Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, Because you said, 'Aha!' over my sanctuary when it was profaned, and over the land of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ when it was made desolate, and over the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ when they went into exile,

[4] therefore behold, I am handing you over to the people of the East for a possession, and they shall set their encampments among you and make their dwellings in your midst. They shall eat your fruit, and they shall drink your milk.

[5] I will make Rabbah a pasture for camels and Ammon a fold for flocks. Then you will know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] "For thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Because you have clapped your hands and stamped your feet and rejoiced with all the malice within your soul against the land of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[8] "Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Because ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ and Seir said, 'Behold, the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ is like all the other nations,'

[9] therefore I will lay open the flank of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ from the cities, from its cities on its frontier, the glory of the country, ⟦beth-jeshimoth|Beit HaYeshimot|Beth-jeshimoth⟧, ⟦baal-meon|Baal Meon|Baal-meon⟧, and ⟦kiriathaim|Qiryatayim|Kiriathaim⟧.

[10] I will give it along with the ⟦ammonites|Ammonim|Ammonites⟧ to the people of the East as a possession, that the ⟦ammonites|Ammonim|Ammonites⟧ may be remembered no more among the nations,

[11] and I will execute judgments upon ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧. Then they will know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] "Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Because Edom acted revengefully against the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and has grievously offended in taking vengeance on them,

[13] therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, I will stretch out my hand against Edom and cut off from it man and beast. And I will make it desolate; from Teman even to Dedan they shall fall by the sword.

[14] And I will lay my vengeance upon Edom by the hand of my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they shall do in Edom according to my anger and according to my wrath, and they shall know my vengeance, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[15] "Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Because the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ acted revengefully and took vengeance with malice of soul to destroy in unending enmity,

[16] therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, Behold, I will stretch out my hand against the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, and I will cut off the ⟦cherethites|Keretim|Cherethites⟧ and destroy the rest of the seacoast.

[17] I will execute great vengeance on them with wrathful rebukes. Then they will know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, when I lay my vengeance upon them."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*; Ezekiel's distinctive doubled divine title.
- **"Seir" retained** (v8) — Hebrew שֵׂעִיר *Se'ir*; "Moab and *Seir* said" — Mount Seir is Edom's territory; *Seir* retained as proper name.
- **"Beth-jeshimoth, Baal-meon, and Kiriathaim" retained** (v9) — Hebrew place names retained; these are towns on the Moabite frontier.
