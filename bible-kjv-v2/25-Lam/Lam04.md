# Lamentations 4

[1] **א** How the gold grows dim,
the good gold changes!
The holy stones are poured out
at the head of every street.

[2] **ב** The sons of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, the precious ones,
weighed against fine gold,
how they are reckoned as jars of earthenware,
the work of the hands of a potter!

[3] **ג** Even jackals draw out the breast,
they suckle their whelps;
the daughter of my people has become cruel,
like the ostriches in the wilderness.

[4] **ד** The tongue of the suckling clings
to his palate in thirst;
children have asked for bread;
there is none to break it for them.

[5] **ה** Those who ate delicacies
are desolate in the streets;
those brought up on scarlet
have embraced ash heaps.

[6] **ו** And the iniquity of the daughter of my people is greater
than the sin of ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧,
which was overthrown as in a moment,
and no hands whirled upon her.

[7] **ז** Her ⟦nazirite|nazirs|Nazirites⟧ were purer than snow,
they were whiter than milk;
they were redder of body than rubies,
sapphire was their cutting.

[8] **ח** Darker than blackness is their form;
they are not recognized in the streets;
their skin has shriveled on their bones;
it has become dry like wood.

[9] **ט** Better off were the slain of the sword
than the slain of famine,
who flow away, pierced,
away from the produce of the field.

[10] **י** The hands of compassionate women
have boiled their children;
they have become food for them
in the breaking of the daughter of my people.

[11] **כ** ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has finished his fury,
he has poured out the burning of his anger;
and he has kindled a fire in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
and it has devoured her foundations.

[12] **ל** The kings of the earth did not believe,
all who dwell in the world,
that adversary and enemy would come
into the gates of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[13] **מ** Because of the sins of her prophets,
the iniquities of her priests,
who shed in her midst
the blood of the righteous,

[14] **נ** they have wandered blind in the streets;
they are defiled with blood,
so that none are able
to touch their garments.

[15] **ס** Turn aside! Unclean! they called to them;
Turn aside, turn aside, do not touch!
For they have fled, they have also wandered;
they said among the nations, They shall not again sojourn.

[16] **פ** The face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has divided them;
he will not again look at them;
the faces of priests they did not lift up,
to elders they did not show favor.

[17] **ע** While we still are, our eyes are spent
for our help, in vain;
in our watching we have watched
for a nation that does not save.

[18] **צ** They have hunted our steps
from walking in our squares;
our end has come near, our days are full,
for our end has come.

[19] **ק** Swifter were our pursuers
than the eagles of the heavens;
on the mountains they hotly pursued us,
in the wilderness they lay in ambush for us.

[20] **ר** The breath of our nostrils, the ⟦anointed|mashiach|anointed⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
was captured in their pits,
of whom we said, In his shade
we shall live among the nations.

[21] **ש** Exult and be glad, daughter of Edom,
who dwells in the land of ⟦uz|Utz|Uz⟧;
also to you the cup shall pass over;
you shall be drunk and lay yourself bare.

[22] **ת** Your iniquity has come to an end, daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧;
he will not again carry you into exile;
he has visited your iniquity, daughter of Edom;
he has uncovered your sins.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v11, 16, 20) — Hebrew יהוה; the chapter has no אֲדֹנָי. No vocative "O" (v21, 22).
- **"How is the gold become dim! how is the most fine gold changed! the stones of the sanctuary are poured out in the top of every street" → "How the gold grows dim, the good gold changes! The holy stones are poured out at the head of every street"** (v1) — Hebrew אֵיכָה "How" (as 1:1; 2:1), once; יוּעַם, only here, "grows dim" (uncertain); יִשְׁנֶא "changes"; הַכֶּתֶם הַטּוֹב "the good gold" (כֶּתֶם, a second word for gold beside זָהָב, its row); אַבְנֵי־קֹדֶשׁ "holy stones" (lit. "stones of holiness", the קֹדֶשׁ row; "of the sanctuary" is a reading); בְּרֹאשׁ כָּל־חוּצוֹת "at the head of every street" (as 2:19).
- **"The precious sons of Zion, comparable to fine gold, how are they esteemed as earthen pitchers" → "The sons of Zion, the precious ones, weighed against fine gold, how they are reckoned as jars of earthenware"** (v2) — Hebrew הַיְקָרִים "the precious ones" after the noun; הַמְסֻלָּאִים, only here, "weighed against"; פָּז "fine gold" (its row); אֵיכָה a second time (in the middle of the verse); נֶחְשְׁבוּ "are reckoned" (חָשַׁב); נִבְלֵי־חֶרֶשׂ "jars of earthenware" (נֵבֶל "jar", חֶרֶשׂ "earthenware", their rows); יוֹצֵר "potter".
- **"Even the sea monsters draw out the breast, they give suck to their young ones: the daughter of my people is become cruel, like the ostriches in the wilderness" → "Even jackals draw out the breast, they suckle their whelps; the daughter of my people has become cruel, like the ostriches in the wilderness"** (v3) — ketiv תנין "sea monster", qere תַּנִּים "jackals" (the qere in the verse; the תַּנִּין row is for the written word); חָלְצוּ שַׁד "draw out the breast"; הֵינִיקוּ "suckle" (its row); גּוּרִים "whelps"; לְאַכְזָר "to cruelty" (= has become cruel; "has become" supplied); ketiv כי ענים, qere כַּיְעֵנִים "like the ostriches" (the qere in the verse).
- **"The tongue of the sucking child cleaveth to the roof of his mouth for thirst: the young children ask bread, and no man breaketh it unto them" → "The tongue of the suckling clings to his palate in thirst; children have asked for bread; there is none to break it for them"** (v4) — Hebrew דָּבַק "cling" (its row); יוֹנֵק "suckling"; חֵךְ "palate" (its row); צָמָא "thirst"; עוֹלָלִים "children"; שָׁאֲלוּ, a perfect, "have asked"; פֹּרֵשׂ אֵין לָהֶם "a breaker there is none for them" ("man" is not written).
- **"They that did feed delicately are desolate in the streets: they that were brought up in scarlet embrace dunghills" → "Those who ate delicacies are desolate in the streets; those brought up on scarlet have embraced ash heaps"** (v5) — Hebrew הָאֹכְלִים לְמַעֲדַנִּים "those who ate delicacies"; נָשַׁמּוּ "are desolate" (שָׁמֵם; of persons also "appalled", its row); הָאֱמֻנִים "those brought up" (אָמַן, as the foster-father row); עֲלֵי תוֹלָע "on scarlet"; חִבְּקוּ "have embraced"; אַשְׁפַּתּוֹת "ash heaps" (as 1 Sam 2:8; "dunghills" in other versions).
- **"For the punishment of the iniquity of the daughter of my people is greater than the punishment of the sin of Sodom, that was overthrown as in a moment, and no hands stayed on her" → "And the iniquity of the daughter of my people is greater than the sin of Sodom, which was overthrown as in a moment, and no hands whirled upon her"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיִּגְדַּל "and … is great(er)", with "and"; עָוֹן "iniquity" and חַטָּאת "sin": both words can also mean what the wrong brings ("punishment": in Changes only, the earlier text had it in the verse); הֲפוּכָה "overthrown" (הָפַךְ, its row); כְמוֹ־רָגַע "as a moment"; לֹא־חָלוּ בָהּ יָדַיִם "hands did not whirl upon her" (חוּל "whirl, writhe": no human hand worked her end; uncertain).
- **"Her Nazarites were purer than snow, they were whiter than milk, they were more ruddy in body than rubies, their polishing was of sapphire" → "Her nazirs were purer than snow, they were whiter than milk; they were redder of body than rubies, sapphire was their cutting"** (v7) — Hebrew נְזִירֶיהָ "her nazirs" (token kept from Num 6; "her princes", the sense of Gen 49:26, is the other reading: uncertain); זַכּוּ "were pure" (זַךְ); צַחוּ "were dazzling, white"; אָדְמוּ "were red"; עֶצֶם "body" (lit. "bone"); פְּנִינִים "rubies" (its row: uncertain, "corals", "pearls" in other versions); סַפִּיר "sapphire" (probably lapis lazuli, its row); גִּזְרָתָם "their cutting" (form, shape: uncertain).
- **"Their visage is blacker than a coal; they are not known in the streets: their skin cleaveth to their bones; it is withered, it is become like a stick" → "Darker than blackness is their form; they are not recognized in the streets; their skin has shriveled on their bones; it has become dry like wood"** (v8) — Hebrew חָשַׁךְ "is dark"; מִשְּׁחוֹר "than blackness" (only here; "soot" is a guess); תֹּאַר "form"; נִכְּרוּ "recognized"; צָפַד "shriveled" (only here); עַצְמָם "their bone" (singular); יָבֵשׁ הָיָה כָעֵץ "it has become dry like wood".
- **"They that be slain with the sword are better than they that be slain with hunger: for these pine away, stricken through for want of the fruits of the field" → "Better off were the slain of the sword than the slain of famine, who flow away, pierced, away from the produce of the field"** (v9) — Hebrew טוֹבִים הָיוּ "good were" (= better off); חַלְלֵי "the slain of" (חָלָל); רָעָב "famine" (its row); יָזוּבוּ "flow away" (זוּב "flow", its row); מְדֻקָּרִים "pierced"; מִתְּנוּבֹת שָׂדָי "from the produce of the field" ("for want of" is the sense: "away from" keeps the מִן; שָׂדַי poetic form of שָׂדֶה).
- **"The hands of the pitiful women have sodden their own children: they were their meat in the destruction of the daughter of my people" → "The hands of compassionate women have boiled their children; they have become food for them in the breaking of the daughter of my people"** (v10) — Hebrew רַחֲמָנִיּוֹת "compassionate" (only here; root of רַחֲמִים); בִּשְּׁלוּ "boiled" (its row); יְלָדִים "children" ("their own" is not written); לְבָרוֹת "for food" (only here); שֶׁבֶר "breaking" (as 2:11).
- **"The LORD hath accomplished his fury; he hath poured out his fierce anger, and hath kindled a fire in Zion, and it hath devoured the foundations thereof" → "Yahweh has finished his fury, he has poured out the burning of his anger; and he has kindled a fire in Zion, and it has devoured her foundations"** (v11) — Hebrew כִּלָּה "finish" (its row); חֵמָה "fury"; חֲרוֹן אַפּוֹ "the burning of his anger" (as 1:12); הִצִּית "kindle"; יְסוֹדוֹת "foundations".
- **"The kings of the earth, and all the inhabitants of the world, would not have believed that the adversary and the enemy should have entered into the gates of Jerusalem" → "The kings of the earth did not believe, all who dwell in the world, that adversary and enemy would come into the gates of Jerusalem"** (v12) — Hebrew לֹא הֶאֱמִינוּ "did not believe" (הֶאֱמִין, its row); ketiv וכל "and all", qere כֹּל "all" (the qere in the verse, without "and"); יֹשְׁבֵי תֵבֵל "those who dwell in the world" (יָשַׁב "dwell"; תֵּבֵל "world"); צַר וְאוֹיֵב "adversary and enemy" (no articles); יָבֹא "would come".
- **"For the sins of her prophets, and the iniquities of her priests, that have shed the blood of the just in the midst of her" → "Because of the sins of her prophets, the iniquities of her priests, who shed in her midst the blood of the righteous"** (v13) — Hebrew מֵחַטֹּאת "because of the sins" (מִן); no "and"; the Hebrew order kept ("in her midst" before "the blood"); צַדִּיקִים "righteous" (its row). The sentence runs on into v14 (the comma at its end).
- **"They have wandered as blind men in the streets, they have polluted themselves with blood, so that men could not touch their garments" → "they have wandered blind in the streets; they are defiled with blood, so that none are able to touch their garments"** (v14) — Hebrew נָעוּ "wandered" (נוּעַ, also v15); עִוְרִים "blind" ("as" and "men" are not written); נְגֹאֲלוּ "are defiled" (גָּאַל II, a passive); בְּלֹא יוּכְלוּ יִגְּעוּ, literally "with (them) not able, they touch": "none" supplied for the unnamed subject; or "what they may not touch, they touch with their garments" (uncertain); לְבוּשִׁים "garments".
- **"They cried unto them, Depart ye; it is unclean; depart, depart, touch not: when they fled away and wandered, they said among the heathen, They shall no more sojourn there" → "Turn aside! Unclean! they called to them; Turn aside, turn aside, do not touch! For they have fled, they have also wandered; they said among the nations, They shall not again sojourn"** (v15) — Hebrew order (the cry first); סוּרוּ "turn aside" (סוּר, its row) three times; טָמֵא "unclean" (no "it is"; the cry of the leper, Lev 13:45); נָצוּ, only here, "fled" (uncertain); גַּם־נָעוּ "they have also wandered" (v14); גּוֹיִם "nations"; לֹא יוֹסִיפוּ לָגוּר "they shall not again sojourn" (יָסַף + infinitive, its row: lit. "add to sojourn"; גּוּר "sojourn"; "there" is not written).
- **"The anger of the LORD hath divided them; he will no more regard them: they respected not the persons of the priests, they favoured not the elders" → "The face of Yahweh has divided them; he will not again look at them; the faces of priests they did not lift up, to elders they did not show favor"** (v16) — Hebrew פְּנֵי יְהוָה "the face of Yahweh" (not "anger"); חִלְּקָם "divided them" (scattered); לֹא יוֹסִיף לְהַבִּיטָם "he will not again look at them" (הִבִּיט); פְּנֵי … לֹא נָשָׂאוּ "did not lift up the faces" (= showed no regard, the נָשָׂא פָּנִים row); ketiv זקנים "elders", qere וּזְקֵנִים "and elders" (the qere has "and"; English keeps the two lines apart: the "and" is named here); חָנַן "show favor" (its row). The letter פ comes before ע here (v16–17), as in ch. 2 and 3.
- **"As for us, our eyes as yet failed for our vain help: in our watching we have watched for a nation that could not save us" → "While we still are, our eyes are spent for our help, in vain; in our watching we have watched for a nation that does not save"** (v17) — ketiv עודינה "still they (our eyes)", qere עוֹדֵינוּ "still we" (the qere in the verse: "while we still are"); תִּכְלֶינָה "are spent" (כָּלָה, as 2:11); עֶזְרָה "help"; הֶבֶל "in vain" (the word is "vapor", its row: adverbial here); צִפִּיָּה, only here, "watching" (or "watchtower"); צִפִּינוּ "we have watched"; גּוֹי לֹא יוֹשִׁעַ "a nation that does not save" ("us" is not written).
- **"They hunt our steps, that we cannot go in our streets: our end is near, our days are fulfilled; for our end is come" → "They have hunted our steps from walking in our squares; our end has come near, our days are full, for our end has come"** (v18) — Hebrew צָדוּ "hunted" (צוּד, as 3:52); צְעָדִים "steps"; מִלֶּכֶת "from walking"; רְחֹבוֹת "squares" (its row); קֵץ "end" (twice, kept); מָלְאוּ "are full".
- **"Our persecutors are swifter than the eagles of the heaven: they pursued us upon the mountains, they laid wait for us in the wilderness" → "Swifter were our pursuers than the eagles of the heavens; on the mountains they hotly pursued us, in the wilderness they lay in ambush for us"** (v19) — Hebrew קַלִּים הָיוּ "swift were"; רֹדְפִים "pursuers"; נֶשֶׁר "eagle" (its row); שָׁמַיִם "heavens"; דְּלָקֻנוּ "they hotly pursued us" (דָּלַק, lit. "burn"); אָרַב "lie in ambush for" (its row); מִדְבָּר "wilderness".
- **"The breath of our nostrils, the anointed of the LORD, was taken in their pits, of whom we said, Under his shadow we shall live among the heathen" → "The breath of our nostrils, the mashiach of Yahweh, was captured in their pits, of whom we said, In his shade we shall live among the nations"** (v20) — Hebrew רוּחַ אַפֵּינוּ "the breath of our nostrils" (אַף of the body); מְשִׁיחַ יְהוָה "the anointed of Yahweh" (token kept; the king is meant, no title); נִלְכַּד "was captured" (לָכַד, its row); שְׁחִיתוֹת "pits" (the שְׁחִית row: uncertain); בְּצִלּוֹ "in his shade" (צֵל, its row); נִחְיֶה "we shall live"; גּוֹיִם "nations"; no quotation marks (the Hebrew marks no speech).
- **"Rejoice and be glad, O daughter of Edom, that dwellest in the land of Uz; the cup also shall pass through unto thee: thou shalt be drunken, and shalt make thyself naked" → "Exult and be glad, daughter of Edom, who dwells in the land of Uz; also to you the cup shall pass over; you shall be drunk and lay yourself bare"** (v21) — Hebrew שִׂישִׂי "exult" (שׂוּשׂ, its row); שִׂמְחִי "be glad"; ketiv יושבתי, qere יוֹשֶׁבֶת "who dwells" (the same sense; the ketiv keeps an old ending); עָבַר "pass over" (its row); כּוֹס "cup"; שָׁכַר "be drunk" (its row); הִתְעָרָה "lay oneself bare".
- **"The punishment of thine iniquity is accomplished, O daughter of Zion; he will no more carry thee away into captivity: he will visit thine iniquity, O daughter of Edom; he will discover thy sins" → "Your iniquity has come to an end, daughter of Zion; he will not again carry you into exile; he has visited your iniquity, daughter of Edom; he has uncovered your sins"** (v22) — Hebrew תַּם עֲוֹנֵךְ "your iniquity has come to an end" (תַּם, its row; עָוֹן, the iniquity and what it brings: "the punishment of" in Changes only); הִגְלָה "carry into exile"; פָּקַד "visited" (its row), a perfect (the earlier text had a future; a prophetic perfect is possible: said here); גִּלָּה עַל "uncovered" (as 2:14).

### Notes
- **Acrostic** — 22 verses, each opening with the next letter of the alphabet; here פ stands before ע (v16 פ, v17 ע), as in ch. 2 and 3. The letters are printed in bold; the translation does not imitate the scheme.
- **Verse form** — each verse has two lines of two halves; the printed lines follow the halves.
- **v20** — "the mashiach of Yahweh": the king of Judah (traditionally Zedekiah, 2 Kgs 25:4–7), captured in flight.
- **v21–22** — the turn: the iniquity of Zion has come to an end; that of Edom is visited.
- The numbering is the same in the Hebrew.
