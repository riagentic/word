# Lamentations 4

[1] **א** How
the gold has grown dim,
how the pure gold is changed!
The holy stones lie scattered
at the head of every street.

[2] **ב** The precious sons of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
worth their weight in fine gold,
how they are regarded as earthen pots,
the work of a potter's hands!

[3] **ג** Even jackals offer the breast;
they nurse their young;
but the daughter of my people has become cruel,
like the ostriches in the wilderness.

[4] **ד** The tongue of the nursing infant sticks
to the roof of its mouth for thirst;
the children beg for food,
but no one gives to them.

[5] **ה** Those who once feasted on delicacies
perish in the streets;
those who were brought up in purple
embrace ash heaps.

[6] **ו** For the chastisement of the daughter of my people
has been greater than the punishment of ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧,
which was overthrown in a moment,
with no hand laid on it.

[7] **ז** Her consecrated ones were purer than snow,
whiter than milk;
their bodies were more ruddy than coral,
the beauty of their form was like sapphire.

[8] **ח** Now their face is blacker than soot;
they are not recognized in the streets;
their skin has shriveled on their bones;
it has become as dry as wood.

[9] **ט** Happier were the victims of the sword
than the victims of hunger,
who wasted away, pierced
by lack of the fruits of the field.

[10] **י** The hands of compassionate women
have boiled their own children;
they became their food
during the destruction of the daughter of my people.

[11] **כ** ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ gave full vent to his wrath;
he poured out his hot anger,
and he kindled a fire in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧
that consumed its foundations.

[12] **ל** The kings of the earth did not believe,
nor any of the inhabitants of the world,
that foe or enemy could enter
the gates of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[13] **מ** This was for the sins of her prophets
and the iniquities of her priests,
who shed in the midst of her
the blood of the righteous.

[14] **נ** They wandered, blind, through the streets;
they were so defiled with blood
that no one was able to touch
their garments.

[15] **ס** "Away! Unclean!" people cried at them.
"Away! Away! Do not touch!"
So they became fugitives and wanderers;
people said among the nations,
"They shall stay with us no longer."

[16] **פ** ⟦yhwh|Yahweh's|The LORD's⟧ face has scattered them;
he will regard them no more;
no honor was shown to the priests,
no favor to the elders.

[17] **ע** Our eyes failed, ever watching
vainly for help;
in our watching we watched
for a nation which could not save.

[18] **צ** They dogged our steps
so that we could not walk in our streets;
our end was near; our days were numbered,
for our end had come.

[19] **ק** Our pursuers were swifter
than the eagles in the heavens;
they chased us on the mountains;
they lay in wait for us in the wilderness.

[20] **ר** The breath of our nostrils, the ⟦anointed|mashiach|anointed⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
was captured in their pits,
of whom we said, "Under his shadow
we shall live among the nations."

[21] **ש** Rejoice and be glad, O daughter of Edom,
you who dwell in the land of ⟦uz|Utz|Uz⟧;
but to you also the cup shall pass;
you shall become drunk and strip yourself bare.

[22] **ת** The punishment of your iniquity, O daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, is accomplished;
he will keep you in exile no longer;
but your iniquity, O daughter of Edom, he will punish;
he will uncover your sins.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v11, 16, 20) — Hebrew יהוה *YHWH*.
- **"LORD" / "*Adonai*"** — no *Adonai* in this chapter; only *YHWH*.
- **"How"** (v1) — Hebrew אֵיכָה *Eikhah*; the third *Eikhah*-opening in Lamentations (1:1, 2:1, 4:1).
- **Acrostic** — chapters 4 uses the non-standard order (פ before ע, vv16-17); marked in the text to preserve the structure visually.
- **"Uz" — kept** (v21) — Hebrew עוּץ *Utz*; the land of Job's dwelling; retained as a proper name.
- **Mourning vocabulary** throughout: "how…dim," "black as soot," "wasted away," "boiled their children" — extreme dirge language translated without softening.
- **"consecrated ones"** (v7) — Hebrew נְזִירֶיהָ *nezireha*; Nazirites or consecrated princes; their once-pure appearance contrasted with their current state.

### Notes
- **v20** — "*mashiach* of Yahweh" = the anointed king (Zedekiah), captured in flight.
- **v21-22** — shift to hope: Zion's punishment finished, Edom's beginning.
