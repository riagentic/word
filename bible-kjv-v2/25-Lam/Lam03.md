# Lamentations 3

[1] **א** I am the man who has seen affliction
under the rod of his wrath;

[2] **א** he has driven and brought me
into darkness without any light;

[3] **א** surely against me he turns his hand
again and again the whole day long.

[4] **ב** He has made my flesh and my skin waste away;
he has broken my bones;

[5] **ב** he has besieged and enveloped me
with bitterness and tribulation;

[6] **ב** he has made me dwell in darkness
like the dead of long ago.

[7] **ג** He has walled me about so that I cannot escape;
he has made my chains heavy;

[8] **ג** though I call and cry for help,
he shuts out my prayer;

[9] **ג** he has blocked my ways with dressed stones;
he has made my paths crooked.

[10] **ד** He is a bear lying in wait for me,
a lion in hiding;

[11] **ד** he turned aside my steps and tore me to pieces;
he has made me desolate;

[12] **ד** he bent his bow and set me
as a target for his arrow.

[13] **ה** He drove into my inmost being
the arrows of his quiver;

[14] **ה** I have become the laughingstock of all peoples,
the object of their taunts all day long.

[15] **ה** He has filled me with bitterness;
he has sated me with wormwood.

[16] **ו** He has made my teeth grind on gravel,
and made me cower in ashes;

[17] **ו** my soul is bereft of peace;
I have forgotten what happiness is;

[18] **ו** so I say, "My endurance has perished;
so has my hope from Yahweh."

[19] **ז** Remember my affliction and my wanderings,
the wormwood and the gall!

[20] **ז** My soul continually remembers it
and is bowed down within me.

[21] **ז** But this I call to mind,
and therefore I have hope:

[22] **ח** The steadfast love of Yahweh — surely we are not consumed,
for his mercies never come to an end;

[23] **ח** they are new every morning;
great is your faithfulness.

[24] **ח** "Yahweh is my portion," says my soul,
"therefore I will hope in him."

[25] **ט** Yahweh is good to those who wait for him,
to the soul who seeks him.

[26] **ט** It is good that one should wait quietly
for the salvation of Yahweh.

[27] **ט** It is good for a man that he bear
the yoke in his youth.

[28] **י** Let him sit alone in silence
when it is laid on him;

[29] **י** let him put his mouth in the dust —
there may yet be hope;

[30] **י** let him give his cheek to the one who strikes,
and let him be filled with insults.

[31] **כ** For Lord will not
cast off forever,

[32] **כ** but, though he cause grief, he will have compassion
according to the abundance of his steadfast love;

[33] **כ** for he does not afflict from his heart
or grieve the children of men.

[34] **ל** To crush underfoot
all the prisoners of the earth,

[35] **ל** to deny a man justice
in the presence of the Most High,

[36] **ל** to subvert a man in his lawsuit,
Lord does not approve.

[37] **מ** Who has spoken and it came to pass,
unless Lord has commanded it?

[38] **מ** Is it not from the mouth of the Most High
that good and bad come?

[39] **מ** Why should a living man complain,
a man, about the punishment of his sins?

[40] **נ** Let us test and examine our ways,
and return to Yahweh!

[41] **נ** Let us lift up our hearts and hands
to El in heaven:

[42] **נ** "We have transgressed and rebelled,
and you have not forgiven.

[43] **ס** You have wrapped yourself with anger and pursued us,
killing without pity;

[44] **ס** you have wrapped yourself with a cloud
so that no prayer can pass through.

[45] **ס** You have made us scum and garbage
among the peoples."

[46] **פ** All our enemies
open their mouths against us;

[47] **פ** panic and pitfall have come upon us,
devastation and destruction;

[48] **פ** my eyes flow with streams of tears
because of the destruction of the daughter of my people.

[49] **ע** My eyes will flow without ceasing,
without respite,

[50] **ע** until Yahweh from heaven
looks down and sees;

[51] **ע** my eyes cause me grief
at the fate of all the daughters of my city.

[52] **צ** I have been hunted like a bird
by those who were my enemies without cause;

[53] **צ** they flung me alive into the pit
and cast stones on me;

[54] **צ** water closed over my head;
I said, "I am lost."

[55] **ק** I called on your name, O Yahweh,
from the depths of the pit;

[56] **ק** you heard my plea, "Do not close
your ear to my cry for help!"

[57] **ק** You came near when I called on you;
you said, "Do not fear!"

[58] **ר** You have taken up my cause, O Lord;
you have redeemed my life.

[59] **ר** You have seen the wrong done to me, O Yahweh;
judge my cause.

[60] **ר** You have seen all their vengeance,
all their plots against me.

[61] **ש** You have heard their taunts, O Yahweh,
all their plots against me.

[62] **ש** The lips and thoughts of my assailants
are against me all the day long.

[63] **ש** Behold their sitting and their rising;
I am the object of their taunts.

[64] **ת** You will repay them, O Yahweh,
according to the work of their hands.

[65] **ת** You will give them hardness of heart;
your curse will be on them.

[66] **ת** You will pursue them in anger and destroy them
from under the heavens of Yahweh.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v18, 22, 24, 25, 26, 40, 50, 55, 59, 66) — Hebrew יהוה *YHWH*.
- **"LORD" / "Lord" / "God" → "Yahweh" / "Lord" / "El"** — chapter 3 uses three divine names: יהוה *YHWH* (→ Yahweh), אֲדֹנָי *Adonai* (v31, 36, 37, 58 → Lord), and אֵל *El* (v41 → El); all three preserved distinctly.
- **Acrostic** — chapter 3 is a triple acrostic: each Hebrew letter governs three verses (vv1–3 = א, vv4–6 = ב, etc., through vv64–66 = ת); 66 verses total. This is the most elaborate acrostic in the book and marks Lam 3 as the structural and theological center.
- **"It is of the LORD'S mercies that we are not consumed" → "The steadfast love of Yahweh — surely we are not consumed"** (v22) — Hebrew חַסְדֵי יהוה כִּי לֹא־תָמְנוּ; *chasdei* is the plural of *chesed* ("acts of covenantal loyalty"); the theological climax of the book. MT תָמְנוּ "we are (not) consumed" kept; the Syriac and Targum read תַּמּוּ "they have (not) ceased" (so "never ceases" in some versions).
- **"mercies"** (v22, 23) — Hebrew רַחֲמִים *rachamim*; womb-compassions, deep tender mercy; kept as "mercies" for familiarity.
- **"*Eikhah*"** — not the opening word of this chapter (uses a different form); the dirge continues.

### Notes
- **vv22-23** — "new every morning, great is your faithfulness" — basis for the hymn; central message of hope at the midpoint of the book.
