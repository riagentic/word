# Lamentations 2

[1] **א** How
⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ in his anger
has set the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ under a cloud!
He has cast down from heaven to earth
the splendor of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
he has not remembered his footstool
in the day of his anger.

[2] **ב** ⟦adonai|Adonai|The Lord⟧ has swallowed up without pity
all the habitations of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧;
in his wrath he has broken down
the strongholds of the daughter of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧;
he has brought down to the ground in dishonor
the kingdom and its rulers.

[3] **ג** He has cut down in fierce anger
all the horn of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
he has withdrawn his right hand from the foe;
he has burned like a flaming fire in ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
consuming all around.

[4] **ד** He has bent his bow like an enemy,
with his right hand set like a foe;
and he has killed all who were delightful in our eyes
in the tent of the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧;
he has poured out his fury like fire.

[5] **ה** ⟦adonai|Adonai|The Lord⟧ has become like an enemy;
he has swallowed up ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
he has swallowed up all its palaces;
he has laid in ruins its strongholds,
and he has multiplied in the daughter of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧
mourning and lamentation.

[6] **ו** He has stripped his booth like a garden;
he has destroyed his meeting place;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has made ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ forget
festival and ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧,
and in his fierce indignation
has spurned king and priest.

[7] **ז** ⟦adonai|Adonai|The Lord⟧ has scorned his altar,
disowned his sanctuary;
he has delivered into the hand of the enemy
the walls of her palaces;
they raised a clamor in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
as on the day of festival.

[8] **ח** ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ determined to lay in ruins
the wall of the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧;
he stretched out the measuring line;
he did not restrain his hand from destroying;
he caused rampart and wall to lament;
they languished together.

[9] **ט** Her gates have sunk into the ground;
he has ruined and broken her bars;
her king and princes are among the nations;
the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ is no more,
and her prophets find
no vision from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[10] **י** The elders of the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧
sit on the ground in silence;
they have thrown dust on their heads
and put on sackcloth;
the young women of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧
have bowed their heads to the ground.

[11] **כ** My eyes are spent with weeping;
my inmost parts are in tumult;
my liver is poured out to the ground
because of the destruction of the daughter of my people,
because infants and babies faint
in the streets of the city.

[12] **ל** They cry to their mothers,
"Where is bread and wine?"
as they faint like a wounded man
in the streets of the city,
as their soul is poured out
on their mothers' bosom.

[13] **מ** What can I say for you, to what compare you,
O daughter of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧?
What can I liken to you, that I may comfort you,
O virgin daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧?
For your ruin is vast as the sea;
who can heal you?

[14] **נ** Your prophets have seen for you
false and deceptive visions;
they have not exposed your iniquity
to restore your fortunes,
but have seen for you oracles
that are false and misleading.

[15] **ס** All who pass along the way
clap their hands at you;
they hiss and wag their heads
at the daughter of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧:
"Is this the city that was called
the perfection of beauty,
the joy of all the earth?"

[16] **פ** All your enemies
rail against you;
they hiss, they gnash their teeth,
they cry: "We have swallowed her!
Ah, this is the day we longed for;
now we have found it, now we have seen it!"

[17] **ע** ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has done what he purposed;
he has carried out his word,
which he commanded long ago;
he has thrown down without pity;
he has made the enemy rejoice over you
and exalted the horn of your foes.

[18] **צ** Their heart cried to ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧.
O wall of the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
let tears stream down like a torrent
day and night!
Give yourself no rest,
your eyes no respite!

[19] **ק** Arise, cry out in the night,
at the beginning of the night watches!
Pour out your heart like water
before the presence of ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧!
Lift your hands to him
for the lives of your children,
who faint for hunger
at the head of every street.

[20] **ר** Look, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, and see!
With whom have you dealt thus?
Should women eat the fruit of their womb,
the children of their care?
Should priest and prophet be killed
in the sanctuary of ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧?

[21] **ש** In the dust of the streets
lie the young and the old;
my young women and my young men
have fallen by the sword;
you have killed them in the day of your anger,
slaughtering without pity.

[22] **ת** You summoned as if to a feast day
my terrors on every side,
and on the day of the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
no one escaped or survived;
those whom I held and raised
my enemy destroyed.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v6, 7, 8, 9, 17, 18, 20, 22) — Hebrew יהוה *YHWH*.
- **"LORD" / "Lord"** — the poem's alternation between אֲדֹנָי *Adonai* (v1, 2, 5, 7, 18, 19, 20) and יהוה *YHWH* (v6, 8, 9, 17, 22) is preserved; *Adonai* rendered as "Lord," *YHWH* as "Yahweh."
- **"How"** (v1) — Hebrew אֵיכָה *Eikhah*; the dirge-opener recurring from Lam 1:1.
- **Acrostic** — Hebrew letters א–ת marked before each verse; chapters 2 and 4 use the non-standard order פ before ע (vv16-17), preserved in the translation's marking sequence.
- **"the law" → "the Law"** (v9) — Hebrew תּוֹרָה *torah*; "the Law is no more" — the divine instruction/law no longer received.
- **"liver"** (v11) — Hebrew כְּבֵד *kaved*; in Hebrew physiology the liver was the seat of intense emotion (like the heart in English); translated literally.
- **"lamentation"** (v5) — Hebrew תַּאֲנִיָּה *ta'aniyah*; mourning-wail; dirge vocabulary.

### Notes
- **Chapters 2 and 4** — the letters ע and פ are reversed (פ before ע), an ancient ordering also found in some early Hebrew inscriptions.
