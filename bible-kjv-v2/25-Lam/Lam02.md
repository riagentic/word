# Lamentations 2

[1] **א** How ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ clouds over in his anger
the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧!
He has thrown from the heavens to the earth
the beauty of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
and has not remembered the footstool of his feet
in the day of his anger.

[2] **ב** ⟦adonai|Adonai|The Lord⟧ has swallowed up, and has not spared,
all the habitations of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧;
he has thrown down in his raging
the fortresses of the daughter of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧;
he has made them touch the ground;
he has profaned the kingdom and its princes.

[3] **ג** He has hewn down in the burning of anger
every horn of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
he has turned back his right hand
from the face of an enemy;
and he has burned in ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ like a fire of flame;
it has devoured all around.

[4] **ד** He has trodden his bow like an enemy,
his right hand stands like an adversary,
and he has killed
all that was desirable to the eye;
in the tent of the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧
he has poured out his fury like fire.

[5] **ה** ⟦adonai|Adonai|The Lord⟧ has become like an enemy;
he has swallowed up ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
he has swallowed up all her citadels,
he has ruined his fortresses;
and he has multiplied in the daughter of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧
mourning and moaning.

[6] **ו** And he has done violence to his booth as to a garden;
he has ruined his appointed place;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has made forgotten in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧
appointed time and ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧,
and he has spurned in the indignation of his anger
king and priest.

[7] **ז** ⟦adonai|Adonai|The Lord⟧ has cast off his altar,
he has disowned his sanctuary;
he has handed over into the hand of an enemy
the walls of her citadels;
they have given voice in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
as on a day of appointed time.

[8] **ח** ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has meant to ruin
the wall of the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧;
he has stretched out a line,
he has not turned back his hand from swallowing up;
and he has made rampart and wall mourn;
together they languish.

[9] **ט** Her gates have sunk into the ground;
he has done away with and broken in pieces her bars;
her king and her princes are among the nations;
there is no ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧;
also her prophets have not found
a vision from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[10] **י** They sit on the ground, they are silent,
the elders of the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧;
they have brought up dust upon their head,
they have girded on sackcloth;
they have brought down their head to the ground,
the virgins of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[11] **כ** My eyes are spent with tears,
my inward parts are in ferment;
my liver is poured out on the ground
over the breaking of the daughter of my people,
as child and suckling grow faint
in the squares of the town.

[12] **ל** To their mothers they say,
Where is grain and wine?
as they grow faint like the slain
in the squares of the city,
as their soul is poured out
into the bosom of their mothers.

[13] **מ** What shall I testify to you, what shall I liken to you,
daughter of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧?
What shall I make equal to you, that I may comfort you,
virgin daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧?
For great as the sea is your breaking;
who can heal you?

[14] **נ** Your prophets have beheld for you
emptiness and whitewash,
and they have not uncovered your iniquity
to restore your fortunes,
and they have beheld for you
burdens of emptiness and things that drive away.

[15] **ס** They have clapped their palms over you,
all who pass by on the way;
they have hissed and wagged their head
over the daughter of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧:
Is this the city of which they said, The perfection of beauty,
an exultation for all the earth?

[16] **פ** They have opened their mouth against you,
all your enemies;
they have hissed and gnashed the tooth;
they have said, We have swallowed her up;
surely this is the day that we waited for;
we have found it, we have seen it.

[17] **ע** ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has done what he devised;
he has carried out his saying,
which he commanded from the days of old;
he has thrown down and has not spared;
and he has made an enemy glad over you,
he has raised the horn of your adversaries.

[18] **צ** Their heart cried out to ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧:
Wall of the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
make tears run down like a torrent
day and night;
give yourself no respite,
let the daughter of your eye not be still.

[19] **ק** Arise, give a ringing cry in the night,
at the head of the watches;
pour out your heart like water
opposite the face of ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧;
lift up your palms to him
for the soul of your children,
who grow faint with hunger
at the head of every street.

[20] **ר** See, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, and look:
to whom have you dealt thus?
Shall women eat their fruit,
the children they have tended?
Shall priest and prophet be killed
in the sanctuary of ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧?

[21] **ש** They lie on the ground in the streets,
boy and old man;
my virgins and my young men
have fallen by the sword;
you have killed in the day of your anger,
you have butchered, you have not spared.

[22] **ת** You call as on a day of appointed time
my terrors on every side;
and there was not, in the day of the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
a fugitive or a survivor;
those whom I tended and reared,
my enemy has made an end of them.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"; "Lord" → "Adonai"** (v6, 7, 8, 9, 17, 20, 22 · v1, 2, 5, 7, 18, 19, 20) — Hebrew יהוה and אֲדֹנָי, kept apart as the poem has them. No vocative "O" (v13, 18, 20).
- **"How hath the Lord covered the daughter of Zion with a cloud in his anger, and cast down from heaven unto the earth the beauty of Israel, and remembered not his footstool" → "How Adonai clouds over in his anger the daughter of Zion! He has thrown from the heavens to the earth the beauty of Israel, and has not remembered the footstool of his feet"** (v1) — Hebrew אֵיכָה "How" (as 1:1); יָעִיב, only here, taken from עָב "cloud" ("clouds over"; from תעב "treat as abhorrent" in other readings: uncertain), an imperfect; הִשְׁלִיךְ "threw" (its row); שָׁמַיִם "the heavens"; תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty"; הֲדֹם רַגְלָיו "the footstool of his feet" (as Isa 66:1).
- **"The Lord hath swallowed up all the habitations of Jacob, and hath not pitied … he hath brought them down to the ground: he hath polluted the kingdom and the princes thereof" → "Adonai has swallowed up, and has not spared … he has made them touch the ground; he has profaned the kingdom and its princes"** (v2) — ketiv לא "not", qere וְלֹא "and not" (the qere in the verse); חָמַל "spare" (its row; also v17, 21); נְאוֹת "habitations" (נָוֶה, its row); הָרַס "throw down"; עֶבְרָה "raging" (its row); מִבְצָרִים "fortresses"; הִגִּיעַ לָאָרֶץ "made (them) touch the ground"; חִלֵּל "profane" (not "pollute"); שָׂרִים "princes".
- **"He hath cut off in his fierce anger all the horn of Israel: he hath drawn back his right hand from before the enemy, and he burned against Jacob like a flaming fire, which devoureth round about" → "He has hewn down in the burning of anger every horn of Israel … from the face of an enemy; and he has burned in Jacob like a fire of flame; it has devoured all around"** (v3) — Hebrew גָּדַע "hew down"; בָּחֳרִי אַף "in the burning of anger" (no "his"); כֹּל קֶרֶן "every horn"; מִפְּנֵי "from the face of"; וַיִּבְעַר בְּ "and he burned in" ("against" is a reading); אֵשׁ לֶהָבָה "fire of flame"; אָכְלָה, a perfect, "it has devoured".
- **"He hath bent his bow like an enemy: he stood with his right hand as an adversary, and slew all that were pleasant to the eye in the tabernacle of the daughter of Zion: he poured out his fury like fire" → "He has trodden his bow like an enemy, his right hand stands like an adversary, and he has killed all that was desirable to the eye; in the tent of the daughter of Zion he has poured out his fury like fire"** (v4) — Hebrew דָּרַךְ קֶשֶׁת "tread the bow" (the bow is bent with the foot; as the 1 Chr 5:18 row); נִצָּב "stands" (be stationed); הָרַג "kill"; מַחֲמַדֵּי־עָיִן "the desirable things of the eye"; by the accents "in the tent of the daughter of Zion" begins the last line, with "poured out" (not with "slew"); אֹהֶל "tent"; חֵמָה "fury".
- **"The Lord was as an enemy … he hath destroyed his strong holds, and hath increased in the daughter of Judah mourning and lamentation" → "Adonai has become like an enemy … he has ruined his fortresses; and he has multiplied in the daughter of Judah mourning and moaning"** (v5) — Hebrew הָיָה כְּ "has become like"; אַרְמְנוֹת "citadels" (its row); שִׁחֵת "ruin" (its row; also v6, 8); "her citadels … his fortresses" as the Hebrew has the suffixes; תַּאֲנִיָּה וַאֲנִיָּה, one root, a play of sound ("mourning and moaning", as Isa 29:2).
- **"And he hath violently taken away his tabernacle, as if it were of a garden: he hath destroyed his places of the assembly: the LORD hath caused the solemn feasts and sabbaths to be forgotten in Zion" → "And he has done violence to his booth as to a garden; he has ruined his appointed place; Yahweh has made forgotten in Zion appointed time and Shabbat"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיַּחְמֹס "and he did violence to" (חָמַס, verb of the חָמָס row); שֹׂךְ "booth" (only here; = סֻכָּה); כַּגַּן "as a garden" ("as to" supplied); מוֹעֲדוֹ "his appointed place" (מוֹעֵד of a place: the meeting place; the same word, of time, in the next line, 1:4, 15; 2:7, 22); שִׁכַּח, piel, "made forgotten"; מוֹעֵד וְשַׁבָּת singular; נִאֵץ "spurn" (its row); זַעַם "indignation".
- **"The Lord hath cast off his altar, he hath abhorred his sanctuary, he hath given up into the hand of the enemy the walls of her palaces; they have made a noise in the house of the LORD, as in the day of a solemn feast" → "Adonai has cast off his altar, he has disowned his sanctuary; he has handed over into the hand of an enemy the walls of her citadels; they have given voice … as on a day of appointed time"** (v7) — Hebrew זָנַח "cast off" (its row); נִאֵר, only here and Ps 89:39, "disown" (renounce; uncertain); הִסְגִּיר בְּיַד "hand over into the hand of" (its row); קוֹל נָתְנוּ "they have given voice"; יוֹם מוֹעֵד "a day of appointed time".
- **"The LORD hath purposed to destroy the wall … he hath stretched out a line, he hath not withdrawn his hand from destroying: therefore he made the rampart and the wall to lament; they languished together" → "Yahweh has meant to ruin … he has not turned back his hand from swallowing up; and he has made rampart and wall mourn; together they languish"** (v8) — Hebrew חָשַׁב "mean" (its row, of the mind); לְהַשְׁחִית "to ruin"; קָו "line"; מִבַּלֵּעַ "from swallowing up" (the בִּלַּע of v2, 5, 16); וַיַּאֲבֶל "and he made mourn", with "and", not "therefore"; חֵל "rampart"; אֻמְלָלוּ "languish".
- **"Her gates are sunk into the ground; he hath destroyed and broken her bars: her king and her princes are among the Gentiles: the law is no more; her prophets also find no vision from the LORD" → "Her gates have sunk into the ground; he has done away with and broken in pieces her bars … there is no Torah; also her prophets have not found a vision from Yahweh"** (v9) — Hebrew טָבְעוּ "have sunk" (as Exod 15:4); אִבַּד "do away with" (piel of אָבַד); שִׁבַּר "break in pieces"; בְּרִיחַ "bar"; גּוֹיִם "nations"; אֵין תּוֹרָה "there is no Torah" (token kept; whether it says the torah is gone or there is none among the nations is left open); חָזוֹן "vision".
- **"The elders of the daughter of Zion sit upon the ground, and keep silence: they have cast up dust upon their heads; they have girded themselves with sackcloth: the virgins of Jerusalem hang down their heads to the ground" → "They sit on the ground, they are silent, the elders of the daughter of Zion; they have brought up dust upon their head, they have girded on sackcloth; they have brought down their head to the ground, the virgins of Jerusalem"** (v10) — Hebrew order kept (the verbs first, the subject last in each half); יִדְּמוּ "they are silent" (דָּמַם); הֶעֱלוּ "brought up"; רֹאשׁ singular; שַׂקִּים "sackcloth"; הוֹרִידוּ "brought down".
- **"Mine eyes do fail with tears, my bowels are troubled, my liver is poured upon the earth, for the destruction of the daughter of my people; because the children and the sucklings swoon in the streets of the city" → "My eyes are spent with tears, my inward parts are in ferment; my liver is poured out on the ground over the breaking of the daughter of my people, as child and suckling grow faint in the squares of the town"** (v11) — Hebrew כָּלוּ "are spent" (כָּלָה); חֳמַרְמְרוּ "are in ferment" (as 1:20); כָּבֵד "liver", the body part (seat of feeling: kept); שֶׁבֶר "breaking"; בַּת־עַמִּי "the daughter of my people"; בֵּעָטֵף "as … grow faint" (עָטַף, also v12, 19); עוֹלֵל "child", יוֹנֵק "suckling" (their row); רְחֹבוֹת "squares" (its row); קִרְיָה "town" (its row).
- **"They say to their mothers, Where is corn and wine? when they swooned as the wounded in the streets of the city, when their soul was poured out into their mothers' bosom" → "To their mothers they say, Where is grain and wine? as they grow faint like the slain in the squares of the city, as their soul is poured out into the bosom of their mothers"** (v12) — Hebrew order "to their mothers they say"; דָּגָן "grain"; חָלָל "slain" (one pierced); הִשְׁתַּפֵּךְ "be poured out"; חֵיק "bosom".
- **"What thing shall I take to witness for thee? what thing shall I liken to thee, O daughter of Jerusalem? what shall I equal to thee … O virgin daughter of Zion? for thy breach is great like the sea" → "What shall I testify to you, what shall I liken to you, daughter of Jerusalem? What shall I make equal to you … virgin daughter of Zion? For great as the sea is your breaking"** (v13) — Hebrew אֲעִידֵךְ "shall I testify to you" (הֵעִיד; "take to witness" is another sense of the same stem; many correct to אֶעֱרָךְ "compare", from memory: the MT is kept); הַבַּת יְרוּשָׁלִַם, literally "the daughter, Jerusalem"; אַשְׁוֶה "make equal"; וַאֲנַחֲמֵךְ "that I may comfort you"; שֶׁבֶר "breaking" (v11); רָפָא "heal".
- **"Thy prophets have seen vain and foolish things for thee: and they have not discovered thine iniquity, to turn away thy captivity; but have seen for thee false burdens and causes of banishment" → "Your prophets have beheld for you emptiness and whitewash, and they have not uncovered your iniquity to restore your fortunes, and they have beheld for you burdens of emptiness and things that drive away"** (v14) — Hebrew חָזָה "behold" (the seer's word); שָׁוְא "emptiness" (its row); תָּפֵל "whitewash" (as Ezek 13:10; also "tasteless", Job 6:6: uncertain); גִּלָּה עַל "uncover"; ketiv שביתך, qere שְׁבוּתֵךְ: לְהָשִׁיב שְׁבוּתֵךְ "to restore your fortunes" (reviewer row; literally "to turn back your captivity / turning", traditional "turn away your captivity"); מַשְׂאוֹת "burdens" (מַשָּׂא, its row: what is lifted up; "oracles"); מַדּוּחִים, only here, from נָדַח "drive away": "things that drive away" (seductions, or what leads to banishment: uncertain).
- **"All that pass by clap their hands at thee; they hiss and wag their head at the daughter of Jerusalem, saying, Is this the city that men call The perfection of beauty, The joy of the whole earth?" → "They have clapped their palms over you, all who pass by on the way; they have hissed and wagged their head over the daughter of Jerusalem: Is this the city of which they said, The perfection of beauty, an exultation for all the earth?"** (v15) — Hebrew order (the subject after the verb); סָפְקוּ כַּפַּיִם "clapped palms" (כַּף); עֹבְרֵי דֶרֶךְ (as 1:12); שָׁרְקוּ "hissed"; עַל "over"; שֶׁיֹּאמְרוּ "of which they said" ("men" is not written; "saying" is not written); כְּלִילַת יֹפִי "the perfection of beauty" (כָּלִיל "whole, complete"; as Ezek 27:3; Ps 50:2 מִכְלַל־יֹפִי); מָשׂוֹשׂ "exultation" (its row).
- **"All thine enemies have opened their mouth against thee: they hiss and gnash the teeth: they say, We have swallowed her up: certainly this is the day that we looked for; we have found, we have seen it" → "They have opened their mouth against you, all your enemies; they have hissed and gnashed the tooth; they have said, We have swallowed her up; surely this is the day that we waited for; we have found it, we have seen it"** (v16) — Hebrew פָּצָה פֶּה "open the mouth"; שֵׁן singular, "tooth"; בִּלָּעְנוּ "we have swallowed up" (no object written: "her" supplied); קִוָּה "wait for" (its row); מָצָאנוּ רָאִינוּ "we have found, we have seen" ("it" supplied). The letter פ comes before ע here (v16–17).
- **"The LORD hath done that which he had devised; he hath fulfilled his word that he had commanded in the days of old … and he hath caused thine enemy to rejoice over thee, he hath set up the horn of thine adversaries" → "Yahweh has done what he devised; he has carried out his saying, which he commanded from the days of old … and he has made an enemy glad over you, he has raised the horn of your adversaries"** (v17) — Hebrew זָמַם "devise" (its row); בִּצַּע "carry out" (bring to an end, cut off: as Isa 10:12); אִמְרָה "saying" (its row); מִימֵי־קֶדֶם "from the days of old"; שִׂמַּח "make glad"; הֵרִים "raise".
- **"Their heart cried unto the Lord, O wall of the daughter of Zion, let tears run down like a river day and night: give thyself no rest; let not the apple of thine eye cease" → "Their heart cried out to Adonai: Wall of the daughter of Zion, make tears run down like a torrent day and night; give yourself no respite, let the daughter of your eye not be still"** (v18) — Hebrew צָעַק "cry out" (a perfect: past); the accents end the first half at "Adonai", so "Wall of the daughter of Zion" is addressed (many correct the line, from memory: the MT is kept); הוֹרִידִי "make … run down" (hifil); נַחַל "torrent" (where the water is meant, its row); דִּמְעָה singular; פוּגַת "respite" (only here); בַּת־עֵינֵךְ, literally "the daughter of your eye" (the pupil: kept); דָּמַם "be still".
- **"Arise, cry out in the night: in the beginning of the watches pour out thine heart like water before the face of the Lord: lift up thy hands toward him for the life of thy young children, that faint for hunger in the top of every street" → "Arise, give a ringing cry in the night, at the head of the watches; pour out your heart like water opposite the face of Adonai; lift up your palms to him for the soul of your children … at the head of every street"** (v19) — ketiv בליל "in night", qere בַלַּיְלָה "in the night"; רֹנִּי "give a ringing cry" (רָנַן; of a cry of sorrow here, as רִנָּה 1 Kgs 8:28); לְרֹאשׁ אַשְׁמֻרוֹת "at the head of the watches"; נֹכַח פְּנֵי "opposite the face of" (נֹכַח, its row); כַּפַּיִם "palms"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul"; עוֹלָלִים "children"; רָעָב "hunger"; חוּצוֹת "streets". The verse has four lines of two, one pair more than the others.
- **"Behold, O LORD, and consider to whom thou hast done this. Shall the women eat their fruit, and children of a span long? shall the priest and the prophet be slain in the sanctuary of the Lord?" → "See, Yahweh, and look: to whom have you dealt thus? Shall women eat their fruit, the children they have tended? Shall priest and prophet be killed in the sanctuary of Adonai?"** (v20) — Hebrew רְאֵה … וְהַבִּיטָה "see … and look" (as 1:11); עוֹלַלְתָּ "dealt" (עָלַל, as 1:12, 22); אִם … אִם, questions; פִּרְיָם "their fruit" (children); עֹלֲלֵי טִפֻּחִים "children of tending" (טִפֻּחִים, only here, from טִפַּח "tend, carry on the hands", v22; "a span long" from another sense of the root: uncertain); הָרַג "kill".
- **"The young and the old lie on the ground in the streets: my virgins and my young men are fallen by the sword; thou hast slain them in the day of thine anger; thou hast killed, and not pitied" → "They lie on the ground in the streets, boy and old man … you have killed in the day of your anger, you have butchered, you have not spared"** (v21) — Hebrew order (verb first); נַעַר וְזָקֵן "boy and old man" (singular); הָרַגְתָּ "you have killed" ("them" is not written); טָבַחְתָּ "you have butchered" (טָבַח, its row); לֹא חָמָלְתָּ "you have not spared" (v2, 17).
- **"Thou hast called as in a solemn day my terrors round about, so that in the day of the LORD's anger none escaped nor remained: those that I have swaddled and brought up hath mine enemy consumed" → "You call as on a day of appointed time my terrors on every side; and there was not … a fugitive or a survivor; those whom I tended and reared, my enemy has made an end of them"** (v22) — Hebrew תִּקְרָא, an imperfect, "you call"; יוֹם מוֹעֵד "a day of appointed time" (v7); מְגוּרַי מִסָּבִיב "my terrors on every side" (מָגוֹר מִסָּבִיב, its row; "my" kept); וְלֹא הָיָה "and there was not" ("so that" is not written); פָּלִיט "fugitive", שָׂרִיד "survivor"; טִפַּחְתִּי "I tended" (v20); רִבִּיתִי "I reared" (lit. ### Notes
- **Acrostic** — 22 verses, each opening with the next letter of the alphabet; here פ stands before ע (v16 פ, v17 ע), as in ch. 3 and 4 (an order known also from some early alphabet lists). The letters are printed in bold; the translation does not imitate the scheme.
- **Verse form** — each verse has three lines of two halves (v19 has four); the printed lines follow the Hebrew halves.
- The numbering is the same in the Hebrew.
