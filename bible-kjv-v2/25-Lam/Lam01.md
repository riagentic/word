# Lamentations 1

[1] **א** How she sits alone,
the city once full of people!
She has become like a widow,
she who was great among the nations!
The princess among the provinces
has become a slave.

[2] **ב** Bitterly she weeps in the night,
her tears on her cheeks;
among all her lovers
she has none to comfort her;
all her friends have dealt treacherously with her;
they have become her enemies.

[3] **ג** ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ has gone into exile because of affliction
and hard servitude;
she dwells now among the nations,
but finds no resting place;
her pursuers have all overtaken her
in the midst of her distress.

[4] **ד** The roads to ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ mourn,
for none come to the appointed feasts;
all her gates are desolate;
her priests groan;
her virgins have been afflicted,
and she herself suffers bitterly.

[5] **ה** Her foes have become the head;
her enemies prosper,
because ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has afflicted her
for the multitude of her transgressions;
her children have gone away,
captives before the foe.

[6] **ו** From the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧
all her majesty has departed.
Her princes have become like deer
that find no pasture;
they fled without strength
before the pursuer.

[7] **ז** ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ remembers
in the days of her affliction and wandering
all the precious things
that were hers from days of old.
When her people fell into the hand of the foe,
and there was none to help her,
her foes gloated over her;
they mocked at her downfall.

[8] **ח** ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ sinned grievously;
therefore she became filthy;
all who honored her despise her,
for they have seen her nakedness;
she herself groans
and turns her face away.

[9] **ט** Her uncleanness was in her skirts;
she took no thought of her future;
therefore her fall is terrible;
she has no comforter.
"O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, behold my affliction,
for the enemy has triumphed!"

[10] **י** The enemy has stretched out his hands
over all her precious things;
for she has seen the nations
enter her sanctuary,
those whom you forbade
to enter your assembly.

[11] **כ** All her people groan
as they search for bread;
they trade their treasures for food
to revive their soul.
"Look, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, and see,
for I am despised."

[12] **ל** Is it nothing to you, all you who pass by?
Look and see
if there is any sorrow like my sorrow,
which was brought upon me,
which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ inflicted
on the day of his fierce anger.

[13] **מ** From on high he sent fire;
into my bones he made it descend;
he spread a net for my feet;
he turned me back;
he has left me stunned,
faint all the day long.

[14] **נ** My transgressions were bound into a yoke;
by his hand they were fastened together;
they were set upon my neck;
he caused my strength to fail;
⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ gave me into the hands
of those whom I cannot withstand.

[15] **ס** ⟦adonai|Adonai|The Lord⟧ rejected
all my mighty men in my midst;
he summoned an assembly against me
to crush my young men;
⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ has trodden as in a winepress
the virgin daughter of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[16] **ע** For these things I weep;
my eyes flow with tears;
for a comforter is far from me,
one to revive my soul;
my children are desolate,
for the enemy has prevailed.

[17] **פ** ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ stretches out her hands,
but there is none to comfort her;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded against ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧
that his neighbors should be his foes;
⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ has become
a filthy thing among them.

[18] **צ** ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is in the right,
for I have rebelled against his word;
but hear, all you peoples,
and see my suffering;
my young women and my young men
have gone into captivity.

[19] **ק** I called to my lovers,
but they deceived me;
my priests and elders
perished in the city,
while they sought food
to revive their strength.

[20] **ר** Look, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, for I am in distress;
my inmost parts are in turmoil;
my heart is wrung within me,
because I have been very rebellious.
In the street the sword bereaves;
in the house it is like death.

[21] **ש** They heard my groaning,
yet there is no one to comfort me.
All my enemies have heard of my trouble;
they are glad that you have done it.
You have brought the day you announced;
now let them be as I am.

[22] **ת** Let all their evildoing come before you,
and deal with them
as you have dealt with me
because of all my transgressions;
for my groans are many,
and my heart is faint.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v5, 8, 9, 11, 12, 17, 18, 20, 21) — Hebrew יהוה *YHWH*; note that the poem also uses *Adonai* (v14, 15) where the MT has אֲדֹנָי rather than יהוה; both forms retained distinctly.
- **"LORD" / "Lord"** — the poem uses both יהוה *YHWH* (→ Yahweh) and אֲדֹנָי *Adonai* (→ "Lord", v14, 15 ×2); the distinction is preserved throughout Lamentations to honor the text's own variation.
- **"How"** (v1) — Hebrew אֵיכָה *Eikhah*; the exclamatory dirge-opener and Hebrew title of the book.
- **Acrostic structure** — Hebrew alphabet letters (א–ת) marked in bold before each verse (1–22); the acrostic cannot be reproduced in English translation but is made visible by the markers.
- **"lovers"** (v2, 19) — Hebrew מְאַהֲבַיִךְ *me'ahavayikh*; political allies/nations who once courted Jerusalem; the word carries both political and erotic connotations, preserved as "lovers."
- **"the virgin, the daughter of Judah" → "the virgin daughter of Judah"** — Hebrew בְּתוּלַת בַּת יְהוּדָה *betulat bat Yehudah* (v15); personification of Judah as a young woman; retained.
- **"sins" / "transgressions"** — Hebrew פְּשָׁעִים *pesha'im* (rebellions/deliberate transgressions); translated contextually as "transgressions."

### Notes
- **Acrostic** (22 verses, Hebrew letters א–ת). Only here (and Ps 119) is the structure named.
- ***Eikhah*** (v1) = "How"; opens this book and Lam 2, 4; Hebrew title of Lamentations.
- Traditionally attributed to Jeremiah; read on **Tisha b'Av** (9th of Av) commemorating Temple's destruction.
