# Lamentations 1

[1] **א** How she sits alone,
the city great with people!
She has become like a widow,
she who was great among the nations;
princess among the provinces,
she has become forced labor.

[2] **ב** She surely weeps in the night,
and her tear is on her cheek;
she has no comforter
among all those who love her;
all her friends have dealt treacherously with her;
they have become enemies to her.

[3] **ג** ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ has gone into exile from affliction
and from greatness of service;
she dwells among the nations,
she has not found a resting place;
all her pursuers have overtaken her
between the narrow places.

[4] **ד** The ways of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ mourn,
for lack of those who come to the appointed time;
all her gates are desolate,
her priests sigh;
her virgins are grieved,
and she — it is bitter to her.

[5] **ה** Her adversaries have become the head,
her enemies are untroubled,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has grieved her
for the multitude of her transgressions;
her children have gone into captivity
before an adversary.

[6] **ו** And from the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ has gone out
all her splendor;
her princes have become like harts
that have found no pasture,
and they have gone without power
before a pursuer.

[7] **ז** ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ has remembered
in the days of her affliction and her wandering
all her desirable things
that were from the days of old,
when her people fell into the hand of an adversary,
and there was no helper for her;
adversaries saw her,
they laughed at her ceasing.

[8] **ח** ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ has sinned a sin;
therefore she has become an impure thing;
all who honored her have despised her,
for they have seen her nakedness;
she too sighs,
and has turned back.

[9] **ט** Her uncleanness is in her skirts;
she did not remember her latter end,
and she has come down wondrously;
there is no comforter for her.
See, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, my affliction,
for an enemy has made himself great.

[10] **י** An adversary has spread out his hand
over all her desirable things;
for she has seen nations
come into her sanctuary,
of whom you commanded,
They shall not come into the assembly that is yours.

[11] **כ** All her people sigh,
seeking bread;
they have given their desirable things for food
to bring back the soul.
See, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, and look,
for I have become despised.

[12] **ל** Is it not to you, all you who pass by on the way?
Look and see
whether there is any pain like my pain,
which has been dealt to me,
with which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has grieved me
in the day of the burning of his anger.

[13] **מ** From on high he has sent fire into my bones,
and he has had dominion over it;
he has spread out a net for my feet,
he has turned me back;
he has made me desolate,
unwell all the day.

[14] **נ** The yoke of my transgressions is bound on by his hand;
they are knit together,
they have come up on my neck;
he has made my power stumble;
⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ has given me into the hands
of those against whom I cannot rise.

[15] **ס** ⟦adonai|Adonai|The Lord⟧ has tossed aside all my mighty ones
in my midst;
he has called an appointed time against me
to break my young men;
a winepress ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ has trodden
for the virgin daughter of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[16] **ע** Over these I am weeping;
my eye, my eye runs down with water;
for far from me is a comforter,
one to bring back my soul;
my sons have become desolate,
for an enemy has prevailed.

[17] **פ** ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ has spread out her hands;
there is no comforter for her;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded concerning ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧
that those around him be his adversaries;
⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ has become
an impurity among them.

[18] **צ** Righteous is he, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
for I have rebelled against his mouth;
hear, please, all the peoples,
and see my pain;
my virgins and my young men
have gone into captivity.

[19] **ק** I called to my lovers;
they deceived me;
my priests and my elders
expired in the city,
when they sought food for themselves
that they might bring back their soul.

[20] **ר** See, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, for I am in distress;
my inward parts are in ferment;
my heart is turned over within me,
for I have surely rebelled;
outside the sword has bereaved,
in the house it is like death.

[21] **ש** They have heard that I sigh;
there is no comforter for me;
all my enemies have heard of my evil,
they have exulted that you have done it;
you have brought the day you called,
and they shall be like me.

[22] **ת** Let all their evil come before you,
and deal with them
as you have dealt with me
for all my transgressions;
for many are my sighs,
and my heart is unwell.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"; "Lord" → "Adonai"** (v5, 9, 11, 12, 17, 18, 20 · v14, 15 ×2) — Hebrew יהוה and אֲדֹנָי, kept apart as the poem has them. No vocative "O" (v9, 11, 20): the Hebrew has no such word.
- **Quotation marks removed** (v9, 11) — the Hebrew marks no speech; the voice moves from the city described (v1–11a) to the city speaking (v11b–22) without a sign, and the verse shows it only by "I".
- **"How doth the city sit solitary, that was full of people! how is she become as a widow! … how is she become tributary!" → "How she sits alone, the city great with people! … she has become forced labor"** (v1) — Hebrew אֵיכָה once, at the head ("how" is not repeated); יָשְׁבָה "she sits" (יָשַׁב of the body); רַבָּתִי עָם "great with people" (the same רַבָּתִי "great" as in "great among the nations"); שָׂרָתִי "princess"; מְדִינוֹת "provinces"; הָיְתָה לָמַס "she has become forced labor" (מַס, the labor gang a ruler sets to work; not "tribute").
- **"She weepeth sore in the night, and her tears are on her cheeks: among all her lovers she hath none to comfort her" → "She surely weeps in the night, and her tear is on her cheek; she has no comforter among all those who love her"** (v2) — Hebrew בָּכוֹ תִבְכֶּה, infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "weeping she weeps"; דִּמְעָתָהּ and לֶחֱיָהּ are singular; מְנַחֵם "comforter" (a noun, as v9, 16, 17, 21); אֹהֲבֶיהָ, the plain participle of אָהַב "those who love her", kept apart from מְאַהֲבַי "my lovers" (v19); בָּגַד בְּ "deal treacherously with".
- **"Judah is gone into captivity because of affliction, and because of great servitude … all her persecutors overtook her between the straits" → "Judah has gone into exile from affliction and from greatness of service … between the narrow places"** (v3) — Hebrew גָּלְתָה "has gone into exile" (גָּלָה); מֵעֹנִי וּמֵרֹב עֲבֹדָה, מִן twice: "from" (out of, or because of: left open); עֲבֹדָה "service"; מָנוֹחַ "resting place"; רֹדְפִים "pursuers"; בֵּין הַמְּצָרִים "between the narrow places" (מֵצַר, as Ps 118:5).
- **"The ways of Zion do mourn, because none come to the solemn feasts … her virgins are afflicted, and she is in bitterness" → "The ways of Zion mourn, for lack of those who come to the appointed time … her virgins are grieved, and she — it is bitter to her"** (v4) — Hebrew מִבְּלִי בָּאֵי מוֹעֵד "for lack of comers to the appointed time" (מוֹעֵד, the fixed time of a feast; the same word v15; 2:6, 7, 22); שֹׁומֵמִין "desolate" (שָׁמֵם); נֶאֱנָחִים "sigh" (נֶאֱנַח, also v8, 11, 21); נוּגוֹת "grieved" (יָגָה nifal; the hifil is v5, 12); וְהִיא מַר־לָהּ, literally "and she, it is bitter to her".
- **"Her adversaries are the chief, her enemies prosper; for the LORD hath afflicted her … her children are gone into captivity before the enemy" → "Her adversaries have become the head, her enemies are untroubled, for Yahweh has grieved her … before an adversary"** (v5) — Hebrew הָיוּ … לְרֹאשׁ "have become the head" (as Deut 28:44); שָׁלוּ "are untroubled" (שָׁלָה); הוֹגָהּ "has grieved her" (יָגָה hifil, one root with v4); פְּשָׁעִים "transgressions"; עוֹלָלִים "children" (young children); שְׁבִי "captivity"; צָר "adversary" (no article).
- **"And from the daughter of Zion all her beauty is departed … they are gone without strength before the pursuer" → "And from the daughter of Zion has gone out all her splendor … and they have gone without power before a pursuer"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיֵּצֵא, with "and", "has gone out"; the ketiv מן־בת and the qere מִבַּת are the same words, "from the daughter of"; הָדָר "splendor"; אַיָּלִים "harts"; מִרְעֶה "pasture"; כֹּחַ "power".
- **"Jerusalem remembered in the days of her affliction and of her miseries all her pleasant things … the adversaries saw her, and did mock at her sabbaths" → "Jerusalem has remembered … all her desirable things … adversaries saw her, they laughed at her ceasing"** (v7) — Hebrew מְרוּדֶיהָ "her wandering" (from רוּד "roam": uncertain, also "homelessness"); מַחֲמֻדִּים "desirable things" (חָמַד, also v10, 11); עוֹזֵר "helper"; שָׂחֲקוּ "laughed"; מִשְׁבַּתֶּהָ, only here, from שָׁבַת "cease": "her ceasing" (her coming to an end; "sabbaths" reads it as שַׁבָּת; "downfall" in other versions: uncertain). The verse has four lines of two, one pair more than the others.
- **"Jerusalem hath grievously sinned; therefore she is removed … yea, she sigheth, and turneth backward" → "Jerusalem has sinned a sin; therefore she has become an impure thing … she too sighs, and has turned back"** (v8) — Hebrew חֵטְא חָטְאָה, a noun with its verb ("sinned a sin": kept, as "dreaded a dread" Job 3:25); לְנִידָה, written with yod: either נִדָּה "impurity" (v17) or a noun of נוּד "shake the head, wander" ("a thing shaken at", "removed"): uncertain, "impure thing" keeps it apart from v17; מְכַבְּדִים "those who honored"; הִזִּיל "despise" (root זלל "be light", as זוֹלֵלָה v11); גַּם־הִיא "she too"; וַתָּשָׁב "and has turned".
- **"Her filthiness is in her skirts; she remembereth not her last end; therefore she came down wonderfully … O LORD, behold my affliction: for the enemy hath magnified himself" → "Her uncleanness … she did not remember her latter end, and she has come down wondrously … See, Yahweh, my affliction, for an enemy has made himself great"** (v9) — Hebrew טֻמְאָה "uncleanness"; אַחֲרִית "latter end"; וַתֵּרֶד פְּלָאִים "and she came down wonders" (= astonishingly), with "and", not "therefore"; רְאֵה "see"; הִגְדִּיל "made himself great".
- **"The adversary hath spread out his hand upon all her pleasant things … whom thou didst command that they should not enter into thy congregation" → "An adversary … of whom you commanded, They shall not come into the assembly that is yours"** (v10) — Hebrew צָר "adversary"; בָּאוּ "come"; לֹא־יָבֹאוּ בַקָּהָל לָךְ, the command quoted (Deut 23:3), "into the assembly belonging to you" (קָהָל "assembly"; לָךְ "to you"); בּוֹא בַקָּהָל as the Deut 23 row.
- **"All her people sigh, they seek bread; they have given their pleasant things for meat to relieve the soul: see, O LORD, and consider; for I am become vile" → "… they have given their desirable things for food to bring back the soul. See, Yahweh, and look, for I have become despised"** (v11) — ketiv מחמודיהם, qere מַחֲמַדֵּיהֶם, the same sense; אֹכֶל "food"; לְהָשִׁיב נָפֶשׁ "to bring back the soul" (= keep alive; also v16, 19); הִבִּיט "look"; זוֹלֵלָה "despised" (one made light of; one root with הִזִּיל v8).
- **"Is it nothing to you, all ye that pass by? behold, and see if there be any sorrow like unto my sorrow … wherewith the LORD hath afflicted me in the day of his fierce anger" → "Is it not to you, all you who pass by on the way? … whether there is any pain like my pain, which has been dealt to me, with which Yahweh has grieved me in the day of the burning of his anger"** (v12) — Hebrew לוֹא אֲלֵיכֶם "not to you": a question ("does it not touch you?") or a wish ("may it not come to you"); uncertain, "nothing" is not written; עֹבְרֵי דֶרֶךְ "passers on the way"; מַכְאוֹב "pain"; עוֹלַל "has been dealt" (poal of עָלַל, the verb of v22); הוֹגָה "grieved" (v5); חֲרוֹן אַפּוֹ "the burning of his anger" (אַף "anger").
- **"From above hath he sent fire into my bones, and it prevaileth against them … he hath made me desolate and faint all the day" → "From on high he has sent fire into my bones, and he has had dominion over it … he has made me desolate, unwell all the day"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיִּרְדֶּנָּה, pointed from רָדָה "have dominion", with a feminine object ("it": the fire? each bone?); also read from יָרַד "and he brought it down": uncertain; פָּרַשׂ רֶשֶׁת "spread out a net"; נְתָנַנִי שֹׁמֵמָה, literally "he has given me desolate"; דָּוָה "unwell" (its row; also v22).
- **"The yoke of my transgressions is bound by his hand: they are wreathed, and come up upon my neck: he hath made my strength to fall, the Lord hath delivered me into their hands, from whom I am not able to rise up" → "The yoke of my transgressions is bound on by his hand; they are knit together … he has made my power stumble; Adonai has given me into the hands of those against whom I cannot rise"** (v14) — Hebrew נִשְׂקַד, only here: "is bound on" (uncertain; some read נִשְׁקַד "is watched"); יִשְׂתָּרְגוּ "they are knit together" (שָׂרַג, as Job 40:17); הִכְשִׁיל "made stumble"; כֹּחַ "power"; בִּידֵי לֹא־אוּכַל קוּם, literally "into hands of — I cannot rise".
- **"The Lord hath trodden under foot all my mighty men … he hath called an assembly against me to crush my young men: the Lord hath trodden the virgin, the daughter of Judah, as in a winepress" → "Adonai has tossed aside all my mighty ones … he has called an appointed time against me to break my young men; a winepress Adonai has trodden for the virgin daughter of Judah"** (v15) — Hebrew סִלָּה, piel of סָלָה "toss aside / make light of" (uncertain); אַבִּירִים "mighty ones"; מוֹעֵד "appointed time" (as v4; "assembly" in Changes only); שָׁבַר "break"; בַּחוּרִים "young men"; גַּת דָּרַךְ … לִבְתוּלַת בַּת־יְהוּדָה, in this order: "a winepress … for the virgin daughter" ("as in" is not written).
- **"For these things I weep; mine eye, mine eye runneth down with water … my children are desolate, because the enemy prevailed" → "Over these I am weeping; my eye, my eye runs down with water … my sons have become desolate, for an enemy has prevailed"** (v16) — Hebrew בוֹכִיָּה, a participle, "I am weeping"; עֵינִי עֵינִי, the doubled word kept; מֵשִׁיב נַפְשִׁי "one to bring back my soul" (v11); בָּנַי "my sons"; גָּבַר "prevail".
- **"Zion spreadeth forth her hands … the LORD hath commanded concerning Jacob, that his adversaries should be round about him: Jerusalem is as a menstruous woman among them" → "Zion has spread out her hands … that those around him be his adversaries; Jerusalem has become an impurity among them"** (v17) — Hebrew פֵּרְשָׂה … בְּיָדֶיהָ "has spread out with her hands"; צִוָּה לְ "commanded concerning"; סְבִיבָיו צָרָיו, literally "those around him (are) his adversaries"; לְנִדָּה "an impurity" (נִדָּה, its row; "as" is not written).
- **"The LORD is righteous; for I have rebelled against his commandment: hear, I pray you, all people" → "Righteous is he, Yahweh, for I have rebelled against his mouth; hear, please, all the peoples"** (v18) — Hebrew צַדִּיק הוּא יְהוָה, in this order; פִיהוּ מָרִיתִי "I have rebelled against his mouth" (מָרָה אֶת־פִּי, its row); נָא "please"; ketiv עמים "peoples", qere הָעַמִּים "the peoples" (the qere in the verse); בַּשֶּׁבִי "into captivity".
- **"I called for my lovers, but they deceived me … gave up the ghost in the city, while they sought their meat to relieve their souls" → "I called to my lovers; they deceived me … expired in the city, when they sought food for themselves that they might bring back their soul"** (v19) — Hebrew מְאַהֲבִים "lovers" (piel); רִמָּה "deceive"; גָּוַע "expire"; כִּי "when" (or "for"); וְיָשִׁיבוּ "that they might bring back" (v11).
- **"Behold, O LORD; for I am in distress: my bowels are troubled; mine heart is turned within me; for I have grievously rebelled: abroad the sword bereaveth, at home there is as death" → "See, Yahweh, for I am in distress; my inward parts are in ferment; my heart is turned over within me, for I have surely rebelled; outside the sword has bereaved, in the house it is like death"** (v20) — Hebrew צַר־לִי, literally "it is narrow to me"; מֵעַי "my inward parts"; חֳמַרְמָרוּ "are in ferment" (only here and 2:11; Job 16:16: uncertain, "foam, burn"); נֶהְפַּךְ "is turned over" (הָפַךְ); מָרוֹ מָרִיתִי, infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "rebelling I have rebelled"; שִׁכְּלָה "has bereaved" (שִׁכֵּל).
- **"They have heard that I sigh … all mine enemies have heard of my trouble; they are glad that thou hast done it: thou wilt bring the day that thou hast called, and they shall be like unto me" → "They have heard that I sigh … all my enemies have heard of my evil, they have exulted that you have done it; you have brought the day you called, and they shall be like me"** (v21) — Hebrew שָׁמְעוּ, a perfect (the Syriac "hear!" from memory); רָעָה "evil"; שָׂשׂוּ "exulted" (שׂוּשׂ); הֵבֵאתָ "you have brought", a perfect (often read as a wish, "bring": the form is a perfect); וְיִהְיוּ "and they shall be" (or "let them be").
- **"Let all their wickedness come before thee; and do unto them, as thou hast done unto me for all my transgressions: for my sighs are many, and my heart is faint" → "Let all their evil come before you, and deal with them as you have dealt with me … and my heart is unwell"** (v22) — Hebrew רָעָתָם "their evil" (as v21); עוֹלֵל … עוֹלַלְתָּ "deal … dealt" (עָלַל, as v12); אֲנָחוֹת "sighs"; דַּוָּי "unwell" (v13).

### Notes
- **Hebrew name** — the book is named in Hebrew from its first word, אֵיכָה *Eikhah* "How" (also 2:1; 4:1); the Talmud also calls it קִינוֹת *Qinot* "Laments". The English and Czech names come from the Greek and Latin.
- **Acrostic** — 22 verses, each opening with the next letter of the alphabet in its usual order (א–ת; in this chapter ע before פ, v16–17). The letters are printed in bold before the verses; the English and Czech do not imitate the scheme.
- **Verse form** — each verse has three lines of two halves (v7 has four); the printed lines follow the Hebrew halves.
- The numbering is the same in the Hebrew.
- Traditionally attributed to Jeremiah (the book itself names no author); read on **Tisha b'Av** (9th of Av), the day of the Temple's destruction.
