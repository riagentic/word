# Lamentations 5

[1] Remember, O Yahweh, what has befallen us;
look, and see our disgrace!

[2] Our inheritance has been turned over to strangers,
our homes to foreigners.

[3] We have become orphans, fatherless;
our mothers are like widows.

[4] We must pay for the water we drink;
the wood we get must be bought.

[5] Our pursuers are at our necks;
we are weary; we are given no rest.

[6] We have given the hand to Egypt, and to Assyria,
to get bread enough.

[7] Our fathers sinned, and are no more;
and we bear their iniquities.

[8] Slaves rule over us;
there is none to deliver us from their hand.

[9] We get our bread at the peril of our lives,
because of the sword in the wilderness.

[10] Our skin is hot as an oven
with the burning heat of famine.

[11] Women are raped in Zion,
young women in the towns of Judah.

[12] Princes are hung up by their hands;
no respect is shown to the elders.

[13] Young men are compelled to grind at the mill,
and boys stagger under loads of wood.

[14] The old men have left the city gate,
the young men their music.

[15] The joy of our hearts has ceased;
our dancing has been turned to mourning.

[16] The crown has fallen from our head;
woe to us, for we have sinned!

[17] For this our heart has become sick,
for these things our eyes have grown dim,

[18] for Mount Zion which lies desolate;
jackals prowl over it.

[19] But you, O Yahweh, reign forever;
your throne endures from generation to generation.

[20] Why do you forget us forever,
why do you forsake us for so many days?

[21] Restore us to yourself, O Yahweh, that we may be restored!
Renew our days as of old —

[22] unless you have utterly rejected us,
and you remain exceedingly angry with us.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 19, 21) — Hebrew יהוה *YHWH*.
- **No acrostic** — chapter 5 has 22 verses (= the Hebrew alphabet count) but is NOT an alphabetic acrostic; the absence of the acrostic may signal the collapse of ordered structure, a formal enactment of the chaos described.
- **"ravished" → "raped"** (v11) — Hebrew עִנּוּ *innu*; to violate/afflict sexually; KJV "ravished" is now archaic; the text describes sexual violence; rendered plainly.
- **"*Eikhah*"** — not present in ch. 5; the book does not close with another *Eikhah* opener but with a communal prayer.
- **Dirge vocabulary**: "remember… our disgrace" (v1), "orphans" (v3), "crown fallen" (v16), "woe to us for we have sinned" (v16) — lament vocabulary translated literally.
- **"restore us"** (v21) — Hebrew הֲשִׁיבֵנוּ *hashivenu*; "turn us back to yourself" — a key concept-word in the book's closing prayer; rendered as "restore."

### Notes
- **In synagogue reading** — v21 is repeated after v22 so as not to end on bleakness.
- **Book ends with open-ended question** — does Yahweh still accept his people?
