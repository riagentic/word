# Deuteronomy 7

[1] "When Yahweh your *Elohim* brings you into the land that you are entering to possess, and clears away many nations before you — the Hittites, the Girgashites, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, seven nations more numerous and mightier than you —

[2] and when Yahweh your *Elohim* gives them over before you and you defeat them, then you shall devote them to utter destruction. You shall make no covenant with them, and show them no mercy.

[3] You shall not intermarry with them; your daughter you shall not give to his son, nor his daughter shall you take for your son.

[4] For he would turn your son away from following me, that they might serve other gods; then the anger of Yahweh would burn against you, and he would destroy you quickly.

[5] But this is what you shall do to them: you shall tear down their altars, smash their pillars, cut down their Asherah poles, and burn their carved images with fire.

[6] For you are a holy people to Yahweh your *Elohim*; Yahweh your *Elohim* has chosen you to be his treasured people out of all the peoples who are on the face of the earth.

[7] Not because you were more numerous than any people did Yahweh set his love on you and choose you — for you were the fewest of all peoples —

[8] but because Yahweh loved you and because he was keeping the oath that he swore to your fathers, Yahweh has brought you out with a mighty hand and redeemed you from the house of slavery, from the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt.

[9] Know therefore that Yahweh your *Elohim* — he is *Elohim*, the faithful *Elohim*, who keeps covenant and lovingkindness with those who love him and keep his commandments, to a thousand generations,

[10] but who repays those who hate him to their face, to destroy them. He will not be slack with the one who hates him; he will repay him to his face.

[11] You shall therefore keep the commandment — the statutes and the ordinances — that I am commanding you today, to do them.

[12] And because you listen to these ordinances and keep and do them, Yahweh your *Elohim* will keep with you the covenant and the lovingkindness that he swore to your fathers.

[13] He will love you and bless you and multiply you; he will bless the fruit of your womb and the fruit of your ground — your grain, your new wine, and your oil, the increase of your herds and the young of your flock — in the land that he swore to your fathers to give you.

[14] You shall be blessed above all peoples; there shall be no male or female barren among you, or among your livestock.

[15] Yahweh will take away from you all sickness, and none of the evil diseases of Egypt that you know of will he inflict upon you; but he will lay them on all those who hate you.

[16] You shall consume all the peoples whom Yahweh your *Elohim* gives over to you; your eye shall not pity them, and you shall not serve their gods, for that would be a snare to you.

[17] If you say in your heart, 'These nations are more numerous than I; how can I dispossess them?' —

[18] you shall not be afraid of them. Remember well what Yahweh your *Elohim* did to Pharaoh and to all Egypt —

[19] the great trials that your eyes saw, the signs and the wonders, the mighty hand and the outstretched arm by which Yahweh your *Elohim* brought you out. So will Yahweh your *Elohim* do to all the peoples before whom you are afraid.

[20] Moreover, Yahweh your *Elohim* will send the hornet among them, until those who are left and hide themselves from you perish.

[21] You shall not be in dread of them, for Yahweh your *Elohim* is in your midst — a great and fearsome El.

[22] Yahweh your *Elohim* will clear away these nations before you little by little; you shall not be able to destroy them all at once, lest the wild animals multiply against you.

[23] But Yahweh your *Elohim* will give them over to you, and throw them into great panic, until they are destroyed.

[24] He will give their kings into your hand, and you shall blot out their name from under heaven; no man shall be able to stand before you, until you have destroyed them.

[25] The carved images of their gods you shall burn with fire. You shall not covet the silver or gold that is on them, nor take it for yourself, lest you be snared by it; for it is an abomination to Yahweh your *Elohim*.

[26] You shall not bring an abomination into your house, and so become devoted to destruction like it. You shall utterly detest it and utterly abhor it, for it is devoted to destruction.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"utterly destroy" → "devote to utter destruction"** (v2) — Hebrew *cherem / hacharem*.
- **"images" → "pillars"** (v5) — Hebrew מַצֵּבֹת *matzevot*. Standing cultic stones, not images.
- **"graven images" → "carved images"** (v5, 25) — as before.
- **"special people" → "treasured people"** (v6) — Hebrew סְגֻלָּה *segullah*. Personal/royal treasure.
- **"a mighty God and terrible" → "a great and fearsome El"** (v21) — Hebrew אֵל גָּדוֹל וְנוֹרָא *El gadol ve-nora*.
- **"mercy" → "lovingkindness"** (v9, 12) — *chesed*.
- **"temptations" → "trials"** (v19) — *massot*.
- **"cursed thing" → "devoted to destruction"** (v26) — Hebrew *cherem*. The abhorrent object/person consecrated to destruction.
- **"corn / wine / oil" → "grain / new wine / oil"** (v13) — Hebrew *dagan / tirosh / yitzhar*. The three Canaanite-agriculture triad staples.
- **"kine" → "herds"** (v13).

### Notes
- **Seven nations** (v1) — the classical list of pre-Israelite Canaan's inhabitants. Not exhaustive; compares with the six-nation list elsewhere (e.g., Ex 3:8 without Girgashites).
- **The *cherem* command** — the most ethically-challenging Deuteronomic command. Total destruction / devotion-to-Yahweh. Context: specific to the seven-nation land-clearing; safeguards Israel's monotheism; later texts show partial fulfillment / partial disobedience, and the practice is not universalized. Joshua's "ban" against Jericho (Josh 6–7).
- **"Treasured people" and "not because you were many"** (v6–8) — foundational doctrine of election by grace, not merit. Cited extensively in later Jewish and Christian theology.
- **"Thousand generations"** (v9) — maximal scope of Yahweh's covenantal faithfulness. The Decalogue's "thousandth generation" (5:10) is echoed here.
- **Little by little** (v22) — gradual rather than sudden dispossession. Pragmatic: sudden depopulation would leave wild animals to multiply. Ex 23:29–30 parallel.
- **Hornet** (v20) — possibly literal insect swarm (cf. Josh 24:12), or metaphorical for panic / plague. "Send the hornet" is a specific ancient-Near-Eastern idiom for divine military aid.
- **"Do not covet their silver"** (v25) — connects to Achan's sin at Jericho (Josh 7): taking devoted goods brings curse on the taker.
