# Deuteronomy 7

[1] "When ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ brings you to the land to which you are going in to possess it, and clears away many nations from before you, the ⟦hittites|Chitti|Hittite⟧ and the ⟦girgashites|Girgashi|Girgashite⟧ and the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧ and the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧ and the ⟦perizzites|Perizzi|Perizzite⟧ and the ⟦hivites|Chivvi|Hivite⟧ and the ⟦jebusites|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧, seven nations more numerous and mightier than you,

[2] and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ sets them before you, and you strike them, you shall surely devote them to destruction. You shall not cut a covenant with them, and you shall not show them favor.

[3] And you shall not intermarry with them: your daughter you shall not give to his son, and his daughter you shall not take for your son.

[4] For he will turn your son aside from after me, and they will serve other gods, and the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will burn against you, and he will destroy you quickly.

[5] But thus you shall do to them: their altars you shall break down, and their pillars you shall break in pieces, and their Asherim you shall hew down, and their carved images you shall burn with fire.

[6] For a holy people you are to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; it is you that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has chosen to be to him a people of treasured possession, out of all the peoples that are on the face of the ground.

[7] Not because you were more than all the peoples did ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ long for you and choose you, for you are the fewest of all the peoples;

[8] but because of the love of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ for you, and because of his keeping the oath that he swore to your fathers, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ brought you out with a strong hand and ransomed you from the house of slaves, from the hand of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[9] And you shall know that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, he is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the faithful ⟦el|El|God⟧, keeping the covenant and the ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ for those who love him and keep his commandments, to a thousand generations,

[10] and repaying those who hate him to his face, to make him perish; he will not delay with the one who hates him: to his face he will repay him.

[11] And you shall keep the commandment and the statutes and the ordinances which I am commanding you today, to do them.

[12] And it shall be, because you hear these ordinances and keep and do them, that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will keep for you the covenant and the ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ that he swore to your fathers.

[13] And he will love you and bless you and multiply you, and he will bless the fruit of your belly and the fruit of your ground, your grain and your new wine and your fresh oil, the offspring of your cattle and the young of your flock, upon the ground that he swore to your fathers to give you.

[14] Blessed you shall be above all the peoples; there shall not be among you a barren male or a barren female, or among your beasts.

[15] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will remove from you all sickness; and all the evil diseases of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ that you have known he will not put on you, and he will place them on all who hate you.

[16] And you shall consume all the peoples that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you; your eye shall not pity them, and you shall not serve their gods, for that is a snare to you.

[17] If you say in your heart, 'These nations are more numerous than I; how can I dispossess them?',

[18] you shall not fear them; you shall surely remember what ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ did to ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ and to all ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,

[19] the great testings that your eyes saw, and the signs and the wonders and the strong hand and the outstretched arm with which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ brought you out; so will ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ do to all the peoples before whom you are afraid.

[20] And also the hornet ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will send among them, until those who are left and those who are concealed from before you perish.

[21] You shall not be terrified because of them, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is in your midst, a great and fearsome ⟦el|El|God⟧.

[22] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will clear away these nations from before you little by little; you will not be able to make an end of them quickly, lest the living things of the field multiply against you.

[23] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will set them before you, and he will throw them into a great confusion, until they are destroyed.

[24] And he will give their kings into your hand, and you shall make their name perish from under the heavens; no man shall take his stand before you, until you have destroyed them.

[25] The carved images of their gods you shall burn with fire; you shall not covet the silver and the gold on them and take it for yourself, lest you be snared by it, for it is an abomination to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[26] And you shall not bring an abomination into your house, and become a thing devoted to destruction like it; you shall surely detest it and surely abominate it, for it is a thing devoted to destruction.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; "gods" in v4, 16, 25.
- **"You"** — singular in the Hebrew, except the plural in v4 ("against you"), v5, v7–8a ("you were more … brought you out"), v12a ("you hear … keep and do") and v25a ("you shall burn"). English cannot show it; the Czech text does.
- **"shall bring thee into the land whither thou goest to possess it, and hath cast out many nations before thee" → "brings you to the land to which you are going in to possess it, and clears away many nations from before you"** (v1) — Hebrew בָא "going in, coming"; נָשַׁל (so v22) is "slip off, drop off" (of a sandal, Exod 3:5; of olives, Deut 28:40), here "make drop away, clear away". **"the Hittites …" → "the Hittite …"** — each name is a singular with the article in the Hebrew, joined by "and". **"greater and mightier than thou" → "more numerous and mightier than you"** — Hebrew רַבִּים וַעֲצוּמִים.
- **"shall deliver them before thee; thou shalt smite them, and utterly destroy them" → "sets them before you, and you strike them, you shall surely devote them to destruction"** (v2) — Hebrew וּנְתָנָם … לְפָנֶיךָ, literally "gives them before your face" (so v23; the phrase of 1:8 "I have set the land before you"); הִכָּה "strike". הַחֲרֵם תַּחֲרִים is the infinitive absolute with its verb ("devoting you shall devote"), from חֵרֶם *cherem* (v26): to give over to Yahweh beyond recall, which here means to destroy and keep nothing (Exod 22:20; Lev 27:28–29; Num 21:2). **"make no covenant" → "not cut a covenant"** — Hebrew כָּרַת. **"nor shew mercy unto them" → "you shall not show them favor"** — Hebrew תְחָנֵּם, from חָנַן "show favor" (the verb of חֵן "favor").
- **"Neither shalt thou make marriages with them" → "And you shall not intermarry with them"** (v3) — Hebrew הִתְחַתֵּן, "make oneself son-in-law / father-in-law to".
- **"For they will turn away thy son from following me, that they may serve other gods" → "For he will turn your son aside from after me, and they will serve other gods"** (v4) — Hebrew יָסִיר "he will turn aside" (singular: the father-in-law of v3, or the nation as one); מֵאַחֲרַי "from after me". "Me" is Yahweh: Moses' speech passes into the words of Yahweh. **"be kindled against you" → "burn against you"** — Hebrew וְחָרָה אַף, literally "the nose will burn". **"suddenly" → "quickly"** — Hebrew מַהֵר.
- **"ye shall destroy their altars, and break down their images, and cut down their groves, and burn their graven images" → "their altars you shall break down, and their pillars you shall break in pieces, and their Asherim you shall hew down, and their carved images you shall burn"** (v5) — the wording of Exod 34:13 and Deut 12:3 (נָתַץ "break down", as Lev 14:45; שִׁבֵּר "break in pieces": two verbs), with גִּדַּע "hew down" where Exodus has "cut down". מַצֵּבָה is a stone set up, "pillar"; אֲשֵׁירִם *Asherim* are wooden objects of worship that bear the name of the goddess Asherah (not "groves"); פְּסִילִים "carved images" (5:8). Each object stands first in the Hebrew.
- **"an holy people unto the LORD" → "a holy people … to Yahweh"** (v6); **"to be a special people unto himself, above all people that are upon the face of the earth" → "to be to him a people of treasured possession, out of all the peoples that are on the face of the ground"** — Hebrew לִהְיוֹת לוֹ לְעַם סְגֻלָּה. סְגֻלָּה *segullah* is a king's own private treasure (Exod 19:5 "a treasured possession"; 1 Chr 29:3); here it is joined to "people". מִכֹּל "out of all" (or "more than all"); הָאֲדָמָה "the ground". "You" stands first: **"the LORD thy God hath chosen thee" → "it is you that Yahweh your Elohim has chosen"**. The sentence returns in 14:2 and is worded the same there.
- **"The LORD did not set his love upon you, nor choose you, because ye were more in number than any people" → "Not because you were more than all the peoples did Yahweh long for you and choose you"** (v7) — Hebrew לֹא מֵרֻבְּכֶם מִכָּל־הָעַמִּים, literally "not from your being many beyond all the peoples"; חָשַׁק בְּ is "be bound to, cling to in love, long for" (Gen 34:8). **"for ye were the fewest" → "for you are the fewest"** — Hebrew הַמְעַט "the few, the little" (no verb).
- **"But because the LORD loved you, and because he would keep the oath" → "but because of the love of Yahweh for you, and because of his keeping the oath"** (v8) — Hebrew מֵאַהֲבַת יְהוָה אֶתְכֶם וּמִשָּׁמְרוּ, two nouns of action with מִן "from". **"redeemed you out of the house of bondmen" → "ransomed you from the house of slaves"** — Hebrew פָּדָה "ransom" (not גָּאַל "redeem"); בֵּית עֲבָדִים as 5:6. "Ransomed you" is singular in the Hebrew.
- **"Know therefore that the LORD thy God, he is God, the faithful God" → "And you shall know that Yahweh your Elohim, he is Elohim, the faithful El"** (v9) — Hebrew וְיָדַעְתָּ; הוּא הָאֱלֹהִים, with the article, "he is the Elohim"; הָאֵל הַנֶּאֱמָן "the El who is faithful", sure, to be trusted (the root of "believe" and of *amen*).
- **"which keepeth covenant and mercy with them that love him" → "keeping the covenant and the chesed for those who love him"** (v9) — Hebrew שֹׁמֵר הַבְּרִית וְהַחֶסֶד, both with the article; חֶסֶד *chesed* "steadfast love". לְאֹהֲבָיו "for / to those who love him", as 5:10. **"to a thousand generations"** kept — Hebrew לְאֶלֶף דּוֹר, "to a thousand generation(s)", exactly so; the phrase stands at the end of the verse. The written text has מצותו (it can be read "his commandment"); the reading tradition has מִצְוֺתָיו "his commandments", which the verse follows.
- **"And repayeth them that hate him to their face, to destroy them: he will not be slack to him that hateth him" → "and repaying those who hate him to his face, to make him perish; he will not delay with the one who hates him"** (v10) — Hebrew וּמְשַׁלֵּם לְשֹׂנְאָיו אֶל־פָּנָיו לְהַאֲבִידוֹ: "those who hate him" is plural, then "to his face, to make him perish" is singular — each of them, in his own person. The words do not say when. הֶאֱבִיד "make perish" (so v24); אֵחַר "delay".
- **"Thou shalt therefore keep the commandments" → "And you shall keep the commandment"** (v11) — Hebrew הַמִּצְוָה, singular (6:1); "therefore" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Wherefore it shall come to pass, if ye hearken to these judgments" → "And it shall be, because you hear these ordinances"** (v12) — Hebrew עֵקֶב "as a consequence of, because" (literally "heel": what follows on); שָׁמַע "hear". **"shall keep unto thee the covenant and the mercy" → "will keep for you the covenant and the chesed"** — as v9.
- **"the fruit of thy womb, and the fruit of thy land, thy corn, and thy wine, and thine oil, the increase of thy kine, and the flocks of thy sheep, in the land" → "the fruit of your belly and the fruit of your ground, your grain and your new wine and your fresh oil, the offspring of your cattle and the young of your flock, upon the ground"** (v13) — Hebrew בֶּטֶן "belly"; אֲדָמָה "ground" (twice); דָּגָן, תִּירֹשׁ, יִצְהָר. שְׁגַר־אֲלָפֶיךָ: שֶׁגֶר is "what is dropped", the offspring of a beast (Exod 13:12); אֲלָפִים is a rarer word for cattle. עַשְׁתְּרֹת צֹאנֶךָ is found only in Deuteronomy (28:4, 18, 51): the word has the form of the plural of the name Ashtoreth (Astarte); it is taken as "the young", "the increase" or "the ewes" of the flock. The meaning is not certain.
- **"there shall not be male or female barren among you, or among your cattle" → "there shall not be among you a barren male or a barren female, or among your beasts"** (v14) — Hebrew עָקָר וַעֲקָרָה, the same word in the masculine and the feminine; בְּהֵמָה "beasts".
- **"will take away from thee all sickness, and will put none of the evil diseases of Egypt, which thou knowest, upon thee; but will lay them upon all them that hate thee" → "will remove from you all sickness; and all the evil diseases of Egypt that you have known he will not put on you, and he will place them on all who hate you"** (v15) — Hebrew הֵסִיר "remove"; שִׂים "put" and נָתַן "give, place"; וְ "and".
- **"thou shalt consume all the people which the LORD thy God shall deliver thee" → "you shall consume all the peoples that Yahweh your Elohim is giving to you"** (v16) — Hebrew וְאָכַלְתָּ, literally "you shall eat"; נֹתֵן לָךְ "is giving to you". **"thine eye shall have no pity upon them" → "your eye shall not pity them"** — Hebrew לֹא־תָחֹס עֵינְךָ. **"for that will be a snare unto thee" → "for that is a snare to you"**.
- **"Thou shalt not be afraid of them: but shalt well remember" → "you shall not fear them; you shall surely remember"** (v18) — Hebrew זָכֹר תִּזְכֹּר, the infinitive absolute with its verb.
- **"The great temptations" → "the great testings"** (v19) — Hebrew הַמַּסֹּת, from נִסָּה "test" (6:16), the plural of the word *Massah*. **"the mighty hand, and the stretched out arm, whereby" → "the strong hand and the outstretched arm with which"** — "with" is supplied; the Hebrew has only אֲשֶׁר. **"of whom thou art afraid" → "before whom you are afraid"** — Hebrew יָרֵא מִפְּנֵיהֶם, literally "afraid from their face".
- **"Moreover the LORD thy God will send the hornet among them, until they that are left, and hide themselves from thee, be destroyed" → "And also the hornet Yahweh your Elohim will send among them, until those who are left and those who are concealed from before you perish"** (v20) — Hebrew הַצִּרְעָה, with the article (Exod 23:28; Josh 24:12): commonly "the hornet"; others take it as "dread, dejection". The meaning is not certain. אָבַד "perish".
- **"Thou shalt not be affrighted at them" → "You shall not be terrified because of them"** (v21) — Hebrew לֹא תַעֲרֹץ מִפְּנֵיהֶם. **"a mighty God and terrible" → "a great and fearsome El"** — Hebrew אֵל גָּדוֹל וְנוֹרָא; נוֹרָא is "to be feared", from יָרֵא "fear" (v18, 19).
- **"will put out those nations before thee by little and little: thou mayest not consume them at once, lest the beasts of the field increase upon thee" → "will clear away these nations from before you little by little; you will not be able to make an end of them quickly, lest the living things of the field multiply against you"** (v22) — Hebrew נָשַׁל as v1; מְעַט מְעָט "little, little"; כִּלָּה "make an end of"; מַהֵר "quickly" (v4); חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה as Exod 23:29.
- **"shall deliver them unto thee, and shall destroy them with a mighty destruction" → "will set them before you, and he will throw them into a great confusion"** (v23) — Hebrew נָתַן לִפְנֵי "set before" (v2); וְהָמָם מְהוּמָה גְדֹלָה, verb and noun of one root: "confuse them with a great confusion" (Exod 23:27).
- **"thou shalt destroy their name from under heaven: there shall no man be able to stand before thee" → "you shall make their name perish from under the heavens; no man shall take his stand before you"** (v24) — Hebrew הֶאֱבִיד; לֹא־יִתְיַצֵּב אִישׁ בְּפָנֶיךָ, "in your face", against you.
- **"thou shalt not desire the silver or gold that is on them, nor take it unto thee" → "you shall not covet the silver and the gold on them and take it for yourself"** (v25) — Hebrew לֹא־תַחְמֹד, the verb of 5:21; one "not" governs both verbs. **"an abomination to the LORD"** — Hebrew תוֹעֲבַת יְהוָה, literally "an abomination of Yahweh".
- **"lest thou be a cursed thing like it: but thou shalt utterly detest it, and thou shalt utterly abhor it; for it is a cursed thing" → "and become a thing devoted to destruction like it; you shall surely detest it and surely abominate it, for it is a thing devoted to destruction"** (v26) — Hebrew חֵרֶם *cherem*, the noun of the verb of v2: a thing given over to Yahweh beyond recall, here for destruction; whoever takes it becomes what it is (Josh 6:18; 7:12). "Cursed" is another word. וְהָיִיתָ "and you become" ("lest" is not in the Hebrew). שַׁקֵּץ תְּשַׁקְּצֶנּוּ וְתַעֵב תְּתַעֲבֶנּוּ: two infinitives absolute with their verbs; שִׁקֵּץ "detest" (Lev 11:11); תִּעֵב is the verb of תּוֹעֵבָה "abomination".

### Notes
- **Seven nations** (v1) — the fullest list of the peoples of Canaan; elsewhere six are named, without the Girgashite (Exod 3:8; 34:11).
- ***Cherem*** (v2, 26) — the word names what is withdrawn from all human use and given over to Yahweh. In this chapter it is said of the seven nations of the land and of the images of their gods; the reason given is that Israel would be turned aside to serve other gods (v4, 16). Josh 6–7 tells of Jericho and of Achan, who took of the devoted things.
- **"Not because you were more"** (v6–8) — the choosing rests on the love of Yahweh and on his oath to the fathers, not on Israel's greatness.
- **"To a thousand generations"** (v9) — 5:10 (Exod 20:6) has only "to thousands"; this verse has the word "generation".
- **Little by little** (v22) — as Exod 23:29–30.
- **The hornet** (v20) — Exod 23:28; Josh 24:12.
- **"You shall not covet the silver and the gold"** (v25) — cf. Achan, Josh 7:21 ("I coveted them").
