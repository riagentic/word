## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"ten commandments" → "the ten words"** (v13) — Hebrew עֲשֶׂרֶת הַדְּבָרִים *aseret ha-devarim*. The "Decalogue" (Greek: "ten words"); Hebrew says "words" not "commandments."
- **"similitude" → "form"** (v12, 15, 16, 23, 25) — Hebrew תְּמוּנָה *temunah*. Visual likeness / shape.
- **"graven image" → "carved image"** (v16, 23, 25) — Hebrew פֶּסֶל *pesel*.
- **"consuming fire, a jealous God" → "consuming fire, a jealous El"** (v24) — Hebrew אֵל קַנָּא *El qanna*.
- **"a merciful God" → "a compassionate El"** (v31) — Hebrew אֵל רַחוּם *El rachum*.
- **"heathen" → "nations"** (v27) — *goyim*.
- **"assayed" → "attempted"** (v34) — modernized.
- **"Sion" (v48)** — Hebrew שִׂיאֹן *Si'on*. Distinct from Zion (ציון). Alternate name for Mt. Hermon.
- **"divided unto all nations" → "apportioned to all the peoples"** (v19) — Hebrew חָלַק *chalaq*. Yahweh has "given" the heavenly bodies to other nations — astral objects are permitted to them but forbidden to Israel. Compare Deut 32:8 (Yahweh apportions nations).

### Notes
- **Transition chapter** — ends the historical prologue (chs 1–3) and begins the hortatory sermon proper. Key theme: obedience, memory, image-less worship.
- **"No form"** (v12, 15) — foundation for aniconic worship. Hearing but no seeing; Israel knows Yahweh by voice, not by visual representation. Anchors the second commandment.
- **Astral deities** (v19) — the sun/moon/stars "apportioned" to other nations. A striking concession: other peoples have astral patrons; Israel's vocation is to worship only Yahweh directly.
- **"Iron furnace" of Egypt** (v20) — striking metaphor. Repeated 1 Kgs 8:51, Jer 11:4.
- **Scattering and return** (v27–31) — the *shema-paradigm*: failure → exile → seeking → return. Programmatic for later prophets.
- **"Yahweh — he is *Elohim*, there is no other"** (v35, 39) — monotheistic declaration. Echoed in 1 Kgs 18:39 (Elijah at Carmel).
- **Three eastern cities of refuge** (v41–43) — fulfillment of Num 35:14 on the eastern side. The western three will be set aside in Josh 20.
- **"Torah"** (v44) — first clear use of *torah* as a term for the Mosaic instruction-book about to be unfolded.
