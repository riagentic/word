# Deuteronomy 4

[1] "Now therefore, O Israel, listen to the statutes and the ordinances that I am teaching you to do, that you may live and go in and take possession of the land that Yahweh the *Elohim* of your fathers is giving you.

[2] You shall not add to the word that I command you, nor shall you take away from it, that you may keep the commandments of Yahweh your *Elohim* that I am commanding you.

[3] Your eyes have seen what Yahweh did at Baal-peor; for every man who followed Baal of Peor, Yahweh your *Elohim* destroyed from among you.

[4] But you who held fast to Yahweh your *Elohim* are all alive today.

[5] Behold, I have taught you statutes and ordinances, as Yahweh my *Elohim* commanded me, that you should do so in the land that you are entering to possess.

[6] Keep them and do them, for that is your wisdom and your understanding in the sight of the peoples, who will hear all these statutes and say, 'Surely this great nation is a wise and understanding people.'

[7] For what great nation has a god so near to it as Yahweh our *Elohim* is to us, whenever we call upon him?

[8] And what great nation has statutes and ordinances so righteous as all this law that I set before you today?

[9] Only take care and watch yourself closely, lest you forget the things that your eyes have seen, and lest they depart from your heart all the days of your life. Make them known to your children and to your children's children —

[10] the day when you stood before Yahweh your *Elohim* at Horeb, when Yahweh said to me, 'Assemble the people to me, that I may make them hear my words, that they may learn to fear me all the days that they live on the earth, and that they may teach their children.'

[11] You came near and stood at the foot of the mountain; and the mountain burned with fire to the heart of the heavens — darkness, cloud, and thick gloom.

[12] Yahweh spoke to you out of the midst of the fire. You heard the sound of words, but saw no form — only a voice.

[13] He declared to you his covenant, which he commanded you to perform — the ten words — and he wrote them on two tablets of stone.

[14] Yahweh commanded me at that time to teach you statutes and ordinances, that you might do them in the land that you are crossing over to possess.

[15] Watch yourselves very carefully — since you saw no form on the day Yahweh spoke to you at Horeb out of the midst of the fire —

[16] lest you corrupt yourselves and make for yourselves a carved image, the form of any figure, the likeness of male or female,

[17] the likeness of any animal that is on the earth, the likeness of any winged bird that flies in the air,

[18] the likeness of anything that creeps on the ground, the likeness of any fish that is in the waters beneath the earth.

[19] And lest you lift up your eyes to heaven and see the sun and the moon and the stars — all the host of heaven — and be drawn away and bow down to them and serve them — things that Yahweh your *Elohim* has apportioned to all the peoples under the whole heaven.

[20] But Yahweh has taken you and brought you out of the iron furnace, out of Egypt, to be a people of his inheritance, as you are today.

[21] Now Yahweh was angry with me on your account and swore that I should not cross over the Jordan and that I should not enter the good land that Yahweh your *Elohim* is giving you as an inheritance.

[22] For I must die in this land; I must not cross over the Jordan. But you are to cross over and take possession of that good land.

[23] Watch yourselves, lest you forget the covenant of Yahweh your *Elohim* that he made with you, and make for yourselves a carved image, the form of anything that Yahweh your *Elohim* has forbidden you.

[24] For Yahweh your *Elohim* is a consuming fire, a jealous *Elohim*.

[25] When you father children and grandchildren and have grown old in the land, and you corrupt yourselves by making a carved image in the form of anything, and do what is evil in the eyes of Yahweh your *Elohim* to provoke him to anger —

[26] I call heaven and earth to witness against you today that you will soon utterly perish from the land that you are crossing over the Jordan to possess; you will not prolong your days on it, but will be utterly destroyed.

[27] Yahweh will scatter you among the peoples, and you shall be left few in number among the nations to which Yahweh will drive you.

[28] There you will serve gods, the work of human hands — wood and stone — that neither see, nor hear, nor eat, nor smell.

[29] But from there you will seek Yahweh your *Elohim*, and you will find him, if you search after him with all your heart and with all your soul.

[30] When you are in tribulation and all these things come upon you, in the latter days, you will return to Yahweh your *Elohim* and listen to his voice;

[31] for Yahweh your *Elohim* is a compassionate *Elohim*. He will not abandon you or destroy you or forget the covenant with your fathers that he swore to them.

[32] For ask now about former days, which were before you, since the day that *Elohim* created humans on the earth — from one end of the heavens to the other — whether anything like this great thing has happened, or whether such has ever been heard.

[33] Did any people ever hear the voice of *Elohim* speaking out of the midst of fire, as you have heard, and live?

[34] Or has any god ever attempted to go and take for himself a nation from the midst of another nation — by trials, by signs, by wonders, by war, by a mighty hand, by an outstretched arm, and by great terrors — as Yahweh your *Elohim* did for you in Egypt, before your eyes?

[35] To you it was shown, that you might know that Yahweh — he is *Elohim*; there is no other besides him.

[36] Out of the heavens he made you hear his voice, to discipline you; and on the earth he showed you his great fire; and you heard his words out of the midst of the fire.

[37] And because he loved your fathers and chose their offspring after them, he himself brought you out of Egypt with his great power,

[38] driving out before you nations greater and mightier than you, to bring you in and to give you their land for an inheritance, as it is today.

[39] Know therefore today, and lay it to your heart, that Yahweh — he is *Elohim* in the heavens above and on the earth beneath; there is no other.

[40] Keep his statutes and his commandments, which I am commanding you today, that it may go well with you and with your children after you, and that you may prolong your days on the land that Yahweh your *Elohim* is giving you forever."

[41] Then Moses set apart three cities beyond the Jordan, toward the east,

[42] that the manslayer might flee there — one who killed his neighbor unintentionally and did not hate him in time past; he may flee to one of these cities and live:

[43] Bezer in the wilderness, in the plateau country, for the Reubenites; Ramoth in Gilead, for the Gadites; and Golan in Bashan, for the Manassites.

[44] This is the Torah that Moses set before the children of Israel.

[45] These are the testimonies, the statutes, and the ordinances that Moses spoke to the children of Israel after they came out of Egypt,

[46] beyond the Jordan in the valley opposite Beth-peor, in the land of Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived at Heshbon, whom Moses and the children of Israel struck down after they came out of Egypt.

[47] They took possession of his land, and the land of Og king of Bashan — the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, toward the east —

[48] from Aroer, which is on the edge of the wadi Arnon, as far as Mount Sion (that is, Hermon),

[49] with all the Arabah beyond the Jordan to the east, as far as the sea of the Arabah under the slopes of Pisgah.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"ten commandments" → "the ten words"** (v13) — Hebrew עֲשֶׂרֶת הַדְּבָרִים *aseret ha-devarim*. The "Decalogue" (Greek: "ten words"); Hebrew says "words" not "commandments."
- **"similitude" → "form"** (v12, 15, 16, 23, 25) — Hebrew תְּמוּנָה *temunah*. Visual likeness / shape.
- **"graven image" → "carved image"** (v16, 23, 25) — Hebrew פֶּסֶל *pesel*.
- **"consuming fire, a jealous God" → "consuming fire, a jealous El"** (v24) — Hebrew אֵל קַנָּא *El qanna*.
- **"heathen" → "nations"** (v27) — *goyim*.
- **"assayed" → "attempted"** (v34) — modernized.
- **"Sion" (v48)** — Hebrew שִׂיאֹן *Si'on*. Distinct from Zion (ציון). Alternate name for Mt. Hermon.
- **"divided unto all nations" → "apportioned to all the peoples"** (v19) — Hebrew חָלַק *chalaq*. Yahweh has "given" the heavenly bodies to other nations — astral objects are permitted to them but forbidden to Israel. Compare Deut 32:8 (Yahweh apportions nations).

### Notes
- **Transition chapter** — ends the historical prologue (chs 1–3) and begins the hortatory sermon proper. Key theme: obedience, memory, image-less worship.
- **"No form"** (v12, 15) — foundation for aniconic worship. Hearing but no seeing; Israel knows Yahweh by voice, not by visual representation. Anchors the second commandment.
- **Astral deities** (v19) — the sun/moon/stars "apportioned" to other nations. A striking concession: other peoples have astral patrons; Israel's vocation is to worship only Yahweh directly.
- **"Iron furnace" of Egypt** (v20) — striking metaphor. Repeated 1 Kgs 8:51, Jer 11:4.
- **Scattering and return** (v27–31) — the *shema-paradigm*: failure → exile → seeking → return. Programmatic for later prophets.
- **"Yahweh — he is *Elohim*, there is no other"** (v35, 39) — monotheistic declaration. Echoed in 1 Kgs 18:39 (Elijah at Carmel).
- **Three eastern cities of refuge** (v41–43) — fulfillment of Num 35:14 on the eastern side. The western three will be set aside in Josh 20.
- **"Torah"** (v44) — first clear use of *torah* as a term for the Mosaic instruction-book about to be unfolded.
