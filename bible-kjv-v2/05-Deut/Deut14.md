# Deuteronomy 14

[1] "You are the children of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. You shall not cut yourselves or make any bald patch between your eyes for the dead.

[2] For you are a holy people to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has chosen you to be a treasured people for himself, above all the peoples that are on the face of the earth.

[3] You shall not eat any detestable thing.

[4] These are the animals you may eat: the ox, the sheep, and the goat;

[5] the deer, the gazelle, the fallow deer, the ibex, the antelope, the wild ox, and the mountain sheep.

[6] Every animal that has a divided hoof — cleft in two — and chews the cud, among animals, you may eat.

[7] Yet these you shall not eat, among those that chew the cud or that have a cleft hoof: the camel, the hare, and the rock badger — because they chew the cud but do not part the hoof; they are unclean to you;

[8] and the pig, because it parts the hoof but does not chew the cud; it is unclean to you. You shall not eat their flesh, and you shall not touch their carcasses.

[9] These you may eat of all that are in the waters: whatever has fins and scales you may eat.

[10] Whatever does not have fins and scales you shall not eat; it is unclean to you.

[11] All clean birds you may eat.

[12] But these are they of which you shall not eat: the eagle, the bearded vulture, the black vulture,

[13] the red kite, the falcon, the kite of any kind,

[14] every raven of any kind,

[15] the ostrich, the nighthawk, the sea gull, the hawk of any kind,

[16] the little owl, the great owl, the white owl,

[17] the desert owl, the Egyptian vulture, the cormorant,

[18] the stork, the heron of any kind, the hoopoe, and the bat.

[19] All winged swarming creatures are unclean to you; they shall not be eaten.

[20] All clean birds you may eat.

[21] You shall not eat of anything that dies of itself. You may give it to the foreigner within your gates, that he may eat it; or you may sell it to a foreigner; for you are a holy people to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. You shall not boil a kid in its mother's milk.

[22] You shall tithe all the yield of your seed, which comes forth from the field year by year.

[23] You shall eat before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, in the place that he will choose to make his name dwell there, the tithe of your grain, your new wine, and your oil, and the firstborn of your herds and your flocks, that you may learn to fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ always.

[24] If the way is too long for you, so that you cannot carry it, when the place is too far from you that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will choose to set his name there, when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ blesses you,

[25] then you shall turn it into money and bind the money in your hand and go to the place that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will choose;

[26] and you shall spend the money for whatever your soul desires — for oxen, for sheep, for wine, for strong drink, for whatever your soul desires; and you shall eat there before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and you shall rejoice — you and your household,

[27] and the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧ who is within your gates; you shall not forsake him, for he has no portion or inheritance with you.

[28] At the end of every three years you shall bring out all the tithe of your yield in that year and store it within your gates;

[29] and the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧, because he has no portion or inheritance with you, and the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow who are within your gates shall come and eat and be filled, that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ may bless you in all the work of your hand that you do.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"peculiar people" → "treasured people"** (v2) — *segullah*, as in ch 7. *Peculiar* archaic = one's own / private.
- **Animal list** (v4–5) — modernized:
  - **"hart / roebuck / fallow deer" → "deer / gazelle / fallow deer"** — three cervids.
  - **"wild goat" → "ibex"** (Heb *aqqo*).
  - **"pygarg" → "antelope"** (Heb *dishon*) — uncertain; addax or oryx.
  - **"chamois" → "mountain sheep"** (Heb *zamer*) — uncertain.
- **"seethe a kid in his mother's milk" → "boil a kid in its mother's milk"** (v21) — archaic *seethe*. Basis for Jewish dairy-meat separation.
- **Bird list (v12–18)** — as in Lev 11. Modernized per best current zoological identifications.
- **"stranger" in v21 has two distinct senses** — Hebrew *ger* (foreigner residing within) vs. *nokhri* (passing foreigner). KJV uses "stranger / alien"; both translated "foreigner" here, but the distinction matters: a resident *ger* is under the covenant's protections (eat unclean meat), while a pure outsider *nokhri* can be sold to commercially.

### Notes
- **Parallel to Lev 11** — Deut 14 and Lev 11 largely overlap on dietary laws but with differences: Deut adds clean animals list (v4–5); Lev has more detail on swarming creatures.
- **"You are Yahweh's children"** (v1) — familial-election framing. The refusal of pagan mourning practices is about identity: Israel does not grieve like those without covenantal hope.
- **Kid in mother's milk** (v21) — three times in Torah (Ex 23:19, 34:26, here). Basis for the Jewish *kashrut* separation of meat and dairy; perhaps originally aimed against a specific Canaanite ritual.
- **Tithe for the Levite, alien, fatherless, widow** (v28–29) — the "third-year tithe" supports the dispossessed. Systematic social welfare through the tithe system.
- **Distance exemption** (v24–26) — if too far to bring the tithe in kind, convert to money, spend on the feast at the central place. Pragmatic accommodation.
- **Strong drink at the festival** (v26) — wine and *shekhar*. The pilgrimage-feast is explicitly joyous, not austere.
