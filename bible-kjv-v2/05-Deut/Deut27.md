# Deuteronomy 27

[1] Moses with the elders of Israel commanded the people, saying, "Keep the whole commandment that I am commanding you today.

[2] On the day you cross the Jordan into the land that Yahweh your *Elohim* is giving you, you shall set up for yourselves large stones and plaster them with plaster.

[3] You shall write on them all the words of this Torah when you have crossed over, so that you may go into the land that Yahweh your *Elohim* is giving you — a land flowing with milk and honey — as Yahweh the *Elohim* of your fathers has promised you.

[4] So when you have crossed the Jordan, you shall set up these stones, concerning which I am commanding you today, on Mount Ebal; and you shall plaster them with plaster.

[5] There you shall build an altar to Yahweh your *Elohim* — an altar of stones; you shall not wield any iron tool upon them.

[6] You shall build the altar of Yahweh your *Elohim* of uncut stones. You shall offer on it burnt offerings to Yahweh your *Elohim*,

[7] and you shall offer peace offerings and eat there, and rejoice before Yahweh your *Elohim*.

[8] You shall write on the stones all the words of this Torah very plainly."

[9] Then Moses and the levitical priests spoke to all Israel, saying, "Be silent and listen, O Israel! This very day you have become the people of Yahweh your *Elohim*.

[10] You shall therefore obey the voice of Yahweh your *Elohim* and do his commandments and his statutes, which I am commanding you today."

[11] Moses charged the people on that day, saying,

[12] "These shall stand on Mount Gerizim to bless the people when you have crossed the Jordan: Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph, and Benjamin.

[13] And these shall stand on Mount Ebal for the curse: Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali.

[14] The Levites shall answer and say to all the men of Israel with a loud voice:

[15] 'Cursed be the man who makes a carved or cast image, an abomination to Yahweh, the work of a craftsman's hands, and sets it up in secret.' And all the people shall answer and say, 'Amen.'

[16] 'Cursed be he who dishonors his father or his mother.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[17] 'Cursed be he who moves his neighbor's boundary marker.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[18] 'Cursed be he who misleads the blind on the way.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[19] 'Cursed be he who perverts justice due to the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[20] 'Cursed be he who lies with his father's wife, because he has uncovered his father's skirt.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[21] 'Cursed be he who lies with any animal.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[22] 'Cursed be he who lies with his sister, the daughter of his father or the daughter of his mother.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[23] 'Cursed be he who lies with his mother-in-law.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[24] 'Cursed be he who strikes down his neighbor in secret.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[25] 'Cursed be he who takes a bribe to strike down an innocent person.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[26] 'Cursed be anyone who does not confirm the words of this Torah by doing them.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"plaister / plaister them with plaister" → "plaster / plaster them with plaster"** (v2, 4) — modernized spelling.
- **"this law" → "this Torah"** (v3, 8, 26).
- **"graven or molten image" → "carved or cast image"** (v15) — *pesel u-massekhah*.
- **"setteth light by" → "dishonors"** (v16) — Hebrew מַקְלֶה *maqleh* = treats lightly / with contempt.
- **"landmark" → "boundary marker"** (v17).
- **"maketh the blind to wander" → "misleads the blind"** (v18) — modernized.
- **"lieth with" (v20–23)** — sexual idiom.
- **"smiteth his neighbour secretly" → "strikes down his neighbor in secret"** (v24).
- **"taketh reward to slay" → "takes a bribe to strike down"** (v25).

### Notes
- **Ceremony at Gerizim / Ebal** (v1–13) — foundational covenant ceremony to take place upon entering the land. Mountains flanking Shechem: Gerizim (south, blessing) and Ebal (north, curse). Joshua 8:30–35 records its performance.
- **Altar on Mount Ebal** (v5–7) — on the curse mountain, an altar of uncut stones. Offerings made there before the covenant ceremony: acknowledgment that Israel deserves the curse, yet approaches via sacrifice.
- **Iron-tool prohibition on altar stones** (v5) — echoes Ex 20:25. Rationale: iron = weapons; the altar is a place of peace-making, not violence.
- **Twelve curses** (v15–26) — introduced as "cursed" with congregational "Amen":
  1. Idol-making
  2. Dishonoring parents
  3. Moving boundaries
  4. Misleading the blind
  5. Perverting justice for vulnerable
  6–9. Sexual transgressions (father's wife, animals, sister, mother-in-law)
  10. Secret murder
  11. Taking bribe for murder
  12. General disobedience
- **Hidden/public distinction** — several curses specifically target secret sins (v15 "in secret," v24 "in secret"). The curse-ceremony explicitly addresses what human courts cannot prosecute.
- **"All the words of this Torah"** (v26) — Paul quotes at Gal 3:10 to argue for universal need of Christ's redemption from the curse of the law.
