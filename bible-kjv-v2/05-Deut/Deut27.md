## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"plaister / plaister them with plaister" → "plaster / plaster them with plaster"** (v2, 4) — modernized spelling.
- **"this law" → "this Torah"** (v3, 8, 26).
- **"graven or molten image" → "carved or cast image"** (v15) — *pesel u-massekhah*.
- **"setteth light by" → "dishonors"** (v16) — Hebrew מַקְלֶה *maqleh* = treats lightly / with contempt.
- **"landmark" → "boundary marker"** (v17).
- **"maketh the blind to wander" → "misleads the blind"** (v18) — modernized.
- **"lieth with" (v20–23)** — sexual idiom.
- **"smiteth his neighbour secretly" → "strikes down his neighbor in secret"** (v24).
- **"taketh reward to slay" → "takes a bribe to strike down"** (v25).

### Notes
- **Ceremony at Gerizim / Ebal** (v1–13) — foundational covenant ceremony to take place upon entering the land. Mountains flanking Shechem: Gerizim (south, blessing) and Ebal (north, curse). Joshua 8:30–35 records its performance.
- **Altar on Mount Ebal** (v5–7) — on the curse mountain, an altar of uncut stones. Offerings made there before the covenant ceremony: acknowledgment that Israel deserves the curse, yet approaches via sacrifice.
- **Iron-tool prohibition on altar stones** (v5) — echoes Ex 20:25. Rationale: iron = weapons; the altar is a place of peace-making, not violence.
- **Twelve curses** (v15–26) — introduced as "cursed" with congregational "Amen":
  1. Idol-making
  2. Dishonoring parents
  3. Moving boundaries
  4. Misleading the blind
  5. Perverting justice for vulnerable
  6–9. Sexual transgressions (father's wife, animals, sister, mother-in-law)
  10. Secret murder
  11. Taking bribe for murder
  12. General disobedience
- **Hidden/public distinction** — several curses specifically target secret sins (v15 "in secret," v24 "in secret"). The curse-ceremony explicitly addresses what human courts cannot prosecute.
- **"All the words of this Torah"** (v26) — Paul quotes at Gal 3:10 to argue for universal need of Christ's redemption from the curse of the law.
