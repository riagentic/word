## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Name of book** — Hebrew דְּבָרִים *Devarim* = "Words," taken from v1. English "Deuteronomy" from LXX *Deuteronomion* = "second law" (a misreading of 17:18; actually a restatement, not a second separate code).
- **"the plain" → "the Arabah"** (v1, 7) — Hebrew הָעֲרָבָה *ha-Arabah*. The rift-valley region from Sea of Galilee through the Dead Sea to the Red Sea. Proper name.
- **"vale" → "Shephelah"** (v7) — Hebrew הַשְּׁפֵלָה *ha-Shephelah*. The lowlands / foothills between the Judean hills and the coast.
- **"south" → "Negev"** (v7) — Hebrew נֶגֶב *Negev*. Proper name.
- **"declare this law" → "expound this Torah"** (v5) — Hebrew בֵּאֵר אֶת הַתּוֹרָה *be'er et ha-Torah*. To explain / make clear the Torah.
- **"cumbrance" → "burden"** (v12) — archaic.
- **"respect persons" → "show partiality"** (v17).
- **"terrible" → "fearsome"** (v19) — Hebrew נוֹרָא *nora*. Awe-inspiring, not morally terrible.
- **"discouraged our heart" → "melted our hearts"** (v28) — Hebrew הֵמַסּוּ אֶת לְבָבֵנוּ *hemassu et levavenu*.
- **"Red sea" → "Sea of Reeds"** (v40).

### Notes
- **Deuteronomy's opening** — sets the scene: Moses stands east of the Jordan, at the threshold. The book is his farewell address(es).
- **Timeline** (v3) — 11th month of year 40. Moses has about one month to speak before he dies (Deut 34:8; 30 days' mourning; Josh 4:19 = first month of year 41).
- **"Expound this Torah"** (v5) — the book is self-consciously an *exposition* of prior law, not a new corpus. Deuteronomy = deuteros nomos, "second law" / restatement.
- **Structure of ch 1–3** — historical recapitulation: from Horeb to the Jordan. Covers Exod 18 (judges), Num 13–14 (scouts), Num 21 (Transjordan).
- **"Today" rhetoric** — Deut addresses the new generation as if they were the Kadesh-barnea generation ("*you* rebelled"). Generational identity is collective; Israel's history is the present audience's own.
- **Moses' fault shifted to the people** (v37) — "Yahweh was angry with me *for your sake*." Num 20 blamed Moses directly; Deut frames it as collective consequence.
- **11 days' journey** (v2) — Horeb to Kadesh-barnea is short. The 40-year detour is emphasized: what should have taken 11 days took a generation.
