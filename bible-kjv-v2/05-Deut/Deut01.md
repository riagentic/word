# Deuteronomy 1

[1] These are the words that ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ spoke to all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, in the wilderness, in the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧, opposite ⟦suph|Suf|Suph⟧, between Paran and ⟦tophel|Tofel|Tophel⟧ and ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ and ⟦hazeroth|Chatzerot|Hazeroth⟧ and ⟦di-zahab|Di Zahav|Di-zahab⟧.

[2] It is eleven days from ⟦horeb|Chorev|Horeb⟧, by the way of the hill country of Seir, as far as ⟦kadesh-barnea|Qadesh Barnea|Kadesh-barnea⟧.

[3] And it came to pass in the fortieth year, in the eleventh month, on the first of the month, that ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ spoke to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ according to all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded him for them,

[4] after he had struck down ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧ king of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧, who dwelt in ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧, and Og king of the Bashan, who dwelt in ⟦ashtaroth|Ashtarot|Ashtaroth⟧, in Edrei.

[5] Across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, in the land of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ undertook to make plain this ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, saying:

[6] "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ spoke to us at ⟦horeb|Chorev|Horeb⟧, saying, 'Enough for you is the dwelling at this mountain.

[7] Turn and set out for yourselves, and go to the hill country of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧ and to all his neighbors, in the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧, in the hill country and in the ⟦shephelah|Shefelah|Shephelah⟧ and in the ⟦negev|Negev|Negeb⟧ and on the shore of the sea, the land of the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧, and ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧, as far as the great river, the river ⟦euphrates|Perat|Euphrates⟧.

[8] See, I have set the land before you. Go in and take possession of the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ swore to your fathers, to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, to ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ and to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, to give to them and to their seed after them.'

[9] And I said to you at that time, saying, 'I am not able alone to bear you.

[10] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has multiplied you, and behold, you are today like the stars of the heavens for multitude.

[11] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of your fathers, add to you a thousand times as many as you are, and may he bless you, as he has spoken to you!

[12] How shall I bear alone your load and your burden and your contention?

[13] Provide for yourselves men, wise and understanding and known, by your tribes, and I will set them as your heads.'

[14] And you answered me and said, 'Good is the thing that you have spoken, to do.'

[15] And I took the heads of your tribes, men wise and known, and I placed them as heads over you, chiefs of thousands and chiefs of hundreds and chiefs of fifties and chiefs of tens, and officers, by your tribes.

[16] And I commanded your judges at that time, saying, 'Hear between your brothers, and you shall judge righteously between a man and his brother and his sojourner.

[17] You shall not recognize faces in judgment; the small as the great you shall hear. You shall not be afraid of the face of a man, for the judgment is ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧. And the matter that is too hard for you, you shall bring near to me, and I will hear it.'

[18] And I commanded you at that time all the things that you should do.

[19] And we set out from ⟦horeb|Chorev|Horeb⟧ and went through all that great and fearsome wilderness that you saw, by the way of the hill country of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ commanded us; and we came as far as ⟦kadesh-barnea|Qadesh Barnea|Kadesh-barnea⟧.

[20] And I said to you, 'You have come as far as the hill country of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧, which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to us.

[21] See, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has set the land before you. Go up, take possession, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of your fathers, has spoken to you. Do not fear and do not be dismayed.'

[22] And you came near to me, all of you, and said, 'Let us send men before us, and let them search out the land for us and bring back word to us: the way by which we shall go up, and the cities to which we shall come.'

[23] And the thing was good in my eyes, and I took from you twelve men, one man for each tribe.

[24] And they turned and went up into the hill country and came as far as the wadi of ⟦eshcol|Eshkol|Eshcol⟧, and they spied it out.

[25] And they took in their hand of the fruit of the land and brought it down to us, and they brought back word to us and said, 'Good is the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to us.'

[26] And you were not willing to go up, and you rebelled against the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[27] And you murmured in your tents and said, 'In the hatred of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ for us he brought us out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, to give us into the hand of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧, to destroy us.

[28] Where are we going up? Our brothers have made our heart melt, saying, "A people greater and taller than we; cities great and fortified in the heavens; and also sons of ⟦anakim|Anaqim|Anakim⟧ we saw there."'

[29] And I said to you, 'You shall not be terrified, and you shall not fear them.

[30] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who goes before you, he will fight for you, according to all that he did with you in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ before your eyes,

[31] and in the wilderness, where you saw how ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ bore you, as a man bears his son, in all the way that you went, until you came to this place.

[32] And in this thing you are not believing in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[33] who goes before you on the way to seek out for you a place for your camping, in the fire by night, to show you the way in which you should go, and in the cloud by day.'

[34] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ heard the voice of your words, and he was wrathful, and he swore, saying,

[35] 'Not a man among these men, this evil generation, shall see the good land that I swore to give to your fathers,

[36] except ⟦caleb|Kalev|Caleb⟧ the son of ⟦jephunneh|Yefunneh|Jephunneh⟧; he shall see it, and to him I will give the land on which he has trodden, and to his sons, because he has followed fully after ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.'

[37] Also with me ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was angry because of you, saying, 'You also shall not go in there.

[38] ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ the son of Nun, who stands before you, he shall go in there. Make him strong, for he shall make ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ inherit it.

[39] And your little ones, of whom you said, "They will be for plunder," and your sons, who today have not known good and evil, they shall go in there, and to them I will give it, and they shall take possession of it.

[40] And you, turn for yourselves and set out into the wilderness, by the way of the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧.'

[41] And you answered and said to me, 'We have sinned against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. We ourselves will go up and fight, according to all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ commanded us.' And you girded on, each man his weapons of war, and you thought it a light thing to go up into the hill country.

[42] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, 'Say to them, "You shall not go up and you shall not fight, for I am not in your midst; and you shall not be smitten before your enemies."'

[43] And I spoke to you, and you did not hear; and you rebelled against the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and acted presumptuously and went up into the hill country.

[44] And the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧ who dwelt in that hill country came out to meet you, and they pursued you as the bees do, and they beat you in pieces in Seir, as far as ⟦hormah|Chormah|Hormah⟧.

[45] And you returned and wept before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ did not listen to your voice, and he did not give ear to you.

[46] And you dwelt in ⟦kadesh|Qadesh|Kadesh⟧ many days, according to the days that you dwelt.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Name of book** — Hebrew דְּבָרִים *Devarim* = "Words," taken from v1. English "Deuteronomy" from LXX *Deuteronomion* = "second law" (a misreading of 17:18; actually a restatement, not a second separate code).
- **"on this side Jordan" → "across the Jordan"** (v1, 5) — Hebrew בְּעֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן, "on the other side of the Jordan". Here it is the east side, where Moses speaks; in 3:20, 25 the same words are the west side. The words themselves do not say which.
- **"the plain" → "the Arabah"** (v1, 7) — Hebrew הָעֲרָבָה *ha-Arabah*. The rift-valley region from Sea of Galilee through the Dead Sea to the Red Sea. Proper name.
- **"over against the Red sea" → "opposite Suph"** (v1) — Hebrew מוֹל סוּף; the Hebrew has no word "sea" here. סוּף is "reeds" (as in the Sea of Reeds, v40).
- **"(There are eleven days' journey from Horeb by the way of mount Seir unto Kadesh-barnea.)" → "It is eleven days from Horeb, by the way of the hill country of Seir, as far as Kadesh-barnea"** (v2) — the Hebrew has no verb, no "journey" and no brackets. הַר is "mountain"; of a region, "hill country" (so of Seir and of the Amorite, v7, 19, 20, 24, 41, 43, 44; of Horeb in v6 "mountain").
- **"on the first day of the month … according unto all that the LORD had given him in commandment unto them" → "on the first of the month … according to all that Yahweh had commanded him for them"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּאֶחָד לַחֹדֶשׁ; צִוָּה … אֹתוֹ אֲלֵהֶם, literally "commanded him to them". The Hebrew number is "in forty year, in eleven month".
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"After he had slain Sihon the king of the Amorites … and Og the king of Bashan, which dwelt at Astaroth in Edrei" → "after he had struck down Sihon king of the Amorite … and Og king of the Bashan, who dwelt in Ashtaroth, in Edrei"** (v4) — Hebrew הִכָּה "strike", here of killing. הָאֱמֹרִי is a singular with the article for the whole people, as in Num 21 (so through the chapter); הַבָּשָׁן has the article. Who "he" is, Moses or Yahweh, is not said. The Hebrew has "in Ashtaroth in Edrei" with no "and"; Josh 12:4 has "in Ashtaroth and in Edrei".
- **"began Moses to declare this law" → "Moses undertook to make plain this Torah"** (v5) — Hebrew הוֹאִיל "undertake, resolve"; בֵּאֵר "make clear, make plain" (27:8, of writing plainly on stones).
- **"Ye have dwelt long enough in this mount" → "Enough for you is the dwelling at this mountain"** (v6) — Hebrew רַב־לָכֶם שֶׁבֶת, literally "much for you is the dwelling" (the same רַב־לָכֶם in 2:3, and רַב־לָךְ in 3:26; Num 16:3).
- **"Turn you, and take your journey, and go to the mount of the Amorites, and unto all the places nigh thereunto" → "Turn and set out for yourselves, and go to the hill country of the Amorite and to all his neighbors"** (v7) — Hebrew וּסְעוּ לָכֶם (as Num 14:25); שְׁכֵנָיו "his neighbors": persons, not places. **"in the vale, and in the south, and by the sea side" → "in the Shephelah and in the Negev and on the shore of the sea"** — הַשְּׁפֵלָה, the lowlands between the hills of Judah and the coast; נֶגֶב, the dry southland, a name; חוֹף הַיָּם. The Hebrew has no "to" before "the land of the Canaanite"; הַכְּנַעֲנִי is singular; "Lebanon" has the article.
- **"Behold, I have set the land before you" → "See, I have set the land before you"** (v8, 21) — Hebrew רְאֵה "see" (singular, to the people as one; הִנֵּה "behold" is another word, v10); נָתַתִּי לִפְנֵיכֶם "set before": the verb is "give", given over, laid open before you (the same phrase of Sihon in 2:31, 33, and of the blessing and the curse in 11:26; 30:15).
- **"go in and possess" → "Go in and take possession of"** (v8, 21, 39) — Hebrew יָרַשׁ, to take possession, one of the chief words of the book.
- **"which the LORD sware unto your fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, to give unto them and to their seed" kept** (v8) — "to" stands before each name in the Hebrew; זֶרַע "seed". Yahweh speaks of himself by name.
- **"I spake unto you … I am not able to bear you myself alone" → "I said to you … I am not able alone to bear you"** (v9) — Hebrew וָאֹמַר; לְבַדִּי "alone". Num 11:14 has the same words with אָנֹכִי "I" added ("I am not able, I alone").
- **"as the stars of heaven" → "like the stars of the heavens"** (v10) — Hebrew שָׁמַיִם is plural.
- **"(The LORD God of your fathers make you a thousand times so many more as ye are, and bless you, as he hath promised you!)" → "May Yahweh, the Elohim of your fathers, add to you a thousand times as many as you are, and may he bless you, as he has spoken to you!"** (v11) — Hebrew יֹסֵף עֲלֵיכֶם כָּכֶם אֶלֶף פְּעָמִים, literally "add upon you, like you, a thousand times"; דִּבֶּר "spoke" (Hebrew has no separate word for "promise"). No brackets in the Hebrew.
- **"your cumbrance, and your burden, and your strife" → "your load and your burden and your contention"** (v12) — Hebrew טֹרַח (only here and Isa 1:14: a wearying load), מַשָּׂא "burden" (Num 11:11, 17), רִיב "contention, a case at law". All three are singular.
- **"Take you wise men, and understanding, and known among your tribes, and I will make them rulers over you" → "Provide for yourselves men, wise and understanding and known, by your tribes, and I will set them as your heads"** (v13) — Hebrew הָבוּ לָכֶם, literally "give for yourselves"; יְדֻעִים "known" (a passive form; it is also taken as "experienced"); לְשִׁבְטֵיכֶם "for / by your tribes"; בְּרָאשֵׁיכֶם, literally "at your heads".
- **"The thing which thou hast spoken is good for us to do" → "Good is the thing that you have spoken, to do"** (v14) — the Hebrew order; "for us" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"the chief of your tribes … captains over thousands … and officers among your tribes" → "the heads of your tribes … chiefs of thousands … and officers, by your tribes"** (v15) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי "heads" (the same word as "heads over you"); שָׂרֵי "chiefs of", as Exod 18:21, 25; שֹׁטְרִים, lower officials who keep order and lists.
- **"I charged your judges … Hear the causes between your brethren, and judge righteously between every man and his brother, and the stranger that is with him" → "I commanded your judges … Hear between your brothers, and you shall judge righteously between a man and his brother and his sojourner"** (v16) — Hebrew צִוָּה "command"; שָׁמֹעַ בֵּין, "hear between" ("the causes" is not in the Hebrew); צֶדֶק, literally "judge righteousness"; גֵּרוֹ "his sojourner".
- **"Ye shall not respect persons in judgment" → "You shall not recognize faces in judgment"** (v17) — Hebrew לֹא־תַכִּירוּ פָנִים: to look at who a man is, to be partial. "Face" comes again in "the face of a man" (מִפְּנֵי־אִישׁ). **"the cause that is too hard for you, bring it unto me" → "the matter that is too hard for you, you shall bring near to me"** — Hebrew הַדָּבָר "the word, the matter"; תַּקְרִבוּן (Exod 18:26).
- **"when we departed from Horeb, we went through all that great and terrible wilderness" → "we set out from Horeb and went through all that great and fearsome wilderness"** (v19) — two main verbs in the Hebrew; נוֹרָא "to be feared".
- **"fear not, neither be discouraged" → "Do not fear and do not be dismayed"** (v21) — Hebrew תֵּחָת, from חָתַת "be shattered, dismayed" (the root of חַת "dread", Gen 9:2). The whole verse speaks to "you" in the singular; v20 and v22 are plural.
- **"We will send men before us, and they shall search us out the land, and bring us word again by what way we must go up" → "Let us send men before us, and let them search out the land for us and bring back word to us: the way by which we shall go up"** (v22) — Hebrew נִשְׁלְחָה, a wish; חָפַר, literally "dig", here "search out" (Josh 2:2). Num 13 has another verb, תּוּר "explore", and there the sending is commanded by Yahweh; here the people ask for it. The two accounts are left as they stand.
- **"the saying pleased me well … one of a tribe" → "the thing was good in my eyes … one man for each tribe"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיִּיטַב בְּעֵינַי הַדָּבָר; אִישׁ אֶחָד לַשָּׁבֶט. "Tribe" is שֵׁבֶט here, מַטֶּה in Num 13:2.
- **"the valley of Eshcol, and searched it out" → "the wadi of Eshcol, and they spied it out"** (v24) — Hebrew נַחַל, a stream bed (Num 13:23); רִגֵּל "spy out", a third verb beside חָפַר (v22) and תּוּר (v33). "It" is feminine in the Hebrew: the land, not the wadi.
- **"in their hands" → "in their hand"** (v25) — singular in the Hebrew. **"It is a good land which" → "Good is the land that"** — the Hebrew order.
- **"Notwithstanding ye would not go up, but rebelled against the commandment of the LORD" → "And you were not willing to go up, and you rebelled against the mouth of Yahweh"** (v26, 43) — Hebrew אָבָה "be willing"; וַתַּמְרוּ אֶת־פִּי, "rebelled against the mouth of" (Num 20:24); "commandment" is another word.
- **"ye murmured in your tents" kept** (v27) — Hebrew וַתֵּרָגְנוּ, from רָגַן (Ps 106:25), not the לוּן "grumble" of Num 14. **"Because the LORD hated us" → "In the hatred of Yahweh for us"** — Hebrew בְּשִׂנְאַת יְהוָה אֹתָנוּ, literally "in Yahweh's hating us". **"to deliver us" → "to give us"** — Hebrew לָתֵת.
- **"Whither shall we go up? our brethren have discouraged our heart" → "Where are we going up? Our brothers have made our heart melt"** (v28) — Hebrew עֹלִים, a participle; הֵמַסּוּ אֶת לְבָבֵנוּ, "made our heart melt". **"The people is greater and taller than we; the cities are great and walled up to heaven; and moreover we have seen the sons of the Anakims there" → "A people greater and taller than we; cities great and fortified in the heavens; and also sons of Anakim we saw there"** — the Hebrew has no verbs and no articles in the first two clauses; בַּשָּׁמָיִם is "in the heavens" (reaching into the sky); בְּנֵי עֲנָקִים "sons of Anakim", without the article. Num 13:28 has "those born of the Anak".
- **"Dread not, neither be afraid of them" → "You shall not be terrified, and you shall not fear them"** (v29) — Hebrew לֹא with the imperfect; עָרַץ "be terrified", יָרֵא "fear".
- **"according to all that he did for you in Egypt" → "according to all that he did with you in Egypt"** (v30) — Hebrew אִתְּכֶם "with you": at your side, or in dealing with you.
- **"where thou hast seen how that the LORD thy God bare thee" → "where you saw how Yahweh your Elohim bore you"** (v31) — "you saw" and "bore you" are singular in the Hebrew; "you went", "you came" are plural.
- **"Yet in this thing ye did not believe the LORD your God, who went in the way before you" → "And in this thing you are not believing in Yahweh your Elohim, who goes before you on the way"** (v32, 33) — Hebrew אֵינְכֶם מַאֲמִינִם, a participle, "you are not believing", and הַהֹלֵךְ "who goes"; הֶאֱמִין בְּ "believe in". The Hebrew has no quotation marks: the two verses are printed here as the end of what Moses said at Kadesh (v29–33). They may also be his words to those who now hear him, "you were not believing". בַּדָּבָר הַזֶּה is "in this thing" or "in this word".
- **"to search you out a place to pitch your tents in, in fire by night, to shew you by what way ye should go, and in a cloud by day" → "to seek out for you a place for your camping, in the fire by night, to show you the way in which you should go, and in the cloud by day"** (v33) — Hebrew תּוּר (the verb of Num 13, and of the ark in Num 10:33); לַחֲנֹתְכֶם "for your camping"; "the fire", "the cloud" have the article.
- **"was wroth" → "was wrathful"** (v34) — Hebrew קָצַף.
- **"Surely there shall not one of these men of this evil generation see" → "Not a man among these men, this evil generation, shall see"** (v35) — the oath in its Hebrew form: אִם־יִרְאֶה אִישׁ, literally "if a man among these men … shall see": "if" in an oath is a strong "not".
- **"to him will I give the land that he hath trodden upon, and to his children, because he hath wholly followed the LORD" → "to him I will give the land on which he has trodden, and to his sons, because he has followed fully after Yahweh"** (v36) — Hebrew מִלֵּא אַחֲרֵי, literally "filled after" (Num 14:24; 32:12). Yahweh speaks of himself by name.
- **"the LORD was angry with me for your sakes" → "with me Yahweh was angry because of you"** (v37) — Hebrew הִתְאַנַּף, the verb of אַף "anger"; בִּגְלַלְכֶם "because of you" (3:26 has לְמַעַנְכֶם, 4:21 עַל־דִּבְרֵיכֶם).
- **"encourage him: for he shall cause Israel to inherit it" → "Make him strong, for he shall make Israel inherit it"** (v38) — Hebrew חַזֵּק, from חָזַק "be strong"; "him" stands first in the Hebrew. יַנְחִלֶנָּה, the causative of נָחַל "inherit".
- **"your little ones, which ye said should be a prey, and your children, which in that day had no knowledge between good and evil" → "your little ones, of whom you said, 'They will be for plunder,' and your sons, who today have not known good and evil"** (v39) — the words of Num 14:31 in the same English; הַיּוֹם "today"; טוֹב וָרָע "good and evil".
- **"turn you, and take your journey" → "turn for yourselves and set out"** (v40) — Hebrew פְּנוּ לָכֶם וּסְעוּ. **"Red sea" → "Sea of Reeds"** — Hebrew יַם־סוּף.
- **"when ye had girded on every man his weapons of war, ye were ready to go up into the hill" → "you girded on, each man his weapons of war, and you thought it a light thing to go up into the hill country"** (v41) — Hebrew כְּלֵי מִלְחַמְתּוֹ, "the implements of his war". וַתָּהִינוּ is found only here and its meaning is uncertain: "regarded as easy" is the best-supported sense; "were ready" (KJV) is the other.
- **"Go not up, neither fight … lest ye be smitten" → "You shall not go up and you shall not fight … and you shall not be smitten"** (v42) — Hebrew לֹא with the imperfect; וְלֹא תִּנָּגְפוּ "and you shall not be smitten" (so that you be not; Num 14:42). בְּקִרְבְּכֶם "in your midst".
- **"ye would not hear … and went presumptuously up" → "you did not hear … and acted presumptuously and went up"** (v43) — Hebrew וְלֹא שְׁמַעְתֶּם; וַתָּזִדוּ, its own verb.
- **"the Amorites, which dwelt in that mountain, came out against you, and chased you, as bees do, and destroyed you in Seir" → "the Amorite who dwelt in that hill country came out to meet you, and they pursued you as the bees do, and they beat you in pieces in Seir"** (v44) — Hebrew לִקְרַאתְכֶם "to meet you"; וַיַּכְּתוּ, "beat in pieces" (Num 14:45). Num 14:45 names "the Amalekite and the Canaanite"; this verse names the Amorite. The Hebrew has "in Seir"; some ancient versions read "from Seir" (from memory).
- **"the LORD would not hearken to your voice" → "Yahweh did not listen to your voice"** (v45) — Hebrew וְלֹא־שָׁמַע … בְּקֹלְכֶם; הֶאֱזִין "give ear", from אֹזֶן "ear".
- **"So ye abode in Kadesh many days, according unto the days that ye abode there" → "And you dwelt in Kadesh many days, according to the days that you dwelt"** (v46) — Hebrew יָשַׁב "dwell"; "there" is not in the Hebrew. The saying gives no number: as long as you did dwell.

### Notes
- **Deuteronomy's opening** — sets the scene: Moses stands east of the Jordan, at the threshold. The book is his farewell address(es).
- **Timeline** (v3) — 11th month of year 40. Moses has about one month to speak before he dies (Deut 34:8; 30 days' mourning; Josh 4:19 = first month of year 41).
- **"To make plain this Torah"** (v5) — the book is self-consciously an *exposition* of prior law, not a new corpus. Deuteronomy = deuteros nomos, "second law" / restatement.
- **Structure of ch 1–3** — historical recapitulation: from Horeb to the Jordan. Covers Exod 18 (judges), Num 13–14 (scouts), Num 21 (Transjordan). Where Deuteronomy tells a thing otherwise than Exodus and Numbers (who proposes the judges and the scouts, which people strikes Israel at Hormah), each book keeps its own words.
- **"Today" rhetoric** — Deut addresses the new generation as if they were the Kadesh-barnea generation ("*you* rebelled"). Generational identity is collective; Israel's history is the present audience's own.
- **"You" singular and plural** — the Hebrew moves between the plural "you" and the singular (v21, 31); English shows no difference.
- **"Because of you"** (v37) — Num 20:12 gives Moses' and Aaron's own unbelief at Meribah as the reason; here Moses says that Yahweh was angry with him because of the people.
- **11 days' journey** (v2) — Horeb to Kadesh-barnea is short. The 40-year detour is emphasized: what should have taken 11 days took a generation.
