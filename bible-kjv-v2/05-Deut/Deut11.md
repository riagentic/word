# Deuteronomy 11

[1] "You shall therefore love Yahweh your *Elohim*, and keep his charge, his statutes, his ordinances, and his commandments always.

[2] Know today — for it is not your children who have neither known nor seen — the discipline of Yahweh your *Elohim*, his greatness, his mighty hand and his outstretched arm,

[3] his signs and his acts that he did in the midst of Egypt to Pharaoh king of Egypt and to all his land;

[4] what he did to the army of Egypt, to their horses and chariots — how he swept the waters of the Sea of Reeds over them as they pursued you, and how Yahweh has destroyed them to this day;

[5] what he did to you in the wilderness, until you came to this place;

[6] and what he did to Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab the son of Reuben — how the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them up with their households, their tents, and every living thing that was at their feet, in the midst of all Israel.

[7] But your own eyes have seen every great act of Yahweh that he did.

[8] You shall therefore keep the whole commandment that I am commanding you today, that you may be strong and go in and possess the land that you are crossing over to possess,

[9] and that you may prolong your days in the land that Yahweh swore to give to your fathers and to their offspring — a land flowing with milk and honey.

[10] For the land that you are going in to possess is not like the land of Egypt from which you came out, where you sowed your seed and watered it with your foot, like a garden of vegetables.

[11] But the land that you are crossing over to possess is a land of hills and valleys, watered by the rain of heaven —

[12] a land that Yahweh your *Elohim* cares for; the eyes of Yahweh your *Elohim* are always upon it, from the beginning of the year to the end of the year.

[13] And if you listen diligently to my commandments that I am commanding you today, to love Yahweh your *Elohim* and to serve him with all your heart and with all your soul,

[14] then I will give the rain of your land in its season — the early rain and the later rain — that you may gather in your grain and your new wine and your oil;

[15] and I will give grass in your fields for your livestock, and you shall eat and be full.

[16] Take care, lest your heart be deceived and you turn aside and serve other gods and bow down to them;

[17] then the anger of Yahweh will be kindled against you, and he will shut up the heavens, so that there will be no rain, and the land will not yield its fruit; and you will perish quickly from the good land that Yahweh is giving you.

[18] You shall lay up these words of mine in your heart and in your soul, and shall bind them for a sign on your hand, and they shall be as frontlets between your eyes.

[19] You shall teach them to your children, speaking of them when you sit in your house, when you walk by the way, when you lie down, and when you rise.

[20] You shall write them on the doorposts of your house and on your gates,

[21] that your days may be multiplied, and the days of your children, in the land that Yahweh swore to your fathers to give them, as the days of the heavens above the earth.

[22] For if you are careful to keep all this commandment that I am commanding you, to do it — loving Yahweh your *Elohim*, walking in all his ways, and holding fast to him —

[23] then Yahweh will drive out all these nations before you, and you shall dispossess nations greater and mightier than you.

[24] Every place on which the sole of your foot treads shall be yours; from the wilderness to the Lebanon, from the River — the river Euphrates — to the western sea, shall be your border.

[25] No man shall be able to stand before you; Yahweh your *Elohim* shall lay the dread and fear of you on all the land that you tread upon, as he has spoken to you.

[26] See, I am setting before you today a blessing and a curse:

[27] the blessing, if you obey the commandments of Yahweh your *Elohim* that I am commanding you today;

[28] and the curse, if you do not obey the commandments of Yahweh your *Elohim* but turn aside from the way that I am commanding you today, to go after other gods that you have not known.

[29] When Yahweh your *Elohim* brings you into the land that you are entering to possess, you shall set the blessing on Mount Gerizim and the curse on Mount Ebal.

[30] Are they not beyond the Jordan, toward the setting sun, in the land of the Canaanites who dwell in the Arabah, opposite Gilgal, beside the oaks of Moreh?

[31] For you are to cross the Jordan to go in and possess the land that Yahweh your *Elohim* is giving you; and you shall possess it and dwell in it.

[32] Be careful to do all the statutes and the ordinances that I am setting before you today.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"miracles and acts" → "signs and acts"** (v3) — Hebrew אֹתֹת וְמַעֲשָׂיו *otot u-ma'asav*.
- **"Red sea" → "Sea of Reeds"** (v4).
- **"all the substance that was in their possession" → "every living thing that was at their feet"** (v6) — Hebrew כָּל־הַיְקוּם אֲשֶׁר בְּרַגְלֵיהֶם *kol ha-yequm asher be-ragleihem*. "All the existence/beings at their feet" — all their possessions and dependents.
- **"champaign" → "Arabah"** (v30) — Hebrew עֲרָבָה *Arabah*; Jordan rift-valley.
- **"plains of Moreh" → "oaks of Moreh"** (v30) — Hebrew אֵלוֹנֵי מֹרֶה *elonei Moreh*. Grove of sacred trees near Shechem (cf. Gen 12:6).
- **"from the wilderness to Lebanon" → "to the Lebanon"** (v24) — modernized.
- **"uttermost sea" → "western sea"** (v24) — Hebrew הַיָּם הָאַחֲרוֹן *ha-yam ha-acharon* = the hinder/western sea. The Mediterranean.

### Notes
- **Generational memory** (v2) — the older hearers saw these events themselves; their children did not. Moses speaks to the adults with firsthand-witness force, urging transmission.
- **Egypt vs Canaan watering** (v10–12) — Egypt: regulated irrigation from the Nile, foot-powered water wheels. Canaan: dependent on rainfall. The latter is more theological: the land's fertility is directly tied to divine favor.
- **Early and later rain** (v14) — Hebrew *yoreh u-malqosh*. The autumn rain (Oct-Nov) and spring rain (Mar-Apr). The agricultural rhythm of Canaan.
- **Gerizim and Ebal** (v29) — two mountains flanking Shechem. Blessing-curse ceremony prescribed here, carried out in Josh 8:30–35, and expanded in Deut 27.
- **Shema repetition** (v18–21) — the second *Shema*-block, identical to 6:6–9 in content. Traditional Judaism recites both.
- **Land extent** (v24) — idealized: wilderness (south) to Lebanon (north), Euphrates (east) to Mediterranean (west). Partially realized under David-Solomon.
