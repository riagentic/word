# Deuteronomy 11

[1] "And you shall love ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and you shall keep his charge and his statutes and his ordinances and his commandments all the days.

[2] And you shall know today that it is not your sons, who have not known and who have not seen the discipline of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, his greatness, his strong hand and his outstretched arm,

[3] and his signs and his works that he did in the midst of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ to ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and to all his land,

[4] and what he did to the force of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, to its horses and to its chariots, how he made the waters of the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧ flow over their faces when they pursued after you, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ did away with them to this day,

[5] and what he did to you in the wilderness until you came to this place,

[6] and what he did to ⟦dathan|Datan|Dathan⟧ and to ⟦abiram|Aviram|Abiram⟧, the sons of ⟦eliab|Eliav|Eliab⟧ the son of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧, how the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them and their houses and their tents and every existing thing that was at their feet, in the midst of all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;

[7] but your eyes are the ones that have seen all the great work of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that he did.

[8] And you shall keep all the commandment that I am commanding you today, so that you may be strong and go in and take possession of the land to which you are passing over to possess it,

[9] and so that you may prolong days on the ground that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ swore to your fathers to give to them and to their seed, a land flowing with milk and honey.

[10] For the land to which you are going in to possess it, it is not like the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, from which you came out, where you would sow your seed and water with your foot, like a garden of vegetables.

[11] And the land to which you are passing over to possess it is a land of mountains and plains; by the rain of the heavens it drinks water,

[12] a land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ seeks; continually the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ are on it, from the beginning of the year and to the end of the year.

[13] And it shall be, if you surely listen to my commandments that I am commanding you today, to love ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and to serve him with all your heart and with all your soul,

[14] then I will give the rain of your land in its time, the early rain and the latter rain, and you shall gather your grain and your new wine and your fresh oil.

[15] And I will give plants in your field for your beasts, and you shall eat and be sated.

[16] Take heed to yourselves, lest your heart be enticed, and you turn aside and serve other gods and bow down to them;

[17] and the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will burn against you, and he will close up the heavens, and there will be no rain, and the ground will not give its produce, and you will perish quickly from upon the good land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is giving to you.

[18] And you shall put these words of mine upon your heart and upon your soul, and you shall bind them for a sign upon your hand, and they shall be for frontlets between your eyes.

[19] And you shall teach them to your sons, to speak of them when you sit in your house and when you walk on the way and when you lie down and when you rise.

[20] And you shall write them upon the doorposts of your house and in your gates,

[21] so that your days may multiply, and the days of your sons, on the ground that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ swore to your fathers to give to them, as the days of the heavens over the earth.

[22] For if you surely keep all this commandment that I am commanding you, to do it, to love ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to walk in all his ways and to cling to him,

[23] then ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will dispossess all these nations from before you, and you shall dispossess nations greater and mightier than you.

[24] Every place on which the sole of your foot treads shall be yours: from the wilderness and the ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧, from the River, the river ⟦euphrates|Perat|Euphrates⟧, and to the hinder sea shall be your border.

[25] No man shall take his stand before you; the dread of you and the fear of you ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will set on the face of all the land on which you tread, as he spoke to you.

[26] See, I am setting before you today a blessing and a curse:

[27] the blessing, when you listen to the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that I am commanding you today,

[28] and the curse, if you do not listen to the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and turn aside from the way that I am commanding you today, to go after other gods that you have not known.

[29] And it shall be, when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ brings you to the land to which you are going in to possess it, that you shall set the blessing on Mount Gerizim and the curse on Mount ⟦ebal|Eival|Ebal⟧.

[30] Are they not across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, beyond the way of the going in of the sun, in the land of the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧ who dwells in the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧, opposite Gilgal, beside the oaks of Moreh?

[31] For you are passing over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ to go in to take possession of the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you, and you shall take possession of it and dwell in it.

[32] And you shall keep to do all the statutes and the ordinances that I am setting before you today.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; of the gods of the nations, "gods" (v16, 28).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"thou / you"** (throughout) — the Hebrew changes between the plural "you" and the singular, often inside one sentence: singular in v1, 8 ("commanding you"), 10 ("you are going in … sow … water"; but "you came out" is plural), 12, 14b–15, 19b–20, 26 ("See"), 29; plural in the rest. English cannot show it.
- **"keep his charge, and his statutes, and his judgments, and his commandments, alway" → "keep his charge and his statutes and his ordinances and his commandments all the days"** (v1) — Hebrew מִשְׁמֶרֶת "charge", what is to be kept (from שָׁמַר "keep"; Gen 26:5); מִשְׁפָּטִים "ordinances", the word "judgment" used of a rule (v32 also); כָּל־הַיָּמִים "all the days". "Therefore" (KJV) is not in the Hebrew.
- **"And know ye this day: for I speak not with your children which have not known, and which have not seen the chastisement" → "And you shall know today that it is not your sons, who have not known and who have not seen the discipline"** (v2) — Hebrew כִּי לֹא אֶת־בְּנֵיכֶם אֲשֶׁר לֹא־יָדְעוּ וַאֲשֶׁר לֹא־רָאוּ אֶת־מוּסַר, a sentence without a verb of its own: literally "that / for not your sons, who did not know and who did not see the discipline of Yahweh …"; it is taken up in v7, "but your eyes are the ones that have seen". "I speak" (KJV) is supplied; אֶת before "your sons" is the object marker or "with" ("not with your sons"). The objects from "the discipline" to v6 may also hang on "you shall know". בָּנִים "sons". מוּסָר "discipline", from יִסֵּר (8:5).
- **"his mighty hand, and his stretched out arm" → "his strong hand and his outstretched arm"** (v2) — Hebrew יָדוֹ הַחֲזָקָה, as Exod 6:1; "strong" is the root of "be strong" in v8.
- **"his miracles, and his acts" → "his signs and his works"** (v3) — Hebrew אֹתֹתָיו וְאֶת־מַעֲשָׂיו *ototav ve-et ma'asav*; מַעֲשֶׂה "work", the word of v7.
- **"the army of Egypt, unto their horses, and to their chariots" → "the force of Egypt, to its horses and to its chariots"** (v4) — Hebrew חֵיל "force" (Exod 14:4); the suffixes are singular ("his"), of Egypt; רֶכֶב is a collective singular. **"how he made the water of the Red sea to overflow them" → "how he made the waters of the Sea of Reeds flow over their faces"** — Hebrew הֵצִיף … עַל־פְּנֵיהֶם; יַם־סוּף "Sea of Reeds". **"hath destroyed them unto this day" → "did away with them to this day"** — Hebrew וַיְאַבְּדֵם, piel of אָבַד "perish": "made them perish" (v17 "perish" is the same root).
- **"unto you" → "to you"** (v5) — Hebrew לָכֶם, "to you" or "for you".
- **"and their households, and their tents, and all the substance that was in their possession" → "and their houses and their tents and every existing thing that was at their feet"** (v6) — Hebrew בָּתֵּיהֶם "their houses" (the households, as Num 16:32); כָּל־הַיְקוּם אֲשֶׁר בְּרַגְלֵיהֶם *kol ha-yequm asher be-ragleihem*: יְקוּם, literally "what stands", is the word of Gen 7:4, 23; "at their feet", literally "in their feet", is what went with them, all that followed them. **"opened her mouth"** — Hebrew פָּצְתָה, as Num 16:30.
- **"But your eyes have seen all the great acts of the LORD" → "but your eyes are the ones that have seen all the great work of Yahweh"** (v7) — Hebrew כִּי עֵינֵיכֶם הָרֹאֹת, literally "for / but your eyes are the seeing ones"; מַעֲשֵׂה is singular, "work".
- **"all the commandments" → "all the commandment"** (v8, 22) — Hebrew כָּל־הַמִּצְוָה, singular: the whole charge as one. "Commanding you" in v8 is singular in the Hebrew among plurals. **"whither ye go to possess it" → "to which you are passing over to possess it"** (v8, 11) — Hebrew עֹבְרִים "passing over" (the Jordan, v31); v10, 29 have בָּא "going in": the two verbs are kept apart.
- **"prolong your days in the land" → "prolong days on the ground"** (v9, 21) — Hebrew תַּאֲרִיכוּ יָמִים, without "your"; אֲדָמָה "ground", beside אֶרֶץ "land" in the same verse. **"unto their seed"** — Hebrew זֶרַע "seed".
- **"where thou sowedst thy seed, and wateredst it with thy foot, as a garden of herbs" → "where you would sow your seed and water with your foot, like a garden of vegetables"** (v10) — the Hebrew verbs tell what was done again and again; "it" is not in the Hebrew. יָרָק is green growth that is eaten. What watering "with your foot" was (a water wheel worked by treading, or opening the channels of earth with the foot) is not said.
- **"a land of hills and valleys, and drinketh water of the rain of heaven" → "a land of mountains and plains; by the rain of the heavens it drinks water"** (v11) — Hebrew הָרִים "mountains"; בְּקָעֹת "plains", broad valley floors; לִמְטַר, literally "according to the rain".
- **"careth for" → "seeks"** (v12) — Hebrew דֹּרֵשׁ "seeks, inquires after": he looks after it. **"always" → "continually"** — Hebrew תָּמִיד, which the accents join to "the eyes". **"from the beginning of the year even unto the end of the year"** — Hebrew וְעַד אַחֲרִית שָׁנָה, "and to the after-part of a year": the second "year" has no article.
- **"if ye shall hearken diligently unto my commandments" → "if you surely listen to my commandments"** (v13) — Hebrew שָׁמֹעַ תִּשְׁמְעוּ, the infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "listening you listen"; "obey" is not a Hebrew word here (v27, 28 also). **"my commandments … I will give"** (v13–15) — Moses' speech passes into the "I" of Yahweh.
- **"the first rain and the latter rain" → "the early rain and the latter rain"** (v14) — Hebrew יוֹרֶה וּמַלְקוֹשׁ *yoreh u-malqosh*. **"in his due season" → "in its time"** — Hebrew בְּעִתּוֹ. **"thy corn, and thy wine, and thine oil" → "your grain and your new wine and your fresh oil"** — Hebrew דָּגָן, תִּירוֹשׁ, יִצְהָר (not יַיִן "wine", שֶׁמֶן "oil").
- **"grass in thy fields for thy cattle, that thou mayest eat and be full" → "plants in your field for your beasts, and you shall eat and be sated"** (v15) — Hebrew עֵשֶׂב "plants, herbage"; שָׂדֶה singular; בְּהֵמָה "beast"; שָׂבַע "be sated".
- **"that your heart be not deceived" → "lest your heart be enticed"** (v16) — Hebrew יִפְתֶּה, qal of the verb "entice" (Exod 22:16): be open to persuasion, be led astray. **"worship them" → "bow down to them"** — Hebrew הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה.
- **"the LORD's wrath be kindled against you, and he shut up the heaven" → "the anger of Yahweh will burn against you, and he will close up the heavens"** (v17) — Hebrew וְחָרָה אַף־יְהוָה; אַף is "nose", hence "anger"; עָצַר "restrain, close up" (Gen 20:18). The clauses of v17 may still hang on "lest" (v16). **"the land yield not her fruit" → "the ground will not give its produce"** — Hebrew אֲדָמָה "ground"; יְבוּל "produce" (Lev 26:4). **"from off the good land" → "from upon the good land"** — Hebrew מֵעַל.
- **"lay up these my words in your heart and in your soul" → "put these words of mine upon your heart and upon your soul"** (v18) — Hebrew שִׂים … עַל "put upon" (6:6 "shall be upon your heart"). **"bind them for a sign upon your hand, that they may be as frontlets" → "bind them for a sign upon your hand, and they shall be for frontlets"** — Hebrew לְאוֹת … לְטוֹטָפֹת, the one preposition לְ "for" twice, worded as Deut 6:8 and Exod 13:16; the meaning of טוֹטָפֹת is uncertain (a band or mark on the forehead).
- **"your children, speaking of them" → "your sons, to speak of them"** (v19) — Hebrew לְדַבֵּר בָּם, an infinitive: who speaks, the fathers or the sons, the form does not say. **"when thou sittest … walkest by the way"** — "you" is singular from here to v20.
- **"upon the door posts of thine house, and upon thy gates" → "upon the doorposts of your house and in your gates"** (v20) — Hebrew מְזוּזוֹת "doorposts"; וּבִשְׁעָרֶיךָ, with בְּ "in"; the words of 6:9.
- **"That your days may be multiplied" → "so that your days may multiply"** (v21) — Hebrew יִרְבּוּ, qal. **"as the days of heaven upon the earth" → "as the days of the heavens over the earth"** — Hebrew עַל "on, over".
- **"if ye shall diligently keep" → "if you surely keep"** (v22) — Hebrew שָׁמֹר תִּשְׁמְרוּן, the infinitive absolute. **"to cleave unto him" → "to cling to him"** — Hebrew דָּבַק, as 10:20.
- **"drive out … ye shall possess greater nations and mightier" → "dispossess … you shall dispossess nations greater and mightier"** (v23) — Hebrew וְהוֹרִישׁ … וִירִשְׁתֶּם, hifil and qal of the one root יָרַשׁ "possess": the qal with persons as its object is to take what is theirs. English has one word for both stems.
- **"possess"** (v8, 10, 11, 29, 31) — Hebrew יָרַשׁ: "take possession of" where the verb stands by itself with the land (v8, 31), "to possess it" for לְרִשְׁתָּהּ.
- **"from the wilderness and Lebanon, from the river, the river Euphrates, even unto the uttermost sea" → "from the wilderness and the Lebanon, from the River, the river Euphrates, and to the hinder sea"** (v24) — the Hebrew has "and the Lebanon", not "to"; הַיָּם הָאַחֲרוֹן *ha-yam ha-acharon* is literally "the sea behind": the western sea for one who faces the sunrise, the Mediterranean. **"the soles of your feet" → "the sole of your foot"** — singular in the Hebrew. **"your coast" → "your border"** — Hebrew גְּבוּל.
- **"There shall no man be able to stand before you" → "No man shall take his stand before you"** (v25) — Hebrew לֹא־יִתְיַצֵּב אִישׁ בִּפְנֵיכֶם, literally "in your face"; "be able" is not in the Hebrew. **"the fear of you and the dread of you" → "the dread of you and the fear of you"** — Hebrew פַּחַד "dread", then מוֹרָא "fear" (from יָרֵא). **"lay … upon all the land" → "set on the face of all the land"** — Hebrew יִתֵּן … עַל־פְּנֵי; נָתַן "give, set" is also the verb of v26, 29, 32. **"as he hath said unto you" → "as he spoke to you"** — Hebrew דִּבֶּר.
- **"Behold, I set before you" → "See, I am setting before you"** (v26) — Hebrew רְאֵה "see" (singular); נֹתֵן, a participle. **"a blessing and a curse"** — Hebrew בְּרָכָה וּקְלָלָה; קְלָלָה is the noun of קִלֵּל "make light of, curse".
- **"A blessing, if ye obey" → "the blessing, when you listen to"** (v27) — the Hebrew has the article, and אֲשֶׁר "that, when" where v28 has אִם "if"; שָׁמַע אֶל "listen to".
- **"which ye have not known"** (v28) — Hebrew יְדַעְתֶּם. **"to go after other gods"** — Hebrew לָלֶכֶת אַחֲרֵי; the verb is the "walk" of v22.
- **"when the LORD thy God hath brought thee in unto the land whither thou goest to possess it, that thou shalt put the blessing" → "when Yahweh your Elohim brings you to the land to which you are going in to possess it, that you shall set the blessing"** (v29) — Hebrew וְהָיָה כִּי "And it shall be, when"; וְנָתַתָּה, the verb of v26.
- **"on the other side Jordan, by the way where the sun goeth down, in the land of the Canaanites, which dwell in the champaign over against Gilgal, beside the plains of Moreh" → "across the Jordan, beyond the way of the going in of the sun, in the land of the Canaanite who dwells in the Arabah, opposite Gilgal, beside the oaks of Moreh"** (v30) — Hebrew בְּעֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן "across the Jordan"; אַחֲרֵי דֶּרֶךְ מְבוֹא הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ, literally "behind the way of the entering of the sun": behind (west of) the road that runs toward the sunset, or along it; the sense is not certain. הַכְּנַעֲנִי is a collective singular. עֲרָבָה *Aravah* is the dry valley of the Jordan. אֵלוֹנֵי מֹרֶה *elonei Moreh*: אֵלוֹן is a great tree, "oak" (the kind is not certain), plural here; Gen 12:6 has the singular, "the oak of Moreh", at Shechem. "Plains" (KJV) follows the Targum (from memory).
- **"ye shall pass over Jordan to go in to possess" → "you are passing over the Jordan to go in to take possession of"** (v31) — Hebrew עֹבְרִים, a participle. **"giveth you" → "is giving to you"** — participle.
- **"ye shall observe to do" → "you shall keep to do"** (v32) — Hebrew וּשְׁמַרְתֶּם לַעֲשׂוֹת; שָׁמַר is "keep" throughout ("be careful to do" gives the sense). **"statutes and judgments" → "the statutes and the ordinances"** — Hebrew הַחֻקִּים וְאֶת־הַמִּשְׁפָּטִים.

### Notes
- **Generational memory** (v2–7) — the older hearers saw these events themselves; their sons did not. Moses speaks to the adults with firsthand-witness force, urging transmission.
- **Egypt vs Canaan watering** (v10–12) — Egypt: regulated irrigation from the Nile. Canaan: dependent on rainfall. The latter is more theological: the land's fertility is directly tied to divine favor.
- **Early and latter rain** (v14) — Hebrew *yoreh u-malqosh*. The autumn rain (Oct-Nov) and spring rain (Mar-Apr). The agricultural rhythm of Canaan.
- **Gerizim and Ebal** (v29) — two mountains flanking Shechem. Blessing-curse ceremony prescribed here, carried out in Josh 8:30–35, and expanded in Deut 27.
- **Shema repetition** (v18–21) — the second *Shema*-block, close to 6:6–9 in content (here mostly in the plural). Traditional Judaism recites both.
- **Land extent** (v24) — idealized: wilderness (south) and Lebanon (north), Euphrates (east) to the hinder sea, the Mediterranean (west). Partially realized under David-Solomon.
