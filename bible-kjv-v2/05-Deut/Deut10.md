# Deuteronomy 10

[1] "At that time ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, 'Hew for yourself two tablets of stone like the first ones, and come up to me to the mountain, and you shall make for yourself an ark of wood.

[2] And I will write on the tablets the words that were on the first tablets, which you broke in pieces, and you shall put them in the ark.'

[3] And I made an ark of acacia wood and hewed two tablets of stone like the first ones, and I went up to the mountain, and the two tablets were in my hand.

[4] And he wrote on the tablets, according to the first writing, the ten words that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to you on the mountain from the midst of the fire on the day of the assembly; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave them to me.

[5] And I turned and went down from the mountain and put the tablets in the ark that I had made; and they were there, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded me.

[6] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ journeyed from ⟦beeroth|Beerot|Beeroth⟧ ⟦bene-jaakan|Benei Yaaqan|Bene-jaakan⟧ to Moserah. There ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ died, and there he was buried; and ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ his son was priest in his place.

[7] From there they journeyed to Gudgodah, and from Gudgodah to ⟦jotbathah|Yotvatah|Jotbathah⟧, a land of wadis of water.

[8] At that time ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ separated the tribe of Levi to carry the ark of the covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to stand before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to minister to him and to bless in his name, to this day.

[9] Therefore Levi has had no share and inheritance with his brothers; ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, he is his inheritance, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ spoke to him.

[10] And I, I stood on the mountain as in the first days, forty days and forty nights; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ listened to me that time also; ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was not willing to ruin you.

[11] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, 'Arise, go to journey before the people, and let them go in and take possession of the land that I swore to their fathers to give to them.'

[12] And now, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, what is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ asking of you, except to fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to walk in all his ways and to love him, and to serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ with all your heart and with all your soul,

[13] to keep the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and his statutes, which I am commanding you today, for your good?

[14] Behold, to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ belong the heavens and the heavens of the heavens, the earth and all that is in it.

[15] Only your fathers did ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ long for, to love them, and he chose their seed after them, you, out of all the peoples, as at this day.

[16] And you shall circumcise the foreskin of your heart, and your neck you shall stiffen no more.

[17] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, he is the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of gods and the Lord of lords, the great ⟦el|El|God⟧, the mighty and the fearsome, who does not lift up a face and does not take a bribe,

[18] doing the judgment of the fatherless and the widow, and loving the sojourner, to give him bread and a cloak.

[19] And you shall love the sojourner, for you were sojourners in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[20] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ you shall fear; him you shall serve, and to him you shall cling, and by his name you shall swear.

[21] He is your praise, and he is your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who has done with you these great things and these fearsome things that your eyes have seen.

[22] With seventy souls your fathers went down to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and now ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has made you as the stars of the heavens for multitude.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"thou / you"** (throughout) — the Hebrew changes between the singular "you" (v1–2 to Moses; v9–10, 12–14, 20–22 to Israel as one) and the plural (v4, 15b–19); English cannot show it. In v15 both stand in one verse: "your fathers" (singular) … "you" (plural).
- **"Hew thee" → "Hew for yourself"** (v1, 3) — Hebrew פְּסָל־לְךָ; פָּסַל "hew, carve", worded as Exod 34:1, 4. **"two tables of stone" → "two tablets of stone"** — Hebrew לֻחֹת אֲבָנִים, literally "tablets of stones". **"like unto the first" → "like the first ones"** — as Exod 34:1. **"into the mount" → "to the mountain"** — Hebrew הָהָרָה. **"an ark of wood"** — Hebrew אֲרוֹן עֵץ; אֲרוֹן is "chest", the word of the ark of the testimony (Exod 25:10).
- **"brakest" → "broke in pieces"** (v2) — Hebrew שִׁבֵּר (piel), as Exod 32:19; 34:1.
- **"shittim wood" → "acacia wood"** (v3) — Hebrew עֲצֵי שִׁטִּים, literally "woods of acacias". **"having the two tables in mine hand" → "and the two tablets were in my hand"** — a clause of its own in the Hebrew.
- **"the ten commandments" → "the ten words"** (v4) — Hebrew עֲשֶׂרֶת הַדְּבָרִים, as Exod 34:28. **"out of the midst" → "from the midst"** — Hebrew מִתּוֹךְ.
- **"came down" → "went down"** (v5) — Hebrew וָאֵפֶן וָאֵרֵד מִן־הָהָר, the words of Exod 32:15. **"and there they be" → "and they were there"** — Hebrew וַיִּהְיוּ שָׁם, a narrative past; that they are still there is not said by the form.
- **"the children of Israel took their journey" → "the sons of Israel journeyed"** (v6, 7) — Hebrew בְּנֵי "sons of"; נָסַע "journey" (literally "pull up" the tent pegs). **"Beeroth of the children of Jaakan"** — Hebrew בְּאֵרֹת בְּנֵי־יַעֲקָן, literally "the wells of the sons of Jaakan"; kept as a place name. **"ministered in the priest's office" → "was priest"** — Hebrew וַיְכַהֵן, the verb of כֹּהֵן "priest". **"and there he was buried"** — the Hebrew says "there" twice. The brackets that earlier stood around v6–9 are not in the Hebrew and are taken out.
- **"a land of rivers of waters" → "a land of wadis of water"** (v7) — Hebrew נַחֲלֵי מָיִם; נַחַל is the stream bed, dry or running: here beds with water in them.
- **"separated"** (v8) — Hebrew הִבְדִּיל, as Num 8:14; 16:9. **"the tribe of Levi"** — Hebrew שֵׁבֶט הַלֵּוִי, with the article: literally "the tribe of the Levite". **"to bear" → "to carry"** — Hebrew נָשָׂא. **"minister unto him"** — Hebrew שֵׁרֵת "attend on"; "serve" (v12, 20) is another verb, עָבַד. **"unto this day" → "to this day"** — Hebrew עַד הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה.
- **"Levi hath no part nor inheritance" → "Levi has had no share and inheritance"** (v9) — Hebrew לֹא־הָיָה, a perfect; חֵלֶק "share". **"the LORD is his inheritance" → "Yahweh, he is his inheritance"** — Hebrew יְהוָה הוּא נַחֲלָתוֹ, with the pronoun. **"promised him" → "spoke to him"** — Hebrew דִּבֶּר "speak"; Hebrew has no word "promise".
- **"I stayed in the mount, according to the first time" → "I, I stood on the mountain as in the first days"** (v10) — Hebrew וְאָנֹכִי עָמַדְתִּי, with the pronoun written out; עָמַד "stand"; כַּיָּמִים הָרִאשֹׁנִים "as the first days". **"hearkened unto me" → "listened to me"** — Hebrew שָׁמַע אֶל. **"would not destroy thee" → "was not willing to ruin you"** — Hebrew לֹא־אָבָה "was not willing"; הַשְׁחִית, the "ruin" of Gen 6:13 and Deut 9:26 ("destroy"). "You" is singular.
- **"take thy journey before the people" → "go to journey before the people"** (v11) — Hebrew לֵךְ לְמַסַּע, literally "go for the setting out". **"that they may go in" → "and let them go in"** — Hebrew וְיָבֹאוּ וְיִרְשׁוּ, two verbs joined by "and" after the command; they give its purpose. **"possess" → "take possession of"** — Hebrew יָרַשׁ.
- **"what doth the LORD thy God require of thee, but" → "what is Yahweh your Elohim asking of you, except"** (v12) — Hebrew שֹׁאֵל "asking", a participle; מֵעִמָּךְ "from with you"; כִּי אִם "except, but only". KJV "O Israel": the Hebrew has only the name. The four infinitives ("to fear … to walk … to love … to serve") are kept; v13 "to keep" goes on without "and".
- **"with all thy heart and with all thy soul"** (v12) — Hebrew בְּכָל־לְבָבְךָ וּבְכָל־נַפְשֶׁךָ; נֶפֶשׁ "soul".
- **"which I command thee this day" → "which I am commanding you today"** (v13) — Hebrew מְצַוְּךָ, a participle. **"for thy good"** — Hebrew לְטוֹב לָךְ, literally "for good to you".
- **"the heaven and the heaven of heavens" → "the heavens and the heavens of the heavens"** (v14) — Hebrew הַשָּׁמַיִם וּשְׁמֵי הַשָּׁמָיִם; the word is always plural. "Belong" stands for the Hebrew לְ "to": "to Yahweh … are the heavens".
- **"Only the LORD had a delight in thy fathers to love them" → "Only your fathers did Yahweh long for, to love them"** (v15) — Hebrew חָשַׁק בְּ, literally "be bound to, be attached to", the verb of Gen 34:8 (and Deut 7:7). **"their seed after them, even you, above all people" → "their seed after them, you, out of all the peoples"** — Hebrew זֶרַע "seed"; מִכָּל־הָעַמִּים "from all the peoples". **"as it is this day" → "as at this day"** — Hebrew כַּיּוֹם הַזֶּה, literally "as this day".
- **"Circumcise therefore the foreskin of your heart" → "And you shall circumcise the foreskin of your heart"** (v16) — Hebrew וּמַלְתֶּם אֵת עָרְלַת לְבַבְכֶם *u-maltem et orlat levavkhem*, word for word; the Hebrew has "and", not "therefore". Paul takes it up: Rom 2:29, Col 2:11. **"be no more stiffnecked" → "your neck you shall stiffen no more"** — Hebrew וְעָרְפְּכֶם לֹא תַקְשׁוּ עוֹד, literally "your neck you shall make hard no more"; the verb of קְשֵׁה־עֹרֶף "stiff-necked" (Exod 32:9; Deut 9:6, 13).
- **"God of gods, and Lord of lords" → "the *Elohim* of gods and the Lord of lords"** (v17) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי הָאֱלֹהִים וַאֲדֹנֵי הָאֲדֹנִים *Elohei ha-Elohim va-Adonei ha-Adonim*, literally "the *Elohim* of the *elohim* and the lords of the lords": in each pair the same Hebrew word stands twice (אֲדֹנֵי is a plural of majesty; it is not the name *Adonai*). The construction is the Hebrew superlative. Worded as Ps 136:2, 3.
- **"a great God, a mighty, and a terrible" → "the great El, the mighty and the fearsome"** (v17) — Hebrew הָאֵל הַגָּדֹל הַגִּבֹּר וְהַנּוֹרָא *ha-El ha-gadol ha-gibbor ve-ha-nora*, each word with the article; נוֹרָא is from יָרֵא "fear" (the "fear" of v12, 20 and "fearsome things" of v21).
- **"regardeth not persons, nor taketh reward" → "does not lift up a face and does not take a bribe"** (v17) — Hebrew לֹא־יִשָּׂא פָנִים, literally; to lift up someone's face is to show him regard, to favor him (Gen 19:21; Lev 19:15): he is partial to no one. שֹׁחַד "bribe".
- **"He doth execute the judgment of the fatherless and widow, and loveth the stranger, in giving him food and raiment" → "doing the judgment of the fatherless and the widow, and loving the sojourner, to give him bread and a cloak"** (v18) — Hebrew עֹשֶׂה … וְאֹהֵב, participles that go on from v17; מִשְׁפַּט "judgment" (he gives them their right); יָתוֹם "fatherless"; גֵּר "sojourner" (v19 also); לֶחֶם "bread"; שִׂמְלָה "cloak", the outer wrap.
- **"Love ye therefore the stranger" → "And you shall love the sojourner"** (v19) — Hebrew וַאֲהַבְתֶּם; no "therefore".
- **"to him shalt thou cleave" → "to him you shall cling"** (v20) — Hebrew דָּבַק, as Gen 2:24. The Hebrew order is kept: the object stands first in each clause.
- **"that hath done for thee these great and terrible things" → "who has done with you these great things and these fearsome things"** (v21) — Hebrew אִתְּךָ "with you"; אֶת־הַגְּדֹלֹת וְאֶת־הַנּוֹרָאֹת, each with its own object marker and article.
- **"with threescore and ten persons" → "With seventy souls"** (v22) — Hebrew בְּשִׁבְעִים נֶפֶשׁ; נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (Gen 46:27; Exod 1:5). The בְּ says what they amounted to: "as seventy souls". **"as the stars of heaven for multitude" → "as the stars of the heavens for multitude"** — Hebrew לָרֹב.

### Notes
- **New tablets, ark of wood** (v1–5) — the ark here is made by Moses of simple wood. Ex 37 tells a different story (Bezalel makes an elaborate gold-overlaid ark). Harmonizers: this is the provisional ark; Ex 37 the permanent one. Source-critics: variant tradition.
- **The wilderness stations** (v6–9) — the verses speak of Israel in the third person and appear to interrupt the narrative (many versions put them in brackets); they preserve traditions about Aaron's death and Levite selection in narrative summary form. Num 33:30–33 gives the stations in another order, and Num 20:22–28; 33:38 place Aaron's death at Mount Hor.
- **"What is Yahweh asking of you?"** (v12) — central Deuteronomic statement. Micah 6:8 asks a like question (reframed as justice, *chesed*, humble walk).
- **"Circumcise the heart"** (v16) — Deut's key theological metaphor. Later: Deut 30:6 promises Yahweh himself will do this. NT: internalization of covenant.
- **Love of sojourners** (v19) — repeated grounding in exodus-experience: "you were sojourners." Unique in ancient law — the immigrant as love-command.
- **"Seventy souls"** (v22) — the family of Jacob entering Egypt (Gen 46:27). From seventy to stars-of-heaven: the promise fulfilled.
