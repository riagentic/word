# Deuteronomy 10

[1] "At that time Yahweh said to me, 'Cut two tablets of stone like the first, and come up to me on the mountain, and make yourself an ark of wood.

[2] I will write on the tablets the words that were on the first tablets, which you broke, and you shall put them in the ark.'

[3] So I made an ark of acacia wood, cut two tablets of stone like the first, and went up the mountain with the two tablets in my hand.

[4] He wrote on the tablets, according to the first writing, the ten words that Yahweh spoke to you on the mountain out of the midst of the fire on the day of the assembly; and Yahweh gave them to me.

[5] I turned and came down from the mountain and put the tablets in the ark I had made; there they are, as Yahweh commanded me.

[6] (The children of Israel journeyed from Beeroth Bene-jaakan to Moserah. There Aaron died and was buried; and his son Eleazar served as priest in his place.

[7] From there they journeyed to Gudgodah, and from Gudgodah to Jotbathah, a land of brooks of water.

[8] At that time Yahweh set apart the tribe of Levi to carry the ark of the covenant of Yahweh, to stand before Yahweh to minister to him, and to bless in his name — to this day.

[9] Therefore Levi has no portion or inheritance with his brothers; Yahweh is his inheritance, as Yahweh your *Elohim* promised him.)

[10] I stayed on the mountain, as at the first time, forty days and forty nights; and Yahweh listened to me also at that time; Yahweh was not willing to destroy you.

[11] Yahweh said to me, 'Arise, go on your journey at the head of the people, that they may go in and possess the land that I swore to their fathers to give them.'

[12] And now, O Israel, what does Yahweh your *Elohim* ask of you, but to fear Yahweh your *Elohim*, to walk in all his ways and to love him, to serve Yahweh your *Elohim* with all your heart and with all your soul,

[13] to keep the commandments of Yahweh and his statutes, which I am commanding you today for your good?

[14] Behold, to Yahweh your *Elohim* belong the heavens and the heaven of heavens, the earth and all that is in it.

[15] Yet it was on your fathers alone that Yahweh set his affection, to love them; and he chose their offspring after them — you — above all peoples, as it is today.

[16] Circumcise therefore the foreskin of your heart, and be no longer stiff-necked.

[17] For Yahweh your *Elohim* is the *Elohim* of gods and the Lord of lords — the great *Elohim*, the mighty, the fearsome — who shows no partiality and takes no bribe.

[18] He executes justice for the fatherless and the widow, and loves the foreigner, giving him food and clothing.

[19] Love the foreigner, therefore, for you were foreigners in the land of Egypt.

[20] You shall fear Yahweh your *Elohim*. Him you shall serve, to him you shall hold fast, and by his name you shall swear.

[21] He is your praise; he is your *Elohim*, who has done for you these great and fearsome things that your eyes have seen.

[22] Your fathers went down to Egypt as seventy persons, and now Yahweh your *Elohim* has made you as numerous as the stars of heaven.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"shittim" → "acacia"** (v3).
- **"ten commandments" → "ten words"** (v4) — as in ch 5.
- **"circumcise the foreskin of your heart"** (v16) — Hebrew וּמַלְתֶּם אֵת עָרְלַת לְבַבְכֶם *u-maltem et orlat levavkhem*. Metaphor for inner covenant transformation. Paul develops: Rom 2:29, Col 2:11.
- **"God of gods, and Lord of lords" → "*Elohim* of gods and the Lord of lords"** (v17) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי הָאֱלֹהִים וַאֲדֹנֵי הָאֲדֹנִים *Elohei ha-*Elohim* va-Adonei ha-Adonim*. Superlative construction.
- **"regardeth not persons, nor taketh reward" → "shows no partiality and takes no bribe"** (v17).
- **"stranger" → "foreigner"** (v18, 19).
- **"raiment" → "clothing"** (v18).
- **"threescore and ten" → "seventy"** (v22) — modernized.

### Notes
- **New tablets, ark of wood** (v1–5) — the ark here is made by Moses of simple wood. Ex 37 tells a different story (Bezalel makes an elaborate gold-overlaid ark). Harmonizers: this is the provisional ark; Ex 37 the permanent one. Source-critics: variant tradition.
- **Parenthesis on wilderness stations** (v6–9) — appears to interrupt the narrative; preserves traditions about Aaron's death and Levite selection in narrative summary form.
- **"What does Yahweh ask of you?"** (v12) — central Deuteronomic statement. Quoted by Micah 6:8 (reframed as justice, mercy, humble walk).
- **"Circumcise the heart"** (v16) — Deut's key theological metaphor. Later: Deut 30:6 promises Yahweh himself will do this. NT: internalization of covenant.
- **Love of foreigners** (v19) — repeated grounding in exodus-experience: "you were foreigners." Unique in ancient law — the immigrant as love-command.
- **"Seventy persons"** (v22) — the family of Jacob entering Egypt (Gen 46:27). From seventy to stars-of-heaven: the promise fulfilled.
