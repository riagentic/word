# Deuteronomy 5

[1] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ called to all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and said to them: "Hear, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, the statutes and the ordinances that I am speaking in your ears today, and you shall learn them and keep to do them.

[2] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ cut a covenant with us at ⟦horeb|Chorev|Horeb⟧.

[3] Not with our fathers did ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ cut this covenant, but with us, we, these here today, all of us living.

[4] Face to face ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke with you on the mountain, from the midst of the fire —

[5] I was standing between ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and you at that time, to tell you the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for you feared because of the fire and did not go up on the mountain — saying:

[6] 'I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who brought you out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, from the house of slaves.

[7] You shall have no other gods before my face.

[8] You shall not make for yourself a carved image, any form that is in the heavens above, or that is on the earth beneath, or that is in the waters under the earth.

[9] You shall not bow down to them, and you shall not serve them; for I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, am a jealous ⟦el|El|God⟧, visiting the iniquity of fathers upon sons, and upon the third and upon the fourth generation, for those who hate me,

[10] and doing ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ to thousands, for those who love me and keep my commandments.

[11] You shall not lift up the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for emptiness, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will not clear the one who lifts up his name for emptiness.

[12] Keep the day of ⟦shabbat|the Shabbat|the Sabbath⟧, to sanctify it, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ commanded you.

[13] Six days you shall labor and do all your work,

[14] and the seventh day is ⟦shabbat|a Shabbat|a Sabbath⟧ to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. You shall not do any work, you and your son and your daughter and your slave and your slave woman and your ox and your donkey and all your beasts, and your sojourner who is in your gates, so that your slave and your slave woman may rest like you.

[15] And you shall remember that you were a slave in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ brought you out from there with a strong hand and with an outstretched arm; therefore ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ commanded you to make the day of ⟦shabbat|the Shabbat|the Sabbath⟧.

[16] Honor your father and your mother, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ commanded you, so that your days may be long, and so that it may go well with you, upon the ground that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you.

[17] You shall not murder.

[18] And you shall not commit adultery.

[19] And you shall not steal.

[20] And you shall not answer against your neighbor as an empty witness.

[21] And you shall not covet the wife of your neighbor; and you shall not crave the house of your neighbor, his field, or his slave, or his slave woman, his ox, or his donkey, or anything that is your neighbor's.'

[22] These words ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to all your assembly on the mountain, from the midst of the fire, the cloud and the dark cloud, with a loud voice, and he added no more. And he wrote them on two tablets of stone and gave them to me.

[23] And it came to pass, when you heard the voice from the midst of the darkness, and the mountain was burning with fire, that you came near to me, all the heads of your tribes and your elders,

[24] and you said, 'Behold, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has shown us his glory and his greatness, and his voice we have heard from the midst of the fire; this day we have seen that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ speaks with man, and he lives.

[25] And now, why should we die? For this great fire will consume us. If we hear the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ any more, we shall die.

[26] For who is there of all flesh who has heard the voice of the living ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ speaking from the midst of the fire, as we have, and lived?

[27] Come near, you, and hear all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ says; and you, you shall speak to us all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ speaks to you, and we will hear and do.'

[28] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ heard the voice of your words when you spoke to me, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, 'I have heard the voice of the words of this people, which they have spoken to you; they have done well in all that they have spoken.

[29] Would that this heart of theirs were theirs, to fear me and to keep all my commandments all the days, so that it may go well with them and with their sons forever!

[30] Go, say to them, "Turn yourselves back to your tents."

[31] And you, stand here with me, and let me speak to you all the commandment and the statutes and the ordinances that you shall teach them, and they shall do them in the land that I am giving to them to possess it.'

[32] And you shall keep to do as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ commanded you; you shall not turn aside to the right or to the left.

[33] In all the way that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ commanded you, you shall walk, so that you may live, and it may go well with you, and you may prolong days in the land that you shall take possession of.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v7 the same word is "gods".
- **"You"** — Moses speaks to Israel in the plural (v1b–5, 22–33), except the first "Hear, Israel" (singular). In the ten words (v6–21) every "you" and "your" is singular, as in Exod 20.
- **"And Moses called all Israel" → "And Moses called to all Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּקְרָא … אֶל "called to". **"Hear, O Israel" → "Hear, Israel"** — Hebrew שְׁמַע יִשְׂרָאֵל, two words (as 6:4). **"the statutes and judgments" → "the statutes and the ordinances"** (v1, 31) — Hebrew הַחֻקִּים וְהַמִּשְׁפָּטִים: חֹק "statute", what is laid down; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment", here of a ruling given, "ordinance". **"which I speak" → "that I am speaking"** — a participle.
- **"that ye may learn them, and keep, and do them" → "and you shall learn them and keep to do them"** (v1) — Hebrew וּלְמַדְתֶּם אֹתָם וּשְׁמַרְתֶּם לַעֲשֹׂתָם: two verbs joined by "and" to the command, not a clause of purpose; שָׁמַר לַעֲשׂוֹת is "keep to do", "watch so as to do" (so v32; "be careful", "observe" are not separate words).
- **"made a covenant" → "cut a covenant"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew כָּרַת בְּרִית.
- **"but with us, even us, who are all of us here alive this day" → "but with us, we, these here today, all of us living"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּי אִתָּנוּ אֲנַחְנוּ אֵלֶּה פֹה הַיּוֹם כֻּלָּנוּ חַיִּים, word for word; the words are heaped up without a verb.
- **"The LORD talked with you face to face" → "Face to face Yahweh spoke with you"** (v4) — Hebrew פָּנִים בְּפָנִים, literally "face in face", first in the sentence (the usual phrase is פָּנִים אֶל־פָּנִים, Exod 33:11; Deut 34:10). **"in the mount" → "on the mountain"** (v4, 5, 22) — Hebrew בָּהָר.
- **"to shew you" → "to tell you"** (v5) — Hebrew לְהַגִּיד. **"ye were afraid by reason of the fire" → "you feared because of the fire"** — Hebrew יְרֵאתֶם מִפְּנֵי הָאֵשׁ, literally "from the face of the fire". "Saying" (לֵאמֹר) continues v4: Yahweh spoke … saying.
- **v6–21 and Exod 20:2–17** — where the Hebrew is the same as in Exod 20, the English is the same. Identical: v6 (20:2), v7 (20:3), v11 (20:7), v13 (20:9), v17 (20:13). The differences of the Hebrew, all kept visible:
  - v8 (20:4): פֶסֶל כָּל־תְּמוּנָה "a carved image, any form" — Exodus has "and / or any form" (וְכָל־).
  - v9 (20:5): "**and** upon the third" (וְעַל־שִׁלֵּשִׁים) — Exodus has no "and" there.
  - v10 (20:6): the written text (ketiv) has מצותו "his commandments"; the reading tradition (qere) has מִצְוֺתָי "my commandments", as Exodus. The verse follows the qere.
  - v12 (20:8): שָׁמוֹר "Keep" for זָכוֹר "Remember"; and the added "as Yahweh your Elohim commanded you".
  - v14 (20:10): "**and** your slave"; the added "and your ox and your donkey and all" before "your beast(s)"; and the added purpose "so that your slave and your slave woman may rest like you".
  - v15 (20:11): another reason for the Shabbat. Exodus: Yahweh made the heavens and the earth in six days and rested on the seventh, and so blessed the day. Here: Israel was a slave in Egypt and Yahweh brought him out. "Remember" (זָכַר), the first word of the Shabbat command in Exodus, stands here in the reason.
  - v16 (20:12): the added "as Yahweh your Elohim commanded you" and "and so that it may go well with you".
  - v18–21 (20:14–17): each begins with "And" (וְלֹא); in Exodus each stands alone.
  - v20 (20:16): עֵד שָׁוְא "an empty witness" for עֵד שָׁקֶר "a false witness".
  - v21 (20:17): the wife stands first and alone, with חָמַד "covet"; then the house, with another verb, הִתְאַוָּה "crave"; "his field" is added; "his ox" has no "and / or" before it. Exodus has the house first and "covet" twice.
- **"from the house of bondage" → "from the house of slaves"** (v6) — Hebrew מִבֵּית עֲבָדִים, the plural of עֶבֶד "slave" (v14, 15, 21).
- **"Thou shalt have none other gods before me" → "You shall have no other gods before my face"** (v7) — Hebrew עַל־פָּנָיַ "upon / before / against my face": in my presence, beside me, in defiance of me. The verse does not choose (see Exod 20:3).
- **"any graven image, or any likeness of any thing that is in heaven above" → "a carved image, any form that is in the heavens above"** (v8) — Hebrew פֶּסֶל *pesel*, a figure hewn or carved; תְּמוּנָה *temunah*, the visible form of a thing. Without "and" the two stand side by side ("a carved image, [that is,] any form"), or as one phrase, "a carved image of any form". "Of any thing" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"nor serve them" → "and you shall not serve them"** (v9) — Hebrew וְלֹא תָעָבְדֵם (pointed as a passive-causative, "be made to serve them"; see Exod 20:5). **"a jealous God" → "a jealous El"** — Hebrew אֵל קַנָּא; "zealous" is the same word.
- **"visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate me" → "visiting the iniquity of fathers upon sons, and upon the third and upon the fourth generation, for those who hate me"** (v9) — Hebrew פֹּקֵד עֲוֺן אָבוֹת עַל־בָּנִים וְעַל־שִׁלֵּשִׁים וְעַל־רִבֵּעִים לְשֹׂנְאָי. "Third ones" and "fourth ones": the word "generation" is supplied. לְשֹׂנְאָי is "to / for / of those who hate me".
- **"And shewing mercy unto thousands of them that love me" → "and doing chesed to thousands, for those who love me"** (v10) — Hebrew וְעֹשֶׂה חֶסֶד לַאֲלָפִים לְאֹהֲבַי: עָשָׂה "do", חֶסֶד *chesed* "steadfast love". The Hebrew says "to thousands"; whether thousands of persons or of generations it does not say here (7:9 has "to a thousand generations"). The earlier "to the thousandth generation" was an interpretation.
- **"take the name … in vain" → "lift up the name … for emptiness"** (v11) — Hebrew נָשָׂא "lift up, bear, take up" and לַשָּׁוְא "for what is empty": falsely, to no purpose, for a worthless thing (see Exod 20:7). **"will not hold him guiltless" → "will not clear"** — Hebrew לֹא יְנַקֶּה.
- **"Keep the sabbath day to sanctify it" → "Keep the day of the Shabbat, to sanctify it"** (v12) — Hebrew שָׁמוֹר, the infinitive absolute as a standing command (like זָכוֹר in Exodus); שָׁמַר is "keep, watch, guard", the verb of v1, 10, 29, 32.
- **"But the seventh day is the sabbath of the LORD thy God: in it thou shalt not do any work" → "and the seventh day is a Shabbat to Yahweh your *Elohim*. You shall not do any work"** (v14) — Hebrew וְ "and"; "a Shabbat" has no article; "in it" is not in the Hebrew. **"thy manservant … thy maidservant … thy cattle … thy stranger" → "your slave … your slave woman … your beasts … your sojourner"** — Hebrew עֶבֶד, אָמָה, בְּהֵמָה (a singular for the whole, "all your beast"), גֵּר. **"may rest as well as thou" → "may rest like you"** — Hebrew יָנוּחַ, from נוּחַ "rest" (not שָׁבַת "cease"); כָּמוֹךָ "like you".
- **"thou wast a servant" → "you were a slave"** (v15) — Hebrew עֶבֶד, the word of v14: the slave is to rest because Israel was one. **"through a mighty hand and by a stretched out arm" → "with a strong hand and with an outstretched arm"** — Hebrew בְּיָד חֲזָקָה וּבִזְרֹעַ נְטוּיָה, the one preposition בְּ twice ("with", as Exod 13:9; Deut 4:34; 26:8). **"to keep the sabbath day" → "to make the day of the Shabbat"** — Hebrew לַעֲשׂוֹת "to do, to make" (as Exod 31:16), not the "keep" of v12.
- **"that thy days may be prolonged, and that it may go well with thee, in the land" → "so that your days may be long, and so that it may go well with you, upon the ground"** (v16) — Hebrew לְמַעַן twice; יִיטַב לָךְ is literally "it may be good for you" (so v29, 33); הָאֲדָמָה is "the ground, the soil", not אֶרֶץ "land". **"which the LORD thy God giveth thee" → "that Yahweh your Elohim is giving to you"** — Hebrew נֹתֵן לָךְ, a participle, worded as everywhere in the book (4:40).
- **"Thou shalt not kill" → "You shall not murder"** (v17) — Hebrew רָצַח *ratsach*, its own verb, not the common words for killing (see Exod 20:13).
- **"Neither shalt thou bear false witness against thy neighbour" → "And you shall not answer against your neighbor as an empty witness"** (v20) — Hebrew עָנָה בְּ "answer against", to give evidence in court; עֵד שָׁוְא "a witness of emptiness": שָׁוְא is the word of v11, what is empty — false, groundless, worthless. "As" is supplied.
- **"Neither shalt thou desire thy neighbour's wife, neither shalt thou covet thy neighbour's house" → "And you shall not covet the wife of your neighbor; and you shall not crave the house of your neighbor"** (v21) — Hebrew תַחְמֹד (חָמַד "covet", the verb of Exod 20:17) and תִתְאַוֶּה (הִתְאַוָּה "crave", the verb of Num 11:4, 34). KJV has the two English verbs the other way round. In the Hebrew a break (the closed-paragraph mark) stands after "the wife of your neighbor".
- **"with a great voice" → "with a loud voice"** (v22) — Hebrew קוֹל גָּדוֹל, with no preposition ("with" is supplied). **"the thick darkness" → "the dark cloud"** — Hebrew הָעֲרָפֶל (as Exod 20:21). **"and he added no more"** kept — Hebrew וְלֹא יָסָף "and he did not add". **"two tables of stone"** — Hebrew לֻחֹת אֲבָנִים, "tablets of stones" (plural).
- **"(for the mountain did burn with fire,)" → "and the mountain was burning with fire"** (v23) — Hebrew וְהָהָר בֹּעֵר בָּאֵשׁ, "and", with a participle.
- **"that God doth talk with man, and he liveth" → "that Elohim speaks with man, and he lives"** (v24) — Hebrew אֶת־הָאָדָם "with the man", mankind. הֵן "behold".
- **"if we hear … any more" kept** (v25) — Hebrew אִם־יֹסְפִים אֲנַחְנוּ לִשְׁמֹעַ … עוֹד, literally "if we are adding to hear … again".
- **"the living God" → "the living Elohim"** (v26) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים חַיִּים: the adjective "living" is plural, in agreement with the form of *Elohim*; the one God is meant.
- **"and speak thou unto us" → "and you, you shall speak to us"** (v27) — Hebrew וְאַתְּ תְּדַבֵּר, the pronoun expressed (written here in the short form אַתְּ). **"and we will hear it, and do it" → "and we will hear and do"** — Hebrew וְשָׁמַעְנוּ וְעָשִׂינוּ, no object (cf. Exod 24:7).
- **"they have well said all that they have spoken" → "they have done well in all that they have spoken"** (v28) — Hebrew הֵיטִיבוּ כָּל־אֲשֶׁר דִּבֵּרוּ, "they have made good all that they spoke"; "in" is supplied. "Voice" (קוֹל) twice: "the voice of your words".
- **"O that there were such an heart in them, that they would fear me … always" → "Would that this heart of theirs were theirs, to fear me … all the days"** (v29) — Hebrew מִי־יִתֵּן וְהָיָה לְבָבָם זֶה לָהֶם, literally "who will give, and this their heart would be theirs": the Hebrew way of saying a wish (as Exod 16:3). כָּל־הַיָּמִים "all the days". **"their children" → "their sons"** — Hebrew בְּנֵיהֶם.
- **"Get you into your tents again" → "Turn yourselves back to your tents"** (v30) — Hebrew שׁוּבוּ לָכֶם: שׁוּב "return, turn back", with לָכֶם "for you", which turns the command back on those addressed.
- **"all the commandments" → "all the commandment"** (v31) — Hebrew כָּל־הַמִּצְוָה, singular: the whole charge. **"and I will speak" → "and let me speak"** — Hebrew וַאֲדַבְּרָה, the form of will or purpose. **"that they may do them" → "and they shall do them"** — Hebrew וְעָשׂוּ. **"which I give them" → "that I am giving to them"** — Hebrew נֹתֵן לָהֶם, a participle with לְ "to", as 4:40.
- **"Ye shall observe to do therefore" → "And you shall keep to do"** (v32) — as v1; "therefore" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"in all the ways" → "In all the way"** (v33) — Hebrew בְּכָל־הַדֶּרֶךְ, singular, first in the sentence. **"prolong your days" → "prolong days"** — Hebrew וְהַאֲרַכְתֶּם יָמִים, no "your". **"which ye shall possess" → "that you shall take possession of"** — Hebrew תִּירָשׁוּן, the verb of "to possess it" (v31).

### Notes
- **The ten words a second time** — Deut 5:6–21 gives the ten words of Exod 20:2–17 again; the differences of wording are listed in Changes. The two chapters are each translated from their own Hebrew.
- **"With us, we, these here today"** (v3) — the generation that stands in Moab is addressed as the one that stood at Horeb. Deuteronomy's "today" binds every generation of hearers into the covenant.
- **Ten Words** — the numbering varies by tradition (see the note at Exod 20). The paragraph break inside v21 (after "the wife of your neighbor") agrees with the counting that makes two commandments of the coveting.
- **Mediator role** (v5, 27) — Moses stands between Yahweh and the people; the people themselves ask for it, and Yahweh approves (v28).
- **"Would that"** (v29) — Yahweh's wish about Israel's heart. Deuteronomy returns to the heart in 10:16 and 30:6.
