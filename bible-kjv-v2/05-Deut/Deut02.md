# Deuteronomy 2

[1] "Then we turned and set out into the wilderness, by the way of the Sea of Reeds, as Yahweh had spoken to me; and we skirted Mount Seir for many days.

[2] Yahweh spoke to me, saying,

[3] 'You have skirted this hill country long enough; turn northward.

[4] Command the people, saying, "You are about to pass through the territory of your brothers, the children of Esau, who dwell in Seir; and they will be afraid of you. So be very careful.

[5] Do not contend with them; for I will not give you of their land, not even a foot's breadth; because I have given Mount Seir to Esau as a possession.

[6] You shall buy food from them for money, that you may eat; and you shall also buy water from them for money, that you may drink.

[7] For Yahweh your *Elohim* has blessed you in all the work of your hand; he has known your walking through this great wilderness. These forty years Yahweh your *Elohim* has been with you; you have lacked nothing."'

[8] So we passed by our brothers, the children of Esau, who dwell in Seir, away from the road of the Arabah, away from Elath and Ezion-geber. We turned and passed on by the way of the wilderness of Moab.

[9] Yahweh said to me, 'Do not harass Moab or contend with them in battle, for I will not give you of their land for a possession, because I have given Ar to the children of Lot as a possession.'

[10] (The Emim dwelt there formerly — a people great and many and tall, like the Anakim.

[11] They also are counted as Rephaim, like the Anakim; but the Moabites call them Emim.

[12] The Horim also dwelt in Seir formerly, but the children of Esau dispossessed them and destroyed them from before them and dwelt in their place, just as Israel did to the land of their possession which Yahweh gave them.)

[13] 'Now rise up and cross over the wadi Zered.' So we crossed over the wadi Zered.

[14] The period during which we came from Kadesh-barnea until we crossed the wadi Zered was thirty-eight years, until the entire generation of men of war were consumed from the midst of the camp, as Yahweh had sworn to them.

[15] For indeed the hand of Yahweh was against them, to destroy them from the midst of the camp, until they were consumed.

[16] So it was, when all the men of war were finished and dead from the midst of the people,

[17] that Yahweh spoke to me, saying,

[18] 'Today you are about to pass through the territory of Moab, by Ar.

[19] When you come near the children of Ammon, do not harass them or contend with them, for I will not give you of the land of the children of Ammon for a possession, because I have given it to the children of Lot as a possession.'

[20] (It is also counted as a land of Rephaim; Rephaim formerly dwelt there, but the Ammonites call them Zamzummim —

[21] a people great and many and tall, like the Anakim — but Yahweh destroyed them before them, and they dispossessed them and dwelt in their place.

[22] Just as he did for the children of Esau who dwell in Seir, when he destroyed the Horim before them; they dispossessed them and dwelt in their place to this day.

[23] As for the Avvim who dwelt in villages as far as Gaza, the Caphtorim, who came from Caphtor, destroyed them and dwelt in their place.)

[24] 'Rise up, set out, and cross over the wadi Arnon. Behold, I have given into your hand Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land. Begin to take possession; contend with him in battle.

[25] Today I will begin to put the dread of you and the fear of you upon the peoples under all the heavens, who shall hear the report of you; they shall tremble and be in anguish because of you.'

[26] So I sent messengers from the wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon king of Heshbon with words of peace, saying,

[27] 'Let me pass through your land. I will go only along the road; I will not turn aside to the right or to the left.

[28] You shall sell me food for money, that I may eat, and give me water for money, that I may drink. Only let me pass through on foot,

[29] as the children of Esau who dwell in Seir and the Moabites who dwell in Ar did for me, until I cross over the Jordan into the land that Yahweh our *Elohim* is giving us.'

[30] But Sihon king of Heshbon would not let us pass by him, for Yahweh your *Elohim* hardened his spirit and made his heart obstinate, in order to deliver him into your hand, as is the case today.

[31] Yahweh said to me, 'Behold, I have begun to give Sihon and his land before you. Begin to take possession, that you may inherit his land.'

[32] Then Sihon came out against us, he and all his people, for battle at Jahaz.

[33] And Yahweh our *Elohim* delivered him before us, and we struck him down with his sons and all his people.

[34] We took all his cities at that time and devoted to destruction every city — men, women, and little ones; we left no survivor.

[35] Only the livestock we took as plunder for ourselves, with the spoil of the cities that we captured.

[36] From Aroer on the edge of the wadi Arnon, and from the city in the wadi, as far as Gilead, there was not a city too strong for us; Yahweh our *Elohim* delivered everything over to us.

[37] Only to the land of the children of Ammon you did not come near — all the banks of the wadi Jabbok, the cities of the hill country, and wherever Yahweh our *Elohim* had forbidden us.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"compassed" → "skirted"** (v1, 3) — archaic.
- **"brook" → "wadi"** (v13, 14, 24, 36, 37) — Hebrew נַחַל *nachal*. Seasonal stream, not permanent brook.
- **"giants" → "Rephaim"** (v11, 20) — Hebrew רְפָאִים *Rephaim*. Specific ancient people; not generic giants. Emim, Zamzummim, and Horim are local/regional names for the same or related groups.
- **"Emims / Horims / Zamzummims / Anakims / Avims / Caphtorims"** — modernized plurals (KJV doubled them). The Hebrew plural ending is *-im*; KJV adds *s* redundantly.
- **"Azzah" → "Gaza"** (v23) — Hebrew עַזָּה *Azzah*; standard English "Gaza."
- **"meddle with them / distress them not" → "contend with them / harass them"** (v5, 9, 19).
- **"meat" → "food"** (v6, 28) — archaic English.
- **"utterly destroyed" → "devoted to destruction"** (v34) — *cherem*.

### Notes
- **Related peoples protected** — Edom (Esau's descendants), Moab and Ammon (Lot's descendants) are kin to Israel. Yahweh gives them their lands; Israel may pass but not conquer. Contrast with the Amorites, who are in the promised land proper.
- **Previous giant-peoples** (v10–12, 20–23) — ethnographic notes in parentheses. The text acknowledges Israel is not the first to dispossess: Edom did so, Ammon did so, even the Caphtorim (= Philistines) did so. Dispossession is a pan-Canaanite pattern; only Yahweh's Israel-directed pattern is specifically framed.
- **Caphtor = Crete / Aegean** (v23) — the Philistines' origin. Confirmed archaeologically with Mycenaean / Aegean material culture on the southern Levantine coast.
- **Sihon's hardened heart** (v30) — parallel to Pharaoh. Divine sovereignty through human refusal.
- **38 years** (v14) — the actual wilderness wandering. Years 1 and 40 are active; 38 in between.
- **First *cherem*** (v34) — Sihon's cities devoted to total destruction. Deuteronomy's war-theology applied prospectively in ch 7, 20.
