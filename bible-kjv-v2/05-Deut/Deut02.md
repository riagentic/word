# Deuteronomy 2

[1] "And we turned and set out into the wilderness, by the way of the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had spoken to me; and we went around the hill country of Seir many days.

[2] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, saying,

[3] 'Enough for you is the going around this hill country; turn for yourselves northward.

[4] And command the people, saying, "You are passing through the border of your brothers, the sons of ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧, who dwell in Seir; and they will fear you, and you shall take great heed.

[5] Do not provoke them, for I will not give you of their land so much as the treading of the sole of a foot; for as a possession to ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧ I have given the hill country of Seir.

[6] Food you shall buy from them for money, and you shall eat; and also water you shall purchase from them for money, and you shall drink.

[7] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has blessed you in all the work of your hand; he has known your going through this great wilderness. These forty years ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has been with you; you have not lacked a thing."'

[8] And we passed on, away from our brothers the sons of ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧, who dwell in Seir, away from the way of the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧, from ⟦elath|Eilat|Elath⟧ and from ⟦ezion-geber|Etzyon Gever|Ezion-geber⟧. And we turned and passed on by the way of the wilderness of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧.

[9] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, 'Do not harass ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, and do not provoke them to war, for I will not give you of his land a possession; for to the sons of Lot I have given Ar as a possession.'

[10] The Emim formerly dwelt in it, a people great and many and tall as the ⟦anakim|Anaqim|Anakim⟧.

[11] ⟦rephaim|Refaim|Rephaim⟧ they are reckoned, they also, as the ⟦anakim|Anaqim|Anakim⟧; and the ⟦moabites|Moavim|Moabites⟧ call them Emim.

[12] And in Seir the ⟦horites|Chorim|Horites⟧ dwelt formerly, and the sons of ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧ dispossessed them and destroyed them from before them and dwelt in their place, as ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ did to the land of his possession, which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave to them.

[13] 'Now rise and pass over for yourselves the wadi Zered.' And we passed over the wadi Zered.

[14] And the days that we went from ⟦kadesh-barnea|Qadesh Barnea|Kadesh-barnea⟧ until we passed over the wadi Zered were thirty-eight years, until all the generation, the men of war, had come to an end from the midst of the camp, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had sworn to them.

[15] And also the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was against them, to throw them into confusion from the midst of the camp, until they had come to an end.

[16] And it came to pass, when all the men of war had come to an end, dying from the midst of the people,

[17] that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to me, saying,

[18] 'You are passing over today the border of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, Ar.

[19] And you will come near, opposite the sons of Ammon; do not harass them and do not provoke them, for I will not give of the land of the sons of Ammon to you a possession; for to the sons of Lot I have given it as a possession.'

[20] A land of ⟦rephaim|Refaim|Rephaim⟧ it is reckoned, it also; ⟦rephaim|Refaim|Rephaim⟧ dwelt in it formerly, and the ⟦ammonites|Ammonim|Ammonites⟧ call them Zamzummim,

[21] a people great and many and tall as the ⟦anakim|Anaqim|Anakim⟧; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ destroyed them from before them, and they dispossessed them and dwelt in their place,

[22] as he did for the sons of ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧, who dwell in Seir, when he destroyed the ⟦horites|Chori|Horite⟧ from before them, and they dispossessed them and have dwelt in their place to this day.

[23] And the Avvim, who dwelt in villages as far as ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧: ⟦caphtorim|Kaftorim|Caphtorim⟧, who came out from ⟦caphtor|Kaftor|Caphtor⟧, destroyed them and dwelt in their place.

[24] 'Rise, set out and pass over the wadi Arnon. See, I have given into your hand ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧ king of ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧, the ⟦amorite|Emori|Amorite⟧, and his land. Begin, take possession, and provoke him to war.

[25] This day I will begin to put the dread of you and the fear of you upon the face of the peoples under all the heavens, who will hear the report of you and be agitated and be in anguish because of you.'

[26] And I sent messengers from the wilderness of ⟦kedemoth|Qedemot|Kedemoth⟧ to ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧ king of ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧, words of ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, saying,

[27] 'Let me pass through your land. By the way, by the way I will go; I will not turn aside to the right or to the left.

[28] Food for money you shall sell me, and I will eat; and water for money you shall give me, and I will drink. Only let me pass through on my feet,

[29] as the sons of ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧, who dwell in Seir, did for me, and the ⟦moabites|Moavim|Moabites⟧, who dwell in Ar; until I pass over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ into the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to us.'

[30] And ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧ king of ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧ was not willing to let us pass through him; for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had made hard his spirit and strengthened his heart, in order to give him into your hand, as at this day.

[31] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, 'See, I have begun to set before you ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧ and his land. Begin, take possession, to possess his land.'

[32] And ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧ came out to meet us, he and all his people, to battle, to ⟦jahaz|Yahtzah|Jahaz⟧.

[33] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ set him before us, and we struck down him and his sons and all his people.

[34] And we captured all his cities at that time, and we devoted to destruction every city of men, and the women and the little ones; we left no survivor.

[35] Only the beasts we plundered for ourselves, and the spoil of the cities that we captured.

[36] From Aroer, which is on the edge of the wadi Arnon, and the city that is in the wadi, and as far as ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧, there was not a town that was too high for us; the whole ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ set before us.

[37] Only to the land of the sons of Ammon you did not come near, all the side of the wadi ⟦jabbok|Yabboq|Jabbok⟧ and the cities of the hill country, and all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ commanded.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"children of Esau / Lot / Ammon" → "sons of"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"took our journey … we compassed mount Seir" → "set out … we went around the hill country of Seir"** (v1, 3) — Hebrew נָסַע "pull up, set out"; סָבַב "go around"; הַר of a region is "hill country" (as 1:2). The words of the command in Num 14:25 ("turn and set out … into the wilderness, by the way of the Sea of Reeds") are told here as done.
- **"Ye have compassed this mountain long enough: turn you northward" → "Enough for you is the going around this hill country; turn for yourselves northward"** (v3) — Hebrew רַב־לָכֶם סֹב, literally "much for you is the going around" (as 1:6); פְּנוּ לָכֶם.
- **"Ye are to pass through the coast of your brethren … take ye good heed unto yourselves therefore" → "You are passing through the border of your brothers … and you shall take great heed"** (v4) — Hebrew עֹבְרִים, a participle; גְּבוּל "border", also the land within it; וְנִשְׁמַרְתֶּם מְאֹד, literally "and you shall keep yourselves greatly".
- **"Meddle not with them … no, not so much as a foot breadth; because I have given mount Seir unto Esau for a possession" → "Do not provoke them … so much as the treading of the sole of a foot; for as a possession to Esau I have given the hill country of Seir"** (v5) — Hebrew הִתְגָּרָה בְּ "stir oneself up against, provoke" (so v9, 19, 24; with מִלְחָמָה "to war"); עַד מִדְרַךְ כַּף־רָגֶל, literally "as far as the treading place of the sole of a foot"; יְרֻשָּׁה "possession", from יָרַשׁ "take possession".
- **"Ye shall buy meat of them for money … ye shall also buy water" → "Food you shall buy from them for money … water you shall purchase"** (v6, 28) — two verbs: שָׁבַר (the verb for buying grain, Gen 42) and כָּרָה "purchase, bargain for". כֶּסֶף is "silver", here of payment. "That ye may eat", "that ye may drink" are plain "and you shall eat", "and you shall drink" in the Hebrew.
- **"he knoweth thy walking through this great wilderness … thou hast lacked nothing" → "he has known your going through this great wilderness … you have not lacked a thing"** (v7) — Hebrew יָדַע, a perfect; דָּבָר "a thing, a word". The whole verse speaks to "you" in the singular. The Hebrew has no quotation marks: where the command to the people ends (after v6 or v7) is not marked.
- **"when we passed by from our brethren … through the way of the plain from Elath" → "we passed on, away from our brothers … away from the way of the Arabah, from Elath"** (v8) — Hebrew וַנַּעֲבֹר מֵאֵת … מִדֶּרֶךְ, "we passed on from … from the way of"; a main verb, not "when". The Masoretic Text has a paragraph break in the middle of the verse, after "Ezion-geber".
- **"Distress not the Moabites, neither contend with them in battle: for I will not give thee of their land" → "Do not harass Moab, and do not provoke them to war, for I will not give you of his land"** (v9) — Hebrew אַל־תָּצַר אֶת־מוֹאָב "do not treat Moab as a foe" (the name, not "Moabites"); "them" and then "his" as the Hebrew has them. "You" is singular (so v18, 19, 24, 25, 30, 31, 37).
- **"The Emims … the Anakims … the Horims … Zamzummims … the Avims … the Caphtorims"** (v10–12, 20–23) — the Hebrew plural ending is *-im*; KJV adds *s*. The brackets of KJV (v20–23) and of the earlier form of this translation (v10–12) are not in the Hebrew.
- **"Which also were accounted giants, as the Anakims" → "Rephaim they are reckoned, they also, as the Anakim"** (v11, 20) — Hebrew רְפָאִים *Rephaim*, the name of an ancient people; "giants" is the Septuagint's rendering (from memory). חָשַׁב nifal "be reckoned". The verbs "are reckoned", "call" are imperfects: what is still said.
- **"the children of Esau succeeded them, when they had destroyed them" → "the sons of Esau dispossessed them and destroyed them"** (v12, 21, 22) — Hebrew יָרַשׁ with persons as object, "dispossess"; הִשְׁמִיד "destroy". **"as Israel did unto the land of his possession, which the LORD gave unto them" kept** — the Hebrew has a perfect, "did": the taking of the land is spoken of as done.
- **"Now rise up, said I, and get you over the brook Zered" → "Now rise and pass over for yourselves the wadi Zered"** (v13) — "said I" is not in the Hebrew; the words follow v9 as the speech of Yahweh. עִבְרוּ לָכֶם; עָבַר "pass over" (so of the Arnon, v24, and the Jordan, v29); נַחַל, a stream bed, dry or running.
- **"the space in which we came … was thirty and eight years; until all the generation of the men of war were wasted out from among the host" → "the days that we went … were thirty-eight years, until all the generation, the men of war, had come to an end from the midst of the camp"** (v14) — Hebrew הַיָּמִים "the days"; תַּם "come to an end" (Num 14:33, 35); מַחֲנֶה "camp". The Hebrew number is "thirty and eight year".
- **"For indeed the hand of the LORD was against them, to destroy them" → "And also the hand of Yahweh was against them, to throw them into confusion"** (v15) — Hebrew וְגַם "and also"; לְהֻמָּם, from הָמַם "throw into confusion, rout" (Exod 14:24), not the "destroy" of v12. It sounds like תֻּמָּם "their coming to an end" at the close of the verse.
- **"when all the men of war were consumed and dead from among the people" → "when all the men of war had come to an end, dying from the midst of the people"** (v16) — Hebrew תַּמּוּ … לָמוּת, literally "had finished to die".
- **"Thou art to pass over through Ar, the coast of Moab, this day" → "You are passing over today the border of Moab, Ar"** (v18) — the Hebrew order; Ar stands beside "the border of Moab" with no word between.
- **"when thou comest nigh over against the children of Ammon, distress them not, nor meddle with them" → "you will come near, opposite the sons of Ammon; do not harass them and do not provoke them"** (v19) — Hebrew מוּל "opposite"; צוּר and הִתְגָּרָה as v9.
- **"That also was accounted a land of giants: giants dwelt therein in old time" → "A land of Rephaim it is reckoned, it also; Rephaim dwelt in it formerly"** (v20) — Hebrew לְפָנִים "formerly" (v10, 12).
- **"when he destroyed the Horims from before them … even unto this day" → "when he destroyed the Horite from before them … to this day"** (v22) — Hebrew הַחֹרִי, a singular with the article for the people (v12 has the plural).
- **"the Avims which dwelt in Hazerim, even unto Azzah" → "the Avvim, who dwelt in villages as far as Gaza"** (v23) — Hebrew בַּחֲצֵרִים "in villages", unwalled settlements, not a place name; עַזָּה *Azzah*, Gaza. The sentence begins with the Avvim and turns to the Caphtorim; it is left so.
- **"pass over the river Arnon … Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon … begin to possess it, and contend with him in battle" → "pass over the wadi Arnon … Sihon king of Heshbon, the Amorite … Begin, take possession, and provoke him to war"** (v24) — the Hebrew order of the titles; הָחֵל רָשׁ, two commands side by side; רְאֵה "see".
- **"upon the nations that are under the whole heaven … and shall tremble, and be in anguish because of thee" → "upon the face of the peoples under all the heavens … and be agitated and be in anguish because of you"** (v25) — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי הָעַמִּים; פַּחַד "dread" and יִרְאָה "fear"; רָגַז "be agitated, tremble"; חוּל "writhe" as one in labor (the noun חִיל "anguish", Exod 15:14).
- **"with words of peace" → "words of *shalom*"** (v26) — Hebrew דִּבְרֵי שָׁלוֹם; "with" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"I will go along by the high way" → "By the way, by the way I will go"** (v27) — Hebrew בַּדֶּרֶךְ בַּדֶּרֶךְ, the word said twice: only by the road. Num 21:22 has "by the way of the king".
- **"only I will pass through on my feet" → "Only let me pass through on my feet"** (v28) — Hebrew אֶעְבְּרָה, a wish (as v27).
- **"(As the children of Esau … did unto me;)" → "as the sons of Esau … did for me"** (v29) — no brackets in the Hebrew. Num 20:18–21 tells that Edom refused the passage through his land, and Deut 23:3–4 that Ammon and Moab did not meet Israel with bread and water; the accounts are left as they stand.
- **"would not let us pass by him: for the LORD thy God hardened his spirit, and made his heart obstinate, that he might deliver him into thy hand, as appeareth this day" → "was not willing to let us pass through him; for Yahweh your Elohim had made hard his spirit and strengthened his heart, in order to give him into your hand, as at this day"** (v30) — Hebrew הַעֲבִרֵנוּ בּוֹ "to make us pass through him" (through his land, as Num 20:18); הִקְשָׁה "make hard" (Exod 7:3); אִמֵּץ "make strong, firm" (another verb than the חִזֵּק "make strong" and הִכְבִּיד "make heavy" of Pharaoh's heart); לְמַעַן תִּתּוֹ "in order to give him"; כַּיּוֹם הַזֶּה "as this day".
- **"I have begun to give Sihon and his land before thee: begin to possess, that thou mayest inherit his land" → "I have begun to set before you Sihon and his land. Begin, take possession, to possess his land"** (v31) — Hebrew נָתַן לִפְנֵי "set before", literally "give before" (as 1:8; so v33 "set him before us" and v36); the one verb יָרַשׁ twice (רָשׁ לָרֶשֶׁת).
- **"came out against us … to fight at Jahaz" → "came out to meet us … to battle, to Jahaz"** (v32) — Hebrew לִקְרָאתֵנוּ; לַמִּלְחָמָה יָהְצָה (Num 21:23).
- **"we smote him, and his sons" → "we struck down him and his sons"** (v33) — the reading tradition (qere) has בָּנָיו "his sons"; the written text (ketiv) has בנו "his son".
- **"we took all his cities … and utterly destroyed the men, and the women, and the little ones, of every city, we left none to remain" → "we captured all his cities … and we devoted to destruction every city of men, and the women and the little ones; we left no survivor"** (v34) — Hebrew לָכַד "capture"; הֶחֱרִים, to give over to Yahweh beyond recall, by destroying (*cherem*, Num 21:2); עִיר מְתִם, literally "city of men": the men of each city; שָׂרִיד "survivor".
- **"Only the cattle we took for a prey" → "Only the beasts we plundered"** (v35) — Hebrew בְּהֵמָה; בָּזַז "plunder".
- **"by the brink of the river of Arnon, and from the city that is by the river … there was not one city too strong for us: the LORD our God delivered all unto us" → "on the edge of the wadi Arnon, and the city that is in the wadi … there was not a town that was too high for us; the whole Yahweh our Elohim set before us"** (v36) — Hebrew שְׂפַת "lip, edge"; בַּנַּחַל "in the wadi"; קִרְיָה "town", another word than עִיר "city"; שָׂגְבָה "was high".
- **"nor unto any place of the river Jabbok, nor unto the cities in the mountains, nor unto whatsoever the LORD our God forbad us" → "all the side of the wadi Jabbok and the cities of the hill country, and all that Yahweh our Elohim commanded"** (v37) — Hebrew כָּל־יַד, literally "all the hand of"; צִוָּה "commanded": what he commanded us to leave. The Hebrew has no "nor" and no word "forbid".

### Notes
- **Related peoples protected** — Edom (the sons of Esau), Moab and Ammon (the sons of Lot) are kin to Israel. Yahweh gives them their lands; Israel may pass but not conquer. Contrast with the Amorite.
- **Earlier peoples** (v10–12, 20–23) — notes on those who dwelt in these lands before. The text says that Israel is not the first to dispossess: the sons of Esau did so, the sons of Ammon did so, and the Caphtorim did so.
- **Caphtor** (v23) — the place from which the Philistines came (Amos 9:7; Jer 47:4); commonly identified with Crete or the Aegean.
- **Sihon's spirit and heart** (v30) — "made hard his spirit and strengthened his heart": compare the heart of Pharaoh in Exodus, where three other verbs are used.
- **38 years** (v14) — from Kadesh-barnea to the wadi Zered.
- ***Cherem*** (v34) — Sihon's cities are devoted to destruction. The rule is given in ch 7 and 20.
- **"You" singular and plural** — the Hebrew moves between the plural "you" (v3–6, 13, 24a) and the singular (v7, 9, 18–19, 24b–25, 30–31, 37); English shows no difference.
