## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"A Syrian ready to perish was my father" → "A wandering Aramean was my father"** (v5) — Hebrew אֲרַמִּי אֹבֵד אָבִי *Arami oved avi*. Referring to Jacob (or Abraham). *Oved* = "perishing / wandering / destitute"; Hebrew phrase famously debated. "Wandering" is the predominant modern reading, evoking Jacob's fleeing to Paddan-Aram. "Syrian" in KJV = Aramean.
- **"evil entreated us" → "treated us harshly"** (v6) — archaic.
- **"great terribleness" → "great terror"** (v8) — Hebrew בְּמֹרָא גָּדֹל *be-mora gadol*.
- **"peculiar people" → "treasured people"** (v18) — *segullah*.
- **"avouched" → "declared"** (v17, 18) — Hebrew הֶאֱמַרְתָּ *he'emarta* / הֶאֱמִירְךָ *he'emirkha*. Hiphil of *amar* — to make declaration. Mutual covenantal avowal.
- **"hallowed thing / any unclean use / for the dead" (v13–14)** — idioms:
  - "Sacred portion" — the consecrated tithe
  - "Removed...while unclean" — ritually improper use
  - "Offered to the dead" — ancestor-worship / funerary offerings
- **"ought" → "any"** (v14) — archaic.

### Notes
- **Two liturgical declarations** — the chapter prescribes two ritual speeches:
  1. **Firstfruits declaration** (v5–10) — the "*Arami oved avi*" confession. Still recited in Passover Haggadah. Compressed salvation-history in first-person-plural.
  2. **Tithe declaration** (v13–15) — the triennial accounting of tithe-obligations.
- **"A wandering Aramean"** (v5) — probably Jacob. Genesis 28–31 accounts: Jacob flees to Aram (Mesopotamia), works 20 years for Laban, returns. Israel's identity begins in migration and precarity.
- **Liturgical performance** — these are not private prayers but required public declarations during pilgrimage. The worshipper performatively inscribes their story in Israel's story.
- **Mutual covenantal declaration** (v17–18) — Israel declares Yahweh to be their *Elohim*; Yahweh declares Israel to be his treasured people. Marriage-covenant structure.
- **Closes the "covenant code"** — chs 12–26 form a legal-corpus body. Chs 27–28 will deliver the blessings/curses; 29–30 the concluding covenant-renewal.
