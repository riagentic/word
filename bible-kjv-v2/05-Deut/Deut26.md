# Deuteronomy 26

[1] "And it shall be, when you come into the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you as an inheritance, and you take possession of it and dwell in it,

[2] that you shall take from the first of all the fruit of the ground that you bring in from your land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you, and you shall put it in the basket and go to the place that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will choose, to make his name abide there.

[3] And you shall come to the priest who shall be in those days and say to him, 'I tell today to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that I have come into the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ swore to our fathers to give us.'

[4] And the priest shall take the basket from your hand and set it down before the altar of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[5] And you shall answer and say before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, 'A perishing ⟦aramean|Arammi|Aramean⟧ was my father, and he went down to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and sojourned there with few men, and he became there a nation, great, mighty and many.

[6] And the ⟦egyptians|Mitzrim|Egyptians⟧ did evil to us and afflicted us and put upon us hard service.

[7] And we cried out to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of our fathers, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ heard our voice and saw our affliction and our toil and our oppression.

[8] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ brought us out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ with a strong hand and with an outstretched arm and with great fear and with signs and with wonders.

[9] And he brought us to this place and gave us this land, a land flowing with milk and honey.

[10] And now, behold, I have brought the first of the fruit of the ground that you have given me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧.' And you shall set it down before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and bow down before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[11] And you shall rejoice in all the good that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has given to you and to your house, you and the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧ and the sojourner who is in your midst.

[12] When you finish tithing all the tithe of your yield in the third year, the year of the tithe, and you give it to the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧, to the sojourner, to the fatherless and to the widow, and they eat in your gates and are sated,

[13] then you shall say before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, 'I have removed the holy thing from the house, and also I have given it to the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧ and to the sojourner, to the fatherless and to the widow, according to all your commandment that you commanded me; I have not overstepped any of your commandments, and I have not forgotten.

[14] I have not eaten of it in my mourning, and I have not removed any of it while unclean, and I have not given any of it for a dead man; I have listened to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; I have done according to all that you commanded me.

[15] Look down from your holy abode, from the heavens, and bless your people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and the ground that you have given us, as you swore to our fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey.'

[16] This day ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is commanding you to do these statutes and the ordinances; and you shall keep and do them with all your heart and with all your soul.

[17] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ you have declared today to be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to you, and to walk in his ways, and to keep his statutes and his commandments and his ordinances, and to listen to his voice.

[18] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has declared you today to be to him a people of treasured possession, as he spoke to you, and to keep all his commandments,

[19] and to set you high above all the nations that he has made, for praise and for a name and for beauty, and for you to be a holy people to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, as he spoke."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"**, **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — the Hebrew names kept.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the confession of v5–10 is one chain of "and he … and we … and Yahweh".
- **"take of the first of all the fruit of the earth … put it in a basket … to place his name there" → "take from the first of all the fruit of the ground … put it in the basket … to make his name abide there"** (v2) — Hebrew רֵאשִׁית "the first" (Exod 23:19); הָאֲדָמָה "the ground"; בַטֶּנֶא has the article (טֶנֶא, only in Deut 26 and 28, is another word than the "basket" of Exod 29:3); לְשַׁכֵּן is "make abide", the verb of מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling".
- **"that shall be in those days"** (v3) — word for word; "in office" is not written. **"I profess this day" → "I tell today"** — Hebrew הִגַּדְתִּי, the verb "tell", a perfect: the act is done in the saying. "Your Elohim" is said to the priest.
- **"A Syrian ready to perish was my father" → "A perishing Aramean was my father"** (v5) — Hebrew אֲרַמִּי אֹבֵד אָבִי, three words: "Aramean — perishing — my father". אֹבֵד is the participle of אָבַד "perish"; this is the rendering closest to the words. Other readings: (1) "a wandering / straying Aramean" — the verb is used of animals that have strayed (1 Sam 9:3; Jer 50:6; Ps 119:176); (2) "an Aramean sought to destroy my father" (of Laban), the old Jewish reading; it takes the participle as "destroying", which the simple stem of the verb does not mean elsewhere, though the accents join אֹבֵד to "my father" and set "Aramean" apart; (3) the Septuagint, "my father left Syria" (from memory). The father is not named. **"with a few" → "with few men"** — Hebrew בִּמְתֵי מְעָט "with men of fewness". **"populous" → "many"** — Hebrew רָב.
- **"evil entreated us … laid upon us hard bondage" → "did evil to us … put upon us hard service"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיָּרֵעוּ, as Num 20:15; עֲבֹדָה קָשָׁה, as Exod 1:14; 6:9.
- **"cried" → "cried out"**, **"labour" → "toil"** (v7) — Hebrew צָעַק; עָמָל.
- **"with a mighty hand … with great terribleness" → "with a strong hand … with great fear"** (v8) — Hebrew חֲזָקָה "strong"; each of the five nouns has בְּ "with" (as 4:34; 5:15). מֹרָא is "fear", singular: what makes afraid (4:34 has the plural, "great fearsome deeds").
- **"the firstfruits of the land, which thou, O LORD, hast given me" → "the first of the fruit of the ground that you have given me, Yahweh"** (v10) — Hebrew רֵאשִׁית פְּרִי הָאֲדָמָה, the words of v2; no "O". **"worship" → "bow down"** — Hebrew הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה.
- **"every good thing … the stranger that is among you" → "all the good … the sojourner who is in your midst"** (v11–13) — Hebrew גֵּר.
- **"When thou hast made an end of tithing all the tithes of thine increase … that they may eat within thy gates, and be filled" → "When you finish tithing all the tithe of your yield … and they eat in your gates and are sated"** (v12) — Hebrew לַעְשֵׂר … מַעְשַׂר, verb and noun of "a tenth" (14:22 "give a tenth of"; here the one root is kept, because "the tenth of all the tithe" would say another thing); תְּבוּאָה "yield". "And you give … and they eat" continue the "when"; the main clause is v13.
- **"I have brought away the hallowed things out of mine house" → "I have removed the holy thing from the house"** (v13, 14) — Hebrew בִּעֵר "clear away, remove", the verb of "purge the evil from your midst" (13:5); nothing evil is meant here, so "remove". הַקֹּדֶשׁ is "the holy thing", singular; "the house" has no "my". **"I have not transgressed thy commandments, neither have I forgotten them" → "I have not overstepped any of your commandments, and I have not forgotten"** — Hebrew עָבַר מִן, literally "pass over from" (17:2); no object after "forgotten".
- **"neither have I taken away ought thereof for any unclean use, nor given ought thereof for the dead" → "I have not removed any of it while unclean, and I have not given any of it for a dead man"** (v14) — Hebrew בְּטָמֵא "in an unclean [state]" or "for an unclean [use]"; לְמֵת "to / for a dead one". What custom is meant is not said. "My mourning" is אֹנִי (Hos 9:4). **"hearkened to" → "listened to"** — Hebrew שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל.
- **"thy holy habitation" → "your holy abode"** (v15) — Hebrew מְעוֹן קָדְשְׁךָ "the abode of your holiness". **"the land which thou hast given us" → "the ground that you have given us"** — Hebrew הָאֲדָמָה; "a land flowing" is אֶרֶץ.
- **"hath commanded thee … thou shalt therefore keep and do them" → "is commanding you … and you shall keep and do them"** (v16) — a participle; no "therefore".
- **"Thou hast avouched the LORD this day to be thy God … the LORD hath avouched thee this day to be his peculiar people" → "Yahweh you have declared today to be Elohim to you … Yahweh has declared you today to be to him a people of treasured possession"** (v17, 18) — Hebrew הֶאֱמַרְתָּ / הֶאֱמִירְךָ, the causative of אָמַר "say", found only in these two verses. The meaning is uncertain: either "cause to say" ("you have made Yahweh say today that he will be Elohim to you"), or "declare, proclaim". "Declare" is kept in both verses; the infinitives that follow ("to be … to walk … to keep") are left as the Hebrew has them, though some belong to Yahweh and some to Israel. "Yahweh" stands first in v17. סְגֻלָּה is "treasured possession", as Exod 19:5. **"as he hath promised thee" → "as he spoke to you"** — Hebrew דִּבֶּר.
- **"to make thee high above all nations … in praise, and in name, and in honour" → "to set you high above all the nations … for praise and for a name and for beauty"** (v19) — Hebrew עֶלְיוֹן "high, highest" (the word used of God as "Most High"); נָתַן "give, set"; תִּפְאֶרֶת as Exod 28:2. Whose praise and name is meant, Israel's or Yahweh's, the Hebrew does not say (Jer 13:11).

### Notes

- **Two speeches** — with the first of the fruit (v5–10) and with the tithe of the third year (v13–15; 14:28–29).
- **"My father"** (v5) — Jacob is commonly understood (Gen 46; the Aramean kin, Gen 25:20; 28:5).
- **The declaration** (v17–19) — closes the statutes and ordinances of chapters 12–26.
