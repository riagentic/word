# Deuteronomy 23

[1] "One wounded by crushing and one cut off in the member shall not come into the assembly of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[2] One born of a forbidden union shall not come into the assembly of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; even the tenth generation of his shall not come into the assembly of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] An ⟦ammonites|Ammoni|Ammonite⟧ and a ⟦moabites|Moavi|Moabite⟧ shall not come into the assembly of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; even the tenth generation of theirs shall not come into the assembly of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, forever,

[4] in the matter that they did not come to meet you with bread and with water on the way, when you came out of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and that he hired against you ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ son of Beor from ⟦pethor|Petor|Pethor⟧ of ⟦aram-naharaim|Aram Naharayim|Mesopotamia⟧ to curse you.

[5] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was not willing to listen to ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ turned for you the curse into a blessing, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ loved you.

[6] You shall not seek their ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ and their good all your days, forever.

[7] You shall not abominate an ⟦edomites|Edomi|Edomite⟧, for he is your brother; you shall not abominate ⟦egyptian|a Mitzri|an Egyptian⟧, for you were a sojourner in his land.

[8] Sons who are born to them: the third generation of theirs may come into the assembly of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[9] When you go out as a camp against your enemies, then you shall keep yourself from every evil thing.

[10] When there is among you a man who is not clean, from what happens at night, then he shall go out to the outside of the camp; he shall not come into the midst of the camp.

[11] And it shall be, at the turning of evening he shall wash in water, and when the sun sets he may come into the midst of the camp.

[12] And you shall have a place outside the camp, and you shall go out there, outside.

[13] And you shall have a peg with your gear, and it shall be, when you sit outside, that you shall dig with it and turn back and cover what has come out of you.

[14] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ walks about in the midst of your camp, to deliver you and to set your enemies before you; and your camp shall be holy, and he shall not see among you the nakedness of a thing and turn back from after you.

[15] You shall not hand over to his lord a slave who delivers himself to you from his lord.

[16] With you he shall dwell, in your midst, in the place that he chooses in one of your gates, where it is good for him; you shall not wrong him.

[17] There shall not be a cult prostitute of the daughters of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and there shall not be a male cult prostitute of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[18] You shall not bring the hire of a prostitute and the price of a dog into the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for any vow, for an abomination to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ are both of them also.

[19] You shall not lend on interest to your brother: interest of money, interest of food, interest of anything that is lent on interest.

[20] To the foreigner you may lend on interest, but to your brother you shall not lend on interest, so that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ may bless you in all that you stretch out your hand to, on the land to which you are going in to possess it.

[21] When you vow a vow to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you shall not delay to repay it, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will surely require it from you, and there would be sin in you.

[22] And when you forbear to vow, there shall be no sin in you.

[23] What goes out of your lips you shall keep and do, as you have vowed to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, a freewill offering, which you have spoken with your mouth.

[24] When you come into your neighbor's vineyard, you may eat grapes according to your soul, your fill, but into your vessel you shall not put any.

[25] When you come into your neighbor's standing grain, you may pluck ears with your hand, but a sickle you shall not wave over your neighbor's standing grain.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept. "You" is one person (singular) throughout, except "to meet you … when you came out" in v4, which is plural.
- **"shall not enter into the congregation of the LORD" → "shall not come into the assembly of Yahweh"** (v1–3, 8) — Hebrew לֹא־יָבֹא … בִּקְהַל יְהוָה: בּוֹא "come"; קָהָל "assembly" (the "congregation" is עֵדָה). What coming into the assembly covers — marriage into Israel, or a share in its gathering for worship and counsel — the verse does not say.
- **"He that is wounded in the stones, or hath his privy member cut off" → "One wounded by crushing and one cut off in the member"** (v1) — Hebrew פְצוּעַ־דַּכָּא וּכְרוּת שָׁפְכָה. פְּצוּעַ is "wounded" (the noun פֶּצַע "wound", Gen 4:23) and דַּכָּא "crushing"; the testicles are meant but not named. שָׁפְכָה, found only here, is from שָׁפַךְ "pour out": the member that pours out. The Hebrew has "and".
- **"A bastard" → "One born of a forbidden union"** (v2) — Hebrew מַמְזֵר, found only here and in Zech 9:6; its meaning is not certain and its origin is unknown. Jewish law takes it as the child of a union that is forbidden under pain of death or of being cut off (incest, adultery); the Septuagint has "one born of a prostitute" (cited from memory); others think of a child of mixed descent (so some in Zech 9:6). The verse keeps the best-supported sense and does not decide more. **"even to his tenth generation shall he not enter" → "even the tenth generation of his shall not come"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew גַּם דּוֹר עֲשִׂירִי לֹא־יָבֹא לוֹ, literally "also a tenth generation shall not come for him".
- **"An Ammonite or Moabite" → "An Ammonite and a Moabite"** (v3) — the Hebrew has "and"; both words are masculine singular. **"for ever"** — Hebrew עַד־עוֹלָם.
- **"Because they met you not" → "in the matter that they did not come to meet you"** (v4) — Hebrew עַל־דְּבַר אֲשֶׁר לֹא־קִדְּמוּ אֶתְכֶם; קִדֵּם is "come before, come to meet". **"because they hired" → "that he hired"** — Hebrew שָׂכַר, singular: Moab, or its king (Num 22:5). **"of Pethor of Mesopotamia" → "from Pethor of Aram Naharaim"** — Hebrew מִפְּתוֹר אֲרַם נַהֲרַיִם, "Aram of the two rivers". **"to curse you"** — Hebrew קִלֵּל, literally "make light of"; "the curse" in v5 is its noun, קְלָלָה.
- **"Nevertheless … would not hearken … but" → "And … was not willing to listen … and"** (v5) — Hebrew וְלֹא־אָבָה … לִשְׁמֹעַ אֶל … וַיַּהֲפֹךְ. "Turned" is הָפַךְ, "turn over".
- **"their peace nor their prosperity" → "their shalom and their good"** (v6) — Hebrew שְׁלֹמָם וְטֹבָתָם; טוֹבָה is "good".
- **"abhor" → "abominate"** (v7) — Hebrew תִּעֵב, the verb of תּוֹעֵבָה "abomination" (v18). **"stranger" → "sojourner"** — Hebrew גֵּר.
- **"The children that are begotten of them shall enter … in their third generation" → "Sons who are born to them: the third generation of theirs may come"** (v8) — Hebrew בָּנִים אֲשֶׁר־יִוָּלְדוּ לָהֶם דּוֹר שְׁלִישִׁי יָבֹא לָהֶם, word for word; the verb "come" is singular and goes with "generation".
- **"When the host goeth forth" → "When you go out as a camp"** (v9) — Hebrew כִּי־תֵצֵא מַחֲנֶה: "you" is the subject; מַחֲנֶה is "camp" (v10–14), here the host in camp. **"wicked thing" → "evil thing"** — Hebrew דָּבָר רָע.
- **"that is not clean by reason of uncleanness that chanceth him by night" → "who is not clean, from what happens at night"** (v10) — Hebrew מִקְּרֵה־לָיְלָה, literally "from a happening of night" (an emission, Lev 15:16); "uncleanness" is not written. **"within the camp" → "into the midst of the camp"** (v10, 11) — Hebrew אֶל־תּוֹךְ.
- **"when evening cometh on" → "at the turning of evening"** (v11) — Hebrew לִפְנוֹת־עֶרֶב. **"wash himself with water" → "wash in water"**. **"when the sun is down" → "when the sun sets"** — Hebrew וּכְבֹא הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ, literally "as the sun goes in".
- **"a place"** (v12) — Hebrew יָד, literally "a hand": a side, a marked place.
- **"a paddle upon thy weapon" → "a peg with your gear"** (v13) — Hebrew יָתֵד, "peg" (Exod 27:19); אָזֵן, found only here, is taken as "tools, equipment"; its exact sense is not certain. **"when thou wilt ease thyself abroad" → "when you sit outside"** — Hebrew בְּשִׁבְתְּךָ חוּץ, literally so. **"that which cometh from thee" → "what has come out of you"** — Hebrew צֵאָתֶךָ, a noun from יָצָא "go out": excrement.
- **"walketh" → "walks about"** (v14) — Hebrew מִתְהַלֵּךְ (Gen 3:8). **"to give up thine enemies before thee" → "to set your enemies before you"** — Hebrew וְלָתֵת אֹיְבֶיךָ לְפָנֶיךָ, literally "to give your enemies before you" (נָתַן לִפְנֵי "set before", as 1:8; 7:2). **"therefore shall thy camp be holy: that he see no unclean thing in thee, and turn away from thee" → "and your camp shall be holy, and he shall not see among you the nakedness of a thing and turn back from after you"** — "therefore" is not in the Hebrew. עֶרְוַת דָּבָר is literally "nakedness of a thing": something exposed that ought to be covered, something indecent; the same two words stand in 24:1. מֵאַחֲרֶיךָ is "from after you": from going with you.
- **"Thou shalt not deliver unto his master the servant which is escaped from his master unto thee" → "You shall not hand over to his lord a slave who delivers himself to you from his lord"** (v15) — Hebrew עֶבֶד "slave"; אָדוֹן "lord"; הִסְגִּיר is "shut up" (Lev 13:4), here "shut up into the hand of, hand over"; יִנָּצֵל is "deliver himself, be delivered", of the root of "deliver" in v14.
- **"where it liketh him best" → "where it is good for him"** (v16) — Hebrew בַּטּוֹב לוֹ. **"oppress" → "wrong"** — Hebrew הוֹנָה (Exod 22:21).
- **"There shall be no whore of the daughters of Israel, nor a sodomite of the sons of Israel" → "There shall not be a cult prostitute of the daughters of Israel, and there shall not be a male cult prostitute of the sons of Israel"** (v17) — Hebrew קְדֵשָׁה and קָדֵשׁ, the feminine and the masculine of one word, literally "consecrated woman" and "consecrated man" (root קדשׁ "holy"). That such persons served at a shrine and what they did there is inferred from the word and from the passages where it stands (Gen 38:21, beside "prostitute"; 1 Kgs 14:24; Hos 4:14); it is not said here. "Male" is in the Hebrew form, not a second word. Neither word is the common word for a prostitute, which follows in v18.
- **"the hire of a whore, or the price of a dog" → "the hire of a prostitute and the price of a dog"** (v18) — Hebrew אֶתְנַן זוֹנָה וּמְחִיר כֶּלֶב. זוֹנָה is the common word "prostitute"; אֶתְנַן is what she is given. כֶּלֶב is the plain word "dog". It is taken (1) of the animal: the price got for a dog; or (2) as a name of contempt for a man who does what the "male cult prostitute" of v17 does, or for a male prostitute in general. The verse does not decide. **"for even both these are abomination" → "for an abomination to Yahweh … are both of them also"** — Hebrew גַּם־שְׁנֵיהֶם: both gifts, or both persons.
- **"usury" → "interest"** (v19, 20) — Hebrew נֶשֶׁךְ, literally "a bite"; the verb הִשִּׁיךְ is "make to bite": any interest, not only a high rate. **"of money"** — Hebrew כֶּסֶף, "silver". **"victuals" → "food"** — Hebrew אֹכֶל.
- **"Unto a stranger" → "To the foreigner"** (v20) — Hebrew לַנָּכְרִי. **"in all that thou settest thine hand to" → "in all that you stretch out your hand to"** — Hebrew בְּכֹל מִשְׁלַח יָדֶךָ, literally "in every stretching out of your hand". **"in the land whither thou goest to possess it" → "on the land to which you are going in to possess it"** — Hebrew עַל־הָאָרֶץ אֲשֶׁר־אַתָּה בָא־שָׁמָּה לְרִשְׁתָּהּ.
- **"thou shalt not slack to pay it" → "you shall not delay to repay it"** (v21) — Hebrew לֹא תְאַחֵר לְשַׁלְּמוֹ; שִׁלֵּם is "make whole, repay". **"will surely require it"** — Hebrew דָּרֹשׁ יִדְרְשֶׁנּוּ, literally "seeking he will seek it". **"it would be sin in thee" → "there would be sin in you"** — Hebrew וְהָיָה בְךָ חֵטְא.
- **"But if thou shalt forbear" → "And when you forbear"** (v22) — Hebrew וְכִי תֶחְדַּל.
- **"keep and perform" → "keep and do"** (v23) — Hebrew תִּשְׁמֹר וְעָשִׂיתָ. **"even a freewill offering, according as thou hast vowed" → "as you have vowed …, a freewill offering"** — the Hebrew order; נְדָבָה: what is given of one's own will. **"promised" → "spoken"** — Hebrew דִּבַּרְתָּ.
- **"thou mayest eat grapes thy fill at thine own pleasure" → "you may eat grapes according to your soul, your fill"** (v24) — Hebrew כְּנַפְשְׁךָ שָׂבְעֶךָ, literally "as your soul, your satiety": as much as you desire, until you are full.
- **"standing corn" → "standing grain"** (v25) — Hebrew קָמָה. **"ears"** — Hebrew מְלִילֹת, found only here: ears that are rubbed in the hand. **"thou shalt not move a sickle unto" → "a sickle you shall not wave over"** — Hebrew הֵנִיף, "wave, swing" (Exod 20:25).

### Notes
- **Verse numbers** — the Hebrew counts our 22:30 as 23:1, so our 23:1–25 are 23:2–26 in the Hebrew.
- **Who may not come into the assembly** (v1–8):
  - the man wounded by crushing or cut off in the member
  - the one born of a forbidden union, to the tenth generation
  - the Ammonite and the Moabite, to the tenth generation, forever
  - the Edomite and the Egyptian are not to be abominated; their third generation may come in
- **Ruth** — Ruth the Moabite stands in the line of David (Ruth 4:17–22). Jewish tradition reads v3 of men only (the words are masculine).
- **Camp holiness** (v9–14) — the camp of the host is to be clean, also in bodily matters, because Yahweh walks about in its midst.
- **The slave who delivers himself** (v15–16) — he is not handed back to his lord and may dwell where he chooses. Other ancient law demanded the return of runaway slaves (Code of Hammurabi §16).
- **Cult prostitute, prostitute, dog** (v17–18) — four different Hebrew words; see Changes.
- **Interest** (v19–20) — none is taken from a brother; from the foreigner it may be taken.
- **Vineyard and standing grain** (v24–25) — one may eat on the spot, but not carry away or reap.
