## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"temptations" → "trials"** (v3) — *massot*, as in ch 7, 11.
- **"gall and wormwood" → "poisonous and bitter fruit"** (v18) — Hebrew רֹאשׁ וְלַעֲנָה *rosh ve-la'anah*. Poison-plant and wormwood (*Artemisia*).
- **"bless himself in his heart" → "congratulates himself in his heart"** (v19) — Hebrew וְהִתְבָּרֵךְ בִּלְבָבוֹ *ve-hitbarekh bi-levavo*. Self-congratulation.
- **"to add drunkenness to thirst" → "sweeping away the watered along with the parched"** (v19) — Hebrew לְמַעַן סְפוֹת הָרָוָה אֶת־הַצְּמֵאָה *le-ma'an sefot ha-ravah et ha-tzme'ah*. Obscure proverbial saying: "to sweep away the well-watered along with the parched" — indiscriminate destruction. KJV "drunkenness to thirst" is a different reading.
- **"whom he had not given unto them" → "whom he had not allotted to them"** (v26) — Hebrew לֹא חָלַק לָהֶם *lo chalaq lahem*. Yahweh had not assigned those gods to Israel.
- **Verse numbering** — MT begins ch 29 at Eng 29:2; Eng 29:1 = MT 28:69. One-verse offset.

### Notes
- **Covenant renewal** — a second covenant, supplementing Sinai (Horeb). Specifically for the new generation entering the land.
- **Inclusive covenant** (v10–11) — everyone stands: leaders, women, children, foreigners, woodcutters, water-drawers. No one is excluded from covenant-accountability.
- **Covenant with absent ones** (v14–15) — "with those not here with us today" — future generations, contemporaries not present. Expansive covenantal scope; each subsequent generation renews this covenant.
- **"No heart to understand"** (v4) — paradox. Yahweh has shown great signs, yet Israel doesn't fully grasp. Paul picks up the theme: Rom 11:8 quotes this verse to explain Israel's partial blindness.
- **Sodom-Gomorrah-Admah-Zeboiim** (v23) — the four cities of the plain destroyed in Gen 19. Prototypical punishment.
- **"Secret things / revealed things"** (v29) — famous verse. Limits human inquiry (divine secrets) but affirms obligation to do what is revealed. Often cited against excess speculation / Gnostic tendencies.
