# Deuteronomy 31

[1] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ went and spoke these words to all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[2] And he said to them, "I am a hundred and twenty years old today; I am no longer able to go out and to come in, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has said to me, 'You shall not pass over this ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧.'

[3] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, he is passing over before you; he will destroy these nations from before you, and you shall dispossess them. ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, he is passing over before you, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken.

[4] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will do to them as he did to ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧ and to Og, the kings of the ⟦amorite|Emori|Amorite⟧, and to their land, whom he destroyed.

[5] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will set them before you, and you shall do to them according to all the commandment that I have commanded you.

[6] Be strong and be firm; do not fear and do not be terrified because of them, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, he is the one going with you; he will not let you drop and will not forsake you."

[7] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ called ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ and said to him before the eyes of all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, "Be strong and be firm, for you, you shall go in with this people to the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ swore to their fathers to give to them, and you, you shall make them inherit it.

[8] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, he is the one going before you; he will be with you; he will not let you drop and will not forsake you. You shall not fear and you shall not be dismayed."

[9] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ wrote this ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ and gave it to the priests, the sons of Levi, who carry the ark of the covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and to all the elders of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[10] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ commanded them, saying, "At the end of seven years, at the appointed time of the year of the release, at ⟦sukkot|the Feast of Sukkot|the Feast of Booths⟧,

[11] when all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ comes to appear before the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in the place that he will choose, you shall read this ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ in front of all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, in their ears.

[12] Assemble the people, the men and the women and the little ones, and your sojourner who is in your gates, so that they may hear and so that they may learn, and they shall fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and keep to do all the words of this ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

[13] And their sons, who have not known, shall hear and learn to fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, all the days that you are living on the ground to which you are passing over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ to possess it."

[14] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Behold, your days have come near to die. Call ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, and take your stand in the tent of meeting, and I will command him." And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ went and took their stand in the tent of meeting.

[15] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ appeared in the tent in a pillar of cloud, and the pillar of cloud stood over the entrance of the tent.

[16] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Behold, you are lying down with your fathers; and this people will rise and play the prostitute after the foreign gods of the land into whose midst it is going in, and it will forsake me and break my covenant that I cut with it.

[17] And my anger will burn against it on that day, and I will forsake them and hide my face from them, and it will be for devouring, and many evils and distresses will find it; and it will say on that day, 'Is it not because my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is not in my midst that these evils have found me?'

[18] And I, I will surely hide my face on that day, on account of all the evil that it has done, for it has turned to other gods.

[19] And now, write for yourselves this song, and teach it to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; put it in their mouth, so that this song may be a witness for me against the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[20] For I will bring it to the ground that I swore to its fathers, flowing with milk and honey, and it will eat and be sated and grow fat, and it will turn to other gods, and they will serve them and spurn me, and it will break my covenant.

[21] And it shall be, when many evils and distresses find it, that this song will answer before it as a witness, for it will not be forgotten from the mouth of its seed; for I know its inclination, which it is making today, before I bring it to the land that I swore."

[22] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ wrote this song on that day and taught it to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[23] And he commanded ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ the son of Nun and said, "Be strong and be firm, for you, you shall bring the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to the land that I swore to them, and I, I will be with you."

[24] And it came to pass, when ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ finished writing the words of this ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ on a book, until they were complete,

[26] "Take this book of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧, and you shall put it at the side of the ark of the covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and it shall be there against you as a witness.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"I am an hundred and twenty years old"** (v2) — Hebrew בֶּן־מֵאָה וְעֶשְׂרִים שָׁנָה, literally "a son of a hundred and twenty years". **"I can no more go out and come in" → "I am no longer able to go out and to come in"** — Hebrew לֹא־אוּכַל עוֹד לָצֵאת וְלָבוֹא. **"also the LORD hath said" → "and Yahweh has said"** — Hebrew וַיהוָה אָמַר; "also" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"go over" → "pass over"** (v2, 3, 13) — Hebrew עָבַר, the same verb each time. **"he will go over before thee" → "he is passing over before you"** (v3) — Hebrew הוּא עֹבֵר, a participle, said in the same words of Yahweh and of Joshua. **"thou shalt possess them" → "you shall dispossess them"** — Hebrew וִירִשְׁתָּם, literally "you shall possess them": take their place and what is theirs. **"hath said" → "has spoken"** — Hebrew דִּבֶּר.
- **"kings of the Amorites, and unto the land of them, whom he destroyed" → "the kings of the Amorite, and to their land, whom he destroyed"** (v4) — Hebrew הָאֱמֹרִי, a singular with the article; אֹתָם "them" is masculine plural: the kings.
- **"shall give them up before your face" → "will set them before you"** (v5) — Hebrew וּנְתָנָם … לִפְנֵיכֶם, literally "give them before your faces" (the phrase of 7:2, 23 and of 30:1, 15, 19, "set before"). "You" is plural in v5 and in the commands of v6 ("be strong … do not fear"), singular in v3 and in "your Elohim … with you … let you drop … forsake you" (v6).
- **"Be strong and of a good courage" → "Be strong and be firm"** (v6, 7, 23) — Hebrew חִזְקוּ וְאִמְצוּ / חֲזַק וֶאֱמָץ, two verbs for being strong: חָזַק "be strong" and אָמֵץ "be strong, firm" (Gen 25:23); "courage" is not a word of the Hebrew. **"fear not, nor be afraid of them" → "do not fear and do not be terrified because of them"** (v6) — Hebrew עָרַץ "tremble, be terrified"; מִפְּנֵיהֶם, literally "from their face".
- **"he will not fail thee, nor forsake thee" → "he will not let you drop and will not forsake you"** (v6, 8) — Hebrew לֹא יַרְפְּךָ, the causative of רָפָה "sink, be slack": let go limp, let sink, let drop; עָזַב "forsake". Heb 13:5 cites the words in the first person ("I will not …"), as Josh 1:5 has them.
- **"in the sight of" → "before the eyes of"** (v7) — Hebrew לְעֵינֵי. **"thou must go with this people" → "you, you shall go in with this people"** — Hebrew אַתָּה תָּבוֹא אֶת־הָעָם, "you shall go in with"; some Hebrew manuscripts and old versions read "you shall bring" as in v23 (cited from memory); the printed text is kept. **"cause them to inherit it" → "make them inherit it"** — Hebrew תַּנְחִילֶנָּה.
- **"fear not, neither be dismayed" → "You shall not fear and you shall not be dismayed"** (v8) — Hebrew לֹא תִירָא וְלֹא תֵחָת, the strong "you shall not"; חָתַת, literally "be shattered".
- **"this law" → "this Torah"** (v9, 11, 12, 24, 26) — Hebrew תּוֹרָה. **"which bare the ark" → "who carry the ark"** (v9) — Hebrew הַנֹּשְׂאִים, a participle. The word אֲרוֹן is "chest".
- **"At the end of every seven years, in the solemnity of the year of release, in the feast of tabernacles" → "At the end of seven years, at the appointed time of the year of the release, at the Feast of Sukkot"** (v10) — Hebrew מִקֵּץ שֶׁבַע שָׁנִים ("every" is not in the Hebrew); מֹעֵד "appointed time"; שְׁמִטָּה "a letting drop, release" (15:1); חַג הַסֻּכּוֹת "the feast of the booths".
- **"to appear before the LORD" → "to appear before the face of Yahweh"** (v11) — Hebrew לֵרָאוֹת אֶת־פְּנֵי יְהוָה, as pointed "to be seen (at) the face of Yahweh"; the consonants also allow "to see the face of Yahweh". **"before all Israel in their hearing" → "in front of all Israel, in their ears"** — Hebrew נֶגֶד … בְּאָזְנֵיהֶם. "Read" is קָרָא "call out".
- **"Gather the people together, men, and women, and children, and thy stranger" → "Assemble the people, the men and the women and the little ones, and your sojourner"** (v12) — Hebrew הַקְהֵל (the verb of קָהָל "assembly", v30); טַף "little ones"; גֵּר "sojourner". **"that they may hear, and that they may learn" → "so that they may hear and so that they may learn"** — Hebrew לְמַעַן twice. **"observe to do" → "keep to do"** — Hebrew וְשָׁמְרוּ לַעֲשׂוֹת. "Assemble … your sojourner … your gates" is singular; "your Elohim" in v12, 13 is plural.
- **"their children, which have not known any thing, may hear" → "their sons, who have not known, shall hear"** (v13) — Hebrew וּבְנֵיהֶם אֲשֶׁר לֹא־יָדְעוּ, no object. **"as long as ye live in the land" → "all the days that you are living on the ground"** — Hebrew כָּל־הַיָּמִים … עַל־הָאֲדָמָה.
- **"thy days approach that thou must die" → "your days have come near to die"** (v14) — Hebrew קָרְבוּ יָמֶיךָ לָמוּת. **"present yourselves in the tabernacle of the congregation, that I may give him a charge" → "take your stand in the tent of meeting, and I will command him"** — Hebrew וְהִתְיַצְּבוּ בְּאֹהֶל מוֹעֵד וַאֲצַוֶּנּוּ; צִוָּה is "command" (so v23).
- **"over the door of the tabernacle" → "over the entrance of the tent"** (v15) — Hebrew עַל־פֶּתַח הָאֹהֶל; עַל is "over" or "at".
- **"thou shalt sleep with thy fathers" → "you are lying down with your fathers"** (v16) — Hebrew הִנְּךָ שֹׁכֵב, a participle. **"go a whoring after the gods of the strangers of the land, whither they go to be among them" → "play the prostitute after the foreign gods of the land into whose midst it is going in"** — Hebrew וְזָנָה אַחֲרֵי אֱלֹהֵי נֵכַר־הָאָרֶץ, literally "the gods of the foreignness of the land"; אֲשֶׁר הוּא בָא־שָׁמָּה בְּקִרְבּוֹ, literally "where it is going in there, in its midst". **"made with them" → "cut with it"** — Hebrew כָּרַתִּי אִתּוֹ.
- **"them … they" → "it"** (v16–21) — the people is a singular in the Hebrew ("it will forsake me", "against it", "it will say … my Elohim … my midst … found me", "its fathers", "its seed", "its inclination"); the plural breaks in only in "I will forsake them and hide my face from them" (v17) and "they will serve them and spurn me" (v20). Both are kept as they stand.
- **"my anger shall be kindled" → "my anger will burn"** (v17) — Hebrew וְחָרָה אַפִּי; אַף is "nose", hence "anger". **"they shall be devoured" → "it will be for devouring"** — Hebrew וְהָיָה לֶאֱכֹל, literally "and it will be for eating". **"many evils and troubles shall befall them" → "many evils and distresses will find it"** (v17, 21) — Hebrew מָצָא "find"; צָרוֹת "distresses". **"because our God is not among us" → "because my Elohim is not in my midst"** — Hebrew אֱלֹהַי, which may also be read "my gods".
- **"I will surely hide my face"** (v18) — Hebrew הַסְתֵּר אַסְתִּיר, literally "hiding I will hide". **"in that they are turned" → "for it has turned"** — Hebrew כִּי פָנָה.
- **"write ye this song for you, and teach it the children of Israel: put it in their mouths" → "write for yourselves this song, and teach it to the sons of Israel; put it in their mouth"** (v19) — "write" is plural (Moses and Joshua), "teach" and "put" are singular. Hebrew בְּנֵי "sons of" (v19, 22, 23). **"that this song may be a witness" → "so that this song may be a witness"** — Hebrew לְמַעַן.
- **"For when I shall have brought them into the land … and they shall have eaten and filled themselves, and waxen fat; then will they turn" → "For I will bring it to the ground … and it will eat and be sated and grow fat, and it will turn"** (v20) — one chain of clauses in the Hebrew, with no "when … then"; הָאֲדָמָה "the ground" (v13, 20), הָאָרֶץ "the land" (v7, 21, 23). **"provoke me" → "spurn me"** — Hebrew נִאֵץ "treat with contempt" (Num 14:11).
- **"this song shall testify against them as a witness" → "this song will answer before it as a witness"** (v21) — Hebrew וְעָנְתָה … לְפָנָיו לְעֵד, literally "answer before its face"; to "answer" is to give testimony (Exod 20:16). **"their imagination which they go about, even now" → "its inclination, which it is making today"** — Hebrew יִצְרוֹ "what it forms" in the heart (Gen 6:5; 8:21); אֲשֶׁר הוּא עֹשֶׂה הַיּוֹם. **"which I sware"** — the Hebrew ends there; nothing is supplied.
- **"he gave Joshua the son of Nun a charge" → "he commanded Joshua the son of Nun"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיְצַו; the Hebrew does not name the subject. The words that follow ("the land that I swore to them, and I, I will be with you") are Yahweh's (v14). אֶהְיֶה עִמָּךְ "I will be with you" is the verb of Exod 3:12, 14.
- **"in a book, until they were finished" → "on a book, until they were complete"** (v24, 30) — Hebrew עַל־סֵפֶר; סֵפֶר is any written document, here a scroll; עַד תֻּמָּם, literally "until their being complete".
- **"which bare the ark" → "the carriers of the ark"** (v25) — Hebrew נֹשְׂאֵי.
- **"put it in the side of the ark … that it may be there for a witness against thee" → "you shall put it at the side of the ark … and it shall be there against you as a witness"** (v26) — Hebrew מִצַּד "at the side of": beside the ark, not in it. "Take" is the Hebrew infinitive לָקֹחַ used as a command; "you shall put" and "your Elohim" are plural, "against you" singular.
- **"thy stiff neck" → "your hard neck"** (v27) — Hebrew עָרְפְּךָ הַקָּשֶׁה. **"ye have been rebellious against the LORD" → "you have been rebelling against Yahweh"** — Hebrew מַמְרִים הֱיִתֶם עִם־יְהוָה, literally "rebelling you have been with Yahweh"; מֶרִי "rebellion" is of the same root. "Your rebellion … your neck" is singular, "with you … you have been" plural.
- **"that I may speak … and call heaven and earth to record against them" → "and let me speak … and call the heavens and the earth to witness against them"** (v28) — Hebrew וַאֲדַבְּרָה … וְאָעִידָה, two forms of wish or purpose.
- **"ye will utterly corrupt yourselves" → "you will surely work ruin"** (v29) — Hebrew הַשְׁחֵת תַּשְׁחִתוּן, literally "ruining you will ruin", with no object (9:12; 32:5); "corrupt yourselves" is KJV's reading of it. **"evil will befall you … because ye will do evil" → "the evil will befall you … for you will do the evil"** — the Hebrew has the article both times. **"to provoke him to anger through the work of your hands" → "to vex him with the work of your hands"** — Hebrew לְהַכְעִיסוֹ, from כַּעַס "vexation" (32:16, 19, 21, 27); "anger" is another word (v17).
- **"the latter days" kept** (v29) — Hebrew בְּאַחֲרִית הַיָּמִים, literally "in the after-part of the days" (Gen 49:1).
- **"in the ears of all the congregation" → "in the ears of all the assembly"** (v30) — Hebrew קְהַל.

### Notes
- **Multiple commissioning scenes** — Joshua is addressed by Moses (v7–8) and commanded by Yahweh himself (v14–15, 23). Layered succession-transfer.
- **Moses aged 120** (v2) — symbolic of fullness of life (Gen 6:3). "No longer able to go out and to come in" = unable to lead militarily and civically.
- **Septennial public Torah reading** (v10–13) — every seven years at Sukkot, the Torah read to all Israel including women, little ones, and sojourners. Literacy-bypassing community knowledge. Revived in post-exilic Nehemiah 8.
- **Torah placed beside the ark** (v26) — not inside (the tablets are inside, Ex 25:16); beside, as witness. 2 Kgs 22 may describe its eventual rediscovery under Josiah.
- **Predicted apostasy** (v16–21) — Yahweh anticipates Israel's future unfaithfulness. The song (ch 32) will be the witness — preserved in memory since it must be sung, more durable than written Torah for oral culture.
- **"I know your rebellion"** (v27) — Moses' realism. Not "if you rebel" but "when."
