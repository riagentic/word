## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Be of good courage" → "Be strong and courageous"** (v6, 7, 23) — Hebrew חֲזַק וֶאֱמָץ *chazaq ve-ematz*. Famous exhortation, repeated to Joshua multiple times (Josh 1:6, 7, 9).
- **"solemnity of the year of release" → "set time of the year of remission"** (v10) — *mo'ed shenat ha-shemittah*.
- **"go a whoring" → "whore"** (v16) — idiom.
- **"waxen fat" → "grown fat"** (v20) — archaic.
- **"imagination" → "what they are disposed to do"** (v21) — Hebrew יֵצֶר *yetzer*. Inner inclination, disposition.
- **"latter days" (v29)** — eschatological phrase.
- **"this law" → "this Torah"** (v9, 11, 12, 24, 26).
- **"in the side of the ark" → "beside the ark"** (v26) — location: next to, not inside. Distinct from the tablets which are *inside* the ark.

### Notes
- **Multiple commissioning scenes** — Joshua commissioned by Moses (v7–8) and by Yahweh himself (v14–15, 23). Layered succession-transfer.
- **Moses aged 120** (v2) — symbolic of fullness of life (Gen 6:3). "Can no longer go out and come in" = unable to lead militarily and civically.
- **Septennial public Torah reading** (v10–13) — every seven years at Sukkot, the entire Torah read to all Israel including women, children, and foreigners. Literacy-bypassing community knowledge. Revived in post-exilic Nehemiah 8.
- **Torah placed beside the ark** (v26) — not inside (the tablets are inside, Ex 25:16); beside, as witness. 2 Kgs 22 may describe its eventual rediscovery under Josiah.
- **Predicted apostasy** (v16–21) — Yahweh anticipates Israel's future unfaithfulness. The song (ch 32) will be the witness — preserved in memory since it must be sung, more durable than written Torah for oral culture.
- **"I know your rebellion"** (v27) — Moses' realism. Not "if you rebel" but "when."
