# Deuteronomy 31

[1] Moses went and spoke these words to all Israel.

[2] He said to them, "I am 120 years old today; I can no longer go out and come in. Yahweh has also said to me, 'You shall not cross this Jordan.'

[3] Yahweh your *Elohim* himself is going over before you; he will destroy these nations from before you, and you shall dispossess them. Joshua is the one who will cross over before you, as Yahweh has said.

[4] Yahweh will do to them as he did to Sihon and to Og, kings of the Amorites, and to their land, which he destroyed.

[5] Yahweh shall give them up before you, and you shall do to them according to all the commandment that I have commanded you.

[6] Be strong and courageous; do not fear or be in dread of them, for it is Yahweh your *Elohim* who goes with you — he will not fail you or forsake you."

[7] Then Moses summoned Joshua and said to him in the sight of all Israel, "Be strong and courageous, for you shall go with this people into the land that Yahweh has sworn to their fathers to give them; you shall cause them to inherit it.

[8] Yahweh himself is the one who goes before you; he will be with you; he will not fail you or forsake you. Do not fear or be dismayed."

[9] Moses wrote this Torah and gave it to the priests, the sons of Levi, who carried the ark of the covenant of Yahweh, and to all the elders of Israel.

[10] Moses commanded them, saying, "At the end of every seven years, at the set time of the year of remission, at the Feast of Booths,

[11] when all Israel comes to appear before Yahweh your *Elohim* in the place that he will choose, you shall read this Torah before all Israel in their hearing.

[12] Assemble the people — the men, the women, the little ones, and the foreigner who is within your gates — that they may hear and learn and fear Yahweh your *Elohim*, and be careful to do all the words of this Torah;

[13] and that their children, who have not known it, may hear and learn to fear Yahweh your *Elohim*, as long as you live in the land that you are crossing the Jordan to possess."

[14] Yahweh said to Moses, "Behold, the days of your death draw near. Call Joshua and present yourselves at the Tent of Meeting, that I may commission him." So Moses and Joshua went and presented themselves at the Tent of Meeting.

[15] Yahweh appeared in the tent in a pillar of cloud; and the pillar of cloud stood at the entrance of the tent.

[16] Yahweh said to Moses, "Behold, you are about to lie down with your fathers. This people will arise and whore after the foreign gods of the land they are entering; they will forsake me and break my covenant that I have made with them.

[17] On that day my anger shall be kindled against them, and I will forsake them and hide my face from them; they shall be consumed, and many evils and troubles shall come upon them. On that day they shall say, 'Have not these evils come upon us because our *Elohim* is not in our midst?'

[18] I will surely hide my face on that day, because of all the evil they have done in turning to other gods.

[19] Now therefore write down for yourselves this song and teach it to the children of Israel. Put it in their mouths, that this song may be a witness for me against the children of Israel.

[20] For when I have brought them into the land flowing with milk and honey that I swore to their fathers, and when they have eaten and are full and grown fat, they will turn to other gods and serve them; they will despise me and break my covenant.

[21] When many evils and troubles come upon them, this song shall testify against them as a witness (for it shall not be forgotten from the mouths of their offspring); for I know what they are disposed to do, even now, before I have brought them into the land that I swore."

[22] So Moses wrote this song that day and taught it to the children of Israel.

[23] And he commissioned Joshua son of Nun and said, "Be strong and courageous, for you shall bring the children of Israel into the land that I swore to give them; I myself will be with you."

[24] When Moses had finished writing the words of this Torah in a book, to the very end,

[25] Moses commanded the Levites who carried the ark of the covenant of Yahweh, saying,

[26] "Take this book of the Torah and put it beside the ark of the covenant of Yahweh your *Elohim*, that it may be there as a witness against you.

[27] For I know your rebellion and your stiff neck. Behold, while I am still alive with you today you have been rebellious against Yahweh; how much more after my death!

[28] Assemble to me all the elders of your tribes and your officers, that I may speak these words in their hearing and call heaven and earth to witness against them.

[29] For I know that after my death you will surely act corruptly and turn aside from the way that I have commanded you; and evil shall befall you in the latter days, because you will do what is evil in the eyes of Yahweh, provoking him to anger through the work of your hands."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Be of good courage" → "Be strong and courageous"** (v6, 7, 23) — Hebrew חֲזַק וֶאֱמָץ *chazaq ve-ematz*. Famous exhortation, repeated to Joshua multiple times (Josh 1:6, 7, 9).
- **"solemnity of the year of release" → "set time of the year of remission"** (v10) — *mo'ed shenat ha-shemittah*.
- **"go a whoring" → "whore"** (v16) — idiom.
- **"waxen fat" → "grown fat"** (v20) — archaic.
- **"imagination" → "what they are disposed to do"** (v21) — Hebrew יֵצֶר *yetzer*. Inner inclination, disposition.
- **"latter days" (v29)** — eschatological phrase.
- **"this law" → "this Torah"** (v9, 11, 12, 24, 26).
- **"in the side of the ark" → "beside the ark"** (v26) — location: next to, not inside. Distinct from the tablets which are *inside* the ark.

### Notes
- **Multiple commissioning scenes** — Joshua commissioned by Moses (v7–8) and by Yahweh himself (v14–15, 23). Layered succession-transfer.
- **Moses aged 120** (v2) — symbolic of fullness of life (Gen 6:3). "Can no longer go out and come in" = unable to lead militarily and civically.
- **Septennial public Torah reading** (v10–13) — every seven years at Sukkot, the entire Torah read to all Israel including women, children, and foreigners. Literacy-bypassing community knowledge. Revived in post-exilic Nehemiah 8.
- **Torah placed beside the ark** (v26) — not inside (the tablets are inside, Ex 25:16); beside, as witness. 2 Kgs 22 may describe its eventual rediscovery under Josiah.
- **Predicted apostasy** (v16–21) — Yahweh anticipates Israel's future unfaithfulness. The song (ch 32) will be the witness — preserved in memory since it must be sung, more durable than written Torah for oral culture.
- **"I know your rebellion"** (v27) — Moses' realism. Not "if you rebel" but "when."
