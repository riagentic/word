# Deuteronomy 13

[1] "If a prophet or a dreamer of a dream arises in your midst and gives you a sign or a wonder,

[2] and the sign and the wonder come, of which he spoke to you, saying, 'Let us go after other gods' — whom you have not known — 'and let us serve them,'

[3] you shall not listen to the words of that prophet or to that dreamer of the dream, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is testing you, to know whether you love ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ with all your heart and with all your soul.

[4] After ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ you shall go, and him you shall fear, and his commandments you shall keep, and to his voice you shall listen, and him you shall serve, and to him you shall cling.

[5] And that prophet or that dreamer of the dream shall be put to death, for he has spoken defection against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who brought you out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and who ransomed you from the house of slaves, to draw you away from the way in which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ commanded you to walk; and you shall purge the evil from your midst.

[6] If your brother, the son of your mother, or your son or your daughter or the wife of your bosom, or your friend who is as your soul, incites you in secret, saying, 'Let us go and serve other gods' — whom you have not known, you and your fathers,

[7] of the gods of the peoples who are around you, those near to you or those far from you, from the end of the earth and to the end of the earth —

[8] you shall not consent to him, and you shall not listen to him, and your eye shall not pity him, and you shall not spare, and you shall not cover over him.

[9] But you shall surely kill him; your hand shall be on him first to put him to death, and the hand of all the people afterward.

[10] And you shall stone him with stones and he shall die, for he sought to draw you away from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who brought you out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, from the house of slaves.

[11] And all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall hear and fear, and they shall not again do anything like this evil thing in your midst.

[12] If you hear in one of your cities, which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you to dwell there, saying:

[13] Men, ⟦belial|sons of Beliyyaal|sons of worthlessness⟧, have gone out from your midst and have drawn away the inhabitants of their city, saying, 'Let us go and serve other gods' — whom you have not known —

[14] then you shall seek and examine and ask well; and behold, it is truth, the thing is established, this abomination has been done in your midst:

[15] you shall surely strike the inhabitants of that city with the edge of the sword; devote it to destruction, and all that is in it, and its beasts, with the edge of the sword.

[16] And all its spoil you shall gather into the midst of its square, and you shall burn with fire the city and all its spoil, whole, to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and it shall be an everlasting mound; it shall not be built again.

[17] And nothing of the devoted thing shall cling to your hand, so that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ may turn back from the burning of his anger and give you compassion and have compassion on you and multiply you, as he swore to your fathers,

[18] when you listen to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to keep all his commandments that I am commanding you today, to do what is right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"a dreamer of dreams" → "a dreamer of a dream"** (v1, 3, 5) — חֹלֵם חֲלוֹם, singular; in v3 and v5 "that dreamer of the dream", with the article.
- **"the sign or the wonder come to pass" → "the sign and the wonder come"** (v2) — וּבָא הָאוֹת וְהַמּוֹפֵת: "and", and the plain verb "come".
- **"hearken unto" → "listen to"** (v3, 8) — שָׁמַע אֶל.
- **"proveth" → "is testing"** (v3) — נִסָּה "test, put to the proof".
- **"Ye shall walk after the LORD … and obey his voice … and cleave unto him" → "After Yahweh … you shall go … to his voice you shall listen … to him you shall cling"** (v4) — the Hebrew order kept: each of the six clauses puts its object first. הָלַךְ אַחֲרֵי is "go after", the words of "Let us go after other gods" in v2. שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל is "listen to the voice of" ("obey" is not a Hebrew word here); דָּבַק "cling".
- **"hath spoken to turn you away from" → "has spoken defection against"** (v5) — דִּבֶּר־סָרָה עַל: סָרָה is a noun, "a turning aside" (root סוּר).
- **"redeemed you out of the house of bondage" → "ransomed you from the house of slaves"** (v5, 10) — פָּדָה "ransom" (גָּאַל is "redeem"); בֵּית עֲבָדִים, as Exod 20:2. "you" is plural in "brought you out" and singular from "ransomed you" on.
- **"to thrust thee out of the way … to thrust thee away … have withdrawn" → "to draw you away from the way … to draw you away … have drawn away"** (v5, 10, 13) — one verb, הִדִּיחַ, lit. "thrust, drive": to push off the way, that is, to lead astray (the passive is "be drawn away" in Deut 4:19).
- **"put the evil away from the midst of thee" → "purge the evil from your midst"** (v5) — וּבִעַרְתָּ הָרָע מִקִּרְבֶּךָ; בִּעֵר is lit. "burn away, clear away". The sentence comes again in ch. 17–24.
- **"entice thee secretly" → "incites you in secret"** (v6) — הֵסִית "incite" (פִּתָּה is "entice"); בַּסֵּתֶר.
- **"the wife of thy bosom … as thine own soul"** (v6) — אֵשֶׁת חֵיקֶךָ and כְּנַפְשְׁךָ kept word for word; "own" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"from the one end of the earth even unto the other end of the earth" → "from the end of the earth and to the end of the earth"** (v7).
- **"Thou shalt not consent unto him … neither shalt thou spare, neither shalt thou conceal him" → "you shall not consent to him … you shall not spare, and you shall not cover over him"** (v8) — אָבָה is "be willing" (lit. "you shall not be willing for him"); חוּס with "eye" is "pity", חָמַל "have pity, spare"; כִּסָּה עַל, lit. "cover over" = conceal, shield.
- **"thou shalt surely kill him"** (v9) — הָרֹג תַּהַרְגֶנּוּ, the infinitive absolute with its verb (lit. "killing you shall kill him"); so also "surely strike" in v15 (הַכֵּה תַכֶּה). The Septuagint is said to read "you shall surely report him" here (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"stone him with stones, that he die" → "stone him with stones and he shall die"** (v10) — וָמֵת.
- **"shall do no more any such wickedness as this is" → "they shall not again do anything like this evil thing"** (v11) — לֹא־יוֹסִפוּ לַעֲשׂוֹת, lit. "they shall not add to do"; כַּדָּבָר הָרָע הַזֶּה, lit. "according to this evil thing".
- **"If thou shalt hear say" → "If you hear … saying:"** (v12) — לֵאמֹר; the report follows in v13. "in one of your cities" may be where it is heard or what it is heard about.
- **"Certain men, the children of Belial" → "Men, sons of Beliyyaal"** (v13) — אֲנָשִׁים בְּנֵי־בְלִיַּעַל: "sons of", and בְּלִיַּעַל is a common noun, "worthlessness" (probably "without profit"); it is not a name in the Old Testament (2 Cor 6:15 uses it as one).
- **"enquire, and make search, and ask diligently … if it be truth, and the thing certain" → "seek and examine and ask well … it is truth, the thing is established"** (v14) — דָּרַשׁ, חָקַר, שָׁאַל הֵיטֵב; the Hebrew has no "if": וְהִנֵּה אֱמֶת נָכוֹן הַדָּבָר.
- **"with the edge of the sword"** (v15) — לְפִי־חָרֶב, lit. "to the mouth of the sword", twice in the verse.
- **"destroying it utterly" → "devote it to destruction"** (v15) — הַחֲרֵם, the verb of חֵרֶם: to give over to Yahweh beyond recall. The form is an imperative or an infinitive absolute. "that city": the written form is ההוא, read הַהִיא; no difference in meaning.
- **"the cattle thereof" → "its beasts"** (v15) — בְּהֵמָה.
- **"the street … every whit … an heap for ever" → "its square … whole … an everlasting mound"** (v16) — רְחוֹב the open place of a town; כָּלִיל "whole", the word also used of a whole offering (Lev 6:22, 23); תֵּל עוֹלָם, a mound of ruins "of the age".
- **"the cursed thing" → "the devoted thing"** (v17) — חֵרֶם.
- **"the fierceness of his anger, and shew thee mercy" → "the burning of his anger and give you compassion"** (v17) — חֲרוֹן אַפּוֹ (אַף is "nose" and "anger"); נָתַן רַחֲמִים, then the verb רִחַם "have compassion".
- **"When thou shalt hearken" → "when you listen"** (v18) — כִּי, which may also be "for" or "if".

### Notes
- **Verse numbers** — the Hebrew Bible counts our 12:32 as 13:1, so the Hebrew numbers in this chapter are one higher than ours (our 13:1–18 = Hebrew 13:2–19).
- **Three apostasy scenarios** — (1) a prophet's miraculous but idolatrous message (v1–5); (2) a family member's secret enticement (v6–11); (3) a whole city falling into idolatry (v12–18). Three escalating levels of authority-based temptation.
- **Signs and wonders insufficient** (v1–3) — a prophet's miracle is not final proof; content matters. If his message draws away from Yahweh, his miracle is itself a test. Remarkable cautionary principle.
- **Family loyalty subordinated to Yahweh-loyalty** (v6–11) — severe: even the closest relation does not warrant protection in apostasy. Jesus' words about putting Yahweh above family (Lk 14:26) echo this priority, though with different force in a non-capital context.
- **The apostate city** (v12–18) — subjected to *cherem* like an enemy city of Canaan. The covenant people are treated like the dispossessed nations if they fall into their sin.
- **"Son of your mother"** (v6) — distinguishes full from half-sibling. Polygamous households had full brothers and half-brothers; the phrase emphasizes greatest closeness.
- **"Sons of Belial"** (v13) — frequent OT expression for troublemakers (1 Sam 2:12, 2 Sam 20:1). In the NT (2 Cor 6:15) Paul uses Belial as a personification of evil opposed to Christ.
