# Deuteronomy 6

[1] "And this is the commandment, the statutes and the ordinances, that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ commanded to teach you, to do in the land to which you are passing over to possess it,

[2] so that you may fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to keep all his statutes and his commandments which I am commanding you, you and your son and the son of your son, all the days of your life, and so that your days may be long.

[3] And you shall hear, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and keep to do, that it may go well with you, and that you may multiply greatly, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of your fathers spoke to you — a land flowing with milk and honey.

[4] Hear, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is one.

[5] And you shall love ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your might.

[6] And these words which I am commanding you today shall be upon your heart.

[7] And you shall impress them upon your sons, and you shall speak of them when you sit in your house and when you walk on the way and when you lie down and when you rise.

[8] And you shall bind them for a sign upon your hand, and they shall be for frontlets between your eyes.

[9] And you shall write them upon the doorposts of your house and in your gates.

[10] And it shall be, when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ brings you to the land that he swore to your fathers, to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, to ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ and to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, to give you — cities great and good that you did not build,

[11] and houses full of every good thing that you did not fill, and hewn pits that you did not hew, vineyards and olive trees that you did not plant — and you eat and are sated,

[12] take heed to yourself, lest you forget ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who brought you out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, from the house of slaves.

[13] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ you shall fear, and him you shall serve, and by his name you shall swear.

[14] You shall not go after other gods, from the gods of the peoples who are around you,

[15] for a jealous ⟦el|El|God⟧ is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in your midst; lest the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ burn against you, and he destroy you from upon the face of the ground.

[16] You shall not test ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, as you tested at Massah.

[17] You shall surely keep the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and his testimonies and his statutes that he commanded you.

[18] And you shall do what is right and good in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, so that it may go well with you, and you may go in and take possession of the good land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ swore to your fathers,

[19] to push all your enemies away from before you, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken.

[20] When your son asks you tomorrow, saying, 'What are the testimonies and the statutes and the ordinances that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ commanded you?',

[21] then you shall say to your son, 'Slaves we were to ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ brought us out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ with a strong hand.

[22] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave signs and wonders, great and evil, upon ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, upon ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ and upon all his house, before our eyes.

[23] And us he brought out from there, in order to bring us in, to give us the land that he swore to our fathers.

[24] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded us to do all these statutes, to fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for good to us all the days, to keep us alive, as at this day.

[25] And righteousness it shall be for us, if we keep to do all this commandment before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, as he commanded us.'

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v14 the same word is "gods".
- **"You"** — the Hebrew changes between the plural (v1, 3 "you may multiply", 14, 16, 17a, 20 "commanded you") and the singular (all the rest, also "that he commanded you" at the end of v17). English cannot show it; the Czech text does.
- **"Now these are the commandments" → "And this is the commandment"** (v1) — Hebrew וְזֹאת הַמִּצְוָה, singular: the whole charge (5:31), set out as "the statutes and the ordinances". **"that ye might do them in the land whither ye go to possess it" → "to do in the land to which you are passing over to possess it"** — Hebrew לַעֲשׂוֹת "to do" (no "them"); עֹבְרִים "passing over" (the Jordan).
- **"which I command thee, thou, and thy son, and thy son's son" → "which I am commanding you, you and your son and the son of your son"** (v2) — a participle; "you and your son …" belongs to "that you may fear … to keep". **"that thy days may be prolonged" → "so that your days may be long"** — as 5:16.
- **"Hear therefore, O Israel, and observe to do it" → "And you shall hear, Israel, and keep to do"** (v3) — Hebrew וְשָׁמַעְתָּ … וְשָׁמַרְתָּ לַעֲשׂוֹת: not imperatives, and no object ("it", "them"). **"as the LORD God of thy fathers hath promised thee, in the land that floweth" → "as Yahweh the Elohim of your fathers spoke to you — a land flowing"** — Hebrew דִּבֶּר "spoke"; "a land flowing with milk and honey" stands at the end without a preposition ("in" is not in the Hebrew), loosely joined to what goes before.
- **"Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God is one LORD" → "Hear, Israel: Yahweh our Elohim, Yahweh is one"** (v4) — English needs one "is"; it is set with the last word, "one", which is the predicate in every reading, and the first half is left open (so also the Greek of Mark 12:29). Hebrew שְׁמַע יִשְׂרָאֵל יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵינוּ יְהוָה אֶחָד, six words: "Hear, Israel, Yahweh, our-Elohim, Yahweh, one". The four last words have no verb, and Hebrew needs none; where "is" belongs is not written. The readings: (1) "Yahweh our Elohim, Yahweh is one"; (2) "Yahweh is our Elohim, Yahweh is one"; (3) "Yahweh is our Elohim, Yahweh alone"; (4) "Yahweh our Elohim is one Yahweh". The verse supplies only the one "is" and does not choose further. The accents of the Masoretic Text divide after "our Elohim", and the comma follows them. אֶחָד *echad* is the number "one". In the scrolls and printed Bibles the last letter of שְׁמַע and the last letter of אֶחָד are written large. The Greek of Mark 12:29 has a verb at the end, "the Lord our God, the Lord is one" (κύριος εἷς ἐστιν).
- **"with all thine heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy might" → "with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your might"** (v5) — "might" is kept as the nearest plain English word; literally "your muchness". Hebrew בְּכָל־לְבָבְךָ וּבְכָל־נַפְשְׁךָ וּבְכָל־מְאֹדֶךָ. מְאֹד *meod* is the common word "very, much, exceedingly" (v3 "greatly"); as a noun it stands only here and in 2 Kgs 23:25, which takes up this verse: "your muchness, your very-much" — all that a man has in abundance, his utmost. It is not the word for "strength" (כֹּחַ, עֹז) nor for "mind". Later readers gave it as "power" (the Septuagint δύναμις, cited from memory), "strength" (ἰσχύς, Mark 12:30; Luke 10:27) or "wealth" (the Aramaic Targum and the Mishnah, cited from memory); the Gospels have "mind" (διάνοια) as well, which answers to no fourth Hebrew word here. **"And"** at the head of v5–9 is the Hebrew וְ.
- **"shall be in thine heart" → "shall be upon your heart"** (v6) — Hebrew עַל־לְבָבֶךָ.
- **"thou shalt teach them diligently unto thy children" → "you shall impress them upon your sons"** (v7) — Hebrew וְשִׁנַּנְתָּם לְבָנֶיךָ. The verb, in this form only here, is commonly taken from שָׁנַן "sharpen" (32:41): literally "you shall sharpen them for your sons", make them cut in; others take it from a root "repeat" ("you shall repeat them to your sons"). The verse gives the sense; which root is meant is not certain. בָּנִים is "sons". **"shalt talk of them"** — Hebrew וְדִבַּרְתָּ בָּם, "speak of them" (or "in them, with them").
- **"bind them for a sign upon thine hand, and they shall be as frontlets" → "bind them for a sign upon your hand, and they shall be for frontlets"** (v8) — Hebrew לְאוֹת … לְטֹטָפֹת, the one preposition לְ "for" twice, worded as Exod 13:16 (so Deut 11:18); the meaning of טֹטָפֹת is uncertain (a band or mark on the forehead).
- **"on the posts of thy house, and on thy gates" → "upon the doorposts of your house and in your gates"** (v9) — Hebrew מְזוּזֹת *mezuzot* "doorposts" (Exod 12:7); בִּשְׁעָרֶיךָ "in your gates".
- **"And it shall be, when … to give thee"** (v10) — one long sentence in the Hebrew: "when Yahweh … brings you … and you eat and are sated (v11), take heed (v12)". **"goodly cities" → "cities … good"** — Hebrew טֹבֹת.
- **"wells digged, which thou diggedst not" → "hewn pits that you did not hew"** (v11) — Hebrew בֹּרֹת חֲצוּבִים: בּוֹר is "pit", here a cistern cut into the rock to hold water (as Gen 37:24). **"olive trees"** — Hebrew זֵיתִים. **"when thou shalt have eaten and be full" → "and you eat and are sated"** — Hebrew וְאָכַלְתָּ וְשָׂבָעְתָּ.
- **"Then beware" → "take heed to yourself"** (v12) — Hebrew הִשָּׁמֶר לְךָ, literally "keep yourself". **"from the house of bondage" → "from the house of slaves"** — as 5:6.
- **"Thou shalt fear the LORD thy God, and serve him, and shalt swear by his name" → "Yahweh your Elohim you shall fear, and him you shall serve, and by his name you shall swear"** (v13) — the Hebrew order, each object first. The verse is quoted in Matt 4:10 and Luke 4:8 in another wording: "You shall bow down to (προσκυνήσεις) the Lord your God, and him alone shall you worship (λατρεύσεις)". The Hebrew has "fear", not "bow down", and no word "alone".
- **"of the gods of the people which are round about you" → "from the gods of the peoples who are around you"** (v14) — Hebrew הָעַמִּים, plural.
- **"(For the LORD thy God is a jealous God among you)" → "for a jealous El is Yahweh your Elohim in your midst"** (v15) — Hebrew כִּי אֵל קַנָּא יְהוָה אֱלֹהֶיךָ בְּקִרְבֶּךָ, "a jealous El" first (5:9); "zealous" is the same word. **"be kindled against thee" → "burn against you"** — Hebrew יֶחֱרֶה אַף, literally "the nose burn". **"from off the face of the earth" → "from upon the face of the ground"** — Hebrew הָאֲדָמָה.
- **"Ye shall not tempt the LORD your God, as ye tempted him in Massah" → "You shall not test Yahweh your Elohim, as you tested at Massah"** (v16) — Hebrew נִסָּה "test, put to the proof" (Exod 17:2, 7); the second verb has no object. מַסָּה *Massah* is "testing", here with the article ("at the Testing"): the name and the verb are one root. Matt 4:7 and Luke 4:12 quote the first half in the singular (οὐκ ἐκπειράσεις, there "You shall not tempt"); the Hebrew is plural.
- **"Ye shall diligently keep" → "You shall surely keep"** (v17) — Hebrew שָׁמוֹר תִּשְׁמְרוּן, the infinitive absolute with its verb ("keeping you shall keep").
- **"that which is right and good in the sight of the LORD" → "what is right and good in the eyes of Yahweh"** (v18) — Hebrew הַיָּשָׁר וְהַטּוֹב בְּעֵינֵי. **"that thou mayest go in and possess the good land which the LORD sware unto thy fathers" → "and you may go in and take possession of the good land that Yahweh swore to your fathers"** — Hebrew וּבָאתָ וְיָרַשְׁתָּ; the Hebrew has no "to give".
- **"To cast out all thine enemies" → "to push all your enemies away"** (v19) — Hebrew לַהֲדֹף, from הָדַף "push, thrust".
- **"in time to come" → "tomorrow"** (v20) — Hebrew מָחָר "tomorrow", used for any later day (Exod 13:14). **"saying"** — Hebrew לֵאמֹר.
- **"We were Pharaoh's bondmen" → "Slaves we were to Pharaoh"** (v21) — Hebrew עֲבָדִים הָיִינוּ לְפַרְעֹה, "slaves" first. **"mighty hand" → "strong hand"** — Hebrew יָד חֲזָקָה.
- **"shewed signs and wonders, great and sore" → "gave signs and wonders, great and evil"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיִּתֵּן "gave"; רָעִים "evil", bad for those they fell on: harmful, grievous. **"upon Egypt, upon Pharaoh, and upon all his household"** — the same בְּ three times ("in / against").
- **"And he brought us out from thence, that he might bring us in" → "And us he brought out from there, in order to bring us in"** (v23) — "us" stands first in the Hebrew.
- **"for our good always, that he might preserve us alive, as it is at this day" → "for good to us all the days, to keep us alive, as at this day"** (v24) — Hebrew לְטוֹב לָנוּ כָּל־הַיָּמִים לְחַיֹּתֵנוּ כְּהַיּוֹם הַזֶּה; לְחַיֹּתֵנוּ is "to make / let us live".
- **"And it shall be our righteousness, if we observe to do all these commandments" → "And righteousness it shall be for us, if we keep to do all this commandment"** (v25) — Hebrew וּצְדָקָה תִּהְיֶה־לָּנוּ, "righteousness" first; כִּי "if / when"; הַמִּצְוָה singular (v1).

### Notes
- **The *Shema*** (v4) — named from its first word, "Hear"; Judaism's central declaration, recited morning and evening. Jesus calls v4–5 the first commandment (Mark 12:29–30; Matt 22:37; Luke 10:27).
- **Heart, soul, might** (v5) — the Hebrew names three; the Gospels' Greek has three or four (heart, soul, mind, strength). The Gospel verses are translated from their Greek, this verse from its Hebrew.
- **Tefillin / Mezuzah** (v8, 9) — binding on the hand and frontlets between the eyes are the basis of *tefillin* (phylacteries); writing on the doorposts, of the *mezuzah*. Literal observance is known from the Second Temple period onward; some (among them the Karaites) read the words as a figure.
- **Forgetting in plenty** (v10–12) — the land is gift: cities not built, houses not filled, pits not hewn, vineyards not planted.
- **The son's question** (v20–25) — one of the "son-questions" of the Torah (Exod 12:26; 13:8, 14), the basis of the "Four Sons" of the Passover Haggadah.
- **"Righteousness it shall be for us"** (v25) — cf. Lev 18:5, and Paul's use of it in Rom 10:5.
