## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"unto the mountain of Nebo, to the top of Pisgah, that is over against Jericho" → "to Mount Nebo, the top of the Pisgah, which is facing Jericho"** (v1) — Hebrew רֹאשׁ הַפִּסְגָּה, literally "the head of the Pisgah", with the article; עַל־פְּנֵי, literally "on the face of". עַרְבֹת מוֹאָב "the plains of Moab" is the dry steppe by the Jordan. "Gilead" has the article in the Hebrew (הַגִּלְעָד).
- **"unto the utmost sea" → "as far as the hinder sea"** (v2) — Hebrew הַיָּם הָאַחֲרוֹן, literally "the sea behind": the sea at the back of one who faces the sunrise, that is, the western sea, the Mediterranean (11:24).
- **"the south, and the plain of the valley of Jericho, the city of palm trees" → "the Negev, and the circle, the plain of Jericho, the city of the palm trees"** (v3) — Hebrew הַנֶּגֶב; הַכִּכָּר, literally "the round": the round district of the lower Jordan (Gen 13:10); בִּקְעַת יְרֵחוֹ, a broad valley floor.
- **"I will give it unto thy seed. I have caused thee to see it with thine eyes, but thou shalt not go over thither" → "'To your seed I will give it.' I have made you see it with your eyes, and there you shall not pass over"** (v4) — Hebrew לְזַרְעֲךָ אֶתְּנֶנָּה (Gen 12:7); הֶרְאִיתִיךָ "I have made you see" (the Hebrew has no "it"); עָבַר "pass over".
- **"the servant of the LORD"** (v5) — Hebrew עֶבֶד, the word for "slave". **"according to the word of the LORD" → "at the mouth of Yahweh"** — Hebrew עַל־פִּי יְהוָה: at his word.
- **"And he buried him" kept** (v6) — Hebrew וַיִּקְבֹּר אֹתוֹ; the Hebrew does not name the subject. The nearest subject is Yahweh (v5); the verb may also be read without a definite subject ("one buried him", that is, "he was buried"). **"in a valley … over against Beth-peor: but no man knoweth of his sepulchre unto this day" → "in the valley … over against Beth-peor; and no man has known his burial place to this day"** — Hebrew בַגַּיְ, with the article; מוּל; לֹא־יָדַע, a past; קְבֻרָתוֹ.
- **"an hundred and twenty years old"** (v7) — Hebrew בֶּן־מֵאָה וְעֶשְׂרִים שָׁנָה, literally "a son of a hundred and twenty years". **"his eye was not dim, nor his natural force abated" → "his eye had not grown dim, and his freshness had not fled"** — Hebrew כָהֲתָה (Gen 27:1); נָס "fled"; לֵחֹה is found only here: from לַח "moist, fresh" (Gen 30:37), so "moisture, freshness", the sap of life; the exact sense is uncertain.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v8, 9) — Hebrew בְּנֵי. **"so the days of weeping and mourning for Moses were ended" → "and the days of the weeping of the mourning for Moses came to an end"** (v8) — Hebrew יְמֵי בְכִי אֵבֶל מֹשֶׁה, a chain of nouns: "the days of the weeping of the mourning of Moses".
- **"hearkened unto him" → "listened to him"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁמְעוּ אֵלָיו. רוּחַ חָכְמָה "the spirit of wisdom" (or "a spirit of wisdom").
- **"there arose not a prophet since" → "there has not risen a prophet again"** (v10) — Hebrew וְלֹא־קָם נָבִיא עוֹד. **"face to face" kept** — Hebrew פָּנִים אֶל־פָּנִים (Exod 33:11).
- **"In all the signs" → "for all the signs"; "none like him" not in the Hebrew** (v11) — Hebrew לְכָל־הָאֹתוֹת וְהַמּוֹפְתִים, "as regards all the signs and the wonders": the sentence of v10 runs on. **"in the sight of" → "before the eyes of"** (v12) — Hebrew לְעֵינֵי.
- **"in all that mighty hand, and in all the great terror which Moses shewed" → "for all the strong hand and for all the great fear that Moses did"** (v12) — Hebrew הַיָּד הַחֲזָקָה; הַמּוֹרָא הַגָּדוֹל, the noun of the verb "fear": the deeds that brought fear; עָשָׂה "did".

### Notes
- **Moses' death** — poignant closing. He sees the land and dies; the burial place is not known — which prevents pilgrimage-veneration; focus remains on Yahweh, not Moses.
- **"He buried him"** (v6) — the Hebrew is ambiguous: "he" could be Yahweh (most natural in context) or an indefinite "one buried him." Jewish tradition: Yahweh buried Moses; Jude 1:9 alludes to a tradition of dispute between Michael and the devil over Moses' body.
- **Age 120** (v7) — full vigor till the end. Gen 6:3 names 120 years; Moses reaches that number.
- **Thirty-day mourning** (v8) — same as Aaron's (Num 20:29).
- **Joshua full of the spirit of wisdom** (v9) — through Moses' laying-on of hands (Num 27:18–23).
- **Moses' unique status** (v10–12) — "no prophet like Moses" — a retrospective statement suggesting later authorship of this epitaph. Hebrew tradition: the Torah ends with an editorial postscript; Moses wrote the body, later prophet(s) wrote this closing.
- **"Face to face"** (v10) — Moses' uniquely intimate prophetic relation with Yahweh. Cf. Num 12:8.
- **Structural closure of Torah** — Deuteronomy ends with Moses' death; the Torah's narrative ends before the land-entry. This opens to Joshua, closing the Pentateuch as a prelude-not-fulfillment.
