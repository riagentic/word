# Deuteronomy 32

[1] Give ear, heavens, and let me speak,
and let the earth hear the sayings of my mouth.

[2] Let my teaching drip as the rain,
let my saying trickle as the dew,
as fine rain upon the vegetation,
and as showers upon the plants.

[3] For the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ I will proclaim;
give greatness to our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!

[4] The Rock, blameless is his work,
for all his ways are judgment;
⟦el|an El|a God⟧ of faithfulness, and there is no injustice,
righteous and upright is he.

[5] It has wrought ruin toward him, not his sons, their blemish,
a generation crooked and twisted.

[6] Is it to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that you deal this,
a people foolish and not wise?
Is not he your father, who got you?
He, he made you and established you.

[7] Remember the days of old,
understand the years of generation and generation;
ask your father, and he will tell you,
your elders, and they will say to you.

[8] When ⟦elyon|Elyon|the Most High⟧ made the nations inherit,
when he parted the sons of man,
he set up the borders of the peoples
by the number of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[9] For the share of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is his people,
⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ the portion of his inheritance.

[10] He found him in a land of wilderness,
and in the waste, the howling of the desert;
he encircled him, he attended to him,
he guarded him as the pupil of his eye.

[11] As an eagle stirs up his nest,
hovers over his young ones,
spreads out his wings, takes him,
bears him on his pinion,

[12] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ alone led him,
and there was no foreign ⟦el|el|god⟧ with him.

[13] He made him ride on the high places of the earth,
and he ate the crops of the field;
and he suckled him with honey from the crag,
and oil from the flint of the rock,

[14] curds of the herd and milk of the flock,
with the fat of lambs,
and rams, sons of Bashan, and he-goats,
with the fat of the kidneys of wheat;
and the blood of the grape you drank, foaming wine.

[15] And ⟦jeshurun|Yeshurun|Jeshurun⟧ grew fat and kicked —
you grew fat, you grew thick, you were gorged —
and he abandoned ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ who made him,
and treated as a fool the Rock of his salvation.

[16] They made him jealous with strangers,
with abominations they vexed him.

[17] They sacrificed to the demons, not ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧,
gods they had not known,
new ones who came from near by,
at whom your fathers had not shuddered.

[18] The Rock who begot you, you neglected,
and you forgot ⟦el|El|God⟧ who was in labor with you.

[19] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ saw and spurned,
from the vexation of his sons and his daughters.

[20] And he said, "Let me hide my face from them,
I will see what their latter end is;
for a generation of perversities are they,
sons in whom there is no faithfulness.

[21] They, they have made me jealous with a no-⟦el|el|god⟧,
they have vexed me with their vapors;
and I, I will make them jealous with a no-people,
with a foolish nation I will vex them.

[22] For a fire has been kindled in my anger,
and it has burned to Sheol beneath,
and it has devoured the earth and its produce,
and it has set aflame the foundations of the mountains.

[23] I will heap evils upon them,
my arrows I will finish on them:

[24] drained by hunger and eaten by burning plague
and bitter destruction;
and the tooth of beasts I will send upon them,
with the venom of the crawlers of the dust.

[25] Outside the sword shall bereave,
and from the chambers, dread;
both young man and virgin,
the suckling with the man of gray hair.

[26] I said, 'I will scatter them to the corners,
I will make their remembrance cease from mortal man,'

[27] were it not that I was afraid of the vexation of the enemy,
lest their adversaries misjudge,
lest they say, 'Our hand is high,
and it is not ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who has wrought all this.'"

[28] For a nation that has lost counsel are they,
and there is no understanding in them.

[29] If they were wise, they would have insight into this,
they would understand their latter end.

[30] How could one pursue a thousand,
and two put ten thousand to flight,
if it were not that their Rock had sold them,
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had handed them over?

[31] For not as our Rock is their rock,
and our enemies are arbiters.

[32] For from the vine of ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ is their vine,
and from the fields of ⟦gomorrah|Amorah|Gomorrah⟧;
their grapes are grapes of poison,
bitter clusters are theirs.

[33] The venom of dragons is their wine,
and the cruel poison of cobras.

[34] Is not this stored up with me,
sealed in my treasuries?

[35] Mine is vengeance and repayment,
for the time when their foot shall slip;
for near is the day of their calamity,
and the things made ready for them hasten.

[36] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will judge his people,
and for his servants he will be sorry,
for he will see that the hand is gone,
and there is none, shut in or left.

[37] And he will say, "Where are their gods,
the rock in whom they took refuge,

[38] who ate the fat of their sacrifices,
drank the wine of their drink offering?
Let them rise and help you,
let it be a hiding place over you!

[39] See now that I, I am he,
and there is no god with me;
I, I put to death and I make alive,
I have struck through and I, I will heal,
and there is none who delivers from my hand.

[40] For I lift up my hand to the heavens,
and I say, 'As I live forever,

[41] if I have whetted the lightning of my sword,
and my hand takes hold of judgment,
I will return vengeance to my adversaries,
and those who hate me I will repay.

[42] I will make my arrows drunk with blood,
and my sword shall devour flesh,
with the blood of the slain and the captives,
from the head of the loose locks of the enemy.'"

[43] Shout for joy, nations, his people,
for the blood of his servants he will avenge,
and vengeance he will return to his adversaries,
and he will make atonement for his ground, his people.

[44] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ came and spoke all the words of this song in the ears of the people, he and Hoshea the son of Nun.

[45] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ finished speaking all these words to all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[46] and he said to them, "Put your heart to all the words with which I am warning you today, which you shall command your sons, to keep to do all the words of this ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

[47] For it is not an empty word for you, for it is your life, and by this word you shall prolong days on the ground to which you are passing over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ to possess it."

[48] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ on that very day, saying,

[49] "Go up to this mountain of ⟦abarim|Avarim|Abarim⟧, Mount ⟦nebo|Nevo|Nebo⟧, which is in the land of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, which is facing ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧, and see the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, which I am giving to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ for a possession.

[50] And die on the mountain to which you are going up, and be gathered to your peoples, as ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ your brother died on Mount Hor and was gathered to his peoples,

[51] because you committed unfaithfulness against me in the midst of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ at the waters of ⟦meribath-kadesh|Merivat Qadesh|Meribath-kadesh⟧, in the wilderness of ⟦zin|Tzin|Zin⟧, because you did not sanctify me in the midst of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[52] For from opposite you shall see the land, and there you shall not go in, to the land that I am giving to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3) — Hebrew *Elohim*. **"God" → "El" / "Eloah"** (v4, 12, 18, 21; v15, 17) — Hebrew אֵל *El* and אֱלוֹהַּ *Eloah*, not *Elohim*; where the word is used of what is no god it has a small letter ("no foreign el", "a no-el"). **"the most High" → "Elyon"** (v8) — Hebrew עֶלְיוֹן. "gods" (v17, 37) and "god" (v39) are אֱלֹהִים of other beings.
- **The song as poetry** (v1–43) — one Hebrew line (colon) to a printed line, in the Hebrew order; v5 has two lines and v25 four, as the Hebrew accents divide them. The song is old poetry: many of its verbs are imperfects that tell of the past (v10–18), rendered as English pasts; "O" before a name is not in the Hebrew and is not added. The quotation marks are English only: Yahweh speaks from v20, and v34–35 are also in his "I".
- **"the words of my mouth" → "the sayings of my mouth"; "my speech" → "my saying"** (v1, 2) — Hebrew אִמְרֵי, אִמְרָתִי, one root. **"and I will speak" → "and let me speak"** — Hebrew וַאֲדַבֵּרָה.
- **"My doctrine shall drop … shall distil" → "Let my teaching drip … let my saying trickle"** (v2) — Hebrew לִקְחִי "what is received", teaching; יַעֲרֹף, תִּזַּל, forms that may be wishes or statements ("my teaching will drip"); נָזַל is "flow". **"as the small rain upon the tender herb, and as the showers upon the grass" → "as fine rain upon the vegetation, and as showers upon the plants"** — Hebrew שְׂעִירִם, found only here: its meaning is uncertain (fine rain, or storm showers); רְבִיבִים "showers"; דֶּשֶׁא "fresh green growth", עֵשֶׂב "plants".
- **"I will publish the name" → "the name of Yahweh I will proclaim"; "ascribe ye greatness" → "give greatness"** (v3) — Hebrew אֶקְרָא "call out"; הָבוּ "give".
- **"He is the Rock, his work is perfect" → "The Rock, blameless is his work"** (v4) — Hebrew הַצּוּר תָּמִים פָּעֳלוֹ; צוּר "rock" is used as a name of God through the song (v4, 15, 18, 30, 31) and written with a capital there; תָּמִים "whole, complete, without blemish", set against "their blemish" in v5. **"all his ways are judgment" kept** — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּט: judgment, what is right. **"a God of truth and without iniquity, just and right" → "an El of faithfulness, and there is no injustice, righteous and upright"** — Hebrew אֱמוּנָה, עָוֶל, צַדִּיק, יָשָׁר.
- **"They have corrupted themselves, their spot is not the spot of his children: they are a perverse and crooked generation" → "It has wrought ruin toward him, not his sons, their blemish, a generation crooked and twisted"** (v5) — Hebrew שִׁחֵת לוֹ לֹא בָּנָיו מוּמָם, word for word "has-wrought-ruin to-him not his-sons their-blemish"; the verb is singular. The line is among the hardest in the song and its sense is uncertain: "they have dealt ruinously with him — no sons of his — it is their blemish"; or, with the Hebrew accents, "Is the ruin his? No: his sons', theirs is the blemish". The verse gives the words in their order and does not decide. פְּתַלְתֹּל "twisted" is found only here.
- **"Do ye thus requite the LORD, O foolish people and unwise? … thy father that hath bought thee? hath he not made thee" → "Is it to Yahweh that you deal this, a people foolish and not wise? … your father, who got you? He, he made you"** (v6) — Hebrew גָּמַל "deal (good or evil) to"; נָבָל "foolish, base" (so v21; the verb of v15 is of this root); קָּנֶךָ, from קָנָה "get, buy, possess"; where God is the subject the sense "make, create" is also defended (Gen 14:19). "You deal" is plural, "your father … you" singular.
- **"consider the years of many generations … he will shew thee" → "understand the years of generation and generation … he will tell you"** (v7) — Hebrew בִּינוּ; דּוֹר־וָדוֹר; וְיַגֵּדְךָ. "Remember" and "ask" are singular, "understand" plural.
- **"divided to the nations their inheritance, when he separated the sons of Adam, he set the bounds of the people according to the number of the children of Israel" → "made the nations inherit, when he parted the sons of man, he set up the borders of the peoples by the number of the sons of Israel"** (v8) — Hebrew בְּהַנְחֵל … גּוֹיִם, "when he caused nations to inherit" (or "gave nations as an inheritance"); בְּנֵי אָדָם "sons of man" or "sons of Adam"; גְּבֻלֹת עַמִּים. The Masoretic Text reads בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל "the sons of Israel" and is translated. A Qumran fragment is reported to read "sons of God" and the Greek "angels of God" (both cited from memory): then the nations are numbered by the heavenly beings; this reading is widely followed, but it is not the printed Hebrew.
- **"the LORD's portion … the lot of his inheritance" → "the share of Yahweh … the portion of his inheritance"** (v9) — Hebrew חֵלֶק; חֶבֶל, literally "cord": the land measured off by the measuring cord (3:4).
- **"in a desert land, and in the waste howling wilderness; he led him about, he instructed him, he kept him as the apple of his eye" → "in a land of wilderness, and in the waste, the howling of the desert; he encircled him, he attended to him, he guarded him as the pupil of his eye"** (v10) — Hebrew מִדְבָּר; תֹהוּ (Gen 1:2); יְלֵל "howling", found only here; יְשִׁמֹן "desert". יְבוֹנְנֵהוּ is of the root "understand": "attended to him, cared for him" or "gave him understanding"; uncertain. אִישׁוֹן, literally "the little man" of the eye.
- **"As an eagle stirreth up her nest, fluttereth over her young, spreadeth abroad her wings, taketh them, beareth them on her wings" → "As an eagle stirs up his nest, hovers over his young ones, spreads out his wings, takes him, bears him on his pinion"** (v11) — Hebrew נֶשֶׁר (the word also covers the great vultures); יְרַחֵף "hover" (Gen 1:2); "him" is singular in the Hebrew (יִקָּחֵהוּ, יִשָּׂאֵהוּ), though the young are plural: the last two lines may go on with the eagle or may already speak of Yahweh and Israel ("he spread out his wings, he took him"). "So" (KJV v12) is not in the Hebrew.
- **"no strange god" → "no foreign el"** (v12) — Hebrew אֵל נֵכָר, literally "an el of foreignness".
- **"the increase of the fields; and he made him to suck honey out of the rock, and oil out of the flinty rock" → "the crops of the field; and he suckled him with honey from the crag, and oil from the flint of the rock"** (v13) — Hebrew תְּנוּבֹת; סֶלַע "crag" and צוּר "rock" are two words; וַיֹּאכַל "and he ate" is the Masoretic Text (the Greek and the Samaritan text have "he fed him", cited from memory). The written form במותי and the reading בָּמֳתֵי "high places of" do not differ in meaning.
- **"Butter of kine, and milk of sheep … rams of the breed of Bashan, and goats, with the fat of kidneys of wheat; and thou didst drink the pure blood of the grape" → "curds of the herd and milk of the flock … rams, sons of Bashan, and he-goats, with the fat of the kidneys of wheat; and the blood of the grape you drank, foaming wine"** (v14) — Hebrew חֶמְאָה; בְּנֵי־בָשָׁן; חֵלֶב "fat" is kept (the best of it); חָמֶר, a rare word for wine (fermenting, foaming); the exact sense is uncertain.
- **"Jeshurun waxed fat … thou art waxen fat, thou art grown thick, thou art covered with fatness; then he forsook God which made him, and lightly esteemed the Rock" → "Jeshurun grew fat … you grew fat, you grew thick, you were gorged — and he abandoned Eloah who made him, and treated as a fool the Rock"** (v15) — Hebrew יְשֻׁרוּן, a name of Israel found only here, in 33:5, 26 and Isa 44:2; it is commonly derived from יָשָׁר "upright", but its meaning is not certain, and no explanation is put in the verse. כָּשִׂיתָ is found only here: "you were gorged" or "you were covered (with fat)"; uncertain. נָטַשׁ "abandon"; וַיְנַבֵּל, of the root of נָבָל "fool" (v6, 21).
- **"They provoked him to jealousy with strange gods, with abominations provoked they him to anger" → "They made him jealous with strangers, with abominations they vexed him"** (v16) — Hebrew בְּזָרִים "with strange ones" ("gods" is not in the Hebrew); הִכְעִיס "vex" (v19, 21, 27; 31:29).
- **"They sacrificed unto devils, not to God; to gods whom they knew not, to new gods that came newly up, whom your fathers feared not" → "They sacrificed to the demons, not Eloah, gods they had not known, new ones who came from near by, at whom your fathers had not shuddered"** (v17) — Hebrew שֵׁדִים, found only here and in Ps 106:37; the word is known from Akkadian for a spirit; "demons" is the best-supported sense and is uncertain. לֹא אֱלֹהַּ "a not-Eloah". מִקָּרֹב "from near": lately. שְׂעָרוּם is uncertain: "shuddered at, dreaded" or "knew".
- **"Of the Rock that begat thee thou art unmindful, and hast forgotten God that formed thee" → "The Rock who begot you, you neglected, and you forgot El who was in labor with you"** (v18) — Hebrew יְלָדְךָ "begot" or "bore"; תֶּשִׁי, a form found only here (commonly taken as "forget, neglect"); מְחֹלְלֶךָ, literally "who writhed with you" as a woman in labor.
- **"when the LORD saw it, he abhorred them, because of the provoking of his sons, and of his daughters" → "Yahweh saw and spurned, from the vexation of his sons and his daughters"** (v19) — Hebrew וַיִּנְאָץ, with no object; מִכַּעַס "from, because of the vexation".
- **"I will hide my face … what their end shall be: for they are a very froward generation, children in whom is no faith" → "Let me hide my face … what their latter end is; for a generation of perversities are they, sons in whom there is no faithfulness"** (v20) — Hebrew אַחֲרִיתָם (so v29); תַּהְפֻּכֹת, from הָפַךְ "turn over"; אֵמֻן, of the root of אֱמוּנָה (v4).
- **"moved me to jealousy with that which is not God; they have provoked me to anger with their vanities … with those which are not a people" → "made me jealous with a no-el, they have vexed me with their vapors … with a no-people"** (v21) — Hebrew בְלֹא־אֵל … בְּלֹא־עָם, the same form twice; הֲבָלִים "vapors, breaths": things of nothing. Rom 10:19 cites the second half.
- **"shall burn unto the lowest hell, and shall consume the earth with her increase" → "it has burned to Sheol beneath, and it has devoured the earth and its produce"** (v22) — Hebrew שְׁאוֹל תַּחְתִּית "Sheol the lower"; the verbs are pasts in the Hebrew. בְאַפִּי "in my anger", literally "in my nose".
- **"I will heap mischiefs upon them; I will spend mine arrows" → "I will heap evils upon them, my arrows I will finish on them"** (v23) — Hebrew אַסְפֶּה, as pointed from a verb "sweep (together)"; רָעוֹת; אֲכַלֶּה.
- **"They shall be burnt with hunger, and devoured with burning heat, and with bitter destruction" → "drained by hunger and eaten by burning plague and bitter destruction"** (v24) — the Hebrew has no verb: מְזֵי רָעָב וּלְחֻמֵי רֶשֶׁף וְקֶטֶב מְרִירִי. מְזֵי is found only here ("sucked out, wasted"); רֶשֶׁף is "flame" and a word for plague; קֶטֶב "destruction, pestilence"; מְרִירִי "bitter" is found only here. All four are uncertain. **"the poison of serpents of the dust" → "the venom of the crawlers of the dust"** — Hebrew חֲמַת, literally "heat" (the word for "fury"; so v33).
- **"The sword without, and terror within, shall destroy both the young man and the virgin, the suckling also with the man of gray hairs" → "Outside the sword shall bereave, and from the chambers, dread; both young man and virgin, the suckling with the man of gray hair"** (v25) — Hebrew תְּשַׁכֶּל "bereave"; מֵחֲדָרִים "from the inner rooms"; אֵימָה.
- **"I said, I would scatter them into corners, I would make the remembrance of them to cease from among men" → "I said, 'I will scatter them to the corners, I will make their remembrance cease from mortal man'"** (v26) — Hebrew אַפְאֵיהֶם, found only here; its meaning is uncertain ("scatter", so the Greek, cited from memory; taken from פֵּאָה "corner, edge"; others "cut them in pieces", "blow them away"). אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man". With v27 the sense is "I would have said".
- **"Were it not that I feared the wrath of the enemy, lest their adversaries should behave themselves strangely … Our hand is high, and the LORD hath not done all this" → "were it not that I was afraid of the vexation of the enemy, lest their adversaries misjudge … 'Our hand is high, and it is not Yahweh who has wrought all this'"** (v27) — Hebrew כַּעַס; אָגוּר "be afraid of, shrink from" (not the usual verb "fear"); יְנַכְּרוּ, of the root "foreign": "treat as strange, misjudge"; uncertain. פָּעַל "work".
- **"void of counsel" → "that has lost counsel"** (v28) — Hebrew אֹבַד עֵצוֹת, literally "perishing of counsels". תְּבוּנָה "understanding".
- **"O that they were wise, that they understood this, that they would consider their latter end!" → "If they were wise, they would have insight into this, they would understand their latter end"** (v29) — Hebrew לוּ "if" or "would that"; יַשְׂכִּילוּ, יָבִינוּ.
- **"How should one chase a thousand … and the LORD had shut them up?" → "How could one pursue a thousand … and Yahweh had handed them over?"** (v30) — Hebrew יִרְדֹּף "pursue"; הִסְגִּירָם, literally "shut them up": gave them over (23:15).
- **"even our enemies themselves being judges" → "and our enemies are arbiters"** (v31) — Hebrew פְּלִילִים, a rare word (Exod 21:22); uncertain.
- **"grapes of gall, their clusters are bitter" → "grapes of poison, bitter clusters are theirs"** (v32) — Hebrew רוֹשׁ, a bitter poisonous plant; שַׁדְמֹת "fields, terraces".
- **"Their wine is the poison of dragons, and the cruel venom of asps" → "The venom of dragons is their wine, and the cruel poison of cobras"** (v33) — Hebrew תַּנִּינִם, great serpents; פְּתָנִים, a venomous snake (the kind is not certain); חֲמַת and רֹאשׁ as in v24, 32.
- **"laid up in store with me, and sealed up among my treasures" → "stored up with me, sealed in my treasuries"** (v34) — Hebrew כָּמֻס, found only here; uncertain.
- **"To me belongeth vengeance, and recompence; their foot shall slide in due time" → "Mine is vengeance and repayment, for the time when their foot shall slip"** (v35) — Hebrew לִי נָקָם וְשִׁלֵּם לְעֵת תָּמוּט רַגְלָם; שִׁלֵּם is of the root of "repay" (v41). The Samaritan text and the Greek read "for the day of vengeance" (cited from memory). Rom 12:19 and Heb 10:30 cite the line in another form, "Vengeance is mine, I will repay"; the Hebrew is kept as it stands. **"the things that shall come upon them make haste" → "the things made ready for them hasten"** — Hebrew עֲתִדֹת.
- **"the LORD shall judge his people, and repent himself for his servants, when he seeth that their power is gone, and there is none shut up, or left" → "Yahweh will judge his people, and for his servants he will be sorry, for he will see that the hand is gone, and there is none, shut in or left"** (v36) — Hebrew יָדִין "judge" (plead the cause of, or pass sentence on); יִתְנֶחָם, of נִחַם "be sorry, relent": feel sorrow for them or relent toward them; אָזְלַת יָד "the hand has gone"; עָצוּר וְעָזוּב, a fixed pair whose sense is uncertain ("bond and free", "kept in and let go"). Heb 10:30 cites the first line.
- **"their rock in whom they trusted" → "the rock in whom they took refuge"** (v37) — Hebrew חָסָיוּ. The Hebrew does not name the one who says it. **"let them be your protection" → "let it be a hiding place over you"** (v38) — Hebrew יְהִי, a singular; סִתְרָה, of the root "hide" (v20).
- **"I, even I, am he, and there is no god with me: I kill, and I make alive; I wound, and I heal: neither is there any that can deliver out of my hand" → "I, I am he, and there is no god with me; I, I put to death and I make alive, I have struck through and I, I will heal, and there is none who delivers from my hand"** (v39) — word for word: אֲנִי אֲנִי הוּא; אֱלֹהִים "god" or "gods"; אָמִית וַאֲחַיֶּה; מָחַצְתִּי, a past; וַאֲנִי אֶרְפָּא.
- **"I lift up my hand to heaven, and say, I live for ever" → "I lift up my hand to the heavens, and I say, 'As I live forever'"** (v40) — the lifted hand is the gesture of an oath; חַי אָנֹכִי, literally "living am I". The "if" of v41 is a condition ("when once I have whetted"), not the oath form.
- **"If I whet my glittering sword … I will render vengeance to mine enemies, and will reward them that hate me" → "if I have whetted the lightning of my sword … I will return vengeance to my adversaries, and those who hate me I will repay"** (v41) — Hebrew בְּרַק חַרְבִּי; אָשִׁיב; צָרָי; אֲשַׁלֵּם.
- **"from the beginning of revenges upon the enemy" → "from the head of the loose locks of the enemy"** (v42) — Hebrew מֵרֹאשׁ פַּרְעוֹת אוֹיֵב; פַּרְעוֹת is found only here and in Judg 5:2 and is uncertain: "loose (long) hair" or "leaders" ("from the head of the leaders of the enemy").
- **"Rejoice, O ye nations, with his people … and will be merciful unto his land, and to his people" → "Shout for joy, nations, his people … and he will make atonement for his ground, his people"** (v43) — Hebrew הַרְנִינוּ גוֹיִם עַמּוֹ, with no "with": "nations, (with) his people", or "nations, acclaim his people"; וְכִפֶּר אַדְמָתוֹ עַמּוֹ, literally "and he will atone his ground, his people". The Greek has a longer verse ("Rejoice, heavens, with him, and let all the sons of God bow down to him; rejoice, nations, with his people …"; cited from memory), part of which is reported from Qumran; Rom 15:10 and Heb 1:6 cite that Greek form. The shorter Masoretic Text is translated, and nothing is added from the other.
- **"in the ears of the people, he, and Hoshea"** (v44) — Hebrew הוֹשֵׁעַ, the earlier name of Joshua (Num 13:8, 16).
- **"Set your hearts unto all the words which I testify among you this day, which ye shall command your children to observe to do" → "Put your heart to all the words with which I am warning you today, which you shall command your sons, to keep to do"** (v46) — Hebrew שִׂימוּ לְבַבְכֶם; מֵעִיד בָּכֶם, literally "testifying against you"; בְּנֵיכֶם; לִשְׁמֹר לַעֲשׂוֹת.
- **"it is not a vain thing for you" → "it is not an empty word for you"** (v47) — Hebrew דָבָר רֵק … מִכֶּם, literally "a word empty from you": too empty for you. **"in the land" → "on the ground"** — Hebrew הָאֲדָמָה.
- **"that selfsame day" → "on that very day"** (v48) — Hebrew בְּעֶצֶם הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה, literally "in the bone of this day".
- **"Get thee up into this mountain Abarim, unto mount Nebo … that is over against Jericho; and behold" → "Go up to this mountain of Abarim, Mount Nebo … which is facing Jericho, and see"** (v49) — Hebrew הַר הָעֲבָרִים, with the article; עַל־פְּנֵי, literally "on the face of"; וּרְאֵה. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v49, 51, 52) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"be gathered unto thy people … in mount Hor" → "be gathered to your peoples … on Mount Hor"** (v50) — Hebrew עַמֶּיךָ, עַמָּיו, plurals: one's kinsfolk; הֹר הָהָר, literally "Hor the mountain".
- **"ye trespassed against me … ye sanctified me not" → "you committed unfaithfulness against me … you did not sanctify me"** (v51) — Hebrew מְעַלְתֶּם בִּי; קִדַּשְׁתֶּם; "you" is plural (Moses and Aaron). מְרִיבַת קָדֵשׁ is "the strife of Kadesh" (Num 20:13; 27:14).
- **"thou shalt see the land before thee; but thou shalt not go thither" → "from opposite you shall see the land, and there you shall not go in"** (v52) — Hebrew מִנֶּגֶד "from over against"; לֹא תָבוֹא.

### Notes
- **The Song of Moses** (v1–43) — one of the OT's oldest and most theologically important poems. Universal in scope (heavens, earth, nations), retrospective-prospective in time, alternating praise and indictment.
- **Apportionment of the nations** (v8–9) — in the Masoretic Text the borders of the peoples are set "by the number of the sons of Israel". With the other reading ("sons of God", see Changes) the verse is read as Elyon assigning the nations to heavenly beings while Yahweh's share is Israel; many modern scholars hold that reading to be the older.
- **Rock motif** (v4, 15, 18, 30, 31, 37) — recurring metaphor; Yahweh's steadfastness contrasts with other gods' inferiority.
- **"Mine is vengeance"** (v35) — cited in Rom 12:19 and Heb 10:30 in another form. Yahweh's exclusive prerogative.
- **"I put to death and I make alive"** (v39) — divine sovereignty over life and death; echoed in Hannah's song (1 Sam 2:6).
- **NT quotations** — v21 in Rom 10:19 (and 11:11, 14); v35 in Rom 12:19; Heb 10:30; v36 in Heb 10:30; v43 (in the Greek form) in Rom 15:10; Heb 1:6. The Song of Moses is a foundational text for NT reflection on Israel, judgment, and eschatological mercy.
- **Moses' death announced** (v48–52) — final-hour command. The land is shown; entry is denied. Mount Nebo / Pisgah — in present-day Jordan.
