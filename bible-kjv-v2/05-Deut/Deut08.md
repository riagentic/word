# Deuteronomy 8

[1] "All the commandment which I am commanding you today you shall keep to do, so that you may live and multiply and go in and take possession of the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ swore to your fathers.

[2] And you shall remember all the way that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has made you walk these forty years in the wilderness, in order to afflict you, to test you, to know what is in your heart, whether you will keep his commandments or not.

[3] And he afflicted you and made you hunger and fed you the manna, which you did not know and your fathers did not know, in order to make you know that not on bread alone shall man live, but on all that goes out of the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall man live.

[4] Your cloak did not wear out from upon you, and your foot did not swell, these forty years.

[5] And you shall know with your heart that as a man disciplines his son, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is disciplining you.

[6] And you shall keep the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to walk in his ways and to fear him.

[7] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is bringing you to a good land, a land of wadis of water, of springs and deeps going out in the plain and in the mountain,

[8] a land of wheat and barley and vine and fig tree and pomegranate, a land of the oil olive and of honey,

[9] a land in which you shall eat bread without scarcity, in which you shall lack nothing, a land whose stones are iron, and out of whose mountains you shall hew copper.

[10] And you shall eat and be sated, and you shall bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for the good land that he has given you.

[11] Take heed to yourself, lest you forget ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, so as not to keep his commandments and his ordinances and his statutes which I am commanding you today;

[12] lest you eat and are sated, and build good houses and dwell,

[13] and your herd and your flock multiply, and silver and gold multiply for you, and all that you have multiplies,

[14] and your heart is lifted up, and you forget ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who brought you out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, from the house of slaves,

[15] who made you walk in the great and fearsome wilderness, ⟦saraf|saraf serpent|fiery serpent⟧ and scorpion, and thirsty ground where there is no water; who brought out water for you from the rock of flint;

[16] who fed you manna in the wilderness, which your fathers did not know, in order to afflict you and in order to test you, to do you good in your latter end;

[17] and you say in your heart, 'My power and the might of my hand have made me this wealth.'

[18] And you shall remember ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for he it is who gives you power to make wealth, in order to establish his covenant that he swore to your fathers, as at this day.

[19] And it shall be, if you surely forget ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and go after other gods and serve them and bow down to them, I warn you today that you shall surely perish.

[20] Like the nations that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is making perish from before you, so you shall perish, because you will not listen to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; "gods" in v19.
- **"You"** — singular in the Hebrew from "commanding you" (v1) to "bow down to them" (v19); plural in v1b ("you shall keep to do … your fathers") and from "I warn you" (v19b) to the end. English cannot show it; the Czech text does.
- **"All the commandments … shall ye observe to do" → "All the commandment … you shall keep to do"** (v1) — Hebrew כָּל־הַמִּצְוָה, singular (6:1); שָׁמַר לַעֲשׂוֹת as 5:1. **"go in and possess" → "go in and take possession of"** — Hebrew וּבָאתֶם וִירִשְׁתֶּם. **"which the LORD sware unto your fathers"** — no "to give" in the Hebrew.
- **"which the LORD thy God led thee" → "that Yahweh your Elohim has made you walk"** (v2, so v15) — Hebrew הֹלִיכֲךָ, the causative of הָלַךְ "walk, go". **"to humble thee, and to prove thee" → "in order to afflict you, to test you"** (v2, 3, 16) — Hebrew עִנָּה, "bow down, bring low, afflict" (the word of Egypt's affliction of Israel, Exod 1:11, 12, and of "afflicting the soul", Lev 16:29); "humble" gives the purpose, "afflict" the act: both are in the word. נִסָּה "test" (6:16). **"to know what was in thine heart"** — "is": the Hebrew has no verb. The written text has מצותו; the reading tradition מִצְוֺתָיו "his commandments" (as 7:9).
- **"suffered thee to hunger, and fed thee with manna" → "made you hunger and fed you the manna"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיַּרְעִבֶךָ, a causative; הַמָּן, with the article. The Hebrew word is *man* (Exod 16:15, 31).
- **"that man doth not live by bread only, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of the LORD doth man live" → "that not on bread alone shall man live, but on all that goes out of the mouth of Yahweh shall man live"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּי לֹא עַל־הַלֶּחֶם לְבַדּוֹ יִחְיֶה הָאָדָם כִּי עַל־כָּל־מוֹצָא פִי־יְהוָה יִחְיֶה הָאָדָם. עַל is "on"; הָאָדָם "the man", mankind; כָּל־מוֹצָא פִי is "every going-out of the mouth", all that comes out of it. There is no word "word" in the Hebrew: what goes out of Yahweh's mouth is his word and command, and what he calls into being by it (the manna). "Shall man live" stands twice. Matt 4:4 quotes the verse in the Greek form, which has "every word" (ῥῆμα) and "God" for "Yahweh", and the verb once; Luke 4:4 has only the first half.
- **"Thy raiment waxed not old upon thee" → "Your cloak did not wear out from upon you"** (v4) — Hebrew שִׂמְלָה, the outer wrap; מֵעָלֶיךָ "from upon you". **"neither did thy foot swell"** kept — Hebrew בָּצֵקָה, found only here and in Neh 9:21; "swell" (as dough, בָּצֵק) is the usual sense, not certain.
- **"Thou shalt also consider in thine heart" → "And you shall know with your heart"** (v5) — Hebrew וְיָדַעְתָּ עִם־לְבָבֶךָ. **"chasteneth" → "disciplines … is disciplining"** — Hebrew יִסֵּר (Lev 26:18): to correct and train, by word or by blows.
- **"a land of brooks of water, of fountains and depths that spring out of valleys and hills" → "a land of wadis of water, of springs and deeps going out in the plain and in the mountain"** (v7) — Hebrew נַחַל "wadi", a stream bed; עַיִן "spring"; תְּהוֹם "deep" (Gen 1:2; 7:11), the waters under the earth; בִּקְעָה "plain", a broad valley floor; הַר "mountain", both singular.
- **"vines, and fig trees, and pomegranates; a land of oil olive" → "vine and fig tree and pomegranate, a land of the oil olive"** (v8) — all singular in the Hebrew, each with "and"; אֶרֶץ־זֵית שֶׁמֶן is "a land of the olive of oil", the olive tree that gives oil.
- **"without scarceness, thou shalt not lack any thing in it … thou mayest dig brass" → "without scarcity, in which you shall lack nothing … you shall hew copper"** (v9) — מִסְכֵּנֻת, found only here, "poverty, scarcity" (from מִסְכֵּן "poor"); חָצַב "hew" (6:11); נְחֹשֶׁת is copper as it comes from the ground (elsewhere "bronze", the metal made from it).
- **"When thou hast eaten and art full, then thou shalt bless" → "And you shall eat and be sated, and you shall bless"** (v10) — three verbs joined by "and" (6:11).
- **"Beware that thou forget not the LORD thy God, in not keeping" → "Take heed to yourself, lest you forget Yahweh your Elohim, so as not to keep"** (v11) — Hebrew הִשָּׁמֶר לְךָ (6:12); לְבִלְתִּי שְׁמֹר. **"judgments" → "ordinances"**.
- **"and dwelt therein" → "and dwell"** (v12) — the Hebrew has only וְיָשָׁבְתָּ "and dwell"; "therein" is not written.
- **"Then thine heart be lifted up" → "and your heart is lifted up"** (v14) — Hebrew וְרָם לְבָבֶךָ, "and your heart is high". **"from the house of bondage" → "from the house of slaves"**.
- **"Who led thee through that great and terrible wilderness, wherein were fiery serpents, and scorpions, and drought, where there was no water" → "who made you walk in the great and fearsome wilderness, saraf serpent and scorpion, and thirsty ground where there is no water"** (v15) — Hebrew נָחָשׁ שָׂרָף וְעַקְרָב וְצִמָּאוֹן, three singular nouns set beside "the wilderness" without a joining word. שָׂרָף *saraf* is "burning one" (Num 21:6). צִמָּאוֹן is "thirsty ground", from צָמָא "thirst". נוֹרָא "fearsome" (7:21). **"out of the rock of flint"** — Hebrew צוּר הַחַלָּמִישׁ.
- **"that he might humble thee, and that he might prove thee, to do thee good at thy latter end" → "in order to afflict you and in order to test you, to do you good in your latter end"** (v16) — Hebrew בְּאַחֲרִיתֶךָ, "in your after-part", your later time.
- **"hath gotten me this wealth" → "have made me this wealth"** (v17), **"power to get wealth" → "power to make wealth"** (v18) — Hebrew עָשָׂה "make, do"; חַיִל is "strength, ability" and what it gains, "wealth". כֹּחַ "power"; עֹצֶם "might".
- **"that he may establish his covenant"** (v18) — Hebrew הָקִים, "make stand".
- **"if thou do at all forget" → "if you surely forget"** (v19) — Hebrew שָׁכֹחַ תִּשְׁכַּח, the infinitive absolute with its verb; so "you shall surely perish" (אָבֹד תֹּאבֵדוּן). **"I testify against you this day" → "I warn you today"** — Hebrew הַעִדֹתִי בָכֶם, literally "I have testified against you": a formal notice given before witnesses (Exod 19:21).
- **"As the nations which the LORD destroyeth before your face" → "Like the nations that Yahweh is making perish from before you"** (v20) — Hebrew מַאֲבִיד, the causative of the "perish" that follows. **"because ye would not be obedient unto the voice" → "because you will not listen to the voice"** — Hebrew עֵקֶב (7:12) לֹא תִשְׁמְעוּן בְּקוֹל.

### Notes
- **"Not on bread alone"** (v3) — quoted by Jesus in the wilderness (Matt 4:4; Luke 4:4).
- **Forty years** (v2–5) — the wilderness as discipline: affliction, testing, the heart made known.
- **Seven fruits of the land** (v8) — wheat, barley, vine, fig, pomegranate, olive, honey: the "Seven Species" (*Shiv'at Ha-Minim*) of later Jewish tradition, in which the honey is taken as date honey.
- **Blessing after eating** (v10) — "you shall eat and be sated and bless Yahweh": the source verse of the Jewish grace after meals (*Birkat ha-Mazon*).
- **Plenty and forgetting** (v11–17) — the sentence runs from "lest you forget" (v11) through v17: eating, building, multiplying, the heart lifted up, forgetting, and at last the saying "My power … has made me this wealth".
