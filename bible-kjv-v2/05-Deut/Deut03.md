## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"giants" → "Rephaim"** (v11, 13) — as in ch 2.
- **"utterly destroyed" → "devoted to destruction"** (v6) — *cherem*.
- **"cubit of a man" → "common cubit"** (v11) — Hebrew בְּאַמַּת אִישׁ *be'ammat ish*. Standard rather than royal cubit.
- **"fenced" → "fortified"** (v5).
- **"Sirion" (v9)** — Phoenician name for Mt Hermon; attested in Ugaritic texts.
- **"Senir" (v9)** — Amorite / Assyrian name for Hermon.
- **"Havvoth-Jair"** (v14) — Hebrew חַוֹּת יָאִיר *Chavvot Ya'ir* = "settlements of Jair"; as in Num 32.
- **"besought" → "pleaded with"** (v23) — modernized.
- **"Lord God" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v24) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai YHWH*. Special compound title.
- **"goodly mountain" → "good hill country"** (v25) — Hebrew *ha-har ha-tov*.

### Notes
- **Og's iron bed** (v11) — the relic is cited as historical evidence for his size. "Bed" (*eres*) possibly a sarcophagus. 9 x 4 cubits = ~13 x 6 feet / 4 x 1.8 m. Still on display at Rabbath-ammon when Deut was composed/edited. Archaeology: dolmen-tombs in the region match this description.
- **Transjordan allotment** — Reuben and Gad receive the southern half (from Arnon to Jabbok); Manasseh's half-tribe receives Bashan (north of Jabbok).
- **Moses' plea** (v23–25) — poignant. He asks once, is refused, and accepts. The *only* record of Moses' personal request to Yahweh that is denied. Theological theme: even the servant of Yahweh is not exempt from consequences; leadership and justice require forbearance.
- **View from Pisgah** (v27) — Moses will see but not enter. Fulfilled in ch 34. The four-direction panorama echoes Abraham's view (Gen 13:14–15).
- **"Yahweh fights for you"** (v22) — Deuteronomy's recurring war-theology. Cf. Deut 20:4, Josh 10:42.
