# Deuteronomy 12

[1] "These are the statutes and the ordinances that you shall be careful to do in the land that Yahweh the *Elohim* of your fathers is giving you to possess, all the days that you live on the earth.

[2] You shall utterly destroy all the places where the nations whom you shall dispossess served their gods — on the high mountains, on the hills, and under every green tree.

[3] You shall break down their altars and shatter their pillars; their Asherah poles you shall burn with fire; you shall cut down the carved images of their gods and obliterate their names from that place.

[4] You shall not worship Yahweh your *Elohim* in that manner.

[5] But to the place that Yahweh your *Elohim* will choose out of all your tribes to put his name there as his dwelling — there you shall seek him, and there you shall come.

[6] There you shall bring your burnt offerings, your sacrifices, your tithes, the contributions of your hand, your vows, your freewill offerings, and the firstborn of your herds and your flocks.

[7] There you shall eat before Yahweh your *Elohim*, and you shall rejoice — you and your households — in all the undertaking of your hands, in which Yahweh your *Elohim* has blessed you.

[8] You shall not do according to all that we are doing here today — each one doing whatever is right in his own eyes.

[9] For you have not yet come to the rest and the inheritance that Yahweh your *Elohim* is giving you.

[10] But when you cross the Jordan and dwell in the land that Yahweh your *Elohim* is giving you to inherit, and he gives you rest from all your enemies around you, so that you dwell in safety,

[11] then to the place that Yahweh your *Elohim* will choose to make his name dwell there, there you shall bring all that I command you: your burnt offerings, your sacrifices, your tithes, the contributions of your hand, and all the choicest of your vows that you vow to Yahweh.

[12] You shall rejoice before Yahweh your *Elohim* — you and your sons and your daughters, your male servants and female servants, and the Levite who is within your gates, since he has no portion or inheritance with you.

[13] Take care, lest you offer your burnt offerings in any place that you happen to see.

[14] But at the place that Yahweh will choose within one of your tribes — there you shall offer your burnt offerings, and there you shall do all that I command you.

[15] However, you may slaughter and eat meat in any of your gates, according to all the desire of your soul, according to the blessing of Yahweh your *Elohim* that he has given you. The unclean and the clean may eat of it, as of the gazelle and the deer.

[16] Only you shall not eat the blood; you shall pour it out on the earth like water.

[17] You are not allowed to eat within your gates the tithe of your grain or of your new wine or of your oil, or the firstborn of your herd or of your flock, or any of your vows that you vow, or your freewill offerings, or the contribution of your hand;

[18] but before Yahweh your *Elohim* you shall eat them, in the place that Yahweh your *Elohim* will choose — you and your son and your daughter, your male servant and your female servant, and the Levite who is within your gates. You shall rejoice before Yahweh your *Elohim* in all the undertaking of your hands.

[19] Take care, lest you forsake the Levite as long as you live in your land.

[20] When Yahweh your *Elohim* enlarges your border, as he has promised you, and you say, 'I will eat meat,' because your soul longs to eat meat, you may eat meat whenever your soul desires.

[21] If the place that Yahweh your *Elohim* will choose to put his name there is too far from you, then you may slaughter from your herd or from your flock, which Yahweh has given you, as I have commanded you; and you may eat within your gates whenever your soul desires.

[22] As the gazelle and the deer are eaten, so you shall eat of it; the unclean and the clean alike may eat of it.

[23] Only be sure that you do not eat the blood, for the blood is the life, and you shall not eat the life with the flesh.

[24] You shall not eat it; you shall pour it out on the earth like water.

[25] You shall not eat it, that it may go well with you and with your children after you, when you do what is right in the eyes of Yahweh.

[26] But your holy things that you have, and your votive offerings, you shall take and go to the place that Yahweh will choose.

[27] You shall offer your burnt offerings — the flesh and the blood — on the altar of Yahweh your *Elohim*; and the blood of your sacrifices shall be poured out on the altar of Yahweh your *Elohim*, and you shall eat the flesh.

[28] Be careful to listen to all these words that I am commanding you, that it may go well with you and with your children after you forever, when you do what is good and right in the eyes of Yahweh your *Elohim*.

[29] When Yahweh your *Elohim* cuts off before you the nations that you are entering to dispossess, and you dispossess them and dwell in their land,

[30] take care not to be ensnared into following them, after they have been destroyed before you, and not to inquire about their gods, saying, 'How did these nations serve their gods? I will do the same.'

[31] You shall not worship Yahweh your *Elohim* in that manner, for every abomination to Yahweh that he hates they have done for their gods; for they even burn their sons and their daughters in the fire to their gods.

[32] Every thing that I command you, you shall be careful to do; you shall not add to it or take away from it.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"heave offering of your hand" → "contribution of your hand"** (v6, 11, 17) — *terumah*.
- **"roebuck / hart" → "gazelle / deer"** (v15, 22) — Hebrew צְבִי *tzvi* / אַיָּל *ayyal*. Modernized.
- **"lusteth after" → "desires"** (v15, 20, 21) — Hebrew *ta'avah*; "lust" in 1611 = strong desire, not specifically sexual.
- **"the blood is the life"** (v23) — Hebrew *ha-dam hu ha-nefesh*. Key theological statement for dietary laws; Lev 17:11 parallel.
- **"corn / wine / oil" → "grain / new wine / oil"** (v17).
- **"menservants / maidservants" → "male servants / female servants"** — modernized.

### Notes
- **The centralization law** — Ch 12 is foundational: only one sanctuary where sacrificial worship may occur. Contrast with the pre-monarchic period's multiple altars (Josh 8:30, Judg 6:24). This "one place" is ultimately Jerusalem.
- **Dating debate** — many scholars see ch 12 as reflecting Josiah's centralizing reform (2 Kgs 22–23) ~622 BC, when a "Book of the Torah" was found and Deuteronomic theology was enacted. Traditionalists maintain Mosaic origin.
- **Secular slaughter permitted** (v15–16, 20–25) — innovation: animals may be slaughtered for food anywhere, without cultic setting, provided the blood is poured out. Previously (Lev 17:3–4) all slaughter had to be at the sanctuary.
- **Blood = life** — repeated prohibition on consuming blood, a core dietary law. Acts 15:20, 29 extends to Gentile believers.
- **Child sacrifice** (v31) — Molech-type offerings, the paradigm of Canaanite-religious abomination.
- **"Whatever is right in his own eyes"** (v8) — anticipates Judg 17:6, 21:25 ("every man did what was right in his own eyes") — the chaos Deut seeks to prevent.
