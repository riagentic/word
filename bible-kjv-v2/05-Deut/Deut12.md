# Deuteronomy 12

[1] "These are the statutes and the ordinances that you shall keep to do in the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of your fathers has given you to possess it, all the days that you are living on the ground.

[2] You shall surely do away with all the places where the nations that you are dispossessing served their gods, on the high mountains and on the hills and under every luxuriant tree.

[3] And you shall break down their altars and break in pieces their pillars, and their Asherim you shall burn with fire, and the carved images of their gods you shall hew down, and you shall do away with their name from that place.

[4] You shall not do so to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[5] But to the place that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will choose out of all your tribes to put his name there — his abode you shall seek, and there you shall come.

[6] And you shall bring there your burnt offerings and your sacrifices and your tithes and the contribution of your hand and your vows and your freewill offerings and the firstborn of your herd and of your flock.

[7] And you shall eat there before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and you shall rejoice in all that you stretch out your hand to, you and your houses, in which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has blessed you.

[8] You shall not do according to all that we are doing here today, each man all that is right in his eyes.

[9] For you have not come until now to the resting place and to the inheritance that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you.

[10] And you shall pass over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ and dwell in the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is making you inherit, and he will give you rest from all your enemies all around, and you shall dwell in security.

[11] And it shall be that the place that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will choose, to make his name abide there, there you shall bring all that I am commanding you: your burnt offerings and your sacrifices, your tithes and the contribution of your hand, and all the choice of your vows that you vow to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] And you shall rejoice before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you and your sons and your daughters and your slaves and your slave women, and the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧ who is in your gates, for he has no share and inheritance with you.

[13] Take heed to yourself, lest you offer up your burnt offerings in every place that you see.

[14] But in the place that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will choose in one of your tribes, there you shall offer up your burnt offerings, and there you shall do all that I am commanding you.

[15] Only, with all the desire of your soul you may slaughter and eat flesh, according to the blessing of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that he has given you, in all your gates; the unclean and the clean may eat it, as the gazelle and as the deer.

[16] Only the blood you shall not eat; on the earth you shall pour it out like water.

[17] You cannot eat in your gates the tithe of your grain and of your new wine and of your fresh oil, and the firstborn of your herd and of your flock, and all your vows that you vow, and your freewill offerings, and the contribution of your hand.

[18] But before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ you shall eat it, in the place that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will choose, you and your son and your daughter and your slave and your slave woman, and the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧ who is in your gates; and you shall rejoice before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in all that you stretch out your hand to.

[19] Take heed to yourself, lest you forsake the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧ all your days on your ground.

[20] When ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ enlarges your border, as he has spoken to you, and you say, 'Let me eat flesh,' because your soul desires to eat flesh, with all the desire of your soul you may eat flesh.

[21] If the place that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will choose to put his name there is far from you, then you may slaughter of your herd and of your flock that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has given you, as I have commanded you, and you may eat in your gates with all the desire of your soul.

[22] Only, as the gazelle and the deer are eaten, so you shall eat it; the unclean and the clean together may eat it.

[23] Only be strong not to eat the blood, for the blood is the soul, and you shall not eat the soul with the flesh.

[24] You shall not eat it; on the earth you shall pour it out like water.

[25] You shall not eat it, so that it may go well with you and with your sons after you, when you do what is right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[26] Only your holy things that you will have, and your vows, you shall take up and come to the place that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will choose.

[27] And you shall offer your burnt offerings, the flesh and the blood, on the altar of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and the blood of your sacrifices shall be poured out on the altar of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and the flesh you shall eat.

[28] Keep and hear all these words that I am commanding you, so that it may go well with you and with your sons after you forever, when you do what is good and what is right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[29] When ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ cuts off from before you the nations to whom you are going in to dispossess them, and you dispossess them and dwell in their land,

[30] take heed to yourself, lest you be snared after them, after they have been destroyed from before you, and lest you seek their gods, saying, 'How do these nations serve their gods? And I will do so, I also.'

[31] You shall not do so to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for every abomination to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that he hates they have done to their gods; for even their sons and their daughters they burn with fire to their gods.

[32] The whole word that I am commanding you, it you shall keep to do; you shall not add to it and you shall not diminish from it.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"statutes and judgments, which ye shall observe to do" → "the statutes and the ordinances that you shall keep to do"** (v1, 32) — חֻקִּים "statutes"; מִשְׁפָּטִים is the word "judgments", of a rule "ordinances" (as Lev 18:4; 26:46); שָׁמַר לַעֲשׂוֹת is "keep to do": שָׁמַר stays "keep" ("observe", "be careful" are not in the Hebrew).
- **"giveth thee" → "has given you"** (v1) — נָתַן is a perfect. "on the ground": הָאֲדָמָה, not אֶרֶץ "land / earth".
- **"utterly destroy" → "surely do away with"** (v2) — אַבֵּד תְּאַבְּדוּן, the infinitive absolute with its verb (lit. "doing away you shall do away"); אִבֵּד is "make perish", the same verb as "do away with their name" in v3 (KJV "destroy").
- **"the nations which ye shall possess" → "the nations that you are dispossessing"** (v2, 29) — יָרַשׁ "possess" with persons as its object: to take their place and what was theirs. English needs "dispossess"; the Hebrew verb is the one of "to possess it" in v1.
- **"green tree" → "luxuriant tree"** (v2) — רַעֲנָן is "fresh, full of leaves and sap", not the colour word.
- **"overthrow … break … groves … graven images" → "break down … break in pieces … Asherim … carved images … hew down"** (v3) — נָתַץ, שִׁבֵּר, אֲשֵׁרִים (the word kept: wooden objects of worship that bear the goddess's name), פְּסִילִים (root "hew, carve"), גִּדַּע. "their name" is singular in the Hebrew (KJV "the names of them").
- **"Ye shall not do so unto" → "You shall not do so to"** (v4, 31) — kept word for word: לֹא־תַעֲשׂוּן כֵּן לַיהוָה. "Worship in that manner" is an explanation, not in the Hebrew.
- **"the place which the LORD your God shall choose" → "the place that Yahweh your Elohim will choose"** (v5, 11, 14, 18, 21, 26) — הַמָּקוֹם אֲשֶׁר יִבְחַר יְהוָה, one wording each time; no place is named. "to put his name there" (v5, 21) is לָשׂוּם; "to make his name abide there" (v11) is לְשַׁכֵּן, the verb of שָׁכַן "abide" and of מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling".
- **"unto his habitation shall ye seek" → "his abode you shall seek"** (v5) — לְשִׁכְנוֹ תִדְרְשׁוּ, lit. "to his abode you shall seek"; the noun שֶׁכֶן is found only here (root שָׁכַן "abide"). Many repoint it as a verb, "to make it abide" (as v11); the Masoretic pointing and accents are kept. "and there you shall come" is singular in the Hebrew after plural verbs; Deuteronomy changes between "you" plural and "you" singular all through this chapter (v1, 5, 7, 9, 16, 32).
- **"heave offerings of your hand" → "the contribution of your hand"** (v6, 11, 17) — תְּרוּמַת יֶדְכֶם, singular: what is "lifted off" and set aside.
- **"all that ye put your hand unto, ye and your households" → "all that you stretch out your hand to, you and your houses"** (v7, 18) — מִשְׁלַח יֶדְכֶם, lit. "the stretching out of your hand"; בָּתֵּיכֶם "your houses".
- **"whatsoever is right in his own eyes" → "all that is right in his eyes"** (v8, 25, 28) — הַיָּשָׁר, as Exod 15:26.
- **"the rest" → "the resting place"** (v9) — מְנוּחָה, as Num 10:33.
- **"But when ye go over Jordan … so that ye dwell in safety; then there shall be a place" → "And you shall pass over … and you shall dwell in security. And it shall be that the place"** (v10–11) — a chain of "and" clauses, not turned into "when … then"; מַנְחִיל "is making inherit"; בֶּטַח "security".
- **"your choice vows" → "the choice of your vows"** (v11) — מִבְחַר נִדְרֵיכֶם.
- **"menservants / maidservants … no part" → "slaves / slave women … no share"** (v12, 18) — עֶבֶד, אָמָה, חֵלֶק.
- **"Take heed to thyself that thou offer not" → "Take heed to yourself, lest you offer up"** (v13, 19, 30) — הִשָּׁמֶר לְךָ פֶּן, lit. "keep yourself, lest"; הֶעֱלָה עֹלָה, lit. "make go up a going-up".
- **"thou mayest kill and eat flesh … whatsoever thy soul lusteth after" → "with all the desire of your soul you may slaughter and eat flesh"** (v15, 20, 21) — בְּכָל־אַוַּת נַפְשְׁךָ; נֶפֶשׁ is "soul". The verb is זָבַח, elsewhere "sacrifice" (as "your sacrifices" in v6, 11, 27): here the killing is for food, away from the altar, and the unclean eat of it, so "sacrifice" would say the opposite of the law; "slaughter" is used and the word named. "may": the Hebrew imperfect is both "shall" and "may" (v15, 20–22).
- **"roebuck / hart" → "gazelle / deer"** (v15, 22) — צְבִי, אַיָּל: game, eaten without an altar.
- **"Thou mayest not eat" → "You cannot eat"** (v17) — לֹא־תוּכַל, lit. "you are not able": it is not allowed.
- **"corn / wine / oil" → "grain / new wine / fresh oil"** (v17) — דָּגָן, תִּירֹשׁ, יִצְהָר.
- **"as he hath promised thee … I will eat flesh … longeth" → "as he has spoken to you … Let me eat flesh … desires"** (v20) — דִּבֶּר "speak"; אֹכְלָה is a wish form; תְאַוֶּה is the verb of אַוָּה "desire" (one root with תַּאֲוָה "craving", here said without blame).
- **"too far from thee" → "far from you"** (v21) — יִרְחַק מִמְּךָ; "too" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Only"** (v15, 16, 22, 23, 26) — רַק; in v22 the word is אַךְ.
- **"be sure that thou eat not the blood: for the blood is the life" → "be strong not to eat the blood, for the blood is the soul"** (v23) — חֲזַק "be strong"; הַדָּם הוּא הַנָּפֶשׁ, with נֶפֶשׁ "soul" as in Lev 17:11, 14 ("the soul of the flesh is in the blood").
- **"that it may go well with thee, and with thy children" → "so that it may go well with you and with your sons"** (v25, 28) — יִיטַב לְךָ, lit. "it may be good for you"; בָּנֶיךָ "your sons".
- **"thy holy things which thou hast … thou shalt take" → "your holy things that you will have … you shall take up"** (v26) — יִהְיוּ is an imperfect; נָשָׂא "lift, carry".
- **"offer"** (v27) — עָשָׂה, lit. "make / do", of preparing an offering (as Num 28:6).
- **"Observe and hear" → "Keep and hear"** (v28) — שְׁמֹר וְשָׁמַעְתָּ.
- **"that thou be not snared by following them … enquire not after their gods" → "lest you be snared after them … lest you seek their gods"** (v30) — תִּנָּקֵשׁ אַחֲרֵיהֶם; דָּרַשׁ לְ "seek", the verb of v5. "How do these nations serve": the imperfect may also be "how did they use to serve".
- **"every abomination to the LORD"** (v31) — תּוֹעֲבַת יְהוָה, lit. "abomination of Yahweh". "burn with fire": שָׂרַף בָּאֵשׁ, the plain words.
- **"What thing soever I command you" → "The whole word that I am commanding you"** (v32) — כָּל־הַדָּבָר; גָּרַע "diminish".

### Notes
- **Verse numbers** — our 12:32 is 13:1 in the Hebrew Bible; from there the Hebrew numbers of chapter 13 are one higher than ours.
- **The centralization law** — Ch 12 is foundational: only one sanctuary where sacrificial worship may occur. Contrast with the pre-monarchic period's multiple altars (Josh 8:30, Judg 6:24). The text does not name the place; later books apply it to Jerusalem.
- **Dating debate** — many scholars see ch 12 as reflecting Josiah's centralizing reform (2 Kgs 22–23) ~622 BC, when a "Book of the Torah" was found and Deuteronomic theology was enacted. Traditionalists maintain Mosaic origin.
- **Slaughter away from the altar permitted** (v15–16, 20–25) — animals may be slaughtered for food anywhere, without cultic setting, provided the blood is poured out. Compare Lev 17:3–4, where slaughter is brought to the tent of meeting.
- **Blood = soul** — repeated prohibition on consuming blood, a core dietary law. Acts 15:20, 29 extends to Gentile believers.
- **Child sacrifice** (v31) — the burning of sons and daughters to the gods, the paradigm of what Yahweh hates in the worship of the nations (compare Lev 18:21; 20:2).
- **"All that is right in his eyes"** (v8) — anticipates Judg 17:6, 21:25 ("every man did what was right in his own eyes") — the chaos Deut seeks to prevent.
