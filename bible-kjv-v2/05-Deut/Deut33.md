# Deuteronomy 33

[1] This is the blessing with which Moses the man of *Elohim* blessed the children of Israel before his death.

[2] He said:

"Yahweh came from Sinai;
he dawned over them from Seir;
he shone forth from Mount Paran;
he came from ten thousands of holy ones,
with flashing lightning at his right hand for them.

[3] Indeed, he loves the peoples;
all his holy ones are in your hand;
they have set themselves at your feet;
each receives your words.

[4] Moses commanded us a Torah,
a possession of the assembly of Jacob.

[5] He was king in Jeshurun,
when the heads of the people were gathered,
the tribes of Israel together."

[6] "Let Reuben live, and not die,
though his men be few."

[7] And this is of Judah; he said:

"Hear, O Yahweh, the voice of Judah,
and bring him to his people.
With his own hands he strives for himself;
be a help against his adversaries."

[8] Of Levi he said:

"Give your Thummim to Levi,
your Urim to your faithful one,
whom you tested at Massah,
with whom you strove at the waters of Meribah.

[9] Who said of his father and mother, 'I regard them not';
he disowned his brothers
and did not know his own children.
For they observed your word
and kept your covenant.

[10] They shall teach your ordinances to Jacob,
your Torah to Israel;
they shall put incense before you,
and whole burnt offerings on your altar.

[11] Bless, O Yahweh, his strength,
and accept the work of his hands.
Crush the loins of those who rise against him,
his haters, so they rise no more."

[12] Of Benjamin he said:

"The beloved of Yahweh dwells in safety beside him;
he shelters him all the day long,
and he dwells between his shoulders."

[13] Of Joseph he said:

"Blessed of Yahweh be his land —
with the choicest from the heavens above, with the dew,
and with the deep that couches beneath;

[14] with the choicest yield of the sun,
and with the choicest produce of the moons;

[15] with the finest of the ancient mountains,
and the abundance of the everlasting hills;

[16] with the choicest of the earth and its fullness,
and the favor of him who dwelt in the bush.
May these come upon the head of Joseph,
upon the brow of him who is set apart from his brothers.

[17] His firstborn bull — majesty is his;
his horns are the horns of a wild ox;
with them he gores the peoples,
all of them, to the ends of the earth.
These are the ten thousands of Ephraim,
these the thousands of Manasseh."

[18] Of Zebulun he said:

"Rejoice, Zebulun, in your going out,
and Issachar, in your tents.

[19] They shall call peoples to their mountain;
there they shall offer righteous sacrifices;
for they shall suck the abundance of the seas
and the hidden treasures of the sand."

[20] Of Gad he said:

"Blessed is he who enlarges Gad!
He crouches like a lioness,
tearing the arm, even the top of the head.

[21] He chose the best for himself —
for there a commander's portion was reserved;
he came with the heads of the people,
executing Yahweh's righteousness
and his ordinances with Israel."

[22] Of Dan he said:

"Dan is a lion's cub
leaping forth from Bashan."

[23] Of Naphtali he said:

"O Naphtali, satisfied with favor,
full of the blessing of Yahweh —
possess the sea and the south."

[24] Of Asher he said:

"Most blessed of sons be Asher;
let him be favored by his brothers,
and let him dip his foot in oil.

[25] Your bars shall be iron and bronze;
and as your days, so shall your strength be.

[26] There is none like the *Elohim* of Jeshurun,
who rides through the heavens to your help,
through the skies in his majesty.

[27] The ancient *Elohim* is a dwelling,
and underneath are the everlasting arms.
He drove out the enemy before you
and said, 'Destroy!'

[28] So Israel lives in safety, alone;
the spring of Jacob is in a land of grain and new wine;
his heavens drop down dew.

[29] Happy are you, O Israel! Who is like you,
a people saved by Yahweh,
the shield of your help,
and the sword of your majesty.
Your enemies shall come fawning to you,
and you shall tread upon their heights."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"law" → "Torah"** (v4, 10) — Hebrew תּוֹרָה *torah*; Mosaic instruction.
- **"saints" → "holy ones"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew קְדֹשִׁים *qedoshim*. Heavenly beings / angels in context.
- **"fiery law" → "flashing lightning"** (v2) — Hebrew אֵשׁ דָּת *esh dat*. Obscure; "fire of law" (KJV), or "flashing" / "lightning" / something about fire going-forth. "Lightning" matches theophanic imagery.
- **"unicorns" → "wild ox"** (v17) — *re'em*.
- **"eternal God / everlasting arms" → "ancient *Elohim* / everlasting arms"** (v27) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי קֶדֶם *Elohei qedem*. "Ancient" = primordial.
- **"corn / wine" → "grain / new wine"** (v28).
- **"found liars" → "come fawning"** (v29) — Hebrew וְיִכָּחֲשׁוּ אֹיְבֶיךָ *ve-yikkachashu oyvekha*. "Cringe / fawn / deny" — submit in fear.

### Notes
- **Moses' blessing** — parallels Jacob's blessing (Gen 49). Tribe-by-tribe prophetic blessings before his death.
- **Opening theophany** (v2–5) — Yahweh came from Sinai / Seir / Paran. The three holy mountains — traditional southern wilderness-theophany sites. Hab 3:3 echoes.
- **Simeon omitted** — notable. Gen 49 blessed Simeon (with Levi) harshly; perhaps Simeon is assumed absorbed into Judah (Josh 19:1–9: Simeon's territory within Judah's).
- **Levi's rehabilitation** (v8–11) — in Jacob's blessing, Levi was cursed with scattering. Here, Levi is praised for *Thummim/Urim* oracle and zealous covenant-keeping. The reversal is mediated by the Sinai golden-calf event (Ex 32:26–29) and Phinehas (Num 25).
- **"Between his shoulders"** (v12) — Jerusalem sits in Benjamin's territory (barely), and the temple mount nestles between two ridges ("shoulders") of that region.
- **Joseph as bull with wild-ox horns** (v17) — military-strength imagery. Ephraim and Manasseh as the powerful northern tribes.
- **"Ancient *Elohim* is a dwelling" / "everlasting arms"** (v27) — famously hymnal: "Leaning on the Everlasting Arms." The *Elohim* of primordial time is the refuge.
