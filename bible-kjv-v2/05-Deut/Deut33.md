# Deuteronomy 33

[1] And this is the blessing with which ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, blessed the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ before his death.

[2] And he said:

"⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ came from Sinai,
and dawned from Seir for them;
he shone forth from Mount Paran,
and he came from holy ten thousands,
from his right hand a fire of law for them.

[3] Yes, he loves the peoples;
all his holy ones are in your hand,
and they, they sat down at your foot;
he takes up of your words.

[4] A ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ commanded us,
a possession, the assembly of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧.

[5] And there was a king in ⟦jeshurun|Yeshurun|Jeshurun⟧,
when the heads of the people gathered themselves,
together the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[6] "Let ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧ live and not die,
and let his men be few."

[7] And this for ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and he said:

"Hear, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, the voice of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,
and to his people bring him;
with his hands he contended for himself,
and a helper from his adversaries you shall be."

[8] And of Levi he said:

"Your ⟦thummim|Tummim|Thummim⟧ and your Urim are for your faithful man,
whom you tested at Massah,
with whom you contended at the waters of ⟦meribah|Merivah|Meribah⟧;

[9] who says of his father and of his mother, 'I have not seen him,'
and his brothers he did not recognize,
and his sons he did not know;
for they kept your saying,
and your covenant they guard.

[10] They shall teach your ordinances to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and your ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
they shall put incense in your nose,
and a whole offering on your altar.

[11] Bless, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, his ability,
and with the work of his hands be pleased;
strike through the loins of those who rise against him,
and of those who hate him, that they rise not."

[12] Of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ he said:

"The beloved of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ abides in security by him;
he covers over him all the day,
and between his shoulders he abides."

[13] And of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ he said:

"Blessed of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is his land,
from the choice things of the heavens, from the dew,
and from the deep lying beneath,

[14] and from the choice things of the yields of the sun,
and from the choice things of what the moons put forth,

[15] and from the top of the mountains of old,
and from the choice things of the everlasting hills,

[16] and from the choice things of the earth and its fullness,
and the good pleasure of him who abides in the bush.
Let it come on the head of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧,
and on the crown of the head of the one set apart from his brothers.

[17] The firstborn of his ox, splendor is his,
and the horns of a wild ox are his horns;
with them he shall gore peoples,
together, the ends of the earth.
And they are the ten thousands of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧,
and they are the thousands of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧."

[18] And of ⟦zebulun|Zevulun|Zebulun⟧ he said:

"Be glad, ⟦zebulun|Zevulun|Zebulun⟧, in your going out,
and ⟦issachar|Yissachar|Issachar⟧, in your tents.

[19] Peoples they shall call to the mountain;
there they shall sacrifice sacrifices of righteousness;
for the abundance of the seas they shall suck,
and the covered things hidden in the sand."

[20] And of Gad he said:

"Blessed is he who enlarges Gad!
As a lioness he abides,
and he tears to pieces the arm, yes, the crown of the head.

[21] And he saw the first for himself,
for there the portion of a commander was covered;
and he came, the heads of the people;
the righteousness of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ he did,
and his judgments with ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[22] And of Dan he said:

"Dan is a lion's cub;
he leaps from the Bashan."

[23] And of ⟦naphtali|Naftali|Naphtali⟧ he said:

"⟦naphtali|Naftali|Naphtali⟧, sated with good pleasure,
and full of the blessing of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
west and south possess!"

[24] And of Asher he said:

"Blessed above sons is Asher;
let him be pleasing to his brothers,
and dipping his foot in oil.

[25] Iron and bronze are your bolts,
and as your days, your strength.

[26] There is none like ⟦el|El|God⟧, ⟦jeshurun|Yeshurun|Jeshurun⟧,
rider of the heavens as your help,
and in his exaltation, of the skies.

[27] An abode is the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of old,
and beneath are the everlasting arms;
and he drove out the enemy from before you,
and he said, 'Destroy!'

[28] And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ abode in security, alone, the spring of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
to a land of grain and new wine;
yes, his heavens drip dew.

[29] Happy are you, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧! Who is like you,
a people saved by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
the shield of your help,
and who is the sword of your exaltation!
And your enemies shall cringe before you,
and you, on their high places you shall tread."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 27) — Hebrew *Elohim*. **"the God of Jeshurun" → "El, Jeshurun"** (v26) — Hebrew אֵל *El*.
- **The blessing as poetry** (v2–29) — one Hebrew line (colon) to a printed line, in the Hebrew order. The blessing is old poetry with many rare words; where a word is found only here or its sense is not known, Changes says so and the verse keeps the best-supported rendering. "O" before a name is not in the Hebrew and is not added.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"rose up from Seir unto them … he came with ten thousands of saints: from his right hand went a fiery law for them" → "dawned from Seir for them … he came from holy ten thousands, from his right hand a fire of law for them"** (v2) — Hebrew וְזָרַח, of the rising sun; וְאָתָה מֵרִבְבֹת קֹדֶשׁ, literally "and he came from ten thousands of holiness" (מִן is "from", not "with"; the Greek has "with ten thousands of Kadesh", cited from memory). The last line is uncertain. The written text (ketiv) has one word, אשדת, whose meaning is not known; the reading tradition (qere) divides it, אֵשׁ דָּת "a fire of law", and that is translated. דָּת "law, decree" is a Persian word found otherwise only in Esther, Ezra and Daniel, which is why the reading is doubted; "flashing lightning", "slopes" and other proposals are conjectures. לָמוֹ is "for them" (or "for him").
- **"Yea, he loved the people; all his saints are in thy hand: and they sat down at thy feet; every one shall receive of thy words" → "Yes, he loves the peoples; all his holy ones are in your hand, and they, they sat down at your foot; he takes up of your words"** (v3) — a verse of uncertain sense. חֹבֵב "love" is found only here; עַמִּים is a plural, "peoples"; the person changes from "his" to "your" in the Hebrew. תֻּכּוּ is found only here and its meaning is not known ("sat down", "bowed down", "followed"); יִשָּׂא is a singular, "he lifts up, takes up"; מִדַּבְּרֹתֶיךָ, a form found only here, "of your words".
- **"Moses commanded us a law, even the inheritance of the congregation of Jacob" → "A Torah Moses commanded us, a possession, the assembly of Jacob"** (v4) — Hebrew תּוֹרָה; מוֹרָשָׁה "possession" (Exod 6:8) stands beside קְהִלַּת יַעֲקֹב without a joining word: "a possession for the assembly of Jacob" or "a possession, O assembly of Jacob".
- **"And he was king in Jeshurun" → "And there was a king in Jeshurun"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיְהִי בִישֻׁרוּן מֶלֶךְ; also "and he became king": the Hebrew does not name who (Yahweh, as most take it, or Moses). יְשֻׁרוּן as in 32:15. **"were gathered together" → "gathered themselves"** — Hebrew בְּהִתְאַסֵּף.
- **"and let not his men be few" → "and let his men be few"** (v6) — Hebrew וִיהִי מְתָיו מִסְפָּר, literally "and let his men be a number": a number that can be counted, that is, few (4:27; Gen 34:30). KJV carries the "not" of the first line over; the Hebrew has no second "not", and whether the line limits the wish ("but let his men be few") or the "not" is meant to carry on is left open.
- **"And this is the blessing of Judah" → "And this for Judah"** (v7) — Hebrew וְזֹאת לִיהוּדָה. **"let his hands be sufficient for him; and be thou an help to him from his enemies" → "with his hands he contended for himself, and a helper from his adversaries you shall be"** — Hebrew יָדָיו רָב לוֹ; רָב is pointed as a verb, "contended" (the word of v8), and may also be read "enough" ("his hands are enough for him"); uncertain. עֵזֶר; צָרָיו.
- **"Let thy Thummim and thy Urim be with thy holy one, whom thou didst prove at Massah, and with whom thou didst strive" → "Your Thummim and your Urim are for your faithful man, whom you tested at Massah, with whom you contended"** (v8) — Hebrew תֻּמֶּיךָ וְאוּרֶיךָ לְאִישׁ חֲסִידֶךָ, a sentence without a verb, literally "… for the man of your faithful one"; חָסִיד is one who keeps *chesed*. The Greek begins "Give to Levi" (cited from memory); the Masoretic Text has no such words. נִסִּיתוֹ בְּמַסָּה and תְּרִיבֵהוּ … מְרִיבָה are plays on the names ("testing", "contention").
- **"Who said unto his father and to his mother, I have not seen him; neither did he acknowledge his brethren, nor knew his own children: for they have observed thy word, and kept thy covenant" → "who says of his father and of his mother, 'I have not seen him,' and his brothers he did not recognize, and his sons he did not know; for they kept your saying, and your covenant they guard"** (v9) — Hebrew הָאֹמֵר, a participle; הִכִּיר; the written text has "his son" (בנו), the reading tradition "his sons" (בָּנָיו), which is translated; אִמְרָתֶךָ (32:2); יִנְצֹרוּ.
- **"They shall teach Jacob thy judgments, and Israel thy law: they shall put incense before thee, and whole burnt sacrifice" → "They shall teach your ordinances to Jacob, and your Torah to Israel; they shall put incense in your nose, and a whole offering"** (v10) — Hebrew יוֹרוּ, the verb of the root of תּוֹרָה; מִשְׁפָּטֶיךָ "judgments, rulings"; קְטוֹרָה בְּאַפֶּךָ, literally "smoke of sacrifice in your nose"; כָּלִיל "whole" ("offering" is supplied).
- **"Bless, LORD, his substance, and accept the work of his hands: smite through the loins of them that rise against him, and of them that hate him, that they rise not again" → "Bless, Yahweh, his ability, and with the work of his hands be pleased; strike through the loins of those who rise against him, and of those who hate him, that they rise not"** (v11) — Hebrew חֵילוֹ: ability, strength, also wealth; תִּרְצֶה; מְחַץ; מִן־יְקוּמוּן, literally "from their rising".
- **"The beloved of the LORD shall dwell in safety by him; and the LORD shall cover him all the day long, and he shall dwell between his shoulders" → "The beloved of Yahweh abides in security by him; he covers over him all the day, and between his shoulders he abides"** (v12) — Hebrew יִשְׁכֹּן, שָׁכֵן "abide"; לָבֶטַח; עָלָיו "upon him, by him"; חֹפֵף is found only here: "cover, shelter"; uncertain. The Hebrew does not say who covers whom or whose shoulders are meant ("the LORD" in KJV's second line is supplied).
- **"for the precious things of heaven, for the dew, and for the deep that coucheth beneath" → "from the choice things of the heavens, from the dew, and from the deep lying beneath"** (v13) — Hebrew מִמֶּגֶד "from the choice gift of", five times in v13–16; מִטָּל "from the dew" is the Masoretic Text (Gen 49:25 has מֵעָל "above"); תְּהוֹם רֹבֶצֶת תָּחַת as Gen 49:25.
- **"the precious fruits brought forth by the sun, and for the precious things put forth by the moon" → "the choice things of the yields of the sun, and from the choice things of what the moons put forth"** (v14) — Hebrew תְּבוּאֹת; גֶּרֶשׁ is found only here ("what is thrust forth"); יְרָחִים "moons, months".
- **"the chief things of the ancient mountains, and for the precious things of the lasting hills" → "from the top of the mountains of old, and from the choice things of the everlasting hills"** (v15) — Hebrew מֵרֹאשׁ, literally "from the head"; קֶדֶם; גִּבְעוֹת עוֹלָם (Gen 49:26).
- **"the good will of him that dwelt in the bush: let the blessing come upon the head of Joseph, and upon the top of the head of him that was separated from his brethren" → "the good pleasure of him who abides in the bush. Let it come on the head of Joseph, and on the crown of the head of the one set apart from his brothers"** (v16) — Hebrew רְצוֹן, of the root "be pleased" (v11, 23, 24); שֹׁכְנִי סְנֶה, a participle; the bush is that of Exod 3:2. "The blessing" is not in the Hebrew. נְזִיר אֶחָיו as Gen 49:26.
- **"His glory is like the firstling of his bullock, and his horns are like the horns of unicorns: with them he shall push the people together to the ends of the earth" → "The firstborn of his ox, splendor is his, and the horns of a wild ox are his horns; with them he shall gore peoples, together, the ends of the earth"** (v17) — Hebrew בְּכוֹר שׁוֹרוֹ הָדָר לוֹ; רְאֵם; יְנַגַּח; no "like" and no "to" in the Hebrew.
- **"Rejoice" → "Be glad"** (v18) — Hebrew שְׂמַח.
- **"They shall call the people unto the mountain; there they shall offer sacrifices of righteousness: for they shall suck of the abundance of the seas, and of treasures hid in the sand" → "Peoples they shall call to the mountain; there they shall sacrifice sacrifices of righteousness; for the abundance of the seas they shall suck, and the covered things hidden in the sand"** (v19) — Hebrew עַמִּים הַר־יִקְרָאוּ ("peoples" may be the object or the subject: "peoples shall call"); שְׂפוּנֵי טְמוּנֵי חוֹל, two words for what is hidden; שְׂפוּנֵי is found only here.
- **"he dwelleth as a lion, and teareth the arm with the crown of the head" → "As a lioness he abides, and he tears to pieces the arm, yes, the crown of the head"** (v20) — Hebrew לָבִיא; שָׁכֵן; טָרַף.
- **"And he provided the first part for himself, because there, in a portion of the lawgiver, was he seated; and he came with the heads of the people, he executed the justice of the LORD, and his judgments with Israel" → "And he saw the first for himself, for there the portion of a commander was covered; and he came, the heads of the people; the righteousness of Yahweh he did, and his judgments with Israel"** (v21) — a verse of uncertain sense. וַיַּרְא "saw", that is, looked out; רֵאשִׁית "the first (part)"; חֶלְקַת מְחֹקֵק "the plot of a commander" (or "of a lawgiver"); סָפוּן "covered, panelled", perhaps "kept in store"; וַיֵּתֵא רָאשֵׁי עָם has no "with" in the Hebrew.
- **"Dan is a lion's whelp: he shall leap from Bashan" → "Dan is a lion's cub; he leaps from the Bashan"** (v22) — Hebrew גּוּר אַרְיֵה (Gen 49:9); יְזַנֵּק is found only here; הַבָּשָׁן has the article.
- **"O Naphtali, satisfied with favour … possess thou the west and the south" → "Naphtali, sated with good pleasure … west and south possess"** (v23) — Hebrew שְׂבַע רָצוֹן; יָם, literally "sea": the west, or the sea (of Kinnereth) itself; דָּרוֹם "south".
- **"Let Asher be blessed with children; let him be acceptable to his brethren, and let him dip his foot in oil" → "Blessed above sons is Asher; let him be pleasing to his brothers, and dipping his foot in oil"** (v24) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ מִבָּנִים, "blessed above sons" or "blessed by sons"; רְצוּי; וְטֹבֵל, a participle.
- **"Thy shoes shall be iron and brass; and as thy days, so shall thy strength be" → "Iron and bronze are your bolts, and as your days, your strength"** (v25) — both nouns are found only here and are uncertain: מִנְעָלֶיךָ "bolts, bars" (or "sandals"), דָּבְאֶךָ "strength" (so the old versions, cited from memory; others "rest").
- **"There is none like unto the God of Jeshurun, who rideth upon the heaven in thy help, and in his excellency on the sky" → "There is none like El, Jeshurun, rider of the heavens as your help, and in his exaltation, of the skies"** (v26) — as pointed, כָּאֵל is "like the El" and Jeshurun is addressed; the old versions read "like the El of Jeshurun" (cited from memory). בְעֶזְרֶךָ, literally "in your help"; גַּאֲוָה (Exod 15:7); שְׁחָקִים "skies, clouds".
- **"The eternal God is thy refuge, and underneath are the everlasting arms: and he shall thrust out the enemy from before thee; and shall say, Destroy them" → "An abode is the Elohim of old, and beneath are the everlasting arms; and he drove out the enemy from before you, and he said, 'Destroy!'"** (v27) — Hebrew מְעֹנָה "abode, den" (26:15); "thy" is not in the Hebrew; אֱלֹהֵי קֶדֶם "the Elohim of old"; the line is uncertain (also "the Elohim of old is an abode above"). The verbs are pasts. "Them" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Israel then shall dwell in safety alone: the fountain of Jacob shall be upon a land of corn and wine; also his heavens shall drop down dew" → "And Israel abode in security, alone, the spring of Jacob, to a land of grain and new wine; yes, his heavens drip dew"** (v28) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁכֹּן, a past; עֵין יַעֲקֹב "the spring (or eye) of Jacob"; אֶל "to, toward"; תִּירוֹשׁ; יַעַרְפוּ (32:2).
- **"O people saved by the LORD, the shield of thy help, and who is the sword of thy excellency! and thine enemies shall be found liars unto thee" → "a people saved by Yahweh, the shield of your help, and who is the sword of your exaltation! And your enemies shall cringe before you"** (v29) — Hebrew נוֹשַׁע בַּיהוָה, "saved in / by Yahweh"; וְיִכָּחֲשׁוּ … לָךְ, literally "shall lie to you": feign submission. בָּמוֹתֵימוֹ "their high places" (or "their backs").

### Notes
- **Moses' blessing** — parallels Jacob's blessing (Gen 49). Tribe-by-tribe blessings before his death.
- **Opening theophany** (v2–5) — Yahweh came from Sinai / Seir / Paran, the southern wilderness sites of his appearing. Hab 3:3 echoes.
- **Simeon omitted** — notable. Gen 49 spoke of Simeon (with Levi) harshly; perhaps Simeon is assumed absorbed into Judah (Josh 19:1–9: Simeon's territory within Judah's).
- **Levi's rehabilitation** (v8–11) — in Jacob's blessing, Levi was to be scattered. Here, Levi is praised for the Thummim and Urim and for covenant-keeping. The reversal is mediated by the golden-calf event (Ex 32:26–29) and Phinehas (Num 25).
- **"Between his shoulders"** (v12) — often referred to Jerusalem, on the edge of Benjamin's territory, the temple hill lying between ridges ("shoulders"); the verse itself does not say.
- **Joseph as ox with wild-ox horns** (v17) — military-strength imagery. Ephraim and Manasseh as the powerful northern tribes.
- **"The Elohim of old" / "everlasting arms"** (v27) — famously hymnal: "Leaning on the Everlasting Arms."
