# Deuteronomy 28

[1] "And it shall be, if you will surely listen to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to keep to do all his commandments that I am commanding you today, that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will set you high above all the nations of the earth.

[2] And all these blessings shall come upon you and overtake you, when you listen to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] Blessed are you in the city, and blessed are you in the field.

[4] Blessed is the fruit of your belly and the fruit of your ground and the fruit of your beasts, the offspring of your cattle and the young of your flock.

[5] Blessed is your basket and your kneading bowl.

[6] Blessed are you in your coming in, and blessed are you in your going out.

[7] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will set your enemies who rise up against you smitten before you; by one way they shall come out against you, and by seven ways they shall flee before you.

[8] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will command the blessing with you in your storehouses and in all that you stretch out your hand to; and he will bless you in the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you.

[9] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will establish you for himself as a holy people, as he swore to you, when you keep the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and walk in his ways.

[10] And all the peoples of the earth shall see that the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is called upon you, and they shall be afraid of you.

[11] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will make you abound for good in the fruit of your belly and in the fruit of your beasts and in the fruit of your ground, upon the ground that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ swore to your fathers to give you.

[12] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will open for you his good treasury, the heavens, to give the rain of your land in its time and to bless all the work of your hand; and you shall lend to many nations, and you, you shall not borrow.

[13] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will make you the head and not the tail, and you shall be only above, and you shall not be below, when you listen to the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that I am commanding you today, to keep and to do,

[14] and you do not turn aside from all the words that I am commanding you today, to the right or to the left, to go after other gods to serve them.

[15] And it shall be, if you do not listen to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to keep to do all his commandments and his statutes that I am commanding you today, that all these curses shall come upon you and overtake you.

[16] Cursed are you in the city, and cursed are you in the field.

[17] Cursed is your basket and your kneading bowl.

[18] Cursed is the fruit of your belly and the fruit of your ground, the offspring of your cattle and the young of your flock.

[19] Cursed are you in your coming in, and cursed are you in your going out.

[20] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will send against you the curse, the confusion and the rebuke, in all that you stretch out your hand to, which you do, until you are destroyed and until you perish quickly, because of the evil of your deeds, in that you have forsaken me.

[21] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will make the pestilence cling to you until he has made an end of you from upon the ground to which you are going in to possess it.

[22] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will strike you with the consumption and with the fever and with the inflammation and with the burning heat and with the sword and with the blight and with the mildew; and they shall pursue you until you perish.

[23] And your heavens that are over your head shall be bronze, and the earth that is under you iron.

[24] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will make the rain of your land fine dust and dust; from the heavens it shall come down upon you until you are destroyed.

[25] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will set you smitten before your enemies; by one way you shall go out against him, and by seven ways you shall flee before him; and you shall become a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth.

[26] And your dead body shall be food for all the birds of the heavens and for the beasts of the earth, and there shall be none who makes them tremble.

[27] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will strike you with the boils of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and with the tumors and with the itch and with the scurf, of which you cannot be healed.

[28] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will strike you with madness and with blindness and with bewilderment of heart.

[29] And you shall be feeling about at noon as the blind man feels about in the gloom, and you shall not make your ways prosper; and you shall be only extorted from and robbed all the days, and there shall be none who saves.

[30] A woman you shall betroth, and another man shall lie with her; a house you shall build, and you shall not dwell in it; a vineyard you shall plant, and you shall not put it to common use.

[31] Your ox shall be butchered before your eyes, and you shall not eat of it; your donkey shall be robbed from before your face, and it shall not return to you; your flock shall be given to your enemies, and you shall have none who saves.

[32] Your sons and your daughters shall be given to another people, and your eyes shall see and wear out toward them all the day, and there shall be nothing in the power of your hand.

[33] The fruit of your ground and all your labor a people that you have not known shall eat; and you shall be only extorted from and crushed all the days.

[34] And you shall be driven mad by the sight of your eyes that you shall see.

[35] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will strike you with evil boils on the knees and on the legs, of which you cannot be healed, from the sole of your foot and to the crown of your head.

[36] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will make you walk, you and your king whom you set up over you, to a nation that you have not known, you and your fathers; and you shall serve there other gods, wood and stone.

[37] And you shall become a thing that appalls, a proverb and a taunt among all the peoples where ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will drive you.

[38] Much seed you shall bring out to the field, and little you shall gather, for the locusts shall devour it.

[39] Vineyards you shall plant and work, and wine you shall not drink, and you shall not gather in, for the worm shall eat it.

[40] Olive trees you shall have in all your border, and with oil you shall not anoint yourself, for your olive shall drop off.

[41] Sons and daughters you shall beget, and they shall not be yours, for they shall go into captivity.

[42] All your trees and the fruit of your ground the cicada shall possess.

[43] The sojourner who is in your midst shall go up above you, higher and higher, and you, you shall go down, lower and lower.

[44] He, he shall lend to you, and you, you shall not lend to him; he, he shall be the head, and you, you shall be the tail.

[45] And all these curses shall come upon you and pursue you and overtake you until you are destroyed, for you did not listen to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to keep his commandments and his statutes that he commanded you.

[46] And they shall be on you for a sign and for a wonder, and on your seed forever.

[47] Because you did not serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ with gladness and with goodness of heart, for the abundance of all,

[48] you shall serve your enemies whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will send against you, in hunger and in thirst and in nakedness and in lack of all; and he will put a yoke of iron upon your neck until he has destroyed you.

[49] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will lift up against you a nation from afar, from the end of the earth, as the eagle swoops, a nation whose tongue you shall not understand,

[50] a nation strong of face, who will not lift up the face of an old man and will not show favor to a boy.

[51] And it shall eat the fruit of your beasts and the fruit of your ground until you are destroyed, which will not leave you grain, new wine or fresh oil, the offspring of your cattle or the young of your flock, until it has made you perish.

[52] And it shall distress you in all your gates until your high and fortified walls come down, in which you trust, in all your land; and it shall distress you in all your gates, in all your land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has given to you.

[53] And you shall eat the fruit of your belly, the flesh of your sons and your daughters whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has given to you, in the siege and in the straits with which your enemy shall straiten you.

[54] The man who is tender among you and very delicate, his eye shall be evil against his brother and against the wife of his bosom and against the rest of his sons whom he leaves over,

[55] so as not to give to one of them of the flesh of his sons that he eats, because nothing is left to him, in the siege and in the straits with which your enemy shall straiten you in all your gates.

[56] The tender and delicate woman among you, the sole of whose foot has not tried to set itself upon the earth for delicateness and for tenderness, her eye shall be evil against the husband of her bosom and against her son and against her daughter,

[57] and against her afterbirth that comes out from between her feet, and against her sons whom she bears; for she shall eat them, in lack of all, in secret, in the siege and in the straits with which your enemy shall straiten you in your gates.

[58] If you do not keep to do all the words of this ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ that are written in this book, to fear this glorious and fearsome name, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[59] then ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will make your blows extraordinary, and the blows of your seed: great and lasting blows, and evil and lasting sicknesses.

[60] And he will bring back upon you every disease of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, before which you were afraid, and they shall cling to you.

[61] Also every sickness and every blow that is not written in the book of this ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will bring them up upon you until you are destroyed.

[62] And you shall be left with few men, in place of your having been as the stars of the heavens for multitude, for you did not listen to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[63] And it shall be, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ exulted over you to do good to you and to multiply you, so ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will exult over you to make you perish and to destroy you; and you shall be torn away from upon the ground to which you are going in to possess it.

[64] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will scatter you among all the peoples, from the end of the earth and to the end of the earth; and you shall serve there other gods that you have not known, you and your fathers, wood and stone.

[65] And among those nations you shall have no repose, and there shall be no resting place for the sole of your foot; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will give you there an agitated heart and wearing out of eyes and pining of soul.

[66] And your life shall be hung up opposite you, and you shall be in dread night and day, and you shall not believe in your life.

[67] In the morning you shall say, 'Would that it were evening!' and in the evening you shall say, 'Would that it were morning!' — from the dread of your heart with which you shall be in dread, and from the sight of your eyes that you shall see.

[68] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will bring you back to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ in ships, by the way of which I said to you, 'You shall not see it again any more'; and you shall sell yourselves there to your enemies as slaves and as maidservants, and there shall be none who buys."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"**, **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — the Hebrew names kept.
- **"And"**, word order (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the lists of blessing and curse keep the Hebrew order, and v3–6 and v16–19 are word for word the same apart from "blessed" / "cursed", the order of the two middle sayings, and "the fruit of your beasts", which v18 does not have. Objects set first in the Hebrew stay first (v30–33, 38–42).
- **"The LORD shall …"** (v7–13, 20–36, 49) — several of these verbs have the short form that Hebrew uses for a wish (v8 יְצַו, v21 יַדְבֵּק, v36 יוֹלֵךְ: "may Yahweh command …"); the others can be read either way. In this chain of promise and threat they are rendered as futures.
- **"if thou shalt hearken diligently unto the voice … to observe and to do" → "if you will surely listen to the voice … to keep to do"** (v1, 15, 58) — Hebrew שָׁמוֹעַ תִּשְׁמַע, literally "listening you listen"; לִשְׁמֹר לַעֲשׂוֹת, two infinitives with no "and" ("be careful to do"); v13 has "to keep and to do". **"set thee on high" → "set you high"** — Hebrew עֶלְיוֹן, as 26:19.
- **"if thou shalt hearken" → "when you listen"** (v2, 9, 13) — Hebrew כִּי, "when", "if" or "for".
- **"Blessed shalt thou be" → "Blessed are you"**, **"Cursed shalt thou be" → "Cursed are you"** (v3–6, 16–19) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה, אָרוּר אַתָּה, with no verb, as 27:15–26.
- **"the fruit of thy body … thy cattle, the increase of thy kine, and the flocks of thy sheep" → "the fruit of your belly … your beasts, the offspring of your cattle and the young of your flock"** (v4, 11, 18, 51, 53) — Hebrew בֶּטֶן "belly"; בְּהֵמָה "beasts"; שְׁגַר אֲלָפֶיךָ "what your cattle drop" (Exod 13:12); עַשְׁתְּרוֹת צֹאנֶךָ, a word found only in this phrase (7:13), of the same letters as the name of the goddess Ashtoreth; "the young" is the usual sense, not certain.
- **"thy basket and thy store" → "your basket and your kneading bowl"** (v5, 17) — Hebrew טֶנֶא (26:2) and מִשְׁאֶרֶת (Exod 12:34).
- **"shall cause thine enemies … to be smitten before thy face" → "will set your enemies … smitten before you"** (v7, 25) — Hebrew נָתַן … לִפְנֵי "set before" (literally "give before", the phrase of 1:8; 7:2) with נִגָּף "smitten" (Lev 26:17). In v25 "against him … before him" is singular, of the enemies.
- **"shall command the blessing upon thee in thy storehouses, and in all that thou settest thine hand unto" → "will command the blessing with you in your storehouses and in all that you stretch out your hand to"** (v8, 20) — Hebrew אִתְּךָ "with you"; אָסָם (only here and Prov 3:10); מִשְׁלַח יָד "the stretching out of the hand", as 12:7.
- **"that thou art called by the name of the LORD" → "that the name of Yahweh is called upon you"** (v10) — word for word.
- **"shall make thee plenteous in goods" → "will make you abound for good"** (v11) — Hebrew הוֹתִיר "leave over, give more than enough"; לְטוֹבָה "for good"; "goods" is not written.
- **"his good treasure, the heaven to give the rain unto thy land in his season" → "his good treasury, the heavens, to give the rain of your land in its time"** (v12) — Hebrew אוֹצָר; מְטַר־אַרְצְךָ, as 11:14. "The work of your hand" is singular.
- **"thou shalt be above only, and thou shalt not be beneath" → "you shall be only above, and you shall not be below"** (v13) — word for word. **"hearken unto the commandments" → "listen to the commandments"** — Hebrew שָׁמַע אֶל.
- **"I command you this day"** (v14) — "you" is plural here; elsewhere in v1–61 it is singular. v62–63 and "you shall sell yourselves" in v68 are plural again.
- **"all these curses"** (v15, 45) — Hebrew קְלָלוֹת, the noun of another root than אָרוּר "cursed" (v16–19) and מְאֵרָה "the curse" (v20).
- **"cursing, vexation, and rebuke … for to do … because of the wickedness of thy doings, whereby thou hast forsaken me" → "the curse, the confusion and the rebuke … which you do … because of the evil of your deeds, in that you have forsaken me"** (v20) — three nouns with the article; מְהוּמָה is of the root of "throw into confusion" (Exod 14:24); מִגְעֶרֶת is found only here. "Me": the speaker passes into the first person of Yahweh.
- **"until he have consumed thee from off the land, whither thou goest to possess it" → "until he has made an end of you from upon the ground to which you are going in to possess it"** (v21, 63) — Hebrew כִּלָּה; הָאֲדָמָה.
- **"with a consumption, and with a fever, and with an inflammation, and with an extreme burning, and with the sword, and with blasting, and with mildew" → "with the consumption and with the fever and with the inflammation and with the burning heat and with the sword and with the blight and with the mildew"** (v22) — seven nouns with the article. שַׁחֶפֶת and קַדַּחַת are the two of Lev 26:16, a wasting sickness and a burning one. דַּלֶּקֶת and חַרְחֻר are found only here, both from roots for "burn"; what sicknesses they are is not known. חֶרֶב is "sword" as the Hebrew is pointed; with other vowels the same letters are "drought" (so the Latin, from memory), which fits the two crop diseases that follow. שִׁדָּפוֹן is a scorching of the grain (Gen 41:6), יֵרָקוֹן a yellowing.
- **"thy heaven … shall be brass" → "your heavens … shall be bronze"** (v23) — as Lev 26:19, where the two metals stand the other way round.
- **"powder and dust" → "fine dust and dust"** (v24) — Hebrew אָבָק (Exod 9:9) and עָפָר.
- **"shalt be removed into all the kingdoms of the earth" → "you shall become a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth"** (v25) — Hebrew זַעֲוָה, a thing that makes one tremble.
- **"thy carcase shall be meat unto all fowls of the air … and no man shall fray them away" → "your dead body shall be food for all the birds of the heavens … and there shall be none who makes them tremble"** (v26) — Hebrew נְבֵלָה, the word used of the carcass of an animal (Lev 11) and of a dead man (21:23); וְאֵין מַחֲרִיד, the words of Lev 26:6; "them" is supplied.
- **"with the botch of Egypt, and with the emerods, and with the scab, and with the itch" → "with the boils of Egypt and with the tumors and with the itch and with the scurf"** (v27, 35) — Hebrew שְׁחִין, the "boils" of Exod 9:9. "Tumors" follows the reading tradition (qere) טְחֹרִים; the written word (ketiv) is עֳפָלִים "swellings" (1 Sam 5:6); both are taken of swellings at the anus. גָּרָב is the "itch" of Lev 21:20; חֶרֶס is found only here, an itching disease of the skin. All four are uncertain.
- **"astonishment of heart" → "bewilderment of heart"** (v28) — Hebrew תִּמָּהוֹן, from the verb "marvel" (Gen 43:33).
- **"thou shalt grope at noonday, as the blind gropeth in darkness … thou shalt be only oppressed and spoiled evermore, and no man shall save thee" → "you shall be feeling about at noon as the blind man feels about in the gloom … you shall be only extorted from and robbed all the days, and there shall be none who saves"** (v29, 31, 33) — Hebrew מָשַׁשׁ "feel with the hands"; אֲפֵלָה (Exod 10:22); עָשׁוּק and גָּזוּל, the two verbs of Lev 19:13; מוֹשִׁיעַ "one who saves".
- **"another man shall lie with her"** (v30) — so the reading tradition (qere) יִשְׁכָּבֶנָּה; the written word (ketiv) is יִשְׁגָּלֶנָּה, a coarser verb for the same act. **"shalt not gather the grapes thereof" → "you shall not put it to common use"** — Hebrew לֹא תְחַלְּלֶנּוּ, literally "you shall not profane it": the first fruit of a vineyard is holy (Lev 19:23–25), and to "profane" it is to begin to use it as common (so 20:6).
- **"Thine ox shall be slain … thine ass shall be violently taken away … thou shalt have none to rescue them" → "Your ox shall be butchered … your donkey shall be robbed … you shall have none who saves"** (v31) — Hebrew participles without a verb: "your ox butchered before your eyes".
- **"thine eyes shall look, and fail with longing for them all the day long: and there shall be no might in thine hand" → "your eyes shall see and wear out toward them all the day, and there shall be nothing in the power of your hand"** (v32) — Hebrew כָּלוֹת, as Lev 26:16; "with longing" is not written. וְאֵין לְאֵל יָדֶךָ is the phrase of Gen 31:29.
- **"So that thou shalt be mad for the sight of thine eyes" → "And you shall be driven mad by the sight of your eyes"** (v34, 67) — Hebrew מַרְאֶה "appearance": what your eyes see.
- **"shall bring thee, and thy king" → "will make you walk, you and your king"** (v36) — Hebrew יוֹלֵךְ, the causative of "walk, go", as 8:2; 29:5. **"wood and stone"** (v36, 64) — the Hebrew has no "of".
- **"an astonishment, a proverb, and a byword" → "a thing that appalls, a proverb and a taunt"** (v37) — Hebrew שַׁמָּה, of the root "be desolate, be appalled" (Lev 26:32); מָשָׁל (Num 23:7 "parable"), here a saying on people's lips; שְׁנִינָה "a sharp word".
- **"the locust shall consume it" → "the locusts shall devour it"** (v38) — Hebrew אַרְבֶּה, a collective singular; חָסַל is found only here.
- **"dress them … nor gather the grapes; for the worms shall eat them" → "work … and you shall not gather in, for the worm shall eat it"** (v39) — Hebrew עָבַד; אָגַר "gather in, store", with no object.
- **"throughout all thy coasts … for thine olive shall cast his fruit" → "in all your border … for your olive shall drop off"** (v40) — Hebrew גְּבוּל, the border and the land within it; יִשַּׁל "drop off". "Anoint yourself" is סוּךְ, of rubbing the body with oil, not the מָשַׁח of anointing a priest or king.
- **"thou shalt not enjoy them" → "they shall not be yours"** (v41) — Hebrew וְלֹא־יִהְיוּ לָךְ.
- **"All thy trees and fruit of thy land shall the locust consume" → "All your trees and the fruit of your ground the cicada shall possess"** (v42) — Hebrew צְלָצַל, found only here as the name of an insect, from a root "whirr, tingle": a whirring insect, a kind of locust or cricket; uncertain. יְיָרֵשׁ is "take possession of".
- **"shall get up above thee very high; and thou shalt come down very low" → "shall go up above you, higher and higher, and you, you shall go down, lower and lower"** (v43) — Hebrew מַעְלָה מָּעְלָה … מַטָּה מָּטָּה, "upward, upward … downward, downward".
- **"with joyfulness, and with gladness of heart" → "with gladness and with goodness of heart"** (v47) — Hebrew שִׂמְחָה; טוּב לֵבָב. "For the abundance of all" may be "because of" or "out of".
- **"in hunger … in want of all things"** (v48, 57) — Hebrew חֹסֶר כֹּל. "He will put": Yahweh or the enemy; the Hebrew does not say. "Whom Yahweh will send" is singular in the Hebrew ("him").
- **"shall bring a nation against thee … as swift as the eagle flieth; a nation whose tongue thou shalt not understand" → "will lift up against you a nation … as the eagle swoops, a nation whose tongue you shall not understand"** (v49) — Hebrew יִשָּׂא "lift up"; דָּאָה "fly swiftly, dart"; שָׁמַע "hear" (so Gen 11:7), here "understand": "not hear" would say in English that the tongue is never heard.
- **"A nation of fierce countenance, which shall not regard the person of the old, nor shew favour to the young" → "a nation strong of face, who will not lift up the face of an old man and will not show favor to a boy"** (v50) — word for word.
- **"corn, wine, or oil" → "grain, new wine or fresh oil"** (v51) — Hebrew דָּגָן, תִּירוֹשׁ, יִצְהָר.
- **"he shall besiege thee … in the siege, and in the straitness, wherewith thine enemies shall distress thee" → "it shall distress you … in the siege and in the straits with which your enemy shall straiten you"** (v52, 53, 55, 57) — two Hebrew roots are kept apart: הֵצַר "press, distress" (v52, root of צָרָה "distress") and מָצוֹק / הֵצִיק "straits / straiten" (v53–57); מָצוֹר is "siege". "Your enemy" is singular.
- **"his eye shall be evil toward"** (v54, 56) — the Hebrew phrase kept: he looks with ill will, grudges. **"the remnant of his children which he shall leave" → "the rest of his sons whom he leaves over"** — Hebrew בָּנָיו; יֶתֶר and הוֹתִיר are one root.
- **"which would not adventure to set the sole of her foot upon the ground" → "the sole of whose foot has not tried to set itself upon the earth"** (v56) — Hebrew נִסְּתָה "has tested, tried", with "the sole of her foot" as subject; הָאָרֶץ.
- **"toward her young one that cometh out from between her feet, and toward her children … for want of all things secretly" → "against her afterbirth that comes out from between her feet, and against her sons … in lack of all, in secret"** (v57) — Hebrew שִׁלְיָה, found only here: the afterbirth. בָּנֶיהָ is "her sons"; the word also covers children of both sexes.
- **"this glorious and fearful name, THE LORD THY GOD" → "this glorious and fearsome name, Yahweh your Elohim"** (v58) — Hebrew הַנִּכְבָּד וְהַנּוֹרָא.
- **"will make thy plagues wonderful … great plagues, and of long continuance, and sore sicknesses, and of long continuance" → "will make your blows extraordinary … great and lasting blows, and evil and lasting sicknesses"** (v59, 61) — Hebrew הִפְלָא, of the root "wonder"; מַכָּה "blow" (Lev 26:21); נֶאֱמָן "faithful, sure", here of what does not let go.
- **"all the diseases of Egypt, which thou wast afraid of" → "every disease of Egypt, before which you were afraid"** (v60) — Hebrew מַדְוֵה, singular here (7:15 plural), followed by "them"; יָגֹר "be afraid". **"bring upon thee" → "bring back upon you"** — Hebrew הֵשִׁיב.
- **"the book of this law" → "the book of this Torah"** (v61) — "this" belongs to "Torah" here; in 29:21 to "book".
- **"ye shall be left few in number, whereas ye were as the stars of heaven" → "you shall be left with few men, in place of your having been as the stars of the heavens"** (v62) — Hebrew בִּמְתֵי מְעָט, as 26:5; תַּחַת אֲשֶׁר, the words rendered "because" in v47.
- **"rejoiced over you … ye shall be plucked from off the land" → "exulted over you … you shall be torn away from upon the ground"** (v63) — Hebrew שׂוּשׂ, another verb than the "rejoice" of 26:11; נִסַּח.
- **"shalt thou find no ease … a trembling heart, and failing of eyes, and sorrow of mind" → "you shall have no repose … an agitated heart and wearing out of eyes and pining of soul"** (v65) — Hebrew הִרְגִּיעַ; רַגָּז, of the root "be agitated, tremble"; כִּלְיוֹן עֵינַיִם and דַּאֲבוֹן נֶפֶשׁ answer to "wear out the eyes and make the soul pine" in Lev 26:16; נֶפֶשׁ is "soul".
- **"thy life shall hang in doubt before thee … and shalt have none assurance of thy life" → "your life shall be hung up opposite you … and you shall not believe in your life"** (v66) — Hebrew תְּלֻאִים לְךָ מִנֶּגֶד; לֹא תַאֲמִין בְּחַיֶּיךָ, the verb "believe".
- **"Would God it were even!" → "Would that it were evening!"** (v67) — Hebrew מִי־יִתֵּן, literally "who will give"; the Hebrew does not name God. "Dread" is פַּחַד, noun and verb.
- **"Thou shalt see it no more again: and there ye shall be sold unto your enemies for bondmen and bondwomen, and no man shall buy you" → "'You shall not see it again any more'; and you shall sell yourselves there to your enemies as slaves and as maidservants, and there shall be none who buys"** (v68) — Hebrew וְהִתְמַכַּרְתֶּם, a reflexive: "offer yourselves for sale"; עֲבָדִים, שְׁפָחוֹת. "Egypt" has no "to" in the Hebrew.

### Notes

- **Blessing and curse** — v1–14 "if you will surely listen"; v15–68 "if you do not listen". Lev 26 is the parallel; much of its wording returns here (v22, 23, 26, 65).
- **The siege** (v52–57) — 2 Kgs 6:28–29 and Lam 4:10 tell of such eating.
- **"A nation from afar"** (v49) — so Jer 5:15.
- **"By the way of which I said to you"** (v68) — 17:16.
