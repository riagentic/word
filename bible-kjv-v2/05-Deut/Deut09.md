## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"stiffnecked" → "stiff-necked"** (v6, 13) — Hebrew קְשֵׁה עֹרֶף *qesheh oref*. Idiom for obstinate.
- **"molten image / molten calf" → "cast image / cast calf"** (v12, 16) — Hebrew *massekhah*.
- **"stamped it, and ground it very small" → "crushed it, grinding it thoroughly"** (v21) — modernized.
- **"Lord God" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v26) — *Adonai YHWH*.

### Notes
- **Key theological statement** (v4–6) — Israel's possession of the land is *not* based on their merit. Two reasons given: (1) the Canaanites' wickedness; (2) Yahweh's oath to the patriarchs. A profound anti-triumphalism.
- **Moses' intercessions recounted** (v18–20, 25–29) — double forty-day fast. His intercessions preserve both people and Aaron. Numbered here three times (v14, 19, 26) — the leader as mediator of mercy.
- **Calf destruction** (v21) — the calf burnt, crushed, cast into water. Matches Ex 32:20 (water consumed by the people). Public and definitive abolition.
- **"Lord Yahweh, do not destroy your people"** (v26) — Moses' argument relies on (a) covenantal history, (b) Yahweh's reputation, (c) Abrahamic oath. Same triad appears in other intercessions.
- **Dependence on promise, not performance** — the chapter's entire argument: Israel's election is sheer grace. Paul picks this up (Rom 9–11).
