# Deuteronomy 9

[1] "Hear, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: you are passing over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ today, to go in to dispossess nations greater and mightier than you, cities great and fortified in the heavens,

[2] a people great and tall, sons of ⟦anakim|Anaqim|Anakim⟧, whom you know, and you have heard, 'Who can take his stand before the sons of ⟦anak|Anaq|Anak⟧?'

[3] And you shall know today that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, he is the one passing over before you, a consuming fire; he will destroy them, and he will bring them low before you, and you shall dispossess them and make them perish quickly, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to you.

[4] Do not say in your heart, when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ pushes them away from before you, saying, 'Because of my righteousness ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has brought me in to take possession of this land'; and because of the wickedness of these nations ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is dispossessing them from before you.

[5] Not because of your righteousness and the uprightness of your heart are you going in to take possession of their land; but because of the wickedness of these nations ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is dispossessing them from before you, and in order to establish the word that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ swore to your fathers, to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, to ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ and to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧.

[6] And you shall know that not because of your righteousness is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ giving you this good land to possess it, for a stiff-necked people you are.

[7] Remember, do not forget, how you made ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ wrathful in the wilderness; from the day that you went out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ until your coming to this place, you have been rebelling against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[8] And at ⟦horeb|Chorev|Horeb⟧ you made ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ wrathful, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was angry with you, to destroy you.

[9] When I went up to the mountain to take the tablets of stone, the tablets of the covenant that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ cut with you, I dwelt on the mountain forty days and forty nights; bread I did not eat, and water I did not drink.

[10] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave me the two tablets of stone, written with the finger of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and on them according to all the words that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke with you on the mountain from the midst of the fire on the day of the assembly.

[11] And it came to pass at the end of forty days and forty nights that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave me the two tablets of stone, the tablets of the covenant.

[12] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, 'Arise, go down quickly from here, for your people, whom you brought out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, have wrought ruin. They have turned aside quickly from the way that I commanded them; they have made for themselves a molten image.'

[13] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, saying, 'I have seen this people, and behold, it is a stiff-necked people.

[14] Let go of me, that I may destroy them and blot out their name from under the heavens, and I will make you into a nation mightier and more numerous than it.'

[15] And I turned and went down from the mountain, and the mountain was burning with fire, and the two tablets of the covenant were upon my two hands.

[16] And I saw, and behold, you had sinned against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; you had made for yourselves a molten calf; you had turned aside quickly from the way that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded you.

[17] And I seized the two tablets and threw them from upon my two hands and broke them in pieces before your eyes.

[18] And I threw myself down before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ as at the first, forty days and forty nights; bread I did not eat, and water I did not drink, because of all your sin that you sinned, doing what is evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to vex him.

[19] For I was afraid of the anger and the fury with which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was wrathful against you, to destroy you. And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ listened to me that time also.

[20] And with ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was very angry, to destroy him; and I prayed also on behalf of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ at that time.

[21] And your sin that you had made, the calf, I took and burned it with fire and beat it in pieces, grinding it well, until it was fine as dust; and I threw its dust into the wadi that goes down from the mountain.

[22] And at ⟦taberah|Taverah|Taberah⟧ and at Massah and at ⟦kibroth-hattaavah|Qivrot HaTaavah|Kibroth-hattaavah⟧ you were making ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ wrathful.

[23] And when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ sent you from ⟦kadesh-barnea|Qadesh Barnea|Kadesh-barnea⟧, saying, 'Go up and take possession of the land that I have given you', you rebelled against the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and you did not believe him, and you did not listen to his voice.

[24] You have been rebelling against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from the day I knew you.

[25] And I threw myself down before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the forty days and the forty nights that I threw myself down, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had said he would destroy you.

[26] And I prayed to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and said, '⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧, do not ruin your people and your inheritance, whom you ransomed by your greatness, whom you brought out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ with a strong hand.

[27] Remember your servants, ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ and ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧; do not turn to the hardness of this people, and to its wickedness and to its sin,

[28] lest the land from which you brought us out say, "Because ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was not able to bring them to the land of which he spoke to them, and because of his hatred of them, he brought them out to put them to death in the wilderness."

[29] And they are your people and your inheritance, whom you brought out by your great power and by your outstretched arm.'

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"You"** — singular in the Hebrew in v1–7a (to "went out from the land of Egypt"); plural from "your coming" (v7b) to v25; in v26–29 Moses speaks to Yahweh. English cannot show it; the Czech text does.
- **"Thou art to pass over Jordan this day, to go in to possess nations" → "you are passing over the Jordan today, to go in to dispossess nations"** (v1) — Hebrew עֹבֵר, a participle; לָרֶשֶׁת, the verb of "take possession of" (v4, 5, 23) with persons as its object: to take over what is theirs. The causative of the same verb, הוֹרִישׁ, is "dispossess" in v3, 4, 5. **"fenced up to heaven" → "fortified in the heavens"** — Hebrew בַּשָּׁמָיִם "in the heavens" (1:28).
- **"the children of the Anakims, whom thou knowest, and of whom thou hast heard say" → "sons of Anakim, whom you know, and you have heard"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּנֵי עֲנָקִים, no article; וְאַתָּה שָׁמַעְתָּ "and you, you have heard", with the saying following at once. **"Who can stand" → "Who can take his stand"** — Hebrew יִתְיַצֵּב (7:24).
- **"Understand therefore this day" → "And you shall know today"** (v3, so v6) — Hebrew וְיָדַעְתָּ. **"is he which goeth over before thee; as a consuming fire" → "he is the one passing over before you, a consuming fire"** — the verb of v1: Israel passes over, and Yahweh passes over before him; "as" is not in the Hebrew (4:24). **"he shall bring them down" → "he will bring them low"** — Hebrew יַכְנִיעֵם, "make them bow, humble them". **"so shalt thou drive them out, and destroy them" → "and you shall dispossess them and make them perish"** — Hebrew הוֹרִישׁ, הֶאֱבִיד.
- **"Speak not thou in thine heart, after that the LORD thy God hath cast them out" → "Do not say in your heart, when Yahweh your Elohim pushes them away"** (v4) — Hebrew בַּהֲדֹף, from הָדַף "push" (6:19). **"For my righteousness" → "Because of my righteousness"** — Hebrew בְּצִדְקָתִי, "in / by / for my righteousness". **"but for the wickedness of these nations the LORD doth drive them out" → "and because of the wickedness of these nations Yahweh is dispossessing them"** — Hebrew וּ "and". Whether this clause still belongs to what Israel says in his heart ("my righteousness, and their wickedness") or is Moses' answer to it, the Hebrew does not mark; v5 states it as Moses' own word.
- **"that he may perform the word" → "in order to establish the word"** (v5) — Hebrew הָקִים, "make stand" (8:18).
- **"thou art a stiffnecked people" → "a stiff-necked people you are"** (v6, 13) — Hebrew עַם־קְשֵׁה־עֹרֶף, "a people hard of neck" (Exod 32:9).
- **"Remember, and forget not, how thou provokedst the LORD thy God to wrath" → "Remember, do not forget, how you made Yahweh your Elohim wrathful"** (v7, 8, 22) — Hebrew הִקְצִיף, the causative of קָצַף "be wrathful" (v19); no "and" between the two commands. **"ye have been rebellious against the LORD" → "you have been rebelling against Yahweh"** (v7, 24) — Hebrew מַמְרִים הֱיִיתֶם עִם־יְהוָה, a participle with "to be"; literally "with Yahweh".
- **"the LORD was angry with you to have destroyed you" → "Yahweh was angry with you, to destroy you"** (v8, so v19, 20) — Hebrew וַיִּתְאַנַּף … לְהַשְׁמִיד, the verb of אַף "anger" with the bare infinitive.
- **"to receive the tables of stone" → "to take the tablets of stone"** (v9) — Hebrew לָקַחַת. **"which the LORD made with you" → "that Yahweh cut with you"** — Hebrew כָּרַת. **"then I abode in the mount" → "I dwelt on the mountain"** — Hebrew וָאֵשֵׁב, from יָשַׁב "sit, dwell" (10:10 has "I stood"). **"I neither did eat bread nor drink water" → "bread I did not eat, and water I did not drink"** (v9, 18) — the Hebrew order, as Exod 34:28.
- **"and on them was written according to all the words" → "and on them according to all the words"** (v10) — the Hebrew has no verb here. **"in the day of the assembly"** — Hebrew בְּיוֹם הַקָּהָל.
- **v12–14 and Exod 32:7–10** — the same Hebrew has the same English ("your people … have wrought ruin", "They have turned aside quickly from the way that I commanded them", "they have made for themselves", "I have seen this people, and behold, it is a stiff-necked people"). The differences: "Arise, go down quickly from here" (Exodus: "Go, go down"); "whom you brought out from Egypt" (Exodus: "brought up from the land of Egypt"); מַסֵּכָה "a molten image" (Exodus: "a molten calf", as v16 here); הֶרֶף מִמֶּנִּי "Let go of me" (Exodus: הַנִּיחָה לִּי "leave me"); "that I may destroy them and blot out their name from under the heavens" (Exodus: "that my anger may burn against them and I may make an end of them"); "a nation mightier and more numerous than it" (Exodus: "a great nation").
- **"have corrupted themselves" → "have wrought ruin"** (v12) — Hebrew שִׁחֵת without an object, the verb of v26 ("do not ruin your people").
- **"Furthermore the LORD spake unto me, saying" → "And Yahweh said to me, saying"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר … לֵאמֹר.
- **"Let me alone" → "Let go of me"** (v14) — Hebrew הֶרֶף מִמֶּנִּי, "slacken, drop your hold from me". **"that I may destroy them"** — Hebrew וְאַשְׁמִידֵם, the form of will. **"a nation mightier and greater than they" → "a nation mightier and more numerous than it"** — Hebrew עָצוּם וָרָב מִמֶּנּוּ.
- **"the two tables of the covenant were in my two hands" → "were upon my two hands"** (v15) — Hebrew עַל. Exod 32:15 has "the two tablets of the testimony … in his hand".
- **"And I looked" → "And I saw"** (v16). **"a molten calf"** — Hebrew עֵגֶל מַסֵּכָה (Exod 32:4, 8).
- **"cast them out of my two hands, and brake them" → "threw them from upon my two hands and broke them in pieces"** (v17) — Hebrew וָאַשְׁלִכֵם … וָאֲשַׁבְּרֵם, the verbs of Exod 32:19.
- **"And I fell down before the LORD" → "And I threw myself down before Yahweh"** (v18, 25) — Hebrew וָאֶתְנַפַּל, the reflexive of נָפַל "fall": to cast oneself down, lie prostrate. **"as at the first"** — Hebrew כָּרִאשֹׁנָה. **"in doing wickedly in the sight of the LORD, to provoke him to anger" → "doing what is evil in the eyes of Yahweh, to vex him"** — Hebrew הָרַע "the evil"; הִכְעִיס "vex, provoke"; "to anger" is not a second word.
- **"the anger and hot displeasure, wherewith the LORD was wroth against you" → "the anger and the fury with which Yahweh was wrathful against you"** (v19) — Hebrew אַף "anger" (literally "nose"), חֵמָה "fury" (literally "heat"), קָצַף "be wrathful". **"I was afraid"** — Hebrew יָגֹרְתִּי, not the common יָרֵא "fear". **"hearkened unto me at that time also" → "listened to me that time also"** — as 10:10.
- **"And the LORD was very angry with Aaron to have destroyed him" → "And with Aaron Yahweh was very angry, to destroy him"** (v20) — "with Aaron" first. Exod 32 does not tell of this. **"for Aaron also" → "also on behalf of Aaron"** — Hebrew בְּעַד.
- **"And I took your sin, the calf which ye had made" → "And your sin that you had made, the calf, I took"** (v21) — Hebrew וְאֶת־חַטַּאתְכֶם אֲשֶׁר־עֲשִׂיתֶם אֶת־הָעֵגֶל: the calf is called "your sin". **"stamped it, and ground it very small, even until it was as small as dust" → "beat it in pieces, grinding it well, until it was fine as dust"** — Hebrew וָאֶכֹּת, from כָּתַת "beat, crush in pieces"; טָחוֹן הֵיטֵב "grinding, doing well"; דַּק "fine" (Exod 32:20). **"into the brook that descended out of the mount" → "into the wadi that goes down from the mountain"** — Hebrew הַנַּחַל. Exod 32:20 has "he strewed it on the face of the waters and gave the sons of Israel to drink"; of the drinking nothing is said here.
- **"ye provoked the LORD to wrath" → "you were making Yahweh wrathful"** (v22) — Hebrew מַקְצִפִים הֱיִיתֶם, a participle with "to be". Taberah: Num 11:1–3; Massah: Exod 17:1–7; Kibroth-hattaavah: Num 11:4–34.
- **"Go up and possess the land" → "Go up and take possession of the land"** (v23). **"ye rebelled against the commandment of the LORD" → "you rebelled against the mouth of Yahweh"** — Hebrew וַתַּמְרוּ אֶת־פִּי יְהוָה (1:26, 43). **"ye believed him not, nor hearkened to his voice" → "you did not believe him, and you did not listen to his voice"** — Hebrew הֶאֱמִין לְ "believe"; שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל.
- **"from the day that I knew you"** (v24) — Hebrew מִיּוֹם דַּעְתִּי אֶתְכֶם, "from the day of my knowing you".
- **"Thus I fell down before the LORD forty days and forty nights, as I fell down at the first; because the LORD had said he would destroy you" → "And I threw myself down before Yahweh, the forty days and the forty nights that I threw myself down, for Yahweh had said he would destroy you"** (v25) — Hebrew אֵת אַרְבָּעִים הַיּוֹם וְאֶת־אַרְבָּעִים הַלַּיְלָה אֲשֶׁר הִתְנַפָּלְתִּי, with the article: the forty days of v18; "as … at the first" is not in this verse. אָמַר … לְהַשְׁמִיד is literally "had said to destroy you".
- **"O Lord GOD, destroy not thy people" → "Lord Yahweh, do not ruin your people"** (v26) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai YHWH*; "O" is not in the Hebrew. אַל־תַּשְׁחֵת is the verb of v12: the people "have wrought ruin"; Moses asks that Yahweh not ruin them. (The "destroy" of v3, 8, 14, 19, 20, 25 is another verb, הִשְׁמִיד.) **"which thou hast redeemed through thy greatness" → "whom you ransomed by your greatness"** — Hebrew פָּדָה "ransom" (7:8). **"mighty hand" → "strong hand"**.
- **v26–29 and Exod 32:11–13** — another wording of the prayer: the Hebrew agrees with Exodus only in "your people", "whom you brought out … with a strong hand" and "Remember … your servants". Exodus names "Abraham, Isaac and Israel"; this verse "Abraham, Isaac and Jacob".
- **"Remember thy servants"** (v27) — Hebrew זְכֹר לַעֲבָדֶיךָ, "remember for your servants" (as Exod 32:13). **"look not unto the stubbornness of this people, nor to their wickedness, nor to their sin" → "do not turn to the hardness of this people, and to its wickedness and to its sin"** — Hebrew אַל־תֵּפֶן אֶל "do not turn to"; קְשִׁי "hardness" (the root of "stiff-necked", v6, 13).
- **"Lest the land whence thou broughtest us out say"** (v28) — the verb is plural in the Hebrew (יֹאמְרוּ): the people of the land. **"which he promised them" → "of which he spoke to them"** — Hebrew דִּבֶּר. **"because he hated them" → "because of his hatred of them"** — Hebrew וּמִשִּׂנְאָתוֹ אוֹתָם. **"to slay them" → "to put them to death"** — Hebrew לַהֲמִתָם. Cf. Num 14:16; Exod 32:12.
- **"Yet they are thy people" → "And they are your people"** (v29) — Hebrew וְהֵם. **"by thy mighty power and by thy stretched out arm" → "by your great power and by your outstretched arm"** — Hebrew בְּכֹחֲךָ הַגָּדֹל.

### Notes
- **Not because of your righteousness** (v4–6) — three times. Two reasons are given for the gift of the land: the wickedness of the nations, and the word sworn to the fathers.
- **The calf retold** (v8–21) — Moses retells Exod 32 as one who was there, in the first person; where the Hebrew of the two chapters is the same, the translation is the same (see Changes).
- **Forty days** (v9, 11, 18, 25) — the forty days on the mountain for the tablets, and forty days of Moses lying before Yahweh for the people (cf. Exod 34:28; Deut 10:10).
- **Moses' prayer** (v26–29) — he pleads the ransoming from Egypt, the fathers, and what the land of Egypt would say.
