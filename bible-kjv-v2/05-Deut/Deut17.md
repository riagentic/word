# Deuteronomy 17

[1] "You shall not sacrifice to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ an ox or a sheep in which there is a blemish, any evil thing, for it is an abomination to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[2] When there is found in your midst, in one of your gates that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you, a man or a woman who does what is evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, overstepping his covenant,

[3] and he has gone and served other gods and bowed down to them, and to the sun or to the moon or to all the host of the heavens, which I have not commanded,

[4] and it is told to you, and you hear, then you shall seek well; and behold, it is truth, the thing is established, this abomination has been done in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[5] then you shall bring out that man or that woman, who have done this evil thing, to your gates, the man or the woman, and you shall stone them with stones, and they shall die.

[6] At the mouth of two witnesses or three witnesses the one who is to die shall be put to death; he shall not be put to death at the mouth of one witness.

[7] The hand of the witnesses shall be on him first, to put him to death, and the hand of all the people afterward; and you shall purge the evil from your midst.

[8] When a matter is too wonderful for you for judgment, between blood and blood, between cause and cause, and between stroke and stroke, matters of contention in your gates, then you shall arise and go up to the place that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will choose.

[9] And you shall come to the priests, the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, and to the judge who shall be in those days, and you shall seek, and they shall tell you the word of the judgment.

[10] And you shall do at the mouth of the word that they tell you from that place that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will choose, and you shall keep to do according to all that they teach you.

[11] At the mouth of the ⟦torah|torah|law⟧ that they teach you, and according to the judgment that they say to you, you shall do; you shall not turn aside from the word that they tell you, right or left.

[12] And the man who acts with presumption, not listening to the priest who stands to minister there to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, or to the judge, that man shall die; and you shall purge the evil from ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[13] And all the people shall hear and fear, and they shall not act presumptuously again.

[14] When you come to the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you, and you take possession of it and dwell in it, and you say, 'Let me set a king over me, like all the nations that are around me,'

[15] you shall surely set over you a king whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will choose; from the midst of your brothers you shall set a king over you; you cannot put over you a foreign man, who is not your brother.

[16] Only he shall not multiply horses for himself, and he shall not make the people return to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ in order to multiply horses, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has said to you, 'You shall return on this way no more again.'

[17] And he shall not multiply wives for himself, and his heart shall not turn aside; and silver and gold he shall not multiply for himself greatly.

[18] And it shall be, when he sits on the throne of his kingdom, that he shall write for himself a copy of this ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ in a book, from before the priests, the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧.

[19] And it shall be with him, and he shall read in it all the days of his life, so that he may learn to fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to keep all the words of this ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ and these statutes, to do them,

[20] that his heart be not lifted up above his brothers, and that he not turn aside from the commandment, right or left, so that he may prolong days over his kingdom, he and his sons, in the midst of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"any bullock, or sheep, wherein is blemish, or any evilfavouredness" → "an ox or a sheep in which there is a blemish, any evil thing"** (v1) — Hebrew שׁוֹר וָשֶׂה, literally "ox and sheep": שׁוֹר is one head of cattle, שֶׂה one animal of the flock, sheep or goat. מוּם "blemish" (15:21; Lev 22:20); כֹּל דָּבָר רָע, literally "any evil thing / word": רַע of quality, "bad". **"an abomination unto the LORD"** — Hebrew תוֹעֲבַת יְהוָה, literally "the abomination of Yahweh".
- **"If" → "When"; "within any of thy gates" → "in one of your gates"** (v2) — Hebrew כִּי; בְּאַחַד שְׁעָרֶיךָ. **"that hath wrought wickedness in the sight of" → "who does what is evil in the eyes of"** — Hebrew יַעֲשֶׂה אֶת־הָרַע בְּעֵינֵי. **"in transgressing his covenant" → "overstepping his covenant"** — Hebrew לַעֲבֹר בְּרִיתוֹ, literally "to pass over his covenant" (עָבַר, as Num 14:41 "overstepping the mouth of Yahweh"); "transgression" is kept for פֶּשַׁע.
- **"either the sun, or moon, or any of the host of heaven" → "and to the sun or to the moon or to all the host of the heavens"** (v3) — Hebrew וְלַשֶּׁמֶשׁ אוֹ לַיָּרֵחַ אוֹ לְכָל־צְבָא הַשָּׁמַיִם; שָׁמַיִם is plural. The verbs are a narrative chain: "and he went and served … and bowed down". **"worshipped" → "bowed down"** — Hebrew הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה. **"which I have not commanded" kept** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר לֹא־צִוִּיתִי: the speaker passes into the "I" of Yahweh.
- **"enquired diligently" → "seek well"** (v4, 9) — Hebrew דָּרַשׁ "seek", here of inquiry into a matter; הֵיטֵב "well". **"it be true, and the thing certain" → "it is truth, the thing is established"** — Hebrew אֱמֶת נָכוֹן הַדָּבָר (13:14); דָּבָר is "word" and "thing". "If" (KJV) is not written. **"is wrought" → "has been done"** — Hebrew נֶעֶשְׂתָה.
- **"which have committed that wicked thing, unto thy gates, even that man or that woman" → "who have done this evil thing, to your gates, the man or the woman"** (v5) — the Hebrew names "the man or the woman" twice. **"till they die" → "and they shall die"** — Hebrew וָמֵתוּ.
- **"At the mouth of two witnesses, or three witnesses" kept** (v6) — Hebrew עַל־פִּי "at the mouth of" (19:15; Num 35:30). **"he that is worthy of death" → "the one who is to die"** — Hebrew הַמֵּת, literally "the one dying / the dead one".
- **"The hands of the witnesses shall be first upon him" → "The hand of the witnesses shall be on him first"; "afterward the hands of all the people" → "the hand of all the people afterward"** (v7) — Hebrew יַד "hand", singular both times; בָרִאשֹׁנָה, בָּאַחֲרֹנָה. **"put the evil away" → "purge the evil"** (v7, 12) — Hebrew בִּעַרְתָּ, piel of בָּעַר: literally "burn away, consume", that is, clear out (13:5; 19:13, 19).
- **"a matter too hard for thee in judgment" → "a matter is too wonderful for you for judgment"** (v8) — Hebrew יִפָּלֵא מִמְּךָ, from the root of פֶּלֶא "wonder" (Gen 18:14): beyond you, past your finding out. **"between plea and plea" → "between cause and cause"** — Hebrew דִּין, the noun of the verb "judge" (Gen 15:14): a suit at law. **"between stroke and stroke" kept** — Hebrew נֶגַע, literally "a touch, a stroke": the word that is "plague" in Lev 13–14. Whether bodily injury or a case of the marks of Lev 13 is meant is not certain. **"being matters of controversy" → "matters of contention"** — Hebrew דִּבְרֵי רִיבֹת, literally "words of contentions" (plural). **"between blood and blood"** — cases of bloodshed (19:4–13).
- **"the priests the Levites" → "the priests, the Levites"** (v9, 18) — Hebrew הַכֹּהֲנִים הַלְוִיִּם, two nouns side by side; "levitical priests" is the same thing said as an adjective. **"that shall be in those days" kept** — "in office" is not in the Hebrew. **"shew thee the sentence of judgment" → "tell you the word of the judgment"** — Hebrew הִגִּיד "tell"; דְּבַר הַמִּשְׁפָּט.
- **"according to the sentence" → "at the mouth of the word" / "at the mouth of the torah"** (v10, 11) — Hebrew עַל־פִּי הַדָּבָר, עַל־פִּי הַתּוֹרָה: the phrase of v6. **"observe to do" → "keep to do"** (v10) — Hebrew וְשָׁמַרְתָּ לַעֲשׂוֹת ("be careful to do"). **"inform thee" / "teach thee" → "teach you"** (v10, 11) — Hebrew הוֹרָה, the verb of תּוֹרָה: "the torah that they teach you" is one root twice. Here תּוֹרָה is the instruction given in the case; in v18, 19 it is "this Torah".
- **"the judgment which they shall tell thee" → "the judgment that they say to you"; "to the right hand, nor to the left" → "right or left"** (v11) — Hebrew אָמַר; יָמִין וּשְׂמֹאל, literally "right and left".
- **"will do presumptuously, and will not hearken" → "acts with presumption, not listening"** (v12) — Hebrew בְזָדוֹן, the noun of זִיד "act presumptuously" (v13; 18:20, 22). **"to minister there before the LORD" → "to minister there to Yahweh"** — Hebrew לְשָׁרֶת שָׁם אֶת־יְהוָה; "before" is not written.
- **"do no more presumptuously" → "not act presumptuously again"** (v13) — Hebrew וְלֹא יְזִידוּן עוֹד.
- **"I will set a king over me" → "Let me set a king over me"** (v14) — Hebrew אָשִׂימָה, a cohortative: a wish or resolve.
- **"Thou shalt in any wise set him king over thee" → "you shall surely set over you a king"** (v15) — Hebrew infinitive absolute with its verb, שׂוֹם תָּשִׂים, literally "setting you shall set". **"from among thy brethren" → "from the midst of your brothers"** — Hebrew מִקֶּרֶב אַחֶיךָ. **"thou mayest not set a stranger over thee" → "you cannot put over you a foreign man"** — Hebrew לֹא תוּכַל, literally "you are not able": of what is not permitted (16:5); לָתֵת "give, put", another verb than "set" (שִׂים); אִישׁ נָכְרִי.
- **"But" → "Only"; "cause the people to return to Egypt, to the end that he should multiply horses" → "make the people return to Egypt in order to multiply horses"** (v16) — Hebrew רַק; the second "horses" is סוּס, a collective singular. **"forasmuch as the LORD hath said" → "and Yahweh has said"** — Hebrew וַיהוָה אָמַר לָכֶם; "you" is plural here and in the saying, singular in the rest of the passage. No such saying is found in these words earlier in the Torah (compare Exod 14:13; Deut 28:68). **"Ye shall henceforth return no more that way" → "You shall return on this way no more again"** — Hebrew לֹא תֹסִפוּן לָשׁוּב … עוֹד, literally "you shall not add to return … again".
- **"that his heart turn not away" → "and his heart shall not turn aside"** (v17) — Hebrew וְלֹא יָסוּר לְבָבוֹ: a second clause joined by "and"; that it gives the purpose of the first is the usual understanding. סוּר is the "turn aside" of v11, 20.
- **"a copy of this law in a book out of that which is before the priests the Levites" → "a copy of this Torah in a book, from before the priests, the Levites"** (v18) — Hebrew מִשְׁנֵה הַתּוֹרָה הַזֹּאת, literally "a double / second of this Torah" (מִשְׁנֶה, as 15:18): a copy. The Septuagint (cited from memory) has τὸ δευτερονόμιον τοῦτο, "this second law", from which the book has its name. עַל־סֵפֶר "on a book": any written document, here a scroll. מִלִּפְנֵי, literally "from before the face of": from the one in their keeping, or under their eyes.
- **"it shall be with him, and he shall read therein"** (v19) — in the Hebrew "it shall be" is feminine (the Torah) and "in it" masculine (the book or the copy). **"to keep all the words of this law and these statutes, to do them"** — Hebrew לִשְׁמֹר … לַעֲשֹׂתָם.
- **"That his heart be not lifted up above his brethren" kept** (v20) — Hebrew לְבִלְתִּי רוּם־לְבָבוֹ מֵאֶחָיו. **"prolong his days in his kingdom" → "prolong days over his kingdom"** — Hebrew יַאֲרִיךְ יָמִים עַל־מַמְלַכְתּוֹ. **"his children" → "his sons"** — Hebrew בָּנָיו.

### Notes
- **Two or three witnesses** (v6) — so also 19:15; Num 35:30. Taken up in Matt 18:16; 2 Cor 13:1; 1 Tim 5:19; Heb 10:28.
- **The matter too wonderful** (v8–13) — a case beyond the judges in the gates goes up to "the place that Yahweh will choose", to the priests and the judge there; what they tell is binding.
- **The king** (v14–20) — the people may set a king over them, one whom Yahweh chooses, from the midst of their brothers; he is not to multiply horses, wives, silver and gold; he writes for himself a copy of this Torah and reads in it all his days.
- **Solomon** — 1 Kgs 10:14–11:8 tells of horses from Egypt, of silver and gold, and of many wives who turned aside his heart, in words close to these.
- **"His heart not lifted up above his brothers"** (v20) — the king stays one of the brothers and under the commandment.
- **"Mishneh Torah"** (v18) — the Hebrew words for "a copy of this Torah" later became a name for Deuteronomy, and the title of the law code of Maimonides (12th century).
