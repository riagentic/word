# Deuteronomy 16

[1] "Keep the month of ⟦abib|Aviv|Abib⟧ and make a ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for in the month of ⟦abib|Aviv|Abib⟧ ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ brought you out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ by night.

[2] And you shall sacrifice a ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, flock and herd, in the place that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will choose, to make his name abide there.

[3] You shall not eat with it what is leavened; seven days you shall eat with it unleavened bread, bread of affliction, for in haste you came out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, so that you may remember the day of your coming out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ all the days of your life.

[4] And leaven shall not be seen with you in all your territory seven days, and nothing of the flesh that you sacrifice in the evening on the first day shall lodge until the morning.

[5] You cannot sacrifice the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ in one of your gates that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you,

[6] but at the place that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will choose, to make his name abide: there you shall sacrifice the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ in the evening, at the going in of the sun, the appointed time of your coming out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[7] And you shall boil and eat in the place that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will choose, and you shall turn in the morning and go to your tents.

[8] Six days you shall eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day is a solemn assembly to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; you shall do no work.

[9] Seven weeks you shall number for yourself; from the beginning of the sickle in the standing grain you shall begin to number seven weeks.

[10] And you shall make a Feast of ⟦weeks|Shavuot|Weeks⟧ to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, by the measure of the freewill offering of your hand that you shall give, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ blesses you.

[11] And you shall rejoice before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you and your son and your daughter and your slave and your slave woman, and the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧ who is in your gates, and the sojourner and the fatherless and the widow who are in your midst, in the place that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will choose, to make his name abide there.

[12] And you shall remember that you were a slave in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and you shall keep and do these statutes.

[13] The Feast of ⟦booths|Sukkot|Booths⟧ you shall make for yourself seven days, when you gather in from your threshing floor and from your winepress.

[14] And you shall rejoice in your feast, you and your son and your daughter and your slave and your slave woman, and the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧ and the sojourner and the fatherless and the widow who are in your gates.

[15] Seven days you shall hold a feast to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in the place that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will choose, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will bless you in all your yield and in all the work of your hands, and you shall be only rejoicing.

[16] Three times in the year all your males shall appear before the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in the place that he will choose: at the Feast of ⟦unleavened-bread|Matzot|Unleavened Bread⟧ and at the Feast of ⟦weeks|Shavuot|Weeks⟧ and at the Feast of ⟦booths|Sukkot|Booths⟧; and none shall appear before the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ empty:

[17] each man according to the gift of his hand, according to the blessing of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that he has given you.

[18] Judges and officers you shall set for yourself in all your gates that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you, for your tribes; and they shall judge the people with a judgment of righteousness.

[19] You shall not turn aside judgment; you shall not recognize faces; and you shall not take a bribe, for the bribe blinds the eyes of the wise and overturns the words of the righteous.

[20] Righteousness, righteousness you shall pursue, so that you may live and take possession of the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you.

[21] You shall not plant for yourself an Asherah, any tree, beside the altar of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that you shall make for yourself.

[22] And you shall not raise up for yourself a pillar, which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ hates.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Observe the month of Abib, and keep the passover" → "Keep the month of Aviv and make a Pesach"** (v1) — Hebrew שָׁמוֹר "keep" (an infinitive used as a command) and וְעָשִׂיתָ פֶּסַח "and you shall make a Pesach", without the article: the wording of Exod 12:48; Num 9:10, 14. פֶּסַח is the animal, the meal and the feast alike. **"Abib"** — אָבִיב, "the month of the fresh ears" (Exod 13:4; 23:15; 34:18).
- **"of the flock and the herd" → "flock and herd"** (v2) — Hebrew צֹאן וּבָקָר, set beside "a Pesach" without a preposition. In Exod 12:3–5, 21 the Pesach is a sheep or a goat of the flock; here the herd is named with it. How the two fit (the herd for the feast sacrifices of the seven days, or a wider rule) is not said, and the verse keeps the words as they stand.
- **"to place his name there" → "to make his name abide there"** (v2, 6, 11) — Hebrew לְשַׁכֵּן שְׁמוֹ שָׁם, piel of שָׁכַן "abide" (the root of מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling").
- **"no leavened bread" → "what is leavened"; "the bread of affliction" kept** (v3) — Hebrew חָמֵץ (Exod 12:15; 13:3); "bread" is not in the word. לֶחֶם עֹנִי: עֳנִי is "affliction" (Exod 3:7). **"with it"** — Hebrew עָלָיו, literally "upon it" (Exod 23:18; 34:25). **"that thou mayest remember" → "so that you may remember"** — Hebrew לְמַעַן תִּזְכֹּר. **"the day when thou camest forth" → "the day of your coming out"** — Hebrew יוֹם צֵאתְךָ.
- **"there shall be no leavened bread seen with thee in all thy coast" → "leaven shall not be seen with you in all your territory"** (v4) — Hebrew שְׂאֹר "leaven", the sourdough (another word than חָמֵץ in v3), as Exod 13:7; גְּבוּל is "border", here the land within it. **"remain all night until the morning" → "lodge until the morning"** — Hebrew לִין "lodge, spend the night" (Exod 23:18; 34:25). **"the first day at even" → "in the evening on the first day"** — Hebrew בָּעֶרֶב בַּיּוֹם הָרִאשׁוֹן.
- **"Thou mayest not sacrifice" → "You cannot sacrifice"** (v5) — Hebrew לֹא תוּכַל, literally "you are not able": the verb of ability, used of what is not permitted (12:17; Gen 43:32). **"within any of thy gates" → "in one of your gates"** — Hebrew בְּאַחַד שְׁעָרֶיךָ.
- **"at even, at the going down of the sun, at the season that thou camest forth" → "in the evening, at the going in of the sun, the appointed time of your coming out"** (v6) — Hebrew כְּבוֹא הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ, literally "as the sun goes in"; מוֹעֵד "appointed time" (Exod 23:15), set beside it without a preposition. Exod 12:6; Lev 23:5; Num 9:3 have "between the two evenings", which is not written here. **"but at the place"** — Hebrew כִּי אִם־אֶל־הַמָּקוֹם, literally "except to the place". By the accents "there" goes with "you shall sacrifice", and "to make his name abide" stands without it.
- **"roast" → "boil"** (v7) — Hebrew בִּשֵּׁל, the usual word for cooking in water or in a pot (Exod 16:23; 29:31; Lev 8:31). Exod 12:9 says of the Pesach in Egypt that it is not to be eaten "boiled at all in water" (the same verb) but roasted with fire. The verb can be used more widely for "cook" (2 Chr 35:13 "boiled the Pesach with fire": cited from memory); the verse keeps the one rendering and the difference stays visible. No object is written: "it" is not supplied. **"which the LORD thy God shall choose"** — the Hebrew adds בּוֹ, "which he chooses [it]".
- **"a solemn assembly" kept** (v8) — Hebrew עֲצֶרֶת, as Lev 23:36; Num 29:35 of the eighth day of Sukkot; the sense is not certain (a day of holding back, or a closing gathering), and "solemn" is customary English, not a word of the Hebrew. Exod 12:16; Lev 23:8 call the seventh day "a holy convocation"; Exod 13:6 "a feast to Yahweh". **"Six days"** — Exod 12:15; 13:6; Lev 23:6 have "seven days you shall eat unleavened bread"; here six, and the seventh day named apart. **"thou shalt do no work therein" → "you shall do no work"** — "therein" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"number"; "from such time as thou beginnest to put the sickle to the corn" → "from the beginning of the sickle in the standing grain"** (v9) — Hebrew סָפַר "number" (Lev 23:15); מֵהָחֵל חֶרְמֵשׁ בַּקָּמָה, literally "from the sickle's beginning in the standing grain". Lev 23:15 numbers "from the morrow of the Shabbat … seven Shabbats"; here "seven weeks" (שָׁבֻעוֹת).
- **"keep the feast of weeks" → "make a Feast of Weeks"** (v10) — Hebrew וְעָשִׂיתָ חַג שָׁבֻעוֹת, without the article (Exod 34:22). **"with a tribute of a freewill offering of thine hand" → "by the measure of the freewill offering of your hand"** — Hebrew מִסַּת נִדְבַת יָדְךָ. מִסַּת is found only here and its meaning is not certain: "sufficiency, measure, proportion" is the best-supported sense; "by" is supplied. **"according as the LORD thy God hath blessed thee" → "as Yahweh your Elohim blesses you"** — Hebrew יְבָרֶכְךָ, an imperfect.
- **"manservant … maidservant" → "slave … slave woman"; "stranger" → "sojourner"** (v11, 14) — Hebrew עֶבֶד, אָמָה, גֵּר. Every member of the list is joined by וְ "and". **"the Levite"** — Hebrew הַלֵּוִי, singular.
- **"bondman" → "slave"; "observe and do" → "keep and do"** (v12) — Hebrew עֶבֶד; וְשָׁמַרְתָּ וְעָשִׂיתָ.
- **"observe the feast of tabernacles" → "the Feast of Sukkot you shall make for yourself"** (v13) — Hebrew חַג הַסֻּכֹּת תַּעֲשֶׂה לְךָ; סֻכּוֹת is "booths" (Lev 23:34, 42). **"after that thou hast gathered in thy corn and thy wine" → "when you gather in from your threshing floor and from your winepress"** — Hebrew בְּאָסְפְּךָ מִגָּרְנְךָ וּמִיִּקְבֶךָ; "corn" and "wine" are not written.
- **"keep a solemn feast" → "hold a feast"** (v15) — Hebrew תָּחֹג, the verb of חַג "feast". **"increase" → "yield"** — Hebrew תְּבוּאָה. **"thou shalt surely rejoice" → "you shall be only rejoicing"** — Hebrew וְהָיִיתָ אַךְ שָׂמֵחַ: אַךְ is "only, nothing but" (or "surely"); שָׂמֵחַ is the adjective of "rejoice" in v11, 14.
- **"appear before the LORD" → "appear before the face of Yahweh"** (v16) — Hebrew יֵרָאֶה … אֶת־פְּנֵי יְהוָה, as Exod 23:17; 34:23, 24. The verb is pointed as a nifal, "be seen, appear", though it is followed by אֶת־פְּנֵי; the same letters can be read as a qal, "see the face of Yahweh", and many hold that this was the older reading. The verse follows the pointed text. **"all thy males"** — Hebrew כָל־זְכוּרְךָ. **"they shall not appear" → "none shall appear"** — Hebrew וְלֹא יֵרָאֶה, singular; Exod 23:15; 34:20 have the plural "they shall not appear before my face empty". **"three times"** — Hebrew שָׁלוֹשׁ פְּעָמִים (Exod 23:17); Exod 23:14 has רְגָלִים.
- **"every man shall give as he is able" → "each man according to the gift of his hand"** (v17) — Hebrew אִישׁ כְּמַתְּנַת יָדוֹ; there is no verb.
- **"shalt thou make thee" → "you shall set for yourself"; "throughout thy tribes" → "for your tribes"; "with just judgment" → "with a judgment of righteousness"** (v18) — Hebrew תִּתֶּן־לְךָ, literally "you shall give for yourself", the verb of "is giving to you" in the same verse; לִשְׁבָטֶיךָ; מִשְׁפַּט־צֶדֶק. **"officers"** — Hebrew שֹׁטְרִים (Exod 5:6; Num 11:16), lower officials who keep order and lists.
- **"wrest judgment" → "turn aside judgment"** (v19) — Hebrew לֹא־תַטֶּה מִשְׁפָּט (Exod 23:6): to bend it from its course, pervert it. **"respect persons" → "recognize faces"** — Hebrew לֹא תַכִּיר פָּנִים, literally "you shall not recognize a face": to regard who a man is, to be partial (1:17). **"a gift doth blind the eyes of the wise, and pervert the words of the righteous" → "the bribe blinds the eyes of the wise and overturns the words of the righteous"** — Hebrew שֹׁחַד, סִלֵּף; the sentence is that of Exod 23:8, with "the eyes of the wise" for "the open-eyed".
- **"That which is altogether just shalt thou follow" → "Righteousness, righteousness you shall pursue"** (v20) — Hebrew צֶדֶק צֶדֶק תִּרְדֹּף: the noun of v18 said twice. **"inherit" → "take possession of"** — Hebrew יָרַשׁ.
- **"a grove of any trees" → "an Asherah, any tree"** (v21) — Hebrew אֲשֵׁרָה כָּל־עֵץ: the word is kept (a wooden object of worship bearing the goddess's name). עֵץ is "tree" and "wood": "an Asherah of any wood" is also possible. **"near unto" → "beside"** — Hebrew אֵצֶל.
- **"Neither shalt thou set thee up any image" → "And you shall not raise up for yourself a pillar"** (v22) — Hebrew מַצֵּבָה, a stone set up; הֵקִים "raise up, make stand".

### Notes
- **The three feasts** (v16) — Matzot, Weeks and Sukkot, as Exod 23:14–17; 34:18–23; in fuller form Lev 23; Num 28–29. Here the place is named each time: "the place that Yahweh will choose" (v2, 6, 7, 11, 15, 16).
- **Pesach and the place** — Exod 12 has the Pesach kept by houses in Egypt; here it is sacrificed "in the place that Yahweh will choose" and not "in one of your gates" (v5–6). Differences from Exod 12 in the wording (flock and herd, "boil", six days and the seventh) are left standing in the translation; see Changes on v2, 7, 8.
- **Who rejoices** (v11, 14) — son and daughter, slave and slave woman, the Levite, the sojourner, the fatherless and the widow are named with the householder.
- **"Bread of affliction"** (v3) — the unleavened bread, tied here to the haste of the coming out (Exod 12:11, 39).
- **"Righteousness, righteousness"** (v20) — the repeated noun is commonly taken as emphasis; an old Jewish reading takes it as righteousness sought by righteous means.
- **Asherah and pillar** (v21–22) — things of the worship of the nations (7:5; 12:3). A pillar was set up by Jacob (Gen 28:18) and by Moses (Exod 24:4); here it is what Yahweh hates.
