# Deuteronomy 16

[1] "Observe the month of Aviv and keep the Passover to Yahweh your *Elohim*; for in the month of Aviv, Yahweh your *Elohim* brought you out of Egypt by night.

[2] You shall sacrifice the Passover to Yahweh your *Elohim* — from the flock and the herd — at the place that Yahweh will choose to make his name dwell there.

[3] You shall eat no leavened bread with it; for seven days you shall eat unleavened bread with it — bread of affliction — for you came out of the land of Egypt in haste. Thus you shall remember the day when you came out of the land of Egypt all the days of your life.

[4] No leaven shall be seen with you in all your territory for seven days; and none of the flesh that you sacrifice in the evening of the first day shall remain until morning.

[5] You are not allowed to sacrifice the Passover within any of your gates that Yahweh your *Elohim* is giving you;

[6] but at the place that Yahweh your *Elohim* will choose to make his name dwell there, you shall sacrifice the Passover, in the evening, at sunset, at the season you came out of Egypt.

[7] You shall cook and eat it at the place that Yahweh your *Elohim* will choose; and in the morning you shall turn and go to your tents.

[8] Six days you shall eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day there shall be a solemn assembly to Yahweh your *Elohim*; you shall do no work.

[9] You shall count seven weeks for yourself; from the time you begin to put the sickle to the standing grain you shall begin to count seven weeks.

[10] Then you shall keep the Feast of Weeks to Yahweh your *Elohim* with a voluntary tribute of your hand, which you shall give as Yahweh your *Elohim* has blessed you.

[11] You shall rejoice before Yahweh your *Elohim* — you and your son and your daughter, your male servant and your female servant, the Levite within your gates, and the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow who are among you — at the place that Yahweh your *Elohim* will choose to make his name dwell there.

[12] You shall remember that you were a slave in Egypt, and you shall be careful to do these statutes.

[13] You shall keep the Feast of Booths seven days, when you gather in the yield from your threshing floor and your winepress.

[14] You shall rejoice in your feast — you and your son and your daughter, your male servant and your female servant, the Levite, the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow who are within your gates.

[15] Seven days you shall keep the feast to Yahweh your *Elohim* at the place that Yahweh will choose, because Yahweh your *Elohim* will bless you in all your produce and in all the work of your hands, so that you will be altogether joyful.

[16] Three times in the year every male among you shall appear before Yahweh your *Elohim* at the place he will choose: at the Feast of Unleavened Bread, at the Feast of Weeks, and at the Feast of Booths. They shall not appear before Yahweh empty-handed.

[17] Each shall give as he is able, according to the blessing of Yahweh your *Elohim* that he has given you.

[18] You shall appoint judges and officers for yourselves in all your gates that Yahweh your *Elohim* is giving you, throughout your tribes; they shall judge the people with righteous judgment.

[19] You shall not distort justice; you shall not show partiality; you shall not take a bribe, for a bribe blinds the eyes of the wise and twists the words of the righteous.

[20] Justice, justice you shall pursue, that you may live and inherit the land that Yahweh your *Elohim* is giving you.

[21] You shall not plant for yourself any kind of tree as an Asherah beside the altar of Yahweh your *Elohim* that you shall make.

[22] You shall not set up for yourself a pillar, which Yahweh your *Elohim* hates.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"feast of tabernacles" → "Feast of Booths"** (v13, 16) — *Sukkot*, as in Lev 23.
- **"corn" → "standing grain / grain"** (v9).
- **"coast" → "territory"** (v4) — archaic.
- **"wrest judgment" → "distort justice"** (v19) — Hebrew לֹא־תַטֶּה מִשְׁפָּט *lo-tatteh mishpat*. Not to twist / bend.
- **"gift" → "bribe"** (v19) — Hebrew שֹׁחַד *shochad*.
- **"just / altogether just" → "justice, justice"** (v20) — Hebrew צֶדֶק צֶדֶק תִּרְדֹּף *tsedeq tsedeq tirdof*. The doubled *tsedeq* is emphatic / intensive, famously rendered "justice, justice you shall pursue."
- **"image" → "pillar"** (v22) — Hebrew *matzevah*. Cultic standing-stone.

### Notes
- **Three pilgrim feasts** (v16) — the three feasts requiring all males at the central sanctuary: Passover/Unleavened Bread (spring), Weeks (Pentecost, 7 weeks later), Booths (autumn). The foundational Jewish pilgrimage-calendar.
- **Passover centralization** — in Ex 12, Passover was celebrated family by family in homes. Deut centralizes: Passover must be sacrificed at the sanctuary. Later, sacrificed at the Temple; with the Temple's destruction (70 CE), home-celebration resumed.
- **Inclusion of marginalized** (v11, 14) — repeated pattern: male servant, female servant, Levite, foreigner, orphan, widow. The feasts are celebrations of all society, not just propertied men.
- **"Bread of affliction"** (v3) — the unleavened bread as memorial of hurried exit from slavery. Passover seders keep this phrase.
- **"Justice, justice pursue"** (v20) — foundational judicial verse. The doubling suggests unrelenting or doubly-pure pursuit. Famously applied by rabbinic tradition to mean "pursue justice by just means."
- **Anti-asherah / anti-matzevah** (v21–22) — Canaanite cultic elements. The standing stone (*matzevah*) was permissible before (Gen 28:18, Jacob), but now forbidden in Israelite worship.
