## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"feast of tabernacles" → "Feast of Booths"** (v13, 16) — *Sukkot*, as in Lev 23.
- **"corn" → "standing grain / grain"** (v9).
- **"coast" → "territory"** (v4) — archaic.
- **"wrest judgment" → "distort justice"** (v19) — Hebrew לֹא־תַטֶּה מִשְׁפָּט *lo-tatteh mishpat*. Not to twist / bend.
- **"gift" → "bribe"** (v19) — Hebrew שֹׁחַד *shochad*.
- **"just / altogether just" → "justice, justice"** (v20) — Hebrew צֶדֶק צֶדֶק תִּרְדֹּף *tsedeq tsedeq tirdof*. The doubled *tsedeq* is emphatic / intensive, famously rendered "justice, justice you shall pursue."
- **"image" → "pillar"** (v22) — Hebrew *matzevah*. Cultic standing-stone.

### Notes
- **Three pilgrim feasts** (v16) — the three feasts requiring all males at the central sanctuary: Passover/Unleavened Bread (spring), Weeks (Pentecost, 7 weeks later), Booths (autumn). The foundational Jewish pilgrimage-calendar.
- **Passover centralization** — in Ex 12, Passover was celebrated family by family in homes. Deut centralizes: Passover must be sacrificed at the sanctuary. Later, sacrificed at the Temple; with the Temple's destruction (70 CE), home-celebration resumed.
- **Inclusion of marginalized** (v11, 14) — repeated pattern: male servant, female servant, Levite, foreigner, orphan, widow. The feasts are celebrations of all society, not just propertied men.
- **"Bread of affliction"** (v3) — the unleavened bread as memorial of hurried exit from slavery. Passover seders keep this phrase.
- **"Justice, justice pursue"** (v20) — foundational judicial verse. The doubling suggests unrelenting or doubly-pure pursuit. Famously applied by rabbinic tradition to mean "pursue justice by just means."
- **Anti-asherah / anti-matzevah** (v21–22) — Canaanite cultic elements. The standing stone (*matzevah*) was permissible before (Gen 28:18, Jacob), but now forbidden in Israelite worship.
