# Numbers 28

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Command the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and say to them: 'My offering, my bread for my offerings by fire, my soothing smell, you shall keep, to bring near to me at its appointed time.'

[3] And you shall say to them: 'This is the offering by fire that you shall bring near to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: lambs a year old, without blemish, two each day, as a burnt offering continually.

[4] The one lamb you shall offer in the morning, and the second lamb you shall offer between the two evenings;

[5] and a tenth of the ephah of fine flour for a grain offering, mixed with a fourth of the hin of beaten oil.

[6] It is a continual burnt offering, which was offered at Mount Sinai, for a soothing smell, an offering by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] And its drink offering: a fourth of the hin for the one lamb; in the Holy pour out a drink offering of strong drink to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[8] And the second lamb you shall offer between the two evenings; like the grain offering of the morning and like its drink offering you shall offer: an offering by fire of a soothing smell to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[9] And on the day of the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧: two lambs a year old, without blemish, and two tenths of fine flour, a grain offering mixed with oil, and its drink offering.

[10] The burnt offering of ⟦shabbat|a Shabbat|a Sabbath⟧ on its ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, in addition to the continual burnt offering and its drink offering.

[11] And at the beginnings of your months you shall bring near a burnt offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: two bulls of the herd and one ram, seven lambs a year old, without blemish;

[12] and three tenths of fine flour, a grain offering mixed with oil, for the one bull; and two tenths of fine flour, a grain offering mixed with oil, for the one ram;

[13] and a tenth each of fine flour, a grain offering mixed with oil, for the one lamb: a burnt offering, a soothing smell, an offering by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[14] And their drink offerings shall be half of the hin for the bull, and a third of the hin for the ram, and a fourth of the hin for the lamb: wine. This is the burnt offering of a month in its month, for the months of the year.

[15] And one male goat for a sin offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; in addition to the continual burnt offering it shall be offered, and its drink offering.

[16] And in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, is a ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[17] And on the fifteenth day of this month is a feast; seven days unleavened bread shall be eaten.

[18] On the first day is a holy convocation; no work of service shall you do.

[19] And you shall bring near an offering by fire, a burnt offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: two bulls of the herd and one ram, and seven lambs a year old; without blemish they shall be for you.

[20] And their grain offering: fine flour mixed with oil; three tenths for the bull and two tenths for the ram you shall offer;

[21] a tenth each you shall offer for the one lamb, for the seven lambs;

[22] and one male goat of sin offering, to make atonement for you.

[23] Besides the burnt offering of the morning, which is for the continual burnt offering, you shall offer these.

[24] Like these you shall offer each day, seven days: bread of an offering by fire of a soothing smell to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; in addition to the continual burnt offering it shall be offered, and its drink offering.

[25] And on the seventh day you shall have a holy convocation; no work of service shall you do.

[26] And on the day of the firstfruits, when you bring near a new grain offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, in your ⟦weeks|Shavuot|Weeks⟧, you shall have a holy convocation; no work of service shall you do.

[27] And you shall bring near a burnt offering for a soothing smell to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: two bulls of the herd, one ram, seven lambs a year old;

[28] and their grain offering: fine flour mixed with oil; three tenths for the one bull, two tenths for the one ram;

[29] a tenth each for the one lamb, for the seven lambs;

[30] one male goat, to make atonement for you.

[31] Besides the continual burnt offering and its grain offering you shall offer — without blemish they shall be for you — and their drink offerings.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew וַ / וְ "and" at the head of a clause is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"My offering, and my bread for my sacrifices made by fire, for a sweet savour unto me, shall ye observe to offer unto me in their due season" → "My offering, my bread for my offerings by fire, my soothing smell, you shall keep, to bring near to me at its appointed time"** (v2) — Hebrew קָרְבָּנִי, "what is brought near to me"; לַחְמִי, "my bread" (Lev 3:11; 21:6); לְאִשַּׁי: אִשֶּׁה is "offering by fire" (commonly taken from אֵשׁ "fire"; the derivation is not certain); רֵיחַ נִיחֹחִי, literally "the smell of my soothing" (נִיחֹחַ from נוּחַ "rest"); תִּשְׁמְרוּ "you shall keep"; לְהַקְרִיב "to bring near"; בְּמוֹעֲדוֹ, "at its appointed time" (singular), the word of Lev 23:2. "To bring near" has no object in the Hebrew.
- **"the offering made by fire which ye shall offer" → "the offering by fire that you shall bring near"** (v3; so v11, 19, 26, 27) — Hebrew הִקְרִיב "bring near"; "offer" in this chapter is עָשָׂה, literally "do, make" (v4, 6, 8, 15, 20, 21, 23, 24, 31). **"two lambs of the first year without spot day by day, for a continual burnt offering" → "lambs a year old, without blemish, two each day, as a burnt offering continually"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי־שָׁנָה, literally "sons of a year"; תְּמִימִם, "whole, complete"; עֹלָה תָמִיד: here תָמִיד stands as an adverb, "continually" (Exod 29:38); in v6, 10, 15, 23, 24, 31 it is עֹלַת (הַ)תָּמִיד, "the continual burnt offering", literally "the burnt offering of continuity": regular, never left off.
- **"the other lamb shalt thou offer at even" → "the second lamb you shall offer between the two evenings"** (v4, 8) — Hebrew הַשֵּׁנִי "the second"; בֵּין הָעַרְבָּיִם, a dual, literally so; which hours are meant is disputed (the dusk between sunset and dark, or the time from the sun's decline to sunset). The wording is that of Exod 29:39.
- **"a tenth part of an ephah of flour for a meat offering, mingled with the fourth part of an hin of beaten oil" → "a tenth of the ephah of fine flour for a grain offering, mixed with a fourth of the hin of beaten oil"** (v5) — Hebrew עֲשִׂירִית הָאֵיפָה: the ephah is named here, and only here in the chapter; elsewhere the Hebrew has only "tenths" (עֶשְׂרֹנִים), and "of an ephah" is not added in the verse. סֹלֶת "fine flour"; מִנְחָה "grain offering"; רְבִיעִת הַהִין as Lev 23:13.
- **"which was ordained in mount Sinai for a sweet savour, a sacrifice made by fire" → "which was offered at Mount Sinai, for a soothing smell, an offering by fire"** (v6) — Hebrew הָעֲשֻׂיָה, the passive of עָשָׂה "do, make", the verb "offer" of v4: "that was made / offered"; "ordained" is another way of taking it. "It is" is supplied.
- **"the drink offering thereof shall be the fourth part of an hin for the one lamb: in the holy place shalt thou cause the strong wine to be poured unto the LORD for a drink offering" → "its drink offering: a fourth of the hin for the one lamb; in the Holy pour out a drink offering of strong drink to Yahweh"** (v7) — Hebrew נֶסֶךְ "drink offering" and הַסֵּךְ "pour out", of one root; בַּקֹּדֶשׁ, "in the Holy" ("place" is not in the Hebrew); שֵׁכָר is "strong drink" (Lev 10:9), where Exod 29:40 and v14 have "wine" (יַיִן).
- **"as the meat offering of the morning, and as the drink offering thereof, thou shalt offer it, a sacrifice made by fire, of a sweet savour" → "like the grain offering of the morning and like its drink offering you shall offer: an offering by fire of a soothing smell"** (v8) — the Hebrew has no "it"; אִשֵּׁה רֵיחַ נִיחֹחַ as Lev 1:9.
- **"two tenth deals of flour for a meat offering, mingled with oil, and the drink offering thereof" → "two tenths of fine flour, a grain offering mixed with oil, and its drink offering"** (v9; so v12, 13) — Hebrew סֹלֶת מִנְחָה בְּלוּלָה בַשֶּׁמֶן. The verse has no verb.
- **"the burnt offering of every sabbath, beside the continual burnt offering" → "The burnt offering of a Shabbat on its Shabbat, in addition to the continual burnt offering"** (v10) — Hebrew עֹלַת שַׁבַּת בְּשַׁבַּתּוֹ, literally so. עַל, literally "upon", is "in addition to" (v10, 15, 24); "besides" is kept for מִלְּבַד (v23, 31).
- **"in the beginnings of your months ye shall offer … two young bullocks, and one ram, seven lambs of the first year without spot" → "at the beginnings of your months you shall bring near … two bulls of the herd and one ram, seven lambs a year old, without blemish"** (v11) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי חָדְשֵׁיכֶם, literally "the heads of your months" (10:10): the first day of each month. פָּרִים בְּנֵי־בָקָר, literally "bulls, sons of the herd"; the phrase says nothing of age. The numbers are put before their nouns, in ordinary English order (the Hebrew has "bulls of the herd, two").
- **"a several tenth deal of flour mingled with oil for a meat offering unto one lamb" → "a tenth each of fine flour, a grain offering mixed with oil, for the one lamb"** (v13, 21, 29) — Hebrew עִשָּׂרֹן עִשָּׂרוֹן, literally "a tenth, a tenth". **"for a burnt offering of a sweet savour, a sacrifice made by fire" → "a burnt offering, a soothing smell, an offering by fire"** (v13).
- **"their drink offerings shall be half an hin of wine unto a bullock … this is the burnt offering of every month throughout the months of the year" → "their drink offerings shall be half of the hin for the bull … : wine. This is the burnt offering of a month in its month, for the months of the year"** (v14) — "wine" stands at the end in the Hebrew; עֹלַת חֹדֶשׁ בְּחָדְשׁוֹ, literally so.
- **"one kid of the goats for a sin offering" → "one male goat for a sin offering"** (v15, 30) — Hebrew שְׂעִיר עִזִּים, a he-goat; חַטָּאת is the one word "sin". In v22 it is שְׂעִיר חַטָּאת, "a male goat of sin offering". **"beside the continual burnt offering, and his drink offering" → "in addition to the continual burnt offering it shall be offered, and its drink offering"** (v15, 24) — the Hebrew order.
- **"in the fourteenth day of the first month is the passover of the LORD" → "in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, is a Pesach to Yahweh"** (v16) — Hebrew פֶּסַח לַיהוָה, as Lev 23:5; "is" is supplied.
- **"in the fifteenth day of this month is the feast" → "on the fifteenth day of this month is a feast"** (v17) — Hebrew חָג, without the article. מַצּוֹת, "unleavened bread", is a plural: flat cakes baked without leaven.
- **"In the first day shall be an holy convocation; ye shall do no manner of servile work therein" → "On the first day is a holy convocation; no work of service shall you do"** (v18, 25, 26) — Hebrew מִקְרָא־קֹדֶשׁ, literally "a calling of holiness", and מְלֶאכֶת עֲבֹדָה, literally "work of service", as Lev 23:7–8. "Therein" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"they shall be unto you without blemish" → "without blemish they shall be for you"** (v19, 31) — the Hebrew order.
- **"three tenth deals shall ye offer for a bullock, and two tenth deals for a ram" → "three tenths for the bull and two tenths for the ram you shall offer"** (v20) — the verb is plural here and singular in v21.
- **"one goat for a sin offering" → "one male goat of sin offering"** (v22) — see v15.
- **"Ye shall offer these beside the burnt offering in the morning, which is for a continual burnt offering" → "Besides the burnt offering of the morning, which is for the continual burnt offering, you shall offer these"** (v23) — the Hebrew order.
- **"After this manner ye shall offer daily, throughout the seven days, the meat of the sacrifice made by fire, of a sweet savour" → "Like these you shall offer each day, seven days: bread of an offering by fire of a soothing smell"** (v24) — Hebrew כָּאֵלֶּה, "like these"; לַיּוֹם as v3; לֶחֶם אִשֵּׁה, "bread of an offering by fire" (Lev 3:11).
- **"in the day of the firstfruits, when ye bring a new meat offering unto the LORD, after your weeks be out" → "on the day of the firstfruits, when you bring near a new grain offering to Yahweh, in your Shavuot"** (v26) — Hebrew בְּשָׁבֻעֹתֵיכֶם, literally "in your weeks": the Feast of Weeks (*Shavuot*, Exod 34:22; Deut 16:10) is meant; the word "feast" is not in the Hebrew here. מִנְחָה חֲדָשָׁה as Lev 23:16.
- **"ye shall offer the burnt offering for a sweet savour" → "you shall bring near a burnt offering for a soothing smell"** (v27).
- **"one kid of the goats, to make an atonement for you" → "one male goat, to make atonement for you"** (v30) — the Hebrew has no "and" here, and no "sin offering".
- **"Ye shall offer them beside the continual burnt offering, and his meat offering, (they shall be unto you without blemish) and their drink offerings" → "Besides the continual burnt offering and its grain offering you shall offer — without blemish they shall be for you — and their drink offerings"** (v31) — the Hebrew order, word for word; תַּעֲשׂוּ "you shall offer" has no object of its own, and "and their drink offerings" follows the clause "without blemish they shall be for you".

### Notes
- **Offerings at the appointed times** — ch 28–29 give the offerings for each appointed time; Lev 23 gives the times themselves. The wording of the daily offering (v3–8) is that of Exod 29:38–42.
- **Order of the chapter** — each day (v3–8); the Shabbat (v9–10); the beginnings of the months (v11–15); Pesach and the seven days of unleavened bread (v16–25); the day of the firstfruits (v26–31). Each is offered in addition to the continual burnt offering.
- **Measures** — "tenths" are tenths of the ephah (v5); the flour and the wine for each animal are those of 15:4–10.
- **The day of the firstfruits** (v26–31) — the fiftieth day of Lev 23:15–21. The animals here (two bulls, one ram, seven lambs) differ from those of Lev 23:18 (one bull, two rams, seven lambs).
- **A male goat with each feast** (v15, 22, 30) — a sin offering, "to make atonement for you".
- **"My bread"** (v2) — the offerings are called the bread of Yahweh (Lev 21:6; cf. Ps 50:12–13).
