## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew יהוה, the Tetragrammaton.
- **Name of book** — Hebrew בְּמִדְבַּר *Ba-midbar* = "In the wilderness," taken from v1. English "Numbers" (LXX *Arithmoi*) after the census chapters.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"tabernacle of the congregation" → "tent of meeting"** (v1) — Hebrew אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד. **"on the first day of the second month, in the second year after they were come out" → "on the first of the second month, in the second year of their going out"** (v1, 18) — Hebrew בְּאֶחָד לַחֹדֶשׁ, without the word "day"; לְצֵאתָם, an infinitive, "of their going out".
- **"Take ye the sum of" → "Lift up the head of"** (v2, 49) — Hebrew נָשָׂא אֶת־רֹאשׁ, the idiom for counting people, kept as in Exod 30:12. The command is plural (שְׂאוּ): Moses and Aaron.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי; so also "the sons of Reuben" and the rest.
- **"after their families, by the house of their fathers" → "by their clans, by their fathers' houses"** (v2, 18, 20–42) — Hebrew לְמִשְׁפְּחֹתָם לְבֵית אֲבֹתָם: מִשְׁפָּחָה is the kin group between the tribe and the father's house; בֵּית אֲבֹתָם is literally "the house of their fathers", the plural of "father's house". One wording through chapters 1–4. In v18 the preposition is עַל ("according to their clans"), elsewhere לְ.
- **"with the number of their names" → "by the number of names"** (v2, 18, 20–42) — Hebrew בְּמִסְפַּר שֵׁמוֹת; no "their".
- **"by their polls" → "head by head"** (v2, 18, 20, 22) — Hebrew לְגֻלְגְּלֹתָם, literally "by their skulls": another word than the "head" (רֹאשׁ) of v2. It stands in v2, 18, 20, 22 and not in v24–42.
- **"every male" kept** (v2, 20, 22) — Hebrew כָּל־זָכָר; זָכָר is "male", not אִישׁ "man".
- **"from twenty years old and upward" kept** (v3, 18, 20–45) — Hebrew מִבֶּן עֶשְׂרִים שָׁנָה וָמַעְלָה, literally "from a son of twenty years and upward".
- **"all that are able to go forth to war" → "everyone who goes out to the host"** (v3, 20–45) — Hebrew כָּל־יֹצֵא צָבָא, literally "everyone going out, host": צָבָא is an army, an ordered multitude (the "hosts" of "Yahweh of hosts"). "Able" and "war" are not in the Hebrew. **"by their armies" → "by their hosts"** (v3, 52) — Hebrew לְצִבְאֹתָם, the same word, as Exod 6:26; 12:51.
- **"number"; "those that were numbered"** (v3, 19, 21–46, 49) — Hebrew פָּקַד and its passive participle פְּקֻדִים: to muster, to pass in review and count. It is the verb "visit" of Gen 21:1; in Exod 30:12–14; 38:25–26 it is "number" as here. פְּקֻדֵיהֶם is "those of them who were numbered"; in v22 the Hebrew adds פְּקֻדָיו, "those of his who were numbered" (the suffix is singular: Simeon), which no other tribe has. In v47 "were not numbered" is הָתְפָּקְדוּ, a rare passive-reflexive form of the same verb (again in 2:33).
- **"of every tribe a man" → "a man for each tribe"** (v4) — Hebrew אִישׁ אִישׁ לַמַּטֶּה, literally "a man, a man for the tribe". מַטֶּה is literally "staff"; as a kin group it is "tribe", like שֵׁבֶט ("rod"; 4:18). **"every one head of the house of his fathers" → "each man is the head of his fathers' house"** — Hebrew אִישׁ רֹאשׁ לְבֵית־אֲבֹתָיו הוּא.
- **"of the tribe of Reuben; Elizur" → "For Reuben, Elizur"** (v5–15) — Hebrew לִרְאוּבֵן, "for / belonging to Reuben"; "tribe" is not written. The same לְ opens v22–40 ("For the sons of Simeon") and stands in "for the tribe of Reuben" (v21–43); "by" in "by their clans" is the same preposition.
- **"the renowned of the congregation" → "the called ones of the congregation"** (v16) — Hebrew qere קְרוּאֵי, passive participle of קָרָא "call": those called, summoned. The written form (ketiv קריאי) is an adjective of the same root and sense.
- **"princes" → "chieftains"** (v16, 44) — Hebrew נְשִׂיאִים, literally "ones lifted up". **"heads of thousands in Israel" → "the heads of the thousands of Israel"** (v16) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי אַלְפֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל; אֶלֶף is "thousand", also used of a division of a tribe (Judg 6:15).
- **"which are expressed by their names" → "who had been designated by names"** (v17) — Hebrew נִקְּבוּ בְּשֵׁמוֹת; נָקַב is "pierce, mark out"; of a name spoken it is "pronounce" (Lev 24:11, 16), which English cannot say of men: here they are marked out by name.
- **"they declared their pedigrees after their families" → "they declared their births according to their clans"** (v18) — Hebrew וַיִּתְיַלְדוּ, a reflexive of יָלַד "bear, beget" found only here: they had themselves entered by birth. The exact sense is not certain.
- **"so he numbered them" → "and he numbered them"** (v19) — Hebrew וַיִּפְקְדֵם; "as Yahweh had commanded Moses" may be read with v18 or with what follows.
- **"by their generations" → "their generations"** (v20–42) — Hebrew תּוֹלְדֹתָם, without a preposition: the word of "These are the generations of" (Gen 2:4; 5:1), from יָלַד "beget": those begotten of them. The sentence of v20 begins "And the sons of Reuben … were" (וַיִּהְיוּ) and is taken up again in v21; v22–40 begin "For the sons of …" (לִבְנֵי), and v42 has "The sons of Naphtali" without לְ. These differences are kept. The Samaritan text and the Septuagint are said to have "for" in v42 also (from memory).
- **"threescore and fourteen thousand and six hundred" → "seventy-four thousand six hundred"** (v21–46) — the numbers are in words, in ordinary English order; the Hebrew has "six and forty thousand and five hundred", and so on.
- **"each one was for the house of his fathers" → "they were one man each for his fathers' house"** (v44) — Hebrew אִישׁ־אֶחָד לְבֵית־אֲבֹתָיו הָיוּ.
- **"So were all those that were numbered … Even all they that were numbered were" → "And all those who were numbered … were — and all those who were numbered were"** (v45, 46) — the Hebrew begins twice with וַיִּהְיוּ "and they were"; the sentence of v45 is taken up again in v46.
- **"after the tribe of their fathers" → "by the tribe of their fathers"** (v47) — Hebrew לְמַטֵּה אֲבֹתָם. **"among them" → "in their midst"** (v47, 49) — Hebrew בְּתוֹךְ.
- **"For the LORD had spoken" → "And Yahweh spoke"** (v48) — Hebrew וַיְדַבֵּר, the ordinary narrative form; "for … had spoken" makes it an explanation of v47, which the Hebrew does not say.
- **"neither take the sum of them" → "and their head you shall not lift up"** (v49) — see v2.
- **"thou shalt appoint the Levites over the tabernacle of testimony" → "And you, appoint the Levites over the dwelling of the testimony"** (v50, 53) — Hebrew הַפְקֵד … עַל, the causative of the verb "number" of this chapter (as Gen 39:4); מִשְׁכַּן הָעֵדֻת: מִשְׁכָּן is "dwelling", from שָׁכַן "abide"; עֵדוּת is "testimony", the tablets in the ark (Exod 25:16; 38:21). **"vessels" → "utensils"** — Hebrew כֵּלִים, any made articles. **"minister unto it"** — Hebrew שֵׁרֵת.
- **"when the tabernacle setteth forward, the Levites shall take it down: and when the tabernacle is to be pitched, the Levites shall set it up" → "when the dwelling sets out, the Levites shall bring it down; and when the dwelling camps, the Levites shall raise it up"** (v51) — Hebrew נָסַע, literally "pull up" (the tent pegs); הוֹרִיד "bring down"; חָנָה "camp", said of the dwelling itself as of the people (v50, 52, 53); הֵקִים "raise up", as Exod 40:2, 18.
- **"the stranger that cometh nigh" → "the stranger who comes near"** (v51) — Hebrew הַזָּר הַקָּרֵב: זָר is one who does not belong, here anyone who is not a Levite.
- **"every man by his own camp, and every man by his own standard" → "each man at his camp and each man at his standard"** (v52) — Hebrew עַל. דֶּגֶל is "standard" in chapters 1–2 and 10: a banner, and the body of men that camps under it (2:3). The sense "division of an army" is also defended; the word is not certain.
- **"that there be no wrath" → "and there shall be no wrath"** (v53) — Hebrew וְלֹא־יִהְיֶה קֶצֶף, joined by "and".
- **"shall keep the charge of"** (v53) — Hebrew שָׁמַר מִשְׁמֶרֶת, verb and noun of one root: "keep the keeping": what is given to be kept or guarded.
- **"And the children of Israel did according to all that the LORD commanded Moses, so did they" → "And the sons of Israel did; according to all that Yahweh had commanded Moses, so they did"** (v54) — the accents divide the verse after "did", as in Exod 40:16.

### Notes

- **First census** — conducted on the first of the second month of the second year after the exodus, one month after the dwelling was raised up (Exod 40:17). The generation counted here dies in the wilderness; Num 26 numbers the new generation.
- **Total 603,550** (v46) — the same figure as Exod 38:26. It implies a total population around 2 million. Numbers much debated: some scholars read *eleph* ("thousand") as a clan / military unit rather than a literal thousand, yielding smaller totals. The text itself presents the large number.
- **Twelve tribes, Levi excluded** — Levi set apart for the service of the dwelling; Joseph is counted as Ephraim and Manasseh, so that there are twelve tribes in the numbering.
- **Order of the tribes** — in v5–15 Gad stands eleventh; in v20–43 Gad stands third, after Reuben and Simeon, with whom he camps (2:10–14).
- **Levite role** (v50–53) — to carry the dwelling, to bring it down and raise it up, and to camp around it, between the dwelling and the camps of Israel.
- **Frame of Numbers** — opens with the numbering of the first wilderness generation, narrates journey and rebellion, leading to their death; then the numbering (ch 26) of the new generation that will enter the land.
