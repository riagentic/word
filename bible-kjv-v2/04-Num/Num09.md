# Numbers 9

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ in the wilderness of Sinai, in the second year of their going out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, in the first month, saying,

[2] "And let the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ make the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ at its appointed time.

[3] On the fourteenth day in this month, between the two evenings, you shall make it at its appointed time; according to all its statutes and according to all its ordinances you shall make it."

[4] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ spoke to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to make the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧.

[5] And they made the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, between the two evenings, in the wilderness of Sinai; according to all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, so the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ did.

[6] And there were men who were unclean by the soul of a man, and they were not able to make the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ on that day; and they came near before ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and before ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ on that day.

[7] And those men said to him, "We are unclean by the soul of a man; why should we be diminished, so as not to bring near the offering of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ at its appointed time in the midst of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?"

[8] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said to them, "Stand, and let me hear what ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will command for you."

[9] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[10] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying: 'Any man, when he is unclean by a soul, or on a far way, of you or of your generations, shall make a ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[11] In the second month, on the fourteenth day, between the two evenings, they shall make it; with unleavened bread and bitter herbs they shall eat it.

[12] They shall not leave any of it until morning, and a bone they shall not break in it; according to all the statute of the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ they shall make it.

[13] And the man who is clean and was not on a way, and forbears to make the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧: that soul shall be cut off from its peoples; for the offering of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ he did not bring near at its appointed time; his sin that man shall bear.

[14] And when a sojourner sojourns with you and makes a ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, according to the statute of the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ and according to its ordinance, so he shall do. [15] And on the day of the raising up of the dwelling the cloud covered the dwelling, the tent of the testimony; and in the evening it would be on the dwelling as the appearance of fire, until morning.

[16] So it would be continually: the cloud would cover it, and the appearance of fire by night.

[17] And according as the cloud was taken up from over the tent, after that the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ would set out; and in the place where the cloud would abide, there the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ would camp.

[18] At the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ would set out, and at the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ they would camp; all the days that the cloud would abide on the dwelling they would camp.

[23] At the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ they would camp, and at the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ they would set out; the charge of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ they kept, at the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew יהוה, the Tetragrammaton.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept, also at the head of the speech in v2.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"in the first month of the second year after they were come out" → "in the second year of their going out … in the first month"** (v1) — the Hebrew order, as 1:1; this word comes a month before the numbering of 1:1.
- **"keep the passover" → "make the *Pesach*"** (v2–6, 10–14) — Hebrew עָשָׂה "do, make", one verb throughout, as Exod 12:47, 48; in v14 "so he shall do" is the same verb. פֶּסַח names the animal, the meal and the feast alike; nothing is added to it. The verb פָּסַח is "pass over" (Exod 12:13).
- **"in his appointed season" → "at its appointed time"** (v2, 3, 7, 13) — Hebrew מוֹעֵד.
- **"at even" → "between the two evenings"** (v3, 5, 11) — Hebrew בֵּין הָעַרְבַּיִם, a dual, as Exod 12:6. Which hours are meant (dusk, or from the sun's decline to sunset) is disputed.
- **"rites … ceremonies" → "statutes … ordinances"** (v3, 12, 14) — Hebrew חֻקָּה and מִשְׁפָּט (the word "judgment": a ruling). In v12 the Hebrew is singular, "all the statute".
- **"that they should keep" → "to make"** (v4) — Hebrew לַעֲשֹׂת.
- **"on the fourteenth day of the first month" → "in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month"** (v5) — Hebrew בָּרִאשׁוֹן, "in the first"; "month" is supplied there.
- **"defiled by the dead body of a man" → "unclean by the soul of a man"** (v6, 7, 10) — Hebrew טְמֵאִים לְנֶפֶשׁ אָדָם: the word is נֶפֶשׁ "soul", here of a dead person, as Lev 21:1, 11; 22:4; Num 5:2; 6:6. "Dead body" is not a word of the Hebrew. In v10 only "by a soul".
- **"they came before" → "they came near before"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיִּקְרְבוּ. In v7 they speak "to him", though Moses and Aaron are both named.
- **"wherefore are we kept back, that we may not offer an offering of the LORD" → "why should we be diminished, so as not to bring near the offering of Yahweh"** (v7) — Hebrew נִגָּרַע, the passive of גָּרַע "diminish, take away from" (Exod 5:8, 11): made less than the others, held back. הַקְרִיב … קָרְבַּן, verb and noun of one root, "bring near what is brought near" (v13 also).
- **"Stand still, and I will hear" → "Stand, and let me hear"** (v8) — Hebrew עִמְדוּ; וְאֶשְׁמְעָה is a wish form. **"concerning you" → "for you"** — Hebrew לָכֶם.
- **"If any man of you or of your posterity" → "Any man … of you or of your generations"** (v10) — Hebrew אִישׁ אִישׁ, literally "a man, a man"; לָכֶם אוֹ לְדֹרֹתֵיכֶם stands after the two cases, as in the verse. **"in a journey afar off" → "on a far way"** — Hebrew בְדֶרֶךְ רְחֹקָה. In the Masoretic text the last letter of רְחֹקָה carries a dot above it (one of the "extraordinary points"); it does not change the word. **"yet he shall keep" → "shall make"** — the Hebrew has no "yet".
- **"eat it with unleavened bread and bitter herbs"** (v11) — Hebrew עַל, literally "upon"; מְרֹרִים, "bitter things", as Exod 12:8.
- **"They shall leave none of it … nor break any bone of it" → "They shall not leave any of it … and a bone they shall not break in it"** (v12) — Hebrew בוֹ "in it", as Exod 12:46 (cited in John 19:36).
- **"forbeareth to keep" → "forbears to make"** (v13) — Hebrew חָדַל, elsewhere "cease"; here of leaving a thing undone. **"is not in a journey" → "was not on a way"** — Hebrew לֹא־הָיָה, a past form.
- **"the same soul shall be cut off from among his people" → "that soul shall be cut off from its peoples"** (v13) — Hebrew מֵעַמֶּיהָ, plural: his kinsfolk. The verse passes from "the man" to "that soul" and back to "that man".
- **"a stranger shall sojourn among you, and will keep" → "a sojourner sojourns with you and makes"** (v14) — Hebrew גֵּר, יָגוּר, one root; as Exod 12:48. No word for "wishes". **"for him that was born in the land" → "for the native of the land"** — Hebrew אֶזְרַח הָאָרֶץ. "For you" stands before "both for the sojourner and for the native".
- **"on the day that the tabernacle was reared up" → "on the day of the raising up of the dwelling"** (v15) — Hebrew בְיוֹם הָקִים אֶת־הַמִּשְׁכָּן, an infinitive without a subject: "on the day of (one's) raising up the dwelling" (Exod 40:17, 34). מִשְׁכָּן is "dwelling", from שָׁכַן "abide" (v17, 18, 22).
- **"namely, the tent of the testimony" → "the tent of the testimony"** (v15) — Hebrew לְאֹהֶל הָעֵדֻת, with לְ "to, for, as to": the dwelling as the tent of the testimony, or the part of the dwelling over the tent of the testimony.
- **"there was upon the tabernacle as it were the appearance of fire" → "it would be on the dwelling as the appearance of fire"** (v15) — Hebrew יִהְיֶה; from here to v23 the verbs are forms that tell what happened each time (as Exod 40:36–38): "would be", "would cover", "would set out", "would camp". The plain past returns in v23, "they kept".
- **"the cloud covered it by day" → "the cloud would cover it"** (v16) — the Hebrew has no "by day"; it is in the Septuagint and other old versions (cited from memory).
- **"when the cloud was taken up from the tabernacle" → "according as the cloud was taken up from over the tent"** (v17) — Hebrew וּלְפִי, literally "and at the mouth of": the same word פֶּה as in "at the mouth of Yahweh" (v18). הֵעָלֹת is the passive of "go up" (Exod 40:36); הָאֹהֶל is "the tent".
- **"journeyed … pitched their tents / rested in their tents" → "would set out … would camp"** (v17–23) — Hebrew נָסַע, literally "pull up" (the tent pegs), and חָנָה "camp"; one English word for each throughout.
- **"abode" → "would abide"** (v17, 18, 22) — Hebrew שָׁכַן.
- **"At the commandment of the LORD" → "At the mouth of Yahweh"** (v18, 20, 23) — Hebrew עַל־פִּי יְהוָה, seven times in v18–23, the same each time: at his word. "Commandment" is another word (מִצְוָה).
- **"as long as the cloud abode" → "all the days that the cloud would abide"** (v18) — Hebrew כָּל־יְמֵי אֲשֶׁר.
- **"when the cloud tarried long upon the tabernacle many days" → "when the cloud prolonged on the dwelling many days"** (v19, 22) — Hebrew הֶאֱרִיךְ, "make long": the verb used with "days" for a long stay or a long life.
- **"so it was, when the cloud was a few days" → "at times the cloud would be a few days"** (v20, 21) — Hebrew וְיֵשׁ אֲשֶׁר, literally "and there is that"; יָמִים מִסְפָּר, literally "days of number", days that can be counted.
- **"whether it was by day or by night that the cloud was taken up" → "or by day and by night, and the cloud would be taken up"** (v21) — Hebrew אוֹ יוֹמָם וָלַיְלָה וְנַעֲלָה הֶעָנָן: either the cloud stayed a day and a night and was then taken up, or it was taken up whether by day or by night. The Hebrew words are kept.
- **"or a year"** (v22) — Hebrew יָמִים, literally "days": a set of days, a year, as Lev 25:29. Others take it as "a longer time".
- **"tarried … remaining thereon" → "prolonged … to abide on it"** (v22) — Hebrew לִשְׁכֹּן עָלָיו.
- **"they rested in the tents … they journeyed: they kept the charge" → "they would camp … they would set out; the charge of Yahweh they kept"** (v23) — מִשְׁמֶרֶת is what is to be kept, from שָׁמַר "keep" (v19).

### Notes
- **Pesach in the second year** (v1–5) — a year after the first (Exod 12), made in the wilderness of Sinai.
- **Pesach in the second month** (v6–14) — for the one unclean by a soul or on a far way at the appointed time: the same day of the month, the same statute. Later Jewish usage calls it *Pesach Sheni*, "second Pesach"; the name is not in the text.
- **The case brought to Moses** (v6–8) — the ruling is not known beforehand; Moses waits to hear what Yahweh will command. Compare Lev 24:12; Num 15:34; 27:1–5.
- **The sojourner** (v14) — one statute for the sojourner and for the native, as Exod 12:48–49.
- **The cloud** (v15–23) — takes up Exod 40:34–38: the camp sets out and camps as the cloud is taken up and abides, "at the mouth of Yahweh".
