# Numbers 15

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and say to them: 'When you come into the land of your dwelling places, which I am giving to you,

[3] and you offer an offering by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, a burnt offering or a sacrifice, to make a special vow or as a freewill offering or at your appointed times, to make a soothing smell to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, from the herd or from the flock,

[4] then he who brings near his offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall bring near a grain offering of fine flour, a tenth, mixed with a fourth of a hin of oil.

[5] And wine for the drink offering, a fourth of a hin, you shall offer with the burnt offering or for the sacrifice, for the one lamb.

[6] Or for the ram you shall offer a grain offering of fine flour, two tenths, mixed with oil, a third of a hin.

[7] And wine for the drink offering, a third of a hin, you shall bring near, a soothing smell to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[8] And when you offer a son of the herd as a burnt offering or a sacrifice, to make a special vow or as peace offerings to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[9] then he shall bring near with the son of the herd a grain offering of fine flour, three tenths, mixed with oil, half a hin.

[10] And wine you shall bring near for the drink offering, half a hin, an offering by fire of a soothing smell to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[11] Thus shall it be done for the one ox or for the one ram, or for the sheep among the lambs or among the goats.

[12] According to the number that you offer, thus you shall do for each one, according to their number.

[13] Every native shall do these things thus, to bring near an offering by fire of a soothing smell to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[14] And when a sojourner sojourns with you, or whoever is in your midst, for your generations, and he offers an offering by fire of a soothing smell to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: as you do, so shall he do.

[15] The assembly: one statute for you and for the sojourner who sojourns, an everlasting statute for your generations; as you, so shall the sojourner be before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[16] ⟦torah|One torah|One law⟧ and one ordinance shall be for you and for the sojourner who sojourns with you.'"

[17] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[18] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and say to them: 'When you come into the land to which I am bringing you,

[19] then it shall be, when you eat of the bread of the land, that you shall lift up a contribution to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[20] The first of your dough, a cake, you shall lift up as a contribution; like the contribution of the threshing floor, so shall you lift it up.

[21] Of the first of your dough you shall give to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ a contribution, for your generations.

[22] And when you err and do not do all these commandments that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧,

[23] all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded you by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, from the day that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded and onward, for your generations,

[24] then it shall be, if away from the eyes of the congregation it was done in error, that all the congregation shall offer one bull of the herd as a burnt offering, for a soothing smell to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and its grain offering and its drink offering according to the ordinance, and one male goat as a sin offering.

[25] And the priest shall make atonement for all the congregation of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and it shall be pardoned them, for it is an error, and they have brought their offering, an offering by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and their sin offering before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for their error.

[26] And it shall be pardoned all the congregation of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and the sojourner who sojourns in their midst, for to all the people it was in error.

[27] And if one soul sins in error, then it shall bring near a female goat a year old as a sin offering.

[28] And the priest shall make atonement for the soul that errs, when it sins in error, before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to make atonement for him, and it shall be pardoned him.

[29] The native among the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and for the sojourner who sojourns in their midst: ⟦torah|one torah|one law⟧ shall be for you, for him who acts in error.

[30] But the soul that acts with a high hand, of the native or of the sojourner: it is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that he reviles, and that soul shall be cut off from the midst of its people.

[31] For the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ he has despised, and his commandment he has broken; that soul shall surely be cut off; its iniquity is in it.'"

[32] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were in the wilderness, and they found a man gathering wood on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ day.

[33] And those who found him gathering wood brought him near to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and to all the congregation.

[34] And they put him in custody, for it had not been declared distinctly what should be done to him.

[35] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "The man shall surely be put to death; all the congregation shall stone him with stones outside the camp."

[36] And all the congregation brought him out to the outside of the camp and stoned him with stones, and he died, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[37] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[38] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and say to them, and they shall make for themselves a tassel on the corners of their garments, for their generations, and they shall place on the tassel of the corner a cord of blue.

[39] And it shall be to you for a tassel, and you shall see it and remember all the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and do them, and you shall not go about after your heart and after your eyes, after which you play the prostitute,

[40] so that you may remember and do all my commandments and be holy to your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[41] I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who brought you out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ to be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to you; I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v40, 41) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים. **"to be your God" → "to be Elohim to you"** (v41) — Hebrew לִהְיוֹת לָכֶם לֵאלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v2, 18, 25, 26, 29, 32, 38) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"the land of your habitations" → "the land of your dwelling places"** (v2) — Hebrew מוֹשְׁבֹתֵיכֶם, from יָשַׁב "dwell".
- **"will make an offering by fire … in making a sweet savour" → "offer an offering by fire … to make a soothing smell"** (v3) — both are the one verb עָשָׂה "do, make", which is also "offer" in v5, 6, 8, 12, 14, 24 and "do" in v11–14. אִשֶּׁה is commonly derived from אֵשׁ "fire"; the derivation is not certain. רֵיחַ נִיחֹחַ: נִיחֹחַ is from נוּחַ "rest" (v3, 7, 10, 13, 14, 24).
- **"in performing a vow" → "to make a special vow"** (v3, 8) — Hebrew לְפַלֵּא־נֶדֶר, as Lev 22:21; the root is "be wonderful, beyond the ordinary", and its exact force with "vow" is not certain ("fulfil a vow" is also read).
- **"in your solemn feasts" → "at your appointed times"** (v3) — Hebrew מוֹעֲדֵיכֶם; "feast" is another word, חַג.
- **"Then shall he that offereth his offering unto the LORD bring a meat offering of a tenth deal of flour" → "then he who brings near his offering to Yahweh shall bring near a grain offering of fine flour, a tenth"** (v4) — Hebrew וְהִקְרִיב הַמַּקְרִיב קָרְבָּנוֹ: verb, participle and noun of one root, "come near". עִשָּׂרוֹן is "a tenth"; the measure (the ephah) is not named. So "two tenths" (v6), "three tenths" (v9). **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** (v4, 6, 9, 24) — Hebrew מִנְחָה.
- **"shalt thou prepare with the burnt offering or sacrifice, for one lamb" → "you shall offer with the burnt offering or for the sacrifice, for the one lamb"** (v5) — Hebrew עַל־הָעֹלָה אוֹ לַזָּבַח, two prepositions ("upon … for"). "You" is singular in v5–8 and v10, plural in v2–3, 12, 14–16; the Hebrew changes, and v4, v9 have "he".
- **"shalt thou offer" → "you shall bring near"** (v7, 10); **"shall he bring" → "he shall bring near"** (v9) — Hebrew הִקְרִיב.
- **"a bullock" → "a son of the herd"** (v8, 9) — Hebrew בֶּן־בָּקָר, without the word פַּר "bull" (which stands in v24: "bull of the herd"; the words do not say "young").
- **"an offering made by fire, of a sweet savour" → "an offering by fire of a soothing smell"** (v10, 13, 14) — Hebrew אִשֵּׁה רֵיחַ־נִיחֹחַ, a construct chain.
- **"for one bullock, or for one ram, or for a lamb, or a kid" → "for the one ox or for the one ram, or for the sheep among the lambs or among the goats"** (v11) — Hebrew שׁוֹר, one head of cattle; שֶׂה, one animal of the flock, sheep or goat (as Exod 12:5); כְּבָשִׂים, עִזִּים.
- **"According to the number that ye shall prepare" → "According to the number that you offer, thus you shall do"** (v12) — the same verb עָשָׂה twice.
- **"All that are born of the country" → "Every native"** (v13, 29, 30) — Hebrew הָאֶזְרָח.
- **"if a stranger sojourn with you" → "when a sojourner sojourns with you"** (v14, 15, 16, 26, 29, 30) — Hebrew גֵּר, of the verb גּוּר; "stranger" is kept for זָר. **"in your generations" → "for your generations"** (v14, 15, 21, 23; v38 "their") — Hebrew לְדֹרֹתֵיכֶם.
- **"One ordinance shall be both for you of the congregation, and also for the stranger" → "The assembly: one statute for you and for the sojourner who sojourns"** (v15) — Hebrew הַקָּהָל חֻקָּה אַחַת לָכֶם וְלַגֵּר הַגָּר: "the assembly" stands alone at the head of the sentence, and the clause has no verb. **"an ordinance for ever" → "an everlasting statute"** — Hebrew חֻקַּת עוֹלָם, literally "a statute of the age".
- **"One law and one manner" → "One torah and one ordinance"** (v16, 29) — Hebrew תּוֹרָה אַחַת וּמִשְׁפָּט אֶחָד; מִשְׁפָּט is "judgment", here a ruling (so v24).
- **"ye shall offer up an heave offering" → "you shall lift up a contribution"** (v19, 20, 21) — Hebrew תָּרִימוּ תְרוּמָה, verb and noun of one root: what is "lifted off" and set aside for Yahweh.
- **"a cake of the first of your dough"** (v20, 21) — Hebrew עֲרִסֹת, found only here, Ezek 44:30 and Neh 10:37; the sense is uncertain ("dough", "coarse meal" or "kneading trough"). It is not the word of Exod 12:34 (בָּצֵק). חַלָּה is a ring-shaped or pierced loaf. רֵאשִׁית is "the first", as Gen 1:1 "beginning".
- **"if ye have erred" → "when you err"** (v22); **"by ignorance", "through ignorance" → "in error", "an error"** (v24–29) — Hebrew שָׁגָה / שָׁגַג and שְׁגָגָה, as Lev 4: a straying, a wrong done by mistake. "Unintentionally" and "through ignorance" say more than the word.
- **"by the hand of Moses"** (v23) — Hebrew בְּיַד־מֹשֶׁה, kept.
- **"if ought be committed by ignorance without the knowledge of the congregation" → "if away from the eyes of the congregation it was done in error"** (v24) — Hebrew מֵעֵינֵי הָעֵדָה, literally "from the eyes of the congregation". **"one young bullock" → "one bull of the herd"** — Hebrew פַּר בֶּן־בָּקָר, literally "a bull, a son of the herd". **"one kid of the goats" → "one male goat"** — Hebrew שְׂעִיר־עִזִּים.
- **"it shall be forgiven them" → "it shall be pardoned them"** (v25, 26, 28) — Hebrew וְנִסְלַח, a passive with no subject named; סָלַח is said only of God. **"make an atonement"** (v25, 28) — Hebrew כִּפֶּר, literally "cover" (or "wipe off").
- **"seeing all the people were in ignorance" → "for to all the people it was in error"** (v26) — Hebrew כִּי לְכָל־הָעָם בִּשְׁגָגָה, a clause without a verb.
- **"if any soul sin through ignorance, then he shall bring a she goat of the first year" → "if one soul sins in error, then it shall bring near a female goat a year old"** (v27) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ אַחַת; the verb is feminine, of the soul. בַּת־שְׁנָתָהּ is literally "a daughter of her year".
- **"for the soul that sinneth ignorantly, when he sinneth by ignorance" → "for the soul that errs, when it sins in error"** (v28) — Hebrew הַנֶּפֶשׁ הַשֹּׁגֶגֶת בְּחֶטְאָה בִשְׁגָגָה. "For him … pardoned him" is masculine in the Hebrew, though "soul" is feminine.
- **"Ye shall have one law for him that sinneth through ignorance" → "one torah shall be for you, for him who acts in error"** (v29) — Hebrew לָעֹשֶׂה, "for the one doing". The Hebrew begins "The native …, and for the sojourner": the first has no preposition.
- **"the soul that doeth ought presumptuously" → "the soul that acts with a high hand"** (v30) — Hebrew בְּיָד רָמָה, literally "with a raised hand" (Exod 14:8). **"the same reproacheth the LORD" → "it is Yahweh that he reviles"** — Hebrew אֶת־יְהוָה הוּא מְגַדֵּף: the object stands first. גִּדֵּף is "revile, insult" (2 Kgs 19:6, 22). **"from among his people" → "from the midst of its people"** — Hebrew מִקֶּרֶב עַמָּהּ; "people" is singular here, and the suffix is feminine, of the soul.
- **"that soul shall utterly be cut off; his iniquity shall be upon him" → "that soul shall surely be cut off; its iniquity is in it"** (v31) — Hebrew הִכָּרֵת תִּכָּרֵת, the infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "being cut off it shall be cut off"; so v35 "shall surely be put to death", literally "dying he shall be made to die". עֲוֺנָה בָהּ is "its iniquity is in it" (or "on it").
- **"while the children of Israel were in the wilderness" → "And the sons of Israel were in the wilderness, and they found"** (v32) — two main clauses in the Hebrew. **"gathered sticks" → "gathering wood"** — Hebrew מְקֹשֵׁשׁ עֵצִים, the verb of Exod 5:7, 12; עֵצִים is "pieces of wood".
- **"brought him" → "brought him near"** (v33) — Hebrew וַיַּקְרִיבוּ, the verb of v4.
- **"they put him in ward, because it was not declared" → "they put him in custody, for it had not been declared distinctly"** (v34) — Hebrew וַיַּנִּיחוּ אֹתוֹ בַּמִּשְׁמָר, literally "they laid him up in the keeping"; פֹרַשׁ, the verb of Lev 24:12.
- **"all the congregation shall stone him with stones"** (v35) — Hebrew רָגוֹם, an infinitive absolute used as a command; רָגַם, another verb than סָקַל "stone".
- **"spake" → "said … saying"** (v37) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר … לֵּאמֹר, not the וַיְדַבֵּר of v1, 17.
- **"bid them that they make them fringes in the borders of their garments" → "say to them, and they shall make for themselves a tassel on the corners of their garments"** (v38) — Hebrew וְאָמַרְתָּ אֲלֵהֶם וְעָשׂוּ, two clauses joined by "and". צִיצִת is singular, a collective: a tassel or fringe (the word is used of a lock of hair in Ezek 8:3). כַּנְפֵי is literally "wings": the corners or edges of the garment. **"a ribband of blue" → "a cord of blue"** — Hebrew פְּתִיל תְּכֵלֶת; תְּכֵלֶת is the blue of the dwelling (Exod 25:4), wool dyed with it.
- **"it shall be unto you for a fringe, that ye may look upon it" → "it shall be to you for a tassel, and you shall see it"** (v39) — Hebrew וּרְאִיתֶם אֹתוֹ.
- **"that ye seek not after your own heart and your own eyes" → "you shall not go about after your heart and after your eyes"** (v39) — Hebrew וְלֹא־תָתֻרוּ אַחֲרֵי, the verb תּוּר, used of the men sent to "explore" the land (13:2; 14:36): to go about and look. "Own" is not in the Hebrew, and "after" stands twice.
- **"after which ye use to go a whoring" → "after which you play the prostitute"** (v39) — Hebrew זֹנִים, a participle of זָנָה.

### Notes

- **Grain offering and drink offering** (v1–16) — the measures of fine flour, oil and wine that go with a lamb, a ram and a head of cattle.
- **The first of the dough** (v17–21) — in later Jewish practice the *challah*, a piece of dough set aside before baking. Rom 11:16 uses the picture.
- **Error and the high hand** (v22–31) — for a wrong done in error there is an offering; for what is done with a high hand none is named. Heb 10:26 is commonly set beside this.
- **The wood-gatherer** (v32–36) — he is kept in custody until Yahweh declares what is to be done; compare the one who cursed in Lev 24:10–23.
- **The tassel** (v37–41) — later *tzitzit*. The cord of blue (*tekhelet*) has the colour of the robe of the ephod (Exod 28:31). Matt 9:20 speaks of the edge (κράσπεδον) of the garment of Jesus, the word the Septuagint is said to use here (from memory, not checked).
