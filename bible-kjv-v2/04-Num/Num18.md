# Numbers 18

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, "You and your sons and your father's house with you shall bear the iniquity of the sanctuary; and you and your sons with you shall bear the iniquity of your priesthood.

[2] And also your brothers, the tribe of Levi, the tribe of your father, bring near with you, and they shall be joined to you and minister to you; and you and your sons with you shall be before the tent of the testimony.

[3] And they shall keep your charge and the charge of all the tent; only to the holy utensils and to the altar they shall not come near, and they shall not die, both they and you.

[4] And they shall be joined to you and shall keep the charge of the tent of meeting, for all the service of the tent; and a stranger shall not come near to you.

[5] And you shall keep the charge of the Holy and the charge of the altar, and there shall be no more wrath on the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[6] And I, behold, I have taken your brothers the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ from the midst of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; to you they are a gift, as given ones to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to serve the service of the tent of meeting.

[7] And you and your sons with you shall keep your priesthood for every matter of the altar and for what is inside the veil, and you shall serve. As a service of gift I give your priesthood; and the stranger who comes near shall be put to death."

[8] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, "And I, behold, I have given to you the charge of my contributions; as to all the holy things of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to you I have given them for the anointing, and to your sons, as an everlasting statute.

[9] This shall be yours from the most holy things, from the fire: every offering of theirs, for every grain offering of theirs and for every sin offering of theirs and for every guilt offering of theirs, which they return to me; it is most holy, for you and for your sons.

[10] As a most holy thing you shall eat it; every male shall eat it; it shall be holy to you.

[11] And this is yours: the contribution of their gift, for all the wave offerings of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; to you I have given them, and to your sons and to your daughters with you, as an everlasting statute; everyone who is clean in your house shall eat it.

[12] All the fat of the fresh oil and all the fat of the new wine and of the grain, the first of them, which they give to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to you I have given them.

[13] The firstfruits of all that is in their land, which they bring to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, shall be yours; everyone who is clean in your house shall eat it.

[14] Every devoted thing in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall be yours.

[15] Every firstling of the womb, of all flesh, which they bring near to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, among man and among beast, shall be yours; only you shall surely ransom the firstborn of man, and the firstborn of the unclean beast you shall ransom.

[16] And its ransom: from a month old you shall ransom, by your valuation, five shekels of silver, by the holy shekel; it is twenty gerahs.

[17] Only the firstborn of an ox or the firstborn of a lamb or the firstborn of a goat you shall not ransom; they are holy. Their blood you shall dash on the altar, and their fat you shall turn into smoke, an offering by fire for a soothing smell to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[18] And their flesh shall be yours; like the breast of the wave offering and like the right leg, it shall be yours.

[19] All the contributions of the holy things, which the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ lift up to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, I have given to you and to your sons and to your daughters with you, as an everlasting statute; it is an everlasting ⟦berit-melach|berit melach|covenant of salt⟧ before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for you and for your seed with you."

[20] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, "In their land you shall not inherit, and a share you shall not have in their midst; I am your share and your inheritance in the midst of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[21] And to the sons of Levi, behold, I have given every tithe in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ for an inheritance, in exchange for their service which they serve, the service of the tent of meeting.

[22] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall no more come near to the tent of meeting, to bear sin, to die.

[23] And the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, they shall serve the service of the tent of meeting, and they shall bear their iniquity: an everlasting statute for your generations; and in the midst of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ they shall not inherit an inheritance.

[24] For the tithe of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, which they lift up to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ as a contribution, I have given to the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ for an inheritance; therefore I have said to them, 'In the midst of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ they shall not inherit an inheritance.'"

[25] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[26] "And to the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ you shall speak and say to them: 'When you take from the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ the tithe that I have given to you from them as your inheritance, then you shall lift up from it the contribution of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, a tithe from the tithe.

[27] And your contribution shall be reckoned to you like the grain from the threshing floor and like the fullness from the winepress.

[28] So you also shall lift up the contribution of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from all your tithes that you take from the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and you shall give from it the contribution of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ the priest.

[29] From all your gifts you shall lift up every contribution of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, from all its fat, its holy part from it.

[30] And you shall say to them: When you lift up its fat from it, then it shall be reckoned to the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ like the yield of the threshing floor and like the yield of the winepress.

[31] And you shall eat it in every place, you and your house, for it is wages for you in exchange for your service in the tent of meeting.

[32] And you shall not bear sin because of it, when you lift up its fat from it; and the holy things of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ you shall not profane, and you shall not die.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"thy father's house"** (v1) — Hebrew בֵּית־אָבִיךָ. **"bear the iniquity"** (v1, 23) — Hebrew נָשָׂא עָוֹן: carry the guilt and what it brings.
- **"thy brethren also of the tribe of Levi, the tribe of thy father, bring thou with thee" → "also your brothers, the tribe of Levi, the tribe of your father, bring near with you"** (v2) — two Hebrew words for "tribe": מַטֵּה לֵוִי, the "staff" of ch. 17, and שֵׁבֶט אָבִיךָ, literally "rod". הַקְרֵב is "bring near", the verb of 16:5, 9, 10. **"that they may be joined unto thee"** (v2, 4) — Hebrew וְיִלָּווּ, וְנִלְווּ, of לָוָה "join": a play on the name Levi (Gen 29:34). The last clause of v2 has no verb; "shall be" is supplied.
- **"the tabernacle of witness" → "the tent of the testimony"** (v2) — Hebrew אֹהֶל הָעֵדֻת.
- **"they shall keep thy charge"** (v3, 4, 5, 8) — Hebrew שָׁמַר מִשְׁמֶרֶת, verb and noun of one root: "keep what is to be kept", also the watch of a guard.
- **"the vessels of the sanctuary" → "the holy utensils"** (v3) — Hebrew כְּלֵי הַקֹּדֶשׁ, literally "the utensils of holiness" (or "of the Holy"). **"that neither they, nor ye also, die" → "and they shall not die, both they and you"** — Hebrew וְלֹא־יָמֻתוּ.
- **"a stranger"** (v4, 7) — Hebrew זָר: one who does not belong there, here anyone who is not a priest or a Levite (v4), or not a priest (v7).
- **"the charge of the sanctuary" → "the charge of the Holy"** (v5) — Hebrew הַקֹּדֶשׁ, not the מִקְדָּשׁ "sanctuary" of v1: the holy room (Exod 26:33) or the holy things.
- **"to you they are given as a gift for the LORD" → "to you they are a gift, as given ones to Yahweh"** (v6) — Hebrew לָכֶם מַתָּנָה נְתֻנִים לַיהוָה, literally "to you a gift, given ones to Yahweh" (נְתֻנִים as 3:9; 8:16, 19). **"to do the service" → "to serve the service"** (v6, 21, 23) — Hebrew עָבַד עֲבֹדָה, verb and noun of one root.
- **"shall keep your priest's office for every thing of the altar, and within the vail" → "shall keep your priesthood for every matter of the altar and for what is inside the veil"** (v7) — Hebrew לְכָל־דְּבַר הַמִּזְבֵּחַ וּלְמִבֵּית לַפָּרֹכֶת. **"I have given your priest's office unto you as a service of gift" → "As a service of gift I give your priesthood"** — Hebrew עֲבֹדַת מַתָּנָה אֶתֵּן אֶת־כְּהֻנַּתְכֶם; "unto you" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"the charge of mine heave offerings" → "the charge of my contributions"** (v8) — Hebrew תְּרוּמֹתָי; so תְּרוּמָה "contribution" and הֵרִים "lift up" through the chapter (v11, 19, 24, 26–30, 32): what is "lifted off" and set aside for Yahweh.
- **"unto thee have I given them by reason of the anointing" → "to you I have given them for the anointing"** (v8) — Hebrew לְמָשְׁחָה. The word has the form of "anointing" (Exod 29:29; Lev 7:35); many take it here as another word of the same form, "measured share, portion". The verse keeps the word as it stands. **"by an ordinance for ever" → "as an everlasting statute"** (v8, 11, 19) — Hebrew לְחָק־עוֹלָם, literally "a statute of the age": the due laid down for the priests, as Lev 7:34.
- **"reserved from the fire" → "from the fire"** (v9) — Hebrew מִן־הָאֵשׁ; "reserved" is not in the Hebrew. **"every oblation of theirs, every meat offering" → "every offering of theirs, for every grain offering"** — Hebrew כָּל־קָרְבָּנָם לְכָל־מִנְחָתָם: the three kinds are joined by לְ "for, as regards". **"which they shall render unto me" → "which they return to me"** — Hebrew יָשִׁיבוּ, "give back".
- **"In the most holy place shalt thou eat it" → "As a most holy thing you shall eat it"** (v10) — Hebrew בְּקֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים, literally "in the holiness of the holinesses", the words of v9 "the most holy things" and also the name of the inner room (Exod 26:33), where nothing is eaten. It is read "as most holy things" or "in a most holy place" (the priests eat such things "in a holy place", Lev 6:16, 26); the Hebrew does not decide.
- **"the heave offering of their gift, with all the wave offerings" → "the contribution of their gift, for all the wave offerings"** (v11) — Hebrew תְּרוּמַת מַתָּנָם לְכָל־תְּנוּפֹת; תְּנוּפָה is from הֵנִיף "wave", to move to and fro before Yahweh.
- **"All the best of the oil, and all the best of the wine, and of the wheat, the firstfruits of them" → "All the fat of the fresh oil and all the fat of the new wine and of the grain, the first of them"** (v12) — Hebrew חֵלֶב "fat", here the best part (so v29, 30, 32); יִצְהָר, the fresh oil of the harvest, not the common word שֶׁמֶן; תִּירוֹשׁ, דָּגָן; רֵאשִׁיתָם, "their first part". "Firstfruits" (v13) is another word, בִּכּוּרִים.
- **"Every thing devoted" → "Every devoted thing"** (v14) — Hebrew חֵרֶם: what is given over to Yahweh beyond recall (Lev 27:28).
- **"Every thing that openeth the matrix in all flesh" → "Every firstling of the womb, of all flesh"** (v15) — Hebrew פֶּטֶר רֶחֶם, literally "what breaks open the womb"; "firstborn" in the same verse is בְּכוֹר. **"which they bring" → "which they bring near"** — Hebrew יַקְרִיבוּ. **"whether it be of men or beasts" → "among man and among beast"** — Hebrew בָּאָדָם וּבַבְּהֵמָה.
- **"redeem" → "ransom"** (v15, 16, 17) — Hebrew פָּדָה; "redeem" is kept for גָּאַל (Lev 27:27 has both). "Surely ransom" is literally "ransoming you shall ransom".
- **"those that are to be redeemed from a month old shalt thou redeem" → "its ransom: from a month old you shall ransom"** (v16) — Hebrew וּפְדוּיָו, the word of 3:46–51: "its ransom", the price; in form "its ransomed ones" (those of it that are to be ransomed), which is also read here. "A month old" is literally "a son of a month". **"according to thine estimation" → "by your valuation"** — Hebrew בְּעֶרְכְּךָ, the fixed form of Lev 27. **"after the shekel of the sanctuary" → "by the holy shekel"** — Hebrew שֶׁקֶל הַקֹּדֶשׁ; the shekel is a weight.
- **"the firstling of a cow, or the firstling of a sheep" → "the firstborn of an ox or the firstborn of a lamb"** (v17) — Hebrew בְּכוֹר (KJV "firstling" here, "firstborn" in v15: one word); שׁוֹר, one head of cattle; כֶּשֶׂב.
- **"sprinkle their blood upon the altar, and shalt burn their fat for an offering made by fire, for a sweet savour" → "dash … turn into smoke, an offering by fire for a soothing smell"** (v17) — Hebrew זָרַק, to throw from a bowl; הִקְטִיר, literally "make smoke"; אִשֶּׁה, commonly derived from אֵשׁ "fire" (the derivation is not certain); רֵיחַ נִיחֹחַ.
- **"as the wave breast and as the right shoulder" → "like the breast of the wave offering and like the right leg"** (v18) — Hebrew שׁוֹק, the upper hind leg.
- **"All the heave offerings of the holy things, which the children of Israel offer" → "All the contributions of the holy things, which the sons of Israel lift up"** (v19) — Hebrew תְּרוּמֹת … יָרִימוּ.
- **"it is a covenant of salt for ever" → "it is an everlasting covenant of salt"** (v19) — Hebrew בְּרִית מֶלַח עוֹלָם, word for word "a covenant of salt of the age". The verse does not say what the salt stands for; see Lev 2:13; 2 Chr 13:5. **"thy seed"** — Hebrew זֶרַע.
- **"Thou shalt have no inheritance in their land, neither shalt thou have any part among them: I am thy part" → "In their land you shall not inherit, and a share you shall not have in their midst; I am your share"** (v20) — Hebrew תִנְחָל, the verb of נַחֲלָה "inheritance"; חֵלֶק.
- **"all the tenth in Israel" → "every tithe in Israel"** (v21, 24, 26, 28) — Hebrew מַעֲשֵׂר, literally "a tenth"; in v26 "a tithe from the tithe". **"for their service which they serve" → "in exchange for their service which they serve"** (v21, 31) — Hebrew חֵלֶף, found only in these two verses.
- **"lest they bear sin, and die" → "to bear sin, to die"** (v22) — Hebrew לָשֵׂאת חֵטְא לָמוּת, two infinitives.
- **"But the Levites shall do the service" → "And the Levites, they shall serve the service"** (v23) — Hebrew וְעָבַד הַלֵּוִי הוּא, literally "and the Levite, he shall serve": a singular for the whole tribe, followed by the plural "they shall bear their iniquity". Whose iniquity is meant, their own or that of the sons of Israel (v22), the Hebrew does not say. **"a statute for ever throughout your generations" → "an everlasting statute for your generations"** — Hebrew חֻקַּת עוֹלָם לְדֹרֹתֵיכֶם. **"they have no inheritance" → "they shall not inherit an inheritance"** (v23, 24) — Hebrew לֹא יִנְחֲלוּ נַחֲלָה.
- **"which they offer as an heave offering" → "which they lift up … as a contribution"** (v24) — Hebrew יָרִימוּ … תְּרוּמָה.
- **"Thus speak unto the Levites" → "And to the Levites you shall speak"** (v26) — Hebrew וְאֶל־הַלְוִיִּם תְּדַבֵּר. **"ye shall offer up an heave offering of it for the LORD" → "you shall lift up from it the contribution of Yahweh"** (v26, 28, 29) — Hebrew תְּרוּמַת יְהוָה, a construct: "of Yahweh".
- **"as though it were the corn of the threshingfloor, and as the fulness of the winepress" → "like the grain from the threshing floor and like the fullness from the winepress"** (v27) — Hebrew מְלֵאָה, as Exod 22:29; יֶקֶב, the vat into which the juice runs. **"reckoned"** (v27, 30) — Hebrew נֶחְשַׁב, as Gen 15:6.
- **"Out of all your gifts ye shall offer every heave offering of the LORD, of all the best thereof, even the hallowed part thereof out of it" → "From all your gifts you shall lift up every contribution of Yahweh, from all its fat, its holy part from it"** (v29) — Hebrew מִכָּל־חֶלְבּוֹ אֶת־מִקְדְּשׁוֹ מִמֶּנּוּ; מִקְדְּשׁוֹ is an unusual form, "its sanctified part" (pointed like "its sanctuary").
- **"as the increase of the threshingfloor" → "like the yield of the threshing floor"** (v30) — Hebrew תְּבוּאָה. "The rest" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"ye and your households: for it is your reward for your service" → "you and your house, for it is wages for you in exchange for your service"** (v31) — Hebrew בֵּיתְכֶם; שָׂכָר.
- **"neither shall ye pollute the holy things …, lest ye die" → "you shall not profane …, and you shall not die"** (v32) — Hebrew חִלֵּל; וְלֹא תָמוּתוּ.

### Notes

- **After chapter 17** — the people asked, "Everyone who comes near … dies" (17:13); here the priests and the Levites keep the charge, and the stranger who comes near is put to death (v3–7, 22).
- **Said to Aaron** (v1, 8, 20) — Yahweh speaks to Aaron alone, as in Lev 10:8.
- **What the priests receive** (v8–19) — the most holy things (grain, sin and guilt offerings), the contributions and wave offerings, the first of oil, wine and grain, the firstfruits, every devoted thing, the firstborn or its ransom.
- **What the Levites receive** (v21–32) — the tithe; of it they lift up a tithe for the priest.
- **"I am your share and your inheritance"** (v20) — compare Deut 10:9; Ps 16:5.
