# Numbers 18

[1] Yahweh said to Aaron, "You and your sons and your ancestral house with you shall bear the iniquity of the sanctuary; and you and your sons with you shall bear the iniquity of your priesthood.

[2] Bring with you also your brothers of the tribe of Levi, the tribe of your father, that they may be joined to you and minister to you; but you and your sons with you shall be before the Tent of the Testimony.

[3] They shall keep your charge and the charge of the whole tent; but they shall not come near the utensils of the sanctuary or the altar, lest both they and you die.

[4] They shall be joined to you and keep the charge of the Tent of Meeting, for all the service of the tent; and no outsider shall come near you.

[5] You shall keep the charge of the sanctuary and the charge of the altar, so that there may be no more wrath upon the children of Israel.

[6] Behold, I myself have taken your brothers the Levites from among the children of Israel; they are given to you as a gift for Yahweh, to do the service of the Tent of Meeting.

[7] You and your sons with you shall attend to your priesthood for all that concerns the altar and all that is within the veil; you shall serve. I give you the priesthood as a gift-service; any outsider who comes near shall be put to death."

[8] Yahweh spoke to Aaron, "Behold, I have given you the charge of my contributions — all the holy things of the children of Israel. To you I have given them as a portion, and to your sons, as a perpetual due.

[9] This shall be yours of the most holy things, reserved from the fire: every offering of theirs — every grain offering of theirs, every sin offering of theirs, and every guilt offering of theirs that they render to me — shall be most holy for you and for your sons.

[10] As something most holy you shall eat it; every male may eat it; it shall be holy to you.

[11] This also is yours: the contribution of their gift, with all the elevation offerings of the children of Israel. I have given them to you and to your sons and daughters with you as a perpetual due; everyone who is clean in your household may eat of it.

[12] All the best of the oil and all the best of the wine and of the wheat — their firstfruits that they give to Yahweh — I have given them to you.

[13] The first fruits of all that is in their land, which they bring to Yahweh, shall be yours; everyone who is clean in your household may eat of it.

[14] Every devoted thing in Israel shall be yours.

[15] Every firstborn that opens the womb of all flesh, which they bring to Yahweh, whether human or animal, shall be yours. But you shall indeed redeem the firstborn of man, and the firstborn of unclean animals you shall redeem.

[16] Their redemption price from a month old you shall redeem at your valuation — five shekels of silver, by the shekel of the sanctuary, which is twenty gerahs.

[17] But the firstborn of a cow, or the firstborn of a sheep, or the firstborn of a goat, you shall not redeem; they are holy. You shall throw their blood against the altar and turn their fat into smoke as an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma to Yahweh.

[18] Their flesh shall be yours — as the breast of the elevation offering and the right thigh are yours.

[19] All the holy contributions that the children of Israel offer to Yahweh, I have given to you, and to your sons and daughters with you, as a perpetual due; it is a covenant of salt forever before Yahweh for you and your descendants with you."

[20] Yahweh said to Aaron, "You shall have no inheritance in their land, nor shall you have any portion among them. I am your portion and your inheritance among the children of Israel.

[21] Behold, I have given to the sons of Levi every tithe in Israel as an inheritance, in return for their service, the service they render — the service of the Tent of Meeting.

[22] The children of Israel shall no longer come near the Tent of Meeting, lest they bear sin and die.

[23] The Levites alone shall do the service of the Tent of Meeting, and they shall bear the guilt of the people. It shall be a perpetual statute throughout your generations that they shall have no inheritance among the children of Israel.

[24] For the tithe of the children of Israel, which they present as a contribution to Yahweh, I have given to the Levites for an inheritance; therefore I have said to them, 'Among the children of Israel they shall have no inheritance.'"

[25] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[26] "Speak to the Levites and say to them: 'When you take from the children of Israel the tithe that I have given you from them as your inheritance, then you shall present a contribution from it to Yahweh — a tithe of the tithe.

[27] Your contribution shall be credited to you as the grain of the threshing floor and as the fullness of the winepress.

[28] So you also shall present a contribution to Yahweh from all your tithes that you receive from the children of Israel; and from it you shall give the contribution of Yahweh to Aaron the priest.

[29] From all your gifts you shall present every contribution of Yahweh — of all the best of it, the hallowed part of it.

[30] Therefore you shall say to them: When you have presented the best part of it, the rest shall be counted to the Levites as the yield of the threshing floor and the yield of the winepress.

[31] You may eat it in any place, you and your households, for it is your wages in return for your service in the Tent of Meeting.

[32] You shall bear no sin by reason of it when you have presented the best part of it; and you shall not profane the holy things of the children of Israel, lest you die.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"heave offering" → "contribution"** (throughout) — Hebrew *terumah*, as in Lev 7.
- **"wave offering" → "elevation offering"** (v11, 18) — Hebrew *tenufah*.
- **"oblation / meat offering / trespass offering" → "offering / grain offering / guilt offering"** (v9) — as in Leviticus.
- **"matrix" → "womb"** (v15) — Hebrew *rechem*.
- **"shoulder" → "thigh"** (v18) — Hebrew *shoq*.
- **"covenant of salt"** (v19) — Hebrew בְּרִית מֶלַח *berit melach*. Permanent covenant, salt as preservation symbol. Cf. Lev 2:13, 2 Chr 13:5.
- **"the Lord's priest" language modernized** throughout.

### Notes
- **Priests bear iniquity of sanctuary** (v1, 23) — Aaron's line absorbs the ritual risk; common Israelites cannot enter without dying (v22), so priests and Levites mediate.
- **Priestly economic structure** — no land inheritance (v20). Priests/Levites live by contributions, firstfruits, firstborn redemption, tithes. Yahweh himself is their "inheritance."
- **Tithes to Levites; Levites' tithe to priests** (v26–28) — layered economy. Priests receive a tithe-of-the-tithe. Later Malachi 3:8–10 ("tithes and contributions") echoes.
- **Covenant of salt** (v19) — three biblical "covenants of salt": here, 2 Chr 13:5 (Davidic), Lev 2:13 (every offering). Salt = enduring preservation.
- **"I am your portion and your inheritance"** (v20) — the priestly vocation forfeits land for direct relation to Yahweh. Ps 16:5, 73:26 develop this as a spiritual ideal for the whole Israelite: "Yahweh is my portion."
- **Structure after Korah** — chs 17 (Aaron's staff) and 18 (priestly prerogatives and dues) respond directly to Korah's challenge. Divine vindication + entrenched legal structure.
