# Numbers 3

[1] These are the generations of Aaron and Moses on the day when Yahweh spoke with Moses on Mount Sinai.

[2] These are the names of the sons of Aaron: Nadab the firstborn, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

[3] These are the names of the sons of Aaron, the anointed priests, whom he consecrated to serve as priests.

[4] But Nadab and Abihu died before Yahweh when they offered strange fire before Yahweh in the wilderness of Sinai, and they had no children; and Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests in the presence of Aaron their father.

[5] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[6] "Bring the tribe of Levi near, and present them before Aaron the priest, that they may serve him.

[7] They shall keep his charge and the charge of the whole congregation before the Tent of Meeting, to do the service of the Dwelling.

[8] They shall keep all the furnishings of the Tent of Meeting, and perform the charge of the children of Israel, to do the service of the Dwelling.

[9] You shall give the Levites to Aaron and to his sons; they are wholly given over to him from the children of Israel.

[10] You shall appoint Aaron and his sons, and they shall attend to their priesthood; any outsider who comes near shall be put to death."

[11] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[12] "Behold, I myself have taken the Levites from among the children of Israel in place of every firstborn that opens the womb among the children of Israel; the Levites shall be mine.

[13] For every firstborn is mine. On the day I struck every firstborn in the land of Egypt I consecrated to myself all the firstborn in Israel, both human and animal. They shall be mine. I am Yahweh."

[14] Yahweh spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, saying,

[15] "Number the children of Levi by their ancestral houses, by their clans; every male from a month old and upward you shall number."

[16] So Moses numbered them according to the word of Yahweh, as he had been commanded.

[17] These were the sons of Levi by their names: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

[18] These are the names of the sons of Gershon by their clans: Libni and Shimei.

[19] The sons of Kohath by their clans: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.

[20] The sons of Merari by their clans: Mahli and Mushi. These are the clans of the Levites by their ancestral houses.

[21] Of Gershon were the clan of the Libnites and the clan of the Shimeites; these are the clans of the Gershonites.

[22] Those numbered of them, counting every male from a month old and upward, were 7,500.

[23] The clans of the Gershonites shall camp behind the Dwelling on the west.

[24] The chief of the ancestral house of the Gershonites shall be Eliasaph son of Lael.

[25] The charge of the sons of Gershon in the Tent of Meeting shall be the Dwelling and the tent, its covering, and the curtain for the entrance of the Tent of Meeting,

[26] and the hangings of the court, and the curtain for the entrance of the court that surrounds the Dwelling and the altar, and its cords, for all its service.

[27] Of Kohath were the clan of the Amramites, the clan of the Izharites, the clan of the Hebronites, and the clan of the Uzzielites; these are the clans of the Kohathites.

[28] Counting every male from a month old and upward, there were 8,600, keeping charge of the sanctuary.

[29] The clans of the sons of Kohath shall camp on the south side of the Dwelling.

[30] The chief of the ancestral house of the clans of the Kohathites shall be Elizaphan son of Uzziel.

[31] Their charge shall be the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars, the utensils of the sanctuary with which they minister, the curtain, and all the service connected with these.

[32] The chief over the chiefs of the Levites shall be Eleazar son of Aaron the priest; he shall have oversight of those who keep charge of the sanctuary.

[33] Of Merari were the clan of the Mahlites and the clan of the Mushites; these are the clans of Merari.

[34] Those numbered of them, counting every male from a month old and upward, were 6,200.

[35] The chief of the ancestral house of the clans of Merari was Zuriel son of Abihail. They shall camp on the north side of the Dwelling.

[36] The assigned charge of the sons of Merari shall be the frames of the Dwelling, its bars, its pillars, its sockets, all its furnishings, and all the work connected with them,

[37] the pillars of the court all around, with their sockets, pegs, and cords.

[38] Those who camp before the Dwelling on the east, in front of the Tent of Meeting toward the sunrise, shall be Moses and Aaron and his sons, keeping the charge of the sanctuary for the children of Israel; and any outsider who comes near shall be put to death.

[39] All those numbered of the Levites, whom Moses and Aaron numbered at the commandment of Yahweh, by their clans — every male from a month old and upward — were 22,000.

[40] Yahweh said to Moses, "Number all the firstborn males of the children of Israel from a month old and upward, and make a list of their names.

[41] You shall take the Levites for me — I am Yahweh — in place of every firstborn among the children of Israel; and the cattle of the Levites in place of all the firstlings among the cattle of the children of Israel."

[42] So Moses numbered, as Yahweh had commanded him, all the firstborn among the children of Israel.

[43] All the firstborn males by the number of names, from a month old and upward — those numbered of them — were 22,273.

[44] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[45] "Take the Levites in place of all the firstborn among the children of Israel, and the cattle of the Levites in place of their cattle; and the Levites shall be mine. I am Yahweh.

[46] For the redemption of the 273 firstborn of the children of Israel who exceed the Levites,

[47] you shall take five shekels apiece, by the head, by the shekel of the sanctuary (the shekel is twenty gerahs);

[48] and you shall give the money, by which the excess among them is to be redeemed, to Aaron and his sons."

[49] So Moses took the redemption money from those who were over and above those redeemed by the Levites;

[50] from the firstborn of the children of Israel he took the money — 1,365 shekels, by the shekel of the sanctuary.

[51] Moses gave the redemption money to Aaron and his sons, according to the word of Yahweh, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"the matrix" → "the womb"** (v12) — Hebrew רֶחֶם *rechem*. Modernized.
- **"wait on their priest's office" → "attend to their priesthood"** (v10) — modernized.
- **"odd number" → "excess"** (v48) — modernized.
- **"candlestick" → "lampstand"** (v31) — as in Leviticus.
- **"pins" → "pegs"** (v37) — Hebrew יְתֵדוֹת *yethedot* = tent-pegs.

### Notes
- **"Generations of Aaron and Moses"** — but only Aaron's descendants are listed. Moses' sons (Gershom, Eliezer) are not elevated to priesthood; only Aaron's line carries the cultic office.
- **Levite substitution** (v12–13, 41–51) — Yahweh's claim on Israel's firstborn (from the Passover, Ex 13) is transferred to the tribe of Levi. The Levites become living firstborn-substitutes.
- **Three Levite clans** — Gershon (west, outer fabrics), Kohath (south, sacred vessels), Merari (north, structural frames), plus Moses & Aaron east (doorway/front). Careful geographic-functional distribution.
- **Firstborn shortfall** (v46) — 22,273 firstborn vs 22,000 Levites; the extra 273 are redeemed by silver (5 shekels apiece), establishing the "redemption of the firstborn" price. Mary and Joseph likely paid the equivalent at Luke 2:22–23.
- **Kohathites handle holiest items** (v31) — the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars. Highest-ranked Levite duty; but they never touch directly — Aaron covers items first (ch 4).
