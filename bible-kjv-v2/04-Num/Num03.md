## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew יהוה, the Tetragrammaton.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"These also are the generations of Aaron and Moses in the day that the LORD spake with Moses in mount Sinai" → "And these are the generations of Aaron and Moses, on the day when Yahweh spoke with Moses on Mount Sinai"** (v1) — Hebrew תּוֹלְדֹת, as Gen 2:4; 5:1; דִּבֶּר אֶת "spoke with"; בְּהַר is "in / on the mountain".
- **"Nadab the firstborn, and Abihu" → "the firstborn, Nadab; and Abihu"** (v2) — the Hebrew order, with a dividing stroke after "the firstborn".
- **"the priests which were anointed, whom he consecrated to minister in the priest's office" → "the anointed priests, whose hand he filled to be priests"** (v3) — Hebrew מִלֵּא יָדָם, "he filled their hand", the phrase for installing in the priesthood (Exod 28:41; Lev 8:33); who "he" is, is not said. לְכַהֵן is the verb made from כֹּהֵן "priest"; "minister" and "office" are not in the Hebrew.
- **"when they offered strange fire" → "when they brought near strange fire"** (v4) — Hebrew הִקְרִיב, as Lev 10:1. **"they had no children" → "they had no sons"** — Hebrew בָּנִים. **"ministered in the priest's office in the sight of Aaron" → "were priests in the presence of Aaron"** — Hebrew וַיְכַהֵן … עַל־פְּנֵי, literally "upon the face of": before him, in his lifetime.
- **"present them before Aaron the priest, that they may minister unto him" → "make it stand before Aaron the priest, and they shall minister to him"** (v6) — Hebrew וְהַעֲמַדְתָּ אֹתוֹ, "make it stand" (the tribe, singular); וְשֵׁרְתוּ, joined by "and", plural.
- **"keep his charge" kept; "to do the service of the tabernacle" → "to serve the service of the dwelling"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew שָׁמַר מִשְׁמֶרֶת, "keep the keeping" (1:53); לַעֲבֹד אֶת־עֲבֹדַת, verb and noun of one root. מִשְׁמֶרֶת is "charge" in every place of chapters 1–4, and עֲבֹדָה "service".
- **"instruments" → "utensils"** (v8) — Hebrew כֵּלִים.
- **"they are wholly given unto him out of the children of Israel" → "given, given are they to him from the sons of Israel"** (v9) — Hebrew נְתוּנִם נְתוּנִם הֵמָּה, the word twice: wholly given. "To him" is לוֹ: Aaron; the Samaritan text and the Septuagint are said to read "to me" as in 8:16 (from memory).
- **"thou shalt appoint Aaron and his sons, and they shall wait on their priest's office" → "Aaron and his sons you shall appoint, and they shall keep their priesthood"** (v10) — Hebrew תִּפְקֹד, the verb "number" of these chapters, here "appoint, give charge to" (Gen 40:4); שָׁמַר "keep".
- **"instead of all the firstborn that openeth the matrix among the children of Israel" → "in place of every firstborn, the firstling of the womb, from the sons of Israel"** (v12) — Hebrew תַּחַת, literally "under, in the place of" (the "eye for eye" of Exod 21:24; "for" would here read as "for the sake of"); פֶּטֶר רֶחֶם, literally "what first opens the womb" (Exod 13:2, 12).
- **"Because all the firstborn are mine; for on the day that I smote" → "For mine is every firstborn: on the day when I struck down"** (v13) — the Hebrew has one כִּי "for"; הִכָּה is "strike", here of killing. **"I hallowed unto me" → "I sanctified to myself"** — Hebrew הִקְדַּשְׁתִּי. **"both man and beast" → "from man to beast"** — Hebrew מֵאָדָם עַד־בְּהֵמָה.
- **"from a month old and upward"** (v15, 22, 28, 34, 39, 40, 43) — Hebrew מִבֶּן־חֹדֶשׁ וָמַעְלָה, literally "from a son of a month and upward".
- **"according to the word of the LORD" → "at the mouth of Yahweh"** (v16, 39, 51) — Hebrew עַל־פִּי יְהוָה; "word" and "commandment" are other Hebrew words. **"as he was commanded"** — Hebrew צֻוָּה, a passive.
- **"the families of the Levites according to the house of their fathers" → "the clans of the Levites, by their fathers' houses"** (v20) — the formula of chapter 1. "The Levites" is here הַלֵּוִי, "the Levite", a singular for the whole (so v32); "the Gershonites", "the Kohathites" and the rest are likewise singular in Hebrew ("the Gershonite").
- **"Of Gershon was the family of the Libnites" → "For Gershon: the clan of the Libnites"** (v21, 27, 33) — Hebrew לְגֵרְשׁוֹן, the לְ of 1:5–15; no verb.
- **"Those that were numbered of them, according to the number of all the males … even those that were numbered of them were" → "Those of them who were numbered, by the number of every male …: those of them who were numbered were"** (v22) — Hebrew פְּקֻדֵיהֶם twice. In v28 the word is absent, in v34 it stands once; the difference is kept. The Hebrew of v22, 28, 34 has no verb before the figure.
- **"shall pitch behind the tabernacle westward" → "shall camp behind the dwelling, westward"** (v23) — Hebrew יָמָּה, "toward the sea". "Southward" (v29) is תֵּימָנָה, "northward" (v35) צָפֹנָה; in v38 "eastward" stands for two words, קֵדְמָה ("toward the front") and מִזְרָחָה ("toward the sunrise").
- **"the chief of the house of the father of the Gershonites" → "the chieftain of the father's house for the Gershonites"** (v24, 30, 35) — Hebrew נְשִׂיא בֵית־אָב לַגֵּרְשֻׁנִּי: "father's house" is singular here. "Shall be" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"the tabernacle, and the tent, the covering thereof, and the hanging for the door" → "the dwelling and the tent, its covering, and the screen of the entrance"** (v25) — Hebrew הַמִּשְׁכָּן וְהָאֹהֶל מִכְסֵהוּ וּמָסַךְ פֶּתַח, the words of Exod 26; 35:11, 15; 40:19: the dwelling is the inner curtains, the tent the goats' hair over it.
- **"the curtain for the door of the court, which is by the tabernacle, and by the altar round about" → "the screen of the entrance of the court which is by the dwelling and by the altar, around"** (v26) — Hebrew מָסָךְ again; אֲשֶׁר עַל־הַמִּשְׁכָּן וְעַל־הַמִּזְבֵּחַ סָבִיב, literally "which is upon the dwelling and upon the altar, around": the court. **"for all the service thereof"** — Hebrew לְכֹל עֲבֹדָתוֹ.
- **"keeping the charge of the sanctuary" → "keepers of the charge of the Holy"** (v28, 32) — Hebrew הַקֹּדֶשׁ, "the Holy" ("holiness"): the holy things or the holy place; "sanctuary" (v38) is another word, מִקְדָּשׁ. The figure of v28, eight thousand six hundred, is that of the printed Hebrew; see the note on v39.
- **"on the side of the tabernacle southward" → "on the flank of the dwelling, southward"** (v29, 35) — Hebrew יֶרֶךְ, literally "thigh", as Exod 40:22, 24.
- **"the vessels of the sanctuary wherewith they minister, and the hanging, and all the service thereof" → "the utensils of the Holy with which they minister, and the screen, and all its service"** (v31) — Hebrew כְּלֵי הַקֹּדֶשׁ; הַמָּסָךְ, "the screen": here the veil before the ark, "the veil of the screen" (4:5). "Its" is masculine singular in Hebrew.
- **"chief over the chief of the Levites, and have the oversight of them that keep the charge" → "the chieftain of the chieftains of the Levites …: the oversight of those who keep the charge"** (v32) — Hebrew נְשִׂיא נְשִׂיאֵי; פְּקֻדַּת, a noun of the root of "number" and "appoint" (v10): a being set over, an office. The Hebrew has no verb ("shall be", "and have").
- **"under the custody and charge of the sons of Merari" → "the oversight of the charge of the sons of Merari"** (v36) — Hebrew וּפְקֻדַּת מִשְׁמֶרֶת, the two nouns of v32 and v25: what is set under their charge. **"the boards … the bars … the pillars … the sockets … vessels" → "boards … bars … pillars … sockets … utensils"** — the words of Exod 26; 40:18.
- **"pins" → "pegs"** (v37) — Hebrew יְתֵדֹת.
- **"those that encamp before the tabernacle toward the east, even before the tabernacle of the congregation eastward, shall be Moses" → "those who camp before the dwelling eastward, before the tent of meeting eastward: Moses"** (v38) — no verb in the Hebrew. **"keeping the charge of the sanctuary for the charge of the children of Israel"** — Hebrew לְמִשְׁמֶרֶת, "for the charge of": as the charge laid on, or kept for, the sons of Israel.
- **"at the commandment of the LORD" → "at the mouth of Yahweh"** (v39) — see v16. In the Hebrew manuscripts the word "and Aaron" is marked with dots over its letters, an old scribal sign of doubt about the word; the command of v14–16 is given to Moses alone. The printed text has the word and it is translated.
- **"twenty and two thousand" → "twenty-two thousand"** (v39) — the numbers are in words, in ordinary English order.
- **"Number all the firstborn of the males of the children of Israel … and take the number of their names" → "Number every firstborn male of the sons of Israel … and lift up the number of their names"** (v40) — Hebrew וְשָׂא אֵת מִסְפַּר שְׁמֹתָם, the verb of "lift up the head" (1:2).
- **"cattle" → "beasts"** (v41, 45) — Hebrew בְּהֵמָה, as v13.
- **"of those that were numbered of them" → "as to those of them who were numbered"** (v43) — Hebrew לִפְקֻדֵיהֶם, as Exod 30:12. The verb "were" is singular in Hebrew (וַיְהִי), agreeing with "every firstborn".
- **"for those that are to be redeemed of the two hundred and threescore and thirteen … which are more than the Levites" → "as the ransom of the two hundred and seventy-three who are over and above the Levites"** (v46) — Hebrew פְּדוּיֵי, from פָּדָה "ransom" (Exod 13:13): in form a passive participle, "the ransomed ones of", here and in v48, 51 taken as a noun, "ransom" (the price). In v49 the same word is "those ransomed by the Levites": the firstborn for whom a Levite stood. The other noun of v49, פִּדְיוֹם (only here), is "ransoming", as פִּדְיֹן in Exod 21:30. עָדַף is "be over and above".
- **"five shekels apiece by the poll, after the shekel of the sanctuary" → "five shekels each, a head; by the holy shekel"** (v47, 50) — Hebrew חֲמֵשֶׁת חֲמֵשֶׁת, "five, five"; לַגֻּלְגֹּלֶת, literally "for the skull" (Exod 38:26); שֶׁקֶל הַקֹּדֶשׁ, literally "the shekel of holiness" or "of the Holy". The shekel is a weight, not a coin.
- **"the money"** (v48–51) — Hebrew כֶּסֶף, "silver". **"wherewith the odd number of them is to be redeemed" → "the ransom of those who are over and above among them"** (v48).
- **"a thousand three hundred and threescore and five shekels" → "one thousand three hundred and sixty-five"** (v50) — "shekels" is not written in the Hebrew. Two hundred and seventy-three times five.

### Notes

- **"Generations of Aaron and Moses"** — but only Aaron's sons are listed. Moses' sons (Gershom, Eliezer) are not named here.
- **Levites in place of the firstborn** (v12–13, 41–51) — Yahweh's claim on Israel's firstborn (Exod 13) is met by the tribe of Levi, a Levite for a firstborn.
- **Three Levite clans** — Gershon (west: the dwelling, the tent, the screens and hangings), Kohath (south: the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars), Merari (north: boards, bars, pillars, sockets), and Moses and Aaron and his sons on the east, before the entrance.
- **The two hundred and seventy-three** (v46) — 22,273 firstborn against 22,000 Levites; the 273 over and above are ransomed with silver, five shekels a head, the valuation of a male from a month to five years old in Lev 27:6. Compare Num 18:16.
- **Levite total** (v39) — the Hebrew gives twenty-two thousand, but the clan figures (v22, 28, 34: 7,500 + 8,600 + 6,200) add up to 22,300. The printed text is kept in all four places: the total fits v46 (22,273 − 22,000 = 273). A common explanation is a copying slip in v28 — שֵׁשׁ "six" for שְׁלֹשׁ "three" (8,300, as some Septuagint manuscripts are said to read; from memory).
- **The Kohathites and the holiest things** (v31) — the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars; Aaron and his sons cover them first (ch 4).
