# Numbers 4

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, saying,

[2] "Lift up the head of the sons of ⟦kohath|Qehat|Kohath⟧ from the midst of the sons of Levi, by their clans, by their fathers' houses,

[3] from thirty years old and upward, and up to fifty years old, everyone who comes to the host, to do work in the tent of meeting.

[4] This is the service of the sons of ⟦kohath|Qehat|Kohath⟧ in the tent of meeting: the Holy of Holies.

[5] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons shall come in when the camp sets out, and they shall bring down the veil of the screen, and they shall cover with it the ark of the testimony.

[6] And they shall place on it a cover of skin of tachash, and they shall spread a cloth wholly of blue above, and they shall put its poles in place.

[7] And on the table of the face they shall spread a cloth of blue, and they shall place on it the dishes and the pans and the bowls and the jars of the drink offering; and the continual bread shall be on it.

[8] And they shall spread over them a cloth of scarlet, and they shall cover it with a covering of skin of tachash, and they shall put its poles in place.

[9] And they shall take a cloth of blue, and they shall cover the lampstand of the light and its lamps and its tongs and its firepans, and all the utensils of its oil with which they minister to it.

[10] And they shall place it and all its utensils into a covering of skin of tachash, and they shall place it on the carrying pole.

[11] And on the altar of gold they shall spread a cloth of blue, and they shall cover it with a covering of skin of tachash, and they shall put its poles in place.

[12] And they shall take all the utensils of ministry with which they minister in the Holy, and they shall place them into a cloth of blue, and they shall cover them with a covering of skin of tachash, and they shall place them on the carrying pole.

[13] And they shall clear the altar of fat ashes, and they shall spread on it a cloth of purple.

[14] And they shall place on it all its utensils with which they minister on it: the firepans, the forks and the shovels and the bowls, all the utensils of the altar; and they shall spread on it a cover of skin of tachash, and they shall put its poles in place.

[15] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons shall finish covering the Holy and all the utensils of the Holy when the camp sets out, and after that the sons of ⟦kohath|Qehat|Kohath⟧ shall come to carry; and they shall not touch the Holy, and die. These are the burden of the sons of ⟦kohath|Qehat|Kohath⟧ in the tent of meeting.

[16] And the oversight of ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the son of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ the priest: the oil of the light and the fragrant incense and the continual grain offering and the oil of anointing; the oversight of all the dwelling and all that is in it, in the Holy and in its utensils."

[17] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, saying,

[18] "Do not cut off the tribe of the clans of the ⟦kohathites|Qehati|Kohathites⟧ from the midst of the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧.

[19] And this do for them, and they shall live and not die when they draw near to the Holy of Holies: ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons shall come in, and they shall put them, each man, at his service and at his burden.

[20] And they shall not come in to see, at the swallowing of the Holy, and die."

[21] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[22] "Lift up the head of the sons of Gershon, them also, by their fathers' houses, by their clans;

[23] from thirty years old and upward, up to fifty years old, you shall number them: everyone who comes to muster in the host, to serve service in the tent of meeting.

[24] This is the service of the clans of the ⟦gershonites|Gershunni|Gershonites⟧, to serve and for burden.

[25] And they shall carry the curtains of the dwelling, and the tent of meeting, its covering and the covering of tachash that is on it above, and the screen of the entrance of the tent of meeting,

[26] and the hangings of the court, and the screen of the entrance of the gate of the court which is by the dwelling and by the altar, around, and their cords, and all the utensils of their service; and all that is to be done for them; and they shall serve.

[27] At the mouth of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons shall be all the service of the sons of the ⟦gershonites|Gershunni|Gershonites⟧, for all their burden and for all their service; and you shall appoint upon them, as a charge, all their burden.

[28] This is the service of the clans of the sons of the ⟦gershonites|Gershunni|Gershonites⟧ in the tent of meeting; and their charge is by the hand of ⟦ithamar|Itamar|Ithamar⟧ the son of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ the priest.

[29] The sons of Merari: by their clans, by their fathers' houses you shall number them;

[30] from thirty years old and upward, and up to fifty years old, you shall number them: everyone who comes to the host, to serve the service of the tent of meeting.

[31] And this is the charge of their burden, for all their service in the tent of meeting: the boards of the dwelling and its bars and its pillars and its sockets,

[32] and the pillars of the court around, and their sockets and their pegs and their cords, for all their utensils and for all their service; and by names you shall number the utensils of the charge of their burden.

[33] This is the service of the clans of the sons of Merari, for all their service in the tent of meeting, by the hand of ⟦ithamar|Itamar|Ithamar⟧ the son of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ the priest."

[34] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and the chieftains of the congregation numbered the sons of the ⟦kohathites|Qehati|Kohathites⟧ by their clans and by their fathers' houses,

[35] from thirty years old and upward, and up to fifty years old, everyone who comes to the host, for the service in the tent of meeting.

[36] And those of them who were numbered, by their clans, were two thousand seven hundred and fifty.

[37] These are those who were numbered of the clans of the ⟦kohathites|Qehati|Kohathites⟧, everyone who serves in the tent of meeting, whom ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ numbered at the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[38] And those who were numbered of the sons of Gershon, by their clans and by their fathers' houses,

[39] from thirty years old and upward, and up to fifty years old, everyone who comes to the host, for the service in the tent of meeting —

[40] and those of them who were numbered, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, were two thousand six hundred and thirty.

[41] These are those who were numbered of the clans of the sons of Gershon, everyone who serves in the tent of meeting, whom ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ numbered at the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[42] And those who were numbered of the clans of the sons of Merari, by their clans, by their fathers' houses,

[43] from thirty years old and upward, and up to fifty years old, everyone who comes to the host, for the service in the tent of meeting —

[44] and those of them who were numbered, by their clans, were three thousand two hundred.

[45] These are those who were numbered of the clans of the sons of Merari, whom ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ numbered at the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[46] All those who were numbered, whom ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and the chieftains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ numbered, the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, by their clans and by their fathers' houses,

[47] from thirty years old and upward, and up to fifty years old, everyone who comes to serve the service of service and the service of burden in the tent of meeting —

[48] and those of them who were numbered were eight thousand five hundred and eighty.

[49] At the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ he numbered them, by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, each man at his service and at his burden; and those of his who were numbered, whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew יהוה, the Tetragrammaton.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Take the sum of" → "Lift up the head of"** (v2, 22) — Hebrew נָשֹׂא אֶת־רֹאשׁ, as 1:2; the verb is an infinitive used as a command. **"after their families, by the house of their fathers" → "by their clans, by their fathers' houses"** — the formula of chapter 1; in v22 the two halves stand in the other order, and in v34, 38, 46 they are joined by "and".
- **"from thirty years old and upward even until fifty years old" → "from thirty years old and upward, and up to fifty years old"** (v3, 23, 30, 35, 39, 43, 47) — literally "from a son of thirty years"; v23 has no "and" before "up to".
- **"all that enter into the host, to do the work" → "everyone who comes to the host, to do work"** (v3, 30, 35, 39, 43) — Hebrew כָּל־בָּא לַצָּבָא: צָבָא "host", the word of 1:3 ("everyone who goes out to the host"), here of the body of Levites that serves at the tent. מְלָאכָה is "work" (v3 only); עֲבֹדָה is "service" everywhere.
- **"about the most holy things" → "the Holy of Holies"** (v4, 19) — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים, with the article, literally "the holiness of the holinesses": the same words as the name of the inner room (Exod 26:33). Here the most holy things are meant, which the Kohathites carry; "about" and "things" are not in the Hebrew.
- **"when the camp setteth forward, Aaron shall come, and his sons, and they shall take down the covering vail" → "Aaron and his sons shall come in when the camp sets out, and they shall bring down the veil of the screen"** (v5) — Hebrew פָּרֹכֶת הַמָּסָךְ, as Exod 35:12; 40:21; הוֹרִיד "bring down", as 1:51.
- **"the covering of badgers' skins" → "a cover of skin of tachash"** (v6, 14); **"a covering of badgers' skins" → "a covering of skin of tachash"** (v8, 10, 11, 12) — two Hebrew nouns of one root: כְּסוּי (only v6, 14) and מִכְסֶה. עוֹר תַּחַשׁ: what animal or kind of leather תַּחַשׁ is, is not known; the word is kept, as in Exod 25:5; 26:14. Here "skin" is singular. In v25 only "the covering of tachash".
- **"a cloth wholly of blue"** (v6–13) — Hebrew בֶּגֶד, the word "garment": here a cloth. כְּלִיל is "whole, entire". "Blue", "scarlet" (v8, literally "worm of scarlet") and "purple" (v13) are the dyes and stand for cloth dyed with them, as Exod 25:4.
- **"shall put in the staves thereof" → "they shall put its poles in place"** (v6, 8, 11, 14) — Hebrew וְשָׂמוּ בַּדָּיו, "and they shall put its poles"; "in place" is supplied for English.
- **"the table of shewbread" → "the table of the face"** (v7) — Hebrew שֻׁלְחַן הַפָּנִים, as "the bread of the face" (Exod 25:30). **"the dishes, and the spoons, and the bowls, and covers to cover withal" → "the dishes and the pans and the bowls and the jars of the drink offering"** — the four vessels of Exod 25:29 (קְעָרֹת, כַּפֹּת, מְנַקִּיֹּת, קְשׂוֹת), the last two here in the other order; their exact kinds are uncertain. **"the continual bread"** — Hebrew לֶחֶם הַתָּמִיד, literally "the bread of continuity".
- **"the candlestick of the light, and his lamps, and his tongs, and his snuffdishes, and all the oil vessels thereof" → "the lampstand of the light and its lamps and its tongs and its firepans, and all the utensils of its oil"** (v9) — the words of Exod 25:37–38; 35:14.
- **"put it upon a bar" → "place it on the carrying pole"** (v10, 12) — Hebrew הַמּוֹט, a pole on which a load is carried (13:23); another word than בַּד, the "poles" of the ark, the table and the altars. "Carrying" is added to keep the two apart.
- **"the golden altar" → "the altar of gold"** (v11) — Hebrew מִזְבַּח הַזָּהָב, as Exod 40:5, 26.
- **"all the instruments of ministry, wherewith they minister in the sanctuary" → "all the utensils of ministry with which they minister in the Holy"** (v12) — Hebrew כְּלֵי הַשָּׁרֵת; בַּקֹּדֶשׁ, "in the Holy".
- **"take away the ashes from the altar" → "clear the altar of fat ashes"** (v13) — Hebrew דִּשֵּׁן, as Exod 27:3.
- **"the censers, the fleshhooks, and the shovels, and the basons, all the vessels of the altar" → "the firepans, the forks and the shovels and the bowls, all the utensils of the altar"** (v14) — the words of Exod 27:3; 38:3.
- **"have made an end of covering the sanctuary, and all the vessels of the sanctuary, as the camp is to set forward" → "shall finish covering the Holy and all the utensils of the Holy when the camp sets out"** (v15) — Hebrew הַקֹּדֶשׁ, "the Holy": here the holy things. **"to bear it: but they shall not touch any holy thing, lest they die" → "to carry; and they shall not touch the Holy, and die"** — the Hebrew has no "it"; וָמֵתוּ is "and they die": the "not" reaches over both verbs (they are not to touch and so die). **"These things are the burden"** — Hebrew מַשָּׂא, what is carried, from נָשָׂא "lift, carry".
- **"to the office of Eleazar … pertaineth … and the oversight of all the tabernacle" → "the oversight of Eleazar …: … the oversight of all the dwelling"** (v16) — Hebrew פְּקֻדָּה twice, as 3:32, 36; no verb. **"the oil for the light, and the sweet incense, and the daily meat offering, and the anointing oil" → "the oil of the light and the fragrant incense and the continual grain offering and the oil of anointing"** — the words of Exod 35:14, 15; 40:9; מִנְחַת הַתָּמִיד (Exod 29:38–42; Lev 6:20). **"in the sanctuary, and in the vessels thereof" → "in the Holy and in its utensils"** — Hebrew בְּקֹדֶשׁ וּבְכֵלָיו; "Holy" has no article here.
- **"Cut ye not off the tribe of the families of the Kohathites" → "Do not cut off the tribe of the clans of the Kohathites"** (v18) — Hebrew אַל־תַּכְרִיתוּ: do not be the cause of their being cut off. "Tribe" is here שֵׁבֶט (literally "rod"), not מַטֶּה.
- **"But thus do unto them, that they may live, and not die, when they approach unto the most holy things" → "And this do for them, and they shall live and not die when they draw near to the Holy of Holies"** (v19) — joined by "and"; see v4. **"appoint them every one to his service and to his burden" → "they shall put them, each man, at his service and at his burden"** — Hebrew וְשָׂמוּ אוֹתָם אִישׁ אִישׁ, literally "a man, a man"; the two prepositions are עַל and אֶל.
- **"But they shall not go in to see when the holy things are covered, lest they die" → "And they shall not come in to see, at the swallowing of the Holy, and die"** (v20) — Hebrew וְלֹא־יָבֹאוּ לִרְאוֹת כְּבַלַּע אֶת־הַקֹּדֶשׁ וָמֵתוּ, word for word. בַּלַּע is the infinitive of בָּלַע "swallow" with כְּ "as, at the time of". It is taken in two ways: (1) "when the Holy is swallowed up", that is, while the holy things are being wrapped and covered (v5–15): they are not to come in and watch this; (2) "for the time of a swallow", an instant (as Job 7:19): they are not to come in to see the Holy even for a moment. In the second, "the Holy" is the object of "see"; in the first, of "swallow". The verse keeps the Hebrew order and does not decide. "And die": as v15.
- **"all that enter in to perform the service, to do the work" → "everyone who comes to muster in the host, to serve service"** (v23) — Hebrew לִצְבֹא צָבָא, verb and noun of one root ("to host in the host"), the verb of Exod 38:8; לַעֲבֹד עֲבֹדָה, likewise verb and noun of one root.
- **"to serve, and for burdens" → "to serve and for burden"** (v24) — Hebrew לַעֲבֹד וּלְמַשָּׂא.
- **"the curtains of the tabernacle, and the tabernacle of the congregation, his covering, and the covering of the badgers' skins that is above upon it, and the hanging for the door" → "the curtains of the dwelling, and the tent of meeting, its covering and the covering of tachash that is on it above, and the screen of the entrance"** (v25) — the words of Exod 26:1, 14, 36.
- **"the hanging for the door of the gate of the court, which is by the tabernacle and by the altar round about" → "the screen of the entrance of the gate of the court which is by the dwelling and by the altar, around"** (v26) — as 3:26. **"all the instruments of their service, and all that is made for them: so shall they serve" → "all the utensils of their service; and all that is to be done for them; and they shall serve"** — Hebrew וְאֵת כָּל־אֲשֶׁר יֵעָשֶׂה לָהֶם וְעָבָדוּ: "for them" may be the things (all that has to be done for them) or the men; the clause is left as it stands.
- **"At the appointment of Aaron and his sons" → "At the mouth of Aaron and his sons"** (v27) — Hebrew עַל־פִּי, as of Yahweh in v37. **"ye shall appoint unto them in charge all their burdens" → "you shall appoint upon them, as a charge, all their burden"** — Hebrew וּפְקַדְתֶּם עֲלֵהֶם בְּמִשְׁמֶרֶת: the verb "number" of these chapters with עַל, "lay upon, commit to"; "burden" is singular.
- **"under the hand of Ithamar" → "by the hand of Ithamar"** (v28, 33) — Hebrew בְּיַד, as Exod 38:21 and as "by the hand of Moses" (v37, 45, 49).
- **"the charge of their burden, according to all their service" → "the charge of their burden, for all their service"** (v31) — Hebrew מִשְׁמֶרֶת מַשָּׂאָם לְכָל־עֲבֹדָתָם.
- **"with all their instruments, and with all their service: and by name ye shall reckon the instruments of the charge of their burden" → "for all their utensils and for all their service; and by names you shall number the utensils of the charge of their burden"** (v32) — Hebrew תִּפְקְדוּ, the verb "number" again: each piece is to be listed by name.
- **"the chief of the congregation" → "the chieftains of the congregation"** (v34) — Hebrew נְשִׂיאֵי הָעֵדָה; in v46 "the chieftains of Israel".
- **"every one that entereth into the service, for the work in the tabernacle" → "everyone who comes to the host, for the service in the tent"** (v35, 39, 43) — Hebrew לַצָּבָא לַעֲבֹדָה.
- **"two thousand seven hundred and fifty"** (v36, 40, 44, 48) — the numbers are in words, in ordinary English order: two thousand seven hundred and fifty, two thousand six hundred and thirty, three thousand two hundred; together eight thousand five hundred and eighty.
- **"all that might do service" → "everyone who serves"** (v37, 41) — Hebrew כָּל־הָעֹבֵד, a participle. **"according to the commandment of the LORD by the hand of Moses" → "at the mouth of Yahweh, by the hand of Moses"** (v37, 45, 49) — Hebrew עַל־פִּי יְהוָה בְּיַד־מֹשֶׁה; v41 has no "by the hand of Moses".
- **"All those that were numbered of the Levites, whom Moses and Aaron and the chief of Israel numbered" → "All those who were numbered, whom Moses and Aaron and the chieftains of Israel numbered, the Levites"** (v46) — the Hebrew order; "the Levites" is the object of "numbered".
- **"every one that came to do the service of the ministry, and the service of the burden" → "everyone who comes to serve the service of service and the service of burden"** (v47) — Hebrew לַעֲבֹד עֲבֹדַת עֲבֹדָה וַעֲבֹדַת מַשָּׂא: the one root four times.
- **"According to the commandment of the LORD they were numbered by the hand of Moses, every one according to his service, and according to his burden: thus were they numbered of him, as the LORD commanded Moses" → "At the mouth of Yahweh he numbered them, by the hand of Moses, each man at his service and at his burden; and those of his who were numbered, whom Yahweh had commanded Moses"** (v49) — Hebrew פָּקַד אוֹתָם, an active verb with no subject named ("one numbered them"); אִישׁ אִישׁ, "a man, a man". The last clause, וּפְקֻדָיו אֲשֶׁר־צִוָּה יְהוָה אֶת־מֹשֶׁה, is hard: literally "and his numbered ones, whom Yahweh commanded Moses". It is translated as it stands. The old versions are said to read "as Yahweh commanded Moses" (from memory).

### Notes

- **The covering before the carrying** — Aaron and his sons cover everything before the sons of Kohath come; the Kohathites carry, but they do not touch the Holy (v15) or come in to see (v20). Compare 2 Sam 6:6–7.
- **Thirty to fifty years** — the years of service here. Compare Num 8:24 (from twenty-five) and 1 Chr 23:24 (from twenty).
- **The three bodies of Levites**: (1) the Kohathites carry the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars and their utensils — 2,750. (2) The Gershonites carry the curtains, coverings, screens and hangings — 2,630. (3) The sons of Merari carry boards, bars, pillars, sockets — 3,200. Together 8,580.
- **Order** — Kohath, the second son of Levi (3:17), stands first here, as the one who carries the most holy things.
