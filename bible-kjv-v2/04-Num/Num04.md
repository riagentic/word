## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"badgers' skins" → "fine leather"** (v6, 8, 10, 11, 12, 14, 25) — Hebrew עֹרֹת תְּחָשִׁים *orot techashim*. Meaning uncertain; KJV "badgers" unsupported. LXX "hyacinth-blue leather"; modern proposals include dolphin/dugong skin (marine mammal), fine-tanned leather, or *tachash* = specific dyed hide. "Fine leather" follows modern consensus.
- **"shewbread" → "Presence bread" / "regular bread"** (v7) — Hebrew לֶחֶם הַפָּנִים *lechem ha-panim* = bread of the Face/Presence. Always before Yahweh.
- **"spoons" → "ladles"** (v7) — Hebrew כַּפּוֹת *kappot*. Incense ladles, not eating spoons.
- **"covers to cover withal" → "pitchers for the drink offering"** (v7) — Hebrew קְשׂוֹת הַנֶּסֶךְ *qesot ha-nesekh*. Pouring vessels for libations.
- **"snuffdishes" → "fire-pans"** (v9) — Hebrew מַחְתּוֹת *machtot*. Coal/ash containers for the lamps.
- **"bar" → "carrying-frame"** (v10, 12) — Hebrew מוֹט *mot* = pole / frame for carrying loads.
- **"fleshhooks" → "forks"** (v14) — Hebrew מִזְלָגֹת *mizlagot*. Large meat-forks.
- **"basons" → "basins"** (v14) — archaic spelling.
- **"sweet incense" → "fragrant incense"** (v16) — Hebrew *qetoret ha-sammim*.
- **"daily meat offering" → "regular grain offering"** (v16).

### Notes
- **Priestly packers** — Aaron's sons wrap/cover everything *before* Kohathites approach. The Kohathites carry — but never see or touch unwrapped holy things. Uzzah later dies (2 Sam 6) for touching the ark; this chapter is that episode's Torah background.
- **Age 30–50** — active service age for Levites. Compare Num 8:24 (25–50 with some reading "25 apprentice, 30 full"); 1 Chr 23:24 (later 20+). Different eras adjusted.
- **Levite subdivision**: (1) Kohathites carry sacred vessels by hand — 2,750 men. (2) Gershonites carry fabric / curtain pieces — 2,630. (3) Merarites carry structural pieces — 3,200. Total 8,580.
- **"Not even for a moment"** (v20) — Kohathites cannot glimpse unwrapped holy things. The sacred is fatal to the unauthorized gaze; cf. Ex 19:21, 33:20.
