## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"families" → "clans"** (throughout) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּחוֹת *mishpachot*. Sub-tribal clan-units.
- **"sign" → "warning"** (v10) — Hebrew נֵס *nes* = sign / standard / warning.
- **"Pharez" → "Perez"** (v20, 21) — standard modern spelling (consistent with Gen 38).
- **"Sarah" → "Serah"** (v46) — Hebrew שֶׂרַח *Serach*, Asher's daughter; distinct from Abraham's wife.
- **Traditional number-forms modernized** throughout.

### Notes
- **Second census** — 38 years after the first (Num 1). The first-generation census totalled 603,550; this new generation 601,730, a slight decrease. The old generation has died out (v64–65); only Caleb and Joshua survive. The numbers hold steady — Yahweh's promise of multiplication continues through the judgment.
- **Dathan, Abiram, Korah narrative inset** (v9–11) — but note: "the sons of Korah did not die." Korah's line survived. This explains how "the sons of Korah" become temple singers (Ps 42–49, 84–88).
- **Zelophehad's daughters** (v33) — introduced here with no narrative context. Their inheritance case drives ch 27 and ch 36.
- **Land allotment logic** (v52–56) — combination of proportionate inheritance (to each tribe by population) and random lot (which specific parcel). Both just allocation and divine assignment.
- **Priesthood genealogy** (v58–61) — the priestly line traced: Levi → Kohath → Amram → Aaron & Moses & Miriam. Aaron's sons: Nadab, Abihu (dead), Eleazar (current high priest), Ithamar.
- **"Not one left"** (v64–65) — Yahweh's judgment on the wilderness generation is complete. The new generation stands ready to enter.
