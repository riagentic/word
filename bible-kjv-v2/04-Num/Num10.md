# Numbers 10

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Make for yourself two trumpets of silver; of hammered work you shall make them; and they shall be for you for the calling of the congregation and for the setting out of the camps.

[3] And they shall blow on them, and all the congregation shall meet with you at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

[4] And if they blow on one, the chieftains, the heads of the thousands of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, shall meet with you.

[5] And you shall blow a blast, and the camps that camp eastward shall set out.

[6] And you shall blow a blast a second time, and the camps that camp southward shall set out; a blast they shall blow for their journeys.

[7] And in assembling the assembly you shall blow, and you shall not sound a blast.

[8] And the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, the priests, shall blow on the trumpets; and they shall be for you an everlasting statute for your generations.

[9] And when you go into war in your land against the adversary who harasses you, you shall sound a blast with the trumpets, and you shall be remembered before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and you shall be saved from your enemies.

[10] And on the day of your gladness, and at your appointed times, and at the beginnings of your months, you shall blow on the trumpets over your burnt offerings and over the sacrifices of your peace offerings; and they shall be for you for a memorial before your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[11] And it came to pass in the second year, in the second month, on the twentieth of the month, that the cloud was taken up from over the dwelling of the testimony.

[12] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ set out by their journeys from the wilderness of Sinai, and the cloud abode in the wilderness of Paran.

[13] And they set out at the first, at the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[14] And the standard of the camp of the sons of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ set out at the first, by their hosts; and over his host was ⟦nahshon|Nachshon|Nahshon⟧ the son of ⟦amminadab|Amminadav|Amminadab⟧.

[15] And over the host of the tribe of the sons of ⟦issachar|Yissachar|Issachar⟧ was ⟦nethanel|Netanel|Nethanel⟧ the son of ⟦zuar|Tzuar|Zuar⟧.

[16] And over the host of the tribe of the sons of ⟦zebulun|Zevulun|Zebulun⟧ was ⟦eliab|Eliav|Eliab⟧ the son of ⟦helon|Chelon|Helon⟧.

[17] And the dwelling would be brought down, and the sons of Gershon and the sons of Merari would set out, the carriers of the dwelling.

[18] And the standard of the camp of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧ would set out, by their hosts; and over his host was ⟦elizur|Elitzur|Elizur⟧ the son of Shedeur.

[19] And over the host of the tribe of the sons of ⟦simeon|Shimon|Simeon⟧ was Shelumiel the son of ⟦zurishaddai|Tzurishaddai|Zurishaddai⟧.

[20] And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Gad was ⟦eliasaph|Elyasaf|Eliasaph⟧ the son of Deuel.

[21] And the ⟦kohathites|Qehati|Kohathites⟧ would set out, the carriers of the sanctuary; and they would raise up the dwelling by the time they came.

[22] And the standard of the camp of the sons of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ would set out, by their hosts; and over his host was Elishama the son of Ammihud.

[23] And over the host of the tribe of the sons of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ was ⟦gamaliel|Gamliel|Gamaliel⟧ the son of ⟦pedahzur|Pedahtzur|Pedahzur⟧.

[25] And the standard of the camp of the sons of Dan would set out, the rear guard for all the camps, by their hosts; and over his host was ⟦ahiezer|Achiezer|Ahiezer⟧ the son of Ammishaddai.

[26] And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Asher was Pagiel the son of Ochran.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew יהוה, the Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v9, 10) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"of a whole piece" → "of hammered work"** (v2) — Hebrew מִקְשָׁה, as of the lampstand (8:4; Exod 25:31). **"trumpets"** — Hebrew חֲצֹצְרָה, the straight trumpet of metal; the *shofar*, the horn, is another word and does not stand in this chapter.
- **"that thou mayest use them for the calling of the assembly, and for the journeying of the camps" → "they shall be for you for the calling of the congregation and for the setting out of the camps"** (v2) — Hebrew וְהָיוּ לְךָ; no word for "use". מִקְרָא "calling" is the word of "holy convocation" (Lev 23:2); עֵדָה is "congregation"; מַסַּע is the noun of נָסַע "set out", elsewhere "journey" (v6, 12, 28).
- **"when they shall blow with them" → "they shall blow on them"** (v3) — Hebrew וְתָקְעוּ בָּהֵן; תָּקַע is "thrust, strike", of a trumpet "blow". The Hebrew does not say "both".
- **"shall assemble themselves to thee" → "shall meet with you"** (v3, 4) — Hebrew נוֹעֲדוּ אֵלֶיךָ, literally "meet by appointment to you": the verb from which מוֹעֵד in "tent of meeting" is made (Exod 25:22). "Assemble" is another word (v7).
- **"the princes, which are heads of the thousands of Israel" → "the chieftains, the heads of the thousands of Israel"** (v4) — Hebrew נְשִׂיאִים, literally "those lifted up"; אַלְפֵי, "thousands": the large kin groups (1:16); so also in v36.
- **"When ye blow an alarm" → "you shall blow a blast"** (v5, 6) — Hebrew תְּרוּעָה, literally "a shouting": the word for the war cry or cry of joy of a people (Josh 6:5), here said of the trumpets: a blast of alarm, set apart from the plain blowing (תָּקַע) of v3, 4, 7. "Shouting" would make an English reader hear voices; the Hebrew speaks of the trumpets. The same root is the verb "sound a blast" in v7, 9 (הֵרִיעַ, literally "shout"); Lev 23:24 has the noun with neither voice nor instrument named ("a memorial of shouting"). The verse joins its two halves with "and".
- **"the camps that lie on the east parts" → "the camps that camp eastward"** (v5, 6) — Hebrew הַחֹנִים, the verb of "camp"; קֵדְמָה, literally "toward the front"; תֵּימָנָה "southward", literally "toward the right". The Hebrew names only the east and the south; the Septuagint goes on with the west and the north (cited from memory).
- **"when the congregation is to be gathered together" → "in assembling the assembly"** (v7) — Hebrew בְהַקְהִיל אֶת־הַקָּהָל, verb and noun of one root; קָהָל "assembly" is another word than עֵדָה "congregation" (v2, 3).
- **"ye shall not sound an alarm" → "you shall not sound a blast"** (v7) — Hebrew לֹא תָרִיעוּ, literally "you shall not shout"; see v5.
- **"an ordinance for ever throughout your generations" → "an everlasting statute for your generations"** (v8) — Hebrew לְחֻקַּת עוֹלָם לְדֹרֹתֵיכֶם; "everlasting" is the noun עוֹלָם, time out of sight.
- **"if ye go to war in your land against the enemy that oppresseth you" → "when you go into war in your land against the adversary who harasses you"** (v9) — Hebrew כִּי "when"; הַצַּר הַצֹּרֵר, noun and verb of one root ("the adversary who is adversary to you"); "enemies" at the end of the verse is another word (אֹיֵב).
- **"ye shall blow an alarm with the trumpets" → "you shall sound a blast with the trumpets"** (v9) — Hebrew וַהֲרֵעֹתֶם, the verb of תְּרוּעָה, literally "you shall shout with the trumpets".
- **"in the day of your gladness, and in your solemn days, and in the beginnings of your months" → "on the day of your gladness, and at your appointed times, and at the beginnings of your months"** (v10) — Hebrew מוֹעֲדִים "appointed times" (Lev 23:2); רָאשֵׁי חָדְשֵׁיכֶם, literally "the heads of your months".
- **"that they may be to you for a memorial" → "and they shall be for you for a memorial"** (v10) — Hebrew וְהָיוּ; זִכָּרוֹן from זָכַר "remember" (v9).
- **"it came to pass" kept; "on the twentieth day of the second month, in the second year" → "in the second year, in the second month, on the twentieth of the month"** (v11) — the Hebrew order. **"was taken up from off the tabernacle of the testimony" → "was taken up from over the dwelling of the testimony"** — Hebrew נַעֲלָה, מֵעַל, מִשְׁכַּן הָעֵדֻת, as 9:17; Exod 40:36.
- **"took their journeys" → "set out by their journeys"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיִּסְעוּ … לְמַסְעֵיהֶם, verb and noun of one root (Exod 17:1). **"rested" → "abode"** — Hebrew שָׁכַן.
- **"they first took their journey according to the commandment of the LORD" → "they set out at the first, at the mouth of Yahweh"** (v13) — Hebrew בָּרִאשֹׁנָה, "at the first": for the first time, or (as in v14) first in order; עַל־פִּי יְהוָה as 9:18–23.
- **"In the first place went the standard" → "the standard … set out at the first"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיִּסַּע … בָּרִאשֹׁנָה. דֶּגֶל is a standard and the body of men under it; some take it as "division".
- **"according to their armies: and over his host" → "by their hosts; and over his host"** (v14–27) — one Hebrew word, צָבָא "host"; "his host" is singular in the Hebrew each time.
- **"the tribe of the children of" → "the tribe of the sons of"** (v15–27) — Hebrew מַטֶּה, literally "staff".
- **"the tabernacle was taken down; and the sons of Gershon and the sons of Merari set forward" → "the dwelling would be brought down, and the sons of Gershon and the sons of Merari would set out"** (v17) — Hebrew הוּרַד, the passive of "bring down" (1:51). From v17 to v25 the verbs of setting out (וְהוּרַד, וְנָסְעוּ, וְנָסַע, וְהֵקִימוּ) are no longer the narrative form of v12–14 but forms that elsewhere tell what was done each time (9:17–23; Exod 40:31): the order of every setting out. They can also be read as plain past, "was brought down … set out". **"bearing the tabernacle" → "the carriers of the dwelling"** — Hebrew נֹשְׂאֵי, a noun form; so in v21.
- **"bearing the sanctuary" → "the carriers of the sanctuary"** (v21) — Hebrew הַמִּקְדָּשׁ "the sanctuary": here the holy things that the Kohathites carry (4:4–15). **"and the other did set up the tabernacle against they came" → "and they would raise up the dwelling by the time they came"** — Hebrew וְהֵקִימוּ … עַד־בֹּאָם, literally "until their coming"; "they" who raise it up are not named (the sons of Gershon and Merari, v17).
- **"the rereward of all the camps" → "the rear guard for all the camps"** (v25) — Hebrew מְאַסֵּף לְכָל־הַמַּחֲנֹת, literally "the gatherer for all the camps": the one who comes last and gathers up (Josh 6:9; Isa 52:12).
- **"Thus were the journeyings … when they set forward" → "These were the journeys … and they set out"** (v28) — Hebrew אֵלֶּה מַסְעֵי; וַיִּסָּעוּ "and they set out".
- **"Raguel" → "Reuel"** (v29) — Hebrew רְעוּאֵל, as Exod 2:18. **"Moses' father in law" → "the father-in-law of Moses"** — Hebrew חֹתֵן מֹשֶׁה, the wife's father. In the Hebrew the words may belong to Hobab or to Reuel; the English word order leaves it so. Exod 2:18–21 names Reuel as the father of Moses' wife, Exod 3:1; 18:1 names Jethro, and Judg 4:11 calls Hobab חֹתֵן מֹשֶׁה.
- **"We are journeying unto the place" → "We are setting out for the place"** (v29) — Hebrew נֹסְעִים. **"I will give it you" → "It I will give to you"** — Hebrew אֹתוֹ אֶתֵּן לָכֶם, with "it" set first. **"we will do thee good" → "we will do good to you"**; **"hath spoken good"** — Hebrew הֵיטִיב and דִּבֶּר־טוֹב; "promised" is not a Hebrew word here.
- **"I will depart to mine own land" → "but to my land and to my kindred I will go"** (v30) — Hebrew כִּי אִם … אֵלֵךְ, the same verb "go" twice; no "own".
- **"Leave us not, I pray thee" → "Please do not forsake us"** (v31) — Hebrew אַל־נָא תַּעֲזֹב; the speaker is not named. **"forasmuch as thou knowest how we are to encamp" → "for therefore you know our camping"** — Hebrew כִּי עַל־כֵּן, literally "for therefore" (Gen 18:5; 19:8; 33:10): "since indeed"; חֲנֹתֵנוּ, "our camping". **"thou mayest be to us instead of eyes" → "you shall be to us for eyes"** — Hebrew וְהָיִיתָ לָּנוּ לְעֵינָיִם.
- **"what goodness the LORD shall do unto us, the same will we do unto thee" → "that good which Yahweh shall do to us, we will do to you"** (v32) — Hebrew וְהָיָה … וְהָיָה, "and it shall be" twice; the verb is "do good" both times (הֵיטִיב, as v29), with עִמָּנוּ "with us"; no "the same".
- **"three days' journey" → "a way of three days"** (v33) — Hebrew דֶּרֶךְ שְׁלֹשֶׁת יָמִים, twice. **"went before them" → "was journeying before them"** — Hebrew נֹסֵעַ, the verb of "set out". **"to search out a resting place" → "to seek out … a resting place"** — Hebrew תּוּר, the verb of the men sent into the land in ch. 13; מְנוּחָה from נוּחַ "rest" (v36).
- **"when they went out of the camp" → "when they set out from the camp"** (v34) — Hebrew בְּנָסְעָם.
- **"when the ark set forward, that Moses said" → "when the ark set out, that Moses said"** (v35, 36) — in v35 the Hebrew has the narrative form (וַיֹּאמֶר), in v36 the form of what was done each time (יֹאמַר, "he would say").
- **"Rise up, LORD" → "Arise, Yahweh"** (v35) — Hebrew קוּמָה. **"flee before thee" → "flee from your face"** — Hebrew מִפָּנֶיךָ. Each of the two wishes begins with "and". Ps 68:1 takes up the verse.
- **"Return, O LORD, unto the many thousands of Israel" → "Return, Yahweh, the ten thousands of the thousands of Israel"** (v36) — Hebrew שׁוּבָה יְהוָה רִבְבוֹת אַלְפֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל. The Hebrew has no "to" and no "O". The line is read as "Return, Yahweh, to the ten thousands …" (a goal without a preposition), as "Bring back, Yahweh, the ten thousands …", or with the last words as a name of Yahweh; the Hebrew words are kept as they stand. רְבָבָה is "ten thousand", אֶלֶף "thousand" (the kin group of v4).
- **Inverted *nun*** (v35–36) — in the Masoretic text an inverted letter *nun* stands before v35 and after v36, setting the two verses apart.

### Notes
- **Two trumpets of silver** (v1–10) — for calling the congregation, for the setting out of the camps, in war and at the appointed times. The Arch of Titus in Rome shows such trumpets among the things carried off from the temple.
- **Setting out from Sinai** (v11–12) — on the twentieth of the second month of the second year; Israel came to Sinai in the third month of the first year (Exod 19:1).
- **Hobab, Reuel, Jethro** — the father of Moses' wife is called Reuel (Exod 2:18), Jethro (Exod 3:1; 18:1) and, in Judg 4:11, Hobab. Whether these are names of one man or of a father and a son the text does not say.
- **"Eyes" in the wilderness** (v29–32) — Moses asks Hobab to go with them; the answer of Hobab to the second request is not told. Judg 1:16; 4:11 find his kin among Israel.
- **The words at the setting out and the resting of the ark** (v35–36) — two short poems, set apart in the Masoretic text by inverted *nun* letters.
