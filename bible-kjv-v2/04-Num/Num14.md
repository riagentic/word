## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"lifted up their voice, and cried" → "lifted up and gave forth their voice"** (v1) — Hebrew וַתִּשָּׂא … וַיִּתְּנוּ אֶת־קוֹלָם, literally "and (the congregation) lifted up, and they gave their voice"; there is no second verb "cried".
- **"murmured" → "grumbled"** (v2, 27, 29, 36) — Hebrew לוּן with עַל, and תְּלֻנּוֹת "grumblings" (Exod 16). **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"Would God that we had died … would God we had died" → "Would that we had died … would that we had died"** (v2) — Hebrew לוּ twice; "God" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"wherefore hath the LORD brought us … that our wives and our children should be a prey? were it not better" → "why is Yahweh bringing us … Our wives and our little ones will be for plunder. Is it not good"** (v3) — Hebrew מֵבִיא, a participle; a new clause begins at "our wives"; טַף "little ones"; לָבַז "for plunder"; טוֹב "good" (the comparison is in the sense, not in a word).
- **"one to another, Let us make a captain" → "each man to his brother, 'Let us appoint a head'"** (v4) — Hebrew אִישׁ אֶל־אָחִיו; נִתְּנָה רֹאשׁ, literally "let us give a head".
- **"which were of them that searched the land, rent their clothes" → "of those who explored the land, tore their garments"** (v6) — Hebrew תּוּר (13:2); בֶּגֶד.
- **"an exceeding good land" → "the land is very, very good"** (v7) — Hebrew טוֹבָה הָאָרֶץ מְאֹד מְאֹד, "good is the land, very, very".
- **"Only rebel not ye against the LORD, neither fear ye" → "Only against Yahweh do not rebel; and you, do not fear"** (v9) — the Hebrew order; מָרַד "rebel". **"their defence is departed from them" → "Their shade has departed from over them"** — Hebrew צִלָּם "their shade, shadow": their shelter.
- **"bade stone them" → "said to stone them"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמְרוּ … לִרְגּוֹם. **"in the tabernacle of the congregation before all" → "in the tent of meeting to all"** — Hebrew בְּאֹהֶל מוֹעֵד אֶל־כָּל.
- **"provoke me" → "spurn me"** (v11, 23) — Hebrew נִאֵץ "treat with contempt". **"for all the signs which I have shewed among them" → "for all the signs that I have done in its midst"** — Hebrew בְּכֹל הָאֹתוֹת, "in / with all the signs": in spite of them. The people is "it" (singular) in v11–12.
- **"smite them with the pestilence, and disinherit them" → "strike it with the pestilence and dispossess it"** (v12) — Hebrew אַכֶּנּוּ (הִכָּה); אוֹרִשֶׁנּוּ, the causative of יָרַשׁ "possess".
- **"Then the Egyptians shall hear it" → "And the Egyptians will hear"** (v13) — Hebrew וְשָׁמְעוּ מִצְרַיִם, "Egypt" with a plural verb; "from their midst" is literally "from its midst". The sentence of v13–16 is broken in the Hebrew, and its clauses are left as they stand.
- **"they will tell it to the inhabitants of this land: for they have heard" → "they will say to the inhabitant of this land — they have heard"** (v14) — Hebrew וְאָמְרוּ אֶל־יוֹשֵׁב … שָׁמְעוּ; יוֹשֵׁב is a singular for the whole people; "for" is not in the Hebrew. Who "they" are each time is not said.
- **"seen face to face" → "eye to eye you are seen"** (v14) — Hebrew עַיִן בְּעַיִן, literally "eye in eye".
- **"if thou shalt kill" → "you will put … to death"** (v15) — Hebrew וְהֵמַתָּה, no "if". **"the fame of thee" → "the report of you"** — Hebrew שִׁמְעֲךָ.
- **"Because the LORD was not able" kept** (v16) — Hebrew מִבִּלְתִּי יְכֹלֶת יְהוָה, literally "from Yahweh's not being able". שָׁחַט "slaughter".
- **"I beseech thee, let the power of my Lord be great, according as thou hast spoken" → "let the power of Adonai be great, please, as you have spoken"** (v17) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי *Adonai*, not the name Yahweh; נָא "please".
- **"The LORD is longsuffering, and of great mercy" → "Yahweh, slow to anger and abounding in *chesed*"** (v18) — the words of Exod 34:6–7, in the same English, and shorter where the Hebrew is shorter: here there is no "El compassionate and gracious", no "and truth", no "guarding chesed for thousands", no "and sin", no "upon sons' sons". אֶרֶךְ אַפַּיִם is literally "long of nostrils"; רַב־חֶסֶד "much of chesed". The Hebrew has no "is".
- **"forgiving iniquity and transgression" kept** (v18) — Hebrew נֹשֵׂא "lifting, bearing, taking away". **"and by no means clearing the guilty" → "and he will surely not clear"** — Hebrew וְנַקֵּה לֹא יְנַקֶּה, literally "and clearing he will not clear"; the Hebrew has no object ("the guilty" is supplied by KJV). **"upon the children unto the third and fourth generation" → "upon sons, upon the third and upon the fourth generation"** — Hebrew עַל־בָּנִים עַל־שִׁלֵּשִׁים וְעַל־רִבֵּעִים.
- **"Pardon, I beseech thee … as thou hast forgiven this people" → "Pardon, please … as you have forgiven this people"** (v19, 20) — two verbs: סָלַח "pardon" (said only of God) and נָשָׂא לְ "forgive", literally "lift up for" (the verb of v18). **"from Egypt even until now"** — Hebrew וְעַד־הֵנָּה, "and until here".
- **"But as truly as I live, all the earth shall be filled with the glory of the LORD" → "Nevertheless, as I live, and all the earth shall be filled with the glory of Yahweh"** (v21) — Hebrew וְאוּלָם חַי־אָנִי וְיִמָּלֵא כְבוֹד־יְהוָה אֶת־כָּל־הָאָרֶץ. וְאוּלָם is "but, nevertheless"; חַי־אָנִי, literally "living am I", is the oath formula. The second clause is joined by "and": it may be a second thing sworn by ("and as the glory … fills") or a statement. Yahweh speaks of himself by name.
- **"Because all those men … surely they shall not see" → "all the men … they shall not see"** (v22, 23) — the oath in its Hebrew form: כִּי "that" … אִם־יִרְאוּ, literally "(I swear) that all the men … if they shall see the land": "if" in an oath is a strong "not". **"my miracles" → "my signs"** — Hebrew אֹתֹתַי. **"tempted me now these ten times, and have not hearkened to my voice" → "tested me these ten times and have not listened to my voice"** — Hebrew נִסָּה; שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל.
- **"neither shall any of them that provoked me see it" → "all who spurn me shall not see it"** (v23) — Hebrew וְכָל־מְנַאֲצַי לֹא יִרְאוּהָ.
- **"because he had another spirit with him, and hath followed me fully" → "because another spirit was with him and he has followed me fully"** (v24) — Hebrew רוּחַ אַחֶרֶת (רוּחַ as 11:17); וַיְמַלֵּא אַחֲרָי, literally "and he filled after me". **"his seed shall possess it"** — Hebrew יוֹרִשֶׁנָּה, the causative form, here "take possession of". עֶבֶד is "slave"; of one who serves Yahweh, "servant".
- **"(Now the Amalekites and the Canaanites dwelt in the valley.)" → "And the Amalekite and the Canaanite dwells in the valley"** (v25, 43, 45) — singulars with the article and a singular participle; the brackets are not in the Hebrew.
- **"turn you, and get you into the wilderness by the way of the Red sea" → "turn and set out for yourselves into the wilderness, by the way of the Sea of Reeds"** (v25) — Hebrew וּסְעוּ לָכֶם; יַם־סוּף *yam suf*, "sea of reeds".
- **"How long shall I bear with this evil congregation, which murmur against me?" → "How long for this evil congregation, that they are grumbling against me?"** (v27) — Hebrew עַד־מָתַי לָעֵדָה הָרָעָה הַזֹּאת, literally "until when (shall there be) for this evil congregation": the sentence has no verb; "shall I bear with" is supplied by KJV.
- **"As truly as I live, saith the LORD" → "As I live, declares Yahweh, surely"** (v28) — Hebrew נְאֻם־יְהוָה "the declaration of Yahweh"; אִם־לֹא, literally "if not": in an oath a strong "surely" (so v35).
- **"all that were numbered of you, according to your whole number" → "all of you who were numbered, by all your number"** (v29) — Hebrew פְּקֻדֵיכֶם (ch 1). "Twenty years old" is literally "a son of twenty years". פֶּגֶר "carcass", a dead body (v29, 32, 33).
- **"Doubtless ye shall not come into the land, concerning which I sware to make you dwell therein" → "You shall not come to the land in which I lifted up my hand to make you abide"** (v30) — the oath form again: אִם־אַתֶּם תָּבֹאוּ, literally "if you shall come". נָשָׂאתִי אֶת־יָדִי "I lifted up my hand" is the gesture of an oath (Exod 6:8). שַׁכֵּן, from שָׁכַן "abide".
- **"which ye said should be a prey" → "of whom you said, 'They will be for plunder'"** (v31) — their words of v3. **"despised" → "rejected"** — Hebrew מָאַס (11:20).
- **"But as for you, your carcases" → "And your carcasses, yours"** (v32) — Hebrew וּפִגְרֵיכֶם אַתֶּם, the pronoun added for stress.
- **"your children shall wander in the wilderness" → "your sons shall be shepherds in the wilderness"** (v33) — Hebrew רֹעִים "shepherds, pasturing"; "wander" in KJV follows other versions (from memory). **"bear your whoredoms" → "bear your prostitutions"** — Hebrew זְנוּתֵיכֶם, the word for unfaithfulness to Yahweh. **"until your carcases be wasted" → "until your carcasses come to an end"** — Hebrew תֹּם (so "come to an end" in v35).
- **"After the number of the days in which ye searched the land, even forty days, each day for a year" → "By the number of the days that you explored the land, forty days, a day for the year, a day for the year"** (v34) — the Hebrew says יוֹם לַשָּׁנָה twice.
- **"ye shall know my breach of promise" → "you shall know my opposition"** (v34) — Hebrew תְּנוּאָתִי, found only here and in Job 33:10; from a verb "hinder, refuse" (30:5). The exact sense is uncertain: my opposition, my turning away. (The "opposition" of Lev 26:21 is another word, קֶרִי.)
- **"I will surely do it … that are gathered together against me" → "surely this I will do … who have met together against me"** (v35) — Hebrew הַנּוֹעָדִים, from the verb "meet by appointment" (the root of מוֹעֵד). **"they shall be consumed" → "they shall come to an end"** — Hebrew יִתַּמּוּ.
- **"made all the congregation to murmur against him, by bringing up a slander upon the land" → "made all the congregation grumble against him, bringing out a report against the land"** (v36) — the reading tradition (qere) וַיַּלִּינוּ, "they made … grumble"; the written form (ketiv) וילונו is "they grumbled". דִּבָּה is a report that defames (13:32); in v37 it has "evil" beside it.
- **"died by the plague" kept** (v37) — Hebrew מַגֵּפָה, literally "a blow".
- **"lived still" → "lived"** (v38) — Hebrew חָיוּ.
- **"gat them up into the top of the mountain … Lo, we be here, and will go up unto the place which the LORD hath promised" → "went up to the top of the hill country … Here we are, and we will go up to the place that Yahweh said"** (v40) — Hebrew רֹאשׁ הָהָר, "the head of the mountain", the hill country of 13:17 (so v44, 45); הִנֶּנּוּ, literally "behold us"; אָמַר "said".
- **"Wherefore now do ye transgress the commandment of the LORD? but it shall not prosper" → "Why is it that you are overstepping the mouth of Yahweh? And it will not prosper"** (v41) — Hebrew עֹבְרִים אֶת־פִּי יְהוָה, "passing over the mouth of Yahweh", his spoken word; "transgression" is another word (v18).
- **"that ye be not smitten" → "and you shall not be smitten"** (v42) — Hebrew וְלֹא תִּנָּגְפוּ, joined by "and": so that you are not smitten.
- **"because ye are turned away from the LORD, therefore the LORD will not be with you" → "for because you have turned back from after Yahweh, Yahweh will not be with you"** (v43) — Hebrew כִּי־עַל־כֵּן שַׁבְתֶּם מֵאַחֲרֵי יְהוָה.
- **"they presumed to go up" → "they were reckless to go up"** (v44) — Hebrew וַיַּעְפִּלוּ, found in this form only here (Hab 2:4 has the root): perhaps "swell up, be heedless"; the sense is uncertain. Deut 1:43 tells it with another verb, "you acted presumptuously". **"departed not out of the camp" → "did not depart from the midst of the camp"** — Hebrew מִקֶּרֶב.
- **"smote them, and discomfited them, even unto Hormah" → "struck them and beat them in pieces as far as Hormah"** (v45) — Hebrew וַיַּכְּתוּם, from כָּתַת "beat, crush to pieces". הַחָרְמָה has the article, "the Hormah": the name is of the root of חֵרֶם, a thing devoted to destruction (21:3).

### Notes
- **The great rebellion** — the turning point of Numbers. The wilderness generation refuses to enter the land; Yahweh sentences them to die in the wilderness. Joshua and Caleb alone will enter.
- **Forty days → forty years** (v34) — a year for each day of the exploring.
- **Moses' intercession** (v13–19) — appeals to (a) what the nations will say of Yahweh; (b) Yahweh's own words about himself (v18 repeats Ex 34:6–7 in a shorter form). As in Ex 32:11–14 Moses speaks of what Egypt will say; here Yahweh "pardons" (v20), there he "relented".
- **"Ten times"** (v22) — rabbinic tradition counts ten testings from the Sea of Reeds to Kadesh; the text itself does not list them.
- **Hormah** (v40–45) — after being told not to go, they go. After being told to go, they refused.
- **Heb 3:7–4:11** — extensive NT reflection on this chapter, treating it as a warning about unbelief.
