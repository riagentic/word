## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"murmured" → "grumbled"** (v2, 27, 29, 36) — Hebrew לון *lun*. Modernized.
- **"Would God" → "If only"** (v2) — Hebrew לוּ *lu* = "if only." KJV uses euphemism.
- **"Red sea" → "Sea of Reeds"** (v25) — Hebrew יַם־סוּף *Yam Suf*. *Suph* = reeds.
- **"of great mercy" → "abundant in lovingkindness"** (v18) — Hebrew רַב־חֶסֶד *rav-chesed*. *Chesed* = covenant love / steadfast kindness.
- **"tempted" → "tested"** (v22) — Hebrew נִסָּה *nissah*. To test / try, not moral temptation.
- **"wander" → "be shepherds"** (v33) — Hebrew רֹעִים *ro'im*. Idiom for nomadic pastoral life. KJV wander loses the shepherd metaphor.
- **"my breach of promise" → "my opposition"** (v34) — Hebrew תְּנוּאָה *tenu'ah*. Obscure; meaning debated — "opposition / thwarting / displeasure." Not God's breach of promise.
- **"discomfited" → "beat them down"** (v45) — modernized.
- **"Hormah" (v45)** — Hebrew חָרְמָה *Chormah* from חָרַם *charam* = "place of the ban / destruction."
- **"sware / swore with uplifted hand" → "swore by lifted hand"** (v30) — Hebrew נָשָׂא יָד *nasa yad*. Oath-gesture.

### Notes
- **The great rebellion** — the turning point of Numbers. The wilderness generation refuses to enter the land; Yahweh sentences them to die in the wilderness. Joshua and Caleb alone will enter.
- **Forty days → forty years** (v34) — punitive correspondence: a year of wandering for each day of the scouts' mission.
- **Moses' intercession** (v13–19) — appeals to (a) Yahweh's reputation among the nations; (b) Yahweh's self-disclosed nature (v18 quotes Ex 34:6–7). Partial success: the people are not destroyed, but still barred from entering.
- **"Ten times"** (v22) — the number of the congregation's rebellions: complaints at Red Sea (Ex 14), Marah (15), wilderness of Sin (16), Rephidim (17), golden calf (32), Taberah (Num 11:1), manna-craving (11:4), Kadesh-barnea (13–14), and in various lesser forms. Rabbinic tradition enumerates them.
- **Presumption at Hormah** (v40–45) — after being told not to go, they go. After being told to go, they refused. The generation is doomed not by events but by refusing Yahweh's timing.
- **Heb 3:7–4:11** — extensive NT reflection on this chapter, treating it as a warning about unbelief that forfeits divine rest.
