# Numbers 35

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ in the plains of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ by the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ of ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧, saying,

[2] "Command the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they shall give to the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, from the inheritance of their possession, cities to dwell in; and pasture land for the cities, around them, you shall give to the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧.

[3] And the cities shall be theirs to dwell in, and their pasture lands shall be for their beasts and for their goods and for all their living things.

[4] And the pasture lands of the cities that you shall give to the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧: from the wall of the city and outward, a thousand cubits all around.

[5] And you shall measure outside the city the east side, two thousand by the cubit, and the south side, two thousand by the cubit, and the west side, two thousand by the cubit, and the north side, two thousand by the cubit, and the city in the middle; this shall be for them the pasture lands of the cities.

[6] And the cities that you shall give to the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧: the six cities of refuge, which you shall give for the murderer to flee there; and in addition to them you shall give forty-two cities.

[7] All the cities that you shall give to the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧: forty-eight cities, them and their pasture lands.

[8] And the cities that you shall give from the possession of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: from the many you shall take many, and from the few you shall take few; each, according to his inheritance that they inherit, shall give of his cities to the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧."

[9] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[10] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and say to them, 'When you are passing over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ to the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧,

[11] then you shall select for yourselves cities; cities of refuge they shall be for you, and a murderer shall flee there, one who strikes down a soul in error.

[12] And the cities shall be for you for refuge from the redeemer, and the murderer shall not die until he stands before the congregation for judgment.

[13] And the cities that you shall give: six cities of refuge they shall be for you.

[14] The three cities you shall give across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, and the three cities you shall give in the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧; cities of refuge they shall be.

[15] For the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and for the sojourner and for the settler in their midst these six cities shall be for refuge, for everyone who strikes down a soul in error to flee there.

[16] But if with an article of iron he struck him and he died, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death.

[17] And if with a stone in the hand, by which one may die, he struck him and he died, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death.

[18] Or with an article of wood in the hand, by which one may die, he struck him and he died, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death.

[19] The redeemer of blood, he shall put the murderer to death; when he comes upon him, he shall put him to death.

[20] And if in hatred he pushes him, or has thrown upon him with lying in wait, and he died,

[21] or in enmity he struck him with his hand and he died, the striker shall surely be put to death; he is a murderer. The redeemer of blood shall put the murderer to death when he comes upon him.

[22] But if suddenly, without enmity, he pushed him, or threw upon him any article without lying in wait,

[23] or with any stone by which one may die, without seeing, and he made it fall upon him and he died, and he was not an enemy to him and was not seeking his harm,

[24] then the congregation shall judge between the striker and the redeemer of blood according to these ordinances.

[25] And the congregation shall deliver the murderer from the hand of the redeemer of blood, and the congregation shall return him to the city of his refuge, to which he fled; and he shall dwell in it until the death of the great priest, whom one anointed with the holy oil.

[26] But if the murderer surely goes out of the border of the city of his refuge, to which he flees,

[27] and the redeemer of blood finds him outside the border of the city of his refuge, and the redeemer of blood slays the murderer, there is no bloodguilt for him.

[28] For in the city of his refuge he shall dwell until the death of the great priest; and after the death of the great priest the murderer shall return to the land of his possession.

[29] And these shall be for you a statute of judgment for your generations in all your dwelling places.

[30] Everyone who strikes down a soul: at the mouth of witnesses one shall slay the murderer; and one witness shall not answer against a soul for him to die.

[31] And you shall not take a ransom for the soul of a murderer who is guilty of death, for he shall surely be put to death.

[32] And you shall not take a ransom for fleeing to the city of his refuge, to return to dwell in the land, until the death of the priest.

[33] And you shall not pollute the land in which you are, for the blood, it pollutes the land; and for the land atonement shall not be made for the blood that was shed in it, except by the blood of the one who shed it.

[34] And you shall not defile the land in which you are dwelling, in the midst of which I am abiding; for I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, am abiding in the midst of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of" → "sons of"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"by Jordan near Jericho" → "by the Jordan of Jericho"** (v1) — Hebrew עַל־יַרְדֵּן יְרֵחוֹ, as 33:48. "Plains" is עַרְבֹת, dry steppe land.
- **"that they give" → "and they shall give"** (v2) — Hebrew וְנָתְנוּ; the verse then turns to "you shall give". **"of the inheritance of their possession"** — both words, נַחֲלָה "inheritance" and אֲחֻזָּה "possession" (land held), as 32:32.
- **"suburbs" → "pasture land(s)"** (v2, 3, 4, 5, 7) — Hebrew מִגְרָשׁ *migrash*, perhaps "a place to drive (cattle) out to": the open land around a city; not modern suburbs. In v2 the word is singular.
- **"for their cattle, and for their goods, and for all their beasts" → "for their beasts and for their goods and for all their living things"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּהֵמָה, רְכוּשׁ, חַיָּה.
- **"shall reach from the wall of the city and outward"** (v4) — the Hebrew has no verb: "from the wall of the city and outward, a thousand cubits all around". **"two thousand cubits" → "two thousand by the cubit"** (v5) — Hebrew אַלְפַּיִם בָּאַמָּה. How the thousand cubits of v4 and the two thousand of v5 fit together the text does not say. **"side"** (v5) — Hebrew פֵּאָה, "edge, side", as 34:3. The four quarters are literally "frontward", "Negev", "sea", "north".
- **"from them that have many ye shall give many; but from them that have few ye shall give few" → "from the many you shall take many, and from the few you shall take few"** (v8) — Hebrew מֵאֵת הָרַב תַּרְבּוּ וּמֵאֵת הַמְעַט תַּמְעִיטוּ, literally "from the many you shall make many, and from the few you shall make few" (the words of 33:54); "take" is supplied for the sense. **"every one … according to his inheritance which he inheriteth" → "each, according to his inheritance that they inherit"** — Hebrew כְּפִי, literally "according to the mouth of"; the verb is plural.
- **"cities for refuge" → "cities of refuge"** (v6, 11–15, 25–28, 32) — Hebrew מִקְלָט *miqlat*, from a root "take in": a place that takes a man in, an asylum. The word is found only in this law (Num 35; Josh 20–21; 1 Chr 6). **"which ye shall appoint for the manslayer, that he may flee thither" → "which you shall give for the murderer to flee there"** (v6) — Hebrew תִּתְּנוּ "give", the verb of the whole passage.
- **"manslayer" / "slayer" / "murderer" → "murderer"** (v6, 11, 12, 16–19, 21, 25–28, 30, 31) — the Hebrew has one word in all these places, רֹצֵחַ *rotzeach*, the participle of רָצַח, the verb of "You shall not murder" (Exod 20:13). It is used here both of the man who killed in error (v6, 11, 12, 25–28) and of the man who killed in hatred (v16–21, 30, 31): the name goes with the deed until the congregation has judged. English "manslayer" for the one and "murderer" for the other makes a distinction of words that the Hebrew does not make; the Hebrew marks the difference by "in error", "without enmity" and their opposites.
- **"the revenger of blood kill the slayer" → "the redeemer of blood slays the murderer"** (v27); **"the murderer shall be put to death" → "one shall slay the murderer"** (v30) — in these two places the same verb רָצַח is used of the lawful killing of the רֹצֵחַ, by the redeemer of blood and on the word of witnesses. English "murder" would say that the act is a crime, which both verses deny; "slay" is used, and it is used for nothing else in this chapter. So the verb itself means the killing of a man by a man, and is not limited to unlawful killing. In v30 the subject is not named ("one").
- **"which killeth any person at unawares" → "one who strikes down a soul in error"** (v11, 15, 30) — Hebrew מַכֵּה־נֶפֶשׁ, "a striker of a soul": הִכָּה "strike" (so v16–18, 21, "the striker" v21, 24), נֶפֶשׁ "soul", a living person. בִּשְׁגָגָה *bi-shegagah* is "in error", by a straying, a mistake (Lev 4:2); "unintentionally" is its sense here.
- **"ye shall appoint you cities" → "you shall select for yourselves cities"** (v11) — Hebrew וְהִקְרִיתֶם, the causative of קָרָה "meet, happen": make them fall right, pick out as suitable. The form has this sense only here; uncertain.
- **"refuge from the avenger" → "refuge from the redeemer"** (v12); **"revenger of blood" → "redeemer of blood"** (v19, 21, 24, 25, 27) — Hebrew גֹּאֵל הַדָּם *go'el ha-dam*. גֹּאֵל is the participle of גָּאַל "redeem": the kinsman whose duty it is to buy back his brother's land and person (Lev 25:25, 48) is the same man who claims back his blood. "Avenger" (נֹקֵם) is another word and is not used here. "Kinsman" is not in the word itself.
- **"for the stranger, and for the sojourner" → "for the sojourner and for the settler"** (v15) — Hebrew גֵּר and תּוֹשָׁב.
- **"instrument of iron" → "article of iron"** (v16); **"hand weapon of wood" → "article of wood in the hand"** (v18); **"any thing" → "any article"** (v22) — Hebrew כְּלִי, any made thing: tool, vessel, weapon. **"with throwing a stone, wherewith he may die" → "with a stone in the hand, by which one may die"** (v17) — Hebrew בְּאֶבֶן יָד, literally "a stone of hand": one that fills the hand, or held in the hand; so "an article of wood of hand" (v18). The object stands first in each case.
- **"shall surely be put to death"** (v16, 17, 18, 21, 31) — Hebrew מוֹת יוּמָת, literally "dying he shall be made to die".
- **"shall slay the murderer: when he meeteth him, he shall slay him" → "he shall put the murderer to death; when he comes upon him, he shall put him to death"** (v19, 21) — Hebrew הֵמִית "put to death"; פָּגַע בְּ "come upon, fall upon".
- **"But if he thrust him of hatred, or hurl at him by laying of wait, that he die" → "And if in hatred he pushes him, or has thrown upon him with lying in wait, and he died"** (v20) — Hebrew הָדַף "push, thrust"; no object is named for "thrown". צְדִיָּה (only v20, 22) is from צָדָה "lie in wait" (Exod 21:13). **"he that smote him" → "the striker"** (v21) — Hebrew הַמַּכֶּה.
- **"or with any stone, wherewith a man may die, seeing him not, and cast it upon him" → "or with any stone by which one may die, without seeing, and he made it fall upon him"** (v23) — Hebrew בְּלֹא רְאוֹת "in not seeing"; וַיַּפֵּל "made fall". **"neither sought his harm"** — Hebrew רָעָתוֹ, literally "his evil".
- **"according to these judgments" → "according to these ordinances"** (v24) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּטִים, rulings; **"for judgment"** (v12) and **"a statute of judgment"** (v29) have the same word in the singular.
- **"shall restore him to the city of his refuge, whither he was fled: and he shall abide in it" → "shall return him to the city of his refuge, to which he fled; and he shall dwell in it"** (v25) — Hebrew הֵשִׁיב, יָשַׁב. **"the high priest, which was anointed with the holy oil" → "the great priest, whom one anointed with the holy oil"** (v25, 28) — Hebrew הַכֹּהֵן הַגָּדֹל, "the great priest" (the "high priest" of later usage; Lev 21:10 "the priest who is great among his brothers"); אֲשֶׁר־מָשַׁח אֹתוֹ is active, "whom he anointed", with no subject named. שֶׁמֶן הַקֹּדֶשׁ is literally "the oil of holiness".
- **"if the slayer shall at any time come without the border" → "if the murderer surely goes out of the border"** (v26) — Hebrew יָצֹא יֵצֵא, the infinitive absolute with its verb, "going out he goes out".
- **"he shall not be guilty of blood" → "there is no bloodguilt for him"** (v27) — Hebrew אֵין לוֹ דָּם, literally "there is no blood for him".
- **"Because he should have remained" → "For in the city of his refuge he shall dwell"** (v28) — Hebrew יֵשֵׁב, a plain imperfect; the place stands first.
- **"throughout your generations in all your dwellings" → "for your generations in all your dwelling places"** (v29) — Hebrew לְדֹרֹתֵיכֶם, מוֹשְׁבֹתֵיכֶם.
- **"Whoso killeth any person, the murderer shall be put to death by the mouth of witnesses" → "Everyone who strikes down a soul: at the mouth of witnesses one shall slay the murderer"** (v30) — the Hebrew sets "everyone who strikes down a soul" first and then begins anew. לְפִי עֵדִים is "at the mouth of witnesses" (plural). **"shall not testify against any person to cause him to die" → "shall not answer against a soul for him to die"** — Hebrew עָנָה בְּ, "answer against" = testify against (Exod 20:16); נֶפֶשׁ "soul".
- **"satisfaction" → "ransom"** (v31, 32) — Hebrew כֹּפֶר *kofer*, the price that covers a life (Exod 21:30), of one root with כִּפֶּר "make atonement" (v33). **"for the life of a murderer, which is guilty of death" → "for the soul of a murderer who is guilty of death"** (v31) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ "soul"; רָשָׁע לָמוּת is literally "wicked, to die": רָשָׁע "wicked" is in law the one in the wrong.
- **"for him that is fled to the city of his refuge, that he should come again" → "for fleeing to the city of his refuge, to return"** (v32) — the Masoretic Text points לָנוּס as an infinitive, "to flee", the same form as in v6 and v15; "for him who has fled" is another pointing of the same letters. The sense is not certain: either no ransom buys the right to flee to the city, or none buys the fugitive's return home before the priest dies. "Until the death of the priest": here "the priest" without "great".
- **"ye shall not pollute the land … blood it defileth the land" → "you shall not pollute the land … the blood, it pollutes the land"** (v33) — Hebrew הֶחֱנִיף, twice, the causative of חָנַף "be polluted, godless" (Isa 24:5; Jer 3:1): one rendering; "defile" (v34) is another verb, טִמֵּא "make unclean". **"the land cannot be cleansed of the blood" → "for the land atonement shall not be made for the blood"** — Hebrew לֹא־יְכֻפַּר, the passive of כִּפֶּר "make atonement". **"shed"** — Hebrew שָׁפַךְ "pour out". "In which you are": the Hebrew has no "dwell" in v33.
- **"Defile not therefore the land which ye shall inhabit, wherein I dwell" → "And you shall not defile the land in which you are dwelling, in the midst of which I am abiding"** (v34) — Hebrew תְטַמֵּא is singular, "you" as one; יֹשְׁבִים "dwelling" and שֹׁכֵן "abiding" are two verbs: שָׁכַן is the verb of מִשְׁכָּן, the "dwelling" of Yahweh.

### Notes
- **Levitical cities** (v1–8) — 48 cities for the Levites, scattered throughout the tribal territories. Among these, 6 are designated cities of refuge. Josh 21 lists them.
- **Cities of refuge** — 3 east of Jordan (Bezer, Ramoth-gilead, Golan), 3 west (Kedesh, Shechem, Hebron). Strategically distributed so no Israelite is far from one.
- **Manslaughter vs murder** — the criterion is intent. The cities protect the one who killed in error from the *go'el ha-dam*. Judicial process: flee to city, stand trial before congregation, if cleared remain until the great priest's death.
- **The great priest's death as temporal limit** (v25, 28) — the exile ends with the death of the great (high) priest. Theological logic debated: (a) his death atones for all incidental blood-guilt; (b) general amnesty marker; (c) some see proto-Christological connection (Heb 9:15).
- **No ransom for murder** (v31) — unique in ancient Near Eastern law. Most codes allowed blood-money for murder; the Torah refuses, arguing only blood atones for shed blood (v33). Murder is a land-polluting act; it cannot be merely compensated.
- **Two-witness rule** (v30) — Deut 17:6, 19:15 expand; NT 1 Tim 5:19 applies to church discipline.
- **Land as covenantal partner** (v33–34) — blood defiles; Yahweh abides among them; defilement of land = defilement of divine dwelling. The ethics of bloodshed is inseparable from the theology of presence.
