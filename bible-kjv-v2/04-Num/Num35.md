# Numbers 35

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ in the plains of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ by the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ at ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧, saying,

[7] All the cities that you give to the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ shall be forty-eight cities, together with their pasturelands.

[8] Of the cities that you give from the possession of the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ — from the many you shall take many, and from the few you shall take few; each shall give some of its cities to the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ in proportion to its inheritance."

[13] The cities you give shall be six cities of refuge for you.

[14] You shall give three cities beyond the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, and three cities in the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧; they shall be cities of refuge.

[15] These six cities shall be a refuge for the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, for the foreigner, and for the sojourner among them, so that anyone who strikes down a person unintentionally may flee there.

[16] But if he struck him with an iron instrument so that he died, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death.

[17] If he struck him with a hand-sized stone that could cause death, and he died, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death.

[18] Or if he struck him with a hand-sized wooden object that could cause death, and he died, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death.

[19] The avenger of blood himself shall put the murderer to death; when he meets him, he shall put him to death.

[20] And if he pushed him out of hatred, or threw something at him lying in wait, so that he died,

[22] But if he pushed him suddenly, without enmity, or threw on him any object without lying in wait,

[26] But if the manslayer at any time goes beyond the border of his city of refuge, where he had fled,

[28] for he should have remained in his city of refuge until the death of the high priest. But after the death of the high priest, the manslayer may return to the land of his possession.

[29] These things shall be a statute of law for you throughout your generations in all your dwellings.

[30] Whoever kills any person, the murderer shall be put to death on the testimony of witnesses; but one witness shall not testify against any person for him to be put to death.

[31] You shall take no ransom for the life of a murderer who is guilty of death; he shall surely be put to death.

[32] You shall take no ransom for one who has fled to his city of refuge, that he should return to dwell in the land before the death of the priest.

[33] So you shall not pollute the land in which you dwell, for blood pollutes the land; and no atonement can be made for the land for the blood shed in it, except by the blood of the one who shed it.

[34] Do not defile the land in which you dwell, in the midst of which I dwell; for I ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ dwell among the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"suburbs" → "pasturelands"** (v2, 3, 4, 5, 7) — Hebrew מִגְרָשׁ *migrash*. Open grazing land around a city; not modern suburbs.
- **"manslayer" / "slayer" → "manslayer" / "slayer"** — kept. Hebrew רוֹצֵחַ *rotzeach*; context distinguishes accidental killer (manslayer) from intentional murderer (murderer).
- **"at unawares / unawares" → "unintentionally"** (v11, 15) — Hebrew בִּשְׁגָגָה *bi-shegagah*.
- **"revenger of blood" → "avenger of blood"** (v19, 21, 24, 25, 27) — Hebrew גֹּאֵל הַדָּם *go'el ha-dam*. The nearest relative responsible for blood-justice.
- **"laying of wait" → "lying in wait"** (v20, 22) — modernized.
- **"satisfaction" → "ransom"** (v31, 32) — Hebrew כֹּפֶר *kofer*. Redemption-payment.
- **"by the mouth of witnesses" → "on the testimony of witnesses"** (v30) — modernized.

### Notes
- **Levitical cities** (v1–8) — 48 cities for the Levites, scattered throughout the tribal territories. Among these, 6 are designated cities of refuge. Josh 21 lists them.
- **Cities of refuge** — 3 east of Jordan (Bezer, Ramoth-gilead, Golan), 3 west (Kedesh, Shechem, Hebron). Strategically distributed so no Israelite is far from one.
- **Manslaughter vs murder** — the criterion is intent. The cities protect the accidental killer from *go'el ha-dam* vendetta. Judicial process: flee to city, stand trial before congregation, if cleared remain until high priest's death.
- **High priest's death as temporal limit** (v25, 28) — the manslayer's exile ends with the high priest's death. Theological logic debated: (a) the high priest's death atones for all incidental blood-guilt; (b) general amnesty marker; (c) some see proto-Christological connection (Heb 9:15).
- **No ransom for murder** (v31) — unique in ancient Near Eastern law. Most codes allowed blood-money for murder; the Torah refuses, arguing only blood atones for shed blood (v33). Murder is a land-polluting act; it cannot be merely compensated.
- **Two-witness rule** (v30) — Deut 17:6, 19:15 expand; NT 1 Tim 5:19 applies to church discipline.
- **Land as covenantal partner** (v33–34) — blood defiles; Yahweh dwells among them; defilement of land = defilement of divine dwelling. The ethics of bloodshed is inseparable from the theology of presence.
