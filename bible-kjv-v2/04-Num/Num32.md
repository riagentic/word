## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"children of" → "sons of"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"Now the children of Reuben … had a very great multitude of cattle" → "And the sons of Reuben … had much livestock, very mighty"** (v1) — Hebrew וּמִקְנֶה רַב הָיָה … עָצוּם מְאֹד: "much livestock … very mighty", that is, strong in number; "livestock" stands first in the Hebrew. מִקְנֶה *miqneh* (v1, 4, 16, 26) is what one has got in animals, herds and flocks alike. **"a place for cattle" → "a place of livestock"** (v1, 4) — a genitive.
- **"spake" → "said … saying"** (v2, 25) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמְרוּ … לֵאמֹר, the verb "say" twice; in v25 the first verb is singular in the Hebrew. **"princes" → "chieftains"** (v2) — Hebrew נְשִׂיאִים, "ones lifted up".
- **"and"** (v3, 34–38) — each name in the lists has its own וְ "and".
- **"the country which the LORD smote" → "the land that Yahweh struck"** (v4) — Hebrew הִכָּה "strike"; the object is the land. **"is a land for cattle" → "it is a land of livestock"** — the Hebrew takes the subject up again with הִוא "it".
- **"thy servants"** (v4, 5, 25, 27, 31) — Hebrew עֲבָדֶיךָ; the word is "slaves", the usual way of speaking of oneself before a superior.
- **"bring us not over Jordan" → "do not make us pass over the Jordan"** (v5) — Hebrew הֶעֱבִיר, the causative of עָבַר "pass over", the verb of v7, 21, 27, 29, 30, 32. **"for a possession"** (v5, 22, 29, 32) — Hebrew אֲחֻזָּה, land held (אָחַז "take hold"); "inheritance" (v18, 19, 32) is another word, נַחֲלָה. Both stand together in v32.
- **"Shall your brethren go to war, and shall ye sit here?" → "Shall your brothers go to the war, and you, shall you dwell here?"** (v6) — Hebrew וְאַתֶּם תֵּשְׁבוּ פֹה, with the pronoun set first. יָשַׁב is "sit" and "dwell" (v17, 40); here both can be heard.
- **"discourage" → "restrain"** (v7, 9) — Hebrew הֵנִיא "hinder, restrain, refuse" (of a father who disallows a vow, 30:5). In v7 the written text (ketiv) has תנואון, the simple stem of the same verb; the reading tradition (qere) has the causative תְנִיאוּן, as v9. The meaning is the same.
- **"valley of Eshcol" → "wadi of Eshcol"** (v9) — Hebrew נַחַל, a stream bed. **"that they should not go" → "so as not to go"** — Hebrew לְבִלְתִּי־בֹא.
- **"the LORD's anger was kindled" → "the anger of Yahweh burned"** (v10, 13) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר־אַף; אַף is "nose", hence "anger".
- **"Surely none of the men … shall see" → "The men … shall not see"** (v11) — Hebrew אִם־יִרְאוּ, the oath formula, literally "if the men … see": a sworn "they shall not". **"from twenty years old"** — literally "from a son of twenty years". **"the land which I sware unto Abraham" → "the ground that I swore to Abraham"** — Hebrew אֲדָמָה "ground", not אֶרֶץ; "to give" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"they have not wholly followed me" → "they have not followed fully after me"** (v11, 12) — Hebrew מִלְאוּ אַחֲרַי, literally "they have not filled after me": gone the whole way behind.
- **"Kenezite" → "Kenizzite"** (v12) — clan of Caleb, distinct from the Kenizzites of Canaan (Gen 15:19).
- **"he made them wander" → "he made them roam"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיְנִעֵם, the causative of נוּעַ "sway, roam about" (Gen 4:12). **"that had done evil … was consumed" → "that did the evil … had come to an end"** — Hebrew הָרַע with the article; תֹּם "be finished, come to an end".
- **"an increase of sinful men" → "a brood of sinful men"** (v14) — Hebrew תַּרְבּוּת אֲנָשִׁים חַטָּאִים *tarbut anashim chatta'im*. תַּרְבּוּת is found only here: from רָבָה "become many", an "increase", what has grown up after the fathers. **"to augment yet the fierce anger of the LORD" → "to add yet more to the burning anger of Yahweh"** — Hebrew לִסְפּוֹת עוֹד עַל, "to add yet upon"; חֲרוֹן אַף, literally "the burning of the nose".
- **"if ye turn away from after him, he will yet again leave them" → "if you turn back from after him, he will yet again leave him"** (v15) — Hebrew שׁוּב "turn back"; לְהַנִּיחוֹ has a singular suffix, "him": Israel (v14). וְיָסַף עוֹד is literally "he will add yet". **"ye shall destroy all this people" → "you will work ruin for all this people"** — Hebrew וְשִׁחַתֶּם לְ, the verb "ruin" (Gen 6:12) with the preposition "to, for".
- **"came near" → "drew near"** (v16) — Hebrew נָגַשׁ. **"sheepfolds" → "folds for the flock"** (v16, 24, 36) — Hebrew גִּדְרֹת צֹאן, literally "walls of the flock": pens of piled stone; צֹאן is the flock of sheep and goats. In v24 the Hebrew has "for your flock" (לְצֹנַאֲכֶם). The object stands first in v16.
- **"we ourselves will go ready armed" → "we ourselves will arm ourselves, hastening"** (v17) — Hebrew נֵחָלֵץ חֻשִׁים. חָלַץ (v17, 20) and חָלוּץ "armed" (v21, 27, 29, 30, 32) is said of men made ready for war; the root sense is not certain ("stripped" for action, or "girded"). חֻשִׁים is a participle of חוּשׁ "hasten". The Septuagint is commonly cited for another word here ("in battle array", as Exod 13:18; from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"fenced cities" → "fortified cities"** (v17, 36) — Hebrew עָרֵי (הַ)מִּבְצָר, "cities of fortification". **"because of the inhabitants of the land" → "because of those who dwell in the land"** (v17) — Hebrew יֹשְׁבֵי, from יָשַׁב "dwell".
- **"until the children of Israel have inherited every man his inheritance"** (v18) — Hebrew הִתְנַחֵל, the reflexive stem of נָחַל "inherit": take as one's own inheritance.
- **"on yonder side Jordan, or forward … on this side Jordan eastward" → "across the Jordan and beyond … across the Jordan eastward"** (v19) — the Hebrew has the same words for both sides: מֵעֵבֶר לַיַּרְדֵּן, "from the side across the Jordan". Which side is meant is told only by "and beyond" and "eastward". So also v32. **"is fallen to us" → "has come to us"** — Hebrew בָּאָה "has come". "Eastward" is literally "toward the sunrise" (מִזְרָחָה).
- **"if ye will go armed before the LORD to war" → "if you arm yourselves before Yahweh for the war"** (v20) — Hebrew לַמִּלְחָמָה with the article (v6, 20, 27, 29).
- **"until he hath driven out his enemies" → "until he has dispossessed his enemies"** (v21) — Hebrew הוֹרִישׁ, the causative of יָרַשׁ "possess": put out of possession. Who "he" is — Yahweh, or each armed man — the Hebrew does not say; the nearest name is Yahweh.
- **"be guiltless before the LORD, and before Israel" → "be clear from Yahweh and from Israel"** (v22) — Hebrew נְקִיִּים מִן, "clear, free from": free of what is owed to them. The Hebrew clauses run on with "and"; where the conclusion begins is not marked, and the accents set it at "and afterward you return" or after it.
- **"be sure your sin will find you out" → "know your sin, which will find you"** (v23) — Hebrew וּדְעוּ חַטַּאתְכֶם אֲשֶׁר תִּמְצָא אֶתְכֶם, word for word. "You have sinned" is a Hebrew perfect: the thing is as good as done.
- **"do that which hath proceeded out of your mouth" → "what has gone out of your mouth you shall do"** (v24) — the Hebrew order.
- **"our flocks, and all our cattle" → "our livestock and all our beasts"** (v26) — Hebrew מִקְנֶה and בְּהֵמָה.
- **"every man armed for war … to battle" → "every one armed for the host … to the war"** (v27) — Hebrew חֲלוּץ צָבָא, "armed of the host": צָבָא is the army in its ranks; "war" is מִלְחָמָה.
- **"the chief fathers of the tribes" → "the heads of the fathers of the tribes"** (v28) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי אֲבוֹת הַמַּטּוֹת. Two Hebrew words are "tribe" in this chapter: מַטֶּה here (literally "staff", 17:2) and שֵׁבֶט in v33 (literally "rod"). They name the same thing, and English has one word for both. The priestly chapters of Numbers mostly use מַטֶּה. **"commanded" → "gave command concerning them to"** — Hebrew וַיְצַו לָהֶם … אֵת: "for them", about them.
- **"they shall have possessions among you" → "they shall take possession in your midst"** (v30) — Hebrew וְנֹאחֲזוּ, the verb of אֲחֻזָּה "possession" (Gen 47:27).
- **"As the LORD hath said" → "What Yahweh has spoken"** (v31) — Hebrew אֵת אֲשֶׁר דִּבֶּר. Moses spoke (v29); they call it the word of Yahweh.
- **"that the possession of our inheritance on this side Jordan may be ours" → "and with us shall be the possession of our inheritance across the Jordan"** (v32) — Hebrew וְאִתָּנוּ, "and with us"; no verb.
- **"half the tribe of Manasseh" → "the half of the tribe of Manasseh"** (v33) — Hebrew שֵׁבֶט, the other word for "tribe" (v28 has מַטֶּה). **"the land, with the cities thereof in the coasts, even the cities of the country round about" → "the land by its cities with their borders, the cities of the land all around"** — Hebrew הָאָרֶץ לְעָרֶיהָ בִּגְבֻלֹת עָרֵי הָאָרֶץ סָבִיב, literally "the land, as to its cities in borders, the cities of the land around"; "their" is supplied. גְּבוּל is a border and the land inside it. "The Amorites" is a singular in the Hebrew, "the Amorite" (v33, 39).
- **"(their names being changed)" → "changed in name"** (v38) — Hebrew מוּסַבֹּת שֵׁם, "turned round as to name"; whether it belongs to both names or to Baal-meon alone, and who changed them, is not said. **"gave other names unto the cities" → "called by names the names of the cities"** — Hebrew וַיִּקְרְאוּ בְשֵׁמֹת אֶת־שְׁמוֹת הֶעָרִים, word for word; "other" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"took it, and dispossessed" → "captured it, and he dispossessed"** (v39) — Hebrew לָכַד "capture" (v39, 41, 42); וַיּוֹרֶשׁ is singular, "he dispossessed" (Machir).
- **"small towns" → "tent villages"** (v41) — Hebrew חַוֺּת, the word in the name Havvoth-jair, "the tent villages of Jair"; "their" has no stated antecedent.
- **"the villages thereof" → "its daughters"** (v42) — Hebrew בְּנֹתֶיהָ, "her daughters": the settlements that belong to a city. **"after his own name" → "by his name"** — Hebrew בִּשְׁמוֹ.

### Notes
- **Reuben and Gad's request** — they want the Transjordan (east of Jordan), with its grasslands for livestock. Moses initially reads this as the earlier scout's rebellion redux — opting out of entering the land.
- **Compromise** — they agree to cross over and fight alongside the other tribes until Canaan is conquered; only then return to their Transjordan inheritance. Their families and livestock remain in the east.
- **"Be sure your sin will find you out"** (v23) — widely-quoted proverb. Hebrew: "know your sin, which will find you."
- **Half-tribe of Manasseh included** (v33) — additionally granted Transjordan territory (Bashan, conquered from Og). Originally Reuben + Gad; Manasseh's Machir clan adds itself.
- **Joshua 22** — after the conquest Reuben, Gad and half-Manasseh return east and build an altar of witness (Josh 22:10–34) to affirm their continued bond with the western tribes.
- **Later consequences** — the Transjordan tribes were the first to fall to Assyrian exile (1 Chr 5:25–26). Commentators see their choice-for-east-grasslands as exposing them earlier to invasion.
