## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"cattle" → "livestock"** (v1, 4, 16, 26) — Hebrew מִקְנֶה *miqneh* = cattle / flocks / livestock generally.
- **"an increase of sinful men" → "a brood of sinful men"** (v14) — Hebrew תַּרְבּוּת אֲנָשִׁים חַטָּאִים *tarbut anashim chatta'im*. A collective of sinners.
- **"Kenezite" → "Kenizzite"** (v12) — clan of Caleb, distinct from the Kenizzites of Canaan (Gen 15:19).
- **"princes" → "chiefs"** (v2).
- **"fenced cities" → "fortified cities"** (v17, 36).

### Notes
- **Reuben and Gad's request** — they want the Transjordan (east of Jordan), with its grasslands for livestock. Moses initially reads this as the earlier scout's rebellion redux — opting out of entering the land.
- **Compromise** — they agree to cross over and fight alongside the other tribes until Canaan is conquered; only then return to their Transjordan inheritance. Their families and livestock remain in the east.
- **"Be sure your sin will find you out"** (v23) — widely-quoted proverb. Hebrew: "know that your sin will find you."
- **Half-tribe of Manasseh included** (v33) — additionally granted Transjordan territory (Bashan, conquered from Og). Originally Reuben + Gad; Manasseh's Machir clan adds itself.
- **Joshua 22** — fulfills the covenant: after conquest, Reuben, Gad, half-Manasseh return east; build an altar of witness (Josh 22:10–34) to affirm their continued bond with the western tribes.
- **Later consequences** — the Transjordan tribes were the first to fall to Assyrian exile (1 Chr 5:25–26). Commentators see their choice-for-east-grasslands as exposing them earlier to invasion.
