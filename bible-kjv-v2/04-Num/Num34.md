# Numbers 34

[1] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[2] "Command the children of Israel and say to them: 'When you come into the land of Canaan (this is the land that shall fall to you as an inheritance — the land of Canaan within its borders):

[3] your southern side shall be from the wilderness of Zin alongside Edom, and your southern border shall be from the end of the Salt Sea on the east.

[4] Your border shall turn south of the ascent of Akrabbim and cross to Zin; and its endpoint shall be south of Kadesh-barnea, and it shall go on to Hazar-addar and pass to Azmon.

[5] The border shall turn from Azmon to the Wadi of Egypt, and it shall end at the Sea.

[6] As for the western border, you shall have the Great Sea as a border; this shall be your western border.

[7] This shall be your northern border: from the Great Sea you shall draw a line to Mount Hor,

[8] from Mount Hor you shall draw a line to Lebo-hamath, and the border shall end at Zedad.

[9] The border shall continue to Ziphron, and its endpoint shall be at Hazar-enan; this shall be your northern border.

[10] You shall draw your eastern border from Hazar-enan to Shepham.

[11] The border shall go down from Shepham to Riblah on the east side of Ain, and the border shall descend and reach the eastern slope of the Sea of Chinnereth.

[12] The border shall go down to the Jordan, and it shall end at the Salt Sea. This shall be your land with its borders all around.'"

[13] Moses commanded the children of Israel, saying, "This is the land that you shall inherit by lot, which Yahweh has commanded to be given to the nine tribes and the half-tribe;

[14] for the tribe of the children of Reuben according to their ancestral houses and the tribe of the children of Gad according to their ancestral houses have already received their inheritance, and half the tribe of Manasseh has received its inheritance.

[15] The two tribes and the half-tribe have received their inheritance on this side of the Jordan at Jericho, toward the east."

[16] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[17] "These are the names of the men who shall apportion the land to you: Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun.

[18] You shall take one chief from each tribe to apportion the land.

[19] These are the names of the men: from the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh;

[20] from the tribe of the children of Simeon, Shemuel son of Ammihud;

[21] from the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad son of Chislon;

[22] from the tribe of the children of Dan a chief, Bukki son of Jogli;

[23] from the children of Joseph: from the tribe of the children of Manasseh a chief, Hanniel son of Ephod;

[24] from the tribe of the children of Ephraim a chief, Kemuel son of Shiphtan;

[25] from the tribe of the children of Zebulun a chief, Elizaphan son of Parnach;

[26] from the tribe of the children of Issachar a chief, Paltiel son of Azzan;

[27] from the tribe of the children of Asher a chief, Ahihud son of Shelomi;

[28] from the tribe of the children of Naphtali a chief, Pedahel son of Ammihud."

[29] These are the ones whom Yahweh commanded to apportion the inheritance to the children of Israel in the land of Canaan.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"coasts" → "borders"** (v2, 12) — Hebrew גְּבוּל *gevul* = border. KJV *coast* = old English for boundary, not coastline.
- **"fetch a compass" → "turn" / "draw a line"** (v5, 7) — archaic idiom.
- **"salt sea" → "Salt Sea"** (v3, 12) — the Dead Sea.
- **"great sea" → "Great Sea"** (v6, 7) — the Mediterranean.
- **"Chinnereth" (v11)** — the Sea of Galilee / Lake Kinneret.
- **"entrance of Hamath" → "Lebo-hamath"** (v8) — likely a specific place name (Lebweh in Lebanon), not generic "entrance to Hamath."
- **"prince" → "chief"** (v18, 22–28) — Hebrew *nasi*.

### Notes
- **Idealized borders** — this is the largest "promised land" extent: from the Wadi of Egypt in the south to Lebo-hamath in the north; from the Mediterranean to the Jordan. Actually approximated only under David and Solomon (1 Kgs 4:21, 8:65).
- **Nine-and-a-half tribes** — Reuben, Gad, and half-Manasseh are already settled east of the Jordan (ch 32); the remaining 9.5 tribes will receive land west of the Jordan.
- **Distribution commissioners** (v17–28) — Eleazar + Joshua + one chief from each of the 10 relevant tribes (no commissioner needed for Transjordan tribes). Names otherwise unknown, preserving tribal records.
- **Caleb representing Judah** (v19) — the faithful scout (Num 13–14) stands in place of the deceased Nahshon (the earlier Judahite chief).
- **Execution in Joshua** — Joshua 13–21 carries out this allotment; the lot falls, each tribe receives its inheritance.
