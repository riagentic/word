# Numbers 11

[1] And the people were like those who complain, evil in the ears of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ heard, and his anger burned, and the fire of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ burned among them and consumed at the end of the camp.

[2] And the people cried out to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, and ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ prayed to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the fire sank down.

[3] And he called the name of that place ⟦taberah|Taverah|Taberah⟧, for the fire of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had burned among them.

[4] And the rabble that was in its midst craved a craving; and the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ also wept again and said, "Who will give us flesh to eat?

[5] We remember the fish that we used to eat in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ for nothing, the cucumbers and the melons and the leeks and the onions and the garlic.

[6] And now our soul is dried out; there is nothing at all, only toward the manna are our eyes."

[7] And the manna was like coriander seed, and its look was like the look of bdellium.

[8] The people would go about and gather, and grind in the handmill or beat in the mortar, and boil in the pot and make it into cakes; and its taste was like the taste of a dainty of oil.

[9] And when the dew came down upon the camp at night, the manna would come down upon it.

[10] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ heard the people weeping by its clans, each man at the entrance of his tent; and the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ burned greatly, and in the eyes of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ it was evil.

[11] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "Why have you done evil to your servant, and why have I not found favor in your eyes, to put the burden of all this people upon me?

[12] Was it I who conceived all this people, or I who gave birth to it, that you say to me, 'Bear it in your bosom, as the foster father bears the suckling,' to the ground that you swore to its fathers?

[13] From where do I have flesh to give to all this people? For they weep against me, saying, 'Give us flesh, that we may eat.'

[14] I am not able, I alone, to bear all this people, for it is too heavy for me.

[15] And if thus you are doing to me, surely kill me, please, if I have found favor in your eyes, and let me not look upon my evil."

[16] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Gather for me seventy men from the elders of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, whom you know to be the elders of the people and its officers, and you shall take them to the tent of meeting, and they shall take their stand there with you.

[17] And I will come down and speak with you there, and I will set aside from the spirit that is upon you and put it upon them, and they shall bear with you the burden of the people, and you shall not bear it, you alone.

[18] And to the people you shall say, 'Sanctify yourselves for tomorrow, and you shall eat flesh, for you have wept in the ears of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying, "Who will give us flesh to eat? For it was good for us in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧." And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will give you flesh, and you shall eat.

[19] Not one day shall you eat, and not two days, and not five days, and not ten days, and not twenty days,

[20] until a month of days, until it comes out from your nose and becomes a loathsome thing to you, because you have rejected ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who is in your midst and have wept before him, saying, "Why is it that we came out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧?"'"

[21] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said, "Six hundred thousand on foot are the people in whose midst I am, and you, you have said, 'Flesh I will give them, and they shall eat a month of days.'

[22] Shall flock and herd be slaughtered for them, and it suffice for them? Or shall all the fish of the sea be gathered for them, and it suffice for them?"

[23] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Is the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ short? Now you shall see whether my word befalls you or not."

[24] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ went out and spoke to the people the words of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he gathered seventy men from the elders of the people and made them stand around the tent.

[25] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came down in the cloud and spoke to him, and he set aside from the spirit that was upon him and placed it upon the seventy men, the elders; and it came to pass, when the spirit rested upon them, that they prophesied, and they did not do so again.

[26] And two men were left in the camp; the name of the one was Eldad, and the name of the second ⟦medad|Meidad|Medad⟧; and the spirit rested upon them — and they were among those written, and they had not gone out to the tent — and they prophesied in the camp.

[27] And the young man ran and told ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and said, "Eldad and ⟦medad|Meidad|Medad⟧ are prophesying in the camp."

[28] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ the son of Nun, the minister of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ from his youth, answered and said, "My lord ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, restrain them."

[29] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said to him, "Are you zealous for me? And would that all the people of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ were prophets, that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ would place his spirit upon them!"

[30] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ gathered himself to the camp, he and the elders of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[31] And a wind set out from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and brought quail over from the sea and left them upon the camp, about a day's way on this side and about a day's way on that side, around the camp, and about two cubits upon the face of the earth.

[32] And the people rose up all that day and all the night and all the day of the morrow and gathered the quail; he who gathered little gathered ten homers; and they surely spread them out for themselves around the camp.

[33] The flesh was still between their teeth, it was not yet cut off, and the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ burned against the people, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ struck among the people a very great blow.

[34] And he called the name of that place ⟦kibroth-hattaavah|Qivrot HaTaavah|Kibroth-hattaavah⟧, for there they buried the people who craved.

[35] From ⟦kibroth-hattaavah|Qivrot HaTaavah|Kibroth-hattaavah⟧ the people set out for ⟦hazeroth|Chatzerot|Hazeroth⟧, and they were in ⟦hazeroth|Chatzerot|Hazeroth⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"when the people complained, it displeased the LORD" → "the people were like those who complain, evil in the ears of Yahweh"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי הָעָם כְּמִתְאֹנְנִים רַע בְּאָזְנֵי יְהוָה. The verb הִתְאוֹנֵן "complain" is found only here and in Lam 3:39 (another word than לוּן "grumble", ch 14). רַע "evil" stands loosely beside it: by the accents with "in the ears of Yahweh" (a thing evil in his ears); it can also be the object, "those who complain of evil". The same word is in v10 ("in the eyes of Moses it was evil"), v11 ("done evil to") and v15 ("my evil").
- **"his anger was kindled" → "his anger burned"** (v1, 10, 33) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר אַפּוֹ; אַף is "nose" and "anger" (in v20 the nose itself).
- **"burnt among them, and consumed them that were in the uttermost parts of the camp" → "burned among them and consumed at the end of the camp"** (v1) — Hebrew וַתֹּאכַל בִּקְצֵה הַמַּחֲנֶה, literally "and ate at the end of the camp"; the verb has no object. בָּעַר "burn" (v1, 3) is the root of the name Taberah, "burning"; it is another verb than חָרָה "burn" of anger.
- **"the fire was quenched" → "the fire sank down"** (v2) — Hebrew וַתִּשְׁקַע.
- **"he called the name of the place Taberah" kept** (v3, 34) — Hebrew וַיִּקְרָא, "he called": the one who names is not said ("one called"). The meanings of the names ("Burning", "Graves of the Craving") are not in the Hebrew verse and were taken out of it.
- **"the mixt multitude that was among them fell a lusting" → "the rabble that was in its midst craved a craving"** (v4) — Hebrew הָאסַפְסֻף, found only here: "what is gathered together", a gathered crowd (root אָסַף "gather", which runs through the chapter: v16, 22, 24, 30, 32). Its exact sense is uncertain; "mixed multitude" is the word of Exod 12:38, another Hebrew word. הִתְאַוּוּ תַּאֲוָה is verb and noun of one root; the noun is in the name of v34.
- **"wept again" kept** (v4) — Hebrew וַיָּשֻׁבוּ וַיִּבְכּוּ, literally "and they turned back and wept". **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"Who shall give us flesh to eat?" kept** (v4, 18) — Hebrew מִי יַאֲכִלֵנוּ בָּשָׂר, literally "who will make us eat flesh?" בָּשָׂר is "flesh" (so Exod 16:3, 8, 12).
- **"which we did eat in Egypt freely" → "that we used to eat in Egypt for nothing"** (v5) — Hebrew חִנָּם "without payment". Five kinds, each joined by "and"; חָצִיר is elsewhere "grass", here taken as leeks.
- **"our soul is dried away: there is nothing at all, beside this manna, before our eyes" → "our soul is dried out; there is nothing at all, only toward the manna are our eyes"** (v6) — Hebrew נַפְשֵׁנוּ יְבֵשָׁה; נֶפֶשׁ "soul" is also the throat and the appetite. בִּלְתִּי אֶל־הַמָּן עֵינֵינוּ, literally "except: to the manna our eyes".
- **"manna"** (v6, 7, 9) — the Hebrew is מָן *man* (Exod 16:15, 31).
- **"the colour thereof as the colour of bdellium" → "its look was like the look of bdellium"** (v7) — Hebrew וְעֵינוֹ כְּעֵין הַבְּדֹלַח, literally "its eye like the eye of the bdellium"; "appearance" is another word (מַרְאֶה). בְּדֹלַח (Gen 2:12) is not certainly known: a fragrant gum, or a stone.
- **"went about, and gathered it, and ground it in mills" → "would go about and gather, and grind in the handmill"** (v8) — the verbs are forms of repeated action, and only "make" has an object ("it"). רֵחַיִם is a dual, the two millstones; מְדֹכָה "mortar" and פָּרוּר "pot" are singular (פָּרוּר is another word than סִיר "pot"). **"baked it in pans" → "boil in the pot"** — Hebrew בִּשֵּׁל.
- **"as the taste of fresh oil" → "like the taste of a dainty of oil"** (v8) — Hebrew לְשַׁד הַשָּׁמֶן. לָשָׁד is found only here and in Ps 32:4 and its meaning is uncertain: "juice, sap" (so Ps 32:4), hence a juicy or rich morsel; "cake baked with oil" and "cream of oil" are also given. Exod 16:31 says "like a wafer in honey".
- **"the manna fell upon it" → "the manna would come down upon it"** (v9) — Hebrew יָרַד "come down", of the dew and of the manna; "it" may be the dew or the camp.
- **"throughout their families, every man in the door of his tent" → "by its clans, each man at the entrance of his tent"** (v10) — Hebrew לְמִשְׁפְּחֹתָיו; פֶּתַח.
- **"Moses also was displeased" → "in the eyes of Moses it was evil"** (v10) — Hebrew וּבְעֵינֵי מֹשֶׁה רָע. What was evil in his eyes is not said.
- **"Wherefore hast thou afflicted thy servant" → "Why have you done evil to your servant"** (v11) — Hebrew הֲרֵעֹתָ, the verb of רַע. עֶבֶד is "slave"; of one who serves Yahweh, "servant". **"that thou layest the burden" → "to put the burden"** — Hebrew לָשׂוּם, an infinitive. מַשָּׂא "burden" is the noun of נָשָׂא "bear" (v12, 14, 17).
- **"Have I conceived all this people? have I begotten them" → "Was it I who conceived all this people, or I who gave birth to it"** (v12) — Hebrew הֶאָנֹכִי … אִם־אָנֹכִי, the "I" stressed both times. יָלַד beside "conceive" is the mother's bearing; "gave birth to" is used because "bear" stands for נָשָׂא in the same verse. The people is "it" (singular) throughout.
- **"as a nursing father beareth the sucking child, unto the land which thou swarest" → "as the foster father bears the suckling, to the ground that you swore"** (v12) — Hebrew הָאֹמֵן, a masculine: one who brings up a child (Isa 49:23); הַיֹּנֵק "the one that sucks"; אֲדָמָה "ground".
- **"Whence should I have flesh" → "From where do I have flesh"** (v13) — Hebrew מֵאַיִן לִי בָּשָׂר. **"they weep unto me" → "they weep against me"** — Hebrew עָלַי, literally "upon me" (as Judg 14:16).
- **"it is too heavy for me" kept** (v14) — Hebrew כָבֵד מִמֶּנִּי, literally "heavy from me": heavier than I can bear.
- **"kill me, I pray thee, out of hand" → "surely kill me, please"** (v15) — Hebrew הָרְגֵנִי נָא הָרֹג, the verb with its infinitive absolute, literally "kill me, please, killing". "You" is here written with the feminine form אַתְּ (as Deut 5:27).
- **"let me not see my wretchedness" → "let me not look upon my evil"** (v15) — Hebrew בְּרָעָתִי: the evil that has come on me. An old scribal tradition (the *tiqqune sopherim*) says the text once read "your evil" (from memory).
- **"officers over them" → "its officers"** (v16) — Hebrew שֹׁטְרָיו (Exod 5:6). **"bring them … that they may stand" → "you shall take them … and they shall take their stand"** — Hebrew וְלָקַחְתָּ … וְהִתְיַצְּבוּ.
- **"I will take of the spirit which is upon thee, and will put it upon them" → "I will set aside from the spirit that is upon you and put it upon them"** (v17, 25) — Hebrew וְאָצַלְתִּי מִן־הָרוּחַ; אָצַל is "lay aside, keep back" (Gen 27:36). The verbs have no object in the Hebrew ("it" is supplied in English). In v17 "put" is שִׂים, in v25 and v29 "place" is נָתַן, literally "give".
- **"spirit" / "wind"** (v17, 25, 26, 29, 31) — one Hebrew word, רוּחַ: "the spirit" that is on Moses, "his spirit" (v29), and the "wind" from Yahweh (v31).
- **"Sanctify yourselves against to morrow" → "Sanctify yourselves for tomorrow"** (v18) — Hebrew הִתְקַדְּשׁוּ. **"it was well with us" → "it was good for us"** — Hebrew טוֹב לָנוּ. **"therefore the LORD will give" → "And Yahweh will give"** — the Hebrew has only "and".
- **"Ye shall not eat one day, nor two days … but even a whole month" → "Not one day shall you eat, and not two days … until a month of days"** (v19, 20) — the Hebrew order; חֹדֶשׁ יָמִים, literally "a month of days", a full month (v21); "but" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"until it come out at your nostrils, and it be loathsome unto you" → "until it comes out from your nose and becomes a loathsome thing to you"** (v20) — Hebrew מֵאַפְּכֶם, singular; זָרָא is found only here and its meaning is uncertain (a thing loathed, nausea).
- **"ye have despised the LORD which is among you" → "you have rejected Yahweh who is in your midst"** (v20) — Hebrew מָאַס "reject, refuse". **"Why came we forth out of Egypt?" → "Why is it that we came out from Egypt?"** — Hebrew לָמָּה זֶּה.
- **"The people, among whom I am, are six hundred thousand footmen" → "Six hundred thousand on foot are the people in whose midst I am"** (v21) — the Hebrew order; רַגְלִי "on foot". "You" and "flesh" stand first in their clauses.
- **"Shall the flocks and the herds be slain for them, to suffice them?" → "Shall flock and herd be slaughtered for them, and it suffice for them?"** (v22) — Hebrew צֹאן וּבָקָר, collective singulars; שָׁחַט; וּמָצָא לָהֶם, literally "and one find for them" (be enough for them), twice.
- **"Is the LORD's hand waxed short? … whether my word shall come to pass unto thee" → "Is the hand of Yahweh short? … whether my word befalls you"** (v23) — Hebrew תִּקְצָר, "is / shall be short"; הֲיִקְרְךָ, from קָרָה "meet, happen to".
- **"told the people" → "spoke to the people"** (v24) — Hebrew וַיְדַבֵּר. **"the seventy men … set them" → "seventy men … made them stand"** — the Hebrew has no article in v24; וַיַּעֲמֵד.
- **"gave it unto the seventy elders" → "placed it upon the seventy men, the elders"** (v25) — Hebrew עַל־שִׁבְעִים אִישׁ הַזְּקֵנִים.
- **"they prophesied"** (v25, 26, 27) — Hebrew הִתְנַבֵּא, the reflexive stem of the verb of נָבִיא "prophet" (v29): behave or speak as a prophet. What they said is not told.
- **"and did not cease" → "and they did not do so again"** (v25) — Hebrew וְלֹא יָסָפוּ, literally "and they did not add" (from יָסַף, as Deut 5:22): they did it this once. KJV's "did not cease" reads the same letters as a form of סוּף "come to an end"; so the Aramaic Targum and the Latin Vulgate (from memory).
- **"there remained two of the men" → "two men were left"** (v26) — Hebrew וַיִּשָּׁאֲרוּ שְׁנֵי־אֲנָשִׁים. **"the name of the other" → "the name of the second"** — Hebrew הַשֵּׁנִי. **"they were of them that were written" → "they were among those written"** — Hebrew בַּכְּתֻבִים; where they were written is not said.
- **"there ran a young man" → "the young man ran"** (v27) — Hebrew הַנַּעַר with the article; נַעַר is "boy", also of a grown attendant.
- **"the servant of Moses, one of his young men" → "the minister of Moses from his youth"** (v28) — Hebrew מְשָׁרֵת, one who attends on a person (Exod 24:13; 33:11). מִבְּחֻרָיו is uncertain: "from his youth" (בְּחוּרִים "time of youth"), or "of his chosen men" (בָּחוּר "chosen, young man"); the Septuagint has "the chosen one" (from memory).
- **"forbid them" → "restrain them"** (v28) — Hebrew כְּלָאֵם, from כָּלָא "keep back, shut in".
- **"Enviest thou for my sake?" → "Are you zealous for me?"** (v29) — Hebrew הַמְקַנֵּא אַתָּה לִי; קִנֵּא is "be jealous" and, with לְ, "be zealous for".
- **"would God that all the LORD's people were prophets, and that the LORD would put his spirit upon them" → "And would that all the people of Yahweh were prophets, that Yahweh would place his spirit upon them"** (v29) — Hebrew וּמִי יִתֵּן … כִּי־יִתֵּן, literally "and who will give all the people of Yahweh prophets, that / when Yahweh gives his spirit upon them": the same verb twice. "God" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"gat him into the camp" → "gathered himself to the camp"** (v30) — Hebrew וַיֵּאָסֵף, the passive-reflexive of אָסַף "gather": he withdrew.
- **"there went forth a wind from the LORD, and brought quails from the sea, and let them fall by the camp" → "a wind set out from Yahweh and brought quail over from the sea and left them upon the camp"** (v31) — Hebrew נָסַע, the verb of Israel's setting out (v35); וַיָּגָז, from a rare verb "pass over" (Ps 90:10), here "bring over" (uncertain); וַיִּטֹּשׁ "left, let lie"; עַל "upon, by". שַׂלְוִים is the plural of the collective "quail" of v32 and Exod 16:13. "From the sea": יָם is also "west".
- **"as it were a day's journey on this side … as it were two cubits high upon the face of the earth" → "about a day's way on this side … about two cubits upon the face of the earth"** (v31) — Hebrew כֹּה … כֹּה, literally "thus … thus". Whether the two cubits are the depth of the heap or the height at which the birds flew is not said; "high" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"stood up" → "rose up"; "he that gathered least" → "he who gathered little"** (v32) — Hebrew הַמַּמְעִיט, as Exod 16:17, 18. "Gather" here is אָסַף; in v8 it is לָקַט, the word of Exod 16. The homer (חֹמֶר) is a dry measure, a donkey's load.
- **"they spread them all abroad" → "they surely spread them out"** (v32) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁטְחוּ … שָׁטוֹחַ, the verb with its infinitive absolute, literally "they spread out … spreading out".
- **"ere it was chewed" → "it was not yet cut off"** (v33) — Hebrew טֶרֶם יִכָּרֵת, from כָּרַת "cut": before it was bitten through, or before the supply came to an end.
- **"the wrath of the LORD was kindled … smote the people with a very great plague" → "the anger of Yahweh burned … struck among the people a very great blow"** (v33) — Hebrew וַיַּךְ … מַכָּה, verb and noun of one root; "plague" is another word.
- **"that lusted" → "who craved"** (v34) — Hebrew הַמִּתְאַוִּים, the verb of v4. קִבְרוֹת הַתַּאֲוָה is "the graves of the craving".
- **"journeyed … unto Hazeroth; and abode at Hazeroth" → "set out for Hazeroth, and they were in Hazeroth"** (v35) — Hebrew נָסְעוּ; וַיִּהְיוּ "and they were".

### Notes
- **Two named murmurings** — Taberah (v3, "burning") and Kibroth-hattaavah (v34, "graves of the craving") bracket this chapter. The wilderness generation's characteristic rebellion-pattern begins here.
- **Manna description** (v7–9) — the only detailed physical description. Similar to Ex 16:14, 31 but more pictorial.
- **Moses' despair** (v11–15) — remarkable OT expression of leadership exhaustion. Compare Elijah (1 Kgs 19:4), Jonah (Jon 4:3). Moses asks to die.
- **Seventy elders share the *ruach*** (v16–17, 25) — prophecy distributed. Acts 2 (Pentecost) echoes: "your sons and your daughters shall prophesy" (Joel 2:28).
- **Eldad and Medad** (v26–29) — two men receive the spirit *outside* the tent. Moses' response: "would that all the people of Yahweh were prophets!"
- **Quail and blow** (v31–34) — Yahweh provides excess, but the craving is punished. Ps 78:26–31, 106:14–15 reflect on this event.
