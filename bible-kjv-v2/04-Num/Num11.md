## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"complained" → "became like those who complain of misfortune"** (v1) — Hebrew כְּמִתְאֹנְנִים *ke-mit'onenim* = "as grumblers" — specifically, those who mutter misfortune.
- **"Taberah" (v3)** — Hebrew תַּבְעֵרָה *Tav'erah* from בָּעַר *ba'ar* = burn. Etymology added.
- **"mixt multitude" → "mixed multitude"** (v4) — Hebrew אֲסַפְסֻף *asafsuf* — a rabble of non-Israelites who left Egypt with them.
- **"fell a lusting" → "had a strong craving"** (v4) — Hebrew הִתְאַוּוּ תַּאֲוָה *hit'avvu ta'avah* = craved a craving.
- **"bdellium" (v7)** — Hebrew בְּדֹלַח *bedolach*. A resin / gemstone of uncertain identification (cf. Gen 2:12). Kept transliterated.
- **"pans" → "pots"** (v8) — Hebrew פָּרוּר *parur* = cooking pot.
- **"sucking child" → "nursing child"** (v12) — modernized.
- **"out of hand" → "at once"** (v15) — idiom for immediate.
- **"Kibroth-hattaavah" (v34)** — Hebrew קִבְרוֹת הַתַּאֲוָה *Qivrot ha-ta'avah* = Graves of Craving. Etymology added.
- **"waxed short" → "too short"** (v23) — modernized.

### Notes
- **Two named murmurings** — Taberah (v3, "burning") and Kibroth-hattaavah (v34, "graves of craving") bracket this chapter. The wilderness generation's characteristic rebellion-pattern begins here.
- **Manna description** (v7–9) — the only detailed physical description. Similar to Ex 16:14, 31 but more pictorial. "Bdellium" is mentioned for its color/appearance (yellowish-white).
- **Moses' despair** (v11–15) — remarkable OT expression of leadership exhaustion. Compare Elijah (1 Kgs 19:4), Jonah (Jon 4:3). Moses asks to die.
- **Seventy elders share the *ruach*** (v16–17, 25) — charismatic prophecy distributed. Acts 2 (Pentecost) echoes: "your sons and your daughters shall prophesy" (Joel 2:28).
- **Eldad and Medad** (v26–29) — two elders receive the spirit *outside* the tent. Moses' response, "Would that all the people of Yahweh were prophets!" becomes a programmatic prophecy.
- **Quail and plague** (v31–34) — Yahweh provides excess, but the craving is punished. Ps 78:26–31, 106:14–15 reflect on this event.
