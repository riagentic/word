# Numbers 17

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and take from each of them a staff — one for each ancestral house, from all their chiefs, twelve staffs. Write each man's name on his staff.

[3] Write ⟦aaron|Aharon's|Aaron's⟧ name on the staff of Levi, for there shall be one staff for the head of each ancestral house.

[4] You shall lay them up in the Tent of Meeting before the Testimony, where I meet with you.

[5] The staff of the man whom I choose shall sprout; so I will still the grumblings of the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from upon me, which they murmur against you."

[6] ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ spoke to the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and each of their chiefs gave him a staff — for each chief one — according to their ancestral houses, twelve staffs; and ⟦aaron|Aharon's|Aaron's⟧ staff was among their staffs.

[8] On the next day ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ went into the Tent of the Testimony, and behold, the staff of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, of the house of Levi, had sprouted — it had put forth buds, produced blossoms, and yielded almonds.

[9] ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ brought out all the staffs from before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to all the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they looked, and each took his staff.

[10] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Put ⟦aaron|Aharon's|Aaron's⟧ staff back before the Testimony, to be kept as a sign for the rebels, that you may put an end to their grumblings against me, lest they die."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"rod" → "staff"** (throughout) — Hebrew מַטֶּה *matteh* = staff / tribal-staff. Double meaning: a walking-staff and a tribe (the tribes themselves are *mattot*).
- **"tabernacle of witness" → "Tent of the Testimony"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew אֹהֶל הָעֵדוּת *ohel ha-edut*. *Edut* = the tablets ("testimony") inside the ark.
- **"shall be consumed with dying" → "are we then to perish entirely"** (v13) — Hebrew הַאִם תַּמְנוּ לִגְוֹעַ *ha-im tamnu ligwo'a*. A cry of dismay after Korah's punishment.
- **Hebrew/English chapter numbering** — MT numbers this chapter 17:16–28 (continuing from 16 where Eng 17 begins). Eng translations since KJV start fresh at 17:1.

### Notes
- **Aaron's staff blooms** — miraculous overnight budding, blossoming, fruiting. A definitive divine vindication of Aaron's priesthood.
- **Almond tree** — Hebrew שָׁקֵד *shaqed*, from *shaqad* "to watch / hasten" — the first tree to bloom in spring. Cf. Jer 1:11–12 (Yahweh watching/hastening over his word). Symbolically: Yahweh hastens to vindicate his chosen priest.
- **Staff kept before the Testimony** (v10) — stored inside or beside the ark. Heb 9:4 mentions it with the manna jar and the two tablets as the ark's contents.
- **Covenant sign vs the rebels** — the staff becomes the antidote to recurring claims about the priesthood. Future disputes resolved by reference to this sign.
- **People's fear** (v12–13) — after Korah, the people grasp the lethality of proximity to the holy. Sets up the next chapter (18) on priestly intercession and buffering.
