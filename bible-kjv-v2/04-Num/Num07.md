## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"princes" → "chiefs"** (throughout) — Hebrew *nasi*, as elsewhere.
- **"charger" → "platter"** (v13, 84, 85) — Hebrew קְעָרָה *qe'arah*. A large flat dish, not a war-horse or meaning "one who charges."
- **"spoon" → "ladle"** (v14, 86) — Hebrew *kap*. Incense-ladle / censer-spoon.
- **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** (throughout) — as in Leviticus.
- **"heard the voice of one speaking" → "heard the Voice speaking"** (v89) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁמַע אֶת־הַקּוֹל *wayyishma et-ha-qol*. Definite article: *the* Voice.

### Notes
- **Longest chapter in the book** — twelve nearly identical offerings, one per tribal chief over twelve days. Repetition is theological: each tribe receives equal honor, equal dedication, equal presence before Yahweh.
- **Order matches Num 2** — Judah first, then Issachar, Zebulun (east camp); Reuben, Simeon, Gad (south); Ephraim, Manasseh, Benjamin (west); Dan, Asher, Naphtali (north).
- **Wagons for Gershon and Merari but not Kohath** — the holiest items (ark, table, lampstand, altars) are carried by shoulder, not wagon. Later David's Uzzah-incident (2 Sam 6) violates this by putting the ark on a cart.
- **Voice from between the cherubim** (v89) — where Yahweh promised to meet Moses (Ex 25:22). The tabernacle is functional; revelation now arrives through the completed sanctuary.
