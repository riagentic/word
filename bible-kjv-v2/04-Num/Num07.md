## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. Where a verse has no "and" in the Hebrew (v7, 18, 24 and the like), none is written.
- **"had fully set up the tabernacle" → "finished raising up the dwelling"** (v1) — Hebrew כַּלּוֹת … לְהָקִים אֶת־הַמִּשְׁכָּן (Exod 40:17–18). **"all the instruments thereof … all the vessels thereof" → "all its utensils" twice** — Hebrew כֵּלָיו both times; in v85 the same word is "vessels". The verse says "anointed and sanctified" twice, and so does the translation.
- **"princes" → "chieftains"** (throughout) — Hebrew נְשִׂיאִים, literally "ones lifted up". **"offered" → "brought near"** (v2, 10–12, 18, 19) — Hebrew הִקְרִיב; "offering" is its noun קָרְבָּן, "what is brought near". In v2 the verb has no object; the sentence runs on into v3. **"heads of the house of their fathers" → "the heads of their fathers' houses"** (v2) — Hebrew בֵּית אֲבֹתָם, as 1:2. **"were over them that were numbered" → "stood over those who were numbered"** — Hebrew הָעֹמְדִים עַל־הַפְּקֻדִים (1:5).
- **"six covered wagons, and twelve oxen" → "six covered wagons and twelve of the herd"** (v3, 6–8, 17, 87, 88) — Hebrew בָּקָר, the collective "herd, cattle"; the single animal is שׁוֹר "ox" (v3). צָב "covered" is found only here and in Isa 66:20, and its meaning is uncertain. **"a wagon for two of the princes"** — Hebrew עַל, "upon".
- **"spake" → "said … saying"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר … לֵּאמֹר.
- **"that they may be to do the service of the tabernacle of the congregation" → "they shall be for serving the service of the tent of meeting"** (v5) — Hebrew לַעֲבֹד אֶת־עֲבֹדַת, verb and noun of one root; אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד. **"according to his service"** (v5, 7, 8) — Hebrew כְּפִי, literally "according to the mouth of".
- **"under the hand of Ithamar" → "by the hand of Ithamar"** (v8) — Hebrew בְּיַד. "The priest" may belong to Ithamar or to Aaron.
- **"because the service of the sanctuary belonging unto them was that they should bear upon their shoulders" → "for the service of the Holy was upon them; on the shoulder they carried"** (v9) — Hebrew עֲבֹדַת הַקֹּדֶשׁ עֲלֵהֶם בַּכָּתֵף יִשָּׂאוּ. הַקֹּדֶשׁ is the word for the first room of the tent; here, as in 4:15, the holy things are meant; "things" is not written. "He did not give" has no object in the Hebrew.
- **"offered for dedicating of the altar" → "brought near the dedication of the altar"** (v10, 11, 84, 88) — Hebrew חֲנֻכַּת הַמִּזְבֵּחַ: the dedication, and what is brought for it. **"in the day that it was anointed"** (v10, 84) — Hebrew בְּיוֹם הִמָּשַׁח אֹתוֹ.
- **"each prince on his day" → "One chieftain a day, one chieftain a day"** (v11) — Hebrew נָשִׂיא אֶחָד לַיּוֹם נָשִׂיא אֶחָד לַיּוֹם, said twice.
- **"of the tribe of Judah" → "for the tribe of Judah"** (v12) — Hebrew לְמַטֵּה.
- **The twelve paragraphs** (v12–83) — from "one dish of silver" to "five lambs a year old" the Hebrew of the twelve is the same word for word, and so is the translation. The openings differ and are kept apart: "And the one who brought near his offering on the first day was …" (v12) and "And his offering" (v13); "On the second day Nethanel … brought near" (v18) and "He brought near his offering" (v19); from the third day on a heading without a verb, "On the third day, the chieftain for the sons of Zebulun, Eliab the son of Helon" (v24), and "His offering" (v25). Judah's and Issachar's chieftains are named before their tribe, the others after it; only the first is "for the tribe of", the second "the chieftain of Issachar", the rest "for the sons of".
- **"one silver charger, the weight thereof was an hundred and thirty shekels" → "one dish of silver, its weight one hundred and thirty"** (v13 and the like) — Hebrew קְעָרָה, a dish (Exod 25:29); "shekels" is not written after this number. **"one silver bowl of seventy shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary" → "one bowl of silver, seventy shekels by the holy shekel"** — Hebrew מִזְרָק, the bowl for dashing blood (Exod 27:3); בְּשֶׁקֶל הַקֹּדֶשׁ, literally "by the shekel of holiness" or "of the Holy", the sanctuary's weight (Exod 30:13). The shekel is a weight, not a coin. **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** — Hebrew מִנְחָה.
- **"One spoon of ten shekels of gold" → "one pan, ten of gold"** (v14 and the like) — Hebrew כַּף אַחַת עֲשָׂרָה זָהָב; כַּף is literally "palm", a shallow bowl (Exod 25:29); "shekels" is not written (v86 names the holy shekel).
- **"One young bullock" → "one bull of the herd"** (v15 and the like) — Hebrew פַּר אֶחָד בֶּן־בָּקָר, literally "one bull, a son of the herd"; the phrase says nothing of age. **"one lamb of the first year" → "one lamb a year old"** — Hebrew כֶּבֶשׂ … בֶּן־שְׁנָתוֹ, literally "a son of its year".
- **"One kid of the goats" → "one male goat"; "five he goats"** (v16, 17 and the like) — Hebrew שְׂעִיר־עִזִּים and עַתּוּדִים, two words. **"for a sacrifice of peace offerings, two oxen" → "for the sacrifice of the peace offerings: two of the herd"** — Hebrew וּלְזֶבַח הַשְּׁלָמִים בָּקָר שְׁנַיִם. **"this was the offering"** — the Hebrew has no verb.
- **"On the eleventh day"; "On the twelfth day"** (v72, 78) — Hebrew בְּיוֹם עַשְׁתֵּי עָשָׂר יוֹם, בְּיוֹם שְׁנֵים עָשָׂר יוֹם, literally "on the day of eleven day": Hebrew has no ordinal above ten.
- **"chargers of silver … spoons of gold"** (v84–86) — the numbers are written in words in ordinary English order; the Hebrew has "dishes of silver, twelve" and so on. **"Each charger of silver weighing an hundred and thirty shekels" → "one hundred and thirty the one dish of silver"** (v85) — "shekels" and "weighing" are not in the Hebrew. **"weighing ten shekels apiece" → "ten each pan"** (v86) — Hebrew עֲשָׂרָה עֲשָׂרָה הַכַּף, "ten, ten the pan".
- **"All the oxen for the burnt offering were twelve bullocks" → "All the herd for the burnt offering: twelve bulls"** (v87, 88) — Hebrew כָּל־הַבָּקָר … פָּרִים. **"with their meat offering" → "and their grain offering"** (v87) — Hebrew וּמִנְחָתָם.
- **"into the tabernacle of the congregation" → "into the tent of meeting"** (v89) — Hebrew אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד. **"to speak with him"** — Hebrew לְדַבֵּר אִתּוֹ; who "him" is the verse does not name.
- **"he heard the voice of one speaking unto him" → "he heard the voice speaking to him"** (v89) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁמַע אֶת־הַקּוֹל מִדַּבֵּר אֵלָיו. "Of one" is not in the Hebrew; "the voice" has the article. מִדַּבֵּר is pointed as a reflexive form (also Ezek 2:2; 43:6), "speaking with itself" or "making itself heard"; the same letters can be read as the ordinary "speaking".
- **"from off the mercy seat that was upon the ark of testimony" → "from above the kapporet that was on the ark of the testimony"** (v89) — Hebrew הַכַּפֹּרֶת, the "atonement cover", from כִּפֶּר (Exod 25:17–22). **"and he spake unto him" → "and he spoke to him"** — Hebrew וַיְדַבֵּר אֵלָיו; the subject is not named.

### Notes

- **Twelve days, twelve offerings** — each tribe's chieftain brings the same offering on his own day, and the text writes it out in full each time.
- **The order** follows the camp order of chapter 2: Judah, Issachar, Zebulun; Reuben, Simeon, Gad; Ephraim, Manasseh, Benjamin; Dan, Asher, Naphtali.
- **The wagons** (v3–9) — two for the sons of Gershon, four for the sons of Merari; none for the sons of Kohath, who carry the holy things on the shoulder (4:15; compare 2 Sam 6:3–7).
- **The totals** (v84–88) — the silver is 12 × 130 + 12 × 70 = 2,400; the gold 12 × 10 = 120.
- **The voice** (v89) — from above the kapporet, from between the two cherubim, as Exod 25:22 says.
