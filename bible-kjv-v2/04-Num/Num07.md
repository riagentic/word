# Numbers 7

[1] On the day when Moses had finished setting up the Dwelling, and had anointed and consecrated it with all its furnishings, and had anointed and consecrated the altar with all its utensils,

[2] the chiefs of Israel, heads of their ancestral houses, who were the chiefs of the tribes, who were over those who were numbered, made an offering.

[3] They brought their offering before Yahweh: six covered wagons and twelve oxen — a wagon for every two of the chiefs and an ox for each one; and they brought them before the Dwelling.

[4] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[5] "Accept these from them, that they may be used to do the service of the Tent of Meeting; and you shall give them to the Levites, to each man according to his service."

[6] So Moses took the wagons and the oxen and gave them to the Levites.

[7] Two wagons and four oxen he gave to the sons of Gershon, according to their service;

[8] four wagons and eight oxen he gave to the sons of Merari, according to their service, under the hand of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.

[9] But to the sons of Kohath he gave none, because the service of the sanctuary belonging to them was to carry on their shoulders.

[10] The chiefs offered at the dedication of the altar on the day it was anointed; the chiefs offered their offering before the altar.

[11] Yahweh said to Moses, "They shall offer their offering, each chief on his day, for the dedication of the altar."

[12] The one who offered his offering on the first day was Nahshon son of Amminadab, of the tribe of Judah.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"princes" → "chiefs"** (throughout) — Hebrew *nasi*, as elsewhere.
- **"charger" → "platter"** (v13, 84, 85) — Hebrew קְעָרָה *qe'arah*. A large flat dish, not a war-horse or meaning "one who charges."
- **"spoon" → "ladle"** (v14, 86) — Hebrew *kap*. Incense-ladle / censer-spoon.
- **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** (throughout) — as in Leviticus.
- **"heard the voice of one speaking" → "heard the Voice speaking"** (v89) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁמַע אֶת־הַקּוֹל *wayyishma et-ha-qol*. Definite article: *the* Voice.

### Notes
- **Longest chapter in the book** — twelve nearly identical offerings, one per tribal chief over twelve days. Repetition is theological: each tribe receives equal honor, equal dedication, equal presence before Yahweh.
- **Order matches Num 2** — Judah first, then Issachar, Zebulun (east camp); Reuben, Simeon, Gad (south); Ephraim, Manasseh, Benjamin (west); Dan, Asher, Naphtali (north).
- **Wagons for Gershon and Merari but not Kohath** — the holiest items (ark, table, lampstand, altars) are carried by shoulder, not wagon. Later David's Uzzah-incident (2 Sam 6) violates this by putting the ark on a cart.
- **Voice from between the cherubim** (v89) — where Yahweh promised to meet Moses (Ex 25:22). The tabernacle is functional; revelation now arrives through the completed sanctuary.
