## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v1, 12, 13, 19, 22, 24) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"congregation" / "assembly"** (throughout) — two Hebrew words kept apart: עֵדָה "congregation" (v1, 2, 8, 11, 22, 27, 29) and קָהָל "assembly" (v4, 6, 10, 12). The verbs "assembled against" (v2, נִקְהַל עַל) and "assemble" (v8, 10, הִקְהִיל) are from the root of קָהָל; עַל in v2 may also be "to, around".
- **"abode" → "dwelt"** (v1, 15) — Hebrew יָשַׁב.
- **"chode" → "contended"** (v3, 13) — Hebrew רִיב, to contend, bring a case; the same verb as Exod 17:2. The name *Meribah* (מְרִיבָה "strife") is from this root, and the same name was given at Rephidim (Exod 17:7).
- **"and spake, saying" → "and they said, saying"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמְרוּ לֵאמֹר.
- **"Would God that we had died when our brethren died" → "Would that we had expired when our brothers expired"** (v3) — Hebrew וְלוּ גָוַעְנוּ בִּגְוַע אַחֵינוּ, literally "and would that we had expired in the expiring of our brothers". The Hebrew has no word "God". גָּוַע "expire" (also v29) is another word than מוּת "die" (v1, 4, 26, 28).
- **"that we and our cattle should die there" → "to die there, we and our animals"** (v4) — Hebrew לָמוּת שָׁם, an infinitive. בְּעִיר (v4, 8, 11) is beasts of burden; מִקְנֶה "livestock" (v19) is another word.
- **"made us to come up" → "brought us up"** (v5) — Hebrew הֶעֱלִיתֻנוּ, as Exod 17:3. The verbs in v4–5 are plural: Moses and Aaron are addressed.
- **"it is no place of seed, or of figs, or of vines, or of pomegranates" → "It is not a place of seed and fig and vine and pomegranate"** (v5) — Hebrew לֹא מְקוֹם זֶרַע וּתְאֵנָה וְגֶפֶן וְרִמּוֹן: four singulars joined by "and"; "It is" is supplied. זֶרַע is "seed" (sown grain).
- **"from the presence of the assembly" → "from the face of the assembly"** (v6) — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי. **"door of the tabernacle of the congregation" → "entrance of the tent of meeting"** — Hebrew פֶּתַח אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד.
- **"rod" → "staff"** (v8, 9, 11) — Hebrew מַטֶּה, as Exod 17:5. In v8, 9 it is "the staff", taken "from before Yahweh"; in v11 "his staff".
- **"rock" → "crag"** (v8, 10, 11) — Hebrew סֶלַע, a cliff or crag. In Exod 17:6 the word is צוּר "rock"; the two accounts use two different words.
- **"gather thou the assembly together" → "assemble the congregation"** (v8) — Hebrew וְהַקְהֵל אֶת־הָעֵדָה. The verbs change number: "take", "assemble" are singular; "you shall speak" (וְדִבַּרְתֶּם) is plural, Moses and Aaron; "you shall bring out" and "you shall give to drink" are singular again.
- **"and it shall give forth his water" → "and it shall give its water"** (v8) — Hebrew וְנָתַן מֵימָיו, a plain statement of what will follow, not a purpose clause.
- **"before the rock" → "before the face of the crag"** (v10) — Hebrew אֶל־פְּנֵי הַסָּלַע, literally "to the face of the crag".
- **"Hear now, ye rebels; must we fetch you water out of this rock?" → "Hear now, you rebels: from this crag shall we bring out water for you?"** (v10) — Hebrew שִׁמְעוּ־נָא הַמֹּרִים: נָא "now / please"; הַמֹּרִים is the participle of מָרָה "rebel, be defiant" (another verb than מָרַד "rebel", Num 14:9; English has one word), the verb used of Moses and Aaron themselves in v24. הֲמִן־הַסֶּלַע הַזֶּה נוֹצִיא לָכֶם מָיִם: "from this crag" stands first; the verb is a plain imperfect, "shall we / must we / can we bring out": the Hebrew does not say which. "He said" is singular; "we" is plural.
- **"lifted up his hand" → "raised his hand"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיָּרֶם, as Exod 17:11. **"smote" → "struck"** — Hebrew וַיַּךְ, the verb of Exod 17:6. **"the water came out abundantly" → "much water came out"** — Hebrew מַיִם רַבִּים, literally "many waters".
- **"Because ye believed me not, to sanctify me" → "Because you did not believe in me, to sanctify me"** (v12) — Hebrew לֹא־הֶאֱמַנְתֶּם בִּי "you did not believe in me" (the verb of Gen 15:6; Exod 14:31; also "trust"); לְהַקְדִּישֵׁנִי, the causative of "be holy": to sanctify me, treat me as holy, show me holy. The verse does not say in what act the unbelief lay. "You" is plural: Moses and Aaron.
- **"This is the water of Meribah; because the children of Israel strove with the LORD, and he was sanctified in them" → "These are the waters of Meribah, where the sons of Israel contended with Yahweh, and he was sanctified among them"** (v13) — Hebrew הֵמָּה מֵי מְרִיבָה "they are the waters of *Merivah* (strife)"; אֲשֶׁר is "where", "which" or "because". וַיִּקָּדֵשׁ בָּם is the passive / reflexive of "be holy": "he was sanctified" or "he showed himself holy"; בָּם is "among them", "in them" or "through them", and "them" may be the sons of Israel or the waters. The verb sounds like the name *Kadesh* (קָדֵשׁ, v1, 14).
- **"all the travail that hath befallen us" → "all the hardship that has found us"** (v14) — Hebrew הַתְּלָאָה "hardship" (literally "weariness"), as Exod 18:8; מְצָאָתְנוּ "has found us". "Messengers" is מַלְאָכִים, the plural of the word rendered "angel" in v16.
- **"a long time" → "many days"** (v15) — Hebrew יָמִים רַבִּים. **"the Egyptians vexed us" → "the Egyptians did evil to us"** — Hebrew וַיָּרֵעוּ לָנוּ מִצְרַיִם: the word is מִצְרַיִם "Egypt", here with a plural verb; הֵרַע לְ "do evil to" (Exod 5:22, 23).
- **"sent an angel" kept** (v16) — Hebrew מַלְאָךְ, "messenger"; rendered "angel" as in Exod 23:20; 33:2 of the one sent before Israel. The verse does not say who or what he was.
- **"in the uttermost of thy border" → "at the end of your border"** (v16) — Hebrew קְצֵה גְבוּלֶךָ. גְּבוּל (v16, 17, 21, 23) is the border and also the land within it.
- **"Let us pass, I pray thee, through thy country" → "Let us pass, please, through your land"** (v17) — Hebrew נַעְבְּרָה־נָּא בְאַרְצֶךָ. עָבַר (v17–21) is "pass over, pass through"; "until we have passed over your border" is נַעֲבֹר גְּבוּלֶךָ with a direct object.
- **"the water of the wells" → "the water of a well"** (v17) — Hebrew מֵי בְאֵר, singular.
- **"by the king's high way" → "The way of the king"** (v17) — Hebrew דֶּרֶךְ הַמֶּלֶךְ, "the way of the king": a royal road. "Highway" in v19 is another word, מְסִלָּה, a raised road.
- **"turn" kept** (v17, 21) — Hebrew נָטָה "turn"; in v21 וַיֵּט מֵעָלָיו, literally "turned from upon him".
- **"Thou shalt not pass by me, lest I come out against thee with the sword" → "You shall not pass through me, lest I come out with the sword to meet you"** (v18) — Hebrew לֹא תַעֲבֹר בִּי; לִקְרָאתֶךָ "to meet you", here and in v20 of a hostile meeting.
- **"then I will pay for it: I will only, without doing any thing else, go through on my feet" → "then I will give its price. Only — it is nothing — on my feet let me pass through"** (v19) — Hebrew וְנָתַתִּי מִכְרָם "I will give their price" (of the waters); רַק אֵין־דָּבָר בְּרַגְלַי אֶעֱבֹרָה, word for word "only — there is not a thing — on my feet let me pass through". Israel speaks as one man: "I and my livestock".
- **"with much people" → "with a heavy people"** (v20) — Hebrew בְּעַם כָּבֵד, literally "heavy": a numerous, weighty host.
- **"refused to give Israel passage" → "refused to allow Israel to pass"** (v21) — Hebrew וַיְמָאֵן … נְתֹן אֶת־יִשְׂרָאֵל עֲבֹר, literally "refused to give Israel to pass".
- **"journeyed" → "set out"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיִּסְעוּ. The subject "the sons of Israel, the whole congregation" stands with the second verb, "came".
- **"mount Hor" kept** (v22, 23, 25, 27) — Hebrew הֹר הָהָר *Hor ha-har*, literally "Hor the mountain".
- **"spake" → "said"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר.
- **"gathered unto his people" → "gathered to his peoples"** (v24) — Hebrew אֶל־עַמָּיו, plural: his kinsfolk (Gen 25:8). **"enter" → "come into"** — Hebrew יָבֹא אֶל.
- **"because ye rebelled against my word" → "because you rebelled against my mouth"** (v24) — Hebrew מְרִיתֶם אֶת־פִּי: "my mouth" is what he said, his command (Exod 17:1 "at the mouth of Yahweh"). "You" is plural. The verb מָרָה is the one Moses used of the people in v10.
- **"bring them up unto mount Hor" → "bring them up Mount Hor"** (v25) — Hebrew הֹר הָהָר without a preposition.
- **"put them upon Eleazar" → "clothe Eleazar … with them"** (v26, 28) — Hebrew הִלְבִּישׁ "clothe" with two objects.
- **"Aaron shall be gathered unto his people" → "Aaron shall be gathered"** (v26) — Hebrew וְאַהֲרֹן יֵאָסֵף: the verb stands alone here; "unto his people" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"in the sight of" → "before the eyes of"** (v27) — Hebrew לְעֵינֵי, as v8, 12.
- **"when all the congregation saw that Aaron was dead, they mourned for Aaron" → "all the congregation saw that Aaron had expired, and they wept for Aaron"** (v29) — Hebrew וַיִּרְאוּ … וַיִּבְכּוּ, two main verbs; גָּוַע "expire" (v3); בָּכָה "weep". "All the house of Israel" stands at the end of the verse in the Hebrew.

### Notes
- **Fortieth-year chapter** — multiple deaths frame the transition: Miriam (v1), Aaron (v28). The year of "the first month" (v1) is not named in the verse; Num 33:38 puts Aaron's death in the fortieth year.
- **The episode at the crag** — Moses is commanded to *speak* to the crag (v8), and *strikes* it twice (v11). The verse (v12) names the fault only as not believing in Yahweh, to sanctify him before the eyes of the sons of Israel, and v24 as rebelling against his mouth; in what act it lay is debated: striking rather than speaking; striking twice; calling the people "rebels"; "shall we bring out water?". Consequence: Moses and Aaron do not bring the assembly into the land.
- **Compare Exod 17:1–7** — the earlier water from the rock at Rephidim. There Moses was commanded to *strike* the rock (צוּר); here, to *speak* to the crag (סֶלַע). Both places receive the name Meribah.
- **Edom refuses passage** (v14–21) — Israel turns away from Edom, lengthening the journey. Later prophets (Obadiah) revisit Edom's conduct toward his brother.
- **Aaron's transfer of garments** (v26, 28) — Eleazar succeeds as high priest. Continuity of office maintained despite Aaron's death. First priestly succession.
- **Thirty days of weeping** (v29) — the same duration later for Moses (Deut 34:8).
- **1 Cor 10:4** — "they drank from the spiritual rock that followed them, and that rock was Christ" — a NT typological reading of the rock episodes.
