## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"chode" → "quarreled"** (v3) — archaic past of *chide*. Hebrew *riv*.
- **"sent an angel" → "sent a messenger"** (v16) — Hebrew מַלְאָךְ *mal'akh* = messenger / angel. Contextually: the pillar / angel of Yahweh who led them from Egypt (Ex 14:19, 23:20).
- **"King's high way" → "King's Highway"** (v17) — well-known ancient trade route through Transjordan.
- **"Meribah" (v13)** — Hebrew מְרִיבָה *Meribah* from רִיב *riv* = dispute / quarrel. Etymology added.
- **"uttermost of thy border" → "edge of your border"** (v16) — modernized.
- **"Mount Hor" (v22, 23, 25, 27)** — Hebrew הֹר הָהָר *Hor ha-Har*. Traditional location disputed; many place it in northern Edom.

### Notes
- **Fortieth-year chapter** — multiple deaths frame the transition: Miriam (v1), Aaron (v28). Compresses the otherwise silent 38-year wilderness interval.
- **The rock episode** — Moses commanded to *speak* to the rock (v8), but *strikes* it twice (v11). Precise sin debated: striking rather than speaking; striking twice; calling the people "rebels"; "must we bring water?" (taking credit). Yahweh reads it as not treating him as holy (v12). Consequence: Moses and Aaron barred from the land.
- **Compare Ex 17** — earlier water-from-rock (Rephidim). There Moses was commanded to *strike*. Here, to *speak*. Moses repeats an authorized gesture that is no longer authorized.
- **Edom refuses passage** (v14–21) — Israel detours around Edom, lengthening the journey. Later prophets (Obadiah) revisit Edom's inhospitality.
- **Aaron's transfer of garments** (v26, 28) — Eleazar succeeds as high priest. Continuity of office maintained despite Aaron's death. First priestly succession.
- **Thirty-day mourning** (v29) — same duration later for Moses (Deut 34:8). Standard mourning for national leaders.
- **1 Cor 10:4** — "they drank from the spiritual rock that followed them, and that rock was Christ" — NT typological reading of both rock-episodes.
