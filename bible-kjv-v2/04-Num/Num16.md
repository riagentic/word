# Numbers 16

[1] Now Korah son of Izhar son of Kohath son of Levi, with Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab, and On son of Peleth — descendants of Reuben — took men,

[2] and they rose up before Moses, along with some of the children of Israel — two hundred and fifty chiefs of the congregation, called to the assembly, men of renown.

[3] They assembled against Moses and Aaron and said to them, "You have gone too far! All the congregation are holy, every one of them, and Yahweh is among them; why then do you exalt yourselves above the assembly of Yahweh?"

[4] When Moses heard it, he fell on his face.

[5] He spoke to Korah and all his company, saying, "Tomorrow morning Yahweh will show who is his and who is holy, and will bring him near to himself; the one he chooses he will bring near to himself.

[6] Do this: take censers — Korah and all his company —

[7] and put fire in them and place incense in them before Yahweh tomorrow; the man whom Yahweh chooses shall be the holy one. You sons of Levi have gone too far!"

[8] Moses said to Korah, "Hear now, sons of Levi!

[9] Is it not enough for you that the *Elohim* of Israel has separated you from the congregation of Israel, bringing you near to himself to do the service of the Dwelling of Yahweh, and to stand before the congregation to minister to them?

[10] He has brought you near to himself, you and all your brothers the sons of Levi with you; and do you seek the priesthood also?

[11] Therefore it is against Yahweh that you and all your company have gathered together. And what is Aaron, that you grumble against him?"

[12] Moses sent to summon Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab; but they said, "We will not come up.

[13] Is it a small thing that you have brought us up out of a land flowing with milk and honey to kill us in the wilderness, but you must also make yourself a ruler over us?

[14] Moreover, you have not brought us into a land flowing with milk and honey or given us an inheritance of fields and vineyards. Will you gouge out the eyes of these men? We will not come up."

[15] Moses was very angry, and said to Yahweh, "Do not regard their offering. I have not taken one donkey from them, nor have I wronged one of them."

[16] Moses said to Korah, "You and all your company be before Yahweh — you, and they, and Aaron — tomorrow.

[17] Let each man take his censer and put incense on it, and each of you bring his censer before Yahweh — two hundred and fifty censers; you also, and Aaron, each his censer."

[18] Each man took his censer, put fire in them, laid incense on them, and stood at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting with Moses and Aaron.

[19] Korah assembled all the congregation against them at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting; and the glory of Yahweh appeared to all the congregation.

[20] Yahweh spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,

[21] "Separate yourselves from among this congregation, that I may consume them in a moment."

[22] But they fell on their faces and said, "O *Elohim*, *Elohim* of the spirits of all flesh, shall one man sin and you be angry with the whole congregation?"

[23] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[24] "Speak to the congregation, saying, 'Get away from around the dwelling of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram.'"

[25] Moses rose and went to Dathan and Abiram, and the elders of Israel followed him.

[26] He spoke to the congregation, saying, "Turn away, please, from the tents of these wicked men, and touch nothing of theirs, lest you be swept away in all their sins."

[27] So they moved away from around the dwelling of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram. And Dathan and Abiram came out and stood at the entrance of their tents, with their wives, their sons, and their little ones.

[28] Moses said, "By this you shall know that Yahweh has sent me to do all these works, and that it is not of my own doing:

[29] if these men die the common death of all men, or if what happens to all people happens to them, then Yahweh has not sent me.

[30] But if Yahweh creates something new, and the ground opens its mouth and swallows them up with all that belongs to them, and they go down alive into Sheol, then you shall know that these men have spurned Yahweh."

[31] As he finished speaking all these words, the ground under them split apart;

[32] the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them up with their households and all the people who belonged to Korah and all their possessions.

[33] They and all that belonged to them went down alive into Sheol; the earth closed over them, and they perished from the midst of the assembly.

[34] All Israel who were around them fled at their cry, for they said, "Lest the earth swallow us up too!"

[35] Fire came out from Yahweh and consumed the two hundred and fifty men who offered incense.

[36] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[37] "Say to Eleazar son of Aaron the priest that he should take up the censers out of the burning and scatter the fire some distance away — for they are holy;

[38] the censers of these sinners at the cost of their lives — make of them hammered plates as a covering for the altar, since they offered them before Yahweh and they have become holy; and they shall be a sign to the children of Israel."

[39] So Eleazar the priest took the bronze censers that had been presented by those who were burned, and they were hammered out as a covering for the altar —

[40] a memorial to the children of Israel, that no outsider who is not of the descendants of Aaron should come near to offer incense before Yahweh, lest he become like Korah and his company — as Yahweh said to him by the hand of Moses.

[41] But on the next day all the congregation of the children of Israel grumbled against Moses and Aaron, saying, "You have killed the people of Yahweh!"

[42] And when the congregation had gathered against Moses and Aaron, they turned toward the Tent of Meeting, and behold, the cloud had covered it, and the glory of Yahweh appeared.

[43] Moses and Aaron came before the Tent of Meeting.

[44] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[45] "Get away from this congregation, that I may consume them in a moment." And they fell on their faces.

[46] Moses said to Aaron, "Take the censer and put fire on it from the altar, and lay incense on it, and carry it quickly to the congregation and make atonement for them; for wrath has gone out from Yahweh — the plague has begun."

[47] So Aaron took the censer, as Moses had spoken, and ran into the midst of the assembly; and behold, the plague had begun among the people. He put on the incense and made atonement for the people.

[48] He stood between the dead and the living, and the plague was halted.

[49] Now those who died in the plague were 14,700, besides those who died in the matter of Korah.

[50] Aaron returned to Moses at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, when the plague had been halted.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"princes" → "chiefs"** (v2) — Hebrew *nesi'im*.
- **"famous / men of renown" → "called to the assembly / men of renown"** (v2) — Hebrew קְרִאֵי מוֹעֵד *qeri'ei mo'ed* = called to the appointed assembly; members of the ruling council.
- **"take too much upon you" → "gone too far"** (v3, 7) — Hebrew רַב־לָכֶם *rav-lakhem*. "Enough from you" / "too much for you."
- **"put out the eyes" → "gouge out the eyes"** (v14) — Hebrew עֵינֵי הָאֲנָשִׁים הָהֵם תְּנַקֵּר *einei ha-anashim ha-hem tenaqer*. Metaphor for deception / blinding.
- **"quick / pit" → "alive / Sheol"** (v30, 33) — archaic *quick* = living; Hebrew שְׁאוֹל *Sheol* = the grave / underworld realm of the dead. Kept transliterated.
- **"appertained" → "belonged to"** (v32, 33) — modernized.
- **"brasen" → "bronze"** (v39) — modernized.
- **"stranger" → "outsider"** (v40) — Hebrew *zar* = non-priest.

### Notes
- **Three-fold rebellion** — (1) Korah the Levite disputes priestly exclusivity; (2) Dathan and Abiram (Reuben) dispute Moses' political leadership; (3) the 250 chiefs seek priestly access. Three fronts, one uprising.
- **Fate proportional to role** — Dathan, Abiram, and Korah's people are swallowed by the earth (ground-level rebellion against land-based leadership). The 250 incense-offerers consumed by fire (cultic sin = cultic punishment). Later (ch 26:11) Korah's sons survive and become temple musicians (Ps 42–49, 84, 85, 87, 88).
- **"*Elohim* of the spirits of all flesh"** (v22) — rare phrase. Numbers 27:16 repeats. Signals Yahweh's universal jurisdiction beyond Israel.
- **Censer-plates for the altar** (v37–40) — visible memorial. The instruments of rebellion become a warning-covering on the altar itself.
- **Aaron standing between the dead and the living** (v48) — iconic image of priestly intercession. Aaron's incense — the same act the 250 were punished for — atones because he is authorized.
- **Death toll** — 14,700 by plague + 253 (Dathan, Abiram, and 250 + Korah, with associated families).
- **Jude 1:11** — NT warning uses "the rebellion of Korah" as a type of destructive religious self-aggrandizement.
