## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v13) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew וַ / וְ "and" at the head of a clause is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"abode in Shittim" → "dwelt in Shittim"** (v1) — Hebrew יָשַׁב "dwell". The name has the article (הַשִּׁטִּים, "the acacias").
- **"began to commit whoredom with" → "began to play the prostitute with"** (v1) — Hebrew זָנָה, the verb of זוֹנָה "prostitute", used of sexual unfaithfulness and of unfaithfulness to Yahweh alike. The preposition is אֶל, literally "toward". וַיָּחֶל is taken as "began" (from חלל hifil); the same letters could be read "profaned itself".
- **"they called the people unto the sacrifices of their gods"** kept (v2) — Hebrew וַתִּקְרֶאןָ, a feminine plural: the daughters of Moab called (invited). אֱלֹהֵיהֶן, with a feminine suffix, is "their gods" or "their god" (the Baal of v3): the Hebrew form is the same.
- **"joined himself unto" → "yoked himself to"** (v3, 5) — Hebrew נִצְמַד, from the root of צֶמֶד "a yoke, a pair": to be coupled to. Israel is spoken of as one man ("himself").
- **"Baal-peor"** (v3, 5) — Hebrew בַּעַל פְּעוֹר, "the Baal (lord) of Peor": the god worshiped at Peor (23:28).
- **"the anger of the LORD was kindled" → "the anger of Yahweh burned"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר־אַף; אַף is literally "nose". **"the fierce anger" → "the burning anger"** (v4) — Hebrew חֲרוֹן אַף, literally "the burning of the nose".
- **"hang them up before the LORD against the sun" → "expose them to Yahweh before the sun"** (v4) — Hebrew הוֹקַע, found only here and in 2 Sam 21:6, 9, 13. The meaning is not certain: a form of execution or of public display of the body (the root is used of a joint put out of place, Gen 32:25); "hang" and "impale" are other renderings. לַיהוָה is "to / for Yahweh". "Them" may be the heads of the people or, through them, the guilty: the Hebrew does not say. **"that the fierce anger … may be turned away" → "and let the burning anger … turn back"** — Hebrew וְיָשֹׁב, "and let it turn back" (שׁוּב).
- **"Slay ye every one his men" → "Kill, each man his men"** (v5) — Hebrew הִרְגוּ אִישׁ אֲנָשָׁיו.
- **"one of the children of Israel came and brought unto his brethren a Midianitish woman in the sight of" → "a man of the sons of Israel came and brought near to his brothers the Midianite woman, before the eyes of"** (v6) — Hebrew אִישׁ; וַיַּקְרֵב, "brought near"; הַמִּדְיָנִית has the article; לְעֵינֵי. **"who were weeping before the door of the tabernacle of the congregation" → "and they were weeping at the entrance of the tent of meeting"** — Hebrew וְהֵמָּה בֹכִים, a clause joined by "and"; פֶּתַח אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד.
- **"when Phinehas … saw it" → "Phinehas … saw"** (v7) — the Hebrew has no object. **"a javelin" → "a spear"** — Hebrew רֹמַח.
- **"into the tent" → "into the vaulted tent"** (v8) — Hebrew הַקֻּבָּה, a word found only here. The meaning is not certain: a vaulted or domed tent, or an inner alcove of a tent (the Arabic word of the same form, from which "alcove" comes, is cited from memory). **"thrust both of them through … the woman through her belly" → "pierced the two of them … the woman, into her stomach"** — Hebrew דָּקַר "pierce"; אֶל־קֳבָתָהּ: קֵבָה is the stomach (Deut 18:3), not the word "belly" (בֶּטֶן); it sounds like *qubbah* just before. Some take it here of the womb or the private parts. **"the plague was stayed from" → "the plague was restrained from upon"** — Hebrew וַתֵּעָצַר … מֵעַל. No plague has been named before this verse.
- **"twenty and four thousand" → "twenty-four thousand"** (v9) — numbers in words, in ordinary English order.
- **"hath turned my wrath away from … while he was zealous for my sake among them, that I consumed not" → "has turned back my fury from upon … in his being jealous with my jealousy in their midst, and I did not make an end of"** (v11) — Hebrew חֵמָה "fury" (literally "heat"); בְּקַנְאוֹ אֶת־קִנְאָתִי, verb and noun of one root: "in his being jealous with my jealousy". קִנֵּא / קִנְאָה is one word for "jealousy" and "zeal" (Exod 20:5; 34:14 "a jealous El"); it is kept as "jealous / jealousy" all four times in v11, 13. וְלֹא־כִלִּיתִי is joined by "and"; כִּלָּה is "bring to an end".
- **"my covenant of peace" → "my covenant of shalom"** (v12) — Hebrew בְּרִיתִי שָׁלוֹם, literally "my covenant, shalom": the suffix "my" stands on "covenant" although another noun follows (as "my covenant with Jacob", Lev 26:42). שָׁלוֹם is wholeness, well-being, peace.
- **"his seed after him, even the covenant of an everlasting priesthood" → "his seed after him a covenant of an everlasting priesthood"** (v13) — Hebrew זֶרַע "seed"; בְּרִית כְּהֻנַּת עוֹלָם, literally "a covenant of a priesthood of the age". **"because he was zealous for his God" → "because he was jealous for his Elohim"** — Hebrew קִנֵּא לְ, the verb of v11; with לְ it is "be jealous / zealous for". תַּחַת אֲשֶׁר is literally "in the place of (the fact) that".
- **"that was slain, even that was slain with the Midianitish woman" → "who was struck down, who was struck down with the Midianite woman"** (v14, 15, 18) — Hebrew הַמֻּכֶּה אֲשֶׁר הֻכָּה, the passive of הִכָּה "strike", twice; so also "strike them down" in v17. **"a prince of a chief house among the Simeonites" → "a chieftain of a father's house of the Simeonites"** — Hebrew נְשִׂיא בֵית־אָב לַשִּׁמְעֹנִי.
- **"he was head over a people, and of a chief house in Midian" → "he was head of the tribes of a father's house in Midian"** (v15) — Hebrew רֹאשׁ אֻמּוֹת בֵּית־אָב. אֻמָּה (Gen 25:16) is a people from one stock; it is not the usual word for "tribe" (מַטֶּה, שֵׁבֶט). How "tribes" and "a father's house" stand together is not clear; the words are kept as they are.
- **"Vex the Midianites, and smite them: for they vex you with their wiles, wherewith they have beguiled you" → "Harass the Midianites, and you shall strike them down; for they are harassing you with their plots that they plotted against you"** (v17, 18) — Hebrew צָרוֹר, an infinitive used as a command, and צֹרְרִים, a participle, of צָרַר "treat as an adversary, press hard" (the root of צַר "adversary"; "harass" as 10:9; 33:55). נִכְלֵיהֶם אֲשֶׁר־נִכְּלוּ: noun and verb of one root, "be crafty, plot" (the verb of Gen 37:18); לָכֶם is literally "to you".
- **"in the matter of Peor … for Peor's sake" → "in the matter of Peor … in the matter of Peor"** (v18) — Hebrew עַל־דְּבַר three times in the verse; kept the same. **"a prince of Midian" → "a chieftain of Midian"** — Hebrew נְשִׂיא.

### Notes
- **Shittim** — the last camp east of the Jordan (33:49; Josh 2:1). Balak could not have Israel cursed (ch 22–24); 31:16 names Balaam as the one by whose word the women of Midian drew Israel to Peor (so Rev 2:14).
- **Moab and Midian** — the women are "daughters of Moab" in v1 and the woman is a Midianite in v6–18; the command of v17 names Midian only (carried out in ch 31).
- **Phinehas** — Ps 106:30–31 recalls the deed: "it was reckoned to him as righteousness". Later readers have judged it differently; the text here gives the word of Yahweh in v11–13.
- **Covenant of everlasting priesthood** (v13) — 1 Chr 6:4–15 traces the later priestly line from Phinehas.
- **Plague figure** (v9) — twenty-four thousand. 1 Cor 10:8 speaks of twenty-three thousand falling "in one day".
- **Hinge chapter** — the last deaths of the wilderness generation. Ch 26 numbers the generation that will enter the land.
