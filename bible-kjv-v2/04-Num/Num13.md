# Numbers 13

[1] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[2] "Send men to scout out the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the children of Israel; from each ancestral tribe you shall send one man, each a chief among them."

[3] So Moses sent them from the wilderness of Paran at the command of Yahweh; all of them were men who were heads of the children of Israel.

[4] These were their names: from the tribe of Reuben, Shammua son of Zaccur;

[5] from the tribe of Simeon, Shaphat son of Hori;

[6] from the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh;

[7] from the tribe of Issachar, Igal son of Joseph;

[8] from the tribe of Ephraim, Hoshea son of Nun;

[9] from the tribe of Benjamin, Palti son of Raphu;

[10] from the tribe of Zebulun, Gaddiel son of Sodi;

[11] from the tribe of Joseph, namely from the tribe of Manasseh, Gaddi son of Susi;

[12] from the tribe of Dan, Ammiel son of Gemalli;

[13] from the tribe of Asher, Sethur son of Michael;

[14] from the tribe of Naphtali, Nahbi son of Vophsi;

[15] from the tribe of Gad, Geuel son of Machi.

[16] These are the names of the men Moses sent to scout out the land. And Moses called Hoshea son of Nun Joshua.

[17] Moses sent them to scout out the land of Canaan and said to them, "Go up this way by the Negev, and ascend into the hill country,

[18] and see what the land is like; and the people who dwell in it, whether they are strong or weak, few or many;

[19] and what the land is in which they dwell, whether good or bad; and what the cities are in which they dwell, whether in camps or in fortresses;

[20] and what the land is, whether fat or lean, whether there are trees in it or not. Be of good courage, and bring some of the fruit of the land." Now the time was the season of the first ripe grapes.

[21] So they went up and scouted out the land from the wilderness of Zin as far as Rehob, by Lebo-hamath.

[22] They went up by the Negev and came to Hebron; Ahiman, Sheshai, and Talmai, descendants of Anak, were there. (Hebron was built seven years before Zoan in Egypt.)

[23] They came to the wadi of Eshkol, and from there cut down a branch with a single cluster of grapes, and carried it on a pole between two of them; and they brought also some pomegranates and figs.

[24] That place was called the wadi of Eshkol because of the cluster of grapes that the children of Israel cut down there.

[25] They returned from scouting out the land at the end of forty days.

[26] They went and came to Moses and Aaron and to all the congregation of the children of Israel in the wilderness of Paran, at Kadesh; they brought back word to them and to all the congregation, and showed them the fruit of the land.

[27] They told him and said, "We came to the land to which you sent us; it does indeed flow with milk and honey, and this is its fruit.

[28] However, the people who dwell in the land are strong, and the cities are fortified and very large; and moreover, we saw the descendants of Anak there.

[29] Amalek dwells in the land of the Negev; the Hittites, Jebusites, and Amorites dwell in the hill country; and the Canaanites dwell by the sea and along the Jordan."

[30] Then Caleb quieted the people before Moses and said, "Let us go up at once and take possession of it, for we are well able to overcome it."

[31] But the men who had gone up with him said, "We are not able to go up against the people, for they are stronger than we are."

[32] They brought an evil report of the land they had scouted to the children of Israel, saying, "The land through which we went to scout it out is a land that devours its inhabitants; and all the people we saw in it are men of great stature.

[33] There we saw the Nephilim — the sons of Anak, who come from the Nephilim — and in our own eyes we were like grasshoppers, and so we were in their eyes."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"southward / south / land of the south" → "Negev"** (v17, 22, 29) — Hebrew נֶגֶב *Negev*. The southern semi-desert region; proper name, not mere "south."
- **"strong holds" → "fortresses"** (v19) — modernized.
- **"spy out" → "scout out"** (v2, 16, 17, 21, 25) — Hebrew לָתוּר *latur* = to explore / reconnoiter; neutral, not necessarily clandestine.

### Notes
- **Twelve scouts** — one per tribe (Levi excluded). Notable figures: Caleb (Judah), Hoshea/Joshua (Ephraim). The other ten become spokesmen for the rebellion.
- **Forty days** (v25) — mirrors Moses' forty days on Sinai (Ex 24:18, 34:28); Jesus' forty days (Mt 4:2); prototypes of revelation / testing-in-solitude.
- **Milk and honey confirmed** (v27) — the land is all Yahweh promised, and more. The fruit is real. The report is factually mixed, then catastrophized.
- **Anak / Nephilim** — gigantic / larger-than-life peoples, linked back to Gen 6:4's antediluvian giants. Josh 11:21–22 tells of Joshua and Caleb later destroying them. Interpretations vary: actual hybrid tradition, cultural memory of Aegean or Philistine peoples, or rhetorical hyperbole.
- **"Grasshoppers"** (v33) — the psychological crisis. The physical facts (fortified cities, tall people) become an interpretive spiral — seeing themselves as grasshoppers, they see the enemy as giants.
- **Two reports, one land** — Caleb (and later Joshua, ch 14) believe Yahweh's promise is stronger than enemies. The ten see enemies as stronger than Yahweh's promise. This chapter is the hinge of the wilderness generation's fate.
