## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — every stage begins with וַ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"journeys" kept; "with their armies under the hand of" → "by their hosts, by the hand of"** (v1) — Hebrew מַסְעֵי, from נָסַע "pull up (the tent pegs), set out": the stretches travelled from camp to camp. צְבָאוֹת "hosts" (Exod 12:51); בְּיַד "by the hand of".
- **"their goings out according to their journeys by the commandment of the LORD" → "their goings out by their journeys, at the mouth of Yahweh"** (v2) — Hebrew מוֹצָאֵיהֶם, from יָצָא "go out": the places they went out from. The two nouns change places in the second half of the verse. עַל־פִּי יְהוָה is literally "at the mouth of Yahweh" (also v38); it can go with "wrote" or with "journeys" (9:18).
- **"departed … pitched" → "set out … camped"** (v3–48) — Hebrew וַיִּסְעוּ מִ… וַיַּחֲנוּ בְּ…, the same two verbs in every stage, rendered the same each time: נָסַע "set out" and חָנָה "camp" (the verb of מַחֲנֶה "camp").
- **"on the morrow after the passover" → "on the day after the Pesach"** (v3) — Hebrew מִמָּחֳרַת הַפֶּסַח. **"with an high hand" → "with a high hand"** — Hebrew בְּיָד רָמָה *be-yad ramah*, a raised hand (Exod 14:8); the same phrase is used of defiant sin in 15:30. **"in the sight of all the Egyptians" → "before the eyes of all Egypt"** — Hebrew לְעֵינֵי כָּל־מִצְרָיִם; the word is the name "Egypt".
- **"For the Egyptians buried all their firstborn, which the LORD had smitten among them" → "and the Egyptians were burying those whom Yahweh had struck down among them, every firstborn"** (v4) — Hebrew וּמִצְרַיִם מְקַבְּרִים, the same word "Egypt" with a plural participle, hence "the Egyptians"; the object is "those whom Yahweh struck among them", and "every firstborn" stands after it. **"executed judgments" → "had done judgments"** — Hebrew עָשָׂה שְׁפָטִים (Exod 12:12).
- **"in the edge of the wilderness" → "at the edge of the wilderness"** (v6); **"in the edge of the land of Edom" → "at the edge of the land of Edom"** (v37) — Hebrew בִּקְצֵה, "at the end of" (as Exod 13:20).
- **"turned again unto Pihahiroth, which is before Baalzephon" → "turned back to Pi-hahiroth, which is facing Baal-zephon"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיָּשָׁב, a singular ("and he / it turned back": the people as one), between two plural verbs; עַל־פִּי הַחִירֹת is "upon / at Pi-hahiroth", a name that sounds like "the mouth of Hahiroth"; עַל־פְּנֵי is literally "on the face of".
- **"from before Pihahiroth" → "from before Hahiroth"** (v8) — the Masoretic Text has מִפְּנֵי הַחִירֹת "from before Hahiroth"; "from Pi-hahiroth" (מִפִּי) is commonly read with other witnesses (from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept. **"passed through the midst of the sea into the wilderness" → "passed over in the midst of the sea to the wilderness"** — Hebrew עָבַר "pass over", בְּתוֹךְ "in the midst of". **"three days' journey" → "a way of three days"** — Hebrew דֶּרֶךְ שְׁלֹשֶׁת יָמִים; "journey" in this chapter is מַסָּע.
- **"fountains of water" → "springs of water"** (v9) — Hebrew עֵינֹת מַיִם *einot mayim*.
- **"Red sea" → "Sea of Reeds"** (v10, 11) — Hebrew יַם־סוּף, as elsewhere.
- **"where was no water" → "and there was no water there"** (v14) — Hebrew וְלֹא־הָיָה שָׁם מַיִם, a clause with "and".
- **Place names** (v3–49) — every name was checked against the Hebrew and keeps its form. "Mount Shepher" (v23) is הַר־שָׁפֶר; "Mount Hor" (v37–41) is הֹר הָהָר, "Hor the mountain"; "Bene-jaakan" (v31) is "the sons of Jaakan"; "Iye-abarim" (v44) is עִיֵּי הָעֲבָרִים "the ruin heaps of the Abarim", shortened to "Iyim" in v45; "the mountains of Abarim" (v47) has the article, "the Abarim" (the regions beyond); "Almon-diblathaim" (v46) is written with the ending of direction, דִּבְלָתָיְמָה; so also Kehelathah (v22), Jotbathah (v33).
- **"which is Kadesh" → "it is Kadesh"** (v36) — Hebrew הִוא קָדֵשׁ.
- **"at the commandment of the LORD" → "at the mouth of Yahweh"** (v38) — see v2. **"in the fortieth year after the children of Israel were come out" → "in the fortieth year of the going out of the sons of Israel"** — Hebrew לְצֵאת, an infinitive. **"the first day of the fifth month" → "in the fifth month, on the first of the month"** — the Hebrew order; "day" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"an hundred and twenty and three years old"** (v39) — literally "a son of three and twenty and a hundred years".
- **"And king Arad the Canaanite, which dwelt in the south" → "And the Canaanite, the king of Arad, heard — and he was dwelling in the Negev"** (v40) — Hebrew הַכְּנַעֲנִי מֶלֶךְ עֲרָד; the clause "and he was dwelling …" stands after the verb. בְּבֹא is "at the coming of", "when the sons of Israel came"; what he heard is not said. הַנֶּגֶב is the dry south country, kept as a name. What followed is told in 21:1–3; the verse breaks off.
- **"in the border of Moab"** (v44) — Hebrew בִּגְבוּל: the border or the land within it.
- **"in the plains of Moab by Jordan near Jericho" → "in the plains of Moab by the Jordan of Jericho"** (v48, 50) — Hebrew עַל יַרְדֵּן יְרֵחוֹ, "by the Jordan of Jericho": the stretch of the river at Jericho. עַרְבֹת is the plural of עֲרָבָה, dry steppe land; the usual word for "plain" is another.
- **"When ye are passed over Jordan" → "When you are passing over the Jordan"** (v51) — Hebrew כִּי אַתֶּם עֹבְרִים, a participle.
- **"drive out" → "dispossess"** (v52, 55) — Hebrew הוֹרִישׁ, the causative of יָרַשׁ "possess": put out of possession. **"take possession of the land"** (v53) is the same form with the land as its object, and "to possess it" is the simple stem.
- **"destroy all their pictures, and destroy all their molten images, and quite pluck down all their high places" → "do away with all their figures, and all their molten images you shall do away with, and all their high places you shall destroy"** (v52) — two verbs: אִבֵּד (twice), literally "make perish", and הִשְׁמִיד "destroy" (Lev 26:30). מַשְׂכִּית is a thing with a figure worked on it (Lev 26:1 "a figured stone"; Ezek 8:12; Prov 25:11); what kind of object is meant here is not certain, and "stones" is not in this verse. צַלְמֵי מַסֵּכֹתָם is literally "the images of their molten things": images cast in metal. בָּמוֹת "high places" are places of worship; the word says "heights", and what such a place looked like is not certain.
- **"I have given you the land" → "to you I have given the land"** (v53) — the Hebrew order.
- **"ye shall divide the land by lot for an inheritance among your families" → "you shall inherit the land by lot according to your clans"** (v54) — Hebrew וְהִתְנַחַלְתֶּם, the reflexive stem of נָחַל "inherit": take as your inheritance (so again at the end of the verse). מִשְׁפָּחָה is "clan". **"to the more ye shall give the more inheritance, and to the fewer ye shall give the less inheritance" → "for the many you shall make his inheritance large, and for the few you shall make his inheritance small"** — Hebrew לָרַב תַּרְבּוּ … וְלַמְעַט תַּמְעִיט, the adjective and its own verb each time ("for the many you shall make many … for the few you shall make few"); "his" is singular. **"in the place where his lot falleth" → "to where the lot comes out for him, there it shall be his"** — Hebrew יֵצֵא "comes out". **"tribes" kept** — Hebrew מַטּוֹת, literally "staffs" (see 32:28).
- **"pricks in your eyes, and thorns in your sides" → "barbs in your eyes and thorns in your sides"** (v55) — Hebrew שִׂכִּים *sikkim* (only here) and צְנִינִם *tzeninim* (Josh 23:13): pointed things; the exact sense of both is uncertain. **"those which ye let remain" → "those of them whom you leave over"** — Hebrew הוֹתִיר. **"shall vex you" → "shall harass you"** — Hebrew צָרַר, the verb of צַר "adversary": treat as an enemy, press hard.
- **"as I thought to do" → "as I intended to do"** (v56) — Hebrew דִּמִּיתִי, "I imagined, I had in mind".

### Notes
- **Wilderness itinerary** — 42 stages (or 41 movements from 42 locations). The text compresses a 40-year journey into a brief catalog. Genuine geographical-memory archive, though many locations are no longer identifiable.
- **"Moses wrote"** (v2) — explicit claim of Mosaic authorship for the itinerary at least. Narrative frame.
- **Correspondence to earlier narratives** — stations 1–14 correspond to Ex 12–17 (Rameses to Rephidim); 15 (Sinai) = Ex 19; then 18 stations in the silent 38-year gap before returning to Kadesh (v36). These hidden station-names preserve otherwise-lost chapters of the wilderness life.
- **Aaron's death date precise** (v38) — first day of fifth month, year 40. The only precise death-date in the Pentateuch for a major figure.
- **Command to dispossess** (v52–56) — dispossession + cult-smashing. The threat in v56: failure to carry it out means their own dispossession — later fulfilled in the Assyrian and Babylonian exiles.
- **Structure of Numbers** ends approaching this point: the wilderness is behind, the land is ahead. The old generation's journey is finished; the new generation stands on the threshold.
