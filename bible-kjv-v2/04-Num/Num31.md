## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"avenge" → "execute vengeance"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew נָקַם *naqam*. Judicial vengeance.
- **"goodly castles" → "encampments"** (v10) — Hebrew טִירָה *tirah*. Encampment / nomadic enclosure, not fortified castle.
- **"trespass" → "treachery"** (v16) — Hebrew *ma'al*.
- **"water of separation" → "water of impurity-cleansing"** (v23) — as in ch 19.
- **"beeves" → "cattle"** — archaic.
- **"asses" → "donkeys"** — modernized.
- **"brass" → "bronze"** (v22) — modernized.
- **"chains, and bracelets, rings, earrings, and tablets" → "armlets, bracelets, signet rings, earrings, and pendants"** (v50) — Hebrew אֶצְעָדָה *etza'dah* / צָמִיד *tzamid* / טַבַּעַת *tabba'at* / עָגִיל *agil* / כּוּמָז *kumaz*. Various items of jewelry; translations vary.
- **Traditional number-forms modernized** throughout.

### Notes
- **Harsh war narrative** — the *cherem* ("ban") against Midian, punishment for the Peor seduction (ch 25). One of the OT's most disturbing texts: non-combatant male children and married women executed; virgin girls spared as captives.
- **Interpretation disputed** — traditional Jewish and Christian readings treat it as historically specific divine judgment, not paradigmatic. Modern ethical readings wrestle with the text: the genre is partly ancient-Near-Eastern war rhetoric, partly literal narrative, unavoidably raising hard questions.
- **Balaam killed with the Midianites** (v8) — the ambiguous prophet meets his end. Num 22–24 showed his words were true; 25:1–3 and 31:16 suggest his counsel instigated the Peor seduction. Conflict-within-Balaam tradition develops in later Judaism.
- **Purification process** (v19–24) — seven-day quarantine for all who killed or touched the dead; red-heifer water cleansing; fire-purification of non-combustibles. Even a holy-war expedition contracts corpse-impurity.
- **Spoil division** — 50/50 between warriors (12,000) and congregation (~2.5 million). Warriors pay 1/500 tribute; congregation pays 1/50. Equity-balancing: the small active group compensates with lower tribute-rate since they bore the danger.
- **Census of warriors** (v49) — "not one missing." Remarkable: 12,000 Israelites against (presumably) many more Midianites, zero casualties. Theological signal: this is Yahweh's war.
- **Pendants for memorial** (v50, 54) — the officers' gratitude offering deposited in the Tent as memorial. Financial permanentization of a moment of deliverance.
