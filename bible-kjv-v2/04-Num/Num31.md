# Numbers 31

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Avenge the vengeance of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ on the ⟦midianites|Midyanim|Midianites⟧; afterward you shall be gathered to your peoples."

[3] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ spoke to the people, saying, "Arm yourselves, men from among you, for the host, and let them be against ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧, to put the vengeance of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ on ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧.

[4] A thousand for each tribe, for all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, you shall send to the host."

[5] And there were handed over, from the thousands of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, a thousand for a tribe: twelve thousand armed for the host.

[6] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ sent them, a thousand for a tribe, to the host, them and ⟦phinehas|Pinchas|Phinehas⟧ the son of ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest, to the host, and the holy utensils and the trumpets of the blast in his hand.

[7] And they waged war against ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, and they killed every male.

[8] And the kings of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ they killed, together with their slain: Evi and ⟦rekem|Reqem|Rekem⟧ and ⟦zur|Tzur|Zur⟧ and ⟦hur|Chur|Hur⟧ and ⟦reba|Reva|Reba⟧, the five kings of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧; and ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ the son of Beor they killed with the sword.

[9] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ took captive the women of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ and their little ones; and all their beasts and all their livestock and all their wealth they plundered.

[10] And all their cities in their dwelling places and all their encampments they burned with fire.

[11] And they took all the spoil and all the booty, of man and of beast.

[12] And they brought to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and to ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest and to the congregation of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ the captives and the booty and the spoil, to the camp, to the plains of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, which are by the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ of ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧.

[13] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest and all the chieftains of the congregation went out to meet them, to the outside of the camp.

[14] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ was wrathful with the appointed ones of the force, the commanders of the thousands and the commanders of the hundreds, who came from the host of the war.

[15] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said to them, "Have you let every female live?

[16] Behold, these were to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, by the word of ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧, for committing unfaithfulness against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in the matter of Peor, and the plague was in the congregation of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[17] And now, kill every male among the little ones; and every woman who has known a man by the lying of a male, kill.

[18] And all the little ones among the women, who have not known the lying of a male, keep alive for yourselves.

[19] And you, camp outside the camp seven days; everyone who has killed a soul, and everyone who has touched a slain one, you shall purge yourselves of sin on the third day and on the seventh day, you and your captives.

[20] And every garment and every article of skin and every work of goats' hair and every article of wood you shall purge of sin."

[21] And ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest said to the men of the host who had come to the war, "This is the statute of ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧:

[22] Only the gold and the silver, the bronze, the iron, the tin and the lead,

[23] every thing that goes into the fire, you shall make pass through the fire, and it shall be clean; only, with the water of impurity it shall be purged of sin; and all that does not go into the fire you shall make pass through the water.

[24] And you shall wash your garments on the seventh day, and you shall be clean; and afterward you shall come into the camp."

[25] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[26] "Lift up the head of the booty of the captivity, of man and of beast, you and ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest and the heads of the fathers of the congregation.

[27] And you shall divide the booty in half between those who took hold of the war, who went out to the host, and all the congregation.

[28] And you shall lift up a levy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from the men of war who went out to the host: one soul out of five hundred, of man and of the herd and of the donkeys and of the flock.

[29] From their half you shall take, and you shall give to ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest the contribution of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[30] And from the half of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ you shall take one seized out of fifty, of man, of the herd, of the donkeys and of the flock, of all the beasts; and you shall give them to the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, who keep the charge of the dwelling of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[31] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest did as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[32] And the booty, the remainder of the plunder that the people of the host had plundered, was: of the flock, six hundred and seventy-five thousand;

[33] and of the herd, seventy-two thousand;

[34] and donkeys, sixty-one thousand;

[35] and the souls of man, of the women who had not known the lying of a male: all the souls were thirty-two thousand.

[36] And the half, the share of those who went out in the host, was: the number of the flock, three hundred and thirty-seven thousand five hundred;

[37] and the levy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from the flock was six hundred and seventy-five.

[38] And the herd: thirty-six thousand; and their levy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, seventy-two.

[39] And donkeys: thirty thousand five hundred; and their levy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, sixty-one.

[40] And the souls of man: sixteen thousand; and their levy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, thirty-two souls.

[41] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ gave the levy of the contribution of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[42] And from the half of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, which ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ had divided off from the men who waged war —

[43] and the half of the congregation was, of the flock, three hundred and thirty-seven thousand five hundred;

[44] and of the herd, thirty-six thousand;

[45] and donkeys, thirty thousand five hundred;

[46] and the souls of man, sixteen thousand —

[47] and ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ took from the half of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ the seized one, one out of fifty, of man and of beast, and he gave them to the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, who keep the charge of the dwelling of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[48] And the appointed ones who were over the thousands of the host, the commanders of the thousands and the commanders of the hundreds, came near to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧,

[49] and they said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Your servants have lifted up the head of the men of war who are in our hand, and not a man of us is missing.

[50] And we have brought near the offering of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, each man what he found: articles of gold, armlet and bracelet, signet ring, earring and pendant, to make atonement for our souls before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[51] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest took the gold from them, every article of workmanship.

[52] And all the gold of the contribution that they lifted up to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was sixteen thousand seven hundred and fifty shekels, from the commanders of the thousands and from the commanders of the hundreds.

[53] The men of the host had plundered, each man for himself.

[54] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest took the gold from the commanders of the thousands and of the hundreds, and they brought it into the tent of meeting, a memorial for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew וַ / וְ "and" at the head of a clause is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"Avenge the children of Israel of the Midianites" → "Avenge the vengeance of the sons of Israel on the Midianites"** (v2) — Hebrew נְקֹם נִקְמַת … מֵאֵת, verb and noun of one root, literally "avenge the vengeance of the sons of Israel from the Midianites". **"gathered unto thy people" → "gathered to your peoples"** — Hebrew עַמֶּיךָ, a plural: his kinsfolk (Gen 25:8; Num 27:13).
- **"Arm some of yourselves unto the war, and let them go against the Midianites, and avenge the LORD of Midian" → "Arm yourselves, men from among you, for the host, and let them be against Midian, to put the vengeance of Yahweh on Midian"** (v3) — Hebrew הֵחָלְצוּ מֵאִתְּכֶם אֲנָשִׁים, a reflexive command, and then "they": the Hebrew passes from "you" to "them". חָלַץ is said of men made ready for war; the root sense is not certain ("stripped" for action, or "girded"). צָבָא is "host", the army in its ranks (v3–7, 14, 21, 27, 28, 32, 36, 48, 53); "war" is מִלְחָמָה (v14, 21, 27, 28, 49). וְיִהְיוּ עַל is literally "and let them be upon / against"; לָתֵת נִקְמַת־יְהוָה בְּ is literally "to give the vengeance of Yahweh on". In v2 it is the vengeance "of the sons of Israel", here "of Yahweh". The Hebrew has "Midian", the people as one, in v3, 7–9, and "the Midianites" in v2.
- **"Of every tribe a thousand" → "A thousand for each tribe"** (v4) — Hebrew אֶלֶף לַמַּטֶּה אֶלֶף לַמַּטֶּה, literally "a thousand for the tribe, a thousand for the tribe"; in v5, 6 the phrase stands once ("a thousand for a tribe"). מַטֶּה "tribe" is the word for "staff".
- **"So there were delivered out of the thousands of Israel" → "And there were handed over, from the thousands of Israel"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיִּמָּסְרוּ. The verb מָסַר stands in the Hebrew Bible only here and in v16; its sense ("hand over, deliver", as in later Hebrew; here also taken as "were counted off, furnished") is not certain. **"armed for war" → "armed for the host"** — Hebrew חֲלוּצֵי צָבָא (32:27).
- **"with the holy instruments, and the trumpets to blow in his hand" → "and the holy utensils and the trumpets of the blast in his hand"** (v6) — Hebrew כְּלֵי הַקֹּדֶשׁ, "the utensils of holiness" (which ones is not said), and חֲצֹצְרוֹת הַתְּרוּעָה: תְּרוּעָה is a loud cry raised, a shout or the blast of alarm (10:5, 9; 29:1). The Hebrew has "and", not "with"; "to the host" stands twice.
- **"they warred against the Midianites" → "they waged war against Midian"** (v7; so v42) — Hebrew וַיִּצְבְּאוּ, the verb of צָבָא "host". **"all the males" → "every male"** — Hebrew כָּל־זָכָר.
- **"beside the rest of them that were slain" → "together with their slain"** (v8) — Hebrew עַל־חַלְלֵיהֶם, literally "upon their slain" (חָלָל, "one pierced"); "the rest" is not in the Hebrew. The Hebrew order "the kings of Midian they killed … Balaam … they killed" is kept; the names are joined by "and".
- **"took all the women of Midian captives … and took the spoil of all their cattle, and all their flocks, and all their goods" → "took captive the women of Midian … and all their beasts and all their livestock and all their wealth they plundered"** (v9) — Hebrew בְּהֵמָה "beasts", מִקְנֶה "livestock", חַיִל "wealth" (the word of "force", v14), בָּזַז "plunder"; the Hebrew has no "all" before "the women".
- **"all their cities wherein they dwelt, and all their goodly castles" → "all their cities in their dwelling places and all their encampments"** (v10) — Hebrew בְּמוֹשְׁבֹתָם and טִירֹתָם (Gen 25:16): a camp enclosed with a wall of stones; "goodly" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"the spoil … the prey" → "the spoil … the booty"** (v11, 12, 26, 27, 32) — Hebrew שָׁלָל "spoil" and מַלְקוֹחַ, from לָקַח "take": "what is taken", here the living booty of man and beast. With בָּז "plunder" (v32) and שְׁבִי "captives" (v12, 19, 26) the four words are kept apart. **"both of men and of beasts" → "of man and of beast"** — Hebrew בָּאָדָם וּבַבְּהֵמָה, literally "in man and in beast".
- **"unto the camp at the plains of Moab, which are by Jordan near Jericho" → "to the camp, to the plains of Moab, which are by the Jordan of Jericho"** (v12) — Hebrew עַרְבֹת מוֹאָב (עֲרָבָה is dry steppe) and יַרְדֵּן יְרֵחוֹ, a genitive: the stretch of the Jordan by Jericho (22:1; 33:48).
- **"the princes of the congregation" → "the chieftains of the congregation"** (v13) — Hebrew נְשִׂיאֵי, literally "ones lifted up". **"without the camp" → "to the outside of the camp"** — Hebrew אֶל־מִחוּץ לַמַּחֲנֶה.
- **"Moses was wroth with the officers of the host, with the captains over thousands, and captains over hundreds, which came from the battle" → "Moshe was wrathful with the appointed ones of the force, the commanders of the thousands and the commanders of the hundreds, who came from the host of the war"** (v14; so v48) — Hebrew קָצַף "be wrathful"; פְּקוּדֵי הֶחָיִל, from פָּקַד "appoint, number": those set over the army (in Num 1–4 the same form is "those who were numbered"); חַיִל "force"; שַׂר "prince, chief", of an army "commander"; מִצְּבָא הַמִּלְחָמָה, literally "from the host of the war": from the campaign.
- **"Have ye saved all the women alive?" → "Have you let every female live?"** (v15) — Hebrew הַחִיִּיתֶם כָּל־נְקֵבָה: נְקֵבָה is "female", the word that answers זָכָר "male" of v7, 17.
- **"these caused the children of Israel, through the counsel of Balaam, to commit trespass against the LORD" → "these were to the sons of Israel, by the word of Balaam, for committing unfaithfulness against Yahweh"** (v16) — Hebrew הֵנָּה הָיוּ לִבְנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל בִּדְבַר בִּלְעָם לִמְסָר־מַעַל בַּיהוָה. The words are hard. לִמְסָר is the verb of v5, "hand over": literally "these were to the sons of Israel … for the handing over of unfaithfulness against Yahweh"; what is meant is that the women were the occasion of it (25:1–3). The sense "commit" is taken from the context and is not certain; the text is often corrected to לִמְעָל־מַעַל "to commit unfaithfulness", the usual phrase (5:6; the correction is cited from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept. דְּבַר is "word"; "counsel" (עֵצָה) is not the word. מַעַל is "unfaithfulness", a breach of trust. **"in the matter of Peor"** — Hebrew עַל־דְּבַר־פְּעוֹר: the same דָּבָר, here "matter". **"there was a plague among the congregation" → "the plague was in the congregation"** — Hebrew הַמַּגֵּפָה, with the article: the plague of 25:8, 9, literally "the blow".
- **"kill every woman that hath known man by lying with him" → "every woman who has known a man by the lying of a male, kill"** (v17) — Hebrew יֹדַעַת אִישׁ לְמִשְׁכַּב זָכָר, literally "knowing a man as to the lying of a male": אִישׁ "man" and זָכָר "male" both stand. In v18, 35 it is לֹא־יָדְעוּ מִשְׁכַּב זָכָר, "have not known the lying of a male". The verb "kill" stands at the head and at the end of the verse.
- **"all the women children" → "all the little ones among the women"** (v18) — Hebrew הַטַּף בַּנָּשִׁים: טַף, the small children of a household, the word of v9, 17. **"keep alive for yourselves"** kept — Hebrew הַחֲיוּ לָכֶם; the causative, beside "let live" of v15.
- **"abide without the camp" → "camp outside the camp"** (v19) — Hebrew חֲנוּ, the verb of מַחֲנֶה "camp". **"whosoever hath killed any person" → "everyone who has killed a soul"** — Hebrew הֹרֵג נֶפֶשׁ: נֶפֶשׁ "soul" is the person (so v28, 35, 40, 46, 50). **"purify both yourselves and your captives" → "you shall purge yourselves of sin … you and your captives"** (v19, 20, 23) — Hebrew הִתְחַטֵּא, the reflexive of חִטֵּא, literally "de-sin oneself" (8:21; 19:12): the uncleanness from the dead is removed; it is not the verb טָהֵר "be clean" of v23, 24. In v20 the same reflexive form stands with the things as its object.
- **"all your raiment, and all that is made of skins, and all work of goats' hair, and all things made of wood" → "every garment and every article of skin and every work of goats' hair and every article of wood"** (v20) — Hebrew כְּלִי "article"; "hair" is supplied: the Hebrew has "work of goats".
- **"the men of war which went to the battle" → "the men of the host who had come to the war"** (v21) — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי הַצָּבָא הַבָּאִים לַמִּלְחָמָה. **"This is the ordinance of the law" → "This is the statute of the torah"** — Hebrew חֻקַּת הַתּוֹרָה (19:2): תּוֹרָה is "instruction".
- **"Every thing that may abide the fire, ye shall make it go through the fire" → "every thing that goes into the fire, you shall make pass through the fire"** (v23) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־יָבֹא בָאֵשׁ, literally "that comes into the fire": that can bear it. **"nevertheless it shall be purified with the water of separation" → "only, with the water of impurity it shall be purged of sin"** — Hebrew אַךְ "only", as at the head of v22; מֵי נִדָּה, the water of chapter 19: נִדָּה is "impurity" (Lev 12:2; 15:19), a word whose root sense, "separation" or "casting off", is not certain.
- **"ye shall wash your clothes" → "you shall wash your garments"** (v24) — Hebrew בֶּגֶד, the word of v20.
- **"And the LORD spake" → "And Yahweh said"** (v25) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר.
- **"Take the sum of the prey that was taken" → "Lift up the head of the booty of the captivity"** (v26; so v49) — Hebrew שָׂא אֵת רֹאשׁ, the phrase of the census (1:2); מַלְקוֹחַ הַשְּׁבִי: שְׁבִי is the word "captives" of v12, 19, here of all that was carried off, man and beast, and so "captivity". **"the chief fathers of the congregation" → "the heads of the fathers of the congregation"** — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי אֲבוֹת.
- **"divide the prey into two parts; between them that took the war upon them, who went out to battle" → "divide the booty in half between those who took hold of the war, who went out to the host"** (v27) — Hebrew חָצָה "halve"; תֹּפְשֵׂי הַמִּלְחָמָה, literally "the graspers of the war": those who handled it. The Hebrew says "between … and between".
- **"levy a tribute unto the LORD" → "lift up a levy to Yahweh"** (v28) — Hebrew וַהֲרֵמֹתָ, "lift up, lift off", the verb of תְּרוּמָה "contribution" (v29, 41, 52); מֶכֶס (only in this chapter) is a fixed share counted off: "levy". **"one soul of five hundred, both of the persons, and of the beeves, and of the asses, and of the sheep" → "one soul out of five hundred, of man and of the herd and of the donkeys and of the flock"** — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ "soul", here of beasts as well as of men: one living being; אָדָם "man"; בָּקָר "herd" (cattle); צֹאן "flock" (sheep and goats).
- **"Take it of their half, and give it unto Eleazar the priest, for an heave offering of the LORD" → "From their half you shall take, and you shall give to Eleazar the priest the contribution of Yahweh"** (v29) — the Hebrew has no "it"; "you shall take" is plural and "you shall give" singular. תְּרוּמָה is "what is lifted off".
- **"one portion of fifty" → "one seized out of fifty"** (v30; so v47 "the seized one") — Hebrew אֶחָד אָחֻז, the passive participle of אָחַז "take hold": one drawn out; "portion" is not the word. **"of all manner of beasts" → "of all the beasts"**. **"which keep the charge of the tabernacle of the LORD" → "who keep the charge of the dwelling of Yahweh"** — Hebrew שֹׁמְרֵי מִשְׁמֶרֶת מִשְׁכַּן יְהוָה.
- **"the booty, being the rest of the prey which the men of war had caught" → "the booty, the remainder of the plunder that the people of the host had plundered"** (v32) — Hebrew הַמַּלְקוֹחַ יֶתֶר הַבָּז אֲשֶׁר בָּזְזוּ עַם הַצָּבָא: what was left of the plunder when it came to be counted.
- **Numbers** (v32–46, 52) — written in words, in the ordinary English order. The Hebrew builds them in its own order ("six hundred thousand and seventy thousand and five thousand", v32; "two and seventy thousand", v33; "six hundred, five and seventy", v37). The figures: flock 675,000, herd 72,000, donkeys 61,000, souls of man 32,000 (v32–35); the half 337,500, 36,000, 30,500, 16,000, and the levy of one in five hundred 675, 72, 61, 32 (v36–40); the half of the congregation the same (v43–46); the gold 16,750 shekels (v52). The shekel is a weight, not a coin.
- **"thirty and two thousand persons in all, of women that had not known man by lying with him" → "the souls of man, of the women who had not known the lying of a male: all the souls were thirty-two thousand"** (v35; so v40, 46) — Hebrew וְנֶפֶשׁ אָדָם … כָּל־נֶפֶשׁ, literally "and soul of man … every soul": נֶפֶשׁ is a collective singular, the persons.
- **"the half, which was the portion of them that went out to war" → "the half, the share of those who went out in the host"** (v36) — Hebrew חֵלֶק "share".
- **"the LORD'S tribute" → "the levy to Yahweh"** (v37–40) — Hebrew הַמֶּכֶס לַיהוָה, "the levy for Yahweh".
- **"the tribute, which was the LORD'S heave offering" → "the levy of the contribution of Yahweh"** (v41) — Hebrew מֶכֶס תְּרוּמַת יְהוָה, a chain of genitives.
- **"which Moses divided from the men that warred" → "which Moshe had divided off from the men who waged war"** (v42) — Hebrew חָצָה "halve" and הַצֹּבְאִים, as v7. The sentence begun in v42 is taken up again in v47; v43–46 stand between.
- **"Thy servants have taken the sum of the men of war which are under our charge, and there lacketh not one man of us" → "Your servants have lifted up the head of the men of war who are in our hand, and not a man of us is missing"** (v49) — Hebrew עֲבָדֶיךָ (the word is "slaves"); בְּיָדֵנוּ "in our hand"; נִפְקַד, the passive of פָּקַד: "be missed" at a muster.
- **"We have therefore brought an oblation for the LORD, what every man hath gotten, of jewels of gold, chains, and bracelets, rings, earrings, and tablets" → "And we have brought near the offering of Yahweh, each man what he found: articles of gold, armlet and bracelet, signet ring, earring and pendant"** (v50) — Hebrew וַנַּקְרֵב אֶת־קָרְבַּן, verb and noun of one root ("bring near what is brought near"); מָצָא "found"; כְּלִי "article". The five ornaments are singular in the Hebrew: אֶצְעָדָה (2 Sam 1:10; a band for the arm, or for the ankle), צָמִיד "bracelet", טַבַּעַת "signet ring", עָגִיל (Ezek 16:12; a round ornament, an earring), כּוּמָז (Exod 35:22; meaning unknown). Only "bracelet" and "ring" are fairly sure. **"to make an atonement for our souls"** kept — Hebrew לְכַפֵּר עַל־נַפְשֹׁתֵינוּ (Exod 30:15, 16), literally "cover".
- **"all wrought jewels" → "every article of workmanship"** (v51) — Hebrew כֹּל כְּלִי מַעֲשֶׂה, "every article of work": worked gold. **"took"** (v51, 54) — Hebrew לָקַח.
- **"the gold of the offering that they offered up" → "the gold of the contribution that they lifted up"** (v52) — Hebrew זְהַב הַתְּרוּמָה אֲשֶׁר הֵרִימוּ, noun and verb of one root, as v28, 29.
- **"(For the men of war had taken spoil, every man for himself.)" → "The men of the host had plundered, each man for himself."** (v53) — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי הַצָּבָא בָּזְזוּ אִישׁ לוֹ; there is no "for".
- **"for a memorial" → "a memorial"** (v54) — Hebrew זִכָּרוֹן (Exod 30:16).

### Notes
- **The war against Midian** — vengeance for the matter of Peor (ch 25). One of the OT's most disturbing texts: every male is killed, then by Moses' command the male children and every woman who has known a man; the girls who have not are kept alive. The words are translated as they stand.
- **Interpretation disputed** — traditional Jewish and Christian readings treat it as historically specific divine judgment, not paradigmatic. Modern ethical readings wrestle with the text: the genre is partly ancient-Near-Eastern war rhetoric, partly literal narrative, unavoidably raising hard questions.
- **Balaam killed with the Midianites** (v8) — Num 22–24 showed his words were true; 31:16 lays the matter of Peor (25:1–3) to "the word of Balaam".
- **Purging of sin** (v19–24) — seven days outside the camp for all who killed or touched the slain; the water of impurity (ch 19) on the third and seventh day; metal is passed through the fire, the rest through water.
- **Division of the booty** — half to the twelve thousand who went out, half to the congregation. The men of war give one in five hundred to Eleazar; the congregation gives one in fifty to the Levites.
- **Count of the men of war** (v49) — "not a man of us is missing": twelve thousand went out and all came back.
- **The gold** (v50–54) — the commanders' offering, brought into the tent of meeting as a memorial.
