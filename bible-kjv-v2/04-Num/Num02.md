## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"ensign" → "banners"** (v2) — Hebrew אֹתֹת *otot* = signs / ensigns.
- **"far off about the tabernacle" → "facing the Tent of Meeting on every side, at a distance"** (v2) — Hebrew מִנֶּגֶד *mi-neged* = opposite / facing. Camp is disposed around the center at distance, not dispersed away.
- **"captain" → "chief"** (throughout) — Hebrew נָשִׂיא *nasi* = chieftain. *Captain* too military-specific.
- **"go hindmost" → "set out last"** (v31) — modernized.
- **Reuel (v14) vs Deuel (1:14)** — textual variant; some MSS have *Reuel*, others *Deuel* (דעואל / רעואל — daleth/resh confusion).
- **Traditional number-forms modernized** — "an hundred thousand and fourscore thousand" → "186,400" etc.

### Notes
- **Arrangement of the camp** — tribes grouped in four camps of three, each at a cardinal direction, with the Dwelling at the center:
  - East (front): Judah, Issachar, Zebulun — largest camp, sets out first
  - South: Reuben, Simeon, Gad
  - West: Ephraim, Manasseh, Benjamin (Rachel's descendants)
  - North: Dan, Asher, Naphtali — sets out last
- **Leah's tribes east and south** — her elder sons; Rachel's tribes on the west; concubines' tribes on the north. Mirrors Jacob's wives' household geography.
- **Judah first** — though not firstborn, marches first. Prefigures messianic leadership (Gen 49:10).
- **Dwelling at center, Levites around, tribes around Levites** — concentric rings of holiness: Most Holy Place → Holy Place → Levite courts → Israelite camps → outside (impurity). Ezekiel 48 uses the same schema for the restored land.
