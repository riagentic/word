## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew יהוה, the Tetragrammaton.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; where it has none (v7, 9, 10, 18, 25, 31) none is added.
- **"Every man of the children of Israel shall pitch by his own standard, with the ensign of their father's house" → "Each man at his standard, with signs, by their fathers' houses, the sons of Israel shall camp"** (v2) — Hebrew אִישׁ עַל־דִּגְלוֹ בְאֹתֹת לְבֵית אֲבֹתָם. דֶּגֶל is "standard" as in 1:52: a banner, and the body of men that camps under it (v3, "the standard of the camp of Judah"); the sense "division of an army" is also defended. אֹתֹת is the plural of אוֹת "sign" (Gen 1:14; 9:12); what the signs were is not said. לְבֵית אֲבֹתָם is the phrase of chapter 1, "by their fathers' houses"; it may also be joined to "signs" ("the signs of their fathers' houses"). חָנָה is "camp", the verb of מַחֲנֶה "camp".
- **"far off about the tabernacle of the congregation" → "opposite, around the tent of meeting"** (v2) — Hebrew מִנֶּגֶד, "over against, opposite" (Gen 21:16): facing the tent, at some distance from it. "Far off" is not said.
- **"on the east side toward the rising of the sun" → "on the east, eastward"** (v3) — Hebrew קֵדְמָה מִזְרָחָה, two words for the east: literally "toward the front, toward the sunrise". "Southward" (v10) is תֵּימָנָה, "toward Teman"; "westward" (v18) is יָמָּה, "toward the sea"; "northward" (v25) is צָפֹנָה.
- **"throughout their armies" → "by their hosts"** (v3, 9, 10, 16, 18, 24, 25, 32) — Hebrew לְצִבְאֹתָם, as 1:3. In v31 the phrase is absent.
- **"captain" → "chieftain"** (throughout) — Hebrew נָשִׂיא, literally "one lifted up". The clauses of v3–31 have no verb: "shall be" is not in the Hebrew; "is" is supplied.
- **"And his host, and those that were numbered of them, were" → "And his host, and those of them who were numbered:"** (v4–30) — Hebrew וּצְבָאוֹ וּפְקֻדֵיהֶם, two nouns joined by "and", with no verb. The suffix changes: "those of them" (פְקֻדֵיהֶם: v4, 13, 15, 19, 21, 23, 26, 28, 30) and "those of his" (פְקֻדָיו: v6, 8, 11). The difference is kept.
- **"those that do pitch next unto him" → "those who camp next to him"** (v5, 12, 27) — Hebrew עָלָיו, literally "upon him, beside him". In v20 only "And next to him".
- **"Then the tribe of Zebulun" → "The tribe of Zebulun"** (v7) — the Hebrew has no "and" here; v14, 22, 29 have it.
- **"an hundred thousand and fourscore thousand and six thousand and four hundred" → "one hundred and eighty-six thousand four hundred"** (v9, 16, 24, 31, 32) — the numbers are in words, in ordinary English order. **"in the camp of Judah" → "for the camp of Judah"** — Hebrew לְמַחֲנֵה.
- **"These shall first set forth" → "They shall set out first"** (v9, 16, 24, 31) — Hebrew נָסַע, literally "pull up" (the tent pegs).
- **"the son of Reuel" kept** (v14) — the printed Hebrew has רְעוּאֵל here, and דְּעוּאֵל "Deuel" for the same man in 1:14; 7:42, 47; 10:20. The letters ד and ר are much alike. The verse follows the printed text; some Hebrew manuscripts and versions are said to read "Deuel" here too (from memory).
- **"Then the tabernacle of the congregation shall set forward with the camp of the Levites in the midst of the camp" → "And the tent of meeting shall set out, the camp of the Levites, in the midst of the camps"** (v17) — Hebrew וְנָסַע אֹהֶל־מוֹעֵד מַחֲנֵה הַלְוִיִּם בְּתוֹךְ הַמַּחֲנֹת: "the camp of the Levites" stands beside "the tent of meeting" with no word between ("with" is not in the Hebrew), and "camps" is plural. **"every man in his place"** — Hebrew עַל־יָדוֹ, literally "on his hand".
- **"They shall go hindmost with their standards" → "They shall set out last, by their standards"** (v31) — Hebrew לָאַחֲרֹנָה יִסְעוּ לְדִגְלֵיהֶם.
- **"These are those which were numbered of the children of Israel by the house of their fathers" → "These are those who were numbered of the sons of Israel, by their fathers' houses"** (v32) — Hebrew פְּקוּדֵי, the participle of פָּקַד "number", as in chapter 1.
- **"But the Levites were not numbered among the children of Israel" → "And the Levites were not numbered in the midst of the sons of Israel"** (v33) — Hebrew הָתְפָּקְדוּ, as 1:47; בְּתוֹךְ.
- **"And the children of Israel did according to all that the LORD commanded Moses: so they pitched" → "And the sons of Israel did; according to all that Yahweh had commanded Moses, so they camped"** (v34) — the accents divide the verse after "did", as in 1:54. **"every one after their families, according to the house of their fathers" → "each man by his clans, according to his fathers' house"** — Hebrew אִישׁ לְמִשְׁפְּחֹתָיו עַל־בֵּית אֲבֹתָיו: the suffixes are singular, and the second preposition is עַל, not the לְ of the formula.

### Notes

- **Arrangement of the camp** — tribes grouped in four camps of three, each at a cardinal direction, with the tent of meeting at the center:
  - East (front): Judah, Issachar, Zebulun — largest camp, sets out first
  - South: Reuben, Simeon, Gad
  - West: Ephraim, Manasseh, Benjamin (Rachel's descendants)
  - North: Dan, Asher, Naphtali — sets out last
- **The figures** — each tribe's figure is that of chapter 1; the four camps (186,400 + 151,450 + 108,100 + 157,600) give the 603,550 of v32 and 1:46.
- **Leah's tribes east and south** — her sons (with Gad, the son of her maidservant, in the place of Levi); Rachel's tribes on the west; the other sons of the maidservants on the north.
- **Judah first** — though not firstborn, sets out first (Gen 49:8–10).
- **Tent at center, Levites around, tribes around the Levites** — 1:53; 3:23–38. Ezekiel 48 uses a like plan for the land.
