# Numbers 36

[1] And the heads of the fathers of the clan of the sons of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ the son of Machir the son of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧, from the clans of the sons of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, came near and spoke before ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and before the chieftains, the heads of the fathers of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[2] And they said, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ commanded my lord to give the land as an inheritance by lot to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and my lord was commanded by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to give the inheritance of ⟦zelophehad|Tzelofchad|Zelophehad⟧ our brother to his daughters.

[3] And should they become wives to one of the sons of the tribes of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, then their inheritance will be taken away from the inheritance of our fathers and added to the inheritance of the tribe to which they shall belong; and from the lot of our inheritance it will be taken away.

[4] And when the jubilee comes for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, then their inheritance will be added to the inheritance of the tribe to which they shall belong; and from the inheritance of the tribe of our fathers their inheritance will be taken away."

[5] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ commanded the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ at the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying, "The tribe of the sons of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ are speaking rightly.

[6] This is the thing that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded for the daughters of ⟦zelophehad|Tzelofchad|Zelophehad⟧, saying, 'Let them become wives to the one who is good in their eyes; only, to the clan of the tribe of their father they shall become wives.

[7] And an inheritance of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall not turn about from tribe to tribe, for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall cling, each man to the inheritance of the tribe of his fathers.

[8] And every daughter who possesses an inheritance from the tribes of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall become a wife to one of the clan of the tribe of her father, so that the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ may possess, each man the inheritance of his fathers.

[9] And an inheritance shall not turn about from a tribe to another tribe, for the tribes of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall cling, each to its inheritance.'"

[10] As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, so the daughters of ⟦zelophehad|Tzelofchad|Zelophehad⟧ did.

[11] And ⟦mahlah|Machlah|Mahlah⟧, ⟦tirzah|Tirtzah|Tirzah⟧ and ⟦hoglah|Choglah|Hoglah⟧ and ⟦milcah|Milkah|Milcah⟧ and Noah, the daughters of ⟦zelophehad|Tzelofchad|Zelophehad⟧, became wives to the sons of their uncles.

[12] They became wives from among the clans of the sons of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ the son of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, and their inheritance was with the tribe of the clan of their father.

[13] These are the commandments and the ordinances that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ in the plains of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ by the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ of ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of" → "sons of"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"the chief fathers of the families of the children of Gilead" → "the heads of the fathers of the clan of the sons of Gilead"** (v1) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי הָאָבוֹת לְמִשְׁפַּחַת, "clan" in the singular the first time, plural the second. **"families" → "clan(s)"** (v1, 6, 8, 12) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּחָה, the kin group between the tribe and the father's house. **"princes" → "chieftains"** (v1) — Hebrew נְשִׂאִים. **"came near"** — Hebrew קָרַב.
- **"for an inheritance" → "as an inheritance"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּנַחֲלָה (34:2). The Hebrew sets "my lord" first in both halves: "my lord Yahweh commanded … and my lord was commanded by Yahweh". **"inheritance"** is נַחֲלָה in every place in this chapter (seventeen times); אֲחֻזָּה "possession" does not stand in it.
- **"And if they be married to any of the sons of the other tribes" → "And should they become wives to one of the sons of the tribes"** (v3) — Hebrew וְהָיוּ לְאֶחָד … לְנָשִׁים, literally "and they shall be to one … for wives"; the Hebrew has no "if" (the clause chain makes the case) and no "other". So "become wives" in v6, 8, 11, 12: the same words, הָיָה לְ … לְאִשָּׁה.
- **"tribes" / "tribe" kept** (v3–9, 12) — two Hebrew words, both "tribe". In v3 "the sons of the tribes of the sons of Israel" has שִׁבְטֵי (literally "rods"), its only place in the chapter; "the inheritance of the tribe to which they shall belong" in the same verse, and every other "tribe" in v4–9 and v12, has מַטֶּה (literally "staff"). The two words name the same thing (see 32:28).
- **"shall their inheritance be taken from … shall it be taken from" → "will be taken away from"** (v3, 4) — Hebrew נִגְרַע, the passive of גָּרַע "diminish, take away from" (Exod 5:8). **"shall be put to" → "added to"** — Hebrew נוֹסַף. **"the tribe whereunto they are received" → "the tribe to which they shall belong"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר תִּהְיֶינָה לָהֶם, literally "which they shall be to them".
- **"when the jubile of the children of Israel shall be" → "when the jubilee comes for the sons of Israel"** (v4) — Hebrew וְאִם־יִהְיֶה הַיֹּבֵל לִבְנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל, "and if / when there is the jubilee for the sons of Israel". יֹבֵל is the word for a ram's horn (Exod 19:13) and then for the fiftieth year (Lev 25:10), when sold land goes back; land that passed by inheritance would not go back.
- **"according to the word of the LORD" → "at the mouth of Yahweh"** (v5) — Hebrew עַל־פִּי יְהוָה. **"hath said well" → "are speaking rightly"** — Hebrew כֵּן … דֹּבְרִים, a plural participle after "tribe": כֵּן is "right, so" (27:7).
- **"Let them marry to whom they think best" → "Let them become wives to the one who is good in their eyes"** (v6) — Hebrew לַטּוֹב בְּעֵינֵיהֶם תִּהְיֶינָה לְנָשִׁים. **"only to the family of the tribe of their father shall they marry" → "only, to the clan of the tribe of their father they shall become wives"** — the same words again. Where the words of Yahweh end is not marked; they are taken to run to the end of v9.
- **"So shall not the inheritance … remove from tribe to tribe" → "And an inheritance … shall not turn about from tribe to tribe"** (v7, 9) — Hebrew לֹא־תִסֹּב, from סָבַב "turn, go round" (the verb of the border in 34:4). **"keep himself to" → "cling"** — Hebrew דָּבַק *davaq* = cling to (Gen 2:24). The word order is "each man to the inheritance of the tribe of his fathers they shall cling, the sons of Israel".
- **"every daughter, that possesseth an inheritance in any tribe" → "every daughter who possesses an inheritance from the tribes"** (v8) — Hebrew יֹרֶשֶׁת, from יָרַשׁ "possess, be heir to"; not נָחַל "inherit". So also "may possess" at the end of the verse (KJV "enjoy"). **"shall be wife unto one of the family of the tribe of her father"** — Hebrew לְאֶחָד מִמִּשְׁפַּחַת מַטֵּה אָבִיהָ.
- **"were married unto their father's brothers' sons" → "became wives to the sons of their uncles"** (v11) — Hebrew בְּנֵי דֹדֵיהֶן; דֹּד is the father's brother. The order of the names differs from 26:33 and 27:1; "and" stands where the Hebrew has it.
- **"they were married into the families of the sons of Manasseh" → "They became wives from among the clans of the sons of Manasseh"** (v12) — Hebrew מִמִּשְׁפְּחֹת … הָיוּ לְנָשִׁים, "from the clans … they were for wives": to men from those clans. **"remained in the tribe of the family of their father" → "was with the tribe of the clan of their father"** — Hebrew וַתְּהִי … עַל, "was upon".
- **"judgments" → "ordinances"** (v13) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּטִים. **"by Jordan near Jericho" → "by the Jordan of Jericho"** — Hebrew עַל יַרְדֵּן יְרֵחוֹ (33:48).

### Notes
- **Sequel to ch 27** — Zelophehad's daughters had won the right to inherit. Now the Manassite clan-heads raise the follow-up issue: if heiress-daughters marry outside the tribe, the inheritance transfers to that other tribe's portion, permanently altering tribal territorial boundaries at jubilee.
- **Resolution** — heiress-daughters must marry within their own tribe. Not a blanket rule for all women (they retain their freedom of choice generally) — only for heiresses.
- **Zelophehad's daughters obey** — they marry cousins within Manasseh. Preserves both their inheritance right and Manasseh's territorial integrity.
- **Book closing** (v13) — "These are the commandments and the ordinances... in the plains of Moab by the Jordan of Jericho." Parallels Leviticus' close ("These are the commandments ... on Mount Sinai," Lev 27:34). Signals the end of the Sinai-plus-wilderness legal corpus. Deuteronomy will re-present and expand this material in Moses' farewell sermons.
- **Geography of closure** — the book ends *at* the Jordan, *at* Jericho. Moses' death in Deut 34; Jericho falls in Josh 6. Numbers closes at the threshold.
