## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"families" → "clans"** (throughout) — Hebrew *mishpachot*.
- **"princes" → "chiefs"** (v1).
- **"said well" → "spoken rightly"** (v5).
- **"keep himself to" → "hold fast to"** (v7, 9) — Hebrew דָבַק *davaq* = cling to.

### Notes
- **Sequel to ch 27** — Zelophehad's daughters had won the right to inherit. Now the Manassite clan-heads raise the follow-up issue: if heiress-daughters marry outside the tribe, the inheritance transfers to that other tribe's portion, permanently altering tribal territorial boundaries at jubilee.
- **Resolution** — heiress-daughters must marry within their own tribe. Not a blanket rule for all women (they retain their freedom of choice generally) — only for heiresses.
- **Zelophehad's daughters obey** — they marry cousins within Manasseh. Preserves both their inheritance right and Manasseh's territorial integrity.
- **Book closing** (v13) — "These are the commandments and the ordinances... in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho." Parallels Leviticus' close ("These are the commandments ... on Mount Sinai," Lev 27:34). Signals the end of the Sinai-plus-wilderness legal corpus. Deuteronomy will re-present and expand this material in Moses' farewell sermons.
- **Geography of closure** — the book ends *at* the Jordan, *at* Jericho. Moses' death in Deut 34; Jericho falls in Josh 6. Numbers closes at the threshold.
