# Numbers 5

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Command the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and let them send away from the camp every leper, and everyone who flows, and everyone unclean for a soul.

[3] From male to female you shall send away; to outside the camp you shall send them, and they shall not defile their camps, in the midst of which I abide."

[4] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ did so, and they sent them away to outside the camp; as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had spoken to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, so the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ did.

[5] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[6] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: 'A man or a woman, when they do any of all the sins of man, to commit unfaithfulness against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and that soul is guilty,

[7] then they shall confess their sin that they have done; and he shall return his guilt in its principal, and its fifth he shall add to it, and he shall give it to him toward whom he is guilty.

[8] And if the man has no redeemer to whom to return the guilt, the guilt that is returned is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧'s, the priest's, besides the ram of atonement with which he makes atonement for him.

[9] And every contribution of all the holy things of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ that they bring near to the priest shall be his.

[10] And each man, his holy things shall be his; what a man gives to the priest shall be his.'"

[11] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[12] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and say to them: 'Any man, when his wife strays and commits unfaithfulness against him,

[13] and a man lies with her with an emission of seed, and it is hidden from the eyes of her husband, and she is concealed, and she is defiled, and there is no witness against her, and she was not caught,

[14] and a spirit of jealousy passes over him and he is jealous of his wife, and she is defiled; or a spirit of jealousy passes over him and he is jealous of his wife, and she is not defiled:

[15] then the man shall bring his wife to the priest, and he shall bring her offering for her, a tenth of the ephah of barley meal; he shall not pour oil on it and shall not place frankincense on it, for it is a grain offering of jealousies, a grain offering of memorial, bringing iniquity to remembrance.

[16] And the priest shall bring her near and make her stand before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[17] And the priest shall take holy water in an earthen vessel, and of the dust that is on the floor of the dwelling the priest shall take and put it into the water.

[18] And the priest shall make the woman stand before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he shall let the head of the woman go loose, and he shall put on her palms the grain offering of memorial — it is a grain offering of jealousies — and in the hand of the priest shall be the water of bitterness that curses.

[19] And the priest shall make her swear, and he shall say to the woman, "If no man has lain with you, and if you have not strayed into uncleanness under your husband, be clear from this water of bitterness that curses.

[20] But you, if you have strayed under your husband, and if you are defiled, and a man has given his lying in you, apart from your husband" —

[21] then the priest shall make the woman swear with the oath of the curse, and the priest shall say to the woman, "May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ make you a curse and an oath in the midst of your people, when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ makes your thigh fall and your belly swell;

[22] and this water that curses shall come into your inward parts, to make the belly swell and to make the thigh fall." And the woman shall say, "Amen, amen."

[23] And the priest shall write these curses in a book, and he shall blot them out into the water of bitterness.

[24] And he shall give the woman to drink the water of bitterness that curses, and the water that curses shall come into her for bitterness.

[25] And the priest shall take from the hand of the woman the grain offering of jealousies, and he shall wave the grain offering before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and bring it near to the altar.

[26] And the priest shall take a handful from the grain offering, its memorial portion, and turn it into smoke on the altar; and afterward he shall give the woman to drink the water.

[27] And he shall give her to drink the water; and it shall be, if she is defiled and has committed unfaithfulness against her husband, that the water that curses shall come into her for bitterness, and her belly shall swell and her thigh shall fall; and the woman shall be a curse among her people.

[28] And if the woman is not defiled, and she is clean, then she shall be clear and shall be sown with seed.

[29] This is ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ of jealousies: when a woman strays under her husband and is defiled,

[30] or a man, when a spirit of jealousy passes over him and he is jealous of his wife: then he shall make the woman stand before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the priest shall do to her all this ⟦torah|torah|law⟧.

[31] And the man shall be clear from iniquity, and that woman shall bear her iniquity.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"that they put out of the camp" → "and let them send away from the camp"** (v2–4) — Hebrew וִישַׁלְּחוּ, piel of שָׁלַח "send". In v3 the first "you shall send away" has no object in the Hebrew.
- **"every leper"** (v2) — Hebrew צָרוּעַ, one struck with צָרַעַת (Lev 13–14): a scaly outbreak that makes unclean; it is not the leprosy of modern medicine. **"every one that hath an issue" → "everyone who flows"** — Hebrew זָב, the participle of זוּב "flow" (Lev 15).
- **"whosoever is defiled by the dead" → "everyone unclean for a soul"** (v2) — Hebrew טָמֵא לָנָפֶשׁ. נֶפֶשׁ is the word "soul"; here it is said of a dead person (Lev 21:1, 11; Num 6:6). לְ is "for, with regard to"; the word has the article.
- **"Both male and female" → "From male to female"** (v3) — Hebrew מִזָּכָר עַד־נְקֵבָה. **"that they defile not" → "and they shall not defile"** — Hebrew וְלֹא יְטַמְּאוּ, joined by "and". **"in the midst whereof I dwell" → "in the midst of which I abide"** — Hebrew שֹׁכֵן, the verb of מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling".
- **"When a man or woman shall commit any sin that men commit" → "A man or a woman, when they do any of all the sins of man"** (v6) — Hebrew כִּי יַעֲשׂוּ מִכָּל־חַטֹּאת הָאָדָם. "The sins of man" may be the sins that men do or the sins done against a man; the Hebrew does not decide (v7–8 speak of a wrong done to another person).
- **"to do a trespass against the LORD" → "to commit unfaithfulness against Yahweh"** (v6, 12, 27) — Hebrew לִמְעֹל מַעַל, verb and noun of one root: a breach of trust (Lev 6:2). **"and that person be guilty" → "and that soul is guilty"** — Hebrew הַנֶּפֶשׁ הַהִוא.
- **"he shall recompense his trespass with the principal thereof" → "he shall return his guilt in its principal"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew וְהֵשִׁיב אֶת־אֲשָׁמוֹ בְּרֹאשׁוֹ, literally "in its head": the full sum. אָשָׁם is the one word "guilt"; here it stands for the thing owed. The verse goes from the plural ("they shall confess") to the singular. **"unto him against whom he hath trespassed" → "to him toward whom he is guilty"** — Hebrew לַאֲשֶׁר אָשַׁם לוֹ.
- **"kinsman" → "redeemer"** (v8) — Hebrew גֹּאֵל, the one who has the right and the duty to redeem (Lev 25:25). **"let the trespass be recompensed unto the LORD, even to the priest" → "the guilt that is returned is Yahweh's, the priest's"** — Hebrew הָאָשָׁם הַמּוּשָׁב לַיהוָה לַכֹּהֵן, two datives side by side without "and" or "even". **"whereby an atonement shall be made for him" → "with which he makes atonement for him"** — Hebrew יְכַפֶּר, active; the subject is not named. כִּפֶּר is literally "cover".
- **"every offering" → "every contribution"** (v9) — Hebrew תְּרוּמָה, what is lifted off for Yahweh or the priest. **"which they bring" → "that they bring near"** — Hebrew הִקְרִיב.
- **"And every man's hallowed things shall be his" → "And each man, his holy things shall be his"** (v10) — Hebrew וְאִישׁ אֶת־קֳדָשָׁיו לוֹ יִהְיוּ. Whether "his" is the giver or the priest of v9 the Hebrew does not say; so also in the second half.
- **"If any man's wife go aside" → "Any man, when his wife strays"** (v12, 19, 20, 29) — Hebrew אִישׁ אִישׁ, literally "a man, a man"; שָׂטָה is "turn off the way" (only here and Prov 4:15; 7:25).
- **"lie with her carnally" → "lies with her with an emission of seed"** (v13) — Hebrew שִׁכְבַת־זֶרַע, literally "a lying of seed" (Lev 15:18). **"and be kept close" → "and she is concealed"** — Hebrew וְנִסְתְּרָה, beside נֶעְלַם "is hidden". **"neither she be taken with the manner" → "and she was not caught"** — Hebrew נִתְפָּשָׂה, "seized"; "in the act" is not in the Hebrew. אִישׁ is both "man" and "husband" in this chapter, אִשָּׁה both "woman" and "wife".
- **"she be defiled"** (v13, 14, 20, 27–29) — Hebrew נִטְמְאָה, from טָמֵא "unclean": "is defiled" or "has defiled herself"; the form allows both.
- **"the spirit of jealousy come upon him" → "a spirit of jealousy passes over him"** (v14, 30) — Hebrew עָבַר עַל. קִנֵּא אֶת is "be jealous of".
- **"barley meal"** (v15) — Hebrew קֶמַח שְׂעֹרִים; קֶמַח is ordinary meal, not the סֹלֶת "fine flour" of the grain offerings. **"an offering of jealousy" → "a grain offering of jealousies"** (v15, 18, 25, 29) — Hebrew מִנְחַת קְנָאֹת, a plural. **"an offering of memorial, bringing iniquity to remembrance"** — Hebrew מִנְחַת זִכָּרוֹן מַזְכֶּרֶת עָוֹן: the noun and the verb are of the root זָכַר "remember".
- **"set her before the LORD" → "make her stand before Yahweh"** (v16, 18, 30) — Hebrew הֶעֱמִיד, causative of "stand".
- **"in the floor of the tabernacle" → "on the floor of the dwelling"** (v17) — Hebrew הַמִּשְׁכָּן.
- **"uncover the woman's head" → "let the head of the woman go loose"** (v18) — Hebrew וּפָרַע אֶת־רֹאשׁ, as Lev 10:6; 13:45: the hair unbound, or the head bared; the verse does not decide. **"in her hands" → "on her palms"** — Hebrew כַּפֶּיהָ.
- **"the bitter water that causeth the curse" → "the water of bitterness that curses"** (v18, 19, 22–24, 27) — Hebrew מֵי הַמָּרִים הַמְאָרֲרִים, literally "the waters of the bitter ones, the cursing ones". מָרִים is the plural of מַר "bitter"; whether the water is bitter to the taste or is named from what it brings (v24, 27 "for bitterness") is not certain. מְאָרֲרִים is a participle of אָרַר "curse": "cursing", that is, carrying or bringing the curse.
- **"charge her by an oath" → "make her swear"** (v19, 21) — Hebrew הִשְׁבִּיעַ. **"gone aside to uncleanness with another instead of thy husband" → "strayed into uncleanness under your husband"** (v19, 20, 29) — Hebrew שָׂטִית טֻמְאָה תַּחַת אִישֵׁךְ. תַּחַת is "under"; whether it says "while under your husband's authority" or "in place of your husband" the word does not decide. "With another" is not in the Hebrew. **"be thou free" → "be clear"** (v19, 28, 31) — Hebrew נָקָה.
- **"some man have lain with thee beside thine husband" → "a man has given his lying in you, apart from your husband"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיִּתֵּן אִישׁ בָּךְ אֶת־שְׁכָבְתּוֹ, word for word (Lev 18:20). The sentence breaks off and is taken up by v21.
- **"an oath of cursing" → "the oath of the curse"** (v21) — Hebrew שְׁבֻעַת הָאָלָה; אָלָה is a curse called down by an oath (v23, 27). **"The LORD make thee a curse" → "May Yahweh make you a curse"** — Hebrew יִתֵּן יְהוָה אוֹתָךְ לְאָלָה, literally "give you for a curse and for an oath": a name used in cursing and swearing.
- **"when the LORD doth make thy thigh to rot, and thy belly to swell" → "makes your thigh fall and your belly swell"** (v21, 22, 27) — Hebrew בְּתֵת יְהוָה אֶת־יְרֵכֵךְ נֹפֶלֶת וְאֶת־בִּטְנֵךְ צָבָה, literally "in Yahweh's giving your thigh falling and your belly swelling". נָפַל is the plain verb "fall"; "rot" is not in the Hebrew. צָבָה is found only in this chapter and its meaning is uncertain; "swell" is the usual rendering. What happens to the body the text does not explain. In v22 the Hebrew has "belly" and "thigh" without "your".
- **"into thy bowels" → "into your inward parts"** (v22) — Hebrew מֵעַיִךְ. **"Amen, amen"** — Hebrew אָמֵן אָמֵן, "sure, firm".
- **"blot them out with the bitter water" → "blot them out into the water of bitterness"** (v23) — Hebrew וּמָחָה אֶל־מֵי הַמָּרִים; מָחָה is "wipe out"; "them" is supplied. **"in a book"** — Hebrew בַּסֵּפֶר, any written document; the Hebrew has the article.
- **"cause the woman to drink" → "give the woman to drink"** (v24, 26, 27) — Hebrew הִשְׁקָה. **"shall enter into her, and become bitter" → "shall come into her for bitterness"** (v24, 27) — Hebrew לְמָרִים.
- **"offer it upon the altar" → "bring it near to the altar"** (v25) — Hebrew הִקְרִיב. **"wave"** — Hebrew הֵנִיף.
- **"the memorial thereof, and burn it" → "its memorial portion, and turn it into smoke"** (v26) — Hebrew אַזְכָּרָה (Lev 2:2); הִקְטִיר, literally "make smoke".
- **"her thigh shall rot" → "her thigh shall fall"** (v27) — see v21. **"among her people"** — Hebrew בְּקֶרֶב; in v21 בְּתוֹךְ "in the midst of".
- **"shall conceive seed" → "shall be sown with seed"** (v28) — Hebrew וְנִזְרְעָה זָרַע, the passive of "sow" with its own noun: she will bear children.
- **"the law of jealousies" → "the torah of jealousies"** (v29, 30) — Hebrew תּוֹרָה, the instruction for one matter. **"shall execute upon her all this law" → "shall do to her all this torah"** (v30) — Hebrew וְעָשָׂה לָהּ.
- **"guiltless from iniquity" → "clear from iniquity"** (v31) — Hebrew וְנִקָּה … מֵעָוֹן.

### Notes

- **The camp** (v1–4) — three kinds of unclean persons are sent outside it, because Yahweh abides in its midst.
- **Guilt returned** (v5–8) — the passage takes up Lev 6:1–7 and adds the case where the wronged man has no redeemer: what is returned goes to the priest.
- **The torah of jealousies** (v11–31) — a husband who suspects his wife, with no witness, brings her before Yahweh; an oath, written curses wiped off into water, and a grain offering. The rite has no counterpart for a suspected husband.
- **Later practice** — the Mishnah (tractate *Sotah*) describes the rite and says that it was given up (Sotah 9:9).
