# Numbers 12

[1] Miriam and Aaron spoke against Moses because of the Cushite woman whom he had married; for he had married a Cushite woman.

[2] They said, "Has Yahweh indeed spoken only through Moses? Has he not also spoken through us?" And Yahweh heard it.

[3] (Now the man Moses was very humble, more than any person on the face of the earth.)

[4] Suddenly Yahweh said to Moses, Aaron, and Miriam, "Come out, you three, to the Tent of Meeting." So the three of them came out.

[5] Yahweh came down in the pillar of cloud and stood at the entrance of the tent, and called Aaron and Miriam; and the two of them came forward.

[6] He said, "Hear my words now: If there is a prophet of Yahweh among you, I make myself known to him in a vision; I speak to him in a dream.

[7] Not so with my servant Moses; he is faithful in all my house.

[8] With him I speak mouth to mouth, clearly and not in riddles; and he beholds the form of Yahweh. Why then were you not afraid to speak against my servant Moses?"

[9] The anger of Yahweh burned against them, and he departed.

[10] When the cloud departed from over the tent, behold, Miriam was stricken with skin disease, white as snow; and Aaron turned toward Miriam, and behold, she had skin disease.

[11] Then Aaron said to Moses, "Oh, my lord, please do not lay upon us the sin, which we have done foolishly, and in which we have sinned.

[12] Let her not be like one dead, whose flesh is half-eaten away when he comes out of his mother's womb."

[13] So Moses cried out to Yahweh, saying, "O El, please heal her."

[14] Yahweh said to Moses, "If her father had but spit in her face, would she not be shamed seven days? Let her be shut outside the camp for seven days, and after that she may be brought back in."

[15] So Miriam was shut outside the camp for seven days, and the people did not set out until Miriam was brought in again.

[16] After that the people moved from Hazeroth and encamped in the wilderness of Paran.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Ethiopian" → "Cushite"** (v1) — Hebrew כֻּשִׁית *Kushit*. Cush = region south of Egypt (roughly Nubia / modern northern Sudan), not modern Ethiopia. May refer to Zipporah (if she had Cushite ancestry) or a second wife; debated.
- **"meek" → "humble"** (v3) — Hebrew עָנָו *anav*. Lowly / humble; "meek" now carries overtones of weakness the Hebrew does not.
- **"apparently" → "clearly"** (v8) — Hebrew מַרְאֶה *mar'eh* = visibly / clearly. Archaic English *apparently* = openly.
- **"dark speeches" → "riddles"** (v8) — Hebrew חִידוֹת *chidot* = riddles / enigmas.
- **"similitude of the Lord" → "form of Yahweh"** (v8) — Hebrew תְּמוּנַת יְהוָה *temunat YHWH*. The shape / likeness of Yahweh. A rare phrase; notable theological claim.
- **"O God" → "O El"** (v13) — Hebrew אֵל *El* — short, cry-like form. Moses' brief emotive prayer.

### Notes
- **Shortest crisis in Numbers** — briefer than ch 11, more intense. Authority challenge from Moses' own siblings.
- **Miriam named first, stricken alone** — Hebrew verb *va-tedabber* is feminine singular though the subject is "Miriam and Aaron," suggesting Miriam as ringleader. Only she contracts *tsara'at*; Aaron, as priest, would be disqualified from office.
- **Cushite wife mystery** — no follow-up in the text. Possibilities: (a) Zipporah being of Midianite-Cushite ancestry; (b) a second wife taken during wilderness; (c) the real issue is envy rather than the wife itself.
- **Moses' prophetic uniqueness** (v6–8) — Yahweh distinguishes Moses from prophets generally: Moses receives direct speech, not vision/dream; sees Yahweh's "form." Deut 34:10 echoes: "no prophet has arisen since like Moses."
- **Moses' short prayer for Miriam** (v13) — five Hebrew words. Intercession despite the attack against him. Sets the pattern of his repeated intercessions in Numbers.
- **Seven days outside** (v14–15) — reduced from standard *tsara'at* quarantines (Lev 13–14); corresponds to the public-shame period. The whole nation waits for her — she is not abandoned.
