# Song of Songs 3

[1] On my bed in the nights
I sought him whom my soul loves;
I sought him, and I did not find him.

[2] Let me rise now and go around in the city,
in the streets and in the squares;
let me seek him whom my soul loves.
I sought him, and I did not find him.

[3] The watchmen found me,
who go around in the city.
"Him whom my soul loves have you seen?"

[4] Scarcely had I passed from them
when I found him whom my soul loves.
I seized him, and I would not let him go
until I had brought him to the house of my mother,
and to the chamber of her who conceived me.

[5] I make you swear, daughters of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
by the gazelles or by the does of the field,
that you do not stir up and do not awaken love
until it pleases.

[6] Who is this coming up from the wilderness
like columns of smoke,
censed with myrrh and frankincense,
from all the powders of the trader?

[7] Behold, his bed, which is ⟦solomon|Shlomo's|Solomon's⟧!
Sixty mighty ones are around it,
of the mighty ones of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[8] all of them holding the sword,
taught in war,
each man his sword upon his thigh,
because of dread in the nights.

[9] A palanquin King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ made for himself
from the trees of the ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧.

[10] Its pillars he made of silver,
its support of gold,
its seat of purple;
its inside was inlaid with love
from the daughters of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[11] Go out and look, daughters of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
upon King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧,
with the crown with which his mother crowned him
on the day of his wedding,
and on the day of the gladness of his heart.

## Changes

- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order.
- **"By night on my bed" → "On my bed in the nights"** (v1) — Hebrew עַל־מִשְׁכָּבִי בַּלֵּילוֹת, Hebrew order, plural "the nights" (night after night; also v8). מִשְׁכָּב "bed". **"I sought him, but I found him not" → "I sought him, and I did not find him"** (v1, 2) — Hebrew וְלֹא, "and not".
- **"I will rise now, and go about the city … I will seek" → "Let me rise now and go around in the city … let me seek"** (v2) — Hebrew cohortatives אָקוּמָה נָּא … וַאֲסוֹבְבָה … אֲבַקְשָׁה, with נָא "now"; סָבַב "go around" (also v3); בָעִיר "in the city". **"in the broad ways" → "in the squares"** — Hebrew רְחֹבוֹת, the open places of a town; שְׁוָקִים "streets".
- **"The watchmen that go about the city found me: to whom I said, Saw ye him whom my soul loveth?" → "The watchmen found me, who go around in the city. 'Him whom my soul loves have you seen?'"** (v3) — Hebrew הַשֹּׁמְרִים "the watchmen" (participle of שָׁמַר "keep"); "to whom I said" is not written; the Hebrew order puts "him whom my soul loves" first, and the question has no question word.
- **"It was but a little that I passed from them" → "Scarcely had I passed from them"** (v4) — Hebrew כִּמְעַט "as a little". **"I held him, and would not let him go" → "I seized him, and I would not let him go"** — Hebrew אֲחַזְתִּיו "I seized him" (perfect), וְלֹא אַרְפֶּנּוּ (imperfect: "and I would not / will not let him go"). **"into my mother's house" → "to the house of my mother"** — Hebrew בֵּית אִמִּי; הוֹרָתִי "her who conceived me" (participle of הָרָה "conceive").
- **"I charge you … till he please" → "I make you swear, daughters of Jerusalem, by the gazelles or by the does of the field, that you do not stir up and do not awaken love until it pleases"** (v5) — the same Hebrew words as 2:7, and the same wording; the oath form "if you stir up and if you awaken" is given by its meaning, as there.
- **"Who is this that cometh out of the wilderness"** (v6) — Hebrew מִי זֹאת, "who is this" (feminine): a person, not "what". עֹלָה "coming up". **"perfumed with myrrh and frankincense" → "censed with myrrh and frankincense"** — Hebrew מְקֻטֶּרֶת, passive of קָטַר "make smoke" (the verb of incense): made fragrant with their smoke. **"with all powders of the merchant" → "from all the powders of the trader"** — Hebrew מִכֹּל אַבְקַת רוֹכֵל, with מִן "from"; אַבְקָה (from אָבָק "fine dust") "powder"; רוֹכֵל "trader".
- **"Behold his bed, which is Solomon's" → "Behold, his bed, which is Solomon's!"** (v7) — Hebrew מִטָּתוֹ שֶׁלִּשְׁלֹמֹה, "his bed, that of Solomon"; מִטָּה "bed": here a bed that is carried (a litter) in a procession; the Hebrew word is "bed". **"threescore valiant men … of the valiant of Israel" → "Sixty mighty ones … of the mighty ones of Israel"** — Hebrew גִּבֹּרִים (the "mighty one" row).
- **"They all hold swords, being expert in war" → "all of them holding the sword, taught in war"** (v8) — Hebrew אֲחֻזֵי חֶרֶב, passive participle, "grasped of sword" (= holding the sword), singular "sword"; מְלֻמְּדֵי מִלְחָמָה "taught of war" (pual of לִמֵּד "teach"). **"every man hath his sword upon his thigh because of fear in the night" → "each man his sword upon his thigh, because of dread in the nights"** — Hebrew יָרֵךְ "thigh"; פַּחַד "dread"; plural "the nights".
- **"King Solomon made himself a chariot of the wood of Lebanon" → "A palanquin King Solomon made for himself from the trees of the Lebanon"** (v9) — Hebrew אַפִּרְיוֹן, only here, a loan word (from Persian or Greek: uncertain), a carried seat or bed; Hebrew order; עֲצֵי "trees, wood"; הַלְּבָנוֹן has the article.
- **"He made the pillars thereof of silver, the bottom thereof of gold, the covering of it of purple" → "Its pillars he made of silver, its support of gold, its seat of purple"** (v10) — Hebrew עַמּוּדָיו "its pillars"; רְפִידָתוֹ, only here, from רָפַד "spread, support" (2:5): the back, base or spread of the seat, uncertain; מֶרְכָּבוֹ "its seat" (lit. "what is ridden on"; "saddle" in Lev 15:9, which does not fit a palanquin); אַרְגָּמָן "purple".
- **"the midst thereof being paved with love, for the daughters of Jerusalem" → "its inside was inlaid with love from the daughters of Jerusalem"** (v10) — Hebrew תּוֹכוֹ רָצוּף אַהֲבָה מִבְּנוֹת יְרוּשָׁלִָם; רָצַף "pave, inlay" (רִצְפָּה "pavement"); אַהֲבָה "love" (some read another word, "leather", by a change of the letters: from memory; MT kept); מִן "from": by the daughters, or love coming from them (not decided).
- **"Go forth, O ye daughters of Zion, and behold king Solomon" → "Go out and look, daughters of Zion, upon King Solomon"** (v11) — Hebrew צְאֶינָה וּרְאֶינָה … בַּמֶּלֶךְ, feminine imperatives; רָאָה בְּ "look upon"; no "O". **"wherewith his mother crowned him in the day of his espousals" → "with which his mother crowned him on the day of his wedding"** — Hebrew עִטְּרָה־לּוֹ "crowned (for) him"; חֲתֻנָּה "wedding" (only here). **"and in the day of the gladness of his heart"** — Hebrew וּבְיוֹם, with "and"; שִׂמְחָה "gladness".

### Notes
- **Speakers** — the Hebrew has no speaker names: v1–5 are a woman's words (feminine "I", "my mother"); v6–11 have no marked speaker (v11 is said to the daughters of Zion). In v6 "this" is feminine: the woman, or the bed of v7 (also feminine).
