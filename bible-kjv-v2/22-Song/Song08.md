# Song of Songs 8

[1] Oh that you were like a brother to me,
who sucked the breasts of my mother!
Should I find you outside, I would kiss you;
also they would not despise me.

[2] I would lead you, I would bring you
into the house of my mother;
she would teach me.
I would give you to drink of the spiced wine,
of the juice of my pomegranate.

[3] His left is under my head,
and his right embraces me.

[4] I make you swear, daughters of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
why should you stir up and why should you awaken love
until it pleases?

[5] Who is this coming up from the wilderness,
leaning upon her beloved?

Under the apple tree I awakened you;
there your mother was in labor with you;
there she who bore you was in labor.

[6] Set me as the seal upon your heart,
as the seal upon your arm;
for strong as death is love,
hard as Sheol is jealousy;
its flames are flames of fire,
a flame of ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧.

[7] Many waters are not able to quench love,
and rivers do not overflow it.
If a man gave
all the wealth of his house for love,
they would surely despise him.

[8] We have a little sister,
and breasts she has none;
what shall we do for our sister
on the day when she is spoken for?

[9] If she is a wall,
we will build upon her a battlement of silver;
and if she is a door,
we will enclose her with a board of cedar.

[10] I am a wall,
and my breasts are like the towers;
then I was in his eyes
as one who finds ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧.

[11] ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had a vineyard at ⟦baal-hamon|Baal Hamon|Baal-hamon⟧;
he gave the vineyard to the keepers;
each would bring for its fruit a thousand of silver.

[12] My vineyard, which is mine, is before me;
the thousand is for you, ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧,
and two hundred for those who keep its fruit.

[13] You who dwell in the gardens,
comrades are giving heed to your voice;
let me hear!

[14] Flee, my beloved,
and make yourself like a gazelle
or a fawn of the deer
upon the mountains of spices.

## Changes

- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order.
- **"O that thou wert as my brother" → "Oh that you were like a brother to me"** (v1) — Hebrew מִי יִתֶּנְךָ כְּאָח לִי, lit. "Who will give you as a brother to me?" (a wish). **"that sucked the breasts of my mother" → "who sucked"** — יוֹנֵק, participle. **"when I should find thee without, I would kiss thee" → "Should I find you outside, I would kiss you"** — two imperfects side by side, no "if" or "when" written. **"yea, I should not be despised" → "also they would not despise me"** — Hebrew גַּם לֹא־יָבוּזוּ לִי, plural "they" with no subject (= one would not despise me); בּוּז "despise" (also v7).
- **"I would lead thee, and bring thee" → "I would lead you, I would bring you"** (v2) — two verbs without "and" (נִהַג "lead", row). **"who would instruct me" → "she would teach me"** — Hebrew תְּלַמְּדֵנִי (לִמֵּד "teach") can be "she would teach me" (the mother) or "you would teach me" (the man, as all the other verbs of the verse are said to "you"); the verse takes the first, as the line stands after "the house of my mother", and the second is equally possible. **"spiced wine of the juice of my pomegranate" → "to drink of the spiced wine, of the juice of my pomegranate"** — מִן "of" twice; יַיִן הָרֶקַח, lit. "the wine of the spicing" (רֹקַח "perfume", row); עָסִיס "juice, sweet wine".
- **"His left hand … his right hand" → "His left … his right"** (v3) — as 2:6: "hand" is not written; תְּחַבְּקֵנִי may also be a wish.
- **"I charge you … that ye stir not up, nor awake my love, until he please" → "I make you swear … why should you stir up and why should you awaken love until it pleases?"** (v4) — the refrain of 2:7; 3:5, with two differences that are kept: there is no "by the gazelles or by the does of the field", and the oath form אִם … וְאִם "if … and if" (= "that you do not") is here מַה … וּמַה, lit. "what … and what" (= "why should you", a question that forbids). הִשְׁבַּעְתִּי "I make you swear" (row; "adjure" as 2:7). הָאַהֲבָה "the love", with the article; "my" is not written; עַד שֶׁתֶּחְפָּץ "until it pleases" (חָפֵץ "delight, desire").
- **"Who is this that cometh up from the wilderness"** (v5) — as 3:6, "Who is this coming up from the wilderness". מִתְרַפֶּקֶת "leaning" only here. **"I raised thee up under the apple tree" → "Under the apple tree I awakened you"** — Hebrew order; עוֹרַרְתִּיךָ, the polel of v4 "awaken"; "you" is masculine. **"brought thee forth … brought thee forth" → "was in labor with you … was in labor"** — חִבֵּל "be in labor" (or "conceive"); the second line, lit. "there labored she who bore you".
- **"Set me as a seal upon thine heart" → "as the seal"** (v6) — כַחוֹתָם, with the article, twice. **"for love is strong as death; jealousy is cruel as the grave" → "for strong as death is love, hard as Sheol is jealousy"** — Hebrew order (the adjective first); עַז "strong" (row), קָשֶׁה "hard" (not "cruel"); שְׁאוֹל "Sheol" (not "the grave"); קִנְאָה "jealousy" (row; "zeal" in the sense of a burning claim). **"the coals thereof are coals of fire" → "its flames are flames of fire"** — רֶשֶׁף, lit. "flame" (row; elsewhere also "burning plague", Deut 32:24).
- **"which hath a most vehement flame" → "a flame of Yah"** (v6) — the Leningrad Codex as printed by the tool has שַׁלְהֶבֶתְ יָה, two words: "a flame of Yah", so the token `yah` is kept. Many read the letters as one word, שַׁלְהֶבֶתְיָה, in which -יָה makes a superlative ("a mighty flame", KJV "a most vehement flame"); the verse follows the printed text. The previous "the very flame" added "very". If יָה is the divine name, it is the only one in the book.
- **"Many waters cannot quench love, neither can the floods drown it" → "Many waters are not able to quench love, and rivers do not overflow it"** (v7) — יָכֹל "be able"; נְהָרוֹת "rivers"; שָׁטַף "overflow, sweep away" (another sense than the "rinse" of the Leviticus row). **"if a man would give" → "If a man gave"** — אִישׁ "man". **"it would utterly be contemned" → "they would surely despise him"** — בּוֹז יָבוּזוּ לוֹ, infinitive absolute with its verb, lit. "despising they would despise"; לוֹ "him" (or "it", the wealth).
- **"and she hath no breasts" → "and breasts she has none"** (v8) — Hebrew order. **"in the day when she shall be spoken for" → "on the day when she is spoken for"** — דִּבֶּר בְּ "speak for", of asking for a woman in marriage (row, 1 Sam 25:39).
- **"a palace of silver" → "a battlement of silver"** (v9) — טִירָה is "encampment" in its row (Gen 25:16); built "upon" a wall, a walled row on top of it is meant, so "battlement" (uncertain), kept against the row. **"if she be a door" → "and if she is a door"** — וְ "and" (was "but"). **"boards of cedar" → "a board of cedar"** — לוּחַ singular; נָצוּר עָלֶיהָ "enclose upon her" (צוּר "shut in").
- **"I am a wall, and my breasts like towers" → "I am a wall, and my breasts are like the towers"** (v10) — a clause without a verb, present (the previous "I was" was not written); כַּמִּגְדָּלוֹת with the article. **"as one that found favour" → "as one who finds shalom"** — שָׁלוֹם (reviewer row: token `shalom`); מוֹצֵאת, participle. A play on שְׁלֹמֹה (v11–12) and הַשּׁוּלַמִּית (6:13).
- **"he let out the vineyard unto keepers" → "he gave the vineyard to the keepers"** (v11) — נָתַן "give"; לַנֹּטְרִים with the article (נָטַר "keep, guard"). **"every one for the fruit thereof was to bring a thousand pieces of silver" → "each would bring for its fruit a thousand of silver"** — אִישׁ "each" (lit. "a man"); "pieces" is not written (אֶלֶף כָּסֶף "a thousand of silver"; the unit, as often, is not named).
- **"My vineyard, which is mine" — kept** (v12) — Hebrew כַּרְמִי שֶׁלִּי. **"thou, O Solomon, must have a thousand" → "the thousand is for you, Solomon"** — הָאֶלֶף, with the article; no "O". **"those that keep the fruit thereof"** — "two hundred for those who keep its fruit", הַנֹּטְרִים אֶת־פִּרְיוֹ.
- **"Thou that dwellest in the gardens, the companions hearken to thy voice: cause me to hear it" → "You who dwell in the gardens, comrades are giving heed to your voice; let me hear!"** (v13) — הַיּוֹשֶׁבֶת, feminine participle with the article (a vocative); יָשַׁב "dwell" (row). חֲבֵרִים without the article, "comrades" (Song 1–4 row, as 1:7; "companion" is kept for רַעְיָה). הִקְשִׁיב "give heed" (row). הַשְׁמִיעִינִי, "make me hear", has no object: "it" removed.
- **"Make haste, my beloved" → "Flee, my beloved"** (v14) — Hebrew בְּרַח, "flee" (row בָּרַח). **"be thou like to a roe or to a young hart upon the mountains of spices" → "make yourself like a gazelle or a fawn of the deer upon the mountains of spices"** — as 2:17: דְּמֵה־לְךָ, imperative with לְךָ; עֹפֶר הָאַיָּלִים "a fawn of the deer" (אַיָּל "deer", row); הָרֵי בְשָׂמִים "mountains of spices" (2:17 "the mountains of Bether").

### Notes
- **Who speaks** — v1–3 a woman to a man (masculine "you"); v4 to the daughters of Jerusalem; v5a a group; v5b–7 the woman to the man (masculine "you"); v8–9 a group ("we", "our sister"); v10 the woman; v11–12 a speaker who says "my vineyard" (not marked); v13 a man to a woman (feminine "you"); v14 the woman ("my beloved").
- **v6–7** — sayings on love itself (אַהֲבָה, three times): "strong as death … Many waters are not able to quench love".
- **The end** — the book ends with the call of v14, without a closing scene.
