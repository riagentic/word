# Revelation 22

[1] And he showed me a river of water of life, bright as crystal, going forth out of the throne of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and of the Lamb

[2] in the midst of its broad street. And on this side of the river and on that, a tree of life making twelve fruits, rendering its fruit each month; and the leaves of the tree are for healing of the nations.

[3] And there will no longer be any accursed thing. And the throne of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and of the Lamb will be in it, and his slaves will worship him,

[4] and they will see his face, and his name will be on their foreheads.

[5] And night will be no more, and they have no need of light of a lamp and of light of the sun, because the Lord ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will give light upon them, and they will reign unto the ages of the ages.

[6] And he said to me, "These words are faithful and true, and the Lord, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of the spirits of the prophets, sent his angel to show to his slaves the things that must come to pass shortly."

[7] "**And behold, I am coming quickly. Blessed is the one who keeps the words of the prophecy of this book.**"

[8] And I, ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, am the one hearing and seeing these things. And when I heard and saw, I fell to bow down before the feet of the angel who was showing me these things.

[9] And he says to me, "See that you do not! I am a fellow slave of you and of your brothers the prophets and of those who keep the words of this book. Bow down to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!"

[10] And he says to me, "Do not seal the words of the prophecy of this book, for the time is near.

[11] Let the one who does wrong do wrong still, and let the filthy one be made filthy still, and let the righteous one do righteousness still, and let the holy one be sanctified still."

[12] "**Behold, I am coming quickly, and my wage is with me, to repay each one as his work is.**

**[13] I am the Alpha and the Omega, the first and the last, the beginning and the end.**"

[14] Blessed are those who wash their robes, so that the right to the tree of life will be theirs and they may enter by the gates into the city.

[15] Outside are the dogs and the sorcerers and the sexually immoral and the murderers and the idolaters and everyone who has affection for and does a lie.

[16] "**I, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, sent my angel to testify these things to you for the churches. I am the root and the offspring of David, the bright morning star.**"

[17] **And the Spirit and the bride say, "Come." And let the one who hears say, "Come." And let the one who thirsts come; let the one who wants take water of life as a gift.**

[18] I testify to everyone who hears the words of the prophecy of this book: if anyone adds to them, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will add to him the plagues that are written in this book;

[19] and if anyone takes away from the words of the book of this prophecy, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will take away his part from the tree of life and out of the holy city, which are written in this book.

[20] The one who testifies these things says, "**Yes, I am coming quickly.**"

Amen. Come, Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧!

[21] The grace of the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ be with all.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 3, 5, 6, 9, 18, 19) — systematic.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v16, 20, 21) — systematic.
- **"a pure river of water of life, clear as crystal, proceeding" → "a river of water of life, bright as crystal, going forth"** (v1) — Greek ποταμὸν ὕδατος ζωῆς λαμπρὸν ὡς κρύσταλλον, without articles; λαμπρός, "bright". "Pure" is in the Byzantine text only. The Greek has "he showed" without a subject; "the angel" is not in the Greek.
- **"In the midst of the street of it" → "in the midst of its broad street."** (v2) — Greek ἐν μέσῳ τῆς πλατείας αὐτῆς; πλατεῖα, "broad street" (as 21:21). The printed text sets a stop after these words, so that they belong to the river of v1. The old manuscripts have no punctuation; the words can also begin the next sentence: "In the midst of its broad street and of the river, on this side and on that, a tree of life".
- **"on either side of the river, was there the tree of life" → "on this side of the river and on that, a tree of life"** (v2) — Greek τοῦ ποταμοῦ ἐντεῦθεν καὶ ἐκεῖθεν ξύλον ζωῆς: no verb and no article. ξύλον is "wood", here a tree, as in the Greek of Gen 2:9; one tree or the trees along both banks (Ezek 47:12).
- **"which bare twelve manner of fruits, and yielded her fruit every month" → "making twelve fruits, rendering its fruit each month"** (v2) — Greek ποιοῦν καρποὺς δώδεκα, "making twelve fruits" (twelve crops, or twelve kinds; "kinds" is not in the Greek); ἀποδιδοῦν, "rendering, giving what is due"; κατὰ μῆνα ἕκαστον.
- **"were for the healing" → "are for healing"** (v2) — the Greek has no verb; εἰς θεραπείαν, without the article.
- **"there shall be no more curse" → "there will no longer be any accursed thing"** (v3) — Greek πᾶν κατάθεμα, a thing laid under a curse (as ἀνάθεμα, "accursed").
- **"his servants shall serve him" → "his slaves will worship him"** (v3) — Greek δοῦλος, "slave" (also v6; v9 σύνδουλος); λατρεύω, the word for the service of worship given to God, "worship" or "serve". "His" and "him" are singular after "God and the Lamb".
- **"his name shall be in their foreheads" → "his name will be on their foreheads"** (v4) — Greek ἐπὶ τῶν μετώπων; the clause has no verb.
- **"there shall be no night there; and they need no candle, neither light of the sun" → "night will be no more, and they have no need of light of a lamp and of light of the sun"** (v5) — Greek ἔτι, "more" (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28); ἔχουσιν, present; λύχνος, "lamp". The verse follows φωτὸς ἡλίου, "of light of the sun" (Treg, NA28); WH and SBLGNT read φῶς ἡλίου.
- **"the Lord God giveth them light" → "the Lord *Elohim* will give light upon them"** (v5) — Greek φωτίσει ἐπʼ αὐτούς, future, with "upon" (ἐπί); φωτίζω is "illumine" in 18:1 and 21:23.
- **"for ever and ever" → "unto the ages of the ages"** (v5) — Greek εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας τῶν αἰώνων.
- **"These sayings are faithful and true" → "These words are faithful and true"** (v6) — Greek πιστοὶ καὶ ἀληθινοί, as 21:5.
- **"the Lord God of the holy prophets" → "the Lord, the *Elohim* of the spirits of the prophets"** (v6) — Greek ὁ κύριος, ὁ θεὸς τῶν πνευμάτων τῶν προφητῶν (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28).
- **"the things which must shortly be done" → "the things that must come to pass shortly"** (v6) — Greek ἃ δεῖ γενέσθαι ἐν τάχει, as 1:1.
- **"I come quickly" → "I am coming quickly"** (v7, 12, 20) — Greek ἔρχομαι ταχύ; ταχύ is "quickly", and can mean "soon". In v7 the Greek runs on from v6 with "and behold" and marks no change of speaker; the quotation marks are an interpretation.
- **"And I John saw these things, and heard them" → "And I, John, am the one hearing and seeing these things"** (v8) — Greek Κἀγὼ Ἰωάννης ὁ ἀκούων καὶ βλέπων ταῦτα: present participles, hearing first, and no verb.
- **"I fell down to worship before the feet of the angel" → "I fell to bow down before the feet of the angel"** (v8) — Greek προσκυνέω, to bow down, to prostrate oneself (also v9); many translations: "worship".
- **"Then saith he unto me, See thou do it not" → "And he says to me, 'See that you do not!'"** (v9) — Greek Ὅρα μή, as 19:10; the verb "do" is not in the Greek.
- **"He that is unjust, let him be unjust still" → "Let the one who does wrong do wrong still"** (v11) — Greek ὁ ἀδικῶν ἀδικησάτω, a verb, "do wrong" (in Revelation otherwise "harm").
- **"he which is filthy, let him be filthy still" → "let the filthy one be made filthy still"** (v11) — the verse follows ῥυπανθήτω (WH, Treg, NA28), a passive; SBLGNT prints ῥυπαρευθήτω, with the same sense.
- **"let him be righteous still … let him be holy still" → "let the righteous one do righteousness still, and let the holy one be sanctified still"** (v11) — Greek δικαιοσύνην ποιησάτω, "do righteousness" (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28); ἁγιασθήτω, passive of ἁγιάζω, "sanctify".
- **"my reward is with me, to give every man according as his work shall be" → "my wage is with me, to repay each one as his work is"** (v12) — Greek μισθός, "wage", the pay that is due (often rendered "reward"); ἀποδοῦναι, "to repay"; ἐστίν, present (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28).
- **"I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end, the first and the last" → "I am the Alpha and the Omega, the first and the last, the beginning and the end"** (v13) — the Greek order; ἐγώ stands without a verb, "am" is supplied; the Greek writes the last letter itself, Ὦ.
- **"Blessed are they that do his commandments" → "Blessed are those who wash their robes"** (v14) — the printed text reads οἱ πλύνοντες τὰς στολὰς αὐτῶν (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28); the Byzantine text reads οἱ ποιοῦντες τὰς ἐντολὰς αὐτοῦ, "those who do his commandments". στολή, "robe" (as 7:14).
- **"that they may have right to the tree of life" → "so that the right to the tree of life will be theirs"** (v14) — Greek ἵνα ἔσται ἡ ἐξουσία αὐτῶν ἐπὶ τὸ ξύλον τῆς ζωῆς, literally "so that their authority will be over the tree of life"; ἐξουσία is "authority", here the right to something. ξύλον, "wood", is the tree.
- **"For without are dogs" → "Outside are the dogs"** (v15) — Greek ἔξω οἱ κύνες: no "for" and no verb; every group has the article.
- **"whoremongers … whosoever loveth and maketh a lie" → "the sexually immoral … everyone who has affection for and does a lie"** (v15) — Greek πόρνος; φιλῶν, from φιλέω, "have affection for" (not ἀγαπάω "love"); ποιῶν ψεῦδος, "doing a lie".
- **"I Jesus have sent mine angel to testify unto you these things in the churches" → "I, *Yeshua*, sent my angel to testify these things to you for the churches"** (v16) — Greek ἔπεμψα, aorist; ὑμῖν, "to you", plural; ἐπὶ ταῖς ἐκκλησίαις, "over, for, in the matter of the churches".
- **"the root and the offspring of David" kept** (v16) — Greek ἡ ῥίζα καὶ τὸ γένος Δαυίδ; γένος is "race, family, descent".
- **"the bright and morning star" → "the bright morning star"** (v16) — Greek ὁ ἀστὴρ ὁ λαμπρός, ὁ πρωϊνός, literally "the star, the bright one, the morning one"; no "and" (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28).
- **"the bride" kept** (v17) — Greek νύμφη, as 21:2, 9. "Come" (Ἔρχου) is singular, the same word as "Come, Lord" in v20.
- **"let him that is athirst come. And whosoever will, let him take the water of life freely" → "let the one who thirsts come; let the one who wants take water of life as a gift"** (v17) — Greek ὁ θέλων, "the one who wants"; ὕδωρ ζωῆς, without the article; δωρεάν, "as a gift, for nothing" (as 21:6).
- **"For I testify" → "I testify"** (v18) — Greek Μαρτυρῶ ἐγώ, with a stressed "I" and no "for" (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28); not "I warn".
- **"If any man shall add unto these things, God shall add unto him" kept in sense** (v18) — Greek ἐπιθῇ ἐπʼ αὐτά … ἐπιθήσει … ἐπʼ αὐτόν, one verb, "lay upon": "if anyone lays more upon them, God will lay upon him". τὰς γεγραμμένας, "that are written".
- **"God shall take away his part out of the book of life" → "*Elohim* will take away his part from the tree of life"** (v19) — the printed text reads ἀπὸ τοῦ ξύλου τῆς ζωῆς, "from the tree of life" (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28); "book of life" has no support in the editions. μέρος, "part" (as 20:6; 21:8).
- **"and from the things which are written in this book" → "which are written in this book"** (v19) — Greek τῶν γεγραμμένων ἐν τῷ βιβλίῳ τούτῳ, a genitive with no "and": "the things written in this book", said of the tree and the city.
- **"He which testifieth these things saith, Surely I come quickly" → "The one who testifies these things says, 'Yes, I am coming quickly.'"** (v20) — Greek Ναί, "Yes".
- **"Amen. Even so, come, Lord Jesus" → "Amen. Come, Lord *Yeshua*!"** (v20) — Greek Ἀμήν· ἔρχου, κύριε Ἰησοῦ (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28); the Byzantine text has a second "Yes".
- **"The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen." → "The grace of the Lord *Yeshua* be with all."** (v21) — the editions differ: τοῦ κυρίου Ἰησοῦ, "of the Lord Yeshua" (SBLGNT, Treg, NA28; WH adds Χριστοῦ); μετὰ πάντων, "with all" (SBLGNT, NA28), against μετὰ τῶν ἁγίων, "with the saints" (WH, Treg); the Byzantine text reads "with all the saints. Amen." On a tie NA28 decides. The printed text has no "Amen"; "be" is supplied.
