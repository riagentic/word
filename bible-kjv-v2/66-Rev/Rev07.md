# Revelation 7

[1] After this I saw four angels standing at the four corners of the earth, holding back the four winds of the earth, that no wind might blow on earth or sea or against any tree.

[2] Then I saw another angel ascending from the rising of the sun, with the seal of the living ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and he called with a loud voice to the four angels who had been given power to harm earth and sea,

[3] saying, "Do not harm the earth or the sea or the trees, until we have sealed the servants of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ on their foreheads."

[4] And I heard the number of the sealed, 144,000, sealed from every tribe of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:

[5] 12,000 from the tribe of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ were sealed,
12,000 from the tribe of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧,
12,000 from the tribe of Gad,
[6] 12,000 from the tribe of Asher,
12,000 from the tribe of ⟦naphtali|Naftali|Naphtali⟧,
12,000 from the tribe of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧,
[7] 12,000 from the tribe of ⟦simeon|Shimon|Simeon⟧,
12,000 from the tribe of Levi,
12,000 from the tribe of ⟦issachar|Yissachar|Issachar⟧,
[8] 12,000 from the tribe of ⟦zebulun|Zevulun|Zebulun⟧,
12,000 from the tribe of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧,
12,000 from the tribe of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ were sealed.

[9] After this I looked, and behold, a great multitude that no one could number, from every nation, from all tribes and peoples and languages, standing before the throne and before the Lamb, clothed in white robes, with palm branches in their hands,

[10] and crying out with a loud voice, "Salvation belongs to our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ who sits on the throne, and to the Lamb!"

[11] And all the angels were standing around the throne and around the elders and the four living creatures, and they fell on their faces before the throne and worshiped ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[12] saying, "Amen! Blessing and glory and wisdom and thanksgiving and honor and power and might be to our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ forever and ever! Amen."

[13] Then one of the elders addressed me, saying, "Who are these, clothed in white robes, and from where have they come?"

[14] I said to him, "Sir, you know." And he said to me, "These are the ones coming out of the great tribulation. They have washed their robes and made them white in the blood of the Lamb.

[15] Therefore they are before the throne of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and serve him day and night in his temple;
and he who sits on the throne will shelter them with his presence.
[16] **They shall hunger no more, neither thirst anymore;
the sun shall not strike them,
nor any scorching heat.
[17] For the Lamb in the midst of the throne will be their shepherd,
and he will guide them to springs of living water,
and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will wipe away every tear from their eyes**."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"an hundred and forty and four thousand" → "144,000"** (v4) — numerals used throughout for readability.
- **"twelve thousand" → "12,000"** (v5-8) — modernized numeral format.
- **"a great multitude, which no man could number" → "a great multitude that no one could number"** (v9) — modernized.
- **"of all nations, and kindreds, and people, and tongues" → "from every nation, from all tribes and peoples and languages"** (v9) — "kindreds" → "tribes"; "tongues" → "languages"; modernized.
- **"These are they which came out of great tribulation" → "These are the ones coming out of the great tribulation"** (v14) — modernized.
- **"have washed their robes … in the blood of the Lamb" → "They have washed their robes and made them white in the blood of the Lamb"** (v14) — unchanged; vivid paradox preserved.
- **"shall hunger no more, neither thirst any more" → "They shall hunger no more, neither thirst anymore"** (v16) — OT allusion (Isa 49:10); modernized.
- **"he that sitteth on the throne shall dwell among them" → "he who sits on the throne will shelter them with his presence"** (v15) — Greek σκηνώσει ἐπ᾽ αὐτούς *skēnōsei ep' autous* = "will tent/tabernacle over them / shelter them"; echoes the divine *Shekinah* (dwelling presence); "dwell among them" is adequate but "shelter them with his presence" captures the tabernacle imagery.
- **"God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes" → "*Elohim* will wipe away every tear from their eyes"** (v17) — OT allusion (Isa 25:8); "God" → "*Elohim*."

### Notes
- **v16-17** echoes Isa 49:10; 25:8.
