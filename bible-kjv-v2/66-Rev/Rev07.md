# Revelation 7

[1] After this I saw four angels standing on the four corners of the earth, holding the four winds of the earth, so that no wind should blow on the earth nor on the sea nor on any tree.

[2] And I saw another angel ascending from the rising of the sun, having a seal of the living ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and he cried out with a loud voice to the four angels to whom it was given to harm the earth and the sea,

[3] saying, "Do not harm the earth nor the sea nor the trees, until we have sealed the slaves of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ on their foreheads."

[4] And I heard the number of the sealed, a hundred and forty-four thousand, sealed out of every tribe of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:

[5] out of the tribe of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ twelve thousand sealed,
out of the tribe of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧ twelve thousand,
out of the tribe of Gad twelve thousand,
[6] out of the tribe of Asher twelve thousand,
out of the tribe of ⟦naphtali|Naftali|Naphtali⟧ twelve thousand,
out of the tribe of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ twelve thousand,
[7] out of the tribe of ⟦simeon|Shimon|Simeon⟧ twelve thousand,
out of the tribe of Levi twelve thousand,
out of the tribe of ⟦issachar|Yissachar|Issachar⟧ twelve thousand,
[8] out of the tribe of ⟦zebulun|Zevulun|Zebulun⟧ twelve thousand,
out of the tribe of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ twelve thousand,
out of the tribe of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ twelve thousand sealed.

[9] After these things I saw, and behold, a great crowd, which no one was able to number, out of every nation and tribes and peoples and tongues, standing before the throne and before the Lamb, clothed in white robes, and palms in their hands;

[10] and they cry out with a loud voice, saying, "The salvation to our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the one sitting on the throne, and to the Lamb!"

[11] And all the angels stood around the throne and the elders and the four living creatures, and they fell before the throne on their faces and bowed down to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[12] saying, "Amen! The blessing and the glory and the wisdom and the thanksgiving and the honor and the power and the strength to our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ unto the ages of the ages! Amen."

[13] And one of the elders answered, saying to me, "These who are clothed in the white robes, who are they, and from where did they come?"

[14] And I said to him, "My lord, you know." And he said to me, "These are the ones coming out of the great affliction, and they washed their robes and made them white in the blood of the Lamb.

[15] Because of this they are before the throne of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and they worship him day and night in his sanctuary;
and the one sitting on the throne will tent over them.
[16] **They will not hunger any more, nor thirst any more,
nor shall the sun by any means fall upon them,
nor any burning heat;
[17] because the Lamb in the midst of the throne will shepherd them,
and will guide them to springs of waters of life,
and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will wipe away every tear out of their eyes**."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"And after these things" → "After this"** (v1) — Greek Μετὰ τοῦτο (SBLGNT, WH, NA28); Treg and RP begin with Καί "And". Verse 9 has the plural Μετὰ ταῦτα, "After these things".
- **"holding" kept** (v1) — Greek κρατοῦντας, "holding, gripping"; "back" is not in the Greek.
- **"nor on any tree"** (v1) — Greek ἐπὶ πᾶν δένδρον (SBLGNT, WH, NA28), "on every / any tree"; Treg and RP read ἐπί τι δένδρον. The case changes: "earth" and "sea" stand in the genitive, "tree" in the accusative.
- **"the seal of the living God" → "a seal of the living *Elohim*"** (v2) — Greek σφραγῖδα θεοῦ ζῶντος, no article with any of the three words.
- **"to whom it was given to hurt" → "to whom it was given to harm"** (v2) — Greek οἷς ἐδόθη αὐτοῖς, literally "to whom it was given to them": the pronoun repeats the relative, a Semitic turn that English cannot carry; no "power" in the Greek. ἀδικέω "harm", as 6:6.
- **"the servants of our God" → "the slaves of our *Elohim*"** (v3) — Greek δούλους.
- **"an hundred and forty and four thousand" → "a hundred and forty-four thousand"**, **"twelve thousand" kept** (v4–8) — Greek ἑκατὸν τεσσεράκοντα τέσσαρες χιλιάδες, δώδεκα χιλιάδες; the numbers are written out as in the Greek. Twelve times twelve thousand.
- **"of all the tribes" → "out of every tribe"** (v4) — Greek ἐκ πάσης φυλῆς, singular. "Sealed" (ἐσφραγισμένοι) stands in the nominative after the genitive "of the sealed"; it is written with the first and the last tribe only (v5, 8).
- **the tribes** (v5–8) — the order and the names are those of the Greek: Judah first; Manasseh and Joseph both stand in the list; Dan and Ephraim are not named.
- **"a great multitude, which no man could number" → "a great crowd, which no one was able to number"** (v9) — Greek ὄχλος πολύς (ὄχλος "crowd" throughout); ὃν ἀριθμῆσαι αὐτὸν οὐδεὶς ἐδύνατο, literally "which no one was able to number it" (the repeated pronoun as in v2).
- **"of all nations, and kindreds, and people, and tongues" → "out of every nation and tribes and peoples and tongues"** (v9) — Greek ἐκ παντὸς ἔθνους καὶ φυλῶν καὶ λαῶν καὶ γλωσσῶν: "every" stands once, with the singular "nation"; the other three are plurals. γλῶσσα is "tongue".
- **"stood before the throne … clothed with white robes, and palms in their hands" → "standing before the throne … clothed in white robes, and palms in their hands"** (v9) — the Greek changes case: ἑστῶτες (nominative), περιβεβλημένους (accusative), then φοίνικες (nominative) with no verb. φοίνικες are "palms"; "branches" is not in the Greek.
- **"and cried" → "and they cry out"** (v10) — Greek κράζουσι, present tense.
- **"Salvation to our God which sitteth upon the throne" → "The salvation to our *Elohim*, the one sitting on the throne"** (v10) — Greek Ἡ σωτηρία τῷ θεῷ ἡμῶν, with the article and no verb: the salvation is (or: be ascribed) to him; "belongs" is not in the Greek.
- **"worshipped God" → "bowed down to *Elohim*"** (v11) — Greek προσεκύνησαν, "bowed down, did obeisance" (commonly "worshiped"); "worship" in v15 is a different verb.
- **"Blessing, and glory …" → "The blessing and the glory …"** (v12) — each of the seven nouns has the article in Greek (ἡ εὐλογία καὶ ἡ δόξα …); no verb. "might" → "strength" (ἰσχύς); "power" is δύναμις.
- **"for ever and ever" → "unto the ages of the ages"** (v12) — Greek εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας τῶν αἰώνων.
- **"What are these which are arrayed in white robes?" → "These who are clothed in the white robes, who are they"** (v13) — Greek word order kept (Οὗτοι … τίνες εἰσίν); the robes have the article (those of v9). "answered" is kept (ἀπεκρίθη), though no question was put.
- **"Sir" → "My lord"** (v14) — Greek Κύριέ μου. "I said" is a perfect in Greek, εἴρηκα (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28; RP εἶπον), used as a narrative past.
- **"These are they which came out of great tribulation, and have washed" → "These are the ones coming out of the great affliction, and they washed"** (v14) — Greek οἱ ἐρχόμενοι, present participle; τῆς θλίψεως τῆς μεγάλης with the article twice; θλῖψις is "affliction" throughout; ἔπλυναν … ἐλεύκαναν are aorists.
- **"Therefore" → "Because of this"** (v15) — Greek διὰ τοῦτο.
- **"serve him day and night in his temple" → "worship him day and night in his sanctuary"** (v15) — Greek λατρεύουσιν, the verb of service given to God ("serve God"); ναός *naos*, the sanctuary itself (the temple building, not the whole precinct).
- **"he that sitteth on the throne shall dwell among them" → "the one sitting on the throne will tent over them"** (v15) — Greek σκηνώσει ἐπʼ αὐτούς *skēnōsei ep' autous*, "will pitch his tent over them" (as John 1:14 "tented among us"); the word recalls the tent of meeting. "Shelter them with his presence" is an interpretation.
- **"neither shall the sun light on them, nor any heat" → "nor shall the sun by any means fall upon them, nor any burning heat"** (v16) — Greek οὐδὲ μὴ πέσῃ, the strong negation; πίπτω "fall"; καῦμα *kauma* "burning heat". Echo of Isa 49:10.
- **"shall feed them, and shall lead them unto living fountains of waters" → "will shepherd them, and will guide them to springs of waters of life"** (v17) — Greek ποιμανεῖ "will shepherd" (the Lamb as shepherd); ὁδηγήσει "will guide"; ἐπὶ ζωῆς πηγὰς ὑδάτων, literally "to life's springs of waters": "life" is a noun in the genitive, set first. RP has both verbs in the present.
- **"God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes" → "*Elohim* will wipe away every tear out of their eyes"** (v17) — Greek πᾶν δάκρυον, singular; ἐκ "out of". Echo of Isa 25:8.

### Notes
- **v16-17** echoes Isa 49:10; 25:8.
- **v1, 3** — "the earth" (γῆ) may also be read "the land".
