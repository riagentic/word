# Revelation 5

[1] And I saw upon the right hand of the one sitting on the throne a book written within and on the back, sealed up with seven seals.

[2] And I saw a strong angel proclaiming in a loud voice, "Who is worthy to open the book and to loose its seals?"

[3] And no one was able, in heaven or on the earth or under the earth, to open the book or to look at it.

[4] And I was weeping much, because no one was found worthy to open the book or to look at it.

[5] And one of the elders says to me, "Do not weep; behold, **the Lion who is of the tribe of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, the Root of David, has overcome**, to open the book and its seven seals."

[6] And I saw in the midst of the throne and of the four living creatures, and in the midst of the elders, a Lamb standing as slaughtered, having seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven spirits of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ sent out into all the earth.

[7] And he came and has taken it out of the right hand of the one sitting on the throne.

[8] And when he took the book, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell before the Lamb, each having a harp, and golden bowls full of incense, which are the prayers of the saints.

[9] And they sing a new song, saying,

"**Worthy are you to take the book
and to open its seals,
for you were slaughtered, and you bought for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in your blood
out of every tribe and tongue and people and nation,
[10] and you made them a kingdom and priests to our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and they reign upon the earth**."

[11] And I saw, and I heard the voice of many angels around the throne and the living creatures and the elders, and their number was myriads of myriads and thousands of thousands,

[12] saying with a loud voice,

"**Worthy is the Lamb who was slaughtered
to receive the power and riches and wisdom and strength
and honor and glory and blessing.**"

[13] And every creature which is in heaven and on the earth and under the earth and on the sea, and all the things in them, I heard saying,

"**To the one sitting on the throne and to the Lamb
be the blessing and the honor and the glory and the might
unto the ages of the ages.**"

[14] And the four living creatures were saying, "Amen." And the elders fell and bowed down.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v6, 9, 10) — systematic.
- **"in the right hand of him that sat on the throne" → "upon the right hand of the one sitting on the throne"** (v1, 7) — Greek ἐπὶ τὴν δεξιάν, "upon"; τοῦ καθημένου, a present participle.
- **"a book written within and on the backside, sealed" kept as "book"** (v1–9) — Greek βιβλίον, a book in the form of a roll ("scroll"), written on both sides; κατεσφραγισμένον, "sealed up" (ὄπισθεν "on the back" WH Treg NA28; RP "outside").
- **"a strong angel proclaiming with a loud voice" kept** (v2) — Greek ἰσχυρόν, κηρύσσοντα.
- **"to loose the seals thereof" kept** (v2) — Greek λῦσαι, "loose", the verb of 1:5; not "break".
- **"neither to look thereon" → "or to look at it"** (v3, 4) — Greek βλέπειν αὐτό, "to see it, look at it"; not "into it".
- **"I wept much" → "I was weeping much"** (v4) — Greek ἔκλαιον, imperfect; "I" (ἐγώ) WH Treg RP, lacking in NA28.
- **"saith unto me, Weep not" → "says to me, Do not weep"** (v5) — Greek λέγει, present.
- **"hath prevailed to open the book" → "has overcome, to open the book"** (v5) — Greek ἐνίκησεν … ἀνοῖξαι: the verb of "the one who overcomes" (chs. 2–3; 3:21), followed by a plain infinitive; "so that he can" is not in the Greek. "The Lion who is of the tribe": ὁ λέων ὁ ἐκ τῆς φυλῆς.
- **"in the midst of the throne and of the four beasts, and in the midst of the elders" kept** (v6) — Greek ἐν μέσῳ … καὶ ἐν μέσῳ; the place is "in the midst", not defined more closely.
- **"stood a Lamb as it had been slain" → "a Lamb standing as slaughtered"** (v6, 9, 12) — Greek ἀρνίον ἑστηκὸς ὡς ἐσφαγμένον; ἀρνίον is the word of John 21:15 ("lambs"), in this book always of the Mashiach; σφάζω is "slaughter", the word for killing a sacrifice.
- **"having seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven Spirits of God sent forth" kept** (v6) — ἔχων "having", οἵ "which" and ἀπεσταλμένοι "sent out" are masculine, although "Lamb", "eyes" and "spirits" are not: the grammar follows the person (WH Treg NA28; RP neuter forms).
- **"he came and took the book" → "he came and has taken it"** (v7) — Greek ἦλθεν καὶ εἴληφεν, an aorist and then a perfect; the Greek names no object ("it" is supplied).
- **"fell down before the Lamb, having every one of them harps, and golden vials full of odours" → "fell before the Lamb, each having a harp, and golden bowls full of incense"** (v8) — Greek κιθάραν, singular; φιάλας, shallow bowls; θυμιαμάτων, plural.
- **"they sung a new song" → "they sing a new song"** (v9) — Greek ᾄδουσιν, present.
- **"thou wast slain, and hast redeemed us to God by thy blood" → "you were slaughtered, and you bought for *Elohim* in your blood"** (v9) — Greek ἠγόρασας *ēgorasas*, "you bought" (a market word); ἐν τῷ αἵματί σου, "in your blood" (ἐν may be "by, at the price of"). The verb has no object in WH NA28 (the ones bought are "out of every tribe …"); Treg RP add "us" (ἡμᾶς). Nothing is supplied.
- **"out of every kindred, and tongue, and people, and nation" → "out of every tribe and tongue and people and nation"** (v9) — Greek φυλή, γλῶσσα, λαός, ἔθνος.
- **"hast made us unto our God kings and priests: and we shall reign" → "you made them a kingdom and priests to our *Elohim*, and they reign"** (v10) — Greek αὐτούς "them"; βασιλείαν "a kingdom" WH Treg NA28 (RP "kings"), cf. 1:6; βασιλεύουσιν "they reign" (present) WH Treg; βασιλεύσουσιν "they will reign" NA28 RP.
- **"I beheld, and I heard" → "I saw, and I heard"** (v11) — Greek εἶδον.
- **"ten thousand times ten thousand" → "myriads of myriads and thousands of thousands"** (v11) — Greek μυριάδες μυριάδων καὶ χιλιάδες χιλιάδων *myriades myriadōn kai chiliades chiliadōn*; preserving the Greek numerical expressions.
- **"to receive power, and riches, and wisdom, and strength" → "to receive the power and riches and wisdom and strength"** (v12) — Greek δύναμις, πλοῦτος, σοφία, ἰσχύς; one article for all seven nouns. "Saying" (λέγοντες) does not agree in case with "angels": the grammar is loose.
- **"every creature which is in heaven" kept** (v13) — Greek κτίσμα, "created thing"; ἐπὶ τῆς θαλάσσης, "on the sea". "Saying" (λέγοντας) is masculine plural after the neuter "every creature".
- **"Blessing, and honour, and glory, and power" → "the blessing and the honor and the glory and the might"** (v13) — Greek κράτος "might", a different word from "power" (δύναμις) and "strength" (ἰσχύς) in v12; each noun has the article; "be" is supplied. "On the throne": τῷ θρόνῳ NA28; τοῦ θρόνου WH Treg RP (no difference in English).
- **"for ever and ever" → "unto the ages of the ages"** (v13) — Greek εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας τῶν αἰώνων; "Amen" at the end of v13 is RP only.
- **"the four beasts said, Amen" → "the four living creatures were saying, Amen"** (v14) — Greek ἔλεγον, imperfect.
- **"fell down and worshipped him that liveth for ever and ever" → "fell and bowed down"** (v14) — Greek ἔπεσαν καὶ προσεκύνησαν; the verse ends there in the editions. "Four and twenty" is not in the editions here.

### Notes
- **v5** echoes Gen 49:9 + Isa 11:10.
