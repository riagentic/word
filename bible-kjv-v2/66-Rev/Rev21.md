# Revelation 21

[1] And I saw **a new heaven and a new earth**; for the first heaven and the first earth went away, and the sea is no more.

[2] And the holy city, **new ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧**, I saw coming down out of heaven from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, made ready as a bride adorned for her husband.

[3] And I heard a loud voice out of the throne, saying, "**Behold, the tent of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is with men, and he will tent with them, and they will be his peoples, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ himself will be with them,**

**[4] and he will wipe away every tear out of their eyes, and death will be no more; neither mourning nor crying nor pain will be any more, because the first things went away.**"

[5] And the one sitting on the throne said, "**Behold, I make all things new**." And he says, "Write, because these words are faithful and true."

[6] And he said to me, "**They have come to pass. I am the Alpha and the Omega, the beginning and the end. I will give to the one who thirsts from the spring of the water of life as a gift.**

[7] The one who overcomes will inherit these things, and **I will be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to him and he will be a son to me**.

[8] But as for the cowardly and unbelievers and the abominable and murderers and the sexually immoral and sorcerers and idolaters and all the false, their part is in the lake that burns with fire and sulfur, which is the second death."

[9] And there came one of the seven angels who have the seven bowls, full of the seven last plagues, and he spoke with me, saying, "Come, I will show you the bride, the wife of the Lamb."

[10] And he carried me away in the Spirit onto a great and high mountain, and showed me the holy city ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ coming down out of heaven from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[11] having the glory of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; its luminary is like a most precious stone, as a jasper stone shining as crystal;

[12] having a great and high wall, having twelve gates, and at the gates twelve angels, and names written on them, which are the names of the twelve tribes of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;

[13] on the east three gates, and on the north three gates, and on the south three gates, and on the west three gates;

[14] and the wall of the city has twelve foundations, and on them twelve names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb.

[15] And the one speaking with me had a measure, a golden reed, so that he might measure the city and its gates and its wall.

[16] And the city lies foursquare, and its length is as much as the width. And he measured the city with the reed, at twelve thousand stadia; its length and width and height are equal.

[17] And he measured its wall, a hundred and forty-four cubits, a measure of a man, which is of an angel.

[18] And the material of its wall is jasper, and the city is pure gold, like pure glass.

[19] The foundations of the wall of the city are adorned with every precious stone: the first foundation jasper, the second sapphire, the third chalcedony, the fourth emerald,

[20] the fifth sardonyx, the sixth sardius, the seventh chrysolite, the eighth beryl, the ninth topaz, the tenth chrysoprase, the eleventh jacinth, the twelfth amethyst;

[21] and the twelve gates are twelve pearls; each one of the gates was of one pearl. And the broad street of the city is pure gold, as transparent glass.

[22] And a sanctuary I did not see in it, for the Lord ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the Almighty, is its sanctuary, and the Lamb.

[23] And the city has no need of the sun nor of the moon, that they should shine on it, for the glory of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ illumined it, and its lamp is the Lamb.

[24] And the nations will walk through its light; and the kings of the earth bring their glory into it;

[25] and its gates will by no means be shut by day, for there will be no night there;

[26] and they will bring the glory and the honor of the nations into it.

[27] And there will by no means enter into it anything common, nor the one who does abomination and a lie, but only those who are written in the book of life of the Lamb.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"were passed away" / "are passed away" → "went away"** (v1, 4) — Greek ἀπῆλθαν, the aorist of ἀπέρχομαι, "go away", both times.
- **"there was no more sea" → "the sea is no more"** (v1) — Greek οὐκ ἔστιν ἔτι, present tense.
- **"And I John saw the holy city" → "And the holy city, new Jerusalem, I saw"** (v2) — the name "John" is not in the text of the editions; the object stands first in the Greek. Ἰερουσαλὴμ καινήν has no article.
- **"prepared" → "made ready"** (v2) — Greek ἑτοιμάζω, as 19:7.
- **"a great voice out of heaven" → "a loud voice out of the throne"** (v3) — the printed text reads ἐκ τοῦ θρόνου (SBLGNT, WH, NA28); Treg reads ἐκ τοῦ οὐρανοῦ, "out of heaven".
- **"the tabernacle of God is with men, and he will dwell with them" → "the tent of *Elohim* is with men, and he will tent with them"** (v3) — Greek ἡ σκηνή, "tent" (the word for the tent in the wilderness); σκηνώσει, the verb of the same word, as John 1:14. No verb stands in the first clause; "is" is supplied (Ezek 37:27).
- **"they shall be his people" → "they will be his peoples"** (v3) — Greek λαοί, plural (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28); the Byzantine text has the singular.
- **"God himself shall be with them, and be their God" → "*Elohim* himself will be with them"** (v3) — the printed text ends with μετʼ αὐτῶν ἔσται (SBLGNT, WH, Treg); NA28 adds αὐτῶν θεός, "as their God".
- **"God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes" → "he will wipe away every tear out of their eyes"** (v4) — the Greek has no "God" here (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28); πᾶν δάκρυον, singular; ἐκ, "out of".
- **"neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain" → "neither mourning nor crying nor pain will be any more"** (v4) — Greek πένθος, "mourning" (as 18:7); κραυγή, an outcry; πόνος, "pain" here and in 16:10–11, elsewhere "toil".
- **"for the former things are passed away" → "because the first things went away"** (v4) — the verse follows ὅτι, "because" (Treg, NA28); WH and SBLGNT have no ὅτι: "The first things went away." τὰ πρῶτα, "the first things", the same word as "the first heaven" in v1.
- **"he that sat upon the throne" → "the one sitting on the throne"** (v5) — Greek ὁ καθήμενος, a present participle.
- **"he said unto me, Write: for these words are true and faithful" → "he says, 'Write, because these words are faithful and true'"** (v5) — Greek λέγει, present, without "to me" (SBLGNT, WH, NA28; Treg adds μοι); πιστοὶ καὶ ἀληθινοί, "faithful and true", the two words of 19:11. ὅτι can also only introduce what is to be written: "Write: These words are faithful and true."
- **"It is done" → "They have come to pass"** (v6) — Greek Γέγοναν, third person plural: "they have come to pass", "they are done" — the words, or all these things. The verse follows Γέγοναν. ἐγώ εἰμι, "I am" (Treg, NA28); WH and SBLGNT read ἐγώ without εἰμι; the Byzantine text reads Γέγονα, "I have become".
- **"Alpha and Omega" → "the Alpha and the Omega"** (v6) — Greek τὸ Ἄλφα καὶ τὸ Ὦ, with the article; the Greek writes the last letter itself, Ὦ.
- **"I will give unto him that is athirst of the fountain of the water of life freely" → "I will give to the one who thirsts from the spring of the water of life as a gift"** (v6) — Greek πηγή, "spring"; δωρεάν, "as a gift, for nothing" (also 22:17). ἐγώ, "I", is stressed.
- **"He that overcometh shall inherit all things" → "The one who overcomes will inherit these things"** (v7) — Greek νικάω, "overcome"; ταῦτα, "these things" (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28).
- **"I will be his God, and he shall be my son" → "I will be *Elohim* to him and he will be a son to me"** (v7) — Greek ἔσομαι αὐτῷ θεὸς καὶ αὐτὸς ἔσται μοι υἱός, with datives (2 Sam 7:14).
- **"the fearful, and unbelieving, and the abominable" → "the cowardly and unbelievers and the abominable"** (v8) — Greek δειλός, "cowardly"; ἄπιστος, "unbeliever" (it can also mean "unfaithful"); ἐβδελυγμένοις, a perfect participle, "those who have been made abominable" (βδέλυγμα, "abomination", v27). The Byzantine text adds "and sinners".
- **"whoremongers, and sorcerers, and idolaters, and all liars" → "the sexually immoral and sorcerers and idolaters and all the false"** (v8) — Greek πόρνος; φάρμακος, one who uses drugs and spells (as φαρμακεία, 18:23); τοῖς ψευδέσιν, the adjective "false", not the noun "liar" (ψεύστης).
- **"shall have their part" → "their part is"** (v8) — Greek τὸ μέρος αὐτῶν, "their part" (as 20:6); the Greek has no verb.
- **"the seven vials full of the seven last plagues" → "the seven bowls, full of the seven last plagues"** (v9) — Greek φιάλη, "bowl"; τῶν ἐχόντων, "who have", a present participle. "Full" (τῶν γεμόντων) stands in the genitive, the case of "angels", though the sense joins it to the bowls.
- **"talked with me" → "spoke with me"** (v9, 15) — Greek λαλέω μετά.
- **"the bride, the Lamb's wife" → "the bride, the wife of the Lamb"** (v9) — Greek τὴν νύμφην τὴν γυναῖκα τοῦ ἀρνίου: νύμφη, "bride"; γυνή, "woman, wife" (as 19:7).
- **"in the spirit" → "in the Spirit"** (v10) — Greek ἐν πνεύματι, without the article (as 1:10; 4:2; 17:3); it can also be read "in spirit".
- **"that great city, the holy Jerusalem" → "the holy city Jerusalem"** (v10) — "great" is in the Byzantine text only.
- **"her light was like unto a stone most precious, even like a jasper stone, clear as crystal" → "its luminary is like a most precious stone, as a jasper stone shining as crystal"** (v11) — Greek φωστήρ, a light-giving body, a luminary (as Phil 2:15), not φῶς, "light"; ὅμοιος, "like"; ὡς, "as"; κρυσταλλίζοντι, a participle, "being as crystal". τίμιος, "precious". The Greek has no verb.
- **"had a wall … had twelve gates" → "having … having"** (v12) — Greek ἔχουσα, a participle, twice. πυλών is a gateway, the gate-building of a city.
- **"names written thereon, which are the names of the twelve tribes" kept** (v12) — the verse follows ἅ ἐστιν τὰ ὀνόματα, "which are the names" (Treg, NA28); WH and SBLGNT have no second "the names".
- **"On the east three gates" kept** (v13) — Greek ἀπὸ ἀνατολῆς, literally "from the east", the Greek way of naming a side; so with each direction. The order in the Greek is east, north, south, west.
- **"had twelve foundations, and in them the names" → "has twelve foundations, and on them twelve names"** (v14) — Greek ἔχων, a participle; ἐπʼ αὐτῶν, "on them"; δώδεκα ὀνόματα, "twelve names" (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28).
- **"a golden reed to measure" → "a measure, a golden reed, so that he might measure"** (v15) — Greek μέτρον κάλαμον χρυσοῦν; μέτρον, "a measure" (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28), is the same word as "measure" in v17.
- **"the length is as large as the breadth" → "its length is as much as the width"** (v16) — Greek τὸ μῆκος αὐτῆς ὅσον τὸ πλάτος.
- **"twelve thousand furlongs" → "at twelve thousand stadia"** (v16) — the verse follows ἐπὶ σταδίων δώδεκα χιλιάδων (WH, NA28); Treg and SBLGNT read σταδίους, the same sense. A stadion is about 185 m; the measure stays as the Greek has it.
- **"an hundred and forty and four cubits, according to the measure of a man, that is, of the angel" → "a hundred and forty-four cubits, a measure of a man, which is of an angel"** (v17) — Greek μέτρον ἀνθρώπου, ὅ ἐστιν ἀγγέλου, without articles: the measure a man uses, which is the one the angel uses. The Greek does not say whether the height or the thickness of the wall is measured. A cubit is about 45 cm.
- **"the building of the wall of it was of jasper" → "the material of its wall is jasper"** (v18) — Greek ἡ ἐνδώμησις, that which is built in, the structure or material; the Greek has no verb here or in v19, 21b ("was" stands in Treg and the Byzantine text).
- **"pure gold, like unto clear glass" → "pure gold, like pure glass"** (v18, 21) — Greek καθαρός both times, the word rendered "clean" of the linen in 19:8, 14; of gold and glass English says "pure".
- **"garnished with all manner of precious stones" → "adorned with every precious stone"** (v19) — Greek παντὶ λίθῳ τιμίῳ, singular; κεκοσμημένοι, the verb of v2.
- **"sardonyx … sardius … jacinth" kept** (v19–20) — the Greek names of the stones are kept: χαλκηδών, chalcedony; σαρδόνυξ, sardonyx; σάρδιον, sardius (as 4:3); ὑάκινθος, jacinth. Which stones the ancient names meant is not certain in every case (σάπφιρος is probably lapis lazuli).
- **"every several gate was of one pearl" → "each one of the gates was of one pearl"** (v21) — Greek ἀνὰ εἷς ἕκαστος τῶν πυλώνων ἦν ἐξ ἑνὸς μαργαρίτου, "one by one, each of the gates".
- **"the street of the city" → "the broad street of the city"** (v21) — Greek ἡ πλατεῖα, "the broad one": the main street or open square.
- **"as it were transparent glass" → "as transparent glass"** (v21) — Greek ὡς ὕαλος διαυγής.
- **"I saw no temple therein: for the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are the temple of it" → "a sanctuary I did not see in it, for the Lord *Elohim*, the Almighty, is its sanctuary, and the Lamb"** (v22) — Greek ναός, the sanctuary, the temple building itself; ἐστιν is singular and "and the Lamb" stands after it, at the end.
- **"the glory of God did lighten it" → "the glory of *Elohim* illumined it"** (v23) — Greek ἐφώτισεν, aorist, from φωτίζω (as 18:1).
- **"the Lamb is the light thereof" → "its lamp is the Lamb"** (v23) — Greek λύχνος, "lamp".
- **"the nations of them which are saved shall walk in the light of it" → "the nations will walk through its light"** (v24) — Greek διὰ τοῦ φωτὸς αὐτῆς, "through, by means of its light"; "of them which are saved" is not in the text of the editions.
- **"do bring their glory and honour into it" → "bring their glory into it"** (v24) — Greek φέρουσιν, present; "and honour" is in the Byzantine text only (it stands in v26).
- **"shall not be shut at all by day" → "will by no means be shut by day"** (v25) — Greek οὐ μή, the strong denial; also v27.
- **"any thing that defileth, neither whatsoever worketh abomination, or maketh a lie" → "anything common, nor the one who does abomination and a lie"** (v27) — Greek πᾶν κοινόν, "anything common", that is, not holy, profane or unclean; the verse follows ὁ ποιῶν, "the one who does", masculine with the article (WH, Treg, NA28; SBLGNT prints it without the article); βδέλυγμα καὶ ψεῦδος, "abomination and a lie", joined by "and".
- **"they which are written in the Lamb's book of life" → "those who are written in the book of life of the Lamb"** (v27) — Greek ἐν τῷ βιβλίῳ τῆς ζωῆς τοῦ ἀρνίου; εἰ μή, "except, but only".

### Notes
- **v1** echoes Isa 65:17; 66:22.
- **v3** echoes Ezek 37:27.
- **v4** echoes Isa 25:8.
- **v7** echoes 2 Sam 7:14.
