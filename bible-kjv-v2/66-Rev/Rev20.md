# Revelation 20

[1] And I saw an angel coming down out of heaven, having the key of the abyss and a great chain upon his hand.

[2] And he took hold of the dragon, the ancient serpent, who is Devil and the Satan, and bound him a thousand years,

[3] and cast him into the abyss, and shut and sealed over him, so that he might not deceive the nations any more, until the thousand years should be finished; after these things he must be loosed a little time.

[4] And I saw thrones, and they sat upon them, and judgment was given to them; and the souls of those who had been beheaded because of the testimony of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ and because of the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and those who did not bow down to the beast nor to its image, and did not receive the mark on the forehead and on their hand; and they lived and reigned with ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ a thousand years.

[5] The rest of the dead did not live until the thousand years should be finished. This is the first resurrection.

[6] Blessed and holy is the one who has part in the first resurrection; over these the second death has no authority, but they will be priests of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, and they will reign with him the thousand years.

[7] And when the thousand years are finished, the Satan will be loosed out of his prison,

[8] and he will go out to deceive the nations that are in the four corners of the earth, Gog and Magog, to gather them together to the war, whose number is as the sand of the sea.

[9] And they went up over the breadth of the earth and encircled the camp of the saints and the beloved city. And fire came down out of heaven and devoured them;

[10] and the devil who deceives them was cast into the lake of fire and sulfur, where also the beast and the false prophet are, and they will be tormented day and night unto the ages of the ages.

[11] And I saw a great white throne and the one sitting on it, from whose face the earth and the heaven fled, and no place was found for them.

[12] And I saw the dead, the great and the small, standing before the throne, and books were opened; and another book was opened, which is that of life; and the dead were judged out of the things written in the books, according to their works.

[13] And the sea gave the dead who were in it, and death and Hades gave the dead who were in them, and they were judged, each one, according to their works.

[14] And death and Hades were cast into the lake of fire. This is the second death, the lake of fire.

[15] And if anyone was not found written in the book of life, he was cast into the lake of fire.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v4, 6) — systematic; the Greek has the article both times, τοῦ χριστοῦ.
- **"the key of the bottomless pit and a great chain in his hand" → "the key of the abyss and a great chain upon his hand"** (v1) — Greek ἄβυσσος, "abyss" (also v3); ἐπὶ τὴν χεῖρα αὐτοῦ, "upon his hand".
- **"he laid hold on the dragon, that old serpent, which is the Devil, and Satan" → "he took hold of the dragon, the ancient serpent, who is Devil and the Satan"** (v2) — Greek κρατέω, "take hold of"; ὁ ὄφις ὁ ἀρχαῖος, "the ancient serpent"; Διάβολος stands without the article and ὁ Σατανᾶς with it, as in 12:9. *Diabolos* means "slanderer"; *Satanas* is the Hebrew *satan*, "adversary". The Byzantine text adds "who deceives the whole inhabited earth"; SBLGNT, WH, Treg and NA28 do not have it.
- **"bound him a thousand years" kept** (v2, 4) — the Greek has a plain accusative of time, with no "for".
- **"cast him … shut him up, and set a seal upon him" → "cast him … and shut and sealed over him"** (v3) — Greek βάλλω, "cast" (also v10, 14, 15); ἔκλεισεν καὶ ἐσφράγισεν ἐπάνω αὐτοῦ: the two verbs have no object in the Greek.
- **"till the thousand years should be fulfilled" → "should be finished"** (v3, 5, 7) — Greek τελέω, "finish".
- **"after that he must be loosed a little season" → "after these things he must be loosed a little time"** (v3) — Greek μετὰ ταῦτα; λύω, "loose" (also v7), the opposite of "bound" in v2; μικρὸν χρόνον.
- **"judgment was given unto them" kept** (v4) — Greek κρίμα ἐδόθη αὐτοῖς. κρίμα is elsewhere "sentence"; here it is either the right to judge that is given to those sitting, or a judgment given in their favour (Dan 7:22). "Sentence was given to them" would say in English that they were condemned, so "judgment" stands. The Greek does not say who "they" are.
- **"and I saw the souls" → "and the souls"** (v4) — the Greek has no second "I saw"; τὰς ψυχάς is an accusative that still depends on "I saw" at the head of the verse.
- **"beheaded for the witness of Jesus" → "beheaded because of the testimony of Yeshua"** (v4) — Greek πεπελεκισμένων, beheaded with the axe (πέλεκυς); διά with the accusative, "because of"; μαρτυρία, "testimony".
- **"which had not worshipped the beast, neither his image" → "those who did not bow down to the beast nor to its image"** (v4) — Greek προσκυνέω, to bow down, to prostrate oneself; many translations: "worship". καὶ οἵτινες, "and those who", may name a second group beside the beheaded or describe the same people further.
- **"his mark upon their foreheads, or in their hands" → "the mark on the forehead and on their hand"** (v4) — Greek τὸ χάραγμα ἐπὶ τὸ μέτωπον καὶ ἐπὶ τὴν χεῖρα αὐτῶν: singular, "and", and "their" only once.
- **"they lived … lived not again" → "they lived … did not live"** (v4, 5) — Greek ἔζησαν in both verses, the aorist of ζάω, "live", rendered by the same word both times. The form can mean (a) "they came to life", the aorist marking the beginning of the state, as it does in 2:8 and 13:14 and in Rom 14:9; or (b) "they lived", "they were alive", for the thousand years. The verse itself calls this "the first resurrection" (v5). Whether a bodily rising is meant in both verses, or a life of the souls with the Mashiach in v4, the verb does not decide. "Again" is not in the Greek of v5.
- **"But the rest of the dead" → "The rest of the dead"** (v5) — the printed text has no connecting word (SBLGNT, WH, NA28); Treg reads Καὶ οἱ λοιποί, "And the rest".
- **"he that hath part" → "the one who has part"** (v6) — Greek ὁ ἔχων μέρος.
- **"on such the second death hath no power" → "over these the second death has no authority"** (v6) — Greek ἐπὶ τούτων, "over these"; ἐξουσία, "authority".
- **"shall reign with him a thousand years" → "the thousand years"** (v6) — the verse follows τὰ χίλια ἔτη, with the article (WH, Treg, NA28); SBLGNT prints it without the article, "a thousand years".
- **"Satan" → "the Satan"** (v7) — Greek ὁ Σατανᾶς, as in v2.
- **"in the four quarters of the earth" → "in the four corners of the earth"** (v8) — Greek ἐν ταῖς τέσσαρσι γωνίαις.
- **"to gather them together to battle" → "to gather them together to the war"** (v8) — Greek εἰς τὸν πόλεμον, with the article (as 16:14; 19:19).
- **"the number of whom is as the sand" → "whose number is as the sand"** (v8) — Greek ὧν ὁ ἀριθμὸς αὐτῶν, literally "of whom the number of them", a Hebrew way of speaking.
- **"compassed the camp of the saints about" → "encircled the camp of the saints"** (v9) — Greek ἐκύκλευσαν (SBLGNT, WH, NA28; Treg ἐκύκλωσαν, the same sense).
- **"fire came down from God out of heaven" → "fire came down out of heaven"** (v9) — the printed text reads ἐκ τοῦ οὐρανοῦ only (SBLGNT, WH, NA28); Treg reads ἀπὸ τοῦ θεοῦ ἐκ τοῦ οὐρανοῦ, "from God out of heaven".
- **"the devil that deceived them" → "the devil who deceives them"** (v10) — Greek ὁ πλανῶν, a present participle: "the one deceiving them".
- **"where the beast and the false prophet are" → "where also the beast and the false prophet are"** (v10) — Greek ὅπου καί; the Greek has no verb, "are" is supplied ("were" is also possible).
- **"for ever and ever" → "unto the ages of the ages"** (v10) — Greek εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας τῶν αἰώνων.
- **"brimstone" → "sulfur"** (v10) — Greek θεῖον.
- **"him that sat on it" → "the one sitting on it"** (v11) — Greek τὸν καθήμενον, a present participle. The printed text has ἐπʼ αὐτοῦ (SBLGNT, WH); NA28 reads ἐπʼ αὐτόν and Treg ἐπάνω αὐτοῦ, all with the same sense.
- **"from whose face the earth and the heaven fled away" kept** (v11) — Greek ἀπὸ τοῦ προσώπου, "from the face"; οὐρανός, "heaven".
- **"small and great" → "the great and the small"** (v12) — the Greek order and articles, τοὺς μεγάλους καὶ τοὺς μικρούς.
- **"stand before God" → "standing before the throne"** (v12) — Greek ἐνώπιον τοῦ θρόνου (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28).
- **"which is the book of life" → "which is that of life"** (v12) — Greek ὅ ἐστιν τῆς ζωῆς, literally "which is of life"; the noun "book" is not repeated. The word in v12 is βιβλίον; in v15 it is βίβλος; both are "book".
- **"out of those things which were written in the books" kept** (v12) — Greek ἐκ τῶν γεγραμμένων, "out of the things written".
- **"gave up the dead … delivered up the dead" → "gave the dead … gave the dead"** (v13) — Greek ἔδωκεν … ἔδωκαν, the plain verb "give", twice.
- **"death and hell" → "death and Hades"** (v13, 14) — Greek ὁ θάνατος καὶ ὁ ᾅδης; Hades is the realm of the dead, not the place of punishment.
- **"every man" → "each one"** (v13) — Greek ἕκαστος; then κατὰ τὰ ἔργα αὐτῶν, "according to their works", in the plural.
- **"This is the second death" → "This is the second death, the lake of fire"** (v14) — Greek οὗτος ὁ θάνατος ὁ δεύτερός ἐστιν, ἡ λίμνη τοῦ πυρός (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28); the KJV's Greek text lacked the last words.
- **"whosoever was not found written" → "if anyone was not found written"** (v15) — Greek εἴ τις οὐχ εὑρέθη … γεγραμμένος; "name" is not in the Greek.

### Notes
- **v8** — *Gog* and *Magog* echoes Ezek 38-39.
