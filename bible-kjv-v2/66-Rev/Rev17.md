# Revelation 17

[1] And one of the seven angels having the seven bowls came and spoke with me, saying, "Come, I will show you the sentence of the great prostitute who sits on many waters,

[2] with whom the kings of the earth committed sexual immorality, and those who dwell on the earth were made drunk from the wine of her sexual immorality."

[3] And he carried me away into a wilderness in the Spirit. And I saw a woman sitting on a scarlet beast, full of names of blasphemy, having seven heads and ten horns.

[4] And the woman was clothed in purple and scarlet, and gilded with gold and precious stone and pearls, having in her hand a golden cup full of abominations and the unclean things of her sexual immorality,

[5] and on her forehead a name written, a mystery: "**⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ the great, the mother of the prostitutes and of the abominations of the earth**."

[6] And I saw the woman drunk from the blood of the saints and from the blood of the witnesses of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

And seeing her I marveled with a great marvel.

[7] And the angel said to me, "Why did you marvel? I will tell you the mystery of the woman and of the beast that carries her, which has the seven heads and the ten horns.

[8] The beast which you saw was and is not, and is about to come up out of the abyss, and goes into destruction. And those who dwell on the earth will marvel, whose name is not written on the book of life from the foundation of the world, seeing the beast, that it was and is not and will be present.

[9] Here is the mind which has wisdom. The seven heads are seven mountains, where the woman sits on them. And they are seven kings:

[10] the five fell, the one is, the other has not yet come, and when he comes he must abide a little while.

[11] And the beast which was and is not, he himself is also an eighth, and is of the seven, and goes into destruction.

[12] And the ten horns which you saw are ten kings, who have not yet received a kingdom, but they receive authority as kings one hour with the beast.

[13] These have one purpose, and they give their power and authority to the beast.

[14] These will war with the Lamb, and the Lamb will overcome them, because he is **Lord of lords and King of kings**, and those with him are called and elect and faithful."

[15] And he says to me, "The waters which you saw, where the prostitute sits, are peoples and crowds and nations and tongues.

[16] And the ten horns which you saw, and the beast, these will hate the prostitute, and will make her desolate and naked, and will eat her flesh, and will burn her up with fire;

[17] for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave into their hearts to do his purpose, and to do one purpose, and to give their kingdom to the beast, until the words of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ shall be finished.

[18] And the woman whom you saw is the great city which has a kingdom over the kings of the earth."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v17) — systematic.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v6) — systematic.
- **"And" kept at the head of the sentences** (throughout) — Greek καί; not turned into "then" or "but".
- **"talked with me, saying unto me, Come hither; I will shew unto thee the judgment of the great whore that sitteth upon many waters" → "spoke with me, saying, 'Come, I will show you the sentence of the great prostitute who sits on many waters'"** (v1) — Greek κρίμα "sentence", the verdict (κρίσις is "judgment"); πόρνη "prostitute"; "unto me" after "saying" is not in the Greek.
- **"have committed fornication, and the inhabitants of the earth have been made drunk with the wine of her fornication" → "committed sexual immorality, and those who dwell on the earth were made drunk from the wine of her sexual immorality"** (v2) — Greek ἐπόρνευσαν, ἐμεθύσθησαν, aorists; πορνεία "sexual immorality"; ἐκ "from".
- **"in the spirit into the wilderness" → "into a wilderness in the Spirit"** (v3) — Greek εἰς ἔρημον ἐν πνεύματι, both without the article; "in the Spirit" or "in spirit".
- **"a scarlet coloured beast, full of names of blasphemy, having seven heads" kept** (v3) — Greek θηρίον κόκκινον, γέμοντα ὀνόματα βλασφημίας, ἔχων: "full" is not in the neuter singular that "beast" requires, and "having" is a masculine nominative (γέμοντα … ἔχων WH NA28; γέμον τὰ … ἔχον Treg; γέμον … ἔχον RP). The Greek breaks the agreement, which English does not show.
- **"arrayed in purple and scarlet colour, and decked with gold and precious stones" → "clothed in purple and scarlet, and gilded with gold and precious stone"** (v4) — Greek περιβεβλημένη "clothed" (as 12:1); κεχρυσωμένη χρυσίῳ, "gilded with gold", verb and noun of one root; λίθῳ τιμίῳ singular.
- **"full of abominations and filthiness of her fornication" → "full of abominations and the unclean things of her sexual immorality"** (v4) — Greek γέμον βδελυγμάτων καὶ τὰ ἀκάθαρτα: a genitive and then an accusative after "full of"; the case breaks in Greek. ἀκάθαρτα "unclean things", the word of the "unclean spirits" (16:13).
- **"upon her forehead was a name written, MYSTERY, BABYLON THE GREAT, THE MOTHER OF HARLOTS AND ABOMINATIONS OF THE EARTH" → "on her forehead a name written, a mystery: 'Babylon the great, the mother of the prostitutes and of the abominations of the earth'"** (v5) — Greek ὄνομα γεγραμμένον, μυστήριον, Βαβυλὼν ἡ μεγάλη …, with no verb. "Mystery" may be the first word of the inscription (KJV) or stand in apposition to "name" (compare v7); the Greek word order is kept. The articles of ἡ μήτηρ τῶν πορνῶν καὶ τῶν βδελυγμάτων are kept.
- **"drunken with the blood of the saints, and with the blood of the martyrs of Jesus" → "drunk from the blood of the saints and from the blood of the witnesses of *Yeshua*"** (v6) — Greek ἐκ … καὶ ἐκ "from … and from" (καί WH Treg NA28; not in RP); μαρτύρων "witnesses", the usual word; "martyr" is a later sense.
- **"I wondered with great admiration" → "I marveled with a great marvel"** (v6, 7) — Greek ἐθαύμασα … θαῦμα μέγα, verb and noun of one root; v7 ἐθαύμασας, an aorist, "did you marvel".
- **"shall ascend out of the bottomless pit, and go into perdition" → "is about to come up out of the abyss, and goes into destruction"** (v8, 11) — Greek μέλλει ἀναβαίνειν ἐκ τῆς ἀβύσσου; ἀπώλεια "destruction". ὑπάγει "goes" WH NA28; ὑπάγειν "(is about) to go" Treg RP.
- **"they that dwell on the earth shall wonder, whose names were not written in the book of life from the foundation of the world, when they behold the beast" → "those who dwell on the earth will marvel, whose name is not written on the book of life from the foundation of the world, seeing the beast"** (v8) — the Greek word order is kept: ὧν οὐ γέγραπται τὸ ὄνομα ἐπὶ τὸ βιβλίον τῆς ζωῆς ἀπὸ καταβολῆς κόσμου. "From the foundation of the world" can attach either way: (a) to "written", the name not written since the foundation of the world; (b) to "the book of life", the book that is from the foundation of the world. The Lamb is not named here (compare 13:8). τὸ ὄνομα "name" is singular after the plural "whose" (WH Treg NA28; τὰ ὀνόματα RP); ἐπί "on, upon" the book; θαυμασθήσονται WH NA28 ] θαυμάσονται Treg RP, both "will marvel".
- **"the beast that was, and is not, and yet is" → "the beast, that it was and is not and will be present"** (v8) — Greek ὅτι ἦν καὶ οὐκ ἔστιν καὶ παρέσται: ὅτι is "that" or "because"; παρέσται "will be present" (the verb of παρουσία "coming"). τὸ θηρίον ὅτι ἦν WH Treg NA28 ] ὅτι ἦν τὸ θηρίον RP.
- **"And here is the mind which hath wisdom … seven mountains, on which the woman sitteth" → "Here is the mind which has wisdom … seven mountains, where the woman sits on them"** (v9) — Greek ὅπου … ἐπʼ αὐτῶν, "where … on them", a doubled reference in the Hebrew manner (as 12:6, 14), kept.
- **"And there are seven kings" → "And they are seven kings"** (v9) — Greek καὶ βασιλεῖς ἑπτά εἰσιν: "they (the heads) are seven kings" or "there are seven kings".
- **"five are fallen, and one is, and the other is not yet come; and when he cometh, he must continue a short space" → "the five fell, the one is, the other has not yet come, and when he comes he must abide a little while"** (v10) — Greek οἱ πέντε, ὁ εἷς with the article; ἔπεσαν, an aorist; μεῖναι "abide"; ὀλίγον "a little".
- **"And the beast that was, and is not, even he is the eighth, and is of the seven" → "And the beast which was and is not, he himself is also an eighth, and is of the seven"** (v11) — Greek καὶ αὐτὸς ὄγδοός ἐστιν: αὐτός is masculine, though "beast" is neuter (αὐτός WH NA28 RP; οὗτος "this one" Treg); ὄγδοος without the article; ἐκ τῶν ἑπτά "of (out of) the seven". The SBLGNT prints a full stop after "is not" and so joins "and the beast which was and is not" to v10; the verse here follows the sense, with the beast as the subject of what follows.
- **"which have received no kingdom as yet; but receive power as kings one hour with the beast" → "who have not yet received a kingdom, but they receive authority as kings one hour with the beast"** (v12) — Greek βασιλείαν "a kingdom"; ἐξουσίαν "authority"; λαμβάνουσιν, a present.
- **"These have one mind … to fulfil his will, and to agree" → "These have one purpose … to do his purpose, and to do one purpose"** (v13, 17) — Greek γνώμη three times, one word: μίαν γνώμην, τὴν γνώμην αὐτοῦ, μίαν γνώμην. Elsewhere in this translation γνώμη is "opinion" (1 Cor 7:25; 2 Cor 8:10); here it is what one has resolved, so "purpose". ποιῆσαι "to do" twice.
- **"shall give their power and strength unto the beast" → "they give their power and authority to the beast"** (v13) — Greek δύναμιν καὶ ἐξουσίαν; διδόασιν, a present.
- **"These shall make war with the Lamb" → "These will war with the Lamb"** (v14) — Greek πολεμήσουσιν, the verb; νικήσει "will overcome".
- **"they that are with him are called, and chosen, and faithful" → "those with him are called and elect and faithful"** (v14) — Greek οἱ μετʼ αὐτοῦ κλητοὶ καὶ ἐκλεκτοὶ καὶ πιστοί, without a verb ("are" supplied); it can also be read "and those with him, the called and elect and faithful (will overcome)".
- **"And he saith unto me … where the whore sitteth, are peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues" → "And he says to me … where the prostitute sits, are peoples and crowds and nations and tongues"** (v15) — Greek λέγει, a present, with no named subject; ὄχλοι "crowds".
- **"the ten horns which thou sawest upon the beast, these shall hate the whore" → "the ten horns which you saw, and the beast, these will hate the prostitute"** (v16) — Greek καὶ τὸ θηρίον "and the beast"; οὗτοι "these" is masculine after neuter nouns.
- **"shall eat her flesh, and burn her with fire" → "will eat her flesh, and will burn her up with fire"** (v16) — Greek τὰς σάρκας, a plural ("pieces of flesh"); κατακαύσουσιν "burn up".
- **"God hath put in their hearts to fulfil his will … until the words of God shall be fulfilled" → "*Elohim* gave into their hearts to do his purpose … until the words of *Elohim* shall be finished"** (v17) — Greek ἔδωκεν "gave"; τελεσθήσονται, from τελέω "finish" (as 15:1, 8).
- **"which reigneth over the kings of the earth" → "which has a kingdom over the kings of the earth"** (v18) — Greek ἡ ἔχουσα βασιλείαν, the noun of v12, 17.
- **"Lord of lords, and King of kings" kept** (v14) — Greek κύριος κυρίων … καὶ βασιλεὺς βασιλέων (compare Deut 10:17; Dan 2:47).
