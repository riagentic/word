# Revelation 2

[1] "To the angel of the church in ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧ write: 'These things says the one who holds the seven stars in his right hand, the one who walks in the midst of the seven golden lampstands:

[2] "'I know your works, and your labor and endurance, and that you cannot bear evil ones, and you tested those who call themselves apostles, and are not, and you found them false;

[3] and you have endurance, and you bore because of my name, and you have not grown weary.

[4] But I have against you that you left your first love.

[5] Remember therefore from where you have fallen, and repent, and do the first works; but if not, I am coming to you, and I will move your lampstand out of its place, unless you repent.

[6] But this you have, that you hate the works of the ⟦nikolaitans|Nikolaitans|Nicolaitans⟧, which I also hate.

[7] "'The one who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who overcomes, to him I will give to eat of the tree of life, which is in the paradise of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.'

[8] "And to the angel of the church in Smyrna write: 'These things says the first and the last, who became dead and came to life:

[9] "'I know your affliction and poverty — but you are rich — and the blasphemy from those who say that they themselves are ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, and are not, but are a synagogue of Satan.

[10] Fear none of the things that you are about to suffer. Behold, the devil is about to throw some of you into prison, that you may be tested, and you will have an affliction of ten days. **Be faithful until death, and I will give you the crown of life**.

[11] "'The one who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches. The one who overcomes will by no means be harmed by the second death.'

[12] "And to the angel of the church in ⟦pergamum|Pergamon|Pergamum⟧ write: 'These things says the one who has the sharp two-edged sword:

[13] "'I know where you dwell: where the throne of Satan is; and you hold my name, and you did not deny my faith even in the days of Antipas, my witness, my faithful one, who was killed among you, where Satan dwells.

[14] But I have a few things against you, that you have there those who hold the teaching of ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧, who was teaching ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧ to cast a stumbling block before the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to eat things sacrificed to idols and to commit sexual immorality;

[15] so you also have those who hold the teaching of the ⟦nikolaitans|Nikolaitans|Nicolaitans⟧ likewise.

[16] Repent therefore; but if not, I am coming to you quickly, and I will make war with them with the sword of my mouth.

[17] "'The one who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who overcomes, to him I will give of the hidden manna, and I will give him a white pebble, and on the pebble a new name written, which no one knows except the one who receives it.'

[18] "And to the angel of the church in ⟦thyatira|Thyateira|Thyatira⟧ write: 'These things says the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the one who has his eyes as a flame of fire, and his feet are like burnished bronze:

[19] "'I know your works, and your love and faith and service and endurance, and your last works are more than the first.

[20] But I have against you that you let alone the woman ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧, who calls herself a prophetess, and she teaches and deceives my slaves to commit sexual immorality and to eat things sacrificed to idols.

[21] And I gave her time that she might repent, and she is not willing to repent of her sexual immorality.

[22] Behold, I throw her into a bed, and those who commit adultery with her into great affliction, unless they repent of her works;

[23] and her children I will kill with death. And all the churches will come to know that I am the one who searches kidneys and hearts, and I will give to you, to each one, according to your works.

[24] But to you I say, to the rest who are in ⟦thyatira|Thyateira|Thyatira⟧, as many as do not have this teaching, who did not come to know the deep things of Satan, as they say: I do not throw upon you another burden;

[25] only, what you have, hold until I come.

[26] And the one who overcomes and the one who keeps my works until the end, to him I will give authority over the nations,

[27] **and he will shepherd them with a rod of iron, as the vessels of pottery are shattered**, as I also have received from my Father.

[28] And I will give him the morning star.

[29] "'The one who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches.'"

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v7, 18) — systematic.
- **"These things saith he that holdeth" kept** (v1, 8, 12, 18) — Greek Τάδε λέγει, the formula of a royal or prophetic decree ("Thus says"); not "The words of him".
- **"in the midst of" kept** (v1) — Greek ἐν μέσῳ, as 1:13.
- **"thy labour, and thy patience" → "your labor and endurance"** (v2, 3, 19) — Greek κόπος, ὑπομονή; "patient" is not a separate word.
- **"thou hast tried them which say they are apostles" → "you tested those who call themselves apostles"** (v2) — Greek ἐπείρασας, the verb elsewhere rendered "tempt"; here it is putting to the proof, "test", as in v10 and 3:10 ("tested … trial … test": one root in Greek).
- **"hast borne … and hast not fainted" → "you bore … and you have not grown weary"** (v3) — Greek ἐβάστασας (the verb of v2, "bear"; no object is named) and οὐ κεκοπίακες, the verb of the noun "labor" (κόπος, v2), here in its sense "grow weary". διὰ τὸ ὄνομά μου, "because of my name".
- **"I have somewhat against thee, because thou hast left thy first love" → "I have against you that you left your first love"** (v4) — Greek has no "somewhat" or "this"; τὴν ἀγάπην σου τὴν πρώτην ἀφῆκες, not "the love you had at first".
- **"I will come unto thee quickly, and will remove thy candlestick" → "I am coming to you, and I will move your lampstand"** (v5) — Greek ἔρχομαί σοι, present; κινήσω "move"; "quickly" is RP only. "The first works": τὰ πρῶτα ἔργα.
- **"He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches" → "The one who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches"** (v7, 11, 17, 29) — refrain formula.
- **"To him that overcometh will I give" → "To the one who overcomes, to him I will give"** (v7, 17) — Greek τῷ νικῶντι δώσω αὐτῷ: the pronoun repeats the dative. νικάω is "overcome", as in John 16:33 and 1 John 5:4.
- **"the tree of life" kept** (v7) — Greek ξύλον, "wood, tree" (Gen 2:9 LXX). "Paradise of God": RP adds "my".
- **"which was dead, and is alive" → "who became dead and came to life"** (v8) — Greek ἐγένετο νεκρὸς καὶ ἔζησεν, literally "and lived" (aorist).
- **"tribulation" → "affliction"** (v9, 10, 22) — Greek θλῖψις.
- **"blasphemy" kept** (v9) — Greek βλασφημία, abusive speech against God or against people ("slander"). ἐκ, "from" those who say.
- **"the synagogue of Satan" → "a synagogue of Satan"** (v9) — Greek συναγωγὴ τοῦ Σατανᾶ, without the article before "synagogue".
- **"Fear none of those things which thou shalt suffer" kept in sense** (v10) — μηδέν "nothing" NA28 RP; μή "do not (fear)" WH Treg. "You will have" (ἕξετε) Treg NA28 RP; WH "that you may have" (ἔχητε). "Ten days" is a genitive: "an affliction of ten days".
- **"be thou faithful unto death" → "Be faithful until death"** (v10) — Greek γίνου, "be, become"; ἄχρι θανάτου. "Crown" is στέφανος, the victor's wreath.
- **"shall not be hurt of the second death" → "will by no means be harmed by the second death"** (v11) — Greek οὐ μή, the strong negation; ἀδικέω "wrong, harm"; ἐκ "from, by".
- **"Satan's seat" → "the throne of Satan"** (v13) — Greek θρόνος, the same word as God's throne.
- **"holdest fast my name" → "hold my name"** (v13, 14, 15, 25) — Greek κρατέω, "hold", as in v1.
- **"hast not denied my faith" → "did not deny my faith"** (v13) — Greek τὴν πίστιν μου, a plain genitive: faith in me, or my faithfulness.
- **"Antipas was my faithful martyr" → "Antipas, my witness, my faithful one"** (v13) — Greek ὁ μάρτυς μου, ὁ πιστός μου (the second "my" WH Treg NA28; RP lacks it); μάρτυς is "witness". The name stands in the nominative after "in the days of", where a genitive is expected: the grammar is broken, or the name is treated as indeclinable.
- **"who taught Balac" → "who was teaching Balak"** (v14) — Greek ἐδίδασκεν, imperfect (WH Treg NA28; RP aorist).
- **"things sacrificed unto idols" kept; "to commit fornication" → "to commit sexual immorality"** (v14, 20, 21) — Greek εἰδωλόθυτα (no "food" in the word); πορνεῦσαι, πορνεία.
- **"the doctrine" → "the teaching"** (v14, 15, 24) — Greek διδαχή. In v15 "of the Nicolaitans": τῶν Treg NA28 RP; WH has no article. The verse ends with "likewise" (ὁμοίως), not with KJV "which thing I hate".
- **"will fight against them" → "I will make war with them"** (v16) — Greek πολεμήσω μετʼ αὐτῶν; "with" in the hostile sense. "With the sword": ἐν.
- **"to eat of the hidden manna" → "of the hidden manna"** (v17) — "to eat" is RP only.
- **"a white stone" → "a white pebble"** (v17) — Greek ψῆφος, a small stone, used for voting and as a token; "stone" elsewhere is λίθος.
- **"his eyes like unto a flame of fire" → "his eyes as a flame of fire"** (v18) — ὡς "as", ὅμοιοι "like", as in 1:14–15. The sentence passes from a participle ("the one who has") to a new clause ("and his feet are like"). For χαλκολίβανον see 1:15.
- **"charity, and service, and faith" → "love and faith and service"** (v19) — order ἀγάπην … πίστιν WH NA28 RP; Treg "faith and love". "Your last works are more than the first": πλείονα.
- **"thou sufferest that woman Jezebel" → "you let alone the woman Jezebel"** (v20) — Greek ἀφεῖς, "let, leave alone"; "the woman" WH Treg NA28, RP "your woman / wife" (+ σοῦ). ἡ λέγουσα "who calls" is nominative after an accusative, and the sentence goes on with finite verbs: "and she teaches and deceives".
- **"to seduce my servants" → "deceives my slaves"** (v20) — Greek πλανᾷ, δούλους.
- **"she repented not" → "she is not willing to repent"** (v21) — Greek οὐ θέλει μετανοῆσαι.
- **"I will cast her into a bed" → "I throw her into a bed"** (v22) — Greek βάλλω, present; κλίνη "bed" ("sick" is not in the Greek). "Her works": the editions read αὐτῆς, not "their".
- **"I will kill her children with death" kept** (v23) — Greek ἀποκτενῶ ἐν θανάτῳ, a Hebrew turn of speech; "death" can stand for pestilence, as in 6:8.
- **"the reins and hearts" → "kidneys and hearts"** (v23) — Greek νεφροὺς καὶ καρδίας, the kidneys as the seat of the inmost feelings (Jer 17:10); not "mind". "To you, to each one": ὑμῖν ἑκάστῳ.
- **"the depths of Satan, as they speak" → "the deep things of Satan, as they say"** (v24) — Greek τὰ βαθέα; "what some call" is not in the Greek.
- **"power over the nations" → "authority over the nations"** (v26) — Greek ἐξουσία. "The one who overcomes and the one who keeps" stands in the nominative and is taken up by "to him".
- **"he shall rule them with a rod of iron; as the vessels of a potter shall they be broken to shivers" → "he will shepherd them with a rod of iron, as the vessels of pottery are shattered"** (v27) — Greek ποιμανεῖ "will shepherd" (Ps 2:9 LXX); συντρίβεται, present (WH Treg NA28; RP future).
- **"even as I received of my Father" → "as I also have received from my Father"** (v27) — Greek ὡς κἀγὼ εἴληφα; no object is named. The editions count this clause as the beginning of v28.

### Notes
- **v27** quotes Ps 2:9.
- **v14** — Num 25:1–2; 31:16.
