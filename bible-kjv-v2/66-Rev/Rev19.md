# Revelation 19

[1] After these things I heard as it were a loud voice of a great crowd in heaven, saying,

"**⟦hallelujah|Halleluyah|Hallelujah⟧!**
The salvation and the glory and the power are of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
[2] because true and righteous are his judgments;
because he judged the great prostitute
who corrupted the earth in her sexual immorality,
and he avenged the blood of his slaves from her hand."

[3] And a second time they have said,

"**⟦hallelujah|Halleluyah|Hallelujah⟧!** And her smoke goes up unto the ages of the ages."

[4] And the twenty-four elders and the four living creatures fell and bowed down to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the one sitting on the throne, saying, "Amen, ⟦hallelujah|Halleluyah|Hallelujah⟧!"

[5] And a voice came out from the throne, saying,

"Praise our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
all you his slaves,
and you who fear him,
the small and the great."

[6] And I heard as it were a voice of a great crowd and as a voice of many waters and as a voice of strong thunders, saying,

"**⟦hallelujah|Halleluyah|Hallelujah⟧,
because the Lord our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
the Almighty, reigned.**
[7] Let us rejoice and exult,
and we will give the glory to him,
because the wedding of the Lamb has come,
and his wife has made herself ready;
[8] and it was given to her that she should clothe herself
in fine linen, bright, clean" —

for the fine linen is the righteous deeds of the saints.

[9] And he says to me, "Write: **Blessed are those who have been called to the supper of the wedding of the Lamb**." And he says to me, "These are the true words of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[10] And I fell before his feet to bow down to him. And he says to me, "See that you do not! I am a fellow slave of you and of your brothers who have the testimony of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧. Bow down to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧! For the testimony of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ is the spirit of prophecy."

[11] And I saw heaven opened, and behold, a white horse, and the one sitting on it, called Faithful and True, and in righteousness he judges and wars.

[12] And his eyes are a flame of fire, and on his head are many diadems; he has a name written which no one knows except himself,

[13] and he is clothed in a garment dipped in blood, and his name has been called **The Word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧**.

[14] And the armies that are in heaven were following him on white horses, clothed in fine linen, white, clean.

[15] And out of his mouth goes forth a sharp sword, so that with it he may strike the nations, and he himself will shepherd them with a rod of iron; and he himself treads the winepress of the wine of the rage of the wrath of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ the Almighty.

[16] And he has on the garment and on his thigh a name written: **King of kings and Lord of lords**.

[17] And I saw one angel standing in the sun, and he cried out in a loud voice, saying to all the birds that fly in mid-heaven, "Come, be gathered together to the great supper of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[18] so that you may eat flesh of kings and flesh of chiliarchs and flesh of strong men and flesh of horses and of those sitting on them, and flesh of all, both free and slaves, and small and great."

[19] And I saw the beast and the kings of the earth and their armies gathered together to make the war with the one sitting on the horse and with his army.

[20] And the beast was seized, and with it the false prophet who did the signs before it, by which he deceived those who received the mark of the beast and those who bow down to its image. Living, the two were cast into the lake of fire that burns with sulfur.

[21] And the rest were killed with the sword of the one sitting on the horse, the sword that came out of his mouth, and all the birds were filled from their flesh.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 13, 15, 17) — systematic.
- **"Alleluia" → "**Hallelujah!**"** (v1, 3, 4, 6) — Greek Ἁλληλουϊά, the Hebrew הַלְּלוּיָהּ *Halleluyah*, "Praise Yah!", written in Greek letters and left untranslated by the Greek; the verse leaves it so too. Revelation is the only NT book to use this word (4 times); bolded as a liturgical exclamation.
- **"a great voice of much people" → "as it were a loud voice of a great crowd"** (v1, 6) — Greek ὡς φωνὴν … ὄχλου πολλοῦ: ὡς, "as"; ὄχλος, "crowd". In v6 ὡς φωνήν stands three times.
- **"Salvation, and glory, and honour, and power, unto the Lord our God" → "The salvation and the glory and the power are of our *Elohim*"** (v1) — Greek ἡ σωτηρία καὶ ἡ δόξα καὶ ἡ δύναμις τοῦ θεοῦ ἡμῶν: three nouns, each with the article, and a plain genitive with no verb; "are" is supplied. "Honour" and "the Lord" are not in the text of the editions.
- **"which did corrupt the earth with her fornication" → "who corrupted the earth in her sexual immorality"** (v2) — Greek φθείρω, "corrupt"; ἐν τῇ πορνείᾳ, "in".
- **"hath avenged the blood of his servants at her hand" → "he avenged the blood of his slaves from her hand"** (v2) — Greek δοῦλος, "slave" (also v5; v10 σύνδουλος, "fellow slave"; v18); ἐκ χειρὸς αὐτῆς, "out of her hand".
- **"again they said" → "a second time they have said"** (v3) — Greek δεύτερον εἴρηκαν, a perfect.
- **"for ever and ever" → "unto the ages of the ages"** (v3) — Greek εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας τῶν αἰώνων.
- **"worshipped God that sat on the throne" → "bowed down to *Elohim*, the one sitting on the throne"** (v4) — Greek προσκυνέω, to bow down, to prostrate oneself (also v10, 20); many translations: "worship". τῷ καθημένῳ, a present participle.
- **"and ye that fear him" kept** (v5) — καί, "and", is read by Treg, NA28 and SBLGNT; WH omits it ("all his slaves, those who fear him").
- **"mighty thunderings" → "strong thunders"** (v6) — Greek βροντῶν ἰσχυρῶν.
- **"the Lord God omnipotent reigneth" → "the Lord our *Elohim*, the Almighty, reigned"** (v6) — the verse follows κύριος ὁ θεὸς ἡμῶν, "the Lord our God" (WH, Treg, NA28); SBLGNT prints it without ἡμῶν, "the Lord God". ἐβασίλευσεν is an aorist, "reigned", the verb and tense of 11:17; of a state it often marks the entry into it ("has taken up his reign", "has become king"), but "begun" is not a separate word in the Greek.
- **"be glad and rejoice, and give honour to him" → "rejoice and exult, and we will give the glory to him"** (v7) — Greek χαίρω, "rejoice"; ἀγαλλιάω, "exult"; δόξα, "glory". The printed text reads δώσομεν, a future, "we will give" (SBLGNT, WH); NA28 reads δώσωμεν and Treg δῶμεν, "let us give".
- **"the marriage of the Lamb" → "the wedding of the Lamb"** (v7, 9) — Greek γάμος, the wedding feast.
- **"his wife" kept** (v7) — Greek ἡ γυνὴ αὐτοῦ, "his woman, his wife"; γυνή is the general word for a woman. "Bride" is νύμφη (21:2, 9; 22:17); 21:9 has both words.
- **"that she should be arrayed in fine linen, clean and white" → "that she should clothe herself in fine linen, bright, clean"** (v8) — Greek περιβάληται, middle voice; λαμπρὸν καθαρόν, "bright, clean", with no "and" (also v14 λευκὸν καθαρόν, "white, clean").
- **"the righteousness of saints" → "the righteous deeds of the saints"** (v8) — Greek τὰ δικαιώματα, plural: righteous acts; elsewhere also "righteous ordinances".
- **"they which are called unto the marriage supper" → "those who have been called to the supper of the wedding"** (v9) — Greek κεκλημένοι, from καλέω, "call" (to call to a meal is to invite); δεῖπνον, "supper" (also v17).
- **"And he saith unto me" kept** (v9, 10) — the Greek has λέγει, "he says", and no subject; "the angel" is not in the Greek.
- **"at his feet to worship him" → "before his feet to bow down to him"** (v10) — Greek ἔμπροσθεν τῶν ποδῶν αὐτοῦ.
- **"See thou do it not" → "See that you do not!"** (v10) — Greek Ὅρα μή, "See — not!"; the verb "do" is not in the Greek.
- **"that have the testimony of Jesus" kept** (v10) — Greek τῶν ἐχόντων τὴν μαρτυρίαν Ἰησοῦ, "who have", not "hold to". The genitive is plain: the testimony that Yeshua gave, or the testimony about Yeshua.
- **"he that sat upon him was called Faithful and True" → "the one sitting on it, called Faithful and True"** (v11) — Greek πιστὸς καλούμενος καὶ ἀληθινός (SBLGNT, WH, Treg; NA28 has the order καλούμενος πιστὸς καὶ ἀληθινός, with the same sense); a participle, no finite verb. ἀληθινός, "true".
- **"doth judge and make war" → "judges and wars"** (v11) — Greek πολεμέω, the verb "war"; "make war" is ποιῆσαι τὸν πόλεμον (v19).
- **"His eyes were as a flame of fire" → "his eyes are a flame of fire"** (v12) — the printed text has no ὡς (SBLGNT, WH, Treg); NA28 reads ὡς φλὸξ πυρός, "as a flame of fire". The Greek has no verb here.
- **"a vesture dipped in blood" → "a garment dipped in blood"** (v13) — Greek ἱμάτιον, "garment" (also v16). βεβαμμένον, "dipped" (Treg, NA28, SBLGNT); WH reads ῥεραντισμένον, "sprinkled".
- **"his name is called" → "his name has been called"** (v13) — Greek κέκληται, a perfect.
- **"The Word of God" → "The Word of *Elohim*"** (v13) — Greek ὁ Λόγος τοῦ Θεοῦ *ho Logos tou Theou*.
- **"that with it he should smite the nations: and he shall rule them" → "so that with it he may strike the nations, and he himself will shepherd them"** (v15) — Greek πατάσσω, "strike"; ποιμανεῖ, "will shepherd" (Ps 2:9 in the Greek version; as 2:27; 12:5); αὐτός, "he himself", twice.
- **"the winepress of the fierceness and wrath of Almighty God" → "the winepress of the wine of the rage of the wrath of *Elohim* the Almighty"** (v15) — Greek τὴν ληνὸν τοῦ οἴνου τοῦ θυμοῦ τῆς ὀργῆς τοῦ θεοῦ τοῦ παντοκράτορος: a chain of genitives; θυμός, "rage"; ὀργή, "wrath". πατεῖ is a present, "treads".
- **"on his vesture" → "on the garment"** (v16) — Greek ἐπὶ τὸ ἱμάτιον, with the article and no "his".
- **"KING OF KINGS, AND LORD OF LORDS" → "King of kings and Lord of lords"** (v16) — the caps of the inscription rendered without shouting in the main text.
- **"an angel" → "one angel"** (v17) — Greek ἕνα ἄγγελον (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28). "In a loud voice": the verse follows ἐν φωνῇ μεγάλῃ (WH, NA28); Treg and SBLGNT have no ἐν; the sense is the same.
- **"in the midst of heaven" → "in mid-heaven"** (v17) — Greek ἐν μεσουρανήματι, the point where the sun stands at noon (as 8:13; 14:6).
- **"gather yourselves together" → "be gathered together"** (v17) — Greek συνάχθητε, passive; also v19 συνηγμένα.
- **"the flesh of captains" → "flesh of chiliarchs"** (v18) — Greek χιλίαρχος, a commander of a thousand. σάρκας, "flesh", is plural in Greek all five times ("pieces of flesh"), and so in v21.
- **"of them that sit on them" kept** (v18) — Greek τῶν καθημένων ἐπʼ αὐτῶν (NA28, SBLGNT; WH and Treg read ἐπʼ αὐτούς, with the same sense); no "riders".
- **"all men, both free and bond" → "all, both free and slaves"** (v18) — Greek πάντων; "men" is not in the Greek.
- **"to make war against him" → "to make the war with the one sitting on the horse"** (v19) — Greek ποιῆσαι τὸν πόλεμον μετά: the article "the war" (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28; as 16:14; 20:8); μετά, "with".
- **"the beast was taken" → "the beast was seized"** (v20) — Greek πιάζω, "seize".
- **"wrought miracles before him" → "did the signs before it"** (v20) — Greek σημεῖα, "signs"; ἐνώπιον, "before". The beast is "it" (θηρίον is neuter).
- **"them that worshipped his image" → "those who bow down to its image"** (v20) — Greek τοὺς προσκυνοῦντας, a present participle.
- **"These both were cast alive" → "Living, the two were cast"** (v20) — Greek ζῶντες ἐβλήθησαν οἱ δύο; "living" stands first.
- **"a lake of fire burning with brimstone" → "the lake of fire that burns with sulfur"** (v20) — Greek εἰς τὴν λίμνην τοῦ πυρὸς τῆς καιομένης ἐν θείῳ, with the article: "the lake of the fire". "That burns" (τῆς καιομένης) is a feminine genitive: by its gender it belongs to "lake", by its case to "fire". θεῖον, "sulfur".
- **"the remnant were slain with the sword of him that sat upon the horse, which sword proceeded out of his mouth" → "the rest were killed with the sword of the one sitting on the horse, the sword that came out of his mouth"** (v21) — Greek ἀποκτείνω, "kill"; ῥομφαία, "sword", the large sword (also v15), a different word from μάχαιρα.
- **"were filled with their flesh" → "were filled from their flesh"** (v21) — Greek ἐχορτάσθησαν ἐκ τῶν σαρκῶν αὐτῶν.
