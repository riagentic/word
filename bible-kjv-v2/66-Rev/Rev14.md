# Revelation 14

[1] And I saw, and behold, the Lamb standing on Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, and with him a hundred and forty-four thousand, having his name and the name of his Father written on their foreheads.

[2] And I heard a voice out of heaven as a voice of many waters and as a voice of great thunder, and the voice which I heard was as of harpers harping on their harps.

[3] And they sing as it were a new song before the throne and before the four living creatures and the elders; and no one was able to learn the song except the hundred and forty-four thousand, those bought from the earth.

[4] These are they who were not defiled with women, for they are virgins. These are the ones following the Lamb wherever he goes. These were bought from among men, firstfruits to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and to the Lamb,

[5] and in their mouth no lie was found; they are without blemish.

[6] And I saw another angel flying in mid-heaven, having an eternal gospel to preach to those sitting on the earth, and to every nation and tribe and tongue and people,

[7] saying with a loud voice, "Fear ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and give him glory, because the hour of his judgment has come, and bow down to the one who made the heaven and the earth and sea and springs of waters."

[8] And another, a second angel, followed, saying, "**Fallen, fallen is ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ the great**, who of the wine of the rage of her sexual immorality has given all the nations to drink."

[9] And another angel, a third, followed them, saying with a loud voice, "If anyone bows down to the beast and its image, and receives a mark on his forehead or on his hand,

[10] he also will drink of the wine of the rage of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, which is mixed undiluted in the cup of his wrath, and he will be tormented with fire and sulfur before holy angels and before the Lamb.

[11] And the smoke of their torment goes up unto ages of ages, and they have no rest day and night, those bowing down to the beast and its image, and if anyone receives the mark of its name."

[12] Here is the endurance of the saints, those keeping the commandments of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and the faith of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[13] And I heard a voice out of heaven saying, "Write: Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord from now on." "Yes," says the Spirit, "that they shall rest from their labors, for their works follow with them."

[14] And I saw, and behold, a white cloud, and on the cloud one sitting like a son of man, having on his head a golden crown and in his hand a sharp sickle.

[15] And another angel came out of the sanctuary, crying out with a loud voice to the one sitting on the cloud, "Send your sickle and reap, because the hour to reap has come, because the harvest of the earth is dried up."

[16] And the one sitting on the cloud cast his sickle upon the earth, and the earth was reaped.

[17] And another angel came out of the sanctuary which is in heaven, he also having a sharp sickle.

[18] And another angel came out from the altar, the one having authority over the fire, and he called with a loud voice to the one having the sharp sickle, saying, "Send your sharp sickle and gather the clusters of the vine of the earth, because its grapes have ripened."

[19] And the angel cast his sickle into the earth, and gathered the vine of the earth, and cast it into the winepress of the rage of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the great one.

[20] And the winepress was trodden outside the city, and blood came out of the winepress up to the bridles of the horses, for a thousand six hundred stadia.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4, 7, 10, 12, 19) — systematic.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v12) — systematic.
- **"And" kept at the head of the sentences** (throughout) — Greek καί; not turned into "then" or "so".
- **"And I looked, and, lo, a Lamb stood" → "And I saw, and behold, the Lamb standing"** (v1, 14) — Greek εἶδον "I saw"; τὸ ἀρνίον with the article; ἑστός, a participle.
- **"having his Father's name written in their foreheads" → "having his name and the name of his Father written on their foreheads"** (v1) — Greek τὸ ὄνομα αὐτοῦ καὶ τὸ ὄνομα τοῦ πατρὸς αὐτοῦ; the KJV follows a shorter text.
- **"the voice of harpers harping with their harps" → "as of harpers harping on their harps"** (v2) — Greek ὡς κιθαρῳδῶν κιθαριζόντων ἐν ταῖς κιθάραις αὐτῶν, three words of one root; φωνή "voice" four times in the verse, not "roar" or "sound".
- **"they sung as it were a new song" → "they sing as it were a new song"** (v3) — Greek ᾄδουσιν, a present; ὡς "as" (WH Treg NA28; not in RP).
- **"redeemed" → "bought"** (v3, 4) — Greek ἠγορασμένοι, ἠγοράσθησαν, from ἀγοράζω "buy"; "redeem" is another word.
- **"These are they which follow the Lamb whithersoever he goeth" → "These are the ones following the Lamb wherever he goes"** (v4) — Greek οὗτοι οἱ ἀκολουθοῦντες, without a verb ("are" supplied; RP adds εἰσιν); ὑπάγῃ NA28 RP ] ὑπάγει WH Treg, no difference in English.
- **"from among men, being the firstfruits unto God" → "from among men, firstfruits to *Elohim*"** (v4) — Greek ἀπὸ τῶν ἀνθρώπων ἀπαρχή.
- **"in their mouth was found no guile: for they are without fault before the throne of God" → "in their mouth no lie was found; they are without blemish"** (v5) — Greek ψεῦδος "lie"; ἄμωμοι "without blemish", the word for an offering with no defect. No "for" (WH NA28; γάρ Treg RP), and "before the throne of God" is not in the Greek.
- **"in the midst of heaven … to preach unto them that dwell on the earth" → "in mid-heaven … to preach to those sitting on the earth"** (v6) — Greek ἐν μεσουρανήματι, the highest point of the sky; τοὺς καθημένους "those sitting", not the usual κατοικοῦντας "those who dwell"; ἐπί "upon" twice. "Another" (ἄλλον) WH Treg NA28; not in RP.
- **"worship him that made heaven, and earth, and the sea, and the fountains of waters" → "bow down to the one who made the heaven and the earth and sea and springs of waters"** (v7) — Greek προσκυνήσατε; the article stands with "heaven" and "earth" only.
- **"Babylon is fallen, is fallen, that great city, because she made all nations drink of the wine of the wrath of her fornication" → "Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great, who of the wine of the rage of her sexual immorality has given all the nations to drink"** (v8) — Greek ἔπεσεν, ἔπεσεν Βαβυλὼν ἡ μεγάλη (Isa 21:9); "city" is not in the Greek; ἥ "who" (WH Treg NA28), not "because". θυμός "rage", as in v10, 19 (the word can also mean "passion"); πορνεία "sexual immorality". δεύτερος ἄγγελος WH Treg RP ] ἄγγελος δεύτερος NA28, no difference in sense.
- **"worship" → "bows down to"** (v9, 11) — Greek προσκυνέω.
- **"the wine of the wrath of God, which is poured out without mixture into the cup of his indignation" → "the wine of the rage of *Elohim*, which is mixed undiluted in the cup of his wrath"** (v10) — Greek θυμός "rage" and ὀργή "wrath", two words, each kept to one rendering; κεκερασμένου ἀκράτου, literally "mixed unmixed": prepared, but with no water added.
- **"in the presence of the holy angels" → "before holy angels"** (v10) — Greek ἐνώπιον ἀγγέλων ἁγίων, without the article (WH Treg NA28; with it RP). ἐν πυρὶ καὶ θείῳ: "with" renders ἐν "in".
- **"for ever and ever" → "unto ages of ages"** (v11) — Greek εἰς αἰῶνας αἰώνων, here without articles.
- **"who worship the beast and his image, and whosoever receiveth the mark" → "those bowing down to the beast and its image, and if anyone receives the mark"** (v11) — Greek καὶ εἴ τις λαμβάνει, "and if anyone receives".
- **"Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus" → "Here is the endurance of the saints, those keeping the commandments of *Elohim* and the faith of *Yeshua*"** (v12) — Greek ὑπομονή "endurance"; no "call for". οἱ τηροῦντες is a nominative set beside the genitive "of the saints": the Greek breaks the case, which English cannot show. τὴν πίστιν Ἰησοῦ, a plain genitive: "faith in Yeshua" or "the faith (faithfulness) of Yeshua".
- **"Blessed are the dead which die in the Lord from henceforth: Yea, saith the Spirit, that they may rest from their labours; and their works do follow them" → "Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord from now on." "Yes," says the Spirit, "that they shall rest from their labors, for their works follow with them"** (v13) — Greek ἀπʼ ἄρτι "from now on" is taken with "die", as printed; with other punctuation it begins the next sentence ("From now on, yes, says the Spirit"). ἵνα ἀναπαήσονται, ἵνα with a future indicative (WH Treg NA28; subjunctive RP); γάρ "for" (WH Treg NA28; δέ RP); ἀκολουθεῖ μετʼ αὐτῶν "follow with them". "Unto me" after "saying" is not in the Greek.
- **"upon the cloud one sat like unto the Son of man" → "on the cloud one sitting like a son of man"** (v14) — Greek ὅμοιον υἱὸν ἀνθρώπου, without articles; υἱόν is an accusative where a dative is expected (WH NA28; υἱῷ Treg RP), and ἔχων "having" is a nominative: the grammar is broken in Greek.
- **"temple" → "sanctuary"** (v15, 17) — Greek ναός, the temple building itself.
- **"Thrust in thy sickle … thrust in" → "Send your sickle … cast"** (v15, 16, 18, 19) — Greek πέμψον "send" in the commands, ἔβαλεν "cast" in the doing.
- **"the harvest of the earth is ripe" → "the harvest of the earth is dried up"** (v15) — Greek ἐξηράνθη "was dried up, withered": the grain is dry and ready. A different word from "ripened" in v18 (ἤκμασαν, "came to their prime").
- **"which had power over fire; and cried with a loud cry" → "the one having authority over the fire, and he called with a loud voice"** (v18) — Greek ἐξουσίαν ἐπὶ τοῦ πυρός; φωνῇ μεγάλῃ (WH Treg NA28; κραυγῇ "cry" RP). "Came out" (ἐξῆλθεν) is read with WH Treg NA28 RP; the SBLGNT omits it with WH margin.
- **"gathered the vine of the earth, and cast it into the great winepress of the wrath of God" → "gathered the vine of the earth, and cast it into the winepress of the rage of *Elohim*, the great one"** (v19) — Greek εἰς τὴν ληνὸν τοῦ θυμοῦ τοῦ θεοῦ τὸν μέγαν: "great" is masculine and stands last, while "winepress" is feminine here. It is kept at the end; by sense it goes with the winepress.
- **"even unto the horse bridles, by the space of a thousand and six hundred furlongs" → "up to the bridles of the horses, for a thousand six hundred stadia"** (v20) — Greek ἄχρι τῶν χαλινῶν τῶν ἵππων ἀπὸ σταδίων χιλίων ἑξακοσίων; a *stadion* is about 185 metres. ἀπό, literally "from", gives the distance.
