# Obadiah 1

[1] The vision of ⟦obadiah|Ovadyah|Obadiah⟧. Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ concerning Edom:

A report we have heard from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and an envoy has been sent among the nations:
Rise up, and let us rise up against her for the battle!

[2] Behold, small I have made you among the nations;
you are greatly despised.

[3] The presumption of your heart has deceived you,
you who abide in the clefts of the crag,
the height of his dwelling,
who says in his heart, Who will bring me down to the earth?

[4] Though you go high like the eagle,
and though among the stars your nest is set,
from there I will bring you down,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] If thieves came to you,
if wasters by night —
how you are undone! —
would they not steal their enough?
If grape-gatherers came to you,
would they not leave gleanings?

[6] How ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧ has been searched out,
his hidden things sought out!

[7] To the border they have sent you away,
all the men of your covenant;
they have deceived you, they have prevailed against you,
the men of your ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧;
your bread they set as a snare beneath you;
there is no understanding in him.

[8] Is it not in that day, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
that I will make the wise perish out of Edom,
and understanding out of Mount ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧?

[9] And your mighty ones shall be dismayed, Teman,
so that each man is cut off from Mount ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧ by slaughter.

[10] Because of the violence done to your brother ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
shame shall cover you,
and you shall be cut off forever.

[11] In the day that you stood aloof,
in the day that strangers carried off his wealth captive,
and foreigners entered his gates
and cast lots over ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
you also were as one of them.

[12] And do not look on the day of your brother
in the day of his misfortune;
and do not rejoice over the sons of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧
in the day of their perishing;
and do not make your mouth great
in the day of distress.

[13] Do not enter the gate of my people
in the day of their calamity;
do not look, you also, on his evil
in the day of his calamity;
and do not reach out for his wealth
in the day of his calamity.

[14] And do not stand at the crossroads
to cut off his fugitives;
and do not hand over his survivors
in the day of distress.

[15] For near is the **day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧** upon all the nations.
As you have done, it shall be done to you;
your dealing shall return on your head.

[16] For as you have drunk on my holy mountain,
all the nations shall drink continually;
and they shall drink and swallow down,
and shall be as though they had not been.

[17] And on Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ there shall be escape,
and it shall be holy;
and the house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ shall possess their possessions.

[18] And the house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ shall be a fire,
and the house of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ a flame,
and the house of ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧ stubble;
and they shall burn among them and devour them,
and there shall be no survivor to the house of ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧;
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken.

[19] And the ⟦negev|Negev|Negeb⟧ shall possess Mount ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧, and the ⟦shephelah|Shefelah|Shephelah⟧ the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧; and they shall possess the field of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ and the field of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧.

[20] And the exiles of this host of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, who are among the ⟦canaanites|Kenaanim|Canaanites⟧ as far as ⟦zarephath|Tzarfat|Zarephath⟧, and the exiles of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ who are in ⟦sepharad|Sefarad|Sepharad⟧, shall possess the cities of the ⟦negev|Negev|Negeb⟧.

[21] And saviors shall go up on Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ to judge Mount ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧, and **the kingship shall be ⟦yhwh|Yahweh's|the LORD's⟧**.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton; אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה "Lord Yahweh" (v1), both words.
- **Layout and speech marks** — v1–18 are poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line by the accents; the heading (v1a) and v19–21 prose. No quotation marks: the Hebrew does not mark where speech begins and ends. No "O" before Teman (v9): the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"The vision of Obadiah" restored** (v1) — חֲזוֹן עֹבַדְיָה stands in the Hebrew and was missing from the verse; the short form of the name (עֹבַדְיָה, not עֹבַדְיָהוּ).
- **"We have heard a rumour from the LORD, and an ambassador is sent among the heathen, Arise ye, and let us rise up against her in battle" → "A report we have heard from Yahweh, and an envoy has been sent among the nations: Rise up, and let us rise up against her for the battle!"** (v1) — שְׁמוּעָה "report"; צִיר "envoy"; שֻׁלָּח pual perfect "has been sent"; קוּמוּ וְנָקוּמָה, the one verb twice, the second a cohortative. Jer 49:14 has the same words with "I have heard", the participle שָׁלוּחַ "is sent" and another call ("Gather yourselves, and come against her, and rise up for the battle"): each as printed.
- **"I have made thee small among the heathen: thou art greatly despised" → "Behold, small I have made you among the nations; you are greatly despised"** (v2) — הִנֵּה "behold" kept; נְתַתִּיךָ perfect, "I have made (lit. given) you", the Hebrew order as Jer 49:15; מְאֹד "greatly". Jer 49:15 has "despised among mankind" (בָּאָדָם).
- **"The pride of thine heart hath deceived thee, thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock, whose habitation is high; that saith in his heart" → "The presumption of your heart has deceived you, you who abide in the clefts of the crag, the height of his dwelling, who says in his heart"** (v3) — זְדוֹן לִבְּךָ "the presumption of your heart" and שָׁכַן "abide", as Jer 49:16; סֶלַע "crag" (perhaps also the name Sela, Edom's stronghold; uncertain). The Hebrew turns to the third person ("his dwelling", "says in his heart"): kept. אָרֶץ "the earth".
- **"Though thou exalt thyself as the eagle, and though thou set thy nest among the stars" → "Though you go high like the eagle, and though among the stars your nest is set"** (v4) — תַּגְבִּיהַּ hifil "make high", here with no object ("go high"; Jer 49:16 has "your nest" as its object); שִׂים passive participle "is set"; וְאִם "and though"; נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh" (lit. "the declaration of").
- **"If thieves came to thee, if robbers by night, (how art thou cut off!) would they not have stolen till they had enough?" → "If thieves came to you, if wasters by night — how you are undone! — would they not steal their enough?"** (v5) — שֹׁדְדֵי לַיְלָה "wasters of the night" (שָׁדַד "lay waste"); נִדְמֵיתָה nifal of דָּמָה "be undone" (its row, as Jer 47:5; or "be silenced": uncertain); דַּיָּם "their enough". Obad 5 asks with הֲלוֹא "would they not"; Jer 49:9 states "they would not leave … they would ruin": each as printed, not levelled.
- **"would they not leave some grapes?" → "would they not leave gleanings?"** (v5) — עֹלֵלוֹת "gleanings", as Jer 49:9; בֹּצְרִים "grape-gatherers".
- **"How are the things of Esau searched out! how are his hidden things sought up!" → "How Esau has been searched out, his hidden things sought out!"** (v6) — נֶחְפְּשׂוּ, plural verb with Esau (the people); נִבְעוּ "sought out"; מַצְפֻּנָיו "his hidden things" (from צָפַן "hide, treasure up"; Jer 49:10 has another word, מִסְתָּרָיו "his hidden places"). "Pillaged" was not the word.
- **"All the men of thy confederacy have brought thee even to the border: the men that were at peace with thee have deceived thee, and prevailed against thee; they that eat thy bread have laid a wound under thee: there is none understanding in him" → "To the border they have sent you away, all the men of your covenant; they have deceived you, they have prevailed against you, the men of your shalom; your bread they set as a snare beneath you; there is no understanding in him"** (v7) — אַנְשֵׁי בְרִיתֶךָ "the men of your covenant" (not "allies"); אַנְשֵׁי שְׁלֹמֶךָ "the men of your shalom"; לַחְמְךָ is "your bread" alone: KJV "they that eat" is supplied (perhaps "the men of" runs on from the line before); "as" supplied. מָזוֹר elsewhere is "wound" (Hos 5:13; Jer 30:13); here the sense is uncertain: "snare" by the context ("wound" KJV). אֵין תְּבוּנָה בּוֹ "in him", third person (Edom), not "you have".
- **"Shall I not in that day … even destroy the wise men out of Edom" → "Is it not in that day, declares Yahweh, that I will make the wise perish out of Edom"** (v8) — הֲלוֹא בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא "is it not in that day" (בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא "in that day"), the clause carried on by וְהַאֲבַדְתִּי ("that" for the וְ); הֶאֱבִיד "make perish"; חֲכָמִים "the wise" (no "men").
- **"And thy mighty men, O Teman, shall be dismayed, to the end that every one of the mount of Esau may be cut off by slaughter" → "And your mighty ones shall be dismayed, Teman, so that each man is cut off from Mount Esau by slaughter"** (v9) — גִּבּוֹר "mighty one"; חַתּוּ "be dismayed (shattered)"; לְמַעַן "so that"; אִישׁ "each man". מִקָּטֶל "by slaughter" stands in v9 by the accents; the Greek and Latin join it to v10 ("because of the slaughter, because of the violence", from memory).
- **"For thy violence against thy brother Jacob" → "Because of the violence done to your brother Jacob"** (v10) — מֵחֲמַס אָחִיךָ "from the violence of your brother" = done to him (the Gen 16:5 row); "done" supplied.
- **"In the day that thou stoodest on the other side, in the day that the strangers carried away captive his forces, and foreigners entered into his gates, and cast lots upon Jerusalem, even thou wast as one of them" → "In the day that you stood aloof, in the day that strangers carried off his wealth captive, and foreigners entered his gates and cast lots over Jerusalem, you also were as one of them"** (v11) — עֲמָדְךָ מִנֶּגֶד "your standing at a distance"; שְׁבוֹת "carry off captive"; חֵילוֹ "his wealth" (חַיִל also "force, army": one word, as v13); זָרִים "strangers", נָכְרִים "foreigners"; the qere שְׁעָרָיו "his gates" is read (written שערו "his gate"); גַּם־אַתָּה "you also".
- **"But thou shouldest not have looked on the day of thy brother in the day that he became a stranger; neither shouldest thou have rejoiced over the children of Judah in the day of their destruction; neither shouldest thou have spoken proudly in the day of distress" → "And do not look on the day of your brother in the day of his misfortune; and do not rejoice over the sons of Judah in the day of their perishing; and do not make your mouth great in the day of distress"** (v12) — וְאַל + jussive is a prohibition, "do not" (KJV "thou shouldest not have" reads it of the past; the Hebrew form does not); רָאָה בְּ "look on" (with pleasure: "gloat" is the sense, not the word); נָכְרוֹ "his misfortune" (lit. "his strangeness"; uncertain, the נֵכֶר row); בְּנֵי "sons"; אָבְדָם "their perishing"; הִגְדִּיל פֶּה "make the mouth great" = boast.
- **"thou shouldest not have entered … looked on their affliction … nor have laid hands on their substance" → "Do not enter … do not look, you also, on his evil … and do not reach out for his wealth"** (v13) — prohibitions as v12; גַם־אַתָּה "you also" kept; רָעָתוֹ "his evil"; אֵיד "calamity" (three times); תִּשְׁלַחְנָה "send out", with no noun: "hand" is understood ("reach out"); חֵילוֹ "his wealth" as v11.
- **"Neither shouldest thou have stood in the crossway … neither shouldest thou have delivered up those of his that did remain" → "And do not stand at the crossroads to cut off his fugitives; and do not hand over his survivors"** (v14) — פֶּרֶק only here in this sense, "crossroads" (a fork or pass; uncertain); הִכְרִית "cut off"; פָּלִיט "fugitive"; שָׂרִיד "survivor"; הִסְגִּיר "hand over" (lit. "shut up to").
- **"For the day of the LORD is near upon all the heathen … thy reward shall return upon thine own head" → "For near is the day of Yahweh upon all the nations … your dealing shall return on your head"** (v15) — the Hebrew order; גְּמֻלְךָ "your dealing" (what you have done to others, its row); בְּרֹאשֶׁךָ "on your head" (no "own").
- **"For as ye have drunk upon my holy mountain, so shall all the heathen drink continually, yea, they shall drink, and they shall swallow down, and they shall be as though they had not been" → "For as you have drunk on my holy mountain, all the nations shall drink continually; and they shall drink and swallow down, and shall be as though they had not been"** (v16) — שְׁתִיתֶם "you" plural; הַר קָדְשִׁי "my holy mountain" (lit. "the mountain of my holiness"); no "so" in the Hebrew; לָעוּ "swallow down".
- **"But upon mount Zion shall be deliverance, and there shall be holiness; and the house of Jacob shall possess their possessions" → "And on Mount Zion there shall be escape, and it shall be holy; and the house of Jacob shall possess their possessions"** (v17) — פְּלֵיטָה "escape" (the escaping or those who escape; not "deliverance"); וְהָיָה קֹדֶשׁ "and it shall be holiness" = holy (noun); מוֹרָשֵׁיהֶם "their possessions" (the Greek, from memory, reads "those who dispossessed them"; the MT kept).
- **"they shall kindle in them, and devour them; and there shall not be any remaining of the house of Esau" → "and they shall burn among them and devour them, and there shall be no survivor to the house of Esau"** (v18) — דָּלְקוּ בָהֶם "burn among them"; אָכַל "devour" (lit. "eat"); לְקַשׁ "for stubble" ("for" left out in English, as KJV); שָׂרִיד "survivor".
- **"And they of the south shall possess the mount of Esau; and they of the plain the Philistines … the fields of Ephraim, and the fields of Samaria: and Benjamin shall possess Gilead" → "And the Negev shall possess Mount Esau, and the Shephelah the Philistines; and they shall possess the field of Ephraim and the field of Samaria, and Benjamin Gilead"** (v19) — the regions themselves are the subject (= their people: "those of" was supplied); שְׂדֵה "field", singular; the verb is not repeated before Benjamin.
- **"And the captivity of this host of the children of Israel shall possess that of the Canaanites, even unto Zarephath; and the captivity of Jerusalem, which is in Sepharad, shall possess the cities of the south" → "And the exiles of this host of the sons of Israel, who are among the Canaanites as far as Zarephath, and the exiles of Jerusalem who are in Sepharad, shall possess the cities of the Negev"** (v20) — גָּלֻת "exiles"; הַחֵל "host" (חֵל may also be "rampart": uncertain); אֲשֶׁר־כְּנַעֲנִים has no preposition ("who are among" supplied; KJV takes it as an object, "that of the Canaanites"). The atnach after Sepharad makes both groups the subject of the one verb.
- **"And saviours shall come up on mount Zion to judge the mount of Esau; and the kingdom shall be the LORD'S" → "And saviors shall go up on Mount Zion to judge Mount Esau, and the kingship shall be Yahweh's"** (v21) — מוֹשִׁעִים hifil "saviors" (the Greek, from memory, "those who are saved"); שָׁפַט "judge" (not "rule"); מְלוּכָה "kingship" (the rule itself).

### Notes
- **v1–9 beside Jer 49:7–22** — Obad 1–4 ≈ Jer 49:14–16; Obad 5 ≈ Jer 49:9; Obad 6 ≈ Jer 49:10. The same Hebrew has the same words; the differences are kept as printed (v1, 2, 4, 5, 6).
- **Shortest book in the Old Testament** — 21 verses.
- **Edom** — the sons of Esau; frequent prophetic target for their part at Jerusalem's fall (cf. Ps 137:7).
