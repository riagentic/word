# Obadiah 1

[1] Thus says Lord Yahweh concerning Edom:

We have heard a report from Yahweh,
and a messenger has been sent among the nations:
"Rise up! Let us rise against her for battle!"

[2] Behold, I will make you small among the nations;
you shall be utterly despised.

[3] The pride of your heart has deceived you,
you who live in the clefts of the rock,
in your lofty dwelling,
who say in your heart, "Who will bring me down to the ground?"

[4] Though you soar aloft like the eagle,
though your nest is set among the stars,
from there I will bring you down,
declares Yahweh.

[5] If thieves came to you,
if plunderers came by night —
how you have been destroyed! —
would they not steal only enough for themselves?
If grape gatherers came to you,
would they not leave gleanings?

[6] How Esau has been pillaged,
his treasures sought out!

[7] All your allies have driven you to your border;
those at peace with you have deceived you;
they have prevailed against you;
those who eat your bread have set a trap beneath you;
you have no understanding.

[8] Will I not on that day, declares Yahweh,
destroy the wise men out of Edom,
and understanding out of Mount Esau?

[9] And your mighty men shall be dismayed, O Teman,
so that every man from Mount Esau will be cut off by slaughter.

[10] Because of the violence done to your brother Jacob,
shame shall cover you,
and you shall be cut off forever.

[11] On the day that you stood aloof,
on the day that strangers carried off his wealth
and foreigners entered his gates
and cast lots for Jerusalem,
you were like one of them.

[12] But do not gloat over the day of your brother
in the day of his misfortune;
do not rejoice over the people of Judah
in the day of their ruin;
do not boast
in the day of distress.

[13] Do not enter the gate of my people
in the day of their calamity;
do not gloat over his disaster
in the day of his calamity;
do not loot his wealth
in the day of his calamity.

[14] Do not stand at the crossroads
to cut off his fugitives;
do not hand over his survivors
in the day of distress.

[15] For the **Day of Yahweh** is near upon all the nations.
As you have done, it shall be done to you;
your deeds shall return on your own head.

[16] For as you have drunk on my holy mountain,
so all the nations shall drink continually;
they shall drink and swallow,
and shall be as though they had never been.

[17] But in Mount Zion there shall be those who escape,
and it shall be holy,
and the house of Jacob shall possess their own possessions.

[18] The house of Jacob shall be a fire,
and the house of Joseph a flame,
and the house of Esau stubble;
they shall burn them and consume them,
and there shall be no survivor for the house of Esau,
for Yahweh has spoken.

[19] Those of the Negev shall possess Mount Esau, and those of the Shephelah shall possess the land of the Philistines; they shall possess the land of Ephraim and the land of Samaria, and Benjamin shall possess Gilead.

[20] The exiles of this host of the people of Israel shall possess the land of the Canaanites as far as Zarephath, and the exiles of Jerusalem who are in Sepharad shall possess the cities of the Negev.

[21] Saviors shall go up to Mount Zion to rule Mount Esau, and **the kingdom shall be Yahweh's**.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v1) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוָה.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (vv1-18) — KJV prose; the entire oracle against Edom is Hebrew poetry.
- **"a rumour from the LORD" → "a report from Yahweh"** (v1) — Hebrew שְׁמוּעָה מֵאֵת יְהוָה *shemuah me'et Yahweh* = a report/message from Yahweh; "report" more precise than "rumour."
- **"an ambassador is sent among the heathen" → "a messenger has been sent among the nations"** (v1) — Hebrew צִיר שֻׁלַּח בַּגּוֹיִם *tzir shullach baggoyim*; *tzir* = envoy/messenger; "nations" for *goyim*.
- **"Arise ye, and let us rise up against her in battle" → "Rise up! Let us rise against her for battle"** (v1) — modernized; exclamatory form preserved.
- **"I have made thee small among the heathen: thou art greatly despised" → "I will make you small among the nations; you shall be utterly despised"** (v2) — future tense for prophetic announcement; "nations" for *goyim*.
- **"whose habitation is high" → "in your lofty dwelling"** (v3) — Hebrew מְרוֹם שִׁבְתּוֹ *merom shivto* = height of his dwelling; "lofty dwelling" more vivid.
- **"thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock" → "you who live in the clefts of the rock"** (v3) — modernized; Edom's mountain stronghold (Petra).
- **"Though thou exalt thyself as the eagle" → "Though you soar aloft like the eagle"** (v4) — Hebrew תַּגְבִּיהַ כַּנֶּשֶׁר *tagbiha kannnesher*; "soar aloft" vivid.
- **"(how art thou cut off!)" → "— how you have been destroyed! —"** (v5) — Hebrew אֵיךְ נִדְמֵיתָ *eikh nidmeita* = how you are destroyed/silenced; parenthetical interjection preserved with dashes.
- **"if robbers by night" → "if plunderers came by night"** (v5) — Hebrew שֹׁדְדֵי לַיְלָה *shodedey laylah* = night plunderers; "plunderers" more specific.
- **"would they not leave some grapes" → "would they not leave gleanings"** (v5) — Hebrew עֹלֵלוֹת *olelot* = gleanings left after harvest; "gleanings" technical term retained.
- **"How are the things of Esau searched out! how are his hidden things sought up" → "How Esau has been pillaged, his treasures sought out"** (v6) — Hebrew נִבְעוּ עֵשָׂו *niv'u Esav* = Esau has been ransacked; "pillaged/treasures" more vivid.
- **"All the men of thy confederacy" → "All your allies"** (v7) — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי בְרִיתֶךָ *anshey veritkhah* = men of your covenant; "allies" captures the political relationship.
- **"the men that were at peace with thee" → "those at peace with you"** (v7) — modernized.
- **"they that eat thy bread have laid a wound under thee" → "those who eat your bread have set a trap beneath you"** (v7) — Hebrew מַזּוֹר *mazzor* = wound/trap; "trap" follows the context of betrayal.
- **"there is none understanding in him" → "you have no understanding"** (v7) — Hebrew אֵין תְּבוּנָה בּוֹ; direct address to Edom.
- **"Shall I not in that day, saith the LORD, even destroy the wise men out of Edom" → "Will I not on that day, declares Yahweh, destroy the wise men out of Edom"** (v8) — modernized interrogative.
- **"For thy violence against thy brother Jacob" → "Because of the violence done to your brother Jacob"** (v10) — Hebrew מֵחֲמַס אָחִיךָ יַעֲקֹב *mechamac achikha Ya'aqov*; restructured.
- **"In the day that thou stoodest on the other side" → "On the day that you stood aloof"** (v11) — Hebrew בְּיוֹם עֲמָדְךָ מִנֶּגֶד *beyom amadkhah minneged*; "stood aloof" = deliberately uninvolved.
- **"the strangers carried away captive his forces" → "strangers carried off his wealth"** (v11) — Hebrew שְׁבוּ חֵילוֹ *shevu cheylo*; *chayil* = wealth/army; "wealth" fits context of looting.
- **"thou shouldest not have looked on the day of thy brother … neither shouldest thou have rejoiced … neither shouldest thou have spoken proudly" → "do not gloat … do not rejoice … do not boast"** (v12) — KJV past jussive; Hebrew uses prohibitive jussive (אַל תִּרְאֶה *al tir'eh*); present prohibitives more vivid.
- **"spoken proudly" → "boast"** (v12) — Hebrew תַּגְדֵּל פִּיךָ *tagdel pikha* = enlarge your mouth = boast; "boast" clearer.
- **"their affliction" → "his disaster"** (v13) — Hebrew רָעָתוֹ *ra'ato* = his evil/disaster; consistent with "calamity."
- **"nor have laid hands on their substance" → "do not loot his wealth"** (v13) — Hebrew חֵילוֹ *cheylo*; "loot" more vivid.
- **"stood in the crossway" → "stand at the crossroads"** (v14) — Hebrew פֶּרֶק *pereq* = crossroads/mountain pass; "crossroads" more natural.
- **"those of his that did escape" → "his fugitives"** (v14) — Hebrew פְּלִיטָיו *pelitav* = his fugitives/escapees.
- **"those of his that did remain" → "his survivors"** (v14) — Hebrew שְׂרִידָיו *seridav* = his survivors.
- **"as thou hast done, it shall be done unto thee: thy reward shall return upon thine own head" → "As you have done, it shall be done to you; your deeds shall return on your own head"** (v15) — modernized; "deeds" for *gemulkhah* = your dealing/recompense.
- **"the day of the LORD" → "the Day of Yahweh"** (v15) — consistent eschatological formula.
- **"upon all the heathen" → "upon all the nations"** (v15) — *goyim*.
- **"they shall drink, and they shall swallow down" → "they shall drink and swallow"** (v16) — modernized.
- **"they shall be as though they had not been" → "shall be as though they had never been"** (v16) — modernized; "never" stronger.
- **"But upon mount Zion shall be deliverance, and there shall be holiness" → "But in Mount Zion there shall be those who escape, and it shall be holy"** (v17) — Hebrew פְּלֵיטָה *peleytah* = escape/those who escape; "holiness" → "it shall be holy."
- **"they shall kindle in them, and devour them" → "they shall burn them and consume them"** (v18) — modernized.
- **"there shall not be any remaining of the house of Esau" → "there shall be no survivor for the house of Esau"** (v18) — Hebrew שָׂרִיד לְבֵית עֵשָׂו *sarid leveit Esav*; "survivor" more precise.
- **"they of the south" → "Those of the Negev"** (v19) — Hebrew הַנֶּגֶב *haNegev*; the Negev is the specific southern region.
- **"the fields of Ephraim, and the fields of Samaria" → "the land of Ephraim and the land of Samaria"** (v19) — Hebrew שְׂדֵה *sedeh* = field/land; "land" more natural.
- **"the kingdom shall be the LORD's" → "the kingdom shall be Yahweh's"** (v21) — divine name restored; the climactic closing declaration emphasized in bold.

### Notes
- **Shortest book in OT** — 21 verses.
- **Edom** — descendants of Esau; frequent prophetic target for their treachery at Jerusalem's fall (cf. Ps 137:7).
