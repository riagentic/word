# 1 John 4

[1] Beloved, do not believe every spirit, but test the spirits, whether they are of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, because many false prophets have gone out into the world.

[2] By this you know the Spirit of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: every spirit that confesses ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ having come in flesh is of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[3] and every spirit that does not confess ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ is not of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and this is that of the antichrist, of which you have heard that it is coming, and now it is in the world already.

[4] You are of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, little children, and have overcome them, because **he who is in you is greater than he who is in the world**.

[5] They are of the world; because of this they speak from the world, and the world hears them.

[6] We are of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; the one who knows ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ hears us; he who is not of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ does not hear us. From this we know the Spirit of truth and the spirit of error.

[7] Beloved, let us love one another, because love is of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and everyone who loves has been begotten of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and knows ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[8] The one who does not love did not know ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, because **⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is love**.

[9] In this the love of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was manifested in us, that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has sent his only Son into the world so that we might live through him.

[10] In this is love, not that we have loved ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, but that he loved us and sent his Son as propitiation for our sins.

[11] Beloved, if ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ so loved us, we also ought to love one another.

[12] No one has ever beheld ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; if we love one another, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ abides in us and his love has been perfected in us.

[13] By this we know that we abide in him and he in us, because he has given us of his Spirit.

[14] And we have beheld and testify that the Father has sent the Son as Savior of the world.

[15] Whoever confesses that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ is the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ abides in him, and he in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[16] And we have come to know and have believed the love which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has in us. **⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is love, and the one who abides in love abides in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ abides in him.**

[17] In this love has been perfected with us, that we may have boldness in the day of judgment, because just as that one is, we also are in this world.

[18] **There is no fear in love, but perfect love casts out fear**, because fear has punishment, and the one who fears has not been perfected in love.

[19] We love, because he first loved us.

[20] If anyone says, "I love ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧," and hates his brother, he is a liar; for the one who does not love his brother whom he has seen is not able to love ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ whom he has not seen.

[21] And this commandment we have from him, that the one who loves ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ love his brother also.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v2) — systematic.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v3, 15) — systematic.
- **"try the spirits whether they are of God" → "test the spirits, whether they are of *Elohim*"** (v1) — Greek δοκιμάζετε, "put to the proof"; εἶναι ἐκ is "be of" throughout the letter (2:16, 19; 3:8–10, 19).
- **"Hereby know ye" → "By this you know"** (v2) — Greek γινώσκετε, which can be a statement or a command ("know").
- **"confesseth that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh" → "confesses *Yeshua* the *Mashiach* having come in flesh"** (v2) — Greek ὁμολογεῖ Ἰησοῦν Χριστὸν ἐν σαρκὶ ἐληλυθότα: a direct object with a perfect participle, not a "that" clause; no article with "flesh". Compare 2 John 7 ("coming", a present participle).
- **"confesseth not that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh" → "does not confess *Yeshua*"** (v3) — NA28 ὃ μὴ ὁμολογεῖ τὸν Ἰησοῦν; the longer wording is not in the critical text.
- **"this is that spirit of antichrist, whereof ye have heard that it should come" → "this is that of the antichrist, of which you have heard that it is coming"** (v3) — Greek τὸ τοῦ ἀντιχρίστου, a neuter article without a noun ("spirit" is understood from the context); ἔρχεται, present.
- **"therefore speak they of the world, and the world heareth them" → "because of this they speak from the world, and the world hears them"** (v5, 6) — Greek ἐκ τοῦ κόσμου λαλοῦσιν, the same ἐκ as "of the world": the world is the source of their speech, not its subject; ἀκούει "hear".
- **"Hereby know we" → "From this we know"** (v6) — Greek ἐκ τούτου, not ἐν τούτῳ.
- **"the spirit of error" kept** (v6) — Greek πλάνη, "wandering, error", the noun of the verb "deceive" (πλανάω, 1:8; 2:26; 3:7). The capital in "Spirit of truth" is the translator's; Greek does not mark it.
- **"is born of God" → "has been begotten of *Elohim*"** (v7) — Greek γεγέννηται; see 2:29.
- **"knoweth not God" → "did not know *Elohim*"** (v8) — Greek οὐκ ἔγνω, an aorist, as in 3:1.
- **"toward us" → "in us"** (v9) — Greek ἐν ἡμῖν, "in us" or "among us"; the same words in v12, 16.
- **"God sent his only begotten Son" → "*Elohim* has sent his only Son"** (v9) — Greek ἀπέσταλκεν, a perfect (so v14; an aorist in v10); τὸν υἱὸν αὐτοῦ τὸν μονογενῆ: μονογενής is "the only one of its kind" (μόνος + γένος), used of an only child (Luke 7:12; 8:42; Heb 11:17); it is not formed from γεννάω "beget", the verb of v7.
- **"to be the propitiation" → "as propitiation"** (v10) — Greek ἱλασμόν without the article and without "to be"; see 2:2.
- **"No man hath seen God at any time" → "No one has ever beheld *Elohim*"** (v12, 14) — Greek τεθέαται, τεθεάμεθα, the verb of 1:1 ("beheld"), a different word from "seen" in v20 (ἑώρακεν).
- **"his love is perfected in us" → "his love has been perfected in us"** (v12, 17, 18) — Greek τετελειωμένη ἐστίν, τετελείωται, perfects.
- **"sent the Son to be the Saviour of the world" → "has sent the Son as Savior of the world"** (v14) — Greek σωτῆρα τοῦ κόσμου, without "to be".
- **"the love that God hath to us" → "the love which *Elohim* has in us"** (v16) — Greek ἐν ἡμῖν, as in v9; "believed" takes "the love" as its direct object (πεπιστεύκαμεν τὴν ἀγάπην).
- **"Herein is our love made perfect" → "In this love has been perfected with us"** (v17) — Greek ἡ ἀγάπη μεθ' ἡμῶν, "love … with us", not "our love". "In this" can point back (v16) or forward ("that we may have boldness").
- **"as he is" → "just as that one is"** (v17) — Greek ἐκεῖνος, as in 2:6.
- **"fear hath torment" → "fear has punishment"** (v18) — Greek κόλασιν ἔχει; κόλασις is "punishment" (Matt 25:46). ἔξω βάλλει is "throws outside".
- **"We love him, because he first loved us" → "We love, because he first loved us"** (v19) — NA28 ἡμεῖς ἀγαπῶμεν, with no object; the form can also be "let us love".
- **"how can he love God whom he hath not seen?" → "is not able to love *Elohim* whom he has not seen"** (v20) — NA28 οὐ δύναται, a statement; KJV follows πῶς δύναται, a question.
- **"That he who loveth God love his brother also" kept** (v21) — Greek ἵνα … ἀγαπᾷ; there is no word for "must".
