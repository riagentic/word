# 1 John 4

[1] Beloved, do not believe every spirit, but test the spirits to see whether they are from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for many false prophets have gone out into the world.

[2] By this you know the Spirit of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: every spirit that confesses that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ has come in the flesh is from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[3] and every spirit that does not confess ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ is not from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. This is the spirit of the antichrist, which you heard was coming and now is in the world already.

[4] Little children, you are from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and have overcome them, for **he who is in you is greater than he who is in the world**.

[5] They are from the world; therefore they speak from the world, and the world listens to them.

[6] We are from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. Whoever knows ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ listens to us; whoever is not from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ does not listen to us. By this we know the Spirit of truth and the spirit of error.

[7] Beloved, let us love one another, for love is from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and whoever loves has been born of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and knows ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[8] Anyone who does not love does not know ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, because **⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is love**.

[9] In this the love of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was made manifest among us, that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ sent his only Son into the world, so that we might live through him.

[10] In this is love, not that we have loved ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ but that he loved us and sent his Son to be the propitiation for our sins.

[11] Beloved, if ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ so loved us, we also ought to love one another.

[12] No one has ever seen ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; if we love one another, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ abides in us and his love is perfected in us.

[13] By this we know that we abide in him and he in us, because he has given us of his Spirit.

[14] And we have seen and testify that the Father has sent his Son to be the Savior of the world.

[15] Whoever confesses that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ is the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ abides in him, and he in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[16] So we have come to know and to believe the love that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has for us. **⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is love, and whoever abides in love abides in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ abides in him.**

[17] By this is love perfected with us, so that we may have confidence for the day of judgment, because as he is so also are we in this world.

[18] **There is no fear in love, but perfect love casts out fear.** For fear has to do with punishment, and whoever fears has not been perfected in love.

[19] We love because he first loved us.

[20] If anyone says, "I love ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧," and hates his brother, he is a liar; for he who does not love his brother whom he has seen cannot love ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ whom he has not seen.

[21] And this commandment we have from him: whoever loves ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ must also love his brother.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v2) — systematic.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v3, 15) — systematic.
- **"charity" → "love"** (throughout) — KJV uses "charity" for Greek ἀγάπη *agapē*; "charity" in 1611 English meant "Christian love"; in modern English it has narrowed to mean philanthropic giving; "love" restores the intended meaning throughout.
- **"Try the spirits" → "test the spirits"** (v1) — "try" in KJV = "test / examine"; modernized.
- **"Fear hath torment" → "fear has to do with punishment"** (v18) — Greek ὁ δὲ φοβούμενος οὐ τετελείωται ἐν τῇ ἀγάπῃ; κόλασιν *kolasin* = "punishment / penalty / torment"; "has to do with punishment" is more accurate than "hath torment."
