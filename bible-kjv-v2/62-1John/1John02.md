# 1 John 2

[1] My little children, these things I write to you so that you may not sin. And if anyone sins, we have an advocate with the Father, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ the righteous,

[2] and he is the propitiation for our sins, and not for ours only but also for the whole world.

[3] And by this we know that we have come to know him, if we keep his commandments.

[4] The one who says, "I have come to know him," and does not keep his commandments is a liar, and the truth is not in this one;

[5] but whoever keeps his word, truly in this one the love of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has been perfected. By this we know that we are in him:

[6] the one who says he abides in him ought himself also to walk just as that one walked.

[7] Beloved, I write no new commandment to you, but an old commandment which you had from the beginning; the old commandment is the word which you heard.

[8] Again, a new commandment I write to you, which thing is true in him and in you, because the darkness is passing away and the true light already shines.

[9] The one who says he is in the light and hates his brother is in the darkness until now.

[10] The one who loves his brother abides in the light, and there is no stumbling block in him;

[11] but the one who hates his brother is in the darkness and walks in the darkness, and does not know where he is going, because the darkness blinded his eyes.

[12] I write to you, little children,
because your sins have been forgiven you on account of his name.
[13] I write to you, fathers,
because you have come to know him who is from the beginning.
I write to you, young men,
because you have overcome the evil one.

[14] I wrote to you, young children,
because you have come to know the Father.
I wrote to you, fathers,
because you have come to know him who is from the beginning.
I wrote to you, young men,
because you are strong,
and the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ abides in you,
and you have overcome the evil one.

[15] **Do not love the world nor the things in the world.** If anyone loves the world, the love of the Father is not in him;

[16] because all that is in the world — the desire of the flesh and the desire of the eyes and the boastfulness of livelihood — is not of the Father but is of the world;

[17] and the world is passing away, and its desire, but the one who does the will of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ abides forever.

[18] Young children, it is the last hour, and just as you heard that antichrist is coming, even now many antichrists have come to be; from this we know that it is the last hour.

[19] They went out from us, but they were not of us; for if they had been of us, they would have abided with us; but — so that they might be manifested, that they are not all of us.

[20] And you have an anointing from the Holy One, and you all know.

[21] I did not write to you because you do not know the truth, but because you know it, and because no lie is of the truth.

[22] Who is the liar, if not the one who denies that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ is the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧? This is the antichrist, the one who denies the Father and the Son.

[23] Everyone who denies the Son does not have the Father either; the one who confesses the Son has the Father also.

[24] As for you, what you heard from the beginning, let it abide in you. If what you heard from the beginning abides in you, you also will abide in the Son and in the Father.

[25] And this is the promise which he himself promised us: the eternal life.

[26] These things I wrote to you concerning those who deceive you.

[27] And as for you, the anointing which you received from him abides in you, and you have no need that anyone teach you; but as his anointing teaches you concerning all things, and is true and is not a lie, and just as it taught you, abide in him.

[28] And now, little children, abide in him, so that if he is manifested we may have boldness and not be put to shame away from him at his coming.

[29] If you know that he is righteous, you know that everyone also who does righteousness has been begotten of him.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v1) — systematic.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v22) — systematic.
- **"little children"** (v1, 12, 28) / **"young children"** (v14, 18) — two Greek words: τεκνία (from τέκνον, a child by birth) and παιδία (from παῖς, a child by age). KJV has "little children" for both.
- **"Jesus Christ the righteous" kept** (v1) — Greek Ἰησοῦν Χριστὸν δίκαιον has no article before "righteous": "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*, a righteous one". παράκλητος "advocate" is one called to someone's side; the same word is used of the Spirit in John 14:16.
- **"propitiation" kept** (v2) — Greek ἱλασμός (without the article), the means by which sins are covered and favor restored; also 4:10.
- **"but also for the sins of the whole world" → "but also for the whole world"** (v2) — Greek ἀλλὰ καὶ περὶ ὅλου τοῦ κόσμου; "the sins of" is not in the Greek.
- **"hereby we do know that we know him" → "by this we know that we have come to know him"** (v3, 4, 13, 14) — Greek γινώσκομεν (present) and ἐγνώκαμεν, ἔγνωκα, ἐγνώκατε (perfect), as in 2 John 1.
- **"in him verily is the love of God perfected" → "truly in this one the love of *Elohim* has been perfected"** (v5) — Greek ἐν τούτῳ … τετελείωται, a perfect; "the love of *Elohim*" (ἡ ἀγάπη τοῦ θεοῦ) can be his love for us or our love for him.
- **"even as he walked" → "just as that one walked"** (v6) — Greek ἐκεῖνος, "that one", the letter's word for the Son (3:3, 5, 7, 16; 4:17), distinct from αὐτός "he / him". NA28 and RP add οὕτως, "so to walk"; SBLGNT, WH and Treg do not.
- **"Brethren" → "Beloved"** (v7) — NA28 Ἀγαπητοί. KJV's second "from the beginning" at the end of the verse is not in the critical text.
- **"which thing is true" kept** (v8) — Greek ὅ ἐστιν ἀληθές, neuter: it does not refer to "commandment" (feminine) as such.
- **"is in darkness even until now" → "is in the darkness until now"** (v9) — Greek ἐν τῇ σκοτίᾳ ἐστὶν ἕως ἄρτι.
- **"there is none occasion of stumbling in him" → "there is no stumbling block in him"** (v10) — Greek σκάνδαλον, a trap or a thing one stumbles over; ἐν αὐτῷ can be "in him" or "in it" (the light).
- **"hath blinded" → "blinded"** (v11) — Greek ἐτύφλωσεν, an aorist.
- **"are forgiven you for his name's sake" → "have been forgiven you on account of his name"** (v12) — Greek ἀφέωνται, a perfect; διὰ τὸ ὄνομα αὐτοῦ. In v12–14 ὅτι can be "because" or "that".
- **"I write unto you, little children" (KJV v13) → "I wrote to you, young children"** (v14) — NA28 ἔγραψα, an aorist, three times in v14 after three presents (γράφω) in v12–13; the critical text counts this line with v14.
- **"lust" → "desire"** (v16, 17) — Greek ἐπιθυμία, singular all three times.
- **"abideth for ever" → "abides forever"** (v17) — Greek εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα, literally "unto the age"; the fixed phrase is kept as "forever", as in 2 John 2.
- **"the pride of life" → "the boastfulness of livelihood"** (v16) — Greek ἡ ἀλαζονεία τοῦ βίου: ἀλαζονεία is the empty boasting of a braggart; βίος is the course and means of living (as in 3:17), a different word from ζωή, the "life" of 1:1–2; 2:25; 3:14.
- **"it is the last time" → "it is the last hour"** (v18) — Greek ἐσχάτη ὥρα ἐστίν, without the article.
- **"antichrist shall come, even now are there many antichrists" → "antichrist is coming, even now many antichrists have come to be"** (v18) — Greek ἔρχεται, present, and γεγόνασιν, "have come into being", a different verb.
- **"they would no doubt have continued with us" → "they would have abided with us"** (v19) — Greek μεμενήκεισαν, the letter's verb μένω "abide".
- **"but they went out, that they might be made manifest that they were not all of us" → "but — so that they might be manifested, that they are not all of us"** (v19) — Greek ἀλλ' ἵνα φανερωθῶσιν ὅτι οὐκ εἰσὶν πάντες ἐξ ἡμῶν; "they went out" is not repeated in the Greek. The words can mean "none of them is of us" or "not all are of us".
- **"ye have an unction from the Holy One, and ye know all things" → "you have an anointing from the Holy One, and you all know"** (v20) — Greek χρῖσμα "anointing", the root of χριστός; NA28 οἴδατε πάντες, "you all know" (KJV follows the reading πάντα, "all things").
- **"I have not written" → "I did not write"** (v21, 26) — Greek ἔγραψα.
- **"He is antichrist" → "This is the antichrist"** (v22) — Greek οὗτός ἐστιν ὁ ἀντίχριστος, with the article.
- **"Whosoever denieth the Son, the same hath not the Father" → "Everyone who denies the Son does not have the Father either"** (v23) — Greek οὐδὲ τὸν πατέρα ἔχει. The second half of the verse, in italics in the KJV, is in the critical text.
- **"Let that therefore abide in you" → "As for you, what you heard from the beginning, let it abide in you"** (v24) — Greek ὑμεῖς stands first, outside the clause; "therefore" is not in the critical text.
- **"the promise that he hath promised us" → "the promise which he himself promised us"** (v25) — Greek αὐτὸς ἐπηγγείλατο, an emphatic "he" and an aorist.
- **"them that seduce you" → "those who deceive you"** (v26) — Greek τῶν πλανώντων, a present participle of the verb in 1:8 and 3:7; there is no word for "trying".
- **"the same anointing" → "his anointing"** (v27) — NA28 τὸ αὐτοῦ χρῖσμα.
- **"even as it hath taught you, ye shall abide in him" → "just as it taught you, abide in him"** (v27) — NA28 μένετε, which can be a command ("abide") or a statement ("you abide"); KJV follows the future μενεῖτε. The subject of "taught" can be "it" (the anointing) or "he"; ἐν αὐτῷ can be "in him" or "in it".
- **"when he shall appear, we may have confidence, and not be ashamed before him" → "if he is manifested we may have boldness and not be put to shame away from him"** (v28) — NA28 ἐὰν φανερωθῇ, "if" (the time is left open, not the fact; KJV follows ὅταν "when"), and the passive of φανερόω as in 1:2; παρρησία "boldness" (3:21; 4:17; 5:14); αἰσχυνθῶμεν ἀπ' αὐτοῦ, literally "be shamed from him". παρουσία "coming" is also "presence".
- **"ye know that every one that doeth righteousness is born of him" → "you know that everyone also who does righteousness has been begotten of him"** (v29) — ὅτι καὶ πᾶς, "that everyone also" (NA28, Treg); SBLGNT, WH and RP have ὅτι πᾶς, without "also". γινώσκετε can be a statement or a command ("know"); it is a different verb from the first "know" (εἰδῆτε). γεγέννηται, a perfect of γεννάω: with ἐκ and the father named it is "beget" (so 3:9; 4:7; 5:1, 4, 18); the same verb also means "be born".
