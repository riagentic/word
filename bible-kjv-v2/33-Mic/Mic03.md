# Micah 3

[1] And I said:
Hear, you heads of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧
and rulers of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧!
Is it not for you to know justice? —

[2] you who hate the good and love the evil,
who tear the skin from off my people
and their flesh from off their bones,

[3] who eat the flesh of my people,
and flay their skin from off them,
and break their bones in pieces
and chop them up like meat in a pot,
like flesh in a cauldron.

[4] Then they will cry to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
but he will not answer them;
he will hide his face from them at that time,
because they have made their deeds evil.

[5] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ concerning the prophets who lead my people astray, who cry "Peace" when they have something to eat, but declare war against him who puts nothing into their mouths.

[6] Therefore it shall be night to you, without vision,
and darkness to you, without divination.
The sun shall go down on the prophets,
and the day shall be black over them;

[7] the seers shall be disgraced,
and the diviners put to shame;
they shall all cover their lips,
for there is no answer from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[8] But as for me, I am filled with power,
with the Spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and with justice and might,
to declare to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ his transgression
and to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ his sin.

[9] Hear this, you heads of the house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧
and rulers of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
who detest justice
and make crooked all that is straight,

[10] who build ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ with blood
and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ with iniquity.

[11] Its heads give judgment for a bribe;
its priests teach for a price;
its prophets practice divination for money;
yet they lean on ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and say,
"Is not ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in the midst of us?
No disaster shall come upon us."

[12] Therefore because of you
**⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ shall be plowed as a field;
⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ shall become a heap of ruins,
and the mountain of the house a wooded height.**

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v7) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **"the spirit of the LORD" → "the Spirit of Yahweh"** (v8) — Hebrew רוּחַ יְהוָה *ruach Yahweh*; *Ruach* = spirit/breath/wind; retained.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; the entire chapter is poetry.
- **"I pray you, O heads of Jacob, and ye princes of the house of Israel" → "Hear, you heads of Jacob and rulers of the house of Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew שִׁמְעוּ נָא רָאשֵׁי יַעֲקֹב וּקְצִינֵי בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל; "rulers" for *qetziney* = chiefs/rulers; more precise than "princes."
- **"Is it not for you to know judgment?" → "Is it not for you to know justice?"** (v1) — Hebrew הֲלוֹא לָכֶם לָדַעַת אֶת הַמִּשְׁפָּט; *mishpat* = justice; "justice" consistent.
- **"who pluck off their skin from off them" → "who tear the skin from off my people"** (v2) — Hebrew גֹּזְלֵי עוֹר מֵעַל *gozley or me'al*; "tear" more vivid than "pluck."
- **"Who also eat the flesh of my people, and flay their skin from off them; and they break their bones, and chop them in pieces, as for the pot, and as flesh within the caldron" → "who eat the flesh of my people, and flay their skin from off them, and break their bones in pieces and chop them up like meat in a pot, like flesh in a cauldron"** (v3) — modernized; "cauldron" for *qalachat*.
- **"but he will not hear them" → "but he will not answer them"** (v4) — Hebrew לֹא יַעֲנֶה *lo ya'aneh* = will not answer; more precise than "hear."
- **"as they have behaved themselves ill in their doings" → "because they have made their deeds evil"** (v4) — modernized.
- **"the prophets that make my people err" → "the prophets who lead my people astray"** (v5) — Hebrew הַמַּתְעִים אֶת עַמִּי; "lead astray" more vivid.
- **"he that putteth not into their mouths, they even prepare war against him" → "he who puts nothing into their mouths, they declare war against him"** (v5) — modernized.
- **"Therefore night shall be unto you, that ye shall not have a vision; and it shall be dark unto you, that ye shall not divine" → "Therefore it shall be night to you, without vision, and darkness to you, without divination"** (v6) — restructured; "without vision … without divination" more compact.
- **"Then shall the seers be ashamed, and the diviners confounded" → "the seers shall be disgraced, and the diviners put to shame"** (v7) — Hebrew יֵבֹשׁוּ הַחֹזִים *yevoshu hachozim* = ashamed/disgraced; "disgraced" more vivid.
- **"for there is no answer of God" → "for there is no answer from *Elohim*"** (v7) — Hebrew אֵין מַעֲנֵה אֱלֹהִים *eyn ma'aneh Elohim*; *Elohim* retained.
- **"But truly I am full of power by the spirit of the LORD, and of judgment, and of might" → "But as for me, I am filled with power, with the Spirit of Yahweh, and with justice and might"** (v8) — Hebrew כִּי אָנֹכִי מָלֵאתִי כֹחַ אֶת רוּחַ יְהוָה; *Ruach Yahweh* retained; "justice" for *mishpat*.
- **"ye princes of the house of Israel, that abhor judgment, and pervert all equity" → "rulers of the house of Israel, who detest justice and make crooked all that is straight"** (v9) — Hebrew הַמְתַעֲבִים מִשְׁפָּט *hamte'avim mishpat* = who abhor justice; "detest" more vivid; "make crooked all that is straight" for *kol hayyesharah yeaqqeshu*.
- **"The heads thereof judge for reward, and the priests thereof teach for hire, and the prophets thereof divine for money" → "Its heads give judgment for a bribe; its priests teach for a price; its prophets practice divination for money"** (v11) — modernized; "bribe" for *shochad*; "price" for *mekhir*; "practice divination" for *yiqsamu*.
- **"yet will they lean upon the LORD" → "yet they lean on Yahweh"** (v11) — modernized.
- **"none evil can come upon us" → "No disaster shall come upon us"** (v11) — Hebrew לֹא תָבוֹא עָלֵינוּ רָעָה; "disaster" for *ra'ah*.
- **"Therefore shall Zion for your sake be plowed as a field; and Jerusalem shall become heaps, and the mountain of the house as the high places of the forest" → "Zion shall be plowed as a field; Jerusalem shall become a heap of ruins, and the mountain of the house a wooded height"** (v12) — Hebrew יַעַר *ya'ar* = forest/wooded place; "wooded height" more precise than "high places of the forest"; verse emphasized in bold as a climactic oracle.

### Notes
- **v12** — quoted by elders defending Jeremiah in Jer 26:18.
