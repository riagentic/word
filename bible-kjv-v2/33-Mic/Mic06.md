# Micah 6

[1] Hear, please, what ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is saying:
Arise, contend with the mountains,
and let the hills hear your voice.

[2] Hear, mountains, the case of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and you enduring ones, foundations of the earth;
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has a case with his people,
and with ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ he will argue it.

[3] My people, what have I done to you?
And how have I wearied you? Answer against me.

[4] For I brought you up from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
and from the house of slaves I ransomed you;
and I sent before you ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and ⟦miriam|Miryam|Miriam⟧.

[5] My people, remember, please, what ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧ king of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ counseled,
and what ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ son of Beor answered him —
from Shittim to Gilgal —
so that you may know the righteous acts of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] With what shall I come to meet ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
bow myself to the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of the height?
Shall I come to meet him with burnt offerings,
with calves a year old?

[7] Will ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ be pleased with thousands of rams,
with ten thousands of torrents of oil?
Shall I give my firstborn for my transgression,
the fruit of my belly for the sin of my soul?

[8] **He has declared to you, man, what is good;
and what does ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ seek from you
but to do judgment,
and to love ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
and to walk humbly with your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?**

[9] The voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ calls to the city —
and sound wisdom sees your name —
hear the rod, and who has appointed it.

[10] Are there yet, in the house of the wicked, treasures of wickedness,
and the lean ephah that is cursed?

[11] Shall I be pure with scales of wickedness
and with a bag of stones of deceit?

[12] For her rich men are full of violence,
and her inhabitants have spoken falsehood,
and their tongue is treachery in their mouth.

[13] And I also, I have made you sick, striking you,
making you desolate because of your sins.

[14] You, you shall eat and not be satisfied,
and your emptiness shall be in your midst;
and you shall move away and not bring to safety,
and what you bring to safety I will give to the sword.

[15] You, you shall sow and not reap;
you, you shall tread olives and not anoint with oil,
and new wine, and not drink wine.

[16] And the statutes of Omri are kept,
and all the work of the house of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧,
and you walk in their counsels,
so that I may give you to a thing that appalls, and her inhabitants to hissing;
and the reproach of my people you shall bear.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton.
- **Layout and speech marks** — poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line by the accents. The quotation marks are dropped: the Hebrew does not mark where Yahweh, the questioner of v6–7 and the prophet begin and end. No "O" before "my people" and "man" (v3, 5, 8): the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"Hear ye now what the LORD saith; Arise, contend thou before the mountains" → "Hear, please, what Yahweh is saying: Arise, contend with the mountains"** (v1) — נָא "please"; אֹמֵר participle "is saying"; רִיב אֶת "contend with" (the רִיב row).
- **"Hear ye, O mountains, the LORD's controversy, and ye strong foundations of the earth: for the LORD hath a controversy with his people, and he will plead with Israel" → "Hear, mountains, the case of Yahweh, and you enduring ones, foundations of the earth; for Yahweh has a case with his people, and with Israel he will argue it"** (v2) — רִיב "case" (the noun, in law); הָאֵתָנִים "the enduring ones" (in apposition to "foundations of the earth"); יִתְוַכָּח hitpael of the הוֹכִיחַ root, "argue it (out)".
- **"O my people, what have I done unto thee? and wherein have I wearied thee? testify against me" → "My people, what have I done to you? And how have I wearied you? Answer against me"** (v3) — הֶלְאֵתִיךָ "wearied you" (its sound answers הֶעֱלִתִיךָ "I brought you up", v4); עֲנֵה בִּי "answer against me" (= testify; the עָנָה בְּ row).
- **"For I brought thee up out of the land of Egypt, and redeemed thee out of the house of servants" → "For I brought you up from the land of Egypt, and from the house of slaves I ransomed you"** (v4) — פָּדָה "ransom" (not גָּאַל "redeem"); עֲבָדִים "slaves" (the עֶבֶד row); the three names stand with one "and", as the Hebrew.
- **"remember now what Balak king of Moab consulted, and what Balaam the son of Beor answered him from Shittim unto Gilgal; that ye may know the righteousness of the LORD" → "remember, please, what Balak king of Moab counseled, and what Balaam son of Beor answered him — from Shittim to Gilgal — so that you may know the righteous acts of Yahweh"** (v5) — זְכָר־נָא "remember, please"; יָעַץ "counsel"; מִן־הַשִּׁטִּים עַד־הַגִּלְגָּל stands with no verb ("what happened" was supplied: the crossing of the Jordan, Josh 3–4, is meant); לְמַעַן "so that"; צִדְקוֹת "righteous acts" (plural of צְדָקָה).
- **"Wherewith shall I come before the LORD, and bow myself before the high God? shall I come before him with burnt offerings, with calves of a year old?" → "With what shall I come to meet Yahweh, bow myself to the Elohim of the height? Shall I come to meet him with burnt offerings, with calves a year old?"** (v6) — קִדֵּם "come to meet" (its row, twice); כָּפַף "bow down" (its row); אֱלֹהֵי מָרוֹם "the Elohim of the height"; עֲגָלִים בְּנֵי שָׁנָה lit. "calves, sons of a year".
- **"with ten thousands of rivers of oil … the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul" → "with ten thousands of torrents of oil … the fruit of my belly for the sin of my soul"** (v7) — רָצָה "be pleased"; נַחֲלֵי שֶׁמֶן "torrents of oil" (the נַחַל row: "torrent" of the water itself); בֶּטֶן "belly"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul".
- **"He hath shewed thee, O man, what is good; and what doth the LORD require of thee, but to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy God?" → "He has declared to you, man, what is good; and what does Yahweh seek from you but to do judgment, and to love chesed, and to walk humbly with your Elohim?"** (v8) — הִגִּיד "declared" (no subject named: "he"); אָדָם "man" (mankind, the vocative; no "O"); דּוֹרֵשׁ "seek" (the דָּרַשׁ row; "require" in its sense); כִּי אִם after the question "what … ?" is "but" (the reviewer's row: one thing in place of the offerings of v6–7); עֲשׂוֹת מִשְׁפָּט "to do judgment" (מִשְׁפָּט "judgment", the rightful ruling and its doing); אַהֲבַת חֶסֶד "to love chesed" (the token, not "mercy", "kindness"); הַצְנֵעַ לֶכֶת "to walk humbly": הַצְנֵעַ hifil infinitive of צָנַע, only here and Prov 11:2 צְנוּעִים: "humbly" is the received sense; "carefully, wisely, attentively" is also read (lit. "to make the walking modest / circumspect"): uncertain.
- **"The LORD's voice crieth unto the city, and the man of wisdom shall see thy name: hear ye the rod, and who hath appointed it" → "The voice of Yahweh calls to the city — and sound wisdom sees your name — hear the rod, and who has appointed it"** (v9) — קָרָא "call"; תּוּשִׁיָּה "sound wisdom" (its row) is the subject of יִרְאֶה "sees" (the Greek and others read יִרְאַת "the fear of", from memory; the MT kept); מַטֶּה "rod" (also "tribe": שִׁמְעוּ מַטֶּה may be "hear, tribe"; uncertain); יְעָדָהּ "has appointed it" (feminine: the rod, or the city).
- **"Are there yet the treasures of wickedness in the house of the wicked, and the scant measure that is abominable?" → "Are there yet, in the house of the wicked, treasures of wickedness, and the lean ephah that is cursed?"** (v10) — הַאִשׁ "is there?" ("are" for the English plural; אִשׁ = יֵשׁ, as 2 Sam 14:19; the verse as printed is hard: "can I forget" reads הַאֶשֶּׁה, a change of the text); רָשָׁע "wicked", רֶשַׁע "wickedness"; אֵיפַת רָזוֹן "a lean ephah" (a short measure); זְעוּמָה "cursed" (denounced).
- **"Shall I count them pure with the wicked balances, and with the bag of deceitful weights?" → "Shall I be pure with scales of wickedness and with a bag of stones of deceit?"** (v11) — הַאֶזְכֶּה qal "shall I be pure?" (as printed; "count pure" reads the piel); מֹאזְנֵי רֶשַׁע "scales of wickedness"; אַבְנֵי מִרְמָה "stones of deceit" (the weights).
- **"For the rich men thereof are full of violence, and the inhabitants thereof have spoken lies, and their tongue is deceitful in their mouth" → "For her rich men are full of violence, and her inhabitants have spoken falsehood, and their tongue is treachery in their mouth"** (v12) — אֲשֶׁר "for" (lit. "which"); "her" (the city of v9); דִּבְּרוּ perfect "have spoken"; שֶׁקֶר "falsehood"; רְמִיָּה "treachery" (beside מִרְמָה "deceit", v11).
- **"Therefore also will I make thee sick in smiting thee, in making thee desolate because of thy sins" → "And I also, I have made you sick, striking you, making you desolate because of your sins"** (v13) — וְגַם־אֲנִי "and I also"; הֶחֱלֵיתִי perfect "I have made sick" (the Greek reads "I have begun", הַחִלּוֹתִי, from memory; the MT kept); הַכּוֹתֶךָ "striking you"; הַשְׁמֵם "making desolate" ("you" carried over).
- **"Thou shalt eat, but not be satisfied; and thy casting down shall be in the midst of thee; and thou shalt take hold, but shalt not deliver; and that which thou deliverest will I give up to the sword" → "You, you shall eat and not be satisfied, and your emptiness shall be in your midst; and you shall move away and not bring to safety, and what you bring to safety I will give to the sword"** (v14) — the stressed אַתָּה (v14, 15); יֶשַׁח, only here, "emptiness" (hunger; uncertain); תַּסֵּג "move away" (or "remove", "take hold": uncertain); פָּלַט hifil "bring to safety".
- **"thou shalt tread the olives, but thou shalt not anoint thee with oil; and sweet wine, but shalt not drink wine" → "you, you shall tread olives and not anoint with oil, and new wine, and not drink wine"** (v15) — זַיִת singular (the olive crop); סוּךְ "anoint" (no object); תִּירוֹשׁ "new wine" (the line has no verb of its own: "tread" carries over).
- **"For the statutes of Omri are kept, and all the works of the house of Ahab, and ye walk in their counsels; that I should make thee a desolation, and the inhabitants thereof an hissing: therefore ye shall bear the reproach of my people" → "And the statutes of Omri are kept, and all the work of the house of Ahab, and you walk in their counsels, so that I may give you to a thing that appalls, and her inhabitants to hissing; and the reproach of my people you shall bear"** (v16) — וְ "and"; יִשְׁתַּמֵּר hitpael "are kept (carefully)"; חֻקּוֹת "statutes"; מַעֲשֵׂה "work" (singular); לְמַעַן "so that"; נָתַן לְ "give to"; שַׁמָּה "a thing that appalls" (its row); שְׁרֵקָה "hissing"; "her inhabitants" (the city, third person); חֶרְפָּה "reproach".

### Notes
- **v6–8** — the questioner offers the costliest gifts; the answer names judgment, chesed and the humble walk with Elohim.
