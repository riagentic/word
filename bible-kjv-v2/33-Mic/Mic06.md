# Micah 6

[1] Hear what Yahweh says:
Arise, plead your case before the mountains,
and let the hills hear your voice.

[2] Hear, you mountains, the dispute of Yahweh,
and you enduring foundations of the earth,
for Yahweh has a case against his people,
and he will contend with Israel.

[3] "O my people, what have I done to you?
How have I wearied you? Answer me!

[4] For I brought you up from the land of Egypt
and redeemed you from the house of slavery,
and I sent before you Moses, Aaron, and Miriam.

[5] O my people, remember what Balak king of Moab devised,
and what Balaam the son of Beor answered him,
and what happened from Shittim to Gilgal,
that you may know the righteous acts of Yahweh."

[6] "With what shall I come before Yahweh,
and bow myself before the *Elohim* on high?
Shall I come before him with burnt offerings,
with calves a year old?

[7] Will Yahweh be pleased with thousands of rams,
with ten thousands of rivers of oil?
Shall I give my firstborn for my transgression,
the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?"

[8] **He has told you, O man, what is good;
and what does Yahweh require of you
but to do justice,
and to love steadfast love,
and to walk humbly with your *Elohim*?**

[9] The voice of Yahweh cries to the city —
and it is sound wisdom to fear your name:
"Hear of the rod and of him who appointed it!

[10] Can I forget any longer the treasures of wickedness in the house of the wicked,
and the scant measure that is accursed?

[11] Shall I acquit the man with wicked scales
and with a bag of deceitful weights?

[12] Your rich men are full of violence;
your inhabitants speak lies,
and their tongue is deceitful in their mouth.

[13] Therefore I strike you with a grievous blow,
making you desolate because of your sins.

[14] You shall eat, but not be satisfied,
and there shall be hunger within you;
you shall put away, but not preserve,
and what you preserve I will give to the sword.

[15] You shall sow, but not reap;
you shall tread olives, but not anoint yourselves with oil;
you shall tread grapes, but not drink wine.

[16] For you have kept the statutes of Omri,
and all the works of the house of Ahab;
and you have walked in their counsels,
that I may make you a desolation, and your inhabitants a hissing;
so you shall bear the scorn of my people."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv7, 8) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; the entire chapter is poetry (the covenant lawsuit).
- **"the LORD's controversy" → "the dispute of Yahweh"** (v2) — Hebrew רִיב יְהוָה *riv Yahweh* = Yahweh's covenant lawsuit; *riv* retained; same legal vocabulary as Hosea 4 and 12.
- **"ye strong foundations of the earth" → "you enduring foundations of the earth"** (v2) — Hebrew מֹסְדֵי אֶרֶץ *mosedey eretz*; "enduring foundations" more vivid.
- **"for the LORD hath a controversy with his people, and he will plead with Israel" → "for Yahweh has a case against his people, and he will contend with Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew רִיב לַיהוָה עִם עַמּוֹ *riv laYahweh im ammo*; "case" for *riv*; "contend" for *yokach*.
- **"Arise, contend thou before the mountains" → "Arise, plead your case before the mountains"** (v1) — Hebrew רִיב אֶת הֶהָרִים; "plead your case" for *riv* (to contend in court).
- **"wherein have I wearied thee? testify against me" → "How have I wearied you? Answer me"** (v3) — Hebrew עֲנֵה בִי *aneh vi* = answer/testify against me; "Answer me" more direct.
- **"the house of servants" → "the house of slavery"** (v4) — Hebrew מִבֵּית עֲבָדִים *mibeit avadim*; "slavery" more vivid.
- **"Balak king of Moab consulted" → "what Balak king of Moab devised"** (v5) — Hebrew יָעַץ *ya'atz* = planned/devised; "devised" more precise.
- **"that ye may know the righteousness of the LORD" → "that you may know the righteous acts of Yahweh"** (v5) — Hebrew צִדְקוֹת יְהוָה *tzidqot Yahweh* = righteous deeds/acts of Yahweh; "righteous acts" more concrete than "righteousness."
- **"bow myself before the high God" → "bow myself before the *Elohim* on high"** (v6) — Hebrew אֵל מָרוֹם *El Marom* = the exalted God / God on high.
- **"to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy God" → "to do justice, and to love steadfast love, and to walk humbly with your *Elohim*"** (v8) — Hebrew עֲשׂוֹת מִשְׁפָּט וְאַהֲבַת חֶסֶד וְהַצְנֵעַ לֶכֶת עִם אֱלֹהֶיךָ; *mishpat* = justice; *chesed* = covenant love retained; "walk humbly" for *hatzne'a lekhet* = to walk modestly/humbly; *Elohim* retained; the famous triad preserved precisely.
- **"The LORD's voice crieth unto the city, and the man of wisdom shall see thy name: hear ye the rod, and who hath appointed it" → "The voice of Yahweh cries to the city — and it is sound wisdom to fear your name: 'Hear of the rod and of him who appointed it'"** (v9) — Hebrew וְתוּשִׁיָּה יִרְאֶה שְׁמֶךָ *vetushiyyah yir'eh shemekha* = sound wisdom sees/fears your name; the verse restructured; Yahweh's name should be feared.
- **"Are there yet the treasures of wickedness in the house of the wicked, and the scant measure that is abominable?" → "Can I forget any longer the treasures of wickedness in the house of the wicked, and the scant measure that is accursed?"** (v10) — Hebrew הַאֶשְׁכַּח עוֹד *ha'eshkach od* = shall I forget still?; rhetorical question retained.
- **"Shall I count them pure with the wicked balances" → "Shall I acquit the man with wicked scales"** (v11) — Hebrew הַאֶזְכֶּה בְּמֹאזְנֵי רֶשַׁע; *tzakkeh* = acquit/declare pure; "acquit" preserves the legal metaphor.
- **"Therefore also will I make thee sick in smiting thee, in making thee desolate because of thy sins" → "Therefore I strike you with a grievous blow, making you desolate because of your sins"** (v13) — modernized; "grievous blow" for *hachelo hakkhot*.
- **"thy casting down shall be in the midst of thee; and thou shalt take hold, but shalt not deliver" → "there shall be hunger within you; you shall put away, but not preserve"** (v14) — Hebrew יֵשְׁחֲךָ בְּקִרְבֶּךָ *yeshachakha beqirbekha* = your emptiness within you; "hunger within you" captures the futility.
- **"ye shall bear the reproach of my people" → "so you shall bear the scorn of my people"** (v16) — Hebrew חֶרְפַּת עַמִּי *cherpat ammi*; "scorn" for *cherpah* = shame/reproach; the scorn of the nations against Yahweh's people returns on Israel.

### Notes
- **v8** — the famous summary of what Yahweh requires: justice, steadfast love, humble walk.
