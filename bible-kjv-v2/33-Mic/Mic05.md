# Micah 5

[1] Now muster your troops, O daughter of troops;
siege is laid against us;
with a rod they strike the judge of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧
on the cheek.

[2] **But you, O ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧ ⟦ephrathah|Efratah|Ephrathah⟧,
who are too little to be among the clans of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,
from you shall come forth for me
one who is to be ruler in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
whose ⟦motzaot|motza'ot — goings forth — are|coming forth is⟧ from of old,
from ancient days.**

[3] Therefore he shall give them up until the time
when she who is in labor has given birth;
then the rest of his brothers shall return
to the people of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[4] And he shall stand and shepherd his flock in the strength of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
in the majesty of the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.
And they shall dwell secure, for now he shall be great
to the ends of the earth.

[5] And he shall be their peace.
When ⟦assyria|Ashur|the Assyrian⟧ comes into our land
and treads in our palaces,
then we will raise against him seven shepherds
and eight princes of men;

[6] they shall shepherd the land of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ with the sword,
and the land of Nimrod at its entrances;
and he shall deliver us from ⟦assyria|Ashur|the Assyrian⟧
when he comes into our land
and treads within our border.

[7] Then the remnant of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ shall be
in the midst of many peoples
like dew from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
like showers on the grass,
which delay not for a man
nor wait for the children of man.

[8] And the remnant of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ shall be among the nations,
in the midst of many peoples,
like a lion among the beasts of the forest,
like a young lion among the flocks of sheep,
which, when it goes through, treads down
and tears in pieces, and there is none to deliver.

[9] Your hand shall be lifted up over your adversaries,
and all your enemies shall be cut off.

[10] "And in that day," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
"I will cut off your horses from among you
and will destroy your chariots;

[11] and I will cut off the cities of your land
and throw down all your strongholds;

[12] and I will cut off sorceries from your hand,
and you shall have no more tellers of fortunes;

[13] and I will cut off your carved images
and your pillars from among you,
and you shall bow down no more
to the work of your hands;

[14] and I will root out your Asherah poles from among you
and destroy your cities.

[15] And in anger and wrath I will execute vengeance
on the nations that did not obey."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; the entire chapter is poetry.
- **"Now gather thyself in troops, O daughter of troops" → "Now muster your troops, O daughter of troops"** (v1) — Hebrew גְּדֻדִי בַת גְּדוּד *gedudi bat gedud*; "muster your troops" more vivid.
- **"he hath laid siege against us" → "siege is laid against us"** (v1) — modernized passive.
- **"they shall smite the judge of Israel with a rod upon the cheek" → "with a rod they strike the judge of Israel on the cheek"** (v1) — modernized; may refer to Hezekiah or a messianic figure.
- **"yet out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel" → "from you shall come forth for me one who is to be ruler in Israel"** (v2) — modernized.
- **"until the time that she which travaileth hath brought forth" → "until the time when she who is in labor has given birth"** (v3) — modernized.
- **"then the remnant of his brethren shall return unto the children of Israel" → "then the rest of his brothers shall return to the people of Israel"** (v3) — modernized.
- **"And he shall stand and feed in the strength of the LORD, in the majesty of the name of the LORD his God" → "And he shall stand and shepherd his flock in the strength of Yahweh, in the majesty of the name of Yahweh his *Elohim*"** (v4) — Hebrew רָעָה *ra'ah* = shepherd/feed; "shepherd his flock" more vivid; *Elohim* retained.
- **"when he shall tread in our palaces" → "and treads in our palaces"** (v5) — Hebrew וְיִדְרֹךְ בְּאַרְמְנֹתֵינוּ; modernized.
- **"eight principal men" → "eight princes of men"** (v5) — Hebrew שְׁמֹנָה נְסִיכֵי אָדָם *shemonah nesikhe adam*; *nasikh* = prince/leader.
- **"they shall waste the land of Assyria with the sword, and the land of Nimrod in the entrances thereof" → "they shall shepherd the land of Assyria with the sword, and the land of Nimrod at its entrances"** (v6) — Hebrew וְרָעוּ *vera'u* = and they shall shepherd/rule; same root as v4; "shepherd the land" = rule it by force; "at its entrances" for *bepetacheha*.
- **"that tarrieth not for man, nor waiteth for the sons of men" → "which delay not for a man nor wait for the children of man"** (v7) — modernized.
- **"as a lion among the beasts of the forest, as a young lion among the flocks of sheep: who, if he go through, both treadeth down, and teareth in pieces, and none can deliver" → "like a lion among the beasts of the forest, like a young lion among the flocks of sheep, which, when it goes through, treads down and tears in pieces, and there is none to deliver"** (v8) — modernized.
- **"among the Gentiles" → "among the nations"** (v8) — Hebrew בַּגּוֹיִם *baggoyim*; "nations" consistent.
- **"Thine hand shall be lifted up upon thine adversaries" → "Your hand shall be lifted up over your adversaries"** (v9) — modernized.
- **"I will cut off witchcrafts out of thine hand; and thou shalt have no more soothsayers" → "and I will cut off sorceries from your hand, and you shall have no more tellers of fortunes"** (v12) — Hebrew כְּשָׁפִים *kesafim* = sorceries; עוֹנְנִים *onenim* = cloud-readers/fortune-tellers; modernized.
- **"thy standing images" → "your pillars"** (v13) — Hebrew מַצֵּבֹתֶיךָ *matzevotekha* = sacred pillars/standing stones; "pillars" more precise.
- **"upon the heathen, such as they have not heard" → "on the nations that did not obey"** (v15) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר לֹא שָׁמֵעוּ *asher lo shame'u* = who did not hear/obey; "that did not obey" more precise.

### Notes
- **v2** — quoted by the chief priests in Matt 2:6 concerning Yeshua's Bethlehem birthplace.
- **"Goings forth from of old"**: Christian interpretation finds pre-existence.
