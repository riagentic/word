# Micah 5

[1] Now gash yourself, daughter of a raiding band;
a siege he has laid against us;
with the rod they strike on the cheek
the judge of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[2] **And you, ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧ ⟦ephrathah|Efratah|Ephrathah⟧,
small to be among the thousands of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,
from you one shall come out to me
to be ruler in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
and his ⟦motzaot|motzaot|comings forth⟧ are from of old,
from days of eternity.**

[3] Therefore he will give them up until the time
that she who is giving birth has given birth;
and the rest of his brothers shall return
to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[4] And he shall stand and shepherd in the strength of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
in the majesty of the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
and they shall dwell, for now he shall be great
to the ends of the earth.

[5] And this one shall be ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧.
⟦assyria|Ashur|the Assyrian⟧ — when he comes into our land,
and when he treads in our citadels,
we will raise against him seven shepherds
and eight princes of mankind;

[6] and they shall shepherd the land of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ with the sword,
and the land of Nimrod in its entrances;
and he shall deliver from ⟦assyria|Ashur|the Assyrian⟧,
when he comes into our land,
and when he treads within our border.

[7] And the remnant of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ shall be
in the midst of many peoples
like dew from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
like showers on the grass,
which does not wait for a man
nor hope for the sons of man.

[8] And the remnant of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ shall be among the nations,
in the midst of many peoples,
like a lion among the beasts of the forest,
like a young lion among the droves of the flock,
which, if it passes through, tramples
and tears, and there is none to deliver.

[9] Let your hand be high over your adversaries,
and all your enemies shall be cut off.

[10] And it shall be in that day, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
that I will cut off your horses from your midst
and make your chariots perish;

[11] and I will cut off the cities of your land
and break down all your fortresses;

[12] and I will cut off sorceries from your hand,
and you shall have no soothsayers;

[13] and I will cut off your carved images
and your pillars from your midst,
and you shall bow down no more
to the work of your hands;

[14] and I will pluck up your Asherim from your midst
and destroy your cities.

[15] And I will execute in anger and in wrath vengeance
on the nations that have not heard.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton.
- **Layout and speech marks** — poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line by the accents. The quotation marks of v10–15 are dropped: the Hebrew does not mark them. No "O" before "daughter" and Bethlehem (v1, 2): the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"Now gather thyself in troops, O daughter of troops: he hath laid siege against us: they shall smite the judge of Israel with a rod upon the cheek" → "Now gash yourself, daughter of a raiding band; a siege he has laid against us; with the rod they strike on the cheek the judge of Israel"** (v1, Heb 4:14) — תִּתְגֹּדְדִי "gash yourself" (the הִתְגֹּדֵד row: the mourner's cutting; "gather in troops" takes it from גְּדוּד; the sound plays on בַּת־גְּדוּד); גְּדוּד "raiding band" (its row); שָׂם "he has laid" (no subject named); שֵׁבֶט "rod"; the Hebrew order.
- **"But thou, Bethlehem Ephratah, though thou be little among the thousands of Judah, yet out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel; whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting" → "And you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, small to be among the thousands of Judah, from you one shall come out to me to be ruler in Israel, and his goings out are from of old, from days of eternity"** (v2, Heb 5:1) — word for word: וְאַתָּה "and you"; צָעִיר לִהְיוֹת "small to be" (no "though … yet"); אַלְפֵי "thousands" (the clans counted by thousands); מִמְּךָ לִי יֵצֵא "from you to me shall come out" ("one" supplied for the unnamed subject); מוֹשֵׁל "ruler"; וּמוֹצָאֹתָיו "and his goings out" (plural noun of יָצָא "come out" of the line before: his origins, or his comings forth; the token kept, its forms without the gloss and the verb they carried: *motzaot* / "comings forth", the meaning "goings out" here); מִקֶּדֶם "from of old"; מִימֵי עוֹלָם "from days of eternity" (עוֹלָם, time out of sight). Matt 2:6 quotes the verse in another form ("by no means least among the rulers of Judah … who will shepherd my people Israel"), joined with 2 Sam 5:2: the Hebrew is translated as it stands, the two not levelled.
- **"Therefore will he give them up, until the time that she which travaileth hath brought forth: then the remnant of his brethren shall return unto the children of Israel" → "Therefore he will give them up until the time that she who is giving birth has given birth; and the rest of his brothers shall return to the sons of Israel"** (v3) — יוֹלֵדָה יָלָדָה "she who gives birth has given birth"; יֶתֶר "rest"; עַל "to" (lit. "upon, with"); בְּנֵי "sons".
- **"And he shall stand and feed in the strength of the LORD … and they shall abide: for now shall he be great unto the ends of the earth" → "And he shall stand and shepherd in the strength of Yahweh … and they shall dwell, for now he shall be great to the ends of the earth"** (v4) — רָעָה "shepherd" (no object: "his flock" was supplied); גָּאוֹן "majesty"; יָשַׁב "dwell" ("secure" was supplied); אַפְסֵי־אָרֶץ "the ends of the earth".
- **"And this man shall be the peace, when the Assyrian shall come into our land: and when he shall tread in our palaces, then shall we raise against him seven shepherds, and eight principal men" → "And this one shall be shalom. Ashur — when he comes into our land, and when he treads in our citadels, we will raise against him seven shepherds and eight princes of mankind"** (v5, Heb 5:4) — וְהָיָה זֶה שָׁלוֹם "and this one shall be shalom" (זֶה "this one", the ruler, or "this" of what follows: open; the accent divides after שָׁלוֹם); אַשּׁוּר stands first, a hanging subject; אַרְמְנוֹת "citadels"; נְסִיכֵי אָדָם "princes of mankind" (the נָסִיךְ row).
- **"And they shall waste the land of Assyria with the sword, and the land of Nimrod in the entrances thereof: thus shall he deliver us from the Assyrian" → "and they shall shepherd the land of Ashur with the sword, and the land of Nimrod in its entrances; and he shall deliver from Ashur"** (v6) — וְרָעוּ "they shall shepherd" (the verb of v4, of a rod of rule; KJV "waste" reads רָעַע "break"); בִּפְתָחֶיהָ "in its entrances" (the Greek and others read "with the drawn sword", פְּתִחָה, from memory; the MT kept); וְהִצִּיל "and he shall deliver" (no object: "us" was supplied).
- **"as the showers upon the grass, that tarrieth not for man, nor waiteth for the sons of men" → "like showers on the grass, which does not wait for a man nor hope for the sons of man"** (v7) — רְבִיבִים "showers"; עֵשֶׂב "grass"; קִוָּה "wait for", יִחֵל "hope for" (the קִוָּה row); לְאִישׁ "for a man"; בְּנֵי אָדָם "sons of man" (the reviewer's row).
- **"as a young lion among the flocks of sheep: who, if he go through, both treadeth down, and teareth in pieces, and none can deliver" → "like a young lion among the droves of the flock, which, if it passes through, tramples and tears, and there is none to deliver"** (v8) — כְּפִיר "young lion"; עֶדְרֵי צֹאן "droves of the flock" (the צֹאן / עֵדֶר rows); רָמַס "trample"; טָרַף "tear"; וְאֵין מַצִּיל "and there is none to deliver".
- **"Thine hand shall be lifted up upon thine adversaries" → "Let your hand be high over your adversaries"** (v9) — תָּרֹם jussive "let … be high"; צָרִים "adversaries"; the second line is a plain imperfect ("shall be cut off").
- **"And it shall come to pass in that day, saith the LORD, that I will cut off thy horses out of the midst of thee, and I will destroy thy chariots" → "And it shall be in that day, declares Yahweh, that I will cut off your horses from your midst and make your chariots perish"** (v10) — "And it shall be in that day" (the prophets' row); הִכְרִית "cut off" (four times, v10–13); הֶאֱבִיד "make perish".
- **"throw down all thy strong holds" → "break down all your fortresses"** (v11) — הָרַס "break down"; מִבְצָר "fortress".
- **"And I will cut off witchcrafts out of thine hand; and thou shalt have no more soothsayers" → "and I will cut off sorceries from your hand, and you shall have no soothsayers"** (v12) — כְּשָׁפִים "sorceries"; מְעוֹנְנִים "soothsayers" (the עוֹנֵן row: the exact practice is not known); "more" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Thy graven images also will I cut off, and thy standing images out of the midst of thee" → "and I will cut off your carved images and your pillars from your midst"** (v13) — פְּסִילִים "carved images"; מַצֵּבָה "pillar" (a stone set up); מִקִּרְבֶּךָ "from your midst" (as v10, 14).
- **"And I will pluck up thy groves out of the midst of thee" → "and I will pluck up your Asherim from your midst"** (v14) — אֲשֵׁרִים "Asherim" (the wooden cult poles; not "groves"); נָתַשׁ "pluck up"; הִשְׁמִיד "destroy".
- **"And I will execute vengeance in anger and fury upon the heathen, such as they have not heard" → "And I will execute in anger and in wrath vengeance on the nations that have not heard"** (v15) — the Hebrew order; בְּאַף וּבְחֵמָה "in anger and in wrath" (the בְּ twice); אֲשֶׁר לֹא שָׁמֵעוּ "that have not heard" (the שָׁמַע row: "obey" is not the word; KJV "such as they have not heard" takes it of the vengeance).

### Notes
- **Numbering** — our 5:1 = Heb 4:14; our 5:2–15 = Heb 5:1–14.
- **v2** — quoted by the chief priests and scribes in Matt 2:6 for the birthplace of the Mashiach. "His goings out are from of old, from days of eternity" is read by Christian interpreters of the ruler's being before his birth, by others of his descent from David's ancient house (Bethlehem); the Hebrew words allow both.
