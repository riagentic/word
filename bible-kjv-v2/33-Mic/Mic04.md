# Micah 4

[1] It shall come to pass in the latter days
that the mountain of the house of Yahweh
shall be established as the highest of the mountains,
and it shall be lifted up above the hills;
and peoples shall flow to it,

[2] and many nations shall come, and say:
"Come, let us go up to the mountain of Yahweh,
to the house of the *Elohim* of Jacob,
that he may teach us his ways,
and that we may walk in his paths."
For out of Zion shall go forth the Law,
and the word of Yahweh from Jerusalem.

[3] He shall judge between many peoples,
and shall decide for strong nations afar off;
**and they shall beat their swords into plowshares,
and their spears into pruning hooks;
nation shall not lift up sword against nation,
neither shall they learn war anymore;**

[4] but they shall sit every man under his vine and under his fig tree,
and no one shall make them afraid,
for the mouth of Yahweh of Hosts has spoken.

[5] For all the peoples walk
each in the name of its god,
but we will walk in the name of Yahweh our *Elohim*
forever and ever.

[6] In that day, declares Yahweh,
I will assemble the lame
and gather those who have been driven away
and those whom I have afflicted;

[7] and the lame I will make the remnant,
and those who were cast off, a strong nation;
and Yahweh will reign over them in Mount Zion
from this time forth and forevermore.

[8] And you, O tower of the flock,
hill of the daughter of Zion,
to you shall it come,
the former dominion shall come,
kingship for the daughter of Jerusalem.

[9] Now why do you cry aloud?
Is there no king in you?
Has your counselor perished,
that pain seized you like a woman in labor?

[10] Writhe and groan, O daughter of Zion,
like a woman in labor,
for now you shall go out from the city
and dwell in the open country;
you shall go to Babylon.
There you shall be rescued;
there Yahweh will redeem you
from the hand of your enemies.

[11] Now many nations
are assembled against you,
saying, "Let her be defiled,
and let our eyes gaze upon Zion."

[12] But they do not know
the thoughts of Yahweh;
they do not understand his plan,
that he has gathered them as sheaves to the threshing floor.

[13] Arise and thresh, O daughter of Zion,
for I will make your horn iron,
and I will make your hoofs bronze;
you shall beat in pieces many peoples;
and I will devote their gain to Yahweh,
their wealth to the Lord of the whole earth.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv2, 5) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v4) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת; *Tzeva'ot* retained.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; the entire chapter is poetry.
- **"But in the last days it shall come to pass" → "It shall come to pass in the latter days"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּאַחֲרִית הַיָּמִים *be'acharit hayamim* = in the end/latter days; restructured for poetic order.
- **"shall be established in the top of the mountains, and it shall be exalted above the hills" → "shall be established as the highest of the mountains, and it shall be lifted up above the hills"** (v1) — modernized.
- **"people shall flow unto it" → "peoples shall flow to it"** (v1) — Hebrew עַמִּים *amim* = peoples (plural); "peoples" more precise.
- **"the house of the God of Jacob" → "the house of the *Elohim* of Jacob"** (v2) — *Elohim* retained.
- **"He shall judge among many people, and rebuke strong nations afar off" → "He shall judge between many peoples, and shall decide for strong nations afar off"** (v3) — Hebrew שָׁפַט *shaphat* = judge; יָכַח *yakach* = decide/rebuke; "decide" more precise for legal arbitration.
- **"their spears into pruninghooks" → "their spears into pruning hooks"** (v3) — modernized spelling.
- **"neither shall they learn war any more" → "neither shall they learn war anymore"** (v3) — modernized.
- **"none shall make them afraid" → "no one shall make them afraid"** (v4) — modernized.
- **"for the mouth of the LORD of hosts hath spoken it" → "for the mouth of Yahweh of Hosts has spoken"** (v4) — *Tzeva'ot* retained.
- **"For all people will walk every one in the name of his god" → "For all the peoples walk each in the name of its god"** (v5) — Hebrew כִּי כָּל הָעַמִּים יֵלְכוּ; "peoples" plural; lowercase "god" for foreign deities.
- **"we will walk in the name of the LORD our God for ever and ever" → "but we will walk in the name of Yahweh our *Elohim* forever and ever"** (v5) — *Elohim* retained.
- **"In that day, saith the LORD" → "In that day, declares Yahweh"** (v6) — modernized prophetic formula.
- **"will I assemble her that halteth" → "I will assemble the lame"** (v6) — Hebrew הַצֹּלֵעָה *hatzolea'ah* = the lame/limping one; "lame" more natural.
- **"her that is driven out" → "those who have been driven away"** (v6) — Hebrew הַנִּדָּחָה *haniddachah*.
- **"the LORD shall reign over them in mount Zion from henceforth, even for ever" → "Yahweh will reign over them in Mount Zion from this time forth and forevermore"** (v7) — modernized.
- **"the strong hold of the daughter of Zion" → "hill of the daughter of Zion"** (v8) — Hebrew עֹפֶל בַּת צִיּוֹן *Ofel bat Tzion* = the hill/mound of the daughter of Zion; *Ofel* = specific hill south of the Temple Mount.
- **"the first dominion" → "the former dominion"** (v8) — Hebrew הַמֶּמְשָׁלָה הָרִאשֹׁנָה; "former" captures the restoration of past sovereignty.
- **"for pangs have taken thee as a woman in travail" → "that pain seized you like a woman in labor"** (v9) — Hebrew חִיל כַּיּוֹלֵדָה *chil kayyoledah*; "in labor" more natural than "in travail."
- **"Be in pain, and labour to bring forth, O daughter of Zion, like a woman in travail" → "Writhe and groan, O daughter of Zion, like a woman in labor"** (v10) — Hebrew גֹּחִי וּפֹחִי *gochi upochi* = writhe and groan; more vivid.
- **"thou shalt dwell in the field" → "dwell in the open country"** (v10) — Hebrew בַּשָּׂדֶה *bassadeh* = in the field/open country; "open country" more natural.
- **"Let her be defiled, and let our eye look upon Zion" → "'Let her be defiled, and let our eyes gaze upon Zion'"** (v11) — Hebrew וְתַחֵז בְּצִיּוֹן עֵינֵינוּ; "eyes" plural; "gaze" more vivid.
- **"for he shall gather them as the sheaves into the floor" → "that he has gathered them as sheaves to the threshing floor"** (v12) — Hebrew כְּעָמִיר גֹּרֶן *ke'amir goren*; "threshing floor" more explicit.
- **"and I will consecrate their gain unto the LORD, and their substance unto the Lord of the whole earth" → "and I will devote their gain to Yahweh, their wealth to the Lord of the whole earth"** (v13) — Hebrew וְהַחֲרַמְתִּי לַיהוָה בִּצְעָם *vehacharamti laYahweh bitza'am* = consecrate/devote as *cherem* (sacred ban); "devote" preserves the *cherem* concept; MT vocalizes 1st person "I will devote"; LXX, Syriac and Targum read the consonants as an archaic 2nd fem. sg. ("you shall devote", continuing the address to the daughter of Zion); *Adon* retained.

### Notes
- **vv1-3** — parallel to Isa 2:2-4 (swords into plowshares).
