# Micah 4

[1] And it shall be in the latter days:
the mountain of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be established
at the head of the mountains,
and it shall be lifted up above the hills;
and peoples shall stream to it,

[2] and many nations shall go and say:
"Come, and let us go up to the mountain of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and to the house of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and let him teach us of his ways,
and let us walk in his paths."
For from ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ shall go out ⟦torah|Torah|the law⟧,
and the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[3] And he shall judge between many peoples,
and shall decide for mighty nations far away;
**and they shall beat their swords into mattocks,
and their spears into pruning knives;
nation shall not lift up sword against nation,
and they shall not learn war any more.**

[4] And they shall sit, each man under his vine and under his fig tree,
and there is none to make them tremble;
for the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts has spoken.

[5] For all the peoples walk,
each man in the name of his god;
and we, we will walk in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧
forever and ever.

[6] In that day, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
let me gather her who limps,
and her who was driven away let me assemble,
and her to whom I have done evil.

[7] And I will make her who limps a remnant,
and her who was far removed a mighty nation;
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall reign over them on Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧
from now and forever.

[8] And you, tower of the drove,
mound of the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
to you it shall come;
and the former dominion shall come,
the kingdom to the daughter of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[9] Now why do you cry out a shout?
Is there no king in you?
Or has your counselor perished,
that anguish has seized you like a woman giving birth?

[10] Writhe and burst forth, daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
like a woman giving birth;
for now you shall go out from the town
and abide in the field,
and you shall come to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧;
there you shall be delivered;
there ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will redeem you
from the palm of your enemies.

[11] And now many nations
are gathered against you,
who say, Let her be polluted,
and let our eye gaze on ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

[12] But they do not know
the thoughts of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and they do not understand his counsel;
for he has gathered them like sheaves to the threshing floor.

[13] Arise and thresh, daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
for your horn I will make iron,
and your hoofs I will make bronze;
and you shall crush many peoples;
and I will devote their unjust gain to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and their wealth to the Lord of all the earth.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton; יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת "Yahweh of Hosts" (v4); אֲדוֹן כָּל־הָאָרֶץ "the Lord of all the earth" (v13), the plain word אָדוֹן.
- **Layout** — poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line by the accents. No "O" before "tower" and "daughter of Zion" (v8, 10, 13): the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **v1–3 beside Isa 2:2–4** — the same Hebrew has the same words as the finished Isa 2; the differences are kept as printed (named under each verse). Micah adds v4.
- **"But in the last days it shall come to pass, that the mountain of the house of the LORD shall be established in the top of the mountains, and it shall be exalted above the hills; and people shall flow unto it" → "And it shall be in the latter days: the mountain of the house of Yahweh shall be established at the head of the mountains, and it shall be lifted up above the hills; and peoples shall stream to it"** (v1) — וְהָיָה "and it shall be"; בְּאַחֲרִית הַיָּמִים "in the latter days"; Micah has יִהְיֶה הַר … נָכוֹן (the verb first, "established" after the subject; Isa 2:2 נָכוֹן יִהְיֶה "established shall be"); בְּרֹאשׁ "at the head of"; Micah adds הוּא ("lifted up is it"); נָהַר "stream" (the verb of נָהָר "river"); Micah עַמִּים "peoples" and עָלָיו, Isa 2:2 כָּל־הַגּוֹיִם "all the nations" and אֵלָיו.
- **"And many nations shall come, and say, Come, and let us go up to the mountain of the LORD, and to the house of the God of Jacob; and he will teach us of his ways, and we will walk in his paths: for the law shall go forth of Zion" → "and many nations shall go and say: Come, and let us go up to the mountain of Yahweh, and to the house of the Elohim of Jacob, and let him teach us of his ways, and let us walk in his paths. For from Zion shall go out torah …"** (v2) — הָלַךְ "go"; Micah גּוֹיִם רַבִּים "many nations" (Isa 2:3 עַמִּים רַבִּים "many peoples") and וְאֶל "and to" (Isa אֶל); וְיֹרֵנוּ jussive "let him teach us", וְנֵלְכָה cohortative "let us walk", as Isa 2:3; מִדְּרָכָיו "of his ways"; תּוֹרָה the token (no article in the Hebrew).
- **"And he shall judge among many people, and rebuke strong nations afar off; and they shall beat their swords into plowshares, and their spears into pruninghooks: nation shall not lift up a sword against nation, neither shall they learn war any more" → "And he shall judge between many peoples, and shall decide for mighty nations far away; and they shall beat their swords into mattocks, and their spears into pruning knives; nation shall not lift up sword against nation, and they shall not learn war any more"** (v3) — בֵּין "between"; הוֹכִיחַ לְ "decide for" (as Isa 2:4); Micah עַמִּים רַבִּים "many peoples" and adds גּוֹיִם עֲצֻמִים עַד־רָחוֹק "mighty nations far away" (Isa 2:4 "the nations … many peoples"); the swords line as Isa 2:4: אִתִּים "mattocks" (the tool of 1 Sam 13:20, there beside the plowshare מַחֲרֶשֶׁת; KJV "plowshares"), מַזְמֵרוֹת "pruning knives" (from זָמַר "prune"); וְלֹא "and … not". Micah has יִלְמְדוּן, the long form of Isaiah's יִלְמְדוּ: the same sense.
- **"But they shall sit every man under his vine and under his fig tree; and none shall make them afraid" → "And they shall sit, each man under his vine and under his fig tree, and there is none to make them tremble"** (v4) — וְ "and" (not "but"); אִישׁ "each man"; וְאֵין מַחֲרִיד "and there is none who makes tremble" ("them" supplied; the phrase of Lev 26:6); פִי יְהוָה … דִּבֵּר "the mouth of Yahweh … has spoken".
- **"For all people will walk every one in the name of his god, and we will walk in the name of the LORD our God for ever and ever" → "For all the peoples walk, each man in the name of his god; and we, we will walk in the name of Yahweh our Elohim forever and ever"** (v5) — אִישׁ בְּשֵׁם אֱלֹהָיו "each man in the name of his god"; the stressed וַאֲנַחְנוּ "and we"; לְעוֹלָם וָעֶד "forever and ever".
- **"In that day, saith the LORD, will I assemble her that halteth, and I will gather her that is driven out, and her that I have afflicted" → "In that day, declares Yahweh, let me gather her who limps, and her who was driven away let me assemble, and her to whom I have done evil"** (v6) — בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא "in that day"; נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh"; אֹסְפָה, אֲקַבֵּצָה cohortatives ("let me"); הַצֹּלֵעָה, הַנִּדָּחָה feminine singular (the flock, or Zion, as one: "her who …"); הֲרֵעֹתִי "I have done evil (to)" (the root of רָעָה; not "afflicted").
- **"And I will make her that halted a remnant, and her that was cast far off a strong nation: and the LORD shall reign over them in mount Zion from henceforth, even for ever" → "And I will make her who limps a remnant, and her who was far removed a mighty nation; and Yahweh shall reign over them on Mount Zion from now and forever"** (v7) — הַנַּהֲלָאָה, only here, "her who was far removed"; עָצוּם "mighty"; מֵעַתָּה וְעַד־עוֹלָם "from now and forever".
- **"And thou, O tower of the flock, the strong hold of the daughter of Zion, unto thee shall it come, even the first dominion; the kingdom shall come to the daughter of Jerusalem" → "And you, tower of the drove, mound of the daughter of Zion, to you it shall come; and the former dominion shall come, the kingdom to the daughter of Jerusalem"** (v8) — מִגְדַּל־עֵדֶר "tower of the drove" (עֵדֶר "drove", its row; the place Migdal-eder of Gen 35:21 bears the name); עֹפֶל "mound" (the hill of Jerusalem called Ophel bears the word; Isa 32:14); תֵּאתֶה "it shall come" (the verb אָתָה) and וּבָאָה "and shall come": two verbs; הַמֶּמְשָׁלָה הָרִאשֹׁנָה "the former dominion".
- **"Now why dost thou cry out aloud? is there no king in thee? is thy counsellor perished? for pangs have taken thee as a woman in travail" → "Now why do you cry out a shout? Is there no king in you? Or has your counselor perished, that anguish has seized you like a woman giving birth?"** (v9) — תָּרִיעִי רֵעַ "cry out a shout" (the verb with its noun; the הֵרִיעַ row: of voices "shout"); אִם "or"; כִּי "that"; חִיל "anguish" (the writhing of one in labor, its row); יוֹלֵדָה "a woman giving birth".
- **"Be in pain, and labour to bring forth, O daughter of Zion, like a woman in travail: for now shalt thou go forth out of the city, and thou shalt dwell in the field, and thou shalt go even to Babylon; there shalt thou be delivered; there the LORD shall redeem thee from the hand of thine enemies" → "Writhe and burst forth, daughter of Zion, like a woman giving birth; for now you shall go out from the town and abide in the field, and you shall come to Babylon; there you shall be delivered; there Yahweh will redeem you from the palm of your enemies"** (v10) — חוּלִי "writhe" (verb of חִיל); גֹּחִי "burst forth" (uncertain: "bring forth" or "groan"); קִרְיָה "town" (beside עִיר "city"); שָׁכַן "abide"; שָׂדֶה "field"; בּוֹא עַד "come to"; נִצַּל "be delivered"; גָּאַל "redeem"; כַּף "palm".
- **"Now also many nations are gathered against thee, that say, Let her be defiled, and let our eye look upon Zion" → "And now many nations are gathered against you, who say, Let her be polluted, and let our eye gaze on Zion"** (v11) — חָנֵף "be polluted" (as the land, Num 35:33); עֵינֵינוּ plural "our eyes" with a singular verb ("our eye"); חָזָה בְּ "gaze on" (with pleasure).
- **"But they know not the thoughts of the LORD, neither understand they his counsel: for he shall gather them as the sheaves into the floor" → "But they do not know the thoughts of Yahweh, and they do not understand his counsel; for he has gathered them like sheaves to the threshing floor"** (v12) — מַחְשְׁבוֹת "thoughts"; עֵצָה "counsel" (not "plan"); קִבְּצָם perfect "he has gathered them"; עָמִיר "sheaves" (cut grain).
- **"Arise and thresh, O daughter of Zion: for I will make thine horn iron, and I will make thy hoofs brass: and thou shalt beat in pieces many people: and I will consecrate their gain unto the LORD, and their substance unto the Lord of the whole earth" → "Arise and thresh, daughter of Zion, for your horn I will make iron, and your hoofs I will make bronze; and you shall crush many peoples; and I will devote their unjust gain to Yahweh, and their wealth to the Lord of all the earth"** (v13) — the objects first, as the Hebrew; נְחוּשָׁה "bronze"; הֵדַק "crush"; וְהַחֲרַמְתִּי "and I will devote" (first person, as printed; the Greek and others read "you shall devote", from memory; the MT kept); הֶחֱרִים "devote" (its row); בֶּצַע "unjust gain"; חַיִל "wealth".

### Notes
- **v1–3** — the same oracle as Isa 2:2–4; Micah has v4 in addition (the vine and the fig tree, Zech 3:10).
