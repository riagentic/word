# Micah 7

[1] Alas for me! For I have become
like the gatherings of summer fruit,
like the gleanings of the vintage:
there is no cluster to eat;
a first-ripe fig my soul desires.

[2] The faithful one has perished from the land,
and the upright among mankind there is none;
all of them lie in wait for blood;
each man hunts his brother with a net.

[3] On evil are both palms, to do it well;
the prince asks, and the judge, for a payment;
and the great one speaks the destruction of his soul, he;
and they weave it together.

[4] The best of them is like a brier,
the upright one worse than a thorn hedge;
the day of your watchmen, your visitation, has come;
now shall be their confusion.

[5] Do not believe in a neighbor;
do not trust in a close friend;
from her who lies in your bosom
guard the doors of your mouth.

[6] For a son treats a father as a fool,
a daughter rises up against her mother,
a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law;
the enemies of a man are the men of his house.

[7] But I, I will watch for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
let me hope in the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my salvation;
my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will hear me.

[8] Do not rejoice over me, my enemy;
though I have fallen, I have risen;
though I sit in the darkness,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is a light to me.

[9] The rage of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ I will bear,
for I have sinned against him,
until he contends my case
and does my judgment;
he will bring me out to the light;
I shall look on his righteousness.

[10] And my enemy shall see,
and shame shall cover her who said to me,
Where is he, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?
My eyes shall look on her;
now she shall be for trampling,
like the mud of the streets.

[11] A day for building your walls!
That day the statute shall be far off.

[12] That day — and to you one shall come,
from ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ and the cities of Matzor,
and from Matzor even to the River,
and from sea to sea, and from mountain to mountain.

[13] And the land shall become a desolation because of its inhabitants,
from the fruit of their doings.

[14] Shepherd your people with your rod,
the flock of your inheritance,
abiding alone, a forest
in the midst of ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧;
let them feed in Bashan and ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧
as in the days of old.

[15] As in the days of your going out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
I will show him wonders.

[16] Nations shall see and be ashamed
of all their might;
they shall put hand on mouth;
their ears shall be deaf.

[17] They shall lick dust like the serpent,
like the crawlers of the earth;
they shall come trembling out of their enclosures;
to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ they shall come in dread,
and they shall fear because of you.

[18] **Who is ⟦el|an El|a God⟧ like you,
forgiving iniquity
and passing over transgression
for the remnant of his inheritance?
He has not held fast his anger forever,
for he delights in ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧.**

[19] He will turn back, he will have compassion on us;
he will subdue our iniquities;
and you will cast into the depths of the sea
all their sins.

[20] You will give truth to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧,
which you swore to our fathers
from the days of long ago.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton.
- **Layout and speech marks** — poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line by the accents (v13 two lines). The quotation marks of v10 are dropped. No "O" before "my enemy" (v8): the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"Woe is me! for I am as when they have gathered the summer fruits, as the grapegleanings of the vintage: there is no cluster to eat: my soul desired the firstripe fruit" → "Alas for me! For I have become like the gatherings of summer fruit, like the gleanings of the vintage: there is no cluster to eat; a first-ripe fig my soul desires"** (v1) — אַלְלַי לִי "alas for me" (another word than אוֹי / הוֹי "woe"; only here and Job 10:15); הָיִיתִי "I have become"; אָסְפֵּי־קַיִץ "gatherings of summer fruit"; עֹלְלֹת "gleanings" (as Obad 5); בָּצִיר "vintage"; בִּכּוּרָה "first-ripe fig"; אִוְּתָה "desires", the object first.
- **"The good man is perished out of the earth: and there is none upright among men: they all lie in wait for blood; they hunt every man his brother with a net" → "The faithful one has perished from the land, and the upright among mankind there is none; all of them lie in wait for blood; each man hunts his brother with a net"** (v2) — חָסִיד "faithful one" (its row: one who keeps chesed); אֶרֶץ "land"; אָדָם "mankind"; אָרַב "lie in wait"; אִישׁ אֶת־אָחִיהוּ "each man his brother"; חֵרֶם "net" (the sound of חֵרֶם "devoted thing").
- **"That they may do evil with both hands earnestly, the prince asketh, and the judge asketh for a reward; and the great man, he uttereth his mischievous desire: so they wrap it up" → "On evil are both palms, to do it well; the prince asks, and the judge, for a payment; and the great one speaks the destruction of his soul, he; and they weave it together"** (v3) — עַל־הָרַע כַּפַּיִם "on evil (are) palms" (dual: both); לְהֵיטִיב "to do it well"; שִׁלּוּם "payment"; הַוַּת נַפְשׁוֹ "the destruction of his soul" (the reviewer's הַוָּה row, singular: the ruin he craves; "desire" reads it from אָוָה: uncertain); the stressed הוּא "he"; עִבֵּת "weave together" (only here).
- **"The best of them is as a brier: the most upright is sharper than a thorn hedge: the day of thy watchmen and thy visitation cometh; now shall be their perplexity" → "The best of them is like a brier, the upright one worse than a thorn hedge; the day of your watchmen, your visitation, has come; now shall be their confusion"** (v4) — יָשָׁר מִמְּסוּכָה "the upright one (is) beyond a hedge" (the comparative: "worse than"); מְצַפֶּיךָ "your watchmen" (the prophets; the root of v7 "watch"); פְּקֻדָּתְךָ "your visitation" (the פָּקַד row); בָּאָה perfect "has come"; מְבוּכָה "confusion".
- **"Trust ye not in a friend, put ye not confidence in a guide: keep the doors of thy mouth from her that lieth in thy bosom" → "Do not believe in a neighbor; do not trust in a close friend; from her who lies in your bosom guard the doors of your mouth"** (v5) — הֶאֱמִין בְּ "believe in" and בָּטַח בְּ "trust in" (two verbs); רֵעַ "neighbor"; אַלּוּף "close friend" (the Prov row); חֵיק "bosom"; שָׁמַר "guard"; the Hebrew order.
- **"For the son dishonoureth the father … a man's enemies are the men of his own house" → "For a son treats a father as a fool … the enemies of a man are the men of his house"** (v6) — מְנַבֵּל "treats as a fool" (piel of נָבֵל; noun נָבָל "fool"); no articles; "own" is not in the Hebrew. Matt 10:35–36 quotes the verse.
- **"Therefore I will look unto the LORD; I will wait for the God of my salvation: my God will hear me" → "But I, I will watch for Yahweh; let me hope in the Elohim of my salvation; my Elohim will hear me"** (v7) — the stressed וַאֲנִי "but I"; צִפָּה בְּ "watch for" (as the watchmen of v4); אוֹחִילָה cohortative "let me hope" (יִחֵל "hope", its row); יֶשַׁע "salvation".
- **"Rejoice not against me, O mine enemy: when I fall, I shall arise; when I sit in darkness, the LORD shall be a light unto me" → "Do not rejoice over me, my enemy; though I have fallen, I have risen; though I sit in the darkness, Yahweh is a light to me"** (v8) — אֹיַבְתִּי "my enemy" (feminine, as v10); כִּי "though" (twice); נָפַלְתִּי קָמְתִּי perfects "I have fallen, I have risen" (KJV makes them future); no verb in the last line ("is" supplied).
- **"I will bear the indignation of the LORD … until he plead my cause, and execute judgment for me … I shall behold his righteousness" → "The rage of Yahweh I will bear … until he contends my case and does my judgment … I shall look on his righteousness"** (v9) — זַעַף "rage" (as Jonah 1:15); the object first; רִיב רִיבִי "contend my case"; עָשָׂה מִשְׁפָּטִי "does my judgment" (gives the ruling for me); רָאָה בְּ "look on".
- **"Then she that is mine enemy shall see it … which said unto me, Where is the LORD thy God? mine eyes shall behold her: now shall she be trodden down as the mire of the streets" → "And my enemy shall see … who said to me, Where is he, Yahweh your Elohim? My eyes shall look on her; now she shall be for trampling, like the mud of the streets"** (v10) — וְ "and"; אַיּוֹ "where is he"; לְמִרְמָס "for trampling" (a noun); טִיט "mud".
- **"In the day that thy walls are to be built, in that day shall the decree be far removed" → "A day for building your walls! That day the statute shall be far off"** (v11) — יוֹם לִבְנוֹת "a day for building" (no "in"); גְּדֵרִים "walls" (of stone, around a vineyard or a field; feminine "your", Zion); יוֹם הַהוּא "that day" (no בְּ); חֹק "statute" (its row: "boundary", the bounds widened, is the reading of many: uncertain); יִרְחַק "shall be far off".
- **"In that day also he shall come even to thee from Assyria, and from the fortified cities, and from the fortress even to the river, and from sea to sea, and from mountain to mountain" → "That day — and to you one shall come, from Ashur and the cities of Matzor, and from Matzor even to the River, and from sea to sea, and from mountain to mountain"** (v12) — יוֹם הוּא "a day it is"; יָבוֹא singular, no subject ("one"); מָצוֹר "Matzor", a name of Egypt (as Isa 37:25 = 2 Kgs 19:24; Isa 19:6) that sounds like "siege, fortress" (KJV); the token `egypt` is not used, the Hebrew word is not מִצְרַיִם; נָהָר "the River" (the Euphrates); וְיָם מִיָּם וְהַר הָהָר lit. "and sea from sea, and mountain the mountain" ("to" supplied).
- **"Notwithstanding the land shall be desolate because of them that dwell therein, for the fruit of their doings" → "And the land shall become a desolation because of its inhabitants, from the fruit of their doings"** (v13) — וְ "and" (not "notwithstanding"); הָיְתָה לִשְׁמָמָה "become a desolation"; אֶרֶץ "land"; מִפְּרִי "from the fruit".
- **"Feed thy people with thy rod, the flock of thine heritage, which dwell solitarily in the wood, in the midst of Carmel: let them feed in Bashan and Gilead, as in the days of old" → "Shepherd your people with your rod, the flock of your inheritance, abiding alone, a forest in the midst of Carmel; let them feed in Bashan and Gilead as in the days of old"** (v14) — רָעָה "shepherd" (of the people; "feed" of the flock grazing, its row); שֵׁבֶט "rod" (as 5:1); נַחֲלָה "inheritance"; שֹׁכְנִי "abiding" (שָׁכַן); יַעַר "forest" (in apposition); כַּרְמֶל the name Carmel (or "garden land": both); כִּימֵי עוֹלָם "as in the days of old" (the phrase of 5:2 מִימֵי עוֹלָם "from days of eternity": the same words; here ancient times are plainly meant: say so).
- **"According to the days of thy coming out of the land of Egypt will I shew unto him marvellous things" → "As in the days of your going out from the land of Egypt, I will show him wonders"** (v15) — צֵאתְךָ "your going out"; אַרְאֶנּוּ "I will show him" (the people, singular); נִפְלָאוֹת "wonders".
- **"The nations shall see and be confounded at all their might: they shall lay their hand upon their mouth" → "Nations shall see and be ashamed of all their might; they shall put hand on mouth"** (v16) — גוֹיִם without the article; בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed"; יָד עַל־פֶּה "hand on mouth" (singular nouns, no "their"); חָרַשׁ "be deaf".
- **"They shall lick the dust like a serpent, they shall move out of their holes like worms of the earth: they shall be afraid of the LORD our God, and shall fear because of thee" → "They shall lick dust like the serpent, like the crawlers of the earth; they shall come trembling out of their enclosures; to Yahweh our Elohim they shall come in dread, and they shall fear because of you"** (v17) — זֹחֲלֵי אֶרֶץ "crawlers of the earth" (Deut 32:24); רָגַז "tremble"; מִסְגְּרוֹת "enclosures" (strongholds, as Ps 18:45); פָּחַד אֶל "come in dread to" (as Hos 3:5; "come" for the אֶל); יָרֵא מִן "fear because of".
- **"Who is a God like unto thee, that pardoneth iniquity, and passeth by the transgression of the remnant of his heritage? he retaineth not his anger for ever, because he delighteth in mercy" → "Who is El like you, forgiving iniquity and passing over transgression for the remnant of his inheritance? He has not held fast his anger forever, for he delights in chesed"** (v18) — as printed: מִי־אֵל כָּמוֹךָ (the sound of the prophet's name מִיכָה, "who is like Yah?"); נֹשֵׂא עָוֺן "forgiving iniquity" (the Exod 34:7 row: נָשָׂא "lift, bear, take away"); עֹבֵר עַל־פֶּשַׁע "passing over transgression"; לִשְׁאֵרִית "for the remnant"; הֶחֱזִיק perfect "has not held fast"; לָעַד "forever"; חָפֵץ חֶסֶד הוּא "a delighter in chesed is he" (the token).
- **"He will turn again, he will have compassion upon us; he will subdue our iniquities; and thou wilt cast all their sins into the depths of the sea" → "He will turn back, he will have compassion on us; he will subdue our iniquities; and you will cast into the depths of the sea all their sins"** (v19) — יָשׁוּב יְרַחֲמֵנוּ "he will turn back, he will have compassion" (שׁוּב with a second verb: = again; kept, as its row); כָּבַשׁ "subdue"; the turn to "you" as printed; מְצֻלוֹת "depths" (the מְצוֹלָה row); חַטֹּאותָם "their sins" (as printed; the Greek and others read "our sins", from memory; the MT kept).
- **"Thou wilt perform the truth to Jacob, and the mercy to Abraham, which thou hast sworn unto our fathers from the days of old" → "You will give truth to Jacob, chesed to Abraham, which you swore to our fathers from the days of long ago"** (v20) — תִּתֵּן "give"; אֱמֶת "truth" (its row; KJV "the truth"); no "and"; חֶסֶד the token; נִשְׁבַּעְתָּ "swore"; מִימֵי קֶדֶם "from the days of long ago" (קֶדֶם, as מִקֶּדֶם "from of old" 5:2; beside עוֹלָם of v14).

### Notes
- **v18** — מִי־אֵל כָּמוֹךָ "who is El like you?" echoes the prophet's name מִיכָה ("who is like Yah?") at the book's close; v18–20 take up the words of Exod 34:6–7.
- **v6** — cited by Yeshua in Matt 10:35–36.
