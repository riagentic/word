[14] "For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: As I purposed to bring disaster to you when your fathers provoked me to wrath, and I did not relent, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts,

[16] These are the things that you shall do: Speak the truth to one another; render in your gates judgments that are true and make for peace;

[18] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts came to me, saying,

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (throughout) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת *Yahweh Tzeva'ot*; the most densely-used chapter in Zechariah for this title.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv8, 23) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*; "I will be their *Elohim*" and "*Elohim* is with you."
