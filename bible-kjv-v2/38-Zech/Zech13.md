# Zechariah 13

[1] In that day there shall be a fountain opened for the house of David and for the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, for sin and for impurity.

[2] And it shall be in that day, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, I will cut off the names of the idols from the land, and they shall be remembered no more; and also the prophets and the spirit of uncleanness I will make pass away from the land.

[3] And it shall be, when a man prophesies again, his father and his mother who bore him shall say to him, You shall not live, for falsehood you have spoken in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and his father and his mother who bore him shall pierce him when he prophesies.

[4] And it shall be in that day, the prophets shall be ashamed, each man of his vision when he prophesies; and they shall not put on a mantle of hair in order to deceive.

[5] And he shall say, I am not a prophet; a man who works the ground am I, for a man made me his possession from my youth.

[6] And one shall say to him, What are these blows between your hands? And he shall say, Those with which I was struck in the house of my lovers.

[7] Sword, awake against my shepherd,
and against the man who is my fellow,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.
**Strike the shepherd, and the flock shall be scattered;**
and I will turn back my hand upon the little ones.

[8] And it shall be in all the land, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
two parts in it shall be cut off, shall expire,
and the third shall be left in it.

[9] And I will bring the third into the fire,
and refine them as silver is refined,
and try them as gold is tried.
He shall call on my name,
and I will answer him;
I have said, He is my people;
and he shall say, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v9) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **Layout** — v1–6 prose, v7–9 poetry, one Hebrew line per printed line; the quotation marks are dropped: the Hebrew does not mark them.
- **"a fountain opened … for sin and for uncleanness" → "a fountain opened … for sin and for impurity"** (v1) — מָקוֹר "fountain" (its row); לְחַטָּאת וּלְנִדָּה "for sin and for impurity" (נִדָּה "impurity", its row; "to cleanse them" is not in the Hebrew); "for" is repeated, as the Hebrew לְ before both the house of David and the inhabitants.
- **"And it shall come to pass in that day … I will cut off the names of the idols … and also I will cause the prophets and the unclean spirit to pass out of the land" → "And it shall be in that day … and also the prophets and the spirit of uncleanness I will make pass away from the land"** (v2) — וְהָיָה בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא the formula; עֲצַבִּים "idols" (its row); וְלֹא יִזָּכְרוּ עוֹד "and they shall be remembered no more" (a statement, not "so that"); רוּחַ הַטֻּמְאָה "the spirit of uncleanness" (טֻמְאָה, its row; a genitive, not an adjective); אַעֲבִיר "I will make pass away" (הֶעֱבִיר "make pass over", its row), the objects first as in the Hebrew.
- **"when any shall yet prophesy … that begat him … thou speakest lies … shall thrust him through" → "when a man prophesies again … who bore him … falsehood you have spoken … shall pierce him"** (v3) — אִישׁ "a man"; יֹלְדָיו "who bore him" (of both parents; the verb יָלַד); שֶׁקֶר "falsehood" (its row), placed first, דִּבַּרְתָּ perfect "you have spoken"; דָּקַר "pierce" (its row, the verb of 12:10).
- **"every one of his vision … a rough garment to deceive" → "each man of his vision … a mantle of hair in order to deceive"** (v4) — וְהָיָה kept; יֵבֹשׁוּ "be ashamed" (בּוֹשׁ row); אִישׁ "each man"; חִזָּיוֹן "vision"; אַדֶּרֶת שֵׂעָר "a mantle of hair" (אַדֶּרֶת "mantle", its row; as Elijah's, 2 Kgs 1:8); לְמַעַן כַּחֵשׁ "in order to deceive" (כִּחֵשׁ "lie", its row; לְמַעַן + infinitive "in order to").
- **"I am an husbandman; for man taught me to keep cattle from my youth" → "a man who works the ground am I, for a man made me his possession from my youth"** (v5) — אִישׁ־עֹבֵד אֲדָמָה "a man who works the ground" (עָבַד of the ground "work", אֲדָמָה "ground", their rows), the Hebrew order kept; אָדָם הִקְנַנִי, hifil of קָנָה "get, buy": "a man made me his possession" (he was a bought slave; "taught me to keep cattle" takes it from מִקְנֶה "livestock"; the redivision אֲדָמָה קִנְיָנִי "the ground has been my possession" is a common proposal, from memory) — the meaning is not certain.
- **"What are these wounds in thine hands? … Those with which I was wounded in the house of my friends" → "What are these blows between your hands? … Those with which I was struck in the house of my lovers"** (v6) — מַכּוֹת "blows" (מַכָּה row); בֵּין יָדֶיךָ "between your hands" (on the chest or arms; not "back"); הֻכֵּיתִי "I was struck" (hofal of הִכָּה, its row); מְאַהֲבָי "my lovers" (piel participle of אָהַב, as Hos 2:7–13 of the idols' lovers; "friends" would be another word); the subject of "one shall say" is not named.
- **"Awake, O sword, against my shepherd, and against the man that is my fellow … smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered: and I will turn mine hand upon the little ones" → "Sword, awake against my shepherd, and against the man who is my fellow … Strike the shepherd, and the flock shall be scattered; and I will turn back my hand upon the little ones"** (v7) — the word order of the Hebrew kept, no "O" (the Hebrew has no word for it); גֶּבֶר "man" (the standing decision's "mighty man" would say a warrior here; the word is the strong, grown man), עֲמִיתִי "my fellow" (עָמִית, its row); הַךְ "Strike" (הִכָּה); וּתְפוּצֶיןָ "and it shall be scattered" (purpose or result after the command); הַצֹּאן "the flock" (its row); וַהֲשִׁבֹתִי "I will turn back" (הֵשִׁיב); הַצֹּעֲרִים "the little ones" (participle of צָעַר "be small"; whether the flock's young or the under-shepherds are meant is not said). Matt 26:31 and Mark 14:27 quote the verse in the Greek ("I will strike"): Changes only.
- **"two parts therein shall be cut off and die; but the third shall be left therein" → "two parts in it shall be cut off, shall expire, and the third shall be left in it"** (v8) — וְהָיָה kept; פִּי־שְׁנַיִם "two parts" (its row: lit. "a mouth of two", here two thirds, against הַשְּׁלִשִׁית "the third"); יִכָּרְתוּ יִגְוָעוּ two verbs without "and"; גָּוַע "expire" (its row); וְ "and", not "but"; יִוָּתֶר "be left" (נוֹתַר row).
- **"will refine them as silver is refined, and will try them as gold is tried: they shall call on my name … I will say, It is my people: and they shall say" → "refine … try … He shall call on my name, and I will answer him; I have said, He is my people; and he shall say"** (v9) — וְהֵבֵאתִי "I will bring"; צָרַף "refine" (its row), בָּחַן "try" (reviewer row), each with its infinitive; "them" (plural) in the first half, then the singular הוּא "he" (the third as one people), each as printed; קָרָא בְשֵׁם "call on the name" (its row); אָמַרְתִּי perfect "I have said" (a prophetic perfect: "I will say" is the sense).

### Notes
- **v7** — Jesus quotes "I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep … will be scattered" of himself on the night of his arrest (Matt 26:31; Mark 14:27).
