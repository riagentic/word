# Zechariah 14

[1] Behold, a day is coming for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, when the spoil taken from you will be divided in your midst.

[2] For I will gather all the nations against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to battle, and the city shall be taken and the houses plundered and the women raped. Half of the city shall go out into exile, but the rest of the people shall not be cut off from the city.

[3] Then ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will go out and fight against those nations as when he fights on a day of battle.

[4] **On that day his feet shall stand on ⟦mount-of-olives|Har HaZeitim|the Mount of Olives⟧ that lies before ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ on the east**, and ⟦mount-of-olives|Har HaZeitim|the Mount of Olives⟧ shall be split in two from east to west by a very wide valley, so that one half of the Mount shall move northward, and the other half southward.

[5] And you shall flee to the valley of my mountains, for the valley of the mountains shall reach to ⟦atzel|Atzel|Azal⟧. And you shall flee as you fled from the earthquake in the days of ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧. Then ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will come, and all the holy ones — with him.

[6] On that day there shall be no light, cold, or frost.

[7] And there shall be a unique day, which is known to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, neither day nor night, but at evening time there shall be light.

[8] On that day living waters shall flow out from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, half of them to the eastern sea and half of them to the western sea. It shall continue in summer as in winter.

[9] **And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will be king over all the earth. On that day ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will be one and his name one.**

[10] The whole land shall be turned into a plain from ⟦geba|Geva|Geba⟧ to Rimmon south of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. But ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ shall remain aloft on its site from the Gate of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ to the place of the former gate, to the Corner Gate, and from the Tower of ⟦hananel|Chananel|Hananel⟧ to the king's winepresses.

[11] And it shall be inhabited, for there shall never again be a decree of utter destruction. ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ shall dwell in security.

[12] And this shall be the plague with which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will strike all the peoples that wage war against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧: their flesh will rot while they are still standing on their feet, their eyes will rot in their sockets, and their tongues will rot in their mouths.

[13] And on that day a great panic from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall fall on them, so that each will seize the hand of another, and the hand of the one will be raised against the hand of the other.

[14] Even ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ will fight at ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And the wealth of all the surrounding nations shall be collected, gold, silver, and garments in great abundance.

[15] And a plague like this plague shall fall on the horses, the mules, the camels, the donkeys, and whatever beasts may be in those camps.

[16] Then everyone who survives of all the nations that have come against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ shall go up year after year to worship the King, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, and to keep the Feast of ⟦sukkot|Sukkot|Booths⟧.

[17] And if any of the families of the earth do not go up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to worship the King, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, there will be no rain on them.

[18] And if the family of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ does not go up and present themselves, then on them there shall be no rain; there shall be the plague with which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ afflicts the nations that do not go up to keep the Feast of ⟦sukkot|Sukkot|Booths⟧.

[19] This shall be the punishment to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and the punishment to all the nations that do not go up to keep the Feast of ⟦sukkot|Sukkot|Booths⟧.

[20] And on that day there shall be inscribed on the bells of the horses, "**Holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.**" And the pots in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be as the bowls before the altar.

[21] And every pot in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ shall be Holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, so that all who sacrifice may come and take of them and boil the meat of the sacrifice in them. And there shall no longer be a trader in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts on that day.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (vv16, 17, 21) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת *Yahweh Tzeva'ot*; the military-covenantal divine title preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*; "Yahweh my *Elohim* will come."
- **"HOLINESS TO THE LORD" → "Holy to Yahweh"** (v20-21) — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ לַיהוָה *Qodesh la-Yahweh*, "holiness/consecration to Yahweh"; the priestly inscription (cf. Exod 28:36) rendered as English.
- **"feast of tabernacles" → "Feast of Booths"** (vv16, 18, 19) — Hebrew חַג הַסֻּכּוֹת *chag ha-Sukkot* = pilgrimage-festival of booths/tabernacles; English rendering preserved from KJV's "tabernacles" simplified.

### Notes
- **v9** — "Yahweh shall be one" — echoes Shema (Deut 6:4).
- **v4** — Mount of Olives earthquake; cited in Christian eschatology.
- **v8** — "living waters": echoed John 4:10; 7:38; Rev 22:1.
