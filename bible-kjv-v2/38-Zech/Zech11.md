# Zechariah 11

[1] Open, ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧, your doors,
and let fire devour among your cedars.

[2] Wail, cypress, for the cedar has fallen,
because the mighty ones are laid waste;
wail, oaks of Bashan,
for the impenetrable forest has come down.

[3] The sound of the wailing of the shepherds,
for their splendor is laid waste;
the sound of the roaring of young lions,
for the pride of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ is laid waste.

[4] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: Shepherd the flock of the slaughter,

[5] whose buyers kill them and are not guilty, and whose sellers each says, Blessed is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and I have become rich; and their shepherds do not pity them.

[6] For I will no more pity the inhabitants of the land, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and behold, I am handing over mankind, each man into the hand of his neighbor and into the hand of his king; and they shall beat the land in pieces, and I will not deliver from their hand.

[7] And I shepherded the flock of the slaughter, therefore the afflicted of the flock. And I took for myself two staffs: one I called Pleasantness, and one I called Binders; and I shepherded the flock.

[8] And I cut off the three shepherds in one month; and my soul was short with them, and their soul also loathed me.

[9] And I said: I will not shepherd you. What is dying, let it die; and what is being cut off, let it be cut off; and those that are left, let them devour, each one the flesh of her neighbor.

[10] And I took my staff Pleasantness, and I cut it apart, to break my covenant which I had cut with all the peoples.

[11] And it was broken in that day; and so the afflicted of the flock, who were watching me, knew that it was the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] And I said to them: If it is good in your eyes, give my wage; and if not, cease. And they weighed my wage: **thirty of silver**.

[13] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me: Throw it to the potter — the glorious price at which I was priced by them. And I took the thirty of silver and threw it into the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to the potter.

[14] And I cut apart my second staff, Binders, to break the brotherhood between ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and between ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[15] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me: Take to yourself again the gear of a senseless shepherd.

[16] For behold, I am raising up a shepherd in the land: those being cut off he will not visit, the young he will not seek, and the broken he will not heal, the one that stands he will not sustain; and the flesh of the fat he will eat, and their hoofs he will tear off.

[17] Woe to my worthless shepherd,
who forsakes the flock!
A sword upon his arm
and upon his right eye!
His arm shall surely wither,
and his right eye shall surely grow dim.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **Layout** — v1–3 and 17 in lines by the accents, v4–16 prose (as the existing layout); quotation marks dropped: the Hebrew does not mark them.
- **"Open thy doors, O Lebanon, that the fire may devour thy cedars" → "Open, Lebanon, your doors, and let fire devour among your cedars"** (v1) — Hebrew order kept; no "O" (vocative row); וְתֹאכַל "and let (fire) devour"; בַּאֲרָזֶיךָ "among your cedars".
- **"Howl, fir tree … because the mighty are spoiled: howl, O ye oaks of Bashan; for the forest of the vintage is come down" → "Wail, cypress … because the mighty ones are laid waste; wail, oaks of Bashan, for the impenetrable forest has come down"** (v2) — Hebrew הֵילֵל / הֵילִילוּ "wail" (its row); בְּרוֹשׁ "cypress" (kind uncertain); אַדִּרִים "mighty ones" (the trees; as the אַדִּיר row); שֻׁדָּדוּ "are laid waste" (שָׁדַד, its row; also v3); qere הַבָּצִיר, taken as "impenetrable" (KJV "of the vintage" reads the same word as בָּצִיר "vintage"); the ketiv הבצור "the fortified" has the same sense; יָרַד "has come down".
- **"There is a voice of the howling of the shepherds; for their glory is spoiled: a voice of the roaring of young lions; for the pride of Jordan is spoiled" → "The sound of the wailing of the shepherds, for their splendor is laid waste; the sound of the roaring of young lions, for the pride of the Jordan is laid waste"** (v3) — Hebrew קוֹל "sound" (its row; a verbless exclamation, no "there is"); יְלָלָה "wailing" (its row); אַדַּרְתָּם "their splendor" (the noun is "mantle" in Josh 7:21 and 2 Kgs 2; here of the trees or pastures, from אַדִּיר: the one word named here); כְּפִירִים "young lions"; גְּאוֹן הַיַּרְדֵּן "the pride of the Jordan" (its thickets: the row word kept).
- **"Thus saith the LORD my God; Feed the flock of the slaughter" → "Thus says Yahweh my Elohim: Shepherd the flock of the slaughter"** (v4) — Hebrew רְעֵה "shepherd" (רָעָה, its row; also v7, 9, 16); צֹאן הַהֲרֵגָה "the flock of the slaughter" (the killing).
- **"Whose possessors slay them, and hold themselves not guilty: and they that sell them say, Blessed be the LORD; for I am rich: and their own shepherds pity them not" → "whose buyers kill them and are not guilty, and whose sellers each says, Blessed is Yahweh, and I have become rich; and their shepherds do not pity them"** (v5) — Hebrew קֹנֵיהֶן "their buyers" (קָנָה "buy", its row); יַהַרְגֻן "kill" (הָרַג, its row); לֹא יֶאְשָׁמוּ "are not guilty" (אָשֵׁם, its row); יֹאמַר singular verb with a plural subject ("each says"); בָּרוּךְ יְהוָה "Blessed is Yahweh" (no verb: the אָרוּר / בָּרוּךְ row); וַאעְשִׁר "and I have become rich" (no "for"); חָמַל "pity" (its row; also v6).
- **"For I will no more pity the inhabitants of the land … but, lo, I will deliver the men every one into his neighbour's hand … and they shall smite the land, and out of their hand I will not deliver them" → "… and behold, I am handing over mankind, each man into the hand of his neighbor … and they shall beat the land in pieces, and I will not deliver from their hand"** (v6) — Hebrew הָאָרֶץ "the land" ("this" is not written); מַמְצִיא "handing over" (lit. "causing to be found", hifil of מָצָא; participle); הָאָדָם "mankind"; כִּתְּתוּ "beat in pieces" (כָּתַת, its row); לֹא אַצִּיל "I will not deliver" (הִצִּיל, its row), no object written.
- **"And I will feed the flock of slaughter, even you, O poor of the flock. And I took unto me two staves; the one I called Beauty, and the other I called Bands" → "And I shepherded the flock of the slaughter, therefore the afflicted of the flock. And I took for myself two staffs: one I called Pleasantness, and one I called Binders"** (v7) — Hebrew וָאֶרְעֶה wayyiqtol "And I shepherded" (past, as the narrative of v8–14); לָכֵן עֲנִיֵּי הַצֹּאן, word for word "therefore the afflicted of the flock" (hard: "truly" or "that is" in other renderings); the Septuagint reading "for the Canaanites (traders) of the flock" (לִכְנַעֲנִיֵּי, as v11), which the former verse followed ("by the sheep traders"), is not the MT (from memory); עָנִי "afflicted" (its row); מַקְלוֹת "staffs"; נֹעַם "Pleasantness" (KJV "Beauty", others "Favor"); חֹבְלִים "Binders" (participle of חָבַל "bind": "Union", "Bands" in other versions); קָרָאתִי "I called".
- **"Three shepherds also I cut off in one month; and my soul lothed them, and their soul also abhorred me" → "And I cut off the three shepherds in one month; and my soul was short with them, and their soul also loathed me"** (v8) — Hebrew וָאַכְחִד "and I cut off" (כָּחַד hifil; nifal in v9, 16); the article "the three shepherds" (who they are is not said); וַתִּקְצַר נַפְשִׁי בָּהֶם "my soul was short with them" (the קָצְרָה נֶפֶשׁ row: impatience); בָּחֲלָה "loathed" (only here in this sense).
- **"Then said I, I will not feed you: that that dieth, let it die … and let the rest eat every one the flesh of another" → "And I said: I will not shepherd you. What is dying, let it die … and those that are left, let them devour, each one the flesh of her neighbor"** (v9) — Hebrew הַמֵּתָה … הַנִּכְחֶדֶת participles, feminine (of the sheep); וְהַנִּשְׁאָרוֹת "those that are left"; אִשָּׁה אֶת־בְּשַׂר רְעוּתָהּ, lit. "a woman the flesh of her companion" (the feminine of the "each man … his neighbor" row).
- **"And I took my staff, even Beauty, and cut it asunder, that I might break my covenant which I had made with all the people" → "And I took my staff Pleasantness, and I cut it apart, to break my covenant which I had cut with all the peoples"** (v10) — Hebrew גָּדַע "cut apart" (hew; also v14); לְהָפֵיר "to break" (the הֵפֵר בְּרִית row); כָּרַתִּי "I had cut" (the covenant row); עַמִּים plural.
- **"And it was broken in that day: and so the poor of the flock that waited upon me knew that it was the word of the LORD" → "And it was broken in that day; and so the afflicted of the flock, who were watching me, knew that it was the word of Yahweh"** (v11) — Hebrew וַתֻּפַר "and it was broken"; בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא "in that day" (formula row); עֲנִיֵּי הַצֹּאן as v7 (MT; the "sheep traders" of the former verse is the Septuagint reading again); הַשֹּׁמְרִים "who were watching" (שָׁמַר, its row).
- **"If ye think good, give me my price; and if not, forbear. So they weighed for my price thirty pieces of silver" → "If it is good in your eyes, give my wage; and if not, cease. And they weighed my wage: thirty of silver"** (v12) — Hebrew אִם־טוֹב בְּעֵינֵיכֶם "if it is good in your eyes"; הָבוּ "give" (no "me"); שְׂכָרִי "my wage" (שָׂכָר, its row); חֲדָלוּ "cease" (חָדַל, its row); שָׁקַל "weigh" (its row); שְׁלֹשִׁים כָּסֶף "thirty of silver" ("pieces" and "shekels" are not written: thirty weighed units of silver).
- **"Cast it unto the potter: a goodly price that I was prised at of them. And I took the thirty pieces of silver, and cast them to the potter in the house of the LORD" → "Throw it to the potter — the glorious price at which I was priced by them. And I took the thirty of silver and threw it into the house of Yahweh, to the potter"** (v13) — Hebrew הַשְׁלִיכֵהוּ "throw it" (הִשְׁלִיךְ, its row); אֶל־הַיּוֹצֵר "to the potter" (MT, the יוֹצֵר row; the Syriac and many interpreters read הָאוֹצָר "the treasury", from memory: not in the verse); אֶדֶר הַיְקָר "the glory of the price" (irony; אֶדֶר "magnificence"); יָקַרְתִּי מֵעֲלֵיהֶם "I was priced by them" (lit. "from upon them"); בֵּית יְהוָה with no preposition, "(into) the house of Yahweh", word order kept ("the house of Yahweh, to the potter"); אֹתוֹ "it" (singular: the sum). Matt 27:9–10 quotes these words freely and names Jeremiah; the Hebrew is translated as it stands, the citation is not carried into the verse (the former Notes line on Matthew's attribution is replaced by this one).
- **"Then I cut asunder mine other staff, even Bands, that I might break the brotherhood between Judah and Israel" → "And I cut apart my second staff, Binders, to break the brotherhood between Judah and between Israel"** (v14) — Hebrew הַשֵּׁנִי "the second"; אַחֲוָה "brotherhood" (only here); בֵּין … וּבֵין "between … and between" kept.
- **"Take unto thee yet the instruments of a foolish shepherd" → "Take to yourself again the gear of a senseless shepherd"** (v15) — Hebrew עוֹד קַח־לְךָ "again take to yourself" (imperative with לְךָ; "Get yourself" of the reviewer's לֶךְ־לְךָ row is a verb of going and does not fit "take"); כְּלִי "gear" (the כְּלִי rows: "gear" as נֹשֵׂא כֵלִים); אֱוִלִי "senseless" (as אֱוִיל, the Proverbs row; not כְּסִיל "fool").
- **"For, lo, I will raise up a shepherd in the land, which shall not visit those that be cut off, neither shall seek the young one, nor heal that that is broken, nor feed that that standeth still: but he shall eat the flesh of the fat, and tear their claws in pieces" → "For behold, I am raising up a shepherd in the land: those being cut off he will not visit, the young he will not seek … the one that stands he will not sustain; and the flesh of the fat he will eat, and their hoofs he will tear off"** (v16) — Hebrew מֵקִים participle; the objects stand first in each clause, kept; הַנִּכְחָדוֹת "those being cut off" (as v8–9); לֹא־יִפְקֹד "he will not visit" (פָּקַד, its row; not "care for"); הַנַּעַר "the young" (uncertain: "the scattered" in other versions); הַנִּשְׁבֶּרֶת "the broken"; הַנִּצָּבָה "the one that stands" (נִצָּב, its row: the sound one, or the one standing still); כִּלְכֵּל "sustain" (its row); הַבְּרִיאָה "the fat" (בָּרִיא, its row); פַּרְסֵיהֶן "their hoofs"; פָּרַק "tear off" (its row).
- **"Woe to the idol shepherd that leaveth the flock! the sword shall be upon his arm, and upon his right eye: his arm shall be clean dried up, and his right eye shall be utterly darkened" → "Woe to my worthless shepherd, who forsakes the flock! A sword upon his arm and upon his right eye! His arm shall surely wither, and his right eye shall surely grow dim"** (v17) — Hebrew הוֹי "Woe" (formula row; "to" supplied); רֹעִי הָאֱלִיל, "my shepherd, the worthless one" (the yod may be the old linking vowel, "shepherd of worthlessness": open; אֱלִיל "worthless / idol"); עֹזְבִי "who forsakes" (עָזַב, its row); חֶרֶב עַל "a sword upon" (no verb; KJV "shall be"); יָבוֹשׁ תִּיבָשׁ, כָּהֹה תִכְהֶה infinitives absolute: "shall surely wither", "shall surely grow dim" (the כָּהָה of the eye row); "May … let" of the former verse read wishes into plain imperfects.
