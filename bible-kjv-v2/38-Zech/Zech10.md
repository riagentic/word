# Zechariah 10

[1] Ask from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ rain
in the time of the latter rain;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is making the storm flashes,
and the shower of rain he will give them,
to each man plants in the field.

[2] For the ⟦teraphim|teraphim|household gods⟧ have spoken wickedness,
and the diviners have beheld falsehood,
and dreams of emptiness they speak;
with vapor they comfort;
therefore they have journeyed like a flock,
they are afflicted, for there is no shepherd.

[3] Against the shepherds my anger has burned,
and on the he-goats I will visit;
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts has visited his drove, the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,
and will make them like the horse of his majesty in the war.

[4] From him the corner,
from him the peg,
from him the bow of war,
from him shall go out every taskmaster together.

[5] And they shall be like mighty ones,
trampling in the mud of the streets in the war;
and they shall fight, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is with them,
and they shall put to shame the riders of horses.

[6] And I will make mighty the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,
and the house of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ I will save;
and I will bring them back, for I have had compassion on them,
and they shall be as though I had not cast them off;
for I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and I will answer them.

[7] And ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ shall be like a mighty one,
and their heart shall be glad as with wine;
and their sons shall see and be glad;
their heart shall exult in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[8] I will whistle for them and gather them,
for I have ransomed them;
and they shall be many as they were many.

[9] And I will sow them among the peoples,
and in the far places they shall remember me;
and they shall live with their sons, and return.

[10] And I will bring them back from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
and from ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ I will gather them,
and to the land of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ and ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ I will bring them,
and it shall not be enough for them.

[11] And he shall pass over in the sea of distress,
and shall strike the waves in the sea,
and all the depths of the ⟦nile|Yeor|Nile⟧ shall dry up;
and the pride of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ shall be brought down,
and the rod of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ shall turn aside.

[12] And I will make them mighty in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and in his name they shall walk about,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v3) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת, plain "of Hosts".
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v6) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **Layout** — lines by the accents (as the existing layout); quotation marks dropped: the Hebrew does not mark where Yahweh's speech begins (v3, 6, 8) and ends.
- **"Ask ye of the LORD rain in the time of the latter rain; so the LORD shall make bright clouds, and give them showers of rain, to every one grass in the field" → "Ask from Yahweh rain in the time of the latter rain; Yahweh is making the storm flashes, and the shower of rain he will give them, to each man plants in the field"** (v1) — Hebrew מָטָר "rain", מַלְקוֹשׁ "latter rain" (its row); עֹשֶׂה participle; חֲזִיזִים "storm flashes" (lightning or storm clouds: uncertain); מְטַר־גֶּשֶׁם "the shower of rain" (the Job row); לְאִישׁ "to each man"; עֵשֶׂב "plants" (its row; not "grass").
- **"For the idols have spoken vanity, and the diviners have seen a lie, and have told false dreams; they comfort in vain: therefore they went their way as a flock, they were troubled, because there was no shepherd" → "For the teraphim have spoken wickedness, and the diviners have beheld falsehood, and dreams of emptiness they speak; with vapor they comfort; therefore they have journeyed like a flock, they are afflicted, for there is no shepherd"** (v2) — Hebrew תְּרָפִים (token `teraphim`, its row); אָוֶן "wickedness" (its row); קוֹסְמִים "diviners" (קָסַם); חָזוּ "have beheld" (חָזָה, its row); שֶׁקֶר "falsehood"; חֲלֹמוֹת הַשָּׁוא "dreams of emptiness" (שָׁוְא, its row); הֶבֶל "vapor" (its row: "with vapor" = in vain); נָסְעוּ "journeyed" (its row); צֹאן "flock"; יַעֲנוּ "they are afflicted" (עָנָה, root of עֳנִי "affliction").
- **"Mine anger was kindled against the shepherds, and I punished the goats: for the LORD of hosts hath visited his flock the house of Judah, and hath made them as his goodly horse in the battle" → "Against the shepherds my anger has burned, and on the he-goats I will visit; for Yahweh of Hosts has visited his drove … and will make them like the horse of his majesty in the war"** (v3) — Hebrew חָרָה אַפִּי "my anger has burned" (the אַף standing decision); עַתּוּדִים "he-goats" (its row; the leaders are meant: not said in the verse); פָּקַד עַל "visit on" and פָּקַד אֶת "visit" (one verb, for punishment and for care: its row), kept the same both times; עֵדֶר "drove" (its row); וְשָׂם "and will make" (lit. "put"); כְּסוּס הוֹדוֹ "like the horse of his majesty" (הוֹד, its row); מִלְחָמָה "war" (its row).
- **"Out of him came forth the corner, out of him the nail, out of him the battle bow, out of him every oppressor together" → "From him the corner, from him the peg, from him the bow of war, from him shall go out every taskmaster together"** (v4) — Hebrew פִּנָּה "corner" (its row; "cornerstone" interprets); יָתֵד "peg" (its row); נוֹגֵשׂ "taskmaster" (its row; here of rulers, as "ruler" in other versions: the one word kept); יֵצֵא "shall go out" stands only in the last line.
- **"which tread down their enemies in the mire of the streets in the battle … and the riders on horses shall be confounded" → "trampling in the mud of the streets in the war … and they shall put to shame the riders of horses"** (v5) — Hebrew בּוֹסִים "trampling" (no object: "their enemies" is supplied in KJV); טִיט חוּצוֹת as 9:3; וְהֹבִישׁוּ hifil "they shall put to shame".
- **"And I will strengthen the house of Judah … and I will bring them again to place them; for I have mercy upon them: and they shall be as though I had not cast them off" → "And I will make mighty the house of Judah … and I will bring them back, for I have had compassion on them, and they shall be as though I had not cast them off"** (v6) — Hebrew גִּבַּרְתִּי "make mighty" (root of גִּבּוֹר; also v12); וְהוֹשְׁבוֹתִים is a mixed form of הֵשִׁיב "bring back" and הוֹשִׁיב "make dwell": KJV joins both; "bring back" kept, the other reading here; רִחַמְתִּים perfect "I have had compassion"; זְנַחְתִּים "cast off"; וְאֶעֱנֵם "and I will answer them".
- **"And they of Ephraim shall be like a mighty man … yea, their children shall see it, and be glad; their heart shall rejoice in the LORD" → "And Ephraim shall be like a mighty one … and their sons shall see and be glad; their heart shall exult in Yahweh"** (v7) — Hebrew וְהָיוּ כְגִבּוֹר אֶפְרַיִם, plural verb with the name; לִבָּם "their heart" (singular); בְּנֵיהֶם "their sons" (standing decision); יִרְאוּ "shall see" (no object); יָגֵל "exult" (גִּיל).
- **"I will hiss for them … for I have redeemed them: and they shall increase as they have increased" → "I will whistle for them … for I have ransomed them; and they shall be many as they were many"** (v8) — Hebrew שָׁרַק "whistle"; פְּדִיתִים "I have ransomed" (פָּדָה, its row; גָּאַל is "redeem"); רָבוּ כְּמוֹ רָבוּ "be many as they were many".
- **"And I will sow them among the people: and they shall remember me in far countries; and they shall live with their children, and turn again" → "And I will sow them among the peoples, and in the far places they shall remember me; and they shall live with their sons, and return"** (v9) — Hebrew וְאֶזְרָעֵם "and I will sow them" (no "though … yet"); עַמִּים plural; מֶרְחַקִּים "far places"; וָשָׁבוּ "and return".
- **"I will bring them again also out of the land of Egypt … and place shall not be found for them" → "And I will bring them back … and it shall not be enough for them"** (v10) — Hebrew וַהֲשִׁיבוֹתִים "and I will bring them back"; וְלֹא יִמָּצֵא לָהֶם "and it shall not be enough for them" (lit. "it shall not be found for them": the Josh 17:16 row).
- **"And he shall pass through the sea with affliction, and shall smite the waves in the sea, and all the deeps of the river shall dry up: and the pride of Assyria shall be brought down, and the sceptre of Egypt shall depart away" → "And he shall pass over in the sea of distress, and shall strike the waves in the sea, and all the depths of the Nile shall dry up; and the pride of Assyria shall be brought down, and the rod of Egypt shall turn aside"** (v11) — Hebrew וְעָבַר בַּיָּם צָרָה, word for word "and he shall pass over in the sea, distress" ("of" supplied; the verb is masculine, so צָרָה "distress", feminine, is not its subject; who "he" is the verse does not say); עָבַר "pass over" (its row); מְצוּלוֹת "depths" (its row); יְאֹר "the Nile" (token `nile`; KJV "the river"); הֹבִישׁוּ "shall dry up"; גְּאוֹן "pride"; שֵׁבֶט "rod" (its row; "scepter" in Changes); יָסוּר "shall turn aside" (סוּר, its row).
- **"and they shall walk up and down in his name" → "and in his name they shall walk about"** (v12) — Hebrew וּבִשְׁמוֹ יִתְהַלָּכוּ, hitpael "walk about" (the הִתְהַלֵּךְ row), "in his name" first; נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh".
