# Zechariah 3

[1] And he showed me ⟦joshua-priest|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ the high priest standing before the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the adversary standing at his right hand to be his adversary.

[2] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to the adversary, "May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ rebuke you, adversary! And may ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who chooses ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ rebuke you! Is not this a brand snatched from fire?"

[3] And ⟦joshua-priest|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ was clothed in filthy garments, and was standing before the angel.

[4] And he answered and said to those who were standing before him, saying, "Remove the filthy garments from upon him." And he said to him, "See, I have made your iniquity pass from upon you, and I will clothe you with festal robes."

[5] And I said, "Let them put a clean turban on his head." And they put the clean turban on his head, and clothed him with garments; and the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was standing.

[6] And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ warned ⟦joshua-priest|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, saying,

[7] "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: If in my ways you will walk, and if my charge you will keep, then you also shall judge my house, and you also shall keep my courts, and I will give you passages among these who are standing.

[8] Hear, please, ⟦joshua-priest|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ the high priest, you and your companions who sit before you, for they are men of wonder: for behold, I am bringing my servant **Sprout**.

[9] For behold, the stone that I have set before ⟦joshua-priest|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧: upon one stone seven eyes. Behold, I am engraving its engraving, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, and I will remove the iniquity of that land in one day.

[10] In that day, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, you shall call, each man to his neighbor, to come under a vine and under a fig tree."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v7, 9, 10) — the prophets' formula row. נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh" (v9, 10); כֹּה אָמַר "Thus says" (v7); בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא "in that day" (v10).
- **"And he shewed me" → "And he showed me"** (v1) — וַיַּרְאֵנִי; who shows is not named ("Then" of the earlier text is "And"). **"the angel of the LORD" → "the angel of Yahweh"** (v1, 5, 6; v3, 4 "the angel") — מַלְאַךְ יְהוָה, the one word "angel" of chapters 1–2 (the WORDS-HE row: see 1:11). **"and Satan standing at his right hand to resist him" → "and the adversary standing at his right hand to be his adversary"** — Hebrew וְהַשָּׂטָן, *ha-satan*, with the article: "the adversary", a common noun, as in Job 1–2 (the reviewer row; traditional "Satan"; the Hebrew does not make it a name); לְשִׂטְנוֹ is the verb of the same root, "to be an adversary to him" (to accuse or oppose him), so the play of noun and verb is kept.
- **"The LORD rebuke thee, O Satan; even the LORD that hath chosen Jerusalem rebuke thee" → "May Yahweh rebuke you, adversary! And may Yahweh who chooses Jerusalem rebuke you!"** (v2) — יִגְעַר twice, a jussive (wish), with וְ "And" the second time; גָּעַר "rebuke" (its row); הַשָּׂטָן is addressed (the Hebrew article of address is not given in English; no "O"); הַבֹּחֵר, a participle "who chooses" (the בָּחַר row; as 1:17; 2:12 "choose again"). **"is not this a brand plucked out of the fire?" → "Is not this a brand snatched from fire?"** — אוּד מֻצָּל מֵאֵשׁ, hofal of נצל (the root of הִצִּיל "deliver"); אֵשׁ without the article.
- **"Now Joshua was clothed with filthy garments, and stood before the angel" → "And Joshua was clothed in filthy garments, and was standing before the angel"** (v3) — וִיהוֹשֻׁעַ הָיָה לָבֻשׁ … וְעֹמֵד, a participle "was standing"; צוֹאִים "filthy", literally soiled with excrement (צֵאָה, Deut 23:13 row "what has come out").
- **"And he answered and spake unto those that stood before him, saying" → "And he answered and said to those who were standing before him, saying"** (v4) — the speaker is not named (the angel). **"Take away the filthy garments from him" → "Remove the filthy garments from upon him"** — הָסִירוּ (the הֵסִיר "remove" row); מֵעָלָיו "from upon him" (also "from upon you"). **"Behold, I have caused thine iniquity to pass from thee, and I will clothe thee with change of raiment" → "See, I have made your iniquity pass from upon you, and I will clothe you with festal robes"** — רְאֵה "See" (not הִנֵּה); הֶעֱבַרְתִּי "make pass over" (the הֶעֱבִיר row); עָוֹן "iniquity" (its row; also v9); וְהַלְבֵּשׁ, an infinitive absolute standing for the finite verb ("and clothing you": "I will clothe you"); מַחֲלָצוֹת "festal robes" (garments taken off and put on for feasts; only here and Isa 3:22).
- **"And I said, Let them set a fair mitre upon his head" → "And I said, Let them put a clean turban on his head"** (v5) — וָאֹמַר, the first person as the MT (the prophet speaks; the Greek and Latin have "he said": from memory); יָשִׂימוּ, a jussive; צָנִיף "turban" (another word than the high priest's מִצְנֶפֶת, Exod 28:4); טָהוֹר "clean" (its row). **"So they set" → "And they put"** — wayyiqtol "And"; הַצָּנִיף הַטָּהוֹר with the article the second time ("the clean turban"). **"And the angel of the LORD stood by" → "and the angel of Yahweh was standing"** — עֹמֵד, a participle; "by" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"And the angel of the LORD protested unto Joshua, saying" → "And the angel of Yahweh warned Joshua, saying"** (v6) — וַיָּעַד בִּיהוֹשֻׁעַ, the הֵעִיד בְּ row "warn" (literally "testify to": a formal charge before witnesses).
- **"If thou wilt walk in my ways, and if thou wilt keep my charge" → "If in my ways you will walk, and if my charge you will keep"** (v7) — the Hebrew order (the object first both times); מִשְׁמֶרֶת "charge" (its row). **"then thou shalt also judge my house, and shalt also keep my courts" → "then you also shall judge my house, and you also shall keep my courts"** — וְגַם־אַתָּה … וְגַם, "also" twice; תָּדִין, the דִּין "judge" row ("govern", "rule" in other versions narrow it); שָׁמַר "keep" (its row); חֲצֵרָי "my courts" (the חָצֵר row). Where the "if" ends and the "then" begins is not marked in the Hebrew: the four clauses can all be the condition, with "then I will give you" as the only promise (the accents put the break after "my courts"); the earlier text's division is kept, the other in this note. **"and I will give thee places to walk among these that stand by" → "and I will give you passages among these who are standing"** — מַהְלְכִים, plural of מַהֲלָךְ "passage" (as Ezek 42:4): the right to go among those who stand before Yahweh; "access" in other versions; הָעֹמְדִים הָאֵלֶּה "these who are standing" (v4, 5).
- **"Hear now, O Joshua the high priest" → "Hear, please, Joshua the high priest"** (v8) — שְׁמַע־נָא, נָא "please"; no "O" (vocative row). **"thy fellows that sit before thee" → "your companions who sit before you"** — רֵעֶיךָ (the רֵעַ row "friend", here of those who share his office); יָשַׁב "sit" (its row). **"for they are men wondered at" → "for they are men of wonder"** — אַנְשֵׁי מוֹפֵת, מוֹפֵת "wonder" (its row: a portent, a sign of what is to come, as Isa 8:18). **"I will bring forth my servant the BRANCH" → "I am bringing my servant Sprout"** — הִנְנִי מֵבִיא, a participle; צֶמַח "sprout" (the Isa 4:2 row; Jer 23:5; 33:15 lower case). Here it has no article and stands beside "my servant" as a name stands ("my servant David", Ezek 34:23), and 6:12 says "Sprout is his name": so a capital in both places. "Branch" (the earlier text and KJV) names a part of a tree; צֶמַח is what springs up (the verb צָמַח, 6:12 "and from his place he shall sprout"). The bold of the earlier text is kept.
- **"For behold the stone that I have laid before Joshua; upon one stone shall be seven eyes" → "For behold, the stone that I have set before Joshua: upon one stone seven eyes"** (v9) — נָתַתִּי "I have set" (the נָתַן "place" row: literally "given"); the Hebrew has no verb in "upon one stone seven eyes"; עֵינַיִם "eyes" (also "springs", "faces / facets": the sense of the image is not said; 4:10). **"behold, I will engrave the graving thereof" → "Behold, I am engraving its engraving"** — הִנְנִי מְפַתֵּחַ פִּתֻּחָהּ, a participle and its own noun (the פִּתַּח · פִּתּוּחֵי row). **"and I will remove the iniquity of that land in one day" kept** — וּמַשְׁתִּי, from מוּשׁ "depart, remove" (not the מָשַׁשׁ / מוּשׁ "feel" row); הָאָרֶץ הַהִיא "that land"; בְּיוֹם אֶחָד "in one day".
- **"shall ye call every man his neighbour under the vine and under the fig tree" → "you shall call, each man to his neighbor, to come under a vine and under a fig tree"** (v10) — תִּקְרְאוּ אִישׁ לְרֵעֵהוּ (the אִישׁ … רֵעֵהוּ row); אֶל־תַּחַת "to under": "to come" is supplied, because English cannot say "call to under"; גֶּפֶן · תְּאֵנָה without the article or "his" (as Mic 4:4 has "his vine": from memory).

### Notes
- **v1–2** — *ha-satan*, "the adversary", stands with the article as in Job 1–2; 1 Chr 21:1 has שָׂטָן without it. The traditional reading "Satan" and the New Testament scenes (Jude 9; Rev 12:10) are reception, not the wording of this verse.
- **v8** — צֶמַח is read of a coming king of David's line (Jer 23:5; 33:15); here and in 6:12 it is given as a name. Zerubbabel and the Mashiach are the two readings of who is meant; the verse names neither.
