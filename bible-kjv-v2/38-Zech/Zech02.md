# Zechariah 2

[1] And I lifted up my eyes and saw, and behold, a man, and in his hand a measuring cord.

[2] And I said, "Where are you going?" And he said to me, "To measure ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, to see how much is her breadth and how much is her length."

[3] And behold, the angel who spoke with me was going out, and another angel was going out to meet him.

[4] And he said to him, "Run, speak to this young man, saying, ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ shall dwell as open country, because of the multitude of man and beast in her midst.

[5] And I will be to her, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, a wall of fire all around, and for glory I will be in her midst."

[6] Ho, ho! And flee from the land of the north, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
for like the four winds of the heavens I have spread you out,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] Ho, ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧! Escape, you who dwell with the daughter of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧!

[8] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: After glory he has sent me to the nations who plunder you, for he who touches you touches ⟦apple-of-the-eye|bavat eino|the apple of his eye⟧.

[9] For behold, I am waving my hand over them, and they shall be spoil for their slaves; and you shall know that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts has sent me.

[10] Shout for joy and be glad, daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
for behold, I am coming, and I will abide in your midst,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[11] And many nations shall be joined to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in that day,
and they shall be to me a people.
And I will abide in your midst,
and you shall know that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts has sent me to you.

[12] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall inherit ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, his share, upon the holy ground, and shall again choose ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[13] **Hush, all flesh, before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for he has roused himself from his holy abode.**

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v8, 9, 11) — the prophets' formula row. נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh" (v5, 6 twice, 10); כֹּה אָמַר "thus says" (v8).
- **Layout and quotation marks** — v1–5, 7–9, 12–13 prose, v6, 10, 11 lines as before (they follow the accents). Double quotation marks only for the words spoken inside the vision (v2, 4–5); the outer marks of the earlier text (v9–12) are removed: the Hebrew has none, and where Yahweh's speech begins and ends is not marked.
- **"a man with a measuring line in his hand" → "a man, and in his hand a measuring cord"** (v1) — וְהִנֵּה־אִישׁ וּבְיָדוֹ חֶבֶל מִדָּה, a clause of its own; חֶבֶל "cord" (its row; קָו "line" is 1:16), מִדָּה "measure": literally "a cord of measure".
- **"Then said I, Whither goest thou?" → "And I said, Where are you going?"** (v2). **"what is the breadth thereof, and what is the length thereof" → "how much is her breadth and how much is her length"** — כַּמָּה twice; the suffix is feminine (the city).
- **"the angel that talked with me went forth, and another angel went out to meet him" → "the angel who spoke with me was going out, and another angel was going out to meet him"** (v3) — יֹצֵא twice, a participle; מַלְאָךְ "angel" as in chapter 1.
- **"And said unto him, Run, speak to this young man, saying" → "And he said to him, Run, speak to this young man, saying"** (v4) — the speaker is not named (the second angel or the first); נַעַר "young man" (its row; of a grown man: Zechariah or the man with the cord). **"Jerusalem shall be inhabited as towns without walls" → "Jerusalem shall dwell as open country"** — פְּרָזוֹת תֵּשֵׁב, יָשַׁב "dwell" (its row) with the city as subject; פְּרָזוֹת "open regions" (land without walls). **"for the multitude of men and cattle therein" → "because of the multitude of man and beast in her midst"** — אָדָם וּבְהֵמָה (the בְּהֵמָה row "beast"); בְּתוֹכָהּ "in her midst".
- **"For I, saith the LORD, will be unto her a wall of fire round about" → "And I will be to her, declares Yahweh, a wall of fire all around"** (v5) — וַאֲנִי "And I", the formula in the Hebrew place. **"and will be the glory in the midst of her" → "and for glory I will be in her midst"** — וּלְכָבוֹד אֶהְיֶה, "for glory", no article. אֶהְיֶה "I will be" plain (the Exod 3:14 row: no token).
- **"Ho, ho, come forth, and flee from the land of the north" → "Ho, ho! And flee from the land of the north"** (v6) — הוֹי הוֹי: here a call to attention, not a lament; "Woe" of the prophets' row would say a threat the verse does not have ("Ho" as Isa 55:1, which names this verse); וְנֻסוּ, weqatal with the force of a command, "and flee" ("come forth" is not in the Hebrew). **"for I have spread you abroad as the four winds of the heaven" → "for like the four winds of the heavens I have spread you out"** — the Hebrew order; פֵּרַשְׂתִּי a perfect, פָּרַשׂ "spread out"; הַשָּׁמַיִם "the heavens" (its row).
- **"Deliver thyself, O Zion, that dwellest with the daughter of Babylon" → "Ho, Zion! Escape, you who dwell with the daughter of Babylon!"** (v7) — הוֹי again (the earlier text dropped it); Zion is addressed (not "to Zion"); no "O"; הִמָּלְטִי "escape" (נִמְלַט); יוֹשֶׁבֶת בַּת־בָּבֶל, "dweller of the daughter of Babylon": "with" is supplied for English (or "in").
- **"After the glory hath he sent me unto the nations which spoiled you" → "After glory he has sent me to the nations who plunder you"** (v8) — אַחַר כָּבוֹד שְׁלָחַנִי, word for word: "glory" has no article and no "his", and it is not the subject ("he has sent me"); what "after glory" means (after the vision of glory, or in pursuit of glory) is not said. Who "me" is (the prophet, or the angel of Yahweh) is also not said. הַשֹּׁלְלִים, a participle "who plunder" (the שָׁלָל row, v9). **"for he that toucheth you toucheth the apple of his eye" kept** — נָגַע בְּ "touch" (its row); בָּבַת עֵינוֹ, the token `apple-of-the-eye` (בָּבָה only here: "pupil", literally perhaps "little gate"); "his eye" as the MT; the scribes' tradition lists this verse among the "corrections of the scribes" for an earlier "my eye" (from memory).
- **"For, behold, I will shake mine hand upon them, and they shall be a spoil to their servants" → "For behold, I am waving my hand over them, and they shall be spoil for their slaves"** (v9) — הִנְנִי מֵנִיף, a participle; הֵנִיף "wave" (its row); שָׁלָל "spoil" (its row); עַבְדֵיהֶם "their slaves" (the עֶבֶד row: those who served them as slaves). **"and ye shall know" kept** — וִידַעְתֶּם (also v11, feminine singular וְיָדַעַתְּ).
- **"Sing and rejoice, O daughter of Zion: for, lo, I come, and I will dwell in the midst of thee" → "Shout for joy and be glad, daughter of Zion, for behold, I am coming, and I will abide in your midst"** (v10) — רָנִּי "shout for joy" (the רָנַן row), שִׂמְחִי "be glad" (the root of שִׂמְחָה "gladness"); no "O"; הִנְנִי־בָא a participle; שָׁכַן "abide" (its row; also v11).
- **"And many nations shall be joined to the LORD in that day, and shall be my people" → "And many nations shall be joined to Yahweh in that day, and they shall be to me a people"** (v11) — נִלְוָה אֶל "be joined to" (its row; KJV wording kept against the earlier "join themselves"); בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא "in that day" (the prophets' row); וְהָיוּ לִי לְעָם, "they shall be to me for a people". The Greek has "his people" and "they shall dwell" (from memory); the MT is kept.
- **"And the LORD shall inherit Judah his portion in the holy land, and shall choose Jerusalem again" → "And Yahweh shall inherit Judah, his share, upon the holy ground, and shall again choose Jerusalem"** (v12) — נָחַל "inherit" (its row); חֶלְקוֹ "his share" (the חֵלֶק row); עַל אַדְמַת הַקֹּדֶשׁ, "upon the ground of holiness" (אֲדָמָה "ground"; the קֹדֶשׁ genitive row "holy"); עוֹד "again" as 1:17.
- **"Be silent, O all flesh, before the LORD: for he is raised up out of his holy habitation" → "Hush, all flesh, before Yahweh, for he has roused himself from his holy abode"** (v13) — הַס "hush" (the הֵהַס row); מִפְּנֵי, literally "from the face of" (as Hab 2:20; Zeph 1:7); נֵעוֹר, nifal of עוּר "rouse oneself, wake"; מְעוֹן קָדְשׁוֹ "his holy abode" (the מָעוֹן row; literally "the abode of his holiness").

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — our 2:1–13 = Heb 2:5–17 (our 1:18–21 = Heb 2:1–4).
- **v11** — the nations joined to Yahweh become "a people to me", the covenant words used of Israel (Exod 6:7; Jer 31:33).
