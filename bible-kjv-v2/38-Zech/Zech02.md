# Zechariah 2

[1] And I lifted my eyes and saw, and behold, a man with a measuring line in his hand!

[2] Then I said, "Where are you going?" And he said to me, "To measure ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, to see what is its width and what is its length."

[3] And behold, the messenger who talked with me came forward, and another messenger came forward to meet him

[4] and said to him, "Run, say to that young man, '⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ shall be inhabited as villages without walls, because of the multitude of people and livestock in it.

[5] And I will be to her a wall of fire all around, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and I will be the glory in her midst.'"

[6] Up! Up! Flee from the land of the north, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.
For I have spread you abroad as the four winds of the heavens,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] Up! Escape to ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, you who dwell with the daughter of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[8] For thus said ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, after his glory sent me to the nations who plundered you, for he who touches you touches the ⟦apple-of-the-eye|bavat eino — apple of his eye|apple of his eye⟧:

[9] "Behold, I will shake my hand over them, and they shall become plunder for those who served them. Then you will know that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts has sent me.

[10] "Sing and rejoice, O daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
for behold, I come and I will dwell in your midst,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[11] And many nations shall join themselves to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in that day,
and shall be my people.
And I will dwell in your midst,
and you shall know that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts has sent me to you.

[12] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will inherit ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ as his portion in the holy land, and will again choose ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧."

[13] **Be silent, all flesh, before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for he has roused himself from his holy dwelling.**

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (vv8, 9, 11) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת *Yahweh Tzeva'ot*, "Yahweh of Armies/Hosts"; the military-covenantal divine title preserved.

### Notes
- **"Apple of his eye"** (v8) — translates *bavat ayin*, literally "pupil of the eye"; unique expression.
- **v11** — "many nations shall join themselves to Yahweh": echoes universal scope.
