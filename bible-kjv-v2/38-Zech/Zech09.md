# Zechariah 9

[1] An oracle. The word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is against the land of ⟦hadrach|Chadrach|Hadrach⟧ and ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧ is its resting place. For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has an eye on mankind and on all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[2] and on ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧ also, which borders on it, ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ and ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧, though they are very wise.

[3] ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ has built herself a rampart and heaped up silver like dust, and fine gold like the mud of the streets.

[4] But behold, ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ will strip her of her possessions and strike down her power on the sea, and she shall be devoured by fire.

[5] ⟦ashkelon|Ashqelon|Ashkelon⟧ shall see it, and be afraid; ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧ too, and shall writhe in anguish; ⟦ekron|Eqron|Ekron⟧ also, because its hopes are confounded. The king shall perish from ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧; ⟦ashkelon|Ashqelon|Ashkelon⟧ shall be uninhabited;

[6] a mixed people shall dwell in Ashdod, and I will cut off the pride of ⟦philistia|Peleshet|Philistia⟧.

[7] I will take away its blood from its mouth, and its abominations from between its teeth; it too shall be a remnant for our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; it shall be like a clan in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and ⟦ekron|Eqron|Ekron⟧ shall be like the ⟦jebusites|Yevusim|Jebusites⟧.

[8] Then I will encamp at my house as a guard, so that none shall march to and fro; no oppressor shall again march over them, for now I see with my own eyes.

[9] Rejoice greatly, O daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧!
Shout aloud, O daughter of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧!
**Behold, your king is coming to you;
righteous and having salvation is he,
humble and mounted on a donkey,
on a colt, the foal of a donkey.**

[10] I will cut off the chariot from ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧
and the war horse from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧;
and the battle bow shall be cut off,
and he shall speak peace to the nations;
his rule shall be from sea to sea,
and from the River to the ends of the earth.

[11] As for you also, because of the blood of my covenant with you,
I will set your prisoners free from the waterless pit.

[12] Return to your stronghold, O prisoners of hope;
today I declare that I will restore to you double.

[13] For I have bent ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ as my bow;
I have made ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ its arrow.
I will stir up your sons, O ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
against your sons, O ⟦greece|Yavan|Greece⟧,
and wield you like a warrior's sword.

[14] Then ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will appear over them,
and his arrow will go forth like lightning;
⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|The Lord GOD⟧ will sound the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧
and will march forth in the whirlwinds of the south.

[15] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ of Hosts will protect them,
and they shall devour, and tread down the sling stones,
and they shall drink and roar as if drunk with wine,
and be full like a bowl,
drenched like the corners of the altar.

[16] On that day ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will save them,
as the flock of his people;
for like the jewels of a crown
they shall shine on his land.

[17] For how great is his goodness, and how great his beauty!
Grain shall make the young men flourish,
and new wine the young women.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v15) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת *Yahweh Tzeva'ot*; the military-covenantal divine title preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv7, 16) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*; "a remnant for our *Elohim*" and "Yahweh their *Elohim* will save them."
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v14) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוָה *Adonai Yahweh*; the doubled divine address: *Adonai* ("my Lord") + the Tetragrammaton; both preserved.

### Notes
- **v9** — fulfilled at Yeshua's triumphal entry (Matt 21:5; John 12:15).
