# Zechariah 9

[1] The burden of the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is in the land of ⟦hadrach|Chadrach|Hadrach⟧, and ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧ is its resting place; for to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is the eye of man, and all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;

[2] and also ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧, which borders on it; ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ and ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧, for she is very wise.

[3] And ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ built a fortress for herself, and heaped up silver like the dust, and fine gold like the mud of the streets.

[4] Behold, ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ will dispossess her, and strike her force in the sea; and she shall be devoured by the fire.

[5] ⟦ashkelon|Ashqelon|Ashkelon⟧ shall see and fear, and ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧, and shall writhe greatly, and ⟦ekron|Eqron|Ekron⟧, for her expectation has been put to shame; and the king shall perish from ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧, and ⟦ashkelon|Ashqelon|Ashkelon⟧ shall not be inhabited.

[6] And one born of a forbidden union shall dwell in Ashdod, and I will cut off the pride of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧.

[7] And I will take away his blood from his mouth, and his detestable things from between his teeth; and he also shall be left for our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and he shall be like a chief in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and ⟦ekron|Eqron|Ekron⟧ like a ⟦jebusite|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧.

[8] And I will camp for my house as an outpost, against him who passes over and him who returns; and no taskmaster shall pass over them again; for now I have seen with my eyes.

[9] Exult greatly, daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧!
Shout, daughter of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧!
**Behold, your king comes to you;
righteous and saved is he,
afflicted and riding on a donkey,
and on a male donkey, the son of she-donkeys.**

[10] And I will cut off the chariot from ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧,
and the horse from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
and the bow of war shall be cut off;
and he shall speak ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ to the nations;
and his rule shall be from sea to sea,
and from the River to the ends of the earth.

[11] You also, by the blood of your covenant,
I have let go your prisoners from the pit in which there is no water.

[12] Return to the stronghold, prisoners of the hope;
also today I am declaring: a double I will return to you.

[13] For I have trodden ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ for myself;
a bow I have filled with ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧;
and I will rouse your sons, ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
against your sons, ⟦greece|Yavan|Greece⟧,
and I will make you like the sword of a mighty one.

[14] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be seen over them,
and his arrow shall go out like the lightning;
and ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ shall blow on the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧,
and shall go in the storms of the south.

[15] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ of Hosts shall shield them,
and they shall devour, and subdue the stones of the sling;
and they shall drink, they shall roar as with wine;
and they shall be filled like the bowl,
like the corners of the altar.

[16] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ shall save them
in that day, as the flock of his people;
for stones of a crown,
lifting themselves up over his ground.

[17] For how great is his goodness, and how great his beauty!
Grain the young men,
and new wine shall make the virgins flourish.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v15) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת, plain "of Hosts" as the earlier books.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v7, 16) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v14) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words (token `adonai-yhwh`); v4 אֲדֹנָי alone (token `adonai`).
- **Layout** — v1–8 prose, v9–17 in lines by the accents (as the existing layout); v16 relaid at the atnach ("in that day" opens the second line).
- **"The burden of the word of the LORD in the land of Hadrach, and Damascus shall be the rest thereof: when the eyes of man, as of all the tribes of Israel, shall be toward the LORD" → "The burden of the word of Yahweh is in the land of Hadrach, and Damascus is its resting place; for to Yahweh is the eye of man, and all the tribes of Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew מַשָּׂא "burden" (the prophets' formula row; "oracle" in other versions; the word is "what is lifted up"); בְּאֶרֶץ "in the land" (or "against"); מְנֻחָתוֹ "its resting place" (the word's); כִּי לַיהוָה עֵין אָדָם, word for word "for to Yahweh (is) the eye of man": either "Yahweh has an eye on mankind" or "the eye of man is toward Yahweh" (KJV): open; reading אֲרָם "Aram" for אָדָם is a conjecture (from memory), the MT kept; שֵׁבֶט "tribe" (its row).
- **"And Hamath also shall border thereby; Tyrus, and Zidon, though it be very wise" → "and also Hamath, which borders on it; Tyre and Sidon, for she is very wise"** (v2) — Hebrew תִּגְבָּל־בָּהּ "borders on it" (גָּבַל, the Joshua border row); כִּי חָכְמָה מְאֹד, singular feminine "she is very wise" (Tyre, as v3); כִּי "for" (KJV "though" takes it as a concession: possible).
- **"a strong hold … fine gold as the mire of the streets" → "a fortress … fine gold like the mud of the streets"** (v3) — Hebrew מָצוֹר, a play on צֹר "Tyre" (the siege row is another sense: here the thing built); וַתִּבֶן wayyiqtol "And … built"; חָרוּץ "fine gold" (a poetic word for gold; not the "diligent" of Proverbs); טִיט "mud"; חוּצוֹת "streets".
- **"the Lord will cast her out, and he will smite her power in the sea; and she shall be devoured with fire" → "Adonai will dispossess her, and strike her force in the sea; and she shall be devoured by the fire"** (v4) — Hebrew יוֹרִשֶׁנָּה "dispossess" (hifil of יָרַשׁ, its row); חֵילָהּ "her force" (חַיִל, its row: of an army or of wealth); בָּאֵשׁ with the article; no "But" in the Hebrew.
- **"Ashkelon shall see it, and fear; Gaza also shall see it, and be very sorrowful, and Ekron; for her expectation shall be ashamed … Ashkelon shall not be inhabited" → "Ashkelon shall see and fear, and Gaza, and shall writhe greatly, and Ekron, for her expectation has been put to shame … shall not be inhabited"** (v5) — Hebrew וְתָחִיל "and shall writhe" (חִיל, the "anguish" row); מֶבָּטָהּ "her expectation" (its row, as Isa 20:5); הֹבִישׁ, hifil perfect "has put to shame" (also read from יָבֵשׁ "has dried up": open); לֹא תֵשֵׁב, lit. "shall not dwell / sit" (= be without inhabitant).
- **"And a bastard shall dwell in Ashdod, and I will cut off the pride of the Philistines" → "And one born of a forbidden union shall dwell in Ashdod, and I will cut off the pride of the Philistines"** (v6) — Hebrew מַמְזֵר (only here and Deut 23:2; the meaning is uncertain: its row; "a mixed people", "a bastard" in other versions); גָּאוֹן "pride" (its row); פְּלִשְׁתִּים plural "the Philistines" (token `philistines`; the `philistia` token, "Philistia", is פְּלֶשֶׁת).
- **"And I will take away his blood out of his mouth, and his abominations from between his teeth: but he that remaineth, even he, shall be for our God, and he shall be as a governor in Judah, and Ekron as a Jebusite" → "And I will take away his blood … and his detestable things … and he also shall be left for our Elohim, and he shall be like a chief in Judah, and Ekron like a Jebusite"** (v7) — Hebrew דָּמָיו "his blood" (plural "bloods": blood eaten with the meat); שִׁקֻּצָיו "his detestable things" (שִׁקּוּץ, its row; not תּוֹעֵבָה "abomination"); וְנִשְׁאַר גַּם־הוּא "and he also shall be left" (נִשְׁאַר, its row; no "but"); כְּאַלֻּף "like a chief" (אַלּוּף as the chiefs of Edom; the pointing אֶלֶף "clan" is another reading); כִּיבוּסִי singular, no article (token `jebusite`).
- **"And I will encamp about mine house because of the army, because of him that passeth by, and because of him that returneth: and no oppressor shall pass through them any more: for now have I seen with mine eyes" → "And I will camp for my house as an outpost, against him who passes over and him who returns; and no taskmaster shall pass over them again; for now I have seen with my eyes"** (v8) — Hebrew חָנָה "camp" (its row); מִצָּבָה as pointed, "as a post / guard" (the מַצָּבָה outpost row; KJV "because of the army" reads מִצָּבָא); מֵעֹבֵר וּמִשָּׁב "from one passing over and from one returning" (= so that none goes to and fro); עָבַר "pass over" (its row, both times); נֹגֵשׂ "taskmaster" (its row); רָאִיתִי perfect.
- **"Rejoice greatly, O daughter of Zion; shout, O daughter of Jerusalem: behold, thy King cometh unto thee: he is just, and having salvation; lowly, and riding upon an ass, and upon a colt the foal of an ass" → "Exult greatly … Shout … Behold, your king comes to you; righteous and saved is he, afflicted and riding on a donkey, and on a male donkey, the son of she-donkeys"** (v9) — Hebrew גִּילִי "exult" (גִּיל); הָרִיעִי "shout" (its row); no "O" (vocative row); יָבוֹא "comes" (imperfect); צַדִּיק "righteous" (its row); וְנוֹשָׁע הוּא, nifal participle as pointed, "and saved" (one who has been saved; KJV "having salvation" and the active "saving" read the word otherwise); עָנִי "afflicted" (its row; not "lowly", "humble": that is עָנָו); חֲמוֹר "donkey"; עַיִר "male donkey" (a young male donkey; KJV "colt"); בֶּן־אֲתֹנוֹת "the son of she-donkeys" (plural, אָתוֹן its row): one animal, named twice in parallel lines. The verse is quoted in Matt 21:5 and John 12:15 from the Greek, shortened and in other words; the Hebrew is translated as it stands (the former Notes line on its fulfilment is taken out: the text does not say it).
- **"the chariot … the horse … the battle bow … he shall speak peace unto the heathen: and his dominion shall be from sea even to sea, and from the river even to the ends of the earth" → "the chariot … the horse … the bow of war … he shall speak shalom to the nations; and his rule … from the River to the ends of the earth"** (v10) — Hebrew רֶכֶב (collective, its row), סוּס "horse" ("war horse" adds); קֶשֶׁת מִלְחָמָה "bow of war"; שָׁלוֹם token (reviewer row); מָשְׁלוֹ "his rule" (מָשַׁל, its row); הַנָּהָר "the River" (the Euphrates; no name written, no token); אַפְסֵי־אָרֶץ "the ends of the earth".
- **"As for thee also, by the blood of thy covenant I have sent forth thy prisoners out of the pit wherein is no water" → "You also, by the blood of your covenant, I have let go your prisoners from the pit in which there is no water"** (v11) — Hebrew גַּם־אַתְּ (feminine: Zion); בְּדַם־בְּרִיתֵךְ "your covenant" ("my covenant with you" turns the suffix); שִׁלַּחְתִּי perfect, "let go" (the שִׁלַּח row: of one who is held); בּוֹר "pit" (its row).
- **"Turn you to the strong hold, ye prisoners of hope: even to day do I declare that I will render double unto thee" → "Return to the stronghold, prisoners of the hope; also today I am declaring: a double I will return to you"** (v12) — Hebrew שׁוּבוּ "return" (its row); בִּצָּרוֹן "stronghold" (only here; from בָּצוּר "fortified"); הַתִּקְוָה with the article; מַגִּיד participle; מִשְׁנֶה "double" (its row), placed first; אָשִׁיב "I will return" (הֵשִׁיב, its row: give back).
- **"When I have bent Judah for me, filled the bow with Ephraim, and raised up thy sons, O Zion, against thy sons, O Greece, and made thee as the sword of a mighty man" → "For I have trodden Judah for myself; a bow I have filled with Ephraim; and I will rouse your sons, Zion, against your sons, Greece, and I will make you like the sword of a mighty one"** (v13) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; דָּרַכְתִּי "I have trodden" (the bow is bent with the foot: the 1 Chr 5:18 row); לִי "for myself"; קֶשֶׁת מִלֵּאתִי אֶפְרַיִם, word for word "a bow I have filled, Ephraim" ("with" supplied: Ephraim is the arrow laid on, or the bow filled; open); עוֹרַרְתִּי "rouse"; יָוָן Javan (Greece; token `greece`); שַׂמְתִּיךְ "I will make you" (lit. "put"); גִּבּוֹר "mighty one" (its row).
- **"And the LORD shall be seen over them … and the Lord GOD shall blow the trumpet, and shall go with whirlwinds of the south" → "And Yahweh shall be seen over them … and Lord Yahweh shall blow on the shofar, and shall go in the storms of the south"** (v14) — Hebrew יֵרָאֶה nifal "shall be seen"; כַבָּרָק "like the lightning" (בָּרָק, its row); בַּשּׁוֹפָר יִתְקָע "blow on the shofar" (תָּקַע בְּ, its row; token `shofar`); סַעֲרוֹת "storms" (סְעָרָה, its row); תֵּימָן "south" (the Directions row; also a name, Teman).
- **"The LORD of hosts shall defend them; and they shall devour, and subdue with sling stones; and they shall drink, and make a noise as through wine; and they shall be filled like bowls, and as the corners of the altar" → "Yahweh of Hosts shall shield them, and they shall devour, and subdue the stones of the sling; and they shall drink, they shall roar as with wine; and they shall be filled like the bowl, like the corners of the altar"** (v15) — Hebrew יָגֵן עֲלֵיהֶם "shall shield over them"; וְכָבְשׁוּ אַבְנֵי־קֶלַע, "and subdue the sling stones" (כָּבַשׁ, its row; no "with" in the Hebrew: they tread down the enemy's stones, or the enemies as stones): open; הָמוּ "they shall roar" (הָמָה, its row; no "and"); כְּמוֹ־יָיִן "as (with) wine"; מִזְרָק "bowl" (its row: the bowl for dashing the blood), singular; זָוִיּוֹת "corners".
- **"And the LORD their God shall save them in that day as the flock of his people: for they shall be as the stones of a crown, lifted up as an ensign upon his land" → "And Yahweh their Elohim shall save them in that day, as the flock of his people; for stones of a crown, lifting themselves up over his ground"** (v16) — Hebrew וְהוֹשִׁיעָם "And … shall save them" (יָשַׁע, its row); בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא "in that day" (the prophets' formula row); אַבְנֵי־נֵזֶר "stones of a crown" (נֵזֶר, its row), no "like" and no verb "be" in the Hebrew; מִתְנוֹסְסוֹת, hitpolel, from נֵס "signal": "lifting themselves up" as a signal, or "sparkling" (uncertain); עַל־אַדְמָתוֹ "over his ground" (אֲדָמָה, its row).
- **"For how great is his goodness, and how great is his beauty! corn shall make the young men cheerful, and new wine the maids" → "For how great is his goodness, and how great his beauty! Grain the young men, and new wine shall make the virgins flourish"** (v17) — Hebrew כִּי מַה־טּוּבוֹ וּמַה־יָפְיוֹ, "for what his goodness and what his beauty!" ("great" supplied for English; whose goodness — Yahweh's, the people's or the land's — is not said); יֹפִי "beauty" (reviewer row); דָּגָן "grain", תִּירוֹשׁ "new wine" (their row); the one verb יְנוֹבֵב "make flourish" stands in the second line and serves both, kept so; בְּתֻלוֹת "virgins" (בְּתוּלָה; not "young women": that is עַלְמָה).
