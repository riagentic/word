# 3 John 1

[1] The elder to the beloved Gaius, whom I love in truth.

[2] Beloved, I pray that all may go well with you and that you may be in good health, as it goes well with your soul.

[3] For I rejoiced greatly when the brothers came and testified to your truth, as indeed you are walking in the truth.

[4] I have no greater joy than to hear that my children are walking in the truth.

[5] Beloved, it is a faithful thing you do in all your efforts for these brothers, strangers as they are,

[6] who testified to your love before the church. You will do well to send them on their journey in a manner worthy of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[7] For they have gone out for the sake of the name, accepting nothing from the Gentiles.

[8] Therefore we ought to support people like these, that we may be fellow workers for the truth.

[9] I have written something to the church, but Diotrephes, who likes to put himself first, does not acknowledge our authority.

[10] So if I come, I will bring up what he is doing, talking wicked nonsense against us. And not content with that, he refuses to welcome the brothers, and also stops those who want to and puts them out of the church.

[11] Beloved, do not imitate evil but imitate good. Whoever does good is from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; whoever does evil has not seen ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[12] ⟦demetrius|Demetrios|Demetrius⟧ has received a good testimony from everyone, and from the truth itself. We also add our testimony, and you know that our testimony is true.

[13] I had much to write to you, but I would rather not write with pen and ink.

[14] I hope to see you soon, and we will talk face to face. Peace be to you. The friends greet you. Greet the friends, each by name.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v6, 11) — systematic.
- **"church"** (v6, 9, 10) — Greek ἐκκλησία *ekklēsia* = "assembly/congregation"; KJV "church" retained.
- **"I wrote unto the church" → "I have written something to the church"** (v9) — "church"; modernized.
- **"who loveth to have the preeminence among them" → "who likes to put himself first"** (v9) — Greek ὁ φιλοπρωτεύων αὐτῶν Διοτρέφης *ho philoprōteuōn autōn Diotrephes* = "Diotrephes who loves to be first among them"; "likes to put himself first" is natural.
- **"prating against us with malicious words" → "talking wicked nonsense against us"** (v10) — Greek φλυαρῶν ἡμᾶς λόγοις πονηροῖς *phlyarōn hēmas logois ponērois* = "babbling nonsense against us with wicked words."
