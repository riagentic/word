# Genesis 43

[1] And the famine was heavy in the land.

[2] And it came to pass, when they had finished eating the grain that they had brought from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, that their father said to them, "Return, buy us a little food."

[3] And ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ said to him, saying, "The man surely warned us, saying, 'You shall not see my face unless your brother is with you.'

[4] If you are letting our brother go with us, we will go down and buy food for you.

[5] But if you are not letting him go, we will not go down, for the man said to us, 'You shall not see my face unless your brother is with you.'"

[6] And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said, "Why did you do evil to me, to tell the man that you had yet a brother?"

[7] And they said, "The man surely asked about us and about our kindred, saying, 'Is your father yet alive? Have you a brother?' And we told him according to these words. Could we surely know that he would say, 'Bring down your brother'?"

[8] And ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ said to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ his father, "Send the boy with me, and let us arise and go, and we shall live and not die, both we and you and our little ones.

[9] I myself will be surety for him; from my hand you shall require him. If I do not bring him to you and set him before you, I shall have sinned against you all the days.

[10] For if we had not lingered, surely by now we would have returned twice."

[11] And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ their father said to them, "If it is so, then do this: take of the choice produce of the land in your vessels, and bring down to the man an offering: a little balm and a little honey, gum and resin, pistachios and almonds.

[12] And take double silver in your hand; and the silver that was returned in the mouth of your bags you shall return in your hand. Perhaps it was a mistake.

[13] And take your brother, and arise, return to the man.

[14] And may ⟦el-shaddai|El Shaddai|God Almighty⟧ give you compassion before the man, and may he let go to you your other brother and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧. And I, as I am bereaved, I am bereaved."

[15] And the men took this offering, and double silver they took in their hand, and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧. And they arose and went down to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and stood before ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧.

[16] And ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ saw ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ with them, and he said to the one who was over his house, "Bring the men into the house, and slaughter an animal and make ready, for the men will eat with me at noon."

[17] And the man did as ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ said, and the man brought the men to the house of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧.

[18] And the men were afraid because they were brought to the house of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, and they said, "Because of the matter of the silver that came back in our bags at the beginning we are being brought in, to roll upon us and to fall upon us and to take us for slaves, and our donkeys."

[19] And they drew near to the man who was over the house of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ and spoke to him at the entrance of the house.

[20] And they said, "Oh, my lord, we surely came down at the beginning to buy food.

[21] And it came to pass, when we came to the lodging place, that we opened our bags, and behold, each man's silver was in the mouth of his bag, our silver in its weight; and we have returned it in our hand.

[22] And other silver we have brought down in our hand to buy food. We do not know who put our silver in our bags."

[23] And he said, "⟦shalom|Shalom|Peace⟧ to you; do not fear. Your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of your father has given you treasure in your bags. Your silver came to me." And he brought out ⟦simeon|Shimon|Simeon⟧ to them.

[24] And the man brought the men to the house of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, and he gave water, and they washed their feet, and he gave fodder to their donkeys.

[25] And they made ready the offering until ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ came at noon, for they had heard that they would eat bread there.

[26] And ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ came to the house, and they brought to him the offering that was in their hand, into the house, and they bowed down to him to the earth.

[27] And he asked them about their ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, and he said, "Is there ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ with your old father, of whom you spoke? Is he yet alive?"

[28] And they said, "There is ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ with your servant, our father; he is yet alive." And they bowed their heads and bowed down.

[29] And he lifted up his eyes and saw ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ his brother, the son of his mother, and he said, "Is this your youngest brother, of whom you spoke to me?" And he said, "May ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ show you favor, my son."

[30] And ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ hastened, for his compassion was stirred toward his brother, and he sought to weep; and he went into the chamber and wept there.

[31] And he washed his face and came out, and he restrained himself and said, "Set out bread."

[32] And they set out for him by himself, and for them by themselves, and for the ⟦egyptians|Mitzrim|Egyptians⟧ who were eating with him by themselves, for the ⟦egyptians|Mitzrim|Egyptians⟧ cannot eat bread with the ⟦hebrews|Ivrim|Hebrews⟧, for that is an abomination to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[33] And they sat before him, the firstborn according to his birthright and the youngest according to his youth, and the men marveled, each man to his neighbor.

[34] And he had portions carried from before him to them, and the portion of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ was five times greater than the portions of all of them. And they drank and became drunk with him.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"sore" → "heavy"** (v1) — Hebrew כָּבֵד, as 41:31.
- **"Go again" → "Return"** (v2, 13) — Hebrew שֻׁבוּ. "Buy" is שִׁבְרוּ, the verb "buy grain", here with the object "a little food"; so also v4, 20, 22.
- **"did solemnly protest" → "surely warned"** (v3) — Hebrew הָעֵד הֵעִד, the infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "warning he warned" (the verb is "testify, warn solemnly"). The same form is "surely asked" (v7, שָׁאוֹל שָׁאַל), "Could we surely know" (v7, הֲיָדוֹעַ נֵדַע) and "surely came down" (v20, יָרֹד יָרַדְנוּ).
- **"send" → "let go"** (v4, 5, 14) — Hebrew שִׁלַּח (piel), "send away, let go" one who is held; in v8 "Send" is the simple form שִׁלְחָה.
- **"dealt ye so ill with me" → "did you do evil to me"** (v6) — Hebrew הֲרֵעֹתֶם לִי. "That you had yet a brother" is in Hebrew the question itself: "Have you yet a brother?"
- **"according to the tenor of these words" → "according to these words"** (v7) — Hebrew עַל־פִּי הַדְּבָרִים הָאֵלֶּה, literally "upon the mouth of these words".
- **"lad" → "boy"** (v8) — Hebrew הַנַּעַר.
- **"let me bear the blame for ever" → "I shall have sinned against you all the days"** (v9) — Hebrew וְחָטָאתִי לְךָ כָּל־הַיָּמִים, literally so. "Require" is תְּבַקְשֶׁנּוּ, "you shall seek him".
- **"the best fruits in the land" → "the choice produce of the land"** (v11) — Hebrew מִזִּמְרַת הָאָרֶץ; זִמְרָה in this sense stands only here and its meaning is uncertain (the best, praised products of the land).
- **"a present" → "an offering"** (v11, 15, 25, 26) — Hebrew מִנְחָה, a gift brought to a superior; the same word as the grain offering of the altar.
- **"spices, and myrrh, nuts, and almonds" → "gum and resin, pistachios and almonds"** (v11) — Hebrew נְכֹאת וָלֹט בָּטְנִים וּשְׁקֵדִים. The first two (as 37:25) and the third, found only here, are of uncertain meaning; "balm" is צֳרִי.
- **"double money" → "double silver"** (v12, 15) — Hebrew כֶסֶף מִשְׁנֶה; כֶּסֶף is silver by weight, here the price (also v18, 21, 22, 23).
- **"sacks" → "bags"** (v12, 18, 21, 22, 23) — Hebrew אַמְתַּחַת throughout this chapter; "sack" (שַׂק, 42:25, 27, 35) does not occur here.
- **"an oversight" → "a mistake"** (v12) — Hebrew מִשְׁגֶּה.
- **"God Almighty" → "El Shaddai"** (v14) — Hebrew אֵל שַׁדַּי, as in 17:1; the meaning of שַׁדַּי is not certain.
- **"mercy" → "compassion"** (v14, 30) — Hebrew רַחֲמִים, both times. In v30 the Hebrew is נִכְמְרוּ רַחֲמָיו, "his compassions grew warm"; the KJV has "bowels".
- **"If I be bereaved of my children, I am bereaved" → "as I am bereaved, I am bereaved"** (v14) — Hebrew כַּאֲשֶׁר שָׁכֹלְתִּי שָׁכָלְתִּי; "if" and "of my children" are not in the Hebrew (the verb means to be made childless).
- **"ruler of his house" → "the one who was over his house"** (v16, 19) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר עַל־בֵּיתוֹ, literally so.
- **"slay" → "slaughter an animal"** (v16) — Hebrew וּטְבֹחַ טֶבַח, literally "slaughter a slaughter"; the root of "guards" (41:10), a different word from שָׁחַט.
- **"seek occasion against us" → "roll upon us"** (v18) — Hebrew לְהִתְגֹּלֵל עָלֵינוּ, literally "to roll oneself upon us"; the Hebrew does not name who.
- **"at the first time" → "at the beginning"** (v18, 20) — Hebrew בַּתְּחִלָּה, as 41:21.
- **"O sir" → "Oh, my lord"** (v20) — Hebrew בִּי אֲדֹנִי, literally "upon me, my lord", a form of asking leave to speak.
- **"the inn" → "the lodging place"** (v21) — Hebrew הַמָּלוֹן, as in 42:27. "In its weight" is בְּמִשְׁקָלוֹ.
- **"I had your money" → "Your silver came to me"** (v23) — Hebrew כַּסְפְּכֶם בָּא אֵלָי.
- **"Peace be to you" → "*Shalom* to you"** (v23); **"asked them of their welfare … Is your father well" → "asked them about their peace … Is there peace with"** (v27, 28) — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם each time: וַיִּשְׁאַל לָהֶם לְשָׁלוֹם, literally "he asked them as to peace"; הֲשָׁלוֹם אֲבִיכֶם, "Is your father peace?"
- **"the old man of whom ye spake" → "your old father, of whom you spoke"** (v27) — Hebrew אֲבִיכֶם הַזָּקֵן.
- **"bowed down their heads, and made obeisance" → "bowed their heads and bowed down"** (v28) — Hebrew וַיִּקְּדוּ וַיִּשְׁתַּחֲווּ. The written text (ketiv) has the second verb without the plural ending; the reading tradition (qere) has the plural, with no difference of meaning.
- **"your younger brother" → "your youngest brother"** (v29) — Hebrew הַקָּטֹן, literally "the small one"; in v33 "the youngest" is another word, הַצָּעִיר, "the young one".
- **"God be gracious unto thee" → "May *Elohim* show you favor"** (v29) — Hebrew יָחְנְךָ, the verb of חֵן "favor".
- **"he sought where to weep" → "he sought to weep"** (v30) — Hebrew וַיְבַקֵּשׁ לִבְכּוֹת; "where" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"refrained himself" → "restrained himself"** (v31) — Hebrew וַיִּתְאַפַּק, as 45:1.
- **"abomination unto the Egyptians" → "abomination to Egypt"** (v32) — Hebrew תּוֹעֵבָה … לְמִצְרָיִם: the verse has "the Egyptians" (הַמִּצְרִים) twice and then "Egypt" (מִצְרָיִם).
- **"marvelled one at another" → "marveled, each man to his neighbor"** (v33) — Hebrew אִישׁ אֶל־רֵעֵהוּ.
- **"he took and sent messes" → "he had portions carried"** (v34) — Hebrew וַיִּשָּׂא מַשְׂאֹת מֵאֵת פָּנָיו, literally "and he lifted liftings from before his face". "Five times" is חָמֵשׁ יָדוֹת, "five hands".
- **"were merry" → "became drunk"** (v34) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁכְּרוּ, the verb "be drunk".

### Notes

- **Judah steps up** — v8–9: Judah offers himself as surety for Benjamin, where Reuben had offered his own sons (42:37). Judah's leadership takes shape here and culminates in his speech of ch 44.
- **The fivefold portion** — Benjamin's portion at table sets the youngest above his brothers before their eyes, as Joseph once was.
- **Joseph's repeated tears** — the narrative foregrounds Joseph's emotion even as he keeps himself unknown to them.
