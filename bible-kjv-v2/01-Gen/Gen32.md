# Genesis 32

[1] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ went on his way, and angels of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ came upon him.

[2] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ said when he saw them, "This is the camp of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧." And he called the name of that place ⟦mahanaim|Machanayim|Mahanaim⟧.

[3] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ sent messengers before him to ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧ his brother, to the land of Seir, the field of Edom.

[4] And he commanded them, saying, "Thus you shall say to my lord, to ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧: 'Thus says your servant ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧: With ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ I have sojourned, and I have tarried until now.

[5] And I have oxen and donkeys, a flock, and male slaves and maidservants; and I have sent to tell my lord, to find favor in your eyes.'"

[6] And the messengers returned to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, saying, "We came to your brother, to ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧, and he is also coming to meet you, and four hundred men with him."

[7] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ was very afraid and was distressed; and he divided the people who were with him, and the flock and the herd and the camels, into two camps.

[8] And he said, "If ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧ comes to the one camp and strikes it, then the camp that is left will be for an escape."

[9] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ said, "⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my father ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my father ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, who said to me, 'Return to your land and to your kindred, and I will do good to you':

[10] I am too small for all the ⟦steadfast-love|chasadim|steadfast love⟧ and for all the truth that you have done to your servant; for with my staff I passed over this ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, and now I have become two camps.

[11] Deliver me, please, from the hand of my brother, from the hand of ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧; for I fear him, lest he come and strike me, mother upon sons.

[12] And you yourself said, 'I will surely do good to you, and I will make your seed as the sand of the sea, which cannot be counted for multitude.'"

[13] And he lodged there that night; and he took from what had come into his hand an offering for ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧ his brother:

[14] two hundred she-goats and twenty he-goats, two hundred ewes and twenty rams,

[15] thirty nursing camels and their young, forty cows and ten bulls, twenty she-donkeys and ten donkey colts.

[16] And he gave them into the hand of his slaves, each drove by itself; and he said to his slaves, "Pass over before me, and put a space between drove and drove."

[17] And he commanded the first, saying, "When ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧ my brother meets you and asks you, saying, 'Whose are you, and where are you going, and whose are these before you?'

[18] then you shall say, 'Your servant ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧'s; it is an offering sent to my lord, to ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧. And behold, he also is behind us.'"

[19] And he commanded also the second, also the third, also all who went behind the droves, saying, "According to this word you shall speak to ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧ when you find him;

[20] and you shall say, 'Also, behold, your servant ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ is behind us.'" For he said, "Let me appease his face with the offering that goes before my face, and afterward I will see his face; perhaps he will lift up my face."

[21] And the offering passed over before his face, and he himself lodged that night in the camp.

[22] And he arose in that night and took his two wives and his two maidservants and his eleven children, and passed over the ford of ⟦jabbok|Yabboq|Jabbok⟧.

[23] And he took them and made them pass over the wadi, and he made what was his pass over.

[24] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ was left alone; and a man wrestled with him until the going up of the dawn.

[25] And he saw that he could not overcome him, and he touched the hollow of his thigh; and the hollow of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧'s thigh was put out of joint as he wrestled with him.

[26] And he said, "Let me go, for the dawn has gone up." And he said, "I will not let you go unless you have blessed me."

[27] And he said to him, "What is your name?" And he said, "⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧."

[28] And he said, "Your name shall no more be called ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, but ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; for you have striven with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and with men, and have overcome."

[29] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ asked and said, "Tell, please, your name." And he said, "Why is this, that you ask for my name?" And he blessed him there.

[30] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ called the name of the place Peniel: "For I have seen ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ face to face, and my soul has been delivered."

[31] And the sun rose upon him as he passed Penuel, and he was limping upon his thigh.

[32] Therefore the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ do not eat the sinew of the hip, which is upon the hollow of the thigh, to this day; because he touched the hollow of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧'s thigh, at the sinew of the hip.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And" kept at the head of the clauses** (throughout) — Hebrew וַ / וְ; "when", "so", "then", "but" are not put in its place.
- **"angels of God" → "angels of *Elohim*"; "messengers"** (v1, 3, 6) — one Hebrew word: מַלְאֲכֵי אֱלֹהִים (v1) and מַלְאָכִים (v3, 6), "messengers". Those of *Elohim* are rendered "angels" (as "the angel of *Elohim*", 31:11), those Jacob sends "messengers"; the Hebrew makes no difference between them, and the echo is intended: messengers of *Elohim* come upon Jacob, and Jacob sends messengers to Esau. "Came upon" is וַיִּפְגְּעוּ־בוֹ (פָּגַע בְּ); "meet" in v17 is another verb, פָּגַשׁ.
- **"God's host" → "the camp of *Elohim*"** (v2) — Hebrew מַחֲנֵה אֱלֹהִים *machaneh Elohim*; מַחֲנֶה is "camp" all through the chapter (v7, 8, 10, 21). The name מַחֲנָיִם *Machanayim* is its dual, "two camps"; the Hebrew verse does not explain the name.
- **"the country of Edom" → "the field of Edom"** (v3) — Hebrew שְׂדֵה אֱדוֹם.
- **"thy servant Jacob" kept: "your servant Jacob"** (v4, 18, 20; the courtesy form before a superior, as Gen 18:3; 42:10) — Hebrew עַבְדְּךָ, the word עֶבֶד "slave", the same as "male slaves" (v5) and "his slaves" (v16), spoken to "my lord" (אֲדֹנִי); in 25:23 and 27:29, 37, 40 it is Esau who is to serve (עָבַד) Jacob. To *Elohim* (v10) the same word is "your servant", as everywhere of Yahweh's servants.
- **"oxen and donkeys, a flock, and male slaves and maidservants"** (v5) — the Hebrew has each in the singular, as a kind: "ox and donkey, flock and slave and maidservant" (שׁוֹר, חֲמוֹר, צֹאן, עֶבֶד, שִׁפְחָה).
- **"find grace in thy sight" → "find favor in your eyes"** (v5) — Hebrew לִמְצֹא־חֵן בְּעֵינֶיךָ.
- **"was distressed"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיֵּצֶר לוֹ, literally "it was narrow for him".
- **"And said" kept** (v8) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר "and he said"; "thinking" is not in the Hebrew. "Will be for an escape": וְהָיָה … לִפְלֵיטָה.
- **"O God" → "*Elohim*"** (v9) — the Hebrew has no "O". "Who said" is a participle, הָאֹמֵר, "the one saying". "I will do good to you" (v9, 12) is literally "with you" (עִמָּךְ); in v12 with the infinitive absolute, הֵיטֵב אֵיטִיב, "doing good I will do good" = "surely".
- **"I am not worthy of the least of all the mercies" → "I am too small for all the *chasadim*"** (v10) — Hebrew קָטֹנְתִּי מִכֹּל הַחֲסָדִים, literally "I am smaller than all the *chasadim*"; "worthy" and "the least" are not in the Hebrew. חֲסָדִים is the plural of *chesed*; אֱמֶת is "truth". "Done to" is עָשָׂה אֶת.
- **"passed over this Jordan"** (v10) — Hebrew עָבַר, the verb that runs through the passage: v16, 21, 22, 23 (twice, "made pass over"), 31; 33:3, 14. "With only" is not in the Hebrew: "with my staff".
- **"the mother with the children" → "mother upon sons"** (v11) — Hebrew אֵם עַל־בָּנִים, without articles: a set phrase for a slaughter that spares no one (Hos 10:14). בָּנִים is "sons".
- **"seed"** (v12) — Hebrew זֶרַע.
- **"that which came to his hand"; "present" → "offering"** (v13, 18, 20, 21) — Hebrew מִן־הַבָּא בְיָדוֹ, "from what had come into his hand"; מִנְחָה, the word for a gift brought to a superior, later the word for the grain offering.
- **"their colts" → "their young"; "foals" → "donkey colts"** (v15) — Hebrew בְּנֵיהֶם, literally "their sons"; עְיָרִם, young male donkeys. The numbers stand after the nouns in Hebrew ("she-goats two hundred").
- **"every drove by themselves"** (v16) — Hebrew עֵדֶר עֵדֶר לְבַדּוֹ, "drove, drove, by itself".
- **"They be thy servant Jacob's" → "Your servant Jacob's"** (v18) — Hebrew לְעַבְדְּךָ לְיַעֲקֹב answers the question "Whose?"; "they belong" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"On this manner" → "According to this word"** (v19) — Hebrew כַּדָּבָר הַזֶּה.
- **"I will appease him with the present … peradventure he will accept of me" → "Let me appease his face … he will lift up my face"** (v20) — Hebrew אֲכַפְּרָה פָנָיו … לְפָנָי … אֶרְאֶה פָנָיו … יִשָּׂא פָנָי, and v21 עַל־פָּנָיו: "face" five times, all kept. אֲכַפְּרָה is the verb כִּפֶּר, elsewhere "make atonement" (its root sense is given as "cover" or "wipe"); with a man's face as its object the sense is "appease". "Lift up the face of" = receive kindly.
- **"eleven sons" → "eleven children"** (v22) — Hebrew יְלָדָיו *yeladav*, "his children" (not בָּנִים "sons").
- **"womenservants" → "maidservants"** (v22) — Hebrew שִׁפְחֹת.
- **"brook" → "wadi"** (v23) — Hebrew נַחַל. The Jabbok (יַבֹּק *Yabboq*) echoes both the name Jacob (*Yaaqov*) and the verb "wrestled" (*ye'aveq*, v24).
- **"there wrestled a man with him"** (v24, 25) — Hebrew וַיֵּאָבֵק אִישׁ עִמּוֹ: אִישׁ, "a man", nothing more; the verb נֶאֱבַק is found only in these two verses (probably from אָבָק "dust"). "The going up of the dawn" is עֲלוֹת הַשָּׁחַר; v26 "the dawn has gone up".
- **"when he saw" → "And he saw"** (v25) — the Hebrew names no subject for "saw", "could not" or "touched"; "the man" is not supplied.
- **"prevailed not against him" → "could not overcome him"; "hast prevailed" → "have overcome"** (v25, 28) — Hebrew לֹא יָכֹל לוֹ, וַתּוּכָל: יָכֹל "be able", with לְ "be able for, overcome".
- **"touched the hollow of his thigh"** (v25, 32) — Hebrew וַיִּגַּע בְּכַף־יְרֵכוֹ: נָגַע is "touch" (it can be a touch that harms); כַּף is "palm, hollow", יָרֵךְ "thigh": the hollow of the hip joint. "Struck" and "socket" say more than the words.
- **"out of joint"** (v25) — Hebrew וַתֵּקַע, "was wrenched, dislocated".
- **"except thou bless me" → "unless you have blessed me"** (v26) — Hebrew כִּי אִם־בֵּרַכְתָּנִי, a perfect. "Let me go" and "let you go" are שִׁלַּח, "send away".
- **"as a prince hast thou power with God" → "you have striven with *Elohim*"** (v28) — Hebrew שָׂרִיתָ עִם־אֱלֹהִים וְעִם־אֲנָשִׁים וַתּוּכָל. שָׂרָה, "strive, contend", is found only here and in Hos 12:3–4; the name יִשְׂרָאֵל *Yisrael* is explained by it. KJV's "as a prince" takes it from שַׂר "prince". אֱלֹהִים stands beside אֲנָשִׁים "men" without the article; it is the word rendered *Elohim* throughout, and can also be read "divine beings".
- **"Wherefore is it that thou dost ask after my name?"** (v29) — Hebrew לָמָּה זֶּה תִּשְׁאַל לִשְׁמִי.
- **"Peniel … Penuel"** (v30, 31) — two forms of one name, פְּנִיאֵל / פְּנוּאֵל, "face of El". The words after the name are Jacob's own, without "saying".
- **"my life is preserved" → "my soul has been delivered"** (v30) — Hebrew וַתִּנָּצֵל נַפְשִׁי: נֶפֶשׁ "soul", and the passive of הִצִּיל "deliver" (v11).
- **"halted upon his thigh" → "was limping upon his thigh"** (v31) — Hebrew צֹלֵעַ עַל־יְרֵכוֹ.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v32) — Hebrew בְנֵי־יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"the sinew which shrank" → "the sinew of the hip"** (v32) — Hebrew גִּיד הַנָּשֶׁה *gid ha-nasheh*. The meaning of נָשֶׁה is uncertain; the word is found only in this verse. Best supported: the nerve or sinew of the hip (the sciatic nerve); "which shrank" is an old guess from a verb. The practice still stands: the sciatic nerve is removed from kosher meat (*nikkur*).

### Notes

- **Verse numbers** — in the Hebrew Bible this chapter begins one verse earlier (our 31:55 is Hebrew 32:1), so each Hebrew verse number here is one higher than ours (our 32:1 = Hebrew 32:2 … our 32:32 = Hebrew 32:33).
- **Who did Jacob wrestle?** The text calls him "a man" (v24), then Jacob says "I have seen *Elohim* face to face" (v30); Hos 12:4 speaks of a מַלְאָךְ, an angel. Interpretations:
  1. Yahweh himself in theophanic form.
  2. A divine messenger (angel).
  3. Esau's guardian spirit (rabbinic tradition, cast as the angel of Esau).
  4. Jacob's own conscience / Esau-fear externalized.
  The text leaves the identity hidden — when Jacob asks the name (v29), the answer is withheld.
- **The rename** — Jacob ("heel-grasper / supplanter") becomes Israel (explained in v28 by "you have striven with *Elohim*"). The blessing secured here is given, not stolen (contrast ch 27). Yet Jacob limps — the blessing costs him something permanent.
- **Climax of the Jacob cycle** — Jacob spends the next day meeting Esau (ch 33), and the reconciliation succeeds. The wrestling foreshadows the reunion: if Jacob survives the divine encounter, he can survive Esau.
