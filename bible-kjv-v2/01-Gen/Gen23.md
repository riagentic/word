## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Kirjath-arba" → "Kiriath-Arba"** (v2) — Hebrew קִרְיַת אַרְבַּע *Qiryat Arba* = "city of Arba" (named for the giant Arba, Josh 14:15). Kiriath = city (compound in place names). KJV transliteration slightly off; standardized.
- **"sons of Heth" (v3, 5, 10, 16, 18, 20)** — Hebrew בְּנֵי־חֵת *bne-Chet*. The Hittites. Hittite presence in Canaan is debated historically (the Hittite empire was in Anatolia, north of this region); biblical "Hittites" may denote related or confused subgroups. Original term preserved.
- **"a stranger and a sojourner" → "a resident alien and a sojourner"** (v4) — Hebrew גֵּר־וְתוֹשָׁב *ger we-toshav*. *Ger* = resident foreigner with partial legal rights; *toshav* = dweller / temporary resident. Both terms together emphasize Abraham's non-citizen status — relevant to his need to purchase the burial ground (he owns no inherited land).
- **"a possession of a buryingplace" → "a burial holding"** (v4, 9, 20) — Hebrew אֲחֻזַּת־קֶבֶר *achuzzat-qever*. *Achuzzah* = legal holding / property. Abraham wants a permanent legal possession, not just use-privileges.
- **"mighty prince" → "prince of *Elohim*"** (v6) — Hebrew נְשִׂיא אֱלֹהִים *nesi Elohim*. Could be (a) superlative: "mighty prince" (*Elohim* used adjectivally as "mighty"); or (b) literal: "prince of God / a prince appointed by God." The sons of Heth recognize Abraham's stature as one touched by the divine. Translated literally; superlative reading possible.
- **"in the choice of our sepulchres" → "in the choicest of our burial-places"** (v6) — Hebrew בְּמִבְחַר קְבָרֵינוּ. The best available.
- **"communed" → "spoke"** (v8) — modernized.
- **"if it be your mind" → "if it is your desire"** (v8) — Hebrew אִם־יֵשׁ אֶת־נַפְשְׁכֶם *im-yesh et-naphshekhem* = "if it is with your *nephesh* / your will."
- **"intreat" → "intercede"** (v8) — Hebrew פִּגְעוּ־לִי *pig'u-li* = "intercede for me / make intercession on my behalf."
- **"as much money as it is worth" → "for the full price"** (v9) — Hebrew בְּכֶסֶף מָלֵא *be-khesef male* = "for full silver." Abraham insists on paying full market value.
- **"in the audience of" → "in the hearing of"** (v10, 13, 16) — modernized ("audience" in modern English = listeners, not the act of hearing).
- **"current money with the merchant" → "according to the weights current among the merchants"** (v16) — Hebrew עֹבֵר לַסֹּחֵר *over la-socher* = "passing for the merchant" = accepted by trade standard (coins or weights of silver accepted in commerce). Pre-minted coinage; silver was weighed out by standard merchant-scales.

### Notes

- **Why a chapter on a property transaction?** The text devotes unusual space to the purchase of the Machpelah cave. Reason: Abraham's first actual foothold in the promised land is a tomb. The patriarch whose offspring will inherit the whole land owns only a burial cave during his life — a tension the chapter highlights. It is also a legal record (detailed formula of the transfer: field, cave, trees, borders, witnesses) serving as a title-deed in narrative form.
- **Oriental bargaining** — the exchange follows Near Eastern custom: Ephron appears to offer the land free, Abraham insists on paying, Ephron names a price (400 shekels — a very substantial sum), Abraham pays in full. The apparent generosity is part of the bargaining ritual; the price is steep.
- **Machpelah's later significance** — Sarah (ch 23), Abraham (25:9), Isaac (35:29), Rebekah (49:31), Leah (49:31), and Jacob (50:13) are all buried there. The traditional site in modern Hebron (Tomb of the Patriarchs / al-Haram al-Ibrahimi) is contested sacred space for Jews, Christians, and Muslims.
