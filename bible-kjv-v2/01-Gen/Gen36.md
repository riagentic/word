## Changes

- **"dukes" → "chiefs"** (v15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 21, 29, 30, 40–43) — Hebrew אַלּוּף *alluf*, the head of a clan; one rendering throughout. "Duke" (via Latin *dux*) is medieval European. The word is related to אֶלֶף "thousand / clan".
- **"Aholibamah" → "Oholibamah"** (v2, 5, 14, 18, 25, 41) — Hebrew אָהֳלִיבָמָה *Oholivamah*. Modern transliteration.
- **"that is, Edom" → "he is Edom"** (v1, 8, 19, 43) — Hebrew הוּא אֱדוֹם, "he [is] Edom"; in v43 הוּא עֵשָׂו "he is Esau". In v19 the words stand at the end of the verse, after "their chiefs", and are left there.
- **"the daughter of Anah the daughter of Zibeon" kept** (v2, 14) — Hebrew בַּת־עֲנָה בַּת־צִבְעוֹן, "daughter" twice. The second "daughter" may stand beside the first (Oholibamah as daughter of Anah and granddaughter of Zibeon) or describe Anah. In v24–25 Anah is a son of Zibeon, and some ancient versions read "son of Zibeon" here (from memory: Septuagint, Samaritan, Syriac); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"Jeush"** (v5, 14) — the written text (ketiv) has יעיש *Yeish*; the reading tradition (qere) has יְעוּשׁ *Yeush*, as v18 writes it. The qere is translated.
- **"all the persons of his house" → "all the souls of his house"** (v6) — Hebrew נַפְשׁוֹת בֵּיתוֹ, plural of נֶפֶשׁ *nephesh*.
- **"cattle … beasts … substance" → "livestock … beasts … property"** (v6) — Hebrew מִקְנֶה, בְּהֵמָה, קִנְיָן: three words, three renderings. "Acquired" is רָכַשׁ, the verb of רְכוּשׁ "goods" in v7.
- **"into the country from the face of" → "to a land away from the face of"** (v6) — Hebrew אֶל־אֶרֶץ מִפְּנֵי יַעֲקֹב: "to a land", with no name (from memory: the Syriac reads "to the land of Seir"). מִפְּנֵי is literally "from the face of"; the same word is "because of" in v7.
- **"more than that they might dwell together" → "too many for dwelling together"** (v7) — Hebrew רָב מִשֶּׁבֶת יַחְדָּו. "Sojournings" is plural in Hebrew (מְגוּרֵיהֶם); "bear" is נָשָׂא, to carry.
- **"mount Seir" → "the hill country of Seir"** (v8, 9) — Hebrew הַר שֵׂעִיר, literally "the mountain of Seir", the name of a region.
- **"father of Edom"** (v9, 43) — Hebrew אֲבִי אֱדוֹם: the name of the people is the same word as the name of the man in v1.
- **"These were the sons" → "These are the sons"** (v12, 16) — Hebrew אֵלֶּה בְּנֵי without a verb; in v13, 14 the Hebrew has הָיוּ "were".
- **"found the mules" → "found the hot springs"** (v24) — Hebrew הַיֵּמִם *ha-yemim*, a word found only here; its meaning is uncertain. "Hot springs" is the sense of the Latin Vulgate (*aquas calidas*, from memory); Jewish tradition and the KJV have "mules"; the Septuagint only transliterates the word (from memory). One rendering is kept, without certainty.
- **"both Aiah and Anah"** (v24) — Hebrew וְאַיָּה וַעֲנָה, with "and" before the first name as well.
- **"as he fed the asses of Zibeon" → "as he pastured the donkeys of Zibeon"** (v24) — Hebrew בִּרְעֹתוֹ אֶת־הַחֲמֹרִים לְצִבְעוֹן, literally "the donkeys belonging to Zibeon".
- **"the children of Anah" → "the sons of Anah"** (v25) — Hebrew בְנֵי־עֲנָה, "sons", though one son and one daughter follow.
- **"Dishon" → "Dishan"** (v26) — the Masoretic Text has דִישָׁן *Dishan* here, the same name as in v28, although the list of v21 leads one to expect Dishon; 1 Chr 1:41 has Dishon. The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"among their dukes" → "according to their chiefs"** (v30) — Hebrew לְאַלֻּפֵיהֶם, the same word "chief": chief by chief. "According to their clans" is not what the Hebrew says.
- **"before there reigned any king over the children of Israel" → "before there reigned a king of the sons of Israel"** (v31) — Hebrew לִפְנֵי מְלָךְ־מֶלֶךְ לִבְנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל. The לְ ("of / for / belonging to") leaves open whether the king reigns over Israel or is an Israelite king reigning over Edom. בְּנֵי is "sons".
- **"smote Midian in the field of Moab" → "struck Midian in the field of Moab"** (v35) — Hebrew הַמַּכֶּה … בִּשְׂדֵה מוֹאָב; שָׂדֶה is "field".
- **"Hadad / Hadar"** (v35, 39) — MT v35 has *Hadad*, v39 has *Hadar*. 1 Chr 1:50 has *Hadad* in both places; the letters d and r look alike in Hebrew. Left as MT.
- **"Rehoboth by the river" → "Rehoboth of the River"** (v37) — Hebrew רְחֹבוֹת הַנָּהָר, a construct: "Rehoboth of the River". "The River" is often the Euphrates.
- **"families" → "clans"; "habitations" → "dwelling places"** (v40, 43) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּחָה; מוֹשָׁב, from יָשַׁב "dwell".

### Notes

- **Why this long Esau genealogy?** The Genesis structure dedicates one chapter each to the non-covenant lines (Ishmael, ch 25; Esau, ch 36) before focusing entirely on the covenant line. The chapter establishes Edom as a structured nation with kings "before there reigned a king of the sons of Israel" (v31).
- **Note v31's implication** — the clause implies the text knows of later Israelite kings; this is one data point in the discussion of the composition history of Genesis (the phrase reads as post-monarchical retrospect).
- **Overlapping / redundant lists** — the chapter interleaves different tribal registers (Esau's sons, Horite sons, chiefs of Edom, kings of Edom, later chiefs by place), sometimes with different names for the same figures. Translated as it stands.
- **Horites** — pre-Edomite inhabitants of Seir, later displaced (Deut 2:12, 22). Possibly identified with the Hurrians; etymology disputed.
- **Amalek (v12)** — Timna's son by Eliphaz, the ancestral eponym of Amalek, Israel's archetypal desert enemy (Ex 17:8–16, Deut 25:17–19). His mother is called a "concubine".
- **Korah (v16)** — chief Korah stands among the chiefs of Eliphaz in the Masoretic Text, though v11 does not name him among the sons of Eliphaz and v18 lists him under Oholibamah. Translated as it stands.
