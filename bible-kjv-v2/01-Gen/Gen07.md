# Genesis 7

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧, "Come, you and all your house, into the ark, for you I have seen righteous before me in this generation.

[2] Of every clean beast you shall take to yourself seven by seven, a male and his mate; and of the beasts that are not clean, two, a male and his mate;

[3] also of the birds of the heavens seven by seven, male and female, to keep seed alive on the face of all the earth.

[4] For in yet seven days I am causing it to rain on the earth forty days and forty nights, and I will wipe out every existing thing that I have made from the face of the ground."

[5] And ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ did according to all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded him.

[6] And ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ was six hundred years old, and the flood came, waters upon the earth.

[7] And ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ came, and his sons and his wife and his sons' wives with him, into the ark, because of the waters of the flood.

[8] Of the clean beasts, and of the beasts that are not clean, and of the birds, and everything that creeps on the ground,

[9] two by two they came to ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ into the ark, male and female, as ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had commanded ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧.

[10] And it came to pass after the seven days that the waters of the flood were upon the earth.

[11] In the six hundredth year of ⟦noah|Noach's|Noah's⟧ life, in the second month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on that day all the springs of the great deep were split open, and the windows of the heavens were opened.

[12] And the rain was upon the earth forty days and forty nights.

[13] On that very day ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ came, and Shem and ⟦ham|Cham|Ham⟧ and ⟦japheth|Yefet|Japheth⟧, the sons of ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧, and ⟦noah|Noach's|Noah's⟧ wife and the three wives of his sons with them, into the ark,

[14] they, and every living thing after its kind, and every beast after its kind, and every creeping thing that creeps on the earth after its kind, and all the flying things after their kind, every bird of every wing.

[15] And they came to ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ into the ark, two by two of all flesh in which was the spirit of life.

[16] And those that came, male and female of all flesh they came, as ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had commanded him; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shut behind him.

[17] And the flood was forty days upon the earth; and the waters increased and lifted the ark, and it rose from upon the earth.

[18] And the waters prevailed and increased greatly upon the earth, and the ark went on the face of the waters.

[19] And the waters prevailed exceedingly upon the earth, and all the high mountains that were under all the heavens were covered.

[20] Fifteen cubits upward the waters prevailed, and the mountains were covered.

[21] And all flesh expired that creeps on the earth, of birds and of beasts and of living things, and of every swarming thing that swarms on the earth, and every man.

[22] All in whose nostrils was the breath of the spirit of life, of all that was on the dry ground, died.

[23] And he wiped out every existing thing that was on the face of the ground, from man to beast, to creeping thing and to the birds of the heavens, and they were wiped out from the earth; and only ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ was left, and those who were with him in the ark.

[24] And the waters prevailed upon the earth a hundred and fifty days.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"for thee have I seen righteous" kept** (v1) — Hebrew כִּי־אֹתְךָ רָאִיתִי צַדִּיק, "you" first.
- **"by sevens" → "seven by seven"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew שִׁבְעָה שִׁבְעָה, literally "seven seven", the same doubling as שְׁנַיִם שְׁנַיִם "two two" = "two by two" (v9, 15). Whether it means seven of each kind or seven pairs is not said by the Hebrew; the verse does not decide. In v2 the unclean beasts have a single שְׁנַיִם, "two".
- **"the male and his female" → "a male and his mate"** (v2) — Hebrew אִישׁ וְאִשְׁתּוֹ, literally "a man and his wife", said here of beasts. v3, 9, 16 have the other pair of words, זָכָר וּנְקֵבָה "male and female".
- **"clean beast / beasts that are not clean"** (v2, 8) — Hebrew בְּהֵמָה, "beast" (see 6:7). No rule about clean and unclean beasts has yet been given in the book.
- **"fowls" → "birds"** (v3, 8, 23) — Hebrew עוֹף, all that flies. In v14, where it stands beside צִפּוֹר "bird", it is rendered "flying things".
- **"For yet seven days, and I will cause it to rain" → "For in yet seven days I am causing it to rain"** (v4) — Hebrew לְיָמִים עוֹד שִׁבְעָה אָנֹכִי מַמְטִיר, a participle.
- **"living substance" → "existing thing"** (v4, 23) — Hebrew יְקוּם *yequm*, from קוּם "stand": what stands, what exists. A rare word (elsewhere only Deut 11:6).
- **"destroy" → "wipe out"** (v4, 23) — Hebrew מָחָה *machah* = wipe away, blot out. As in 6:7.
- **"six hundred years old"** (v6) — Hebrew בֶּן־שֵׁשׁ מֵאוֹת שָׁנָה, literally "a son of six hundred years".
- **"the flood of waters was upon the earth" → "the flood came, waters upon the earth"** (v6) — Hebrew וְהַמַּבּוּל הָיָה מַיִם עַל־הָאָרֶץ: "the flood was" (came to be), with "waters upon the earth" set beside it, as in 6:17. The two halves are joined by "and" in Hebrew, not by "when".
- **"went in" → "came"** (v1, 7, 9, 13, 15, 16) — one Hebrew verb, בּוֹא, throughout.
- **"because of the waters"** (v7) — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי, literally "from the face of".
- **"after seven days" → "after the seven days"** (v10) — Hebrew לְשִׁבְעַת הַיָּמִים, with the article: the seven days of v4.
- **"In the six hundredth year"** (v11) — Hebrew בִּשְׁנַת שֵׁשׁ־מֵאוֹת שָׁנָה לְחַיֵּי־נֹחַ, literally "in the year of six hundred years of the life of Noah"; בְּשִׁבְעָה־עָשָׂר יוֹם לַחֹדֶשׁ "on the seventeenth day of the month". Numbers stand in the ordinary English order; in v24 the Hebrew order is "fifty and a hundred" (חֲמִשִּׁים וּמְאַת יוֹם).
- **"the same day" → "on that day"** (v11) — Hebrew בַּיּוֹם הַזֶּה, literally "on this day". "On that very day" in v13 is another phrase, בְּעֶצֶם הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה, literally "in the bone of this day".
- **"fountains of the great deep broken up" → "springs of the great deep were split open"** (v11) — Hebrew נִבְקְעוּ כָּל־מַעְיְנֹת תְּהוֹם רַבָּה. *Tehom* = "the deep" of 1:2; בָּקַע is "cleave, split".
- **"windows of heaven" → "windows of the heavens"** (v11) — Hebrew אֲרֻבֹּת הַשָּׁמַיִם. *Arubbah* is a latticed opening; the "window" of the ark in 8:6 is another word (חַלּוֹן).
- **"every beast after his kind" → "every living thing after its kind"** (v14, 21) — Hebrew חַיָּה *chayyah*, "living thing", beside בְּהֵמָה "beast". The word itself says "living", not "wild".
- **"every fowl after his kind, every bird of every sort" → "all the flying things after their kind, every bird of every wing"** (v14) — Hebrew וְכָל־הָעוֹף לְמִינֵהוּ כֹּל צִפּוֹר כָּל־כָּנָף, literally "every bird of every wing".
- **"breath of life" → "spirit of life"** (v15) — Hebrew רוּחַ חַיִּים, as in 6:17.
- **"they that went in, went in male and female"** (v16) — Hebrew וְהַבָּאִים … בָּאוּ; the verb is repeated.
- **"the LORD shut him in" → "Yahweh shut behind him"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיִּסְגֹּר יְהוָה בַּעֲדוֹ; no object is named (as "shut the door behind you", 2 Kgs 4:4).
- **"increased"** (v17, 18) — Hebrew רָבָה, the "multiply" of 6:1; said of waters, "become much".
- **"it was lift up above the earth" → "it rose from upon the earth"** (v17) — Hebrew וַתָּרָם מֵעַל הָאָרֶץ.
- **"the waters prevailed" kept** (v18, 19, 20, 24) — Hebrew גָּבַר = be strong, prevail; the root of גִּבּוֹר "mighty one" (6:4).
- **"the ark went"** (v18) — Hebrew וַתֵּלֶךְ, "went, walked"; there is no word "floated".
- **"exceedingly"** (v19) — Hebrew מְאֹד מְאֹד, "very, very".
- **"high hills" → "high mountains"** (v19) — Hebrew הֶהָרִים הַגְּבֹהִים; הַר = mountain. "Under all the heavens": תַּחַת כָּל־הַשָּׁמָיִם.
- **"Fifteen cubits upward did the waters prevail" kept** (v20) — Hebrew חֲמֵשׁ עֶשְׂרֵה אַמָּה מִלְמַעְלָה גָּבְרוּ הַמָּיִם; from where the fifteen cubits are counted is not said.
- **"died" → "expired"** (v21) — Hebrew וַיִּגְוַע (6:17); "died" in v22 is מֵתוּ.
- **"moved" → "creeps"** (v21) — Hebrew הָרֹמֵשׂ, the verb of "creeping thing"; here of all flesh on the earth.
- **"every man"** (v21) — Hebrew וְכֹל הָאָדָם.
- **"breath of life" → "breath of the spirit of life"** (v22) — Hebrew נִשְׁמַת־רוּחַ חַיִּים: *neshamah* (breath, 2:7) + *ruach* (spirit / breath / wind) + *chayyim* (life).
- **"dry land" → "dry ground"** (v22) — Hebrew חָרָבָה, from חָרֵב "be dry" (8:13); "dry land" in 1:9 is יַבָּשָׁה.
- **"was destroyed" → "he wiped out"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיִּמַח, as pointed an active form without a named subject.
- **"Noah only remained alive" → "only Noah was left"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיִשָּׁאֶר אַךְ־נֹחַ; "alive" is not in the Hebrew.
### Notes

- **Numbers in the flood narrative** — seven by seven of clean beasts, two of the unclean, seven days, forty days of rain, a hundred and fifty days of the waters prevailing.
- **"Yahweh shut behind him" (v16)** — Noah does not close the ark himself; Yahweh closes it.
