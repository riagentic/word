# Genesis 7

[1] Then Yahweh said to Noah, "Come, you and all your household, into the ark, for I have seen that you are righteous before me in this generation.

[2] Of every clean animal take with you seven pairs, the male and his female; and of the animals that are not clean, a pair, the male and his female;

[3] also of the birds of the heavens, seven pairs, male and female, to keep seed alive on the face of all the earth.

[4] For in seven more days I will send rain on the earth forty days and forty nights, and I will wipe from the face of the ground every living thing that I have made."

[5] And Noah did all that Yahweh commanded him.

[6] Noah was 600 years old when the flood of waters came on the earth.

[7] Noah went in, and his sons, his wife, and his sons' wives with him, into the ark, because of the waters of the flood.

[8] Of the clean animals, and of the animals that are not clean, and of the birds, and of everything that creeps on the ground,

[9] two by two they went in to Noah into the ark, male and female, as *Elohim* had commanded Noah.

[10] And after seven days the waters of the flood came on the earth.

[11] In the 600th year of Noah's life, in the second month, on the 17th day of the month — on that very day all the springs of the great deep burst forth, and the windows of the heavens were opened.

[12] The rain was on the earth forty days and forty nights.

[13] On that very day Noah entered the ark, with Shem, Ham, and Japheth, the sons of Noah, and Noah's wife and the three wives of his sons with them,

[14] they and every wild animal according to its kind, and all the livestock according to their kinds, and every creeping thing that creeps on the earth according to its kind, and every bird according to its kind — every winged creature.

[15] They went in to Noah into the ark, two by two of all flesh in which was the breath of life.

[16] Those that entered, male and female of all flesh, went in as *Elohim* had commanded him; and Yahweh shut him in.

[17] The flood came on the earth for forty days; and the waters increased and lifted up the ark, and it rose above the earth.

[18] The waters grew strong and increased greatly on the earth, and the ark floated on the face of the waters.

[19] The waters grew exceedingly strong on the earth, so that all the high mountains under the whole heavens were covered.

[20] The waters rose fifteen cubits higher, and the mountains were covered.

[21] All flesh that moved on the earth perished — birds, livestock, wild animals, all the swarming creatures that swarm on the earth, and all humankind.

[22] Everything in whose nostrils was the breath of the spirit of life, of all that was on the dry land, died.

[23] He wiped out every living thing that was on the face of the ground — from humans to livestock, to creeping things, and to the birds of the heavens — they were wiped from the earth. Only Noah remained, and those with him in the ark.

[24] And the waters prevailed on the earth 150 days.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"by sevens" → "seven pairs"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew שִׁבְעָה שִׁבְעָה *shiv'ah shiv'ah*. The doubled form indicates pairs, not individuals: "seven by seven" = seven pairs (male and female). Some take it as seven individuals; the pairing language ("the male and his female") in the same verse strongly supports seven pairs.
- **"clean beast / beast that are not clean" → "clean animal / animal not clean"** (v2, 8) — Hebrew בְּהֵמָה טְהוֹרָה / בְּהֵמָה אֲשֶׁר אֵינֶנָּה טְהֹרָה *behemah tehorah / behemah asher einennah tehorah*. "Beast" is archaic for this context (domestic animal distinction, prefiguring Leviticus).
- **"fowls" → "birds"** (v3, 8, 14, 23) — as in Gen 1.
- **"living substance" → "living thing"** (v4, 23) — Hebrew יְקוּם *yequm* = that which exists / stands / lives. "Substance" in modern English suggests material, not life.
- **"destroy" → "wipe out / wipe from"** (v4, 23) — Hebrew מָחָה *machah* = to wipe / blot out. As in 6:7.
- **"selfsame day" → "that very day"** (v13) — modernized. Same idiom.
- **"every beast after his kind" → "every wild animal according to its kind"** (v14) — חַיָּה *chayyah* here = wild (non-domestic) animal, paired with *behemah* (livestock). KJV's "beast" is generic; modernized.
- **"every fowl after his kind, every bird of every sort" → "every bird according to its kind — every winged creature"** (v14) — Hebrew כָּל־עוֹף כָּל־צִפּוֹר כָּל־כָּנָף *kol-oph kol-tsippor kol-kanaph*. "Every bird, every bird-type, every winged-one." Condensed for readability.
- **"fountains of the great deep" → "springs of the great deep"** (v11) — Hebrew מַעְיְנֹת תְּהוֹם רַבָּה *ma'yenot tehom rabbah*. *Tehom* = "the deep" of Gen 1:2; *ma'yenot* = springs / fountains (welling up from below). "Fountains" is accurate; "springs" more natural modern.
- **"windows of heaven" → "windows of the heavens"** (v11) — Hebrew אֲרֻבֹּת הַשָּׁמַיִם *arubbot ha-shamayim*. *Arubbot* = lattices / floodgates / sluices. Cosmology of the *raqia* dome (1:6) with waters above held back by the dome, now released through opened sluices. Traditional "windows" kept; "floodgates" captures it too.
- **"the waters prevailed" → "the waters grew strong"** (v18, 19, 24) — Hebrew וַיִּגְבְּרוּ *wa-yigberu* (from גָּבַר *gavar* = be strong / prevail). "Prevailed" accurate but archaic; "grew strong" more natural.
- **"high hills" → "high mountains"** (v19) — Hebrew הֶהָרִים הַגְּבֹהִים *he-harim ha-gevohim*. הַר *har* = mountain (or significant hill). In biblical geography "mountain" is the standard rendering.
- **"breath of life" → "breath of the spirit of life"** (v22) — Hebrew נִשְׁמַת־רוּחַ חַיִּים *nishmat-ruach chayyim*. Unique compound: *neshamah* (breath) + *ruach* (spirit / wind) + *chayyim* (life). More than the simple "breath of life" of 6:17. Preserved.
- **"and Noah only remained alive, and they that were with him in the ark" → "only Noah remained, and those with him in the ark"** (v23) — Hebrew אַךְ־נֹחַ *akh-Noach* = "only Noah." A restrictive: the single exception to universal destruction.

### Notes

- **Numbers in the flood narrative** — 7 pairs of clean animals + 2 of unclean + 7 days' warning + 40 days of rain + 150 days of waters prevailing. Numerology is deliberate; flood = cosmic un-creation reversed later by covenant.
- **"Yahweh shut him in" (v16)** — touching detail: Noah does not close the ark himself; Yahweh closes it. (Only instance in the narrative of direct divine action on the ark after commands were given.)
