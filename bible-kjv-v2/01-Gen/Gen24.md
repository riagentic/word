# Genesis 24

[1] And ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ was old, advanced in days, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had blessed ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ in everything.

[2] And ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ said to his slave, the elder of his house, who ruled over all that he had, "Put, please, your hand under my thigh,

[3] and I will make you swear by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of the heavens and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of the earth, that you will not take a wife for my son from the daughters of ⟦canaanites|the Kenaani|the Canaanite⟧, in whose midst I dwell,

[4] but to my land and to my kindred you shall go, and you shall take a wife for my son, for ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧."

[5] And the slave said to him, "Perhaps the woman will not be willing to go after me to this land. Shall I surely bring your son back to the land from which you came out?"

[6] And ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ said to him, "Take heed to yourself, lest you bring my son back there.

[7] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of the heavens, who took me from the house of my father and from the land of my kindred, and who spoke to me, and who swore to me, saying, 'To your seed I will give this land,' he will send his angel before you, and you shall take a wife for my son from there.

[8] And if the woman is not willing to go after you, then you will be clear from this oath of mine; only my son you shall not bring back there."

[9] And the slave put his hand under the thigh of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ his lord and swore to him concerning this matter.

[10] And the slave took ten camels from the camels of his lord and went, with all the good things of his lord in his hand. And he arose and went to ⟦aram-naharaim|Aram Naharayim|Mesopotamia⟧, to the city of ⟦nahor|Nachor|Nahor⟧.

[11] And he made the camels kneel outside the city at the well of water at the time of evening, at the time when the women who draw water come out.

[12] And he said, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my lord ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, make it happen before me today, please, and do ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ with my lord ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧.

[13] Behold, I am standing by the spring of water, and the daughters of the men of the city are coming out to draw water.

[14] And let it be that the girl to whom I say, 'Let down your jar, please, that I may drink,' and she says, 'Drink, and I will also give your camels to drink' — her you have appointed for your servant, for ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧; and by her I shall know that you have done ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ with my lord."

[15] And it came to pass, before he had finished speaking, that behold, ⟦rebekah|Rivqah|Rebekah⟧ was coming out, who was born to ⟦bethuel|Betuel|Bethuel⟧ the son of ⟦milcah|Milkah|Milcah⟧, the wife of ⟦nahor|Nachor|Nahor⟧ the brother of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, and her jar was on her shoulder.

[16] And the girl was very good in appearance, a virgin, and no man had known her. And she went down to the spring and filled her jar and came up.

[17] And the slave ran to meet her and said, "Let me sip, please, a little water from your jar."

[18] And she said, "Drink, my lord," and she hastened and let down her jar upon her hand and gave him to drink.

[19] And she finished giving him to drink, and she said, "For your camels also I will draw, until they have finished drinking."

[20] And she hastened and emptied her jar into the trough and ran again to the well to draw, and she drew for all his camels.

[21] And the man was gazing at her, keeping silent, to know whether ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had made his way prosper or not.

[22] And it came to pass, when the camels had finished drinking, that the man took a ring of gold, a ⟦beka|beqa|beka⟧ in weight, and two bracelets for her hands, ten of gold in weight,

[23] and he said, "Whose daughter are you? Tell me, please. Is there at the house of your father a place for us to lodge?"

[24] And she said to him, "I am the daughter of ⟦bethuel|Betuel|Bethuel⟧ the son of ⟦milcah|Milkah|Milcah⟧, whom she bore to ⟦nahor|Nachor|Nahor⟧."

[25] And she said to him, "Both straw and fodder are plentiful with us, and also a place to lodge."

[26] And the man bowed his head and bowed down to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[27] And he said, "Blessed is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my lord ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, who has not forsaken his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and his truth from my lord. As for me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has led me on the way to the house of the brothers of my lord."

[28] And the girl ran and told the house of her mother according to these words.

[29] And ⟦rebekah|Rivqah|Rebekah⟧ had a brother, and his name was ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧. And ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ ran to the man, outside, to the spring.

[30] And it came to pass, when he saw the ring, and the bracelets on the hands of his sister, and when he heard the words of ⟦rebekah|Rivqah|Rebekah⟧ his sister, saying, "Thus the man spoke to me," that he came to the man, and behold, he was standing by the camels at the spring.

[31] And he said, "Come, blessed of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧! Why do you stand outside, when I have cleared the house, and a place for the camels?"

[32] And the man came into the house, and he ungirded the camels and gave straw and fodder to the camels, and water to wash his feet and the feet of the men who were with him.

[33] And food was set before him to eat, and he said, "I will not eat until I have spoken my words." And he said, "Speak."

[34] And he said, "I am the slave of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧.

[35] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has blessed my lord greatly, and he has become great; and he has given him flock and herd, and silver and gold, and slaves and maidservants, and camels and donkeys.

[36] And Sarah, the wife of my lord, bore a son to my lord after she had grown old, and he has given to him all that he has.

[37] And my lord made me swear, saying, 'You shall not take a wife for my son from the daughters of ⟦canaanites|the Kenaani|the Canaanite⟧, in whose land I dwell,

[38] but to the house of my father you shall go, and to my clan, and you shall take a wife for my son.'

[39] And I said to my lord, 'Perhaps the woman will not go after me.'

[40] And he said to me, '⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧, before whom I have walked, will send his angel with you and will make your way prosper, and you shall take a wife for my son from my clan and from the house of my father.

[41] Then you will be clear from my oath, when you come to my clan; and if they do not give her to you, then you will be clear from my oath.'

[42] And I came today to the spring and said, '⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my lord ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, if you are, please, making my way prosper, on which I am going —

[43] behold, I am standing by the spring of water; and let it be that the young woman who comes out to draw, to whom I say, "Give me to drink, please, a little water from your jar,"

[44] and she says to me, "Both drink yourself, and for your camels also I will draw" — she is the woman whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has appointed for the son of my lord.'

[45] Before I had finished speaking to my heart, behold, ⟦rebekah|Rivqah|Rebekah⟧ was coming out, and her jar was on her shoulder, and she went down to the spring and drew. And I said to her, 'Give me to drink, please.'

[46] And she hastened and let down her jar from upon her and said, 'Drink, and I will also give your camels to drink.' And I drank, and she also gave the camels to drink.

[47] And I asked her and said, 'Whose daughter are you?' And she said, 'The daughter of ⟦bethuel|Betuel|Bethuel⟧ the son of ⟦nahor|Nachor|Nahor⟧, whom ⟦milcah|Milkah|Milcah⟧ bore to him.' And I put the ring on her nose and the bracelets on her hands.

[48] And I bowed my head and bowed down to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and I blessed ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my lord ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, who had led me in the way of truth to take the daughter of the brother of my lord for his son.

[49] And now, if you are doing ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and truth with my lord, tell me; and if not, tell me, that I may turn to the right hand or to the left."

[50] And ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ and ⟦bethuel|Betuel|Bethuel⟧ answered and said, "From ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the word has gone out; we cannot speak to you bad or good.

[51] Behold, ⟦rebekah|Rivqah|Rebekah⟧ is before you; take her and go, and let her be a wife to the son of your lord, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken."

[52] And it came to pass, when the slave of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ heard their words, that he bowed down to the earth to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[53] And the slave brought out articles of silver and articles of gold and garments and gave them to ⟦rebekah|Rivqah|Rebekah⟧, and precious things he gave to her brother and to her mother.

[54] And they ate and drank, he and the men who were with him, and they lodged. And they arose in the morning, and he said, "Send me away to my lord."

[55] And her brother and her mother said, "Let the girl dwell with us some days, or ten; afterward she shall go."

[56] And he said to them, "Do not delay me, when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has made my way prosper. Send me away, that I may go to my lord."

[57] And they said, "Let us call the girl and ask her mouth."

[58] And they called ⟦rebekah|Rivqah|Rebekah⟧ and said to her, "Will you go with this man?" And she said, "I will go."

[59] And they sent away ⟦rebekah|Rivqah|Rebekah⟧ their sister, and her nurse, and the slave of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ and his men.

[60] And they blessed ⟦rebekah|Rivqah|Rebekah⟧ and said to her,

"Our sister, may you become thousands of ten thousands,
and may your seed possess the gate of those who hate him."

[61] And ⟦rebekah|Rivqah|Rebekah⟧ arose, and her girls, and they rode on the camels and went after the man. And the slave took ⟦rebekah|Rivqah|Rebekah⟧ and went.

[62] And ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ had come from the entrance of ⟦beer-lahai-roi|Beer Lachai Roi|Beer-lahai-roi⟧, and he was dwelling in the land of the ⟦negev|Negev|Negeb⟧.

[63] And ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ went out to meditate in the field at the turning of evening, and he lifted up his eyes and saw, and behold, camels were coming.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"well stricken in age" → "advanced in days"** (v1) — Hebrew בָּא בַּיָּמִים, literally "gone into the days".
- **"his eldest servant of his house" → "his slave, the elder of his house"** (v2) — Hebrew עַבְדּוֹ זְקַן בֵּיתוֹ. עֶבֶד *eved* is "slave", and so he is called through the chapter (v5, 9, 10, 17, 34, 52, 53, 59, 61, 65, 66); he is not named. In v14 the same word is used of Isaac before Yahweh: "your servant".
- **"put, I pray thee, thy hand under my thigh" → "Put, please, your hand under my thigh"** (v2, 9) — Hebrew שִׂים־נָא יָדְךָ תַּחַת יְרֵכִי *sim-na yadkha tachat yerekhi*, word for word. The gesture goes with a solemn oath (again only in 47:29). The "thigh" is the place from which a man's children come (46:26, "who came out of his thigh"); the oath concerns Abraham's seed. More than this the text does not say.
- **"the Canaanites, among whom I dwell" → "the Canaanite, in whose midst I dwell"** (v3, 37) — Hebrew הַכְּנַעֲנִי, singular, for the whole people.
- **"unto my country" → "to my land"** (v4) — Hebrew אַרְצִי. The Hebrew ends "for my son, for Isaac".
- **"must I needs bring thy son again" → "Shall I surely bring your son back"** (v5) — Hebrew הֶהָשֵׁב אָשִׁיב, infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "bringing back shall I bring back".
- **"Beware thou" → "Take heed to yourself"** (v6) — Hebrew הִשָּׁמֶר לְךָ, from שָׁמַר "keep, guard".
- **"unto thy seed" kept** (v7, 60) — Hebrew זֶרַע *zera*.
- **"his angel"** (v7, 40) — Hebrew מַלְאָכוֹ; the word מַלְאָךְ *mal'akh* means "messenger".
- **"thou shalt be clear from this my oath" kept** (v8, 41) — Hebrew נָקָה "be clear, free of". In v8 the oath is שְׁבוּעָה; in v41 it is אָלָה, an oath that calls a curse on the one who breaks it.
- **"his master" → "his lord"** (v9, 10 and throughout) — Hebrew אָדוֹן *adon*, the same word as the address "my lord" (v18).
- **"for all the goods of his master were in his hand" → "with all the good things of his lord in his hand"** (v10) — Hebrew וְכָל־טוּב אֲדֹנָיו בְּיָדוֹ *we-khol-tuv adonav be-yado*, literally "and all the good of his lord in his hand": either all kinds of his lord's good things, which he took with him, or all his lord's goods, which were in his charge.
- **"Mesopotamia" → "Aram Naharayim"** (v10) — Hebrew אֲרַם נַהֲרַיִם *Aram Naharayim* = "Aram of the two rivers". KJV follows the Septuagint's Μεσοποταμία.
- **"the time that women go out to draw water" → "the time when the women who draw water come out"** (v11) — Hebrew הַשֹּׁאֲבֹת, "the (women) drawers"; "water" is supplied.
- **"send me good speed this day" → "make it happen before me today, please"** (v12) — Hebrew הַקְרֵה־נָא לְפָנַי הַיּוֹם, literally "cause to meet / to happen before me": let what I am looking for come my way. No object is named.
- **"shew kindness unto my master" → "do *chesed* with my lord"** (v12, 14, 49) — Hebrew עָשָׂה חֶסֶד עִם, literally "do *chesed* with". חֶסֶד *chesed* is loyal love shown to one to whom one is bound.
- **"well" / "spring"** (v11, 13, 16, 20, 29, 42) — two words in the Hebrew: בְּאֵר "well" (v11, 20) and עַיִן "spring" (v13, 16, 29, 30, 42, 43, 45), both of the same water.
- **"damsel" → "girl"; "virgin" kept; "the virgin" → "the young woman"** (v14, 16, 28, 43, 55, 57) — three Hebrew words. נַעֲרָה *na'arah* (v14, 16, 28, 55, 57): a girl, a young unmarried woman. בְּתוּלָה *betulah* (v16): a virgin; the verse adds "and no man had known her". עַלְמָה *almah* (v43, the slave's own retelling of v14): a young woman of marriageable age; the word itself does not say "virgin". All three are used of Rebekah. עַלְמָה is the word of Isa 7:14, where the Septuagint has παρθένος "virgin" (so Matt 1:23); here the Septuagint has παρθένος as well (cited from memory). KJV has "virgin" in v43.
- **"pitcher" → "jar"** (v14–20, 43–46) — Hebrew כַּד *kad*, an earthenware water-jar.
- **"let the same be she that thou hast appointed" → "her you have appointed"** (v14, 44) — Hebrew אֹתָהּ הֹכַחְתָּ: the verb הוֹכִיחַ is "decide, set right" (elsewhere "reprove"); "appointed" here. **"thereby shall I know" → "by her I shall know"** — Hebrew וּבָהּ, "by her" (or "by it").
- **"very fair to look upon" → "very good in appearance"** (v16) — Hebrew טֹבַת מַרְאֶה מְאֹד.
- **"Let me, I pray thee, drink a little water" → "Let me sip, please, a little water"** (v17) — Hebrew הַגְמִיאִינִי, a rare verb, "let me swallow / sip"; in v43, 45 the servant says הַשְׁקִינִי "give me to drink".
- **"she hasted" → "she hastened"** (v18, 20, 46) — Hebrew וַתְּמַהֵר, a verb of its own, not an adverb.
- **"wondering at her held his peace" → "was gazing at her, keeping silent"** (v21) — Hebrew מִשְׁתָּאֵה לָהּ מַחֲרִישׁ; the first word is found only here and its sense ("gaze", "wonder") is not certain. **"had made his journey prosperous" → "had made his way prosper"** (v21, 40, 42, 56) — Hebrew דֶּרֶךְ "way".
- **"a golden earring of half a shekel weight" → "a ring of gold, a beka in weight"** (v22, 30, 47) — Hebrew נֶזֶם *nezem* is a ring worn in the nose or the ear; v47 says it was put "on her nose". בֶּקַע *beqa* is a weight, half a shekel (Exod 38:26). **"of ten shekels weight of gold" → "ten of gold in weight"** — "shekels" is not in the Hebrew. "For her hands": the Hebrew says "hands" (v22, 30, 47).
- **"room" → "a place"** (v23, 25, 31) — Hebrew מָקוֹם.
- **"bowed down his head, and worshipped" → "bowed his head and bowed down"** (v26, 48) — Hebrew וַיִּקֹּד … וַיִּשְׁתַּחוּ; הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה is "bow down" (v52, "to the earth").
- **"Blessed be" → "Blessed is"** (v27) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ יְהוָה, with no verb.
- **"who hath not left destitute my master of his mercy and his truth" → "who has not forsaken his *chesed* and his truth from my lord"** (v27) — Hebrew לֹא־עָזַב חַסְדּוֹ וַאֲמִתּוֹ מֵעִם אֲדֹנִי, word for word ("has not left off … from with my lord"). אֱמֶת *emet* is "truth": what is firm and can be relied on, faithfulness. The pair חֶסֶד וֶאֱמֶת stands again in v49.
- **"I being in the way, the LORD led me" → "As for me, Yahweh has led me on the way"** (v27) — Hebrew אָנֹכִי בַּדֶּרֶךְ נָחַנִי יְהוָה, "I — on the way Yahweh led me". **"my master's brethren" → "the brothers of my lord"** — Hebrew אֲחֵי, plural: "brother" also of a kinsman.
- **"told them of her mother's house these things" → "told the house of her mother according to these words"** (v28) — Hebrew כַּדְּבָרִים הָאֵלֶּה.
- **"I have prepared the house" → "I have cleared the house"** (v31) — Hebrew פִּנִּיתִי, "turned out, made clear".
- **"he ungirded his camels" → "he ungirded the camels"** (v32) — the Hebrew names no subject for "ungirded" and "gave"; it may be Laban or the man.
- **"there was set meat before him" → "food was set before him"** (v33) — the written text (ketiv) is ויישם, the reading tradition (qere) וַיּוּשַׂם "and it was set"; the qere is translated. "Food" is supplied: the Hebrew has "it was set before him to eat". **"until I have told mine errand" → "until I have spoken my words"** — Hebrew דִּבַּרְתִּי דְּבָרָי.
- **"flocks, and herds … and menservants, and maidservants" → "flock and herd … and slaves and maidservants"** (v35) — Hebrew צֹאן וּבָקָר, עֲבָדִם וּשְׁפָחֹת.
- **"when she was old" → "after she had grown old"** (v36) — Hebrew אַחֲרֵי זִקְנָתָהּ, literally "after her old age".
- **"But thou shalt go" → "but … you shall go"** (v38) — Hebrew אִם־לֹא, literally "if not": after "you shall not" it means "but rather"; it is also the form of a sworn "surely".
- **"my kindred" → "my clan"** (v38, 40, 41) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּחָה, the kin group; in v4, 7 "kindred" is מוֹלֶדֶת. **"if they give not thee one" → "if they do not give her to you"** (v41) — "her" is supplied.
- **"if now thou do prosper my way" → "if you are, please, making my way prosper"** (v42) — Hebrew אִם־יֶשְׁךָ־נָּא מַצְלִיחַ; the sentence is not finished and runs on into v43–44.
- **"before I had done speaking in mine heart" → "… speaking to my heart"** (v45) — Hebrew אֶל־לִבִּי.
- **"from her shoulder" → "from upon her"** (v46) — Hebrew מֵעָלֶיהָ. **"I put the earring upon her face" → "I put the ring on her nose"** (v47) — Hebrew עַל־אַפָּהּ.
- **"in the right way" → "in the way of truth"** (v48) — Hebrew בְּדֶרֶךְ אֱמֶת, the word of v27.
- **"deal kindly and truly" → "are doing *chesed* and truth"** (v49) — Hebrew עֹשִׂים חֶסֶד וֶאֱמֶת, a participle.
- **"The thing proceedeth from the LORD" → "From Yahweh the word has gone out"** (v50) — Hebrew מֵיְהוָה יָצָא הַדָּבָר; דָּבָר is "word" or "matter". "Bad or good": רַע אוֹ־טוֹב, that is, anything at all.
- **"worshipped the LORD, bowing himself to the earth" → "bowed down to the earth to Yahweh"** (v52) — one verb in the Hebrew.
- **"jewels of silver, and jewels of gold, and raiment" → "articles of silver and articles of gold and garments"** (v53) — Hebrew כְּלֵי "vessels, articles"; מִגְדָּנֹת "precious things".
- **"tarried all night" → "lodged"** (v54) — Hebrew לוּן, as in v23, 25.
- **"Let the damsel abide with us a few days, at the least ten" → "Let the girl dwell with us some days, or ten"** (v55) — Hebrew יָמִים אוֹ עָשׂוֹר, literally "days or ten": the sense is not certain ("some days, or ten", "a year or ten months"). The last verb, תֵּלֵךְ, is "she shall go" or "you shall go".
- **"enquire at her mouth" → "ask her mouth"** (v57) — Hebrew וְנִשְׁאֲלָה אֶת־פִּיהָ: ask what she herself says.
- **"be thou the mother of thousands of millions" → "may you become thousands of ten thousands"** (v60) — Hebrew הֲיִי לְאַלְפֵי רְבָבָה; "mother" is not in the Hebrew. **"those which hate them" → "those who hate him"** — Hebrew שֹׂנְאָיו, singular "him", agreeing with "seed", as in 22:17.
- **"her damsels" → "her girls"** (v61) — Hebrew נַעֲרֹתֶיהָ, the word of v14, here of her attendants.
- **"came from the way of the well Lahai-roi" → "had come from the entrance of Beer Lachai Roi"** (v62) — Hebrew בָּא מִבּוֹא בְּאֵר לַחַי רֹאִי, literally "had come from the coming of Beer-lahai-roi": the words are hard and their sense is not certain ("from the approach to", "from going to"). The name is that of 16:14. **"the south country" → "the land of the Negev"** — Hebrew אֶרֶץ הַנֶּגֶב.
- **"to meditate" (v63)** — Hebrew לָשׂוּחַ *la-suach*, found only here; the meaning is not known ("meditate", "walk about", "lament" have all been proposed). "Meditate" is kept as the best-supported sense (the verb שִׂיחַ "muse, speak to oneself"). **"at the eventide" → "at the turning of evening"** — Hebrew לִפְנוֹת עָרֶב.
- **"she lighted off the camel" → "she alighted from the camel"** (v64) — Hebrew וַתִּפֹּל מֵעַל הַגָּמָל, literally "she fell from upon the camel": she got down quickly (the same of Naaman, 2 Kgs 5:21).
- **"told" → "recounted"** (v66) — Hebrew סִפֵּר.
- **"into his mother Sarah's tent" → "into the tent of Sarah his mother"** (v67) — Hebrew הָאֹהֱלָה שָׂרָה אִמּוֹ, literally "into the tent — Sarah his mother": "the tent" has the article and is not joined to the name in the usual way.
- **"was comforted after his mother's death" → "was comforted after his mother"** (v67) — Hebrew וַיִּנָּחֵם יִצְחָק אַחֲרֵי אִמּוֹ; "death" is not in the Hebrew.

### Notes

- **Longest chapter in Genesis** (67 verses). Careful narrative art; many details repeated (the slave retells the whole episode in vv34–49), suggesting the importance of careful witness in covenantal betrothal.
- **Rebekah's consent (v57–58)** — striking: when the household wants to delay, the slave insists; they appeal to Rebekah herself, and her "I will go" decides the matter. The text foregrounds her choice.
- **"Laban and Bethuel" (v50)** — Laban is named first, and elsewhere it is "her brother and her mother" who act (v53, 55); Laban (Rebekah's brother) manages the household.
- **The slave unnamed** — traditionally identified with Eliezer of Damascus (15:2). The narrative declines to name him — focusing on the office (Abraham's agent) rather than the man.
