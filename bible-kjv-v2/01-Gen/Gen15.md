# Genesis 15

[1] After these things the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧ in a vision, saying, "Do not fear, ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧; I am a shield to you; your reward is very great."

[2] And ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧ said, "⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧, what will you give me, and I go childless, and the heir of my house is Eliezer of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧?"

[3] And ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧ said, "Behold, to me you have given no seed, and behold, a son of my house is my heir."

[4] And behold, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to him, saying, "This one shall not be your heir; but he who will come out of your inward parts, he shall be your heir."

[5] And he brought him outside and said, "Look, please, toward the heavens and number the stars, if you are able to number them." And he said to him, "So shall your seed be."

[6] And he believed in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he reckoned it to him as righteousness.

[7] And he said to him, "I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who brought you out from Ur of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ to give you this land, to possess it."

[8] And he said, "⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧, by what shall I know that I shall possess it?"

[9] And he said to him, "Take for me a three-year-old heifer and a three-year-old she-goat and a three-year-old ram and a turtledove and a young bird."

[10] And he took for him all these and cut them in two in the middle, and put each half opposite its fellow; but the birds he did not cut in two.

[11] And the birds of prey came down upon the carcasses, and ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧ drove them away.

[12] And it came to pass, when the sun was about to go down, that a deep sleep fell upon ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧; and behold, a dread, a great darkness, was falling upon him.

[13] And he said to ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧, "Know surely that your seed will be a sojourner in a land not theirs, and they will serve them, and they will afflict them, four hundred years.

[14] And also the nation which they will serve I will judge; and afterward they will come out with great goods.

[15] And you, you will go to your fathers in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧; you will be buried in a good old age.

[16] And in the fourth generation they will return here, for the iniquity of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧ is not complete until now."

[17] And it came to pass, when the sun had gone down and there was thick darkness, that behold, a smoking oven and a torch of fire, which passed between these pieces.

[18] On that day ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ cut a covenant with ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧, saying, "To your seed I have given this land, from the river of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ as far as the great river, the river ⟦euphrates|Perat|Euphrates⟧:

[19] the ⟦kenites|Qeni|Kenite⟧ and the ⟦kenizzites|Qenizzi|Kenizzite⟧ and the ⟦kadmonites|Qadmoni|Kadmonite⟧,

[20] and the ⟦hittites|Chitti|Hittite⟧ and the ⟦perizzites|Perizzi|Perizzite⟧ and the ⟦rephaim|Refaim|Rephaim⟧,

[21] and the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧ and the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧ and the ⟦girgashites|Girgashi|Girgashite⟧ and the ⟦jebusites|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And" kept at the head of the clauses** (throughout) — Hebrew וַ / וְ.
- **"After these things" kept** (v1) — Hebrew אַחַר הַדְּבָרִים הָאֵלֶּה; דְּבָרִים is "words" and also "things, matters".
- **"the word of the LORD came unto Abram" → "the word of Yahweh came to Abram"** (v1, 4) — Hebrew הָיָה דְבַר־יְהוָה אֶל, literally "the word of Yahweh was to Abram"; the first time this expression stands in the Bible. "In a vision" is בַּמַּחֲזֶה, with the article.
- **"Fear not" → "Do not fear"** (v1) — Hebrew אַל־תִּירָא.
- **"I am thy shield, and thy exceeding great reward" → "I am a shield to you; your reward is very great"** (v1) — Hebrew אָנֹכִי מָגֵן לָךְ שְׂכָרְךָ הַרְבֵּה מְאֹד, word for word "I (am) a shield to you, your reward very much". The Hebrew has no verb in either half and allows two readings: (1) a new sentence, "your reward is (will be) very great"; (2) a second predicate of "I", "I am a shield to you, your very great reward". The verse has the first; the second is equally possible. מָגֵן "shield" is of one sound with מִגֵּן "delivered up" in 14:20; שָׂכָר is "wages, pay", here "reward".
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v2, 8) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יֱהוִה: two words, אֲדֹנָי *Adonai* ("my Lord") and the Tetragrammaton, both kept. The Masoretes pointed the name here with the vowels of *Elohim*, so that it is read "Adonai Elohim" and not "Adonai Adonai"; hence KJV "Lord GOD".
- **"seeing I go childless" → "and I go childless"** (v2) — Hebrew וְאָנֹכִי הוֹלֵךְ עֲרִירִי, "and I am going childless": "going" through life, or going away (to death).
- **"the steward of my house is this Eliezer of Damascus" → "the heir of my house is Eliezer of Damascus"** (v2) — Hebrew וּבֶן־מֶשֶׁק בֵּיתִי הוּא דַּמֶּשֶׂק אֱלִיעֶזֶר, literally "and the son of *mesheq* of my house, he is Damascus Eliezer". מֶשֶׁק is found only here and its meaning is not known ("possession"? hence "heir"; others "steward"); it sounds like דַּמֶּשֶׂק *Dammeseq*. "Heir" is taken from the parallel in v3 and is uncertain. "Damascus Eliezer" stands without "of"; the sentence is difficult in Hebrew.
- **"seed" kept** (v3, 5, 13, 18) — Hebrew זֶרַע, singular, as 12:7; in v13 the singular "a sojourner" and the plural "theirs … them" stand side by side.
- **"one born in my house is mine heir" → "a son of my house is my heir"** (v3) — Hebrew בֶן־בֵּיתִי יוֹרֵשׁ אֹתִי, literally "a son of my house is inheriting me": one who belongs to the household. "Slave" is not in the Hebrew. יָרַשׁ is "be heir" here and in v4, "possess" in v7, 8: one verb.
- **"saying" kept** (v1, 4, 18) — Hebrew לֵאמֹר.
- **"out of thine own bowels" → "out of your inward parts"** (v4) — Hebrew מִמֵּעֶיךָ, the inner organs as the place from which a child comes; "own" and "son" are not in the Hebrew.
- **"Look now" → "Look, please"** (v5) — Hebrew הַבֶּט־נָא.
- **"tell the stars" → "number the stars"** (v5) — Hebrew סָפַר, twice; a different verb from "count" in 13:16 (מָנָה).
- **"he believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness" → "he believed in Yahweh, and he reckoned it to him as righteousness"** (v6) — Hebrew וְהֶאֱמִן בַּיהוָה וַיַּחְשְׁבֶהָ לּוֹ צְדָקָה, word for word "and he believed in Yahweh, and he reckoned it to him righteousness". הֶאֱמִין (hiphil of אמן "be firm", the root of "amen") is "believe, trust"; with בְּ "believe in". The verb form is a perfect with "and", not the usual narrative form: it may mark a lasting state ("and he kept believing"). חָשַׁב is "reckon, count"; "it" is a feminine pronoun (his believing). Neither subject is named: by the context Abram believed and Yahweh reckoned. "As" is supplied: the Hebrew sets "righteousness" beside "it" without a preposition. Cited in Rom 4:3; Gal 3:6; Jas 2:23.
- **"to inherit it" → "to possess it"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew לְרִשְׁתָּהּ, אִירָשֶׁנָּה.
- **"whereby shall I know" → "by what shall I know"** (v8) — Hebrew בַּמָּה אֵדַע.
- **"Take me an heifer of three years old" → "Take for me a three-year-old heifer"** (v9) — Hebrew קְחָה לִי, "take for me" (not "bring"). מְשֻׁלֶּשֶׁת / מְשֻׁלָּשׁ is literally "tripled": "three years old" is the usual sense; "threefold" (three of each) is also possible.
- **"a young pigeon" → "a young bird"** (v9) — Hebrew גּוֹזָל, the young of a bird (Deut 32:11); the kind is not named.
- **"divided them in the midst, and laid each piece one against another" → "cut them in two in the middle, and put each half opposite its fellow"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיְבַתֵּר … בִּתְרוֹ … בָתָר, one root three times: "cut in two", "its half"; אִישׁ … רֵעֵהוּ, literally "a man his half toward his neighbor". "The birds" is הַצִּפֹּר, singular for the kind. "He took for him" (וַיִּקַּח־לוֹ) answers "take for me" in v9.
- **"when the fowls came down" → "the birds of prey came down"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיֵּרֶד הָעַיִט, a plain "and … came down", not "when".
- **"when the sun was going down" → "when the sun was about to go down"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיְהִי הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ לָבוֹא, literally "the sun was to go in".
- **"an horror of great darkness" → "a dread, a great darkness"** (v12) — Hebrew אֵימָה חֲשֵׁכָה גְדֹלָה, two nouns side by side; "was falling" is a participle (נֹפֶלֶת).
- **"Know of a surety" → "Know surely"** (v13) — Hebrew יָדֹעַ תֵּדַע, the infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "knowing you shall know".
- **"a stranger" → "a sojourner"** (v13) — Hebrew גֵּר.
- **"shall serve them; and they shall afflict them" kept** (v13) — Hebrew וַעֲבָדוּם וְעִנּוּ אֹתָם; the Hebrew does not name the two subjects (the seed will serve the people of that land, and these will afflict the seed).
- **"I will judge"** (v14) — Hebrew דָּן אָנֹכִי, a participle, "I am judging" (about to judge); דִּין, not שָׁפַט.
- **"great substance" → "great goods"** (v14) — Hebrew רְכֻשׁ, as 12:5.
- **"in peace"** (v15) — Hebrew בְּשָׁלוֹם.
- **"a good old age" kept** (v15) — Hebrew בְּשֵׂיבָה טוֹבָה, literally "in good gray hair".
- **"in the fourth generation they shall come hither again" → "in the fourth generation they will return here"** (v16) — Hebrew וְדוֹר רְבִיעִי יָשׁוּבוּ הֵנָּה, literally "and a fourth generation, they will return here"; "in" is supplied.
- **"not yet full" → "not complete until now"** (v16) — Hebrew לֹא־שָׁלֵם … עַד־הֵנָּה; "the Amorite" is singular for the people.
- **"when the sun went down, and it was dark" → "when the sun had gone down and there was thick darkness"** (v17) — Hebrew עֲלָטָה, a rare word for deep darkness.
- **"a smoking furnace, and a burning lamp" → "a smoking oven and a torch of fire"** (v17) — Hebrew תַנּוּר עָשָׁן וְלַפִּיד אֵשׁ; תַּנּוּר is a baking oven, לַפִּיד a torch. "Pieces" is גְּזָרִים, a different word from "half" in v10.
- **"made a covenant" → "cut a covenant"** (v18) — Hebrew כָּרַת … בְּרִית, the usual Hebrew expression, literally "cut"; here it follows the cutting of the animals.
- **"have I given" kept** (v18) — Hebrew נָתַתִּי, a perfect: "I have given" (12:7 and 13:15 have "I will give").
- **"river of Egypt"** (v18) — Hebrew נְהַר מִצְרַיִם, with נָהָר "river", not נַחַל "wadi" (the "wadi of Egypt" of Num 34:5).
- **"the land of" not supplied; singular names kept** (v19–21) — the Hebrew continues "this land" with ten names, each marked as an object (אֶת) and all but "the Rephaim" in the singular: "the Kenite and the Kenizzite …".

### Notes

- **The covenant rite (v9–17)** — animals are cut in two and the halves laid opposite each other; the oven and the torch pass between the pieces. Jer 34:18–19 speaks of men who "cut" a covenant and passed between the parts of a calf. Here Abram does not pass through.
- **v6 in the New Testament** — Rom 4:3, Gal 3:6 and Jas 2:23 cite the verse in the Greek form "Abraham believed God, and it was reckoned to him for righteousness" (passive, with "God").
- **Four hundred years (v13)** — Exod 12:40 gives four hundred and thirty years.
- **Ten peoples (v19–21)** — the longest such list; others (Exod 3:8; Deut 7:1; Josh 3:10) have six or seven names.
