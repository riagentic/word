# Genesis 4

[1] And the man knew ⟦eve|Chavvah|Eve⟧ his wife, and she conceived and bore ⟦cain|Qayin|Cain⟧, and she said, "I have gotten a man with ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[2] And again she bore his brother, ⟦abel|Hevel|Abel⟧. And ⟦abel|Hevel|Abel⟧ was a shepherd of the flock, and ⟦cain|Qayin|Cain⟧ was a worker of the ground.

[3] And it came to pass at the end of days that ⟦cain|Qayin|Cain⟧ brought of the fruit of the ground an offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[4] And ⟦abel|Hevel|Abel⟧, he also brought of the firstborn of his flock and of their fat. And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ looked to ⟦abel|Hevel|Abel⟧ and to his offering,

[5] but to ⟦cain|Qayin|Cain⟧ and to his offering he did not look. And ⟦cain|Qayin|Cain⟧ burned greatly with anger, and his face fell.

[6] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦cain|Qayin|Cain⟧, "Why do you burn with anger, and why has your face fallen?

[7] If you do well, is there not a lifting up? And if you do not do well, sin is lying at the entrance, and toward you is its desire, and you shall rule over it."

[8] And ⟦cain|Qayin|Cain⟧ said to ⟦abel|Hevel|Abel⟧ his brother — and it came to pass, when they were in the field, that ⟦cain|Qayin|Cain⟧ rose up against ⟦abel|Hevel|Abel⟧ his brother and killed him.

[9] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦cain|Qayin|Cain⟧, "Where is ⟦abel|Hevel|Abel⟧ your brother?" And he said, "I do not know; am I my brother's keeper?"

[10] And he said, "What have you done? The voice of your brother's blood is crying out to me from the ground.

[11] And now cursed are you from the ground, which has opened its mouth to take your brother's blood from your hand.

[12] When you work the ground, it shall no more give its strength to you; restless and a wanderer you shall be on the earth."

[13] And ⟦cain|Qayin|Cain⟧ said to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "My iniquity is too great to bear.

[14] Behold, you have driven me out today from the face of the ground, and from your face I shall be hidden, and I shall be restless and a wanderer on the earth, and it shall come to pass that whoever finds me will kill me."

[15] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to him, "Therefore, whoever kills ⟦cain|Qayin|Cain⟧, it shall be avenged sevenfold." And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ put a sign for ⟦cain|Qayin|Cain⟧, so that whoever found him would not strike him.

[16] And ⟦cain|Qayin|Cain⟧ went out from before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and dwelt in the land of Nod, east of Eden.

[17] And ⟦cain|Qayin|Cain⟧ knew his wife, and she conceived and bore ⟦enoch|Chanoch|Enoch⟧; and he was building a city, and he called the name of the city after the name of his son, ⟦enoch|Chanoch|Enoch⟧.

[18] And to ⟦enoch|Chanoch|Enoch⟧ was born Irad, and Irad begot ⟦mehujael|Mechuyael|Mehujael⟧, and ⟦mehujael|Mechuyael|Mehujael⟧ begot ⟦methushael|Metushael|Methushael⟧, and ⟦methushael|Metushael|Methushael⟧ begot ⟦lamech|Lemech|Lamech⟧.

[19] And ⟦lamech|Lemech|Lamech⟧ took for himself two wives; the name of the one was Adah, and the name of the second ⟦zillah|Tzillah|Zillah⟧.

[20] And Adah bore ⟦jabal|Yaval|Jabal⟧; he was the father of those who dwell in tents and with livestock.

[21] And the name of his brother was ⟦jubal|Yuval|Jubal⟧; he was the father of all who handle the lyre and the pipe.

[22] And ⟦zillah|Tzillah|Zillah⟧, she also bore ⟦tubal-cain|Tuval-Qayin|Tubal-cain⟧, a forger of every cutting tool of bronze and iron; and the sister of ⟦tubal-cain|Tuval-Qayin|Tubal-cain⟧ was Naamah.

[23] And ⟦lamech|Lemech|Lamech⟧ said to his wives,

"Adah and ⟦zillah|Tzillah|Zillah⟧, hear my voice;

[24] If ⟦cain|Qayin|Cain⟧ is avenged sevenfold,

[25] And Adam knew his wife again, and she bore a son and called his name ⟦seth|Shet|Seth⟧: "For ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has set for me another seed in place of ⟦abel|Hevel|Abel⟧, for ⟦cain|Qayin|Cain⟧ killed him."

[26] And to ⟦seth|Shet|Seth⟧, to him also a son was born, and he called his name Enosh. Then it was begun to call on the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"the LORD" → "Yahweh"; "God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout; v25) — the names as the Hebrew has them. From here יְהוָה stands alone.
- **"Adam knew Eve his wife" → "the man knew *Chavvah* his wife"** (v1) — Hebrew וְהָאָדָם with the article; in v25 אָדָם has none and is the name "Adam". "Knew" (יָדַע) is kept. The word order (subject before the verb) may also mean "had known".
- **"I have gotten a man from the LORD" → "I have gotten a man with Yahweh"** (v1) — Hebrew קָנִיתִי אִישׁ אֶת־יְהוָה. קָנָה is "get, acquire" (also "buy"; of God in 14:19 some read "create"); it sounds like the name קַיִן *Qayin*. אֶת־יְהוָה is left as it stands: אֶת is either the preposition "with" (so the verse; "with the help of" would be an addition) or the mark of the object, which would give "I have gotten a man, Yahweh". The Hebrew allows both. אִישׁ "man" is said of the newborn son.
- **"she again bare" kept; "Abel"** (v2) — Hebrew וַתֹּסֶף לָלֶדֶת, literally "she added to bear". הֶבֶל *Hevel* is the word "breath, vapor". צֹאן "flock" is sheep and goats.
- **"a tiller of the ground" → "a worker of the ground"** (v2) — Hebrew עֹבֵד אֲדָמָה, the verb of 2:5, 15; 3:23.
- **"in process of time" → "at the end of days"** (v3) — Hebrew מִקֵּץ יָמִים, literally so: after a time whose length is not given.
- **"an offering" kept** (v3, 4, 5) — Hebrew מִנְחָה, a gift brought to a superior; the same word for Qayin's fruit and Hevel's animals.
- **"the firstlings of his flock and of the fat thereof" → "the firstborn of his flock and of their fat"** (v4) — Hebrew מִבְּכֹרוֹת צֹאנוֹ וּמֵחֶלְבֵהֶן.
- **"had respect unto" → "looked to"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew שָׁעָה אֶל, "look at, regard". That the look was favorable follows from the story, not from the word.
- **"Cain was very wroth" → "Qayin burned greatly with anger"** (v5, 6) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר לְקַיִן מְאֹד, literally "it burned to Qayin greatly"; the word "anger" is not written.
- **"shalt thou not be accepted?" → "is there not a lifting up?"** (v7) — Hebrew הֲלוֹא אִם־תֵּיטִיב שְׂאֵת, literally "Is it not: if you do well, lifting up". The Hebrew of the verse is hard and is kept as it stands. שְׂאֵת is the infinitive of נָשָׂא "lift, bear": a lifting up of the face (the opposite of the fallen face of v5–6: acceptance, or a glad face), or a bearing away, that is, forgiveness (the same verb in v13).
- **"sin lieth at the door" → "sin is lying at the entrance"** (v7) — Hebrew לַפֶּתַח חַטָּאת רֹבֵץ. רָבַץ is said of an animal lying down. חַטָּאת "sin" is feminine but the participle is masculine; so some take רֹבֵץ as a noun, "sin is a lurker at the entrance". חַטָּאת can also be "sin offering".
- **"unto thee shall be his desire, and thou shalt rule over him" → "toward you is its desire, and you shall rule over it"** (v7) — Hebrew וְאֵלֶיךָ תְּשׁוּקָתוֹ וְאַתָּה תִּמְשָׁל־בּוֹ, the words of 3:16 with the same rendering. The suffixes are masculine ("his / its"): they point to the one lying at the entrance (so the verse); KJV's "his … him" allows Hevel. תִּמְשָׁל is an imperfect: "you shall rule", also "you must rule" or "you may rule".
- **"Cain talked with Abel his brother" → "Qayin said to Hevel his brother —"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר קַיִן אֶל־הֶבֶל אָחִיו, "And Qayin said to Hevel his brother", and nothing that he said follows. The sentence is left unfinished as in the Masoretic Text. The Samaritan text, the Septuagint, the Syriac and the Vulgate have here "Let us go out to the field" (from memory); these words are not in the Hebrew text translated.
- **"slew" → "killed"** (v8, 14, 15, 23, 25) — Hebrew הָרַג.
- **"the voice of thy brother's blood crieth" → "the voice of your brother's blood is crying out"** (v10) — Hebrew קוֹל דְּמֵי אָחִיךָ צֹעֲקִים: "blood" is plural ("bloods", blood that has been shed) and so is "crying". קוֹל may also be a call, "Listen! your brother's blood is crying".
- **"cursed from the earth" → "cursed are you from the ground"** (v11) — Hebrew מִן־הָאֲדָמָה: away from the ground, or from the ground as the source of the curse. אֲדָמָה "ground" (v2, 3, 10, 11, 12, 14) is kept apart from אֶרֶץ "earth" (v12, 14, 16 "land").
- **"to receive" → "to take"** (v11) — Hebrew לָקַחַת.
- **"it shall not henceforth yield unto thee her strength" → "it shall no more give its strength to you"** (v12) — Hebrew לֹא־תֹסֵף תֵּת, literally "it shall not add to give".
- **"a fugitive and a vagabond" → "restless and a wanderer"** (v12, 14) — Hebrew נָע וָנָד, two participles: נוּעַ "sway, be unsteady, roam" and נוּד "wander"; the second is the root of "Nod" in v16.
- **"My punishment is greater than I can bear" → "My iniquity is too great to bear"** (v13) — Hebrew גָּדוֹל עֲוֹנִי מִנְּשֹׂא. עָוֹן is "iniquity", the guilt and what it brings. נְשֹׂא is "to bear" (I cannot carry it) or "to take away, forgive" (it is too great to be forgiven); no subject is named.
- **"Behold" kept; "every one that findeth me shall slay me" → "it shall come to pass that whoever finds me will kill me"** (v14) — Hebrew הֵן; וְהָיָה.
- **"Therefore" kept** (v15) — Hebrew לָכֵן, the Masoretic Text. The Septuagint, the Syriac and the Vulgate read "Not so" (לֹא כֵן; from memory).
- **"vengeance shall be taken on him sevenfold" → "it shall be avenged sevenfold"** (v15) — Hebrew שִׁבְעָתַיִם יֻקָּם, a passive with no subject named: the killing (so the verse), or Qayin ("he shall be avenged", as v24).
- **"set a mark upon Cain, lest any finding him should kill him" → "put a sign for Qayin, so that whoever found him would not strike him"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיָּשֶׂם … לְקַיִן אוֹת: אוֹת is "sign"; לְ is "for" (also "on"). הַכּוֹת is "strike" (here: strike down).
- **"from the presence of the LORD" → "from before Yahweh"** (v16) — Hebrew מִלִּפְנֵי. "Dwelt" is יָשַׁב; קִדְמַת־עֵדֶן is "in front of Eden", on the east.
- **"he builded a city" → "he was building a city"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיְהִי בֹּנֶה, with a participle. "He" may be Qayin or Chanoch.
- **"begat" → "begot"** (v18) — Hebrew יָלַד, here of the father. The second name is written in two ways in the Hebrew verse, מְחוּיָאֵל and מְחִיּיָאֵל; the verse has one form.
- **"the father of such as dwell in tents, and of such as have cattle" → "the father of those who dwell in tents and with livestock"** (v20) — Hebrew אֲבִי יֹשֵׁב אֹהֶל וּמִקְנֶה, literally "the father of the dweller of tent and livestock"; "with" is supplied. מִקְנֶה is livestock as property.
- **"all such as handle the harp and organ" → "all who handle the lyre and the pipe"** (v21) — Hebrew כִּנּוֹר, a stringed instrument held in the hand, and עוּגָב, a wind instrument whose exact kind is not certain.
- **"an instructer of every artificer in brass and iron" → "a forger of every cutting tool of bronze and iron"** (v22) — Hebrew לֹטֵשׁ כָּל־חֹרֵשׁ נְחֹשֶׁת וּבַרְזֶל, hard Hebrew: לָטַשׁ is "hammer, sharpen"; חֹרֵשׁ is "one who cuts or works (metal)", a craftsman or a tool. So also "a sharpener of every worker in bronze and iron". נְחֹשֶׁת is copper or bronze, not brass.
- **"hearken unto my speech" → "give ear to my saying"** (v23) — Hebrew הַאְזֵנָּה אִמְרָתִי.
- **"I have slain a man to my wounding, and a young man to my hurt" → "a man I have killed for my wound, and a child for my stripe"** (v23) — Hebrew לְפִצְעִי, לְחַבֻּרָתִי: for a wound and a stripe given to me. יֶלֶד is "child", here probably a youth. The perfect may also be a boast of what he would do ("I would kill").
- **"If Cain shall be avenged sevenfold, truly Lamech seventy and sevenfold" → "If Qayin is avenged sevenfold, then Lemech seventy-seven"** (v24) — Hebrew כִּי "if" (or "for"); שִׁבְעִים וְשִׁבְעָה is the plain number "seventy and seven", without "-fold".
- **"hath appointed me another seed instead of Abel, whom Cain slew" → "has set for me another seed in place of Hevel, for Qayin killed him"** (v25) — Hebrew שָׁת, the verb of 3:15, which sounds like the name שֵׁת *Shet*; זֶרַע "seed" as 3:15; the last clause begins with כִּי "for". The Hebrew has no "she said".
- **"then began men to call upon the name of the LORD" → "Then it was begun to call on the name of Yahweh"** (v26) — Hebrew אָז הוּחַל לִקְרֹא בְּשֵׁם יְהוָה, a passive with no subject: who began is not said. אֱנוֹשׁ *Enosh* is a word for "man" (mortal man).

### Notes

- **v8** — the gap after "said" is the reading of the Masoretic Text; the versions' words are given in Changes only.
- **v17–22** — the line of Qayin; several names are close to names in the line of Shet in ch 5 (Chanoch, Lemech).
- **v23–24** — Lemech's words are verse; the lines follow the Hebrew accents. Matt 18:22 uses the number of v24.
