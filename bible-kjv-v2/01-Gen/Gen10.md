## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"generations"** (v1, 32) — Hebrew תּוֹלְדֹת *toledot*, the heading formula of Genesis (6:9).
- **Lists** (throughout) — the names are joined by "and" as in the Hebrew (in v4 in two pairs).
- **"Dodanim"** (v4) — the Hebrew text printed here (the Masoretic Text) has דֹדָנִים *Dodanim*. The parallel list in 1 Chr 1:7 has רוֹדָנִים *Rodanim* (ד and ר are easily confused). The traditional form of the name in this verse is the Masoretic one; the other form shown is that of 1 Chr 1:7.
- **"isles of the Gentiles" → "coastlands of the nations"** (v5) — Hebrew אִיֵּי הַגּוֹיִם. אִי = coast or island, land reached by sea; גּוֹיִם = nations.
- **"divided" → "were parted"** (v5, 32) — Hebrew נִפְרְדוּ, nifal of פָּרַד "part, separate"; another word than "divided" in v25. "In their lands" is בְּאַרְצֹתָם.
- **"every one after his tongue" → "each man after his tongue"** (v5) — Hebrew אִישׁ לִלְשֹׁנוֹ. לָשׁוֹן is "tongue" (v20, 31).
- **"families" → "clans"** (v5, 18, 20, 31, 32) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּחָה *mishpachah*, the kin group larger than a household.
- **"Mizraim"** (v6, 13) — Hebrew מִצְרַיִם *Mitsrayim*, the Hebrew name of Egypt, here as a son of Ham.
- **"Phut" → "Put"** (v6) — Hebrew פּוּט.
- **"begat" → "begot"** (v8, 13, 15, 24, 26) — Hebrew יָלַד.
- **"he began to be a mighty one in the earth" kept** (v8) — Hebrew הוּא הֵחֵל לִהְיוֹת גִּבֹּר בָּאָרֶץ. גִּבֹּר is the word of 6:4. הֵחֵל is "began" (as 6:1; 9:20).
- **"a mighty hunter" → "mighty in hunting"** (v9) — Hebrew גִּבֹּר־צַיִד, literally "a mighty one of hunting" (or "of game"), twice.
- **"before the LORD" → "before Yahweh"** (v9) — Hebrew לִפְנֵי יְהוָה, "before the face of Yahweh". The Hebrew does not say whether this is praise or blame.
- **"went forth Asshur, and builded" → "he went out to Assyria, and built"** (v11) — Hebrew מִן־הָאָרֶץ הַהִוא יָצָא אַשּׁוּר. Two readings, and the Hebrew allows both:
  1. "From that land Asshur went out" (Asshur as the subject, the son of Shem in v22; so KJV). This is the plainer word order; no word for "to" stands in the Hebrew.
  2. "From that land he (Nimrod) went out to Asshur" (Asshur as the land, Assyria; cf. Mic 5:6, where "the land of Nimrod" stands beside Assyria).
  The verse follows reading 2, which continues "the beginning of his kingdom" in v10.
- **"the city Rehoboth" → "Rehoboth-Ir"** (v11) — Hebrew רְחֹבֹת עִיר, literally "broad places of a city"; taken as a name. It can also be read "the broad places of the city" (of Nineveh).
- **"the same is a great city" → "that is the great city"** (v12) — Hebrew הִוא הָעִיר הַגְּדֹלָה; which city is meant is not said.
- **"out of whom came Philistim" → "from where the Philistines went out"** (v14) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר יָצְאוּ מִשָּׁם פְּלִשְׁתִּים, literally "from there". The clause stands after Casluhim in the Hebrew and is left there (Amos 9:7 and Jer 47:4 connect the Philistines with Caphtor). The names in v13–14 have no article in the Hebrew.
- **"the Jebusite, and the Amorite …" → "the Jebusites and the Amorites …"** (v16–19) — the Hebrew has the singular with the article each time (הַיְבוּסִי, "the Jebusite"), a name for the whole people.
- **"were … spread abroad" → "were scattered"** (v18) — Hebrew נָפֹצוּ, the verb of 9:19 and 11:4, 8, 9.
- **"as thou comest to" kept** (v19, 30) — Hebrew בֹּאֲכָה, literally "your coming": in the direction of.
- **"tongues"** (v20, 31) — Hebrew לְשֹׁנֹת.
- **"Unto Shem also … even to him were children born" → "And to Shem, to him also sons were born"** (v21) — Hebrew וּלְשֵׁם יֻלַּד גַּם־הוּא, literally "and to Shem there was born, also he"; the Hebrew names no one born, and "sons" is supplied (as v1, 25).
- **"the children of Eber" → "the sons of Eber"** (v21) — Hebrew בְּנֵי־עֵבֶר.
- **"the brother of Japheth the elder" → "the brother of Japheth, the elder"** (v21) — Hebrew אֲחִי יֶפֶת הַגָּדוֹל, literally "the brother of Japheth, the great one". "The elder" can be Shem ("the elder brother of Japheth", the usual construction) or Japheth; the verse keeps the Hebrew order.
- **"Arphaxad" → "Arpachshad"** (v22, 24) — Hebrew אַרְפַּכְשַׁד.
- **"Salah" → "Shelah"** (v24) — Hebrew שֶׁלַח.
- **"Peleg … was the earth divided"** (v25) — Hebrew פֶּלֶג *Peleg*, from פלג "divide": "in his days the earth was divided" (נִפְלְגָה). What division is meant is not said.
- **"their dwelling"** (v30) — Hebrew מוֹשָׁבָם, from יָשַׁב "dwell". "The mountain of the east": הַר הַקֶּדֶם, a mountain or a hill country.
- **"after their nations"** (v31) — Hebrew לְגוֹיֵהֶם; v5, 20, 32 have בְּגוֹיֵהֶם "in their nations".

### Notes

- **The Table of Nations (Gen 10)** — peoples and lands of the world known to ancient Israel, set out as the sons of Noah's three sons. Some names stand in two lines (Sheba and Havilah under Cush in v7 and under Joktan in v28–29; Asshur in v11 and v22).
- **Relation to ch 11 (Babel)** — the tongues of v5, 20, 31 stand here before the account of 11:1–9, where all the earth has "one lip". The text does not say how the two are ordered in time.
- **Nimrod (v8–12)** — Hebrew נִמְרֹד; the name is sometimes connected with מָרַד *marad* ("to rebel"). The text itself says only what v8–12 say.
