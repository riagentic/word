# Genesis 9

[1] *Elohim* blessed Noah and his sons and said to them, "Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth.

[2] The fear of you and the dread of you shall be on every wild animal of the earth and on every bird of the heavens — on everything that moves on the ground and all the fish of the sea. Into your hand they are given.

[3] Every moving thing that lives shall be food for you; just as I gave you the green plants, I give you everything.

[4] But flesh with its life — that is, its blood — you shall not eat.

[5] And for your own lifeblood I will require an accounting: from every animal I will require it, and from each human; from each person's brother I will require the life of a human.

[6] Whoever sheds human blood, by a human shall his blood be shed; for in the image of *Elohim* he made humankind.

[7] And you, be fruitful and multiply; swarm on the earth and multiply in it."

[8] *Elohim* said to Noah and to his sons with him,

[9] "And I, behold, I am establishing my covenant with you and with your offspring after you,

[10] and with every living being that is with you — birds, livestock, and every wild animal of the earth with you — all that came out of the ark, every wild animal of the earth.

[11] I establish my covenant with you: never again shall all flesh be cut off by the waters of a flood, and never again shall there be a flood to destroy the earth."

[12] And *Elohim* said, "This is the sign of the covenant that I am making between me and you and every living being that is with you, for generations forever:

[13] I have set my bow in the cloud, and it shall be a sign of the covenant between me and the earth.

[14] When I bring a cloud over the earth, the bow shall be seen in the cloud;

[15] and I will remember my covenant between me and you and every living being of all flesh, and the waters shall never again become a flood to destroy all flesh.

[16] The bow shall be in the cloud, and I will look upon it to remember the everlasting covenant between *Elohim* and every living being of all flesh that is on the earth."

[17] And *Elohim* said to Noah, "This is the sign of the covenant that I have established between me and all flesh that is on the earth."

[18] The sons of Noah who went out of the ark were Shem, Ham, and Japheth; and Ham was the father of Canaan.

[19] These three were the sons of Noah, and from them the whole earth was populated.

[20] Noah, a man of the ground, was the first to plant a vineyard.

[21] He drank some of the wine and became drunk, and he lay uncovered in his tent.

[22] Ham, the father of Canaan, saw the nakedness of his father and told his two brothers outside.

[23] Then Shem and Japheth took a garment, laid it on both their shoulders, and walked in backward and covered the nakedness of their father; their faces were turned away, and they did not see their father's nakedness.

[24] When Noah awoke from his wine and knew what his youngest son had done to him,

[25] he said,

"Cursed be Canaan;
a servant of servants shall he be to his brothers."

[26] And he said,

"Blessed be Yahweh, *Elohim* of Shem;
and let Canaan be his servant.

[27] May *Elohim* enlarge Japheth,
and let him dwell in the tents of Shem;
and let Canaan be his servant."

[28] Noah lived after the flood 350 years.

[29] All the days of Noah were 950 years; and he died.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"replenish" → "fill"** (v1) — same false-friend as 1:28. Hebrew מָלֵא *male* = fill. "Replenish" in modern English = refill, which wrongly implies a prior population.
- **"fowl of the air" → "bird of the heavens"** (v2) — as in Gen 1.
- **"meat" → "food"** (v3) — same false-friend as 1:29.
- **"the life thereof, which is the blood thereof" → "its life — that is, its blood"** (v4) — Hebrew בְּנַפְשׁוֹ דָמוֹ *be-naphsho damo*. *Nephesh* (life / soul / animating principle) identified with blood. Foundational for Levitical blood-prohibition (Lev 17:11). Clarified.
- **"your blood of your lives will I require" → "for your own lifeblood I will require an accounting"** (v5) — Hebrew אֶת־דִּמְכֶם לְנַפְשֹׁתֵיכֶם אֶדְרֹשׁ *et-dimkhem le-naphshotekhem edrosh*. *Darash* = seek / inquire / require a reckoning. A legal "I will demand an accounting" — human blood is protected; its shedder will be called to account.
- **"whoso sheddeth man's blood, by man shall his blood be shed" → "whoever sheds human blood, by a human shall his blood be shed"** (v6) — Hebrew שֹׁפֵךְ דַּם הָאָדָם בָּאָדָם דָּמוֹ יִשָּׁפֵךְ. Tight chiastic Hebrew (A-B-B-A: sheds-blood-human; human-blood-shed). Preposition בָּאָדָם *ba-adam* could mean "by a human" (agent) or "for a human" (in exchange) or "on account of a human." "By a human" is standard; the structure is poetic.
- **"in the image of God made he man" → "in the image of *Elohim* he made humankind"** (v6) — Hebrew כִּי בְּצֶלֶם אֱלֹהִים עָשָׂה אֶת־הָאָדָם. Echo of 1:27. Humanity's divine image is the ground for the prohibition of murder.
- **"bring forth abundantly" → "swarm"** (v7) — Hebrew שִׁרְצוּ *shirtsu*, same root as 1:20 (*sheretz*). Creation-language of proliferation.
- **"seed" → "offspring"** (v9) — same option as 3:15; "offspring" more natural modern.
- **"creature" → "being"** (v10, 12, 15, 16) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה *nephesh chayyah*. Consistency with Gen 1–2.
- **"token" → "sign"** (v12, 13, 17) — Hebrew אוֹת *ot*. Same word as the "mark" on Cain (4:15). Standard word for a covenant sign / pledge.
- **"for perpetual generations" → "for generations forever"** (v12) — Hebrew לְדֹרֹת עוֹלָם *le-dorot olam*. Modernized.
- **"my bow" — rainbow** (v13) — Hebrew קֶשֶׁת *qeshet* = bow. In the Hebrew Bible *qeshet* is the warrior's bow (the rainbow is not called by a separate word). The poetic imagery: Yahweh hangs up his battle-bow — the warrior-God sets aside his weapon against the earth. Significant theological image often missed.
- **"overspread" → "populated"** (v19) — Hebrew נָפְצָה *naphtsah* = scattered / spread abroad. Anticipates Babel (ch 11) where the verb reappears.
- **"began to be an husbandman" → "a man of the ground, was the first to plant a vineyard"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיָּחֶל נֹחַ אִישׁ הָאֲדָמָה וַיִּטַּע כָּרֶם. Hebrew is ambiguous between:
  1. "Noah, a man of the ground (*ish ha-adamah*), was the first to plant a vineyard."
  2. "Noah began to be a man of the ground / farmer; he planted a vineyard."
  Either reading works. The first preserves the *adam / adamah* language and introduces Noah's new post-flood vocation.
- **"his younger son" → "his youngest son"** (v24) — Hebrew בְּנוֹ הַקָּטָן *beno ha-qatan*. *Qatan* = small / young. Here superlatively the youngest. Creates a puzzle: Noah's sons are usually listed Shem-Ham-Japheth, with Ham as middle. Either Ham is in fact the youngest (listing order by precedence rather than age), or *qatan* is comparative ("younger" — relative to Shem). Translation "youngest" is the more natural Hebrew.
- **v25 "Cursed be Canaan" — why not Ham?** — the narrative says Ham committed the offense, but Noah curses Canaan (Ham's son). This oddity has been variously explained: (a) Canaan was the actual perpetrator and v22 is introductory; (b) the curse is retrojective, reflecting Israel's later justification for displacing the Canaanites; (c) Ham is cursed *through* his son Canaan, since cursing Ham directly would undo *Elohim*'s prior blessing of 9:1. Text does not explain. Translation kept literal.
- **v26 "Yahweh, *Elohim* of Shem"** — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵי שֵׁם *barukh Yahweh Elohei Shem*. Unique construction: "Yahweh, *Elohim* of Shem" (not "Blessed be Yahweh, the God of Shem, bless him"). Shem's distinctive: Yahweh is specifically his God — a foreshadowing of the covenant-line narrowing through Shem → Abraham.
- **v27 "enlarge Japheth" — wordplay preserved** — Hebrew יַפְתְּ אֱלֹהִים לְיֶפֶת *yapht Elohim le-Yaphet*. *Yaphet* (Japheth) = name; *yapht* (from פָּתָה *patah*, "make wide / open / enlarge") = verb. The name and verb are the same sounds. Given here.

### Notes

- **Noahic covenant (v8–17)** — the first explicit covenant in the Bible, universal in scope (with all flesh, not only humans), unilateral (Yahweh establishes, no human obligation), and marked by a natural sign (the rainbow). Contrast the later Abrahamic and Sinaitic covenants.
- **"Ham's offense" (v22–25)** — "saw his father's nakedness." Two readings: (a) voyeuristic irreverence — he looked, did not cover, and told; (b) a stronger sexual idiom (in Levitical law, "to uncover one's father's nakedness" = to have relations with the father's wife, one's own mother; Lev 18:7, 20:11). The text's reserve is probably deliberate; the canonical weight of the curse suggests a graver offense than merely seeing.
- **Curse of Canaan — historical misuse** — this passage was weaponized in centuries of slavery apologetics ("curse of Ham" applied to Africans). The text names *Canaan* (Mediterranean-coast peoples displaced by Israel), not Ham generally, and says nothing about skin color. The racial misreading is a later distortion, not the text.
