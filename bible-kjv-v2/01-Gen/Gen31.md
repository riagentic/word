# Genesis 31

[1] And he heard the words of the sons of ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧, saying, "⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ has taken all that was our father's, and from what was our father's he has made all this wealth."

[2] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ saw the face of ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧, and behold, he was not with him as yesterday and the day before.

[3] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, "Return to the land of your fathers and to your kindred, and I will be with you."

[4] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ sent and called Rachel and Leah to the field, to his flock,

[5] and he said to them, "I see the face of your father, that he is not toward me as yesterday and the day before; but the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my father has been with me.

[6] And you yourselves know that with all my strength I have served your father.

[7] And your father has cheated me and changed my wages ten times; but ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ did not allow him to do evil to me.

[8] If he said thus, 'The speckled shall be your wages,' then all the flock bore speckled; and if he said thus, 'The striped shall be your wages,' then all the flock bore striped.

[9] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has taken away the livestock of your father and given it to me.

[10] And it came to pass at the time when the flock was in heat, that I lifted up my eyes and saw in a dream, and behold, the he-goats that went up on the flock were striped, speckled, and mottled.

[11] And the angel of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said to me in the dream, '⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧.' And I said, 'Here I am.'

[12] And he said, 'Lift up your eyes, please, and see: all the he-goats that go up on the flock are striped, speckled, and mottled; for I have seen all that ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ is doing to you.

[13] I am the ⟦el|El|God⟧ of ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧, where you anointed a pillar, where you vowed a vow to me. Now arise, go out from this land and return to the land of your kindred.'"

[14] And Rachel and Leah answered and said to him, "Is there still a portion and an inheritance for us in the house of our father?

[15] Are we not reckoned by him as foreigners? For he has sold us, and he has also surely eaten up our money.

[16] For all the riches that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has taken away from our father, it is ours and our sons'. And now, all that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has said to you, do."

[17] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ arose and lifted his sons and his wives onto the camels.

[18] And he drove all his livestock and all his goods that he had acquired, the livestock of his property that he had acquired in ⟦paddan-aram|Paddan Aram|Paddan-Aram⟧, to come to ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ his father, to the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧.

[19] And ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ had gone to shear his flock, and Rachel stole the ⟦teraphim|teraphim|household gods⟧ that were her father's.

[20] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ stole the heart of ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ the ⟦aramean|Arammi|Aramean⟧, in that he did not tell him that he was fleeing.

[21] And he fled, he and all that was his; and he arose and passed over the River, and set his face toward the hill country of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧.

[22] And it was told to ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ on the third day that ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ had fled.

[23] And he took his brothers with him and pursued after him a way of seven days, and he caught up with him in the hill country of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧.

[24] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ came to ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ the ⟦aramean|Arammi|Aramean⟧ in a dream of the night and said to him, "Take heed to yourself, lest you speak with ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ either good or evil."

[25] And ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ overtook ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧. And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ had pitched his tent in the hill country, and ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ pitched with his brothers in the hill country of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧.

[26] And ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ said to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, "What have you done, that you have stolen my heart and driven away my daughters like captives of the sword?

[27] Why did you hide yourself to flee, and deceive me, and not tell me? And I would have sent you away with joy and with songs, with timbrel and with lyre.

[28] And you did not let me kiss my sons and my daughters. Now you have acted foolishly in doing this.

[29] It is in the power of my hand to do evil to you; but the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of your father said to me last night, saying, 'Take heed to yourself not to speak with ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ either good or evil.'

[30] And now, you have surely gone because you surely longed for the house of your father. Why have you stolen my gods?"

[31] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ answered and said to ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧, "Because I was afraid, for I said, 'Lest you tear your daughters away from me.'

[32] With whomever you find your gods, he shall not live. Before our brothers, recognize for yourself what is with me and take it for yourself." And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ did not know that Rachel had stolen them.

[33] And ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ came into the tent of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ and into the tent of Leah and into the tent of the two slave women, and he did not find them. And he went out from the tent of Leah and came into the tent of Rachel.

[34] And Rachel had taken the ⟦teraphim|teraphim|household gods⟧ and put them in the saddle of the camel and sat upon them. And ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ felt through all the tent and did not find them.

[35] And she said to her father, "Let not anger burn in the eyes of my lord that I am not able to rise before you, for the way of women is upon me." And he searched and did not find the ⟦teraphim|teraphim|household gods⟧.

[36] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ burned with anger and contended with ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧. And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ answered and said to ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧, "What is my transgression? What is my sin, that you have hotly pursued after me?

[37] For you have felt through all my goods: what have you found of all the goods of your house? Set it here before my brothers and your brothers, and let them decide between us two.

[38] These twenty years I have been with you. Your ewes and your she-goats have not miscarried, and the rams of your flock I have not eaten.

[39] What was torn I did not bring to you; I myself bore the loss of it. From my hand you sought it, stolen by day and stolen by night.

[40] I was — by day the heat consumed me, and the frost by night; and my sleep fled from my eyes.

[41] These twenty years I have been in your house. I served you fourteen years for your two daughters and six years for your flock, and you changed my wages ten times.

[42] Unless the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my father, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ and the Fear of ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧, had been for me, surely now you would have sent me away empty. My affliction and the labor of my palms ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has seen, and he decided last night."

[43] And ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ answered and said to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, "The daughters are my daughters, and the sons are my sons, and the flock is my flock, and all that you see, it is mine. And for my daughters, what can I do for these today, or for their sons whom they have borne?

[44] And now, come, let us cut a covenant, I and you, and let it be for a witness between me and you."

[45] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ took a stone and raised it up as a pillar.

[46] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ said to his brothers, "Gather stones." And they took stones and made a heap, and they ate there upon the heap.

[47] And ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ called it ⟦jegar-sahadutha|Yegar Sahaduta|Jegar-sahadutha⟧, and ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ called it ⟦galeed|Galed|Galeed⟧.

[48] And ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ said, "This heap is a witness between me and you today." Therefore he called its name ⟦galeed|Galed|Galeed⟧,

[49] and the ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧, because he said, "May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ watch between me and you, when we are hidden each man from his neighbor.

[50] If you afflict my daughters, and if you take wives besides my daughters — no man is with us; see, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is witness between me and you."

[51] And ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ said to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, "Behold this heap, and behold the pillar that I have set up between me and you.

[52] This heap is a witness, and the pillar is a witness, that I will not pass over this heap to you, and that you will not pass over this heap and this pillar to me, for evil.

[53] May the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ and the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦nahor|Nachor|Nahor⟧ judge between us — the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of their father." And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ swore by the Fear of his father ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧.

[54] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ sacrificed a sacrifice in the hill country and called his brothers to eat bread; and they ate bread and lodged in the hill country.

[55] And ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ rose early in the morning and kissed his sons and his daughters and blessed them; and ⟦laban|Lavan|Laban⟧ went and returned to his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And" kept at the head of the clauses** (throughout) — Hebrew וַ / וְ; the chain of clauses is not turned into "so", "then", "but" or subordinate clauses.
- **"And he heard"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁמַע has no named subject; "Jacob" is not in the verse.
- **"glory" → "wealth"** (v1) — Hebrew כָּבוֹד *kavod*, elsewhere "glory"; its root sense is "weight", and here it is what Jacob has "made" out of Laban's property: riches. A different sense of the one word.
- **"countenance" → "face"; "it was not toward him" → "he was not with him"** (v2, 5) — Hebrew פָּנִים *panim* "face" is plural in form, while אֵינֶנּוּ "he is not" is singular: it points to Laban himself. v2 עִמּוֹ "with him", v5 אֵלַי "toward me".
- **"as before" → "as yesterday and the day before"** (v2, 5) — Hebrew כִּתְמוֹל שִׁלְשׁוֹם, literally "as yesterday, the third day": the Hebrew way of saying "as formerly".
- **"deceived me" → "cheated me"** (v7) — Hebrew הֵתֶל בִּי *hetel bi*, "mock, trifle with, cheat"; a different word from "deceive" (רִמָּה, 29:25) and from "steal the heart" (v20). "Ten times" (v7, 41) is the Hebrew number, עֲשֶׂרֶת מֹנִים.
- **"suffered him not to hurt me" → "did not allow him to do evil to me"** (v7) — Hebrew לֹא־נְתָנוֹ … לְהָרַע עִמָּדִי, literally "did not give him to do evil with me"; so also v29 "to do evil to you", v52 "for evil" (רַע / רָעָה).
- **"said thus"** (v8) — Hebrew כֹּה "thus" restored, twice.
- **"taken away"** (v9, 16) — Hebrew הִצִּיל, elsewhere "deliver" (32:11); its root sense is "snatch away", which is the sense here.
- **"the cattle conceived" → "the flock was in heat"** (v10) — Hebrew יַחֵם הַצֹּאן, "be hot"; צֹאן is the flock of sheep and goats. "Went up on" is the literal הָעֹלִים עַל (KJV "leaped upon").
- **"grisled" → "mottled"** (v10, 12) — Hebrew בְּרֻדִּים *berudim* = dappled / mottled. "Grisled" is archaic.
- **"angel of God" → "angel of *Elohim*"** (v11) — Hebrew מַלְאַךְ הָאֱלֹהִים; the word מַלְאָךְ means "messenger" (so 32:3, 6 of Jacob's men); for the messenger of *Elohim* / of Yahweh one rendering, "angel", is kept in the whole Old Testament.
- **"Here am I" → "Here I am"** (v11) — Hebrew הִנֵּנִי, literally "behold me".
- **"Lift up now" → "Lift up …, please"** (v12) — Hebrew נָא.
- **"God of Beth-el" → "El of Bethel"** (v13) — Hebrew הָאֵל בֵּית־אֵל *ha-El Beit-El*, literally "the El Beth-El"; the Hebrew has no "of" and no verb between the two. The Septuagint is commonly cited here for a longer reading ("who appeared to you in …", from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"vowedst a vow"** (v13) — Hebrew נָדַרְתָּ … נֶדֶר, verb and noun kept. "A pillar": מַצֵּבָה without the article.
- **"strangers" → "foreigners"** (v15) — Hebrew נָכְרִיּוֹת *nokhriyyot*, foreign women (distinct from *ger*, sojourner).
- **"hath quite devoured also our money" → "has also surely eaten up our money"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיֹּאכַל גַּם־אָכוֹל אֶת־כַּסְפֵּנוּ, literally "and he has eaten, also eating, our silver": the infinitive absolute ("surely") and כֶּסֶף "silver", here the price paid for them (Jacob's fourteen years, v41). "Sold us": מְכָרָנוּ.
- **"children" → "sons"** (v16, 43) — Hebrew בָּנֵינוּ, בָּנִים, בְּנֵיהֶן.
- **"all his goods … the livestock of his property"** (v18) — Hebrew רְכוּשׁ "goods", רָכַשׁ "acquire" (twice), מִקְנֵה קִנְיָנוֹ "the livestock of his property (what he had got)".
- **"images" → "teraphim"** (v19, 34, 35) — Hebrew תְּרָפִים, kept as the Hebrew word in all three verses; what exactly they were is not known (household images; Laban calls them "my gods", v30).
- **"stole away unawares to Laban" → "stole the heart of Laban"** (v20, 26) — Hebrew וַיִּגְנֹב … אֶת־לֵב לָבָן, literally so; the meaning is "deceived, outwitted", not "won his love". The verb is the one used of Rachel (v19, 32), of the gods (v30) and of the stolen animals (v39).
- **"steal away from me" → "deceive me"** (v27) — Hebrew וַתִּגְנֹב אֹתִי, literally "and you stole me", short for "stole my heart" (v26); the literal words say something else in English, so the meaning is given.
- **"Syrian" → "Aramean"** (v20, 24) — as in 25:20.
- **"passed over the river" → "passed over the River"** (v21) — Hebrew הַנָּהָר *ha-nahar* = the River, the Euphrates; capitalized. "Pass over" is עָבַר, as in v52.
- **"mount Gilead" → "the hill country of Gilead"** (v21, 23, 25, 54) — Hebrew הַר "mountain", here of a region; the one word is also "in the hill country" (בָּהָר, v25, 54).
- **"brethren" → "brothers"** (v23, 25, 32, 37, 46, 54) — Hebrew אַחִים *achim*, here kinsmen, the men of the clan; the Hebrew word is kept.
- **"seven days' journey" → "a way of seven days"** (v23) — Hebrew דֶּרֶךְ שִׁבְעַת יָמִים; דֶּרֶךְ is "way", the same word as "the way of women" (v35).
- **"overtook" → "caught up with"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיַּדְבֵּק "clung to, came close upon"; "overtook" in v25 is another verb, וַיַּשֵּׂג.
- **"either good or bad" → "either good or evil"** (v24, 29) — Hebrew מִטּוֹב עַד־רָע, literally "from good to evil": anything at all.
- **"carried away" → "driven away"** (v26) — Hebrew וַתְּנַהֵג, the verb of v18 ("he drove all his livestock").
- **"tabret and harp" → "timbrel and lyre"** (v27) — Hebrew תֹף *toph*, hand-drum; כִּנּוֹר *kinnor*, lyre (as in 4:21).
- **"thou hast now done foolishly in so doing"** (v28) — Hebrew הִסְכַּלְתָּ עֲשׂוֹ, "you have been foolish to do".
- **"to do you hurt" → "to do evil to you"; "your father"** (v29) — "you" and "your" are plural here (עִמָּכֶם, אֲבִיכֶם): Jacob and his household. יֶשׁ־לְאֵל יָדִי is literally "there is to the power of my hand"; אֵל here is "power".
- **"thou wouldest needs be gone … thou sore longedst" → "you have surely gone … you surely longed"** (v30) — two infinitives absolute, הָלֹךְ הָלַכְתָּ, נִכְסֹף נִכְסַפְתָּה, literally "going you have gone", "longing you longed".
- **"take by force" → "tear away"** (v31) — Hebrew תִּגְזֹל, "seize, rob". The Hebrew gives Jacob's thought as direct speech: "for I said, Lest …".
- **"discern thou what is thine with me" → "recognize for yourself what is with me"** (v32) — Hebrew הַכֶּר־לְךָ מָה עִמָּדִי; "what is yours" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"found them not"** (v33, 34) — Hebrew וְלֹא מָצָא, "and he did not find", with no object; "them" is supplied for English.
- **"maidservants" → "slave women"** (v33) — Hebrew אֲמָהֹת.
- **"camel's furniture" → "the saddle of the camel"** (v34) — Hebrew כַּר הַגָּמָל; כַּר in this sense is found only here, probably a saddle with a basket or cushion; the exact meaning is uncertain.
- **"Let it not displease my lord" → "Let not anger burn in the eyes of my lord"** (v35) — Hebrew אַל־יִחַר בְּעֵינֵי אֲדֹנִי, literally "let it not burn in the eyes of my lord".
- **"custom of women" → "way of women"** (v35) — Hebrew דֶּרֶךְ נָשִׁים *derekh nashim*, the monthly flow.
- **"was wroth, and chode with" → "burned with anger and contended with"** (v36) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר לְיַעֲקֹב, literally "it burned to Jacob"; וַיָּרֶב, "contend" (רִיב). "Hotly pursued": דָּלַקְתָּ, literally "burned after".
- **"trespass" → "transgression"** (v36) — Hebrew פֶּשַׁע.
- **"judge betwixt us both" → "decide between us two"; "rebuked thee yesternight" → "decided last night"** (v37, 42) — Hebrew הוֹכִיחַ both times: Jacob asks the brothers to decide, and says that Elohim has already decided. v42 has no object ("thee" is not in the Hebrew).
- **"I bare the loss of it"** (v39) — Hebrew אֲחַטֶּנָּה, from חטא "miss, sin": "I made good the miss". The verbs of v39 tell what used to happen. "Of beasts" (KJV) is not in the Hebrew: טְרֵפָה is "a torn one".
- **"Thus I was" → "I was —"** (v40) — Hebrew הָיִיתִי alone. "Consumed" is אָכַל "eat", the verb of v15 and v38. חֹרֶב is dry heat.
- **"Except … had been with me" → "Unless … had been for me"** (v42) — Hebrew הָיָה לִי, "had been mine / for me".
- **"Fear of Isaac"** (v42, 53) — Hebrew פַּחַד יִצְחָק *Pachad Yitzchaq*, a name for the *Elohim* of Isaac found only here: the one Isaac dreads. פַּחַד is "dread, terror", a stronger word than the usual "fear" (יִרְאָה). Some read *pachad* as "kinsman" ("the Kinsman of Isaac"); the plain sense of the word is kept.
- **"labour of my hands" → "the labor of my palms"** (v42) — Hebrew יְגִיעַ כַּפַּי: כַּף "palm", not יָד "hand".
- **"make a covenant" → "cut a covenant"** (v44) — Hebrew נִכְרְתָה בְרִית. The Hebrew order "I and you" is kept.
- **"upon the heap"** (v46) — Hebrew עַל־הַגָּל, "on" or "by" the heap.
- **"Jegar-sahadutha … Galeed"** (v47) — Laban's name is Aramaic, יְגַר שָׂהֲדוּתָא, the first two Aramaic words in the Bible; Jacob's is Hebrew, גַּלְעֵד. Both mean "heap of witness". The two words are kept as they are.
- **"was the name of it called" → "he called its name"** (v48) — Hebrew קָרָא־שְׁמוֹ, active, with no named subject ("one called").
- **"and Mizpah" → "and the Mizpah"** (v49) — Hebrew וְהַמִּצְפָּה, with the article: "the watch-post", from the verb of the next clause, יִצֶף "may he watch". The Hebrew has no explanation of the name in the verse.
- **"when we are absent one from another" → "when we are hidden each man from his neighbor"** (v49) — Hebrew נִסָּתֵר אִישׁ מֵרֵעֵהוּ.
- **"other wives beside" → "wives besides"** (v50) — Hebrew נָשִׁים עַל־בְּנֹתַי; "other" is not in the Hebrew. אֵין אִישׁ עִמָּנוּ, "no man is with us", stands alone, without "though". "Afflict": עִנָּה.
- **"I have cast" → "I have set up"** (v51) — Hebrew יָרִיתִי, "I have thrown, cast up"; Laban says it of himself, though Jacob raised the pillar (v45).
- **"that I will not pass over"** (v52) — Hebrew אִם־אָנִי לֹא־אֶעֱבֹר … וְאִם־אַתָּה לֹא־תַעֲבֹר, the oath form "if I … / if you …"; the meaning is given.
- **"judge betwixt us" → "May … judge between us"** (v53) — Hebrew יִשְׁפְּטוּ is a **plural** verb, a wish: "may they judge". Laban names the *Elohim* of Abraham and the *Elohim* of Nahor as two, and Jacob swears by neither name but by the Fear of Isaac. "The *Elohim* of their father" stands after the verb in Hebrew, as here; it is commonly said to be missing in the Septuagint (from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"offered sacrifice" → "sacrificed a sacrifice"; "tarried all night" → "lodged"** (v54) — Hebrew וַיִּזְבַּח … זֶבַח; וַיָּלִינוּ.
- **"sons and daughters"** (v55) — Hebrew לְבָנָיו וְלִבְנוֹתָיו, "his sons and his daughters"; the sons are his daughters' sons (v43 "the sons are my sons"). The Hebrew word is kept.

### Notes

- **Verse numbers** — v55 is 32:1 in the Hebrew Bible, so that in chapter 32 the Hebrew numbers are one higher than ours.
- **Rachel and the *teraphim*** — her motives are not stated. Ancient Near East parallels from Nuzi suggest possession of the household gods signified inheritance right — Rachel may be laying claim to her father's estate for her and her children (coinciding with her complaint v14–16). Alternatively: she may retain personal devotion to these household gods. The text leaves both readings open.
