# Genesis 2

[1] And the heavens and the earth were finished, and all their host.

[2] And on the seventh day ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ finished his work that he had made, and he ceased on the seventh day from all his work that he had made.

[3] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ blessed the seventh day and sanctified it, because on it he ceased from all his work that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had created to make.

[4] These are the generations of the heavens and the earth when they were created, in the day that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ made earth and heavens.

[5] And no shrub of the field was yet in the earth, and no plant of the field had yet sprung up, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had not caused it to rain on the earth, and there was no man to work the ground.

[6] And a stream would go up from the earth and water the whole face of the ground.

[7] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ formed the man, dust from the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life, and the man became a living soul.

[8] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ planted a garden in Eden, in the east, and there he put the man whom he had formed.

[9] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ caused to spring up from the ground every tree desirable in appearance and good for food, and the tree of life in the midst of the garden, and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.

[10] And a river was going out from Eden to water the garden, and from there it was parted and became four heads.

[11] The name of the first is Pishon; it is the one that goes around all the land of ⟦havilah|Chavilah|Havilah⟧, where there is gold;

[12] and the gold of that land is good; bdellium is there, and the onyx stone.

[13] And the name of the second river is ⟦gihon|Gichon|Gihon⟧; it is the one that goes around all the land of ⟦cush|Kush|Cush⟧.

[14] And the name of the third river is ⟦tigris|Chiddeqel|Tigris⟧; it is the one that goes east of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧. And the fourth river is the ⟦euphrates|Perat|Euphrates⟧.

[15] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ took the man and put him in the garden of Eden to work it and to keep it.

[16] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ commanded the man, saying, "Of every tree of the garden you may surely eat,

[17] but of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, you shall not eat of it, for in the day that you eat of it you shall surely die."

[18] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, "It is not good for the man to be alone; I will make for him a helper corresponding to him."

[19] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ formed from the ground every living thing of the field and every bird of the heavens, and brought them to the man to see what he would call them; and whatever the man called a living soul, that was its name.

[20] And the man gave names to all the beasts and to the birds of the heavens and to every living thing of the field; but for Adam he did not find a helper corresponding to him.

[21] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ caused a deep sleep to fall on the man, and he slept; and he took one of his ribs and closed up flesh in its place.

[22] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ built the rib that he had taken from the man into a woman, and brought her to the man.

[23] And the man said,

"This one, this time, is bone of my bones and flesh of my flesh;
this one shall be called Woman,
for from Man this one was taken."

[24] Therefore a man leaves his father and his mother and clings to his wife, and they become one flesh.

[25] And the two of them were naked, the man and his wife, and they were not ashamed.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 3) — as in ch 1.
- **"the LORD God" → "Yahweh *Elohim*"** (v4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 15, 16, 18, 19, 21, 22) — Hebrew יְהוָה אֱלֹהִים, both words every time. The name יהוה stands here for the first time. KJV "LORD" follows the custom of reading *Adonai* ("Lord") in its place.
- **"host" kept** (v1) — Hebrew צָבָא, an army, an ordered multitude; the word in "Yahweh of hosts".
- **"ended his work which he had made; and he rested" → "finished his work that he had made, and he ceased"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew כִּלָּה "finish" (v1 the passive of the same verb) and שָׁבַת "cease, stop", the root of שַׁבָּת "Sabbath". "Rest" is another word (נוּחַ, Exod 20:11). The Hebrew word order in v2 is "And *Elohim* finished on the seventh day". The Masoretic Text has "on the seventh day"; the Samaritan text and the Septuagint have "on the sixth" (from memory).
- **"which God created and made" → "that *Elohim* had created to make"** (v3) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־בָּרָא אֱלֹהִים לַעֲשׂוֹת, literally "which *Elohim* created to make": the two verbs of ch 1 together. The infinitive may say how he created ("which *Elohim* created by making it") or to what end. The name *Elohim* stands twice in the verse.
- **"sanctified" kept** (v3) — Hebrew קִדֵּשׁ, "make holy, set apart".
- **"the generations" kept** (v4) — Hebrew תּוֹלְדוֹת, from יָלַד "bear, beget": what is begotten of someone, his line and its story; the heading of each part of Genesis (5:1; 6:9; 10:1 …). Here it is said of the heavens and the earth.
- **"in the day that" kept** (v4, 17) — Hebrew בְּיוֹם with an infinitive, "in the day of Yahweh *Elohim*'s making"; "earth and heavens" have no article and stand in this order.
- **"every plant of the field before it was in the earth" → "no shrub of the field was yet in the earth"** (v5) — Hebrew וְכֹל שִׂיחַ הַשָּׂדֶה טֶרֶם יִהְיֶה בָאָרֶץ, literally "and every shrub of the field was not yet in the earth". The two clauses may also be tied to v4 or to v7 ("when no shrub … was yet …"); the Hebrew sets them side by side with "and".
- **"to till the ground" → "to work the ground"** (v5, also v15; 3:23; 4:2, 12) — Hebrew עָבַד, "serve, work", the verb of עֶבֶד "slave".
- **"there was not a man" → "there was no man"** (v5) — Hebrew אָדָם without the article: no human being. In v7–25 it has the article, הָאָדָם "the man". The word sounds like אֲדָמָה "ground" (v5, 7): the man is formed from the ground.
- **"a mist" → "a stream"** (v6) — Hebrew אֵד, found only here and in Job 36:27. The meaning is uncertain: water welling up from under the earth (a flow, a flood), or a mist. The verbs ("would go up … and water") describe what kept happening.
- **"of the dust of the ground" → "dust from the ground"** (v7) — Hebrew עָפָר מִן־הָאֲדָמָה, set beside "the man" without a preposition: "formed the man, dust from the ground".
- **"the breath of life" kept; "a living soul" kept** (v7) — Hebrew נִשְׁמַת חַיִּים: נְשָׁמָה is "breath", a different word from רוּחַ (1:2). נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה "a living soul" is the same pair of words that is said of the animals in 1:20, 21, 24, 30 and in v19 below: the man does not receive a soul, he becomes one, as they are. "Nostrils" is אַפַּיִם.
- **"eastward in Eden" → "in Eden, in the east"** (v8) — Hebrew מִקֶּדֶם, literally "from the front", that is, on the east side; also possible "from the east" or "of old". "Put" in v8 is שִׂים; in v15 it is another verb, הִנִּיחַ, literally "caused him to rest".
- **"made to grow" → "caused to spring up"** (v9) — Hebrew הִצְמִיחַ, the causative of the verb in v5.
- **"pleasant to the sight" → "desirable in appearance"** (v9) — Hebrew נֶחְמָד לְמַרְאֶה: נֶחְמָד is "desired, desirable" (the verb "covet"); the same word in 3:6.
- **"good and evil" kept** (v9, 17) — Hebrew טוֹב וָרָע. רַע is "evil" and "bad" in one word. "The tree of the knowledge of good and evil" is literally "the tree of the knowing good and evil".
- **"went out … was parted, and became into four heads" → "was going out … was parted and became four heads"** (v10) — Hebrew participle and imperfects: the river is described, in the past or as still flowing ("goes out … is parted"). רָאשִׁים is "heads": the beginnings of four rivers.
- **"compasseth" → "goes around"** (v11, 13) — Hebrew הַסֹּבֵב, "going around". In v11 "Havilah" has the article in Hebrew.
- **"bdellium and the onyx stone" kept** (v12) — Hebrew בְּדֹלַח and שֹׁהַם: what substance and what stone are meant is not certain.
- **"Ethiopia" → "Cush"; "Hiddekel" / "Assyria" / "Euphrates"** (v13, 14) — Hebrew כּוּשׁ, חִדֶּקֶל, אַשּׁוּר, פְּרָת: the names as the Hebrew has them, in the tokens. קִדְמַת אַשּׁוּר is "in front of Asshur", that is, east of it.
- **"to dress it and to keep it" → "to work it and to keep it"** (v15) — Hebrew לְעָבְדָהּ וּלְשָׁמְרָהּ. The suffixes are feminine, though "garden" is masculine elsewhere.
- **"thou mayest freely eat" → "you may surely eat"; "thou shalt surely die" kept** (v16, 17) — Hebrew אָכֹל תֹּאכֵל and מוֹת תָּמוּת, literally "eating you shall eat" and "dying you shall die": the infinitive before its verb gives it weight, rendered "surely" both times. "You" is singular.
- **"an help meet for him" → "a helper corresponding to him"** (v18, 20) — Hebrew עֵזֶר כְּנֶגְדּוֹ. עֵזֶר is "help, helper", a masculine noun used elsewhere mostly of God as the help of his people (Exod 18:4; Deut 33:7; Ps 33:20); it says nothing of rank. כְּנֶגְדּוֹ is literally "as in front of him", "as his counterpart": one who matches him. KJV "meet" meant "fitting".
- **"every beast of the field" → "every living thing of the field"; "cattle" → "beasts"** (v19, 20) — as in ch 1.
- **"brought them … to see what he would call them" kept** (v19) — the Hebrew has no object after "brought" and has "what he would call it" (singular); "them" is supplied twice for English.
- **"whatsoever Adam called every living creature" → "whatever the man called a living soul"** (v19) — Hebrew וְכֹל אֲשֶׁר יִקְרָא־לוֹ הָאָדָם נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה, literally "and all that the man would call it, a living soul, that was its name". נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה stands loosely beside "it"; the same words as v7.
- **"Adam" → "the man"** (v19, 20a, 21, 22, 23) — Hebrew הָאָדָם with the article, as in the whole chapter.
- **"for Adam there was not found" → "for Adam he did not find"** (v20) — Hebrew וּלְאָדָם לֹא־מָצָא. The Masoretic pointing has no article here (לְאָדָם), so "for Adam", or "for a man"; the consonants would also allow "for the man". The verb is active with no subject named: Yahweh *Elohim*, or the man, or "one did not find" (= "there was not found").
- **"one of his ribs" kept** (v21, 22) — Hebrew אַחַת מִצַּלְעֹתָיו, הַצֵּלָע. Everywhere else in the Hebrew Bible צֵלָע is a "side" (of the ark, Exod 25:12; of a hill, 2 Sam 16:13) or a side room or plank of a building; only here is it part of a body. So "one of his sides" is a possible rendering. "Rib" rests on the related words in the sister languages (the Aramaic word in Dan 7:5 is the ribs in the bear's mouth) and on the plural "one of his …"; the verse keeps it, and the other rendering is not excluded.
- **"closed up the flesh instead thereof" → "closed up flesh in its place"** (v21) — Hebrew בָּשָׂר without the article.
- **"made he a woman" → "built … into a woman"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיִּבֶן … לְאִשָּׁה, the verb "build".
- **"This is now" → "This one, this time,"** (v23) — Hebrew זֹאת הַפַּעַם, literally "this (feminine), the time": after the animals, this one at last. "This one" (זֹאת) stands three times in the verse.
- **"Woman … Man"** (v23) — Hebrew אִשָּׁה and אִישׁ, which sound alike. אִישׁ is a different word from אָדָם "man" in "And the man said": English has one word for both. From here אִישׁ and אִשָּׁה are also "husband" and "wife" (v24, 25).
- **"shall a man leave … shall cleave … shall be" → "leaves … clings … become"** (v24) — Hebrew imperfect and perfects with "and", said of what regularly happens; "shall leave … shall cling … shall become" is equally possible. דָּבַק is "cling, stick to".
- **"naked"** (v25) — Hebrew עֲרוּמִּים; the next verse calls the serpent עָרוּם "shrewd", a play on the sound.

### Notes (context, not changes)

- **v4** — the verse is one sentence in the Masoretic Text. From here the name is "Yahweh *Elohim*" to the end of ch 3 (except in the speech of the serpent and the woman, 3:1–5).
- **v10–14** — the description of the rivers is in nominal clauses: "is" is supplied.
- **v23** — the man's words are verse; the lines follow the Hebrew accents.
