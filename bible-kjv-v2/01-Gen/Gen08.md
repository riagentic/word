# Genesis 8

[1] *Elohim* remembered Noah, and all the wild animals and all the livestock that were with him in the ark. *Elohim* made a wind pass over the earth, and the waters subsided.

[2] The springs of the deep and the windows of the heavens were closed, and the rain from the heavens was restrained.

[3] The waters receded from off the earth steadily, and after 150 days the waters had abated.

[4] The ark came to rest in the seventh month, on the 17th day of the month, on the mountains of Ararat.

[5] The waters continued to recede until the tenth month; in the tenth month, on the first day of the month, the tops of the mountains appeared.

[6] At the end of forty days Noah opened the window of the ark that he had made,

[7] and he sent out a raven, which went back and forth until the waters had dried up from the earth.

[8] Then he sent out a dove from him to see whether the waters had receded from the face of the ground;

[9] but the dove found no resting place for the sole of her foot, and she returned to him into the ark, for the waters were still on the face of the whole earth. He reached out his hand and took her and brought her in to him into the ark.

[10] He waited another seven days, and again sent out the dove from the ark.

[11] The dove came in to him toward evening, and there was a freshly plucked olive leaf in her beak; so Noah knew that the waters had receded from the earth.

[12] He waited another seven days and sent out the dove, and she did not return to him again.

[13] In the 601st year, in the first month, on the first day of the month, the waters were dried up from the earth. Noah removed the covering of the ark and looked, and behold, the face of the ground was dry.

[14] In the second month, on the 27th day of the month, the earth was dry.

[15] Then *Elohim* spoke to Noah, saying,

[16] "Go out from the ark, you and your wife, your sons and your sons' wives with you.

[17] Bring out with you every living thing that is with you — of all flesh, of birds, of livestock, of every creeping thing that creeps on the earth — so that they may swarm on the earth and be fruitful and multiply on the earth."

[18] So Noah went out, and his sons and his wife and his sons' wives with him.

[19] Every wild animal, every creeping thing, every bird, everything that moves on the earth — they went out of the ark according to their families.

[20] Then Noah built an altar to Yahweh, and he took from every clean animal and from every clean bird, and offered burnt offerings on the altar.

[21] Yahweh smelled the soothing aroma, and Yahweh said in his heart, "I will not again curse the ground because of humankind, for the inclination of the human heart is evil from its youth; nor will I again strike down every living thing as I have done.

[22] As long as the earth endures, seedtime and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, and day and night shall not cease."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"remembered" (v1)** — Hebrew זָכַר *zakhar*. Not merely "recalled" (as if Yahweh had forgotten) but "acted on behalf of," a covenant-relationship term — to "remember" someone is to act for them (cf. Ex 2:24, Lk 1:72). Rendered "remembered" but the covenantal weight should be heard.
- **"wind" (v1)** — Hebrew רוּחַ *ruach* — same word as the *Ruach Elohim* hovering over the waters in 1:2. Deliberate echo: creation begins again with the divine wind over the receding waters.
- **"asswaged" → "subsided"** (v1) — archaic → modern.
- **"was abated" → "had abated"** (v3) — archaic verb form modernized.
- **"mountains of Ararat" (v4)** — Hebrew הָרֵי אֲרָרָט *harey Ararat*. Ararat = ancient Urartu, the mountainous region of modern eastern Turkey / Armenia. Plural "mountains" indicates the mountain range, not one specific peak (later Christian tradition fixed on Mt. Ararat proper).
- **"pluckt off" → "freshly plucked"** (v11) — Hebrew טָרָף *taraph*. Some read as hapax "freshly broken off" (indicating new growth after flood). Modernized spelling.
- **"in her mouth" → "in her beak"** (v11) — Hebrew בְּפִיהָ *be-fiha* = "in her mouth." Dove's mouth = beak in modern English.
- **"Noah removed the covering" (v13)** — Hebrew מִכְסֵה *mikhseh* = covering / roof (related to *tsohar* in 6:16, though different word). The upper covering of the ark, removed so Noah could see.
- **"that they may breed abundantly" → "so that they may swarm"** (v17) — Hebrew יִשְׁרְצוּ *yishretsu* = swarm / teem. Same root as Gen 1:20's *sheretz* ("swarming creatures"). Creation-language echo of re-populating.
- **"after their kinds" → "according to their families"** (v19) — Hebrew לְמִשְׁפְּחֹתֵיהֶם *le-mishpechoteyhem*. Unusual: not *le-minehem* ("according to their kinds," as in Gen 1) but *le-mishpechoteyhem* ("according to their families / clans"). The family-language of human lineages applied to animals — subtle humanizing of the creaturely re-start.
- **"sweet savour" → "soothing aroma"** (v21) — Hebrew רֵיחַ הַנִּיחֹחַ *reach ha-nichoach*. *Nichoach* from root נוח (same as Noah's name) = "restful / soothing / pacifying" aroma. Technical sacrificial term: the aroma that pacifies divine anger. Noah's name-root sounds in the word: another *Noah / nicho(ach)* wordplay. KJV "sweet savour" is archaic and loses the pacification sense.
- **"curse the ground for man's sake" → "curse the ground because of humankind"** (v21) — Hebrew בַּעֲבוּר הָאָדָם *ba'avur ha-adam*. "For man's sake" in modern English suggests "on behalf of" / "for his benefit," which reverses the sense. *Ba'avur* here means "on account of" (the human's wickedness) — corrected.
- **"imagination" → "inclination"** (v21) — same as 6:5. Hebrew יֵצֶר *yetser*.
- **"from his youth" → "from its youth"** (v21) — Hebrew מִנְּעֻרָיו *minne'urav*. *Ne'urim* = youth / early years. The inclination has been evil from humanity's early life / development.
- **"smite" → "strike down"** (v21) — modernized.
- **v22 poem formatting** — Hebrew is rhythmic and parallelistic; rendered in prose here for space but the reader should hear the oath-formula: "*seedtime and harvest / cold and heat / summer and winter / day and night.*"

### Notes

- **"Yahweh said in his heart" (v21)** — a rare depiction of internal divine deliberation. The flood's resolution: not because humanity has improved (the *yetser* is still evil) but because Yahweh determines, unilaterally and unconditionally, to refrain from destruction. The first divine "never again."
