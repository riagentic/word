# Genesis 8

[1] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ remembered ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧, and every living thing and every beast that was with him in the ark; and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ caused a wind to pass over the earth, and the waters subsided.

[2] And the springs of the deep and the windows of the heavens were stopped up, and the rain from the heavens was restrained.

[3] And the waters returned from upon the earth continually; and the waters decreased at the end of a hundred and fifty days.

[4] And the ark rested in the seventh month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on the mountains of Ararat.

[5] And the waters kept decreasing until the tenth month; in the tenth, on the first of the month, the tops of the mountains were seen.

[6] And it came to pass at the end of forty days that ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ opened the window of the ark which he had made,

[7] and he sent out the raven, and it went out, going out and returning, until the waters were dried out from upon the earth.

[8] And he sent out the dove from him, to see whether the waters had abated from upon the face of the ground;

[9] but the dove found no resting place for the sole of her foot, and she returned to him to the ark, for waters were on the face of all the earth; and he stretched out his hand and took her and brought her to him into the ark.

[10] And he waited yet seven other days, and again he sent out the dove from the ark.

[11] And the dove came to him at the time of evening, and behold, a plucked olive leaf in her mouth; and ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ knew that the waters had abated from upon the earth.

[12] And he waited yet seven other days and sent out the dove, and she did not return to him again any more.

[13] And it came to pass in the six hundred and first year, in the first month, on the first of the month, that the waters were dried up from upon the earth; and ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ removed the covering of the ark and saw, and behold, the face of the ground was dry.

[14] And in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the earth was dried out.

[15] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ spoke to ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧, saying,

[16] "Go out from the ark, you and your wife and your sons and your sons' wives with you.

[17] Every living thing that is with you of all flesh, of birds and of beasts and of every creeping thing that creeps on the earth, bring out with you; and they shall swarm on the earth and be fruitful and multiply on the earth."

[18] And ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ went out, and his sons and his wife and his sons' wives with him.

[19] Every living thing, every creeping thing and all the birds, everything that creeps on the earth, after their clans they went out from the ark.

[20] And ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ built an altar to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he took of every clean beast and of all the clean birds, and offered up burnt offerings on the altar.

[21] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ smelled the soothing smell, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to his heart, "I will not again curse the ground any more for the sake of man, for the inclination of the heart of man is evil from his youth; and I will not again any more strike all that lives, as I have done.

[22] While all the days of the earth continue, seedtime and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night shall not cease."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"remembered"** (v1) — Hebrew זָכַר *zakhar*.
- **"every living thing, and all the cattle" → "every living thing and every beast"** (v1) — Hebrew כָּל־הַחַיָּה … כָּל־הַבְּהֵמָה, as in 7:14.
- **"wind"** (v1) — Hebrew רוּחַ *ruach*, the one word for wind / spirit / breath (1:2; 6:3, 17).
- **"asswaged" → "subsided"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיָּשֹׁכּוּ, sink down, grow quiet.
- **"stopped" → "stopped up"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיִּסָּכְרוּ, were shut, dammed.
- **"returned … continually" kept** (v3) — Hebrew וַיָּשֻׁבוּ … הָלוֹךְ וָשׁוֹב, literally "returned … going and returning". The same form in v5, הָיוּ הָלוֹךְ וְחָסוֹר, literally "were going and decreasing" ("kept decreasing"). In v7 the literal form is kept ("going out and returning").
- **"were abated" → "decreased"** (v3, 5) — Hebrew חָסֵר, grow less, be lacking; another word than "abated" in v8, 11.
- **"after the end of the hundred and fifty days" → "at the end of a hundred and fifty days"** (v3) — Hebrew מִקְצֵה חֲמִשִּׁים וּמְאַת יוֹם; מִקְצֵה is "at the end of".
- **"rested"** (v4) — Hebrew וַתָּנַח, the root נוּחַ of the name Noah; the same root in "resting place" (v9, מָנוֹחַ) and "soothing" (v21).
- **"mountains of Ararat"** (v4) — Hebrew הָרֵי אֲרָרָט, plural: a mountain country, not one named peak.
- **"in the tenth month, on the first day of the month" → "in the tenth, on the first of the month"** (v5) — Hebrew בָּעֲשִׂירִי בְּאֶחָד לַחֹדֶשׁ; so also v13 ("on the first of the month").
- **"tops of the mountains"** (v5) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי הֶהָרִים, literally "heads".
- **"window"** (v6) — Hebrew חַלּוֹן, another word than the "windows" (אֲרֻבֹּת) of v2 and the צֹהַר of 6:16.
- **"a raven" → "the raven"** (v7) — Hebrew הָעֹרֵב, with the article; so "the dove" (v8).
- **"went forth to and fro" → "went out, going out and returning"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיֵּצֵא יָצוֹא וָשׁוֹב.
- **"were dried up" → "were dried out"** (v7, 14) — Hebrew יָבֵשׁ; "dried up / dry" in v13 is another verb, חָרֵב. The passage moves from the one to the other: the waters gone (v13), the earth dried out (v14).
- **"were abated"** (v8, 11) — Hebrew קַלּוּ, literally "had become light"; the root of "curse" in v21.
- **"rest" → "resting place"** (v9) — Hebrew מָנוֹחַ.
- **"the waters were on the face" → "waters were on the face"** (v9) — Hebrew מַיִם without the article.
- **"put forth his hand, and took her, and pulled her in unto him" → "stretched out his hand and took her and brought her to him"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיָּבֵא, "caused her to come".
- **"other seven days"** (v10, 12) — Hebrew עוֹד שִׁבְעַת יָמִים אֲחֵרִים, "yet seven other days". The two verbs for "waited" differ slightly in form (וַיָּחֶל, וַיִּיָּחֶל).
- **"again he sent" / "did not return again"** (v10, 12) — Hebrew וַיֹּסֶף שַׁלַּח, וְלֹא־יָסְפָה שׁוּב, literally "he added to send", "she did not add to return".
- **"in the evening" → "at the time of evening"** (v11) — Hebrew לְעֵת עֶרֶב.
- **"an olive leaf pluckt off" → "a plucked olive leaf"** (v11) — Hebrew טָרָף, found only here as an adjective, from טָרַף "tear": torn off, fresh.
- **"in her mouth" kept** (v11) — Hebrew בְּפִיהָ.
- **"in the six hundredth and first year"** (v13) — Hebrew בְּאַחַת וְשֵׁשׁ־מֵאוֹת שָׁנָה, the Hebrew does not say here whose year (7:11: of Noah's life). Numbers stand in the ordinary English order; the Hebrew order is "in the one and six hundredth year", "fifty and a hundred days" (v3), "on the seven and twentieth day" (v14).
- **"looked" → "saw"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיַּרְא.
- **"covering"** (v13) — Hebrew מִכְסֵה, from "cover" (7:19); another word than the צֹהַר of 6:16.
- **"Bring forth with thee every living thing"** (v17) — the Hebrew order puts the long object first. The written text has הוֹצֵא and the reading tradition הַיְצֵא: two forms of the same command, "bring out".
- **"that they may breed abundantly" → "and they shall swarm"** (v17) — Hebrew וְשָׁרְצוּ, the verb of 1:20, 21.
- **"creepeth" → "creeps"** (v19) — Hebrew רוֹמֵשׂ, as in 7:21.
- **"after their kinds" → "after their clans"** (v19) — Hebrew לְמִשְׁפְּחֹתֵיהֶם, the word for the clans of men (10:5), not מִין "kind".
- **"offered burnt offerings" → "offered up burnt offerings"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיַּעַל עֹלֹת, literally "made go up goings-up", verb and noun of one root.
- **"sweet savour" → "soothing smell"** (v21) — Hebrew רֵיחַ הַנִּיחֹחַ. רֵיחַ is the noun of the verb "smelled" (וַיָּרַח); נִיחֹחַ is from נוּחַ "rest": what brings rest, soothes. The root is that of Noah's name.
- **"said in his heart" → "said to his heart"** (v21) — Hebrew אֶל־לִבּוֹ.
- **"curse"** (v21) — Hebrew לְקַלֵּל, piel of קָלַל "be light": make light of, curse. Another word than "cursed" (אָרַר) in 3:17 and 5:29.
- **"for man's sake" → "for the sake of man"** (v21) — Hebrew בַּעֲבוּר הָאָדָם, "on account of man".
- **"for"** (v21) — Hebrew כִּי; it can also be taken as "though". The verse keeps the usual "for".
- **"imagination" → "inclination"** (v21) — Hebrew יֵצֶר, as in 6:5.
- **"from his youth"** (v21) — Hebrew מִנְּעֻרָיו; "his" is man's.
- **"smite" → "strike"** (v21) — Hebrew לְהַכּוֹת.
- **"every thing living" → "all that lives"** (v21) — Hebrew כָּל־חַי, as in 6:19.
- **"While the earth remaineth" → "While all the days of the earth continue"** (v22) — Hebrew עֹד כָּל־יְמֵי הָאָרֶץ, literally "yet all the days of the earth"; the verb is supplied.
- **"seedtime"** (v22) — Hebrew זֶרַע, "seed": here the sowing, beside "harvest".
- **"shall not cease"** (v22) — Hebrew לֹא יִשְׁבֹּתוּ, the verb of 2:2, 3 (שָׁבַת). The four pairs are joined by "and" throughout.

### Notes

- **"Yahweh said to his heart" (v21)** — the verse gives Yahweh's own thought. The inclination of man's heart is said to be evil still, as before the flood (6:5).
