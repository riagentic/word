# Judges 2

[1] And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ went up from Gilgal to Bochim. And he said, "I brought you up from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and brought you to the land that I swore to your fathers, and I said, 'I will not break my covenant with you forever.

[2] And you, you shall not cut a covenant with the inhabitants of this land; their altars you shall break down.' And you have not listened to my voice. What is this that you have done?

[3] And I also have said, 'I will not drive them out from before you, and they shall be to you for sides, and their gods shall be to you for a snare.'"

[4] And it came to pass, when the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke these words to all the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, that the people lifted up their voice and wept.

[5] And they called the name of that place Bochim, and they sacrificed there to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ sent the people away, and the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went, each man to his inheritance, to take possession of the land.

[7] And the people served ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ all the days of ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ and all the days of the elders who prolonged days after ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, who had seen all the great work of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that he did for ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[8] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ the son of Nun, the servant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, died, a hundred and ten years old.

[9] And they buried him in the border of his inheritance, in ⟦timnath-heres|Timnat Cheres|Timnath-heres⟧, in the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, north of Mount Gaash.

[10] And also all that generation were gathered to its fathers, and another generation arose after them, who did not know ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, nor yet the work that he had done for ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[11] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ did the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and served the Baals.

[12] And they forsook ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of their fathers, who brought them out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and they went after other gods, of the gods of the peoples who were around them, and they bowed down to them, and they vexed ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] And they forsook ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and served the Baal and the ⟦ashtaroth-goddesses|Ashtarot|Ashtaroth⟧.

[14] And the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ burned against ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and he gave them into the hand of pillagers, and they pillaged them; and he sold them into the hand of their enemies all around, and they were no longer able to stand before their enemies.

[15] Wherever they went out, the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was against them for evil, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had spoken and as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had sworn to them; and they were in great distress.

[16] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ raised up judges, and they saved them from the hand of their pillagers.

[17] And also to their judges they did not listen, for they played the prostitute after other gods and bowed down to them. They turned aside quickly from the way in which their fathers had walked, to hear the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; they did not do so.

[18] And when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ raised up judges for them, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ would be with the judge and would save them from the hand of their enemies all the days of the judge; for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ would be sorry because of their groaning because of their oppressors and those who crowded them.

[19] And it would be, when the judge died, that they would turn back and work ruin more than their fathers, going after other gods to serve them and to bow down to them; they let nothing fall of their deeds and of their hard way.

[20] And the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ burned against ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and he said, "Because this nation has overstepped my covenant that I commanded their fathers, and they have not listened to my voice,

[21] I also will no more dispossess any man from before them, of the nations that ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ left, and he died,

[22] in order to test ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ by them, whether they are keeping the way of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to walk in them, as their fathers kept it, or not."

[23] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ left these nations, so as not to dispossess them quickly, and he did not give them into the hand of ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v12) — Hebrew *Elohim*, of Yahweh. Of the gods of the peoples (v3, 12, 17, 19) the same word is the plain "gods".
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of" → "sons of"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"an angel of the LORD" → "the angel of Yahweh"** (v1, 4) — Hebrew מַלְאַךְ יְהוָה; the word מַלְאָךְ means "messenger". He speaks as Yahweh himself ("I brought you up", "my covenant").
- **"from Gilgal to Bochim"** (v1) — both names have the article in the Hebrew: "from the Gilgal to the Bochim" ("the weepers"; the place gets this name in v5). The Leningrad Codex has a paragraph break in the middle of the verse, after "Bochim". The Greek translation adds "and to Bethel and to the house of Israel" (cited from memory); it is not in the Hebrew.
- **"I made you to go up out of Egypt" → "I brought you up from Egypt"** (v1) — Hebrew אַעֲלֶה, an imperfect form ("I bring up / I will bring up") where a past is expected; the verbs that follow are past, and the sense is past.
- **"I will never break my covenant with you" → "I will not break my covenant with you forever"** (v1) — Hebrew לֹא־אָפֵר … לְעוֹלָם. "Which I sware unto your fathers": the Hebrew has no "to give".
- **"ye shall make no league" → "you shall not cut a covenant"** (v2) — Hebrew כָּרַת בְּרִית, the same word "covenant" as in v1. **"throw down" → "break down"** — Hebrew נָתַץ. **"ye have not obeyed my voice" → "you have not listened to my voice"** — Hebrew שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל. **"why have ye done this?" → "What is this that you have done?"** — Hebrew מַה־זֹּאת עֲשִׂיתֶם.
- **"Wherefore I also said" → "And I also have said"** (v3) — Hebrew וְגַם אָמַרְתִּי, a perfect: a word already spoken (Num 33:55; Josh 23:13), or a sentence passed now ("so now I say").
- **"they shall be as thorns in your sides" → "they shall be to you for sides"** (v3) — Hebrew וְהָיוּ לָכֶם לְצִדִּים, word for word; צַד is "side". "Thorns" is not in the Hebrew; it is supplied from Num 33:55 and Josh 23:13 ("thorns in your sides"). The meaning of the Hebrew as it stands is not certain: "they shall be at your sides", or a word for "snares" or "adversaries" has been seen here (the Greek has "for distresses": cited from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept, word for word.
- **"drive them out"** (v3) — Hebrew גֵּרֵשׁ; "dispossess" in v21, 23 is another verb (הוֹרִישׁ).
- **"lifted up their voice"** (v4) — the Hebrew has the singular, "their voice".
- **"Bochim"** (v5) — Hebrew בֹּכִים, "weepers", from the verb "wept" in v4. No explanation stands in the verse.
- **"when Joshua had let the people go" → "And Joshua sent the people away"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיְשַׁלַּח, a main clause. v6–9 repeat Josh 24:28–31 in another order, and the same words are used for the same Hebrew. **"to possess the land" → "to take possession of the land"** — Hebrew לָרֶשֶׁת.
- **"the elders that outlived Joshua" → "the elders who prolonged days after Joshua"** (v7) — Hebrew הֶאֱרִיכוּ יָמִים אַחֲרֵי, as Josh 24:31. Here they "had seen" (רָאוּ) the work, there they "knew" it; here it is "the great work".
- **"being an hundred and ten years old" → "a hundred and ten years old"** (v8) — Hebrew בֶּן־מֵאָה וָעֶשֶׂר שָׁנִים, literally "a son of a hundred and ten years". "Servant" is עֶבֶד, "slave"; of Yahweh's man "servant".
- **"in the border of his inheritance in Timnathheres, in the mount of Ephraim, on the north side of the hill Gaash" → "… in Timnath-heres, in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash"** (v9) — הַר is "mountain / mount" and, of a region, "hill country"; both stand here. Josh 24:30 has the name as Timnath-serah (the same letters in another order).
- **"were gathered unto their fathers" → "were gathered to its fathers"** (v10) — Hebrew אֶל־אֲבוֹתָיו, with a singular suffix: the generation as one. **"which knew not the LORD, nor yet the works" → "who did not know Yahweh, nor yet the work"** — Hebrew הַמַּעֲשֶׂה, singular.
- **"did evil in the sight of the LORD" → "did the evil in the eyes of Yahweh"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲשׂוּ … אֶת־הָרַע בְּעֵינֵי יְהוָה, with the article: "the evil". The first of the book's standing sentences (3:7, 12; 4:1; 6:1; 10:6; 13:1). **"Baalim" → "the Baals"** — Hebrew הַבְּעָלִים, the plural of Baal ("lord, owner").
- **"followed other gods" → "went after other gods"** (v12, 19) — Hebrew הָלַךְ אַחֲרֵי. **"provoked the LORD to anger" → "vexed Yahweh"** — Hebrew וַיַּכְעִסוּ; "anger" is not a second word in the Hebrew.
- **"served Baal and Ashtaroth" → "served the Baal and the Ashtaroth"** (v13) — Hebrew לַבַּעַל וְלָעַשְׁתָּרוֹת: "the Baal" in the singular with the article, "the Ashtaroth" in the plural (the plural of the name Ashtoreth). The verb "serve" has לְ here ("did service to"), אֶת in v11.
- **"the anger of the LORD was hot against Israel" → "the anger of Yahweh burned against Israel"** (v14, 20) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר־אַף יְהוָה בְּיִשְׂרָאֵל; אַף is "nose", then "anger".
- **"into the hands of spoilers that spoiled them" → "into the hand of pillagers, and they pillaged them"** (v14, 16) — Hebrew שֹׁסִים, שָׁסָה: a verb of its own beside בָּזַז "plunder"; "hand" is singular; "and they pillaged" is a main clause. **"sold them into the hands of" → "sold them into the hand of"** — Hebrew וַיִּמְכְּרֵם בְּיַד.
- **"Whithersoever they went out" → "Wherever they went out"** (v15) — Hebrew בְּכֹל אֲשֶׁר יָצְאוּ, literally "in all that they went out": in every going out to war. **"against them for evil"** — Hebrew לְרָעָה. **"as the LORD had said" → "as Yahweh had spoken"** — דִּבֶּר. **"they were greatly distressed" → "they were in great distress"** — Hebrew וַיֵּצֶר לָהֶם מְאֹד, literally "and it was very narrow for them".
- **"Nevertheless the LORD raised up judges, which delivered them" → "And Yahweh raised up judges, and they saved them"** (v16, 18) — Hebrew הֵקִים "raise up" (literally "make stand"); הוֹשִׁיעַ "save", the verb from which "savior" (3:9, 15) comes. "Deliver" is another verb (הִצִּיל). שֹׁפֵט "judge": one who rules and sets right, not only one who sits in court.
- **"they would not hearken unto their judges, but they went a whoring after other gods" → "to their judges they did not listen, for they played the prostitute after other gods"** (v17) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; זָנָה "play the prostitute", of unfaithfulness to Yahweh. **"turned quickly out of the way … obeying the commandments of the LORD; but they did not so" → "turned aside quickly from the way … to hear the commandments of Yahweh; they did not do so"** — Hebrew לִשְׁמֹעַ, "to hear" (and so to do): how the fathers walked.
- **"then the LORD was with the judge, and delivered them" → "Yahweh would be with the judge and would save them"** (v18, 19) — the Hebrew forms (וְהָיָה, וְהוֹשִׁיעָם, יִנָּחֵם, יָשֻׁבוּ) tell what happened again and again.
- **"it repented the LORD because of their groanings by reason of them that oppressed them and vexed them" → "Yahweh would be sorry because of their groaning because of their oppressors and those who crowded them"** (v18) — Hebrew יִנָּחֵם, the nifal of נחם: to be sorry, to be moved to pity and so to relent; "repent" would say that Yahweh had done wrong. "Because of" stands for two Hebrew words, מִן and מִפְּנֵי (literally "from the face of"). לָחַץ is "oppress" (literally "press"); דָּחַק, found only here and in Joel 2:8, is "thrust, crowd".
- **"corrupted themselves more than their fathers" → "work ruin more than their fathers"** (v19) — Hebrew וְהִשְׁחִיתוּ, with no object, as Deut 4:25. **"they ceased not from their own doings, nor from their stubborn way" → "they let nothing fall of their deeds and of their hard way"** — Hebrew לֹא הִפִּילוּ מִן, literally "they did not make fall from"; קָשֶׁה is "hard".
- **"this people hath transgressed my covenant" → "this nation has overstepped my covenant"** (v20) — Hebrew הַגּוֹי הַזֶּה: Israel is called a גּוֹי, the word used of the nations in v21, 23. עָבַר is "pass over, overstep". "They have not listened": the Hebrew verbs are plural.
- **"I also will not henceforth drive out any from before them" → "I also will no more dispossess any man from before them"** (v21) — Hebrew לֹא אוֹסִיף לְהוֹרִישׁ אִישׁ, literally "I will not add to dispossess a man". **"which Joshua left when he died" → "that Joshua left, and he died"** — Hebrew וַיָּמֹת, a main clause.
- **"That through them I may prove Israel" → "in order to test Israel by them"** (v22) — Hebrew לְמַעַן נַסּוֹת, an infinitive with no subject named; it can depend on "I will no more dispossess" (v21) or on "Joshua left". נִסָּה is "test". **"to walk therein" → "to walk in them"** — Hebrew בָּם, a plural, though "the way" is singular: the Hebrew as it stands. "It" after "kept" is supplied.
- **"Therefore the LORD left those nations, without driving them out hastily" → "And Yahweh left these nations, so as not to dispossess them quickly"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיַּנַּח, "let remain"; לְבִלְתִּי הוֹרִישָׁם.

### Notes
- **Programmatic summary** — ch 2 lays out the cyclical pattern governing all Judges: apostasy → oppression → cry → judge → deliverance → apostasy again.
- **"Did not know Yahweh"** (v10) — not denial of his existence but failure of relational knowing. The new generation inherits facts but not relationship.
- **Baal and Ashtaroth** — chief male and female deities of Canaanite pantheon. Baal: storm god, fertility. Ashtoreth / Astarte: love, war, fertility.
- **Seven major judges follow**: Othniel, Ehud, Deborah/Barak, Gideon, Jephthah, Samson, plus minor judges (Shamgar, Tola, Jair, Ibzan, Elon, Abdon). Each a local deliverer, not yet national sovereign.
- **"To test Israel"** (v22) — divine purpose in partial conquest: test fidelity. The remaining nations are both punishment for prior failure and test for future generations.
