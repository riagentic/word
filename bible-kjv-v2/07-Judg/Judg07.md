# Judges 7

[1] ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧ (that is, ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧) and all the people with him rose early and encamped beside the spring of ⟦harod|Charod|Harod⟧, so that the camp of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ was to the north of them, by the hill of Moreh, in the valley.

[2] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ said to ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧, "The people with you are too many for me to give ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ into their hand, lest ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ boast over me, saying, 'My own hand has delivered me.'

[3] Now therefore proclaim in the hearing of the people, 'Whoever is afraid and trembling, let him return and depart from Mount ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧.'" And 22,000 of the people returned, and 10,000 remained.

[4] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ said to ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧, "The people are still too many. Bring them down to the water, and I will test them for you there. Of whom I say to you, 'This one shall go with you,' he shall go with you; and of whom I say to you, 'This one shall not go with you,' he shall not go."

[5] So he brought the people down to the water, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧, "Everyone who laps the water with his tongue, as a dog laps, set him by himself; likewise everyone who kneels down to drink."

[6] The number of those who lapped, putting their hand to their mouth, was three hundred men; but all the rest of the people knelt down to drink water.

[7] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ said to ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧, "With the three hundred men who lapped I will save you and give ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ into your hand. Let all the other people go, each to his place."

[8] So the people took provisions in their hands, and their horns; and he sent all the rest of the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, each to his tent, keeping the three hundred men. The camp of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ was below him in the valley.

[9] That same night ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to him, "Arise; go down against the camp, for I have given it into your hand.

[10] But if you are afraid to go down, go with your servant Purah down to the camp;

[11] you shall hear what they are saying, and afterward your hands shall be strengthened to go down against the camp." So he went down with his servant Purah to the outposts of the armed men who were in the camp.

[12] The ⟦midianites|Midyanim|Midianites⟧ and the ⟦amalekites|Amaleqim|Amalekites⟧ and all the children of the east lay along the valley like locusts for multitude, and their camels were without number — as numerous as the sand on the seashore.

[13] When ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ came, behold, a man was recounting a dream to his companion. He said, "Behold, I dreamed a dream: a cake of barley bread tumbled into the camp of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧, came to the tent, struck it so that it fell, and turned it upside down — and the tent collapsed."

[14] His companion answered, "This is nothing else than the sword of ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ son of ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧, a man of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; into his hand ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has given ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ and all the camp."

[15] When ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ heard the telling of the dream and its interpretation, he bowed in worship. He returned to the camp of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and said, "Arise, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has given the camp of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ into your hand."

[16] He divided the three hundred men into three companies and put horns into the hands of each man, along with empty jars, with torches inside the jars.

[17] He said to them, "Look at me, and do as I do. When I come to the edge of the camp, as I do, so you shall do.

[18] When I blow the horn, I and all who are with me, then blow the horns also on every side of all the camp, and say, 'For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and for ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧!'"

[19] ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ and the hundred men who were with him came to the edge of the camp at the beginning of the middle watch, just after they had changed the guard. They blew the horns and smashed the jars that were in their hands.

[20] The three companies blew the horns and broke the jars; they held the torches in their left hands and the horns in their right hands to blow them, and they cried, "A sword for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and for ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧!"

[21] Each man stood in his place around the camp; and all the camp ran — they cried out and fled.

[22] As the three hundred horns blew, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ set each man's sword against his companion throughout the camp. The camp fled as far as ⟦beth-shittah|Beit HaShittah|Beth-shittah⟧ toward ⟦zererah|Tzererah|Zererah⟧, as far as the border of ⟦abel-meholah|Avel Mecholah|Abel-meholah⟧ by ⟦tabbath|Tabbat|Tabbath⟧.

[23] The men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were called out from ⟦naphtali|Naftali|Naphtali⟧, from Asher, and from all ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧, and they pursued ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧.

[24] ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ sent messengers throughout all the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, saying, "Come down against ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ and seize the waters against them as far as ⟦beth-barah|Beit Barah|Beth-barah⟧, and also the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧." So all the men of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ were called out, and they seized the waters as far as ⟦beth-barah|Beit Barah|Beth-barah⟧ and the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧.

[25] They captured two princes of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧, ⟦oreb|Orev|Oreb⟧ and ⟦zeeb|Zeev|Zeeb⟧; they killed ⟦oreb|Orev|Oreb⟧ at the Rock of ⟦oreb|Orev|Oreb⟧, and ⟦zeeb|Zeev|Zeeb⟧ they killed at the Winepress of ⟦zeeb|Zeev|Zeeb⟧; they pursued ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ and brought the heads of ⟦oreb|Orev|Oreb⟧ and ⟦zeeb|Zeev|Zeeb⟧ to ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"trumpet(s)" → "horn(s)"** (v8, 16, 18–20, 22) — Hebrew שׁוֹפָר *shofar* = ram's horn, not a metal trumpet.
- **"vaunt themselves" → "boast"** (v2) — modernized.
- **"pitchers" → "jars"** (v16, 19, 20) — archaic.
- **"lamps" → "torches"** (v16, 20) — Hebrew לַפִּידִים *lappidim*. Flaming torches, not oil lamps.
- **"went to meet"** idiomatic simplification.
- **Numbers modernized** throughout.

### Notes
- **Gideon's 300** — the iconic narrative. Army reduced from 32,000 to 300 (less than 1%). Criteria: (1) fear-test: those afraid sent home; (2) drinking-test: those who lap like a dog vs kneel. Many interpretations of the lapping test — ready-for-battle vigilance, physical discipline, or simple divine selection.
- **"Lest Israel boast"** (v2) — theological core. Yahweh reduces the force so victory is unmistakably his.
- **Dream of the barley cake** (v13–14) — barley is the poor-man's grain; a loaf of it crushes the Midianite tent. Self-interpreting omen of Midianite doom.
- **Horns, jars, torches** (v16–22) — non-standard weapons. Simultaneous smashing of jars reveals the torches, accompanied by horn-blasts from three sides, creating chaos. Midianites attack each other in panic.
- **"For Yahweh and for Gideon"** (v18, 20) — double cry. Gideon's humility slipping? The battle is Yahweh's, but Gideon's name is joined.
- **Pursuit to the Jordan** (v24–25) — Ephraim belatedly joins to block the fugitives. Two princes captured: Oreb ("raven") at Raven's Rock, Zeeb ("wolf") at Wolf's Winepress.
