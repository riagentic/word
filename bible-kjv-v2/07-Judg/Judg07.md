# Judges 7

[1] And ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧, that is ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧, rose early, and all the people who were with him, and they camped by the spring of ⟦harod|Charod|Harod⟧; and the camp of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ was to the north of him, from the hill of Moreh, in the valley.

[2] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧, "The people who are with you are too many for me to give ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ into their hand, lest ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ glorify itself over me, saying, 'My hand has saved me.'

[3] And now, call, please, in the ears of the people, saying, 'Whoever is fearful and trembling, let him return and depart from Mount ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧.'" And there returned of the people twenty-two thousand, and ten thousand were left.

[4] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧, "The people are still many. Bring them down to the water, and I will refine it for you there; and it shall be, he of whom I say to you, 'This one shall go with you,' he shall go with you; and everyone of whom I say to you, 'This one shall not go with you,' he shall not go."

[5] And he brought the people down to the water. And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧, "Everyone who laps with his tongue from the water, as the dog laps, you shall set him by himself; and everyone who bends down on his knees to drink."

[6] And the number of those lapping with their hand to their mouth was three hundred men; and all the rest of the people bent down on their knees to drink water.

[7] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧, "By the three hundred men who lapped I will save you and give ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ into your hand; and all the people, let them go, each man to his place."

[8] And the people took provisions in their hand, and their ⟦shofar|shofars|trumpets⟧; and every man of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ he sent away, each man to his tents, and the three hundred men he held fast. And the camp of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ was below him in the valley.

[9] And it came to pass that night that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to him, "Arise, go down against the camp, for I have given it into your hand.

[10] And if you are afraid to go down, go down, you and Purah your young man, to the camp;

[11] and you shall hear what they speak, and afterward your hands shall be strong, and you shall go down against the camp." And he went down, he and Purah his young man, to the end of those in battle array who were in the camp.

[12] And ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ and ⟦amalek|Amaleq|Amalek⟧ and all the sons of the east were lying in the valley like locusts for multitude, and for their camels there was no number, like the sand that is on the shore of the sea for multitude.

[13] And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ came, and behold, a man was recounting a dream to his neighbor, and he said, "Behold, I have dreamed a dream, and behold, a round loaf of barley bread was tumbling in the camp of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧, and it came to the tent and struck it, and it fell, and it turned it over, upward, and the tent fell."

[14] And his neighbor answered and said, "This is nothing other than the sword of ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ the son of ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧, a man of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has given into his hand ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ and all the camp."

[15] And it came to pass, when ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ heard the recounting of the dream and its interpretation, that he bowed down; and he returned to the camp of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and said, "Arise, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has given into your hand the camp of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧."

[16] And he divided the three hundred men into three companies, and he put ⟦shofar|shofars|trumpets⟧ in the hand of all of them, and empty jars, and torches inside the jars.

[17] And he said to them, "Look at me, and do likewise; and behold, I am coming to the end of the camp, and it shall be, as I do, so you shall do.

[18] And I will blow on the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧, I and all who are with me, and you also shall blow on the ⟦shofar|shofars|trumpets⟧, all around the whole camp, and you shall say, 'For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and for ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧!'"

[19] And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ came, and the hundred men who were with him, to the end of the camp at the beginning of the middle watch; they had only just set up the watchmen. And they blew on the ⟦shofar|shofars|trumpets⟧ and shattered the jars that were in their hand.

[20] And the three companies blew on the ⟦shofar|shofars|trumpets⟧ and broke the jars, and they held fast the torches in their left hand, and in their right hand the ⟦shofar|shofars|trumpets⟧ to blow; and they called out, "A sword for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and for ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧!"

[21] And they stood, each man in his place, all around the camp; and all the camp ran, and they shouted and fled.

[22] And they blew the three hundred ⟦shofar|shofars|trumpets⟧, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ set the sword of each man against his neighbor, and in all the camp; and the camp fled as far as ⟦beth-shittah|Beit HaShittah|Beth-shittah⟧ toward ⟦zererah|Tzererah|Zererah⟧, as far as the edge of ⟦abel-meholah|Avel Mecholah|Abel-meholah⟧, by ⟦tabbath|Tabbat|Tabbath⟧.

[23] And the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were called out from ⟦naphtali|Naftali|Naphtali⟧ and from Asher and from all ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧, and they pursued after ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧.

[24] And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ sent messengers through all the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, saying, "Come down to meet ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧, and capture the waters against them as far as ⟦beth-barah|Beit Barah|Beth-barah⟧, and the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧." And all the men of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ were called out, and they captured the waters as far as ⟦beth-barah|Beit Barah|Beth-barah⟧, and the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧.

[25] And they captured the two princes of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧, ⟦oreb|Orev|Oreb⟧ and ⟦zeeb|Zeev|Zeeb⟧; and they killed ⟦oreb|Orev|Oreb⟧ at the rock of ⟦oreb|Orev|Oreb⟧, and ⟦zeeb|Zeev|Zeeb⟧ they killed at the winepress of ⟦zeeb|Zeev|Zeeb⟧; and they pursued to ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧; and the head of ⟦oreb|Orev|Oreb⟧ and of ⟦zeeb|Zeev|Zeeb⟧ they brought to ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧, across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v14) — Hebrew הָאֱלֹהִים, with the article: "the Elohim", in the mouth of a man of Midian.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"trumpet(s)" → "shofar(s)"** (v8, 16, 18–20, 22) — Hebrew שׁוֹפָר *shofar*, a ram's horn, not a metal trumpet. **"blew"** — Hebrew תָּקַע בְּ "blow on".
- **"Jerubbaal, who is Gideon" → "Jerubbaal, that is Gideon"** (v1) — Hebrew הוּא גִדְעוֹן, "he is Gideon". **"pitched beside the well of Harod" → "camped by the spring of Harod"** — Hebrew עֵין "spring"; the name חֲרֹד sounds like חָרֵד "trembling" in v3. **"so that the host of the Midianites were on the north side of them, by the hill of Moreh" → "and the camp of Midian was to the north of him, from the hill of Moreh"** — Hebrew הָיָה־לוֹ מִצָּפוֹן מִגִּבְעַת הַמּוֹרֶה, word for word; "him" is singular; מִן "from" may mean "from the hill on" or "on the side of the hill". The name has the article, "the Moreh" ("the teacher"). **"host" → "camp"** (throughout) — Hebrew מַחֲנֶה, the camp and the host in it.
- **"are too many for me to give"** (v2) — Hebrew רַב … מִתִּתִּי, literally "many … from my giving". **"vaunt themselves against me" → "glorify itself over me"** — Hebrew יִתְפָּאֵר עָלַי, singular: Israel. **"Mine own hand hath saved me" → "My hand has saved me"** — Hebrew יָדִי הוֹשִׁיעָה לִּי; הוֹשִׁיעַ "save" as in 6:14–37 and v7.
- **"proclaim in the ears of the people" → "call, please, in the ears of the people"** (v3) — Hebrew קְרָא נָא בְּאָזְנֵי; נָא "please". **"Whosoever is fearful and afraid" → "Whoever is fearful and trembling"** — Hebrew יָרֵא וְחָרֵד (Deut 20:8 has "fearful and soft of heart"). **"depart early" → "depart"** — Hebrew וְיִצְפֹּר, a verb found only here; its meaning is not known ("depart", "turn away", "fly off like a bird", צִפּוֹר, have been proposed). **"mount Gilead"** — Hebrew הַר הַגִּלְעָד, "the mountain of the Gilead". Gilead lies east of the Jordan, and the camp is west of it, by the spring of Harod; the Masoretic Text is kept (a common conjecture reads "Gilboa"). **Numbers** — in words, as the Hebrew: "twenty-two thousand", "ten thousand".
- **"The people are yet too many" → "The people are still many"** (v4) — Hebrew עוֹד הָעָם רָב. **"I will try them for thee" → "I will refine it for you"** — Hebrew וְאֶצְרְפֶנּוּ, the verb of the smith who smelts metal to part the pure from the dross; "it" is the people (singular), after "bring them down" (plural).
- **"Every one that lappeth of the water with his tongue, as a dog lappeth, him shalt thou set by himself; likewise every one that boweth down upon his knees to drink" → "Everyone who laps with his tongue from the water, as the dog laps, you shall set him by himself; and everyone who bends down on his knees to drink"** (v5) — word for word. The second half has no verb of its own in the Hebrew ("likewise" is not written); it is left so. "The dog" has the article.
- **"the number of them that lapped, putting their hand to their mouth" → "the number of those lapping with their hand to their mouth"** (v6) — Hebrew הַמֲלַקְקִים בְּיָדָם אֶל־פִּיהֶם, word for word; "putting" is not written. In v5 the lapping is "with his tongue … as the dog laps"; here it is "with their hand to their mouth". How the two fit together the Hebrew does not say, and the verse is not smoothed. **"bowed down upon their knees" → "bent down on their knees"** (v5, 6) — Hebrew כָּרַע עַל־בִּרְכָּיו.
- **"will I save you, and deliver the Midianites into thine hand" → "I will save you and give Midian into your hand"** (v7) — "you" is plural (אֶתְכֶם), "your hand" singular. **"let all the other people go" → "all the people, let them go"** — "other" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"So the people took victuals in their hand" → "And the people took provisions in their hand"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיִּקְחוּ אֶת־צֵדָה הָעָם בְּיָדָם. As the Masoretic Text is pointed (צֵדָה, not "the provisions of"), "the people" is the subject. Many read "they took the provisions of the people" (the three hundred take over what the others leave): a change of one vowel, not followed. **"he sent all the rest of Israel every man unto his tent, and retained those three hundred men" → "every man of Israel he sent away, each man to his tents, and the three hundred men he held fast"** — "the rest of" is not in the Hebrew; "tents" is plural; הֶחֱזִיק בְּ "hold fast".
- **"get thee down unto the host" → "go down against the camp"** (v9, 11) — Hebrew רֵד בַּמַּחֲנֶה, with בְּ: "against" or "into"; in v10, 11b the Hebrew has אֶל "to". **"I have delivered it" → "I have given it"** — Hebrew נָתַן.
- **"Phurah thy servant" → "Purah your young man"** (v10, 11) — Hebrew נַעַר, "boy, young man", here an attendant.
- **"shall thine hands be strengthened" → "your hands shall be strong"** (v11) — Hebrew תֶּחֱזַקְנָה. **"unto the outside of the armed men" → "to the end of those in battle array"** — Hebrew קְצֵה הַחֲמֻשִׁים; חֲמֻשִׁים is uncertain ("armed", "in battle array", or "in fifties"; so Exod 13:18; Josh 1:14).
- **"lay along in the valley like grasshoppers" → "were lying in the valley like locusts"** (v12) — Hebrew נֹפְלִים, literally "falling": settled, lying spread out (so Gen 25:18). אַרְבֶּה "locust". **"as the sand by the sea side" → "like the sand that is on the shore of the sea"** — Hebrew שְׂפַת הַיָּם, literally "the lip of the sea".
- **"told a dream unto his fellow" → "was recounting a dream to his neighbor"** (v13, 14) — Hebrew מְסַפֵּר לְרֵעֵהוּ; רֵעַ is the word of v22. **"a cake of barley bread tumbled into the host" → "a round loaf of barley bread was tumbling in the camp"** — Hebrew צְלִיל (so the reading tradition; written צלול), found only here: the meaning is uncertain (a round loaf, a cake). מִתְהַפֵּךְ "turning itself over and over". **"smote it that it fell, and overturned it, that the tent lay along" → "struck it, and it fell, and it turned it over, upward, and the tent fell"** — five short clauses in the Hebrew; וַיַּהַפְכֵהוּ לְמַעְלָה, literally "turned it over upward"; נָפַל "fall" twice.
- **"This is nothing else save the sword"** (v14) — Hebrew אֵין זֹאת בִּלְתִּי אִם, "this is not anything except". **"into his hand hath God delivered" → "*Elohim* has given into his hand"** — Hebrew נָתַן.
- **"the telling of the dream, and the interpretation thereof" → "the recounting of the dream and its interpretation"** (v15) — Hebrew מִסְפַּר, from סִפֵּר "recount" (in v6, 12 the same form means "number"); שִׁבְרוֹ, literally "its breaking": the breaking open of the dream, found in this sense only here. **"worshipped" → "bowed down"** — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁתָּחוּ.
- **"companies"** (v16, 20) — Hebrew רָאשִׁים, literally "heads". **"a trumpet in every man's hand" → "shofars in the hand of all of them"** — Hebrew plural. **"pitchers" → "jars"**; **"lamps" → "torches"** — Hebrew כַּדִּים; לַפִּדִים, burning torches, not oil lamps.
- **"Look on me, and do likewise"** (v17) — Hebrew מִמֶּנִּי תִרְאוּ וְכֵן תַּעֲשׂוּ, literally "from me you shall see, and so you shall do". **"the outside of the camp" → "the end of the camp"** (v17, 19) — Hebrew קְצֵה, as v11.
- **"The sword of the LORD, and of Gideon" → "For Yahweh and for Gideon"** (v18) — the Hebrew of v18 has only לַיהוָה וּלְגִדְעוֹן; "sword" stands in v20 alone. לְ is "for" or "belonging to".
- **"they had but newly set the watch" → "they had only just set up the watchmen"** (v19) — Hebrew אַךְ הָקֵם הֵקִימוּ אֶת־הַשֹּׁמְרִים, the infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "only setting up they had set up the keepers"; with אַךְ "only" its force is "just now", so "surely" is not used here. **"brake the pitchers" → "shattered the jars"** (v19); **"brake" → "broke"** (v20) — two Hebrew verbs: נָפַץ (v19, an infinitive: "and a shattering of the jars") and שָׁבַר (v20). **"in their hand"** — Hebrew singular.
- **"held the lamps in their left hands" → "held fast the torches in their left hand"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיַּחֲזִיקוּ בְ, as v8. **"they cried" → "they called out"** — Hebrew קָרָא. **"The sword of the LORD, and of Gideon" → "A sword for Yahweh and for Gideon"** — Hebrew חֶרֶב לַיהוָה וּלְגִדְעוֹן, without the article and without a verb.
- **"every man in his place"** (v21) — Hebrew תַּחְתָּיו, literally "under himself". **"all the host ran, and cried, and fled" → "all the camp ran, and they shouted and fled"** — Hebrew וַיָּרִיעוּ, "shout" (the war cry or the cry of alarm): of voices here, not of the shofars. The reading tradition has וַיָּנוּסוּ "and they fled"; the written text has ויניסו, which is read "and they put to flight" (then the three hundred shout and drive the camp to flight). The reading tradition is translated.
- **"the three hundred blew the trumpets" → "they blew the three hundred shofars"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיִּתְקְעוּ שְׁלֹשׁ־מֵאוֹת הַשּׁוֹפָרוֹת, here without בְּ; also "the three hundred shofars blew". **"every man's sword against his fellow, even throughout all the host" → "the sword of each man against his neighbor, and in all the camp"** — Hebrew וּבְכָל־הַמַּחֲנֶה "and in all the camp", as it stands. **"to the border of Abel-meholah" → "as far as the edge of Abel-meholah"** — Hebrew שְׂפַת, literally "the lip of": edge or bank. **"in Zererath" → "toward Zererah"** — Hebrew צְרֵרָתָה, the name with the ending of direction.
- **"gathered themselves together" → "were called out"** (v23, 24) — Hebrew וַיִּצָּעֵק, the passive of "cry out", as 6:34, 35 (there with ז). **"the men of Israel"** — Hebrew אִישׁ־יִשְׂרָאֵל, a collective singular, "the man of Israel" (so v8, 14 "man of Israel", v24 "all the men of Ephraim").
- **"Come down against the Midianites, and take before them the waters" → "Come down to meet Midian, and capture the waters against them"** (v24) — Hebrew לִקְרַאת "to meet"; וְלִכְדוּ לָהֶם, literally "capture for them": ahead of them, to their loss. לָכַד "capture" also in v25. **"throughout all mount Ephraim" → "through all the hill country of Ephraim"** — Hebrew הַר אֶפְרַיִם, "the mountain of Ephraim", of a region.
- **"two princes of the Midianites" → "the two princes of Midian"** (v25) — Hebrew שְׂרֵי "princes", the leaders of the host. **"they slew" → "they killed"** — Hebrew הָרַג. **"the rock Oreb … the winepress of Zeeb"** — Hebrew צוּר־עוֹרֵב, יֶקֶב־זְאֵב; the names mean "raven" and "wolf". **"pursued Midian" → "pursued to Midian"** — Hebrew אֶל־מִדְיָן, with "to". **"the heads" → "the head"** — Hebrew singular. **"on the other side Jordan" → "across the Jordan"** — Hebrew מֵעֵבֶר לַיַּרְדֵּן; the same words are used of either bank.

### Notes
- **Gideon's 300** — the people are reduced from thirty-two thousand to three hundred. Two siftings: the fearful return (v3); those who lap are set apart at the water (v4–7). The text gives no reason why the lappers are chosen.
- **"Lest Israel glorify itself"** (v2) — Yahweh reduces the people so that the saving cannot be claimed by Israel's own hand.
- **Dream of the barley loaf** (v13–14) — a loaf of barley bread overturns the tent; the Midianite's neighbor reads it as the sword of Gideon.
- **Shofars, jars, torches** (v16–22) — no weapon is named in the hands of the three hundred; the jars are shattered, the torches shown, the shofars blown around the camp, and Yahweh sets each man's sword against his neighbor.
- **"For Yahweh and for Gideon"** (v18, 20) — the cry joins Gideon's name to Yahweh's.
- **Pursuit to the Jordan** (v24–25) — Ephraim is called out to hold the waters. Two princes are captured: Oreb ("raven") at the rock of Oreb, Zeeb ("wolf") at the winepress of Zeeb.
