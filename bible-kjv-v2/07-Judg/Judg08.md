# Judges 8

[1] And the men of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ said to him, "What is this thing that you have done to us, not to call us when you went to fight against ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧?" And they contended with him strongly.

[2] And he said to them, "What have I done now like you? Are not the gleanings of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ better than the vintage of ⟦abiezer|Aviezer|Abiezer⟧?

[3] Into your hand ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has given the princes of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧, ⟦oreb|Orev|Oreb⟧ and ⟦zeeb|Zeev|Zeeb⟧; and what was I able to do like you?" Then their spirit let go of him, when he spoke this word.

[4] And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ came to the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, passing over, he and the three hundred men who were with him, weary and pursuing.

[5] And he said to the men of ⟦succoth|Sukkot|Succoth⟧, "Give, please, loaves of bread to the people who are at my feet, for they are weary, and I am pursuing after ⟦zebah|Zevach|Zebah⟧ and ⟦zalmunna|Tzalmunna|Zalmunna⟧, the kings of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧."

[6] And the princes of ⟦succoth|Sukkot|Succoth⟧ said, "Is the palm of ⟦zebah|Zevach|Zebah⟧ and ⟦zalmunna|Tzalmunna|Zalmunna⟧ now in your hand, that we should give bread to your host?"

[7] And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ said, "Therefore, when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gives ⟦zebah|Zevach|Zebah⟧ and ⟦zalmunna|Tzalmunna|Zalmunna⟧ into my hand, I will thresh your flesh with the thorns of the wilderness and with the briers."

[8] And he went up from there to Penuel and spoke to them like this; and the men of Penuel answered him as the men of ⟦succoth|Sukkot|Succoth⟧ had answered.

[9] And he said also to the men of Penuel, saying, "When I return in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, I will break down this tower."

[10] And ⟦zebah|Zevach|Zebah⟧ and ⟦zalmunna|Tzalmunna|Zalmunna⟧ were in ⟦karkor|Qarqor|Karkor⟧, and their camps with them, about fifteen thousand, all who remained from all the camp of the sons of the east; and those who had fallen were a hundred and twenty thousand men drawing the sword.

[11] And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ went up by the way of those who abide in tents, east of ⟦nobah|Novach|Nobah⟧ and ⟦jogbehah|Yogbehah|Jogbehah⟧, and he struck the camp, and the camp was in security.

[12] And ⟦zebah|Zevach|Zebah⟧ and ⟦zalmunna|Tzalmunna|Zalmunna⟧ fled, and he pursued after them; and he captured the two kings of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧, ⟦zebah|Zevach|Zebah⟧ and ⟦zalmunna|Tzalmunna|Zalmunna⟧, and all the camp he made tremble.

[13] And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ the son of ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ returned from the battle, from the ascent of ⟦heres|Cheres|Heres⟧.

[14] And he captured a young man of the men of ⟦succoth|Sukkot|Succoth⟧ and asked him; and he wrote down for him the princes of ⟦succoth|Sukkot|Succoth⟧ and its elders, seventy-seven men.

[15] And he came to the men of ⟦succoth|Sukkot|Succoth⟧ and said, "Behold ⟦zebah|Zevach|Zebah⟧ and ⟦zalmunna|Tzalmunna|Zalmunna⟧, about whom you reproached me, saying, 'Is the palm of ⟦zebah|Zevach|Zebah⟧ and ⟦zalmunna|Tzalmunna|Zalmunna⟧ now in your hand, that we should give bread to your men who are weary?'"

[16] And he took the elders of the city, and the thorns of the wilderness and the briers, and with them he made the men of ⟦succoth|Sukkot|Succoth⟧ know.

[17] And the tower of Penuel he broke down, and he killed the men of the city.

[18] And he said to ⟦zebah|Zevach|Zebah⟧ and to ⟦zalmunna|Tzalmunna|Zalmunna⟧, "Where are the men whom you killed at ⟦tabor|Tavor|Tabor⟧?" And they said, "As you are, so were they: each one like the form of the sons of the king."

[19] And he said, "They were my brothers, the sons of my mother. As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives, if you had kept them alive, I would not kill you."

[20] And he said to ⟦jether|Yeter|Jether⟧ his firstborn, "Arise, kill them." But the boy did not draw his sword, for he was afraid, for he was still a boy.

[21] And ⟦zebah|Zevach|Zebah⟧ and ⟦zalmunna|Tzalmunna|Zalmunna⟧ said, "Arise, you, and fall upon us, for as the man is, so is his might." And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ arose and killed ⟦zebah|Zevach|Zebah⟧ and ⟦zalmunna|Tzalmunna|Zalmunna⟧, and he took the crescents that were on the necks of their camels.

[22] And the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said to ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧, "Rule over us, both you and your son and your son's son, for you have saved us from the hand of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧."

[23] And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ said to them, "I will not rule over you, and my son will not rule over you; ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will rule over you."

[24] And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ said to them, "Let me ask a request of you: give me, each man, the ring of his spoil." For they had rings of gold, for they were ⟦ishmaelites|Yishmeelim|Ishmaelites⟧.

[25] And they said, "We will surely give." And they spread out the cloak, and they threw there, each man, the ring of his spoil.

[26] And the weight of the rings of gold that he asked was one thousand seven hundred of gold, apart from the crescents and the pendants and the garments of purple that were on the kings of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧, and apart from the collars that were on the necks of their camels.

[27] And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ made it into an ephod and set it in his city, in ⟦ophrah|Ofrah|Ophrah⟧; and all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ played the prostitute after it there, and it became a snare to ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ and to his house.

[28] And ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ was brought low before the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they did not again lift up their head. And the land was quiet forty years in the days of ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧.

[29] And ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧ the son of ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ went and dwelt in his house.

[30] And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ had seventy sons who came out of his thigh, for he had many wives.

[31] And his concubine who was in Shechem, she also bore him a son, and he set his name ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧.

[32] And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ the son of ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ died in a good old age, and he was buried in the grave of ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ his father, in ⟦ophrah|Ofrah|Ophrah⟧ of the ⟦abiezrites|Aviezri|Abiezrite⟧.

[33] And it came to pass, when ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ was dead, that the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ turned back and played the prostitute after the Baals, and they set for themselves ⟦baal-berith|Baal Berit|Baal-berith⟧ as a god.

[34] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ did not remember ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who delivered them from the hand of all their enemies all around.

[35] And they did not do ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ with the house of ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧, ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧, according to all the good that he had done with ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 34) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v33 the same word is "a god", of Baal-berith.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Why hast thou served us thus, that thou calledst us not" → "What is this thing that you have done to us, not to call us"** (v1) — Hebrew מָה־הַדָּבָר הַזֶּה עָשִׂיתָ לָּנוּ לְבִלְתִּי קְרֹאות לָנוּ. **"the men of Ephraim"** — Hebrew אִישׁ אֶפְרַיִם, a collective singular (so "the men of Israel", v22). **"did chide with him sharply" → "contended with him strongly"** — Hebrew וַיְרִיבוּן … בְּחָזְקָה, "with strength"; רִיב is the verb of the name Jerubbaal (6:31, 32).
- **"What have I done now in comparison of you?" → "What have I done now like you?"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew כָּכֶם "like you". **"the gleaning of the grapes of Ephraim" → "the gleanings of Ephraim"** — Hebrew עֹלְלוֹת, what is left on the vine after the picking; "grapes" is not written. בָּצִיר "vintage".
- **"Then their anger was abated toward him" → "Then their spirit let go of him"** (v3) — Hebrew רָפְתָה רוּחָם מֵעָלָיו, literally "their spirit sank / slackened from upon him"; רוּחַ "spirit" here of their temper. **"when he had said that" → "when he spoke this word"** — Hebrew בְּדַבְּרוֹ הַדָּבָר הַזֶּה.
- **"and passed over" → "passing over"** (v4) — Hebrew עֹבֵר, a participle. **"faint, yet pursuing" → "weary and pursuing"** (v4, 5, 15) — Hebrew עֲיֵפִים וְרֹדְפִים; "yet" is not written. Some read from the Greek "weary and hungry" (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"the people that follow me" → "the people who are at my feet"** (v5) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר בְּרַגְלָי. **"loaves of bread"** — Hebrew כִּכְּרוֹת לֶחֶם, literally "rounds of bread".
- **"Are the hands of Zebah and Zalmunna now in thine hand" → "Is the palm of Zebah and Zalmunna now in your hand"** (v6, 15) — Hebrew הֲכַף, singular: "palm", beside יָד "hand". The verb "said" is singular in the Hebrew, with the plural subject "the princes". **"thine army" → "your host"** — Hebrew צְבָאֲךָ.
- **"I will tear your flesh" → "I will thresh your flesh"** (v7) — Hebrew וְדַשְׁתִּי, the verb of threshing grain. **"with the thorns of the wilderness and with briers"** (v7, 16) — Hebrew אֶת "with" (together with); בַּרְקֳנִים is found only in these two verses: a thorny plant, the kind is not known.
- **"spake unto them likewise" → "spoke to them like this"** (v8) — Hebrew כָּזֹאת.
- **"When I come again in peace" → "When I return in *shalom*"** (v9) — Hebrew בְּשָׁלוֹם: whole, unharmed. **"break down"** (v9, 17) — Hebrew נָתַץ, the verb of 6:28–32.
- **"their hosts" → "their camps"** (v10–12) — Hebrew מַחֲנֶה "camp", as in chapter 7. **"in Karkor"** — Hebrew בַּקַּרְקֹר, with the article. **"about fifteen thousand men"** — "men" is not written here. **"for there fell" → "and those who had fallen were"** — Hebrew וְהַנֹּפְלִים, a participle. **Numbers** — in ordinary English order: "a hundred and twenty thousand", "seventy-seven" (v14), "one thousand seven hundred" (v26).
- **"them that dwelt in tents" → "those who abide in tents"** (v11) — Hebrew הַשְּׁכוּנֵי בָאֳהָלִים, from שָׁכַן "abide". **"on the east of"** — Hebrew מִקֶּדֶם לְ, literally "from the front of". **"for the host was secure" → "and the camp was in security"** — Hebrew וְהַמַּחֲנֶה הָיָה בֶטַח, a noun: unsuspecting.
- **"discomfited all the host" → "all the camp he made tremble"** (v12) — Hebrew הֶחֱרִיד, the causative of חָרַד "tremble" (7:3).
- **"before the sun was up" → "from the ascent of Heres"** (v13) — Hebrew מִלְמַעֲלֵה הֶחָרֶס, literally "from the ascent of the *cheres*". חֶרֶס is a rare word for "sun" (Job 9:7; cf. 14:18 MT), so the words can also be read "from above the sun", "before the sun rose"; the verse takes it as the name of a pass (cf. Har-heres, 1:35). Uncertain.
- **"a young man"** (v14); **"the youth" → "the boy"** (v20) — one Hebrew word, נַעַר, of the man of Succoth and of Gideon's firstborn. **"enquired of him" → "asked him"** — Hebrew שָׁאַל. **"he described unto him" → "he wrote down for him"** — Hebrew וַיִּכְתֹּב אֵלָיו, "wrote to him". **"threescore and seventeen" → "seventy-seven"**.
- **"with whom ye did upbraid me" → "about whom you reproached me"** (v15) — Hebrew חֵרַפְתֶּם אוֹתִי.
- **"with them he taught the men of Succoth" → "with them he made the men of Succoth know"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיֹּדַע בָּהֶם, as the Masoretic Text is printed: the causative of יָדַע "know" (the form is unusual). The Greek and Latin versions have "threshed", the verb of v7 (וַיָּדָשׁ, a difference of one letter; from memory), and many follow them. The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"beat down the tower" → "broke down"**; **"slew" → "killed"** (v17, 18–21) — Hebrew נָתַץ; הָרַג.
- **"What manner of men were they" → "Where are the men"** (v18) — Hebrew אֵיפֹה, "where"; "what manner of" is not what the word means elsewhere. The answer does not say where. **"As thou art, so were they; each one resembled the children of a king" → "As you are, so were they: each one like the form of the sons of the king"** — Hebrew כָּמוֹךָ כְמוֹהֶם אֶחָד כְּתֹאַר בְּנֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ, literally "like you, like them; one, like the form of the sons of the king"; תֹּאַר is the form of the body; "the king" has the article.
- **"if ye had saved them alive, I would not slay you" → "if you had kept them alive, I would not kill you"** (v19) — Hebrew לוּ הַחֲיִתֶם … לֹא הָרַגְתִּי. **"As the LORD liveth"** — Hebrew חַי־יְהוָה, the oath.
- **"Up, and slay them" → "Arise, kill them"** (v20, 21) — Hebrew קוּם. **"Rise thou, and fall upon us"** (v21) — Hebrew פְּגַע־בָּנוּ; פָּגַע is "meet, come upon", of an attack "fall upon". **"for as the man is, so is his strength" → "so is his might"** — Hebrew כִּי כָאִישׁ גְּבוּרָתוֹ, literally "for as the man, his might"; גְּבוּרָה is of one root with גִּבּוֹר "mighty one" (6:12).
- **"ornaments" → "crescents"** (v21, 26) — Hebrew שַׂהֲרֹנִים, ornaments in the shape of the moon (Isa 3:18).
- **"both thou, and thy son, and thy son's son also"** (v22) — Hebrew גַּם three times. **"thou hast delivered us" → "you have saved us"** — Hebrew הוֹשַׁעְתָּנוּ, the verb of 6:14, 15; 7:2, 7. **"Rule"** (v22, 23) — Hebrew מָשַׁל; the word "king" is not used.
- **"I would desire a request of you, that ye would give me every man the earrings of his prey" → "Let me ask a request of you: give me, each man, the ring of his spoil"** (v24, 25) — Hebrew אֶשְׁאֲלָה … שְׁאֵלָה, verb and noun of one root; נֶזֶם is a ring of the nose or the ear (the word says only "ring"), singular; שָׁלָל "spoil". **"because they were Ishmaelites"** — the name is used here of the men of Midian (as Gen 37:25–28).
- **"We will willingly give them" → "We will surely give"** (v25) — Hebrew נָתוֹן נִתֵּן, the infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "giving we will give". **"a garment" → "the cloak"** — Hebrew הַשִּׂמְלָה, with the article.
- **"a thousand and seven hundred shekels of gold" → "one thousand seven hundred of gold"** (v26) — "shekels" is not in the Hebrew. **"beside ornaments, and collars, and purple raiment … and beside the chains" → "apart from the crescents and the pendants and the garments of purple … and apart from the collars"** — Hebrew נְטִפוֹת, literally "drops": ornaments that hang like drops; עֲנָקוֹת, neck ornaments.
- **"made an ephod thereof" → "made it into an ephod"** (v27) — Hebrew לְאֵפוֹד. The ephod is the priestly garment of Exod 28; here it is a thing set up in a town, and the text does not say what it looked like. **"put it in his city" → "set it"** — Hebrew וַיַּצֵּג, the verb of 6:37. **"went thither a whoring after it" → "played the prostitute after it there"** (v27, 33) — Hebrew זָנָה אַחֲרֵי. **"house"** — Hebrew בַּיִת.
- **"Thus was Midian subdued" → "And Midian was brought low"** (v28) — Hebrew וַיִּכָּנַע; the frame sentence of the book (3:30; 4:23; 11:33). **"so that they lifted up their heads no more" → "and they did not again lift up their head"** — Hebrew וְלֹא יָסְפוּ לָשֵׂאת רֹאשָׁם, literally "they did not add to lift up their head". **"the country was in quietness" → "the land was quiet"** — Hebrew וַתִּשְׁקֹט הָאָרֶץ, the frame sentence (3:11, 30; 5:31).
- **"threescore and ten sons of his body begotten" → "seventy sons who came out of his thigh"** (v30) — Hebrew יֹצְאֵי יְרֵכוֹ: his own offspring.
- **"whose name he called Abimelech" → "and he set his name Abimelech"** (v31) — Hebrew וַיָּשֶׂם אֶת־שְׁמוֹ, the verb "put, set", not the usual "called his name". The name means "my father is king".
- **"sepulchre" → "grave"** (v32) — Hebrew קֶבֶר. **"a good old age"** — Hebrew שֵׂיבָה טוֹבָה, literally "good gray hair". **"Ophrah of the Abiezrites" → "of the Abiezrite"** — Hebrew singular, as 6:11, 24.
- **"turned again, and went a whoring after Baalim" → "turned back and played the prostitute after the Baals"** (v33) — Hebrew וַיָּשׁוּבוּ … וַיִּזְנוּ: "turned back", or "again played the prostitute". הַבְּעָלִים, plural with the article. **"made Baal-berith their god" → "set for themselves Baal-berith as a god"** — Hebrew וַיָּשִׂימוּ לָהֶם בַּעַל בְּרִית לֵאלֹהִים; בַּעַל בְּרִית is "lord of covenant", "Baal of the covenant" (9:4; in 9:46 "El-berith").
- **"who had delivered them out of the hands of all their enemies on every side" → "who delivered them from the hand of all their enemies all around"** (v34) — Hebrew הַמַּצִּיל, a participle of הִצִּיל "deliver" (not the "save" of v22); יָד singular.
- **"Neither shewed they kindness to the house of Jerubbaal, namely, Gideon, according to all the goodness which he had shewed unto Israel" → "And they did not do *chesed* with the house of Jerubbaal, Gideon, according to all the good that he had done with Israel"** (v35) — Hebrew עָשׂוּ חֶסֶד עִם; חֶסֶד is loyal love, faithfulness to a bond. יְרֻבַּעַל גִּדְעוֹן, the two names side by side; "namely" is not written. הַטּוֹבָה "the good".

### Notes
- **Ephraim's complaint** (v1–3) — Ephraim contends with Gideon as later with Jephthah (12:1–6). Gideon answers with the saying about the gleanings and the vintage.
- **Succoth and Penuel** (v5–17) — towns across the Jordan that refuse bread to the pursuers; Gideon does to them on his return what he said.
- **Zebah and Zalmunna** — the two kings of Midian. The names as pointed can be heard as "sacrifice" (*zevach*) and "shade withheld".
- **The brothers at Tabor** (v18–21) — only here does the reader learn that the kings had killed Gideon's brothers.
- **Rule refused** (v22–23) — "Yahweh will rule over you." What follows (the gold, the ephod, many wives, a son named "my father is king") stands beside the refusal without comment.
- **The ephod** (v24–27) — made of the rings of the spoil and set in Ophrah; Israel "played the prostitute after it", and it became a snare.
- **Baal-berith** (v33) — "lord of covenant", the god with a house in Shechem (9:4).
- **Seventy sons** (v30) — prepares chapter 9.
