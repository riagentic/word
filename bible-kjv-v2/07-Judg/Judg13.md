## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"angel" → "Messenger"** (v3, 6, 13, 15, 16, 17, 18, 20, 21) — *mal'akh*.
- **"terrible" → "awesome"** (v6) — Hebrew *nora*.
- **"secret" → "wonderful"** (v18) — Hebrew פֶּלִאי *peli'i*. "Beyond understanding" — the same root as Isa 9:6 "*Wonderful* Counselor."
- **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** (v19, 23).
- **"Samson" → "Shimshon"** (v24) — Hebrew שִׁמְשׁוֹן *Shimshon*; the name is given once, without the added gloss "— Samson."

### Notes
- **Samson's annunciation** — parallels the pattern of barren women bearing deliverers: Sarah/Isaac, Rachel/Joseph, Hannah/Samuel, Elizabeth/John, Mary/Jesus.
- **Nazirite from birth** — Samson's Nazirite status is lifelong, not temporary (contrast Num 6). No haircuts, no wine-products, no corpse-contact.
- **Name "Wonderful"** (v18) — the Messenger's name is *peli* — secret / wonderful. Same root as Isa 9:6's "Wonderful Counselor." Christian tradition reads this as christophanic.
- **Theophany via fire** (v20) — the Messenger ascends in the altar flame. Parallels Yahweh's appearance to Moses at the burning bush, and on Sinai.
- **Manoah's wife** — wiser than her husband. She reassures him that they will not die. The unnamed wife models faith.
- **Mahaneh-dan** (v25) — "the camp of Dan." A Danite encampment area before Dan's migration north (ch 18).
