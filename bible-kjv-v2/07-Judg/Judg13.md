# Judges 13

[1] The children of Israel again did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, and Yahweh gave them into the hand of the Philistines for forty years.

[2] There was a certain man of Zorah, of the clan of the Danites, whose name was Manoah; his wife was barren and had borne no children.

[3] The Messenger of Yahweh appeared to the woman and said to her, "Behold, you are barren and have borne no children, but you shall conceive and bear a son.

[4] Now therefore take care; drink no wine or strong drink, and eat nothing unclean.

[5] For behold, you shall conceive and bear a son; no razor shall come upon his head, for the child shall be a Nazirite to *Elohim* from the womb. He shall begin to deliver Israel out of the hand of the Philistines."

[6] The woman came and told her husband, "A man of *Elohim* came to me, and his appearance was like the appearance of a Messenger of *Elohim* — very awesome. I did not ask him where he was from, and he did not tell me his name.

[7] He said to me, 'Behold, you shall conceive and bear a son; so now drink no wine or strong drink, and eat nothing unclean, for the child shall be a Nazirite to *Elohim* from the womb to the day of his death.'"

[8] Then Manoah entreated Yahweh, saying, "O Lord, please let the man of *Elohim* whom you sent come again to us and teach us what we shall do with the boy who will be born."

[9] *Elohim* listened to the voice of Manoah; the Messenger of *Elohim* came again to the woman as she sat in the field, but Manoah her husband was not with her.

[10] The woman ran quickly and told her husband, "Behold, the man who came to me the other day has appeared to me."

[11] Manoah arose and went after his wife and came to the man and said to him, "Are you the man who spoke with the woman?" He said, "I am."

[12] Manoah said, "Now when your words come true, what shall be the rule for the boy, and what is to be his doing?"

[13] The Messenger of Yahweh said to Manoah, "From all that I said to the woman, let her beware.

[14] She shall not eat anything that comes from the grapevine; she shall not drink wine or strong drink, or eat anything unclean. All that I commanded her let her observe."

[15] Manoah said to the Messenger of Yahweh, "Please let us detain you and prepare a young goat for you."

[16] The Messenger of Yahweh said to Manoah, "Though you detain me, I will not eat of your food; but if you prepare a burnt offering, you must offer it to Yahweh." For Manoah did not know that he was the Messenger of Yahweh.

[17] Manoah said to the Messenger of Yahweh, "What is your name, so that when your words come to pass, we may honor you?"

[18] The Messenger of Yahweh said to him, "Why do you ask my name? It is wonderful."

[19] So Manoah took the young goat with the grain offering and offered it on the rock to Yahweh, and the Messenger worked wonders while Manoah and his wife looked on.

[20] As the flame went up toward the heavens from the altar, the Messenger of Yahweh ascended in the flame of the altar. Manoah and his wife looked on, and they fell on their faces to the ground.

[21] The Messenger of Yahweh did not appear again to Manoah and his wife. Then Manoah knew that he had been the Messenger of Yahweh.

[22] Manoah said to his wife, "We shall surely die, for we have seen *Elohim*."

[23] But his wife said to him, "If Yahweh had meant to kill us, he would not have accepted a burnt offering and grain offering from our hands, nor would he have shown us all these things, nor let us hear such things as these at this time."

[24] The woman bore a son and called his name Shimshon — Samson. The boy grew, and Yahweh blessed him.

[25] The spirit of Yahweh began to stir him at Mahaneh-dan, between Zorah and Eshtaol.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"angel" → "Messenger"** (v3, 6, 13, 15, 16, 17, 18, 20, 21) — *mal'akh*.
- **"terrible" → "awesome"** (v6) — Hebrew *nora*.
- **"secret" → "wonderful"** (v18) — Hebrew פֶּלִאי *peli'i*. "Beyond understanding" — the same root as Isa 9:6 "*Wonderful* Counselor."
- **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** (v19, 23).

### Notes
- **Samson's annunciation** — parallels the pattern of barren women bearing deliverers: Sarah/Isaac, Rachel/Joseph, Hannah/Samuel, Elizabeth/John, Mary/Jesus.
- **Nazirite from birth** — Samson's Nazirite status is lifelong, not temporary (contrast Num 6). No haircuts, no wine-products, no corpse-contact.
- **Name "Wonderful"** (v18) — the Messenger's name is *peli* — secret / wonderful. Same root as Isa 9:6's "Wonderful Counselor." Christian tradition reads this as christophanic.
- **Theophany via fire** (v20) — the Messenger ascends in the altar flame. Parallels Yahweh's appearance to Moses at the burning bush, and on Sinai.
- **Manoah's wife** — wiser than her husband. She reassures him that they will not die. The unnamed wife models faith.
- **Mahaneh-dan** (v25) — "the camp of Dan." A Danite encampment area before Dan's migration north (ch 18).
