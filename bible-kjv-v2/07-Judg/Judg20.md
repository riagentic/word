# Judges 20

[1] And all the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went out, and the congregation assembled as one man, from Dan and as far as ⟦beersheba|Beer Sheva|Beersheba⟧, and the land of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧, to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ at ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧.

[2] And the chiefs of all the people, all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, took their stand in the assembly of the people of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: four hundred thousand men on foot, drawing the sword.

[3] And the sons of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ heard that the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had gone up to ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧. And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said, "Speak: how did this evil come to be?"

[4] And the man, the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧, the husband of the woman who was murdered, answered and said, "To ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧, which belongs to ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, I came, I and my concubine, to lodge.

[5] And the lords of ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧ rose against me and surrounded the house against me by night; me they intended to kill, and my concubine they humbled, and she died.

[6] And I took hold of my concubine and cut her in pieces and sent her into all the field of the inheritance of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, for they have done depravity and an outrage in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[7] Behold, all of you are sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; provide for yourselves a word and counsel here."

[8] And all the people arose as one man, saying, "We will not go, any man to his tent, and we will not turn aside, any man to his house.

[9] And now, this is the thing that we will do to ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧: against it by lot.

[10] And we will take ten men of a hundred, of all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and a hundred of a thousand, and a thousand of ten thousand, to take provisions for the people, to do, at their coming to ⟦geba|Geva|Geba⟧ of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, according to all the outrage that it has done in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[11] And all the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were gathered to the city, as one man, companions.

[12] And the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ sent men through all the tribes of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, saying, "What is this evil that has come to be among you?

[13] And now, give the men, the ⟦belial|sons of Beliyyaal|sons of worthlessness⟧, who are in ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧, and let us put them to death and purge evil from ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧." But the sons of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ were not willing to listen to the voice of their brothers, the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[14] And the sons of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ were gathered from the cities to ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧, to go out to battle with the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[15] And the sons of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ were numbered on that day from the cities: twenty-six thousand men drawing the sword, apart from the inhabitants of ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧, who were numbered: seven hundred chosen men.

[16] Out of all this people there were seven hundred chosen men, left-handed; every one of these could sling a stone at a hair and not miss.

[17] And the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were numbered, apart from ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧: four hundred thousand men drawing the sword; every one of these was a man of war.

[18] And they arose and went up to ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧ and asked of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said, "Who shall go up for us first to the battle with the sons of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧?" And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said, "⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ first."

[19] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ arose in the morning and camped against ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧.

[20] And the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went out to battle with ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, and the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ drew up for battle with them at ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧.

[21] And the sons of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ came out from ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧, and they destroyed in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ on that day twenty-two thousand men, to the ground.

[22] And the people, the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, strengthened themselves, and they again drew up for battle in the place where they had drawn up on the first day.

[23] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went up and wept before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ until the evening, and they asked of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying, "Shall I again draw near to the battle with the sons of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ my brother?" And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said, "Go up against him."

[24] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ came near to the sons of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ on the second day.

[25] And ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ went out to meet them from ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧ on the second day, and they destroyed among the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ yet eighteen thousand men, to the ground; all these were drawers of the sword.

[26] And all the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and all the people went up and came to ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧, and they wept and sat there before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and fasted on that day until the evening; and they offered up burnt offerings and peace offerings before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[27] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ asked of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ — and the ark of the covenant of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was there in those days,

[28] and ⟦phinehas|Pinchas|Phinehas⟧ the son of ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧, the son of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, was standing before it in those days — saying, "Shall I yet again go out to the battle with the sons of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ my brother, or shall I cease?" And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said, "Go up, for tomorrow I will give him into your hand."

[29] And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ set men in ambush against ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧ all around.

[30] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went up against the sons of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ on the third day, and they drew up against ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧ as at the other times.

[31] And the sons of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ went out to meet the people; they were drawn out from the city, and they began to strike down some of the people, slain, as at the other times, on the highways, of which one goes up to ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧ and one to ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧, in the field: about thirty men in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[32] And the sons of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ said, "They are smitten before us as at the first." But the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had said, "Let us flee and draw him out from the city to the highways."

[33] And all the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ rose from their place and drew up at ⟦baal-tamar|Baal Tamar|Baal-tamar⟧; and the ambush of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ was bursting out from its place, from the open ground of ⟦geba|Geva|Geba⟧.

[34] And ten thousand chosen men out of all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ came from opposite ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧, and the battle was heavy; and they did not know that the evil was touching them.

[35] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ smote ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ before ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ destroyed in ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ on that day twenty-five thousand one hundred men; all these were drawing the sword.

[36] And the sons of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ saw that they were smitten. And the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ gave place to ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, for they trusted in the ambush that they had set against ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧.

[37] And the ambush hastened and made a raid on ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧; and the ambush drew on and struck all the city with the edge of the sword.

[38] And the appointment that the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had with the ambush was that they should make a great rising of smoke go up from the city.

[39] And the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ turned about in the battle; and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ had begun to strike down slain among the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, about thirty men, for they said, "Surely he is smitten before us, as in the first battle."

[40] And the rising began to go up from the city, a pillar of smoke; and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ turned behind him, and behold, the whole of the city went up toward the heavens.

[41] And the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ turned about, and the men of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ were dismayed, for they saw that the evil had touched them.

[42] And they turned before the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ toward the way of the wilderness, and the battle caught up with him; and those who were from the cities were destroying him in his midst.

[43] They encircled ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, they chased him, at the resting place they trod him down, as far as opposite ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧, on the east.

[44] And there fell of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ eighteen thousand men; all these were men of ability.

[45] And they turned and fled toward the wilderness, to the crag of Rimmon; and they gleaned him on the highways, five thousand men; and they kept close after him as far as Gidom and struck down of him two thousand men.

[46] And all who fell of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ were twenty-five thousand men drawing the sword on that day; all these were men of ability.

[47] And six hundred men turned and fled toward the wilderness, to the crag of Rimmon, and they dwelt at the crag of Rimmon four months.

[48] And the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ returned to the sons of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ and struck them with the edge of the sword, from the city in its entirety to beast, to all that was found; also all the cities that were found they set on fire.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton kept.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 18, 27) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v2 and v27 with the article (הָאֱלֹהִים).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"children of Israel / of Benjamin" → "sons of"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי. **"the men of Israel"** (v11, 17, 20, 22, 33, 36, 38, 39, 41, 42, 48) is אִישׁ יִשְׂרָאֵל, a singular used of the whole body ("the man of Israel"); so also "the men of Benjamin" (v41). Benjamin is often spoken of as one man ("him", v23, 28, 32, 39, 42–45): kept where English can bear it.
- **"was gathered together" → "assembled"** (v1) — Hebrew וַתִּקָּהֵל, the verb of קָהָל "assembly" (v2). **"from Dan even to Beersheba, with the land of Gilead" → "from Dan and as far as Beersheba, and the land of Gilead"** — Hebrew וְאֶרֶץ הַגִּלְעָד "and the land of the Gilead". **"in Mizpeh" → "at Mizpah"** (v1, 3) — Hebrew הַמִּצְפָּה, with the article: "the watchtower".
- **"the chief of all the people" → "the chiefs of all the people"** (v2) — Hebrew פִּנּוֹת, literally "the corners" (the corner-stones: the leading men; so 1 Sam 14:38; Isa 19:13). **"presented themselves" → "took their stand"** — Hebrew הִתְיַצֵּב. **"footmen that drew sword" → "men on foot, drawing the sword"** — Hebrew אִישׁ רַגְלִי שֹׁלֵף חָרֶב.
- **Numbers** (v2, 15–17, 21, 25, 34, 35, 44–47) — written in words in the ordinary English order; each figure is as the Hebrew has it: 400,000 (v2, 17); 26,000 and 700 (v15); 700 (v16); 22,000 (v21); 18,000 (v25); 10,000 (v34); 25,100 (v35); 18,000 (v44); 5,000 and 2,000 (v45); 25,000 (v46); 600 (v47). The figures of v15 (26,700) and of v35 / v44–47 (25,100; 18,000 + 5,000 + 2,000 = 25,000; 600 left) do not add up to one another; nothing is adjusted.
- **"Tell us, how was this wickedness?" → "Speak: how did this evil come to be?"** (v3, 12) — Hebrew דַּבְּרוּ "speak" (plural; no "us"); נִהְיְתָה is the verb "be" in the niphal, as 19:30; רָעָה is "evil".
- **"the woman that was slain" → "the woman who was murdered"** (v4) — Hebrew הַנִּרְצָחָה, the verb רָצַח "murder".
- **"the men of Gibeah" → "the lords of Gibeah"** (v5) — Hebrew בַּעֲלֵי הַגִּבְעָה, the town's men of standing (so Josh 24:11; Judg 9). **"thought to have slain me" → "me they intended to kill"** — Hebrew דִּמּוּ לַהֲרֹג; "me" stands first. Chapter 19:22 tells what the men asked for in other words.
- **"my concubine have they forced, that she is dead" → "my concubine they humbled, and she died"** (v5) — Hebrew עִנּוּ, the verb עִנָּה (piel), literally "afflict, bring low", as 19:24; וַתָּמֹת "and she died".
- **"took" → "took hold of"** (v6) — Hebrew וָאֹחֵז, the verb אָחַז; 19:25, 29 have another verb (הֶחֱזִיק) with the same English. **"throughout all the country of the inheritance of Israel" → "into all the field of the inheritance of Israel"** — Hebrew שְׂדֵה "field".
- **"lewdness and folly" → "depravity and an outrage"** (v6, 10) — Hebrew זִמָּה (a shameful design, Lev 18:17) and נְבָלָה, as 19:23.
- **"give here your advice and counsel" → "provide for yourselves a word and counsel here"** (v7) — Hebrew הָבוּ לָכֶם דָּבָר וְעֵצָה, as Deut 1:13; Josh 18:4.
- **"We will not any of us go to his tent" → "We will not go, any man to his tent"** (v8) — Hebrew לֹא נֵלֵךְ אִישׁ לְאָהֳלוֹ, literally "we will not go, a man to his tent".
- **"we will go up by lot against it" → "against it by lot"** (v9) — Hebrew עָלֶיהָ בְּגוֹרָל; the Hebrew has no verb. The Septuagint is commonly cited as having "we will go up" (from memory).
- **"to fetch victual" → "to take provisions"** (v10) — Hebrew צֵדָה. **"when they come to Gibeah of Benjamin" → "at their coming to Geba of Benjamin"** — the Masoretic Text has לְגֶבַע "to Geba", another town of Benjamin (Josh 18:24), where "Gibeah" is expected; the printed text is translated (so also v33). "To do … according to all the outrage that it has done": the verb is singular (the town, or Benjamin).
- **"knit together as one man" → "as one man, companions"** (v11) — Hebrew חֲבֵרִים "companions, joined ones".
- **"through all the tribe of Benjamin" → "through all the tribes of Benjamin"** (v12) — Hebrew שִׁבְטֵי, plural (its clans are meant; so 1 Sam 9:21).
- **"deliver us the men, the children of Belial" → "give the men, the sons of Beliyyaal"** (v13) — Hebrew תְּנוּ "give"; בְּנֵי־בְלִיַּעַל as 19:22. **"put away evil" → "purge evil"** — Hebrew וּנְבַעֲרָה רָעָה, the verb of Deut 13:5; 17:7. **"the children of Benjamin"** — the word בְּנֵי "sons of" is read (qere) but not written; the written text has "Benjamin were not willing".
- **"hearken to" → "listen to the voice of"; "would not" → "were not willing"** (v13) — Hebrew לֹא אָבוּ … לִשְׁמֹעַ בְּקוֹל.
- **"were numbered" kept; "beside" → "apart from"** (v15, 17) — Hebrew הִתְפָּקְדוּ and לְבַד מִן.
- **"lefthanded" → "left-handed"** (v16) — Hebrew אִטֵּר יַד־יְמִינוֹ, literally "bound (hindered) as to his right hand", as 3:15. **"could sling stones at an hair breadth, and not miss" → "could sling a stone at a hair and not miss"** — Hebrew קֹלֵעַ בָּאֶבֶן אֶל־הַשַּׂעֲרָה וְלֹא יַחֲטִא, literally "slinging with the stone at the hair, and he does not miss"; "miss" is the verb חָטָא, elsewhere "sin".
- **"the house of God" → "Bethel"** (v18, 26, 31) — Hebrew בֵּית־אֵל, the name of the town; the words also say "the house of El". **"asked counsel of God" → "asked of Elohim"** (v18) / **"of the LORD" → "of Yahweh"** (v23, 27) — Hebrew שָׁאַל בְּ "ask of", as 1:1. The first asking names *Elohim* and the answer comes from Yahweh; the second and third name Yahweh.
- **"Which of us shall go up first" → "Who shall go up for us first"** (v18) — Hebrew מִי יַעֲלֶה־לָּנוּ בַתְּחִלָּה, the words of 1:1.
- **"put themselves in array to fight against them" → "drew up for battle with them"** (v20, 22, 30, 33) — Hebrew עָרַךְ מִלְחָמָה, literally "arrange battle", as Gen 14:8.
- **"destroyed down to the ground" → "destroyed … to the ground"** (v21, 25, 35, 42) — Hebrew הִשְׁחִית, the verb "ruin" of Gen 6:13; "ruined" is not English of men cut down, so "destroyed" stands; אַרְצָה "to the ground".
- **"encouraged themselves" → "strengthened themselves"** (v22) — Hebrew הִתְחַזֵּק. **"set their battle again in array" → "again drew up for battle"** — literally "added to arrange".
- **"Shall I go up again … my brother" kept in the singular** (v23, 28) — Hebrew הַאוֹסִיף … אָחִי: Israel speaks as one man. "Go up against him": Hebrew אֵלָיו "to him".
- **"went forth against them" → "went out to meet them"** (v25, 31) — Hebrew לִקְרָאתָם. **"all these drew the sword" → "all these were drawers of the sword"**.
- **"enquired of the LORD" → "asked of Yahweh"** (v27) — the same verb as v18, 23. **"stood before it"** (v28) — Hebrew לְפָנָיו "before it" (the ark) or "before him" (Yahweh).
- **"I will deliver them into thine hand" → "I will give him into your hand"** (v28) — Hebrew אֶתְּנֶנּוּ, singular.
- **"liers in wait" → "men in ambush" / "the ambush"** (v29, 33, 36–38) — Hebrew אֹרְבִים (plural, v29) and הָאֹרֵב (singular, of the whole body), as Josh 8.
- **"as at other times" → "as at the other times"** (v30, 31) — Hebrew כְּפַעַם בְּפַעַם, literally "as time by time".
- **"were drawn away from the city" → "were drawn out from the city"** (v31, 32) — Hebrew הָנְתְּקוּ and וּנְתַקְּנֻהוּ, one verb (נָתַק "pull off"), as Josh 8:16. **"began to smite of the people, and kill" → "began to strike down some of the people, slain"** (v31, 39) — Hebrew לְהַכּוֹת … חֲלָלִים, literally "to strike … pierced ones". **"the other to Gibeah in the field"** — the Masoretic Text has גִּבְעָתָה "to Gibeah"; "Gibeon" is a common conjecture and is not followed.
- **"smitten down" → "smitten"** (v32, 35, 36, 39) — Hebrew נָגַף, the verb of a blow from God (Exod 12:23); in v35 Yahweh is its subject. In v39 the verb stands twice (נִגּוֹף נִגָּף, the infinitive absolute with its verb): "surely he is smitten".
- **"came forth out of their places, even out of the meadows of Gibeah" → "was bursting out from its place, from the open ground of Geba"** (v33) — Hebrew מִמַּעֲרֵה־גָבַע: מַעֲרֶה is found only here and its meaning is uncertain ("bare, open place"); the old versions are commonly cited as reading "west of" (from memory). The name is Geba in the Masoretic Text.
- **"came against Gibeah" → "came from opposite Gibeah"** (v34) — Hebrew מִנֶּגֶד לַגִּבְעָה. **"the battle was sore" → "the battle was heavy"** — Hebrew כָּבֵדָה. **"that evil was near them" → "that the evil was touching them"** (v34, 41) — Hebrew נֹגַעַת עֲלֵיהֶם הָרָעָה, literally "the evil was touching upon them".
- **"gave place to the Benjamites" → "gave place to Benjamin"** (v36) — Hebrew וַיִּתְּנוּ … מָקוֹם.
- **"hasted, and rushed upon Gibeah; and the liers in wait drew themselves along" → "hastened and made a raid on Gibeah; and the ambush drew on"** (v37) — Hebrew וַיִּפְשְׁטוּ (פָּשַׁט "strip off; make a raid") and וַיִּמְשֹׁךְ (מָשַׁךְ "draw, pull"): the ambush moved on, or spread out; which is not certain.
- **"there was an appointed sign … that they should make a great flame with smoke rise up" → "the appointment … was that they should make a great rising of smoke go up"** (v38) — Hebrew וְהַמּוֹעֵד "the appointment" (the thing agreed; the word of "appointed time"); מַשְׂאַת הֶעָשָׁן is literally "the lifting up of the smoke" (v40 "the rising"). Before לְהַעֲלוֹתָם stands the word הֶרֶב, pointed as an imperative, "make much"; its place in the sentence is not clear, and it is rendered by "great".
- **"retired" → "turned about"** (v39, 41) — Hebrew הָפַךְ "turn over, turn about"; "turned" (v40, 42, 45, 47) is another verb, פָּנָה.
- **"the flame began to arise … with a pillar of smoke" → "the rising began to go up … a pillar of smoke"** (v40) — as v38. **"the flame of the city ascended up to heaven" → "the whole of the city went up toward the heavens"** — Hebrew כְלִיל־הָעִיר: כָּלִיל is "whole", also the word for an offering burned whole (Deut 13:16; 33:10).
- **"were amazed" → "were dismayed"** (v41) — Hebrew וַיִּבָּהֵל.
- **"the battle overtook them" → "the battle caught up with him"** (v42) — Hebrew הִדְבִּיקָתְהוּ, the hifil of דָּבַק "cling", as Gen 31:23; in v45 וַיַּדְבִּיקוּ אַחֲרָיו "they kept close after him" is the same verb. **"them which came out of the cities they destroyed in the midst of them" → "those who were from the cities were destroying him in his midst"** — Hebrew וַאֲשֶׁר מֵהֶעָרִים מַשְׁחִיתִים אוֹתוֹ בְּתוֹכוֹ, word for word; who "those from the cities" are (Israelites coming out of the captured town, or men of the towns on the way) and whose "midst" is meant the Hebrew does not say.
- **"inclosed the Benjamites round about, and chased them, and trode them down with ease over against Gibeah toward the sunrising" → "They encircled Benjamin, they chased him, at the resting place they trod him down, as far as opposite Gibeah, on the east"** (v43) — three verbs without "and"; the verse is hard. מְנוּחָה is "resting place" (so the word list); it is also read as "without rest", "with ease", or as a place name ("from Nohah"). "On the east" is literally "from the rising of the sun".
- **"men of valour" → "men of ability"** (v44, 46) — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי־חָיִל; חַיִל is strength and worth, of fighters "valor"; one English word is kept for it (Gen 47:6; Josh 1:14).
- **"the rock of Rimmon" → "the crag of Rimmon"** (v45, 47) — Hebrew סֶלַע; twice with the article, הָרִמּוֹן "the Rimmon" ("the pomegranate"). **"gleaned of them" → "gleaned him"** (v45) — Hebrew וַיְעֹלְלֻהוּ, the verb of going over a vineyard a second time. **"slew" → "struck down"**.
- **"abode" → "dwelt"** (v47) — Hebrew יָשַׁב.
- **"turned again upon" → "returned to"** (v48) — Hebrew שָׁבוּ אֶל. **"as well the men of every city, as the beast" → "from the city in its entirety to beast"** — Hebrew מֵעִיר מְתֹם עַד־בְּהֵמָה: מְתֹם as pointed is "soundness, entirety"; with other vowels (מְתִם) the words are "from the city of men to beast", as Deut 2:34; 3:6, and so it is commonly read. The printed vowels are translated. **"set on fire"** — Hebrew שִׁלְּחוּ בָאֵשׁ, literally "sent into the fire", as 1:8.
- **"with the edge of the sword" kept** (v37, 48) — Hebrew לְפִי־חֶרֶב, literally "to the mouth of the sword".

### Notes
- **From Dan to Beersheba** (v1) — the whole land from north to south; here for the first time in Judges.
- **The Levite's account** (v4–6) — he says that the lords of Gibeah intended to kill him; 19:22 gives their words as "Bring out the man … and let us know him", and 19:25 says that he himself took hold of his concubine and brought her out. The narrator sets the two accounts side by side without comment.
- **Two defeats** — Israel asks and is told to go up, and loses twenty-two thousand and eighteen thousand men (v21, 25); only the third answer has a promise (v28).
- **"Phinehas the son of Eleazar"** (v28) — Phinehas (Num 25) is still alive: these events are set early in the time of the judges.
- **"The ark of the covenant … was there"** (v27) — at Bethel in those days; later it is at Shiloh (1 Sam 1–4).
- **The ambush** (v29–41) — told twice over (v29–36a and v36b–41), much as the taking of Ai in Josh 8.
- **Six hundred men** (v47) — what is left of Benjamin; chapter 21 turns to them.
