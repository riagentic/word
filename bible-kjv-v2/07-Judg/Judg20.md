# Judges 20

[1] Then all the children of Israel went out, and the congregation was gathered as one man, from Dan to Beersheba, with the land of Gilead, to Yahweh at Mizpah.

[2] The chiefs of all the people, of all the tribes of Israel, took their stand in the assembly of the people of *Elohim* — 400,000 foot soldiers drawing the sword.

[3] (The children of Benjamin heard that the children of Israel had gone up to Mizpah.) The children of Israel said, "Tell us, how did this evil happen?"

[4] The Levite, the husband of the murdered woman, answered, "I came to Gibeah that belongs to Benjamin, I and my concubine, to spend the night.

[5] The lords of Gibeah rose against me and surrounded the house against me at night; they sought to kill me. My concubine they violated, and she is dead.

[6] I took hold of my concubine, cut her in pieces, and sent her throughout the country of the inheritance of Israel; for they have committed depravity and outrage in Israel.

[7] Behold, you are all children of Israel; give your advice and counsel here."

[8] All the people arose as one man, saying, "None of us will go to his tent, and none of us will turn aside to his house.

[9] Now this is what we will do to Gibeah: by lot we will go up against it.

[10] We will take ten men out of a hundred throughout all the tribes of Israel, and a hundred out of a thousand, and a thousand out of ten thousand, to supply provisions for the people, so that when they come to Gibeah of Benjamin they may repay it for all the outrage they have committed in Israel."

[11] So all the men of Israel gathered against the city, united as one man.

[12] The tribes of Israel sent men through all the tribe of Benjamin, saying, "What is this evil that has been done among you?

[13] Now therefore surrender the men — the sons of Belial — who are in Gibeah, that we may put them to death and purge the evil from Israel." But the children of Benjamin would not listen to the voice of their brothers, the children of Israel.

[14] The children of Benjamin were gathered out of the cities to Gibeah to go out to battle against the children of Israel.

[15] The children of Benjamin were mustered on that day — 26,000 men drawing the sword, from the cities, besides the inhabitants of Gibeah — 700 chosen men.

[16] Among all these, 700 chosen men were left-handed; every one of them could sling a stone at a hair and not miss.

[17] The men of Israel, besides Benjamin, were mustered — 400,000 men drawing the sword, all of them men of war.

[18] The children of Israel arose, went up to Bethel, and asked counsel of *Elohim*, saying, "Who shall go up first for us to battle against the children of Benjamin?" Yahweh said, "Judah first."

[19] The children of Israel rose in the morning and encamped against Gibeah.

[20] The men of Israel went out to battle against Benjamin; the men of Israel drew up in battle order against them at Gibeah.

[21] The children of Benjamin came out of Gibeah and destroyed on the ground that day 22,000 men of Israel.

[22] But the people, the men of Israel, encouraged themselves and again drew up in battle order in the place where they had arrayed themselves the first day.

[23] (The children of Israel went up and wept before Yahweh until evening, and they asked counsel of Yahweh, saying, "Shall we again draw near for battle against the children of Benjamin our brother?" Yahweh said, "Go up against him.")

[24] The children of Israel drew near against the children of Benjamin on the second day.

[25] Benjamin went out against them from Gibeah on the second day and destroyed again on the ground 18,000 men of the children of Israel — all drawing the sword.

[26] Then all the children of Israel and all the people went up and came to Bethel; they wept and sat there before Yahweh and fasted that day until evening, and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before Yahweh.

[27] The children of Israel inquired of Yahweh (for the ark of the covenant of *Elohim* was there in those days,

[28] and Phinehas son of Eleazar son of Aaron stood before it in those days), saying, "Shall we yet again go out to battle against the children of Benjamin our brother, or shall we cease?" Yahweh said, "Go up, for tomorrow I will give them into your hand."

[29] So Israel set men in ambush around Gibeah.

[30] The children of Israel went up against the children of Benjamin on the third day, and drew up against Gibeah as at other times.

[31] The children of Benjamin went out against the people and were drawn away from the city. They began to strike down some of the people, killing them, as at other times, in the highways — one of which goes up to Bethel and the other to Gibeah — in the open country, about thirty men of Israel.

[32] The children of Benjamin said, "They are struck down before us as at the first." But the children of Israel said, "Let us flee and draw them away from the city to the highways."

[33] All the men of Israel rose up from their place and drew up in battle order at Baal-tamar; the men of Israel who had been lying in ambush burst forth from their place, from the plain of Geba.

[34] Ten thousand chosen men from all Israel came against Gibeah, and the fighting was fierce; but Benjamin did not know that disaster was close upon them.

[35] Yahweh struck Benjamin before Israel; the children of Israel destroyed 25,100 men of Benjamin that day — all drawing the sword.

[36] The children of Benjamin saw that they were beaten. The men of Israel had given ground before Benjamin because they trusted in the ambush that they had set against Gibeah.

[37] The men in ambush hurried and rushed against Gibeah; the ambushers deployed and struck the whole city with the edge of the sword.

[38] Now the appointed signal between the men of Israel and the ambushers was that they should make a great column of smoke rise from the city.

[39] When the men of Israel turned in the battle, Benjamin began to strike down about thirty men of Israel, for they said, "Surely they are struck down before us as in the first battle."

[40] But when the column of smoke began to rise from the city as a pillar, the Benjamites looked behind them — and behold, the whole city was going up in smoke to the heavens.

[41] When the men of Israel turned back, the men of Benjamin were dismayed, for they saw that disaster had overtaken them.

[42] So they turned their backs before the men of Israel toward the way of the wilderness; but the battle overtook them, and those who came out of the cities they destroyed in their midst.

[43] They surrounded the Benjamites, pursued them, and trod them down easily from near Gibeah toward the sunrise.

[44] Eighteen thousand men of Benjamin fell — all of them men of valor.

[45] They turned and fled toward the wilderness to the rock of Rimmon. They gleaned of them five thousand men on the highways, and pursued them as far as Gidom, killing two thousand men of them.

[46] All who fell of Benjamin that day were 25,000 men drawing the sword, all of them men of valor.

[47] Six hundred men turned and fled to the wilderness to the rock of Rimmon, and they remained at the rock of Rimmon four months.

[48] The men of Israel turned back against the children of Benjamin and struck them with the edge of the sword — the people of every city as well as the livestock and all that came to hand; and they set on fire all the cities they came to.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Beth-aven" / "Bethel" uncertainty** — MT "Bethel" in v18, 26, 31. KJV has "house of God" interchangeably.
- **"lewdness and folly" → "depravity and outrage"** (v6) — *zimmah u-nevalah*.
- **"lords of Gibeah" (v5)** — Hebrew *ba'alei Giv'ah*.
- **"asked counsel" → "inquired"** — modernized.
- **"men of Belial" → "sons of Belial"** (v13).

### Notes
- **From Dan to Beersheba** (v1) — stock idiom for "all Israel," first here in Judges.
- **First two defeats** — Yahweh directs Israel but allows initial losses (22,000 + 18,000). Theological question: why? Perhaps Israel's self-righteousness was excessive; perhaps the Levite's false testimony (v5 claims the mob sought to kill *him*, though 19:22–25 indicates they sought him sexually; this detail is glossed).
- **Third try succeeds** — with ambush. Israel virtually wipes out Benjamin: 25,100 dead, only 600 survivors.
- **"Phinehas son of Eleazar"** (v28) — chronological anchor: Phinehas (Num 25) is still alive. Places these events early in the judges period, not late.
- **"Ark of the covenant there"** (v27) — Bethel is briefly the sanctuary. Later Shiloh is primary (1 Sam 1–4).
- **Pyrrhic victory** — the near-annihilation of a whole tribe. Ch 21 will address the crisis of how to preserve Benjamin.
