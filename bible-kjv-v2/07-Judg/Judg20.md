## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Beth-aven" / "Bethel" uncertainty** — MT "Bethel" in v18, 26, 31. KJV has "house of God" interchangeably.
- **"lewdness and folly" → "depravity and outrage"** (v6) — *zimmah u-nevalah*.
- **"lords of Gibeah" (v5)** — Hebrew *ba'alei Giv'ah*.
- **"asked counsel" → "inquired"** — modernized.
- **"men of Belial" → "sons of Belial"** (v13).

### Notes
- **From Dan to Beersheba** (v1) — stock idiom for "all Israel," first here in Judges.
- **First two defeats** — Yahweh directs Israel but allows initial losses (22,000 + 18,000). Theological question: why? Perhaps Israel's self-righteousness was excessive; perhaps the Levite's false testimony (v5 claims the mob sought to kill *him*, though 19:22–25 indicates they sought him sexually; this detail is glossed).
- **Third try succeeds** — with ambush. Israel virtually wipes out Benjamin: 25,100 dead, only 600 survivors.
- **"Phinehas son of Eleazar"** (v28) — chronological anchor: Phinehas (Num 25) is still alive. Places these events early in the judges period, not late.
- **"Ark of the covenant there"** (v27) — Bethel is briefly the sanctuary. Later Shiloh is primary (1 Sam 1–4).
- **Pyrrhic victory** — the near-annihilation of a whole tribe. Ch 21 will address the crisis of how to preserve Benjamin.
