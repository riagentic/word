# Judges 9

[1] And ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ the son of ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧ went to Shechem, to the brothers of his mother, and he spoke to them and to all the clan of the house of the father of his mother, saying,

[2] "Speak, please, in the ears of all the lords of Shechem: 'What is better for you: that seventy men rule over you, all the sons of ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧, or that one man rule over you?' And remember that I am your bone and your flesh."

[3] And the brothers of his mother spoke concerning him in the ears of all the lords of Shechem all these words; and their heart turned after ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧, for they said, "He is our brother."

[4] And they gave him seventy of silver from the house of ⟦baal-berith|Baal Berit|Baal-berith⟧, and with them ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ hired empty and reckless men, and they went after him.

[5] And he came to the house of his father at ⟦ophrah|Ofrah|Ophrah⟧ and killed his brothers, the sons of ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧, seventy men, on one stone; but ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧ the son of ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧, the youngest, remained, for he hid himself.

[6] And all the lords of Shechem were gathered, and all ⟦beth-millo|Beit Millo|Beth-millo⟧, and they went and made ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ reign as king, by the oak of the pillar that was in Shechem.

[7] And they told ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧, and he went and stood on the top of Mount Gerizim, and he lifted up his voice and called out, and he said to them:

"Listen to me, lords of Shechem,
and may ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ listen to you.

[8] The trees surely went to anoint a king over them.
And they said to the olive tree, 'Reign over us.'

[9] And the olive tree said to them,
'Have I ceased my fatness,
by which through me they honor gods and men,
and shall I go to sway over the trees?'

[10] And the trees said to the fig tree,
'Come, you, reign over us.'

[11] And the fig tree said to them,
'Have I ceased my sweetness and my good crop,
and shall I go to sway over the trees?'

[12] And the trees said to the vine,
'Come, you, reign over us.'

[13] And the vine said to them,
'Have I ceased my new wine,
which gladdens gods and men,
and shall I go to sway over the trees?'

[14] And all the trees said to the bramble,
'Come, you, reign over us.'

[15] And the bramble said to the trees,
'If in truth you are anointing me as king over you,
come, take refuge in my shade;
and if not, let fire come out from the bramble
and consume the cedars of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧.'

[16] "And now, if you have acted in truth and in blamelessness, and have made ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ reign, and if you have done good with ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧ and with his house, and if you have done to him according to the dealing of his hands —

[17] for my father fought for you and threw away his soul and delivered you from the hand of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧;

[18] and you, you have risen against the house of my father today and have killed his sons, seventy men, on one stone, and have made ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧, the son of his slave woman, reign over the lords of Shechem, because he is your brother —

[19] and if you have acted in truth and in blamelessness with ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧ and with his house this day, rejoice in ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧, and let him also rejoice in you.

[20] And if not, let fire come out from ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ and consume the lords of Shechem and ⟦beth-millo|Beit Millo|Beth-millo⟧; and let fire come out from the lords of Shechem and from ⟦beth-millo|Beit Millo|Beth-millo⟧ and consume ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧."

[21] And ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧ took flight and fled, and he went to Beer and dwelt there, from the face of ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ his brother.

[22] And ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ was prince over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ three years.

[23] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ sent an evil spirit between ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ and the lords of Shechem, and the lords of Shechem dealt treacherously with ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧,

[24] that the violence done to the seventy sons of ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧ might come, and to put their blood on ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ their brother, who killed them, and on the lords of Shechem, who made strong his hands to kill his brothers.

[25] And the lords of Shechem set men in ambush for him on the tops of the mountains, and they robbed all who passed by them on the way; and it was told to ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧.

[26] And Gaal the son of ⟦ebed|Eved|Ebed⟧ came, and his brothers, and they passed over into Shechem; and the lords of Shechem trusted in him.

[27] And they went out to the field and picked their vineyards and trod, and they made praises; and they came into the house of their god and ate and drank, and they cursed ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧.

[28] And Gaal the son of ⟦ebed|Eved|Ebed⟧ said, "Who is ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧, and who is Shechem, that we should serve him? Is he not the son of ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧, and ⟦zebul|Zevul|Zebul⟧ his overseer? Serve the men of ⟦hamor|Chamor|Hamor⟧, the father of Shechem! And why should we, we serve him?

[29] And would that this people were in my hand! Then I would remove ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧." And he said to ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧, "Multiply your host, and come out."

[30] And ⟦zebul|Zevul|Zebul⟧, the prince of the city, heard the words of Gaal the son of ⟦ebed|Eved|Ebed⟧, and his anger burned.

[31] And he sent messengers to ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ in deceit, saying, "Behold, Gaal the son of ⟦ebed|Eved|Ebed⟧ and his brothers are coming to Shechem, and behold, they are shutting up the city against you.

[32] And now, arise by night, you and the people who are with you, and lie in ambush in the field.

[33] And it shall be in the morning, as the sun dawns, you shall rise early and make a raid on the city; and behold, he and the people who are with him will be coming out to you, and you shall do to him as your hand finds."

[34] And ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ arose, and all the people who were with him, by night, and they lay in ambush against Shechem in four companies.

[35] And Gaal the son of ⟦ebed|Eved|Ebed⟧ went out and stood at the entrance of the gate of the city; and ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ arose, and the people who were with him, from the place of ambush.

[36] And Gaal saw the people, and he said to ⟦zebul|Zevul|Zebul⟧, "Behold, people are coming down from the tops of the mountains." And ⟦zebul|Zevul|Zebul⟧ said to him, "The shadow of the mountains you are seeing as men."

[37] And Gaal spoke yet again and said, "Behold, people are coming down from beside the navel of the land, and one company is coming by the way of the oak of the soothsayers."

[38] And ⟦zebul|Zevul|Zebul⟧ said to him, "Where then is your mouth, you who said, 'Who is ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧, that we should serve him?' Is not this the people that you rejected? Go out, please, now, and fight against it."

[39] And Gaal went out before the lords of Shechem and fought against ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧.

[40] And ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ pursued him, and he fled from his face; and many fell slain, as far as the entrance of the gate.

[41] And ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ dwelt in Arumah; and ⟦zebul|Zevul|Zebul⟧ drove out Gaal and his brothers from dwelling in Shechem.

[42] And it came to pass on the morrow that the people went out to the field; and they told ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧.

[43] And he took the people and divided them into three companies and lay in ambush in the field; and he saw, and behold, the people were coming out from the city, and he rose against them and struck them down.

[44] And ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ and the companies that were with him made a raid and stood at the entrance of the gate of the city, and the two companies made a raid on all who were in the field and struck them down.

[45] And ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ fought against the city all that day, and he captured the city, and the people who were in it he killed; and he broke down the city and sowed it with salt.

[46] And all the lords of the tower of Shechem heard, and they came into the vault of the house of ⟦el-berith|El Berit|El-berith⟧.

[47] And it was told to ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ that all the lords of the tower of Shechem had gathered themselves together.

[48] And ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ went up Mount ⟦zalmon|Tzalmon|Zalmon⟧, he and all the people who were with him; and ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ took the axes in his hand and cut down a bough of trees, and he lifted it up and put it on his shoulder, and he said to the people who were with him, "What you have seen me do, hasten, do like me."

[49] And all the people also cut down, each man his bough, and they went after ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ and put them against the vault and set the vault on fire over them; and all the men of the tower of Shechem also died, about a thousand men and women.

[50] And ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ went to ⟦thebez|Tevetz|Thebez⟧ and camped against ⟦thebez|Tevetz|Thebez⟧ and captured it.

[51] And a tower of strength was in the midst of the city, and all the men and the women fled there, and all the lords of the city, and they shut themselves in and went up on the roof of the tower.

[52] And ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ came as far as the tower and fought against it, and he drew near as far as the entrance of the tower to burn it with fire.

[53] And a certain woman threw an upper millstone on the head of ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ and crushed his skull.

[54] And he called hastily to the young man, his weapon-bearer, and said to him, "Draw your sword and put me to death, lest they say of me, 'A woman killed him.'" And his young man pierced him, and he died.

[55] And the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ saw that ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ was dead, and they went, each man to his place.

[56] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ returned the evil of ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ that he had done to his father, to kill his seventy brothers.

[57] And all the evil of the men of Shechem ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ returned on their head; and the curse of ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧ the son of ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧ came to them.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v7, 23, 56, 57) — Hebrew *Elohim*. The name Yahweh does not stand in this chapter. In v9, 13 the same word is rendered "gods", and in v27 "their god".
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"men of Shechem" → "lords of Shechem"** (v2, 3, 6, 7, 18, 20, 23–26, 39; so v46, 47, 51) — Hebrew בַּעֲלֵי שְׁכֶם *ba'alei Shechem*, the plural of בַּעַל "lord, owner": the freemen who have the say in the town. The word is the same as the god's name Baal. In v57 the Hebrew has אַנְשֵׁי שְׁכֶם "the men of Shechem" (and v49 "the men of the tower").
- **"communed with them" → "spoke to them"** (v1) — Hebrew דִּבֶּר. **"all the family of the house of his mother's father" → "all the clan of the house of the father of his mother"** — Hebrew מִשְׁפַּחַת "clan".
- **"Whether is better for you, either that all the sons of Jerubbaal, which are threescore and ten persons, reign over you, or that one reign over you?" → "What is better for you: that seventy men rule over you, all the sons of Jerubbaal, or that one man rule over you?"** (v2) — the Hebrew order; מָשַׁל "rule", the verb of 8:22, 23 ("reign" is another verb: v8). **"in the ears of"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew בְּאָזְנֵי.
- **"spake of him" → "spoke concerning him"** (v3) — Hebrew עָלָיו. **"their hearts inclined to follow Abimelech" → "their heart turned after Abimelech"** — Hebrew וַיֵּט לִבָּם אַחֲרֵי; "heart" is singular.
- **"threescore and ten pieces of silver" → "seventy of silver"** (v4) — "pieces" (or "shekels") is not in the Hebrew. **"vain and light persons" → "empty and reckless men"** — Hebrew רֵיקִים "empty"; פֹּחֲזִים, a rare word (Zeph 3:4; the noun in Gen 49:4): wanton, reckless; its exact sense is uncertain. **"which followed him" → "and they went after him"**.
- **"slew" → "killed"** (v5, 18, 24, 45, 54, 56) — Hebrew הָרַג. **"upon one stone"** — Hebrew עַל־אֶבֶן אֶחָת. **"was left" → "remained"** — Hebrew וַיִּוָּתֵר. **"the youngest"** — Hebrew הַקָּטֹן, literally "the small one".
- **"gathered together" → "were gathered"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיֵּאָסְפוּ. **"made Abimelech king" → "made Abimelech reign as king"** (v6, 16, 18) — Hebrew וַיַּמְלִיכוּ … לְמֶלֶךְ, the causative of מָלַךְ "reign" (v8); in v16, 18 without "as king". **"the house of Millo" → "Beth-millo"** (v6, 20) — Hebrew בֵּית מִלּוֹא, "the house of the Millo" (מִלּוֹא "filling": a raised earthwork); whether a place or a body of people is not said. **"by the plain of the pillar" → "by the oak of the pillar"** — Hebrew עִם־אֵלוֹן מֻצָּב. אֵלוֹן is a great tree ("oak"; the kind is not certain), not "plain". מֻצָּב is a participle, "set up" (so of the ladder, Gen 28:12), used here as a noun: a thing set up, a post or pillar; of one root with מַצֵּבָה "pillar" (cf. Josh 24:26). The exact sense is uncertain.
- **"cried" → "called out"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיִּקְרָא. **"Hearken unto me, … that God may hearken unto you" → "Listen to me, … and may *Elohim* listen to you"** — Hebrew וְיִשְׁמַע "and may he listen": a wish joined with "and".
- **"The trees went forth on a time" → "The trees surely went"** (v8) — Hebrew הָלוֹךְ הָלְכוּ, the infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "going they went"; "on a time" is not written. **"over them"** — Hebrew עֲלֵיהֶם. **"Reign"** (v8, 10, 12, 14) — Hebrew מָלַךְ, the verb of "king"; in v8, 12 the written text has a longer form of the same word.
- **"Should I leave my fatness, wherewith by me they honour God and man, and go to be promoted over the trees?" → "Have I ceased my fatness, by which through me they honor gods and men, and shall I go to sway over the trees?"** (v9, 11, 13) — Hebrew הֶחֳדַלְתִּי אֶת־… וְהָלַכְתִּי לָנוּעַ עַל־הָעֵצִים, the same words in all three answers and the same English. הֶחֳדַלְתִּי is from חָדַל "cease", in a form of unusual pointing, a perfect: "have I ceased …?" (also taken as "shall I cease"). דִּשְׁנִי "my fatness": the oil. אֲשֶׁר־בִּי יְכַבְּדוּ, literally "which in me they honor": "by me", or "which in me gods and men honor". נוּעַ is "sway, wave, stagger"; "be promoted" is not the word.
- **"God and man" → "gods and men"** (v9, 13) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים וַאֲנָשִׁים; אֲנָשִׁים is plural. אֱלֹהִים can be "gods" or "*Elohim*" (God): oil and wine are used in honor of God and of men, and wine "gladdens" both; the fable is spoken to the men of the house of Baal-berith. The Hebrew does not decide.
- **"Come thou, and reign over us"** (v10, 12, 14) — Hebrew לְכִי־אַתְּ מָלְכִי, feminine to the fig tree and the vine, לֵךְ אַתָּה מְלָךְ, masculine to the bramble: the same English. **"my good fruit" → "my good crop"** (v11) — Hebrew תְּנוּבָה. **"my wine, which cheereth" → "my new wine, which gladdens"** (v13) — Hebrew תִּירוֹשׁ; הַמְשַׂמֵּחַ.
- **"bramble"** (v14, 15) — Hebrew אָטָד, a thorn bush (Ps 58:9; cf. Gen 50:10), which gives no shade to speak of. **"put your trust in my shadow" → "take refuge in my shade"** (v15) — Hebrew חֲסוּ בְצִלִּי. **"devour" → "consume"** (v15, 20) — Hebrew אָכַל "eat".
- **"if ye have done truly and sincerely" → "if you have acted in truth and in blamelessness"** (v16, 19) — Hebrew בֶּאֱמֶת וּבְתָמִים, two nouns (Josh 24:14); בֶּאֱמֶת "in truth" as in v15. **"if ye have dealt well with" → "if you have done good with"** — Hebrew טוֹבָה עֲשִׂיתֶם עִם, as 8:35. **"according to the deserving of his hands" → "according to the dealing of his hands"** — Hebrew כִּגְמוּל יָדָיו: what his hands have done and earned. The "if" of v16 is taken up again in v19; v17, 18 stand between.
- **"adventured his life far" → "threw away his soul"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיַּשְׁלֵךְ אֶת־נַפְשׁוֹ מִנֶּגֶד, literally "threw his soul from in front": flung his life away, risked it. **"fought for you"** — Hebrew נִלְחַם עֲלֵיכֶם.
- **"the son of his maidservant" → "the son of his slave woman"** (v18) — Hebrew אֲמָתוֹ; in 8:31 she is "his concubine". **"this day" → "today"** — Hebrew הַיּוֹם; in v19 הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה "this day".
- **"ran away, and fled" → "took flight and fled"** (v21) — two Hebrew verbs, נוּס and בָּרַח, both "flee". **"for fear of Abimelech" → "from the face of Abimelech"** — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי; "fear" is not written.
- **"had reigned three years over Israel" → "was prince over Israel three years"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיָּשַׂר, from the root of שַׂר "prince": neither "reign" (מָלַךְ, v6, 8) nor "rule" (מָשַׁל, v2). The verse is a sentence of its own in the Hebrew.
- **"Then God sent an evil spirit" → "And *Elohim* sent an evil spirit"** (v23) — Hebrew רוּחַ רָעָה, word for word; רוּחַ is "spirit, wind, breath", רָעָה "evil, bad" (the word of "the evil of Abimelech", v56). Cf. 1 Sam 16:14.
- **"That the cruelty done to the threescore and ten sons of Jerubbaal might come, and their blood be laid upon" → "that the violence done to the seventy sons of Jerubbaal might come, and to put their blood on"** (v24) — Hebrew לָבוֹא חֲמַס שִׁבְעִים בְּנֵי־יְרֻבָּעַל וְדָמָם לָשׂוּם עַל, literally "for the violence of the seventy sons of Jerubbaal to come, and their blood, to put on"; "the violence of" is the violence done to them. **"which aided him" → "who made strong his hands"** — Hebrew חִזְּקוּ אֶת־יָדָיו.
- **"set liers in wait for him" → "set men in ambush for him"** (v25) — Hebrew מְאָרְבִים; לוֹ "for him": against him, to his loss. **"robbed"** — Hebrew גָּזַל.
- **"his brethren" → "his brothers"** (v26, 31, 41) — Hebrew אֶחָיו, also of kinsmen. **"went over to Shechem" → "passed over into Shechem"** — Hebrew וַיַּעַבְרוּ בִּשְׁכֶם. **"put their confidence in him" → "trusted in him"** — Hebrew בָּטַח בְּ.
- **"gathered their vineyards, and trode the grapes, and made merry" → "picked their vineyards and trod, and they made praises"** (v27) — Hebrew בָּצַר; דָּרַךְ, with no object; וַיַּעֲשׂוּ הִלּוּלִים, a word found only here and in Lev 19:24 ("a holy thing of praise"), from הִלֵּל "praise": a feast of praise at the vintage. What exactly was done is not known. **"cursed"** — Hebrew קִלֵּל, literally "made light of".
- **"his officer" → "his overseer"** (v28) — Hebrew פָּקִיד. **"serve the men of Hamor the father of Shechem: for why should we serve him?" → "Serve the men of Hamor, the father of Shechem! And why should we, we serve him?"** — Hebrew עִבְדוּ, a command, as the Masoretic Text is pointed; the pronoun "we" is written out. The sense of the whole verse is disputed (some read "did not the son of Jerubbaal and Zebul … serve the men of Hamor?"); the Masoretic Text is kept. Hamor: Gen 34.
- **"would to God this people were under my hand" → "would that this people were in my hand"** (v29) — Hebrew מִי יִתֵּן, literally "who will give"; "God" is not written. **"And he said to Abimelech"** — so the Masoretic Text; the Greek has "and I would say" (from memory). **"Increase thine army" → "Multiply your host"** — Hebrew רַבֶּה צְבָאֲךָ.
- **"the ruler of the city" → "the prince of the city"** (v30) — Hebrew שַׂר. **"his anger was kindled" → "his anger burned"** — Hebrew וַיִּחַר אַפּוֹ; אַף is literally "nose".
- **"privily" → "in deceit"** (v31) — Hebrew בְּתָרְמָה, found only here. It is commonly taken from רָמָה "deceive" ("by a trick", "secretly"); others take it as a place name, "in Tormah", or correct it to "in Arumah" (v41). Uncertain. **"they fortify the city against thee" → "they are shutting up the city against you"** — Hebrew צָרִים אֶת־הָעִיר עָלֶיךָ; צוּר is "bind, shut in, besiege"; with the city as its object the sense is uncertain ("stirring up" is a guess from the context).
- **"lie in wait" → "lie in ambush"** (v32, 34, 43); **"from lying in wait" → "from the place of ambush"** (v35) — Hebrew אָרַב; מַאְרָב.
- **"as soon as the sun is up" → "as the sun dawns"** (v33) — Hebrew כִּזְרֹחַ הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ. **"set upon the city" → "make a raid on the city"** (v33, 44) — Hebrew פָּשַׁט עַל, of a sudden attack (1 Sam 23:27). **"as thou shalt find occasion" → "as your hand finds"** — Hebrew כַּאֲשֶׁר תִּמְצָא יָדֶךָ.
- **"companies"** (v34, 37, 43, 44) — Hebrew רָאשִׁים, literally "heads".
- **"entering of the gate" → "entrance of the gate"** (v35, 40, 44) — Hebrew פֶּתַח שַׁעַר.
- **"Thou seest the shadow of the mountains as if they were men" → "The shadow of the mountains you are seeing as men"** (v36) — the Hebrew order; צֵל is the word of v15 ("shade").
- **"by the middle of the land" → "from beside the navel of the land"** (v37) — Hebrew מֵעִם טַבּוּר הָאָרֶץ. טַבּוּר is found only here and in Ezek 38:12; "navel" is the meaning in later Hebrew and in the Greek (from memory); "height" has also been proposed. Uncertain. **"by the plain of Meonenim" → "by the way of the oak of the soothsayers"** — Hebrew אֵלוֹן מְעוֹנְנִים, without the article: "an oak of soothsayers", the participle of the verb of Lev 19:26; Deut 18:10. **"another company" → "one company"** — Hebrew רֹאשׁ־אֶחָד.
- **"Where is now thy mouth, wherewith thou saidst" → "Where then is your mouth, you who said"** (v38) — Hebrew אַיֵּה אֵפוֹא פִיךָ אֲשֶׁר תֹּאמַר, literally "… your mouth, that you say". **"despised" → "rejected"** — Hebrew מָאַס. **"go out, I pray now" → "Go out, please, now"** — Hebrew צֵא־נָא עַתָּה. **"fight with them" → "fight against it"** — Hebrew בּוֹ, singular: the people.
- **"fled before him, and many were overthrown and wounded" → "fled from his face; and many fell slain"** (v40) — Hebrew מִפָּנָיו; וַיִּפְּלוּ חֲלָלִים, "fell pierced": the word for the slain.
- **"thrust out Gaal … that they should not dwell" → "drove out Gaal … from dwelling"** (v41) — Hebrew וַיְגָרֶשׁ … מִשֶּׁבֶת.
- **"the people"** (v42–45) — Hebrew הָעָם, of the people of Shechem (v42, 43b, 45) and of Abimelech's men (v43a) alike.
- **"the company that was with him" → "the companies that were with him"** (v44) — Hebrew וְהָרָאשִׁים, plural, as the Masoretic Text has it, although "the two companies" follow; the versions have the singular (from memory).
- **"beat down the city" → "broke down the city"** (v45) — Hebrew נָתַץ, the verb of 6:28–32; 8:9, 17. **"sowed it with salt"** — Hebrew וַיִּזְרָעֶהָ מֶלַח.
- **"an hold of the house of the god Berith" → "the vault of the house of El-berith"** (v46, 49) — Hebrew צְרִיחַ, found only here and in 1 Sam 13:6: an underground room, a vault, or a stronghold; uncertain. אֵל בְּרִית "El of covenant"; in v4 and 8:33 the name is Baal-berith. אֵל is "god" and the name El.
- **"were gathered together" → "had gathered themselves together"** (v47) — Hebrew הִתְקַבְּצוּ.
- **"took an axe in his hand" → "took the axes in his hand"** (v48) — Hebrew הַקַּרְדֻּמּוֹת, plural with the article. **"a bough from the trees" → "a bough of trees"** (v48, 49) — Hebrew שׂוֹכַת עֵצִים, שׂוֹכֹה, a word found only here: a bough, or a bundle of brushwood. **"make haste, and do as I have done" → "hasten, do like me"** — Hebrew מַהֲרוּ עֲשׂוּ כָמוֹנִי.
- **"set the hold on fire upon them"** (v49) — Hebrew וַיַּצִּיתוּ עֲלֵיהֶם … בָּאֵשׁ, "over them". **"all the men of the tower … about a thousand men and women"** — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי "men of", then אִישׁ וְאִשָּׁה "man and woman".
- **"encamped against" → "camped against"**; **"took it" → "captured it"** (v50) — Hebrew חָנָה בְּ; לָכַד.
- **"a strong tower" → "a tower of strength"** (v51) — Hebrew מִגְדַּל־עֹז. **"all they of the city" → "all the lords of the city"** — Hebrew בַּעֲלֵי הָעִיר. **"shut it to them" → "shut themselves in"** — Hebrew וַיִּסְגְּרוּ בַּעֲדָם, literally "shut behind themselves".
- **"went hard unto the door" → "drew near as far as the entrance"** (v52) — Hebrew וַיִּגַּשׁ עַד־פֶּתַח.
- **"a piece of a millstone" → "an upper millstone"** (v53) — Hebrew פֶּלַח רֶכֶב, literally "a millstone of riding": the upper stone that rides on the lower (Deut 24:6; 2 Sam 11:21); פֶּלַח is also "a slice, a piece", so "a piece of an upper millstone" is possible. **"all to brake his skull" → "crushed his skull"** — Hebrew וַתָּרִץ; archaic "all to" = completely. **"a certain woman"** — Hebrew אִשָּׁה אַחַת, "one woman".
- **"the young man his armourbearer" → "the young man, his weapon-bearer"** (v54) — Hebrew נֹשֵׂא כֵלָיו, "the bearer of his gear"; no armor is named. **"slay me" → "put me to death"** — Hebrew מוֹתְתֵנִי, an intensive form of "die": make an end of me. **"that men say not of me" → "lest they say of me"** — Hebrew פֶּן־יֹאמְרוּ לִי. **"thrust him through" → "pierced him"** — Hebrew דָּקַר.
- **"every man unto his place"** (v55) — Hebrew אִישׁ לִמְקֹמוֹ; "the men of Israel" is the collective singular אִישׁ־יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"Thus God rendered the wickedness of Abimelech" → "And *Elohim* returned the evil of Abimelech"** (v56, 57) — Hebrew וַיָּשֶׁב, הֵשִׁיב, the causative of שׁוּב "return": brought it back on him; רָעָה "evil". **"in slaying" → "to kill"** — Hebrew לַהֲרֹג. **"upon their heads" → "on their head"** — Hebrew singular. **"upon them came the curse" → "the curse … came to them"** — Hebrew קִלֲלַת, the noun of the verb in v27.

### Notes
- **Abimelech** — the son of Gideon's concubine at Shechem (8:31); with silver from the house of Baal-berith he hires men and kills his seventy brothers on one stone.
- **Jotham's fable** (v7–15) — the trees look for a king. The olive tree, the fig tree and the vine each answer in the same words and will not leave what they give; the bramble accepts and offers its shade.
- **Fire from the bramble** (v15, 20) — Jotham's words to Shechem: fire from Abimelech on the lords of Shechem, and fire from them on Abimelech. Both come about (v45, 49, 53–57).
- **"An evil spirit"** (v23) — sent by *Elohim* between Abimelech and the lords of Shechem. Compare 1 Sam 16:14.
- **Salt on the city** (v45) — the broken-down city is sown with salt. Compare Deut 29:23.
- **The millstone** (v53) — a woman's millstone ends Abimelech; 2 Sam 11:21 remembers it.
- **The end** (v56–57) — the narrator names what has happened: *Elohim* returned the evil of Abimelech and of the men of Shechem, and Jotham's curse came.
