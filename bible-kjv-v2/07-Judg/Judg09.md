# Judges 9

[1] ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ son of ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧ went to Shechem, to his mother's brothers, and spoke to them and to the whole clan of his mother's ancestral house, saying,

[2] "Speak, please, in the hearing of all the lords of Shechem: 'Which is better for you — that all seventy of the sons of ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧ rule over you, or that one man rule over you?' Remember also that I am your bone and your flesh."

[3] His mother's brothers spoke these words on his behalf in the hearing of all the lords of Shechem, and their hearts inclined to follow ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧, for they said, "He is our brother."

[4] They gave him seventy pieces of silver from the temple of ⟦baal-berith|Baal Berit|Baal-berith⟧, and with them ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ hired worthless and reckless men, who followed him.

[5] He went to his father's house at ⟦ophrah|Ofrah|Ophrah⟧ and killed his brothers, the sons of ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧ — seventy men — on one stone; but ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧ the youngest son of ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧ was left, because he hid himself.

[6] All the lords of Shechem and all ⟦beth-millo|Beit Millo|Beth-millo⟧ gathered together; they went and made ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ king, by the oak of the pillar that was at Shechem.

[7] When they told ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧ of it, he went and stood on the top of Mount Gerizim, and lifted up his voice and cried out, saying to them:

"Listen to me, lords of Shechem,
that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ may listen to you.

[8] The trees once went to anoint a king over themselves.
They said to the olive tree, 'Reign over us.'

[9] But the olive tree said to them,
'Shall I leave my richness,
by which gods and men are honored,
and go to sway over the trees?'

[10] So the trees said to the fig tree,
'Come, you, reign over us.'

[11] But the fig tree said to them,
'Shall I leave my sweetness and my good fruit,
and go to sway over the trees?'

[12] Then the trees said to the vine,
'Come, you, reign over us.'

[13] But the vine said to them,
'Shall I leave my new wine,
which cheers gods and men,
and go to sway over the trees?'

[14] Then all the trees said to the bramble,
'Come, you, reign over us.'

[15] And the bramble said to the trees,
'If in truth you are anointing me as king over you,
come, take refuge in my shade;
but if not, let fire come out of the bramble
and devour the cedars of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧.'

[16] "Now therefore, if you have acted truly and sincerely in making ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ king, and if you have dealt well with ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧ and with his house, and have done to him as his hands deserved —

[17] (for my father fought for you and risked his life to deliver you out of the hand of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧;

[18] but you have risen up against my father's house this day, have killed his sons — seventy men — on one stone, and have made ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧, the son of his maidservant, king over the lords of Shechem because he is your brother) —

[19] if then you have acted truly and sincerely with ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧ and his house this day, rejoice in ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ and let him also rejoice in you.

[20] But if not, let fire come out from ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ and devour the lords of Shechem and ⟦beth-millo|Beit Millo|Beth-millo⟧; and let fire come out from the lords of Shechem and from ⟦beth-millo|Beit Millo|Beth-millo⟧ and devour ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧."

[21] ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧ ran away and fled; he went to Beer and dwelt there because of ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ his brother.

[22] When ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ had ruled over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ three years,

[23] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ sent an evil spirit between ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ and the lords of Shechem, and the lords of Shechem dealt treacherously with ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧,

[24] so that the violence done to the seventy sons of ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧ might come back, and their blood be laid on ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ their brother, who killed them, and on the lords of Shechem, who aided him in the killing of his brothers.

[25] The lords of Shechem set men in ambush against him on the mountaintops, and they robbed everyone who passed by them along the road; and it was told ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧.

[26] Gaal son of ⟦ebed|Eved|Ebed⟧ came with his kinsmen, passing over to Shechem; and the lords of Shechem put their confidence in him.

[27] They went out into the fields and gathered the grapes of their vineyards, trod them, and held a festival; they went into the house of their god, ate and drank, and cursed ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧.

[28] Gaal son of ⟦ebed|Eved|Ebed⟧ said, "Who is ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧, and who is Shechem, that we should serve him? Is he not the son of ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧, and ⟦zebul|Zevul|Zebul⟧ his officer? Serve the men of ⟦hamor|Chamor|Hamor⟧, the father of Shechem! Why should we serve him?

[29] Would that this people were under my authority! Then I would remove ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧." He said to ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧, "Gather your army and come out."

[30] When ⟦zebul|Zevul|Zebul⟧, the governor of the city, heard the words of Gaal son of ⟦ebed|Eved|Ebed⟧, his anger was kindled.

[31] He sent messengers to ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ secretly, saying, "Behold, Gaal son of ⟦ebed|Eved|Ebed⟧ and his kinsmen have come to Shechem, and they are stirring up the city against you.

[32] Now therefore, at night go up — you and the people who are with you — and lie in wait in the field.

[33] In the morning, as soon as the sun rises, you shall rise early and rush upon the city. And behold, when he and the people with him come out against you, you shall do to them whatever your hand finds to do."

[34] ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ and all the people with him rose up by night, and they lay in wait against Shechem in four companies.

[35] Gaal son of ⟦ebed|Eved|Ebed⟧ went out and stood at the entrance of the gate of the city. ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ and the people with him rose up from the ambush.

[36] When Gaal saw the people, he said to ⟦zebul|Zevul|Zebul⟧, "Behold, people are coming down from the mountaintops!" But ⟦zebul|Zevul|Zebul⟧ said to him, "You see the shadow of the mountains as if they were men."

[37] Gaal spoke again: "Look, people are coming down by the heart of the land, and another company is coming by way of the Diviners' Oak."

[38] Then ⟦zebul|Zevul|Zebul⟧ said to him, "Where now is your bluster, you who said, 'Who is ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧, that we should serve him?' Is this not the people you despised? Go out now and fight with them."

[39] Gaal went out before the lords of Shechem and fought with ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧.

[40] ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ pursued him, and he fled before him; many fell wounded, even to the entrance of the gate.

[41] ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ stayed at Arumah, and ⟦zebul|Zevul|Zebul⟧ drove out Gaal and his kinsmen, so that they could not dwell at Shechem.

[42] On the next day the people went out into the field, and it was told ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧.

[43] He took his forces, divided them into three companies, and lay in wait in the field. When he saw that the people were coming out of the city, he rose against them and struck them down.

[44] ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ and the company that was with him rushed forward and stood at the entrance of the gate of the city, while the other two companies rushed upon all who were in the fields and struck them down.

[45] ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ fought against the city all that day; he captured the city and killed the people who were in it; he razed the city and sowed it with salt.

[46] When all the lords of the tower of Shechem heard of it, they entered the stronghold of the house of ⟦el-berith|El Berit|El-berith⟧.

[47] It was told ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ that all the lords of the tower of Shechem had gathered together.

[48] ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ went up to Mount ⟦zalmon|Tzalmon|Zalmon⟧, he and all the people with him; he took an axe in his hand, cut down a branch from the trees, lifted it, laid it on his shoulder, and said to the people with him, "What you have seen me do, hurry and do as I have done."

[49] So every one of the people likewise cut down his branch and followed ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧; they laid them against the stronghold and set it on fire with those inside; so all the people of the tower of Shechem also died — about a thousand men and women.

[50] Then ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ went to ⟦thebez|Tevetz|Thebez⟧, encamped against ⟦thebez|Tevetz|Thebez⟧, and captured it.

[51] But there was a strong tower within the city, and all the men and women, and all the lords of the city fled there, shut themselves in, and went up to the roof of the tower.

[52] ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ came to the tower, fought against it, and approached the door of the tower to burn it with fire.

[53] A certain woman threw an upper millstone on ⟦abimelech|Avimelech's|Abimelech's⟧ head and crushed his skull.

[54] He called hurriedly to the young man who carried his armor and said to him, "Draw your sword and kill me, so that no one may say of me, 'A woman killed him.'" So his young man thrust him through, and he died.

[55] When the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ saw that ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ was dead, each one departed to his home.

[56] Thus ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ repaid the evil of ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧, which he had done to his father, by killing his seventy brothers.

[57] Also ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ repaid on their heads all the evil of the men of Shechem; and upon them came the curse of ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧ son of ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"men of Shechem" → "lords of Shechem"** (throughout) — Hebrew בַּעֲלֵי שְׁכֶם *ba'alei Shechem*. "Lords / citizens" — city rulers, not commoners.
- **"communed with" → "spoke to"** (v1).
- **"vain and light persons" → "worthless and reckless men"** (v4) — Hebrew רֵיקִים וּפֹחֲזִים *reiqim u-fochazim*.
- **"plain of the pillar" → "oak of the pillar"** (v6) — Hebrew אֵלוֹן מֻצָּב *elon mutzav*. Sacred tree and pillar at Shechem.
- **"adventured his life" → "risked his life"** (v17).
- **"Meonenim" → "Diviners' Oak"** (v37) — Hebrew מְעוֹנְנִים *me'onenim* = "diviners."
- **"hold" → "stronghold"** (v46, 49) — Hebrew צְרִיחַ *tzeriach*.
- **"all to brake" → "crushed"** (v53) — archaic "all to" = completely.
- **"the god Berith" → "El Berit"** (v46) — Hebrew אֵל בְּרִית *El Berit*, "El of the covenant"; the name is *El*, not *Elohim*.

### Notes
- **Abimelech's coup** — son of a Shechemite concubine (8:31), he kills his 70 half-brothers on one stone (the fulfillment of "sons of the maidservant" usurping legitimate inheritance).
- **Jotham's parable** (v7–15) — the "trees choosing a king." Useful fruit-trees refuse; only the worthless bramble accepts. Classic anti-monarchy fable: fit leaders reject power; only worthless seekers-of-office accept.
- **Fire from the bramble** (v15, 20) — prophetic curse: Abimelech will burn the Shechemites, and the Shechemites will destroy Abimelech. Both come true.
- **"Sent an evil spirit"** (v23) — divine-send to cause dissolution. Compare 1 Sam 16:14 (Saul's evil spirit).
- **Salt on the razed city** (v45) — symbolic de-consecration: the city will not be rebuilt. Compare Deut 29:23.
- **Millstone from a woman** (v53) — ironic reversal of Abimelech's pride. A woman kills him; he begs to be finished off to hide the shame. Years later 2 Sam 11:21 remembers him.
- **Jotham's curse fulfilled** (v56–57) — the narrative explicitly notes: *Elohim* worked through the events to repay both Abimelech and Shechem.
