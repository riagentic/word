## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Shibboleth / Sibboleth"** (v6) — Hebrew שִׁבֹּלֶת *shibbolet* = "ear of grain / flowing stream." Pronunciation test: Ephraimites unable to articulate the initial *sh* sound. Famous linguistic password.
- **"nephews" → "grandsons"** (v14) — *nephew* in 1611 English = grandson (Latin *nepos*).
- **"threescore and ten" → "seventy"** (v14).
- **"ass colts" → "donkeys"** (v14).

### Notes
- **Ephraim's jealousy again** (v1–6) — same complaint as with Gideon (8:1–3), but now lethal. Gideon had diplomacy; Jephthah has bloodshed. The judges era degrades.
- **Shibboleth** — first recorded language-based identification. The Ephraimite dialect lacked or differently rendered the *sh* phoneme. Modern term "shibboleth" — a distinguishing mark or test of group membership.
- **42,000 Ephraimites killed** (v6) — civil war scale. Internal Israelite violence increases in the book.
- **Three minor judges** (v8–15):
  - **Ibzan** of Bethlehem (likely the northern Bethlehem in Zebulun). Thirty daughters married out, thirty daughters-in-law brought in: diplomatic / networking pattern.
  - **Elon** of Zebulun, 10 years.
  - **Abdon** of Pirathon (Ephraim), 70 donkey-riding descendants — wealth signal.
- **Total 70 sons/grandsons on donkeys** (cf. Jair in ch 10). The repeated motif: prosperity and many sons, sign of affluent judges. Quasi-monarchical.
