## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v3) — the Tetragrammaton kept.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"the men of Ephraim gathered themselves together" → "the men of Ephraim were called out"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּצָּעֵק אִישׁ אֶפְרַיִם: the nifal of צָעַק "cry out" (summoned by a cry, as 10:17), and אִישׁ "man" in the singular for the whole body; the next verb is singular too, then "they said".
- **"went northward" → "passed over to Zaphon"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲבֹר צָפוֹנָה. The word is either "northward" (צָפוֹן "north") or "to Zaphon", a town in the Jordan valley on the east side (Josh 13:27); the form is the same. The verse has the place; the other reading is KJV's.
- **"didst not call us" → "us you did not call"** (v1) — the Hebrew sets "us" first; so also "Your house we will burn over you". **"passedst thou over" → "pass over"** (v1, 3, 5) — Hebrew עָבַר throughout, the root of מַעְבְּרוֹת "fords" (v5, 6).
- **"I and my people were at great strife with the children of Ammon" → "I was a man of contention, I and my people, and the sons of Ammon, greatly"** (v2) — Hebrew אִישׁ רִיב הָיִיתִי אֲנִי וְעַמִּי וּבְנֵי־עַמּוֹן מְאֹד, word for word. The Hebrew has "and the sons of Ammon", not "with"; "greatly" (מְאֹד) stands at the end.
- **"when I called you, ye delivered me not" → "I cried out to you, and you did not save me"** (v2) — Hebrew וָאֶזְעַק אֶתְכֶם, the verb of 10:10, here with "you" as its object: "I summoned you"; הוֹשִׁיעַ "save".
- **"that ye delivered me not" → "that you were not saving"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּי־אֵינְךָ מוֹשִׁיעַ: a participle, and the "you" is singular (Ephraim as one); no object is written.
- **"I put my life in my hands" → "I put my soul in my palm"** (v3) — Hebrew וָאָשִׂימָה נַפְשִׁי בְכַפִּי; נֶפֶשׁ "soul", כַּף "palm". The saying means to risk one's life (1 Sam 19:5; 28:21).
- **"fought with Ephraim" kept** (v4) — Hebrew נִלְחַם אֶת, "fight with". **"smote" → "struck"** — Hebrew הִכָּה.
- **"Ye Gileadites are fugitives of Ephraim among the Ephraimites, and among the Manassites" → "Fugitives of Ephraim are you, Gilead, in the midst of Ephraim, in the midst of Manasseh"** (v4) — Hebrew פְּלִיטֵי אֶפְרַיִם אַתֶּם גִּלְעָד בְּתוֹךְ אֶפְרַיִם בְּתוֹךְ מְנַשֶּׁה, word for word; the names are the tribes' names, and there is no "and" before the last. Who says this to whom, and what the taunt means, is not clear; the words are kept as they stand. פָּלִיט is "fugitive, one who has escaped"; the same phrase, "the fugitives of Ephraim", names the fleeing Ephraimites in v5.
- **"took the passages of Jordan before the Ephraimites" → "captured the fords of the Jordan toward Ephraim"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיִּלְכֹּד … אֶת־מַעְבְּרוֹת הַיַּרְדֵּן לְאֶפְרָיִם, as 3:28; לְ is "to, for, belonging to": the fords that lead to Ephraim, or held against Ephraim.
- **"and it was so, that when … said" → "And it would be, when … said"** (v5) — Hebrew וְהָיָה כִּי יֹאמְרוּ: the forms tell of what happened each time. "Let me pass over" and "he said" are singular: each man for himself.
- **"Art thou an Ephraimite?" (v5)** — Hebrew הַאֶפְרָתִי אַתָּה, "an Ephrathite", the form used for a man of Ephraim (1 Sam 1:1; 1 Kgs 11:26).
- **"If he said, Nay; then said they" → "And he said, 'No.' And they said"** (v5, 6) — the Hebrew has no "if … then": the clauses follow one another with "and".
- **"Say now Shibboleth: and he said Sibboleth" kept** (v6) — Hebrew שִׁבֹּלֶת *shibbolet* and סִבֹּלֶת *sibbolet*; both words are left as sounds, because the test is the sound. שִׁבֹּלֶת means "ear of grain" (Gen 41:5; Ruth 2:2) and also "flood, flowing stream" (Ps 69:2, 15; Isa 27:12); which is meant here is of no weight. The man of Ephraim says *s* for *sh*.
- **"for he could not frame to pronounce it right" → "and he could not manage to speak so"** (v6) — Hebrew וְלֹא יָכִין לְדַבֵּר כֵּן, literally "and he would not make firm / make ready to speak thus": he did not get it right. Some Hebrew manuscripts read יָבִין "understand" (cited from memory).
- **"took him, and slew him" → "took hold of him and slaughtered him"** (v6) — Hebrew אָחַז "take hold of" and שָׁחַט "slaughter", the word for killing an animal by cutting the throat (Gen 22:10). **"at the passages" → "at the fords"** — Hebrew אֶל־מַעְבְּרוֹת, "to / at".
- **"forty and two thousand" → "forty-two thousand"** (v6).
- **"was buried in one of the cities of Gilead" → "was buried in the cities of Gilead"** (v7) — Hebrew בְּעָרֵי גִלְעָד, a plural, as it stands. "One of" is supplied by KJV; the Septuagint has "in his city in Gilead" (cited from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"whom he sent abroad, and took in thirty daughters from abroad for his sons" → "thirty daughters he sent away outside, and thirty daughters he brought in for his sons from outside"** (v9) — Hebrew שִׁלַּח הַחוּצָה … הֵבִיא … מִן־הַחוּץ: "outside". Outside of what (his house, his clan, his town) is not said; "in marriage" is not written.
- **"Elon … Aijalon"** (v11, 12) — the two names have the same consonants (אילון); only the vowels differ.
- **"thirty nephews, that rode on threescore and ten ass colts" → "thirty sons of sons, riding on seventy donkey colts"** (v14) — Hebrew בְּנֵי בָנִים "sons of sons"; עֲיָרִם "donkey colts", as 10:4.
- **"in the mount of the Amalekites" → "in the hill country of the Amalekite"** (v15) — Hebrew בְּהַר הָעֲמָלֵקִי, a singular with the article.

### Notes
- **Ephraim again** (v1–6) — the same complaint as to Gideon (8:1–3); there it was turned aside with words, here it ends with forty-two thousand dead.
- **Shibboleth** — a test by speech: the men of Ephraim said *s* where the men of Gilead said *sh*. The English word "shibboleth", a mark that tells a group apart, comes from this verse.
- **Three minor judges** (v8–15):
  - **Ibzan** from Bethlehem (Judah's Bethlehem or the one in Zebulun, Josh 19:15: the text does not say), seven years.
  - **Elon** the Zebulunite, ten years.
  - **Abdon** from Pirathon in Ephraim, eight years; seventy sons and grandsons on donkey colts (cf. Jair, 10:4).
