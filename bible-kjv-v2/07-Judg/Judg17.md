# Judges 17

[1] And there was a man from the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, and his name was ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧.

[2] And he said to his mother, "The eleven hundred of silver that was taken from you, and you, you uttered a curse and also said it in my ears — behold, the silver is with me; I, I took it." And his mother said, "Blessed be my son by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[3] And he returned the eleven hundred of silver to his mother, and his mother said, "I have surely sanctified the silver to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from my hand for my son, to make a carved image and a molten image; and now I will return it to you."

[4] And he returned the silver to his mother, and his mother took two hundred of silver and gave it to the refiner, and he made it into a carved image and a molten image, and it was in the house of ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧.

[5] And the man ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧ had a house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and he made an ephod and ⟦teraphim|teraphim|household gods⟧, and he filled the hand of one of his sons, and he became priest to him.

[6] In those days there was no king in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; each man did what was right in his own eyes.

[7] And there was a young man from ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧ of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, from the clan of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and he was a ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧, and he was sojourning there.

[8] And the man went from the city, from ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧ of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, to sojourn wherever he might find, and he came to the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, as far as the house of ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧, in making his way.

[9] And ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧ said to him, "From where do you come?" And he said to him, "I am a ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧ from ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧ of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and I am going to sojourn wherever I may find."

[10] And ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧ said to him, "Dwell with me, and be to me a father and a priest, and I, I will give you ten of silver for the days, and a set of garments, and your sustenance." And the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧ went.

[11] And the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧ undertook to dwell with the man, and the young man became to him like one of his sons.

[12] And ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧ filled the hand of the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧, and the young man became priest to him, and he was in the house of ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧.

[13] And ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧ said, "Now I know that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will do good to me, for the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧ has become priest to me."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"mount Ephraim" → "the hill country of Ephraim"** (v1, 8) — Hebrew הַר אֶפְרַיִם: הַר "mountain", of a region.
- **"Micah"** (v1, 4) — in these two verses the Hebrew has the long form of the name, מִיכָיְהוּ *Mikhayehu* ("Who is like Yahweh?"); from v5 on the short form מִיכָה *Mikhah*. One English form is kept.
- **"The eleven hundred shekels of silver" → "The eleven hundred of silver"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew אֶלֶף וּמֵאָה הַכֶּסֶף, "the thousand and hundred of silver"; no unit is named (so also "two hundred of silver", v4; "ten of silver", v10). The sum of 16:5.
- **"that were taken from thee, about which thou cursedst, and spakest of also in mine ears" → "that was taken from you, and you, you uttered a curse and also said it in my ears"** (v2) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר לֻקַּח־לָךְ וְאַתְּ אָלִית וְגַם אָמַרְתְּ בְּאָזְנַי. אָלָה is to utter a curse (on the unknown thief), the verb of the noun "curse / oath". The sentence breaks off and begins again with "behold". The written text (ketiv) has the old form ואתי for "you"; the qere is וְאַתְּ. "It" is supplied.
- **"Blessed be thou of the LORD, my son" → "Blessed be my son by Yahweh"** (v2) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ בְּנִי לַיהוָה, said of the son, not to him; לַיהוָה is literally "to / for Yahweh".
- **"restored" → "returned"** (v3, 4) — Hebrew הֵשִׁיב, the same verb in "I will return it to you".
- **"I had wholly dedicated" → "I have surely sanctified"** (v3) — Hebrew הַקְדֵּשׁ הִקְדַּשְׁתִּי, infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "sanctifying I have sanctified"; the perfect may also be the act itself, "I hereby sanctify".
- **"a graven image and a molten image" → "a carved image and a molten image"** (v3, 4) — Hebrew פֶּסֶל וּמַסֵּכָה, the two words of Deut 27:15: an image carved (of wood or stone) and an image cast of metal. In v4 the verb that follows is singular, "and it was": whether one object or two are meant is not said (18:17, 18 name them apart; 18:20, 30, 31 name only the carved image).
- **"now therefore I will restore it unto thee" → "and now I will return it to you"** (v3) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה אֲשִׁיבֶנּוּ לָךְ.
- **"the founder" → "the refiner"** (v4) — Hebrew צוֹרֵף, one who smelts and refines metal: a gold- or silversmith. **"who made thereof" → "and he made it into"** — Hebrew וַיַּעֲשֵׂהוּ "and he made it" (the two hundred of the eleven hundred).
- **"had an house of gods" → "had a house of Elohim"** (v5) — Hebrew בֵּית אֱלֹהִים, without the article: "a house of Elohim" (the words used of the house at Shiloh, 18:31, there with the article) or "a house of gods" (cf. 18:24 "my gods that I made"). The word is the same; the verse does not decide.
- **"ephod, and teraphim"** (v5) — Hebrew אֵפוֹד וּתְרָפִים. The ephod is the priestly garment of Exod 28, or here a thing made after it and used to ask a god (cf. 8:27); *teraphim* are household images (Gen 31:19). What exactly either was in Micah's house is not known; the words are kept.
- **"consecrated one of his sons" → "filled the hand of one of his sons"** (v5, 12) — Hebrew וַיְמַלֵּא אֶת־יַד, word for word; it is the term for installing a priest (Exod 28:41; 29:9).
- **"who became his priest" → "and he became priest to him"** (v5, 12, 13) — Hebrew וַיְהִי־לוֹ לְכֹהֵן.
- **"every man did that which was right in his own eyes" → "each man did what was right in his own eyes"** (v6) — Hebrew אִישׁ הַיָּשָׁר בְּעֵינָיו יַעֲשֶׂה, literally "a man, the right in his eyes he would do". הַיָּשָׁר is "what is straight, upright", as Deut 12:8. The verb is an imperfect, of what used to be done: "would do". "Own" is not a separate word. The sentence returns in 21:25, and its first half in 18:1; 19:1; one wording is kept.
- **"of the family of Judah" → "from the clan of Judah"** (v7) — Hebrew מִמִּשְׁפַּחַת יְהוּדָה. A Levite is not of the tribe of Judah by descent; he lived among it. **"Bethlehemjudah" → "Bethlehem of Judah"** (v7, 8, 9) — Hebrew בֵּית לֶחֶם יְהוּדָה.
- **"young man"** (v7, 11, 12) — Hebrew נַעַר, "boy", also a grown young man or an attendant; in v8 he is "the man".
- **"where he could find a place" → "wherever he might find"** (v8, 9) — Hebrew בַּאֲשֶׁר יִמְצָא; "a place" is not written. **"as he journeyed" → "in making his way"** — Hebrew לַעֲשׂוֹת דַּרְכּוֹ, literally "to make his way".
- **"Whence comest thou?"** (v9) — Hebrew מֵאַיִן תָּבוֹא.
- **"ten shekels of silver by the year, and a suit of apparel, and thy victuals. So the Levite went in" → "ten of silver for the days, and a set of garments, and your sustenance. And the Levite went"** (v10) — Hebrew לַיָּמִים "for the days", that is, by the year (as "days" in 1 Sam 1:3; 27:7); עֵרֶךְ בְּגָדִים, literally "an arrangement of garments"; מִחְיָה "what one lives on". וַיֵּלֶךְ הַלֵּוִי is "and the Levite went": the Hebrew says no more ("in" is not written).
- **"was content to dwell" → "undertook to dwell"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיּוֹאֶל, as in Exod 2:21; Josh 17:12.
- **"seeing I have a Levite to my priest" → "for the Levite has become priest to me"** (v13) — Hebrew כִּי הָיָה־לִי הַלֵּוִי לְכֹהֵן, with the article: "the Levite".

### Notes
- **Epilogue narrative begins** — Judges 17–21 forms a two-story appendix: the story of Micah's house and the Danite migration (17–18); and the war with Benjamin (19–21).
- **"No king in Israel / each man did what was right in his own eyes"** (v6) — key refrain of the appendix (also 18:1; 19:1; 21:25).
- **Micah's silver** — eleven hundred of silver, the sum of 16:5. Taken, cursed, returned, sanctified to Yahweh, and two hundred of it made into an image.
- **"Sanctified to Yahweh … to make a carved image"** (v3) — the mother sees no conflict between the two; the law forbids it (Exod 20:4; Deut 27:15).
- **The Levite** — a Levite without a place, hired as priest of a house. In 18:30 he is named: Jonathan the son of Gershom.
- **"The Levite has become priest to me"** (v13) — Micah expects good from Yahweh because of it.
