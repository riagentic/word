## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"trumpet" → "horn"** (v27) — Hebrew שׁוֹפָר *shofar* = ram's horn, not a metal trumpet.
- **"Mesopotamia" → "Aram-naharaim"** (v8) — proper name.
- **"Spirit of the Lord" → "spirit of Yahweh"** (v10) — kept.
- **"quarries" → "sculptured stones"** (v19, 26) — Hebrew פְּסִילִים *pesilim* = idols / sculptured stones. Likely pagan cultic monuments near Gilgal.
- **"summer parlour / cool chamber" → "upper cool room"** (v20, 24) — Hebrew עֲלִיַּת הַמְּקֵרָה *aliyat ha-meqerah*. Upper-story ventilated room.
- **"covereth his feet" → "relieving himself"** (v24) — Hebrew euphemism for defecation.
- **"the dirt came out" → "the contents came out"** (v22) — Hebrew *parshedonah* (hapax); likely "feces / excrement came out" — graphic detail emphasizing Eglon's humiliation.
- **"haft" → "hilt"** (v22) — modernized.
- **"lusty" → "strong"** (v29) — Hebrew *shamen* = fat / stout.
- **"fourscore" → "eighty"** (v30).

### Notes
- **Cycle established** — ch 3 launches the first round: sin → oppression → cry → deliverer → peace → sin again.
- **Othniel** (v7–11) — Caleb's nephew / younger kinsman (the Hebrew can mean either). First judge; minimal narrative detail.
- **Ehud's assassination of Eglon** (v15–30) — extended comedic grotesquery. Eglon's name puns on "fattened calf." Left-handed Benjamite (Benjamin means "son of my right hand" — ironic). The Moab king becomes the sacrificial "fat."
- **Shamgar** (v31) — the briefest judge. Only 1 verse. Mother "Anath" (a Canaanite goddess name) suggests a possibly non-Israelite warrior-ally. Six hundred Philistines with an ox-goad — legendary strength.
- **Eighty years peace** (v30) — longest rest-period in Judges. The middle judges grow shorter; the pattern degrades over time.
