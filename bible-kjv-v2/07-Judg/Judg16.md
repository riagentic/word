# Judges 16

[1] And ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧ went to ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧, and he saw there a woman, a prostitute, and he went in to her.

[2] To ⟦gazites|the Azzatim|the Gazites⟧, saying, "⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧ has come here." And they went around and lay in ambush for him all the night at the gate of the city, and they kept silent all the night, saying, "Until the light of the morning, and we will kill him."

[3] And ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧ lay until midnight, and he arose at midnight and took hold of the doors of the gate of the city and of the two doorposts, and he pulled them up with the bar and put them on his shoulders and brought them up to the top of the mountain that is facing ⟦hebron|Chevron|Hebron⟧.

[4] And it came to pass afterward that he loved a woman in the wadi of ⟦sorek|Soreq|Sorek⟧, and her name was Delilah.

[5] And the lords of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ went up to her and said to her, "Entice him, and see by what his power is great, and by what we may overcome him, and we will bind him to afflict him; and we, we will give you each man eleven hundred of silver."

[6] And Delilah said to ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧, "Tell me, please, by what your power is great, and by what you may be bound, to afflict you."

[7] And ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧ said to her, "If they bind me with seven fresh bowstrings that have not been dried, then I shall become weak and be like one of mankind."

[8] And the lords of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ brought up to her seven fresh bowstrings that had not been dried, and she bound him with them.

[9] And the ambush was sitting for her in the chamber. And she said to him, "The ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ are upon you, ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧!" And he snapped the bowstrings as a strand of tow is snapped when it smells fire. And his power was not known.

[10] And Delilah said to ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧, "Behold, you have cheated me and spoken lies to me. Now tell me, please, by what you may be bound."

[11] And he said to her, "If they surely bind me with new cords with which no work has been done, then I shall become weak and be like one of mankind."

[12] And Delilah took new cords and bound him with them and said to him, "The ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ are upon you, ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧!" And the ambush was sitting in the chamber. And he snapped them from off his arms like a thread.

[13] And Delilah said to ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧, "Until now you have cheated me and spoken lies to me. Tell me by what you may be bound." And he said to her, "If you weave the seven locks of my head with the web —"

[14] And she thrust with the peg and said to him, "The ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ are upon you, ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧!" And he awoke from his sleep and pulled up the peg of the loom and the web.

[15] And she said to him, "How do you say, 'I love you,' and your heart is not with me? These three times you have cheated me and have not told me by what your power is great."

[16] And it came to pass, when she straitened him with her words all the days and pressed him, that his soul was short to death.

[17] And he told her all his heart and said to her, "A razor has not gone up on my head, for I am a ⟦nazirite|nazir|Nazirite⟧ of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ from the belly of my mother. If I am shaved, then my power will turn aside from me, and I shall become weak and be like all mankind."

[18] And Delilah saw that he had told her all his heart, and she sent and called for the lords of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, saying, "Come up this time, for he has told me all his heart." And the lords of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ came up to her and brought up the silver in their hand.

[19] And she made him sleep upon her knees, and she called for the man and shaved the seven locks of his head. And she began to afflict him, and his power turned aside from upon him.

[20] And she said, "The ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ are upon you, ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧!" And he awoke from his sleep and said, "I will go out as time after time and shake myself." And he did not know that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had turned aside from upon him.

[21] And the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ took hold of him and gouged out his eyes, and they brought him down to ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧ and bound him with fetters of bronze, and he was grinding in the house of the bound.

[22] And the hair of his head began to spring up when it had been shaved.

[23] And the lords of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ gathered to sacrifice a great sacrifice to Dagon their god, and for gladness, and they said,

"Our god has given into our hand
⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧ our enemy."

[24] And the people saw him and praised their god, for they said,

"Our god has given into our hand our enemy,
and the one who laid waste our land,
and who multiplied our slain."

[25] And it came to pass, when their heart was good, that they said, "Call for ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧, and let him play for us." And they called for ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧ from the house of the bound, and he played before them, and they stood him between the pillars.

[26] And ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧ said to the boy who was holding him fast by his hand, "Leave me, and let me feel the pillars on which the house is established, and let me lean on them."

[27] And the house was full of the men and the women, and all the lords of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ were there, and on the roof were about three thousand, man and woman, who were looking on at the playing of ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧.

[28] And ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧ called to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and said, "⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧, remember me, please, and make me strong, please, only this time, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and let me avenge myself, one vengeance for my two eyes, on the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧."

[29] And ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧ grasped the two pillars of the middle, on which the house was established, and he braced himself on them, one with his right hand and one with his left.

[30] And ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧ said, "Let my soul die with the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧." And he bent with power, and the house fell on the lords and on all the people who were in it. And the dead whom he put to death in his death were more than those whom he put to death in his life.

[31] And his brothers and all the house of his father came down and lifted him up, and they went up and buried him between ⟦zorah|Tzorah|Zorah⟧ and Eshtaol, in the grave of ⟦manoah|Manoach|Manoah⟧ his father. And he had judged ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ twenty years.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v17, 28) — Hebrew *Elohim*. Of Dagon the same word is "their god", "our god" (v23, 24).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"an harlot" → "a woman, a prostitute"** (v1) — Hebrew אִשָּׁה זוֹנָה, two nouns, as in Josh 2:1.
- **"And it was told the Gazites, saying" → "To the Gazites, saying"** (v2) — the Masoretic Text has only לַעַזָּתִים לֵאמֹר, "to the Gazites, saying": no verb stands before it. The Greek has "and it was told" (cited from memory); nothing is supplied here.
- **"compassed him in, and laid wait for him" → "went around and lay in ambush for him"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיָּסֹבּוּ, without an object ("went around", or "surrounded" the place), and אָרַב "lie in ambush", the root of "the ambush" in v9, 12. **"were quiet" → "kept silent"** — Hebrew וַיִּתְחָרְשׁוּ.
- **"In the morning, when it is day, we shall kill him" → "Until the light of the morning, and we will kill him"** (v2) — Hebrew עַד־אוֹר הַבֹּקֶר וַהֲרְגְנֻהוּ; the saying has no verb before "until" ("wait" is not written).
- **"took the doors … and went away with them, bar and all" → "took hold of the doors … and he pulled them up with the bar"** (v3) — Hebrew אָחַז בְּ "take hold of" (the verb of v21) and וַיִּסָּעֵם "pulled them up" (the verb of v14; the usual word for pulling up tent pegs). **"carried them up to the top of an hill that is before Hebron" → "brought them up to the top of the mountain that is facing Hebron"** — Hebrew הָהָר with the article; עַל־פְּנֵי "on the face of" may be "facing, in the direction of" or "in front of, near".
- **"midnight"** (v3) — Hebrew חֲצִי הַלַּיְלָה, literally "the half of the night", twice.
- **"in the valley of Sorek" → "in the wadi of Sorek"** (v4) — Hebrew נַחַל, a stream bed. The verse does not say of what people Delilah was.
- **"lords"** (v5, 8, 18, 23, 27, 30) — Hebrew סְרָנִים, a word used only of the five rulers of the Philistines (Josh 13:3).
- **"see wherein his great strength lieth, and by what means we may prevail against him, that we may bind him to afflict him" → "see by what his power is great, and by what we may overcome him, and we will bind him to afflict him"** (v5) — Hebrew בַּמֶּה כֹּחוֹ גָדוֹל, literally "in what his power is great"; כֹּחַ is the bodily power of v9, 15, 17, 19, 30. לְעַנֹּתוֹ is from עִנָּה "afflict, bring low" (v6, 19).
- **"eleven hundred pieces of silver" → "eleven hundred of silver"** (v5) — Hebrew אֶלֶף וּמֵאָה כָּסֶף, "a thousand and a hundred of silver"; no unit is named. The same sum stands in 17:2.
- **The four askings** (v6, 10, 13, 15) — the Hebrew varies them, and the verses follow it: "Tell me, please, by what your power is great, and by what you may be bound, to afflict you" (v6); "Now tell me, please, by what you may be bound" (v10); "Tell me by what you may be bound" (v13, without נָא "please"); "you have not told me by what your power is great" (v15).
- **The answers** (v7, 11, 17) — "I shall become weak and be like one of mankind" (v7, 11: כְּאַחַד הָאָדָם) and "like all mankind" (v17: כְּכָל־הָאָדָם); וְחָלִיתִי is "become weak" (the verb is also "be sick"). "Another man" (KJV) is not in the Hebrew.
- **"green withs that were never dried" → "fresh bowstrings that have not been dried"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew יְתָרִים לַחִים, cords of gut or sinew, still moist; the word is used of the string of a bow (Ps 11:2) and of a tent cord (Job 30:11).
- **"there were men lying in wait, abiding with her in the chamber" → "the ambush was sitting for her in the chamber"** (v9, 12) — Hebrew וְהָאֹרֵב יֹשֵׁב לָהּ בַּחֶדֶר: a singular, "the one lying in ambush", used of the whole party; לָהּ is "for her" (in v12 it is not written).
- **"he brake the withs, as a thread of tow is broken when it toucheth the fire" → "he snapped the bowstrings as a strand of tow is snapped when it smells fire"** (v9) — one verb twice, נִתֵּק / יִנָּתֵק; בַּהֲרִיחוֹ אֵשׁ is literally "when it smells fire". פְּתִיל is a twisted cord; "strand" is used because "cords" stands for עֲבֹתִים (v11, 12).
- **"mocked me, and told me lies" → "cheated me and spoken lies to me"** (v10, 13, 15) — Hebrew הֵתַלְתָּ בִּי, "trifle with, deceive".
- **"new ropes that never were occupied" → "new cords with which no work has been done"** (v11, 12) — Hebrew עֲבֹתִים חֲדָשִׁים, the words of 15:13; אֲשֶׁר לֹא־נַעֲשָׂה בָהֶם מְלָאכָה. "Surely bind" is the infinitive absolute, אָסוֹר יַאַסְרוּנִי "binding they bind me".
- **v13–14: the Masoretic Text is shorter than the Greek** — in the Hebrew Samson's third answer breaks off: "If you weave the seven locks of my head with the web —", and v14 goes on "And she thrust with the peg". The Greek has more between the two (in substance: "and fasten it with the peg into the wall, then I shall become weak and be like one of mankind. And it came to pass, when he slept, that Delilah took the seven locks of his head and wove them with the web": cited from memory), and many translations fill the verse from it. Nothing is supplied here; the dash marks the place.
- **"locks"** (v13, 19) — Hebrew מַחְלְפוֹת, found only in this chapter: plaits or locks of hair. **"web"** — Hebrew מַסֶּכֶת, found only here: the warp threads stretched on the loom. Both are uncertain in detail.
- **"she fastened it with the pin" → "she thrust with the peg"** (v14) — Hebrew וַתִּתְקַע בַּיָּתֵד: תָּקַע "thrust, drive in", יָתֵד "peg"; what she drove it into is not said. **"went away with the pin of the beam" → "pulled up the peg of the loom"** — Hebrew הַיְתַד הָאֶרֶג, literally "the peg, the weaving": the grammar is unusual (the first noun has the article); אֶרֶג is the weaving or the loom.
- **"How canst thou say" → "How do you say"** (v15) — Hebrew אֵיךְ תֹּאמַר. **"when thine heart is not with me" → "and your heart is not with me"** — Hebrew וְ "and".
- **"she pressed him daily with her words, and urged him, so that his soul was vexed unto death" → "she straitened him with her words all the days and pressed him, that his soul was short to death"** (v16) — Hebrew הֵצִיקָה לּוֹ "put him in straits"; וַתְּאַלֲצֵהוּ is found only here ("pressed, urged": uncertain); וַתִּקְצַר נַפְשׁוֹ לָמוּת, literally "his soul was short, to die": a soul that is "short" has no more patience (Num 21:4). נֶפֶשׁ is "soul".
- **"There hath not come a razor upon mine head; for I have been a Nazarite unto God from my mother's womb" → "A razor has not gone up on my head, for I am a nazir of Elohim from the belly of my mother"** (v17) — the words of 13:5. Hebrew נְזִיר אֱלֹהִים, "a *nazir* of Elohim" (13:5, 7 "a *nazir* of Elohim … from the belly"): one kept apart for Elohim (Num 6). מוֹרָה "razor" is another word than the תַּעַר of Num 6:5. בֶּטֶן is "belly".
- **"my strength will go from me" → "my power will turn aside from me"** (v17, 19, 20) — one Hebrew verb, סוּר "turn aside, depart", three times: of his power (v17 מִמֶּנִּי "from me", v19 מֵעָלָיו "from upon him") and of Yahweh (v20 יְהוָה סָר מֵעָלָיו "Yahweh had turned aside from upon him").
- **"he hath shewed me all his heart"** (v18) — the written text (ketiv) has לה "told *her*", the reading tradition (qere) לִי "told *me*"; the qere is translated. **"brought money" → "brought up the silver"** — Hebrew הַכֶּסֶף. The form וְעָלוּ ("and they came up") is an unusual one in narrative.
- **"she called for a man, and she caused him to shave off" → "she called for the man and shaved"** (v19) — Hebrew לָאִישׁ "the man" (with the article), and וַתְּגַלַּח, "and *she* shaved": the verb is feminine. Whether she did it by the man's hand is not said. **"his strength went from him"** — see v17.
- **"I will go out as at other times before, and shake myself" → "I will go out as time after time and shake myself"** (v20) — Hebrew כְּפַעַם בְּפַעַם, as in Num 24:1.
- **"the LORD was departed from him" → "Yahweh had turned aside from upon him"** (v20) — see v17.
- **"took him, and put out his eyes" → "took hold of him and gouged out his eyes"** (v21) — Hebrew אָחַז (v3) and נִקֵּר "bore out, gouge".
- **"fetters of brass" → "fetters of bronze"** (v21) — Hebrew נְחֻשְׁתַּיִם, a dual: "two bronzes", a pair of bronze fetters.
- **"he did grind in the prison house" → "he was grinding in the house of the bound"** (v21, 25) — the qere is הָאֲסוּרִים "the bound ones", from אָסַר "bind", the verb of this whole story (v5–13, 21); the ketiv is האסירים "the prisoners". Grinding grain with the hand mill was the work of slave women (Exod 11:5).
- **"began to grow again after he was shaven" → "began to spring up when it had been shaved"** (v22) — Hebrew לְצַמֵּחַ "sprout"; כַּאֲשֶׁר גֻּלָּח is "when / as soon as it (or he) had been shaved". "Again" is not written.
- **"to offer a great sacrifice … and to rejoice" → "to sacrifice a great sacrifice … and for gladness"** (v23) — Hebrew לִזְבֹּחַ זֶבַח־גָּדוֹל … וּלְשִׂמְחָה, verb and noun of one root, then a noun. **"our enemy"** (v23) — the form אוֹיְבֵינוּ is written as a plural ("our enemies"); v24 has the singular אוֹיְבֵנוּ.
- **"the destroyer of our country, which slew many of us" → "the one who laid waste our land, and who multiplied our slain"** (v24) — Hebrew מַחֲרִיב אַרְצֵנוּ וַאֲשֶׁר הִרְבָּה אֶת־חֲלָלֵינוּ. The two sayings of v23, 24 rhyme in Hebrew on the ending *-enu* ("our").
- **"when their hearts were merry" → "when their heart was good"** (v25) — the qere כְּטוֹב לִבָּם; the ketiv כי טוב is "because their heart was good".
- **"that he may make us sport … and he made them sport" → "let him play for us … and he played before them"** (v25, 27) — Hebrew וִישַׂחֶק and וַיְצַחֵק, two spellings of one verb (שׂחק / צחק, the root of the name Isaac), "laugh, play"; v27 has the noun, בִּשְׂחוֹק "at the playing". What kind of play is meant is not said: music, dancing, a show of strength, or being made a laughingstock.
- **"the lad that held him by the hand, Suffer me that I may feel" → "the boy who was holding him fast by his hand, Leave me, and let me feel"** (v26) — Hebrew נַעַר "boy" (also a young attendant); הַנִּיחָה אוֹתִי "leave me, let me be"; the qere וַהֲמִשֵׁנִי "let me feel" (the ketiv is another form of the same sense). **"whereupon the house standeth" → "on which the house is established"** (v26, 29) — Hebrew נָכוֹן.
- **"men and women"** (v27) — the Hebrew has the article, "the men and the women"; then אִישׁ וְאִשָּׁה "man and woman".
- **"O Lord GOD, remember me, I pray thee, and strengthen me, I pray thee, only this once, O God" → "Lord Yahweh, remember me, please, and make me strong, please, only this time, Elohim"** (v28) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יֱהֹוִה … הָאֱלֹהִים: both names of the address are kept, *Adonai Yahweh* at its head and *Elohim* (with the article, "the Elohim") within; נָא "please" twice. "O" is not written.
- **"that I may be at once avenged of the Philistines for my two eyes" → "and let me avenge myself, one vengeance for my two eyes, on the Philistines"** (v28) — Hebrew וְאִנָּקְמָה נְקַם־אַחַת מִשְּׁתֵי עֵינַי מִפְּלִשְׁתִּים, literally "and let me avenge myself a vengeance of one, from my two eyes, from the Philistines". It may also be read "vengeance for one of my two eyes".
- **"took hold of the two middle pillars … and on which it was borne up" → "grasped the two pillars of the middle … and he braced himself on them"** (v29) — Hebrew וַיִּלְפֹּת, a rare verb (Ruth 3:8; Job 6:18): "grasp" or "twist, turn about"; וַיִּסָּמֵךְ עֲלֵיהֶם "he leaned / pressed himself on them" (another verb than "lean" in v26).
- **"Let me die" → "Let my soul die"** (v30) — Hebrew תָּמוֹת נַפְשִׁי. **"he bowed himself with all his might" → "he bent with power"** — Hebrew וַיֵּט בְּכֹחַ: נָטָה "stretch out, bend"; "all his" is not written. **"slew" → "put to death"** — Hebrew הֵמִית.
- **"all the house of his father … took him, and brought him up" → "… lifted him up, and they went up"** (v31) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׂאוּ אֹתוֹ וַיַּעֲלוּ; the second verb has no object. **"buryingplace" → "grave"** — Hebrew קֶבֶר. **"he judged" → "he had judged"** — Hebrew וְהוּא שָׁפַט, a look back (15:20).

### Notes
- **Gaza gate** (v1–3) — Samson brings the doors of the city gate up to the mountain facing Hebron; Hebron lies some 60 km from Gaza, in the hill country.
- **Delilah** — the meaning of her name is not certain. Her people is not stated; she is not called a Philistine, but she works for their lords.
- **Eleven hundred of silver** (v5) — from each of the lords of the Philistines (five, Josh 13:3). The same sum appears in 17:2 (Micah's silver).
- **Four attempts** (v7, 11, 13, 17) — Samson answers falsely three times and tells all his heart the fourth.
- **"Yahweh had turned aside from upon him"** (v20) — Samson does not know. His power was never merely bodily (14:6, 19; 15:14).
- **Dagon** — a god of the Philistines; the meaning of the name is disputed ("grain" or "fish"). He falls before the ark in 1 Sam 5.
- **Final prayer and death** (v28–30) — the second prayer of Samson (15:18). More put to death in his death than in his life.
- **Heb 11:32** — Samson is named among those who acted by faith.
