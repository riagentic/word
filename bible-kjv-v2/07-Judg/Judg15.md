# Judges 15

[1] After some time, at the time of wheat harvest, ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧ visited his wife with a young goat. He said, "I will go in to my wife into the chamber." But her father would not let him go in.

[2] Her father said, "I really thought that you utterly hated her, so I gave her to your companion. Is not her younger sister more beautiful than she? Please take her instead."

[3] ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧ said to them, "This time I shall be blameless regarding the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ when I do them harm."

[4] ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧ went and caught three hundred foxes; he took torches, turned them tail to tail, and put a torch between every pair of tails.

[5] When he had lit the torches, he let the foxes go into the standing grain of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧; he burned up the sheaves, the standing grain, and also the vineyards and olive groves.

[6] Then the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ said, "Who has done this?" They answered, "⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧, the son-in-law of the ⟦timnite|Timni|Timnite⟧, because he took his wife and gave her to his companion." So the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ came up and burned her and her father with fire.

[7] ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧ said to them, "Since you act in this way, I will surely take revenge on you, and after that I will quit."

[8] He struck them leg and thigh with a great slaughter. Then he went down and lived in the cleft of the rock of ⟦etam|Eitam|Etam⟧.

[9] The ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ went up and encamped in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and spread out in ⟦lehi|Lechi|Lehi⟧.

[10] The men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ said, "Why have you come up against us?" They answered, "We have come up to bind ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧, to do to him as he has done to us."

[11] Then three thousand men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ went down to the cleft of the rock of ⟦etam|Eitam|Etam⟧ and said to ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧, "Do you not know that the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ rule over us? What is this you have done to us?" He said to them, "As they did to me, so I have done to them."

[12] They said to him, "We have come down to bind you, that we may give you into the hand of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧." ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧ said to them, "Swear to me that you will not fall upon me yourselves."

[13] They said to him, "No, we will only bind you and give you into their hand; we will surely not kill you." So they bound him with two new cords and brought him up from the rock.

[14] When he came to ⟦lehi|Lechi|Lehi⟧, the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ shouted as they met him; and the spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ rushed upon him, and the cords that were on his arms became like flax that has caught fire, and his bonds melted off his hands.

[15] He found a fresh jawbone of a donkey; he reached out his hand and took it, and with it he struck down a thousand men.

[16] ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧ said:

"With the jawbone of a donkey,
heap upon heap!
With the jawbone of a donkey
I have struck down a thousand men!"

[17] When he had finished speaking, he threw the jawbone from his hand; and that place was called ⟦ramath-lehi|Ramat Lechi|Ramath-lehi⟧ — "Jawbone Hill."

[18] He was very thirsty, and he called on ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and said, "You have given this great deliverance into the hand of your servant. Now shall I die of thirst and fall into the hand of the uncircumcised?"

[19] So ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ split open the hollow place that is at ⟦lehi|Lechi|Lehi⟧, and water came out of it. When he drank, his spirit returned and he revived. Therefore he named it ⟦en-hakkore|Ein HaQore|En-hakkore⟧ ("the spring of him who called"), which is at ⟦lehi|Lechi|Lehi⟧ to this day.

[20] He judged ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ in the days of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ twenty years.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"corn" → "grain"** (v5).
- **"hip and thigh" → "leg and thigh"** (v8) — Hebrew שׁוֹק עַל־יָרֵךְ *shoq al yarekh*. Archaic English idiom.
- **"ass" → "donkey"** (v15, 16).
- **"sore athirst" → "very thirsty"** (v18) — archaic.
- **Hebrew wordplay in v16** — "heap upon heap" is *chamor chamorotayim*, playing on *chamor* (donkey) and *chomer* (heap).

### Notes
- **Samson's feud** — the Philistines burn his wife + father-in-law; he massacres in retaliation; they besiege Judah; he kills 1,000 with a donkey jawbone.
- **Foxes and firebrands** (v4–5) — ingenious arson. 300 torch-trailing foxes scattered through wheat fields at harvest time. Ecological devastation.
- **Judah delivers him up** (v11–13) — Judah is a vassal to the Philistines; does not defend Samson. Three thousand Judahites bind one Israelite to hand him to the oppressor. The tribes do not support their own judge.
- **Jawbone of a donkey** — donkey bones are bone-dry and can break easily. His 1,000-kill with this "unclean" weapon underscores miraculous strength.
- **Samson's prayer** (v18) — rare moment of prayer. Yahweh answers by providing water. A glimpse of a relationship beyond brute exploits.
- **"Twenty years"** (v20) — Samson's judgeship. Repeated at 16:31 — closing formula of his career.
