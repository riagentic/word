## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"defend Israel" → "deliver Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew *le-hoshia*. To save.
- **"ass colts" → "donkeys"** (v4) — modernized.
- **"vexed" → "crushed"** (v8) — Hebrew רָעַץ *ra'atz*. Broken / crushed.
- **"sore distressed" → "in great distress"** (v9).
- **"strange gods" → "foreign gods"** (v16).
- **"Syria" → "Aram"** (v6).
- **"his soul was grieved" → "his soul could no longer bear"** (v16) — Hebrew וַתִּקְצַר נַפְשׁוֹ *va-tiqtzar nafsho*. "His soul was shortened" — idiom for compassion breaking.

### Notes
- **Two minor judges** (v1–5):
  - **Tola** — Issachar. 23 years.
  - **Jair** — Gilead. 22 years. Thirty sons on thirty donkeys in thirty cities — prosperity signal.
- **Seven-god apostasy** (v6) — most comprehensive list. Israel embraces nearly every neighbor's deity.
- **Yahweh refuses to save** (v13) — unprecedented moment. Israel's pattern of crisis-based repentance has worn thin. "Go and cry to the gods you've chosen."
- **Israel's genuine repentance** (v15–16) — puts away the foreign gods, serves Yahweh. Only then does his compassion break.
- **Ammonite crisis sets up Jephthah** — the people need a leader; the chiefs of Gilead make public offer (v18). Jephthah will answer.
