# Judges 21

[1] And the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had sworn at ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧, saying, "No man of us shall give his daughter to ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ as a wife."

[2] And the people came to ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧ and sat there until the evening before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and they lifted up their voice and wept a great weeping.

[3] And they said, "Why, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, has this come to be in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, that there should be missing today from ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ one tribe?"

[4] And it came to pass on the morrow that the people rose early and built an altar there, and they offered up burnt offerings and peace offerings.

[5] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said, "Who is there, out of all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, that did not come up in the assembly to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?" For the great oath had been against him who did not come up to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ at ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧, saying, "He shall surely be put to death."

[9] And the people were numbered, and behold, there was no man there of the inhabitants of ⟦jabesh-gilead|Yavesh Gilad|Jabesh-gilead⟧.

[10] And the congregation sent there twelve thousand men of the sons of ability, and they commanded them, saying, "Go, and you shall strike the inhabitants of ⟦jabesh-gilead|Yavesh Gilad|Jabesh-gilead⟧ with the edge of the sword, and the women and the little ones.

[12] And they found of the inhabitants of ⟦jabesh-gilead|Yavesh Gilad|Jabesh-gilead⟧ four hundred girls, virgins, who had not known a man by the lying of a male; and they brought them to the camp at Shiloh, which is in the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧.

[15] And the people were sorry for ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had made a breach in the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[16] And the elders of the congregation said, "What shall we do for those who remain, for wives? For woman has been destroyed from ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧."

[17] And they said, "A possession for those of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ who have escaped! And a tribe shall not be wiped out from ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[18] And we, we cannot give them wives of our daughters, for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ have sworn, saying, 'Cursed is he who gives a wife to ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧.'"

[20] And they commanded the sons of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, saying, "Go, and you shall lie in ambush in the vineyards;

[24] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went their ways from there at that time, each man to his tribe and to his clan, and they went out from there each man to his inheritance.

[25] In those days there was no king in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; each man did what was right in his own eyes.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton kept.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v2 with the article (הָאֱלֹהִים).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"children of Israel / of Benjamin" → "sons of"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי. "The men of Israel" (v1) is אִישׁ יִשְׂרָאֵל, a singular used of the whole body.
- **"There shall not any of us give" → "No man of us shall give"** (v1) — Hebrew אִישׁ מִמֶּנּוּ לֹא־יִתֵּן, literally "a man of us shall not give".
- **"to the house of God" → "to Bethel"** (v2) — Hebrew בֵּית־אֵל, the name of the town, as 20:18, 26. **"abode" → "sat"** — Hebrew יָשַׁב. **"lifted up their voices, and wept sore" → "lifted up their voice and wept a great weeping"** — Hebrew קוֹלָם (singular) and וַיִּבְכּוּ בְּכִי גָדוֹל, verb and noun of one root.
- **"O LORD God of Israel, why is this come to pass" → "Why, Yahweh, Elohim of Israel, has this come to be"** (v3) — the Hebrew begins with לָמָה "why"; there is no "O". **"that there should be to day one tribe lacking" → "that there should be missing today … one tribe"** — Hebrew לְהִפָּקֵד, the niphal of פָּקַד "number, muster": to be found missing at the count.
- **"offered" → "offered up"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲלוּ עֹלוֹת, verb and noun of one root (עָלָה "go up").
- **"that came not up with the congregation" → "that did not come up in the assembly"** (v5, 8) — Hebrew בַקָּהָל; "congregation" is עֵדָה (v10, 13, 16). **"they had made a great oath concerning him" → "the great oath had been against him"** — Hebrew הַשְּׁבוּעָה הַגְּדוֹלָה הָיְתָה לַאֲשֶׁר, literally "the great oath was for him who".
- **"shall surely be put to death" kept** (v5) — Hebrew מוֹת יוּמָת, the infinitive absolute with its verb: literally "dying he shall be made to die".
- **"repented them for Benjamin" → "were sorry for Benjamin"** (v6, 15) — Hebrew וַיִּנָּחֲמוּ אֶל and נִחָם לְ, the niphal of נָחַם: to feel sorrow, to be grieved over what has been done (as Gen 6:6). "Their brother" is literally "his brother".
- **"cut off" → "hewn off"** (v6) — Hebrew נִגְדַּע, the verb of hewing down a tree or a sacred pole (Deut 7:5).
- **"How shall we do for wives for them that remain" → "What shall we do for them, for those who remain, for wives?"** (v7, 16) — word for word. **"seeing we have sworn" → "And we, we have sworn"** — Hebrew וַאֲנַחְנוּ.
- **"there came none" → "no man had come"** (v8) — Hebrew לֹא בָא־אִישׁ.
- **"twelve thousand men of the valiantest" → "twelve thousand men of the sons of ability"** (v10) — Hebrew מִבְּנֵי הֶחָיִל; חַיִל as 20:44. **"smite" → "strike"** — Hebrew הִכָּה.
- **"every woman that hath lain by man" → "every woman who has known the lying of a male"** (v11); **"that had known no man by lying with any male" → "who had not known a man by the lying of a male"** (v12) — Hebrew מִשְׁכַּב זָכָר, as Num 31:17, 18. **"utterly destroy" → "devote to destruction"** — Hebrew תַּחֲרִימוּ, the verb of חֵרֶם: given over to Yahweh beyond recall by destroying it.
- **"four hundred young virgins" → "four hundred girls, virgins"** (v12) — Hebrew נַעֲרָה בְתוּלָה, two nouns.
- **"the rock Rimmon" → "the crag of Rimmon"** (v13) — Hebrew סֶלַע, as 20:45, 47. **"to call peaceably unto them" → "called out to them *shalom*"** — Hebrew וַיִּקְרְאוּ לָהֶם שָׁלוֹם, literally "they called to them: peace".
- **"which they had saved alive" → "whom they had let live"** (v14) — Hebrew חִיּוּ (piel). **"and yet so they sufficed them not" → "and even so they did not find enough for them"** — Hebrew וְלֹא־מָצְאוּ לָהֶם כֵּן, literally "and they did not find for them so"; "enough" is supplied from the sense (as Num 11:22).
- **"seeing the women are destroyed out of Benjamin" → "For woman has been destroyed from Benjamin"** (v16) — Hebrew אִשָּׁה, singular without the article.
- **"There must be an inheritance for them that be escaped of Benjamin" → "A possession for those of Benjamin who have escaped!"** (v17) — Hebrew יְרֻשַּׁת פְּלֵיטָה לְבִנְיָמִן, three words without a verb, literally "a possession of escape for Benjamin": יְרֻשָּׁה is what one takes possession of or inherits, פְּלֵיטָה "escape", those who have escaped. How the words hang together is not certain ("the escaped must have heirs" is another reading). **"be not destroyed" → "shall not be wiped out"** — Hebrew יִמָּחֶה.
- **"Howbeit we may not give" → "And we, we cannot give"** (v18) — Hebrew לֹא נוּכַל "we are not able". **"Cursed be he that giveth" → "Cursed is he who gives"** — the Hebrew has no verb.
- **"yearly" → "from year to year"** (v19) — Hebrew מִיָּמִים יָמִימָה, literally "from days to days", as Exod 13:10. **"on the north side of … on the east side of … on the south of"** → "north of … toward the rising of the sun from … south of": Hebrew מִצְּפוֹנָה לְ, מִזְרְחָה הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ לְ, מִנֶּגֶב לְ. It is not said who speaks these words of place, the elders or the narrator.
- **"Therefore they commanded"** (v20) — the qere וַיְצַוּוּ "they commanded" is translated; the written form (ketiv) is singular, "he commanded". **"lie in wait" → "lie in ambush"** — Hebrew אָרַב, as 20:29.
- **"catch you every man his wife" → "snatch for yourselves each man his wife"** (v21) — Hebrew וַחֲטַפְתֶּם, a verb found only here and in Ps 10:9 (of a lion seizing prey).
- **"to complain" → "to contend"** (v22) — the qere לָרִיב is translated (ketiv לרוב, the same verb in another spelling). **"Be favourable unto them for our sakes" → "Favor us with them"** — Hebrew חָנּוּנוּ אוֹתָם, the verb חָנַן "show favor" with two objects, as Gen 33:5; "them" is masculine in form. **"because we reserved not to each man his wife in the war" → "for we did not take each man his wife in the battle"** — Hebrew לֹא לָקַחְנוּ "we did not take" (in the battle against Jabesh-gilead). **"for ye did not give unto them at this time, that ye should be guilty" → "for it was not you who gave to them: at this time you would be guilty"** — Hebrew כִּי לֹא אַתֶּם נְתַתֶּם לָהֶם כָּעֵת תֶּאְשָׁמוּ, word for word; the object ("them") and the link ("otherwise") are not written. The sense is that the fathers have not broken the oath, because they did not give.
- **"took them wives" → "took wives"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׂאוּ נָשִׁים, literally "lifted up women" (the later expression for taking a wife, as Ruth 1:4). **"of them that danced, whom they caught" → "from the dancers, whom they had taken by robbery"** — Hebrew גָּזָלוּ, the verb "rob" (Lev 6:2, 4). **"repaired the cities" → "built the cities"** — Hebrew וַיִּבְנוּ.
- **"departed thence" → "went their ways from there"** (v24) — Hebrew וַיִּתְהַלְּכוּ (hitpael), "walked off, this way and that". **"every man" → "each man"** (v24, 25).
- **"every man did that which was right in his own eyes" → "each man did what was right in his own eyes"** (v25) — Hebrew אִישׁ הַיָּשָׁר בְּעֵינָיו יַעֲשֶׂה, literally "a man, the right in his eyes he would do" (the verb is a form of what was usual). יָשָׁר is "straight, right" (of a person "upright"), as Deut 12:8. The same sentence stands in 17:6; its first half in 18:1; 19:1.

### Notes
- **The oath** (v1, 7, 18) — Israel had sworn not to give daughters to Benjamin; six hundred men are left (20:47). Two ways are taken:
  1. **Jabesh-gilead** (v8–12) — the town that did not come up is devoted to destruction; four hundred virgins are brought to Shiloh.
  2. **Shiloh** (v19–23) — the rest snatch wives from the dancers at the feast of Yahweh.
- **The letter of the oath** — the fathers did not "give" (v22); the women are taken. The narrator reports it without comment.
- **Jabesh-gilead** — the tie with Benjamin goes on: Saul of Benjamin rescues the town (1 Sam 11), and its men bury him (1 Sam 31:11–13).
- **The last sentence** (v25) — the book closes with the words of 17:6.
