# Judges 18

[1] In those days there was no king in Israel, and in those days the tribe of the Danites was seeking an inheritance for themselves to dwell in, for up to that day no inheritance had fallen to them among the tribes of Israel.

[2] The children of Dan sent five men of their clan from their territory — men of valor, from Zorah and from Eshtaol — to scout out the land and search it. They said to them, "Go, search the land." They came to the hill country of Ephraim, to the house of Micah, and lodged there.

[3] When they were near the house of Micah, they recognized the voice of the young man the Levite; and they turned aside there and said to him, "Who brought you here? What are you doing in this place? What is your business here?"

[4] He said to them, "Thus and thus Micah has dealt with me: he has hired me, and I am his priest."

[5] They said to him, "Please inquire of *Elohim*, that we may know whether the journey on which we are going will succeed."

[6] The priest said to them, "Go in peace. The way in which you are going is before Yahweh."

[7] So the five men departed and came to Laish; they saw the people who were in it, living quietly after the manner of the Sidonians, peaceful and unsuspecting, with none in the land possessing authority who would humiliate them in anything; they were far from the Sidonians and had no dealings with anyone.

[8] When they came to their brothers at Zorah and Eshtaol, their brothers said to them, "What do you say?"

[9] They said, "Arise, let us go up against them, for we have seen the land, and behold, it is very good. Will you do nothing? Do not be slack in going in to take possession of the land.

[10] When you go, you will come to an unsuspecting people; the land is broad; *Elohim* has given it into your hands — a place where there is no lack of anything that is on the earth."

[11] Six hundred men of the clan of the Danites, armed with weapons of war, set out from Zorah and Eshtaol.

[12] They went up and encamped at Kiriath-jearim in Judah; therefore that place has been called Mahaneh-dan to this day; it lies west of Kiriath-jearim.

[13] From there they passed on to the hill country of Ephraim and came to the house of Micah.

[14] Then the five men who had gone to scout the region of Laish said to their brothers, "Do you know that there are in these houses an ephod, household gods, a carved image, and a cast image? Now therefore consider what you will do."

[15] They turned aside there and came to the house of the young man the Levite, to the house of Micah, and greeted him.

[16] Now the six hundred men of the children of Dan, armed with weapons of war, stood at the entrance of the gate.

[17] The five men who had gone to scout the land went up and entered and took the carved image, the ephod, the household gods, and the cast image, while the priest stood at the entrance of the gate with the six hundred men armed with weapons of war.

[18] When these men went into Micah's house and took the carved image, the ephod, the household gods, and the cast image, the priest said to them, "What are you doing?"

[19] They said to him, "Be silent; put your hand over your mouth. Come with us and be to us a father and a priest. Is it better for you to be priest to the house of one man, or to be priest to a tribe and a clan in Israel?"

[20] The priest's heart was glad; he took the ephod, the household gods, and the carved image, and went in the midst of the people.

[21] They turned and departed, putting the little ones, the livestock, and the goods in front of them.

[22] When they were some distance from Micah's house, the men who were in the houses near Micah's house were summoned and overtook the children of Dan.

[23] They called out to the children of Dan, who turned around and said to Micah, "What is wrong with you, that you have called out your men?"

[24] He said, "You have taken my gods that I made, and the priest, and gone away. What more do I have? How can you say to me, 'What is wrong with you?'"

[25] The children of Dan said to him, "Do not let your voice be heard among us, lest angry men fall upon you and you lose your life, with the lives of your household."

[26] The children of Dan went on their way; and when Micah saw that they were too strong for him, he turned and went back to his house.

[27] They took what Micah had made, and the priest he had, and came to Laish — to a people quiet and unsuspecting — and struck them with the edge of the sword and burned the city with fire.

[28] There was no one to deliver it, because it was far from Sidon and they had no dealings with anyone. It was in the valley that belongs to Beth-rehob. They rebuilt the city and dwelt in it.

[29] They called the name of the city Dan, after the name of Dan their ancestor, who was born to Israel — though the name of the city was formerly Laish.

[30] The children of Dan set up the carved image for themselves; and Jonathan son of Gershom son of Moses — he and his sons were priests to the tribe of the Danites until the day of the captivity of the land.

[31] So they set up Micah's carved image, which he made, all the time that the house of *Elohim* was at Shiloh.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"teraphim" → "household gods"** (throughout) — *teraphim*.
- **"graven / molten image" → "carved / cast image"** (v14, 17, 18, 30, 31).
- **"Manasseh" → "Moses"** (v30) — MT reads Manasseh, but with a suspended letter *nun* marking a scribal tradition that the original was "Moses" (*mosheh*). The scribes found it too scandalous that Moses' grandson would officiate an idolatrous priesthood. Modern translations restore "Moses."
- **"coasts" → "territory"** (v2).
- **"carriage" → "goods"** (v21) — archaic English.
- **"suited him" → "greeted him"** (v15).

### Notes
- **Danite migration** — the tribe that failed to hold its coastal inheritance (Judg 1:34) now migrates north. A morally ambiguous re-settlement: they conquer an unsuspecting city.
- **Laish / Dan** — peaceful northern city, destroyed. Becomes the northernmost Israelite city ("from Dan to Beersheba").
- **Theft of the idol** (v14–20) — the Danites brazenly steal Micah's entire cultic apparatus. The priest is delighted by the upgrade.
- **"Moses' grandson as idolatrous priest"** (v30) — if Jonathan truly descended from Moses via Gershom, the tribal Levite tradition is thoroughly corrupted. The scribal *nun*-suspension is a commentary on the tradition's discomfort.
- **Captivity of the land** (v30) — northern exile under Assyria (722 BC). The idolatrous cult at Dan continued centuries.
- **1 Kgs 12:29** — Jeroboam sets up a golden calf at Dan, institutionalizing northern-kingdom idolatry at the same city.
- **Shiloh vs Dan** (v31) — two competing cultic centers: the legitimate Shiloh-sanctuary and the illegitimate Dan-shrine. The appendix to Judges shows how early the northern corruption began.
